# Character Creation

Everyone starts at 1st level, on a wagon train leaving Tessine. All three
variant rules in play hand you more to work with rather than less, so build
the character you actually want to play and assume the numbers will keep up.

## Variant rules

| Rule | What it gives you | When you feel it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free Archetype** | An extra class feat slot at every even level, which can only be spent on archetype feats | Not at 1st level. From 2nd you can take a Dedication without giving up anything your class was going to give you |
| **Automatic Bonus Progression** | The numerical bonuses that normally come off magic items arrive on their own as you level | Not at 1st level either, but it changes what you buy from here on. See below |
| **Ancestry Paragon** | Two ancestry feats at 1st level instead of one, and another at every odd level after that | Immediately. Pick two |

**Automatic Bonus Progression is the one that changes your habits**, so it is
worth reading once properly. Attack potency arrives at 2nd level, a skill
bonus at 3rd, a second damage die at 4th, AC at 5th, Perception at 7th and
saves at 8th, all of it automatic and none of it bought. In practice:

- **Never budget for a fundamental rune.** No potency, no striking, no
  resilient, at any level. The pluses are yours already.
- **Magic items are still worth having**, and they are worth having for what
  they *do*. A weapon with a property rune, a wondrous item, a staff, a thing
  that lets you take an action nobody else can take.
- **Armour still carries its own item bonus to AC**, so armour is still a real
  purchase and still worth upgrading.

## Allowed content

All of it, as long as it makes sense in the world. Any book, any class, any
ancestry, any archetype, uncommon and rare included.

The only filter is fit. There is no separate approved list, and I would
rather say yes to something odd than run a table where everyone picked from
the same page. **If you are unsure whether something belongs here, ask me
first** — that is a two-minute conversation, and it beats finding out at
session three that the order your character swore to does not exist on this
continent. [The Nations](#The%20Nations) is the fastest way to check whether
somewhere would plausibly produce your character.

## Starting out

- **1st level.**
- **15 gp to spend**, the standard 150 silver, on common equipment. Buy the
  kit, and remember food and travelling gear.
- **Something special?** If there is an item, a heirloom or a piece of gear
  your character's backstory needs, bring it to me rather than working out
  whether you can afford it. Most of the time the answer is yes and it costs
  nothing.
- **You do not need to know the others.** A wagon train is how strangers end
  up sharing a fire for a fortnight. Knowing one of them is fine too, if you
  and they would rather.
