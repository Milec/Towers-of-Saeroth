# Gods, Peoples and the Roads

## The gods

Worship is ordinary and public. Most people keep one god closely and nod to
several others, and almost every nation has a patron whose priorities you can
read off its laws.

The ones you will meet most often: **Abadar** of contracts and honest wealth,
kept by merchants, courts and two whole nations; **Sarenrae** of healing and
redemption, whose hospices are everywhere; **Iomedae** of honourable valour,
who underwrites knightly orders; **Torag** the maker, in every forge-hall;
**Desna** of the road and the stars, kept by anyone who travels; **Gozreh** of
sea and storm; **Erastil** of the homestead; **Pharasma**, who governs birth
and death both, and is *not* a grim figure in most of the world.

Kept more quietly: **Calistria** for intrigue and revenge, **Norgorber** for
secrets, **Urgathoa** in exactly one nation, and the **Green Faith**, which
reveres the living world directly and finds the idea of a god who lives
elsewhere faintly absurd.

## The peoples

Humans are the most numerous and the most scattered. Beyond them the world is
genuinely various: dwarves in the high holds, several distinct elven peoples —
woodland, arctic, desert, aquatic and the dark-elven kin under the mountains —
gnomes and halflings thick in the inventive lowlands, centaurs and kholo and
catfolk out on the grasslands, leshys where the forests are oldest, and
orc and half-kin populations nearly everywhere.

A few nations have ancestry hierarchies written into law. Most do not. It is
worth knowing which is which before you arrive somewhere expecting to be
served.

## How anything gets anywhere

Four corridors carry most of the world's goods, and if your character has
travelled for a living they have been on at least one.

- **The Middle Sea run.** The Thurion Merchant Alliance and Aquoniti, between
  the continents. Silk and porcelain west, grain and armour and timber east.
- **The Salt Road.** Caravans and wells across the eastern deserts, from the
  great river to the frontier markets and out to the coast.
- **The Uplad circuit.** The overland road across the western south, where a
  nomad confederacy sells safe passage and the finest horses in the world.
- **The Voskreld roads and canals.** Grain, steel and machinery across the
  western centre, on the best-maintained roadbed anywhere.

Where they cross is where the money is, and where the trouble is.
