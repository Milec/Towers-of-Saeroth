# The Nations

Twenty-eight of them, as a traveller would rank them: by what they are known
for, not by what they can field.

## The western continent

| | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Vaelic Principality** | Law and chivalry. A High Prince elected for life by seven Elector Princes. Reliable allies, slow to change — and the only people in the world who ride drakes |
| **Voskreld Union** | Unity through integration. Standardized the coin, the measure and the roadbed, then built the network they run on |
| **Thesal Theocracy** | Faith and compassion. Sarenrae's hospices are its most visible export, and its clergy are trusted as mediators everywhere |
| **Corvane Republic** | Liberty and self-governance. An elected assembly, a printing trade, and a standing offer of shelter to anyone fleeing a pulpit |
| **Dalstan** | Zealotry and opulence. Devout to the point of cruelty, and richer than its piety should allow |
| **Quivar** | Culture and espionage. The cultural heart of the world, and never fully trusted |
| **Elven Confederacy** | Ancient traditions. A council of tribal chiefs, patient on a scale that annoys everyone else |
| **Nordheim** | Honour and resilience. Timber, longships and a High King elected at the Great Thing |
| **Stoneborn Holds** | Endurance and craftsmanship. Steel, gold and gems, and an assembly of High Thanes |
| **Undertide Reaches** | Subterranean survival and secrets. Sells secret-keeping as a service. Unnervingly hard to surprise |
| **Melisor Magocracy** | Arcane supremacy. Arrogant, brilliant, indispensable — in that order |
| **Silicar** | Innovation and agriculture. Small, and astonishingly inventive |
| **Kelvary March** | War as a trade. Twenty-odd chartered companies, the best drilled on the continent, available to whoever pays |
| **Tal Ulad** | Herds and horizons. Nomads who regard a border as a thing that happens to other people |
| **Sarrowmere** | Physic and poison. Everyone sends their dying there, and nobody entirely trusts them at dinner |
| **Lazarian Lichdom** | Undeath and forbidden knowledge. An immortal Lich Emperor. Universally feared |
| **Tessine** | Refuge and revenue. One harbour city, everyone's money, and no questions asked |

## The eastern continent

| | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Xian Ti** | Harmony and bureaucracy. A Celestial Emperor and an examination system, sophisticated and powerful |
| **Khazan Khaganate** | Martial conquest. Fearsome cavalry, and surprisingly honourable about it |
| **Thurigypt** | Strength through life. A Warrior Pharaoh, a great river, and a discipline everyone remarks on |
| **Qeshara Sultanate** | Trade, star-craft, and a court that argues in verse. Cosmopolitan and famously hard to negotiate with |
| **Sahenna Compact** | Clans, caravans, and memory kept without ink. The Salt Road runs on their word |
| **Kesmarch Frontier** | Settlement and self-reliance. Rugged, independent, and a little lawless at the edges |
| **Thornwild Confederation** | Primal instinct and beast-kinship. Wild, fiercely loyal to their own, dangerously easy to underestimate |
| **Cindral Ashlands** | Elemental fire and forging. Intense, unyielding, quietly generous to anyone who has earned it |
| **Ashkar Pale** | Endurance in the ash. They live under a volcano on purpose and are impossible to frighten |

## The sea powers

| | Known for |
| --- | --- |
| **Thurion Merchant Alliance** | Commerce above all. Rich, influential, and politically neutral when profitable. Almost nothing crosses the sea without touching them |
| **Aquoniti** | Mastery of the seas. Island fleets, pearls, and the shortest crossings in the world |
