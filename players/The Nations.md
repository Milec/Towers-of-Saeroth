# The Nations

Twenty-eight of them, as a traveller would rank them: by what they are known
for, not by what they can field. The second column is who you can expect to
see on the street there, and the third is whose name gets said over dinner —
the patron first, then whoever else keeps a temple worth finding. Two of these
nations have ancestry written into their law, and both say so below.

Tap the map for any of it. Tap a country's name in the table and the map will
show you where it is.

## The western continent

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vaelic Principality** | Humans, half-elves, half-orcs. Halflings live here and may not hold rank — ancestry is written into the law | Iomedae. Also Abadar, Torag | Law and chivalry. A High Prince elected for life by seven Elector Princes. Reliable allies, slow to change — and the only people in the world who ride drakes |
| **Voskreld Union** | Humans of five once-rival provinces, plus dwarves, halflings, goblins and half-elves | Abadar. Also Erastil, Gorum | Unity through integration. Standardized the coin, the measure and the roadbed, then built the network they run on |
| **Thesal Theocracy** | Everyone. The charter says so and the precinct bears it out | Sarenrae. Also Shelyn, Desna, Pharasma | Faith and compassion. Sarenrae's hospices are its most visible export, and its clergy are trusted as mediators everywhere |
| **Corvane Republic** | Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, tieflings, and whoever came off the last boat | Milani. Also Cayden Cailean, Abadar | Liberty and self-governance. An elected assembly, a printing trade, and a standing offer of shelter to anyone fleeing a pulpit |
| **Dalstan** | Humans hold the church. Aasimar are treated as living proof of favour. Tieflings get blamed on principle | Zon-Kuthon. Also Asmodeus | Zealotry and opulence. Devout to the point of cruelty, and richer than its piety should allow |
| **Quivar** | Humans and half-elves, and very few others anywhere near the court | Shelyn. Also Calistria | Culture and espionage. The cultural heart of the world, and never fully trusted |
| **Elven Confederacy** | Forest, winter, desert and sea elves, and elven peoples with no name in the common tongue | Ketephys. Also Yuelral, Findeladlara, the Green Faith | Ancient traditions. A council of tribal chiefs, patient on a scale that annoys everyone else |
| **Nordheim** | Humans, dwarves and goliaths | Cayden Cailean. Also Gorum, Desna, and Trudd in the old sagas | Honour and resilience. Timber, longships and a High King elected at the Great Thing |
| **Stoneborn Holds** | Dwarves, and a few human merchant families there long enough to stop being called visitors | Torag. Also Magrim, Abadar | Endurance and craftsmanship. Steel, gold and gems, and an assembly of High Thanes |
| **Undertide Reaches** | Duskwalkers, umbral gnomes, kobolds, and automatons built for ground that kills flesh | Norgorber. Also Pharasma | Subterranean survival and secrets. Sells secret-keeping as a service. Unnervingly hard to surprise |
| **Melisor Magocracy** | Humans, elves, gnomes, tieflings, fetchlings. Aptitude counts for more here than ancestry | Nethys. Also Irori | Arcane supremacy. Arrogant, brilliant, indispensable — in that order |
| **Silicar** | Gnomes, halflings and leshys | Brigh. Also Erastil | Innovation and agriculture. Small, and astonishingly inventive |
| **Kelvary March** | Humans, half-orcs, hobgoblins, dwarves, orcs and centaurs, sorted by what a company needs | Gorum. Also Abadar | War as a trade. Twenty-odd chartered companies, the best drilled on the continent, available to whoever pays |
| **Tal Ulad** | Humans, centaurs, kholo, catfolk, half-orcs and gnolls, and all of them ride | Gozreh. Also Desna, Erastil | Herds and horizons. Nomads who regard a border as a thing that happens to other people |
| **Sarrowmere** | Humans, grippli, lizardfolk, halflings, leshys and ratfolk | Pharasma. Also Milani | Physic and poison. Everyone sends their dying there, and nobody entirely trusts them at dinner |
| **Lazarian Lichdom** | The living are the minority. Dhampirs, vampires, necromancers, and the intelligent dead who hold most of the posts | Urgathoa. Also Norgorber, and Pharasma by people who would rather not be raised | Undeath and forbidden knowledge. An immortal Lich Emperor. Universally feared |
| **Tessine** | Every ancestry on the continent, in numbers nobody has managed to count | Abadar. Also Cayden Cailean, Desna | Refuge and revenue. One harbour city, everyone's money, and no questions asked |

## The eastern continent

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Xian Ti** | Humans, tengu, kitsune, samsarans, wayang, nagaji and tanuki | Irori. Also Shizuru, Daikitsu, Abadar | Harmony and bureaucracy. A Celestial Emperor and an examination system, sophisticated and powerful |
| **Khazan Khaganate** | Orcs, hobgoblins, goblins, bugbears, ogres, kobolds, centaurs and minotaurs | Gorum. Also Desna | Martial conquest. Fearsome cavalry, and surprisingly honourable about it |
| **Thurigypt** | Lizardfolk, nagaji and kobolds | Pharasma, for births rather than deaths. Also Sarenrae, Erastil | Strength through life. A Warrior Pharaoh, a great river, and a discipline everyone remarks on |
| **Qeshara Sultanate** | Humans, and an ifrit minority who hold the glassworks and the star-towers | Abadar. Also Shelyn | Trade, star-craft, and a court that argues in verse. Cosmopolitan and famously hard to negotiate with |
| **Sahenna Compact** | A kholo majority, with humans and duskwalkers in every clan | Pharasma. Also Gorum | Clans, caravans, and memory kept without ink. The Salt Road runs on their word |
| **Kesmarch Frontier** | Humans, halflings, dwarves, half-orcs and gnomes, out of every neighbour's overflow | Erastil. Also Desna, Cayden Cailean | Settlement and self-reliance. Rugged, independent, and a little lawless at the edges |
| **Thornwild Confederation** | Catfolk, gnolls, ratfolk, vanara, kholo and grippli | The Green Faith. Also Gozreh | Primal instinct and beast-kinship. Wild, fiercely loyal to their own, dangerously easy to underestimate |
| **Cindral Ashlands** | Ifrits, oreads, humans, and dwarves of the volcanic forge lines | Angradd. Also Torag, Sarenrae | Elemental fire and forging. Intense, unyielding, quietly generous to anyone who has earned it |
| **Ashkar Pale** | Humans, oreads, dwarves, ifrits and halflings | Erastil. Also Pharasma | Endurance in the ash. They live under a volcano on purpose and are impossible to frighten |

## The sea powers

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Thurion Merchant Alliance** | Every ancestry, weighted toward whoever crews the ships | Abadar. Also Desna, Norgorber | Commerce above all. Rich, influential, and politically neutral when profitable. Almost nothing crosses the sea without touching them |
| **Aquoniti** | Merfolk, tritons, azarketi, sea elves, athamaru and tripkee | Gozreh. Also Desna, Besmara | Mastery of the seas. Island fleets, pearls, and the shortest crossings in the world |
