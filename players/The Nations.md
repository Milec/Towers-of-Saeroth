# The Nations

Twenty-eight of them, as a traveller would rank them: by what they are known
for, not by what they can field. The second column is who you can expect to
see on the street there, the third is [whose name gets said over dinner](#Gods%2C%20Peoples%20and%20the%20Roads/The%20gods) (the
patron first, then whoever else keeps a temple worth finding), and the fourth
is who is actually in charge. Two of these nations have ancestry written into
their law, and both say so below.

Tap the map for any of it. Tap a country's name in the table and the map will
show you where it is.

## The western continent

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Government | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vaelic Principality** | Humans, half-elves, half-orcs. Halflings live here and may not hold rank — ancestry is written into the law | Iomedae. Also Abadar, Torag | Elective monarchy — a High Prince chosen for life by seven Elector Princes | Law and chivalry. Reliable allies, slow to change, and the only people in the world who ride drakes |
| **Voskreld Union** | Humans of five once-rival provinces, plus dwarves, halflings, goblins and half-elves | Abadar. Also Erastil, Gorum | Crown with a Provincial Council of the old duchies | Unity through integration. Standardized the coin, the measure and the roadbed, then built the network they run on |
| **Thesal Theocracy** | Everyone. The charter says so and the precinct bears it out | Sarenrae. Also Shelyn, Desna, Pharasma | Theocracy — a Council of High Clerics, one seat per recognised faith | Faith and compassion. Sarenrae's hospices are its most visible export, and its clergy are trusted as mediators everywhere |
| **Corvane Republic** | Humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, tieflings, and whoever came off the last boat | Milani. Also Cayden Cailean, Abadar | Republic — an elected Assembly under a term-limited Chancellor | Liberty and self-governance. A printing trade, and a standing offer of shelter to anyone fleeing a pulpit |
| **Dalstan** | Humans hold the church. Aasimar are treated as living proof of favour. Tieflings get blamed on principle | Zon-Kuthon. Also Asmodeus | Theocratic monarchy — a Grand Inquisitor-King anointed for life | Zealotry and opulence. Devout to the point of cruelty, and richer than its piety should allow |
| **Quivar** | Humans and half-elves, and very few others anywhere near the court | Shelyn. Also Calistria | Absolute monarchy, with a court that does the real work | Culture and espionage. The cultural heart of the world, and never fully trusted |
| **Elven Confederacy** | Forest, winter, desert and sea elves, and elven peoples with no name in the common tongue | Ketephys. Also Yuelral, Findeladlara, the Green Faith | Council of tribal chiefs | Ancient traditions, and patience on a scale that annoys everyone else |
| **Nordheim** | Humans, dwarves and goliaths | Cayden Cailean. Also Gorum, Desna, and Trudd in the old sagas | Elective kingship — a High King chosen by the Jarls at the Great Thing | Honour and resilience. Timber, longships, and a king who can be argued with in public |
| **Stoneborn Holds** | Dwarves, and a few human merchant families there long enough to stop being called visitors | Torag. Also Magrim, Abadar | Assembly of High Thanes | Endurance and craftsmanship. Steel, gold and gems, and a very long memory for debts |
| **Undertide Reaches** | Duskwalkers, umbral gnomes, kobolds, and automatons built for ground that kills flesh | Norgorber. Also Pharasma | Warden-Conclave of the deep holds | Subterranean survival and secrets. Sells secret-keeping as a service. Unnervingly hard to surprise |
| **Melisor Magocracy** | Humans, elves, gnomes, tieflings, fetchlings. Aptitude counts for more here than ancestry | Nethys. Also Irori | Magocracy — a Council of Archmages seated by academy | Arcane supremacy. Arrogant, brilliant, indispensable — in that order |
| **Silicar** | Gnomes, halflings and leshys | Brigh. Also Erastil | Council of guildmasters and elected engineers | Innovation and agriculture. Small, and astonishingly inventive |
| **Kelvary March** | Humans, half-orcs, hobgoblins, dwarves, orcs and centaurs, sorted by what a company needs | Gorum. Also Abadar | Muster Court of company captains and paymasters | War as a trade. Twenty-odd chartered companies, the best drilled on the continent, available to whoever pays |
| **Tal Ulad** | Humans, centaurs, kholo, catfolk, half-orcs and gnolls, and all of them ride | Gozreh. Also Desna, Erastil | Herd-councils, with a Season-Speaker elected each spring | Herds and horizons. Nomads who regard a border as a thing that happens to other people |
| **Sarrowmere** | Humans, grippli, lizardfolk, halflings, leshys and ratfolk | Pharasma. Also Milani | Confederation of eleven cantons, each run by its physicians’ guild | Physic and poison. Everyone sends their dying there, and nobody entirely trusts them at dinner |
| **Lazarian Lichdom** | The living are the minority. Dhampirs, vampires, necromancers, and the intelligent dead who hold most of the posts | Urgathoa. Also Norgorber, and Pharasma by people who would rather not be raised | Autocracy under an immortal Lich Emperor | Undeath and forbidden knowledge. Universally feared, and it has never had to say so |
| **Tessine** | Every ancestry on the continent, in numbers nobody has managed to count | Abadar. Also Cayden Cailean, Desna | Signory of resident factors, chaired by a Harbourmaster | Refuge and revenue. One harbour city, everyone's money, and no questions asked |

## The eastern continent

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Government | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Xian Ti** | Humans, tengu, kitsune, samsarans, wayang, nagaji and tanuki | Irori. Also Shizuru, Daikitsu, Abadar | Imperial bureaucracy — a Celestial Emperor and his scholar officials | Harmony and bureaucracy. An examination system anyone may sit, sophisticated and powerful |
| **Khazan Khaganate** | Orcs, hobgoblins, goblins, bugbears, ogres, kobolds, centaurs and minotaurs | Gorum. Also Desna | Elective khaganate — a Great Khagan chosen by the assembled khans | Martial conquest. Fearsome cavalry, and surprisingly honourable about it |
| **Thurigypt** | Lizardfolk, nagaji and kobolds | Pharasma, for births rather than deaths. Also Sarenrae, Erastil | Warrior Pharaoh advised by priest-kings | Strength through life. A great river, and a discipline everyone remarks on |
| **Qeshara Sultanate** | Humans, and an ifrit minority who hold the glassworks and the star-towers | Abadar. Also Shelyn | Sultan chosen by the Court of Viziers from the caravan-houses | Trade, star-craft, and a court that argues in verse. Cosmopolitan and famously hard to negotiate with |
| **Sahenna Compact** | A kholo majority, with humans and duskwalkers in every clan | Pharasma. Also Gorum | Council of Elders, one matriarch per clan | Clans, caravans, and memory kept without ink. The Salt Road runs on their word |
| **Kesmarch Frontier** | Humans, halflings, dwarves, half-orcs and gnomes, out of every neighbour's overflow | Erastil. Also Desna, Cayden Cailean | Elected Territorial Council under a founding charter | Settlement and self-reliance. Rugged, independent, and a little lawless at the edges |
| **Thornwild Confederation** | Catfolk, gnolls, ratfolk, vanara, kholo and grippli | The Green Faith. Also Gozreh | The Circle of First Packs, one voice per founding lineage | Primal instinct and beast-kinship. Wild, fiercely loyal to their own, dangerously easy to underestimate |
| **Cindral Ashlands** | Ifrits, oreads, humans, and dwarves of the volcanic forge lines | Angradd. Also Torag, Sarenrae | The Emberlord, chosen by trial-by-forge | Elemental fire and forging. Intense, unyielding, quietly generous to anyone who has earned it |
| **Ashkar Pale** | Humans, oreads, dwarves, ifrits and halflings | Erastil. Also Pharasma | A Warden elected for seven years, who may order an evacuation alone | Endurance in the ash. They live under a volcano on purpose and are impossible to frighten |

## The sea powers

| | Who you'll meet | Who they keep | Government | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Thurion Merchant Alliance** | Every ancestry, weighted toward whoever crews the ships | Abadar. Also Desna, Norgorber | Merchant Senate elected by the great trade houses | Commerce above all. ## Naming

Every nation draws on a real language for the way its names sound, which is
the fastest way to make a character sound like they come from somewhere. Pick
the row, borrow the register, and invent freely inside it. The place named in
the last column is the capital.

| | Names sound like | For example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vaelic Principality** | Germanic, in the Holy Roman register: hard consonants and compound surnames | Konrad Vosseler, Adelheid von Marck. The capital is **Reichsmund** |
| **Voskreld Union** | Slavic | Miroslav Petrek, Danika Vetrova. **Voskgrad** |
| **Thesal Theocracy** | Latin and ecclesiastical, often a title in place of a surname | Serapha of the Third Hospice, Brother Ottavian. **Concord** |
| **Corvane Republic** | Plain Anglo-Dutch, mercantile, short | Wilhelmina Croft, Ansel Deeb. **Vantry** |
| **Dalstan** | Slavic given names under gilded titles | Yevka Doranin, Inquisitor Vasil Krume. **Ostravin**, called the Gilded See |
| **Quivar** | French | Amélie Bonnard, Gaspard Villiers. **Valmont** |
| **Elven Confederacy** | Elvish: long vowels, soft endings, no hurry | Sylandriel, Thaeloth Anuvar. The council grove is **Sylanthir** |
| **Nordheim** | Old Norse, with patronymics rather than surnames | Sigrún Ketilsdóttir, Hravn Bjarnsson. **Hravnfjord** |
| **Stoneborn Holds** | Compounds that describe a trade or a deed | Durgan Ironmark, Halla Stonewright. **Highforge** |
| **Undertide Reaches** | The same compounds gone quiet and downward | Vell Downkeep, Ashen Marrow. **Sunkenhold** |
| **Melisor Magocracy** | Latinate and invented for effect, because half of them chose it | Thessaly Orne, Magister Calvane. **Thelemar** |
| **Silicar** | English compounds out of a working countryside | Pim Applewhite, Tilda Locke. **Brightfurrow** |
| **Kelvary March** | Blunt northern English, one syllable where two would do | Rook Harrow, Beatrix Vane. **Harrowgate** |
| **Tal Ulad** | Berber and Maghrebi Arabic, with the clan in the name | Idir ag Ulad, Tanit oult Meskan. No capital; the Speaker's camp moves |
| **Sarrowmere** | English fenland, damp and old | Wren Ilmen, Cobb Marrish. **Ilmen Wharf** |
| **Lazarian Lichdom** | Germanic gone gothic, and the dead keep the name they died with | Isolde Vane, Ehrhardt Mool. **Grauthaven** |
| **Tessine** | Italian, with the house name doing the work | Lucrezia Sarni, Matteo Falier. The city is the nation: **Tessine** |
| **Xian Ti** | Chinese, family name first, and a rank if they have one | Lin Renshu, Minister Zhao Wei. **Renshan**, the Celestial City |
| **Khazan Khaganate** | Mongol and Turkic | Batu, Altani of the Iron Hills. **Ordu-Khazan**, which strikes camp and moves |
| **Thurigypt** | Egyptian | Nefret, Setha-Ankh. **Setharu** |
| **Qeshara Sultanate** | Persian and Arabic, and the court prefers the longer form | Roshanak, Vizier Tahir al-Myrrh. **Myrrhkand** |
| **Sahenna Compact** | West African given names; clan names are plain compounds | Ashira of Clan Longmarch, Bem of Clan Salthollow. **Bonemarket** |
| **Kesmarch Frontier** | Colonial English, scriptural first names, hard surnames | Josiah Pike, Nell Awber. **Charterhold** |
| **Thornwild Confederation** | Descriptions rather than names, and no surnames at all | Bramble, Fennec Nine-Rivers. The Circle meets at **Rootmeet** |
| **Cindral Ashlands** | Fire compounds, and ifrit names that predate the language | Zafira Cinderlock, Bhast. **Emberthrone** |
| **Ashkar Pale** | Clipped and practical, often after the ground itself | Hesper Vetch, Orin Dalk. **Vetch Landing** |
| **Thurion Merchant Alliance** | Italian maritime, the house before the person | Renzo of Casa Ilvano, Serafina Doro. **Vessene** |
| **Aquoniti** | Greek, and the sea words are theirs | Nerissa, Kallias of the Reef. **Thalassar** |
