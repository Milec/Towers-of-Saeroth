---
title: Clan Salthollow
type: faction
matriarch: Ket Salthollow
---

Salthollow works the salt pans nearest [[Thurigypt]], which made it the
richest clan in the [[Sahenna Compact]] for three generations and has made
it the most exposed one for the last two years. The witchcraft accusation
circulating Thurigypt's trade routes started on Salthollow's own doorstep,
and Ket Salthollow has watched her clan's caravans get searched at
crossings that never used to bother.

- **Matriarch** **Ket Salthollow** — sharp-tempered where the accusation is
  concerned, and the loudest voice at Council for answering it rather than
  waiting it out
- **Holdings** The salt pans along the Thurigypt border, worked in shifts
  that never fully stop
- **Want** The Council to let her send a formal reply to Setharu instead of
  treating two years of silence as a strategy.
- **Won't** Stop trading with Thurigypt over an insult, whatever it costs
  her pride — the pans are worth more than Setharu's opinion of her rites.
