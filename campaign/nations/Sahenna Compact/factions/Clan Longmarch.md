title: Clan Longmarch
type: faction
matriarch: Vashtu Longmarch
---

The oldest warband clan in the [[Sahenna Compact]], and the one every
caravan-guard contract still gets measured against. Longmarch riders held
the routes to [[Kesmarch Frontier]] alone for two generations before any
other clan sold a blade abroad, and Vashtu Longmarch has never quite
accepted that the arrangement stopped being exclusive.

- **Matriarch** **Vashtu Longmarch** — proud, exacting, and increasingly
  aware that pride doesn't cover a warband's costs the way it used to
- **Holdings** The northern grazing march toward Kesmarch, and first right
  to caravan-guard contracts by a custom nobody has formally overturned
- **Want** Vashtu wants Council precedence — the first word at any
  session, by old right — restored to something more than ceremony.
- **Won't** Take a contract Clan [[Clan Duskherd|Duskherd]] is bidding
  against, no matter how much it costs her to refuse it on principle.
- **Relations** Longmarch's warbands are proud and increasingly broke;
  [[Clan Duskherd|Duskherd]]'s cattle wealth is new and increasingly the
  only thing funding them. Neither matriarch will say which fact bothers
  Vashtu more.
