---
title: Clan Farsight
type: faction
matriarch: Oba Farsight
---

Farsight trains more Storytellers than every other clan in the
[[Sahenna Compact]] combined, which means Farsight's memory of a treaty is,
in practice, the Compact's memory of it. Oba Farsight has never once had to
argue that point. Nobody has ever tried to argue it with her.

- **Matriarch** **Oba Farsight** — measured, unreadable, and the only
  matriarch who can recite every other clan's own treaties back to them
  word for word
- **Holdings** The Storytellers' training circles, based nearest Bonemarket
  of any clan's own ground, though the city itself belongs to none of them
- **Want** A Storyteller seated at every negotiation the Compact makes with
  an outside power, not summoned after the terms are already spoken.
- **Won't** Let a Storyteller's account be entered as evidence in a dispute
  between two other clans. Farsight's neutrality is the whole of its
  authority, and it has never once bent it for advantage.
