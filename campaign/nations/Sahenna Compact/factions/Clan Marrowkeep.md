title: Clan Marrowkeep

---

Every clan in the [[Sahenna Compact]] keeps its own Bonekeepers, but
Marrowkeep's ossuaries are the oldest, and a rite performed nowhere else is
performed there. When a dispute turns on what the ancestors would have
wanted — which is more disputes than outsiders assume — it's a Marrowkeep
Bonekeeper both sides send for.

- **Matriarch** **Yara Marrowkeep** — solemn in public and, by every account
  of the clans who've hosted her, considerably less so at home
- **Holdings** The oldest ossuaries in the Compact, kept rather than owned —
  Marrowkeep's authority is customary, not territorial
- **Want** For the newer clans to stop treating a Bonekeeper's rite as a
  formality to get through before the real negotiating starts.
- **Won't** Perform a rite for coin. Every other clan pays Marrowkeep in
  standing, which is worth more and considerably harder to spend.
