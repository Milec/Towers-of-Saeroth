Borders in the Feyreach move when the mood of the land changes, and
contracts here are binding in ways lawyers elsewhere can't replicate — a
Feyreach bargain enforces itself, with consequences that scale to exactly
how cleverly it was worded. Diplomats from other nations are warned, more
than once, never to accept a gift, a favor, or a compliment without
understanding what it will cost.

- **Races** [[Ancestries/Sprite|Sprites]], [[Ancestries/Changeling (Player Core)|Changelings]], [[Ancestries/Heritages/Fey-touched Gnome (Player Core)|Fey-Touched Gnomes]], [[Ancestries/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshies]]
- **Economic Specialties** Bound bargains, glamour-work, wild-magic components, seasonal fruits that exist nowhere else, favor-brokering
- **Culture** A promise is sacred because it is literally binding. Cleverness is prized above honesty — nobody in the Feyreach lies outright, but plenty of ruin has been caused by a truth spoken exactly.
- **Military** Glamoured skirmishers, beast-bonded riders, hedge witches, oathbound duelists
- **Government** The Shifting Throne — rulership passes at the whim of the court's fey patrons
- **Reputation** Beautiful, generous, and never to be bargained with carelessly
- **Relations** Claims an ancient, half-forgotten kinship with the [[Elven Confederacy]] through the First World; unsettles [[Silicar]] engineers, whose clockwork behaves strangely near Feyreach borders
