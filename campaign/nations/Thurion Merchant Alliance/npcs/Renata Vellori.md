*Proposed NPC for [[Session 5 - The Search Camp]]. Her introduction and the reports described here have not happened in play.*

Renata reports on the expedition for a Thurion commercial office. Her dispatches concern spending, missing shipments, and whether the search is likely to produce anything worth the money. She writes down the speaker's name before their statement, a habit that makes some people more careful and others much louder.

She comes from [[Thurion Merchant Alliance|Thurion]], where an inaccurate report can follow a clerk longer than a failed venture. She has no authority to settle [[Casa Berruel]]'s insurance claim. Being a correspondent does not give her access to every house's private accounts.

**Want:** a report her principals can act on, with her own work attached to it.

**Won't:** alter a witness's words or certify a death, delivery, or loss she cannot support.

**At the table:** she offers to read a statement back before asking anyone to sign it. She has a dry laugh and a carefully packed spare pen for the person who always borrows hers.

> "You think the owners meant you to die. I will record that you think it. Now show me which part you can prove."

## The caravan report

Renata asks for [[Milo Calvetti]]'s account and lets the party add theirs. The cargo-only insurance policy, Melisor flask, and lost wagons matter to her. She separates these observations from the party's belief that they were intended victims. She does not produce a secret instruction to murder them just because they ask whether one exists.

She can send a request for the dispatch manifest and cargo policy through her ordinary commercial contacts. It is a request for a later answer, not instant remote access to a bank. Before introducing any reply, the GM should decide what those records actually establish about the existing conspiracy.

For [[The Missing Survey Stores]], she can write the recovery agreement and witness Tavian's count on return. She makes the terms explicit before anyone leaves, including payment for a negotiated recovery.

## Relationships and pressure

- [[Octavia Sere]] thinks Renata reports uncertainty as delay. Renata thinks Octavia promises too much to keep the sponsors patient. Both need the search to continue honestly.
- [[Tavian Calvo]] knows the stores better than she does. She trusts his count once she has seen it, and puts corrections in the margin instead of accusing him of theft.
- [[Karoline]] can speak for herself. Renata records her account of the waystation if offered, without treating the party's explanation as the captain's sworn statement.

**A useful choice:** Renata offers a copy of the party's own statement. Accepting it creates a clear record; refusing to sign does not bar them from camp or the recovery job.

## In danger

Renata finds cover, calls for the guards, and protects the witness notes. She carries a knife for the road but does not volunteer to fight the raiders.
