title: Casa Berruel

One of the great houses of the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]], and the one the
Merchant Senate's tension is about. Casa Berruel underwrote a decade of
insurance on terms that were excellent every year the sea was kind, and has
spent the last two years discovering how much of its book it cannot actually
pay. Nothing has failed yet. Everything is about to.

The house still looks entirely solvent, which is the problem: it is solvent in
the way a ship is dry until it isn't, and the factors who suspect are the ones
deciding whether to warn the other houses or trade ahead of them.

- **Seat** Vessene, in the third-oldest counting-house on the harbour front
- **Head** Factor-Principal Idelmo Berruel, who inherited the book and did not
  read all of it for two years
- **Trade** Marine insurance, freight brokerage, and lately any charter it can
  get at any margin
- **Reputation** Old money, dull, safe — which is worth more to them right now
  than any single cargo
- **Faith** [[Abadar]], loudly, in a counting-house with a shrine at the door.
  There is also a [[Norgorber]] alcove behind the strongroom, which every great
  house has and none discuss
- **Tension** It has taken a [[Melisor Magocracy]] charter it cannot profit
  from honestly, insured the consignment with the rival Signory at
  [[Tessine]], and arranged through intermediaries for the consignment not to
  arrive. The settlement covers the book. Half of it goes to somebody else
- **Weakness** The fraud requires the loss to look natural, which means it
  requires nobody competent to be standing next to it when it happens

*Casa Berruel is not a villain with a plan for the world. It is a respectable
institution doing arithmetic, and the arithmetic says some people on a road
have to lose everything so that the house does not.*
