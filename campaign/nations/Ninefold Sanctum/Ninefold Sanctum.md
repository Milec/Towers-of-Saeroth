The Sanctum keeps records. Not spells, not treasure — records: treaties,
lineages, debts, borders, the exact wording of oaths sworn four centuries ago
by nations that have since changed their names. Nine abbeys in nine mountain
valleys hold nine copies of everything, and the order's single rule is that it
takes no side, ever, about anything. This makes it the only institution every
nation on the continent will accept as a witness, and the only one every
nation is quietly frightened of.

- **Capital** Vault Abbey, the first of the nine, cut into a valley head where the only road in is overlooked for its entire length
- **Geography** Nine high valleys strung along the mountain spine, linked by mule roads that close in winter; the abbeys are the only settlements of any size
- **Races** [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Human|Humans]], [[Samsaran]], [[Gnome|Gnomes]], [[Duskwalker|Duskwalkers]], [[Automaton|Automatons]]
- **Economic Specialties** Archives and certified copies, arbitration, escrow, cartography, bookbinding and inks meant to last a thousand years
- **Military** Abbey wardens only — enough to hold a valley road, and pointedly never enough to threaten anyone
- **Government** Nine abbots in permanent conclave; a ruling requires all nine, which is why rulings are rare and nobody appeals them
- **Reputation** Incorruptible, humourless, and in possession of far too much
- **Faith** [[Irori]] for the discipline the work demands, and [[Pharasma]] for the keeping of what is finished. The abbots insist these are the same virtue
- **Tension** A nation has formally requested that a record be corrected. The conclave has not answered in two years, and the request itself is now in the archive

*A neutral party with leverage over everyone. Useful as a quest-giver, a
McGuffin vault, and a place where a party can learn something true.*
- **Relations**
    - [[Stoneborn Holds]] — **Friendly**: The Holds cut the abbeys and keep the passes open; the Sanctum keeps the Holds' charters, which is the older debt
    - [[Tal Ulad]] — **Friendly**: The only power that ever wrote the herd routes down as the Ulad describe them, rather than as someone else's border
    - [[Kelvary March]] — **Trade**: Every Kelvary contract is lodged with the Sanctum, which is the only reason any of them are enforceable
    - [[Vaelic Principality]] — **Trade**: Every Vaelic coronation is witnessed and recorded at Vault Abbey, and has been for four centuries
    - [[Melisor Magocracy]] — **Friction**: The academies have wanted copying rights for two centuries and the conclave has refused for two centuries
    - [[Undertide Reaches]] — **Friction**: Something in the archive concerns the Reaches, and the Reaches have asked, politely and repeatedly, to read it
