The Skyholds drift on wind-currents no surface-bound cartographer has ever
mapped correctly, and Windrift citizens consider that a feature, not an
inconvenience. A Skyhold that stays anchored too long is thought to be
hiding something. Trade caravans that reach the surface are treated as
expeditions, not commerce — landing is the dangerous part.

- **Races** [[Ancestries/Strix|Strix]], [[Ancestries/Sylph|Sylphs]], [[Ancestries/Aasimar|Aasimar]], [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|Humans]] (born aloft, rarely visit the ground)
- **Economic Specialties** Windsilk, altitude-cured goods, aerial cartography, skyhold engineering, rare cloud-borne minerals
- **Culture** Movement is freedom, and freedom is the highest good. A Windrift captain's word is only as strong as their willingness to leave if it's broken.
- **Military** Griffon riders, skyhold gunnery crews, wind-current raiders, sky scouts
- **Government** Highwind Assembly of Skyhold captains
- **Reputation** Elusive, fiercely independent, hard to pin down in every sense
- **Relations** Contests trade-route dominance with [[Aquoniti]]; occasionally contracts aerial scouts to the [[Vaelic Principality]] in exchange for safe harbor rights
