Kesmarch is young, expanding, and built almost entirely out of other
countries' overflow. Second sons and disinherited daughters. Failed
harvests, and wars somebody else lost. Barn-raising and an armed homestead
are the same instinct here, because the frontier does not forgive going it
alone, and because land out here is claimed rather than granted. Claimed
land has to be held.

- **Founded** 2317 AR, the youngest nation on the board. The Territorial Council's charter is dated the year after the [[The Pilgrim's Peace|Pilgrim's Peace]]. Half the first homesteads went to families shipped out of the Hungry Year who had nothing to go back to.
- **Capital** Charterhold, a stockade town that has outgrown its walls twice and rebuilt them further out both times
- **Geography** Open plains giving way to jungle edge in the south; the border is wherever the newest homestead stands this year
- **Government** Elected Territorial Council, ratified under a founding charter
- **Races** [[Human|Humans]], [[Halfling|Halflings]], [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Half-Orc|Half-Orcs]], [[Gnome|Gnomes]]; settlers out of every neighbouring nation's overflow
- **Faith** Patron [[Erastil]], god of the homestead, the hunt, and the family that holds both. [[Desna]] is kept by anyone still travelling. [[Cayden Cailean]] by everyone who left somewhere else to get here
- **Economic Specialties** Furs, timber, ranching, homestead grain, prospecting, wagon-trade
- **Military** Militia rangers, homestead defense companies, scout cavalry, frontier marshals
- **History** Kesmarch has never fought a war, and has been in a low-grade shooting quarrel on two frontiers for most of its existence. Both have been before the Concord Table: the Thornwild charters eleven times, the Khazan grazing eight. Neither has ever been settled there. The Council has learned to treat that as the point.
- **Reputation** Rugged, independent, and a little lawless at the edges
- **Tension** The Territorial Council keeps issuing homestead charters for land it has never surveyed, and some of those deeds describe [[Thornwild Confederation]] ground
- **Relations** — in brief; every tie in full, and the web, are in [[Political Relations]]
    - [[Nordheim]] — **Friendly**: Half the frontier's charters are held by second sons out of the fjords, and the Territorial Council still runs its founding assembly on Nordheim's Thing procedure
    - [[Silicar]] — **Friendly**: The frontier was built with Silicar ploughs and Silicar pumps, and Silicar's engineers treat the Kesmarch homesteads as a proving ground they get paid to visit
    - [[Aquoniti]] — **Trade**: The island fleet works the frontier's river mouths, which is how a landlocked-feeling frontier turns out to have a coast after all
    - [[Ashkar Pale]] — **Trade**: Grain and ash-glaze ware to the frontier towns, and the Pale's salvage crews hire out to Kesmarch after a bad season
    - [[Qeshara Sultanate]] — **Trade**: The frontier end of the Salt Road — furs and ore for salt, cloth and the wells' protection
    - [[Sahenna Compact]] — **Trade**: Salt and hides for grain and iron, at the seasonal markets the clans keep on the savanna edge
    - [[Thurigypt]] — **Trade**: Grain south into the frontier by the wagon-road, and the reason a homestead season that fails does not become a famine
    - [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] — **Trade**: Furs, timber and prospected ore, which is how a frontier with no fleet reaches a market
    - [[Vaelic Principality]] — **Trade**: Not commerce but people: Kesmarch's settlers are largely Vaelic second sons and disinherited daughters, and the goods follow the families
    - [[Xian Ti]] — **Friction**: The Territorial Council issues charters on ground the Empire has called tributary since before the Council existed, and neither of them will submit the question to anyone
    - [[Khazan Khaganate]] — **Territorial**: Homestead charters issued across ground the Khagan's riders have grazed unchallenged for generations. …
    - [[Thornwild Confederation]] — **Territorial**: The Territorial Council is issuing homestead charters for land it has never surveyed, and the Circle of First Packs has adjourned twice without deciding what to do about it
