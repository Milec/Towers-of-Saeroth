Kesmarch is young, expanding, and made almost entirely of people who came
from somewhere else's overflow — second sons, disinherited daughters,
failed harvests, and wars other nations lost. Barn-raising camaraderie and
an armed homestead are treated as the same instinct here: neighbors help
each other because the frontier doesn't forgive going it alone, and land
claimed is land that has to be defended, not land that was granted.

- **Races** [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|Humans]], [[Ancestries/Halfling (Player Core)|Halflings]], [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|Dwarves]], [[Ancestries/Half-Orc|Half-Orcs]], [[Ancestries/Gnome (Player Core)|Gnomes]] — settlers drawn from every neighboring nation's overflow
- **Economic Specialties** Furs, timber, ranching, homestead grain, prospecting, wagon-trade
- **Military** Militia rangers, homestead defense companies, scout cavalry, frontier marshals
- **Government** Elected Territorial Council, ratified under a founding charter
- **Reputation** Rugged, independent, and a little lawless at the edges
- **Relations** Many Kesmarch settlers are [[Vaelic Principality]]-born second sons and disinherited daughters who left for a fresh start; homesteaders clearing jungle for farmland increasingly press on [[Thornwild Confederation]] land the Thornwild consider already spoken for
