Duty, law, and noble obligation define Vaelic society. Knights and judges
hold nearly equal prestige, and a Vaelic noble's oath is treated as binding
as any statute. Change comes slowly here — precedent is trusted over
novelty — but an ally sworn is an ally kept.

- **Races** [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|Humans]], [[Ancestries/Halfling (Player Core)|Halfling]] minorities
- **Economic Specialties** Grain, wool, steel, fine armor, horses
- **Military** Longbowmen, heavy cavalry, men-at-arms, castle engineers
- **Government** Elector princes choose the High Prince
- **Reputation** Reliable allies, slow to change
- **Faith** [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]]'s code of honorable valor underpins the knightly orders
- **Relations** Long-standing alliance with the [[Thesal Theocracy]], whose clergy anoint Vaelic knights; generational border pressure from the [[Khazan Khaganate]]
