The first anyone knew of it was three deepwater charts coming back wrong in the
same season, each disagreeing with the others about the depth of the same
stretch of open water. The fourth ship sent to settle the question did not come
back at all.

The Spire stands on the seabed and rises to within forty feet of the surface,
which is shallow enough that a keel would have found it long before a chart did
if it had been there. Its entrance is below the waterline and stands open, and
the water does not enter it. There is a face of held-back sea, and dry stone
beyond.

- **Where** Outer survey lanes, a day and a half from the nearest reef-city
- **Depth** Crown to within forty feet of the surface; base unmeasured
- **Entrance** Below the waterline, open, and dry past the threshold
- **Entered by** Two Aquoniti survey crews, one Thurion-underwritten venture, and one party nobody has claimed
- **Returned** Nobody
- **Claim** [[Aquoniti]]'s, in the sense that it lies inside lanes the Tide
  Lords have policed for centuries. The Council has never said so out loud

The Council of Tide Lords stopped sending survey ships after the second crew
was lost and has given no explanation to anyone, including its own captains.
The working theory among the reef-cities is that the Council has understood it
cannot take the Spire and would rather nobody learned there is anything to take.

The [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] knows exactly where it is. It underwrote the
third attempt, and it has not offered terms on a fourth.
