Curiosity and practicality are celebrated in equal measure. Innovation is
encouraged regardless of age or status — a fifteen-year-old with a working
prototype outranks a guildmaster with only a theory. Silicar is small, but
its irrigation canals and clockwork feed half the continent.

- **Capital** Brightfurrow, a canal-town where the guildhalls sit level with the locks they govern
- **Geography** A fertile lowland basin threaded by an irrigation network that took four generations to dig and is maintained obsessively
- **Races** [[Ancestries/Gnome (Player Core)|Gnomes]], [[Ancestries/Halfling (Player Core)|Halflings]], [[Ancestries/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshies]]
- **Economic Specialties** Agriculture, irrigation, machinery, clockwork, food exports
- **Military** Engineers, crossbow corps, defensive fortifications, construct support
- **Government** Council of guildmasters and elected engineers
- **Reputation** Small but astonishingly inventive
- **Tension** An upstream canal has begun silting faster than it can be cleared, and no one has told the export partners who depend on the harvest yet
- **Relations** Machinery and engineering exports flow steadily to [[Stoneborn Holds]] and the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]]; [[Melisor Magocracy]] academics can't decide whether Silicar's clockwork is beneath magic or quietly outperforming it
