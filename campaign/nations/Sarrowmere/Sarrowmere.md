Sarrowmere is a country of standing water and very good doctors. Its cantons
grew up around the fen-physicians who learned which of the mere's plants heal
and which kill — and, being the same guild, learned both at once. Sarrowmere
exports the finest medicines in the world and has never once been able to
convince anyone that it does not also export the other thing. The cantons find
the suspicion useful and resent it simultaneously.

- **Capital** Ilmen Wharf, a town built entirely on pilings, where the guildhalls float and are moored rather than founded
- **Geography** A vast freshwater fen of channels, reed islands and drowned forest; nothing is more than a mile from navigable water and nothing at all is more than ten feet above it
- **Races** [[Human|Humans]], [[Grippli|Grippli]], [[Lizardfolk]], [[Halfling|Halflings]], [[Leshy|Leshys]], [[Ratfolk]]
- **Economic Specialties** Medicines, antitoxins, surgical work, rare fen reagents, leeches and cultured moulds, punts and shallow-draft boats
- **Military** Almost none in the open field; the fen is the defence, and canton levies who know the channels have drowned two invasions without a pitched battle
- **Government** A confederation of eleven cantons, each governed by its physicians' guild, meeting four times a year and agreeing on very little
- **Reputation** Skilled, indispensable, and never entirely trusted at dinner
- **Faith** [[Pharasma]] above all — the goddess of birth and death is the patron of a nation that presides over both professionally. [[Milani]] is kept by the canton physicians who treat whoever arrives, whatever they have done
- **Tension** A canton has begun selling a compound it will only describe as a "sleeping draught", and the other ten have discovered they have no mechanism to make it stop

*Full of hooks: a cure only Sarrowmere can make, a poison only Sarrowmere
could have made, and a confederation with no way to police its own members.*
- **Relations**
    - [[Thesal Theocracy]] — **Friendly**: Thesal's hospices are stocked by Sarrowmere and staffed by Thesal, and neither has ever wanted to change the arrangement
    - [[Ashkar Pale]] — **Trade**: Ash-glaze vessels are the only containers that hold the mere's compounds without spoiling them
    - [[Quivar]] — **Trade**: Physicians and poisons, bought on the same manifest and never itemised separately
    - [[Melisor Magocracy]] — **Rivalry**: Whether the fen's compounds are medicine or a shabby kind of magic; both nations are certain and neither will test it
    - [[Lazarian Lichdom]] — **Covert**: Reagents that grow nowhere but the mere, shipped to a customer the cantons have never been willing to name
