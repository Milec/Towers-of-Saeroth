Cassian Merovel has been an adjunct at Thelemar for eleven years. He is a
better theorist than the man who holds the chair he wants, which he knows, and
which is not the same as being better at getting the chair. He has been passed
over twice. The second time, the Council of Archmages gave the seat to the
archmage now sponsoring the fourth-tower expedition.

He is unfailingly pleasant about it. He is the first to congratulate, the first
to volunteer for committee work, and the first to notice that an expedition
which fails expensively in its first season takes its sponsor's standing down
with it.

- **Want** The chair, and the discipline reorganised under him once he has it
- **Won't** Do any of it himself, or be anywhere it happens. He has never met
  [[Wenzel Grauth]] and could pass a truth-telling on that
- **Method** Requisition numbers. Everything he has arranged is documented
  somewhere as something else, correctly filed, initialled by someone real
- **Leverage on him** He needs the settlement money as badly as
  [[Casa Berruel]] does, for the least dramatic reason available: he has been
  funding his own research out of pocket for six years and is nearly finished
- **Tell** He asks about the expedition constantly and with real warmth, and
  he is the only person in Thelemar who already knows what was on the wagons

He is not a necromancer and considers the art vulgar. What he supplied was
money, a reagent requisition, and the name of a man on the Vaelic frontier who
would not ask which academy the money came from. He would describe that as
three separate favours to three separate people.

*Sits in the same academy politics as
[[Ashwin Devaraj (The Drink)|Ashwin Devaraj]] — both chairless, both certain
the Council is the obstacle — and they loathe each other. Ashwin is a possible
lead, a possible red herring, and the one person who would recognise a Melisor
reagent flask on sight.*
