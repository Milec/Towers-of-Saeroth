*Proposed NPC for [[Session 5 - The Search Camp]]. His introduction and the stores described here have not happened in play.*

Tavian issues the [[Expedition Site]]'s food, rope, tools, and lamp oil. He checks rope by hand while people explain why they need another coil. His ledgers are neat until an emergency, when he writes on his cuffs and transfers the figures before sleeping.

He trained in the [[Melisor Magocracy]]'s academy stores. The promise of field work sounded better than another year counting reagents for people who could not remember his name. He now knows how heavy a crate gets when it has to be carried uphill.

**Want:** enough sound equipment and food to bring every search crew home.

**Won't:** issue a damaged climbing line or conceal a missing meal by changing the ration count.

**At the table:** he asks what an item is for before asking who signed for it. He has a soft spot for people who return borrowed tools clean. He can keep talking while a small spell lifts the right key from his belt.

> "I can write 'rope' in the book as many times as you like. It still won't hold anyone until we have the rope."

## The missing stores

Tavian can show the party the actual inventory for [[The Missing Survey Stores]] and explain the marks on each container. The lost shipment carried food and climbing equipment for the search teams. It is separate from the destroyed Berruel caravan.

The food was packed in two blue-waxed sacks. Two squat tool chests bear an academy issue mark and a diagonal white stripe Tavian painted himself. He records contents and quantities before asking the party to recover them. The inventory is useful evidence, not a clue that every academy marking belongs to the ambush plot.

He authorizes a negotiated settlement that recovers the climbing equipment and at least half the food. If the party can recover more, he is grateful. He would rather share a sack than have a dozen people injured over it.

## Relationships and pressure

- [[Octavia Sere]] wants the crews moving. Tavian makes her name the route and expected return before he opens the stores.
- [[Renata Vellori]] has caught two honest copying errors in his accounts. He appreciates it and still dislikes having her stand behind him while he counts.
- [[Milo Calvetti]] understands transport losses. Tavian asks Milo what can be salvaged and does not make jokes about the missing wagons.

**A useful concession:** he will loan ordinary climbing gear for the recovery job and log it as a loan, rather than make newly arrived survivors buy it. He cannot replenish the party's entire inventory from an unlimited camp shop.

## In danger

Tavian gets people out from behind falling stores and defends a doorway long enough for them to pass. He is not an expedition escort. His sheet is available if trouble reaches camp, and his presence is not included in the recovery encounter budget.

This is a homebrew level-3 support NPC built from the GM Core creature-building tables. His weapon attack and damage are moderate; Fortitude is high and Reflex is low. His limited magic serves the job rather than replacing the party's skills.
