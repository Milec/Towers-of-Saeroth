*Proposed NPC for [[Session 5 Prep - The Search Camp]]. Her introduction and the camp conversations below have not happened in play.*

Octavia directs the [[Melisor Magocracy]]'s search from the [[Expedition Site]]. She surveys ground herself and returns with mud on the same boots she wears to meetings. Her maps distinguish a measured route, a witness's account, and a guess. She gets angry when someone copies one into another column.

**Want:** a route to the tower that a second team can follow and survive.

**Won't:** send a survey party into a route she has marked unsafe merely to satisfy a sponsor's deadline.

**At the table:** she makes room at the map table before asking questions. When someone offers a theory, she asks who saw the event it rests on. She keeps a cup of cold tea beneath a paperweight and occasionally drinks from the wrong cup.

> "Put your packs down. Tell me what you saw first. We can argue about what it means once everyone has eaten."

## What she can offer

Octavia can assign paid recovery work, permit access to the survey table, and give the party space for its three tents. She can confirm that Melisor funds the search and that the tower has not been located. Her map marks routes already checked and leads that still need work; it has no hidden tower marker.

She listens to [[Milo Calvetti]] and the party together when they report the caravan's destruction. She wants the surviving witnesses protected and the evidence recorded before accusations reach the sponsors. She does not know who arranged the ambush. In this preparation she is not part of [[Cassian Merovel]]'s scheme.

[[The Missing Survey Stores]] gives her an immediate problem she can put in the party's hands. She needs supplies recovered while her remaining crews keep the camp and search parties running. Prisoners are acceptable; killing the thieves is not a condition of payment.

## Relationships and pressure

- [[Tavian Calvo]] can stop an expedition from leaving without safe equipment. Octavia resents the delay, then usually backs him in front of the crew.
- [[Renata Vellori]] wants a written loss report before the next dispatch. Octavia will sign observations and inventories, but refuses to sign a conclusion about sabotage that the evidence does not yet support.
- [[Karoline]] is an independent arrival, not a guard Octavia already commands. Octavia can offer work if Karoline wants it; the party does not get to accept for her.

**A private worry:** an earlier survey bearing was copied incorrectly under Octavia's supervision. It cost a scouting team a day. The corrected sheet and the original both remain in her case. If confronted, she admits the mistake. This is an ordinary failure of supervision, not evidence that she is hiding the tower.

## In danger

Octavia covers a retreat and protects the survey records. She uses mist to break a dangerous line of sight, then moves civilians away. She does not join the recovery party or turn their encounter into a demonstration of her magic.

This is a homebrew level-4 field caster built from the GM Core creature-building tables. Her spell DC is high, her Fortitude save and weapon attacks are low, and her HP is in the low range. Her survey action is a custom support ability.
