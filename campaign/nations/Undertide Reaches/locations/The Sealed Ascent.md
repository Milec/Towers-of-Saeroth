The Warden-Conclave has voted twice not to investigate a deep hold that sealed
itself from the inside, and has told the other holds nothing. That is the entire
public record.

What the neighbouring holds have worked out for themselves is that the sealing
happened within a month of the first towers being sighted on the surface, and
that whatever is behind it goes *up*.

- **Where** Below the deep frontier, past a hold that shut its own doors
- **Access** None sanctioned. The hold sealed from within and has not answered
- **Entered by** Unknown. Whoever was already inside when it closed
- **Returned** Unknown, which is not the same as nobody, and the Conclave has
  not clarified which it means
- **Claim** [[Undertide Reaches]], who maintain there is nothing there

If the Ascent is a tower, it is the only one that was not found by someone
walking up to it — and it raises the question the surface has been careful not
to ask. A tower that can be reached from below was not necessarily built a year
ago. It may only have been *reached* a year ago.

The [[Stoneborn Holds]] have their own silent hold on their own deep frontier
and have been no more forthcoming, which the Undertide finds pointed, given how
much noise the thanes usually make about who owns which stone.

The [[Melisor Magocracy]] has made three separate offers for access, each larger
than the last. All three were declined without explanation, which in the
Reaches is a form of confirmation.

*See [[The Towers]] for what is known about towers generally, which is very
little.*
