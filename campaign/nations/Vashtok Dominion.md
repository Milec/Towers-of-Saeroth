Dragons rule Vashtok openly, and the Dominion sees no reason to pretend
otherwise. Kobold and dragonblood households serve individual wyrms in
exchange for protection and a share of hoarded wealth, an arrangement
everyone involved describes, without irony, as fair. Ambition is not a
flaw here — a dragonblood who impresses their patron enough can inherit
territory a human dynasty would need three generations to claim.

- **Races** [[Ancestries/Dragonblood|Dragonblood]], [[Ancestries/Dragonet|Dragonets]], [[Ancestries/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobolds]], [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|Humans]] (tribute-bearers and go-betweens)
- **Economic Specialties** Hoarded gemstones, dragon-scale armor, treasure appraisal, tribute brokerage, ancient artifact recovery
- **Culture** Status flows from a wyrm's favor, not birthright. Loyalty is expected to be earned fresh in every generation — a dragon's memory is long, and it does not forget who impressed it.
- **Military** Dragon riders, wyrm-bonded legions, hoard guardians, kobold sapper corps
- **Government** Wyrmthrone Council — one seat per allied dragon lineage
- **Reputation** Wealthy beyond reason, and never to be trusted with a bargain you haven't read twice
- **Relations** An uneasy, mutually respectful non-aggression with the [[Khazan Khaganate]], neither side eager to test the other; [[Melisor Magocracy]] scholars covet Vashtok's draconic magical lore far more than the Dominion is willing to sell
