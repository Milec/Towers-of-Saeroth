A [[Vaelic Principality]] comital house, descended from the commoner-born
Grand Marshal who broke [[House Dravensk]]'s grip on the
[[Drake Rider Order]] four High Princes ago. He was raised to comital rank
and granted land carved out of the Crown's own holdings rather than any
elector's. Varnick is not an elector house and never will be, since the
seven seats are fixed by charter. It is influential anyway. It is also the
proof, standing in the room, that the Order's meritocracy is not
theoretical.

- **Head of House** **Count Ansel Varnick**, who keeps close ties to the Order his great-great-grandfather once led. The older houses watch him with grudging respect and quiet suspicion. House Dravensk watches him hardest.
- **Holdings** Comital land granted from Crown holdings
- **Want** For his house's standing to be understood as earned rather than as a fluke of one ancestor's talent. A second generation of Varnicks distinguishing themselves would settle it.
- **Won't** Seek an elector seat, even informally. The charter is fixed, and pushing at it would hand [[House Dravensk]] the grievance it has been waiting for.
- **Relations** [[House Dravensk]] watches Ansel Varnick more closely than it watches any of the actual electors
