A [[Vaelic Principality]] comital house descended from the commoner-born
Grand Marshal who broke [[House Dravensk]]'s grip on the
[[Drake Rider Order]]'s leadership four High Princes ago, ennobled to comital
rank and granted land carved from the Crown's own holdings rather than any
elector's territory. Not an elector house, and never will be — the seven seats
are fixed by charter —
but genuinely influential, and living proof at court that the Order's
meritocracy isn't theoretical.

- **Head of House** **Count Ansel Varnick** — maintains close ties to the Order his great-great-grandfather once led. Watched by the older houses, House Dravensk above all, with a mix of grudging respect and quiet suspicion.
- **Holdings** Comital land granted from Crown holdings
- **Want** Ansel wants his house's standing understood as earned, not a fluke of one ancestor's talent — a second generation of Varnicks distinguishing themselves would settle the question.
- **Won't** He won't seek an elector seat, even informally — the charter is fixed, and pushing the point would hand [[House Dravensk]] exactly the grievance it's looking for.
- **Relations** [[House Dravensk]] watches Ansel Varnick more closely than it watches any of the actual electors
