Descendants of [[Vaelic Principality]]'s founding High Prince, and the
[[Drake Rider Order]]'s first ruling line. That was a fine thing to be until
four High Princes ago, when no Dravensk scion cleared the Order's own
standard for Grand Marshal and a commoner-born rider took the seat instead,
under rules the founder had written himself. The dukedom passed down
undisturbed. So did the elector seat. So did the grievance.

- **Head of House** **Duke Konrad Dravensk**, proud and quietly bitter, who has never accepted that his family's claim to the Order's leadership lapsed. He has spent his whole tenure lobbying for stricter eligibility standards and getting nowhere. He calls it discipline. The Diet understands it as an attempt to put the seat back out of a commoner's reach.
- **Heir** **Lady Petronella Dravensk**, said to be far less consumed by the old grievance than her father is. It worries him more than it should.
- **Holdings** The Dukedom of Dravengard
- **Want** Konrad wants the eligibility standards tightened before he dies, so the family name means something at the Order again.
- **Won't** Say the quiet part at the Diet. The standards argument is always framed as discipline, never as "keep commoners out."
- **Relations** The precedent that broke their claim on the Grand Marshal seat founded [[House Varnick]], whose current head Konrad watches with more suspicion than he affords any actual elector
