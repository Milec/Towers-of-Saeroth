The oldest allied house in [[Vaelic Principality]] — its support was
decisive in the founder's original unification, and its loyalty to the
Crown and the [[Drake Rider Order]] has never wavered since.

- **Head of House** **[[Reinhardt von Aldreich|Duke Reinhardt von Aldreich]]**, who has also been High Prince since 2347. Elderly, in failing health, staunchly pro-Order out of genuine conviction rather than politics.
- **Succession** No heir named publicly, and because the Duke is also the High Prince, his death opens Aldrmark and the crown together. Every other elector house is maneuvering to make sure whoever inherits Aldrmark stays friendly to their own interests, which now means shaping one of the seven votes that elects his successor.
- **Holdings** The Dukedom of Aldrmark
- **Want** Reinhardt wants to name an heir he trusts to keep faith with the Order before his health decides the question for him.
- **Won't** He won't be pressured into naming one before he's ready, no matter how many houses are circling.
- **Relations** The Order's most dependable elector vote — for now
