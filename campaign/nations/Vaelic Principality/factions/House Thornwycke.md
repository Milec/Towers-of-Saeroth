Trade-wealthy, militarily unremarkable, and famously willing to vote with
whoever offers the best terms. The March controls trade routes half of
[[Vaelic Principality]] depends on, which makes the house impossible to do
without even though almost no one at court likes dealing with it.

It held the crown once. **Rutger Thornwycke** was elected at Concord in 2316
because he had no faction to reward, and reigned thirty-one years. By the time
he died the house had one, since thirty-one years of Crown business had gone
through the Palatine March. The Diet of 2347 read that as a house that had
converted a compromise candidate into a trade advantage, and elected
[[Reinhardt von Aldreich]] instead. Thornwycke has sold its vote in the open
ever since. Aymar's position is that a house called opportunist either way may
as well be paid for it.

- **Head of House** **Count-Palatine Aymar Thornwycke** — treats his elector vote as a negotiating asset first and a duty second. Predictable in his unpredictability: everyone at court knows he's for sale, they just have to find his price.
- **Holdings** The Palatine March
- **Want** Aymar wants every vote to cost someone something — preferably trade concessions, not gold, which is harder to trace back to him.
- **Won't** He won't sell the same vote twice to two different buyers; his reputation for a clean transaction is the only thing more valuable than the vote itself.
- **Relations** Deliberately unaligned with any elector bloc — that's the entire business model
