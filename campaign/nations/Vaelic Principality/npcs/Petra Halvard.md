Grand Marshal of the [[Drake Rider Order]], which is the same sentence as
saying she holds the Marshal-Elector seat. The founding charter ties the two
together. Whoever leads the Order sits among the seven, is raised to comital
rank, and is a legitimate candidate for High Prince if the electors ever want
one. See [[Vaelic Principality]].

She came up the command track. A knight's daughter out of the Marchgate
valleys, which is nobody, then a fireteam at nineteen and a Wing at
thirty-one. Her combat record is respectable and not storied. What she is good
at is keeping the Wings fed and in the air across four hundred miles of range,
which is duller than a Knight-Rider's reputation and is what the electors were
voting for.

[[House Kesslin]] is assembling votes to bring the chapter aeries under local
authority, and the next Diet will be an ugly one. Halvard is the Order's only
voice in that room. She is also, by the same charter, eligible for the throne
those votes are ultimately about. She has never once raised it.

- **Want** The aeries left standing on Imperial ground. Most other things she
  will trade for that, and the electors know it
- **Won't** Let the Order fly for any candidate, herself included. She has read
  what the nine years of standing down cost and holds that the oath is sworn to
  the office, and that the office is not a man
- **Watch for** She is the first Grand Marshal in three generations who came out
  of no house at all. [[House Dravensk]] has been civil about it for eleven
  years and has not once been warm
