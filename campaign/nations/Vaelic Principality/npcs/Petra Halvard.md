---
title: Petra Halvard
type: npc
role: Marshal-Elector of Vaelic Principality
---

A veteran border warden, elected to the Marshal-Elector seat by
[[Vaelic Principality]]'s conventional military commanders — a fossil
seat from before the [[Drake Rider Order]] existed as its own Imperial
institution, now filled from whoever leads the ordinary army the Order
overshadows in prestige. She isn't hostile to the Order, but she's
acutely aware her own officer corps offers nothing like its meritocratic
ladder for a commoner soldier to become a Count, and it's a comparison
she's raised at the Diet more than once.

- **Want** Real advancement paths for common-born officers, without waiting for the Order to hand hers down.
- **Won't** Undermine the Order publicly — she needs its cooperation too often for that.
