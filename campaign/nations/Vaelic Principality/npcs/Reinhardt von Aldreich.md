High Prince of the [[Vaelic Principality]] since 2347. He is also still Duke of
Aldrmark, which is where the trouble is. He is in his seventies and failing.
When he dies two successions open at once. The Diet elects the crown. For
Aldrmark he has named no heir at all.

The [[Drake Rider Order]] answers to the High Prince directly, and it has had
twenty-nine years of a prince who believes in it. That is conviction, not
policy. [[House Aldreich]] was decisive in the founder's unification and has
never voted against the Order since. Reinhardt inherited the position rather
than choosing it.

[[House Kesslin]] stopped arguing with him about the aeries years ago. It
counts votes for the Diet that follows him instead. Everyone at Reichsmund
knows what the counting is for, and nobody does it where he can hear.

- **Want** An heir for Aldrmark he trusts to keep faith with the Order, named
  before his health names one for him
- **Won't** Be hurried. Three houses have tried. Refusing them is most of what
  he does with a day now
- **The vote he cannot control** House Aldreich's is the Order's most dependable
  elector vote. At the Diet that elects his successor it will be cast by whoever
  has just inherited Aldrmark, and he has not decided who that is
- **Who follows him** The five territorial heads, the High Judiciar, and by
  charter [[Petra Halvard]], since the Grand Marshal's seat is throne-eligible.
  A Diet told for a decade that the Order is too powerful would be electing its
  commander
