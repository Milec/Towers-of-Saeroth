Frohn is a poor, sparsely populated village in the mountains of the [[Vaelic Principality]], about a day’s walk from the [[Expedition Site]]. Most villagers earn their living as masons, smiths, or seamstresses. There is little ground level enough to plow. The few families who farm cannot grow enough to feed the village, so even households with steady work buy much of what they eat.

Merchants from the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] have taken advantage of that dependence to raise food prices. Grain still comes up the mountain road, but the poorest households can no longer afford it. Some villagers have turned to banditry to feed their families. In [[Session 6 Prep - The Bandits of Frohn]], the party must decide how much of the expedition’s food it can bring home once it knows who took it and why.
