The Moon Tower stands in the mountains of the [[Vaelic Principality]], near and northwest of the [[Expedition Site]]. The expedition has not yet located it.

## Campaign role (GM)

This is planned to be the first of [[The Towers]] the party encounters. As of [[Session 5 Play - The Broken Bridge]], they have not reached it; they are still searching for [[The Missing Supply Caravan]].

Its pitch is **Above**: encounters sit a notch above whoever enters. That relation stays fixed as the party grows stronger. The rating cannot be learned from outside the tower.

The tower follows the established one-way threshold rules. Its appearance, interior, inhabitants, and encounters have not yet been specified.
