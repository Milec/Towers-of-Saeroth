Honour, hospitality, and perseverance. Great deeds are kept in song and in
telling rather than in writing, and every Nordheimer works to leave behind a
saga worth the keeping. A hard death is not the thing they are afraid of. A
forgotten life is.

- **Founded** c. 1180 AR, when the Great Thing was first recorded, three centuries before the kingship. Jarls met at the same fjord to settle blood-debt long before they agreed to elect anybody, and the High King's authority still stops where the assembly says.
- **Capital** Hravnfjord, a deepwater harbor town that swells fourfold whenever the Great Thing is called
- **Geography** Fjords, taiga, and whale coasts; the interior is mountain and the sea is the road
- **Government** Four ranks, not always all filled. A **chief** holds a town. A **jarl** stands over several chiefs from a fortified hall. A **king** may stand over many jarls from a hall no grander — the rank exists and often sits empty. Chief, jarl and king are hereditary; the **High King** is not. He is elected at the Great Thing from the sitting jarls and kings, regardless of ancestry, and the Thing caps his authority the same way. His own jarldom still passes to his heir, so a dead king's son is a jarl by morning and stands for election like anyone else. One house can hold the crown three reigns running and never hold it by right. A jarldom with no heir is filled by the Thing
- **Races** [[Human|Humans]], [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Jotunborn|Jotun]] — mostly Jotun in the far north, mostly human in the south, which is about the cold and nothing else. Rank is open to either: a jarl is a jarl everywhere, and eleven of the twenty-six High Kings since c. 1520 have been Jotun
- **Naming** Old Norse and Gaelic mixed freely, patronymics not surnames. Norse is the [[Jotunborn|Jotun]] half, Gaelic came north with the human founders, and nobody sorts them anymore. Sigrún Ketilsdóttir, Hravn Bjarnsson, Ronan Hravnsson, Aífe Ketilsdóttir
- **Faith** Patron [[Cayden Cailean]], god of bravery, freedom and the guest-cup, which is the closest thing Nordheim has to a national creed. [[Gorum]] is kept for war and [[Desna]] for the long voyages; the oldest sagas still invoke [[Trudd]]
- **Economic Specialties** Timber, furs, amber, iron, shipbuilding, whaling
- **Military** Shield walls, berserkers, longship fleets, elite axemen, mountain scouts. Over any of it stands a **war chief** — a command, not a title. Whoever leads Nordheimers under arms holds it while it lasts, from a six-oar crew to the whole nation's strength. A jarl or chief usually takes it for their own raiders; if not, the warband names someone who can. In a national war the High King commonly takes it himself and fights beside the men he called up
- **History** Founded twice. The human half fled the [[Stoneborn Holds]]' northern field-holdings after the winter of c. 1124, when the gates stayed shut for eleven weeks and a third died on the road — see [[The Shut Gate]]. The [[Jotunborn|Jotun]] clans took them in, taught them the sea, and got metallurgy, rank and the raid back. Joined neither crown in the [[The Two-Crown War|Two-Crown War]] and raided both coasts for nine years. Nordheim got rich, and they remember it better than Nordheim does. Signed the [[The Pilgrim's Peace|Pilgrim's Peace]] four years late, after a Thing argument, and has kept it without complaint since.
- **Reputation** Stoic explorers, fierce warriors, and steadfast allies
- **Tension** The High King is dying and the Great Thing must be called before the ice closes the fjords, which means the jarls who arrive late will have no say at all
