title: Edmure Voss

role: High Judiciar of Vaelic Principality

A career judge, elected to the High Judiciar's seat by his own peers in
[[Vaelic Principality]]'s Imperial judiciary rather than born to it — the
same commoner's path to real power that the [[Drake Rider Order]] offers,
just through law instead of a saddle. Elderly, and near-universally
respected as the Diet's most neutral voice; when the territorial elector
houses deadlock, he's frequently the deciding broker, which makes him
quietly one of the most powerful people in the Principality despite
holding no land and commanding no soldiers.

- **Want** A ruling record clean enough that no elector house can credibly accuse him of favoring another.
- **Won't** Cast a deciding vote to settle a grudge, only a genuine deadlock.
