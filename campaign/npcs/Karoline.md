Karoline is an independent level 4 guardian. She used to captain the guard at a waystation in the mountains of the [[Vaelic Principality]]. She is a lesbian, and desire drives her decisions.

When the party couldn’t afford the toll, Karoline agreed to let them through if they dealt with the necromancer. She also wanted a girls’ night. Aurele and Yrsa joined her, got her drunk, and robbed her while the rest of the party killed the necromancer. After the theft came to light the next morning, the party persuaded her to leave with them.

**Last seen:** At the expedition site with the party. See [[Session 4 - Waystation WLW]].
