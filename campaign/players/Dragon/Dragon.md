## Character overview

- **Name:** Seneca.
- **Age:** 27.
- **Ancestry / heritage:** Dragonblood.
- **Class:** Cleric.
- **Homeland:** [[Thesal Theocracy]].
- **Current faith:** Lady of the North Star.
- **Former faith:** Previously a priest of [[Pharasma]]; has since converted.
- **Current work:** Travelling chaplain serving Pharasma worshippers in the [[Lazarian Lichdom]].

## Backstory

Seneca was born in the [[Thesal Theocracy]] twenty-seven years ago. For most
of his life he was a dutiful vowed monk in the service of [[Pharasma]]. His
monastic upbringing is part of his history; his character class is Cleric.

On a journey to minister to the scattered Pharasma worshippers of the
[[Lazarian Lichdom]], he saw how much more time immortals had to affect the
world. He turned his devotion to the Lady of the North Star and began
seeking eternal life. He wants those additional years to serve the
downtrodden, rather than to pursue his own comfort.

Despite his conversion, Seneca still holds his position as a travelling
chaplain to the Lichdom's Pharasma worshippers. His personal faith has
changed, but he has continued caring for the people entrusted to him.

## Why he joined the caravan

News of a reportedly discovered tower drew Seneca to the caravan. He offered
healing and spiritual care for any worshippers of Pharasma travelling with
it, though he expects the organizers to value the healing more.

Seneca believes [[The Towers]] are a divine sign. His conviction that he
must investigate outweighs his deference to authority, even though his
superiors would likely consider the journey a dereliction of duty. He hopes
the towers will offer immortality, or knowledge he can use to find another
means of attaining it.

## GM follow-up

- **Established motive:** More years in which to help people who need him.
- **Unresolved obligation:** Who oversees his chaplaincy, and do they know he has left for the tower?
- **Faith and method:** His belief that the towers offer a divine path to immortality is his conviction, not a confirmed property of the towers. He has not specified what methods he would accept.
