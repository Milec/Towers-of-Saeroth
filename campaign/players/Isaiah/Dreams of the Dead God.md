**GM tool, for a table with an exemplar in it.** What arrives in an exemplar's
sleep is not a story about a god. It is a god's own night, handed over whole,
and about half of those nights are from before anyone had thought of killing
it. See [[Exemplars]] and [[The God Sundering]] for why.

The point of rolling for it is that the exemplar does not get to choose. A
player who has been dreading the knives gets Elysium instead, wakes up happy,
and cannot tell a single person at the table why. That is the whole texture of
the class here.

## When to roll

Not every night. Once in an adventuring day at the outside, and only when
something has pulled on what they carry:

- the night after they have leaned hard on the spark, or put an ikon to work
  in front of witnesses
- any night spent where the Red is close to the surface — a deep mine, a well
  shaft, a canal cut, the floor of a ruin, anywhere in a tower
- any night after somebody has tried to read them
- any night in [[The Wildlands]], where the pull comes from. **There, roll
  twice and take the lower.**

## The save

On waking, the exemplar attempts a **Will save** against the level-based DC
for their level: 15 at 1st, 20 at 5th, 27 at 10th, 34 at 15th, 43 at 20th.

The save does different work on each half of the table. On a **good** dream you
are holding on to something. On a **bad** one you are getting clear of it.

| Degree | Good dream (11–20) | Bad dream (1–10) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical success | The effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, higher result | No effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, higher result |
| Success | The effect | No effect |
| Failure | No effect. You remember splendour and cannot get back to it | The effect |
| Critical failure | No effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, lower result | The effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, lower result |

Every effect lasts until the exemplar's next daily preparations unless it says
otherwise.

## The dreams

Read to the player alone, in the second person, and do not rush them. They are
not hearing about a god. For the length of the passage they are one.

The five below the line are what the killing was like from inside it. The five
above are worse in a slower way, and the hospice sisters in Concord noticed
that before anybody else did.

### 1&#8209;2 Forgotten

A name is not a label when you are the thing being named. It is the whole of
your body. Eleven million mouths are carrying you and you can feel every one
of them, the way a person feels their own hands without looking. Then a temple
in a border province is rededicated to a neighbour of yours, and it is like a
finger going numb. It happens again the following spring, four provinces over,
and there is no thread between the two that you can find. A scribe copies out
a liturgy and drops one syllable, and you lose the ability to be spoken of in
the past tense, and nobody notices because nobody has needed to speak of you in
the past tense yet. Somewhere in the second generation of it you try to say
your own name and discover that you are not one of the mouths that count.

By the end there are three provinces of very old men who still have you. They
say you badly. You love them for it in a way that would be embarrassing if
there were anybody left to be embarrassed in front of. Then there is a public
health measure.

You are still there afterwards. That is the part that does not stop. Nothing
kills you, you are simply no longer held up by anything, and you can feel
yourself going thin, and you have several months to watch it happen.

### 3&#8209;4 The nothing

There is nothing in this one, which is why the exemplars who get it come out
worse than the ones who get the knives.

No floor. No direction, no light and no dark, and nothing arriving from
anywhere. You are entirely intact, and that is the trouble: every faculty you
ever had for attending to a world is open and working and has nothing
whatsoever to attend to, so it turns around and begins attending to itself,
and finds that it has already been doing that for some time. You count. You
lose count, start again, and cannot establish whether the second count is the
first one. You compose an argument about your own situation, follow it to the
end, and find at the end of it the same argument, which you now suspect you
have composed before.

At some point you understand that this is not a punishment and not a place. It
is what is left over when there is nothing to be the god of. It has no edges
and no duration and no door.

The exemplar wakes with it still in their mouth, and spends the morning
touching the walls of the room to confirm that the room has walls. That habit
does not always go away.

### 5&#8209;6 Twelve ways

Twelve of them take hold at once and no two pull the same direction, which you
understand straight away is the design rather than a failure of coordination.

Something your size has no limbs to tear off. What it has is aspects, and they
come apart along seams you never knew were seams. The part of you that hears
prayer goes one way and the part that answers goes another, and both keep
working for a while afterwards, separately, each of them casting about for
where the other has got to. Your sense of what a year is becomes twelve
smaller senses of what a year is, and they do not agree, and you are obliged
to hold all twelve disagreements at once because there is no longer anything
in you that arbitrates.

Every piece stays awake. Every piece can feel the other eleven being worked on
and cannot reach any of them. Every piece is certain that it is the real one,
and every piece is right.

It does not finish when you are apart. It goes on for as long as the work
takes, and you are present in twelve places for all of it, and in one of those
places you are still trying to be polite about it.

### 7&#8209;8 The bleeding

The knives are small. Nobody expects that. A thing like you cannot be killed
by anything the size of a sword, so they use something the size of a sword
eleven thousand times, and eleven thousand more, and the wounds close behind
them at precisely the rate they are opened. It never becomes an injury and it
never stops being a wound. You have time to get bored, and then time to be
frightened by how bored you are, and then time to work out that being bored is
also part of the method.

All the while you are draining, and you can feel where it goes. Down, in a
long fall, onto a green world with rivers in it and people living on the
rivers who have done nothing to anybody and are, by the second day, looking
up.

It runs for seven days and you watch every hour of it land. You watch a woman
on a riverbank put her hands over her son's head to keep you off him. There is
nothing you can do about that, and there is no version of you left that could
have done anything about it, because you are the weather now.

### 9&#8209;10 The murdered

Three provinces of old priests, the last few hundred anywhere who can still
say it correctly, and you are inside every one of them while it is done,
because being inside them is what being their god consists of.

It is not done cruelly. It is done as a sanitation order, signed in one city
and countersigned in another, carried out over eleven days by men working to a
schedule who break for the midday meal and complain about the walking. One of
them is decent about it and apologises first. You are in the priest being
apologised to. You are also in the man apologising, because he was baptised in
your name forty years ago and nobody ever told him whose name it was.

You go out of the world three hundred and some times. Each one is a small door
closing and you are on the far side of every one of them, still there, with
fewer doors.

The last is a woman of eighty who gets your name wrong at the end because she
is frightened and her mouth is not working properly. That is the version that
survives. That is the last time you are ever said.

### 11&#8209;12 The office

You are doing the thing you are for, and it is going well.

You will not be able to say afterwards what it is. It takes both hands and the
whole of your attention and some faculty a person does not have and has never
missed. It is enormous. It is also completely routine, and you have done it
every day since before there was anybody in the world to notice you doing it,
and there is a deep and unshowy satisfaction in doing it properly.

That satisfaction is the injury. The exemplar wakes in a rented bed with their
hands still moving through a motion that has no name, and they will make that
motion again for the rest of their life without meaning to, at a table, in a
fight, halfway through a sentence, and never be able to explain to anybody why
stopping partway feels like putting down something they were carrying for
somebody else.

Nothing in Saeroth needs doing that way any more. Nobody has done it in two and
a half thousand years. Nobody has noticed that it has gone undone.

### 13&#8209;14 Worship

A city says your name at the same time, and it is not the begging kind.

It is the ordinary kind. Over bread, at a door before a journey, under the
breath of a man doing sums he does not trust, by a girl who is furious with
her sister and wants you specifically to know about it. It comes in all day
from every direction like weather, and not one word of it asks you to be
impressive. They assume you are there the way they assume the floor is there.
You are held up so completely and so continuously that standing on your own
has never once been a thing you have had to think about, or could think about,
or would know how to begin.

Then the exemplar wakes up in a room where nobody is saying anything. They
have to get out of a bed, in a body, and carry it around all day among people
who like them well enough.

Two of the four exemplars Thesal has firsthand records of began going wrong
after this dream rather than after any of the ones with knives in them.

### 15&#8209;16 The judgement

Something has got in that should not have got in, and you take it apart.

The taking apart is not a fight. It has the unhurried competence of a man
rehanging a gate that has been dragging all summer: you find the place where
it is wrong, you open it there, and you go on opening until there is no more
of it to open. It screams the whole time, in a register that would stop the
heart of anything mortal standing nearby, and you note that the way you would
note rain on the way to somewhere. Mercy does not present itself as an option
you are declining. It does not present itself at all. You are not being cruel.
You are being correct, and from where you are standing the difference is as
plain as the difference between wet and dry.

The exemplar wakes with that certainty still in them, entire, for about four
seconds.

A few of them spend the rest of their lives trying to get back to it, and
those are not the ones you want anywhere near a courtroom.

### 17&#8209;18 The strength

You could put your hand through the sky.

Not as a boast. You know the thickness of it the way you know the width of
your own shoulders, you know exactly how it would go and what the sound would
be, and you know what would come through afterwards and roughly how long it
would take to deal with. You do not do it. There is no reason to. The
not-doing takes no effort at all and leaves no mark on the day.

Then the exemplar wakes into a body that gets short of breath going up a hill,
that can be held down by two men with a rope, that will one day be finished
off by something as small as bad water in a town they were only passing
through. They spend the whole of that day very slightly wrong about doors,
about what things weigh, about how far away the ceiling is.

One of Thesal's four came out of it and walked off a harbour wall in the dark,
in the flat certainty that the drop was nothing, and kept the limp for thirty
years.

### 19&#8209;20 Elysium

All of them are there.

The light is going through everything, including you, and you are one of the
things it is worth going through. Somebody you have known since before the
world had a name says something to you from across the room and it is not
important and everybody laughs, and you laugh, and you belong there so
completely that belonging is not a thing it would occur to you to notice, any
more than you notice being the right temperature.

That is the entire dream. Nothing in it is frightening. There is no blood
anywhere in it.

The exemplar wakes in a rented bed, in a body that has to be fed and emptied
and kept warm, among four or five people who like them well enough and will be
dead within fifty years. They now know the size of the thing they are not
going to get.

It is the roll every exemplar hopes for. In two of Thesal's four case files it
is the entry the sisters later marked as where the bad years started.

## Running it

- **Hand it over privately.** The exemplar cannot describe any of this to the
  party — see [[The God Sundering]] — so passing the player a note or a
  message keeps the table honest without anybody having to act out being
  unable to speak. What the others see is somebody who came down to breakfast
  wrong.
- **The good half hurts too.** Elysium and the worship are memories of a life
  that ended, dreamt by somebody who has never had one. A player who rolls 19
  three sessions running is not being let off.
- **Let a bad roll cost the day, not the session.** These are one-day effects
  on purpose. If a debuff is going to sit on a character through a dungeon,
  roll on the morning of a travel day instead.
- **Do not explain the numbers.** Twelve ways is twelve because there were
  twelve of them. The office is the domain nobody has held since. Neither is
  ever going to be confirmed to anyone, and both are worth more unconfirmed.
