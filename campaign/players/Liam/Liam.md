## Character overview

- **Name:** Aurèle Lamora.
- **Ancestry:** Half human, half elf.
- **Class:** Gunslinger, Spellshot.
- **Homeland:** [[Quivar]].
- **Background:** Son of a minor noble.
- **Weapons:** Guns.
- **Training:** Arcane theory in Melisor, engineering in Silicar, and service in the Quivari reserve musketeers.
- **Appearance:** Missing his right arm, replaced by a mechanical prosthesis; burn scars across his ribs and side.

## Backstory

### The House of Lamora

House Lamora rose from the service of Percival Lamora, a common-born courier
who knew the roads of [[Quivar]]. During the [[The Two-Crown War]], his
riding and shooting earned him a place in a cavalry company and then an
appointment as his commander's aide-de-camp.

Late in the war, the company escorted seven grain wagons toward the secured
granaries of the Crownlands as winter approached. Men serving a robber-baron
ambushed the returning column and killed its commander. Percival rallied
the survivors and brought five wagons through. The king rewarded him with a
modest Crownlands estate, the foundation of the family's standing over the
sixty years of the Pilgrim's Peace. Breeding and selling horses later became
one of the household's principal sources of wealth.

### The failed mage

Aurèle is Matthis Lamora's fifth child and the first born to Enora, his elven
mother. Matthis had married Colette when young and later brought Enora into
the household as his concubine. Others disapproved of Enora's elven blood,
but Matthis remained affectionate toward his children and generous to both
women. There were eight children in all. Enora's second child, Manon, is
Aurèle's younger sister.

An early aptitude for small workings of magic delighted his father, who
sent him to the [[Melisor Magocracy]] to study. Aurèle excelled at theory:
the structure of spells, the meaning of sigils, and the principles behind
magical effects. Casting was another matter. Even simple effects demanded
enormous concentration and often weakened or failed.

After several years, his tutors sent him home because he could not meet
their practical standard. Aurèle continued studying with tutors hired by
Matthis, buying books and filling notebooks with spells and observations.
He wanted to understand magic and prove that Melisor had been wrong about
him.

### The engineer

Back in Quivar, Aurèle studied languages, literature, philosophy, history,
and the manners expected of a gentleman. He preferred a quill to a blade,
despite his brothers' efforts to involve him in their games of war. Riding
and hunting suited him better. His family's old hound, Soleil, usually
stayed near him, and hunts with his father and brothers introduced him to
firearms.

In his middle teenage years, an elderly gnome from [[Silicar]] visited the
estate to buy a horse. Aurèle's questions about the visitor's instruments
and mechanisms revealed a gift for understanding systems. The gnome left
with a generously discounted horse and a new pupil.

In Silicar, Aurèle learned drafting, mathematics, metallurgy, and chemistry.
Engineering gave him a way to test his understanding: he could build a
device, examine a failure, and try again. There he discovered [[Magitech]],
in which spellcraft was part of the engineering itself. It offered a way to
bring magic into the world without relying on his shortcomings as a caster.

### The prodigal son's return

Aurèle returned home at seventeen. He loved Silicar's workshops and teachers,
but had missed his family and their château. Soleil recognized him on the
estate road before he had even reached the house.

His return brought an obligation to serve in the Quivari reserves. Matthis
helped secure a place for him among the musketeers, where he refined his
shooting, reloading, weapon care, riding, and drill. He took naturally to
firearms without developing much affection for soldiering.

His room soon filled with tools, books, and unfinished mechanisms. With his
father's agreement, he moved into a village house that could serve as a
workshop. He repaired locks and tools, shod horses, made hinges and nails,
and mended mechanisms for the estate's tenants. The income bought materials
and gave him room for his own experiments.

His growing interest in magitech brought the first serious accident. Soleil
swallowed one of his experimental crystals, whose magic injured the hound
badly enough to cost him a leg. Aurèle blamed his own carelessness and began
storing his materials more securely. He then rebuilt an old armored greave
into an articulated prosthesis that helped Soleil walk again.

### The cavern accident

In the spring of Aurèle's twenty-second year, heavy rains exposed a cavern
beneath Lamora land. Inside stood an apparatus unlike anything he had seen
in Silicar. He believed it was magitech and returned for days, then weeks,
measuring, sketching, and eventually experimenting with it.

Manon brought food and water when he forgot to eat or rest. Sometimes she
stayed while he worked and listened to his explanations, though she
understood little of the device.

Aurèle applied slightly too much pressure, awakening the apparatus. The
eruption shattered and burned his right arm nearly to the shoulder. Manon
stood farther away, but the flash blinded her and left branching scars
around her eyes. Injured and barely conscious himself, Aurèle dragged his
sister out of the cavern into the rain.

### The man of tomorrow

Aurèle spent months recovering, struggling with the loss of his dominant
arm and teaching himself everyday tasks with his left hand. He could not
escape reminders of the accident: his burns, Manon's uncertain footsteps,
and the sound of Soleil's artificial leg. The reserves formally declared
him unfit for further service.

He feared that his family hated him, but never asked. Their hatred is his
fear, not something the submitted story establishes. He blamed his
curiosity, pride, and need to vindicate his understanding of magic for the
injuries to Soleil, Manon, and himself.

Eventually he left the château at night without saying goodbye and returned
to his workshop. Remembering what he had made for Soleil, he began turning
another suit of armor into an arm. Over months he improved its weight,
joints, harnesses, and fingers until he could hold a cup and work with both
hands again.

The prosthesis worked, but Aurèle hated how far its gears and cables fell
short of his own arm. The submitted story ends with him reopening his books
on magic and magitech to begin another attempt.

## People to remember

- **Matthis Lamora:** His father, a minor noble who supported his education and workshop.
- **Enora:** His elven mother, also Manon's mother.
- **Colette:** Matthis's wife and part of the household in which he grew up.
- **Manon:** His younger sister, blinded in the cavern accident; her feelings about Aurèle afterward are unknown.
- **Soleil:** The family hound, whose prosthetic leg became the starting point for Aurèle's own work.
- **The unnamed gnome:** His engineering mentor in Silicar.
- **Percival Lamora:** The courier and cavalryman whose service founded the family's landed standing; the submitted story does not specify his exact relationship to Aurèle.

## GM-only details and open questions

**The gunpowder secret:** During his education in Silicar, Aurèle obtained its
guarded gunpowder formula. He memorized what he needed, hid any remaining
record, and told no one. The story leaves how he obtained it undisclosed.
Do not treat the rest of the party as knowing this secret.

**Joining the caravan:** His reason for leaving home and signing on has not
yet been supplied. His interest in magitech is established, but a specific
tower objective, a cure for Manon, or a pursuit by Silicar would be an
addition to discuss with Liam rather than an existing fact.

**Unfinished details:** His present age, his mentor's name, the apparatus's
origin and purpose, and any magical capabilities of his prosthesis remain
unspecified. The draft places his return at seventeen and the accident in
his twenty-second year, while also describing nearly six years at home;
confirm that interval before assigning exact dates.
