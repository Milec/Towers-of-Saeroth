## Character overview

- **Name:** Belithûr Brightforge.
- **Age:** 30.
- **Ancestry:** Dwarf.
- **Class:** Runesmith.
- **Homeland:** [[Stoneborn Holds]].
- **Background:** Merchant.
- **Weapon:** Gun.

## Appearance and manner

Belithûr stands 4 feet 5 inches tall, with flame-red hair and bright hazel
eyes. Runic tattoos cover his arms; his hands bear small burns and callouses
from his work. He is an honorable man and a poor liar who takes pride in
understanding a rune before he carves it.

## Backstory

### The Brightforge household

Belithûr grew up in a comfortable merchant family in the [[Stoneborn Holds]].
He apprenticed under his father, Brundir, learning both runesmithing and
trade. His elder brother Baldor showed an early gift for runes and was
raised to lead the clan. The family spent most of its education money on
Baldor, leaving Belithûr with less thorough training.

Belithûr loves his brother, who has always treated him well. He resents some
of the attention their father gives Baldor, but hopes to earn a place beside
him rather than take his place. Much of his youth went into helping his
father and brother prosper through the family's trade routes.

### Runes and the road

Belithûr helped ship sulfur and other goods, mostly within the Holds. He met
merchants and occasionally a high lord, selling wherever he could. Some
looked down on the Brightforges for dealing in sulfur, though that trade
also helped the family acquire and sell firearms. Belithûr has begun
combining firearms with rune-work and is eager to test what he has learned.

He has long wanted to see the port cities of the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]],
the cities of [[Xian Ti]], and the frozen country of [[Nordheim]]. His
apprenticeship kept him close to home: a poorly made weapon could be
reforged, but careless rune-work could cause a disaster.

## Why he joined the caravan

Belithûr is travelling as a merchant with the supplies for the
[[Melisor Magocracy]] expedition seeking a rumored fourth tower. The journey
takes him farther from home than he has been in some time.

He hopes to profit from the expedition and make a name of his own. Even a
share of the towers' supposed wealth might support generations of
Brightforges and help establish the family as master runesmiths. He fears
what exploring a tower might cost, but wants the chance to stand beside
Baldor as an equal.

## People to remember

- **Brundir:** His father, runesmithing teacher, and guide to the family trade.
- **Baldor:** His elder brother, a runic prodigy and the expected future clan leader.
