The oldest allied house in [[Vaelic Principality]] — its support was
decisive in the founder's original unification, and its loyalty to the
Crown and the [[Drake Rider Order]] has never wavered since.

- **Head of House** **Duke Reinhardt von Aldreich** — elderly, in failing health, staunchly pro-Order out of genuine conviction rather than politics.
- **Succession** No heir named publicly. Every other elector house is quietly maneuvering to make sure whoever inherits Aldrmark stays friendly to their own interests.
- **Holdings** The Dukedom of Aldrmark
- **Want** Reinhardt wants to name an heir he trusts to keep faith with the Order before his health decides the question for him.
- **Won't** He won't be pressured into naming one before he's ready, no matter how many houses are circling.
- **Relations** The Order's most dependable elector vote — for now
