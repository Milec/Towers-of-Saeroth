The Sanctum keeps records. Not spells, not treasure — records: treaties,
lineages, debts, borders, the exact wording of oaths sworn four centuries ago
by nations that have since changed their names. Nine abbeys in nine mountain
valleys along the [[Stoneborn Holds]] spine hold nine copies of everything,
and the order's single rule is that it takes no side, ever, about anything.
Every nation on the continent will accept a Sanctum scribe as a witness. Every
one of them is also quietly frightened of what nine abbeys full of other
people's oaths adds up to.

- **Holdings** Nine abbeys in nine mountain valleys, foremost Vault Abbey —
  cut into a valley head where the only road in is overlooked for its
  entire length
- **Leadership** Nine abbots in permanent conclave; a ruling requires all
  nine, which is why rulings are rare and nobody appeals them
- **Members** [[Dwarf|Dwarves]], [[Human|Humans]], [[Samsaran]],
  [[Gnome|Gnomes]], [[Duskwalker|Duskwalkers]], [[Automaton|Automatons]] —
  novices sent from any nation willing to trust the order with its own
  records
- **Specialties** Archives and certified copies, arbitration, escrow,
  cartography, bookbinding and inks meant to last a thousand years
- **Reputation** Incorruptible, humourless, and in possession of far too much
- **Faith** [[Irori]] for the discipline the work demands, and [[Pharasma]]
  for the keeping of what is finished. The abbots insist these are the same
  virtue
- **Want** To remain the one party every crown will accept as a witness —
  which means never, under any pressure, becoming a party to anything
- **Won't** Take a side. Not once, not quietly, not even when a ruling would
  cost it nothing to make
- **Tension** A nation has formally requested that a record be corrected.
  The conclave has not answered in two years, and the request itself is now
  in the archive
- **Relations** Chartered to keep [[Vaelic Principality]]'s coronation
  record for four centuries running, and every [[Kelvary March]] mercenary
  contract for exactly the same reason: an unenforceable contract is worth
  the parchment it's on. [[Stoneborn Holds]] cuts the abbeys' roads and
  keeps the passes open, the older debt between the two. [[Tal Ulad]] is
  the one power that ever wrote the herd routes down as the Ulad
  themselves describe them. [[Melisor Magocracy]] has wanted copying
  rights for two centuries and been refused for two centuries; whatever
  concerns [[Undertide Reaches]] in the archive, the Reaches have asked to
  read it, politely and repeatedly, and been refused just as long

*A neutral party with leverage over everyone. Useful as a quest-giver, a
McGuffin vault, and a place where a party can learn something true.*
