A remote, wild territorial holding on [[Vaelic Principality]]'s edge —
and, not incidentally, the site of the [[Drake Rider Order]]'s primary
drake breeding grounds, meaning every Trial cycle happens on Corvane land.

- **Head of House** **Countess Yseult Corvane** — young, recently inherited, ambitious. The Order's most reliable elector ally, since the Trial's continued success is bound up with her own family's prestige. Watched closely by the older houses to see which way she'll lean on anything contested.
- **Holdings** The Corvane Reach, including the Order's breeding grounds
- **Want** Yseult wants a Trial cycle to go flawlessly on her watch — proof to the older houses that her seat isn't a formality.
- **Won't** She won't cross the Order publicly, whatever else she does — Corvane's prestige and the Order's success are the same asset now.
- **Relations** Structurally bound to the [[Drake Rider Order]] by geography as much as politics
