title: Cult of the North Star
type: faction
seat: Hidden mountain sanctuaries in Xian Ti
faith: The Lady of the North Star
---

The [[Cult of the North Star]] is the largest organized tradition devoted to
[[The Lady of the North Star]]. It survives in concealed monasteries and
mountain retreats across [[Xian Ti]], without public sanction from the empire.
Its adherents call the Lady the Mother of Secrets and believe that mortality is
a condition to be studied, not accepted.

The cult's great work is the **Ninefold Rite**, a ritual of immortality divided
into nine fragments and hidden across Xian Ti. No sanctuary holds the whole
rite. Each fragment is guarded, encoded, or transmitted only to a successor;
the cult's factions disagree over whether all nine can still be assembled, who
has the right to attempt it, and what completion would cost. The ritual is a
promise, not a proven road.

Outside Xian Ti, the Lady's followers are rarely a single organization. They
are healers pursuing a cure, scholars chasing a forgotten method, alchemists,
occultists, hermits, and martial adepts refining body, mind, and spirit over a
lifetime. They trade signs, stories, and partial teachings while following very
different paths. Some seek a long life to serve others; some cannot bear the
loss of a loved one; some simply fear the grave. The cult recruits from those
people, but does not command them all.

- **Holdings** Hidden mountain sanctuaries, old observatories, sealed libraries, and waystations maintained by trusted households
- **Leadership** No acknowledged head; keepers of individual ritual fragments exercise authority only over their own school or sanctuary
- **Members** Seekers of immortality, including scholars, healers, occultists, alchemists, and martial cultivators
- **Specialties** Lost lore, coded correspondence, preserving dangerous records, secluded training, and the recovery of ritual fragments
- **Reputation** A rumor among officials and a promise among the desperate; outsiders usually mistake one school for the whole cult
- **Faith** [[The Lady of the North Star]], whose teachings forbid cruelty and the destruction of knowledge even when the search becomes desperate
- **Want** To locate, understand, and safely reunite the nine fragments of the Ninefold Rite
- **Won't** Treat innocent lives as material, destroy knowledge to keep a monopoly, or make a secret public merely to prove it exists
- **Tension** Different fragment-keepers have begun receiving the same dream of a northern light over a place none of them recognize. Each fears that another keeper has already found a missing stage

The cult's secrecy is practical rather than uniform. One sanctuary may test a
student for years before revealing a single verse; another may shelter a dying
stranger for one night and give them only a direction. Its members call this
the path of cultivation: advancing through discipline, discovery, and the
refinement of self. No school agrees on the final step.
