Descendants of [[Vaelic Principality]]'s founding High Prince, and the
[[Drake Rider Order]]'s first ruling line — a claim to prestige that
curdled four High Princes ago when no Dravensk scion cleared the Order's
own standard for Grand Marshal, and a commoner-born rider took the seat
instead, under rules the founder himself had written. The dukedom and
elector seat passed down undisturbed regardless; the grievance didn't.

- **Head of House** **Duke Konrad Dravensk** — proud, quietly bitter, never accepted that his family's claim to the Order's leadership lapsed. Spends his tenure lobbying, so far unsuccessfully, for stricter Order eligibility standards — publicly framed as discipline, privately understood at the Diet as an attempt to make the seat harder for commoners to reach again.
- **Heir** **Lady Petronella Dravensk** — reportedly less consumed by the old grievance than her father, which worries him more than it should.
- **Holdings** The Dukedom of Dravengard
- **Want** Konrad wants the eligibility standards tightened before he dies, so the family name means something at the Order again.
- **Won't** He won't say the quiet part at the Diet — the standards argument is always framed as discipline, never as "keep commoners out."
- **Relations** The precedent that broke their claim on the Grand Marshal seat founded [[House Varnick]], whose current head Konrad watches with more suspicion than he affords any actual elector
