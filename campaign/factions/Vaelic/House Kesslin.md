Rulers of a border march that submitted to [[Vaelic Principality]]'s
founder rather than fight him outright, and never fully forgave the terms.
A [[Drake Rider Order]] chapter house sits on Kesslin land, garrisoned and
administered entirely outside Margravine authority — a standing insult
the current head has turned into her signature political cause.

- **Head of House** **Margravine Ilsabet Kesslin** — sharp, patient, well-liked, slowly building the votes at the Diet to force the Order to return the aerie on her land to local control.
- **Holdings** The Kessmark, a border march
- **Want** Ilsabet wants the aerie under Kessmark authority within her lifetime, and has the patience to actually get there.
- **Won't** She won't force the issue with a bloc she hasn't fully secured — one failed vote would set her back a decade.
- **Relations** The clearest live example of the [[Drake Rider Order]]'s recurring friction with electors over aeries built on their land
