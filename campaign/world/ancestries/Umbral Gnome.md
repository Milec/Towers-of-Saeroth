*Gnomes of the lightless deep places, who see in darkness and find open sky disquieting.*

The gnomes of the [[Undertide Reaches]], long separated from their surface kin. Umbral gnomes keep the Reaches' fungal farms and archives, and are disproportionately represented among the Warden-Conclave's record-keepers — which, in a nation where information is currency, is not a minor role.
