*Elves shaped by heat and open sky, at ease in country that offers little shade and less water.*

The [[Elven Confederacy]]'s oasis-grove tribes, who tend isolated stands of ancient trees in otherwise hard country. They trade further afield than any other elven people, since almost nothing they need grows where they live.
