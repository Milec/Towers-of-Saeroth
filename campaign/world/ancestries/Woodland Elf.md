*Elves at home in the deep forest, who move through canopy and undergrowth as easily as open ground.*

The most numerous of the [[Elven Confederacy]]'s peoples, woodland elves keep the temperate heartwood forests and treat the canopy as usable ground rather than scenery. Their settlements are built into living trees and are famously hard to find from below.
