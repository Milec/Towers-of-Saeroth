*The living children of Lazarian's vampire houses, born with their parents' hunger and none of their parents' immortality.*

A [[Dhampir|dhampir]] is an accident. The Sanguinor are what happens when the
accident keeps occurring in the same city for four hundred years. Grauthaven's
[[Vampire|vampire]] houses have kept mortal households since the founding, and
those households have married into each other long enough that the blood now
runs true. Sanguinor parents have Sanguinor children. They are born, they age,
and they die on an ordinary span.

What they inherited is the thirst. A Sanguinor eats bread and mutton like
anyone and gets no harm from it, and still wants blood, and the wanting does
not stop at any point a physician has been able to find. Most manage it the way
a person manages a bad knee. Some don't.

## You Might...

- Keep a stoppered flask on your belt and decline to say what is in it.
- Read a room by who is bleeding before you read it by who is armed.
- Have left a house that still considers you property.

## Others Probably...

- Assume you are a vampire being polite about it.
- Watch your hands at dinner.
- Ask, eventually, whether you have considered the alternative.

## Physical Description

Sanguinor look like whoever they descend from, run pale and cold to the touch.
Their eyes take light badly and throw back a red cast at the wrong angle, which
is the tell most people go by. The incisors are long enough to notice and not
long enough to use. Old Grauthaven lines keep the vampire build, tall and
narrow. The river districts have been marrying out for six generations and it
shows. Most Sanguinor look like anybody else until they smile.

## Society

The [[Lazarian Lichdom|Lichdom]] classes Sanguinor as living and taxes them as
such. The vampire houses have fought that in the courts twice and lost twice.

Their practical use is daylight. Somebody has to sign contracts, take delivery,
and sit across from a foreign factor while the master of the house is in the
ground until dusk. A great house keeps a dozen Sanguinor for the purpose and
calls them cousins. In Grauthaven the word for one is *factor*, and it is not
quite an insult, and nobody uses it to a Sanguinor's face without meaning
something by it.

Outside the delta they get read as vampires and handled accordingly. The
[[Vaelic Principality]] grants them no legal standing whatsoever, and a hooded
traveller who will not eat at the table is close enough to what the
[[Drake Rider Order]] rides out for.

## Faith

[[Urgathoa]]'s clergy call the thirst a sacrament left half-finished and press
Sanguinor toward completing it. Every Sanguinor family has had that
conversation. Some go.

The ones who refuse tend to drift toward [[Pharasma]], whose faith the Lichdom
outlawed, and who is the only god in Grauthaven that would count a Sanguinor
death as a death rather than as waste.

## Names

Sanguinor take Lazarian names, which run Germanic and gothic. A house-born
Sanguinor carries the house name whether or not the house acknowledges them,
and dropping it is the loudest thing a Sanguinor can do in Grauthaven.

### Sample Names

Alsbet Vane, Corvin Mool, Editha Grausser, Hesper Krall, Ottmar Sieveking,
Reinhild Ash, Tobias Drex, Wilhelmine Storr

### Low-Light Vision

You can see in dim light as though it were bright light, so you ignore the
concealed condition due to dim light.

### Void Healing

You are harmed by vitality damage and are not healed by vitality healing
effects. You do not take void damage, and you are healed by void effects that
heal undead. You remain a living creature, so Treat Wounds and Battle Medicine
work on you normally and you use the ordinary dying rules.

### Disease and Poison Protection

You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws, and to any other defenses,
against diseases and poisons.

### Sunlight Sensitivity

While directly exposed to sunlight, you are clumsy 1. Covering yourself with a
hood, cloak, veil, or similar garment prevents this.

### Fledgling Fangs

Your incisors are elongated and you can drink blood directly from a creature.
They are not a functional weapon and grant no unarmed attack.

### Balanced Hunger

You crave blood and always will, but ordinary food keeps your body running. You
are **unfed** unless you have drunk at least a pint of blood within the last 24
hours. Drinking blood takes 1 minute and requires a willing, grabbed,
restrained, unconscious, or freshly dead creature that has blood, or a stored
supply. Being unfed carries no penalty on its own.

### The Red Thirst

While you are unfed, spilled blood pulls at you. The first time in an encounter
that a creature within 30 feet takes piercing or slashing damage, the Red
Thirst rises, and it stays until the encounter ends. While it is on you:

- You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to melee damage rolls and to Intimidation checks.
- You take a -1 circumstance penalty to Will saves.
- On each of your turns, at least one of your actions must be a Strike against, or movement toward, the nearest creature you can see that has taken piercing or slashing damage. If no such creature is visible, this restriction does not apply that turn.

### Ancestry Feats

You can select from dhampir feats and Sanguinor feats whenever you gain an
ancestry feat. No Sanguinor feats are written yet. Dhampir feats cover 1st
through 17th level in the meantime.

## Heritages

Choose one at 1st level. A versatile heritage such as nephilim or aiuvarin
replaces this choice, the same as it would for any other ancestry.

### Gravebound

Your line sits close to the vampire stock and holds a feeding a long time. You
become unfed only after seven days without blood rather than 24 hours.

### Thirstborn

The Thirst runs hot in you. While the Red Thirst is on you, the circumstance
bonus to melee damage rolls is +2 and the penalty to Will saves is -2.

### Daylight Factor

Your family has done its house's business in the sun for generations. You are
trained in Society, and ordinary outdoor clothing with a broad hat is enough to
prevent Sunlight Sensitivity.

### Pale Line

Generations away from any house have thinned what you carry. You gain a fangs
unarmed attack that deals 1d4 piercing damage, in the brawling group with the
finesse and unarmed traits. The Red Thirst never rises for you, and drinking
blood does nothing for you that a meal would not.

## At the table

Two dials worth setting before the first session.

The Red Thirst says *creature*, not *enemy*, so the nearest bleeding thing can
be a party member. That is the horror in it, and the reason to play one. It can
also wreck a fight in a way nobody at the table enjoys. Narrow it to enemies if
that happens; nothing else about the ancestry changes.

The +1 stops mattering somewhere around 9th level. The action restriction
never does. Left alone the feature decays into a tax, so fix it with Sanguinor
feats rather than by scaling the number here.
