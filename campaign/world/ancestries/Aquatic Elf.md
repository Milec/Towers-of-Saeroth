*Elves who spend most of their lives underwater, able to breathe both air and water though unable to remain on the surface indefinitely.*

Called sea elves by the surface world. They hold the [[Elven Confederacy]]'s drowned coastal woods, and are the only elven people with a standing relationship with [[Aquoniti]] — a fact the land-bound tribes find faintly suspicious and rely on constantly.
