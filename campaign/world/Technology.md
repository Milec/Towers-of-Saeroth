Printing exists. So do canal locks, blast furnaces, plate armour, standardised
weights, and roads that are maintained through a winter. That last one sounds
like the least of them and is the reason the freight goes through
[[Voskreld Union]] rather than around it.

The world is not static and has not been for a lifetime. The
[[The Pilgrim's Peace]] took the money that used to go into armies and put it
into roadbeds, canals and workshops, and sixty years of that compounds.
[[Corvane Republic]] has a printing trade and a habit of using it on things
crowns would rather it did not. [[Silicar]] dug an irrigation network over
four generations and then built machinery to work it.

## Firearms

They exist, they are Silicaran, and the question worth asking is who has them.

Nobody set out to build one. [[Silicar]]'s farm cantons had been working the
same canal-fed soil for four generations by the time the fertilizer guilds
went looking for a nitre salt strong enough to keep the yields up, and in
2319 AR a cellar batch of it went up instead of into the ground, taking the
roof off a curing shed outside Brightfurrow and most of that season's
seedbed with it. The guild spent a year arguing about what they'd nearly
killed themselves growing. Then somebody asked what else it was good for,
and the first firelock came out of the same workshop three years later.

[[Silicar]]'s guilds built the first working firelock in the decade after the
Peace, designed for a militia that has to be raised in a fortnight and can
never be large. They still make the best ones. They have never licensed the
trade to anybody, which is most of why a firelock in private hands is a rare
thing and an expensive one.

Armies are another matter. Silicar drills flintlock companies in volley and
reload. [[Quivar]] fields musketeer-style skirmishers as one of its four arms.
[[Voskreld Union]] runs royal cannon foundries on [[Stoneborn Holds]] steel,
at a weight standard Voskreld invented and Stoneborn adopted while
complaining. Where gunpowder exists at all it is a state industry, and
Silicaran powder is on the [[The Pilgrim's Peace|Peace]]'s embargo list beside
Ulad remounts and Melisor artillery.

**Silicar has never licensed the trade, and Quivar has musketeers.** Nobody at
the Concord Table has asked how, out loud. Silicar has never said what it
thinks happened, which is not at all the same as not knowing — and three years
ago it stopped selling Quivar anything at all, still without saying why. See
[[Economy]].

A firelock is loud, slow to load and expensive whoever is carrying it. They
work. They are not what wins a battle, and [[Kelvary March]] would tell you so
at length, having declined to stake a contract on one.

**Nobody has built a magical firearm, and the one guild that could has never
tried.** [[Silicar]] holds half of [[Magitech]] in the same workshops that
hold the firelock trade, and the two have never once been asked to talk to
each other. A weapon that came out of a fertilizer shed by accident is a
story the guilds tell on themselves. A weapon built on purpose, out of the
same trade that builds a wayfinder, is a different thing to have done, and in
twenty years nobody in Brightfurrow has volunteered to be the one who does it.
