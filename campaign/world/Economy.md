Two landmasses, and each of them eats its own harvest. What crosses the ocean
is what one side has and the other cannot grow, make or dig: silk and porcelain
and spice going west, grain and armour and worked steel going east. The volume
is enormous and the dependency is thinner than it looks, which is the first
thing to understand about all of it.

The second is that almost nothing moves on its own money. It moves on credit
written in [[Tessine]], underwritten by the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] and
carried in [[Aquoniti]] bottoms, and none of those three grows a thing.

For the roads and lanes themselves see [[Trade Routes]].

## The western continent

Seventeen nations, and **three of them grow the bread**.

| Feeds the continent | How |
| --- | --- |
| [[Vaelic Principality]] | Rolling grain plains and river valleys, and the largest single harvest in the west |
| [[Voskreld Union]] | Its own river plains, and the redistribution that moves everyone's surplus on its own canals |
| [[Silicar]] | A small irrigated basin worked four generations deep, and food exports out of proportion to its size |

Everyone else buys. [[Stoneborn Holds]] cut their living space downward into a
mountain and sell steel for flour. [[Undertide Reaches]] grow luminous fungus,
which keeps a hold alive and has never fed a city. [[Melisor Magocracy]] sits
on a high plateau chosen for its ley-lines rather than its soil.
[[Kelvary March]] is horse pasture and drill ground, poor for grain on purpose.
[[Tessine]] is a bay and a day's walk of terraces behind it. [[Dalstan]]'s hill
country of cathedral-towns is prosperous in the valleys and has never fed
itself. [[Thesal Theocracy]] has no farmland worth the name and eats on two lines at
once, which is deliberate.

What they send back is the interesting part, because it interlocks:

- **Steel goes two ways out of the mountains.** Stoneborn steel into
  [[Voskreld Union]]'s foundries and cannon-works at a weight standard
  Voskreld invented, and into [[Silicar]]'s gearwork, which is the only
  Stoneborn export that is not simply steel by the ton.
- **Horses come off the grass.** [[Tal Ulad]] moves fifteen thousand remounts
  a year, mostly to [[Kelvary March]], with the Diet's heavy cavalry and
  [[Quivar]]'s court taking the pick before the March sees them.
- **The north sells what grows slowly.** [[Nordheim]] timber and amber into
  [[Corvane Republic]]'s yards, Corvane hulls back north, on the single
  busiest run in the northern water. [[Elven Confederacy]] timber and herbs
  down to Stoneborn and the academies.
- **[[Sarrowmere]] sells the difference between a wound and a death**, to
  Kelvary by the campaign and to Quivar in quantities that say something about
  Quivar.
- **Nobody makes their own paper.** Corvane prints, and prints things crowns
  would rather it did not.
- **And one economy is off the books entirely.** The
  [[Lazarian Lichdom]] sells alchemy, relics and artifacts, and the courts
  that buy them do it through intermediaries who are never the same twice and
  pay in wine, art and favours rather than in anything that leaves a ledger
  entry. Its labour does not eat, which is a fact about undead work before it
  is a fact about anything else, and it matters below.

## The Hungry Year

This is why the [[The Two-Crown War]] killed most of its dead in countries
that never raised a soldier, and it is worth being precise about the mechanism,
because it is the whole argument for the [[The Pilgrim's Peace]].

**Both crowns were Vaelic.** The war was not fought on the grain belt's border;
it was fought inside the grain belt, by two claimants to the same throne, and
in the fifth year each of them worked out that the other's harvest was easier
to reach than the other's army. Granaries fired, mills broken, seed grain
seized as contraband, canal works cut.

Then three things made it a continental famine rather than a regional one.

1. **The surplus had one route.** Voskreld's redistribution is a genuine
   institution and it runs on Voskreld's canals — and Voskreld was a
   belligerent from the first month. The mechanism that fed the countries with
   no farmland was inside the war.
2. **Silicar's canal works were cut**, and took nine years to put back. That is
   not a harvest lost. That is a decade of harvests at reduced yield out of the
   one nation whose whole export was food.
3. **Seed grain is next year's crop.** A granary fired in the fifth year does
   not kill in the fifth year. It kills in the winter after it, and again in
   the year after that, when there is nothing to sow.

The countries that starved were the ones with nothing to eat but what they
bought: the deep holds, the Reaches, the academies, Harrowgate's muster towns,
Tessine's bay, and Dalstan's own cathedral-towns, which had backed one of the
crowns. Stoneborn had steel and no bread and there was nobody left with bread
to sell it to.

**One nation on the continent was not affected at all.** A workforce that does
not eat cannot be starved, and the [[Lazarian Lichdom]] declared for nobody,
waited four years and took a hundred miles of Vaelic's southern frontier in the
year everyone else was burying people. Nobody said this out loud at Concord.
Everybody there had done the arithmetic. The hundred miles is still where the
Lichdom left it.

Sixty years later, no western nation keeps less than two years of grain in
store, and none of them will say so out loud, because the storing is an
admission.

## The eastern continent

Nine nations, and **the food comes off three different weathers**, which is the
whole difference.

| Feeds the continent | On what |
| --- | --- |
| [[Thurigypt]] | A river delta and its flood, with almost the entire population living on it |
| [[Kesmarch Frontier]] | Homestead grain and ranching, wherever the newest homestead is |
| [[Ashkar Pale]] | Grain and vine crops of exceptional quality, on volcanic slope |

A bad flood does not touch the frontier, a hard frontier season does not touch
the Pale, and the Pale evacuates for the mountain rather than for the weather.
No single failure takes the east's bread, and the east has never had a Hungry
Year — which is not virtue, and is not planning. It is three climates.

Binding them is the **Salt Road**: [[Thurigypt]] out through
[[Qeshara Sultanate]] to the [[Sahenna Compact]] and on to
[[Kesmarch Frontier]], salt and hides and dyed cloth one way, grain and iron
and worked goods the other. Sahenna clans guarantee the road, Qeshara
guarantees the wells, and it is the oldest working alliance on either
continent because both halves would starve without it inside two seasons.

Around that:

- **[[Xian Ti]]** manufactures and does not need anybody's grain. Silk, tea,
  porcelain and paper go out, and its ministries have been writing down the
  price of all four for eighteen centuries.
- **[[Khazan Khaganate]]** sells iron, livestock, horses and companies of
  cavalry, the last brokered and insured through the Alliance like any other
  cargo.
- **[[Cindral Ashlands]]** sell heat. Obsidian into Qeshara's glassworks, which
  cut it and sell it back at four times the price, and fire-forged steel to
  imperial kilns that cannot reach their own temperatures.
- **[[Thornwild Confederation]]** sells what only grows under a canopy —
  medicinals to Thesal's hospices across the ocean, venom and pelts to the two
  coastal factories the Circle allows.

## The crossing

Three doors, and both fleets.

- **Qeshara's coast**, where the Salt Road meets the water and everything the
  caravans carried out of the eastern deserts goes onto Alliance hulls.
- **Xian Ti's ports**, the eastern terminus of the Middle Sea run and the run
  the Alliance would fight for.
- **The Island Crossing** — Thurigypt to [[Aquoniti]] to Xian Ti, the shortest
  hop in the world and the one the island fleet owns outright.

Everything then clears through Tessine, which produces almost nothing, asks
almost nothing, and takes a percentage of all of it.

**One food link crosses the ocean**, and Thesal keeps it on purpose. Concord
is fed by [[Vaelic Principality]] — grain out of the Diet's estates, over the
Pilgrim's Stair, alongside the coin that endows the hospices. That is the
staple and it comes from one neighbour, so Thesal also sends hospice medicine
and trained physicians to [[Thurigypt]] and takes delta grain back, temple to
temple rather than through any factor.

A holy city that one neighbour can starve is a holy city with a price, and
Thesal's entire usefulness is that it has no price. Both crowns denounced it
in the same season of the Two-Crown War and neither could make that mean
anything, because the second line was still running. In the Hungry Year it was
the ocean line that held.

## Trade wars

Nobody on the western continent will fight a war. Everybody on it will cut a
supply, and the [[The Pilgrim's Peace]] does not cover that at all — its
clauses bind what may be sold *to a war*, and say nothing about what may be
refused in peacetime. The result is that the continent's quarrels are now
conducted in schedules of goods.

They are slow, they are deniable, and they hurt the third party worst. Cut a
nation's steel and you have also cut every workshop downstream of it in two
countries that were not arguing with anybody.

**The newest one has been running three years, and nobody has stated its
cause.** [[Silicar]] stopped selling machinery, clockwork, pumps, powder and
field kit into [[Quivar]], in that order, over about a season. Quivar has
asked formally three times and been answered with a revised schedule of goods
and no reason attached.

Everyone can name the reason and nobody has put it in a document. Quivar
fields musketeer skirmishers carrying firelocks Silicar invented and has never
licensed to anybody, and Silicar has accused no one of anything — which is
what makes it unarbitrable. There is no charge for Quivar to answer, and the
Concord Table cannot make a finding on a case that has not been brought.

Who is actually paying for it: [[Kelvary March]], whose field kit is built to
Silicaran specification and whose pumps keep a camp alive, and which sells to
both of them. Harrowgate has begun quietly buying Quivar's share and reselling
it at no margin, and calls this commercial.

## The six a war needs

A war between great powers is not fought out of a treasury. It is fought on
Alliance credit, with Kelvary regiments, Ulad remounts, Silicar powder and
Melisor artillery, carried in Aquoniti bottoms and fed on somebody else's
grain — and the [[The Pilgrim's Peace]] binds every one of those six rather
than binding the crowns.

Five of the six are western. That is why the Peace is a wall on this continent
and an inconvenience on the other one, and it is worth remembering the next
time somebody says the world is at peace.
