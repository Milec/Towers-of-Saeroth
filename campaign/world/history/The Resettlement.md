From about 100 AR there is something to find. Post holes, hearths, a wall
somebody bothered to face with stone, middens deep enough to argue over. Six
centuries later there are granaries in the delta and a civil service sitting
examinations in the east.

Everything in between is guesswork with pottery in it. What matters about the
period is not the pottery. It is that every people in the world begins its own
story inside the same six hundred years, and not one of them has anything
older to measure that story against.

## Nobody inherited anything

A resettlement usually means somebody moving into somebody else's ruins. This
one did not. There were no older fields to reopen, no roads to repair, no
language written on anything, and no neighbour further along to copy from.
Every technique in the world was worked out again from nothing by people who
did not know it had ever been worked out before.

That is the piece scholars find hardest to accept, and they are right to. It
does not happen. The [[Melisor Magocracy]] has a paper arguing the evidence
must be incomplete, and the paper is now ninety years old and has not been
answered because there is nothing to answer it with.

## The four that stuck

Most of what got started in these centuries did not last. Four things did, and
all four are still running.

- **Around 180**, elven tribes entered the great forest belts. The
  [[Elven Confederacy]]'s memory is continuous from that year, which makes it
  the longest record anybody keeps, and the elves are quietly certain it goes
  back further than the archives will grant.
- **Around 310** the [[Stoneborn Holds]] reopened Highforge. The Holds
  maintain it was never closed. They cannot prove that, they know they cannot,
  and a thane will still correct a visitor who says otherwise.
- **Around 420** the first Pharaoh organised the granaries of the delta.
  [[Thurigypt]] has dated itself from the flood ever since, and counts its
  dynasties from that harvest rather than from the Red.
- **Around 600** [[Xian Ti]] held its first examination. That is the year Xian
  Ti counts from in preference to any emperor, and the ministry that ran it
  has not stopped running it since.

## The myth-histories all agree by accident

Ask any people in the world where they came from and you get the flood. The
details are incompatible — the number of survivors, the mountain they came
down from, which god was owed thanks — and the shape is identical everywhere,
including between peoples who had no contact for a thousand years afterwards.

Comparative scholarship on this is a small and slightly disreputable field.
The theocracies dislike it because it makes their revelation one of forty.
[[Melisor Magocracy]] tolerates it. The one institution that funds it properly
is [[Xian Ti]]'s ministry of rites, which wants a filing category rather than
a truth and has been perfectly open about that for four hundred years.

## At the table

This is where a ruin comes from when a ruin needs to be old but explicable. A
settlement abandoned around 300 AR is nine tenths of the way to the present
day and still predates every nation that could claim it, so nobody's law
covers it and three countries can plausibly want it.

## The empty floor

**GM.** See [[The Nameless Empire]]. The erasure left both continents open ground and
left the survivors nothing to inherit, so the entire world restarted from the
same floor within a few generations of each other. That is why the
myth-histories are the same age, why the techniques were reinvented rather
than passed on, and why Melisor's paper about incomplete evidence will never
be answered.

## Threads

- **A settlement older than 100 AR.** It would be the earliest thing in the
  world by a wide margin, and worth an academy's whole budget. What is
  actually down there is empire.
- **The elven claim.** The Confederacy says its memory runs further back than
  the archives allow, and has never produced a name from before the gap. A
  party who got one out of a tribal keeper would be holding something that
  contradicts every calendar in the world.
- **The ministry of rites.** Somebody in that building has forty versions of
  the flood story filed side by side and has begun to notice what they share.
