Eleven years, the whole western continent, and it started because a road was
shut in the wrong month.

It is the war every living thing in Saeroth is still standing inside the shape
of. The alliances, the trade routes, the reason [[Tessine]] exists in its
present form, the reason no crown has raised an army against another in sixty
years — all of it is the Two-Crown War, either as consequence or as
overcorrection. Anyone over seventy remembers it. Everyone else was raised by
someone who did.

## The deadlocked Diet

The High Prince of the [[Vaelic Principality]] died in autumn without an heir
of his own body, which was ordinary — Vaelic's crown is elective and has never
been anything else. The Diet of seven Elector Princes convened at Reichsmund to
choose the next, which was also ordinary.

Then it deadlocked three against three, with the seventh seat vacant on a
technicality nobody had thought to fix in two hundred years, and a hard early
snow closed the **Pilgrim's Stair** — the single mountain pass between Vaelic
and [[Thesal Theocracy]], and the only road a Thesal celebrant can travel to
crown a Vaelic prince.

Everything after that followed from two decisions that were each, on their own,
perfectly defensible:

- The faction holding Reichsmund found a Thesal celebrant who had wintered on
  the wrong side of the pass, and crowned **Gerhard Dravensk** with him.
- The faction that had walked out took its candidate east to Ostravin, where
  the Cathedral Court of [[Dalstan]] anointed **Aleksander Kesslin** with the
  Concord's own four-hundred-year-old rite — performed correctly, by a church
  with every historical claim to perform it and no standing whatsoever to crown
  a Vaelic prince.

Two crowned High Princes, six weeks apart, each legitimate by an argument the
other could not refute. Vaelic's official regnal list still skips the second
name entirely.

## The sides

Because half the continent had decided which crown it preferred before either
head was under one.

- **[[Voskreld Union]]**, **[[Corvane Republic]]**, **[[Stoneborn Holds]]** and
  **[[Silicar]]** stood with Reichsmund. Voskreld has no defensible border and
  a hostile Vaelic on it was unsurvivable; the rest followed the trade.
- **[[Dalstan]]** stood with Ostravin, and got what it actually wanted, which
  was not a Vaelic prince but a precedent: that the Cathedral Court's rite is
  the valid one. It has never given that claim up.
- **[[Quivar]]** declared for Ostravin in the second year, for Reichsmund in
  the seventh, and has spent sixty years explaining that this was statecraft.
- **[[Thesal Theocracy]]** refused both. It would not disown the celebrant who
  crowned Gerhard and would not call the Ostravin rite invalid, and was
  denounced by both crowns in the same season for it — which is exactly why
  both crowns could eventually sit in the same room at Concord.
- **[[Melisor Magocracy]]** sold arcane artillery and weather to both sides
  through separately incorporated academies, and has never once apologised.
- **[[Kelvary March]]** chartered regiments to both sides, as it always had.
- **[[Thurion Merchant Alliance]]** lent to both sides, which was its usual
practice and had never yet cost it anything.
- **[[Nordheim]]** joined nobody and raided both coasts for nine years.
- **[[Tal Ulad]]** sold remounts to whoever came to the spring camps with
  silver, and regards the whole question of who was right as a settled
  people's disease.
- **[[Sarrowmere]]** was invaded once, by an Ostravin column trying to turn the
  Reichsmund flank through the fen. The fen took it. There was no battle.
- **[[Undertide Reaches]]** sold each side everything it knew about the other,
  which is the origin of nearly every covert arrangement the Warden-Conclave
  still holds today.
- **[[Aquoniti]]** blockaded whichever coast paid better, and grew very rich.
- **[[Elven Confederacy]]** stayed out, and had grove-shrines on its southern
  edge burned by Dalstani inquisitorial companies operating well beyond any
  front. That grievance is younger than the war and has outlived it.
- The **[[Lazarian Lichdom]]** declared for nobody, waited four years, and then
  took a hundred miles of Vaelic's southern frontier while both crowns were
  looking north.

Only the eastern continent kept any real distance. [[Xian Ti]] sent observers
and wrote them up. The [[Khazan Khaganate]] was offered money by both crowns
and took neither.

## The Drake Riders stood down

The [[Drake Rider Order]] swears to the office of High Prince — not to a
bloodline, and not to a man. With two crowned princes there was no office, and
the Order, reading its own oath the only way that oath can be read, refused to
fly for either.

It held that position for nine years, while the Lichdom came north.

Vaelic has never entirely forgiven the Order and has never seriously proposed
changing the oath, because the alternative is an Order that picks. Its whole
present form — answering to the High Prince directly, outside the conventional
chain of command, with a throne-eligible Grand Marshal's seat — was rewritten
in the decade after the Peace by people arguing about those nine years.
[[House Kesslin]] is still arguing about them.

## The Hungry Year

The fifth year is why this is remembered as the worst war and not merely the
biggest one.

Both crowns, stalemated, went for each other's harvests: granaries fired, mills
broken, seed grain seized as contraband, canal works cut. It worked. It worked
on both sides at once, on a continent that fed itself, and the winter that
followed killed more people in five months than nine years of fighting had
managed — most of them in nations that never raised a soldier.

The Hungry Year is why the [[The Pilgrim's Peace|Pilgrim's Peace]] reads as it does. Every clause
in it with teeth is about credit, supply and food rather than about armies.

## Ambry Ford

The war's other memory belongs to [[Kelvary March]]. In the eighth year two
chartered companies — both Kelvary, both properly sealed by the Muster Court —
met at a ford neither paymaster could afterwards prove he had ordered taken.
They recognised each other. They fought anyway, for eleven hours, and between
them lost more men than the March had lost in the preceding century of selling
soldiers.

They were neighbours. Kelvary is a small country and its regiments recruit from
the same valleys. Harrowgate buried both companies in one season and has not
chartered opposite sides of the same conflict since — a rule that appears in no
statute and has never once been broken.

## A bank and a muster court

Not with a battle. With a bank and a muster court.

By the eleventh year both crowns were fighting entirely on credit. The
[[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] had lent to both, could no longer pretend either
would repay, and called the loans. In the same month the Muster Court at
Harrowgate suspended new charters to either crown, for reasons Kelvary has
always described as commercial and everyone else understands as Ambry Ford.

The money stopped and the soldiers stopped within about six weeks of each
other, and neither crown could raise another season.

Gerhard Dravensk was dead of fever by then. Aleksander Kesslin was not, and
signed. Both delegations went to Concord because [[Thesal Theocracy]] was the
only capital on the continent that had denounced neither of them; the Diet
reconvened there under Thesal mediation and elected a third man nobody had
proposed in eleven years — **Rutger Thornwycke**, who reigned thirty-one years
and is generally reckoned the best High Prince Vaelic ever had, largely because
he had no faction to reward.

What they signed is the [[The Pilgrim's Peace|Pilgrim's Peace]].

## The inheritance

- **A continent that believes wars are stopped by ledgers.** This is not
  cynicism. It is the one thing that demonstrably worked, and it is the weight
  the Peace actually carries.
- **A hundred miles of Vaelic frontier the Lichdom never gave back**, and the
  Drake Rider Order in the shape that grew around that fact.
- **Dalstan's precedent, unretracted.** The Cathedral Court still holds its
  rite valid, which is why licensing inquisitions abroad feels to Dalstan like
  an old right and to everyone else like the same move again.
- **[[Tessine]] as it now is** — a neutral harbour that took both sides'
  fortunes and both sides' exiles and never gave either back.
- **Nobody who won.** This matters at the table more than any of the above.
  There is no victor's narrative in Saeroth, no nation that remembers the war
  fondly, and no crown that can invoke it to justify anything. Every court's
  version of the Two-Crown War is a story about how it was somebody else's
  fault and everybody's disaster.

## Threads

- **Vaelic's next Diet.** The current one is unsettled: [[House Aldreich]]'s
  own succession is in doubt and [[House Kesslin]] is assembling votes against
  the Drake Rider Order. Everyone in that room knows precisely what a
  deadlocked Diet costs, which is the best reason to think it will not happen
  again and the reason it would be catastrophic if it did.
- **The seventh seat.** The technicality that left a seat vacant was fixed
  afterwards. Whether it was fixed *well* has never been tested, because there
  has not been a contested Diet since.
- **Sixty years is one lifetime.** The last people who fought in it are in
  their eighties. The last people who remember the Hungry Year as children are
  in their sixties and run things. When that generation goes, the Peace loses
  the only argument for it that has never needed explaining.
