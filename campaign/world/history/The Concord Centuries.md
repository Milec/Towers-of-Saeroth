For most of this age there was a church-state sitting in the middle of the
western continent holding three doctrines at once, and everybody else arranged
themselves around it. That state is covered in
[[The Sundering of the Concord]], which is also where it ends. This note is
the rest of the world during the same five centuries.

Three countries were founded in them. After that nothing new appeared anywhere
until the Sundering broke one state into three, and the world has added only
two countries since.

## Three foundings, none of them planned

- **Around 1560** [[Kelvary March]] began as a border march the crown never
  garrisoned properly. It started renting the garrison out instead, and found
  that paid better than holding a border ever had. The Muster Court is that march-warden's
  court with the warden removed and the accountants left in place.
- **Around 1700** seven duchies with nothing but farmland between themselves
  and the steppe unified out of fear of open ground, on terms written so that
  none of the seven got the capital. That is the [[Voskreld Union]]. That clause is why the Union held and why a Voskreld road is
  cleared in winter.
- **Around 1820** a harbour refused a duke. The list of things the town would
  not do for him was read out to his face, and it is now the
  [[Corvane Republic]]'s charter, which still opens with a refusal.

None of the three was a conquest, and that is the age in miniature. Nobody was
taking anything by force, because the one institution that could have ruled on
a taking was still in the room and everybody used it.

## The frontier hardens

In 1776 the [[Khazan Khaganate]] and [[Xian Ti]] fortified the same line from
opposite sides within four years of each other, and neither has taken it or
left it since. See [[The Long Frontier]]. It has not gone a year without a
funeral on both sides of it, and it began in this age — which the west almost
never registers, because in the west these were the quiet centuries.

## The quiet is the thing to notice

Nothing else happened. No general war on either continent for four hundred and
eighty years, and the reason is not virtue. The Concord was the arbiter
everybody used, including the countries that resented it, because a ruling
that came out of Concord could be complied with without anybody losing face.

That is the case for arbitration, and it is worth putting in front of players
before they hear the case against it. The same arrangement produced
[[Dalstan]].

## The whispering

**GM.** From around 1940, [[Zon-Kuthon]] and [[Urgathoa]] worked the second and third
orders of the Concord clergy, one high cleric at a time, over the better part
of a generation. Nothing showed. The Sundering of 1976 is the end of this age
and the whole of the next note, and the people inside it never knew what had
happened to them.

## Threads

- **Kelvary's first charter.** It exists, it is four hundred years old, and
  it is a lease. Somebody with a copy could argue about who Kelvary's
  companies actually answer to, which is a question nobody has wanted asked.
- **The Voskreld capital clause.** Written to stop any one duchy holding it.
  Six of the seven provinces are content. The seventh has been quietly
  building the case that the clause was never ratified.
- **A Concord ruling still on the books.** Four centuries of arbitration went
  into a record that [[Thesal Theocracy]] still keeps, and some of those
  rulings bind countries that have forgotten they were party to them.
