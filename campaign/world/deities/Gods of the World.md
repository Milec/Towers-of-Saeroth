The deities actually venerated somewhere in the setting, one note per god,
pulled from the Pathfinder pantheon and kept here in `campaign/` so the
worldbuilding vault stands on its own without the full rules reference.

Most nations keep a patron plus two or three secondary faiths, in the Greek
city-state pattern — the pantheon is shared, the patron is local.
