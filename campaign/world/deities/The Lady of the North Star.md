**The Lady of the North Star (Mother of Secrets)**

The Lady's true name is absent from surviving records, whether lost to time or
deliberately hidden. Her scattered followers preserve only her epithets and a
promise: death is not the only possible end. Her faith concerns the knowledge
that people bury, the dead and forgotten who deserve to be remembered, winter's
patient preservation, and the difficult search for immortality.

Her followers say that the Lady once lived among mortals and watched friends
age, fail, and die. Moved by that grief, she devised a forbidden ritual that
could grant immortality to someone capable of completing it. She divided it
into nine stages and hid its fragments across Tian Xia. Whether the complete
ritual survives, and what it asks of the person who performs it, remains the
central uncertainty of her worship.

- **Areas of Concern** secrets, remembrance, winter, and immortality
- **Edicts** seek lost knowledge, uncover hidden mysteries, honor that which is forgotten, pursue immortality
- **Anathema** destroy sources of knowledge, reveal what should remain secret, desecrate a memorial, grow complacent about ignorance or mortality, commit torture or murder, knowingly harm an innocent
- **Divine Font** Harm or Heal
- **Divine Sanctification** may choose holy
- **Divine Skill** Occultism
- **Favored Weapon** Glaive
- **Domains** Cold, Darkness, Secrecy, Vigil
- **Alternate Domains** Change, Star
- **Cleric Spells** 1st: *penumbral shroud*; 2nd: *stupefy*; 6th: *blanket of stars*
- **Worship** also kept in [[Xian Ti]]

The Lady is not a public god of the empire and has no temple recognized by its
ministries. Her largest organized tradition is the [[Cult of the North Star]],
but devotion does not require membership. A dying scholar, a cursed traveller,
or an old master who refuses to accept an ending may receive the same dream and
begin a solitary search.
