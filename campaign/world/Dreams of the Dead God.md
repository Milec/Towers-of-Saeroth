**GM tool, for a table with an exemplar in it.** What arrives in an exemplar's
sleep is not a story about a god. It is a god's own night, handed over whole,
and about half of those nights are from before anyone had thought of killing
it. See [[Exemplars]] and [[The God Sundering]] for why.

The point of rolling for it is that the exemplar does not get to choose. A
player who has been dreading the knives gets Elysium instead, wakes up happy,
and cannot tell a single person at the table why. That is the whole texture of
the class here.

## When to roll

Not every night. Once in an adventuring day at the outside, and only when
something has pulled on what they carry:

- the night after they have leaned hard on the spark, or put an ikon to work
  in front of witnesses
- any night spent where the Red is close to the surface — a deep mine, a well
  shaft, a canal cut, the floor of a ruin, anywhere in a tower
- any night after somebody has tried to read them
- any night in [[The Wildlands]], where the pull comes from. **There, roll
  twice and take the lower.**

## The save

On waking, the exemplar attempts a **Will save** against the level-based DC
for their level: 15 at 1st, 20 at 5th, 27 at 10th, 34 at 15th, 43 at 20th.

The save does different work on each half of the table. On a **good** dream you
are holding on to something. On a **bad** one you are getting clear of it.

| Degree | Good dream (11–20) | Bad dream (1–10) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical success | The effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, higher result | No effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, higher result |
| Success | The effect | No effect |
| Failure | No effect. You remember splendour and cannot get back to it | The effect |
| Critical failure | No effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, lower result | The effect, and your first Will save today is rolled twice, lower result |

Every effect lasts until the exemplar's next daily preparations unless it says
otherwise.

## The dreams

Read to the player alone, in the second person. They are the god.

**1–2 Forgotten.** They are taking your name out of the liturgy one province at
a time, and every clerk doing it believes he is filing. You reach for the
people who hold you and there are fewer of them than yesterday, and then fewer
than that, and the reaching is what you do for three generations.

**3–4 The nothing.** No floor, no direction, and nothing arriving. You wait for
something to happen to you for what you work out afterwards was several
hundred years.

**5–6 Twelve ways.** Twelve hands take hold at once and no two of them pull the
same direction. You come apart along seams you did not know you had, and every
piece stays awake for it.

**7–8 The bleeding.** The knives do not stop and neither do you. Each wound
closes and the next one opens, and all of it runs off you and falls a very
long way down onto a green world that has done nothing to anybody.

**9–10 The murdered.** Three provinces of old priests, the last ones who can
still say it correctly. You watch it carried out as a sanitation order,
signed, countersigned, and performed by men who are bored by four in the
afternoon.

**11–12 The office.** You are doing the thing you are for. It is enormous, it
is routine, you have done it every day since before there was anybody to
notice, and you wake with your hands still moving.

**13–14 Worship.** A city says your name at the same time. Not begging, either.
The ordinary kind, said over bread and at the door before a journey, and it
comes in all day like weather.

**15–16 The judgement.** Something got in that should not have. You take it
apart with the sure hands of a man rehanging a gate, and it does not occur to
you that this is difficult.

**17–18 The strength.** You could put your hand through the sky. You do not,
because there is no reason to, and the not-doing is the part that stays with
you afterwards.

**19–20 Elysium.** All of them are there and the light is going through
everything. Somebody says something to you that you will never be able to
repeat, and you laugh.

## Running it

- **Hand it over privately.** The exemplar cannot describe any of this to the
  party — see [[The God Sundering]] — so passing the player a note or a
  message keeps the table honest without anybody having to act out being
  unable to speak. What the others see is somebody who came down to breakfast
  wrong.
- **The good half hurts too.** Elysium and the worship are memories of a life
  that ended, dreamt by somebody who has never had one. A player who rolls 19
  three sessions running is not being let off.
- **Let a bad roll cost the day, not the session.** These are one-day effects
  on purpose. If a debuff is going to sit on a character through a dungeon,
  roll on the morning of a travel day instead.
- **Do not explain the numbers.** Twelve ways is twelve because there were
  twelve of them. The office is the domain nobody has held since. Neither is
  ever going to be confirmed to anyone, and both are worth more unconfirmed.
