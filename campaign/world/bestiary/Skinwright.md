Something built these and stopped tending them a long time ago. The joints
still show tool marks, and the ribs are riveted where a spine should sit, but
whatever last serviced one did not mean for what it does now. Somewhere in
the working it went wrong, and what climbs out of a cave mouth or a
tree-line wants what it was never given.

- **Found** Caves, root-hollows, and the dark under old growth, on the wrong side of any road that runs alone
- **Hunts** By ambush, and it is patient about it. A fed one is the dangerous kind. Dressed in a stolen face it can close the last thirty feet in the open, and most people don't feel the wrongness until a hand is already on their wrist
- **Wants** A face that fits. It is not particular about whose

It has no words for any of this. If it did, this is what they would be:

> I want hair like yours\
> Hands like yours\
> Fingers that curl in the cold like yours\
> I want eyes to stare\
> And to cry when I'm feeling scared like you
>
> I want skin that burns\
> Lungs that ache\
> A chest that rises when the morning breaks\
> I want blood to spill\
> And to bruise\
> When I'm standing still like you
>
> I want words that shake\
> Lips that fail\
> A voice that splinters when it tells the tale\
> I want dreams to keep\
> And to lose\
> When I fall asleep like you
>
> I want years to waste\
> Time to mourn\
> A name that someone whispered when I'm born\
> I want death to fear\
> And to face\
> When it's finally here, like you

**Cast Off the Skin** `[one-action]` (emotion, fear, mental, visual) **Requirements** The skinwright is wearing a skin; **Effect** The skinwright destroys the worn skin and reverts to its true form. A creature already frightened by this ability today is temporarily immune.

**GM:** the weak Will save is the lever — whatever broke in it is a mental
fault, not a physical one, so an enchantment or a fear effect works on a
skinwright the way it never would on an ordinary construct. It doesn't speak
and it doesn't negotiate. It only stops when the skin it's wearing runs out,
or it does.
