Old Man Conrad appears where children have learned that an empty room can still
have someone in it. He speaks gently, asks to be let in, and wears a child's
voice like a borrowed coat when the answer is no.

- **Hunts** The frightened, the lonely, and children left alone after dark
- **Wants** A small body that can open doors, carry him past thresholds, and
  make adults listen
- **Won't** Cross a threshold without an invitation, however carelessly given

```pf2e-stats
**AC** 20; **Fort** +8, **Ref** +11, **Will** +10

**HP** 36 (rejuvenation, void healing); **Immunities** bleed, death effects,
disease, paralyzed, poison, precision, unconscious; **Resistances** all damage
5 (except force, ghost touch, spirit, or vitality; double resistance against
non-magical damage)

**Rejuvenation** (divine) Conrad can move on only after a child he possessed
is safely heard and believed by the adults charged with their care.

---

**Speed** fly 25 feet

**Melee** `[one-action]` ghostly hand +13 (agile, finesse, magical),
**Damage** 2d6+2 void

**Frightful Rebuke** `[one-action]` (auditory, divine, emotion, fear, mental)
DC 21 A living creature within 30 feet hears Conrad's patient voice naming a
fear it tries to hide and must attempt a Will save.
	**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3.
	**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.
	**Success** The creature is temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Borrowed Plaything** `[two-actions]` (divine, mental, possession) **Frequency**
once per round; **Requirements** Conrad is adjacent to a living humanoid;
**Effect** The target must attempt a DC 21 Will save. Conrad enters the
target's space on a failure and possesses it for 1 minute. While possessing a
creature, Conrad is hidden inside it, can't use his own actions, and controls
the creature's movement and actions. At the end of each of the possessed
creature's turns, it can attempt a DC 21 Will save to force Conrad out. Conrad
prefers children, but will take an adult only when he needs a door opened or a
voice believed.
	**Critical Success** The target is temporarily immune for 24 hours.
	**Success** The target is temporarily immune for 1 minute.
	**Critical Failure** Conrad possesses the target for 10 minutes instead.
```

**GM:** Conrad is a social horror encounter before he is a fight. Let the
possessed child repeat a phrase no child should know, then give the party a
clear chance to notice the ghost's influence before combat begins. The normal
rules for an unwillingly possessed character can be adjudicated at the table;
the listed saves give the group repeated ways to drive him out.
