---
title: Luaris
type: reference
visibility: The moon itself is common knowledge. That it was ever reached is a GM secret — see below
---

Saeroth's moon, named the way most old things in the sky are: by whichever
temple got there first. Sailors read it, farmers plant by it, and
[[Desna]]'s and [[Ketephys]]'s clergy both claim it as a domain and have
never quite settled which of them it belongs to.

---

**GM secret.** The empire that built [[The Towers]] reached it. At the
height of its power, the same portal network that connected its own cities
also opened onto Luaris — the furthest anything in [[Magitech]] has ever
gone, before or since. Nothing about how, or what was found there, survived
the erasure described in [[The Nameless Empire]]. No book, no scholar, and
no clergy on either continent knows Luaris was ever anything but a light in
the sky.
