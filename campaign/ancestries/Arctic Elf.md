*Elves adapted to bitter cold, who endure winters that would kill their southern kin.*

The [[Elven Confederacy]]'s northern pine tribes, called winter elves by outsiders. They hold the coldest forest belts and are the most isolationist of the elven peoples — a Confederacy council can wait a season simply because the arctic tribes' representatives have not yet come south.
