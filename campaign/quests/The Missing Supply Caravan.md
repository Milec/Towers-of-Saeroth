## The request

In [[Session 5 Play - The Broken Bridge]], the party learned that the expedition camp had only five days of food left. After they spent the night, [[Octavia Sere]] asked them to find another missing supply caravan. The scouts she had previously sent to find it had also gone missing.

## Progress

The party left the [[Expedition Site]] and reached a broken bridge. There they fought two trolls and their mother. The troll was killed, and [[Bobby|Belithûr Brightforge]] died to the trolls after being abandoned. The missing scouting party was dead at the bottom of the ravine.

The party has not yet located the caravan. Play resumes at the bridge, with the crossing still to be worked out. No reward or recovery terms have been recorded.

[[Session 6 Prep - The Bandits of Frohn]] covers the planned discovery of the wreck, Bobby’s new character, and the starving villagers who have the supplies.

[[The Missing Survey Stores]] is a separate, unplayed quarry-recovery proposal. Its raiders, inventory, and rewards are not established details of this caravan search.
