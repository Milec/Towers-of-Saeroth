## Session 1: Milo’s Manners

We begin on a stormy night, a few days’ journey from the mountain foothills and the border of the Lichdom. Our band of misbegotten adventurers includes:

- **Aurele Lamora**, the effeminate and knowledgeable gunslinger.
- **Belithûr**, the stocky and stinky runesmith.
- **Seneca**, the wyrmlike and holy cleric.
- **Victarion**, the obscure and blood-obsessed exemplar.
- **Yasmin Al-Amin**, the mysterious, brown-skinned summoner.
- **Yrsa Ægirsdóttir**, the blue and towering barbarian.

As the storm battered the campsite, our adventurers introduced themselves through thoughtful discussion of such timeless subjects as tickle time and weewees. Victarion also learned two important things: Milo does not appreciate having his slumber interrupted, and John Guard has no intention of getting *that* close to his coworkers.

When the caravan resumed its journey, it encountered **Gibble-Gobble**, a vicious goblin bandit with designs on its precious cargo. Thinking himself a cunning strategist, Vic offered up his belongings to lure a goblin close enough to pour alchemist’s fire down its throat.

And attempt it he did!

Unfortunately, the dastardly goblins were observant enough to interrupt this perfectly reasonable act of diplomacy.

What followed was a battle of wits and mettle as goblins descended upon our heroes. Spells flew, weapons swung, and gunshots rang out across the road. Despite their questionable introductions, our adventurers proved considerably more competent at violence, emerging victorious over the bandits. Another glorious triumph for the side of good!

Vic then complicated that last point by drinking goblin blood like some kind of freak. Surely he has a reasonable explanation. Time will tell.

Milo rewarded the party with a pat on the back. They cleaned themselves up and set off down the road once more, forever changed by their brush with death—and now **level 2**.

Surely they are prepared for whatever lies ahead.
