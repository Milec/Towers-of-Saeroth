# Session 0 checklist

Use this before play begins. The aim is a clear table agreement, characters
with reasons to travel together, and a first session that starts moving.

## The campaign

- [ ] Give the short pitch: a caravan is carrying supplies toward a rumoured
  fourth tower, and the party is present when the job goes wrong.
- [ ] Confirm the intended tone: adventurous fantasy, political pressure,
  uncertain loyalties, and consequences that follow from the party's choices.
- [ ] Ask what each player wants most from the campaign: tactical combat,
  character drama, exploration, mysteries, faction play, or something else.
- [ ] Agree on how much spotlight each player wants and whether any character
  themes should remain in the background.
- [ ] Explain that the party can choose its own direction after the opening
  disaster; no one route is the required story.

## Table safety and comfort

- [ ] Set lines: subjects that will not appear in play.
- [ ] Set veils: subjects that may exist but fade to black or stay off-screen.
- [ ] Choose a pause tool. Anyone can stop, skip, or rewind a scene without
  having to explain why in the moment.
- [ ] Confirm how to handle conflict between player characters: consent first,
  then keep the disagreement fun for everyone at the table.
- [ ] Ask privately whether anyone has topics, imagery, names, or situations
  they would prefer you avoid.

## Characters and party ties

- [ ] Confirm ancestry, class, background, pronouns, level, and starting gear
  for each character.
- [ ] Give every character a reason to be on the caravan: guard, factor,
  passenger, stowaway, hired hand, or another agreed role.
- [ ] Have each player name one thing their character wants and one thing they
  refuse to abandon.
- [ ] Create at least one tie between every pair of characters: a shared job,
  obligation, debt, old favor, relative, belief, or bad first impression.
- [ ] Establish why the group will stay together after the caravan is lost.
- [ ] Confirm what player information is public, private to the GM, or secret
  from the other characters.

## Pathfinder 2e expectations

- [ ] Confirm the game version, allowed books, and any house rules.
- [ ] Explain how hero points, secret checks, recalls, and rules questions are
  handled at this table.
- [ ] Remind players that encounters reward movement, terrain, skill actions,
  and solving the problem beyond simply defeating enemies.
- [ ] Agree on rules lookup timing: make a quick ruling at the table, then
  review it between sessions if needed.
- [ ] Confirm digital tools, character-sheet access, and how players can ask
  for help with Foundry or their sheets.

## Practical agreements

- [ ] Confirm regular play time, session length, breaks, and quorum.
- [ ] Decide how late arrivals, absences, and character control are handled.
- [ ] Agree on food, voice chat, cameras, music, and other table logistics.
- [ ] Choose where session notes, rules clarifications, and scheduling updates
  will live.
- [ ] Set a brief end-of-session check-in: what worked, what dragged, and what
  each player hopes to see next time.

## Ready for Session 1

- [ ] The party has a shared reason to travel with the [[Thurion Merchant Alliance]] caravan.
- [ ] Everyone knows the caravan begins at [[Tessine]] and is bound toward
  [[Vaelic Principality]].
- [ ] Each player has one personal detail the GM can bring into the opening.
- [ ] The party agrees to start in motion: on the road, with the caravan,
  before the first trouble begins.
- [ ] End with the opening question: **What did your character notice about
  the caravan before anyone else did?**
