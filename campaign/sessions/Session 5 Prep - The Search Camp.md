## Where play resumes

The party reached the [[Expedition Site]] at the end of [[Session 4 Play - Waystation WLW]]. [[Karoline]] came with them as an independent person. Wenzel is dead. John Guard, the caravan's horses, and its carts were lost earlier; the party has three tents. Do not replay the waystation or restore the wagons.

The expedition is searching for the tower. It has not found it. The party knows about the cargo-only insurance, the Melisor flask, and the expedition's funding. They believe they were meant to die; that remains their conclusion, not a fact established by this preparation.

Everything after this starting position is **proposed, unplayed preparation**. The three new camp NPCs are available to introduce now. Keep this note as prep until the session actually happens, then record the choices and outcome in a separate recap.

## Tonight's focus

Give the party a place to recover, people worth talking to, and a practical decision about the search. [[The Missing Survey Stores]] can take the rest of the session, or remain the next job if camp conversations carry the evening. Negotiation and investigation are complete scenes; they do not need a fight added to justify them.

**A useful opening:**

> The guard at the trail counts the people coming uphill, then looks past them for wagons. Behind her, a cook is scraping a pot and two surveyors are arguing over a wet map. Someone calls for the quartermaster. "They're here. But the stores aren't."

Let the party answer. Once the missing caravan is clear, someone brings water and calls [[Octavia Sere]]. No check is needed for basic shelter or for the survivors to make themselves understood.

These three are not a replacement adventuring party. Their sheets exist for trouble at camp; they remain there during the recovery job. Karoline's decision to take work or accompany the party belongs to her and the conversation at the table.

The rest of the expedition staff remain available without requiring ten introductions at once: Melisor's chef and archaeologist; Thurion's laborers, guards, and medic; one Melisor scouting party; and one scouting party of [[Kelvary March]] mercenaries. Introduce individual names when they matter rather than inventing a full cast during the arrival scene.

## The first conversation

Octavia asks the survivors to put down their packs. She hears [[Milo Calvetti]] and the PCs together, then asks Renata to distinguish witnesses from inferences. Use three questions and follow whichever answer gives the players something to pursue:

1. **What did you see happen to the caravan?** Record the trap, undead, and destroyed goods in the party's own terms. Let them decide what to say about Wenzel and the waystation.
2. **What evidence did you bring?** Ask to see what was actually recovered. Do not conjure the flask or a signed confession into their inventory.
3. **What do you want from us now?** Food, answers, work, money, shelter, and a route forward are all reasonable starting points.

Octavia confirms that Melisor funds the search. Renata confirms that she can request records but cannot settle a cargo claim herself. Neither denies something merely to prolong the mystery. Neither knows Cassian's role or offers his name as the solution.

If the party accuses the leadership, let them identify a claim and ask for evidence of it. Octavia opens ordinary survey records; Tavian allows a supervised stores count; Renata offers to preserve the accusation as an attributed statement. Refusing to sign that statement does not eject the party from camp.

## Let them look around

Use the existing **Session 3 — Expedition Search Camp** scene despite its older scene title. It is still the correct search camp, with no visible tower. If the party only wants a conversation, theater of the mind is enough.

- **Survey table:** checked paths, corrected bearings, and unresolved leads. A character can help compare witness accounts or ask what makes a lead worth following. No roll discovers the tower from this table.
- **Stores:** Tavian shows the missing-shipment inventory and introduces the returning porter. This establishes the recovery hook without a perception gate.
- **Cookfire:** let the group eat and talk. The caravan's destruction affects people whose work depended on it; everyone need not treat the survivors as either heroes or suspects.
- **Camp boundary:** space is available for the party's three tents and six bedrolls. Existing expedition tents belong to its staff. Confirm watches only if they will matter this session.

Give the PCs time to recover their ordinary resources before offering an optional combat job. Resolve healing by their usual abilities. The camp medic is a person with work to do, not an unlimited refill button.

## The recovery offer

Tavian puts two marked chests on his list and explains the food sacks. The returning porter last saw three orc raiders taking the goods toward an old quarry sorting yard. Octavia cannot strip the camp's remaining crews to chase them.

> "Bring back the equipment and enough food to keep the crews out. If you can do it without more funerals, do."

Use [[The Missing Survey Stores]] for the complete inventory, terms, map, approaches, three-creature roster, and reward. State the payment before the party agrees. Tavian explicitly permits negotiating away one sack of food to recover the equipment and remaining sack.

**If they decline:** ask which survey lead or caravan question they want to pursue. Octavia can offer a short reconnaissance task with no prepared combat, and Renata can take their request for records. Do not force the recovery job by making camp suddenly refuse food.

**If they accept:** use **Old Survey Quarry — Supply Recovery**. The default encounter is moderate for five level-2 PCs, and it has negotiation and stealth routes. The expedition NPCs stay at camp. Do not start initiative simply because the battle map has been opened.

## Character openings

Use one or two when the relevant player takes an interest. These invite decisions; they do not assign a reaction.

- **Belithûr:** Tavian wants a second pair of eyes on worn fittings. Belithûr can ask about the old powder shipment without being told his merchant factor's motives.
- **Aurele:** Renata asks whether he wants his name attached to his account. Let him decide how much his family connections enter the conversation.
- **Yrsa:** a surveyor asks how she would get a heavy crate across rough ground. Karoline may have her own view of the journey, but nobody has decided their relationship for them.
- **Seneca:** the missing caravan prompts a request for rites for John if the party wants them. Any discussion of immortality or the North Star begins with Seneca; no automatic revelation follows.
- **Victarion:** Tavian asks about practical food requirements privately if Vic raises them. Hunger is his player's tracked ancestry state, not an excuse for the GM to force a public confession.

## Close the session

If the party returns from the quarry, Tavian counts what came back, Renata witnesses the agreed payment, and Octavia asks what they learned. Pay for a negotiated success on the same terms as combat. Any alliance, threat, prisoner, missing item, or broken promise goes into the next recap.

If the evening ends in camp, close on a chosen job or a specific unanswered question. The search map can point toward the next area to investigate without placing the tower there.
