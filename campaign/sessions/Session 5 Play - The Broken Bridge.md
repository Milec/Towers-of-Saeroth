## Session record

At the [[Expedition Site]], the party met expedition leader [[Octavia Sere]], quartermaster [[Tavian Calvo]], and Thurion representative [[Renata Vellori]]. [[Ashwin Devaraj (The Drink)]] was also at camp. The party reached level 3.

They learned that the camp had only five days of food left. After the party spent the night, Octavia asked them to find another supply caravan that had gone missing. The scouts she had already sent to look for it had also failed to return.

The party paid for a gender transformation potion and force-fed it to [[Liam|Aurèle]].

They left camp to search for [[The Missing Supply Caravan]]. On the way, they came to a broken bridge and fought two trolls and their mother. The troll was killed. [[Bobby|Belithûr Brightforge]] died to the trolls after being abandoned. The missing scouting party was dead at the bottom of the ravine.

The session ended as the party prepared to work out how to cross the broken bridge.
