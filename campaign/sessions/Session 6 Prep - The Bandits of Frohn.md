## The broken bridge

Open where [[Session 5 Play - The Broken Bridge]] ended. The scouts lie dead in the ravine. Ask the four survivors how they mean to cross and who goes first. A rope secured on the far bank could serve them twice. On the return trip they'll have the weight of the supplies to reckon with.

## A sound from the wreck

A few hundred feet beyond the bridge, the road bends around the wreck of [[The Missing Supply Caravan]].

> A dead horse lies across the traces. Beyond it you can see the other horses, the splintered carts and a few guards lying where they fell. In the quiet between footsteps, someone draws a shallow breath.

The survivor is bloodied and barely breathing. Bring [[Bobby]] into play as soon as someone reaches them. Ask Bobby what the others notice first and, once the character can speak, what name they give. Leave their history and how much of the attack they saw to Bobby.

Give anyone searching the carts the trail. The cargo is gone, and bootprints lead off the road beside the drag marks of a heavy load. A torn sack has spilled grain where the carriers stopped to lift it. The players can follow even if the survivor remembers nothing. Someone who scouts ahead can spot the first lookout in time for the party to decide how to approach.

## Who has the supplies?

Villagers from around Frohn have turned to banditry as their villages starve. These bandits have the caravan's supplies. At their stopping place, one of them holds a sack open while another measures grain into the smaller bags people have brought from home.

> “You can take the cart gear. But before you take that sack, tell me what I’m supposed to feed my girls tonight.”

Give the party room to answer. The expedition was down to five days of food when the party heard the news last session. Both sides have people waiting to eat.

- **Negotiate.** The bandits can offer to carry the expedition’s share back to the bridge in exchange for food to take home. Let the players propose a division. Establish how much goes to each group and who will answer for the deal at camp.
- **Threaten.** Ask what the party demands and whether the villagers have a clear way to leave. If the bandits yield, give the party the supplies they demanded. The villagers can carry word of the threat home.
- **Fight.** The party can kill the bandits and recover the stores. Keep the supplies in view during the fight. A villager who sees the others fall might surrender, or grab one bag and run. Let the players decide whether it is worth pursuing them.

## Back at the expedition

Once the supplies are secure, ask how the party gets them back across the ravine. At the [[Expedition Site]], [[Tavian Calvo]] counts what came back. Give each payment its own moment.

1. **The message from Casa Berruel.** [[Renata Vellori]], the Thurion correspondent, delivers the house’s letter and its **60 gp** for the original caravan’s four surviving PCs, **15 gp each**. The letter is in Renata’s entry. Give the players a moment to decide what they make of the gesture.
2. **Vic’s contract.** Pay Victarion what his contract promises. Confirm the sum against the agreement at the table. This goes to Vic personally.
3. **The recovery reward.** [[Octavia Sere]] offers the party its choice of **gold or magical items** for bringing back the supplies. Set the gold amount and the items on offer before play so the players can compare them. The old quarry proposal in [[The Missing Survey Stores]] has its own reward for a different job.

Record Bobby’s character name, the food that reached each destination, any promises made at Frohn, and the payments actually accepted.
