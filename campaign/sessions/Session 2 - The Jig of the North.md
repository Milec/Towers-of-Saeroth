Following the turbulent events of the day, our brave band unwinds and sets up camp, articulately debating who should keep watch and when. They eventually agree upon an order after much tribulation.

First up were the dwarf and the half-elf. Typically an opportunity for racial tension, neither seemed to give it much care. An uneventful watch passed as the half-elf tinkered with his weapon and shrewdly arrived at an invention he had been agonizing over for an untold amount of time. The dwarf noticed his runic tattoos beginning to glow and surge with an unknown power, to which he simply shrugged and woke the next watch.

Next were the giant and the brown-skinned one. A rather uneventful watch passed for them as well, outside of one of the caravan’s horses entering a frenzy and attempting to flee. However, through deft hands and gentle souls, the two were able to calm the horse back to sleep. Over the course of this watch, the giant found herself uncharacteristically cold, with ice nipping at her appendages. The brown-skinned one took her boots off and started grounding. Peculiar, but what isn’t about our merry band?

The giant, confused about the order of watch, went to reawaken the dwarf through a generous cultural display of a Northern Jig. The dwarf, ungrateful in nature, accosted the giant and demanded she find somewhere else to sleep, resulting in her sharing a tent with the half-elf.

Watch three: the Wyrm. Another uneventful watch. Seneca found himself extremely vigilant and, to remain prepared for any evildoers, rested his eyes during his watch so he would be fresh for combat. However, upon opening them, he discovered a note and a star-shaped fruit next to him. He bravely devoured the fruit without question and found a new power residing within his body. Only time will tell how he can cultivate this.

Final watch: the Blood Freak. Caught in a restless nightmare of pain and suffering, Vic was shaken awake by Seneca. He found his weapons mysteriously missing and a new mark upon his hands. While brilliantly endeavoring to find his weapons for his watch, he was left aghast as they miraculously appeared in his hands. Vic proceeded to ingeniously stab himself with them, discovering that he could manifest and store them through his blood. An uneventful watch followed.

After a night of transformations and unknown boons, our brave adventurers departed once again upon the road. After hours of travel, the party was halted by the brave John Guard, who had spotted something. Unwilling to put others in danger, John Guard climbed down from the cart and inspected the shining silver item. His last act: an act of selflessness and extreme bravery. As he meticulously picked up the silver flask, a massive explosion went off, incinerating John Guard to the bone. A truly brave hero, he will be missed by many, including his wife, three children, and local community, where he was an avid participant in the fostering program.

Alas, this was not the end of the danger, merely a signal. Upon the lighting of the John Guard Flare, undead descended upon the party. Zombies and skeletons alike had seemingly only one dastardly goal in mind: destroy the caravan. And it appeared someone had prepared them for this mission, as several zombies had casks of alchemist’s fire strapped into their torsos.

Despite our brave adventurers’ best efforts, they were unable to save the righteous caravan from the clutches of the malevolent undead flames. This was due in part to the serendipitously placed barrels of black powder provided by the dwarf’s merchant factor. Why there were three barrels in each cart instead of having them concentrated in one to reduce the risk is unknown to our adventurers. However, it matters little now. In the ensuing battle, they managed to bravely destroy the undead.

After the heroic victory over the malevolent undead forces, our adventurers were enlightened to several facts by both Milo, the steadfast caravan leader, and the evidence left upon the dead:

- The caravan had an unusually large insurance policy taken out on its cargo.
- The trap flask belonged to the Melisor Magocracy.
- According to Milo, this was not the doing of the Lichdom’s undead, for they treat theirs with more esteem.
- The expedition and this caravan were both funded by the Magocracy.
- This caravan was being run through the Merchant Republic house of Casa Berruel.

With these facts, our shrewd adventurers came to the conclusion that fraud was afoot and that they were not intended to survive. Whether this is true remains to be seen, but one thing is certain: this was no random undead attack.

In the end, our intrepid band of miscreants decided to continue heading toward the expedition site, which, according to Milo, is about four days away.
