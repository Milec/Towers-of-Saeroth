The party reached a waystation with an exorbitant toll. Unable to pay, they agreed to do the guard captain, [[Karoline]], a favor: get rid of the weird necromancer. Karoline also wanted a girls’ night, so the party split up to handle both.

Aurele dressed up as a woman and joined Yrsa for girls’ night. Together, they got the captain plastered and robbed her. Meanwhile, the rest of the party killed the necromancer, with some proving more useful than others.

By morning, they’d been caught stealing. Somehow, they still managed to convince Karoline to come with them.

After what felt like ages, the party finally reached the [[Expedition Site|expedition site]].
