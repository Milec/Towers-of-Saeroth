## Session record

Each party member had their level-up sequence to level 2. The caravan then reached the ambush.

[[John Guard]] was incinerated and killed. The ambush succeeded: all the carts and horses were destroyed. No one else died.

The party learned that the flask came from the [[Melisor Magocracy]] and that the caravan's owners had taken out a large insurance policy. The policy covered **cargo only**, not the guards or crew.

The party is convinced they were meant to die in the ambush too. This is their belief, not a confirmed revelation about the attackers' or owners' intentions.

## Where the session ended

The party decided to continue toward the tower expedition, which is six days away. All party members are alive and level 2, but the caravan's carts and horses are gone.
