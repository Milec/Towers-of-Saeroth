## Starting position

Continue from [[Session 2 - The Jig of the North]]. John Guard is dead; all carts and horses were destroyed. The other travellers survived. Milo estimates about four days to the expedition. The expedition is looking for the tower and has not found it.

The cargo insurance, Melisor flask and expedition funding are known. The party believes they were intended victims; do not turn that belief into proof. The insurance covers cargo only.

The following scenes are proposed prep, not a record of events. Use the mountain crossing and waystation as this session's core; expedition arrival can close the session or open the next. Do not require every obstacle to become a fight.

## Before leaving the wreck

Ask what the party actually recovered, who carries it, and whether they take anything to remember John. Record their answers rather than inventing a lost inventory. Give them time to recover before another fight. Milo wants the survivors to reach the expedition; he can repeat what he told them, not reveal the private conspiracy from the GM notes.

## Scene 1: Broken Mountain Crossing

**Map:** Session 3 — Broken Mountain Crossing. West entrance; east exit. The ravine runs north–south. The bridge's missing deck is about ten feet across at the scene scale. Loose rock lies beside the upper western approach.

**Opening:** The path ends at two wooden bridge sections hanging above the stream. Rope still joins the broken ends. On the western slope, fresh pale stone marks where the mountain has shed part of its face.

**Choices:** secure a rope crossing, brace the missing span with salvaged material, or find a longer route around. Tell players the span and drop before they commit. A visible lower ledge can provide a rescue position; establish the actual drop before resolving any fall. Do not spring an unannounced instant-death fall.

For an uncertain repair or route assessment, a suggested level-2 obstacle DC is 16 (Crafting or Survival as appropriate). A sound plan with adequate tools may need no check. Use the actual Leap, Long Jump, Climb and Grab an Edge rules for those actions, rather than replacing them with a generic group roll. A failed preparation check costs time or material; it does not automatically drop everybody into the ravine.

**Landslide option:** rocks have already blocked part of the western trail. Clear a narrow path together or take the visible detour. If you want an active slide, telegraph cracking stone and give time to withdraw; this prep does not include a damaging hazard statblock or automated rockfall.

**Optional wolves:** three published Monster Core Wolves, level 1 each, total encounter budget 90 XP (Low for six level-2 PCs; not 90 XP awarded to each character). Stage them in the western brush before the crossing or after everyone has reached safe ground. Growls and tracks provide warning. Food, deterrence or retreat can resolve the scene. Do not combine an attack with a split party hanging over the ravine. The wolves retreat when the prey is too dangerous; there are no automatic reinforcements.

Foundry: cliff and railing walls block ordinary token movement but not sight. The GM moves tokens across the broken span after resolving a successful crossing. Falls and terrain costs are manual.

## Scene 2: Mountain Toll Waystation

**Map:** Session 3 — Mountain Toll Waystation. The western building contains a kitchen, office and bunk room. The eastern shelter houses the visiting necromancer. The toll boom blocks movement but not sight.

**Opening:** A striped timber bars the road. Behind it, a keeper looks from the travellers' soot-stained clothes to the empty road behind them. “On foot? Where's the rest of your caravan?”

**Toll keeper:** working role, not a new named canon NPC. Wants the crossing recorded and the station maintained. Will not leave the post unattended or surrender the ledger. Start with questions, not a fight. Offer payment at a price you choose before play, useful maintenance work, or Milo's written undertaking. Do not manufacture a fixed toll or faction affiliation as established lore.

**Wenzel Grauth:** use [[Wenzel Grauth]], not a new NPC called Wendell. His presence at the station is the proposed scene hook. He wants to leave the frontier and will run if confronted. His existing actor and portrait are already in the Saeroth module; do not create a duplicate.

### What Wenzel knows

- He prepared the undead and strapped supplied alchemist's fire into their bodies.
- He was told the wagons were a Melisor consignment and nobody aboard mattered.
- He was supplied twenty-four lesser alchemist's fire flasks; he did not manufacture them.
- He can describe the packing crate and its maker's mark.
- He was instructed to leave a Melisor reagent flask and did not understand why.
- He can identify the intermediary who hired him. He has never met [[Cassian Merovel]] and cannot give the party that name.
- He was not told about the insurance. Do not let him explain the whole fraud.

**Ways forward:** question him quietly, confront him with the flask, follow him when he leaves, or promise safe passage in exchange for testimony. Basic visible evidence and his accessible possessions should not all sit behind a single roll. If the players let him leave, preserve a later lead through his departure direction or the keeper's ordinary recollection; do not force capture.

**If it turns violent:** use his existing level-3 sheet. He tries to escape, not fight to the death. The toll keeper protects the station and does not automatically join either side. Do not add undeclared undead reinforcements. Decide the intermediary's identity before providing an actual name; it is not supplied by this prep.

## Scene 3: Expedition Search Camp

**Map:** Session 3 — Expedition Search Camp. Southern arrival trail, central fire, southwest supply enclosure, southeast command pavilion, northward scouting route. No tower is visible or located here.

**Opening:** Canvas tents fill a sheltered patch of mountain ground. Survey stakes and muddy boots stand outside the command pavilion. Someone stops counting crates long enough to ask, “Where are the wagons?”

The leader wants a usable account of the attack and enough supplies to keep the search parties in the mountains. Their name and personal history remain open. They are not automatically a conspirator because Melisor funds the expedition.

Ask the party for witnesses, recovered evidence and an account of the losses. The search has not found the tower. Maps show surveyed routes and remaining leads, not an exact tower location. Let the party's suspicions shape the conversation without making everyone hostile.

### Recovery job for the following session

The user's quest premise is recovery of resources taken by orc bandits. Suggested cargo: food and climbing equipment. The leader cannot spare their remaining camp staff while keeping search teams supplied. This is a proposed reason to hire the party, not a requirement that they accept.

Recovering the goods is the objective. Negotiation, an exchange, stealth or combat are all valid. Leave the bandits' numbers, location, motives and reward for the next encounter prep; no balanced bandit fight is claimed ready here. Keep the theft separate from the caravan conspiracy unless you deliberately connect them.

**End beat:** the leader unfolds a survey map and points out the last known location of the stolen supplies. The tower remains somewhere beyond the mapped routes.

## Foundry setup

The three scenes are inactive by default, 160-foot squares with 5-foot grids. Lighting is prepared for evening; daylight is the default. Walls and doors follow the artwork. Tents in the search camp are exterior scenery, not furnished playable interiors. The command pavilion is accessible through its open flap.

No combat, weather damage, falls or scene activation is automated. Place party tokens at the entrance when ready; do not move existing campaign tokens or alter their HP. For the optional wolf scene, import three tokens from the published Wolf actor. Place the existing Wenzel actor inside the eastern waystation shelter. Toll keeper and leader remain roleplay positions until you choose actors for them.

Rules references checked in the local vault: Monster Core Wolf (level 1); GM Core Level-Based DCs (level 2: DC 16). Encounter budgets assume six level-2 PCs.
