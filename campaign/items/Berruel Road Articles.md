The engagement a player character signs to travel with the
[[Session 01 — The Berruel Consignment|Berruel consignment]] as a hired guard
from [[Tessine]] onward. It is a printed [[Casa Berruel]] form with four lines
filled in by hand, countersigned by [[Milo Calvetti]] because the house keeps
no agent past the harbour, and stamped by the Tessine registry because the
Signory takes its duty on every instrument executed in the city.

Everything under the next heading is the document. Hand it over, read it out,
or print the page and stop at the rule. What follows the rule is for the GM.

## The articles

**Articles of road service.** Made at Tessine between **Casa Berruel of
Vessene**, of the Thurion Merchant Alliance, hereafter the House, and the hand
subscribed below, hereafter the Hand.

**1. Term.** From this signing to the receipt of the consignment at the staging
yard below the Sallow Gate. The House reckons the journey at twenty-eight days,
water and road. Days served beyond the twenty-eighth are paid at the same wage
on the countersignature of a factor of the House, and not otherwise.

**2. Service.** The Hand keeps what watch the caravan master sets, by night and
by day, walks or rides with the wagon assigned, and obeys the caravan master in
every matter of the road. On the river leg the Hand serves aboard the barge as
the master directs.

**3. Charge.** The Hand answers for the wagon assigned and the stores upon it:
victual, water, lamp oil, cordage and feed, and may open and count the same at
will. Shortfall is deducted from the balance.

**4. Sealed goods.** Two wagons carry goods sealed at Vessene under charter to
the Melisor Magocracy. No hand of the train may open, count or inspect them. A
broken seal ends this engagement and the wage with it.

**5. Arms.** The Hand finds arms, armour and bedroll. What the train issues
from its stores is valued by the caravan master and deducted.

**6. Wage.** Five silver for each day served, paid in coin at each stage-house,
and one gold earnest at signing, which is not returnable and which binds these
articles.

**7. Balance.** Five gold on the receipt of the consignment, drawn on the House
at Vessene and payable at any counting-house of the Alliance.

**8. Perils not required.** The Hand is not required to stand against an
organised force of arms, the act of a crowned power, war declared or
undeclared, fire, or the rising of the dead. Where the consignment is lost to
any of these the Hand is discharged without fault, and without further wage.

**9. Salvage.** Whatever is recovered from a loss is the House's, and after the
House the underwriter's. A hand holding recovered goods after discharge holds
them against these articles.

**10. Death.** ~~Twenty~~ **Ten** gold to the person named below, on the
caravan master's report, where the Hand dies in service. Not payable where the
loss falls under Article 8. *(struck and initialled in a clerk's hand, in
different ink from the rest of the form)*

**11. Discharge and forfeit.** A hand who leaves the train before the receipt
forfeits wage and balance, and is entered on the register at Vessene. The
register is honoured in every port of the Alliance.

**12. Law and forum.** These articles are made under the commercial code of the
Thurion Merchant Alliance and answerable at Vessene, wherever executed.
Registration at Tessine is for the Signory's duty and admits no other
jurisdiction.

Three copies: one to the House, one to the caravan master, one to the Hand.

> - **The Hand** ______________________ *name, or mark*
> - **Named for Article 10** ______________________
> - **For the House** *Milo Calvetti, caravan master, for Casa Berruel of
>   Vessene*
> - **Registered** Tessine harbour registry, entry number and stamp, duty paid

## What it pays

| | Amount | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earnest | 1 gp | At signing, not returnable |
| Wage | 5 sp per day served | In coin, at each stage-house |
| Balance | 5 gp | On receipt at the far end, on Berruel paper |
| Death benefit | 10 gp | To a named person, and not under Article 8 |

Twenty gold for a full run of twenty-eight days, of which fifteen is coin on
the road and five is a draft on a house that will not be honouring drafts.

**Why these numbers.** Five silver a day is the published rate for a skilled
hireling, which is what an armed road hand is. A first-level character who
stayed home and took work off a notice board would earn two silver a day at a
level 1 task. The road pays better because the road is worse. The earnest is
the part that makes a signature binding and the balance is the part a house can
withhold, which is the shape of every contract in this campaign. None of it
outpaces the treasure a first-level party should be carrying, because most of
it is spent on the road or promised at the end of it.

*Rates: skilled hireling, 5 sp per day, and Table 4-2 Income Earned —*
*Player Core.*

**In hand when the wagons burn.** Wages stop at the last stage-house behind
them, and the next one is past the tri-point. Count the earnest, plus five
silver for every day from Tessine to the last stage, and nothing since. Four
days past Quivar, that is two gold owed and unpayable, five gold of balance
that no longer exists, and whatever the character walked off the barge with.

## What the clauses are actually for

**Article 4 puts the sealed wagons in the players' hands on the first day.**
The guard is told in writing which two wagons they are not to look at. That is
the only sentence in the document the house needed anybody to read.

**Article 3 is the session's answer.** Water is stores, stores are the guard's
charge, and a hand who has counted the barrels knows where they are and is
within their articles to open them. The wagons burn because killing a kindled
shambler beside one sets it alight, and a guard who has counted their own load
has the douse already in reach.

**Article 8 is not a plant.** The same clerks draft Casa Berruel's marine
policies and its road articles, and the excluded perils were copied across so
the house never pays a hand for a loss it is claiming on. It has read that way
on every Berruel form for thirty years. As evidence it is worthless. As a
thread it is the best thing in the document: a player who notices that their
own contract excluded fire and the rising of the dead will go looking for the
policy that excludes the same two perils, and that policy is at [[Tessine]].

**Article 9 is a problem the party will not see coming.** Every unburnt flask
of alchemist's fire, the maker's mark on it, the manifest, the Melisor reagent
flask in the brush: all of it is the underwriter's property the moment the loss
is declared, and holding it is a breach of the articles the house can prove.
The evidence and the contract cannot both be kept.

**The strike on Article 10 was made at Tessine, days before the road.** A house
four months from insolvency cutting a cost line is the dullest thing in the
world, and it is deniable, and it was done by somebody whose initials are on
the form and whose name is written out in full in the registry entry. Pick who
that is before the party goes back to ask.

**Article 12 is the reason any of this survives.** Casa Berruel's position
after the loss will be that the hands were Calvetti's, hired on his own account
out of his own purse, and that the house's draft is a matter between him and
them. Milo has been paying the guards out of his own purse for three weeks,
which makes that story true in every particular a court would test. What breaks
it is the Signory's stamp. The entry is in the Tessine registry, and Tessine
has never in sixty years handed a document to whoever asked nicely.

## At the table

The articles give a first-level character standing rather than power. They can
open a wagon and be obeyed, send another hand to the far side of the train, and
ask the caravan master a question and get an answer, which nobody riding as a
passenger can. [[John Guard]] is senior by eleven seasons on
the road and by nothing else; rank in the train runs through Milo.

Breaking the articles costs no gold and a great deal. A hand who runs is not
fined, they are listed, and the list is read in every Alliance port by every
factor who might have hired them. Thurion's whole product is that a contract
sealed in one of its cities is enforced in the rest. For a character who keeps
[[Abadar]], the articles are also a religious instrument, sealed at a shrine by
the door, and walking away from one is not a business decision.

If more than one player wants in, they sign the same form. It is printed by the
ream.

## Assumptions this note makes

The river leg from Tessine up to [[Quivar]] and the wagons taken up there are
this note's reading of the route in [[Session 01 — The Berruel Consignment]],
which names the road and not the water. The staging yard below the Sallow Gate
is where the Magocracy's forward depot receipts what comes up the pass. Change
either and only Articles 1 and 2 move.
