# Elemental  *Spell List*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 203

The [[Archetypes/Elementalist|elementalist]] archetype uses this elemental spell list. It includes spells from all hardcover rulebooks up to this release.

 You can discuss with the GM any spells from other sources you want to add to your list. As a general rule, spells with the [[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait belong on the list, including spells that add one of those traits depending on how they're cast, such as [[Spells/Elemental Zone|_elemental zone_]]. Spells that are general and appear on every tradition's spell list also make good candidates.
