# Storming Gaze two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hungerseed]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 37

---

You can open or energize a notable third eye on your forehead to strike with storming power. You deal 3d4 [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage in a 15-foot cone; Storming Gaze gains this trait. Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Reflex saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. You can't use this ability again for 1d4 rounds.

At 7th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d4.
