# Patron Reborn  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 63

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

---

You have fully realized the ability to serve as a patron.

Your newfound abilities enhance your senses, rolling back the veil of a realm hidden to most mortals where great beings vie for unimaginable influence over vast worlds. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]], constant [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]], and the ability to see into all other transitive planes that overlap the one you are in.

You can be a patron for other witches. Choose a single type of patron you represent, such as [[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/The Resentment]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Starless Shadow]]. An allied witch you can observe who has that patron counts as and gains the benefits of your [[Feats/Witch's Charge (Player Core)|Witch’s Charge]] feat, even if you did not select them as your charge during your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]].

You learn the [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual and can impart that knowledge to your charges. Once per month, when one of your charges performs the _wish_ ritual, you can roll the skill check in their place.
