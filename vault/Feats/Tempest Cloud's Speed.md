# Tempest Cloud's Speed  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 116

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]].

---

You and your familiar coordinate to channel elemental air. Your familiar gains the following ability.

**Activate** two actions Path of the Tempest;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]]) Your familiar summons winds that speed up and protect you. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed until the end of your turn. When you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] this turn, you don’t trigger reactions. In addition, you don’t need to Stride before a [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] this turn.
