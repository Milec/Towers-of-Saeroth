# Parthenogenic Hatchling  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

---

You were hatched from an unfertilized egg during hard times for your people, and are a biological copy of your mother. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]]. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease, you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|battle hardened fighter class feature]]), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.

 You take damage only every 2 hours from thirst and every 2 days from starvation, [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|rather than every hour and every day]].

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level.
