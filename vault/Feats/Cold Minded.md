# Cold Minded  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 48

---

The subtle strands of beguiling magic leave little impression on your mind. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects, and whenever you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion effect, you get a critical success instead.
