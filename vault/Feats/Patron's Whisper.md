# Patron's Whisper  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 63

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

---

You gain the ability to speak to your charge over great distances. You gain [[Spells/Message (Player Core)|_message_]] as an innate cantrip of your former patron’s tradition. You can send a message to a creature regardless of range if they have ever been your charge. If you [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] as part of the message, the target receives a –2 circumstance penalty to their [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] DC and to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] checks to reveal the lie.
