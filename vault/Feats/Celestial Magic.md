# Celestial Magic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Angelkin (Player Core)|Angelkin]], [[Feats/Lawbringer (Player Core)|Lawbringer]], [[Feats/Musetouched (Player Core)|Musetouched]], or or another lineage feat associated with [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestials]]

---

You possess celestial magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[Spells/Clear Mind|_clear mind_]], [[Spells/Everlight|_everlight_]], [[Spells/Humanoid Form (Player Core)|_humanoid form_]], [[Spells/Revealing Light|_revealing light_]], [[Spells/Share Life|_share life_]], or [[Spells/Sure Footing|_sure footing_]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine [[Rules/Innate Spells|innate spells]]. [[Feats/Angelkin]] typically take _clear mind_ and _humanoid form_, [[Feats/Lawbringer|lawbringers]] typically have _everlight_ and _share life_, and [[Feats/Musetouched (Player Core)|musetouched]] typically have _revealing light_ and _sure footing_.
