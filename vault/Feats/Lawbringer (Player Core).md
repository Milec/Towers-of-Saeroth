# Lawbringer  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 79

---

You trace your lineage to [[Bestiary/Families/Archon|archons]], guardians of the seventiered mountain of [[Setting/Planes/Heaven]] and nurturers of law and virtue within mortals. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects, and if you roll a success on a save against an emotion effect, you get a critical success instead.
