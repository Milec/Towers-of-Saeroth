# Creative Prodigy  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 53

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Proteankin]]

---

You have a powerful creative drive that is clear to any who interact with you. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. If you were already trained in Performance (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Impressive Performance (Player Core)|Impressive Performance]] skill feat, and when you use Performance to Make an Impression, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the check.
