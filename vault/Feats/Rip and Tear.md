# Rip and Tear single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 56

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal|animal instinct]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Untamed|untamed order]]

**Requirements**
Your last action dealt piercing or slashing damage using an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] Strike granted by a [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|morph]] or [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect.

---

You've learned to debilitate your enemies in the same way a hunter tears at their prey. Make an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] Strike against the same target. If the Strike hits and deals piercing or slashing damage, the target takes an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. If you're at least 12th level, increase this to 2d6 persistent bleed damage.
