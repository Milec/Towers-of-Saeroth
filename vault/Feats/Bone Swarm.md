# Bone Swarm two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Skeleton]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 57

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You scatter your body and transform into a whirling storm of bones for up to 1 minute or until you spend a single action to return to your normal shape. You become Huge, gain the [[Traits/Swarm|swarm]] trait, and gain a fly Speed of 40 feet. As a swarm, you have the following characteristics.

- You are immune to the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grappled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] conditions.
- You have weakness 5 to area and [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage.
- You can occupy the same space as other creatures and must do so to use your damaging ability.
- As a 2-action activity, you can deal 10d6 bludgeoning damage to all creatures sharing your space ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save with a DC equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher).
- You can't speak, Cast Spells, use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions requiring your hands, Activate magic items, or make any Strikes with your normal body.
- You don't gain the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swarm Mind|swarm mind]] ability, so you are still affected normally by [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. You also retain the benefits of skeleton feats that don't require communication or the use of hands.
