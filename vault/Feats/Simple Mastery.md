Other casters’ never-ending hunger for new magic prevents them from truly understanding just how far simple magic can be pushed when truly mastered. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a spell]] you prepared from your keepsake, you Cast the Spell as though it is 1 rank higher, up to the highest rank spell you can cast.
