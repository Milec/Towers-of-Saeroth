# Eternal Elixir  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Extend Elixir]]

---

Your body readily accepts and retains minor changes. When you drink one of your alchemical items that has the [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] and [[Traits/Infused|infused]] traits and a duration of 1 minute or more, you can make the elixir’s duration indefinite. You can do so only if the elixir’s level is half your level or lower. If you later consume a different elixir and make it indefinite, the effect of the previous indefinite elixir ends.
