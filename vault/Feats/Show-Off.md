# Show-Off free action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
You succeed at an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check deemed suitably challenging and meaningful by the GM.

---

As is the tradition with many Firebrands, you call your shot and look to impress with an extra bit of panache or a greater stunt. Attempt the triggering check again, using the second result. On a success, you gain the normal results of the check, plus you feel invigorated by your stunt and gain temporary Hit Points equal to your level (double your level on a critical success). You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute. Typically, a challenging task is one with at least a [[Rules/Level-Based DCs|standard DC for your level]], though the difficulty may be higher depending on the situation. A meaningful context is one where the action's success or failure is relevant to the pursuit of your goals.
