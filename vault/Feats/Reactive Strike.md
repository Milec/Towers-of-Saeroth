# Reactive Strike ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Guardian]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Marshal]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]] (Level 8)

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach uses a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action or a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, makes a ranged attack, or leaves a square during a move action it’s using.

---

You lash out at a foe that leaves an opening. Make a melee Strike against the triggering creature. If your attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a manipulate action, you disrupt that action. This Strike doesn't count toward your multiple attack penalty, and your multiple attack penalty doesn't apply to this Strike.
