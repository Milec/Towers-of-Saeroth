# Unbreakable Bond  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mammoth Lord Dedication]]

---

You and your megafauna companion are bound together; as long as you live, it's much harder for your companion to die. If your megafauna companion gains the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition, it automatically succeeds at the recovery check to stabilize at 0 Hit Points on its turn. Additionally, your megafauna companion only dies at dying 5 rather than dying 4. Finally, the first time each day your megafauna companion would reach dying 5 and die, as long as you're still alive, your companion's dying value decreases to 0 instead.
