# Fortified Elixirs  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 66

---

Your treatments for [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] can be very efficacious, though they tend not to last as long. A creature that is under the effect of an [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] [[Equipment/Antidote (Player Core 2)|antidote]] or [[Equipment/Antiplague (Player Core 2)|antiplague]] that you created can choose to reroll a failed Fortitude saving throw against an appropriate affliction (poison for antidote and disease for antiplague). The creature doesn't gain the elixir's usual item bonus to the reroll, and after the reroll, the elixir's duration immediately ends. This is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect.
