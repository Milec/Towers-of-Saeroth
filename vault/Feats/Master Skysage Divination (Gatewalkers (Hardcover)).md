# Master Skysage Divination  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 239

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Expert Skysage Divination (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Expert Skysage Divination]]

---

You unlock the most powerful secrets hidden in the stars above. You choose one of the following 7th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Retrocognition (Player Core)|_retrocognition_]] or [[Spells/True Target (Player Core)|_true target_]]. At 18th level, add [[Spells/Pinpoint|_pinpoint_]] to your spell repertoire, and at 20th level, add [[Spells/Foresight (Player Core)|_foresight_]] to your spell repertoire. You gain master proficiency with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
