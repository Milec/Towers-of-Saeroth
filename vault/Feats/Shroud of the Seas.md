You've learned to camouflage yourself among the shadows and currents of the ocean so you're hard to see from above. When you're fully underwater, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to all opponents who are out of the water, and [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to those who are out of the water and more than 10 feet away from you.
