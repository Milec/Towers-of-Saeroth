# Otherworldly Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 48

---

Your elven magic manifests as a simple arcane spell, even if you aren't formally trained in magic. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] spell list. You can cast this cantrip as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
