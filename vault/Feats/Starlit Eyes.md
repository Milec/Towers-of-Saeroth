# Starlit Eyes  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 44

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Starlit Span|starlit span hybrid study]]

---

Starlight shines in your sight, enhancing your perception and range. When you make a ranged Strike while in Arcane Cascade stance, you lower the DC of your flat check to target a creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from you. The DC is reduced to 3 instead of 5 against a concealed creature and to 9 instead of 11 against a hidden one. When you cast [[Spells/Shooting Star|_shooting star_]] and target a hidden creature, you don't have to attempt the flat check for targeting a hidden creature with a ranged Strike.
