# Magic Rider  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 43

---

Your people used powerful magic to travel between distant worlds, and the remnants of that magic make such transportation easier for you. When you are the target of a [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] spell that transports more than one person, it can affect an additional person beyond the normal limit, chosen by the caster. Additionally, when you're the target of a [[Spells/Teleport|_teleport_]] spell, you and the other targets arrive no farther than 1 mile off target, regardless of distance traveled.
