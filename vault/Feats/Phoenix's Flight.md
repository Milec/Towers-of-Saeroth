# Phoenix's Flight  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 117

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're adjacent to or sharing the same space as your familiar.

---

You and your familiar learn the secrets of elemental fire, allowing you to merge together to become a legendary creature—a [[Bestiary/Phoenix (Monster Core)|phoenix]]. You can cast monstrosity form as an innate [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spell once per day, except you can transform only into a phoenix, and your familiar must be adjacent to you before you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]]. When you Cast the Spell, your familiar merges into your form. While transformed, you gain the Blazing Conflagration action.  

**Activate** three actions Blazing Conflagration;    **Requirements** You’re transformed into phoenix form by the monstrosity form spell granted by this feat; **Effect** ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) You dismiss phoenix form while a fiery corona erupts from you as searing light. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] takes 16d6 fire damage with a basic Fortitude save against your [[Rules/Spell DC|spell DC]]; creatures that critically fail are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round. You gain 8d6 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]]. At 18th level and again at 20th level, the burst deals an additional 2d6 fire damage, and you gain an additional 1d6 temporary Hit Points.
