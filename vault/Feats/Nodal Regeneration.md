# Nodal Regeneration  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 51

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nodal Healing]]

---

Your body processes magic so effectively that you can induce true regeneration, replacing limbs and restoring organs. You can cast [[Spells/Regenerate (Player Core)|_regenerate_]] once per day as an innate spell. Instead of being deactivated by acid or fire, your regeneration from the spell is deactivated by [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], which slows your metabolism. You can target only yourself with the spell, but you can Cast the Spell as a reaction if you're reduced to 0 Hit Points by a non-cold effect.
