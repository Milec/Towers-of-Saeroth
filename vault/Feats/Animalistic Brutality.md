# Animalistic Brutality single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal (Player Core 2)|animal instinct]]

---

You deepen the connection into your animal instincts. Your unarmed attack from bestial rage gains one of the following traits until you stop raging: [[Traits/Backswing (Player Core)|backswing]], [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]], [[Traits/Razing (Player Core 2)|razing]], or [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]]. You can't use this ability again until the next time you Rage. You can choose a different trait each time you use Animalistic Brutality
