Your taunts and threats earn your foes' ire. When you successfully [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature, its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition can't decrease to less than 1 at the end of its turn until it either uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against you or can no longer observe or sense you for at least 1 round.
