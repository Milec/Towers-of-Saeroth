# Draconic Scent  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 168

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Instinct of the Wyrm|Dragon Disciple]]

**Prerequisites**

---

Your sense of smell is uncanny, much like a dragon's. You gain [[Rules/Scent|imprecise scent]] with a range of 30 feet. The GM might double the range if you're downwind from the creature or halve the range if you're upwind, at their discretion.
