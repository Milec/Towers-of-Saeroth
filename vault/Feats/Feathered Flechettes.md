# Feathered Flechettes two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Winged Warrior]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Winged Warrior Dedication]]

---

Unfurling your wings in a quick snap, you send feathers scattering in all directions. Creatures in a 15-foot emanation take 6d6 slashing damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC). This increases to 10d6 if your unarmed attacks have a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_greater striking_]] rune, such as by etching it onto [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|_handwraps of mighty blows_]], and 16d6 if they have a _major striking_ rune.
