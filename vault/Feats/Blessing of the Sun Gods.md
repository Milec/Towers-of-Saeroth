# Blessing of the Sun Gods  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -172- Secrets of the Temple City|Pathfinder #172: Secrets of the Temple City]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bright Lion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bright Lion Dedication]]

---

The [[Setting/Deity Categories/Old Sun Gods]] share their power with you. Select the [[Magic/Domains/Ambition|ambition]], [[Magic/Domains/Cities|cities]], [[Magic/Domains/Darkness|darkness]], [[Magic/Domains/Dreams|dreams]], [[Magic/Domains/Family|family]], [[Magic/Domains/Fire|fire]], [[Magic/Domains/Freedom|freedom]], [[Magic/Domains/Healing|healing]], [[Magic/Domains/Moon|moon]], [[Magic/Domains/Passion|passion]], or [[Magic/Domains/Sun|sun]] domain. You gain an initial domain spell for that domain. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can regain using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity to pray to the Old Sun Gods; if you already have a focus pool, increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. Your Bright Lion focus spells are divine spells; you become trained in divine spell attacks and spell DCs, using Charisma as your spellcasting ability score.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, selecting a different domain each time and gaining its domain spell.
