# Hungry Eyes  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hungerseed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 36

---

Your eyes can see through darkness with an oni’s visual acuity. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision (Monster Core)|low-light vision]], or you gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]] if you already have low-light vision.

**Special** You can select this feat twice, once to gain low-light vision and a second time to gain darkvision.
