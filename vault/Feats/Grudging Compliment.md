# Grudging Compliment ⤾  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
An enemy within 30 feet damages you or succeeds at a skill check.

---

Every Firebrand can recognize the skill of others, but you've turned it into an art you can wield in combat. You compliment a foe's form or technique, and your unexpected praise distracts them from their surroundings. Attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check against that enemy's Will DC. Regardless of your result, they are temporarily immune to your Grudging Compliments for 10 minutes.

**Critical Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round.  

**Success** The target becomes flat-footed against your attacks for 1 round.
