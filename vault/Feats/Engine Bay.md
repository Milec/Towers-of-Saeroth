# Engine Bay  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 56

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Unions|Vehicle Mechanic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vehicle Mechanic Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You are practiced at working in vast garages of vehicles and can work on many machines at a time. When [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repairing]] and spending the full 10 minutes, you can fix up to two objects. If you are a master in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], you can fix up to four targets. If you’re legendary in Crafting, you can fix up to eight targets. This feat has no effect when using [[Feats/Quick Repair (Player Core)|Quick Repair]] or another ability to reduce the time spent Repairing.
