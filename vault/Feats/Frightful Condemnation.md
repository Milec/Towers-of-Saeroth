# Frightful Condemnation free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Vigilante]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Harsh Judgment]]; [[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

**Trigger**
You hit your condemned foe with a melee Strike in the same turn during which you condemned it with [[Feats/Harsh Judgment|Harsh Judgment.]]

**Requirements**
You have condemned a foe to death with
Harsh Judgment.

---

Your willingness to follow through on your pitiless judgment is frightful to the person you have condemned, and they begin to dread what you will do to them next. Your condemned foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 1, or [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 2 if the triggering Strike was a critical hit.
