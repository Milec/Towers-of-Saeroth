# Agile Maneuvers  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 166

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

Your easily maneuver against your foes. Your [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] actions have a lower multiple attack penalty. Even if your weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack doesn't have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] trait, the penalty is –4 if the action is your second attack on your turn, or –8 if it's your third or subsequent attack. If your weapon or unarmed attack is agile and you have panache, the penalty is reduced further, to –3 if it's the second attack on your turn or –6 if it's the third or subsequent.
