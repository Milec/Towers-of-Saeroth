# Hybrid Study Spell  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Magus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magus Dedication]]

---

Gain the conflux spell from a [hybrid study](HybridStudies.aspx) of your choice. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can [[Actions/Refocus]] by studying your [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank)|spellbook]] and performing a physical regimen. You don't gain any of the hybrid study's other benefits.
