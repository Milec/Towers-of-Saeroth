# Reincarnated Ridiculer  *Feat 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 319

---

You’ve practiced the art of taunting an enemy, ensuring they know death will only make you stronger. When you [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] an opponent, the target doesn’t become temporarily immune to your attempts to Demoralize it unless you critically fail the check. If the target critically hit you with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] on its previous turn, the DC for this check is reduced by 4.
