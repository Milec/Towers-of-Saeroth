# Clobber two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 49

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium or heavy armor.

---

With a mighty blow, you send an enemy flying backward and onto the ground. Strike a foe. This Strike deals an extra die of weapon damage. If you hit and deal damage, you can automatically [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] the target, with the same benefits as the Shove action (including the critical success effect, if your Strike was a critical hit). The target then falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. This counts as two attacks when calculating your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]].
