# Mortal Healing  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]] (Level 2)

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]; you follow the Laws of Mortality

---

You grant greater healing when the gods don't interfere. When you roll a success to [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]] for a creature that hasn't regained Hit Points from divine magic in the past 24 hours, you get a critical success on your check instead and restore the corresponding amount of Hit Points.
