# Slippery Prey  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 234

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

You're able to escape bonds more easily than others. When you attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] using Acrobatics or Athletics, you reduce the multiple attack penalty for repeated attempts to –4 and –8 if you're trained in the skill. The penalty becomes –3 and –6 if you're a master in the appropriate skill. If you're legendary in the skill, you don't take penalties for multiple Escape attempts in the same turn. Regardless of your training, your attempts to Escape still have the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] trait and incur a multiple attack penalty with other actions.
