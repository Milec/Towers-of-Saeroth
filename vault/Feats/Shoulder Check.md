# Shoulder Check single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 42

---

You hit a foe with your armor to throw them off balance. Make a fist Strike even if you don't have a hand free; if you're  wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Gauntlet (Player Core)|gauntlet]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet (Player Core)|spiked gauntlet]], you can make a Strike with one of those weapons instead. The Strike gains the following additional results.  
**Critical Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against melee attacks you attempt against it until the end of your next turn.  
**Success** The target is off-guard against the next melee attack you attempt against it before the end of your current turn.  
**Critical Failure** You are off-guard against melee attacks the target attempts against you until the end of your next turn.
