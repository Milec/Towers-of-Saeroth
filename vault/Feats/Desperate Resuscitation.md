# Desperate Resuscitation three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 34

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Officer's Medical Training]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

**Requirements**
You are holding a [[Equipment/Healer's Toolkit|healer's toolkit]] or are wearing one and have a free hand; the target's body is mostly intact; the target was not killed by a death effect.

---

You can use your training in combat medicine to revive the recently deceased. Attempt a DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check to revive a dead creature that has been dead for no more than 3 rounds. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of raise dead, except it still has the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition it had before dying, increased by 1 (or wounded 1 if it wasn't wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is temporarily immune to Desperate Resuscitation for 1 day.
