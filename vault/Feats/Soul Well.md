# Soul Well two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

Your brush your fingers across your [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality_]], momentarily creating a metaphysical vortex from which mortal spirits struggle to escape. For the next minute, [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] treat all squares within 30 feet of you as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and living creatures within the same area die from the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition at dying 5 rather than dying 4. As it comes close to tasting a living soul, the _splinter of finality_ is empowered. If a living creature’s dying value increases while within your Soul Well, your _spectral dagger_ deals one additional weapon die of damage until the end of your next turn.
