# Brains! free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zombie]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Feast]]

**Trigger**
You Feast upon a [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid's]] brain. The humanoid can't be [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] (or, if you're Feasting on a corpse, can't have been mindless while alive).

---

You consume the brains of your foe, causing synapses long rotten to fire and enabling you to shake off your mental torpor. You gain a +1 status bonus on skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] for 1 hour. If the humanoid had a higher modifier to the skill check you're using to Recall Knowledge than you do, the status bonus increases to +2.
