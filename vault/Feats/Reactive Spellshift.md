# Reactive Spellshift ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 103

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellshifter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellshifter Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature within 30 feet casts a spell.

**Requirements**
You are holding your conduit.

---

You can reflexively shift a spell even as it’s being cast. You attempt to Shift the triggering Spell even if you do not meet the other requirements to Shift Spell. On a success, the spellshift applies to the spell’s effects as appropriate for the chosen spellshift. After you choose your spellshift, but before you attempt your [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] check, the caster can voluntarily let their spell be [[Rules/Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupted]]. Regardless of the outcome, the caster then becomes immune to your Reactive Spellshift for 10 minutes.
