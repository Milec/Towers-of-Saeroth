# Spirit Spells  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Hunter Dedication]]

---

Your ability to confront incorporeal menaces develops into full-fledged spellcasting. Choose a 1st-level occult spell. You can Cast this Spell as an innate occult spell. At 6th level, you gain a 2nd-level occult spell, and at 8th level, you gain a 3rd-level occult spell. Each of these spells must have the [[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], or [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] trait. You can cast each of these spells once per day.
