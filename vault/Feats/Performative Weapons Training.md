You're skilled with flashy weapons. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Dueling Cape|dueling cape]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain|spiked chain]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sword Cane|sword cane]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip|whip]]. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the weapons listed.
