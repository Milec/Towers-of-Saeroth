# Remember thy Names two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 124

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

---

You peer into the eyes of a target of your [Ultimatum of Liberation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2993), as you say aloud the names of their victims, searing them into the oppressor’s mind, cursing them to feel their victims’ pain. The target takes 12d6 mental damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher). If the target fails or critically fails this check, they also take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]]. For the next hour, when the target stops taking this persistent damage, you or one of your allies can mention a victim’s name as a single action to the target, forcing them to succeed at another Will save or begin taking the persistent damage again. This is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] effect. Once you use Remember Their Names on a target, the target is immune to it for 24 hours.
