# Ever-Vigilant Senses  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

---

Your eidolon has enhanced senses. They gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] and can’t be [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanked]] except by creatures that are higher level than them. They can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures and objects, which appear translucent and are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]]. When your eidolon moves within 30 feet of an illusion that can be disbelieved, the GM rolls a secret check to disbelieve it, even if your eidolon didn’t spend an action to [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]].
