# Improbable Elixirs  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 81

---

Your mastery of alchemical secrets enables you to replicate effects most believe can be achieved only via magic. Select a number of [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|potions]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1); these potions must be of 9th level or lower. You gain formulas to create these potions as alchemical items with the [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] trait. When making these alchemical elixirs, you can substitute alchemical reagents for an equal value of magical components, and you can use [[Equipment/Alchemist's Tools|alchemist's tools]] (for [[Feats/Quick Alchemy]]) or an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Lab|alchemist's lab]] (for the [[Actions/Craft]] activity) instead of any other required tool kits. Other than that, the formula does not change. Once you've chosen the potion formulas, they can't be changed.
