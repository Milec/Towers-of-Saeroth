# Dispelling Slice two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 189

---

Your sneak attack slices through the threads binding magic to a target. Make a Strike against a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature. If your Strike deals sneak attack damage, you attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a single spell active on the target (your choice). Your counteract level is equal to half your level (rounded up), and your counteract check modifier is equal to your class DC – 10.
