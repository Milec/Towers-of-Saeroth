# Smoke Curtain two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 115

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a loaded firearm and are wearing or holding a dose of black powder.

---

You load an extra dose of powder into your shot, causing it to belch a cloud of smoke. You make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with your firearm and create a cloud of smoke in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] centered on your location. Creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] while within the smoke, and creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within the smoke. The smoke dissipates at the start of your next turn. If your Strike is a critical failure, your firearm misfires.
