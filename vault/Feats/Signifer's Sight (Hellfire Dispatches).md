# Signifer's Sight  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 26

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Preferment]]

---

The magic flowing through your signifer's mask allows you to see in the dark, and the mask dampens the distraction caused by sudden flashes of bright light. While wearing your signifer's mask, you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]; if you already have darkvision, you instead gain greater darkvision. Additionally, if you're [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] while wearing your signifer's mask and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] only because you're dazzled, you reduce the flat check DC to target that creature from 5 to 3.
