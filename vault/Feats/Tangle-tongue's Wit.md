# Tangle-tongue's Wit  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

---

Your talent for language is a blessing from the yaksha makers of old. You learn two new languages, chosen from languages you have access to. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your saving throws against [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] effects.
