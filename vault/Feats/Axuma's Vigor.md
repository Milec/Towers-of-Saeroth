# Axuma's Vigor  *Feat 11*

> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 71

Exposure to the Well of Axuma

Your vigor grants anyone using [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks to [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] to you, or use any of the Treat actions on you, a +2 circumstance bonus to their check. During the [[Actions/Long-Term Rest]] activity, you heal at twice the normal rate.
