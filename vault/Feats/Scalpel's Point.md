# Scalpel's Point  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 62

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Forensic Medicine|forensic medicine methodology]]

---

Your stratagems benefit from your precise knowledge of anatomy. When you critically hit with an attack on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem|Devising a Stratagem]], if your attack dealt piercing or slashing damage, you also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to your target.
