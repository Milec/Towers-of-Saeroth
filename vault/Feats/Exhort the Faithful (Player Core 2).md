# Exhort the Faithful  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 230

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]; follower of a specific religion

---

Your knowledge of the tenets of your faith gives you insight into the best ways to get others of your faith to help you or to follow your directions. When you [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] something of or [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] members of your own faith, you can attempt a Religion check instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check. On a critically failed attempt to make a Request, the target's attitude toward you doesn't worsen.
