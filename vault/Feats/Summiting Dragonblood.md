# Summiting Dragonblood  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 216

---

Thanks to your draconic blood, there are no mountains or hills you can’t surmount. You gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 20 feet; if you already have a base climb Speed, it increases by 5 feet. You also gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber (Player Core)|Combat Climber]] skill feat, even if you aren’t trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]. If you choose a draconic exemplar, you must choose a dragon with a climb Speed.
