# Lingering Echoes  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 80

---

Not everyone is reincarnated, and you feel a strong connection with the spiritual echoes of those who didn't receive the same opportunity as you. You gain [[Spells/Talking Corpse (Player Core)|_talking corpse_]] as an occult innate spell that you can cast once per day. You can ask a fourth question of a corpse when you cast this spell. The corpse attempts its Will save against your spell DC or your class DC, whichever is higher.
