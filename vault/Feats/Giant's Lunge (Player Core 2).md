# Giant's Lunge single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Giant (Player Core 2)|Giant instinct]]

---

You extend your body and prepare to attack foes outside your normal reach. Until your rage ends, all your melee weapons and unarmed attacks gain a [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] of 10 feet. This doesn't increase the reach of any weapon or unarmed attack that already has the reach trait, but it does combine with abilities that increase your reach due to increased size, such as [[Feats/Giant's Stature]].
