# Web Hunter  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Anadi]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 104

---

Through the careful cultivation of highly sensitive hairs, you've developed the ability to sense creatures without seeing them. You gain imprecise [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] at a range of 15 feet. When you and a creature are both touching the same anchored, threadlike object (such as a rope, webbing, or wire), your imprecise tremorsense can sense that creature at a range of 60 feet.
