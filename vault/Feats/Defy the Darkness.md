# Defy the Darkness  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 42

**Prerequisites**
[[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]

---

Using ancient dwarven methods developed to fight enemies wielding magical darkness, you've honed your darkvision and sworn not to use such magic yourself. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], enabling you to see through magical darkness even if it normally hampers darkvision (such as the darkness created by a 4th-level [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]] spell). You can't cast spells with the [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] trait, use item activations with the darkness trait, or use any other ability with the darkness trait.
