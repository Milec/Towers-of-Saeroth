# Terrifying Invocation single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

---

You spit and roar as you pronounce your rune's terrible name. You attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a single target within 30 feet and then [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke one Rune]] upon that target. You don't take a penalty to your check if the creature doesn't understand your language.
