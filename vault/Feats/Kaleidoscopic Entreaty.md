# Kaleidoscopic Entreaty single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 293

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Emissary]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Emissary Dedication]]

---

If your next action is to cast entreat spirit, you can cause that spirit to unleash a brilliant display of color, light, and sound in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] centered on you. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 1 minute.
