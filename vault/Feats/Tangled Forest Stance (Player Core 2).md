# Tangled Forest Stance single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 123

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

You extend your arms like gnarled branches to interfere with your foes' movements. You can make lashing branch unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 slashing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

 While you're in Tangled Forest Stance and can act, every enemy in your reach that tries to move away from you must succeed at a Reflex save, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check, or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for that action. If you prefer, you can allow the enemy to move.
