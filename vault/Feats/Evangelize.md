# Evangelize single action  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 105

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) For the purposes of Society play, remove the final clause of Evangelize that states “at the GM’s discretion, a target that genuinely changes its perspective to support your faith as a result of the argument is also otherwise unaffected.”_

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Steel Falcon]] (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]; you follow a deity or philosophy

---

You point out a detail that incontrovertibly supports your faith, causing a listener’s mind to whirl. Attempt a Diplomacy check and compare the result to the Will DC of a single target that can hear you and understands your language; that target is then temporarily immune to Evangelize with respect to your deity or philosophy for 1 day. A creature that already agrees with you is unaffected, and at the GM’s discretion, a target that genuinely changes its perspective to support your faith as a result of the argument is also otherwise unaffected.

**Critical Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 round.  

**Success** The target is stupefied 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.
