You can parry attacks against you with your Aldori dueling  sword. [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling swords]] you're wielding gain the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait. If you have your other hand or hands free while wielding an Aldori dueling sword, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC while parrying instead of the usual +1.
