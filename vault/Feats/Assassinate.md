# Assassinate two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 158

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Assassin Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have designated a mark using [[Feats/Assassin Dedication|Mark for Assassination]] and are completely [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed|unnoticed]] by your mark.

---

You strike with one swift movement, trying to instantly slay your mark. Make a Strike against your mark. If you hit, your mark takes 6d6 extra precision damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. If the mark critically fails, they die. This is an [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] effect. The creature then becomes temporarily immune to your Assassinate for 1 day.
