# Stay Here! two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marine Marauder Dedication]]

---

You topple your foes with your legs and attack them while they’re down. You [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] a creature within reach without needing a free hand. If you succeed, you can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against that creature. Your multiple attack penalty doesn’t increase until after you’ve made both the Trip and Strike.
