# Call of Elysium  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 10

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Musetouched]]

---

The wild wonder of [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]] harmonizes within you, and in dire straits, you can share its benefits with your allies. Your Speed increases by 5 feet; the Speed increase from this feat isn't cumulative with any Speed increase from your ancestry feats (such as [[Feats/Nimble Elf]]). Allies who begin their turn in combat adjacent to you gain a +5-foot status bonus to their Speed for the round.
