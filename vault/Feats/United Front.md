# United Front  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 124

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

---

Thanks to your powerful speeches, your movement finds allies in all sectors of society and even across enemy lines, all eager to help you accomplish your goals. If you spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] when you use [[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication|Ultimatum of Liberation]], you can ask four questions related to that creature, as if you succeeded at four [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] checks. This information comes to you over the course of the next 1 to 2 hours, representing tidbits of information you glean from local supporters. After receiving this information, the first time you roll [[Rules/Initiative (Player Core)|Initiative]] in an encounter with the target of your Ultimatum of Liberation or their allies, you and all your allies can make your Initiative check with mythic proficiency.
