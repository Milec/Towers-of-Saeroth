# Grippli Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 119

---

You are well versed in grippli culture and tactics. You gain the trained proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Traits/Grippli|Grippli Lore]].
