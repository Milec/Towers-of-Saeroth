# Yamaraj's Grandeur two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 31

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You have been granted a sliver of the power of the [[Bestiary/Yamaraj|yamaraj]], the magistrates of the dead who agreed to allow duskwalkers to exist at all. You exhale a blast of icy wind and ravenous insects. This is a 30-foot cone that deals 14d6 slashing damage and 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent slashing damage]] (basic Reflex save using your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher). On a critical failure, targets also become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 2]].
