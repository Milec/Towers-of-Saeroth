# Thrash single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 81

**Requirements**
You have an enemy [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You thrash your foe around. An enemy you have [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier plus your weapon specialization damage plus your [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC.
