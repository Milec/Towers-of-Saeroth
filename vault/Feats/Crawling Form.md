# Crawling Form  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 85

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Born of Animal]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Born of Celestial]] heritage

---

You can transform into a small [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] or [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] creature to scurry and spy. You can Change Shape to assume a crawling form. While in crawling form, you gain the effect of a 1st-rank [[Spells/Pest Form (Player Core)|_pest form_]], except you can transform only into a shape matching your heritage; for example, if you were once were a wolf, you turn into a wolf puppy.
