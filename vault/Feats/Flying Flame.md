# Flying Flame two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 28

---

A Tiny shape of flame appears, taking a form of your choice—such as a bird, an arrow, or a simple sphere. It Flies from you up to 30 feet in a path you choose. Each creature it passes through takes 1d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature attempts only one save, even if the flame passes through it multiple times.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
