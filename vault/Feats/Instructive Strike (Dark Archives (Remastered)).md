# Instructive Strike single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Thaumaturge (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 44

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Vindicator]] (Level 4)

---

You attack your foe and analyze how it reacts. Make a Strike. On a hit, you can immediately attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about the target. On a critical hit, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check to Recall Knowledge.
