# One-Toed Hop single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 27

---

Assuming a peculiar stance, you make a short hop on each toe. You make a vertical [[Actions/Leap]] without triggering reactions that can be triggered by [[Traits/Move|move]] actions or upon leaving or entering a square.
