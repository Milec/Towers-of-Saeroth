# Crowd Mastery  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lion Blade (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lost in the Crowd (Shining Kingdoms)|Lost in the Crowd]]

---

You use the crowd to your advantage, diverting your foes’ attention to potential hidden dangers in the crowd. When determining if your foe is [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanked]], treat all squares occupied by a crowd as if they were occupied by an ally with a melee reach of 5 feet.
