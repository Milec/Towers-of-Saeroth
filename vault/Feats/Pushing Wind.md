# Pushing Wind  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a fan.

---

As you spin and glide your fans alongside your allies, you kick up a mild wind that gently carries you all forward. So long as you’re holding a fan, you and allies who start their turn in a 30- foot aura [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanating]] around you gain a +5-foot circumstance bonus to land Speed for 1 round; you also gain this bonus to your [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] if you possess one, but it can’t grant you the ability to fly if you wouldn’t otherwise be able to.

Additionally, the air impedes the movements of your foes. While holding a fan, the area in a 10-foot aura emanating around you is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for all enemies.
