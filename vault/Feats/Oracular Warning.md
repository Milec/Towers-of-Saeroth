# Oracular Warning free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 80

**Trigger**
You are about to roll initiative.

---

You have a flash of insight about impending danger related to one ally, and you can shout or gesture to warn that ally of the threat a moment before it materializes. One ally of your choice can roll twice for initiative and use the better result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. If you are legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]], you receive two visions and can warn two allies, granting them both this benefit.

 You are momentarily thrown off by the divine insight, however, and you must roll twice for your initiative roll and use the worse result. This is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect. The two effects are tied together; if you would avoid the misfortune effect for any reason, or if any of your allies would negate their fortune effect, your Oracular Warning does nothing. Depending on whether you use gestures or call out, this action gains either the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] or [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait, respectively.
