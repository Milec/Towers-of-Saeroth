# Mutant Physique  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 67

---

Basic mutagens that affect your physical form can bring out the beast within you, turn you nigh invincible, or make your body more elastic. While you're affected by one of the listed mutagens, you get an additional benefit.

- **[[Equipment/Bestial Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Bestial Mutagen]]** You gain the mutagen's item bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks. In addition, you [[Rules/Increasing Die Size|increase the damage die size]] of your claws and jaws by one step, and they gain the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]] trait.
- **[[Equipment/Juggernaut Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Juggernaut Mutagen]]** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to all physical damage equal to half your level.
- **[[Equipment/Quicksilver Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Quicksilver Mutagen]]** You can stretch your legs and Step up to 10 feet, and you can squish and compress your body, allowing you to make it through tight spaces as if you were one size smaller, in addition to any effect from [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeezing]].

**Special** If you can be under the effects of multiple mutagens (with the mutagenist greater field discovery, for example), you get all relevant benefits.
