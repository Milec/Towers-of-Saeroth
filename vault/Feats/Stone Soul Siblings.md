Your connection with nature's spirits has progressed to the point where you can even have casual conversation with pebbles and boulders. You gain [[Spells/Stone Tell|_stone tell_]] as a primal innate spell that you can cast once per day. You get a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]] from stones.
