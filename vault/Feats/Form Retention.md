# Form Retention  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 142

---

You have trained your mind and body to tolerate [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] magic for longer periods of time, so long as you prepare for the change appropriately. When preparing a polymorph spell that lasts 1 minute and grants a battle form, you can prepare the spell in a slot 2 levels higher than normal. This doesn't grant any of the normal benefits of heightening a spell, but the spell lasts up to 10 minutes. For example, if you prepared [[Spells/Animal Form|_animal form_]] in a 4th-level slot with Form Retention, you would cast a 2nd-level _animal form_ that lasts for up to 10 minutes. If the spell can be Dismissed, that doesn't change.
