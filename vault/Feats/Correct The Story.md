# Correct The Story ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|Misfortune]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 82

**Trigger**
An enemy critically succeeds at an attack roll or saving throw.

---

You know the story of your journey well and can correct it when your enemy tells it incorrectly. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. The enemy must reroll the triggering [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] roll or save and take the new result.
