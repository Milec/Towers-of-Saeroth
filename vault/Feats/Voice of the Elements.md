# Voice of the Elements  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kineticist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 21

---

You can speak with the secret tones of elements you channel, finding words in the crackle of flame, the grinding of stone. Your kinetic aura grants you these three benefits while active.

- You can speak all the languages of your kinetic elements ([[Setting/Languages/Sussuran]] for air, [[Setting/Languages/Petran]] for earth, [[Setting/Languages/Pyric]] for fire, [[Setting/Languages/Talican]] for metal, [[Setting/Languages/Thalassic]] for water, [[Setting/Languages/Muan]] for wood).
- You can communicate with [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] [[Traits/Elemental|elementals]] on a basic level if they have a trait that matches one of your kinetic elements or are made of one of those elements. This allows you to use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on them and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]].
- You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Charisma-based skill checks you attempt against elementals of one of your kinetic elements.
