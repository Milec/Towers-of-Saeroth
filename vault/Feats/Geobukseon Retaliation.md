# Geobukseon Retaliation ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Modification (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Modification]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 111

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Armor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|armor innovation]]

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

**Trigger**
You would take damage.

---

Your armor has a retractable spike-covered shell reminiscent of Hwanggot’s heavily armored geobukseon, or “turtle ships.” The shell unfolds across your body, giving you a +4 circumstance bonus to AC and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the end of your next turn. Spiked chains propelled by small bursts of black powder also erupt from the shell, dealing 3d4 piercing damage plus 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] (basic Reflex save against your class DC) to all creatures in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]].
