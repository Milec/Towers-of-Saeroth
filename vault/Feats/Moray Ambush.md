# Moray Ambush ⤾  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 21

**Trigger**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] and a creature who hasn’t detected you passes within 20 feet of you.

---

Like the moray eels athamaru communities raise and train, you know how to quickly strike from hiding. Stride or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] directly toward the triggering creature and make a melee Strike against it. The target creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to this attack.

**Special** If you’re mounted on an eel, you can have the eel move instead of yourself.
