# Improved Invigorating Elixir  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 67

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Invigorating Elixir]]

---

Choose physical invigoration or mental invigoration, and add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: **Physical Invigoration** [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]]; **Mental Invigoration** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]].

**Special** You can select this feat a second time to choose a different type of invigoration and add its options to those you can choose.
