# Juggernaut Charge two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 45

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium or heavy armor.

---

As you move forward in a rush, you put the weight of your armor behind an attack that can drag a foe with you. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. If you end your movement within melee reach of at least one enemy, you can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against that enemy, then Stride again. If your Strike hit and dealt damage, that enemy is [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|pulled]] with you and is moved the same direction and distance as your second Stride.
