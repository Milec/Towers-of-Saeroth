# Helpful Poppet  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 63

---

You're particularly skilled at helping others with a task. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on checks to [[Actions/Aid]], and you don't give your ally a –1 circumstance penalty to their check if you critically fail your attempt to Aid.
