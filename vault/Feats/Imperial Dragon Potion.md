# Imperial Dragon Potion  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 41

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The Imperial Dragon Potion feat grants the greater version of the energy breath potion._

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]; [[Ancestries/Heritages/Heavenscribe Kobold]] heritage

---

During your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], you can spend 10 minutes to create a variant energy breath potion, which has the [[Ancestries/Kobold (Player Core 2)|kobold]] trait in addition to its normal traits. The variant potion is influenced by imperial [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]] magic, granting it the effect corresponding to your dragon benefactor; the DC for the breath is the higher of your [[Rules/Class DC (Player Core)|class DC]] or [[Rules/Spell DC|spell DC]]. The potion becomes inert if not used by your next daily preparations, so it has no value if sold.  

##

| Dragon | Effect (Save) |
| --- | --- |
| Forest | Insects dealing piercing damage in a 15-foot cone (Reflex) |
| Sea | Water dealing bludgeoning damage in a 30-foot line (Reflex) |
| Sky | Lightning dealing electricity damage in a 30-foot line (Reflex) |
| Sovereign | A psychic roar dealing mental damage in a 15-foot cone (Will) |
| Underworld | Flames that deal fire damage in a 15-foot cone (Reflex) |
