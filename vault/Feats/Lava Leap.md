# Lava Leap two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 36

---

You wreath yourself in molten stone and hurtle toward your enemy. [[Actions/Leap]] up to your Speed. At the end of your Leap, a wave of lava crashes onto all creatures in a 10-foot emanation. Each creature in the area takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 fire damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC.

 The cooling remains of the lava form a temporary protective shell around you, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

---
**Level (+3)** Each type of damage increases by 1d6.
