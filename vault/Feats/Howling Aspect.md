# Howling Aspect single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

Pulling your hair loose into flames and gnashing your teeth into ragged fangs, you howl a vow of ire, and your form surges to meet the demands of your deadly promise. For the next minute, you gain two unarmed attacks. You gain a tusks melee unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling group]], and has the [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. You also gain a flame-hair melee unarmed attack that deals 1d4 fire damage, is in the brawling group, and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], finesse, and unarmed traits.
