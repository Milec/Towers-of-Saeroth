# Trampling Charge three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 193

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]], [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are riding a mount that has a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] using its legs (claw, talons, hoof, etc.).

---

You urge your mount to charge into the fray, trampling all enemies who stand in your path. You command your mount to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to double its Speed (or to [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], if it has the corresponding movement type), moving through the spaces of any foes in your path up to one size smaller than your mount. Your mount deals the damage of one of its melee [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] to each creature whose space you move through, with a basic Reflex save against your mount’s [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC. On a critical failure, the creature also becomes off-guard until the end of your next turn. Roll the damage only once. You can damage a given creature only once during this movement.
