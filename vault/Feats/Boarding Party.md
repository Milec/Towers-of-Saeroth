# Boarding Party  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undersea Privateer Dedication]]

---

You're practiced at scaling ships, and even a wet and slippery exterior won't stand in your way. You can [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] a sea vehicle even if you have one hand occupied. If both your hands are free, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Athletics check. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balancing]] or Climbing on any part of a sea vehicle.
