# Butterfly Blade Dedication  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Butterfly Blade]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in [butterfly swords](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=162)

**Access**
Defeat a member of the Butterfly Blades in a sanctioned bout.

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. Whenever your proficiency rank in any weapon increases to expert or beyond, you also gain that new proficiency rank with [butterfly swords](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=162). You gain access to butterfly swords.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from this archetype.
