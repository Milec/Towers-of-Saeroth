# Awakened Animal Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 24

---

You have taken the time to learn about the process of awakening and the experiences of your fellow awakened animals. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] and [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Awakened Animal Lore]].
