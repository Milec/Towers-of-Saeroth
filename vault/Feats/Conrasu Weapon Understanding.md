# Conrasu Weapon Understanding  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conrasu]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 109

---

You have come to know conrasu weapons as you know yourself. Whenever you critically hit using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspear]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]], or a [[Traits/Conrasu|conrasu]] weapon, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
