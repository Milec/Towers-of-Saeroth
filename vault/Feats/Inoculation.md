# Inoculation  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 207

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

You have practice combating plague, and your patients are less likely to succumb to the same [[Traits/Disease|disease]] again for a time. When you successfully [[Actions/Treat Disease]] on someone and they fully recover from the disease, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against that same disease for 1 week.
