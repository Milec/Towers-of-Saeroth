**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Dedication]]

**Access**
You were executed with a   final blade  

---

You were condemned to death and executed with one of Galt’s infamous _final blades_, where your soul was trapped for years or even decades. You might have been released alongside a host of other spirits when your prison was destroyed after the [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]], or powerful magic or other unique circumstances might have facilitated your escape. Whatever the method, you have unfinished business preventing you from passing on to the afterlife. You gain the Remove Head action.

**Remove Head** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Effect** You [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to remove your head from your ghostly body and hold it in a free hand. While your head is detached, you can use it to look around and over obstacles while remaining behind [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]], although you don’t have line of effect to target creatures unless the effect originates from your head. You can leave your head behind and move your body around independently, but your head gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] condition and your body becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blind]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deaf]], and unable to take any action that has the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait that requires speech or vocalization. You can cause your head to dematerialize and reappear atop your neck as a free action, which occurs automatically if it takes damage while detached or is separated from your body by more than 50 feet.
