# Fast Channel  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|harmful font or healing font]]

---

Divine power is always at your fingertips, swiftly responding to your call. When you cast [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] by spending 2 actions, you can get the effects of the 3-action version instead of the 2-action version.

 You can do this with _harm_ if you have harmful font or _heal_ if you have healing font (or both if you have Versatile Font).
