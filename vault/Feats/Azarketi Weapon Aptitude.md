# Azarketi Weapon Aptitude  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Absalom, City of Lost Omens]] pg. 395, [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 5

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Azarketi Weapon Familiarity]]

---

You become familiar with using your weapons both in and out of water. Whenever you critically hit using an [[Traits/Azarketi|azarketi]] weapon or one of the weapons listed in Azarketi Weapon Familiarity, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization]] effect.
