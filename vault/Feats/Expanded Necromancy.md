# Expanded Necromancy  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

---

You delve into other magical traditions. Add the [[Spells/Grisly Growths (Player Core 2)|_grisly growths_]] spell to your dirge. Add the following spells to your dirge as soon as you gain the ability to cast necromancer spells of that rank: 7th: [[Spells/Execute|_execute_]], 8th: [[Spells/Desiccate|_desiccate_]], 9th: massacre. You can prepare and cast these spells as [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spells.
