# Tragic Lament ⤾  *Feat 18*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 83

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]] (Level 18), [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 18)

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]] or [[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

**Trigger**
A creature you’re aware of critically succeeds on a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against you and would deal damage to you.

---

You dramatically cry out in poetic verse about the tragedies that led to your doom at this very moment, instilling guilt in your attacker. The Strike deals normal damage rather than critical damage as your foe relents on their attack at the last moment as they’re moved by your words, though other effects that happen on a critical hit still occur. The creature that made the triggering Strike must then succeed on a Will save against your Class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round by guilt at its actions against you.
