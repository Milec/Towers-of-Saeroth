# Pact of Infernal Prowess  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 167

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbinder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pactbinder Dedication]]

---

You've sworn a pact with [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devils]], granting you success in life in exchange for your soul in the afterlife. Once per hour, when you critically fail a check, you can reroll the check as a free action; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. Additionally, you can choose to automatically succeed at checks to [[Actions/Earn Income]] for tasks below your level, without rolling.

 However, when you die, your soul is consigned to [[Setting/Planes/Hell]]. You can't be returned to life except by powerful magic such as [[Spells/Miracle|_miracle_]], and even then, the devils responsible for the contract can track your every move for 1 year. The devils gain the effects of a [[Spells/Discern Location|_discern location_]] spell. They can also use 10th-level [[Spells/Scrying|_scrying_]] on you at will, and you automatically critically fail your saving throw.

**Special** You can't retrain out of a Pact of Infernal Prowess without journeying to Hell and destroying the devil's written contract spelling out the terms of your pact.
