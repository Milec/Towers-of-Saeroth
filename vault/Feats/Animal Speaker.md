# Animal Speaker  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 130

**Prerequisites**
ability to communicate with some type of animals (such as bats or arthropods)

---

You can speak to all [[Traits/Animal|animals]], not just a particular subset. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on animals (which usually uses the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill).
