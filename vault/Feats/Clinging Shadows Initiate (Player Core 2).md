# Clinging Shadows Initiate  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 123

**Prerequisites**
Qi Spells

---

You learn a mystical stance that transforms your qi into sticky smoke that shrouds your limbs. You gain the [[Spells/Clinging Shadows Stance (Player Core 2)|_clinging shadows stance_]] qi. While entering the stance is a qi spell, the shadow grasp attacks it grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.
