# Gnome Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 44

---

You favor unusual weapons tied to your people, such as blades with curved and peculiar shapes. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]] and [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]].

 In addition, you gain access to kukris and all uncommon [[Traits/Gnome|gnome]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial gnome weapons are simple weapons and advanced gnome weapons are martial weapons.
