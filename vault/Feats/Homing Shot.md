# Homing Shot single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eldritch Archer (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

Your shots zip around corners and fly at impossible angles to reach your target. Make a bow or crossbow Strike against a foe you can see; the ammunition travels to your target, even going around corners. You ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition and all [[Rules/Cover|cover]].
