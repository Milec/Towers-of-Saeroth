# Esteemed Visitor  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shoony]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 72

---

Your polite manners and natural ability to comfort others make it easy for you to break down cultural barriers and earn strangers’ trust. While you are in a settlement, when you roll a critical failure on a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check to [[Actions/Gather Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression]], you get a failure instead.
