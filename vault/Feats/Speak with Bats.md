# Speak with Bats  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 130

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Nyktera]] heritage

---

You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with bats. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on all Diplomacy checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on bats or [[Actions/Request]] something from bats.
