# Consume Magic ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
You attempt a saving throw against an area effect from the tradition matching your magiphage ability.

---

You consume the magic of an incoming spell or effect to protect your allies, though you might give yourself indigestion. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the triggering effect; your counteract rank equals half your level (rounded up), and for the roll, use either your class DC – 10 or your spellcasting attribute modifier plus your spellcasting proficiency bonus. If you counteract the triggering effect, you end the effect for all other creatures in the area, but you become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] as your body struggles to absorb the influx of magic.
