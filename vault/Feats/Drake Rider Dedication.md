# Drake Rider Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 220

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Drake Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]; Charisma +1

---

You gain the service of a young [riding drake](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=77), [riding dragonet](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=122), or another animal companion with the [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]] trait you have access to. Your bond with your dragon companion lets you communicate and issue commands to it telepathically with a range of 100 feet. You must still use the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] action to direct it but don’t require words or gestures to do so.
