# Gaze of Veracity  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 26

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Preferment]]

---

You can channel your mask's power to pierce through lies and see the truth in any situation. You learn the [[Spells/Glimpse the Truth (Player Core)|_glimpse the truth_]] focus spell. This has the same tradition trait as the spellcasting class feature used as a prerequisite for Hellknight Signifer Preferment. You gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point if you don't already have a focus pool or increase your focus pool by 1 (to a maximum of 3). You can [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by studying tomes of law or by contemplating the Measure and the Chain.
