# Agent of All Holds  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Tattooed Historian]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tattooed Historian Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You critically fail a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check.

---

Orcs across Belkzen recognize you as an honored lorekeeper. You’re adept at leveraging this status and showing off your tattoos to resolve conflicts, change minds, or distract others from a faux pas. You get a failure on the check, rather than a critical failure. If the triggering check was made against a creature with the [[Traits/Orc (Player Core)|orc]] trait, you can instead add the [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] trait to this ability and reroll the check, treating any critical failure as a failure.
