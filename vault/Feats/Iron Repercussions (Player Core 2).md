# Iron Repercussions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 94

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Obedience|obedience cause]]

---

Disobeying your Iron Command has lasting consequences. If an enemy refuses to kneel to you, you can deal [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]] instead of normal mental damage. You must decide whether the mental damage will be persistent before your enemy chooses whether to kneel or not. The amount of damage is unchanged.
