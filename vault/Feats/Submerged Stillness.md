# Submerged Stillness two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Elf]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

**Frequency**
once per day

---

With a mix of magic and physical training, you have learned to render yourself [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] while hiding underwater. You mimic the subtle movement of water so as not to disturb its surface, and you change your skin color to blend in with your surroundings. You gain the effects of [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] for up to 1 hour, as long as you remain submerged.
