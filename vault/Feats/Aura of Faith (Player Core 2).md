# Aura of Faith  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
holy or unholy

---

You radiate an aura of belief that imbues your attacks and those of nearby allies with divine power. Each willing ally in your champion's aura adds the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait to their Strikes if you're holy or adds the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait to their Strikes if you're unholy.
