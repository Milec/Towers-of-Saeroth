# Overwhelming Breath single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
Qi Spells

---

You control your breathing and align it with your qi, exhaling with controlled force in order to overcome your opponent's resistance. If you use your next action to Cast a Spell that has the [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|monk]] trait and no duration, the spell and any Strikes you make as a result of casting it ignore an amount of the target's resistance to physical damage equal to your level.
