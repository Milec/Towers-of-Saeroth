# Purge System three actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

---

You can sense the influence of harmful toxins in your body, and you heat and cool various parts of your body to expel them. Choose one [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|poison]] that is affecting you. Reduce the stage of that poison by one.

**Enhancement** When you use Purge System, you instead reduce the stage of the poison by two.
