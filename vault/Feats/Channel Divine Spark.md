# Channel Divine Spark single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ascended Celestial]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

---

You embrace your divine spark, unleashing your full potential. Your nimbus automatically activates (if it isn’t already) and the area of your nimbus doubles; you shed [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] for 60 feet (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] to the next 60 feet). You can’t suppress your nimbus while Channeling your Divine Spark. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level, and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half your level. You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition and can use the extra action each round only for [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] and [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] actions. Each time that you damage a creature with a melee Strike, you can attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] that creature as a free action. These effects last for 1 minute.
