# Noble Sacrifice ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 136

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Prophesied Monarch]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Prophesied Monarch Dedication]]

**Trigger**
One of your designated knights is within 15 feet of you and would take damage from an enemy’s [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].

---

As your knights devote their lives and swords to you, so too do you return their devotion with your own fierce protection. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. The knight [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Steps]] as a free action, and you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] into the space previously occupied by the knight; this movement ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain. You then take the triggering damage instead. If you have a [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|shield raised]] and have the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction, you can use it as part of this ability, applying damage to the shield as though you were the original target of the attack. The enemy whose [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] triggered this reaction is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.
