# Scroll Trickster Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 189

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scroll Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

---

You've studied [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|scrolls]] in depth. This might have been a comprehensive education in formal setting, or the sort of education where you somehow obtain a number of scrolls and try not to explode anything you didn't mean to explode. You gain the [[Feats/Trick Magic Item]] feat, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks to Trick scrolls. If you roll a critical failure to Trick a Magic Item that's a scroll, you get a failure instead.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the scroll trickster archetype.
