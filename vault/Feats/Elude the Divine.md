# Elude the Divine  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 101

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bright Lion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bright Lion Dedication]]

---

You are able to escape inquisitions involving divine magic, allowing you to blend in well in Mzali but also to avoid unwanted attention in other oppressive theocracies. You gain the [[Feats/Slippery Secrets]] skill feat, and its benefits also apply against [[Traits/Divination|divination]] effects that attempt to discern your deity. When using Slippery Secrets against a divine divination, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus, and on a critical success, the divination reveals false information appropriate to your false persona, rather than nothing.
