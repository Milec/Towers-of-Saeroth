# Uncanny Agility  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 34

---

You have near-supernatural poise that lets you move swiftly across the most unsteady surfaces. You gain the [[Feats/Steady Balance (Player Core)|Steady Balance]] skill feat. You can Step into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by uneven ground, such as a rocky field, hilly incline, or rocking ship.
