# Stumbling Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 119

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You enter a seemingly unfocused stance that mimics the movements of the inebriated—bobbing, weaving, leaving false openings, and distracting your enemies from your true movements. While in this stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]]. The only Strikes you can make are stumbling swing unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. If an enemy hits you with a melee Strike while in this stance, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the next stumbling swing Strike you make against it before the end of your next turn.
