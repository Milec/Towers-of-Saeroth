When you transform, you can take on a [[Bestiary/Families/Bear (Monster Core)|bear’s]] bulk and size. You can spend an additional action when using Ursine Avenger Form to gain the effects of a 1st-level [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]] spell, which lasts for the spell’s normal duration or until you leave your Ursine Avenger Form, whichever comes first.
