# Traditional Resistances  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Arcane Dragonblood]], [[Feats/Divine Dragonblood]], [[Feats/Occult Dragonblood]], or [[Feats/Primal Dragonblood]]

---

Due to your blood, you have some resistance to certain types of magic. You gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saves against spells and other magical effects from the same tradition as your lineage. This bonus increases to +2 against [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralysis]] effects.
