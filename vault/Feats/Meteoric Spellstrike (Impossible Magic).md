# Meteoric Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Starlit Span (Impossible Magic)|starlit span hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a ranged or [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown]] weapon, or have a ranged unarmed attack.

---

A trail of energy flows from your target back to you. Make a ranged [[Actions/Spellstrike]] against a target within your ranged weapon or unarmed attack’s first range increment. The spell you cast for the Spellstrike can’t be a [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] or [[Traits/Focus|focus]] spell. Each creature in a [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] between you and the target (not including you and the target) takes damage equal to double the spell’s rank. The damage type is the same as that dealt by the spell you cast (choose one type if the spell dealt multiple types of damage).
