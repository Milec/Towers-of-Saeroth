# Veil Dancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You’re able to shift yourself into the spaces between to avoid notice. You gain the Part the Veil action.

**Part the Veil** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Requirements** You are not observed by any enemies; **Effect** You phase yourself into the interstices, veiling yourself from physical reality. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]]. You can use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] as your disassociation from reality makes you difficult to focus on. If you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]], this effect ends after the action is complete; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute.
