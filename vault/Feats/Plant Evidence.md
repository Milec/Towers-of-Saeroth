# Plant Evidence single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 134

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Alkenstar Agent]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pickpocket]]

---

You can put a single item you're holding of light or negligible Bulk onto a person without them noticing by succeeding at a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] check against their Perception DC. If you have the [[Classes/Subclasses/Rackets/Ruffian|ruffian]] racket, you can do this as a free action when you successfully [[Actions/Shove]] a target.
