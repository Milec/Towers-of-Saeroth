# Metabolize Element ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You take damage from a foe's spell or magical ability with the [[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait.

---

Your rapidly metabolize the elemental particles in your opponent's spell to gain a boost of energy. You gain the [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] condition until the end of your next turn. You can use the extra action only to Step or Stride.
