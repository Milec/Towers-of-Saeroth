# What's That up Your Sleeve?  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 73

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Gambling Lore]]

---

Whether as a dealer or player, you've learned to spot cheaters. When another creature attempts to [[Actions/Conceal an Object]] from you, use your Gambling Lore DC if it's higher than your Perception DC to determine whether they succeed. When you search a creature for a concealed object, you can use Gambling Lore in place of Perception.
