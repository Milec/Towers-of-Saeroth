# Uncanny Suction  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oozemorph Dedication]]

---

Your limbs produce strange, sucker-like growths that allow you to adhere to walls, objects, and most other surfaces. You gain a climb Speed equal to your half your land Speed (minimum 5 feet). If you have the [[Feats/Quick Climb|Quick Climber]] feat, your climb Speed is equal to your land Speed. In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC to avoid being [[Actions/Disarm|Disarmed]].
