# Disarming Assault single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You attack with enough force to knock the weapon out of your foe's hands. Make a melee Strike; if you hit, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] that enemy.
