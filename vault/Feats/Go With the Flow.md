# Go With the Flow ⤾  *Feat 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Secret]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 71

**Frequency**
Once per day

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Trigger**
A creature detects your attempt to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]], and you're within 30 feet of at least one other creature.

---

You have an occult connection to the world that helps you blend in with the cacophony of life around you. Attempt an Occultism check against the creature's Perception DC. On a success, the creature mistakenly identifies another creature within 30 feet of you as you instead.
