# Tactical Cadence  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cadence Call (Player Core 2)|Cadence Call]]; [[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

Your remarkable breath control and concise instructions allow you to coordinate your allies more effectively, even in desperate situations. When you grant allies the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition using [[Feats/Cadence Call (Player Core 2)|Cadence Call]], they can use the extra action to either [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], and they aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 on their following turn if they use the extra action.
