# Hobbling Criticals  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

---

Your eidolon’s unarmed attacks batter your foes into submission, hampering their movement. Whenever your eidolon gets a critical hit with one of their unarmed Strikes, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC after taking the damage. This is a [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect.

**Critical Success** The target takes no additional effect.  

**Success** The target takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 and takes a –15-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.
