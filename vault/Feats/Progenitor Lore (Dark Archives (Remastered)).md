# Progenitor Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 120

---

You didn't just inherit your physical form from your progenitor; you also retained some of their knowledge. You're trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] corresponding to your ancestry (e. g., Elf Lore if your progenitor is an elf).
