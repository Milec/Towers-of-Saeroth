# Overwhelming Blow three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 127

---

You throw your weight into a powerful attack that leaves you vulnerable. Make a melee Strike. This counts as three attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If this Strike hits, you get a critical hit. If you roll a critical hit, your attack also gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]] trait. Whether or not you hit, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of your next turn.
