# Lavasoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

Your elemental lineage manifests as magma and molten rock, and you can focus the magma into your hand to attack your foes. You gain a magma spike unarmed attack, which requires a free hand to use. This is an [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group that deals 1d4 piercing damage, as well as 1 additional fire damage. Instead of the normal critical specialization effect, the target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]; you gain a bonus on this persistent damage equal to your item bonus to attack rolls on unarmed attacks.
