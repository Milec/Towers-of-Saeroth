# Consume Energy ⤾  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 9, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 100

**Trigger**
An enemy within 60 feet uses an ability that has the trait corresponding to your deviation damage type.

---

Your connection to energy is so much stronger than the offending display in front of you, allowing you to claim it for yourself. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check against the triggering effect as you draw it into your body. If you successfully counteract the ability, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to double the counteract level of the ability, which last for 1 minute.
