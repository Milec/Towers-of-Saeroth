# Cobra Envenom single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 124

**Frequency**
once per minute.

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cobra Stance (Player Core 2)|Cobra Stance]]; expert in unarmed strikes

**Requirements**
You are in Cobra Stance.

---

You slightly dislocate your joints to lash out with devious intent and the power to envenom your foe. Make a cobra fang Strike. Your [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] with this Strike is 5 feet greater than normal. If this Strike hits, the [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] from your [[Traits/Venomous|venomous]] trait increases to 1d4 persistent poison damage per weapon damage die.

**Special** If you have this feat, the circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves and Fortitude DC granted by Cobra Stance increases from +1 to +2.
