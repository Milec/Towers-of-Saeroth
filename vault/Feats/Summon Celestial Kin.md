# Summon Celestial Kin  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 36

**Prerequisites**
any aasimar [[Traits/Lineage|lineage]] feat

---

You have a connection to the celestial realms, allowing you to summon a celestial ally. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Summon Celestial|_summon celestial_]] as a 5th-level divine innate spell, but the celestial summoned must be from the same category as your own lineage, such as a creature with the [[Traits/Angel|angel]] trait if you are angelkin.
