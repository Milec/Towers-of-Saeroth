# Oath of The Defender  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Oath]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 276

---

During your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], you can swear an oath to defend against a certain kind of enemy during your deeds that day. Choose [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberrations]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestials]], [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragons]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. You can’t choose celestials if you’re [[Traits/Holy|holy]], nor can you choose fiends if you’re [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]. You gain an edict to defend against creatures of that kind.

Allies in your champion’s aura, not including you, get [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 2 to damage dealt by creatures with the chosen trait. If such a creature deals more than one damage type at once, apply this resistance only to the highest amount of damage. The resistance increases to 3 at 7th level, 4 at 12th level, and 5 at 17th level. If a creature with the chosen trait triggers your champion’s reaction and your champion’s reaction grants resistance against the triggering damage, the resistance increases by 5.
