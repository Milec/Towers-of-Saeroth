# Cheaters Always Prosper  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dirty Trick]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

---

You’re no champion of justice and are perfectly willing to fight dirty if it helps you survive. You can attempt a check to perform a [[Feats/Dirty Trick]] using your melee weapon even if you don’t have a free hand. If you critically fail a check to Dirty Trick using a weapon, you can drop the weapon to take the effects of a failure instead of a critical failure.
