You and your spotter are adept at watching out for one another. As long as you can see or hear each other, neither of you is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], or [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] creatures of your level or lower, or to creatures of your level or lower using a surprise attack. However, they can still help their allies flank.
