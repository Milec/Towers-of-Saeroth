# Athletic Strategist  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 107

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You know how to calculatedly manipulate joints and body weight. In addition to using an attack stratagem's roll substitution for a Strike, you can use it to modify a [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] attempt, substituting your [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]] roll for the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check. You must apply the substitution to the first eligible attack you make, whether it's a Strike or one of the Athletics actions.

 You can also use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength for the Athletics check when you substitute your Devise a Stratagem roll, unless you're using a weapon for the maneuver and the weapon doesn't fit the restrictions for using Intelligence with a stratagem.
