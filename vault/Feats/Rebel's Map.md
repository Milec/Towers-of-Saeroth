# Rebel's Map free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 79

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Horizon Walker]], [[Archetypes/Scout]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Horizon Walker Dedication]] or [[Feats/Scout Dedication]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
You roll initiative.

---

Rebellions require an intricate knowledge of one's surroundings—because you might need to escape at any moment. You remember the locations of escape routes, ambush spots, and cover from a scouting expedition or a previous visit to the area. Roll a d20. At any point during the subsequent combat, you can substitute the result of this roll instead of rolling a d20 on one [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]] or on one [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Hide]].
