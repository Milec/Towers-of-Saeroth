# Perplex two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 35

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Steel Falcon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Steel Falcon Dedication]]

---

You weave a confusing web of half-truths, conspiracy theories, and plausible motives to goad an enemy into lashing out wildly. Choose a target within 30 feet that can hear you, and attempt a Diplomacy check against its Will DC. The creature is then immune to Perplex for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 3]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.  

**Success** The target is stupefied 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.
