# Wolf in Sheep's Clothing  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 64

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

With a bit of hide, some antlers, and some cast-off fur, you can create a disguise that will fool even an experienced hunter. You can [[Actions/Impersonate]] creatures with the [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Beast|beast]], or [[Traits/Plant|plant]] traits, so long as they are either the same size or one size larger than you and have a body shape that conforms at least vaguely to your own (in other words, a typical humanoid could Impersonate a [[Bestiary/Families/Bear|bear]] or an [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal|arboreal]], but not a [[Bestiary/Families/Horse|horse]], [[Bestiary/Giant Scorpion|giant scorpion]], or [[Bestiary/Assassin Vine|assassin vine]]). Impersonating a larger creature doesn't change your actual size.
