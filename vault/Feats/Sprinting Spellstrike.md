# Sprinting Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 24

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
Your Spellstrike is charged.

---

Magic propels you across the battlefield as you speed toward your target. Make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]], casting a spell that has a [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] area. Before making your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] in a straight line to a distance up to the line’s length; you can move through enemies during this movement, and your movement doesn’t trigger reactions. You can use Sprinting Spellstrike while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Expansive Spellstrike]] feat, the spell’s area uses the [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] of your movement instead of the normal method of determining the line.
