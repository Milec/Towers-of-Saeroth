# Vanara Weapon Trickery  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 56

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vanara Weapon Familiarity]]

---

You've learned how to trick your foes using vanara weapons. Whenever you critically hit using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Chakram|chakram]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Katar|katar]], [panabas](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=265), [urumi](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=102), or [[Traits/Vanara|vanara]] weapon, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
