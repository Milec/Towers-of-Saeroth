# Warped Reflection single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 120

---

Due to your origins, your form is more flexible than most. You shift your appearance to resemble a creature within 30 feet but with an unsettling, horrifying twist. Though this transformation is momentary, witnessing it gives the target deep existential dread. It must succeed at a Will save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure) and be [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 as long as the frightened condition lasts. Once you've used Warped Reflection against a creature, it's temporarily immune for 24 hours.
