# Purifying Spell single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 200

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Wave|wave order]]

---

You purify the water within a creature's body to cleanse them of illness. If the next action you use is to cast [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] targeting a single living creature, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison|poison]] affecting the target, in addition to the other benefits of _heal_. If you do, _heal_ gains the [[Traits/Water|water]] trait.
