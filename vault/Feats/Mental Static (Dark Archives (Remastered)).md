# Mental Static ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|Nonlethal]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 27

**Trigger**
You roll a critical success on a Will save against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect from a creature.

---

Your thoughts sting back when a lesser mind tries to invade your own. You deal mental damage equal to your level to the triggering creature. If you so desire, you can also send the creature a single mental image to coincide with the damage, such as a watchful eye or the dread of floating in an infinite sea.
