# Hurling Charge two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 220

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Viking (Player Core 2)|Viking]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Viking Dedication (Player Core 2)|Viking Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown]] weapon.

---

You make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your thrown weapon, [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], and then Interact to draw another weapon. This Interact action doesn't trigger reactions.

**Special** If you are [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|raging]] and end the Stride adjacent to an enemy, that enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the next Strike you make against it with the weapon you drew before the end of your next turn.
