# Spirit Soother  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

You are attuned to the minute sounds and sensations of restless spirits. Even if you aren't [[Actions/Search|Searching]], you can attempt a check to find [[Traits/Haunt|haunts]] that normally require you to be Searching. You still need to meet any other requirements to find the haunt.
