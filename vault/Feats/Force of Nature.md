You're a nigh-unstoppable killing machine, with only [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] able to quell your rampage. When in hybrid or animal shape, you gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing 5]]. If you take damage from a silver weapon, your fast healing deactivates until the end of your next turn.
