# Shooter's Camouflage  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 117

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

You specialize in blending into a particular type of environment, able to disappear after taking your shot. Choose either natural or urban terrain. In the chosen terrain, you can [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] even without cover or being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]].
