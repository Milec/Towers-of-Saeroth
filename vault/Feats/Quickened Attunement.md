# Quickened Attunement free action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 213

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Geomancer]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Geomancer Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was Attunement Shift.

---

If your next action is to cast a spell that would grant your terrain attunement bonus, reduce the number of actions to cast it by 1 (to a minimum of 1 action).

**Special** You can't use Quickened Attunement and [[Feats/Quickened Casting]] in the same round.
