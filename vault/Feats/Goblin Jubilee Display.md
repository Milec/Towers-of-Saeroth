# Goblin Jubilee Display  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 135

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Describing Your Fireworks Display|Firework Technican]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Firework Technician Dedication]]

---

Flames and explosions sear the air in a riot of color and devastation that rivals that wrought by even the wildest goblin party. The display is both so beautiful and chaotic that, while mesmerizing, it poses a significant risk to the physical health, sight, and hearing of anyone who gets too close. You gain the Goblin Jubilee fireworks display.

**Goblin Jubilee** three actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) Cost 3 batches of infused reagents; **Effect** Chaos fills a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 120 feet. All creatures in the area takes 3d6 fire damage and 3d6 sonic damage and must attempt a Fortitude save. A goblin jubilee display costs 3 batches of infused reagents, rather than 1.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] until the end of its next turn and takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled and deafened for 1 minute and takes full damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round, dazzled and deafened for 1 minute, and takes double damage.
