# Tools of the Trade  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 163

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bounty Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bounty Hunter Dedication]]

---

You're well-versed in weapons that allow you to bring bounties in alive. You are trained with the following weapons: [bola](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=123), [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap|sap]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip|whip]]. You gain access to bolas. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the weapons listed.

 In addition, you take no penalty when making a nonlethal attack with a weapon without the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait.
