# Paragon Battle Medicine  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 72

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]] (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Battle Medicine]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

Kassi Aziril taught you her techniques that originated the modern use of [[Feats/Battle Medicine]]. When you successfully use Battle Medicine, you can also reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] condition by 1 (this has no effect if you are subject to an effect continually applying the clumsy condition, like [[Spells/Enlarge|_enlarge_]]). If you are legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]], you can choose to reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] condition by 1 instead, and if you have the [[Feats/Godless Healing]] feat, you can choose to reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition by 1 instead. If you have the [[Feats/Mortal Healing]] feat, you can reduce all available conditions by 1 for a target who hasn't benefited from positive or healing magic in the last 24 hours, and if you roll a critical success before applying the effects of the feat, you reduce all available conditions by 2 for that target instead.

## Kassi's Techniques

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 71

While Kassi shares her greatest discoveries with the entire medical community, some of her techniques are either still experimental or extremely difficult to perform. Any students who Kassi takes on gain access to her techniques.
