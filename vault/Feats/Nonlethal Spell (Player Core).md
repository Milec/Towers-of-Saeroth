# Nonlethal Spell single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 201

---

You can alter offensive spells to be less deadly. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that deals damage and doesn't have the death or void trait, that spell gains the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait.
