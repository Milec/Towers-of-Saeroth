# Swan Dive single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bravado]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

---

You soar through the air and into the water, using your momentum to propel yourself forward. You intentionally [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] or otherwise fall into a body of water, taking no falling damage regardless of the distance, and then [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to your Speed. Until the end of your turn, when you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to Swim you get a critical success instead, and when you roll a critical failure you get a failure instead.
