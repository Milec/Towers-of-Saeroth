# Messenger  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 110

---

You were built with a special ability to relay private messages to your fellow automatons, but you can luckily use it with anyone you would like. You gain [[Spells/Message (Player Core)|_message_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] cantrip.

**Enhancement** You can target up to five creatures at once when you cast _message_.
