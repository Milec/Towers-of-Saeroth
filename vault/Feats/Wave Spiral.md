# Wave Spiral two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 202

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Reflective Ripple Stance]]

**Requirements**
You're in Reflective Ripple Stance.

---

You dip and spin, unleashing a wide whirlpool of water. Make an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip]] each creature standing on the ground in a 10-foot emanation. These attacks all count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after you make all the attacks.
