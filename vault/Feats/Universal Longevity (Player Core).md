You’ve perfected your ability to keep up with all the skills you’ve learned over your long life, so you’re almost never truly untrained at a skill. You reflect on your life experiences, changing the skill you selected with [[Feats/Ancestral Longevity (Player Core)|Ancestral Longevity]] (and the skill you selected with [[Feats/Expert Longevity (Player Core)|Expert Longevity]] if you have that feat).
