# Vigorous Health  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

---

You can withstand blood loss startlingly well. Whenever you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition, you can attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, you don't gain the drained condition.
