# Legendary Monster Hunter  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 176

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]; [[Feats/Master Monster Hunter]]

---

Your knowledge of monsters is so incredible that it reveals glaring flaws in your prey. Your bonus from [[Feats/Monster Hunter]] (and the bonus from [[Feats/Monster Warden]] if you have it) increases from +1 to +2 for you and any allies who benefit.
