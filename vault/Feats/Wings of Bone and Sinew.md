# Wings of Bone and Sinew single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

You can harness the power of death to craft wings from bones and a thin membrane of flesh. You destroy a thrall within 30 feet, fusing it with your body and shaping it into gruesome, bony wings. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 30 feet for 1 minute. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] these wings.

At 16th level, this action loses its frequency and gains an unli mited duration.
