# Elemental Embellish  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Suli]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 110

---

You can summon a harmless but impressive elemental display. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]. If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. When you [[Actions/Demoralize]] a foe, if you choose to Demoralize via an elemental display, Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language.
