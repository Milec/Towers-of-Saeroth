# Catchy Tune single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 130

---

You create a tune so catchy, your foes can't help but dance along. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check against the Will DCs of all enemies in a 30-foot emanation.

**Critical Success** The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed and is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], both for 1 round.  

**Success** The target takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is temporarily immune for 1 hour.
