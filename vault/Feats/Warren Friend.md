# Warren Friend  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 54

---

You're adept at making contact with other ysoki. When you arrive at a new settlement, you automatically find the nearest ratfolk enclave by spending 1 day of downtime searching, provided one exists and its members aren't actively hiding from you. Once you've made contact, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Gather Information]] and [[Actions/Earn Income]] in the settlement.
