The echoes of the [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality's_]] original purpose resonate through the artifact. For the next minute, your _spectral dagger_ gains the benefits of a [[Equipment/Vorpal (GM Core)|_vorpal_]] rune.
