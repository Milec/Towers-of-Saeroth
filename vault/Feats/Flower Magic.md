# Flower Magic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 38

---

Certain magics in the world flow easily through your sap. You can cast [[Spells/Barkskin|_barkskin_]] and [[Spells/Tree Shape|_tree shape_]] as 2nd-level arcane innate spells once per day each. A _tree shape_ spell you cast this way transforms you into a Large flowering plant like a rosebush or lilac bush instead of a tree.
