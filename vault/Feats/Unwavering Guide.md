# Unwavering Guide  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

---

Sharp-eyed and sure-footed, your presence is a boon to those you guide. When you [[Actions/Scout (Player Core)|Scout]], you grant your party a +2 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls instead of the normal +1. When your group uses the [[Actions/Hustle (Player Core)|Hustle]] activity, they can use your Constitution modifier to determine how fast the group can Hustle together, even if it isn’t the lowest in the group.
