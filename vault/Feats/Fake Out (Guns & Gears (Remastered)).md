# Fake Out ⤾  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 112

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) For the purpose of gunslinger feats and abilities, if the ability states that you fire your weapon, you fire your weapon; if it does not state as such, you do not have to fire your weapon. Notably, Fake Out does not require you to actually fire your weapon._

**Trigger**
An ally is about to use an action that requires an attack roll, targeting a creature within your weapon's first range increment.

**Requirements**
You're wielding a loaded firearm or crossbow.

---

With a skilled flourish of your weapon, you force an enemy to acknowledge you as a threat. Make an attack roll to [[Actions/Aid]] the triggering attack. If you dealt damage to that enemy with the same weapon since the start of your last turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to this roll.
