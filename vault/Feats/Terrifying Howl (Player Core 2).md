# Terrifying Howl single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 82

---

You unleash a terrifying howl. Attempt [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet: you don't take a penalty if the creature doesn't understand your language. Regardless of the results of your checks, each target is then temporarily immune to Terrifying Howl for 1 minute.
