# Bullet and a Broadside single action or two actions  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Pathfinder Society does not use siege weaponry._

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearm]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbow]] and are adjacent to a [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|siege weapon]].

---

You’re familiar with ranged weapons of all sizes, and can efficiently move to use them in tandem. If you spent one action, you [[Rules/Launching (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Launch]] the siege weapon and [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with your firearm or crossbow in any order, using the same multiple attack penalty for both attacks. If you spent two actions, you [[Rules/Loading (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Load]] the siege engine and [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to reload your firearm or crossbow in any order, and don’t need a free hand to do so.
