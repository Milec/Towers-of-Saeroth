# Master Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Expert Captivator Spellcasting]]

---

You master the most powerful spells that enchant and deceive. You learn a 7th-level occult spell. At 18th level, you learn an 8th-level spell, and at 20th level, you learn a 9th-level spell. Each of these spells must come from either the [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] or [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] school. You cast these spells as occult innate spells. You become a master with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
