# Bizarre Transformation single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Untamed Form]]

---

The forms you take on defy belief—chimerical sights of twisted antlers or acidic drool. If your next action is to cast untamed form, you can change the damage type of a single unarmed attack granted by untamed form to one of the following: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], bludgeoning, [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], piercing, or slashing. The chosen attack gains the appropriate trait.
