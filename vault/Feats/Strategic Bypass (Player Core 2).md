# Strategic Bypass  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 112

---

Your plans account for your foes' resistances, enabling you to strike a telling blow. When you hit with a Strike on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devising a Stratagem]], you ignore an amount of resistance equal to your Intelligence modifier for each resistance that applies against your attack.
