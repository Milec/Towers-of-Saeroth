# Shory Aerialist  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 15

**Prerequisites**
Garundi ethnicity, Mauxi ethnicity, or Tian-Yae ethnicity; [[Feats/Shory Aeromancer]] or ability to cast [[Spells/Fly|_fly_]]

---

Unique in Golarion’s history, the Shory people developed fighting styles dedicated to combat in the air. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to Maneuver in Flight and a +5-foot status bonus to your fly Speed whenever you are flying via magic.
