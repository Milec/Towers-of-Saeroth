# Drow Shootist Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Drow Shootist]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbow]]

---

Every chance you get, you practice with the hand crossbow to increase your draw speed and accuracy. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbow]]. If you're at least an expert in a hand crossbow you're using, you gain access to its critical specialization effect. You also gain the Shootist's Draw action.

 **Shootist's Draw** single action **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** You [[Actions/Interact]] to draw a hand crossbow and [[Actions/Strike]] with it, or you Strike with a loaded hand crossbow you're already holding and then Interact to stow it.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you've gained two other feats from the drow shootist archetype.
