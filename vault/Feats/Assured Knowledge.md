# Assured Knowledge  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 113

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Loremaster]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Enigma|enigma muse]]

---

You can procure information with confidence. Whenever you [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] using any skill (including [[Feats/Bardic Lore]]), you can forgo rolling your check to instead receive a result of 10 + your proficiency bonus (don't apply any other bonuses, penalties, or modifiers). As long as you are an expert in a skill, you meet the prerequisites for the [[Feats/Automatic Knowledge]] skill feat in that skill, even if you don't have [[Feats/Assurance]] in that skill.
