# Mature Beastmaster Companion  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 188

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Beastmaster (Player Core 2)|Beastmaster]], [[Archetypes/Undead Master]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beastmaster Dedication (Player Core 2)|Beastmaster Dedication]]

---

All of your animal companions grow up, becoming [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions (Player Core)|mature animal companions]] and gaining additional capabilities.

Your animal companion has greater independence. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] action, your active animal companion can still use 1 action that round on your turn to Strike or Stride (or [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] if it has that Speed).. It can do this at any point during your turn, as long as you aren't currently taking an action. If it does, that's all the actions it gets that round— you can't Command it later.
