# Do You Know Who I Am? two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

Your reputation casts a long shadow, allowing you to throw your cultural weight around, especially in larger settlements. With a well-timed tirade or attention-getting shout, you can break a foe's mind and bring them low. Choose a foe within 30 feet, and attempt an Intimidation check against the target's Will DC. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to this check if you're in a city or a +2 circumstance bonus to this check if you're in a metropolis. If you're specifically in Absalom, the bonus is instead +3. The target is then temporarily immune to your Do You Know Who I Am? for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The target takes 12d6 mental damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 3. It can't reduce its frightened condition below 1 if it ends its turn adjacent to you.  

**Success** The target takes 6d6 mental damage and becomes frightened 2.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.  

**Critical Failure** The target is unaffected, and the embarrassment causes you to take 6d6 mental damage.
