# Vampire Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

You were raised by your vampiric parent, or you've invested substantial time and energy researching the secrets of your undead progenitors. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Traits/Vampire|Vampire Lore]].
