# Shattering Strike (Weapon Improviser) ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Improvised Weapon Ideas|Weapon Improviser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Improvised Pummel]]

**Trigger**
An improvised weapon that you are wielding becomes broken as a result of a critical success on an Improvised Pummel.

---

The improvised weapon shatters, destroying it completely but dealing an additional 3d6 piercing damage to the creature you hit with the [[Feats/Improvised Pummel]]. This extra damage you apply on the critical hit doesn't double from the critical hit, as normal.
