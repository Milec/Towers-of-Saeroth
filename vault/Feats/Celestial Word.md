# Celestial Word  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 36

---

You can call forth a holy word from the celestial realms to punish your foes. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Divine Decree|_divine decree_]] as a 7th-level divine innate spell. You must choose good for the spell's alignment, and you can Cast the Spell regardless of whether you worship a deity or what their alignment is.
