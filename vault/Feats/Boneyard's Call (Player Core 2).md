# Boneyard's Call  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

You have a powerful connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]] and its courts, granting you the ability to travel to and from the Boneyard. You can cast [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] as a divine innate spell twice per week. This can be used only to travel to the Boneyard or from the Boneyard to [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]]. Due to your unique connection to the Boneyard, your body serves as the locus, and you don't require a planar key.
