# Lifeblood's Call  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 51

---

The impulse to survive and continue fighting resonates deep within you. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage rolls on your melee Strikes equal to twice the sum of your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] conditions (to a maximum of a +8 circumstance bonus to damage) whenever you have one or both conditions.
