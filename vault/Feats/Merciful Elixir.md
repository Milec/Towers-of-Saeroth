# Merciful Elixir free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive|Additive 2]], [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 79

**Frequency**
once per round

**Trigger**
You craft an [[Equipment/Elixir of Life|elixir of life]] using Quick Alchemy, and that elixir is at least 2 levels lower than your advanced alchemy level.

---

You mix a special additive into your elixir that calms the drinker's body and mind. The elixir of life attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one fear effect or one effect imposing the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] condition on the drinker, using the item's level and a counteract modifier equal to your class DC – 10.
