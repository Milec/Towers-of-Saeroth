# Betraying Shank single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 243

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Speaker (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Twilight Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Speaker Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Twilight Speaker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are within melee reach of a target, but it is not in combat with you.

---

While your usual diplomacy is well intentioned, you know that sometimes those same skills will need to be turned to deception. In a flash, you draw a sheathed or concealed [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] weapon and make a melee Strike. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against your Strike. You then roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] for initiative.
