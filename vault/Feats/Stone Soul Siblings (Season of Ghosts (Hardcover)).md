# Stone Soul Siblings  *Feat 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 320

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Plant Soul Siblings (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Plant Soul Siblings]]

Your connection with nature’s spirits has progressed to the point where you can even have casual conversation with pebbles and boulders. You gain [[Spells/Speak with Stones|_speak with stones_]] as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that you can cast once per day. You get a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Rules/Gather Information (GM Core)|Gather Information]] from stones.
