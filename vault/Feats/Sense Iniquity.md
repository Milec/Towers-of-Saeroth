# Sense Iniquity single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 26

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]

---

Your Hellknight training gives you the ability to focus your senses and root out those who would attempt to deceive or hide from you. For 1 minute, you gain a +2 status bonus to your Perception DC against attempts to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]], [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] you, or [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you. During this time, if you [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] to locate a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] creature or to see through a creature's [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] attempt, you gain a +2 status bonus to the Perception check.
