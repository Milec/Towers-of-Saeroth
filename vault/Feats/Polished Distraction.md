# Polished Distraction two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 33

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Golden Legionnaire Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You aren't in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]].

---

You take pride in the sheen of your armor, and how useful it is at distracting your enemies when you reflect light from it. Creatures within a 5-foot emanation that can see you must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. Once a creature attempts a save against this effect, it's temporarily immune for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for
2 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 2 rounds and dazzled for 1 minute.
