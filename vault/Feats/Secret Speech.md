# Secret Speech  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 73

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

Choose a secret society (such as the Lacunafex or the Bellflower Network). You’ve learned that society’s secret cant or jargon, enabling you to share hidden messages when signing or speaking with others who’ve also learned it. If an observer succeeds at a Perception check against your [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] DC when you do this, they realize you are passing a message but fail to discern its content, unless they subsequently succeed at a Society check against your Deception DC. On a critical success, they learn not only the message, but also who you’re passing the message to.

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times. You learn the secret speech of a different society each time.

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 72

If the PCs earn Mialari Docur's trust and prove their worth by defeating the Scarlet Triad, she rewards them by granting them access to several feats and spells she and her students have developed over the years.
