# Night's Shine  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 239

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oatia Skysage Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage Dedication]]; [[Feats/Night's Glow (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Night's Glow]]

---

Your power from the moon and stars becomes strong enough to illuminate the night sky. You gain your choice of the [[Spells/Asterism (Divine Mysteries)|_asterism_]] or [[Spells/Touch of the Moon (Player Core)|_touch of the moon_]] domain spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your pool by 1.

**Special** You can take this feat a second time, gaining the focus spell that you didn’t gain the first time. Increase the number of Focus Points in your pool by 1.
