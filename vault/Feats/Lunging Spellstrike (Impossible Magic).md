# Lunging Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twisting Tree|twisting tree hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]].

---

Your spell unwinds the structure of your staff to make it exceptionally long, or even separate it into shards held together by magical power. Make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a staff, with a spell that isn’t a [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] or [[Traits/Focus|focus]] spell. Increase the staff’s reach by 5 feet × the spell’s rank.
