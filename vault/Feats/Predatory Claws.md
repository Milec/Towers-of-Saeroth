# Predatory Claws  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Vampire Vulnerabilities|Vampire]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vampire Dedication]]

---

Your nails grow into sharp claws, perfect for seizing and tearing your prey. You gain a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

 If you hit the same enemy with two consecutive claw Strikes in the same round, you can attempt to [[Actions/Grapple]] that same target as a free action, provided you take that action immediately after the second Strike.
