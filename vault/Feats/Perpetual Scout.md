# Perpetual Scout  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 177

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Horizon Walker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Horizon Walker Dedication]]

---

You spot anything out of place in the areas you know best. While in your favored terrain, you always gain the benefits of the [[Actions/Scout]] exploration activity, even if you are performing another exploration activity. If you're legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]], you gain these benefits in any terrain.
