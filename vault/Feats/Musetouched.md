# Musetouched  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 35

---

Your blood sings with the liberating power of the [[Bestiary/Families/Azata|azatas]], living embodiments of freedom from the wild realm of [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]]. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Escape]]. When you roll a critical failure on a check to Escape, you get a failure instead, and when you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
