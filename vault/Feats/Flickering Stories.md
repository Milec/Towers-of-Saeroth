# Flickering Stories  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 35

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Campfire Chronicler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Campfire Chronicler Dedication]]

---

Stories, like fires, can create shadows as much as they illuminate. When you Offer a Story, you can become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] by shifting shadows for the duration instead of the normal benefits. The action then gains the shadow trait. You can’t use this concealment to Hide, as normal for concealing effects that leave your location obvious. The creature you share a story with can choose this benefit even if you did not.
