# Dazzling Block free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Sparkling Targe (Impossible Magic)|sparkling targe hybrid study]]

**Trigger**
You use [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]].

---

As your shield blocks an assault, you create a flash of brilliant, multicolored light in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC, with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 1 minute. The creature can spend an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action rubbing its eyes to end this blinded condition.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 round and dazzled for 1 hour.
