# Many Faces  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 68

**Frequency**
three times per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Everyday Form]]

---

Putting on a new face offers a great way to get a fresh perspective on life. When you Change Shape into your everyday form, you gain the effects of a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] for 1 hour or until you shift back, whichever comes first, except it’s a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect rather than an [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]]. However, your transformations can be broken with a good shock; if you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] condition while transformed, your transformation ends, returning you to your tanuki form, and your confidence is so shaken you can’t attempt to resume that specific disguise again until your next daily preparations.
