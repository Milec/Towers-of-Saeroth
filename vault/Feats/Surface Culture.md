# Surface Culture  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 20

**Access**
any surface dwarf ethnicity

---

Your interactions with other cultures on the surface have not only taught you about many other ancestries, but have also helped you realize the value of your own. You gain the trained proficiency rank in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill (or another skill of your choice if you were already trained in Society), and you gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] skill feat for the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] corresponding to your culture (for instance, Ouat Lore or Pahmet Lore).
