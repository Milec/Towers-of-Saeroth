# Plant Soul Siblings  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 80

---

You're now able to connect with the spirits present in the greenery of the natural world. You gain [[Spells/Speak with Plants (Player Core)|_speak with plants_]] as a primal innate spell that you can cast once per day. You get a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]] from animals or plants.
