# Bon Mot single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 203

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]

---

You launch an insightful quip at a foe, distracting them. Choose a foe within 30 feet and roll a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check against the target's Will DC.

**Critical Success** The target is distracted and takes a –3 status penalty to Perception and Will saves for 1 minute. The target can end the effect early with a retort to your Bon Mot. This can either be a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait or an appropriate skill action to frame their retort. The GM determines which skill actions qualify, though they must take at least 1 action. Typically, the retort needs to use a [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] Charisma-based skill action.  

**Success** As critical success, but the penalty is –2.  

**Critical Failure** Your quip is atrocious. You take the same penalty an enemy would take had you succeeded. This ends after 1 minute or if you issue another Bon Mot and succeed.
