You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet|hatchet]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]].
