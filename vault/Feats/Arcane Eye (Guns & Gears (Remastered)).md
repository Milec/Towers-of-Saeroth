**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

Your eye has been magically enhanced to pierce darkness. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

**Enhancement** Your eye can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures in brief spurts. You can cast [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]] as an arcane innate spell once per hour.
