# Hobgoblin Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 50

---

You are trained with [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow|composite longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|composite shortbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaives]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow|longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longswords]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbows]]. In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Hobgoblin|hobgoblin]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial hobgoblin weapons are simple weapons and advanced hobgoblin weapons are martial weapons.
