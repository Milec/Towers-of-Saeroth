# Horizon Walker Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 177

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Horizon Walker]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

---

You've mastered travel within a specific type of terrain. You gain the [[Feats/Favored Terrain]] feat. When in your favored terrain, you gain a +10-foot circumstance bonus to your travel Speed. When other creatures [[Actions/Follow the Expert]] with you as a guide in your favored terrain, they gain both the ability to ignore non-magical difficult terrain for the purpose of their travel Speed and the bonus to travel Speed.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the horizon walker archetype.
