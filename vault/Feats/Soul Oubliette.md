# Soul Oubliette ⤾  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spectral Dagger]]

**Trigger**
You reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with an attack from your _spectral dagger_.

**Requirements**
Your [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality's_]]  doesn’t contain a trapped soul.

---

Your mortal strike wrenches the soul from your victim’s body and imprisons it in your [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality_]], with the effects of [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]]. The _splinter of finality_ can only hold one soul at a time, but you can release a trapped soul as a free action. If you don’t free a soul willingly, it escapes automatically after 24 hours. However, if you touch the _splinter of finality_ to an unbroken final blade while a soul is trapped within, the soul is transferred to the [[Equipment/Final Blade|_final blade_]] and imprisoned indefinitely.

While your _splinter of finality_ contains a trapped soul, you gain [[Rules/Fast Healing and Regeneration (Player Core)|fast healing]] 15.
