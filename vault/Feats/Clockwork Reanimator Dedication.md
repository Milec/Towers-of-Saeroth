# Clockwork Reanimator Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Outlaws of Alkenstar

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clockwork Reanimator]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You can animate corpses through cunning engineering. You gain a [[Rules/Construct Companions (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|prototype construct companion]] to serve as a minion. Unlike normal construct companions, yours is a corpse animated by clockwork gears and technological prowess.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you've gained two other feats from the clockwork reanimator archetype. If you retrain out of this feat, you lose the rituals learned from it.
