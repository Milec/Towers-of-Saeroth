# Intuitive Crafting  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aphorite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 76

---

Your affinity with process and order makes you a natural artisan. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (or another skill of your choice, if you're already trained in Crafting). You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat in a specialty of your choice.
