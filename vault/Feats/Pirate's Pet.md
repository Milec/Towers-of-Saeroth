# Pirate's Pet  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

---

An animal has become your loyal ally, perhaps after you liberated it from another ship or found it on a remote island. You gain the [[Feats/Pet]] general feat. Your pet gains an additional pet Shackles Options ability, which you can choose from the standard pet abilities or the following familiar abilities: [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Accompanist (Player Core)|accompanist]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Damage Avoidance (Player Core)|damage avoidance]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Independent (Player Core)|independent]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Partner in Crime (Player Core)|partner in crime]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech (Player Core)|speech]], and [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Valet (Player Core)|valet]].

In addition, you learn a common 1st- or 2nd-rank spell that targets your familiar, and can cast it once per day as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]]. You can instead learn [[Spells/Phase Familiar (Player Core)|_phase familiar_]] and can cast it once per hour as a primal innate spell; it’s not a focus spell for you. When you cast the chosen spell, you target your pirate’s pet instead of a familiar.
