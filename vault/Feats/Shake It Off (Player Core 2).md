# Shake It Off single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 78

---

You concentrate on your rage, overcoming fear and fighting back sickness. Reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition value by 1, and attempt a Fortitude save to recover from the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition as if you had spent an action retching; you reduce your sickened condition value by 1 on a failure (but not on a critical failure), by 2 on a success, or by 3 on a critical success.
