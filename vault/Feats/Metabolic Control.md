# Metabolic Control  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 51

---

You're able to better control your body's metabolism to preserve energy. After eating a proper meal, you can go without food and water for 1 week before taking damage from [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starvation and thirst]]. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] during this week.
