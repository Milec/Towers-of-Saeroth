# Forge-Day's Rest  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 20

**Access**
underground dwarf ethnicity

---

Your unusual rest cycle allows you to recover faster. As long as you rest for 12 hours, you gain the effects of the [[Feats/Fast Recovery]] general feat and you can go 20 hours without resting before becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].
