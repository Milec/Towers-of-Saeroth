# Pressure Change single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 107

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Unclear if this ability frees a Grabbed creature._

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marine Marauder Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]], are underwater, and have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You sink or rise rapidly alongside your target, altering their internal pressure. You [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to your swim Speed either up or down, and the required creature moves with you and must attempt a Fortitude save. For this movement, swimming up or down isn’t [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].

**Success** The target is unaffected  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is sickened 2.
