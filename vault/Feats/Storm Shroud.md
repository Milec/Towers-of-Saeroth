# Storm Shroud two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

You become surrounded by a swirling storm of rain, sand, snow, or clouds, whichever of those suits your bound terrain. This activity has traits appropriate to the storm you create— typically [[Traits/Earth|earth]] for sand and [[Traits/Water|water]] for rain, snow, or clouds. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] for 1 minute. You can't use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]], as normal for concealment that makes your position obvious.
