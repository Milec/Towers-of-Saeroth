# Weakening Assault two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 47

---

With a barrage of blows, you diminish an enemy's strength. Strike an enemy affected by your [[Actions/Taunt (Battlecry!)|Taunt]] twice. If either Strike hits, the target is enfeebled 1 until the beginning of your next turn. If both Strikes hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 3 instead.
