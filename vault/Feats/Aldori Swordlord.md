# Aldori Swordlord  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]; you have taken the last name of Aldori, sworn to the Aldori swordpact, and demonstrated skill at swordplay by winning a duel using only a sword in the presence of an Aldori swordlord.

---

You are an Aldori swordlord. You can always obtain free room and board at the Aldori Academy for yourself and your allies.  You can use Dueling [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] instead of Diplomacy to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] provided that the target you are Making an Impression on would be aware of the Aldori swordlords or your Request is related to your standing as a swordlord. Whenever you successfully Strike a creature with an [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]], you gain 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] (or 10 temporary Hit Point on a critical success) that last for 1 round.

**Special** You can’t take both this feat and [[Feats/Swordlord Exile]].
