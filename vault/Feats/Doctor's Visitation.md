# Doctor's Visitation single action or two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Medic Dedication]]

---

You move to provide immediate care to those who need it. Stride, then use one of the following: [[Feats/Battle Medicine]] or [[Actions/Treat Poison]]. You can spend a second action to instead Stride and then [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] or [[Feats/Treat Condition|Treat a Condition]] (if you have it).
