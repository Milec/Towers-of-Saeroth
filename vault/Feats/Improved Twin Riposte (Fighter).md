# Improved Twin Riposte (Fighter)  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 153

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Avenger]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twin Riposte]]

---

Your weapons are a blur, blocking and biting at your foes. At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to perform a Twin Riposte. You can use this extra reaction even if you are not benefiting from Twin Parry.
