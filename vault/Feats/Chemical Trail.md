# Chemical Trail  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

---

You can leave a faint chemical trail as you move and can use the chemical trails of other surkis to navigate. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all checks and saves to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or reorient yourself.
