# Lead Climber  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 207

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

When climbing, you can prepare routes for others to follow, and you can pull your allies up to avoid disaster. When your allies attempt to [[Actions/Climb]] a route you set using the [[Actions/Follow the Expert]] exploration activity, if any of them critically fail their checks to Climb, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the same DC. If you succeed, your ally fails instead of critically failing. If you also critically fail, you both experience the consequences of the critical failure.
