# Lethal Edge free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spectral Dagger]]

**Trigger**
Your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your _spectral dagger_ hits a [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] creature and deals [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage.

---

When the terrified are struck by your spectral weapon, they feel the anguish of every life taken by _Silent Lenore_. You apply one of the following effects, which lasts until the end of your next turn.

- The target can’t use reactions.
- The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1.
