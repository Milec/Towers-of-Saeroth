# Improved Poison Weapon  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue (Player Core)|Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 173

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Assassin]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Poisoner]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Assassin (Player Core 2)|Assassin]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Poison Weapon (Player Core)|Poison Weapon]]

---

You deliver poisons in ways that maximize their harmful effects. When you apply a simple poison with Poison Weapon, the poison deals 2d4 poison damage instead of 1d4 poison damage. You don’t waste a poison you apply with Poison Weapon on a critically failed attack roll.
