# Devastating Manifestation two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Soulforger (Impossible Magic)|Soulforger]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Soulforger Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Soulforger Dedication]]

**Requirements**
At least one of your soulforged armaments is not manifested.

---

Your armaments are especially powerful the moment after you manifest them. You Manifest Soulforged Armament. If you manifested a weapon, make up to two [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with that weapon, each targeting a different creature and each dealing an additional 1d6 spirit damage. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but your penalty doesn’t increase until you have made all your attacks. If you Manifested an armor or shield, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to all damage until the start of your next turn. If you manifested both a weapon and an armor or shield, you gain both effects.
