# Disarming Twist single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter (Player Core)|Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 147

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Duelist]] (Level 12), [[Archetypes/Duelist (Player Core 2)|Duelist]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a single one-handed melee weapon and hold nothing else in your hands

---

After your initial attack redirects your foe’s defenses, your follow-up wrests their weapon from their grasp. Make a melee Strike with a one-handed melee weapon. In addition to its other effects, this Strike gains the success and critical success effects of the [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] action. The Strike also has the following failure effect.  

**Failure** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your current turn.

**Special** If you’re in Disarming Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the attack roll.
