# Eerie Environs  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

The natural world can be scary to those not used to it—and you make it scarier still. If you're [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from a creature, you can attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] it without losing your hidden condition— imitating the sounds of strange beasts or causing the foliage to rustle menacingly. When you do so, you don't take a penalty to your check if the target doesn't understand your language.
