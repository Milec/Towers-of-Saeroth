# Sever the Dreaming Shadow  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 75

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Inherit the Dreaming Heirloom]]

---

As you whisper to your pusaka, it attempts to cut away an enemy’s shadow, leaving them in an eternal sleep. Your pusaka gains the following Activation.

**Activate—Cut the Shadow** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your pusaka becomes a [[Equipment/Weapons/Kris|kris]] made of darkness and drives itself into an adjacent enemy’s shadow. That enemy takes 80 mental damage with a basic Fortitude save against your spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher. If this reduces the creature to 0 Hit Points, the target doesn’t die, but its shadow is severed from its body, becoming a [[Bestiary/Shadow (Monster Core)|shadow spawn]] that attempts to flee from the target’s body. As long as the shadow spawn exists, the target remains in a dreamless slumber; if destroyed, the target regains its shadow and awakens (if its body was kept properly while [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]]).
