# Spirit Strikes  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

Your connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] empowers you to bring death to all forms of life and unlife, exploiting their weaknesses. All your weapon and unarmed Strikes are [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and deal 1 additional negative damage to living creatures and 1 additional positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]].
