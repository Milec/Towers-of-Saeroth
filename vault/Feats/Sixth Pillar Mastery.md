# Sixth Pillar Mastery  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Fists of the Ruby Phoenix Hardcover]] pg. 223, [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sixth Pillar]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sixth Pillar Dedication]]

---

You align your magical and physical potential. Whenever you succeed at an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike against an opponent, they have a -1 status penalty to saves against your spells until the start of your next turn. Whenever you cast a spell using spell slots, you gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls with your unarmed Strikes until the start of your next turn.
