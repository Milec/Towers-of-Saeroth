# Surprising Leap two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 83

---

Your eidolon jumps into the air to attack a foe. Your eidolon makes a [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] and attempts one melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] at any point during that jump. Immediately after the Strike, your eidolon falls to the ground if they’re in the air, even if they haven’t reached the maximum distance of their jump. If the distance your eidolon falls is no more than the height of their jump, your eidolon takes no damage and lands upright.

When attempting a High Jump or Long Jump during a Surprising Leap, determine the jump distance using Long Jump’s rules, and change your eidolon’s maximum distance to double their Speed.
