**Prerequisites**

---

Even when overpowered, Eagle Knights hold out hope. If your next action is to use Commitment to Equality, you roll the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check twice and take the higher result. If you succeed, the target also gains 25 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 minute.
