# Nature's Precision  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 66

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Beastmaster (Player Core 2)|Beastmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beastmaster Dedication]]; animal companion with an [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] unarmed attack

---

Your companions pick at an enemy's weak points. When your companion strikes a [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature with an [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack, it deals an additional 1d4 precision damage (2d4 if the animal companion is specialized). If the companion already deals precision damage, combine the precision damage.
