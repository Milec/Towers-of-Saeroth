# Animal Magic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beastkin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 80

---

Your shapeshifting grants you a magical connection to the animal world. You can cast [[Spells/Animal Messenger|_animal messenger_]], [[Spells/Calm Emotions|_calm emotions_]] (animals only), and [[Spells/Speak with Animals|_speak with animals_]] as 2nd-level primal innate spells once per day each. These spells use your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.
