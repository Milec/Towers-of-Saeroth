# Defy Death  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 50

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Orc Ferocity]]

---

You're exceptionally difficult to kill. The DC of your recovery checks is equal to 9 + your dying value, or 8 + your dying value if you have the [[Feats/Toughness]] general feat. In addition, whenever someone returns you to life using magic that would normally leave you debilitated for a week (such as [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]] or the [[Rituals/Resurrect|resurrect]] ritual), you don't suffer that condition.
