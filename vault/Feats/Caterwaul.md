# Caterwaul ⤾  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Catfolk]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 11

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
An ally within 30 feet would be reduced to 0 HP but not immediately killed.

---

You give a daring yowl in the face of adversity, calling your companion back from the brink of unconsciousness. Your ally isn't knocked out and remains at 1 Hit Point. Your ally's [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition still increases by 1 as though they had been [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] and recovered.
