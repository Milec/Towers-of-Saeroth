# Vicious Vengeance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 94

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Iniquity|iniquity cause]]

---

While your vengeance is painful for both you and your foe, none can doubt that your enemies suffer more. You gain a circumstance bonus to the damage you deal from [[Actions/Destructive Vengeance (Player Core 2)|Destructive Vengeance]] equal to the number of damage dice from the reaction. This applies to only the damage the enemy takes, not the damage to yourself or the extra damage you gain after using the reaction.
