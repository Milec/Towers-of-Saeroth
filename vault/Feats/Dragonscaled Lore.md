# Dragonscaled Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 214

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Dragonscaled Kobold (Player Core 2)|Dragonscaled Kobold]] heritage

---

The study of dragons is not merely academic to you but part of your family tree. Tracking lineages, benefactors, and communities has become second nature. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] and your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]. For each of these skills in which you were already trained, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Dragon Lore]].
