# Weighty Impact  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
Your eidolon has an unarmed attack with the [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]] or [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait.

---

Your eidolon hurls enemies to the ground or across the battlefield. Choose one of the eidolon’s unarmed attacks that has the [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]] or [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait. The attack gains the other trait as well, and your eidolon gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Knockdown (Rage of Elements)|Knockdown]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Push (Rage of Elements)|Push]] actions for that attack.
