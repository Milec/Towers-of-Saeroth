# Skeletal Resistance  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Skeleton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 57

---

Your body becomes resistant, your hardened bones deflecting weapons and other forms of damage. You gain resistance 2 to [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], piercing, and slashing damage. This resistance increases to 3 at 13th level and 4 at 17th level.
