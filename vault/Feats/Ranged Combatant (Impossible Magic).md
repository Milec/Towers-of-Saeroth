# Ranged Combatant  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 79

---

Spines, flame jets, and holy blasts are just some of the ways your eidolon might strike from a distance. Your eidolon gains a ranged unarmed attack with a range increment of 30 feet that deals 1d4 damage and has the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] and [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]] traits. When you select this feat, choose a damage type for the attack: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], bludgeoning, [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], piercing, slashing, [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]].
