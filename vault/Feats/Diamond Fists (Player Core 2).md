# Diamond Fists  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 127

---

Your body hardens as you combine your attacks, making your finishing blows more damaging. Your unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]] and [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]] traits. Any that already had one or both of these traits increase their weapon damage dice by one step.
