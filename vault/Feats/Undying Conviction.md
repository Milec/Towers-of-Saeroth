# Undying Conviction  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 33

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Reanimator]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
able to create or control [[Traits/Undead|undead]]; [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]] with a negative font, [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-335)|oracle]] of [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Bones|bones]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Necromancy|necromancer]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizard]]

---

Your presence has a bolstering effect on your undead [[Traits/Minion|minions]]. Undead creatures under your control and within a 30-foot emanation around you gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws to resist [[Traits/Positive|positive]] damage and to Will saving throws to resist effects that would make them [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]].

**Special** This feat has the trait matching your spellcasting tradition.
