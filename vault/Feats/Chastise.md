# Chastise two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 280

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fear of God]]

---

With but a mere word from your deity, you exploit a foe’s fear to see their inner being brought low. You chastise the spirit of a foe within 30 feet that has the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition. The target must attempt a Will saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent spirit damage]]. The first time the creature fails their [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to recover from this, the persistent spirit damage doubles.  

**Failure** The target takes 4 persistent spirit damage. The first time the creature fails their flat check to recover from this, the persistent spirit damage doubles.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes 6 persistent spirit damage. The first time the creature fails their flat check to recover from this, the persistent spirit damage doubles.
