# Tupilaq Carver  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 13

**Prerequisites**
You have a spellcasting class feature with the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] tradition

**Access**
Erutaki ethnicity

---

You know the truth behind old stories that tell of sending a fetish of bone and sinew to seek vengeance. These old magics allow you to conjure constructs with ease. Add the [[Spells/Summon Construct|_summon construct_]] spell to your spell list. The constructs you summon have a distinct ivory scrimshaw appearance, and if you include a drop of blood, lock of hair, or other portion of a creature’s body as part of the spell’s material component, the summoned construct gains a +4 status bonus to Perception checks to sense or locate that creature.
