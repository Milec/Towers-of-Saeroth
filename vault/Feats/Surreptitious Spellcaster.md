# Surreptitious Spellcaster  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 114

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellmaster Dedication]]

---

You’ve learned that it pays for a Pathfinder to be able to cast spells without being noticed. You gain the [[Feats/Conceal Spell]] wizard class feat, though it’s not a wizard feat for you. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to Conceal a Spell and to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to conceal verbal components.
