# Statue single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stonebound]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stonebound Dedication]]

---

Your body and equipment appear to be made of stone as you hold perfectly still. Until the next time you act, you appear to be a statue and do not need to eat, drink, or breathe, but you are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]]. Your [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] DC to pass as a statue is equal to 10 + your proficiency bonus (do not apply any other bonuses, penalties, or modifiers).
