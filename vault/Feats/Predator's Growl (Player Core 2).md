# Predator's Growl ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 11

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Trigger**
You successfully [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] creature.

---

You give a throaty growl to attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature you just found. You don't take the –4 penalty for not sharing a language with the creature.
