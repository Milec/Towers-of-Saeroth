# Made for Combat  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 48

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tsukumogami Poppet]] heritage

---

You can use your body as a deadly weapon. You gain one of the following melee unarmed attacks, which must be one that matches your body (for instance, tsukumogami wrap for a tsukumogami that’s a bolt of cotton).  
• A blade unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing, has the [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile]] P trait, and is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]] weapon group.  
• A spoke unarmed attack that deals 1d4 piercing, has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] traits, and is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]] weapon group.  
• A wrap unarmed attack that deals 1d4 bludgeoning, has the [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] traits, and is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail|flail]] weapon group.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into or out of this feat.
