# Spell Swallow  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 71

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ostilli Host Dedication]]

---

Your ostilli has grown strong enough to completely consume a spell cast at you, granting you the Devour Ambient Magic action.

 **Devour Ambient Magic** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature Casts a Spell with you as the only target; **Effect** Your ostilli radiates cyan light as its tentacle-like filters attempt to consume the magical effect. You can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell with an [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check and a counteract rank equal to half your level.
