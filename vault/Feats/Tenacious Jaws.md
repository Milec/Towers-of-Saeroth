# Tenacious Jaws  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 216

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Aspect]]; [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|jaws unarmed attack]]

---

Your teeth are curved or hooked to hold on to your enemies. Your jaws unarmed attack gains the [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]] trait. When you use your [[Feats/Breath of the Dragon (Player Core 2)|Breath of the Dragon]], an opponent [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] in your jaws uses the outcome one degree of success worse than the result of their saving throw.
