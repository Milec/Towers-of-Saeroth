# Prepare Papers  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Alkenstar Agent]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Alkenstar Agent Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You're practiced at creating forgeries that establish your outlaw credentials or augment your legal authority. You can [[Actions/Create Forgery|Create a Forgery]] as an [[Traits/Exploration|exploration]] activity. It takes you about 1 hour per page of the document that you're forging. This time is reduced to 10 minutes per page if you're legendary in Society.
