# Telling Blow single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

---

Your determination empowers a telling blow to defeat your most powerful enemies. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. Make a Strike at mythic proficiency. If the Strike hits, you deal an extra die of weapon damage. You can spend additional Mythic Points after determining the outcome of the attack to deal one more die of weapon damage per extra Mythic Point spent. If the target of your attack is your nemesis or an ally of your nemesis, you gain two die of weapon damage per extra Mythic Point spent.

If you have no Mythic Points remaining after using this ability, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition.
