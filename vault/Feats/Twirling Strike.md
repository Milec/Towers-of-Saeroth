# Twirling Strike two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]; [[Feats/Twirl Through]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a fan.

---

Your fans, one raised up alongside your head and the other alongside your hip, become a blur as you twirl across the battlefield. Attempt to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] an enemy’s space using [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]; on a success, you can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the enemy with a fan you’re wielding at any point during the movement. On a critical success, the enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against this attack.
