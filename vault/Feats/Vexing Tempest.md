# Vexing Tempest single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 117

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]; [[Feats/Tempest Cloud's Speed]]

---

Your familiar channels elemental air around it to unleash a disruptive gust that sends creatures flying. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] trait, all creatures within a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of your familiar must attempt a Reflex save against your spell DC or be pushed 10 feet away. On a critical failure, they’re also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
