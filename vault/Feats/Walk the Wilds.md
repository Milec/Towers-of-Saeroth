# Walk the Wilds  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 24

---

You know the ways of birds and beasts and have gained the right to wear their forms. You add [[Spells/Animal Form (Player Core)|_animal form_]] to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting.

**Special** If you cast the [[Spells/Darkened Forest Form|_darkened forest form_]] focus spell to turn into a form granted by animal form, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] it up to 5 minutes instead of its normal duration.
