# Claws of the Dragon  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 168

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Instinct of the Wyrm|Dragon Disciple]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragon Disciple Dedication]]

---

Your fingernails grow into razor-sharp claws, and scales speckle your fingers, hands, and wrists. Your claws are [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks that deal 1d6 slashing damage and are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group.

 If you're a draconic sorcerer, when you cast [[Spells/Dragon Claws|_dragon claws_]], increase the spell's slashing damage die from d4 to d6 and increase the resistance to 10 at 1st level, 15 at 5th level, and 20 at 9th level.
