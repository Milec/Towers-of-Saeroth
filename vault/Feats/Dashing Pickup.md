# Dashing Pickup single action to three actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 70

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]; expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] to order your mount to Stride (or to Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim if it has the corresponding movement type). At any point during this movement, it must move adjacent to you. As it passes you, you [[Actions/Mount (Player Core)|Mount]] your mount. You can increase the number of actions you use with Dashing Pickup to 2 to make your mount Stride twice, or to 3 to make your mount Stride four times; you must use enough actions to ensure your mount is able to move adjacent to you at some point during this movement, and you cannot use this activity if it is not possible for your mount to do so.
