# Speak with Days Long Dead  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 38

---

You channel the essences and echoes of spirits long dead that reside within a location, an object, or a bloodline. You spend 10 minutes and must be within the location, be able to touch a piece of the object, or drink a drop of blood of the bloodline as appropriate to the spirits you wish to channel. You learn one piece of information or about one significant event relating directly to the location, object, or bloodline that has occurred within the last year. You can channel any given target only once every 24 hours.

At 12th level, you can learn about information or events that took place within the last decade, within the last century if you are 16th level, or at any point in history at 20th level.
