You’re a living weapon honed to destroy [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]]. Whenever you critically Strike a fiend, your attack deals an extra weapon damage die, and the fiend is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 until the beginning of your next turn.
