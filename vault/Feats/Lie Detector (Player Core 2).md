# Lie Detector  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 108

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Empiricism (Player Core 2)|empiricism]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Interrogation (Player Core 2)|interrogation]] methodology

---

You're adept at noticing the tells of a liar—sweat, flushing, a quavering voice, a quickening pulse. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] and to Perception DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you. When you determine someone is lying to you, you can use their deceit to your advantage to gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the next [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check you attempt against that creature within the next minute.

 If you would gain your [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]] investigation bonus to any of the above checks, that bonus increases by 1 instead of you gaining the +1 bonus listed.
