# Reactive Transformation ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]; [[Feats/Dragon Shape]], [[Feats/Elemental Shape]], [[Feats/Plant Shape]], or [[Feats/Soaring Shape]]

**Trigger**
varies

---

You transform reflexively when in danger. You cast [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] to transform into one form granted by one of the prerequisite feats you have, depending on the trigger. Your form's resistances and weaknesses apply against the triggering damage.

- **Trigger** You fall 10 feet or more; **Effect** Choose a form from [[Spells/Aerial Form|_aerial form_]].
- **Trigger** You take acid, cold, electricity, fire, or poison damage; **Effect** Choose a form from [[Spells/Dragon Form|_dragon form_]] that resists the triggering damage.
- **Trigger** You take fire damage; **Effect** Choose a fire elemental form from [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]].
- **Trigger** You take poison damage; **Effect** Choose a form from [[Spells/Plant Form|_plant form_]].
