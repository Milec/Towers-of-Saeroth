# Suli Amir  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Suli]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 111

You have power connected to the powerful jann amirs. You can cast [[Spells/Read Omens|_read omens_]] and 4th-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] each once per day as arcane innate spells, and you can cast [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] at will as an arcane innate cantrip, heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
