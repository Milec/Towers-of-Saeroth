# Aeon Resonance  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 116

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The Aeon Resonance feat acts as if an aeon stone was embedded in a wayfinder, and thus would not allow a second aeon stone to provide its resonance power from being embedded in a wayfinder._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runelord (Rival Academies)|Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Embed Aeon Stone (Rival Academies)|Embed Aeon Stone]]

---

You gain the resonant power of one embedded aeon stone as if it were placed in a [[Equipment/Wayfinder (GM Core)|wayfinder]]. While you can embed multiple _[[Equipment/Aeon Stone (GM Core)|aeon stones]]_ in your flesh, you can gain the resonance power from only one embedded stone at a time, selected each day when you make your daily preparations.

**Special** At 8th level, you can take this feat again. If you do, you gain the resonance powers of up to four invested aeon stones instead of only one.
