# Toughness  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 268

---

You can withstand more punishment than most before succumbing. Increase your maximum Hit Points by your level. The DC of [[Rules/Recovery Checks|recovery checks]] is equal to 9 + your [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition value.
