# Cutting Rebuke  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 82

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Demagogue's Calling|demagogue's calling]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Thespian's Calling|thespian's calling]]

---

If words could kill, the poet might rule the world. Your words can. You gain the [[Feats/Bon Mot (Player Core 2)|Bon Mot]] skill feat. You can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] when rolling the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check for Bon Mot to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. If the check succeeds, you can also deal mental damage equal to your level to the target (double on a critical success).
