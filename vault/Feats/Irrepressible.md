# Irrepressible  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 53

---

You are easily able to ward off attempts to play on your fears and emotions. When you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect, you get a critical success instead. If your heritage is [[Ancestries/Heritages/Gutsy Halfling|gutsy halfling]], when you roll a critical failure on a saving throw against an emotion effect, you get a failure instead.
