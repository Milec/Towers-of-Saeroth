# Dual Thrower  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication]]

---

You know how to throw two weapons as easily as strike with them. Whenever a dual-weapon warrior feat allows you to make a melee Strike, you can instead make a ranged Strike with a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon or a one-handed ranged weapon you are wielding. Any effects from these feats that apply to one-handed melee weapons or melee Strikes also apply to one-handed ranged weapons and ranged Strikes.
