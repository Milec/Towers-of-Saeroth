# True Perception  *Feat 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 209

**Prerequisites**
legendary in Perception

---

Your perceptive abilities and ability to process sensory information are so far beyond the pale that you notice minute discrepancies in all sorts of [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]] and [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutations]]. You are constantly under the effects of a 6th-level [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]] spell, using your Perception modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check.
