# Scamper Underfoot  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shoony]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 72

---

You are used to ducking under foes and can scurry around the battlefield with ease. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] the spaces of Medium or larger enemies, and the presence of an enemy doesn’t make the squares difficult terrain.
