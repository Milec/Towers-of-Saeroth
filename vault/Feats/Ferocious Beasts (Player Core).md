# Ferocious Beasts  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 73

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bonded Animal (Player Core)|Bonded Animal]], animal companion, or [[Feats/Pet]]; [[Feats/Orc Ferocity (Player Core)|Orc Ferocity]]

---

Since ancient times, the mightiest orc beast tamers would draw out the true fighting spirit of their companion beasts by feeding the creatures a draft incorporating the orc’s own blood. All [[Rules/Animal Companions|animal companions]], pets, [[Rules/Familiars|familiars]], and bonded animals you have gain the [[Feats/Orc Ferocity]] feat, and gain one reaction per round they can use only for Orc Ferocity. If you have the [[Feats/Undying Ferocity]] ancestry feat, all animal companions, pets, familiars, and bonded animals you have also gain the benefits of that feat when using the Orc Ferocity reaction.
