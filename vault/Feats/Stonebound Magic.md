# Stonebound Magic  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stonebound]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stonebound Dedication]]

---

You've learned to manifest your connection to stone by conjuring it to your side. You gain [[Spells/Scatter Scree|_scatter scree_]] as a primal innate cantrip. At 8th level, you gain [[Spells/Meld into Stone|_meld into stone_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. At 10th level, you gain [[Spells/Shape Stone|_shape stone_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. At 12th level, you gain [[Spells/Wall of Stone|_wall of stone_]] as a primal innate spell once per day.
