# Powerful Shove  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aggressive Block]] or [[Feats/Brutish Shove]]

---

You can push larger foes around with your attack. You can use Aggressive Block or Brutish Shove against a creature up to two sizes larger than you.

 When a creature you [[Actions/Shove]] has to stop moving because it would hit an object, it takes damage equal to your Strength modifier (minimum 1). This happens regardless of how you Shoved the creature.
