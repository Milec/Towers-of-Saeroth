For the purposes of [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracting]] effects with invigorating elixirs, treat your level as though it were 2 higher. Add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]]. You can also choose a [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] regardless of what, if any, conditions it imposes.
