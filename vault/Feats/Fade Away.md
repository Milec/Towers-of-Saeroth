# Fade Away  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Easily Dismissed]]

---

Your ability to blend into the background allows you to fade away entirely or appear innocuous even to magical effects. You gain [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] and [[Spells/Misdirection|_misdirection_]] as 2nd-level innate occult spells. You can target only yourself with _invisibility_, and you must be the primary target of _misdirection_. You can cast each spell once per day.
