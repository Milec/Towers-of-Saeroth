# Resilient Chassis ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Defensive Automaton|defensive automaton heritage]]

**Trigger**
You would take damage.

---

You are tough enough to draw on your own internal stores of energy when you get injured. You gain 4 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 round.

At 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the temporary Hit Points increase by 4.

**Enhancement** Resilient Chassis’s frequency becomes once per 10 minutes.
