# Signature Weapon  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 86

---

Your energy saturates one of your weapons; it might be one inherited or one tied to your history. Pick a weapon and give it a name; this weapon gains the [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|unique]] trait. You gain access to its [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect. You can spend one action saying the name of your weapon, summoning it into your empty hand if the weapon is unattended and within 1 mile; this is an [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] effect.

If your signature weapon is destroyed, you can spend three days and three nights saturating a new weapon with your energy, creating a new signature weapon.
