# Sun's Fury  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 101

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bright Lion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bright Lion Dedication]]

---

The [[Setting/Deity Categories/Old Sun Gods]] have granted you a gift in your fight against [[Setting/Deities/Walkena]]. You gain the [[Spells/Sun's Fury|_sun's fury_]] focus spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can regain using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity to pray to the Old Sun Gods; if you already have a focus pool, increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. Your Bright Lion focus spells are divine spells; when you gain this feat, if you aren't already, you become trained in divine spell attacks and spell DCs, using Charisma as your spellcasting ability score.
