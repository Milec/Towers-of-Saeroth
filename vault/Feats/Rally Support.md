# Rally Support  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Guerrilla Dedication]]; trained in either [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

You often rely on the local populace for supplies and shelter. Once per day, you can attempt a check to [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] or [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] against an easy DC for your level when speaking with  someone who is a non-hostile, long-term resident of your favored location. On a success, choose one of the following to obtain.

- A nonmagical item of your level or lower
- A safe place to shelter for 24 hours
- 3 days' worth of food and water for you and up to 5 allies
