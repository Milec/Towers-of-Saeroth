# Four-armed Aspect single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 80

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You intone vows to shoulder others’ pains; in response, your torso sprouts an additional pair of arms to help you bear those burdens. For the following minute, you can carry 3 more Bulk than normal before becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]] and up to a maximum of 6 more Bulk. You can also use these arms as free hands to perform only the following actions or activities: [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]]. After this minute or when you become [[Rules/Unconscious|unconscious]], whichever comes first, these additional arms fade away.
