# Eerie Flicker ⤾  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 11, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 102

**Trigger**
A enemy's Strike would hit you and you weren't already [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by that enemy.

---

Your body flickers momentarily into the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]]. You become concealed for 1 round, and the flat check for concealment applies to the Strike that would have hit you. If the flat check fails, the Strike misses you.
