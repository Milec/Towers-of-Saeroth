# Summon Wood Elemental  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 49

---

Your connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] allows you to summon elemental allies. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]] as a 5th-rank primal innate spell, but the elemental must be a [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Wood|wood elemental]].
