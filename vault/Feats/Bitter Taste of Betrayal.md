# Bitter Taste of Betrayal  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

---

Spend enough time in Hell Harbor, and you pick up the paranoia encouraged by Arronax Endymion. Maybe you served under him on the _Tyrannous_, escaped his executioners, or had a friend who wasn’t so lucky; whatever the reason, you have some of his wariness. Unless you’re a willing target, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and saves against creatures you consider an ally and creatures who are [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by you. If a creature you thought was an ally reveals itself as an enemy, you get the same bonus against them until the end of your next turn.
