# Fey Transcendence  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 128

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fey Influence]]

---

You become a powerful fey creature, gaining strengths tied to the [[Setting/Planes/First World]] but also some of the weakness against which all fey must contend. You gain a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], and [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] effects, and you can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of [[Traits/Animal|animals]] and [[Traits/Plant|plants]]. You regain twice the number of Hit Points when resting in a natural wooded area, such as a forest or jungle. You have weakness to [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] equal to half your level.

**Special** This feat gains the trait appropriate for your ancestry ([[Traits/Human|human]] for [[Ancestries/Human|human]], [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] for [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]], etc.).
