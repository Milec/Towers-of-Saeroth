# Reflect Harm  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 156

---

You can wrap your magic around you like a cloak that causes those who dare to target you with spells to suffer a similar fate. You know the following blood magic effect.  
**Blood Magic—Reflect Harm** Your blood ensures that those who harm you with magic are harmed in return. The first time you take damage from a spell before the beginning of your next turn, attempt spell [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] roll against the creature who cast the triggering spell. On a hit, the creature takes the same amount and type of damage that you just took. If you critically hit, the creature takes twice the damage.
