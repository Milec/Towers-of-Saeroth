# Suplex single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

---

Flexing your entire body, you heave your opponent over your head and slam them into the ground. Make an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee Strike against the creature you have grabbed or restrained; on a success, the target lands [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], and on a critical success, the target lands prone and takes an additional 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Regardless of whether the Strike is successful, you immediately release your hold on the target.
