# Sleeper Hold single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 124

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You pinch crucial points of your target's nervous system, impeding its ability to function. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature, with the following success and critical success effects instead of the usual effects.

**Critical Success** The target falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] for 1 minute, though it remains standing and doesn't drop what it holds.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] until the end of its next turn.
