# Visual Fidelity  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 28

---

You’ve found a way to use a hodgepodge combination of devices to enhance your visual abilities in every situation. You gain darkvision and [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], and you can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures and objects as translucent shapes, though these shapes are indistinct enough to be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to you.

If an effect would give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] condition, the effect must attempt a counteract check against your class DC, with your counteract level equaling half your level, rounded up. On a failed counteract check, you aren’t blinded—your various devices are able to compensate.
