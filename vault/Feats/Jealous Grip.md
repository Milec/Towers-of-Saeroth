# Jealous Grip ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 212

**Trigger**
A creature in your reach that you’re aware of attempts to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] you, or damage or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] an item in your possession.

---

You are protective of your hoard, and woe betide anyone who tries to take what’s yours. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the triggering creature. If your attack is a critical hit and the triggering action was a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action, you disrupt that action. This Strike doesn’t count toward your multiple attack penalty, and your multiple attack penalty doesn’t apply to this Strike.
