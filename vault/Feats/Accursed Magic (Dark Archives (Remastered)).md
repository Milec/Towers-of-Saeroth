# Accursed Magic  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 169

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Manifestations (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Curse Maelstrom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Curse Maelstrom Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Curse Maelstrom Dedication]]

---

When the curse within you spills out, you can lay curses on others and claim them for yourself with ease. You can cast [[Spells/Claim Curse|_claim curse_]]. At 10th level, you can also cast [[Spells/Seal Fate (Player Core 2)|_seal fate_]] (Player Core 2 251), and at 12th level, you can also cast [[Spells/Inevitable Disaster|_inevitable disaster_]] . You can cast these spells once per day as occult innate spells, but only while within your curse maelstrom state. If you couldn't already cast occult spells, these spells use Wisdom as your spellcasting attribute, and you become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics.
