# Propelling Sorcery  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 153

---

The force of your magic can be used to propel yourself or another. You know the following blood magic effect.  
**Blood Magic—Propelling Sorcery** You channel your magic outward into a rush of movement. Either you [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action or [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] the target 5 feet in a direction of your choice.
