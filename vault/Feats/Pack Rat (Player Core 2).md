# Pack Rat  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 30

---

You've learned how to cram vast quantities into small spaces. You can fit an additional 50% of the listed Bulk capacity into mundane storage containers or vehicles. For example, you can fit 6 Bulk in a [[Equipment/Backpack (Player Core)|backpack]], or 12 Bulk in a [[Equipment/Chest (Player Core)|chest]]. This doesn't alter the items' Bulk, nor does it change how much you can store in a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] or [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|extradimensional]] storage space, such as a [[Equipment/Spacious Pouch|_spacious pouch_]].
