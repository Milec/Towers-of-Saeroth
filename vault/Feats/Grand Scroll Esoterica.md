# Grand Scroll Esoterica  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elaborate Scroll Esoterica]]

---

You've completed the third and final step in your assimilation of scroll esoterica, granting you daily [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|scrolls]] of incredible power. In addition to your daily scrolls from [[Feats/Scroll Esoterica]] and Elaborate Scroll Esoterica, add a single scroll with a 6th-level spell. At 20th level, add a scroll with a 7th-level spell.
