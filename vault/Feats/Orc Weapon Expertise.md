# Orc Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 59

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Orc Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your orc affinity blends with your class training, granting you great skill with orc weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion|falchion]], the [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe|greataxe]], and all [[Traits/Orc|orc]] weapons in which you are trained.
