# Think Twice ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Trigger**
A creature that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] targets you with an attack.

---

You make a sudden threat toward a creature about to attack you that makes them flinch, throwing off their aim. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. This reaction can’t be triggered by the same creature again for 1 day.

**Special** If you have [[Feats/Intimidating Glare (Player Core)|Intimidating Glare]], when you use this feat, you can have it lose the auditory trait and gain the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait.
