# Swamp Stealth single action  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Requirements**
You are in a [[Rules/Swamp (GM Core)|swamp]] environment near an environmental feature that would allow you to [[Actions/Take Cover (Player Core)|Take Cover]].

---

In swamplands, you are particularly skilled at quickly hiding behind bits of underbrush or foliage, or moving quietly through murky water. You Take Cover and then use that cover to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]].
