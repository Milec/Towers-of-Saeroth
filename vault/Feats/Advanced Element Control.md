You gain one [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|kineticist feat]]. For the purpose of meeting its prerequisites, your kineticist level is equal to half your level. Your options include [[Traits/Impulse|impulse]] feats for your kinetic element, as normal for a kineticist.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once. Each time you select it, you gain another kineticist feat.
