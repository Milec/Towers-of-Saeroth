# Positioning Assault two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 149

**Requirements**
You are wielding a two-handed melee weapon and your target is within your reach.

---

With punishing blows, you force your opponent into position. Make a Strike with the required weapon. If you hit, you move the target 5 feet into a space in your reach. This follows the [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]] rules.
