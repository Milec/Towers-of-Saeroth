# Improved Hijack Undead  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clockwork Reanimator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clockwork Reanimator Dedication]]; [[Feats/Hijack Undead]]

---

Undead creatures find it all but impossible to resist your commands. When you use Hijack Undead, if the undead fails its save, it becomes your [[Traits/Minion|minion]] for 10 minutes. If it critically fails, it becomes your minion for 24 hours.
