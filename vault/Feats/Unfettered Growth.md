# Unfettered Growth  *Feat 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 70

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

---

By tapping into the limitless power of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]], you can experience sudden, dramatic growth for a short time. Select one of the following benefits when you gain this feat. This choice is permanent and can’t be changed.

- You gain 2nd-rank [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]] as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]]. You can cast this spell twice per day and must target yourself. The spell’s duration is 30 minutes.
- You gain 4th-rank _enlarge_ as a primal innate spell. You can cast this spell once per day and must target yourself.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
