# Share Burden ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 169

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Manifestations|Curse Maelstrom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Curse Maelstrom Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally within 60 feet would be affected by a [[Traits/Curse|curse]] or [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect, and you wouldn't be affected but you are an eligible target for the effect.

---

You draw the misfortune or curse into yourself, potentially enabling you to enter your curse maelstrom state. Instead of affecting your ally, the triggering curse or misfortune effect affects you.
