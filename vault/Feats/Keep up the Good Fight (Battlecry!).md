# Keep up the Good Fight ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 48

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
An enemy reduces you to 0 Hit Points but doesn't kill you.

---

Your commitment to protecting others keeps you going, even  against insurmountable odds. Instead of being knocked out,  you're reduced to 1 Hit Point. You increase your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] value by 1 and gain a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level that last for 1 round.
