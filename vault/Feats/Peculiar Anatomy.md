# Peculiar Anatomy  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Disturbing Defense]]

---

Your circulatory system contains viscous protoplasmic fluid instead of ordinary blood. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|disease]] and [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and resistance to poison damage equal to half your level.

 In addition, your resistance to precision damage from Disturbing Defense increases to an amount equal to 7 + your number of class feats from the oozemorph archetype, and the DC of your flat checks to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] is 8 rather than 13.
