# Sculpt Shadows three actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Fetchling]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 87

---

You coax nearby shadows into the shape of a simple weapon or a simple tool or item, using a tiny piece of your shadow to make it solid; in doing so, you lose 1 Hit Point. You can create only level-0 common, non-consumable [weapons](Weapons.aspx), or [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|adventuring gear]] with no intricate parts, written text, or other complicated components. The item lasts until used for a single activity, until you Sculpt Shadows again, or for 1 minute, whichever comes first. Once the duration expires, the object dissipates, and you regain the lost Hit Point unless the item was [[Rules/Conditions/Broken]] or Destroyed. As the object holds a tiny sliver of your shadow, you can't recover the lost Hit Point until after the object dissipates.
