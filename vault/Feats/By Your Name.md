# By Your Name single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Requirements**
You must know the target’s name.

---

In a moment of inspiration, you understand the target perfectly, allowing you to craft a rune that draws effects to the target by their very name. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check against a [[Rules/Level-Based DCs (GM Core)|standard DC]] of the target creature’s level. Regardless of the outcome, you can’t attempt to use By Your Name against the target again for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** You understand the target’s runic name and temporarily learn how to write their name as a special diacritic rune. This diacritic rune magnifies the effects of the base rune when targeting the named creature. When the rune is invoked, the target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to their saving throw against the effects of the base rune, and if the base rune deals damage, it gains a status bonus to its damage equal to half your level. The inspiration used to understand this diacritic rune fades quickly, and you forget how to use the diacritic rune after 1 minute.  

**Success** As critical success, but the rune doesn’t grant a bonus to damage.
