# Castigating Weapon  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Holy Castigation]]

---

The force of your deity’s castigation strengthens your body so you can strike down the wicked. After you damage a fiend using a [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spell, your weapon or unarmed Strikes deal extra good damage to fiends equal to half the level of the _heal_ spell until the end of your next turn. This is cumulative with any good damage the weapon already deals (such as from a holy rune).
