# Catrina's Presence  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 30

---

You've adopted the calming presence of [[Bestiary/Catrina|catrinas]], the festive dead; you might have even danced with a catrina yourself! So long as you are visibly carrying fresh flowers or colorful ribbons somewhere on your person, you can cast [[Spells/Calm Emotions|_calm emotions_]] twice per day as a 2nd-level divine innate spell.
