# Mutate Weapon single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fleshwarp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 92

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Weapon]]

---

Select one unarmed attack you gained with Living Weapon. The damage die for that attack increases by one step until the end of your turn. In addition, choose to either have the attack gain a [[Traits/Reach|reach]] of 10 feet until the end of your turn or for you to gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls with the chosen unarmed attack until the end of your turn.
