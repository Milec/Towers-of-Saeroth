# Fierce Grasp single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 26

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Climbing Animal]] heritage

**Requirements**
You have an opponent [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Once you get your hands on someone, it's hard for them to get away. Your opponent takes a –2 circumstance penalty to their next attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] from being grabbed or restrained by you, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC against any attacks they make against you while you have them grabbed.
