# Repositioning Block free action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]]

**Trigger**
You used Shield Block to prevent damage from an adjacent creature’s attack.

---

As you absorb a blow from an enemy, you can use their attack's momentum against them. Attempt to [[Actions/Reposition]] the creature whose attack you used [[Bestiary/Abilities/Shield Block (Monster Core)|Shield Block]] against. You don't need to have a hand free to do so. You gain a +1 item bonus to the Athletics check if your shield is at least 4th level, a +2 item bonus if your shield is at least 10th level, and a +3 item bonus if your shield is at least 16th level.
