# Terrain Form single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

You crumble to pieces, becoming composed of the very terrain to which you're bound, with the effects of the [[Spells/Gaseous Form|_gaseous form_]] spell, or you reconstitute into your normal form. The substance you're formed of depends upon your bound terrain, and the action gains the corresponding trait or traits.


**Arctic** Snow ([[Traits/Water|water]])  
**Desert** Sand ([[Traits/Earth|earth]])  
**Mountain** Cloud ([[Traits/Water|water]])  
**Swamp** Mud ([[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Water|water]])
