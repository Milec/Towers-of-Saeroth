# Envenomed Edge  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 121

---

With a combination of your poison glands and herbal concoctions, you can consistently deliver venomous attacks when you hit an enemy's weak points. When you critically hit using a Strike with a weapon or unarmed attack and deal slashing or piercing damage with that Strike, you deal an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] to your target.
