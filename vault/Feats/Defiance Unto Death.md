# Defiance Unto Death  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 26

**Access**
Spiresworn elf

---

You loathe the [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu|alghollthus]] and their mind magic, and you’ve been trained to be willing to die rather than give into mental manipulation. If you would start your turn [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] due to a failed Will save, you can attempt a Will save against the same DC; on a success, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] until your next turn, rather than act against your will.
