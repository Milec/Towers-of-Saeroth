# Stave Off Catastrophe  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swordmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Shoulder Catastrophe]]

---

You fully protect your allies from the worst blows. When you use the [[Feats/Shoulder Catastrophe]] reaction, you can choose to take the full critical damage and effects from the attack, including effects that apply on a critical hit, and have your ally take no damage and avoid the attack's effects.
