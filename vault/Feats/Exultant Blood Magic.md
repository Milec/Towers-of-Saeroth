# Exultant Blood Magic  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bloodrager]]

**Prerequisites**
Surging Blood Magic; legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], depending on your chosen tradition

---

The magic in your blood sings an exultant song of battle. You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Player Core)|master spellcasting benefits]]. Increase the spell slots you gain from the bloodrager archetype feats by 1 for each spell rank. In addition, when you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from your repertoire and you are at least [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2, double the extra damage dealt by that spell from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] instead.
