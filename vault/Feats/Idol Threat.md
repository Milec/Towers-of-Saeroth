# Idol Threat single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 33

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beastbrood]]

**Requirements**
You're holding something sacred or precious to a creature, such as a relic or personal depiction of their deity or a precious personal memento.

---

You brandish the precious object you're holding menacingly, at once mocking it and threatening its safety. Attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the creature whose precious item you threaten. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to Demoralize, and on a success, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round out of distraction for the object's safety and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute (or stupefied 2 on a critical success). These conditions are both in addition to the normal effects of Demoralize. The creature is then temporarily immune to any Idol Threat using that specific object for 1 day.
