# Ten Lives  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Evade Doom]]

---

When your character would die, you can attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, you're instead reduced to 0 HP and a [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] value 1 less than would normally kill you (typically dying 3). This doesn't change the result for any other creature affected.
