# Necromantic Physiology  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

Your unusual physiology has developed in a way that makes it difficult for parasites and other infestations to prey upon you. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against diseases. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|battle hardened fighter class feature]]), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.
