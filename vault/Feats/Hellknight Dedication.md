# Hellknight Dedication  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 84

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Armiger Dedication]]; lawful alignment; member of a Hellknight Order; passed the Hellknight Test

---

You have forged your body to comply with the commands of your mind—orders steeped in the Measure and the Chain. You gain expert proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] (or in another skill in which you’re trained of your choice, if you were already an expert in Intimidation). You gain the armor specialization effects of Hellknight plate, and your resistance from that armor specialization is 1 higher than normal. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Intimidation checks while wearing your Hellknight plate.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Hellknight or [[Archetypes/Hellknight Armiger|Hellknight armiger]] archetypes.
