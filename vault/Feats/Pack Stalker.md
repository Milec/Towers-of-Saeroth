# Pack Stalker  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnoll]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pack Hunter]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

Ambushes are an honored gnoll tradition. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Stalker]] feat and can extend its effects to a single ally so long as they remain within 10 ft. of you. If you have master proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]], you can extend the effect to two allies. If you have legendary proficiency in Stealth, you can extend it to four allies.
