# Perfume Cloud two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 38

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

You issue a cloud of your fragrance like a full-body sneeze—but one that might save your life as the powerful perfume makes your attackers' eyes sting and water. [[Traits/Plant|Plants]] and [[Traits/Fungus|fungi]] are immune to this effect, but all other creatures in a 10-foot emanation must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) with the following results.

**Critical Success** No effect.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled and can see only 10 feet away for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.
