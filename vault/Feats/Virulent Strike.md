# Virulent Strike single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 108

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

---

Those afflicted by certain maladies are closer to death in your presence. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a creature. If your attack hits, the target must attempt a new saving throw against the highest-level [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] that is currently affecting it, possibly progressing it to a higher stage even if the [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|affliction]]’s interval hasn’t passed. If the target successfully saves or critically saves against the affliction, the affliction’s stage isn’t reduced. If your attack was a critical hit, the target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to the save.
