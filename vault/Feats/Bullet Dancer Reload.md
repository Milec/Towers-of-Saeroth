# Bullet Dancer Reload single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 132

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bullet Dancer Burn]]

**Requirements**
You’re in Bullet Dancer Stance.

---

You’ve learned to reload your simple firearms with uncanny grace, the ammunition simply falling into place as though part of a well-practiced kata. You [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a simple firearm, and then [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to reload that same firearm. You don’t need a free hand to reload your simple firearm in this way.
