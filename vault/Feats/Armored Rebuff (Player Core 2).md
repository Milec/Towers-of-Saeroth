# Armored Rebuff ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 215

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Sentinel (Player Core 2)|Sentinel]], [[Archetypes/Stalwart Defender]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sentinel Dedication (Player Core 2)|Sentinel Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An adjacent enemy critically fails an attack roll to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] you with a melee weapon or unarmed attack.

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium armor or heavier.

---

You rebuff puny attacks with your armor, knocking back your foe. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] the triggering enemy, even if you don't have a hand free. If you succeed, you can't [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] to follow the enemy, as you're knocking the enemy back with the rebounded attack, not by physically moving towards them.
