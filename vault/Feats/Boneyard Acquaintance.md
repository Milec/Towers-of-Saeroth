# Boneyard Acquaintance  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 81

---

Before your soul returned to the Material Plane, you interacted with a host of other souls on their way to the Boneyard. One of these acquaintances owes you a favor. You can temporarily summon it to come to your aid, and it appears as a swirling mass of ectoplasm with indiscernible features and a translucent dagger, the favored weapon of Pharasma. This functions as [[Spells/Spiritual Guardian (Player Core)|_spiritual guardian_]], which you can cast once per day as a divine innate spell, but you don't need to worship a deity to do so.
