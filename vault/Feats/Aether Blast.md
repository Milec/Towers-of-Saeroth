# Aether Blast single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 96

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are overflowing.

---

You channel some of the raw magical energy in your internal nexus into a devastating blast. You direct the blast at one creature within 30 feet, dealing 1d12 force damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your nexus DC. The force damage increases by 1d12 at 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.
