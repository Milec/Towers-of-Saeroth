# Fabricated Credentials  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 37

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Talon Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You're so familiar with the paperwork necessary to maintain your cover that you can easily whip up documents that support your fabrications. You can [[Actions/Create Forgery (Player Core)|Create a Forgery]] using [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], rather than [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]], and doing so only takes 10 minutes, or 1 minute if you're legendary in Deception. With your paranoid nature, you're always on the lookout for forgeries. The GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret (Player Core)|secret]] Perception or Society check for you any time you encounter a forgery, even if you don't examine it closely.
