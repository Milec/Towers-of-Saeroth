# Fearsome Form  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 27

A fearsome illusion covers you, evoking the terror of the most dangerous animals. You can cast [[Spells/Mask of Terror (Player Core)|_mask of terror_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. You can target only yourself, and your appearance is always a monstrous version of yourself.
