# Small Speak  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

---

You understand the languages of most small insects and can speak to them. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill with insects, arthropods, and terrestrial invertebrates. The GM determines which creatures count for this ability.
