# Greater Interpose  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sentinel]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sacrifice Armor]]

---

Your divine grace empowers you to avoid hits when you otherwise could not. You can use [[Feats/Sacrifice Armor]] to reduce any type of damage from an attack or effect requiring a Reflex save. When you use Sacrifice Armor against a critical hit or a critical failure on a Reflex save that would otherwise cause you to take double damage, you take the normal amount of damage instead.
