# Improved Reflexive Shield  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 153

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bastion (Player Core 2)|Bastion]] (Level 18), [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]] (Level 18)

[[Feats/Reflexive Shield]]

---

Your shield can help save nearby allies. When you use [[Feats/Shield Block]] against damage resulting from a Reflex save, adjacent allies who would take damage due to Reflex saves against the same effect also benefit from the damage reduction.
