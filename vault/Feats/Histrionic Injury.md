# Histrionic Injury ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 64

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
A creature you can see damages you with a melee Strike.

---

You respond with a startled look, dramatic pratfall, or theatrical arm-flailing that stymies your attacker. The attacker must succeed at a Will saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 round (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
