# Bleed Out single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 153

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast]] a non-cantrip spell that granted you a blood magic effect.

---

You channel the residual energy from the last spell you cast into a ranged bloodletting. Make a ranged spell [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] roll against the AC of a target within 60 feet. This attack deals [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to the rank of the spell you just cast.
