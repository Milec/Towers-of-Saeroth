# Blanket Defense three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 48

---

You become a bulwark against foes' attacks that target you and your allies. [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]]. Until the start of your next turn or until you no longer have your shield raised, each ally that ends their turn adjacent to you gains the benefits of your raised shield. You can [[Bestiary/Abilities/Shield Block (Monster Core)|Shield Block]] a hit against any adjacent ally (each one costs you a reaction as normal). If you  are wielding a [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|tower shield]], [[Equipment/Shields/Fortress Shield (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|fortress shield]], or another shield that grants a higher circumstance bonus to AC when you [[Actions/Take Cover (Player Core)|Take Cover]] behind it, any adjacent ally can Take Cover behind your shield to gain the increased bonus to Armor Class.
