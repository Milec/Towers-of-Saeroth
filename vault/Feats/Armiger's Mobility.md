# Armiger's Mobility  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hellknight Armiger]]

**Prerequisites**
expert in heavy armor; [[Feats/Hellknight Armiger Dedication]]

---

You’ve learned how best to move in heavy armor. When wearing heavy armor, if you meet the Strength requirement, instead of reducing the movement penalty by 5 feet, you reduce the penalty by 10 feet (typically reducing the penalty to 0). If you are a dwarf with the [[Feats/Unburdened Iron]] feat, increase your Speed by 5 feet.
