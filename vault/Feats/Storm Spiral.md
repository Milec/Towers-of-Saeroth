# Storm Spiral three actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 25

---

Dark thunderclouds swirl in a miniature storm, crisscrossed with lightning bolts; a tremendous thunderclap fills the air. The storm appears in a 20-foot burst within 60 feet. Each creature in the area takes 3d12 electricity damage and 1d10 sonic damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] until the end of its next turn (or for 1 minute on a critical failure). A creature wearing metal armor or made of metal takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its save.

---
**Level (+3)** The electricity damage increases by 1d12.
