# Awakened Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 25

---

When you awakened, primal magic was released within you. Choose one [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an primal innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
