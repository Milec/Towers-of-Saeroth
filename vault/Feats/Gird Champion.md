# Gird Champion  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 132

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Godling]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Godling Dedication]]

---

Your will manifests into divine weapons to protect and arm your followers. When you gain this feat, choose one type of weapon with special significance to you, such as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]] or a [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickle]]. This becomes your favored weapon. You and your hierophant both treat this weapon as a simple weapon for the purposes of proficiency. You and your hierophant gain the [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect of your favored weapon and deal an additional 1d6 spirit damage with [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] made with your favored weapon. If one of your current domain spells can deal a type of damage other than [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], you can choose to match this damage type (for instance, wreathing your weapon in sacred ashes that deal fire damage if you have the [[Magic/Domains/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] domain).
