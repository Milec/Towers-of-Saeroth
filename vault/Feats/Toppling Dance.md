# Toppling Dance  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 46

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dance Underfoot]]

---

While sharing a creature's space using Dance Underfoot, your weapons and unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Trip|trip]] trait, but only against the creature whose space you share. You can be in the same space as a Large or larger [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] creature, even if it's not your ally.
