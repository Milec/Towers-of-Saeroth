# Clear the Way two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 207

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Mauler (Player Core 2)|Mauler]], [[Archetypes/Warrior Of Legend]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Mauler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You put your body behind your massive weapon and swing, shoving enemies to clear a wide path. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] up to five creatures adjacent to you, rolling a separate [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check for each target and ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. Then [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions from any of the creatures you successfully Shoved. Each attack counts toward your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
