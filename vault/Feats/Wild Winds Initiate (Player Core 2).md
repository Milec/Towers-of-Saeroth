# Wild Winds Initiate  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 124

**Prerequisites**
Qi Spells

---

You learn a stance that lets you attack from a distance. You gain the [[Spells/Wild Winds Stance (Player Core 2)|_wild winds stance_]] qi spell. Though entering the stance is a qi spell, the wind crash Strikes the stance grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.
