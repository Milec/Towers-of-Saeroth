# Called  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

You have heard the Call, and in constantly resisting that persistent demand, you've developed a mental bulwark against effects that would target your mind. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saving throws against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. If you roll a success on your saving throw against a mental effect that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]], you get a critical success instead.
