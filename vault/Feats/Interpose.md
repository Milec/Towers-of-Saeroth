You put yourself between an ally and danger. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your Speed. You must end your movement adjacent to an ally. You and your ally then swap positions with each other. After changing positions, you can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an enemy within your reach.
