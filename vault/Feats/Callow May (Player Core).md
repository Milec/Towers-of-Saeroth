# Callow May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 77

---

You are among the most common of changelings—those born to sweet hags—and a vibrant green eye signifies that parentage. Your mother’s manipulative nature and versatile voice gives you an edge when deceiving others. You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar]] skill feat. If you roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] for initiative, foes in that combat encounter that haven’t acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against you.
