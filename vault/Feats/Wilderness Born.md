# Wilderness Born  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

---

You’re at home in the wilderness and easily disappear into it. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] in natural terrain, and a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] in natural terrain. While in natural terrain, you’re always considered to be [[Actions/Cover Tracks (Player Core)|Covering Tracks]], even if you chose a different activity in exploration mode.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
