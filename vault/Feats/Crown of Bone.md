# Crown of Bone  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 61

---

When you came of age, you were blessed by the Mother Earth with a thickened skull, which was celebrated as an auspicious sign among your community. Your horns come together to form a bony plate that protects your head from heavy impacts. The damage die for your horns unarmed attack increases to 1d8, and it gains the [[Traits/Concussive (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|concussive]] trait.
