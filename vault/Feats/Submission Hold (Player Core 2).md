# Submission Hold single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 223

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Your iron grip saps your opponent’s strength. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature you have grabbed or restrained, with the following additional effects if you succeed.

**Critical Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 until the end of its next turn and then is enfeebled 1 for 1 minute.  

**Success** The target is enfeebled 1 until the end of its next turn.
