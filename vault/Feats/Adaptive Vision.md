# Adaptive Vision  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 200

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

After years of using black powder weaponry in dimly lit spaces, you’ve become accustomed to dazzling flashes of light and sudden shifts in illumination. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against visual effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
