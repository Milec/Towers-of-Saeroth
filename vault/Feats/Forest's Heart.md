# Forest's Heart single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 26

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Forest [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Herbalism Lore as one of its apparition skills.

---

You adopt a stance that allows your apparitions to channel spiritual energy down through you to inhabit roots, control vines, and command plant-life from the ground you stand on to fight on your behalf. You call forth roots, vines, or other plant growth native to the region and can use them to make unarmed attacks. These deal 4d8 bludgeoning damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling group]], and have the [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]], and [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] 30 feet traits. Attacks made with these unarmed attacks don’t gain additional damage from [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] runes, but can benefit from the item bonus to attack rolls and [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]] of [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|handwraps of mighty blows]] you wear.
