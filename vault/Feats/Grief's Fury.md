# Grief's Fury ⤾  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Warrior Of Legend]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hero-God's Bond]]

**Trigger**
Your bonded partner gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition or their dying value would increase.

---

A lost love. A broken [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]]. Rage burning through a hero-god. When a myth speaks of a wounded partner, the hero-god often rejects the future. Too many find themselves on a battlefield, clutching a body that they swore to protect. In that moment, the wellspring of power ignites.

Increase the value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition by 1, to a maximum of doomed 3. You gain a +2 status bonus to attack rolls against any creature that attacked or dealt damage to your partner in the last minute. If you reduce a creature to 0 HP that attacked or damaged your partner, you gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice their level. These benefits last until your bonded partner is no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] or for 1 minute, whichever duration is shorter.
