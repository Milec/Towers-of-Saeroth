# Bamboo and Silt Repose  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

---

At home among mire and copse, you flit from river to tree with a dragonfly’s shimmering grace. You ignore non-magical [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] due to light undergrowth and shallow bogs, mud, and water, and you treat non-magical greater difficult terrain due to these features as difficult terrain instead.
