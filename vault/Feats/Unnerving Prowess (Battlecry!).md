# Unnerving Prowess free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You critically succeed at a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] with your [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]], or succeed at a Strike or Disarm attempt with your Aldori dueling sword against an opponent during your first turn of an encounter.

---

Your incredible skill with your blade unnerves your foes. You attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] the target of your Strike or Disarm. This Demoralize attempt does not have the auditory trait, nor do you take a penalty to the check if the target doesn't understand your language.
