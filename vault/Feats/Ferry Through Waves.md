# Ferry Through Waves two actions or three actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/River Adaptation]]

**Requirements**
You are [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|swimming]] and have a free hand.

---

The waters around you permit you passage, a privilege you can share with a willing ally. You hold on to an adjacent ally and [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], carrying them along with you. If you spend three actions Ferrying through Waves, you instead Swim twice. If your ally would need to attempt any [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to Swim while you’re Ferrying through Waves, you roll the check and your result applies to them.
