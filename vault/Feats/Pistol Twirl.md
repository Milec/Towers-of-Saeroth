# Pistol Twirl single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 112

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Bullet Dancer]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a loaded one-handed ranged weapon

---

Your quick gestures and flair for performance distract your opponent, leaving it vulnerable to your follow-up attacks. You [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]] against an opponent within the required weapon’s first range increment, rather than an opponent within melee reach. If you succeed, the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] against your melee and ranged attacks, rather than only your melee attacks. On a critical failure, you’re flat-footed against the target’s melee and ranged attacks, rather than only its melee attacks.
