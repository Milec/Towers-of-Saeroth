# Spiritual Aid  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 241

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scions of Domora]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scion of Domora Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scion of Domora Dedication]]

---

When you call your spirit guide for aid, its presence bolsters your will and agility. You gain the Spiritual Aid reaction.

**Spiritual Aid** ⤾ **Trigger** You fail a Reflex or Will saving throw; **Effect** You reroll the triggering saving throw. You must use the second result, even if it’s worse. If you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
