# Guide the Timeline single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 186

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chronoskimmer]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chronoskimmer Dedication]]

---

You know the result you want and subtly nudge the timeline to your intended destination. Choose an ally or a foe. If you choose an ally, the next time within the next round that ally makes an attack roll or skill check, they roll it twice and take the higher result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. If you choose a foe, the next time within the next round that foe makes an attack roll or skill check, they must roll twice and take the lower result unless they succeed at a Will save against your chronoskimmer DC; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect. Regardless of your choice, the target becomes temporarily immune for 24 hours.
