# Sunder Enchantment  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sunder Spell (Player Core 2)|Sunder Spell]]

---

You can focus your superstition to break the magic of an item, in addition to destroying freestanding spells and those active on creatures. When you Sunder a Spell, you can instead attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] either an unattended magic item or one of your target's magic items. If your counteract attempt succeeds, the item becomes a mundane item of its type for 10 minutes. If you target an [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|artifact]], an [[Traits/Intelligent (GM Core)|intelligent item]], or a similarly particularly powerful item, your counteract attempt automatically fails.
