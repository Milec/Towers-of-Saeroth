# Unassuming Heroes  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 42

**Frequency**
once per day

**Access**
 peachchild leshy heritage

---

You have a talent for making friends through simple kindness, often among stray animals or other creatures that people tend to overlook, and your inherent magic can make these acts take on additional power. As an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, you can feed a small treat, such as a millet dumpling, to an animal that has an indifferent or better attitude toward you. For the next 1 minute, one of the animal’s unarmed attacks becomes a +1 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] unarmed attack. If it was already a +1 striking attack, it instead gains the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]] rune.
