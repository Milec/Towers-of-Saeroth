# Magus Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Magus]]

**Prerequisites**
Strength 14 or Dexterity 14; Intelligence 14

---

You cast spells like a magus, gaining a [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank)|spellbook]] with four common [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] cantrips of your choice. You gain the Cast a Spell activity. You can prepare two cantrips each day from your spellbook. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new level from the magus archetype, add a spell of that level or lower to your spellbook. You're trained in arcane spell attack rolls and spell DCs. Your key spellcasting ability for magus archetype spells is Intelligence, and they are arcane magus spells. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]]; if you were already trained in Arcana, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in simple weapons.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the magus archetype.
