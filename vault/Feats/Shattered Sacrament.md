When Vigil fell to Tar-Baphon’s Radiant Fire, that lost innocence brought you a deeper understanding of what faith means in the context of your own magic. When you cast a halcyon spell, you can choose for it to be a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spell instead of [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]]. You gain a halcyon cantrip and a 1st-rank halcyon spell.
