# Become as Spirit  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Grim Fascinations/Spirit|spirit grim fascination]]

---

You can move through an unwilling creature’s spaces as if they were [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Additionally, you [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore natural difficult terrain]] and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] attempts. Whenever you destroy a thrall, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to all damage (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], or [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) equal to half your level for 1 round.
