# System Override  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

---

When someone takes control of your consciousness or your body, you innately resist their domination with everything you have. At the beginning of your turn, if you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition, you must attempt a DC 11 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. On a success, you ignore the controlled condition for 1 round.
