# Holistic Care  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]; [[Feats/Treat Condition]]

---

You provide emotional and spiritual care. Add [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] to the list of conditions you can reduce with [[Feats/Treat Condition]]. If the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can't use Treat Condition to reduce it.
