# Sheltering Pulse two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

---

You thrust your hand or weapon into the ground and release a pulse that creates a sheltering nexus of energy for you and your allies. Choose an unoccupied square within 15 feet. The nexus appears in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around that square and lasts for 3 rounds. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC while in the area.
