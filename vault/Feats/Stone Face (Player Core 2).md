# Stone Face  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

You've mastered the art of composure, even in the face of fear. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against effects with the [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] trait and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Will DC against [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] skill actions, such as [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]].
