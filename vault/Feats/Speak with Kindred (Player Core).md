# Speak with Kindred  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 69

---

You have a connection with creatures that share your physiology. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with plants and fungi. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks with plants or fungi of your kind. Generally, [[Ancestries/Heritages/Fungus Leshy|fungus leshies]] get this bonus with mushrooms and fungi; [[Ancestries/Heritages/Gourd Leshy|gourd leshies]] with gourds, melons, and similar fruiting plants; [[Ancestries/Heritages/Leaf Leshy|leaf leshies]] with deciduous trees; and [[Ancestries/Heritages/Vine Leshy|vine leshies]] with vines and climbing plants.
