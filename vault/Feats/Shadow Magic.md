# Shadow Magic  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 192

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Call of Shadows|Shadowdancer]], [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Shadowdancer Dedication]]

---

You gain the ability to fuse and play with shadows by using focus spells specific to shadowdancers. You learn a shadowdancer focus spell of your choice: [[Spells/Dance of Darkness|_dance of darkness_]] or [[Spells/Shadow Jump|_shadow jump_]]. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can [[Actions/Refocus]] by meditating within an area of dim light or darkness. The rules for focus spells appear [[Rules/Focus Spells|here]].

Shadowdancer focus spells are occult spells. You become trained in occult spell attack rolls and spell DCs, and your spellcasting ability for these spells is Charisma.
