You [[Actions/Leap]] and then Strike using your staff. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage for the Strike equal to twice the number of weapon damage dice. Regardless of whether your Strike hit, you can then attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip]] the target.
