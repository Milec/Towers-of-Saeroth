# Cantorian Reinforcement  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 34

---

The life energy that helped create the first hobgoblins is particularly potent in you, protecting you from ongoing maladies. When you succeed at a saving throw against a [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison|poison]], you reduce the stage by 2 (or 1 for a [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] disease or poison), and when you critically succeed, you reduce the stage by 3 (or 2 for a virulent disease or poison).
