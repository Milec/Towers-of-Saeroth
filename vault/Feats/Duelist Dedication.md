# Duelist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 171

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in light armor and simple weapons

---

You are always ready to draw your weapon and begin a duel, no matter the circumstances. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Draw]] ranger feat, enabling you to both draw and attack with a weapon as 1 action. This serves as Quick Draw for the purpose of meeting prerequisites.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the duelist archetype.
