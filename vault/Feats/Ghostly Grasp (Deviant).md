# Ghostly Grasp (Deviant) single action  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 11, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 102

---

An invisible arm extends from you to grab and manipulate objects. The arm reaches up to 15 feet, grabs an unattended object of 1 Bulk or less, and immediately deposits it in one of your free hands or at your feet. Alternatively, it reaches up to 15 feet and performs a simple Interact action like pushing open a window, though it can't perform actions that require significant mechanical dexterity. For every 5 levels you have, the amount the hand can lift increases by 1 Bulk, and the arm's range increases by 15 feet. Being ghostly, the arm can affect [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghosts]], [[Traits/Spirit|spirits]], and other [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] entities, though in most cases, you need an awakening for this to be useful.
