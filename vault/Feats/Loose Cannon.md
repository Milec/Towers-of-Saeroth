# Loose Cannon single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Alkenstar Agent]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Alkenstar Agent Dedication]]

---

Your unpredictable gunfire often catches enemies off guard or hits unprotected areas. Make a ranged Strike with a firearm against a creature that you haven't already attacked on this turn. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage on this Strike equal to the number of weapon damage dice. The Strike gains the following failure effect.

**Failure** The firearm [[Rules/Classic Firearms (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|misfires]], but it doesn't cause the other critical failure effects listed below.  

**Critical Failure** The firearm misfires and also explodes. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]], and it deals its normal weapon damage to all creatures in a 20-foot burst centered on the firearm, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. This damage includes any from the weapon's [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|fundamental]] and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|property]] runes.
