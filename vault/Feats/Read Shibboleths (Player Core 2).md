# Read Shibboleths  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 203

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Linguist (Player Core 2)|Linguist]], [Venture-Gossip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=311) (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Linguist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Linguist Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]

---

All communication is filled with slight tells and signals. If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, their regional words, pronunciation changes, and nonverbal cues provide you with a minor fact about their social environment, such as their hometown or certain groups they might belong to. If a target of this ability is being deceptive about their social environment, such as a commoner pretending to be a noble, they secretly attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]], or appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] check against your Society DC, providing you a false minor fact consistent with their assumed identity on a success.

Once you’ve gleaned one or more true minor facts about a person, you can then incorporate these mannerisms into your speech to present yourself in a way that they find familiar. This grants you a +1 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] and Deception checks with them.
