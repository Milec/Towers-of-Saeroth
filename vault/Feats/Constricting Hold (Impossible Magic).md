# Constricting Hold single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 82

**Requirements**
Your eidolon has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Your eidolon constricts the creature, dealing bludgeoning damage equal to your eidolon’s level plus their Strength modifier, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save against your spell DC.
