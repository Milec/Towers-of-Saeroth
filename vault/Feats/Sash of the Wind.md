# Sash of the Wind  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 43

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

At your request, the nature spirits of the air and wind spin themselves into an iridescent sash loosely draped around your shoulders that buoys you through the sky. You can cast [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]] on yourself as a 4th-rank innate primal spell once per day. If the sash created by this feat is removed, the spell immediately ends.
