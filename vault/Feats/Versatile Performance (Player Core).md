# Versatile Performance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 101

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Polymath|polymath muse]]

---

You can rely on the grandeur of your performances rather than ordinary social skills. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] and instead of [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]]. You can also use an acting Performance instead of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]]. In addition, you can use your proficiency rank in Performance to meet the prerequisites of skill feats that require a particular proficiency rank in Deception, Diplomacy, or Intimidation.
