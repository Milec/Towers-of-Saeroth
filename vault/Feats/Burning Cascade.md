# Burning Cascade  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 18

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade (Impossible Magic)|Arcane Cascade]]

---

Energy flares out around you when you drop into your signature stance. When you use [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes damage equal to the extra damage from Arcane Cascade. This damage is the same type as that of Arcane Cascade. A creature that takes this damage is temporarily immune to further uses of Burning Cascade for 1 minute.
