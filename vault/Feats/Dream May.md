# Dream May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

You are the child of a [[Bestiary/Night Hag|night hag]], making one of your eyes violet or even black. Your mother's powers over sleep and dreams have given you a degree of resistance to those effects, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] effects and effects that cause or alter dreams.

 In addition, sleep is more restorative for you. You regain HP equal to your Constitution modifier times double your level instead of just times your level, and you reduce any [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] conditions you have by 2 instead of by 1.
