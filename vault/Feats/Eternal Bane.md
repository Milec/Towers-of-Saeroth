# Eternal Bane  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 126

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]] (Level 18)

**Prerequisites**
evil alignment

---

A life of evil has made you a nexus for your deity’s vile power. You’re continuously surrounded by a [[Spells/Bane|_bane_]] spell with a spell level equal to half your level (rounded up). The radius is 15 feet, and you can’t increase it. You can Dismiss the spell; if you do, it returns automatically after 1 minute.
