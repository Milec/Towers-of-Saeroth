# Stubborn Defiance  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 33

---

You're brave and stubborn and refuse to back down or bow to the wishes of another. You gain a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. Additionally, you gain the I Defy You! reaction.

 **I Defy You!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** You fail a save against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect; **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** You reroll the triggering save and must take the second result.
