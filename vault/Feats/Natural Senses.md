# Natural Senses  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 25

---

You have retained your sharp animal senses even after awakening. Choose one of the following senses appropriate to your kind of animal: [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], echolocation 10 feet, [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[Rules/Scent|scent]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]]) 30 feet, or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] (imprecise) 30 feet. (Echolocation allows you to use hearing as a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise sense]] at the listed range.) If your animal kind doesn't typically have a specific type of sense, you can't gain that sense with this feat.

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times, choosing a different sense each time.
