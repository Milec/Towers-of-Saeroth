# Advanced First Aid  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 202

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

You use your medical training to ameliorate sickness or assuage fears. When you use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] to [[Actions/Administer First Aid]], instead of Stabilizing a character or Stopping Bleeding, you can reduce an ally's [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition by 2, or remove either of those conditions entirely on a critical success. You can remove only one condition at a time. The DC for the Medicine check is usually the DC of the effect that caused the condition.
