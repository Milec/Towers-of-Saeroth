# Animal Skin  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 78

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal (Player Core 2)|animal instinct]]

---

Your proficiency in unarmored defense increases to expert. When you are raging and unarmored, your skin transforms into a thick hide. You gain a +2 item bonus to AC (+3 if you have the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|greater juggernaut class feature]]). The thickness of your hide gives you a Dexterity modifier cap to your AC of +3. This item bonus to AC is cumulative with [[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|_armor potency_]] runes on your [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer's clothing]], [[Spells/Mystic Armor|_mystic armor_]], and [[Equipment/Bands of Force|_bands of force_]].
