# Aura of Righteousness  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 98

**Prerequisites**
champion's aura, holy

---

Your righteous aura dampens evil's might and prevents the unholy from escaping you. You and all allies in your champion's aura gain resistance 5 to [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] spells, unholy Strikes, and other unholy effects. If a [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] spell or effect would teleport an unholy creature out of your champion's aura, your aura attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it, using the spell rank and DC of your devotion spells.
