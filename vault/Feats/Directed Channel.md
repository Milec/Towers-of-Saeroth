# Directed Channel  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 123

---

You can shape the energy you channel in a single direction, reaching farther and in a more directed fashion. When you cast a version of [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] that has an area, you can make its area a 60-foot cone instead of a 30-foot emanation.
