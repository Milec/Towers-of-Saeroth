# Phase Out three actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 70

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Using the Phase Out summoner ability means that your eidolon cannot make any Strikes, nor can it take any actions requiring a physical form_

**Prerequisites**
your eidolon is a [[Traits/Phantom|phantom]]

---

Your eidolon phases out of reality slightly, gaining resistance to all damage (except [[Traits/Force|force]] and [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) equal to half your level. It's not [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]], but it can't use Strikes or actions that require a fully physical form, such as [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]]. Your eidolon can return to its normal form with a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.
