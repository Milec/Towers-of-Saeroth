# Frog Tide two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 21

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You have a connection to water and are able to call upon it to batter your foes and carry them away. You create a massive wave of water that crashes in a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 120 feet that moves in the direction of your choice. All creatures in the area take 14d6 bludgeoning damage and could be swept away. At the beginning of subsequent turns for 3 rounds, the wave moves 30 feet in the direction you chose; creatures in the area who aren’t already swept away by the wave take the bludgeoning damage. Creatures being swept away are trapped within the wave and can attempt to Escape against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, to end the effect. Otherwise, the creature remains swept away until the wave stops moving. The wave also extinguishes all non-magical fires in its path automatically.

Creatures are affected based on the result of their Reflex saves with a DC equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|moved]] 10 feet in the direction of the wave, but not swept away.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is fully swept away by the wave. The creature moves along with the wave until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]].  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the creature takes double damage and must hold its breath or begin [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|suffocating]] (unless it can breathe in water).
