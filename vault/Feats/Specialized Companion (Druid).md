# Specialized Companion (Druid)  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 139

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Nidalese Horselord]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Incredible Companion (Druid)]]

---

Your animal companion continues to grow in power and ability, and it is now cunning enough to become specialized. Your animal companion gains one [specialization](Companions.aspx?Type=Specialized) of your choice.

**Special** You can select this feat up to three times. Each time, add a different specialization to your companion.
