# Athamaru Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 18

---

You wield weapons designed to work underwater with ease. You have familiarity with weapons with the [[Traits/Athamaru|athamaru]] trait and with [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow (Player Core)|crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Heavy Crossbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|heavy crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spears]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|tridents]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
