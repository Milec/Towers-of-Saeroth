# Pinpoint Poisoner  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 107

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]] (Level 10)

---

Unsuspecting targets are especially vulnerable to your poisons. When you successfully Strike a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature with a poisoned weapon or expose a flat-footed creature to an inhaled poison, the flat-footed condition also gives that creature a –2 circumstance penalty to its initial save against that poison.
