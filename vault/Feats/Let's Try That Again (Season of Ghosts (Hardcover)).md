# Let's Try That Again ⤾  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 319

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You fail a skill check.

---

You feel a sense of deja vu when you’ve made a mistake, almost as if you weren’t so careless in a past life and could certainly do better if given another chance. Reroll the triggering skill check and use the new result, even if it’s worse than your first roll.
