# Intuitive Crafting  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 53

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aeonbound]]

---

Your affinity with process and order makes you a natural artisan. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]. If you were already trained in Crafting, you instead gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting (Player Core)|Specialty Crafting]] skill feat in a specialty of your choice; if you have both, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. In addition, when you critically fail to Repair an item, you get a failure instead.
