# Protective Pose two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 109

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

**Requirements**
You and your [[Traits/Eidolon|eidolon]] are within 30 feet of one another.

---

Many physical poses can fortify one’s health, but some poses need more than one body to accomplish. As you and your eidolon pose in tandem, you each channel qi into a protective barrier in a 30-[[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] until the start of your next turn. Creatures within range of either you or you eidolon gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to your choice of [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage equal to half your level. In addition, any Medicine check targeting a creature with this resistance gets a +1 status bonus (or a +2 status bonus if you’re a master in Medicine).
