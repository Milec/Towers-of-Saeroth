# Wrath of the Hold two actions  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Tattooed Historian]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tattooed Historian Dedication]]

---

Your tattoos carry the strength of an innumerable horde, and you can expend one use of your storied skin’s Living History ability to animate your tattoos as ghostly assailants that swarm your foes. These spirits attack all foes in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], dealing 4d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. The damage increases by 1d6 at 10th level and every 2 levels thereafter. Each affected creature must attempt a basic Will saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC.
