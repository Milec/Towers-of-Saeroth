Charging a living body with electricity may seem like a bad idea, but you know better. When a creature gains temporary Hit Points from your In Lightning, Life, they gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition until the end of their next turn. They can use the extra action only for Stride and Strike actions.
