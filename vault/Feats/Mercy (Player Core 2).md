# Mercy  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 95

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Blessed One]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Lay on Hands (Player Core 2)|_lay on hands_]]

---

Your touch soothes the body or mind. You can cast [[Spells/Lay on Hands (Player Core 2)|_lay on hands_]] targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one condition of your choice affecting the target. When you select this feat, choose one of the following options, which determines the conditions you can choose: **Mercy of the Body** [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]]; **Mercy of Grace** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]]; **Mercy of the Mind** [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]].

**Special** You can select this feat up to three times. Each time, choose a different type of mercy and add its options to those you can choose when you cast a 2-action _lay on hands_.
