# Punishing Shove  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

When you push a foe away, you put the entire force of your armored form into it. When you successfully [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] a creature, that creature takes an amount of bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier (or double that amount on a critical success). This damage increases by 2 when you become an expert in Athletics, 6 when you become a master, and 12 when  you become legendary.
