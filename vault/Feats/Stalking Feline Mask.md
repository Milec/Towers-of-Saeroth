# Stalking Feline Mask  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -174- Shadows of the Ancients|Pathfinder #174: Shadows of the Ancients]] pg. 78

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Druid]], [[Archetypes/Wizard]] (Level 20), [[Archetypes/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]] (Level 20)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Druid Dedication]] or [[Feats/Wizard Dedication]]

---

Like Azure Leopard, the Patient Warden, your mask makes you most at home in the night and teaches you how to use the darkness to hunt. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|greater darkvision]]. Immediately after you Cast a non-cantrip Spell with the [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]] or [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] trait, you can attempt to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]] as a free action. You must still meet the usual requirements of the action you choose, such as having cover or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] to Hide and being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to Sneak.

## Masks of the Magic Warriors

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -174- Shadows of the Ancients|Pathfinder #174: Shadows of the Ancients]] pg. 78

In becoming Jatembe's new Magic Warriors, the heroes have unlocked secrets of magical potential that none have reached since the earliest Ten Magic Warriors walked the plazas of the Magaambya.

 On reaching 20th level, the heroes should have the option of taking one of these archetype feats, based on the type of animal they chose for their mask when they first created it in _Pathfinder Adventure Path #169: Kindled Magic_. These options are each themed to one of Jatembe's magic warriors, but if a hero's mask doesn't fit with a specific animal from this list or the player feels their animal embodies a different effect from the list, feel free to reflavor the feat names to fit the hero's spirit face. For instance, the Cunning Trickster mask could easily be a rodent or a fox, not just a spider.
