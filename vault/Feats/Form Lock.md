# Form Lock single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 125, [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 130

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]] (Level 14)

**Requirements**
You have a foe [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Your ability to control your own qi empowers you to pressure other creatures into resuming their true forms. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect currently affecting your target. If the target is somehow under the effect of multiple polymorph effects, you can choose which one to attempt to counteract, or the GM chooses randomly if the separate effects aren't obvious. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 day.
