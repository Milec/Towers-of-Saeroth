# Steady Spellcasting  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Psychic]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 26, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 211, [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]] (Level 8)

---

Confident in your technique, you don’t easily lose your concentration when you Cast a Spell. If a reaction would disrupt your spellcasting action, attempt a DC 15 flat check. If you succeed, your action isn’t disrupted.
