# Ferocious Will ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 213

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
You succeed at a saving throw against a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.

---

You send a blast of magical feedback at the effect's source, dealing 5d6 mental damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. If the target critically fails, it’s also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round. At 11th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
