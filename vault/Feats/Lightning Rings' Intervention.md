# Lightning Rings' Intervention  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]

---

Your familiar learns to empower spells with an electrical discharge. Your familiar gains the following ability.

**Activate** single action Lightning Needles;    **Requirements** You Cast a Spell that had the air or electricity traits this turn; **Effect** ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) Your familiar absorbs excess electrical charge and unleashes a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of static electricity that flows into creatures’ meridians. Creatures in the emanation that fail a Fortitude save against your [[Rules/Spell DC|spell DC]] become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 until the end of their next turn. If a creature is soaked or standing over or in water inside the emanation, it also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent electricity damage]] on a failed save.
