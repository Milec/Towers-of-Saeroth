You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Player Core)|basic spellcasting benefits]] as a prepared divine caster. In addition to standard [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition spells, you can prepare your bonded apparition’s apparition spells in your spell slots of the appropriate level from the animist archetype.
