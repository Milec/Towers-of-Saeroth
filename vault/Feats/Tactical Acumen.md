# Tactical Acumen  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 232

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]]

---

Even if you don’t know much about its constituent creatures, you can always recognize a troop’s capabilities through features like the formation they move in or even more subtle hints others might miss. You can always use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] when you [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] to identify a troop. This works like using an applicable Lore to identify a creature. If you’re legendary in Warfare Lore, reduce any adjustment to the Recall Knowledge check’s DC caused by the troop’s rarity by 5.

If you [[Rules/Skirmish Units|lead a troop]], when you successfully use Warfare Lore to identify a troop, that troop takes a –1 circumstance penalty to saves against your troop’s offensive abilities until the end of your next turn.
