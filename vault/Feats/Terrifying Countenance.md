# Terrifying Countenance free action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Vigilante]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

---

You can erect a magical cowl that terrorizes your enemies. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Mask of Terror|_mask of terror_]] as an innate occult spell, targeting yourself. If you critically hit a creature that's temporarily immune to the spell, the creature's temporary immunity ends.
