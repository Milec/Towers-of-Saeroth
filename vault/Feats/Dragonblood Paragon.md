# Dragonblood Paragon  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Strongjaw Kobold]] heritage, [[Ancestries/Heritages/Venomtail Kobold]] heritage, [[Feats/Dracomancer]], or [[Feats/Kobold Breath]]

---

Your draconic anatomy becomes especially pronounced. You increase the power of one of the following kobold heritages or feats you have.

**Dracomancer** Increase the number of times you can cast each of the granted 1st- and 2nd-level innate spells by 1.

**Kobold Breath** Creatures that critically fail their save against your Kobold Breath take 3d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] of the type dealt by your draconic patron.

**Strongjaw Kobold** Your jaws unarmed attack gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d6]] trait.

**Venomtail Kobold** You produce enough venom to use the [[Actions/Tail Toxin]] action twice per day.
