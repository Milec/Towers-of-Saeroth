# Hell-Lancer Shot two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 33

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Golden Legionnaire Dedication]]; trained in martial firearms

---

Make a ranged Strike with a firearm against a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], ignoring the fiend's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition and any [[Rules/Cover|lesser cover]]. If this Strike hits, you gain a circumstance bonus to damage equal to twice the number of weapon damage dice. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty.
