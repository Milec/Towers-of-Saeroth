# Mariner's Fire  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 34

---

You conjure uncanny orbs of spiritual flame that float above or below the water's surface. You can cast the [[Spells/Ignition|_ignition_]] cantrip as a primal innate spell at will, heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. Your _ignition_ spell takes the form of orbs of fire, and you can cast it underwater.
