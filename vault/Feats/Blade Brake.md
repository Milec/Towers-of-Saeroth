# Blade Brake ⤾  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter (Player Core)|Fighter]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 141

**Trigger**
You would be moved by a forced movement effect

**Requirements**
You are wielding a melee weapon and are within reach of a hard surface

---

You plow your weapon into a floor, wall, or another sturdy surface, anchoring yourself in place. If you would be moved 20 feet or more, you are moved only 5 feet. Otherwise, you don’t move at all.
