# Like a Roach  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 319

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This feat is not available for PFS play._

---

Living with the bare necessities isn’t terribly hard for someone who has defied death, and like a cockroach, you always know how to survive. When you [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] during downtime, the DC for your skill check is reduced by 2. You don’t need a minimum proficiency rank to Subsist in unusual environments, nor do you take a –5 penalty if you Subsist after 8 hours or less of exploration.
