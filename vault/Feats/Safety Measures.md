# Safety Measures  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 133

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Demolitionist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Demolitionist Dedication]]

---

You’re often careful to be sure you’re not hoisted on your own petard, or anyone else’s, for that matter. When you have cover from a bomb, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to damage caused by that bomb equal to half your level, plus a value equal to the circumstance bonus to AC granted by the [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]]. You gain these benefits against your own bombs regardless of whether you have cover.
