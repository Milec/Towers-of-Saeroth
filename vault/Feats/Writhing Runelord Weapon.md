# Writhing Runelord Weapon  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Avenging Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Avenging Runelord Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a polearm or spear.

---

Your prowess with long-hafted weapons catches your opponents by surprise, as it bends or elongates in an obviously supernatural manner. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. Your weapon gains the [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] trait until the end of your turn, if it doesn’t have it already. You can Stride and Strike twice, in any order. The movement from your Stride doesn’t provoke reactions. Your Strikes are made at mythic proficiency and gain the critical specialization effect of the weapon. Apply your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]] to your Strikes as normal, but the target of your second Strike is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to it.
