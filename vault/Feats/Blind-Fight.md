# Blind-Fight  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Investigator]], [[Traits/Ranger]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 63, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 173

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Horizon Walker]] (Level 10), [[Rules/Sidebars/Spirit Dwellings and Remnants|Exorcist]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Eagle Knight]] (Level 10), [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
master in Perception

---

Your battle instincts make you more aware of [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] opponents. You don't need to succeed at a flat check to target concealed creatures. You're not [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to creatures that are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from you (unless you're flat-footed to them for reasons other than the hidden condition), and you need only a successful DC 5 flat check to target a hidden creature.

 While you're adjacent to an [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creature of your level or lower, it is instead only hidden from you.
