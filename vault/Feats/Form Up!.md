# Form Up! two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 91

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

You’ve trained in battle formations for a plethora of situations. Choose a formation. Allies within your aura can use their reaction to Stride up to their Speed and take a place in the chosen formation. Allies can’t benefit from this movement if there isn’t room for them in the chosen formation. If there are more eligible allies than there are available spaces in the chosen formation, you choose which allies benefit.

**Line** Your allies gather adjacent to you and each other to form a straight line in a direction of your choosing.

**Wedge** Your allies gather into any space that would be covered by a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] originating from you in a direction of your choosing.

**Cluster** Your allies gather into any space that would be covered by a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] in an aura around you.
