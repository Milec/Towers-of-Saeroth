# Bright Lion Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -172- Secrets of the Temple City|Pathfinder #172: Secrets of the Temple City]] pg. 79, [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 101

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bright Lion]]

**Prerequisites**
member of the Bright Lions; not a worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Walkena]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

**Access**
 Bright Lion  background

---

You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Mzali Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. You incorporate enough worship of Walkena in your daily life to avoid suspicion. You typically don't need to [[Actions/Lie]] or [[Actions/Impersonate]] to pass yourself off as a worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Walkena]]. Against careful inspection, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Deception checks specifically to pass yourself off as a version of yourself faithful to Walkena.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Bright Lion archetype.
