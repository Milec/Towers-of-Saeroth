# Fight or Flight ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Trigger**
You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition.

---

You’ve learned to make use of the surge of adrenaline that comes with fear. You can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against a creature that gave you the frightened condition if it’s within range, or you can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your speed away from the effect or creature that gave you the frightened condition.
