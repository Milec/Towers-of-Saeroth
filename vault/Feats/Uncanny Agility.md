# Uncanny Agility  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 58

---

You have near-supernatural poise that lets you move swiftly across the most unsteady surfaces. You gain the [[Feats/Steady Balance]] skill feat. You can Step into difficult terrain caused by uneven ground, such as a rocky field or hilly incline.
