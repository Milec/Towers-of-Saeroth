# Devil you Know  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 216, [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 8

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellbreaker Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Devil Lore]] or Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Hellknight Lore]]

---

Your knowledge of your enemies gives you an advantage against them, granting you a swift reaction to their devilry. If you know you're fighting against any devils or Hellknights at the beginning of an encounter, you can roll the respective [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill for your initiative roll.

 In addition, whenever you successfully [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about a creature using Devil Lore or Hellknight Lore, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your next skill check against that creature before the end of your next turn. If you're a master in Devil Lore or Hellknight Lore, the bonus increases to +2.
