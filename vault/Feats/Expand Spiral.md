Your prayers encompass your companions, granting you the ability to share [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasma's]] blessings. When you use Spiral Sworn, you can spend 2 actions instead of 1 to grant the benefits to all allies who are in the light of your spiral when you take the action.
