# Grow Tool three actions  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 70

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

---

You grow a simple tool from your own body, coaxing flowers, vines, roots, and wood to grow and sprout from your flesh into the desired shape. You create a one level 0 common simple [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|tool]] with no intricate parts or written text, such as a [[Equipment/Crowbar (Player Core)|crowbar]], [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]], or shovel. You can’t replicate a tool kit or weapon with this ability. This item lasts for 1 minute, or until you create another tool with this ability, whichever comes first. When duration runs out, the item crumbles to mulch.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
