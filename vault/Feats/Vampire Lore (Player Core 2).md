# Vampire Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

You were raised by your vampiric parent, or you've invested substantial time and energy researching the secrets of your undead progenitors. You're trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] and [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Vampire Lore]].
