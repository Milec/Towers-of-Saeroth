# Waterweave Tripkee  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 21

---

Your body has adapted to remain underwater indefinitely. You can breathe in water just as easily as you can breathe air. You gain the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait.

**Special** If you have the [[Ancestries/Heritages/Riverside Tripkee|riverside tripkee heritage]], your [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] increases to 20 feet.
