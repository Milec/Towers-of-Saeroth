# Vitality-Manipulating Stance single action  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -168- King of the Mountain|Pathfinder #168: King of the Mountain]] pg. 77

---

You have learned to attack meridians and pressure points like your rival Shino Hakusa. You gain a vitality blast ranged unarmed Strike that deals 1d4 negative damage, has a range of 30 feet, and has the [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] and [[Traits/Versatile|versatile positive]] traits.

 Additionally, when you use [[Feats/Stunning Fist]], instead of saving against being [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]], you can choose to have the target make a Fortitude save against your class DC with the following results. You must choose to do this before the target rolls its save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target's muscles become temporarily weaker. For 1 round, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]], and any time it attempts to take an action with the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait, the action is disrupted unless it succeeds at a flat check with a DC equal to 5 + its enfeebled value.  

**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute, the flat check is DC 11, and the target takes –10-foot status penalty to all Speeds for the duration.
