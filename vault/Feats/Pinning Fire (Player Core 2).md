# Pinning Fire ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 123

**Trigger**
You use Flurry of Blows to make two ranged piercing weapon [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] against the same target.

---

You target your opponent's clothing, equipment, or loose flesh with a series of piercing projectiles to pin them to the ground or a nearby surface. If both your attacks hit, the target must succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until it or an adjacent creature succeeds at a DC 10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to remove the pinning projectiles. The creature doesn't become stuck if it is incorporeal, is liquid (like a [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water (Monster Core)|water elemental]] or some [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze (Monster Core)|oozes]]), or could otherwise escape without effort.
