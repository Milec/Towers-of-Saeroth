# Primal Dragonblood  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]], [[Traits/Lineage (Player Core)|Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 45

---

A dragon with a deep connection to the natural world, such as an [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Adamantine|adamantine dragon]] or a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Horned|horned dragon]], resides somewhere on your family tree. You tend to trust your instincts and might take on the role of a protector of the wilderness. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in Nature (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can use Nature to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] and [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] in the wilds. If you choose a draconic exemplar, you must choose a primal dragon.
