# Read Lips  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 265

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You can read lips of others nearby who you can clearly see. When you're at your leisure, you can do this automatically. In encounter mode or when attempting a more difficult feat of lipreading, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] during each round in which you focus on lip movements, and you must succeed at a [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] check (DC determined by the GM) to successfully read someone's lips. In either case, the language read must be one that you know.
