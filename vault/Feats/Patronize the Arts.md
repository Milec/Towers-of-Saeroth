# Patronize the Arts ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 223

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chelaxian Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chelaxian Scion Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally within 30 feet of you critically succeeds at an attack roll or skill check.

---

A true aristocrat knows how to encourage their best hirelings, ensuring loyalty and future excellence. You praise the triggering ally, granting them one of the following benefits. The ally is then immune to this ability for 1 day.

**Eagerness:** Invigorated by praise, the ally can [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]], [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] with a nearby object, [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|Reload]], or [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a reaction.

**Persistence:** For 1 minute, the ally gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half your level plus your Charisma modifier.

**Validation:** If the ally has the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-1002)|panache class feature]], they gain panache.
