# Orc Sight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dromaar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]

---

Your orc blood grants you the keen vision of your forebears. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], allowing you to see in [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]] and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] just as well as you can in [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]]. However, in darkness, you see in black and white only.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can’t retrain out of this feat or into this feat.
