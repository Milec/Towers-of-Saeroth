# Amplifying Touch  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
devotion spell ([[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]])

---

Your healing energies empower attacks. An ally that recovers Hit Points from your [[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]] gains a +1 status bonus to their attack rolls and deals 1 additional spirit damage on all their Strikes until the end of their next turn. In addition, all their Strikes are holy until the end of their next turn.
