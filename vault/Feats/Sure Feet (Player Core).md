# Sure Feet  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 60

---

Whether keeping your balance or climbing, your feet easily find purchase. If you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] or an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] when you attempt to Balance or Climb.
