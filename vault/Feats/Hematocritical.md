# Hematocritical free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bloodrager]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bloodrager Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] that critically hit a creature that isn’t immune to bleed damage.

---

You bathe in the arterial fluids of your enemy, drinking deep of their freed essence to empower your magic and increase your spellcasting efficacy. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that requires a spell attack roll, you roll the spell attack roll twice and use the better result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect. Alternatively, if your next action is to cast a spell that requires the creature damaged by the triggering strike to attempt a save, they roll twice and take the lower result; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect.
