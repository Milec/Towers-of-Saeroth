# Giant Snare  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Snarecrafter (Player Core 2)|Snarecrafter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snarecrafter Dedication (Player Core 2)|Snarecrafter Dedication]]

---

You can create bigger [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|snares]] for quick deployment, making it more likely for a creature to walk into your snares. When you prepare snares for quick deployment, you can prepare some of them as giant snares. A giant snare takes up a 10-foot-by-10-foot area but costs two of your quick deployment snares. A giant snare can trigger from a creature entering any portion of its area, and all its effects apply over the full area.
