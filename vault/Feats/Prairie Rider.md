# Prairie Rider  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 46

---

You grew up riding your clan's shaggy ponies and riding dogs. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in Nature (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also get a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Command an Animal]] if the target is a traditional halfling mount, such as a [[Bestiary/Riding Pony|pony]] or [[Bestiary/Riding Dog|riding dog]].
