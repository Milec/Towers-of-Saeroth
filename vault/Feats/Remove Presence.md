# Remove Presence  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Amp]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 27

---

Your spell reaches into the mind of a creature and removes you from it. The amped cantrip must be one that has one or more targets and must either require a spell attack roll or have a saving throw. Use this amp in place of the psi cantrip's normal amp entry.

---
**Amp** Choose one target of the spell or one creature in its area. If that enemy fails its save or the spell hits it, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by that creature, disappearing from its senses for 1 minute or until you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]]. This is similar to being [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], but effects like [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]] don't reveal you—you're affecting the target's mind, not its vision. [[Spells/True Seeing|_True seeing_]] can still see through this illusion if the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check succeeds.
