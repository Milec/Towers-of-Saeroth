# Dual Energy Heart  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Energy Heart]]

---

A second energy dwells in your eidolon. Choose an additional energy damage type. Your eidolon's unarmed attack that deals energy damage gains the [[Traits/Versatile|versatile]] trait for the second energy damage type, and your eidolon gains resistance to the second type of energy damage equal to half your level.
