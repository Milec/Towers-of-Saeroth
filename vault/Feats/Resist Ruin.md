# Resist Ruin  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

Your duskwalker heritage allows you to extend protection against negative energy to yourself or an ally in need. You gain resistance 5 to negative energy, and once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Death Ward|_death ward_]] as a divine innate spell.
