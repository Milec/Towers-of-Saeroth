# Clear the Way two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 183

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Mauler]], [[Archetypes/Warrior Of Legend]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You put your body behind your massive weapon and swing, shoving enemies to clear a wide path. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] up to five creatures adjacent to you, rolling a separate [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check for each target. Then Stride up to half your Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions from any of the creatures you successfully Shoved.
