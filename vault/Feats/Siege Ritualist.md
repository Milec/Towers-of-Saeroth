# Siege Ritualist  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/War Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/War Mage Dedication]]

---

Sometimes, magical warfare requires the application of more complex arcana. When you attempt a Warfare [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] check as one of the casters of a ritual, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check. You learn a siege ritual of 2nd rank or lower. You must meet all prerequisites to be the primary caster of a ritual to select it.  

At 8th level and every 4 levels thereafter, you learn another siege ritual with the same restriction and with a maximum rank of half that level.
