# Upset Balance ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 110

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stalwart Defender]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stalwart Defender Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
You are damaged by a melee Strike.

**Requirements**
You are in Tenacious Stance.

---

You lean into your foe's attack, using the weight of your body and armor to send them reeling. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the  creature's Reflex DC. On a success, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until the end of its next turn (clumsy 2 on a critical success). If you are [[Feats/Tunnel Wall|anchored]], you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check.
