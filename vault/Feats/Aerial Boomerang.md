# Aerial Boomerang two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 24

---

A blade of shearing wind races away from you in a 60-foot line. Each creature in the area takes 2d4 slashing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. In the final square of the line, the boomerang whirls in place until the end of your next turn. Any creature that ends its turn in that square has to save against the boomerang.

 On your next turn, you can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to return the boomerang to you. It returns in a line from its square to your current location, with the same effect as the initial line, then the impulse ends. You must have line of effect to the boomerang and be within 60 feet of it.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d4.
