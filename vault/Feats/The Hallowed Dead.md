# The Hallowed Dead  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 32

**Prerequisites**
You aren’t [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]].

---

While many necromancers seek [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] and perverse power, you have dedicated yourself to the practice of necromancy to stop the creation of permanent undead and the corruption of souls. You and your thralls gain the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait. Strikes your thralls make gain the holy trait and deal 1 additional [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “purposefully create undead to do undue harm.”
