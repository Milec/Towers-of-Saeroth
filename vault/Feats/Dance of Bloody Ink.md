# Dance of Bloody Ink three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a melee weapon that bears a rune.

---

As you lash out, your weapon is your brush, wounds forming words of power in your target’s flesh. You [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke a Rune]] on a melee weapon you are wielding and [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. Then, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] and Strike again with the same weapon. If the second Strike hits, you [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] onto the target of the Strike. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
