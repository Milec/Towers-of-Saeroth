# Eagle Eyes  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 139

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sniping Duo Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Sniping Duo Dedication]]

---

You and your spotter are adept at watching out for one another. As long as you can see or hear each other, neither of you is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]], or flanking creatures of your level or lower, or to creatures of your level or lower using a surprise attack. However, they can still help their allies flank.
