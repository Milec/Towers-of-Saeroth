# Azarketi Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Absalom, City of Lost Omens]] pg. 394, [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 4

---

You are familiar with weapons that excel underwater. You are trained with [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow|crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spears]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|tridents]]. In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Azarketi|azarketi]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial azarketi weapons are simple weapons and advanced azarketi weapons are martial weapons.
