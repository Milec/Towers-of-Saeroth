# Challenge Insight ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 289

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Palatine Detective]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Palatine Detective Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature you can see [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Casts a Spell]] that is from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] tradition.

**Requirements**
The triggering caster must be able to hear and understand you.

---

Your deep grasp of the mystical lets you know exactly what to say to make others doubt how well they understand the source of their own power. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check against the triggering spell. Your counteract rank is half your level rounded up. You use your [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] modifier as your counteract modifier if the triggering spell is from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] tradition, or your [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] modifier if the triggering spell is from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition. If you successfully counteract the spell, the caster becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until the end of their next turn.
