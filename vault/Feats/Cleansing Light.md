# Cleansing Light two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Kashrishi]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 45

**Frequency**
once per day

---

A burst of light from your horn cleanses your allies of ailments and sends your foes reeling. You and all allies in a 20-foot emanation benefit from a 4th-level [[Spells/Restoration|_restoration_]] spell. All enemies within the emanation are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.
