# Roaring Heart two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Mountain [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] as an apparition skill.

---

You surge forward inexorably. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] twice. At any point during this movement, you can [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] up to two creatures you pass adjacent to. When you end the movement, the turbulent spirits you’re attuned to reward you for acting in an appropriately fierce manner: you and each ally in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level if you successfully Shoved at least one enemy, or equal to your level if you succeeded at Shoving both. These temporary Hit Points last until the beginning of your next turn.
