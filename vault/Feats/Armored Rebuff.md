# Armored Rebuff ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 191

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Sentinel]], [[Archetypes/Stalwart Defender]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sentinel Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An adjacent foe critically fails an attack roll to Strike you with a melee weapon or unarmed attack.

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium armor or heavier.

---

You rebuff puny attacks with your armor, knocking back your foe. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Shove]] the triggering foe, even if you don't have a hand free. If you succeed, you can't Stride to follow the foe, as you're knocking the foe back with the rebounded attack, not by physically moving towards them.
