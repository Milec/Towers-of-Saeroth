# Parry and Riposte  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 93

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-361)|Opportune Riposte]]

---

Your parries and finishers allow you to riposte with the slightest provocation. You can use [[Actions/Opportune Riposte]] against an enemy who fails at a Strike against you (not just critically fails), provided you have both damaged that creature with a finisher on your last turn and currently have a circumstance bonus to AC from the [[Traits/Parry|parry]] weapon trait, [[Feats/Dueling Parry (Swashbuckler)|Dueling Parry]], or [[Feats/Twin Parry]].
