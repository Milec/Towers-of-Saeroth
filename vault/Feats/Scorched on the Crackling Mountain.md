# Scorched on the Crackling Mountain  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 66

---

By ritualistically marking your fur with fire, like an infamous tanuki of legend, you protect yourself against future flames. You gain a black stripe down your back that looks charred. Your flat check to remove [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] is DC 10 instead of DC 15, which can be reduced to DC 5 with appropriate assistance. The first time each day you would be reduced to 0 Hit Points by a fire effect, you avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point, and your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition increases by 1. Two hairs plucked from your tail can be used as flint and steel to create a fire, emitting a strange crackling sound as they ignite.
