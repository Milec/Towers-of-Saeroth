# Amphibious Form  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 68

---

Your eidolon adapts to life on land and underwater. It gains the [[Traits/Amphibious|amphibious]] trait, allowing it to breathe in water and air and to avoid the normal –2 penalty for making bludgeoning and slashing unarmed Strikes underwater. It gains a swim Speed equal to its land Speed or 25 feet, whichever is less; or if it is normally [[Traits/Aquatic|aquatic]], it gains a land Speed equal to its swim Speed or 25 feet, whichever is less.
