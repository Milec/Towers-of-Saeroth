# Dodge Away ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication (Player Core 2)|Acrobat Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You are the target of a melee attack.

**Requirements**
You're aware of the attack and aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]].

---

You use your acrobatic prowess to evade an attack, using momentum to keep yourself moving if you choose. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can Step after the Strike. If you're a master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], you can move 10 feet for this Step instead of 5 feet.
