# Reflective Ripple Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 80

**Requirements**
You’re unarmored.

---

You enter a stance of fluid grace as small amounts of water flow with your movements and attacks. You can make flowing wave attacks that deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage. They are in the brawling group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] traits.

 While in Reflective Ripple Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC to avoid being Disarmed and Tripped.

**Special** This feat gains your choice of either the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] or [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] trait, matching your qi spell tradition if possible.
