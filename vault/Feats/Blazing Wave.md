# Blazing Wave two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 28

---

Flames flow out of you in a cascade, engulfing everyone in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area takes 4d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature that critically fails its save is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
