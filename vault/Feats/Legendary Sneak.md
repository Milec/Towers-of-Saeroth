# Legendary Sneak  *Feat 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 263

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]; [[Feats/Swift Sneak]]

---

You’re always sneaking unless you choose to be seen, even when there’s nowhere to hide. You can [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] even without [[Rules/Cover|cover]] or being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]. When you employ an exploration tactic other than [Avoiding Notice](https://2e.aonprd.com/Activities.aspx?ID=1), you also gain the benefits of Avoiding Notice unless you choose not to.
