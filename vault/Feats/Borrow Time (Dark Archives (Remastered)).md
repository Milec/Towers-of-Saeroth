# Borrow Time free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

---

You reach ahead and make use of time that's yet to be. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] and can use the extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike. You gain this extra action immediately and can use it this turn. At the end of your turn, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
