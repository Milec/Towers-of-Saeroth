# Centaur Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

---

You've trained in the weapons favored by your people, becoming a skilled archer and jouster. You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance (Player Core)|lance]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow (Player Core)|longbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspear]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|shortbow]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spear]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
