# Martial Artist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]]

---

You have trained to use your fists as deadly weapons and can deliver devastatingly powerful blows with them. The damage die for your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]] unarmed attacks becomes 1d6 instead of 1d4. You don’t take the –2 circumstance penalty for making a lethal attack with your [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] unarmed attacks.
