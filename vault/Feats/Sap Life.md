# Sap Life  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 122

---

You draw the life force out of your enemies to heal your own wounds. When you cast a [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spell and damage at least one living creature, you regain Hit Points equal to the spell level of your _harm_ spell. If you aren’t a living creature, you gain no benefit from this feat.
