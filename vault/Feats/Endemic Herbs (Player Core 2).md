# Endemic Herbs  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive (Player Core 2)|Additive]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 202

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) For Pathfinder Society play, Endemic Herbs does not have the additive trait._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist (Player Core 2)|Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Herbalist Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

---

The herbal items you create use medicinal plants endemic to your location. When a creature consumes one of your herbal items, that creature gains the benefit matching the location where you created that item. If the benefit is not immediate, it lasts for 1 minute unless otherwise stated.

- **Aquatic** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves.
- **Arctic** For 1 hour, treat [[Rules/Environmental Damage (GM Core)|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less severe.
- **Desert** For 1 hour, treat environmental heat effects as if they were one step less severe.
- **Forest** Gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] effects.
- **Mountain** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves.
- **Plains** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves.
- **Swamp** Remove one source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent]] bleed damage.
- **Underground** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]].
