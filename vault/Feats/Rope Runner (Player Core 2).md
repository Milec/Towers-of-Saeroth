# Rope Runner  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 209

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You run and climb across ropes and rigging almost as easily as on the ground. You gain a climb Speed of 15 feet, but only when climbing ropes and similar objects. Whenever you succeed at an Athletics check to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] or an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]], you get a critical success instead. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while Climbing or [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balancing]] on a rope or similar object.
