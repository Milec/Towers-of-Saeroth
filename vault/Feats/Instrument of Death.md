# Instrument of Death  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lethal Edge]]

---

Your [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality_]]  has reached the apex of its death-dealing potential. Whenever you critically succeed at an attack roll against an enemy and use Lethal Edge, add the following effects to the list you can choose from.

- The target is cursed and can’t benefit from circumstance or status bonuses. This is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] effect.
- The target gains [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] 10 to [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage.
