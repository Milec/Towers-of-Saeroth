# Many Guises  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Vigilante (Player Core 2)|Vigilante]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication (Player Core 2)|Vigilante Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You can take on any number of mundane guises. Whenever you change your identity, instead of taking on your social or vigilante identity, you can become someone completely ordinary. This identity isn't a specific individual—rather, you become a nondescript member of your ancestry, of any gender, and from a mundane occupation such as common laborer, farmer, or peasant. Spells and abilities detect you as if you were this ordinary identity, rather than either of your two real identities, unless they succeed at a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check against the same Deception DC used for Vigilante Dedication. Using class or dedication abilities ends this disguise.
