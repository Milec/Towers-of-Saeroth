# Guiding Shot single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 30

**Requirements**
You are wielding a ranged weapon.

---

Your ranged attack helps guide your allies into striking your enemy's weak point. Attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the required weapon. If the Strike hits, the next creature other than you to attack the same target before the start of your next turn gains  a +1 circumstance bonus to their roll, or a +2 circumstance bonus if your Strike was a critical hit.
