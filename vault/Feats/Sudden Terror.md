# Sudden Terror single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 48

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tsukumogami Poppet]] heritage

---

You leverage your body’s amorphous nature to spook others. Attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature within 30 feet. Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, and you don’t take a penalty if the creature doesn’t understand your language. If you were hidden to the creature, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your check, and if you succeed, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 instead of frightened 1. All creatures that witnessed you use Sudden Terror are then temporarily immune to your Sudden Terror for 24 hours.
