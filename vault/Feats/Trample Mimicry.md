# Trample Mimicry three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Mimic Dedication]]; you have seen a creature use [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample]] or have identified a creature with Trample in combat

---

You Stride up to double your speed and can move through the spaces of creatures at least one size smaller, trampling each creature whose space you enter. You can attempt to trample the same creature only once in a single use of Trample Mimicry. You deal 3d12 bludgeoning damage to each trampled creature ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC). This damage increases by 1d12 at levels 14 and 18.
