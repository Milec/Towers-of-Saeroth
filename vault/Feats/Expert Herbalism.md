# Expert Herbalism  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 176

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

Your advanced alchemy level for herbalism increases to your level – 3. You gain the formulas for [[Equipment/Elixir of Life|elixirs of life]] as soon as your advanced alchemy level is high enough to create them: lesser at 8th level, moderate at 12th level, greater at 16th level, and major at 18th level.
