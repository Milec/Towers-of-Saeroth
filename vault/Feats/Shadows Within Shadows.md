# Shadows Within Shadows ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|Misfortune]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 26

**Trigger**
You would be detected by an enemy [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeking]] you, or an enemy would succeed at a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check against a spell making you [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]].

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Hunting [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Underworld Lore as one of its apparition skills.

---

Your apparition possesses a furtive and elusive nature that can influence and blend with your spiritual energy to form a protective and nearly impenetrable shroud of nondetection around you. The enemy must reroll the triggering check and take the lower result.
