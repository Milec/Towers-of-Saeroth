# Undead Companion  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 27

**Prerequisites**
an animal companion or a familiar

---

Your companion is also touched by undeath, a mix of a traditional companion and a skeleton, zombie, or stranger form of unlife. Your companion gains the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait and gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]]. It otherwise uses the normal rules for an animal companion or familiar; that means it can still be affected by many conditions or effects to which most undead are immune.
