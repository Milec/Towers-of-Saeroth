# Illusion Sense  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 44

---

Your ancestors spent their days cloaked and cradled in illusions, and as a result, sensing [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] magic is second nature to you. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to both Perception checks and Will saves against illusions. When you come within 10 feet of an illusion that can be disbelieved, the GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] check for you to disbelieve it, even if you didn't spend an action to Interact with the illusion.
