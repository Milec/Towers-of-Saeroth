# Discordant Voice  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 115

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]]

---

Your [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]] lets loose a discordant shriek that imbues your allies' attacks with powerful sonic reverberations. While your allies are affected by your inspire courage, their weapon Strikes and unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d6 sonic damage.
