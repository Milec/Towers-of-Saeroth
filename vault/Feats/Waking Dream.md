# Waking Dream  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sleepwalker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication]]

---

While in your trance, you can blur the line between dreams and reality for others. You gain the [[Spells/Waking Dream|_waking dream_]] focus spell, which you can use only while you're in a [[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication|Daydream Trance]]. You gain a pool of Focus Points with 1 Focus Point and you can [[Actions/Refocus]] by taking a nap for 10 minutes. You become trained in occult spell attack rolls and spell DCs, and your spellcasting ability for these spells is Wisdom.
