# Slay two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Avenger]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Avenger Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your target is [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 2 or greater.

---

You finish your target with a vicious attack. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the target. If you hit, the target takes 4d6 extra precision damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC. If the target critically fails, they die. This is an [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect. The creature then becomes temporarily immune to your Slay for 1 day.
