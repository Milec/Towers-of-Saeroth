# Wild Speech  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Mimic Dedication]]; [[Feats/Animal Empathy (Ranger)]] or the ability to speak with a type of animal

---

Your connection to the denizens of the wilds grows ever stronger, and you find yourself able to speak with animals like you would in your native tongue. You can speak with all [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] and [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]]. If you have [[Feats/Animal Empathy (Ranger)|Animal Empathy]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on animals.
