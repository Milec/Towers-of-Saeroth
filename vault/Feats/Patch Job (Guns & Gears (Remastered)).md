# Patch Job  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 56

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Unions (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Vehicle Mechanic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vehicle Mechanic Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Vehicle Mechanic Dedication]]

---

You can patch up damage to an object with a quick fix. You gain the [[Feats/Improvised Repair]] general feat. When you use Improvised Repair, you can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check with the same DC as [[Actions/Repair]]. If you succeed, you can use the object as a normal object of its type until it takes damage rather than as a [[Rules/Shoddy Items|shoddy]] object of its type.
