# World-protector's Hospitality  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Meticulous Restorer]]

---

Through your spiritual power, you impede starvation and restore pantries. You can cast [[Spells/Create Food (Player Core)|_create food_]] and [[Spells/Cleanse Cuisine|_cleanse cuisine_]] as 2nd-rank [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] innate spells, each once per day. Additionally, when you cast these spells, you instantly restore Hit Points to the containers storing these magical comestibles as though you’ve succeeded at a [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] action using your proficiency in your spell DC in place of your [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] proficiency.
