# Zephyr Guard Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zephyr Guard]]

---

As a Zephyr Guard, you’re always vigilant against crime and threats to the city’s safety. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks against attempts to [[Actions/Palm an Object]], [[Actions/Steal]], or [[Actions/Conceal an Object]] (including you’re [[Actions/Seek|Seeking]] concealed objects). You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and Katapesh [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Zephyr Guard archetype.
