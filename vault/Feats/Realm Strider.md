# Realm Strider  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 112

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archfiend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archfiend Dedication]]

---

You can teleport across the battlefield, disappearing and appearing in a burst of hellfire and brimstone, a flash of lightning, a cloud of caustic gas, or other environmental hazard common within your planned realm. You can cast [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]] as a 4th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] innate spell at will. When you do, spaces adjacent to the one you leave from and the one you appear in are momentarily filled with a damaging energy common to your realm, dealing 4d6 damage of your realm’s damage type to creatures in the affected spaces ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher).
