# Armor Assist  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 227

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You are practiced in helping yourself and others don heavy gear. You can attempt an Athletics or Warfare Lore check with a DC determined by the GM (usually DC 15 for common armor, DC 20 for uncommon armor, and DC 25 for rare armor) to halve the time you take to [[Rules/Donning and Removing Armor (Player Core)|don armor]]. You can halve an ally's time to don armor by working with them to don the armor and succeeding at an Athletics or Warfare Lore check against the same DC.
