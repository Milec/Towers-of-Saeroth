# Scalding Spit  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Torch Goblin]]

---

Your bodily fluids burn with surprising volatility, as if you ran on oil instead of blood. As long as you are taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], you gain a boiling spit ranged unarmed attack with a range of 30 feet that deals 1d6 fire damage.
