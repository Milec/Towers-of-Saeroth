The power within your blood grows. You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Player Core)|basic spellcasting benefits]]. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the bloodrager archetype, add a common spell of the appropriate tradition and rank to your repertoire. In addition, when you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from your repertoire and you are at least [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1, increase the extra damage dealt by that spell from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] by 1.
