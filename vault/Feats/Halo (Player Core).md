# Halo  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

---

You have a halo of light and goodness that sheds [[Rules/Light|light]] with the effects of a divine [[Spells/Light|_light_]] cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You can [[Actions/Sustain a Spell|Sustain]] to suppress or reestablish the halo.
