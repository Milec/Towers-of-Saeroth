# Guerrilla Weaponry  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Guerrilla Dedication]]

---

You are especially skilled in transforming simple weapons that can be easily assembled from readily available materials into deadly weapons. In your hands, a blowgun's base damage increases from 1 to a 1d4 damage die, and it gains the deadly  d4 trait. In addition, you gain the following action.  

**Guerrilla Assault** single action **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** You Interact to reload a sling or blowgun and then Strike. If the Strike is successful and you were undetected or unnoticed by the target when you made the attack, you are now [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from the target after the attack, as they cannot tell  where the attack came from.
