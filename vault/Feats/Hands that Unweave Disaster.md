# Hands that Unweave Disaster  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Thief's Calling]]

---

Your hands move with uncanny sureness, dismantling danger. When you attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. You can expend a Mythic Point when attempting to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable a Device]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. As normal when attempting a check with mythic proficiency, this allows you to attempt to Disable a Device even if the check normally requires expert or greater proficiency.
