# See the Unseen  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goloma]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 117

---

You notice things that others can't see at all. While you're adjacent to an [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creature of your level or lower, it is instead only [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from you. You only need a successful DC 5 flat check to target a hidden creature.
