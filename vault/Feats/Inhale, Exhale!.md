# Inhale, Exhale! single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 212

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragonet Breath]]

---

You take an especially deep breath as a prelude to unleashing your dragonet breath on your foes. If your next action is to use your Dragonet Breath, add 5 feet to the length of the [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] or [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. If your breath deals damage, you also deal one additional die of damage.
