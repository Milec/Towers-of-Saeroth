# Catfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

---

Growing up among catfolk has taught you the traditional values of freedom, stewardship, and quick reactions to trouble. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Catfolk Lore]].
