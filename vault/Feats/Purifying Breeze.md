# Purifying Breeze single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 85

**Prerequisites**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spells

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to cast a non-cantrip spell with the [[Traits/Healing|healing]] trait.

---

You transfer the divine essence of your magic to the air around you, cleansing it of toxins. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against each airborne [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effect in a 15-foot radius around you. Regardless of your success or failure, until the beginning of your next turn, all creatures in the purified area gain a +1 status bonus to Fortitude saving throws.
