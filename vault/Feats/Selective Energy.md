# Selective Energy  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 123

---

As you call down divine power, you can prevent some enemies from benefiting or some allies from being hurt. When you cast a version of [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] that has an area, you can designate a number of creatures equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) that are not targeted by the spell.
