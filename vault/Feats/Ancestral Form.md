# Ancestral Form  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Bakuwa Lizardfolk]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Makari Lizardfolk]] heritage

Casting your memory back deep into the past, you take the form of an ancestral guardian. You can cast [[Spells/Animal Form (Player Core)|_animal form_]] on yourself as a 3rd-rank primal innate spell once per day. You must choose the crocodile form.
