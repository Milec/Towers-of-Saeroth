# Extend Elixir  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 67

---

Integrating your own personal energy into the elixirs you create causes them to affect you for longer. When you consume one of your alchemical items that has the [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] and [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] traits and a duration of 1 minute or longer, that elixir's duration is doubled. If you created the item with [[Actions/Quick Alchemy (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]], this can't cause it to exceed the 10-minute duration limit.
