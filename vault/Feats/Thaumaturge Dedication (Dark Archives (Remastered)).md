# Thaumaturge Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass (Player Core)|Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 49

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thaumaturge (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Thaumaturge]]

**Prerequisites**
Charisma +2

---

You've uncovered basic thaumaturgy. You become trained in thaumaturge class DC. Choose an [implement](Implements.aspx); you can use it to Glimpse Vulnerability, but don't gain its benefits. You also gain a few esoterica, allowing you to use [[Traits/Esoterica (Dark Archives (Remastered))|esoterica]] actions. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]; if you were already trained in these, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the Glimpse Vulnerability action.

 **Glimpse Vulnerability** single action ([[Traits/Esoterica (Dark Archives (Remastered))|esoterica]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirement** You are holding your implement; **Effect** You glimpse a hidden vulnerability and then strengthen it to aid you. Select a creature you can see. Until you Glimpse Vulnerability again, that target gains weakness 2 against your [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] and weapon Strikes.
