# Arcane Communication  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 40

---

You can deliver silent messages. You gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Touch Telepathy (Player Core)|touch telepathy]], allowing you to communicate silently and mentally with any creature you’re touching, as long as you share a language.

**Enhancement** You can send your messages even further. Your telepathy gains a range of 10 feet, but you still have to share a language with your target.
