# Drifting Pollen single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 35

---

A haze of pollen floats around you. A creature in your kinetic aura when you enter the stance, or who later enters the aura or ends its turn in the aura, attempts at a Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (sickened 2 on a critical failure) and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until it's no longer sickened. A creature attempts this save no more than once per round and doesn't attempt a new save if already affected.
