# Multilingual Cipher  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 178

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Linguist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Linguist Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You use multiple languages to create and break codes. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Decipher Writing]]. If another creature attempts to Decipher Writing you encoded, they take a –2 circumstance penalty unless they speak all the languages you used when you created the writing.
