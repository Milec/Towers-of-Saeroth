# Dueling Dance (Swashbuckler) single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 92

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dueling Parry (Swashbuckler)]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding only a single one-handed melee weapon and have your other hand or hands free.

---

Using your free hand as pivot and balance, you both attack and defend with your weapon. While you are in this stance, you constantly have the benefits of [[Feats/Dueling Parry (Swashbuckler)|Dueling Parry]].
