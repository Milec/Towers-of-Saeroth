# Goloma Courage  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goloma]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 116

---

The most important thing you've learned living with fear is how to overcome it. When you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect, you get a critical success instead. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves against fear effects and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Will DC against attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize]] you.
