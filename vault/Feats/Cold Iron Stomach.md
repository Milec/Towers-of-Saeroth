# Cold Iron Stomach  *Feat 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 81

---

You have steeled yourself against potent odors. Whenever you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition, you reduce the value of that condition by 1; if this reduces the value to 0, you avoid becoming sickened entirely. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] effects created by [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]] or [[Rules/Environmental Hazards|environmental hazards]] associated with the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]] (including all environmental hazards found in Tanglebriar).
