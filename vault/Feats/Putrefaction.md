# Putrefaction two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

---

You spoil the food and water nearby, even within your foes’ bodies. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. Enemies within 60 feet of you must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. You also cause edible food to rot and potable water to turn brackish within the area. Choose a number of [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|potions]] or alchemical [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixirs]] within the area equal to your key attribute modifier and attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] against each of them using [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] at mythic proficiency. Your counteract rank is half your level. If you succeed, the elixir or potion spoils and becomes a mundane item.
