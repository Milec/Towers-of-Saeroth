# Blessed One Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 162

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One]]

---

You are touched by a deity and gifted with the ability to alleviate the suffering of others. You gain the [[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]] devotion spell. It costs 1 Focus Point to cast a focus spell. This feat grants a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity. You can Refocus by meditating, whether you reflect on the deity granting the blessing or not, allowing your blessing to refill your focus pool. Your devotion spells from the blessed one archetype are divine spells.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the blessed one archetype.
