# Cindersoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

The fire of your elemental ancestor manifests like dying embers of a blaze, and your inner charcoal helps staunch bleeding, purify simple poisons, and absorb acid. The DC for you to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid, bleed, and poison damage]] is 10 instead of 15 (or 5 if you have particularly effective assistance).
