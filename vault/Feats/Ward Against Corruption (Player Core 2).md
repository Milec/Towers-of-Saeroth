# Ward Against Corruption  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

Your soul is warded against the power of undeath and plague, as well as that of the evil former psychopomps known as [[Bestiary/Families/Sahkil|sahkils]]. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], and all effects from [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] or [[Traits/Sahkil|sahkils]]. This bonus increases to +2 against an undead or sahkil's death effect or disease.
