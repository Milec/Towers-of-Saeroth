# Unerring Runic Attraction single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

---

Your runes act as beacons for your arrows and bolts. Make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against a foe you’re aware of that’s currently bearing a rune and within your first [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment]]. The ammunition homes in on your target, even going around corners. Your Strike doesn’t require [[Rules/Line of Sight (Player Core)|line of sight]], ignores the target’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition, and ignores all circumstance bonuses to AC granted from [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] and [[Rules/Shields (rules-3174)|shields]].
