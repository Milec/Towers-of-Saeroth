# Haphazard Repair single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 25

---

You quickly fix your innovation, at the cost of its stability. You [[Actions/Repair]] your innovation almost immediately. You don't have to place the innovation on a flat surface, but you do need to be adjacent to it (including holding or wearing it), as well as having a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]] as normal.
