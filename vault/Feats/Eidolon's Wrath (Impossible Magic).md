# Eidolon's Wrath  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

---

Your eidolon gains the [[Spells/Eidolon's Wrath|_eidolon’s wrath_]] link spell, which they cast, instead of you. You determine the damage type when you gain the feat: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]]. As normal, your eidolon shares your Focus Points.
