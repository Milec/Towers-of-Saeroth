# Sarangay Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 61

---

You listened carefully to the tales passed down among your community. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Sarangay Lore.
