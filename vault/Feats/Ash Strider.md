# Ash Strider two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 36

---

You discorporate into a cloud of whirling ash and Stride. If you have a fly Speed, you can [[Actions/Fly]] instead. This movement doesn't trigger reactions, you can move through occupied spaces and tiny cracks, and you ignore any [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain that wouldn't impede smoke. The first creature you pass through during this movement takes 3d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Ash lingers around you after your movement is complete, granting you [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] until the start of your next turn.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
