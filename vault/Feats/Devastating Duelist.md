# Devastating Duelist single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]

---

You pointedly note your enemy's deficiencies, filling them with fear and dismay. Strike an opponent with your [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]].

If the Strike is successful, attempt a Dueling [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] check against the target's Will DC. On a success, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2. This is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.
