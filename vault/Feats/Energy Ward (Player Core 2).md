# Energy Ward free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 154

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bloodrager]] (Level 12)

**Frequency**
once per turn

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast]] a non-cantrip spell that dealt energy damage.

---

You convert energy from the last spell you cast into a protective ward. Until the start of your next turn, you gain resistance to one type of energy ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]]) equal to 4 + the rank of the spell.
