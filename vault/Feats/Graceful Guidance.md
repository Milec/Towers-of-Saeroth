# Graceful Guidance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 18

---

You can demonstrate how to quickly twist out of the way of dangerous effects. You can use the [[Actions/Aid]] reaction to grant a bonus to an ally's Reflex save. As usual for Aid, you need to prepare by using an action on your turn to encourage the ally.
