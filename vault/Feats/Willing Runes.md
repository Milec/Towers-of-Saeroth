# Willing Runes  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 73

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Avenging Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Avenging Runelord Dedication]]

---

Magical markings of every kind seem eager to aid your endeavors. Whenever you read text, be it mundane or magical, the writing itself seems to almost to leap into your thoughts. You gain a +2 status bonus to checks to [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]], or any [[Rules/Research (GM Core)|Research]] check or [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check involving reading.  
You gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws and to your AC against effects from hazards that involve reading, such as those created by a [[Rituals/Rune Trap|_rune trap_]] ritual. If you were the one who triggered the hazard by reading the triggering runes, the status bonus increases to +4.  
If you prepare spells from a spellbook, you gain the following activity.
