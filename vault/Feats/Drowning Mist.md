# Drowning Mist two actions  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 228

---

Though not unique to Chelaxian kineticists, this [[Bestiary/Sarglagon (Monster Core)|sarglagon]]-inspired technique floods enemies’ lungs. Your elemental gate exudes a faint mist that feels as thick as standing water. The moisture doesn’t just tug at limbs; it forcibly seeps into the nostrils and lungs of air-breathing creatures. While in this stance, your aura is difficult terrain for any creature that doesn’t have a swim Speed. Creatures that can’t breathe water must [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|hold their breath]] while in the aura, and when such creatures take an action that reduces their remaining air by 1 or more rounds, they must also attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC (maximum once per round). You’re immune to the effects of your own Drowning Mist.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to the effects of this impulse for 24 hours.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** Moisture seeps into the creature’s lungs, making the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but sickened 2.
