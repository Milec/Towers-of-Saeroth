# Red Mantis Assassin Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Prey for Death

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 108

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Red Mantis Assassin (Prey for Death)|Red Mantis Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber (Player Core)|sawtooth sabers]]; worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek]]; member of the Red Mantis assassins

**Access**
You are from Mediogalti Island.

---

You have learned how to stalk your foes and slay them with a sawtooth saber. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Assassin Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. Whenever your proficiency in any weapon increases to expert or beyond, you also gain that new proficiency with sawtooth sabers.

 You become bound by [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek's]] anathema and can receive his sanctification.
