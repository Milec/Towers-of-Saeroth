# Elucidation free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 87

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Brilliant Vision]]

**Trigger**
You succeed at a saving throw to disbelieve an illusion.

---

You can cut through [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusions]] with such precision that all those around you find their senses sharpened as well. Whenever you disbelieve an illusion, all allies within 15 feet can use a free action to attempt a saving throw to disbelieve the illusion as well.
