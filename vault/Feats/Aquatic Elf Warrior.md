# Aquatic Elf Warrior  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

---

You have trained in the use of weapons both above and below water. You have familiarity with [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow (Player Core)|crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Heavy Crossbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|heavy crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|daggers]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spears]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|tridents]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons. You don’t take a –2 circumstance penalty to melee slashing or bludgeoning attacks while underwater.
