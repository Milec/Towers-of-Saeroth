# Ancestral Insight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 13

---

You have extensive knowledge of your ancestors' ancient origins. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Alghollthu Lore]] and Azlanti Lore. If you roll a critical failure on an [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu]] Lore or Azlanti Lore check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], you get a failure instead.
