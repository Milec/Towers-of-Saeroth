# Wall of Roses  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 85

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rose Warden Dedication]]

---

Roses are the symbol of Milani, and you can call forth a wall of them to stymie those who don't follow her teachings. You can cast [[Spells/Wall of Thorns (Player Core)|_wall of thorns_]] once per day as a 4th-rank divine innate spell. The wall is immune to fire and the piercing damage its thorns deal counts as silver for the purposes of resistances. Followers of Milani don't treat the wall's spaces as difficult terrain and don't take damage when they enter one of the wall's spaces.
