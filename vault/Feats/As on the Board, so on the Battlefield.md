# As on the Board, so on the Battlefield  *Feat 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 231

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Games Lore]]

---

You’re a master of strategy games, able to translate strategies and mechanics from these games to the actual battles they were created to simulate. You can use your proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Games Lore]] for anything that requires proficiency in Warfare Lore (such as prerequisites) and use your Games Lore modifier in place of your Warfare Lore modifier for checks. You can use Games Lore to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on military professionals, such as mercenaries and commanders. Your reputation as a strategist opens up opportunities while you’re in a military camp, near a battlefield, or somewhere else that has numerous non‑hostile soldiers, allowing you to [[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]] with Games Lore with a task level of at least your level – 1.

If you [[Rules/Skirmish Units|lead a troop]] and have had time to create a [[Rules/Skirmish Bonds|skirmish bond]] with them, you’ve also spent time going over the finer points of strategy and even played against many members of your troop. Once per day, you can roll Games Lore for initiative when leading this troop.
