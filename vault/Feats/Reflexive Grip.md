# Reflexive Grip  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 52

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swordmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Swordmaster Dedication]]

---

You keep hold of your weapons even when knocked out. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC when defending against checks to [[Traits/Disarm]] you. In addition, when you fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], you don't drop any weapons that you're currently wielding, though somebody else can remove them from you.
