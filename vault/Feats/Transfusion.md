5 or more sanguimancy Hit Points

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sanguimancer Dedication]]

---

You bring forth the regenerative properties of blood on behalf of an ally or yourself. You touch a willing creature and grant them [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 1 for 5 rounds. You can spend an additional 5 sanguimancy Hit Points to increase the fast healing by 1, to a maximum of 8.
