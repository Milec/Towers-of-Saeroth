# Flash your Badge two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Alkenstar Agent]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Alkenstar Agent Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

You make a fearsome show of your authority. Roll Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] each creature in a 30-foot cone. When you do so, Demoralize gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait, and creatures are affected if they can see you. If a target creature hasn't broken a law in the past week (as determined by the GM), the result of your check against that creature is one degree of success worse than the result you rolled.
