# Pitborn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 40

---

Your blood bears the mark of a [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|demon]], a living embodiment of sin from the fetid depths of the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]]. Demonic power pulses through your veins and manifests in a different way for each pitborn, whether you have webbed fingers and thrive in the water, large hands capable of wrestling larger foes, or some other manifestation. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. If you were already trained in Athletics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain any one common 1st-level skill feat with a prerequisite of trained in Athletics, as reflects the manifestation of your Abyssal blood.
