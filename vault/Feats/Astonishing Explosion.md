# Astonishing Explosion free action  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Additive|Additive 2]], [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 72

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Trigger**
You use [[Actions/Quick Alchemy]] to craft an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical bomb]] that deals [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage and is at least 2 levels lower than your advanced alchemy level.

---

Your bomb incorporates an explosive spark of wonder that leaves witnesses dumbfounded. Any target hit by your Strike with the bomb is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] by you for 1 round. On a critical success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].
