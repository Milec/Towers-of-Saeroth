# Craft Anything  *Feat 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 260

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You can find ways to craft just about anything, despite restrictions. As long as you have the appropriate Crafting skill feat (such as [[Feats/Magical Crafting]] for magic items) and meet the item’s level and proficiency requirement, you ignore just about any other requirement, such as being of a specific ancestry or providing spells. The only exceptions are requirements that add to the item’s cost, including castings of spells that themselves have a cost, and requirements of special items such as the _philosopher’s stone_ that have exclusive means of access and Crafting. The GM decides whether you can ignore a requirement.
