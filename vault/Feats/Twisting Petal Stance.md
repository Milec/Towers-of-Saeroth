# Twisting Petal Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 119

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

**Requirements**
You're [[Equipment/Armor/Unarmored (Player Core)|unarmored]].

---

Your body moves like a petal twisting on the wind, confounding your opponents while using their energy against them. You can make gale blossom strikes that deal 1d6 slashing damage. These strikes are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. While in Twisting Petal Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC to avoid being Shoved. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC to resist an opponent’s Feint attempt.
