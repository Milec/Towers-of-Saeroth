# Swimming Charge two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 18

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Add the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]] trait to Swimming Charge._

**Requirements**
You are swimming in water.

---

You swim directly into a foe, attempting to push them out of the way. [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to your Speed, attempting to enter the space of a foe that’s your size or smaller. Roll an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check and compare the result of the Fortitude DC of the target creature. If the foe can’t move into an empty space (if it is surrounded by solid objects or other creatures, for example), you automatically critically fail this check.

**Critical Success** The target takes bludgeoning damage equal to your level plus twice your Strength modifier and must move into the empty space directly behind it; you move into the space it vacated. The target must move even if doing so places it in harm’s way.  

**Success** As critical success, but the damage is equal to your level plus your Strength modifier.  

**Failure** The target takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier and you end your movement before entering its space.  

**Critical Failure** The target is unaffected and you end your movement before entering its space.
