# Intimidating Glare  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 257

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

You can [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] with a mere glare. When you do, Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, and you don’t take a penalty if the creature doesn’t understand your language.
