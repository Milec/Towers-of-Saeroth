# Sovereign's Blade two actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 137

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Prophesied Monarch]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Prophesied Monarch Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a melee weapon.

---

As you raise your weapon into the air, it begins to shed a [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] that pierces [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] and calls your knights to your side. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; you automatically [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] any magical darkness whose counteract rank is 7 or lower. Any knight who can see your raised weapon can immediately use their reaction to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice directly toward you, and any of your knights who are adjacent to you after this movement get a +2 status bonus to their attack rolls until the start of your next turn.
