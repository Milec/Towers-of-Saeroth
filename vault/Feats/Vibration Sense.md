# Vibration Sense  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

---

Your eidolon gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet. If [[Traits/Aquatic|aquatic]], it gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Wavesense|wavesense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet instead. If [[Traits/Amphibious|amphibious]], it gains both.
