# We've Met Before single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

**Requirements**
You’ve spent at least 1 minute in mutual conversation with a creature.

---

Your ability to discern information about those you interact with is without peer. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to gain a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill specific to the required creature for the next 24 hours; you have mythic proficiency with that skill and can use it to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about the creature, [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writings]] created by the creature, [[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]] or [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] in areas ruled by the creature, or perform other appropriate tasks as determined by the GM.
