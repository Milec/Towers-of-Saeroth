# Phase Out three actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
Your eidolon is a [[Companions/Eidolons/Anger Phantom|phantom]].

---

Your eidolon phases out of reality slightly, gaining resistance to all damage (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) equal to half your level. They’re not [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]], but can’t use [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] or actions that require a fully physical form, such as [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]]. Your eidolon can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect to return to their normal form.
