You dip and spin, unleashing a wide whirlpool of water. Make an Athletics check to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] each creature standing on the ground in a 10-foot emanation. These attacks all count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn’t increase until after you make all the attacks.
