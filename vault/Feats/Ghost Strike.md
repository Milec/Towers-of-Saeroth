# Ghost Strike two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Hunter Dedication]]

---

Once per day, you can infuse a weapon you are carrying with magical energies that allow it to strike true against [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. The weapon gains the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune for 10 minutes.

 At 10th level, you can apply this effect to two weapons instead of one.
