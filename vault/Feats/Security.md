# Security  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 95

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Shields of the Spirit|_shields of the spirit_]]

---

Those you shield can benefit from your lasting protection. You can cast [[Spells/Shields of the Spirit|_shields of the spirit_]] using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, you can choose one ally in your champion's aura to gain a spirit shield that accompanies it. For 1 minute, that ally gains the benefits of _shields of the spirit_, even while the ally isn't in your champion's aura and even if your shield isn't raised. If you create another companion shield, any previous one ends.
