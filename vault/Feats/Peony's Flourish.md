# Peony's Flourish three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 99

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding two fans, one in each hand.

---

As you spin around and create wide arching circles with your fans, you manifest a mosaic of peonies that confuses your enemies. Stride twice and then attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check against the Will DC of each creature you passed adjacent to.

**Critical Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for as long as they’re stunned.  

**Success** The creature is stunned 1 and dazzled for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune to further uses of Peony’s Flourish for 24 hours.
