# Rough Rider  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 48

---

You are especially good at riding traditional goblin mounts. You gain the [[Feats/Ride]] feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks to use [[Actions/Command an Animal]] on a [[Bestiary/Goblin Dog|goblin dog]] or [[Bestiary/Wolf|wolf]] mount. You can always select a [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|wolf]] as your animal companion, even if you would usually select an animal companion with the mount special ability, such as for a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|champion's steed ally]].
