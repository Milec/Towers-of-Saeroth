# Daring Flourish  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Firebrand Braggart]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Daring Act]]

---

You make quick use of an opening from your daring stunts. At the end of a successful Daring Act, you can make a melee Strike against the target or attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] the target. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the Strike you make during Daring Act as well as the next melee attack you attempt against it before the end of your turn.
