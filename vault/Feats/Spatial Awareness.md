# Spatial Awareness  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 109

---

Your study of different planes of existence makes you more attuned to teleportation effects. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus when you attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] effect.

When a teleportation effect occurs within 60 feet of you, you detect the location of the effect as a [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]]. If the effect is a creature teleporting away from that space, you know the general direction of the location to which it teleported but not the distance. However, you can attempt to [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]] within an hour of the teleportation to know the location to which the creature teleported within 10 miles on a success or 1 mile on a critical success.
