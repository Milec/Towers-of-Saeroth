# Psychic Rapport  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 24

---

Your telepathic sensitivity intensifies when you're affected by mental magic, making it easier for you to link to other creatures. After you Cast a Spell with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait, until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, and Intimidation checks, and to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]].
