# Friend of the Sea  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

---

You have established a companionship with a sea creature, often carrying them in a small, waterfilled globe when you are on land. You gain the [[Feats/Pet]] general feat. Your pet must be [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]], and it gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]] and tough abilities in addition to the two abilities you normally choose for a pet.
