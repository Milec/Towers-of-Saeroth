# Grim Insight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 32

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Umbral Gnome]] heritage

---

Others’ attempts to scare you often grant you insights about your would-be bullies that you can then exploit. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect, you get a critical success instead, and the source of the fear effect is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to you until the end of your next turn.
