# Seal of the Golden Dragon ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 117

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]; [[Feats/Golden Dragon's Bounty]]

**Trigger**
You would take [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage.

**Requirements**
Your familiar has the resistance ability and is adjacent to you.

---

Your familiar coils around you, protecting you with its magical resistance. You gain the benefits of your familiar’s [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] ability until the start of your next turn. If your familiar has any upgrades to this ability, such as [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Major Resistance (Player Core)|major resistance]], you gain them as well. Once per day, if your familiar’s resistance is a different damage type, your familiar’s resistance ability changes to the appropriate type. If you already have resistance of the same type, choose the higher of the two.
