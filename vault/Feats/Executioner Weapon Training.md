# Executioner Weapon Training  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 64

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

---

Your Gray Gardener training has made you deeply skilled with the weapons that execute with a swift chop to the neck. You become trained with the following weapons: [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion|falchion]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe|greataxe]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]]. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency with a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency for these weapons.
