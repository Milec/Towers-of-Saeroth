# Moray Eel Mount  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 20

---

You have either trained a [[Bestiary/Giant Moray Eel (Monster Core)|giant moray eel]] since its hatching or have tamed one in the wild. You gain the [[Feats/Bonded Animal (Player Core)|Bonded Animal]] skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites, to bond with a giant moray eel. When you [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command this Animal]] while you are mounted on it to take a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, you automatically succeed instead of needing to attempt a check.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Elver Pet]] or [[Feats/Growing Eel Friend]] ancestry feats, you can choose to have that pet become your bonded animal. You don’t need to spend 7 days of downtime to bond with the eel, and you automatically succeed at the [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check. You can then [[Actions/Retraining (Player Core)|retrain]] those feats.
