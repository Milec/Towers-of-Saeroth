# Affliction Mercy  *Feat 12*

The divine grace that flows through you grants reprieve from an affliction. Add mercy of recuperation to the types of mercy you can provide, allowing you to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an affliction instead of an effect causing a condition (typically an affliction is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]).
