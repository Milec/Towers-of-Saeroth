# Impressive Landing ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 82

**Trigger**
You fall at least 10 feet and land on a solid surface.

---

You slam into the ground, shattering it around you. Treat the fall as 10 feet shorter. You land on your feet. The ground in a 5-foot emanation becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Any creature in the emanation when you land takes 5 bludgeoning damage as the debris hits it and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of its next turn.
