# Vasodilation  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 84

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

Using the power of your own blood, you encourage increased blood flow to nourish damaged tissue or stifle the flow to clot wounds or prevent the spread of poison and disease. You no longer require a healer’s toolkit to [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]], [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]], [[Actions/Treat Poison (Player Core)|Treat Poison]], or [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], though you take 1 damage each time you take one of these actions without the proper tools from the expenditure of your blood. You can’t reduce or prevent this damage.

You gain a +1 item bonus when performing these actions using your blood, which increases to +2 if you’re a master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] and +3 if you’re legendary.
