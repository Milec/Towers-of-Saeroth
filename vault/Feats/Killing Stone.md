# Killing Stone  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Star Orb]]

---

Your star orb hides a deadly miasma. You can cast 5th-level [[Spells/Cloudkill|_cloudkill_]] as a divine innate spell once per day. The spell's normal components are replaced with a focus component (your star orb) and a somatic component to aim the orb towards the location for the spell.
