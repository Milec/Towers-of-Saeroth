# Necromantic Lifesense  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

---

Your connection to life and death allows you to sense the life force of a creature with a glance. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet. This allows you to sense the life force of living and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures within the listed range. The sense can distinguish between the vitality energy animating living creatures and the void energy animating undead creatures, much as sight distinguishes colors.

Additionally, you know the current Hit Points and [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] of any living or undead creature you can see within 30 feet.
