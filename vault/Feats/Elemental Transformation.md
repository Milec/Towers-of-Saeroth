# Elemental Transformation single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 23

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
exactly one kinetic element

**Requirements**
Your kinetic gate is deactivated.

---

You open your kinetic gate, but rather than activating your kinetic aura, you instead become overwhelmed with transformative elemental energy. You're affected by an [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]] spell with the same trait as your kinetic element. You can heighten it to any spell rank up to half your level rounded up.
