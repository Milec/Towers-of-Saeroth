# Momentary Respite free action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You lose Hit Points from persistent damage.

---

Your sense of destiny manifests as rapid physical healing. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. You regain 8d8 Hit Points and remove any [[Rules/Persistent Damage (Player Core)|persistent damage]] condition affecting you. Until the end of your next turn, you’re immune to persistent damage (though you would take any other effects of an attack or spell that would also give you persistent damage).
