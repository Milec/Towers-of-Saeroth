# Master Siege Engineer  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 129

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Leading with a Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist]], [[Archetypes/Munitions Master]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Artillerist Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist Dedication]]

---

What you don't know about artillery isn't worth knowing and your mastery allows you to perform an impossible number of actions as part of a [siege weapon](SiegeWeapons.aspx)'s crew. You're permanently [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]]. You can only use the extra action to [[Rules/Loading (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Aim]] or [[Rules/Launching (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Launch]] a siege weapon.
