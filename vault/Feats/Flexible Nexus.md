# Flexible Nexus  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

---

In bending the basic essences of magic around yourself, you have seen the ways they connect and flow into each other, changing from one to the next. Each time you use an ability granted by the Living Nexus archetype that deals [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage, you may change the damage type from force to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]]. The altered ability loses the force trait and gains the trait of the new damage type.
