# Hungry Blade single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spectral Dagger]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your _spectral dagger_ that dealt spirit damage.

---

Once your _spectral dagger_ has tasted an enemy’s soul, your [[Equipment/Splinter of Finality|_splinter of finality_]] becomes increasingly relentless in its attempts to claim the soul. Your _spectral dagger_ gains the effects of a [[Equipment/Keen (GM Core)|_keen_]] property rune that applies only to Strikes against the target of the required Strike. This effect lasts for 10 minutes or until that target dies, whichever comes first.
