# Scrutinizing Gaze  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 201

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

Your family’s traditions of defending against Geb’s undead have granted you a sixth sense for detecting spirits, haunts, and other restless dead, no matter their form or the strength of their presence. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks to [[Rules/Sense Motive (GM Core)|Sense Motive]] when trying to determine if a creature is possessed or under the influence of an effect that would make them [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks made to see through disguises worn by [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], and a +2 circumstance bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] haunts or undead within 30 feet of you.

If you aren’t using the Seek action or searching, the GM automatically rolls a secret check for you to notice [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]] or undead within 30 feet anyway. This check doesn’t gain the usual +2 circumstance bonus, and instead takes a –2 circumstance penalty.
