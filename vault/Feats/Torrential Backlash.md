# Torrential Backlash two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 169

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Manifestations|Curse Maelstrom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Curse Maelstrom Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are in a curse maelstrom state.

---

Calling upon forbidden practices, you release the curse from within yourself, allowing it free rein to destroy everything around you. All creatures within your curse maelstrom emanation except you take 1d6 negative damage for each level you have, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. Your curse maelstrom state then ends.
