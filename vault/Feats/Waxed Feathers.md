# Waxed Feathers  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 58

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Wavediver Tengu]] heritage

---

Your feathers are coated in a waxy substance that repels water. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects that have the [[Traits/Water|water]] trait. So long as you're in a dry place, you can spend one action to shake off any water that clings to your clothing and feathers to instantly become dry.
