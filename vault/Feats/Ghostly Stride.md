# Ghostly Stride single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 35

---

You fade into a slightly incorporeal form as you move. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions, and you can move through enemies’ spaces during it. If you have a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]], you can instead [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to half your fly Speed.
