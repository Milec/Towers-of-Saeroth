# Creed Magic  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 274

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Battle Harbinger]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Battle Harbinger Dedication]]

---

You’ve expanded your divine capabilities, granting you magic that better supports your combat focus. You gain two special 2nd-rank creed spell slots, which can be used to prepare [[Spells/Resist Energy (Player Core)|_resist energy_]], [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]], [[Spells/Sure Strike|_sure strike_]], and [[Spells/Water Breathing (Player Core)|_water breathing_]] as [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spells. At 10th level, the extra slots increase to 3rd rank and you add [[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]] and [[Spells/Heroism (Player Core)|_heroism_]] to the spells you can prepare in your creed spell slots. At 14th level, the extra slots increase to 4th rank and you add [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]] and [[Spells/Unfettered Movement|_unfettered movement_]].
