# History of Violence two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

---

Your attacks bring with them echoes of previous battles, calling the unquiet victims of your most brutal acts to savage your opponents. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If this Strike hits, you deal additional [[Traits/Void|void]] damage equal to your key attribute modifier to the target and all enemies within 30 feet. If your attack is a critical hit, you deal twice as much damage instead, and the target of your Strike takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]] equal to your key attribute modifier as your fallen foes attempt to drag the target into oblivion with them. This Strike counts as two attacks for calculating your multiple attack penalty.
