# Irresistible Bloom single action  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Olfactory]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 70

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

---

You bloom, sprouting flowers and releasing a pleasant smell, becoming irresistible. All creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Will save with a DC equal to your Class DC or Spell DC, whichever is higher. On a failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with you, and must spend at least one of its actions on its next turn moving toward you. On a critical failure, you acting [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] to that creature allows them to attempt another save, rather than automatically ending the fascination.

These effects last until the beginning of your next turn. You can spend one action at the beginning of your turn to [[Actions/Sustain]] the effects for an additional round. You can Sustain it in this way for a maximum of 1 minute.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
