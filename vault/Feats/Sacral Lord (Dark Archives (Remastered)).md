# Sacral Lord  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 177

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

By right of ritual or sheer stubbornness, you have claimed rulership of the land. Claim a defined territory, such as a small section of forest or river, or a district of a larger city. Within its borders, you can cast [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]], [[Spells/Guidance (Player Core)|_guidance_]], and [[Spells/Sigil (Player Core)|_sigil_]] as innate divine cantrips, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on creatures that have the [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]], or [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] traits.

**Special** You and your land are linked. Changes to one mirror themselves on the other—if you're depressed, gray rain and clammy mist might set in. These effects have no immediate mechanical impact, but they might give clues about problems afflicting the land.
