# Strix Vengeance ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Strix]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 137

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
You, or a strix ally you can see, are damaged by an enemy's critical hit.

---

You dedicate yourself to destroying those who harm your kin. Until the end of your next turn, you deal an additional +1d6 damage on Strikes against the triggering enemy. The bonus increases to +2d6 if you use a [[Equipment/Striking|_striking_]] weapon or unarmed attack and +3d6 if you use a _major striking_ weapon or unarmed attack.
