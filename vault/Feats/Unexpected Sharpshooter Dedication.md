# Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 142

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky|Unexpected Sharpshooter]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]]

---

Who needs skill when you’ve got dumb luck? Sometimes your shots hit miraculously, regardless of the fact that you seemed to slip when you pulled the trigger or that you weren’t aiming at your foe when you fired. You gain the Accidental Shot activity.

**Accidental Shot** two actions ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You make a Strike with a ranged weapon, rolling the attack and damage twice and using the better results for each. The attack ignores circumstance penalties to the attack roll and any [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] required due to the target being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]].

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained two other feats from the unexpected sharpshooter archetype.
