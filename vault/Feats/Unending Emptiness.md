# Unending Emptiness  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 226

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster]]

**Prerequisites**

You pool the power of shadow within your eyes, gaining the ability to gather darkness into a turbulent orb of crushing emptiness within your line of sight. You gain the [[Spells/Darklight|_darklight_]] domain spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.
