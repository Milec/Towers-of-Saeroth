# Slingshot Maneuver ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thlipit Contestant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Thlipit Contestant Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A willing ally enters a space within your lash’s reach using a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action.

---

You spin your lash out for your ally to grab on to, giving them a boost to their movement. You move the ally 15 feet into a square of their choice that is within reach of your lash. If the ally has movement remaining, they can continue their movement after completing the Slingshot Maneuver.
