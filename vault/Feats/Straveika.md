# Straveika  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

You descend from one of the truly ancient vampires, a nosferatu cursed to eternal life but not eternal youth. People call you straveika, or ancient-born. The vestiges of your forebear's powers of domination have left you with an understanding of how a creature's intentions change its behaviors. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive]] and Perception DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Lie]] to you.
