# Unending Subsistence  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 82

---

Your mythic power allows you to take a small amount of food and replicate it near endlessly. When you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] or [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] check to [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. Succeeding at the check always provides enough food and shelter for you and up to a dozen other creatures. On a critical success, all creatures you obtain food and shelter for gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saving throws for the next 24 hours.
