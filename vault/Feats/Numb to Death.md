# Numb to Death  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 105

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) For the purposes of Society play, remove the Prerequisites of Numb to Death that states “you have died at least once.''_

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Diehard]]; you have died at least once

---

Your past has left you numb to death’s call. The first time each day that you regain Hit Points while you are [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]], you regain additional Hit Points equal to your level, and you neither gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition nor increase the value of this condition.
