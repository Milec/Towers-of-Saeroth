# Hell of 1,000,000 Needles three actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 31

---

The landscape fills with monumental filaments of metal. The needles lance into a cube 30 feet on a side within 500 feet. Each creature in the area takes 13d6 piercing damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Each creature that fails its save is impaled, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (the DC is your class DC); a creature that critically failed is also [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] as long as it's impaled.

 The hell remains until the end of your next turn, but you can Sustain it up to 1 minute. Using this impulse again ends any previous one. The first time you Sustain it each round, lightning crisscrosses the needles. Each creature in the area takes 3d12 electricity damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Squares in the area are [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]. A creature takes 6 piercing damage for every square of the area it moves through.

---
**Level (20th)** The initial damage is 17d6, and the hazardous terrain damage is 7.
