# Call the Hurricane two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 32

---

Massive waves spiral around you, with you as the eye of the hurricane. The waves appear in a 20-foot emanation, or a 30-foot emanation if you're in a body of water. Each creature in the area takes 6d8 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature that fails its save is battered by the waves and [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet (or 20 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
