# Wylderheart Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 146

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wylderheart]]

**Prerequisites**
member of the Wylderhearts

**Access**
You’re from Kyonin.

---

You’re primed to face off against threats from the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]]. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] skill feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Demon Lore]]. If you were already trained in Demon Lore, you also become trained in a Lore skill of your choice. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls in encounters against [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], and if you tie with a fiend’s initiative roll, you go first.

Certain wylderheart feats give you focus spells. The rules for focus spells appear [[Rules/Focus Spells (Player Core)|here]]. When you gain your first wylderheart focus spell, you become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for the wylderheart archetype spells is Wisdom, and they’re [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] spells. You can Refocus by celebrating life or spending time in nature.
