# Underworld Investigator  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 60

---

Your work as an investigator makes you familiar with the criminal element. You might be a criminal yourself, or you might have gained an understanding of how they operate from the outside. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Underworld Lore]], and you gain your [[Actions/Pursue a Lead]] circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks to investigate the subject of your lead (such as checks to [[Actions/Steal]] a clue from a suspect or [[Actions/Pick a Lock]] to open a safe with damning documents).
