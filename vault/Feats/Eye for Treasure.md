# Eye for Treasure  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 42

---

You know good artisanship when you see it and can wax poetic about crafting techniques and forms. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] and gain a +1 circumstance bonus on all Crafting checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]]. If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. In addition, you gain the [[Feats/Crafter's Appraisal]] skill feat, enabling you to identify magic items using the Crafting skill.
