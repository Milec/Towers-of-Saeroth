Your stratagems benefit from your precise knowledge of anatomy. When you critically hit with an attack on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devising a Stratagem]], if your attack dealt piercing or slashing damage, you also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to your target.
