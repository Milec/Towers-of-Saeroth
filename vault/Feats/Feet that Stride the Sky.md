# Feet that Stride the Sky  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Acrobat's Calling]]

---

You feel the call of the sky, or perhaps a desperate urge to test the limits of the earth’s hold on you. When you or a creature you’re mounted on attempts a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to allow you or the mount to attempt the check at mythic proficiency; determine the DC using the Long Jump DCs, and increase your maximum distance to double your Speed.
