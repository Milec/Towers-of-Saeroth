# Majestic Proclamation two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Starlit Sentinel]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Starlit Sentinel Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re in your sentinel form.

---

You announce your name to your enemies, bringing your constellation to bear in a blinding display. Attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] all enemies within 30 feet. Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait when used this way. In addition to the regular effects of Demoralize, enemies become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute on a successful check (and also blinded for 1 round on a critical success).

You can use Majestic Proclamation as a single action if your previous action was Starlit Transformation.
