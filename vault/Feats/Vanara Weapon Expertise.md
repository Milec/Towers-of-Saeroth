# Vanara Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 57

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vanara Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Thanks to numerous joyous practice exercises, you've become an expert in vanara weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Chakram|chakram]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Katar|katar]], [panabas](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=265), [urumi](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=102), and all [[Traits/Vanara|vanara]] weapons in which you're trained.
