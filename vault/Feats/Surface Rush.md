You make a dedicated dash to enter the water or emerge onto land. If you begin on land when using this ability, you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], then [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] twice if you are in water. If you begin in water when using this ability, you Swim, then Stride twice if you are on land.
