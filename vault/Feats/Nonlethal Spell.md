# Nonlethal Spell single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 142

---

You can alter offensive spells to be less deadly. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that deals damage and doesn't have the [[Traits/Death|death]] or [[Traits/Negative|negative]] trait, that spell gains the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait.
