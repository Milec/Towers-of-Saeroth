# Shatter Defenses single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 149

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Blackjacket]] (Level 8), [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]] (Level 8)

**Requirements**
A frightened creature is in your melee reach.

---

Your offense exploits your enemy's fear. Make a melee Strike against a [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] creature. If you hit and deal damage, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until its frightened condition ends. If the target was already flat-footed to you when you damaged it with this Strike, it can't reduce its frightened value below 1 until the start of your next turn.
