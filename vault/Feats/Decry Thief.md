# Decry Thief  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zephyr Guard]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Zephyr Guard Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

When you attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]], you can make it harder for the target to flee, in place of the action’s normal effects.

**Critical Success** For 1 round, the target takes a –10-foot status penalty to Speed and a –4 status penalty to Stealth.  

**Success** For 1 round, the target takes a –5-foot status penalty to Speed and a –2 status penalty to Stealth.
