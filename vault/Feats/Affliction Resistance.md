# Affliction Resistance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 18

---

Your diet has strengthened you against diseases and poisons. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]]. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease or poison, you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|battle hardened fighter class feature]]), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.
