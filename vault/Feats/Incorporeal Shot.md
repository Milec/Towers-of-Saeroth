# Incorporeal Shot single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eldritch Archer (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

You can concentrate an immense amount of magic to create a piece of ammunition that phases through everything but your target. Make a bow or crossbow Strike against a foe who is [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to you (but not [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]). The ammunition travels to your target in a straight line, passing through any non-magical barriers or walls in its way, though magical barriers stop the arrow. The shot ignores all [[Rules/Cover|cover]], the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition, the hidden condition, and circumstance bonuses to AC from shields. It has a +4 status bonus to hit creatures wearing any type of armor. The Strike's damage can't be reduced with a [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction using a non-[[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] shield.
