# Obscured Emergence  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You are, by nature, a thing of gloomy woods and barren wilds, and when you choose show yourself, a bit of this gloom clings to you, obscuring your form with strange distortions or mist. When you stop being [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] due to your own actions (not due to someone successfully finding you), you gain [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] until the start of your next turn as people's eyes find it oddly hard to focus on you. As usual for concealment involving an obvious visual manifestation, you can't use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]].
