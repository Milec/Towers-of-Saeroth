# Channeled Protection single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 24

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Channeler's Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Channeler’s Stance and your last action was to Cast a Spell from your spell slots

---

Your apparition uses excess energy to protect you. You and all adjacent allies gain a +1 status bonus to your AC and to your Reflex saving throws until the start of your next turn. If the spell was at least 7th rank, the status bonus increase to +2.
