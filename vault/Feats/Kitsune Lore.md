# Kitsune Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 124

---

You excel at navigating social situations and keeping secrets. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Kitsune Lore]].
