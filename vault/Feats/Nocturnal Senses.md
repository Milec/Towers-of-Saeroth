# Nocturnal Senses  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision (Monster Core)|low-light vision]] or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent (Monster Core)|scent]]

---

Your senses gain even greater clarity. While raging, if you have [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]], and if you have scent the range of your [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]] [[Rules/Scent (Player Core)|scent]] increases to 60 feet.
