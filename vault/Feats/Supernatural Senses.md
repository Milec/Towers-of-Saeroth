# Supernatural Senses  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 109

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acute Scent]] or precise or imprecise [[Rules/Scent|scent]]

---

Your scent is preternaturally sharp, and you can always rely on your sense of smell to help guide you when your vision is compromised. When you target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] opponent while you are raging, you reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or to 9 for a hidden one.
