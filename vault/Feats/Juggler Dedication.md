# Juggler Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -151- The Show Must Go On|Pathfinder #151: The Show Must Go On]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Juggler]]

**Prerequisites**
Dexterity 14

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]; if you were already trained in Performance, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Juggle]] skill feat, even if you don’t meet its prerequisites.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained one other feat from the juggler archetype.
