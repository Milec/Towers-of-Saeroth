# Void Siphon (Necromancer) ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 35

**Trigger**
You would take [[Traits/Void|void]] damage.

**Requirements**
You are living.

---

You open your body to absorb [[Traits/Void|void]] energy. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to your level to the triggering void damage. After you take the triggering damage, you gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 round.
