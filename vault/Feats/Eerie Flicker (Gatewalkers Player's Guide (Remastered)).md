# Eerie Flicker ⤾  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 102

**Trigger**
An enemy’s Strike would hit you and you weren’t already [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by that enemy.

---

Your body flickers momentarily into the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]]. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] for 1 round, and the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] for concealment applies to the Strike that would have hit you. If the flat check fails, the [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] misses you.
