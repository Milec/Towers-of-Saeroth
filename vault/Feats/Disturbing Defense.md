# Disturbing Defense  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oozemorph Dedication]]

---

Your internal organs and blood vessels have shifted and changed, and even you don't know precisely where in your body they are. You gain resistance to precision damage equal to 2 + your number of class feats from the oozemorph archetype. The DC of your flat checks to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] is 13, rather than 15.
