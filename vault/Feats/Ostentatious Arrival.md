# Ostentatious Arrival free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

---

If the next action you take is to [[Actions/Manifest Eidolon|Manifest your Eidolon]] as a three-action activity, or to Cast a three-action summoning Spell, the creature appears in an explosion. All creatures except you in a 10-foot emanation around the creature you summoned or manifested take 1d4 fire damage per spell level for a summoning spell, or 1d4 damage per 2 levels for Manifesting your Eidolon. If the creature you summoned or manifested has the [[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]], or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait, the damage is that type instead (or cold damage for the water trait). If the creature has more than one of these traits, you choose which damage type to deal.
