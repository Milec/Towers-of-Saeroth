# Aura of Despair  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 94

**Prerequisites**
champion’s aura, unholy

---

Your presence as an avatar of evil makes your foes more susceptible to terror and makes it almost impossible for them to shake off fear when you are near. Enemies in your champion's aura take a –1 circumstance penalty to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]]. In addition, an enemy that ends its turn in  your champion's aura can't reduce the value of its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition below 1.
