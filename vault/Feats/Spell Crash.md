# Spell Crash two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 21

**Prerequisites**
Your [[Actions/Spellstrike]] is charged, and you have at least one spell prepared in a spell slot.

---

Instead of blending a spell into an attack, you can funnel the spell’s magic into a pure blast that expands out from you. You smash your weapon or fist into the ground and expend a spell prepared in one of your spell slots. The magic of the spell explodes out in your choice of a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], or 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. Each creature in the area takes 1d10 force damage per spell rank of the expended spell, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC. If your weapon has the [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]] trait, you push any creature that fails its save 10 feet; if your weapon has the [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait, any creature that fails its save falls prone. Your [[Actions/Spellstrike]] is no longer charged.

If the spell you expended could deal a type of damage other than force, you can change the explosion’s damage to that type and replace Spell Crash’s force trait with the corresponding trait.
