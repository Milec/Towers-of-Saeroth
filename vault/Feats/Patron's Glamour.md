# Patron's Glamour  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 62

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

---

Gathering up threads of your old connection, you can temporarily disguise yourself as your missing patron. You gain [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] as an innate spell of your patron’s tradition, automatically heightened to the rank of your witch cantrips, but you can use it only to disguise yourself as the [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] representation of your patron. Detection spells of lower rank than this _illusory disguise_ give your patron’s information rather than your own.
