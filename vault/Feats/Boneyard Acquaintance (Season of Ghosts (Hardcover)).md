# Boneyard Acquaintance  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 321

---

Before your soul returned from death, you interacted with a host of other souls on their way to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]]. One of these acquaintances owes you a favor. You can temporarily summon it to come to your aid, and it appears as a swirling mass of ectoplasm with indiscernible features and a translucent [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]], the favored weapon of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]]. This functions as [[Spells/Spiritual Guardian (Player Core)|_spiritual guardian_]] that you can cast once per day as a 9th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]].
