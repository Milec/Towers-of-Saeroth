# Morphic Strike  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 85

---

You can lash out with a portion of your previous self. You gain a melee unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] depending on your heritage, described below. You can use this unarmed Strike in either your humanoid form or your yaoguai form. These Strikes are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling group]]. Like other unarmed attacks, you can improve this attack with [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|handwraps of mighty blows]].

- Animal claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]), Damage 1d6 slashing
- Celestial spirit touch ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]), Damage 1d4 spirit
- Elements elemental current (magical), Damage 1d4; this ability deals the same damage type and gains the same elemental traits of the cantrip you gained from your heritage
- Object striking surface (sweep), Damage 1d8 bludgeoning or slashing (chosen when you gain this feat)
- Vegetation root ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]]), Damage 1d6 bludgeoning
