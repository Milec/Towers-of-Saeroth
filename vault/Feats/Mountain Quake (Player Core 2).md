# Mountain Quake single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 126

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mountain Stronghold (Player Core 2)|Mountain Stronghold]]

**Requirements**
You are in Mountain Stance.

---

You stomp, shaking the earth beneath you. Creatures on the ground within a 20-foot emanation take damage equal to your Strength modifier (minimum 0), with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, they also fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. After you use this action, you can't use it again for 1d4 rounds.

**Special** If you have this feat, the Dexterity modifier cap to your AC while using Mountain Stance increases from +1 to +2.
