# I Meant to Do That single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 143

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unexpected Sharpshooter]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was a ranged weapon Strike that missed a foe within 60 feet.

---

Somehow your stray bullet causes an unintended reaction that creates a problem for your enemy: perhaps a ricochet knocks your foe's weapon away or they stumble over stray debris in an attempt to dodge your bullets. Roll a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to attempt to [[Actions/Shove]], [[Actions/Trip]], or [[Actions/Disarm]] the foe you missed.
