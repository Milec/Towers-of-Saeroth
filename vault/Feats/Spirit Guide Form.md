# Spirit Guide Form two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scion of Domora Dedication]]

---

You fuse with your spirit guide, becoming a hybrid being with enhanced physical and spiritual power. This effect lasts 1 minute; you can end it early as a free action. During this time, you can't use any other actions granted by this archetype.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities, regardless of your type of spirit guide.

- You become [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]].
- You gain resistance 10 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], or [[Traits/Positive|positive]]; double resistance vs. non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]]).
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- An [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attack of the same type as your spirit guide (either claws or jaws), which is the only attack you can Strike with. You're trained with this attack. Your attack modifier is +29 or your normal unarmed attack modifier, whichever is higher. You use the listed statistics for the unarmed attack type:
- **Claws** ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Backstabber|backstabber]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]]) , **Damage** 3d6+5 slashing plus 1d6 force;
- **Jaws** ([[Traits/Forceful|forceful]]) , **Damage** 3d10+5 piercing plus 1d6 force.
