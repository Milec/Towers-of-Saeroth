# See You in Hell ⤾  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 81

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You are reduced to 0 Hit points by a creature but not immediately killed.

---

You aren't sure if you'll be reincarnated again, so you might as well take this foe with you. A blast of spiritual energy lashes out from the depths of your soul, targeting the creature who attacked you. The creature takes mental damage equal to the amount that reduced you to 0 Hit Points (basic Will save with a DC equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher).
