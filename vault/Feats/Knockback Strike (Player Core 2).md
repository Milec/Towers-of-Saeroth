# Knockback Strike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 124

---

You focus your strength into a blow powerful enough to push an enemy away from you. Make an unarmed Strike. If you hit, attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] the target. This attack uses the same multiple attack penalty as your Strike, and doesn't count toward your multiple attack penalty.
