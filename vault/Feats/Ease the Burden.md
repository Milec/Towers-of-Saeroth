An adjacent creature regains 100 Hit Points and reduces their [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] conditions by 1 (to a minimum of 0).
