# Transcend the Azimuth  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 81

---

As the earth drinks the light of sun and moon, so does your steady vow imbibe superlunary forces, ready to be unleashed with a moment’s mantra. You can cast [[Spells/Cosmic Form|_cosmic form_]] on yourself as a 7th-rank [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] innate spell once per day, your shadow lengthening and becoming a mandala of fundamental energies. In addition to the spell’s normal effects, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to switch between the sun battle form and the moon battle form once per turn.
