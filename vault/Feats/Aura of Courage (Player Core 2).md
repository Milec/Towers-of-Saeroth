# Aura of Courage  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 94

**Prerequisites**
champion’s aura, holy

---

You stand strong in the face of danger and inspire your allies to do the same. Whenever you become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], reduce the condition value by 1 (to a minimum of 0). At the end of your turn, each ally in your champion's aura reduces its frightened value by 1.
