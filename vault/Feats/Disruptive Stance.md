# Disruptive Stance single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 150

---

The slightest distraction can provoke your wrath, and you're prepared to foil enemies' actions. As long as you are in this stance, you can use [[Actions/Attack of Opportunity]] when a creature within your reach uses a [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] action, in addition to [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] and [[Traits/Move|move]] actions. Furthermore, you [[Rules/Disrupting Actions|disrupt]] a triggering concentrate or manipulate action if your Strike hits (not only if it's a critical hit).
