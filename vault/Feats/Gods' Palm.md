You control your spirit energy when you attack, using it to reinforce yourself or to thrust past your enemy’s physical defenses. Make a fist [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]; on a success, you can choose to either deal all damage from the attack as spirit damage, or deal damage as normal but gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to half your level that last for 1 round.
