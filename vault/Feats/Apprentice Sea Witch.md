# Apprentice Sea Witch  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 37

---

You're familiar with the basic spells that most educated merfolk know. Once per day, you can cast either [[Spells/Create Water (Player Core)|_create water_]] or [[Spells/Hydraulic Push (Player Core)|_hydraulic push_]] as a 1st-rank primal innate spell. Each time you cast _create water_, you choose if you create fresh water or salt water.
