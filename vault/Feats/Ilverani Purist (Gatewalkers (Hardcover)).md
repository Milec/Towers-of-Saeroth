All twilight speakers are trained to resist the alluring customs of younger peoples, but you have made it your mandate to keep the Ilverani way unchanged. Such dedication has given you a trained eye for subtlety and deception. When you attempt to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense the Motive]] of a non-elf humanoid creature and you roll a critical failure, you fail instead.
