# Lifesense  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

You have a limited ability to sense life force, like your [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomp]] forebears. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]] with a range of 10 feet. This allows you to sense the life force of living and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures within the listed range. The sense can distinguish between the vitality energy animating living creatures and the void energy animating undead creatures, much as sight distinguishes colors.
