# Athlete's Ally single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 85

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Warshard Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Warshard Warrior Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding your warshard weapon.

---

You channel the power of your warshard through your weapon to better perform athletic maneuvers. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. For 1 minute, when you attempt Athletics checks to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] with your warshard weapon, you do so at mythic proficiency. Note that this ability doesn’t grant additional weapon traits to your weapon, so your options for which of these maneuvers you can attempt might be limited by your weapon’s existing traits.
