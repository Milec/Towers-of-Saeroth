# Runtsage  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 34

---

Unlike most of your kind, who dismiss goblins as embarrassments or expendable annoyances, you have studied the methodology behind their irresponsible and incomprehensible actions. You gain the [[Feats/Adopted Ancestry]] general feat and must select goblin as the feat's chosen ancestry. You also gain one 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|goblin ancestry feat]].
