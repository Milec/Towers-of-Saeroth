# Abjure Harm ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ascended Celestial]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You would take damage from an enemy’s critical [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].

---

You’re nigh invincible, and it’s clear that your foe is so far beneath you that they have no hope of winning. Spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. The triggering [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] becomes a regular hit instead of a critical hit and you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to physical damage equal to your level until the beginning of your next turn. During your next turn, if you attempt a [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] check against the triggering creature, you do so at mythic proficiency.
