# Inspiring Recitation single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lion Blade (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lion Blade Dedication (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade Dedication]]

---

You quietly speak or recall coded phrases and metaphors whose meanings inspire you to complete your mission. You gain a +1 status bonus to one skill of your choice until the beginning of your next turn. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this effect for up to 1 minute. If you’re an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]], the bonus increases to +2. If you’re a master in Performance, the bonus is +3, and if you’re legendary, the bonus is +4.
