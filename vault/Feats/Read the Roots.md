# Read the Roots two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Scrying]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 48

**Requirements**
You are standing on the ground, and there are plants rooted in the ground within 100 feet of you.

---

The root systems of trees are a far-reaching network of information, and you know how to access them and extract their secrets. You [[Actions/Seek]] within 30 feet using [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]], an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense, instead of one of your own senses. You can also detect subjects touching or burrowing through the surface you are standing on in this way.
