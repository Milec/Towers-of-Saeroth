# Armor Break two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 46

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium or heavy armor that doesn’t have the broken condition, and your current Hit Points are at half your maximum or lower.

---

You flex your muscles to crack your damaged armor, blasting jagged shards into nearby enemies. Each enemy in a 10-foot  [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 11d6 piercing damage with a Reflex save against your class DC. You can [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|push]] any enemy that fails its save 5 feet away from you (or up to 10 feet on a critical failure). The damage increases by 1d6 per level beyond 14th. Your armor gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] condition. While your armor remains broken due to Armor Break, you don't take its penalty to Speed. In addition, if the broken armor has the [[Traits/Bulwark (Player Core)|bulwark]] trait, you retain its bonus to Reflex saves, but the bonus is reduced by 1.
