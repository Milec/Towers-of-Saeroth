# Courageous Opportunity ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Warrior|warrior muse]]

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach uses an [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] effect, [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] action, or [[Traits/Move|move]] action; makes a ranged attack; or leaves a square during its move action.

**Requirements**
You are affected by [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]].

---

You bellow a ferocious call to arms, inspiring yourself to lash out at a foe. Make a melee Strike against the triggering creature. If the attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a manipulate action, the action is disrupted.
