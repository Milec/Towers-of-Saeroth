# Cloaking Pulse  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 70

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ostilli Host Dedication]]

---

Your ostilli can emit illusion magic to mask your position, granting you the Drape Ambient Magic action.

 **Drape Ambient Magic** single action ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Your ostilli turns clear as it converts its stored magic into a bubble of refracting light around you. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to all creatures until the end of your turn. If you Strike a creature, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against that attack, and you then become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]].
