# Avenger of Gluttony  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

---

You've become the champion of the runelords of gluttony, and their hungry spirits are always near you. Whenever a spell or other magical effect restores Hit Points to you, you can choose to reduce the number of Hit Points you regain by any amount. The first time each day you reduce the Hit Points by an amount  equal to your level, you gain 1 Mythic Point. You gain the Gluttonous Feast activity.

**Activate—Gluttonous Feast** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Spend 1 Mythic Point. The spirits of four runelords of gluttony (Kaliphesta, Atharend, Aethusa, and Goparlis) briefly appear in spaces within 60 feet of you. Each living creature adjacent to at least one of the spirits takes 4d6 void damage per adjacent spirit, to a maximum of 16d6 void damage if they are adjacent to all four spirits. Each target receives a basic Fortitude save against the damage, against the higher of your class DC or spell DC, but Xanderghul, the Runelord of Pride, treats the result of his saving throw as one degree of success worse.

 You gain temporary Hit Points equal to half the damage a single creature takes from this effect; calculate these temporary Hit Points using the creature that took the most damage. These temporary Hit Points last 1 minute.

## Vengeance Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

During Chapter 2 of this adventure, the PCs can take on the vengeful mantle of a specific runelord's vengeance and embody that need to avenge the violations Runelord Xanderghul has inflicted upon their legacies by creating risen runelords. These rewards manifest in the form of a bonus feat, chosen from the following. A PC can only select one vengeance feat. If you wish to use these feats in the context of another campaign, a mythic PC should be required to use one of their mythic feat slots granted from their mythic destiny.
