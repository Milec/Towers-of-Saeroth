# Stalwart Chant  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stalwart Defender]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stalwart Song]]

---

You know story after story of unyielding, valiant dwarven heroes whose minds were bulwarks against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confusion]]. You can use Stalwart Song when attempting a saving throw against any [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect, and if you fail the saving throw, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to mental damage against any resulting effect equal to half your level.
