# Fluttering Distraction ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|Misfortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 99

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An enemy attempts a ranged or melee Strike against an ally within 30 feet.

**Requirements**
You're wielding two fans, one in each hand.

---

You’re trained in snapping and fluttering your fans to draw the eyes of observers around you. You manipulate your fans to create a distraction. The enemy must roll its attack twice and take the lower result.
