# Call Draconic Ally  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Draconic Acolyte]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Acolyte Dedication]]

---

Either by beseeching a dragon or bending it to your will, you conjure a mighty creature to assist you. Once per day while you are Channeling Draconic Essence, you can cast [[Spells/Summon Dragon (Player Core)|_summon dragon_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] with a tradition matching your draconic exemplar, heightened to half your level rounded up.
