# Herald's Weapon single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 280

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

Your weapon becomes a wrathful instrument of your deity’s power. Choose a weapon you are currently wielding or an unarmed attack, which is wreathed in pure spiritual energy for 1 minute. For the duration, the chosen weapon and unarmed attack deals [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage instead of its regular damage. In addition, the chosen weapon or unarmed attack gains the sanctified trait and deals an additional 2d4 spirit damage.
