# Bristle single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shisk]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 124

---

You curl into a posture that splays out your bone spines. You gain a +1 circumstance to AC until the start of your next turn.

**Special** If you have the [[Actions/Barbed Quills]] reaction or a quills unarmed attack, each gains a +1 circumstance bonus to damage rolls until the start of your next turn.
