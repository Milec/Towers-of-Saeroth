# No Stranger to Death  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication]]

---

Your regular exposure to the insidious putrescence of undeath inured you to its effects. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws made against spells and effects with the [[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Disease|disease]], and [[Traits/Evil|evil]] traits. Whenever you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], reduce the status penalty you take from the condition by 1, to a minimum of 0.
