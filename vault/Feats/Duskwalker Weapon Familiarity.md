# Duskwalker Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow (Player Core)|longbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|composite longbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe (Player Core)|scythe]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
