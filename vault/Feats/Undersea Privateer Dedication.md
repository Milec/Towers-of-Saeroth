# Undersea Privateer Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 96

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

Your training has taught you the basics of underwater combat. You become an expert in Athletics and gain the [[Feats/Underwater Marauder (Player Core)|Underwater Marauder]] skill feat. If you're at least an expert in the weapon you're using when fighting in or underwater, you get the critical specialization effect of that weapon when you get a critical hit with it.
