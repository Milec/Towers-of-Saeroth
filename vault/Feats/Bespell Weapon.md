# Bespell Weapon free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 79, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 199

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bloodrager]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Battle Harbinger]] (Level 6)

**Frequency**
once per turn

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to cast a non-cantrip spell.

---

You siphon the residual energy from the last spell you cast into one weapon you're wielding. Until the end of your turn, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 damage of a type depending on the school of the spell you just cast.

- **[[Traits/Abjuration]]** force damage
- **[[Traits/Conjuration]] or [[Traits/Transmutation]]** the same type as the weapon
- **[[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], or [[Traits/Illusion]]** mental damage
- **[[Traits/Evocation]]** a type the spell dealt, or force damage if the spell didn't deal damage
- **[[Traits/Necromancy]]** negative damage
