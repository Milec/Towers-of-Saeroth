# Coerce the Current two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 18

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Can be used in less than 20 feet of water, ending its effect at the water line._

**Requirements**
You are swimming in water.

---

With a flick of your tail, you direct the water around you to move creatures. You move water in a 20-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]; this line must be entirely in water. Each creature in this line must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. A creature can choose to fail the save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|pushed]] or pulled 5 feet in the direction of your choice. All creatures must be moved in the same direction.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature is pushed or pulled 10 feet.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the creature is pushed or pulled 10 feet, and the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.
