# Eye for Smugglers  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 35

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Steel Falcon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Gray Corsair Training]]

---

Your time checking seemingly innocent cargo ships for illicit goods has made you as canny as you are suspicious. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to locate hidden panels, secret doors or hatches, [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] objects, and similar items (at the GM's discretion). If you aren't using the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action or [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Search]], the GM automatically rolls a [[Traits/Secret (Player Core)|secret]] check for you to notice these types of objects when you pass within 10 feet of them.
