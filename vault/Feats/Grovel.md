# Grovel single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Kobold]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

With obsequious words and begging gestures, you convince your foe you're less of a threat. You attempt to [[Actions/Feint]] against a creature. Unlike a normal Feint, the creature can be within 30 feet instead of in your melee reach, and you make your check against its Will DC instead of its Perception DC.
