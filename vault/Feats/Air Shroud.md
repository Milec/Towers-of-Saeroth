# Air Shroud single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 24

---

Turbulent air suffuses your kinetic aura. This doesn't displace other matter but provides air for creatures in your kinetic aura that need air to breathe. Attacks with ranged weapons take a –1 circumstance penalty to their attack rolls if their paths pass through your kinetic aura. Air in your kinetic aura is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for flying creatures other than yourself.
