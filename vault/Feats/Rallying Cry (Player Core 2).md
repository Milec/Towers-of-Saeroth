# Rallying Cry two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 15

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You call out to your allies and push them to give their all in combat. All allies in a 30-foot emanation that can hear you gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level. In addition, these allies become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 minute but can use the extra action only to Step, Stride, or Strike.
