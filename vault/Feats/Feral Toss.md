# Feral Toss two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; your animal shape grants an antler, horn, or tusk unarmed attack

**Requirements**
You’re in hybrid or animal shape.

---

You hurl foes away with a shake of your head. You Strike with your antler, horn, or tusk. If you hit and damage a creature of your size or smaller, [[Rules/Forced Movement|push]] it 5 feet from you (10 feet on a critical hit). This is forced movement.
