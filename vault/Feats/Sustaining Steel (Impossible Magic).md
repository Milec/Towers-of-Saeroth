# Sustaining Steel free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Inexorable Iron (Impossible Magic)|inexorable iron hybrid study]]

**Trigger**
You [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] using a spell slot.

---

You pull magic from the surrounding area into your weapon, using its substance to transform the magic into restorative energy. You regain Hit Points equal to the triggering spell’s rank and roll to end any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]] you have. If you’re in the [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, the healing is equal to double the spell’s rank.

If the spell had the [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] or [[Traits/Void|void]] trait, any persistent vitality or void damage you have ends automatically, and you can change the damage type from Arcane Cascade to vitality or void if you wish. (Vitality damage typically damages only [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] or other creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]].)
