# Sawtooth Grapple two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
trained in [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber (Player Core)|sawtooth sabers]]

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

---

Your sawtooth saber training enables you to pin down an enemy using your twin blades. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with each weapon against the same creature. If you deal sneak attack damage, you can immediately attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature, even if you don’t have a hand free. If you dealt damage with both Strikes, your multiple attack penalty is not applied to the Grapple attempt.

**Special** You’re wielding two weapons, at least one of which is a sawtooth saber.
