# Scavenger's Search  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 26

---

You're always on the lookout for supplies and valuables. Each time you use the [[Actions/Seek]] action to search for objects (including secret doors and hazards), you can search for objects in your choice of a 10-foot emanation around you or an adjacent 15-foot-by-15-foot area, rather than a single adjacent 10-foot-by-10-foot area.
