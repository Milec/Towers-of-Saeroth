# Telluric Power  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 45

---

You channel strength from the earth beneath your feet to pummel your enemies. When making a melee [[Actions/Strike]] against a target who is standing on the same earth or stone surface as you are, you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to the number of weapon damage dice.
