# Expressive Face  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 110

---

Your face is constructed to move organically and show your emotions, making you more relatable to organic creatures. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. If you are already trained in Diplomacy, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. Additionally, you gain the [[Feats/Glad-Hand (Player Core)|Glad-Hand]] skill feat, even if you do not meet its prerequisites.

**Enhancement** You are particularly skilled at pouring your heart out to others. You gain the Plaintive Plea action.

**Plaintive Plea** single action ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You make an emotional appeal to convince someone during a diplomatic encounter. If your next activity is to attempt a Diplomacy check to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]], or to attempt to influence a creature during an [[Rules/Influence (GM Core)|influence encounter]], you can roll twice and take the better result.
