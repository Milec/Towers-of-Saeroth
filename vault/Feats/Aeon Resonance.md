# Aeon Resonance  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 240

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Embed Aeon Stone]]

---

You gain the resonant power of one embedded _[[Equipment/Aeon Stone|aeon stone]]_ as if it were placed in a [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|_wayfinder_]]. While you can embed multiple _aeon stones_ in your flesh, you can gain the resonance power from only one embedded stone at a time, selected each day when you make your daily preparations.

**Special** At 8th level, you can take this feat again. If you do, you gain the resonance powers of up to four invested _aeon stones_ instead of only one.
