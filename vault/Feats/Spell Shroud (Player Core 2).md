# Spell Shroud single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 157

---

Your spell shrouds you in a billowing cloud. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that targets you, you surround yourself in a 15-foot emanation of dense magical mist that lasts until the start of your next turn. All creatures within the cloud become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the cloud are concealed to creatures within it.
