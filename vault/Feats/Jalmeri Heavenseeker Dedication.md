# Jalmeri Heavenseeker Dedication  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [Jalmeri Heavenseeker](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=86)

**Access**
trained by a current or former member of a House of Perfection

---

Your skill and dedication have set you on a path to master the techniques of Jalmeray's greatest martial artists: victors of the Challenge of Sky and Heaven. You become trained in either [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]. You gain either the [[Feats/Ki Rush]] or [[Feats/Ki Strike]] monk feat, which grants you a ki spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] activity as a monk does. If you already have both these feats, you can instead choose a single 1st-level monk feat.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Jalmeri heavenseeker archetype.
