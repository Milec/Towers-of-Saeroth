# Anarchic Arcana  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ganzi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 97

---

The magic in your blood is unpredictable. When you make your daily preparations, roll 1d12 twice and consult the following list. 1: [[Spells/Acid Arrow|_acid arrow_]]; 2: [[Spells/Blur|_blur_]]; 3: [[Spells/Hideous Laughter|_hideous laughter_]]; 4: [[Spells/Humanoid Form|_humanoid form_]]; 5: [[Spells/Illusory Object|_illusory object_]]; 6: [[Spells/Mirror Image|_mirror image_]]; 7: [[Spells/Resist Energy|_resist energy_]]; 8: [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]]; 9: [[Spells/Shatter|_shatter_]]; 10: [[Spells/Sound Burst|_sound burst_]]; 11: [[Spells/Spider Climb|_spider climb_]]; 12: [[Spells/Telekinetic Maneuver|_telekinetic maneuver_]]. You can cast each of those two spells once during the following day (or one spell twice if you rolled doubles) as 2nd-level divine innate spells.
