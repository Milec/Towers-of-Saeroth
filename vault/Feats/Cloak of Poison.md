# Cloak of Poison two actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 43

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) “An unarmed attack or melee weapon without the reach trait” refers to all unarmed attacks (whether they have the reach trait or not), as well as melee weapons without the reach trait._

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You secrete a cloak of concentrated poison that harms any who dare attack you. For 1 minute, any creature that touches you or damages you with an unarmed attack or melee weapon without the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait takes 3d6 poison damage.
