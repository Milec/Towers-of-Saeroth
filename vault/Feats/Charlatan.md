# Charlatan  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 104

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

You’ve learned to mimic the abilities of spellcasters by studying their flourishes and words. When you activate a magic item to generate a spell effect, attempt a Deception check. If you succeed against an onlooker’s Perception DC, to that onlooker, you appear to [[Actions/Cast a Spell|Cast that Spell]] instead. Onlookers who successfully [[Feats/Recognize Spell|Recognize the Spell]] realize you used a magic item, though those who later successfully [[Actions/Identify Magic|Identify the Magic]] still can’t determine that the source of the effect was a magic item.
