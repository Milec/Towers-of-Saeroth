# Sensitive Nose  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 18

---

Your large black nose isn't just for show. You can pick up on the faintest scents near you and track them down. You gain [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Rules/Scent|scent]] with a range of 30 feet.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain out of this feat or into this feat.
