# Anarchic Arcana  *Feat 9*

The magic in your blood is unpredictable. When you make your daily preparations, roll 1d12 twice and consult the following list. 1: [[Spells/Acid Grip|_acid grip_]]; 2: [[Spells/Blur (Player Core)|_blur_]]; 3: [[Spells/Gecko Grip|_gecko grip_]]; 4: [[Spells/Humanoid Form (Player Core)|_humanoid form_]]; 5: [[Spells/Illusory Object (Player Core)|_illusory object_]]; 6: [[Spells/Laughing Fit|_laughing fit_]]; 7: [[Spells/Noise Blast|_noise blast_]]; 8: [[Spells/Resist Energy (Player Core)|_resist energy_]]; 9: [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]]; 10: [[Spells/Shatter (Player Core)|_shatter_]]; 11: [[Spells/Shrink (Player Core)|_shrink_]]; 12: [[Spells/Telekinetic Maneuver (Player Core)|_telekinetic maneuver_]]. You can cast each of those two spells once during the following day (or one spell twice if you rolled the same number) as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.
