# Defensive Needles  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 77

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Cactus Leshy]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Pine Leshy]] heritage

**Access**
Pine leshies have access to this feat

---

Your body is covered in sharp needles or spines which you can use to defend yourself. Each time you become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by a creature or an adjacent creature hits you with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, you twist and thrash your body around, dealing 2 piercing damage to that creature. This damage increases to 3 at 9th level, 4 at 13th level, and 5 at 17th level.
