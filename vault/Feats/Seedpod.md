# Seedpod  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 54

---

Your body produces a nearly endless supply of hard seedpods. You gain a seedpod ranged [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack with a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 10 feet that deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage; these Strikes have the [manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=639) trait. On a critical hit, a seedpod bursts, issuing forth a tangle of vegetation that imposes a –10-foot circumstance penalty on the target's Speed for 1 round. Seedpods do not add critical specialization effects.
