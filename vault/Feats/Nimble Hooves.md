# Nimble Hooves  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 40

---

Your legs end in hooves rather than feet, with joints and tendons that allow you to move with great haste. Your Speed increases by 5 feet.

**Special** The Speed increase from this feat isn't cumulative with any Speed increase from your ancestry feats (such as [[Feats/Nimble Elf]]).
