# Scholastic Identification  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archaeologist (Player Core 2)|Archaeologist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archaeologist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Archaeologist Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]

---

You have the knowledge needed to understand ancient texts and cultural artifacts. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] when [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Deciphering Writing]], no matter the type of writing you are examining. You can also use Society to [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]] when examining a magic item or location with cultural significance.
