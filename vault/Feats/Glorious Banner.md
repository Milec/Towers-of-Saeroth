# Glorious Banner  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 35

---

Your banner is an awesome sight to behold. Your commander's banner now affects a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (or an 80-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] if you are using Plant Banner); the status bonus to Will saves and DCs against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects granted to your allies increases  to +2; and you and affected allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Fortitude saves, and Reflex saves. Enemies within the aura of your commander's banner take a –2 status penalty to Will saves as long as they can see your banner.
