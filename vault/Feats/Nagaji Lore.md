# Nagaji Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 49

---

You're the vassal or apprentice of a learned [[Bestiary/Families/Naga|naga]], and you've studied the secrets of naga magic and concocting intricate poisons. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] and [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in your choice of Nagaji [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] or Naga Lore.
