# Provocator Dedication  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -159- All or Nothing|Pathfinder #159: All or Nothing]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Provocator]]

**Prerequisites**
Strength 14; Charisma 14

**Access**
Successfully defeat the  eberarks  in the Irorium.

---

For you, fighting is as much about style as skill. You become trained in simple weapons, martial weapons, and one advanced weapon of your choice. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using your chosen advanced weapon, you also apply the critical specialization of that weapon. You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] and your choice of either [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. If you were an expert in all three of these skills, you instead become an expert in a skill of your choice.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the provocator archetype.
