# Sour Bomb  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive (Player Core 2)|Additive]], [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|Olfactory]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 104

---

You can mix ingredients with a powerful sour smell into an alchemical bomb to make its explosion an overpowering stench. Any creature hit by the bomb must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC in addition to the bomb’s normal effects.

**Success** The creature is unaffected beyond a strong craving for pickles.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round from the smell.
