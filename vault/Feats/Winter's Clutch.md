# Winter's Clutch two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 32

---

Gleaming flakes of chilling snow fall in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet. Each creature in the area takes 2d4 cold damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. The ground in the area is covered in a snow drift, which is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Each square of the drift lasts until it melts, either naturally or until fire damage is dealt in that square.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d4.
