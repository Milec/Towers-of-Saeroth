# Overpowering Charge  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 110

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Barreling Charge]]

---

You trample your foes as you charge past them. When you use Barreling Charge and successfully move through a creature's space, that creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier. If you critically succeed, the creature takes twice this amount of damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of your next turn.
