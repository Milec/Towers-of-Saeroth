# Basic Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Pervasive Magic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 219

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cantrip Casting]]

---

You increase your ability to cast spells. You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes|basic spellcasting benefits]] granted by spellcasting archetypes, gaining a spell repertoire. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new level from the basic, expert, and master spellcasting benefits from pervasive magic feats, add a spell of the appropriate spell level to your repertoire: a common spell of the magical tradition matching your trait, or another spell of that tradition that you have learned or discovered.
