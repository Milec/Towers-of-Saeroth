# Unsheathing the Sword-Light two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Aloof Firmament|aloof firmament hybrid study]]; [[Actions/Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
Your Spellstrike is charged, and you're wielding a one-handed weapon in the sword group.

---

Honed through 10,000 battles, your sword’s mere light can shatter ambitions and break armies. Make a melee [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a sword, with a spell that isn’t a [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] or [[Traits/Focus|focus]] spell. Countless copies of your sword, made of light, fall around you, dealing damage equal to double the spell’s rank to each creature within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of the target, excluding you and the target of the Spellstrike. The damage is of the same type dealt by your sword Strike and is treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] and [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]].
