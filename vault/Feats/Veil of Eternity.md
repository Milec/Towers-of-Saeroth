You can remain within the interstices of reality longer. Your Part the Veil has a duration of one hour, though it still ends if you take a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]]. Additionally, while [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoiding Notice]] during exploration, you can also Part the Veil.
