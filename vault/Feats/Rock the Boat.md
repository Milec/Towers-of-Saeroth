# Rock the Boat ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach causes you to [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|fall]] or knocks you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

---

You know how easy it is for a falling person to cause another to lose their footing at the same time, and how to use that to your advantage. As you fall, you grab onto an enemy using your hands or legs, and pull them down with you. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the triggering creature’s Reflex DC. On a success, the creature falls with you (though it can still attempt to [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]]) or is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
