# Clotting Elixirs  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 63

---

Your restorative concoctions have a clotting effect on the drinker's blood. When a living creature drinks an [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait that you have created, it can immediately attempt a DC 10 flat check to remove any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].
