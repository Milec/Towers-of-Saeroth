# Menacing Prowess  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden League Xun]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Golden League Xun Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

Scaring off multiple people at once is your specialty. When you [[Actions/Demoralize]], you can target up to 2 creatures within 30 feet of you at once. If you are a master of Intimidation, you can Demoralize up to 4 creatures at once; if you're legendary, you can Demoralize up to 8 creatures at once.
