# Form a Flock three actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 213

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You call nearby wild dragonets to swarm to your side. The flock grants you 25 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last up to 1 minute. The flock disperses and the effect ends when the temporary Hit Points are gone, the minute elapses, or you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] Form a Flock. As long as you’re surrounded by your flock, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] 5 to area and splash damage and have a flock aura in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Enemies in this aura are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]].

When you use Form a Flock and while it lasts, you can take the Acrid Barrage action.

**Acrid Barrage** two actions **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Each enemy in your aura takes 5d6 acid damage and 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]] with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. The initial damage increases to 6d6 at 15th level and 7d6 at 18th level.
