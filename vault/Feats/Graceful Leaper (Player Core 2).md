# Graceful Leaper  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication (Player Core 2)|Acrobat Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

Mass and muscle are meaningless when you propel yourself through the air; only grace and balance matter. You can roll an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check instead of an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check when making a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]].
