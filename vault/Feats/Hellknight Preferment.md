# Hellknight Preferment  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]; passed the Hellknight Test; trained in [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight plate]]

---

You've forged your body to be able to stand toe-to-toe with a devil, and your mind has been shaped by the strictures of the Measure and the Chain. You've become a true Hellknight. You gain expert proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (or in another skill in which you're trained of your choice, if you were already an expert in Intimidation). You gain the armor specialization effects of [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Breastplate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight breastplate]], [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Half Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight half plate]], and [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight plate]], and your resistance from that armor specialization is 1 higher than normal. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Intimidation checks while wearing any Hellknight armor.

**Special** You can't take both this feat and [[Feats/Hellknight-Errant]] or [[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Preferment]].
