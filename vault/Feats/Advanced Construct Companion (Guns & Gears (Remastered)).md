# Advanced Construct Companion  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 26

**Prerequisites**
[construct companion](Rules.aspx?ID=1600)

---

You've upgraded your construct companion's power and decision-making ability. It becomes an [[Rules/Advanced Construct Companions|advanced construct companion]]. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Rules/Construct Companions (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Command a Construct]] action, your construct companion can still use 1 action on your turn that round to Stride or Strike.
