# Surki Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

---

Either through the tales of elders in your settlement or through the messages thumped by those who oversaw your generation dig, you hold the knowledge of many surkis before you. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] and the skill associated with the magical tradition from your magiphage ability ([[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] for [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] for [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] for [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] for [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]). If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Surki Lore]].
