# Ferocious Shape  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 136

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

You have mastered the shape of ferocious dinosaurs. Add the forms listed in [[Spells/Dinosaur Form|_dinosaur form_]] to your [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] list. Whenever you use _wild shape_ to take a form that grants you a specific Athletics modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to your Athletics checks.
