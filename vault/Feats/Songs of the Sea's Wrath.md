# Songs of the Sea's Wrath  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

---

You have learned songs that overwhelm and curse your foes. You can cast [[Spells/Repulsion (Player Core)|_repulsion_]] and [[Spells/Spellwrack (Player Core)|_spellwrack_]] as [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spells]] once per day each. When you cast these spells, you do so by performing haunting songs; each spell gains the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait.
