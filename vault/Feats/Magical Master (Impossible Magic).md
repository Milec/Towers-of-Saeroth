# Magical Master  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magical Adept (Impossible Magic)|Magical Adept]]

---

Your eidolon gains a bevy of lower-rank spells. Choose an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] of your eidolon’s tradition of each of the spell ranks from 1 to 7 in which your eidolon doesn’t currently have an innate spell from [[Feats/Magical Adept]]. Your eidolon can cast each once per day as innate spells. You can swap these spells at every even level, but your eidolon can never know more than one spell of each spell rank 1 through 7.
