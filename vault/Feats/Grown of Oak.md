# Grown of Oak  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 131

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]]

---

You can make your skin take on the woody endurance of an ancient tree and have your familiar follow suit. You can cast [[Spells/Oaken Resilience|_oaken resilience_]] at will as an innate primal spell with a spell rank one lower than the highest rank of spells you can cast. This spell targets you, as well as your leshy familiar if it’s within 30 feet of you.
