# Predictive Purchase (Rogue)  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue (Player Core)|Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 174

---

You somehow manage to have just the thing for the situation. You gain the [[Feats/Prescient Planner (Player Core)|Prescient Planner]] and [[Feats/Prescient Consumable (Player Core)|Prescient Consumable]] feats . When using Prescient Planner, you can draw the item from your backpack (or a similar container) as a 2-action activity, instead of taking 1 minute to do so.
