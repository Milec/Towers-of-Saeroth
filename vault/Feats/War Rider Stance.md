# War Rider Stance single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 220

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Drake Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Drake Rider Dedication]]; expert in martial weapons; dragon companion with a support benefit that includes damage

---

Your dragon’s constant breath threatens your foes. While in this stance, your dragon companion has an aura in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Each creature that starts its turn in the aura takes the damage listed under the dragon’s support benefit with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your mount’s [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC.
