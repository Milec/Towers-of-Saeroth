# Commitment to Liberty two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eagle Knight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eagle Knight Dedication]]

**Requirements**
A creature has an ally [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You can’t abide when a foe has one of your allies in its grip. Make a Strike against the required creature. If this Strike hits, the grabbed or restrained ally can immediately attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as a free action. If the Strike was a critical hit, that ally gains a +2 circumstance bonus to their Escape attempt.
