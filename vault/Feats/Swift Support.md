**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nidalese Horselord Dedication]]

---

Nidalese horses are known as some of the fastest across all Golarion, and your mount has trained to ride quickly into battle. When you command your horse to take the [[Actions/Support (Player Core)|Support]] action, it gains a +5-foot circumstance bonus to its Speed until the start of your next turn.
