# Emissary of Peace three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Vigilant]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]

---

Even when bloodshed is inevitable, you would rather see your foes surrender. You attempt a Diplomacy check to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] against a single foe whose Hit Points are at less than half of their maximum value, followed by an immediate [[Actions/Request]] to convince that creature to cease fighting. You take a –5 penalty to your Diplomacy check. The GM sets the DC of the Request based on the circumstances; it's generally at least a very hard DC for the creature's level. Some creatures might simply refuse, and those who agree to parley might find your arguments lacking and start fighting again.
