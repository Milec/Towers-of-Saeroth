# Animal Senses  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beastkin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 80

---

You gain one of the following senses available to your inherent animal: [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], or [[Rules/Scent|scent (imprecise)]] 30 feet. You must have low-light vision before you can gain darkvision with this feat. If your inherent animal doesn't typically have a specific type of sense, you can't gain that sense with this feat.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different sense each time.
