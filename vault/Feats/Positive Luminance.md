# Positive Luminance  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hallowed Necromancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hallowed Necromancer Dedication]]

---

You learn how to create a luminous reservoir of positive energy that you can use to punish undead who dare attack you. You gain the domain spell [[Spells/Positive Luminance|_positive luminance_]]. It costs 1 Focus Point to cast a focus spell. This feat grants a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity.
