# Adrenaline Rush  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 108

---

In the heat of battle, you are capable of amazing feats of strength. While you are raging, increase your encumbered and maximum Bulk limits by 2; you also gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to lift heavy objects, [[Actions/Escape]], and [[Actions/Force Open]].
