# Grimspawn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 79

---

Your lineage traces back to a [[Bestiary/Families/Daemon|daemon]], one of the manifestations of horrific forms of death that devour souls within their foul home of [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon]]. As a result, you cling tenaciously to the last shreds of your own life force. You gain the [[Feats/Diehard]] general feat.
