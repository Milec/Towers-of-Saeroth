# Blessed Blood (Nephilim)  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

---

Your spilled blood is sanctified, with effects similar to those of [[Equipment/Holy Water|holy water]]. Whenever a [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], [[Traits/Undead|undead]], or creature with a weakness to holy drinks your blood or deals piercing or slashing damage to you with jaws, fangs, or a similar attack, that creature takes 1d6 spirit damage with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] checks to [[Actions/Craft]] holy water using your blood as one of the ingredients.
