# Winter's Embrace  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Gelid Shard]]

---

Your eyes are accustomed to the harsh glare of the sun on snow and ice. You gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects that inflict the [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] condition. Snow doesn't impair your vision; you ignore [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] from snowfall. Your skin becomes cold to the touch, and sometimes frost forms on you. You are protected from [[Rules/Temperature|severe cold and heat]].
