# Unfettering Strike single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 85

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rose Warden Dedication]]

**Requirements**
A creature within your reach has an ally [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

With a damaging blow, you cause a foe to loosen their grip on an ally they're holding down. Make a melee Strike against the creature. If you hit and deal damage, your ally can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] from being grabbed or restrained as a free action. If your Strike is a critical hit, your ally gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the attempt.
