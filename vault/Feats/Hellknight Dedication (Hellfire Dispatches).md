# Hellknight Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 24

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
trained by a Hellknight Order

**Access**
You're from Old Cheliax.

---

You've started walking the path toward becoming a true Hellknight, though you're currently considered only an armiger. You've been taught how to terrorize others into compliance, but you also continually study the structure and hierarchy of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]]. You must survive painful tests of your body and mind called reckonings that steady your willpower against all sorts of trauma. You gain resistance to mental damage equal to 1 + your number of archetype class feats from the Hellknight archetype. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead.

In addition, you gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Hell Lore; if you were already trained in Hell Lore, you also become trained in a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill of your choice.
