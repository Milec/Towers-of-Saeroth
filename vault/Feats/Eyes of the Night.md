# Eyes of the Night  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

**Prerequisites**
[[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]

---

You can see in the darkness as easily as a true vampire. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into or out of this feat.
