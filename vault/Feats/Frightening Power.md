# Frightening Power single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Draconic Acolyte]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Acolyte Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are holding or wearing your _draconic gift_.

---

Draconic essence coruscates around you, sapping the resilience of your foes. Each enemy in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Will saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If you are Channeling Draconic Essence, your spectral dragon can be the origin of this emanation. Regardless of the result of its saving throw, a creature that attempts a save is temporarily immune to your Frightening Power for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 4.
