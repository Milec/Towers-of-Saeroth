# Ceremony of the Evened Hand  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conrasu]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 108

---

You have cultivated your exoskeleton's form for close combat, using knots, whorls, and reinforced branches. When you select this feat, you gain a claws [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits or a branch unarmed attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage and has the [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]] trait. Each of these unarmed attacks is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group and uses one of your hands.

**Special** You can select this feat twice, choosing the other unarmed attack the second time you take it.
