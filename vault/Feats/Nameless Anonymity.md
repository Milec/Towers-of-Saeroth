# Nameless Anonymity  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 95

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Magic Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magic Warrior Dedication]]

---

Your mask protects you further from divination. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] on yourself, heightened to the highest level of spell you can cast; the spell ends immediately if your mask is removed for even a moment. The spell is of the same tradition as your focus spells.
