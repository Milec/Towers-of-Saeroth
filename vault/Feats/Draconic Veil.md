# Draconic Veil  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 47

---

Like some dragons, your forebear had the supernatural ability to change their shape to walk among humanoids. You have learned how to mimic this ability. You can cast [[Spells/Humanoid Form (Player Core)|_humanoid form_]] as a 5th-rank innate spell once per day, except that the duration is increased to 1 hour. The spell's tradition matches your draconic exemplar's.
