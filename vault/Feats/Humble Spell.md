# Humble Spell single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hedge Mage]]

**Prerequisites**

---

For basic spells, basic movements; you can do without flamboyant gestures or showy signs of power. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a non-cantrip Spell]], it loses the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait. The trait hides only the spell’s spellcasting actions and manifestations, not its effects, so an observer might still see a ray streak out from you or see you vanish into thin air.
