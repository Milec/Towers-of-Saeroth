**Prerequisites**

You wave around fabricated evidence that contradicts what your enemies know to be true. When you Fabricate Truth, you  can affect all enemies within 30 feet of you. Compare your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check result to the Will DCs of all targets. It is possible to get a different degree of success for each target.
