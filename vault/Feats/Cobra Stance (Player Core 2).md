# Cobra Stance single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 121

---

You enter a tightly coiled stance, with your hands poised as venomous fangs. While in this stance, the only Strikes you can make are cobra fang unarmed attacks. These deal 1d4 piercing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Venomous|venomous]] traits.

 While in Cobra Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves and your Fortitude DC, and you gain poison resistance equal to half your level.
