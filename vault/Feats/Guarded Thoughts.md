# Guarded Thoughts  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 50

---

Your mind, like a [[Bestiary/Dark Naga|dark naga's]], foils attempts to read your thoughts. Any effect that specifically attempts to read your mind to glean information must succeed at a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the higher of your class DC or your spell DC to do so successfully; otherwise, it gains no information. The counteract level is equal to half your level rounded up.
