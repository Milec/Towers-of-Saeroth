# Shape of the Cloud Dragon  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Instinct of the Wyrm|Dragon Disciple]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragon Disciple Dedication]]

---

You've discovered how to transform yourself into a cloud dragon. Once per day, you can cast 7th-level [[Spells/Dragon Form|_dragon form_]] as an innate arcane spell, transforming into a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud|cloud dragon]]. The spell automatically heightens to 8th level if you're 16th level and 9th level if you're 18th level. On a critical hit with an unarmed Strike gained from _dragon form_, you recharge the spell's breath weapon immediately.

 While in this form, you gain the following cloud dragon–specific abilities:

- **Cloud** cloud walk (you can walk on clouds or fog); **Melee** jaws, **Damage** 2d10 piercing plus 1d12 electricity; **Melee** claw ([[Traits/Agile|agile]]), **Damage** 3d10 slashing; **Melee** tail ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10 bludgeoning; **Melee** horn ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 3d8 piercing; breath weapon 30-foot cone, 6d12 electricity.

**Special** You can use this feat in place of [[Feats/Shape of the Dragon]] to fulfill feat requirements.

## Bloodsalt Dragon Disciple Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 79

The following archetype feats are for the dragon disciple archetype. You must take the Dragon Disciple Dedication feat before selecting any of these feats, and you must select cloud dragon (which is associated with the electricity energy type) as the type of dragon you've studied. The feats below are dragon disciple archetype feats. By studying the ruins (see the AP for full details), you gain access to the dragon disciple archetype and to these feats

 If you have the [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic|draconic sorcerer]] bloodline, you must choose cloud dragon as your dragon type. This choice affects the [[Spells/Dragon Breath|_dragon breath_]] and [[Spells/Dragon Claws|_dragon claws_]] bloodline spells and several of the feats below.
