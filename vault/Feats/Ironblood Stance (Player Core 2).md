# Ironblood Stance single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 123

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

You enter the stance of impenetrable iron, refusing to yield to any blow. You can make iron sweep unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]], [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

While in Ironblood Stance, you gain resistance 2 to all damage. The resistance increases to 3 at 12th level, to 4 at 16th level, and to 5 at 20th level.
