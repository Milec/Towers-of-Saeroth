# Awesome Blow  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knockback (Player Core 2)|Knockback]]

---

Your attacks are so powerful, they can flatten your opponents. When you use Knockback, you can attempt an Athletics check against your target's Fortitude DC.

**Critical Success** You gain the critical success effect of a [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], then the critical success effect of a [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] against the target.  

**Success** You gain the success effect of a Shove, then the success effect of a Trip against the target.  

**Failure** You gain the normal effect of Knockback.
