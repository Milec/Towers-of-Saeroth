# Transcendent Deflection ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An enemy within your reach would damage you or your ally with an attack.

**Requirements**
You're wielding a one-handed, [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] melee weapon.

---

You charge your weapon with spiritual energy and intercept the attack. The weapon becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]], and the target is unharmed by the attack. If you’re carrying another one-handed, [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] melee weapon, you can immediately [[Rules/Drawing and Stowing Items|Swap]] it for the broken weapon.
