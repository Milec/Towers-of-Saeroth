# Trampling Charge three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 165

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]], [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are riding a mount that has a melee Strike using its legs (claw, talons, hoof, etc.).

---

You urge your mount forward, trampling enemies in your path. You command your mount to Stride up to its Speed (or to [[Actions/Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim]], if it has the corresponding movement type), moving through the spaces of any foes in your path up to one size smaller than your mount. Your mount deals damage equal to the melee Strike using its legs to each creature whose space you move through, subject to a basic Reflex save against your mount's [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] DC. On a critical failure, the creature also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of your next turn. You can damage a given creature only once during this movement.
