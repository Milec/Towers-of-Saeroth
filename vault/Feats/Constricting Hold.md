# Constricting Hold single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 70

**Requirements**
Your eidolon has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

---

Your eidolon constricts the creature, dealing bludgeoning damage equal to your eidolon's level plus its Strength modifier, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your spell DC.
