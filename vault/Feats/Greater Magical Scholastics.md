# Greater Magical Scholastics  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 156

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archaeologist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archaeologist Dedication]]; [[Feats/Magical Scholastics]]

---

You broaden your magical studies, allowing you to find the right path, detect objects, and conceal those objects from unscrupulous rivals. You can cast [[Spells/Augury|_augury_]], [[Spells/Locate|_locate_]], and [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] as occult innate spells, each once per day. You can cast this _nondetection_ spell only on an object, and it is automatically heightened to the same spell level as your cantrips from [[Feats/Magical Scholastics]].
