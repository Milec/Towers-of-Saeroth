# Student of Perfection Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Student of Perfection]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|powerful fist]] or expert in unarmed attacks; member of a House of Perfection

**Access**
You are from Jalmeray.

---

You have learned the martial arts techniques of your House of Perfection. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and Warfare [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. You gain the [[Feats/Ki Strike]] monk class feat, which grants you the [[Spells/Ki Strike|_ki strike_]] ki spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the Refocus activity as a monk does. Your ki spells from Student of Perfection are occult spells.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the student of perfection archetype.
