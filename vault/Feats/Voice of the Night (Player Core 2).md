# Voice of the Night  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

You hear and understand the words of creatures you share the darkness with. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill with bats, rats, and wolves. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Make an Impression on such animals.
