# Vicious Fangs  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Prerequisites**

You aim for your victim's prominent arteries. Your fangs Strike deals an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] on a critical hit. At 14th level, the persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6, and at 20th level, the persistent bleed damage increases to 3d6.
