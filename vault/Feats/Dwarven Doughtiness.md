# Dwarven Doughtiness  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 42

---

You are either naturally calm and collected in the face of imminent danger, or you are very good at faking it. At the end of your turn, reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition by 2 instead of 1.
