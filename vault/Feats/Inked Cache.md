# Inked Cache  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 37

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Talon's Mark]]

---

You've learned to use your Twilight Talon's mark to secret away contraband. The tattoo's level increases to 10, unless it was already higher, and it gains the abilities of a [[Equipment/Retrieval Belt|_greater retrieval belt_]]. As a part of activating the tattoo to Store Item, you can attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check against the Perception DCs of all creatures that are currently observing you. On a success, a creature doesn't notice you use Store Item.
