# Ascendant Blood  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 217

**Prerequisites**
[draconic exemplar](DraconicBenefactors.aspx) associated with a specific plane (such as a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Diabolic|diabolic dragon]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Empyreal|empyreal dragon]])

---

Twice per week, you can cast [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] spell of the tradition corresponding to your draconic exemplar. This can be used only to travel to the plane associated with your draconic exemplar (for instance, [[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]] if your draconic nature is tied to an empyreal dragon) or from that plane to [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]]. Due to your draconic blood, your body serves as the locus, and you don’t require a planar key. If there’s any question about the plane to which your draconic exemplar is associated, the GM determines the answer.
