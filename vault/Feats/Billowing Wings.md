# Billowing Wings  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 67

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Beastmaster (Player Core 2)|Beastmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beastmaster Dedication]]; animal companion with wings

---

You've trained your winged companions to gather air in their wings, whipping up a concentrated gust of wind at opponents, potentially staggering them. Your companions with wings gain a gust ranged [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack, which deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage with a range of 30 feet and has the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] and [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]] traits. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] back 5 feet. This is forced movement.
