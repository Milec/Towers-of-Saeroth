# Windborne Shove  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Guardian's Calling|guardian's calling]]

---

The hero-god of phalanxes and winds, grand Konebrys, led a force of dozens against an army of thousands. They gathered their soldiers in tight ranks, then marched against the wind to take the enemy by surprise. When a gust pushed the foes back, they struck their victorious blow.

You emulate Konebrys’s tactics in pushing small forces against mighty foes. Whenever you are adjacent to an ally, creatures that attempt to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] your space take a –2 circumstance penalty to their [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check.

When you critically succeed on a melee Strike against an enemy, you can immediately attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] your target as a free action. You can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] on this Shove attempt to do so at [[Rules/Mythic Proficiency|mythic proficiency]].
