# Stonewall ⤾  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 45

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
An enemy or hazard’s effect hits you or you fail a Fortitude save against one

---

The strength of stone overcomes you so strongly that it replaces your stout body. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]] until the end of the current turn. You don't take any damage from the triggering effect or any other ill effects that couldn't affect stone.
