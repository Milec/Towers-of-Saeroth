# Suplex single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 223

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Flexing your entire body, you heave your opponent over your head and slam them into the ground. Make an unarmed melee Strike against the creature you have [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]. On a success, the target lands [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and on a critical success, the target lands prone and takes an additional 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Regardless of whether the Strike is successful, you immediately release your hold on the target.
