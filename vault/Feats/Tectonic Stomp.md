# Tectonic Stomp two actions  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 10, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 101

---

You cause minor tremors that topple your enemies. All creatures in a 30-foot emanation who are standing on the ground take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 2 levels you have, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that fails its save also falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
