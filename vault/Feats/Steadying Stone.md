# Steadying Stone  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 198

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Stone|stone order]]

---

The earth has taught you how to remain unyielding and firm. If you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check made to [[Actions/Balance]] on uneven ground composed of earth or rock, you get a critical success instead. As long as you remain on the ground, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip]] you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against spells or effects that would attempt to knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. If you're a [[Ancestries/Heritages/Rock Dwarf|rock dwarf]], this bonus increases to +3.
