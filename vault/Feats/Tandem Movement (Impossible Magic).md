# Tandem Movement single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]], [[Traits/Tandem (Impossible Magic)|Tandem]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

---

You and your eidolon move together. You each use a single action to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. Either of you that has the corresponding movement type can [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead.
