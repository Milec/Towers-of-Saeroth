# Overburdening Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

---

You have learned how to weigh down your enemies’ life essence. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that targets a single creature, unless the target is unaffected by the spell, the target takes a –15-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round (this penalty is doubled for [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]], [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]], and [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speeds]]).
