# Hair Trigger  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-509)|initial deed]] that allows you to Interact to draw a weapon

---

No one can react faster than you can pull your trigger. When using your initial deed, instead of [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interacting]] to draw a weapon, you can [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearm]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbow]] you’re already wielding. If this Strike hits, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your first turn of the encounter.

**Special** If your [initial deed](Ways.aspx?ID=) allows you to Interact to draw a weapon more than once, this feat allows you to replace one of these Interact actions and use the other one to draw a weapon. You can't replace both Interact actions with Strikes, but you can draw a loaded firearm or crossbow with one of the actions and immediately shoot it with the other.
