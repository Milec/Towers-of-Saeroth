# Fascinating Performance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 255

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]

---

When you Perform, compare your result to the Will DC of one observer. If you succeed, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] by you for 1 round. If the observer is in a situation that demands immediate attention, such as combat, you must critically succeed to fascinate it and the [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]] action gains the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait. You must choose which creature you're trying to fascinate before you roll your check, and the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.

If you're an expert in Performance, you can fascinate up to four observers; if you're a master, you can fascinate up to 10 observers; and if you're legendary, you can fascinate any number of observers at the same time.
