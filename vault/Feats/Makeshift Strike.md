# Makeshift Strike single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Improvised Weapon Ideas|Weapon Improviser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Weapon Improviser Dedication]]

---

You quickly snatch up whatever's nearby and then attack with it as an improvised weapon. You Interact to pick up a nearby object you could use as an improvised weapon. It must be located at around your chest level, such as a mug on a table, rather than an object on the floor. You then either Strike or make an [[Feats/Improvised Pummel]] with the improvised weapon.
