# Defensive Growth ⤾  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 75

**Trigger**
You’re the target of a physical [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]].

---

You grow a shield of woven flowers and branches, then [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise that Shield]] to defend yourself from the triggering attack. If you would take damage from the attack, you immediately [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]]. This is a [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|wooden shield]]. The shield remains in your possession for a number of rounds equal to your level, or until it’s destroyed.
