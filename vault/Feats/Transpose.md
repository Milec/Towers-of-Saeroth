# Transpose single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Summoner]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 71

---

You switch places with your eidolon. You each teleport to the other's position.

**Special** This feat has the trait matching your eidolon's tradition ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal|primal]]).
