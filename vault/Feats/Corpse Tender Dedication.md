# Corpse Tender Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Corpse Tender]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]; evil alignment

---

You have a connection to [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]] that allows you to communicate your intentions to them on a rudimentary level. You can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on mindless undead and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of them; their mindless trait doesn't grant immunity to these [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. Most mindless undead are likely [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] toward you by default and must be kept at a safe distance to give you time to Make an Impression.

 You become trained in Diplomacy; if already trained, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the corpse tender archetype.
