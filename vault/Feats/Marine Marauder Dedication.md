# Marine Marauder Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 106

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You’re as skilled in combat underwater as on land. You gain the [[Feats/Underwater Marauder (Player Core)|Underwater Marauder]] skill feat, and when you roll a success on an Athletics check to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], you get a critical success instead. In addition, checks to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] or [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] nets you use are attempted against your class DC instead of the listed DC, and you can keep hold of a target you’ve grappled with a net with one hand.
