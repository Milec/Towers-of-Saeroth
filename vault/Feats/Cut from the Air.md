# Cut from the Air ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 127

**Trigger**
You are the target of a physical ranged Strike.

**Requirements**
You're aware of the attack, not [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against it, and either have a hand free or are wielding a melee weapon.

---

You can knock aside ranged attacks. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, you knocked or cut it out of the air.
