# Uncanny Cheeks  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 31

---

You gain the [[Feats/Prescient Consumable (Player Core)|Prescient Consumable]] and [[Feats/Prescient Planner (Player Core)|Prescient Planner]] feats, except that you always withdraw the objects from your cheek pouches. This means the object you retrieve must fit in your cheek pouches; this is generally an item of negligible Bulk unless you also have the [[Feats/Cheek Pouches (Player Core 2)|Cheek Pouches]] feat.
