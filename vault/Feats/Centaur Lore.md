# Centaur Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

---

You've learned how to nurture your environment and to care for your people. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] and [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Centaur Lore]].
