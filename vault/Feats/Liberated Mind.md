# Liberated Mind ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 85

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rose Warden Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail a saving throw against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.

---

Your mind won't be held hostage by those who would seek to control you. You reroll the triggering save, taking the new result. If the effect would cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition, you gain a +2 status bonus to the reroll.
