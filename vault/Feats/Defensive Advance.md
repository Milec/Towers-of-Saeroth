# Defensive Advance two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 93

---

With the protection of your shield, you dive into battle! You [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise your Shield]] and Stride. If you end your movement within melee reach of at least one enemy, you can make a melee Strike against that enemy. You can use Defensive Advance while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
