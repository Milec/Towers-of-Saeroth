# Staff Acrobat Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -151- The Show Must Go On|Pathfinder #151: The Show Must Go On]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Staff Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
Dexterity 16; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; trained with at least one of the following weapons: [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Halfling Sling Staff|halfling sling staff]], or any weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearm]] group (referred to in this archetype as “your staff”)

---

When you [[Actions/High Jump]], [[Actions/Leap]], or [[Actions/Long Jump]] while wielding your staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to any [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check required and add 5 feet to the distance you can Leap vertically and horizontally. You can [[Actions/Shove]] and [[Actions/Trip]] even if you don’t have a free hand, provided you are wielding your staff. Finally, when you roll a success on a check to [[Actions/Balance]] while wielding your staff, you get a critical success instead.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the staff acrobat archetype.
