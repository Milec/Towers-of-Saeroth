# Lingering Flames  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 124

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Fruits of Research|Spell Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spell Trickster Dedication]]; ability to cast [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]]

---

When you cast _fireball_, you can modify its effects, decreasing the base damage to 5d6 and causing it to deal 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] to creatures that fail their save, doubled as normal on a critical failure. If you do, replace its heightened entry with the following.

 **Heightened (+1)** The damage is increased by 1d6 and the persistent fire damage is increased by 2.
