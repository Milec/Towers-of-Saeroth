# Devastating Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Inexorable Iron (Impossible Magic)|inexorable iron hybrid study]]; [[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
Your Spellstrike is charged.

---

Your [[Actions/Spellstrike]] sweeps are so wide and devastating, they smash into nearby foes. Make a Spellstrike with a spell that can deal damage. Enemies in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around the target (not including the target) take 2 damage of the same damage type your spell could deal. This damage increases to 3 at 9th level, 4 at 14th level, and 5 at 19th level.

If you’re in the [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, the emanation’s damage gains a status bonus to damage equal to the extra damage from your Arcane Cascade.
