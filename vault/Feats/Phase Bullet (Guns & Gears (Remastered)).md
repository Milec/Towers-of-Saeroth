# Phase Bullet single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 141

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellshot (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Spellshot]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellshot Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Spellshot Dedication]]

---

You can aim your bullets not just in three normal dimensions, but in additional dimensions as well, removing all obstacles between you and your target. Attempt a crossbow or firearm [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a foe who’s [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to you (but not [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]). The ammunition travels to your target in a straight line, passing through any non‑magical barriers or walls in its way, though magical barriers and force effects stop the bullet. The shot ignores all [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]], the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition, the hidden condition, and circumstance bonuses to AC from shields. It has a +4 status bonus to hit creatures wearing any type of armor. The Strike’s damage can’t be reduced with a [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction using a non‑magical shield.
