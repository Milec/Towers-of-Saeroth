# Disorienting Opening  *Feat 8*

You use your foes' openings to expose even greater weaknesses. When you hit a creature as part of an Attack of Opportunity, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of your next turn.
