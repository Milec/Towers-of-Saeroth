# Venom Purge single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 63

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

You produce a specialized venom that burns out other toxins and impurities in your body. For each [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison|poison]] currently affecting you, attempt a Medicine check to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] that disease or poison. As normal, your counteract level is equal to half your level rounded up.
