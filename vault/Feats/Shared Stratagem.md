# Shared Stratagem  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 61

---

The plans you make include your allies as well as yourself. When you hit a creature with an attack on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem|Devising a Stratagem]], designate one ally. The creature you hit is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to that ally on the next attack the designated ally makes against that creature before the start of your next turn.
