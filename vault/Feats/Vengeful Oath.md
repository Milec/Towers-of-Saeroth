# Vengeful Oath  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Oath]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 111

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Paladin|paladin cause]]

---

You’ve sworn an oath to hunt down wicked evildoers and bring them to judgment. You gain the following edict: “You must hunt down and exterminate creatures that have committed heinous atrocities.”

 You can use [[Spells/Lay on Hands (Player Core 2)|_lay on hands_]] to damage a creature you witness harming an innocent or a  ally as if it were undead; in this case, lay on hands deals spirit damage instead of vitality damage and gains the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait. This doesn’t prevent you from healing such a creature with lay on hands; you choose whether to heal or harm.
