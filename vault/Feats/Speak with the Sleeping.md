# Speak with the Sleeping  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Sprite (Monster Core)|Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 47

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

You can speak to all inanimate objects and the slumbering spirits within them. Once per day, you can speak to an object and ask it a question to gain the effects of [[Spells/Object Reading (Player Core 2)|_object reading_]], though the object gives you its response 1 minute later (as it takes the spirit within the object some time to rouse). An object’s ability to answer is limited by its particular nature; a pair of shoes might know where their owner has walked, while a house knows the history of the denizens within, but not what they do elsewhere.
