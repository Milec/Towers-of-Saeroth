# Merfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 36

---

You paid close attention to your lessons in school. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] and [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Merfolk Lore]].
