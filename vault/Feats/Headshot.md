# Headshot two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Ways/Way of the Sniper|way of the sniper]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a loaded firearm or crossbow.

---

You attempt to finish your target with a final, well-placed shot. Make a crossbow or firearm [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If you’ve damaged the target within the last minute with the same weapon and you get a critical success on your attack roll, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or die; this is a [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] and [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect. A creature that survives is temporarily immune for 1 day. Creatures with no head (such as [[Bestiary/Dullahan (Monster Core)|dullahans]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze (Monster Core)|oozes]]) are immune to this ability, and creatures with redundant heads (such as [[Bestiary/Ettin|ettins]]) might survive the destruction of a head.
