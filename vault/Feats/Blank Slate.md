# Blank Slate  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 188

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]] (Level 18)

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

Your deceptions confound even the most powerful mortal divinations. [[Traits/Detection]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]], and [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] effects pass right over you, your possessions, and your auras, detecting nothing unless the detecting effect has a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] level of 10 or higher. For example, [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] would still detect other magic in the area but not any magic on you, [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]] wouldn't reveal you, [[Spells/Locate|_locate_]] or [[Spells/Scrying|_scrying_]] wouldn't find you, and so on.
