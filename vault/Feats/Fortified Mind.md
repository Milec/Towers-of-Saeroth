# Fortified Mind  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kashrishi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 44

---

Your mind is unusually resilient and resistant to influence. When you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition,  you can attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, you don't gain the fascinated or stupefied condition.
