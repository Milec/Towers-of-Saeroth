# Prescient Consumable  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 208

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Prescient Planner]]

---

You can predict which consumables you might need in advance. When using [[Feats/Prescient Planner]], you can procure a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|consumable]] item from your [[Equipment/Backpack|backpack]], instead of a piece of adventuring gear. The consumable item must still be common with a level no higher than half your level, and its Bulk must be low enough that carrying it wouldn't have made you [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered|encumbered]].
