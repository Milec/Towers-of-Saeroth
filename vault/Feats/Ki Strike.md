# Ki Strike  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 158

---

Your study of the flow of mystical energy allows you to harness it into your physical strikes. You gain the [[Spells/Ki Strike|_ki strike_]] ki spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. The rules for ki spells are summarized in the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|Key Terms section on the monk class page]], and the full rules for focus spells appear [[Rules/Focus Spells|here]].
