# Cannon Corner Shot  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 129

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Leading with a Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Artillerist Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist Dedication]]

---

By loading special ammunition in a particular way, you can make your [siege weapons](SiegeWeapons.aspx) do all manner of tricks that other siege engineers can barely even imagine. If you personally contribute an additional [[Rules/Aiming (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Load]] action to the siege weapon's loading, above the minimum, you can change a burst into a line twice as long as the size of the burst, or vice versa (so a 10-foot burst turns into a 20-foot line, or a 20-foot line turns into a 10-foot burst).
