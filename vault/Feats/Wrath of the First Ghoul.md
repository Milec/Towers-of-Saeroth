# Wrath of the First Ghoul two actions  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -184- The Ghouls Hunger|Pathfinder #184: The Ghouls Hunger]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Ghoul|Ghoul]]

**Frequency**
Once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Secret Eater]]

---

The ravenous maggots inside you become capable of exploding forth from your attacks into the wounds of an opponent, whispering telepathic words of destruction that invoke horrific flashbacks of Kabriri's transformation from [elf](https://2e.aonprd.com/Ancestries.aspx?ID=371) to [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghoul]] or psychic images of hundreds of maggots bursting from the victim's flesh all at once. Make a melee Strike against a target creature. If it hits, in addition to taking damage from the Strike, the target is affected by a 10th-level casting of [[Spells/Power Word Kill|_power word kill_]]. If Wrath of the First Ghoul is used against a target [[Traits/Elf|elf]], [[Bestiary/Leng Ghoul|Leng ghoul]], or worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]] and does not instantly kill the creature, the target takes double damage.
