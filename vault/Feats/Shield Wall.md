# Shield Wall ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 84

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
Your shield is raised and an adjacent ally [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raises a Shield]].

---

You use your shield in tandem with an ally's, forming a barricade. Both you and the triggering creature have [[Rules/Cover|standard cover]] as long as you remain adjacent to each other and you both have your shields raised; when this is no longer the case, the effect ends. You and your ally can't use this cover to [[Actions/Hide]], as it's obvious you're standing there holding your shields, but you and your ally can use it to [[Actions/Take Cover]]. Normally, if you and your ally don't move, Shield Wall lasts until the beginning of your next turn, when your shield is no longer raised.
