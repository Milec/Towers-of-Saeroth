# Scout's Charge two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 188

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scout]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scout Dedication]]

---

You meander around unpredictably, and then ambush your opponents without warning. Choose one enemy. Stride, [[Actions/Feint]] against that opponent, and then make a Strike against that foe. For your Feint, you can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check instead of the [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check that's usually required, because you use the terrain around you to catch your foe off-guard.
