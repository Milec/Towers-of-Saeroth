# Flensing Slice single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 196

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a [[Feats/Double Slice (Player Core)|Double Slice]], and both attacks hit the target.

---

When you hit with both attacks with Double Slice, you flense the target, making it bleed and creating a weak spot. The target takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] per weapon damage die of whichever of the weapons you used that has the most weapon damage dice (maximum 4d8 for a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_major striking_]] weapon). In addition, until the start of your next turn the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], and its resistances to any physical damage types are reduced by 5.
