When you cast _obscuring mist_, you can modify it to add the following heightened entry to its effects.

 **Heightened (3rd)** Your mist is particularly laden with moisture. Non-magical fires within the area are automatically extinguished. Choose one magical fire or [[Traits/Fire|fire]] spell in the area and attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it.
