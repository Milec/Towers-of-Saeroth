# Meditate on This! single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 109

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] or Tian Xia Lore

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

You meditate to alter your qi, coloring it with your resentment and anger. Until the start of your next turn, whenever you hit a creature with an unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], or an adjacent creature deals piercing, slashing, or [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed damage]] to you, the creature that you hit or that damaged you must attempt a Will save against your class DC. The creature is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] until the end of its next turn.
