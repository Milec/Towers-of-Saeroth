# Embrace the Pain ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 82

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach damages you with a melee attack.

---

Ignoring your pain, you reach out and grab the creature or weapon that caused you harm. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the triggering creature or to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the creature of the weapon it damaged you with.
