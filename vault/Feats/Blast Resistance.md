# Blast Resistance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 200

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

You’re used to the clamor of steel, the crack of a firearm, and the thunderous blast of explosions. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 3 to sonic. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] effect that causes the [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] condition, you get a critical success instead.
