# Angel of Vindication  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Assassin Dedication]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Requirements**
Your mark is an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature.

---

To aid with your mission of destroying undead, you've specialized in techniques to destroy them, much as other assassins learn to kill the living. When you Strike a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] undead foe with a weapon that has the [[Traits/Backstabber|backstabber]] weapon trait, you deal 3 extra precision damage instead of 1. If the weapon is a +3 weapon, you deal 6 extra precision damage instead of 2. If you have the [[Feats/Angel of Death]] feat, your attacks also instantly destroy undead at 0 Hit Points, and attempts for the undead to rejuvenate—such as via a [[Bestiary/Families/Lich|lich's]] _soul cage_ or a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost's]] rejuvenation ability—fail unless the effect's [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] level is higher than half your level when you killed the creature (rounded up), or originates from an [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]] or deity. Finally, if you have the [[Feats/Assassinate]] feat and an undead mark critically fails its save against your Assassinate, it is destroyed.
