# Fiendish Magic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Grimspawn (Player Core)|Grimspawn]], [[Feats/Pitborn (Player Core)|Pitborn]], or [[Feats/Hellspawn (Player Core)|Hellspawn]], or another lineage feat associated with fiends

---

You possess fiendish magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[Spells/Disguise Magic|_disguise magic_]], [[Spells/False Vitality|_false vitality_]], [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]], [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]], [[Spells/Shatter (Player Core)|_shatter_]], or [[Spells/Paranoia (Player Core)|_paranoia_]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine [[Rules/Innate Spells|innate spells]]. [[Feats/Grimspawn]] typically take _false vitality_ and _see the unseen_, [[Feats/Pitborn|pitborn]] typically take _paranoia_ and _shatter_, and [[Feats/Hellspawn|hellspawn]] typically take _invisibility_ and _disguise_.
