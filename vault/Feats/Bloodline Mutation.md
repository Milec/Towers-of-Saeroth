# Bloodline Mutation  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 141

**Prerequisites**
a bloodline based on a specific type of creature

---

You permanently mutate to become more like the creatures of your bloodline. You gain the appropriate trait or traits for those types of creatures ([[Traits/Aberration|aberration]] for [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant|aberrant]], [[Traits/Angel|angel]] and [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]] for [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic|angelic]], [[Traits/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] and [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]] for [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Psychopomp|psychopomp]], and so on). You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] or [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], if one is appropriate for creatures with those traits. Choose one of the following.

- If the creatures associated with your bloodline have the ability to fly, you gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed.
- If the creatures associated with your bloodline are [[Traits/Aquatic|aquatic]] or [[Traits/Amphibious|amphibious]], you become amphibious, able to breathe water and air equally well, and you gain a swim Speed equal to your Speed.
- If creatures associated with your bloodline have a resistance or immunity to acid, cold, electricity, fire, negative, or sonic, choose an energy type your bloodline is immune or resistant to and gain resistance 20 against that energy type.
