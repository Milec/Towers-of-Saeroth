# Free and Fair  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Kingdom]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 531

---

Your reputation for transparency and fairness in conducting elections, appointments, and other changes in government inspires tremendous public trust. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Loyalty-based checks attempted as part of the [[Actions/New Leadership]] and [[Actions/Pledge of Fealty]] activities. If you fail or critically fail such a check, you can spend 2 RP to reroll the check (but without the +2 circumstance bonus); attempting this adds the [[Traits/Fortune]] trait. You must take the result of the second roll, even if it is worse than the original roll.
