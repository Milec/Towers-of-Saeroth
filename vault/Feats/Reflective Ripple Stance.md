# Reflective Ripple Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 202

**Requirements**
You're unarmored.

---

You enter a stance of fluid grace as small amounts of water flow with your movements and attacks. You can make flowing wave attacks that deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage. They are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Trip|trip]], [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Water|water]] traits.

 While in Reflective Ripple Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Swim]], or [[Actions/Trip]], and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC to avoid being Disarmed and Tripped.

**Special** This feat gains your choice of either the [[Traits/Divine|divine]] or [[Traits/Occult|occult]] trait, matching your ki spell tradition if possible.
