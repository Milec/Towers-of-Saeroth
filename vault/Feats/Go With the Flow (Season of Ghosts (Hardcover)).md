# Go With the Flow ⤾  *Feat 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Secret]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 287

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Access**
Character follows the ways of Sangpotshi or studies texts and lore containing Sangpotshi secrets

**Trigger**
A creature detects your attempt to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]], and you’re within 30 feet of at least one other creature.

---

You have an occult connection to the world that helps you blend in with the cacophony of life around you. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] check against the creature’s [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] DC. On a success, the creature mistakenly identifies another creature within 30 feet of you as you instead.
