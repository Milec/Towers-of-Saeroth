# Glitter Crystals  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive (Player Core 2)|Additive]], [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 104

---

You can mix a blend of crushed flowers and alchemical salt into an alchemical elixir with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait or [[Rules/Alchemical Foods (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|alchemical food]]. Choose one of the following effects: reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition by 1, reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition by 1, remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] condition, or remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] condition. The creature who consumes the modified healing elixir or alchemical food gains this benefit in addition to the food or elixir’s normal effects. This has no effect against permanent conditions.
