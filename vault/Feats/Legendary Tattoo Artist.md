# Legendary Tattoo Artist  *Feat 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tattoo Artist]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

Your craftsmanship at decorating flesh is unparalleled. You become legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]. Whenever you craft a magical tattoo whose level is at least 3 lower than your level, increase all attack roll and DC values of the tattoo’s activated abilities by 1, and increase all [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistances]] granted by the tattoo by 1.
