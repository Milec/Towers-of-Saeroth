# All This Has Happened Before ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Samsaran (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Samsaran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 56

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You're about to roll initiative.

---

You instantly recall a memory from one of your countless past lives that heightens your awareness of your surroundings and quickens your response. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to the triggering roll. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round and can use your extra action only to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] or [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]].
