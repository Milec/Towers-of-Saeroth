# Haughty Obstinacy  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 57

---

Your powerful ego makes it harder for others to order you around. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect that attempts to directly control your actions, you critically succeed instead. If a creature rolls a failure on a check to [[Actions/Coerce]] you using [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], it gets a critical failure instead (so it can't try to Coerce you again for 1 week).
