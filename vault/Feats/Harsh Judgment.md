# Harsh Judgment single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Vigilante]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 64

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

---

Choose one creature you can see, and loudly declare the creature's life forfeit by your judgment. That creature becomes your condemned foe until they are defeated, you use Harsh Judgment on a different creature, or the encounter ends. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]] your condemned foe and on Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] them. You have a –1 circumstance penalty to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]] creatures other than your condemned foe and on Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] anyone other than your condemned foe. You typically can use Harsh Judgment only during an encounter, and if you try to use it to declare someone's life forfeit during exploration or downtime, you are likely to start an encounter.
