# Resounding Bravery  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 127

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|bravery]]

---

Even your fears serve as fuel for your fighting spirit. When you critically succeed at a Will save against a foe's ability, you gain a +1 status bonus to saves and a number of temporary Hit Points equal to half your level for 1 minute. These benefits are doubled if you critically succeed against a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect.
