# Live Off Borrowed Time free action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Timewracked]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Timewracked Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

You tap into the future to steal a bit of time from yourself a few seconds from now that you can use immediately. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round, and can use the extra action to take any basic or [[Rules/Specialty Basic Actions (Player Core)|specialty basic]] 1-action action. You gain this extra action immediately and can use it this turn. At the end of your turn, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and are immune to the quickened condition until the end of your next turn.
