# Debilitating Dichotomy two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|Cursebound]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 140

---

You reveal a glimpse of the impossible conflicts between the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] anathema behind your [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], forcing you to reckon with another's conflicts as well. You and one creature within 30 feet each take 9d6 mental damage with a basic Will save, and the target is stunned 1 if it critically fails its save. You get a degree of success one better than you rolled for your saving throw. At 10th level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 3d6.
