# Remorseless Lash  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

You're skilled at beating an enemy when their morale is already breaking. When you succeed at a melee weapon Strike against a [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] enemy, that enemy can't reduce their frightened condition below 1 until the beginning of your next turn.
