# Polymorphic Escape ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 85

**Trigger**
You would become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You polymorph into a tiny vermin, a cloud of leaves, or another shape that allows you to escape. Attempt a DC 16 flat check; on a success, you ignore the triggering effect and can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]. You then return to your [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] shape.
