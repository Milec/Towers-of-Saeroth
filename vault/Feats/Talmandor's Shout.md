# Talmandor's Shout two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eagle Knight]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eagle Knight Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Requirements**
You witnessed a creature deal damage to an ally within 30 feet since your last turn.

---

After seeing an enemy harm one of your allies, you deliver a righteous shout in the name of the celestial Talmandor. Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]], comparing the result to the Will DC of each enemy within a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]; this Demoralize attempt doesn’t take any penalty for not sharing a language. It’s possible to get a different degree of success for each target.
