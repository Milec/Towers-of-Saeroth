# Cursed Effigy single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Esoterica (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Esoterica]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Exploit Vulnerability (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Exploit Vulnerability]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a successful Strike against the target of your Exploit Vulnerability, and the Strike dealt physical damage.

---

After your attack, you grab a bit of blood, cut hair, or other piece of the creature's body. You incorporate the material into a premade doll, paper figure, or other effigy to create a sympathetic link that makes it harder to resist your abilities. As long as you are [[Actions/Exploit Vulnerability (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Exploiting Vulnerability]] against that creature, it takes a –2 status penalty to its saving throws against thaumaturge abilities or items that use your thaumaturge class DC.
