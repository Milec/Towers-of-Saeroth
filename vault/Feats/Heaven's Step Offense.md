# Heaven's Step Offense ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 58

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Kitharodian Actor]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kitharodian Actor Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your melee Strike reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points

---

Your lessons from the stage find life in the storied mercy and fearlessness of Grand Prince Gennaris III. With a theatrical flourish, the Strike becomes [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and you can Stride up to half your Speed in a straight line toward another enemy. This attention-arresting movement does not provoke reactions from enemies unless they are immune to mental effects.
