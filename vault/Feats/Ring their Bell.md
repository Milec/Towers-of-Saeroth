# Ring their Bell single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 44

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Add the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]] trait to the feat._

**Requirements**
You are wearing medium or heavy armor, and your taunted enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] because it didn't target you or include you in an area effect.

---

Using your armor, you pummel a foe that isn't focused on you in the head or face to stagger them. Make a fist Strike against your taunted enemy even if you don't have a hand free; if you're wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Gauntlet (Player Core)|gauntlet]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet (Player Core)|spiked gauntlet]], you can make a Strike with one of those weapons instead. If the Strike hits  and deals damage, the creature must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC; this is an incapacitation effect.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  
**Failure** The creature is stunned 2.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned 3
