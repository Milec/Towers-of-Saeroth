# Operatic Adventurer  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 82

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]] (Level 12), [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]] (Level 12), [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 12), [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication (Player Core 2)|Acrobat Dedication]] or [[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]]

---

Your training has focused you for the opera, and it certainly shows. You become master in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]], and at 15th level your skill in Performance increases to Legendary. You also become expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Theater Lore]] or become master in Theater Lore if you were already expert. When you’re on a stage, in an arena, or otherwise at the focus of a crowd there to see you perform, your eagerness to begin grants a +3 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls if you roll [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], or Performance for initiative.
