# Roar Mimicry two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Mimic Dedication]]; you have been targeted by a creature’s [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] ability that effects an area or have identified a creature with an auditory ability that effects an area

---

You unleash a roar so loud that it's felt as deeply as it's heard. All creatures within a 15-foot emanation take 8d6 sonic damage and must attempt a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. This damage increases by 2d6 at 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]]. If the creature is adjacent to you, they're also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is stunned 2. If the creature is adjacent to you, they're also deafened for 1 minute.
