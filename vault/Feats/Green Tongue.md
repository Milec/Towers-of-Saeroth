# Green Tongue  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 138

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Green Empathy]]

---

You share a special kinship with all things green and living. You (and your leshy familiar, if you have one) are constantly under the effects of [[Spells/Speak with Plants|_speak with plants_]]. Most non-creature plants recognize you as a druid of the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]] and are friendly to you.
