# Greater Vital Evolution  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Primal Evolution (Player Core 2)|Primal Evolution]] or [[Feats/Divine Evolution (Player Core 2)|Divine Evolution]]

---

Vital power surges through you like a font of energy. Twice per day, you can [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast a spell]] after you've run out of spell slots of the appropriate spell rank; the two spells you cast with this feat must be of different spell ranks.
