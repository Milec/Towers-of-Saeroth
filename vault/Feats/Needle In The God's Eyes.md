With sinews of bronze and thews of iron, you leap to the heavens, piercing the arrogant eyes of the gods. [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] up to your total Speed, either horizontally or vertically, and [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] twice at any point or points during the Leap. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but don’t increase your penalty until you have made both attacks.
