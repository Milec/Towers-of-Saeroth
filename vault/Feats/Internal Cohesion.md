# Internal Cohesion  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aphorite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 76

---

Axis's order suffuses your body and helps it maintain coherency. You and your allies can [[Actions/Treat Wounds|Treat your Wounds]] without [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]]. Once per day, when someone rolls a failure or a critical failure on a check to Treat your Wounds, you can focus on your internal cohesion to increase the degree of success by one step.
