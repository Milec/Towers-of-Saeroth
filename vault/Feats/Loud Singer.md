# Loud Singer  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Goblin Song]]

---

Staying on pitch, proper breath control, and remembering the words are all less important than the real measure of a good singer: volume! The range of your [[Feats/Goblin Song]] is increased to 60 feet, and you can target one additional enemy when you use it.
