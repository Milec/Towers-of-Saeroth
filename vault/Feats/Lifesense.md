# Lifesense  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

You have a limited ability to sense life force, like your [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] forebears. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 10 feet. This allows you to sense the life force within living creatures and its counterforce that animates the undead, though you can't distinguish between the two.
