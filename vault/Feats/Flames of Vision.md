# Flames of Vision  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Goblin (Player Core)|Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 35

**Access**
 dokkaebi goblin heritage  or Tian Xia origin

---

Dokkaebi were among the first goblins to pioneer gazing through flames, spectral or physical, to cast their senses far and wide. You can cast [[Spells/Clairvoyance (Player Core)|_clairvoyance_]] as a 4th rank innate occult spell twice per day. To see through the spell’s eye, you must be staring into a source of fire.

**Special** If you can cast [[Spells/Ignition|_ignition_]], including from the [Dokkaebi Fire](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=6516) feat, then you can cast that spell as part of casting clairvoyance. Instead of the cantrip’s normal effect, you conjure flames in your hand, which persist for the duration of your clairvoyance spell for you to gaze through.
