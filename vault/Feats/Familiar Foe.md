# Familiar Foe  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] or Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Undead Lore]]

---

You regularly face off against [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and can identify many with a glance. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about undead. Whenever you roll a critical failure on a check to Recall Knowledge about undead, you get a failure instead.
