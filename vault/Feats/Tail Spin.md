# Tail Spin two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tailed Goblin]] heritage; [[Feats/Hard Tail]]

---

You excel at using your tail as a weapon to upend your foes. Attempt a single [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Trip up to two adjacent creatures. If you roll a success against a target, you get a critical success against that target instead.
