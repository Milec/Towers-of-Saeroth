# Master Scroll Cache  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 213

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scroll Trickster (Player Core 2)|Scroll Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Expert Scroll Cache (Player Core 2)|Expert Scroll Cache]]

---

Your scroll collection is incredible, brimming with eldritch power, and you can prepare far more of them than an ordinary scroll trickster. In addition to your daily scrolls from the [[Feats/Basic Scroll Cache|Basic]] and Expert Scroll Caches, add a single scroll with a 6th-rank spell. At 20th level, add a scroll with a 7th-rank spell.
