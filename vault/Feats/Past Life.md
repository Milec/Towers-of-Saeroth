# Past Life  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Skeleton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 56

---

Flashes of your life become clearer and more frequent, allowing you to apply skills you developed while alive. You become trained in a skill of your choice and gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] feat for a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] subcategory tied to your life.
