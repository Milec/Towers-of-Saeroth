# Stand Still ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 121

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach uses a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action or leaves a square during a move action it’s using.

---

You strike out when your foe tries to flee. Make a melee Strike against the triggering creature. If the attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, you disrupt that action.
