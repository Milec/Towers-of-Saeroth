# Enigma's Knowledge  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 106

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Loremaster]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Assured Knowledge (Player Core)|Assured Knowledge]]

---

Your muse whispers knowledge to you at all the right times. You gain the benefits of the [[Feats/Automatic Knowledge (Player Core)|Automatic Knowledge]] skill feat with any skill you can use to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]. As per the special clause in the Automatic Knowledge feat, you can still only use Automatic Knowledge once per round.
