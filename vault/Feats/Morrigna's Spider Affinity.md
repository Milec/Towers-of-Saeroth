# Morrigna's Spider Affinity  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 31

---

Much like [[Bestiary/Morrigna|morrignas]], you feel an affinity for spiders. You can cast [[Spells/Animal Messenger|_animal messenger_]] (spiders only), [[Spells/Speak with Animals|_speak with animals_]] (spiders only), and [[Spells/Spider Climb|_spider climb_]] each once per day as 2nd-level divine innate spells.
