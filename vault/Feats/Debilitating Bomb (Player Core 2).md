# Debilitating Bomb  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive (Player Core 2)|Additive]], [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 66

---

You can add a crystalline additive to an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical bomb]] to have it undermine your enemy. Choose [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], or a –5-foot status penalty to Speeds. If the attack with the bomb hits, in addition to the bomb's normal effects, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or take that effect until the start of your next turn. Use your class DC for this saving throw, even if someone else throws the bomb.
