# Channeled Succor  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 124

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|healing font]]

---

You can remove conditions with divine grace. You can sacrifice one [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spell you’ve prepared in your extra slots from healing font to cast one of the following spells instead: [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]], [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]], [[Spells/Remove Paralysis|_remove paralysis_]], or [[Spells/Restoration|_restoration_]]. The spell is heightened to the same level as the _heal_ spell you sacrificed.
