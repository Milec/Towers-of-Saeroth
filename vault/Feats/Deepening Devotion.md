# Deepening Devotion  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Draconic Acolyte]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Acolyte Dedication]]

---

Your investment in your _draconic gift_ has unlocked greater power. When your spectral dragon shares your space, the status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralysis]] increases to +2.

In addition, you can use Draconic Salvation if you succeeded or failed on your saving throw, not only if you critically failed.
