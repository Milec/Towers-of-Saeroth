# Terrifying Croak single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

You release a haunting croak that reminds those around you of their fears. Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature within 30 feet; you do not take a penalty to this check for not speaking a language. If the check is successful, the target cannot reduce their [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition below 1 for 1 round.
