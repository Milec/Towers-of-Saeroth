# Hammer Quake three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mauler (Player Core 2)|Mauler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Mauler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a non-[[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]  melee weapon in two hands.

---

You smash the ground with your weapon, knocking nearby creatures to the ground. Choose a square within your reach, including your own space. If there’s an enemy in the chosen square, you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] that enemy with your two-handed weapon. Regardless of whether you attempted a Strike, you then attempt to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] every enemy in the chosen square plus each square adjacent to that square, ignoring Trip’s requirement that you have a hand free.

Hammer Quake counts as three attacks toward your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but the penalty doesn’t increase until after you’ve made the Strike, if any, and all the Trip attempts.
