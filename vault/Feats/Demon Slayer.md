# Demon Slayer single action  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elf]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 81

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Demon Hunter]]

---

You can deliver a melee blow to a demon that creates an explosion of holy light to try to finish the demon off. In addition to the normal damage from your melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], you also inflict 10d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage to the demon. The [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]] can resist this additional damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. If the demon is slain by this strike, it explodes in a blast of holy light that fills a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. All creatures in this area that have the demon trait take 10d6 spirit damage and must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The demon takes no damage.  

**Success** The demon takes half damage.  

**Failure** The demon takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The demon takes full damage and is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]].
