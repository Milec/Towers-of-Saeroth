# Powerful Tail  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 110

---

You resemble an [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] or a type of [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] who has a tail. You gain a [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|tail unarmed attack]] that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This tail is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group.

**Enhancement** Your tail is not only powerful but also flexible. You gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 10 feet, and you reduce the number of free hands required to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] by one.
