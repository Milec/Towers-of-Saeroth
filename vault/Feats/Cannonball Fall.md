# Cannonball Fall single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Requirements**
You are above an enemy.

---

You’ve learned how to make a quick descent with a sudden stop—directly on an enemy. You intentionally fall onto an enemy within 10 feet of you horizontally and can accurately target them from up to a height equal to 5 feet times your level. You take damage as [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|normal]]; the enemy must attempt a Reflex save against your class DC with the following results, instead of the usual rules for [[Rules/Falling on a Creature (Player Core)|falling on a creature]]. If an ability reduces the amount of damage you take from falling, the enemy’s damage is based on the damage you would have taken before that reduction. In addition, if you have a means of controlling your descent, such as holding onto a rope or sliding down an adjacent wall, you do not land [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] even if you take damage from the fall.

**Critical Success** The enemy takes no damage.  

**Success** The enemy takes bludgeoning damage equal to one quarter the falling damage you took.  

**Failure** The creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to half the falling damage you took and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes the same amount of bludgeoning damage you took from the fall, is knocked prone, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
