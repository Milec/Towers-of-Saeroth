# Invincible Mutagen  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 79

---

The fortifying additives you brew into your mutagens make your juggernaut form impervious. Whenever you’re affected by a [[Equipment/Juggernaut Mutagen|juggernaut mutagen]], you gain resistance to all physical damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0).
