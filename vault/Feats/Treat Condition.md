# Treat Condition two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Medic Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are holding [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], or you are wearing them and have a hand free.

---

You treat an adjacent creature in an attempt to reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition. If a creature has multiple conditions from this list, choose one. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the condition, using your [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] modifier as your counteract modifier and the condition's source to determine the DC. You can't treat a condition that came from an artifact or effect above 20th level unless you have [[Feats/Legendary Medic]]; even if you do, the counteract DC increases by 10. Treating a Condition that is continually applied under certain circumstances (for instance, the enfeebled condition a good character gains from carrying an [[Equipment/Unholy|_unholy_]] weapon) has no effect as long as the circumstances continue.

**Critical Success** Reduce the condition value by 2.  

**Success** Reduce the condition value by 1.  

**Critical Failure** Increase the condition value by 1.
