# Rose Warden Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 84

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]; you worship [[Setting/Deities/Milani (Divine Mysteries)|Milani]]

---

As a follower of the Everbloom, you stand up against tyranny and oppression, understanding that revolution against an unjust ruler must sometimes be enacted in secret. You become trained in Deception. If you're already trained in Deception, you become an expert in your choice of Deception or Stealth. When you get a critical success to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to a figure of authority, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC and saving throws against being [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by that figure of authority for 24 hours.

 You add the edicts and anathema of Milani to your own (see sidebar). Failing to uphold edicts or committing acts that violate the anathema result in you losing access to any abilities granted by this archetype, as determined by your GM. You can regain these lost abilities through the atone ritual as normal.
