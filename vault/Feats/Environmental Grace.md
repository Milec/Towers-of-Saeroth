# Environmental Grace  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 105

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]; deity who grants the [[Magic/Domains/Cold|cold]], [[Magic/Domains/Fire|fire]], [[Magic/Domains/Nature|nature]], or [[Magic/Domains/Travel|travel]] domain

---

You rapidly adapt to environments through a combination of skill, meditation, and divine grace. After spending an hour in an environment of [[Rules/Temperature|severe cold or colder]], you are protected from the effects of severe cold until you leave that environment. After spending an hour in an environment of severe heat or hotter, you are protected from the effects of severe heat until you leave that environment.
