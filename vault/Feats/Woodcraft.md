# Woodcraft  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 26

---

You have a innate familiarity with forested areas. When in a forest or jungle environment, if you roll a critical failure on a [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill check to Sense Direction, Subsist, or Cover Tracks, you get a failure instead, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
