# Free Heart  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 148

---

You follow all your passions, unlimited in creativity and zeal. Choose a common [background](Backgrounds.aspx) that relates to a passion you’ve pursued; you’re trained in the skills and gain the skill feat associated with that background in addition to those in your normal background. As usual for backgrounds, if you would gain the trained proficiency rank for one of these skill from your class at 1st level, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice.
