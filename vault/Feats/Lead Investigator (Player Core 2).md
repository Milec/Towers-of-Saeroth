# Lead Investigator  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clue Them All In (Player Core 2)|Clue Them All In]]

---

You spend 1 minute briefing up to four allies about one of your open investigations. Those allies gain the same circumstance bonus you do from [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]] to checks to investigate the question at the heart of that investigation. This bonus lasts until you cease Pursing that Lead or for 1 day, whichever comes first. This doesn't confer any other benefits of pursuing a lead, such as adding the circumstance bonus to your saves with [[Feats/Detective's Readiness]].
