# Dominion Aura single action  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 33

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The DC of the Dominion Aura feat is the higher of your class DC or spell DC._

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Faultspawn]]

---

For a short time, you fully manifest the expectations of the god destroyer within you. All creatures within a 10-foot emanation take 8d6 force damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that fails this save is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. For 1 minute, any creature who ends its turn within the 10-foot emanation takes 5d6 force damage with a basic Fortitude save. If you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], you can choose to become drained 2 when you use Dominion Aura to increase the radius of the emanation from 10 feet to 20 feet.
