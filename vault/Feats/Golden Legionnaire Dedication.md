# Golden Legionnaire Dedication  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 32

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in heavy armor

**Access**
You're invited by a current member of the Eagle Knights or the People's Council.

---

You're a wall of metal standing against the tide of Andoran's enemies. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]], you also gain your shield's circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC against attempts to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] you. You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Warfare Lore. If you were already trained in Warfare Lore, you also become trained in a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill of your choice.

**Special** You can take this dedication even if you haven't taken more than two non-dedication feats from the [[Archetypes/Eagle Knight|Eagle Knight archetype]].
