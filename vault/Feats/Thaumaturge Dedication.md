# Thaumaturge Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 49

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thaumaturge]]

**Prerequisites**
Charisma 14

---

You've uncovered basic thaumaturgy. You become trained in thaumaturge class DC. Choose an [implement](Implements.aspx); you can use it to Glimpse Vulnerability, but don't gain its benefits. You also gain a few esoterica, allowing you to use [[Traits/Esoterica|esoterica]] actions. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]; if you were already trained in these, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the Glimpse Vulnerability action.

 **Glimpse Vulnerability** single action ([[Traits/Esoterica|esoterica]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]); **Frequency** once per round; **Requirement** You are holding your implement; **Effect** You glimpse a hidden vulnerability and then strengthen it to aid you. Select a creature you can see. Until you Glimpse Vulnerability again, that target gains weakness 2 against your [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] and weapon Strikes.
