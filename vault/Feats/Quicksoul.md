# Quicksoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Talos]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 52

---

The elemental metal in your bloodline literally courses through your veins in the form of liquid metals like mercury, rubidium, gallium, and [[Equipment/Djezet|djezet]], giving your actions a languid fluidity. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]. If you would automatically become trained in Acrobatics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. As these metals are largely toxic to organic life, you also gain the Toxic Touch action.

 **Toxic Touch** single action ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Tumble Through]], and you successfully moved through an enemy's space; **Effect** Make a melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike against the enemy whose space you moved through. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (or takes 2d6 persistent poison and is sickened 2 on a critical hit).
