# Multifaceted Will  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 62

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

---

Without the firm hand of a patron, your power begins to reflect deeper complexities, sometimes latching onto entirely unexpected sources of magical potency. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast]] or [[Actions/Sustain]] manifest will, you can choose any tradition’s manifestation. While the spell is active, you and allies in its area gain a +1 status bonus to saves against spells with that tradition trait.
