# Blessed Denial ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 162

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Blessed One Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally within 30 feet would become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]].

---

You exude peace and protection, lessening a harmful condition as it falls upon an ally. Reduce the value of the condition your ally would take by 1 (to a minimum of 0). If the ally takes more than one condition at the same time, choose one to reduce.
