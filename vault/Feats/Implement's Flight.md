# Implement's Flight  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 47

---

By weakening your relation to the ground and strengthening your relation to the sky, you've learned to soar through the air, your [implement](Implements.aspx) carrying you as surely as any broomstick or pestle. As long as you're holding a thaumaturge implement, you gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed.
