# Backup Runic Enhancement  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

While you are not a spellcaster, you have a working knowledge of the most fundamental runic magic. You can cast the [[Spells/Sigil (Player Core)|_sigil_]] cantrip as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] at will. In addition, choose either [[Spells/Runic Body|_runic body_]] or [[Spells/Runic Weapon|_runic weapon_]]. You can cast this spell once per day as an innate spell. The rank of these spells is equal to half your level, rounded up, and the tradition can be any tradition for which you are at least trained in the related skill (Arcana for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]], Nature for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]], Occultism for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]], and Religion for [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]).
