# Halyard Strike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Requirements**
A rope is within your reach, or you have a [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]] or [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grappling]] weapon in your hand.

---

You’ve learned to fight confidently while swinging from a line. You take hold of a nearby rope, throw a rope from your hand, or fire a grappling weapon at a solid surface, and then swing up to twice your Speed. You can make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] using a one-handed weapon or unarmed attack at any point during this movement. If you critically fail on this Strike, your movement ends at the point you attempted the Strike.

**Special** If you have [[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]] and you boarded or disembarked from a boat or similar vehicle during this movement, the Strike deals one additional weapon damage die.
