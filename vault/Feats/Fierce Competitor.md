# Fierce Competitor single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 32

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You challenge a single ally to a physical competition. This competition must involve accomplishing a specific goal for a specific use of the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill, such as [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]] to the top of a wall, [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Tripping]] a specific foe, or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jumping]] across a chasm. You and your selected ally gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the associated skill check for 1 minute or until one of you wins the challenge, whichever is first. If you win this competition, you gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks for 1 hour.
