# Attuned Electroreceptors  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 20

---

You grow specialized organs that allow you to detect even the faintest of heartbeats. You can sense living creatures and any creature with the [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] trait as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a range of 20 feet. This range doubles while you are underwater, though only in the body of water you're in.
