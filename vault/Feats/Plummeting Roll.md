# Plummeting Roll  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 164

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

Through nimble movements, a graceful landing, and a bit of luck, you can manage to fall from spectacular heights without a scratch. You gain the [[Feats/Cat Fall (Player Core)|Cat Fall]] skill feat and the following reaction.

 **Plummeting Roll** ⤾ **Trigger** You fall at least 10 feet and take no damage from the fall; **Effect** You tuck and roll with the motion. You land on your feet and Stride up to half your Speed.
