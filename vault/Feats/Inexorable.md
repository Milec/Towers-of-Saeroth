# Inexorable  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You imbue yourself with the resilience of the [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worm]]. When you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] condition, roll a DC 15 flat check. On a success you ignore the condition.

 When using [[Feats/Worm Form]] you are immune to penalties to your Speed and the immobilized condition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip]] you, and you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain.
