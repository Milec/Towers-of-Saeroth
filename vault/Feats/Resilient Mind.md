# Resilient Mind  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 82

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You're firm in your convictions and have girded your mind against outside influence. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. This bonus increases to +2 against mental effects originating from [[Traits/Undead|undead]].
