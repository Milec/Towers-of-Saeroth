# Metal-Veined Strikes  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oread]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 107

---

Metallic veins grow throughout your body, giving your unarmed attacks a metallic sheen. Choose either [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]. Your unarmed attacks are treated as the chosen type of metal.
