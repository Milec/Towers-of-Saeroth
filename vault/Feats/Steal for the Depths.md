# Steal for the Depths two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

**Requirements**
You are swimming in water at least 10 feet deep.

---

You reach out and grab an adjacent creature (on nearby land or in the water) and pull it below the surface. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature. On a success, if the creature is on land, in addition to the normal effects of Grapple, you pull the creature into the water in a space adjacent to you. If the creature is already in the water, on a success, in addition the normal effects of Grapple, you drag the creature 10 feet deeper into the water, moving 10 feet with the creature. Moving a creature into water or deeper into water using Steal for the Depths is [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] for the creature but not for you.
