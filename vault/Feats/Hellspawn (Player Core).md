# Hellspawn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 79

---

Your lineage descends from [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devils]], conniving schemers of [[Setting/Planes/Hell]]. You’re as skilled at noticing lies and twisted wordings as you are at constructing them. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and Legal [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]. If you were already trained in Deception (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Lie to Me]] skill feat.
