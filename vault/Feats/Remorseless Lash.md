# Remorseless Lash  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 50

---

You’re skilled at beating a foe when their morale is already breaking. When you succeed at a melee weapon Strike against a [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] foe, that foe can’t reduce their frightened condition below 1 until the beginning of your next turn.
