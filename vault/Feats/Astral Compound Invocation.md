# Astral Compound Invocation single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

---

Invoking runes from two schools of magic that both manipulate the intangible realm of thought results in waves of mental interference that stagger your foes. You [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke two Runes]]; one must be an [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] rune, and one must be an [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] rune. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, one creature within 30 feet of both invoked runes must attempt a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected by the compound invocation.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2 until the end of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 3 until the end of your next turn.
