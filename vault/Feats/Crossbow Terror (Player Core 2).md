# Crossbow Terror single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archer (Player Core 2)|Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Archer Dedication]]

---

Your skill with a crossbow strikes terror into your opponents when you threaten them with the next bolt. Interact to reload a crossbow and then attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]]. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check if you succeeded at a Strike with a crossbow this turn.
