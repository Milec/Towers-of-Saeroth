# Ensnaring Wrappings ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
Your Strike deals physical damage to a creature that is within reach of your arms and isn't more than one size larger than you.

---

Some of your wrappings come loose to ensnare a creature you recently damaged. You attempt to [[Actions/Grapple]] the creature you damaged.
