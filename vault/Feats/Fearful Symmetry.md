# Fearful Symmetry single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; weretiger

**Requirements**
You’re in hybrid or tiger shape and your last action was a critically successful Strike with your unarmed attack.

---

You attack with a feline grace as captivatingly beautiful as it is deadly. All enemies within 30 feet of the creature you critically hit must attempt at a Will save against your class DC. Enemies that fail their saves become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] and are [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with you for 1 round. Your [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile actions]] don't end this fascination, but those of your allies do. Regardless of the effects of the save, the creature is immune to Fearful Symmetry for 1 hour.
