# Impossible Snares  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 133

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ubiquitous Snares]]

---

You can create endless shorter-lived snares, protecting your lair or using them against foes. Once per minute, you can [[Actions/Craft]] one of the snares you've prepared for quick deployment without expending the prepared snare. Once deployed, such a snare lasts for 10 minutes before losing its effectiveness.
