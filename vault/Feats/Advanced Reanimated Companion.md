# Advanced Reanimated Companion  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clockwork Reanimator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clockwork Reanimator Dedication]]

---

Your corpselike construct companion becomes much more powerful and can act with limited autonomy to follow your greater directives. It becomes an [[Rules/Advanced Construct Companions|advanced construct companion]]. During an encounter, even if you don't use the Command a Minion action, your construct companion can still use 1 action on your turn that round to Stride or Strike.
