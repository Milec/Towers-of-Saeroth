# Fade Presence  *Feat 6*

You weave magic around you to mask your presence from observers. Once per hour each, you can cast [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] and [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] as [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] 2nd-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spells. You can target only yourself with these spells.
