# Vengful Remnant two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]]

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You wreathe yourself in the tatters of all the souls claimed by your blade. Until the start of your next turn, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to all damage except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], doubled if the source of the damage is non-magical. If you slay a significant foe not immune to spirit damage with your _spectral dagger_ while under this effect, its duration increases until the end of your next turn.
