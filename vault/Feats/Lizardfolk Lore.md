# Lizardfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 58

---

You listened carefully to the tales passed down among your community. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in Iruxi [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]].
