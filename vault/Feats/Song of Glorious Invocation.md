# Song of Glorious Invocation single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rune-Singer]]

---

You weave the true names of several runes you’ve drawn into a beautiful song, invoking them all simultaneously. Choose up to three rune-bearers within 30 feet and Invoke one Rune on each of them. The song also inspires the rune-bearers, granting them a +1 status bonus to skill checks and saves against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects for 1 minute.
