# Ancestor's Transformation  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Strix]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 137

---

You can commune with ancestor spirits to assume the form of an enormous primeval strix. You can cast 5th-level [[Spells/Aerial Form|_aerial form_]] as a primal innate spell once per day, but you take the form of a primeval strix, using the statistics for a bird form.
