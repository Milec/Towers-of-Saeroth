# Hag Claws  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

When you came of age, your fingernails grew long and sharp. You gain a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

**Special** claws can develop at any point in a changeling's life, allowing you to select this feat anytime you would gain an ancestry feat, but once you've selected it, you can't retrain out of it.
