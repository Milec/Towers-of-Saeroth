# Arboreal Conversationalist  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Gnome (Player Core)|Gnome]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Kijimuna Gnome]] heritage or at least one [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] innate spell from a gnome heritage or feat

kijimuna gnome heritage or Tian Xia origin

With your close connection to primal energies and the First World, you can communicate with trees. You can cast [[Spells/Speak with Plants (Player Core)|_speak with plants_]] as a 4th-rank innate primal spell twice per day.
