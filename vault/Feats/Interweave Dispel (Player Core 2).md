# Interweave Dispel single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 156

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] in your spell repertoire

---

You weave dispelling energy into a spell. If your next action is to cast a single-target spell against an enemy, and you either hit the enemy with the spell [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] roll or the enemy fails its saving throw, you can cast [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] on the enemy as a free action, expending a spell slot as normal and targeting one spell effect affecting the enemy.
