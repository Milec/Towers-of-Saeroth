# Incredible Scout  *Feat 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 231

Master in Perception

---

When you scout, you are particularly alert for danger, granting your allies precious moments to prepare to fight. When using the [[Actions/Scout (Player Core)|Scout]] exploration activity, you grant your allies a +2 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls instead of a +1 circumstance bonus.
