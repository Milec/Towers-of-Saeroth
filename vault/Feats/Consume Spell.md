# Consume Spell  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 141

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Counterspell]]

---

When you successfully [[Feats/Counterspell]] a spell of the tradition that matches your bloodline, you consume it, replenishing yourself with its energy. When you do, you are nourished as if you had eaten a meal and regain Hit Points equal to twice the level of the counteracted spell.
