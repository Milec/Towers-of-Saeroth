# Numb  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zombie]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Zombie Dedication]]

---

As your body continues to rot and putrefy, your senses deaden and your undead powers grow. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]], as well as a +1 circumstance bonus on saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] and pain effects. While your body is deteriorated, your numbness amplifies in two ways: the circumstance bonus from this feat increases to +2, and if you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion or pain effect, you get a critical success instead.
