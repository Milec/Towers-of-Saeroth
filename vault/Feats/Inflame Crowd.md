# Inflame Crowd  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 53

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Andoran have access to this option._

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Impressive Performance (Player Core)|Impressive Performance]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

---

Your performances are a call to action for the crowds who listen to you. For 1 hour after you successfully [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] using Performance, you can make a Request of a member of the affected audience using Performance instead of Diplomacy.
