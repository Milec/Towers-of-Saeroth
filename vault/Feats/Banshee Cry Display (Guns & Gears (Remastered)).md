# Banshee Cry Display  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 135, [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 134

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Describing Your Fireworks Display (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Firework Technician]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Firework Technician Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Firework Technician Dedication]]

---

You've learned how to unleash a sudden and disruptive noisemaker at just the right moment in your display, a technique that can cause a spellcaster to fumble the phrasing of their magic. You gain the Banshee Cry fireworks display.

**Banshee Cry** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within 30 feet Casts a Spell or Activates an Item using a concentrate Activation; **Effect** You set off a firework that explodes with a loud screech near the creature. The creature must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature must use an additional action on the triggering action or activity, or it’s [[Rules/Disrupting Actions (Player Core)|disrupted]].  

**Critical Failure** The triggering action is disrupted.
