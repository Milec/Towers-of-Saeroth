# Smoldering Explosion  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 124

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Fruits of Research|Spell Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spell Trickster Dedication]]; ability to cast [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]]

---

When you cast _fireball_ heightened to at least 4th level, you can modify the spell to add the following to its standard effects: Your _fireball_ leaves behind a brief cloud of smoke in its area. While it's smoke instead of mist, this cloud otherwise has the effects of [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]], except that it lasts only 1 round.
