# Touch of Lunacy  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]

---

In moments of viciousness, your curse can partially infect your prey. Whenever you critically succeed with an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack from the werecreature archetype, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 2]] until the beginning of your next turn as their bones elongate and form warps. On a critical failure, the target is also clumsy 1 for one minute as the transformation lingers.
