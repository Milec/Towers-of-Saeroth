# Glamour  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 128

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fey Influence]]

---

You unlock a new level of fey power and can manipulate the perceptions of others using a font of First World magic. You gain the [[Spells/Fey Glamour|_fey glamour_]] focus spell as a primal spell that doesn't have the [[Traits/Sorcerer|sorcerer]] trait. If you don't already have a focus pool, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point.

**Special** This feat gains the trait appropriate for your ancestry ([[Traits/Human|human]] for [[Ancestries/Human|human]], [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] for [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]], etc.).
