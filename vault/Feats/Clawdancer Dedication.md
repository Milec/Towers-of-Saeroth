# Clawdancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
permanent unarmed claw or talon attack (such as one granted by your ancestry or a graft)

---

You've practiced the art of fighting with your natural claws, hooking them into prey and thrashing at targets surrounding you. You gain the following two actions, which let you assume specific stances to strike more effectively with your claws.

 **Claw Stance** single action ([[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|stance]]) **Effect** You extend the claws in your hands and focus your attention to take down single targets. The only Strikes you can make are frenzied claw unarmed attacks. These deal 1d6 slashing damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile piercing]] traits.

 **Talon Stance** single action ([[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|stance]]) **Effect** You sway in a loose stance that lets you sweep in wide arcs with the claws on your feet. The only Strikes you can make are spinning talon unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 slashing damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. You don't need a free hand to use spinning talon strikes.
