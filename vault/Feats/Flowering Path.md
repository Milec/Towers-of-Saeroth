# Flowering Path two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 49

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Vitality and elemental wood flows out from your body and into the earth, infusing it with a stream of new life. When you walk, you leave blooming flowers and sprouting vines in your wake. For the next minute, each time you Step or Stride, you create [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] in each square you leave during your movement.
