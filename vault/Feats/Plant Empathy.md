# Plant Empathy  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 127

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The Plant Empathy druid feat can be used to speak to any plant or fungus. However, GMs should remember that this feat does not alter a plant's intelligence, and that everyday plants would therefore know very little about their surroundings._

---

You have a connection to flora that allows you to communicate with them on a rudimentary level. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the Diplomacy skill with plants and fungus.
