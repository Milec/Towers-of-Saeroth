# Familiar's Resolve single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]

---

When you weave magic, your familiar traces symbols that throw off fear and doubt. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]], you or an ally within 30 feet gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against effects that would cause you to be [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] for 1 round. At 14th level, you or an ally gain a +3 status bonus to saving throws against these effects instead.
