# Maze of Runes single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 53

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

You can trap a foe in a twisting, extradimensional labyrinth of ever-shifting runes. You can cast [[Spells/Quandary|_quandary_]] once per day as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]]. The rank of this spell is equal to half your level rounded up, and the tradition can be any tradition for which you are at least legendary in the related skill. Instead of the usual skill checks to solve the puzzle, the target of your _quandary_ can attempt a [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] check, a [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] check to [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], or a check in the skill related to the tradition of the spell. When you [[Actions/Sustain]] this spell, you can also [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] or [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke one Rune]] on the target creature as if it were adjacent to you.
