# Water to Water  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Samsaran (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Samsaran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 57

---

You allow your body to collapse into water, then reconstitute your corporeal form elsewhere. You can cast [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]] as an [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] innate spell twice per day; the spell additionally gains the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait. If you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]] while standing in a body of water of at least ankle depth, you don’t need to perform any incantations or gestures to Cast the Spell (typically preventing reactions, such as [[Actions/Reactive Strike]]), and the spell gains the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait as you simply fall into the water without so much as a splash.
