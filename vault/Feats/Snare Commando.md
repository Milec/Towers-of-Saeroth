# Snare Commando ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snare Crafting]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Trigger**
A creature fails or critically fails a save against your [[Traits/Snare|snare]].

---

Your snares are so befuddling that your victims rarely notice you slipping out of sight. If you have expert proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], you can [[Actions/Create a Diversion]] against the triggering creature. If you have expert proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], you can instead [[Actions/Demoralize]] the triggering creature. If you have both, choose one of the two effects.
