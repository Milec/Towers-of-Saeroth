# The Harder They Fall (Rogue)  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 135

---

You make your foes fall painfully when you trip them. When you successfully [[Actions/Trip]] a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] foe, your target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage. On a critical success, the target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage plus your sneak attack damage.
