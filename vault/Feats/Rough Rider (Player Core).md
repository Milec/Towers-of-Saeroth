# Rough Rider  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 56

---

You are especially good at riding traditional goblin mounts. You gain the [[Feats/Ride]] feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Magic/Domains/Nature]] checks to use [[Actions/Command an Animal]] on a [[Bestiary/Goblin Dog|goblin dog]] or [[Bestiary/Wolf|wolf]] mount.

If you gain a [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|wolf]] as an animal companion, it gains the [[Rules/Riding Animal Companions|mount]] special ability (and if you gain an ability that grants you an animal companion with the mount special ability, you can choose a wolf).
