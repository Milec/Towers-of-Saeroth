# Improved Twin Riposte (Ranger)  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 176

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twin Riposte]]

---

At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to perform a [[Feats/Twin Riposte]] against your hunted prey. You can use this extra reaction even if you are not benefiting from [[Feats/Twin Parry]].
