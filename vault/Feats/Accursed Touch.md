# Accursed Touch  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 52

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

You've learned to harness the supernatural curse that animates you, imbuing your most powerful attacks with a terrible curse. When you critically hit with a fist Strike, negative energy binds to it; this is a [[Traits/Curse|curse]] and a [[Traits/Disease|disease]]. The creature takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent negative damage]]. While the creature has this persistent damage, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]]. The persistent damage increases to 3d6 at 14th level and 4d6 at 20th level. This is a [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
