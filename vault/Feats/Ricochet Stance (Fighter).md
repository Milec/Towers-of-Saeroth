# Ricochet Stance (Fighter) single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 126

---

You adopt a stance designed to rebound your [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapons back toward you. While you are in this stance, any thrown weapons you use as part of a ranged Strike to deal bludgeoning or slashing damage immediately return to your hand, enabling you to use them for additional Strikes. You must be within the weapon's listed [[Traits/Range|range increment]] and have a hand free to catch the weapon. If you make a ranged Strike with a thrown weapon outside of its listed range increment, it instead flies back toward you a number of feet equal to its listed range increment and then falls to the ground.
