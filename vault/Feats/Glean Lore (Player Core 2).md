# Glean Lore single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]], [[Traits/Secret (Player Core)|Secret]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 138

---

You tap into the collected lore of the divine, accessing a variety of potentially useful information. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] check to understand the information you gain. The GM sets the DC (similar to the DC to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]), potentially adjusting the DC of the check for topics far removed from your mystery.

**Critical Success** You comprehend the lore accurately or gain a
useful clue from the divine about your situation.  

**Success** You learn two pieces of information about the topic, one true and one erroneous, but you don’t know which is which.  

**Failure** You recall incorrect information or gain an erroneous or misleading clue.  

**Critical Failure** You recall two pieces of incorrect information or gain two erroneous or misleading clues.
