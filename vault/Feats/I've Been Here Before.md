# I've Been Here Before  *Feat 14*

[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

You adapt quickly to new settings and terrains. When you make your daily preparations in a region you’ve never visited before (such as a city, geographical region, or small demiplane, but not in a dungeon, structure, or other location that focuses on encounter mode play), you spend part of that time introspectively digging into the memories of your past life to get a feel for the adventure that awaits. Choose one of the following Exploration activities: [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]], [[Actions/Defend (Player Core)|Defend]], [[Actions/Investigate (Player Core)|Investigate]], [[Actions/Scout (Player Core)|Scout]], or [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Search]]. You perform that exploration activity at full speed rather than at half speed, and any skill check you attempt as a result of this Exploration activity is made at mythic proficiency. These benefits end as soon as you enter a new region, as determined by the GM.
