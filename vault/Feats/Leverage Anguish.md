# Leverage Anguish ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 294

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Invoker Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail or critically fail a saving throw against a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], or [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effect.

---

You leverage your emotional turmoil to mend your physical form. You regain 2d8 Hit Points. If you’re a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], you regain 4d8 Hit Points instead. If you’re legendary in Athletics, you regain 6d8 Hit Points instead.
