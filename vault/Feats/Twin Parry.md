# Twin Parry single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Ranger]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 90, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 147

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Avenger]] (Level 6)

**Requirements**
You are wielding two melee weapons, one in each hand.

---

You use your two weapons to parry attacks. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn, or a +2 circumstance bonus if either weapon has the [[Traits/Parry|parry]] trait. You lose this circumstance bonus if you no longer meet this feat's requirement.
