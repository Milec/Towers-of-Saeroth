# Feline Dance ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 69

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clawdancer Dedication]]

**Access**
 catfolk  ancestry

**Trigger**
You attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

**Requirements**
You’re in either claw stance or talon stance.

---

You fluidly move between stances in the process of dodging. Roll the save twice and take the higher result. If you were in claw stance, you shift to talon stance, and vice versa.
