# Standby Spell  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 22

---

You’ve honed one spell as your favored combat technique, and you’ve practiced it enough that you no longer need to prepare it to cast it. Choose a spell in your spellbook that you could use with [[Actions/Spellstrike]] and that has a rank no higher than the highest-rank magus spell slot you have. You can cast this spell without having it prepared ahead of time by expending a spell slot of a sufficient rank to cast your chosen spell. You can change this spell whenever you add new spells to your [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank) (Player Core)|spellbook]], or by studying your spellbook for 1 hour.
