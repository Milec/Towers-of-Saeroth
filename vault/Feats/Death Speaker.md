# Death Speaker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 32

---

Your voice and magic speak to the core existence within [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures, circumventing the need for the creature to comprehend. Your spells and abilities ignore the [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|mindless]] trait and any immunity to [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects when targeting or affecting undead creatures. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus when you attempt to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on and make a [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] of an undead creature.
