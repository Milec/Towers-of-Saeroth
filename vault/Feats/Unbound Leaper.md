# Unbound Leaper  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 42

---

Your jumping prowess is unmatched. You can [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] up to 30 feet in any direction without touching the ground and without requiring an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check; when doing so, you must land on a space of solid ground within 30 feet of you, or else you fall after using your next action. You can exceed your normal Speed while Leaping.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Fantastic Leaps]] feat, the total distance you can Leap horizontally is 40 feet, and the total distance you can Leap vertically is 35 feet.
