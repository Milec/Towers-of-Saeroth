# Dual Energy Heart  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Energy Heart (Impossible Magic)|Energy Heart]]

---

A second energy dwells in your eidolon. Choose an additional damage type from the list in [[Feats/Energy Heart]]. Your eidolon’s unarmed attack that deals energy damage gains the [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile]] trait for the second energy damage type, and your eidolon gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to the second type of energy damage equal to half your level.
