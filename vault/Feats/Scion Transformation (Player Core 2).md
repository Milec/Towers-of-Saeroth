# Scion Transformation  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 27

---

You go into hibernation for twenty-four hours and perform a ritual dedicated to your ancestors, undergoing a one-time metamorphosis. You permanently gain the effects of [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]], and your maximum Hit Points increase by your level. The ritual transforms most of your gear to the appropriate size for your new body (though powerful items like [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|artifacts]] or items strongly tied to their original size may not transform, at the GM's discretion).
