# Expert Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Basic Captivator Spellcasting (Impossible Magic)|Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]

---

You learn more powerful spells to enchant and deceive. You learn a 4th-rank [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spell. At 12th level, you learn a 5th-rank spell, and at 14th level, you learn a 6th-rank spell. These spells must each have either the [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait. You cast these spells as [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate occult spells]]. You become an expert in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics.
