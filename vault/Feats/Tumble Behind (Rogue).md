# Tumble Behind (Rogue)  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 134

---

You tumble under and behind your foe to catch them off guard. When you successfully [[Actions/Tumble Through]], the foe whose space you passed through is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.
