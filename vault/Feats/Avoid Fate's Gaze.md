# Avoid Fate's Gaze  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

---

Your past and current enemies may know you by reputation, but spying on you with magic is much more difficult. Whenever you attempt a saving throw against a scrying or effect, you do so at mythic proficiency. Scrying effects that attempt to observe you that don’t allow saving throws to resist (such as [[Spells/Clairaudience (Player Core)|_clairaudience_]] or [[Spells/Clairvoyance (Player Core)|_clairvoyance_]]) treat you as if you were concealed and actively trying to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] unless they are mythic as well.
