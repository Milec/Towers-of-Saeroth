# Ultimate Mercy  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 99

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]] (Level 20)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mercy (Player Core 2)|Mercy]]

---

Your mercy transcends the bounds of life and death. Add ultimate mercy to the types of mercy you can provide. When you use Mercy, you can target a creature that died since your last turn to return it to life. The target returns to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded 1]]. You can't return the target to life if it died from [[Spells/Disintegrate (Player Core)|_disintegrate_]] or a [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effect. The creature gains the other benefits of _lay on hands_ after it returns to life.
