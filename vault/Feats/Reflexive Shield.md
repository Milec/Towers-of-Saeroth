# Reflexive Shield  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 148

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bastion]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Bastion (Player Core 2)|Bastion]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Golden Legionnaire]] (Level 8)

---

You can use your shield to fend off the worst of area effects and other damage. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raise your Shield]], you gain your shield's circumstance bonus to Reflex saves. If you have the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction, damage you take as a result of a Reflex save can trigger that reaction, even if the damage isn't physical damage.
