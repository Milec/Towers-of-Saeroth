# Impressive Mount  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 192

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]

---

You’ve trained your mount to become a powerful force on the battlefield. The mount you gained through the [[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]] feat becomes a mature animal companion, granting it additional capabilities. Your mount has greater independence. During an encounter, even if you don’t use the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] action, your mount can still use 1 action that round on your turn to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. It can do this at any point during your turn, as long as you aren’t currently taking an action. If it does, that’s all the actions it gets that round—you can’t Command it later.
