# Winter's Embrace  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard]]

---

Your eyes are accustomed to the harsh glare of the sun on snow and ice. You gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects that inflict the [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] condition. Snow doesn’t impair your vision; you ignore [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]] from snowfall. Your skin becomes cold to the touch, and sometimes frost forms on you. You are protected from [[Rules/Temperature (Kingmaker Companion Guide)|severe cold and heat]].
