Your proficiency in commander class DC increases to master, and you become a master in Warfare Lore. If you're already a  master in Warfare [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]], you instead become a master in another Lore skill you're an expert in, an expert in another Lore skill you're trained in, or trained in another Lore skill of your choice.
