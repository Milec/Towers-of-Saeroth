# Crystal Healing  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 78

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]

---

You know how to stimulate a body's natural healing abilities through the application of resonant crystals. Doing so requires you to use at least two semiprecious stones with a total value of 5 gp or more in place of [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], which allows you to attempt to [[Actions/Treat Disease]] or [[Actions/Treat Poison]] using Occultism rather than [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]. Examples and prices of semiprecious stones can be found under [[Rules/Gems]].

 If you're an expert in Occultism and you use gems whose total value equals or exceeds 50 gp, you gain a +1 item bonus to your Occultism check. If you're a master and the gems' value equals or exceeds 700 gp, the item bonus increases to +2; if you're legendary in Occultism and the gems' value equals or exceeds 15,000 gp, the item bonus increases to +3.
