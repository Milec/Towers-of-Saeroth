# Observational Analysis  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 31

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Combat Assessment (Player Core)|Combat Assessment]]

---

You are able to rapidly discern relevant details about your opponents in the heat of combat. When you use [[Feats/Combat Assessment (Player Core)|Combat Assessment]] against a target that you or an ally has targeted with a Strike or spell since the start of your last turn, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to the [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check (+4 if the Strike from Combat Assessment is a critical hit.)
