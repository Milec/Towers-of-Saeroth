# Cel Rau  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 26

---

You are a cel rau, child of a strigoi, one of the first vampires to walk Golarion. Your heritage ties you to the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]] and grants you strange resilience to light and darkness alike. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]], [[Traits/Light|light]], or [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]] traits, and whenever you become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for at least 2 rounds, you can reduce the duration by 1 round.
