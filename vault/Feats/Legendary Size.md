# Legendary Size  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 56

---

The vanaras of old were tricksters able to step across wide rivers or slip into impossibly small spaces. You can cast [[Spells/Enlarge|_enlarge_]] and [[Spells/Shrink|_shrink_]] once per day each as 2nd-level divine innate spells. You can target only yourself and other vanaras with these spells.
