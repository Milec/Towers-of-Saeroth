Your dramatic appearances can frighten bystanders. When you make a Startling Appearance, you can also attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy in a 10-foot emanation you were [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by before your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
