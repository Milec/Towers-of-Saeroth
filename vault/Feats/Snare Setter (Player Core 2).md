# Snare Setter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 22

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

You are adept at the time-honored kobold art of making traps. You gain the trained proficiency in Crafting. If you were already trained in Crafting, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|kobold]] [[Traits/Snare|snares]]. You gain the [[Feats/Snare Crafting]] feat, though when choosing your formulas for that feat, you can also choose from uncommon kobold snares, as well as common snares.
