# Cutting Flattery  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 79

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]], [[Archetypes/Pirate]] (Level 5)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication]] or [[Feats/Pirate Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

---

Repeated entries in Firebrand bragging and flyting contests have taught you how to twist pretty words into concealed insults. When you succeed at your check to [[Actions/Coerce]] a creature, the target's attitude toward you improves by one step, to a maximum of [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly|friendly]], and they don't become [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] 1 day later.
