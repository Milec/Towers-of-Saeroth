# Reclaim Power single action  *Feat 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 36

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

You use your thralls to restore yourself. Destroy up to three of your thralls within 60 feet and regain Hit Points equal to your level per thrall destroyed. If you destroy three thralls total, you can also decrease one of your [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition values by 1.
