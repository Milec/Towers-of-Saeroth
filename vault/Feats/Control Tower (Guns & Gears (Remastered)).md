# Control Tower  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Overwatch Training (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Overwatch]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Overwatch Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Overwatch Dedication]]; master in Perception

---

Your keen senses and ability to process battlefield information have dramatically improved, allowing you to drastically reduce the advantage of unseen foes. You and allies in your overwatch field gain a +2 circumstance bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creatures within the overwatch field. You and your allies don't have to succeed at a flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] creature within your overwatch field. When you or an ally targets a hidden creature in your overwatch field, reduce the DC of the flat check to 5.
