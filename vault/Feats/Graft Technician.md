# Graft Technician  *Feat 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

You can create and implant [[Rules/Grafting Rules|grafts]]. When you select this feat, you gain the formulas for four common grafts of 3rd level or lower. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks to implant grafts. If you’re a master in Medicine, this bonus increases to +2.
