# Deceptive Worship  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 229

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

---

Members of your cult frequently pass themselves off as worshippers of other religions. You can use Occultism instead of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] a typical worshipper of another faith or to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] specifically to claim you are a member of the faith you are Impersonating. You still need to use the Deception skill to Impersonate a specific worshipper or to perform other deceptive actions, such as attempting to Lie about any other matter.
