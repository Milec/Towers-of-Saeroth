# Impaling Thrust two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 111

**Requirements**
You are wielding a melee weapon that deals piercing damage.

---

You impale your enemy and hold them in place with your weapon. Make a melee Strike with the required weapon. If the Strike hits and deals damage, your target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] until they successfully [[Actions/Escape]], you attack with the required weapon, or you Release the required weapon, whichever comes first. When the target is no longer grabbed, they take [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to the number of weapon damage dice.
