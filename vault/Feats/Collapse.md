# Collapse ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Skeleton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 56

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
An enemy's Strike against you is a critical hit.

---

You collapse into a pile of bones, mitigating the worst of the damage you would have taken, and the triggering attack deals only the amount it would deal on a hit (typically full damage instead of double damage). Any other effects caused by a critical hit still occur. While collapsed, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and can't act except to Stand, which re-forms you into a skeleton in a standing position.
