# Advanced Weaponry  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 67

---

Your eidolon's attack evolves. Choose one of your eidolon's starting melee unarmed attacks. It gains one of the following traits, chosen when you gain the feat: [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Shove|shove]], [[Traits/Trip|trip]], or [[Traits/Versatile|versatile bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing]].
