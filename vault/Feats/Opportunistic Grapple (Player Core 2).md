# Opportunistic Grapple ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 191

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bounty Hunter (Player Core 2)|Bounty Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bounty Hunter Dedication (Player Core 2)|Bounty Hunter Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your [[Actions/Hunt Prey (Player Core)|prey]] critically fails on an attack roll against you with a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].

**Requirements**
Your prey is within your reach, you have one free hand, and your target is no more than one size larger than you.

---

You use an opening to capture your prey. You attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] your prey.
