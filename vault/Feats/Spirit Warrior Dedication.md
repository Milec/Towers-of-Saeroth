# Spirit Warrior Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

You’re a warrior who trains your spirit and body to work in perfect harmony, enhancing your attacks with your spiritual energy while fighting with a ferocious martial technique that combines blade and fist. The damage die for your fist changes to 1d6 instead of 1d4, and your fist gains the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait. You don’t take the normal –2 circumstance penalty when making a lethal attack with your fist or any other unarmed attacks. You gain the Overwhelming Combination action.

**Activate—Overwhelming Combination** single action ([[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|flourish]])   **Requirements** You’re wielding a one-handed melee weapon or a melee weapon with the agile or finesse trait; **Effect** Make two [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] against a target within your reach, one with the required weapon and one with your fist [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack. If both hit the same target, combine their damage for the purposes of its [[Rules/Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances (GM Core)|resistances]] and weaknesses. Apply your multiple attack penalty to each Strike normally.
