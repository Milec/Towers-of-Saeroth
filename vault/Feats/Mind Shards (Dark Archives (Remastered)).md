# Mind Shards two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mind Smith (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mind Smith Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith Dedication]]

---

With a swing and a thought, you detonate your mind weapon into a burst of psychic shards that shred the mind. You concentrate and unleash a 15-foot cone that deals 3d6 mental damage to all creatures in the area, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. After the attack, your mind weapon automatically re-forms, and you can't use this ability again for 1 minute. Mind Shards' damage increases by 1d6 at level 7 and every two levels thereafter.
