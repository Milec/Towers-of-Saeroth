# Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior]]

---

You're exceptional in your use of two weapons. You gain the [[Feats/Double Slice]] fighter feat. This serves as Double Slice for the purpose of meeting prerequisites.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you gain two other feats from the dual-weapon warrior archetype.
