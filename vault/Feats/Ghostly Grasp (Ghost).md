# Ghostly Grasp (Ghost)  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Incorporeality, Flight, and Rejuvenation|Ghost]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Dedication]]

---

Your control over your ghostly form grows. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]] and can interact with physical objects, with limits. You can attempt Strength-based skill checks against physical creatures and objects. You can Interact with physical objects, but the action has no effect unless you succeed at a DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] check.
