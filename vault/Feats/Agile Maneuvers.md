**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

---

Your easily maneuver against your foes. Your [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip]] actions have a lower multiple attack penalty: –4 instead of –5 if they're the second attack on your turn, or –8 instead of –10 if they're the third or subsequent attack on your turn.
