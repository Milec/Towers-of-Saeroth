# Ghosts in the Storm single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 25

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Add the following text to the Ghosts in the Storm impulse for Pathfinder Society play: "If the Strike is magical and already has the maximum number of property runes, the wielder can choose one to suppress to gain shock."_

---

Clouds, like eager pets, are drawn to you and to those in your good graces. Fast-moving gray clouds surround you, as well as your allies while they're in your kinetic aura. Anyone surrounded by these clouds gains a +2 status bonus to Reflex saves and [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks. When an affected creature uses a [[Traits/Move|move]] action, clouds dance around it. Until the start of its next turn, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and its Strikes gain the [[Equipment/Shock|_shock_]] rune.
