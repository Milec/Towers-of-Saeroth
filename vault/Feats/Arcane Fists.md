# Arcane Fists  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 42

---

Your physical training encompasses unarmed combat forms. The damage die for your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist|fist]] changes to 1d6 instead of 1d4. You don't take the normal –2 circumstance penalty when making a lethal attack with your fist or any other [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks. Your unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]] trait, making them [[Traits/Magical|magical]]. At 5th level, you also gain the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effects]] of unarmed attacks in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and weapons in the brawling group, and the critical specialization effect uses your spell DC instead of your class DC.
