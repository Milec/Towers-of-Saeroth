# Enigma's Knowledge  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Assured Knowledge]]

---

Your muse whispers knowledge to you at all the right times. You gain the benefits of the [[Feats/Automatic Knowledge]] skill feat with any skill you can use to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]]. As per the special clause in the Automatic Knowledge feat, you can still only use Automatic Knowledge once per round.
