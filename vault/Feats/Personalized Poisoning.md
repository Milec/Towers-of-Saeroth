# Personalized Poisoning  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Requires cross-scenario tracking._

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Alchemical Sciences (Player Core 2)|alchemical sciences methodology]]

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

---

You developed techniques for discovering a target’s weaknesses to poisons using your forensic knowledge. When you study a location that a creature spends a lot of time in, such as a bedroom or office, you learn medical conditions, reactions, or sensitivities that make them susceptible to poisons. Subsequently, whenever you craft an [[Rules/Alchemical Poisons (GM Core)|alchemical poison]], you can tailor it to a creature you have studied in this way, giving that creature a –2 circumstance penalty to Fortitude saves against the poison and giving other creatures a +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves against it.
