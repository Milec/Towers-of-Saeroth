# Read The Wind  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 82

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Hunter's Calling]]

---

You can derive meaning from the tiniest details when tracking an enemy. Whenever you succeed at a check to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] at [[Rules/Mythic Rules|mythic proficiency]], you can immediately attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about the creature you’re tracking, also at mythic proficiency and with a suitable skill determined by the GM.
