# Release Spores two actions  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Fungus]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 74

---

You create a miasma of spores. These spores fill a 20-foot burst within 100 feet and remain for 1 round per level you have. All creatures within the spores become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the spores become concealed to creatures within it. You can see through the spores without difficulty. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spores.
