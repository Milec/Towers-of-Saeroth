# Rushing Goat Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 119

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You're [[Equipment/Armor/Unarmored (Player Core)|unarmored]].

---

You enter the stance of an unruly goat, lowering your body and preparing to butt heads with your foes. The only [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] you can make are ramming horn attacks. These strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|shove]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. When you make a ramming horn attack and your previous action was to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], Stride, or [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], you gain a circumstance bonus to damage equal to the number of weapon damage dice. You can make ramming horn Strikes while Climbing even if you don’t have a free hand.
