# Bouncy Goblin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 37

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Unbreakable Goblin]] heritage

---

You have a particular elasticity that makes it easy for you to bounce and squish. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] (or another skill of your choice, if you were already trained in Acrobatics). You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks to Tumble Through a foe’s space.
