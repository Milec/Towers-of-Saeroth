# Rat Form single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Ratfolk]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 23

---

You can transform into an innocuous-looking rat to scout an area or slip through tight spaces. You gain the effects of a 1st-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]] spell, except that you must assume the battle form of a Tiny rat.
