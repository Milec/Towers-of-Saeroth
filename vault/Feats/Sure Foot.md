# Sure Foot  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 177

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Horizon Walker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Horizon Walker Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You know the secrets of moving in your favored terrain. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks to move within your favored terrain, such as [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance]] on ice, [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim]] in rough water, Acrobatics checks to [[Actions/Fly]] in windy conditions, and so forth.
