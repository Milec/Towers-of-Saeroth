# To War! three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 108

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are riding your apocalypse [[Actions/Mount (Player Core)|mount]].

---

You inspire your allies to greater battle prowess while trampling enemies in your way. You Command your apocalypse mount to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice its Speed (or to [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], if it has the corresponding movement type), moving through the space of any foes in your path up to one size smaller than your apocalypse mount. Your mount deals damage equal to one of its unarmed attack [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] to each creature whose space you move through (basic Reflex save against your apocalypse mount’s [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC). On a critical failure, the creature also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn. You can damage a given creature only once during this movement.

In addition, all allies who witnessed your charge gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level and a +2 status bonus to damage against any foe damaged by your apocalypse mount this round. This has the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait. These benefits last until the end of your next turn.
