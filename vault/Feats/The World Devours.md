# The World Devours two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

The ground in your waking world opens to consume a creature standing on it, which must attempt a Reflex save against your world rouser DC. The creature must be Medium or smaller; if your waking world is a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], the creature can be Large or smaller, or Huge or smaller if your waking world is a 30-foot burst or larger. If you use this ability while your waking world already has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or devoured, that creature is automatically freed. If your waking world ends, any creature affected by this ability is automatically freed.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the earth. The creature can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] against your world rouser DC.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is pulled into the earth, which closes above it. It takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage immediately and is devoured. While devoured, it becomes grabbed and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1, and takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage at the end of each of its turns, and cannot be targeted by any effect. A creature can attempt to Escape against your world rouser DC; in addition to the normal checks to Escape, it can attempt a [[Magic/Domains/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check. If it Escapes, it is returned to the area where it was when grabbed. A creature with a [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]] can Escape automatically and move up to its burrow Speed as part of that action.
