# Clashing Compound Invocation single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 51

---

As you invoke runes from disparate traditions of magic, their diametrically opposed effects repel each other in a destructive backlash. You [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke two Runes]], which must be from opposed traditions of magic; either 1 [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] and 1 [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] rune, or 1 [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] and 1 [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] rune. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, one creature within 30 feet of both invoked runes must also attempt a Fortitude saving throw as destructive magical harmonics clash.

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, but if it spends a single action attempting to recover, it automatically succeeds.  

**Failure** The target is sickened 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is sickened 2.
