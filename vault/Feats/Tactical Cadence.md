# Tactical Cadence  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 181

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cadence Call]]

---

Your remarkable breath control and concise instructions allow you to coordinate your allies more effectively. When you grant allies the [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] condition using [[Feats/Cadence Call]], they can use the extra action to either Stride or Strike, and they aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] on their following turn if they use the extra action.
