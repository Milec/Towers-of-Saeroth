# Powder Punch Stance  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Outlaws of Alkenstar

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Martial Artist Dedication]]

**Access**
You're from Alkenstar or the Mana Wastes.

---

You infuse your handwraps with [[Equipment/Black Powder (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|black powder]]. On your first melee Strike each round with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, [[Equipment/Weapons/Knuckle Duster (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|knuckle duster]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Black Powder Knuckle Dusters (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|black powder knuckle duster]], you deal an additional 1 fire damage. If you critically succeed at an attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] while in this stance, the target is pushed back an additional 5 feet.

## Powder Punch Stance leads to...

[[Feats/Black Powder Flash]], [[Feats/Thunder Clap]]

## Black Powder Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 55

If the characters agree to Nixbrix's challenge to defeat Bitey or otherwise impress the gang leader, he offers to teach them a few tricks. The characters gain access to the feats in the Black-Powder Berserkers section on page 79, even if they aren't from Alkenstar or the Mana Wastes, though they still must meet the feats' other prerequisites to take them.

**Related Feats**: [[Feats/Black Powder Flash]], [[Feats/Inured to Alchemy]], [[Feats/Pain Tolerance]], [[Feats/Thunder Clap]]
