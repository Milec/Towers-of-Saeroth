# Scintillating Spell single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 141

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wellspring Mage]] (Level 16)

---

Your spells become a radiant display of light and color. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that doesn't have the [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] trait, has no duration, and requires creatures to attempt a Reflex save, the spell explodes in a spray of scintillating lights, in addition to its other effects. Each creature that failed its Reflex save against the spell is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round, and those who critically failed are instead [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.
