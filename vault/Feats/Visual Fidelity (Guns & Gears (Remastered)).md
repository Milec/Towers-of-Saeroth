# Visual Fidelity  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 27

---

You've found a way to use a hodgepodge combination of devices to enhance your visual abilities in every situation. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] and [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and you can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects as translucent shapes, though these shapes are indistinct enough to be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to you.

 If an effect would give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] condition, the effect must attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against your class DC, with your counteract level equaling half your level, rounded up. On a failed counteract check, you aren't blinded—your various devices are able to compensate.
