# Borrow Time free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 186

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chronoskimmer]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chronoskimmer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

You reach ahead and make use of time that's yet to be. You become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] and can use the extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike. You gain this extra action immediately and can use it this turn. At the end of your turn, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].
