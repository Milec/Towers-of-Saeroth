# Improvise Strategy single action  *Feat 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bravado]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 231

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]]

---

You’re a wild card on the battlefield, inventing unpredictable tactics and surprising foes. You employ one of these strategies, adding either your choice of the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait. Attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] check against the [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] DC of a creature within 30 feet, after which the target becomes temporarily immune to further uses of Improvise Strategy for 1 hour.

**Special** Once per day, if you lead a troop that has a [[Rules/Skirmish Bonds|skirmish bond]], you can Improvise Strategy to attempt a Warfare Lore check against a standard DC for your troop’s level, rather than targeting a foe. If you succeed, your troop replaces its current skirmish bond with another of your choice until the end of your next turn.

**Critical Success** The target gains one of the following conditions of your choice until the end of your next turn: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1, [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1. The target can end the effect by using either a single action with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait to regain their bearings (if your strategy had the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait) or an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to rub their eyes (if your strategy had the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait). In addition, the target isn’t temporarily immune to further uses of Improvise Strategy.  

**Success** As critical success, but the target is temporarily immune to further uses of Improvise Strategy.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.  

**Critical Failure** The target sees through the gambit, leaving you [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the beginning of your next turn.
