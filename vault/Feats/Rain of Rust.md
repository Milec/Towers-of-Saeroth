# Rain of Rust three actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 36

---

You conjure a red raincloud in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet, which pours rust-colored rain directly below it. Any creature in the rain with the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait, that is made of metal, or is wearing metal armor is covered in corrosive build-up. Any such creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] as long as it remains in the area. If such a creature is in the rain at the start of its turn, it takes 3d6 damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC, plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] if it fails. Damage from this impulse ignores Hardness. The cloud lasts 1 minute but ends if you use the impulse again.

---
**Level (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d6.
