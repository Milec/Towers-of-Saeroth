# Knowledge is Power  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 45

---

When you critically succeed at a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] check about a creature, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your next attack roll against the creature, to your AC against its next attack roll, and to your save against its next effect requiring a save. If you share this information with your allies, they gain the benefits as well. If not used, these bonuses end after 1 minute.
