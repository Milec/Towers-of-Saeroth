# Insect Shape  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 136

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

Your understanding of life expands, allowing you to mimic a wider range of creatures. Add the forms in [[Spells/Insect Form|_insect form_]] to your [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] list. Whenever you use _wild shape_ to polymorph into the non-flying insect form listed in [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]], the duration is 24 hours instead of 10 minutes.
