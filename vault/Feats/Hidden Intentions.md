# Hidden Intentions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 37

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Talon Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You layer meanings in your words, and only those you've taken into your confidence can divine your true plans. You gain the [[Feats/Doublespeak (Player Core 2)|Doublespeak]] and [[Feats/Slippery Secrets (Player Core)|Slippery Secrets]] skill feats. On the first round of combat, if you roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] for initiative, creatures that haven't acted are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you.
