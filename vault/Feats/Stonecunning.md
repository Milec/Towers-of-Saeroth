# Stonecunning  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 36

---

You have a knack for noticing even small inconsistencies and craftsmanship techniques in the stonework around you. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to notice unusual stonework. This bonus applies to checks to discover mechanical traps made of stone or hidden within stone.

 If you aren't using the [[Actions/Seek]] action or [[Actions/Search|searching]], the GM automatically rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] check for you to notice unusual stonework anyway. This check doesn't gain the circumstance bonus, and it takes a –2 circumstance penalty.
