# Basic Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Captivator Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Captivator Dedication]]

---

Your innate ability to fascinate others develops into full‑fledged spellcasting. This spellcasting comes naturally and instinctively to you rather than as a product of training. Choose a 1st-rank [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spell that has the [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait. You can cast this spell as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate occult spell]]. At 6th level, you gain a 2nd-rank occult spell, and at 8th level you gain a 3rd-rank occult spell. These spells must also each have either the illusion or mental trait.
