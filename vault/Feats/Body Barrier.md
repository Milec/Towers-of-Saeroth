# Body Barrier ⤾  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 112

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Vigilant]]

**Trigger**
You and at least one ally are in the area of an effect that requires a Reflex save.

---

When your allies are in danger, you don’t hesitate to take the brunt of the blow for them. You grant all allies in the area standard [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] against the triggering effect, and you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] to all damage against the triggering effect equal to twice the number of allies that gain cover in this way.
