# Gossip Lore  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 195

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]], [Venture-Gossip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=311) (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Prophet of Kalistrade]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]

---

Your time gossiping in fashionable salons keeps you informed on every topic. You are trained in Gossip Lore, a special Lore skill that can be used only to [[Rules/Skills/Folklore|Recall Knowledge]], but on any topic. If you fail a check to Recall Knowledge with Gossip Lore, you get the effects of the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge (Player Core)|Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat. If you have legendary proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]], you gain expert proficiency in Gossip Lore, but you can’t increase your proficiency rank in Gossip Lore by any other means.
