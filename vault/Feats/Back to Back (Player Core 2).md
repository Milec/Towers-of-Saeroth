# Back to Back  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

You excel at watching your allies’ backs and helping them watch yours. Any ally adjacent to you can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] due to [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] unless you’re also flanked. Similarly, while you’re adjacent to at least one ally, you can’t be off-guard due to flanking unless at least one ally you’re adjacent to is flanked as well.
