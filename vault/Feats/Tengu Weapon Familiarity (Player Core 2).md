# Tengu Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 34

---

You gain access to all uncommon weapons with the [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|tengu]] trait plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Katana (Tian Xia Character Guide)|katana]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Khakkhara|khakkara]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Temple Sword (Tian Xia Character Guide)|temple sword]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Wakizashi (Tian Xia Character Guide)|wakizashi]]. You have familiarity with these weapons—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 During your daily preparations, you can practice with a weapon from the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]] group that's in your possession. You gain familiarity with that weapon as well. This lasts until you practice with a different sword in the same way.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its critical specialization effect.
