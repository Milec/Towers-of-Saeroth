# Topple Giants  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Overwatch Training (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Overwatch]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Overwatch Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Overwatch Dedication]]

---

When you observe an ally try and fail to execute a strategic maneuver against your enemies, you coordinate with your other allies to finish the job, using the opening from the first attempt to keep your foe off-balance and ensure your eventual success.

 When an ally within your overwatch field attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] an opponent within your overwatch field and fails, you coordinate efforts to provide a +2 circumstance bonus to the next [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check rolled by a different ally to attempt the same action on the same target before that target's next turn.
