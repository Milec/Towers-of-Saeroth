# Instinctive Strike two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 110

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acute Scent]] or precise or imprecise [[Rules/Scent|scent]]

---

You trust your instincts and your sense of smell, using all of your senses to pinpoint your opponent's location. Make a melee Strike against an opponent that isn't hiding its scent with an effect like [[Spells/Negate Aroma|_negate aroma_]]. This attack ignores any flat check required due to the target being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]].
