# Immovable Object single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]; master in Fortitude or Reflex Saves

---

You plant your feet in a wide stance, resisting all movement not of your own volition. While in this stance and on the ground, you are [[Rules/Immunities (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|immune]] to [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] and if an effect would knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], you aren’t knocked prone and can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action.
