# Scout Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 188

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scout]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

---

You are a highly skilled scout, capable of providing your allies a timely warning of any danger. You gain the [[Feats/Scout's Warning]] ranger feat. When you're using the [[Actions/Scout]] exploration activity, you grant your allies a +2 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls instead of a +1 circumstance bonus.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the scout archetype.
