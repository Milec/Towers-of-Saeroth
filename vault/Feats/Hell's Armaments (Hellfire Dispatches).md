# Hell's Armaments  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]]; expert in at least one of your [order's favored weapons](HellknightOrders.aspx)

---

You've trained relentlessly with your order's weapon to maximize their deadliness. When you get a critical success on a Strike with one of your order's favored weapons, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. If you have access to that weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization effect]], you must choose whether to use that effect or this one.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** As success, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] until the end of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As success, and the target is slowed 2 until the end of your next turn.
