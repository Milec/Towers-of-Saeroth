# Lightslayer Oath  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Oath]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Evil|tenets of evil]]

---

You've sworn to rid the world of the self-righteousness of celestial beings. Add the following tenet to your code, after the other tenets. “You must banish or slay [[Traits/Celestial|celestials]] you encounter as long as you have a reasonable chance of success; in the incredibly unlikely event you find an evil celestial, you don't have to banish or kill it.”

 When you use your champion's reaction against a celestial, the extra damage you deal with Strikes increases by 1 (or by 2 at 9th level and by 3 at 16th level).
