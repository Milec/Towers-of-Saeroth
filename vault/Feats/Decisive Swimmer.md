# Decisive Swimmer  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 18

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You are a talented athlete trained to compete in swimming competitions, and would sometimes be asked to help your community settle disputes with others by participating in swimming races. When settling disputes with other [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] or [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] people, you can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]].
