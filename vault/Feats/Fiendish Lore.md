# Fiendish Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 39

---

You were raised by a tiefling or a fiendish relative, or you've devoted yourself to researching the secrets of the fiendish realms. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] and [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill related to the fiendish plane from which you trace your lineage (usually [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon]] Lore, [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]] Lore, or [[Setting/Planes/Hell]] Lore).
