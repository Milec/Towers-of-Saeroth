# Kitsune Spell Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 125

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kitsune Spell Mysteries]]

---

Your tails show your magical mastery. During your daily preparations, choose [[Spells/Confusion|_confusion_]], [[Spells/Death Ward|_death ward_]], or [[Spells/Illusory Scene|_illusory scene_]]. You can Cast this Spell as a 5th-level divine innate spell once that day. You become an expert in divine spell DCs and divine spell attack rolls.
