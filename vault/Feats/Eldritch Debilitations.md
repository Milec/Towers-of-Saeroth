# Eldritch Debilitations  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Rackets/Eldritch Trickster|eldritch trickster racket]]; [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|Debilitating Strike]]

You know how to apply debilitations that are particularly troublesome for spellcasters. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use Debilitating Strike.

- **Debilitation** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]].
- **Debilitation** The target can't Step.
