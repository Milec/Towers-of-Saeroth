# Sniper's Aim two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 114

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Ways/Way of the Sniper|way of the sniper]]

---

You take an extra moment to carefully sync your aim and breathing, then fire a shot with great accuracy. Make a ranged weapon Strike. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this Strike’s attack roll and ignore the target’s [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]]. If you’re using a [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] firearm, you don’t take the normal circumstance penalty on this [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] for not having the required Strength score or firing without using a stabilizer.
