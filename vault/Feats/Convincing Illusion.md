# Convincing Illusion ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 142

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

**Trigger**
A creature succeeded, but didn't critically succeed, at a Perception check or Will save to disbelieve an [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] from a spell that you cast.

**Requirements**
You are within 30 feet of the observer and the illusion.

---

You use your deceptive skills to make your illusions seem even more real. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check against the target's Perception DC. If you succeed, the target fails to disbelieve the illusion.
