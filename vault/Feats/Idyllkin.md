# Idyllkin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 9

---

Your wild, unique features betray your [[Bestiary/Families/Agathion|agathion]] birthright. Serene celestials from the plane of [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana]], agathions seek the peaceful path, ensuring a better world for all to coexist. Your halcyon demeanor leads you to search for peaceful resolution to conflict. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in Nature (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You know the [[Setting/Languages/Sylvan]] language and gain the [[Feats/Natural Medicine]] skill feat.
