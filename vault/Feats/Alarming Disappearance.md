# Alarming Disappearance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

Your ability to move unseen is startling for one your size, causing panic among your enemies. When you successfully [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] when previously [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]], creatures you are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]]. They are then temporarily immune to Alarming Disappearance for 1 hour. Typically, creatures that have traveled with you for a significant time, such as your fellow party members, are immune to your Alarming Disappearance.
