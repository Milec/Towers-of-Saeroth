# Surging Blood Magic  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Prerequisites**
Rising Blood Magic; master in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], depending on your chosen tradition

Your blood courses with magic. You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Player Core)|expert spellcasting benefits]]. In addition, when you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from your repertoire and you are at least [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2, increase the extra damage dealt by that spell from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] by an additional 2 (for a total of 3).
