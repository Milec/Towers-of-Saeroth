# Rallying Cry two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 35

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You call out to your allies and push them to give their all in combat. All allies within 30 feet that can hear you gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level. In addition, these allies become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 minute but can only use the extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike.
