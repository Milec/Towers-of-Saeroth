# Banishing Blow free action  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 72

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fiendsbane Oath]]

**Trigger**
You use your champion's reaction, triggered by a [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] within your reach.

---

The erratic teleportation magic you experienced in the Kortos Mounts has taught you a few tricks. You attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Shove]] the fiend that triggered your champion's reaction; on a success, the target can't teleport or summon creatures for 1 minute. On a critical success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and can't teleport or summon creatures for 1 hour, and if you are on your home plane and the target is a fiend of level 18 or lower from another plane, it is also banished to its home plane. Your multiple attack penalty doesn't apply to this attack.
