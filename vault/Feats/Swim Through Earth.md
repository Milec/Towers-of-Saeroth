# Swim Through Earth two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 27

---

The earth parts before you, letting you swim through it. You gain a burrow Speed equal to your land Speed and can immediately [[Actions/Burrow]] once. You don't gain the ability to breathe while in the earth, so you must [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|hold your breath]]. The impulse ends at the end of your next turn, but you can Sustain it up to 1 minute. If you're inside the earth when the impulse ends, you immediately return to the surface directly above you, fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] when you reach the surface, and are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] until the end of your next turn.

---
**Level (14th)** You can burrow through rock and similar dense earthen matter, leaving no tunnels or signs of your passing.
