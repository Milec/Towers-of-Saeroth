# Arcane Eye  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 40

**Prerequisites**
[[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]]

---

Your eye has been magically enhanced to pierce darkness. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]].

**Enhancement** Your eye can see invisible creatures in brief spurts. You can cast _[[Spells/See the Unseen|_see invisibility_]]_ as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] once per hour.
