# Agonizing Rebuke  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 50

---

When you terrorize your enemies, you also cause them painful mental distress. When you successfully [[Actions/Demoralize]] a foe, that foe takes 1d4 mental damage at the start of each of its turns as long as it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] and continues to engage in combat with you. If you have master proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], the damage increases to 2d4, and if you have legendary proficiency, the damage increases to 3d4.
