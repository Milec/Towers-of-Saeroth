# Draconic Paragon  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 215

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Benefactor's Strike]]

---

Your connection to your draconic benefactor becomes especially pronounced. You gain additional effects for the prerequisite kobold feat. If you have more than one of the prerequisite feats, you gain the appropriate additional effects.

- **Benefactor’s Strike** Your unarmed strikes made with your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|jaws, claws, or tail]] gain the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d6]] trait.
- **Dracomancer** Increase the number of times per day you can cast each of the granted 1st and 2nd-rank [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spells]] by 1.
- **Kobold Breath** Creatures that critically fail their save against your Kobold Breath take 3d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] of the type dealt by your draconic benefactor.
