# Call and Response single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 113

---

Your composition takes the form of a call-and-response chant, which lets your allies continue the effect without you. If your next action is to cast a composition cantrip with a duration of 1 round, it becomes a call. While the spell is active, one ally affected by the spell can respond to your call as a single action that has the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] and [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] traits to extend the spell's duration by 1 round. Only one ally can respond to a given call, and responding to the ally's response has no additional effect.
