# Slither  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 38

---

You are an expert at moving through extremely tight spaces. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Squeeze]] skill feat, even if you're not trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]. When you roll a success on an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Squeeze]], you get a critical success instead.
