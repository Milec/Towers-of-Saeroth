# Spelunker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shisk]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 124

---

Living underground, you have learned to find your way without landmarks or even solid ground. If you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] check to [[Actions/Sense Direction]] or an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] when you attempt to Climb.
