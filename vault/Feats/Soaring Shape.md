# Soaring Shape  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 137

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

Wings free you from the shackles of the ground below. Add the bat and bird forms in [[Spells/Aerial Form|_aerial form_]] to your [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] list. If you have [[Feats/Insect Shape]], you also add the wasp form to your _wild shape_ list. If you have [[Feats/Ferocious Shape]], you also add the pterosaur form to your _wild shape_ list. Whenever you use _wild shape_ to gain a form that grants you a specific [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to Acrobatics checks.
