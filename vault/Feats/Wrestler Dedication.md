# Wrestler Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 126

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; trained in [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks, and unarmored defense

---

Your training in the wrestling arts has made you particularly adept at moving, striking, and grappling while unencumbered. You become an expert in Athletics and gain the [[Feats/Titan Wrestler]] skill feat. You don't take the –2 circumstance penalty for making a lethal attack with your [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] unarmed attacks. In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC when resisting an opponent's attempts to [[Actions/Grapple]] you or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole|Swallow you Whole]].

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the wrestler archetype.
