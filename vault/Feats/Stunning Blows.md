# Stunning Blows  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
flurry of blows

---

The focused power of your flurry threatens to overwhelm your opponent. When you target the same creature with two Strikes from your Flurry of Blows, you can try to stun the creature. If either Strike hits and deals damage, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]] (or stunned 3 on a critical failure). This is an [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect.
