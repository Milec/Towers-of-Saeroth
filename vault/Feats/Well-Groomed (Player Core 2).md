# Well-Groomed  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

---

You are fastidious about keeping clean. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]]. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease, you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|battle hardened fighter class feature]]), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.
