# Spinning Release three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thlipit Contestant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Thlipit Contestant Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] with your lash.

---

You wind your lash around your opponent, then spin them rapidly to discombobulate them. You release your opponent, sending them wobbling 10 feet in a direction of your choice; this is [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]]. Your opponent must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC, with the following effects.

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] until the end of their next turn. They are then temporarily immune to being confused or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] by Spinning Release for 10 minutes, though the ability can still be used to move them.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is also sickened 1 from being spun around.
