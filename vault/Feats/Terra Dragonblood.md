# Terra Dragonblood  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

You gain [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]] as an imprecise sense to a range of 30 feet; if you already have tremorsense, increase its range by 5 feet. If you choose a draconic exemplar, you must choose a dragon with a [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]].
