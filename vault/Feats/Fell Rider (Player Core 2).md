# Fell Rider  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 15

**Prerequisites**
animal companion

---

You have trained with your [[Rules/Animal Companions (Player Core)|animal companion]] to become a terrifying juggernaut on the battlefield. Your animal companion becomes trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]. If your animal companion uses [[Actions/Support (Player Core)|Support]] while serving as your mount, it grants you the effects of the [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] reaction on your first Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] on the same turn, even though it can't take reactions.
