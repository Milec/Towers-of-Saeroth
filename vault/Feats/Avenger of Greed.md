# Avenger of Greed  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

---

You've become a champion of the runelords of greed. Their whispers encourage you to prepare for disasters that might never come to pass. The first time each day you touch an object worth at least 2,000 gp that you've never touched before, you gain 1 Mythic Point. You gain the Convocation of Greed activity.

**Activate—Convocation of Greed** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Spend 1 Mythic Point. The spirits of the eight runelords of greed (Kaladurnae, Fethryr, Gimmel, Ligniya, Mazmiranna, Aethusa, Haphrama, and Karzoug) appear and bolster the armaments of you and your companions. For each of the spirits, one creature of your choice within 60 feet gains a +2 item bonus to AC, attack rolls, or saving throws (chosen by the creature) for 1 minute. These bonuses increase to +4 while the creature is within 100 feet of Xanderghul, the Runelord of Pride. A creature can receive bonuses from multiple runelords at once (gaining bonuses to both AC and attack rolls, for example), but the bonuses aren't cumulative.

## Vengeance Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

During Chapter 2 of this adventure, the PCs can take on the vengeful mantle of a specific runelord's vengeance and embody that need to avenge the violations Runelord Xanderghul has inflicted upon their legacies by creating risen runelords. These rewards manifest in the form of a bonus feat, chosen from the following. A PC can only select one vengeance feat. If you wish to use these feats in the context of another campaign, a mythic PC should be required to use one of their mythic feat slots granted from their mythic destiny.
