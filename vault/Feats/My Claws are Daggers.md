# My Claws are Daggers  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 211

---

Your claws are as sharp as your teeth. You gain a [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|claw]] unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage. Your claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with your claws or jaws, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect.

**Special** If your dragonet heritage is house drake, your claws count as [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]].
