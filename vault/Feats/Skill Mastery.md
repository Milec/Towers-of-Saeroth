# Skill Mastery  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 178

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Investigator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Investigator Dedication]]; trained in at least one skill and expert in at least one skill

---

Increase your proficiency rank in one of your skills from expert to master and in another of your skills from trained to expert. You gain a skill feat associated with one of the skills you chose.

**Special** You can select this feat up to five times.
