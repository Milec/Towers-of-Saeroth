# Ever Dreaming  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sleepwalker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication]]

---

You draw no distinction between the sleeping and waking worlds. Your [[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication|Daydream Trance]] has an unlimited duration, and you no longer need to take an action to enter it. If you're [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] due to sleep, you don't take the –4 penalty to AC, Perception, and Reflex saves, and don't have the [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] condition. You're still [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] while asleep. You can act on your turn while asleep, though you're [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 2]] until you fully awaken.
