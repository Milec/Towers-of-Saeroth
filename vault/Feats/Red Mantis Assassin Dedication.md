# Red Mantis Assassin Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 71

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Red Mantis Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber|sawtooth sabers]]; worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek]]; lawful evil alignment; member of the Red Mantis assassins

**Access**
You are from Mediogalti Island.

---

You have learned how to stalk your foes and slay them with a sawtooth sabre. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] and Assassin [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. Whenever your proficiency in any weapon increases to expert or beyond, you also gain that new proficiency with [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber|sawtooth sabers]].

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Red Mantis assassin archetype.
