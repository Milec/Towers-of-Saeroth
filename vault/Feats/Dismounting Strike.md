# Dismounting Strike single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Nidalese Horselord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nidalese Horselord Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are mounted on your horse, and your last action was to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] to order your horse to Stride at least once.

---

You leap from your horse, using the momentum of its movement to deal a devastating blow with your melee weapon. You use the [[Actions/Mount (Player Core)|Mount]] action to dismount your horse but can move to any space within 10 feet of your mount, as long as your horse moved at least 15 feet. You then make a melee Strike. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If this Strike hits, you deal an extra die of weapon damage. If you're at least 14th level, increase this to two extra dice.
