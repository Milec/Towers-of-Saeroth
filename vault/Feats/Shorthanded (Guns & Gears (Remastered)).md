# Shorthanded  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 129

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Leading with a Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Artillerist Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist Dedication]]

---

Typically, a [siege weapon](SiegeWeapons.aspx) is meant to be run by a crew with a very specific minimum number of members designed to ensure that your crew can account for every possible variable and necessity in loading, aiming, and firing the weapon. In a pinch, however, you can operate it with fewer people, provided you know what you're doing. You can operate a siege weapon with fewer than the minimum number of crew, at an increasing penalty. You and your crew take a –2 penalty to checks to [[Rules/Aiming (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Load]], [[Rules/Loading (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Aim]], [[Rules/Launching (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Launch]], move, or [[Actions/Repair]] the weapon for each person below the minimum crew. The maximum number of missing minimum crew members you can handle with this feat is 5. For example, if a siege weapon had a minimum crew size of 8, you would still need a crew of 3, and you would take a –10 penalty when doing so.
