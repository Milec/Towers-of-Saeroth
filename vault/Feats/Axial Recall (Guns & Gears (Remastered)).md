# Axial Recall  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 43

---

You have reconfigured the magical resonance of your core to attune to the planar energies of [[Setting/Planes/Axis]]. You can cast [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] twice per week as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] that can only target yourself; you can travel only to Axis or the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane|Universe]], and your body serves as the focus component.
