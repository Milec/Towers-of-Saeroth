# Flying Kick two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 121

---

You launch yourself at a foe. Make a [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] or attempt a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. At the end of the jump, if you're adjacent to a foe, you can immediately Strike that foe with an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack, even if the foe is in midair. You fall to the ground after the Strike. If the distance you fall is no more than the height of your jump, you land upright and take no damage.
