# Arcane Tattoos  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 11

**Access**
Varisian ethnicity or New Thassilon nationality

---

You have tattoos on your body corresponding to one of the ancient Thassilonian schools of magic. Choose one of the following schools of magic: abjuration ([[Spells/Shield|_shield_]]), conjuration ([[Spells/Tanglefoot|_tanglefoot_]]), enchantment ([[Spells/Daze|_daze_]]), evocation ([[Spells/Electric Arc|_electric arc_]]), illusion ([[Spells/Ghost Sound|_ghost sound_]]), necromancy ([[Spells/Chill Touch|_chill touch_]]), or transmutation ([[Spells/Sigil|_sigil_]]). You can cast the associated cantrip (listed in parentheses) as an innate arcane spell at will.
