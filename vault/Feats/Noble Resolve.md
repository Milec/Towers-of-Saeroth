# Noble Resolve  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Oread]], [[Traits/Suli]], [[Traits/Sylph]], [[Traits/Talos]], [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 31

---

Once, genies ruled vast kingdoms, and a remnant of that confidence and power is reflected in your strong will. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves against effects with the [[Traits/Mental|mental]] trait. Regardless of success or failure, you become aware of the person who used the effect on you if they were visible to you at the time of use.
