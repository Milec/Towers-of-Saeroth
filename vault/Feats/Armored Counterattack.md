# Armored Counterattack free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 46

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Intercept Attack]]

**Trigger**
You use Intercept Attack against a melee Strike and are adjacent to the creature that made the Strike.

---

With the might of your armor behind you, you hit back at a foe who would dare try to hurt your allies. After [[Actions/Intercept Attack|Intercepting the Attack]], make your own Strike against the triggering enemy. If your Strike hits, you [[Actions/Taunt (Battlecry!)|Taunt]] the target; this Taunt gains the  visual trait.
