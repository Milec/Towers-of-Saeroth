# Well-Groomed  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 11

---

You are fastidious about keeping yourself clean, whether licking your fur or carefully using traditional catfolk hygiene products, to salubrious effect. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|diseases]]. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease, you get a critical success instead.
