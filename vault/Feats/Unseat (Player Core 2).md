# Unseat single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 193

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are riding your mount and wielding a [[Traits/Jousting (Player Core)|Jousting]] weapon.

---

With a mighty blow from atop your companion, you attempt to knock an opponent off their mount. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a mounted creature. If your attack hits, attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the target’s Fortitude DC. If you succeed, the target is knocked off its mount into a space of its choice adjacent to its mount. If you critically succeed, the target also lands prone.
