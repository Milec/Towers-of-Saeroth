# Countercharm ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Basic Captivator Spellcasting (Impossible Magic)|Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]

**Trigger**
A creature [Casts a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) with the [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait.

**Requirements**
You must have a non-cantrip innate captivator spell with the same trait.

---

Your charms and illusions are so dominant that you can use them to counteract similar magic cast by others. When a foe [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|casts a spell]] with the [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait and you can see its manifestations, you can use your own innate magic to disrupt it. You lose an innate, non-cantrip spell with the same trait as the triggering spell as if you had cast the spell. You then attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the triggering spell.
