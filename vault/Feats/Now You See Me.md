# Now You See Me single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Timewracked]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Timewracked Dedication]]

---

You flash through several possible timelines as you move. Stride up to your Speed. This movement doesn’t provoke reactions, and you are [[Rules/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of your turn. All creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you until the end of your turn. You can use Now You See Me while [[Rules/Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Rules/Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
