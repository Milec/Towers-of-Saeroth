**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Parry (Battlecry!)|Aldori Parry]]

**Trigger**
An opponent within your reach critically fails a Strike against you.

**Requirements**
You are using an [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]] to parry.

---

You riposte with your Aldori dueling sword when your enemy leaves an opening. Make a melee Strike against or attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the triggering opponent using your Aldori dueling sword.
