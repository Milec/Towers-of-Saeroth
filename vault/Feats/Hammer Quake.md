# Hammer Quake three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Open]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mauler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a non-[[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] melee weapon in two hands.

---

You smash the ground with your weapon, knocking nearby creatures to the ground. Choose a square within your reach, including your own space. If there's a foe in the chosen square, Strike that foe with the required weapon. Then attempt to [[Actions/Trip]] every foe in the chosen square plus each square adjacent to that square, ignoring Trip's requirement that you have a hand free. This counts as three attacks toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after you've made the Strike, if any, and all the Trip attempts.
