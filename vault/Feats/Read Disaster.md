# Read Disaster  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Exploration]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Prediction]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 80

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Time Mage]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Time Mage (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage]] (Level 10)

---

You spend 10 minutes and open yourself to the divine mysteries of the world, peering into the most sinister portents of the future. You gain the effects of [[Spells/Augury|_augury_]], except you learn only about the dangers ahead; any results of “weal” are instead “nothing,” and results of “weal and woe” are instead “woe.” If you are legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]], you take only 1 minute to Read Disaster.
