# Hallowed Initiate  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 24

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hallowed Necromancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hallowed Necromancer Dedication]]

---

You've expanded your focus spellcasting to techniques used by clerics and wizards. You gain an additional focus spell, either the [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Necromancy|necromancer school]] spell [[Spells/Call of the Grave|_call of the grave_]] or the initial domain spell of the [[Magic/Domains/Death|death domain]], [[Spells/Death's Call|_death's call_]]. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time, choosing the other initial focus spell.
