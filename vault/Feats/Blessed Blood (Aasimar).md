# Blessed Blood (Aasimar)  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 36

---

Your freshly spilled blood is sanctified, and ingesting it causes effects similar to those of [[Equipment/Holy Water|holy water]]. Whenever a [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], [[Traits/Undead|undead]], or creature with a weakness to good damage drinks your blood or deals piercing or slashing damage to you with jaws, fangs, or a similar attack, that creature takes 1d6 good damage. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] checks to [[Actions/Craft]] holy water using your own blood as one of the ingredients.
