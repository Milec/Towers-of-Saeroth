# Intimidating Prowess  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 257

**Prerequisites**
Strength +3; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

In situations where you can physically menace the target when you [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] or [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Intimidation check and you ignore the penalty for not sharing a language. If your Strength modifier is +5 or higher and you are a master in Intimidation, this bonus increases to +2.
