# Dizzying Spell single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 108

---

Your spell leaves a foe’s head spinning and unable to concentrate. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that requires a Will saving throw and targets a single creature, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 1 round if it fails its saving throw (or stupefied 3 on a critical failure).
