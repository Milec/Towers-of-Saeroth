# Inner Fire (SoM)  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 202

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stoked Flame Stance]]

---

While you're in Stoked Flame Stance, you have [[Traits/Cold|cold]] and [[Traits/Fire|fire]] resistance equal to half your level, and any creature that hits you with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, tries to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] or [[Actions/Grapple]] you, or otherwise touches you takes fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). A creature can take this damage no more than once per turn.
