# Warrior Of Legend Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Class]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 66

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Warrior Of Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
Warrior of legend

---

You have been given a powerful blessing of battle that is also your cursed doom. As long as you aren’t wearing heavy armor or wielding a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spears]] and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearms]] you wield gain the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait. If the weapon already has the parry trait, increase the bonus when parrying to +2. In addition, you deal an additional amount of damage equal to your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] value with weapons in the spear and polearm groups. This damage is the same type as the required weapon.
