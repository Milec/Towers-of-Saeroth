You've learned devious ways to make the most of your grippli weapons. Whenever you critically hit using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowgun]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet|hatchet]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe|scythe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]], or [[Traits/Grippli|grippli]] weapon, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
