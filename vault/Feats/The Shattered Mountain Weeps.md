# The Shattered Mountain Weeps three actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 27

---

A massive sphere of rock explodes, unleashing a cataclysm of falling debris and deadly shrapnel. Its destruction falls in a 20-foot burst within 120 feet. Creatures in the area take 9d10 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. Those who fail are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. For the next minute, rocks continue to fall, making the area [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and dealing 3d10 bludgeoning damage to any creature that ends its turn in the area. If you use this impulse again, any previous one ends.

---
**Level (20th)** The initial damage is 10d10, and the area damage is 4d10.
