# Catfolk Dance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 18

---

You have a habit of always being in the way when other creatures attempt to move. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check against an adjacent creature's Reflex DC.

**Critical Success** The target creature gains a –2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves and is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of your next turn.  

**Success** The target creature gains a –2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves until the start of your next turn.
