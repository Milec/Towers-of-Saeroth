# Warren Navigator  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 22

---

You're particularly good at solving mazes and navigating twists and turns. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice. When you [[Actions/Sense Direction]] or attempt a roll against a [[Spells/Maze|_maze_]] spell, you get a result one degree of success better than you rolled. You don't take a penalty to Sense Direction when you lack a [[Equipment/Compass|compass]].
