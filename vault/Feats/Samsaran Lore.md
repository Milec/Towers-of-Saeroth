# Samsaran Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Samsaran (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Samsaran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 56

---

You have untold ages of knowledge beneath your belt, focused on the pursuit of enlightenment. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] and [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Samsaran Lore.
