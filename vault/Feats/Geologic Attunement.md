# Geologic Attunement single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 26

---

You tune to the earth and stone around you, extending your senses through it in rippling waves. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]]) in your kinetic aura. Once per round, you can [[Actions/Point Out]] a creature you can sense with tremorsense as a free action.

---
**Level (13th)** The tremorsense becomes [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]].
