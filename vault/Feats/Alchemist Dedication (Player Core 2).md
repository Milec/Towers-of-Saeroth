# Alchemist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass (Player Core)|Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 175

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Prerequisites**
Intelligence +2

---

You put your alchemical interest into practice. You become trained in alchemical bombs, the alchemist class DC, and Crafting; if you were already trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You gain the [[Rules/Alchemical Archetypes (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]] benefits, creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. Add the formulas for four additional common alchemical items to your formula book, in addition to those you gain from [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting (Player Core)|Alchemical Crafting]].
