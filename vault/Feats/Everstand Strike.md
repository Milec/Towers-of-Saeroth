# Everstand Strike single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 90

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bastion (Player Core 2)|Bastion]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Bastion]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Everstand Stance]]

**Access**
A character who is a member of the Knights of Lastwall has access to this option.

**Requirements**
You are wielding a shield in two hands.

---

Your follow-up blow leaves you an opening to set your shield. Make a Strike with the wielded shield. If the Strike hits and deals damage, you also [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raise your Shield]].

The following class feats are available to Knights of Lastwall. Those with the champion trait are champion class feats. Those with both the champion and fighter traits are both champion class feats and fighter class feats; when you take one of these feats, it loses the trait that doesn’t apply to your class.
