# Ponpoko  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 68

---

No true tanuki would ever be caught without a musical instrument to play under the full moon. You can attempt [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks and cast spells by drumming on your belly; this requires that you have at least one hand free, and like playing an instrument, it grants the Perform action the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] and [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] traits. When [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Casting a Spell]], your gestures can include drumming your belly. If you do, any attempt to [[Feats/Recognize Spell (Player Core)|recognize a spell]] cast this way gains a -2 circumstance penalty.
