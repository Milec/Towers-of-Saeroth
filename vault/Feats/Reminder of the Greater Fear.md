# Reminder of the Greater Fear ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Vigilante]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Vigilante]]

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]; [[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Another creature within 30 feet of you that can see you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition.

---

You give a target a stern glower to remind them that it is you, and no one else, they should truly fear. Reduce the value of the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition the creature would take by 1.
