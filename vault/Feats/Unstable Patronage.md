# Unstable Patronage  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 63

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seneschal Witch Dedication]]

---

You can act as a magical patron for your charges. When you finish your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], choose a spell your [[Feats/Familiar (Player Core)|familiar]] knows and one of your charges. Once before your next daily preparations, that creature can [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]] as an innate spell; this spell can be of no higher a rank than the highest rank of spells you can cast –2.
