# Legend of Combat single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

---

You can deliver a lightning-fast series of blows and strikes, bringing all the strength of your legend to bear against those who have yet to experience your prowess. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. For the next minute, you gain an extra reaction at the start of each of your turns. You also gain the Speed of Arms reaction.

**Speed of Arms** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mythic|mythic]]) **Trigger** Your turn begins; **Effect** You make a Strike against one opponent within your reach, or Stride directly towards an opponent. If this is the first time you've struck that opponent during this encounter, the Strike is made at mythic proficiency.
