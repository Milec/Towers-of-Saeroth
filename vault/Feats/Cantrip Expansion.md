# Cantrip Expansion  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Psychic]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 24, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 100, [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 101, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 42

A greater understanding of your magic broadens your range of simple spells.

**Prepared Caster (Cleric, Wizard, etc.)**: You can prepare two additional cantrips each day.  
**Spontaneous Caster (Bard, Sorcerer, etc.)**: Add two additional cantrips from your spell list to your repertoire.
