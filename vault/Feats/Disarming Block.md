# Disarming Block free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 159

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bastion Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
You [[Feats/Shield Block]] a melee Strike made with a held weapon.

---

You attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] the creature whose attack you blocked of the weapon they attacked you with. You can do so even if you don't have a hand free.
