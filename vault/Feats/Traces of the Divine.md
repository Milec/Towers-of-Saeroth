# Traces of the Divine  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pure Legion Enforcer Dedication]]

---

You can detect those who use divine magic like an additional sense. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] and Survival checks to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] creatures that can cast divine spells. In addition, you automatically succeed at the flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creature that can cast divine spells, and the flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] creature that can cast divine spells is reduced to DC 5.
