# Pinpoint Poisoner  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 67

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]] (Level 10)

Unsuspecting targets are especially vulnerable to your poisons. When you successfully Strike an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature with a poisoned weapon or expose an off-guard creature to an [[Traits/Inhaled (GM Core)|inhaled]] poison, that creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its initial save against that poison.
