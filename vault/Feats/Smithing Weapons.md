# Smithing Weapons  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

---

Though you are an artisan, you are well versed in using the tools of your trade to fend off enemies. While wielding weapons in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|hammer]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]], and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick|pick]] weapon groups, you can trace runes using the weapon without holding or wearing an [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|artisan’s toolkit]]. Additionally, a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a hammer, knife, or pick deals 1 additional fire damage to an enemy bearing one of your runes as sparks fly on impact like a hammer to an anvil.
