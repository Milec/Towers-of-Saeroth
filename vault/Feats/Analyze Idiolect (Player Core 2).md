# Analyze Idiolect  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 203

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Linguist (Player Core 2)|Linguist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Linguist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Linguist Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]

---

You break down a specific individual’s idiolect, memorizing their speech mannerisms and habits. If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, when you later attempt to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] that individual, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks and DCs. Due to the intense character study required, if you Analyze Idiolect again, it replaces any previous idiolect you memorized.
