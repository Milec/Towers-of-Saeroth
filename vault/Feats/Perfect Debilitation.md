# Perfect Debilitation  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 81

---

You have perfected the formulas for bombs that impede your enemies. When you use [[Feats/Debilitating Bomb]], your target avoids the condition the bomb imposes only if it critically succeeds at its saving throw.
