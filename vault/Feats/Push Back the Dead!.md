# Push Back the Dead! single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You call to your ally and strengthen their resolve and abilities to defend against the horrific tricks [[Traits/Undead|undead]] bring to bear. The ally gains a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against effects originating from undead. It also gains resistance to damage from Strikes made by undead creatures equal to half your Charisma modifier.
