# Captain Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Table Dynamics|Captain]]

**Prerequisites**
Charisma +2; you don't have an [[Rules/Animal Companions (Player Core)|animal companion]], [[Rules/Construct Companions (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|construct companion]], or other companion that functions similarly

---

You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]; if you are already trained in both of these skills, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain your choice of the [[Feats/Group Impression (Player Core)|Group Impression]] or the [[Feats/Group Coercion (Player Core)|Group Coercion]] skill feat. If you already have both of these skill feats, you gain another 1st level skill feat for which you qualify.  

You also gain the assistance of a dedicated follower, who has agreed to accompany you on your journeys. They have the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait and begin as a novice follower. [See followers](Followers.aspx) for more information.

**Special** Once you have a follower, you can never take a feat or class feature that grants an animal companion or another companion that prevents you from having an animal companion.
