# Labyrinthine Echoes  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

---

You can make your voice reverberate off any surface. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Ventriloquism (Player Core)|_ventriloquism_]] as an occult innate spell. At 7th level, this spell is heightened to 2nd rank.
