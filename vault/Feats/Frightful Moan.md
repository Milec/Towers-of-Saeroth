# Frightful Moan two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Incorporeality, Flight, and Rejuvenation|Ghost]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Dedication]]

---

You lament your fate, forcing each living creature in a 30-foot emanation to attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune to Frightful Moans for 1 minute.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2.
