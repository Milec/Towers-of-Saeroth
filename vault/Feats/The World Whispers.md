# The World Whispers single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 106

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

Your waking world tells you about the unseen creatures it holds. You [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] in your waking world. If you succeed or critically succeed against a creature that was [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by you, you [[Actions/Point Out (Player Core)|Point Out]] the creature to any allies in your waking world. When you Point Out the creatures in this way, the action does not have the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] or [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] traits, but still requires your allies to be able to hear and understand you.
