# Inexhaustible Countermoves  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 93

---

You can react whenever a foe leaves a gap in their defenses. At the start of each enemy's turn, you gain an extra reaction you can use only during that turn to perform an [[Actions/Opportune Riposte]], or an [[Feats/Attack of Opportunity]] if you have that reaction.
