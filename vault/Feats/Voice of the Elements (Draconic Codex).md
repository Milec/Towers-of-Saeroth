# Voice of the Elements  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 216

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Primal Dragonblood]]

---

You inherit a primal dragon’s bond to base elements. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on creatures with the [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]] trait. You can also ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the Diplomacy skill with elementals that don’t speak any language.
