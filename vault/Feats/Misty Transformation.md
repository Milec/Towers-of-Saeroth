# Misty Transformation ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 56

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Trigger**
You transform due to a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect.

---

Wild mists cover your form. You create a hazy cloud in a 5-foot burst centered on one corner of your space. If your new form is Large or larger, the cloud covers your entire space instead. All creatures within the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all others are concealed to them. The cloud lasts until the beginning of your next turn but is immediately dispersed by a strong wind.
