# Tracing Trance free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 50

**Frequency**
Your turn begins.

---

Your hands flow unbidden, tracing runes as if by purest instinct. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] until the end of your turn and can use the extra action only to [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] (including to supply 1 action if using the 2-action version of Trace Rune). Focused on the act of creation, you can’t use invocation actions this turn.
