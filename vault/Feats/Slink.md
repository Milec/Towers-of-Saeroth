# Slink  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fetchling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 86

---

You can move through gloom with the speed of darkness. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the [[Actions/Sneak]] action, up to your Speed. In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your Stealth check, you don't become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] if you end a Sneak action in dim light or darkness, as long as you have cover or greater cover or are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] at the end of your turn.
