# Wind Seeker  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Walk the Wilds]]

---

Wings free you from the shackles of the ground below. You add [[Spells/Aerial Form (Player Core)|_aerial form_]] to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting. Whenever you use _aerial form_ to gain a form that grants you a specific [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to Acrobatics checks.

**Special** If you are attuned to an apparition of darkened boughs, add the bat and bird forms in aerial form to your [[Spells/Darkened Forest Form|_darkened forest form_]] lists.
