# Log Roll single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Turpin Rowe Lumberjack]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Turpin Rowe Lumberjack Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You and an adjacent creature are both on a narrow surface or uneven ground and the other creature is flat-footed.

---

You shuffle your feet to dislodge the surface beneath you or otherwise cause your opponent to become unsteady. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check against the adjacent creature’s Reflex saving throw DC. On a success, the creature falls. On a critical failure, you fall.
