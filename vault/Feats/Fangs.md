# Fangs  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

Your incisors have grown into true fangs: long, sharp, and well-suited to drawing blood. You gain a fangs unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your fangs are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
