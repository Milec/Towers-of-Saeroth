# Fey Tracker  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Investigator]], [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 129

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Greenwatch Initiate]]

---

You are exceptionally skilled at noticing the subtle techniques fey use to avoid notice. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Track]] [[Traits/Fey|fey]] creatures, to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]] for hidden fey, and to your Perception DC to resist a fey creature's attempt to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]].
