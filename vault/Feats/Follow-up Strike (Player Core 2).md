# Follow-up Strike single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Martial Artist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a missed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a melee unarmed attack.

---

You have trained rigorously to use all parts of your body as a weapon, and when you miss with an attack, you can usually continue the attack with a different body part and still deal damage. Make another [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a melee unarmed attack, using the same [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]] as the missed Strike, if any.
