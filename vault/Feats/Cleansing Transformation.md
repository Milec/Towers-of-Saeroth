# Cleansing Transformation  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 57

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Healing Transformation (Player Core)|Healing Transformation]]

---

You learn to cleanse bodily toxins alongside the transformations of your shape-changing magic. When you use Healing Transformation, your [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] spell can also attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] afflicting the target. If you don't successfully counteract it, you can't use Cleansing Transformation against that specific affliction for 24 hours.
