# Battle Medicine single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 258

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

**Requirements**
You are holding [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], or you are wearing them and have a hand free

---

You can patch up wounds, even in combat. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check with the same DC as for [[Actions/Treat Wounds]] and restore the corresponding amount of HP; this doesn't remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition. As with Treat Wounds, you can attempt checks against higher DCs if you have the minimum proficiency rank. The target is then temporarily immune to your Battle Medicine for 1 day.
