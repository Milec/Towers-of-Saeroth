# Keep Pace ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 191

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bounty Hunter (Player Core 2)|Bounty Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bounty Hunter Dedication (Player Core 2)|Bounty Hunter Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your prey is within reach and attempts to [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] away from you.

---

Once you are upon your prey, they can’t escape. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your Speed, following the foe and keeping it in reach throughout its movement until it stops moving or you’ve moved your full Speed. You can use Keep Pace to [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type.
