# Redirect Teleportation ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Teleportation]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 109

**Trigger**
A creature teleports into a space within 60 feet of you.

---

You’re able to intercept a teleportation effect and change where a creature appears. You expend a prepared spell that has the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait. You lose your spell slot as if you had cast the triggering spell. You then attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the triggering spell. The counteract rank for this effect is equal to the rank of the expended spell. If you succeed on your counteract check, you don’t [[Rules/Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupt]] the spell. Instead, you choose an unoccupied space within 60 feet of you for the creature to teleport into. You can’t choose a space that is obviously harmful (such as into a [[Spells/Wall of Fire (Player Core)|_wall of fire_]] or [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (GM Core)|hazardous terrain]]) or would cause the target to fall.
