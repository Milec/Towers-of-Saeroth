# Harrow Ritualist  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Stolen Fate Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Harrower]]

**Access**
This feat is available to all players in a Stolen Fate campaign

---

You immediately learn two of the following rituals: [[Rituals/Astral Projection|_astral projection_]], [[Rituals/Call Spirit|_call spirit_]], [[Rituals/Commune|_commune_]], [[Rituals/Commune with Nature|_commune with nature_]], [[Rituals/Legend Lore|_legend lore_]], or [[Rituals/Planar Binding|_planar binding_]]. When you perform any ritual, you can incorporate harrow cards into the ritual to gain a +2 status bonus to all skill checks made to resolve the effects of the ritual. You can take this feat more than once; each time you do, you learn two new rituals.
