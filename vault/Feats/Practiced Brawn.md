# Practiced Brawn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

---

You're accustomed to long days filled with hard physical labor. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] and [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and to Fortitude saving throws to resist becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]]. When you roll a success on an Athletics check to Shove, you get a critical success instead.
