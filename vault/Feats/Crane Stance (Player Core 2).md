# Crane Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 118

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

Your arms flutter like a crane's wings. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC, but the only Strikes you can make are crane wing attacks. These deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

 While in Crane Stance, reduce the DC for [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] and [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] by 5, and when you [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], you can move an additional 5 feet horizontally or 2 feet vertically.
