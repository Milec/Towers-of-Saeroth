# Eternal Blessing  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 126

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]] (Level 18)

**Prerequisites**
good alignment

---

Your good deeds have brought your deity’s grace to you for all of eternity. You’re continuously surrounded by a [[Spells/Bless|_bless_]] spell with a spell level equal to half your level (rounded up). The radius is 15 feet, and you can’t increase it. You can Dismiss the spell; if you do, it returns automatically after 1 minute.
