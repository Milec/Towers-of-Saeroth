# Reincarnated Ridiculer  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 80

---

You've practiced the art of taunting an enemy, ensuring they know death will only make you stronger. When you [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] an opponent, the target doesn't become temporarily immune to your attempts to Demoralize it unless you critically fail the check. If the target critically hit you with a Strike on its previous turn, the DC for this check is reduced by 4.
