# Horselord's Bond  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Nidalese Horselord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nidalese Horselord Dedication]]

---

Your bond with your horse becomes supernaturally strong as the two of you continue your crusade against Zon-Kuthon's dominion. You and your horse gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|greater darkvision]]. In addition, when your horse succeeds at a save against effects that would cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] condition, it gets a critical success instead.
