# Gift of the Moon  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beastkin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 81

---

You can share your power with others, but they can't escape the werecreature's curse as easily as you can. You can cast a 5th-level [[Spells/Moon Frenzy|_moon frenzy_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. At 15th level, you cast a 6th-level _moon frenzy_ instead. At 17th level, your 6th-level _moon frenzy_ grants its standard effects, except the temporary Hit Points increase to 15 and the silver weakness increases to 15. At 20th level, you cast a 10th-level _moon frenzy_ instead. The spell uses your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.
