# Targeted Redirection single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 138

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sniping Duo]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sniping Duo Dedication]]

---

You aim a shot at your spotter, intending for your spotter to redirect that shot off their own weapon or shield to hit your foe from a better angle, and avoid your foe’s [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]]. If your next action is to make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], you treat your spotter’s position as your own for the purposes of determining cover and range. Your spotter must be within your weapon’s first range increment and they must use a reaction to redirect your shot.
