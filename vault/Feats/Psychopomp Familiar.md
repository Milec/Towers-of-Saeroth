# Psychopomp Familiar  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Soul Cycle|Soul Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Soul Warden Dedication]]; familiar

---

Your [familiar](Familiars.aspx) becomes a masked [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]]—an extraplanar guardian of the cycle of souls. This familiar continues to use all the same rules as other familiars, but one of its abilities must always be [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech|speech]] and it has the [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]] and [[Traits/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] traits. As normal when a familiar must always have a certain ability, the speech ability counts against your familiar's abilities each day.

 You can select three familiar or master abilities each day, instead of two, but one must be one of the following psychopomp familiar abilities.

- **Soul Sight** Your familiar gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]] with a range of 30 feet.
- **Spirit Touch** Your familiar can touch [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures. If you have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Spell Delivery|spell delivery]] master ability from your familiar, any spell the familiar delivers with it gains the benefits of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.
