# Steal Time two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**

---

You reach into another creature's timeline and attempt to alter their flow of time. Select a creature within 30 feet. That creature attempts a Fortitude saving throw against your chronoskimmer DC. The creature takes the effects of [[Spells/Slow|_slow_]] based on the result of its saving throw.
