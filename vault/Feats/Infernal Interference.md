# Infernal Interference two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding your order's favored weapon.

---

Your attacks force a foe to glimpse the iron, soul-wrenching landscapes of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]]. Strike an enemy with the required weapon twice. If either Strike hits, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] and can't use reactions until the beginning of your next turn. If both Strikes hit, the target is stupefied 3 instead.
