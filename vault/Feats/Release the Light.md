# Release the Light two actions  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 81

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Many people report seeing a bright light before their death. You're one of these people, and at rare intervals when hope seems dim, the light exudes outward from your soul. You shed bright light in a 30-foot radius (and dim light for another 30 feet beyond that) until the end of your next turn. Enemy creatures in the area who rely on sight must attempt a Fortitude save equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 2 rounds and dazzled for 1 minute.
