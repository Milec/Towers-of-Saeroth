# Greater Despair  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy's Despair]]

---

You increase the frequency of Mummy's Despair to once per 10 minutes instead of once per hour. A creature that critically fails its Will saving throw is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]]. Mummy's Despair gains the [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait.
