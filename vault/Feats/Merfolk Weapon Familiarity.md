# Merfolk Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 36

---

You were taught the most effective weapons of the sea. You gain access to all uncommon weapons with the [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|merfolk]] trait. You have familiarity with [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow (Player Core)|crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Heavy Crossbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|heavy crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spears]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|tridents]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
