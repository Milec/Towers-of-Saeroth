# Improved Signature Weapon  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 87

---

Your signature weapon takes on the properties of mystical materials renowned for defeating undead, spirits, and other such creatures. Your signature weapon is treated as [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]], [[Equipment/Duskwood|duskwood]], and [peachwood](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1541) for the purposes of overcoming resistance.
