# Marine Ally  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 14

---

You've befriended a sea creature, which becomes magically bonded to you. You gain a [familiar](Familiars.aspx). The type of creature is up to you, but one of its abilities must always be a swim Speed or the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]] familiar ability.
