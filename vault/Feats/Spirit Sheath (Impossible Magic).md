# Spirit Sheath  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 18

---

You’ve magically constructed an extradimensional sheath for your weapon that you access through your clothing, typically through a pocket, sleeve, or seam. This sheath is hard to notice, granting a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Conceal an Object (Player Core)|Conceal an Item]] within it. A spirit sheath can hold up to 1 Bulk worth of weapons.

During a [[Actions/Spellstrike]], you can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to draw one weapon from the sheath before you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]]. You can draw or return a weapon to a spirit sheath as an Interact action, as can anyone else holding or wearing the clothing that contains the sheath. A [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] spell or similar effect can be used against the spirit sheath to expel the weapon. The [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check is made against your spell DC and the counteract rank is half your level rounded up. The weapon is also expelled if you become [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].
