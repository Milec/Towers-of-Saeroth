# Ghost Eater Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Eater]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

---

You have trained as a ghost eater, a hunter specialized in the destruction of spirits. You know how to channel your own spiritual energy to harm spirits. Your weapon and unarmed attack Strikes become [[Traits/Magical|magical]]. Against [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures, they also gain the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from this archetype.
