# Halfling Weapon Trickster  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 53

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Halfling Weapon Familiarity]]

---

You are particularly adept at fighting with your people's favored weapons. Whenever you critically succeed at an attack roll using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]], a [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling|sling]], or a [[Traits/Halfling|halfling]] weapon, you apply the weapon's critical specialization effect.
