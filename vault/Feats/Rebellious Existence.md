# Rebellious Existence  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

---

You have tied yourself so absolutely to the cause of liberty that your very existence is an act of rebellion. You ignore any [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects without the [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] trait that would rob you of your agency (for example, on a failed save against [[Spells/Charm (Player Core)|_charm_]] you would still be able to take hostile actions, and against [[Spells/Laughing Fit|_laughing fit_]] you would still be able to take reactions but would remain [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], but on a critical failure against fear you would still be fleeing because it’s a fear effect). Additionally, you are [[Rules/Immunities (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|immune]] to effects that would imprison your soul (such as [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]], or a [[Equipment/Final Blade|final blade]]).
