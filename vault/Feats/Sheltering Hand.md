# Sheltering Hand single action or two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 106

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

Animals, plants, and the earth itself rush to protect you from harm, interposing themselves between you and danger. You and your allies in your waking world gain [[Rules/Cover (GM Core)|cover]] from enemies you are aware of until the start of your next turn. The cover is based on the number of actions you spend. If you use one action, it is lesser cover; if you spend two actions, it is standard cover.
