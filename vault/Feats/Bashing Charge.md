# Bashing Charge two actions  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 108

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zombie]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You smash, bust, and charge through solid obstacles without hesitation. Stride twice. Once during your movement, if your movement passes through or ends adjacent to a door, window, fence, wall, or similar obstacle, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Force Open]] the obstacle with a +1 circumstance bonus to the roll; on a failure, your movement ends at that obstacle.
