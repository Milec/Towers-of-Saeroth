# Tongue Tether  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Snaptongue Tripkee]] heritage

---

Your tongue can momentarily latch on as readily as your hands. So long as you can freely open your mouth, you do not need free hands in order to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]], [[Actions/Reposition]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]]. If you have the [[Feats/Long Tongue (Player Core 2)|Long Tongue]] feat, you can Disarm, Grab an Edge, Reposition, and Trip with your tongue at a distance that is 5 feet beyond your normal reach.
