# Poultice Preparation  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 202

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist (Player Core 2)|Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Herbalist Dedication]]

---

When you create an [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] as an herbal item, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the [[Traits/Oil (GM Core)|oil]] trait (but remains [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]], not [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) and loses the elixir trait. You can apply a poultice by [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interacting]] with one hand. When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item’s normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent]] acid, bleed, or fire damage, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.
