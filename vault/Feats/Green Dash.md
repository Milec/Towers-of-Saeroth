# Green Dash single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Requirements**
You're adjacent to a tree, shrub, or plant that occupies at least a 5-foot square.

---

The spirits of vegetation send you on your way, letting you move in a burst of speed. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] with a +15-foot status bonus to your Speed. You can [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead if you have the corresponding movement type.
