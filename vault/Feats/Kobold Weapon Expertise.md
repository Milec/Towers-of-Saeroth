# Kobold Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kobold Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow|crossbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatpick|greatpick]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Pick|light pick]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick|pick]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]], as well as any [[Traits/Kobold|kobold]] weapons in which you are trained.
