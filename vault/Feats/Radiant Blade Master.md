# Radiant Blade Master  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 115

---

Your divine ally turns your chosen weapon into a paragon of its type. When you choose the weapon for your blade divine ally during your preparations, add the following property runes to the list of effects you can choose from: [[Equipment/Dancing|_dancing_]], [[Equipment/Disrupting|_greater disrupting_]], and [[Equipment/Keen|_keen_]].
