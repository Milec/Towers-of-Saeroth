# Vampiric Spell single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 35

---

Your magic drains the life from your enemy. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that deals damage, you gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the initial damage a single creature takes from this spell; calculate these temporary Hit Points using the creature that took the most damage from the spell. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.
