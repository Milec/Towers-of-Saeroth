# Mask of The 12th Step  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 291

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Razmiran Priest]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Razmiran Priest Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

You have reached the 12th Step and been instructed in greater secrets of [[Setting/Deities/Razmir|Razmir’s]] doctrine. You can [[Rules/Craft (GM Core)|Craft]] your [[Equipment/Razmiri Mask|Razmiri mask]] out of [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] or upgrade your existing mask to a silver one in the same time it takes to Craft one made of iron . A silver Razmiri mask grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]], is a 10th-level item, and gains the following activation in addition to that possessed by the standard mask.

**Activate—Call Upon Razmir’s Mercy** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** three times per day; **Effect** Exhorting Razmir to purge impurities from your target, you lay hands on a creature within reach and cast [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell with a spell rank equal to half your level. Unlike a normal _cleanse affliction_ spell, this doesn’t reduce the stage of the affliction; instead, if the counteract check is successful, the [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|affliction’s]] stage is temporarily reduced by 1 and its effects are suppressed for 24 hours, after which the affliction resumes in full force. If the target would have been required to attempt additional saves against the affliction during the 24 hours it was suppressed, they must attempt all of those saving throws as soon as the 24-hour duration ends. This could mean that the target saves successfully and it is as if the affliction were truly healed, or it could mean that the affliction returns and advances multiple stages all at once.
