# Performer's Treatment  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 91

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

---

With your experience as a performer, you can keep a set of medical tools handy at all times. You can wear a [[Equipment/Healer's Toolkit|healer's toolkit]] as part of any costume or disguise. Creatures will generally only notice the toolkit it if they use a [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action and succeed at a Perception check against your Performance DC.

 In addition, you can realize at the last moment when you should just be pretending to avoid doing any lasting harm. When you critically fail a Medicine check to [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]], [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]], [[Actions/Treat Poison (Player Core)|Treat Poison]], or [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], you can attempt a Performance check against the patient’s Perception DC. On a success, your critical failure at the Medicine check becomes a failure.
