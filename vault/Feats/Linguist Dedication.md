# Linguist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 178

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Linguist]]

**Prerequisites**
you speak at least three languages

---

You have studied languages and their development. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]; if you were already trained in Society, you instead become an expert in Society. You gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat twice.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the linguist archetype.
