# Distracting Shadows  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 60

---

You have learned to remain hidden by using larger folk as a distraction to avoid drawing attention to yourself. You can use creatures that are at least one [[Rules/Size, Space, and Reach|size]] larger than you (usually Medium or larger) as cover for the [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] actions, though you still can't use such creatures as cover for other uses, such as the [[Actions/Take Cover]] action.
