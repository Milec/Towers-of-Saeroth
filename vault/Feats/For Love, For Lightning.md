# For Love, For Lightning  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]

**Access**
A character who performs a specialized mission for Clarethe Iomedar has access to this option.

---

Rumors suggest this technique was pioneered by knights who undertook missions for Clarethe Iomedar at the half-moon, but many Crimson Reclaimers have since mastered it. You learn the [[Spells/For Love, For Lightning|_for love, for lightning_]] focus spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can regain using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity to recite the Crimson Oath and meditate on its teachings; if you already have a focus pool, increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. Your knight reclaimant focus spells are divine spells; when you gain this feat, you become trained in divine spell attacks and spell DCs. Charisma is the key spellcasting ability for knight reclaimant focus spells.
