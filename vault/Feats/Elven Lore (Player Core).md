# Elven Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 48

---

You've studied traditional elven arts, learning about arcane magic and the world around you. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] and [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] general feat for Elf [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]
