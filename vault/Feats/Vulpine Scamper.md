# Vulpine Scamper  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 50

**Prerequisites**
fox alternative form

---

You’re especially nimble while in your full fox shape. While you’ve Changed Shape into a fox, you gain the following benefits.

- You can use your own Speed if it’s higher than 20 feet.
- You can use your own [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier for checks to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]].
- You can scale short distances with a bit of a running start. When you attempt to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], and your previous action was to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] at least 20 feet along solid ground, you gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 20 feet for the duration of that Climb action.
