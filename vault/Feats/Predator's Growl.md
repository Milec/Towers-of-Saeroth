# Predator's Growl ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Trigger**
You successfully [[Actions/Seek]] a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creature.

---

You give a throaty growl to attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]] a creature you just found. You don't take the –4 penalty for not sharing a language with the creature.
