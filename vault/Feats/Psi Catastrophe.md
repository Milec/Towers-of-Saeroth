# Psi Catastrophe two actions  *Feat 12*

You stop holding your power back, unleashing it all in a catastrophic blast. All creatures in a 20-foot emanation take 1d6 bludgeoning damage per level with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. Your psyche subsides as your power is expended.
