# Grippli Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 119

---

You've trained with weapons ideally suited to marshes and dense forests. You are trained with [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowguns]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet|hatchets]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe|scythes]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbows]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbows]]. You also gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Grippli|grippli]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial grippli weapons are simple weapons, and advanced grippli weapons are martial weapons.
