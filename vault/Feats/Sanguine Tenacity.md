# Sanguine Tenacity  *Feat 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 84

**Prerequisites**
Constitution +4

---

Your blood carries permanent regenerative properties. The penalties from [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] and the penalties and lost HP from [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] both affect you as if the condition values were 1 lower than they actually are. You also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to persistent bleed damage and reduce the DC of checks to recover from persistent bleed damage to 11, or 6 if you have particularly effective assistance.
