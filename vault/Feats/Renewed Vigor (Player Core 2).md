# Renewed Vigor single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 81

---

Through the haze of battle, you quickly recover your raging vigor. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level plus your Constitution modifier. If you made an [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] action this turn, increase the number of temporary Hit Points to your level plus your Constitution modifier. These temporary Hit Points last until the end of your rage.
