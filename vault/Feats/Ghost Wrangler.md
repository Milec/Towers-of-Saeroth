# Ghost Wrangler single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Spirit|spirit instinct]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

Spirits around you coalesce upon your fists, enabling you to seize and harm other ghosts and spirits. Until the beginning of your next turn, your fists gain the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune, and you can make Strength-based skill checks against [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures.
