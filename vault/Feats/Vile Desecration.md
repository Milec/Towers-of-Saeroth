# Vile Desecration  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 122

**Prerequisites**
evil alignment

---

You combine unholy might with negative energy to damage [[Bestiary/Families/Angel|angels]], [[Bestiary/Families/Archon|archons]], and other self-righteous denizens of the Outer Planes. When you cast a [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spell, you can change the damage you deal to [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]] creatures to evil damage instead of negative damage.
