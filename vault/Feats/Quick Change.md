# Quick Change  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 197

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Vigilante]], [Venture-Gossip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=311) (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; [[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]

---

You can shift between your identities with ease. Instead of spending 1 minute to change your identity, you can now do so as a 3-action activity. If you are legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], you can perform this change as a 1-action activity.
