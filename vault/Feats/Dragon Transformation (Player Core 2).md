# Dragon Transformation single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 84

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragon's Rage Wings (Player Core 2)|Dragon's Rage Wings]]

---

You transform into a ferocious Large dragon, gaining the effects of 6th-rank [[Spells/Dragon Form (Player Core)|_dragon form_]] except that you use your own AC and attack modifier, you apply your extra damage from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]], and the Dragon Breath action uses your class DC. Add the temporary Hit Points from _dragon form_ to any you already have from entering a rage (or any other action with the [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|rage]] trait). The action to Dismiss the transformation gains the [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|rage]] trait.

 At 18th level, you gain a +20-foot status bonus to your fly Speed, your damage bonus with dragon Strikes increases to +12, and you gain a +14 status bonus to your Dragon Breath damage.
