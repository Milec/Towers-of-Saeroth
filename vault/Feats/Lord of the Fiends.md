# Lord of the Fiends two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archfiend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archfiend Dedication]]

---

You consider every fiend a future subject and can assess them at a glance. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. Attempt a check at mythic proficiency to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about every [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] you can see. For the next hour, whenever you roll a success on a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check against a fiend, you get a critical success instead.
