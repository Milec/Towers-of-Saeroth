# Tinkering Fingers  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 22

---

You're good with your hands and can quickly improvise a fix for broken or damaged equipment. You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can [[Actions/Repair]] an item without using a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]] without taking the –2 circumstance penalty, improvising tools from whatever you have at hand.
