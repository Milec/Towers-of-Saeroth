# Lunge single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thlipit Contestant]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You are wielding a melee weapon.

---

Extending your body to its limits, you attack an enemy that would normally be beyond your reach. Make a Strike with a melee weapon, increasing your reach by 5 feet for that Strike. If the weapon has the [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Shove|shove]], or [[Traits/Trip|trip]] trait, you can use the corresponding action instead of a Strike.
