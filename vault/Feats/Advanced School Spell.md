# Advanced School Spell  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 211

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Red Mantis Assassin (Prey for Death)|Red Mantis Assassin]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|arcane school]]

---

You gain access to a powerful new school spell depending on your arcane school. If you’re an abjurer, you gain [[Spells/Energy Absorption|_energy absorption_]]; if you’re a conjurer, you gain [[Spells/Dimensional Steps|_dimensional steps_]]; if you’re a diviner, you gain [[Spells/Vigilant Eye|_vigilant eye_]]; if you’re an enchanter, you gain [[Spells/Dread Aura|_dread aura_]]; if you’re an evoker, you gain [[Spells/Elemental Tempest|_elemental tempest_]]; if you’re an illusionist, you gain [[Spells/Invisibility Cloak|_invisibility cloak_]]; if you’re a necromancer, you gain [[Spells/Life Siphon|_life siphon_]]; and if you’re a transmuter, you gain [[Spells/Shifting Form|_shifting form_]]. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.
