# Golden League Xun Dedication  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden League Xun]]

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Underworld Lore]]

---

You are a xun for the Golden League, aiding the organization. You gain expert proficiency in two of the following skills (or in two other skills of your choice in which you're trained, if you were already an expert in the listed skills): [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]. In addition, if you use Underworld Lore to [[Actions/Earn Income]] or [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], when you get a success, you get a critical success instead.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you've gained two other feats from the Golden League Xun archetype.
