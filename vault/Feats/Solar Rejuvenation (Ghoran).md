# Solar Rejuvenation (Ghoran)  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 39

---

The warmth and light of the sun gives you life. If you rest outdoors for 10 minutes during the day, you regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier × half your level. You gain this benefit in addition to any healing from [[Actions/Treat Wounds]].
