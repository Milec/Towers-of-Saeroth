# Debilitating Breath  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 217

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Breath of the Dragon (Player Core 2)|Breath of the Dragon]]

---

Your breath causes lasting hindrance to those it harms. Creatures who fail their saves against your Breath of the Dragon gain one of the following conditions, based on the damage type of your breath: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage), [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], physical, or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage), or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 ([[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]] damage). The condition lasts until the end of your next turn.
