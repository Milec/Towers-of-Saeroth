# Divine Apex  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]] (Level 20)

---

You can pour divine power into an item, letting the wearer exceed their limits. When you complete your daily preparations, you can touch one worn magical item that doesn't have the apex trait. It gains the [[Traits/Apex (GM Core)|apex]] trait until your next preparations, and you choose one of your deity's [[Rules/Religion (Player Core)|divine attributes]]. The item increases that attribute modifier by 1 or to +4, whichever is higher. A character can benefit from only one apex item at a time, as normal.
