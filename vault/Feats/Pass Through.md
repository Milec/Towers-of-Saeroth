# Pass Through three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Incorporeality, Flight, and Rejuvenation|Ghost]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]

---

Filtering your form through the substance of an object, you can pass through walls, doors, and more. You Fly up to your Speed. During this movement, you can try to move through one object. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check as you try to enter its space. The DC is typically 30 to move through a wall of up to 5 feet, 15 for an ordinary door, and 10 for thinner structures like windows; the GM might set the DC higher for especially dense materials like [[Equipment/Adamantine|adamantine]] or lead, or for barriers that are magically reinforced. You can't Pass Through an obstacle made of magical force, such as a [[Spells/Wall of Force|_wall of force_]].

**Success** You move through the object, treating the square within it as difficult terrain. If you end your turn inside an object, you can move out of it only if you Pass Through again or use some other means of moving through a solid object. As normal for being [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]], starting your turn inside an object makes you [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for that turn.  

**Failure** Your movement ends, and you trigger reactions as if you moved out of the square you started in.
