# Moisture Bath single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 39

**Frequency**
one per day

---

Your moist skin is especially resistant to damage from temperature-altering effects. Attempt a DC 10 flat check; on a success you recover from any ongoing [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire and persistent cold damage]]. Additionally, you gain fire and cold resistance equal to half your level for 1 minute.
