# Worm Empathy  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You can connect with [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worms]] (and other worm-related creatures, at the GM's discretion) on a rudimentary level. You can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on cave worms and to make simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of them. In most cases, the cave worm's starting attitude is indifferent and they will give you time to establish contact.
