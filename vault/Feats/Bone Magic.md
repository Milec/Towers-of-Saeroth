# Bone Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 45

---

Your connection to your iruxi ancestors manifests as a simple primal spell that you cast using a fragment of an ancestor's bones. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal spell list]]. You can cast this cantrip as a primal innate spell at will, and it's heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
