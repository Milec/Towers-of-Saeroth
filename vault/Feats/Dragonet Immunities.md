# Dragonet Immunities  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 213

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragonet Resistances]]

---

You will not fall asleep unless you choose to allow it, nor will you sit still. Your resistances to sleep and paralysis increase, letting you completely ignore such effects, as befits a dragon. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] to [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] effects and effects that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]].
