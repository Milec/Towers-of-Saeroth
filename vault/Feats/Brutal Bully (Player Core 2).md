# Brutal Bully  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 79

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You push your foes around and leave bruises. While raging, when you successfully [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] an enemy, you deal that enemy bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier; add this to the damage from a critical success to Trip.
