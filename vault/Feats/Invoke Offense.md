# Invoke Offense single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 294

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Invoker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re under the effects of Enter Spirit Trance.

---

You manifest a physical attack of the spirits all around you, such as the claw of an animal spirit or the whipping vine of a nature spirit. You gain an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage for the duration of your spirit trance. This unarmed attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] traits. At 5th level, this unarmed attack gains the benefits of a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_striking_]] rune. At 12th level, this unarmed attack gains the benefits of a _greater striking_ rune. At 20th level, this unarmed attack gains the benefits of a _major striking_ rune.
