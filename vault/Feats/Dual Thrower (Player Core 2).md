# Dual Thrower  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 196

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication]]

---

You know how to throw two weapons as easily as strike with them. Whenever a feat you gained from the dual-weapon warrior archetype allows you to make a melee Strike, you can instead make a ranged Strike with a [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown]] weapon or a one-handed ranged weapon you are wielding (weapons that need “1+” hand, such as [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow (Player Core)|longbows]], don't qualify). Any effects from these feats that apply to one-handed melee weapons or melee Strikes also apply to one-handed ranged weapons and ranged Strikes.
