# Fan Dancer Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

You sweep across the battlefield, manifesting both the gentle spring breeze and the crisp autumn gusts. You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. At 7th level you become a master in Performance, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Performance. Whenever you [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] while holding a fan, you can give your Feint the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] trait. If you do, you can Stride 10 feet before or after as part of the same action.
