# Fey Disguise  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 131

---

You can disguise yourself with magical illusions. You can cast [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] once per day as a 1st-level primal innate spell. At 7th level, the spell is heightened to 2nd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the spell is heightened an additional spell level.
