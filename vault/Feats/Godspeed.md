# Godspeed single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 82

---

Mythic power grants you speed to rival [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]] and [[Rules/Demigods and Other Divinities|demigods]]. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; for the next minute, you gain a +10-foot status bonus to all your Speeds and are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]]. You can use the extra action each round to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], or [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]].
