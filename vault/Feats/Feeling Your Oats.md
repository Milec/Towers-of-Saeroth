# Feeling Your Oats single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Open]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 79

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]], [[Archetypes/Dandy]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 6)

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication]] or [[Feats/Dandy Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

---

You are incredible, impeccable, an icon of style and grace—and everyone knows it. In this moment, you are living your fantasy, and no one can shake you out of it. Roll a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check. You can substitute the result of your Deception check for one Will save you would be required to attempt in the next minute.
