# Focused Shot single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 124

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Monastic Archer Stance (Player Core 2)|Monastic Archer Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Monastic Archer Stance.

---

You achieve a state of perfect calm before unleashing your attack, opening your mind to the minute movements of the world around you to increase your accuracy. Make a ranged weapon Strike against a target within your weapon's first range increment. This Strike ignores the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition and all [[Rules/Cover|cover]].
