# Friend of the Family  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Heavenscribe Kobold]] heritage

---

You come from a long line of imperial kobolds—if you meet a stranger who has some political standing in Tian Xia, they might know someone from your family line. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] when speaking to politicians or other officials.
