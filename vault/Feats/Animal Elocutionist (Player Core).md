# Animal Elocutionist  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 52

---

You hear animal sounds as conversations instead of unintelligent noise, and you can respond in turn. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with animals. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on animals.
