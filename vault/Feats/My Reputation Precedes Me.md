# My Reputation Precedes Me  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

---

Long years of brave exploits haven’t gone unnoticed. Whether you know just the right things to say or those you’re speaking with recognize you as a reincarnated hero, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks made to [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make An Impression]], and to Intimidation checks made to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]]. At 18th level, these circumstance bonuses increase to +3. If you spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic point]] when you attempt such a check, you do so at mythic proficiency.
