# Embodied Dreadnought Subjectivity  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fleshwarp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 29

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Cataphract Fleshwarp]] heritage

**Access**
You're from Nex

---

Your colossal might marks you as a behemoth, the pride of Nex's Fleshforges, and the heaviest ordnance is a child's toy in your monumental grasp. You're a dreadnought, a living siege weapon, albeit one that wields other siege weapons in battle as your endoskeleton and flesh toughens. You gain the [[Feats/Hefty Hauler]] skill feat, and your maximum Bulk limit further increases by 3, for a total of 2 to your [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered|encumbered]] limit and 5 to your maximum limit.
