# Malleable Movement free action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mind Smith (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mind Smith Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
You [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]].

---

You shift the shape of your weapon to help you Leap farther and faster. You shift your weapon into a long flexible pole, climbing hook, or similar aid, adding an extra 5 feet to the distance you're able to Leap. As normal, this can't increase the distance of your Leap beyond your Speed.
