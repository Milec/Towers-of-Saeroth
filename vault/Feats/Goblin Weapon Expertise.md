# Goblin Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 49

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Goblin Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your goblin affinity blends with your class training, granting you great skill with goblin weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Dogslicer|dogslicer]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Horsechopper|horsechopper]], and all [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] weapons in which you are trained.
