# Dwarven Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 37

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dwarven Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your dwarven affinity blends with your training, granting you great skill with dwarven weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in certain weapons, you also gain that proficiency for [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axes]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick|picks]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer|warhammers]], and all [[Traits/Dwarf|dwarven]] weapons in which you are trained.
