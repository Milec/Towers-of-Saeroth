# Draconic Sight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 46

---

You have a fraction of the senses of your draconic forebear. You gain low-light vision, or you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if your ancestry already grants you low-light vision.
