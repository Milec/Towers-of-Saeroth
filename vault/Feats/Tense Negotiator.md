# Tense Negotiator  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swordmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Swordmaster Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]

---

You often find that the best way to win a conflict means avoiding a fight in the first place. You're good at talking to potential opponents without making things worse. If you would critically fail at a check to [[Actions/Make an Impression]], you instead fail. You can attempt checks to make [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of creatures who're [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] toward you.
