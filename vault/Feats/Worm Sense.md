# Worm Sense  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You emulate the senses of the [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worm]]. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] as an imprecise sense within 5 feet.

 The range of the tremorsense increases to 10 feet at 8th level and to 15 feet at 12th level.
