# Treespeech  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 49

**Prerequisites**
You speak [[Setting/Languages/Muan]].

---

Your connection to elemental wood allows you to communicate with trees in the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane|Universe]] in Muan, the language of wood elementals. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with woody plants like trees and shrubs.
