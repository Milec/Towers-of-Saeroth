# Dart Between two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

---

In the blink of an eye, you transport yourself and any items you’re wearing and holding from your current space to an unoccupied space within 60 feet that you can see. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an extradimensional container—you cannot Dart Between. When you arrive at your destination, you can Part the Veil as a free action, even if you wouldn’t normally meet its requirements. If you are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], this action does not cause you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]] provided you still have [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover, greater cover,]] or [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]] from the creatures you were hidden from after the teleportation.
