# Rolling White Bottle Form ⤾  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 68

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Teakettle Form]]

**Trigger**
You [[Actions/Drop Prone (Player Core)|Drop Prone]] or othwerwise become [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]

---

As you fall to the ground, you transform into a white bottle, gourd, or sake jar, then roll away before anyone can pick you up. You [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]] up to two times, and your movement doesn’t provoke reactions—you somehow always roll just out of reach. When you come to a stop, you return to your tanuki form, face down on the ground.
