# Spell Shroud single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 141

---

Your spell shrouds you in a billowing cloud. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that targets you, you surround yourself in a 15-foot emanation of dense magical mist that lasts until the start of your next turn. All creatures within the cloud become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and all creatures outside the cloud are concealed to creatures within it.
