# Surprise Snare three actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 194

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Snarecrafter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snarecrafter Dedication]]

---

You install one of your snares prepared for quick deployment in a space that's occupied by an opponent. It must be a snare that normally takes 1 minute or less to [[Actions/Craft]]. The snare automatically triggers, but it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to any applicable save DC, as well as any attack rolls or other checks the snare attempts.
