# Keen Nose  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

---

Accustomed to the foul air of underground tunnels, your sense of smell is sharpened, and noxious scents are less offensive. You gain [[Rules/Scent|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a range of 30 feet. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves against [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] effects that cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition.
