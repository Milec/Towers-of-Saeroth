# Inner Strength single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 79

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Spirit (Player Core 2)|spirit instinct]]

---

Your strength is part of your rage. As long as your anger remains, you can gather your strength and rage to overcome any sort of enfeeblement. You reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition's value by 1.
