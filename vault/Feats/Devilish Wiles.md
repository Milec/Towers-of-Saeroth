# Devilish Wiles  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 62

---

Traditionally, fiends are exceptionally good at persuading people to do things, even when their targets know it's a bad idea. You're also rather good at it, compelling the weak and weak-willed to your bidding. You can cast [[Spells/Charm|_charm_]] once per day as a 1st-level divine innate spell.
