# Tenacious Net  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 121

---

Escaping your nets is no simple task. The [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] DC to [[Actions/Force Open]] or [[Actions/Escape]] your nets increases from 16 to 18. After a creature you grabbed with a net Escapes or Forces Open the net, stray strands of the net cling to the creature, causing them to remain [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the beginning of their next turn.
