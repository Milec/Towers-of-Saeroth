# Kobold Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 22

---

You attentively learned key kobold survival strategies from your elders. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Kobold Lore]].
