# Blessed Denial ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 190

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Blessed One Dedication (Player Core 2)|Blessed One Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally within 30 feet would become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]].

---

You exude peace and protection, lessening a harmful condition as it falls upon an ally. Reduce the value of the condition your ally would take by 1 (to a minimum of 0). If the ally has more than one eligible condition at the same time, choose one to reduce.
