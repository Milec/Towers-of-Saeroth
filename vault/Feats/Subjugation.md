# Subjugation two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Avenging Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Avenging Runelord Dedication]]

---

You have managed to gain a degree of control over the runelord’s ancient soul within your mind, enough that you can project this invasive influence into the minds of others. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. You attempt to send your runelord’s influence into the mind of a creature you can sense within 30 feet. The creature attempts a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Giants take a –2 status penalty to this saving throw. Regardless of the result, the target is temporarily immune to this ability for 24 hours once the Subjugation ends.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as it fights off your runelord’s influence. You gain awareness of its abilities and weaknesses as if you achieved a critical success on a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check to identify the creature.  

**Failure** The creature relinquishes control of its faculties and abilities. You gain awareness of its abilities and weaknesses as success. You [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|control]] the creature until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but you control the creature for 1 minute.
