# Catfolk Dance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

---

You have a habit of always being in the way when other creatures attempt to move. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check against an adjacent creature's Reflex DC.

**Critical Success** The target creature gains a –2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.  

**Success** The target creature gains a –2 circumstance penalty to Reflex saves until the start of your next turn.
