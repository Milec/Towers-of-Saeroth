# Suli-Jann  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Suli]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 110

---

Your elemental ancestor was a [[Bestiary/Janni|janni]], genies that walk the mortal world and embody all four elements together. You inherited a janni's nature as a wanderer and ability to survive in many types of environment, from the hottest desert to the coldest tundra. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Forager]] skill feat as a bonus feat.
