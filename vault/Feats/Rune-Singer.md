# Rune-Singer  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

---

You practice the lost art of using music to guide your runecarving, singing the runes into existence as much as crafting them. You can use Performance instead of [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] when attempting Crafting checks related to runes.

Once per 10 minutes, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] with song alone. When you do, you use the 2-action version of Trace Rune as a single action without needing an [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|artisan's toolkit]], and you remove the action's [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait. You don't need to be able to move your hands when Tracing a Rune using song, but you do need to be able to sing in a clear voice.
