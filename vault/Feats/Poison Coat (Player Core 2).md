# Poison Coat  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 210

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Poisoner Dedication (Player Core 2)|Poisoner Dedication]]

---

In nature, certain animals are covered in venomous spines, and just brushing against them can prove deadly. You expend a [[Traits/Contact (GM Core)|contact]] or [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] poison and spend 10 minutes brushing that poison onto your clothing or weaving sharp, poisoned needles into the garment's fabric. You can have only one poison suffused into your clothing at a time. You can use the Once Bitten reaction while you have a poison suffused in this way.

**Once Bitten** ⤾A creature adjacent to you hits you with a melee unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]; **Requirements** You have prepared your clothing to poison attackers; **Effect** The triggering creature is exposed to the suffused poison, and the poison becomes inert.
