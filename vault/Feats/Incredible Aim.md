# Incredible Aim two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 149

---

By spending a moment to focus, you can ensure your attack strikes true. Make a ranged weapon Strike. On this Strike, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition.
