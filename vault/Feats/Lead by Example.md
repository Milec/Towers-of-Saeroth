# Lead by Example single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blackjacket]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Blackjacket Dedication]]

---

You telegraph your next attack to let your allies in on your strategy. Make a melee or ranged [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a –2 circumstance penalty. On a hit, the next ally to target the same creature with a Strike gains a +2 circumstance bonus to their attack roll. On a critical hit, the bonus applies to any allies who act before the start of your next turn.
