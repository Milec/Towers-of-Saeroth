# Right-hand Blood  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 19

---

It's said that the flesh of the right side of a hyena can heal diseases, but that the flesh of the left side is deadly and poisonous. You can deal 1 slashing damage to yourself to feed someone blood from your right side and [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]] or take 2d8 damage to [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]] or [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]]; in either case, you don't need a [[Equipment/Healer's Toolkit|healer's toolkit]], and you gain a +1 item bonus to your check. Blood from your left side causes the check to critically fail automatically.
