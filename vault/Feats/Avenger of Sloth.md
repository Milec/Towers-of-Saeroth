# Avenger of Sloth  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 85

---

You've become a champion of the runelords of sloth. They spur you toward idleness, and their spirits are slow to come to your aid. Once they arrive, however, their combined might is undeniable. The first time each day you spend 10 minutes resting, you gain 1 Mythic Point. During this time, you don't need to sleep, but you can't perform activities like [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] or [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]]. You gain the Summon Sloth activity.

**Activate—Summon Sloth** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Spend 1 Mythic Point. You summon the spirit of one of the seven runelords of sloth (Xirie, Ilthyrius, Azeradni, Zalelet, Krenlith, Ivarinna, or Krune) into an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Allies can move through a space occupied by one of these spirits, but a spirit's space is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|greater difficult terrain]] for your enemies.

 For the next minute, you can use a two-action activity with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait to summon another runelord spirit in a different space within 30 feet, to a maximum of seven spirits. While you are within 120 feet of Xanderghul, the Runelord of Pride, you can summon an additional spirit as a single action (instead of as a two-action activity). At the end of the each of your turns, each spirit fires arcane energy at a different enemy of your choice within 120 feet of it (or does nothing if no enemy is within range), dealing 2d4+2 force damage. After 1 minute, all spirits disappear.

## Vengeance Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

During Chapter 2 of this adventure, the PCs can take on the vengeful mantle of a specific runelord's vengeance and embody that need to avenge the violations Runelord Xanderghul has inflicted upon their legacies by creating risen runelords. These rewards manifest in the form of a bonus feat, chosen from the following. A PC can only select one vengeance feat. If you wish to use these feats in the context of another campaign, a mythic PC should be required to use one of their mythic feat slots granted from their mythic destiny.
