# Empathy Incarnate  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 319

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This feat is not available for PFS play._

---

Reincarnation has given you a compassionate perspective and enabled you to relate to almost everyone you speak with, putting them at ease and quickly generating trust. Whenever you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make An Impression]] or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] something, the DC is reduced by 2, and if the creature you’re speaking with is lower level than you, any success is a critical success. Whenever you attempt a Diplomacy check to Gather Information, you can’t critically fail.
