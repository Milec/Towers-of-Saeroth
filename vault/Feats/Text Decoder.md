# Text Decoder  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 289

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Palatine Detective]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Palatine Detective Dedication]]

---

Thanks to your constant involvement with cryptic texts, you have attained an almost-supernatural ability to decipher complex codes and understand ancient writings. You can always use [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] when you [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], no matter the subject matter. In addition, you can ignore language constraints when Deciphering Writing, and when y ou critically fail to Decipher Writing, you get a failure instead.
