# Trapsmith Dedication  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 54

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Trapsmith (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trapsmith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snarecrafter Dedication]] or [[Feats/Snare Specialist]]

---

You use glimmering gears and gnashing mechanisms to manufacture [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|snares]] using specialized parts. Your snares include clockwork and steam-powered cogwheels and gears, and their gush of steam can hinder those you ensnare. If you choose to construct a snare using gears and a creature fails their saving throw against the snare, all creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to that creature for 1 round, as a burst of steam obscures its vision.
