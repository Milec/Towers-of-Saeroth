# The Dead Walk two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|Cursebound]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 141

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ancestors (Player Core 2)|ancestors mystery]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Battle (Player Core 2)|battle mystery]]

---

You beseech warrior spirits to come forth and aid you. Two ghostly warriors manifest within a 30-foot emanation of you and each attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an adjacent enemy, using your spell attack modifier. The warriors' Strikes each deal 4d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage and the warriors can flank with one another and with you and your allies. If you are [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] 2 when you use The Dead Walk, you instead summon three warriors, and if you are cursebound 3, you instead summon four warriors. The warriors disappear at the start of your next turn.
