Your [benefactor’s](DraconicBenefactors.aspx)  blood flows through your veins, and you channel their power into your body. When you critically hit with an unarmed Strike, the target takes an additional 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] of the same type as the damage dealt by the breath of your draconic benefactor. Some examples are shown on the Draconic Benefactors table [here.](DraconicBenefactors.aspx)
