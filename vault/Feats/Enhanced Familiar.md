# Enhanced Familiar  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge]], [[Traits/Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 43, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 134, [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 102, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 42

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Familiar Master]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Elementalist]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Elementalist (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Familiar Master (Player Core 2)|Familiar Master]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Rivethun Emissary]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
a [familiar](Familiars.aspx)

---

You infuse your familiar with additional magical energy. You can select four [familiar or master abilities](Familiars.aspx) each day, instead of two.

**Special** (Wizard) If your arcane thesis is [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Theses/Improved Familiar Attunement|improved familiar attunement]], your familiar's base number of familiar abilities, before adding any extra abilities from the arcane thesis, is four.  
(Witch) Add the bonus familiar abilities you gain for being a witch to this amount.
