# Radiant Circuitry single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Android]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 72

---

Your biological circuitry emits light like a torch, casting bright light in a 20-foot radius (and dim light for the next 20 feet). The light shuts off when you take this action again or are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
