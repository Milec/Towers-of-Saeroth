# Play Dead single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Skeleton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 56

---

You lie [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and pretend to be an ordinary skeleton. As long as you don't move or take any actions requiring anything other than your mind, you appear dead. To see through your ruse, another creature must succeed at a check against your [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] DC, either by [[Actions/Seek|Seeking]] or [[Actions/Recall Knowledge|Recalling Knowledge]] (typically using [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] or an appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill). This ability doesn't work against creatures that already know you're an animate undead unless you have changed your appearance.
