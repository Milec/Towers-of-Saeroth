# Torrent in the Blood two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Vitality]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 32

---

A healing wave splashes across creatures in a 30-foot cone, its cleansing water driving afflictions from the body. Each creature in the area regains 3d8 Hit Points and can attempt a new save against one [[Traits/Poison|poison]] or [[Traits/Disease|disease]] affliction affecting it; on a failed save, the condition doesn't worsen.

 Each creature that benefited from this impulse becomes temporarily immune to Torrent in the Blood for 10 minutes.

---
**Level (+2)** The healing increases by 1d8.
