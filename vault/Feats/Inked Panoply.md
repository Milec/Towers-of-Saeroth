# Inked Panoply ⤾  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Tattooed Historian]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tattooed Historian Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature targets you with an [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], and you can see the attacker.

---

Your tattoos can briefly animate and extend from your skin, granting you a spectral shieldbearer who protects you from harm. Doing so expends one use of your storied skin, granting you a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. In addition, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and [[Traits/Void|void]] damage equal to twice your number of tattooed historian feats against the triggering attack.
