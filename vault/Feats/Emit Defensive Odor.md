# Emit Defensive Odor single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]], [[Traits/Inhaled (GM Core)|Inhaled]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 19

**Frequency**
once per day

---

While athamarus' natural pheromones are typically used for communication, you have developed yours into a defense mechanism to ward off foes. You emit a thick spray of defensive pheromones at an adjacent creature, who takes 2d6 poison damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC.

 At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
