# High-Speed Regeneration free action  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 10, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 101

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

Your wounds knit together with barely a thought. You regain Hit Points equal to double your level. When you gain this deviation, the GM secretly selects one type of energy damage or precious material, such as fire or [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]]. When you take damage of that type, your wound smokes until the end of your next turn, preventing you from using High-Speed Regeneration.
