# Pact of Infernal Prowess  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 167

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbinder (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Pactbinder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pactbinder Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Pactbinder Dedication]]

---

You've sworn a pact with devils, granting you success in life in exchange for your soul in the afterlife. Once per hour, when you critically fail a check, you can reroll the check as a free action; this is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect. Additionally, you can choose to automatically succeed at checks to Earn Income for tasks below your level, without rolling.

 However, when you die, your soul is consigned to Hell. You can't be returned to life except by powerful magic such as the [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual, and even then, the devils responsible for the contract can track your every move for 1 year. The devils gain the effects of a [[Spells/Pinpoint|_pinpoint_]] spell. They can also use 10th-rank [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] on you at will, and you automatically critically fail your saving throw.

**Special** You can't retrain out of a Pact of Infernal Prowess without journeying to Hell and destroying the devil's written contract spelling out the terms of your pact.
