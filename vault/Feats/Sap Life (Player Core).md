# Sap Life  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 115

---

You draw the life force out of your enemies. When you cast a [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]] spell and damage at least one living creature, you regain Hit Points equal to the spell rank of your harm spell. If you aren't a living creature, you gain no benefit from this feat.
