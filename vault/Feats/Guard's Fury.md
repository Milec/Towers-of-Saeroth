# Guard's Fury  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 172

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ulfen Guard]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ulfen Guard Dedication]]; you can’t already use the [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] action

---

Some Ulfen Guards tap into a well of fury to protect their charges. You can use the [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] action. While raging, you take a –1 penalty to AC. If you’re adjacent to your designated ally while raging, increase the additional damage from Rage from 2 to 4.
