# Infused Void single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

---

If the next action you use is to cast a grave spell that deals damage, creatures damaged by the spell take additional [[Traits/Void|void]] damage equal to the rank of the spell. If this void damage or the spell reduces one or more creatures to 0 Hit Points, you regain 1 [[Rules/Focus|Focus Point]]. You can regain a Focus Point this way only once per 10 minutes.
