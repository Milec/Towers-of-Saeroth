# Corgi Mount  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 129

---

The smallest of fey have ridden corgis as mounts since time immemorial, leading to a pattern on corgis' backs called a “faerie saddle.” You have formed a magical connection with a corgi that can serve as your mount. Your corgi [familiar](Familiars.aspx) is Small rather than Tiny, and it's appropriate for use as your mount, unlike most familiars. It has the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Scent|scent]] ability, which counts against your limit for familiar and master abilities as normal. Furthermore, it can never gain a familiar ability that grants it any Speeds other than a land Speed. If you're a [[Ancestries/Heritages/Pixie|pixie]], you can't ride a corgi due to your Size, but you can take this feat to gain a corgi familiar.
