# Vishkanya Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 61

---

You favor weapons historically used in subtle work and in ceremony. You gain access to and are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowgun]], [fighting fan](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=93), [[Equipment/Weapons/Kris|kris]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]]. In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Vishkanya|vishkanyan]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial vishkanyan weapons are simple weapons and advanced vishkanyan weapons are martial weapons.
