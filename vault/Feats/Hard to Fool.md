# Hard to Fool  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fetchling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 86

---

You're accustomed to seeing through shifting darkness and bewildering magic. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on Perception checks against illusions as well as to Will saves against [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]] and [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]] effects.
