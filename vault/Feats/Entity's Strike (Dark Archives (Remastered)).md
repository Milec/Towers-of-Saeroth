# Entity's Strike  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 140

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Vessel (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Living Vessel]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Vessel Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Living Vessel Dedication]]

---

The more you try to force your body to stay yours, the more it warps to channel the energy flowing through you. Whether you grow horns to match the wild hunt fey within you, develop a claw like that of the treachery demon sharing your soul, or feel a tentacle rip out of you from the outer being that imprinted itself on your psyche, the entity within you refuses to be contained. You gain an unarmed attack of your choice with its type determined by your entity. It deals 1d6 damage of a damage type appropriate for the unarmed attack (such as bludgeoning for the tentacle). This unarmed attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] traits.
