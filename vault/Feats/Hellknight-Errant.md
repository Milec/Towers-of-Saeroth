# Hellknight-Errant  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]; you've refused to take or have failed the Hellknight Test.

---

Either through unfortunate circumstances or your own choice, you remain a Hellknight armiger instead of entering the true ranks of your order, though you still believe in the tenets of law and order. You likely take on an adventuring life with a group of like-minded individuals who aren't Hellknights. The resistance to mental damage you gained from the Hellknight Dedication feat increases to 5 + your number of archetype class feats from the Hellknight archetype.

 You also gain the Warding Shift action, which allows you to keep your allies from harm.

 **Warding Shift** single action **Requirements** You're adjacent to a willing ally; **Effect** You move the adjacent willing ally 5 feet in any direction and can Step into the space the ally vacated.

**Special** You can't take both this feat and [[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]] or [[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Preferment]].
