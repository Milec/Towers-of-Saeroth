# Duel Spell Advantage  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 203

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Psychic Duelist (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Psychic Duelist Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic Duelist Dedication]]

---

When you start a psychic duel, you gain a third natural psychic spell. You can't choose the same spell twice.

 If you're 9th level or higher, add the following spells to the list of natural psychic spells you can choose: [[Spells/Confusion (Player Core)|_confusion_]], [[Spells/Hallucination (Player Core)|_hallucination_]], [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]], [[Spells/Synaptic Pulse (Player Core)|_synaptic pulse_]], [[Spells/Synesthesia (Player Core 2)|_synesthesia_]], and [[Spells/Wave of Despair|_wave of despair_]].
