# Cauterize  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 113

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a loaded firearm and you or an adjacent ally are taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].

---

You use the smoking barrel of your firearm to sear shut a bleeding wound. Make a Strike with your firearm. You then press the heated barrel to the wounds of you or an ally within reach that is taking persistent bleed damage, giving an immediate [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to end the bleed with the lower DC for [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|particularly effective assistance]].
