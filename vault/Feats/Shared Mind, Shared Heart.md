# Shared Mind, Shared Heart  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 63

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Iridian Choirmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Iridian Choirmaster Dedication]]

---

Your thoughts and dreams are your student's thoughts and dreams, and vice versa. You develop a permanent mental  connection, allowing you to communicate telepathically as long as you're on the same plane. When one of you fails a save against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect, the other can choose to take the results of the failed save instead. If both of you are targeted by the same mental effect requiring a saving throw, you and your student take the better of each of your die rolls for the save. You each still add your own save modifier to the die result. This is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect.
