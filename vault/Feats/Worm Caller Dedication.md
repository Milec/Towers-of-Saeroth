# Worm Caller Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 80

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Shake it Off is a reaction_

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cave Worm Lore]]

---

You pledge yourself to understanding and emulating one of the most powerful creatures of the Darklands: the mighty [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worm]]. You gain the following action.

 **Shake It Off** single action ([[Traits/Fortune|fortune]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail or critically fail a saving throw against a condition or adverse effect (such as [[Spells/Baleful Polymorph|_baleful polymorph_]]); **Effect** You reroll the triggering save and use the better result.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Worm Caller archetype.
