# Momentous Charge two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rushing Goat Stance]]

**Requirements**
You’re in Rushing Goat Stance.

---

You rush into your enemies with great and reckless force. You [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], Stride, or [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], then make a ramming horn Strike. On a success, the target of the Strike is pushed up to 10 feet directly away from you. On a critical success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
