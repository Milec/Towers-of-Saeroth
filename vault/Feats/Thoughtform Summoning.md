# Thoughtform Summoning  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 25

---

You can create a prototypical creature from the depths of your imagination instead of calling one from elsewhere. Whenever you summon a creature, you can choose to instead create a psychic construct of that creature. This grants it resistance to physical damage equal to half its level, weakness 5 to force and mental damage, and the ability to pass through enemies' spaces without needing to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] (though it can't end its turn in an occupied space).
