# Viking Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 220

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Viking (Player Core 2)|Viking]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Viking Dedication (Player Core 2)|Viking Dedication]]

---

From childhood, you have been exposed to traditional viking combat techniques, and you soon learned to handle axe, sword, and shield in battle. Now, you can raid proudly alongside your fellows. You gain the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction. Additionally, you have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe (Player Core)|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet (Player Core)|hatchet]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|shield boss]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|shortsword]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these weapons as simple weapons.

At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [critical specialization effect](WeaponGroups.aspx).
