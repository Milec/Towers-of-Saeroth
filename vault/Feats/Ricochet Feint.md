# Ricochet Feint  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 136

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ricochet Stance (Rogue)]]

---

While in Ricochet Stance, you can bounce a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon off a foe to distract them. You can attempt a [[Actions/Feint]] against a creature within the first range increment of a thrown weapon you are wielding, rather than only creatures within your melee reach.
