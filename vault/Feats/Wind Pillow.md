# Wind Pillow  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sylph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 31

---

The winds have whispered to you all your life, and the essence of air itself helps you at times, making you somewhat buoyant when you're suspended in space. Treat all [[Rules/Falling|falls]] as though they were 10 feet less than the actual distance traveled. You also gain the [[Feats/Powerful Leap]] skill feat, even if you don't meet that feat's prerequisites.
