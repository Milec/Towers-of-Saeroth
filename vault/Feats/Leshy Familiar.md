# Leshy Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 133

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]]

---

You gain a leshy familiar, a Tiny plant (or fungus for a fungus leshy) that embodies one of the many spirits of nature. Other than taking the form of a plant instead of an animal, this familiar uses all the same rules as other familiars, which are detailed [here](Familiars.aspx).
