# Tandem Movement single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner]], [[Traits/Tandem]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

---

You and your eidolon move together. You each use a single action to Stride. Either of you that has the corresponding movement type can [[Actions/Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim]] instead.
