# Tidal Wave three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undersea Privateer Dedication]]

---

Like the ocean in a storm, you overwhelm your opponents with speed and force. Move up to your Speed and make two Strikes. These Strikes can happen at any point during your movement. If either Strike reduces a target to 0 Hit Points, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition for 1 round. You can only use the extra action to Stride or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]].
