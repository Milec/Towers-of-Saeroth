# Ghost Hunter Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

---

You have focused your training on hunting and defeating ghosts, spirits, and all manner of haunts. Pick [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Spirit Lore]] or Haunt Lore; you become trained in this skill. If you were already trained in both skills, you become trained in a new Lore skill of your choice.

 Choose two cantrips from the occult spell list. Each cantrip must have the [[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], or [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] trait. You can cast these spells as innate occult spells. You gain access to the Cast a Spell activity if you didn't have it already. You're trained in occult spell attack rolls and spell DCs. You can usually replace material components with somatic components, so you don't need a spell component pouch. Your key spellcasting ability for these spells is Charisma.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the ghost hunter archetype.
