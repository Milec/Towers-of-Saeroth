# Invigorating Breath single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 241

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scions of Domora]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scion of Domora Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scion of Domora Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are adjacent to your spirit guide or it is sharing your space.

---

Your spirit guide exhales its sweet-smelling breath on you, granting you the strength to push through your pain. You gain 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] for 10 minutes. At 15th level, you instead gain 30 temporary Hit Points for 10 minutes.
