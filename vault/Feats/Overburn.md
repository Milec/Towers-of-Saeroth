# Overburn  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

---

You engage your core, giving yourself more energy and speed for a fight. You gain [[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] 3rd-rank spell that you can cast once per day. You can target only yourself with this spell.

**Enhancement** You instead can cast _haste_ as a 7th-rank innate arcane spell once per day. You must be one of the targets of the spell.
