# Barreling Charge two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Fighter (Player Core)|Fighter]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 143, [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 78

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Wylderheart]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You rush forward, moving enemies aside to reach your foe. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], attempting to move through your enemies’ spaces. Roll an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check and compare the result to the Fortitude DC of each creature whose space you attempt to move through during your Stride, moving through its space on a success but ending your movement before entering its space on a failure. You can then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], regardless of how your Stride ended. You can use Barreling Charge to [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type.
