# Scout's Warning free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]], [[Traits/Rogue (Player Core)|Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 159

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]] (Level 8)

**Trigger**
You are about to roll a Perception or Survival check for initiative

---

You visually or audibly warn your allies of danger, granting them each a +1 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls, or a +2 circumstance bonus if you're using the Scout exploration activity. Depending on whether you use gestures or call out, this action gains either the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] or the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait, respectively.
