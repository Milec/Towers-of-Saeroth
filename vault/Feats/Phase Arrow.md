# Phase Arrow single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 173

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Elven Arrows|Eldritch Archer]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

You can concentrate an immense amount of magic to create a piece of ammunition that phases through everything but your target. Make a bow Strike against a foe who is [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to you (but not [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]]). The ammunition travels to your target in a straight line, passing through any non-magical barriers or walls in its way, though magical barriers stop the arrow. The shot ignores all cover, the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition, the [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] condition, and circumstance bonuses to AC from shields. It has a +4 status bonus to hit creatures wearing any type of armor. The Strike's damage can't be reduced with a [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction using a non-magical shield.
