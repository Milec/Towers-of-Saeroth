# Unfettered Halfling  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 60

---

You were either pressed into indentured servitude or shackled in a prison, but you've since escaped and have trained to ensure you'll never be caught again. Whenever you roll a success on a check to [[Actions/Escape]] or a saving throw against an effect that would impose the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] condition on you, you get a critical success instead. Whenever a creature rolls a failure on a check to [[Actions/Grapple]] you, it gets a critical failure instead.
