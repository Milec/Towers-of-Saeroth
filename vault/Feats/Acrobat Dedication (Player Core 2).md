# Acrobat Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]. At 7th level, you become a master in Acrobatics, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Acrobatics. Whenever you critically succeed at an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] an enemy's space, you don't treat the enemy's space as difficult terrain.
