# You're So Cute!  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 26

---

You know how to put on the charm, doing playful tricks to win favor. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] and gain the [[Feats/Impressive Performance (Player Core)|Impressive Performance]] feat. When performing for [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoids]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] with Performance.
