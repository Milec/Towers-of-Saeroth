# Inventor Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 49

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Inventor]]

**Prerequisites**
Intelligence 14

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] and inventor class DC. You gain the [[Feats/Inventor (Player Core)|Inventor]] skill feat, even if you don’t meet the skill feat’s requirements. Choose an innovation. You gain that innovation, though you don’t gain any other abilities that modify or use that innovation, such as modifications or Explode.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have first gained two other feats from the inventor archetype.
