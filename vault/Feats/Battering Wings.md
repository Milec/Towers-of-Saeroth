# Battering Wings  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Winged Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Winged Warrior Dedication]]

---

Though not powerful enough for flight, your wings can deliver a thrashing to anyone who gets close. You gain a wing unarmed attack that deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage. Your wing attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.
