# Vomit Stomach ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 38

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition or fail a saving throw against an [[Traits/Ingested (GM Core)|ingested]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]].

---

In dire circumstances you can vomit out your stomach to expel toxins. Reduce your sickened condition by 2 and immediately attempt a saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus against any ingested poisons you have been exposed to within the last minute. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round as your exposed stomach makes you especially vulnerable to attacks.
