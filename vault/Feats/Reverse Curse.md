# Reverse Curse  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 169

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Manifestations|Curse Maelstrom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Counter Curse]]

---

When you counter a [[Traits/Curse|curse]] or [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect, you send it back to destroy its originator. Whenever you succeed at the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check to Counter Curse, the creature is affected by its own curse or misfortune effect, attempting a saving throw against its own DC if the effect allows a saving throw. In the case of a constant ability, such as a misfortune aura, the creature is affected only until the beginning of its next turn, when the ability reasserts itself.
