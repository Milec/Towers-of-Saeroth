# Golden Erinys Stance single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 109

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sister of the Golden Erinys]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sister of the Golden Erinys Dedication]]

---

Whether you're a keen student of vengeance or you picked up techniques after being a common target for the sisters' punishment, you've learned to dig painfully into your opponent's flesh. In this stance, you can make fury's fang unarmed attacks. These deal 1d6 piercing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

 While in the stance, if you critically hit with a melee Strike that deals piercing damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (with a DC to remove equal to your class DC); this is in addition to any critical specialization effect you might gain from the attack.
