# Called  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 77

---

You’ve heard the Call, and by resisting it, you’ve developed a bulwark against effects that target your mind. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saving throws against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. If you roll a success on your saving throw against a mental effect that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]], you get a critical success instead.
