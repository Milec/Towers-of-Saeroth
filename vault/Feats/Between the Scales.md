# Between the Scales  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 15

---

Underestimating you is a grave mistake, but it's one others keep making. You've learned to take advantage of slips in your foes' defenses. When you Strike a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature using a melee weapon or unarmed attack that has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits, it gains the [[Traits/Backstabber|backstabber]] trait.
