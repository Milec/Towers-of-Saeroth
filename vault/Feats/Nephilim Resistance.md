# Nephilim Resistance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

---

You resist energy like your extraplanar predecessors. Choose one of the following energy damage types: [[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]. You gain [[Rules/Resistance|resistance]] 5 to that damage type. The damage type typically matches an extraplanar entity associated with your bloodline. For instance, an [[Feats/Angelkin|angelkin]] might choose resistance to cold, while a [[Feats/Hellspawn|hellspawn]] might choose resistance to fire.
