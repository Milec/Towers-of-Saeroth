# Battle Harbinger Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Class]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 274

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Battle Harbinger]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Doctrines/Battle Creed|battle creed]]

---

You have trained extensively in combat, battlefield tactics, and stamina, focusing on being an exceptional warrior for your faith in exchange for less time studying the traditional spells and scriptures. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] or [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], if you are already trained in both skills, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Toughness (Player Core)|Toughness]] general feat. If you already have this feat, you gain another general feat of your choice.
