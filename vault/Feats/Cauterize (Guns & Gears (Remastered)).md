# Cauterize single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 113

**Trigger**
You're wielding a loaded firearm and you or an adjacent ally are taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

---

You use the smoking barrel of your firearm to sear shut a bleeding wound. Make a Strike with your firearm. You then press the heated barrel to the wounds of you or an ally within reach that is taking persistent bleed damage, giving an immediate flat check to end the bleed with the lower DC for [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|particularly effective assistance]].
