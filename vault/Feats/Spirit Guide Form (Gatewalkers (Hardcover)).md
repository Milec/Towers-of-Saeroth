# Spirit Guide Form two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 241

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scions of Domora]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scion of Domora Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Scion of Domora Dedication]]

---

You fuse with your spirit guide, becoming a hybrid being with enhanced physical and spiritual power. This effect lasts 1 minute; you can end it early as a free action. During this time, you can’t use any other actions granted by this archetype. You gain the following statistics and abilities, regardless of your type of spirit guide.

- You become [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]].
- You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]], or [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]; double resistance vs. non-magical).
- Darkvision.
- An unarmed melee attack of the same type as your spirit guide (either claws or jaws), which is the only attack you can Strike with. You’re trained with this attack. Your attack modifier is +29 or your normal unarmed attack modifier, whichever is higher. You use the listed statistics for the unarmed attack type:
- **Claws** ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]) , **Damage** 3d6+5 slashing plus 1d6 force;
- **Jaws** ([[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]]) Damage 3d10+5 piercing plus 1d6 force.
