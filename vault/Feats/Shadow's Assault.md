# Shadow's Assault  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fetchling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 87

---

Your shadow assaults your foes. You can cast [[Spells/Shadow Blast|_shadow blast_]] as a 5th-level occult innate spell once per day. At 17th level, your _shadow blast_ is heightened to 6th level.
