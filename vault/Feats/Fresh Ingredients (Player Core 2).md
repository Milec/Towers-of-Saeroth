# Fresh Ingredients  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Herbalist Dedication]]

---

It is amazing the difference fresh herbs can make. When using [[Feats/Natural Medicine (Player Core)|Natural Medicine]] to [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], you gain the +2 circumstance bonus from having fresh ingredients, even if not in wilderness. If you do this in the wilderness, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus instead.
