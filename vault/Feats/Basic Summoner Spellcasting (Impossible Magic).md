# Basic Summoner Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 91

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Summoner Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Summoner Dedication]]

---

You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Player Core)|basic spellcasting benefits]]. You gain the [Cast a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) activity. Your key spellcasting attribute for summoner archetype spells is Charisma, and they are summoner spells of your eidolon’s tradition. You become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. You gain a spell repertoire and two cantrips. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the summoner archetype, add a spell of the appropriate spell rank to your repertoire.
