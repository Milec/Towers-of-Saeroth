# Invisible Ink  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

---

After your rune is drawn, it flares once and then fades, leaving only the barest mark. When you apply a rune on an object or creature, you can [[Actions/Conceal an Object (Player Core)|Conceal an Object]] as a free action to hide that rune. If a creature would get a chance to find the rune, the GM uses your [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] DC instead of your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] DC.
