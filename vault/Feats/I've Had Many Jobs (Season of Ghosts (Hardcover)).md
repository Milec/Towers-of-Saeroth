# I've Had Many Jobs  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 319

---

Finding and excelling at odd jobs is child’s play to one who has worked many occupations during many lives. When you attempt to [[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]], you function as if you were trained in the skill used. If you’re at least an expert in the skill used, the DC for your skill check is reduced by 2.
