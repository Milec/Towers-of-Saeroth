# Distant Waterbird's Poise ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Aloof Firmament|aloof firmament hybrid study]]; [[Actions/Spellstrike]]

**Trigger**
You hit a target with a Spellstrike.

---

You flow around your foe, using the magic of your attack to carry you away. You [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], and this movement doesn’t trigger reactions from the creature you hit. If you High Jump or Long Jump, you don’t have to perform the initial Stride (nor do you fall if you don’t Stride 10 feet).

The lightness of your steps persists after the jump, granting you the ability to land on or walk on the surface of water or other liquids until the end of your next turn.
