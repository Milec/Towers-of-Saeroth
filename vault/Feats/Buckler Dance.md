# Buckler Dance single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 91

**Requirements**
You are wielding a [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler|buckler]].

---

You spin your buckler defensively toward every attack. While you are in this stance, you constantly have your buckler raised as if you'd used [[Actions/Raise a Shield]], as long as you meet that action's requirements.
