# Searing Restoration single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 27

---

They told you there was no way that explosions could heal people, but they were fools… Fools who didn't understand your brilliance! You create a minor explosion from your innovation, altering the combustion to cauterize wounds using vaporized medicinal herbs. You or a living creature adjacent to you regains 1d10 Hit Points. In addition, the creature you heal can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]], with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

 At 3rd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, increase the healing by 1d10.

**Special** If your innovation is a [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]], it can take this action rather than you, though because it's not a living creature, it can't use the ability on itself.
