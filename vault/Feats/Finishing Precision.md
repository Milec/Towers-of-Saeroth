# Finishing Precision  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 153

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swashbuckler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Swashbuckler Dedication]]

---

You've learned how to land daring blows when you have panache. You gain the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-361)|precise strike class feature]] but you deal 1 additional damage on a hit and 1d6 damage on a finisher. This damage doesn't increase as you gain levels. In addition, you gain the Basic Finisher action.

 **Basic Finisher** single action ([[Traits/Finisher]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]) You make a graceful, deadly attack. Make a Strike; if you hit and your weapon qualifies for precise strike, you deal the full 1d6 damage from precise strike.
