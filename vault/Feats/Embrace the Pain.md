# Embrace the Pain ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 111

**Trigger**
A creature damages you with a melee attack.

---

Ignoring your pain, you reach out and grab the creature or weapon that caused you harm. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple]] the triggering creature or to [[Actions/Disarm]] the creature of the triggering weapon.
