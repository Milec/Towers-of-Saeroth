# Adroit Manipulation  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 44

**Access**
Mihrini ethnicity

---

You’ve fiddled with knots, locks, and blacksmith’s puzzles since you were a child to give your hands something to do. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (or another skill of your choice, if you’re already trained in Thievery). If you roll a success on a Thievery check to Pick a Lock, you get a critical success instead.
