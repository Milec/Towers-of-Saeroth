# Safe House  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 196

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) A vigilante's Safe House, can be located in any city or near any lodge they have received a Pathfinder briefing. A vigilante can use their downtime to relocate their safe house. A vigilante can use their safe house only if it is in the town or location where their current adventure is taking place._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Vigilante]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]

---

You establish a safe house—a secure space in which to hide your secrets from the outside world. This safe house is roughly the size of a 10-foot cube. It's in a location you have access to, and it can be part of a larger building or structure, like a hidden room or an underground cave. The safe house protects objects and people inside it from magical detection. This has the effects of [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]], using your [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC and half your level rounded up for the counteract level. Setting up or moving your safe house takes a week of downtime. The size of the safe house expands to four 10-foot cubes if you're an expert in Deception, eight cubes if you're a master, and 16 cubes if you're legendary.
