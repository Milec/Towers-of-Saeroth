# Twin Eidolon  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

---

You can transform to match your eidolon. When you [[Actions/Manifest Eidolon|Manifest your Eidolon]], you can call upon their powers to transform yourself into a duplicate until your eidolon unmanifests. You can’t use this if you Manifest your Eidolon in an unusual way, such as [[Feats/Meld into Eidolon]]. While using Twin Eidolon, you use your eidolon’s statistics other than your own Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma attribute modifiers, including their evolutions. You can’t cast spells (except spells your eidolon could cast), Activate or otherwise benefit from magic items that normally benefit you and not your eidolon, or use other actions they can’t perform. Any of their spells you cast with limited uses count against your eidolon’s uses. You can Manifest your Eidolon again to end this effect.

**Special** This feat has the trait matching your eidolon’s tradition ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]).
