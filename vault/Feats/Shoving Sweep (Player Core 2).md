# Shoving Sweep ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mauler (Player Core 2)|Mauler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Mauler Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
An enemy within your reach leaves a square during a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action it’s using.

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You swing your weapon at your foe, rebuffing them back. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] the triggering creature, ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. Unless you critically succeed at your check, the creature continues its movement after the Shove.
