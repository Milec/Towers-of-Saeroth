# Grave Mummification  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 52

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

Exposure to the elements, magic, alchemy, and other phenomena intensified the changes from your mummification. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]].

 In addition, you gain resistance equal to half your level against any damage you'd take from your bound terrain, including its [[Rules/Temperature|dangerous temperatures, weather, hazardous terrain, and environmental hazards]].
