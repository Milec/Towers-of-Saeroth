# Primal Rampage  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 59

---

You tap into the unstoppable, primeval strength of your ancient kin. You gain [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]] and [[Spells/Stoneskin|_stoneskin_]] as 4th-level primal innate spells that you can cast once per day. As a 3-action activity, you can Cast a Spell twice to cast both of these innate spells, as long as they are both still available for the day.
