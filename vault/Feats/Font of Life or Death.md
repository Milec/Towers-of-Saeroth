**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

---

As you reach ever higher in your quest to achieve true divinity, the powers of life and death flow evermore easily from you. Choose either [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] or [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]]; you gain the chosen spell as an 8th rank [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] you can cast three times per day. Your hierophant can cast these innate spells as well by expending one of your daily uses.
