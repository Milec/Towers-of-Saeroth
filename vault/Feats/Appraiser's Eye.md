# Appraiser's Eye  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

---

You have experience handling and evaluating magical items, which are common in merfolk society. You can cast the [[Spells/Read Aura (Player Core)|_read aura_]] cantrip as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You also gain the [[Feats/Quick Identification (Player Core)|Quick Identification]] skill feat, even if you don’t meet the prerequisites.
