# Deimatic Display single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 124

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

Imitating animal threat displays, you make yourself appear larger and more imposing. Roll one [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] and compare the result to the Will DC of each [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Fungus|fungus]], and [[Traits/Plant|plant]] creature in a 15-foot cone. When you do, Demoralize gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait, and creatures are affected if they can see you. You don't take a penalty if the creature doesn't understand your language.
