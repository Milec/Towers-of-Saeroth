# Expansive Spellstrike  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 17

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

---

You can blast the energy of a spell out around the target of your attack. When you [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a spell that has an area of a [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], or [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], the spell creates an area instead of affecting only the target. The target must be in the area, and Expansive Spellstrike has no effect if your spell was disrupted (such as if you critically fail your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]). A burst is centered on any corner you choose of the target’s square (or the corner of any square in its space for a Large or larger creature). A cone or line emits from you and must include the target; if you’re not adjacent to the target (using a [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] weapon or [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Starlit Span|starlit span]], for example), choose any square adjacent to the target as the source. The spell affects all creatures in the area as normal, but the Strike still targets only one creature.
