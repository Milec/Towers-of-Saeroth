# Certain Strategem  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 107

---

You know exactly where to strike a foe to target their weak spots. When you make a Strike that you're adding your Intelligence modifier to due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devising a Stratagem]] and miss, you deal half of your strategic strike damage to the target. This damage type is that of the weapon or unarmed attack you used for the Strike.
