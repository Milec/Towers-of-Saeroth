# Phantom Charm free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]], [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 45

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Ghost Bull Minotaur]] heritage

**Trigger**
You attempt a check that would be subject to a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect.

---

You can harness the spiritual connection in your blood, allowing you to shift the negative threads of fate. You negate the misfortune effect and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the triggering check.
