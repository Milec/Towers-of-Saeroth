# Perfect Form Control  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 139

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Form Control]]; Strength 18

---

Thanks to magic and muscle memory, you can stay in your alternate forms indefinitely; you may have even forgotten your original form. When you use Form Control, instead of lasting 1 hour, [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] is permanent until you Dismiss it.
