# Vicious Venom single action  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 63

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Envenom]]

---

The venom you produce is exceptionally potent and difficult to overcome. Your vishkanyan venom gains the [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] trait, requiring two consecutive saves to reduce the venom's stage by 1. A critical success reduces your venom's stage by only 1 instead of by 2.
