# Smoke Sight  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 201

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

You’re used to fighting and hiding in a haze of smoke and flame. You automatically succeed at the DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by smoke. When you are concealed or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] in smoke, increase the DC of the flat check to target you to 6 if you’re concealed or 12 if you’re hidden.
