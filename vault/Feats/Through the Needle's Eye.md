# Through the Needle's Eye  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 36

---

Your weapon strikes with the perfection your will demands.  
**Transcendence—Blinding of the Needle** two actions (transcendence) You aim your weapon in a superficial cut above your opponent’s eye. Make a Strike with the imbued ikon. If that Strike is successful, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round or until it uses an Interact action to clear the blood from its vision.
