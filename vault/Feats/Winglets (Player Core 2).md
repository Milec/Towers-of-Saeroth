# Winglets  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 22

---

Through the lingering influence of your warren's patron, you've sprouted a small set of wings. While not suitable for full flight, these weak wings can help you jump further with a small flap. When [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leaping]] horizontally, you move an additional 5 feet. This additional distance isn't cumulative with the increased Leap distance from the [[Feats/Powerful Leap (Player Core)|Powerful Leap]] feat. In addition, when you attempt a [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], you can jump a distance up to 10 feet farther than you normally would based on the result of your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check, though you still can't jump farther than your Speed. You don't automatically fail your checks to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or Long Jump if you don't Stride at least 10 feet first.
