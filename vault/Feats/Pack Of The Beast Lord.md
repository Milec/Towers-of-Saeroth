# Pack Of The Beast Lord three actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Beast Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beast Lord Dedication]]

---

You and your united [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]] call upon the service of others of its kind, creating figment duplicates of your companion to form a massive swarm of formidable power. Spend a Mythic Point. Your united companion becomes Gargantuan for 1 minute. It gains [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunity]] to precision damage, [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half your level, and a [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to area damage equal to half your level. While in this form, your united companion gains the Amassed Assault action, which you immediately Command them to use as the final step of this activation.

**Amassed Assault** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]]) Each enemy in your united companion's space takes damage equal to one of its unarmed Strikes plus your level ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher).
