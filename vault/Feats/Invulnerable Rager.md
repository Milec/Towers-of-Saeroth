# Invulnerable Rager  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 81

---

Even the heaviest armors serve only to enhance your unbridled fury. You are trained in heavy armor. Whenever you gain a barbarian class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in medium armor, you also gain that proficiency in heavy armor. If you have the [[Actions/Quick-Tempered]] action, you can use it while wearing heavy armor.
