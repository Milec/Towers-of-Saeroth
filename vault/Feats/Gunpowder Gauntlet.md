# Gunpowder Gauntlet single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 136

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing A Spotter|Pistol Phenom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pistol Phenom Dedication]]

---

Your flair keeps foes’ attention, as your motions with your gun draw your foes into a deadly game. As you toy with them and performatively brandish your weapon, you build their tunnel vision, leaving them trapped in a perceptual gauntlet of your own making. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check against the Will DC of a single target within your one-handed firearm’s first range increment.

**Critical Success** The target takes a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you until the beginning of your next turn.  

**Success** The target takes a –1 status penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you until the beginning of your next turn.
