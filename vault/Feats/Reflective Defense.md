# Reflective Defense ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Talos]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 52

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
A creature within 30 feet of you targets you, and you can see the attacker.

**Requirements**
You are in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim]] or [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]].

---

Your body's natural luster has been polished to a gleaming shine. You use this to reflect light back into your enemy's eyes, disrupting its aim and focus; it must succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.
