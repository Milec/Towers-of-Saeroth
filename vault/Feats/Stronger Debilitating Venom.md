# Stronger Debilitating Venom  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 62

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Debilitating Venom]]; [[Feats/Moderate Enhance Venom]]

---

Add one of the following to the debilitations from which you can choose when you [[Actions/Envenom]]. These debilitations reduce the damage of all stages as noted.

- **Bungling** (level 13) Reduce damage by 1 die; **Stage 1** no effect; **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]]; **Stage 3** clumsy 2
- **Devitalizing** (level 13) Reduce damage by 3 dice; **Stage 1** no effect; **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]; **Stage 3** drained 2
- **Nauseating** (level 13) Reduce damage by 2 dice; **Stage 1** no effect; **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]]; **Stage 3** sickened 2
- **Weakening** (level 13) Reduce damage by 1 die; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]]; **Stage 2** enfeebled 1; **Stage 3** enfeebled 2

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different debilitation each time.
