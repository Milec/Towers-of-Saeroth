# Medical Researcher  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 72

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

Kassi has taught you not only how to apply medicines with your medical skills but how to craft them as well. When you [[Actions/Craft]] [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's kits]], [vaccines](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=773), [[Equipment/Addiction Suppressant|addiction suppressants]], [[Equipment/Antidote|antidotes]], [[Equipment/Antiplague|antiplagues]], [[Equipment/Elixir of Life|elixirs of life]], or other non-magical medical or healing items, you can use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]].

## Kassi's Techniques

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 71

While Kassi shares her greatest discoveries with the entire medical community, some of her techniques are either still experimental or extremely difficult to perform. Any students who Kassi takes on gain access to her techniques.
