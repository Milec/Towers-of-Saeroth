# Vishkanya Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 63

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vishkanya Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Extensive training has granted you an elegant control in the use of vishkanyan weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowgun]], [fighting fan](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=93), [[Equipment/Weapons/Kris|kris]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]], and all [[Traits/Vishkanya|vishkanyan]] weapons in which you are trained.
