# Safeguard Soul  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Soul Cycle|Soul Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Soul Warden Dedication]]

---

You've girded your soul against outside interference. You gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Possession|possession]] effects, and effects that attempt to manipulate or steal your soul. You can't be transformed into an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] by any means. While your spiral is glowing, your allies in the light of the spiral gain this benefit as well.
