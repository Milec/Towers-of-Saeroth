# Rivethun Devotion  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 293

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Emissary]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Emissary Dedication]]

**Access**
Followers of Rivethun have access to this feat.

---

Through study, dedication, and introspection, you've learned to wield magic that affects the spirit. You gain [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]] and [[Spells/Spirit Link (Player Core)|_spirit link_]] as 2nd-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day. If you're a master in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], _spirit link_ is heightened to 3rd rank. If you're legendary in Religion, _spirit link_ is heightened to 4th rank.
