# Pinch Time  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Half-Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 47

---

One of your parents has a human life span and another an elven life span, with your own somewhere between. As a result, you have an unusual perspective on time, which you've learned to manifest to aid yourself in moments of stress. You gain [[Spells/Haste|_haste_]] as a 3rd-level arcane innate spell, though you can target only yourself. You can Cast this Spell once per day.
