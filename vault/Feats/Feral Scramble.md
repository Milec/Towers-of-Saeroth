# Feral Scramble two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; your animal shape grants a claw unarmed attack

**Requirements**
You’re in hybrid or animal shape.

---

Sinking your claws in, you propel yourself at shocking speed. You [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] up to your land Speed and make a claw Strike at any point during that movement. After you finish the movement, attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the surface's Climb DC.

**Success** You maintain your position.  

**Failure** You slide down 60 feet or to the ground without taking falling damage.
