# Seven Changes Performance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 50

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

---

While kitsune are known for their magical [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] powers, you’re skilled at stage magic, especially costuming and quick changes. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Disguise (Player Core)|Quick Disguise]] feat. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] in place of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] for the purposes of [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonating]] someone.
