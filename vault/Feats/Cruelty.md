# Cruelty single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 119

**Prerequisites**
devotion spell ([[Spells/Touch of Corruption|_touch of corruption_]])

---

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. If your next action is to cast _touch of corruption_ on a living creature, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute if it fails its save (enfeebled 2 if it critically fails).
