# Daywalker  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]]

[[Feats/Vampire Dedication]]

---

Through a profane pledge or a bloodline quirk, you can tolerate the sun's light. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]] and can't be destroyed by sunlight. This doesn't prevent you from becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] by exposure to the sun.
