# Moon May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 22

---

Your mother was a moon hag, a handmaiden of [[Setting/Deities/Groetus]], and you have a single scarlet eye and a sliver of her supernal foresight. You can cast [[Spells/Guidance|_guidance_]] as an occult innate cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
