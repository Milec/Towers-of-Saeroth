# Meld Into Eidolon three actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 78

---

Your physical form can combine with that of your eidolon. You Manifest your Eidolon, but instead of summoning them into an adjacent open space, you become them. You can also Meld into Eidolon while your eidolon is manifested in an adjacent space. While Manifested in this way, you use your eidolon’s statistics, and you can’t act except to direct the eidolon to use Manifest an Eidolon to unmanifest themself. Your eidolon is affected by [[Spells/Boost Eidolon|_boost eidolon_]] while Manifested in this way.

Since you can’t act, you can’t cast spells, activate or benefit from magic items that normally benefit you and not your eidolon, perform actions that have the [[Traits/Tandem|tandem]] trait, or use other abilities that require you, and not the eidolon, to act. You can’t be separately targeted while you are melded into the eidolon. When you reach 0 HP, your eidolon unmanifests, leaving your body behind, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]].
