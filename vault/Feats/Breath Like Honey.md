# Breath Like Honey  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnoll]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Sweetbreath Gnoll]] heritage

---

You smell of honey and savory things. You can cast [[Spells/Enthrall|_enthrall_]] as a 3rd-level occult innate spell once per day, except the spell has a range of 30 feet and the [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait. Targets don't gain any circumstance bonus for disagreeing with you. Your circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] if the target can smell your breath increases to +2.
