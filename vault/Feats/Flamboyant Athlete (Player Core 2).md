# Flamboyant Athlete  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 165

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

Your panache allows you to perform incredible feats: climbing, swimming, and leaping far beyond your normal capacity. While you have panache, you gain the following benefits.

- You gain climb and swim Speeds equal to half your land Speed.
- The DCs of your [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jumps]] and [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jumps]] decrease by 10. This doesn't combine with other abilities that reduce those DCs.
- The distance you can move with a vertical [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] increases to 5 feet. Your distance for a horizontal Leap increases to 15 feet if your Speed is at least 15 feet, or to 20 feet if your Speed is at least 30 feet.
