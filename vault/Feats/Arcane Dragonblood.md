# Arcane Dragonblood  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]], [[Traits/Lineage (Player Core)|Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 45

---

You descend from a dragon that wields mastery of their magical abilities, such as a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Fortune|fortune dragon]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Mirage|mirage dragon]]. As such, you can instinctively grasp the intricacies of magic. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]]. If you would automatically become trained in Arcana (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Arcane Sense (Player Core)|Arcane Sense]] skill feat. If you choose a draconic exemplar, you must choose an arcane dragon.
