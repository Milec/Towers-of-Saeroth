# Fiendish Form  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 277

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|[Champion]]] [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]

---

Your features reshape into those of a type of [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] who serves your [[Rules/Deity (Player Core)|deity]]; for example, as a [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demon]] you might have putrid scales, twisted horns, and red eyes. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] if you don’t already have it, and you gain the fiend trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor you’ve become (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Daemon (Monster Core)|daemon]], demon, or [[Bestiary/Families/Devil (Monster Core)|devil]]).
