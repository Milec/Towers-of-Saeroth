# Leaping Hedgehog Strike two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

---

You pounce toward an enemy and then barrel them over with one fluid movement, ending this maneuver with an attack that may catch them off-balance. [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] horizontally toward an enemy you can see; treat your Speed as 30 feet or more for this Leap if it is less than 30 feet. If you end this movement adjacent to that enemy, attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] that enemy’s space. On a success, the target is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. You then Strike that same enemy. If the target has been knocked prone, you can spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] as part of this Strike to make it at mythic proficiency.
