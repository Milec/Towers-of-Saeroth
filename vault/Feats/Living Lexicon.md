# Living Lexicon  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 53

---

Your knowledge of runes knows no bounds. You add an [additional rune](RunesmithRunes.aspx) to your runic repertoire of any level. Additionally, each day during your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], you can swap any one rune in your runic repertoire with another rune of the same level.
