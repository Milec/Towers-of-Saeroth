# The Taste of Magic  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 96

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

---

Each working of magic has a unique scent and frequency of vibration. You have trained yourself to recognize the minute differences in magics, as if distinguishing between different odors. You gain [[Rules/Scent (NPC Core)|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet. This scent can only detect creatures, items, and effects that are currently under the effect of a spell or have the [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] trait. In addition, you may use [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] creatures you could sense with this ability.
