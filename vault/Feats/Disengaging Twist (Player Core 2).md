# Disengaging Twist ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 222

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Wrestler Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature gives you the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] condition.

---

Your ability to twist your opponents’ bodies into painful locks and holds makes you particularly adept at escaping such predicaments. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] the triggering condition. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check.
