# Tweak Appearances  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 73

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You spend 1 minute making quick adjustments to someone’s clothing and appearance, causing their words to carry more weight with an audience of your choice. While speaking to that audience, the target gains a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks. If you have master proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], the bonus increases to +2; if you have legendary proficiency in Crafting, the bonus increases to +3. This bonus lasts until this ability is used on the target again, the situation they were prepared for ends, or they next change their outfit.

## Lacunafex Lore

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 72

If the PCs earn Mialari Docur's trust and prove their worth by defeating the Scarlet Triad, she rewards them by granting them access to several feats and spells she and her students have developed over the years.
