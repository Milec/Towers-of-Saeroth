# Undead Hunter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Newly Minted Automaton|newly minted automaton heritage]]

---

You were created by a Nexian mage specifically for the purpose of fighting [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], and you are infused with [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] magic. You gain [[Spells/Vitality Lash|_vitality lash_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] cantrip.

**Enhancement** You can infuse your attacks with vitality. You can cast [[Spells/Infuse Vitality|_infuse vitality_]] as a 3rd-rank arcane innate spell once per day, targeting only yourself.
