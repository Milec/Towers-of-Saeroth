# Jotun's Transposition  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 15

---

You’ve learned to fully move across planes, beginning with the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]]. You can cast [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] twice per week as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that can target only yourself. Your body serves as the locus for the spell and allows you to travel between the Ethereal Plane, [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], or the space between the planes known as the Fray without needing a planar key. You can use these castings to travel to other planes if you have the appropriate planar keys, as normal.
