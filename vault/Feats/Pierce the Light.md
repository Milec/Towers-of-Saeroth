# Pierce the Light  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fetchling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 87

---

You tear the veil between the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]] and its shadow. You can cast [[Spells/Plane Shift|_plane shift_]] twice per week as an occult innate spell that can only target yourself; you can travel only to the Material or [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane|Shadow Planes]], and your body serves as the focus component.
