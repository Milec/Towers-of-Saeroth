# Set-up Strike single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 31

---

Your attack makes it difficult for your enemy to defend themselves against your allies' attacks. Attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an enemy. If the Strike is successful, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off guard]] against the next attack that one of your allies attempts against it before the start of your next turn.
