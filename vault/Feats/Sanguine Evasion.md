# Sanguine Evasion ⤾  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

**Trigger**
You’re reduced to 0 Hit Points and are not already [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]].

---

You avoid death by turning into a spray of red mist. You remain at 1 Hit Point and transform into a vaporous state with the effects of [[Spells/Vapor Form|_vapor form_]], except your [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] is equal to your land Speed and you can occupy the same space as another creature. Creatures in the same space as you in this form are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]].

You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this form, returning to your normal form with 1 Hit Point and becoming wounded 1. If you don’t Dismiss this form within 1 minute, you return to your normal form with 0 Hit Points and become [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] 1.
