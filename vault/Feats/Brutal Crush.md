# Brutal Crush single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 56

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal|animal instinct]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Untamed|untamed order]]

**Requirements**
Your last action dealt bludgeoning damage using an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] Strike granted by a [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|morph]] or [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect.

---

You've learned how to cloud your foes' minds with the brutal impact of your repeated attacks. Make an unarmed Strike against the same target. If the Strike hits and deals bludgeoning damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]] for 1 round (stupefied 3 on a critical hit).
