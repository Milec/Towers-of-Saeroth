# Alabaster Eyes  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 60

---

In place of the normal vishkanyan golden eyes, you were born with especially perceptive snow-white eyes. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain out of this feat or into this feat.
