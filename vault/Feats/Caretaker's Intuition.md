# Caretaker's Intuition  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 13

---

Your forebears had a deep understanding of the natural world around them, and you’ve learned some of their knowledge and techniques. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in Nature (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

Additionally, while outdoors, you can spend 10 minutes to read the sky and determine upcoming weather. You get a general impression of weather up to 8 hours in advance. You can use this to determine that clear skies or a rainstorm are ahead, but you can’t determine specifics like wind speeds and direction, or that a tornado would be occurring later.
