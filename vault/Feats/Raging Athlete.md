# Raging Athlete  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 89

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Physical obstacles can't hold back your fury. While you are raging, you gain a climb Speed and swim Speed equal to your land Speed and the DC of [[Actions/High Jump|High Jumps]] and [[Actions/Long Jump|Long Jumps]] decreases by 10. Your distance for a vertical [[Actions/Leap]] increases to 5 feet vertically, and your distance for a horizontal Leap increases to 15 feet if your Speed is at least 15 feet and to 20 feet if your Speed is at least 30 feet.
