# Guarded Mind free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 172

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ulfen Guard]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ulfen Guard Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail a saving throw against an effect that has the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait.

---

When your enemies try to turn your mind against you, thoughts of your anathema bolster you. You can reroll the triggering saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus, but you must use the new result, even if it’s worse.
