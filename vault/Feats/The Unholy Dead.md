# The Unholy Dead  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 32

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This feat makes the PC Unholy which is not allowed in PFS Play._

**Prerequisites**
You aren’t [[Traits/Holy|holy]].

---

You have turned your necromancy against the divine powers that seek to stop you from achieving your unholy goals. You and your thralls gain the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait. [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] your thralls make gain the unholy trait and deal 1 additional [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. This additional spirit damage increases to 2 at 10th level. You also gain the following anathema: “destroy undead of no harm to you.”
