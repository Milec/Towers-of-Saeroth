# Pantheon Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

The touch of divine meddling still runs through you, whether you want it or not. Choose one [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spell list. You can cast this cantrip as a divine innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
