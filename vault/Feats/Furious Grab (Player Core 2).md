# Furious Grab single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 82

**Requirements**
Your last action was a successful [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], and either you have a hand free or your Strike used a weapon with the [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple trait]].

---

You grab your foe while it's distracted by your attack. The enemy you hit becomes grabbed by you, as if you had succeeded at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] it.
