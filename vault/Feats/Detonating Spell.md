# Detonating Spell single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 83

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

Your spell becomes volatile and explosive. If the next action you use is to Cast a Spell that deals damage to a single target and the spell successfully damages that target, the spell explodes, dealing [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage equal to the level of the spell cast to adjacent creatures. Unlike normally, this splash damage doesn't apply to the target. The splash damage dealt is of the same type the spell deals.
