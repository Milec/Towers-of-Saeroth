# Acrobat Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 155

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]

---

You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]. At 7th level, you become a master in Acrobatics, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Acrobatics. Whenever you critically succeed at an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] an enemy's space, you don't treat the enemy's space as difficult terrain.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the acrobat archetype.
