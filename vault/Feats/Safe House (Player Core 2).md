# Safe House  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Vigilante (Player Core 2)|Vigilante]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication (Player Core 2)|Vigilante Dedication]]

---

You establish a safe house—a secure space in which to hide your secrets from the outside world. This safe house is roughly the size of two 10-foot cubes. It's in a location you have access to, and it can be part of a larger building or structure, like a hidden room or an underground cave. The safe house protects objects and people inside it from magical detection. This has the effects of [[Spells/Veil of Privacy|_veil of privacy_]], using your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC and half your level rounded up for the counteract rank. Setting up or moving your safe house takes a week of downtime. The size of the safe house expands to four 10-foot cubes if you're an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], eight cubes if you're a master, and 16 cubes if you're legendary.
