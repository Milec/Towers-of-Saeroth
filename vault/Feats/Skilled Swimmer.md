# Skilled Swimmer  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 20

---

You move through the water with ease, mastering your movements with years of practice. When in an aquatic environment, you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. In addition, swimming up or down isn't difficult terrain for you.
