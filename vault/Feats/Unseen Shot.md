Your attacks are deadly, silent, and nearly untraceable. The first time each round that you attempt to Strike a target you are undetected or unnoticed by while using a weapon you have familiarity with from this archetype, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from the target after the attack, as they cannot tell where the attack came from.
