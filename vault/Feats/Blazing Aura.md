# Blazing Aura ⤾  *Feat 17*

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**

---

You explode in flame. Enemies in a 20-foot emanation take 7d6 fire damage (basic Reflex save using your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher). Allies in the area are [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 round and can use the additional action to Strike or Stride.
