# Rallying Charge two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

Your fearless charge into battle reinvigorates your allies to carry on the fight. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your Speed and make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If your Strike hits and damages an enemy, each ally within 60 feet who saw you hit gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Charisma modifier. These temporary Hit Points last until the start of your next turn.
