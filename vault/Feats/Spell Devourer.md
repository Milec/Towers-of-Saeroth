# Spell Devourer  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 19

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The temporary Hit Points granted by the Spell Devourer orc ancestry feat are applied as soon as the character succeeds at their saving throw; for an effect that causes Hit Point damage on a successful save, such as a fireball, this means that the character gains the temporary Hit Points before taking damage._

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pervasive Superstition]]

---

You don't just resist magic; you devour it. Whenever you succeed at a saving throw against a spell or magical effect, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to double the spell's level, or equal to the level if the magical effect isn't a spell. These temporary Hit Points last until the end of your next turn.
