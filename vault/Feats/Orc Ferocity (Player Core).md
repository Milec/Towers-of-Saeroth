# Orc Ferocity ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Trigger**

Fierceness in battle runs through your blood, and you refuse to fall from your injuries no matter how terrible they may be. You avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point, and your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition increases by 1.
