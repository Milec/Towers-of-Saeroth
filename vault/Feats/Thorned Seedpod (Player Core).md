# Thorned Seedpod  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 69

When your seedpods [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|strike]] a felling blow, they burst into viciously thorny vines. When you critically succeed at a seedpod Strike, vines twist and stab your foe, dealing 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent]] piercing damage.
