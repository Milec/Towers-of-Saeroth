With a rousing call, you exhort an ally to advance. If your next action is to cast the [[Spells/Courageous Anthem|_courageous anthem_]] composition cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]].
