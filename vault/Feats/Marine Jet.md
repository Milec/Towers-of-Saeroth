# Marine Jet two actions or three actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marine Marauder Dedication]]; you have a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]]

---

You shoot through the water at a great speed. [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to five times, or up to eight times if you used 3 actions. All this movement must be in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] (though the GM might allow reasonable variance). If you are moving up or down in this way, you aren’t considered to be moving through [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] while doing so.
