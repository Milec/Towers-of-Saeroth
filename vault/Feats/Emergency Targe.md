# Emergency Targe ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 44

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Sparkling Targe|sparkling targe hybrid study]]

**Trigger**
An enemy hits you with a melee Strike or a melee spell attack roll, or you fail a save against an enemy's spell.

**Requirements**
You are wielding a shield or have the [[Spells/Shield|_shield_]] spell prepared.

---

You immediately [[Actions/Raise a Shield]] or cast _shield_. The circumstance bonus applies to your defenses when determining the outcome of the triggering attack or spell.
