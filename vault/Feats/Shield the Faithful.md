# Shield the Faithful single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 280

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

You and those closest to you are protected by your deity’s power. For 1 minute, you and all allies within 10 feet of you gain a +1 item bonus to AC and [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to spirit damage. In addition, when a creature adjacent to you hits you with a melee attack, or when a creature touches you or hits you with an unarmed attack, that creature takes 5 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage.

At 20th level, the spirit resistance increases to 15 and the spirit damage increases to 10.
