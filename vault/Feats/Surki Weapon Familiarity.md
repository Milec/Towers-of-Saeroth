# Surki Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

---

Surki weapons favor utility and practicality over grace and brutality. You have familiarity with [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick (Player Core)|picks]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Hammer (Player Core)|light hammers]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickles]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe (Player Core)|scythes]], and one additional common weapon of your choice from the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axe]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|hammer]] group—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
