When you successfully [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your _spectral dagger_ and damage a foe with the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition, they can’t reduce their frightened condition below 1 before the end of your next turn.
