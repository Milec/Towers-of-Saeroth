# Soulsight  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|[Sorcerer]]] bloodline that grants [[Traits/Divine|divine]] or [[Traits/Occult|occult]] spells

---

Your muse has opened your senses to the world beyond. You gain spiritsense as an imprecise sense with a range of 60 feet. Spiritsense enables you to sense the spirits of creatures, including living creatures, most non-mindless [[Traits/Undead|undead]], and [[Traits/Haunt|haunts]] within the listed range. As with your hearing and other imprecise senses, you still need to [[Actions/Seek]] to locate an undetected creature. As spiritsense detects spiritual essence, not physical bodies, it can detect spirits projected by spells such as [[Spells/Project Image|_project image_]] or possessing otherwise soulless objects. It can't detect soulless bodies, [[Traits/Construct|constructs]], or objects, and like most senses, it doesn't penetrate through solid objects.
