# Brutal Critical  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 93

---

Your critical hits are particularly devastating. On a critical hit with a melee Strike, add one extra damage die. This is in addition to any extra dice you gain if the weapon is [[Traits/Deadly|deadly]] or [[Traits/Fatal|fatal]]. The target also takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to two damage dice.
