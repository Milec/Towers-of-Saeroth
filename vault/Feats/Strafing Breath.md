# Strafing Breath three actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 221

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Drake Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/War Rider Stance]]

**Requirements**
War Rider Stance is active.

---

You urge your dragon mount forward, wreathed in magical power. Your mount [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] three times. If your mount has a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]], it can substitute a [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] action for any of these Stride actions. As it moves, your dragon companion’s magical breath billows around you. The aura from War Rider Stance increases to a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] during this movement, and each creature that’s in the emanation at any point during your movement takes the damage from it (with the normal save). A creature can take damage only once, even if you move past it multiple times.
