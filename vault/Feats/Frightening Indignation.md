**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Steel Falcon]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Steel Falcon Dedication]]

---

Your anger at the world's many injustices boil out from you in a wave of palpable energy. An enemy that ends its turn within a 15-foot emanation around you must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. This aura lasts for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to your Frightening Indignation for 1 minute.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2 and can't reduce its frightened condition below 1 while it's in your aura.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but frightened 3.
