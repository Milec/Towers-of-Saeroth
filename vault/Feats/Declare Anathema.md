# Declare Anathema two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 280

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

You declare a creature you are aware of within 30 feet anathema, causing their friends and strangers to shy away from them. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] check against the target’s Will DC.

**Critical Success** Any creature that attempts to target the anathema creature with a beneficial effect must succeed at a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] or the action is disrupted. This curse remains until removed by [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] or a similar effect, or an [[Rituals/Atone (Player Core)|atone]] ritual. In addition, the creature gains [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] 10 to [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage for 1 minute.  

**Success** As critical success, but the curse lasts for 1 minute.  

**Failure** As critical success, but the curse lasts for 1 round and the target doesn’t gain weakness to spirit damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target is unaffected.
