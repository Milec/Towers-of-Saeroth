# Magus Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 88

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Prerequisites**
Strength +2 or Dexterity +2; Intelligence +2

---

You cast spells like a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Secrets of Magic)|magus]], gaining a spellbook with four common [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] cantrips of your choice. You gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity. You can prepare two cantrips each day from your spellbook. You’re trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for magus archetype spells is Intelligence, and they are arcane magus spells. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]]; if you were already trained in Arcana, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
