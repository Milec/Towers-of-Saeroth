# Echoing Spell single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 157

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Wellspring Mage]] (Level 18), [[Archetypes/Time Mage]] (Level 20), [[Archetypes/Time Mage (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage]] (Level 20)

---

You time the components of your spell with exacting precision, setting up a resonance that duplicates the spell's effects. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] of 4th rank or lower that has no duration, the spell's energy reverberates and echoes. You can Cast the Spell a second time before the end of your next turn without expending a spell slot.
