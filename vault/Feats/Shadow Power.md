You can focus the shadows into a powerful blast. You can cast [[Spells/Shadow Blast|_shadow blast_]] as an innate occult spell once per day. Its level is always 1 level lower than the level of your shadowdancer focus spells. You become an expert in occult spell attack rolls and DCs.
