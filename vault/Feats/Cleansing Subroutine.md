# Cleansing Subroutine  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Android]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 71

---

Your nanites help purge your body of harmful chemicals and toxins. Each time you succeed at a Fortitude save against an ongoing [[Traits/Poison|poison]], you reduce its stage by 2, or by 1 against a [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] poison. Each critical success you achieve against an ongoing poison reduces its stage by 3, or by 2 against a virulent poison.
