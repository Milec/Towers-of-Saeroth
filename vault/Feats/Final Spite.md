# Final Spite free action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
A creature reduces you or a living ally within 30 feet to 0 Hit Points. You can trigger this action even while [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]].

---

You can channel the power of death into a poisonous spite. The triggering creature takes 4d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save against your spell DC. If the creature kills you or your ally as part of the trigger, the creature gets a result one degree of success worse than it rolls on this save.
