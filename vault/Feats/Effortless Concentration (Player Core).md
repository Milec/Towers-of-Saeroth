# Effortless Concentration free action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 106, [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 157, [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

**Trigger**
Your turn begins

---

You can maintain a spell with hardly a thought. You immediately gain the effects of the [[Actions/Sustain]] action, allowing you to extend the duration of one of your active spells.

**Special** This can only be used on a cantrip or spell from the class matching the one you gained this feat from.
