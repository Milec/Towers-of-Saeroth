# Implausible Purchase  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 113

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]] (Level 18)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Predictive Purchase]]

---

It seems impossible, but you've analyzed every angle and are able to just keep pulling out exactly the item you need, even in far-flung locations. You can use [[Feats/Prescient Planner (Player Core)|Prescient Planner]] even if you have already used it after purchasing goods, and you can use it as a single action instead of a 2-action activity, during which you Interact to draw the item. In addition, five times per day, you can use Prescient Planner to pull out a common [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]] item up to 6 levels lower than your level.
