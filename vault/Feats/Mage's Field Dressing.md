# Mage's Field Dressing single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/War Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/War Mage Dedication]]; [[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was to [Cast a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3271) from a wizard spell slot, and the spell affected an ally within 60 feet.

---

As your spell takes hold on your ally, you use some of its magic to quickly dress their wounds. You use Battle Medicine on one ally affected by the required spell. You do not need to be holding or wearing a [[Equipment/Healer's Toolkit|healer's toolkit]], as you conjure glowing threads, bandages, or similar supplies out of pure magic, which adds the arcane trait to Battle Medicine.
