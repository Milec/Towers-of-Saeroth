# Sacred Defender  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 99

---

You are a great warrior that can stand strong against the fiercest opponents to your cause. You gain resistance 5 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. If you're [[Traits/Holy|holy]], this resistance increases to 10 against unholy creatures, and if you're [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] it increases to 10 against holy creatures. In addition, creatures and effects that roll a natural 20 on an attack roll against you don't improve their result by one degree of success.
