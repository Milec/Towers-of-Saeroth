# Thorned Seedpod  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 43

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Seedpod]]

---

When your seedpods strike a felling blow, they burst into viciously thorny vines. When you critically succeed at a seedpod Strike, vines twist and stab your foe, dealing 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent piercing damage]].
