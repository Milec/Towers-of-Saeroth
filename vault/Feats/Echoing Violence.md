**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]]

once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Martial Artist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You critically succeed with an unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an opponent.

---

Your powerful attack causes damage that reverberates through your opponent’s body, shaking muscle from bone. The creature takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC. If it critically fails, it immediately dies as your blow tears apart its body internally.
