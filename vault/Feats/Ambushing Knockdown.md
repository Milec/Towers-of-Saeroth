# Ambushing Knockdown two actions  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -159- All or Nothing|Pathfinder #159: All or Nothing]] pg. 75

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Rackets/Ruffian|ruffian racket]]

**Access**
Side with the Washboard Dogs in the gang conflict.

**Requirements**
You're wielding a melee weapon that deals bludgeoning damage and you're [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by an enemy.

---

You emerge from hiding and deliver a blow that can knock an enemy sprawling to the ground. You Stride up to half your Speed, but you must end your movement next to a foe you're hidden from or undetected by. You then Strike that enemy with the required weapon; if you hit, the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
