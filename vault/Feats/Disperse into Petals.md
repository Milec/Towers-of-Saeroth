# Disperse into Petals two actions  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 75

---

You merge with the blossoms sprouting from you and disperse, becoming a visible cloud of blossoms and petals. You’re amorphous. You lose any item bonus to AC and all other effects and bonuses from armor, and you use your proficiency modifier for unarmored defense. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half your level and are immune to precision damage. You can’t cast spells, activate items, or use actions that have the attack or [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 10 feet. You can remain in this form for up to 1 minute. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect.
