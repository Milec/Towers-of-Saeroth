# Sharkskin Grip  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

---

You served on [[Ancestries/Nephilim|nephilim]] pirate Ila “Sharkskin” Clare’s ship, the _Scorched Shark_, and have adopted xir practice of embellishing clothing and armor with sharkskin. As long as you’re wearing your clothing, you gain a +1 item bonus to unarmed attack rolls, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks, and Reflex saves and DCs to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]], [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]], or avoid falling [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. In addition, when you successfully [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] a creature, you deal slashing damage equal to your fist damage dice to that creature as you abrade their skin.
