# All Returns to Slumber single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Sleep]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 106

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

Your waking world returns to slumber, and lays the peace of deep sleep on all within it. You [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] your waking world, and all creatures in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animals]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]] native to this world treat the result of their save as one degree worse. Creatures in the area then become temporarily immune to this ability for 1 hour.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature can’t use reactions for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.
