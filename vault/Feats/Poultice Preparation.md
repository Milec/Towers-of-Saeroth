# Poultice Preparation  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 176

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication]]

---

When you create an herbal elixir, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the [[Traits/Oil|oil]] trait (but remains alchemical, not magical) and loses the [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] and [[Traits/Ingested|ingested]] traits. You can apply a poultice by Interacting with one hand. When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item's normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid, bleed, or fire damage]], with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.
