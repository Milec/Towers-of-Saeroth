# Well-Met Traveler  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

---

You naturally adopt a pleasant and affable demeanor when meeting others. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. If you would automatically become trained in Diplomacy (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 In addition, you gain the [[Feats/Hobnobber (Player Core)|Hobnobber]] skill feat.
