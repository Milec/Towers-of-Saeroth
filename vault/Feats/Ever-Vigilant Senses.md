# Ever-Vigilant Senses  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 73

---

Your eidolon has enhanced senses. It gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception, and can't be flanked except by creatures that are higher level than it. It can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects; they appear translucent and are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]. When your eidolon moves within 30 feet of an [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] that can be [[Rules/Illusion|disbelieved]], the GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] check to disbelieve it, even if your eidolon didn't spend an action to Interact.
