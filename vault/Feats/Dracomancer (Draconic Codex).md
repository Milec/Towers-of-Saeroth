# Dracomancer  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 215

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Dragonscaled Kobold (Player Core 2)|Dragonscaled Kobold]] heritage

---

The power infused into you by your draconic benefactor’s presence allows you to manifest their most iconic spells. Choose one 1st-rank spell and one 2nd-rank spell from those listed for a dragon spellcaster of your draconic benefactor’s type, such as those presented here (for example, [[Spells/Fear (Player Core)|_fear_]] and [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] for a [[Bestiary/Adult Conspirator Dragon|conspirator dragon]] benefactor). You can cast each of these spells once per day as [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] spells of the same tradition as your draconic benefactor. You gain the trained proficiency rank in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics, and your key spellcasting attribute is Charisma.
