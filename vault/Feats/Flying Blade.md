# Flying Blade  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 88

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-361)|precise strike]]

---

You've learned to apply your flashy techniques to thrown weapons as easily as melee attacks. When you have panache, you apply your precise strike damage on ranged Strikes you make with a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon within that weapon's first range increment. The thrown weapon must be an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] weapon. This also allows you to make a thrown weapon ranged Strike for [[Actions/Confident Finisher]] and any other finisher that includes a Strike that can benefit from your precise strike.
