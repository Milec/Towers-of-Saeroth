# Divine Health  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 94

---

Your faith makes you resistant to disease, protecting you as you offer succor to the ill. You gain a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] and to flat checks to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]]. Allies in your champion's aura get this benefit, but their bonus is +1.

 In addition, if you roll a success on a save against a disease or poison, you get a critical success instead. (Your allies don't share this benefit.) If you have the sacred body class feature, when you roll a critical failure on a save against a disease or poison, you get a failure instead.
