# Time Mage Dedication  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Time Mage (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
You have a spellcasting class feature.

---

You might be a novice in the temporal arts now, but in the future, you'll hold time in the palm of your hand. As some of your future knowledge leaks back to your present self, you gain the [[Spells/Delay Consequence (Divine Mysteries)|_delay consequence_]] domain spell. The rules for focus spells appear on [[Rules/Focus Spells (Player Core)|here]]. You can [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by revisiting moments from your past and contemplating futures yet to come. If you already knew delay consequence from a feat or ability that allows you to gain your choice of domain spell, such as the [[Feats/Domain Initiate (Player Core)|Domain Initiate]] feat, then when you take Time Mage Dedication, you can retrain that option to select a different domain spell instead as your personal timeline rearranges itself. You also gain [[Spells/Time Sense (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_time sense_]] as an innate cantrip usable at will. This innate spell and your focus spells from the time mage archetype are of the same tradition as the spells you used to meet the archetype's prerequisites.
