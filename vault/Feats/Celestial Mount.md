# Celestial Mount  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 115

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|divine ally (steed)]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Good|tenets of good]]

---

Your steed gains incredible celestial powers granted by your deity. It gains darkvision, its maximum Hit Points increase by 40, and it gains weakness 10 to unholy.

 Additionally, it grows wings appropriate to a servitor of your deity (such as metallic wings for an archon), granting it a fly Speed equal to its Speed. It gains the [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]] trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor it has become ([[Traits/Archon|archon]], [[Traits/Angel|angel]], or [[Traits/Azata|azata]], for example).
