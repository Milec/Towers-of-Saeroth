# Morphic Manipulation  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 79

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]

---

You've attuned your natural aura to that of plant life, coaxing it to grow faster than normal. You sit for 10 minutes with a small, natural non-creature plant—such as a lone flower, a small bush, or a potted succulent—while you bring your aura in tune through meditation. You coax the plant to advance one stage in its life cycle: from seed to sprout, sprout to plant, plant to flower, or flower to fruit. If the plant doesn't have proper nutrients (if you're sprouting a seed without soil or water, for example), the plant withers 5 minutes after you complete the activity. Performing this activity more than once per day renders you [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] and unable to use Morphic Manipulation until your next full night's rest.
