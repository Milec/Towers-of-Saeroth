# Angelkin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 79

---

You descend from an [[Bestiary/Families/Angel|angel]], a winged messenger from [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana]] or one of the other celestial realms. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in Society (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You know the [[Setting/Languages/Empyrean]] language, and you gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat.
