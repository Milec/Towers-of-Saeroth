# Feeding Frenzy  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; wereshark

---

Your serrated teeth rip and tear at your prey. When you critically hit with your jaws Strike from Werecreature Dedication, your target takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to jaws Strikes against the target until the end of your turn. If you have [[Rules/Scent|scent]] as a special sense, it's a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]] sense with double its normal range when locating creatures taking persistent bleed damage.
