# Snare Expert  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Guerrilla Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

Your knowledge of traps aids you in defending your hideout. You gain the [[Feats/Snare Crafting (Player Core 2)|Snare Crafting]] feat. When you set a snare, any saving throw it requires uses the higher of your class DC or the snare's DC. Snares you set within your favored location increase their Stealth DC by 2.
