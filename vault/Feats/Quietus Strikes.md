# Quietus Strikes  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

Your connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]] empowers you to bring death to all forms of life and unlife, exploiting their weaknesses. All your weapon and unarmed Strikes are [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] and deal 1 additional void damage to living creatures and 1 additional vitality damage to [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. The extra damage increases to 2 if the Strike has a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+3 potency rune]]_.
