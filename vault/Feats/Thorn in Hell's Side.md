# Thorn in Hell's Side  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 217, [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 9

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Defy Hell]]

---

Your devotion to the ideals of justice, freedom, and revolution allow you to truly punish those who stand in your way. When you critically succeed at a Hellbreaker Strike, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (stunned 2 on a critical failure). This effect has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait.
