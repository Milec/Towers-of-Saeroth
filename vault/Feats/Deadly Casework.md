# Deadly Casework  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Planning assassinations is anathema to the Pathfinder Society._

**Prerequisites**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

---

You treat a well-planned assassination with the diligence that you would bring to any investigation. You can [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]] and open an [[Rules/Investigations (GM Core)|investigation]] regarding a target you want to assassinate. This investigation doesn’t count against the number of active investigations you can have at a time, but you can only have one assassination investigation.

Your Skill Stratagem bonus increases by 1 when investigating details about your assassination target.
