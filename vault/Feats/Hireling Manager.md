# Hireling Manager  *Feat 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 206

**Prerequisites**
Charisma 14

---

You are able to find and secure better labor than most. When securing a [[Equipment/Hireling|hireling]] for a service, that hireling gains a +2 circumstance bonus to all skill checks. This circumstance bonus applies to both trained and untrained hirelings and has no effect on the cost of the service or labor provided.
