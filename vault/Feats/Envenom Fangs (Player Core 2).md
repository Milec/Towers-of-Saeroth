# Envenom Fangs single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Iruxi Armaments]]

---

You envenom your fangs. If the next fangs Strike you make before the end of your next turn hits and deals damage, the Strike deals an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]]. The poison becomes inert after you hit, or at the end of your next turn, whichever comes first.
