# Proud Mentor  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 32

---

You enjoy mentoring others and take pride in your students' accomplishments. If you get a critical failure on an attempt to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]], you get a failure instead. Whenever you successfully Aid an ally who critically succeeds on the check you Aided them on, you feel a rush of pride, gaining temporary Hit Points equal to your level, which last for 1 minute.
