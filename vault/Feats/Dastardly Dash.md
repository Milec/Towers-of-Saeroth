# Dastardly Dash single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 165

---

You dash past an opponent, confounding them with underhanded tactics. Stride up to half your speed. You can attempt a [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] or a [[Feats/Dirty Trick]] (if you have that action) against one opponent you pass within range of at any point during your movement.
