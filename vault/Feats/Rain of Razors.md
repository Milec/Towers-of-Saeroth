# Rain of Razors three actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 31

---

Razor-sharp slivers of metal fall from the sky. Each creature in a 20-foot burst within 60 feet takes 9d6 slashing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. The razors embed in all surfaces in the area, making them [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]] for 1 minute. A creature that moves through this hazardous terrain takes 3 slashing damage for every square of the area it moves into.

---
**Level (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d6 and the hazardous terrain damage increases by 1.
