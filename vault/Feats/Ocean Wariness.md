# Ocean Wariness  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 19

---

Stories about those who have mistreated your people in the past (particularly stories about [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]] mind control) left you untrusting of those outside your community. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your defenses against [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks.
