# Widespread Fascination  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 33

---

You are not content to restrict yourself to one field. Instead, you've studied a wider range of necromantic magic to better expand your horizons and explore your unscrupulous fascinations. You learn the grave spell for a [grim fascination](GrimFascinations.aspx) other than yours. You must have access to that fascination. Access can be gained from independent study or sought-out tutelage.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once, choosing a different grave spell each time.
