# Alter Admixture  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 66

**Requirements**
You are holding or wearing an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist's toolkit]].

---

Sometimes, you need to adapt your alchemical mixtures to new situations. In a process that takes 10 minutes, you change an item you created with advanced alchemy into a similar item. You can change an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical bomb]] into  another type of alchemical bomb, an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixir]] into another type of elixir, or a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|poison]] into another type of poison. If it's unclear whether two alchemical consumables are similar, the GM decides. You must know the formula for the new item, and the new item must be of the same or lower item level than the original item. The new item still keeps the [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] trait, and it remains potent as long as the original item would have.
