# Swift Elusion ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 137

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]

**Trigger**
A foe ends its movement adjacent to you.

---

Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check against the foe's Reflex DC. If you succeed, either Stride to a different space adjacent to the foe or move the foe to a different space adjacent to you.
