# Nagaji Spell Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 51

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nagaji Spell Mysteries]]

---

Your magical skill rivals that of accomplished [[Bestiary/Families/Naga|naga]] spellcasters. During your daily preparations, choose [[Spells/Blink|_blink_]], [[Spells/Control Water|_control water_]], or [[Spells/Subconscious Suggestion|_subconscious suggestion_]]. You can Cast this Spell as a 5th-level occult innate spell once that day. You become an expert in occult spell DCs and occult spell attack rolls.
