# Unrivaled Retort ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 83

**Trigger**
You are about to attempt a Fortitude or Reflex save against an [[Rules/Area|area]] effect and are wielding a ranged weapon.

---

You defy the death and destruction around you while using it to fuel your mythic power, spurring you on to acts of incredible speed and empowering a mighty ranged counterattack. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] and attempt the triggering save at mythic proficiency. If the save is successful, you can immediately [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|reload]] and make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] targeting the originator of the triggering effect.
