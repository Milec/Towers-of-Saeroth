# Sunwrecker  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 42

---

Your weapon has the might of legends, capable of shooting or striking any light out of the sky. The imbued ikon gains the following abilities.

**Immanence** (darkness) On a successful critical hit with the weapon, the weapon casts 2nd-rank darkness centered on the target of the attack.  
**Transcendence—Break the Sun’s Legs** (cold, concentrate, darkness, transcendence) two actions You make a slashing motion over the brightest source of light, whether it’s a torch or the sun in the sky. A gash appears over the light source, visible to all, and devours the light. The entire area within a 120 foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] around you is affected by a 9th-rank darkness spell for 1 minute. The stolen light is channeled to your eyes, granting you greater [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]] for the duration of the effect.
