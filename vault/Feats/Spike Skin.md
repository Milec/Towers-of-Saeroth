# Spike Skin two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 27

---

You touch a willing creature, causing its skin to harden and form spiky protrusions. The target gains resistance 4 to all physical damage (except [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]]). Whenever a creature damages the target with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack or non-[[Traits/Reach|reach]] melee weapon, the attacking creature takes 2 piercing damage.

 This impulse lasts for 10 minutes, but each time the target takes physical damage, the duration decreases by 1 minute. The target is temporarily immune to this impulse for 1 hour. If you use Spike Skin again, any previous one ends.

---
**Level (+2)** The resistance and damage each increase by 2.
