# Disarming Stance single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 147

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Duelist]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Duelist (Player Core 2)|Duelist]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding only a single one-handed melee weapon and have your other hand or hands free.

---

You adopt a fencing stance that improves your control over your weapon. While you are in this stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to [[Actions/Disarm]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC when defending against checks to Disarm you. In addition, you can attempt to Disarm creatures up to two sizes larger than you.
