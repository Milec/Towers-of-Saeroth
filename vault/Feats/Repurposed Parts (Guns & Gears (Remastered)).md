# Repurposed Parts  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 54

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Trapsmith (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trapsmith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Trapsmith Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trapsmith Dedication]]

---

You are familiar with the fittings and cogwheels of your [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|snares]] and can repurpose parts from prepared quick-deploy snares to assemble a new creation on the fly. Using two of the snares you prepared for quick deployment during daily preparations, you can quickly deploy a different snare that you know how to [[Actions/Craft]], even if you didn't prepare that snare during your daily preparations.
