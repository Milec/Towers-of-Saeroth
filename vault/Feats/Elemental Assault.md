# Elemental Assault single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Suli]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 110

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You shroud your arms and held weapons in elemental magic. Choose one element. Until the end of your next turn, your Strikes deal an additional 1d6 damage of the indicated type and have the trait corresponding to the element: electricity for [[Traits/Air|air]], bludgeoning for [[Traits/Earth|earth]], fire for [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or cold for [[Traits/Water|water]].
