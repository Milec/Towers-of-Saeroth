# Ratfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 22

---

Years of experience among ratfolk communities have made you nimble, and you've learned to run and hide when enemies threaten. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Ratfolk Lore]].
