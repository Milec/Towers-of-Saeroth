# Voice of Authority  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Vigilante]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 64

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]; [[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

---

You easily command the attention of others, and when the time comes to turn threats into action, your authoritative voice causes foes to falter.  
Your voice can be heard easily by any creatures within 300 feet if you wish, regardless of the level of ambient noise, although your voice can't penetrate silence or similar efect. If you roll [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] for initiative, on the frst round of combat, creatures that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to you.
