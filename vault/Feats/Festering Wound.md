# Festering Wound  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zombie]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Zombie Dedication]]

---

Wounds you inflict with your [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks fester and rot, becoming infected and difficult to heal. The DC to [[Actions/Treat Wounds|Treat these Wounds]] using [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] increases by 5, and magically or alchemically healing these wounds requires a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Succeeding at any of these removes the disease. The effect also ends if the creature is healed to full Hit Points by any means.
