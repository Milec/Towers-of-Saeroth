# Sister of the Golden Erinys Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 109

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This feat does not allow Pathfinder Society members to become unholy or add the unholy trait to their weapons._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sister of the Golden Erinys]]

**Prerequisites**
You're from Isger or a member of the Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys.

**Access**
You're from Isger or a member of the Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys.

---

The fighting style of the Sisterhood of the Golden Erinys is rooted in their understanding of devils. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Devil Lore. When you choose this feat, you can choose to become sanctified as unholy. You gain access to the asp coil. You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Asp Coil|asp coil]] and [[Equipment/Weapons/Scourge (Battlecry!)|scourge]], treating them as simple weapons for the purposes of proficiency. If you're unholy, your Strikes using those weapons and your unarmed Strikes gain the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Monastic Weaponry (Player Core 2)|Monastic Weaponry]] feat, the asp coil gains the monk trait for you.
