You gain the on the case class feature, which grants you both the [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]] activity and [[Actions/Clue In (Player Core 2)|Clue In]] reaction. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] and another skill of your choice.
