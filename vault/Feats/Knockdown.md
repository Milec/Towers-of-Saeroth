# Knockdown two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Mauler]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Thlipit Contestant]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Mauler (Player Core 2)|Mauler]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You make an attack to knock a foe off balance, then follow up immediately with a sweep to topple them. Make a melee Strike. If it hits and deals damage, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip]] the creature you hit. If you're wielding a two-handed melee weapon, you can ignore Trip's requirement that you have a hand free. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after you've made both of them.
