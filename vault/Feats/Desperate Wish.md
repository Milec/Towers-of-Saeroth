# Desperate Wish  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Starlit Sentinel]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Starlit Sentinel Dedication]]

---

You call out a desperate wish to the stars to save a friend. You gain [[Spells/Breath of Life (Player Core)|_breath of life_]] as an innate [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] spell, which you can cast once per day only while in sentinel form. Interceding in such a direct way temporarily exhausts your constellation’s magic, causing you to revert from your sentinel form once the spell is cast.
