# Extend Elixir  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 79

---

Integrating your own personal energy into the elixirs you create causes them to affect you for longer. When you consume one of your alchemical items that has the [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] and [[Traits/Infused|infused]] traits and a duration of 1 minute or longer, that elixir’s duration is doubled.
