# Comforting Presence ⤾  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 286

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Access**
Character follows the ways of Sangpotshi or studies texts and lore containing Sangpotshi secrets

**Trigger**
An adjacent ally becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]].

---

Your occult connection to your allies lets you take on fear and confusion they feel. Focus on your calmness and pull in nearby allies’ emotions. Reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition values of adjacent allies by 1, and increase your frightened and stupefied condition values by the same amount.
