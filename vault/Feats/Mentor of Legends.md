# Mentor of Legends  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 32

---

You're an extraordinary teacher, capable of inspiring your students to amazing feats of athletics and ingenuity. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Heroism (Player Core)|_heroism_]] as an innate divine spell; in addition to its normal effects, the spell also grants temporary Hit Points equal to half your level. You can't cast this spell on yourself.
