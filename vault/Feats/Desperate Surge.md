# Desperate Surge single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You can conjure up at will the kind of strength that most beings only attain in life-or-death situations. You can attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grab]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] a creature using a spell attack roll instead of an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check.

You can recharge this ability early by making an unarmed Strike to destroy one of your thralls within your reach.
