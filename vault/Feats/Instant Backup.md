# Instant Backup ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 112

**Trigger**
Your attack with a firearm misfires.

---

Even as your firearm misfires, you quickly draw a backup weapon. [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Release]] the misfired weapon if you so choose, and [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to draw a one-handed weapon.
