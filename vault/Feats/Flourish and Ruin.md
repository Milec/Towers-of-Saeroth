# Flourish and Ruin  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 43

---

You can call upon the vital essence of your spirit to restore life to your allies and call forth vines to ensnare and bludgeon your foes. You can cast [[Spells/Field of Life|_field of life_]] and [[Spells/Tangling Creepers|_tangling creepers_]] as 6th-level primal innate spells once per day each. You become an expert in primal spell attack rolls and primal spell DCs.
