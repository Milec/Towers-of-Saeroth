# Liberty or Death two actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

**Requirements**
A creature has been the target of your  [Ultimatum of Liberation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2993) for at least 1 minute

---

You have given a despot enough time to think, and now is the time for them to forfeit their control or die. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] and make a Strike at mythic proficiency against the required creature. If you succeed and deal damage, the target must succeed on a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or die. If the target succeeds, they are immune to this ability for 24 hours.
