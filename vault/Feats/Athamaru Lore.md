# Athamaru Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 18

---

You've immersed yourself in community learning, particularly around navigating the waters and tending to animals. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]. If you automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Athamaru Lore]].
