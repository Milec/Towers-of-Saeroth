# Improved Elemental Blast  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 57

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Kineticist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kineticist Dedication]]

---

The power of your [[Actions/Elemental Blast|elemental blast]] improves. The damage of your elemental blast increases by one die.

**Special** You can take Improved Elemental Blast a second time at 14th level to increase your Elemental Blast to three damage die, and a third time at 18th level to increase your Elemental Blast to four damage die.
