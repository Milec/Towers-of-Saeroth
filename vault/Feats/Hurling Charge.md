# Hurling Charge two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

You make a ranged Strike with a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon you already have in your hand, Stride, and then Interact to draw another weapon. If you are raging, you can add the additional damage with melee weapons you receive from raging to your damage with the thrown weapon.
