# Awakened Jewel  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 61

---

You once held the head gem of a deceased ancestor or loved one, and it awakened a mysterious power in your own. You gain one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult spell list]]. As long as you possess your head gem, you can cast this spell as an innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
