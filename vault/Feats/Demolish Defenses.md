# Demolish Defenses two actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 49

---

With a powerful blow, you crack a foe's armor or tough hide, opening it up for further attacks. Make a melee Strike. This Strike deals one additional weapon die of damage; if the target is your taunted enemy, this increases to two additional weapon die of damage. This counts as two attacks for the purposes of calculating your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]]. If you hit and deal damage, the target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC.  
**Critical Success** The target takes no additional effect.  
**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.  
**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 until the end of your next turn. Once it loses this clumsy condition, it's off-guard for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is clumsy 3.
