# Expert Backstabber  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

You excel at quickly dispatching your foes. When you Strike an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] foe with a weapon that has the [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]] weapon trait, you deal 2 extra precision damage instead of 1. If the weapon is a _+3 weapon_, you deal 4 extra precision damage instead of 2.
