# Terrifying Transformation single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; you were afflicted with the curse of the werecreature

---

Your flesh tears and warps as you transform. Attempt [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet. This Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, and you don't take a penalty if a creature doesn't understand your language. Regardless of the results of your checks, each creature is temporarily immune to Terrifying Transformation for 1 minute.
