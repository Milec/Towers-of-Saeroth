# None Shall Know  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shisk]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 125

---

Even magical means have a hard time prying information from you. You gain [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] as an innate occult spell once per day, which can only be cast on yourself and is automatically heightened to half your level rounded up.
