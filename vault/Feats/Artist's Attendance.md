# Artist's Attendance two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

---

Your runes call you to better attend to your art. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] twice. If you end your movement within reach of a creature that is bearing one of your runes, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] upon that creature or another target adjacent to you. You can use Artist’s Attendance while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
