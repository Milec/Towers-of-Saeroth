# Grievous Blow two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Martial Artist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist Dedication]]

---

You know how to deliver focused, powerful blows that bypass your enemies’ resistances. Make an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If this Strike hits, it deals two additional weapon damage dice and ignores an amount of [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to physical damage (or to a specific physical damage type) equal to your level. This Strike counts as two attacks when calculating your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]].

If you are at least 18th level, increase the additional damage to three weapon damage dice.
