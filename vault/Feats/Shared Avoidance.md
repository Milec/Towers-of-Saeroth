# Shared Avoidance  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 122

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Premonition of Avoidance]]

---

You can project your premonitions of danger to your allies. When you use [[Feats/Premonition of Avoidance]], allies within 15 feet of you who are rolling the same saving throw against the same hazard also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their saving throws.
