# Slip into Shadow  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 27

**Prerequisites**
ability to cast at least one spell with the [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] trait

---

Your shadow magic bleeds into the space around you. Whenever you cast a spell with the darkness trait, a lingering aura of shadows reduces bright light within a 5-foot emanation around you to dim light until the end of your turn. Like in natural dim light, you can use these shadows to [[Actions/Hide]], but the aura still makes your overall position obvious, so you can't become [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] through the use of these shadows alone.
