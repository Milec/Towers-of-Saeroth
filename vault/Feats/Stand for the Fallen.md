# Stand for the Fallen ⤾  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 282

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally takes damage that would bring them to 0 Hit Points.

---

You protect your allies at the cost of your own blood, but your deity shields you from the worst of the harm. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] twice to be adjacent to the triggering ally.

You can [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type. You then take the damage that would have brought your ally to 0 Hit Points, though you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to all damage against that damage equal to your level.
