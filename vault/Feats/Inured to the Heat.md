# Inured to the Heat  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shisk]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 124

---

You have a love for extreme heat. You gain resistance 4 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]. You treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on).
