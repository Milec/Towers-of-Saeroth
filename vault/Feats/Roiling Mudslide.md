# Roiling Mudslide two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 37

---

You form water and earth into a mudslide that smashes your opponents and coats them in mud. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. A creature that fails is also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure) and coated in mud until the end of its next turn. While coated in mud, the creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] at the end of its movement any time it ends a [[Traits/Move|move]] action other than a Crawl or Step. The creature can attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save against your class DC, avoiding the fall if it succeeds.

---
**Level (+4)** The damage increases by 1d8.
