# Risky Surgery  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 233

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

Your surgery can bring a patient back from the brink of death, but might push them over the edge. When you [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], you can deal 1d8 slashing damage to your patient just before applying the effects of Treat Wounds. If you do, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Medicine check to Treat Wounds, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
