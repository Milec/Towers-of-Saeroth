# Crown of Rule  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 40

---

This badge of office proclaims your sovereignty. The imbued ikon gains the following ability.

**Transcendence—Heavy is the Crown** two actions (concentrate, mental, spirit, transcendence) The weight of authority falls heavily upon you, as well as nearby creatures. All enemies in a 15 foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 60 feet take spirit damage equal to your level with a basic Will save against your class DC. Any enemy that fails its save must immediately kneel, dropping prone as a free action.
