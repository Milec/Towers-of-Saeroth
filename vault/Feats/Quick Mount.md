# Quick Mount single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 164

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]], [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

**Requirements**
You are adjacent to a creature that is at least one size larger than you and is willing to be your mount.

---

You and your mount can spring into action at a moment's notice. You [[Actions/Mount]] the creature and [[Actions/Command an Animal]] to issue it an order of your choice.
