# Bardic Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 100

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Enigma|enigma muse]]

---

Your studies make you informed on every subject. You are trained in Bardic Lore, a special Lore skill that can be used only to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]], but on any topic. If you have legendary proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], you gain expert proficiency in Bardic Lore, but you can’t increase your proficiency rank in Bardic Lore by any other means.
