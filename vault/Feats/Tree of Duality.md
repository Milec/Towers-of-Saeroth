# Tree of Duality three actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 37

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) A Tree of Duality creates both the Cleansing Pollen and Hallucinogenic Spore effects, not just one._

---

An ephemeral tree bursts forth, floating on air in an unoccupied square of your choice within 60 feet. Blooming flowers and fungal growths shed pollen and spores, which a swirling gust of air spreads in a 10-foot emanation around it. The tree lasts until the end of your next turn, and you can Sustain it up to 1 minute.

- **Cleansing Pollen** ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) Each living ally that's in the area or enters it regains 3d4 HP and is then temporarily immune to regaining HP from Tree of Duality for 10 minutes.
- **Hallucinogenic Spores** ([[Traits/Mental|mental]]) Enemies in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]]. An enemy that leaves the area remains dazzled until the start of its next turn.

---
**Level (+2)** The healing increases by 1d4.
