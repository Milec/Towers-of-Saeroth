# Masked Casting free action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

[[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Preferment]]

**Trigger**

**Requirements**
You're wearing your signifer's mask.

---

You harness the magical energy flowing through your eyeless mask to effortlessly protect you from [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effects. You [[Actions/Avert Gaze (Player Core)|Avert your Gaze]]. While Averting your Gaze in this way, you also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws and checks to disbelieve [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusions]].
