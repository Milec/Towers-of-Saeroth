# Additional Servings  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 100

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wandering Chef]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wandering Chef Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or Cooking Lore

---

Your number of [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|versatile vials]] per day increases to 5.

**Special** At 12th level or higher, you can select this feat a second time to increase your number of versatile vials per day to 6. At 18th level or higher, you can select this feat a third time to increase your number of versatile vials per day to 7.
