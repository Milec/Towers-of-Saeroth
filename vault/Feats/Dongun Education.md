# Dongun Education  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 200

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

You’re knowledgeable in trades that are important to your people’s survival and commercial interests. Pick two of the following Lore skills: [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Engineering Lore]], Explosive Lore, or Firearm Lore. At 2nd level, you gain expert proficiency in these Lore skills; at 7th level, you gain master proficiency in these Lore skills; and at 15th level, you gain legendary proficiency in them.
