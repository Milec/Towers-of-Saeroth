# Aether Beam two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are overflowing.

---

You can open your internal nexus, unleashing a destructive beam of magical energy that emanates from your hand, eyes, or chest. You fire a beam that deals 6d6 force damage in a 60- foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] and pushes back anyone who stands within. Each creature in the area must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your nexus DC. A creature that fails is also pushed back 10 feet (20 feet on a critical failure). The damage increases by 2d6 at 10th level and every 2 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.
