# Paradoxical Mystery  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 143

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Greater Revelation (Player Core 2)|Greater Revelation]]

---

The truest depths of your [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] mystery are fathomless and contradictory, granting you changing powers that even you can barely begin to fathom. Each day during your daily preparations, choose one basic or advanced domain spell from any domain from _Player Core_, one of the domains granted by your mystery, another domain to which you have access, or any initial or advanced revelation spell from another mystery. You gain that spell as a revelation spell until your next daily preparations.
