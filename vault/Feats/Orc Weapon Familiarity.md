# Orc Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 59

---

In combat, you favor the brutal weapons that are traditional for your orc ancestors. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion|falchion]] and [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe|greataxe]]. In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Orc|orc]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial orc weapons are simple weapons and advanced orc weapons are martial weapons.
