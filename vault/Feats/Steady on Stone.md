# Steady on Stone  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oread]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 106

---

Your connection to the earth makes natural uneven surfaces less of a hindrance for you. You can ignore difficult terrain caused by rubble and uneven ground made of stone and earth. In addition, when you use the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] skill to [[Actions/Balance]] on narrow surfaces or uneven ground made of stone and earth, you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], and when you roll a success at one of these Acrobatics checks, you get a critical success instead.
