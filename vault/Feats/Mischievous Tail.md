# Mischievous Tail  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ganzi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Skillful Tail (Ganzi)]]

---

Your tail can bedevil your foes. You can [[Actions/Disarm]] and [[Actions/Trip]] creatures with your tail, even if you don't have a free hand. All restrictions of Skillful Tail otherwise continue to apply. If you have [[Feats/Smashing Tail]], your tail unarmed attack gains the [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]] and [[Traits/Trip|trip]] traits.
