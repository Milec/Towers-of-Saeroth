# Bone Speaker  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 33

---

To truly understand the mechanical advantages of an undead skeleton, you must understand how it functioned in life. You can [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] on living creatures with an internal or external skeleton using [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Undead Lore]]. If you do, you can learn only information pertaining to the biology of a creature—not any learned abilities or abilities granted by items.

Additionally, you can perform a forensic examination on a corpse using Undead Lore instead of [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] if the corpse came from a creature with a skeleton.
