# Icy Apotheosis  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 185

You are as much a creature of cold as whatever ancestry you were born to. You are immune to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage and gain the cold trait. You automatically succeed on saving throws against effects that have the cold trait.
