# Bloodline Mutation  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 157

**Prerequisites**
a [bloodline](Bloodlines.aspx?ID=) based on a specific type of creature

---

You permanently mutate to become more like the creatures of your bloodline. You gain the appropriate trait or traits for those types of creatures ([[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberration]] for aberrant, [[Bestiary/Families/Angel (Monster Core)|angel]] and [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] for angelic, [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demon]] and [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] for demonic, and so on). You gain low-light vision or darkvision, if one is appropriate for creatures with those traits. Choose one of the following.

- If the creatures associated with your bloodline have the ability to [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|fly]], you gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed.
- If the creatures associated with your bloodline are [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] or [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]], you become amphibious, able to breathe water and air equally well, and you gain a [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|swim]] Speed equal to your land Speed.
- If creatures associated with your bloodline have a resistance or immunity to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Void|void]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]],choose an energy type your bloodline is immune or resistant to and gain resistance 20 against that energy type.
