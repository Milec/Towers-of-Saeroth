You practiced fan dance as a member of a large group, coordinating your moves together to create living art and heightening your sense of spatial awareness. When you wield two fans, each in a different hand, you gain [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 20 feet.
