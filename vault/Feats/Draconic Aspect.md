# Draconic Aspect  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 46

---

You have an obvious draconic feature, such as sharp claws, a snout full of sharp teeth, or strong reptilian tail, that you can use offensively. You gain your choice of one of the following unarmed attacks. The attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and has the listed damage die and traits.

- **Claw** 1d4 slashing ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]])
- **Jaws** 1d6 piercing ([[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]])
- **Tail** 1d6 bludgeoning ([[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]], [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|trip]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]])

**Special** You can select this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into or out of this feat, nor can you change the type of attack you gained.
