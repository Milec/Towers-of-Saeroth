# Spell Survivor  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Orc Superstition (Player Core)|Orc Superstition]]

---

Once you’ve resisted an opponent’s magic, anything else they throw your way becomes less effective. When you attempt a saving throw against a spell using Orc Superstition or [[Feats/Pervasive Superstition (Player Core)|Pervasive Superstition]] and critically succeed, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 1 to all damage dealt by that triggering spell’s caster until the end of their next turn. At 9th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the resistance increases by 1.
