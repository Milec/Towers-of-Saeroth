# Elemental Defense single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 108

---

You attune yourself to elemental power so that you gain a burst of protection when you call upon it. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has at least one element trait ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]]), you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to effects that have the element traits of your spell equal to twice the spell’s rank. The resistance lasts until the beginning of your next turn.
