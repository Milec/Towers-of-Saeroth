# Split Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twofold Tine|twofold tine hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding two melee weapons, each in a different hand, and your [[Actions/Spellstrike]] is charged.

---

Like knives slicing through magic itself, both your weapons carry your spell, delivering it to separate foes. Make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a spell that can target at least 2 creatures or that has an area. You can make two melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] instead of one, but each must be made with a different weapon and target a different creature. Increase your multiple attack penalty normally after each Strike rather than increasing it for Spellstrike as a whole. If you’re in [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], you can apply twofold tine’s extra benefit between the two Strikes.
