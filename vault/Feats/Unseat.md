# Unseat single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 165

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are riding your mount and wielding a [[Traits/Jousting|jousting]] weapon.

---

You attempt to knock an opponent off their mount. Make a melee Strike against a mounted opponent. If your attack hits, attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the opponent's Fortitude DC. If you succeed, the foe is knocked off its mount into a space of its choice adjacent to its mount. If you critically succeed, it lands [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
