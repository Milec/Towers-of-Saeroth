# Murderous Thorns  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hidden Thorn]] or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Thorned Rose]] heritage

---

Your thorns are many, vicious, and sharp. If you have the Hidden Thorn feat, your thorn attack gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d6]] trait. If you have the thorned rose heritage, you can use the Wicked Thorns reaction once every hour.
