# Henge Gate two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You hold a rune in your heart and strike the earth, causing two standing stones to rise nearby, your chosen rune carved upon their surfaces and blazing in the air above them. The stones each appear in an unoccupied 5-foot space within 30 feet of you and within 15 feet of each other. When you raise the henge gate, choose one rune that can be drawn on a creature to carve into the stones. If you or an ally makes a successful ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] using physical ammunition and that attack passes through the henge gate, the ammunition gains that rune in flight and automatically traces it on the target. The henge gate lasts for 1 minute.
