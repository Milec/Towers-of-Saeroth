# Defend Our Union ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Beast Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beast Lord Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You and your [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]] are within 15 feet of each other, and a foe successfully [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] your united companion.

---

You can protect your [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]] from danger with a welltimed attack. You can immediately [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] toward the triggering enemy and can make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against them if they would be in the first range increment of your ranged weapon or within your melee [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]]. If this Strike is successful, reduce the damage to your companion by your level, or twice your level if the Strike was a critical success.
