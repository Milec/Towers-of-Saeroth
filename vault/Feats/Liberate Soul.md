# Liberate Soul two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Soul Cycle|Soul Warden]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Soul Warden Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your spiral is glowing.

---

The light flows from your sigil of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]] to shatter the bonds imprisoning a soul. You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Possession|possession]] effect, or an effect, object, or spell that is imprisoning a soul (such as [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]], a _soul gem_, or a [[Bestiary/Devourer|devourer's]] devoured souls). You must be within 60 feet of the possessed creature or object, or of the receptacle imprisoning the soul. Your counteract level is half your level rounded up, and your counteract modifier is your divine spell DC – 10. If the attempt fails, you can't use Liberate Soul against that particular effect again unless circumstances have changed drastically, as determined by the GM.
