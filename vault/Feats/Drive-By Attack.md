You can smoothly line up an attack without disrupting your control. You [[Actions/Drive (Varies)|Drive]], gaining the effects of the Drive action for the same number of actions you spent to Drive- By Attack. At any point during your vehicle’s movement, you [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]].
