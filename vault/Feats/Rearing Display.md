# Rearing Display single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 71

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Requirements**
You are riding your mount.

---

You've trained your mount to make an impressive attack and can leverage its presence to terrify your enemies. You  [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] to order your mount to rear up and make a melee Strike against a creature within its reach. On a successful hit, you can attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] the target. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to this Intimidation check (+2 if the Strike was a critical hit).
