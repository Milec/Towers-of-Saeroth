# Thrash single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 91

**Requirements**
You have a foe grabbed.

---

You thrash the grabbed foe around. It takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier plus your weapon specialization damage plus your [[Actions/Rage]] damage. The foe must attempt a basic Fortitude save against your class DC.
