# Ultimate Mercy  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 115

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]] (Level 20)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mercy]]

---

Your mercy transcends the bounds of life and death. When you use Mercy, you can cast [[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]] on a creature that died since your last turn to return it to life. The target returns to life with 1 hit point and becomes wounded 1. You can’t use Ultimate Mercy if the triggering effect was [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] or a death effect.
