# Inner Strength single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 109

---

Your strength is part of your rage, so as long as your anger remains, you can gather your strength and rage to overcome any sort of enfeeblement. You reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition's value by 1.
