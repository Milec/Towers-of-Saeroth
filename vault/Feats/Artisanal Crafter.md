# Artisanal Crafter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 42

---

Like many minotaurs, you take pride in creating items made from stone or metal. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (or another skill of your choice, if you're already trained in Crafting) and gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting (Player Core)|Specialty Crafting]] skill feat in your choice of blacksmithing, leatherworking, or stonemasonry.
