# Unified Stance single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Beast Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beast Lord Dedication]]

---

You and your companion enter a unified fighting style that allows you to fight in tandem more succinctly. When you enter this stance, your united [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]] gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition and, when Commanded, can use the extra action to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
