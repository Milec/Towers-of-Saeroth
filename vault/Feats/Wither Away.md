# Wither Away two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 108

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]; [[Feats/Battle Harbinger Dedication]]

---

You hasten the passage of time for a living creature, prematurely aging them into decrepitude. A living creature within 60 feet takes 14d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage and a part of its body you chose withers, taking the following penalties depending on the result of their a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

- **Arms** The target’s arms grow weak. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1.

- **Head** The target has trouble thinking straight. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1.

- **Legs** The target’s legs buckle with every movement. The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds.

- **Torso** The target’s skin becomes thin and papery. The target gains [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 10 to slashing damage.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and the withering penalty lasts until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and the withering penalty lasts for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, and you can choose a second part of the body to wither. Both penalties last for 1 minute.
