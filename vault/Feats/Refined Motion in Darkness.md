# Refined Motion in Darkness  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 73

---

The ability to move elegantly and undetected through darkness aided your people as they fled the Netherworld. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] action, up to your Speed. In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check, you don’t become observed if you end a Sneak action in dim light or darkness, as long as you have [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] or greater cover or are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] at the end of your turn.
