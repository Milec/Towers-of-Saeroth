# Enjoy the Show  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 165

---

You address an ally or any onlookers to your combat, flaunting to your foes how little attention they require. When you [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]], you can choose single creature within 30 feet and use the following success, critical success, and critical failure effects instead of the normal results; if you do, Perform gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait, as well as the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] and [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] trait as normal for oration.

**Critical Success** The target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you until the end of its next turn.  

**Success** The target takes a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against creatures other then you until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The target gains a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls targeting you until the end of its next turn.
