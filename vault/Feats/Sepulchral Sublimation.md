# Sepulchral Sublimation single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 33

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Reanimator]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
ability to create or control [[Traits/Undead|undead]]; [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]] with a negative font, [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-335)|oracle]] of [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Bones|bones]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Necromancy|necromancer]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizard]]

**Requirements**
You have at least one undead [[Traits/Minion|minion]] that you permanently control.

---

You repurpose the negative energy that flows through your minions at a moment's notice. Instantly destroy one undead minion within 60 feet that is permanently under your control. If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] spell from a spell slot, you don't expend the slot. The spell's level must be half the destroyed minion's level or lower and the spell can't have a duration.

**Special** This feat has the trait matching your spellcasting tradition.
