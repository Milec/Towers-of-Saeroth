# Forward Gaze into Life two actions  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Vitality]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 79

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Epithets/Trespasser in Death’s Realm|trespasser in death’s realm]]

---

Despite the constant cold of death at your back, you keep your gaze forward into the realm of life. You release two 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cones]], aimed exactly opposite each other, one ahead of you that deals 5d10 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage and one behind you that deals 5d10 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage, each with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Creatures that critically fail also take 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]], of the same damage type dealt.
