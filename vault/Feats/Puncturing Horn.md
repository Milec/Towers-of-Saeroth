# Puncturing Horn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kashrishi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 43

---

While many kashrishi never use their horns for more than cracking open hard-shelled fruits or amplifying their psychic powers, you've practiced using yours offensively. You gain a horn unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your horn is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and has the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

**Special** If you have the [[Ancestries/Heritages/Xyloshi|xyloshi]] heritage, your horn instead deals 1d8 piercing damage.
