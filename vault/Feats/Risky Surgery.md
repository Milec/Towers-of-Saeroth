# Risky Surgery  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 208

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

---

Your surgery can bring a patient back from the brink of death, but might push them over the edge. When you [[Actions/Treat Wounds]], you can deal 1d8 slashing damage to your patient just before applying the effects of Treat Wounds. If you do, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check to Treat Wounds, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
