# Defensive Stratagem  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 109

---

By scrutinizing a foe, you learn how to better avoid its attacks. When you use [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]], you can choose a defensive stratagem instead of an attack or skill stratagem after you roll a d20.

 **Defensive Stratagem** You can't attempt to Strike the target until the start of your next turn. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all your defenses against effects caused by the target until the start of your next turn.
