# Expert Skysage Divination  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 238

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Basic Skysage Divination (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Basic Skysage Divination]]

---

Your studies of the stars have taught you about the mysteries of the cosmos. You choose one of the following 4th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Clairvoyance (Player Core)|_clairvoyance_]] or [[Spells/Read Omens (Player Core)|_read omens_]]. At 12th level, choose one of the following 5th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Mind Probe (Player Core)|_mind probe_]] or [[Spells/Scouting Eye|_scouting eye_]]. At 14th level, choose one of the following 6th-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Scrying (Player Core)|_scrying_]] or [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]]. You become an expert with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
