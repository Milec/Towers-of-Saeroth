# Preternatural Parry ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spell Parry (Impossible Magic)|Spell Parry]]

**Trigger**
You’re targeted by a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] or a spell attack, or you’re about to roll a saving throw against a spell effect.

**Requirements**
You have one or more hands free.

---

You snap your weapon or limb into place to ward off harm. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against the triggering effect until the start of your next turn.
