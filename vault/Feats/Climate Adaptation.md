# Climate Adaptation  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 77

**Access**
Pine leshies have access to this feat

---

Like evergreen trees, which stay healthy and vibrant from winter through summer, you too can adapt to a variety of extreme climates. You're unaffected by mild cold or heat environmental effects, and when you're in severe cold or heat, you take the effects of mild cold or heat instead (see [[Rules/Temperature|Table 10–13]]). This doesn't protect you from extreme or incredible cold or heat.
