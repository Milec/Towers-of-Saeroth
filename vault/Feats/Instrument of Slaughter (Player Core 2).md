# Instrument of Slaughter  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 99

**Prerequisites**
champion’s reaction that grants extra damage with your Strikes (including the desecration, iniquity, and obedience causes)

---

Your blessed armament thirsts for the blood of your foes. Whenever you critically hit an enemy with a Strike that has the extra damage from your champion's reaction, the target also takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to two of the weapon's damage dice.
