# Wayang Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 73

---

You can use your knowledge to enhance your performances or aid you in hiding. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Wayang Lore.
