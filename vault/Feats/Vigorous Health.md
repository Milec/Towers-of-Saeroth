# Vigorous Health  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 50

---

Your physique is robust and can withstand blood loss startlingly well. Whenever you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition, you can attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, you don’t gain the drained condition.
