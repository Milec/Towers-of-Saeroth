# Enchanting Shot two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eldritch Archer (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

With a single whisper carried on the wind, you enchant your ammunition to make a foe more vulnerable to your attacks. Make a bow or crossbow Strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d6 mental damage. On a critical hit, the target also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]]. The mental damage increases to 3d6 if your weapon has a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_greater striking rune_]], or to 4d6 if your weapon has a _major striking rune_.
