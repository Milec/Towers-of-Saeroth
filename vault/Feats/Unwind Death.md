# Unwind Death three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clockwork Reanimator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clockwork Reanimator Dedication]]

**Requirements**
The target's body must be mostly intact.

---

Combining necromancy with the mysterious temporal powers of clockwork, you wind a creature backward from the moment of its death. Attempt a DC 40 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check to revive a dead creature that has been dead for no more than 3 rounds. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]], except it still has the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition it had before dying, increased by 1 (or wounded 1 if it wasn't wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is then temporarily immune to Unwind Death for 1 day.
