# Bonded Animal  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 253

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

You forge strong connections with animals. You can spend 7 days of downtime regularly interacting with a normal animal (not a companion or other special animal) that is friendly or helpful to you. After this duration, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check against the animal's Will DC. If successful, you bond with the animal. The animal is permanently bonded to you until you form a bond with a different animal or do something egregious to break your bond.

A helpful animal is easier to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|direct]]. If your bonded animal is level –1, it gains the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait, allowing you to command it more efficiently. The maximum level of creature that can be your minion increases to 3 if you're a master in Nature and to 11 if you're legendary.

**Special** You can’t have both a bonded animal and an animal companion (though you can have both a bonded animal and a pet or familiar).
