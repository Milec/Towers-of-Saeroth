# Call Ursine Ally  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Summoned|Summoning]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ursine Avenger Hood (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Ursine Avenger Hood]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

You can cast a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Summon Animal (Player Core)|_summon animal_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] spell, but only to summon a [[Bestiary/Black Bear|black bear]]. At 10th level, the _summon animal_ spell is heightened to 4th rank, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Grizzly Bear (Monster Core)|grizzly bear]]. At 12th level, your _summon animal_ innate spell is heightened to 5th rank, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Polar Bear|polar bear]]. At 14th level, it’s heightened to 6th rank, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Cave Bear (Monster Core)|cave bear]].
