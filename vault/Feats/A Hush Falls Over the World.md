# A Hush Falls Over the World single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

Sound is absorbed in your waking world, covering everything in a shroud of quiet until the start of your next turn. Creatures in your waking world become hidden to [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise hearing]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise hearing]]. A creature in your waking world attempting to use spell or effect with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] trait must succeed at a DC 4 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]; on a failure, the spell or effect is [[Rules/Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupted]]. Anytime a creature in the area is affected by an auditory or sonic effect, they must attempt a DC 16 flat check. On a success, they are not affected by the auditory or sonic effect, though they still may be affected by other aspects of an ability (for example, a creature hit by a weapon with a [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|_thundering_]] rune would not take sonic damage but would still take the normal damage from the weapon).
