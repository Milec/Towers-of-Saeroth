# Shooting Stars Stance single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 120

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Monastic Weaponry (Player Core 2)|Monastic Weaponry]]

---

You enter a stance that lets you throw [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken (Tian Xia Character Guide)|shuriken]] with lightning speed. While in this stance, you can use your monk feats or monk abilities that normally require [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks with shuriken instead.
