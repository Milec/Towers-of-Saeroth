# Mistsoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 118

---

You have a connection to vaporous forms of water, such as fog and mist. You gain the Mist Blending reaction.

 **Mist Blending** ⤾ ([[Traits/Undine|undine]]) **Trigger** A creature attempts a flat check to target you because you're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] due to fog, haze, mist, or smoke; **Effect** You shroud yourself in mist, making it harder for your foe to hit you. If you're concealed, the DC of the flat check increases from 5 to 7; if you're hidden, it increases from 11 to 13.
