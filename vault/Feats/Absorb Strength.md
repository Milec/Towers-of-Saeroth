# Absorb Strength single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 18

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
You are adjacent to an enemy's corpse who has been dead for no longer than 1 minute.

---

You consume a piece of your enemy, absorbing their strength. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the enemy's level that last for 1 minute.
