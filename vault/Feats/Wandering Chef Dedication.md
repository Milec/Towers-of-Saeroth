# Wandering Chef Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 100

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wandering Chef]]

---

Whether trained in a noble kitchen or a humble home, you know how to cook delicious dishes and source ingredients from the wilderness or urban settings. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]; if you were already trained in Crafting, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can use [[Equipment/Cookware (Player Core)|cookware]] instead of an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist’s toolkit]] to craft alchemical foods. When using the [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] downtime activity, you can use Crafting or Cooking [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] in place of [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]], and if you roll a failure, you get a success instead.

You gain the [[Rules/Alchemical Archetypes (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]] benefits, but can use it only to create [[Rules/Consumables (GM Core)|consumables]], and the consumables must be [[Rules/Alchemical Foods (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|alchemical food]]. Any items you choose with Alchemical Crafting must be alchemical food, but they can be 1st level or 2nd level instead of only 1st level.

You create up to 4 [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|versatile vials]] during your daily preparations. Typically, a wandering chef’s versatile vials take the form of parcels of foraged ingredients.
