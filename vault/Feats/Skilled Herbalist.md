# Skilled Herbalist  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

---

You've been taught how to craft healing remedies with a variety of herbs and plants. You gain the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting (Player Core)|Alchemical Crafting]] feat, except you must select the following items to add to your formula book: [lesser antidote](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2879), [lesser antiplague](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2880), and [minor elixir of life](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2887), as well as a fourth 1st-level common alchemical formula of your choice. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] an [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]] item that has the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait, rather than [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]].
