# Genie Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Oread]], [[Traits/Suli]], [[Traits/Sylph]], [[Traits/Talos]], [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 99

---

You've trained with weapons used by your genie ancestors. You are trained with [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion|falchions]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Ranseur|ranseurs]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitars]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|tridents]]. In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Geniekin|geniekin]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial geniekin weapons are simple weapons and advanced geniekin weapons are martial weapons.
