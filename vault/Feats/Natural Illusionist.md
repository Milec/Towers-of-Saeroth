# Natural Illusionist  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 33

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Illusion Sense]]

---

By drawing upon the First World’s magic, you can siphon a portion of that malleable world to create a convincing illusion. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]], [[Spells/Item Facade|_item facade_]], or [[Spells/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]]. At 7th level, the spell is heightened to 2nd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the spell is heightened an additional spell level.
