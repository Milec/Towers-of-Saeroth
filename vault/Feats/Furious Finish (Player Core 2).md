# Furious Finish single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 77

---

Desperate to finish the fight, you pour all your rage into one final blow. Make a Strike. If it hits, you gain a circumstance bonus to damage equal to the number of rounds remaining in your Rage (maximum 10). After this Strike, your [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] immediately ends, and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] until you rest for at least 10 minutes.
