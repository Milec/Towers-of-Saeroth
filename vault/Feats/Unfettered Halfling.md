# Unfettered Halfling  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 52

---

You were forced into service as a laborer, either pressed into indentured servitude or shackled by the evils of slavery, but you've since escaped and have trained to ensure you'll never be caught again. Whenever you roll a success on a check to [[Actions/Escape]] or a saving throw against an effect that would impose the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] condition on you, you get a critical success instead. Whenever a creature rolls a failure on a check to [[Actions/Grapple]] you, they get a critical failure instead. If a creature uses the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] ability on you, it must succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to grab you instead of automatically grabbing you.
