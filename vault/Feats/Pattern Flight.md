# Pattern Flight two actions  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

---

You place a bit of magic in a physical projectile, causing it to fly in a runic pattern through the air once you loose it. Make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against a target within your weapon’s first [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment]]. Because of the erratic nature of its flight, this Strike ignores any circumstance bonus to AC from [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]]. After your Strike, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] on one target in a straight line between you and the target of your Strike (including the original target).
