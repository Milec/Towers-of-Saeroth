# Specialized Mount  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 165

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Incredible Mount]]

---

You've trained your mount to perform in unusual or even extreme circumstances, granting it abilities far beyond others of its kind. Your mount gains one [specialization](Companions.aspx?Type=Specialized) of your choice.

**Special** You can select this feat up to three times. Each time, add a different specialization to your mount. You mount can't have more than three specializations.
