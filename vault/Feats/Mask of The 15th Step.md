# Mask of The 15th Step  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 291

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Razmiran Priest]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Razmiran Priest Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

You have ascended to the 15th Step and stand among the greatest of [[Setting/Deities/Razmir|Razmir’s]] priests. You can [[Rules/Craft (GM Core)|Craft]] your [[Equipment/Razmiri Mask|Razmiri mask]] out of gold or upgrade your existing mask to a gold one in the same time it takes to Craft one made of iron. A gold Razmiri mask grants a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]], is a 14th-level item, and gains the following activation in addition to those possessed by the standard and silver masks.

**Activate—Call Upon Razmir’s Wrath** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You cry out to Razmir to reveal his fiery wrath, as he did upon the unbelievers at Melcat. You cast [[Spells/Sunburst (Player Core)|_sunburst_]] as an 8th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell, but for [[Bestiary/Adjustments/Undead|undead]] targets the spell has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait.
