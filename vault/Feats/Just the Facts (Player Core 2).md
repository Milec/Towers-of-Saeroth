# Just the Facts  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Thorough Research (Player Core 2)|Thorough Research]]

---

You fundamentally understand everything to the point where your research can't possibly be wrong. You are permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] and can use the extra action to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]. In addition, you gain the following benefits with Recall Knowledge.

- Your checks to Recall Knowledge are no longer [[Traits/Secret (Player Core)|secret]].
- When you Recall Knowledge, you use the outcome for one degree of success better than the result of your check.
- If an effect (such as [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge (Player Core)|Dubious Knowledge]]) would give you inaccurate information from your Recall Knowledge check, you know which information is inaccurate.
- When one of your allies Recalls Knowledge and gains false information, you also know that information is inaccurate if they share it with you.
