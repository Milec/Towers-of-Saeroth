# Skillful Tail  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

---

You’ve always had a tail, but with practice, you’ve learned to use it for more than signaling your mood. You can perform simple [[Actions/Interact]] actions with your tail, such as opening an unlocked door. Your tail can’t perform actions that require fingers or significant manual dexterity, including any action that would require a check to accomplish, and you can’t use it to hold items.
