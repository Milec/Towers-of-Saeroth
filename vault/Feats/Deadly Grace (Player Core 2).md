# Deadly Grace  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 169

---

Your graceful attacks are especially powerful. When you score a critical hit with an [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] melee weapon that has the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly]] trait, you double the number of dice from that trait. When you wield an agile or finesse melee weapon that doesn't have the deadly trait, it gains the deadly d8 trait instead.
