# Dodge Away ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 155

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You are the target of a melee attack.

**Requirements**
You're aware of the attack and aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]].

---

You use your acrobatic prowess to evade an attack, using momentum to keep yourself moving, if you choose. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can Step after the Strike. If you're a master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], you can move 10 feet on this Step instead of 5 feet.
