# Dwarven Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 36

---

Your kin have instilled in you an affinity for hard-hitting weapons, and you prefer these to more elegant arms. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick|pick]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer|warhammer]].

 You also gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Dwarf|dwarf]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial dwarf weapons are simple weapons and advanced dwarf weapons are martial weapons.
