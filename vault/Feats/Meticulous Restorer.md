# Meticulous Restorer  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

---

Waste not, want not; your steady eye allows you to restore craftwork quickly while conserving valuable material. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Repair (Player Core)|Quick Repair]] feat. In addition, when you roll a critical failure while attempting to [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] an item, you get a failure instead.
