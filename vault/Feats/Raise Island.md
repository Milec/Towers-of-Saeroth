# Raise Island three actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Exemplar]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 37

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Epithets/Born of the Bones of the Earth|born of the bones of the earth]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Epithets/Restless as the Tides|restless as the tides]]

---

You churn the sea and fish up stone so your enemies together can receive their punishment. Each enemy in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be swept up to 15 feet to another location of your choice within the affected area and become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn. Then, if you’re on a surface, a pillar of earth rises underneath you and lasts for 1 minute. The pillar is 10 feet tall, and its surface includes your space and a 5-foot emanation around you. After the pillar rises, you can attempt a melee Strike against one enemy in reach.
