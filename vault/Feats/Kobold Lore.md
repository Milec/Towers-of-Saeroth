# Kobold Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 14

---

You attentively learned key kobold survival strategies and mythology from your elders. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Kobold Lore]].
