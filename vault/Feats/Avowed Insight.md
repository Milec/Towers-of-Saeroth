# Avowed Insight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

**Prerequisites**
Yaksha heritage with an edict that requires you to confront a certain type of creature.

---

You become trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill corresponding to one creature type mentioned by your vow (for example, Fey Lore if your heritage is deny the firstborn pursuit). Whenever you successfully [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about this type of creature, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against that creature’s attacks and abilities for 1 round.
