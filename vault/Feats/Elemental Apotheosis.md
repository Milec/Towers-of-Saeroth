# Elemental Apotheosis  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kineticist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Nourishing Gate]]; exactly one kinetic element

---

You take on an elemental countenance, appearing like a type of elemental of your element. You gain any Speeds listed in the [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]] spell for your element and gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you don't already have it. You also gain the [[Traits/Elemental|elemental]] trait and the trait of your chosen element.
