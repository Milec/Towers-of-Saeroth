# Caustic Nectar  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait

---

You have specialized glands inside your throat, which produce acidic nectar that you can spit long distances. You gain a nectar ranged [[Rules/Unarmed Attacks (Secrets of Magic)|unarmed attack]] with a range increment of 20 feet that deals 1d4 acid damage. On a critical hit, the target is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. Your nectar doesn’t add [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effects. You’re [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to your own nectar.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
