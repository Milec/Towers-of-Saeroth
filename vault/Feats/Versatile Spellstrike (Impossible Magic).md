# Versatile Spellstrike  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

---

You can channel the entire realm of possibilities of your spellbook into an attack. Once during your daily preparations, you can use a spell slot to hold that infinite potential, rather than using it to prepare a spell. When you make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]], you can expend this special spell slot to immediately prepare and cast a spell from your spellbook at 2 ranks lower than the slot’s rank. (As normal for preparing spells, you can use a spell of an even lower rank, heightening it accordingly.) You don’t have any spell prepared in that slot until you cast from that slot.
