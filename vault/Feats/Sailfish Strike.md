# Sailfish Strike ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undersea Privateer Dedication]]; [[Feats/Propelled Leap]]

**Trigger**
You reach the top of your Propelled Leap.

---

You've learned how to convert your upward momentum into a powerful attack. At the top of your Propelled Leap, you can make a melee or ranged Strike as a reaction instead of choosing to [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]]. Your Strike gains a +4 circumstance bonus to damage. After your attack, hit or miss, you fall back into the water.
