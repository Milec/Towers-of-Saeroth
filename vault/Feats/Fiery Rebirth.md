# Fiery Rebirth free action  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 82

**Trigger**
You regain Hit Points while you are [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].

---

When you escape death, you return to life with retributive fire that burns those who harmed you. You expend 1 Mythic Point, return to consciousness, [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]], and regain additional Hit Points equal to your level. You then erupt in flames, dealing fire damage equal to twice your level to adjacent foes, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Foes that critically fail the save are also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet away from you.
