# Animal Elocutionist  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Burrow Elocutionist]]

---

You hear animal sounds as conversations instead of unintelligent noise, and can respond in turn. You can speak to all animals, not just animals with a burrow Speed. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on animals (which usually uses the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill).
