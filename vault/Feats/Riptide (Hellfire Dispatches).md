# Riptide single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Anchor Stance]]

**Requirements**
You're in Anchor Stance, and your target is within 5 feet of the edge of a water vehicle or already in the water.

---

You swirl water around you to make a current that pulls creatures closer. Attempt an Athletics check against the Fortitude DC of a creature within 20 feet. If the creature is willing, you increase your degree of success by one (turning a failure into a success and a success into a critical success).

**Critical Success** The target is pulled 10 feet toward you.  

**Success** The target is pulled 5 feet toward you.  

**Critical Failure** Your movements cause you to be [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the beginning of your next turn.
