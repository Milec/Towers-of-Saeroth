# Heal Companion  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 132

**Prerequisites**
an animal companion

---

You have a deep devotion to your animal companion that enables you to magically heal their wounds. You gain the [[Spells/Heal Companion|_heal companion_]] warden spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point.
