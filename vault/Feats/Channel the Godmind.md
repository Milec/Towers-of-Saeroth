# Channel the Godmind two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aphorite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 77

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You tap into the Godmind's collective cognitive well, rapidly parsing near-infinite variables to read your surroundings. For 1 minute, you gain precise motion sense out to 60 feet, you can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects, and you gain the effects of a 9th-level [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]].
