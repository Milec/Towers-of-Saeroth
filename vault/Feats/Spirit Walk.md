# Spirit Walk  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apparition Sense]]

---

Your allied apparitions ward you against the predations of their restless peers. You and allies in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain a +2 status bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] checks about [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirits]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]], and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. While you’re [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]] or [[Actions/Detect Magic (Player Core)|Detecting Magic]] in exploration mode, this bonus also applies to AC and saves against reactions any of you trigger from haunts and spirits. During your first turn in an encounter, you and allies in the aura have resistance equal to half your level against damage dealt by haunts or spirits.
