# Ricochet Shot single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 117

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Bullet Dancer]] (Level 14), [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer]] (Level 14)

---

You bounce your shot off a nearby surface to strike your opponent from an unexpected angle. When determining whether a target has [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from your ranged attack with a firearm or crossbow, you can choose one solid surface within your weapon’s first range increment to ricochet your shot off of. If you do, you determine the target’s cover from the point where you ricochet your shot instead of from you.
