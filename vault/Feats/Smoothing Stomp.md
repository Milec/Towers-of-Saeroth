# Smoothing Stomp two actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 15

---

You stomp and unleash a wave of the magic of creation in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that transforms non-magical [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain into normal terrain. This terrain transformation remains in place for 1 minute, after which the terrain returns to its original state.

Your stomp can also [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] magical difficult terrain and magical greater difficult terrain. Your stomp’s counteract rank equals half your level (rounded up), and for the roll, use either your class DC – 10 or your spellcasting attribute modifier plus your spellcasting proficiency bonus. If you successfully counteract the triggering effect, you suppress the effect for 1 minute, after which the effect returns if its duration hasn’t expired.
