# Betraying Shank single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Ilverani and the Stars|Twilight Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Speaker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are within melee reach of a target, but it is not in combat with you.

---

While your usual diplomacy is well intentioned, you know that sometimes those same skills will need to be turned to deception. In a flash, you draw a sheathed or concealed [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] weapon and make a melee Strike. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against your Strike. You then roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] for initiative.
