# Shiny Button Eyes  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 64

---

You've polished your eyes to reflect the smallest details in dimmest light. You gain the [[Feats/Canny Acumen]] skill feat as a bonus feat, but you must choose Perception. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks against [[Traits/Visual|visual]] [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]].
