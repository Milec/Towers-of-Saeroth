# Snare Setter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You are adept at the time-honored kobold art of trap making. You gain the trained proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you were already trained in Crafting, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Kobold|kobold]] snares. You gain the [[Feats/Snare Crafting]] feat, though when choosing your formulas for that feat, you can also choose from uncommon kobold snares, as well as common snares.
