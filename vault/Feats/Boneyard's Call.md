# Boneyard's Call  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

You have a powerful connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] and its courts, granting you the ability to travel to and from the Boneyard. You gain [[Spells/Plane Shift|_plane shift_]] as a divine innate spell. You can cast it twice per week. This can be used only to travel to the Boneyard or from the Boneyard to the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]]. Due to your unique connection to the Boneyard, your body serves as the focus, and you don't require a tuning fork.
