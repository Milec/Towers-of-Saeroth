# Clockwork Celerity free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 28

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Armor|armor]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Construct|construct]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Weapon|weapon innovation]]

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

You can use unstable clockwork devices in your innovation to push your invention to act more quickly. You are quickened for this turn. How you can use the extra action depends on your innovation.

- **Armor** You can use the extra action to [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], or use another movement action granted by your innovation (such as [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] if you have the [[Feats/Diving Armor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Diving Armor]] feat).
- **Construct** You can use the extra action to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command]] your construct innovation (or to provide 1 of the actions if you spend 2 actions to Command your construct).
- **Weapon** You can use the extra action to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with your innovation or [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|Reload]] your innovation.
