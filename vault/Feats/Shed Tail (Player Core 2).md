# Shed Tail ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Iruxi Armaments]]

**Trigger**
You become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]].

**Requirements**
You have a fully grown tail.

---

You can shed a portion of your tail to escape. You cease being grabbed, then Stride without triggering any reactions from the creature that grabbed you. It takes 1 day for your tail to fully grow back. Until it does, you can't use your tail unarmed attack, and you take a –2 circumstance penalty on checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]].
