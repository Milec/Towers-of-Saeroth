# Familiar Form  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 48

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellmaster Dedication]]

---

You can use your magic to transform into a small creature of a type that commonly serves as a spellcaster's familiar, potentially allowing you to safely scout for your party while retaining a degree of spellcasting ability. You gain the [[Spells/Familiar Form|_familiar form_]] focus spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.
