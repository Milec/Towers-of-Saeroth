# Basic Skysage Divination  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oatia Skysage Dedication]]

---

The stars move, granting you a sliver of their magic. Choose a 1st-level spell from any spell list to add to your spell repertoire. You can Cast this Spell as an occult Oatia skysage spell.

 At 6th level, you gain a 2nd-level spell, and at 8th level, you gain a 3rd-level spell. Each of these spells must be from the [[Traits/Divination|divination]] school.
