# Master Skysage Divination  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Expert Skysage Divination]]

---

You unlock the most powerful secrets hidden in the stars above. You learn a 7th-level spell from any spell list. At 18th level, you learn an 8th-level spell, and at 20th level, you learn a 9th-level spell. Each of these spells must come from the [[Traits/Divination|divination]] school. You gain master proficiency with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
