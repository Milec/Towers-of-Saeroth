# Choking Smoke  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Fruits of Research|Spell Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Smoldering Explosion]]

---

When you cast [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]] heightened to at least 6th level, you can modify the spell's standard effects as follows: Reduce the spell's damage by 6d6. Your fireball leaves behind a lingering cloud of toxic smoke in its area. While it's smoke instead of mist, this cloud otherwise has the effects of [[Spells/Stinking Cloud|_stinking cloud_]].
