# Contortionist  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 155

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]

---

You can squeeze out of tight situations surprisingly quickly, gaining an advantage against foes that try to pin you down. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Squeeze]] skill feat, and if you're a master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], you can Squeeze at full Speed. Whenever you successfully [[Actions/Escape]] using Acrobatics, the creature you Escaped from is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the next attack you make against it before the end of your next turn.
