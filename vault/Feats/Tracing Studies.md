# Tracing Studies  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 90

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runesmith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Runesmith Dedication]]

---

You can now create runes in the midst of combat. You gain the Trace Rune action.

**Trace Rune** single action or two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Runesmith|runesmith]]) **Requirements** You're holding or wearing an [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|artisan's toolkit]]; **Effect** You apply one rune to an adjacent target matching the rune's usage entry. You can have any number of runes traced at a time, but each rune remains only until the end of your next turn. If you spend 2 actions to Trace a Rune, you draw the rune in the air, causing it to appear on a target within 30 feet.
