# Quick Fix  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 217

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Talisman Dabbler (Player Core 2)|Talisman Dabbler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Talisman Dabbler Dedication (Player Core 2)|Talisman Dabbler Dedication]]

---

You can attach a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talisman]] using only a bit of glue and some string. You gain the [[Feats/Rapid Affixture]] skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. When you use it, you can affix or remove up to four talismans in 1 minute instead of just one.  
You gain the ability to [[Actions/Affix a Talisman (GM Core)|Affix a Talisman]] as a 3-action activity from that feat at 12th level, regardless of your [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] proficiency.
