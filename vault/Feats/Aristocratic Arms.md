# Aristocratic Arms  *Feat 4*

Through consistent (and often punishing) practice, you’ve learned how to use your house’s preferred weapons. You have familiarity with either one martial weapon of your choice; two simple or martial weapons of your choice from your house’s preferred weapons list; or one advanced weapon of your choice from your house’s preferred weapons list. For the purpose of proficiency, treat any of these selections that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons. If you’re at least an expert in one of the selected weapons, you get the critical specialization effect of that weapon when you get a critical hit with it.

**Charthagnion**: Weapons with the [[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]] or [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait  
**Elliendo**: [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling]] weapons and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knives]]  
**Henderthane**: [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|Hammers]] and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick|picks]]  
**Jeggare**: One-handed [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|swords]]  
**Leroung**: [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|Clubs]] and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shields]]  
**Narikopolus**: [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|Bows]]  
**Sarini**: [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail (Player Core)|Flails]]  
**Thrune**: [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|Clubs]] and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbows]]

In addition, you gain the For My House! action.

**For My House!** free action **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** You announce your house affiliation, either by declaring it aloud (giving this ability the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait) or openly displaying your house heraldry (giving this ability the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait). Until the beginning of your next turn, your weapon Strikes and spells deal an additional 1 damage to any creature that witnessed the display. If you’re an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Cheliax Lore]], Genealogy Lore, Heraldry Lore, [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]], or Warfare Lore, the additional damage increases to 2. The damage increases to 3 if you’re a master in the skill or 4 if you’re legendary in the skill.
