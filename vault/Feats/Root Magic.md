# Root Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 208

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]

---

Your talismans ward against foul magic. During your daily preparations, you can assemble a small pouch with bits of herbs, hair, sacred oils, and other ritual ingredients, which you give to one ally. The first time that day the ally attempts a saving throw against a spell or [[Traits/Haunt|haunt]], they gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the roll. This bonus increases to +2 if you're an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or +3 if you're legendary.
