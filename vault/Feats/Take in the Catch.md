# Take in the Catch two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marine Marauder Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re holding a [[Equipment/Net (Player Core)|net]].

---

Your skills in marine fishing can also be applied to combat. Attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] a creature with your net, with the normal results. Then you [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to draw a weapon, and [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] the creature you attempted to grapple. If you succeeded on your check to Grapple, the creature remains [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] after your Step.
