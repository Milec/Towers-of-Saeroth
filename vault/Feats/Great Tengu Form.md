# Great Tengu Form  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 27

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Long-Nosed Form]]

---

You take on the imposing form of a large, winged oni. Once per day, as part of using Long-Nosed Form, you also gain the benefits of 4th-level [[Spells/Enlarge|_enlarge_]] and [[Spells/Fly|_fly_]]. This lasts for 5 minutes or until you shift out of your Long-Nosed Form, whichever happens first.
