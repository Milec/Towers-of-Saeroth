# Shieldmarshal Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 109

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) As usual for archetypes, players who take the Shieldmarshal dedication feat can't select another dedication feat until they have gained two other feats from the Shieldmarshal archetype._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Shieldmarshal]]

**Access**
Characters from Alkenstar have access to this archetype

---

Your elite shieldmarshal training makes you equally at home in the squalor of Smokeside and the splendor of Skyside. You're intimately familiar with the city's labyrinthine streets and statutes. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert in Society instead. In urban environments, you can attempt Society checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction]]. You also gain the [[Feats/Courtly Graces]] and [[Feats/Streetwise]] skill feats.
