# Halfling Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 52

---

You favor traditional halfling weapons, so you've learned how to use them more effectively. You have the trained proficiency with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Frying Pan (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|frying pan]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling|sling]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Halfling Sling Staff|halfling sling staff]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]]. You gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Halfling|halfling]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial halfling weapons are simple weapons and advanced halfling weapons are martial weapons.
