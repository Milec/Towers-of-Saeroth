# Gorilla Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 128

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

---

You lower yourself to the ground and take an imposing, knuckle-walking stance. While in this stance, the only Strikes you can make are gorilla slam unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]], [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]], [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. While you are in Gorilla Stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb]], and if you roll a success on an Athletics check to Climb, you get a critical success instead.
