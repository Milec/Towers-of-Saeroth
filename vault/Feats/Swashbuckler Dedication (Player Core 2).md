# Swashbuckler Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass (Player Core)|Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 182

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Prerequisites**
Charisma +2; Dexterity +2

---

Choose a swashbuckler's style. You gain the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-1002)|panache and stylish combatant]] class features, and you apply the bravado trait to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] and any actions indicated in your swashbuckler style, allowing you to gain panache. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or the skill associated with your style. If you were already trained in both skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in swashbuckler class DC. You don't gain any other effects of your chosen style.
