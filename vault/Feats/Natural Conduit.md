# Natural Conduit single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 59

**Prerequisites**
warden spells

**Requirements**
You have line of effect to your animal companion or another [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly (Player Core)|friendly]] [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] within 60 feet.

---

You can use an animal as a conduit for your magic. If the next action you use is to Cast a Spell that has a range, the spell uses the animal as its origin point.
