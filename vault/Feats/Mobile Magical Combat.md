# Mobile Magical Combat single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 48

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellmaster Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a melee weapon and have Cast a Spell requiring at least 1 action this turn.

---

You dart around the battlefield, casting spells while you carefully make melee attacks with your weapon. Stride once. If you end your movement within melee reach of at least one enemy, you can make a melee Strike against that enemy. You can use Mobile Magical Combat while [[Actions/Burrow|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
