# Timber Sentinel two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 34

---

A slim, symmetrical tree travels from the Plane of Wood. You conjure a tree within 30 feet, as a [[Spells/Protector Tree|_protector tree_]] spell of a rank equal to half your level rounded up. If you use this impulse again, any previous one ends, and an ordinary tree remains.
