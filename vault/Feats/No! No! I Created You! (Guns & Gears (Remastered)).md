# No! No! I Created You! ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lepidstadt Surgeon]] (Level 6)

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[construct companion](Rules.aspx?ID=1600)

**Trigger**
Your construct companion would become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]].

---

You appeal to your construct companion's bond with its creator to have it break free of a controlling effect. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the effect that confused or controlled your construct companion, using your [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] modifier for the counteract check and half your level rounded up for the counteract level.
