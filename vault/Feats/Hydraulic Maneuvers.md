# Hydraulic Maneuvers single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 15

---

You batter your foes with a torrent of water siphoned from your surroundings. If the next action you take is an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]], that action has a range of 15 feet. You don't receive any item bonuses to that check or any circumstance bonuses related to your physical body, but if you roll a critical failure on that Athletics check, you get a failure instead.
