# Born to the Trees  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 73

---

You adapt a creature to live among the trees, improving its ability to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|climb]], [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|balance]], and [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|leap]]. Target a willing creature within 30 feet. For 10 minutes, it gains a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] equal to its Speed and a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to Balance and Athletics checks to [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] and [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]]. When it Leaps, it increases the distance it can Leap horizontally by 5 feet and vertically by 2 feet. If you use Born to the Trees again, any existing one ends.

---
**Level (6th)** You can target up to 5 willing creatures.
