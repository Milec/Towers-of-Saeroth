# Living God  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 291

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Razmiran Priest]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Razmiran Priest Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

You have reached heights of power equaling [[Setting/Deities/Razmir|Razmir’s]] himself. You can [[Rules/Craft (GM Core)|Craft]] your [[Equipment/Razmiri Mask|Razmiri mask]]  out of porcelain laced with precious metals, or upgrade your existing mask to a porcelain one in the same time it takes to Craft one made of iron. A porcelain Razmiri mask grants a +4 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]], is an 18th-level item, and is an apex item that increases your Charisma modifier by 1 or increases it to +4, whichever would give you a higher score. It also gives you the following activation in addition to those granted by the standard, silver, and gold masks.

**Activate—Power of the Living God** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You demand power from the world, using your mask as a locus to force reality to bend to your will. You cast [[Spells/Manifestation|_manifestation_]] as a 10th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell, but no matter what spell you emulate with it, that spell has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait.
