# Engine of Destruction three actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 32

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Construct|construct innovation]]

---

Your construct unleashes a broad swath of devastation by separating its limbs, deploying hidden armaments, or using a similar technique to wreak havoc. You [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command]] your innovation. Instead of its normal actions, it [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] once, then makes a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against each foe within 30 feet of it with a +2 circumstance bonus to its attack rolls. The multiple attack penalty doesn’t increase until after the construct makes all the attacks.
