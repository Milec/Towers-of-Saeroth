# First to Strike, First to Fall  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 19

---

Whenever you successfully Strike a creature that has not acted in the first round of combat, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn. If that creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points before the end of your next turn, you and all allies within 30 feet of the creature become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] until the end of your next turn. You can use the extra action only to Step, Stride, or Strike.
