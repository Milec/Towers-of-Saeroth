# Radiant Armament  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
blessed armament

---

Your blessed armament radiates power, further enhancing your chosen weapon. When you choose the weapon for your blessed armament during your daily preparations, add the [[Equipment/Astral|_astral_]] and [[Equipment/Brilliant (GM Core)|_brilliant_]] property runes to the list of effects you can choose from. If you're [[Traits/Holy|holy]], also add the [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|_holy_]] rune, and if you're [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], also add the [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|_unholy_]] rune.

 In addition, you can change the rune you've selected for the day to a different rune from your list as a 10-minute activity that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], and [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|exploration]] traits. Changing the rune doesn't restore abilities that can be used only a limited number of times, such as Holy Healing for the _holy_ rune.
