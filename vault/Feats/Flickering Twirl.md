# Flickering Twirl single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lion Blade Dedication (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade Dedication]]

You use your movements and disguises to make yourself a flickering blur. You gain [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]] until the beginning of your next turn. As usual for concealment involving an obvious visual manifestation, you can’t use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]].
