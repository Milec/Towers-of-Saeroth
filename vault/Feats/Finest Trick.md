# Finest Trick  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 63

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Devil in Plain Sight]]

---

Many fiends—[[Bestiary/Raja Rakshasa|rakshasa rajas]] and [[Bestiary/Succubus|demonic succubi]] most famously—can take on the forms of others. With practice, you can do the same. When you use Devil in Plain Sight, you can alter your appearance to match a generic member of your base ancestry, rather than one that's obviously you without your tieflings features. In addition, you can imitate a specific person's appearance (of any ancestry of the same size and kind as your base ancestry, typically a [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] for most ancestries) if you consume a bit of their blood, a lock of their hair, or appropriate equivalents as part of the action. You still must know what the creature looks like, and you must be aware that the consumed portion comes from their body.
