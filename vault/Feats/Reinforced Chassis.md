# Reinforced Chassis  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 40

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) An automaton character with the Reinforced Chassis feat uses their unarmored proficiency._

---

Your body is designed to be particularly resilient. Your chassis is medium armor in the [[Equipment/Armor Groups/Plate|plate]] armor group that grants a +4 item bonus to AC, a Dex cap of +1, a check penalty of –2, a speed penalty of –5 feet, and a Strength value of 16. You can never wear other armor or remove your chassis; however, you still don't become fatigued from sleeping. Finally, you can etch armor runes onto your chassis as normal.

**Enhancement** Your chassis becomes difficult to overcome. You gain the Chassis Deflection reaction.
