# Hidden Thorn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 37

---

Some flowers can hide their thorns, and yours happen to be hidden along your arms. You gain a thorns unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your thorns are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]] weapon group and have the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
