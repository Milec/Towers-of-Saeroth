# Read Disaster  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]], [[Traits/Prediction (Player Core)|Prediction]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 141

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Time Mage]] (Level 10), [[Archetypes/Time Mage (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage]] (Level 10)

---

You spend 10 minutes and open yourself to the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] mysteries of the world, peering into the most sinister portents of the future. You gain the effects of [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]], except you learn only about the dangers ahead; any results of “good” are instead “nothing,” and results of “mixed” are instead “bad.” If you are legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], you take only 1 minute to [[Feats/Read Disaster]].
