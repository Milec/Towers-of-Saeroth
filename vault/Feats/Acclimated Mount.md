# Acclimated Mount  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 71

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Faithful Steed]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

---

By the grace of your god, your mount can overcome the natural challenges and hazards present in a specific terrain. Choose  aquatic, arctic, desert, forest, mountain, plains, sky, swamp, or underground. Your mount must have a swim Speed to select  aquatic, and a fly Speed to select sky. Your mount ignores the effects of [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] while in the selected terrain.  

If you become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]], you can pray during your daily preparations each day to change the type of terrain to which your mount is acclimated.
