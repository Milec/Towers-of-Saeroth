# Arcane Sense  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 258

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]]

---

Your study of magic allows you to instinctively sense its presence. You can cast 1st-level [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] at will as an arcane innate spell. If you’re a master in Arcana, the spell is heightened to 3rd level; if you’re legendary, it is heightened to 4th level.
