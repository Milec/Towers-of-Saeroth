# Poisoner's Twist single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 210

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Poisoner Dedication (Player Core 2)|Poisoner Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a successful melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] that dealt damage against a target afflicted by a poison, and you know which poison.

---

Poisons attack specific parts of the body—one venom might target the lungs, another the circulatory system, while another deteriorates the nerves. You know how to take advantage of such weaknesses. You deal 1d6 damage of the required [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]'s damage type and 1d6 [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage to the target. If you're at least 18th level, you deal 2d6 damage of each type.
