# Vellumis Excision  *Feat 16*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Halcyon Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Halcyon Spellcasting Adept]]

**Access**
You’re a member of the Vellumis Scholars.

---

You seek to excise the rot from the Gravelands and have made progress to do so. You can cast [[Spells/Field of Life (Player Core)|_field of life_]] as a 6th-rank halcyon spell, and you gain a 6th-rank halcyon spell slot. When you cast your halcyon _field of life_ as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] spell, any living creatures in the area when you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curses]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]], and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] for 1 round.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Halcyon Spellcasting Sage]] feat, you treat the _field of life_ halcyon spell gained from this feat as a signature spell, even though halcyon spells normally can’t be signature spells.
