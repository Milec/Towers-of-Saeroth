# Mobile Thralls  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 35

---

Your thralls can now follow you wherever you go. Any thrall you create has your choice of a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] or [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]]. The additional Speed is equal to its land Speed, and any time you let the thrall [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], you can have it use the relevant movement action instead.

At 8th level, add a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] to your choices. A flying thrall remains airborne at the end of your turn even if it didn’t [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] that turn.
