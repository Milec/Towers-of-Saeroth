# Expanded Senses  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 67

---

Your eidolon evolves more acute senses. Your eidolon gains [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] and [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], as well as [[Rules/Scent|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet.
