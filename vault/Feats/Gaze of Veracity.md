# Gaze of Veracity  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 85

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hellknight Signifer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Signifer Dedication]]; ability to cast focus spells

---

You can channel your mask’s power to pierce through lies and see the truth in any situation. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. While wearing your mask, you can cast the [[Spells/Glimpse the Truth|_glimpse the truth_]] cleric domain spell as a divine focus spell.
