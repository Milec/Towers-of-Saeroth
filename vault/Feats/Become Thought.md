# Become Thought  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Psychic]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 29

---

You shed some of your material form, becoming a being of pure thought. This has the following effects.

- You gain resistance 10 to physical damage but weakness 5 to mental damage and weakness 10 to any effect that deals damage by targeting the spirit, such as [[Spells/Spirit Blast|_spirit blast_]].
- You can change your appearance on a whim. This takes a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, has the effects of [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]], and lasts until you change your appearance again.
- Once per year, when you die, you automatically return to life the next day as long as one living sentient creature on your plane remembers you; the memories enable you to reconstruct yourself from their thoughts. You appear in the vicinity of the creature that remembers you most strongly.
