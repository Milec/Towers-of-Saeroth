# Spirit of Vigil  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 112

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/No Stranger to Death]]

---

Though the city of Vigil itself has been destroyed, you will carry on its warrior spirit to the end of your days. The value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition at which you die increases by 1 (normally to dying 5, or dying 6 if you have [[Feats/Diehard (Player Core)|Diehard]]), and the reduction to your maximum dying value from the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition is 1 less severe (to a minimum of 0).
