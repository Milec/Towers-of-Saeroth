# Boost Summons  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

Augmenting your eidolon extends to creatures you summon. When you cast [[Spells/Boost Eidolon|_boost eidolon_]], in addition to your eidolon, it also targets your [[Traits/Summoned (Player Core)|summoned]] creatures within 60 feet.
