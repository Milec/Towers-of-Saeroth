# Dread Marshal Stance single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 204

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

Putting on a grim face for the battle ahead, you encourage your allies to strike fear into their foes with vicious attacks. When you use this action, attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check. The DC is usually an easy DC of your level, but the GM can assign a different DC based on the circumstances. The effect depends on the result of your check.

**Success** Your marshal’s aura grants you and your allies in the aura a +1 status bonus to damage rolls. When you or an ally in the aura critically hit an enemy with a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], that enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. If you’re wielding a weapon that has more than one damage die (typically due to a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] rune), you can have the status bonus equal the weapon’s number of damage dice instead of +1.  

**Failure** You fail to enter the stance.  

**Critical Failure** You fail to enter the stance and can’t take this action again for 1 minute.
