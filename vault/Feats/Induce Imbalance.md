# Induce Imbalance two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 90

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Five-breath Vanguard]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Five-breath Vanguard Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're in an elemental stance.

---

Your blows can disrupt the delicate balance of elemental energies that keep a body in good health. [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] the target using the unarmed attack associated with your current elemental stance. On a success, the target must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 until the end of your next turn. On a critical failure, the target is clumsy 3 for 1 minute. Elementals take a –2 circumstance penalty to their save.
