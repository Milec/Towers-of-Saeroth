# Hit and Run two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Guerrilla Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Requirements**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by your target.

---

Leaping from a hiding place, you quickly attack your foe before retreating. You Stride or Step, then attempt a melee or  ranged Strike against a creature. After your Strike, you [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] away. None of the movement taken as part of this activity  triggers reactions.
