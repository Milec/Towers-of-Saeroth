# Touch of the Sea  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

---

The power of the sea flows through you, allowing you to manifest a portion of its strength. Choose either the [[Spells/Gale Blast (Player Core 2)|_gale blast_]] or [[Spells/Spout (Player Core 2)|_spout_]] cantrip. You can cast the chosen spell as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
