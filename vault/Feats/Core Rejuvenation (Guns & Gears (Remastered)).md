# Core Rejuvenation free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 43

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You have the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition and are about to attempt a recovery check.

---

Your soul taps into your core's power to push against the grasp of death and allow you to recover consciousness. You're restored to 1 Hit Point, lose the dying and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] conditions, and can act normally on this turn. You gain or increase the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition as normal when losing the dying condition in this way.

**Enhancement** Your soul can draw even more power from your core, granting you additional benefits. When you use Core Rejuvenation, you also gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to three times your level. These Hit Points remain for 1 minute.
