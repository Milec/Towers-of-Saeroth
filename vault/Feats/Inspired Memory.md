# Inspired Memory  *Feat 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 96

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

---

A memory of each spell you have absorbed remains within your soul. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about creatures whose magic you have absorbed within the last minute. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]] that you have absorbed within the last week.
