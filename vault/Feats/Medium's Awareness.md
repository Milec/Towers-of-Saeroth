# Medium's Awareness  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

---

Your apparitions watch over you. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks made to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] and when using Perception for your [[Rules/Initiative|initiative]] roll. At 12th level, this status bonus increases to +3, and at 20th level, to +4.
