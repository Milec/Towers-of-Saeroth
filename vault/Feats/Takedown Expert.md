# Takedown Expert  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 60

---

You've mastered combat practices that let you get up close and bring down perpetrators alive. You can use your Intelligence modifier on attack rolls when you [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem]] if you're using a weapon of the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]] group in one hand (like a [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap|sap]]), in addition to the usual weapons, and those Strikes qualify for your strategic strike. Additionally, you can make any of your Strikes [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] without taking the normal –2 penalty.
