# Wrapped in Smoke single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Munitions Master]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Munitions Master Dedication]]

---

You ignite a small portion of powder in a rapid, inefficient burn, creating a hazy cloud. You create a cloud of smoke in a 5-foot  [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] centered on your innovation, which lasts for 1 minute. All creatures and objects within the smoke become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and those outside the smoke become concealed to those within.
