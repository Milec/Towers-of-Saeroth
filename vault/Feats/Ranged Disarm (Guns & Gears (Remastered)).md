# Ranged Disarm single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 132

[[Feats/Bullet Dancer Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Bullet Dancer Dedication]]; [[Feats/Trick Shot (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trick Shot]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a simple firearm.

---

When using a simple firearm, you can shoot objects right out of your enemy's hands. Attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] the target using a ranged attack roll with your simple firearm, instead of attempting an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the target's Reflex DC.
