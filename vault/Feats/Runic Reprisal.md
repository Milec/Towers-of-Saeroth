# Runic Reprisal  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fortifying Knock]]

---

When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise your Shield]], you can bury a runic trap into it, which is set off by the clash of an enemy weapon. When you use Fortifying Knock, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] with a damaging invocation on your shield, even if it normally couldn’t be applied to a shield. The traced rune doesn’t have its normal effect, instead fading into your shield. If you [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] with the shield against a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], you can Invoke the Rune as part of the reaction, causing the rune to detonate outward and apply its invocation effect to the attacking creature.
