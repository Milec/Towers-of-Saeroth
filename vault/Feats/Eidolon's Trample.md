# Eidolon's Trample three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

---

Your eidolon [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] up to double their Speed and can move through the spaces of creatures at least one size smaller, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample (Monster Core)|Trampling]] each creature whose space they enter. They can attempt to Trample the same creature only once in a single Trample. Your eidolon deals bludgeoning damage equal to the damage of their secondary attack against these creatures, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC.
