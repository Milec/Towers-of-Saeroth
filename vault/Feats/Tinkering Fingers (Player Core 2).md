# Tinkering Fingers  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 30

---

You're trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]. If you are trained in Crafting from another source (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] an item without using a [[Equipment/Repair Toolkit|repair toolkit]] without taking the –2 circumstance penalty, improvising tools from whatever you have at hand. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Crafting checks to Repair an item when you have a repair toolkit.
