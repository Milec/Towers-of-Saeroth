# Creature Comforts ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal|animal instinct]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Untamed|untamed order]]

**Trigger**
You become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]].

You’re under a [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|morph]] or [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect.

You've found comfort in the forms of other creatures, insulating yourself from feelings of fear. Reduce your frightened condition value by 1 (to a minimum of 0).
