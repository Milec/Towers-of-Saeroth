# Fascinated by Society  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 25

---

Just as some humanoids find themselves driven to study nature, you are obsessed with the artificial constructs of society and can't get enough. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in Society, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 As a newcomer, sometimes your understanding is a bit off target. When you fail, but don't critically fail, a Society check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] or [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], you learn the correct information (as you would on a success) and erroneous information (as you would on a critical failure). You don't have any way to differentiate which is which.
