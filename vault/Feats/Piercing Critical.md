# Piercing Critical  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 119

---

Your attacks with your preferred weapons deal especially devastating wounds. When you [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a firearm or crossbow for which you have legendary proficiency, you critically succeed if you roll a 19 on the die, as long as that result is a success.
