# Fluttering Misdirection  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]

---

You’ve trained in drawing the eyes of your audience to specific aspects of your performance through the careful manipulation of your fans and body. While wielding a fan, you can snap open, flutter, or otherwise manipulate the fan to briefly distract observers around you, giving you and adjacent allies a constant +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to secretly [[Actions/Conceal an Object (Player Core)|Conceal an Object]] and to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] or [[Actions/Palm an Object (Player Core)|Palm an Object]].
