# Pristine Weapon  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 124

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Verdant Weapon]]

---

Your [[Feats/Verdant Weapon|verdant weapon]] can cut through the resistances of magical creatures. The weapon is treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] and [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]. If you critically hit a creature that has a weakness to cold iron or silver, the target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] as the primal energies within your weapon slow its natural healing.
