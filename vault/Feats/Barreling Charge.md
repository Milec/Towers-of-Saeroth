# Barreling Charge two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 108

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Wylderheart]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You rush forward, moving enemies aside to reach your foe. You Stride, attempting to move through your enemies' spaces and make a melee Strike. Roll an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check and compare the result to the Fortitude DC of each creature whose space you attempt to move through during your Stride, moving through its space on a success but ending your movement before entering its space on a failure. You can use Barreling Charge to [[Actions/Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim]] instead of Stride, as long as you have the corresponding movement type.
