# Quick Contacts  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 208

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]; [[Feats/Connections]] or [[Feats/Criminal Connections]]

---

You know where to go, who to talk to, and how to make new connections, fast. Upon entering a new settlement, spending 1 day of downtime allows you to build enough connections to make use of the Connections or Underworld Connections feats. If you're legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]], you can form the required connections within 1 hour of entering a new settlement.
