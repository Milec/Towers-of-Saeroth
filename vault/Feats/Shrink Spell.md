# Shrink Spell  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 109

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Counterspell (Player Core)|Counterspell]]

---

You’ve learned to manipulate space to narrow the impact of other spells. When you use [[Feats/Counterspell (Player Core)|Counterspell]] to attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] a spell that has an area of a [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], or [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] and does not have a duration, you reduce the area of the spell if you fail to counteract it. Remove 5 feet from the radius of a burst or [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] or 10 feet from the length of a cone or line.
