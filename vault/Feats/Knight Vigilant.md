# Knight Vigilant  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]; any good alignment; member of the Knights of Lastwall of knight rank

**Access**
A character who is a member of the Knights of Lastwall has access to this option.

---

You are dedicated to the cause of the Shining Sentinels. You gain expert proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. When you are between an ally and a foe and would provide lesser cover to your ally against the foe’s attacks, you twist to become even more obtrusive, providing standard cover instead.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Knight Vigilant archetype.
