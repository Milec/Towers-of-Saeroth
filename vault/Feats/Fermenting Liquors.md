# Fermenting Liquors single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 104

**Frequency**
once per day

**Requirements**
You have a free hand

---

You always have a special batch of preserved liquor ready for each day. You serve your liquor to a willing adjacent creature. The creature recovers Hit Points equal to twice your level. Additionally, for 1 hour, all [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Influence (GM Core)|influence]] the creature gain a +1 status bonus.
