# Jester's Gambol single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 26

**Requirements**
You are attuned to an apparition that grants Circus [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Fey Lore as one of its apparition skills.

---

You adopt a stance that makes your movements carefree and sublimely unpredictable, allowing you to move past impediments with ease and resist the attacks and importunateness of natural threats. You ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], and you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to half your level against damage dealt to you by [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungi]], and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]].
