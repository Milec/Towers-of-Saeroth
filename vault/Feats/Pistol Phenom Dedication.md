# Pistol Phenom Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 136

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing A Spotter|Pistol Phenom]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in at least one type of one-handed firearm; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]

---

You catch an opponent off-guard by spinning your weapon. You gain the [[Feats/Pistol Twirl (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Pistol Twirl]] gunslinger feat, enabling you to Feint against creatures within your weapon’s first range increment. This otherwise serves as Pistol Twirl for the purpose of meeting prerequisites, although as normal, it doesn’t count as another pistol phenom feat for the purpose of meeting Pistol Phenom Dedication’s special entry and taking another archetype. Whenever you [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]] with a one-handed firearm, you can choose to attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check instead of a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained at least two other feats from the pistol phenom archetype.
