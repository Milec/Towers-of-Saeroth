# Terrain Advantage  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 59

---

You can take advantage of the terrain to bypass foes’ defenses. Non-lizardfolk creatures in difficult terrain are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to you. If you have a swim Speed, non-lizardfolk creatures that are in water and lack a swim Speed are also flat-footed to you.
