# Elemental Existence  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stonebound]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stonebound Dedication]]

---

Your body becomes infused with elemental earth. You gain the [[Feats/Adopted Ancestry]] feat and gain a 1st level [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|oread ancestry feat]] as a bonus feat, ignoring the limitations of the [[Traits/Lineage|lineage]] trait. When you gain new ancestry feats, you can select oread feats in addition to feats allowed by your ancestry and heritage.
