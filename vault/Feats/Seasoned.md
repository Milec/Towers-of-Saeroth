# Seasoned  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 209

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Alcohol Lore]], Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]], or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You've mastered the preparation of many types of food and drink. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Craft]] food and drink, including [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|potions]]. If you are a master in one of the prerequisite skills, this bonus increases to +2.
