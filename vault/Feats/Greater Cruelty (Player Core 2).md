# Greater Cruelty  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 96

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cruelty (Player Core 2)|Cruelty]]

---

The extent of your corruption devastates your foes' minds and reflexes. When you use Cruelty, you can choose to make the target [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] instead, with the same condition value. Make the choice when you Cast the Spell (before you know the result of the target's save).
