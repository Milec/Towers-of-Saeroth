# Lunging Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Secrets of Magic)|Spellstrike]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twisting Tree|twisting tree hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
You're wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]].

---

Your spell unwinds the structure of your staff to make it exceptionally long, or even separate it into shards held together by magical power. Make a Spellstrike with a staff, with a spell that isn't a cantrip or focus spell. Increase the staff's reach by 5 feet × the spell's level.
