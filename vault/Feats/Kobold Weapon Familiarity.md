# Kobold Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 38

---

You've trained with weapons ideal for subterranean efficiency. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow|crossbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatpick|greatpick]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Pick|light pick]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick|pick]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]]. You also gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Kobold|kobold]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial kobold weapons are simple weapons, and advanced kobold weapons are martial weapons.
