# Desiccating Inhalation two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

You draw in the moisture from nearby creatures, draining them dry to heal your wounds. Creatures in a 30-foot cone take 6d8 negative damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature that critically fails this saving throw is also [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. As long as at least one creature was damaged by your Desiccating Inhalation, you regain HP equal to your level.

 At 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d8.
