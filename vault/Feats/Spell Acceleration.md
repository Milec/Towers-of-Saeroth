Special time-weaving techniques let you compress your spellcasting gestures and incantations into a short burst so you can cast with incredible speed—though it causes temporal strain. You gain the [[Feats/Quickened Casting (Player Core)|Quickened Casting]] feat. It applies to your cantrips and spells from the class you used to qualify for the Time Mage Dedication.
