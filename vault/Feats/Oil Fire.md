# Oil Fire single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 110

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Armor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|armor innovation]]

**Requirements**
You have a foe [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]].

---

Your armor includes flame-resistant gauntlets with oil-filled finger joints. These joints can split apart, dousing your opponent in flammable oil and then igniting it. The opponent must attempt a Reflex save against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The grab ends.  

**Success** The grab ends, and the target takes 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]].  

**Failure** The target takes persistent fire damage equal to half your level.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes persistent fire damage equal to your level.
