# Wake of Devastation  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kaiju Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Kaiju Stance and are touching the ground.

---

Like a [[Traits/Kaiju|kaiju]], you leave a trail of destruction in your wake. Your shattering earth attacks gain the [[Traits/Razing (Player Core 2)|razing]] trait. Whenever you succeed with a shattering earth attack while standing on the ground, the earth buckles under the force of the blow, and all the squares beneath you become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Whenever you critically succeed with a shattering earth attack against a creature standing on the ground in an area of difficult terrain, you pummel the creature into the earth; they become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until they succeed at an [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] attempt against your class DC.
