# Fiendish Form  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Evil|tenets of evil]]

---

Your features reshape into those of a type of fiend who serves your deity; for example, as a demon you might have putrid scales, twisted horns, and red eyes. You gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you don't already have it, and you gain the [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor you've become (such as [[Traits/Daemon|daemon]], [[Traits/Demon|demon]], or [[Traits/Devil|devil]]).
