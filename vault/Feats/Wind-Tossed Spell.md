# Wind-Tossed Spell single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication]]

---

You enhance your spell with elemental air, using the wind to find your target and carry your magic around cover. If the next action you use is to Cast a Spell that requires a spell attack roll, you ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition and any cover they have from you. The spell gains the [[Traits/Air|air]] trait.
