# Brightsoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

The fire inside you asserts itself as beaming, radiant light. Your body is naturally luminescent, glowing with the effects of a primal [[Spells/Light|_light_]] cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up. The light is involuntary and constant; if [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracted]], it returns in 1d4 rounds. You suffer a –2 circumstance penalty on [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]] and gain a +1 circumstance bonus on saves against [[Traits/Light|light]] effects and effects that inflict the [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] conditions.
