# Master Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Expert Captivator Spellcasting (Impossible Magic)|Expert Captivator Spellcasting]]

---

You master the most powerful spells that enchant and deceive. You learn a 7th-rank occult spell. At 18th level, you learn an 8th-rank spell, and at 20th level, you learn a 9th-rank spell. These spells must each have either the [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait. You cast these spells as [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate occult spells]]. You become a master in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics.
