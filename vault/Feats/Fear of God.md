# Fear of God free action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 280

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Trigger**
You successfully [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] an enemy.

---

You embed a divine fear into the triggering foe that lasts for 10 minutes. During that time, you ignore that creature’s temporary immunity to your Demoralize attempts and whenever you successfully Demoralize the triggering enemy, you can instead increase their existing [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition by 1 (or 2 on a critical success) to a maximum of frightened 4.
