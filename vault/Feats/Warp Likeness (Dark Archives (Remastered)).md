# Warp Likeness single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 121

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Morph-Risen (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Morph-Risen]]

**Requirements**
You hit a creature with a melee Strike on your last action.

---

You channel some of your own nature through your blow, temporarily warping your target's form to resemble your own. The creature you hit must attempt a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target resists the effect but its form twists in the process. It's [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The target's body briefly warps to resemble yours. It's clumsy 1 until the end of its next turn and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target’s body warps to resemble yours for a significant time. It’s clumsy 1 for 1 minute and sickened 2.
