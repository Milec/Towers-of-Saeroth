# Vacate Vision ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oozemorph Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail (but don't critically fail) a saving throw against an effect with the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait.

---

You cause your eyes to liquefy and run out of your eye sockets, leaving them empty. You get a success on the save against the triggering effect, but you are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for the duration the effect would have had on a failure. At that time, your eyes regrow and your normal vision returns.
