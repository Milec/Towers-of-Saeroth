# Precious Ammunition single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 199

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eldritch Archer (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

You have learned to mimic the power that lies between magic and material. You enchant a piece of ammunition on your person with the magical essence of a precious material, allowing you to leverage certain creatures' weaknesses. Choose [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]; if you shoot the arrow or bolt before the end of your turn, it counts as that material. At 14th level, add [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] to the list of materials you can choose from.
