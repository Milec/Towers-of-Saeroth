# Steam Knight single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 37

---

You shape your kinetic aura into swirling armor of steam that scalds your enemies and propels you on super-heated jet streams. A creature can take damage from Steam Knight only once per round. Your steam armor has the following effects.

- You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed.
- When you [[Actions/Leap]], you can jump up to your Speed. You don't immediately fall at the end of a jump, provided you Leap again with your next action. If you Leap over a creature and come within 10 feet, that creature takes 2d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC.
- At the start of each of your turns, you can emit steam as a free action. It deals 2d6 fire damage to each creature in your kinetic aura with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. A creature that fails is also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet.

---
**Level (+5)** The fire damage from a jump or blast of steam increases by 1d6.
