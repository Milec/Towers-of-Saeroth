# Cavalier's Banner  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 164

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have pledged your service to a specific organization or ideal (see [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges]] sidebar).

---

You fly the banner of your pledge from your mount, raising your allies' spirits. You and all allies within 30 feet gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves and DCs against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects. If your banner is destroyed or removed, allies within 30 feet become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].
