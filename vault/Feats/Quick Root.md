# Quick Root ⤾  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

**Trigger**
A creature successfully [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoves]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trips]] you, or you fail a saving throw against an effect that would move you or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

---

You react instinctively, sprouting roots from your legs and plunging them deep into the ground to keep your footing. If a force would move you, you move half that distance, to a minimum of 5 feet. If you would be knocked prone, resolve the effect, then you [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]].

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
