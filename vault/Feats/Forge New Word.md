# Forge New Word  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 53

---

At the pinnacle of your art, you have forged a new great rune of power, a new ideal expression or utterance to be added to the craft. You create a rune and add it to your runic repertoire. When you gain this feat, choose a spell of 4th rank or lower. It must be common, or you must otherwise have access to it. The spell must take 1 or 2 actions to cast. You can choose a variable action spell, deciding when you create the rune whether the rune uses the 1-action or 2-action version of the spell. Name your rune. This rune has the [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|unique]] trait and a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] trait of your choosing ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]).

This rune can be drawn on a creature or object, regardless of what the spell can target. When invoked, this rune has the effect of the chosen spell. The rune-bearer is the point of origin for the spell’s effect; calculate range and cover from its space. If the spell has targets, you decide who or what the spell targets, and the rune-bearer is a possible target if your chosen spell can target it. If the spell can affect only you (such as [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]]), invoking it affects only you, and has no effect if the rune is drawn anywhere other than on you. If the spell requires a spell attack roll, use your class DC – 10 in place of the spell attack modifier. If your chosen spell has a duration and that duration is longer than 1 minute, the invoked rune’s duration is 1 minute instead.
