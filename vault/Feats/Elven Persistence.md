# Elven Persistence free action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 148

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You fail a skill check you’re at least trained in.

---

Elves don’t give up easily on their pursuits. Instead of the result you received for the triggering skill check, you receive a result of 12 + your [[Rules/Proficiency (Player Core)|proficiency]] bonus (don’t apply any other bonuses, penalties, or modifiers).
