# Didactic Strike  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Shared Stratagem]]

---

When you find a glaring weakness, you can set your allies up to annihilate your foe. When you use [[Feats/Shared Stratagem]], you can designate up to 10 allies instead of just one. The foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the first attack from each designated ally before your next turn, and each ally's first attack deals an extra 2d6 precision damage to the target if it hits.
