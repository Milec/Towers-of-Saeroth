# Abjure the False Kin  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 80

---

Your vow reminds you that there can be brackish vinegar hiding within honeyed words, helping you detect attempts to influence your mind. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect that doesn’t deal damage, you get a critical success instead.
