# Mental Balm  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Amp (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Amp]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 24

---

Your spells release soothing mental waves. Use this amp in place of a psi cantrip's normal amp entry. You can use this amp only on a psi cantrip that targets or affects you or one or more of your allies and doesn't target or affect any enemies.

---
**Amp** You or one ally within 30 feet gains a +2 status bonus to Will saves against [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects for 1 minute. You can also have the amped psi cantrip attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one effect imposing the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition on yourself or the chosen ally, or an effect imposing the [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition that has a duration of 1 hour or less. If you successfully counteract the effect, you remove only the frightened and stupefied conditions, not any other part of the effect.
