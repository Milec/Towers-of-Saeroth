# Heart of Earth  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 217

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Terra Dragonblood]]

---

The earth parts to allow you passage. You gain a [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]] of 15 feet. While burrowing, your [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]] becomes precise up to 15 feet. When you [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], you can choose to leave behind a tunnel of your size or smaller for others to traverse through.
