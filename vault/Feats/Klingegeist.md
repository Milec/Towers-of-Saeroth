# Klingegeist two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Possession]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 122

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Ghosts are not permitted for PFS play._

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Incorporeality, Flight, and Rejuvenation|Ghost]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Headless Haunt]]

---

Your imprisonment inside a [[Equipment/Final Blade|_final blade_]] used to perform countless executions has instilled in you an aptitude for entering and possessing similar sharp implements of murder. You project your mind and incorporeal body into an unattended weapon of 2 Bulk or less that deals slashing damage.

While possessing a blade, you have a space of 5 feet, but you don’t block or impede enemies attempting to move through your space, nor do you benefit from or provide [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]]. You’re trained in the use of the weapon, unless your proficiency was already better, and you can make [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] actions with the weapon as if you were wielding it. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|Fly speed]] of 30 feet and can only take Strike and [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] actions, which don’t trigger reactions. You have an AC of 18 + your level and the [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] and Hit Points of the weapon you’re possessing, which for most one-handed slashing weapons is Hardness 5 and HP 20. You can’t be [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarmed]], nor can your true body be directly injured except by [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, which bypasses both the weapon’s Hardness and Hit Points to damage you directly.

If you’re reduced to 0 Hit Points or the weapon you’re inhabiting is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Destroyed|destroyed]], you’re immediately ejected from it and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 1 round. Otherwise, the effect lasts for 10 minutes or until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] it.
