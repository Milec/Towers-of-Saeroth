# Azarketi Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Absalom, City of Lost Omens]] pg. 395, [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 5

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Azarketi Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your mastery with weapons both above and below water is unmatched. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in certain weapons, you also gain that proficiency in [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow|crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hand Crossbow|hand crossbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spears]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|tridents]], and all [[Traits/Azarketi|azarketi]] weapons in which you are trained.
