You use your body's expansion as leverage to displace a nearby creature. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] an adjacent creature. For the purposes of determining what size creature you can affect, use your final size after the triggering effect.
