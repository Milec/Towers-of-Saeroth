# Story Crooner  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Strix]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 136

---

You're a talented story weaver and use your voice effectively. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]. If you would automatically become trained in Performance, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the Impressive Performance skill feat and gain a +1 circumstance bonus when Performing for an audience of strix.
