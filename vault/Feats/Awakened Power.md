# Awakened Power  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 9, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 100

---

Your power spontaneously evolves or reconfigures itself. You gain one awakening benefit for a [deviant feat](DeviantFeats.aspx) of 4th level or lower.

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, choose a different deviant feat of 4th level or lower, and gain one of its awakening benefits.
