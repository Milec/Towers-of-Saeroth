# Social Camouflage  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 61

---

You have the uncanny ability to quickly blend into the lifestyle of whatever settlement in which you find yourself. After residing in a particular settlement for at least 1 day, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Impersonate]] to pass yourself off as a resident of that settlement. Additionally, you can use [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] in place of [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Gather Information]] while in that settlement. The GM might decide you need more time in particularly large settlements to gain these benefits, or might decide you gain the benefits only while in a specific district or part of the settlement.
