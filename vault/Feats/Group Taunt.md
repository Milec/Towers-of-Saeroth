# Group Taunt  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 45

---

Your taunts draw the attention of multiple enemies at once. When you use [[Actions/Taunt (Battlecry!)|Taunt]], you can choose up to three targets within range, and you can have up to three taunted enemies at a time. Each time you Taunt, you can choose which enemies remain taunted and which the effect ends for. You must remain at or below this limit.
