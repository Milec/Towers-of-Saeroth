# Underwater Volcano  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 15

---

You can cast [[Spells/Volcanic Eruption|_volcanic eruption_]] as a 7th-level primal innate spell once per day, except that you can only evoke the volcano on the floor of a body of water, and the effects that normally happen to flying creatures happen to swimming creatures instead.
