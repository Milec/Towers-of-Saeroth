# Ever Dreaming  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sleepwalker (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker Dedication]]

---

You draw no distinction between the sleeping and waking worlds. Your Daydream Trance has an unlimited duration, and you no longer need to take an action to enter it. If you're [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] due to sleep, you don't take the –4 penalty to AC, Perception, and Reflex saves, and don't have the [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] condition. You're still [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] while asleep. You can act on your turn while asleep, though you're [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 2 until you fully awaken.
