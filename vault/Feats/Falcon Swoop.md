Like the fastest of predatory birds, you soar toward your prey at great speeds. [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] twice. At any point during this movement, you can make a Strike against one enemy within reach or within the first range increment of a ranged weapon. That enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the Strike if you're attacking them from above.
