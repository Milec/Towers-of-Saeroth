# Current Spell single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist Dedication]]; air or water is in your elemental philosophy

---

As you use your magic to manipulate air or water, you divert some of its currents to form a barrier around you. If your next action is to Cast a Spell with the [[Traits/Air|air]] or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait, until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC or a +2 circumstance bonus against ranged attacks. This effect has the air or water trait, or both, depending on the traits of the spell you cast. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all saves against effects with the air trait, water trait, or both until the start of your next turn, depending on the spell's traits.
