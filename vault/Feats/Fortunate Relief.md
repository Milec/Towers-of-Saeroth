# Fortunate Relief  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 119

Your god favors your attempts to remove afflictions and conditions. When you cast a healing spell that attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an effect, you can roll the counteract check twice and take the higher value.
