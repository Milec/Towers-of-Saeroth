# Volcanic Escape ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 28

**Trigger**
An enemy in your kinetic aura damages you.

---

Lava reflexively explodes underneath you and the creature that hurt you. The triggering enemy takes 1d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. You [[Actions/Leap]] up to half your Speed in any direction; this movement doesn't trigger reactions.

---
**Level (+4)** The damage increases by 1d6.
