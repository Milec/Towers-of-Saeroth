# Death from Above two actions  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Canopy Predator]]; [[Feats/Verduran Shadow Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Requirements**
You are standing, [[Traits/Climbing|climbing]], or [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|balancing]] on a surface at least 10 feet above your target.

---

You leap down on your target, using their body to cushion your fall and your momentum to empower your attack. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to Leap to a space adjacent to the target. If you land adjacent to the target, compare the Athletics check result against your target’s Reflex DC.

When you leap onto a creature in this way, you do not also deal damage for [[Rules/Falling on a Creature (Player Core)|falling on a creature]]. Regardless of your result, the target is temporarily [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to your Death from Above for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target falls and lands prone, reducing the falling damage you take by an amount equal to twice your level. You then can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the target. The Strike gains the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d8]] weapon property and deals an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This additional damage increases to 2d6 if you are a master in Athletics, and to 3d6 if you are legendary in Athletics.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the beginning of your next turn, reducing the falling damage you take by an amount equal to your level. You can then make a melee Strike against the target, adding the deadly d8 weapon property to the attack.  

**Failure** You can make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the target.  

**Critical Failure** You fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], whether you take falling damage or not.
