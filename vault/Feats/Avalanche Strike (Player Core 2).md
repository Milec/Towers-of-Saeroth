You attack all nearby adversaries. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your two-handed weapon against each enemy within its melee reach. Each attack counts toward your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
