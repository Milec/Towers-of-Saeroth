# Divine Presence single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 281

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

Your spirit becomes one with your deity, granting you the bearing of a god. As long as you are in this stance, you have a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that has the [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]] trait. Whenever you take an action, you can take it from your space or any space within the emanation as if it were the space you occupy. An enemy that ends its turn completely within the emanation is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to your Strikes. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] the emanation to increase its radius by 5 feet, up to a maximum of 15 feet.
