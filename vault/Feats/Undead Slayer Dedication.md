# Undead Slayer Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 28

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

---

Your training has prepared you to recognize most [[Traits/Undead|undead]] on sight. You become trained in two [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skills, each about a specific type of undead, or become an expert if you were already trained. You gain the Slayer's Identification free action.

 **Slayer's Identification** free action **Trigger** You roll initiative and can observe a creature you know is undead; **Effect** You attempt to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] to identify the undead creature with a +1 circumstance bonus. If you're a master in the skill you're using to Recall Knowledge, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus instead.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the undead slayer archetype.
