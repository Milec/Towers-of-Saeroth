# Shifting Faces  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kitsune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 125

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
tailless alternate form

---

You wear many faces, even those that don't belong to you. When you [[Actions/Change Shape (Ancestry Guide)|Change Shape]] into your tailless form, you gain the effects of 3rd-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] for 1 hour or until you shift back, except it's a [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]] effect rather than an [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]].
