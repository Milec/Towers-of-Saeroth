# Gossip Lore  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 167

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Dandy]], [Venture-Gossip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=311) (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Prophet of Kalistrade]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication]]

---

Your time gossiping in fashionable salons keeps you informed on every topic. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Gossip Lore]], a special Lore skill that can be used only to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], but on any topic. If you fail a check to Recall Knowledge with Gossip Lore, you get the effects of the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat.

 If you have legendary proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]], you gain expert proficiency in Gossip Lore, but you can't increase your proficiency rank in Gossip Lore by any other means.
