# Silencing Strike single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 82

---

A quick strike to the face or mouth silences your opponent. Make a melee Strike against an enemy. If it hits, the enemy must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is dazed and can barely vocalize. It's [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]] and its speech is raspy and hard to understand. It must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to use [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] actions or Cast a Spell, unless the spell has the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is stunned 3 instead of stunned 1.
