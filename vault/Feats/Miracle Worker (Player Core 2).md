# Miracle Worker  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 69

**Frequency**
once every 10 minutes

---

Your alchemical mastery can resuscitate the recently slain. You can administer a [[Equipment/Elixir of Life (Player Core 2)|major or true elixir of life]] to a creature who has been dead for no more than 2 rounds. When you do, that creature is immediately returned to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded 1]].
