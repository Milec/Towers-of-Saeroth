# Skull Creeper  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 54

---

You wear skulls to demoralize foes. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] and gain the [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] skill feat. If you're already trained in Intimidation, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. If you pay 50 gp for a splendid skull mask, the mask grants you a +1 item bonus to Intimidation; its usage is worn (mask).
