# Witch Warden  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 13

**Access**
Kellid ethnicity

---

You and your family have fought long and hard against witches, particularly the winter witches of Irrisen, and you've learned to be wary of their curses and the otherworldly powers their patrons grant. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Curse|curses]], and to saving throws against spells cast by a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-392)|witch]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Hag|hag]]. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a curse or a spell cast by a witch or hag, you get a critical success instead.
