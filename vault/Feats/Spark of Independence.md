# Spark of Independence two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 64

---

You touch a creature with the [[Traits/Minion|minion]] trait and share the heightened sense of autonomy you've discovered. The minion is [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 round and is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes. It can use this extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike.
