# Vanish into The Land  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 53

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Andoran have access to this option._

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]; [[Feats/Terrain Stalker (Player Core)|Terrain Stalker]]

---

You’re adept at using the land’s natural features to find places to hide. When in the [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] you’ve selected for the Terrain Stalker feat, you can [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] even without [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] or being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]].
