# Goblin Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 48

---

Others might look upon them with disdain, but you know that the weapons of your people are as effective as they are sharp. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Dogslicer|dogslicer]] and [[Equipment/Weapons/Horsechopper|horsechopper]].

 In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial goblin weapons are simple weapons and advanced goblin weapons are martial weapons.
