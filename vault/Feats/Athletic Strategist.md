# Athletic Strategist  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 60

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You know how to calculatedly manipulate joints and body weight. In addition to using [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem]] to modify a Strike, you can use it to modify a [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] attempt, substituting your Devise a Stratagem roll for the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check. You must apply the substitution to the first eligible attack you make, whether it's a Strike or one of the Athletics actions.

 You can also use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength for the Athletics check when you substitute your Devise a Stratagem roll, unless you're using a weapon for the maneuver and the weapon doesn't fit the restrictions for using Intelligence with a stratagem.
