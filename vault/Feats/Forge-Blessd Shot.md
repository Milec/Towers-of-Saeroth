# Forge-Blessd Shot free action  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 201

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Torag (Divine Mysteries)|Torag]] or [[Setting/Deities/Angradd (Divine Mysteries)|Angradd]]

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

**Trigger**
You attempt a ranged Strike.

---

You intone a prayer to the dwarven deities Torag or Angradd, willing a higher power to guide your aim in the heat of battle. On your next Strike with a ranged weapon before the end of your turn, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your attack roll and ignore your target’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition, as well as your target’s [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|lesser, standard, and greater cover.]]
