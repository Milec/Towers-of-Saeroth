# Orchard's Endurance single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Stance]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 35

---

Patches of bark appear on the skin of you and your nearby allies. You and your allies in your kinetic aura gain resistance 5 to bludgeoning and piercing damage. In addition, you and your affected allies roll flat checks to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] twice and take the higher result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.

---
**Level (+4)** The resistance increases by 2.
