# Learn by Watching  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 25

---

Before your awakening, you watched humanoids performing some skill near you frequently. You didn't know it at the time, but your curiosity led to you picking up the skill. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. In addition, when you [Follow the Expert](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2672) in exploration mode, you add 2 + your level as your proficiency bonus if you're untrained instead of adding your level alone.
