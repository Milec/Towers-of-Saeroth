# Ears of the Forest  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Zoophonia|zoophonia muse]]

---

Beyond relying on your own senses, you keep an ear open for nearby creatures for help in exploring your surroundings. When you [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]], if there are any willing [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] in the selected area, you can borrow their senses. Doing so lets you Seek with [[Rules/Scent|scent]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] as [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] senses, along with any other senses appropriate to the animals in the area, at the GM's discretion.
