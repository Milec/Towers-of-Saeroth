# Instant Backup ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 112

**Trigger**
Your attack with a firearm misfires.

---

Even as your firearm [[Rules/Classic Firearms (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|misfires]], you quickly draw a backup. Release the misfired weapon and Interact to draw a different
weapon that requires the same number of hands or fewer.
