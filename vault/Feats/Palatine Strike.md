Your strikes are particularly deadly against supernatural creatures. Whenever you deal precision damage with [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|strategic strike]] to a creature about which you have successfully used [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]], you deal an additional 2d6 precision damage with your strategic strike.
