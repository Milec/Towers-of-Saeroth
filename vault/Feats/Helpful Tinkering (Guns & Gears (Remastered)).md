# Helpful Tinkering single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 29

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|offensive boost]]

---

You don't just tinker with your own innovation, you fiddle with your allies' weapons as well (for their benefit, of course). Choose an ally in your reach and one of their weapons. Attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check against a high DC for your level.

**Success** For 1 minute, the chosen ally's Strikes with the chosen weapon gain the same offensive boost your innovation has.  

**Critical Failure** Your ally takes damage of the type and amount that your offensive boost normally deals on a successful Strike.
