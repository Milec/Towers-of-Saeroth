# Mature Beastmaster Companion  *Feat 4*

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Beastmaster Dedication]]

---

All of your animal companions grow up, becoming [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|mature animal companions]] and gaining additional capabilities. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action, your animal companion can still use 1 action on your turn to either Stride or Strike.
