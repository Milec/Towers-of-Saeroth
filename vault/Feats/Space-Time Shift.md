# Space-Time Shift free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 187

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chronoskimmer]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chronoskimmer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

You travel just a few moments into the future to immediately arrive at your destination. Your Strides are augmented until the end of your turn, allowing you to instantly teleport to any point you could reach with your Stride instead of traversing normally to the location. Your augmented Strides have the [[Spells/Teleport|_teleport_]] trait and don't trigger reactions that can be triggered by move actions or upon leaving or entering a square. You also augment your [[Actions/Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim]] actions in this way if you have the corresponding movement type.
