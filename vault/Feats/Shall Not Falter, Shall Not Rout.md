# Shall Not Falter, Shall Not Rout  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 69

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]

---

You gain the [[Spells/Shall Not Falter, Shall Not Rout|_shall not falter, shall not rout_]] focus spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can regain using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity to recite the Crimson Oath and meditate on its teachings. If you already have a focus pool, increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. Your knight reclaimant focus spells are divine spells; when you gain this feat, you become trained in divine spell attacks and spell DCs and your spellcasting ability is Charisma.
