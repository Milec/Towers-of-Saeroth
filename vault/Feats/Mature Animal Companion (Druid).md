# Mature Animal Companion (Druid)  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 135

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Involutionist]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Nidalese Horselord]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Animal Companion]]

---

Your animal companion grows up, becoming a mature animal companion, which grants it additional capabilities. See the [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|animal companion rules]] for more information. Your animal companion is better trained than most. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action, your animal companion can still use 1 action on your turn that round to Stride or Strike.
