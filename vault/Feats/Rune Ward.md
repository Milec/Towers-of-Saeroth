# Rune Ward ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

**Trigger**
You are targeted by a spell.

**Requirements**
You are trained in the skill associated with the tradition of the triggering spell ([[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]], and [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] for [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]).

---

You quickly sketch a rune in the air to gain a modicum of protection against the triggering magic. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your saving throw and AC against the spell.
