# Shake Off the Gods single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

[[Feats/Pure Legion Enforcer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're under an effect with the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] trait that required a saving throw.

---

Your trust in the Pure Legion rushes forth as you fight off the influence of a god. You can attempt a new saving throw against the required effect with a +2 status bonus to the save. You can't get a worse result than your original saving throw.
