# Mooneater two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Bakuwa Lizardfolk]] heritage

---

You call on the spirit of the dragon that devoured the moon to temporarily swallow a celestial body and blind onlookers with its visage. You can cast [[Spells/Eclipse Burst (Player Core)|_eclipse burst_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. When you Cast the Spell, the sun or moon appears to eclipse to all creatures within 500 feet of you. For 1 round after Casting the Spell, the area is reduced to dim light.
