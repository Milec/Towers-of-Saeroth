# Animal Accomplice  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 52

---

You build a rapport with an animal, which becomes magically bonded to you. You gain a [familiar](Familiars.aspx). The type of animal is up to you, but most gnomes choose animals with a burrow Speed.
