# Master of Apprentice  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 73

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], Master in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]], or Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|a Lore skill]]

---

Teaching is a difficult art form, but you've gotten a pretty good handle on it. When you [[Actions/Craft]] or [[Actions/Earn Income]] during downtime, you can also attempt to [[Actions/Aid]] another creature doing the same with the same skill if they have a lower proficiency bonus than you. If you succeed, they can use the better result between your check and their own check.
