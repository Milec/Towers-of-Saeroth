# Summoner's Call ⤾  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 84

**Trigger**
You or your eidolon take damage from a foe or hazard.

**Requirements**
Your eidolon is manifested and is more than 5 feet away from you.

---

In a moment of danger, you call your eidolon to your side. Your eidolon teleports to an open space adjacent to you.

**Special** This feat has the trait matching your eidolon’s tradition ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]).
