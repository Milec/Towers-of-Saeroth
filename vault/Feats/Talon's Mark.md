# Talon's Mark  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 36

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Talon Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a magical or mundane tattoo.

---

On the rare occasions that Twilight Talons need to identify themselves as members of the organization, many rely on an easily concealed tattoo to reveal their allegiance. Choose a tattoo you have. If the tattoo wasn't already a magic item, it becomes a level 6 item with the [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], and [[Traits/Tattoo|tattoo]] traits. It also gains the following abilities.

 **Activate—Twist Design** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) You transform your tattoo to a different form of approximately the same size. You can Dismiss this effect.

 **Activate—Adopt Persona** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) Frequency once per day; Effect Your tattoo helps you create a temporary cover to infiltrate a protected area or escape a dangerous situation. It casts 2nd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] and 2nd-rank [[Spells/Ventriloquism (Player Core)|_ventriloquism_]] on you. The duration of both effects is 10 minutes.
