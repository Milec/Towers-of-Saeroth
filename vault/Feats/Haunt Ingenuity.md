# Haunt Ingenuity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 42

---

Your cunning knowledge grants you the ability to notice the emotional echo of a soul that passed on, leaving a haunt in its wake. Even when you aren't [[Actions/Search|Searching]] while in exploration mode, the GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] check for you to notice [[Traits/Haunt|haunts]] that usually require you to be Searching.

 You can disable haunts that require master proficiency in a skill as long as you're at least trained in the skill. If you have master proficiency in the skill, you can disable haunts that require a proficiency rank of legendary instead.
