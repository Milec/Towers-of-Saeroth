# A Tale to Believe In single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]]

You recite a tale of prowess that rewrites the combat unfolding around you. You can attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect that's currently affecting an ally within 30 feet, using [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] for the counteract check and half your level as the counteract rank.
