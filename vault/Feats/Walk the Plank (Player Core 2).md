# Walk the Plank two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 209

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]]

---

You [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frighten]]  a foe into moving where you want them, traditionally demanding they walk the plank. Attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] an opponent. On a success, in addition to the normal effects, you can also force the target to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to its Speed immediately. You choose the path the target takes, but you can't force it to move into an obviously harmful space (such as into hazardous terrain or a space where it would fall) unless your check was a critical success. As normal for forced movement, this movement doesn't trigger reactions. The target then becomes temporarily immune to Walk the Plank for 10 minutes.
