# Irradiate two actions  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 74

---

You exude an aura of radiation, making everyone around you ill. All creatures in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must succeed at a Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. On a critical failure, they’re also [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] for 1 minute. You’re [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to your own radiation. The value of this sickened condition increases by 1 for every 5 levels you have beyond 6th.
