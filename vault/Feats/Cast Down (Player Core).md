# Cast Down single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 116

---

The sheer force of your faith can bring a foe crashing down. If the next action you use is to cast [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] to damage one creature, the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] if it takes any damage from the spell. If the target critically fails its save against the spell, it also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 minute.
