# Summon Air Elemental  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sylph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 115

---

You have a connection to the Inner Spheres, allowing you to summon an elemental ally. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]] as a 5th-level primal innate spell, but the elemental summoned must be an [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Air|air elemental]].
