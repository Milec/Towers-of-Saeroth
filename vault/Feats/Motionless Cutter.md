# Motionless Cutter  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 37

---

Your weapon is so sharp even an insect alighting upon its still blade would be severed. Your weapon ikon gains the following ability.

**Transcendence—Sever Four Dragonfly Wings** three actions (transcendence) Make a Strike that deals slashing damage with your weapon ikon. If that Strike is successful, you can immediately make an additional Strike against a different target within your reach. You can continue making Strikes in this manner, each against a different target, until you have made a total of four Strikes or you miss with a Strike, whichever comes first. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
