# Warren Navigator  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 30

---

You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice. When you [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or attempt a roll against a [[Spells/Quandary|_quandary_]] spell, you get a result one degree of success better than you rolled. You don't take a penalty to Sense Direction when you lack a [[Equipment/Compass (Player Core)|compass]].
