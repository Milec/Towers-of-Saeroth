# We're on the List ⤾  *Feat 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 78

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Alter Egos in Golarion|Alter Ego]], [[Archetypes/Archaeologist]] (Level 3)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Alter Ego Dedication]] or [[Feats/Archaeologist Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
An ally you can see and hear is about to attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Impersonate]] someone or [[Actions/Lie]].

---

Emulating Shensen whenever she arrives at a show, you share your knowledge of the region and drop a few important local names to make a good impression and help your allies do the same. Attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] check to [[Actions/Aid]] your ally. If you succeed, you recall a few specific details about local culture and personalities to help support your ally's deception. In addition to the benefits of your Aid, the triggering ally rolls their Deception check twice and takes the better result. This is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.
