# Gladiator's Roar two actions  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]] (Level 12)

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Gladiator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Gladiator Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

You can project your voice into a high-pitched screech that damages your foes. Your roar can be heard twice as far as normal, and creatures within a 15-foot-[[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] take 6d10 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage and must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. If your Gladiator’s Roar triggers [[Feats/Play to the Crowd (Player Core 2)|Play to the Crowd]], you gain a +2 status bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check to do so.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, becomes frightened 3, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
