# Tenacious Net  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 38

---

Escaping your nets is no simple task. The [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] or [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] your nets increases from 16 to 18. After a creature you [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] with a net Escapes or Forces Open the net, stray strands of the net cling to the creature, causing them to remain [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the beginning of their next turn.
