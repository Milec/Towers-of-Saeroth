# Throttle the Divine  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|Misfortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 61

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pure Legion Enforcer Dedication]]

---

You grasp the godly by their throats and damage their connection to their deities. When you have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], it must roll its flat check to use manipulate actions with the divine trait twice and take the worse result. Each time it attempts such an action while you have it grabbed, you tighten your grip further, dealing bludgeoning damage equal to 2d6 + your Strength modifier to that target.
