# Death Warden  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hallowed Necromancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hallowed Necromancer Dedication]]

---

With your intimate familiarity with the duality of positive and negative energy, you're able to stand as a bulwark against death and undeath alike. You gain resistance to negative damage equal to half your level and a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Negative|negative]] trait.
