# Straveika  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage (Player Core)|Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

You descend from one of the truly ancient vampires, a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire, Nosferatu|nosferatu]] cursed to eternal life but not eternal youth. People call you straveika, or ancient-born. The vestiges of your forebear's powers of domination have left you with an understanding of how a creature's intentions change its behaviors. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] and Perception DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you.
