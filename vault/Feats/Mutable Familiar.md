# Mutable Familiar  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 174

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Master]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Master Dedication]]

---

Your familiar's supernatural spirit has outgrown its corporeal body. You can conduct a special 10-minute activity to reselect certain familiar abilities, switching one or more of the following abilities for other abilities on this list: [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower|burrower]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Climber|climber]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Fast Movement|fast movement]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity|manual dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Resistance|resistance]], and [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Scent|scent]]. You can reselect only familiar abilities you would normally be able to reselect each day, not required familiar abilities for your familiar. You can't remove an ability that is required for another ability your familiar has (for instance, you can't remove manual dexterity if the familiar has [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Lab Assistant|lab assistant]]).
