# Tender Ministrations single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hedge Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hedge Mage Dedication]]

---

Your gentle magic eases even the most skittish of souls, a gift that almost guarantees you popularity among parents and farmers alike. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a non-cantrip Spell]] that targets a single creature, you can reduce the target’s [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition by 1. If the target is an [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], you can also attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] an effect imposing the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] condition.
