# Tumbling Teamwork  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 234

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

Your tumbling distracts a foe enough to create an advantage for one of your allies. When you successfully [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] an enemy's space, an ally who is adjacent to that enemy can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a reaction, but they must remain adjacent to that enemy.
