# Jalmeri Heavenseeker Dedication  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 224

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The Jalmeri Heavenseeker archetype as printed in Lost Omens: Impossible Lands is legal for play. Players may not use the version printed in Pathfinder #158: Six Feet Under._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Jalmeri Heavenseeker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Student of Perfection Dedication]], or you've trained with a champion of the Challenge of Sky and Heaven

---

You become trained in either [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]; if you were already trained in both these skills, you become an expert in one of them instead. You gain either the [[Feats/Ki Rush]] or [[Feats/Ki Strike]] monk feat, which grants you a ki spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point that you can recover using the [[Actions/Refocus]] activity as a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|monk]] does. If you already have both feats, you can instead choose a single 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|monk feat]].

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you gain two other feats from the Jalmeri Heavenseeker or [[Archetypes/Student of Perfection]] archetypes.
