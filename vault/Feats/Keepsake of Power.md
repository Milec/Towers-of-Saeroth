# Keepsake of Power  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 95

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hedge Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hedge Mage Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], Master in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], Master in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or Master in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

Your keepsake might be disdained by your more orthodox peers as a crutch to your studies, but you know it grants you an understated, yet undeniable, power. Increase your limit on [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]] items from 10 to 12. If you have [[Feats/Incredible Investiture (Player Core)|Incredible Investiture]], instead increase your limit to 14.
