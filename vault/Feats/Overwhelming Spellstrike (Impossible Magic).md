# Overwhelming Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 24

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
Your Spellstrike is charged.

---

You pierce through your foe’s resistance. Make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]]. The spell you cast as part of the Spellstrike ignores an amount of the target’s resistance to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage equal to your level. This applies to all damage the spell deals, including [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] and damage caused by an ongoing effect of the spell. A creature’s immunities are unaffected.
