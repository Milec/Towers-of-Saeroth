# Stoic as Hell ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 223

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chelaxian Scion]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chelaxian Scion Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]; expert in Will

**Trigger**
You’re targeted by an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect or effect that would give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition.

---

Like the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Archdevils|archdevil]] [[Setting/Deities/Dispater (Divine Mysteries)|Dispater]], you maintain an unshakable poise even under impossible stress. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw or to your Will DC against the triggering effect. If you critically succeed at the saving throw (or if the source of the effect critically fails its skill check, such as to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] you), you gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half your level until the end of the effect’s source’s next turn. If the effect originated from a creature, you can Demoralize that creature once as a free action on your next turn; if the creature was temporarily immune to your attempts to Demoralize it, it loses that immunity.
