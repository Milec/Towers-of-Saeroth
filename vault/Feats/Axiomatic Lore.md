# Axiomatic Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aphorite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 76

---

You possess a strong understanding of process among mortals and lawful outsiders alike. The source of this knowledge might be intuition, study, or a childhood spent among other aphorites. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Axis Lore]].
