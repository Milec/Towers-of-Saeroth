# Tough Skin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kashrishi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 44

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Lethoci]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Xyloshi]] heritage

---

Your skin is medium armor in the [[Equipment/Armor Groups/Plate|plate]] armor group that grants a +4 item bonus to AC, and has a Dex cap of +1, a check penalty of –2, a Speed penalty of –5 feet, a Strength value of 16, and the [[Traits/Comfort|comfort]] trait. You can never wear other armor or remove your hide. You can etch armor runes onto your hide.

 If you're a lethoci kashrishi who takes this feat, your modifications also make you more buoyant, allowing you to [[Actions/Swim]] across the surface of even turbulent bodies of water without needing to make an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain into this feat.
