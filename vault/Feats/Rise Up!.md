**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rose Warden Dedication]]

---

Sometimes, you must even slip the chains of gravity to rebel. You shout a brief call to Milani, and you and two allies within 60 feet who can hear and understand you can [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action without triggering reactions. All targets then gain a fly Speed equal to their Speed or 20 feet, whichever is greater, for 1 minute.
