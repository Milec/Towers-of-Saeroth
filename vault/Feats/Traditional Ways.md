# Traditional Ways  *Feat 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 80

---

You are a fervent follower of the long-standing elven traditions established when your people returned to Kyonin. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill checks related to Kyonin or the elves that live there, as well as a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] effects that would force you to act against your will.
