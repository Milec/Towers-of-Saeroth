# Master Qi Spells  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 126

**Prerequisites**
Qi Spells

---

You have achieved near-total mastery of your qi. You gain [[Spells/Medusa's Wrath (Player Core 2)|_medusa's wrath_]], [[Spells/Touch of Death|_touch of death_]], or another 8th-rank qi spell you have access to.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once, choosing a different spell each time.
