# Venipuncture two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 84

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sanguimancer]]

**Cost**
all your remaining sanguimancy Hit Points (minimum 3)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sanguimancer Dedication]]

---

A shell made up of hundreds of razor-thin needles of blood forms around you before exploding outward, lacerating foes in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. The needles deal piercing damage equal to double the number of sanguimancy Hit Points you spent, to a maximum of 80 damage. Creatures caught in the blast must attempt a basic Reflex save. The DC for this save is your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.
