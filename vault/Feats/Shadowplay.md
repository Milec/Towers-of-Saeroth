# Shadowplay single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 74

**Requirements**
Your last action was a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] that damaged your opponent.

---

As your blow lands, you detach from your shadow to get the drop on your enemy. You attempt to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] the opponent’s space, with a +2 circumstance bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check. If you succeed, your shadow rises up from the ground, [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] your opponent until the beginning of your next turn.
