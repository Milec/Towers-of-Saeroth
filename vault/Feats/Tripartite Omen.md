# Tripartite Omen  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Halcyon Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dualistic Synergy]]; [[Feats/Shattered Sacrament]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

You invoke religious portents to ward yourself against danger without losing sight of the history that led to this point. When you use Dualistic Synergy, if your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from your spell slots and that spell is [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]], you gain a +1 status bonus to AC for 1 round. If the spell is a halcyon spell, you gain this benefit in addition to the benefits described in Dualistic Synergy.
