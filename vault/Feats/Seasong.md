# Seasong  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 36

---

Sound carries far beneath the waves, and your people have strong, enchanting voices. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. If you would automatically become trained in Performance (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. In addition, you gain the [[Feats/Virtuosic Performer (Player Core)|Virtuosic Performer]] skill feat for singing, and your voice carries equally well above and below water.
