# Ratfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 30

---

Years of experience among ratfolk communities have made you nimble, and you've learned to run and hide when enemies threaten. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Ratfolk Lore]].
