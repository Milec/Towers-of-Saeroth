# Scattering in Spring ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twisting Petal Stance]];

**Trigger**
An enemy within your reach targets you with a melee attack, and you're aware of the attack.

**Requirements**
You’re in Twisting Petal Stance.

---

You evade an incoming attack by twisting your enemy’s tactics in your favor. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can immediately attempt a [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] against the triggering attacker; if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
