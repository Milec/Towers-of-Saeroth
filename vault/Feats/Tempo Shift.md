# Tempo Shift free action  *Feat 16*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 83

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]] (Level 16)

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]] or [[Feats/Gladiator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Gladiator Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

**Trigger**
You [[Actions/Delay (Player Core)|Delay]].

---

You are always ready to embrace to a change in tempo and adapt your actions to take advantage of unforeseen developments. When you return to the initiative order after you Delay, you do so in an unpredictable way. All enemies are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you until the start of your next turn, and you become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|Quickened]] for the current round but can only use the extra action to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
