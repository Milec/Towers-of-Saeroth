# Shadowdancer Dedication  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 192

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Call of Shadows|Shadowdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]

---

You dedicate yourself to the shadows. Your coloring becomes gray and gloomy, your senses adjust to the darkness, and you can sneak through darkness with ease. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]]. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks when you are within dim light or darkness.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the shadowdancer archetype.
