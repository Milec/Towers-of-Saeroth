# Break the Cycle  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 123

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

---

Sometimes people with no underlying quarrel have been fighting each other simply out of spite. You come to break that vicious cycle of hate. You gain the [[Feats/Legendary Negotiation (Player Core)|Legendary Negotiation]] skill feat, even if you would not normally qualify. Additionally, whenever you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check, you may spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to do so at mythic proficiency.
