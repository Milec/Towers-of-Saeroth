# I've Had Many Jobs  *Feat 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 79

---

Finding and excelling at odd jobs is child's play to one who has worked many occupations during many lives. When you attempt to [[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]], you function as if you were trained in the skill used. If you are at least expert in the skill used, the DC for your skill check is reduced by 2.
