# Celestial Form  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 277

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|[Champion]]] [[Traits/Holy|holy]]

---

You take on a [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] countenance, gaining the features of a type of celestial who serves your deity; for example, as an [[Bestiary/Families/Angel (Monster Core)|angel]], you gain a halo and feathery wings. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] if you don’t already have it, and you gain the celestial trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor you’ve become ([[Bestiary/Families/Archon (Monster Core)|archon]], angel, or [[Bestiary/Families/Azata (Monster Core)|azata]], for example).
