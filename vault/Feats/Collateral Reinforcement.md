# Collateral Reinforcement single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

With a combination of bony armor, extra flesh layers, and tactical incorporeal phasing, you protect your thralls from collateral damage. For 1 round, your thralls become immune to area and [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage.
