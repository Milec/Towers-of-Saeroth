# Nidalese Horselord Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Nidalese Horselord]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]; [horse](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=76) animal companion

**Access**
You’re from Nidal.

---

Thanks to the deep bond you share with your Nidalese horse, the two of you gain remarkable abilities. You both gain darkvision and a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] or [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] trait. Your horse's Speed also increases by 5 feet. You gain the following edicts and anathema.

 **Edicts** oppose the forces of the Umbral Court where possible, provide comfort to those who suffer in darkness  
 **Anathema** mistreat your horse, worship Zon-Kuthon or permanently ally with those who worship Zon-Kuthon

**Special** You can pledge yourself to the cause of the Nidalese horselords if you've already taken the [[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]] feat, allowing you to take this dedication feat even if you haven't taken two additional cavalier feats.
