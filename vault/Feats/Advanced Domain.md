# Advanced Domain  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 123

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Soul Cycle|Soul Warden]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Vindicator]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Campfire Chronicler]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Domain Initiate]]

---

Your studies or prayers have unlocked deeper secrets of your deity’s [domain](Domains.aspx). You gain an advanced domain spell from one of your domains. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times. Each time, you must select a different advanced domain spell from a domain for which you have an initial domain spell.
