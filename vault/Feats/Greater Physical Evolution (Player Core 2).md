# Greater Physical Evolution  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 155

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Primal Evolution (Player Core 2)|Primal Evolution]] or [[Feats/Arcane Evolution (Player Core 2)|Arcane Evolution]]

---

You change form readily. Once per day, you can use a sorcerer spell slot to cast any common polymorph battle form spell of the spell slot's rank as if it were a signature spell in your repertoire. You can use the extra spell slot from or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]] Evolution instead of a sorcerer spell slot.
