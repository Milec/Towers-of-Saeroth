# Iivlar's Boundary Break  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 14

---

You can bend the boundary between planes with the natural skill of an iivlar. You can cast [[Spells/Flicker|_flicker_]] as a 4th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] once per day. When you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell, increase the distance you teleport to 15 feet.
