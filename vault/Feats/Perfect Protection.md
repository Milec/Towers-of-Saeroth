# Perfect Protection  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 49

---

Your armor protects you from the deadliest hits. Each time you're critically hit while wearing the medium or heavy armor, attempt a DC 14 flat check. On a success, it becomes a normal hit.

**Special** If your armor has a [[Equipment/Fortification (GM Core)|_fortification_]] rune, you roll only one flat check, but the DC is 12 (or 10 if it's a _greater fortification_ rune).
