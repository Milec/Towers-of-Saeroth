# Blazing Talon Surge two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rain of Embers Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in [[Feats/Rain of Embers Stance]].

---

You rush forward and latch onto your enemy with talons of hungering fire. Stride once. If you end your movement within reach of at least one enemy, you can make a fire talon Strike against that enemy that deals an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. If it hits and deals damage, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple]] the creature you hit.

## Rain of Embers Style

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 118

The martial artist Meleeka Sanvara (CN female human monk) originally met agents of the Pathfinder Society as an enemy when the agents were sent to retrieve an artifact she had stolen. Now, Meleeka works as a Pathfinder agent and occasionally shares the knowledge of her family's unique martial arts techniques with agents who earn her trust.
