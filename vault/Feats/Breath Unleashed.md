# Breath Unleashed  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 215

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kobold Breath (Draconic Codex)|Kobold Breath]]

---

You’re able to channel more of your draconic benefactor’s power and focus it into your Kobold Breath, increasing its effectiveness but requiring more time to recover. When you use Kobold Breath, you can increase the damage dice to d8s and increase the area to 30 feet for a [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] or 60 feet for a [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. If you do, you can’t use Kobold Breath again for 1 hour. During this time, Kobold Breath cannot be recharged using [[Feats/Kobold Momentum]].
