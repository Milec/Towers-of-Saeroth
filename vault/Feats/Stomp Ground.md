# Stomp Ground two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 44

---

You bring your booted foot down on the ground with enough force to rattle your foes. Each creature in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Reflex saving throw against your class DC.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your turn.  
**Failure** The creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  
**Critical Failure** The creature is knocked prone and takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage from the fall.
