# Tengu Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 26

---

You learned skills for surviving in the place where your people were dispersed. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Tengu Lore]].
