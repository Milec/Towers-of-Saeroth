# Leap and Fire  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 115

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hit the Dirt!]]

You’re quick enough to line up a shot even while diving to the ground. When you use your Hit the Dirt! reaction, at any point during your [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] you can also make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a loaded firearm or crossbow, targeting the creature whose attack triggered the reaction.
