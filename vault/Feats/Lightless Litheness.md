# Lightless Litheness  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fetchling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 86

---

Your body is almost as flexible as your shadow. When you critically fail at [[Actions/Squeeze|Squeezing]], you get a failure instead. Additionally, when you roll a success at an [[Actions/Escape]] check, you get a critical success instead; if you roll a critical success, you can Step instead of Striding up to 5 feet.
