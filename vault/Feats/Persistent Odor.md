# Persistent Odor  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 20

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Emit Defensive Odor]]

---

Your pheromones now linger on creatures. When a target fails its save against your Emit Defensive Odor ability, they also take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]]. The damage increases to 2d6 at 13th level and 3d6 at 17th level.
