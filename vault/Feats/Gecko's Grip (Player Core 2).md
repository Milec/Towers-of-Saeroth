# Gecko's Grip  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

---

You cling to walls with a preternatural grip. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber (Player Core)|Combat Climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to climb, you get a critical success instead.

**Special** If you have the [[Ancestries/Heritages/Cliffscale Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|cliffscale lizardfolk heritage]], you instead gain a climb Speed of 15 feet.
