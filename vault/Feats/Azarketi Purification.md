# Azarketi Purification  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 14

---

You can cast [[Spells/Purify Food and Drink|_purify food and drink_]] as a primal innate spell, but only on liquids. You can cast this spell once every 10 minutes. You can also use your innate _purify food and drink_ on a touched creature instead of a liquid to purify their blood, giving that creature a +2 status bonus for 1 minute on their ongoing saves against [[Traits/Poison|poisons]] already present in their body when you cast the spell.
