# Shooting Stars Stance single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 128

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Monastic Weaponry]]

---

You enter a stance that lets you throw [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]] with lightning speed. While in this stance, you can use your monk feats or monk abilities that normally require unarmed attacks with shuriken instead.
