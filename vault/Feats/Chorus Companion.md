# Chorus Companion  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Zoophonia|zoophonia muse]], mature animal companion

---

Animals can be musical partners as much as the subjects or audience. Your animal companion becomes trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (or expert if it was already trained). When you cast a [[Traits/Composition (Player Core)|composition]] spell, you can have it originate either from you or from your animal companion, if your animal companion is within 30 feet of you.
