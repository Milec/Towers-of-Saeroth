# Stretching Reach single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 44

---

You can leverage your size and muscle to extend your reach and attack more distant foes. While in this stance, when you wield a melee weapon that requires two hands and doesn't have [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]], the weapon gains a reach of 10 feet.
