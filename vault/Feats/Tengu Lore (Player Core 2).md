# Tengu Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 34

---

You learned skills for surviving in the place where your people were dispersed. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Tengu Lore]].
