# First Frost  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Gelid Shard]]

---

The [[Equipment/Gelid Shard (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_gelid shard_]] within your heart may sap your ability to feel and experience emotion, but it also lets you create and manipulate cold. You learn to cast arcane [[Rules/Spontaneous Spells (Player Core)|spontaneous spells]], and you gain a spell repertoire with the [[Spells/Frostbite|_frostbite_]] and [[Spells/Frost's Touch (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_frost’s touch_]] (see below) cantrips. You’re trained in spell attack modifier and spell DC. Your key spellcasting attribute is Charisma.
