# Reflect Foe  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 121

---

You create a copy of a foe by calling them forth from a nearby reflection, clonally growing them in an instant from one of your hairs or otherwise creating them in a way that matches your own nature as a reflection. You can cast [[Spells/Duplicate Foe (Player Core)|_duplicate foe_]] once per day as an innate occult spell. At 20th level, your innate _duplicate foe_ is heightened to 8th rank.
