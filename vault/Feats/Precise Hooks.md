# Precise Hooks  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 69

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clawdancer Dedication]]

---

Your body is a varied and deadly weapon. When you critically hit a target with your claw or talon, you can apply a critical specialization effect based on the stance you're in. For claw stance, apply the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]] weapon group's effect. For talon stance, apply the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axe]] group's effect. These effects are in addition to the claw's or talon's normal critical specialization effect if you apply it.
