# Rockslide Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication]]

---

You enhance your spell with elemental earth, causing chunks of stone to litter the ground. If the next action you use is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that affects an area, a number of 5-foot squares in the area equal to the spell level become difficult terrain for 1 round. These squares must be on the ground, and the entire area of difficult terrain must be contiguous. The spell gains the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] trait.
