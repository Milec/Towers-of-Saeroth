# Snare Genius  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 22

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snare Crafting]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

If the [[Traits/Snare|snare]] normally takes 1 minute to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]], you can Craft it with 3 Interact actions instead. Each day during your daily preparations, you can prepare three snares from your formula book for quick deployment (increasing to four snares if you're a master in Crafting and five if you're legendary). Snares prepared in this way don't cost you any resources to Craft.

 When a creature critically fails its saving throw against and takes damage from the initial effect of a snare you Crafted and deployed, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of its next turn.
