# No Hands, No Problems  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 49

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tsukumogami Poppet]] heritage

---

After a lifetime of using temporary, ghostly limbs to manipulate objects, you hardly need real hands. You can extend your limbs to perform simple [[Rules/Interact]] actions at a range 5 feet beyond your normal range, after which your limbs contract back to their normal length. You can also cast the [[Spells/Telekinetic Hand|_telekinetic hand_]] cantrip as an arcane or occult innate spell at will, which always takes the ghostlike form of telekinetic hand and is tethered to your body via a snaking, illusory arm. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
