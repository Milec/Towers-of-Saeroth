# Prophet of Kalistrade Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Prophet of Kalistrade]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Mercantile Lore]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]; Charisma +2

**Access**
You’re from Druma.

---

After induction into the ranks of the Kalistocrats, you learned the skills necessary to succeed in business and rise above others by following a set of detailed strictures. Unless you take measures to hide your affiliation, anyone who has heard of the [[Setting/Deities/Prophecies of Kalistrade|Prophets of Kalistrade]] will know you’re one of them when they see you. You become an expert in Society and can use Society instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on merchants and traders.

Additionally, if you don’t already cast spells from spell slots, you learn to cast [[Rules/Spontaneous Spells (Player Core)|spontaneous spells]] and gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity. You gain a spell repertoire with two common [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] cantrips, plus either [[Spells/Prestidigitation (Player Core)|_prestidigitation_]] or [[Spells/Read Aura (Player Core)|_read aura_]]. You’re trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for these spells is Charisma, and they’re occult spells.
