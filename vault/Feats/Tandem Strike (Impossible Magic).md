# Tandem Strike two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]], [[Traits/Tandem (Impossible Magic)|Tandem]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 82

**Requirements**
A creature is in reach of both you and your eidolon.

---

You and your eidolon each attack, seamlessly targeting the same foe without interfering with each other’s movements. Your eidolon makes a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], and then you make a melee Strike against the same creature. If both Strikes hit, combine their damage for the purpose of [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistances]] and [[Rules/Weakness|weaknesses]]. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn’t increase until after both attacks have been made.
