# Shore Step  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 198

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Wave|wave order]]

---

The shallows and tide pools have always called to you and let you pass unhindered. You ignore difficult terrain resulting from shallow water. In addition, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] on a slippery or wet surface, or on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Swim]], you get a critical success instead.
