# Barbarian Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 221

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Barbarian]]

**Prerequisites**
Strength 14; Constitution 14

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; if you were already trained in Athletics, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in barbarian class DC.

 You can use the Rage action.

 Choose an [instinct](Instincts.aspx) as you would if you were a barbarian. You have that instinct for all purposes and become bound by its anathema, but you don’t gain any of the other abilities it grants.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the barbarian archetype.
