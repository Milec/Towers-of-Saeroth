# Right-Hand Blood  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnoll]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 113

---

It's said that the flesh of the right side of a hyena can heal diseases, but that of the left side is poisonous. You can take 1 damage to feed someone blood from your right side and [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] or take 2d8 damage to [[Actions/Treat Disease]] or [[Actions/Treat Wounds]]; in either case, you don't need [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], and gain a +1 item bonus. Blood from your left side causes the check to critically fail automatically.
