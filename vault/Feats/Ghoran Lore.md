# Ghoran Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 37

---

The very first memories ghorans have are those of their creation, and even after thousands of years, those memories have never faded for you. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Ghoran Lore]].
