# Elemental Shape  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 137

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

You understand the fundamental elements of nature such that you can imbue them into your body and manifest as a living embodiment of those elements. Add the forms in [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]] to your [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]] list. Whenever you’re polymorphed into another form using _wild shape_, you gain resistance 5 to fire.
