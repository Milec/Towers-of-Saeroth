# Sacred Wilds Oath  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

---

You’ve sworn an oath to protect the holy places within nature and the spirits that dwell within, from ancient trees to primordial wild guardians. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on animals and to make very simple [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Requests]] of them. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks to Make an Impression when interacting with a [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], or [[Traits/Kami|kami]]. You gain the following edict.

**Edict** You must aid any animal or nature spirit in need unless it violates your other tenets or puts someone else at risk of immediate harm.
