# Along the Deep River  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Sprite (Monster Core)|Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 47

**Access**
Tian Xia origin, or you're a follower of  Sangpotshi .

---

Once per year, when you die, you turn into an immaterial ball of light. After a week, you return to life with the effects of a successful (but not critically successful) 9th-rank [[Rituals/Reincarnate (Player Core 2)|reincarnate]] ritual. You always reincarnate into a sprite, but you reincarnate as a randomly selected sprite heritage.
