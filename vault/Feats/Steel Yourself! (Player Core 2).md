# Steel Yourself! single action  *Feat 4*

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

You encourage an ally to toughen up, giving them a fighting chance. Choose one ally in your marshal’s aura. The ally gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Charisma modifier and a +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves. Both benefits last until the start of your next turn.
