# Distracting Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Laughing Shadow (Impossible Magic)|laughing shadow hybrid study]]; [[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
You have a hand free, and your Spellstrike is charged.

---

You weave distracting magic with one hand to enhance the efficacy of your [[Actions/Spellstrike]]. Make a Spellstrike and [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]] against the target of your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. Execute your Feint immediately before making the Strike, but after choosing your target. The Feint gains the [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] traits, and it always has the basic effects of a Feint, rather than applying any adjustments or alternate effects from other feats or abilities.

If you’re in the [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, you gain a status bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check equal to your extra damage from Arcane Cascade (typically 1, increasing to 2 if you have weapon specialization and 3 if you have greater weapon specialization).
