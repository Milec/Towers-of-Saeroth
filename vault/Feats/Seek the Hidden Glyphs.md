# Seek the Hidden Glyphs  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

---

You are well-versed in the use of magical runes in traps and hazards. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks to find magical traps. Even if you aren’t [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]], you get a check to find magical traps that normally require you to be Searching. You still need to meet any other requirements to find the trap. As long as you are holding or wearing an [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|artisan’s toolkit]], you can use [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to disable a magical trap, treating your proficiency in Crafting as your proficiency in Thievery for requirements.

In addition, if you trigger a trap by critically failing a check to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable]] it, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] on yourself as a reaction, gaining the benefits of the rune before the trap affects you.
