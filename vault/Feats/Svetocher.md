# Svetocher  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

You were born to one of the moroi, the most common vampires, and you share their supernatural fortitude and power of persuasion. When you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition, calculate the penalty to your Fortitude saves and your Hit Point reduction as though the condition value were 1 lower. You are trained [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]. If you would automatically be trained in it, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
