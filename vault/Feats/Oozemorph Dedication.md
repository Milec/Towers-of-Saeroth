# Oozemorph Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Slithering

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

---

You are plagued by sporadic anatomical rearrangements, which grant you insights into other creatures with unusual anatomies. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] and [[Traits/Ooze|Ooze Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves to avoid being [[Bestiary/Abilities/Engulf|Engulfed]] by creatures, and to checks to [[Actions/Escape]] after being Engulfed.

 As you develop more abilities based on your amorphous affliction, you become increasingly off-putting to mundane creatures. You take a penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks against [[Traits/Animal|animals]] and [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]]. This penalty is equal to your number of class feats from the oozemorph archetype, to a maximum of –4 for four or more feats.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the oozemorph archetype.
