# Elegant Buckler  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 163

---

You've learned a flexible way to position your [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|buckler]] to provide more protection. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]] to gain a circumstance bonus to AC from a buckler, increase the bonus from +1 to +2.

 While you have a buckler raised, if a creature critically misses you with a Strike, you gain panache until the end of your next turn.
