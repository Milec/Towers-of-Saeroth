# Thrown Voice  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Strix]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 137

---

You've learned how to throw your voice through the winds, tricking others as to your location. You can cast [[Spells/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]] as a primal innate spell once per day. If you're a [[Ancestries/Heritages/Songbird Strix|songbird strix]], you can cast it twice per day instead.
