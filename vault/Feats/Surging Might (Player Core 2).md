# Surging Might single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 141

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wellspring Mage]] (Level 10)

---

You focus the power of your spell to overcome enemies' resistances. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]], the spell ignores an amount of the target's resistance equal to your level against the following types of damage: [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], and [[Traits/Void|void]]. This applies to all damage the spell deals, including [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] and damage caused by an ongoing effect of the spell. This doesn't cause the spell to ignore immunities, only resistances.
