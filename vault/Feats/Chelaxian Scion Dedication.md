# Chelaxian Scion Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 222

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chelaxian Scion]]

**Access**
You’re an aristocrat or honored retainer from a Chelaxian noble house.

---

A noble house has invested in your training, imparting special techniques the family has honed over generations. You gain [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] as a skill feat, choosing from among Cheliax Lore, Devil Lore, Hell Lore, or a Lore associated with your house. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks, and your Will DC against Chelaxian citizens and [[Bestiary/Families/Devil (Monster Core)|devils]].
