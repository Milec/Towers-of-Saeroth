# Emergency Targe ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Sparkling Targe (Impossible Magic)|sparkling targe hybrid study]]

**Trigger**
An enemy you’re aware of hits you with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] or a spell attack, or you fail a save against an enemy’s spell.

**Requirements**
You are wielding a [[Classes/Subclasses/Implements/Shield|shield]] or have the [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]] spell prepared.

---

You immediately [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]] or cast [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]]. The circumstance bonus applies to your defenses when determining the outcome of the triggering attack or spell.
