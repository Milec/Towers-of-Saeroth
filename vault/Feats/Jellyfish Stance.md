# Jellyfish Stance single action  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Fists of the Ruby Phoenix Hardcover]] pg. 212, [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 75

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

You relax your posture and loosen your joints, allowing yourself to move with incredible fluidity. You can make stinging lash attacks that deal 1d6 slashing damage. These attacks are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Reach|reach]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

 While in Jellyfish Stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves and on checks to [[Actions/Escape]] and [[Actions/Squeeze]].
