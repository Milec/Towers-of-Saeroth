# Unshakable Idealism  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Vigilant]]; expert in Will saves

---

Your faith and hope can’t be shaken, and you won’t flee when lives are at stake. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on saves against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects and [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects that inflict despair. When you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] condition, you can choose to stand your ground and be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] for the effect’s duration as well, preventing you from running and allowing you to re-enter the fight more quickly when the effect ends.
