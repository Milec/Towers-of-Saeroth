# Marshal Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 204

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]; trained in martial weapons

---

You’ve combined your social skills and combat training to become a talented combat leader. Choose [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]. You become trained in that skill or become an expert if you were already trained in it.

In addition, while you’re conscious, you’re surrounded by a marshal’s aura in a 15-foot emanation. Your aura has the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] traits and grants you and allies in the aura a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]].
