# Share Weakness single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Esoterica (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Esoterica]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Exploit Vulnerability (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Exploit Vulnerability]] mortal weakness

**Requirements**
You are [[Actions/Exploit Vulnerability (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Exploiting a creature's Vulnerability]] using mortal weakness.

---

You select an object from your esoterica that has great personal value to you, such as a locket or treasured ring, and you grant it to an adjacent ally, establishing a personal link that allows your ally to affect an enemy as if they were you. The ally's Strikes apply the weakness from your mortal weakness the same way your Strikes do. This benefit ends when your Exploit Vulnerability ends or you Share Weakness again.
