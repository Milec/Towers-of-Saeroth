# Investigator Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 151

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Investigator]]

**Prerequisites**
Intelligence 14

---

You gain the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Advanced Player's Guide)|on the case]] class feature, which grants you both the Pursue a Lead activity and Clue In reaction. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and another skill of your choice. If you were already trained in Society, you instead become trained in an additional skill of your choice. You also become trained in investigator class DC.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the investigator archetype.
