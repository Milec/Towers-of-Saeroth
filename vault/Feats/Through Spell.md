# Through Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 122

---

Spells you cast can transform briefly into vital essence, traveling harmlessly through living and [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures between you and your target, blocked only by solid objects. If the next action you use is to Cast a Spell that requires a spell attack roll, your targets don't benefit from cover provided by living or undead creatures.
