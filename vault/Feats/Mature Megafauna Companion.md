# Mature Megafauna Companion  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mammoth Lord Dedication]]

---

You've raised your megafauna companion to be a more powerful force on the battlefield than most of its kind. The companion you gained through the Mammoth Lord Dedication feat becomes a [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|mature animal companion]], granting it additional capabilities. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action, your megafauna animal companion can still use 1 action on your turn to Stride or Strike.
