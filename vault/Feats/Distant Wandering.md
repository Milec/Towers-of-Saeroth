# Distant Wandering single action  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 11, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 103

---

Your body falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] and your spirit projects out of it. While in spirit form, you can't consciously move your body, and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] and inaudible, though a creature still might notice the signs of your presence and passing, just like for an invisible creature affected by [[Spells/Silence|_silence_]], or a [[Spells/Prying Eye|_prying eye_]]. You can freely move about, though you can't touch or move anything, cast spells, attack, or otherwise affect anything around you. Despite your ghostly form, you are not [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] and can't pass through barriers you couldn't in your body. Most effects can't harm your spirit form, though some spells, like [[Spells/Spirit Blast|_spirit blast_]] and [[Spells/Spirit Song|_spirit song_]], explicitly damage a creature's spirit. You can return to your body as a free action. If you are in spirit form at the beginning of your turn, you must attempt another backlash check for your deviation, returning to your body if you fail.
