# Drawn in Vital Ink free action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 50

**Requirements**
During your last action, you succeeded at a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] that dealt physical damage to a creature that can [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]].

---

After striking the target, you run a brush or finger along your weapon to collect a bit of its blood. For the next minute, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] targeting the creature you drew blood from at a range of 60 feet (even if you’re Tracing a Rune as a single action). Using Drawn in Vital Ink against a different creature requires you to wipe off the blood already on your weapon, ending the effect for the previous creature.
