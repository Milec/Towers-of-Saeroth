You gain the [[Feats/Command Undead]] feat, but you can use it only to transform the effects of _harm_ spells you cast on [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. These spells can control creatures with a level equal to or lower than your level – 1 (instead of your level – 3).
