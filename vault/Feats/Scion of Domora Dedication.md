# Scion of Domora Dedication  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Master Dedication]]; you have befriended a spirit guide and it bonded with you using its Bond with Mortal ability.

---

You gain a spirit guide ally. Your familiar takes on the form of a [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Ooze Defense|spirit guide]] specific familiar.

**Special** You can take Scion of Domora Dedication even if you have the Familiar Master Dedication and have not taken two other [[Archetypes/Familiar Master]] feats. You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained at least two other feats from the scion of Domora or familiar master archetypes.
