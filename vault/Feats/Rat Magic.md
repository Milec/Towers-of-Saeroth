# Rat Magic  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 23

---

There always seems to be a little rat around to carry messages for you. You can cast [[Spells/Animal Messenger|_animal messenger_]] once per day as a primal innate spell. When you do, the animal that responds is always a rat. If there are no rats within range, the spell is lost.
