# Necromantic Resistance  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 28

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

[[Feats/Undead Slayer Dedication]]

---

As part of your training, you've inured yourself against necromancy through grueling, repeated exposure. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] effects and gain resistance to [[Traits/Negative|negative]] damage equal to half your level.
