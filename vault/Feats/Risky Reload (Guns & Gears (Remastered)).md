# Risky Reload single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 112

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unexpected Sharpshooter]] (Level 4), [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky|Unexpected Sharpshooter]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You're wielding a firearm.

---

You've practiced a technique for rapidly reloading your firearm, but attempting to use this technique is a dangerous gamble with your firearm's functionality. Interact to reload a firearm, then make a Strike with that firearm. If the Strike fails, the firearm [[Rules/Classic Firearms (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|misfires]].
