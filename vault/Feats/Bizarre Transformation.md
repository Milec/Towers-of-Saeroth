# Bizarre Transformation single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

The forms you take on defy belief—chimerical sights of twisted antlers or acidic drool. If your next action is to cast [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]], you can change the damage type of a single unarmed attack granted by the wild shape form to one of the following: [[Traits/Acid|acid]], bludgeoning, [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], piercing, or slashing. The chosen attack gains the appropriate trait.
