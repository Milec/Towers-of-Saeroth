# Soulforger Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 98

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Soulforger (Impossible Magic)|Soulforger]]

**Prerequisites**
 or ability to cast [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spells

---

You can manifest the power of your spirit in combat to realize your motivations. You bind a suit of armor, shield, or weapon as a [[Rules/Binding an Armament (Impossible Magic)|soulforged armament]]. Choose a motivation or cause known as a soul path (page 101) that ties you to your soulforged armament; acting against or ignoring your soul path is anathema to you and could lead you to be afflicted with a curse called [[Rules/Corruption (Impossible Magic)|soulforged corruption]]. Choose one essence power for the armament (see archetype). Changing an essence power requires 1 week of retraining, and you can’t retrain them while you have soulforged corruption.

You can Manifest Soulforged Armament to summon your soulforged armaments in combat.

**Manifest Soulforged Armament** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|extradimensional]]) **Requirements** If summoning a soulforged weapon or shield, you have the hands free to wield it; if summoning soulforged armor, you aren’t wearing any armor; **Effect** You immediately wield or wear the soulforged armament bound to you. The soulforged armament remains manifested until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect.

Once per day when you use this ability, you can manifest the armament’s essence form. You gain the armament’s essence power until it’s Dismissed. After 1 minute, the essence form armament is automatically Dismissed.
