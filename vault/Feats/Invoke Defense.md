# Invoke Defense single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 294

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Invoker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re under the effects of Enter Spirit Trance.

---

You manifest a defensive quality of spirits all around, such as the thick hide of an animal spirit or the sturdy bark of a nature spirit. Choose bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] equal to half your level to that damage type for the duration of your spirit trance. If you use Invoke Defense again, you can choose a different type of damage, but you lose the previous resistance.
