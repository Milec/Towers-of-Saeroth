# Keep up the Good Fight ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Vigilant]]

**Trigger**
A foe's Strike reduces you to 0 Hit Points.

**Requirements**
You had more than 1 Hit Point before the triggering Strike, and the Strike wouldn't kill you.

---

Your devotion to helping others and doing good keeps you going, even against insurmountable odds. Instead of being knocked out, you are reduced to 1 Hit Point, gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to your level for 1 round, and increase your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] value by 1.
