# Invoke the Crimson Oath  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 95

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]

---

You can invoke the Crimson Oath to unleash blasts of ruby energy from your weapon. You gain the [[Spells/Invoke the Crimson Oath|_invoke the Crimson Oath_]] focus spell. If you don’t already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can regain using the Refocus activity to recite the Crimson Oath and meditate on its teachings; if you already have a focus pool, increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. Your knight reclaimant focus spells are divine spells; when you gain this feat, you become trained in divine spell attacks and spell DCs. Your key spellcasting ability for these spells is Charisma.
