# Energy Blessed  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 21

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Elemental Heart Dwarf]] heritage

---

Energy runs through your blood more powerfully. When you use [[Actions/Energy Emanation]], you can create an emanation of 5 feet, 10 feet, or 15 feet. The damage increases to 6d6 plus an additional 1d6 for every level you have above 9th, instead of 1d6 plus an additional 1d6 for every 2 levels above 1st.
