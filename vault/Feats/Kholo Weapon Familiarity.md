# Kholo Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 18

---

You gain access to all [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]] weapons with the [[Traits/Kholo|kholo]] trait. You have familiarity with weapons with the kholo trait plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail (Player Core)|flail]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Khopesh (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|khopesh]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Mambele (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|mambele]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/War Flail (Player Core)|war flail]]—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
