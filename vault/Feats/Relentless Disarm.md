# Relentless Disarm  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Zephyr Guard]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Zephyr Guard Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

When you succeed, but don’t critically succeed, at a check to [[Actions/Disarm]] a creature, your circumstance bonus to further checks to Disarm that creature is +4 instead of +2. Other creatures gain only the normal bonus.

 When you critically succeed at a Disarm check, you can immediately [[Actions/Demoralize]] your target as a reaction.
