# Grave Sight  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication]]; [[Feats/Grave Sense]]

---

Your ability to sense undead has improved. You sense undead as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a range of 60 feet: an uncomfortable sensation brushes against your spirit and stinks of rot. When in proximity to an undead, you can sense its presence, but it has the [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] condition instead of the [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] condition. An undead using a disguise or otherwise trying to hide its presence must attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check against your Perception DC to hide its presence from you. If the creature succeeds, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]], and you have to use the [[Actions/Seek]] basic action to detect the creature. As with hearing and other imprecise senses, you still need to Seek to locate an undetected creature.
