# Lizardfolk Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

---

You listened carefully to the tales passed down among your community. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] and either [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for either [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Astrology Lore]] or Lizardfolk Lore.
