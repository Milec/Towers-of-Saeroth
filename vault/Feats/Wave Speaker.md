# Wave Speaker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 37

---

Being part fish yourself, you can speak the language of the sea. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill with [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] that have the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait, [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] trait, or a swim Speed, such as fish, crabs, and some seabirds (like penguins). The GM determines which animals count for this ability.
