# Ringmaster's Introduction ⤾  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 73

**Trigger**
An ally's turn begins.

**Requirements**
The ally hasn't acted in the current combat.

---

You announce the triggering ally with a litany of their legendary achievements, spurring them to action. The target is [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] this turn and can spend the extra action to Stride, Strike, [[Actions/Demoralize]], [[Actions/Feint]], or [[Actions/Perform]].
