# Sky and Heaven Stance single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 224

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Jalmeri Heavenseeker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Jalmeri Heavenseeker Dedication (Impossible Lands)|Jalmeri Heavenseeker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

Your jagged blows are like lightning. The only Strikes you can make are skyward slash unarmed attacks. These unarmed attacks deal 1d10 slashing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Versatile|versatile P]] traits. While in Sky and Heaven Stance, you gain resistance 2 to electricity and resistance 2 to sonic damage. These resistances increase to 5 at 12th level and to 8 at 18th level.
