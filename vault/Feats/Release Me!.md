# Release Me! single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 123

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

**Requirements**
An enemy has you [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

Your retribution against one who would detain you is swift and dangerous. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the creature who has you [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]. You can still make this attack even if you are restrained, though not if you are wielding a two-handed weapon. Unless your Strike is a critical failure, you can then attempt a check to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]]. If the creature who has you grabbed or restrained is the target of your [Ultimatum of Liberation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2993), you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check. Both the Strike and the Escape count toward your multiple attack penalty, but it doesn’t increase until you’ve made both rolls.
