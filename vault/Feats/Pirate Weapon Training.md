# Pirate Weapon Training  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication]]

---

You're particularly skilled at wielding traditional pirate weapons. You become trained with the following weapons: [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet|hatchet]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip|whip]]. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency with a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency rank for these weapons.
