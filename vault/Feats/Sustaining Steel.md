# Sustaining Steel free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Inexorable Iron|inexorable iron hybrid study]]

**Trigger**
You Cast a Spell using a spell slot.

**Requirements**
You're in Arcane Cascade stance.

---

You pull magic into your weapon, using its substance to transform the magic into restorative energy. You regain Hit Points equal to double the spell's level and roll to end any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent negative damage]] you have.

 If the spell was of the [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] school, any persistent negative damage you have ends automatically, and you can change the damage type from Arcane Cascade to positive if you wish. (Positive damage typically damages only [[Traits/Undead|undead]] or other creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]].)
