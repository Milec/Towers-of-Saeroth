# Friendly Nudge single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You don't always have time to wait for your allies to make room, so it's best to politely move them yourself. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] an ally. You don't need to have a free hand to attempt this check. If your ally is willing, treat the result as one degree of success higher.
