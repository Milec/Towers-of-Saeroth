# Hobgoblin Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

You gain access to all [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]] weapons with the [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|hobgoblin]] trait. You have familiarity with weapons with the hobgoblin trait plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|composite longbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow (Player Core)|composite shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|glaive]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow (Player Core)|longbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|shortbow]]—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
