# Afterimage free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 105

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You use an action or cast a spell with the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait.

---

You leave behind an afterimage of yourself in your previous square. This afterimage looks like you and can perform simple behaviors to make the deception more plausible but otherwise cannot move or take actions. A creature can distinguish between you and your afterimage easily. Your afterimage automatically fails all checks and DCs and is destroyed if it would take any damage; otherwise, it lasts for 1 minute. Whenever you take the [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] action, you can instead teleport to an afterimage instead. If you do, the action gains the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait, and the afterimage you teleport to is destroyed.
