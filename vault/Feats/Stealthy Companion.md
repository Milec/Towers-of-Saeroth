# Stealthy Companion  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 176

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Animal Companion]]; [[Feats/Camouflage]]

---

You’ve trained your animal companion to blend in to its surroundings. Your animal companion gains the benefit of the [[Feats/Camouflage]] feat. If your companion is a specialized ambusher, its proficiency rank for Stealth increases to master (or legendary if it was already master).
