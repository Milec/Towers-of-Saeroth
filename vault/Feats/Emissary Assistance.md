# Emissary Assistance  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 20

---

As an emissary, you have assisted land-bound creatures in journeying underwater to visit your settlements. You gain [[Spells/Feet to Fins (Player Core)|_feet to fins_]] and [[Spells/Water Breathing (Player Core)|_water breathing_]] as 3rd-rank primal innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day.
