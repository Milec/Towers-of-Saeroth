# Reach of the Dead free action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 32

---

You channel your necrotic magic through a thrall, expending it in the process. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has range, you cast that spell from one of your thralls within 60 feet. That thrall becomes the point of origin for your spell; calculate range and [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from its space instead of yours. The thrall is then destroyed.
