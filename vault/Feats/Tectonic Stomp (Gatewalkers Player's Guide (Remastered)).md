# Tectonic Stomp two actions  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 101

---

You cause minor tremors that topple your enemies. All creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] who are touching the ground take 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 2 levels you have, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that fails its save also falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
