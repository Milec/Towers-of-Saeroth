# Uncanny Bombs  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 68

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Far Lobber (Player Core 2)|Far Lobber]]

---

You lob bombs unerringly, despite obstructions or distance. When you throw an [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]] item with the [[Traits/Bomb (Player Core)|bomb]] trait, its [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment]] increases to 60 feet, you reduce any circumstance bonus to the target's AC from [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] by 1, and you automatically succeed at the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] when targeting a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creature.
