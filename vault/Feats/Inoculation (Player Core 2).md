# Inoculation  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 232

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

You have practice combating plague, and your patients are less likely to succumb to the same disease again for a time. When you successfully [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat a Disease]] on someone and they fully recover from the disease, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against that same disease for 1 week.
