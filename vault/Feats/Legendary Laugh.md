# Legendary Laugh  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Laughing Kholo]]

---

Your laugher echoes in the minds of your enemies. You can [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] creatures up to 60 feet away. Additionally, whenever you successfully Demoralize a creature, it takes 3d8 mental damage (or 6d8 mental damage on a critical success).
