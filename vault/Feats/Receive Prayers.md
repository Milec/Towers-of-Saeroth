# Receive Prayers  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 132

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Godling]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Godling Dedication]]

---

You open your senses so the prayers of mortals can better reach your ears. You gain a +1 status bonus to checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]], but only when attempting to ascertain hopes, prayers, wishes, and other strongly held desires of the target. You gain an even greater sense of your hierophant's prayers, which grants you a constant [[Spells/Status (Player Core)|_status_]] as a constant [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spell targeting your hierophant. The spell has unlimited duration and functions at any range, included across planar boundaries, and [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteracting]] the spell merely suppresses it until your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], though it automatically ends if the creature stops being your hierophant.

You gain the ability to cast [[Spells/Breath of Life (Player Core)|_breath of life_]] once per day as a 5th-rank divine innate spell. You can cast this spell only on your hierophant, and the spell has unlimited range (your status spell typically alerting to you to when your hierophant would die).
