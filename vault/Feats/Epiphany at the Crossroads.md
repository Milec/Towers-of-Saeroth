# Epiphany at the Crossroads free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 142

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

**Requirements**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]]. and have the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying condition]].

---

The crossroads between life and death is a place that can reveal many secrets. You gain the effects of an [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]] spell in a strange, near-death vision. Empowered by the realization, you then lose the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition (becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] 1 or increasing your wounded value by 1, as normal), regain a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level, and can [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]].
