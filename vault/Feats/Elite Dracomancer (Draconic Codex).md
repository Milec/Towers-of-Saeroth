# Elite Dracomancer  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 215

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dracomancer (Draconic Codex)|Dracomancer]]

---

Your draconic power equals that of a lesser dragon. Choose one 3rd-rank spell and one 4th-rank spell from those listed for a dragon spellcaster of your draconic benefactor’s type, such as those here (for example, [[Spells/Clairvoyance (Player Core)|_clairvoyance_]] and [[Spells/Paralyze (Player Core)|_paralyze_]] for a [[Bestiary/Adult Conspirator Dragon|conspirator dragon]] benefactor). You can cast each of these spells once per day as [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spells]] of the same tradition as your draconic benefactor.
