# Zoophonic Communication  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Zoophonia|zoophonia muse]]

---

Your studies have expanded your ability to parley and negotiate into the animal realm. You can [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] from [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] using [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] as though you shared a language, although this gives you no special ability to understand their responses. You can also use Performance instead of [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]].
