# Liberty's Promise  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 35

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Steel Falcon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Steel Falcon Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

Your devotion to Andoran's ideals is as much spiritual as philosophical. You gain the cleric's [[Feats/Domain Initiate (Player Core)|Domain Initiate]] feat but must select [[Magic/Domains/Freedom (Player Core)|freedom]], [[Magic/Domains/Truth (Player Core)|truth]], or [[Magic/Domains/Zeal (Player Core)|zeal]] as your domain. You cast that domain's domain spell as a divine focus spell, and you [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by contemplating or spreading the ideas of the Common Rule rather than praying to a god. When an ability would manifest a divine symbol—such as the [[Spells/Word of Truth (Player Core)|_word of truth_]] focus spell—it instead manifests as an eagle clutching a sword in its talons.

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times, selecting a different domain each time.
