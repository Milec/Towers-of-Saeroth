# Strategist Stance single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 91

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]]; Warfare Lore

---

You find the most strategic options for you and your allies. When you use this action, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] or Warfare [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] check. The DC is usually a standard-difficulty DC of your level, but the GM can assign a different DC based on the circumstances.

**Critical Success** Your marshal’s aura increases to a 20-foot emanation, and it grants you and allies a +1 status bonus to Reflex saving throws. Once per turn, when you succeed or critically succeed at a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check to gain information about an enemy creature, the target of the Recall Knowledge check becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the next attack made against it by you or an ally in your aura.  

**Success** As critical success, but your aura’s size doesn’t increase.  

**Failure** You fail to enter the stance.  

**Critical Failure** You fail to enter the stance and can’t take this action again for 1 minute.
