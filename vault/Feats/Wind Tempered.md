# Wind Tempered  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sylph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 114

---

You are at home in tempestuous weather and accustomed to even the harshest winds. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Air|air]] and [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]] effects. If you roll a success on a save against an air effect, you get a critical success instead.
