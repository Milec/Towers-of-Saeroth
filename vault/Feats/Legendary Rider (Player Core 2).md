You and your mount move like one being, effortlessly reading each other’s signals and synchronizing even in the thick of battle. Whenever you’re riding your mount, you are quickened; you can use the extra action only to command your mount using the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] skill action.
