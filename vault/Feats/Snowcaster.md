# Snowcaster  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard]]

---

Your magical power grows as the shard’s icy influence spreads ever deeper into your being. You gain the [[Rules/Spellcasting Archetypes (Advanced Player's Guide)|basic spellcasting benefits]]. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new level from this archetype, add a spell of the appropriate spell level (including heightened versions of lower-level spells) to your repertoire, either a common spell of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition that has the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] trait or another cold spell you have access to.
