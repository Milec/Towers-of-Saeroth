# Treat Condition two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 208

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic (Player Core 2)|Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Medic Dedication (Player Core 2)|Medic Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are holding [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], or you are wearing them and have a hand free.

---

You treat an adjacent creature in an attempt to reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition. If a creature has multiple conditions from this list, choose one. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check against the condition using your [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] modifier as your counteract modifier and the condition’s source to determine the DC. You can’t treat a condition that came from an [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|artifact]] or effect above 20th level unless you have [[Feats/Legendary Medic (Player Core)|Legendary Medic]]; even if you do, the counteract DC increases by 10. Treating a Condition that is continually applied under certain circumstances (for instance, the enfeebled condition a [[Traits/Holy|holy]] character gains from carrying a weapon with the [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|_unholy_]] rune) has no effect as long as the circumstances continue.

**Critical Success** Reduce the condition value by 2.  

**Success** Reduce the condition value by 1.  

**Critical Failure** Increase the condition value by 1.
