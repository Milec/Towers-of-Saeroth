# Violent Unleash single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mindshift (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mindshift]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 25

**Requirements**
Your previous action was to [[Actions/Unleash Psyche (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Unleash your Psyche]].

---

The force of your mind unleashing itself releases a violent shock wave that batters your enemies. You deal 2d6 force damage to all creatures in a 20-foot emanation, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

 At 5th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
