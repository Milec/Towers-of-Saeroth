# Archer's Aim two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archer (Player Core 2)|Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archer Dedication (Player Core 2)|Archer Dedication]]

---

You slow down, focus, and take a careful shot. Make a ranged Strike with a weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbow]] weapon group. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], reduce the flat check from being hidden from 11 to 5.
