# Five-gods Ram three actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rushing Goat Stance]]

**Requirements**
You’re in Rushing Goat Stance.

---

You attack in an unceasing flurry of charging headbutts. Stride or [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] and then make a ramming horn Strike. If the Strike is successful, you immediately push the target back 10 feet, then Stride or Climb and make a second ramming horn Strike against them. If the second Strike is successful, you immediately knock the target [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], but if the second Strike is a failure or if you’re unable to complete all the required actions, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1. Both Strikes count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn’t increase until you’ve made both of them.
