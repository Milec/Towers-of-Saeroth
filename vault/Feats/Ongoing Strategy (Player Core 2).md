# Ongoing Strategy  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 110

**Prerequisites**
strategic strike

---

You're constantly studying small aspects of everyone's movements, even if you don't have a stratagem in place ahead of time. On any Strike for which you didn't [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]], you still deal precision damage equal to your number of strategic strike damage dice so long as the weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack you used is one that would have let you use your Intelligence modifier had you Devised a Stratagem.
