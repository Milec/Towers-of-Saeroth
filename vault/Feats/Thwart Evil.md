# Thwart Evil ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

**Trigger**
A creature you can see uses a spell or ability that has the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] or [[Traits/Negative|negative]] traits.

---

You react instinctively when you sense evil forming, thrusting your spiritual energy toward the source to snuff out evil before it fully manifests. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell or ability using your Religion modifier as your counteract modifier.
