# Hellknight Signifer Preferment  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]; passed the Hellknight Test; trained in [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Breastplate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight breastplate]]; you have a spellcasting class feature.

---

You've bolstered your force of will with the power of the Measure and the Chain. You've officially joined the ranks of Hellknight signifers, and you receive a signifer's mask, often devoid of eyeholes or other decorative features. The mask doesn't obscure your vision, though it makes it impossible for others to see your eyes. While wearing your signifer's mask, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]], and Deception DCs against [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]]. You gain expert proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (or in another skill in which you're trained of your choice, if you were already an expert in Intimidation), as well as in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]].

**Special** You can't take both this feat and [[Feats/Hellknight-Errant]] or [[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]].
