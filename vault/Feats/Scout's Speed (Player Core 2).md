# Scout's Speed  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 212

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scout (Player Core 2)|Scout]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scout Dedication (Player Core 2)|Scout Dedication]]

---

You move faster, especially when traveling, allowing you to scout out ahead and report back without slowing your allies. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed. When calculating your[[Rules/Uneven Ground (Player Core)|travel speed]], this increases to a +20-foot status bonus.
