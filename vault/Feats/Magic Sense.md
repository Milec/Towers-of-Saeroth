# Magic Sense  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Detection]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 48, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 211, [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Soul Cage|Lich]] (Level 14)

---

You have a literal sixth sense for ambient magic in your vicinity. You can sense the presence of magic auras as though you were always using a 1st-level [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] spell. This detects magic in your field of vision only. When you Seek, you gain the benefits of a 3rd-level [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] spell on things you see (in addition to the normal benefits of Seeking). You can turn this sense off and on with a free action at the start or the end of your turn.

**Special** This feat has the trait corresponding to the tradition of spells you cast ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal|primal]]).
