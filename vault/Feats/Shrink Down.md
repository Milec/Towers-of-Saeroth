# Shrink Down single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

---

Your eidolon can shrink to fit into small spaces. Your eidolon reduces in size, to a minimum of size Small. It can use this action again while shrunk to return to its normal size. Its [[Rules/Range and Reach (Player Core)|reach]] becomes 5 feet, or 10 feet if shrinking to Large with [[Feats/Towering Size]].
