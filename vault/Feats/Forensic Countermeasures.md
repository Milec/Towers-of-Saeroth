# Forensic Countermeasures  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Empiricism (Player Core 2)|empiricism methodology]]

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

---

Your knowledge of deductive reasoning allows you to derail fellow [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|investigators]]. Over the course of a minute, you can tamper with a single location (such as a small room) to either obscure evidence or add misleading evidence. If you add misleading evidence, you choose what incorrect details it implies. A creature can see through these countermeasures with a successful [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check against your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] DC, and only if they actively look for tampering.
