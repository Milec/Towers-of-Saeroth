# Basic Skysage Divination  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 238

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oatia Skysage Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage Dedication]]

---

The stars move, granting you a sliver of their magic. Choose one of the following 1st-rank spells to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Object Reading (Player Core 2)|_object reading_]] or [[Spells/Sure Strike|_sure strike_]]. You can [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]] as an occult Oatia skysage spell.

At 6th level, choose one of the following 2nd-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]] or [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]]. At 8th level, you choose one of the following 3rd-rank spells to add to your spell repertoire: [[Spells/Clairaudience (Player Core)|_clairaudience_]] or [[Spells/Locate (Player Core)|_locate_]].
