# Animal Companion  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 133

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|[Druid]]] [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Animal|animal order]]

---

You gain the service of a [young animal companion](Companions.aspx) that travels with you on your adventures and obeys any simple commands you give it to the best of its abilities. See [[Rules/Animal Companions]] for more information.

**[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-204)|Ranger]]**: When you [[Actions/Hunt Prey]], your animal companion gains the action's benefits and your hunter's edge benefit if you have one.
