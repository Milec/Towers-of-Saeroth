# Eye for Masonry  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

---

Your familiarity with architecture allows you to easily spot subtle stonework. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to notice unusual stonework. This bonus applies to checks to discover [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|mechanical]] traps made of stone or hidden within stone, as well as secret doors in stone.

 When using [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] a stone door or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable a Device]] made primarily of stone, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to that check.
