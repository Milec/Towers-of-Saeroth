# Gigaton Strike  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 27

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Megaton Strike (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Megaton Strike]]

---

When you use a full-power Megaton Strike, you can knock your foe back. When you succeed at your Strike while using an [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|unstable]] Megaton Strike, your target must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is pushed back 5 feet.  

**Failure** The creature is pushed back 10 feet.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is pushed back 20 feet.

**Special** If your innovation is a [[Traits/Minion|minion]], this benefit applies on its unstable Megaton Strikes.
