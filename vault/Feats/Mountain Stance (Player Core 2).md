# Mountain Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 118

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You are unarmored and touching the ground.

---

You enter the stance of an implacable mountain—a technique created by [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarven]] monks—allowing you to strike with the weight of an avalanche and block blows with your garments. The only Strikes you can make are falling stone unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

 While in Mountain Stance, you gain a +4 item bonus to AC and a +2 circumstance bonus to any defenses against [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], and other [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|forced movement]] effects. You have a Dexterity modifier cap to your AC of +0, meaning you don't add your Dexterity to your AC, and your Speeds are all reduced by 5 feet. The item bonus to AC from Mountain Stance is cumulative with [[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|_armor potency_]] runes on your [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer's clothing]], [[Spells/Mystic Armor|_mystic armor_]], and [[Equipment/Bands of Force|_bands of force_]].
