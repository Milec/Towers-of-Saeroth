# Animal Trainer Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Animal Trainer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

You gain the services of a [[Rules/Young Animal Companions|young animal companion]] that travels with you and obeys simple commands as best as it can. This [trained animal](Companions.aspx) is trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] instead of the skill listed for its type.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained two other feats from the animal trainer archetype.
