# Spell Gem  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Soul Cage|Lich]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lich Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

Inspired by the degenerate [[Bestiary/Demilich|demiliches]] your kind sometimes devolves into, you've embedded a gem into your brow, fusing it to your necrotic flesh. This gem has been specially calibrated to hold one of the following spells: [[Spells/Repulsion|_repulsion_]], [[Spells/Scrying|_scrying_]], or [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]]. You must decide which spell your gem contains when you take this feat; changing the spell requires retraining the feat. Once per day, you can drain the gem's magical energy to Cast the Spell without spending a spell slot.

 At 18th level, you can heighten whichever spell you chose to 7th level. At 20th level, you can heighten whichever spell you chose to 8th level; regardless of which spell you chose, you can instead drain the gem's magical energy to cast an 8th-level [[Spells/Spell Turning|_spell turning_]] without spending a spell slot.
