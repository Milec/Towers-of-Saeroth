# Emphatic Emissary ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Ilverani and the Stars|Twilight Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Disarming Smile]]

**Trigger**
You roll initiative.

---

You can tell when diplomacy has failed and a meeting is close to unraveling or falling to blows. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your initiative roll. During your first turn in combat, you can use your Disarming Smile, targeting every [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] intelligent [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] creature that can see you but has yet to act. If you choose to sustain your Disarming Smile, you sustain the effect for only one creature, as normal.
