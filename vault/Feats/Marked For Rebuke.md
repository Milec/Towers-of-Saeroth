# Marked For Rebuke single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 282

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

You declare that an enemy is fit for further punishment in the sight of your [[Rules/Deity (Player Core)|deity]]. If the next action you perform is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that deals damage, one creature of your choice that takes damage from the spell is marked for rebuke until the end of your next turn. A creature marked this way gains [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] 10 to all damage and becomes immune to Marked for Rebuke for 24 hours.
