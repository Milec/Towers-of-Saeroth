**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sister of the Golden Erinys Dedication]]

---

You channel the unending wrath of the erinyes all around you. You have a 30-foot aura for 1 minute or until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] it. You and your allies in the aura gain a +3 status bonus to damage with Strikes. Your enemies who end their turn within the aura can't reduce their [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] value below 1.
