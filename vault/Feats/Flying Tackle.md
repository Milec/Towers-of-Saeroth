# Flying Tackle two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 43

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You barrel forward, gathering enough momentum to take down a threatening foe. Stride and then [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], or attempt to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. If you end your movement adjacent to a foe, you can attempt to Trip that foe. If you succeed at the Athletics check to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], you get a critical success instead. You can use Flying Tackle while Burrowing, Climbing, Flying, or Swimming instead of Striding if you have the corresponding  movement type.
