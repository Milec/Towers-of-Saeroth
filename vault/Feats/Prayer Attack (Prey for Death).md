# Prayer Attack single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 109

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Red Mantis Assassin (Prey for Death)|Red Mantis Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Red Mantis Assassin Dedication (Prey for Death)|Red Mantis Assassin Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a sawtooth saber in each hand.

---

You've mastered the signature assassination style of the Red Mantis. Attempt to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] an enemy within 30 feet. If your Feint is successful, when you use Prayer Attack on subsequent turns you automatically make the target flat-footed against your melee attacks for that turn without rolling a check to Feint, so long as you remain visible to the target and the target remains within 30 feet of you. If you use your Prayer Attack against a different target, you must attempt to Feint the target normally.

 When you use Prayer Attack, your next successful Strike with a sawtooth saber that turn deals 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to the target.
