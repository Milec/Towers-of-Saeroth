# Ghostly Resistance  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Incorporeality, Flight, and Rejuvenation|Ghost]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Dedication]]

---

Your ghostly form becomes innately weaker but also gains resistance to many forms of damage. Your maximum HP is reduced by your level. You gain resistance 1 to all damage except for [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]], and any damage done by a weapon with the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] rune (or any other source that acts like a _ghost touch_ rune). This resistance increases to 2 if the source is non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]].

 At 10th level, the resistance increases to 2, or 4 if the source is non-magical. At 16th level, the resistance increases to 3, or 5 if the source is non-magical.
