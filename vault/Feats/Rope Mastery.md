# Rope Mastery  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Golden League Xun]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Golden League Xun Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Ropes are your tool of choice, and you deploy them in versatile ways. When [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] a rope, you move 5 more feet on a success and 10 more feet on a critical success, to a maximum of your Speed. This additional movement stacks with the effects of [[Feats/Quick Climb]].

 In addition, you can use a rope to hamper your foes. When holding a rope, you can attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple]], or [[Actions/Trip]] a creature up to 10 feet away instead of only adjacent creatures. If you critically fail such a check while holding a rope, you can drop the rope to take the effects of a failure instead of a critical failure.
