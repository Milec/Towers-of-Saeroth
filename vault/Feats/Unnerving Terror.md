# Unnerving Terror two actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 87

**Frequency**
once per day

**Requirements**
You’re in yaoguai form.

---

You let out a mighty howl, chant of death, or speak with eerie repetition that plants fear in others. All enemies within 30 feet must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is frightened 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]].  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 2 and off-guard.
