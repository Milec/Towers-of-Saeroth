# Summon Fiendish Kin  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tiefling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
any tiefling [[Traits/Lineage|lineage]] feat

---

You have a deep connection to the fiendish realms, allowing you to summon a fiend matching your own lineage. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]] as a 5th-level divine innate spell. The fiend you summon must match your own lineage.
