# Staggering Blow two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mammoth Lord Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You're riding a megafauna companion that has a melee Strike.

---

You've trained your megafauna companion to stagger its enemies, impeding their ability to fight back. You [[Actions/Command an Animal]] to order your megafauna companion to spend both its actions making a single, powerful Strike. The attack deals an extra die of weapon damage, and if the attack hits and deals damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] until the end of its next turn.
