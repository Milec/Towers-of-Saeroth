# Instrument of Slaughter  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|divine ally (blade)]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Evil|tenets of evil]]

---

Your blade ally thirsts for the blood of your foes. Whenever you critically hit an enemy with the weapon inhabited by your blade ally while dealing extra damage from your champion's reaction, the target takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to two of the weapon's damage dice.
