# Wild Winds Initiate  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 163

**Prerequisites**
ki spells

---

You learn a mystical stance that lets you attack from a distance. You gain the [[Spells/Wild Winds Stance|_wild winds stance_]] ki spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1. While entering the stance is a ki spell, the wind crash Strikes the stance grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.
