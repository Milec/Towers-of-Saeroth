# Winter's Kiss  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard]]

---

Whether in the heart of a volcanic passageway or the glacial tundras of the Crown of the World, the only temperature you ever personally experience is an oddly comfortable chill. You are now protected from [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|extreme cold and extreme heat]], and you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to fire equal to half your level. If you would already have resistance to fire equal to half your level from a heritage, ancestry feat, class feat, or another archetype feat, you instead gain resistance to fire equal to your level.
