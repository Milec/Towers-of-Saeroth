# Shared Clarity  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 123

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]] (Level 20)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Premonition of Clarity]]

---

You can project your premonitions of clarity to your allies. When you use [[Feats/Premonition of Clarity]], allies within 15 feet of you who failed the same saving throw against the same mental effect can also spend their reactions to reroll the failed saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus.
