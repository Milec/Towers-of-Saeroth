# Channeler's Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 22

---

You enter a stance that allows power to flow through you. While in this stance, whenever you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast]] or [[Actions/Sustain]] an apparition spell or vessel spell that deals energy damage, you gain a status bonus to the spell’s damage equal to the spell’s rank.

Each time you Cast a Spell that has the [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] or [[Traits/Void|void]] traits and that restores Hit Points while in this stance, the spells’ targets gain a status bonus to the initial amount of healing received equal to the spell’s rank. This bonus healing does not apply to healing over time effects (such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration (Monster Core)|regeneration]]).
