# Swordlord Exile  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]; you have broken or forsaken the Aldori swordpact.

---

You are a swordlord exile. You have abandoned or been abandoned by the Aldori Academy. Aldori swordlords are honor-bound to challenge you on sight. Additionally, your unbridled skill strikes terror in those around you. You can use Dueling [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] instead of Intimidation to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] and [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] while your [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]] is visible to the target of your attempt.

With broken pride, your blade is now a disgraced tool used to remove obstacles between you and your goals, whatever they may be. The first time you hit a creature with an Aldori dueling sword each round, you deal 5 additional precision damage.

**Special** You can’t take both this feat and [[Feats/Crossbow Infiltrator Dedication|Aldori Swordlord]].
