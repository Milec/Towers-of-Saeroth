# Wild-haired Fury  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 81

---

Your broken bones jut at odd angles, yet this pain only adds to your anger and prowess. You add the combined value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] conditions as a circumstance bonus to damage rolls with your melee attacks (for example, if you were doomed 1 and wounded 2, you would gain a +3 circumstance bonus to your melee damage rolls).
