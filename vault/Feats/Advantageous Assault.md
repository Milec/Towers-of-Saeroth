# Advantageous Assault single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 147

---

When an enemy's movement is compromised, you deliver a more deadly blow. Make a Strike against a creature that is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]]. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage on this Strike equal to the number of weapon damage dice, or that number + 2 if you're wielding the weapon in two hands. The Strike gains the following failure effect.

**Failure** You deal damage to the target equal to the number of weapon damage dice, or that number + 2 if you're wielding the weapon in two hands. This damage has the same damage type as the weapon.
