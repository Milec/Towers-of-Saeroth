# Gnome Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Gnome Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your gnome affinity blends with your class training, granting you great skill with gnome weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in a given weapon or weapons, you also gain that proficiency in the [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]], and all [[Traits/Gnome|gnome]] weapons in which you are trained.
