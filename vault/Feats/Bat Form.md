You can transform into a bat. You gain the effects of a 4th-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]] spell. At 14th level, you can choose to spend two actions instead of one to gain the effects of a 4th- or 5th-level _aerial form_ spell instead (bat form only).
