# Stonemason's Eye  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 44

---

You understand the intricacies of stonework. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you're already trained in Crafting, you instead gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat for stonemasonry.

You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to notice unusual stonework. This bonus applies to checks to discover mechanical traps made of stone or hidden within stone.

If you aren't using the [[Actions/Seek]] action or searching, the GM automatically rolls a secret check for you to notice unusual stonework anyway. This check doesn't gain your normal circumstance bonus.
