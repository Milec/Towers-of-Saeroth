# Desperate Revival ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 40

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You would be reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed.

---

On the verge of death, you drain the life of all around you in a desperate attempt to remain alive. You avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point. Each creature in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your spell DC. You regain Hit Points equal to the total damage dealt, up to a maximum of half your maximum Hit Points.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes [[Traits/Void|void]] damage equal to half its level.  

**Failure** The target takes void damage equal to its level.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes void damage equal to double its level.
