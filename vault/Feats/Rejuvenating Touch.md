# Rejuvenating Touch  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Blessed One]] (Level 20), [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]] (Level 20)

**Prerequisites**
devotion spell ([[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]])

---

Your healing energies linger after you cast, providing continual benefits. An ally that recovers Hit Points from your [[Spells/Lay on Hands|_lay on hands_]] gains 10 temporary Hit Points at the start of their turn during each of the next 10 rounds. This effect ends if the ally is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
