# March of Law single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 27

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Frequency**
once per round

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]]

**Requirements**
The target has attempted a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against you since your previous turn.

---

When faced with lawbreakers, you become almost unstoppable, and your purposeful steps instill terror. Stride toward the required target with a +10-foot status bonus to your Speeds and ignoring [[Rules/Forced Movement|difficult terrain]]. If you end your movement adjacent to the required target, each enemy adjacent to you who saw your movement becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]]. You can use March of Law while Burrowing, Climbing, Flying, or Swimming instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
