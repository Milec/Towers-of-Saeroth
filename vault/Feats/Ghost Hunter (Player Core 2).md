# Ghost Hunter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker (Player Core 2)|Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 49

---

Your connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]] ensures that your blows strike true against spectral beings. Your weapon and unarmed Strikes against [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures become [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]. If they are already magical, they instead gain the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune.
