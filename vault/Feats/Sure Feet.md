# Sure Feet  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 52

---

Whether keeping your balance or scrambling up a tricky climb, your hairy, calloused feet easily find purchase. If you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] or an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] when you attempt to Balance or Climb.
