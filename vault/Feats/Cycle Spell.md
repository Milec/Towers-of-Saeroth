# Cycle Spell  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 26

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Soul Cycle|Soul Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Soul Warden Dedication]]

---

Your devotion to the cycle of souls grants you an additional divine spell. Choose from [[Spells/Bless|_bless_]], [[Spells/Disrupting Weapons|_disrupting weapons_]], or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]]. You must make this selection when you take this feat and it can't be changed. Once per day, while your spiral is glowing, you can cast the selected spell as a divine innate spell.
