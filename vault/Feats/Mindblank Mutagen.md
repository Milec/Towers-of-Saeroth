# Mindblank Mutagen  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 81

---

With a minor adjustment of ratios in the formula for your [[Equipment/Serene Mutagen|serene mutagen]], you gain mental protections. When you’re affected by a serene mutagen, [[Traits/Detection|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]], and [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] effects of 9th level or lower detect nothing from you or your possessions and auras. For instance, [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] would still detect other magic in the area, but not any magic on you.
