# Plant Shape  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 138

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]] or [[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

You can take the form of a plant creature. Add the forms listed in [[Spells/Plant Form|_plant form_]] to your [[Feats/Wild Shape|wild shape]] list; if you don't have _wild shape_, you can instead cast _plant form_ once per day, heightened to the same level as your highest-level druid spell slot. Whenever you're polymorphed into another form using _wild shape_, you gain resistance 5 to poison.
