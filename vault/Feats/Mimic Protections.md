# Mimic Protections  *Feat 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 72

---

By adopting _resonant reflections_, you have learned to copy the abilities of other creatures as well. When you use [[Actions/Hunt Prey]] on a foe within 100 feet, you gain resistance 10 to all damage types that target has resistance or immunity to for as long as the foe remains within 100 feet.
