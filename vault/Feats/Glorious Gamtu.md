# Glorious Gamtu single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin (Player Core)|Goblin]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 35

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Dokkaebi Goblin]] heritage

**Access**
 dokkaebi goblin heritage

---

You can access the mystical power that dokkaebi refer to as a hat. You conjure a magical gamtu hat, which is a magic item of light Bulk. The gamtu persists for 1 hour, and you can give the gamtu to another creature to wear.

**Activate—Hat Trick** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You tap the hat and gain the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] spell, which lasts for the spell's normal duration, until the hat is removed, or until the hat's normal duration runs out, whichever comes first. After the spell ends, the gamtu disappears.
