# Blessed Spell single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 190

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Blessed One (Player Core 2)|Blessed One]]

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Blessed One Dedication (Player Core 2)|Blessed One Dedication]]; [[Feats/Mercy (Player Core 2)|Mercy]]; ability to cast spells from spell slots

---

When you focus your magic on an ally, you can remove harmful conditions. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from a spell slot, and that spell targets only a single ally, you can also attempt to remove a harmful condition from that ally. The condition must be one that could be removed by your [[Feats/Mercy (Player Core 2)|Mercy]] feat, including those granted by later feats such as [[Feats/Greater Mercy (Player Core 2)|Greater Mercy]]. Attempt a counteract check based on the spell’s DC and rank. This effect is in addition to the normal effects of your spell.
