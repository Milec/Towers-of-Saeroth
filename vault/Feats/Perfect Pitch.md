# Perfect Pitch  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 82

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]] (Level 12), [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]] or [[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]

---

Your ear for harmony has enhanced your ability with musical magic. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] to [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]] or [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]] spells and effects that are musical in nature. In addition, you gain the Pitch-Perfect Projection action.

**Pitch-Perfect Projection** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) By using your perfect pitch, you can amplify your voice to cause magical auditory effects to reach further. If your next action this round is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] or use an action that creates an auditory effect with a range, increase that range by 30 feet.
