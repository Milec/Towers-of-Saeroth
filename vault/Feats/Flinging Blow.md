# Flinging Blow two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 130

---

You take a moment to center yourself, then unleash a powerful blow that sends your target tumbling away. Make an unarmed Strike. If you hit, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be pushed up to 10 feet directly away from you (or 20 feet, on a critical failure) and fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. If another creature or physical object would prevent this movement, both your target and the blocking creature or object take 1d6 bludgeoning damage per 5 feet of movement that was prevented.
