# Petal Step  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**

You have a mastery over your own movement and can distribute your weight to dance lightly over the ground like petals drifting on the water. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and aren’t detectable by [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]].
