# Avenger Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Rackets/Avenger]]

---

Your training regimen has given you particular advantages when it comes to enforcing the interests of your faith. You gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] spells and effects that deal [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]], [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]], or [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] as long as you are in a town or city with a church dedicated to your deity, extracting clues from the faithful with displays of your piety.
