# Tumbling Diversion single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

Attempt to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] an opponent's space. If you succeed or critically succeed and don't end your movement adjacent to that opponent, you can attempt to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] to distract that opponent. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the Deception check (or a +2 circumstance bonus if you critically succeeded at the Acrobatics check to Tumble Through) to Create a Diversion. If you succeed or critically succeed to Create a Diversion, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to only the creature whose space you Tumbled Through.
