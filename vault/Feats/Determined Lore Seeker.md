# Determined Lore Seeker  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 45

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scrollmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lore Seeker]]

---

You've mastered useful spells that let you find hidden truths others might miss, and a preferred spell comes naturally to you. When you gain this feat, choose one of the innate spells granted by the [[Feats/Lore Seeker]] feat: [[Spells/Comprehend Language|_comprehend language_]], [[Spells/Restore Senses|_restore senses_]], or [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]]. You can cast this spell three times per day instead of once per day.

 When you cast any of the spells granted by Lore Seeker as innate spells, they gain additional effects. When you cast _comprehend language_, the spell is heightened to 4th level. When you cast _restore senses_, the spell is heightened to half your level, rounded up. When you cast _see invisibility_, for the duration of the spell you can use an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to see invisible creatures normally (instead of seeing them as translucent shapes that are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to you) until the end of your next turn.
