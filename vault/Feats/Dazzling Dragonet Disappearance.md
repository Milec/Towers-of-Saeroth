# Dazzling Dragonet Disappearance ⤾  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 213

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
An adjacent enemy hits you with an [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]].

---

A brilliant sheen on your scales flashes when you’re hit, then you’re nowhere to be found. The triggering enemy becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round, and you become affected by a 4th-rank [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] spell.
