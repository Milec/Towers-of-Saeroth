# Crown of the Saumen Kar  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 10

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbound Initiate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pactbound Dedication]]

---

Your body begins to evolve and adapt, taking the first steps towards blending your very self with the ancient curse of the saumen kar. You become larger and bulkier, pushing the very edge of height and weight for your size. You gain [[Rules/Scent|scent]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks made to [[Actions/Sneak]] or [[Actions/Hide]] in areas of forest and snow. You sprout a pair of horns from your forehead, gaining a horns [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage. Your horns are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Shove|shove]] and [[Traits/Trip|trip]] traits.
