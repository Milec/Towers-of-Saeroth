# Breath Like Honey  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Sweetbreath Kholo]] heritage

---

You smell of honey and savory things. Your circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] if the target can smell your breath increases to +2. In addition, you can cast [[Spells/Enthrall (Player Core)|_enthrall_]] as a 3rd-rank occult innate spell once per day, except the spell has a range of 30 feet and the [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait. Targets don't gain any circumstance bonus for disagreeing with you.
