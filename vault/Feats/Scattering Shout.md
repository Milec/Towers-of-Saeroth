# Scattering Shout three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

---

With a flex of your mighty thews and a guttural bellow that can make ears bleed, you drive back any enemies surrounding you. Each enemy within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is pushed 5 feet away from you.  

**Failure** The creature is pushed 10 feet away from you and takes 8d6 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is pushed 15 feet away from you and takes 16d6 sonic damage.
