# Improbable Elixirs  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 69

---

Your mastery of alchemical secrets enables you to replicate effects most believe can be achieved only via magic. Select a number of [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|potions]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1); these potions must be of 9th level or lower. You gain formulas to create these potions as alchemical consumables with the [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] trait. When making these alchemical elixirs, you can substitute alchemical reagents for an equal value of magical components, and you can use an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist's toolkit]] (for [[Actions/Quick Alchemy (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]]) or an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Lab (Player Core)|alchemist's lab]] (for the [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] activity) instead of any other required toolkits. Other than that, the formula doesn't change. Once you've chosen the potion formulas, they can't be changed.
