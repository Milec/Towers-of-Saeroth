# Cautious Word free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 113

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Five Branches of Scholarship|Magaambyan Attendant]]

**Frequency**
once per round

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Uzunjati Storytelling]]; ability to cast [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]]

**Trigger**
An enemy hits you with a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].

**Requirements**
You’re currently unable to cast _shield_ because you used [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] with it.

---

You let your words defend you, just as your allies in the Vellumis Scholars defend through knowledge. You immediately cast _shield_ and gain its bonus to AC even though you normally couldn’t cast it, and you become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.
