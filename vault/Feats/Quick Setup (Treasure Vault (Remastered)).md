# Quick Setup  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 159

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

_Note from Nethys:_ this skill feat is intended to be used with the [[Rules/Complex Crafting (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Complex Crafting]] variant crafting rules.

If you reduce the setup time for a batch of [[Rules/Consumables (GM Core)|consumables]] at least 6 levels lower than you to less than 1 day, you can set it up in just 2 hours (allowing you to do so during an adventuring day if you have access to your tools and materials). If you rush the setup of a permanent item at least 6 levels lower than you and reduce the setup time to less than 1 day, you can perform the setup in 4 hours.
