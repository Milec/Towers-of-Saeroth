# Stone Blood  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Monolith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ka Stone Ritual]]

---

You can selectively stop your bleeding with ease. Reduce the DC of flat checks to stop persistent bleed damage you have from 15 to 10, and you can use a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait to gain an early flat check without physically binding the wounds. You recover naturally from the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition twice as quickly as normal.
