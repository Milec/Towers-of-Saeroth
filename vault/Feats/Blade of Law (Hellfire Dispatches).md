# Blade of Law two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 26

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Preferment]]

**Requirements**
The target has attempted a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]] against you since your previous turn.

---

You call upon the power of law against a foe who would dare stand against you. Make a weapon or unarmed Strike against the target. This counts as two Strikes for your multiple attack penalty. The Strike deals one extra weapon damage die and 1d8 spirit damage. At 18th level, this spirit damage increases to 2d8.
