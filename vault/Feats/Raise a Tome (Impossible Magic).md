# Raise a Tome  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 17

---

You can flip open a book to simultaneously study and deflect attacks. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]], you can raise a book you’re wielding instead of a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]]. While you have a book raised, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] checks to identify creatures using a skill related to the subject of the book ([[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] if you’re using your spellbook). If consulting the book normally grants an item bonus to the check for consulting the book, you gain this bonus as well.

If you have [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]], you can use a raised tome for that feat as though it were a shield. The tome has Hardness 3, HP 12, and BT 6.

In addition, you can inscribe the [[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|_reinforcing rune_]] into your spellbook to strengthen your tomes, using the same process as [[Rules/Transferring Runes (GM Core)|transferring a rune]]. The rune takes the form of a specialized diagram in your spellbook, occupying one page. The rune’s benefits apply to any one tome in your possession chosen during your daily preparations. If your spellbook leaves your possession, the rune-granting benefit is suppressed until you get the book back.
