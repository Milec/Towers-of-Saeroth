# Unbelievable Luck  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 143

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unexpected Sharpshooter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication]]

---

While no one, not even you, might appear to understand why, you demonstrate a greater ability to hit your targets regardless of the obstacles in your way. You can use the [[Feats/Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Accidental Shot]] activity granted to you by the Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication once per hour instead of once per day.
