# Runtsage  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

Unlike most of your kind, who dismiss goblins as embarrassments or expendable annoyances, you have studied the methodology behind their irresponsible and incomprehensible actions. You gain the [[Feats/Adopted Ancestry (Player Core)|Adopted Ancestry]] general feat and must select [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]] as the feat's chosen ancestry. You also gain one 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|goblin ancestry feat]].
