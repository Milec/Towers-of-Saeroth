# Barrier of Boreal Frost three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 33

---

You form an intricate structure of ice, such as a wall of bricks made of packed snow or a screen of enormous icicles. This has the effect of a [[Spells/Wall of Ice|_wall of ice_]] spell with a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. It can only be a wall (not a hemisphere), and you choose whether the ice is transparent or opaque. The wall lasts until the end of your next turn, but you can Sustain it up to 1 minute.
