# Channel the Godmind two actions  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 55

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aeonbound]]

---

You tap into the collective consciousness of the Godmind, the governing entity of axiomite society, to rapidly parse near-infinite variables to read your surroundings. For 1 minute, you gain precise motion sense out to 60 feet, you can see invisible creatures and objects, and you gain the benefits of [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] (with a counteract check modifier equal to your Perception modifier).
