**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

---

The spotlight should always be on you and you alone. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check against the Will DCs of all enemies within 30 feet who can see or hear you. On a success, the targets focus on you and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to hit you, but they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to all creatures other than yourself. On a critical success, the effect lasts for 3 rounds.
