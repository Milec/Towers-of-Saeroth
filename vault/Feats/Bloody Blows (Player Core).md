# Bloody Blows  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 72

---

Your lethal unarmed attacks leave bloody gouges or cause severe internal bleeding. When you critically hit with a [[Actions/Strike]] using an unarmed attack that isn’t [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], the target takes 1d4 persistent bleed damage. This can be because you’re taking the penalty to use a fist for a lethal attack or because you have an unarmed attack without the nonlethal trait due to Tusks or a similar ability.
