With a burst of divine liberation, your ally's movement from your [[Actions/Liberating Step (Player Core 2)|Liberating Step]] is unaffected by [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], greater difficult terrain, [[Rules/Narrow Surfaces (Player Core)|narrow surfaces]], and [[Rules/Uneven Ground (Player Core)|uneven ground]]. If you have the exalted reaction benefit, this applies to everyone who Steps.
