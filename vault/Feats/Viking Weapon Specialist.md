# Viking Weapon Specialist  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 198

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Viking]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Viking Weapon Familiarity]]

---

You are even more skilled in the weapons of your people. You gain the critical specialization effects of the [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet|hatchet]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]]. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in certain weapons, you also gain that proficiency rank in the above four weapons.
