# Mature Animal Companion (Ranger)  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 173

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wylderheart]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Animal Companion]]

---

Your animal companion grows up, becoming a [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|mature animal companion]] and gaining additional capabilities. If you have the [[Actions/Hunt Prey]] action, your animal companion assaults the prey even without your orders. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action, your animal companion can still use 1 action that round on your turn to Stride toward or Strike your prey.
