# Embed Aeon Stone  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 240

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Runelord Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You discover the secrets to embedding [[Equipment/Aeon Stone|_aeon stones_]] into your flesh. You spend 1 day attuning to an _aeon stone_ and physically embedding it in your skin. While the stone is embedded this way, you gain the benefits of the _aeon stone_ as if it were orbiting above your head, but it protects the stone from being noticed or stolen as easily. _Aeon stones_ in your flesh must be invested to function, as usual.

 You can also use this activity to safely remove an embedded _aeon stone_ in 1 day. Someone without this feat can attempt to surgically remove it safely by spending 1 day and succeeding at a DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check, or hastily by simply ripping it from a corpse.
