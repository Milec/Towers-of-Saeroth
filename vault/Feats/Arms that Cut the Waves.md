# Arms that Cut the Waves  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

---

River, lake, sea, or ocean, the water calls to you, and you don’t fear its depths. When you attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. When you would run out of air and begin [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|suffocating]], you can instead spend a Mythic Point to hold your breath for 10 more rounds.
