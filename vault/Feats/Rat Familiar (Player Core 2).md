# Rat Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 30

---

You have a pet rat that has become magically bonded to you. You gain a [[Rules/Familiars (Player Core)|familiar]], and this familiar must be a rat. It still gets the benefits of familiar abilities, but its base form remains a rat.
