# Ghostly Resonance  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

---

Your runes don’t just draw power from the world of the spirits— they can let even the most mundane objects harm spirits as well. When you etch or trace a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] rune on a weapon or creature, you can grant it the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] weapon property rune. For a weapon, this doesn’t count toward the weapon’s limit on property runes. For a creature, the rune applies to all unarmed Strikes the creature makes. This benefit lasts as long as the rune remains.
