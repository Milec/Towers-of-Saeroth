When you succeed at identifying a creature using [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against your attacks until the start of your next turn; if you critically succeed, it's flat-footed against your attacks for 1 minute.
