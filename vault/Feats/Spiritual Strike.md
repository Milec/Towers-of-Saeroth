# Spiritual Strike two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scions of Domora]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scion of Domora Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a weapon with at least one [[Rules/Fundamental Runes|fundamental rune]] and your familiar is adjacent to you or in your space.

---

Your spirit guide enhances your attacks with occult power. Make a Strike with a weapon that has at least one fundamental rune. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. The Strike has the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deals an additional 2d6 force damage. If your weapon has a [[Equipment/Striking|_major striking_]] rune, this Strike instead deals 3d6 force damage.
