# Sudden Mindfulness ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 55

**Trigger**
You roll a success on a saving throw against an effect that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]].

---

Your mind retains full awareness despite the best attempts of others, and your focus throws others off-balance. You get a critical success on the triggering saving throw, and if the triggering effect was caused by a creature, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to you until the end of your next turn.
