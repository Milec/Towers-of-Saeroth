# Marshal Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 180

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in martial weapons; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

You've combined your social skills and combat training to become a talented combat leader. Choose [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]. You become trained in that skill or become an expert if you were already trained in it.

 In addition, you're surrounded by a marshal's aura in a 10-foot emanation. Your aura has the [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], and [[Traits/Visual|visual]] traits and grants you and allies within the aura a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear|fear]].

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the marshal archetype.
