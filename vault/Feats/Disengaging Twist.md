**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Trigger**
A creature gives you the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] condition.

---

Your ability to twist your opponents' bodies into painful locks and holds makes you particularly adept at escaping such predicaments. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Escape]] the triggering condition. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check.
