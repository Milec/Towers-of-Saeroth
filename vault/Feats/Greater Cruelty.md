# Greater Cruelty  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 120

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cruelty]]

---

The extent of the corruption within you does more than simply sap your foes' strength, it devastates their minds and reflexes. When you use [[Feats/Cruelty]], choose whether you want to make the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] as normal, to make it [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] on a failed save (clumsy 2 on a critical failure), or to make it [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] on a failed save (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
