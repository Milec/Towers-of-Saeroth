# Elemental Shape  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 133

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Untamed Form]]

---

You understand the fundamental elements of nature such that you can imbue them into your body and manifest as a living embodiment of those elements. Add the shapes in [[Spells/Elemental Form (Player Core)|_elemental form_]] to your untamed form list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another shape using untamed form, you gain resistance 5 to fire.
