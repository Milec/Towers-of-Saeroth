# Can't You See?  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 64

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

The eye rebels, the mind recoils—no matter how much those who can see you try to explain what's there, their friends' gazes just skitter over you, like a bird afraid to land. A character who attempts to [[Actions/Point Out (Player Core)|Point Out]] your location must attempt a DC 14 flat check. If they fail, their allies misunderstand them and aren't sure where you are. On a critical failure, their allies think they pointed you out in a different location entirely, chosen by the GM. Similarly, when a creature critically fails to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] you while you're [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by it, it thinks you're in a different location chosen by the GM. In either case, you appear to be hidden to a creature that thinks you're in a different location, though you're actually undetected by it for targeting and further uses of the Seek action.
