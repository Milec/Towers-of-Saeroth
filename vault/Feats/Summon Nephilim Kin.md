# Summon Nephilim Kin  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
any [[Ancestries/Nephilim|nephilim]] lineage feat

---

You have a connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss|Outer Planes]], allowing you to summon a divine ally. Choose either [[Spells/Summon Celestial|_summon celestial_]], [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]], or another 5th-rank spell capable of summoning an extraplanar creature appropriate to your lineage. Once per day, you can cast the chosen spell as a 5th-rank divine [[Rules/Innate Spells|innate spell]], but the creature summoned must be from the same category as your own lineage, such as a creature with the [[Traits/Angel|angel]] trait if you are an [[Feats/Angelkin|angelkin]].
