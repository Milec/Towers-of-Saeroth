# Lost in the Crowd  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lion Blade (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lion Blade Dedication (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade Dedication]]

---

You’ve learned how to move as one with crowds and hide among them, vanishing into a busy street in the flicker of an eye. You move at full Speed in crowds and can use [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from crowds to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks when in a crowd of at least 10 creatures and a +4 circumstance bonus to your Stealth checks when in a crowd of at least 100 creatures.
