# Conceited Mindset  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Evil|tenets of evil]]

---

Because you know your mind is superior, you scoff at anyone who would alter your mental state—but this hubris gets the better of you when you're wrong. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. If you succeed at a save against a mental effect, you critically succeed instead; similarly, if you fail at a save against a mental effect, you critically fail instead.
