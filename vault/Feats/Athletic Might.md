**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

---

Surviving in hostile terrain has given you a great talent for mobility. Whenever you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]] or [[Actions/Swim]], you get a critical success instead.
