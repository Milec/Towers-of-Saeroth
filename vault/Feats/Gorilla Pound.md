# Gorilla Pound single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 129

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]; [[Feats/Gorilla Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Gorilla Stance.

---

You pound your chest before slamming into your foes. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]], then make one gorilla slam Strike against the same target. If your Strike hits, you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to triple the value of the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition.

**Special** If you have this feat, while you are in Gorilla Stance, you gain a climb Speed of 15 feet.
