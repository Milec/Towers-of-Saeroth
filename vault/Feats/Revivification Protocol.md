# Revivification Protocol free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Android]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 73

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You have the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition and are about to attempt a recovery check.

---

Your nanites are programmed to automatically revive you. You're restored to 1 Hit Point, lose the dying and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] conditions, and can act normally on this turn. You gain or increase the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition as normal when losing the dying condition in this way.
