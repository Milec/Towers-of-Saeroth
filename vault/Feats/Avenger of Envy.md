# Avenger of Envy  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

---

You've become the champion of the runelords of envy. Their support gives you a heightened awareness of your own failings, but you can call their spirits to your side to protect you from harm. The first time each day you critically fail a skill check or saving throw, you gain 1 Mythic Point. You gain the Aegis of Envy activity.

**Activate—Aegis of Envy** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Spend 1 Mythic Point. The spirits of eight runelords of envy (Naaft, Tannaris, Ivamura, Jurah, Chalsardra, Esedrea, Zarve, Desamelia, and Phirandi) swirl about you for 1 minute. While at least one of the spirits remains, whenever you take damage, you can use a reaction that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait to gain resistance 10 to that damage, or resistance 20 if the damage was dealt by Xanderghul, the runelord of pride. Each time you use this feat to gain resistance, one of the spirits disappears.

## Vengeance Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 84

During Chapter 2 of this adventure, the PCs can take on the vengeful mantle of a specific runelord's vengeance and embody that need to avenge the violations Runelord Xanderghul has inflicted upon their legacies by creating risen runelords. These rewards manifest in the form of a bonus feat, chosen from the following. A PC can only select one vengeance feat. If you wish to use these feats in the context of another campaign, a mythic PC should be required to use one of their mythic feat slots granted from their mythic destiny.
