# Guardian's Deflection ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 165

**Trigger**
An ally within your melee reach is hit by an attack, you can see the attacker, and a +2 circumstance bonus to AC would turn the critical hit into a hit or the hit into a miss.

**Requirements**
You are wielding a single one-handed melee weapon and have your other hand or hands free.

---

You use your weapon to deflect the attack against your ally, granting a +2 circumstance bonus to their AC against the triggering attack. This turns the triggering critical hit into a hit, or the triggering hit into a miss. You gain panache until the end of your next turn.
