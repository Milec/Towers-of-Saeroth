# Mythic Casting single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 84

---

You can infuse mythic power directly into your spells to make them exceptionally potent. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; if the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]], use mythic proficiency to determine your [[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack rolls]] and save DC for that spell.
