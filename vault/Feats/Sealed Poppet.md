# Sealed Poppet  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 64

---

You've reinforced your interior parts with sealant or other flame-retardant materials, protecting you from fire. You no longer have the weakness to [[Traits/Fire|fire]] from the flammable ability.

**Special** You can't take this feat if you're a [[Ancestries/Heritages/Stuffed Poppet|stuffed poppet]].
