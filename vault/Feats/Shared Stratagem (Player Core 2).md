# Shared Stratagem  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 107

---

The plans you make include your allies as well as yourself. When you hit a creature with an attack on which you substituted your attack roll due to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devising a Stratagem]], designate one ally. The creature you hit is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to that ally on the next attack that ally makes against that creature before the start of your next turn.
