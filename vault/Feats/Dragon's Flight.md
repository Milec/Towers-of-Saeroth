# Dragon's Flight single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 46

**Frequency**
once per round

---

You have grown a small pair of draconic wings or have honed your use of the wings you've had since birth. You [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]]. If you don't normally have a fly Speed, you gain a fly Speed of 20 feet for this movement. If you aren't on solid ground at the end of this movement, you fall.
