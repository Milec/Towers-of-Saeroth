**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

Your eidolon evolves more acute senses. Your eidolon gains [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]], as well as [[Rules/Scent (Player Core)|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet.
