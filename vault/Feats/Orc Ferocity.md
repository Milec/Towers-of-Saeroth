**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 59

**Trigger**

---

Fierceness in battle runs through your blood, and you refuse to fall from your injuries. You avoid being knocked out and remain at 1 Hit Point, and your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition increases by 1.
