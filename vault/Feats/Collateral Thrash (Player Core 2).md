# Collateral Thrash  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 84

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Thrash (Player Core 2)|Thrash]]

---

You smack the foe you're thrashing into someone else. When you Thrash, choose another enemy adjacent to the grabbed enemy. That enemy also takes your Thrash damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC.
