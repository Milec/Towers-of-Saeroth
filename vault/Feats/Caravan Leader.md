# Caravan Leader  *Feat 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 204

**Prerequisites**
Constitution 18; [[Feats/Pick up the Pace]]

---

You know how to get the most effort out of your allies on the road. When [[Actions/Hustle|Hustling]] in a group during exploration mode, your group can Hustle for as long as the member who could Hustle longest on their own, plus an additional 20 minutes beyond that.
