# Pollinate ⤾  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Conrasu]], [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 70

**Prerequisites**
[[Traits/Ardande|ardande]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]

**Trigger**
You take bludgeoning damage.

---

As you’re hit, you emit a dense cloud of pollen, obscuring you from sight. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the beginning of your next turn.

**Special** This feat gains the trait for your ancestry.
