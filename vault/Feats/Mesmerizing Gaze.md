# Mesmerizing Gaze two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 166

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Celebrity]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication]]

---

When you meet someone's gaze, they are unable to look away. Choose one target creature. That creature must succeed at a Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] until the end of your next turn; the DC for this save is equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. If the creature succeeds at its save or its fascination ends due to a hostile action, it becomes temporarily immune to your Mesmerizing Gaze for 1 day.

When you select this feat, it gains either the [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]] or [[Traits/Occult|occult]] trait; once you make this choice, you can't change it.
