# Shoving Sweep ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mauler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mauler Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach leaves a square during a move action it's using.

**Requirements**
You're wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You swing your weapon at your foe, rebuffing them back. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] the triggering creature, ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. The creature continues its movement after the Shove.
