# Combat Assessment single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 126

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Butterfly Blade]] (Level 4), [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]] (Level 2)

---

You make a telegraphed attack to learn about your foe. Make a melee Strike. On a hit, you can immediately attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about the target. On a critical hit, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check to Recall Knowledge. The target is temporarily immune to Combat Assessment for 1 day.
