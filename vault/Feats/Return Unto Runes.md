# Return Unto Runes ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 53

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
An enemy targets you with a spell attack or other magical attack against your AC.

---

You attempt to deconstruct an incoming spell into its individual magical components, storing them temporarily as a rune. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the spell becomes sealed in a rune, which floats around you. Until the end of your next turn, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace this Rune]] onto a creature as normal. This rune doesn’t have an effect until it’s invoked.

When the rune is invoked, the rune-bearer is affected by the spell or magical attack captured in the rune. Make a check, using your class DC – 10 as the modifier against the rune‑bearer’s AC to determine the effects.
