# Oath of The Slayer  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Oath]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 276

---

During your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], you can swear an oath to defeat, topple, or destroy a certain kind of enemy during your deeds that day. Choose [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberrations]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestials]], [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragons]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. You can’t choose celestials if you’re [[Traits/Holy|holy]], nor can you choose fiends if you’re [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]. You gain an edict to banish or slay creatures of that kind.

Your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] and devotion spells that deal damage do an additional 1 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage against a creature with the chosen trait. This damage increases to 2 at 7th level and 3 at 14th level. If a creature with the chosen trait triggers your champion’s reaction, this additional damage doubles until the end of your next turn.
