# Hurl at the Horizon  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 35

---

Your weapon flies from your hand as if propelled under its own power. The imbued ikon gains the following ability.

**Immanence** Your weapon gains the [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown]] 15 feet trait, or increases its thrown distance by 10 feet if it already has the thrown trait.
