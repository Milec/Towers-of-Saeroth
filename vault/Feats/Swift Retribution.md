# Swift Retribution  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 99

**Prerequisites**
champion's reaction

---

The transgressions of your enemies fuel you to retaliate with divine speed. When an enemy triggers your champion's reaction, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] on your next turn. You can use the extra action to move closer to that enemy or to Strike that enemy.
