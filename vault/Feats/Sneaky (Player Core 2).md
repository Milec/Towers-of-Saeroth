# Sneaky  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

Stealth is an important tool in your arsenal. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] action, up to your Speed.

In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your Stealth check, you don't become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]] if you don't have [[Rules/Cover|cover]] or greater cover and aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] at the end of the Sneak action, as long as you have cover or greater cover or are concealed at the end of your turn.
