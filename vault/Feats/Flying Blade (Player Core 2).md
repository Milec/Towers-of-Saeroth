# Flying Blade  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 163

**Prerequisites**
precise strike

---

You apply your flashy techniques to thrown weapons as easily as melee attacks. You apply your precise strike damage on ranged Strikes you make with a thrown weapon within that weapon's first range increment. The thrown weapon must be an agile or finesse weapon. This also allows you to make a thrown weapon ranged Strike for [[Actions/Confident Finisher (Player Core 2)|Confident Finisher]] and any other [[Traits/Finisher (Player Core 2)|finisher]] that includes a Strike that can benefit from your precise strike.
