# Arcane Shroud single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 48

**Frequency**
once per turn

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], [[Actions/Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] from a spell slot or make a [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a spell from a spell slot.

---

Your magic has a powerful aftereffect, briefly granting you a certain spell. When you take this feat, choose three of [[Spells/False Vitality|_false vitality_]], [[Spells/Fire Shield (Player Core)|_fire shield_]], [[Spells/Fleet Step (Player Core)|_fleet step_]], [[Spells/Flicker|_flicker_]], [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]], [[Spells/Mountain Resilience|_mountain resilience_]], and [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]]. You use [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] and are subject to an additional aftereffect spell of your choice from the three you selected. This aftereffect spell’s duration lasts until the end of your next turn or its normal duration, whichever is shorter. Using Arcane Shroud again ends any existing spell you gained from Arcane Shroud.
