# Witness of Earth  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 81

---

The primal magic in your soul offers the patient earth’s timeless counsel. You learn the [[Rituals/Atone (Player Core)|atone]] ritual. When you use the atone ritual on yakshas and you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
