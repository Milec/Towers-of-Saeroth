# Five-breath Vanguard Dedication  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 90

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Five-breath Vanguard]]

**Prerequisites**
Two of the five elemental stances ([[Feats/Ironblood Stance (Player Core 2)|Ironblood Stance]], [[Feats/Mountain Stance (Player Core 2)|Mountain Stance]], [[Feats/Reflective Ripple Stance]], [[Feats/Stoked Flame Stance]], and [[Feats/Tangled Forest Stance (Player Core 2)|Tangled Forest Stance]])

**Access**
Tian Xia origin or exposure to Tian elementalism

---

You flow like the elemental cycle, adapting your stance and techniques constantly in response to whatever circumstances you face. You gain the Cycle Elemental Stance action.

**Activate** single action Cycle Elemental Stance;    **Requirements** You’re in one of the elemental stances; **Effect** [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], and then enter a different elemental stance from the one you’re currently in.
