# Bolster Soul Cage  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Soul Cage|Lich]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lich Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

Your constant efforts to augment and perfect your [_soul cage_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1545) have bolstered its efficiency. Your _soul cage_ has Hardness equal to your level and HP equal to four times your level. The time it takes your _soul cage_ to rebuild your body is reduced to 1d6 days rather than 1d10 days.

 At 20th level, the time it takes your _soul cage_ to rebuild your body is reduced to 2d12+12 hours.
