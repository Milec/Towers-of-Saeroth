# Know Your Own  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

---

You've spent countless hours studying the history of elves on your world and beyond and are a studied expert in your people's ways. If you critically fail a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about elves, elven society, or elven history, you get a failure instead.
