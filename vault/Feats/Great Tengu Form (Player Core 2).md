# Great Tengu Form  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 35

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Long-Nosed Form (Player Core 2)|Long-Nosed Form]]

---

You take on the imposing form of a large, winged spirit. Once per day, as part of using Long-Nosed Form, you also gain the benefits of 4th-rank [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Fly|fly]]. This lasts for 5 minutes or until you shift out of your Long-Nosed Form, whichever happens first.
