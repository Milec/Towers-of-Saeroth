# Staggering Fire single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archer Dedication]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You've learned how to fire at enemies to slow them down, allowing innocents and fellow knights alike time to escape. Make a ranged Strike with a weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] weapon group. On a hit, the target takes a –5-foot circumstance penalty to its Speed until the end of its turn, or a –10-foot circumstance penalty on a critical hit.
