# Deadly Mutation  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 71

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ostilli Host Dedication]]

---

The dart fired by your ostilli is more dangerous. The damage dice of your Spit Ambient Magic increase to d8s, and when a target critically fails its save against the ability, it also takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. If you dealt a type of energy damage with Spit Ambient Magic (such as when dealing fire damage with [[Feats/Versatile Mutation]]), the persistent damage is damage of that type instead of bleed.
