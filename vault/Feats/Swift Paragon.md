# Swift Paragon  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 99

**Prerequisites**
blessed swiftness

---

The movement of you and your allies is swift and decisive as the judgment of your deity. If your ally starts a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action in your champion's aura, their movement during that action doesn't trigger reactions. In addition, you're permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]]. You can use your extra action only to Step or Stride. If you have a fly Speed, add [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] to this list. If you have an animal companion and are mounted on it at the start of your turn, you can have your mount be quickened that turn instead of you.
