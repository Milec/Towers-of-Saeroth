# Student of the Staff  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 20

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twisting Tree (Impossible Magic)|twisting tree hybrid study]]

---

You’ve studied the [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]] extensively to learn the hidden capabilities of your chosen weapon. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using a staff, you can apply the [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect of the [[Equipment/Weapons/Club (Player Core)|club]] group. (If you use the critical specialization on a [[Actions/Spellstrike]], the spell still affects the target even if you pushed the target out of reach.)

While you’re in [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, your staff gains the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d6]] trait, with the damage from the deadly die being the same damage type as the extra damage from Arcane Cascade.

In addition, you can place [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]] into a magic staff you prepare, even though a magic staff can’t typically hold property runes. You must first inscribe the property runes into your spellbook, using the same process as [[Rules/Transferring Runes (GM Core)|transferring a rune]]. The rune takes the form of a specialized diagram in your [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank) (Player Core)|spellbook]], occupying one page. You can’t inscribe runes that can’t be placed on a non-magical staff, nor can you inscribe the [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|_shifting_]] rune. When you prepare a staff, you can impart any number of property runes from your spellbook into the staff, up to the limit imposed by the staff’s [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|_weapon potency_]] rune.
