# Surging Interference ⤾  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 141

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wildspell]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wildspell Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature [[Rules/Cast a Spell|Casts a Spell]] within your [_spellsurge_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2150) aura.

---

You absorb the scattered energy of others’ failed spells, using it to recharge your own magic. You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the spell. If you’re successful, you absorb the dispersed magical energy, regaining one spell or spell slot of 8th rank or lower of your choice. If you spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] when you successfully counteract the triggering creature’s spell, you instead regain a 9th-rank spell or spell slot of your choice.
