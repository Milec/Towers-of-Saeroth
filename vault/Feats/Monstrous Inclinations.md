# Monstrous Inclinations  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 26

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Forest [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Ocean Lore as one of its apparition skills.

---

Your apparitions share with you the monstrous nature of those creatures who dwell in deep seas or ancient woods. You add [[Spells/Monstrosity Form (Player Core)|_monstrosity form_]] to your apparition spell repertoire, allowing you to cast it with your apparition spellcasting.

**Special** If you are attuned to a stalker in darkened boughs, add the phoenix form from [[Spells/Monstrosity Form (Player Core)|_monstrosity form_]] to your available [[Spells/Darkened Forest Form|_darkened forest form_]]  options when you cast the spell heightened to 8th rank.

If you are attuned to a lurker in devouring dark, add the cave worm and sea serpent forms to your [[Spells/Devouring Dark Form|_devouring dark form _]] options as a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect when you cast the spell heightened to 8th rank.
