# Arcane Sensitivity  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Edgewatch Detective]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Edgewatch Detective Dedication]]

---

You can sense the presence of the supernatural, even if you don't have any magical ability yourself. You can spend a single action (this action has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait) to determine if any magical auras are present in the area. This has the effects of a 1st-level [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] spell but isn't magical. By straining, you can also discover the source of the magic and its school (with the effect of a 4th-level _detect magic_ spell), but doing so prevents you from using this ability again until you've rested for 8 hours.
