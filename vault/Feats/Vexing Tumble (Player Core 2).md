# Vexing Tumble single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bravado]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 166

---

You tumble around your foes, expertly avoiding their reactions. Stride up to half your Speed and roll an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check. Compare the result to the Reflex DC of each enemy whose reach you began in or enter during the movement, in sequence.

**Critical Success** This movement doesn't trigger reactions from the enemy, and the enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you until the end of your turn  

**Success** This movement doesn't trigger reactions from the enemy.  

**Critical Failure** Your movement immediately stops when you enter the creature's reach; if you began in the creature's reach, you don't move.
