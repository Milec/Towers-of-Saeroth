# Emotionless  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Android]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 72

---

Your malfunctioning emotional processors make it difficult for you to feel strong emotions. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] and [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion or fear effect, you get a critical success instead.
