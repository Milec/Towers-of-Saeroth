# Schooled in Secrets  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 208

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]

---

You notice the signs and symbols that members of mystery cults and other secret societies use to declare their affiliation to fellow members. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] in place of [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Gather Information]] about such groups. If you belong to a secret cult, lodge, sect, or similar organization, you automatically recognize members of your group unless they are specifically attempting to conceal their presence from you.
