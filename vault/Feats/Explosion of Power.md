# Explosion of Power  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 154

---

Your magic explodes. You know the following blood magic effect.  
**Blood Magic—Explosion of Power** Raw power explodes outward from you. Each creature within a 5-foot emanation takes 1d6 damage per rank of the spell you just cast (basic Reflex save). The type of damage depends on the tradition of your bloodline. If you cast [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] spells, you deal [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage. If you cast [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] spells, you deal [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. If you cast [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spells, you deal [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage. If you cast [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] spells, you deal [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage.
