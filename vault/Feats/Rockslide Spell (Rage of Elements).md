# Rockslide Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist Dedication]]; earth is in your elemental philosophy

---

You enhance your spell with elemental earth, causing chunks of stone to litter the ground. If the next action you use is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that affects an area, a number of 5-foot squares in the area equal to the spell rank become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for 1 round. These squares must be on the ground, and the entire area of difficult terrain must be contiguous. The spell gains the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] trait.
