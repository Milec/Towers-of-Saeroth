# Clan Pistol  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 200

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

Raised in the ancient halls of Dongun Hold or the surrounding region, you display your lineage with a clan pistol, rather than a clan dagger. You get one clan pistol of your clan for free, as it was given to you at birth. This replaces your [[Equipment/Weapons/Clan Dagger|clan dagger]]. Selling this pistol is a terrible taboo and earns you the disdain of other dwarves. You are trained with the clan pistol. In addition, when your clan pistol is visible, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on checks to [[Actions/Gather Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression]] when interacting with citizens of Alkenstar, Dongun Hold, or their allies.
