# Instinctive Maneuvers  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Relinquish Control]]

---

When you allow an apparition control over your body, it might vent its fury against your foes. When you Relinquish Control, you add [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] to the list of actions you can take. You gain a +2 status bonus to the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to attempt these actions.
