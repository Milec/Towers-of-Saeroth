# Starlit Eyes  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Starlit Span (Impossible Magic)|starlit span hybrid study]]

---

Starlight shines in your sight, enhancing your perception and range. When you make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], you lower the DC of your [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to target a creature that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from you. The DC is reduced to 4 instead of 5 against a concealed creature and to 10 instead of 11 against a hidden one. If you’re in [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, you instead reduce the DC to 3 against a concealed creature or 9 against a hidden one.

In addition, when you cast shooting star and target a hidden creature, you don’t have to attempt the flat check for targeting a hidden creature with a ranged Strike.
