Your [[Rules/Deity (Player Core)|deity]] grants you extra power that you have learned to channel into your weapons. Select one weapon or [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|_handwraps of mighty blows_]] when you make your daily preparations. While in your hands it gains the effect of one [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property rune]]. Choose either [[Equipment/Fearsome (GM Core)|_fearsome_]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]], [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|_returning_]], [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|_shifting_]], or [[Equipment/Vitalizing|_vitalizing_]]. This rune does not count toward your maximum rune count, and this choice lasts 24 hours or until you make your next daily preparations, whichever comes first.
