You meander around unpredictably, and then ambush your opponents without warning. Choose one enemy. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] against that opponent, and then make a Strike against it. For your Feint, you can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check instead of the [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check that's usually required, using the terrain around you to surprise your foe.
