# Communal Healing  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 122

---

You’re a conduit for positive energy, and as you channel it through you, it heals some of your minor injuries. When you cast the [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spell to heal a single creature other than yourself, you regain Hit Points equal to the spell level of the _heal_ spell.
