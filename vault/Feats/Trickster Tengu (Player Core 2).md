# Trickster Tengu  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 37

---

You can transform yourself or the bodies of others to express your displeasure. Once per day, you can cast either [[Spells/Aerial Form (Player Core)|_aerial form_]] or [[Spells/Cursed Metamorphosis|_cursed metamorphosis_]] as a 7th-rank primal innate spell. You must take the form of a bird when casting this _aerial form_.
