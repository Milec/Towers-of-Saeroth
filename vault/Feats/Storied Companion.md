# Storied Companion ⤾  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Handler's Calling|handler's calling]]; you have an [animal companion](Companions.aspx) or [[Feats/Pet|pet]]

**Trigger**
Your animal companion or pet would fail a saving throw.

---

Your companion is an integral part of your story, and you can expend your mythic power to protect it, defying events that would prevent it from being so. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; your companion rerolls the triggering save at mythic proficiency and gains temporary Hit Points equal to twice your level that last for 1 minute.
