You've become comfortable in using weapons that you might not have picked up before training with the Swords. Choose one weapon group, such as [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|hammer]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]]. Whenever your proficiency rank in any weapon increases to expert or beyond, you also gain that new proficiency rank with both simple and martial weapons of your chosen weapon group.
