# Radiant Burst two actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 103

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Your skin glows with intensity. Creatures within 10 feet who can see you must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 4 rounds and dazzled for 10 minutes.
