# Master's Counterspell ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 48

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The trigger for Master’s Counterspell should begin with “A creature Casts a Spell that you have prepared or is in your repertoire...” to provide a trigger for spontaneous spellcasters as well as prepared spellcasters._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellmaster Dedication]]; master in spell attack rolls and spell DCs

**Trigger**
A creature Casts a Spell that you have prepared, and that spell was cast or created with a tradition of magic in which you have master proficiency in spell attack rolls and spell DCs.

---

When a foe Casts a Spell and you can see its manifestations, you can use your magic to disrupt it. If you're a prepared caster, you can expend a prepared spell to counter the triggering creature's casting of a spell. If you're a spontaneous caster, you can expend a spell slot. The level of the prepared spell or spell slot must be at least as high as the triggering spell's level. You lose your prepared spell or spell slot as if you had Cast the Spell. You then attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell, and you take a –2 penalty to your counteract check; for prepared casters, the GM might waive the penalty if the spell you're expending to counterspell is especially appropriate—such as countering with the same spell, or using a [[Spells/Cone of Cold|_cone of cold_]] to counteract a [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]].
