# Interstitial Escape ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 105

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You would be hit by a melee or ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], and you are not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]].

---

To evade your foe, you shift yourself to another location, teleporting from your current square to another unoccupied square within 30 feet. Additionally, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to all damage against the triggering attack equal to 5 + your level.
