# A Home in Every Port  *Feat 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 202

**Prerequisites**
Charisma 16

---

You have a reputation in towns and villages you've visited, and residents are always willing to open their doors to you. When in a town or village, during downtime, you can spend 8 hours to locate a resident willing to provide lodging for you and up to six allies for up to 24 hours at no charge. [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|The standard of living within the acquired lodging is comfortable, and square meals are provided at no cost]]. After 24 hours, you must pay standard prices for further lodging and meals or use this feat again to find a new resident willing to host you.
