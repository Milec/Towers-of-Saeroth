# Dust Cloud single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

Dust and pollen fill your waking world, obscuring creatures from sight. All creatures within your waking world become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the area become concealed to creatures within it until the start of your next turn. In addition, a creature that ends its turn in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw against your world rouser DC as it inhales the particles. On a failure, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] on a critical failure.
