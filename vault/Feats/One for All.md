# One for All single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 88

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]

---

With precisely the right words of encouragement, you bolster an ally's efforts. Designate an ally within 30 feet; this action counts as sufficient preparation to [[Actions/Aid]] that ally. When you use the Aid reaction to help that ally, you can roll [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] in place of the usual check. If your swashbuckler's style is [[Classes/Subclasses/Styles/Wit|wit]] and your Diplomacy check to Aid meets or exceeds the very hard DC for your level, you gain panache.
