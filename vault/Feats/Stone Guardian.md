You learn to call a powerful guardian of earth and stone to your side. You can cast [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]] as an innate primal spell once per day, and can only summon elementals with the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] trait. This spell is automatically heightened to half your level rounded up.
