# Bloodletting Claws  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 68

---

Your eidolon inflicts bleeding wounds on a telling blow. If your eidolon critically hits with a melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike that deals slashing or piercing damage, its target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. Your eidolon gains an item bonus to this bleed damage equal to the unarmed attack's item bonus to attack rolls. This is a [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
