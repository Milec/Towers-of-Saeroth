# Stay Down! ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 137

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
A [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] foe within your reach [[Actions/Stand|Stands]].

---

You have ways of keeping your foes down. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the triggering foe's Fortitude DC. On a success, they don't stand up and remain prone. On a critical success, they can't Stand until their next turn.
