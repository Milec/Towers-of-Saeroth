# Well-armed single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Skeleton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 56

---

Your detachable limbs offer flexibility. You Interact to remove your arm and wield it in the other one, increasing your [[Traits/Reach|reach]] by 5 feet for any one-handed weapon held in that arm. If your next action is a Strike with that weapon, creatures that were outside your reach that you can now hit are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against your first attack. You don't have a free hand while holding the arm. You can Interact to reattach the arm while holding it.
