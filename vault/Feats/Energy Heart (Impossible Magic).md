# Energy Heart  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 78

---

Your eidolon’s heart beats with energy. Choose [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]. One of their unarmed attacks changes its damage type to the chosen type, and they gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to that type equal to half your level (minimum 1).
