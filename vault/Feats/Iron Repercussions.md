# Iron Repercussions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Tyrant|tyrant cause]]

---

Disobeying your [[Actions/Iron Command]] has lasting consequences. If an enemy refuses to kneel to you, you can deal [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]] instead of normal mental damage. You must decide whether the mental damage will be persistent before your enemy chooses whether to kneel or not. The amount of damage is unchanged.
