Your sudden appearance leaves your foe unable to respond. When you use [[Feats/Startling Appearance (Vigilante)|Startling Appearance]], if your foe's level is equal to or lower than yours, they are also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] on a hit, or stunned 2 on a critical hit.
