# Mind Projectiles  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mind Smith (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mind Smith Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith Dedication]]

---

You've learned to stretch your mind's influence further, releasing projectiles with a swing of your mind weapon. You can make ranged mind weapon Strikes; these are ranged Strikes with a maximum [[Traits/Range|range]] of 30 feet that deal 1d6 damage of the same type as your mind weapon. Your ranged mind weapon Strike gains all the benefits of your mind weapon's runes as long as they still apply to a ranged weapon. For example, if your weapon had _+1, striking_, and [[Equipment/Spell Reservoir|spell reservoir]] runes, you would get a +1 item bonus to hit with your ranged mind weapon Strike, and it would deal the additional damage from the [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] rune, but it wouldn't be able to unleash a spell from the _spell reservoir_	 rune, as that rune can be etched onto only melee weapons.
