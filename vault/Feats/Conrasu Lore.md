# Conrasu Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conrasu]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 108

---

You have learned about your history and your people from shapers. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] and [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Traits/Conrasu|Conrasu Lore]].
