# Alchemical Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 63

---

You have used alchemy to create life, a simple creature formed from alchemical materials, reagents, and a bit of your own blood. You gain a [[Rules/Familiars (Player Core)|familiar]], which has the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct|construct]] familiar ability; this is permanent, doesn't require the familiar to have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough (Player Core)|tough]] familiar ability, and doesn't count against your usual limit of familiar abilities (typically 2). The familiar uses your Intelligence modifier to determine its Perception, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] modifiers.
