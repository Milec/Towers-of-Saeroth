# Ostentatious Arrival free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

---

If the next action you take is to [[Actions/Manifest Eidolon|Manifest your Eidolon]] as a three-action activity, or to Cast a 3-action [[Traits/Summon|summon]] or [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|incarnate]] Spell, the creature appears in an explosion. All creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around the creature you summoned or manifested except you take 1d4 fire damage per spell rank for a summon or incarnate spell, or 1d4 fire damage per 2 levels for Manifesting your Eidolon. If the creature you summoned or manifested has the [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]], or [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait, the damage is that type instead (or bludgeoning damage for the water trait). If the creature has more than one of these traits, you choose which damage type to deal.
