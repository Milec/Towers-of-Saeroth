# Slayer's Presence  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 29

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Frighten Undead]]

---

Your very presence strikes fear into the [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. When you use [[Feats/Undead Slayer Dedication|Slayer's Identification]] and succeed at your check, you can Frighten Undead as a free action, targeting the creature you identified. Any time you get a critical success to Frighten Undead, if the target's level is lower than yours, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.
