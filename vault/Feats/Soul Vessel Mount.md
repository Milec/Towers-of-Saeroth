# Soul Vessel Mount  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 109

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

---

Your mount has a supernatural hunger for mortal souls that you can feed. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]] as a divine innate spell. The spell gains a cost of 1 [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] but you can use your apocalypse mount as the item in which you store the soul. Your apocalypse mount counts as an [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|artifact]] for the purpose of the spell. At any point while your apocalypse mount contains a soul, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to allow your apocalypse mount to consume the soul. It regains a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level. For the next minute, it also gains a +20-foot status bonus to its Speed and its unarmed attack [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] are made at mythic proficiency.
