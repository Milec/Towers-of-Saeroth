# Avenge Ally single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 43

**Frequency**
once every 10 minutes

**Requirements**
You are adjacent to an ally with the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition.

---

Though you know that you will eventually outlive your companions, seeing them at death's door brings clarity to your attacks. Make a Strike. Roll twice on the attack roll and use the higher result.
