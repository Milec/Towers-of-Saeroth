# Crystalline Dust two actions  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aphorite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 76

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You've learned to disperse the crystalline motes coating your body as a haze. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], though you can't use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]], as normal for concealment where your position is still obvious. The motes stay within an inch of your skin, move with you, and last for a number of rounds equal to half your level.
