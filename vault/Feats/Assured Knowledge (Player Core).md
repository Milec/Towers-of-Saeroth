# Assured Knowledge  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 103

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Loremaster]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Enigma|enigma muse]]

---

You can procure information with confidence. Whenever you [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] using any skill (including [[Feats/Bardic Lore (Player Core)|Bardic Lore]]), you can forgo rolling your check to instead receive a result of 10 + your proficiency bonus (don’t apply any other bonuses, penalties, or modifiers).

As long as you are an expert in a skill, you meet the prerequisites for the [[Feats/Automatic Knowledge (Player Core)|Automatic Knowledge]] skill feat for that skill, even if you don’t have the [[Feats/Assurance (Player Core)|Assurance]] feat for that skill.
