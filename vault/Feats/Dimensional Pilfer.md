# Dimensional Pilfer two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You have a preternatural ability to whisk items out from under their owners’ noses. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] check to [[Actions/Palm an Object (Player Core)|Palm an Object]] or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal an object]] you can see within 60 feet. If you are veiled, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to this check. If you succeed, the object vanishes from its current location and appears on your person.
