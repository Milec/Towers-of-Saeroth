# Paragon Battle Medicine  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 60

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]] (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

**Access**
You’re from Rahadoum.

---

You've learned the modern uses of Battle Medicine originated by Kassi Aziril. When you successfully use Battle Medicine, you can also reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] condition by 1 (this has no effect if you are subject to an effect continually applying the clumsy condition, like enlarge). If you are legendary in Medicine, you can choose to reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] condition by 1 instead, and if you have the [[Feats/Godless Healing (Hellfire Dispatches)|Godless Healing]] feat, you can choose to reduce the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition by 1 instead. If you have the [[Feats/Mortal Healing (Hellfire Dispatches)|Mortal Healing]] feat, you can reduce all available conditions by 1 for a target who hasn't benefited from [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] or [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] magic in the last 24 hours, and if you roll a critical success before applying the effects of the feat, you reduce all available conditions by 2 for that target instead.
