# Lie Detector  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 62

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Empiricism|empiricism methodology]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Interrogation|interrogation methodology]]

---

You're adept at noticing the tells of a liar—sweat, flushing, a quavering voice, a quickening pulse. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive]] and to Perception DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Lie]] to you. When you determine someone is lying to you, you can use their deceit to your advantage to gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the next [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check you attempt against that creature within the next minute.
