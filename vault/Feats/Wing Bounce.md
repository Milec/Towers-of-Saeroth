# Wing Bounce two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Winged Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Winged Warrior Dedication]]

---

You rush forward, flapping your wings for a bit of lift, and can use that momentum to knock down a foe. [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], and if you land within melee reach of at least one enemy, you can attempt to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] that enemy. You don't need to have one hand free. If you successfully knock the target [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], you can Leap again. If you have a fly Speed, you can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] instead of Leap.
