# Stalwart Standard  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 81

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]], [[Archetypes/Knight Vigilant]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier's Banner]]; [[Feats/Knight Vigilant]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

Your banner makes it harder for your foes to push your allies around and encourages your allies to protect the innocent. Allies benefiting from your Cavalier's Banner feat also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to their Fortitude and Reflex DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip]]. Each benefited ally also gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls against any creature that has dealt damage to an innocent of a lower level than that ally within the last round; [[Traits/Summoned|summoned]] creatures, companions, or any creatures you or your allies put into danger on purpose to attempt to gain this bonus don't count.
