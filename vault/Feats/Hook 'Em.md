# Hook 'Em two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pirate Dedication]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Requirements**
A rope is within your reach, or you have a [[Equipment/Rope|rope]] or [[Traits/Grapple|grappling]] weapon in your hand.

---

The Salt Breakers learned to use whatever they could during the Vidric Revolution, and they quickly mastered the art of making a rope into a useful tool in combat. You take hold of a nearby rope, throw a rope from your hand, or fire a grappling weapon at a solid surface—and then swing up to twice your Speed. If you end your movement next to an enemy, attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple]] that enemy, tying them up in your rope.
