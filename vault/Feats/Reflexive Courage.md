# Reflexive Courage ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Warrior|warrior muse]]

**Trigger**
A creature within your reach uses an auditory effect, manipulate action, or move action; makes a ranged attack; or leaves a square during its move action

**Requirements**
You are affected by courageous anthem

---

You bellow a ferocious call to arms, inspiring yourself to lash out at a foe. Make a melee Strike against the triggering creature. If the attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a manipulate action, the action is disrupted.
