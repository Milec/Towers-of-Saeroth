# Legendary Tattoo Artist  *Feat 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 118

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tattoo Artist (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Tattoo Artist]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

Your craftsmanship at decorating flesh is unparalleled. You become legendary in Crafting. Whenever you craft a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|magical tattoo]] whose level is at least 3 lower than your level, increase all attack roll and DC values of the tattoo's activated abilities by 1, and increase all resistances granted by the tattoo by 1.
