# Dormant Eruption  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Aftermath (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Aftermath]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 57

**Prerequisites**
You've been brought to 0 Hit Points by an enemy that has the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait or an enemy's ability that has the fire trait.

---

A sweltering heat and sparks of flame that take the form of tiny copies of you surround your body. You're hot to the touch, gaining resistance 5 to cold and fire. Whenever an adjacent creature Strikes you with a melee attack or touches you, it takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. You gain the Thermal Eruption action.

 **Thermal Eruption** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** You concentrate your thermal energy and explode it outward. All creatures in a 20-foot emanation take 14d6 fire damage with a basic Reflex save. Afterward, you lose all effects of the Dormant Eruption feat until your next daily preparations.
