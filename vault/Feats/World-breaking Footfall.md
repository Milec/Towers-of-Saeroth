# World-breaking Footfall two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kaiju Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Kaiju Stance and are touching the ground.

---

You slam the ground and unleash a fearsome roar, sending broken shards of earth flying in all directions. The ground in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], or greater difficult terrain if it was already difficult terrain. All other creatures within the emanation take 7d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex save against your class DC. You then can’t use World-breaking Footfall for 1d4 rounds.
