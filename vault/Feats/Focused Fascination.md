# Focused Fascination  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 88

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lion Blade (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fascinating Performance]]

---

When you use [[Feats/Fascinating Performance]] in a combat encounter, you need only a success, rather than a critical success, to [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinate]] your target. This works only if you're attempting to fascinate one target. For example, if you were an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]], you could choose to target multiple creatures normally or target one creature and fascinate it on a success.
