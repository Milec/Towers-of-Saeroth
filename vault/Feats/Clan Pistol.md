# Clan Pistol  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dwarf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 200

**Access**
You are from Dongun Hold or Alkenstar.

---

Raised in the ancient halls of Dongun Hold or the surrounding region, you display your lineage with a [[Equipment/Weapons/Clan Pistol (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|clan pistol]], rather than a [[Equipment/Weapons/Clan Dagger (Player Core)|clan dagger]]. You get one clan pistol of your clan for free, as it was given to you at birth. This replaces your clan dagger. Selling this pistol is a terrible taboo and earns you the disdain of other dwarves. You are trained with the clan pistol. In addition, when your clan pistol is visible, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on checks to [[Rules/Gather Information (GM Core)|Gather Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] when interacting with citizens of Alkenstar, Dongun Hold, or their allies.
