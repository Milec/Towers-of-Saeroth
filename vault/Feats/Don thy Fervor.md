# Don thy Fervor single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archfiend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archfiend Dedication]]

---

With a thought, the passion of your fiendish desires becomes evident in your eyes and posture. For the next minute, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] skill checks. In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] checks. At 18th level, these circumstance bonuses increase to +3.
