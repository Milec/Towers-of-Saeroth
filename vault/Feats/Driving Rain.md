# Driving Rain three actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 32

---

Heavy drops of rain batter down, hitting like sling stones and impeding vision. Each creature in a 15-foot burst within 120 feet takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Until the start of your next turn, all creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] while in the area, and all creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within it.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
