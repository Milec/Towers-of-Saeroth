# Seasoned Command single action  *Feat 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 232

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

Allies intuitively follow your orders. Choose an ally within 30 feet and then attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check against a hard DC for that ally’s level. This counts as a preparatory action to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] that ally. Regardless of your result, the ally is then temporarily immune to your further uses of Seasoned Command for 1 minute.

**Special** Once per hour, you can [[Rules/Establishing a New Command|Take the Lead]] of a troop as a free action (rather than 2 actions). Outside of combat, you can establish leadership of a troop and grant a [[Rules/Skirmish Bonds|skirmish bond]] in half the normal time (5 minutes and 4 hours, respectively).

**Critical Success** You immediately gain an additional reaction that can be used only to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] the chosen ally, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your skill check or attack roll to Aid.  

**Success** As critical success, but you don’t gain the circumstance bonus.  

**Failure** You don’t gain an additional reaction.
