# Stealthy as an Octopus  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undersea Privateer Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You're an expert at using the dark of the ocean to conceal yourself. As long as you're separated from a creature by 20 or more feet of water, the turbulence of the water grants you enough cover to attempt to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] from that creature.
