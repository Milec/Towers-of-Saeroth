[[Feats/Steel Falcon Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

You strike for Andoran on the waters of the Inner Sea and beyond. You gain the [[Feats/Pirate Dedication (Player Core 2)|Pirate Dedication]] feat even if you haven't taken two other feats from the Steel Falcon archetype or Eagle Knight archetype. You can select feats from the Pirate archetype as if they were Steel Falcon archetype feats.
