# Liberating Dive two actions  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Composite]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 229

---

You sprout wings with steel feathers, resembling those of the [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] [[Bestiary/Talmandor]]. With a powerful flap, the wings propel you across the battlefield, slicing foes and restraints along your path. Until the end of your turn, you gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your land Speed or 40 feet, whichever is greater. You then [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to your Speed, and this movement doesn't trigger reactions.

Choose one foe and one ally you're adjacent to at any point during this movement. Using one wing's sharpened edge, you can use a 1-action [[Actions/Elemental Blast]] as a melee Strike using the metal element against the foe. With the other wing, you can help the ally break free of an effect [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restraining]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilizing]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzing]] them; they either attempt a new save against one such effect that allows a save, or they attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as a free action. The ally can use your [[Traits/Impulse|impulse]] attack modifier in place of their unarmed attack modifier or skill check modifier.
