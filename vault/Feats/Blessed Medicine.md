# Blessed Medicine  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 28

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undead Slayer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail a check to [[Actions/Treat Disease|Treat a Disease]] that was inflicted by an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] (such as ghoul fever or mummy rot).

---

Your experience battling undead has uniquely prepared you to fight the diseases they inflict on the survivors, and you know that leaving these maladies untreated for long will eventually lead to more undead. Reroll the failed check and use the new result.
