# Prophesied Monarch Dedication  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Destiny]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 135

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Prophesied Monarch]]

---

Your Calling has culminated in a destiny of rulership, and you’re able to forge a mythic bond with noble knights whose destinies become intertwined with your own. Designate up to five allies as your knights; as long as you’re conscious, your knights’ [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition can’t rise above 3, and if they’re ever in mortal peril (such as being [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] with the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition or imprisoned in an enemy fortress), you instantly receive a vision revealing their location and condition.

In addition, you gain the Royal Grace exploration activity.

**Royal Grace** ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Rules/Skills/Exploration|exploration]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) You spend 10 minutes conferring with one of your knights who has the doomed condition. By discussing plans for your future kingdom with them and describing the benefits of your rule, you can attempt to soothe their spirits. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] check against a [[Rules/Level-Based DCs (GM Core)|hard DC of your knight's level]]. Regardless of the result, that knight gains the benefits of the [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] activity (if they have a focus pool) but is temporarily immune to Royal Grace for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The knight’s doomed condition is reduced by 2 (minimum 0).  

**Success** The knight’s doomed condition is reduced by 1 (minimum 0).  

**Failure** The knight’s doomed condition isn’t reduced.
