# Oni's Mask  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hungerseed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 37

---

You aren’t as malleable as your oni ancestors but still can disguise yourself. You can cast [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] as a 1st-rank primal innate spell once per day. This spell can’t disguise your horns, though you can still hide them normally with a hairstyle or a hood.
