# Too Much to Swallow ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 57

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Untamed Form]]

**Trigger**
You’re subject to an effect you could attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].

---

While you might be small enough to grab normally, you can change that at a moment's notice. You cast [[Spells/Untamed Form|_untamed form_]]. If you took on a new form that's too large for the effect, you automatically escape. Otherwise, you attempt to Escape. If this ends an effect where you entered the creature's space, such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallow Whole]], exit that creature to an adjacent area where your new form could fit.
