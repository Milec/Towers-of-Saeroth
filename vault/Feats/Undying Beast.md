You can be injured by non-[[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] sources but recover with staggering speed. When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points by non-silver damage and don't immediately die from a [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effect or a high [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] value, you stabilize immediately afterward. You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition as normal.
