# Sociable  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Half-Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 15

You’re extremely extroverted, and you often spend your time carousing or otherwise socializing. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (or another skill of your choice if you were already trained in Diplomacy), and you gain the [[Feats/Hobnobber]] skill feat.
