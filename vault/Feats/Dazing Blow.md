# Dazing Blow single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 126

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].

---

You pummel a held foe, hoping to stagger them. Make a melee Strike against a creature you have grabbed. The weapon damage from this Strike is bludgeoning damage. If the Strike hits, the creature must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC; this is an [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] effect.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  

**Failure** The creature is stunned 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned 3.
