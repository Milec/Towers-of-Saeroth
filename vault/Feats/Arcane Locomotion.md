# Arcane Locomotion  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 42

---

You’ve modified your body to allow you to augment your movement. You gain either a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 20 feet or a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] of 20 feet, your choice.

**Enhancement** Your body allows for even further forms of movement. You can either select the option from this feat you haven’t chosen yet ([[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] or [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]]), or you can increase your land Speed by 5 feet and increase the Speed you chose from this feat increase to be equal to your adjusted land Speed.
