# Regurgitate Mutagen single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 65

**Requirements**
You are under the effects of a mutagen.

---

You redirect a mutagen within your body to spit a stream of stomach acid at a foe. A creature within 30 feet takes 1d6 acid damage for every 2 levels you have, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. On a failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] (or sickened 2 on a critical failure). The mutagen's duration immediately ends.
