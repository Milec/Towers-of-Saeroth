# Spear Dancer single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter (Player Core)|Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 67

**Requirements**
You are wielding a weapon from the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearm]] group

---

You favor weapons that allow you to lash out viciously while keeping enemies at bay, giving you an opportunity to strike without fear of reprisal. You [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] and then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearm]] you are wielding, or Strike with the spear or polearm and then Step.
