# Ragdya's Revelry ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 56

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
An adjacent creature you can see fails an attack roll against you.

---

You dart away from your foe's attack with a casual laugh, using the opening to cause mischief. You attempt to [[Actions/Steal]] an item from the triggering creature, ignoring the usual restriction that the creature can't be in combat.
