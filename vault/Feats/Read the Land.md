# Read the Land  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 213

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Geomancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Geomancer Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

You've learned how to commune with the land to learn information. You learn the [[Rituals/Commune with Nature|_commune with nature_]] ritual if you didn't know it already. You can perform this ritual with a casting time of 1 hour instead of 1 day and without a secondary caster.
