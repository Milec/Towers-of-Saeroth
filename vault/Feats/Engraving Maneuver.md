# Engraving Maneuver single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 49

**Requirements**
You are wielding a melee weapon with the [[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]], [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]], or [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait.

---

After quickly drawing a rune on your weapon, you can transfer the rune to a foe as you knock them down, push them away, or disarm their weapon. Attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] a target; your weapon must have the corresponding trait. If your skill check is successful, you [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] onto the target of the [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] even if the target is pushed out of range.
