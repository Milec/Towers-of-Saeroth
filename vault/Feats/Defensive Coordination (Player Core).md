# Defensive Coordination single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 103

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Warrior|warrior muse]]; [[Feats/Rallying Anthem]]

---

Like the storied heroes who persist in the face of overwhelming odds, you and your allies will hold the line. If your next action is to cast the [[Spells/Rallying Anthem|_rallying anthem_]] composition cantrip, you can [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]], and one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to Raise a Shield.
