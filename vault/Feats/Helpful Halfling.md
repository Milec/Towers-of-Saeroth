# Helpful Halfling  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 45

---

When you aid a friend with a task, you find many ways to help and avoid interfering. On a critical success to [[Actions/Aid]], you grant your ally a +3 circumstance bonus if you have expert proficiency in the skill (rather than +2), and you grant your ally a +4 circumstance bonus if you have master proficiency (rather than +3). If you roll a critical failure on a check to Aid, you don’t give your ally a –1 circumstance penalty to their check.
