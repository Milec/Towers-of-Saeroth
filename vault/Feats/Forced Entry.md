# Forced Entry  *Feat 6*

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; [[Feats/Pathfinder Agent Dedication]]

**Access**
A character who is a member of the Pathfinder Society has access to this option.

---

You are trained to preserve the archaeological value of your location when entering. You don’t take a penalty to [[Actions/Force Open]] doors, windows, or containers without a crowbar. If you roll a success on a check to Force Open, you get a critical success instead.
