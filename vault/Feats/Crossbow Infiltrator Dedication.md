# Crossbow Infiltrator Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 56

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Crossbow Infiltrator]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You have mastered stealthy weapons that allow you to strike carefully from a distance far enough to avoid reprisal, yet close enough to confirm your kills. You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Gauntlet Bow (Battlecry!)|gauntlet bow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Hand Crossbow (Player Core)|hand crossbow]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Repeating Hand Crossbow (Battlecry!)|repeating hand crossbow]], treating the repeating  hand crossbow as a martial weapon for the purposes of proficiency and the gauntlet bow as a simple weapon for the purposes of proficiency. If you're at least an expert in any of these weapons, you gain access to that weapon's critical specialization effect. You also gain the Infiltrator's Draw action.  

**Infiltrator's Draw** single action **Frequency** once per round; **Effect**  You Interact to draw a gauntlet bow, hand crossbow, or repeating hand crossbow, then Strike with it. Alternatively, you can Strike with a loaded hand crossbow  or repeating hand crossbow you're already holding and then Interact to stow it.
