# Shadow Master  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 192

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Call of Shadows|Shadowdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Shadowdancer Dedication]]

---

The shadows protect you better than any armor can, and they give you power to plunge others into darkness. Whenever you are in an area of dim light or darkness, you gain resistance 5 to all damage except force and damage from Strikes with the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune. When you attempt a Reflex saving throw in an area of dim light or darkness, you can roll twice and take the higher result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.
