# Aboleth Transmutation  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Absalom, City of Lost Omens]] pg. 395, [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 5

---

You have tapped into the ancient magic used by [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu|alghollthu]] masters (also known as aboleths) to mold the flesh of your ancestors. You can use this magic to transfigure other beings. You gain 3rd-level [[Spells/Feet to Fins|_feet to fins_]] and 2nd-level [[Spells/Water Breathing|_water breathing_]] as arcane innate spells. You can cast each of these arcane innate spells once per day.
