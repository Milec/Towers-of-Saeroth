# Essence Overflow two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Draconic Acolyte]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Acolyte Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are Channeling Draconic Essence.

---

Uncontainable power explodes from your spectral dragon. Your Channel Draconic Essence ends, and all creatures (except you) in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from your spectral dragon take 6d6 damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. The damage’s type matches the breath of your [draconic benefactor](DraconicBenefactors.aspx). You then can’t use Essence Overflow again for 1d4 rounds.

At 8th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
