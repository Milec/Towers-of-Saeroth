# Growing Eel Friend  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elver Pet]]

---

Your eel pet grows larger and stronger. Your eel pet's size changes to Small, and the eel gains the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Scent (Player Core)|scent]] and [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough (Player Core)|tough]] pet abilities. However, it becomes too large to carry in a water-filled globe.
