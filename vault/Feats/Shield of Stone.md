# Shield of Stone  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Ikon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 79

---

You stole a [[Bestiary/Families/Gorgon|gorgon’s]] gaze and trapped it in your ikon. You’ve learned how to brandish it for your advantage, subduing the rage of the trapped gaze by turning it on your enemies.

**Transcendence—Brandish the Gorgon’s Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Transcendence|transcendence]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Effect** You Raise your mirrored aegis, though it seems to lose some of its usual luster in favor of the dull sheen of stone. If you [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] a melee attack with the mirrored aegis before the beginning of your next turn, the gorgon’s eyes upon it flash at the triggering creature, who must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, they are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 minute as their body partially petrifies. Regardless of the outcome, that creature is temporarily immune to this effect for 24 hours.
