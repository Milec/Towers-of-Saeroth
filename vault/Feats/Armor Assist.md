# Armor Assist  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 203

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The DCs for the Armor Assist feat are not subject to GM adjudication. Rather, simply use the default DCs listed in the feat: 15 for common armor, 20 for uncommon armor, and 25 for rare armor._

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Warfare Lore]]

---

After your service aiding armored combatants, you are practiced in helping yourself and others don heavy gear. You can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Warfare Lore]] check with a DC determined by the GM (but usually 15 for common armor, DC 20 for uncommon armor, and DC 25 for rare armor) to halve the time you take to don armor. You can halve an ally's time to don armor by working with them to don the armor and succeeding at an Athletics or Warfare Lore check against the same DC.
