# Daywalker  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 27

---

Whether through painstaking exposure, devoted prayer, or simple luck, you've found a greater affinity for life through the sun's light. When in an area of direct natural sunlight, your [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]] is suppressed, meaning you are healed by [[Traits/Positive|positive]] [[Traits/Healing|healing]] effects and harmed by negative energy.
