# Rippling Spin ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Reflective Ripple Stance (Rival Academies)|Reflective Ripple Stance]]

**Trigger**
You’re hit by a physical melee attack by an attacker you can see that’s in reach.

**Requirements**
You’re in Reflective Ripple Stance

---

After the triggering attack is done, [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]. You must end this Step within the attacker’s reach. Then, you can attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] the attacker.
