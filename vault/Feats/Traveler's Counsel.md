# Traveler's Counsel  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 61

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Half Moon Sarangay]] heritage

---

On your travels, you’ve resolved countless disputes and counseled those in need. You’re trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. If you would automatically become trained in Diplomacy (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. The first time each day you would roll a critical failure when [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gathering Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Making an Impression]], you get a failure instead.
