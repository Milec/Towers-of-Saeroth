# Fight Choreography  *Feat 18*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 83

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]] (Level 18), [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]] (Level 18)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication (Player Core 2)|Acrobat Dedication]] or [[Feats/Gladiator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Gladiator Dedication]]; master in Theater Lore

---

You’ve spent enough time training in stage fighting and teaching a wide range of stunts that you can prepare your companions with directions on how to avoid peril in battle. You can [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] an ally’s [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check with a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Theater Lore]] check. You gain the Swift Choreography reaction.

**Swift Choreography** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]]) **Trigger** An ally within 30 feet would become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]; **Effect** You call out a quick reminder to your ally about a special move you’d suggested to them at some point in the past, allowing the ally to avoid the triggering effect completely.
