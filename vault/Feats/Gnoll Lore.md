# Gnoll Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnoll]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 112

---

You paid attention to your senior hunters to learn their tricks. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Traits/Gnoll|Gnoll Lore]].
