# Slayer's Blessing  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 29

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Slayer's Strike]]

---

You carry scraps of materials dangerous to undead and can apply them to your weapon. When you use Slayer's Strike, before making the Strike, you can choose either the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune or a precious material: [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]], [[Equipment/Darkwood Weapon|darkwood]], or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]. Any Strike that gets extra positive damage from this use of Slayer's Strike also gets the benefits of the rune or counts as that material.

 At 14th level, add [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] and [[Equipment/Mithral Weapon|mithral]] to the list of materials you can choose from.
