# Come in From the Wilderness two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your waking world is active.

---

A willing ally on the ground in your waking world is swaddled in earth. For each source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] it is taking, the creature can immediately attempt a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to recover, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance. While [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|swallowed]], the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] and cannot be targeted or affected by any new effects (ongoing effects, such as [[Rules/Afflictions (GM Core)|afflictions]] or persistent damage, continue to affect it as normal). At the start of your next turn, the creature regains 6d8 Hit Points and emerges in its previous location. If another creature is in that location, it is pushed to the nearest available space. The target creature then becomes temporarily immune to this ability until your next daily preparations.
