# Raging Intimidation  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 77

---

Your fury fills your foes with fear. While you are raging, your [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] and [[Feats/Scare to Death (Player Core)|Scare to Death]] actions (from the Intimidation skill and an Intimidation skill feat, respectively) gain the [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|rage]] trait, allowing you to use them while raging. As soon as you meet the prerequisites for the skill feats [[Feats/Intimidating Glare (Player Core)|Intimidating Glare]] and Scare to Death, you gain these feats.
