# Fulu Familiar  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

You can imprint your familiar’s spiritual essence into a [[Traits/Fulu|fulu]], a small paper charm that can be affixed to objects, creatures, or structures. You gain the Create Familiar Fulu action, which you can use once per day; at 12th level, this increases to twice per day, and at 18th level, it increases to three times per day.
