# Uncanny Cheeks  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 55

---

You squirrel away useful objects and seem to retrieve them at just the right time. You gain the [[Feats/Prescient Consumable]] and [[Feats/Prescient Planner]] feats, except that you always withdraw the objects from your cheek pouches. This means the object you retrieve must fit in your cheek pouches; this is generally an item of negligible Bulk unless you also have the [[Feats/Cheek Pouches]] feat.
