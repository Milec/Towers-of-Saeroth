# Forlorn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 40

---

Watching your friends age and die fills you with moroseness that protects you against harmful emotions. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion effect, you get a critical success instead.
