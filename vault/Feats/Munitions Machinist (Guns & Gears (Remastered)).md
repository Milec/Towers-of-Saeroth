**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Munitions Crafter (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Munitions Crafter]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You're adept at crafting ammunition. You gain the [[Rules/Alchemical Archetypes (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy benefits]], creating up to 4 versatile vials during your daily preparations. You can use Quick Alchemy only to create bombs or alchemical ammunition. Using Quick Alchemy to create alchemical ammunition also counts as 1 action toward activating that ammunition.
