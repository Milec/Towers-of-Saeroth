# Subtle Delivery  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 106

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]] (Level 4)

---

You can capably deliver toxins with a [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowgun]]. Your blowgun Strikes can apply [[Traits/Injury|injury]] poisons even if they deal no damage due to a creature's resistance. If you critically succeed at an attack roll using a blowgun loaded with a dart you've poisoned and the target rolls a failure on the poison's initial save, the target critically fails instead.
