# Hellknight Mobility  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]; expert in [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Half Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight half plate]] or [[Equipment/Armor/Hellknight Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Hellknight plate]]

---

You've learned how best to move in your heavy armor. When wearing heavy armor, if you meet the Strength requirement, instead of reducing the movement penalty by 5 feet, you reduce the penalty by 10 feet (typically reducing the penalty to 0). If you're a [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarf]] with the [[Feats/Unburdened Iron (Player Core)|Unburdened Iron]] feat, increase your Speed by 5 feet.
