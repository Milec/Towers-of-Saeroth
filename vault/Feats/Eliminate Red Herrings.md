# Eliminate Red Herrings  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 106

---

You have a keen sense for avoiding spurious lines of inquiry. When you roll a critical failure on a skill check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] related to a question about one of your active investigations, you get a failure instead.
