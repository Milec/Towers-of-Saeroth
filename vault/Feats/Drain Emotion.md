# Drain Emotion two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 127

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fey Influence]]

---

You touch an adjacent creature and attempt to draw out its excess emotion. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against a single [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] or [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect affecting the touched creature. If you succeed, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to your level for a number of rounds equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 round).

**Special** This feat gains the trait appropriate for your ancestry ([[Traits/Human|human]] for [[Ancestries/Human|human]], [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] for [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]], etc.).
