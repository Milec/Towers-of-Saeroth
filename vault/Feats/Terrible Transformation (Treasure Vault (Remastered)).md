# Terrible Transformation  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ursine Avenger Hood (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Ursine Avenger Hood]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ursine Avenger Form (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Ursine Avenger Form]]

---

You let out a mighty roar as part of your transformation. When you use Ursine Avenger Form, you can make an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] against each enemy within 30 feet that can see you, and you don't take a penalty to your Demoralize check if the creature doesn't understand your language.
