# Seeker of Truths  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eldritch Researcher]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Researcher Dedication]]

---

Your devotion to discovering and hoarding abstruse truths borders on the fanatical, and you can bring this devotion to bear. You gain the cleric's [[Feats/Domain Initiate]] feat but must select [[Magic/Domains/Knowledge|knowledge]], [[Magic/Domains/Secrecy|secrecy]], or [[Magic/Domains/Truth|truth]] as your domain. You cast that domain's domain spell as a focus spell of the same tradition as your cantrip from Eldritch Researcher Dedication, and you [[Actions/Refocus]] by contemplating abstruse mysteries rather than praying to a god. When an ability would manifest a divine symbol—such as the [[Spells/Word of Truth|_word of truth_]] focus spell—it instead manifests as a strange shape unique to you that seems to defy geometry.

**Special** You can take this feat up to three times, selecting a different domain each time.
