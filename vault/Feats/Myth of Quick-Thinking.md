# Myth of Quick-Thinking  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Godling]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Godling Dedication]]; [[Rules/Hero-Gods of Iblydos|hero-god accomplishment]]

---

Your myth includes amusing parables of clever plans and sly maneuvers, often applied to small things in life such as disguising yourself as a sheep to ambush a [[Bestiary/Families/Wolf (Monster Core)|wolf]] or tricking a [[Bestiary/Families/Snake (Monster Core)|snake]] into shedding its skin. You can [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] as a two-action activity, regardless of how elaborate your tale. When you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check, you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] to do so at [[Rules/Mythic Proficiency|mythic proficiency]]. If you succeed at a Deception check while in the presence of your hierophant, any of your worshippers that you can see automatically succeed at an attempt to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] or Lie in such a way that supports your story (for example, the target of your Lie claiming you are a teacher sees all of your worshippers as students).

You also lay claim to one of the following domains of your choice: [[Magic/Domains/Confidence (Player Core)|confidence]], [[Magic/Domains/Secrecy (Player Core)|secrecy]], or [[Magic/Domains/Trickery (Player Core)|trickery]].
