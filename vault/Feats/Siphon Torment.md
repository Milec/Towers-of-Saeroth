# Siphon Torment  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 44

---

Your progenitors suffered greatly at the hands of a curse, a fate you willingly take upon yourself rather than let fall upon others. Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Claim Curse|_claim curse_]] as an innate divine spell. When you do, the target creature gains temporary Hit Points equal to your level.
