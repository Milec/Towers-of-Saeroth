# Subtle Shift  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 103

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellshifter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellshifter Dedication]]

---

You’ve learned how to hide your meddling from the casters of spells you’re shifting. If your [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] check when Shifting a Spell is greater than its caster’s [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] DC, you can prevent the caster from learning the effects of the shift you’re attempting to apply. On a critical success, they don’t become aware that you’re applying a shift at all (although the effect of the shift might still give you away).
