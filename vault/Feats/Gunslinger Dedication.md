# Gunslinger Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Leading With a Cannon|Gunslinger]]

**Prerequisites**
Dexterity 14

---

You become trained in simple and martial crossbows and simple and martial firearms. You become trained in [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-509)|gunslinger]] class DC.

Choose a gunslinger [here](Ways.aspx). You become trained in your way’s associated skill; if you were already trained in this skill, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You don’t gain any other abilities from your choice of way.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained two other feats from the gunslinger archetype.
