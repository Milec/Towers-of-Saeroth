# Cleansing Spell single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ascended Celestial]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

---

Infused with celestial light, your magic can more effectively heal wounds and purge afflictions. If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait on a living creature, one target of that spell regains a number of Hit Points equal to your level (in addition to any Hit Points they would normally gain). In addition, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] affecting one target of that spell. These two targets can be the same creature or different creatures, as long as they are both targets of the healing spell.
