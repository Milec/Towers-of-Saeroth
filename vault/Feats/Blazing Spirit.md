# Blazing Spirit ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Trigger**
A creature damages you with a melee attack

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Battlegrounds [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Volcano Lore as an apparition skill.

---

Your apparition grants fiery defenses. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to your level against the triggering damage, and the triggering creature takes 1d6 fire damage and 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]]. This damage increases to 2d6 fire damage and 2 persistent fire damage at 12th level, and 3d6 fire damage and 3 persistent fire damage at 18th level.
