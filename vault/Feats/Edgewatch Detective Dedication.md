# Edgewatch Detective Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Edgewatch Detective]]

**Prerequisites**
Intelligence 14 or Wisdom 14

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]]; if you are already trained in both of these skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can use Perception instead of [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] to [[Actions/Track]], and you gain the [[Feats/Experienced Tracker]] skill feat.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from this archetype.
