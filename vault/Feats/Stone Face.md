# Stone Face  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 34

---

Through conditioning or experience, you've mastered the art of composure, even in the face of fear. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against effects with the [[Traits/Fear|fear]] trait, and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Will DC against [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation|Intimidate]] skill actions, such as [[Actions/Demoralize]].
