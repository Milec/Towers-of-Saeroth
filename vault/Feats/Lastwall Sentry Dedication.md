# Lastwall Sentry Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
member of the Knights of Lastwall; [[Feats/Shield Block]]

**Access**
You are from the Eye of Dread region.

---

You’ve sworn yourself as a sentry for the Knights of Lastwall, the first line of defense against incursions by the Whispering Tyrant throughout Golarion. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and in Undead [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]; if you were already trained, you become an expert instead. Finally, you gain the [[Feats/Reactive Shield]] fighter feat.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Lastwall sentry archetype.
