# Terrain Scout  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scout (Player Core 2)|Scout]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scout Dedication (Player Core 2)|Scout Dedication]]

---

You have mastered movement in many different environments. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Stalker (Player Core)|Terrain Stalker]] skill feat twice, choosing a different terrain each time. If you [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]] while exploring and any allies use [[Actions/Follow the Expert (Player Core)|Follow the Expert]] to follow you as you do so, you can choose one of those allies to gain the benefit of any one Terrain Stalker feat you have.
