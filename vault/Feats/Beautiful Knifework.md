# Beautiful Knifework  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 95

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lepidstadt Surgeon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lepidstadt Surgeon Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

You perform surgical adjustments to disguise a willing ally or your construct companion. This counts as preparing a disguise to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]], taking 10 minutes and requiring a healer’s toolkit instead of a disguise kit. The DC to see through the deception with Seek is your Medicine DC unless the disguised character’s Deception DC is higher. If the target directly interacts with someone while disguised, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their Deception check.

 You can reverse this effect with another 10-minute surgery. The disguised character can also end it as a free action when they receive any healing effect.
