# Stone Blood  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stonebound]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Stonebound Dedication]]

---

Your blood becomes thick and sluggish, but no less effective at oxygenating your cells. You can selectively stop your bleeding with ease. Reduce the DC of flat checks to stop [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] from 15 to 10, and you can use a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait to gain an early flat check without physically binding the wounds. You recover from the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition twice as quickly as normal.
