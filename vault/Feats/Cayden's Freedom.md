# Cayden's Freedom  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 48

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Starstone Aspirant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Starstone Aspirant Dedication]]

---

Even if you've never downed a tankard of ale nor indulged in a night of rowdy revelry, you appreciate the dedication to freedom from Cayden Cailean and his worshippers. After much physical practice, you've become difficult to restrain, and if you're ever grabbed, you can access a little bit of the Accidental God's luck. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude DC when resisting an opponent's attempts to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] you or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallow you Whole]]. In addition, you gain the Liberate Self action.
