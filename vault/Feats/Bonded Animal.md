# Bonded Animal  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Downtime]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 259

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

You forge strong connections with animals. You can spend 7 days of downtime regularly interacting with a normal animal (not a companion or other special animal) that is friendly or helpful to you. After this duration, attempt a DC 20 [[Magic/Domains/Nature]] check. If successful, you bond with the animal. The animal is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful|helpful]] to you, unless you do something egregious to break your bond. A helpful animal is easier to direct, as described under [[Actions/Command an Animal]].

 Bonding with a new animal ends any previous bond you had. You can't have both a bonded animal and an animal companion (though you can have both a bonded animal and a familiar).
