# Soaring Shape  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 132

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Untamed Form]]

---

Wings free you from the shackles of the ground below. Add the bat and bird shapes in [[Spells/Aerial Form (Player Core)|_aerial form_]] to your untamed form list. If you have [[Feats/Insect Shape (Player Core)|Insect Shape]], you also add the wasp shape to your untamed form list. If you have [[Feats/Ferocious Shape (Player Core)|Ferocious Shape]], you also add the pterosaur shape to your untamed form list. Whenever you use untamed form to take a shape that grants you a specific Acrobatics modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to Acrobatics checks.
