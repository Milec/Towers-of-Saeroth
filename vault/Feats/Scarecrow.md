**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bellflower Tiller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bellflower Dedication]]

---

You fight fiercely to protect your crop. When you hit a creature that is adjacent to two or more members of your crop with a weapon or unarmed attack, you gain a circumstance bonus to your damage roll equal to the number of weapon damage dice. When you hit a creature who has a member of your crop grabbed, that member can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] using a reaction.
