# Slag May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

Your mother was an [[Bestiary/Annis Hag|annis hag]], known for physical prowess, and you have one violet or steel-gray eye. You have thick, sturdy claws made of cold iron that grow naturally from your body. You gain a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage. Your claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, have the [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] and [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]] traits, and are [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]].
