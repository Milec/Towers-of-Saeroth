# Scouring Rage free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 80

**Prerequisites**
an instinct that allows you to change your additional damage from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] to a different damage type

**Trigger**
You Rage.

---

You emit a powerful surge of instinctual energy when you unleash your anger. Each enemy within 20 feet takes damage equal to your level ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC) of the same type as your additional damage during that Rage.
