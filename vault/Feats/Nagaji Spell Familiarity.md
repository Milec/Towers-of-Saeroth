# Nagaji Spell Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 49

---

Either through study, exposure, or familial devotion, you have the magic of [[Bestiary/Families/Naga|nagas]] bubbling in your blood. During your daily preparations, choose [[Spells/Daze|_daze_]], [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]], or [[Spells/Mage Hand|_mage hand_]]. Until your next daily preparations, you can cast the chosen spell as an occult innate cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
