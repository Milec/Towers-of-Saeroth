# Skyseeker two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [Jalmeri Heavenseeker](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=86)

**Requirements**
You are in Sky and Heaven Stance.

---

You [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] toward an opponent. At any point in the Leap, you can make a single Strike against a creature within your reach. At 12th level, if you hit with this Strike, instead of finishing your Leap, you can immediately attempt a second Leap from the space where you struck the target of your Strike. You can make a single Strike against a target within your reach during this second Leap as well. At 16th level, you can attempt a third Leap if your second Strike hits, and you can attack a target at any point during this third Leap. All Leaps made during this activity gain a +10-foot status bonus to the total height or distance of your leap, and no two Strikes can target the same creature.
