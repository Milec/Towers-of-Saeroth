# Lepidstadt Surgeon Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 94

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lepidstadt Surgeon]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]; attended the University of Lepidstadt

---

Before everything else, before the blood and the monsters and the Stasian technology and the horror, you are a doctor. You are, in fact, an exceptionally good doctor. You become an expert in Medicine. When you successfully [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]] to stabilize a [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] creature that doesn’t yet have the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition, it regains 2d8 Hit Points; this healing increases by 10 when you are a master of Medicine and by another 10 when you are legendary in Medicine. When you successfully Administer First Aid to stop bleeding, the target rolls the flat check (with lowered DC for an assisted recovery) twice and takes the better result; this is a fortune effect.
