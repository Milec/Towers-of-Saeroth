# Reptile Speaker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 58

---

You hear the sounds of reptiles as language. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with animals that are reptiles (the GM determines which animals count as reptiles).
