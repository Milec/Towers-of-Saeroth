# Contingency Leap ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fall, and there is an edge or handhold within 30 feet.

---

When imperiled by a fall, you swiftly transport yourself out of danger. You teleport to any edge or handhold in range, stopping your fall. If your destination is one you couldn’t normally stand on (such as a handhold), you must succeed at your choice of an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check or a Reflex save, usually at the [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] DC, as [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]], but you take no damage unless you critically fail.
