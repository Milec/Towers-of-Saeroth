# Exploding Bullet single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Volatile Spark|volatile spark hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding an unloaded [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearm]].

---

You load your gun with a bullet forged of magic instead of matter, barely contained in an arcane shell. [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|reload]] your firearm, creating an arcane round made from your choice of [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] energy. When you shoot this round, the Strike gains the [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] trait and the corresponding energy trait. The firearm gains the [[Traits/Scatter (NPC Core)|scatter 5 feet]] trait, or gains a +1 status bonus to its splash damage if it already has the scatter trait. The scatter trait’s splash damage uses the energy type you chose. The bullet disappears if not fired by the end of your next turn.

If your firearm takes more than 1 action to reload, you need to use Exploding Bullet for all the actions needed to reload it to get the benefits.

**Special** When you use volatile spark’s Reloading Cascade action, you can use Exploding Bullet instead of an Interact action to reload a firearm.
