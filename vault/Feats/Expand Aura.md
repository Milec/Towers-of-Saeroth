# Expand Aura single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
You have at least one [[Traits/Aura|aura]] from a champion feat or feature.

---

You focus your divine power to extend your influence. Select one [[Traits/Aura|aura]] you have from a champion feat or feature, such as [[Feats/Aura of Courage]] or [[Feats/Aura of Life]]. The radius of the aura is doubled until the start of your next turn.
