# Marshall Fiendish Forces  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archfiend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archfiend Dedication]]

---

You have been in contact with a retinue of [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], brokering all manner of deals so that they may eventually serve you. When you [Manifest your Realm](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2986), you can choose to have one such fiend come to your aid. This fiend is a [[Traits/Common (Player Core)|common]] fiend of level 11 or lower, which you select when you take this feat. The fiend appears in a place of your choosing within your manifested realm and has the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait. They remain for as long as your realm is manifested, until they’re reduced to 0 Hit Points, or until you command them to leave, at which point they immediately vanish and return to their place of origin.

At 20th level, you can choose a common fiend of level 15 or lower to appear instead when you Manifest your Realm.
