# Inner Fire  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

You can call the fire inside you into the palm of your hand. You can cast the [[Spells/Produce Flame|_produce flame_]] cantrip as an innate primal or arcane spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
