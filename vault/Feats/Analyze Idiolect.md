# Analyze Idiolect  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 178

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Linguist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Linguist Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You break down a specific individual's idiolect, memorizing their speech mannerisms and habits. If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, when you later attempt to [[Actions/Impersonate]] that individual, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to your Deception checks and DCs. Due to the intense character study required, you can't remember more than one idiolect at a time.
