# Animal Actor  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 58

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Kitharodian Actor]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Kitharodian Actor Dedication]]

---

You participated in a performance of _The Leaping Lion_, a play focusing on Cyricas, the animal-loving adventurer who traveled with his ape friend Mardu and remains a favorite hero among Taldan youth. You become trained in Nature and a Lore skill related to a particular type of animal commonly used in show business (such as Canine Lore, Ursine Lore, or Primate Lore); if you were already trained in either, you become an expert in that skill instead. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to all checks to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] and [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about creatures with the animal trait. You can use your chosen animal Lore to Command an Animal of that type.
