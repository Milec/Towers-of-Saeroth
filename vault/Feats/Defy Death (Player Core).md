# Defy Death  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 72

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Orc Ferocity (Player Core)|Orc Ferocity]]

---

You’re exceptionally difficult to kill. The DC of your [[Rules/Recovery Checks|recovery checks]] is equal to 9 + your [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] value, or 8 + your dying value if you have the [[Feats/Toughness]] general feat. In addition, whenever someone returns you to life using magic that would normally leave you debilitated for a week (such as [[Spells/Raise Dead|raise dead]] or the [[Rituals/Resurrect|resurrect]] ritual), you don’t suffer that condition.
