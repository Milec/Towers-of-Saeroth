# Elven Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 40

---

You favor bows and other elegant weapons. You are trained with [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow|longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow|composite longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longswords]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapiers]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbows]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbows]].

 In addition, you gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Elf|elf]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial elf weapons are simple weapons and advanced elf weapons are martial weapons.
