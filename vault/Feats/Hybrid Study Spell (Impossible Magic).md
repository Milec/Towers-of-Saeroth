# Hybrid Study Spell  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 88

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magus Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Magus Dedication]]

---

You gain the conflux spell from a [hybrid study](HybridStudies.aspx) of your choice. If you don’t already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 [[Rules/Focus]] Point. You can [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by studying your [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank) (Player Core)|spellbook]] and performing a physical regimen. You don’t gain any of the hybrid study’s other benefits.
