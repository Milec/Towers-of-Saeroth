# Rope Runner  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; [[Feats/Pirate Dedication]]

---

You run and climb across ropes and rigging almost as easily as on the ground. You gain a climb Speed of 15 feet, but only on ship's rigging or similar ropes. Whenever you succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]] a rope or an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] on a rope, you get a critical success instead. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] while Climbing or Balancing on a rope.
