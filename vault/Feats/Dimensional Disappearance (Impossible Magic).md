# Dimensional Disappearance  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Dimensional Assault|_dimensional assault_]] focus spell,; [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Laughing Shadow (Impossible Magic)|laughing shadow hybrid study]]

---

You take advantage of certain idiosyncrasies of [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] magic to better hide your position. When you cast [[Spells/Dimensional Assault|_dimensional assault_]], you’re affected by an [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] spell at the end of the teleport. You can choose not to make the [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] that is normally part of dimensional assault. If you do make the Strike, your invisibility ends after the Strike, as normal for invisibility.
