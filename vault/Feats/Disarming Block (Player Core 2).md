# Disarming Block free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 187

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bastion (Player Core 2)|Bastion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bastion Dedication (Player Core 2)|Bastion Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
You [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] made with a held weapon.

---

With deft and practiced movement, you block at an angle to potentially dislodge the weapon. You attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the creature whose attack you blocked of the weapon they attacked you with. You can do so even if you don’t have a hand free.
