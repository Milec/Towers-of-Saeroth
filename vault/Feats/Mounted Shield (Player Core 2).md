# Mounted Shield  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 193

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cavalier (Player Core 2)|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication (Player Core 2)|Cavalier Dedication]]

---

You’ve trained with your shield to defend both yourself and your mount. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]] while mounted, both you and your mount gain the shield’s circumstance bonus to AC. If you have the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction, you can use it when your mount would take physical damage, as long as you’re riding your mount. If you do, the shield prevents your mount from taking damage instead of preventing you from taking damage, following the normal rules for Shield Block.
