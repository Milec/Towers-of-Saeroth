# Thunderous Landing single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Winged Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Winged Warrior Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You currently have a fly Speed and are airborne.

---

You hurl away enemies with the force of your landing. You [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] and, if you end your movement on a solid surface, your landing sends out a shock wave of air. Each creature within 10 feet of your landing position must succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet away. This is forced movement.
