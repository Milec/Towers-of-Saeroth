# Callow May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

You are among the most common of changelings—those born to [[Bestiary/Green Hag|green hags]]—and a vibrant green eye signifies that parentage. Your mother's manipulative nature and versatile voice gives you an edge when deceiving others. You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar]] skill feat. If you roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] for initiative, foes in that combat encounter that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against you.
