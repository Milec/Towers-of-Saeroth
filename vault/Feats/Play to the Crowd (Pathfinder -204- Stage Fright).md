# Play to the Crowd  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 12)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You have a knack for knowing just how to manipulate groups by saying exactly what needs to be said. When you Lie in a public area in front of a crowd, you can gauge the reactions of the crowd as you speak to know how your [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] is working, and Lie loses its [[Traits/Secret (Player Core)|secret]] trait. You gain the Reactive Falsehood reaction.

**Reactive Falsehood** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You fail to Lie to a creature in public; Effect As your target sees through your lie, you spin your falsehood in a way that utilizes the crowd’s reaction to bolster believability. Re-roll your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check to Lie with a +2 status bonus and use this check’s result as the actual result to Lie.
