# Wolf Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 119

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

You enter the stance of a wolf, low to the ground with your hands held like fangs. You can make wolf jaw unarmed attacks. These deal 1d8 piercing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.

 If you're flanking a target while in Wolf Stance, your wolf jaw unarmed attacks also gain the [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait.
