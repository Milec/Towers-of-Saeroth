# Disorienting Venom  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Anadi]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 104

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Venomous Anadi]] heritage

---

The venom injected by your fangs disorients your foes. A creature damaged by your anadi venom must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round.
