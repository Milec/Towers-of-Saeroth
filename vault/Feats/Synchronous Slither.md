# Synchronous Slither ⤾  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 51

**Trigger**
An ally within your reach leaves a square during a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action they're using and ends their movement adjacent to an enemy.

---

You’ve learned to travel at your ally’s rhythm and use their movement to adjust your own position. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions from the enemy your ally moved adjacent to.
