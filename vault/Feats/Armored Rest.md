# Armored Rest  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 52

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swordmaster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Swordmaster Dedication]]; trained in light, medium, or heavy armor

---

You've slept many nights in your armor and gotten used to it. You can sleep in armor overnight without being [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] the next day. If you were already fatigued before sleeping, the armor doesn't prevent you from recovering from being fatigued.
