# Hail of Splinters two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 34

---

A fusillade of jagged splinters flies from you. Creatures in a 30-foot cone take 1d4 piercing damage and 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC.

---
**Level (+2)** Each type of damage increases by 1d4.
