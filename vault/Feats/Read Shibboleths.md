# Read Shibboleths  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 178

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Linguist]], [Venture-Gossip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=311) (Level 7)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Linguist Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

All communication is filled with slight tells and signals. If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, their regional words, pronunciation changes, and nonverbal cues provide you with a minor fact about their social environment, such as their hometown or certain groups they might belong to. If a target of this ability is being deceptive about their social environment, such as a commoner pretending to be a noble, they must attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]], or appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] check against your Society DC, providing you a false minor fact consistent with their assumed identity on a success.

 Once you've gleaned one or more true minor facts about a person, you can then incorporate these mannerisms into your speech to present yourself in a way that they find familiar. This grants you a +1 circumstance bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and Deception checks with them.
