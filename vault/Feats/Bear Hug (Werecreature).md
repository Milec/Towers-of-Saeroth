# Bear Hug (Werecreature) two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; werebear

**Requirements**
You’re in bear or hybrid shape, and your last action was a successful claw Strike.

---

You hug your opponent. You make another claw Strike against the same target. If this Strike hits, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] until the end of your next turn, unless you move or your target [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]].
