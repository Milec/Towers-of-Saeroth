# Underhanded Assault two actions  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 134

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Red Mantis Assassin (Prey for Death)|Red Mantis Assassin]] (Level 4)

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

You capitalize on the distraction provided by an ally. You [[Actions/Sneak]] up to a foe adjacent to one of your allies. You can roll against the foe you're Sneaking up on, even if it's currently [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observing]] you, as though you were [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]]. You take a –2 penalty on your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check. If your Stealth check against the chosen foe succeeds, you can make a melee Strike against that foe at the end of your Sneak.
