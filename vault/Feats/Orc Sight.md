# Orc Sight  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Half-Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 59

**Prerequisites**
[[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]

---

Your orc blood is strong enough to grant you the keen vision of your orc forebears. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], allowing you to see in darkness and dim light just as well as you can in bright light. However, in darkness, you see in black and white only.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can’t retrain out of this feat or into this feat.
