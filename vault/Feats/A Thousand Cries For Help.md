# A Thousand Cries For Help  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ascended Celestial]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

---

You’ve learned to communicate with creatures from all over the world, overcoming language barriers to lend a hand and help where you’re needed. You’re affected by a constant [[Spells/Truespeech|_truespeech_]] spell.

When you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]], you can spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to make the check at mythic proficiency.
