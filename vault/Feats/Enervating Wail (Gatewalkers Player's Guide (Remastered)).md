# Enervating Wail two actions  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 102

---

You release a terrible wail that tears at the spirits of all nearby. All living creatures in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] take [[Traits/Void|void]] damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. The damage is 4d6, plus 1d6 for every level you have beyond 6th.
