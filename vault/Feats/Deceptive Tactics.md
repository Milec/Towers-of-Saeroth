# Deceptive Tactics  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 30

**Requirements**
You can prepare at least three tactics.

---

Your training has taught you that the art of war is the art of deception. You can use your [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] modifier in place of your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] modifier for Deception checks to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]], and can use your proficiency rank in Warfare Lore instead of your proficiency rank in Deception to meet the prerequisites of feats that modify the Create a Diversion or Feint actions (such as [[Feats/Lengthy Diversion (Player Core)|Lengthy Diversion]]). You gain the Lengthy Diversion feat.
