# Elemental Explosion two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 54

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Elemental|elemental instinct]]

**Requirements**
You're [[Actions/Rage|raging]], and you haven't used this ability since you last Raged.

---

You unleash the energy roiling within you, exploding elemental matter in a 15-foot emanation. Each creature in the area takes 1d8 damage per level you possess, with the same type you chose for elemental rage. Each creature in the area must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. Elemental Explosion gains the trait of your element.
