# Serpentcoil Slam single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 50

**Frequency**
once per minute

---

Your people's ancient and storied rivalry with [[Bestiary/Garuda|garudas]] led you to develop special techniques against flying foes. Make a melee Strike against a flying creature up to one size larger than you; if you hit, you use your neck or coils to smash the creature into the ground. In addition to the normal effects of your Strike, the creature moves to the nearest unoccupied ground space adjacent to you and can't [[Actions/Fly]], [[Spells/Levitate|_levitate_]], or otherwise leave the ground for 1 round. On a critical hit, it can't Fly, levitate, or otherwise leave the ground for 1 minute.
