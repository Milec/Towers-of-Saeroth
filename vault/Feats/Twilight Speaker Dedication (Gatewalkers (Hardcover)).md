# Twilight Speaker Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 242

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Speaker (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Twilight Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]; you are an Ilverani elf

---

You have carefully studied the histories, customs, and etiquette of many of the younger peoples of Golarion. You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]. At 7th level, you become a master in Society, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Society. You can use the Society skill instead of the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill when you attempt to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on a non-elven intelligent humanoid creature.
