# Mortal Healing  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 105

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) As Mortal Healing requires you to specifically use the Treat Wounds action, it does not apply when used with other actions related to medicine or healing, such as Battle Medicine._

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pure Legion Enforcer]] (Level 2)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Godless Healing]], you follow the [[Setting/Deities/Laws of Mortality]]

---

You grant greater healing when the gods don’t interfere. When you roll a success to [[Actions/Treat Wounds]] for a creature that hasn’t regained Hit Points from divine magic in the past 24 hours, you get a critical success on your check instead and restore the corresponding amount of Hit Points.
