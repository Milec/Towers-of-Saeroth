# Innocuous  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 44

---

Halflings have been unobtrusive assistants of larger folk for untold ages, and your people count on this assumption of innocence. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (or another skill of your choice, if you’re already trained in Deception). If you fail a Deception check to Create a Diversion, humanoid creatures aren’t aware that you were trying to trick them unless you get a critical failure on your roll.
