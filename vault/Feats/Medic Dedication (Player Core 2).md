# Medic Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 208

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic (Player Core 2)|Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

---

You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]. When you succeed with [[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]] or [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], the target gains a circumstance bonus to the number of Hit Points they regain equal to 5 HP at DC 20, 10 HP at DC 30, or 15 HP at DC 40.  
 Once per day, you can use Battle Medicine on a creature that’s temporarily immune due to having already been treated with Battle Medicine. If you’re a master in Medicine, you can do so once per hour.
