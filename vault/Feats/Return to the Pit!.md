# Return to the Pit!  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 217, [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 9

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellbreaker Dedication]]

---

You can send the fiends who have vexed you back to their home plane where they belong. You can cast [[Spells/Banishment (Player Core)|_banishment_]] once per day as a divine innate spell. When you take this feat, you craft a personal symbol of the Hellbreakers League that's anathema to devils. If you wield this symbol in one hand when you spend an extra action while casting banishment on a devil, you don't need to pay the extra cost to give that creature a –2 circumstance penalty to its save.
