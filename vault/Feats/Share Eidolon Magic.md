# Share Eidolon Magic  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 72

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magical Understudy]]

---

Your eidolon shares innate spells with you. You can cast the innate spells your eidolon gained from Magical Understudy, [[Feats/Magical Adept]], and [[Feats/Magical Master]]. You and your eidolon share the same daily uses of these innate spells.
