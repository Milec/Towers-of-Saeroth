# Spellshot Dedication single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Class]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 140

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellshot]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Ways/Way of the Spellshot|way of the spellshot]]

---

You cast arcane spells like a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|wizard]], gaining a spellbook with four common [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] [[Rules/Cantrips (Player Core)|cantrips]] of your choice. You gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity. You can prepare two cantrips each day from your spellbook. You’re trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics. Your key spellcasting attribute for spellshot archetype spells is Intelligence, and they are arcane spells. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]]; if you were already trained in Arcana, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. This counts as the [[Archetypes/Wizard (Player Core)|wizard archetype]] for the benefits of [[Feats/Basic Wizard Spellcasting (Player Core)|Basic Wizard Spellcasting]].

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat other than Beast Gunner Dedication until you’ve gained two other feats from the spellshot or beast gunner archetypes.
