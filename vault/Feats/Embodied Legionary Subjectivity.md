# Embodied Legionary Subjectivity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fleshwarp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 29

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Cataphract Fleshwarp]] heritage

**Access**
You're from Nex

---

Nex's Fleshforges shaped you not to achieve individual greatness at arms, but rather to become a part of a legion, a modular piece integral to the combined arms stratagems of Nexian battlemages. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills, you instead gain the trained rank in another skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Warfare Lore]].
