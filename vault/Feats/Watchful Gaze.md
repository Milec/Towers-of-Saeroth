# Watchful Gaze single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Goloma]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 116

---

You use your many eyes to look in all directions at once, making you extremely observant for a short period of time. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/All-Around Vision|all-around vision]] until the start of your next turn. This lets you see in all directions and prevents you from being flanked.
