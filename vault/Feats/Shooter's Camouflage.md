# Shooter's Camouflage  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 117

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

You specialize in blending into a particular type of environment, able to disappear after taking your shot. Choose either natural or urban terrain. In the chosen terrain, you can [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] even without [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] or being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]].
