# Enduring Alchemy  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 64

---

You've learned how to make your personal energy last just a little bit longer when quickly brewing ad hoc concoctions. When using [[Feats/Quick Alchemy]] to create an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical tool]] or [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixir]], that tool or elixir remains potent until the end of your next turn, instead of losing its potency at the start of your next turn.
