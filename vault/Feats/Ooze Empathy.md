# Ooze Empathy  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oozemorph]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oozemorph Dedication]]; at least two other class feats from the oozemorph archetype

---

You can connect with [[Traits/Ooze|oozes]] (and other amorphous [[Traits/Aberration|aberrations]], at the GM's discretion) on a rudimentary level. You can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on oozes and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of them. In most instances, oozes will give you time to make your case.
