# Become Shadow  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 81

---

You unleash your mythic power to shroud yourself in darkness and silence to stalk your prey. When you attempt a check to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], or when you roll [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] for [[Rules/Initiative (Player Core)|initiative]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] and to attempt the check or roll using mythic proficiency.
