# Undead Spotter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -4|Wake the Dead #4]] pg. 28

---

Your studies of the undead have taught you telltale signs of a disguised undead creature. When you Pursue a Lead that involves a corpse or creature within your reach, you can touch that corpse or creature to immediately determine if it is undead. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Recall Knowledge checks about undead creatures and to determine if a creature is undead.
