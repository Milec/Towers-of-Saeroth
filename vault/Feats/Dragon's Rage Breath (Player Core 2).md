# Dragon's Rage Breath two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 79

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Dragon (Player Core 2)|dragon instinct]]

---

You breathe deeply and exhale powerful energy in a 30-foot cone, dealing 1d6 damage per level to each creature in the area with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. The damage type matches your instinct's dragon breath, and this action gains the trait of your dragon instinct's tradition.
