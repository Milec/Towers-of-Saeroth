# Expert Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Basic Captivator Spellcasting]]

---

You learn more powerful spells to enchant and deceive. You learn a 4th-level occult spell. At 12th level, you learn a 5th-level spell, and at 14th level, you learn a 6th-level spell. Each of these spells must come from either the [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] or [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] school. You Cast these Spells as occult innate spells. You become an expert with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
