# Worm Form two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You take on the form of a purple worm or transform into a humanoid-worm hybrid, retaining your own head but with a worm's body, transforming into a Huge battle form. You must have enough space to expand into or the ability is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait. You can Dismiss the form.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 40 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and you use the listed damage. Melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +30, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of monster you choose:

- **Purple Worm** Speed 40 feet, burrow 30 feet, swim 20 feet; **Melee** jaws ([[Traits/Deadly|deadly 2d10]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10+15 piercing; **Melee** stinger ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+15 piercing plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison]]; **Melee** body ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]) **Damage** 1d10+13 bludgeoning
- **Hybrid** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** stinger ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+15 piercing plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison]]; **Melee** body ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 1d10+13 bludgeoning; **Hybrid Caster** You retain the ability to speak, and can cast spells as long as they don't have somatic or material components.
