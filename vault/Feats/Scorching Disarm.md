# Scorching Disarm single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Ifrit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 103

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You make a weapon unbearable to hold. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]] a target creature. You don't need to have a hand free to make this Disarm attempt. If the Disarm attempt succeeds, the target takes 4d6 fire damage unless it drops the weapon, and on a critical success, it takes 4d6 fire damage and drops the weapon. If you're legendary in Athletics, this increases to 8d6 fire damage.
