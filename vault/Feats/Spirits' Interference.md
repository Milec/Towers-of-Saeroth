# Spirits' Interference single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 91

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Spirit|spirit instinct]]

---

You call forth protective spirits to ward off ranged attacks. Until your rage ends, anyone making a ranged attack against you must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or the attack misses with no effect.
