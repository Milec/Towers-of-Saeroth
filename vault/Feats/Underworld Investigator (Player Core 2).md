# Underworld Investigator  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 107

---

Your work as an investigator makes you familiar with the criminal element. You might be a criminal yourself, or you might have gained an understanding of how they operate from the outside. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Underworld Lore]], and you gain your Pursue a Lead investigation bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to investigate a case (such as checks to [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] a clue from a suspect or [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]] to open a safe with damning documents).
