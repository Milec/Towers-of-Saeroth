# Eyes Unclouded  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ascended Celestial]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ascended Celestial Dedication]]

---

Your senses are enhanced by your divine spark, enabling you see things as they truly are, piercing through illusions, transformations, and lies. You’re affected by a constant [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] spell. Additionally, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] and to Perception DCs against attempts to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you. When a creature attempts to Lie to you, they roll the [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check to Lie twice and use the worse result; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect.
