# Energized Spark  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 35

---

The energy of your spirit manifests as crackling lightning, the chill of winter, or the power of an element. Choose one of the following traits: [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] (slashing), [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]] (bludgeoning), [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]] (slashing), [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], [[Traits/Void|void]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] (bludgeoning), or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] (piercing). You can choose for any [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage dealt by your exemplar abilities to instead gain the trait and deal the corresponding damage type.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different damage type each time.
