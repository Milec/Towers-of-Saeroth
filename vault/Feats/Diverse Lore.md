# Diverse Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 42

---

Your wandering studies mean you've heard rumors or theories about almost every topic... though admittedly, your sources aren't always the most reliable. You can take a –2 penalty to your check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] with [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Esoteric Lore]] to Recall Knowledge about any topic, not just the usual topics available for Esoteric Lore. Additionally, when you succeed at your check to [[Actions/Exploit Vulnerability|Exploit a Vulnerability]], compare the result of your Esoteric Lore check to the DC to Recall Knowledge for that creature; if that number would be a success or a critical success, you gain information as if you had succeeded at the Recall Knowledge check.
