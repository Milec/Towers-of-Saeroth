# Rat Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ratfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 22

---

You have a pet rat that has become magically bonded to you. You gain a [familiar](Familiars.aspx) using the rules [[Rules/Familiars|here]], and this familiar must be a rat. It still gets the benefits of familiar abilities, but its base form remains a rat.
