# Soothing Mist  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 132

**Prerequisites**
warden spells

---

You have a connection to the healing properties of nature and can produce a magical mist to heal damage and stop burning or bleeding. You gain the [[Spells/Soothing Mist|_soothing mist_]] warden spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.
