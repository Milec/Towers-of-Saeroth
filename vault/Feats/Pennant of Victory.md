# Pennant of Victory single action  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 35

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You wave your banner in victory, signaling to your allies that the fight is won. You and all allies within the aura of your  commander's banner gain a +4 status bonus to attack and damage rolls, a +10-foot status bonus to all your Speeds, and  40 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]]. These benefits last until the start of your next turn.
