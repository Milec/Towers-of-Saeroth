# Mirror-Risen  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 120

---

You were created from magic involving mirrors and still retain some ability to seek refuge in reflected illusions. You gain the Mirror's Trickery reaction.

**Mirror's Trickery** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would be hit by a Strike; **Effect** You create an illusory duplicate at the last instant and attempt to trick your foe into striking it instead of you. The attacker attempts a DC 11 flat check; on a failure, the attack hits the duplicate, changing the result from a critical success to a success or a success to a failure.
