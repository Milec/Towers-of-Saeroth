# Lightspeed Assault single action  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 11

---

You zoom from enemy to enemy, striking each in an instant. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] to an enemy and attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against it. If successful, you can add another action to Lightspeed Assault. Stride again and attempt another Strike; if this second Strike is successful, you can spend another action to Stride again and attempt a third Strike. You can use Lightspeed Assault while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type. You attempt a backlash check for your deviation only once, regardless of how many actions you end up spending on your Lightspeed Assault.
