# Magical Adept  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 82

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magical Understudy (Impossible Magic)|Magical Understudy]]

---

Your eidolon gains more magic. Choose one 2nd-rank spell and one 1st-rank spell of your eidolon’s tradition. Your eidolon can cast them each once per day as [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spells]]. At every even level after you take this feat, your eidolon can swap one of these innate spells for a new innate spell that’s 2 or more ranks lower than your highest-rank spell slot.
