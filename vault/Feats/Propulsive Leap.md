# Propulsive Leap single action  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide]] pg. 9, [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 101

---

By expelling flames from your feet or levitating through electrical repulsion, you propel yourself through the air. For 1 minute, you gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed or 20 feet, whichever is greater. If you aren't on solid ground at the end of your turn, you must attempt another backlash check for your deviation, though you remain airborne even if you fail your check unless the damage from the check renders you [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
