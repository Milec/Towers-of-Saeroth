# Second Wind  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 77

---

You can enter a second rage, but afterward you need to catch your breath. When you [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]], you can choose to gain  temporary HP from the Rage even if 1 minute hasn't passed (or a full turn if you have the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|revitalizing rage class feature]]). If you do, once this Rage ends you become [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] until you rest for 10 minutes.
