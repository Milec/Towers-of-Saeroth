# Fluid Contortionist  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 119

---

You can ignore difficult terrain caused by moving through tight spaces that aren't tight enough to force you to [[Actions/Squeeze]]. When you roll a critical failure on a check to Squeeze, you get a failure instead, and when you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
