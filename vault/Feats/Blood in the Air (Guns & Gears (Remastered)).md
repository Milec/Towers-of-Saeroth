# Blood in the Air single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 116

**Requirements**
An enemy you've damaged with a ranged weapon during the last minute is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from you.

---

Once you've locked on to a target, little can obscure your aim. Make a ranged weapon Strike against the required target. This Strike ignores the target's concealed condition and reduces the flat check for the hidden condition from 11 to 5.
