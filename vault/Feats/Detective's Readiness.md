# Detective's Readiness  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 61

---

When you're on a case, you stay watchful and resolute to avoid tricks your suspect might pull. You gain your circumstance bonus from [[Actions/Pursue a Lead]] on saving throws (and their DCs, as normal) against the subject of a lead, and you can use [[Actions/Clue In]] to assist an ally's saving throw against that subject.
