# Growth Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist Dedication]]; wood is in your elemental philosophy

---

You enhance your spell with elemental wood, causing your magic to expand and grow beyond its original limitations. If the next action you use this turn is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that affects an area, expand the area of the spell. For bursts, expand the radius by 5 feet; for cones, expand the cone by 10 feet; and for lines, expand the length by 15 feet. The spell gains the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait.
