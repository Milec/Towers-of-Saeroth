# Freeze It! single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 39

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Snow Goblin]] heritage

---

You are no rime hag or Jadwiga, but the magic of the north has still left a mark on you. Frigid ice runs through your veins, and you can expel frost from your body to freeze your foes. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the Fortitude DC of an adjacent foe. If you have master proficiency in Athletics, you can affect up to two adjacent foes, rolling one Athletics check against each foe.

**Critical Success** The target becomes clumsy 2 for 1 round.  

**Success** The target becomes clumsy 1 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is temporarily immune for 1 minute.
