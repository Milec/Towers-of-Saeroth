# Familiar Oddities  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 169

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Manifestations|Curse Maelstrom]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Curse Maelstrom Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Curse Lore]]

---

Perhaps due to the curse within you, curses occasionally perceive you as an extension of themselves and readily reveal themselves to you. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Identify Magic]] on a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|cursed item]] or a spell that has the [[Traits/Curse|curse]] trait.
