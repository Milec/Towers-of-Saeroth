You use your imposing demeanor to set up the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] for defeat. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an undead creature using a weapon with which you have master proficiency. If you hit, all Strikes against that creature gain the benefits of Reaper of Repose for 1 round.
