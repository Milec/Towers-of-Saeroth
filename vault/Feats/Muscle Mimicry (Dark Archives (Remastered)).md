# Muscle Mimicry ⤾  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Alter Egos in Golarion (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Alter Ego]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Alter Ego Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Alter Ego Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

**Trigger**
An enemy makes a successful [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] attempt.

---

Your study of another allows you to mirror their movements subtly, even in the heat of combat. You study the successful maneuver of an enemy and instinctively learn to do it. The next time you take the same action they did, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Athletics check, or a +2 circumstance bonus if the enemy critically succeeded on their triggering action. You lose this benefit if you don't use it before the end of your next turn.
