# Empiricist's Eye  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 111

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Methodologies/Alchemical Sciences (Player Core 2)|empiricism]] methodology

---

Sharp and piercing, your eyes see all and convey more. Your [[Actions/Point Out (Player Core)|Point Out]] actions lose the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait, and you don't need to be heard to convey the information to your allies. In addition, a creature you Point Out is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to your allies until the start of your next turn.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Blind-Fight (Player Core)|Blind-Fight]] feat, your allies gain that feat's benefits against any creature that's off-guard due to Empiricist's Eye.
