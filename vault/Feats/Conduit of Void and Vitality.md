# Conduit of Void and Vitality two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|Cursebound]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 143

**Prerequisites**
any oracle mystery

**Requirements**
You have [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] or [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]] as a signature spell and an available spell slot to cast it with.

---

You use the unstable energy of your [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] to manipulate the most basic of divine magic. You cast a 3-action [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] or [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]] spell, expending the slot as normal. If the spell restores Hit Points to one or more creatures, then one creature healed by this spell regains a number of additional Hit Points equal to 1d8 × your [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] value; if the spell damages one or more creatures, then one creature harmed by this spell takes additional damage equal to 1d8 × your cursebound value.
