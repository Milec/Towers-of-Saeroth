# Celestial Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 35

---

You were raised with an aasimar or celestial relative, or you've devoted yourself to researching the secrets of the celestial realms. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill related to the celestial realm from which you trace your lineage (usually [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]] Lore, [[Setting/Planes/Heaven]] Lore, or [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana]] Lore).
