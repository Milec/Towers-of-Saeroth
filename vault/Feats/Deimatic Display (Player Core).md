# Deimatic Display single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 131

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

Imitating animal threat displays, you make yourself appear larger and more imposing. Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet. This attempt has the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, loses the auditory trait, and you don't take a penalty if the creature doesn't understand your language. Roll your check only once and compare it to the Will DC of each target. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check against animal, fungus, and plant creatures and take a –2 circumstance penalty against other creatures. Each target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.
