# Form Control single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 135

**Prerequisites**
Strength 14; [[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

With additional care and effort, you can take on an alternate form for a longer period of time. If your next action is to cast [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]], _wild shape’s_ spell level is 2 lower than normal (minimum 1st level), but you can remain transformed for up to 1 hour or the listed duration (whichever is longer). You can still Dismiss the form at any time, as permitted by the spell.
