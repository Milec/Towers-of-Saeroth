# Divine Disharmony single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Esoterica]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 42

---

From your collection of religious trinkets, you pull out opposing divine objects—such as the religious symbols of two deities that are hated enemies—and combine them in a display that causes discordant clashes of divine energy that are especially distracting to the faithful. Roll your choice of a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check against the Will DC of a creature you can see within 60 feet, with the following results. If the creature is particularly devoted to a deity, such as a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]], [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], or other creature with [[Traits/Divine|divine]] spells, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your skill check. The GM might determine that a creature that despises all deities, such as an [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu|alghollthu]], is unaffected.

**Critical Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to your attacks until the end of your next turn.  

**Success** The creature is flat-footed against your attacks until the end of your current turn.
