Your knowledge of items' construction allows you to discern their magical effects as well. You can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] instead of a skill associated with a magic tradition to [[Actions/Identify Magic]] on magic items, though not on any other sorts of magic.
