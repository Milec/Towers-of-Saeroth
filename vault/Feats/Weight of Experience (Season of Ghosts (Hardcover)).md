The experience you’ve accumulated over multiple lifetimes envelops you like a weighted blanket, providing comfort and bestowing confidence under pressure. You gain the trained proficiency rank in one skill of your choice and the [[Feats/Assurance (Player Core)|Assurance]] skill feat in that skill as a bonus feat.
