# Snap Out of It! (Pathfinder Agent) two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 41

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pathfinder Agent]]

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]; [[Feats/Pathfinder Agent Dedication]]

---

Pathfinders are trained to help each other be safe and successful, and you know how to jolt your allies back to their senses. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check on an adjacent ally who is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] and choose one of those conditions. The DC is the DC for the effect that caused the condition.

**Critical Success** Reduce the value of the chosen condition by 2. If you chose fascinated, that condition ends.  

**Success** Reduce the value of the chosen condition by 1. If you chose fascinated, that condition ends.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.  

**Critical Failure** You increase the value of the chosen condition by 1. If you chose fascinated, increase the duration by 1 round instead.
