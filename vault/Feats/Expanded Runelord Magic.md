# Expanded Runelord Magic  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 73

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Avenging Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Avenging Runelord Dedication]]

---

Your ability to use magic associated with your chosen sin grows. You gain the ability to cast one more arcane spell as an innate spell. This spell must be chosen from those listed in Avenging Runelord Dedication above, as well as [[Spells/Contingency (Player Core)|_contingency_]], [[Spells/Spell Riposte|_spell riposte_]], or any 7th-rank spell from your chosen sin’s school. You can cast this spell as an arcane innate spell once per day. The spell is automatically heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. Use mythic proficiency to determine any attack rolls or spell DCs.
