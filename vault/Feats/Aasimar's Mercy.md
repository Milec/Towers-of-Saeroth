# Aasimar's Mercy  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 36

---

Your celestial powers allow you to remove lesser afflictions with ease. Each day, you can cast two 4th-level divine innate spells. You can choose from the following spells each time you cast: [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]], [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]], and [[Spells/Neutralize Poison|_neutralize poison_]].
