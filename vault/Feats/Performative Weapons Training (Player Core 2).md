# Performative Weapons Training  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 201

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Gladiator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Gladiator Dedication]]

---

You're skilled with flashy weapons and can use them to great effect. You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|bo staff]], [[Equipment/Dueling Cape (Player Core)|dueling cape]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain (Player Core)|spiked chain]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sword Cane (Tian Xia Character Guide)|sword cane]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|trident]], [[Equipment/Weapons/War Flail (Player Core)|war flail]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]]. For the purposes of proficiency, you treat these as simple weapons. The GM may add other martial weapons to this list as appropriate for the world or region you are playing in.

 Whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
