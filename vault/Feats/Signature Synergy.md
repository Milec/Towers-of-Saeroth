# Signature Synergy  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Summoner]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Advanced Synergy]]

---

Your [eidolon](Eidolons.aspx) gains an evolution integral to its form, and it comes more easily than your other synergies. You gain one evolution feat from the following list: [[Feats/Airborne Form]], [[Feats/Burrowing Form]], [[Feats/Ever-Vigilant Senses]], or [[Feats/Hulking Size]]. For the purpose of meeting its prerequisites, your summoner level is equal to your character level – 4.
