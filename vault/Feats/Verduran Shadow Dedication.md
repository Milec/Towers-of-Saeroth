# Verduran Shadow Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

---

Your awareness of primal energies allows you to explore forests with preternatural ease. You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. You can substitute your proficiency in Survival for your proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] when meeting feat prerequisites and gaining additional benefits from feats for being an expert, master, or legendary in Stealth; however, unless you also fulfill the Stealth prerequisite, you can only use those feats in forest terrain. While in forests, you can use your Survival modifier in place of your Stealth modifier when you [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]], [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]], [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], or would use Stealth to roll initiative.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you’ve gained two other feats from the Verduran Shadow archetype.
