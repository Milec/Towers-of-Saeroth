# Relinquish Control free action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 22

**Trigger**
Your turn begins.

---

Your apparition takes over and shields you from outside influence. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +4 status bonus on saves against spells and effects that give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition or attempt to influence your actions (such as [[Spells/Charm (Player Core)|_charm_]], [[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_command_]], or a [[Bestiary/Nosoi (Monster Core)|nosoi’s]] haunting melody). However, the only actions you can take are to [[Rules/Recall Knowledge (GM Core)|Recall Knowledge]], [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast an apparition Spell]], Cast a vessel Spell, [[Actions/Sustain]] a vessel spell, or use an action that has the [[Traits/Apparition|apparition]] trait. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on all Recall Knowledge checks made using Lore skills granted by your attuned apparitions.

**Special** This feat requires a particularly strong bond with a specific apparition to learn. Choose one apparition you have access to; once you learn this feat, you must always choose that apparition as one of the apparitions you attune to each day.
