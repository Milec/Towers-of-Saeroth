# Razmiran Priest Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 290

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Razmiran Priest]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

You have earned entry to the priesthood of Razmir, either at the Exalted Lodge or another temple where Razmir’s holy masks are forged. You gain a [[Equipment/Razmiri Mask|Razmiri mask]] and can [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] a replacement in 4 hours if yours is ever damaged or lost. There is no value in selling your Razmiri mask, and only you gain its benefits. The abilities of your Razmiri mask use the higher of your class DC or spell DC.

You can take the [[Feats/Cleric Dedication (Player Core)|Cleric Dedication]] feat without needing to meet its prerequisites and before you take two other feats from the Razmiran priest archetype, but you must choose [[Setting/Deities/Razmir]] as your deity. All spells granted by the cleric archetype when gained in this way are [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spells instead of [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spells, and cleric feats that normally have the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] trait instead have the [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] trait. Your key spellcasting attribute for these spells is Charisma, rather than Wisdom.
