# Pounce Mimicry single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Mimic Dedication]]; you have seen a creature use an ability that lets them [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] and [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] as a single action (such as Pounce) or have identified a creature with such an ability in combat

**Requirements**
You’re unarmored or wearing light armor

---

You have mastered the art of stalking your prey and striking when they are most vulnerable. You Stride and then Strike. If you began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], you remain hidden until after the Strike. You can use Pounce Mimicry while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
