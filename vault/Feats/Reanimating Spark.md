# Reanimating Spark  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 65

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You have the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition and are about to attempt a recovery check.

---

The magic that animates you strains to keep you alive. You're restored to 1 Hit Point, lose the dying and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] conditions, and can act normally on this turn. You gain or increase the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition as normal when losing the dying condition in this way.
