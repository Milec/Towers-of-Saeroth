# Combat Premonition  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 187

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Chronoskimmer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Chronoskimmer Dedication]]

---

By narrowing your sense of the future, you can improve that of your allies. When you roll initiative, instead of stabilizing or destabilizing your own timestream, you can grant your allies a flash of insight into their future. Choose two allies. Those allies roll their initiative roll twice and take the better result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. You roll your initiative roll twice and take the worse result; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect. The two effects are tied together: if you would avoid the misfortune effect for any reason, or if any of your allies would negate their fortune effect, your Combat Premonition does nothing.
