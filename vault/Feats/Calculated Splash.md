# Calculated Splash  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 77

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Demolitionist]] (Level 6)

---

You have calculated all the angles to maximize a bomb’s splash. When you throw an alchemical bomb with the [[Traits/Splash|splash]] trait, you can cause the bomb to deal splash damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0) instead of the normal amount.
