# Pact of the Fey Paths  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 47

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbinder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pactbinder Dedication]]

---

You’ve sworn a pact with a powerful fey creature that longs to see the wonders of the Universe. You gain the Fey Jump ability. In exchange, you can no longer see the moon or stars as the fey steals those wonders from your eyes, and you must gaze at the empty sky for at least 10 minutes each night. If you do not, the fey revokes your benefits for the next day. If you go a week without gazing, the fey revokes your benefits until you atone.

**Activate** single action Fey Jump;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] You jump through the First World for a brief second, teleporting up 60 feet away to an empty space you can see. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an extradimensional container—the action is lost.
