# Archaeologist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archaeologist (Player Core 2)|Archaeologist]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]]

---

You are a student of peoples and their histories, and are in constant pursuit of knowledge and artifacts from the past. You become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]], and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about ancient history, peoples, and cultures.
