# Call from Death's Door ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 84

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mythic Callings/Caretaker's Calling]]

**Trigger**
The [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition of an ally you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observing]] would increase.

---

Even death halts in the face of your power. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; the triggering ally loses the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition, their [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition doesn’t increase, and they heal a number of Hit Points equal to twice your level.
