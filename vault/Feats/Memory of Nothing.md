# Memory of Nothing two actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 109

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Apocalypse Rider]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Apocalypse Rider Dedication]]

---

With a look and a gesture, you turn a thinking creature’s mind against itself, causing the creature to struggle to remember how to perform more complex actions. Choose a target within 30 feet and spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]. The target must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) at mythic proficiency.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** For the next 3 rounds, if the target performs an activity that requires three or more actions, they take 12d8 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage.  

**Failure** For the next 3 rounds, if the target performs an activity that requires two or more actions, they take 12d8 mental damage.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 as its mind struggles to function.
