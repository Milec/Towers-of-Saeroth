# Arcane Safeguards  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 41

---

The magic powering you makes it difficult for outside magic to affect you. You gain the Resist Magic reaction.

**Enhancement** Your safeguards improve. When you Resist Magic, you gain a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to the spell level of the triggering effect.
