# Whirlpool's Pull single action  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 83

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Resurgent Maelstrom|Resurgent Maelstrom Hybrid Study]]

---

You briefly conjure a tendril of water that allows you to grab an improvised weapon and then subsume that water to power your magic. You Interact to pick up an unattended object within 15 feet you could use as an improvised weapon and then Cast a Spell that is a cantrip or conflux spell that takes 1 action to cast.
