You call out a quick cadence, guiding your allies into a more efficient rhythm. Each willing ally in your marshal’s aura is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] until the end of their next turn, and they can use the extra action only to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. If an ally uses this extra action, at the end of its turn that ally becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until the end of its following turn.
