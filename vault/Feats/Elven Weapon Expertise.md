# Elven Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elven Weapon Familiarity]]

---

Your elven affinity blends with your class training, granting you great skill with elven weapons. Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in certain weapons, you also gain that proficiency in [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow|longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow|composite longbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longswords]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapiers]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbows]], and all elf weapons in which you are trained.
