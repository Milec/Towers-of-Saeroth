# Esoteric Reflexes  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 46

**Prerequisites**
an implement that grants a reaction

---

Your bond to your [implements](Implements.aspx) is so strong that you can use their reactive abilities almost by instinct. At the start of your turn, you gain an additional reaction, which you can use for only the reactions granted by your implements.
