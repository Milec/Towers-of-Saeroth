# Purge Sins single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 11

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You call on your celestial forebears to rid your body of all toxins and impurities. For each [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison|poison]] currently affecting you, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] check to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] that disease or poison. As normal, your counteract level is equal to half your level, rounded up.
