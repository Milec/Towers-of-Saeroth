You can use ki to move with extraordinary speed and make yourself harder to hit. You gain the [[Spells/Ki Rush|_ki rush_]] ki spell and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. The rules for ki spells are summarized in the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|Key Terms section on the monk class page]], and the full rules for focus spells appear [[Rules/Focus Spells|here]].
