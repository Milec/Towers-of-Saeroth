# Energize Bite free action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 217

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Breath of the Dragon (Player Core 2)|Breath of the Dragon]]; [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|jaws unarmed attack]]

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to use Breath of the Dragon.

---

The deadly energy of your breath remains in your jaws to empower your attacks. Until the end of your turn, your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|jaws]] unarmed attack deals an extra 1d6 damage of a type matching that of your [draconic benefactor.](DraconicBenefactors.aspx) This ability has the trait associated with the type of damage it deals.
