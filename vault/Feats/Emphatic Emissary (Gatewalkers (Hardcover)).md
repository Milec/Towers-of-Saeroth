# Emphatic Emissary ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 243

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Speaker (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Twilight Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Disarming Smile (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Disarming Smile]]

**Trigger**
You roll initiative.

---

You can tell when diplomacy has failed and a meeting is close to unraveling or falling to blows. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your initiative roll. During your first turn in combat, you can use your Disarming Smile, targeting every [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] intelligent humanoid creature that can see you but has yet to act. If you choose to sustain your Disarming Smile, you sustain the effect for only one creature, as normal.
