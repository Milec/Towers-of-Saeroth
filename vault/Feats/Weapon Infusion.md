# Weapon Infusion free action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infusion]], [[Traits/Kineticist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 21

---

With a signature flair, you shape blasts of elemental power into the form of a weapon, such as a sword made of whirling wind or a bow that shoots flames. If your next action is an [[Actions/Elemental Blast]], choose a weapon shape for it to take. You can choose to change the blast's damage type to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing—whichever suits the weapon shape—and you can choose other alterations depending on whether you make a melee or ranged blast.

 **Melee** Add one of the following traits of your choice: [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]], [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]], [[Traits/Reach|reach]], [[Traits/Sweep|sweep]].

 **Ranged** Choose one of three options: [[Traits/Range|range increment 100 feet]] and the [[Traits/Volley|volley 30 feet]] trait, range increment 50 feet and the [[Traits/Propulsive|propulsive]] trait, or range increment 20 feet and the [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] trait.
