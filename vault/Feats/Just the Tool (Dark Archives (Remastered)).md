# Just the Tool single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mind Smith (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mind Smith Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith Dedication]]

---

You temporarily change your weapon's shape to assist you in the field. You morph your weapon into a single simple [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|tool]], such as a shovel or [[Equipment/Crowbar (Player Core)|crowbar]], to help with a mundane task. You can't replicate entire toolkits with this ability. You can use this action again to change your mind weapon back to a weapon.
