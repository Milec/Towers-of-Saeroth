# Cloud Gazer  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sylph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 114

---

Your connection to air allows you to see through obscuring mists. You can see well enough through fog, mist, and clouds that would normally cause creatures to be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] that you don't need to succeed at a flat check to target creatures benefiting from such concealment.
