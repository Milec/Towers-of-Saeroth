# Anticipate Ambush  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 135

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

Using your extensive knowledge of ambush tactics, you keep watch for any hidden creatures or dangerous sites, moving at half your travel speed. If you're Anticipating an Ambush at the start of an encounter, you roll a Perception check for initiative, and all enemies rolling [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] for initiative take a –2 circumstance penalty to their Stealth checks.
