# Monk Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 227

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Monk]]

**Prerequisites**
Strength 14; Dexterity 14

---

You become trained in unarmed attacks and gain the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|powerful fist]] class feature. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]; if you are already trained in both of these skills, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in monk class DC.

**Special** You can’t select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the monk archetype.
