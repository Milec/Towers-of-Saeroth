# Crusader's Masquerade single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 13

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Five Branches of Scholarship|Magaambyan Attendant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mask Familiar]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Requirements**
You’re wearing your mask familiar in its mask form, and it has the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Skilled (Player Core)|skilled]] (Religion) familiar ability.

---

Your mask can hide you from the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to undead using vision or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]], allowing you to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] without other sources of cover or concealment. This effect lasts only as long as you continue to take no actions other than to Hide, Sneak, [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]], [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] to direct your mask familiar, or other surreptitious actions. The GM determines which actions end the effect, but attacking, casting spells, activating items, and the like always do.
