# Courageous Onslaught single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 106

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Courageous Advance (Player Core)|Courageous Advance]]; [[Feats/Courageous Assault (Player Core)|Courageous Assault]]

You use your performance to orchestrate an onslaught against your enemies. If your next action is to cast the [[Spells/Courageous Anthem|_courageous anthem_]] [[Traits/Composition (Player Core)|composition]] cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] and then make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
