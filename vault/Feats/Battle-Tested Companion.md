# Battle-Tested Companion  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 31

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Commander's Companion]]

---

Your companion is a tried and tested ally of unshakable reliability. The companion you gained with Commander's  Companion is now a [[Feats/Mature Animal Companion (Ranger) (Player Core)|mature animal companion]]. While your banner is affixed to this companion, the banner's aura is 10 feet greater than it normally is (typically this means the banner's 30-foot aura becomes a 40-foot aura).
