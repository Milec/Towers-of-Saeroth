# Selfless Parry  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 197

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Duelist (Player Core 2)|Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dueling Parry (Fighter) (Player Core)|Dueling Parry (Fighter)]]; [[Feats/Duelist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Duelist Dedication]]

---

You protect those near to you with a flash of steel and a stroke of good luck. When you're benefiting from Dueling Parry, allies adjacent to you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC. If you have [[Feats/Dueling Riposte (Player Core)|Dueling Riposte]], you can use it when an enemy within your reach critically fails a Strike against an ally adjacent to you, not just against yourself.
