# Perfect Resistance  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 222

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Student of Perfection]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Student of Perfection Dedication]]

---

You gain resistance equal to half your level to all damage from effects with the appropriate trait for your School of Perfection: [[Traits/Fire|fire]] for Unblinking Flame, [[Traits/Water|water]] for Unbreaking Waves, [[Traits/Air|air]] for Unfolding Wind, and [[Traits/Earth|earth]] for Untwisting Iron. After using a Student of Perfection ki spell, the resistance increases to be equal to your level for 1 round.
