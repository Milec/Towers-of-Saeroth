You can take on the shape of some of the world's most fearsome creatures. Add the shapes listed in [[Spells/Dragon Form (Player Core)|_dragon form_]] to your untamed form list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another form using [[Spells/Untamed Form|_untamed form_]], you gain resistance 5 to your choice of bludgeoning or poison.
