# Spray Ink ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Cecaelia Merfolk]] heritage

**Trigger**
A creature hits you with a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]].

---

Your body provides you with natural defenses common to other creatures of the sea. You spray ink against the triggering creature. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] while the ink remains. The creature can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to remove the ink.
