# Resuscitate three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 208

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic (Player Core 2)|Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Medic Dedication (Player Core 2)|Medic Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]]

**Requirements**
You are holding [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]], or you are wearing them and have a hand free.

---

You resuscitate the dead. Attempt a DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check to revive a dead creature who has been dead for no more than 3 rounds and whose body is mostly intact. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of [[Spells/Raise Dead (Player Core)|_raise dead_]], except it still has the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition it had before dying, increased by 1 (or wounded 1 if it wasn’t wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is temporarily immune to Resuscitate for 1 day.
