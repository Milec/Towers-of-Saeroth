# Verduran Ambush three actions  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Verduran Shadow Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

You prepare to ambush a foe. You [[Actions/Ready (Player Core)|Ready]] an activity that takes 2 actions, though that activity must either be [[Feats/Death from Above]] or an activity with the requirement that you be [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by all your opponents (such as [[Feats/Scout's Pounce (Player Core 2)|Scout’s Pounce]]). After using the reaction, you cannot use this feat again for 10 minutes.
