# To Thine Own Self ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You fail a saving throw against an effect that would alter your memory or cause you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] by another creature.

---

Awareness of your past perils protects you from those who would manipulate your thoughts. Attempt to counteract the triggering effect using your Incarnation Lore modifier as your counteract modifier and half your level as your counteract rank.
