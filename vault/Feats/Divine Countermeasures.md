# Divine Countermeasures  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 36

---

You've studied your celestial heritage with the intent of better defending yourself, and you've found that your techniques are equally powerful against [[Traits/Celestial|celestials]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]], and other divine entities. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Divine|divine]] effects.
