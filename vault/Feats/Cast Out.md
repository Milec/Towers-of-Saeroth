# Cast Out two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 23

**Archetypes** [[Rules/Sidebars/Spirit Dwellings and Remnants|Exorcist]], [[Archetypes/Palatine Detective]] (Level 6)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Exorcist Dedication]]

---

You attempt to cast a malevolent entity out of the creature or object it's possessing. Roll a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the [[Traits/Possession|possession]] effect; you must be within 30 feet of the possessed creature or object to do so. Your counteract level is half your level rounded up, and your counteract modifier is your [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] modifier, [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] modifier, or spell DC – 10, whichever is highest. If you succeed, the entity is driven out and can't attempt to possess that creature or object for 1 week. If you fail, the possessing creature is immune to your Cast Out for a year and a day.
