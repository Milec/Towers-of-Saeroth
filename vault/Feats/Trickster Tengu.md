# Trickster Tengu  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 59

---

You can transform yourself or the bodies of others to express your displeasure. Once per day, you can cast either [[Spells/Aerial Form|_aerial form_]] or [[Spells/Baleful Polymorph|_baleful polymorph_]] as a 7th-level primal innate spell. You must take the form of a bird when using this aerial form.
