# Titan's Stature  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 92

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Giant|giant instinct]]; [[Feats/Giant's Stature]]

---

You grow to even greater size. When using [[Feats/Giant's Stature|Giant’s Stature]], you can instead become Huge (increasing your reach by 10 feet if you were Medium or smaller) while you are raging. You have the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] condition as long as you are Huge.
