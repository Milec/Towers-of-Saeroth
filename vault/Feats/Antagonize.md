# Antagonize  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 88

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]] (Level 4)

---

Your taunts and threats earn your foes ire. When you successfully [[Actions/Demoralize]] a creature, its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition can't decrease to less than 1 at the end of its turn until it either uses a hostile action against you or can no longer observe or sense you for at least 1 round.
