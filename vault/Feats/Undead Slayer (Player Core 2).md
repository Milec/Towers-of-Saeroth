# Undead Slayer  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

Your knowledge of your own not-quite-living anatomy, combined with your experience in combat, helps you take down undead foes with ease. Against [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures, you gain a circumstance bonus to damage with weapon and unarmed Strikes equal to the number of weapon damage dice for the Strike. This bonus increases to double the number of weapon damage dice if your target has the [[Traits/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]] trait.
