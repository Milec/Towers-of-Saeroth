# Consecrated Aura  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Hallowed Necromancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hallowed Necromancer Dedication]]

---

Your body radiates positive energy, making your very presence disorienting to [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and encouraging them to move away from you. The effect plays upon the instincts flowing through their bodies from negative energy and can thus affect even [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] creatures. You gain an aura of positive energy in a 20-foot emanation. Each undead creature that ends its turn in your aura must succeed at a Will save against your spell DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Special** Your aura gains the trait of the magical tradition for the spells you used to qualify for Hallowed Necromancer Dedication.
