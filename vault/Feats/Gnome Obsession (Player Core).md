# Gnome Obsession  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 52

---

You might have a flighty nature, but when a topic captures your attention, you dive into it headfirst. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] feat and the [[Feats/Assurance]] feat for the chosen [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]]. As gnome obsessions can come and go quickly, you can retrain Gnome Obsession to a different Lore subcategory in 1 day of downtime.
