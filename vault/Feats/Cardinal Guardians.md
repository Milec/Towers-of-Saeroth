# Cardinal Guardians  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 26

---

You focus your [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] magic to allow your apparitions to work in concert using the magic from one to weaken an enemy against the power of the next. Whenever you succeed at a spell attack with an apparition spell or vessel spell, or when a creature fails its save against such a spell, you gain a +2 status bonus to your spell attack modifier against that creature with spells granted by any of your other apparitions, and it takes a –2 status penalty to its saves against such spells. These benefits last until the end of your next turn.
