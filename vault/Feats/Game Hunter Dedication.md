# Game Hunter Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Game Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

---

You are skilled at tracking big game animals and similar creatures. You gain the [[Actions/Hunt Prey]] action, but you can designate only [[Traits/Animal|animals]], [[Traits/Beast|beasts]], and [[Traits/Dragon|dragons]] as prey. In addition to the other benefits of Hunt Prey, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks against your hunted prey.

 When you succeed at a Strike against your hunted prey while it's [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], it must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC. On a failure, the prey's Speeds are each reduced by 10 feet for 1 round; on a critical failure, the duration of this effect is 1 minute. The prey is then temporarily immune to this effect for 10 minutes.

 If you already have Hunt Prey, you become an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. You apply the Stealth bonus and the Speed reduction only when your prey is an animal, beast, or dragon.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the game hunter archetype.
