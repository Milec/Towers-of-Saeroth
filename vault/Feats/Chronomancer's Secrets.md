# Chronomancer's Secrets  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Time Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Time Mage Dedication]]

---

The secrets of time peel back, revealing deeper truths. You gain either the [[Spells/Stasis|_stasis_]] domain spell or the [[Spells/Path of Least Resistance|_path of least resistance_]] focus spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your pool by 1.

**Special** You can take this feat a second time, gaining the focus spell that you didn't gain the first time. Increase the number of Focus Points in your pool by 1.
