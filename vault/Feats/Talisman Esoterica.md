# Talisman Esoterica  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Esoterica]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 43

---

You know how to assemble the supernatural objects in your esoterica into a number of temporary [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]]. Each day during your daily preparations, you can make two talismans with an item level no higher than half your level. You must know each talisman's formula. A talisman created this way is a temporary item and loses its magic the next time you make your daily preparations.

You know the formulas for all common talismans in the _Core Rulebook_ of your level or lower. You remember your talisman formulas through oral tradition or mnemonics, so you don't need a [[Equipment/Formula Book (Blank)|formula book]] for them.
