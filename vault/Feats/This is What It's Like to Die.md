# This is What It's Like to Die  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 321

---

It’s one thing to remember your previous lives. It’s quite another to remember how you died in each of those lives. You can recall dozens, if not more, of excruciating, humiliating, and devastating ends you’ve suffered over the eons, and you can inflict upon your enemies a vision of these pains. You gain [[Spells/Phantasmagoria|_phantasmagoria_]] as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that you can cast once per day. When you gain this feat, choose any two of the following damage types: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], bludgeoning, [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], piercing, [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], slashing, or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]. These damage types caused your deaths more often than not in your previous lives, and whenever a creature takes damage from a _phantasmagoria_ spell you cast, you can swap the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage that spell normally causes for one of the two damage types you chose.
