# Feast Planner  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 101

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wandering Chef]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wandering Chef Dedication]]

---

You learn the [[Rituals/Fortifying Brew|fortifying brew]] ritual, but you instead prepare dishes for a lavish meal that have the same effect as the drinks from the normal version of the ritual. You perform both aspects of the ritual yourself, acting as both primary and a secondary caster. You must still attempt the secondary check normally performed by a secondary caster. In addition, you can use [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or Cooking [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] for both the primary and secondary checks. The ritual gains the [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] trait as nature spirits attend to you and your guests.
