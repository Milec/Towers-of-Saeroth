# Interweave Dispel single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 201

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] in your spell repertoire

---

You weave dispelling energy into a spell, sending both effects at a foe. If your next action is to cast a single-target spell against a creature, and you either hit the foe with the spell attack roll or the foe fails its saving throw, you can cast [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] on the foe as a free action, expending a spell slot as normal and targeting one spell effect affecting the foe.
