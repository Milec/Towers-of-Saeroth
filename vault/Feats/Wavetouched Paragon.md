# Wavetouched Paragon  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 13

**Prerequisites**
Bonuwat ethnicity

---

You have been blessed by the sea, granting you the ability to swim like a fish. You gain a 15-foot swim Speed.

**Special** If you have the [[Backgrounds/Bonuwat Wavetouched]] background, you can take this feat at 1st level instead of 5th.
