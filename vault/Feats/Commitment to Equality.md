# Commitment to Equality two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 50

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eagle Knight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eagle Knight Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]

---

You help an ally shake off any impediment that would give an enemy an unfair advantage. You rally a creature within 30 feet in an attempt to reduce its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition. If a creature has multiple conditions from this list, choose one. When you’re a master in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], add [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] to the list of conditions. When you’re legendary in Diplomacy, add [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] to the list of conditions; if the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can’t use Commitment to Equality to reduce it.

Attempt a Diplomacy check against the saving throw DC of the effect that caused the condition; if there was no saving throw DC, use the hard DC for the level of the creature, hazard, or item that caused the effect. You can’t treat a condition that came from an artifact or effect above 20th level unless you’re legendary in Diplomacy; even if you can, the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC increases by 10. You can’t treat a condition that is part of a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] or [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or is a natural state of the target. Once you attempt to treat a target’s condition, that target is immune to further attempts for 1 hour, regardless of the result.

**Critical Success** Reduce the condition’s value by 2.  

**Success** Reduce the condition’s value by 1.  

**Critical Failure** Increase the condition’s value by 1.
