Your church ensures that you are cared for. You can use [[Rules/Religion (GM Core)|Religion]] to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]], [[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]], [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], or make a [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] as long as you are in a town or city with a church dedicated to your deity. If the target of the check is also a worshipper of your deity or is your hunted prey, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check.
