**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

---

Long hours in darkened observatories have sharpened your senses. If you don't have [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]] or [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], you gain low-light vision, or if you already have low-light vision, you gain darkvision.

**Special** You can take this feat a second time, upgrading your low-light vision to darkvision.
