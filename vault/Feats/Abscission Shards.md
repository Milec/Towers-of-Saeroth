# Abscission Shards single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clawdancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your last action was a successful frenzied claw
or spinning talon Strike.

---

You twist your claw, breaking off a piece in your target. The shard deals 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. This increases to 2d6 if your unarmed attacks deal three weapon damage dice, such as under a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_greater striking_]] rune, and 3d6 if they deal four weapon damage dice.
