# Eyes of the City  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 230

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]]

---

You can track down targets with the help of locals. You can use Diplomacy or Society, whichever you're trained in, to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] creatures in settlements. You chat with locals to help follow the trail of creatures you Track. At the GM's discretion, there might not be enough people to speak with to keep following the trail.
