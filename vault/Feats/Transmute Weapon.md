# Transmute Weapon  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 86

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Warshard Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Warshard Warrior Dedication]]

---

When you choose a warshard weapon during daily preparations, you can transform the material it’s composed of so that the weapon is made of a precious material (like [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]], [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]], or [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]]). The material and quality of the material you select must be of a level equal to or less than your character level. You can transmute the weapon into a standard-grade item of that material only if the weapon is at least level 9. You can transmute the weapon into high-grade item of that material only if the weapon is at least level 15.
