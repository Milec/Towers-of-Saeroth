# Golden Dragon's Bounty single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Familiar Sage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Familiar Sage Dedication]]

---

Your familiar alters the material of a spell into that of a precious metal. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that deals bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, that damage is your choice of [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]], [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]], or steel for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, vulnerabilities, and the like. At 14th level, [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] is added to the list of metal choices. The spell gains the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait.
