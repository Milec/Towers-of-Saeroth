# Unbreaking Castle two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 84

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]] or a weapon with the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait.

---

You’re the bulwark that doesn’t break. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]], and [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]] or position the required weapon to parry; for the next minute, whenever you Raise a Shield or position a weapon to parry (including when you first use this ability), all allies you can see gain the shield or weapon’s circumstance bonus to AC. When your shield is raised or your weapon is positioned to parry while this effect is active, enemies treat all squares within your reach as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. When using the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction, after the block is complete, you can attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Bash (Player Core)|shield bash]] or an attached weapon on the required shield (such as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|shield boss]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]]) against an adjacent enemy at mythic proficiency.

The first time each round an enemy misses you with a melee Strike while your weapon is positioned to parry, you can Strike them with that weapon at mythic proficiency as a reaction.
