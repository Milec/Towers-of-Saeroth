# Vital Compound Invocation single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Runesmith]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 50

---

You can invoke runes from traditions that manipulate vital energy to restore flesh. You [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke two Runes]]—one must be a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] rune, and one must be a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] rune. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, one creature that’s within 30 feet of both invoked runes regains Hit Points equal to 5 + double your level.
