# Wayang Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 73

---

You learned to fight with weapons that help you keep your distance or make the most of striking from shadows. You gain access to and familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun (Player Core)|blowgun]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Fighting Fan (Tian Xia Character Guide)|fighting fan]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kris|kris]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspear]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Machete (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|machete]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sai (Tian Xia Character Guide)|sai]], and trident. You have familiarity with these weapons—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect.
