# Spirit's Absolution single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 23

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Spirit Dwellings and Remnants|Exorcist]]

**Cost**
1 spirit wisp or spirit remnant from your  spirit dwelling

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Exorcist Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are holding your _spirit dwelling_.

---

You purify a spirit by absolving it of its sins and regrets. This cathartic experience restores your Hit Points or those of an adjacent ally. If you expend a spirit wisp, the target recovers 1d4 Hit Points per level you have. If you expend a spirit remnant, the target recovers 1d6 Hit Points per level of the [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]] or [[Traits/Haunt|haunt]] from which you gained the remnant.
