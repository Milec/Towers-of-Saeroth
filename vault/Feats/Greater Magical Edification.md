You've continued to expand your compendium of tools to compile knowledge. You can cast [[Spells/Comprehend Language|_comprehend language_]], [[Spells/Mindlink|_mindlink_]], and [[Spells/Secret Page|_secret page_]] as innate occult spells, each once per day. When you cast _secret page_, it is automatically heightened to the same spell level as your cantrips from Magical Edification.
