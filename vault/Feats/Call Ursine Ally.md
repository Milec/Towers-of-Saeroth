# Call Ursine Ally  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Summon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ursine Avenger Hood]]

---

You can cast a 3rd-level [[Spells/Summon Animal (Player Core)|_summon animal_]] as an innate spell, but only to summon a [[Bestiary/Black Bear|black bear]]. At 10th level, the _summon animal_ spell is heightened to 4th level, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Grizzly Bear (Monster Core)|grizzly bear]]. At 12th level, your _summon animal_ innate spell is heightened to 5th level, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Polar Bear|polar bear]]. At 14th level, it’s heightened to 6th level, and you can summon a [[Bestiary/Cave Bear (Monster Core)|cave bear]].
