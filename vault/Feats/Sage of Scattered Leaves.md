# Sage of Scattered Leaves  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 79

---

The epics of your people’s tragic yet proud history blaze like burning leaves in the forests of ancestral memory. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] and [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Yaksha Lore.
