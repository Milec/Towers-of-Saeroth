**Requirements**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by the target creature.

---

You can startle foes who are unaware of your presence. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against your target. That creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against this Strike, as normal. If your Strike hits, the target remains off-guard for the rest of your turn and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical hit).
