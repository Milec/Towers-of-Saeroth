# Investigator's Stratagem  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 178

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Investigator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Investigator Dedication]]

---

You play out battles in your head, allowing you to strike when the time is right. You gain the [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]] action; however, when you substitute its result for your attack roll, you can’t use your Intelligence modifier in place of your Strength or Dexterity modifier. You also can’t use your Intelligence modifier on other rolls from abilities that expand Devise a Stratagem, such as the [[Feats/Athletic Strategist (Player Core 2)|Athletic Strategist]] feat.
