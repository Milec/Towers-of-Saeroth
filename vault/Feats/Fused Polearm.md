# Fused Polearm  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 241

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Runelord Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your arcane bonded item is a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearm]].

---

During your daily preparations, you can magically fuse your arcane bonded weapon and a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|magical staff]] together into one item, with the staff making up the haft of the weapon. You prepare the staff at the same time you do this, and you can do this only with a staff you're able to prepare. This fusion lasts until the next time you make your daily preparations. The staff and the weapon share their fundamental runes, using whichever weapon potency and whichever striking rune is higher level. They don’t share any other runes or specific abilities; use the polearm's.

While the two are fused, the weapon’s haft takes on aesthetic aspects of the staff, and it can't change forms, such as with the shifting rune.
