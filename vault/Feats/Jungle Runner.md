# Jungle Runner  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 55

---

You run through the jungle as easily as you would through an open field. You ignore difficult terrain from underbrush within forests and jungles. You can always use the [[Actions/Take Cover]] action when you're within forest or jungle terrain to gain [[Rules/Cover|cover]], even if you aren't adjacent to an obstacle you can Take Cover behind.
