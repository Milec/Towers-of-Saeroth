# Directional Bombs  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 66

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Demolitionist (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Demolitionist]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Demolitionist]] (Level 8)

---

You can lob bombs with great force and a precise trajectory to angle the splash in a cone that sprays in a single direction, potentially allowing you to avoid allies and splash deeper into enemy lines. When throwing an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical bomb]] with the [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] trait, instead of splashing all squares adjacent to the target, you can create a splash in a 15-foot cone. You choose the cone's direction, but its first square must be a square in the target's space.

**Special** If you have the [[Feats/Expanded Splash]] feat or another ability that increases the radius of splash damage, you can have the splash damage be a 20-foot cone.
