# Living Hair  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 102

---

You can instantly grow or shrink your hair, eyebrows, beard, or mustache by up to several feet and manipulate your hair for use as a weapon, though your control isn't fine enough for more dexterous tasks. You gain a hair unarmed attack that deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage; is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Trip|trip]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
