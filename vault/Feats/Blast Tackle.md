# Blast Tackle two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 117

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Ways/Way of the Vanguard|way of the vanguard]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a two-handed crossbow or a firearm that has the [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] or [[Traits/Scatter (NPC Core)|scatter]] trait.

---

You throw yourself at an enemy, bracing your weapon against it and pulling it close before releasing a destructive shot. Attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]]; if you’re wielding your weapon in both hands, you [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Release]] one hand to do so. If your Grapple succeeds, you can immediately [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] the target of the Grapple with the required weapon, even if it’s a two-handed weapon you’re holding in one hand. This Strike deals an additional 3d6 precision damage.

As long the creature remains [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], you can use just one hand to [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|reload]] the weapon and Strike that creature with it.
