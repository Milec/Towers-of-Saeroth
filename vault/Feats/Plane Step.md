# Plane Step two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Jotunborn]], [[Traits/Move]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 14

---

You temporarily move just beyond the threshold of the current plane, granting you [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] until the end of your next turn. You remain clearly visible while stepping outside of a plane in this way and you can’t use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]].
