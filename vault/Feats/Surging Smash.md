# Surging Smash two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Nexus Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are overflowing.

---

Lashing forward, your weapon becomes an extension of your nexus, washing your enemies in a torrent of magical fury. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. Regardless of the result, you create a 30- foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of magical energy dealing 3d6 force damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save against your nexus DC. You choose the cone’s direction, but its first square must be a square in the target of your strike’s space. A creature that critically fails the save is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. The force damage increases by 1d6 at 8th level and every 2 levels thereafter. You are no longer overflowing.
