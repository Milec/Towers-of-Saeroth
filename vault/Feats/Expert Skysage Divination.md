# Expert Skysage Divination  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Basic Skysage Divination]]

---

Your studies of the stars have taught you about the mysteries of the cosmos. You learn a 4th-level spell from any spell list. At 12th level, you learn a 5th-level spell, and at 14th level, you learn a 6th-level spell. Each of these spells must come from the [[Traits/Divination|divination]] school. You become an expert with spell attack rolls and spell DCs for occult spells.
