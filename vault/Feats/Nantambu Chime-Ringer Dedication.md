# Nantambu Chime-Ringer Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Nantambu Chime-Ringer]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You've studied the basics of harmony and resonance in magic, exemplified by the ringing of chimes. You gain a single [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] cantrip of your choice, heightened to a spell level equal to half your level, rounded up. If you weren't already, you become trained in that tradition's spell DCs and spell attack rolls, with Intelligence as your spellcasting ability if you choose arcane or Charisma as your spellcasting ability if you choose occult. Regardless of your choice, you also either become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or an expert in one of these skills if you are already trained in both.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the Nantambu Chime-Ringer archetype.
