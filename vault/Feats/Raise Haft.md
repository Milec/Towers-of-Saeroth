# Raise Haft  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 42

---

You know how to use the haft of larger weapons to block your enemies' attacks. Two-handed weapons you wield gain the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait. If the weapon already has the parry trait, you increase the circumstance bonus to AC it provides to +2.
