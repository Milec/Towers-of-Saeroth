# Tricksterbane Oath  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 92

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

---

You’ve sworn an oath to ferret out and destroy malevolent shapechangers who pose as mortals with evil intent. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to detect a shapechanged creature’s disguise, and a +2 circumstance bonus to attempts to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about shapechangers. Whenever you use Overwhelming Combination against a shapechanged creature, you attempt to counteract one polymorph effect on that creature. The counteract rank is half your level rounded up, and the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check modifier is your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus with the weapon used to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] as part of your Overwhelming Combination. You gain the following edict.

**Edict** You must reveal and slay evil or predatory shapechangers you discover or encounter, as long as you have a reasonable chance of success.
