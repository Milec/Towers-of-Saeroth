# Follow-Up Assault single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage]]

---

Even in the face of failure you press the attack, determined to succeed. Make a Strike with the same weapon, adding the effects of the [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]] and [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]] weapon traits.
