# Mythic Strike single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 84

---

You infuse your weapon or unarmed attack with mythic power before lashing out at an enemy with devastating force. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] and then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] a creature with a weapon you’re wielding or an unarmed attack you have available. This Strike is made at mythic proficiency, and the weapon or unarmed attack counts as a mythic weapon for the purposes of overcoming mythic resistance or mythic immunity.
