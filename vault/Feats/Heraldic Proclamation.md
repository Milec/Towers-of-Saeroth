# Heraldic Proclamation single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 281

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

You proclaim your spell with the might of your god behind you. If the next action you use is to [[Rules/Cast a Spell|Cast a non-cantrip Spell]], then all allies within 30 feet gain Hit Points equal to double the rank of the spell, and all enemies within 30 feet take [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage equal to double the rank of the spell (basic Will save against your spell DC).
