# Ganzi Gaze  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ganzi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 96

---

You have unusual sensory abilities passed down from your chaotic forebears. You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If you already have low-light vision, you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] instead.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time to gain darkvision in addition to low-light vision.
