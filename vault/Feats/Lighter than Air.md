# Lighter than Air  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 142

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Water Walker]]

Your mysterious steps become even lighter, transcending the mortal world altogether. When you have the [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] condition, you gain the effects of [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]]. If you are cursebound 3 or greater, you gain a +10-foot status bonus to your fly Speed.
