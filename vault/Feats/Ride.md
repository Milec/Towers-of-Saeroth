# Ride  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 266

---

When you [[Actions/Command an Animal]] you’re mounted on to take a move action (such as Stride), you automatically succeed instead of needing to attempt a check. Any animal you’re mounted on acts on your turn, like a minion. If you Mount an animal in the middle of an encounter, it skips its next turn and then acts on your next turn.
