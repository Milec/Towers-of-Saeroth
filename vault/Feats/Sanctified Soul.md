# Sanctified Soul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exemplar]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 35

---

You’ve drawn a line in the sand in the cosmic struggle between good and evil and chosen a side. You gain either the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait or the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait. All your exemplar abilities that deal [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage gain the [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]] trait, allowing you to apply your holy or unholy trait to them to better affect your chosen enemies
