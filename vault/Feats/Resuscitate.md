# Resuscitate three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 184

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Medic]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Medic Dedication]]; Legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]

**Requirements**
You are holding [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]] or are wearing them and have a hand free. Also, the target's body is mostly intact.

---

You can use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] to resuscitate the dead. Attempt a DC 40 Medicine check to revive a dead creature that has been dead for no more than 3 rounds. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]], except it still has the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition it had before [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]], increased by 1 (or wounded 1 if it wasn't wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is temporarily immune to Resuscitate for 1 day.
