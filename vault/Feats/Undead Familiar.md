# Undead Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 32

---

You gain the ability to raise an undead familiar who will serve you as your loyal and sometimes expendable partner as you explore your necromantic magic. You gain a [familiar](Familiars.aspx). It has the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait and is immune to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]], [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]. You can choose only one familiar ability per day for this familiar instead of two, and you can’t choose an ability that changes the creature’s trait (such as [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct|construct]] or [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Dragon|dragon]]). Additionally, this familiar can be targeted by spells and abilities that target your thralls. If you destroy your familiar, you can raise them back to undeath during your next daily preparations.
