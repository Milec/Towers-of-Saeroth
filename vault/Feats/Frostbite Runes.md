# Frostbite Runes  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 11

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbound Initiate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pactbound Dedication]]

---

Thick tattoos, each one a rune fragment from the saumen kar's long-lost language spelling out their pact with their equally lost deity, cover your body and stretch across your limbs, torso, and face. You gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Evil|evil]] and [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] spells and effects. You can sense the presence of evil [[Traits/Undead|undead]] as a pervasive taste of oily corruption in the air; this is a [[Rules/Vague Senses|vague]] sense that allows you to know when such a creature is within 60 feet of you, though you can't precisely pinpoint their location with this sense. As normal for a vague sense, it can be fooled, but undead might not realize they need to take precautions against it. This sense also extends to living creatures who have the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]] ability, like [[Rules/Sidebars/Dhampir Settlements|dhampir]].

 You deal an additional 1 cold damage with all Strikes made with an ice-crafted weapon. If you have the [[Feats/Crown of the Saumen Kar]] feat, you deal an additional 1 cold damage with your unarmed horns Strikes.
