# Distant Wandering single action  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 103

---

Your body falls unconscious and your spirit projects out of it. While in spirit form, you can’t consciously move your body, and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] and inaudible, though a creature still might notice the signs of your presence and passing, just like for an invisible creature affected by [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]], or a [[Spells/Scouting Eye|_scouting eye_]]. You can freely move about, though you can’t touch or move anything, cast spells, attack, or otherwise affect anything around you. Despite your ghostly form, you are not [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] and can’t pass through barriers you couldn’t in your body. Most effects can’t harm your spirit form, though some spells, like [[Spells/Spirit Blast (Player Core)|_spirit blast_]] and [[Spells/Spirit Song (Player Core 2)|_spirit song_]], explicitly damage a creature’s spirit.

You can return to your body as a free action. If you are in spirit form at the beginning of your turn, you must attempt another backlash check for your deviation, returning to your body if you fail.

**Awakening** Your spirit moves at the speed of thought. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] and a +20-foot status bonus to your Speed while in spirit form.

**Awakening** Time seems to pass more slowly while you are projecting your spirit. When you enter spirit form, you can [[Rules/Recall Knowledge (GM Core)|Recall Knowledge]] or [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]]. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] while in spirit form and can use the extra action only to Recall Knowledge or Seek.
