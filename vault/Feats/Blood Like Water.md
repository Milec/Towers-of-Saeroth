# Blood Like Water  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Samsaran (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Samsaran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 56

---

The watery fluid in your veins doesn’t flow in the same way as traditional blood. Your DC on flat checks to end [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] is reduced from 15 to 10, or from 10 to 5 after receiving especially appropriate assistance.
