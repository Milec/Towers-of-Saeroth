# Pelagic Aptitude  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 14

---

You've learned special tricks to survive underwater. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. When in an aquatic environment, if you roll a critical failure on a Survival skill check to [[Actions/Sense Direction]] or [[Actions/Subsist]], you get a failure instead.
