# Cut them Down, Burn them Out single action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 111

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]; master proficiency in a weapon

---

Inspired by the sixth line of the Crimson Oath, when you oppose the enemies of Lastwall, you aim to excise the rot from the land itself. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature using a weapon with which you have master proficiency. On a success, in addition to the attack’s other effects, the creature takes a –2 status penalty to the DC of any [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curses]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] it can inflict for 1 round. On a critical success, the undead creature takes the –2 status penalty for 1 round, then a –1 status penalty for an additional round.
