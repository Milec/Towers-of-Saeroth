# True Shapeshifter two actions  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 139

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dragon Shape]]; [[Feats/Wild Shape]]

---

You transcend the limitations of form. While under the effects of [[Spells/Wild Shape|_wild shape_]], you can change into any other form on your _wild shape_ list; if the durations of the forms would vary, use the shorter of the two durations.

 Once per day, you can transform into a kaiju, with the effects of [[Spells/Nature Incarnate|_nature incarnate_]]; if you have [[Feats/Plant Shape]], you can instead transform into a green man.
