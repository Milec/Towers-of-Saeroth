# Unstoppable Force  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 129

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Immovable Object]]

---

While you are in the stance provided by Immovable Object, you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain. Once per 10 minutes, if you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] your Immovable Object stance, you can Stride as a free action. At the end of that Stride, your final footfall causes an explosion of energy. Each creature within a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 14d6 force damage (basic Reflex save against your class DC). Creatures that fail the save are also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
