Your animal companion grows up, becoming a [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|mature animal companion]]. Increase its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] to expert instead of one of the three skill increases for mature. It gains the Mesmerizing Performance advanced maneuver, rather than the normal advanced maneuver for its type.
