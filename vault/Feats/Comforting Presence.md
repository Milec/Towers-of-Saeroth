# Comforting Presence ⤾  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 70

**Prerequisites**
Master in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Trigger**
An adjacent ally becomes fightened or stupefied

---

You use your occult connection to your allies to take on the fear and confusion they feel. Focus on your own calmness and pull in your nearby allies' emotions. Reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]]condition values of adjacent allies by 1, and increase your frightened and stupefied condition values by the same amount.
