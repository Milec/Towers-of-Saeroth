# Warped Reflection single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Reflection]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 120

**Frequency**
once per minute

---

Due to your origins, your form is more flexible than most. You shift your appearance to resemble a creature within 30 feet but with an unsettling, horrifying twist. Though this transformation is momentary, witnessing it gives the target deep existential dread. It must succeed at a Will save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure) and be stupefied 1 as long as the frightened condition lasts. Once you've used Warped Reflection against a creature, it's temporarily immune for 24 hours.
