# Snakebird's Shadow two actions  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 81

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Waterfowl Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Waterfowl Stance and are wielding a monk weapon from the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]] group.

---

Ganhil developed this technique through observing the sharp-beaked darters fishing Prada Hanam’s waters, seeking to match their speed and suppleness with the elemental flash of his blade. Make a melee Strike with the required weapon against each enemy in a 15-foot line as razor-sharp shadows of water surge from your blade. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you do not increase your penalty until you have made all your attacks.
