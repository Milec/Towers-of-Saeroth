# Beguiling Aura single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Captivator Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Captivator Dedication]]

---

You channel your manipulative magic to subtly give off mental signals that either drive enemies away or draw them closer. You gain a 30-foot aura that lasts for 1 minute. When an enemy within your aura fails a saving throw against one of your [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate captivator spells]], you can also have them [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] in a direction of your choice, though they must remain within your aura. You can’t choose to have a creature Step into [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (GM Core)|hazardous terrain]], off a ledge, or the like.
