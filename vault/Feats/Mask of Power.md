# Mask of Power  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 50

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Orc Warmask]]

---

Your _warmask_ sharpens your connection to the source of your mask's power. During your daily preparations, choose [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]], [[Spells/Phantom Pain|_phantom pain_]], or [[Spells/True Strike|_true strike_]]. Until your next daily preparations, you can cast this spell as a 1st-level innate spell once per day; the spell's tradition is determined by the tradition tied to your _warmask_. You must be wearing your _warmask_ to Cast the Spell.
