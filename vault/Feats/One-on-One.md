# One-on-One single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist]]

**Frequency**
once per round

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication (Battlecry!)|Aldori Duelist Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]

---

Your true skill can be seen in a one-on-one fight where you have room to dance around your opponent. You attempt to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] and then Strike an adjacent creature with an [[Equipment/Weapons/Aldori Dueling Sword (Battlecry!)|Aldori dueling sword]]. The Feint attempt must be against the same target as your Strike.
