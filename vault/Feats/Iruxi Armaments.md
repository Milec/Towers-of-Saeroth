# Iruxi Armaments  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

---

Your weapons are those you were born with. Choose one of the following options:

- **Claws** Your claw attack deals 1d6 slashing damage instead of 1d4 and gains the [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]] trait.
- **Fangs** You gain a fangs [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d8 piercing damage and is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group.
- **Tail** You gain a tail unarmed attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and has the [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]] trait.

At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of the unarmed attacks you have gained or improved with this feat, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times, choosing a different unarmed attack option each time.
