# Free from Bonds  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 281

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Flash of Omnipresence]]

---

As a mortal herald, you cannot easily be captured, nor can your allies. If you are [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] when you use Flash of Omnipresence, the creature or effect that is imparting that condition on you takes [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage equal to your level. If you teleport adjacent to a grabbed or restrained ally using Flash of Omnipresence, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] check against the hard DC for the level of the creature or effect that is imparting that condition. On a success, your ally no longer has that condition.
