# Veil May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 22

---

Born of a blood hag, a fiery blood drinker hidden behind a false skin, you have one blood-red eye and the ability to slough off your appearance in an instant. You gain the [[Feats/Lie to Me]] skill feat, and you can shed a disguise quickly by peeling off the outer layer of your skin. Changing from a disguise used to [[Actions/Impersonate]] back to your true appearance is a 3-action activity for you; if you're an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], it's a 2-action activity, and if you're legendary in Deception, it takes only a single action. This doesn't allow you to don or remove armor or other equipment any faster, but your physical appearance otherwise returns to its normal state.
