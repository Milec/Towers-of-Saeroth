# Nimble Crawl  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 259

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]

---

You can [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]] incredibly swiftly—up to half your Speed, rather than 5 feet. If you're a master in Acrobatics, you can Crawl at full Speed, and if you're legendary, you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
