# Infiltration Automation  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

---

You have quiet, well-oiled joints, and you move around as silently as a shadow, though you sometimes use a magical boost. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks against hearing-based [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks. You can cast [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] once per day on yourself as a 2nd-rank [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]].

**Enhancement** You can cast _silence_ twice per day on yourself, and you can choose whether to cast it as a 2nd- or 4th-rank spell.
