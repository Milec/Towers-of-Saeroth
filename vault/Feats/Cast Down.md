# Cast Down single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 123

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|harmful font or healing font]]

---

The sheer force of your faith can bring a foe crashing down. If the next action you use is to cast [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] to damage one creature, the target is knocked prone if it takes any damage from the spell. If the target critically fails its save against the spell, it also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 minute.
