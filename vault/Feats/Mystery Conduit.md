# Mystery Conduit  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 81

---

The power of your mystery enables you to cast more than just revelation spells. When you cast a spell of 5th level or lower that has no duration, instead of spending a spell slot, you can advance the effects of your oracular curse in the same manner as when you cast a revelation spell. A spell cast in this way gains the [[Traits/Cursebound|cursebound]] trait.
