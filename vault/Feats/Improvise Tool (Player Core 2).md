# Improvise Tool  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 231

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]

---

You can jury-rig solutions when you don't have the proper tools on hand. You can attempt to [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] damaged items without a [[Equipment/Repair Toolkit|repair toolkit]].  
If you have the raw materials, you can [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] a [[Equipment/Caltrops (Player Core)|basic caltrop set,]] [[Equipment/Candle (Player Core)|candle]], [[Equipment/Compass (Player Core)|compass]], [[Equipment/Crowbar (Player Core)|crowbar]], [[Equipment/Fishing Tackle (Player Core)|fishing tackle]], [[Equipment/Flint and Steel (Player Core)|flint and steel]],[[Equipment/Hammer (Player Core)|hammer]], [[Equipment/Ladder (10 ft.) (Player Core)|ladder]], [[Equipment/Piton (Player Core)|piton]], [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]], [[Equipment/Ten-Foot Pole (Player Core)|10-foot pole]], [[Equipment/Thieves' Toolkit|replacement thieves' picks]], [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|long]] or [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|short tool]], or [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]] as if you had their formulas.
