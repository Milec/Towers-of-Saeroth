# Shield Spell Reinforcement free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/War Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/War Mage Dedication]]; you can cast the [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]] cantrip

**Requirements**
You are wielding a shield.

---

You can empower your defensive magic by channeling it through a physical shield. If your next action is to cast the _shield_ cantrip, you use your shield as a locus to cast the spell, raising a magical barrier in the form of a large force projection of your worn shield. You can choose for an adjacent ally to gain the benefits of the _shield_ spell instead of yourself. If the ally would take damage from a physical attack while protected by your shield cantrip, you can use your reaction to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Shield Block (Monster Core)|Shield Block]] with the spell on their behalf.
