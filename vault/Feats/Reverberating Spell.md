# Reverberating Spell single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 59

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Elementalist (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elementalist Dedication (Rage of Elements)|Elementalist Dedication]]; metal is in your elemental philosophy

---

Your spell disorients your targets with a metallic clangor. If the next action you use this turn is to Cast a non-cantrip Spell that deals damage in an area (such as a burst, line, or cone), the spell deals an additional 1d8 sonic damage and all creatures who fail their save against the spell are [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round. Targets who critically fail their saves against this spell are instead deafened for 1 minute. The spell gains the [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] trait.
