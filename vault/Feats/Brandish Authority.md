# Brandish Authority two actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 112

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archfiend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archfiend Dedication]]

---

With a terrifying pronouncement, you manifest a visible sign of your authority and fiendish power, such as a crown of flame upon your brow, a scepter forged of solidified souls, or a throne comprised of a dozen dragon skulls. This object appears in your space or in your hand, as appropriate, and remains for 1 minute. When your sign of power appears, attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet who can see or hear you. These Demoralize attempts don’t take any penalty for not sharing a language. As long as your sign of power remains manifested, your enemies can’t reduce the value of their [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition below 1.

You can spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] as part of performing this action; if you do, frightened creatures grovel before you, in awe of your terrible power. At the beginning of their turn, a creature that has the frightened condition from Brandish Authority must attempt a Will saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. On failure, they must Drop Prone. On a critical failure, they must [[Actions/Drop Prone (Player Core)|Drop Prone]] and can’t Stand this turn.
