# Ocean's Bite  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 36

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Abyssal Merfolk]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Carcharodon Merfolk]] heritage

---

Your jaws open wider than a humanoid mouth should, showing off multiple rows of serrated shark teeth or a viperfish-like nest of needles. You gain a jaws [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d8 piercing damage. Your jaws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group.
