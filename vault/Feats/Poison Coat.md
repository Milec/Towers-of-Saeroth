# Poison Coat ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 186

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Poisoner Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature adjacent to you hits you with a melee unarmed Strike.

**Requirements**
You have prepared your clothing to poison attackers (see below).

---

In nature, certain animals are covered in venomous spines, such that even touching them is deadly. To use this reaction, you must have brushed poison onto your clothing or woven sharp poisoned needles into the garment's fabric. This takes 10 minutes and uses one dose of [[Traits/Contact|contact]] or [[Traits/Injury|injury]] poison. You can have only one such poison suffused into your clothing at a time.

 When you use this reaction, the triggering creature is exposed to the suffused poison.
