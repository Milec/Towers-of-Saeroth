# Quickened Casting free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]], [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 105, [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 141, [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 38

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Time Mage]] (Level 12)

**Frequency**
once per day

---

If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] or a spell that is at least 2 ranks lower than the highest-rank spell slot you have, reduce the number of actions to cast it by 1 (minimum 1 action).

**Special** This feat can only be used on a cantrip or spell that is at least 2 ranks lower than the highest-rank spell slot from the class or archetype matching the one you gained this feat from.
