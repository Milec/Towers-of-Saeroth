# Lion Blade Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lion Blade (Shining Kingdoms)|Lion Blade]]

**Prerequisites**
member of the Lion Blades; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

**Access**
You’re from Taldor.

---

Your Lion Blade spy training helps you conceal yourself and your identity. You become trained in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]; if you were already trained in that skill, you become an expert instead. You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] skill feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Espionage Lore]]. If you were already trained in Espionage Lore, you also become trained in a Lore skill of your choice.

You never take circumstance penalties for attempting to take a disguise of a different ancestry, age, and so on, as long as the disguised form is appropriate for your size.
