# Heal Mount  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Faithful Steed]]; [[Spells/Lay on Hands (Player Core 2)|_lay on hands_]]

---

Your devotion to your mount manifests as a surge of vital energy. When you cast [[Spells/Lay on Hands (Player Core 2)|_lay on hands_]] on your mount, instead of the normal amount, the spell restores 10 Hit Points, plus 10 for each heightened rank.
