# Grove-Harbored  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 48

---

You are accustomed to exploring thick woodlands and defending yourself against the attacks of magical plants. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Plant|plant]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Wood|wood]] effects, and if you roll a success on a save against a poison effect, you get a critical success instead.
