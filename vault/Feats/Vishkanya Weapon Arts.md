# Vishkanya Weapon Arts  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vishkanya]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 62

**Prerequisites**

You are graceful and efficient in the use of vishkanyan weapons. Whenever you critically hit using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun|blowgun]], [fighting fan](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=93), [[Equipment/Weapons/Kris|kris]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]], or [[Traits/Vishkanya|vishkanya]] weapon, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
