# Ocular Flesh single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 39

---

You create a fleshy eyeball that is the size of a marble. You can take a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, to look through any of your eyeballs until the start of your next turn. An eyeball can be manipulated like a marble, such as being rolled or thrown. One can also be affixed to a surface, for example a wall, with 1 minute’s work. An eyeball lasts for 24 hours, and you can maintain a total of three eyeballs at any given time. Creating a fourth destroys the oldest eyeball.

Eyeballs are very small but are easy to spot. A creature actively [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeking]] or [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]] automatically finds an eyeball. They’re also not very sturdy and can be destroyed with an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action.
