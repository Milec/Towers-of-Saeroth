# Supernatural Senses  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 78

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acute Scent (Player Core 2)|Acute Scent]] or [[Rules/Scent (Player Core)|scent]]

---

Your scent is preternaturally sharp, and you can always rely on your sense of smell to help guide you when your vision is compromised. When you target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] opponent while you are raging, you reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or to 9 for a hidden one.
