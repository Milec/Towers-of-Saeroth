# Invisible Hand  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 281

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

Sometimes your deity requires you to act in the shadows, accomplishing things without drawing attention to yourself— or them. Once per day, when you roll [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] for initiative at the start of a combat, you can choose to be [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] for 1 minute. If you spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] while beginning the [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]] exploration activity, you are invisible for as long as you continue to Avoid Notice.
