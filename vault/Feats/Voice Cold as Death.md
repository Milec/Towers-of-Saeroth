# Voice Cold as Death single action  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Requirements**
You are not [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].

---

Channeling the energy of the Crimson Oath into your voice, you pronounce a threat in a tone so icy it freezes the blood. Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize]]; you don't suffer a penalty if the target doesn't understand the language you're speaking, but this otherwise functions as normal. You can't use [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] or any other effect that would cause your Demoralize attempt to lose the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait as part of using this feat. If you critically succeed at your Intimidation check, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]], or stunned 2 if the target is [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. After using this feat, you are fatigued until the end of your next turn, and you can't use this feat again until the end of your next turn.
