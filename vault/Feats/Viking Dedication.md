# Viking Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 198

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Viking]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Vikings spend long periods of time at sea, only to leap from their boats at a moment's notice, charge through the surf, and take their enemies by storm. You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Sailing Lore]]. When moving through water using your land Speed, you ignore difficult terrain resulting from shallow water, such as in bogs or on beaches. In addition, if you roll a success for your [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Swim]], you get a critical success instead.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the viking archetype.
