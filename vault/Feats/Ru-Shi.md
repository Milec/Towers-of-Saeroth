# Ru-Shi  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 26

---

You were born to a jiang-shi, one of the so-called hopping vampires found most often in Tian-Xia. Your parent had a supernatural obsession with counting small objects, which manifests in you as a preternatural gift for linguistics and mathematics. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and gain the [[Feats/Eye for Numbers]] skill feat. If you would automatically become trained in Society (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
