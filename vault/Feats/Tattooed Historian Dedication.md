# Tattooed Historian Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Tattooed Historian]]

**Prerequisites**
Constitution +1; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Belkzen Lore]], Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Orc Lore]], Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Orc Pantheon Lore]], or (at the GM's discretion) a related [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]; if you are already trained in both skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain access to all uncommon [[Rules/Magical Tattoos|magical tattoos]] with the [[Traits/Orc (Player Core)|orc]] trait.

You gain a [[Equipment/Storied Skin|_storied skin_]] for free upon gaining this feat (or another magical tattoo of 2nd level or lower if you already have _storied skin_). You cannot have more than one _storied skin_ tattoo, but the frequency of its Living History ability increases by one use per minute for every three tattooed historian feats you have. For every two tattooed historian feats you have, you can [[Rules/Investments (GM Core)|invest]] one magical tattoo that does not count against the maximum number of items you can have invested at one time.
