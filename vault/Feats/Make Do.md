**Requirements**

---

You sometimes need to act in a pinch, even if you haven't had the time to repair your gear. For the next minute, you ignore the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] condition of the tool or weapon you're holding. You can't use Make Do again on that item for 24 hours.
