# Tremor two actions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 26

---

You unleash a small, localized tremor in a 10-foot burst within 30 feet. Each creature in the area takes 1d8 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. A creature that critically fails is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. Earth and stone in the area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] until the start of your next turn.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
