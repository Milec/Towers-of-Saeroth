# Shoki's Argument  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 30

---

You feel a connection to [[Bestiary/Shoki|shokis]], the rambling collectors of wayward souls, and might have found old texts written by one. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]. If you were already trained in Diplomacy (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can also cast [[Spells/Disrupt Undead|_disrupt undead_]] as a divine innate cantrip at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
