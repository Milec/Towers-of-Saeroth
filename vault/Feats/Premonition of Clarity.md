# Premonition of Clarity ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 123

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Seneschal]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]] (Level 16)

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
You fail a saving throw against a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect.

---

Your deity sends you a vision of faith to steel you against mental attacks. Reroll the triggering saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus. You must use the second result, even if it's worse.
