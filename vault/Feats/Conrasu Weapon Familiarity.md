# Conrasu Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conrasu]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 108

---

You have taken it upon yourself to learn the ways of combat to aid with your tasks, perhaps in a role as a corrector. You are trained with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspear]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]]. You also gain access to all uncommon [[Traits/Conrasu|conrasu]] weapons. For the purpose of determining your proficiency, martial conrasu weapons are simple weapons and advanced conrasu weapons are martial weapons.
