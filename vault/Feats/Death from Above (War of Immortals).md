# Death from Above two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are adjacent to a climbable surface at least 10 feet tall.

---

Seemingly to defy gravity, you use a nearby vertical surface to propel yourself upward and then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|strike]] downward like the driving rain. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed upward on the required surface; if you have a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]], you may use your full climb Speed instead. The surface does not need to span the full height of your Stride. You can then either make a ranged Strike at a target within your weapon’s first range increment and land in the space you began (or an adjacent space) without taking [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falling]] damage, or you can land in a space adjacent to a creature within 10 feet of the required surface without taking falling damage and make a melee Strike against that creature. Either Strike deals an extra two dice of weapon damage. In addition, the target dealt damage by your Strike must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
