**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]]

---

You've trained your mount to become a powerful force on the battlefield. The mount you gained through the [[Feats/Cavalier Dedication]] feat becomes a [[Rules/Mature Animal Companions|mature animal companion]], granting it additional capabilities. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action, your animal companion can still use 1 action on your turn to Stride or Strike.
