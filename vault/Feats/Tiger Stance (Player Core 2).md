# Tiger Stance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 119

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You are unarmored.

---

You enter the stance of a tiger and can make tiger claw attacks. These deal 1d8 slashing damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. On a critical success with your tiger claws, if you deal damage, the target takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

 As long as your Speed is at least 20 feet while in Tiger Stance, you can Step 10 feet.
