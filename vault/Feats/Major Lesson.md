# Major Lesson  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 103

---

Your patron grants you even greater secrets. Choose a [major, greater, or basic lesson](Lessons.aspx). You gain its associated hex, and your familiar learns the associated spell. Increase the number of Focus Points in your focus pool by 1.

**Special** You can select this feat a second time if you're 14th level or higher, and a third time if you're 18th level or higher. Choose a different lesson each time.
