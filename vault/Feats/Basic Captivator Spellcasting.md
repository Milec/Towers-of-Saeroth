# Basic Captivator Spellcasting  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 120

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Captivator Dedication]]

---

Your innate ability to fascinate others develops into full-fledged spellcasting. This spellcasting comes naturally and instinctively to you, rather than as a product of training. Choose a 1st-level occult spell from either the [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] or [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] school. You can Cast this Spell as an occult innate spell. At 6th level, you gain a 2nd-level spell, and at 8th level, you gain a 3rd-level spell. Each of these spells must be from either the enchantment or illusion school.
