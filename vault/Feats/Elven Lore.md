# Elven Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 40

---

You've studied in traditional elven arts, learning about arcane magic and the world around you. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Elven Lore]].
