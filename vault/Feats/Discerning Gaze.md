# Discerning Gaze  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gray Gardener]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vigilante Dedication]]; member of the Gray Gardeners

---

You have a sense for when people are lying. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on your secret Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive]].  
If you use [[Actions/Sense Motive]] on a creature you have currently condemned with [[Feats/Harsh Judgment]], your GM rolls the Perception check twice and takes the higher result (this is a fortune effect).
