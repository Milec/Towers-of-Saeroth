# Intimidating Strike two actions  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Battle Harbinger]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Blackjacket]] (Level 4)

Your blow not only wounds creatures but also shatters their confidence. Make a melee Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]], or frightened 2 on a critical hit.
