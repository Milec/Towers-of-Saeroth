# Persistent Creation  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Halcyon Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Halcyon Speaker Dedication]]

---

You can cast [[Spells/Creation|_creation_]] as an innate spell at will, choosing whether it is arcane or primal each time. The created object lasts until you spend 1 hour not within its immediate vicinity or until it is destroyed. Such objects are called persistent creations. If you cast _creation_ again, any persistent creation you previously created with the spell immediately disintegrates.
