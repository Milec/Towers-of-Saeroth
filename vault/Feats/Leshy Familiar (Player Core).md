# Leshy Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 127

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]]

---

You call a minor spirit of nature into a plant body, creating a leshy companion to aid you in your spellcasting. You gain a familiar, which has your choice of either the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Plant|plant]] or [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Fungus|fungus]] familiar ability; this doesn't count against your usual limit of familiar abilities (typically 2). The spirit you call has a more tenuous connection to its plant body than fully independent leshies, so it is Tiny in size like other familiars.
