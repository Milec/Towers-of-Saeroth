# Marsh Runner  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 26

**Prerequisites**
You have a swim Speed.

---

When you use the Step action, you can ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by flooding, swamps, or quicksand. In addition, when you use the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] skill to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] on narrow surfaces or uneven marshy ground, you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], and if you roll a success on the Acrobatics check, you get a critical success instead.
