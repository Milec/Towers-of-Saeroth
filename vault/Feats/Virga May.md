# Virga May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 22

---

Born of a storm hag, you have one crystal-blue or rain-gray eye. You share your mother's power over wind and storm. You can cast [[Spells/Electric Arc|_electric arc_]] as an occult innate cantrip. As normal, a cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
