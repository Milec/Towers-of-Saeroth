# Investigate Haunting  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Hunter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Hunter Dedication]]

---

You are skilled at noticing the telltale signs of ghosts and hauntings. Even when you aren't [[Actions/Investigate|Investigating]] in exploration mode, you get a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and [[Traits/Haunt|haunts]] active in the area. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks to disable haunts.
