# Kaiju's Footfalls  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Mightyfall Kobold]] heritage

---

You channel the might and mass of a kaiju. You can cast [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]] as an innate primal spell twice per day, targeting yourself. The first time each turn you [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] while affected by this spell, creatures of your size or smaller adjacent to where you land must attempt a Reflex save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Failure** The creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  
**Critical Failure** The creature is knocked prone and takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage.

 At 17th level, you can choose to heighten this innate enlarge spell to 4th rank.
