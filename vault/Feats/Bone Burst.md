# Bone Burst ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 35

**Trigger**
A creature adjacent to one of your thralls uses a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action or a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, makes a ranged [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], or leaves a square during a move action it’s using, and you are within 30 feet of the thrall.

---

You destroy the thrall in an explosion of bone shards that fly toward the triggering creature, dealing 2d10 piercing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC.

The damage increases to 3d10 at 12th and to 4d10 at 18th level.
