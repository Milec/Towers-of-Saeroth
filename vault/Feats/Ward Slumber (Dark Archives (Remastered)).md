# Ward Slumber  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 207

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sleepwalker (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker Dedication]]

---

Just before going to sleep, you can perform a 10-minute ward to protect the slumber of up to eight creatures within 30 feet of you. All creatures affected by the ritual gain a +4 status bonus on saving throws against [[Spells/Nightmare (Player Core)|_nightmare_]] or other effects influencing dreams (such as those provided by this archetype) until your next daily preparations. They heal double the usual amount of Hit Points from resting.
