# Striker's Scroll  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 20

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

---

You can attach a [[Traits/Scroll (GM Core)|scroll]] to your weapon or [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|_handwraps of mighty blows_]] to blend its spell into an attack, carefully folding it around a part of the weapon or sealing it to the weapon’s surface. Attaching a scroll requires using the [[Actions/Affix a Talisman (GM Core)|Affix a Talisman]] action. You can have only one scroll affixed to a weapon at a time, and you can’t have both a talisman and scroll attached.

You can [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]] from the scroll as part of a [[Actions/Spellstrike]], provided the spell is one you could normally use with Spellstrike. You can’t Cast the Spell in any other way while it’s affixed, though you can use Affix a Talisman again to remove the scroll and use it as a normal—if a bit crumpled—scroll.
