# Crimson Breath two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You gain a fire breath weapon similar to that of a [[Bestiary/Crimson Worm|crimson worm]]. You breathe a blast of flame in a 30-foot cone that deals 12d6 fire damage to all creatures in the area ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). This damage increases by 2d6 at 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter.
