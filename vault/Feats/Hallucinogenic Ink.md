# Hallucinogenic Ink  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Merfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spray Ink]]; [[Ancestries/Heritages/Cecaelia Merfolk]] heritage

---

Your defensive ink spray has developed the capacity to cause haunting hallucinations. A creature sprayed with your ink must attempt a Will saving throw against either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. On a failure, the creature also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).
