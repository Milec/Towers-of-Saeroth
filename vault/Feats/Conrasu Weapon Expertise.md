# Conrasu Weapon Expertise  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conrasu]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 109

---

Whenever you gain a class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in certain weapons, you also gain that proficiency for [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspear]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]], and all [[Traits/Conrasu|conrasu]] weapons in which you are trained.
