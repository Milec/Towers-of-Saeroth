# Advanced General Training  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Human]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 65

---

Over the long course of your adventuring life, your adaptability has let you pick up numerous useful abilities. You gain a  [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|general feat]] of 7th level or lower. You must meet the feat’s prerequisites.

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a different feat each time.
