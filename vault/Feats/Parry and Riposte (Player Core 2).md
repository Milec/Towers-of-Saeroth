# Parry and Riposte  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 169

**Prerequisites**
Opportune Riposte

---

Your parries and finishers allow you to riposte with the slightest provocation. You can use Opportune Riposte against an enemy who fails at a Strike against you (not just critically fails), provided you have both damaged that creature with a [[Traits/Finisher (Player Core 2)|finisher]] on your last turn and currently have a circumstance bonus to AC from the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] weapon trait, or [[Feats/Extravagant Parry]].
