# Haunt Ingenuity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|Detection]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Thaumaturge (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Thaumaturge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 42

---

Your cunning knowledge grants you the ability to notice the emotional echo of a soul that passed on, leaving a haunt in its wake. Even when you aren't [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]] while in exploration mode, the GM rolls a secret check for you to notice haunts that usually require you to be Searching.

 You can disable haunts that require master proficiency in a skill as long as you're at least trained in the skill. If you have master proficiency in the skill, you can disable haunts that require a proficiency rank of legendary instead.
