# Draconic Sycophant  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 214

---

You possess features that dragons find particularly cute or charming, making it easier to endear yourself to them, or at least cause them to momentarily forgive your insolence. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks and saving throws against dragons. In addition, whenever you meet a creature with the [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]] trait in a social situation, you can attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on that creature immediately, rather than after conversing for 1 minute; you take a –5 circumstance penalty to this check. If you fail, you can choose to engage in 1 minute of conversation and then attempt a new check rather than accept the failure or critical failure result.
