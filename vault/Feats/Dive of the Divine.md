# Dive of the Divine two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 281

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]; [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of at least 20 feet

**Access**
You are at least 20 feet above the ground.

---

You dive from the sky, bringing down the might of the divine with you. You [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] straight down, landing safely in the nearest unoccupied space directly below you. When you land, all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] take 8d10 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC). On a failure, the target is pushed 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure).

At 20th level, the spirit damage increases to 10d10.
