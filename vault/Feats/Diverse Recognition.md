**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pathfinder Agent]]

**Frequency**
once per round

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pathfinder Agent Dedication]]; master in a skill used to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]]

**Trigger**
You successfully [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about a creature you can see, using a skill in which you are a master.

---

Your Pathfinder training allows you to quickly size up numerous threats. Attempt to Recall Knowledge against a different kind of creature you can see using the same skill. You must still meet the requirements to attempt this check.
