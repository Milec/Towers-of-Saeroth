# Killshot's Report ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Volatile Spark|volatile spark hybrid study]]

**Requirements**
Your Spellstrike reduces an enemy to 0 HP.

---

As you drop an enemy, a loud echo issues out as you chain the spell’s energy to a nearby creature. Choose a secondary target within 10 feet of the creature you reduced to 0 HP. This secondary target is affected by the spell you used for your [[Actions/Spellstrike]]. The creature gets any defense against the spell that Spellstrike normally allows, and you use your Strike result for a spell attack roll if needed. If the secondary target was already affected by the spell in some way (in the area of an [[Feats/Expansive Spellstrike]], for example), it can’t be affected a second time.
