# Feral Lunge single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Werecreature|Werecreature]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Werecreature Dedication]]; your animal shape grants a fangs or jaws unarmed attack

**Requirements**
You’re in hybrid or animal shape.

---

You spring on an unsuspecting opponent, tearing at them with your teeth. You Stride up to 10 feet and make a jaws Strike at the end of that movement. If you began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], you remain hidden until after this Strike. If you have a fly or swim Speed, you can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to 10 feet instead of Stride.
