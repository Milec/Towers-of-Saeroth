# Heightened Captivation  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Captivator Dedication]]

---

You can infuse your [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantments]] and [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]] with more power. Choose one of your spells granted by captivator archetype feats; this spell can't be from your highest level of non-cantrip captivator spells. The spell you choose is heightened to the highest level of captivator spell you can cast, not including cantrips. You can't later change the spell that is heightened by this feat.

**Special** You can select this feat more than once. Each time you select it, you choose a different spell.
