# Dimensional Disappearance  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 47

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Dimensional Assault|_dimensional assault_]] focus spell, [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Laughing Shadow|laughing shadow hybrid study]]

---

You take advantage of teleportation to better hide your position. When you cast [[Spells/Dimensional Assault|_dimensional assault_]], you're affected by an [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell at the end of the teleport. You can choose not to make the Strike that is normally part of _dimensional assault_. If you do make the Strike, your invisibility ends after the Strike, as normal for _invisibility_.
