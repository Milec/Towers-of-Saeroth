# Svetocher  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir (Player Core 2)|Dhampir]], [[Traits/Lineage (Player Core)|Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 43

---

You were born to a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|moroi]], the most common vampires, and you share their supernatural fortitude and power of persuasion. When you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition, calculate the penalty to your Fortitude saves and your Hit Point reduction as though the condition value were 1 lower. You're trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. If you would automatically be trained in it, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
