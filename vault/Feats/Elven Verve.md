# Elven Verve  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 26

---

While all elves are immune to the paralyzing touch of [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghouls]], you can shake off flesh-numbing magic of all kinds. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against effects that would impose the [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] conditions. When you would be immobilized, paralyzed, or slowed for at least 2 rounds, reduce that duration by 1 round.
