# Tumbling Opportunist free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Attack]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 155

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] or Tumbling Strike, and you successfully moved through an enemy's space.

---

You use a burst of stamina to perform a breathtaking feat of [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] as you speed through a foe's space, leaving your foe lying flat on their back. You attempt to [[Actions/Trip]] the enemy whose space you moved through. You can use Acrobatics instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] for this check.
