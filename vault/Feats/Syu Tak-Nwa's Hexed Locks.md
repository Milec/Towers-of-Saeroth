# Syu Tak-Nwa's Hexed Locks  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 74

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Living Hair]]

---

Your hair unarmed attack gains the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait. You can deliver [[Traits/Hex|hexes]] through your hair. When you successfully cast a hex focus spell that requires 2 or more actions to cast and that doesn't require a spell attack roll, if your target is within your reach, as part of the spellcasting activity you can make a hair Strike against the foe before applying any effects of the hex. If this Strike misses, the hex has no effect.

## Feats

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 74

The player characters might learn some of these techniques from friends and rivals during their stay on Bonmu, allowing them to select the feats the next time they would gain a feat. Surviving combat against Syu Tak-nwa allows the characters to take the three witch feats, while defeating or impressing the Ahmoza twins allows them to take Entwined Energy Ki. Lighting the candles and praying in the temple of Irori's prayer room (area A8) gives them access to Wronged Monk's Wrath.
