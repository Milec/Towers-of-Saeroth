# First World Magic  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gnome]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 52

---

Your connection to the First World grants you a primal innate spell, much like those of the fey. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] spell list. You can cast this spell as a primal innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
