# Resounding Cascade  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 25

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade (Impossible Magic)|Arcane Cascade]]

---

The magical reinforcement from your [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] extends to nearby allies. While you’re in Arcane Cascade stance, you grant its extra damage and resistance in an aura affecting all allies in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. This uses only the base values of your stance; it doesn’t use any increase you get from bonuses, the [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Laughing Shadow|laughing shadow hybrid study]], or the like. As normal for duplicate effects, multiple Arcane Cascades affecting the same creature aren’t cumulative.
