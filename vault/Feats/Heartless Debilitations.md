You debilitate your enemies with ruthless and terrifying efficiency. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use [[Actions/Debilitating Strike (Player Core)|Debilitating Strike]].

- **Debilitation** The target gains [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage.
- **Debilitation** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.
