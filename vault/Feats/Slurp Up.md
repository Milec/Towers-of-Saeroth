# Slurp Up single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 75

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Thlipit Contestant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Thlipit Contestant Dedication]]; unarmed attack with your tongue

---

Your tongue darts out in search of refreshment. Choose a liquid container of up to light Bulk within reach of your lash, such as a [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|potion]] or [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]]. The container must be held or in the open, not stowed in a pack or bandoleer. If the container is unattended or held by a willing ally, you Interact to drink the contents. If the container is held by an unwilling creature, you must succeed on a check using your [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack modifier or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] modifier against the creature's Reflex DC to drink the contents; this adds the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] trait.
