# Weft and Warp three actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

---

With graceful poise, you perform a dance of dangerous attacks. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your speed toward an enemy; if you end this movement adjacent to them make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against that foe. If you hit, you can [[Actions/Reposition]] the target into your square at the same time that you [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into their square. You then make another Strike against the same target, who is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the attack. Finally, you can Step away from that enemy.
