# Stone Brawler Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Stone Brawler]]

**Prerequisites**
Constitution 14

---

You've learned to call the primal power of elemental stone to your aid. You gain the Stonestrike Stance action.

 **Stonestrike Stance** single action **Requirements** You are standing on the ground; **Effect** You enter the stance of unyielding stone and draw upon the power of the living rock, encasing your fists in stone and allowing you to make stonestrike attacks. These deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the stone brawler archetype.
