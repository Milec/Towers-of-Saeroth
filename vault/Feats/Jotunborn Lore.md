# Jotunborn Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 13

---

You were taught the history of your people’s planar travels as well as the skills necessary to thrive in sub-planar environments. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Jotunborn Lore]].

**Special** If you have the sage jotunborn heritage, you gain the Additional Lore feat a second time for a lore of your choice.
