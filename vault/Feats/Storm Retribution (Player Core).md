# Storm Retribution ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 131

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Storm|storm order]]; [[Spells/Tempest Surge|_tempest surge_]] order spell

**Trigger**
An opponent adjacent to you critically hits you with a melee weapon or melee unarmed attack

**Requirements**
You have at least 1 available Focus Point

---

You lash out, directing a burst of storming fury toward a creature that has harmed you. You cast [[Spells/Tempest Surge (Player Core)|_tempest surge_]] on the triggering opponent and push that creature, moving it 5 feet away from you if it fails its Reflex save, or 10 feet if it critically fails. This movement is forced movement.
