# Cruelty  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 95

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Touch of the Void|_touch of the void_]]

---

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. You can cast [[Spells/Touch of the Void|_touch of the void_]] targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute if it fails its save (enfeebled 2 if it critically fails).
