# One For All single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 164

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]

---

With precisely the right words of encouragement, you bolster an ally's efforts. Designate an ally within 30 feet; this action counts as sufficient preparation to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] that ally. When you use the Aid reaction to help that ally, you can roll [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] in place of the usual check and the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.
