# Moment of Clarity single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 88

---

You push back your rage for a moment in order to think clearly. Until the end of this turn, you can use actions with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait even if those actions don’t have the [[Traits/Rage|rage]] trait.
