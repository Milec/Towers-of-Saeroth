# Shadow Mark  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 209

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

You have learned special tricks that help you follow individuals without them noticing you. When you attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Avoid Notice]] while following a specific target, the target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to their Perception DC. If you have master proficiency in Stealth, the penalty is –3 or –4 if you're legendary. If you start an encounter with the target while shadowing them, the target takes this penalty to their initiative roll and to their Perception DC to determine if they notice you, as normal for [[Actions/Sneak]].
