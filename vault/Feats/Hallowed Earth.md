# Hallowed Earth single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 36

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/The Hallowed Dead]]

---

The earth is both where the dead rise from and where they are put to rest. Your presence sanctifies the earth around you. When an [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature ends its turn within 10 feet of you, it takes 2 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. If it’s an unholy [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature, it takes an additional 2 vitality damage. At 10th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the spirit and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage each increase by 2. Hallowed Earth lasts for 1 minute, but you can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] it.
