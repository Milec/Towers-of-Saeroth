# I Meant to Do That single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 142

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Skilled or Lucky|Unexpected Sharpshooter]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; [[Feats/Unexpected Sharpshooter Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was a ranged weapon [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] that missed a foe within 60 feet.

---

Somehow your stray bullet causes an unintended reaction that creates a problem for your enemy: perhaps a ricochet knocks your foe’s weapon away or they stumble over stray debris in an attempt to dodge your bullets. Roll a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check to attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], or [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the foe you missed.
