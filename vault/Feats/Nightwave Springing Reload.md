# Nightwave Springing Reload single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 77

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

---

Training with the crew of the Nightwave has taught you their technique for reloading while leaping to board another ship. You [[Actions/Leap]] and then Interact to reload, or Interact to reload and then Leap. You can perform this ability as a 2-action activity to use your Leap to [[Actions/High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump]] without needing to Stride at least 10 feet first.
