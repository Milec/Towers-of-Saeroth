# Kashrishi Revivification free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kashrishi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 45

**Frequency**
once pre day

**Trigger**
You have the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition and are about to attempt a recovery check. You can use Kashrishi Revivification even if you're otherwise unable to act.

---

The well of psychic energy held within your mind can exceed the limitations of your physical body. You're restored to 1 Hit Point, lose the dying and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] conditions, and can act normally on this turn. You gain or increase the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition as normal for losing the dying condition.
