# Jungle Strider  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 120

---

You are adept at dodging roots, foliage, and other jungle obstacles. You ignore difficult terrain in forests and jungles. In addition, when you use the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] skill to [[Actions/Balance]] on narrow surfaces or uneven ground made of plant material, you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], and when you roll a success at one of these Acrobatics checks, you get a critical success instead.
