# Garden Path  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bellflower Tiller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bellflower Dedication]]

---

You can guide your crop to move without notice. When you and any of your crop are [[Rules/Category Pages/Activities|Avoiding Notice]], you can choose one member of your crop to temporarily gain the benefits of the [[Feats/Quiet Allies]] skill feat until the group is no longer Avoiding Notice. The group rolls two checks instead of one, using the higher die roll. This is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.
