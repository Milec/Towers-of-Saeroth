# Archaeologist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 156

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archaeologist]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in Perception; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]]

---

You are a student of peoples and their histories, and are in constant pursuit of knowledge and artifacts from the past. You become an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]], and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about ancient history, peoples, and cultures.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the archaeologist archetype.
