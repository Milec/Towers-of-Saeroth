# Silk Bracelet two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 110

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Armor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|armor innovation]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Construct (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|construct innovation]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Innovations/Weapon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|weapon innovation]]

**Access**
Tian Xia origin

---

Taking inspiration from [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna’s]] image as a giant silk moth in Tian Xia, you’ve attached a bracelet to your innovation that generates ultra-strong silk strands mixed with venom. You can unleash this substance as part of an attack. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If it hits, the target takes damage from the Strike as normal and must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The silk imposes a –10 foot status penalty to all the target’s Speeds for 1 round.  

**Failure** As success, and the target takes an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. The poison damage increases to 2d4 at 12th level and 3d4 at 18th level.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the end of their next turn.
