# Emberkin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 9

---

A [[Bestiary/Peri|peri's]] roaring spirit burns in your soul. Peris engage in constant struggle to right the wrongs of their fallen forebears, and that struggle has been passed on to you. You have a direct impulse to right wrongs and better the world. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fire|fire]] effects, and your perception is unaffected by non-magical flames, fog and smoke.
