# Imposing Destrier  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 97

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Loyal Warhorse (Player Core 2)|Loyal Warhorse]]

---

Under your care, your mount has realized its innate potential. The mount you gained with Faithful Steed is now a [[Rules/Nimble Animal Companions (Player Core)|nimble]] or [[Rules/Savage Animal Companions (Player Core)|savage]] animal companion.

 Your animal companion has greater independence. During an encounter, even if you don't use the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] action, your animal companion can still use 1 action that round on your turn to Stride or Strike. It can do this at any point during your turn, as long as you aren't currently taking an action. If it does, that's all the actions it gets that round—you can't Command it later.
