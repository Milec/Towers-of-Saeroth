# Smoke Through Bamboo  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 62

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/New Moon Sarangay]] heritage

---

You’ve spent significant time slipping silently between bamboo thickets, and your light-footedness allows you to navigate even the thickest forests and brush with ease. When you [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], you can move 10 feet instead of 5. This enables you to Step into and within [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] as long as you can meet the extra movement cost.
