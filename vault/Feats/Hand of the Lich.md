# Hand of the Lich  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 51

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Soul Cage|Lich]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lich Dedication]]

---

Negative energy intensifies your undead form and makes your very touch the antithesis of life. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]]. The damage die for your fist increases to 1d6 instead of 1d4, it deals negative damage instead of bludgeoning damage, and it loses the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait. Your fist becomes [[Traits/Magical|magical]].

 When you critically hit a living creature with your fist Strike, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] until the end of your next turn unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save against your spell DC. This is a [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
