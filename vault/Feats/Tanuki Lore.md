# Tanuki Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 67

---

Tanuki love to play two things: tricks and music. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Tanuki Lore.
