# Quill Spray two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shisk]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 125

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Quillcoat Shisk]] heritage or quill [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack

---

You can launch a mass of quills at opponents. You spray quills in a 30-foot cone, dealing 5d8 piercing damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] saving throw against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. On a critical failure, a creature also takes 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. At 11th level and every even level thereafter, the piercing damage increases by 1d8 and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
