# Path of Iron three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Martial Artist Dedication (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist Dedication]]

---

With a burst of effort and speed, you weave a nimble path through your many enemies, striking each in turn as you move past them. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]; this movement doesn’t trigger reactions. You can attempt a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] up to three times at any point during your movement, each against a different enemy. Each attack counts toward your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but your multiple attack penalty doesn’t increase until you have made all your attacks.
