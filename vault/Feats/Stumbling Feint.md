# Stumbling Feint  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 129

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; [[Feats/Stumbling Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Stumbling Stance.

---

You lash out confusingly with what seems to be a weak move but instead allows you to unleash a dangerous flurry of blows upon your unsuspecting foe. When you use [[Actions/Flurry of Blows]], you can attempt a check to [[Actions/Feint]] as a free action just before the first Strike. On a success, instead of making the target [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against your next attack, they become flat-footed against both attacks from the Flurry of Blows.
