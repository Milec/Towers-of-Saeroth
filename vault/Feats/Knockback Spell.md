# Knockback Spell single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 109

---

You imbue your spells with magical force to knock creatures back. If the next action you use is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has an area of a [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], or [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]; has no duration; and requires creatures to attempt a Reflex or Fortitude save, the spell unleashes a forceful blast that pushes creatures away, in addition to its other effects. Each creature that failed its save against the spell is pushed 5 feet, and those who critically failed are pushed 10 feet. If the spell is a cone, emanation, or a line, you push the creature away from yourself. If the spell is a burst, you push the creature away from the center of the burst.
