# Holy Bloom  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 84

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rose Warden Dedication]]

---

Though you might not be a priest of Milani, her divine spirit flows through you. You gain holy sanctification. Select the [[Magic/Domains/Freedom (Player Core)|freedom]] or [[Magic/Domains/Zeal (Player Core)|zeal]] domain. You gain the initial domain spell for that domain. If you don't have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. You can [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by praying to Milani or by working with others to plan a just uprising. You become trained in spell attack modifier and spell DC, and your spellcasting attribute for these spells is Wisdom.

**Special** You can choose this feat a second time, gaining the initial domain spell of the domain you didn't gain the first time.
