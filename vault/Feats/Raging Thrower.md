# Raging Thrower  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 88

---

Thrown weapons become especially deadly in your fury. You apply the additional damage from Rage to your thrown weapon attacks. If you have the [[Feats/Brutal Critical]] feat or the devastator class feature, apply their benefits to thrown weapon attacks.
