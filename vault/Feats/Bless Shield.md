# Bless Shield single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication]]; you worship a good-aligned deity

---

Your uttered prayers become an aegis, drawing the blessing of your deity across the surface of your shield. Until the beginning of your next turn your shield, any [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss|shield boss]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]] gain the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune. Additionally, damage you take as a result of an incorporeal creature's Strike can trigger your [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=1140) reaction, even if the damage isn't physical damage.
