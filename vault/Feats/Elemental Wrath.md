# Elemental Wrath  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 26

---

You are so attuned to the land that you can call forth a bolt of energy from your surroundings. When you gain this feat, select acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You can call to the land to cast the [[Spells/Acid Splash|_acid splash_]] cantrip as an innate primal spell at will, except the spell has only verbal components and deals the type of damage you chose instead of acid damage; the spell gains the trait appropriate to its damage instead of the [[Traits/Acid|acid]] trait. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
