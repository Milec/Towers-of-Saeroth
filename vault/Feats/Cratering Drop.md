# Cratering Drop single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 83

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Winged Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pluck From the Sky]]

**Requirements**
Your previous action was Pluck from the Sky using a melee Strike, and the target failed or critically failed its saving throw.

---

You follow up your enemy's fall, driving you both to the earth in a meteoric descent. You [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] straight down, increasing the amount the enemy falls by your Fly speed. If you reach the ground, you drive the enemy into the ground, dealing double the normal [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]].
