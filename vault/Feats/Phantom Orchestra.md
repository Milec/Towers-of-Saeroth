# Phantom Orchestra  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tanuki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 68

---

An easy way to trick others is to throw your voice into a nearby forest or thicket to make strange noises. You can cast 2nd-rank [[Spells/Ventriloquism (Player Core)|_ventriloquism_]] as an innate [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] spell once per day. You can also cast the [[Spells/Figment|_figment_]] cantrip as a primal innate spell at will, heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
