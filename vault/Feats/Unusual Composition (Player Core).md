# Unusual Composition single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 105

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Polymath|polymath muse]]

---

You can translate the emotion and power of a composition to other mediums. If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Composition (Player Core)|composition]] spell that is [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]], you can have it affect creatures who can witness it with any sense, not just those the traits indicate, even including [[Rules/Special Senses (Player Core)|special senses]] like [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]]. You can choose to exclude any senses you choose.
