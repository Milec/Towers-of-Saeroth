# Minotaur Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Minotaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 43

---

Your elders taught you to wield weapons that can split skulls and smash through shields. You have familiarity with the [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe (Player Core)|battle axe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion (Player Core)|falchion]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|glaive]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe (Player Core)|greataxe]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat these martial weapons as simple weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
