# Vibration Sense  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

---

Your eidolon gains [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet. If [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]], they instead gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Wavesense (Monster Core)|wavesense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. If [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]], they gain both.
