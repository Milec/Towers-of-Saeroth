# Translocational Adept  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 105

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

---

Your command over teleportation grows. You can cast [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] 4th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell once per day. At 14th level, the spell is heightened to 5th rank. When you cast _translocate_ this way, you can Part the Veil as a free action when you arrive at your destination, even if you wouldn’t normally meet its requirements.
