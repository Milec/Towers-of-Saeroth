# Vow of Mortal Defiance single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exemplar]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 35

**Prerequisites**
You are not [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]] with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] or [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.

---

Having seen the wreckage left by the gods and their servitors as they play in their great war of good and evil, you’ve come to the only reasonable conclusion: they all must be cut from their silken thrones. You swear a vow to defeat one creature within 60 feet that has the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] or [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait. The first time each round that you deal damage to that creature, you deal an additional 1d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. You can’t use Vow of Mortal Defiance again until you or the target is defeated, flees, or the encounter ends.

**Special** If you take this feat, you can’t become sanctified with the holy or unholy trait. Retraining out of this feat typically requires a major change of philosophy.
