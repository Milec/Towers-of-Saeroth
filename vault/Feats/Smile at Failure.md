# Smile at Failure  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 74

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Prophet of Kalistrade]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Prophet of Kalistrade Dedication]]

---

You know staying cool is key to prevailing in social situations, as anger leads to mistakes. When a creature you’re interacting with decreases its attitude from [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent (Player Core)|indifferent]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly (Player Core)|unfriendly]] or from unfriendly to [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]], you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to any attempts to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on the creature for the next hour. If this decrease in attitude leads to combat, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the subsequent initiative roll instead.
