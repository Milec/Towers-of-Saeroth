# Folk Healer  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 61

---

Your community recognizes you as a shaman or other animist, a status you obtained through lineage, apprenticeship, or a supernatural event. You’re trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] and [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Medicine checks to [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]] and [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]].
