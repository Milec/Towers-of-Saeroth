# Spinning Stand single action or two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 68

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clawdancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

---

You flare your legs around, using the momentum to both lash out and return to a standing position. You enter talon stance if you weren't in it already, make a spinning talon Strike against an enemy in reach, and then [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]]. You don't take the -2 circumstance penalty for attacking while prone, and your Stand action doesn't trigger reactions. You can spend two actions using Spinning Stand to make two spinning talon Strikes instead of one; these Strikes must be against separate targets.
