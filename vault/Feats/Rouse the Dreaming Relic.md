# Rouse the Dreaming Relic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 74

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Inherit the Dreaming Heirloom]]

---

The spirit within your pusaka resonates with other magical items in your possession. Your pusaka gains the following activation.

**Activate—Replenish Heirloom** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your pusaka uses its power to spark one of your depleted magic items to life. You [[Actions/Activate an Item]] you’ve [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]] even after you’ve used that activation the maximum number of times for its frequency. You can do so only if the item’s level is half your level or lower, the activation has a frequency of once per day or more frequent, and you haven’t already used the activation this round.
