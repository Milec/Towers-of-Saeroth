# Corrupted Ground single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Spirit]], [[Traits/Unholy]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 36

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/The Unholy Dead]]

---

The ground on which you stride is mere fuel that you burn with unholy void energies, scarring the earth in your wake. When a [[Traits/Holy|holy]] creature or [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomp]] ends its turn within 10 feet of you, it takes 2 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. If it’s a holy living creature, it takes an additional 2 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage. At 10th level and every 4 levels thereafter, the spirit and void damage each increase by 2. Corrupted Ground lasts for 1 minute, but you can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] it.
