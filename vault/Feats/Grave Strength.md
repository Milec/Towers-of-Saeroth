# Grave Strength  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 49

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Ghoul|Ghoul]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghoul Dedication]]

---

The flesh you've consumed over the course of your existence as a ghoul has made you stronger, gifting you with strange insights from the minds of the sapient creatures you've devoured and bringing you closer to a state of undead perfection. You gain the [[Rules/Advanced Undead Benefits|advanced undead benefits]]. In addition, you gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed while you're satiated.
