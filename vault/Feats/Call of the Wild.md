# Call of the Wild  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 134

---

You call upon the creatures of nature to come to your aid. You can spend 10 minutes in concert with nature to replace one of the spells you’ve prepared in one of your druid spell slots with a [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]] or [[Spells/Summon Plant or Fungus|_summon plants and fungus_]] spell of the same level.
