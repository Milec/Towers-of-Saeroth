# Reel 'Em In single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Nets|Marine Marauder]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marine Marauder Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Harpoon (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|harpoon]], a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]] weapon, or a [[Equipment/Net (Player Core)|net]] attached to a [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]].

---

You’ve developed combat techniques inspired by the methods fishermen use to catch large fish. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a required weapon or [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] with a net. If the attack hits, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the target’s Fortitude DC. On a success, you pull the target 10 feet closer to you.
