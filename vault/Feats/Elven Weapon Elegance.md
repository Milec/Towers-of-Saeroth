# Elven Weapon Elegance  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Elven Weapon Familiarity]]

---

You are attuned to the weapons of your elven ancestors and are particularly deadly when using them. Whenever you critically hit using an [[Traits/Elf|elf]] weapon or one of the weapons listed in [[Feats/Elven Weapon Familiarity]], you apply the weapon's critical specialization effect.
