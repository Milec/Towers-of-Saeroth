# Enhanced Glyph Familiar  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Glyph Familiar]]

---

Your living rune is imbued with more magical power. You can select two familiar or master abilities each day instead of one. In addition, when you [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] with a single action, your target can be adjacent to your familiar instead of adjacent to you. Your familiar must be within 30 feet of you for this benefit.
