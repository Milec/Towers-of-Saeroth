# Night Magic  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

You can tap into the magic that runs through your blood. You gain [[Spells/Animal Form|_animal form_]] (wolf only, using the statistics for a canine) and [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]] as 2nd-level divine innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day.
