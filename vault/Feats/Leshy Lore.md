# Leshy Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 54

---

You deeply understand your people’s cultural traditions and innate strengths. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in Leshy [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]].
