# Graceful Poise single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 153

**Prerequisites**

---

With the right positioning, your off-hand weapon can strike like a scorpion's stinger. While you are in this stance, if you make your second Strike from Double Slice with an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] weapon, Double Slice counts as one attack when calculating your multiple attack penalty.
