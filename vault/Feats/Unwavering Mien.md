# Unwavering Mien  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 40

---

Your mystic control and meditations allow you to resist external influences upon your consciousness. Whenever you are affected by a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect that lasts at least 2 rounds, you can reduce the duration by 1 round.

 You still require natural sleep, but you treat your saving throws against effects that would cause you to fall asleep as one degree of success better. This protects only against [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] effects, not against other forms of falling [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
