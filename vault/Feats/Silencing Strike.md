# Silencing Strike single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 110

---

A quick strike to the face or mouth silences your opponent. Make a melee Strike against a foe. The foe must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is dazed and can barely vocalize. It's [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and its speech is raspy and hard to understand. It must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to use [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] actions or supply verbal spellcasting components until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is stunned 3 instead of stunned 1.
