# Eidolon's Retort ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

**Trigger**
A creature within your eidolon’s reach uses a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action or a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, makes a ranged attack, or leaves a square during a move action it’s using.

---

Your eidolon makes a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against the triggering creature. If the attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action, your eidolon disrupts that action. This Strike doesn’t count toward your multiple attack penalty, and your multiple attack penalty doesn’t apply to this Strike.
