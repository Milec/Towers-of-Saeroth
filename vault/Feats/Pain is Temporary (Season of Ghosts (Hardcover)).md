# Pain is Temporary two actions  *Feat 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 320

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

Sangpotshi practitioners know that suffering is a fleeting sensation that ultimately strengthens the soul and prepares it for a new reincarnation. Bolstered by this understanding, you’ve learned to temporarily steel yourself against all forms of pain. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to all damage until the end of your next turn.
