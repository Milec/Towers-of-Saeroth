# Dustsoul  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Oread]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 106

---

Your [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Earth|earth elemental]] ancestor's influence manifests in you as dust and dirt, like the soil of a field or the hot desert sands. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You can sustain yourself on dirt and ash instead of normal food and water.
