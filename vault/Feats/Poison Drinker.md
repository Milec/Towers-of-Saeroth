# Poison Drinker ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 37

**Trigger**
You critically succeed on a Fortitude save against a [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] effect.

---

You temporarily transform your digestive system to instead gain a burst of sustenance from the poison you ingest. You regain Hit Points equal to the level of the [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] (or double the spell’s rank if the poison was caused by a spell), and you gain as much nourishment as one square meal.
