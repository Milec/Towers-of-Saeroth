# Elite Dracomancer  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 15

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dracomancer]]

---

Your magic rivals that of lesser dragons. Choose one 3rd-level spell and one 4th-level spell from those listed for a dragon spellcaster of your draconic exemplar's type (such as [[Spells/Paralyze|_paralyze_]] and [[Spells/Stinking Cloud|_stinking cloud_]] for a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Black|black dragon]] exemplar). Links to all types of dragons can be found [[Traits/Dragon|here]]. You can cast each of these spells once per day as arcane innate spells.
