# Community-Minded  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Athamaru]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 19

---

You are familiar with all the members of your community and their typical movements, and you have learned how to apply these patterns outside those circles. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC against someone attempting to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you and to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] or to detect a creature trying to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] someone they are not.
