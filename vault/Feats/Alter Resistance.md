# Alter Resistance ⤾  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ganzi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 97

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Trigger**
You take acid, electricity, or sonic damage.

---

You focus on your [[Bestiary/Families/Protean|protean]] forebears to change your energy resistance. The energy resistance you gain from your ganzi heritage changes to match the triggering damage. This change occurs after the damage is dealt.
