# Aura Shaping  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kineticist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 23

---

You control the size of your kinetic aura. You can make your kinetic aura's emanation any size from a 5-foot emanation to a 20-foot emanation (you must choose a multiple of 5). You choose the size when you [[Actions/Channel Elements]] and can change it when you use a stance impulse that affects your kinetic aura. Increase the maximum size you can choose by 5 feet at 15th and 20th levels.
