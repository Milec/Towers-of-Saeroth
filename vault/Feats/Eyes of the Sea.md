# Eyes of the Sea  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Aquatic Elf]] heritage

---

Your eyes have adapted to the rigors of the sea. If you didn’t already have [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]], you gain darkvision. Additionally, you can see through natural phenomena that would obscure your vision underwater, such as cloudy waters, ink, smoke, and so forth, and don’t need to succeed at a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to target creature within these phenomena. Finally, you can cast [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] once per day.
