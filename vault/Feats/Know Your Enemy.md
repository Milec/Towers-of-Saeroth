You aim to turn knowledge into power against your foes. Attempt a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check against an enemy you’re observing. Any ally within your aura who’s trained in the skill you use to attempt the Recall Knowledge check can use their reaction to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] you on the check without having prepared to help first.
