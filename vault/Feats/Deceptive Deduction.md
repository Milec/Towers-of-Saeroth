# Deceptive Deduction ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 37

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Twilight Talon]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twilight Talon Dedication]]

**Trigger**
A creature you can see uses [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]].

---

You've had to take on many roles while working undercover, so you're practiced at following the example of those around you to pretend expertise in a variety of fields. You learn what skill the creature was using to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] and whether that creature is trained in the skill. If it's trained, you can use [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to Recall Knowledge as if it were that skill for the next minute.
