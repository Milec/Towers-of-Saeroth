# Enshroud Soul Cage  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 50

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Soul Cage|Lich]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Lich Dedication]]

---

You wreathe your [_soul cage_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1545) in an ever-growing collection of obfuscating illusions and protective abjurations to hide it from your enemies. You disguise the _soul cage_ as any non-magical object of the  same Bulk, and it gains the effects of the [[Spells/Magic Aura|_magic aura_]] and [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] spells. The effects have unlimited durations, are heightened to half your level rounded up, and use your spell DC against any attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] them. The magic aura makes your _soul cage_ appear non-magical. You can ignore the effects of any of these spells when dealing with your own _soul cage_. You are mentally alerted whenever your _soul cage_ is damaged.
