# Gift of the Hoard  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Aftermath]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 56

**Prerequisites**
You have succeeded at an important task given by a [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]], such as procuring a special treasure for their hoard.

---

In pleasing the cravings of a mighty dragon, they have deigned to impart some of their magical essence into your body. Your eyes begin to glow and change color while your limbs and neck grow a veneer of scales, all the same color as the associated dragon, accompanied by a near-insatiable desire to amass a hoard of your own. You gain the [[Spells/Dragon Breath|_dragon breath_]] sorcerer bloodline spell, emulating the breath of the associated dragon, and a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. If you already have a focus pool, increase your number of Focus Points by 1. You [[Actions/Refocus]] by counting and arranging your treasure.
