# Plant Soul Siblings  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 320

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Animal Soul Siblings (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Animal Soul Siblings]]

---

You’re now able to connect with the spirits present in the greenery of the natural world. You gain [[Spells/Speak with Plants (Player Core)|_speak with plants_]] as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that you can cast once per day. You get a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Rules/Gather Information (GM Core)|Gather Information]] from animals or plants.
