# It's Not Over ⤾  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 83

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]] (Level 14), [[Archetypes/Gladiator (Player Core 2)|Gladiator]] (Level 14)

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]] or [[Feats/Gladiator Dedication (Player Core 2)|Gladiator Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You’re reduced to 0 Hit Points

---

In the world of drama, the finale of any performance doesn’t have to be the end, and for you, this now extends to life itself. When damage reduces you to 0 Hit Points, you can use this reaction to suddenly spring back into action. Instead of gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition and falling [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], you regain a number of Hit Points equal to 10 + your level + your Constitution modifier.
