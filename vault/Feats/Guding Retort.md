With a cunning twist of their unarmed attack, your eidolon shifts the position of a foe. When your eidolon uses Eidolon’s Retort to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] and hits, your eidolon can [[Actions/Reposition]] the target up to 10 feet.
