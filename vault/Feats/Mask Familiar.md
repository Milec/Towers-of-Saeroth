# Mask Familiar  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 101

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Five Branches of Scholarship|Magaambyan Attendant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magaambyan Attendant Dedication]]

---

Your mask takes on a personality of its own, allowing it to serve as your [familiar](Familiars.aspx). Though you can still wear it as a mask, you can detach it from your face to grant it a bodily form of colored light that allows it to move around; usually, this is a miniature form of the animal the mask represents. Detaching or reattaching the familiar to transform it is a two-action activity. Even in mask form, the familiar can move around on its master’s face and speaks in a distinct voice (if it can talk). It is easily recognized as more than a simple mask unless the familiar succeeds at a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Impersonate]] a mask. Other than its appearance and the fact that it can be worn, it functions as other familiars. Your mask familiar has access to the mask freeze familiar ability

**Mask Freeze**: When in mask form, your familiar can hide its obvious supernatural qualities to pass as a simple, unassuming mask. It doesn’t need to Impersonate to fool a passing glance, and it gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its Deception DC against an active observer [[Actions/Seek|Seeking]] or otherwise studying it.
