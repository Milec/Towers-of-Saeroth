# Fautless Defense ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You would take damage from a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] made by a creature.

---

As you are struck, your steely determination reminds those who fight you that they are destined to be no more than footnotes in your tale. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to half your level to the triggering damage, and then attempt a Strike at mythic proficiency against the creature that attacked you. If the Strike hits and deals damage, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.
