# Paralyzing Jewel single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Sarangay]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 62

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Your dedication to the ideals of art and beauty allows your head gem to access the power of the waning moon. While you possess your head gem, you can overwhelm foes with a sense of reverent wonder. Enemies within a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Will save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC.

**Critical Success** The enemy is unaffected.  

**Success** The enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 1 round.
