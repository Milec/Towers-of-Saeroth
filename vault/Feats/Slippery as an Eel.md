# Slippery as an Eel  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 97

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undersea Privateer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undersea Privateer Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]

---

You mimic the serpentine movements of an eel. While underwater, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics checks to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]], [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]], and [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]].
