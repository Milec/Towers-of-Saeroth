# Whirling in the Summer Storm three actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 121

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Twisting Petal Stance]]

**Requirements**
You’re in Twisting Petal Stance.

---

Your hands move in a hypnotic and distracting flurry that throws your foes off-balance before you cast them away with a violent pirouette. You [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]. Then each enemy within your reach must attempt a Will save against the higher of your class DC or your Deception DC; on a failure, they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to melee attacks from you and your allies until the end of your next turn. Then, [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] up to three adjacent enemies; each Shove counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but you don’t increase your penalty until after you’ve resolved all the Shove attempts.
