# Ranger Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 228

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ranger]]

**Prerequisites**
Dexterity 14

---

You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]; if you were already trained in Survival, you instead become trained in another skill of your choice. You become trained in ranger class DC.

 You can use the Hunt Prey action.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the ranger archetype.
