# Voice of the Night  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 33

---

You hear and understand the words of creatures you share the darkness with. You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] with [[Bestiary/Families/Bat|bats]], [[Bestiary/Families/Rat|rats]], and [[Bestiary/Families/Wolf|wolves]]. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on such animals.
