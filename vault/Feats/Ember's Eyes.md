# Ember's Eyes  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

Your eyes blaze with inner flame. You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], or you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if your ancestry already has low-light vision.

**Special** If your ancestry has neither low-light vision nor darkvision, you can take this ancestry feat a second time to gain darkvision. You can't retrain out of this feat.
