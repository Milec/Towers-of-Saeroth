# Unyielding Disguise  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
ability to cast [[Spells/Humanoid Form (Player Core)|_humanoid form_]] or [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]]

---

When you cast [[Spells/Humanoid Form (Player Core)|_humanoid form_]] or [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] to alter your appearance, the spell's duration is 2 hours. At the end of the duration, you can extend the spell's duration for another hour, but doing so is taxing, making you [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1. You can keep extending it in this way, increasing your drained condition by 1 each time, to a maximum duration of 6 hours.
