# Demonblood Frenzy ⤾  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Orc]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 93

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
tusks [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack

**Access**
Matanji ethnicity

**Trigger**
You successfully Strike a living creature that has the [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] trait with your tusks unarmed attack

---

Just the taste of fiendish blood is enough to send blood rushing through your veins in a supernatural fury. You become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] until the end of your next turn and you can use the extra action to make a tusks Strike, Stride, or Step.
