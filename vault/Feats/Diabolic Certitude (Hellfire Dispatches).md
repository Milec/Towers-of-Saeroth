# Diabolic Certitude free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 25

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Hellknight Dedication (Hellfire Dispatches)|Hellknight Dedication]]

**Trigger**
Your turn begins, and you can observe a [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]].

---

Your Hellknight training drilled details of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell's]] denizens deep into your mind. Attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about a devil you're observing. If you critically fail this check, you fail instead. If you succeed at this check, you critically succeed instead.
