# Piercing Jab two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 84

**Requirements**
Your eidolon’s primary attack deals piercing damage.

---

Your eidolon runs an enemy through with a horn or similar attack. Your eidolon makes a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with their primary attack. If the Strike hits and deals damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by your eidolon until it successfully [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]], your eidolon attacks with their primary attack again, or your eidolon is no longer manifested, whichever comes first. When the target is no longer grabbed, it takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] equal to the primary attack’s number of weapon damage dice.
