# Cursebreaker  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 289

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Palatine Detective]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Palatine Detective Dedication]]

---

Using various implements and esoteric methods, you can attempt to remove a curse from an unfortunate victim. It takes 10 minutes to perform these rites on a willing creature, who must remain within your reach the entire time. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] one [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] afflicting the creature; your counteract rank is half your level rounded up, and you use either your [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] modifier as your counteract modifier. If the afflicted creature or the individual who placed the curse on the afflicted creature is the target of your active investigation through [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to this counteract check. If the curse comes from a cursed item or other external source, a success indicates that the target creature can rid itself of the cursed item, but it doesn’t remove the curse from the item.
