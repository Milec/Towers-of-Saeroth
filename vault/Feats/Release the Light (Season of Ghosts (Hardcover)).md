# Release the Light two actions  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 321

**Frequency**
once per day

**Requirements**
You’re below 20 Hit Points.

---

Many people report seeing a bright light before their death. You’re one of these people, and at rare intervals when hope seems dim, the light exudes outward from your soul. You shed brigh[[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|t light]] in a 30-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for another 30 feet beyond that) until the end of your next turn. Enemy creatures in the area that rely on sight must attempt a Fortitude save equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 2 rounds and dazzled for 1 minute.
