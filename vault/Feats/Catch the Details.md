# Catch the Details  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goloma]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 116

---

You are quick at recognizing broad details about people and things around you. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and gain the [[Feats/Eye for Numbers]] skill feat. If you would automatically become trained in Society (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
