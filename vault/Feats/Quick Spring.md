# Quick Spring  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

---

Ishii Bunji is a skilled tumbler, and learning his technique allows you to push yourself off a foe and propel yourself across the battlefield faster. If you succeed at an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] an enemy's space, you can Stride again as a free action after you complete your current movement.
