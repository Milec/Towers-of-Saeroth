# Defensive Coordination single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 113

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Warrior|warrior muse]]; [[Feats/Inspire Defense]]

---

Like the storied heroes who persist in the face of overwhelming odds, you and your allies will hold the line. If your next action is to cast the [[Spells/Inspire Defense|_inspire defense_]] composition cantrip, you can [[Actions/Raise a Shield]], and one ally who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use a reaction to Raise a Shield.
