# Dual Onslaught  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 170

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dual-Weapon Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication]]

---

When you lash out with both weapons, you leave no room for the target to escape your attack. When you use [[Feats/Double Slice]], if you miss with both Strikes, choose one of the two weapons and apply the effects of a hit with that weapon. You can't choose a weapon if your attack roll with that weapon was a critical failure, meaning you still miss entirely if both attack rolls were critical failures.
