# Draconic Familiar  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 219

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Draconic Acolyte]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Draconic Acolyte Dedication]]

---

Your devotion to dragons has attracted a loyal servant. You gain a [[Feats/Familiar (Player Core)|familiar]]. You can select four [[Rules/Familiar Abilities|familiar]] or [[Rules/Master Abilities|master abilities]] each day, instead of two, but one of them must always be the dragon familiar ability. When you Channel Draconic Essence, you can also [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command]] your familiar as a free action.
