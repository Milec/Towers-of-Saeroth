# Axial Recall  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 43

---

You have reconfigured the magical resonance of your core to attune to the planar energies of [[Setting/Planes/Axis (GM Core)|Axis]]. You can cast [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_plane shift_]] twice per week as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that can only target yourself; you can travel only to Axis or the [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Material Plane]], and your body serves as the focus component.
