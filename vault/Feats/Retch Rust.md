# Retch Rust two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Kineticist]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 31

---

You exhale tendrils formed from flakes of rusted metal. All creatures in a 30-foot cone take 4d10 slashing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your class DC. A metal creature that fails its save also takes 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent slashing damage]].

---
**Level (+2)** The cloud's slashing damage increases by 1d10 and the persistent damage increases by 1d4.
