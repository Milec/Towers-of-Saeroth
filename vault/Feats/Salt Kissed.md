# Salt Kissed  *Feat 8*

You have a supernatural ability to survive at sea and keep your companions from drowning. You can cast [[Spells/Air Bubble (Player Core)|_air bubble_]] and [[Spells/Water Breathing (Player Core)|_water breathing_]] as 4th-rank divine innate spells once per day each. Add the [[Magic/Domains/Water (Player Core)|water]] domain to those that you can select with [[Feats/Liberty's Promise]].
