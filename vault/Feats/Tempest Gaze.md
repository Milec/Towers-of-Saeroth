# Tempest Gaze  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hungerseed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 37

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Storming Gaze]]

---

Your third eye grows stronger and more powerful. When you use [Storming Gaze](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=6838), you can increase the area to a 30-foot cone and double the number of damage dice; half deal electricity damage, and half deal sonic damage. If you Storming Gaze in this way, you can’t use it again for 1 hour.
