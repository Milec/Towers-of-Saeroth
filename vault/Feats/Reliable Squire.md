# Reliable Squire  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 82

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You know the path to victory isn't traveled alone, and you lend aid to your companions whenever you can. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Aid]]. If you roll a critical failure on a check to Aid, you get a failure instead.
