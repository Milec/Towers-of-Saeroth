# Flailtongue  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -184- The Ghouls Hunger|Pathfinder #184: The Ghouls Hunger]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Ghoul|Ghoul]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghoul Dedication]]

---

Your tongue has mutated into a lashing weapon reminiscent of [[Setting/Deities/Kabriri|Kabriri's]] bone-laden [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail|flail]]. You can use a free action to unhinge your lower jaw and sprout rigid bones from your tongue. You gain an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. Until you use an Interact action to reset your jaw and retract your tongue, you can't make a jaws Strike. Your flailtongue is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail|flail]] weapon group and has the [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Sweep|sweep]], and [[Traits/Trip|trip]] traits.
