# Brawling Focus  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 160

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 4)

---

You know how to make the most of your attacks when fighting hand-to-hand. You gain access to the critical specialization effects of unarmed strikes in the brawling group and weapons in the brawling group. If you have [[Feats/Monastic Weaponry]], you also gain the critical specialization effects of all monk weapons in which you are trained.
