You understand the frequency of energies that have damaged you and can extend the magical network of power in your internal nexus to diffuse incoming attacks. Choose one type of energy damage you have taken within the last minute. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to that energy type equal to half your level for 1 minute.
