# Wave the Flag single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cavalier Pledges|Cavalier]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cavalier's Banner]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You wave your banner, inspiring allies to throw off the shackles of fear. Each ally presently benefiting from your Cavalier's Banner feat reduces their current [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition by 1 and can choose to immediately attempt a new saving throw against any one [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect currently affecting them. Regardless of the result, any ally that attempts a save is temporarily immune to Wave the Flag for 10 minutes.
