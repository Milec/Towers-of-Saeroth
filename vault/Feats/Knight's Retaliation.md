# Knight's Retaliation ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 84

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
An undead creature within your reach critically fails a Strike against you.

---

You knock the enemy's blow aside and twist, unbalancing your foe. You attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] the triggering creature.
