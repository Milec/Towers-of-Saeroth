# Psychic Rapport  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 24

---

Your telepathic sensitivity intensifies when you're affected by mental magic, making it easier for you to link to other creatures. After you Cast a Spell with the [[Traits/Mental|mental]] trait, until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks, and to Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive]].
