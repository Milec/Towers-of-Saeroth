# Wayfinder Resonance Tinkerer  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 23

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pathfinder Agent]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pathfinder Agent Dedication]]

---

You can use the unusual resonance of your [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|_wayfinder_]] to grant it additional effects. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]], or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] list. You can activate your [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|_wayfinder_]] to cast that cantrip at will. As normal, this cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up. Additionally, you can Activate your [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|_wayfinder_]] with a single action (envision) activation to transform it into a nondescript pin or brooch to hide your affiliation with the Pathfinder Society. This transformation lasts for 1 hour.
