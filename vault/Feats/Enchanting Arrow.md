# Enchanting Arrow two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 173

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Elven Arrows|Eldritch Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

With a single whisper carried on the wind, you enchant your ammunition to make a foe more vulnerable to your attacks. Make a bow Strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d6 mental damage. On a critical hit, the target also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]]. The mental damage increases to 3d6 if your bow has a [[Equipment/Striking|_greater striking_]] rune, or to 4d6 if your bow has a _major striking_ rune.
