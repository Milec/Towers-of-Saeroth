# Arcane Propulsion two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Automaton]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 41

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You redirect energy from your core to ports on your back or feet, allowing you to [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|fly]] for a short time. You can maintain this redirected energy for 5 minutes. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed while redirecting the energy.

**Enhancement** You can maintain your flight for longer. You can redirect your energy at all times, allowing you to fly whenever you want, rather than just once per day for 5 minutes.
