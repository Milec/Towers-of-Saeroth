# Brine May  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 31

---

Your mother was a [[Bestiary/Sea Hag|sea hag]], granting you one sea-green or blue eye. You find comfort both in the sea and on the shore. Whenever you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Swim]], you get a critical success instead. If you end your turn in water without having succeeded at a Swim action that round, you don't sink (but you might still be moved by the current).
