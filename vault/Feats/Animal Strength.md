# Animal Strength  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 59

**Prerequisites**
[[Spells/Animal Feature (Player Core)|_animal feature_]] warden spell

---

You tap into the primal strength of your _animal feature_. When you gain a claw or jaws attack from _animal feature_, you can choose a weapon you're carrying and apply all runes from that weapon (if they're applicable) to that [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack. This replaces any runes the unarmed attacks would normally have from other sources, like [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|_handwraps of mighty blows_]]. In addition, when you critically hit with a claw or jaws attack from animal feature, you deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].
