The secrets of time peel back, revealing deeper truths. You gain either the [[Spells/Stasis (Divine Mysteries)|_stasis_]] domain spell or the [[Spells/Path of Least Resistance (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_path of least resistance_]] focus spell.

**Special** You can take this feat a second time, gaining the focus spell that you didn't gain the first time.
