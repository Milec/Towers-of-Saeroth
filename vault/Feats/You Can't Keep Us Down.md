# You Can't Keep Us Down three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 124

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Broken Chain]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Broken Chain Dedication]]

---

Seeing your allies falter, you fill their hearts with your unbreakable hope, rousing them to stay in the fight. All allies within 60 feet that are [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]], are healed a number of Hit Points equal to three times your level. Then, all allies within 60 feet that are [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] may stand up as a free action that does not provoke reactions.
