# Holistic Care  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

---

You provide emotional and spiritual care. Add [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] to the list of conditions you can reduce with [[Feats/Treat Condition (Player Core 2)|Treat Condition]]. If the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can’t use Treat Condition to reduce it.
