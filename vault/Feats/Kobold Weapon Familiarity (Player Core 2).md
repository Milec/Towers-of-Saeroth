# Kobold Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 22

---

You gain access to all [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]] weapons with the [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|kobold]] trait. You have familiarity with weapons with the kobold trait plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatpick (Player Core)|greatpick]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Pick (Player Core)|light pick]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick (Player Core)|pick]]—for the purposes of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
