# Warp Path single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Veil Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Veil Dancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are veiled.

---

You fold space around you, transporting yourself to an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. When you arrive, make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against one creature within your weapon’s reach. On a critical success, you remain veiled until the end of your next turn or until you take another [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]].
