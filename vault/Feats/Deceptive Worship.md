# Deceptive Worship  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 204

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]]

---

Members of your cult frequently pass themselves off as worshippers of other religions. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate]] a typical worshipper of another faith or to [[Actions/Lie]] specifically to claim you are a member of the faith you are Impersonating. You still need to use the Deception skill to Impersonate a specific worshipper or to perform other deceptive actions, such as attempting to Lie about any other matter.
