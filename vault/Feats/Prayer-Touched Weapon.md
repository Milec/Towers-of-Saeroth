# Prayer-Touched Weapon free action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 83

**Frequency**
once per turn

**Prerequisites**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spells, you follow a good-aligned deity

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to cast a non-cantrip divine spell.

---

You coat a weapon you're wielding in prayers and the divine power given to you by your deity. Until the end of your turn, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 positive damage. As usual for positive damage, this damage harms only [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]].
