# The Truth as I See it  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 58

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Field Propagandist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Field Propagandist Dedication]]

---

Your knack for spinning a believable reality from threads of story is so practiced that sometimes even you begin to believe  what you're saying. When you use [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to Lie, you can carefully structure your deceits so that each individual statement you make is the truth, from a certain perspective. Once per day when using Deception to Lie, you can roll twice and take the better result. This is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect.  

You don't take a penalty to Deception checks while subject to the [[Spells/Ring of Truth|_ring of truth_]] spell or similar effects.  Whenever you are subject to _ring of truth_ or a similar effect, you can attempt a Deception check when you first begin  speaking to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] the spell's effects, with a counteract rank equal to half your level (rounded up); succeeding at this  check doesn't end the spell or effect, but it does cause it to indicate that you are speaking the truth, even when you are  actually lying.
