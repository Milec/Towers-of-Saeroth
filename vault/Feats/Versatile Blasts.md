# Versatile Blasts  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kineticist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 21

---

Accessing a wider realm of elemental manifestations, you can make blasts with rare forms of your elements. Add the following damage types to those you can choose for [[Actions/Elemental Blast|Elemental Blasts]] of that element: **air** cold, **earth** poison, **fire** cold, **metal** electricity, **water** acid, **wood** poison.
