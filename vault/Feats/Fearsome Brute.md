# Fearsome Brute  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 151

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Edicts and Anathema|Hellknight]] (Level 8)

---

Fear makes your foes weak and more vulnerable to your attacks. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage rolls for Strikes against [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] creatures. The bonus is equal to double the target's frightened value.

 If you have master proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], increase the bonus to triple the target's frightened value.
