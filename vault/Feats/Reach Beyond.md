# Reach Beyond  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Ghost Eater]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost Eater Dedication]]

---

You've learned to move your spiritual energy past yourself to attack spirits where others can't. You can make melee Strikes against [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures that are inside solid objects as long as the object is within your reach. An incorporeal creature inside of a solid object is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], requiring you to succeed at a DC 11 flat check when you target it. You can spend an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to focus on a creature inside of a solid object. If you do, the next melee Strike you make this turn requires you to succeed at only a DC 6 flat check to affect the creature.
