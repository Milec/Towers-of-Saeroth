# Monstrous Peacemaker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dromaar]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 83

---

Your dual human and orc nature has given you a unique perspective, allowing you to bridge the gap between humans and the many intelligent creatures in the world that humans consider monsters. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks against non-humanoid intelligent creatures and against humanoids that are marginalized in human society (at the GM’s discretion, but typically at least including [[Bestiary/Families/Giant|giants]], [[Bestiary/Families/Goblin|goblins]], [[Bestiary/Families/Kobold|kobolds]], and [[Bestiary/Families/Orc|orcs]]). You also gain this bonus on [[Rules/Perception (rules-405)|Perception]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive|Sense the Motives]] of such creatures.
