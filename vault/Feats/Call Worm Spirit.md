# Call Worm Spirit two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

You call forth a ghostly manifestation of the worm spirit. Choose an intersection on the ground within 60 feet; the ghostly maw of a worm emerges from the ground, attacking all creatures in a 10 foot burst. Each creature in the area must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 1d10+13 bludgeoning damage as it is battered by the worm's body.  

**Failure** The target takes 3d10+15 piercing damage as it is bitten by the worm. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the damage increases to 6d10+30 and the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
