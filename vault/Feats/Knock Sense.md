# Knock Sense single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 83

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You attack carefully to knock some sense into a creature whose actions aren't their own. Make a Strike against a [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] ally. The Strike is [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] and doesn't take a –2 penalty for using a weapon without the nonlethal trait; if you hit and deal damage, your ally automatically succeeds at the flat check to remove the confused condition when taking damage.
