# Angel of Death  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 158

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Assassin]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Assassin Dedication]]

---

All your Strikes against a creature you have Marked for Death have the [[Traits/Death|death]] trait, causing the mark to be instantly killed when reduced to 0 Hit Points. When killed in this way, attempts to communicate with it, return it to life, turn it into an undead, or otherwise disturb its afterlife fail unless the effect's [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] level is higher than half your level when you killed the creature (rounded up), or originates from an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|artifact]] or a deity.
