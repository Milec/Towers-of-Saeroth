# Swashbuckler Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]], [[Traits/Multiclass]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 153

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Swashbuckler]]

**Prerequisites**
Charisma 14; Dexterity 14

---

Choose a [swashbuckler style](Styles.aspx). You gain the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-361)|panache]] class feature, and you can gain panache in all the ways a swashbuckler of your style can. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or the skill associated with your style. If you were already trained in both skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in swashbuckler class DC. You don't gain any other effects of your chosen style.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the swashbuckler archetype.
