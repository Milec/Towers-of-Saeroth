# Topple Foe ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 181

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
An ally succeeds at a melee Strike against an opponent you are both adjacent to.

---

You take advantage of the opening created by your ally to tip your foe off their feet. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip]] the target of the triggering attack.
