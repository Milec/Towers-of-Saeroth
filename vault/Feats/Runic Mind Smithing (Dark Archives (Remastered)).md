# Runic Mind Smithing  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mind Smith (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mind Smith Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Mind Smith Dedication]]

---

You focus your mind on thoughtforms of fundamental magical forces, shaping them into a property rune that you mentally etch onto your mind weapon. During your daily preparations, choose one rune from the following list of weapon property runes: [[Equipment/Corrosive (GM Core)|corrosive]], [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]], [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|frost]], [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]], [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]], and [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]]. You enhance your weapon with the chosen rune until your next daily preparations. This rune counts toward your maximum limit of runes as normal.
