# Tumbling Opportunist free action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 183

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication (Player Core 2)|Acrobat Dedication]]

**Requirements**
Your most recent action was to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] or [[Feats/Tumbling Strike (Player Core 2)|Tumbling Strike]], and you successfully moved through an enemy’s space.

---

You use a burst of stamina to perform a breathtaking feat of Acrobatics as you speed through a foe's space, leaving your foe lying flat on their back. You attempt to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] the enemy whose space you moved through. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] for this check.
