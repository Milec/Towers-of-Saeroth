# Elemental Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]], [[Traits/Ifrit]], [[Traits/Oread]], [[Traits/Suli]], [[Traits/Sylph]], [[Traits/Talos]], [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 99

---

You've devoted yourself to researching the secrets of the Inner Sphere. You gain the trained proficiency in your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] and either [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]. If you would automatically become trained in Survival (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] associated with your Elemental Plane of origin (such as [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire]] Lore).
