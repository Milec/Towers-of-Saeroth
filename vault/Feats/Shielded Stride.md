# Shielded Stride  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bastion]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Viking]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Bastion (Player Core 2)|Bastion]] (Level 6), [[Archetypes/Viking (Player Core 2)|Viking]] (Level 6)

---

When your shield is up, your enemies' blows can't touch you. When you have your shield raised, you can Stride to move half your Speed without triggering reactions that are triggered by your movement (such as [[Actions/Attack of Opportunity|Attacks of Opportunity]]). You can use Shielded Stride while [[Actions/Fly|Flying]] or [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
