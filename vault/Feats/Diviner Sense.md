# Diviner Sense  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 143

---

You sense magical dangers. When using the [[Actions/Detect Magic]] exploration activity, you can roll initiative twice and use the better result so long as at least one opponent has a [[Traits/Magical|magical]] effect or magic item and is in range of your [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]].
