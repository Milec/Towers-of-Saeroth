# Bounce Boost ⤾  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 21

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Trigger**
An adjacent ally attempts to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]].

---

You’ve learned to use your own athletic abilities and springy legs to help others who need a boost with their leaps. The triggering ally gains a +1 circumstance bonus to their [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. This bonus increases to +2 if you’re an expert in Athletics, +3 if you’re a master, and +4 if you’re legendary. Additionally, the triggering ally’s [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] distance on a successful check increases by 2 feet for a High Jump or 5 feet for a Long Jump, even if it would allow the ally to Leap further than their land Speed.
