# Tip the Scales  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 215

---

You’re a cunning adversary in close quarters and are well-versed in hit-and-run tactics that exploit the advantages of your small stature. As part of your training, you learned what to do when you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to Escape, and after resolving a successful [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] action, you can immediately use your reaction to [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] or to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] the creature from whom you just Escaped.
