# Sixth Sense  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 26

---

Your psychic abilities also allow you to detect the lingering thoughts of spirits and similar entities. While you're exploring but not [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]], the GM rolls a secret check for you to find haunts that usually require Searching, as well as spirits, creatures on the Ethereal Plane, and beings made entirely of spiritual essence, such as celestials, fiends, and monitors.

 You can also potentially notice ethereal creatures and spirits inside solid objects, provided they are within 30 feet of you. This applies while Searching, [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeking]], and on the automatic secret check from Sixth Sense. You can still notice spirits only on a successful check, and you can't see them if they're more than 5 feet inside an object.

 When you notice a creature with your Sixth Sense, you also learn its location, making it [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to you if it had been [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]].
