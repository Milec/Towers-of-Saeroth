# Living Nexus Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 96

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Living Nexus]]

**Prerequisites**
trained in martial weapons; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

Whether through happenstance or hours of rigorous training, you have discovered within yourself the ability to breathe in the magic around you.

You have an invisible, internal nexus of energy and have learned how to control your absorption of others’ magical energy and store it in this internal nexus. You gain the Absorb Magic reaction and the Overflowing Blade action. The DC for an ability gained via this archetype is either your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, and is called your nexus DC.

**Absorb Magic** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Requirements** You are not overflowing; **Trigger** Another creature within 30 feet [Casts a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) or uses an action, activity, or reaction with the [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] trait; **Effect** You breathe in a small fraction of the magic produced by the triggering effect, storing it in your internal nexus. You become overflowing. You remain overflowing for up to 1 minute before the stored magical energy fades. While overflowing, you gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects with the arcane, divine, magical, occult, and primal traits. This bonus becomes +2 at 15th level.

**Overflowing Blade** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Requirements** You are overflowing; **Effect** You conduct the magic in your internal nexus to coat your weapon or unarmed strike, empowering it with magical energy for a devastating attack. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If the Strike hits, you deal an additional 1d4 force damage. At 10th level, the additional damage increases to 2d4 force damage. At 18th level, the additional damage increases to 3d4 force damage. You are no longer overflowing.
