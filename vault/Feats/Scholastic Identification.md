# Scholastic Identification  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 156

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archaeologist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archaeologist Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]

---

You have the knowledge needed to understand ancient texts and cultural artifacts. You can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] when [[Actions/Decipher Writing|Deciphering Writing]], no matter the type of writing you are examining. You can also use Society to [[Actions/Identify Magic]] when examining a magic item or location with cultural significance.
