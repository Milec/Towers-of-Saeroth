# Pirate Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 209

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pirate (Player Core 2)|Pirate]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

---

As a pirate, you sail the seas in search of enemy ships to plunder and great adventures to embark on. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Sailing Lore or for a specific coastal city you have a connection to (such as Port Peril Lore). You ignore the effects of [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] or [[Rules/Uneven Ground (Player Core)|uneven ground]] caused by unstable ground (such as the deck of a ship). Additionally, you gain the Boarding Assault action.

 **Boarding Assault** two actions  ([[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|flourish]]) Either [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] twice or attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check (DC determined by the GM, but usually DC 20) to swing up to twice your Speed on a rope or similar object, then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If you boarded or disembarked from a boat or similar vehicle during this movement, the Strike deals one additional weapon damage die.
