# Distant Archer  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Centaur]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 32

---

You've trained to harry your foes from great distances, using your swiftness to maintain distance and keen eyesight to stay on target. You reduce the penalty for attacking beyond your first [[Traits/Range|range increment]] by 1, to a minimum of 0, when using weapons in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] weapon group.
