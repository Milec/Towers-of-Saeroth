# Glyph Familiar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 48

---

You have taken a living rune as a familiar to aid you in your adventures. You gain a [familiar](Familiars.aspx). You choose one familiar or master ability per day instead of two, but your familiar also always has three additional familiar abilities: [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct|construct]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier (Player Core)|flier]], and [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough (Player Core)|tough]].
