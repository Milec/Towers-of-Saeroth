# Lingering Echoes  *Feat 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 320

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This feat is not available for PFS play._

---

Not everyone is reincarnated, and you feel a strong connection with the spiritual echoes of those who didn’t receive the same opportunity as you. You gain [[Spells/Talking Corpse (Player Core)|_talking corpse_]] as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] that you can cast once per day. You can ask a fourth question of a corpse when you cast this spell. The corpse attempts its Will save against your spell DC or your class DC, whichever is higher.
