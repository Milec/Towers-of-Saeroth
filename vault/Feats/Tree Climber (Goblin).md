# Tree Climber (Goblin)  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tailed Goblin]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Treedweller Goblin]] heritage

---

Your time in forest or jungle canopies has taught you how to scramble across branches with sure feet. You gain a climb Speed of 10 feet. If you also have the [[Feats/Cave Climber]] ancestry feat, your total climb Speed increases to your land Speed when climbing trees.
