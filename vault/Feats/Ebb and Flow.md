# Ebb and Flow single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 123

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Versatile Font]]

---

You can pull forth both positive and negative energy simultaneously to harm your enemies and heal your allies. If your next action is to cast a 1-action or 2-action [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] or [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spell, choose one creature in range that would be harmed by the spell, and choose another creature within range that would be healed by the spell. Your _heal_ or _harm_ targets both creatures.
