# Get Used to Disappointment free action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bravado]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 168

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Requirements**
Your turn begins, and one target within 30 feet failed an attack roll or skill check against you on its last turn.

---

You capitalize on an opponent's failure with smug attitude and swagger, reminding them of the gap in skill between you and your opponent. You attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] the opponent. Regardless of the result, the target becomes temporarily immune for 1 minute.
