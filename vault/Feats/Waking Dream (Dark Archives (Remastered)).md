# Waking Dream  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 206

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Sleepwalker (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sleepwalker Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Sleepwalker Dedication]]

---

While in your trance, you can blur the line between dreams and reality for others. You gain the [[Spells/Waking Dream (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_waking dream_]] focus spell, which you can use only while you're in a Daydream Trance. You gain a pool of Focus Points with 1 Focus Point, and you can [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] by taking a nap for 10 minutes. You become trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics, and your spellcasting attribute for these spells is Wisdom.
