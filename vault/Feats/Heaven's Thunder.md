# Heaven's Thunder single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 78

**Archetype** [Jalmeri Heavenseeker](https://2e.aonprd.com/Archetypes.aspx?ID=86)

---

With a loud shout, you unleash your ki in a crackling shroud of thunder and lightning that engulfs your body. Until the end of your next turn, your unarmed attacks and weapons you wield that have the monk trait deal additional electricity damage equal to one-half your level and additional sonic damage equal to one-half your level.

 If you also know the [[Spells/Ki Blast|_ki blast_]] ki spell, you can choose to deal either electricity or sonic damage with the spell instead of force while this effect is active (_ki blast_ loses the [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] trait instead). Any creature who successfully [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapples]] you or is successfully Grappled by you also takes this damage immediately after the Grapple check is resolved.
