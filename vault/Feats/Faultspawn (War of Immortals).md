# Faultspawn  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage (Player Core)|Lineage]], [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 53

---

You bear a grudge, inherited from a blasphemous asura forebear in your bloodline, against a particular deity and their works. You gain the Find Fault reaction.

**Find Fault** ⤾ (concentrate) **Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against a spell or magic effect but haven’t rolled yet; Effect You find some kind of fault with the magic, using that flaw to protect yourself from the effect. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your saving throw against the triggering effect, which increases to a +2 circumstance bonus if the effect is [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] and originates from a worshipper of the deity you chose for your grudge.
