# Command Attention single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 166

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Celebrity]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication]]

---

You command the attention of all around you with style, ensuring their gaze falls only upon you until the end of your next turn. When creatures within 30 feet of you attempt saving throws against other [[Traits/Visual|visual]] effects, they use the outcome for one degree of success better than the result they rolled. An enemy within the area attempting to use a visual effect that involves focusing its attention on a particular creature (such as a [[Bestiary/Medusa|medusa's]] Focus Gaze) must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, in order to target any creature except you. Allies in the area can attempt to [[Actions/Hide]] even if they don't have cover, as you are continually providing a distraction.
