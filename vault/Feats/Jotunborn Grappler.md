# Jotunborn Grappler  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 13

---

Wrestling is a common pastime for jotunborn, and you’ve done your fair share. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]. If you would automatically become trained in Athletics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Titan Wrestler (Player Core)|Titan Wrestler]] skill feat.
