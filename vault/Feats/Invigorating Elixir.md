# Invigorating Elixir  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive (Player Core 2)|Additive]], [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 65

---

You can mix an aromatic salve into an elixir with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait to soothe physical maladies. In addition to its normal effects, the elixir can be imbibed by a creature prevented from doing so (such as a [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] creature). In addition to its other effects, the elixir attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] an effect imposing one of the following conditions of the imbiber's choice: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], sickened, or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]]. Use half your level rounded up for the counteract rank and your class DC – 10 for the counteract modifier. The imbiber is then temporarily immune to the effects of this additive for 10 minutes. The additive can't counteract [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curses]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]], or conditions that are part of the creature's normal state.
