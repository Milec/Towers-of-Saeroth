# Precious Arrow single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 173

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Elven Arrows|Eldritch Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eldritch Archer Dedication]]

---

You enchant a piece of ammunition with the magical essence of a precious material, allowing you to leverage certain creatures' weaknesses. Choose [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]; if you shoot the arrow before the end of your turn, it counts as that material. At 14th level, add [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] to the list of materials you can choose from.
