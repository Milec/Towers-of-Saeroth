# Assured Identification  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General (Player Core)|General]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 227

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

You rarely [[Rules/Cursed Items (GM Core)|misidentify]] an item. When using Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion checks to [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], if you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead. If you would misidentify a [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|cursed]] item because you roll a success but not a critical success, you simply can't identify it instead.
