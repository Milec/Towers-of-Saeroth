# Combat Grab single action  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 146

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Zombie]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]] (Level 4)

**Requirements**
You have one hand free, and your target is within reach of that hand.

---

You swipe at your opponent and grab at them. Make a melee Strike while keeping one hand free. If the Strike hits, you grab the target using your free hand. The creature remains [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] until the end of your next turn or until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]], whichever comes first.
