# Look but Don't Touch two actions  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 39

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Many flowers are beautiful to look upon but deadly to touch—and you're one of them. For 1 minute, any creature that touches you or damages you with a melee weapon without the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait or with a melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack takes 3d6 poison damage.
