# Augment Senses single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fleshwarp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 93

---

You open vestigial eyes, unfurl tympanic flaps of skin, or otherwise enhance your senses. Until the start of your next turn, you gain the following benefits: you can't be flanked; when you [[Actions/Seek]] for creatures, you can scan a 60-foot cone or a 30-foot burst instead of the normal area; and when you Seek for hidden objects, you can search a 15-foot square instead of the normal area.
