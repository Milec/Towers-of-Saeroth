# Claw and Talon Flow single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 69

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Clawdancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Clawdancer Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re in either claw stance or talon stance.

---

You fluidly shift between your two stances. You use the unarmed attack associated with your current clawdancer stance, then switch into the other stance (if you're in claw stance, make a frenzied claw Strike and then shift to talon stance, or vice versa).
