# Tiger Slash two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 123

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Martial Artist]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]] (Level 8), [[Archetypes/Martial Artist (Player Core 2)|Martial Artist]] (Level 8)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Tiger Stance (Player Core 2)|Tiger Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Tiger Stance.

---

You make a fierce swipe with both hands. Make a tiger claw Strike. It deals two extra weapon damage dice (three extra dice if you're 14th level or higher), and you can [[Rules/Forced Movement|push]] the target 5 feet away as if you had successfully [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] them. If the attack is a critical success and deals damage, add your Strength modifier to the [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] from your tiger claw.
