# Catfolk Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

---

You gain access to all uncommon weapons with the [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|catfolk]] trait. You have familiarity with weapons with the catfolk trait plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Kama (Tian Xia Character Guide)|kama]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri (Tian Xia Character Guide)|kukri]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|scimitar]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickle]]—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.

 At 5th level, whenever you get a critical hit with one of these weapons, you get its [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]].
