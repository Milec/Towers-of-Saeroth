# Elemental Trade  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oread]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 106

---

You have a strong connection with stone and metal. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat for both Stonemasonry and Blacksmithing.
