# Necromantic Deflection  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Druid]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 85

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You imbue your shield with life-giving energy to deflect harmful necromantic magic. When you have your shield raised, you gain the shield's circumstance bonus to your AC and saving throws against [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] spells. If you have the [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=1140) reaction, damage you take as a result of a necromancy spell can trigger that reaction, even if it isn't physical damage.
