Your in-depth knowledge of your foes grants you insight into their weaknesses, granting you an advantage when fighting against them. When you critically succeed at a skill check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about a devil or Hellknight, you gain a +4 status bonus to damage against that creature for 1 minute.
