# Exaltation Overwhelming three actions  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

---

Raising your weapon or fist aloft, you begin to gleam with dazzling radiance. You fulminate with power and glory, making it clear to all who see you that your legend is real and possibly even greater than purported. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]].  All enemies who can see you must succeed at a Will saving throw against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 3 (frightened 4 on a critical failure). You and any ally who can see you may choose to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] as a free action. Any of these Strikes are made at mythic proficiency.
