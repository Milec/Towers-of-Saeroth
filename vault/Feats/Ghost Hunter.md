# Ghost Hunter  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Duskwalker]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 38

---

Your connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] ensures that your blows strike true against spectral beings. Your weapon and unarmed attack Strikes against [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures become [[Traits/Magical|magical]]. If they are already magical, they instead gain the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.
