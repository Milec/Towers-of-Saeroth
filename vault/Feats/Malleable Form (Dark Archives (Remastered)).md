# Malleable Form single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Reflection (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
ability to cast at least one [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] spell

**Requirements**
You're [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorphed]] by a spell that has multiple choices of battle form.

---

You adroitly shift your form, taking on another appearance. Choose a different battle form of the [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] spell affecting you, and change to that form instead of the current one. This destabilizes the spell's magic, reducing its duration by 1 round. Use the rank of the spell to determine the statistics, as normal.
