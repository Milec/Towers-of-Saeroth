# Deflecting Pulse  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ostilli Host Dedication]]

---

Your ostilli can use its stored magic to protect you from other magic, granting you the Turn Aside Ambient Magic action.

 **Turn Aside Ambient Magic** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) Your ostilli glows a faint yellow as it establishes a barrier. Choose acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain resistance against the chosen damage type equal to half your level.
