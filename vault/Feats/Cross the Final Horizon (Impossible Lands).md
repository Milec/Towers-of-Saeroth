# Cross the Final Horizon three actions  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 225

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Jalmeri Heavenseeker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sky and Heaven Stance (Impossible Lands)|Sky and Heaven Stance]]

**Requirements**
You are in Sky and Heaven Stance.

---

You Stride up to your Speed, and your ki enshrouds your limbs in terrifying stormy energy, increasing the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] of your Sky and Heaven Stance [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks by 5 feet. At the end of your movement, make up to three Strikes against a target you can reach, each at a –2 penalty. Each attack counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but the multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until after you've made all your attacks. Instead of the usual slashing or piercing damage, the damage for these Strikes is either electricity or sonic, as you choose with each Strike. If you successfully hit with all three Strikes, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 3]].
