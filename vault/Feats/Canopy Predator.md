# Canopy Predator  *Feat 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Verduran Shadow Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

---

You are as at home in the treetops as you are beneath their branches. You gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 15 feet when moving through trees, vines, and other foliage. Whenever you succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] a tree or an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] on a branch, you get a critical success instead. You aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while Climbing or Balancing on a tree.
