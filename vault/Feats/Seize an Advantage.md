# Seize an Advantage single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 93

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dirty Trick]]

**Access**
You're from the Shackles

**Requirements**
Your last action this turn was to attempt a [[Feats/Dirty Trick]] against a foe.

---

You take any momentary advantage you can when fighting dirty. You make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] or [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|Reload]]; doing so does not trigger any reactions from the required foe. If you succeeded or critically succeeded on your Dirty Trick, making ranged attacks and Interacting to Reload do not trigger reactions from that foe for as long as they remain [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] because of your Dirty Trick.
