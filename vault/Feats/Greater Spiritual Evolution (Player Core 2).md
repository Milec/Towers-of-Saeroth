# Greater Spiritual Evolution  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sorcerer (Player Core 2)|Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 155

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Divine Evolution (Player Core 2)|Divine Evolution]] or [[Feats/Occult Evolution (Player Core 2)|Occult Evolution]]

---

Your magical blood allows your spells to be fully effective against [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures. Your spells have the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch property rune]]. They can target or affect a creature projecting its consciousness (such as via [[Spells/Project Image (Player Core)|_project image_]]) or possessing another creature, even if its body is elsewhere, though you must know about the possession or projection and choose to do so. Your spells can affect creatures on the Ethereal Plane, though this doesn't grant you the ability to locate them.
