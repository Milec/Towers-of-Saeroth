# Azure Fins  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Worm Caller]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Worm Caller Dedication]]

---

When submerged in water or another liquid, you form small fin-like arms along your body, granting you far greater maneuverability while swimming. You gain a swim Speed equal to your land Speed. Furthermore, while swimming on the surface of a liquid, you can [[Actions/Leap]] out of the liquid and do not fall until the end of your turn, allowing you to spend the rest of the turn above the surface.
