# Push it  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 55

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Using Vehicles (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trick Driver]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Trick Driver Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Trick Driver Dedication]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Driving Lore]]

---

You can push your [vehicle's](Vehicles.aspx) performance beyond the limits envisioned by its makers. Whenever you succeed at a piloting check, the vehicle you are piloting gains a +5-foot circumstance bonus to all its Speeds until the end of your turn. If you critically succeed, this bonus increases to +10 feet.
