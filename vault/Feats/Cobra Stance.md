# Cobra Stance single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 128

---

You enter a tight stance, coiled up like a lashing cobra with your hands poised as venomous fangs. While in this stance, the only Strikes you can make are cobra fang unarmed attacks. These deal 1d4 poison damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d10]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

 While in Cobra Stance, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Fortitude saves and your Fortitude DC, and you gain poison resistance equal to half your level.
