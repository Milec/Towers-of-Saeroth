# Jotunborn Weapon Familiarity  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Jotunborn]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 13

---

You gain access to all uncommon weapons with the jotunborn trait. You have familiarity with weapons with the jotunborn trait plus the [bola](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=433), [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe (Player Core)|greataxe]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Halberd (Player Core)|halberd]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Maul (Player Core)|maul]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspear]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/War Flail (Player Core)|war flail]]—for the purpose of proficiency, you treat any of these that are martial weapons as simple weapons and any that are advanced weapons as martial weapons.
