# Elbow Breaker single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 126

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

---

You bend your opponent's body or limbs into agonizing positions that make it difficult for them to maintain their grip. Make an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee Strike against the creature you have grabbed or restrained. This Strike has the following effects in addition to its usual effects.

**Critical Success** You knock one held item out of the creature's grasp. It falls to the ground in the creature's space.  

**Success** You weaken your opponent's grasp on one held item. Until the start of that creature's turn, attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]] the opponent of that item gain a +2 circumstance bonus, and the target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attacks with the item or other checks requiring a firm grasp on the item.
