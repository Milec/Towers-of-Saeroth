# Spirit of the Blade single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

---

You charge your blade with spiritual energy, allowing it to cut through [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirits]] and [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]] with fearsome efficiency. The next [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] you make with a melee weapon or fist unarmed attack deals an additional 1d6 spirit damage. This bonus damage is lost if you don’t attempt a Strike before the start of your next turn.

At 10th level, you deal an additional 2d6 spirit damage with this ability, and at 18th level, you deal an additional 3d6 spirit damage.
