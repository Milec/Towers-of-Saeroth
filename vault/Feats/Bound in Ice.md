# Bound in Ice  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 11

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbound Initiate]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Frostbite Runes]]

---

You become a hybrid of your ancestry and a saumen kar. Your size increases to Large, and your reach becomes 10 feet. Your [[Traits/Cold|cold]] resistance increases to be equal to your level, and you gain [[Traits/Fire|fire]] resistance equal to half your level.

 You also gain the Blizzard Evasion reaction.

 **Blizzard Evasion** ⤾ **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take 40 or more physical damage from a single attack; **Effect** You discorporate into a whirling blizzard for up to 3 rounds. During this time, you gain resistance 10 to physical damage and weakness 10 to [[Traits/Force|force]] damage. You gain a fly speed of 40 feet, but the only action you can take is to [[Actions/Fly]]. The first time each round you enter another creature's space while discorporated, or the first time a creature enters your space, that creature takes 4d8 cold damage and must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or else be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]]. At the start of your turn, you can end this effect as a free action.
