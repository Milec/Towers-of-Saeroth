# Sense Alignment three actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Secret]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Edgewatch Detective]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Edgewatch Detective Dedication]]

---

You read the body language of an intelligent creature within 30 feet and pick up on subtle cues to detect its general propensity for good or evil and law or chaos. The GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] Perception check, comparing the result against the target's [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] DC.

**Critical Success** You ascertain the target's alignment.  

**Success** You ascertain the target's alignment, but only along one axis (your choice of either the good-evil axis or the lawful-chaotic axis).  

**Failure** You do not learn the target's alignment.  

**Critical Failure** You incorrectly ascertain the target's alignment, misidentifying their alignment along either one axis or both (GM's choice).
