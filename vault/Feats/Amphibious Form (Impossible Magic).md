# Amphibious Form  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 79

---

Your eidolon adapts to life on land and underwater. They gain the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait, allowing them to breathe in water and air and to avoid the normal –2 penalty for making bludgeoning and slashing unarmed Strikes underwater. They gain a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] equal to their land Speed or 25 feet, whichever is less; if they’re normally aquatic, they gain a land Speed equal to their swim Speed or 25 feet, whichever is less.
