# Crosscurrent Counter ⤾  *Feat 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 83

**Trigger**
You become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by a creature.

---

You counter the creature with the power of elemental water, a tendril of it encasing your limb or weapon. You attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the triggering creature, even if it’s outside your reach or you don’t have a free hand. If you succeed, you also remove the triggering condition, and the tendril of water pulls the creature adjacent to you before dissipating.
