# Ashen Veil three actions  *Feat 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]; Master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

---

By smearing ash or tainted earth over your skin and armor, you shroud yourself from the dead. You become concealed to [[Traits/Undead|undead]] using vision or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]], allowing you to [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] without other sources of [[Rules/Cover|cover]] or concealment. This effect lasts only as long as you continue to take no actions other than to Hide, Sneak, or other surreptitious actions. The GM determines which actions end the effect, but attacking, casting spells, activating items, and the like always do.
