# Disarming Intercept free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 43

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Intercept Attack]]

**Trigger**
You Intercept an Attack that was made with a melee weapon by a creature you’re adjacent to.

---

When you catch a weapon in your armor, you can move your body to wrench it from your foe's grasp. After [[Actions/Intercept Attack|Intercepting the Attack]], attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the weapon used for that attack. You don't need to have a hand free, and you gain an item bonus to the Athletics check equal to the value of your armor's potency rune.
