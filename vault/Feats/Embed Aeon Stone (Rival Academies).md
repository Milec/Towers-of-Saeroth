# Embed Aeon Stone  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Downtime (Player Core)|Downtime]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 116

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Runelord (Rival Academies)|Runelord]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]; [[Feats/Runelord Dedication (Rival Academies)|Runelord Dedication]]

---

You discover the secrets to embedding _[[Equipment/Aeon Stone (GM Core)|aeon stones]]_ into your flesh. You spend 1 day attuning to an _aeon stone_ and physically embedding it in your skin. While the stone is embedded this way, you gain the benefits of the _aeon stone_ as if it were orbiting above your head, but it protects the stone from being noticed or stolen as easily. _Aeon stones_ in your flesh must be invested to function, as usual.

 You can also use this activity to safely remove an embedded _aeon stone_ in 1 day. Someone without this feat can attempt to surgically remove it safely by spending 1 day and succeeding at a DC 30 Medicine check, or hastily by simply ripping it from a corpse.
