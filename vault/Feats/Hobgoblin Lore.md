# Hobgoblin Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 50

---

You’ve studied traditional hobgoblin exercises and fieldcraft, all of which have a militaristic bent. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. For each of these skills in which you were already trained, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You also become trained in Hobgoblin [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]].
