# Pain is Temporary two actions  *Feat 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 80

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

Sangpotshi practitioners know that suffering is a fleeting sensation that ultimately strengthens the soul and prepares it for a new reincarnation. Bolstered by this understanding, you've learned to temporarily steel yourself against all forms of pain. You gain resistance 10 to all damage until the end of your next turn.
