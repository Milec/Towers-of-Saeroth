# Germination of Resolve free action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaksha]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 81

**Frequency**
once per day

---

Vitality floods your trammeled soul, offering you rejuvenation, if not quite rest. For each effect that causes the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] conditions to affect you, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check to counteract that effect. The counteract rank of Germination of Resolve is equal to half your level rounded up.
