# Brastlewark Snare Engineering  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 72

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Snarecrafter (Player Core 2)|Snarecrafter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Snarecrafter Dedication (Player Core 2)|Snarecrafter Dedication]]; [[Feats/Snare Crafting (Player Core 2)|Snare Crafting]]

---

You've trained with the Brastlewark gnomes or studied their teachings, and learned the art of crafting their more magical snares. More information on magical snares can be found [here](Equipment.aspx?Category=31&Subcategory=120). You learn the formulas for crafting the _[[Equipment/Pit Illusion Snare|pit illusion snare]]_ and the _[[Equipment/Shadow Cloak Snare|shadow cloak snare]]_ or two uncommon magical snares of your level or lower that you have access to. When you set a magical snare, the DC of any saving throw it requires uses your spell DC if that's higher. When you craft a non-magical snare, you can choose to give it the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] trait. When you do so and the snare is one that normally deals damage, it deals an additional amount of force damage equal to its level.
