# Emotional Partitions  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kashrishi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 43

---

You have created strong mental partitions in your mind to filter out negative emotional influences. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion effect, you get a critical success instead.
