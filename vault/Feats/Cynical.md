# Cynical  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Azarketi]]

**Source** [[Sources/Absalom, City of Lost Omens]] pg. 394, [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 4

---

You've been approached with suspicion and distrust throughout your life and return these sentiments back to the strangers you encounter. As a result, you are difficult to deceive. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC against [[Actions/Lie|Lies]] and [[Actions/Impersonate|Impersonations]].
