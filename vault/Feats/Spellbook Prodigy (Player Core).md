# Spellbook Prodigy  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 201

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]]

---

You are particularly adept at learning spells to add to your spellbook. You gain the [[Feats/Magical Shorthand (Player Core)|Magical Shorthand]] skill feat without needing to meet its prerequisites. Further, when you roll a critical failure on your check to [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]], you get a failure instead.
