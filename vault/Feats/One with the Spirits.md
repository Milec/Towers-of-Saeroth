# One with the Spirits single action  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 294

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Rivethun Invoker]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Rivethun Invoker Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You’re under the effects of Enter Spirit Trance.

---

The spirits you’ve invoked throughout your life have left behind echoes and spiritual fragments, which you can manifest across your own flesh, temporarily transforming you into something more akin to spirit than mortal. For 1 minute, you gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 5 and you become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]]. You can’t use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]]. Your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] gain the benefits of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune. Whenever you deal damage with a Strike, you can choose to have that Strike deal [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, rather than its usual damage type.

If you would be knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] or your [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] value would increase while One with the Spirits is active, the spirits revive you at the beginning of your next turn, restoring you to 30 Hit Points. One with the Spirits then immediately ends.
