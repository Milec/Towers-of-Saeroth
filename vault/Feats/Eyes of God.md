# Eyes of God  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 282

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mortal Herald]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mortal Herald Dedication]]

---

You can see things as they truly are. You are constantly under the effect of 8th-rank [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]] and [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] spells. Additionally, you gain a +4 status bonus to your [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] checks and to your [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] DC against [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lies]].
