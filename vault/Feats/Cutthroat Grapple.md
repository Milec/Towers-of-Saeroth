# Cutthroat Grapple ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 65

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sawtooth Grapple]]

**Access**
member of the Red Mantis assassins

**Trigger**
A creature attempts to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] from your Sawtooth Grapple.

---

You can punish a creature that tries to escape your blades. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against the triggering creature. On a hit, the creature takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] attempt and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]] damage.
