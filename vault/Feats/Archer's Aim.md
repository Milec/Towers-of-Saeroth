# Archer's Aim two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 157

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Archer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Archer Dedication]]

---

You slow down, focus, and take a careful shot. Make a ranged Strike with a weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] weapon group. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], reduce the flat check from being hidden from 11 to 5.
