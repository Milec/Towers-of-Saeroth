# Tactile Magic Feedback  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 70

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ostilli Host Dedication]]

---

Your ostilli can sense nearby spellcasters. You gain an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense known as spellsense, which has a range of 60 feet and detects only creatures capable of casting spells (including creatures with innate spells). You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] checks about creatures you're detecting with spellsense.
