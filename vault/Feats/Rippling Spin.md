# Rippling Spin ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 202

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Reflective Ripple Stance]]

**Trigger**
You are hit by a physical melee attack by an attacker you can see that's in reach.

**Requirements**
You're in Reflective Ripple Stance.

---

After the triggering attack is done, Step. You must end this Step within the attacker's reach. Then, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]] or [[Actions/Trip]] the attacker.
