# Burn It!  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 48

---

Fire fascinates you. Your spells and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical items]] that deal fire damage gain a status bonus to damage equal to half the spell's level or one-quarter the item's level (minimum 1). You also gain a +1 status bonus to any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] you deal.
