# Topple Foe ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Trigger**
An ally succeeds at a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against an enemy you are both adjacent to.

---

You take advantage of the opening created by your ally to tip your foe off their feet. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] the target of the triggering attack.
