# Fey Cantrips  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Sprite]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 130

---

You've learned the cantrips sprites have used since time immemorial. You gain [[Spells/Dancing Lights|_dancing lights_]] and [[Spells/Ghost Sound|_ghost sound_]] as primal innate cantrips. If you have the grig heritage, you also gain [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]], and if you have the draxie heritage, you gain [[Spells/Prestidigitation|_prestidigitation_]].
