# True Transmogrification  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 73

---

You transmogrify your eidolon each day. During your daily preparations, you can swap out any number of your [[Traits/Evolution|evolution]] feats, each for a different evolution feat of an appropriate level for which you meet the prerequisites. These swaps last until your next daily preparations.
