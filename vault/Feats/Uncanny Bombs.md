# Uncanny Bombs  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 79

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Far Lobber]]

---

You lob bombs unerringly, despite obstructions or distance. When you throw an alchemical item with the [[Traits/Bomb|bomb]] trait, its range increment increases to 60 feet, you reduce any circumstance bonus to the target’s AC from cover by 1, and you automatically succeed at the flat check when targeting a concealed creature.
