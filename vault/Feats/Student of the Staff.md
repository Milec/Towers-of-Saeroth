# Student of the Staff  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 45

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twisting Tree|twisting tree hybrid study]]

---

You've studied the staff extensively to learn the hidden capabilities of your chosen weapon. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using a staff, you apply the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]] of the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]] group. While you're in Arcane Cascade stance, your staff gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d6]] trait, with the damage from the deadly die being the same damage type as the extra damage from Arcane Cascade.

 In addition, you can place [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|property runes]] into a magic staff you prepare, even though a magic staff can't typically hold property runes. You must first inscribe the property runes into your [[Equipment/Spellbook (Blank)|spellbook]], using the same process as [[Rules/Transferring Runes|transferring a rune]]. The rune takes the form of a specialized diagram in your spellbook, occupying one page. You can't inscribe runes that can't be placed on a non-magical staff, nor can you inscribe the [[Equipment/Shifting|_shifting_]] rune. When you prepare a staff, you can impart any number of property runes from your spellbook into the staff, up to the limit imposed by the staff's [[Equipment/Weapon Potency|_weapon potency_]] rune.
