# Scroll Trickster Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication (Player Core)|Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 213

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Scroll Trickster (Player Core 2)|Scroll Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]

---

You've studied [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|scrolls]] in depth. This might have been a comprehensive education in a formal setting or the sort of education where you somehow obtain a number of scrolls and try not to explode anything you didn't mean to explode. You gain the [[Feats/Trick Magic Item (Player Core)|Trick Magic Item]] feat, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks to Trick scrolls. If you roll a critical failure to Trick a Magic Item that's a scroll, you get a failure instead.
