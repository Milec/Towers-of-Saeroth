# Hardened Chassis  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Automaton]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 111

---

Your exterior is so tough that only the strongest of weapons can fully damage it. Choose one type of damage among bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 3 to that type of damage. This resistance does not apply if the weapon or attack is [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]].

**Enhancement** Your chassis gets even tougher. Choose one of the following benefits: you gain resistance 3 to all physical damage, or your chosen resistance increases to a value equal to half your level (minimum 3). This resistance is still bypassed by adamantine.
