# True Transmogrification  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 85

---

You transmogrify your eidolon each day. During your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], you can swap out any number of your evolution feats, each for a different evolution feat of an appropriate level for which you meet the prerequisites. These swaps last until your next daily preparations.
