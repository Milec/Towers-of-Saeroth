# Titan Swing two actions  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 101

---

Your limbs tense as you deliver a mighty swing. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If it hits, you deal an extra die of weapon damage and push the target back by 5 feet. If you’re at least 10th level, increase this to two extra dice and push the target by 10 feet, and if you’re at least 18th level, increase it to three extra dice and push the target by 15 feet.
