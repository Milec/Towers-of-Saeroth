# Reactive Charm ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Captivator (Impossible Magic)|Captivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Captivator Dedication (Impossible Magic)|Captivator Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You are the target of a spell or attack.

---

You’re so charming and distracting that when pressed, you can cast a simple charm in time to foil an attacker. You can cast one of your [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate 1st-rank spells]] with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait that normally takes two actions or fewer to cast as a reaction. This takes effect before the triggering spell or attack, and if the attacker is affected by your spell, it can make different decisions for its attack or spell (for instance, if you charm the attacker, it can choose a different target than you for its attack or spell). The attacking creature must be in range of your chosen spell, and if your spell requires targets, the attacking creature must be a valid target. Your spell affects only the attacking creature, even if it would normally affect more targets or an area.
