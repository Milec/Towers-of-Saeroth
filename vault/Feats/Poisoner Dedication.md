# Poisoner Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 186

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]

---

You make a point of always having a few toxins near at hand—you never know when some arsenic or the like might prove useful. You gain the [[Rules/Alchemical Archetypes|basic alchemy benefits]], though they can be used only for [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical poisons]]. You gain batches of infused reagents per day equal to your level. Your advanced alchemy level for creating these is 1 and doesn't increase on its own.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained at least two other feats from the poisoner archetype.
