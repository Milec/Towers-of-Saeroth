# Mummy's Despair two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 52

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Mummy]]

**Frequency**
once per hour

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Mummy Dedication]]

---

You force your mental anguish outward, projecting it upon those around you. You gain an aura of despair in a 30-foot emanation lasting 5 rounds. A creature that enters or begins its turn in the aura must succeed at a Will save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at the save is temporarily immune to Mummy's Despair for 10 minutes.
