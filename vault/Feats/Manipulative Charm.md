# Manipulative Charm  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 58

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Vampire Vulnerabilities|Vampire]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Vampire Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]

---

Against [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie]], and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Gather Information]] and [[Actions/Make an Impression]].

 Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Charm|_charm_]] as a divine innate spell using your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Casting it requires staring into the target's eyes, giving the spell the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait. At 5th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the spell is heightened by an additional level, to a maximum of a 9th-level charm when you are 19th level.
