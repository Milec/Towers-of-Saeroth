# Divert Streamflow ⤾  *Feat 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 80

**Trigger**
You’re damaged by a melee Strike by a creature in your reach.

---

With a brief gesture, you adapt swiftly to an attack like a flowing stream. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Reposition]] the creature who dealt the triggering damage. If you’re successful, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action.
