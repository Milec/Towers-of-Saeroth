# High-Speed Regeneration free action  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 13, [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 101

**Trigger**
Your turn begins

---

Your wounds knit together with barely a thought. You regain Hit Points equal to double your level. When you gain this deviation, the GM secretly selects one type of energy damage or precious material, such as [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] or [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]]. When you take damage of that type, your wound smokes until the end of your next turn, preventing you from using High-Speed Regeneration.
