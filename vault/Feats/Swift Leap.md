# Swift Leap single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 49

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying a Ghoul|Ghoul]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghoul Dedication]]

---

Your undead physiology allows you to leap quickly toward or away from your opponent. You [[Actions/Leap]]. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.

 If you are satiated, you can choose to end your satiation to instead [[Actions/High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump]] without triggering reactions, as you take a quick, enormous jump before any foe can react to your movement.
