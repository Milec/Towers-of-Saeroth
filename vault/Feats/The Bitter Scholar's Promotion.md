# The Bitter Scholar's Promotion  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 115

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Cultivator]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Ghost-path Epiphany]]

---

Tian Xia’s shining cities belie its empires’ long shadow of death, darkened by thousands of years of suffering and injustice. You pursue immortality not through tuft-hunting with Heaven’s dragons or their imperial brats; yours is the power to crack its corrupt wheel, for you can now release the ghost gates to expedite the dead’s revenge at being cheated of their fates.

You cease aging and, regardless of your actual existential state, now register as an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] to effects that can detect undead (such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]] or spiritsense). In addition, you learn [[Rituals/Create Undead (Player Core)|_create undead_]] rituals for [[Bestiary/Gashadokuro|gashadokuro]], [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire, Jiang-Shi|jiangshi]], and [[Bestiary/Shui Gui|shui gui]]. On a success with one of these rituals, you gain the effects of a critical success instead.
