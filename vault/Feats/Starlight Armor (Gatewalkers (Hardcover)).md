# Starlight Armor three actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 239

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Oatia Skysage Dedication (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage Dedication]]

---

You armor yourself in the light of distant suns, shielding yourself from harm. The armor grants you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 7 to piercing, bludgeoning, and slashing damage. The armor also sheds [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] for 20 feet (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet).

Whenever an adjacent creature attacks you, the attacker must attempt a Will save against your spell DC at the end of its action. On a failure, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn. Regardless of the result of the save, the attacker is temporarily immune until the end of its next turn. The dazzling effect has the [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]] and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] traits.

When you reach 17th level, the resistance increases to 10.
