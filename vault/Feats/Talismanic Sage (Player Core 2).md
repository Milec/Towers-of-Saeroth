Normally, affixing more than one [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talisman]] to an item causes the talismans to be suppressed, but when you [[Actions/Affix a Talisman (GM Core)|Affix a Talisman]], you can specially treat one item you're working on, allowing it to have two active talismans at once. This special treatment ends if you use Affix a Talisman to treat a new item for this ability.
