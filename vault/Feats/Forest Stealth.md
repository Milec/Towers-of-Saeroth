# Forest Stealth single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elf]]

Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

**Requirements**
You are in a forest or jungle environment near an environmental feature that would allow you to Take Cover.

---

You are skilled at quickly hiding behind bits of underbrush or foliage. You [[Actions/Take Cover]] and then use that cover to Hide.
