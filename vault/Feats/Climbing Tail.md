# Climbing Tail  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vanara]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 55

---

Your tail makes it much easier for you to climb. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb]], you gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat, and you reduce the number of free hands required to Climb or [[Actions/Trip]] by one.
