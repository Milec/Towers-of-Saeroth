# Steal Vitality  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 125

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Fruits of Research|Spell Trickster]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spell Trickster Dedication]]; ability to cast [[Spells/Vampiric Touch|_vampiric touch_]]

---

When you cast _vampiric touch_, you can modify its standard effects as follows: Instead of gaining temporary Hit Points, if your target fails or critically fails its saving throw, you can attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check to remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] conditions on yourself, using the source of that condition to determine the counteract level and DC. If the condition was caused by an ongoing effect and you don't remove that effect, the condition returns at the end of your next turn.
