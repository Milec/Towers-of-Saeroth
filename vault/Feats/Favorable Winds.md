# Favorable Winds single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Changeling]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 23

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Virga May]]

---

You wrap a shell of favorable wind around a weapon you wield. On your next Strike with a ranged weapon before the start of your next turn, you don't take circumstance penalties on the attack roll due to wind. In addition, if that weapon has a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 10 feet or less, increase its range increment by 10 feet for that Strike.
