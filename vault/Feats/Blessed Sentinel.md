You infuse a weapon with the blessing of [[Setting/Deities/Ketephys]] and attack. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. This Strike gains the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait and deals an additional 1d4 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, or 2d4 spirit damage against [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]]. This counts as two attacks for your multiple attack penalty.
