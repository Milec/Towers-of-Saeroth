# To Battle! single action or two actions  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 205

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Marshal (Player Core 2)|Marshal]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Marshal Dedication (Player Core 2)|Marshal Dedication]]

---

With a resounding cry, you rally your ally to take the offensive. Choose one ally in your marshal’s aura who has a reaction available. If you spend 1 action, that ally can use their reaction to immediately [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. If you spend 2 actions, that ally can use their reaction to immediately [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
