# Define the Canvas two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 52

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You produce rune-inscribed stones, which whirl in the air before darting away to form a perimeter for a runic canvas. The canvas is a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] on the ground centered on a corner in your space. As long as you’re in or adjacent to the canvas, you can [[Actions/Trace Rune|Trace a Rune]] directly into the ground, which causes the rune to expand across the entire space, tracing it onto any number of eligible targets within the area. The canvas lasts for 1 minute.
