# Ceaseless Shadows  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Halfling]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 53

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Distracting Shadows]]

---

You excel at going unnoticed, especially among a crowd. You no longer need to have cover or be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]]. If you would have lesser cover from creatures, you gain cover and can [[Actions/Take Cover]], and if you would have cover from creatures, you gain greater cover.
