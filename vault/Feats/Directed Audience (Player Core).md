# Directed Audience  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 101

---

You can shape the area of your [[Traits/Composition (Player Core)|composition]] spells. Whenever you cast a composition spell whose area is an [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], you can change the spell’s area to a cone 10 feet larger, to a maximum of twice the original area. For example, you could modify a composition spell that affects a 30-foot emanation to affect a 40-foot cone, but a 5-foot emanation could only become a 10-foot cone.
