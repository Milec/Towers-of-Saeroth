# Urban Jungle  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Awakened Animal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 26

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fascinated by Society]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] or Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

---

Cities are just a new form of wilderness. You've adapted to this wilderness and now can navigate it as easily as where you once came from. When in an urban area, you can move at your full Speed while you [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] and you can use your choice of Survival or Society to Track.
