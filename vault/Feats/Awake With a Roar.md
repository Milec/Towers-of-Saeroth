# Awake With a Roar free action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 107

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/World Rouser]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/World Rouser Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You Rouse the World.

---

When you Rouse the World, it awakens with a mighty yawp that stuns the unsuspecting. All enemies in the area must attempt a Will save against your world rouser DC. A creature that attempts this save or observes this ability becomes temporarily immune to it for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature becomes frightened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes frightened 3 and stunned 1.
