# Shrink Down single action  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution (Impossible Magic)|Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 81

---

Your eidolon can shrink to fit into small spaces. Your eidolon reduces to a smaller size of your choosing, to a minimum of size Small. They can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this action to return to their normal size. Their reach becomes 5 feet, or 10 feet if shrinking to Large with Towering Size.
