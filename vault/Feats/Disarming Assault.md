# Disarming Assault single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Rage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 110

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

You attack with enough force to knock the weapon out of your foe's hands. Make a melee Strike; if you hit, you can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]] that foe.
