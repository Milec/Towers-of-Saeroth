# Soaring Dynamo  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 53

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Prosthetics in Golarion|Sterling Dynamo]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Sterling Dynamo Dedication]]

---

You’ve built clockwork wings or jets into your dynamo, allowing you to use your prosthesis to fly. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. Each minute of flight reduces the operational time of your sterling dynamo by 1 hour, measured from the first time you use the [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] action in a given minute.
