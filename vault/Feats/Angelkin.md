# Angelkin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aasimar]], [[Traits/Lineage]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 35

---

You descend from an [[Bestiary/Families/Angel|angel]]—a winged messenger from [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana]] or one of the other celestial realms—which gives you a knack for cultures and languages. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]. If you would automatically become trained in Society (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You know the [[Setting/Languages/Celestial]] language, and you gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat.
