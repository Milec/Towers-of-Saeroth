# Fresh Ingredients  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 176

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Herbalist Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]

---

When using [[Feats/Natural Medicine]] to [[Actions/Treat Wounds]], you can spend a batch of herbs to gain the +2 circumstance bonus from having fresh ingredients, even if not in wilderness. If you spend a batch of herbs in wilderness, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus instead.
