# Spore Cloud two actions  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 43

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

You unleash a cloud of pollen or similar spores. All creatures in a 10-foot emanation around you must attempt a Fortitude save using your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) with the following results. [[Traits/Plant|Plants]] and [[Traits/Fungus|fungi]] are immune to this effect.

**Critical Success** No effect.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled and can see only 10 feet away for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.
