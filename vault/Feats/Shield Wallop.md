# Shield Wallop single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Guardian]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 45

**Requirements**
You are wearing a shield.

---

Attacks with your shield knock the sense out of your foes. Make a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Bash (Player Core)|shield bash]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|shield boss]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]] Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until the start of your next turn (stupefied 2 on a critical hit). If your shield is a [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|tower shield]], [[Equipment/Shields/Fortress Shield (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|fortress shield]], or another shield that grants a higher circumstance bonus to AC when you [[Actions/Take Cover (Player Core)|Take Cover]] behind it, the creature is instead stupefied 2 if you hit and deal damage to it (stupefied 3 on a critical hit).
