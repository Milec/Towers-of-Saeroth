# Blowgun Poisoner  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 63

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Poisoner (Player Core 2)|Poisoner]] (Level 4), [[Archetypes/Guerrilla]] (Level 4)

---

You can capably deliver toxins with a [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun (Player Core)|blowgun]]. Your blowgun [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] can apply [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] even if they deal no damage due to a creature's resistance. If you critically succeed at a blowgun Strike using a poisoned dart, the target's initial save against the poison is one degree of success worse than the creature rolls; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect.

 In addition, if you make a blowgun Strike while [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], you don't automatically become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]]. Instead, immediately attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check against the Perception DC of the target. If you succeed, you don't become observed, and are hidden (if you were undetected, you still become hidden rather than remaining undetected).
