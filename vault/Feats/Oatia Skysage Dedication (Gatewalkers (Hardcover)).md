# Oatia Skysage Dedication  *Feat 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 238

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Oatia Skysage (Gatewalkers (Hardcover))|Oatia Skysage]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

---

Your study of the stars unlocks supernatural insights and magic. You either become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Astronomy Lore]] or an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]].

You learn to cast [[Rules/Spontaneous Spells (Player Core)|spontaneous spells]] and gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity. You gain a spell repertoire with two of the following cantrips of your choice: [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]], [[Spells/Guidance (Player Core)|_guidance_]], [[Spells/Know the Way|_know the way_]], or [[Spells/Read Aura (Player Core)|_read aura_]]. You cast these spells as occult spells as you draw on the stars and night sky. You’re trained in occult spell attack rolls and spell DCs. Your key spellcasting ability for these spells is Intelligence.
