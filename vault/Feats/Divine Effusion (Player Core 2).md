# Divine Effusion  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 143

---

The power of your mystery surges through you like a font of [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] energy, even when your own magic dwindles. Twice per day, you can cast a spell after you've run out of spell slots of the appropriate spell rank; the two spells you cast with this feat must be of different spell ranks.
