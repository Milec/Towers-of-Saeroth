# Swift Intervention ⤾  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 50

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Overwatch Training|Overwatch]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Overwatch Dedication]]

**Trigger**
An ally within your overwatch field fails in their attempt to [[Actions/Arrest a Fall (Player Core)|Arrest a Fall]], [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]], [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab a Ledge]], or [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a ranged weapon.

---

You’re burdened with noticing potential threats at all times. Because of this, you’ve learned that a well-placed shot can prevent disaster. You make a shot to either pin an ally’s clothing to stop a fall or secure them against a ledge, or you bounce an attack off their armor to correct their course as they stumble while attempting to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|balance]] or [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|fly]].

Make a single ranged attack roll against the DC of the triggering check; your ally uses the result of your ranged attack roll in place of their original roll. You must have [[Rules/Line of Effect (rules-2382)|line of effect]] to your ally, and your attack roll includes any normal bonuses or penalties for a ranged attack with that weapon (such as the penalty for making a ranged attack beyond your first range increment).
