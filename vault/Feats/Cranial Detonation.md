# Cranial Detonation free action  *Feat 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Mindshift]], [[Traits/Psyche]], [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 29

**Frequency**
once per round

**Trigger**
You successfully reduce one or more non-[[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] enemies to 0 Hit Points with a spell.

---

As your overwhelming psyche fells a creature, you use its flickering consciousness to detonate psychic energy from the creature's head. Each enemy you reduced to 0 Hit Points dies and its head explodes. Each exploding head generates a shockwave in a 15-foot emanation around that enemy. Each creature in any of the emanations takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. If this damage reduces an enemy to 0 Hit Points, its head also explodes, potentially damaging more creatures and potentially causing more detonations. A given creature can take damage only once from a single use of Cranial Detonation.
