# Showstopper  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 118

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Bullet Dancer]] (Level 16), [[Rules/Sidebars/What Makes a Phenom|Pistol Phenom]] (Level 16), [[Archetypes/Bullet Dancer]] (Level 16), [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing A Spotter|Pistol Phenom]] (Level 16)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Pistol Twirl]]; Master in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]

---

Your twirling weapons create a grand distraction that’s difficult to look away from. When using Pistol Twirl to [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]], you can target all enemies within your weapon’s first range increment who you can perceive and who can see you. You roll one [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check and compare it against each target’s Perception DC.
