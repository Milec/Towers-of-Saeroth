# Nature Prowler  *Feat 6*

When you begin your turn [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by your hunted prey, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you until the end of your turn.
