# Scavenger's Search  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tengu (Player Core 2)|Tengu]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 34

---

You're always on the lookout for supplies and valuables. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to locate objects (including secret doors and hazards) you search for within 30 feet with a [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action.
