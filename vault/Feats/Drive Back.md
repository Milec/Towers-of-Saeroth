# Drive Back ⤾  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Bastion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bastion Dedication]]

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
An enemy within your reach Strikes an ally adjacent to you.

**Requirements**
You have your shield raised.

---

You push the enemy back and attempt to intercede between the foe and your ally. You attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] the triggering enemy with your shield just as it attacks, pushing it back just after the attempt. Your shield has the [[Traits/Shove|shove]] trait for this Shove attempt. If the shove succeeds, you throw off your enemy's timing, and the enemy takes a –1 circumstance penalty on its attack roll against your ally.
