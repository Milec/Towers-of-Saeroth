# Hypnotic Gaze  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nagaji]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 51

---

You can widen your field of vision, allowing you to affect more creatures with your [[Feats/Hypnotic Lure]]. Once per day, when you use Hypnotic Lure, you can affect all creatures in a 30- foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], rather than one creature within 30 feet.
