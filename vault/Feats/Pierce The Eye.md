# Pierce The Eye two actions  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 128

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Eternal Legend]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Eternal Legend Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are wielding a ranged weapon.

---

You aim a shot from your weapon directly at a foe’s eye, blinding them in the process. Make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. On a hit, the target additionally takes 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. The target must also attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The target is blinded for as long as they are taking the persistent bleed damage and for 1 additional round thereafter.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target also takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to remove the persistent bleed damage; a creature [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administering First Aid]] to stop this persistent bleed damage takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check.
