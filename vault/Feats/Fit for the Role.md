# Fit for the Role  *Feat 14*

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]] (Level 14), [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]] (Level 14)

Not only are you a flamboyant performer, but you’ve mastered an occult secret that allows you to outfit yourself and your companions with magical visages. You gain the ability to cast [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] as a 7th-rank innate occult spell once per day.
