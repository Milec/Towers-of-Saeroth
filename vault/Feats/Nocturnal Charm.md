# Nocturnal Charm  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dhampir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 26

---

The supernatural charm of your vampiric progenitor hangs about you, and you've learned to use it on mortals. Against [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]] (or other creatures of your type, if you're not a humanoid), you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie]], and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Gather Information]] and [[Actions/Make an Impression]].
