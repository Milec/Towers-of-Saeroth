# Perfected Gamtu  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin (Player Core)|Goblin]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 35

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Glorious Gamtu]]

---

Your hat embodies the illusory grandeur of a master dokkaebi. Your gamtu now grants its wearer a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks when worn, and its Hat Trick activation can grant your choice of 2nd-rank [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] or 6th-rank _invisibility_.

In addition, the gamtu can be removed while Hat Trick is activated. Doing so removes the effects of invisibility from the original wearer, but the spell doesn’t end. Instead, the effect continues to elapse; if another creature puts the gamtu on, they’re immediately affected by _invisibility_, which lasts for the remaining duration of Hat Trick.
