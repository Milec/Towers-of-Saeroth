# Lucky Break  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk (Player Core 2)|Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 10

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Cat's Luck (Player Core 2)|Cat's Luck]]

---

You are luckier even than most catfolk. You can trigger Cat's Luck when you fail or critically fail a Fortitude saving throw, Will saving throw, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check, or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check in addition to Reflex saving throws. You still can use Cat's Luck only once per day.
