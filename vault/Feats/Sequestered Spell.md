# Sequestered Spell  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Surki]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 50

---

You consumed so much of a type of magic as a larva that you can now cast it purely on instinct. Choose one [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from the spell list of your magiphage ability. You can cast this cantrip as an innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
