# Swift Banishment ⤾  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 120

**Trigger**
You critically hit a creature that is not on its home plane

**Requirements**
You have a banishment spell prepared

---

The force of your blow sends your victim back to its home plane. You expend a [[Spells/Banishment|_banishment_]] spell you have prepared, affecting the creature you critically hit without needing to cast the spell. The creature can attempt to resist the spell as normal.
