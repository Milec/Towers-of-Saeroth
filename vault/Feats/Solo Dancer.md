# Solo Dancer  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Fan Dancer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fan Dancer Dedication]]; Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]

You often dance alone with a grace exceeding that of most other performers, carrying yourself with a poise and confidence that draws the attention of those around you. You can always roll [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] for [[Rules/Initiative|initiative]], and during the first round of combat, creatures that act after you are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you.
