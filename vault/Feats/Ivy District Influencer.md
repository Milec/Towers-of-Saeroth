# Ivy District Influencer  *Feat 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 46

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Dandy (Player Core 2)|Dandy]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Dandy Dedication]]

---

After mingling alongside the tastemakers of the Ivy District, you understand how rumors spread through larger settlements. When you use the [[Feats/Dandy Dedication (Player Core 2)|Influence Rumor]] downtime activity in a city or metropolis, decrease the DC of the check by 5. When you're in the metropolis of Absalom, the DC is reduced by an additional 5. At the GM's discretion, this second reduction might apply to the DC of Influence Rumor's skill check when you manipulate a rumor outside of Absalom but among a group of Absalomians.
