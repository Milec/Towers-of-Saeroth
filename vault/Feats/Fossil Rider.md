# Fossil Rider  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 27

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bone Magic (Player Core 2)|Bone Magic]]

---

Using your ancestors' bones, you manifest their spirits around you in a fossilized form. You can cast [[Spells/Mask of Terror (Player Core)|_mask of terror_]] on yourself as an innate spell once per day. When you do, you appear as a skeletal lizardfolk ancestor, twisted in a way that inspires the deepest fears of those who observe you.
