# Sneaky  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 34

---

Stealth is an important tool in your arsenal. You can move 5 feet farther when you take the [[Actions/Sneak]] action, up to your Speed.

 In addition, as long as you continue to use Sneak actions and succeed at your Stealth check, you don't become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] if you don't have cover or greater cover and aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] at the end of the Sneak action, as long as you have cover or greater cover or are concealed at the end of your turn.
