# Grasping Reach  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 68

---

You can extend a tangle of vines or tendrils to support your arms and extend your reach. When you wield a melee weapon that requires two hands, doesn’t have [[Traits/Reach|reach]], and deals at least 1d6 damage, you can change between a typical two-handed grip and an extended two-handed grasp using an [[Actions/Interact]] action. Weapons wielded in your extended grasp gain reach of 10 feet. This grasp is less stable and powerful than a typical grip, reducing the weapon’s damage die by 1 step.
