# Form Lock (Wrestler) single action  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Attack]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 127

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]], [[Archetypes/Wrestler (Player Core 2)|Wrestler]] (Level 14)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

---

Your ability to manipulate your enemy's body is potent enough that you can tear and break apart alternate forms. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect currently affecting the creature you have grabbed or restrained. If the target is somehow under the effect of multiple polymorph effects, you can choose which one to attempt to counteract; the GM chooses randomly if the separate effects aren't obvious. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 day.
