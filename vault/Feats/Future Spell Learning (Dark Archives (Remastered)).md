# Future Spell Learning  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Time Mage (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Time Mage Dedication (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Time Mage Dedication]]

---

More future memories of time magic percolate back to the present, teaching you spells you've yet to learn. Add [[Spells/Behold the Weave (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_behold the weave_]], [[Spells/Cast into Time|_cast into time_]], [[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]], [[Spells/Loose Time's Arrow (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_loose time's arrow_]], [[Spells/Quicken Time (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_quicken time_]], [[Spells/Slow (Player Core)|_slow_]], and [[Spells/Stagnate Time (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_stagnate time_]] to your spell list. If you have a repository of spells, like a witch's familiar or a spellbook, add these spells to it. If you have a spell repertoire, when you gain this feat you can retrain one spell in it, replacing it with one of the added spells.
