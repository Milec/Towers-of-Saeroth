# Impaling Bone  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kholo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 19

---

Once per day, you can cast [[Spells/Impaling Spike (Player Core)|_impaling spike_]] as a 7th-rank innate occult spell, except the spike is made of bone instead of cold iron. The bone from the spell has the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune and can immobilize [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures as if they were corporeal.
