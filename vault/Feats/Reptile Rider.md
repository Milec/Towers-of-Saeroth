# Reptile Rider  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 46

---

To you, crocodilians, giant lizards, and dinosaurs are loyal steeds, not monsters. You gain the [[Feats/Ride]] feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks to [[Traits/Animal|Handle an Animal]] as long as the animal is a reptile, [[Traits/Dinosaur|dinosaur]], or even a non-sapient [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]].
