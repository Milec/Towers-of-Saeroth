# Blood Frenzy single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 69

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bloodletting Claws]]

**Requirements**
Your eidolon's last action dealt bleed damage to a living creature.

---

Your eidolon flies into a frenzy. It gains the benefits of [[Spells/Boost Eidolon|_boost eidolon_]] and gains temporary HP equal to your level, but takes a –2 penalty to AC. It can't voluntarily end the frenzy or start another frenzy while in the frenzy. The frenzy lasts for 1 minute, after which your eidolon is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] for 1 minute and can't start another frenzy for 1 minute.
