# Hilt Hammer  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 76

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/The Crimson Oath|Knight Reclaimant]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Knight Reclaimant Dedication]]

---

You know how to turn any weapon into a bone-crushing implement of skeletal destruction. Melee weapons you wield gain the [[Traits/Versatile|versatile B]] trait. You can use the weapon only to deal bludgeoning damage when making melee attacks. At the GM's discretion, specific weapons might not be able to gain the versatile B trait.
