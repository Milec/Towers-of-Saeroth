# Final Union  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 41

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Bind Heroic Spirit]]

---

You and the heroic spirit have formed an eternal pact and now share your body. You are constantly under the effect of a 10th-rank bind heroic spirit. You choose whether the heroic spirit remains as a separate entity bound to you or whether you become a fused spirit that lives in unison, possibly identifying as an entirely new person.

You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] feat for a topic the spirit knew in life, usually [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]].

If you would fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], you don’t fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] or drop any items. Instead, your heroic spirit takes control of your body, allowing you to perform actions as if you were still conscious. If you would die, your heroic spirit puppets your corpse for up to 1 minute after your death or until your body is destroyed. While dead, you are an undead object, granting you [[Rules/Immunities (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|immunities]] to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]], [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Rules/Nonlethal Attacks (Player Core)|nonlethal attacks]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and [[Traits/Void|void]], as well as the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], and unconscious conditions. As an object, you have 200 Hit Points and a [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|Broken]] Threshold of 100 Hit Points. While broken, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1. If you are destroyed, your body must be mended before you can be brought back to life. A creature can spend 8 hours to attempt a DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, mending your body on a success. Alternatively, powerful magic such as the [[Spells/Remake (Player Core)|_remake_]] spell or the [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual can also be used to mend your body.
