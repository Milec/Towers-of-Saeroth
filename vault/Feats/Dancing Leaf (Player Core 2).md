# Dancing Leaf  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 120

---

You are as light as a leaf on the breeze. When you [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] or succeed at a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], increase the distance you jump by 5 feet. When calculating the damage you take from [[Rules/Falling|falling]], don't count any distance fallen while you are adjacent to a wall.
