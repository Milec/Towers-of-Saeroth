# Assured Identification  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 203

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]

---

You rarely misidentify an item. When using [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Magic/Domains/Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] checks to [[Actions/Identify Magic]], if you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead. If you would [[Rules/Cursed Items|misidentify a cursed item because you roll a success but not a critical success]], you simply can't identify it instead.
