Your ability to wield spirit magic has increased. You can cast [[Spells/Speak with Stones|_speak with stones_]] and [[Spells/Spiritual Guardian (Player Core)|_spiritual guardian_]] as a 5th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day. If you’re legendary in [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], _spiritual guardian_ is heightened to 7th rank.
