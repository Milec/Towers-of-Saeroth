# Nephilim Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Nephilim]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 80

---

You know the secrets of [[Rules/The Multiverse|the Multiverse]]. You gain the trained proficiency rank in either [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] general feat for a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] subcategory of a plane to which you trace your lineage.
