# Aquatic Eyes  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Undine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 118

---

As your connection to water grows, you can see more easily in places with less light, such as the depths of the ocean. You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], or you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if your ancestry already has low-light vision.

**Special** If your ancestry has neither low-light vision nor darkvision, you can take this ancestry feat a second time to gain darkvision. You can't retrain out of this feat.
