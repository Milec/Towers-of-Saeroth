# First Frost  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 185

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Gelid Shard]]

---

The _gelid shard_ within your heart may sap your ability to feel and experience emotion, but it also lets you create and manipulate cold. You learn to cast spontaneous spells and gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity. You gain a spell repertoire with the [[Spells/Frost's Touch (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_frost's touch_]] and [[Spells/Ray of Frost|_ray of frost_]] cantrips. You're trained in spell attack rolls and spell DCs for arcane spells. Your key spellcasting ability is Charisma.
