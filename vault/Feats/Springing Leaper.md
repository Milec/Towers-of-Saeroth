# Springing Leaper  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 10

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Your powerful legs allow you to make sudden and dramatic leaps. You can [[Actions/Leap]] as a 2-action activity to double the distance you can Leap vertically, or Leap as a 3-action activity to triple the distance you can Leap vertically. You don't automatically fail [[Actions/Long Jump|Long Jumps]] for jumping in a different direction than your Stride.
