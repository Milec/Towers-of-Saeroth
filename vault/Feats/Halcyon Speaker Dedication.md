# Halcyon Speaker Dedication  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 104

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Halcyon Speaker]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Magaambyan Attendant Dedication]]; member of the Magaambya of conversant rank

**Access**
A character who is Magaambyan of at least attendant rank has access to this option.

---

You devote much of your study to halcyon magic. You gain two common Halcyon cantrips and two common 1st-level halcyon spells (see [[Archetypes/Halcyon Speaker|Halcyon Spells]]). In addition to being able to cast your halcyon spells via your arcane or primal spell slots, you also gain a 1st-level halcyon spell slot. You can use your halcyon spell slots to spontaneously cast your halcyon spells.

**Special** You cannot select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the halcyon speaker archetype.
