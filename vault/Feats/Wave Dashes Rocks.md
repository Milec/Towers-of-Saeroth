# Wave Dashes Rocks single action  *Feat 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 81

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

You slam an unlucky foe with the tremendous power of a plunging wave. Make a melee Strike against the required creature, after which they are no longer grabbed or restrained by you. This Strike must be made with an unarmed attack or a monk weapon. If you deal damage with this Strike, the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 until the end of its next turn.
