# Magus's Analysis single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 17

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]

---

You make an assessment informed by your knowledge of how a creature fights. Attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about a creature, then recharge your Spellstrike. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your check if you previously hit the creature with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] this turn. The subject of your check is temporarily immune to Magus’s Analysis for 1 day.
