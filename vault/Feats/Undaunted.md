# Undaunted  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 54

---

Your spirit has endured many challenges over its long existence, and you are certain you can overcome whatever hardships life throws your way. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an emotion effect, you get a critical success instead.
