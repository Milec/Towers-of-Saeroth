# Scout's Pounce two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 188

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Scout]], [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scout Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by all of your opponents, and you aren't within 10 feet of any enemy.

---

You leap from the shadows to strike your foes. Stride up to your Speed, then Strike twice. If you were hidden or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed|unnoticed]] by the target of these Strikes, your foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against both attacks. Your multiple attack penalty applies normally for both attacks.
