# Alchemical Scholar  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Hobgoblin (Player Core 2)|Hobgoblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 14

---

You gain the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting (Player Core)|Alchemical Crafting]] feat. If you would automatically gain that feat (from your background or the alchemist class, for example), you instead gain a different 1st-level Crafting skill feat. Add an additional common 1st-level [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]] formula to your formula book when you take this feat. Each time you gain a level beyond 1st, add one common alchemical formula of that level to your formula book.
