# Distracting Performance  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 205

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]

---

Your performances are especially distracting, allowing your allies to [[Actions/Sneak]] away with ease. When you [[Actions/Aid]] an ally who is trying to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]], instead of the usual effects of Aid, you can roll a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check and use that result to determine the outcome of the diversion, instead of the ally rolling a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check.
