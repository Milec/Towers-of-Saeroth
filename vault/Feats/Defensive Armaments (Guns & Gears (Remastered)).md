# Defensive Armaments  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 111

---

You use bulky firearms or crossbows to shield your body. Any two-handed firearms and two-handed crossbows you wield gain the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait. If an appropriate weapon already has the parry trait, increase the circumstance bonus to AC it grants when used to parry from +1 to +2. While parrying with your weapon, you are immune to splash damage.
