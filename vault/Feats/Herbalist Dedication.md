# Herbalist Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 176

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Herbalist]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]; [[Feats/Natural Medicine]]

---

You can create remedies and other herbal products. You gain the [[Rules/Alchemical Archetypes|basic alchemy benefits]], though they apply only for herbal items: alchemical items with the [[Traits/Healing|healing]] trait, plus [[Equipment/Antidote|antidote]] and [[Equipment/Antiplague|antiplague]]. You gain batches of infused reagents per day equal to your level or half your level if you didn't make your daily preparations in the wilderness. Your advanced alchemy level for creating these is 1 and doesn't increase on its own. You become an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] and can use Nature instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] to [[Actions/Craft]] herbal items. You don't need to be trained in Crafting or have the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] feat to do so, and you can use [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]] instead of [[Equipment/Alchemist's Tools|alchemist's tools]].

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you have gained two other feats from the herbalist archetype.
