# Grudge Strike two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Apparition]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Wandering]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 25

**Requirements**
Your attuned apparition grants Heraldry [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] or Underworld Lore as an apparition skill.

---

You channel the spiritual power of spiteful grudges. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a creature within your reach. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your attack roll and deal an additional 2d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage to the target; if the target is [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] or otherwise has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]], this Strike instead deals an additional 2d6 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage. This ability gains the vitality trait if it deals vitality damage, or the void trait if it deals void damage.
