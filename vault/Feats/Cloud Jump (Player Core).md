# Cloud Jump  *Feat 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 253

**Prerequisites**
Legendary in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Your unparalleled athletic skill allows you to jump impossible distances. Triple the distance you jump on a successful [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] (so you could jump 90 feet with an Athletics result of 30). When you successfully [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], use the distance jumped and distance limit for a Long Jump but don’t triple the distance.

 You can jump a distance greater than your Speed by spending additional actions when you Long Jump or High Jump. For each additional action spent, add your Speed to the distance limit.
