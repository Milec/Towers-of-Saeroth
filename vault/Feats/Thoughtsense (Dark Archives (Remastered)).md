# Thoughtsense  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 27

---

You can detect the flickers of mental activity let off by all thinking creatures. You gain thoughtsense as a [[Rules/Vague Senses (Player Core)|vague sense]] that has a range of 30 feet—like all vague senses, it's only about as precise as an average human's sense of smell, meaning you generally can predict only if a creature is present. Your thoughtsense detects only thinking creatures, so a creature that is unthinking or otherwise immune to mental effects (such as many constructs and oozes) can't be perceived using your thoughtsense, regardless of how precise it is.

 While your [[Actions/Unleash Psyche (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psyche is Unleashed]], your thoughtsense upgrades to an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]], letting you use it to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] out creatures.
