# Nocturnal Tripkee  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 38

---

You tend to do most of your hunting and work at night and have adapted to the requirements of nocturnal life. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], allowing you to see in [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]] and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] just as well as you can in bright light. However, in darkness, you see in black-and-white only.

**Special** You can take this feat only at 1st level, and you can't retrain out of this feat or into this feat.
