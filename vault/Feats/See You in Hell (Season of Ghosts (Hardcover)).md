# See You in Hell ⤾  *Feat 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Reincarnated (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Reincarnated]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 321

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You’re reduced to 0 Hit Points by a creature but not immediately killed.

---

You aren’t sure if you’ll be reincarnated again, so you might as well take this foe with you. A blast of spiritual energy lashes out from the depths of your soul, targeting the creature who attacked you. The creature takes spirit damage equal to the amount that reduced you to 0 Hit Points ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save with a DC equal to your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher).
