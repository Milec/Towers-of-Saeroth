# Frighten Undead single action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 29

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Undead Slayer]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Undead Slayer Dedication]]; Trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

Your very presence chills creatures of the night down to their core and you can use the spiritual power and faithful life force of religious verses to frighten even [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. Attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]] an undead target using your [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] modifier instead of your [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] modifier if it's higher. If you use your Religion modifier, the Demoralize action loses the [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] and [[Traits/Mental|mental]] traits, as your faith connects to the undead on an instinctual level.
