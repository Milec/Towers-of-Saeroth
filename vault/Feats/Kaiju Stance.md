# Kaiju Stance single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 120

**Access**
You've encountered a  kaiju  and survived.

**Requirements**
You're [[Equipment/Armor/Unarmored (Player Core)|unarmored]].

---

Your encounter with one of the rare forces of nature known as [[Traits/Kaiju|kaiju]] has imbued you with knowledge of a martial art that allows you to emulate a fraction of their power. When you enter Kaiju Stance, you become Large and are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1. The only Strikes you can make are shattering earth attacks. These strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and have the [[Traits/Backswing (Player Core)|backswing]], [[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal]] d12, [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. On a critical success with a shattering earth attack, all creatures other than you that are within 10 feet of the target, including the target itself, take 1 point of bludgeoning [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage per weapon damage die.

While in Kaiju Stance, you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].
