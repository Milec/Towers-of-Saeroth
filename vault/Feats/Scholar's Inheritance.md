# Scholar's Inheritance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Suli]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 110

---

Your parents devoted themselves to alchemical experiments involving elemental energy. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] skill feat.
