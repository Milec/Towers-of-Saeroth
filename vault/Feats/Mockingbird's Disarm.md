# Mockingbird's Disarm ⤾  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 80

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Acrobat (Player Core 2)|Acrobat]]

**Frequency**
once per minute

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Acrobat Dedication]]

**Access**
Members of the Firebrands at the rank of second mark or higher have access to this feat.

**Trigger**
You succeed at a check to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] or use [[Feats/Tumbling Strike]], and you moved through an enemy's space.

---

The Mockingbird is a master at running into foes and coming away with their weapons. Mimicking their skill, you strike at a foe's wrist while tumbling past them, weakening their grip. You attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] the enemy whose space you moved through. You can use [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] for this check.
