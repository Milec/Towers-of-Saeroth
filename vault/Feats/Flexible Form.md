# Flexible Form  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ghoran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 37

**Prerequisites**
any heritage except [[Ancestries/Heritages/Strong Oak|strong oak]]

---

Your body is as supple as a sapling, capable of bending without breaking. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], and if you roll a success to [[Actions/Squeeze]], you get a critical success instead. If you would automatically become trained in Acrobatics (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.
