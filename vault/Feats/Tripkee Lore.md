# Tripkee Lore  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Tripkee]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 38

---

You are well versed in tripkee culture and tactics. You gain the trained proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]. If you would automatically become trained in one of those skills, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

 You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Tripkee Lore]].
