# Spinebreaker single action  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

---

You squeeze your opponent in a vicious bear hug, putting intense pressure on their nerves, joints, or other pain points. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple]] the creature you have grabbed or restrained, with the following effects instead of the usual effects.

**Critical Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] until the end of its next turn and clumsy 1 for 1 minute.  

**Success** The target is clumsy 1 until the end of its next turn.
