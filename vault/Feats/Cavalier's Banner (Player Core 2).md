**Requirements**
You have pledged your service to a specific organization or ideal.

---

You fly the banner of your pledge from your mount, raising your allies’ spirits. You and all allies in a 30-foot emanation from your mount gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves and DCs against fear effects. If your banner is destroyed or removed, allies within 30 feet become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.
