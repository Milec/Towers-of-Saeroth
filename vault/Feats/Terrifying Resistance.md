# Terrifying Resistance  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/General]], [[Traits/Skill]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 209

**Prerequisites**
Expert in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

---

The spells of those you have [[Actions/Demoralize|Demoralized]] are less effective on you. If you succeed in Demoralizing a creature, for the next 24 hours you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against that creature's spells.
