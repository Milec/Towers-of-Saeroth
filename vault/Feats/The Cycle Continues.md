# The Cycle Continues ⤾  *Feat 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Samsaran (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Samsaran]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 57

**Frequency**
once per day

**Trigger**
You are reduced to 0 Hit Points and would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition or would otherwise die.

---

Death is as natural as breathing to you, and you can move past it as easily as the other troubles in your life. You prevent yourself from [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] and regain Hit Points equal to 6d8 plus half your level. If the cause of your death was a condition or effect that would still cause you to die after regaining Hit Points, such as a high value on the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition, you suppress that effect for 1 minute.
