# Insulated Poppet  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Poppet]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 49

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Tsukumogami Poppet]] heritage; you have a metal or ceramic body

---

You’ve used insulating wraps or special glazes to reduce your weakness to current and temperature. You no longer have the [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to either [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] or [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] caused by your tsukumogami heritage.
