# Magus's Analysis single action  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 42

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Spellstrike]]

---

You make an assessment informed by your knowledge of how a creature fights. Attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about a creature. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your check if you previously hit the creature with a Strike this turn. If your check is successful, recharge your Spellstrike. The subject of your check is temporarily immune to Magus's Analysis for 1 day.
