# Vigorous Victor  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 79

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Heroic Scion]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Heroic Scion Dedication]]

---

Long years of success have not only shown you where your limits lie, they’ve expanded them. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects that would cause you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]]. Whenever you attempt such a save, you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] do so at mythic proficiency. The bonus increases to +3 at 18th level.
