# Imbue Mindlessness  *Feat 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Corpse Tender]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Command Corpse]]

---

You grasp the mind of an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature and temporarily wipe it as empty as a [[Bestiary/Families/Zombie|zombie's]] brain. You can target any undead with your divine innate [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spell, not just [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] undead. When you target one non-mindless undead creature with this spell, you can transform its effects. Instead of harm's normal effects, the target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2 for 1 minute. While it is stupefied, you can use your Corpse Tender feats on the target as though it were mindless.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the target is stupefied 3 for 10 minutes.
