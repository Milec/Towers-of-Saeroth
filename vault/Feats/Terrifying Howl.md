# Terrifying Howl single action  *Feat 10*

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Intimidating Glare]]

You unleash a terrifying howl. Attempt [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation|Intimidate]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet. Regardless of the results of your checks, each creature is then temporarily immune to Terrifying Howl for 1 minute.
