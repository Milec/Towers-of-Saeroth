# Enhance Spell single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 103

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spellshifter]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spellshifter Dedication]]

---

You attune your senses to the magical frequencies generated by an allied spellcaster, allowing you to amplify or modify their spells to suit the needs of the moment. Select one willing ally within 30 feet that you can see and choose one of the following spellshape feats: [[Feats/Conceal Spell (Player Core)|Conceal Spell]], [[Feats/Nonlethal Spell (Player Core)|Nonlethal Spell]], [[Feats/Reach Spell (Player Core)|Reach Spell]], or [[Feats/Widen Spell (Player Core)|Widen Spell]]. The target gains the benefits of the feat on their next turn as though they had taken the [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]] action themselves; this requires them to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] as their first action on their turn.
