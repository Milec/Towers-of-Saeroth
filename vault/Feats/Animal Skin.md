# Animal Skin  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 90

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal|animal instinct]]

---

Your proficiency in unarmored defense increases to expert. When you are raging and unarmored, your skin transforms into a thick hide. You gain a +2 item bonus to AC (+3 if you have the greater juggernaut class feature). The thickness of your hide gives you a Dexterity modifier cap to your AC of +3. This item bonus to AC is cumulative with [[Equipment/Armor Potency|_armor potency runes_]] on your [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing|explorer's clothing]], [[Spells/Mage Armor|_mage armor_]], and [[Equipment/Bracers of Armor|_bracers of armor_]].
