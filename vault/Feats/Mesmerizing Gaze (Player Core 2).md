# Mesmerizing Gaze two actions  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Celebrity (Player Core 2)|Celebrity]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Celebrity Dedication (Player Core 2)|Celebrity Dedication]]

---

When you meet someone's gaze, they're unable to look away from you. Choose one target creature you can see and that can see you. That creature must succeed at a Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with you until the end of your next turn; the DC is the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If the creature succeeds at its save or its fascination ends due to a hostile action, it becomes temporarily immune to your Mesmerizing Gaze for 1 day.
