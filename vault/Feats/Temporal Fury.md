# Temporal Fury two actions  *Feat 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 82

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Timewracked]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Timewracked Dedication]]

---

You shift and skip through time, vanishing during your movement to appear in unexpected and advantageous locations for combat. Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. Strike, then Step up to 10 feet. This movement is affected normally by barriers and [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]; you still move physically through the area, just so swiftly that it appears that you’ve teleported. Then, make another Strike. Apply your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]] to both Strikes normally, but this second Strike is made at mythic proficiency and your target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off guard]] against it.
