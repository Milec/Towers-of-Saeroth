# Myth of Realm-Walking  *Feat 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 65

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Godling]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Godling Dedication]]; [[Rules/Hero-Gods of Iblydos|hero-god accomplishment]]

---

Your myth speaks of distant travel to countless countries, planes, or dimensions. When any effect attempts to anchor you to a plane (such as [[Spells/Planar Tether|_planar tether_]]) or to banish you from the current plane (such as [[Spells/Banishment (Player Core)|_banishment_]]), that effect gains the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait. If it already has the incapacitation trait, you gain a +4 status bonus to your save against the effect. If you successfully save against such an effect, the creature that attempted to use this effect on you must succeed at a Will save against your class or spell DC (whichever is higher) to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 minute (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).

You also lay claim to one of the following domains of your choice: [[Magic/Domains/Dreams (Player Core)|dreams]], [[Magic/Domains/Fate (Player Core)|fate]], or [[Magic/Domains/Travel (Player Core)|travel]].
