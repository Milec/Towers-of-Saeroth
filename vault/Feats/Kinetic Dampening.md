# Kinetic Dampening single action  *Feat 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Deviant]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers Player's Guide (Remastered)]] pg. 12

---

You feed off energy in your immediate surroundings, allowing you to dampen the force imparted by incoming blows. Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to physical damage. At 10th level, and every 4 levels thereafter, the resistance increases by 5. Any blows that strike you during this time make no sound as their energy is drained.
