# Earthbond  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Dragonblood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 216

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Terra Dragonblood]]

---

Your bond to the terra firma under your feet increases as your connection to your draconic aspect grows. You can [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore difficult terrain]] caused by stone (such as rubble or gravel) and uneven ground made of stone and earth. In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Reposition]] or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against spells or effects that attempt to force you to move.
