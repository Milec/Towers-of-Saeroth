# Fiendish Mount  *Feat 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 121

**Prerequisites**
[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|divine ally (steed)]]; [[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Evil|tenets of evil]]

---

Your steed ally manifests a suite of powerful and sinister gifts granted to it by your deity. It gains [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], its maximum Hit Points increase by 40, and it gains weakness 10 to holy.

 Additionally, it grows wings appropriate for a servitor of your deity (such as bat wings for a devil), granting it a fly Speed equal to its Speed. It gains the [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] trait and the trait appropriate to the type of servitor it has become (such as [[Traits/Daemon|daemon]], [[Traits/Demon|demon]], or [[Traits/Devil|devil]]).
