# Live Ammunition  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 129

**Archetype** [[Rules/Sidebars/Leading with a Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Artillerist Dedication (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Artillerist Dedication]]

---

There are generally few things as ill-advised as being shot out of a cannon or launched by a trebuchet, but people use the tactic every so often in a desperate situation. While it's more humane to use this strategy with mindless [[Traits/Construct|constructs]] or [[Traits/Undead|undead]], occasionally an exceedingly foolhardy adventurer demands the opportunity to try it. Loading a creature requires two additional [[Rules/Aiming (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Load]] actions, which you must conduct personally. The creature must be willing, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] throughout the process, and the [siege weapon](SiegeWeapons.aspx) must be physically capable of firing the creature in question, based on their size and shape. Typically, that means ballistas don't qualify, for example—nor do auto-catapults which require specifically-sized balls—but normal catapults and onagers work just fine. When you [[Rules/Launching (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Launch]] the weapon, if the weapon usually targets an area, you target a single 5-foot square instead. The siege weapon deals its normal damage to its target, or to the modified area, and to the creature fired.
