Your life force can fuel the stuff of miracles. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 or increase the value of your drained condition by 1 if you already have the drained condition. Before the end of your next turn, if you [[Rules/Cast a Spell]] of 4th rank or lower, you don’t expend the spell’s slot when you cast it, or if you cast a [[Traits/Focus|focus]] spell, it does not cost a Focus Point. The spell loses whatever tradition trait it would normally have and gains the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]
