# Natural Magnetism  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Talos]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 53

---

Your connection with elemental iron grants you a natural field of weak magnetic attraction. You can cast [[Spells/Magnetic Attraction|_magnetic attraction_]] and [[Spells/Magnetic Repulsion|_magnetic repulsion_]] once per day each as 2nd-rank primal innate spells.

**Special** If you chose the [[Feats/Gildedsoul|gildedsoul]] lineage, you can replace one of the above spells with [[Spells/Enthrall|_enthrall_]].
