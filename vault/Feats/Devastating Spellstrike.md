# Devastating Spellstrike two actions  *Feat 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 44

**Prerequisites**
[[Actions/Arcane Cascade]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Inexorable Iron|inexorable iron hybrid study]], [[Actions/Spellstrike]]

**Requirements**
You are in Arcane Cascade stance and your Spellstrike is charged.

---

Your Spellstrike sweeps are so wide and devastating, they splash onto nearby foes. Make a Spellstrike. Foes adjacent to the target take [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage equal to 2 + the extra damage from Arcane Cascade. The damage type is the same as Arcane Cascade; if the spell already deals splash damage of the same type, combine the damage together before applying weaknesses or resistances.
