# Incredible Ricochet single action  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fighter]], [[Traits/Press]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 151

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Incredible Aim]]

---

After your first shot singles out your opponent's position, you direct another that ricochets around obstacles and strikes unerringly. Make a ranged weapon Strike against a creature you previously attacked this turn. You ignore the target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition and all cover.
