# Dragon Grip  *Feat 9*

---

Your hold has a unique draconic connection. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus when you attempt to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on a creature with the [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] trait. You gain access to the [Riding Drake](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=21) animal companion.
