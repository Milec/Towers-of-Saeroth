# Woodworker  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ardande]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 48

---

Denizens of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] take immense pride in the magnificent works of art they can craft from wood, and practicing your own woodcraft helps you feel a connection with your planar heritage. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. If you would automatically become trained in Crafting (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat for woodworking.
