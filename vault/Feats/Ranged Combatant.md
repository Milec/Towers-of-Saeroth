# Ranged Combatant  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evolution]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 68

---

Spines, flame jets, and holy blasts are just some of the ways your eidolon might strike from a distance. It gains a ranged [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack with a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 30 feet that deals 1d4 damage and has the [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and [[Traits/Propulsive|propulsive]] traits. When you select this feat, choose a damage type: acid, bludgeoning, cold, electricity, fire, negative, piercing, positive, or slashing. If your eidolon is a [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], or [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]] with an alignment other than true neutral, you can choose a damage type in its alignment.
