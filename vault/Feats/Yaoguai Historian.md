# Yaoguai Historian  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yaoguai]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 86

---

You know extensively about your own kind. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]. If you would automatically become trained in this skill (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] general feat for Yaoguai Lore.

Once per day, you can gain the benefits of the [[Feats/Assurance (Player Core)|Assurance]] feat for either  Occultism or Yaoguai Lore. You choose to use this as a free action when you attempt the affected skill check.
