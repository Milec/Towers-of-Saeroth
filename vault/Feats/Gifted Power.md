# Gifted Power  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 140

---

Your mystery grants you additional magic. You have an extra spell slot of your highest rank, which you can use only to cast one of your mystery's granted spells, heightened to this rank.

**Special** If you have the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] access class feature or [[Feats/Mysterious Repertoire]] feat, you can cast spells that you learned from those abilities using the additional spell slot from Gifted Power.
