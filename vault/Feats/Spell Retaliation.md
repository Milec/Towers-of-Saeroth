# Spell Retaliation free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 21

**Trigger**
You succeed at a saving throw against an enemy’s spell, or an enemy fails a spell attack roll against you.

**Requirements**
Your [[Actions/Spellstrike]] isn’t charged.

---

Battling other spellcasters is one of your major focuses, and harnessing magic from spells you defend against gives you everything you need to fight back. You recharge your [[Actions/Spellstrike]].
