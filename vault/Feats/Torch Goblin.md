# Torch Goblin single action  *Feat 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Goblin]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 38

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Charhide Goblin]] heritage

---

You’ve spent enough time on fire that you know how to use it to your advantage. You can light yourself thoroughly on fire with a held [[Equipment/Torch|torch]], a bottle of [[Equipment/Alchemist's Fire|alchemist’s fire]], or a similar incendiary, dealing yourself 1d6 persistent fire damage. As long as you are suffering persistent fire damage, all your melee attacks against adjacent creatures deal an additional 1 fire damage per weapon damage die. Any creature that successfully [[Actions/Grapple|Grapples]], [[Actions/Shove|Shoves]], or [[Actions/Trip|Trips]] you takes 1d6 fire damage; if it uses a weapon for that action, the weapon takes the damage instead. You must still attempt the flat check to remove the persistent fire damage each round, as normal.
