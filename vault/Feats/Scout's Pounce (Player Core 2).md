# Scout's Pounce two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 212

**Archetypes** [[Archetypes/Scout (Player Core 2)|Scout]], [[Archetypes/Verduran Shadow]] (Level 10)

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Scout Dedication (Player Core 2)|Scout Dedication]]

**Requirements**
You are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by all of your opponents, and you aren't within 10 feet of any enemy.

---

You leap from the shadows to strike at your foes. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your Speed, then [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] twice. If you were hidden or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by the target of these Strikes, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against both attacks. Your multiple attack penalty applies normally for both attacks.
