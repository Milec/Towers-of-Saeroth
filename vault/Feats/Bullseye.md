# Bullseye single action  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 136

---

You carefully take aim before making your attack in order to avoid your foe's concealment and cover. On your next Strike with a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon before the end of your turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your attack roll and ignore your target's [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition, as well as the target's lesser cover or standard cover; if the target had greater cover, they instead have only standard cover against the Strike.
