# Crocodile's Twin  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lizardfolk (Player Core 2)|Lizardfolk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 44

**Prerequisites**
[[Ancestries/Heritages/Bakuwa Lizardfolk]] heritage or [[Ancestries/Heritages/Makari Lizardfolk]] heritage

---

Your egg contained your twin, a crocodile. Considered a rare and auspicious event, you share a magical bond with this sibling. You gain a tiny crocodile as a [[Feats/Familiar (Player Core)|familiar]].

**Special** You can only take this feat at 1st level.
