# Elysium's Cadence  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Aftermath (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Aftermath]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 56

**Prerequisites**
You've partied extensively with an [[Bestiary/Families/Azata (Monster Core)|azata]] or entered a romantic relationship with an azata.

---

You've been ensnared by an azata's infectious enthusiasm for life, love, and freedom that manifests as a pearlescent afterimage in your graceful movements, a spring in your soft steps, an ever-present melody in your euphonic voice, and eddies of passionate colors in your mesmerizing eyes. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], or a +2 circumstance bonus if the target is holy. You gain the Set Free reaction. **Set Free** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You attempt a check to remove or [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an effect that causes the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] condition; **Effect** You roll twice and use the better result.
