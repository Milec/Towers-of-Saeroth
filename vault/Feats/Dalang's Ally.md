# Dalang's Ally single action  *Feat 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Wayang (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Wayang]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 74

**Frequency**
once per hour

---

You move your shadow as a master puppeteer, allowing it to dance around your enemies and serve you in combat. Until the start of your next turn, your shadow darts around with your foes, providing [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] for you against all enemies within your reach. Flanking with your shadow is the same as flanking with an ally and so is subject to effects like [[Bestiary/Abilities/All-Around Vision (Monster Core)|all-around vision]] or the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|deny advantage]] class feature.
