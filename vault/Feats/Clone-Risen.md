# Clone-Risen  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Lineage]], [[Traits/Reflection]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 120

---

You were formed from a [[Rituals/Clone|_clone_]] ritual or alchemical experiment. Created as a vessel for another creature, you can suppress your sentience momentarily, making your mind harder to alter. You gain the Empty Vessel reaction.

 **Empty Vessel** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would take mental damage or be affected by a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect; **Effect** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Will saves and resistance to mental damage equal to your level against the triggering effect. This applies only to the initial effect, not successive saves, [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]], or other repeated effects.
