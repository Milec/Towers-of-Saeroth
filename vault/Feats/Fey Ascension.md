# Fey Ascension  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Universal Ancestry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 127

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Fey Influence]]

---

The fey influence on your form and features grows even more powerful and notable. You gain one of the following features and abilities; this must be the same animal or fey type as you chose for Fey Influence.

- **Anteater** You become an expert in primal DCs and spell attack rolls. You can cast [[Spells/Slow|_slow_]] as an innate primal spell once per day.
- **Cat Sith** You laugh off the outraged curses of those you have tricked or wronged. Whenever a creature targets you with a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect, make a DC 13 flat check. On a success, you are unaffected.
- **Cursed Bluebird** You have enough control over your curse to turn a bane into a minor blessing. Once per day, you can temporarily transform yourself or an ally into a blue bird, as a 4th-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]] (bird only) targeting one willing creature.
- **Dryad** Your hair becomes intermixed with delicate but razor-edged leaves that move as you command. You gain a leaves ranged unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage. Your leaves are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Dart|dart]] weapon group and have the [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
- **Faun** You can cast [[Spells/Charm|_charm_]], [[Spells/Sleep|_sleep_]], and [[Spells/Triple Time|_triple time_]] as innate primal spells once per day each. Whenever you cast one of these spells, you can Step or Stride as part of the activity.
- **Gremlin** You become an expert in primal DCs and spell attack rolls. You can cast [[Spells/Mad Monkeys|_mad monkeys_]] as an innate primal spell once per day.
- **Monarch** Once per day, you gain the ability to manifest a pair of exquisite butterfly wings. These wings remain for 10 minutes. You gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed while you've manifested your wings.
- **Unicorn** You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]], or an expert if you were already trained. You gain a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Poison|poison]] and [[Traits/Charm|charm]] effects.

**Special** This feat gains the trait appropriate for your ancestry ([[Traits/Human|human]] for [[Ancestries/Human|human]], [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] for [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]], etc.).
