# Chain of Words two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Invocation]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 51

**Frequency**
once pr 10 minutes

---

You see the thin lines of magic between your creations, and you can exploit that connection with a small shift of power from one rune to another. You [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke two Runes]] within 60 feet. In addition to the runes’ normal effects, a chain of glowing script flows between them, dealing 5d6 force damage to all creatures in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] between them with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex saving throw; creatures bearing the invoked runes are not in the area of this line. A creature who critically fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]], as the runes seal its movements. The DC to Escape is your class DC.

At 12th level, and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.
