# Emergency Regeneration ⤾  *Feat 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 81

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wild Mimic]]

**Frequency**
once per day

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wild Mimic Dedication]]; you have deactivated a creature’s [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration|regeneration]] for at least 1 round or have identified a creature with regeneration in combat

**Trigger**
You’re reduced to 0 hit points from damage that’s neither [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] nor [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]].

---

You have battled against foes whose bodies naturally regenerate, and while yours doesn't do so all the time, it can happen in a pinch. You gain the effects of a 7th-rank [[Spells/Regenerate (Player Core)|_regenerate_]] spell. At 20th level, you gain the effects of a 9th-rank _regenerate_ instead.
