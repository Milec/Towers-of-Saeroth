# Mythic Magic  *Feat 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 83

---

You convert your mythic power into a magical effect. When you gain this feat, choose up to three spells of 1st or 2nd rank that take no more than 3 actions to cast and that are either instantaneous or have a duration of 10 minutes or less. You can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to cast any of those spells for their normal action cost and use mythic proficiency to determine your [[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack rolls]] and spell DCs, and the spells are automatically heightened to a rank equal to half your level (rounded up). If you don’t normally have the ability to cast spells, you use Charisma as your spellcasting attribute modifier; otherwise, you use the spellcasting ability associated with your spellcasting class. You gain the [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity only for spells cast with this feat; you don’t otherwise gain the typical benefits of being a spellcaster.

**Special** At 14th level, add one spell of 3rd rank for you to cast using this ability. At 20th level, add one 4th-rank spell.
