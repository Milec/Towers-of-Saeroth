# Pactbound Dedication  *Feat 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Dedication]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 10

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Pactbound Initiate]]

**Prerequisites**
Trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]; [[Setting/Languages/Erutaki]] or [[Setting/Languages/Jotun|Giant]] language, must have earned the trust of a saumen kar who initiates you into the archetype

---

You befriended one of the last saumen kar and were infused with a fraction of their power, granting you the potential to become a guardian of their ancient pact. You gain [[Traits/Cold|cold]] resistance equal to half your level. You can [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] while it's snowing even lightly, even if it wouldn't be thick enough to normally grant [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]]. Finally, all saumen kar can sense the mark of their pact upon you, and their attitude toward you is typically one step better than it would be otherwise ([[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]], unfriendly to [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]], etc.) Most saumen kar are unfriendly to outsiders; this would make them indifferent to you.

**Special** You can't select another dedication feat until you've gained two other feats from the pactbound initiate archetype.
