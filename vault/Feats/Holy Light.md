# Holy Light two actions  *Feat 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 85

**Prerequisites**
you follow a good-aligned deity

**Access**
Knights of Lastwall have access to this feat.

---

You raise your arms to the sky and pray fervently, surrounding yourself in a beacon of holy light. The beacon sheds bright light in a 30-foot-radius, 100-foot-high cylinder centered on you until the beginning of your next turn. Evil creatures in the area must succeed at a Will save equal to your class DC or spell DC (whichever is higher) or become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.
