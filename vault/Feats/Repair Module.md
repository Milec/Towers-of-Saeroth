# Repair Module single action  *Feat 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Android]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 73

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You trigger your body's repair programming, causing your body's nanites to heal your wounds. You gain [[Rules/Fast Healing and Regeneration|fast healing]] equal to half your level for 1 minute. While Repair Module is active, you can't use other abilities that require the use of your nanites.
