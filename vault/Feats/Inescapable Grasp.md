# Inescapable Grasp  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 127

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Wrestler]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Wrestler Dedication]]

---

Your grasp has a supernatural quality to it, preventing your foes from easily escaping—with or without magical assistance. If a creature you have grabbed attempts to use a [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] spell or effect, it must succeed at a DC 15 flat check or the spell fails. If a creature you have grabbed attempts to [[Actions/Escape]] while under the effect of [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]], it must succeed at a DC 15 flat check or be forced to roll the Escape attempt normally, rather than automatically succeeding.
