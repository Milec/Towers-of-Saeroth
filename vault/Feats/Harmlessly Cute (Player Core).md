# Harmlessly Cute  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Leshy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 68

---

Your size and demeanor make it easy for you to convince others that you mean no harm. You gain the [[Feats/Shameless Request]] skill feat. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to initiative checks when you roll [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] for initiative.
