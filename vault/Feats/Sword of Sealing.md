# Sword of Sealing two actions  *Feat 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype (Player Core)|Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 93

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Spirit Warrior]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Spirit Warrior Dedication]]

---

Your spiritual power pierces both body and soul, pinning your foe in place. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a one-handed, [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], or [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] weapon, or your fist unarmed attack. On a successful hit, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or your spell DC, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]]. The target can end this immobilization with a successful [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] attempt using the higher of your class DC or your spell DC as the Escape DC.
