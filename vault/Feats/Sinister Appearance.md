# Sinister Appearance  *Feat 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Ifrit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 102

---

You possess horns, a tail, or red eyes, or could otherwise be mistaken for a [[Rules/Sidebars/Tiefling Settlements|tiefling]]. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]. If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation (from your background or class, for example), you instead become trained in a skill of your choice. You gain the [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] skill feat, as well as a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Impersonate]] when pretending to be a version of yourself who is a tiefling.
