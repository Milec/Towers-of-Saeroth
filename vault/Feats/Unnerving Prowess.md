# Unnerving Prowess free action  *Feat 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Archetype]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 35

**Archetype** [[Archetypes/Aldori Duelist]]

**Prerequisites**
[[Feats/Aldori Duelist Dedication]]

**Trigger**
You critically succeed at a Strike or Disarm with your Aldori dueling sword.

---

Your incredible skill with your blade unnerves your foes. You attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the target of your Strike or [[Actions/Disarm]]. This Demoralize attempt does not have the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait, nor do you take a penalty to the check if the target doesn’t understand your language.
