# Create Undead  *Ritual 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 393

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** black onyx, see Creature Creation Rituals

**Secondary Casters** 1

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (expert), [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert), or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert)

**Secondary Checks** Religion

**Range** 10 feet

**Target(s)** 1 dead creature

---

You transform the target into an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature with a level up to that allowed in the Creature Creation Rituals table. There are many versions of this ritual, each specific to a particular type of undead (one ritual for all [[Bestiary/Families/Zombie|zombies]], one for [[Bestiary/Families/Skeleton|skeletons]], one for [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghouls]], and so on), and the rituals that create [[Traits/Rare|rare]] undead are also rare. Some forms of undead, such as [[Bestiary/Lich|liches]], form using their own unique methods and can't be created with a version of _create undead_.

**Critical Success** The target becomes an undead creature of the appropriate type. If it's at least 4 levels lower than you, you can make it a minion. This gives it the [[Traits/Minion|minion]] trait, meaning it can use 2 actions when you command it, and commanding it is a single action that has the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] and [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=683) traits. You can have a maximum of four minions under your control. If it's intelligent and doesn't become a minion, the undead is [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful|helpful]] to you for awakening it, though it's still a horrid and evil creature. If it's unintelligent and doesn't become a minion, you can give it one simple command. It pursues that goal single-mindedly, ignoring any of your subsequent commands.  

**Success** As critical success, except an intelligent undead that doesn't become your minion is only [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly|friendly]] to you, and an unintelligent undead that doesn't become your minion leaves you alone unless you attack it. It marauds the local area rather than following your command.  

**Failure** You fail to create the undead.  

**Critical Failure** You create the undead, but its soul, tortured by your foul necromancy, is full of nothing but hatred for you. It attempts to destroy you.
