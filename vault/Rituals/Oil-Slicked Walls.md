# Oil-Slicked Walls  *Ritual 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 94

**Cast** 8 hours

**Secondary Casters** 1

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] (expert)

**Secondary Checks**  Architecture Lore  or  Crafting

**Area** 7-mile radius [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] centered on you

**Target(s)** the outer walls of a settlement

**Duration** 1 week

---

The outer walls are a city’s first defense against invaders, and you want to make certain no attackers can climb over them. Upon successful completion of this ritual, the surfaces of a settlement’s walls within the area are covered with a thin, slippery sheen that can’t be washed away.

**Critical Success** The oil covering the walls also coats any climber’s hands. The DC for [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to Climb the affected walls increases by 10, and a creature who fails a check to climb also drops anything they are carrying.  

**Success** The DC for [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] the affected walls increases by 5.  

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.  

**Critical Failure** Phantom oil constantly pools around your feet during times of stress. For 1 week, the first time each encounter that a primary or secondary caster takes an action to move, they must attempt a Reflex save or [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] against the ritual’s casting DC or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. A caster can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] or [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]] without having to attempt a check or save.
