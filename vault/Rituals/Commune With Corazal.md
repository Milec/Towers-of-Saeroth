# Commune With Corazal  *Ritual 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 33

**Cast** 4 hours

**Secondary Casters** 3

**Primary Check** DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (master) or DC 29 Nature (expert)

**Secondary Checks** DC 24  Forest Lore ,  Perception , or  Survival

---

You tap into Corazal’s innumerable senses to identify disturbances throughout the Verduran Forest.

**Critical Success** You and Corazal successfully identify a flashpoint: an imminent event that could stoke distrust and unrest. You experience a sensory premonition of the event, can cast [[Spells/Know the Way|_know the way_]] as a 7th-rank cantrip to point toward the location, and know which Elder Oak is closest to it.  

**Success** As success, but the ritual heavily taxes your mind and body. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1, and until the drained condition ends, you are also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1.  

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.  

**Critical Failure** The ritual has no effect beyond burning your mind with conflicting information. You are drained 2, and until the drained condition ends, you are also stupefied 1 and have the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge (Player Core)|Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat. Whenever you would decrease this drained value, the value does not decrease unless you succeed at a DC 11 flat check.
