# Still Life Storage  *Ritual 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|Extradimensional]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 198

**Cast** 1 hour

**Cost** a primed canvas and paints worth the highest target’s level × 10 gp

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained)

**Range** touch

**Target(s)** up to 15 Bulk of non-magical objects that can fit in a 20-foot cube

**Duration** unlimited

---

As you paint, you transform objects into a still life painting. The items disappear from the world around you as the canvas fills. You can reach into this painting and retrieve objects stored within it with an Interact action, though you cannot return objects that are removed or add additional objects to the scene. Other creatures can retrieve objects as well, though it requires a successful [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], Occultism or Perception check against the higher of your class DC or spell DC to Identify the _still life storage_. If a creature is already aware of the properties of the _still life storage_, they can Interact to take objects.

**Critical Success** You create the still life scene as described, and you can designate up to 3 Bulk of objects that only you can Interact with.  

**Success** You create the still life scene as described.  

**Failure** You fail to capture the objects within the canvas. They are otherwise unchanged by the attempt.  

**Critical Failure** The still life twists the objects you store in it, altering their appearance and inflicting each with the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] condition.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** You can target magical objects with a level that does not exceed twice the _still life storage's_ rank.
