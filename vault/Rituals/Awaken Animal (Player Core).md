# Awaken Animal  *Ritual 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 390

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** herbs, 1/5 the value on Creature Creation Rituals

**Secondary Casters** 3

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (master)

**Secondary Checks**  Lore  (any),  Society ,  Survival

**Range** 10 feet

**Target(s)** 1 animal of up to the level on the table

---

You grant intelligence to the target, transforming it into a beast. If it was previously an animal companion or minion, it can no longer serve as one.

**Critical Success** The target's Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma modifiers each increase to +2 if they were lower, and it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful|helpful]] to you for awakening it.  

**Success** The target's Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma modifiers increase to +0 if they were lower and it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly|friendly]] to you for awakening it.  

**Failure** You fail to awaken the target.  

**Critical Failure** You accidentally awaken the target with a pure bestial hatred toward you. The target's Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma modifiers increase to –2 if they were lower. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] to you, attempting to destroy you.
