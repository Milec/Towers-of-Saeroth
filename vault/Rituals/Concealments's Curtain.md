# Concealments's Curtain  *Ritual 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 191

**Cast** 6 days

**Cost** an ornate mirror worth at least 100 gp, naturally occurring round fruit the size and number of the target’s eyes, and the same amount of flawless silver needles worth at least 1 gp each

**Secondary Casters** 3

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Secondary Checks**  Arcana ,  Deception ,  Stealth

**Range** planetary

**Target(s)** 1 living creature

**Duration** 1 week

---

For 5 days, you line the fruit up in front of the mirror, chanting the name of the target. At exactly midnight on the last day, you drive the pins through the fruits, miming the action of methodically blinding the target to you and your companions. The target is immediately affected and can't visually detect any of the casters involved in the ritual—provided that the ritual was successful, the target is unaware of the effects. Since the ritual affects the target's ability to see the casters, rather than making the casters invisible, effects like [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] are ineffective against _concealment's curtain_; instead, the target would need to remove the curse with effects like a 4th-rank [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]]. To perform this ritual, you must have been within 5 feet of the target at least once, close enough to get a good look at their eyes. The target is temporarily immune to further castings of _concealment's curtain_ for 1 year.

**Critical Success** The target is unable to visually detect the casters for the duration, and the target's [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]] spells don't detect the casters.  

**Success** The target is unable to visually detect the casters for the duration, but their detection spells function normally.  

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.  

**Critical Failure** The ritual backfires and all casters participating in it are blinded for the next 24 hours.

---
**Heightened (8th)** You aren't satisfied with simply concealing yourself from the target. The cost of the ritual mirror increases to 1,000 gp, and a personal effect of the target is required. After completing the ritual, the target takes 6d6 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 week, requiring both a sound body and 4th-rank _cleanse affliction_ to end the effect early. The target is aware that the ritual was cast on them; however, it doesn't have any additional information.
