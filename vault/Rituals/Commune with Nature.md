# Commune with Nature  *Ritual 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Prediction]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 410

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** rare incense worth a total value of 60 gp

**Secondary Casters** 1

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (master)

**Secondary Checks** Nature

**Duration** up to 10 minutes

---

As [[Rituals/Commune|_commune_]], except you contact the primal spirits of nature, which know about animals, beasts, fey, fungi, plants, topography, and natural resources within a 3-mile radius of the ritual’s location.
