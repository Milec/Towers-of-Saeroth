# Unbearable Cacophony  *Ritual 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 162

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** valuable crystals and tuning forks worth 300 gp

**Secondary Casters** 2

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (mythic)

**Secondary Checks**  Arcana ,  Nature ,

**Area** 1-mile radius circle centered on you

**Duration** 3 days

---

You amplify the sound of every item and creature to impossible levels. [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|Deaf]] creatures and creatures [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage remain unaffected in most cases.

**Critical Success** As success, and you and the secondary casters gain sonic resistance equal to your level for the duration.  

**Success** Any sound produced by a creature or object (such as a cricket, a ringing anvil, or a lion’s roar) deals sonic damage equal to the level of the creature or item (minimum 1) to every creature within 15 feet (basic Fortitude save with a DC equal to the ritual’s casting DC). For every size category over Tiny of the item or creature making noise, this damage increases by 2. Organized groups of creatures, such as a singing quartet or a swarm of insects, count as the next size category larger for this effect. Effects that normally deal sonic damage to creatures and objects ignore half the creature or object’s sonic [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] or [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]].  

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.  

**Critical Failure** An intense sound of ringing echoes in each caster’s ears, imparting a [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to sonic damage equal to their level for 1 day.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The increase in damage made by creatures larger than Tiny increases to 6 per size category.
