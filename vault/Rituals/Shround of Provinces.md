# Shround of Provinces  *Ritual 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 198

**Cast** 1 day

**Secondary Casters** 1

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (proficiency)

**Secondary Checks** Nature or  Society

**Area** 1-mile emanation

**Duration** 1 week

---

By gathering dewdrops collected on a foggy morning, you can distill the essence of that fog and release it over a settlement. While this is generally used to protect a settlement from the attention of nearby battles, raiders targeting the port city of Ridonport once used it to disorient the town before their attack. While the shroud hangs, a fine mist coats that area. It dampens uncovered surfaces and pools into thick drops of condensation.

 The shroud shades the settlement. The [[Rules/Light (Player Core)|light level]] is one level darker than normal (bright light becomes dim light, and dim light becomes darkness). Creatures within the shroud are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from those outside. Within the shroud, sound does not travel farther than 30 feet, and creatures are concealed to all creatures further than 30 feet away.

**Critical Success** The foggy shroud emanates from you, and creatures within the area aren't concealed from you.  

**Success** The foggy shroud emanates from you.  

**Failure** The foggy shroud fails to appear.  

**Critical Failure** The shroud's area is a 1-mile burst centered on you instead of an aura. The shadows lash out at creatures in the mist and seek to push them away. Everyone within the shroud feels the urge to leave it.

---
**Heightened (8th)** Additionally, creatures become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] while they are inside the shroud to creatures outside of the shroud.
