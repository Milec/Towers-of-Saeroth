# Ash-Strewn Ending  *Ritual 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 66

**Cast** 8 hours

**Secondary Casters** 3

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] ([[Rules/Mythic Proficiency|mythic]]) or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (mythic)

**Secondary Checks**  Crafting ,  Pharasma Lore , or  Survival

**Area** 1,000-foot radius

**Range** 10 feet

**Duration** 1 week

---

Lo, speak of the hero-gods and their ash-strewn endings! Sing of their might as it falls on the land! Taste the bone and char that binds their life to yours!

This ritual focuses on the burning of a mythic creature’s corpse on a pyre in an outdoor location. Once the corpse has been reduced to ashes, the wind scatters those ashes across a 1,000-foot radius. As the ash lands on creatures within this radius, a fragment of the dead creature’s mythic power flows into them.

**Critical Success** Death has a lessened claim upon those in the ritual’s area of effect. All creatures that were of a level equal to or lower than the level of the mythic creature incinerated as part of the ritual gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Void|void]] damage, gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], and reduce the DC of [[Rules/Recovery Checks (Player Core)|recovery checks]] by 2.  

**Success** As critical success, but only as long as the creature remains in the ritual’s area.  

**Failure** The ritual has no effect.  

**Critical Failure** Grief washes over the area, and the threat of death looms more closely over the casters, who all gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] 10 to [[Traits/Void|void]] damage, take a –2 status penalty to saving throws against [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], and increase the DC of [[Rules/Recovery Checks (Player Core)|recovery checks]] by 2. This is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] effect.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The area increases to a 1-mile radius, the duration increases to 1 month, the void resistance (or weakness) increases to 15, and the status bonus (or penalty) increases to +3.

**Heightened (8th)** The area increases to 10 miles, the duration increases to 1 year, the void resistance (or weakness) increases to 20, and the status bonus (or penalty) increases to +4.

**Heightened (10th)** As 8th rank, but one GM-selected non-mythic creature in the area becomes mythic. This creature’s attitude and personality is favorable toward the primary caster.
