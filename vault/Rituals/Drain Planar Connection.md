# Drain Planar Connection  *Ritual 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 60

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** six sets of rare herbs, each worth a total of 50 gp

**Secondary Casters** 5

**Primary Check** DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (master)

**Secondary Checks** Secondary Checks DC 26  Diplomacy  or  Nature  or  Occultism

---

You draw the planar energy from Idyllis, through the [[Rules/Ley Lines|ley lines]], and into the six latticework spheres.

**Critical Success** You sever Idyllis’s connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] permanently.  

**Success** You sever Idyllis’s connection to the Plane of Wood for 10 years.  

**Failure** You sever Idyllis’s connection to the Plane of Wood for 1 year, but the ritual backfires, dealing 4d12+26 [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage to all secondary casters (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Reflex save)—this damage kills Madge.  

**Critical Failure** The ritual backfires as a failure but doesn’t sever Idyllis’s connection.
