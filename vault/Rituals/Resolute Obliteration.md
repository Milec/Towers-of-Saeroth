# Resolute Obliteration  *Ritual 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 197

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** precious metals to create a ritual circle worth 10 gp × the ritual’s rank

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (trained)

**Range** touch

**Target(s)** up to 25 Bulk of non-magical items

---

Disposing of unwanted or dangerous objects can be more difficult than simply keeping them in a vault. Cases where something needs to be truly destroyed often require a specialist. Whether it's due to the risks of keeping rusty weapons around or a desire to destroy the treasury before opponents steal it, the best way to handle it is through obliteration.

 You gather non-magical objects into your ritual circle and expose them to the forces of the Void. They lose color and turn brittle as they age in moments. With this magical acceleration, even items that typically last for centuries, such as gold or statues, can fall to time. Any item reduced to 0 HP turns to dust.

**Critical Success** The rapid aging deals 10 damage to all targets, which bypasses any Hardness.  

**Success** As critical success, but 5 damage.  

**Failure** The items are undamaged.  

**Critical Failure** The harsh nature of the Void rebounds onto you, dealing 1d6 void damage to you per rank of the ritual.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage dealt to items by 5 (or 10 on a critical success).
