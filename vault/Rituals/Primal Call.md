# Primal Call  *Ritual 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 415

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** faerie circle ingredients worth a total value of 1 gp × the spell level × the target’s level

**Secondary Casters** 4

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (master)

**Secondary Checks**  Crafting ,  Diplomacy ,  Survival

**Range** 100 miles

**Target(s)** 1 animal, beast, fey, fungus, or plant

**Duration** see text

---

This functions as [[Rituals/Planar Ally|planar ally]] except you craft a faerie circle and call an animal, beast, fey, fungus, or plant from within 100 miles.
