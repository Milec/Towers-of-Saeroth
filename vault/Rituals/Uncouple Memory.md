# Uncouple Memory  *Ritual 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 198

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** surgical supplies worth 400 gp

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (master), [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (master), [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (master), or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (master)

**Duration** unlimited

---

Some knowledge cannot persist in the world and haunts those who possess it. Unfortunately, some of that knowledge belongs to you. With the assistance of your secondary casters, you split your mind into uneven sections. Your primary mind contains your personality, goals, general knowledge, and abilities. Your uncoupled mind exists to hold the knowledge that you have decided to hide within your own consciousness, plus a personality that oversees it. You can select the personality that appears, such as making it a copy of your own or a version of yourself tailored to protect that knowledge. While your primary mind is active, your uncoupled mind is not accessible. Accessing it requires subduing your primary mind, such as by falling asleep or entering a trance.

 This does not remove all traces of the knowledge you've sequestered from the world. If you shared it with others (or if someone else also discovered that knowledge), they can still spread it. However, if you hear, see, or otherwise encounter the uncoupled knowledge while your primary mind is active, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 3 and your uncoupled mind becomes active instead. Your uncoupled mind can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] themself based on a set condition when performing this ritual or based on their own judgment, reactivating your primary mind.

**Critical Success** The ritual succeeds. When your uncoupled mind would become active, you can attempt a DC 5 flat check. On a success, your primary mind stays active.  

**Success** As critical success, by the flat check is DC 10.  

**Failure** You fail to uncouple your memory from your mind, leaving your memory and personality unaltered.  

**Critical Failure** You uncouple the wrong memories, creating an uncoupled mind with unintended knowledge that your primary mind cannot access (as determined by the GM).
