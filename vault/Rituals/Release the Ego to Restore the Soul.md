# Release the Ego to Restore the Soul  *Ritual 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 196

**Cast** 1 hour

**Cost** incense and perfumes worth 20 gp × the spell’s rank

**Secondary Casters** 1

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (trained) or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained)

**Secondary Checks** Nature, Occultism, or  Performance

**Area** 30-foot emanation

**Duration** 4 hours

---

You create abundant music and rally a crowd into a dance. You and every creature in the area move together in laughter and connection. The dancers lose themselves and their minds within it, as their bodies and spirits are overtaken with jubilation.

**Critical Success** The joy of movement cleanses those who participate. Whenever a creature within the emanation [Performs](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3868), they can also attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] their highest-rank detrimental [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect using the rank of this ritual as the counteract rank. If they do, they gain a +4 status bonus to their check. They can only attempt to counteract an effect once every hour.  

**Success** As critical success, except creatures do not gain a status bonus to their checks.  

**Failure** The dance fails to release the participants from their worries.  

**Critical Failure** You lose control of the dance and become swept along in its energy. After 4 hours of dancing, you finally return to your senses and gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition. Other creatures in the area are unaffected.

---
**Heightened (4th)** Add curses to the effect that you attempt to counteract on a success.
