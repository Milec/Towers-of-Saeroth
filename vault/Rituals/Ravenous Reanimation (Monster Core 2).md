# Ravenous Reanimation  *Ritual 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 269

**Cast** 1 day

**Cost** valuable treasures from the target dragon’s hoard worth a total value of 50,000 gp

**Primary Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (master), [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (master), or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (master)

**Requirements** You must be a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]

---

You destroy the gathered treasures with a natural magical effect or spell, then invoke necromantic energies before you feed upon the charred and melted remains. As you do so, void energy courses through your flesh, automatically killing you. Each individual ravener’s ravenous reanimation requires three to five unique additional components. Whether or not you return as a ravener depends on the success of the ritual.

**Critical Success** You immediately transform into a [[Bestiary/Ravener|ravener]] upon finishing the ritual; your soul ward starts at full Hit Points (equal to 5 × your level).  

**Success** You rise as a ravener 24 hours after completing the ritual, as long as your body remains relatively intact. Your soul ward starts at 1 Hit Point.  

**Failure** You rise as a [[Bestiary/Ravener Husk|ravener husk]] 24 hours after completing the ritual.  

**Critical Failure** You die.
