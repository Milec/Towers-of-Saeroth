# Living Nexus  *Archetype 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 96

There are places in the world where the essences of magic coalesce and build on each other, creating churning wells of power and streams of the energies. These forces can make up the fabric of the planes or produce entropic fields where magic is simply nullified, consumed before it can be brought into being. Your body is one such place for these powers. Whether by exposure to magical radiation, experimentation, happenstance of birth, or intense training, the essences of magic have warped themselves around you, creating in you a living nexus of power. You have trained yourself to sense with your very being the thrum and pulse of energy that permeates all things. As you absorb the magical energy around you, your soul filters it into pure potential. This power, however, cannot be held for long, as it urges to be released. For others, magic is a formula to be memorized or the will of a deity channeled through them. For you, magic is as easy as breathing.

On Golarion, the accidental transformation into a living nexus usually occurs in places where magic is wild and unpredictable, often suffusing the very air people breathe. Certain parts of the Mana Wastes fit this criteria as the ancient conflict between Geb and Nex scarred this once-beautiful land with stray bolts of energy and clouds of magical radiation. While not many settlements exist in the Mana Wastes outside of Alkenstar, adventurers undertaking explorations into the wastelands have been known to return with the uncanny ability to absorb any magic around them and control it as a living nexus.
