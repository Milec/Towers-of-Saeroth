# Rogue  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 221

You’ve learned to sneak, steal, and disable traps. With time and luck, you’ll become capable of moving through the shadows, striking unseen, and escaping without notice.

### Multiclass Rogue characters

Nearly any character can benefit from the many skills and tricks the rogue archetype grants.

- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|Bard]] rogues expand their robust base of skills. They are also well-suited to take advantage of the distractions caused by their performances and otherwise apply their skills to combat.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-646)|Cleric]] rogues often follow gods that support trickery in combat, such as [[Setting/Deities/Calistria]], learning a combat style that fits their god. However, many clerics might need stealth and trickery when their voices are unwelcome.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|Druid]] rogues can ambush foes in other forms, seamlessly fading into the shadows of the natural world.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-688)|Fighter]] rogues combine the fighter’s accuracy with extra damage, a deadly combination for archers or finesse fighters.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|Ranger]] rogues benefit from the shared focus on trailing foes and catching them off guard. The [[Classes/Subclasses/Hunter's Edges/Flurry|flurry]] edge can get you many sneak attacks, and the [[Classes/Subclasses/Hunter's Edges/Precision|precision]] edge doubles down on precision damage.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|Witch]] rogues apply the hexes of their patrons from the shadows and improve many of the simple weapons they’re trained in.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|Wizard]] rogues use the rogue multiclass to shore up skills or to pick up tricky rogue feats to help keep them safe.
