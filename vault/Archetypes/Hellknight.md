# Hellknight  *Archetype 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 84

Hellknights are among the fiercest warriors in the Inner Sea region, as they have emerged from hell-forged trials ready to serve as faceless bastions of law wherever chaos might threaten to rear.

### Hellknight Archetypes

The following archetypes represent fully inducted members of a Hellknight order. You can select the dedication feats for these archetypes even if you haven’t yet gained three feats from the [[Archetypes/Hellknight Armiger|Hellknight armiger]] archetype. A character can’t have both the Hellknight and [[Archetypes/Hellknight Signifer|Hellknight signifer]] archetypes.
