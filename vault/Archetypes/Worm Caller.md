# Worm Caller  *Archetype 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 80

You follow the ancient [[Bestiary/Families/Duergar|hryngar]] tradition of the worm caller—mystics who can call upon the strength and destructive force of the mighty [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worms]] that inhabit the Darklands. You can manifest worms to attack your enemies, draw upon their senses and resistances, summon one to do your bidding and, eventually, become a worm yourself.
