# Thaumaturge  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 49

Scraps of knowledge have taught you how to exploit the vulnerabilities of creatures and the powers of mystical objects.

### Multiclass Thaumaturge Characters

Thaumaturge suits characters who want to glean strange knowledge. It's especially useful for those who tend to fight with one-handed weapons, like [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-361)|swashbucklers]] or [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|rogues]], since they have their other hand free for an implement.
