# Ranger  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 220

You have studied hunting, tracking, and wilderness survival, adding a ranger’s tools to your skill set. Your keen eyes while hunting prey help gather more information.

### Multiclass Ranger characters

The ranger archetype has access to excellent options to improve a character’s monster knowledge and survival skills, but ranger is of particular interest to any character wanting to become a dedicated archer.

- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|Bard]] rangers support their allies and animal companions with expanded combat options. The bard’s knowledge can also complement the ranger’s investigation of their foes.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-646)|Cleric]] rangers are particularly fitting for deities who connect thematically to nature or have a bow as their favored weapon. (Or, in the case of [[Setting/Deities/Erastil]], both!)
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|Druids]] have the most thematic overlap with rangers among the spellcasters, allowing you to create a true master of the wilderness.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-688)|Fighter]] rangers are among the most fearsome archers, combining the feats from both classes to create a fighting style unique to each fighter ranger. Some warden spells can also be particularly helpful to a fighter.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|Rogue]] rangers make excellent snipers or ranged skirmishers, with their rogue skills naturally playing into the ranger’s strengths.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|Witch]] rangers add a new prowess scouting to the information they can gain through their familiar and patron.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|Wizard]] rangers benefit greatly from ranger’s feat support, backing up their spells with ranged weapon attacks.
