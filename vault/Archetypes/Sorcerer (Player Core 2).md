# Sorcerer  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 181

You coax the magic power in your blood to manifest, accessing magic others don't expect you to have.

### Multiclass Sorcerer Characters

The sorcerer archetype is a method for anyone to pick up commonly useful spells, such as _[[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]]_ and _[[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]]_. You might choose to become a sorcerer with a bloodline that matches other aspects of your character, such as the dragon bloodline if you have the dragonblood versatile heritage or the undead bloodline if you're a dhampir.
