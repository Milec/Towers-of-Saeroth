# Knight Vigilant  *Archetype 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 94

Taking their lead from heroic stories of the Shining Crusade, the knights vigilant stand as a courageous example of morality and honor.

### Knights of Lastwall Archetypes

The following archetypes are uncommon but are available to members of the Knights of Lastwall. Qualifying characters with the [[Archetypes/Lastwall Sentry|Lastwall sentry]] archetype can select one of these dedications even without gaining three feats from the Lastwall sentry archetype, and each of these archetypes’ feats fulfills the [[Feats/Lastwall Sentry Dedication|Lastwall Sentry Dedication’s]] special requirement despite not being from the Lastwall sentry archetype.
