# Talisman Dabbler  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 195

The classic idea of magic is that of a hoary wizard, poring over a crumbling book of spells. But magic is so much more than that—it is thought, will, and action, and with the right [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]], you can make even the cut of a sword a deeply magical act. This all hinges on the small magical talismans you affix to your gear. You know how to make and use the perfect talismans for any job.
