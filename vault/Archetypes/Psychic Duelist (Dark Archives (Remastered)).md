# Psychic Duelist  *Archetype 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 203

The mental landscape of the psychic duel rises to meet your steps and reshapes at your touch. Shattered minds and broken wills from your previous duels exhibit your prowess. You understand that the binary mindscape of a duel links two minds, making its foundation only as strong as the wills of those involved. Two psychic castles in a siege—patch every loose brick in your walls and single out every flaw in your foe's defenses.
