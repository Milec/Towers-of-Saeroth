# Geomancer  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 212

You know how to draw power from the land around you, no matter the terrain, by attuning your magic to match its mystical properties.

 You might be a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druid]] with an elemental focus (such as the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Flame|flame]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Stone|stone]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Wave|wave]] orders) or a naturalist with a touch of magic at your disposal. You could concentrate on a single type of terrain to wring every last drop of power from it or choose to wander the land to experience every type of terrain possible.
