# Spellshifter  *Archetype 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 102

You may or may not cast spells, but you’ve always possessed an uncanny knack for attuning yourself to the ebb and flow of the mystical currents that power them. Thanks to specialized training or natural aptitude, you’ve honed this gift into the ability to exert a modicum of control over magical spells cast nearby, allowing you to reshape and repurpose their energies to suit your own designs. You might use this talent for any number of purposes: to enhance the spells cast by you or your allies, to weaken and confound magic-wielding enemies, or even just to covertly humiliate rival spellcasters foolish enough to cross you.

As a spellshifter, you carry a special implement called a conduit, a seemingly mundane tool or object that you use to tap into the magical vibrations that suffuse the world around you. Only you can use this conduit, and every spellshifter’s conduit is unique. Yours might represent something distinctive about your personality, your relationship to magic, or the circumstances under which you first discovered your powers.

Spellshifters aren’t widely known in many parts of the world, and individuals with the innate potential to become one are sometimes regarded by distrustful spellcasters as jinxes whose mere presence might cause magic to malfunction or behave unpredictably. Past experience with these sorts of superstitions might drive you to actively conceal your powers from those who might fear them, or it might motivate you to seek out any opportunity to flaunt them openly, refusing to be cowed by the prejudice of others.
