# Gunslinger  *Archetype 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 127

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All Pathfinder Society agents have access to the Gunslinger archetype._

You like a weapon that makes an impression almost as much as you like making an impression.

### Multiclass Gunslinger Characters

Gunslinger is a great fit for dexterous characters who want a weapon that hits hard from a distance, especially [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|rangers]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|rogues]]. Spellcasters with [[Spells/Sure Strike|_sure strike_]], especially [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|witches]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bards]] with their one-action cantrips, might favor firearms with the [[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal]] property and gunslinger abilities that improve options for supporting their allies.
