# World Rouser  *Archetype 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 106

Life abounds from the highest mountain peaks to the driest deserts to the deepest seas, worlds that nurture plants and shelter animals. But you know worlds are wonders beyond merely sustaining life. A world like Golarion can cage a god, can recover from 1,000 years of darkness, can destroy a civilization like Tar Khadurrm.

You know what worlds can truly do—and why they don’t. You know they sleep, as they have since almost the moment they were created. But more importantly, you know how to wake a world, if only for a few moments. You call to them, and they stir from their slumber. And during those brief moments where they wake, these worlds speak back to you, whispering in a language only you can hear. Alert, they take actions to sustain you, working their will via the animals, plants, and terrain that call it home.

Those who share this secret are known as world rousers. On Golarion, they are most common among those who live in the wild—[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|rangers]] especially—where the voice of the world isn’t drowned out by the noise of civilization. [[Ancestries/Gnome (Player Core)|Gnomes]], with their roots in the restless [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]], often learn to bestir the world, as do those who live along [[Rules/Ley Lines|ley lines]] and where the barrier between [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] and the First World is thin. In those places, the world seems to rest more fitfully.
