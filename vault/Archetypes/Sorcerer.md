# Sorcerer  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 230

You coax the magic power in your blood to manifest, accessing magic others don’t expect you to have.

### Multiclass Sorcerer characters

The sorcerer archetype is a method for anyone to pick up commonly useful spells, such as [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] and [[Spells/Haste|_haste_]].
