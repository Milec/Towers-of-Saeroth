# Summoner  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 76

You have a tenuous connection to an eidolon, a bodiless being that shares your life force, and with effort you can manifest the eidolon into the world.

### Multiclass Summoner Characters

The summoner archetype grants you an eidolon to join you in battle. It's a particularly helpful multiclass for characters with weaker martial abilities.

- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|Alchemist]] summoners can use their eidolons as lab assistants or even as the source of experiments. They are a good fit for [[Companions/Eidolons/Construct|construct]] or [[Companions/Eidolons/Plant|plant]] eidolons.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|Barbarian]] summoners find themselves drawn to the fury of [[Companions/Eidolons/Anger Phantom|anger phantoms]], the two fueled by the same inner instinct.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|Bard]] summoners inspire their eidolons to greater heights with their bardic performances. They often team up with eidolons who pair well with their muse, such as a [[Companions/Eidolons/Fey|fey]] eidolon for a [[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Polymath|polymath muse]] or a phantom eidolon for a [[Classes/Subclasses/Muses/Warrior|warrior muse]].
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|Champion]] summoners get along best with divine eidolons matching the alignment associated with their cause—[[Companions/Eidolons/Angel|angel]] eidolons for the [[Classes/Subclasses/Tenets/Good|tenets of good]], devil eidolons for a [[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Tyrant|tyrant]], and so on. This allows the champion and eidolon to have a unity in philosophy and purpose that other eidolon choices might lack.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|Cleric]] summoners often find a connection to eidolons associated with their deity, such as a [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae|Sarenite]] cleric who chooses an angel eidolon. Clerics' ability to heal and support allows them to keep their eidolon healthy.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|Druid]] summoners find the life link with primal eidolons to be a religious experience. They are especially likely to bond with eidolons that match their order, such as plant eidolons for the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Leaf|leaf order]] or [[Companions/Eidolons/Beast|beast]] eidolons for the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Animal|animal]] or [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Wild|wild]] order.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|Fighter]] summoners use tactics and techniques with their eidolons to best foes. They can partner with eidolons of any kind, but they work especially well with eidolons who provide utilities that complement their fighting style.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|Monk]] summoners often choose eidolons that are on their own path to enlightenment, to help enrich the monk's journey. These might be divine eidolons, especially if the monk is religious or uses divine ki spells, but they also might be phantoms seeking to transcend their phantom existence to reach the afterlife.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-204)|Ranger]] summoners get along best with beast and plant eidolons, but they make a good team with other eidolons as well, especially when an archer ranger teams up with a melee eidolon.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|Rogue]] summoners gain a partner in crime that can disappear when necessary, providing the eidolon with the ultimate getaway as long as no one expects it to carry back loot from a heist. Rogues work well with tricky eidolons, such as fey, and they get a lot of mileage out of sharing their skills with their eidolon. The [[Feats/Unfetter Eidolon]] feat can be especially useful if the rogue wants to establish a quick alibi far from the eidolon's crimes.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|Sorcerer]] summoners often choose eidolons that match closely to their bloodline, such as a [[Companions/Eidolons/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] eidolon for the [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Psychopomp|psychopomp bloodline]]. The eidolon might even be part of the story of how the sorcerer gained their bloodline.
- [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|Wizard]] summoners are especially appreciative of an eidolon to help them in combat, and they tend to favor arcane eidolons like [[Companions/Eidolons/Dragon|dragon]] and construct eidolons.
