# Psychic  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 48

You've awoken the latent abilities of your mind, taking your first steps into wielding psychic magic.

### Multiclass Psychic Characters

Psychic is a good fit for characters who want a touch of occult power. Intelligence-based characters like [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Advanced Player's Guide)|investigators]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|alchemists]], as well as Charisma-based characters like [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|bards]], can choose the path that best suits their inherent abilities.
