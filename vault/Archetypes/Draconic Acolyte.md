# Draconic Acolyte  *Archetype 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 218

You possess an item infused with draconic essence, whether that be an item of significance gifted to you from a powerful dragon, a piece of treasure stolen from a mighty dragon’s hoard, or even a discarded or forcefully removed piece of a dragon’s body. This object allows you to call on a fragment of that dragon’s essence to perform mighty magical deeds.
