# Druid  *Archetype 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 225

You have entered a druidic circle and learned a few of the order’s secrets, granting you primal power.

### Multiclass Druid characters

The druid archetype can provide a bit of healing or elemental damage and can increase your utility in a natural environment with spells like [[Spells/Speak with Animals|_speak with animals_]] and [[Spells/Meld into Stone|_meld into stone_]].
