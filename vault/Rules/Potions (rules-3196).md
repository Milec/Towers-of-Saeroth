# Potions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 259

A potion is a magical liquid that is activated when you drink it, which uses it up. Potions have the [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|potion]] trait. You can activate a potion with an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action as you drink it or feed it to another creature. You can feed a potion only to a creature that is within reach and willing or otherwise so helpless that it can’t resist. You usually need only one hand to consume a potion or feed it to another creature.
