# Removing Afflictions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 431

Apart from waiting them out, afflictions can be removed through certain uses of the skills and spells. The [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]] and [[Actions/Treat Poison (Player Core)|Treat Poison]] uses of [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] are commonly used to treat those afflictions.

The [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] spell is also available to most spellcasters. Spells that counteract conditions at the source, such as [[Spells/Sound Body|_sound body_]], can also be effective against diseases and poisons that cause those conditions.

Curses are trickier, requiring solutions that specifically mention them, such as a 4th-rank _cleanse affliction_ or the [[Feats/Break Curse]] skill feat.
