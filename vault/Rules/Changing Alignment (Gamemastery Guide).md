# Changing Alignment  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 203

Aside from some obviously aligned NPCs, like the villains, most of the NPCs in this gallery are presented as neutral. If you want to change an NPC's alignment, all you have to do is change their alignment trait. You might consider how the new alignment affects the NPC's personality and adjust their skills accordingly; for instance, a lawful evil [[Bestiary/Teacher|teacher]] might have a higher modifier for [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] than [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]].
