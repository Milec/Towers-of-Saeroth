# Constant Abilities  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 219

Some magic items have abilities that always function. PCs don't have to use any actions to do anything special (beyond wearing and investing a worn item or wielding a held item) to make these abilities work. For example, an _[[Equipment/Everlight Crystal|everlight crystal]]_ always sheds light, and a _[[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming weapon]]_ deals fire damage every time it deals damage.
