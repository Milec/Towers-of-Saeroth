# Constant Abilities  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 531

Some magic items have abilities that always function. You don’t have to use any actions to do anything special (beyond wearing and investing a worn item or wielding a held item) to make these abilities work. For example, an [[Equipment/Everburning Torch|_everburning torch_]] always sheds light, and a [[Equipment/Flaming|_flaming_]] weapon deals fire damage every time it deals damage.
