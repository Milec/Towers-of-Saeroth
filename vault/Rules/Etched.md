# Etched  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 222

Runes must be etched onto permanent items, such as armor, shields, weapons, or [[Equipment/Runestone (GM Core)|runestones]] to grant their benefit. Adding or transferring a rune takes downtime to accomplish. The Usage entry indicates the type or types of items a rune can be etched into, such as “etched onto a weapon.” More information about etching runes is found [[Rules/The Etching Process (GM Core)|here]].
