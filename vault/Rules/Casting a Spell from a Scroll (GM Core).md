# Casting a Spell from a Scroll  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 262

Casting a Spell from a scroll requires holding the scroll in one hand and activating it with a [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity using the normal number of actions for that spell. The spell must appear on your spell list. Because you're the one Casting the Spell, use your spell attack modifier and spell DC. The spell also gains the appropriate trait for your tradition ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]).

Any physical costs are provided when a scroll is created, so you don't need to provide them when casting from a scroll. If the spell requires a [[Rules/Costs and Loci|locus]], you must have that locus to Cast the Spell from a scroll.
