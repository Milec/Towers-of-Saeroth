# Diplomatic Relations  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 534

Diplomatic relations refers to acts of leadership that engage with other nations. In order to begin diplomatic relations with another group, PCs must first successfully [[Actions/Send Diplomatic Envoy|Send a Diplomatic Envoy]] to the target group. When they establish diplomatic relations with a group, record the name of that group on the kingdom sheet. Once the PCs have established diplomatic relations with a group, they can use the [[Actions/Establish Trade Agreement]] and [[Actions/Request Foreign Aid]] Leadership activities.
