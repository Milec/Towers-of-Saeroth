# Stabilize  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 200

If a character is [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] at the start of their turn, their player can spend 1 Resolve Point to stabilize at 0 HP, gaining or increasing the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition as normal for stabilizing. At the start of the character’s next turn, they gain 1 HP and wake up (unless they started dying again). The character can act on that turn. Consider this an optional rule best suited for groups that have little access to healing. When using this rule, you might remove the ability for characters to use Hero Points to stabilize.
