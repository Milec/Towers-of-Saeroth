# Background  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

A background represents what a character experienced before they took up the life of an adventurer. Each background grants a feat and training in one or more skills. You can read more about backgrounds in [[Rules/Backgrounds (Player Core)|Chapter 2]].
