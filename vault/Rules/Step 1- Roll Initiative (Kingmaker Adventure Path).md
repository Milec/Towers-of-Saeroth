# Step 1: Roll Initiative  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 578

Each army in the battle makes a Scouting check to determine its initiative. On the first round of a war encounter, armies are usually [[Rules/The Battlefield|near]]. (Armies that have the [[Subsystems/Warfare Tactics/Ambush]] tactic may be able to begin a war encounter [[Rules/Conditions/Engaged|engaged]]; armies that have the [[Subsystems/Warfare Tactics/Opening Salvo]] tactic may be able to begin a war encounter [[Rules/Conditions/Distant|distant]].)
