# Loosing a Troop  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 161

When a leader’s troop is defeated, the leader remains on the battlefield and can place themself anywhere within the space their troop occupied. If the troop is forced to retreat in some way, such as after gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] condition, the leader can usually choose to stay behind, abandoning their troop. If they exercise this option, they can’t become the leader of that troop again in the same encounter.
