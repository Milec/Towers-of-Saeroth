# Eidolon Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 70

An eidolon normally can't Cast a Spell; however, some feats or abilities can grant them this capability. An eidolon that has spells also gains the Cast a Spell activity. They don't have their own spell DC or spell attack modifier; if they need to Cast a Spell, they use your spell DC and spell attack modifier. If your eidolon has focus spells, those spells count toward your focus pool maximum and your eidolon uses your Focus Points to cast them. Your eidolon can't [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]]. Your eidolon can cast only spells they gain from their own abilities. They can't cast your spells, nor can you cast spells they have.
