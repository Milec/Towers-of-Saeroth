# Incredible Construct Companions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 33

To improve an advanced construct companion to an incredible construct companion, make the following adjustments.

- Increase its Strength, Dexterity, Constitution and Wisdom modifiers by 2.
- It deals 2 additional damage with its unarmed attacks. Its attacks become [[Traits/Magical|magical]], allowing it to bypass resistances to non-magical attacks.
- Increase its proficiency ranks in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] to expert.
