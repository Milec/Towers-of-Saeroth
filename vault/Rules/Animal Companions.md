# Animal Companions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 214

An animal companion is a loyal comrade who follows your orders. Your animal companion has the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] and [[Traits/Minion|minion]] traits, and it gains 2 actions during your turn if you use the Command an Animal action to command it; this is in place of the usual effects of Command an Animal, and you don't need to attempt a Nature check. If your companion dies, you can spend a week of downtime to replace it at no cost. You can have only one animal companion at a time.

A list of available animal companions can be found [here](Companions.aspx).
