# Ancestry  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

An ancestry is the broad family of people that a character belongs to. Ancestry determines a character's starting [[Rules/Hit Points (rules-2320)|Hit Points]], [[Rules/Languages (rules-2095)|languages]], [[Rules/Senses (Player Core)|senses]], and [[Rules/Speed (rules-2347)|Speed]], and it grants access to ancestry feats. Ancestries can be found in [[Rules/Chapter 2- Ancestries & Backgrounds (rules-2074)|Chapter 2]].
