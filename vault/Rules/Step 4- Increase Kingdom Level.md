# Step 4: Increase Kingdom Level  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 539

If your kingdom's XP total is above 1,000, and your kingdom isn't at its maximum level, increase your kingdom level by 1 and subtract 1,000 from your XP total. See [[Rules/Leveling Up your Kingdom|Leveling Up Your Kingdom]] for the full rules for leveling up.
