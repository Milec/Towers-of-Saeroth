# Items with Multiple Types  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 293

If an item has multiple types of different levels, each type has its own formula, and you need the formula for the specific type of item you want to Craft. For example, if you have a formula for a [[Equipment/Bag of Holding|_type I bag of holding_]] but not for a _type II bag of holding_, you must acquire a separate formula to Craft a _type II bag of holding_.
