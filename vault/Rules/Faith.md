# Faith  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 21

Perhaps you’d like to play a character who is a devout follower of a specific deity. Pathfinder is a rich world with myriad faiths and philosophies spanning a wide range, from [[Setting/Deities/Cayden Cailean]], the Drunken Hero of good-hearted adventuring; to [[Setting/Deities/Desna]], the Song of Spheres and goddess of dreaming and the stars; to [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]], the Inheritor, goddess of honor, justice, and rulership. Pathfinder’s major deities appear on pages 437–440. Your character might be so drawn to a particular faith that you decide they should be a champion or cleric of that deity; they might instead be a lay worshipper who applies their faith’s teachings to daily life, or simply the child of devout parents.
