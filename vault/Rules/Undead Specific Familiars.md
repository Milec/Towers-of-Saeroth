# Undead Specific Familiars  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 38

Necromancers and other spellcasters with affinity to undeath bind undead creatures to serve as familiars. Undead specific familiars work no differently from any other specific familiars, though they share certain abilities due to being undead. See [[Rules/Familiars]] for more information on familiars and their abilities, and [[Rules/Specific Familiars|Specific Familars]] for more on specific familiars.
