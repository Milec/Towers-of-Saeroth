# Precious Material Shields  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 233

Shields made of precious materials are more expensive and have different durabilities. You can make [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|bucklers]] and most shields out of any of these precious materials, but only [[Equipment/Duskwood|duskwood]] can be used to make [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|tower shields]].
