# Affixed or Etched  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 535

Some items enhance other items. Talismans function only if affixed to other items. They have a Usage entry indicating the type or types of items to which a character can attach them, such as “affixed to armor.” Rules for affixing a talisman are on page 565.

 Runes must be etched onto permanent items, such as armor, weapons, or [[Equipment/Runestone|_runestones_]] to grant their benefit. Adding or transferring a rune takes downtime to accomplish. The Usage entry indicates the type or types of items a rune can be etched into, such as “etched onto a weapon.” More information about etching runes is found [[Rules/The Etching Process|here]].
