# Effects  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 426

Anything you do in the game has an effect. Many of these outcomes are easy to adjudicate during the game. If you tell the GM that you draw your sword, no check is needed. Other times, the specific effect requires more detailed rules governing how your choice is resolved.

Many spells, magic items, and feats create specific effects, and your character will be subject to effects caused by monsters, hazards, the environment, and other characters.

Effects sometimes require checks, but not always. Casting a [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]] spell on yourself creates an effect that allows you to soar through the air, but casting the spell does not require a check. Conversely, using the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidate]] skill to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a foe does require a check, and your result on that check determines the effect's outcome.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Conditions
>
>
>
> Some effects apply conditions to a creature or item. These change your state of being in some way. Conditions are persistent, lasting until the stated duration ends, the condition is removed, or terms dictated in the condition cause it to end. Full rules appear in the [[Rules/Conditions Appendix]].
>
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|Blinded]]:** You're unable to see.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|Broken]]:** This item can't be used for its normal function until repaired.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|Clumsy]]:** You can't move as easily or gracefully as usual.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|Concealed]]:** Fog or similar obscuration makes you difficult to see and target.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|Confused]]:** You attack indiscriminately.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|Controlled]]:** Another creature determines your actions.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|Dazzled]]:** Everything is concealed to you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|Deafened]]:** You're unable to hear.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|Doomed]]:** With your soul in peril, you are now closer to death.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|Drained]]:** Blood loss or something similar has leached your vitality.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|Dying]]:** You're slipping closer to death.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|Encumbered]]:** You're carrying more weight than you can manage.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|Enfeebled]]:** Your strength has been sapped away.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|Fascinated]]:** You are compelled to focus your attention on something.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|Fatigued]]:** Your defenses are lower and you can't use exploration activities while traveling.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|Fleeing]]:** You must run away.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Friendly (Player Core)|Friendly]]:** An NPC with this condition has a good attitude toward you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|Frightened]]:** Fear makes you less capable of attacking and defending.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|Grabbed]]:** A creature, object, or magic holds you in place.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Helpful (Player Core)|Helpful]]:** An NPC with this condition wants to assist you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|Hidden]]:** A creature you're hidden from knows your location but can't see you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|Hostile]]:** An NPC with this condition wants to harm you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|Immobilized]]:** You can't move.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent (Player Core)|Indifferent]]:** An NPC with this condition doesn't have a strong opinion about you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|Invisible]]:** Creatures can't see you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|Observed]]:** You're in plain view.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard]]:** You're unable to defend yourself to your full capability.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|Paralyzed]]:** Your body is frozen in place.  
> **[[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|Persistent Damage]]:** You keep taking damage every round.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|Petrified]]:** You've been turned to stone.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|Prone]]:** You're lying on the ground and easier to attack.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|Quickened]]:** You get an extra action each turn.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|Restrained]]:** You're tied up and can't move, or a grappling creature has you [[Rules/Conditions/Pinned|pinned]].  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|Sickened]]:** You're sick to your stomach.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|Slowed]]:** You lose actions each turn.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|Stunned]]:** You can't use actions.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|Stupefied]]:** You can't access your full mental faculties, and you have trouble casting spells.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|Unconscious]]:** You're asleep or knocked out.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|Undetected]]:** A creature you're undetected by doesn't know where you are.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly (Player Core)|Unfriendly]]:** An NPC with this condition doesn't like you.  
> **[[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|Unnoticed]]:** A creature is entirely unaware you're present.
