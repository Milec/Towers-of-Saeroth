# Dedicated Attendant 3rd  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 64

At 3rd level in a branch, if you have completed your Perquisite, you have proven your commitment to a sufficient degree that your branch promotes and supports you as an affiliated attendant. The first time you reach this level in a branch, you gain the [[Feats/Magaambyan Attendant Dedication]] feat as a bonus feat (even if you haven't taken enough feats with a previous archetype to take another dedication feat).
