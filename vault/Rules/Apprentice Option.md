# Apprentice Option  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 195

If the story you want to tell is about characters who have started training to become a particular class, you can grant them a small number of additional abilities. An apprentice character is trained in the skill or skills specified for their chosen class (such as [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] and [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] for a bard) in addition to the skills they gain through their initial proficiencies. They also gain benefits based on the class.
