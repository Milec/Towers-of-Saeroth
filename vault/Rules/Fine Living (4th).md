# Fine Living (4th)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 513

Your people celebrate your leadership by indulging you with feasts and finery. All PCs associated with the kingdom enjoy a [[Rules/Cost of Living|Fine standard of living]] at no cost whenever they're in the kingdom. Any PCs in hostile wilderness, a monster-filled dungeon, or otherwise cut off from their citizens must provide their own sustenance as usual even if they are within the boundaries of their kingdom.

 You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all checks made to [[Actions/Craft]] or [[Actions/Earn Income]] while in your kingdom.
