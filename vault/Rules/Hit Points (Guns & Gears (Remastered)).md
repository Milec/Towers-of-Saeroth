# Hit Points  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 32

Your construct companion has 10 Hit Points, plus a number of Hit Points equal to 6 plus its Constitution modifier for each level you have.

 While constructs are usually immediately destroyed at 0 Hit Points, your construct companion is a little harder to destroy than other constructs are. When your construct reaches 0 Hit Points, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]] and begins sparking and might be destroyed if you don't [[Actions/Repair]] it. This works the same as the normal [[Rules/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying|dying rules]] and determines if your construct is destroyed, with the following two changes. First, most effects that end the dying condition don't work to save a construct companion, but a construct can be stabilized using the [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] action, using the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill instead of [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]. Second, instead of gaining and tracking the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition, if your construct becomes broken in this way more than twice within a 10-minute period, it's destroyed, and you'll need to reconstruct it by spending a day of downtime.
