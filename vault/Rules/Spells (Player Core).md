# Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 399

Most of the rules for casting spells are in [[Rules/Chapter 7- Spells (rules-2221)|Chapter 7]]. For a spell that requires an attack roll against the target's AC, you'll calculate your **[[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack modifier]]**. For one that causes its subject to attempt a saving throw, you'll need your **[[Rules/Spell DC|spell DC]]**.
