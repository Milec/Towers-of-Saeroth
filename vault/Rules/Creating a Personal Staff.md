# Creating a Personal Staff  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 166

Establishing magical pathways to turn a simple piece of wood or metal into a staff is no simple matter. Without some structure to bind multiple disparate spells to a single staff, the magic would surely fail. Thus, a custom staff must always be created around a single trait. For example, an elemental trait ([[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Water|water]]), energy trait ([[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], fire, [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]], or [[Traits/Force|force]]), alignment trait, the [[Traits/Detection|detection]] trait, the [[Traits/Light|light]] trait, and so on. The staff and its spells must have the trait. A few traits are too broad to use, including [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] and the traits for spell schools and traditions. The GM might add others to this list.
