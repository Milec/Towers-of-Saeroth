# Advanced Undead Benefits  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 44

If your archetype has a feat that gives you advanced undead benefits, you gain the following.

 **Darkvision**: You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you don't already have it.  
 **Greater Disease and Poison Protection**: Your bonus against [[Traits/Disease|disease]] and [[Traits/Poison|poison]] increases to +2. You gain poison resistance equal to half your level.  
 **Paralysis and Sleep Protection**: You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws (or any other defense) against effects that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] or have the [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] trait.
