# Ruin Resistance (5th)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 513

At 5th level and every 3 levels thereafter, your kingdom becomes more resistant to Ruin. Choose one of the four Ruin categories and increase its threshold by 2. When you do so, reset that Ruin's penalty to 0. See [[Rules/Ruin]] for more information.
