# Movement  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 398

Your **[[Rules/Speed (rules-2347)|Speed]]** governs how far you can move. **[[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]** is an action that has the move trait and allows you to move a number of feet up to your Speed. You may need to Stride multiple times in a turn! [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]] actions can often **trigger reactions** or free actions. However, unlike other actions, a move action can trigger reactions not only when you first use the action, but also for every 5 feet you move during that action (see [[Rules/Tactical Movement|Reactions to Movement]]). The **[[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]** action lets you move without triggering reactions, but only 5 feet. Other basic actions with the move trait include **[[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]]**, **[[Actions/Drop Prone (Player Core)|Drop Prone]]**, and **[[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]]**.

This game measures **[[Rules/Grid Movement (Player Core)|movement on a grid]]**. **[[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|Difficult terrain]]** and other types of [[Rules/Terrain (Player Core)|terrain]] may impede your movement.

Creatures can get tactical advantages by careful positioning. The most common are using [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from terrain and other creatures to increase your AC, and **[[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]]**, which requires you and an ally to be on the opposite sides of an enemy to reduce the enemy's AC.
