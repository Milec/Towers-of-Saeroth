# Counting Damage Dice  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 406

Effects based on a weapon's number of damage dice include only the weapon's damage die plus any extra dice from a _[[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking rune]]_. They don't count extra dice from abilities, critical specialization effects, property runes, weapon traits, or the like.
