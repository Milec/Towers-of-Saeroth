# Traits  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 522

The most notable hazard traits are [[Traits/Trap|trap]] (constructed to harm intruders), [[Traits/Environmental|environmental]] (natural hazards), and [[Traits/Haunt|haunt]] (spectral phenomena). Traps have a trait to indicate whether they're [[Traits/Magical|magical]] or [[Traits/Mechanical|mechanical]]. Hazards that have initiative and a routine have the [[Traits/Complex|complex]] trait.
