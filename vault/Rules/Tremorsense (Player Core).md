# Tremorsense  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 433

Tremorsense allows a creature to feel the vibrations through a solid surface caused by movement. It is usually an imprecise sense with a limited range (listed in the ability). Tremorsense functions only if the detecting creature is on the same surface as the subject, and only if the subject is moving along (or [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|burrowing]] through) the surface.
