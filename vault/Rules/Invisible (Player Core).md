# Invisible  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 434

A creature with the [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] condition (by way of an [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] spell, for example) is automatically [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] to any creatures relying on sight as their only [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]]. Precise senses other than sight ignore the invisible condition. You can [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] to attempt to figure out an invisible creature's location, making it only hidden from you. This lasts until the invisible creature successfully uses [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] to become undetected again. If you're already [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observing]] a creature when it becomes invisible, it starts out [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], since you know where it was, though it can then Sneak to become undetected.

Other effects might partially foil invisibility. For instance, if you were tracking an invisible creature's footprints through the snow, the footprints would make it hidden. Throwing a [[Equipment/Net (Player Core)|net]] over an invisible creature would make it observed but [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] for as long as the net is on it.
