# Game Master (GM)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

The Game Master is the player who adjudicates the rules and narrates the various elements of the Pathfinder story and world that the other players explore. The GM uses the rules found in [[Rules/Introduction (GM Core)|Pathfinder GM Core]].
