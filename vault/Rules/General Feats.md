# General Feats  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 68

This section specifies the levels at which your character gains general feats. Most classes grant a general feat at 3rd level and every 4 levels thereafter. At each of these levels, you can select any general feat (including skill feats) as long as your character qualifies for it. More information can be found [here](Feats.aspx).
