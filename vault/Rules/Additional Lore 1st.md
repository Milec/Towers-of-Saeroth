# Additional Lore 1st  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 63

At 1st level in a branch, you learn more of the Magaambya's deep well of lore associated with your branch. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] feat in a Lore skill associated with the branch.
