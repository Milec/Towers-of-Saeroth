# Proficiencies  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 33

Your construct companion is trained in its unarmed attacks, unarmored defense, all saving throws, [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]], [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]. Construct companions can’t use abilities that require greater Intelligence, such as [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] or [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], even if trained in the appropriate skill, unless they have an ability that allows it.
