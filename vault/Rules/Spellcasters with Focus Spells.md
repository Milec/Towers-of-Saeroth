# Spellcasters with Focus Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 302

If you are a spellcaster, your focus spells are the same tradition of spell as the class that gave you the focus spell. A [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|bard’s]] are occult, a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric’s]] are divine, a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druid’s]] are primal, a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizard’s]] are arcane, and a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerer’s]] are determined by their bloodline.
