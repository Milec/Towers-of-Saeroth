# Losing the Dying Condition  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 411

You lose the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition if it ever reaches dying 0. If you're still at 0 Hit Points, you remain [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], but you can wake up as described in that condition. You lose the dying condition automatically and wake up if you ever have 1 Hit Point or more.

Anytime you lose the dying condition, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] 1 condition, or increase your wounded value by 1 if you already have that condition.
