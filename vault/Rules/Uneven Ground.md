# Uneven Ground  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 476

Uneven ground is an area unsteady enough that you need to Balance (see [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]) or risk falling prone and possibly injuring yourself, depending on the specifics of the uneven ground. You are flat-footed on uneven ground. Each time you are hit by an attack or fail a save on uneven ground, you must succeed at a Reflex save (with the same DC as the Acrobatics check to Balance) or fall prone.
