# Attacking  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 398

**[[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]** actions have the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] trait and allow you to attack with a weapon you're wielding or an unarmed attack (such as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]]). If you're using a melee weapon or unarmed attack, your target must be within your **[[Rules/Range and Reach (Player Core)|reach]]**; if you're attacking with a ranged weapon, your target must be within your **[[Rules/Range and Reach (Player Core)|range]]**. Ranged weapons get less effective as you exceed their **[[Rules/Range Penalty (Player Core)|range increments]]**. Striking multiple times in a turn has diminishing returns. A **[[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]]** applies to each attack after the first.
