# Essence Powers  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 236

Your soulforged armament has an essence power you choose when you first bind it. It gains the power temporarily when you manifest its essence form. Each power has a corruption flaw that affects you if you have [[Rules/Corruption (Secrets of Magic)|_soulforged corruption_]]. Essence powers that only apply to some types of armament indicate which.

 A list of available essence powers can be found [[Archetypes/Soulforger|here.]]
