# NPC Pilot  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 36

The PCs might want to have an NPC pilot their ship. If an NPC is piloting a ship during a naval battle and the PCs are primarily fighting, it’s best to assume that the ship is staying on course to allow for combat to take center stage rather than have the pilot make tricky maneuvers. Have the NPC pilot [[Actions/Drive (Varies)|Drive]] with 1 action; assume they succeed rather than rolling. A PC can spend a single action to give specific orders to the NPC, in which case the NPC typically tries to enact those orders the next time they Drive.
