# Urban Grid Borders  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 540

The four sides of the Urban Grid are where you record the types of borders your settlement has. **Land Borders**: By default, all of your settlement's borders are unremarkable transitions from urban to hinterland—these are known as Land Borders. You take a cumulative –1 item penalty on Trade checks for each settlement in your kingdom that has no Land Borders, unless it has at least one Water Border with a [[Rules/Terrain Features|Bridge]].

 **Water Borders**: When you place a settlement in a hex that has lake, river, or swamp terrain, you can locate it so that it has Water Borders. Water Borders provide natural defenses to your settlement during [[Rules/Skills/Warfare]], and some structures can only be constructed in lots adjacent to Water Borders. However, crossing Water Borders that lack Bridges takes a long time (see [[Rules/Navigating an Urban Grid]]).

 If a settlement has only Water Borders, it is on an island; until you build at least one Bridge, that settlement's [[Rules/Influence (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|influence]] is 0.

 **Walled Borders**: Building [[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Wall, Wooden|Walls]] on your borders boosts your settlement's defense in certain events and in Warfare.
