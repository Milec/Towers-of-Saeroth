# Soul Path  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 101

When you take Soulforger Dedication, you must choose a soul path—a motivation, cause, or goal that compels you to act and drives you to face significant danger. Such a motivation can be limited or grand in scope but must be actionable enough to be easily linked to gameplay. Work with your GM to choose a soul path that fits in well with the themes of your campaign and group dynamic, allowing you to pursue your soul path in an interesting fashion.
