# Hit Points (HP)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

[[Rules/Hit Points (rules-2320)|Hit Points]] represent the amount of punishment a creature can take before it falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and begins [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]]. Damage decreases Hit Points on a 1-to-1 basis, while healing restores Hit Points at the same rate.
