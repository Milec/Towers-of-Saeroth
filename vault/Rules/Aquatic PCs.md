# Aquatic PCs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 9

[[Rules/Sidebars/Athamaru Metamorphosis|Athamarus]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Outsea|merfolk]], and some [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing an Animal|awakened animals]] can be amphibious or aquatic. If your players are interested in playing an aquatic character in a primarily terrestrial campaign, consider starting them off with assistive devices such as the [[Equipment/Atmospheric Breathing Suit|atmospheric breathing suit]], [[Equipment/Pelagic Helmet|pelagic helmet]], or [[Equipment/Wheelchair|supramarine chair]] at no additional cost. For a less technological option, you might consider granting a PC feats such as [[Feats/Land Legs]] or [[Feats/Shore Gift]] as a bonus feat at level 1, possibly adding certain narrative limitations like “the merfolk PC gains Shore Gift as a bonus feat, but loses its benefits when the moon is full” if they suit your story.
