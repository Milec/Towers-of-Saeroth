# Counteracting  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 305

Some spells, such as [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]], can be used to eliminate the effects of other spells. At least one creature, object, or manifestation of the spell you are trying to counteract must be within range of the spell that you are using. You attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check using your spellcasting ability modifier and your proficiency bonus for spell attack rolls.
