# Access Entries  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 35

Uncommon elements sometimes have an Access entry in their stat block. A character who meets the specifications listed there has access to that option just like they would to a common option, even though it’s uncommon. These entries include a statement such as “follower of [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn]],” “member of the Pathfinder Society,” or “from Absalom.”
