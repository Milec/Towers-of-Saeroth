# Introduction  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 5

The Pathfinder Roleplaying Game features a high-fantasy setting where almost any story can be told. Where the _Core Rulebook_ provides classic options for play and _Secrets of Magic_ provides options for characters to delve deep into the magical mysteries of Golarion, _Guns & Gears_ presents the secrets of black powder weapons and clockwork technology, combining grit and steampunk to produce a flavor all its own.
