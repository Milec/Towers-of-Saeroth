# Heroic Recovery  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 460

If you have at least 1 [[Rules/Aquatic Combat|Hero Point]], you can spend all of your remaining Hero Points at the start of your turn or when your [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] value would increase. You lose the dying condition entirely and stabilize with 0 Hit Points. You don't gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition or increase its value from losing the dying condition in this way, but if you already had that condition, you don't lose it or decrease its value.
