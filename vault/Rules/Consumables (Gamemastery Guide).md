# Consumables  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 85

Consumables have a slightly narrower range, with top-end items like scrolls, optimum healing potions, or super-useful consumables like a [[Equipment/Invisibility Potion|_potion of invisibility_]] at the high end.
