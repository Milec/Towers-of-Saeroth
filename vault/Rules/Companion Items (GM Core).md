# Companion Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 272

You might want to acquire items that benefit a creature that assists you, such as an [[Rules/Animal Companions (Player Core)|animal companion]], [[Rules/Familiars (Player Core)|familiar]], or [[Feats/Bonded Animal (Player Core)|bonded animal]]. These items have the [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]] trait, meaning they function only for animal companions, familiars, and similar creatures. Normally, these are the only items a companion can use. Other items can qualify at the GM's discretion, but a companion can never [[Rules/Activating Items (GM Core)|Activate an Item]].

Any worn companion item needs to be invested. However, your companion needs to invest it, rather than you doing so. This requires you to use the [[Actions/Invest an Item]] activity alongside your companion, helping them attune to the item and ensuring it is properly fit. A companion has an investiture limit of two items (instead of the 10-item limit a player character has).
