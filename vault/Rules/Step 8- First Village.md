# Step 8: First Village  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 511

Somewhere in that heartland hex lies the kingdom's first village—the capital. Rules for founding settlements begin on [[Rules/Founding a Village]], but the players can skip Step 1 and Step 2 of that process when founding this village. Since this is their first village, the PCs gain 40 kingdom XP as a [[Rules/Milestone XP Awards|milestone award]]; record it on the kingdom sheet, along with the capital's name. If the site the PCs have chosen has any established structures listed in the hex's resources in Chapter 2, place them in blocks of the PCs' choice on an [[Rules/The Urban Grid|Urban Grid]] . (The PCs won't add new structures to the settlement before their first Kingdom turn.)
