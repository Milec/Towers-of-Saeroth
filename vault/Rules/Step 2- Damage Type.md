# Step 2: Damage Type  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 407

Once you've calculated how much damage you deal, you'll need to determine the damage type. The smack of a [[Equipment/Weapons/Club (Player Core)|club]] deals bludgeoning damage. The shock of a _[[Spells/Lightning Bolt (Player Core)|lightning bolt]]_ spell deals electricity damage. Sometimes you might apply precision damage, dealing more damage for hitting a creature in a vulnerable spot or when the target is somehow vulnerable.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Damage Types
>
>
>
> Damage has a number of different types and categories, which are described below.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Physical Damage
>
> Damage dealt by weapons, many physical hazards, and a handful of spells is collectively called physical damage. The main types of physical damage are bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. **Bludgeoning** damage comes from weapons and hazards that deal blunt-force trauma, like a hit from a club or being dashed against rocks. **Piercing** damage is dealt from stabs and punctures, whether from a dragon's fangs or the thrust of a spear. **Slashing** damage is delivered by a cut, be it the swing of the sword or the blow from a scythe blades trap.
>
> [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|Ghosts]] and other [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures have a high resistance to physical attacks that aren't magical (attacks that lack the magical trait). Furthermore, most incorporeal creatures have additional, though lower, resistance to magical physical damage (such as damage dealt from a [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|mace]] with the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] trait) and most other damage types.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Energy Damage
>
> Many spells and other magical effects deal energy damage. Energy damage is also dealt from effects in the world, such as the biting cold of a blizzard to a raging forest fire. The main types of energy damage are acid, cold, electricity, fire, and sonic. **[[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]]** damage can be delivered by gases, liquids, and certain solids that dissolve flesh, and sometimes harder materials. **[[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]]** damage freezes material by way of contact with chilling gases and ice. **[[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]** damage comes from the discharge of powerful lightning and sparks. **[[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]** damage burns through heat and combustion. **[[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]** damage assaults matter with high-frequency vibration and sound waves. Many times, you deal energy damage by casting magic spells, and doing so is often useful against creatures that have immunities or resistances to physical damage.
>
> Two special types of energy damage specifically target the living and the undead. **[[Traits/Vitality]]** damage harms only undead creatures, withering undead bodies and disrupting incorporeal undead. **[[Traits/Void]]** damage saps life, damaging only living creatures.
>
> Powerful and pure magical energy can manifest itself as **[[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]]** damage. Few things can resist this type of damage—not even incorporeal creatures such as ghosts and [[Traits/Wraith (Monster Core)|wraiths]].  
>
>
>
>
> ## Spirit Damage
>
> Directly affecting the spiritual essence of a creature, **[[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]** damage can damage a target projecting its consciousness or possessing another creature even if the target's body is elsewhere. The possessed creature isn't harmed by the blast. Spirit damage doesn't harm creatures that have no spirit, such as [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]]. Many effects that deal spirit damage also have the [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], or [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Mental Damage
>
> Sometimes an effect can target the mind with enough psychic force to actually deal damage to the creature. When it does, it deals **[[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]** damage. [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|Mindless]] creatures and those with only programmed or rudimentary intelligence are often immune to mental damage and effects.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Poison Damage
>
> Venoms, toxins and the like can deal **[[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]** damage, which affects creatures by way of contact, ingestion, inhalation, or injury. In addition to coming from monster attacks, alchemical items, and spells, poison damage is often caused by ongoing afflictions, which follow special rules described [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|here]].  
>
>
>
>
> ## Bleed Damage
>
> Another special type of physical damage is **bleed** damage. This is typically [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]], and represents loss of blood. Typically nonliving creatures and living ones that don't need blood to live are immune to bleed. Weaknesses and resistances to physical damage apply. Bleed damage ends automatically if you're healed to your full Hit Points.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Precision Damage
>
> Sometimes you are able to make the most of your attack through sheer precision. When you hit with an ability that grants you **precision** damage, you increase the attack's listed damage, using the same damage type, rather than tracking a separate pool of damage. For example, a nonmagical [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]] Strike that deals 1d6 precision damage from a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|rogue's]] sneak attack increases the piercing damage by 1d6.
>
> Some creatures are immune to precision damage, regardless of the damage type; these are often amorphous creatures that lack vulnerable anatomy. A creature immune to precision damage would ignore the 1d6 precision damage in the example above, but it would still take the rest of the piercing damage from the Strike. Since precision damage is always the same type of damage as the attack it's augmenting, a creature that is resistant to physical damage, like a [[Bestiary/Gargoyle (Monster Core)|gargoyle]], would resist not only the dagger's damage but also the precision damage, even though it is not specifically resistant to precision damage.  
>
>
>
>
> ## Precious Materials
>
> While not their own damage category, precious materials can modify damage to penetrate a creature's resistances or take advantage of its weaknesses. For instance, [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] weapons are particularly effective against [[Traits/Werecreature (GM Core)|werecreatures]] and bypass the resistances to physical damage that most [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devils]] have.
