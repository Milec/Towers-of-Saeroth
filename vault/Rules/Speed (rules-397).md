# Speed  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 463

Most characters and monsters have a speed statistic—also called land Speed—which indicates how quickly they can move across the ground. When you use the Stride action, you move a number of feet equal to your Speed. Numerous other abilities also allow you to move, from [[Actions/Crawl|Crawling]] to [[Actions/Leap|Leaping]], and most of them are based on your Speed in some way. Whenever a rule mentions your Speed without specifying a type, it’s referring to your land Speed.
