# Damage and Conditions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 430

Any damage listed for a stage happens immediately when you reach that stage. Conditions affect you when you reach the stage and last for their normal duration. For instance, if you were [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] for an affliction with a maximum duration of 5 minutes, you remain drained after the affliction ends, as normal for the drained condition. A condition that automatically changes its value or ends under certain circumstances, like [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], still does so. Any condition that doesn’t have a default duration, such as [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], lasts as long as you’re at that stage unless noted otherwise, as do any penalties or any other effect of the stage that doesn’t list a duration.
