# Divine Adjustments  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 219

The creature gains the [[Traits/Divine|divine]] trait and can cast a single innate spell from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine spell list]] once per day. Use the creature's level (see the table below) to determine the spell's level. Use the spell DC and spell attack roll on the table if the creature does not already have those statistics.
