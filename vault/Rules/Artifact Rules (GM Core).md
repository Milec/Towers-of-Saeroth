# Artifact Rules  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 300

Every artifact is an item with the [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|artifact]] trait. This trait means two things: the item can’t be crafted or have its runes adjusted by normal means, and it can’t be damaged by normal means. Artifacts are always [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|rare]] or [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|unique]]. The ones found here are all 20th level or higher, which is typical for artifacts. The other rules for wearing or using the item still apply.
