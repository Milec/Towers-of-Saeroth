# Halfling  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 204

- Change the [[Traits/Human|human]] trait to [[Traits/Halfling|halfling]].
- Add [[Traits/Halfling]] to the NPC's languages.
- Add the keen eyes ability below.

### Keen Eyes
 Your eyes are sharp, allowing you to make out small details about concealed or even invisible creatures that others might miss. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creatures within 30 feet of you. When you target an opponent that is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] from you or hidden from you, reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or 9 for a hidden one.
