# Death  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 460

After you die, you can’t act or regain actions or be affected by spells that target creatures (unless they specifically target dead creatures), and for all other purposes you are an object. When you die, you are reduced to 0 Hit Points if you had a different amount, and you can’t be brought above 0 Hit Points as long as you remain dead. Some magic can bring creatures back to life, such as the [[Rituals/Resurrect|_resurrect_]] ritual or the [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]] spell.
