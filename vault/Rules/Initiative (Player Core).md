# Initiative  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

At the start of an encounter, all creatures involved roll for initiative to determine the order in which they act. The higher the result of its roll, the earlier a creature gets to act. Initiative and combat are described in [[Rules/Encounter Mode (Player Core)|Chapter 8]].
