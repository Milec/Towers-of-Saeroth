# Spellguns  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 96

The [[Traits/Spellgun (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|spellgun]] trait appears on items that can generate a magical effect that flies out like a bullet. You can make either a spell attack roll with a spellgun or a ranged attack roll using your proficiency with simple firearms. Spellguns have a range increment, which applies regardless of which type of attack roll you make. Though you can use your modifier for simple firearms, a spellgun isn't actually a firearm. You don't get the damage of a firearm Strike, nor other benefits like critical specialization. Similarly, you can't load or reload a spellgun, affix [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]] to one, attach scopes, affix [[Traits/Attached|attached]] weapons like [[Equipment/Weapons/Bayonet (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|bayonets]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Reinforced Stock (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|reinforced stocks]], and so on.
