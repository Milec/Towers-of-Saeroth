# Nonlethal Attacks  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 407

You can make a nonlethal attack to knock someone out instead of killing them (see [[Rules/Getting Knocked Out|Knocked Out]] and [[Rules/Dying (Player Core)|Dying]]). Weapons with the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait (including [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fists]]) do this automatically. You take a –2 circumstance penalty to the attack roll when you make a nonlethal attack using a weapon or unarmed attack that doesn’t have the nonlethal trait. You also take this penalty when making a lethal attack using a nonlethal weapon. Spells and other effects with the nonlethal trait that reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points knock the creature out instead of killing them.
