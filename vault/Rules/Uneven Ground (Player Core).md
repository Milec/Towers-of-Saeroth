# Uneven Ground  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 423

Uneven ground is an area unsteady enough that you need to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] or risk falling [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and possibly injuring yourself, depending on the specifics of the uneven ground. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] on uneven ground. Each time you are hit by an attack or fail a save on uneven ground, you must succeed at a Reflex save (with the same DC as the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to Balance) or fall prone.
