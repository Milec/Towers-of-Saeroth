# Check  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

When a character attempts an action where success is uncertain, they may roll a [[Rules/Checks (rules-2278)|check]]. This is a roll of one twenty-sided die plus the named proficiency bonus. For example, a [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] check would add your Perception proficiency.
