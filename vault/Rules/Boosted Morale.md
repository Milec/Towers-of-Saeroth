# Boosted Morale  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 160

A leader’s presence inures their troop against terror and retreat. Troops in a skirmish encounter have a chance to become afraid or run away, as described under [[Rules/Routing Troops]]. When a troop with a leader attempts a rout roll, it gets a result one degree of success better than it rolled.
