# Attack  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

When a creature tries to harm another creature, it makes a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or uses some other attack action. Most attacks are Strikes made with a weapon, but a character might Strike with their [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]] or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|shove]] with their hands, or attack with a [[Rules/Chapter 7- Spells (rules-2221)|spell]].
