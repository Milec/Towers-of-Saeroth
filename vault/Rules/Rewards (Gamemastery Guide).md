# Rewards  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 38

The _Core Rulebook_ explains ([[Rules/Rewards]]) three types of concrete rewards covered by the rules: Hero Points, Experience Points, and treasure. Experience Points and treasure are the bedrock of progress in a campaign, since attaining a higher level and acquiring magic items let PCs take on more challenging adventures.
