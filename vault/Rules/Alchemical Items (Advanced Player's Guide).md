# Alchemical Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 247

Alchemical items are consumable, non-magical items that rely on the properties of volatile alchemical reagents to produce their effects. Rules for alchemical items, including the rules for [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|bombs]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixirs]], and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|poisons]], are located [[Rules/Alchemical Items|here]].
