# Intelligent Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 304

Magic items with a mind of their own have been a hallmark of myth and fantasy for millennia. When integrated into a campaign, they can become memorable characters in their own right.

Intelligent items are a special kind of magic item that straddles the line between treasure and NPC. An intelligent item might be another type of item as well: it could be an [[Rules/Artifacts (GM Core)|artifact]] (like [[Equipment/Serithtial (GM Core)|Serithtial]]), a [[Rules/Cursed Items (GM Core)|cursed item]], or even a [[Rules/Relics (GM Core)|relic]] that grows with its wielder.

Introducing an intelligent item is an effective way to subtly alter the party dynamic. An intelligent item works well when its personality makes it a natural complement or foil for its partner: the PC [[Rules/Investing Magic Items (GM Core)|investing]], holding, or otherwise interacting with the item. An intelligent item that can communicate only with that particular PC is also a great way to engage players who are a bit quieter, or those slower to speak in a scene where all the PCs can talk to a particular NPC. Due to their inherently limited agency, intelligent items are at less of a risk for stealing the spotlight than other NPCs who travel along with the party.
