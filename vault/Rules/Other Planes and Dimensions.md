# Other Planes and Dimensions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 419

Some planes exist in the same physical or metaphysical space as others, overlapping these other planes like overlays of reality. They include the ghost world of the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]], which overlays the Inner Sphere; the dizzying vastness of the [[Setting/Planes/Astral Plane]], which overlays almost all reality and fills the seemingly endless gulf between the Inner Sphere and the Outer Sphere; and the fey-haunted [[Setting/Planes/First World]] and the sinister [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]], which each overlay the Material Plane. Stranger reaches of the Great Beyond exist as well; some, such as the [[Setting/Planes/Dreamlands]], are visited often (if accidentally), while others, such as the enigmatic [[Setting/Planes/Dimension of Time]], are visited rarely, if ever. In all cases these dimensions cannot be accessed by normal means, and each has their own method of entry and exit.
