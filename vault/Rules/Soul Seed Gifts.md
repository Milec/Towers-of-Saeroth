# Soul Seed Gifts  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 230

The GM should follow the same guidelines and rules for granting soul seeds as for [[Rules/Relics|relics]]. They can have any aspect or gift that a normal relic has, and their DCs, spell attack rolls, and counteract modifiers are determined identically. The following gifts are particularly appropriate for soul seeds, though they might apply to relics in the right circumstances. Dragon gifts are often connected to the souls of ancient dragons, and soul gifts are prevalent across all sorts of soul seeds.
