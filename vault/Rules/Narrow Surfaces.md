# Narrow Surfaces  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 476

A narrow surface is so precariously thin that you need to Balance (see [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]) or risk falling. Even on a success, you are flat-footed on a narrow surface. Each time you are hit by an attack or fail a save on a narrow surface, you must succeed at a Reflex save (with the same DC as the Acrobatics check to Balance) or fall.
