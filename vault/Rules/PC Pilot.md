# PC Pilot  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 36

If a player character is piloting the ship, let them decide how to [[Actions/Drive (Varies)|Drive]] it and roll normally. The Drive action is built to work well when multitasking, so if the pilot has few things to do, the player might be too strongly tempted to take complicated Drive actions. It’s best to make sure the pilot has other options for the remaining 2 actions during their turn, such as casting spells, making ranged attacks, or commanding NPC sailors.
