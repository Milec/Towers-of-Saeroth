# Actions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 398

During an encounter, you get **3 actions and 1 reaction** per [[Rules/Turns (Player Core)|turn]]. Icons indicate whether your abilities take a single action single action, 2 actions two actions, 3 actions three actions, a reaction ⤾, or a free action free action. **Reactions** have **[[Rules/Actions with Triggers (Player Core)|triggers]]**, allowing you to take them whenever they come up. The **[[Actions/Ready (Player Core)|Ready]]** basic action lets you prepare to use a single action as a reaction. Free actions can have triggers like reactions; a free action with no trigger can be used like a single action, but don't cost any of your actions for the turn.

The most important actions to learn are the **[[Rules/Basic Actions (Player Core)|basic actions]]**. [[Rules/Specialty Basic Actions (Player Core)|Specialty basic actions]] come up less frequently, and you typically won't look them up until you need them. **Speaking** normally doesn't take an action.
