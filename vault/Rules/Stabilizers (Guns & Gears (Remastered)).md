# Stabilizers  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 182

The ferocious kickback of firearms is part of the functionality that makes them so appealing as weapons, but it can also present a barrier in wielding them effectively. To increase the functionality of firearms and make them more accessible to a wider audience, gunsmiths and engineers have created a large array of supporting stabilization over the years. From the humble [[Equipment/Tripod (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|tripod]] to more elaborate arrays like the gunner's saddle, these tools can be an invaluable part of any gunner's loadout.
