# Damage  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 399

Attacks, spells, and other dangers **[[Rules/Damage Rolls (rules-2301)|deal damage]]**. The amount is typically determined by a **[[Rules/Damage Rolls (rules-2301)|damage roll]]**, which can use a variety of sizes and numbers of dice.

Damage reduces the **[[Rules/Hit Points (rules-2102)|Hit Points]]** (HP) that measure a creature's overall health or an object's durability. A creature might have **[[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunity]]** to damage or effects of certain kinds, a **[[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]]** that reduces the damage it takes, or a **[[Rules/Weakness|weakness]]** that increases damage it takes. These are typically keyed to **[[Rules/Step 2- Damage Type|damage types]]** such as slashing damage or fire damage.
