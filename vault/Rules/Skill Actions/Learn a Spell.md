# Learn a Spell (Trained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 238

If you’re a spellcaster, you can use the skill corresponding to your magical tradition to learn a new spell of that tradition. Table 4–3: Learning a Spell lists the Price of the materials needed to Learn a Spell of each level.

## Skill Uses

[[Rules/Skills/Arcana|[Arcana]]] Learn a Spell from the arcane tradition.

[[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|[Nature]]] Learn a Spell from the primal tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Occultism|[Occultism]]] Learn a Spell from the occult tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Religion|[Religion]]] Learn a Spell from the divine tradition.
