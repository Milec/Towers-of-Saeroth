# Identify Magic (Trained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 238

Using the skill related to the appropriate tradition, as explained in Magical Traditions and Skills on page 238, you can attempt to identify a magical item, location, or ongoing effect. In many cases, you can use a skill to attempt to Identify Magic of a tradition other than your own at a higher DC. The GM determines whether you can do this and what the DC is.

## Skill Uses

[[Rules/Skills/Arcana|[Arcana]]] Identify Magic , particularly arcane magic.

[[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|[Nature]]] Identify Magic , particularly primal magic.

[[Rules/Skills/Occultism|[Occultism]]] Identify Magic , particularly occult magic.

[[Rules/Skills/Religion|[Religion]]] Identify Magic , particularly divine magic.
