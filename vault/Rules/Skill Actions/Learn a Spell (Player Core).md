# Learn a Spell (Trained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 230

If you're a spellcaster, you can use the skill corresponding to your magical tradition to learn a new spell of that tradition. The Learning a Spell table lists the Price of the materials needed to Learn a Spell of each rank. Learning a Spell is most useful for classes that use a limited list of spells, like the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|bard]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-392)|witch]], or [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizard]], though other classes might use it to gain access to [[Traits/Rare|rare]] or [[Traits/Uncommon|uncommon]] spells.

## Skill Uses

[[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|[Arcana]]] Learn a Spell from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|[Nature]]] Learn a Spell from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|[Occultism]]] Learn a Spell from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|[Religion]]] Learn a Spell from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition.
