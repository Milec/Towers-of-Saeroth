# Tap Ley Line (Trained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 215

You can use your magical prowess to temporarily access the power of a [[Rules/Ley Lines|ley line]]. Tapping the Ley Line requires a skill check using the skill associated with its tradition ([[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] for [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] for [[Traits/Primal|primal]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] for [[Traits/Occult|occult]], and [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] for [[Traits/Divine|divine]]). In cases where a ley line has multiple associated traditions, you can use any of the appropriate skills to Tap the Ley Line. The benefits of a ley line are always [[Traits/Magical|magical]] in nature and can be [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracted]] in a similar way to a magic item.
