# Recall Knowledge (Untrained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 231

To remember useful information on a topic, you can attempt to Recall Knowledge. This action is one you're likely to use frequently. Learning more about the world and people around you is one of the best ways to inform your decisions, and Recalling Knowledge can help you figure out how to best fight monsters and figure out puzzles and social challenges.

 You might know basic information about something without needing to attempt a check, but Recall Knowledge requires you to stop and think for a moment so you can recollect more specific facts and apply them. You might even need to spend time investigating first. For instance, to use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] to learn the cause of death, you might need to conduct a forensic examination before attempting to Recall Knowledge.

## Skill Uses

[[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|[Arcana]]] Recall Knowledge about arcane theories; magic traditions; creatures of arcane significance (like [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], and [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elementals]]); and the [[Setting/Planes/Astral Plane]], Elemental Planes, and [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]].

[[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|[Crafting]]] Recall Knowledge about alchemical reactions, the value of items, engineering, unusual materials, and alchemical or mechanical creatures. The GM determines which creatures this applies to, but it usually includes [[Traits/Construct|constructs]].

[[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|[Lore]]] Recall Knowledge about the subject of the Lore skill's subcategory.

[[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|[Medicine]]] Recall Knowledge about diseases, injuries, poisons, and other ailments. You can use this to perform forensic examinations if you spend 10 minutes (or more, as determined by the GM) checking for evidence such as wound patterns. This is most useful when determining how a body was injured or killed.

[[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|[Nature]]] Recall Knowledge about fauna, flora, geography, weather, the environment, creatures of natural origin (like [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], [[Traits/Fey|fey]], and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]]), the [[Setting/Planes/First World]], and the Elemental Planes.

[[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|[Occultism]]] Recall Knowledge about ancient mysteries; obscure philosophies; creatures of occult significance (like [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberrations]], [[Traits/Spirit|spirits]], and [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|oozes]]); and esoteric planes like the [[Setting/Planes/Astral Plane]], [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge]], the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]], [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]], and [[Setting/Planes/The Void|the Void]].

[[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|[Religion]]] Recall Knowledge about divine agents, the finer points of theology, obscure myths regarding a faith, and creatures of religious significance (like [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestials]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], and [[Traits/Undead|undead]]), [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge]], [[Setting/Planes/The Void|the Void]], and the Outer Sphere.

[[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|[Society]]] Recall Knowledge about local history, important personalities, legal institutions, societal structure, and humanoid cultures. The GM might allow Society to apply to other creatures that are major elements of society in your region, such as the draconic nobility in a kingdom of humans ruled by dragons.
