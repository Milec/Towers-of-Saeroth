# Identify Magic (Trained)  *General Skill Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 230

Using the skill related to the appropriate tradition, as explained in the Magical Traditions and Skills sidebar, you can attempt to identify a magical item, location, or ongoing effect. In many cases, you can use a skill to attempt to Identify Magic of a tradition other than your own at a higher DC. The GM determines whether you can do this and what the DC is.

## Skill Uses

[[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|[Arcana]]] Identify Magic , particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|[Nature]]] Identify Magic , particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|[Occultism]]] Identify Magic , particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] tradition.

[[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|[Religion]]] Identify Magic , particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition.
