# Feats  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 441

You can spend a week of downtime retraining to swap out one of your feats. Remove the old feat and replace it with another of the same type. For example, you could swap a [[Traits/Skill (Player Core)|skill]] feat for another skill feat, but not for a [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|wizard]] feat.
