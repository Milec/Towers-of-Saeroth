# Abjuration  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 297

[[Traits/Abjuration|Abjurations]] protect and ward. They create barriers that keep out attacks, effects, or even certain types of creatures. They also create effects that harm trespassers or banish interlopers.
