# Ranged Attack Challenges  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 36

The [[Rules/Vehicles in Combat (GM Core)|Vehicles in Combat]] rules note that attacks made while on a vehicle that has moved within the last round take a –2 penalty, or a –4 penalty if the vehicle is [[Rules/Uncontrolled Vehicles (GM Core)|uncontrolled]] or any action in the last round had the [[Traits/Reckless (GM Core)|reckless]] trait.

That section also describes the rules on cover. In sailing combat, creatures on a ship’s top deck don’t get [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] against attacks from the other ship’s top deck, though they might be able to find cover behind masts or cargo on the deck. Creatures using hatches on the decks below typically have standard cover and can [[Actions/Take Cover (Player Core)|Take Cover]] as normal.
