# Transmutation  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 298

[[Traits/Transmutation]] spells make alterations to or transform the physical form of a creature or object. The [[Traits/Morph|morph]] and [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] traits appear primarily in transmutation spells.
