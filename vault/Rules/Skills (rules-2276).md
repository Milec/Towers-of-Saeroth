# Skills  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 399

Skill checks are required for all sorts of other tasks related to adventuring and life in general. Most of their rules are in [[Rules/Chapter 4- Skills (rules-2134)|Chapter 4]]. You'll find the rules for **calculating skill modifiers** [[Rules/Skill Checks and DCs|here]]. Many **[[Rules/Exploration Activities (Player Core)|exploration activities]]**, such as [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]] and [[Actions/Investigate (Player Core)|Investigate]], also use skill checks.
