# The Tattoo Trait  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 164

A magical tattoo has the [[Traits/Tattoo|tattoo]] trait. It's permanently a part of the subject's body, and reduces the number of items that creature can invest per day by 1. Each tattoo has the [[Traits/Invested|invested]] trait to indicate this limitation—a magical tattoo is like an invested item that the tattooed creature has no choice but to invest. If the tattoo loses its magic or is destroyed, it no longer reduces your investiture.

 Just like a physical magic item, a magical tattoo can be [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracted]] by spells like [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] or [[Spells/Disjunction|_disjunction_]]. If destroyed, the tattoo fades from the skin.

 If a creature gets a new magical tattoo when their limit on invested items has already been reduced to zero, the new tattoo's magic fails to take hold, and it becomes a non-magical tattoo instead. However, a tattooist can alter an existing tattoo when they Craft a tattoo, modifying the old one into a different magical tattoo and removing the old effect. Magical tattoos can usually be [[Rules/Craft Requirements|upgraded into their greater versions]] by having a tattooist add to or modify the existing tattoo.
