# Outer Sphere  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 419

The scope of reality within the Outer Sphere is difficult to grasp. Here the gods dwell and the souls of the dead gather to be judged by the goddess of the dead, [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]], atop the spire in the realm that is her [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]]. Anything is possible in the Outer Sphere, and its realms are as much manifestations of philosophies and belief as anything else. From the towering mountain of [[Setting/Planes/Heaven]] to the endless gulfs of the demon-held [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]], be it the agonizing pit of [[Setting/Planes/Hell]] or the boundless wonders of [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]], all of reality is represented within the Outer Sphere. Over time this reality erodes away and is recycled back into the raw material of life within the [[Setting/Planes/Positive Energy Plane]].
