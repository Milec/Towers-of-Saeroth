# Companion Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 604

You might want to acquire items that benefit an animal or beast that assists you. These items have the [[Traits/Companion|companion]] trait, meaning they function only for animal companions, familiars, and similar creatures. Normally these are the only items a companion can use. Other items can qualify, at the GM's discretion, but a companion can never Activate an Item.
