# Basic Magic Armor  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 556

The most common special armors are suits of armor with some combination of [[Equipment/Armor Potency|_armor potency_]] and [[Equipment/Resilient|_resilient_]] runes. The [[Equipment/Magic Armor|_magic armor_]] stat block provides a quick reference for these types of armor.
