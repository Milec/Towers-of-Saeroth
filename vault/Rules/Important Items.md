# Important Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 223

Some magical items in the game are an important part of the game's progression, so ensure your players are getting enough of the following items.  

- **Fundamental runes.** Potency runes for [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|weapons]] and [[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|armor]], [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] runes for armor, [[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|reinforcing]] runes for shields, and [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] runes for weapons are all important to ensure characters have the attacks, damage, and defenses suited to their level. Read more about these runes [[Rules/Fundamental Runes (GM Core)|here]].
- **Skill items.** Players seeking true mastery of a skill will be happy to get items that give bonuses to that skill. These are mostly in the categories of [[Rules/Held Items (rules-3210)|held items]] and [[Rules/Worn Items (rules-3226)|worn items]].
- **Spellcaster items**. A staff is as important to a spellcaster as a magic sword is to a fighter. Learn more about staves [[Rules/Staves (rules-3211)|here]]. [[Rules/Scrolls (rules-3197)|Scrolls]] and [[Rules/Wands (rules-3218)|wands]] provide extra spells in a more limited capacity.
- **Weapon alternatives.** Characters using unarmed attacks need [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|handwraps of mighty blows]] or similar items instead of weapon runes.
- **Healing potions.** [[Rules/Potions (rules-3196)|Keep them alive!]]
