# Crafting Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 223

In addition to anything special noted in the Craft Requirements entry, every item has default requirements. These are as follows, including anything listed in the [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] activity.  

- The **crafter's level** must be equal to or higher than the item's.
- If the item is **9th level or higher**, the crafter must be a master in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], and if it's **16th or higher**, the crafter must be legendary.
- The crafter must have the **[[Rules/Formulas (Player Core)|formula]]** for uncommon or rarer items.
- The crafter must have an appropriate **set of tools** and, in many cases, a workshop.
- The crafter must supply **raw materials worth at least half of the item's Price**.
- For an **[[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical item]]**, the crafter must have the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting (Player Core)|Alchemical Crafting]] feat.
- For a **[[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magic item]]**, the crafter must have the [[Feats/Magical Crafting (Player Core)|Magical Crafting]] feat.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Item Rarity
>
>
>
> Like many other aspects of the rules, items have rarities. Player characters might find [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]] magic items for sale, but only infrequently and often by private sellers or in clandestine markets. Their formulas are often guarded and not readily available. Unless the GM decides otherwise, a character cannot purchase [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|rare]] items, and their formulas are lost to time.
>
> Rarities for uncommon and rare treasures are indicated by the first trait in the stat block (or by the items' level if only some types of the item have a different rarity). [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]] items are indicated in the same way, though there are none in this book.
