# Other Encounter Abilties  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 216

An NPC with a pet or familiar might also have one of the following abilities, no matter the shape and form of their companion. If an ability mentions a familiar, that NPC must have a familiar and not a pet.

 **Go Fetch** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Requirements** The NPC has a free hand; **Effect** The NPC commands their companion to retrieve an item of 1 Bulk or less that is unattended and within 20 feet. The companion places the item in the NPC's free hand.

 **Spell Courier** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) If the next action the NPC uses is to Cast a Spell with a range of touch, they can command their familiar to deliver that spell to a target within 15 feet. This effectively increases the range of the spell.
