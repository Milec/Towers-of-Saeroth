# Step 4: If Damage Remains, Reduce the Target's Hit Points  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 453

After applying the target’s immunities, resistances, and weaknesses to the damage, whatever damage is left reduces the target’s Hit Points on a 1-to-1 basis. More information about Hit Points can be found in the [[Rules/Hit Points, Healing, and Dying]] section.
