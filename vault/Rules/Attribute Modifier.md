# Attribute Modifier  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 400

Nearly all checks allow you to add an **attribute modifier** to the roll. Attribute modifiers represent your raw capability and are described [[Rules/Character Creation (Player Core)|here]]. Exactly which attribute modifier you use is determined by what you're trying to accomplish. Usually, a sword swing applies your Strength modifier, whereas remembering the name of the earl's cousin uses your Intelligence modifier.
