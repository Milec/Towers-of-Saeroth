# Social Advancement  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 228

You can follow the same process to increase an NPC's [[Rules/Non-Combat Level (rules-2920)|non-combat level]] to improve their social or skill-based abilities. Each time you advance such an NPC, decide whether the story better suits increasing their combat level, non-combat level, or both at the same time.
