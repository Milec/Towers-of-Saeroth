# Exploration Activities  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 438

While you're traveling and exploring, tell the GM what you'd generally like to do along the way. If you do nothing more than make steady progress toward your goal, you move at the full travel speeds given in the table.

When you want to do something other than simply travel, you describe what you are attempting to do. It isn't necessary to go into extreme detail, such as “Using my dagger, I nudge the door so I can check for devious traps.” Instead, “I'm searching the area for hazards” is sufficient. The GM finds the best exploration activity to match your description and describes the effects of that activity. Some exploration activities limit how fast you can travel and be effective.

These are most common exploration activities.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Skill Exploration Activities
>
>
>
> [[Rules/Chapter 4- Skills (rules-2134)|Chapter 4: Skills]] includes additional exploration activities.
>
> **[[Actions/Borrow an Arcane Spell (Player Core)|Borrow an Arcane Spell]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] to prepare a spell from someone else's spellbook.  
> **[[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to threaten a creature so it does what you want.  
> **[[Actions/Cover Tracks (Player Core)|Cover Tracks]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] to obscure your passing.  
> **[[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]]:** You use a suitable skill to understand archaic, esoteric, or obscure texts.  
> **[[Actions/Gather Information (Player Core)|Gather Information]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to canvass the area to learn about a specific individual or topic.  
> **[[Actions/Identify Alchemy (Player Core)|Identify Alchemy]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Craft]] and [[Equipment/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist's tools]] to identify an [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]] item.  
> **[[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]]:** Using a variety of skills, you can learn about a magic item, location, or ongoing effect.  
> **[[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and usually a [[Equipment/Disguise Kit (Player Core)|disguise kit]] to create a disguise.  
> **[[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]]:** You use the skill corresponding to the spell's tradition to gain access to a new spell.  
> **[[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]]:** You use Diplomacy to make a good impression on someone (page 239).  
> **[[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]]:** With a [[Equipment/Repair Toolkit|repair kit]] and the Crafting skill, you fix a damaged item.  
> **[[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]]:** You use Survival to get a sense of where you are or determine cardinal directions.  
> **[[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]]:** Using [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], you squeeze though very tight spaces.  
> **[[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]]:** You use Survival to follow tracks.  
> **[[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]]:** You use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] to treat a living creature's wounds.
