# Snares  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 247

[[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|Snares]] are small, simple traps to damage or hamper your foes that you can create and deploy quickly enough to utilize on a battlefield. Snares are nonmagical consumable items. The full rules for creating and using snares are located [[Rules/Snares|here]].
