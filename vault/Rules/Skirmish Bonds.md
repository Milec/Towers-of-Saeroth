# Skirmish Bonds  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 161

If you bond more deeply with a troop, you can choose one special action or ongoing benefit. This comes from training with your troop during a day of downtime. You can change your selection with another day of downtime, but you can't have more than one special bond for your troop at a time. Skirmish bonds are between one leader and one troop. A leader can't normally transfer these tactics or bonds to a different troop. As noted above, if a leader has formed a skirmish bond with a troop once, that bond takes effect if they lead the troop again—they don't need to spend another day of downtime.

**Offensive Abilities:** Many skirmish bonds refer to your troop's “offensive abilities.” This term refers to the activities troops have that represent the individual troops attacking together, such as the [[Bestiary/Goblin Rabble|goblin rabble's]] Dogpile ability. Most of them thematically correspond to melee attacks and take a variable number of actions to use, dealing more damage if the troop has more actions to spend on them.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Creating New Bonds
>
>
>
> Since the skirmish bonds can't cover every class and ability combination in the game, you might want to create your own skirmish bonds sometimes. For example, if you have a fire [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Rage of Elements)|kineticist]] in your game, you might let them combine fire impulses with troop abilities that use the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait, following the structure of the [Coordinated Casting](https://2e.aonprd.com/SkirmishBonds.aspx?ID=3) skirmish bond.
