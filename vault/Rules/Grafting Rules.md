# Grafting Rules  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

Grafts are living tissues that are implanted into a host, where they integrate with the hosts’ native biology. To implant a graft, you must be an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] and have the appropriate formula. Graft formulas can be purchased as outlined in the [[Rules/Formulas (Player Core)|formulas rules]].

You can take the [[Feats/Graft Technician]] feat to implant grafts.
