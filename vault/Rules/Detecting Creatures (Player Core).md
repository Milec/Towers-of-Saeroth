# Detecting Creatures  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 434

Three conditions measure the degree to which you can sense a creature: [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]. However, the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] conditions can partially mask a creature, and the [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] condition indicates you have no idea a creature is around.

With the exception of invisible, these conditions are relative to the viewer—it's possible for a creature to be observed to you but hidden from your ally. Most of these rules apply to objects as well as creatures.

Typically, the GM tracks how well creatures detect each other, since neither party has perfect information. For example, you might think a creature is in the last place you sensed it, but it was able to [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] away. Or you might think a creature can't see you in the dark, but it has [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]].

You can attempt to avoid detection by using the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] skill to [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoid Notice]], [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]], or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], or by using [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]].
