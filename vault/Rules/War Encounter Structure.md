# War Encounter Structure  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 578

A war encounter takes place over the course of several rounds, with each round representing an hour of battle. The battle continues until all armies on one side are [[Rules/Conditions/Defeated|defeated]] (reduced to 0 HP) or [[Rules/Conditions/Routed|routed]].
