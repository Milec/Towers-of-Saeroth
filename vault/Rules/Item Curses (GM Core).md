# Item Curses  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 307

Item curses alter their base item, much like a [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property rune]]. A curse can be applied to the specific types of magic items listed in its Usage entry. Curses typically can’t be removed or transferred from the item, though at your discretion, either might be possible after the curse is broken. If the PCs manage to break the curse, the newly uncursed item could be quite valuable.
