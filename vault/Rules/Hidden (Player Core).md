# Hidden  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 434

A creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] is only barely perceptible. You know what space it occupies, but little else. Perhaps the creature just moved behind [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] and successfully used the [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] action. Your target might be behind a waterfall, where you can see some movement but can't determine an exact location. Maybe you've been [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] or the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], but you used the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] basic action to determine its general location based on hearing alone. Regardless of the specifics, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to a hidden creature.

When targeting a hidden creature, before you roll to determine your effect, you must attempt a DC 11 flat check. If you fail, you don't affect the creature, though the actions you used are still expended—as well as any spell slots, costs, and other resources. You remain off-guard to the creature, whether you successfully target it or not.
