# Perception  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 11

Perception measures your character's ability to notice hidden objects or unusual situations, and it usually determines how quickly the character springs into action in combat. It is described in full in [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Chapter 8]].
