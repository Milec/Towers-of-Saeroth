# Critical Hits  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 278

When you make an attack and succeed with a natural 20 (the number on the die is 20), or if the result of your attack exceeds the target's AC by 10, you achieve a critical success (also known as a critical hit).

 If you critically succeed at a Strike, your attack deals [[Rules/Doubling and Halving Damage|double damage]]. Other attacks, such as spell attack rolls and some uses of the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, describe the specific effects that occur when their outcomes are critical successes.
