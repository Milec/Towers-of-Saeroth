# Occult Magic Terrain  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 220

In areas of occult magic, emotions run hotter, passions flare, and a mysterious intent permeates. Any creature casting an occult spell gains a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves, [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks, [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks, and [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks until the end of its next turn.
