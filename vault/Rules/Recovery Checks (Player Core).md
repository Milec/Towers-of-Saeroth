# Recovery Checks  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 411

While you're [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]], attempt a recovery check at the start of each of your turns. This is a [[Rules/Checks (rules-2278)|flat check]] with a DC equal to 10 + your current dying value to see if you get better or worse.

**Critical Success** Your dying value is reduced by 2.  
**Success** Your dying value is reduced by 1.  
**Failure** Your dying value increases by 1.  
**Critical Failure** Your dying value increases by 2.
