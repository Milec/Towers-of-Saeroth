# Proficiencies  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 58

Your eidolon's level is equal to yours. It begins with expert proficiency in Fortitude and Will saves and trained proficiency in Reflex saves and Perception. In addition, it is trained in [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks and unarmored defense. It shares your skill proficiencies. Certain class features increase your eidolon's proficiencies.
