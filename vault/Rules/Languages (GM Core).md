# Languages  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 116

Think about what languages the creature would need to communicate with other creatures in its home. For instance, many intelligent [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] speak [[Setting/Languages/Necril (Player Core)|Necril]], and many creatures from [[Rules/The Darklands|the Darklands]] speak [[Setting/Languages/Sakvroth]]. If you want your creature to be able to speak to the PCs, be sure it has [[Setting/Languages/Common (Player Core)|Common]]; for a creature with no reason to speak the common tongue of your setting (such as most extraplanar creatures in a typical campaign), be sure it doesn’t. Some creatures can understand language but can’t vocalize; in this case, you can state that they can’t speak any language. For creatures that need to be able to infiltrate and communicate wherever they go, you might give them [[Spells/Truespeech|_truespeech_]] or a similar ability as a constant innate spell.
