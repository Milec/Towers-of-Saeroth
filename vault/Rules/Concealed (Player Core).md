# Concealed  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 434

A [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creature is in mist, within [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]], or amid something else that obscures sight but isn’t a physical barrier. When you target a creature that’s concealed from you, you must attempt a DC 5 flat check before you roll to determine your effect. If you fail, you don’t affect the target. The concealed condition doesn’t change which of the main categories of detection apply. A creature in a light fog bank is still observed even though it’s concealed.
