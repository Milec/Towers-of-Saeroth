# Troops in Skirmishes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 160

Skirmish encounters rely heavily on the special creature type called troops. You can find troop stat blocks [[Traits/Troop (NPC Core)|here.]] In skirmish encounters, a troop can be routed, making it flee from the battlefield. This applies to any troop in the encounter, even if it doesn’t have a leader. This section also includes rules for a troop that has a leader but loses them during the battle.
