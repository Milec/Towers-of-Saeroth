# Elemental Spell List  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 203

The [[Archetypes/Elementalist|elementalist]] archetype uses this elemental spell list. It includes spells from all hardcover rulebooks up to this release with a superscript indicating the book.

You can discuss with the GM any spells from other sources you want to add to your list. As a general rule, spells with the [[Magic/Domains/Air|air]], [[Magic/Domains/Earth|earth]], [[Magic/Domains/Fire|fire]], or [[Magic/Domains/Water|water]] trait belong on the list, including spells that add one of those traits depending on how they're cast, such as [[Spells/Elemental Zone|_elemental zone_]]. Spells that are general and appear on every tradition's spell list also make good candidates.
