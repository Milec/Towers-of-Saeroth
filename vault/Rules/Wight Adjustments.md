# Wight Adjustments  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 73

All [[Bestiary/Families/Wight|wights]] can drain life through their unarmed attacks, but some can draw life force through weapons as well.

- Add the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and [[Traits/Wight|wight]] traits.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Necril]] language.
- Add the following immunities: [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
- Add drain life to any number of the creature's Strikes and reduce the damage of each of those Strikes by half the creature's level.
- Add the following abilities. **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision]]** **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing]]** **Drain Life** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]); When the creature damages a living creature with this Strike, it gains temporary HP equal to its level, and the target must succeed at a Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. This save uses the [[Rules/Spell DC and Spell Attack Roll|moderate DC for the wight creature's level]].
