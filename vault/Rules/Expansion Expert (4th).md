# Expansion Expert (4th)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 513

Your kingdom is better at expanding its territory. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks made to [[Actions/Claim Hex]] and can attempt to Claim Hex up to twice during a Kingdom turn.

 At 9th level, you can attempt to Claim Hex up to three times during a Kingdom turn.
