# Step 3: Apply Kingdom XP  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 539

The GM now awards any kingdom XP earned during that turn. If the kingdom experienced a random event, it receives 30 XP. The first Kingdom turn that your kingdom spent 100 RP, gain 80 kingdom XP as a [[Rules/Milestone XP Awards|milestone award]].

 In addition, any RP that remains unspent is now converted to kingdom XP on a 1 to 1 ration, to a maximum of 120 XP per Kingdom turn.
