# Critical Specialization Effects  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 283

Certain feats, class features, weapon runes, and other effects can grant you additional benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success. This is called a critical specialization effect. The exact effect depends on which weapon group your weapon belongs to, as listed [here](WeaponGroups.aspx). You can always decide not to add the critical specialization effect of your weapon.
