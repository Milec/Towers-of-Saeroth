# Trait  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 13

A trait is a keyword that conveys additional information about a rules element, such as a school of magic or rarity. Often, a trait indicates how other rules interact with an ability, creature, item, or another rules element that has that trait. All the traits used in this book are listed [here](Traits.aspx).
