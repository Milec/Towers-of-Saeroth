# Fly Speed  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 420

As long as you have a fly Speed, you can use the [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] and [[Actions/Arrest a Fall (Player Core)|Arrest a Fall]] actions. You can also attempt to [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]] if you're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] skill.

Wind conditions can affect how you use the Fly action. In general, moving against the wind uses the same rules as moving through [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] (or greater difficult terrain, if you're also flying upward), and moving with the wind allows you to move 10 feet for every 5 feet of movement you spend (not cumulative with moving straight downward). For more information on spending movement, see [[Rules/Tactical Movement]].

Upward and downward movement are both relative to the gravity in your area; if you're in zero gravity, moving up or down is no different from moving horizontally.
