# Maximum Duration  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 430

If an affliction lasts only a limited amount of time, it lists a maximum duration. Once this duration passes, the affliction ends. Otherwise, it lasts until you succeed at enough saves to recover, as described in [[Rules/Stages (Player Core)|Stages]] below.
