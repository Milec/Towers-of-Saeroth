# Into the Court  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -190- The Choosing|Pathfinder #190: The Choosing]] pg. 69

The easiest way to enter the Harrow Court is to use the [[Actions/Enter the Harrow Court|Enter Harrow Court]] activity—indeed, this is the only way to enter the demiplane the first time, for the PCs' arrival in the Harrow Court actually finalizes the creation of the demiplane. The act of investing a card from the _Deck of Destiny_ and then bringing that card into the Harrow Court automatically activates that card's incarnation effect on the demiplane as well (see Epitomes). After this initial investiture, that incarnation effect persists even if the card doesn't remain invested over the course of the adventure.

 Before one can use the Enter the Harrow Court activity, six cards —one from each suit—must be brought together. At this point, only the PCs can use a card from the _Deck of Destiny_ to Enter the Harrow Court, as the demiplane's formation is inexorably linked to their souls and fates.
