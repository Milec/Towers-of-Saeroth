# Shipboard Weapons  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 35

Though the focus of boarding combat should be on tactical combat, siege weapons aboard the ships might still come into play. If weapons like [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Ballista (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|ballistae]] are mounted on the top deck, the crew can use them using the standard [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|siege weapon rules]]. These weapons can also be disabled as described [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|here]].
