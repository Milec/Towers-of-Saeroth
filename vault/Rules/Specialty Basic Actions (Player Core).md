# Specialty Basic Actions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 418

These actions are useful under specific circumstances. The [[Actions/Arrest a Fall (Player Core)|Arrest a Fall]], [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], and [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] actions require you to have a special [[Rules/Movement Types (Player Core)|movement type]]. The climb and swim Speeds use the corresponding actions from the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill.
