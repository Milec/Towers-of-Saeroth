# Tetanus  *Disease 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 88

An infection introduced through open wounds, tetanus can produce stiffness, muscle spasms strong enough to break bones, and ultimately death.

**Saving Throw** DC 14 Fortitude

**Onset**
10 days

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 week)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t speak (1 day)

**Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] with spasms (1 day)

**Stage 4** death
