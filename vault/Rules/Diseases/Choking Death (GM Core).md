# Choking Death  *Disease 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 88

This disease is capable of wiping out nations or even entire continents. A few pockets of the disease still remain in Iobaria, keeping that region’s population sparse.

**Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 day

**Stage 1** hoarse voice but no other symptoms (1 day)

**Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 3** drained 2 and can’t speak (1 day)

**Stage 4** death
