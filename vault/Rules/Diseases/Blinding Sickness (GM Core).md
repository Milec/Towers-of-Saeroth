# Blinding Sickness  *Disease 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 89

Endemic to jungles of the Mwangi Expanse, blinding sickness is transmitted by dirty water or the bites of certain creatures.

**Saving Throw** DC 23 Fortitude

**Stage 1** carrier with no effects (1 day)

**Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 3** enfeebled 2 (1 day)

**Stage 4** enfeebled 2 and permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] (1 day)

**Stage 5** enfeebled 4 (1 day)

**Stage 6** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] (1 day)

**Stage 7** death
