# Ayrzul's Blight  *Disease 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 33

Radioactive elemental energy courses through sap and blood, petrifies bark and bone, withers flesh, and mutates life. Creatures with the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] trait treat critical successes against the disease as successes. The [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition caused by Ayrzul's Blight can't be healed until the disease is removed. Ayrzul's Blight presents with obvious but varying symptoms, from coughing up black dust to suddenly becoming covered in itchy patches of flaky stone.

**Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude

**Stage 1** drained 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** drained 2 (1 day)

**Stage 3** drained 3 (1 day)

**Stage 4** drained 4 (1 day)

**Stage 5** mutation (on a failure) or death (on a critical failure)
