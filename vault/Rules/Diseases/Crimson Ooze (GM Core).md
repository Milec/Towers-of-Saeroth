# Crimson Ooze  *Disease 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent (GM Core)|Virulent]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 89

This devastating fungus infects your hand and can be cured by amputating the limb before you reach stage 4.

**Saving Throw** DC 34 Fortitude

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2, and using the infected hand deals 3d6 persistent bleed damage (1 day)

**Stage 3** clumsy 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2, and the infected hand is unusable (1 day)

**Stage 4** clumsy 3, stupefied 3, and infected hand is permanently unusable (1 day)

**Stage 5** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], and damage does not end the confused condition (1 day)

**Stage 6** death
