# Blightburn Sickness  *Disease 15*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -155- Lord of the Black Sands|Pathfinder #155: Lord of the Black Sands]] pg. 73

The ceiling of the vault of the Black Desert is studded with deadly crystals that glimmer like stars above the dark sands. These crystals are radioactive and flood the vault with lethal energy. Creatures other than those native to the vault or who have survived there for a long time develop a disease called blightburn sickness.

Creatures native to the Black Desert are immune, as are creatures who are affected by blightburn sickness but recover from it. The target can't recover from the disease's [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition except by magic.

 The surest way to avoid contracting blightburn sickness is by wearing a [[Equipment/Blightburn Ward|_blightburn ward_]], a piece of protective jewelry often found among [[Bestiary/Families/Urdefhan|urdefhans']] gear or for sale in Shraen.

**Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude

**Onset**
1d4 days

**Stage 1** drained 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** drained 1 and sickened 1 (1 day)

**Stage 3** drained 2 and sickened 2 (1 week)

**Stage 4** drained 3 and sickened 3 (1 month)

**Stage 5** increase drained condition by 1 (1 year)
