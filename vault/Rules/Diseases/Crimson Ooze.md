# Crimson Ooze  *Disease 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 119

This devastating fungus infects your hand and can be cured by amputating the limb before you reach stage 4.

**Saving Throw** DC 34 Fortitude

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] (1 day)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2, and using the infected hand deals 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] (1 day)

**Stage 3** clumsy 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]], and the infected hand is unusable (1 day)

**Stage 4** clumsy 3, stupefied 3, and infected hand is permanently unusable (1 day)

**Stage 5** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], and damage does not end the confused condition (1 day)

**Stage 6** death
