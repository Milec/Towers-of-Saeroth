# Bonechill  *Disease 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 88

If you are [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] and exposed to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent]] cold, you might contract bonechill.

**Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 day

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t heal cold damage until this disease is cured (1 day)

**Stage 3** clumsy 3 and all cold [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|temperature]] effects are one step more severe for the victim (1 day)

**Stage 4** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] and all cold temperature effects are one step more severe for the victim (1 day)
