# Blightburn Sickness  *Disease 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 45

_Nethys Note: No traits were printed with this version of the disease, so the traits are assumed to be the same as the legacy entry._

Caused by exposure to blightburn crystal, blightburn sickness burns and dissolves the afflicted creature from within. The target can't recover from the disease's drained or sickened condition except by magic. It's typically a 15th-level affliction (DC 32 Fortitude), but exposure to other forms of blightburn can change it.

**Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude

**Onset**
1d4 days

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 day)

**Stage 2** drained 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (1 day)

**Stage 3** drained 2 and sickened 2 (1 week)

**Stage 4** drained 3 and sickened 3 (1 month)

**Stage 5** increase drained condition by 1 (1 year)
