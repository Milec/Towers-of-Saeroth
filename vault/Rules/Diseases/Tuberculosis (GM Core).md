# Tuberculosis  *Disease 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 88

An extended respiratory disease, tuberculosis can pose particular challenges to spellcasters and some performers due to the intense coughing fits it produces.

**Saving Throw** DC 15 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 week

**Stage 1** carrier with no effects (1 week)

**Stage 2** coughing requires you to succeed at a DC 5 flat check to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait or [[Actions/Activate an Item]] that has the concentrate trait (1 week)

**Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], can’t recover from the fatigued condition, and coughing requires a successful DC 15 flat check to Cast a Spell that has the concentrate trait or Activate an Item that has the concentrate trait (1 week)

**Stage 4** [[Rules/Unconscious|unconscious]] (1 week)

**Stage 5** death
