# Scarlet Fever  *Disease 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 118

The relatively simple sore throat caused by this disease leads many victims to initially dismiss it as a mild illness, but scarlet fever can be deadly if left untreated. You can’t reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition while affected with scarlet fever.

**Saving Throw** DC 13 Fortitude

**Onset**
2 days

**Stage 1** sickened 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** sickened 2 (1 day)

**Stage 3** sickened 3 and can’t speak (1 day)

**Stage 4** death
