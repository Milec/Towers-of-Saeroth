# Bubonic Plague  *Disease 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 88

This widespread illness can sweep through entire communities, leaving few unaffected. The first indication of the disease is a telltale swelling of glands. In some cases, the disease can move into your lungs (pneumonic plague) or blood (septicemic plague), which is even more fatal. If you have bubonic plague, you can’t remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition while affected.

**Saving Throw** DC 17 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 day

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] (1 day)

**Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 and fatigued (1 day)

**Stage 3** enfeebled 3, fatigued, and take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] every 1d20 minutes (1 day)
