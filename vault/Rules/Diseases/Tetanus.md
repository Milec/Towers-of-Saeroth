# Tetanus  *Disease 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 118

An infection introduced through open wounds, tetanus can produce stiffness, muscle spasms strong enough to break bones, and ultimately death.

**Saving Throw** DC 14 Fortitude

**Onset**
10 days

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] (1 week)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t speak (1 day)

**Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] with spasms (1 day)

**Stage 4** death
