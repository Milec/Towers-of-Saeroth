# Velstrac Tetanus  *Disease 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 12

[[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|Velstracs]] sometimes intentionally infect their chains with dangerous diseases, such as this aggressive form of tetanus. Tetanus can produce stiffness, muscle spasms strong enough to break bones, and ultimately death.

**Saving Throw** DC 21 Fortitude

**Onset**
1d4 days

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 week)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t speak (1 day)

**Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] (1 day)

**Stage 4** death
