# Fungal Rot  *Disease 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 18

This fast-acting disease causes tiny spores to take root under the skin and blossom in the lungs or in the bloodstream. You become easily winded and incapable of moving quickly. You can’t reduce your sickened condition while afflicted with fungal rot.

**Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 hour

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and sickened 2

**Stage 3** slowed 2 and sickened 3 (1 day)

**Stage 4** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] (1 day)

**Stage 5** death
