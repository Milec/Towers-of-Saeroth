# Blueblisters  *Disease 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent]], [[Traits/Virulent]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 44

This flesh-eating disease causes prominent and painful blue blisters on the skin and gums. You can’t reduce your sickened condition while afflicted with blueblisters.

**Saving Throw** DC 17 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 day

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 2** sickened 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day)

**Stage 3** sickened 2 and drained 1 (1 week)

**Stage 4** sickened 2 and drained 2 (1 week)
