# Bonechill  *Disease 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 118

If you are wounded and exposed to persistent cold, you might contract bonechill.

**Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude

**Onset**
1 day

**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] (1 day)

**Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t heal cold damage until this disease is cured (1 day)

**Stage 3** clumsy 3 and all [[Rules/Temperature|cold temperature effects]] are one step more severe for the victim (1 day)

**Stage 4** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] and all cold temperature effects are one step more severe for the victim (1 day)
