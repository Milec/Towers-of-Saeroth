# Talismans  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 263

An item with the [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|talisman]] trait is a magical charm, gem, stone, or other small object affixed to armor, a shield, or a weapon (called the affixed item). Each talisman holds a sliver of combat knowledge or magical energy that can be unleashed for a momentary boost of power or enhanced ability. Many talismans can be activated as a free action when you use a particular action or activity. A talisman is a [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]] item and has the talisman trait.

You must be wielding or wearing an item to activate a talisman attached to it. Once activated, a talisman burns out permanently, usually crumbling into a fine dust.
