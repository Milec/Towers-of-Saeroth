# Unseen Animal Companion  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 65

**Rarity**: [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]  
Cryptid animal companions look a touch peculiar, often with uncanny features or ones that seem to come from a different animal. If you take a feat that would normally allow you to choose to have your companion become a savage or nimble animal companion, you can choose to make it an [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Wildfire Scorcher|unseen animal companion instead]].
