# Item Crafting  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 441

You can spend downtime to use the [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] skill activity, making new items out of raw materials. Crafting can be an excellent way to refine the gear the party uses, including upgrading items and preparing ones you’re likely to need on an upcoming quest.
