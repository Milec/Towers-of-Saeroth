# Arcana  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 241

Arcana measures how much you know about arcane magic and creatures. Even if you’re untrained, you can Recall Knowledge.

## Arcana Untrained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Deception|Recall Knowledge]] about arcane theories; magic traditions; creatures of arcane significance (like dragons and beasts); and the Elemental, Astral, and Shadow Planes.

## Arcana Trained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Decipher Writing]] about arcane theory.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Identify Magic]], particularly arcane magic.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Learn a Spell]] from the arcane tradition.

- [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation|Tap Ley Line]] tapping an arcane ley line
