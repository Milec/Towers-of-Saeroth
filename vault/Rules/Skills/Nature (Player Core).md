# Nature  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 242

You know about the natural world, and you command and train animals and magical beasts.

## Nature Untrained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Folklore|Recall Knowledge]] about fauna, flora, geography, weather, the environment, creatures of natural origin (like [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], [[Traits/Fey|fey]], and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]]), the [[Setting/Planes/First World]], and the Elemental Planes.

## Nature Trained General Actions

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Magical Traditions and Skills (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] tradition.

- [[Rules/Skills/Exploration|Learn a Spell]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] tradition.
