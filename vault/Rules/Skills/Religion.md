# Religion  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 250

The secrets of deities, dogma, faith, and the realms of divine creatures both sublime and sinister are open to you. You also understand how magic works, though your training imparts a religious slant to that knowledge. Even if you’re untrained in Religion, you can use it to Recall Knowledge.

## Religion Untrained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Deception|Recall Knowledge]] about divine agents, the finer points of theology, obscure myths regarding a faith, and creatures of religious significance (like celestials, fiends, and undead), the Outer Sphere, and the Positive and Negative Energy Planes.

## Religion Trained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Decipher Writing]] of a religious nature, including allegories, homilies, and proverbs.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Identify Magic]], particularly divine magic.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Learn a Spell]] from the divine tradition.

- [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation|Tap Ley Line]] tapping a divine ley line
