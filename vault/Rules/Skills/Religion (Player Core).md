# Religion  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 244

The secrets of deities, dogma, faith, and the realms of divine creatures both sublime and sinister are open to you. You also understand how magic works, though your training imparts a religious slant to that knowledge.

## Religion Untrained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Folklore|Recall Knowledge]] about divine agents, the finer points of theology, obscure myths regarding a faith, and creatures of religious significance (like [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestials]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], and [[Traits/Undead|undead]]), [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge]], [[Setting/Planes/The Void|the Void]], and the Outer Sphere.

## Religion Trained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Boating|Decipher Writing]] of a religious nature, including allegories, homilies, and proverbs.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Magical Traditions and Skills (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition.

- [[Rules/Skills/Exploration|Learn a Spell]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition.
