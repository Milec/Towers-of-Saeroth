# Arcana  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 234

Arcana measures how much you know about arcane magic and creatures.

## Arcana Untrained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Folklore|Recall Knowledge]] about arcane theories; magic traditions; creatures of arcane significance (like [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], and [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elementals]]); and the [[Setting/Planes/Astral Plane]], Elemental Planes, and [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]].

## Arcana Trained General Actions

- [[Rules/Skills/Boating|Decipher Writing]] about arcane theory.

- [[Rules/Sidebars/Magical Traditions and Skills (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], particularly magic of the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition.

- [[Rules/Skills/Exploration|Learn a Spell]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition.
