# Intimidation  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 240

You bend others to your will using threats. Unlike [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], Intimidation is typically a blunt instrument with little room for nuance or care.
