# Acrobatics  *Skill*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 240

Acrobatics measures your ability to perform tasks requiring coordination and grace. When you use the [[Actions/Escape]] basic action, you can use your Acrobatics modifier instead of your unarmed attack modifier.
