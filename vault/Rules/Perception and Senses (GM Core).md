# Perception and Senses  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 304

An intelligent item that has any sense of the world around them has a [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] modifier. Intelligent items have only the senses listed within their entry, rather than the assumed assortment of senses that most creatures have. If an intelligent item notices something its partner doesn’t, it might be able to communicate with its partner and let them know.
