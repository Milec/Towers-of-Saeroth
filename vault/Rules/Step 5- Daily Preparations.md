# Step 5: Daily Preparations  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 109

This final step includes time spent to prepare for the adventuring day, as detailed under [[Rules/Daily Preparations]]. You can assume menial tasks like breaking down the campsite are included in this step without impacting how long it takes for the PCs to prepare for the day. Daily preparations take 30 minutes to complete. The zone's Encounter DC returns to its original DC. If the PCs wish to continue camping at this same location, they must use the [[Actions/Prepare Campsite]] activity again (though it takes only 1 hour to Prepare a Campsite that has been used before).
