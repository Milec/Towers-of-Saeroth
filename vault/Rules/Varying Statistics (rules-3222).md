# Varying Statistics  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 282

Each type of wand has a level and Price determined by the spell's rank. The wand's rarity matches the spell's rarity, and it has its spell's traits. A wand has the normal Hardness, BT, and HP of a thin item of its material (see [[Rules/Materials (GM Core)|Materials]]).

The traits for a wand vary based on the spell it contains. A wand always has the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] and [[Traits/Wand (Player Core)|wand]] traits, plus any traits that might be relevant to identifying the item, such as [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]. The GM determines the extra traits.
