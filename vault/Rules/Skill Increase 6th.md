# Skill Increase 6th  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 64

At 6th level in a branch, and every 4 levels thereafter, your studies lead you to deeper understanding of your branch's associated skills, causing you to gain an additional skill increase that you can use to raise one of the branch's associated skills, up to a maximum of expert.

 At 10th branch level or higher, you can use these skill increases to become a master in a skill in which you're already an expert. At 18th level, you can use this skill increase to become legendary in a skill in which you're already a master. For Uzunjati, who have [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] as an associated skill, you gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore]] feat when you choose to improve Lore, instead of gaining a single skill increase to a Lore.
