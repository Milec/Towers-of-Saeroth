# Flying  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 216

**Examples** bat, parrot, raven  
 **Dive Bomb** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The NPC commands their companion to fly at an enemy within 30 feet and flap its wings in their face. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of the NPC's turn.
