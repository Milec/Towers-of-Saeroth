# Modes of Play  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 398

This game presents three main methods of structuring play.

**[[Rules/Encounter Mode (Player Core)|Encounter mode]]** is highly structured and is most often used for combat or stressful situations. Everyone in an encounter rolls **[[Rules/Step 1- Roll Initiative (Player Core)|initiative]]** to determine the order they act, with highest results going first. A participant takes their **[[Rules/Turns (Player Core)|turn]]** when their initiative comes up. You can **[[Actions/Delay (Player Core)|Delay]]** to change when you take your turn.

**[[Rules/Exploration Mode (Player Core)|Exploration mode]]** takes place over minutes or hours. You use your [[Rules/Travel Speed (Player Core)|travel Speed]] if you're moving, and you engage in **[[Rules/Exploration Activities (Player Core)|exploration activities]]** like [[Actions/Avoid Notice (Player Core)|Avoiding Notice]], [[Actions/Detect Magic (Player Core)|Detecting Magic]], [[Actions/Scout (Player Core)|Scouting]], or [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]]. You can rest for the night while exploring to recover HP and abilities, and make [[Rules/Rest and Daily Preparations|daily preparations]] at the start of each day.

**[[Rules/Downtime Mode (Player Core)|Downtime mode]]** takes place over days. You might [[Rules/Earning Income|make money]], [[Rules/Retraining (Player Core)|train]], or [[Rules/Long-Term Rest (Player Core)|recover]], among other things.
