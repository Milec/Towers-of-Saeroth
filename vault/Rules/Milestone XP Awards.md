# Milestone XP Awards  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 540

As the kingdom grows, the kingdom gains XP the first time it reaches a milestone. These XP awards are given only once, the first time each milestone is attained.

## Kingdom Milestone XP Awards

| **XP Award** | **Milestone** |
| --- | --- |
| 40 | Claim your first [[Rules/Terrain Features|Landmark]] |
| 40 | Claim your first Refuge |
| 40 | [[Rules/Step 8- First Village|Establish your first village]] |
| 40 | Reach [[Rules/Kingdom Rules|kingdom Size]] 10 |
| 60 | Establish diplomatic relations for the first time |
| 60 | Expand a village into your first town |
| 60 | All eight leadership roles are assigned |
| 60 | Reach kingdom Size 25 |
| 80 | Establish your first trade agreement |
| 80 | Expand a town into your first city |
| 80 | Reach kingdom Size 50 |
| 80 | Spend 100 RP during a Kingdom turn |
| 120 | Expand a city into your first metropolis |
| 120 | Reach kingdom Size 100 |
