# Metamagic Terrain  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 220

Any eligible spell cast in this terrain has a particular metamagic effect added to it—typically [[Feats/Reach Spell]] or [[Feats/Widen Spell]]. If the caster uses a metamagic action before casting a spell, that action overrides the terrain's metamagic effect. These areas usually appear fairly normal, but fountains of magical energy spring up around a caster when they begin Casting a Spell.
