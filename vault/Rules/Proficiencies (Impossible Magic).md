# Proficiencies  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 69

Your eidolon’s level is equal to yours. They begin with expert proficiency in Fortitude and Will saves and trained proficiency in Reflex saves and Perception. In addition, they’re trained in [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks and unarmored defense. They share your skill proficiencies. Certain class features increase your eidolon’s proficiencies.
