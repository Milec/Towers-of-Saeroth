# Craft Requirements  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 535

An item might require the crafter to provide specific raw materials, supply spells, have a certain alignment, or meet other special requirements to [[Actions/Craft]] it. These appear in the Craft Requirements entry of the stat block. Every item also has default requirements, as listed in the Craft activity. In addition, creating alchemical items requires the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] feat, creating magic items requires the [[Feats/Magical Crafting]] feat, and crafting snares requires the [[Feats/Snare Crafting]] feat. Finally, crafting higher-level items requires greater proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]]. Unless stated otherwise, creating items of 9th level and higher requires you to have the master proficiency rank in Crafting, and items of 16th level and higher require legendary Crafting.

 The GM might allow you to Craft a permanent item from a lower-level version of the same item as an upgrade. For example, you might upgrade a [[Equipment/Bag of Holding|_bag of holding_]] from a type I to a type II bag, but you couldn't upgrade a [[Equipment/Aeon Stone|_clear spindle aeon stone_]] into an _orange prism aeon stone_. The cost for this upgrade is the full difference in Price between the items, and the Crafting check uses a DC for the item's new level.
