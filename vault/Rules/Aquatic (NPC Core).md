# Aquatic  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 216

**Examples** fish, octopus, otter, turtle  
 **Splash** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Requirements** The NPC is in water; **Effect** The NPC commands their companion to splash water on an enemy within 15 feet. That target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the beginning of the NPC's next turn.
