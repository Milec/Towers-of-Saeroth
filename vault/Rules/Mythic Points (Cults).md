# Mythic Points (Cults)  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 65

Adherents’ devotion doesn’t just feed the pantheon’s egos; it also generates additional [[Rules/Mythic Rules|mythic power]] the PCs can draw upon during their adventures. These Mythic Points are typically earned by the [[Setting/Cult Activities/Oversee Rites]] activity, though additional events might affect the total. A cult can have at most a number of Mythic Points equal to 3 or its Size modifier, whichever is greater. When a PC who belongs to the pantheon would spend a Mythic Point but has none remaining, they can gain and expend one of the cult’s Mythic Points.
