# Casting Cantrips from a Staff  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 278

You can cast a [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from a staff without expending any charges, heightened to the same rank as cantrips you cast.
