# Death  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 411

After you die, you can’t act or regain actions, can’t be affected by spells that target creatures (unless they specifically target dead creatures), and for all other purposes, you’re an object. When you die, you’re reduced to 0 Hit Points if you had a different amount, and you can’t be brought above 0 Hit Points as long as you remain dead. Some magic can bring creatures back to life, such as the [[Rituals/Resurrect (Player Core)|_resurrect_]] ritual or the [[Spells/Raise Dead (Player Core)|_raise dead_]] spell, though these abilities aren’t without their risks or effects, and rare artifacts and powers can even interfere with them.
