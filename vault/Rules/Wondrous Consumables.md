# Wondrous Consumables  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 268

Though many consumables are grouped into specific categories, such as potions and talismans, some consumables don’t fit into those categories.

[[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|Click here for the Other Consumables category page]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Expandable Trait
>
>
>
> An item with the [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|expandable]] trait increases to a specific size when activated. Unless otherwise noted, this space must be adjacent to you and on the ground, and the item needs to have enough open space to expand into or else the activation has no effect. When the effect ends, the expanded item disintegrates if it’s a [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]] or shrinks back to its normal size if it’s not a consumable.
