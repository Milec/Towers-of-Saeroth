# Quick Equipment Packages  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 268

_Nethys Note: This page combines Player Core 1 and 2's quick equipment packages._

If you want to quickly decide how to spend your starting money when playing a class from this book, start with one of these kits. The Armor, Weapons, and Gear entries include the basics, and the Options entries suggest additional items you might purchase with your leftover money to fit your character. Note that an [[Equipment/Adventurer's Pack (Player Core)|adventurer’s pack]], which is included in each kit, is 1 Bulk and contains a [[Equipment/Backpack (Player Core)|backpack]], [[Equipment/Bedroll (Player Core)|bedroll]], 10 pieces of [[Equipment/Chalk (Player Core)|chalk]], [[Equipment/Flint and Steel (Player Core)|flint and steel]], 50 feet of [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]], 2 weeks’ [[Equipment/Rations (Player Core)|rations]], [[Equipment/Soap (Player Core)|soap]], 5 [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torches]], and a [[Equipment/Waterskin (Player Core)|waterskin]].
