# Chapter 2: Classes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 53

There are more classes in the world of Pathfinder than those explored in the _Core Rulebook_, and each of those classes has near-infinite variety. This chapter introduces the new investigator, oracle, swashbuckler, and witch classes and offers new options for each of the 12 _Core Rulebook_ classes. The entries on pages 54–105 describe four new classes, including all the information you need to play each class from 1st to 20th level. The chapter continues with new content for the _Core Rulebook_ classes. If you're looking for something new for your favorite Core Rulebook class, those options are listed to the left. The chapter concludes on page 144 with new animal companion and familiar options.
