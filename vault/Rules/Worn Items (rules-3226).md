# Worn Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

This section includes magic items you wear. Most have the [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]] trait, which means you can wear no more than 10. Armor appears in its own section [[Rules/Armor (GM Core)|here]], high-level apex items that can increase ability modifiers are [[Rules/Apex Items (GM Core)|here]], and worn items for pets appear [[Rules/Companion Items (GM Core)|here]].
