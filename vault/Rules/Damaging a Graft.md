# Damaging a Graft  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

Once a graft is accepted, it becomes part of the subject’s body and can’t take damage separately, just like any natural body part. If a graft is ever severed from the subject’s body, it can be reconnected by any method that allows a severed body part to be reattached (such as the [[Spells/Regenerate (Player Core)|_regenerate_]] spell). If the graft is destroyed before it can be reattached, the subject typically can’t restore it.
