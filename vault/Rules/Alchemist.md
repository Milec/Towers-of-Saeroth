# Alchemist  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 195

An apprentice [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|alchemist]] gains the advanced alchemy ability of the alchemy class feature. Their advanced alchemy level is 1, and they have one batch of infused reagents each day. They can make only infused alchemical items.
