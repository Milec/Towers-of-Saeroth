# Step 4: Start Building!  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 543

Your brand new village is now ready to grow! A village must fill a single block of 4 lots before it can expand, so select one block on the Urban Grid for your village's development. Each Kingdom turn, during the [[Rules/Step 3- Civic Activities|Civic Activities]] step of its Activity phase, your settlement has one Civic activity, which can be used to [[Actions/Build Structure]].
