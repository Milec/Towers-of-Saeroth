# Tails  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 108

While a simple prosthetic tail can be represented by a [[Equipment/Basic Prosthesis|basic prosthesis]], the below options come with many useful added functions. Using a prosthetic tail effectively generally requires being a member of an ancestry or species that typically has a tail, though creatures from tailless ancestries or species may be able to learn to utilize one under appropriate conditions.
