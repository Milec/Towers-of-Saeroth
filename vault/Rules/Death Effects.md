# Death Effects  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 412

Some spells and abilities can kill you immediately or bring you closer to death without needing to reduce you to 0 Hit Points first. These abilities have the [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] trait and usually involve energy from the Void, the antithesis of life, or other effects that target the mind or soul. If you're reduced to 0 Hit Points by a death effect, you're slain instantly without needing to reach [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] 4. For instance, the [[Spells/Vision of Death|_vision of death_]] spell automatically kills those it reduces to 0 Hit Points as terrifying hallucinations cause them to drop dead.

A death effect might state that it kills you outright, without dealing damage, in which case you die without having to first reach dying 4 or be reduced to 0 Hit Points. For instance, the [[Bestiary/Grim Reaper (Monster Core)|Grim Reaper]], an extremely powerful entity of death, can automatically slay creatures who fail their save against its death strike ability.
