# Immunity to Nonlethal  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 408

Another exception is immunity to the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait. If you’re immune to nonlethal, you’re immune to all damage from attacks and effects with the nonlethal trait, no matter what other type the damage has. For instance, a typical [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]] has immunity to nonlethal attacks. No matter how hard you hit it with your fist, you’re not going to damage it. However, you can take a penalty to remove the nonlethal trait from your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]], and some abilities give you unarmed attacks without the nonlethal trait.
