# Camping Activities  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 109

When camping in the wilderness, the PCs may perform a number of different Camping activities; see [[Rules/Step 2- Camping Activities|Step 2: Camping Activities]] for detailed rules. Many of them require checks against the Zone DC for the area the party is camping in; see the [[Rules/Campsites|Camping Zones table]].
