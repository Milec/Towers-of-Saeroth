# Bulk Limits  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 272

You can carry an amount of Bulk equal to 5 plus your Strength modifier without penalty; if you carry more, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered|encumbered]] condition. You can’t hold or carry more Bulk than 10 plus your Strength modifier.
