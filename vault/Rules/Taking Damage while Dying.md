# Taking Damage while Dying  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 459

If you take damage while you already have the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition, increase your dying condition value by 1, or by 2 if the damage came from an attacker’s critical hit or your own critical failure. If you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition, remember to add the value of your wounded condition to your dying value.
