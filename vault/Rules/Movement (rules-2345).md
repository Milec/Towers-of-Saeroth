# Movement  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 420

Your movement and position determine how you interact with the world. Moving in exploration and downtime modes is relatively fluid. Movement in encounter mode follows additional rules explained in [[Rules/Tactical Movement]]. The rules below apply in all modes of play.
