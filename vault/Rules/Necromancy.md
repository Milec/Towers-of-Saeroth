# Necromancy  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 298

[[Traits/Necromancy]] spells harness the power of life and death. They can sap life essence or sustain creatures with life-saving healing. Necromancy spells often have the [[Traits/Curse|curse]], [[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]], or [[Traits/Positive|positive]] traits.
