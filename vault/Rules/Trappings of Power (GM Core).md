# Trappings of Power  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 270

This section includes magic items that you wear. Most of the items have the [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]] trait, which means you can wear no more than 10 of the item. If the “worn” usage for an item is followed by another word, like “worn belt,” you’re limited to wearing only one item of that type. If nothing’s listed after “worn,” as in the case of rings, you can wear any number.
