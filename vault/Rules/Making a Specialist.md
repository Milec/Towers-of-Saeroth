# Making a Specialist  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 204

These NPCs contain several specialists, such as barristers and navigators, who are a higher-level challenge at their specialty than in combat, and often have a special ability related to it. To make your own specialists, you can easily switch out skills, but you might want to use the following special abilities to make those NPCs more evocative!

**Butter Up** ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) When someone eats a dish or treat made by this NPC, the NPC can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Baking Lore]], Cooking Lore, or similar check against that creature's Will DC, with the same results as the [[Actions/Make an Impression]] action of [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]. If more than one creature partakes, the NPC uses the same check result against each creature's Will DC.

**Call Your Bluff** When gambling, the NPC may use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Games Lore]] to [[Actions/Sense Motive]] instead of Perception.

**Child Care** When treating infants, the NPC can use their [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Midwifery Lore]] skill in place of [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]], and can use Medicine's trained actions on infants.

**Find Footing** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) The NPC attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Architecture Lore]], Engineering Lore, or similar skill check to find a stable path across uneven ground. This grants them and anyone they share this information with a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance]] across that ground.

**Gone Fishing** The NPC can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Fishing Lore]] to [[Actions/Track]] aquatic creatures or to [[Actions/Aid]] checks to Track them.

**Local Lore** When they first meet a given person from their home settlement, the NPC can automatically attempt one check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about that person. This uses the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill for that settlement.

**Quick Transcription** The NPC can attempt to copy a document faster than normal, provided they don't alter the content. They attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Scribing Lore]] check, completing the task in half the time on a success (one-quarter on a critical success).
