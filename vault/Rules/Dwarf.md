# Dwarf  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 204

- Change the [[Traits/Human|human]] trait to [[Traits/Dwarf|dwarf]].
- Add [[Setting/Languages/Dwarven]] to the NPC's languages.
- Add [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
- Decrease the NPC's Speed by 5 feet. If the NPC already has a reduced Speed due their armor, consider applying the [[Feats/Unburdened Iron]] ancestry feat.
