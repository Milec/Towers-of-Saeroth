# Armor  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 555

Suits of armor can be crafted from precious materials or infused with magic to grant them abilities exceeding those of typical armor. Many suits of magic armor are created by etching runes onto them, as described on page 580. The magic armor stat block lists the Price and attributes of the most common armors you can make with fundamental runes. Other special suits of armor might be made of precious materials, and some are specially crafted items all on their own.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Armor Alternatives
>
>
>
> If you don't want to wear armor, or you're trained in only unarmored defense, you can wear either [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing|explorer's clothing]] or [[Equipment/Bracers of Armor|_bracers of armor_]]. Explorer's clothing can be etched with runes just like armor can, so it can provide item bonuses to AC or saves.
>
>  _Bracers of armor_ give a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dex modifier cap of +5, and they also grant a bonus to saves.
