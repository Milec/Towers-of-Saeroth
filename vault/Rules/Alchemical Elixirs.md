# Alchemical Elixirs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 546

Elixirs are alchemical liquids that are used by drinking them. They have the [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] trait. These potent concoctions grant the drinker some alchemical benefits. While all elixirs follow the same general rules, mutagens (described below) have additional rules that apply to their use.
