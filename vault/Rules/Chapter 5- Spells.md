# Chapter 5: Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 211

Here you'll find spells beyond those listed in the _Core Rulebook_, including ones that dispense horrid curses, make you immune to nearly all types of harm, and manifest mischievous monkey spirits to vex your foes! The following pages contain new magical powers for casters of every tradition.
