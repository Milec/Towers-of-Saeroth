# Animal Companions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 217

Some NPCs, such as the [[Bestiary/Beast Tamer (NPC Core)|beast tamer]], the [[Bestiary/Orc Gamekeeper|orc gamekeeper]], and the [[Bestiary/Pack Leader|pack leader]], take on and train animal companions to help them with their endeavors. The creatures from _Monster Core_ listed below work well in this role. Unlike a PC's animal companion, an NPC's animal companion has the standard number of actions, uses its normal stat block, and counts toward the encounter's XP budget normally.

 Several new creatures suitable for animal companions are presented beginning on page 218. You can use the [[Rules/Weak Adjustments|weak]] and [[Rules/Elite Adjustment|elite]] adjustments to scale their level to be appropriate for an encounter's budget.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Riding Animal Companions
>
>
>
> An NPC can ride a creature that works alongside them as long as the creature is at least one size larger than the rider. Generally, a creature that is carrying a rider can use only its land Speed and can't grant its support benefit. However, certain creatures that have been trained to be ridden, such as horses and riding drakes, ignores those restrictions.
