# Craft Requirements  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 223

An item might require the crafter to provide specific raw materials, supply spells, or meet other special requirements to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] it. These appear in the Craft Requirements entry of the stat block. Any of these Craft Requirements are in addition to the normal requirements for crafting the item.
