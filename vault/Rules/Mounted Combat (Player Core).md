# Mounted Combat  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 437

You can ride some creatures into combat. As noted in the [[Actions/Mount (Player Core)|Mount]] specialty basic action, your mount needs to be willing and at least one size larger than you. Your mount acts on your initiative. You must use the [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] action to get your mount to spend its actions. If you don't, the animal wastes its actions. If you have the [[Feats/Ride (Player Core)|Ride]] general feat, you succeed automatically when you Command an Animal that's your mount.

For example, if you are mounted on a [[Bestiary/Families/Horse (Monster Core)|horse]] and you make three attacks, your horse would remain stationary since you didn't command it. If you instead spent your first action to Command an Animal and succeeded, you could get your mount to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. You could spend your next action to attack or to command the horse to attack, but not both.
