# Chapter 6: Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 247

The investigator's trusty detective kit, a swashbuckler's flashy dueling cape, and a witch's effervescent cauldron are nearly as iconic as the characters who use them. For any hero, the gear and treasure they wield and collect throughout their adventures can play as important a role as any other facet of their character, from weapons steeped in cultural history to fantastic treasures of unparalleled worth!

 This chapter presents a variety of brand-new equipment and treasure that expands on and supplements the options provided in [[Rules/Chapter 6- Equipment (Core Rulebook)|Chapter 6: Equipment]] and [[Rules/Chapter 11- Crafting & Treasure|Chapter 11: Crafting & Treasure]] of the _Core Rulebook_. In particular, the gear that follows is especially suitable for the new ancestries and classes presented in this book, but the multitude of new items can enhance and enrich all types of characters.

 As it combines material from two chapters of the _Core Rulebook_, this chapter is divided into the following sections.
