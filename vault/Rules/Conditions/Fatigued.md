# Fatigued  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 620

You're tired and can't summon much energy. You take a –1 status penalty to AC and saving throws. You can't use exploration activities performed while traveling, such as those listed [[Rules/Exploration Activities|here]].

 You recover from fatigue after a full night's rest.
