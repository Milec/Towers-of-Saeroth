# Grabbed  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 444

You're held in place by another creature, giving you the [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] conditions. If you attempt a [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] action while grabbed, you must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or it is lost; roll the check after spending the action, but before any effects are applied.
