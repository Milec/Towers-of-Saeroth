# Prone  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 445

You're lying on the ground. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] and take a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls. The only [[Traits/Move|move]] actions you can use while you're prone are [[Actions/Crawl]] and [[Actions/Stand]]. Standing up ends the prone condition. You can [[Actions/Take Cover]] while prone to hunker down and gain [[Rules/Cover|greater cover]] against ranged attacks, even if you don't have an object to get behind, which grants you a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks (but you remain off-guard).

 If you would be knocked prone while you're [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] or [[Actions/Fly|Flying]], you [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|fall]]. You can't be knocked prone when [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]].
