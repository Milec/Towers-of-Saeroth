# Prone  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 621

You're lying on the ground. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and take a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls. The only [[Traits/Move|move]] actions you can use while you're prone are [[Actions/Crawl]] and [[Actions/Stand]]. Standing up ends the prone condition. You can [[Actions/Take Cover]] while prone to hunker down and gain [[Rules/Cover|greater cover]] against ranged attacks, even if you don't have an object to get behind, gaining a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks (but you remain flat-footed).

 If you would be knocked prone while you're [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] or [[Actions/Fly|Flying]], you fall (see [[Rules/Falling]] for the rules on falling). You can't be knocked prone when [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]].
