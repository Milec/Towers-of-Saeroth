# Paralyzed  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 445

You're frozen in place. You have the [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] condition and can't act except to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] and use actions that require only your mind (as determined by the GM). Your senses still function, but only in the areas you can perceive without moving, so you can't [[Actions/Seek]].
