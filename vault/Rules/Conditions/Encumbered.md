# Encumbered  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 619

You are carrying more weight than you can manage. While you’re encumbered, you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] and take a 10-foot penalty to all your Speeds. As with all penalties to your Speed, this can’t reduce your Speed below 5 feet.
