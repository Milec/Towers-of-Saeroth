# Invisible  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 444

You can't be seen. You're [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] to everyone. Creatures can [[Actions/Seek]] to detect you; if a creature succeeds at its Perception check against your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] DC, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to that creature until you [[Actions/Sneak]] to become undetected again. If you become invisible while someone can already see you, you start out hidden to them (instead of undetected) until you successfully Sneak. You can't become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] while invisible except via special abilities or magic.
