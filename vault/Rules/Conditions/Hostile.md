# Hostile  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 620

This condition reflects a creature's disposition toward a particular character, and only supernatural effects (like a spell) can impose this condition on a PC. A creature that is hostile to a character actively seeks to harm that character. It doesn't necessarily attack, but it won't accept [[Actions/Request|Requests]] from the character.
