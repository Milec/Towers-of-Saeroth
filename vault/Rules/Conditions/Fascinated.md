# Fascinated  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 619

You are compelled to focus your attention on something, distracting you from whatever else is going on around you. You take a –2 status penalty to Perception and skill checks, and you can't use actions with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait unless they or their intended consequences are related to the subject of your fascination (as determined by the GM). For instance, you might be able to [[Actions/Seek]] and [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about the subject, but you likely couldn't cast a spell targeting a different creature. This condition ends if a creature uses [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile actions]] against you or any of your allies.
