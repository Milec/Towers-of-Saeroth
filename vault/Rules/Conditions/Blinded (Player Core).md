# Blinded  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 442

You can't see. All normal terrain is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] to you. You can't detect anything using vision. You automatically critically fail Perception checks that require you to be able to see, and if vision is your only [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise sense]], you take a –4 status penalty to Perception checks. You are immune to [[Traits/Visual|visual]] effects. Blinded overrides [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]].
