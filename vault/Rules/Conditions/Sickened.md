# Sickened  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 622

You feel ill. Sickened always includes a value. You take a status penalty equal to this value on all your checks and DCs. You can't willingly ingest anything—including [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixirs]] and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|potions]]—while sickened.

 You can spend a single action retching in an attempt to recover, which lets you immediately attempt a Fortitude save against the DC of the effect that made you sickened. On a success, you reduce your sickened value by 1 (or by 2 on a critical success).
