# Hidden  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 444

While you're hidden from a creature, that creature knows the space you're in but can't tell precisely where you are. You typically become hidden by using [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] to [[Actions/Hide]]. When [[Actions/Seek|Seeking]] a creature using only [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] senses, it remains hidden, rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]]. A creature you're hidden from is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you, and it must succeed at a DC 11 flat check when targeting you with an attack, spell, or other effect or it fails to affect you. Area effects aren't subject to this flat check.

 A creature might be able to use the Seek action to try to observe you.
