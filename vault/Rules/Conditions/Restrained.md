# Restrained  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 622

You're tied up and can barely move, or a creature has you pinned. You have the [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] conditions, and you can't use any actions with the [[Traits/Attack|attack]] or [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] traits except to attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] or [[Actions/Force Open]] your bonds. Restrained overrides [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].
