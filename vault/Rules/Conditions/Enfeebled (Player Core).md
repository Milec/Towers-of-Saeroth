# Enfeebled  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 443

You're physically weakened. Enfeebled always includes a value. When you are enfeebled, you take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Strength-based rolls and DCs, including Strength-based melee attack rolls, Strength-based damage rolls, and [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks.
