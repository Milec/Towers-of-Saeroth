# Clumsy  *Condition*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 442

Your movements become clumsy and inexact. Clumsy always includes a value. You take a status penalty equal to the condition value to Dexterity-based rolls and DCs, including AC, Reflex saves, ranged attack rolls, and skill checks using [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]], and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]].
