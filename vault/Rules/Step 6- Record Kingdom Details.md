# Step 6: Record Kingdom Details  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 510

The PCs should choose a name for their kingdom. Record it on their kingdom sheet along with the following initial statistics.

- The kingdom's level (see [[Rules/Leveling Up your Kingdom|Leveling Up Your Kingdom]] is 1, and its maximum level is equal to the party level.
- The kingdom's [[Rules/Kingdom Rules|Size]] is 1.
- The kingdom's [[Rules/Resource Die]] is a d4, and its Resource Dice total is 5. The kingdom's [[Rules/Control DC]] is 14.
- The kingdom's [[Rules/Step 4- Pay Consumption|Consumption]] is 0.
- The kingdom's [[Rules/Unrest]] is 0.
- All four [[Rules/Ruin|Ruins]] start at 0, and each Ruin's initial threshold is 10.

The kingdom's initial [[Rules/Commodities|commodity stores]] are 0, and its maximum storage limit for each is 4.
