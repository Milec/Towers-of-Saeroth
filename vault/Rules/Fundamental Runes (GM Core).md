# Fundamental Runes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 225

Five fundamental runes produce the most essential magic of protection and destruction: [[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|armor potency]] and _[[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient runes]]_ for armor, the _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|reinforcing]]_ rune for shields, and _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|weapon potency]]_ and _[[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking runes]]_ for weapons. A _potency rune_ is what makes a weapon a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|runic weapon]]_ or armor _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|magic armor]]_.

An item can have only one fundamental rune of each type, though etching a stronger rune can upgrade an existing rune to the more powerful version (as described in each rune's entry). As you level up, you typically alternate between increasing an item's potency rune and its _striking_ or _resilient_ rune when you can afford to.
