# The Etching Process  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 225

Etching a rune onto an item follows the same process as using the [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] activity to make an item. You must be able to Craft magic items, have the item you're adding the rune to in your possession throughout the etching process, and meet any special Craft Requirements. The rune has no effect until you complete the Craft activity. You can etch only one rune at a time.
