# Defenses  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 399

Your **[[Rules/Armor Class (rules-2295)|Armor Class]]** (AC) is the main DC used for attacks against you. You might also roll a type of check called a **[[Rules/Saving Throws (rules-2296)|saving throw]]**, also called a save, against spells, afflictions, and a wide variety of other effects. There are three kinds of saving throw: **Fortitude**, **Reflex**, and **Will**.
