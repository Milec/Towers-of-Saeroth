# Purging  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 187

A blighted boon is difficult to get rid of, and the Purging section of each boon entry details the only way to do so. If a [[Rules/Partaking (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|partaker]] satisfies the Purging conditions, all the blighted boon's effects end. For other effects that suppress or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]], treat a blighted boon as an [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]].
