# Example  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 167

Lini selected mostly transmutation spells for her staff, so she chooses the [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]] trait. She could give it the [[Traits/Primal|primal]] trait, but choses to keep it open with the [[Traits/Magical|magical]] trait, much like the [[Equipment/Verdant Staff|_verdant staff_]] in the _Core Rulebook_.

 To Craft the staff, Lini follows the normal rules. She provides 3,250 gp in raw materials, spends 4 days at work, and attempts a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check. Each day she works on it, she prepares the spells she needs to put into the staff given its Craft Requirements.
