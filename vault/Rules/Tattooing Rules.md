# Tattooing Rules  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 164

To craft a magical tattoo, you must be able to craft magic items and have a specialty in tattooing. You can attain these requirements by taking the [[Feats/Tattoo Artist (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Tattoo Artist]] skill feat, or you can take both the [[Feats/Magical Crafting]] and [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feats, choosing artistry as your specialty.
