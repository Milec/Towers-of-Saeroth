# Portcullises  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 515

A portcullis is a wooden or iron grate that descends to seal off a gate or corridor. Most are raised on ropes or chains operated by a winch, and they have locking mechanisms that keep them from being lifted easily. The rules on lifting a portcullis or bending its bars appear in the sidebar on this page. If a portcullis falls on a creature, use a [[Hazards/Slamming Door|slamming door]] trap.
