# Dim Light  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 432

Areas in shadow or lit by weak light sources are in dim light. Creatures and objects in dim light have the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition, unless the seeker has [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] or [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], or a [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]] other than vision.
