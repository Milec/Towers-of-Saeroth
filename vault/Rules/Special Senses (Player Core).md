# Special Senses  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 433

While a [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|human]] might have a difficult time making creatures out in [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]], an [[Ancestries/Elf (Player Core)|elf]] can see those creatures just fine. And though elves have no problem seeing on a moonlit night, their vision can’t penetrate complete [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], whereas a [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|dwarf’s]] can. Special senses allow a creature to ignore or reduce the effects of the [[Rules/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], [[Rules/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], or [[Rules/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] conditions when it comes to situations that foil average vision.
