# Cover  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 13

You determine whether a character has cover. The rules for drawing lines found in the _Core Rulebook_ are useful in simple cases, but in more complicated situations, use your own discretion to make the call. Consider the details of the environment and 3-D space beyond what’s on the battle mat. For instance, hanging banners might give cover, or a PC who has climbed onto a ledge might have a clear shot at an enemy standing behind a short wall. Be generous to PCs who use creativity to get into smart positions, especially if they spend valuable actions to move or [[Actions/Take Cover]].
