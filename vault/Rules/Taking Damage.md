# Taking Damage  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 411

If you take damage while you already have the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition, increase your dying condition value by 1, or by 2 if the damage came from an attacker’s critical hit or your own critical failure.
