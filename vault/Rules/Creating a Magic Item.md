# Creating a Magic Item  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 155

As a crafter, creating a magic item is one of the most intensely personal projects you can undertake. While every [[Equipment/Wondrous Figurine|_serpentine wondrous figurine_]] might more or less resemble a knotted snake carved out of jade, what type of snake is it? And what type of jade? Ten different figurines from ten different crafters could each show a distinct artistic mark, and experts can identify the handiwork of noteworthy crafters on sight. Similarly, items activated with commands typically each have unique utterances determined by the crafter. Before setting out to create a magic item, contemplate how your personality might inhabit and shape the item created.
