# Ability Boosts  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 68

At 5th level and every 5 levels thereafter, your character boosts four different ability scores. Your character can use these ability boosts to increase their ability scores above 18. Boosting an ability score increases it by 1 if it’s already 18 or above, or by 2 if it starts out below 18. For more about ability boosts and applying them during character creation, see [[Rules/Ability Boosts|here]].
