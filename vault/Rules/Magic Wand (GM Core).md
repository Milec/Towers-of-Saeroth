# Magic Wand  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 282

The simplest form of wand contains a spell, with Price and level based on that spell. The name of a [[Equipment/Magic Wand (GM Core)|magic wand]] with a spell in it is simply “wand of,” followed by the spell’s name.
