# Intervals  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 186

Blighted boon stages advance in intervals, amounts of time shown in parentheses for each stage. Once a stage's interval passes, the [[Rules/Partaking (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|partaker]] can attempt a saving throw against the blighted boon. The save DC equals 10 + the partaker's level + the blighted boon's current stage + the number of previous successful saving throws. On a failure, the partaker advances to the next stage. Success means the boon remains at its current stage for the same interval. The partaker can't reduce a blighted boon's stage. To do that, the blight must be subjected to successful purging.
