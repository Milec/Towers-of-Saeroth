# Changeling  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 29

Children of the malevolent, magical crones known as [[Bestiary/Families/Hag|hags]], changelings share some of their mothers' abilities, such as sharp claws and occult magic. Many changelings experience or dread the psychic summons of their hag mothers, known as the Call, urging them to seek out their mothers so they might be transformed into hags themselves.

 Changeling lineages affect the appearance of one of the changeling's eyes, and are as follows: brine may for the child of a [[Bestiary/Sea Hag|sea hag]], callow may for a [[Bestiary/Green Hag|green hag]], dream may for a [[Bestiary/Night Hag|night hag]], and slag may for an [[Bestiary/Annis Hag|annis hag]].
