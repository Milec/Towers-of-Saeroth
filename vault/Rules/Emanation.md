# Emanation  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 457

An emanation issues forth from each side of your space, extending out to a specified number of feet in all directions. For instance, the [[Spells/Bless|_bless_]] spell's emanation radiates 5 or more feet outward from the caster. Because the sides of a creature's space are the starting point for the emanation, an emanation from a Large or larger creature affects a greater overall area than that of a Medium or smaller creature. Unless the text states otherwise, the creature creating an emanation effect chooses whether the creature at its center is affected.
