# Running the Encounter  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 26

Once you’ve rolled initiative and set the scene, it’s time to dive into the encounter. Go around the table in initiative order, asking each player what they would like to do on their turn; when it comes time for any NPCs, monsters, or features of the environment to act, you play them yourself.
