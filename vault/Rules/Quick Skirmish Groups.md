# Quick Skirmish Groups  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 169

If you want to have a skirmish encounter that’s ready to go, here are some skirmish encounters pre-built for certain levels. They include one troop per PC for a four-person party, but you can remove troops or add duplicate troops to each side to adjust. Creatures come from the [[Traits/Troop (NPC Core)|troop]] trait.
