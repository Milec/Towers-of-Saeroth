# Conjuration  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 297

[[Traits/Conjuration]] spells transport creatures via teleportation, create an object, or bring a creature or object from somewhere else (typically from another plane) to follow your commands.

 Conjuration spells often have the [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] trait, and the creatures summoned by conjuration spells have the [[Traits/Summoned|summoned]] trait.
