# Precious Material Armor  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 228

Suits of armor made of precious materials are more expensive and sometimes grant special effects. You can make metal armor out of any precious materials except for [[Equipment/Duskwood|duskwood]], or you can make wooden armor out of duskwood. Because armor’s Bulk is reduced when the armor is worn, use its carried Bulk when determining its material Price. ([[Rules/Materials (GM Core)|Materials are found here]].)
