# Prone and Incapacitated Creatures  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 422

You can share a space with a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] creature if that creature is willing, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], or dead and if it is your size or smaller. The GM might allow you to climb atop the corpse or unconscious body of a larger creature in some situations. A prone creature can’t stand up while someone else occupies its space, but it can [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]] to a space where it’s able to stand, or it can attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] the other creature out of the way.
