# Step 3: Compare to DC  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 401

Whenever you attempt a check, you compare your result against the **Difficulty Class (DC)** of the check. Your check succeeds if it's equal to or greater than the DC. If you roll anything less than the DC, you fail.

Sometimes you'll know the DC and make the comparison yourself. Other times, you might not know the DC right away. [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] across a river would require an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check, but it doesn't have a specified DC—so how will you know if you succeed or fail? You call out your result to the GM and they'll let you know if it's a success, failure, or otherwise. While you might learn the exact DC through trial and error, DCs sometimes change, so asking the GM whether a check is successful is best.
