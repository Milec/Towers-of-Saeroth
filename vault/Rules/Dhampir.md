# Dhampir  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 29

The mortal offspring of undead [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire|vampires]] are caught between life and undeath, mortality and immortality, mundane existence and powerful magic. Dhampirs enjoy long life spans and otherworldly charm, but their ghostly pallor and the nature of their parentage make their lives in humanoid societies difficult.

 The two lineages for dhampirs found here are the svetocher, who come from the most common vampires called the moroi, and the straveika, born of the aged and unsettling nosferatu.
