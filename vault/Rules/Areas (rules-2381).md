# Areas  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 426

Some effects occupy an area of a specified shape and size. An area effect always has a point of origin and extends out from that point. There are four types of areas: emanations, bursts, cones, and lines. See [[Rules/Area]] for details.
