# Routing Troops  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 160

A troop is held together by strong leadership and confidence in their battle skill. A troop must attempt a rout check each time something happens that could massively damage their morale. This happens in two situations:

- The troop is reduced from one HP threshold to another.
- The troop has a leader, and that leader is knocked unconscious.

If both happen at the same time, the troop rolls only one rout check. To attempt its rout check, the troop rolls a Will save against the standard DC for the level of the enemy that damaged it. This is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.  
**Critical Success** The troop is unaffected.  
**Success** The troop is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  
**Failure** The troop is frightened 2.  
**Critical Failure** The members of the troop break formation and run away, removing the troop from the battle.

The result of a rout check applies only to the troop, not its leader. If the troop is led by a PC or NPC, the degree of success increases by one step, as noted in [[Rules/Boosted Morale]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Unroutable Troops
>
>
>
> Because of the traits on a rout check, some troops are entirely immune to them, in particular [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|mindless]] hordes like [[Bestiary/Families/Zombie (Monster Core)|zombies]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Clockworks|clockwork constructs]]. NPCs often use these kinds of troops due to this advantage, but PCs might also be able to raise [[Bestiary/Skeleton Infantry|skeleton infantry]] or build a similar fearless army.
