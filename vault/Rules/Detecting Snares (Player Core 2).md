# Detecting Snares  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 297

Creatures can detect snares they aren't already aware of as they would any [[Rules/Hazards (rules-2846)|trap or hazard]], using the creator's Crafting DC as the snare's Stealth DC. If your proficiency rank is expert or better in Crafting, only creatures actively searching can find your snares
