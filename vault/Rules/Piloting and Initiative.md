# Piloting and Initiative  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 35

You can simplify initiative to make sailing combat run more smoothly. The easiest option is to place the ships’ pilots at the top of the initiative order, rolling [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] for initiative but comparing their results only to one another. This ensures that both ships maneuver into position before anyone else on board takes their actions. The two sides of the conflict need to deal with the same circumstances for their attacks, spells, and other actions.
