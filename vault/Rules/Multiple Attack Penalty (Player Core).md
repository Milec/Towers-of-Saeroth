# Multiple Attack Penalty  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 275

If you use an action with the attack trait more than once on the same turn, your attacks after the first take a penalty called a multiple attack penalty. Your second attack takes a –5 penalty, and any subsequent attacks take a –10 penalty.

The multiple attack penalty doesn't apply to attacks you make when it isn't your turn (such as attacks made as part of a reaction, like Reactive Strike). You can use a weapon with the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] trait (page 282) to reduce your multiple attack penalty.
