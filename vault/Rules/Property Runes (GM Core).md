# Property Runes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 225

Property runes add special abilities to armor or a weapon in addition to the item's fundamental runes. If a suit of armor or a weapon has multiple etchings of the same rune, only the highest-level one applies. You can upgrade a property rune to a higher-level type of that rune in the same way you would upgrade a fundamental rune.

Rune abilities that must be activated follow the rules for [[Rules/Activating Items (GM Core)|activating magic items]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Specific Armor, Weapons, and Shields
>
>
>
> Unlike armor, weapons, and shields enhanced with runes, specific armor, weapons, and shields (such as [[Equipment/Moonlit Chain (GM Core)|moonlit chain]], a [[Equipment/Bloodletting Kukri (GM Core)|bloodletting kukri]], or a [[Equipment/Lion's Shield (GM Core)|lion's shield]]) are created for a specific purpose and can work quite differently from other items of their type. These items can’t gain property runes, but you can add or improve their fundamental runes.
