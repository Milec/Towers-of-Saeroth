# Evocation  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 298

[[Traits/Evocation|Evocations]] capture magical energy and then shape it to harm your foes or protect your allies. Evocation spells often have a trait that comes from the type of damage they deal, such as [[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Force|force]], or [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]].
