# Eidolons  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 69

Though each eidolon is a unique creature and there are many types of eidolons, each draws upon a particular tradition of magic and manifests from related essence. Multiple types of each eidolon exist as well—for instance, the types of construct eidolon presented in this book are the warrior construct and the scout construct.

 [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]] eidolons are usually formed of mental essence, also known as astral essence. They include [[Companions/Eidolons/Construct (Impossible Magic)|construct]] eidolons and [[Rules/Sidebars/Motion Sense|ooze]] eidolons, beings formed into simple shapes through arcane magic and held together through mental fortitude.

 [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]] eidolons are always formed of spiritual essence, much like the divine servitors they resemble. They include representatives of the many families of divine servitors, such as [[Companions/Eidolons/Angel (Impossible Magic)|angels]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Embodiments of Sin (Impossible Magic)|demons]], and [[Companions/Eidolons/Psychopomp (Impossible Magic)|psychopomps]].

 [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]] eidolons are also usually formed of spiritual essence, though they tend toward a more ectoplasmic consistency than divine eidolons. Occult eidolons usually form from the essence of a spirit that is unwilling or unable to pass on and find refuge with a summoner to avoid becoming undead; these types of eidolons are known as phantoms. Each phantom eidolon has a connection to a particular emotion, such as the [[Companions/Eidolons/Anger Phantom (Impossible Magic)|anger phantom]] and [[Companions/Eidolons/Devotion Phantom (Impossible Magic)|devotion phantom]] described in this book.

 [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]] eidolons usually manifest from life essence. Their forms resemble creatures found in the natural world, such as [[Companions/Eidolons/Beast (Impossible Magic)|beasts]], [[Companions/Eidolons/Plant (Impossible Magic)|plants]], [[Companions/Eidolons/Fey (Impossible Magic)|fey]], or some combination thereof.
