# Spellhearts  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 170

Spellhearts are made from the same basic materials as [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]], though their complex construction—magically iterated around the concept of a single kind of spell—gives them a variety of additional powers and means they persist rather than burning out when used.

 Unlike talismans, a spellheart can be used repeatedly, and doesn't burn out after use. Each spellheart can be attached to different items, giving a benefit depending on your choice, which means you can plan ahead in facing expected dangers. They have the [[Traits/Spellheart|spellheart]] trait.
