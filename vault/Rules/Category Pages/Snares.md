# Snares  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 297

Snares are simple traps that can be created quickly with the [[Feats/Snare Crafting (Player Core 2)|Snare Crafting]] to injure or bedevil intruders. Unlike other items, found snares can't be collected or sold in their complete form. Snares have the [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|snare]] trait.
