# Magical Snares  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 73

Magical snares have the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] trait. Unlike nonmagical snares, their presence can be detected with [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]], though not their precise locations. A discovered magical snare can be disabled with [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]]; the DC of the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check is the Crafting DC of the snare's creator. Magical traps with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait aren't triggered by mindless creatures. A snare that is purely magical and doesn't have the [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|mechanical]] trait might not be triggered or destroyed by large effects or by effects that specifically target unattended objects at the GM's discretion.
