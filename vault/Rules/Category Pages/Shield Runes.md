# Shield Runes  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 232

Though shields can't be etched with weapon or armor runes, they can be improved by a specific type of fundamental rune known as a _reinforcing _ rune. _Reinforcing_ runes can be etched only on shields, including specific shields, and every _reinforcing_ rune includes maximum Hardness and Hit Point values. Since the runes work by increasing the structural integrity of a shield by a certain amount, they can't increase the durability of a shield beyond a listed maximum value. Shields can't be etched with property runes, only reinforcing runes.
