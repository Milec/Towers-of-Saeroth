# Lessons  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 185

A witch's knowledge takes the form of lessons, which you can learn by selecting feats like [[Feats/Basic Lesson]] or [[Feats/Greater Lesson]]. Each lesson grants you a hex and teaches your familiar a new spell, adding it to the spells you can prepare using your witch spellcasting. You gain this spell even if it's not on your tradition's spell list.
