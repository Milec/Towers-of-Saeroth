# Base Weapons  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 278

Most characters in Pathfinder carry weapons, ranging from mighty warhammers to graceful bows to even simple clubs. Full details on how you calculate the bonuses, modifiers, and penalties for [[Rules/Attack Rolls (Core Rulebook)|attack rolls]] and [[Rules/Damage (Core Rulebook)|damage rolls]] are given in Chapter 9, but they’re summarized here, followed by the rules for weapons and dozens of weapon choices.
