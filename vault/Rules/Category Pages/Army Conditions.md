# Army Conditions  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 582

When an army becomes affected by a condition, that condition's effects last until the condition's stated duration ends or the condition is removed. As with character conditions, some army conditions [[Rules/Conditions (Core Rulebook)|override others]].
