# Familiars  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 212

Familiars are mystically bonded creatures tied to your magic. Most familiars were originally [[Traits/Animal|animals]], though the ritual of becoming a familiar makes them something more. You gain the [[Feats/Pet]] general feat, except that your pet has special abilities. Common choices for familiars include bats, cats, foxes, ravens, and snakes.
