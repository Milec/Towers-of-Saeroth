# Specific Magic Armor  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 230

These suits of armor have abilities far different from what can be gained by etching runes. A specific magic armor lists its [[Rules/Fundamental Runes (GM Core)|fundamental runes]], which you can upgrade, add, or transfer as normal. You can’t etch or transfer any [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]] onto a specific armor that it doesn’t already have or remove its property runes.
