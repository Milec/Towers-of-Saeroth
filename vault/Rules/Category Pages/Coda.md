# Coda  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 136

While many spellcasters prefer standard magic staves, [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|bards]] often quest to find or create magical instruments to help supplement their songs. Instruments with the [[Traits/Coda (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|coda]] trait work mostly like staves, and have the [[Traits/Staff|staff]] trait. There are two differences: Coda instruments are in the form of [[Equipment/Musical Instrument|musical instruments]], and they can be prepared only by bards. Because they're not physically staves, you can't attack with a coda instrument nor can you etch it with weapon runes.

 The process of preparing a coda instrument involves playing significant portions of songs related to the spells within the instrument—either old standards or ones of your own creation. This leaves magical reverberations within the instrument that allow you to complete the songs by playing their coda later in the day
