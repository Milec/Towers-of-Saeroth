# Companion Items  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 604

You might want to acquire items that benefit an animal or beast that assists you. These items have the [[Traits/Companion|companion]] trait, meaning they function only for animal companions, familiars, and similar creatures. If it's unclear whether a creature can benefit from such an item, the GM decides.
