# Mobility Devices  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 90

Wheelchairs provide comfort and support when traveling. You can use the item either in an ambulatory manner or every day, depending on your character. Wheelchairs come in a variety of sizes to suit every person regardless of height or body type.
