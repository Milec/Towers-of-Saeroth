# Critical Specialization Effects  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 283

Certain effects can grant you benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success. This is called a [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]]. The exact effect depends on which weapon group your weapon belongs to, as listed below. You can always decide not to add the critical specialization effect of your weapon.
