# Lessons  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 99

A witch's knowledge from their patron comes in the form of lessons, which you can learn by selecting feats like [[Feats/Basic Lesson]]. Each lesson grants you a hex and teaches your familiar a new spell, adding it to the spells you can prepare using your witch spellcasting. You gain this spell even if it's not on your tradition's spell list.
