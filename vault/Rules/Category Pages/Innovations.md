# Innovations  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 16

While you're always creating inventions, there's one that represents your preeminent work, the one that you hope—with refinement—might change the world. Choose one of the below innovations. Your innovation's level is equal to your level. If your innovation is destroyed, you can spend 1 day of downtime and attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check with a [[Rules/Level-Based DCs|high DC for your level]]; on a success, you rebuild it. An innovation only works due to your constant maintenance and tinkering, and therefore has no market Price.
