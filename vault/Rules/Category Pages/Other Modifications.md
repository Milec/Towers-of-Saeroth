# Other Modifications  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 182

While holsters, scopes, and stabilizers are some of the more common accessories associated with firearms and crossbows, they're far from the only innovations that support such weapons. The variety of accessories is matched only by the inventors' creativity. Blazons and emblems for paired weapons are popular purchases for fashion-oriented aristocrats who like their weapons to match aesthetically and magically. Talented gunsmiths produce many other commodities for firearm enthusiasts to further customize their weapons to their preferences.
