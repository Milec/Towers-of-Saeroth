# Magic Wands  *Category Page*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 597

The simplest form of wand contains a spell, with Price and level based on that spell. The wand has the [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and [[Traits/Wand|wand]] traits, as well as the traits the spell has. The name of a magic wand with a spell in it is simply “wand of,” followed by the spell's name.
