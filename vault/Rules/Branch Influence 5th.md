# Branch Influence 5th  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 64

At 5th level in a branch, you gain greater influence, making it easier to collaborate, hoodwink, or browbeat members of that branch. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks to interact with members of the branch.

 At 13th level, this increases to a +2 circumstance bonus, and at 17th level, it increases again to a +3 circumstance bonus.
