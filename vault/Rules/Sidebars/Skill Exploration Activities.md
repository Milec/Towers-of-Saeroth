# Skill Exploration Activities  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 479

**Parent page** [[Rules/Exploration Activities]]

---

Chapter 4: Skills includes numerous additional exploration activities, which are summarized here.

**Borrow an Arcane Spell**: You use [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] to prepare a spell from someone else’s spellbook.

**Coerce**: You use [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] to threaten a creature so it does what you want.

**Cover Tracks**: You use [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] to obscure your passing.

**Decipher Writing**: You use a suitable skill to understand archaic, esoteric, or obscure texts (page 234).

**Gather Information**: You use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to canvass the area to learn about a specific individual or topic.

**Identify Alchemy**: You use [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Craft]] and [[Equipment/Alchemist's Tools|alchemist’s tools]] to identify an alchemical item.

**Identify Magic**: Using a variety of skills, you can learn about a magic item, location, or ongoing effect (page 238).

**Impersonate**: You use [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and usually a [[Equipment/Disguise Kit|disguise kit]] to create a disguise.

**Learn a Spell**: You use the skill corresponding to the spell’s tradition to gain access to a new spell (page 238).

**Make an Impression**: You use Diplomacy to make a good impression on someone.

**Repair**: With a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]] and the Crafting skill, you fix a damaged item.

**Sense Direction**: You use Survival to get a sense of where you are or determine the cardinal directions.

**Squeeze**: Using [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], you squeeze though very tight spaces (page 241).

**Track**: You use Survival to find and follow creatures’ tracks.

**Treat Wounds**: You use [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] to treat a living creature’s wounds.
