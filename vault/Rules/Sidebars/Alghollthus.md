# Alghollthus  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Azarketi Ancestry Web Supplement]] pg. 2

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Legacy of Azlant|Azarketi]]

---

Azarketis have a complex relationship with [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu|alghollthus]], who saved but then enslaved their people. Alvadnas have the greatest chance of meeting an alghollthu face to face. Azarketi sentiments toward these creatures vary: some fight fiercely against their ancient enemies; others bargain for power or safety, or even serve through compulsion.
