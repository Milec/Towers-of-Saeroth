# Ifrit Settlements  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 100

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Ifrit Settlements|Ifrit]]

---

The City of Brass, the largest city on the Elemental Planes, has a population that overshadows even the greatest cities on Golarion. More ifrits are born here than anywhere else, though they are born second-class citizens. Cinderveil, an ifrit city on the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane|Plane of Shadow]], was founded 100 years ago when a mountain corresponding to the location of the Eye of Abendego on the Material Plane erupted in ash and dust.
