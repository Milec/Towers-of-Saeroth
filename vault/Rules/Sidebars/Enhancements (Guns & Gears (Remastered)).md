# Enhancements  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 36

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Enhancements (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Automaton]]

---

Automatons are built to receive enhancements and modifications to their bodies. Many automaton ancestry feats have an “Enhancement” line that represents a possible augmentation you can acquire. You don't gain the benefits of the enhancement unless you take a feat that grants you those benefits, such as [[Feats/Lesser Augmentation (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Lesser Augmentation]]. You can only gain the enhancements of a given feat once.
