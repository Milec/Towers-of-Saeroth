# Key Terms  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 192

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|Wizard]]

---

You'll see the following key term in many wizard abilities.

**[[Traits/Spellshape]]:** Actions with the spellshape trait tweak the properties of your spells. These actions usually come from spellshape feats. You must use a spellshape action directly before Casting the Spell you want to alter. If you use any action (including free actions and reactions) other than Cast a Spell directly after, you waste the benefits of the spellshape action. Any additional effects added by a spellshape action are part of the spell's effect, not of the spellshape action itself.
