# Item Categories  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 219

**Parent page** [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|Invest an Item]]

---

Items are grouped into the following categories, shown here with the page number where those items appear and a brief description of the category.

- [[Rules/Armor & Armaments|**Armor & Armaments**]] includes rules for etching runes to enhance weapons and armor, armor runes, [[Rules/Specific Magic Armor (GM Core)|specific suits of armor]], [[Rules/Shields (rules-3174)|shields]] and the [[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|reinforcing rune]], weapon runes, and specific weapons .
- [[Rules/Alchemy|**Alchemy**]] items are powered by the reactions of alchemical reagents. Almost all alchemical items are [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]] items that are used up when you activate them. This category includes [[Rules/Alchemical Bombs (GM Core)|bombs]], [[Rules/Elixirs (Player Core)|elixirs]], [[Rules/Alchemical Poisons (GM Core)|poisons]], and [[Rules/Alchemical Tools (GM Core)|alchemical tools]].
- [[Rules/Materials (GM Core)|**Materials**]] includes the statistics for standard items and structures along with rules for precious materials that can be used to make items with unique properties and other advantages.
- [[Rules/Momentary Magic (GM Core)|**Momentary Magic**]] has a plethora of magical consumables, including [[Rules/Ammunition (rules-3193)|ammunition]], [[Rules/Oils (rules-3195)|oils]] applied to the surface of objects and people, [[Rules/Potions (rules-3196)|potions]] to drink, [[Rules/Scrolls (rules-3197)|scrolls]] used to cast more spells, [[Rules/Talismans (rules-3203)|talismans]] affixed to items, and [[Rules/Wondrous Consumables|wondrous consumables]] of all sorts.
- [[Rules/Trappings of Power (GM Core)|**Trappings of Power**]] are permanent and powerful. They include [[Rules/Apex Items (GM Core)|apex items]] that can increase an attribute modifier, [[Rules/Companion Items (GM Core)|companion items]] for pets, [[Rules/Held Items (rules-3210)|held items]], magical [[Rules/Staves (rules-3211)|staves]] for spellcasters, [[Rules/Wands (rules-3218)|wands]] that can repeatedly cast more spells, and a vast collection of [[Rules/Worn Items (rules-3226)|worn items]].
- [[Rules/Gems & Art Objects|**Gems & Art Objects**]] make for an interesting variety of treasures.
- [[Rules/Artifacts (GM Core)|**Artifacts**]] are extraordinarily powerful magic items ideal to build adventures and plots around.
- [[Rules/Intelligent Items (GM Core)|**Intelligent Items**]] provides options for conscious items.
- [[Rules/Cursed Items (GM Core)|**Cursed Items**]], used sparingly, can add danger and fun to items.
- [[Rules/Relics (GM Core)|**Relics**]] let you build a special item that unlocks new powers over time through the story of a campaign.
