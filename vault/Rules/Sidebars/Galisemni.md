# Galisemni  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 144

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Galisemni|Maelstrom]]

---

Only powerful magic can stabilize the [[Rules/Sidebars/Galisemni|Maelstrom]] for a time, allowing stable islands to form. Most prominent among these is the massive planar trade city of Galisemni, which lies in the Borderlands, a somewhat-more-stable region of the Maelstrom where it brushes up against the other Outer Planes.
