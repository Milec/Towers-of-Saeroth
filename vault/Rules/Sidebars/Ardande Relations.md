# Ardande Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 46

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Ardande Settlements|Ardande]]

---

[[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Forest|Forest dragons]] and [[Bestiary/Dryad|dryad]] are common ancestors for ardandes born before the return of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]], or kizidhars who became trapped in the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane|Universe]] and were forced to make new lives for themselves. Ardandes with [[Bestiary/Wood Scamp|wood scamp]] parents are often short and bushy, like living shrubs, and are often mistaken for [[Rules/Sidebars/Green Men|leshies]] and [[Ancestries/Conrasu|conrasus]].
