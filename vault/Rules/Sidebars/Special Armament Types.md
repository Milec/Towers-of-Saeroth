# Special Armament Types  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 232

**Parent page** [[Rules/Binding an Armament]]

---

### Specific Items
 You can turn a specific item into your armament, such as a [[Equipment/Flame Tongue|flame tongue]] or [[Equipment/Sturdy Shield|sturdy shield]], though some unusual or special items can't be bonded, such as cursed items, artifacts, and intelligent items, in addition to other story-specific items at the GM's discretion. Bonding a specific item follows the standard procedure for upgrading or reshaping your armament.

### Ammunition
 If a soulforged weapon requires ammunition, that ammunition appears with the weapon when you Manifest it. You can choose the way it appears, such as in a magical quiver that appears on your body, or simply floating in the air where you can pluck it to load or shoot your weapon. The form doesn't change how many or what type of actions reloading takes or any other functions of the ammunition. If you want anything other than basic ammunition for your weapon (such as a sleep arrow), you must attain and carry that ammunition separately.

### Dual Weapons
 You can choose two one-handed weapons you want to wield simultaneously as your soulforged weapons. When you Manifest Soulforged Armament, both weapons appear. Because they're a pair, you must Manifest them together, having two hands free to do so, and Dismiss them simultaneously as well. The essence power applies to both weapons, so they share benefits like the new damage type and status bonus to damage from planar pain. However, you get any effects the bond grants you only once, as the dual weapons function as a single armament. For instance, if your essence power is healing grace, you can cast one heal spell when you manifest the essence form of your dual weapons, not two.

### Attached Weapons
 An attached weapon (such as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss|shield boss]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]]) brings the item it's attached to with it when manifested  or  Dismissed.  Manifest Soulforged Armament uses the item the weapon is attached to for its Requirements, so a shield boss would require you to have a hand free to hold the shield. If you have [[Feats/Soul Arsenal]], you could have a soulforged weapon attached to a soulforged shield. These items work normally for Soul Arsenal, except that you can neither Manifest nor Dismiss one without the other.
