This example _magic scroll_ contains a 1st-rank _illusory disguise_ spell.  

### Scroll Of Illusory Disguise  *Item 1*

Consumable

Illusion

Magical

Scroll**Price** 4 gp  
**Usage** held in 1 hand; **Bulk** L  
**Activate** two actions Cast a Spell  
You cast illusory disguise as a 1st-rank spell.  
**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of illusory disguise.
