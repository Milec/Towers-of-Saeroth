# Interacting to Reload  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 105

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-1230)|Gunslinger]]

---

The slinger’s reload action from each gunslinger way, as well as many feats, give the ability to [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to reload a weapon. This is enough to reload a weapon that takes 1 Interact action to reload, but only provides one of the Interact actions needed to reload a weapon with reload 2 or greater. As normal, you can provide the remaining Interact actions separately.
