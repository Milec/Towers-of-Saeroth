# Siege Weapons Vs. Normal Weapons  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 72

**Parent page** [[Rules/Siege Weapons]]

---

Many of the benefits that apply to standard weapons— [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|daggers]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe (Player Core)|battleaxes]], and the like—don’t work on siege weapons. They’re simply too different in form and function. The GM might allow using them in certain circumstances, but this is fairly rare.

Character abilities that affect their attacks might work with portable siege weapons. For example, a [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Battering Ram (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|battering ram]] might get the benefit of a character’s bonuses that apply to attacks with two-handed weapons. The GM can determine what’s reasonable and what’s not. Because siege weapons require special actions to operate, a character can’t use their special actions with them. Sorry, no [[Actions/Attack of Opportunity|Attacks of Opportunity]] with a battering ram!
