# Unobservable Stealth  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 244

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

---

In some cases, it can be impossible for a creature to fully observe you. Typically this happens if you're [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], the observer is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]], or you're in [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]] and the creature can't see in darkness. In such cases, any critical failure you roll on a check to Sneak is a failure instead. You also continue to be [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] if you lose [[Rules/Cover|cover or greater cover]] against or are no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] from such a creature.
