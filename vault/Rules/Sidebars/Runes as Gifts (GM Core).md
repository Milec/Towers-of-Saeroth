# Runes as Gifts  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 309

**Parent page** [[Rules/Relic Gifts (GM Core)|Relic Gifts]]

---

You can substitute runes for gifts. If you choose to allow property runes on the relic, you'll want them to take up rune slots. Otherwise, you can give as many or few as you prefer, just like any other gifts. Runes are sorted into minor, major, and grand categories, but you should use their normal level and Price when you adjust treasure, instead of the number and levels on the Relic Gifts table. If you're using the player-driven relics variant, it's recommended you do not include this option.

### Air

**Armor** (Major) [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant (GM Core)|energy-resistant]] or greater energy resistant (cold or electricity), [[Equipment/Invisibility (GM Core)|invisibility]]; **Armor** (Grand) [[Equipment/Winged (GM Core)|winged]]  
**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]]; **Weapon** (Major) [[Equipment/Animated|animated]], [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]], [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]]; **Weapon** (Grand) greater shock, greater thundering

### Beast

**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Fearsome (GM Core)|fearsome]], [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]]; **Weapon** (Major) greater fearsome, [[Equipment/Quickstrike|quickstrike]]

### Celestial

**Weapon** (Major) [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]]

### Death

**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]], [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]]

### Earth

**Armor** (Minor) energy-resistant or greater energy resistant (acid); **Armor** (Major) [[Equipment/Fortification (GM Core)|fortification]]; **Armor** (Grand) greater fortification  
**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|shifting]]

### Fiend

**Armor** (Minor) energy-resistant or greater energy resistant (acid, cold, or fire)  
**Weapon** (Major) [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|unholy]]

### Fire

**Armor** (Minor) energy-resistant or greater energy resistant (fire)  
**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]]; **Weapon** (Major) greater flaming

### Life

**Weapon** (Minor) [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]]; **Weapon** (Major) greater vitalizing

### Mind

**Armor** (Minor) [[Equipment/Raiment|raiment]]; **Armor** (Major) invisibility

### Plant

**Weapon** (Minor) shifting

### Shadow

**Armor** (Minor) [[Equipment/Shadow (GM Core)|shadow]]; **Armor** (Major) greater shadow;  
**Armor** (Grand) major shadow

### Water

**Armor** (Minor) energy-resistant or greater energy resistant (cold or fire), [[Equipment/Slick (GM Core)|slick]]; **Armor** (Major) greater or major slick  
**Weapon** (Minor) shifting
