# Iruxi  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 56

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Droon|Lizardfolk]]

---

While most lizardfolk permit humans and others to refer to them by that moniker, they call themselves iruxi. This ancient word arose from the [[Setting/Languages/Draconic]] tongue, though its exact etymology is unclear. In Droon, iruxi astronomers point to the jagged constellation of the Maw and the starry figure who stands against it. Today, while this constellation is called the Lone Mother and features in fables about environmental conservation, she once had another name in dragon lore: Erux, meaning “defiance.”
