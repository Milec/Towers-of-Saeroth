# Key Terms  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 8

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Dark Archive)|Psychic]]

---

You'll see the following key terms in many psychic class features.

 **[[Traits/Amp]]**: Amps are special thoughtforms that modify the properties of your psi cantrips. You can apply an amp only to a psi cantrip, which is called the amped cantrip. Applying an amp to a psi cantrip costs 1 [[Rules/Focus]] Point and is part of the actions needed to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]]. The singular focus required to amp a psi cantrip means that unless otherwise noted, you can apply only one amp to a given psi cantrip, and you can’t apply both an amp and a [[Traits/Metamagic|metamagic]] ability to a cantrip at the same time. If both an amp and the amped cantrip deal damage of the same type, combine their damage for the purpose of resistances and weaknesses. Feats with the amp trait provide different amps you can apply to psi cantrips in place of their normal amps. If an amp has its own effect, its level is the same as the amped cantrip’s.

 **Amp Heightened**: Many amps have heightened entries similar to spells. Add the amp's heightened effects when you cast the cantrip at that level or higher. These are in addition to any heightened effects of the cantrip, though some adjust the cantrip's normal heightened entry (typically in the case of damage increases).

 **[[Traits/Mindshift]]**: An action with this trait can always be altered to use the raw power of your mind. When you take this action, you can choose to replace any damage it deals with mental damage, give it the mental trait, and replace any save it requires with a Will save. The action loses any traits matching damage types it no longer deals.

 **[[Traits/Psyche]]**: Your psyche must be unleashed to use abilities that have the psyche trait, and they end automatically when your unleashed psyche subsides.
