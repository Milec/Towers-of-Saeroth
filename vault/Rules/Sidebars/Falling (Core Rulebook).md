# Falling  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 472

**Parent page** [[Actions/Grab an Edge]]

---

When you fall more than 5 feet, you take falling damage when you land, which is bludgeoning damage equal to half the distance you fell. If you take any damage from a fall, you’re knocked prone when you land. You can Grab an Edge as a reaction (page 418) to reduce or eliminate the damage from some falls. More detailed rules for falling damage appear on page 421.
