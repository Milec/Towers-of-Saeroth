# Specific Armor, Weapons, and Shields  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 225

**Parent page** [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|Property Runes]]

---

Unlike armor, weapons, and shields enhanced with runes, specific armor, weapons, and shields (such as [[Equipment/Moonlit Chain (GM Core)|moonlit chain]], a [[Equipment/Bloodletting Kukri (GM Core)|bloodletting kukri]], or a [[Equipment/Lion's Shield (GM Core)|lion's shield]]) are created for a specific purpose and can work quite differently from other items of their type. These items can’t gain property runes, but you can add or improve their fundamental runes.
