# Strike Statistics  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 418

**Parent page** [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]

---

See [[Rules/Attack Rolls (rules-2288)|Attack Rolls]] and [[Rules/Damage Rolls (rules-2301)|Damage]] for details on calculating your attack and damage rolls. The damage roll for a Strike uses the damage die of the weapon or unarmed attack, plus any modifiers, bonuses, and penalties you have to damage. If you're using a type of attack other than a Strike, such as a spell attack or [[Actions/Grapple]] action, you calculate damage differently (or not at all).
