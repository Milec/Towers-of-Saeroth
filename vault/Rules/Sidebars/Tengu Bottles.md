# Tengu Bottles  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 24

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Tengu Settlements|Tengu]]

---

Tengu tend to have a healthy respect for gods that rule over storms and air—or at least, a healthy fear. One ancestral custom that has caught on among other sailors is the tengu bottle: a decorated gourd or other container filled with an offering for the gods, kept on ships and thrown overboard in the event of dire weather. Tengu bottles most often contain fine alcohol to appease the fury of [[Setting/Deities/Hei Feng]] or [[Setting/Deities/Besmara]]; crew puncture the bottles to release these offerings before throwing them into the waters.
