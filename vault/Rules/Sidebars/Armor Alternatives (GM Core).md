# Armor Alternatives  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 228

**Parent page** [[Rules/Armor (GM Core)|Armor]]

---

If you don't want to wear armor, or you're trained in only unarmored defense, you can wear either [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer's clothing]] or _[[Equipment/Bands of Force|bands of force]]_. Explorer's clothing can be etched with runes just like armor can, so it can provide item bonuses to AC or saves. _Bands of force_ give a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dex modifier cap of +5, and they also grant a bonus to saves.
