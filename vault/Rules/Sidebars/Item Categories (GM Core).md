# Item Categories  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 219

**Parent page** [[Rules/Chapter 5- Treasure Trove|Chapter 5: Treasure Trove]]

---

Items are grouped into the following categories, shown here with a brief description of the category.  

- **[[Rules/Armor & Armaments]]** includes rules for etching runes to enhance weapons and armor, armor runes, specific suits of armor, shields and the reinforcing rune, weapon runes, and specific weapons.
- **[[Rules/Alchemy]]** items are powered by the reactions of alchemical reagents. Almost all alchemical items are consumable items that are used up when you activate them. This category includes bombs, elixirs, poisons, and alchemical tools.
- **[[Rules/Materials (GM Core)|Materials]]** includes the statistics for standard items and structures along with rules for precious materials that can be used to make items with unique properties and other advantages.
- **[[Rules/Momentary Magic (GM Core)|Momentary Magic]]** has a plethora of magical consumables, including ammunition, oils applied to the surface of objects and people, potions to drink, scrolls used to cast more spells, talismans affixed to items, and wondrous consumables of all sorts.
- **[[Rules/Trappings of Power (GM Core)|Trappings of Power]]** are permanent and powerful. They include apex items that can increase an attribute modifier, companion items for pets, held items, magical staves for spellcasters, wands that can repeatedly cast more spells, and a vast collection of worn items.
- **[[Rules/Gems & Art Objects]]** make for an interesting variety of treasures.
- **[[Rules/Artifacts (GM Core)|Artifacts]]** are extraordinarily powerful magic items ideal to build adventures and plots around.
- **[[Rules/Intelligent Items (GM Core)|Intelligent Items]]** provides options for conscious items.
- **[[Rules/Cursed Items (GM Core)|Cursed Items]]**, used sparingly, can add danger and fun to items.
- **[[Rules/Relics (GM Core)|Relics]]** let you build a special item that unlocks new powers over time through the story of a campaign.
