# The Lady of Foxes  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 120

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Fox Families|Kitsune]]

---

Though usually referred to with she/her pronouns, the goddess [[Setting/Deities/Daikitsu|Daikitsu's]] nature is fluid; her gender, age, and appearance change at her whim. Kitsune's legends state that they were created when Daikitsu granted her original attendants, fox spirits known as the Nine Bright Grains, one of her own abilities: the power to dance between shapes.
