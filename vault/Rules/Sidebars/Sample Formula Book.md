# Sample Formula Book  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 56

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|Alchemist]]

---

You can choose any 1st-level common alchemical items for your formula book, but the list below contains a good selection of starter formulas. A beginner's book with these formulas called _The Fundamentals of Alchemy_ has become popular among mainstream alchemists by staying away from controversial topics like mutagens, though it has drawn criticism from fringe alchemists for guiding fledgling alchemists away from more innovative experiments. **Alchemical Item Formulas**: [Alchemist's fire](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2876), [[Equipment/Frost Vial|frost vial]], [cheetah's elixir](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2884), [elixir of life](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2887), [glue bomb](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2878), [smoke ball](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2913); **Bomber** [Acid flask](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2875), [[Equipment/Bottled Lightning|bottled lightning]]; **Chirurgeon** [Antidote](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2879), [antiplague](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2880); **Mutagenist** [[Equipment/Bestial Mutagen|Bestial mutagen]], [[Equipment/Cognitive Mutagen|cognitive mutagen]]; **Toxicologist** [Arsenic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2891), [giant centipede venom](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2898)
