# Forced Movement  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 241

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]

---

Reposition and Shove force a creature to move. When an effect forces you to move, or if you start falling, the distance you move is defined by the effect that moved you, not by your Speed. Because you’re not acting to move, this doesn’t trigger reactions triggered by movement. See page 422 for full details.
