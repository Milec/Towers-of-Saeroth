# Dragonet Adventurers  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 208

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Tiny PCs (Draconic Codex)|Dragonet]]

---

Dragonets’ reasons for adventuring are as varied as they are. Some set out to defend their home, while others have an insatiable curiosity about new places or interesting people. Some seek treasure, and others might have bonded with a spellcaster, only to strike out on their own when the spellcaster retired or died.

Even in cities, many dragonets tend to live somewhat apart from society and have solitary backgrounds, such as a [[Backgrounds/Charlatan (Player Core)|charlatan]], [[Backgrounds/Courier (Player Core 2)|courier]], [[Backgrounds/Criminal (Player Core)|criminal]], [[Backgrounds/Scholar (Player Core)|scholar]], [[Backgrounds/Street Urchin (Player Core)|street urchin]], or [[Backgrounds/Tinker (Player Core)|tinkerer]]. Those who live outside cities may have grown up a [[Backgrounds/Hermit (Player Core)|hermit]], [[Backgrounds/Hunter (Player Core)|hunter]], [[Backgrounds/Nomad (Player Core)|nomad]], or [[Backgrounds/Scavenger (Player Core 2)|scavenger]]. Dragonets often rely on their own natural abilities, instead of equipment, and tend to be [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|rangers]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|rogues]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-1002)|swashbucklers]]. Those more magically inclined are likely [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]] or [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerers]], though many house drakes become [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|wizards]]. Especially curious dragonets find success as [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|investigators]].
