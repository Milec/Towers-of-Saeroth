# What Counts as Megafauna?  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 78

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/What Counts as Megafauna|Mammoth Lord]]

---

The megafauna of the Realm of the Mammoth Lords are enormous beasts of intimidating appearance, but they can be hard to otherwise classify. While the most well-known megafauna are mammals—like the mammoth or the woolly rhinoceros—not all are. Large dinosaurs like the stegosaurus and triceratops qualify as megafauna, for example, but smaller dinosaurs like raptors don't. A list of well-established megafauna can be found below, but it isn't an exclusive list; other, more unusual, species might be permitted with GM approval. One rule is certain, however: an adult megafauna is always Large or bigger.

- [[Bestiary/Cave Bear|**Cave Bear**]]: Use a [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Bully|bear's]] statistics for a cave bear animal companion.
- [[Bestiary/Daeodon|**Daeodon**]]: Use a [boar's](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=19) statistics for a daeodon, or giant boar, companion.
- [[Bestiary/Dire Wolf|**Dire Wolf**]]: Use a [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|wolf's]] statistics for a dire wolf companion.
- [[Bestiary/Mammoth|**Mammoth**]]: Use a [elephants's](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=34) statistics for the statistics for an elephant or woolly mammoth animal companion.
- [[Bestiary/Roc|**Roc**]]: Use [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Daredevil|bird]] statistics for a young roc.
- [[Bestiary/Smilodon|**Smilodon**]]: Use [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Racer|cat]] statistics for a smilodon animal companion.
- [[Bestiary/Triceratops|**Triceratops**]]: Use [triceratops](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=36) statistics for a triceratops or other armored dinosaur, such as a stegosaurus or ankylosaurus.
- [[Bestiary/Tyrannosaurus|**Tyrannosaurus**]]: Use [tyrannosaurus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=37) statistics for for a tyrannosaurus, allosaurus, or similar dinosaur.
- [[Bestiary/Rhinoceros|**Woolly Rhinoceros**]]: Use [rhinoceros](https://2e.aonprd.com/Companions.aspx?ID=35) statistics for a rhinoceros or woolly rhinoceros animal companion.
