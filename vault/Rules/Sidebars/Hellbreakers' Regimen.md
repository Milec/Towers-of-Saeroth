# Hellbreakers' Regimen  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 216

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Hellbreakers' Regimen|Hellbreaker]]

---

Hellbreakers work around the clock, as they know their opposition won't be taking a moment off either. Training is vigorous and frequent, constantly testing Hellbreakers' abilities and almost always bringing them to the brink of exhaustion. That being said, discipline in a Hellbreaker station is certainly less painful than in their Chelaxian counterpart's.
