# Runes as Gifts  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 96

**Parent page** [[Rules/Relic Gifts]]

---

You can substitute runes for gifts. If you choose to allow property runes on the relic, you'll want them to take up rune slots. Otherwise, you can give as many or few as you prefer, just like any other gifts. Runes are sorted into minor, major, and grand categories, but you should use their normal level and Price when you adjust treasure, instead of the number and levels on the [[Rules/Advancing a Relic|Table 2–22]]. If you're using the player-driven relics variant, it's recommended you do not include this option.

### Air

 **Armor (Major)** _[[Equipment/Energy-Resistant|energy-resistant]]_ or _greater energy-resistant_ (cold or electricity), _[[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]]_; **Armor (Grand)** _[[Equipment/Ethereal|ethereal]]_  
 **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Returning|returning]]_; **Weapon (Major)** _[[Equipment/Dancing|dancing]]_, _[[Equipment/Shock|shock]]_, _[[Equipment/Thundering|thundering]]_; **Weapon (Grand)** _greater shock_, _greater thundering_

### Celestial

 **Weapon (Major)** _[[Equipment/Anarchic|anarchic]]_ (azata-themed), _[[Equipment/Axiomatic|axiomatic]]_ (archon-themed), _[[Equipment/Holy|holy]]_

### Death

 **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]]_, _[[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]]_

### Earth

 **Armor (Minor)** _energy-resistant_ or _greater energy-resistant_ (acid); **Armor (Major)** _[[Equipment/Fortification|fortification]]_; **Armor (Grand)** _greater fortification_  
 **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Shifting|shifting]]_

### Fiend

 **Armor (Minor)** _energy-resistant_ or _greater energy-resistant_ (acid, cold, or fire)  
 **Weapon (Major)** _anarchic_ (demon-themed), _axiomatic_ (devil-themed), _[[Equipment/Unholy|unholy]]_

### Fire

 **Armor (Minor)** _energy-resistant_ or _greater energy-resistant_ (fire)  
 **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]]_; **Weapon (Major)** _greater flaming_

### Life

 **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Disrupting|disrupting]]_; **Weapon (Major)** _greater disrupting_

### Mind

 **Armor (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Glamered|glamered]]_; **Armor (Major)** _invisibility_

### Plant

 **Weapon (Minor)** _shifting_

### Shadow

 **Armor (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Shadow|shadow]]_; **Armor (Major)** _greater shadow_; **Armor (Grand)** _ethereal_, _major shadow_

### Water

 **Armor (Minor)** _energy-resistant_ or _greater energy-resistant_ (cold or fire), _slick_; **Armor (Major)** _greater or major slick_  
 **Weapon (Minor)** _shifting_

### Metal

**Armor (Major)** _[[Equipment/Fortification|fortification]]</i_, [[Equipment/Malleable|malleable]]_; **Armor (Major)** _[[Equipment/Fortification|greater fortification]]_; **Weapon (Minor)** _[[Equipment/Shifting|shifting]]_; **Weapon (Major)** _[[Equipment/Extending|extending]]_ or _greater extending_, _[[Equipment/Serrating|serrating]]_
