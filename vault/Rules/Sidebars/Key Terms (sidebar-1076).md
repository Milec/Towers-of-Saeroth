# Key Terms  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 94

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|Bard]]

---

You'll see the following key terms in many bard class features.

 **[[Traits/Composition]]**: To cast a composition cantrip or focus spell, you use a type of [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]. If the spell includes a verbal component, you must use an [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] performance, and if it includes a somatic component, you must use a [[Traits/Visual|visual]] one. The spell gains all the traits of the performance you used. You can cast only one composition spell each turn, and you can have only one active at a time. If you cast a new composition spell, any ongoing effects from your previous composition spell end immediately.

 **[[Traits/Metamagic]]**: Actions with the metamagic trait tweak the properties of your spells. These actions usually come from metamagic feats. You must use a metamagic action directly before Casting the Spell you want to alter. If you use any action (including free actions and reactions) other than Cast a Spell directly after, you waste the benefits of the metamagic action. Any additional effects added by a metamagic action are part of the spell's effect, not of the metamagic action itself.
