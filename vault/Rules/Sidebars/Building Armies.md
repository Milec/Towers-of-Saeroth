# Building Armies  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 539

**Parent page** [[Rules/Activity Phase]]

---

Building and maintaining armies dovetails with the Kingdom turn, but these rules can be largely skipped over during times of peace. During wars, a fourth step—Army Activities—occurs at the end of the Activity phase. Rules for armies and warfare are in [[Rules/Appendix 3- Warfare|Appendix 3]]
