# Family Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 104

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Oread Settlements|Oread]]

---

Most oreads are descended from [[Bestiary/Shaitan|shaitans]], genies native to the Plane of Earth, but other planar creatures, especially other [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Earth|earth elementals]], can also have oread descendants. The children of [[Bestiary/Earth Mephit|earth]] and mud mephits are typically born smaller than their mortal parent, with modest vestigial wigs or nubs in their backs where a mephit's wings would have been. When a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Crystal|dragon, crystal]] dragon bears oread offspring, they almost always have the [[Feats/Gemsoul|gemsoul]] lineage in addition to draconic features like wings or a tail. [[Bestiary/Xorn|Xorns]] are the least common elemental ancestors for an oread, as they seldom have relationships with mortals.
