# Damaging Armor  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 274

**Parent page** [[Rules/Armor Statistics]]

---

Your armor’s statistics are based on the material it’s predominantly made from. It’s not likely your armor will take damage, as explained in Item Damage on page 272.

## Damaging Objects

| **Material** | **Hardness** | **HP** | **BT** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cloth (explorer’s clothing, padded armor) | 1 | 4 | 2 |
| Leather (hide, leather, studded leather) | 4 | 16 | 8 |
| Metal (breastplate, chain mail, chain shirt, full plate, half plate, scale mail, splint mail) | 9 | 36 | 18 |
