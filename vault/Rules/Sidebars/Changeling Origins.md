# Changeling Origins  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 30

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Changeling Relationships|Changeling]]

---

Changelings' rarity typically prevents them from gathering in numbers. While changelings can be found anywhere hags lurk, they're most common by far in Ustalav, Irrisen, and neighboring regions. The daughters of [[Bestiary/Night Hag|night hags]] and moon hags can arise far from any known hag activity thanks to their mothers' extraplanar origins, as can the spontaneously arising sorrow may.
