# Armor Alternatives  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 555

**Parent page** [[Rules/Armor (Core Rulebook)|Armor]]

---

If you don't want to wear armor, or you're trained in only unarmored defense, you can wear either [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing|explorer's clothing]] or [[Equipment/Bracers of Armor|_bracers of armor_]]. Explorer's clothing can be etched with runes just like armor can, so it can provide item bonuses to AC or saves.

 _Bracers of armor_ give a +1 item bonus to AC with a Dex modifier cap of +5, and they also grant a bonus to saves.
