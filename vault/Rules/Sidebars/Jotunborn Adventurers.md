# Jotunborn Adventurers  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 10

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Jotunborn Adventurers|Jotunborn]]

---

The brawny frames of jotunborn make them natural [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-841)|barbarians]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|champion]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-688)|fighters]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-1111)|guardians]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-926)|monks]]. Many of these jotunborn take on roles as caravan defenders or local guards. Others become [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|rangers]], especially those that prefer to maintain their nomadic lifestyle or those that have found their bonded site. While jotunborn are no more or less capable of spellcasting than others, most jotunborn tend to favor the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] tradition, with occult [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerers]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (class-feature-443)|summoners]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|witches]] being most prevalent in jotunborn societies.
