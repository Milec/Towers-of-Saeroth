# Alchemical and Magical Items  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 237

**Parent page** [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]]

---

If you want to Craft alchemical items or magic items, you need to select the skill feat for [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] or [[Feats/Magical Crafting]] in addition to being trained. Stat blocks and details of these items appear in _GM Core_, so consult with your GM.
