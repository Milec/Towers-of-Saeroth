# Family Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 116

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Undine Settlements|Undine]]

---

Most undines descend from [[Bestiary/Marid|marids]]— the water genies native to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water]]—and the mortals they take for lovers. These mercurial genies usually show their favor to musicians, dancers, and other artists, inviting mortals of exceptional talents to live with them. Other planar creatures occasionally have undine progeny as well. The children of [[Bestiary/Water Mephit|water]], [[Bestiary/Ice Mephit|ice]], and salt mephits are often born with small statures, nub-like horns, hooked or beak-like faces, or vestigial wings and tails. Those related to [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Brine|brine dragons]] are sometimes born with draconic wings and tails and almost always have the [[Feats/Brinesoul|brinesoul]] lineage.
