# Disabling Siege Weapons  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 72

**Parent page** [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Siege Weapons]]

---

[[Actions/Disable a Device]] can be used to deactivate most siege weapons that have complex moving parts. This typically doesn't require [[Equipment/Thieves' Tools|thieves' tools]], takes one success, and uses the standard DC for the item level of the siege weapon.
