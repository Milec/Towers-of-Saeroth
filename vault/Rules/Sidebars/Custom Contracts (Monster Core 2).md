# Custom Contracts  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 98

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Custom Contracts (Monster Core 2)|Hellbound Creatures]]

---

You can modify the abilities in the Hellbound creature template to suit the devil granting them or the specific agreement. For example, a mortal seeking allure might gain [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] rather than [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Spells/Charm (Player Core)|_charm_]] rather than [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]].
