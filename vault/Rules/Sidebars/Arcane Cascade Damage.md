# Arcane Cascade Damage  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

---

Feats like [[Feats/Burning Cascade]], [[Feats/Devastating Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Devastating Spellstrike]], and [[Feats/Distracting Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Distracting Spellstrike]] have values dependent on your Arcane Cascade damage. Note that abilities that can increase the extra damage, such as the [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Laughing Shadow (Impossible Magic)|laughing shadow]] and [[Classes/Subclasses/Hybrid Studies/Twofold Tine|twofold tine]] hybrid studies, apply only to the Strikes that meet their criteria, not to other abilities. This means feats like the listed ones use the base damage value, not the boosted one!
