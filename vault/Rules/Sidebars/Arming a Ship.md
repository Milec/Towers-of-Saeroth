# Arming a Ship  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 35

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sailing Combat]]

---

A sailing ship unloading a burst of cannon fire on their enemy is one of the most popular images of naval warfare. Though most ship stat blocks don’t include shipboard weapons by default, if PCs are preparing to have naval battles or are joining a ship that’s already armed, you might want to add [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|siege weapons]] to the ship. The most common siege weapons to mount on a ship are [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Ballista (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|ballistae]] (level 5) and [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Heavy Ballista (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|heavy ballistae]] (level 8). If the navy or ship’s crew has access to black powder, these typically get supplanted by [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|cannons]] (level 8) or [[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Hwacha (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|hwachas]] (level 4). It’s usually best to keep the weapons close to the PCs’ level. Ranged weapons, especially cannons, are often mounted below the main deck and shoot out of hatches, so each one can shoot from only one side of the ship. The most common placement is a row of siege weapons on the port and starboard sides of the ship to make broadsides with a large number of weapons at once, but chase guns at the bow of the ship can be useful in pursuits.
