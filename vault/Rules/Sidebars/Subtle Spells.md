# Subtle Spells  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 300

**Parent page** [[Rules/Ranges, Areas, and Targets (Player Core)|Ranges, Areas, and Targets]]

---

A spell with the subtle trait can be cast without incantations and doesn't have obvious manifestations. Most of these spells enhance your subterfuge or stealth, such as invisibility. Some abilities, like the [[Feats/Conceal Spell (Player Core)|Conceal Spell]] feat, allow you to make spells subtle even if they wouldn't normally be.
