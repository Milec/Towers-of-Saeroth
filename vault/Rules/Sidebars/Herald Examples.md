# Herald Examples  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 131

**Parent page** [[Spells/Summon Deific Herald]]

---

Each of the many deities of Golarion have their own herald; these are just a few of the most prominent.

- **Lawful Good**: The Grand Defender ([[Setting/Deities/Torag]]), the Grim White Stag ([[Setting/Deities/Erastil]]), Hand of the Inheritor ([[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]])
- **Neutral Good**: The Spirit of Adoration ([[Setting/Deities/Shelyn]]), Sunlord Thalachos ([[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae]])
- **Chaotic Good**: Night Monarch ([[Setting/Deities/Desna]]), Thais ([[Setting/Deities/Cayden Cailean]])
- **Lawful Neutral**: Lawgiver ([[Setting/Deities/Abadar]]), The Old Man ([[Setting/Deities/Irori]])
- **Neutral**: Arcanotheign ([[Setting/Deities/Nethys]]), Personification of Fury ([[Setting/Deities/Gozreh]]), Steward of the Skein ([[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]])
- **Chaotic Neutral**: The First Blade ([[Setting/Deities/Gorum]]), the Menotherian ([[Setting/Deities/Calistria]])
- **Lawful Evil**: Basileus ([[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus]]), the Prince in Chains ([[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon]])
- **Neutral Evil**: Mother's Maw ([[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]]), [[Bestiary/The Stabbing Beast|the Stabbing Beast]] ([[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]])
- **Chaotic Evil**: Tarrasque ([[Setting/Deities/Rovagug]]), Yethazmari ([[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu]])
