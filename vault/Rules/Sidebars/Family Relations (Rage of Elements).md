# Family Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 50

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Talos Settlements|Talos]]

---

Most taloses descend from [[Bestiary/Families/Genie|zuhras]], the genies native to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]], but there are other possibilities as well. [[Feats/Gildedsoul]] taloses may be distantly descended from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Sky|sky dragon]], lending scaled patterns to their skin and fangs to their mouths. On the other hand, a [[Feats/Quicksoul|quicksoul]] talos may not be descended from any elemental at all, and instead result from their parent's extended exposure to metal-based alchemical fumes during pregnancy.
