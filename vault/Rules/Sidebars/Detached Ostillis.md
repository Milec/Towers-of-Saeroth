# Detached Ostillis  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 70

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Detached Ostillis|Ostilli Host]]

---

If your ostilli is ever removed forcibly from your body (which requires a successful [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check against a hard DC of your level and 30 minutes of delicate surgery), you are [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] and can’t use any of your ostilli’s actions until your ostilli is reattached. This requires you to hold your ostilli to the place from which it was detached for an uninterrupted hour. If the ostilli isn’t reattached within 24 hours, it dies.
