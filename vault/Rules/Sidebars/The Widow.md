# The Widow  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 82

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Fetchling Settlements|Fetchling]]

---

The Widow was one of the Forsaken— sinister demigods who once watched over the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]]. It heard an Azlanti call for rescue, lifted its shivering sickle, and tore a passage into darkness; those it ushered into the Shadow Plane became the first fetchlings. Few remember anything beyond the Widow's name, and fewer still offer it prayers. It grants power to no fetchling priests.
