# The Outer Sphere  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 130

**Parent page** [[Spells/Spiritual Attunement]]

---

The nine main planes of the Outer Sphere are the homes of many deities, [[Traits/Celestial|celestials]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitors]], and [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]]. Their names, along with their alignments, are: [[Setting/Planes/Heaven]] (LG), [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana]] (NG), [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]] (CG), [[Setting/Planes/Axis]] (LN), the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] (N), the [[Rules/Sidebars/Galisemni|Maelstrom]] (CN), [[Setting/Planes/Hell]] (LE), [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon]] (NE), and the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]] (CE).
