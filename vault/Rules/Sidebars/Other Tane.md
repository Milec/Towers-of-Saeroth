# Other Tane  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 2]] pg. 310

**Parent page** [[Traits/Tane]]

---

Originally engineered by the Eldest as living weapons, the Tane are a group of mythical creatures whose might is capable of bringing entire cities to heel. The [[Bestiary/Jabberwock|jabberwock]] and the [[Bestiary/Sard|sard]] are two other examples of the Tane in this book, but others include creatures like the bandersnatch, the jubjub bird, and arguably the most powerful of them all, the unique monster known as Leviathan.
