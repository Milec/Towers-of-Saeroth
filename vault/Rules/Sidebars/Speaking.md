# Speaking  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 469

**Parent page** [[Rules/Basic Actions]]

---

As long as you can act, you can also speak. You don’t need to spend any type of action to speak, but because a round represents 6 seconds of time, you can usually speak at most a single sentence or so per round. Special uses of speech, such as attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] skill check to [[Actions/Lie]], require spending actions and follow their own rules. All speech has the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait. If you communicate in some way other than speech, other rules might apply. For instance, using sign language is [[Traits/Visual|visual]] instead of auditory.
