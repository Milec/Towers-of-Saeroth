# Strike Statistics  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 471

**Parent page** [[Actions/Strike]]

---

See Attack Rolls on page 402 and Damage on page 404 for details on calculating your attack and damage rolls. The damage roll for a Strike uses the damage die of the weapon or unarmed attack, plus any modifiers, bonuses, and penalties you have to damage. If you’re using a type of attack other than a Strike, such as a spell attack or Grapple action, you calculate damage differently (or not at all).
