# Kayal  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 82

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Fetchling Settlements|Fetchling]]

---

Many fetchlings refer to themselves as kayals, an [[Setting/Languages/Aklo]]-derived word with a meaning akin to “shadow people.” Most kayals reject the term fetchling, which they see as imposed on them by individuals bound too closely to the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]]. The word has uncertain origins, but its use is widespread in Nidal. To kayals in Nidal, their name grounds and uplifts them as a worthy people forging their own paths, not as servitors of darkness. Skeptics consider kayal to be a superficial affectation.
