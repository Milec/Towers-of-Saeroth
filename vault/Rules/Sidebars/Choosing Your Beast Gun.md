# Choosing Your Beast Gun  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 130

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing Your Beast Gun|Beast Gunner]]

---

A beast gunner's bond with their beast gun is deep and mystical, so if you hope to be a successful beast gunner, it's important to choose a beast gun that suits your temperament, magic, and combat style. For example, if you favor strength and intimidation, a [[Equipment/Screech Shooter (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|_screech shooter_]] is the right choice for you, whereas a [[Equipment/Spider Gun (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|_spider gun_]] might be optimal if you prefer a more careful style of setting up the battlefield in advance. Similarly, a [[Equipment/Tentacle Cannon (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|_tentacle cannon_]] is slow and methodical and can hold an enemy in place at close range, while a [[Equipment/Drake Rifle (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|_drake rifle_]] is energetic and mobile and can be used to attack from a great distance. Each beast gun has a personality and magical abilities that attract people who value them. For this reason, some experienced beast gunners say the weapon chooses you.
