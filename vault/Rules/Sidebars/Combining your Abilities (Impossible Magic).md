# Combining your Abilities  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

---

As a magus, you have multiple special actions and abilities that can be used in combination with your spells.

 **Arcane Cascade:** Sometimes it's in your best interest to use Arcane Cascade before you Spellstrike at the start of combat, possibly casting a buff spell in the same round, rather than going for a Spellstrike right away. You can usually stay in Arcane Cascade for a long time, though if you find out an enemy has a weakness to a certain damage type, such as fire, you might want to refresh your stance after a fire spell to take advantage of the bonus damage.

 **Conflux Spells:** It's often worth it to cast a conflux spell and make a Strike either on a turn where you can't make a Spellstrike, or as the last action on your turn after a Spellstrike. Even if you think its Strike will miss, it might be worth it to cast a conflux spell if the spell does something else useful for you and recharges your Spellstrike for your next turn.

 **Spellstrike:** Since a lot can ride on your Spellstrike, which uses your multiple attack penalty, it's much better if used as the first attack of the turn! Because spells with DCs still require the target to save if Spellstrike misses, it can be better to use a spell with a DC against a more powerful enemy you're less likely to hit.
