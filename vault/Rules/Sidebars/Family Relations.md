# Family Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 100

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Ifrit Settlements|Ifrit]]

---

The most common elemental ancestors for ifrits are [[Bestiary/Efreeti|efreet]], the fire genies native to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire]], but they have many possible otherworldly progenitors. [[Bestiary/Salamander]]-descended ifrits are usually born with tails and forked tongues. When [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Magma|magma dragon]] mate with mortals, their children are sometimes born as ifrits with draconic tails and vestigial wings.
