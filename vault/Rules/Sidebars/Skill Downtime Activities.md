# Skill Downtime Activities  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 480

**Parent page** [[Rules/Rest and Daily Preparation]]

---

Chapter 4: Skills includes several downtime activities, which are summarized here.

**Craft**: Using the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, you can create items from raw materials.

**Create Forgery**: You forge a document (page 251).

**Earn Income**: You earn money, typically using Crafting, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] (page 236).

**Subsist**: You find food and shelter in the wilderness or within a settlement (page 240).

**Treat Disease**: You spend time caring for a diseased creature in the hope of curing that creature.
