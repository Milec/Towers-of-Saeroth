# Family Relations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 112

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Sylph Settlements|Sylph]]

---

[[Bestiary/Djinni|Djinn]], air genies native to the Plane of Air, are the most common elemental ancestor for sylphs, but they are not the only possibility. [[Bestiary/Invisible Stalker|Invisible stalkers]] look down on corporeal creatures, but occasionally they take mortal lovers, especially illusionists who share their appreciation for the unseen. [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud|Cloud dragons]] sometimes have sylph descendants, born with draconic features like tails or horns.
