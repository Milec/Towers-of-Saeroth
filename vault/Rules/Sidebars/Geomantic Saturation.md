# Geomantic Saturation  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 210

**Parent page** [[Rules/Geomancy]]

---

Areas of geomantic saturation are rare phenomena wherein either elemental matter or life essence strongly saturate a site or location, granting a unique magical effect. For example, the Crystal Womb deep underground is saturated with earth elemental energy. To represent a place of geomantic saturation, the rules for magical terrain in [[Rules/Pervasive Magic]] are a good starting point. To a geomancer, these areas also represent a potential source of power and learning. Tapping into or attuning with a saturated area might present a challenge for the geomancer, potentially requiring [[Rules/Research|research]] or a ritual similar to establish [[Rules/Ley Lines|leyline]]. Successfully attuning to the areas might offer rare spells, feats, or other options for a geomancer to access or even a unique option available only by attuning with that specific saturation.
