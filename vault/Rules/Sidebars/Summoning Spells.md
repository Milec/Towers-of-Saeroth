# Summoning Spells  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 51

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (class-feature-443)|Summoner]]

---

When the [[Feats/Master Summoner]], [[Feats/Ostentatious Arrival]], and [[Feats/Legendary Summoner]] feats refer to summoning spells, they mean spells that conjure a creature with the summoned trait. The spells that qualify from the _Core Rulebook_ and this book are [[Spells/Summon Anarch|_summon anarch_]], [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]], [[Spells/Summon Axiom|_summon axiom_]], [[Spells/Summon Celestial (Player Core)|_summon celestial_]], [[Spells/Summon Construct (Player Core)|_summon construct_]], [[Spells/Summon Dragon (Player Core)|_summon dragon_]], [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]], [[Spells/Summon Entity|_summon entity_]], [[Spells/Summon Fey|_summon fey_]], [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]], [[Spells/Summon Giant (Player Core)|_summon giant_]], [[Spells/Summon Lesser Servitor|_summon lesser servitor_]], and [[Spells/Summon Plant or Fungus|_summon plant or fungus_]]. Your GM might add other spells from future books that work like these spells.
