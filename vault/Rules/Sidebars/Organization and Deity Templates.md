# Organization and Deity Templates  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 203

**Parent page** [[Rules/Customizing NPCs]]

---

In addition to the templates in this book, the _Pathfinder Lost Omens Character Guide_ presents theme templates to quickly and easily make these NPCs members of various organizations. If you're looking for adherents of a specific deity, _Pathfinder Lost Omens Gods & Magic_ offers a theme template that allows you to customize an NPC to fit any deity, or a deity of your own creation!
