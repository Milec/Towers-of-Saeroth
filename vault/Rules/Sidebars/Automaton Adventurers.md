# Automaton Adventurers  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 36

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Enhancements|Automaton]]

---

Many who became automatons had some role in society prior to their transformation and often gravitate toward classes that best represent these roles, even if they no longer remember their former life as more than subconscious flashes. Hunter, sharpshooter, and warrior automatons usually become [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-688)|fighters]], [[Classes/Class Features/Attribute Boosts (class-feature-712)|rangers]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|rogues]]. Spell casting classes, such as [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bards]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|witches]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|wizards]] are common among mage automatons. While an automaton can have any background, the pool of individuals chosen to become automatons were typically of the [[Backgrounds/Acolyte (Player Core)|acolyte]], [[Backgrounds/Bounty Hunter (Player Core)|bounty hunter]], [[Backgrounds/Emissary (Player Core)|emissary]], [[Backgrounds/Gladiator (Player Core)|gladiator]], [[Backgrounds/Guard (Player Core)|guard]], [[Backgrounds/Herbalist (Player Core)|herbalist]], [[Backgrounds/Hunter (Player Core)|hunter]], [[Backgrounds/Martial Disciple (Player Core)|martial disciple]], [[Backgrounds/Scholar (Player Core)|scholar]], [[Backgrounds/Scout (Player Core)|scout]], or [[Backgrounds/Warrior (Player Core)|warrior]] background.
