# Milani's Edicts and Anathema  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 84

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Milani's Edicts and Anathema|Rose Warden]]

---

The Everbloom's faith concerns hope and those who war against oppression. Her edicts and anathema are printed below for convenience.

**Edicts** confront oppression in all its forms, defend the common folk, overcome despair to seize victory

 **Anathema** abandon those in need, enslave or oppress others, harm the innocent through direct or inadvertent action

 Edicts and anathema are intended as tools for roleplay. You, the GM, and other players should work together to determine how they'll influence your game.
