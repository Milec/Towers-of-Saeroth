# Other Focus Spells  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 66

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-335)|Oracle]]

---

You might gain focus spells that aren't revelation spells and don't have the [[Traits/Cursebound|cursebound]] trait (by taking an archetype, for instance). Since these spells aren't drawing on the same divine mystery as your revelation spells, casting them doesn't increase the effects of your curse. They still cost Focus Points as normal, and you still can't increase your focus pool to hold more than 3 Focus Points.
