# Kinetic Specialists  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 13

**Parent page** [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Rage of Elements)|Kineticist]]

---

Kineticists who specialize in a single element are often referred to by a special title.

- **Air**: Aerokineticist
- **Earth**: Geokineticist
- **Fire**: Pyrokineticist
- **Metal**: Metallokineticist
- **Water**: Hydrokineticist
- **Wood**: Phytokineticist
