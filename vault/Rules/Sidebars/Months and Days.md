# Months and Days  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 417

**Parent page** [[Rules/Chapter 8- The Age of Lost Omens (Core Rulebook)|Chapter 8: The Age of Lost Omens]]

---

The names of the months of the year are as follows in the Inner Sea region:  
&emsp; Abadius ( January)  
&emsp; Calistril (February)  
&emsp; Pharast (March)  
&emsp; Gozran (April)  
&emsp; Desnus (May)  
&emsp; Sarenith ( June)  
&emsp; Erastus ( July)  
&emsp; Arodus (August)  
&emsp; Rova (September)  
&emsp; Lamashan (October)  
&emsp; Neth (November)  
&emsp; Kuthona (December)

 The names of the days are as follows:  
&emsp; Moonday (Monday)  
&emsp; Toilday (Tuesday)  
&emsp; Wealday (Wednesday)  
&emsp; Oathday (Thursday)  
&emsp; Fireday (Friday)  
&emsp; Starday (Saturday)  
&emsp; Sunday (Sunday)
