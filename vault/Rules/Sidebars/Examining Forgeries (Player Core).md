# Examining Forgeries  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 244

**Parent page** [[Actions/Create Forgery (Player Core)|Create Forgery]]

---

A creature on the lookout for forgeries, even one who was fooled on a passive glance, can take time to closely examine a document to see if it's a forgery. They apply different techniques and analysis methods to look beyond the surface elements and attempt a secret Perception or [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] check against the forger's Society DC; any bonus you had to create the forgery initially applies to this DC. On a success, the examiner knows the document is a forgery. On a failure, they think the document is genuine and can't try again unless they get a new reason to be suspicious of the document. If a PC examines a genuine document, the GM might still pretend to roll a secret check before revealing the document is genuine.
