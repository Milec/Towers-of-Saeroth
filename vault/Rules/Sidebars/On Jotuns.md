# On Jotuns  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 10

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Jotunborn Adventurers|Jotunborn]]

---

The origins of the term “jotun” is generally unclear to nongiants. In the tongue of [[Bestiary/Families/Giant (Monster Core)|giants]], jotun refers to any giant progenitors. Specifically, this refers to [[Bestiary/Families/Titan (Monster Core 2)|titans]] and [[Traits/Gigas|gigases]], the first giants. The word jotun roughly translates to “free ones,” referring to how titans rebelled against the gods and become an independent people, eventually creating the first gigases. The Jotun language came about in the wake of Earthfall. As the language was born when the [[Setting/Languages/Cyclops]] language was forcibly mixed with Thassilonian during the height of the runelords’ rule, the remaining giants who now spoke this language decided to name it in honor of the free ones as a way to reclaim the tongue for themselves. Jotunborn refer to themselves as vahndyrs, a word that roughly translates to “descendants” and was transformed into “jotunborn” in other languages.
