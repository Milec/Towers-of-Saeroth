# Consumables and Ammunition  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 237

**Parent page** [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]]

---

You can Craft items with the [[Traits/Consumable|consumable]] trait in batches, making up to four of the same item at once with a single check. This requires you to include the raw materials for all the items in the batch at the start, and you must complete the batch all at once. You also Craft non-magical ammunition in batches, using the quantity listed in the Ranged Weapons Table (typically 10).
