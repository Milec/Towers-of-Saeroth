# Item Rarity  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 223

**Parent page** [[Rules/Crafting Items]]

---

Like many other aspects of the rules, items have rarities. Player characters might find [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]] magic items for sale, but only infrequently and often by private sellers or in clandestine markets. Their formulas are often guarded and not readily available. Unless the GM decides otherwise, a character cannot purchase [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|rare]] items, and their formulas are lost to time.

Rarities for uncommon and rare treasures are indicated by the first trait in the stat block (or by the items' level if only some types of the item have a different rarity). [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]] items are indicated in the same way, though there are none in this book.
