# Blightburn Sickness  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 44

**Parent page** [[Equipment/Blightburn Bomb (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Blightburn Bomb]]

---

Caused by exposure to blightburn crystal, blightburn sickness burns and dissolves the afflicted creature from within. The target can't recover from the disease's drained or sickened condition except by magic. It's typically a 15th-level affliction (DC 32 Fortitude), but exposure to other forms of blightburn can change it.

**Onset** 1d4 days; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 2** drained 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** drained 2 and sickened 2 (1 week); **Stage 4** drained 3 and sickened 3 (1 month); **Stage 5** increase drained condition by 1 (1 year)
