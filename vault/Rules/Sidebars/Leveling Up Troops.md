# Leveling Up Troops  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 169

**Parent page** [[Rules/Customizing Troops]]

---

If the GM wants to increase a troop’s level, they can use the [[Rules/Designing Troops|troop creation]] guidelines to generate its new statistics. They might also want to add new abilities, such as a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]], for higher-level troops. These improvements can often be themed as the troop being outfitted with additional magic items or magical abilities.
