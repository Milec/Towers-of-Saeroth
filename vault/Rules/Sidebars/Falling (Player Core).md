# Falling  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 233

**Parent page** [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]]

---

When you fall more than 5 feet, you take falling damage when you land, which is bludgeoning damage equal to half the distance you fell. If you take any damage from a fall, you're knocked prone when you land. You can [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] as a reaction to reduce or eliminate the damage from some falls. More detailed rules for falling damage appear [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|here]].
