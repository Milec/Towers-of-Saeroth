# Example Pervasive Magic Locations  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 220

**Parent page** [[Rules/Magical Terrain]]

---

Use the following list as inspiration when thinking of places in your campaign where the pervasive magic rules might apply.

- A waterfall that grants [[Spells/Heroism|_heroism_]] to anyone who survives a plunge from its heights.
- A cavern that casts [[Spells/Hallucinatory Terrain|_hallucinatory terrain_]], preventing spelunkers from finding the exit.
- A remote spring that removes a curse or disease once per year for those who bathe in its waters.
- An impassable forest that frequently casts [[Spells/Tanglefoot|_tanglefoot_]] and [[Spells/Entangle|_entangle_]] on creatures not native to its reaches.
- A tavern where one patron each night is the target of a [[Spells/Hideous Laughter|_hideous laughter_]] spell.
