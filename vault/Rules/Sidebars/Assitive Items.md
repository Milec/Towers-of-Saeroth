# Assitive Items  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 101

**Parent page** [[Rules/Trappings of Power]]

---

Fantasy gaming is for everyone. It’s incredibly important for all people, regardless of what unique circumstances they may face in life, to be able to see reflections of themselves inside the world of the game. You can find [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|assistive items]] for a variety of disabilities here, in _Guns & Gears_, and in _Pathfinder Lost Omens Grand Bazaar_. Representation in gaming is essential to creating open and welcoming communities, so respectful use of assistive items has a place in all games and stories.
