# Supernatural Origins  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 28

**Parent page** [[Rules/Versatile Heritages (Advanced Player's Guide)|Versatile Heritages]]

---

The circumstances that give rise to individuals with versatile heritages vary widely, from direct biological descendance to clandestine rituals, planar incursions, or unique mystical phenomena. It's up to you to decide the details of your character's heritage and how they came into being. The sections in this book provide some broad suggestions that you can use as a jumping-off point, but these aren't restrictions. [[Bestiary/Families/Hag|Hags]], for example, typically give birth to mammalian offspring, but they are devious creatures and might just as easily change form to that of a [[Rules/Sidebars/Kobold Origins|kobold]] long enough to hatch a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changeling Relationships|changeling]] kobold child—or perhaps your changeling character arose as a result of a hag's occult meddling rather than direct lineage. It's your story to tell!
