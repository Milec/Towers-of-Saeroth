# Iriis  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 182

**Parent page** [[Spells/Summon Irii (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Summon Irii]]

---

These denizens of the [[Setting/Planes/Dimension of Time (GM Core)|Dimension of Time]] are powerful guardians who ward the timeline against outside meddling, even as they war with each other over their divergent philosophies of time. An irii can be either a fate, with the view that time is immutable, or a fortune, with the opinion that time is fluid. Among practitioners of temporal magic, iriis are famed for their ability to see consequences before they occur, sidestepping them at their leisure.
