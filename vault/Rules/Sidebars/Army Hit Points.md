# Army Hit Points  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 569

**Parent page** [[Rules/Army Stat Block]]

---

As with creatures and objects, armies have Hit Points, but an army's HP cannot be reduced by damage— they are reduced only as the result of a war action. Typically, a successful army Strike reduces the target army's HP by 1, or by 2 on a critical hit. An army's HP doesn't automatically recover at the end of a battle.
