# Cantorian Spring  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 48

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Cantorian Spring|Hobgoblin]]

---

In days long past, Cantorys, goddess of life, transformations, and forests, created a staff called the _Cantorian Spring_ and placed it on Golarion for fledgling mortals to find. A powerful [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devil]] named Canzoriant learned of the staff and slew Cantorys, claiming it for himself. Using the _Cantorian Spring_'s power, Canzoriant transformed early [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblins]] into the more powerful and calculating hobgoblins, instilling in them a deep hatred for [[Ancestries/Elf|elves]]. After a failed attempt by elves to claim the staff, the _Cantorian Spring_ disappeared and hasn't been seen since.
