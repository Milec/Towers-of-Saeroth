# Working Together  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 506

**Parent page** [[Rules/Appendix 2- Kingdoms|Appendix 2: Kingdoms]]

---

More so than typical Pathfinder campaigns, _Kingmaker_ assumes that the PCs work together. These rules assume that all of the PCs have roles as leaders in the nation, and that they're all focused on making their kingdom prosperous and stable, rather than stealing from the treasury, oppressing the citizens (or each other), or striking out on their own to start competing kingdoms. Before starting this campaign, you should make sure your players understand this assumption and build appropriate characters.
