# Unroutable Troops  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 160

**Parent page** [[Rules/Routing Troops]]

---

Because of the traits on a rout check, some troops are entirely immune to them, in particular [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|mindless]] hordes like [[Bestiary/Families/Zombie (Monster Core)|zombies]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Clockworks|clockwork constructs]]. NPCs often use these kinds of troops due to this advantage, but PCs might also be able to raise [[Bestiary/Skeleton Infantry|skeleton infantry]] or build a similar fearless army.
