# Choosing A Spotter  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 136

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Choosing A Spotter|Pistol Phenom]]

---

The most important aspect when choosing a spotter for the sniping duo archetype is the out of character component; make sure you team up with another player who is excited about the idea of roleplaying out teamwork and coordination in combat, and with granting you benefits and gaining benefits from you.
