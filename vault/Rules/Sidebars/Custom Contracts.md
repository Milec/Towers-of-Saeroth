# Custom Contracts  *Sidebar*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 3]] pg. 64

**Parent page** [[Rules/Sidebars/Custom Contracts|Hellbound Creature]]

---

You can modify the abilities in the Hellbound creature template to suit the devil granting them or the specific agreement. For example, a mortal seeking allure might gain [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] rather than [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Spells/Charm|_charm_]] rather than [[Spells/Burning Hands|_burning hands_]].
