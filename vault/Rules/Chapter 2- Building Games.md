# Chapter 2: Building Games  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 63

Game Mastering can be an extremely creative endeavor, and this chapter gives you a suite of tools to make your own campaign or adventure. You can even create your own creatures, hazards, and items to populate your game, or even make your own game world!

Where Chapter 1 gave you the information to run a game, whether it was your own creation or someone else's, this chapter digs deep into making the game fully your own. This chapter is organized into the following sections.  

- **[[Rules/Campaign Structure (GM Core)|Campaign Structure]]** discusses how you might connect multiple different events, encounters, and adventures together to create a longer story.
- **[[Rules/Adventure Design (GM Core)|Adventure Design]]** suggests common themes and tropes used in role playing adventures and gives advice on how to make sure your game is fun and exciting for your players.
- **[[Rules/Encounter Design (GM Core)|Encounter Design]]** explains how to create compelling and dynamic conflict in your game, be it violent combat or clever social debate.
- **[[Rules/Variant Rules]]** contains changes to the base Pathfinder rules that offer a different play experience from the baseline.
- **[[Rules/Afflictions (GM Core)|Afflictions]]** provides a plethora of curses and diseases for use in your games.
- **[[Rules/Environment (GM Core)|Environment]]** gives rules for overcoming obstacles and natural hazards that might be found in the surrounding area.
- **[[Rules/Hazards (rules-2846)|Hazards]]** are harrowing traps that might protect a fortress or dungeon. This section is filled with hazards to challenge your players in their explorations.
- **[[Rules/Building Hazards (GM Core)|Building Hazards]]** offers advice on how to create your own brand-new hazards.
- **[[Rules/Building Creatures (GM Core)|Building Creatures]]** demonstrates a topdown approach for quickly and easily constructing the creatures and NPCs you want or need for any possible situation in your game.
- **[[Rules/Building Items (GM Core)|Building Items]]** teaches you how to create new pieces of treasure to delight your PCs.
- **[[Rules/Building Worlds (GM Core)|Building Worlds]]** explains how to go about building your own entire world or setting from scratch.
