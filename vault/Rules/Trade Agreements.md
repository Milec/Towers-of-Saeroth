# Trade Agreements  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 534

The PCs can bolster their kingdom's economy by [[Actions/Establish Trade Agreement|Establishing Trade Agreements]] with other groups with whom they have diplomatic relations. To do so, they must first successfully perform the Establish Trade Agreement activity. There is no need to record the actual physical route of the Trade Agreement on the map, nor does distance play a significant factor.
