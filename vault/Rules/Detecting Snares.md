# Detecting Snares  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 589

Creatures can detect snares as they would any trap or hazard (as described [[Rules/Detecting a Hazard|here]]), using the creator's Crafting DC as the snare's Stealth DC. As you become better at creating snares, your snares become harder to detect by those with lesser ability. If you are an expert in Crafting, only a creature that is trained in Perception can find your snares; if you are a master in Crafting, only a creature that is an expert in Perception can find your snares; and if you are legendary in Crafting, only a creature that is a master in Perception can find your snares.

 If your proficiency rank is expert or better in Crafting, only creatures actively searching can find your snares.
