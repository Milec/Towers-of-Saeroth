# Alchemy  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 244

Alchemical items use the properties of volatile chemicals, exotic minerals, potent plants, and other substances, collectively referred to as alchemical reagents. As such, with rare exceptions, alchemical items don’t radiate magical auras, and they can’t be dismissed or affected by [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]]. Their effects last for a set amount of time or until they are countered in some way, typically physically.

Sometimes the reactions of alchemical reagents create effects that seem magical, and at other times they straddle the line between purely reactive and the inexplicable. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|Alchemists]] can infuse reagents with some of their own essence, allowing them to efficiently create short-lived alchemical items at no monetary cost. Even in these cases, alchemical items don’t radiate magic auras (unless otherwise noted), instead using the alchemist’s infused essence as one additional catalyst for the item’s alchemical effects.

Rules for creating alchemical items are found in the [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] activity, and you must have the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting (Player Core)|Alchemical Crafting]] skill feat to use [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] to create alchemical items. Critically failing a Crafting check to make alchemical items often causes a dangerous effect, such as an explosion for a bomb or accidental exposure for a poison, in addition to losing some of the materials. Some alchemical items have additional requirements beyond those stated in the Craft activity; these items list their requirements in a Craft Requirements entry.

All alchemical items have the [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]] trait. Most also have the [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]] trait, which means that the item is used up once activated. The [[Traits/Bomb (Player Core)|bomb]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]], and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] traits indicate special categories of alchemical items, each of which is described on the following pages. Alchemical items without any of these traits are called alchemical tools.
