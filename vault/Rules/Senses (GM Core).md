# Senses  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 115

Choose or design any special senses for your creature, such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]], or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent|scent]]. If you’re making a sense from scratch, simply decide what it senses, whether it has a range limit, and whether it’s [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise]] or [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]]. For example, a [[Bestiary/Families/Sinspawn|sinspawn]] has “sin scent (imprecise) 30 feet.” This means it can smell creatures bearing its associated sin if they’re within 30 feet, and the sense is imprecise—about as acute as human hearing.
