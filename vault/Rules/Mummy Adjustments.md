# Mummy Adjustments  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 72

All types of creatures can have their corpses preserved and rise as [[Bestiary/Families/Mummy|mummies]].

- Add the [[Traits/Mummy|mummy]] and [[Traits/Undead|undead]] traits.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Necril]] language.
- Add the following immunities: [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
- Add weakness to [[Traits/Fire|fire]], with a value depending on the creature's level.
- Add the following abilities. **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision]]** **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing]]** **Lesser Despair** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) 30 feet. Living creatures of the mummy creature's level or lower are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] while in its despair aura. They can't naturally recover from this fear while in the area but recover instantly once they leave.
