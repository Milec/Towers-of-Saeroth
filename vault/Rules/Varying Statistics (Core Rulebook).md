# Varying Statistics  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 597

Each type of wand has a Level and Price determined by the spell's level. The wand's rarity matches the spell's rarity, and it has its spell's traits. A wand has the normal Hardness, BT, and HP of a thin item of its material (see [[Rules/Material Statistics|Table 11-4]]).
