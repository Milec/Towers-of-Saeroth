# Chapter 2: Ancestry NPCs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 167

This chapter presents entries for every ancestry appearing in _Pathfinder Player Core_ and _Pathfinder Player Core 2_. Each one includes several stat blocks and ideas to spice up your NPCs, come up with quick names, or evoke the ancestries' cultures. If an ancestry also has a section in _Pathfinder Monster Core_, that information is included in the entry's introductory text.
