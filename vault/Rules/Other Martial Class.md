# Other Martial Class  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 195

An apprentice of another martial class ([[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|barbarian]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|champion]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-154)|fighter]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-204)|ranger]], or [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|rogue]]) is trained in light armor, all simple weapons, and one martial weapon listed in the class’s initial proficiencies. If a martial class not listed here lacks light armor or martial weapon training (as the monk does), give it a different ability as well.
