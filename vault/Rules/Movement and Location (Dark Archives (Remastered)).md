# Movement and Location  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 202

Changing location is usually easy within a psychic duel, as a participant can move at the speed of thought. It typically takes only a single action to move to any location the participant wants and range of attacks and spells rarely matter much. You can usually run a psychic duel without a grid. Functions of positioning and movement still matter, but have a more flexible appearance. For instance, a participant can [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]], but this likely means shaping the imaginary environment around them to create a visual screen, glowing phenomenon, or even psychic static. Likewise, spells that [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilize]] or [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grab]] a creature can prevent the easy movement detailed above.
