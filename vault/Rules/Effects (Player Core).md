# Effects  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 398

An effect is the rules term for anything that occurs in the game world. Effects might have limited **[[Rules/Range and Reach (Player Core)|range]]**, and you may need to designate **[[Rules/Targets (rules-2380)|targets]]** or create **[[Rules/Areas (rules-2381)|areas]]** for your effects. Areas include bursts from a single point, cones blasting out from you, emanations surrounding you or another creature, or straight lines.

Effects that last for a period of time list a **[[Rules/Duration (Player Core)|duration]]**. These can last a set increment of time, or can end if certain requirements are met. Many effects apply **[[Rules/Conditions Appendix|conditions]]**, which measure advantages or impediments like being [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]].
