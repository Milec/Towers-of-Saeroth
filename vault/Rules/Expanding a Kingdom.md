# Expanding a Kingdom  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 534

A kingdom grows one hex at a time on the Stolen Lands map, via Region activities like [[Actions/Claim Hex]] and [[Actions/Clear Hex]]. The PCs can pursue these activities during the [[Rules/Activity Phase|Activity phase]] of each Kingdom turn.
