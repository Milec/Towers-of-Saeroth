# Using Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 531

This section presents the rules for how characters use alchemical items, magic items, and other special items during play.

 The myriad types of items give their powerful boons in different ways. Some function automatically, while others need to be activated. While you need only swing a _[[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] greataxe_ to scorch foes, you need to invest a [[Equipment/Diadem of Intellect|_diadem of intellect_]] for it to work, imbibe an [[Equipment/Elixir of Life|_elixir of life_]] to heal yourself, activate [[Equipment/Slippers of Spider Climbing|_slippers of spider climbing_]] to walk up a wall, activate your [[Equipment/Mail of Luck|_mail of luck_]] to protect yourself, and Cast a Spell using a magical scroll.
