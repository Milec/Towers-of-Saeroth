# Specific Magic Armor  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 557

These suits of armor have abilities far different from what can be gained by etching runes. A specific magic armor lists its [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|fundamental runes]], which you can upgrade, add, or transfer as normal. You can't etch or transfer any [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|property runes]] onto a specific armor that it doesn't already have or remove its property runes.
