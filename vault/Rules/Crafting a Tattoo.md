# Crafting a Tattoo  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 164

Inking a magical tattoo onto a creature is much like etching a rune onto an item. The tattooist uses the [[Actions/Craft]] activity, and the subject must be present throughout the process. The tattooist must meet any special Craft requirements, and they can ink only one tattoo at a time. Not only does a magical tattoo not have any effect until the Craft activity is complete, but it also requires a healing period. Though this time frame can last up to a month, applying magical or alchemical healing can shorten it to as little as a day.
