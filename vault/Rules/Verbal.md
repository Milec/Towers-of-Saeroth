# Verbal  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 303

A verbal component is a vocalization of words of power. You must speak them in a strong voice, so it’s hard to conceal that you’re Casting a Spell. The spell gains the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait. You must be able to speak to provide this component.
