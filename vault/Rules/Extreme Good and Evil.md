# Extreme Good and Evil  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 184

In this variant, some creatures exemplify the concepts of extreme good and evil by their very nature. Only [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]], [[Traits/Celestial|celestials]], and other residents of aligned Outer Sphere planes have an alignment. Remove most alignment restrictions— such as the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-80)|champion]] restrictions—but not ones related to those extraplanar creatures. Replace the removed restrictions with appropriate anathema if necessary.
