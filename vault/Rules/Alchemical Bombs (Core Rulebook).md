# Alchemical Bombs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 544

An alchemical bomb combines volatile alchemical components that explode when the bomb hits a creature or object. Most alchemical bombs deal damage, though some produce other effects. Bombs have the [[Traits/Bomb|bomb]] trait.

 Bombs are martial thrown weapons with a range increment of 20 feet. When you throw a bomb, you make a weapon attack roll against the target’s AC, as you would for any other weapon. It takes one hand to draw, prepare, and throw a bomb. Due to the complexity involved in preparing bombs, Strikes to throw alchemical bombs gain the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait. The bomb is activated when thrown as a Strike—you don’t have to activate it separately.
