# Action  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 10

During encounters, each creature gets three [[Rules/Actions (rules-2335)|actions]] during their turn. These actions are spent to attack, interact with objects, move, and use special abilities. Actions available to all characters can be found in [[Rules/Basic Actions (Player Core)|Chapter 8]].
