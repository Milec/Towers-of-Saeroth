# Rock Gardens and Stalagmite Caverns  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 168

An umbral [[Bestiary/Families/Gnome|gnome]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druid]] carefully tends a cavern where the slow drip of limestone grows a forest unlike anything born from soil and sunlight. Gardens of stone and gems are actually more common beneath the surface than gardens containing flowers and herbs, and they can be an excellent source of consumables like [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]], mineral-based [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical items]], and other consumables crafted from gems and stones. Such subterranean gardens are most common among umbral gnomes, though any ancestry or species that makes its home in the Darklands might have the necessary skills to manage a garden of stone and gems. These types of gardens are particularly good for creating consumables like gadgets or talismans but require a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]] in addition to the normal creation costs for establishing a garden.

 When using [[Actions/Earn Income]] to help defray the costs of creating this kind of garden, [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Engineering Lore]], and Mining Lore are appropriate skills.
