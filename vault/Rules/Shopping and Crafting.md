# Shopping and Crafting  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 441

If you're at a location with shops that buy or sell magic items, you can buy, sell, or trade. Ask the GM what types of shopping options are available to you—it can vary greatly depending where you're spending your downtime!

Because of the complexities of finding shops that are looking for items you want to sell or that offer ones you want to buy, dedicated shopping takes 1 day of downtime. It might take longer if you're selling a large number of goods, expensive items that require a wealthy buyer, or items that aren't in high demand.

The Price of an item indicates the full cost to buy it. You can sell an item for half its Price. The GM might adjust these once in a while due to supply and demand or the particular merchants you're dealing with.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Cost of Living
>
>
>
> You might need to pay your [[Rules/Cost of Living (Player Core)|cost of living]] for days spent in downtime. To avoid paying the cost, you can [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]], using [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] in a settlement or [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] in the wild. Experienced adventurers often have friends or patrons who take care of their living expenses. They might even have so much treasure that their cost of living—even one of the more expensive options—becomes a pittance.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Skill Downtime Activities
>
>
>
> [[Rules/Chapter 4- Skills (rules-2134)|Chapter 4: Skills]] includes several downtime activities, which are summarized here.
>
> **[[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]]:** Using the [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] skill, you can create items from raw materials.  
> **[[Actions/Create Forgery (Player Core)|Create Forgery]]:** You forge a document.  
> **[[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]]:** You earn money, typically using Crafting, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]].  
> **[[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]]:** You find food and shelter in the wilderness or within a settlement.  
> **[[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]]:** You spend time caring for a diseased creature in the hope of curing that creature.
