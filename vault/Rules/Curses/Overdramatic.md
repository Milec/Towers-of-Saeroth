# Overdramatic  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 93

**Usage** curses a weapon

The weapon flashes excessive light with each attack. On a critical hit with the weapon, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the end of your turn and take the effects of a [[Spells/Faerie Fire|_faerie fire_]] spell until the start of your next turn.
