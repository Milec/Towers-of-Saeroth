# Reviled of Nature  *Curse 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

This curse makes the hunter into the hunted, drawing the ire of animals wherever you go.

**Saving Throw**
DC 38 Will

**Effect**
Creatures of the natural world abhor you. Whenever an [[Traits/Animal|animal]] becomes aware of you, it must attempt a Will save against the curse. On a failure, it attacks you and fights to the death.
