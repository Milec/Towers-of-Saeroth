# Cowards's Roots  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 86

You find all courage stolen from your heart. When faced with something frightening, you flee in terror or stand frozen in place.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Will

**Effect**
At the start of your turn, if you are frightened, you become your choice of [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] until the end of that turn.
