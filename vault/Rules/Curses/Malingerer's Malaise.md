# Malingerer's Malaise  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 47

This curse channels [[Setting/Deities/Droskar|Droskar's]] disdain for anyone who avoids work or can't endure toil, condemning them to forever be tired.

**Saving Throw**
DC 26 Will

**Effect**
Whenever you rest for 8 hours, you must succeed at a DC 17 flat check or gain no benefits from having rested and become [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]]. If you were already fatigued, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]]. If you critically fail the flat check and were already both fatigued and doomed, increase the value of your doomed condition by 1.
