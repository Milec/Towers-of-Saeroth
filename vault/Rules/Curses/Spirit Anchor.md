# Spirit Anchor  *Curse 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

This curse prevents your soul from moving on after death.

**Saving Throw**
DC 31 Fortitude

**Effect**
If you die while affected, your spirit is anchored to the Material Plane, and you become a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost]] or other incorporeal undead.
