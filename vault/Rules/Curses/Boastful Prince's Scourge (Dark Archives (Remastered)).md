# Boastful Prince's Scourge  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 162

Used as a punishment against the vain or the arrogant, this curse slowly transforms you into a frog in not just body, but mind as well.

**Saving Throw**
DC 23 Will

**Effect**
Your features slowly morph into that of a frog's as you grow a prominent vocal sac. You gain the amphibious trait and can breathe both air and water. You're [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 as long as you're under the effect of this curse, and you can't reduce your stupefied value below 1.

**Tempted Curse**
You transform fully, as [[Spells/Animal Form (Player Core)|_animal form_]], giving you the specific abilities of a frog. The duration of this casting is until you make your daily preparations, and you can't Dismiss the spell. While you're under the effect of animal form due to this curse, you have all the usual limitations of animal form, including the fact that you can't speak (though you can still understand any languages you know).
