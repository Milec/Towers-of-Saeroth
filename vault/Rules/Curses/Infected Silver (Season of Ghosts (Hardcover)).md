# Infected Silver  *Curse 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|Cursed]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 337

After 1 minute of carrying infected silver, it fuses to you. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and remain so as long as the curse persists. As long as you remain cursed, you take a –2 status penalties to all saving throws against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects created by [[Bestiary/Families/Nindoru|nindorus]].
