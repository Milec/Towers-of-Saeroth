# Blackfrost  *Curse 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 76

This affliction can't be reduced below stage 1, nor the damage from it healed, until successfully treated with [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]] or a similar effect; the affliction can then be removed as a normal disease. A creature that dies from cold damage while cursed with blackfrost rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Blackfrost Dead|blackfrost dead]] after 24 hours.

**Saving Throw**
DC 27 Fortitude

**Effect**
**Stage 1** 4d6 cold damage (1 minute); **Stage 2** weakness to [[Traits/Cold|cold]] 5 and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 day); **Stage 3** weakness to cold 10 and drained 2 (1 day)
