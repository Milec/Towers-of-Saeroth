# Curse of Nightmares  *Curse 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 116

This curse plagues you with terrible nightmares whenever you sleep, preventing you from resting properly.

**Saving Throw**
DC 16 Will

**Effect**
You must rest for 12 hours instead of 8 to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] and can’t gain any benefits from resting or long-term rest. You can still make your daily preparations.
