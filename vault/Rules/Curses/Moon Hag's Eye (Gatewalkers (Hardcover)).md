# Moon Hag's Eye  *Curse 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 77

The pupil and iris in your left eye turn yellowish white, pocked with wheat-colored dots like the craters of the moon. This curse enables you to pierce the veil of night, but only at the cost of some of your daylight vision.

**Saving Throw**
DC 29 Will

**Effect**
You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], or darkvision if you already had low-light vision. In daylight, you take a –1 status penalty to vision-based Perception checks.
