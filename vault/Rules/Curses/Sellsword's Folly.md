# Sellsword's Folly  *Curse 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

Even the most experienced combat veteran becomes as reckless as a rookie when suffering from sellsword’s folly.

**Saving Throw**
DC 26 Will

**Effect**
The chaos of combat overwhelms you. Each time you roll initiative for a combat encounter, you must attempt a new saving throw against the curse; on a failure, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 round. This is an [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] effect.
