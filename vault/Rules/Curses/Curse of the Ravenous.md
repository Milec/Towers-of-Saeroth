# Curse of the Ravenous  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 116

This hideous curse kills through constant hunger. You become gaunt and repeatedly gnash your teeth.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Fortitude

**Effect**
No matter how much you eat, you aren’t satiated. After 1 day, you begin [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst|starving]]. Each week, you receive a new saving throw against the curse.
