# Ravenous  *Curse 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|Cursed]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 307

**Usage** curses a ring, staff, or wand

A _ravenous_ curse draws power from the wielder's body. Whenever you activate the item, you become incredibly hungry and immediately begin to [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst|starve]]. You require 10 times as much food as normal for the next day.
