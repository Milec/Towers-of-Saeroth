# Unending Thirst  *Curse 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 87

This curse kills through dehydration in the same vein as the curse of the ravenous, but with deadly speed

**Saving Throw**
DC 32 Fortitude

**Effect**
You gain no benefit from drinking water or other liquids and begin suffering from [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|thirst]]. Each day, you receive a new saving throw against the curse.
