# Curse of Nightmares  *Curse 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 86

This curse plagues you with terrible nightmares whenever you sleep, preventing you from resting properly.

**Saving Throw**
DC 16 Will

**Effect**
You must rest for 12 hours instead of 8 to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] and can't gain any benefits from resting or long-term rest. You can still make your daily preparations.
