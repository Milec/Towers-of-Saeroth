# Amnerian Amnesia  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 45

The Iblydan underworld slowly saps inhabitants’ memories before shunting them to their final afterlife.

**Saving Throw**
DC 24 Will

**Effect**
**Onset** 1 day; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 and stupefied 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** drained 1 and stupefied 2 (1 week); **Stage 4** drained 1, stupefied 2, and whenever the creature would fail a save against this curse, the value of their stupefied condition increases by 1 (1 month);

**Special** A creature who becomes stupefied 5 by this curse cannot recall anything from before they entered Amneros, and over the next week they meld with Amnaia’s landscape and are transported to [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma’s]] Court for judgment.
