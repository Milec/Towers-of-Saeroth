# Curse of the Ravenous  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 86

This hideous curse kills through constant hunger. You become gaunt and repeatedly gnash your teeth as your body begins to consume itself.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Fortitude

**Effect**
No matter how much you eat, you aren't satiated. After 1 day, you begin [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starving]]. Each week, you receive a new saving throw against the curse.
