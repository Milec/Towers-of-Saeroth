# Reviling Earth  *Curse 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

A reviling earth curse usually appears across a specific geographical region, such as a ruined town, a necromancer’s domain, or a similar area.

**Saving Throw**
DC 30 Fortitude

**Effect**
When you enter the area, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]], or doomed 2 on a critical failure.
