# Curse of the Rotting Heart  *Curse 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 176

A shriveled, spectral heart appears inside your body, beating alongside your own.

**Saving Throw**
DC 25 Will

**Effect**
You're harmed by positive energy as if you were [[Traits/Undead|undead]], harmed by negative energy as if you were living, and can't be healed by either one. All other forms of magical healing, such as the [[Spells/Soothe|_soothe_]] spell, restore only half the normal amount of Hit Points. Alchemical healing, healing with the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] skill, and other non-magical methods continue to heal you normally.
