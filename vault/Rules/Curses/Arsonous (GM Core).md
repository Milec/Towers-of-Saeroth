# Arsonous  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|Cursed]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 307

**Usage** curses a ring, staff, or wand

An _arsonous_ curse creates flaws in the mystic pathways that channel magic through an item, allowing excess power to escape as sparks. Whenever you activate the magic item, a random ally within 30 feet takes 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. If no ally is in range, you take the damage instead. At the GM's discretion, this curse might ignite an unattended object or the surrounding environment instead.
