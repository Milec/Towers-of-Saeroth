# Unraveling Skin  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 162

Often used to protect ancient tomes, this curse causes your skin to shed like weathered paper, cracking and bleeding at the slightest touch.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Fortitude

**Effect**
Seeping wounds cover your body as this curse makes your skin brittle. Any time you would gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition, increase the value by 1. Any time you take bleed damage, increase the amount of damage by 1.

**Tempted Curse**
Your skin falls from your body and exposes your raw flesh beneath, but the curse holds your flesh together as its magic increases. You gain resistance [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|to persistent bleed damage]] equal to half your level and are [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1.
