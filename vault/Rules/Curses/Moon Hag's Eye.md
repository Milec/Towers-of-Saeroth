# Moon Hag's Eye  *Curse 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 11

The pupil and iris in your left eye turn yellowish white, pocked with wheat-colored dots like the craters of the moon. This curse enables you to pierce the veil of night, but only at the cost of some of your daylight vision.

**Saving Throw**
DC 29 Will

**Effect**
You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], or [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] if you already had low-light vision. In daylight, you take a –1 status penalty to vision-based Perception checks.
