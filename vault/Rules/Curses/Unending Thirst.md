# Unending Thirst  *Curse 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

This curse kills through dehydration in the same vein as the curse of the ravenous, but with deadly speed.

**Saving Throw**
DC 32 Fortitude

**Effect**
You gain no benefit from drinking water or other liquids and begin suffering from [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst|thirst]]. Each day, you receive a new saving throw against the curse.
