# Lord's Arrogance  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 176

You're filled with the unearned confidence of a young noble and carry yourself as if you can do no wrong.

**Saving Throw**
DC 23 Will

**Effect**
Whenever you make a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] check or a Perception check that would give you information (such as to [[Actions/Sense Motive]]), you always receive at least one piece of false information that you believe with absolute confidence, even on a success or better. If you attempt to act on this false information, you receive a –1 circumstance penalty on any skill checks you attempt.
