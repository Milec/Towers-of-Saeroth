# Oath of the Flesh  *Curse 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

When you swear an oath, you must obey that oath or suffer terrible consequences.

**Saving Throw**
DC 23 Fortitude

**Effect**
Each time you make a promise to someone, an ornate symbol representing that promise is magically carved into your flesh. Breaking any of these promises causes the symbol tattoo to grow into a gaping wound, dealing 3d6 slashing damage to you; damage from the curse can’t be healed as long as the curse is still in effect.
