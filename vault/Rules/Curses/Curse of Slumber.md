# Curse of Slumber  *Curse 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Sleep]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 117

This legendary curse sends you into a sleep indistinguishable from death.

**Saving Throw**
DC 28 Fortitude

**Effect**
You fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|asleep]] for 1 round (or permanently on a critical failure) and seem to be dead; a creature must succeed at a DC 30 Medicine check to realize you are alive. Noise doesn’t awaken you, but taking damage gives you a new saving throw against the curse.
