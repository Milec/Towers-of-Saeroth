# Coward's Roots  *Curse 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 116

You find all courage stolen from your heart. When faced with something frightening, you flee in terror or stand frozen in place.

**Saving Throw**
DC 20 Will

**Effect**
At the start of your turn, if you are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], you become your choice of [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] until the end of that turn.
