# Sellswords's Folly  *Curse 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 87

Even the most experienced combat veteran becomes as reckless as a rookie when suffering from sellsword's folly.

**Saving Throw**
DC 26 Will

**Effect**
The chaos of combat overwhelms you. Each time you roll initiative for a combat encounter, you must attempt a new saving throw against the curse; on a failure, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round. This is an [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect.
