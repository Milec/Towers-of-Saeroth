# The Four Rarities  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 35

Let’s first review the default usage for the four rarities in the game and how these already start to tell a story about your world.

- [[Traits/Common|**Common**]] elements are prevalent enough, at least among adventurers, that a player is assumed to be able to access them provided they meet the prerequisites (if any).
- [[Traits/Uncommon|**Uncommon**]] elements are difficult to access, but a PC can usually find them eventually with enough effort, potentially by choosing a specific character option or spending substantial downtime tracking them down.
- [[Traits/Rare|**Rare**]] elements are lost secrets, ancient magic, and other options that PCs can access only if you specifically make them available.
- [[Traits/Unique|**Unique**]] elements are one of a kind. You have full control over whether PCs can access them. Named NPCs are unique creatures, though that doesn’t mean their base creature type is unique. For instance, an orc named Graytusk is unique, but that doesn’t mean it would be any harder for a PC encountering her to tell she’s an orc—just to discern specific information about her.
