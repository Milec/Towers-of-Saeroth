# Focus Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 228

A focus spell is unique to a class and reflects its individual role and style. This section introduces focus spells for the oracle and witch and presents several new options for some _Core Rulebook_ classes. The full rules for casting focus spells appear [[Rules/Focus Spells|here]].
