# Nonlethal Attacks  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 453

You can make a nonlethal attack in an effort to knock someone out instead of killing them (see [[Rules/Knocked Out and Dying]]). Weapons with the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait (including fists) do this automatically. You take a –2 circumstance penalty to the attack roll when you make a nonlethal attack using a weapon that doesn't have the nonlethal trait. You also take this penalty when making a lethal attack using a nonlethal weapon.

 Spells and other effects with the nonlethal trait that reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points knock the creature out instead of killing them.
