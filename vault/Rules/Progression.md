# Progression  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 186

A blighted boon grows in potency within the user's body, progressing in stages. While in any stage from a blighted boon, the [[Rules/Partaking (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|partaker]] is temporarily immune to other versions of the same blighted boon. Unless the boon's description says otherwise, the effects of each stage combine, often growing stronger over time.
