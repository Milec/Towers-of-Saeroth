# Scrolls  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 262

A scroll contains a single spell that you can cast without having to expend a spell slot. A scroll can be [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Crafted]] to contain nearly any spell. The exceptions are [[Rules/Cantrips (Player Core)|cantrips]], [[Rules/Focus Spells (Player Core)|focus spells]], and [[Rules/Rituals (Player Core)|rituals]], none of which can be put on scrolls. The spell on a scroll can be cast only once, and the scroll is destroyed as part of the casting. The spell on the scroll is cast at a particular spell rank, as determined by the scroll. For instance, a scroll of [[Spells/Force Barrage|_force barrage_]] (1st rank) can be used to cast the 1st-rank version of _force barrage_, but not a force barrage heightened to 2nd rank. If no rank is listed, the scroll can be used to cast the spell at its lowest rank.

If you find a scroll, you can try to figure out what spell it contains. If the spell is a common spell from your spell list or a spell you know, you can spend a single [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] action and automatically succeed at identifying the scroll's spell. If it's not, you must [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]].
