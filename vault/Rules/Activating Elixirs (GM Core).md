# Activating Elixirs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 246

You usually [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to activate an elixir as you drink it or feed it to another creature. You can feed an elixir only to a creature within reach that is either willing or unable to prevent you from doing so. You usually need only one hand to consume an elixir or feed it to another creature.
