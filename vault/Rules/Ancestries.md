# Ancestries  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 7

Expand beyond the populous ancestries found in the _Core Rulebook_. The ancestry entry format is described [[Rules/Ancestry Entries|here]].

- **[[Rules/Sidebars/Other Catfolk|Catfolk]]**, or amurruns, are humanoid felines who believe they were created to protect the wilderness from great danger.
- **[[Rules/Sidebars/Kobold Origins|Kobolds]]** are small reptiles connected to dragons and fond of traps.
- **[[Rules/Sidebars/Orc Settlements|Orcs]]** are warlike and honest people who respect strength, toughness, and survival above all.
- **[[Rules/Sidebars/Ratfolk Settlements|Ratfolk]]** , or ysoki, pack into tight spaces and are markedly industrious and fastidious.
- **[[Rules/Sidebars/Tengu Settlements|Tengus]]** are corvid-like humanoids with an affinity for blades and the rumored mystical ability to absorb bad luck.
