# Toxic  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 216

**Examples** scorpion, spider, viper  
 **Poison** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The NPC commands their companion to deliver a dose of its natural venom to an enemy within 15 feet. That target takes 1d4 poison damage. This increases by 1d4 at 5th level and every 4 levels after that.
