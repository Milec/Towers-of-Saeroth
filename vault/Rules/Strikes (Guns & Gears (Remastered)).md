# Strikes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 32

Your construct companion has two kinds of melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks. Its first unarmed attack deals 1d8 bludgeoning damage. Depending on the shape of your construct this could be a fist, tendril, or other similar unarmed attack. Its other unarmed attack deals 1d6 slashing or piercing damage (choose when you first gain the companion) and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits. Depending on the shape of your construct, this could be a spine or spike, jaws or fangs, a retractable blade, or other similar unarmed attack.
