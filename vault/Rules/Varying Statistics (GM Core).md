# Varying Statistics  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 262

The [[Equipment/Magic Scroll|magic scroll]] stat block indicates the item level and Price of a scroll, both of which are based on the rank of the spell in the scroll. Any costs to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]] are added to the scroll's Price when the scroll is crafted, so a scroll containing a spell with a Cost entry will have a higher Price than what's on the table. The scroll's rarity matches the spell's rarity.

The traits for a scroll vary based on the spell it contains. A scroll always has the [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], and [[Traits/Scroll (GM Core)|scroll]] traits, plus any traits that might be relevant to identifying the item, such as [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]. The GM determines the extra traits, if necessary.
