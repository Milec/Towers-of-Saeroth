# Leveling up your Armies  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 571

When your kingdom gains a level, each army gains a level as well, increasing its stats as detailed on the [[Rules/Basic Armies by Level|Basic Armies table]]. When an army increases its level, it may also increase the maximum number of tactics it can know—these new tactics are not gained automatically, but must instead be learned through the use of the Train Army activity.
