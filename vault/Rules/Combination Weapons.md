# Combination Weapons  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 158

With the advent of firearms, there have been a number of [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-509)|gunslingers]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|inventors]], and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|alchemists]] who have created a variety of combination weapons using black powder. The [[Equipment/Weapons/Explosive Dogslicer (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|explosive dogslicer]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Gnome Amalgam Musket (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|gnome amalgam musket]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Three Peaked Tree (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|three peaked tree]] all have traits corresponding to an ancestry. However, feats such as [[Feats/Elven Weapon Familiarity (Player Core)|Elven Weapon Familiarity]], which normally grants access to all uncommon weapons with the [[Traits/Elf (Player Core)|elf]] trait, do not grant access to firearms with the given trait unless the character separately has access to firearms. You can find more information on this and other firearm access details on page 148.
