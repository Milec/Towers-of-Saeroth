# Magic Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 247

The remainder of this chapter contains the descriptions of dozens of new magic items. These items are divided into two subcategories for ease of reference: consumable magic items, including [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|ammunition]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|oils]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|potions]], and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]]. Permanent items consist of [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|armor]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|held items]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|runes]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|shields]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|staves]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|wands]], [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|weapons]], and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|worn items]]. Items are organized alphabetically within each section. Rules for activating and investing items, for sustaining and dismissing activations, and for reading an item's stat block are located [[Rules/Chapter 11- Crafting & Treasure|here]].
