# Spontaneous Spells  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 298

If you’re a spontaneous spellcaster—such as a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-55)|bard]] or a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerer]]—you choose which spell you’re using a spell slot for at the moment you decide to cast it. This provides you with more freedom in your spellcasting, but you have fewer spells in your spell repertoire, as determined by your character level and class. When you make your daily preparations, all your spell slots are refreshed, but you don’t get to change the spells in your repertoire.
