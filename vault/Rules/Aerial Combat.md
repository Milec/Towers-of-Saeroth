# Aerial Combat  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 478

Many monsters can fly, and PCs can use spells and items to gain the ability to fly. Flying creatures have to use the [[Actions/Fly]] action to move through the air. Performing an especially tricky maneuver—such as trying to reverse course 180 degrees or fly through a narrow gap—might require using [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] to [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight]]. Creatures might fall from the sky, using the [[Rules/Falling|falling rules]]. At the GM’s discretion, some ground-based actions might not work in the air. For instance, a flying creature couldn’t [[Actions/Leap]].
