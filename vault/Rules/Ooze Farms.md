# Ooze Farms  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 168

An [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|alchemist]] from the [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze|ooze]]-loving city of Oenopion establishes a laboratory of strange oozes, generating a beaker-full of odd, mutagenic gloop each day that can be used in the creation of almost any alchemical consumable of an appropriate level. Ooze gardens are most appropriate for generating alchemical consumables, particularly [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|bombs]] and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixirs]], though they might also be used to generate [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical foods]]. This garden requires a full [[Equipment/Alchemist's Lab|alchemist's lab]] in addition to the other requirements mentioned previously, though the alchemist can still make use of that lab when generating alchemical items that aren't part of the daily consumables produced by the garden.

 When using [[Actions/Earn Income]] to help defray the costs of creating this kind of garden, both [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Ooze Lore]] are appropriate skills.
