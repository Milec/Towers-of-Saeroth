# Building a Simplified Ancestry Character  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 194

When choosing an ancestry for a simplified ancestry character, you gain the ancestry’s normal abilities at 1st level, choose a heritage, and gain the appropriate lore feat (Dwarven [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] for dwarves, for example) as your ancestry feat. Simplified ancestry characters never gain ancestry feats beyond that first lore feat. If you want to keep the power level of your game consistent, you can replace the ancestry feats gained at higher levels with general feats.
