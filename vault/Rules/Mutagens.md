# Mutagens  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 546

These elixirs, indicated by the [[Traits/Mutagen|mutagen]] trait, temporarily transmogrify the subject's body and alter its mind. Typically, only alchemists have the expertise to craft mutagens, and some say they are the only ones reckless enough to use them. A mutagen always conveys one or more beneficial effects (listed in the Benefit entry) and one or more detrimental effects (shown in the Drawback entry). Mutagens are [[Rules/Reading Spells|polymorph effects]], and a subsequent polymorph effect attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an existing effect; the counteract check for a mutagen uses the item's level and a modifier equal to that level's DC – 10, as found on Table 10–5: DCs by Level.
