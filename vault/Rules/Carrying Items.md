# Carrying Items  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 267

A character carries items in three ways: held, worn, and stowed. **Held items** are in your hands; a character typically has two hands, allowing them to hold an item in each hand or a single two-handed item using both hands. **Worn items** are tucked into pockets, belt pouches, bandoliers, weapon sheaths, and so forth, and they can be retrieved and returned relatively quickly. **Stowed items** are in a [[Equipment/Backpack|backpack]] or a similar container, and they are more difficult to access.
