# Casting Spells from a Wand  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 282

A wand contains a spell that can be cast once per day. Casting a spell from a wand requires holding the wand in one hand and activating the item with a [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] activity using the normal number of actions for the spell.

To cast a spell from a wand, it must be on your spell list. Because you're the one casting the spell, use your spell attack modifier and spell DC. The spell is of your spellcasting tradition.

If a spell requires a [[Rules/Costs and Loci|locus]], you must still have that locus to cast the spell from a wand, and if the spell has a cost, you must still pay that cost to cast the spell from a wand.
