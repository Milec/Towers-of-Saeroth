# Construct Companions  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 32

A construct companion is a loyal, semi-sentient construct who follows your orders obediently and is roughly as intelligent as an animal. Your construct companion has the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait, and you gain the Command a Construct action.

If your companion is destroyed, you can spend 1 day of downtime and attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check with a high DC for your level. On a success, you rebuild your companion. You can have only one construct companion at a time, and you can have either a construct companion or an [[Rules/Animal Companions|animal companion]], but not both.
