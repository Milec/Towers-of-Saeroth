# Observed  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 434

In most circumstances, you can sense creatures without difficulty and target them normally. Creatures in this state are [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]]. Observing requires a [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]], which for most creatures means sight. If you can’t observe the creature, it’s either [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]], and you’ll need to factor in the targeting restrictions. Even if a creature is observed, it might still be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]].
