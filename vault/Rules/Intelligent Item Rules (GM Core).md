# Intelligent Item Rules  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 304

Every intelligent item has the [[Traits/Intelligent (GM Core)|intelligent]] trait. Intelligent items can’t be crafted by normal means—typically an accident, a divine act, or a major sacrifice on the part of the creator is required to grant the item the mental essence it needs for sentience, and in some rare cases the spiritual essence it needs to have a soul of its own. Because of this, intelligent items are always [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|rare]] or [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|unique]]. The normal statistics and rules for wearing or using an item of its type still apply to an intelligent item. In addition, intelligent items have a few statistics other items lack.
