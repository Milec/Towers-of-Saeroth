# Step 1: Collect Taxes  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 538

You can [[Actions/Collect Taxes]] once per Kingdom turn to attempt to bolster your Economy-based checks for the remainder of the Kingdom turn. If you don't attempt to Collect Taxes, you can instead attempt a DC 11 flat check; on a success, reduce Unrest by 1.
