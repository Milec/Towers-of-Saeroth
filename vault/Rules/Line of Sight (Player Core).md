# Line of Sight  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 427

Some effects require you to have line of sight to your target. As long as you can precisely sense the area (as described in [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|Precise Senses]]) and it is not blocked by a solid barrier (as described in [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|Cover]]), you have line of sight. An area of [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] prevents line of sight if you don’t have [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]], but portcullises and other obstacles that aren’t totally solid do not. Usually a 1-foot-square gap is enough to maintain line of sight, though the GM makes the final call.
