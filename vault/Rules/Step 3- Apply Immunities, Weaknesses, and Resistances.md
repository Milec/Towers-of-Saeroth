# Step 3: Apply Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 407

Defenses against certain types of damage or effects are called immunities or resistances, while vulnerabilities are called weaknesses. Apply immunities first, then weaknesses, and resistances third. See [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance]].
