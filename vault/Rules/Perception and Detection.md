# Perception and Detection  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 399

Your **[[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception modifier]]** indicates how good you are at noticing things around you. You typically use the **[[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]]** basic action to find physical things or the **[[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]]** basic action in social situations. While in exploration mode, the **[[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Search]]** activity lets you keep an eye out for things around you.

Four main conditions indicate how well you can pinpoint and target a creature: **[[Rules/Observed (Player Core)|observed]]**, **[[Rules/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]]**, **[[Rules/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]**, and **[[Rules/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]]**. A creature with the **[[Rules/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]]** or **[[Rules/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]]** condition is harder to find and target.
