# Take Cover  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 13

You’ll often need to determine whether someone can [[Actions/Take Cover]]. They usually just need a large enough object to hide behind. Imagine the character crouching, and picture whether the object could almost entirely cover up their silhouette. Taking Cover might also require them to Drop Prone, such as if they want to take cover under a table. Most of the time, you can let them combine this with the Take Cover action instead of using 2 separate actions.
