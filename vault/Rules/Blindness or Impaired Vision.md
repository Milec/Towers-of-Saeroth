# Blindness or Impaired Vision  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 22

A blind character can't detect anything using vision, critically fails Perception checks requiring sight, is immune to [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effects, and can't be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]]. You might give this character the [[Feats/Blind-Fight (Player Core)|Blind-Fight]] feat for free.

A character with impaired vision might take a –2 to –4 penalty to vision-based Perception checks. Spectacles or other corrective devices might reduce or remove this, which can typically be found in most major settlements.
