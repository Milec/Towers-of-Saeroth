# Proficiencies  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 32

Your construct companion is trained in its unarmed attacks, unarmored defense, all saving throws, Perception, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. Construct companions can't use abilities that require greater Intelligence, such as [[Actions/Coerce]] or [[Actions/Decipher Writing]], even if trained in the appropriate skill, unless they have an ability that allows it.
