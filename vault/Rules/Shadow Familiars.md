# Shadow Familiars  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 228

Familiars bind themselves to mortals in a symbiotic relationship. Shadowcaster witches are among the most likely to obtain a familiar with the power of shadow, particularly those with the night patron. Others likely to take on [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Kinspeech|shadow familiars]] include [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Shadow|shadow]] bloodline [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerers]] and [[Magic/Domains/Darkness|darkness]] domain [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|clerics]].
