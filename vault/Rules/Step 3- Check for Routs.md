# Step 3: Check for Routs  *Rules*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 578

At the end of the round, after every army has acted, there's a chance that armies might rout. An army whose HP is at or below its Rout Threshold must attempt a Morale check; the DC is equal to the highest Morale DC among the remaining enemy armies. On a critical success, that army no longer has to check for routs at this step for the remainder of the encounter (but it can still become routed from other effects). On a failure, the army increases the value of its [[Rules/Conditions/Shaken|shaken]] condition by 1. On a critical failure, the army becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Routed|routed]].
