**Category** Transitive Planes

**Native Inhabitants** shades (untethered), [[Bestiary/Shining Child (Monster Core)|shining children]]

---

The Silver Sea surrounds the [[Rules/Inner Sphere Planes (GM Core)|planes of the Inner Sphere]], separating them from those of the [[Rules/Outer Sphere Planes (GM Core)|Outer Sphere]]. The Astral Plane provides the backdrop against which the River of Souls flows from [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], ushering departed spirits toward the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]] for final judgment. Far from an empty void, the Astral’s silver substance churns with currents and storms from the metaphysical heat of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]], and where it touches the chaos of the [[Setting/Planes/Maelstrom (GM Core)|Maelstrom]], the resulting eddies interact with the memories of the dead to produce fleeting simulacra and even demiplanes.

The River of Souls draws the attention of soul-hunting [[Bestiary/Families/Daemon (Monster Core)|daemons]] and opportunistic [[Bestiary/Night Hag|night hags]] like sharks drawn to the scent of blood. Led by [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomps]], a cross section of nearly every type of [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] and [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]] in existence, along with some [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], defends the proper flow of souls against such predators. Running opposite the River of Souls is the flow of raw quintessence spun off from the so-called Antipode, channeled by aeons back toward the [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge|Creation’s Forge]].

Travelers within the Astral find the plane untouched by the passage of time, a property exploited by many mortals fearing old age. Time, however, isn’t easily escaped, and upon exiting the Astral Plane, a creature finds this debt catching up to them, potentially aging to dust in moments.
