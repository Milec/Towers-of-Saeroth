# Outer Rifts  *Plane*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 181

**Category** Outer Sphere Planes

**Divinities** Camazotz, [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords|demon lords]], [[Setting/Deities/Droskar]], [[Setting/Deities/Ghlaunder]], goblin hero-gods, [[Setting/Deities/Gyronna]], [[Setting/Deities/Lady Nanbyo (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lady Nanbyo]], [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]], nascent demon lords, [[Bestiary/Families/Qlippoth (Monster Core)|qlippoth lords]]

**Native Inhabitants** [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]], shades (larvae), [[Bestiary/Families/Qlippoth (Monster Core)|qlippoth]]

---

Like a corrosive rot in the roots of the [[Rules/Outer Sphere Planes (GM Core)|Outer Sphere]], or an antithetical, rival reality run aground into the [[Setting/Planes/Maelstrom (GM Core)|Maelstrom]] at the dawn of time, the Outer Rifts is a place of horror and destruction fed by mortal sin. Each of its innumerable regions is a unique iteration of chaos and malice, each with its own terrible and twisted environment, with one driving maxim: the strong survive, while the weak suffer and are destroyed. Ruled by demons, and before them by the alien, nightmarish [[Bestiary/Families/Qlippoth (Monster Core)|qlippoth]], the Outer Rifts and its native beings seek only to ruin and destroy. Though [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]] routinely rampage out into other planes when the Outer Rifts manifests a great planar rift, the denizens of the Outer Rifts are fractured and self-destructive. Demon lords such as [[Setting/Deities/Dagon]], [[Setting/Deities/Pazuzu]], and [[Setting/Deities/Zura]], and even gods including the ascended demon lord [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu, the Mother of Monsters]], fight for dominion over the Outer Rifts as much or more than they threaten the other planes.

Though dangerous, not every realm of the Outer Rifts is immediately hostile to the prepared traveler. Shamira’s city of Alushinyrra in the Midnight Isles and [[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]]’s library-city of Diovengia in the realm of [[Bestiary/Pleroma (Monster Core)|Pleroma]] are each welcoming in their own dark ways. Even those who survive a journey through the Outer Rifts, however, inevitably must resist being deeply corrupted by the experience.
