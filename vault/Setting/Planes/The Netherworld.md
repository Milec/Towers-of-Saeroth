# The Netherworld  *Plane*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 178

**Category** Transitive Planes

**Divinities** [[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|velstrac demagogues]], [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]]

**Native Inhabitants** [[Bestiary/D'ziriak|d'ziriaks]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Fetchling Settlements|kayals (fetchlings)]], shades (the mutilated), [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Umbral|umbral dragons]], [[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|velstracs]]

---

A murky, distorted, and imperfect mirror of [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], the Netherworld overlaps the Universe and serves as a buffer or conduit between it and [[Setting/Planes/The Void|the Void]]. The Netherworld exists in a state of perpetually dim half-light, the landscape containing similar features to the overlapping Universe, but in warped or twisted fashions. Cities in the Universe might exist in the Netherworld, sometimes in ruins and sometimes as terrible, frightening replicas. The darkness also holds points of beauty and relative safety, such as the great city of Shadow Absalom. The bleak doppelganger of Golarion’s own Absalom hosts cross-planar trade facilitated by a permanent, one-way portal out of the gloom.

The Netherworld is populated by dark, altered versions of creatures from the Universe, many of them immigrants that have adapted to the shadow after being trapped in the realm for generations. The shadow natives known as [[Rules/Sidebars/Fetchling Settlements|kayals]]—also called fetchlings—were originally [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|humans]] before thousands of years of exposure to the Netherworld and intermarriage with strange beings forever altered them. [[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|Velstracs]], who long ago fled from [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]], have since adopted the Netherworld as their home, spreading their horrific message of perfection through mutilation in service to their victim and patron, the god [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]]. In contrast, [[Bestiary/D'ziriak|d’ziriaks]] originated within the Netherworld itself, as did the fearsome [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Umbral|umbral dragons]] ruling self-crafted fiefdoms within the shadows. Greatest of them is Argrinyxia, who rules over Shadow Absalom.
