The Plane of Air, innermost of the Elemental Planes, is a vast realm of wind, storms, and skies. Illuminated by great artificial globes of flame and distant starlight from [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the material universe]], the plane is populated by [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Air (Monster Core)|air elementals]], [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud|dragons]], [[Bestiary/Air Scamp|air scamps]], and a great empire of [[Bestiary/Jaathoom|jaathoom genies]]. Though mostly clouds and empty skies, the plane isn’t entirely bereft of solid ground, including rock and ice created by the residents or dragged into their realm from the distant [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]] or neighboring [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water (GM Core)|Plane of Water]]. The returning pockets of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] have let loose giant pieces of driftwood into the plane as well. Bizarre, drifting spheres of brass and iron make for the most convenient navigational aids; the former housing the cities of the vast Jaathoom Empire, the latter almost entirely abandoned and shunned by the plane’s inhabitants, who believe them cursed, entrapping forgotten, ancient enemies who once ravaged the plane.

The jaathooms rule from their shining capital city of Armun Kelisk, built atop a series of seven floating islands. Their vast trade network crisscrosses the skies and ventures to other planes, kept aloft by natural and magical flight, including great [[Equipment/Vehicles/Airship (GM Core)|airships]] that allow visitors to quickly and safely traverse the skies. The jaathooms are welcoming and gracious hosts to extraplanar travelers and adventurers, a perspective not shared by [[Setting/Deities/Hshurha|Hshurha the Duchess of All Winds]], one of the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords|elemental lords]] of air.
