# Axis  *Plane*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 179

**Category** Outer Sphere Planes

**Divinities** [[Setting/Deities/Abadar (Divine Mysteries)|Abadar]], [[Setting/Deities/Brigh]], [[Setting/Deities/Chaldira]], halfling pantheon, [[Setting/Deities/Irori (Divine Mysteries)|Irori]], [[Setting/Deities/Lissala]], [[Setting/Deities/Milani]], [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber]], primal monitors

**Native Inhabitants** [[Bestiary/Families/Aeon (Monster Core)|aeons]], shades (remade)

---

Axis is a realm of pure, absolute order, unhindered by moral concerns. The plane takes the form of a vast, gleaming, perfectly structured city. Built at the base of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]]’s Spire, the Perfect City is a bulwark against the chaos of the [[Setting/Planes/Maelstrom (GM Core)|Maelstrom]] and the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]], with vast mechanical armies marching forth to explore, define, and pacify an imperfect, unruly universe. Axis is also home to [[Bestiary/Axiomite (Monster Core)|axiomites]]: beings composed of living mathematics and equations. Axiomites continue to work on their organization of the cosmos but increasingly turn their attentions to the planar city of Axis itself; as with any city, Axis requires nonstop maintenance and improvement in order to resist the march of time.

Axis’s natives are far from the only inhabitants of their cosmopolitan realm. [[Bestiary/Families/Devil (Monster Core)|Devils]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Archon (Monster Core)|archons]] often visit along with shades, mortal travelers, and smaller numbers of most every other kind of extraplanar being. [[Setting/Deities/Abadar (Divine Mysteries)|Abadar]] and other deities make their homes in the realm. The empty, former domain of Aroden languishes here, while below the streets lies [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber’s]] domain, a labyrinthine undercity.
