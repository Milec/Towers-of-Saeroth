# Boneyard  *Plane*

**Traits** [[Traits/Timeless (GM Core)|Timeless]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 179

**Category** Outer Sphere Planes

**Divinities** [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek]], [[Setting/Deities/Groetus]], [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomp ushers]], [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]]

**Native Inhabitants** shades (the dead), [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomps]]

---

The Boneyard spans an impossibly tall and ever-growing spire of gleaming quintessence that rises up into the silver void of the [[Setting/Planes/Astral Plane (GM Core)|Astral Plane]]. As the destination of the River of Souls, the Boneyard is where the souls of the mortal dead arrive for judgment and for [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]] and her psychopomp servitors to direct them to their respective afterlives. Pharasma’s domain is separated into eight courts, each corresponding to one of the other planes of the [[Rules/Outer Sphere Planes (GM Core)|Outer Sphere]] and collecting the souls due to that plane. Not every soul goes unchallenged, and proxies of gods and planes argue and debate over souls, with final arbitration conducted by Pharasma herself.

While Pharasma rules absolutely within the Boneyard, she isn’t the only divinity there. The demigod [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomp]] ushers dwell within the plane in service to her, and the deity [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek]] dwells below at the spire’s root. Looming high above as a skull-faced moon waits [[Setting/Deities/Groetus]], the god of the end times, his orbit slowly decaying in minuscule iterations supposedly counting down to the last days of the cosmos.

Pharasma’s courts and the surrounding Graveyard of Souls are not the only features atop the spire. Beyond the graveyard, the Spirelands manifest environments much like those of the varied worlds of [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], filled with the souls of neutral shades who have nowhere else to go as they are unclaimed by any gods and did nothing in life to suggest any particular ethos.
