# The Universe  *Plane*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 174

**Category** Inner Sphere Planes

**Divinities** Dominion of the Black, Gozreh

**Native Inhabitants** [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|dwarves]], [[Ancestries/Elf (Player Core)|elves]], [[Ancestries/Halfling (Player Core)|halflings]], [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|humans]], [[Ancestries/Gnome (Player Core)|gnomes]], [[Ancestries/Goblin (Player Core)|goblins]], and countless other ancestries

---

The Universe is the prosaic realm and the home of mortal life. Innumerable galaxies play host to countless stars and their planets, each housing unique settings for any campaign, with Golarion as the classic example. Other worlds of note like Castrovel, Akiton, Triaxus, and Eox exist within Golarion’s own solar system, and then beyond this, orbiting other distant stars or in other galaxies still, worlds such as Androffa and even Earth swirl within the Universe’s vast and silent void.

Yet, for all the profound wonder and diversity of life that the Universe houses, strange alien gods and the sinister collective of malevolent beings known as the [[Bestiary/Families/Dominion of the Black]] lurk in the dark places between the stars known as the Dark Tapestry.

The Universe serves as the destination for pre-incarnate souls, each mortal life born, living, and dying before sending its spirit toward the [[Rules/Outer Sphere Planes (GM Core)|planes of the Outer Sphere]] for judgment. The Universe is likewise the focus of the gods, each of whom is invested in fostering their own particular portfolio among mortal worshippers and the material world at large.
