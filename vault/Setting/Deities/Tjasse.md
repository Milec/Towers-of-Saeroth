# Tjasse (Lord of Talons)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 310

Tjasse, the father of [[Setting/Deities/Skode]], is the [[Bestiary/Cloud Giant (Monster Core)|cloud giant]] god and lord of rocs, wind, and high mountain peaks. He is credited for taming rocs to serve cloud giants and possessing shapeshifting abilities second only to [[Setting/Deities/Skrymir]] (much to his chagrin). Stories of his incredible arrogance precede him and most of his accolades, closely followed by cautionary tales of those unfortunate enough to have provoked his ire. The only thing that surpasses his vanity is his love for Skode, and the only thing greater than the wrath incurred by those who cross him is the destructive force he unleashes upon those who wrong his daughter. The Lord of Talons is a colossal being, dwarfing many members of the giant pantheon in comparison. He is always described as strikingly beautiful, though the exact details of his appearance change frequently.

Followers of Tjasse are often those who call the mountains their home, as well as roc tamers and riders who look to him to ensure the winds are in their favor when they take flight. There are a few who turn to him when seeking power to exact revenge, trusting that he understands and shares their need for decisive retaliation.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Giant Gods]]
