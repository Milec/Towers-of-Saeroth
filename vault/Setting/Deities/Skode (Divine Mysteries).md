# Skode (The Horizon Huntress)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 310

Skode, the daughter of [[Setting/Deities/Tjasse]], is the huntress goddess of giants. She is portrayed as a stout and muscular cloud giant woman with her face and arms decorated with intricate tattoos, dressed in leathers and furs. She is well-known for her skills with a longbow and spear, and her intolerance for evil. Skode is wed to [[Setting/Deities/Aegirran]], her might and passion having won his heart, but is separated from him by land and sea. The two are only ever able to embrace one another on the changing of seasons. For the remainder of the year, she forever stalks the land with the three sons she had with Aegirran (the bitter north winds), hunting the finest animals while seeking to destroy the wicked with arrows or spears aimed true. Like [[Setting/Deities/Fandarra]] and [[Setting/Deities/Bergelmir]], she is one of the few giant deities who have a significant following of non-giants as well, largely among the Kellid people in the Realm of the Mammoth Lords.

Though a fair amount of her followers are hunters and the clans who rely on them for food, a great portion are young adult giants beginning coming-of-age rites, or those attempting to earn the respect or even the affection of another. A devotee of the Horizon Huntress can often be identified by the presence of her religious symbol on their hunting equipment, or by taking note of the game they hunt, as ardent followers only ever have the strongest of prey in their sights.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Giant Gods]]
