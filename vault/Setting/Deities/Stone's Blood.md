# Stone's Blood  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 244

Many dwarves choose to venerate the entire pantheon of dwarven gods as one, colloquially known as Stone’s Blood. These deities function as a family unit, with Torag as the patriarch. They get along as well as any family might, disagreeing on many matters but always cooperating for the good of the whole. Worship of Stone’s Blood includes all aspects of dwarven life, and devotees are among the strongest pillars of any dwarven community.

There are a couple of popular alternative versions of Stone’s Blood that slightly alter the pantheon. The Skykeepers exclude Droskar, as this god of unending toil refused to witness the sky with the other dwarven deities. Devotees of the Skykeepers believe he forsook his divine ancestry with this act and is no longer worthy of worship. The Forgekin Pact takes this one step further by also excluding Torag. Followers of the Forgekin Pact consider that as the Father of Creation, Torag is far too busy to bother with guiding everyday dwarven life. The rest of the Stone’s Blood pantheon serve those particular duties. In practice, worship of either of these alternate pantheons is functionally the same as that of Stone’s Blood.
