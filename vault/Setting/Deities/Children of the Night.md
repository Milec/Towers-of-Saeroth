# Children of the Night  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 242

Trust is the last thing you would assume to find at the heart of a pantheon of wicked, murderous gods, but for those who are part of the Children of the Night, it is paramount. As much as these gods might distrust one another, they understand that sometimes such trepidations must be overcome for a greater goal. For this tenuously connected group of immortal powers, that goal is to work together to protect their followers, usually beings who must, at all costs, protect their true natures: assassins, [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampires]], [[Bestiary/Werewolf (Monster Core)|werewolves]], and other creatures of the shadows.

For the mortal and immortal followers of this pantheon, honesty and solidarity are held in very high regard, and those that find themselves labeled traitors are quickly removed as threats. Thus, devotees of this pantheon are naturally hard to find, for even admitting that it exists is considered a betrayal. As such, the most common method of entry to worship is to be an already close confidant or even the offspring of a current practitioner.
