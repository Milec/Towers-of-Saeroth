# Laudinmio (The Sovereign of Alchemy)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 306

Laudinmio, the Sovereign of Alchemy, is the elemental lord of metal, alchemy, and experimentation. They once thrived on the quicksilver change of minerals and metals when correctly combined, seeking the most innovative of crafts, chemical reactions, and fashion. Incursions from [[Setting/Deities/Ayrzul]] have left Laudinmio in a tragic state, trapped in a nightmarish slumber, their short bouts of lucidity haunted by regret and confusion. Their realm on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]], known as the Euphonious Coalition, still houses their loyal subjects, who seek for means to ease Laudinmio’s torment.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords]]

**Edicts** Discover new alloys and concoctions, have multiple concurrent plans, innovate use of metals

**Anathema** Allow your creation to fall into malicious hands, destroy an alchemical formula

**Area of Concern**
Alchemy, discovery, experiments, metal, regret
