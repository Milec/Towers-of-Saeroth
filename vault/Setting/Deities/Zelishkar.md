# Zelishkar (The Bitter Flame)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 302

The Bitter Flame was originally forged by [[Setting/Deities/Szuriel]], the Rider of War, in her domain, the Cinder Furnace. She created Zelishkar as the embodiment of the funeral pyres that often follow in the wake of the wars she brings. The aggressive [[Bestiary/Families/Daemon (Monster Core)|daemonic]] feline performed his duties so well that Szuriel promoted him to harbinger. Though Zelishkar continues to personify the most sadistic uses of fire, he holds a spark of resentment in his cores that he owes all that he is to Szuriel and schemes to someday supplant her.

Zelishkar is shaped like a monstrous three-tongued [[Bestiary/Tiger (Monster Core)|tiger]] made of living crimson flames. His body is protected by a thin, durable cuirass crafted from obsidian. His eyes are jet black and emit a sinister orange glow.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Daemon Harbingers]]

**Edicts** Burn the remains of your defeated enemies and closest allies, destroy the accomplishments of your so-called betters, wield flame as a tool and a weapon

**Anathema** Accept the motivations of others at face value, extinguish a raging fire

**Area of Concern**
Arson, burning alive, cremation
