# Dammar (The Denied)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 314

In the Age of Creation, the first time a soul was plucked from the River of Souls and returned to life, a [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|psychopomp]] usher of life and resurrection emerged. Dammar the Denied is that first piece of resurrection magic made manifest, the psychopomp usher of life and journeys back from the Boneyard, and they ensure mortals don’t make that journey too often or too easily. Alongside souls returning from the grave, Dammar presides over medicine, doctors who stave off death before it even arrives, alcohol (particularly, though not exclusively, used in medical preparations), and the unyielding tenacity of cockroaches and other insects who look upon death and refuse.

On [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], Dammar is worshipped by gamblers, physicians, brewers, and vintners, and by those rare mortals who defy the laws of death. Boneyard lore holds that the Denied created hangovers for mortals so they could look beyond the veil of life and death. Their preferred form is stout and androgynous, face and body shrouded behind layers of opulent silks, with six insectile legs; they manifest arms only as they are needed, sometimes dozens at a time.
