# Luhar (The Setting Sun)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries Web Supplement]] pg. 18

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This deity grants [[Spells/Slither|_slither_]] instead of umbral journey_

The Lioness Goddess of death, dreams, and destiny. She is most commonly depicted as a lioness with the head of a human woman, dark skinned with bright eyes.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Mwangi Gods]]

**Edicts** Learn about the night and prepare yourself to face its creatures and dangers, always make time for sleeping and dreams, ensure others never go to sleep scared

**Anathema** Stay up all night without any breaks for sleeping or dreaming, attack a person or creature while they sleep, leave a badly wounded opponent alive and suffering, create undead or ask questions of the dead

**Area of Concern**
Death, dreams, destiny
