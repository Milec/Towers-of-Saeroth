# Sigek  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 293

_**Nethys Note: This deity entry contains spoilers for Season of Ghosts. Read at your own risk.**_

Evidence related to Sigek’s return as a nindoru Ascetic was only recently discovered in 7214 ic, when a group of Pathfinders defeated three ninsats targeting [[Ancestries/Samsaran|samsaran]] communities in Tianjing. Each ninsat had a lair where dozens of corpses were placed around blue statues that resembled a multi-mawed nindoru with human facial features. The nindorus were in a frenzy when the Pathfinders arrived and fought to the death while repeatedly chanting the phrase “Sigek will be born again!” All the statues were transported to Goka’s Lantern Lodge, where they remain under lock and key to this day.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Nindoru Ascetics]]

**Edicts** betray your superiors and allies, cause crippling pain in others, host parasites

**Anathema** cure parasitic infections in others, maintain an alliance longer than a year, restore a non-nindoru creature to life
