# Cyth-V’sug (Prince of the Blasted Heath)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Cyth-V’sug, Prince of the Blasted Heath, is the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords|demon lord]] of disease, fungus, and parasites. Originally a [[Bestiary/Families/Qlippoth (Monster Core)|qlippoth]] lord, he was exiled by his peers for accepting mortal worshippers. Transformed into one of the [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]] he despises, Cyth-V’sug seeks to devour all life to put an end to demons and, ultimately, himself. He most often appears as an enormous [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|draconic]] figure of snarled vines, fungal growths, and flailing tentacles. Cyth-V’sug is most often worshipped by recluses who seek to bring decay and destruction to their environs, though denizens of [[Rules/The Darklands|the Darklands]] also pay homage to him.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords]]

**Edicts** Corrupt all that exists with parasites or fungus, promote the growth of fungus, feast on rotten flesh or fungus

**Anathema** Purify your food, cure a disease or kill a parasite, tolerate another demon lord or their servants (except [[Setting/Deities/Treerazer (Divine Mysteries)|Treerazer]])

**Area of Concern**
Disease, fungus, parasites
