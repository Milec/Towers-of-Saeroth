# Guardians of the Sacred Self  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 243

The Guardians of the Sacred Self understand something that many others do not: that what a person believes about themselves far outweighs that which others may judge them for. While individually they support the differences of their worshippers, these eleven deities and demigods have come together to become better advocates for change, empowering their followers to do the same. They strive for a world in which everyone can feel safe and secure being who they truly are.

More so than many other pantheons, these deities understand the power of transformations, as well as the heartaches and loss they can bring. Devotees of the Guardians of the Sacred Self work to provide positive examples of embracing positive change, whether in themselves or within a community. They also make their healing abilities available to aid those whose local physicians haven’t received the proper teachings or are unwilling to listen.
