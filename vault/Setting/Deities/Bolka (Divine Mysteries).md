# Bolka (The Golden Gift)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Bolka is the dwarven goddess of marriage. Forged by [[Setting/Deities/Torag]] as a symbol of his love for [[Setting/Deities/Folgrit]], Bolka's golden-brown skin, eyes of pure bismuth, and honey-blonde hair were a symbol of the passion and beauty he saw in Folgrit. When Folgrit received this gift, she immediately breathed life into the child, and claimed Bolka to be a symbol of their marriage and eternal commitment.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Dwarven Gods]]

**Edicts** Encourage those seeking love, see the beauty in others, support others’ relationships

**Anathema** Betray your spouse, disrupt a genuine marriage, prevent a suitor from seeking a partner

**Area of Concern**
Committed relationships, marriage, passion

**Pantheons**
[[Setting/Deities/Stone's Blood]]
