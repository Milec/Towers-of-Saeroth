# Cosmic Caravan  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 242

Astronomers and astrologers know that the Cosmic Caravan is a collection of constellations that form a ring around the star Cynosure. The diverse array of deities and demigods of the Cosmic Caravan pantheon are associated with these and other celestial bodies, as well as the spaces between them. Desna and Sarenrae; the empyreal lords Ashava, Black Butterfly, and Pulura; the elven god Ketephys; and the outer god Yog-Sothoth come together to advocate the hope for the future that one feels while looking up at the night sky. At first, the moon god [[Setting/Deities/Groetus]] was part of this pantheon, but his function as the harbinger of the end of the world put him at odds with the Cosmic Caravan’s outlook. He has since been replaced with the Tian deity Tsukiyo, the Prince of the Moon.

Worshippers of the Cosmic Caravan are stargazers and fortune-tellers who look to the arrangement of constellations during auspicious times for guidance. This pantheon originally rose to prominence in western Avistan, particularly in Varisia, Nidal, and Ravounel, but has spread farther. In Nidal, devotees stand against those who worship the cruel [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]] and espouse the idea that night is a time for evil.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Pantheons]]

**Edicts** aid those who live in regions where Zon-Kuthon (or other religions that espouse the night as a bastion for evil) holds sway, help the desperate or forlorn to see potential for a better life in the future, spend time stargazing or meditating in moonlight, travel with no particular destination in mind

**Anathema** destroy astronomical or astrological equipment, portray the night as a time of evil, spend the night in the place twice in a row (such as the same room in an inn or the same natural clearing)

**Area of Concern**
constellations, hope for a better tomorrow, fortune telling, the night
