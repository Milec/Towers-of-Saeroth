# Dwarven Pantheon  *Deity*

**Traits** [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|LG]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 92

The dwarven gods are one large family, with [[Setting/Deities/Torag]] as its patriarch. He is joined by his brothers [[Setting/Deities/Magrim]] (the Taskmaster) and [[Setting/Deities/Angradd]] (the Forge Fire), his half-sister [[Setting/Deities/Dranngvit]] (the Debt Minder), and his wife [[Setting/Deities/Folgrit]] (the Watchful Mother). His children are [[Setting/Deities/Bolka]] (the Golden Gift), [[Setting/Deities/Grundinnar]] (the Peacemaker), [[Setting/Deities/Kols]] (the Oath-Keeper), and [[Setting/Deities/Trudd]] (the Mighty). Torag’s evil former student [[Setting/Deities/Droskar]] (the Dark Smith), though technically part of the dwarven pantheon, is rarely invoked by any save duergars.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Pantheons]]

**Edicts** develop skills useful to your community (especially crafting, mining, and trading), honor your ancestors through traditions and rituals

**Anathema** dishonor your family, willingly break a contract or oath, irreparably damage an ancestral relic

**Area of Concern**
