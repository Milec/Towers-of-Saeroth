# Abrogail (The Thrice-Damned Queen)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 204

They might have interrupted Abrogail II’s ritual, limiting the borders of her infernal domain, but they couldn’t stop Asmodeus from transforming the infernal majestrix into an [[Setting/Deity Categories/Archdevils|archdevil]]. Compared to even her considerable spellcasting prowess as a mortal, she now commands extraordinary magic and physical might. Further, many in House Thrune have begun leveraging their lineage, be that in worshipping their divine cousin or founding mystery cults like Thrune Ascendant to awaken their shared bloodline and apotheosize.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Archdevils]]

**Edicts** gain power through schemes and quick wits, manipulate people to do what you want

**Anathema** be merciful to those who wrong you, compromise on your ambitions, show your power through sheer strength

**Area of Concern**
ambition, rivalry, schemes, statecraft
