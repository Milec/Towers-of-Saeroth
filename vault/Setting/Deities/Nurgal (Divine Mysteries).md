# Nurgal (The Shining Scourge)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Nurgal, the Shining Scourge, is the demon lord of deserts, senseless warfare, and the sun. He was formerly a fully-fledged deity of ancient Azlant, but fell to demigodhood after his defeat in combat. Nurgal represents the sun’s potential for devastation, and his followers venerate him out of cowed awe. The demon lord appears as a muscular, tanned man with the head and lower body of a golden [[Bestiary/Lion (Monster Core)|lion]] and a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon’s]] tail. He is almost always depicted as wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|mace]] in the form of a miniature sun, held in a taloned hand. Nurgal’s worshippers are primarily found in the deserts of Garund, Ninshabur, and Qadira.
