# Set (Lord of the Dark Desert)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries Web Supplement]] pg. 6

Set is the most reviled of all the Ancient Osirian pantheon. He is the encroaching desert, the invader, the sandstorm destroying everything in its path, as well as the dead who rise from their graves.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Ancient Osirian Gods]]

**Edicts** Bring chaos to society, murder those who stand in your way, defeat your foes with personal strength and cunning, kill [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]]

**Anathema** Permit the spread of the desert to fertile lands, refuse to fight your own battles, aid [[Setting/Deities/Apep]] or other omnicidal gods

**Area of Concern**
Darkness, deserts, murder, storms
