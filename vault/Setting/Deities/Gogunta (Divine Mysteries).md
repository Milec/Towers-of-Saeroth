# Gogunta (Song of the Swamp)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Gogunta, Song of the Swamp, is the demon lord of amphibians, [[Traits/Boggard (Monster Core)|boggards]], and swamps. Gogunta is worshipped as a goddess by boggards, who believe her to be an ascended [[Bestiary/Mobogo|mobogo]], though scholars suspect she was a former [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]] who gained the favor of [[Setting/Deities/Dagon (Divine Mysteries)|Dagon]]. Lending credence to this latter theory, her realm, a stinking salt marsh, is located within Dagon’s oceanic realm. Gogunta appears as an enormous, multi-headed frog with dozens of eyes and even more tongues, though boggards typically depict her as a titanic boggard queen.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords]]

**Edicts** sacrifice creatures by drowning them, frolic or sing in swamps, feed or aid amphibians

**Anathema** grant mercy to boggards who worship other gods

**Area of Concern**
amphibians, boggards, and swamps
