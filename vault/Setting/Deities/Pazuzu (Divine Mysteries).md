# Pazuzu (King of the Wind Demons)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Pazuzu, King of the Wind Demons, is the demon lord of the sky, temptation, and winged creatures. He counts himself among the most ancient of demon lords, though his constant warring with [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]] has hindered his accumulation of power to the point of denying him godhood. He is exceptionally active in meddling in mortal affairs and takes great pleasure in possessing and corrupting good-hearted folk who invoke his name. Pazuzu appears as a humanoid figure with [[Bestiary/Families/Eagle (Monster Core)|eagle’s]] talons, two pairs of bird wings, a [[Bestiary/Families/Scorpion (Monster Core)|scorpion]] tail, and an avian [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demonic]] head. He is worshipped by [[Bestiary/Harpy (Monster Core)|harpies]], other evil winged creatures, and by countless [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|champions]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-646)|clerics]] who fell from grace at his temptations.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords]]

**Edicts** Tempt others to immoral acts, revel in flight, possess or magically influence others to cause calamities

**Anathema** Deny a flying creature the ability to fly, abuse Pazuzu’s name or call on Pazuzu for help, aid worshippers of [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]]

**Area of Concern**
The sky, temptation, winged creatures
