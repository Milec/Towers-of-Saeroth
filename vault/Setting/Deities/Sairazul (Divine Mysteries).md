# Sairazul (The Crystalline Queen)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 306

Sairazul, the Crystalline Queen, is the elemental lord of caves, earth, and gems. Planar scholars who know of the benevolent lord of earth speak of Sairazul as a mother and creator who gave birth to numerous planar creatures, including agrawghs and crysmals. Though many of her progeny were stolen away from her while she was imprisoned, her return has heralded widespread worship on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]], and even among mortal cultists who exalt creation and reproduction.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords]]

**Edicts** Aid childbirths, care for Sairazul’s children, mine responsibly, shelter others within stone and earth

**Anathema** Collapse an earthen structure on a creature, damage subterranean natural wonders

**Area of Concern**
Bounties of the earth, caves, fertility, gems
