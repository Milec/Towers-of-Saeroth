# Baphomet (Lord of the Labyrinth)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 302

Baphomet, Lord of the Labyrinth, is the demon lord of beasts, dungeons, and traitors. Baphomet was originally a consort of [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]] who achieved demon lord status after escaping from imprisonment in a labyrinth constructed by [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus (Divine Mysteries)|Asmodeus]]. Baphomet appears as an enormous emaciated [[Bestiary/Families/Minotaur|minotaur]] with feathered wings and a goat-like head that bears three horns, as well as a blazing pentagram branded into his forehead. As a lord of beasts, he grants his blessings to wild animals (like [[Bestiary/Families/Bear (Monster Core)|bears]], [[Bestiary/Families/Dinosaur (Monster Core)|dinosaurs]], and [[Bestiary/Families/Shark (Monster Core)|sharks]]) who kill humanoids, whether accidentally or intentionally. Baphomet’s cults are among the most prolific in [[Rules/Golarion (Player Core)|Golarion]]—human-dominated secret societies devoted to the demon lord are present in many cities and may have members ensconced in positions of political power.
