# Venkelvore (Most Glorious Neverfull)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 312

Among [[Ancestries/Goblin (Player Core)|goblins]], Venkelvore is considered the most beautiful of gods. Many goblins depict her as a fellow goblin rather than her true form: a [[Bestiary/Families/Barghest|barghest]] with pale blue fur and a trio of tails. Venkelvore has an eternal hunger that’s only satiated by consuming her enemies. She prefers to eat her meals while still alive, as she not only feeds on the meat but also the prey’s suffering, which Venkelvore considers a fantastic side dish.

Among the many ways to venerate Venkelvore, her followers believe that the best way to impress her is by tying up as many prisoners as they can during raids and burning them alive, savoring them as they cook or as part of a celebration once the pillaging ends. Her high priests say that the screams of agony please Venkelvore, who rewards those who follow her with more teeth, or sharpening their teeth and claws, allowing these followers to eat their next meal with greater ease.

Goblins who venerate the Most Glorious Neverfull sometimes worship [[Setting/Deities/Lurlup]] as well. They dedicate their lives to eating everything they can, trying to satiate the hunger of both of their gods. These voracious individuals tend to live isolated lives, as most other goblins tend to find their habits off-putting.
