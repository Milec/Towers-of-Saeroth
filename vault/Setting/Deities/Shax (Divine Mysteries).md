# Shax (The Blood Marquis)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Shax, the Blood Marquis, is the demon lord of envy, lies, and sadistic murders. Shax’s capacity for cruelty is legendary even among the ranks of demon lords, and he takes extreme pleasure in watching the last light of hope fade from the eyes of his victims. He is also known for creating [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]], and those whom he personally flays and corrupts with chthonian influence remain exemplars of their kind. Shax appears as a [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|human]] man with a dove’s head, feet, and wings, carrying countless knives and other weapons all over his body. Shax is most frequently worshipped by sadists, lone murderers, and serial killers.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords]]

**Edicts** Plot and commit murders, tell lies, torture creatures

**Anathema** Sleep in a building with fewer than five rooms, allow a victim to escape due to gloating

**Area of Concern**
Envy, lies, murder
