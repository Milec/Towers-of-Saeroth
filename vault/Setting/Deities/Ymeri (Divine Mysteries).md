# Ymeri (Queen of the Inferno)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 306

Ymeri, Queen of the Inferno, holds dominion over fire, heat, and smoke. Ymeri governs from her Auroric Palace, taking the form of a six-armed reptilian centaur with burning wings. She has systematically destroyed all record of her birth and true origins, and she wages a never-ending war against the other denizens of the Plane of Fire and against the jabalis of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]]. Of all the elemental lords, Ymeri has the largest following. Some scamps and many fire elementals revere her, alongside some [[Bestiary/Ifrit|ifrits]] and a few other creatures of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]], though few munsahirs honor her name, and a covert order of ifrits known as the Secret Fire is opposed to her rule. On Golarion, the Queen of the Inferno is worshipped primarily by fire wizards, arsonists, and [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragons]], especially cinder dragons.
