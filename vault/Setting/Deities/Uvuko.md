# Uvuko (The Diamond Ring)  *Deity*

**Traits** [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|CG]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 142

The god Uvuko dances across the land, his body forming the boundary between earth and sky. His twisting tail churns the air into clouds, his creations spreading water to all those who thirst. His sliding scales till the earth, bringing forth plants that are nourished by Uvuko's rains. The worship of Uvuko is widespread in the Mwangi Expanse, found among many [[Rules/Sidebars/Droon|lizardfolk]] tribes, Mbe'ke and Taralu [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarves]], and in the more cosmopolitan cities.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Mwangi Gods]]

**Edicts** embrace change and the future, master adversity with flexibility, foster freedom and progress for others
