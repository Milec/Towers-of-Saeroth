# Urazra (Breaker of Bones)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 310

Faith in Urazra is relatively new, only coming about within the past few centuries. However, his following has grown rapidly among the young [[Bestiary/Stone Giant (Monster Core)|stone]] and [[Bestiary/Marsh Giant (Monster Core)|marsh giants]] of a more violent incline. Even in the short amount of time that he has been a part of the giant pantheon, there have already been countless tales of his barbarity. In all of them, he is described as a blood-splattered stone giant man with a burly build, pauldrons fashioned from deer or moose skulls, and a jagged onyx-black greatsword with a length that rivals his height.

Followers of the Breaker of Bones are known as the [[Bestiary/Families/Bear (Monster Core)|Bear]] Cult, a faith that is quickly and effectively spreading thanks to their charismatic preachers and barbarians claiming their powerful rage is from the god himself. Many stone giant warriors are among his worshippers, drawn in by his promise of unholy might, resilience, and an immunity to pain for those who enact ruthless violence and conduct sacrifices of flesh in his name. They tout ferocity and strength as aspects more important than the works, knowledge, or history that their elders taught them, causing the cult to be met with great enmity from other stone giants.
