# Ayrzul (The Fossilized King)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 306

Lord of buried secrets, earth, and metal, the Fossilized King Ayrzul is a mystery even on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]]. No one has seen the elemental lord of earth outside of his realm, the Blistering Labyrinth, and few know the truth of Ayrzul’s nature or the form he takes when he appears. His power, motives, and origins are likewise the subjects of innumerable rumors. This speculation is all inconsequential to the Fossilized King; even the politics of his plane falls outside his notice. Instead, the lord of earth spends his time plotting against his bitter rival, [[Setting/Deities/Ymeri]], the elemental lord of fire.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords]]

**Edicts** Obscure your true motives, slowly poison others, use the strength of stone to protect yourself and your secrets

**Anathema** Make fire larger or hotter than necessary, remove a creature’s [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]] condition

**Area of Concern**
Bone, buried secrets, earth, radiation
