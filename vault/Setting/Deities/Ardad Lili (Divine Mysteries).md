# Ardad Lili (The End of Innocence)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 320

When mortals were young, before [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus (Divine Mysteries)|Asmodeus]] conquered [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]], Ardad Lili was already manipulating amorous and lustful mortals to swear fealty to her, amassing power from their souls. She fled the realm of [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana (GM Core)|Nirvana]] during the Exodus, taking up residence in Avernus and continuing to gather an army of damned souls and female devils who share her ambitions. The Serpent Muse has never forgotten the censures and cruel insults spewed by the other natives of Nirvana, and she seeks to someday rule not a layer of Hell, but a realm of the [[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|heavens]]. Ardad Lili draws followers who are as passionate as herself.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Queens of the Night]]

**Edicts** Manipulate others with false promises, aid women who have been unfairly maligned

**Anathema** Give someone more than you receive from them, allow yourself to be swayed by lust

**Area of Concern**
Seduction, snakes, women
