# Alocer (The Pack Lord)  *Deity*

**Traits** [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|LE]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder One-Shot 2- Dinner at Lionlodge|Pathfinder One-Shot 2: Dinner at Lionlodge]] pg. 8

Alocer is a lawful evil diabolic demigod, the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Infernal Dukes|infernal duke]] of [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]], hunters, and tradition. Known also as the Pack Lord, he appears as a fire-breathing, [[Bestiary/Lion (Monster Core)|lion]]-headed, heavily armored archer with [[Bestiary/Families/Eagle (Monster Core)|eagle]] talons for feet. His symbol is a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]-legged horse, said to be a representation of his favored mount.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Infernal Dukes]]

**Edicts** hunt those who are capable of thinking for themselves, gather like-minded hunters into groups based in hunting lodges, burn places of worship devoted to [[Setting/Deities/Erastil (Divine Mysteries)|Erastil]] or corrupt those who follow his faith

**Anathema** sleep in a place sacred to Erastil, willingly harm a [[Bestiary/Lion (Monster Core)|lion]], grant a merciful death to a creature you have wounded

**Area of Concern**
beasts, hunters, and tradition
