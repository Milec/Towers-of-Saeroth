# Eiseth (Hell’s Valkyrie)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 320

[[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell’s]] Valkyrie rules a domain spanning part of Dispater’s realm, commanding her legions of infernal soldiers as she sees fit. Eiseth operates outside Dispater’s rule, and she has forged powerful alliances both in Hell and beyond, having long ago rejected limitations placed upon her by others. Foremost among the Queens of the Night, she embodies battle, revenge, and wrath, and her ambitions are as lofty as her aerie of Widow’s Cry, where she forges souls of the damned into unequaled infernal legions answering to her alone.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Queens of the Night]]

**Edicts** Avenge all insults, claim what you desire and deserve, humiliate your foes in ironic fashion

**Anathema** Allow a slight to go unanswered, show humility or fear

**Area of Concern**
Battle, revenge, wrath
