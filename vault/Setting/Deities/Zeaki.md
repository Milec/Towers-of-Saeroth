# Zeaki (The Matron of Metamorphoses)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 76

 A mythic sea hag, Zeaki offers transformative magic to all comers, and although she bears no malice toward these visitors, she nevertheless extracts a severe price for her services. Her island of Itia is home to countless unique beasts, each said to be some impolite visitor whom Zeaki transformed into a form more befitting their poor manners. Those who worship her— including witches of various patrons—seek personal change, mastery of magic, and control over the tides. The price Zeaki demands in return is unique to each follower and isn't always paid up front.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Iblydos Hero-Gods]]

**Edicts** Inflict poetic justice upon those who wrong you, assist others' personal transformations, learn esoteric secrets that others might value

**Anathema** Become complacent with your current self, offer aid to a stranger without exacting some price (even if the cost isn't obvious)

**Area of Concern**
Tides and transformation
