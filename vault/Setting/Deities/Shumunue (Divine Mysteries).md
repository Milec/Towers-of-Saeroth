# Shumunue (The Carved Lady of Mimicry)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 306

Shumunue, the Carved Lady of Mimicry, is the elemental lord of wood, life, and camouflage. Unlike most of the elemental lords, Shumunue is not a native creature to her plane, claiming to be the offspring of a kodama and a kizidhar wizard. When Shumunue arrived on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]], she found it filled with life, yet deathly still. It was she who taught the creatures of wood to shape themselves, mimicking animals and gaining the power to move themselves from where they had taken root. Shumunue’s left flank bears a blighted patch from [[Setting/Deities/Ayrzul]]’s poison, and it is not yet known if this injury will ever heal.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords]]

**Edicts** Celebrate births, make time for nature, recreate life in your works

**Anathema** Permanently damage a [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] creature, polymorph another without permission

**Area of Concern**
