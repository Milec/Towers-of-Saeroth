# Surveyors of the Deep  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 25

Bound by a shared passion for unearthing the hidden marvels of marine life and exploring uncharted depths, the Surveyors of the Deep inspire their followers—among whom number tomb raiders, archaeologists, and adventurous historians—to embark on daring voyages and seek the treasures that lie beneath the waves and among the stars. These explorers thrive on the thrill of discovery, delving into ancient shipwrecks and forgotten underwater ruins, to study the cryptic artifacts left behind by long-lost civilizations.

Travelers with a passion for learning who lack other attributes might worry they are unsuited for exploration, but the Surveyors of the Deep are known to bolster their believers with the strength and agility required to withstand the harsh elements of the open ocean. In the face of turbulent storms, treacherous currents, and unpredictable sea creatures, devotees of this pantheon receive divine support that prepares them for uncharted waters and the challenging journeys that lie ahead.

Worshippers of the Surveyors are characterized by their insatiable curiosity and dedication to mapping both the physical world and the intricate tapestry of stories and legends possessed by the ocean. Such devotees are hungry for discovery. They strive to be the first to document and share their findings, ensuring the wisdom of the seas is preserved for future generations. The Surveyors’ edicts promote exploration and the respectful study of marine ecosystems, fostering a spirit of stewardship that harmonizes with the divine essence of the water.

With the joy of discovery comes the need to illuminate every corner of the sea. Worshippers of the Surveyors of the Deep thus honor the mesmerizing beauty of bioluminescence, which naturally aligns with their edicts and acts as a guiding light during their explorations. While they celebrate the thrill of uncovering hidden treasures, they also appreciate the artistry of the underwater world. During bioluminescent surges, a devotee might wade into the water to perform the rite of Luminous Reflection. Submerged, they seek to derive meaning not only from the light but also from the void, which carries its own significance. Having honored the delicate balance of life within marine habitats, devotees seize every opportunity to sketch, journal, or document their experiences.

Through their steadfast commitment to exploration and anthropology, the Surveyors of the Deep cultivate a legacy of understanding and reverence for the unfathomable depths. Their teachings ensure that the ocean’s secrets inspire generations to come, encouraging future explorers to brave the tides, embrace the unknown, and take part in the eternal quest for knowledge and adventure upon the High Seas.
