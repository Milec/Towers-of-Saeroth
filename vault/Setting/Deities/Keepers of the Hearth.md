# Keepers of the Hearth  *Deity*

**Traits** [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|NG]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 68

Through art and tales from the continent of Arcadia, the goddess [[Setting/Deities/Kazutal]] (Mother Jaguar) is becoming increasingly popular in the Inner Sea Region due to her devotion to defending and providing for those in need, as well as fostering a powerful sense of community. Joined by [[Setting/Deities/Milani]] (The Everbloom), who opposes oppression in all forms and seeks to help those unable to protect themselves, and by [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]] (The Inheritor) who values fighting for justice, the Keepers of the Hearth stress the importance of defending the weak and defenseless in any community, especially those unwillingly subjugated or taken advantage of. Keepers not only protect those weaker than themselves but also do their best to teach the skills necessary for a community to defend and provide for itself. Many of those most devoted to the aspect of training and teaching self-defense also look to [[Setting/Deities/Irori]] (Master of Masters) for guidance and motivation as part of their faith.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Pantheons]]
