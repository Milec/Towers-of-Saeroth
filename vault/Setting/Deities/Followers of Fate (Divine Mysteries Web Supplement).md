# Followers of Fate  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries Web Supplement]] pg. 5

In the mortal [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]], some mortals worship [[Bestiary/Norn (Monster Core)|norns]] as deities, while others, especially [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-767)|witches]] and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bards]], admire them as patrons or muses. Those who uphold norns as deities are known as Followers of Fate. Norns neither discourage this veneration nor go out of their way to support such worship. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-646)|Clerics]] who venerate norns might worship a specific norn or norn triumvirate, or all norns as a whole, but they gain the same benefits regardless of their choice. The religious symbol of Followers of Fate is a pair of shears cutting a golden thread, and their areas of concern are destiny, fate, and the aging process.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Other Gods]]

**Edicts** make predictions of the future, offer advice and guidance to those in positions of power, provide comfort to the elderly

**Anathema** apologize for making an incorrect prediction, disrespect mothers, accept payment for fortune-telling

**Area of Concern**
destiny, fate, and the aging process.
