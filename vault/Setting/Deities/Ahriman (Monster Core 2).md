# Ahriman (Lord of the Divs)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 110

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This deity has the must choose unholy sanctification and is therefore restricted for society play._

The dread shadow known as Ahriman counts servants mainly among [[Bestiary/Families/Div|div]] as well as a scattering of followers throughout the mortal realm.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Other Gods]]

**Edicts** Foil rulers, the proud, and the powerful; ruin anything created by mortals

**Anathema** Create arts or crafts, serve a mortal, assist in mortal aims except to subvert and corrupt them

**Area of Concern**
Destruction, divs, nihilism
