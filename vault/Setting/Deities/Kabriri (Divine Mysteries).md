# Kabriri (Him Who Gnaws)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 304

Kabriri, Him Who Gnaws, is the demon lord of [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul (Monster Core)|ghouls]], graves, and secrets kept by the dead. According to legend, Kabriri is the reborn form of the first humanoid to ever devour his kin in life. His realm, Everglut, is connected to graveyards throughout [[Rules/The Multiverse (GM Core)|the multiverse]] by a snarled network of tunnels that bring knowledge and sacrifices to the demon lord. Kabriri appears as a hulking ghoul with [[Ancestries/Elf (Player Core)|elven]] ears, teeth filed to points, an unnaturally long tongue, ashen skin, and cloven hooves. He is worshipped primarily by ghouls and [[Bestiary/Ghast|ghasts]].

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords]]

**Edicts** Eat the flesh of your own kind

**Anathema** Reveal secrets of the dead to nonbelievers, despoil grave markers

**Area of Concern**
Ghouls, graves, secrets kept by the dead
