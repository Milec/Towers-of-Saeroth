# Arqueros (The Golden Bulwark)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries Web Supplement]] pg. 11

As strong in will as he is in form, Arqueros is Lord of the Wall, guardian of [[Setting/Planes/Heaven]], and empyreal lord of bodyguards, protection, and watchfulness. He maintains eternal vigil at Heaven's gates, resplendent in gold armor and clutching a diamond-headed spear. Whatever the truth of his heart, Arqueros is composed in the face of dire threats and is a stalwart defender who would die before abandoning his duties.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Empyreal Lords]]

**Edicts** Protect those weaker than you; remain alert for danger; create, maintain, or patrol defenses

**Anathema** Abandon others while you retreat, engage in needless destruction or bloodshed, intentionally fall asleep or grow lax on watch

**Area of Concern**
Bodyguards, protection, watchfulness
