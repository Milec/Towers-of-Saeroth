# Belial (The Pale Kiss)  *Deity*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 300

The lord of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell’s]] fourth layer, Phlegethon, is Belial. The Pale Kiss was created by [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus (Divine Mysteries)|Asmodeus]] as an object of adoration, with perfect form and beauty in the eyes of every creature. As a result, Belial has a virtually unlimited malleability of form, shifting between shapes almost constantly. His appearance is often as dualistic as his personality: half his body beautiful and half grotesque, much as he revels equally in pleasure and pain. As he is a creature of carnal desires, so are his followers: those who crave forbidden pleasures of the flesh but hide behind masks of respectability.

**Category** [[Setting/Deity Categories/Archdevils]]

**Edicts** Indulge your basest desires, create deadly weapons

**Anathema** Impede an act of high hedonism, become too attached to a lover or project

**Area of Concern**
Adultery, deception, desire
