# Order of the Gate  *Hellknight Order*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

**Favored Weapon**
[[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

The creed of the Order of the Gate is “Judgment in the face of depravity.” These Hellknights seek to prevent crime through magic and extraplanar knowledge.

## Locate Lawbreakers  *Lesser Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

Your pursuit is relentless, and you can magically locate stolen objects and outlaws. You gain [[Spells/Locate (Player Core)|_locate_]] as an innate spell of a tradition of your choice, which you can cast once per day. If you're a member of the Order of the Gate, when you reach 14th level, the spell is heightened to 5th rank.

## Devil Allies  *Greater Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

You can call forth devils to assist your efforts in preventing criminal acts. You gain [[Spells/Summon Fiend (Player Core)|_summon fiend_]] as a 6th-rank innate spell of a tradition of your choice, which you can cast once per day. When you cast this spell, you can summon only creatures that have the [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]] trait. At 14th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the spell is heightened by one rank (to a maximum of 10th rank when you reach 20th level).
