# Order of the Nail  *Hellknight Order*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

**Favored Weapons**
[[Equipment/Weapons/Halberd (Player Core)|Halberd]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance (Player Core)|Lance]]

Order of the Nail The creed of the Order of the Nail is “Savagery must bequelled, in the land, home, and mind.” These Hellknights work to tame wild frontier regions.

## Trailblazing Stride  *Lesser Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

While moving on land, you ignore any [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] caused by plants, fungi, or uneven ground. When you succeed at a skill check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about a creature with the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]], or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] trait, you gain a +10-foot circumstance bonus to your Speeds for the rest of your turn.

## Reveal Beasts  *Greater Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

You're adept at revealing—or creating—fear among those who threaten civilization. If you succeed at a check to Recall Knowledge to identify an animal, beast, fungus, or plant, you get a critical success instead. Additionally, whenever you critically succeed at a check to Recall Knowledge against an animal, beast, fungus, or plant, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.
