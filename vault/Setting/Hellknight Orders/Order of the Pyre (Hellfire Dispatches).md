# Order of the Pyre  *Hellknight Order*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 28

**Favored Weapon**
[[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Glaive]]

The creed of the Order of the Pyre is “Reason’s flame consumes the shadow of corruption.” These Hellknights seek to purge what they call blasphemous ideologies from the world.

## Righteous Resistance  *Lesser Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 29

You can cast [[Spells/Resist Energy (Player Core)|_resist energy_]] as a divine innate spell twice per day, except you can target only yourself. If you grant yourself resistance to fire damage, the resistance is equal to either the spell's normal value or to your level, whichever is higher.

## Spiritual Disruption two actions  *Greater Order Benefit*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 29

You channel disruptive power that severs a foe's spiritual connection to their magic. Make a melee Strike. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If the attack hits, the target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent spirit damage]]. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until it stops taking the persistent spirit damage.
