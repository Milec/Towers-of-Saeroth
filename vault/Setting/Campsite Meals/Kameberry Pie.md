# Kameberry Pie  *Campsite Meal 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 117

**Recipe Price**
50 gp

**Ingredients** 3 basic, 2 special

**Preparation** DC 27 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 29 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to Religion checks for 24 hours.

---

Kameberry pie is a tart confection valued by many who claim that its sourness can help provide insights.

**Critical Success** The meal restores 6d8 Hit Points when it's eaten and an additional 6d8 Hit Points when the eater wakes from a night of rest.  

**Success** The meal restores 3d8 Hit Points when it's eaten and an additional 3d8 Hit Points when the eater wakes from a night of rest.  

**Critical Failure** The meal is either far too sweet or far too sour. Worse, the unpleasant flavors linger for 8 hours and are distracting enough to cause the eater to become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for that time.
