# Chocolate Ice Cream  *Campsite Meal 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 115

**Recipe Price**
5 sp

**Ingredients** 2 basic, 1 special

**Preparation** DC 19 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to all [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]].

---

Normally a treat limited to areas where ice is abundant, this version of the recipe utilizes the ability to cast spells that cause cold damage to create a deliciously close approximation of ice cream while camping. When you use the Cook Special Meal activity to create ice cream, you must cast 1 cold spell—typically, [[Spells/Ray of Frost|_ray of frost_]] is utilized.

**Critical Success** The ice cream is perfect. The eater receives a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks for 24 hours.  

**Success** As critical success, but the bonus ends when the eater begins daily preparations.  

**Critical Failure** The ice cream is too cold, too sweet, or both. In addition to not gaining any special benefit from the meal, disappointment causes the eater to take a –1 status penalty to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects for 24 hours or until the character achieves a critical success on a saving throw against an emotion effect, whichever comes first.
