# Haggis  *Campsite Meal 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 116

**Recipe Price**
1 gp

**Ingredients** 2 basic

**Preparation** DC 15 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to Will saves against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects for 24 hours.

---

This dish consists of a combination of grains, onions, spices, and organ meats boiled in an animal's stomach.

**Critical Success** The meal bolsters the eater against illness, granting a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects that cause [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition for 24 hours.  

**Success** The meal bolsters the eater against nausea, granting a +1 status bonus to effects that cause the sickened condition for 24 hours.  

**Critical Failure** The meal makes the eater quite ill. They become sickened 1 until they get a full night's rest. This is a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effect.
