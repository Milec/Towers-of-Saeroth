# Hunter's Roast  *Campsite Meal 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 116

**Recipe Price**
13 gp

**Ingredients** 4 basic

**Preparation** DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks.

---

A classic meal of meat, mushrooms, and root vegetables, this roast meal is served amid helpings of thick gravy.

**Critical Success** The eater gains a number of temporary Hit Points equal to their level, lasting up to 24 hours.  

**Success** As critical success, but the temporary Hit Points last only until the eater begins their daily preparations.  

**Critical Failure** The hunter's roast is more than unpalatable— it's poisonous! The eater takes 2d6 poison damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] until they get a full night's rest. This is a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effect.
