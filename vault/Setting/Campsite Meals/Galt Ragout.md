# Galt Ragout  *Campsite Meal 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 115

**Recipe Price**
5 gp

**Ingredients** 4 basic

**Preparation** DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Tumble Through]].

---

This traditional Galtan dish consists of a highly seasoned stew of meat and vegetables.

**Critical Success** The eater receives a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects that cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] condition or [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigue]], and during the next 2 days, they can go for up to 18 hours without sleep before becoming fatigued.  

**Success** During the next 24 hours, the eater can go for up to 18 hours without sleep before becoming fatigued.  

**Critical Failure** The spicy meal doesn't sit well. The eater is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] until they get a full night's rest.
