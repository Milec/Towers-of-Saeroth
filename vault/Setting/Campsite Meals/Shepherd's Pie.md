# Shepherd's Pie  *Campsite Meal 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 119

**Recipe Price**
2 gp

**Ingredients** 4 basic

**Preparation** DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** For the next 24 hours, the eater's healing ability is enhanced. Whenever they recover Hit Points with a healing effect, they restore 1 additional Hit Point. If an effect would heal them more than once, such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]], the additional Hit Point applies only the first time they're healed

---

This simple but filling meal provides ample amounts of physical nutrition and mental comfort.

**Critical Success** The meal augments physical recovery. When the eater recovers Hit Points from resting during this camping session, they recover an additional 4d6 Hit Points. If the eater was [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], they reduce their drained condition by 2 points rather than by 1.  

**Success** The meal augments the body's ability to recover. When the eater recovers Hit Points from resting during this camping session, they recover an additional 2d6 Hit Points.  

**Critical Failure** The meal doesn't sit well. When the eater rests during this camping session, they regain only half the amount of Hit Points from resting that they normally would have.
