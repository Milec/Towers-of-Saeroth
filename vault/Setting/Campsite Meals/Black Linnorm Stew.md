# Black Linnorm Stew  *Campsite Meal 18*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 114

**Recipe Price**
1,200 gp

**Ingredients** 8 basic, 3 special

**Preparation** DC 43 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +2 status bonus to Perception checks.

---

This thick stew looks unappetizing, with its tar-like black broth and strangely colored chunks of meat.

**Critical Success** The meal grants a +3 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Curse|curse]] and [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effects. In addition, the next time the eater is damaged, they gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 10]]; this fast healing lasts until the eater dies, is healed to its maximum hit points, or for 1 minute, whichever comes first, after which the fast healing effect ends.  

**Success** The meal grants a +3 status bonus to saves against curse and poison effects.  

**Critical Failure** The meal imparts a fragment of the linnorm death curse on the eater, who becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] for 24 hours. This is a curse effect.
