# Whiterose Oysters  *Campsite Meal 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 119

**Recipe Price**
35 gp

**Ingredients** 3 basic, 2 special

**Preparation** DC 26 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** The meal infuses the eater's impressions with additional power, spreading the benefits of the meal to other uses of [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]. The eater gains a +2 status bonus to Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] for the remainder of the camping session and a +2 status bonus to Diplomacy checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] for 24 hours.

---

First inspired by a cook's visit to Whiterose Winery many years ago, this meal has become a favorite of many nobles.

**Critical Success** The meal grants a +2 status bonus to Diplomacy checks to [[Actions/Request]] and Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Coerce]] for the next 24 hours.  

**Success** As critical success, but the effects last for the remainder of the camping session.  

**Critical Failure** The oysters weren't prepared properly. The eater becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]].
