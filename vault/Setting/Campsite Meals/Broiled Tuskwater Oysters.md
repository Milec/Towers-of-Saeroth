# Broiled Tuskwater Oysters  *Campsite Meal 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 114

**Recipe Price**
3 gp

**Ingredients** 2 basic, 1 special

**Preparation** DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Occult|occult]] spells

---

The freshwater oysters that grow in and around the Tuskwater have long been said to help one see into other realities or to understand secrets beyond normal reach.

**Critical Success** The meal grants a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] checks made to [[Actions/Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell]], and [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] for 24 hours.  

**Success** The meal grants a +1 status bonus to Occultism checks made to Identify Magic, Learn a Spell, and Recall Knowledge until the eater begins daily preparations.  

**Critical Failure** The poorly prepared oysters cause mild but distracting hallucinations, resulting in a –1 status penalty to Perception checks for 24 hours. This is a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effect.
