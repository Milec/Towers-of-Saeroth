# Hearty Purple Soup  *Campsite Meal 16*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 116

**Recipe Price**
500 gp

**Ingredients** 6 basic, 3 special

**Preparation** DC 40 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** The bonus against [[Traits/Poison|poison]] and [[Traits/Disease|disease]] effects increases to +4.

---

[[Bestiary/Purple Worm|Purple worm]] meat is not particularly flavorful. Yet when one takes a dose of the worm's poison and distills it in a way that reduces its toxicity, the hearty purple soup that results can be a significant panacea against poisons and diseases.

**Critical Success** The meal grants immunity to diseases and poisons of level 15 or lower. In addition, the meal grants a +3 status bonus to saving throws against poison and disease effects. Any saving throws attempted against [[Bestiary/Families/Cave Worm|cave worm]] poison or any poison of level 15 or lower are rolled twice, using the higher result as the actual result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.  

**Success** As critical success, except that the fortune effect only applies to cave worm poison, not to other poisons of level 15 or lower.  

**Critical Failure** Instead of protecting from poison, the soup is poison itself. The eater becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 3]]; every 24 hours, this enfeebled condition diminishes by 1. This is a poison effect.
