# Cheese Crostata  *Campsite Meal 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 114

**Recipe Price**
8 gp

**Ingredients** 4 basic

**Preparation** DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Cooking Lore]] or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Favorite Meal** +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] checks.

---

This pie-like meal is filled with a delightful mix of different cheeses and spices, all cooked into a flaky dough crust.

**Critical Success** The eater gains a +1 status bonus to saving throws against effects that cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition or [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigue]], and during the next 2 days, they can go for up to 20 hours without sleep before becoming fatigued.  

**Success** For the next 24 hours, the eater can go for up to 18 hours without sleep before becoming fatigued.  

**Critical Failure** The heavy meal doesn't sit well. The eater is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] until they finish a full night's rest.
