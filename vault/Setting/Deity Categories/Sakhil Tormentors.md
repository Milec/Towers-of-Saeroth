# Sakhil Tormentors  *Deity Category*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries Web Supplement]] pg. 20

The most powerful among sahkils are the sahkil tormentors. These deific beings rule over [[Bestiary/Families/Sahkil|sahkils]] as a whole, and many personally direct the actions of their lesser cousins. Others fall into ambivalent routines, focusing more on personal acts of torment rather than launching widespread, ambitious depredations upon unsuspecting mortals.
