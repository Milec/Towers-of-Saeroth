# Faiths & Philosophies  *Deity Category*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 440

Of course, faith can express itself in more ways than venerating a single deity—or a deity at all. A few examples of nondeific religions and philosophies are presented below. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|Clerics]] can work with allied faiths and philosophies, but the organizations here turn to other classes—such as [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerers]] with divine bloodlines, [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druids]], or [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|monks]]—to serve as their leaders. These faiths and philosophies don't have an external godhead that offers benefits to devotees.
