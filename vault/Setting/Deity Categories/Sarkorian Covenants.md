# Sarkorian Covenants  *Deity Category*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 235

While some god callers devote themselves to a single Sarkorian deity or other local divinity, others form covenants with small divine collectives that include elementals, spirit guides, and true Sarkorian deities. These entities work in concert to create a group that offers worshippers a slightly different way to express their faith and perform their worship.
