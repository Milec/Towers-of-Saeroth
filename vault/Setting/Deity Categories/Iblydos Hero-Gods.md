# Iblydos Hero-Gods  *Deity Category*

**Source** [[Sources/Myth-Speakers Players Guide]] pg. 9

Iblydans pay little attention to traditional gods, their ancestors having decided such deities weren't worthy after abandoning Iblydos to weather Earthfall on its own millennia ago. Instead, most worship is directed toward hero-gods, mortal demigods who oversee cults that typically fade after the hero-god's death. Because myth-speaking rarely functions in the Age of Lost Omens, hero-gods are a dying people. The following are many of the surviving hero-gods, including their divine statistics for champions, clerics, and other PCs who choose to worship them.
