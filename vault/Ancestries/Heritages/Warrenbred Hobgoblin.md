# Warrenbred Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 49

Your ancestors lived underground. Your ears are larger than those of other hobgoblins and sensitive to echoes. When you are underground, you can use the [[Actions/Seek]] action to sense [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creatures within a 30-foot burst instead of a 15-foot burst. In addition, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Squeeze]], you get a critical success instead.
