# Fungus Leshy  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 53

Your body was made from fungi that grows in the shade of caves and trees, and you are at home in dark caverns and warrens. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]. You lose the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait and gain the [[Traits/Fungus|fungus]] trait.
