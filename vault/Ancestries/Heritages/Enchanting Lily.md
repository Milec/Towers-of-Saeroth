# Enchanting Lily  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 36

You smell ever so sweet—a delicate, floral scent like the intoxicating fragrance of lilies and the soft scent of lilacs. You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Diplomacy). You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] if the target can smell your fragrance.
