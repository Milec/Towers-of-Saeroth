# Abyssal Merfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 35

You live far, far below the surface of the ocean. Your fish tail might resemble a viperfish or anglerfish, and you might have luminous eyes or translucent skin. Abyssal merfolk have an uncanny reputation, but they can exist in even the most lightless realms. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] and are immune to the crushing pressure of the oceanic depths.
