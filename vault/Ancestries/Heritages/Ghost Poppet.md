# Ghost Poppet  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 62

You awakened when a bit of another person's life force and a fragment of their soul—possibly, but not always, at the moment of their death—found its way into your constructed body. You don't remember anything more than vague flashes of your “before life,” but that life essence still protects you. You gain resistance to [[Traits/Negative|negative]] damage equal to half your level (minimum 1).
