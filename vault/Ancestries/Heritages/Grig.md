# Grig  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 129

You have the hindquarters of a cricket and adore art and music. Your cricket legs grant you a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump]], and you gain [[Spells/Ghost Sound|_ghost sound_]] as a primal innate cantrip.
