# Warrenbred Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 13

Your ancestors lived underground. Your ears are larger than those of other hobgoblins and sensitive to echoes. While you're underground, when you target an opponent that is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from you or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from you, reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or 9 for a hidden one. In addition, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]], you get a critical success instead.
