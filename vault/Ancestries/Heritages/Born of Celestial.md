# Born of Celestial  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 84

You were once a powerful [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] before an escape or great punishment left you trapped in a mortal shell, with only vague memories and limited power. Due to your residual [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] power, the tradition of any spells or magical abilities you gain from a yaoguai heritage or ancestry feat is divine instead of its normal tradition (usually [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]).

- **Humanoid Form** Fragments of divine memory still litter your mind. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] checks.
- **Yaoguai Form** Celestial power flows through you. Choose one cantrip from the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an innate divine cantrip at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
