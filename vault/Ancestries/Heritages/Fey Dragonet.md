# Fey Dragonet  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 210

You have a brightly colored body with gleaming scales that become more lustrous as you age, and iridescent butterfly wings. You can change the coloration and pattern of your wings with a single action to let you blend into an area. When your wings match your environment, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks until your surroundings shift in coloration or pattern. You gain the [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]] trait and add [[Setting/Languages/Fey]] to your list of known languages.
