# Stormtossed Tengu  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 33

Whether due to a blessing from [[Setting/Deities/Hei Feng (Divine Mysteries)|Hei Feng]] or hatching from your egg during a squall, you are resistant to storms. You gain electricity resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You automatically succeed at the flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by rain or fog.
