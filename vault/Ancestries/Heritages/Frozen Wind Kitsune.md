# Frozen Wind Kitsune  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 123

Your ancestors crossed the Crown of the World or resided on snowy peaks. You gain cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on). Your alternate form is a common Medium humanoid ancestry prevalent where you grew up (typically [[Ancestries/Human|human]]), called a tailless form.
