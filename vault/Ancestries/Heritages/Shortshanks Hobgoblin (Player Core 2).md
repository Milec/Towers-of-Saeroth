# Shortshanks Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 13

You have a longer torso and broader shoulders than most hobgoblins, making your legs seem short by comparison. This gives you a strong, muscular core and lowers your center of gravity—features that assist you in riding and climbing—and you've trained at riding in a saddle from an extremely early age. You gain the [[Feats/Ride (Player Core)|Ride]] feat. Additionally, you are not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while you [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]].
