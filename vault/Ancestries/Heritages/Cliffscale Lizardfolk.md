# Cliffscale Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 57

Your toes are adapted for gripping and climbing. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and as long as you aren't wearing footwear, you can use the sticky pads on your feet to climb, leaving your hands free. Additionally, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to climb, you get a critical success instead.
