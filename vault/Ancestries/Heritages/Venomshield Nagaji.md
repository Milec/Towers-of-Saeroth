# Venomshield Nagaji  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 48

Your intrinsic connection to nagas and mundane serpents grants you an innate resistance to toxins of every sort. You gain resistance to poison equal to half your level (minimum 1 resistance), and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Poison|poison]].
