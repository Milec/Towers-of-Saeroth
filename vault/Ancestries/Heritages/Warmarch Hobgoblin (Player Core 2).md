# Warmarch Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 13

You come from a line of wandering mercenaries constantly on the march and scavenging food on the trail. If you fail, but don't critically fail, to [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] in the wilderness, you can still keep yourself fed with poor meals. When exploring, you can [[Actions/Hustle (Player Core)|Hustle]] twice as long before you have to stop.
