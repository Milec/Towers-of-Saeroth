# Old-Blood Vishkanya  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 60

You have a direct matrilineal line to the original group of vishkanyas upon the Vudrani ships that came to reclaim Jalmeray. Since then, your family has adjusted well to a new life in the Inner Sea. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] (or another skill of your choice if you're already trained in Society), and you gain your choice of the [[Feats/Courtly Graces]] or [[Feats/Streetwise]] feat.
