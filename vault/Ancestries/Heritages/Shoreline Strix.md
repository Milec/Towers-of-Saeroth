# Shoreline Strix  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 135

You're the descendant of strix who traveled to the coasts. Your feathers are especially water-repellent and sleekly angled for diving, allowing you to catch fish and other prey in the shallows. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], and you gain the [[Feats/Underwater Marauder]] skill feat as a bonus skill feat.
