# Wildwood Halfling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 59

You hail from deep within a jungle or forest, and you've learned how to use your small size to wriggle through undergrowth and other obstacles. You ignore any [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by plants and fungi, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.
