# Warmarch Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 49

You come from a line of wandering mercenaries, constantly on the march and scavenging food on the trail. If you fail, but not critically fail, to [[Actions/Subsist]] in the wilderness, you can still keep yourself fed with poor meals. When exploring, you can [[Actions/Hustle]] twice as long before you have to stop.
