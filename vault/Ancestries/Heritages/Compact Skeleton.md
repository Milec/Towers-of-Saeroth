# Compact Skeleton  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 55

For tasks that require a lighter touch, [[Ancestries/Gnome|gnome]], [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]], and [[Ancestries/Halfling|halfling]] remains are more suitable than a larger creature's. These smaller, nimbler skeletons fit well in a traveling chest, can be forced through tight spaces, and more easily avoid detection. Your size is Small instead of Medium. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Squeeze]] feat, even if you aren't trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]. Tight spaces not tight enough to require the [[Actions/Squeeze]] activity aren't difficult terrain for you.
