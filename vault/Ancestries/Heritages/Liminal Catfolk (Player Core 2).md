# Liminal Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 9

You've inherited a closeness to the far corners of the world, where the boundaries between dimensions grow thin. You can cast the [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]] cantrip as an occult innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to half your level rounded up. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about creatures that originated on planes other than [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]].
