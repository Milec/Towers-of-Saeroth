# Stalker Minotaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 42

Despite your heavy frame, you walk on shaggy hooves that muffle your footfalls, allowing you to surprise your prey. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Stealth) and gain the [[Feats/Terrain Stalker (Player Core)|Terrain Stalker]] skill feat, except you must choose rubble and you can [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] no more than 10 feet instead of 5 feet without attempting a Stealth check.
