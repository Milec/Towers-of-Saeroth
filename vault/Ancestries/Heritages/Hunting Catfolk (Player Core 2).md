# Hunting Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 9

You come from a long line of skilled hunters and trackers and have a particularly keen sense of smell. You gain [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Rules/Scent|scent]] with a range of 30 feet. This means you can use your sense of smell to determine a creature's location. The GM will usually double the range if you're downwind from the creature or halve the range if you're upwind.

 In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] a creature or object if you've smelled it before.
