# Death Warden Dwarf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 43

Your ancestors were tomb guardians, and their power to ward off death passed on to you. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an effect that has the [[Traits/Void|void]] trait or was created by an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature, you get a critical success instead.
