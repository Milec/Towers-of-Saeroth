# Bright Fetchling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 85

Your body proves that shadow can't exist without light. Perhaps your eyes glow brighter or your veins occasionally pulse with light. You emit dim light within 5 feet of you. You can activate or suppress this ability as an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait. Additionally, you can force fragments of your shadow to glow and cast [[Spells/Dancing Lights|_dancing lights_]] and [[Spells/Light|_light_]] as occult innate cantrips. Cantrips are heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
