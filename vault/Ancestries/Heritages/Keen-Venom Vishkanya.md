# Keen-Venom Vishkanya  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 60

You're practiced with using your venom in combat, allowing you to employ your venom at a moment's notice. Your [[Actions/Envenom]] action doesn't trigger reactions that normally trigger on a [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] action. Additionally, you gain the Venom Draw action.
