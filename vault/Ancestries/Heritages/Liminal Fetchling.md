# Liminal Fetchling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 85

You come from a line of fetchlings with a closer connection to the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]] and [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane|Shadow Planes]], and you share those realms' affinity with the in-between and unseen. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to locate [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creatures within 60 feet when using the [[Actions/Seek]] action. Your flat check to target [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] creatures is DC 3 instead of DC 5, and your flat check to target undetected creatures is DC 9 instead of DC 11.
