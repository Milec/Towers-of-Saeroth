# Warrior Automaton  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 40

Sporting a bulkier, powerful design, your body has been designed for combat. You have a bulky, humanoid shape. The damage die for your fist increases to 1d6 instead of 1d4. You don’t take a penalty when making a lethal attack with your [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]] or any other [[Rules/Unarmed Attacks (Secrets of Magic)|unarmed attack]].
