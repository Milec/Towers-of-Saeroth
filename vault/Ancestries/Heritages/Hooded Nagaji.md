# Hooded Nagaji  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 48

You bear the hooded head of a spitting cobra, and like such cobras, you can shoot streams of venom from your mouth. You gain a venomous spit ranged [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack with a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 10 feet that deals 1d4 poison damage. On a critical hit, the target takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] equal to the number of weapon damage dice. Your spit doesn't have a weapon group or a critical specialization effect.
