# Nomadic Halfling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 52

Your ancestors have traveled from place to place for generations, never content to settle down. You gain two additional [languages](Languages.aspx) of your choice, chosen from among the common and uncommon languages available to you, and every time you take the [[Feats/Multilingual]] feat, you gain another new language.
