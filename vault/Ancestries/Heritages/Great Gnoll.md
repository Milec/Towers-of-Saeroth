# Great Gnoll  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 111

You're a large, powerful gnoll, with tawny fur and brown spots on your hide. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 8 and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip]] foes.
