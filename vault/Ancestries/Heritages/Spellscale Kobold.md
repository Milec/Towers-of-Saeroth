# Spellscale Kobold  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 14

A trace of draconic magic flows through your veins. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane spell list]]. You can Cast this Spell as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up. You gain the trained proficiency rank in arcane spell attack rolls and spell DCs, and your key spellcasting ability is Charisma.
