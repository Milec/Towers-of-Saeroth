# Shapewrought Fleshwarp  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 91

A victim of fleshwarping, you likely bear loose flesh, bony spurs, or features of inhuman creatures sprouting from your skin. You have nevertheless turned the psychic toll of your transformation into a strength. You gain resistance to [[Traits/Mental|mental]] damage equal to half your level (minimum 1).
