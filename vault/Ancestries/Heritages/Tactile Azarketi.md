# Tactile Azarketi  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 13

Your skin can detect the slightest changes in ocean currents. You gain [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Bestiary/Abilities/Wavesense|wavesense]] with a range of 30 feet, allowing you to sense motion in the water around you. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction]] in aquatic environments.
