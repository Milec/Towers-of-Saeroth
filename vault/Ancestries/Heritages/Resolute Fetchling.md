# Resolute Fetchling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 85

Your ancestors have long dealt with the horrific—in the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]] or elsewhere—and you bear their mental fortitude. When you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
