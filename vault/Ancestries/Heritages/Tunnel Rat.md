# Tunnel Rat  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 54

Your incredibly small bones allow you to easily compress your body and squeeze through gaps. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Squeeze]] feat as a bonus feat, even if you aren't trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]. Tight spaces not tight enough to require the [[Actions/Squeeze]] action aren't difficult terrain for you.
