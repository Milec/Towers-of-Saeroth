# Badlands Orc  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 71

You come from sun-scorched badlands, where long legs and an ability to withstand the elements helped you thrive. You can [[Actions/Hustle]] twice as long while exploring before you have to stop, and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on).
