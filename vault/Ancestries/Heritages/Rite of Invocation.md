# Rite of Invocation  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 108

You augmented your exoskeleton with magic. You gain one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
