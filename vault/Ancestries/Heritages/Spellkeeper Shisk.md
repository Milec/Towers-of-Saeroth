# Spellkeeper Shisk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 123

Your magical knowledge allows you to cast simple spells. Choose [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]]. You gain one cantrip from that magical tradition's spell list. You can cast this spell as an innate spell at will, as a spell of your chosen tradition. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
