# Fey-touched Gnome  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 51

The blood of the fey flows through your veins, saturating you with their magic and making you truly one of them. You gain the [[Traits/Fey|fey]] trait, in addition to the [[Traits/Gnome|gnome]] and [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] traits. Choose one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] spell list. You can cast this spell as a primal innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You can change this cantrip to a different one from the same list once per day by meditating to realign yourself with the [[Setting/Planes/First World]]; this is a 10-minute activity that has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.
