# Shadow of the Sailor  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 72

Your shadow flits across the surface of the water, and so do you. You can walk on the surface of still water and other non-damaging liquids, moving at half your normal Speed. You can attempt to walk along the surface of flowing water as well, still moving at half Speed, but to do so, you must succeed at an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] using the DC of a [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] check to move through the water; on a failure, you fall into the water. This Acrobatics check doesn't use an action.
