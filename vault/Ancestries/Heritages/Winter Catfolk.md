# Winter Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 10

You have a thick coat of fur that protects you from the cold. You gain cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
