# Celestial Envoy Kitsune  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 123

Whether due to Daikitsu's grace or faithful forebears, you have a strong connection to the divine, affording you certain protections. You gain the Invoke Celestial Privilege reaction. Your alternate form is a common Medium humanoid ancestry prevalent where you grew up (typically [[Ancestries/Human|human]]), called a tailless form.
