# Insightful Goloma  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 115

You've learned to watch for telltale signs of dangerous sentiments to help you avoid trouble. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC against attempts to [[Actions/Lie]] to you and your Perception checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive]].
