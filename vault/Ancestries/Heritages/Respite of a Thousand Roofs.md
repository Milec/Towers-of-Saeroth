# Respite of a Thousand Roofs  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 78

You swore a vow to shelter and feed the poor. Your vow grants you adroitness with carpentry, cook pot, and cloth; you become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] and Cooking Lore, and you gain the [[Feats/Improvise Tool (Player Core 2)|Improvise Tool]] skill feat.  
**Additional Edict** help the impoverished to the extent you're able by repairing their abodes and clothing or by feeding them
