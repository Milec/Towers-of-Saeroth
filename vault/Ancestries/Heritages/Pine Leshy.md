# Pine Leshy  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 77

You excrete a thick, sticky sap from your bark-like flesh, making it easier to climb and hold onto your gear. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]] you.
