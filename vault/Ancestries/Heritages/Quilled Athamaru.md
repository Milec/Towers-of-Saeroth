# Quilled Athamaru  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 18

Sharp quills on your head can pierce your foes deeply. Most athamarus use such quills for defense, but a well-timed headbash can be devastatingly effective. You gain a quills melee unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your quills are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.
