# Respite of Cloudless Paths  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 78

You swore a vow to protect the waylaid and the lost. Both environmental heat effects and [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|environmental cold]] effects are one step less extreme for you (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on), and you gain a + 1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against environmental features or hazards, such as floods, rockslides, and sandstorms.  
**Additional Edict** assist lost or incapacitated travelers
