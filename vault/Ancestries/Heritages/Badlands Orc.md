# Badlands Orc  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 18

You come from sun-scorched badlands, where long legs and an ability to withstand the elements helped you thrive. You can [Hustle](https://2e.aonprd.com/Activities.aspx?ID=5) twice as long while exploring before you have to stop, and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on).
