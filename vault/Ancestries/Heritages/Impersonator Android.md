# Impersonator Android  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 71

Your body was augmented with processes and an appearance intended to blend in with humans more easily. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (or another skill if you're already trained in Deception). You don't require a [[Equipment/Disguise Kit|disguise kit]] when attempting Deception checks to [[Actions/Impersonate]] a [[Ancestries/Human|human]]. In addition, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Impersonate checks to pretend that you're a human version of yourself, rather than an android. This bonus doesn't apply to other checks to Impersonate humans.
