# Cecaelia Merfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 20

While the bodies of most merfolk resemble the bottom half of a fish, your lower half is akin to a cephalopod. You have multiple tentacles rather than legs or a fish tail, and those tentacles are lined with suction cups. Your land Speed becomes 10 feet, and you gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 10 feet. Additionally, your abundance of legs allows you to [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into non-magical [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].
