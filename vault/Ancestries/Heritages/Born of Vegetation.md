# Born of Vegetation  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 84

You were a [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] or [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungi]] until the rain gifted you a mind. You gain your choice of the plant or fungus trait.

- **Humanoid Form** With fresh vegetation, you can better aid those in need. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks to [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]].
- **Yaoguai Form** When anyone uses the Medicine skill to [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat your Wounds,]] add your level to the Hit Points you regain from that treatment. Additionally, the creature attempting the check gains a +1 circumstance bonus if you have the plant trait and are in bright light, or the fungus trait and are in darkness.
