# Jungle Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 9

You're descended from jungle stalkers and can move swiftly through scrub and underbrush. You ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] from undergrowth, and greater difficult terrain from undergrowth is only difficult terrain for you.
