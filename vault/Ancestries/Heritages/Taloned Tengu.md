# Taloned Tengu  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 26

Your talons are every bit as sharp and strong as your beak. You gain a talons unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your talons are in the brawling group and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Versatile|versatile piercing]] traits.
