# Shortshanks Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 33

You have a longer torso and broader shoulders than most hobgoblins, making your legs seem short by comparison. This gives you a strong, muscular core and lowers your center of gravity—features that assist you in riding and climbing—and you've trained at riding in a saddle from an extremely early age. You gain the [[Feats/Ride]] feat. Additionally, you are not [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] while you [[Actions/Climb]].
