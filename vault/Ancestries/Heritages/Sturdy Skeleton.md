# Sturdy Skeleton  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 56

Certain hazardous conditions are not suitable for the average skeleton, so a necromancer might choose to reanimate the bones of a [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarf]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Orc Settlements|orc]], or other creature with a reputation for sturdiness. You have 10 Hit Points instead of 6 and gain the [[Feats/Diehard]] feat.
