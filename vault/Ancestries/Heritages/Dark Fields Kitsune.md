# Dark Fields Kitsune  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 123

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The temporary Hit Points from Invigorating Fear last for 1 minute._

You can exert your unsettling presence to subtly [[Actions/Demoralize]] others. When you do, Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language. You also gain the Invigorating Fear reaction. Your alternate form is a fox, which has the statistics of 1st-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]].
