# Frilled Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 57

You can flare your neck frill and flex your dorsal spines, [[Actions/Demoralize|Demoralizing]] your foes. When you do, Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language. You also gain the Threatening Approach action.
