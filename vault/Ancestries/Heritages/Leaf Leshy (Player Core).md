# Leaf Leshy  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 67

Your body is made mostly from natural foliage, and like a leaf tumbling from a tree, you land from falls with particular grace. You take no damage from [[Rules/Falling|falling]], regardless of the distance you fall.
