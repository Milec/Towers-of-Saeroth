# Scavenger Strix  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 135

Your ancestors originated from a land where food was scarce and threats were many. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]. You gain the [[Feats/Forager]] skill feat as a bonus feat. Your thoroughness when gathering food provides you a +1 circumstance bonus to Survival checks to [[Actions/Subsist]].
