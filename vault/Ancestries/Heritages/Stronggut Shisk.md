# Stronggut Shisk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 123

Your metabolism is slow, allowing you to go for days without food and process maladies at a slower rate. You can go for 1 week without food before you begin to [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst|starve]]. Additionally, the onset times and lengths of stages for all [[Traits/Disease|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison|poisons]] that affect you are increased by 50%. If the onset time or stage is instant or lasts only 1 round, this ability has no effect.
