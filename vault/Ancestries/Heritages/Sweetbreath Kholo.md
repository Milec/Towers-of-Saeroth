# Sweetbreath Kholo  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 17

You're a striped, pale-furred kholo with oddly pleasant breath, which you can use to entrance your prey. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Diplomacy). You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] if the target can smell your breath.
