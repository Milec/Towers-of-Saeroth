# Dragonscaled Kobold  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 21

Thanks to your warren's association with a dragon, your scales are sturdier than other kobolds'. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against dragon breath, effects with the [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] trait, and effects that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]].
