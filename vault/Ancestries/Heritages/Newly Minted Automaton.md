# Newly Minted Automaton  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 110

You are a brand-new automaton, likely constructed in the city of Quantium. While your body is largely the same as those created many years ago, your mind is fresh, and you learn new information at a rapid pace. You gain the [[Feats/Additional Lore (Player Core)|Additional Lore]] skill feat in one lore of your choice. You know one additional language from the languages that you have access to.
