# Winter Orc  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 71

Your ancestors survived in cold climates. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]], and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
