# Great Kholo  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 17

You're an imposing, powerful kholo, with tawny fur and brown spots on your hide. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 8 and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]].
