# Earthly Wilds Kitsune  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 123

You are a creature of the material world, with an affinity closer to the wilds than urban society. You gain a jaws unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your jaws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. Your alternate form is a fox, which has the statistics of 1st-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]].
