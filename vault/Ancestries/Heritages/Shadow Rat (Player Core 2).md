# Shadow Rat  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 29

Your ancestors lived in dark spaces underground, granting you dark fur and a vaguely unnatural mien. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] and can use Intimidation to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]]. When you [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] an animal, you don't take a penalty for not sharing a language with it. If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice.

 Animals' attitudes toward you begin one degree worse than normal, usually starting at [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly (Player Core)|unfriendly]] instead of indifferent for domesticated animals, and [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] instead of unfriendly for wild animals.
