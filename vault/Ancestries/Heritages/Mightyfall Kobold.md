# Mightyfall Kobold  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 40

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

Your proximity to a mighty [[Traits/Kaiju|kaiju]] has caused you to grow strong and hardy. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6. Instead of the normal attribute boosts and flaws, you can choose to gain a boost to Strength, a boost to Charisma, and a flaw in Intelligence.
