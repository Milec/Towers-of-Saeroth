# Tailed Goblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 37

You have a powerful tail, likely because you descend from a community of monkey goblins. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb]], you gain [[Feats/Combat Climber]] as a bonus feat, and you reduce the number of free hands required to Climb or [[Actions/Trip]] by one.

## Feats that require Tailed Goblin...

[[Feats/Hard Tail]], [[Feats/Tail Spin]], [[Feats/Tree Climber (Goblin)]]
