# Weaver Jotunborn  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 12

You’ve mastered the art of iivlar silk weaving, a technique that requires a fine attention to detail. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]]. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] when searching for hidden details like secret doors or traps.
