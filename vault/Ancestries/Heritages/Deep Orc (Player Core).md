# Deep Orc  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 71

Your calloused hands and red eyes speak to a life spent in the deep darkness of mountain caverns, where you learned to battle on rocky cliffs and survive with minimal resources. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Expertise]] skill feat for underground terrain and the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat.
