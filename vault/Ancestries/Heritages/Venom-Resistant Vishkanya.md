# Venom-Resistant Vishkanya  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 60

Your body contains not just venom but numerous antivenoms. You gain poison resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and each of your successful saving throws against a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] affliction reduces its stage by 2, or by 1 for a [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] poison. Each critical success against an ongoing poison reduces its stage by 3, or by 2 for a virulent poison.
