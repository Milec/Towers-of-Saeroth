# Shadow Rat  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 22

Your ancestors lived in dark spaces underground, granting you dark fur and a vaguely unnatural mien that unnerves sapient creatures and frightens animals. You gain the trained proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] and can use Intimidation to [[Actions/Coerce]] animals. When you [[Actions/Demoralize]] an animal, you don’t take a penalty for not sharing a language with it. If you would automatically become trained in Intimidation (from your background or class, for example), you become trained in another skill of your choice.

 [[Traits/Animal|Animals]]’ attitudes toward you begin one degree worse than normal, usually starting at [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]] for domesticated animals, and [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] instead of unfriendly for wild animals.
