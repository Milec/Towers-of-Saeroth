# Stickytoe Grippli  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 119

Your hands and feet exude a film that helps them adhere to surfaces. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude and Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] you. When ascending trees, vines, and other foliage, if you roll a success on the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb]], you get a critical success instead.
