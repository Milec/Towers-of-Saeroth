# Desert Elf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 25

You live under the desert's blazing heat, gaining incredible resilience to warm environments. You gain [[Traits/Fire|fire]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] are one step less extreme for you (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on).
