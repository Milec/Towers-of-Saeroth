# Makari Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 44

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

You have a trunk-like snout and a connection to the divine. The tradition of any spells or magical abilities you gain from a lizardfolk heritage or ancestry feat is [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] instead of its normal tradition (usually [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]). You gain your choice of the [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]] or [[Spells/Forbidding Ward (Player Core)|_forbidding ward_]] cantrips, which you can cast as a divine innate cantrip at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. When you cast this cantrip, it loses the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait, as you cast purely by roaring and flaring your trunk.
