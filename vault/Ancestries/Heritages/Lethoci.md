# Lethoci  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 42

You come from a kashrishi family adapted to coastal shores, or inland ponds and lakes. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim]]. If you critically fail an Athletics check to Swim, you get a failure instead.
