# Respite of Loam and Leaf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 78

You swore a vow to preserve the very foundation of sanctuary: the great earth and all that grows upon it. Your vow grants you the land's spiritual power; you gain one cantrip from the [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an innate primal spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. Your roots in the world grant you the [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] trait.  
**Additional Edict** cure or remove blight and pollution from the plants and soil you encounter
