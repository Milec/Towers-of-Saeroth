# Plane-Hopper Jotunborn  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 12

You were chosen for your smaller size that allows you to move between planar thresholds with ease. You generally serve as a messenger or scout, and planar exposure has granted you a spark of magical power. Instead of Large, your size is Medium. You gain one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult spell list]]. You can cast this spell as an occult [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]] at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
