# Mountaineer Samsaran  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 54

Your past incarnations dwelled in the caves on the snowcapped mountains of Tian Xia, so your body has become adept at surviving frigid storms. You gain [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You treat [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|environmental cold]] effects as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
