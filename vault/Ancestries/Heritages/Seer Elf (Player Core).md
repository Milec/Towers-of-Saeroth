# Seer Elf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 47

You have an inborn ability to detect and understand magical phenomena. You can cast the [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] cantrip as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Identify Magic]] and to [[Actions/Decipher Writing]] of a magical nature. These skill actions typically use the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] skill.
