# Empty Sky Kitsune  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 123

Your spirit is open to the secrets of beyond, granting you greater access to kitsune magic. You gain the [[Feats/Kitsune Spell Familiarity]] ancestry feat. Your alternate form is a common Medium humanoid ancestry prevalent where you grew up (typically [[Ancestries/Human|human]]), called a tailless form.
