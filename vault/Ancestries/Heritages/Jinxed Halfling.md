# Jinxed Halfling  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 46

You were born with a strange blessing: bereft of the typical halfling luck, you can instead manipulate the fortunes of others. You can never take the [[Feats/Halfling Luck]] feat, and you gain the Jinx action.
