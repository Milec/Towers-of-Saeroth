# Stormtossed Tengu  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 26

Whether due to a blessing from [[Setting/Deities/Hei Feng]] or hatching from your egg during a squall, you are resistant to storms. You gain electricity resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You automatically succeed at the flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by rain or fog.
