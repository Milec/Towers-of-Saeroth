# Artisan Android  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 71

Your body was originally designed to create works of art, complex tools, or maintain advanced machinery. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (or another skill if you're already trained in Crafting), and you gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat in a specialty of your choice.
