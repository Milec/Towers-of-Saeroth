# Sandstrider Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 25

Your thick scales help you retain water and combat the sun's glare. You gain fire resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). [[Rules/Temperature|Environmental heat effects]] are one step less extreme for you, and you can go 10 times as long as normal before you are affected by [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starvation or thirst]]. However, unless you wear protective gear or take shelter, environmental cold effects are one step more extreme for you.
