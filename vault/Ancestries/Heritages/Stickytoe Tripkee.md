# Stickytoe Tripkee  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 37

Your hands and feet exude a film that helps them adhere to surfaces. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude and Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], [[Actions/Reposition]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] you. When ascending trees, vines, and other foliage, if you roll a success on the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], you get a critical success instead.
