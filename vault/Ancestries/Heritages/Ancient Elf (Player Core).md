# Ancient Elf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 47

In your long life, you've dabbled in many paths and many styles. A typical ancient elf is at least 100 years old, though you might be younger at the GM's discretion. Choose a class other than your own. You gain the [[Traits/Multiclass (Player Core)|multiclass dedication]] feat for that class, even though you don't meet its level prerequisite. You must still meet its other prerequisites to gain the feat.
