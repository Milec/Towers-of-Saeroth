# Flexible Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 17

You've inherited flexibility beyond that of most humanoids. You don't treat tight spaces that don't require you to [[Actions/Squeeze]] as difficult terrain. In addition, you get a +1 circumstance bonus to checks when you attempt to [[Actions/Escape]].
