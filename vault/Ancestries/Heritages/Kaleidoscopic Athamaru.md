# Kaleidoscopic Athamaru  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 18

Your scales are a spectrum of color that shimmers in the light. In areas of [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] or [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. You also gain the Dazzle Seeker reaction.

 **Dazzle Seeker** ⤾ ([[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]]) **Trigger** A creature attempts a flat check to target you due to you being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from it; **Effect** You flash your bright scales in the creature's eyes, making it hard for them to pinpoint your exact location. The creature must roll the flat check twice and take the worse result.
