# Tsukumogami Poppet  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 48

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

You have the form and memories of a common tool. Choose one Lore skill related to what kind of tool you were; for instance, Cooking Lore for a kitchen knife or spoon. You're trained in this skill, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to Aid with this skill. If you take the [[Feats/Helpful Poppet]] feat, the bonus increases to +3.  
While most poppets are made of stuffing, cloth, or soft metals, some tsukumogami are made of other materials, changing the poppet weakness to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]. If your body is primarily wood or cloth, you have the normal poppet weakness to fire. If your body is primarily metal, you're instead weak to [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]; if its primarily ceramic, you're instead weak to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]].
