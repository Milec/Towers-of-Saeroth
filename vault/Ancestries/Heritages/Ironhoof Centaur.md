**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 30

Your hooves are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits.
