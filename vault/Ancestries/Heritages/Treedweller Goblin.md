# Treedweller Goblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 37

You have adapted particularly well to living in forested environments. As long as you are in a forest or jungle, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]], [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Subsist]], and your Survival DC to [[Actions/Cover Tracks]].
