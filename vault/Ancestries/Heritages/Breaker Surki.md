# Breaker Surki  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 48

Your claws are especially hard and can break through earth and predators alike. You gain a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile B]] traits.

- **Evolution** Your wrist nodes project magic into a durable digging wedge. You can spend an Interact action to increase your claw unarmed attack's damage to 1d6; grant it the [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Razing (Player Core 2)|razing]], and versatile force traits; and remove the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] trait. You can spend another action to deactivate the wedge and return your claw to its normal statistics.
- **Evolution** Your ankle nodes can emit grounding claw spikes of magic to hold you in place. If any effect would [[Rules/Forced Movement|force you to move]] 10 feet or more, you can choose to move only half the distance. The extra traction from your claw spikes allows you to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] with one hand occupied (or with both hands occupied if you have the [[Feats/Combat Climber (Player Core)|Combat Climber]] feat). You gain the Trench Digging reaction. **Trench Digging** **Trigger** You're subjected to [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]]; **Effect** You deploy your claw spikes to churn the earth asunder, creating [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] in each square of ground you're moved through.
