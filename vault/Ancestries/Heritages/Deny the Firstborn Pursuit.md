# Deny the Firstborn Pursuit  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 78

You swore a vow to shelter others from the First World's cruelties. Wise to [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey's]] mind-affecting abilities, your vow grants [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about fey.  
**Additional Edict** confront cruel fey you encounter (as long as you have a reasonable chance of success)
