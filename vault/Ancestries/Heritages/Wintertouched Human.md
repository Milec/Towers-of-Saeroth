# Wintertouched Human  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 11

The power of winter flows through you. This heritage is most common among the Jadwiga of Irrisen, due to their descent from Baba Yaga, and certain Erutaki touched by the spirits. You gain [[Traits/Cold|cold]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
