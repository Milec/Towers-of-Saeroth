# Gandharva  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 46

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

You're descended from the self-proclaimed musicians, poets, and dancers of the gods, with a body that's part humanoid and part horse or bird. If you gain wings, they come in many different brilliant colors. Due to your [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] connection, the tradition of any spells or magical abilities you gain from a sprite heritage or ancestry feat is [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] instead of its normal tradition. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (or another skill of your choice if you're already trained in Performance), and you can cast [[Spells/Summon Instrument (Player Core)|_summon instrument_]] as a divine innate cantrip.
