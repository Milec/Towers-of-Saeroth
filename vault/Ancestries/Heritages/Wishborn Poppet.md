# Wishborn Poppet  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 63

You were wished to life, either by a powerful spellcaster or by the earnest desire of an innocent person who loved you very much. You're living proof that a hopeful spirit can overcome any obstacle. If you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] or [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
