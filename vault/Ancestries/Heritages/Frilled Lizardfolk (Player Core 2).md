# Frilled Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 25

You can flare your neck frill and flex your dorsal spines, [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralizing]] your foes. When you do, Demoralize loses the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language. You also gain the Threatening Approach action.

 **Threatening Approach** two actions **Effect** You Stride to be adjacent to a foe and Demoralize that foe. If you succeed, the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]] instead of frightened 1.
