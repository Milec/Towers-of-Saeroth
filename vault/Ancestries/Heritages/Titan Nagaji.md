# Titan Nagaji  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 48

You were raised to be a warrior or a bodyguard, and your specialized diet and bulging muscles have made your scales as strong as armored plates. Your scales are medium armor in the [[Equipment/Armor Groups/Plate|plate]] armor group that grant a +4 item bonus to AC, a Dex cap of +1, a check penalty of –2, a Speed penalty of –5 feet, a Strength value of 16, and have the [[Traits/Comfort|comfort]] trait. You can never wear other armor or remove your scales. You can etch armor runes onto your scales.
