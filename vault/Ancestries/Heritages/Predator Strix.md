# Predator Strix  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 135

You come from a line of strix with exceptionally broad wings and lengthy talons. You gain a talon melee unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your talon attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
