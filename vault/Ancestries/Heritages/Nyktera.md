# Nyktera  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 129

You have batlike features, an affinity for batkind, and hearing that is second to none. As long as you can hear normally, you can use the [[Actions/Seek]] action to sense [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] creatures in a 60-foot cone instead of a 30-foot cone. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to locate undetected creatures that you could hear within 30 feet with a Seek action.
