# Deep Fetchling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 85

Your predecessors dwelled in the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane|Shadow Plane's]] deepest, most treacherous regions. You might cast a denser shadow, have a slower pulse, or find comfort in the embrace of darkness. You gain cold or negative resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), chosen when you gain this heritage.
