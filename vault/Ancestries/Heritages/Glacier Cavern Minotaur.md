# Glacier Cavern Minotaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 42

Your fur grows thick and warm, well suited to the frozen mountains in which you reside. You gain cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum of 1). [[Rules/Temperature|Environmental cold effects]] are one step less extreme for you.
