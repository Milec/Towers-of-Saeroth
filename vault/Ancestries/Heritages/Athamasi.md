# Athamasi  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 42

You have a set of small secondary arms adapted for climbing and hanging from trees. You can't hold or retrieve objects with these limbs, but you can [[Actions/Climb]] or [[Actions/Grab an Edge]] even if one or both of your primary hands are full. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] while Climbing.

## Feats that require Athamasi...

[[Feats/Scuttle Up]], [[Feats/Skilled Climber]]
