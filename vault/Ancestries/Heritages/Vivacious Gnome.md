# Vivacious Gnome  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 31

You have an incredibly strong connection to the positive energy that flows through the [[Setting/Planes/First World]], making it harder to attack your life force with negative energy. You gain [[Traits/Negative|negative]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). When you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] condition, the condition affects you as if its value was 1 lower than it actually is (doomed 1 has no effect, doomed 2 causes you to die at dying 3, and so on).
