# Aquatic Elf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 19

You are an aquatic elf who spends most of your life underwater. You have the ability to breathe both air and water, but you cannot live indefinitely on the surface. You gain the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait, and you have a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] of 30 feet. At least once a week, you must take an 8-hour period of rest underwater. If you do not, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], and you cannot recover from this fatigue until you rest underwater.
