# Smokeworker Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 49

Your family have been alchemists, engineers, and scientists for generations, on projects bringing smoke and fire to the field of battle. You gain [[Traits/Fire|fire]] resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You automatically succeed at the DC 5 flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by smoke.
