# Twilight Halfling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 59

Your ancestors performed many secret acts under the concealing cover of dusk, whether for good or ill, and over time they developed the ability to see in twilight beyond even the usual keen sight of halflings. You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].
