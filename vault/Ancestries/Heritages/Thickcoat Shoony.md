# Thickcoat Shoony  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 71

You are a rare shoony who hails from colder climes. You gain cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects as if they were one step less extreme]] (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on). You don’t need to succeed at a flat check to target a concealed creature if that creature is concealed only by snow. Unless you wear protective gear or take shelter, environmental heat effects are one step more extreme for you.
