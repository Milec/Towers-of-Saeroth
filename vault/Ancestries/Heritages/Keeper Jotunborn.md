# Keeper Jotunborn  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 12

Your work as an iivlar keeper has granted you a greater understanding of these sacred creatures and the ability to track them, as elusive as they may be. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]], and you gain the [[Feats/Survey Wildlife (Player Core)|Survey Wildlife]] skill feat. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] animals.
