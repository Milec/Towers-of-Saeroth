# Woodstalker Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 45

You move easily through thick forest and jungle, using every branch or bush. You can always use the [[Actions/Take Cover]] action when in forest or jungle terrain to gain cover, even if you're not next to an obstacle you would normally be able to Take Cover behind. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Stalker]] feat, even if you're not trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]], and you must choose underbrush as your chosen terrain.
