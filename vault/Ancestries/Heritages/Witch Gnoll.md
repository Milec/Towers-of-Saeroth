# Witch Gnoll  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 112

You're a shaggy, dark-furred gnoll capable of making some truly uncanny sounds. You can cast the [[Spells/Ghost Sound|_ghost sound_]] cantrip as an occult innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Impersonate]] and [[Actions/Create a Diversion]] when using only your voice.
