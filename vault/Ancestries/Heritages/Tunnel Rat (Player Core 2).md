# Tunnel Rat  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 29

You can easily compress your body and squeeze through gaps. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Squeeze (Player Core)|Quick Squeeze]] feat as a bonus feat, even if you aren't trained in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]. Tight spaces not tight enough to require the Squeeze action aren't [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for you.
