# Caveclimber Kobold  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 37

You live in a vertically-oriented home, and you're a consummate climber. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You can use your clawed feet and tail to [[Actions/Climb]], leaving your hands free. Additionally, if you succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Climb, you critically succeed instead.
