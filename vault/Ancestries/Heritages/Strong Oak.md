# Strong Oak  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 36

Most ghorans have bodies of pliable vines and tough stems, but you're covered in sturdy, rough bark and your legs are stable as roots. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Grapple]] or [[Actions/Trip]] you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against effects that would [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grab]] you, [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrain]] you, or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance]].
