# Spellhorn Kobold  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 21

Since hatching in the vicinity of a powerful source of magic, a trace of it flows through your veins. Choose one common [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane spell list]]. You can Cast this Spell as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You are trained in the spell attack modifier and spell DC statistics, and your spellcasting ability is Charisma.
