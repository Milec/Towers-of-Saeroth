# Liminal Catfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 17

You've inherited a closeness to the far corners of the world, where the boundaries between dimensions grow thin. You can cast [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] as an occult innate cantrip heightened to half your level, rounded up. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about creatures that originated outside of the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]].
