# Deny Lady Nanbyo's Charity  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 78

You swore a vow to release others from natural calamities. Your vow grants you the strength to carry 1 more Bulk than normal before becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]] and up to a maximum of 2 more Bulk, as well as a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] or [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].  
**Additional Edict** do your utmost to aid or rescue those trapped or affected by natural disasters
