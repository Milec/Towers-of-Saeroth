# Dijiang  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 46

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

You have the form of a faceless, leathery ball with short fur, six legs, and a connection to primordial chaos. While you have no face, head, mouth, eyes, or ears, you can somehow hear, speak, see, eat, and breathe just fine (though it's unclear exactly how). Your erratic nature means you often react to stimuli in unpredictable ways. When you roll a failure (though not a critical failure) on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect, you get a success instead.
