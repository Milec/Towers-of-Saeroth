# Longsnout Rat  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 21

The long snouts that run in your family give you a keener sense of smell than most ratfolk. You gain [[Rules/Scent|imprecise scent]] with a range of 30 feet. This means you can use your sense of smell to determine a creature’s location. The GM will usually double the range if you’re downwind from the creature or halve the range if you’re upwind.

 In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]] a creature or object within the range of your scent.
