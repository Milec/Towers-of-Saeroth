# Shimmertongue Nagaji  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 51

You were born with a keen sense for magic, able to taste its unique effects in the air. You gain magicsense as a  [[Rules/Vague Senses (Player Core)|vague sense]] that has a range of 30 feet—like all vague senses, it's only about as precise as an average human's sense of smell, meaning you generally can predict only if magic is present; however, each tradition of magic has a unique taste to you, allowing you to identify the tradition of magic present.
