# Virtuous Tanuki  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 66

Many tanuki carry a gourd of [[Equipment/Alcohol|alcohol]] to remind themselves to act with virtue, and by these standards, you're quite virtuous indeed. You gain [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to half your level (minimum 1). You can eat and drink things when you're [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]]. You can't become incapacitated by conventional [[Equipment/Alcohol|alcohol]] if you don't wish to be.
