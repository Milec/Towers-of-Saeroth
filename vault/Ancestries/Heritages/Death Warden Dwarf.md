# Death Warden Dwarf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 35

Your ancestors have been tomb guardians for generations, and the power they cultivated to ward off necromancy has passed on to you. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
