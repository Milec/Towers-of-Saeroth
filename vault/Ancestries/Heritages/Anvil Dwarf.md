# Anvil Dwarf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 19

You are a descendant of a famed crafter and have your own amazing talent. Other dwarves might consider this a blessing from your ancestors or from the [[Setting/Deities/Torag|Forgefather]] himself, depending on where you grew up. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Crafting) and gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat, but you can pick two different specialties instead of one.
