# Mage Automaton  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 36

The chamber housing your core has a more direct connection to the rest of your humanoid shape, allowing you to tap into your core's magical energy. You gain one cantrip from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell level equal to half your level rounded up.
