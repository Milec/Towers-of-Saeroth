# Pearl Dragonet  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 210

You have pearlescent scales and a round body, with short legs, small wings, and a long tail. The shape of your body lets you absorb a blow and roll away.

**Bounce Away** ⤾ **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** A melee Strike hits you and deals bludgeoning damage; **Effect** You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] equal to your level to bludgeoning damage from the triggering Strike, then [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed. You can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] instead of Striding if you have a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]].
