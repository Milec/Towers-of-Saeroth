# Budding Speaker Centaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 30

You were born with a spark of magic that could set you on the path to becoming a Greenspeaker or Faithspeaker. Select [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] or [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]]. If you selected divine, you're a Faithspeaker. If you selected primal, you're a Greenspeaker. This choice can't be changed. You gain one [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] from the chosen spell list. You can cast this spell as an innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
