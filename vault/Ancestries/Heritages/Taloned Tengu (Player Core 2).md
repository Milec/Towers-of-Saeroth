# Taloned Tengu  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 33

Your talons are every bit as sharp and strong as your beak. You gain a talons unarmed attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. Your talons are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], and [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]] traits.
