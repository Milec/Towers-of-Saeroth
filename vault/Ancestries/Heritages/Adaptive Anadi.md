# Adaptive Anadi  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 103

You descend from a line of anadi who worked to perfect their transformation magic, allowing them to integrate into a wider variety of cultures. Choose a common, Medium [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] ancestry. Your human form is replaced with a form that matches this choice. You also gain the [[Feats/Adopted Ancestry]] feat for your chosen humanoid ancestry.
