# Gutsy Halfling  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 51

Your family line is known for keeping a level head and staving off fear when the chips were down, making them wise leaders and sometimes even heroes. When you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
