# Kanchil  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 46

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

You have the head of a mouse deer and hooves instead of feet, with a reputation for being clever and persuasive—able to get yourself out of sticky situations with wordplay and a bit of luck. You become trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Deception). You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] when specifically attempting to avoid danger or punishment (such as trying to convince a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] not to eat you), to Deception DCs against [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] checks to uncover such lies, and to initiative rolls when you roll Deception for initiative.
