# Benthic Athamaru  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 18

You are accustomed to rapidly ascending and descending through the depths of the ocean through careful manipulation of your swim bladder. [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] up or down does not count as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for you. Additionally, your eyesight has adapted to dark waters, granting you [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]].
