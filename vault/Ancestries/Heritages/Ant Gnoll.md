# Ant Gnoll  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 111

You're a sharp-featured, big-eared gnoll about three feet tall. Many are skeptical that you are in fact a gnoll. Your size is Small instead of Medium. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Deception). You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception checks to [[Actions/Lie]] when specifically claiming innocence, to Deception DCs against [[Actions/Sense Motive]] checks to uncover such lies, and to initiative checks when you roll Deception for initiative.
