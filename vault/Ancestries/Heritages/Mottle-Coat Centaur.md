# Mottle-Coat Centaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

Your coat is mottled or striped, granting you natural camouflage in your home environment. Select a terrain from the following: arctic, desert, forest, mountain, plains, or swamp. In your selected terrain, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]].
