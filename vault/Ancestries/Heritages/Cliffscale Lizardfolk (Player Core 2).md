# Cliffscale Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 25

Your toes are adapted for gripping and climbing. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber (Player Core)|Combat Climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and as long as you aren't wearing footwear, you can use the sticky pads on your feet to climb, leaving your hands free. Additionally, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to climb, you get a critical success instead.
