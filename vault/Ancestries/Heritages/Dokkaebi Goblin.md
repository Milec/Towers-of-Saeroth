# Dokkaebi Goblin  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 34

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

Your ancestors mastered illusion-based magic, giving you an innate understanding of it. You can cast [[Spells/Figment|_figment_]] as an innate [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] cantrip at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Will saves against [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusions]].
