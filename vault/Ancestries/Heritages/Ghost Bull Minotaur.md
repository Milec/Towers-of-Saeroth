# Ghost Bull Minotaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 42

Your fur is as pale as death, possibly from some connection you or your family has to the afterlife, which lets you supernaturally find your way. You can cast [[Spells/Know the Way|_know the way_]] as an occult innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus against spells or effects that cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] condition.
