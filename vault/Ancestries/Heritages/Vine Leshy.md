# Vine Leshy  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 53

The prehensile vines woven into your body grant you unmatched skill at climbing. You do not need to have any hands free to [[Actions/Climb]]. Additionally, if you roll a success on an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Climb, you get a critical success instead.
