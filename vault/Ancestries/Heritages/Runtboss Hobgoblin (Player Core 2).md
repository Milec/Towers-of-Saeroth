# Runtboss Hobgoblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 13

You come from a long line of hobgoblins who commanded [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblins]]. You are smaller than other hobgoblins, but goblins still listen to any commands you bellow. You gain the [[Feats/Group Coercion (Player Core)|Group Coercion]] skill feat. If you roll a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] a goblin, you get a critical success instead; if you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead.
