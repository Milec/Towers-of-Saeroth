# Razortooth Goblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 48

Your family's teeth are formidable weapons. You gain a jaws unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your jaws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
