# Xyloshi  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 42

You have prominent neck muscles that allow you to use your facial horn as a tool or weapon. You gain a horn [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. Your horn is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and has the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and unarmed traits.
