# Shadow of the Hermit  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 72

Your shadow seems somehow full of secrets and mysteries— secrets that it shares with you. Choose one cantrip from the [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spell list. You can cast this spell as an occult innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.
