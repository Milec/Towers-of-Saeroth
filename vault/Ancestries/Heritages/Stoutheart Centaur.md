# Stoutheart Centaur  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 31

You're a hardy centaur, firm of stance and strong of heart. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 8 and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] and to your Reflex DC to avoid being tripped.
