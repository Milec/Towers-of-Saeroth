# Arctic Elf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 47

You dwell deep in the frozen north and have gained incredible resilience against cold environments, granting you cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
