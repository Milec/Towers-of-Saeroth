# Thickskin Tripkee  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 37

Your thick leathery skin resembles that of a toad. You gain 8 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]].
