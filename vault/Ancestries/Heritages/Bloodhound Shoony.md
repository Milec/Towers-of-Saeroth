# Bloodhound Shoony  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 71

Your ancestors were famous trackers, and you carry in your blood that same gift of the hunt. You gain a special sense: [[Rules/Scent|imprecise scent]] with a range of 30 feet. This means you can use your sense of smell to determine the location of a creature, but it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]]. In addition, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Track]] creatures that you have previously sensed with your scent.
