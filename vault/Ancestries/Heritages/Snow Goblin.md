# Snow Goblin  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 48

You are acclimated to living in frigid lands and have skin ranging from sky blue to navy in color, as well as blue fur. You gain cold resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on).
