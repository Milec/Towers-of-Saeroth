# Forge Dwarf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 43

You have a remarkable adaptation to hot environments. This grants you fire resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1), and you treat [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on).
