# Forge-Blessed Dwarf  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 109

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) A Forge-Blessed dwarf can use their granted 1st-level spell once per scenario._

You are from a family in Highhelm that venerates a particular dwarven deity above all others. Your birth was blessed by your family's patron deity, granting you a fraction of their power. Choose one deity from the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Dwarven Gods|dwarven pantheon]]. You can cast the 1st-level spell they grant clerics (such as [[Spells/Soothe|_soothe_]] if your family's patron is [[Setting/Deities/Bolka]]) as an innate divine spell once per week.
