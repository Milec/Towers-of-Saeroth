# Lotus Leshy  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 42

You effortlessly float on the surface of water. You can walk on the surface of still water and other non-damaging liquids, moving at half your normal Speed. You can attempt to walk along the surface of flowing water as well, still moving at half Speed, but doing so requires a successful [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] using the DC of a [[Actions/Swim]] check to move through the water; on a failure, you fall into the water. This Acrobatics check doesn't use an action.
