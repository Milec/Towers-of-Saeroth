# Technological Fleshwarp  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 91

Your transformation is due to manipulation by advanced technology, like that found in Numeria. Your emotions are dulled and hard to influence. When you roll a success on a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
