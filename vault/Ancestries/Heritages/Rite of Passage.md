# Rite of Passage  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 107

Your exoskeleton remains connected with the plants that created it. You can ignore difficult terrain and uneven ground caused by undergrowth. In addition, when you use the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] skill to [[Actions/Balance]] on narrow surfaces or uneven ground within forests, you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]]. When you roll a success attempting one of these Acrobatics checks, you get a critical success instead.
