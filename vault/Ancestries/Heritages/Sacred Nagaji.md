# Sacred Nagaji  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 48

You stand out from most nagaji, with the upper body of a beautiful human and the lower body of a green or white snake. Legends claim your ancestors were faithful snakes uplifted by [[Setting/Deities/Nalinivati]] rather than nagaji created by the goddess. Instead of a fangs unarmed attack, you have a tail attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group, and has the [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Grapple]] or [[Actions/Trip]] you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against effects that would [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grab]] you, [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrain]] you, or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
