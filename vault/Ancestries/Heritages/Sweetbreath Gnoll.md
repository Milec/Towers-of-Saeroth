# Sweetbreath Gnoll  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 111

You're a striped, pale-furred gnoll with oddly pleasant breath, which you can use to entrance your prey. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (or another skill if you were already trained in Diplomacy). You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] if the target can smell your breath.
