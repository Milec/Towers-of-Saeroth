# Tidepool Dragonet  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 210

You have a slender, eel-like body with azure scales. Your wings sweep along your body like massive fins, making you well suited for watery terrain. You gain the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait, and a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] of 15 feet. You add [[Setting/Languages/Thalassic]] to your list of known languages.
