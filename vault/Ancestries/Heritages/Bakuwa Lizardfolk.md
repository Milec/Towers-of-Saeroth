# Bakuwa Lizardfolk  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 44

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

Your body is covered in bony plates. Your plates are medium armor in the [[Equipment/Armor Groups/Plate|plate armor]] group that grant a +4 item bonus to AC, a Dex cap of +1, a check penalty of –2, a Speed penalty of –5 feet, a Strength value of +3, and have the [[Traits/Aquadynamic (Howl of the Wild)|aquadynamic]] and [[Traits/Comfort (Player Core)|comfort]] traits. You can never wear other armor or remove your plates. You can etch armor runes onto your plates.
