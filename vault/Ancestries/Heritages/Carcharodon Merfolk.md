# Carcharodon Merfolk  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 35

As a fearsome shark merfolk, your lower body is similar to that of one of the apex predators of the ocean. Like a shark, you can smell blood from quite a distance. You gain [[Rules/Scent|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a range of 30 feet. However, you can smell spilled blood at a range of 120 feet in the air and 500 feet in the water.
