# Elusive Vishkanya  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 60

Your flexible body seems unnaturally fluid to some, though they can rarely put a finger on what seems strange about you. You can escape foes with ease. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Escape]]. Additionally, you move twice as fast when [[Actions/Squeeze|Squeezing]].
