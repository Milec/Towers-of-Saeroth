# Created Fleshwarp  *Heritage*

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 91

You were created through occult or alchemical processes, such as being grown in a vat or stitched together from the body parts of other creatures by a scientist in Ustalav. Your manufactured body is durable; you don't need to eat and can't [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst|starve]]. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|diseases]].
