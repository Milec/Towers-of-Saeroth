# Kijimuna Gnome  *Heritage*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 38

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Tian Xia have access to this uncommon heritage_

Your ancestors lived in the trees and fished in all the waters of Tian Xia. You gain your choice of the following benefits. Once made, this choice can't be changed.

- You can climb any banyan. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber (Player Core)|Combat Climber]] feat, and if you roll a success on the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], you get a critical success instead.
- You can catch any fish. You gain a [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|swim]] Speed of 15 feet.
