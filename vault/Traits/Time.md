# Time  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 2]] pg. 310

Time creatures are natives of the Dimension of Time. They do not age, and while some may be driven by supernatural hungers, they do not need to eat or drink to survive. They can survive the basic environmental effects of the [[Setting/Planes/Dimension of Time]].
