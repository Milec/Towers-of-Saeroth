# Mutagen  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 634

An [[Traits/Elixir|elixir]] with the mutagen trait temporarily transmogrifies the subject's body and alters its mind. A mutagen always conveys one or more beneficial effects paired with one or more detrimental effects. Mutagens are [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effects, meaning you can benefit from only one at a time.
