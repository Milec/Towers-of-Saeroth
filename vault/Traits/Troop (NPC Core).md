# Troop  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 238

A troop is an organized collection of component creatures, typically Small or Medium in size, working as a cohesive whole. Over the course of enough attacks and downed comrades, troops shrink in size. A troop typically has the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Defenses (NPC Core)|troop defenses]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]] abilities, and most troops have weaknesses to area damage and splash damage. Because they consist of multiple discrete creatures, they can't be summoned.
