# Sahkil  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -162- Ruins of the Radiant Siege|Pathfinder #162: Ruins of the Radiant Siege]] pg. 84

Sahkils are fiends that delight in spreading fear and unease among mortal creatures. They typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]], immunity to fear, and weakness to good.
