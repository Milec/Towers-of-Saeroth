# Kickback  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 234

A kickback weapon is extra powerful and difficult to use due to its high recoil. A kickback weapon deals 1 additional damage with all attacks. Firing a kickback weapon gives a –2 circumstance penalty to the attack roll, but characters with 14 or more Strength ignore the penalty. Attaching a kickback weapon to a deployed bipod, [[Equipment/Tripod (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|tripod]], or other stabilizer can lower or negate this penalty.
