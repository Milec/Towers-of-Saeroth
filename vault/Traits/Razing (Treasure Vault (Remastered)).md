# Razing  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 221

Razing weapons are particularly good at damaging objects, structures, and vehicles. Whenever you deal damage to an object (including [shields](Shields.aspx) and [[Bestiary/Families/Animated Object|animated objects]]), structure, or vehicle with a razing weapon, the object takes an amount of additional damage equal to double the number of weapon damage dice.
