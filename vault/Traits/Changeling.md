# Changeling  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 266

A creature with this trait has the changeling versatile heritage. Changelings are the children of [[Bestiary/Families/Hag|hags]] and members of other [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] ancestries. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by changelings.
