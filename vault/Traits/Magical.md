# Magical  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 633

Something with the magical trait is imbued with magical energies not tied to a specific tradition of magic. A magical item radiates a magic aura infused with its dominant school of magic.

 Some items or effects are closely tied to a particular tradition of magic. In these cases, the item has the [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal|primal]] trait instead of the magical trait. Any of these traits indicate that the item is magical.
