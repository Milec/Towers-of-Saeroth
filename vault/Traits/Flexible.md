# Flexible  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 275

The armor is flexible enough that it doesn't hinder most actions. You don't apply its check penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks.
