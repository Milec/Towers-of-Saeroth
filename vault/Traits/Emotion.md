# Emotion  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 631

This effect alters a creature's emotions. Effects with this trait always have the [[Traits/Mental|mental]] trait as well. Creatures with special training or that have mechanical or artificial intelligence are immune to emotion effects.
