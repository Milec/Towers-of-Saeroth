# Venomous  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 119

These weapons inject poison into every hit. When you hit a creature with this weapon, it deals an additional 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. This increases to 2 persistent poison damage if the weapon has a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking rune]].
