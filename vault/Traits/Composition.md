# Composition  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 629

To cast a composition cantrip or focus spell, you usually use a type of [[Rules/Skills/Performance]]. If the spell includes a verbal component, you must use an [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] performance, and if it includes a somatic component, you must use a [[Traits/Visual|visual]] one. The spell gains all the traits of the performance you used. You can cast only one composition spell each turn, and you can have only one active at a time. If you cast a new composition spell, any ongoing effects from your previous composition spell end immediately.
