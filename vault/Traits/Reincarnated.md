# Reincarnated  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -198- No Breath to Cry|Pathfinder #198: No Breath to Cry]] pg. 79

This rare ancestry feat is available to any
character who has reincarnated at least once, either as
part of their backstory or because of a reincarnation
that took place during play.
