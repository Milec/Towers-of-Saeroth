# Shove  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 283

You can use this weapon to [[Actions/Shove]] with the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill even if you don't have a free hand. This uses the weapon's [[Traits/Reach|reach]] (if different from your own) and adds the weapon's item bonus to attack rolls as an item bonus to the Athletics check. If you critically fail a check to Shove using the weapon, you can drop the weapon to take the effects of a failure instead of a critical failure.
