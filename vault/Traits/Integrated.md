# Integrated  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 219

This shield has been created to include a weapon in its construction, which works like an [[Traits/Attached|attached]] weapon but can't be removed from the shield. This also prevents other attached weapons from being added to the shield. The integrated weapon's damage is listed alongside the trait, such as “integrated d6 S,” with any traits in parentheses. Unless otherwise noted in the shield's description, an integrated weapon is a martial weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]] weapon group and requires one hand to attack with. The attached weapon can have [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|runes]] etched onto it like other attached weapons. You can continue fighting normally with the integrated weapon if the shield is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]], but if the shield is destroyed, so is the weapon.
