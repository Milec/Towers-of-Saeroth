# Fetchling  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 364

A creature with this trait is a member of the fetchling ancestry. Fetchlings were once [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|human]], but generations of life in [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]] have transformed them.
