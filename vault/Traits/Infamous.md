# Infamous  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 543

The Infamous trait increases your Infamy score when the structure is built. Some structures have both [[Traits/Famous]] and Infamous traits; in this case apply the one that matches your kingdom's preference (see [[Rules/Fame and Infamy]]).
