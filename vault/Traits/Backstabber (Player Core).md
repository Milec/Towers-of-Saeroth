# Backstabber  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 282

When you hit an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature, this weapon deals 1 precision damage in addition to its normal damage. The precision damage increases to 2 if the weapon is a _+3 weapon_.
