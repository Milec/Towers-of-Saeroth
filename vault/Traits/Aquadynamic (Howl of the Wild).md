# Aquadynamic  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 216

This armor is made for use underwater, with streamlined design and buoyant materials used in strategic places. You don’t apply the armor’s [[Rules/Check Penalty (Player Core)|check penalty]] to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks in water or similar liquids.
