# Two-Hand  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 283

This weapon can be wielded with two hands. Doing so changes its weapon damage die to the indicated value. This change applies to all the weapon's damage dice, such as those from _[[Equipment/Striking|striking]]_ runes.
