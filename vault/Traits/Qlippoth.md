# Qlippoth  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 2]] pg. 309

A family of fiends hailing from the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]], most qlippoth are chaotic evil. Their appearance affects the minds of non-qlippoth that view them.
