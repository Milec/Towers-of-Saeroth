# Undead  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 637

Once living, these creatures were infused after death with negative energy and soul-corrupting evil magic. When reduced to 0 Hit Points, an undead creature is destroyed. Undead creatures are damaged by [[Traits/Positive|positive]] energy, are healed by [[Traits/Negative|negative]] energy, and don't benefit from [[Traits/Healing|healing]] effects.
