# Vigilante  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 218

Your class feats and [[Archetypes/Vigilante (Player Core 2)|vigilante]] feats are associated with your vigilante identity, and using them while in your social identity may risk exposing you as a vigilante. If your identity is exposed to the public, you lose the benefits of [[Feats/Vigilante Dedication (Player Core 2)|Vigilante Dedication]] to disguising yourself, but you can spend 1 week of [[Rules/Downtime (Player Core)|downtime]] to create a new social identity.
