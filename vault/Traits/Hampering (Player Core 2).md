# Hampering  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 274

A weapon with the hampering trait includes a disruptive limb or flange. After you hit with the weapon, you can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to give the target a –10-foot circumstance penalty to all Speeds. The penalty ends after the target takes a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, at the start of your next turn, if you attack with the weapon, or if you move out of reach of the target, whichever comes first.
