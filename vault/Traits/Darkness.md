# Darkness  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 630

Darkness effects extinguish non-magical light in the area, and can [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] less powerful magical [[Traits/Light|light]]. You must usually target light magic with your darkness magic directly to counteract the light, but some darkness spells automatically attempt to counteract light.
