# Jotunborn  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 220

A creature with this trait is a member of the [[Rules/Sidebars/Jotunborn Adventurers|jotunborn]] ancestry. Jotunborn are large people forged from the blood of an ancient group of giants called titans and usually have low-light vision. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by jotunborn. A weapon with this trait is created and used by jotunborn.
