# Injection  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 41

This weapon can be filled with an [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]. Immediately after a successful attack with the weapon, you can inject the target with the loaded poison with a single [[Rules/Interact]] action. Refilling the weapon with a new substance requires three Interact actions, and uses both hands.
