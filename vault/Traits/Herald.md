# Herald  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 85

Nearly all deities have a specific representative known as a herald; heralds are as diverse in their appearances and abilities as the gods themselves. A creature with the herald type is always [[Traits/Unique|unique]].
