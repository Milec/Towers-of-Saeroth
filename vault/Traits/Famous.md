# Famous  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 543

The Famous trait increases your Fame score when the structure is built. Some structures have both Famous and [[Traits/Infamous]] traits; in this case apply the one that matches your kingdom's preference (see [[Rules/Fame and Infamy]]).
