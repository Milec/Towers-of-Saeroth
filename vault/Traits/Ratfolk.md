# Ratfolk  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 269

A creature with this trait is a member of the ratfolk ancestry. Ratfolk are [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]] who resemble rats. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by ratfolk.
