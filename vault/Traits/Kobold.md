# Kobold  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 268

A creature with this trait is a member of the kobold ancestry. Kobolds are reptilian [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]] who are usually Small and typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]]. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by kobolds.
