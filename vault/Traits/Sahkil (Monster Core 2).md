# Sahkil  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 365

Sahkils are fiends that delight in spreading fear and unease among mortal creatures. They typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]], immunity to [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and weakness to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
