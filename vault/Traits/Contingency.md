# Contingency  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 252

Spells with this trait grant you an action during the spell's effects, typically a reaction with a special trigger. They typically have a long duration, such as 24 hours. You can have only one spell with the contingency trait, or one [[Spells/Contingency|_contingency_]] spell, active at a time. If you cast another spell with the contingency trait or _contingency_, the newer casting supersedes the older.
