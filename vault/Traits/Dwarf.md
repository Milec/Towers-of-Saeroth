# Dwarf  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 631

A creature with this trait is a member of the dwarf ancestry. Dwarves are stout folk who often live underground and typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]]. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by dwarves. An item with this trait is created and used by dwarves.
