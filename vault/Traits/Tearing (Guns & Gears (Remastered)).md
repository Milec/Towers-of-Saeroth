# Tearing  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 236

This weapon is edged with curved teeth that leave bleeding wounds. When you hit a creature with this weapon, it deals an additional 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. This increases to 2 persistent bleed damage if the weapon has a _[[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]]_ rune.
