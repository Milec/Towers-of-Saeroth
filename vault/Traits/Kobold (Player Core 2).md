# Kobold  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 317

A creature with this trait is a member of the [[Ancestries/Kobold (Player Core 2)|kobold]] ancestry, reptilian humanoids who are usually Small and typically have [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]]. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by kobolds.
