# Positive  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 635

Effects with this trait heal living creatures with positive energy, deal positive energy damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]], or manipulate positive energy. Planes with this trait are awash with life energy of such intensity that it can harm living creatures. Creatures with this trait are natives of the [[Setting/Planes/Positive Energy Plane]]. They can survive the basic environmental effects of the Positive Energy Plane.
