# Tearing  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 222

This weapon is edged with curved teeth that leave bleeding wounds. When you hit a creature with this weapon, it deals an additional 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. This increases to 2 persistent bleed damage if the weapon has a greater striking rune.
