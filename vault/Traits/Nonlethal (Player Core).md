# Nonlethal  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 282

Attacks with this weapon are [[Rules/Nonlethal Attacks (Player Core)|nonlethal]], and are used to knock creatures unconscious instead of kill them. You can use a nonlethal weapon to make a lethal attack with a –2 circumstance penalty.
