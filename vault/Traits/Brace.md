# Brace  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 24

A brace weapon is effective at damaging moving opponents. When you [[Actions/Ready]] to Strike an opponent that moves within your reach, until the start of your next turn Strikes with the brace weapon deal an additional 2 precision damage for each weapon damage die it has.
