# Artifact  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 250

Items with this trait are artifacts. These magic items can't be crafted by normal means, and they can't be damaged by normal means. Artifacts are always [[Traits/Rare|rare]] or [[Traits/Unique|unique]].
