# Dragon  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 631

Dragons are reptilian creatures, often winged or with the power of flight. Most are able to use a breath weapon and are immune to [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralysis]].
