# Duergar  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 631

Subterranean kin of the [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarves]], duergar typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]] and immunity to [[Traits/Poison|poison]]. They are not easily fooled by [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]].
