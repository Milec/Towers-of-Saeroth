# Aeon  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 345

These monitors are the self-styled defenders of reality. Traditional aeons have dualistic natures and forms, and they hold a dichotomy of interests, though axiomites and inevitables do not. Aeons other than [[Bestiary/Axiomite|axiomites]] and [[Traits/Inevitable|inevitables]] communicate via a strange telepathic hodgepodge of sensory sending called envisioning.
