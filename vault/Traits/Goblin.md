# Goblin  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 632

A creature with this trait can be one of several kinds of creature, including [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblins]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Cantorian Spring|hobgoblins]], and [[Bestiary/Families/Bugbear|bugbears]]. Goblins tend to have [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]. An ability with this trait can be used or chosen only by goblins. A weapon with this trait is created and used by goblins.
