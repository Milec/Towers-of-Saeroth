# Gnome  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 632

A creature with this trait is a member of the gnome ancestry. Gnomes are small people skilled at magic who seek out new experiences and usually have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by gnomes. A weapon with this trait is created and used by gnomes.
