# Beast  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 629

A creature similar to an [[Traits/Animal|animal]] but with an Intelligence modifier of –3 or higher is usually a beast. Unlike an animal, a beast might be able to speak and reason.
