# Dream  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 2]] pg. 308

Creatures native to the [[Setting/Planes/Dreamlands|Dimension of Dreams]] can be any alignment and possess a diverse array of abilities, although those associated with the nightmare realm of Leng are almost always evil and immune to that realm’s freezing temperatures.
