# Backstabber  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 282

When you hit a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature, this weapon deals 1 precision damage in addition to its normal damage. The precision damage increases to 2 if the weapon is a _+3 weapon_.
