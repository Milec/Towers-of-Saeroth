# Aquadynamic  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 216

This armor is designed for use underwater, with streamlined design and buoyant materials used in strategic places. You don't apply the armor's check penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks in water or similar liquids.
