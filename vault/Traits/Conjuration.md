# Conjuration  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 630

Effects and magic items with this trait are associated with the conjuration school of magic, typically involving [[Traits/Summoned|summoning]], creation, [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]], or moving things from place to place.
