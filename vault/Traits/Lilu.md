# Lilu  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -190- The Choosing|Pathfinder #190: The Choosing]] pg. 88

All lilus are chaotic evil [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]] who can fly, are immune to [[Traits/Disease|disease]], have mixed avian and [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] appearances, possess [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]] and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]], and resist physical damage (except from [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]]). Most have a selection of [[Traits/Primal|primal]] innate spells (often including a primal version of [[Spells/Dimension Door|_dimension door_]])
