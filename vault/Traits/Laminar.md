# Laminar  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 220

The armor is made up of layered sections, so when it breaks, it isn't as much of a problem. The status penalty to AC if this armor is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]] is –1 for broken medium armor, –2 for broken heavy armor, or no penalty for broken light armor.
