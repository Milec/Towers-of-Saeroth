# Noisy  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 275

This armor is loud and likely to alert others to your presence. The armor's check penalty applies to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks even if you meet the required Strength score.
