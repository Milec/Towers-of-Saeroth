# Construct  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 2]] pg. 346

A construct is an artificial creature empowered by a force other than [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]. Constructs are often [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]]; they are immune to bleed damage, [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks, [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] conditions; and they may have Hardness based on the materials used to construct their bodies. Constructs are not living creatures, nor are they [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. When reduced to 0 Hit Points, a construct creature is destroyed.
