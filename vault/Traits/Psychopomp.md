# Psychopomp  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 347

A family of monitors spawned within the [[Setting/Planes/Axis|Boneyard]] to convey souls to the Outer Planes, most psychopomps are true neutral. They typically have darkvision, lifesense, and spirit touch, and they are immune to death effects.
