# Shadow  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 636

Magic with this trait involves shadows or the energy of the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]]. Creatures with this trait are natives of the Shadow Plane. They can survive the basic environmental effects of the Shadow Plane.
