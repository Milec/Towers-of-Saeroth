# Consumable  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 630

An item with this trait can be used only once. Unless stated otherwise, it's destroyed after activation. Consumable items include [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical items]] and magical consumables such as [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|scrolls]] and [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]]. When a character creates consumable items, they can make them in batches of four.
