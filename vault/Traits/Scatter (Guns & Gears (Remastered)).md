# Scatter  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 235

This weapon fires a cluster of pellets in a wide spray. Scatter always has an area listed with it, indicating the radius of the spray. On a hit, the primary target of attacks with a scatter weapon take the listed damage, and the target and all other creatures within the listed radius around it take 1 point of [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage per weapon damage die.
