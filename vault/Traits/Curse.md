# Curse  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 630

A curse is an effect that places some long-term affliction on a creature. Curses are always [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and are typically the result of a spell or trap. Effects with this trait can be removed only by effects that specifically target curses.

Afflictions with this trait are manifestations of potent ill will. A curse either lasts a specified amount of time or can be removed only by certain actions a character must perform or conditions they must meet. A curse with stages follows the rules for afflictions. (_Gamemastery Guide_ pg. 251)
