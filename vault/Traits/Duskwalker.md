# Duskwalker  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 267

A creature with this trait has the duskwalker versatile heritage. Duskwalkers are planar scions infused with the supernatural energy of [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomps]]. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by duskwalkers.
