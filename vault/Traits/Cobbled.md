# Cobbled  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 231

This firearm is cobbled together and likely to misfire. No matter how well you upkeep it, on a failed attack roll, the attack misses and you must roll a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. If you fail this check, the weapon misfires.
