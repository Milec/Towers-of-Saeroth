# Light  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 633

Light effects overcome non-magical darkness in the area, and can [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] magical [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]]. You must usually target darkness magic with your light magic directly to counteract the darkness, but some light spells automatically attempt to counteract darkness.
