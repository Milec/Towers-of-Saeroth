# Infused  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 75

You created an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical item]] with the infused trait using your infused reagents, and it has a limited time before it becomes inert. Any nonpermanent effects from your infused alchemical items, with the exception of afflictions such as slow-acting [[Traits/Poison|poisons]], end when you make your daily preparations again.
