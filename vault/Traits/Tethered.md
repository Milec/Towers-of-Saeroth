# Tethered  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 274

This weapon is attached to a length of rope or chain that allows you to retrieve it after it has left your hand. If you have a free hand (including if you've just thrown a two-handed tethered weapon and have a hand holding nothing but the weapon's tether), you can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to pull the weapon back into your grasp after you have thrown it as a ranged attack or after it has been [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|disarmed]] (unless it's being held by another creature).
