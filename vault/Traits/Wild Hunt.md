# Wild Hunt  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 616

Creatures with the wild hunt trait are never below 14th level. They possess [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], greensight, planar acclimation, wild gaze, instinctive cooperation, weakness to [[Equipment/Cold Iron|cold iron]], the ability to [[Spells/Speak with Plants|_speak with plants_]] and [[Spells/Speak with Animals|_animals_]], and their own specific wild hunt link.
