# Flowing  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 172

The flow of time is consistently faster or slower. A creature might travel to one of these planes, spend a year there, and find that only an hour passed in the Universe; alternatively, they might spend a minute on this plane and find out an hour passed in the Universe.
