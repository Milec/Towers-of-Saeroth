# Training  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 222

A training weapon is designed to be used when training an animal to participate in combat by identifying the target for the animal to attack. Striking a creature with a training weapon gives your [[Rules/Animal Companions|animal companion]] or your [[Feats/Bonded Animal|bonded animal]] a +1 circumstance bonus to its next attack roll against that target.
