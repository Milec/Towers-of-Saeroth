# Devil  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 630

A family of [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]] from [[Setting/Planes/Hell]], most devils are irredeemably lawful evil. They typically have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], immunity to fire, and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]].
