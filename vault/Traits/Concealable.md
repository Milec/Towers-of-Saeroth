# Concealable  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 266

This weapon is designed to be inconspicuous or easily concealed. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks and DCs to hide or conceal a weapon with this trait.
