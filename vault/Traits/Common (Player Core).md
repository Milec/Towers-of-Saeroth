# Common  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 11

Anything that doesn't list another rarity trait ([[Traits/Uncommon|uncommon]], [[Traits/Rare|rare]], or [[Traits/Unique|unique]]) automatically has the common trait. This rarity indicates that an ability, item, or spell is available to all players who meet the prerequisites for it. A creature of this rarity is generally known and can be summoned with the appropriate _summon_ spell.
