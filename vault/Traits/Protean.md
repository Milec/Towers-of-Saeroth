# Protean  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 347

A family of monitors spawned within the [[Rules/Sidebars/Galisemni|Maelstrom]], these creatures are guardians of disorder and are chaotic neutral. They typically have darkvision, an amorphous anatomy, and a weakness to lawful damage.
