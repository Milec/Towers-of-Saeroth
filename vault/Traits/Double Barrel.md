# Double Barrel  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 232

This weapon has two barrels that are each loaded separately. You can fire both barrels of a double barrel weapon in a single Strike to increase the weapon damage die by one step. If the weapon has the [[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal]] trait, this increases the fatal die by one step.
