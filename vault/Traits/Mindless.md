# Mindless  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 634

A mindless creature has either programmed or rudimentary mental attributes. Most, if not all, of their mental ability modifiers are –5. They are immune to all [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects.
