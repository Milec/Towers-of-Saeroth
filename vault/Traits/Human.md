# Human  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 633

A creature with this trait is a member of the [[Ancestries/Human|human]] ancestry. Humans are a diverse array of people known for their adaptability. An ability with this trait can be used or selected only by humans.
