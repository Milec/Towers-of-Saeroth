# Mutagen  *Trait*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 289

Mutagens are a special type of [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] that temporarily transmogrify the subject's body and mind. Typically, only [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|alchemists]] have the expertise to craft mutagens.

A mutagen always conveys one or more beneficial effects (listed in the Benefit entry) and one or more detrimental effects (shown in the Drawback entry). Mutagens are [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effects, and a subsequent polymorph effect attempts to counteract an existing effect; the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check for a mutagen uses the item's level and a modifier equal to that [[Rules/Fill in the Numbers (GM Core)|level's DC]] – 10.
