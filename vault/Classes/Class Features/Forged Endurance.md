# Forged Endurance  *Class Feature 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 43

**Class** Runesmith

---

Your body is toughened by long days immersed in crafting, without break for food or drink. Your proficiency rank for Fortitude saves increases to master. When you roll a success on a Fortitude save, you get a critical success instead.
