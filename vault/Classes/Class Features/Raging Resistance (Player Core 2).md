# Raging Resistance  *Class Feature 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 70

**Class** Barbarian

---

Repeated exposure and toughened skin allow you to fend off harm. While [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|raging]], you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to 3 + your Constitution modifier to damage types based on your instinct.
