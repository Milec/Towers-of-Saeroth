# Stylish Tricks  *Class Feature 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 82

**Class** Swashbuckler

---

At 3rd level, 7th level, and 15th level, you gain a skill feat. This feat must be for [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or the trained skill from your swashbuckler's style.
