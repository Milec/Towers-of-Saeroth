# Keen Recollection  *Class Feature 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 54

**Class** Investigator

---

You can recall pertinent facts on topics that aren't your specialty. Your proficiency bonus to untrained skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] is equal to your level instead of +0.
