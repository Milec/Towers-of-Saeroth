# Legendary Overdrive  *Class Feature 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Class** Inventor

---

Your peerless inventing and [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] ability has supercharged your Overdrives. You become legendary in Crafting, and on a successful Overdrive, you increase the additional damage by a total of 3, replacing the increase from master overdrive.
