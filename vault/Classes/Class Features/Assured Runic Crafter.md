# Assured Runic Crafter  *Class Feature 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 43

**Class** Runesmith

---

You're so used to tracing and etching runes in the field that when given the peace and quiet of a proper workshop, it's hard for things to go too astray. When you roll a check to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] a magic item in downtime and that item is a permanent rune, you get the outcome one degree of success better than the result of your [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check.
