# Weapon Tricks  *Class Feature 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 178

**Class** Rogue

---

You have become thoroughly familiar with the tools of your trade. You gain expert proficiency in simple weapons as well as the [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap|sap]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks. When you critically succeed at an attack roll against a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature while using an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] simple weapon or unarmed attack, or when using any of the listed weapons, you apply the critical specialization effect for that weapon or unarmed attack.
