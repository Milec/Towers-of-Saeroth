# Brutality  *Class Feature 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 70

**Class** Barbarian

---

Your fury makes your weapons lethal. Your proficiency ranks for simple weapons, martial weapons, and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks increase to expert. While [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|raging]], you have the critical specialization benefits for melee weapons and unarmed attacks.
