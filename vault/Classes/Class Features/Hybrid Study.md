# Hybrid Study  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 35

**Class** Magus

---

Your extensive physical training and carefully chosen magic combine to form a unique and dangerous fighting style that's more than the sum of its parts. You choose one field of hybrid study to represent your particular combination of skills. Your hybrid study gives you a special ability, usually tied to Spellstrike or Arcane Cascade, and it determines your initial conflux spell. Hybrid Studies can be found [here](HybridStudies.aspx).
