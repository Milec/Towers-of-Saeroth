# Revitalizing Rage  *Class Feature 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 70

**Class** Barbarian

---

Your rage sustains you more frequently, leaving you ready to jump into battle anew. Instead of needing to wait 1 minute before you can gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] from [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] again, you need only to spend at least one full turn not in a Rage.
