# Twofold Will  *Class Feature 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Class** Magus

---

Mental diligence and combat resolve combine to fortify you against mental attacks. Your proficiency rank for Will saves increases to master. When you roll a success on a Will save, you get a critical success instead.
