# Animistic Practice  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 10

**Class** Animist

---

At 1st level, you choose an [animistic practice](Practices.aspx) that influences the way your power grows and develops, and you gain its first invocation. At 9th level, you gain its second invocation, and at 17th, you gain its third.
