# Undead Lore  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 27

**Class** Necromancer

---

Your dealings with death have expanded your knowledge of the macabre. You know the dead and what they are capable of. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Undead Lore]], a special lore skill that can be used to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] regarding [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures, [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]], and effects caused by undead creatures or other necromancers, but that can't be used to Recall Knowledge of other topics. At 3rd level, you become an expert in Undead Lore; at 7th level, you become a master in Undead Lore; and at 15th level, you become legendary in Undead Lore.
