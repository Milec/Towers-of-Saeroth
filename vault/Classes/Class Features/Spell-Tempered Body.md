# Spell-Tempered Body  *Class Feature 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Class** Magus

---

The mighty spells flowing through your body have bolstered you physically. Your proficiency rank for Fortitude saves increases to master. When you roll a success on a Fortitude save, you get a critical success instead.
