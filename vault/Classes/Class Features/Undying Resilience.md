# Undying Resilience  *Class Feature 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 27

**Class** Necromancer

---

Your body has been necromantically enhanced. Your proficiency rank for Fortitude saves increases to legendary. When you roll a critical failure on a Fortitude save, you get a failure instead. When you fail a Fortitude save against a damaging effect, you take half damage.
