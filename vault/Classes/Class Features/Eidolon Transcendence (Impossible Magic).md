# Eidolon Transcendence  *Class Feature 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 63

**Class** Summoner

---

Your connection with your eidolon approaches its peak, granting your eidolon access to a powerful new ability depending on their type, as described in Eidolon Abilities in each eidolon’s entry. In addition, choose one of your eidolon’s attribute modifiers to increase by 1.
