# Oracular Clarity  *Class Feature 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 66

**Class** Oracle

---

You now fully grasp the nature of the divine power behind your mystery, allowing you to work magic akin to miracles. Add two common 10th-level divine spells to your repertoire. You gain a single 10th-level spell slot you can use to cast one of those two spells using oracle spellcasting. You don't gain more 10th-level spells as you level up, unlike other spell slots, and you can't use 10th-level slots with abilities that give you more spell slots or that let you cast spells without expending spell slots. You can take the [[Feats/Oracular Providence]] feat to gain a second slot.
