# Medium Armor Expertise  *Class Feature 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 16

**Class** Inventor

---

You’ve learned to better defend yourself against attacks. Your proficiency ranks for light armor, medium armor, and unarmored defense increase to expert.

If you have a medium or heavy armor innovation, you gain access to the [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect with your armor innovation.
