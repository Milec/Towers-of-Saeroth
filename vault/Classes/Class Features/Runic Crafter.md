# Runic Crafter  *Class Feature 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 43

**Class** Runesmith

---

Your study of secret runes leaves you well practiced in crafting their more common cousins. You gain the [[Feats/Magical Crafting (Player Core)|Magical Crafting]] skill feat, even if you don't meet the prerequisites. You automatically learn the formulas for the fundamental armor runes ([[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|armor potency]] and [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]]), fundamental shield rune ([[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|reinforcing]]), and fundamental weapon runes ([[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|weapon potency]] and [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]]) as soon as your level equals each item's. You practice your runes so thoroughly you can draw them by heart and thus do not need a formula book to record the formula of any runes you learn how to create.

At 4th level and every 2 levels thereafter, you learn the formula for an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|armor]] or [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|weapon]] property rune of your choice. The rune must be of your level or lower, and can be a common rune or another rune to which you have access.
