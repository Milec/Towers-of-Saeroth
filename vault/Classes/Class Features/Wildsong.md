# Wildsong  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 122

**Class** Druid

---

You know the [[Setting/Languages/Wildsong]], a secret language known only within druid orders, in addition to any languages you know through your ancestry. The Wildsong is a melodic and tonal language sounding more like animal calls than a spoken tongue, and its alphabet involves fractals and spirals, like the arrangement of a seashell's chambers, a snowflake's crystals, or a fern's fronds. Teaching the Wildsong to non-druids is anathema.
