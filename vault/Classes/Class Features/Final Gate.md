# Final Gate  *Class Feature 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 13

**Class** Kineticist

---

Your kinetic gate reaches a perfect form, its power constantly fighting to be released. If your kinetic aura is inactive, you automatically use the first action of your turn to [[Actions/Channel Elements]] as a free action. You can deliberately suppress the effect. If you're unable to act, final gate still functions, but you don't get to use the [[Actions/Elemental Blast]] or stance impulse you normally do from using Channel Elements.
