# Greater Eidolon Specialization  *Class Feature 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 63

**Class** Summoner

---

Your eidolon’s damage from weapon specialization increases to 4 with unarmed attacks in which they’re an expert, 6 if they’re a master, and 8 if they’re legendary.
