# Inventor Weapon Expertise  *Class Feature 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 16

**Class** Inventor

---

You develop tricks for using your weapons more effectively. Your proficiency ranks for simple and martial weapons and unarmed attacks increase to expert.

If you have a weapon innovation, you gain access to the [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect with your innovation.
