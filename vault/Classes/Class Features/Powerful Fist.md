# Powerful Fist  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 154

**Class** Monk

---

You know how to wield your fists as deadly weapons. The damage die for your fist increases to 1d6 instead of 1d4. Most people take a –2 circumstance penalty when making a lethal attack with [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] unarmed attacks, because they find it hard to use their fists with deadly force. You don't take this penalty when making a lethal attack with your fist or any other unarmed attacks.
