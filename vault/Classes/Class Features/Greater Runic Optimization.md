# Greater Runic Optimization  *Class Feature 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 43

**Class** Runesmith

---

Your weapon runes harmonize with your own innate magic, multiplying their damage even further. Your extra damage from runic optimization increases to 4 with weapons bearing a _striking rune_, 6 for a _greater striking rune_, and 8 for a _major striking rune_.
