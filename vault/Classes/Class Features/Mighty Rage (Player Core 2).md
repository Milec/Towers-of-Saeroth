# Mighty Rage  *Class Feature 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 70

**Class** Barbarian

---

Your rage intensifies and lets you hit harder at the start of a fight. Your proficiency rank for your barbarian class DC increases to expert. In addition, when you use [[Actions/Quick-Tempered]], the first Strike you make during your first turn deals additional damage equal to your [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]] damage.
