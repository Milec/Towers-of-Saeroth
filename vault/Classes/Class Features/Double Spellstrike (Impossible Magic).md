# Double Spellstrike  *Class Feature 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Class** Magus

---

When adding a spell to an attack simply isn’t enough, you combine two spells with your attack instead. You gain the Double Spellstrike activity.
