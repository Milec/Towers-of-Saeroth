# Expert Overdrive  *Class Feature 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Class** Inventor

---

You've increased your skill in crafting, and you can put your devices into a more powerful state of overdrive. You become an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], and on a successful use of Overdrive, you increase the additional damage by 1.
