# Tough to Kill  *Class Feature 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 37

**Class** Guardian

---

The protectiveness of your armor ensures that even if you fall, you take longer to die. You gain the [[Feats/Diehard (Player Core)|Diehard]] general feat. If you already have the Diehard feat, you can retrain it when you get tough to kill. Additionally, the first time each day you’d be reduced to [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] 3 or higher, you stay at dying 2 instead.
