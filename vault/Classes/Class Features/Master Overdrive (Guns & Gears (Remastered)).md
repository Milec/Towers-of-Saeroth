# Master Overdrive  *Class Feature 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Class** Inventor

---

Your mastery of invention and crafting enhances your Overdrive even further. You become a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], and on a successful Overdrive, you increase the additional damage by a total of 2, replacing the increase from expert overdrive.
