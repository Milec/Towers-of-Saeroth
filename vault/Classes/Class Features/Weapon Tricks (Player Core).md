# Weapon Tricks  *Class Feature 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 164

**Class** Rogue

---

You gain expert proficiency in simple weapons, martial weapons, and unarmed attacks. When you critically succeed at an attack roll against an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature while using an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] weapon or an agile or finesse unarmed attack, you apply the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]] for that weapon or unarmed attack.
