# Thaumaturgic Mastery  *Class Feature 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 30

**Class** Thaumaturge

---

You've become a master in paranormal phenomena, and your esoterica grow in power. Your proficiency rank for your thaumaturge class DC increases to master.

You also gain an additional skill increase, which you can apply only to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]].
