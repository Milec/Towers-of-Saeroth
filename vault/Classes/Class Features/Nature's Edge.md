# Nature's Edge  *Class Feature 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 166

**Class** Ranger

---

You always find the weak points in your foes' defenses when they're on unfavorable terrain. Enemies are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to you if they're in natural difficult terrain or in difficult terrain resulting from a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|snare]].
