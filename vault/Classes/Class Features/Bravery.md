# Bravery  *Class Feature 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 140

**Class** Fighter

---

Having faced countless foes and the chaos of battle, you have learned how to stand strong in the face of fear and keep on fighting. Your proficiency rank for Will saves increases to expert. When you roll a success at a Will save against a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect, you get a critical success instead. In addition, anytime you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition, reduce its value by 1.
