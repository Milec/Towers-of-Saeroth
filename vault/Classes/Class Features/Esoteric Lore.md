# Esoteric Lore  *Class Feature 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 30

**Class** Thaumaturge

---

Your experience with the unknown, as well as the tales you've exchanged with other thaumaturges, has taught you about strange phenomena of every kind. You become trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Esoteric Lore]], a special lore skill that can be used to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] regarding [[Traits/Haunt|haunts]], [[Traits/Curse|curses]], and creatures of any type, but that can't be used to Recall Knowledge of other topics. Unlike a normal Lore skill, you use Charisma as your modifier on Esoteric Lore checks. You also gain the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat.

 At 3rd level, you become an expert in Esoteric Lore; at 7th level, you become a master in Esoteric Lore; and at 15th level, you become legendary in Esoteric Lore.
