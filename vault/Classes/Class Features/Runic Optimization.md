# Runic Optimization  *Class Feature 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 43

**Class** Runesmith

---

You've learned to draw upon the magic of weapon runes to deal greater damage. You deal 2 additional damage with weapons bearing a _striking rune_. This damage increases to 3 if the weapon bears a _greater striking rune_ and 4 if it bears a _major striking rune_.
