# Studious Spell  *Class Feature 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 9

**Class** Magus

---

You've deeply studied certain beneficial spells to speedily enter Arcane Cascade when you cast them. When you cast a studious spell, you can use Arcane Cascade as a free action as your next action this turn. Add any studious spell you gain to your spellbook if you don't know it already. Your studious spells are [[Spells/Gecko Grip|_gecko grip_]] and an additional spell depending on your hybrid study, listed in the hybrid study's entry.

 At 11th level, add [[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]] and an additional spell depending on your hybrid study to your studious spells.

 At 13th level, add [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]] and an additional spell depending on your hybrid study to your studious spells.
