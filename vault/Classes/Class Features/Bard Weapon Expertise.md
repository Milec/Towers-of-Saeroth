# Bard Weapon Expertise  *Class Feature 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 94

**Class** Bard

---

You have become thoroughly adept with bardic weapons. You gain expert proficiency in simple weapons and unarmed attacks, plus the [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap|sap]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow|shortbow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip|whip]]. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using one of these weapons or an unarmed attack while one of your compositions is active, you apply the critical specialization effect for that weapon or unarmed attack.
