# Devastator  *Class Feature 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 70

**Class** Barbarian

---

Your Strikes are so devastating that you hardly care about resistance, and your barbarian abilities are unparalleled. Your proficiency rank for your barbarian class DC increases to master. Your melee Strikes ignore up to 10 [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to the physical damage they deal.
