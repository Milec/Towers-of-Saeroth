# Monarch of the Fey Courts  *Animist Apparition*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 19

Monarchs of the fey courts make their homes near places with strong ties to the [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]], or in places where [[Bestiary/Families/Nymph (Monster Core)|nymphs]] once held sway. They are drawn to animists who blend an appreciation for art and nature's beauty with a ruler's ambition. Monarchs of fey courts are vain, capricious, and do not easily forgive slights or poor manners.
