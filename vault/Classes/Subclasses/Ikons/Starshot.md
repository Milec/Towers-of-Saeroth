# Starshot  *Exemplar Weapon Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 46

**Usage** a ranged weapon

---

You might be the only one capable of stringing this bow or pulling this trigger; either way, the ikon’s shots are packed with explosive power, striking like falling stars.

**Immanence** Strikes with the _starshot_ deal an additional 1 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage per weapon damage die.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Giant-Felling Comet;     **Effect** You shoot the _starshot_, causing a detonation in a 5-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must succeed at a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or take spirit damage equal to your normal Strike damage with the starshot. Creatures larger than you take a –2 circumstance penalty to their saving throws. This shot requires any ammunition that would normally be required for the weapon.
