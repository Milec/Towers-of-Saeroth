# Hands Of The Wildling  *Exemplar Weapon Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 44

**Usage** a melee [[Traits/Free-Hand (Player Core)|free-hand]] weapon or a melee [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] Strike

---

Tattooed fists, savage claws, or even powerful gauntlets—you swing each with the fury of an animal from the woods.

**Immanence** [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with your hands of the wildling deal an additional 1 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage per weapon damage die. You are [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to this splash damage.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Feral Swing;     **Effect** You lash out with both arms, rending all before you. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] must succeed at a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or take spirit damage equal to your normal Strike damage with your hands of the wildling. You can choose to swing with abandon, which imposes a –2 circumstance bonus to enemies’ saving throws, but causes you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.
