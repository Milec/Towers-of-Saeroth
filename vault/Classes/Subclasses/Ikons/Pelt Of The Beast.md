# Pelt Of The Beast  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

**Usage** worn cloak or belt

---

This animal hide, whether worn about the shoulders or waist, is all you need to survive even the harshest elements. When you make your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], choose [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage. The pelt attunes to that damage type.

**Immanence** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to half your level to the damage type the pelt is attuned to. You also treat [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|environmental cold and heat effects]] as one step less severe.

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Survive the Wilds;     **Effect** You wrap the pelt around yourself. You can choose to change the damage type the pelt is attuned to. The pelt shines gold, drawing the offending energies into itself. Until the start of your next turn, this shine creates an aura in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. You and all allies in the emanation gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against effects with that trait.
