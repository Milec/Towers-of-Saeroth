# Skybearer's Belt  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 46

**Usage** worn belt

---

This girdle wraps around your waist, magnifying your strength to the point you feel you could carry the sky itself.

**Immanence** Strength flows forth. You can attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than you, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks for these actions and to your saving throws to resist these actions.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Transcendence]])Bear Allies’ Burdens;     **Effect** You move with a speed belying your strength, carrying your allies as easily as straw dolls. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. At any point you are adjacent to a willing ally during the Stride, you can pick that ally up, and you can deposit them into a space adjacent to you at any other point during your movement. You ignore the ally’s Bulk while carrying them during your Stride. You can [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
