# Barrow's Edge  *Exemplar Weapon Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 43

**Usage** melee weapon that deals slashing or piercing damage

---

This blade subtly rattles in its scabbard, as if it wants to be unsheathed so it can consume violence.

**Immanence** The _barrow’s edge_ deals 1 additional [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage per weapon damage die to a creature it Strikes. If the creature is below half its maximum Hit Points, the weapon deals 3 additional spirit damage per weapon damage dice instead. _Barrow’s edge_ rattles more strongly when an enemy below half its maximum Hit Points is within the weapon’s reach to let you know a foe is verging closer to death.

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Transcendence]], [[Traits/Vitality]])Drink of my Foes;    **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with the _barrow’s edge_; **Effect** Your blade glows as it absorbs your foe’s vitality. You regain Hit Points equal to half the damage dealt.
