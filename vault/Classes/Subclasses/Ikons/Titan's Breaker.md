# Titan's Breaker  *Exemplar Weapon Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 46

**Usage** any melee weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|hammer]], or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axe]] group, or any your melee [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] that deals bludgeoning damage

---

You wield a weapon whose blows shatter mountains with ease.

**Immanence** The _titan’s breaker_ deals 2 additional [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage per weapon damage die to creatures it Strikes. [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|Constructs]] and [[Rules/Objects (Player Core)|objects]] are not immune to this spirit damage, and this spirit damage automatically bypasses an amount of their Hardness equal to your level.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Fracture Mountains;     **Effect** Your spirit is so dense it takes on tangible force. Make a melee Strike with the _titan’s breaker_. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If this Strike hits, your additional spirit damage from the ikon’s immanence increases to 4 plus an extra die of weapon damage. If you’re at least 10th level, it’s increased to 6 spirit damage and two extra dice, and if you’re at least 18th level, it’s increased to 8 spirit damage and three extra dice.
