# Mirrored Aegis  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

**Usage** any [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]]

---

This shield is polished so brightly it can reflect even [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spiritual]] and [[Traits/Ethereal (Player Core)|ethereal]] attacks.

**Immanence** The _mirrored aegis_ emits an aura in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that protects you and all allies in the aura from harm, granting a +1 status bonus to AC. If the _mirrored aegis_ houses your divine spark for 10 uninterrupted minutes, it is restored to full Hit Points.

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Raise the Walls;     **Effect** You [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise]] the _mirrored aegis_, which summons ethereal shields that surround you and one ally of your choice within 15 feet in a tortoise shield formation. You and the ally gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Reflex saves, and any save against a force, [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]] effect for 1 minute.
