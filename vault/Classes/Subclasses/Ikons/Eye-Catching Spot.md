# Eye-Catching Spot  *Exemplar Body Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 44

**Usage** imbued on the face

---

A fetching beauty spot under an eye or a smile as warm as the sun distracts foes and captures hearts alike.

**Immanence** Your beauty becomes supernaturally enhanced, distracting foes and imposing a –1 circumstance penalty to melee attack rolls against you.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Transcendence]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]])Captivating Charm;     **Effect** You focus your attention on a creature within 30 feet, overwhelming its senses. The creature must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] by you until the start of your next turn. The condition ends if you use a [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] action against the target, but not if you use a hostile action against its allies.
