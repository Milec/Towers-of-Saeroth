# Thousand-League Sandals  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 46

**Usage** worn shoes

---

Threadbare but trustworthy, your sandals have carried you this far, and they’ll carry you much further still.

**Immanence** Your sandals ease your travels on the path ahead, granting you a +10-foot status bonus to your [[Rules/Speed (GM Core)|Speed]].

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Transcendence]])Marathon Dash;     **Effect** Your feet carry you so quickly they leave a slipstream that speeds your allies on. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. Each ally within 10 feet of you at the start of your movement can Stride as a reaction.
