# Scar Of The Survivor  *Exemplar Body Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

**Usage** imbued in the skin

---

A scar on your body commemorates a time someone tried to end your story and failed—a testament to your resilience and fortitude.

**Immanence** Divine energy spreads outward from your scar, reinforcing your flesh. You gain the benefits of the [[Feats/Diehard (Player Core)|Diehard]] feat and a +1 status bonus to Fortitude saving throws.

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Transcendence]], [[Traits/Vitality]])No Scar but This;     **Effect** Your wounds knit shut with hardly a scratch. You regain 1d8 Hit Points. At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the healing increases by 1d8.
