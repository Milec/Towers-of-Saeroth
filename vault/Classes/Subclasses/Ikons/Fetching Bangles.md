# Fetching Bangles  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 44

**Usage** worn bracers

---

These lovely armbands sparkle and gleam, reflecting your own incredible magnetism.

**Immanence** Others find it hard to leave your captivating presence. An aura surrounds you in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. An enemy in the aura that attempts to move away from you must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or its [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action is disrupted.

**Transcendence** single action ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]])Embrace of Destiny;     **Effect** Choose an enemy within 20 feet of you. It must succeed at a Will save against your class DC or be pulled directly toward you into a square adjacent to you.
