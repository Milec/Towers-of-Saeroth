# Bands Of Imprisonment  *Exemplar Worn Ikon*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 43

**Usage** worn anklets, bracers, or circlet (often a headband)

---

These weighted bands don’t enhance your power—rather, they keep your strength in check, honing your discipline.

**Immanence** The _bands of imprisonment tighten_, keeping your mind sharp. You gain a +1 status bonus to Will saving throws and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage equal to half your level.

**Transcendence** two actions ([[Traits/Transcendence]])Break Free;     **Effect** You can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] with a +2 status bonus on your check, then [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice your Speed in a straight line, and finally make a melee Strike. If you don’t need to Escape or you can’t move or choose not to, you still take the other actions listed.
