# The Unbound Step  *Psychic Conscious Mind*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 23

The mind can flit from thought to thought; why too shouldn't you? You focus on motion in a higher order of spatial dimensions, not just the typical three, allowing for versatile abilities that alter positioning.

## Phase Bolt

Your _phase bolt_ temporarily sends the target's cover out of phase if it hits. On a success, reduce the target's circumstance bonus to AC (if any) by 1 until the beginning of your next turn. Your _phase bolt_ also gains the following amp.

**Amp** Your bolt leaves phase completely, becoming invisible and intangible until it's already embedded in the target—giving the impression it simply teleported itself into place. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the attack. Additionally, the bolt ignores an amount of Hardness or resistance to piercing damage equal to half the spell's rank. On a critical success, the target can't be affected by [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] effects until the start of your next turn.

**Amp Heightened (+1)** The bolt’s damage increases by 2d4 instead of 1d4.

## Warp Step

You warp space more compactly, granting you a +10-foot status to your Speed instead of a +5-foot status bonus when you _warp step_. The spell also gains the following amp.

**Amp** Space contracts with hardly a thought, letting you Cast the Spell as a single action.

**Amp Heightened (4th)** You twist space so completely you don't need to travel the interposing distance. You can choose to instead teleport to a space within your line of sight and line of effect with a range equal to your double your Speed (after applying the status bonus from warp step). This grants the spell the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait.
