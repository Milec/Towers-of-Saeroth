# Coordinating Maneuvers single action  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Offensive

---

Your team works to slip enemies into a disadvantageous position. Signal one squadmate within the aura of your banner; that squadmate can immediately [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action. If they end this movement next to an opponent, they can attempt to [[Actions/Reposition]] that target as a reaction.
