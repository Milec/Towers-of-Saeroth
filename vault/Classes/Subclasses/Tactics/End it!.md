# End it! two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Offensive

**Requirements**
You and your allies currently outnumber enemies on the battlefield, and you or a squadmate have reduced an enemy to 0 Hit Points since the start of your last turn.

---

At your proclamation that victory is already at hand, your allies march forward with an authoritative stomp, scattering your enemies in terror. Signal all squadmates within the aura of your banner; you and each ally can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action directly toward a hostile creature. Any hostile creatures within 10 feet of a squadmate after this movement must attempt a Will save against your class DC; on a failure they become [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round, and on a critical failure they become fleeing for 1 round and [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2. This is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.
