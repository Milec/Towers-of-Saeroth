# Gather to Me! single action  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 25

**Tactic Type** Mobility

---

You signal your team to move into position together. Signal all squadmates; each can immediately Stride as a reaction, though each must end their movement inside your banner’s aura, or as close to your banner’s aura as their movement Speed allows.

Squadmates can use Gather to Me! while [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]], [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climbing]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flying]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swimming]] instead of Striding if they have the corresponding movement type.
