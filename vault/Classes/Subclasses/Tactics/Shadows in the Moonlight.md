# Shadows in the Moonlight single action or two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 227

**Tactic Type** Mobility

---

Your commands help your team evade notice. Signal all squadmates within your banner’s aura; until the end of your next turn, you and each ally count as [[Actions/Follow the Expert (Player Core)|Following the Expert]] for any checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and can ignore the [[Traits/Noisy (Player Core)|noisy]] trait on any armor you wear. If you spend 2 actions to use this tactic, up to two squadmates within your banner’s aura can also either Hide or Sneak as a free action.

---

**Special** If you have this tactic prepared, you can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] in place of [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] for checks you attempt to Hide and Sneak.
