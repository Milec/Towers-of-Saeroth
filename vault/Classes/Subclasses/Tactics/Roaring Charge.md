# Roaring Charge two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 28

**Tactic Type** Master

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You and your squad surge forward with a mighty roar. Signal all squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner. As a reaction, these squadmates can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice their Speed directly toward any enemy they are observing. Any creature within 10 feet of a squadmate once all movement from this tactic has been completed must attempt a Will save against your class DC with the following results. This is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.

**Critical Success** No effect.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.
