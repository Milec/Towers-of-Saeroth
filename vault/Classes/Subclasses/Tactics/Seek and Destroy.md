# Seek and Destroy two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 227

**Tactic Type** Expert

**Frequency**
once per 10 minutes

---

You direct your team to uncover hidden foes and exploit newly discovered weaknesses. Signal up to two squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner; they can immediately [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] as a free action. Afterward, each can spend a reaction to either [[Actions/Point Out (Player Core)|Point Out]] an enemy, [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] toward an enemy they’re observing, or attempt to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] an enemy they’re observing. Each of the squadmates deals additional precision damage equal to half your level with their next successful Strike before the end of your next turn against a creature that has become observed by them from a successful [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] check to Seek.
