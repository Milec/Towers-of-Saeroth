# Naval Training single action  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 25

**Tactic Type** Mobility

---

Your instructions make it easier for you and your allies to swim through dangerous waters. Signal all squadmates; until the end of your next turn, you and each ally gain a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] of 20 feet.

---

**Special** If you have this tactic prepared, you can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] in place of [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] for checks you make to Swim.
