# For Talmandor! For Freedom! two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 227

**Tactic Type** Legendary

**Frequency**
once per day

---

You raise your banner high with a reminder of what you and your allies truly fight for, granting them unshakable conviction. For yourself and each squadmate in your banner’s aura when you use this tactic, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] one effect affecting that creature. Your counteract modifier is your [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] modifier. The effect must have the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait or impose the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] condition.
