# Buckle-cut blitz two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Expert

---

Your squad dashes past enemies, slicing their boot laces and breaking their belt buckles. Signal up to two squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner; these squadmates can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to their Speed as a reaction. Each enemy they are adjacent to at any point during this movement must attempt a Reflex save against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 round (clumsy 2 on a critical failure).
