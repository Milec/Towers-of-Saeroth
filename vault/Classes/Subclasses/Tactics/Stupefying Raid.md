# Stupefying Raid two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 27

**Tactic Type** Expert

---

Your team dashes about in a series of maneuvers that leave the enemy befuddled. Signal up to two squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner; these squadmates can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to their Speed as a reaction. Each enemy they are adjacent to at any point during this movement must attempt a Will save against your class DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round (stupefied 2 on a critical failure); this is a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect.
