# Reload! single action  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Offensive

---

Your drill instruction kicks in, and your allies rapidly reload their weapons to prepare for the next volley. Signal all squadmates; each can immediately [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|reload]] as a reaction.

---

**Special** When leading a [[Rules/Siege Weapons (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|siege weapon]] crew, you can use this tactic to command your squadmates to Load a siege weapon instead.
