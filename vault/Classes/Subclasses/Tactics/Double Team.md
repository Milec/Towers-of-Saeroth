# Double Team two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Offensive

---

Your team works together to set an enemy up for a vicious attack. Signal one squadmate who has an opponent within their reach. That ally can [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Reposition]] an opponent as a free action. If their maneuver is successful and the target ends their movement adjacent to a different squadmate, the second squadmate can attempt a melee Strike against that target as a reaction.
