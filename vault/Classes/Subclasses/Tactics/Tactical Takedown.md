# Tactical Takedown two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 26

**Tactic Type** Offensive

---

You direct a coordinated maneuver that sends an enemy tumbling down. Signal up to two squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner. Each of those allies can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half their Speed as a reaction. If they both end this movement adjacent to an enemy, that enemy must succeed at a Reflex save against your class DC or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
