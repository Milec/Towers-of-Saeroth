# Defensive Retreat two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Brandish]], [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 25

**Tactic Type** Mobility

---

You call for a careful retreat. Signal all squadmates within the aura of your banner; each can immediately [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] up to three times as a free action. Each Step must take them farther away from at least one hostile creature they are observing and can only take them closer to a hostile creature if doing so is the only way for them to move toward safety (such as if they’re surrounded).
