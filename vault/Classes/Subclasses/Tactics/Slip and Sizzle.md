# Slip and Sizzle two actions  *Commander Tactic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Commander]], [[Traits/Tactic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 27

**Tactic Type** Expert

---

Your team executes a brutal technique designed to knock down an opponent and blast them with magical devastation. Signal up to two squadmates within the aura of your commander’s banner; one of these squadmates must be adjacent to an opponent and the other must be capable of casting a spell that deals damage. The first squadmate can attempt to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] the adjacent opponent as a reaction. If this Trip is successful, the second squadmate can cast a ranged spell that deals damage and takes 2 or fewer actions to cast. This spell is cast as a reaction and must either target the tripped opponent or include the tripped opponent in the spell’s area.

If the second squadmate cast a spell using slots or Focus Points as part of this tactic, they are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until the end of their next turn and do not gain a reaction when they regain actions at the start of their next turn.
