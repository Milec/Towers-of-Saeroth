# The Unseen Broker  *Witch Patron Theme*

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 159, [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 298

You have agreed to act as a mediator between your patron and the rest of the world. Your patron might be a god, an enigmatic planar being, or even an alien entity.

**Spell List** [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Patron Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]]

**Lesson of the Bound Words** Your patron teaches you about bargains and how they can hold power over others. You gain the [[Spells/Pact Broker (Dark Archives (Remastered))|_pact broker_]] hex cantrip and your familiar learns [[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_command_]].

**Familiar of Enticing Negotiation** Your familiar constantly whispers distracting offers of temptations to your enemies, even if your familiar normally doesn't have the means of speaking. When you Cast or Sustain a hex and your familiar is within 10 feet of an enemy, the enemy becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn. Your familiar can only distract a single enemy per round in this way.
