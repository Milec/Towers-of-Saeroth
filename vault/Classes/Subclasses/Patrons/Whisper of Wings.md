# Whisper of Wings  *Witch Patron Theme*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 62

Wings and swirling winds announced the approach of your patron. The wings might have been radiantly feathered, jeweled like a dragonfly’s, strangely mechanical, or leathery with the scent of sulfur, but the controlled chaos of their movements contained countless teachings.

**Spell List** [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Patron Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]

**Lesson of the Observed Sky** Your patron teaches that the sky can guide you anywhere you let it. You gain the [[Spells/Murmuration|_murmuration_]] hex cantrip, and your familiar learns [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]].

**Familiar of Nimble Flight** Even if your familiar doesn't have wings, it can sometimes move as if carried aloft by the wind. When you Cast or Sustain a hex, your familiar can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to 15 feet; this movement doesn't trigger reactions.
