# Fury  *Barbarian Instinct*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 87

Your rage comes from a deep and purely personal well within you. You use your rage as you choose.

## Anathema and Instinct Ability

You don't have an anathema or an instinct ability. Instead, you gain an additional 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|barbarian feat]].

## Specialization Ability  *Level 7*

Increase the additional damage from [[Actions/Rage]] from 2 to 6. If you have greater weapon specialization, instead increase the additional damage from Rage to 12.

## Raging Resistance  *Level 9*

You resist physical weapon damage, but not physical damage from other sources (such as unarmed attacks).
