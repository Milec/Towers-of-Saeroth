# Animal  *Barbarian Instinct*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 86

The fury of a wild predator fills you when you [[Actions/Rage]], granting you ferocious unarmed attacks. Cultures that revere vicious [[Traits/Animal|animals]] (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Ape|apes]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Bear|bears]]) give rise to barbarians with this instinct. You might also be at war with an uncontrollable, animalistic side of your personality, or you might be a descendant of a [[Bestiary/Werewolf|werewolf]] or another werecreature. Select an animal from Table 3–3: Animal Instincts that best matches your chosen animal.

## Anathema

Flagrantly disrespecting an [[Traits/Animal|animal]] of your chosen kind is anathema to your instinct, as is using weapons while raging.

## Bestial Rage (Instinct Ability)

When you [[Actions/Rage]], you gain your chosen animal's unarmed attack (or attacks). The specific attack gained, the damage it deals, and its traits are listed on Table 3–3: Animal Instincts. These attacks are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group. Your [[Actions/Rage]] action gains the [[Traits/Morph|morph]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]], and [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]] traits.

## Specialization Ability  *Level 7*

Increase the damage die size for the unarmed attacks granted by your chosen animal by one step, and increase the additional damage from [[Actions/Rage]] from 2 to 5 for your chosen animal's unarmed attacks. The frog's tongue attack and deer's antler attack gain [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]. If you have greater weapon specialization, increase the damage from Rage from 5 to 12 for your chosen animal's unarmed attacks.

## Raging Resistance  *Level 9*

You resist piercing and slashing damage.
