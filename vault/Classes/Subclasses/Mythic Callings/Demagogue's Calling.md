# Demagogue's Calling  *Mythic Calling*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Calling]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 80

Words flow freely from a font within your soul. When you attempt a check to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. The first time each day that you critically succeed on a check to Coerce a creature who isn’t your ally, Create a Diversion using your words, or Make an Impression on an [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent (Player Core)|indifferent]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly (Player Core)|unfriendly]] audience, you regain a Mythic Point.

---

**Edicts** tell stories to audiences who have never heard them before, use words rather than weapons to resolve conflicts

**Anathema** interrupt someone telling a story, resort to violent action without trying to defuse the situation with your words
