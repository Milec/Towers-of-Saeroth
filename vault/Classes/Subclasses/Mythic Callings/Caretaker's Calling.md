# Caretaker's Calling  *Mythic Calling*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Calling]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 80

You have a deep-seated drive or divine directive to care for others, and your power might one day grow to allow you to heal an unimaginable wound. When you [[Actions/Treat Disease (Player Core)|Treat Disease]], [[Actions/Treat Poison (Player Core)|Treat Poison]], [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]], or use [[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]], you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. The first time each day that you critically succeed on a check to Treat Wounds, you regain a Mythic Point.

---

**Edicts** give aid to others, heal those who are wounded regardless of where they come from

**Anathema** deny care to the disadvantaged, refuse to heal the wounds of an enemy who has surrendered
