# Acrobat's Calling  *Mythic Calling*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Calling]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 79

You feel a need to dance amid dangerous heights and soar across empty divides with nothing but your skill and reflexes to protect you. When you [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]], [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]], [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]], or attempt a [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], you can spend a Mythic Point to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. The first time each day that you critically succeed on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check while Balancing, Maneuvering in Flight, or Tumbling Through during combat, you regain a Mythic Point.

---

**Edicts** leap without being sure you’ll land unharmed, test your reflexes by maneuvering through the spaces of significantly more powerful enemies

**Anathema** maneuver around an enemy when tumbling through their space is a viable option, use safety measures to attempt acrobatic stunts
