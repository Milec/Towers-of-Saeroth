# Bookkeeper's Calling  *Mythic Calling*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Calling]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 79

Your passion and destiny are both tied to the preservation of knowledge and a deep-seated love of instruments designed to preserve and propagate knowledge, such as books and scrolls. When you attempt a check to [[Rules/Recall Knowledge (GM Core)|Recall Knowledge]] or [[Rules/Earn Income (GM Core)|Earn Income]] using a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill you’re trained in, you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to attempt the check at mythic proficiency. The first time each day that you critically succeed on a check to Recall Knowledge while researching in a library or similar facility, you regain a Mythic Point.

---

**Edicts** educate others, record new knowledge so that others can share in your discoveries

**Anathema** destroy books, scrolls, or other instruments used to record information; refuse an opportunity to share your knowledge with an earnest knowledge-seeker
