# Guardian's Calling  *Mythic Calling*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Calling]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 80

Your instinctive urge is always to protect others, pushing back aggressors and doing everything in your power to prevent them from harming your allies and charges. When you attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Reposition]], or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] an opponent, you can spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] to make the check at mythic proficiency. You can attempt these maneuvers against a creature of any size when performing them at mythic proficiency. The first time each day that you critically succeed on a check to Disarm, Reposition, or Shove an opponent, you regain a Mythic Point.

---

**Edicts** place yourself in harm’s way to protect your allies or innocent creatures under threat

**Anathema** retreat from a battle while your allies or innocent bystanders are in danger without taking every reasonable effort to rescue them first
