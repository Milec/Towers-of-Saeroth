# Warpriest  *Cleric Doctrine*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 120

You have trained in the more militant doctrine of your church, focusing on both spells and battle.

- **First Doctrine (1st)** You’re trained in light and medium armor, and you have expert proficiency in Fortitude saves. You gain the [[Feats/Shield Block]] general feat, a reaction to reduce damage with a shield. If your deity’s weapon is a simple weapon or an unarmed attack, you gain the [[Feats/Deadly Simplicity]] cleric feat. At 13th level, if you gain the divine defense class feature, you also gain expert proficiency in light and medium armor.
- **Second Doctrine (3rd)** You’re trained in martial weapons.
- **Third Doctrine (7th)** You gain expert proficiency with your deity's favored weapon, simple weapons, and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks. When you critically succeed at an attack roll using your deity's favored weapon, you apply the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]]; use your divine spell DC if necessary.
- **Fourth Doctrine (11th)** Your proficiency ranks for divine spell attack rolls and spell DCs increase to expert.
- **Fifth Doctrine (15th)** Your proficiency rank for Fortitude saves increases to master. When you roll a success at a Fortitude save, you get a critical success instead.
- **Final Doctrine (19th)** Your proficiency ranks for divine spell attack rolls and spell DCs increase to master.
