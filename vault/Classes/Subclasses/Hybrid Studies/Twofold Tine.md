# Twofold Tine  *Magus Hybrid Study*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 14

Magic dances between the two weapons you wield with equally deadly grace. Fighting groups of enemies takes full advantage of your capabilities as you move from one foe to another.

 While in [[Actions/Arcane Cascade (Impossible Magic)|Arcane Cascade]] stance, if you make a Strike with a melee weapon you're holding, you can intensify the stance's power for another melee weapon of your choice you're holding in a different hand. Until the end of your turn, any extra damage the chosen weapon gains from Arcane Cascade is increased to 2. The extra damage increases to 4 if you have weapon specialization or 6 if you have greater weapon specialization.
