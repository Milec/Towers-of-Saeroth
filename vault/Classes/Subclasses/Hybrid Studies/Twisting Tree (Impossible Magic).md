# Twisting Tree  *Magus Hybrid Study*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 14

The staff is perhaps the simplest of weapons, but this simplicity belies its elegance and versatility. To you, a staff is casting implement and martial weapon alike—the foundation of a fighting style.

 While you wield a staff in one hand, the staff adjusts in shape and weight, gaining the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] trait and increasing its damage die size to 1d6. While you wield a staff in both hands, it lengthens, twists, and reshapes, gaining the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]], and [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] traits.

 While you're in[[Actions/Arcane Cascade (Impossible Magic)|Arcane Cascade]] stance, once per round you can [Interact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4174) to change your grip on a staff as a free action.
