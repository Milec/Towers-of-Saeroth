# Twisting Tree  *Magus Hybrid Study*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 40

The staff is perhaps one of the simplest of weapons, but this simplicity belies its elegance and versatility. To you, a staff is casting implement and martial weapon alike—the foundation of a fighting style.

 While you wield a staff in one hand, the staff adjusts in shape and weight, gaining the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] trait and increasing its damage die size to 1d6. While you wield a staff in both hands, it lengthens, twists, and reshapes, gaining the [[Traits/Parry|parry]], [[Traits/Reach|reach]], and [[Traits/Trip|trip]] traits. While you're in [[Actions/Arcane Cascade]] stance, you can Interact or Release to change your grip on the weapon as a free action when you Strike with your staff, including Strikes made in a [[Actions/Spellstrike]]. This happens before you roll your attack roll. You can also Interact to change your grip on the staff as a free action triggered at the end of your turn.
