# Volatile Spark  *Magus Hybrid Study*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 14

Your crashing spells meet their match with booming gunshots in a magical fusillade. By modifying your shots with your magic, you create explosions and cut through defenses.

 When you use [[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]], you can make a ranged firearm Strike, as long as the target is within the first range increment of your firearm. This overrides the actual range of the spell. If your firearm has the [[Traits/Concussive|concussive]] trait, any damage from the spell you use in the Spellstrike uses the target's weakest resistance to any of the following: the spell's damage type, piercing, or bludgeoning. This benefit applies only to creatures you target with your firearm Strike during the Spellstrike, so it wouldn't apply to other creatures damaged with Expansive Spellstrike or similar abilities.

 In addition, you gain the Reloading Cascade action.  
 **Reloading Cascade** single action You use Arcane Cascade and [Interact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4174) to reload a firearm.
