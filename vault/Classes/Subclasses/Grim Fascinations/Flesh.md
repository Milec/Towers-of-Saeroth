# Flesh  *Necromancer Grim Fascination*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 30

Flesh necromancers, also known as caromancers, are experts at the destruction, production, and manipulation of flesh and muscles. Your thralls generally take on the form of zombies and other creatures of dead flesh.

**Grave Spell** [[Spells/Dead Weight]]

**Thrall Enhancement** You can still make use of a destroyed thrall’s flesh. Whenever one of your thralls is destroyed, you can cause the thrall to leave behind [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] in the space it was destroyed. The difficult terrain lasts 10 minutes.
