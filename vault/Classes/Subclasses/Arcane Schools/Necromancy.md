# Necromancy  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 208

As a necromancer, you call upon the powers of life and death. While your school is often vilified for its association with raising the undead, you understand that control over life also means control over healing. You add one 1st-level necromancy spell (such as [[Spells/Grim Tendrils|_grim tendrils_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Call of the Grave|_call of the grave_]] school spell.
