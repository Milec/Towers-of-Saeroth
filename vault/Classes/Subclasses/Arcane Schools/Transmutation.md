# Transmutation  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 208

As a transmuter, you alter the physical properties of things, transforming creatures, objects, the natural world, and even yourself at your whim. You understand that change is inevitable. You add one 1st-level transmutation spell (such as [[Spells/Magic Weapon|_magic weapon_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Physical Boost|_physical boost_]] school spell.
