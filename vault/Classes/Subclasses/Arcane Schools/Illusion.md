# Illusion  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 208

As an illusionist, you use magic to create images, figments, and phantasms to baffle your enemies. You understand that perception is reality. You add one 1st-level illusion spell (such as [[Spells/Illusory Object|_illusory object_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Warped Terrain|_warped terrain_]] school spell.
