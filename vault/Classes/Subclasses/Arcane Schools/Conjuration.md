# Conjuration  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 207

As a conjurer, you summon creatures and objects from places beyond, and use magic to transport to distant locales. You understand that the true key to victory is strength in numbers. You add one 1st-level conjuration spell (such as [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Augment Summoning|_augment summoning_]] school spell.
