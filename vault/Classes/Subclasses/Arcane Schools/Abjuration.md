# Abjuration  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 207

As an abjurer, you master the art of protection, strengthening defenses, preventing attacks, and even turning magic against itself. You understand that an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. You add one 1st-level abjuration spell (such as [[Spells/Feather Fall|_feather fall_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Protective Ward|_protective ward_]] school spell.
