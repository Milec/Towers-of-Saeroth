# Divination  *Wizard Arcane School*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 208

As a diviner, you master remote viewing and prescience, learning information that can transform investigations, research, and battle strategies. You understand that knowledge is power. You add one 1st-level divination spell (such as [[Spells/True Strike|_true strike_]]) to your spellbook. You learn the [[Spells/Diviner's Sight|_diviner’s sight_]] school spell.
