# Interrogation  *Investigator Methodology*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 104

People can’t help but trust you, whether through your inherent likableness or your firm insistence on sticking to the truth. You have a way about you that gets others talking, and you’ve developed interrogative techniques to help you get to the truth of your investigations.

You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. You gain the [[Feats/No Cause for Alarm (Player Core)|No Cause for Alarm]] skill feat. You can also Pursue a Lead at the same time you [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], as long as you ask a question related to the creature with which you’re conversing or a topic of your conversation. You also gain the Pointed Question action.
