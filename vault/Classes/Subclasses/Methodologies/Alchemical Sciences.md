# Alchemical Sciences  *Investigator Methodology*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 57

Your methodology emphasizes chemical and alchemical analysis, collecting information from unusual particles and fluids found on the scene. You possess enough alchemical know-how to whip up a few tinctures to help you with your cases.

 You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] and gain the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] skill feat. In addition, you gain a standard [[Equipment/Formula Book (Blank)|formula book]] for free and learn the formulas for two additional common 1st-level alchemical items, which must be [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixirs]] or [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|tools]]. Each time you gain a level, you learn the formula for one common alchemical elixir or alchemical tool of any level of item you can create.

During your daily preparations, you can create a number of versatile vials—alchemical concoctions that can quickly be turned into elixirs and tools— equal to your Intelligence modifier. You can use the Quick Tincture action to turn one of these vials into an elixir or alchemical tool for which you know the formula.
