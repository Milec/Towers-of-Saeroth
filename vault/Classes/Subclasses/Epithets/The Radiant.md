# The Radiant  *Exemplar Root Epithet 3*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 32

Leaders must live bigger lives than any other, shining so brightly that they attract followers, inspire troops, and change the course of kingdoms. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. After you Spark Transcendence, you inspire an ally within 30 feet to push on, restoring Hit Points equal to 2 + double your level; this is a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] and [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect. The ally is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes.
