# Born of the Bones of the Earth  *Exemplar Dominion Epithet 7*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 33

Your dominion is over stone and soil, the pillars holding up the stage of history's great legends. You gain the Energized Spark feat (page 35) for your choice of earth or fire. When you critically succeed on a Strike and the target of the critical hit is standing on a surface of earth or stone, the target is driven down and mired in the ground. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] and must succeed at an [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] attempt against your class DC to end the immobilization. The creature doesn't become stuck if it is [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]], is liquid (like a [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water (Monster Core)|water elemental]] or some [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|oozes]]), has a burrow speed, or could otherwise escape without effort.

 When you Spark Transcendence while standing on a surface of earth or stone, you can choose to shatter the surface in a 10-foot emanation, making it [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. You are not affected by this difficult terrain.
