# Healer of the World  *Exemplar Sovereignty Epithet 15*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 34

Your legacy is written in the lives you save. In your presence, coughs cease and aches abate, as a soft glow fills any living being in your presence. Magic flowing from one hand stitches wounds, and vigor ebbs from the other. After you Spark Transcendence, choose any number of willing or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] creatures in a 30-foot emanation. Each of these creatures that's [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] loses the dying condition and remains unconscious at 0 Hit Points, and each one that isn't dying gains 10 temporary Hit Points that last until the start of your next turn. This is a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect.
