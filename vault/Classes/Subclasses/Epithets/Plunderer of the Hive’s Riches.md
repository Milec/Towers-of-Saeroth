# Plunderer of the Hive’s Riches  *Exemplar Dominion Epithet 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 78

You take what’s yours, even if an army should stand in your way. You gain the [[Feats/Energized Spark]] feat, with your choice of either [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage. When you critically succeed at a Strike, the taste of honey fills your mouth as you plunder part of your foe’s vitality for yourself, gaining [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the creature’s level that last for 1 minute.

When you Spark Transcendence, a mass of buzzing insects fill the air around you, granting you [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] until the beginning of your next turn. You can’t use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], as normal for concealment when your position is still obvious.
