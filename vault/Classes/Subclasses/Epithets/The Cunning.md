# The Cunning  *Exemplar Root Epithet 3*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 31

Why race a hare across a meadow, or a salmon up a waterfall? Why face a titan in a test of strength? Wouldn't it be better to best your foes with a bit of creativity? After all, the stories that echo throughout history are always those where wits and trickery, rather than raw talent or power, win the day. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]. After you Spark Transcendence, you can [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] as a free action.
