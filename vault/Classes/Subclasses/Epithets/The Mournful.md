# The Mournful  *Exemplar Root Epithet 3*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 32

To be a hero is to endure countless hardships and stand where others have fallen, shouldering dreams and destinies in their stead. Though this weight may reach your eyes, you bear this burden so that those under you can live smiling. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]. After you Spark Transcendence, your act resonates with bittersweet poignancy, making one enemy of your choice within 30 feet who witnessed the act [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] as tears or memories dance in their eyes. This is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect. The enemy remains dazzled until the start of your next turn, and then they are temporarily immune for 10 minutes.
