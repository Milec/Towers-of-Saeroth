# The Brave  *Exemplar Root Epithet 3*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 31

Your deeds show fearlessness: when a beast surfaces, you're there to fight it; when someone's lost in the dark, you're first to the rescue. You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]. After you Spark Transcendence, your body carries you forward, allowing you to Stride up to half your Speed in a straight line toward one enemy of your choice as a free action. Once you have used this ability on a given enemy, you can't use it against that enemy again for 10 minutes.
