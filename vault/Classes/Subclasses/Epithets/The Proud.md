# The Proud  *Exemplar Root Epithet 3*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 32

Whether out of overconfidence, a desire to protect your comrades, or the unslakable thirst for glory, you invite challengers to strike you down. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]. After you Spark Transcendence, you can boast to one enemy within 30 feet to draw its attention; this effect has the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], and [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] traits. Until the start of your next turn, the target takes a –1 status penalty to attack rolls, damage rolls, and skill checks against creatures other than you, and it gains a +1 status bonus to these rolls when targeting you.
