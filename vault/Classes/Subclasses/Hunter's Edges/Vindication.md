# Vindication  *Ranger Hunter's Edge*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 64

This Hunter's Edge can be taken by [[Archetypes/Vindicator|Vindicators]].

Your specialized training gives you a unique edge that allows you to more effectively bring the tools of your faith to bear against the monsters that lurk in the night or hide among the faithful.

You gain a +1 status bonus to your spell attack rolls against your [[Actions/Hunt Prey (Player Core)|hunted prey]], and they take a –1 status penalty to their saving throws against [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] spells you cast. You gain the [[Spells/Vindicator's Mark|_vindicator’s mark_]] warden spell.

## Masterful Hunter  *Level 17*

You combine skill and magic to swiftly defeat your prey. The status bonus to divine spell attack rolls against your hunted prey increases to +2, and the status penalty to their saving throws against divine spells you cast is –2.
