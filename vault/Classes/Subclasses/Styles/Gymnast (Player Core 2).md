# Gymnast  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 161

You reposition, maneuver, and bewilder your foes with daring feats of physical prowess. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]. When you [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

If the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to double the number of weapon damage dice.
