# Rascal  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 161

You aren’t afraid to use underhanded tactics to get the edge over your opponents. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] and gain the [[Feats/Dirty Trick]] general feat. When you use Dirty Trick, the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

The foe takes a –10 circumstance penalty to its speed until the start of your next turn.
