# Fencer  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 161

You move carefully, feinting and creating false openings to lead your foes into inopportune attacks. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]. When you [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]], the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

The foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until your next turn.
