# Gymnast  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 84

You reposition, maneuver, and bewilder your foes with daring feats of physical prowess. You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. You gain panache during an encounter whenever you successfully [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] a foe.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

If the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], you gain a circumstance bonus to the damage roll equal to twice the number of weapon damage dice.
