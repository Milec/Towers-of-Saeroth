# Wit  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 161

You are friendly, clever, and full of humor, always knowing just what to say. Your witticisms leave foes unprepared for the skill and speed of your attacks. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] and gain the [[Feats/Bon Mot (Player Core 2)|Bon Mot]] skill feat. When you use Bon Mot, the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

The foe takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn.
