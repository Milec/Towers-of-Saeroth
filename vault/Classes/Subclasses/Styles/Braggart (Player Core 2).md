# Braggart  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 160

You boast, taunt, and psychologically needle your foes. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]. When you [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]], the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

If the foe was temporarily immune to your
Demoralize, their temporary immunity ends.
