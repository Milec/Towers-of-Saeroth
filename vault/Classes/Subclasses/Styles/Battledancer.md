# Battledancer  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 84

To you, a fight is a kind of performance art, and you command your foes' attention with mesmerizing motions. You are trained in Performance and gain the [[Feats/Fascinating Performance]] skill feat. You gain panache during an encounter when the result of your [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Actions/Perform]] exceeds the Will DC of an observing foe, even if the foe isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]].

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

You can Step as a free action immediately after the finisher.
