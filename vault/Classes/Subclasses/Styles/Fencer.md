# Fencer  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 84

You move carefully, feinting and creating false openings to lead your foes into inopportune attacks. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]. You gain panache during an encounter whenever you successfully [[Actions/Feint]] or [[Actions/Create a Diversion]] against a foe.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

The foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until your next turn.
