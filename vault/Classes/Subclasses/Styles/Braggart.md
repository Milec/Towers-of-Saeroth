# Braggart  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 84

You boast, taunt, and psychologically needle your foes. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]. You gain panache during an encounter whenever you successfully [[Actions/Demoralize]] a foe.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

If the foe was temporarily immune to your [[Actions/Demoralize]], their temporary immunity ends.
