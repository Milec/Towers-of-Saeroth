# Battledancer  *Swashbuckler Style*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 160

To you, a fight is a kind of performance art, and you command your foes’ attention with mesmerizing motions. You are trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] and gain the [[Feats/Fascinating Performance (Player Core)|Fascinating Performance]] skill feat. When you [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]], the action gains the [[Traits/Bravado|bravado]] trait.

## Exemplary Finisher  *9th*

You can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action immediately after the finisher.
