# Lesson of the Elements  *Witch Lesson*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 185

**Lesson Type** Basic

Natural disasters and inclement weather hold more power than the mightiest creature.
You gain the [[Spells/Elemental Betrayal (Player Core)|Elemental Betrayal]] hex. Your familiar learns your choice of [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]], [[Spells/Gust of Wind|_gust of wind_]], [[Spells/Hydraulic Push|_hydraulic push_]], or [[Spells/Pummeling Rubble|_pummeling rubble_]].
