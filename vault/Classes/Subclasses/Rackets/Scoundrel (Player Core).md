# Scoundrel  *Rogue Racket*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 166

You use fast-talking, flattery, and a silver tongue to avoid danger and escape tricky situations. You might be a grifter or con artist, or you might use your skills in certain reputable professions, like a barrister or diplomat

 When you successfully [[Actions/Feint]], the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|off-guard]] against your melee attacks until the end of your next turn. On a critical success, the target is off-guard against all melee attacks until the end of your next turn, not just yours. If you Feint while wielding an agile or finesse melee weapon, you can [[Actions/Step]] immediately after the Feint as a free action.

 You’re trained in [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]]. You can choose Charisma as your key ability score.
