# Mastermind  *Rogue Racket*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 166

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) For the purposes of abilities like the mastermind rogue racket, a character is considered to have successfully identified a creature when they succeed or critically succeed at a Recall Knowledge check, regardless of what information they gain._

You rely on your intellect to craft intricate schemes, planning 10 steps ahead where others might plan three. You might be a driven detective, a spymaster in the service of a powerful house, or an aspiring crime lord or information broker.

 If you successfully identify a creature using [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against your attacks until the start of your next turn; if you critically succeed, it's off-guard against your attacks for 1 minute.

 You're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] and one of the following skills of your choice: [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. You can choose Intelligence as your key attribute.
