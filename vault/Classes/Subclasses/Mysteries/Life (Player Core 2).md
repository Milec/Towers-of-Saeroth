# Life  *Oracle Mystery*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 137

The never-ending flow of life force within living beings is palpable to you. You might uphold the sanctity of life, or perhaps you seek to undermine it. You might draw power from the collective vitality of the world’s living creatures, hold some connection to [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge|Creation’s Forge]], or revere a collection of deities including [[Setting/Deities/Irori (Divine Mysteries)|Irori]], [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], and the god of medicine [[Setting/Deities/Qi Zhong (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Qi Zhong]].

## Curse of Outpouring Life  *Oracular Curse*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

Life energy flows outward from you and connects you to all living things, but you expend your vital essence to do so. Your presence comforts the ill and injured, causes scars to fade slightly, spurs new growth in plants, and otherwise infuses your surroundings with vitality. As your life force seeps outward, it becomes more difficult to keep your body functioning. Magical effects that restore Hit Points to you take a status penalty equal to your level (minimum 1) times your [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] value to the number of HP you recover.
