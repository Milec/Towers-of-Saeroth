# Lore  *Oracle Mystery*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 137

Knowledge and information come freely to you. You might use this lore to gain power or perhaps to understand the divine mysteries of the multiverse. ## Curse of Torrential Knowledge  *Oracular Curse*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

You have a link to true divine knowledge, but your mortal mind struggles to process and act on what you know. Loose materials around you, such as dust, grains of rice, and droplets of water, slowly shift to form strange runes or faint, indecipherable writing, and you sometimes speak unintelligible truths or statements in unknown languages without realizing it. You take a status penalty to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks and Will saving throws equal to your [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] value due to the torrential distractions of unasked-for knowledge flooding your mind. If you are cursebound 4, you additionally can’t speak, use [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] effects, or otherwise communicate with your allies. You can still cast spells, but you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1.
