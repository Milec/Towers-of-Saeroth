# Cosmos  *Oracle Mystery*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 136

Celestial bodies great and small exert influence on you, giving you sublime cosmic power. Perhaps you see the glittering stars as a divine blessing, or perhaps you feel drawn to the infinitely dark spaces between. You might uphold deities like [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], or the deific lovers [[Setting/Deities/Shizuru (Divine Mysteries)|Shizuru]] and [[Setting/Deities/Tsukiyo (Divine Mysteries)|Tsukiyo]] who represent the sun and the moon— or you might draw power from dark entities from beyond the stars, like certain [[Setting/Deity Categories/Outer Gods and Great Old Ones|Outer Gods]], or destructive gods of the night like [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]] or the rat goddess [[Setting/Deities/Lao Shu Po (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lao Shu Po]].

## Curse of the Sky's Call  *Oracular Curse*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

Your body is drawn toward the heavens, making you lighter and less substantial than you should be. Your eyes glow with starry light, and your hair and clothing float and drift around you. When you have the [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] condition, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] with a value equal to your cursebound value, and you take a status penalty to saves and DCs against all forms of [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] equal to your cursebound value.
