# Ash  *Oracle Mystery*

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 284

You see all things in the world as fleeting and temporary, waiting to be purified into their base essence: the ash left behind after a burning fire. While you understand fire as a necessary part of this process, you see it mostly as a tool to achieve final purity, not the true goal. You have much in common with oracles with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames (Player Core 2)|flames]] mystery, but you may consider them to be shortsighted, or at best, simply lacking in understanding of the truths that their burning fires impart.

You might see ash as the only true representation of the impermanent nature of life and its pointless existence. Alternatively, you may see the benefit ash can have on soils and farmlands as part of a larger cycle of death and rebirth. Perhaps you see the [[Bestiary/Phoenix (Monster Core)|phoenix]], with its life cycle of being reduced to ash and reborn, as a sacred creature that has achieved a higher state of being and is worthy of imitation, or perhaps you see it as a tragic creature that can never reach the goal of perfection. If you follow a deity, you might follow gods of fire like [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], those who burn impurities to ash such as [[Setting/Deities/Brigh]] or [[Setting/Deities/Torag (Divine Mysteries)|Torag]], or those who strive to burn all things to ash, such as the archdevil [[Setting/Deities/Moloch]] or the demon lord Flauros.

## Curse Of Creeping Ashes  *Oracular Curse*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

Your body is slowly being consumed by the fires of your internal power, purifying you with each passing day. You are occasionally wracked with dry, wheezing coughs, and wherever you go you leave behind a fine trace of ash that falls from your body.

**Cursebound 1** You gain [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 2 to fire damage. Any [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity or resistance]] you have to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] is suppressed.

**Cursebound 2** Swirling ash imposes a -2 circumstance penalty to ranged attack rolls you make.

**Cursebound 3** Your weakness to fire damage is equal to 5 + your level.

**Cursebound 4** You take a –10-foot status penalty to all your Speeds as your limbs begin to crumble like ash.
