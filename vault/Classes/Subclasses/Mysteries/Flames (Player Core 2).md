## Curse of Engulfing Flames  *Oracular Curse*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Oracle (Player Core 2)|Oracle]]

Fires flare noticeably (though not dangerously) in your presence, you occasionally smoke slightly, and your body is almost painfully hot to the touch. When you have the [[Traits/Cursebound (Player Core 2)|cursebound]] condition, you catch fire, taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] equal to your cursebound value, and any [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunity]] or [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] you have to fire is suppressed. The flames shed [[Rules/Light (Player Core)|light]] like a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]], and if you enter an environment where they could not burn (such as underwater), you simply seethe with flameless heat. The flames subside when you begin [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocusing]] to assuage your curse or if you fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], but they resume if your Refocus activity is interrupted or when you return to consciousness.
