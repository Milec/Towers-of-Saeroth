# Baruiel, Rune of Hold's Bravery  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a willing creature

---

This scratched rune reminds the bearer to stand strong against fear when near allies. As long as the rune-bearer is within 10 feet of one of its allies, whenever it would reduce its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition by 1, it instead reduces it by 2.

**Invocation**
The essence of the rune is released outward, easing the minds of others. Each ally of the rune-bearer within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] reduces their frightened condition by 2.
