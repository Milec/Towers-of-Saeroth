# Piteregrin, Rune of Transposition  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn onto a creature

---

The slanted lines and odd curls of this rune give the impression that it is trying to escape. The rune-bearer can use a single action to swap places with any other willing rune-bearer within 15 feet. (The other rune-bearer can be bearing any rune.) Each creature must have room to appear in its new location or the effect is disrupted. This action has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] and [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] traits.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) The rune grows to an enormous size, swallowing up the rune-bearer and each Large or smaller creature adjacent to them, and then immediately spits them back out. Choose a square within a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from the rune-bearer's original position for each swallowed creature to reappear. A Large creature doesn't necessarily need to reappear completely within the emanation, as long as one of its squares is. An unwilling creature avoids being teleported if it succeeds at a Will save against your class DC.
