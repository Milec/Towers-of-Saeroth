# Nesh-, Diacritic Rune of Contingency  *Runesmith Rune 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn on a rune

---

The wavy lines of this diacritic seem to stretch the base rune into a wider area. When you draw this rune, choose a trigger that when met will invoke the base rune, using the same restrictions as for the trigger of a [[Actions/Ready (Player Core)|Ready]] action. When the trigger is met, you can [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke the base Rune]] as a reaction; you can't invoke any additional runes with that reaction. If you define complicated conditions, as determined by the GM, the trigger might fail.

---
You can have only one _nesh-, diacritic rune of contingency_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't etch or trace it again for 10 minutes.
