# Ichelsu, Rune of Observation  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

A ring of dotted circles, this rune allows a creature marked with it to see all. The target is affected by [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]] and gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/All-Around Vision (Monster Core)|all-around vision]], which prevents it from being flanked.

**Invocation**
The eyes of the rune fly outwards, attaching to all creatures in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. A creature with an eye attached to it that would be invisible is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead, and one that would be concealed for any other reason is not concealed. This effect lasts for 2 rounds.
