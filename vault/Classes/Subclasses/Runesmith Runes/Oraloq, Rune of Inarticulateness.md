# Oraloq, Rune of Inarticulateness  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This hard-to-read rune makes language difficult for the unfortunate rune-bearer. When the rune-bearer attempts a skill check or ability with the [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] trait or attempts to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that doesn't have the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait, they must succeed a DC 5 [[Rules/Checks (rules-2278)|flat check]], or the action is lost.

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer must attempt a Will save as the rune puts a strong mental block on the part of their mind that interprets language. If the rune-bearer fails their save, they take 20 mental damage (or double damage on a critical failure) the first time they use any language, take a linguistic action, or Cast a Spell without the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait. The rune-bearer is aware of this and knows what will trigger the damage. The effect ends once the damage is taken or at the end of the rune-bearer's next turn, whichever comes first.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage of the invocation increases by 5.
