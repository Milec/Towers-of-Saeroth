# Ochygholl, The Poisoned Star  *Runesmith Rune 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 61

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This awe-inspiring rune of pointed curves is rumored to have been part of the magic that [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthus]] used to call down the meteoroids of the Earthfall cataclysm. The rune-bearer senses a foreboding shadow overhead and that something terrible is about to happen. This dread is contagious. The rune-bearer becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed 1]], and its allies that begin their turn adjacent to the rune-bearer also become doomed 1 for 1 round. Each creature doomed this way takes 10 mental damage the first time each round it attempts an attack roll or skill check.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) The rune calls a meteorite down on the rune-bearer, fulfilling its terrible promise. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] centered on a corner in the rune-bearer's space takes 3d10 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. Then, each creature within a 40-foot burst centered on that same corner takes 8d6 fire damage, also with a basic Reflex save. This invocation has the [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] trait for doomed creatures.
