# Sar-, Diacritic Rune of Righteousness  *Runesmith Rune 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 61

**Usage** drawn on a rune

---

Clean arcs of gold faintly glow on the base rune, evoking celestial order and serenity. The base rune gains the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait, as does any damage it deals. If applied to an [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] for as long as it bears the rune.

**Invocation**
When the base rune is invoked, it shines with holy light that harshly judges the target for any of its sins. In addition to the base rune's normal effect, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]].
