# Oljinex, Rune of Cowards' Bane  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 56

**Usage** drawn on a shield

---

This rune resembles a broken arrow. The shield bearing the rune gains a +2 status bonus to its [[Rules/Item Damage (Player Core)|Hardness]] against physical damage from ranged attacks.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) The rune creates an illusion in the minds of all creatures adjacent to the rune-bearer that lasts for 1 round. The illusion is of a large, impassable wall blocking all paths away from the rune-bearer. Creatures affected by this invocation can't willingly move farther away from the rune-bearer for 1 round or until they disbelieve the illusion, usually by using a [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action.

---
**Level (+4)** The status bonus to Hardness increases by 1.
