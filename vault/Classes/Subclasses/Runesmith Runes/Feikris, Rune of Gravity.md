# Feikris, Rune of Gravity  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on a creature wearing armor

---

The lines of this rune overlap strangely, making it seem larger than it really is. The rune-bearer gains a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks. They can [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than themself, or three sizes larger if the rune-bearer is legendary in Athletics.

**Invocation**
All creatures in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around the rune-bearer must succeed at a Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|pulled]] 5 feet toward the rune-bearer (or 10 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Level (17th)** The status bonus increases to +3.
