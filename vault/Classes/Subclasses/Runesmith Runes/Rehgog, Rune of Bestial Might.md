# Rehgog, Rune of Bestial Might  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a willing creature

---

The jagged edges of this rune evoke the claws and talons of mighty beasts. The target's nose becomes an animal-like snout, giving the rune-bearer scent as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise scent]] with a range of 30 feet.

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer's muscles bulge with the strength of a ferocious beast. The bearer can immediately [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action. The next unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] the bearer attempts before the start of your next turn deals an additional die of damage.
