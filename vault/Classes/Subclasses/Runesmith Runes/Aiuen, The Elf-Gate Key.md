# Aiuen, The Elf-Gate Key  *Runesmith Rune 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 61

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This masterful circular rune is a key to the aiudara network of elf gates that stretch across the stars. Once per turn, when the rune-bearer [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]], it can instead teleport a distance equal to its Speed.

**Invocation**
You teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your other runes, as long as it is on the same plane. An unwilling target can be teleported a maximum distance of 60 feet, and it avoids being teleported at all if it succeeds at a Will save against your class DC.

---
If you have the ability to cast the [[Spells/Sigil (Player Core)|_sigil_]] cantrip, then you can use _aiuen, the elf-gate key's_ invocation to teleport the rune-bearer to a location adjacent to any of your sigils on the same plane, in addition to any of your runes.
