# Xinsala, The Well of Virtues  *Runesmith Rune 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 61

**Usage** drawn on an unoccupied square

---

This complex rune comprises many different ligatures from the ancient Thassilonian runes representing the seven virtues of rule, before they were corrupted by the runelords. When drawn, it creates a well of glowing energy that occupies the square it's drawn on and creates an aura of swirling, virtuous runes in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. While in the aura, you or any of your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC against magic, a +1 status bonus to saving throws against magic, and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to damage from magical effects.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) The well explodes outward with invigorating force. The well heals any number of creatures in its area for a total of 70 Hit Points divided as you choose, and you and each of your allies in the area gain a +20-foot status bonus to your Speeds and are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round. The extra action from quickened can be used only to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
