# Ulgatus, Rune of Restraint  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn on armor

---

A faction of the constructed beings called automatons developed this rune to avoid the weaknesses of flesh. When the creature wearing the armor would gain or increase the value of their [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition, the rune attempts to prevent the imposition. The bearer attempts a DC 15 [[Rules/Checks (rules-2278)|flat check]]. On a success, it doesn't gain or increase the value of that condition, but the rune goes temporarily dormant for 10 minutes. While dormant, the rune can't protect the bearer from conditions but can be invoked.

**Invocation**
The rune causes the armor to grow around the creature wearing it, fortifying any vulnerable spots. For 1 minute, the wearer gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to critical hits.

---
**Level (17th)** The DC of the flat check decreases to 10 and the resistance to critical hits increases to 20.
