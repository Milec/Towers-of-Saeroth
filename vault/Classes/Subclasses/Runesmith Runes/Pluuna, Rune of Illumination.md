# Pluuna, Rune of Illumination  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 56

**Usage** drawn on a creature or armor

---

While many runes emit a faint glow, illumination is the focus of this simple rune. The rune sheds [[Rules/Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] to 40 feet). Creatures in the emanation take a –1 item penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks.

**Invocation**
Each creature in the light must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round. The light fades, but the target still sheds dim light in a 20-foot emanation for 1 round.
