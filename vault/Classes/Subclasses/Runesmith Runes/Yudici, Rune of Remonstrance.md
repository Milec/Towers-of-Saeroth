# Yudici, Rune of Remonstrance  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn on a shield

---

This majestic rune grants the shield's wielder the conviction to aid their allies. While a shield bearing this rune is raised, its bearer emits an aura in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that grants their allies a +1 circumstance bonus to AC.

**Invocation**
The shield regains 40 Hit Points, and all of the bearer's allies in a 10-foot emanation gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and skill checks for 1 round.

---
**Level (+3)** The Hit Points restored increases by 10.
