# Sertum, Rune of Preparedness  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

The wavy lines of this rune evoke fields of reeds, the branches of trees blowing in the wind, or other natural phenomena. The rune-bearer gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] checks related to a type of terrain or specific natural location you choose as you draw the rune. The rune-bearer can use Survival to roll [[Rules/Initiative (Player Core)|initiative]], and the status bonus applies to this roll.

**Invocation**
The rune reaches out to the surrounding terrain, shifting it to the rune-bearer's advantage. The rune-bearer can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] up to three times as a free action.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
