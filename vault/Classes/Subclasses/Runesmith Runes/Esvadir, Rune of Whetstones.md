# Esvadir, Rune of Whetstones  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a weapon or unarmed attack that deals piercing or slashing damage

---

This cuspate rune, when placed on a blade, ensures it won't go dull. [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with the weapon or unarmed attack deal an extra 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] per weapon damage die.

**Invocation**
A blast of cutting energy is released outward from the rune, dealing 1d8 slashing damage to a creature adjacent to the rune-bearer, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage of the invocation increases by 1d8.
