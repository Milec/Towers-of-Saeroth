# Tilus, Rune of Vocabulary  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

Upon close inspection, this rune comprises hundreds of smaller characters of various runic languages. When you etch or trace this rune, choose one language you know. The rune-bearer can speak and understand the chosen language and gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] checks and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] checks related to writing (such as Library Lore and Scribing Lore).

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer's voice rings clear and true, allowing them to automatically succeed at any [[Rules/Checks (rules-2278)|flat check]] that could disrupt an [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] action (such as the one imposed by the [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] condition) for 1 round. During this time, the bearer can also speak their true mind even if they are currently [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], or otherwise unable to speak.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
