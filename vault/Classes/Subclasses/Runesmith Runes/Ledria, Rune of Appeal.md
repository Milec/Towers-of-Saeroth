# Ledria, Rune of Appeal  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

No matter what skin, shell, or scale this rune is drawn on, it shines like gold. The rune-bearer gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks.

**Invocation**
The rune draws creatures closer to the bearer. Each creature within 30 feet of the rune-bearer can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] closer to the rune-bearer as a free action.

---
**Level (9th)** The bonus increases to +2.

**Level (17th)** The bonus increases to +3.
