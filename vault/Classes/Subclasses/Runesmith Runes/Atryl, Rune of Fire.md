# Atryl, Rune of Fire  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a crature or object

---

This rune is often placed on a stone in a hearth, its power ensuring the stone remains warm through the night. The bearer’s fire [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]], if any, is reduced by 5. Its [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunities]] are unaffected.

**Invocation**
The bearer takes 1d8 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a critical failure, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.

---
**Level (+2)** The reduction in fire resistance increases by 1, and the damage of the invocation increases by 1d8.
