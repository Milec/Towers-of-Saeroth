# Astillu, Rune of Submersion  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

Originating from Thalassic script, this wavy rune is popular with iruxi practitioners of rune magic who want to easily traverse their watery environs. The rune-bearer can breathe underwater and gains a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]]  equal to its land Speed or 30 feet, whichever is greater. Its melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] don't take the usual circumstance penalty for passing through water.

**Invocation**
The rune's power pushes the bearer forward in a surprising surge of speed. The rune-bearer can [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to 60 feet as a free action, and this movement doesn't trigger reactions.
