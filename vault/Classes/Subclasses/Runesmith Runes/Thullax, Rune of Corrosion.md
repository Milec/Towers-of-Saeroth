# Thullax, Rune of Corrosion  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]], [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a creature or object

---

The swooping lines of this rune seem to melt into each other as if their edges were hazy and indistinct, though the meaning is clear to you. The rune-bearer's [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to each physical damage type is reduced by 1 (if it has any such resistances). Its [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunities]] are unaffected.

**Invocation**
The bearer takes 1d6 acid damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save; on a critical failure, it takes 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]].

---
**Level (+2)**  The reduction in physical resistance increases by 1, the damage of the invocation increases by 1d6, and the persistent damage increases by 1.
