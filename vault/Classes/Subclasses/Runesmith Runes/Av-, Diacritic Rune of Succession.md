# Av-, Diacritic Rune of Succession  *Runesmith Rune 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a rune

---

This diacritic surrounds the base rune with similar-looking smaller runes to give the impression of distant echoes. When the base rune is invoked, you can [[Actions/Invoke Rune|Invoke a single Rune]] on a different rune-bearer within 15 feet of the original rune-bearer as a free action.

---
You can have only one copy of _av-, diacritic rune of succession_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't etch or trace it again for 10 minutes.
