# Kojastri, Rune of Insulation  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on armor

---

This rune insulates from harmful energy of all kinds. The creature wearing the armor gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 4 to your choice of cold, electricity, or fire, and any creature that touches the rune-bearer or damages it with an unarmed attack or non-reach melee weapon takes 2 damage of the chosen type. The rune has the trait of the energy type chosen.

**Invocation**
Any creature that has the armor's wearer engulfed, [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], or swallowed whole takes 8d4 damage of the rune's chosen type with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC. On a failure, it also releases the armor's wearer.

---
**Level (+2)** The resistance and damage granted by insulation both increase by 1, and the damage dealt by the invocation increases by 2d4.
