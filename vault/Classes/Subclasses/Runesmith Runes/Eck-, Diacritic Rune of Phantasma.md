# Eck-, Diacritic Rune of Phantasma  *Runesmith Rune 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn on a rune that deals damage

---

This diacritic frames a base rune with almost invisible lines that turn the rune's effects against the bearer's soul. The damage dealt on the base rune becomes spirit damage, and each of your enemies adjacent to the rune-bearer takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent spirit damage]].

---
**Level (17th)** The persistent spirit damage increases by 1d8.
