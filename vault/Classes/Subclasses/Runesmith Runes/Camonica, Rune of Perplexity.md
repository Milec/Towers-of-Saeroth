# Camonica, Rune of Perplexity  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This rune of looping swirls is difficult to look straight at and stirs up a psychic cacophony in a foe's mind. The first time each round the rune-bearer takes an action with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, the turmoil increases, dealing it 2 mental damage.

**Invocation**
The cacophony fragments and expands rapidly in a mental explosion, dealing 1d4 mental damage to the rune- bearer with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save. On a failure, the rune-bearer is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]]  for 1 round (or stupefied 2 on a critical failure).

---
**Level (+2)** The damage from the mental turmoil increases by 1 and the damage of the invocation increases by 1d4.
