# Zohk, Rune of Homecoming  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 57

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This circular mark allows travelers to always find their way home. The rune-bearer gains a +15-foot status bonus to its Speeds, but only when its movement would end closer to you than where the creature started its turn.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) You call the rune-bearer to your side. You teleport the target to any unoccupied square adjacent to you. If the bearer is unwilling, it must succeed at a Will save to negate the effect.
