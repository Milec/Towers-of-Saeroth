# Sun-, Diacritic Rune of Preservation  *Runesmith Rune 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 58

**Usage** drawn on a rune

---

This spiraling diacritic channels the magic of a rune outwards, then back to the same location, allowing a rune to reconstitute itself. After the base rune is invoked, the base rune automatically traces itself back upon the same target.

---
You can have only one copy of _sun-, diacritic rune of preservation_ applied at a given time, and once you invoke it, you can't Etch or Trace the Rune again for 10 minutes.
