# Inth-, Diacritic Rune of Corruption  *Runesmith Rune 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Diacritic]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 60

**Usage** drawn on a rune

---

This set of angular accents around the base rune channels the essence of fiendish corruption. The base rune gains the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait, as does any damage it deals. If applied to a holy creature, that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] for as long as it bears the rune.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) When the base rune is invoked, it burns away in unholy black fire that lingers on its target. In addition to the base rune's normal effect, the target takes 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].

---
**Level (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 2d4.
