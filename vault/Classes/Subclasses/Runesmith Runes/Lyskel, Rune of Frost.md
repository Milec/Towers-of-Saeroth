# Lyskel, Rune of Frost  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 55

**Usage** drawn on a creature or object

---

This rune is often placed on chests and boxes to keep their contents from spoiling, even in the hottest of climates. The rune-bearer takes a –5-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds and becomes so cold that movement becomes dangerous; if the bearer takes a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] until the beginning of its next turn.

**Invocation**
The rune-bearer takes 1d4 cold damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. All squares on the ground in the bearer's space and adjacent squares are covered in snow, becoming [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for 1 round.

---
**Level (+2)** The damage of the invocation increases by 1d4.
