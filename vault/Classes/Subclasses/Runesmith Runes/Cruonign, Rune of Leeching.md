# Cruonign, Rune of Leeching  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn on a slashing or piercing weapon

---

The jagged shape of this rune resembles a vampire's fangs. [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with the rune-bearing weapon deal an additional 3 void damage. Whenever a living creature is damaged by one of these Strikes, the rune-bearer gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to the void damage the target took (after applying [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistances]] and the like). These temporary Hit Points last 1 round.

**Invocation**
The rune lashes out in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] from the wielder of the weapon, dealing 4d10 void damage to each creature in the area, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. If any creatures take damage, the wielder regains 12 Hit Points.

---
**Level (+2)** The additional void damage increases by 1, the damage of the invocation increases by 1d10, and the healing increases by 3.
