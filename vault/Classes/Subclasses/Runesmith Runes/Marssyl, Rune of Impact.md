# Marssyl, Rune of Impact  *Runesmith Rune 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 56

**Usage** drawn on a weapon or unarmed attack that deals bludgeoning damage

---

This rune magnifies force many times over as it passes through the rune's concentric rings. The weapon or unarmed attack deals 1 bludgeoning [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage per weapon damage die. If the rune is on a melee weapon or melee unarmed attack, you and the rune-bearer are immune to this splash damage.

**Invocation**
The rune vibrates as power concentrates within it. The next successful [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] made with the weapon or unarmed attack before the end of its wielder's next turn deals an additional die of damage, and the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|pushed]] 10 feet away from the attacker (20 feet on a critical failure).
