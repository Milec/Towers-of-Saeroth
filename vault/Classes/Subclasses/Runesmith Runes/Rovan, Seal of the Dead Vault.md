# Rovan, Seal of the Dead Vault  *Runesmith Rune 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 61

**Usage** drawn on a creature

---

This mighty, jagged rune is just one of several that keeps the Destroyer sealed in his prison, deep in Golarion's core. The creature takes a –20-foot status penalty to its land Speed and a –40-foot status penalty to all other Speeds as the rune attempts to bind the creature. If a creature's [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] is reduced to 0 this way, it can't take the [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] or [[Actions/Arrest a Fall (Player Core)|Arrest a Fall]] actions.

**Invocation**
([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]]) The ground in the rune-bearer's space and in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] permanently, and the rune-bearer must attempt a Fortitude saving throw as the earth around it attempts to surround and imprison it. The rune-bearer is then immune to your _rovan, seal of the Dead Vault_ runes for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The bearer is unaffected.  

**Success** The rune-bearer becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The rune-bearer becomes slowed 2 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The bearer becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] as it is trapped in a massive, compressed sphere of earth. While restrained, the bearer is also slowed 2. If the bearer is unable to escape the sphere within 3 rounds, it becomes petrified. Over the course of a year, the sphere forms a small hill around it, hiding its buried prisoner. At the top of the hill, a stone with the image of this rune remains. [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|Counteracting]] the stone frees the prisoner.
