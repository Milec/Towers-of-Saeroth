# Jurroz, Rune of Dragon Fury  *Runesmith Rune 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Rune]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 59

**Usage** drawn onto a creature

---

This craggy rune channels the fury of dragon kind. Whenever an enemy [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steals]] from the rune-bearer, they become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.

**Invocation**
As a free action, the rune-bearer can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to 60 feet toward an enemy that has damaged it or stolen from it in the last minute. If it ends this movement adjacent to the enemy, the enemy becomes off-guard until the end of the rune-bearer's next turn. If the rune-bearer doesn't have a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] and ends its movement in the air, it safely floats back to the ground at the end of its next turn.
