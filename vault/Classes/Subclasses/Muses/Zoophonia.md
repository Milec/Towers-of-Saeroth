# Zoophonia  *Bard Muse*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 65

Your muse is a master of birdsong, wolf howls, trumpeting elephants, and other animal communication, pushing you to new heights and encouraging your connections with nature. If your muse is a creature, it might be an enlightened animal or a [[Bestiary/Families/Spirit Guide|spirit guide]]. If it's a deity, it might be one of the Wardens of the Wild, [[Setting/Deities/Gozreh (Divine Mysteries)|Gozreh]], or another representative of nature.
