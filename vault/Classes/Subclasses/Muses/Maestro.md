# Maestro  *Bard Muse*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 97

Your muse is a virtuoso, inspiring you to greater heights. If it's a creature, it might be a performance-loving creature such as a [[Bestiary/Choral|choral angel]] or [[Bestiary/Lillend|lillend azata]]; if a deity, it might be [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn]]. As a bard with a maestro muse, you are an inspiration to your allies and confident of your musical and oratorical abilities. You gain the [[Feats/Lingering Composition]] feat and add [[Spells/Soothe|_soothe_]] to your spell repertoire.
