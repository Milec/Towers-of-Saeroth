# Polymath  *Bard Muse*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 97

Your muse is a jack of all trades, flitting between skills and pursuits. If it's is a creature, it might be an eclectic creature like a [[Traits/Fey|fey]]; if a deity, it might be [[Setting/Deities/Desna]] or [[Setting/Deities/Calistria]]. As a bard with a polymath muse, you are interested in a wide array of topics but rarely dedicated to any one, and you rarely make up your mind—you want to try everything. You gain the [[Feats/Versatile Performance]] feat and add [[Spells/Unseen Servant|_unseen servant_]] to your spell repertoire.
