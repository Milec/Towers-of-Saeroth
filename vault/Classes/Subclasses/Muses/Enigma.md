# Enigma  *Bard Muse*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 97

Your muse is a mystery, driving you to uncover the hidden secrets of the multiverse. If your muse is a creature, it might be a [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] or otherworldly being; if a deity, it might be [[Setting/Deities/Irori]] or [[Setting/Deities/Nethys]]. You gain the [[Feats/Bardic Lore]] feat and add [[Spells/True Strike|_true strike_]] to your spell repertoire.
