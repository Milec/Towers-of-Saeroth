# Justice  *Champion Cause*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 92

In your deity’s name you seek justice, following the law and punishing those who transgress it.

**Edicts**
follow the law, respect legitimate authorities or leadership

**Anathema**
take advantage of another, cheat

## Champion's Reaction

## Relentless Reaction  *Level 9*

 If your Strike hits, the target takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent spirit damage]] equal to your Charisma modifier.

## Exalted Reaction  *Level 11*

Each ally in your champion’s aura with the target in their melee reach can spend a reaction to Strike the target with a –5 penalty.
