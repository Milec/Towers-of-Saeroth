# Toxicologist  *Alchemist Research Field*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 62

You specialize in toxins and venoms of all types.

## Formulas  *Level 1*

Two common 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical poisons]].

## Field Benefit  *Level 1*

You can Activate an [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] as a single action, provided you apply it to a weapon you're holding or a piece of ammunition on your person or loaded in a weapon you're holding. This can be an item you're holding or you can Interact to draw an eligible item or make one using Quick Alchemy as part of the single action; if you can make more than one item with Quick Alchemy (such as with the double brew class feature), you can still Activate only one.

 In addition, you flexibly mix acidic and poisonous alchemical compounds. Your [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] items with the poison trait can affect creatures [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immune]] to poison. A creature takes acid damage instead of poison damage from such items if either the creature is immune to poison or that would be more detrimental to the creature (as determined by the GM).

## Field Vials  *Level 1*

Your versatile vials have the [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] trait and deal poison damage instead of having the [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] trait and dealing acid damage (though your field benefit still applies). You can apply the contents of a versatile vial to a weapon or piece of ammunition as an [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] poison. Add the versatile vial’s initial damage to the first successful [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with that weapon or ammunition. The substance becomes inert at the end of your current turn.

## Field Discovery  *Level 5*

You have handled enough poisons to become inured to their effects. You gain poison [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to half your level.

## Advanced Vials  *Level 11*

When you damage a creature with a versatile vial you’ve used as an [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] poison, that creature takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] equal to the vial’s splash damage in addition to the initial damage.

## Greater Field Discovery  *Level 13*

When a creature fails its initial saving throw against an [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] [[Traits/Injury (GM Core)|injury]] poison you created, the wound sprays poison onto another creature adjacent to it. The attacker who caused the injury chooses which creature, if there’s more than one, and can choose to forgo this effect. That creature is exposed to the poison. The second creature doesn’t spread the poison further.
