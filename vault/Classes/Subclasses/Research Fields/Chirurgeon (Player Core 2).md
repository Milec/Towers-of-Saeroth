# Chirurgeon  *Alchemist Research Field*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 61

You concentrate on healing others with alchemy.

## Formulas  *Level 1*

Two common 1st-level [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical elixirs]] with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait (like [lesser antidote](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2879), [lesser antiplague](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2880), or [minor elixir of life](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2887)).

## Field Benefit  *Level 1*

You can use your proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] for anything that requires a proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] (such as prerequisites) and use your Crafting modifier in place of your Medicine modifier for all Medicine checks.

## Field Vials  *Level 1*

Your versatile vials can be used to heal a living creature a number of Hit Points equal to the vial’s initial damage. A creature can drink the vial for this benefit, or you can throw the vial at a willing creature within 20 feet as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to heal that creature. In either case, a vial used this way loses the [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] and [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] traits and gains the [[Traits/Coagulant|coagulant]] and [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] traits, plus the [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] trait if a creature drinks it.

## Field Discovery  *Level 5*

Your medicinal elixirs are quite fortifying. When a creature drinks an [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] elixir with the healing trait that you have created, that creature gains a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 0); these temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.

## Advanced Vials  *Level 11*

When you use a field vial to heal a creature that has half its maximum Hit Points or fewer, the [[Traits/Coagulant|coagulant]] trait doesn’t apply to that healing.

## Greater Field Discovery  *Level 13*

When you use [[Actions/Quick Alchemy (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]] to create any type of elixir of life, the creature healed by the item regains the maximum Hit Points possible, instead of rolling to determine the number of Hit Points regained.
