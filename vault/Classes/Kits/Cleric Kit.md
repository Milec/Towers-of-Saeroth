## Class Kits

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 289

If you want to quickly decide how to spend your starting money on what your class needs, start with one of these kits. Note than an [[Equipment/Adventurer's Pack|adventurer’s pack]], which is included in each kit, contains a [[Equipment/Backpack|backpack]], a [[Equipment/Bedroll|bedroll]], [[Equipment/Chalk|10 pieces of chalk]], [[Equipment/Flint and Steel|flint and steel]], [[Equipment/Rope|50 feet of rope]], [[Equipment/Rations|2 weeks’ rations]], [[Equipment/Soap|soap]], [[Equipment/Torch|5 torches]], and a [[Equipment/Waterskin|waterskin]].
