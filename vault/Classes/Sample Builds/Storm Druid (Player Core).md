# Storm Druid  *Class Sample Build*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 132

_You call upon the power of nature to let loose bolts of lightning, intense weather, and elemental spells._

## Attributes

Prioritize Wisdom. You typically stay at range, so take a high Dexterity. You can identify creatures from the safety of the air with high Intelligence.
