# Crane Monk  *Class Sample Build*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 164

_Striving for grace in all things, you mimic the fluid movements of the crane. Your strikes are quick as the wind and your mind clear as a placid pool._

## Attributes

Prioritize Dexterity for finesse attacks and high defenses. A good Wisdom lets you perceive dangers and act quickly, and Constitution improves your hardiness.
