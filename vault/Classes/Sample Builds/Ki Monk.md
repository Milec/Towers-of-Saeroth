# Ki Monk  *Class Sample Build*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 162

_Centering yourself, you call forth the internal magical energy that dwells within all living things. This ki can empower your body or flash out as pure energy._

## Attributes

Prioritize Strength. Wisdom empowers your ki spells, Perception, and Will saves. Constitution and Dexterity help your physical defenses.
