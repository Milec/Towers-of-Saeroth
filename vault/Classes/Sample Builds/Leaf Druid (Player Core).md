# Leaf Druid  *Class Sample Build*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 134

_You’re a nurturing caretaker in tune with the natural world and the magic of life. A little plant creature called a leshy accompanies you._

## Attributes

Prioritize Wisdom. Take Dexterity and Constitution for your defenses, and Charisma to be more diplomatic or Strength if you want to deal more damage in melee.
