# Blood-Soaked Soil  *Hazard 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 64

**Complexity** Simple

The blood of those who died in the area bubbles up from the earth, soaking the soil and turning it into a bloody morass.

---

**Stealth**
DC 16 (trained) to smell blood

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to settle the spirits or DC 19 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to bless the area

**Seep Blood** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the area; **Effect** Blood seeps up from the earth, turning the area into sucking, gory mud. The area becomes difficult terrain for 1 hour. All creatures in or moving through the area must succeed at a DC 16 Reflex save, or they become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until they can [[Actions/Escape]] (DC 16).

---
**Reset**
1 day
