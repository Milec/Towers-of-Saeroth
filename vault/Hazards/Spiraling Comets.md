# Spiraling Comets  *Hazard 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 24

**Complexity** Complex

Three blue comets orbiting the room turn red and attack when intruders enter.

---

**Stealth**
+32 (master)

**Disable**
DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (master) to command each comet to disperse with [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasmin]] prayers, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (8th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 42) to counteract each comet

**AC** 36, **Fort** +25, **Ref** +30

**Comet Hardness**
25

**Comet HP**
100 (BT 50)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Intruder Alert** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]]) **Trigger** An intruder enters the room; **Effect** The comets change from blue to red in color. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 action) The trap loses 1 action each turn for each comet disabled, counteracted, or destroyed. Each comet uses 1 action to attack the nearest intruder with a comet Strike.  
 **Ranged** single action comet +32; **Damage** 2d12+18 bludgeoning plus 1d12 fire

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 hour.
