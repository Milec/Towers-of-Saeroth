# Fungus-Covered Sinkhole  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 110

**Complexity** Simple

A sheet of fungus covers a sinkhole that drops 30 down into area **H7** below.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (or 0 if the fungus sheet covering the pit is destroyed)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 19 to quickly cut a few key strands of fibrous fungus to cause the whole sheet to hang free

**AC** 10, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +11

**Fugus Sheet HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
fire 10

**Pitfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks onto the sheet of fungus; **Effect** The triggering creature falls 30 feet into area **H7**, taking 15 falling damage and an additional 6d6 piercing damage (DC 24 basic Reflex save) from the many stalagmites they land on. That creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling (DC 24).
