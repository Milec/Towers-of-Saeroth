# Collapsing Bridge  *Hazard 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -184- The Ghouls Hunger|Pathfinder #184: The Ghouls Hunger]] pg. 63, [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 67

**Complexity** Simple

An iron bridge's connectors give way to drop into the lake below.

---

**Stealth**
DC 40 (master)

**Disable**
DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to disable one side of the bridge's quick release mechanisms

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A Small or larger creature moves more than 20 feet onto the bridge; **Effect** The bridge chains detach from their connectors, dropping itself and anything on it into the lake 150 feet below. A creature that's adjacent to the ledges to the north or south can attempt a DC 33 Reflex save to [[Actions/Grab an Edge|Grab the Edge]], but creatures who can't reach the edge fall with the bridge into the toxic water below (unless one side of the bridge remains attached, in which case a creature can attempt to Grab an Edge by clinging to the dangling bridge—requiring a successful DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to climb the hanging bridge to safety).
