# Corazal Aflame  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 35

**Complexity** Complex

Fire burns Corazal and threatens to spread to the surrounding forest.

---

**Stealth**
–10

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (expert) to douse flames or DC 32 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] (expert) to smother flames; on the sixth successful check, the flames go out. Casting a non-[[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|cantrip]] [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] spell on the flames counts as one success.

**Blaze** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature approaches Corazal; **Effect** The flames blaze higher, dealing 2d12+13 fire damage to the triggering creature (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) On its turn, the fire deals 2d8+13 fire damage to creatures in the grove, plus an additional 6 fire damage to Corazal and any creature within 15 feet of them (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
