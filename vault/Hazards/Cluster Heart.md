# Cluster Heart  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 45

**Complexity** Simple

A wave of magic passes through the area, causing crystal shards to grow and stab intruders.

---

**Stealth**
DC 45 (master) to hear or feel the energy coalescing

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (master) to chip away at the crystal and disrupt its resonance, or DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to carefully detach the crystals and then safely smash them

**AC** 39, **Fort** +33, **Ref** +27

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
300 (BT 150)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] 20

**Resistances**
[[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] deals double damage, ignoring Hardness, rather than destroying the cluster heart outright

**Crystal Surge** ⤾ ([[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** A non-elemental creature ends its turn adjacent to the cluster heart; **Effect** The heart extends shards of crystal imbued with force, like a jabbing blade, and deals 3d10+20 force damage and 3d10+20 piercing damage to all creatures in a 20-foot radius (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature who takes piercing damage from the surge is exposed to crystal corruption. On a critical failure, a creature also takes 2d6 persistent bleed damage.  
 **Crystal Corruption** ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Curse|curse]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) Creatures afflicted by this curse slowly turn to solid crystal. This affliction's [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] conditions can't be removed until the affliction itself is removed. Creatures with both the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] and [[Traits/Elemental|elemental]] traits instead become carriers and suffer no ill effects aside from gaining the weakness to [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]. **Saving Throw** DC 40 Fortitude; **Stage 1** sickened 1 (1 hour); Stage 2 weakness 5 to sonic and sickened 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** weakness 5 to sonic and slowed 1 (1 day); **Stage 4** weakness 10 to sonic and paralyzed (1 day); **Stage 5** weakness 10 to sonic, and the creature is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]].

---
**Reset**
Once the cluster heart uses its reaction, it automatically resets after 1 minute. As long as the cluster heart has 150 or more Hit Points, it recovers 50 per day.
