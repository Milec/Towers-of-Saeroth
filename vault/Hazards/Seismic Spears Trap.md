# Seismic Spears Trap  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 62

**Complexity** Simple

Lines of searing lava lance through the area, causing targets to shake as if caught in an earthquake and potentially become petrified.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 43 (master) to disable the wards or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (9th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 38) to counteract the ward.

**Cataclysmic Rain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the marked area; **Effect** Fiery spears make one Strike against each living creature within 5 feet of the marked area.

 **Ranged** single action seismic spear +40, **Damage** 3d10+10 fire damage and 3d10+10 piercing damage plus personal quake; no multiple attack penalty

 **Personal Quake** A creature struck by a seismic spear is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 3]] for 1 round as their body shakes uncontrollably. On a critical hit, a target must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]] for 1 minute, or permanently on a critical failure.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 minute.
