# The Cleansing Hall  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 9

**Complexity** Complex

The hall's entry and exit seal with a force barrier, then waves of force surge through the hall.

---

**Stealth**
+29 (master)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to deface the script without triggering, DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (master) to redirect the magical energy, or DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (master) to quote [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasmin]] prayers

**Force Barrier** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** A creature reaches the hall's midpoint; **Effect** Force barriers seal the hall's entrance and exit for 1 minute. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) A wave of force flows down the hall from north to south, dealing 3d10+20 force damage to each creature in the corridor. Each creature must attempt a DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. On a failure, a creature is pushed back 5 feet (10 feet on a critical failure). If this movement would cause a creature to hit another creature, both creatures take 1d6 bludgeoning damage. If this movement would cause a creature to hit a force barrier, the creature takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage. [[Traits/Animal|Animals]], [[Traits/Plant|plants]], and worshippers of Pharasma are immune to this force damage.

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets after 1 minute.
