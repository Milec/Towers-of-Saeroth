# Furious Flame  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 27

**Complexity** Simple

Religious sigils of Scal carefully carved into the doorframe capture Tokainen’s suppressed anger and frustration, unleashing it in a destructive explosion when a creature attempts to open the door.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to scratch out the religious iconography, [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 24 (trained) to pray for safe passage, [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] DC 28 (expert) to soothe the emotions powering the magical ward, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 24) to counteract the magical energy

**Furious Explosion** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]); **Trigger** A creature touches the door directly or with a tool; **Bypass** key; **Effect** The trap explodes in a 15-foot radius [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], dealing 5d10 fire damage to all creatures in the area (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures damaged by the trap must additionally succeed at a DC 26 Will save (against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect), becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 minute on a failure.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 minute.
