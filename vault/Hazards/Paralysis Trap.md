# Paralysis Trap  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 61

**Complexity** Simple

Magical symbols carved in the doorframe magically ward this door; anyone attempting to pick the lock is paralyzed and wracked with pain. Disabling or breaking the trap does not disable or break the lock.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (trained)

**Disable**
**Thievery** DC 28 (expert) to scratch out the magical symbols, **Arcana** DC 28 (expert) to safely siphon the magical energy, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 26); **Bypass** Anyone other than Mirida turns the door handle three times to the right and once to the left in quick succession.

**Debilitating Pain** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature tries to unlock or [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick the Lock]]; **Effect** The trap deals the triggering creature 2d10+11 nonlethal mental damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save) and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent nonlethal mental damage]]. A creature that fails their save is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for as long as they suffer the persistent mental damage.

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically resets. It doesn’t reset if the trap is disabled.
