# Corrupted Draft  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Enmity Cycle]] pg. 38

**Complexity** Complex

Discordant music, dance, and poetry combine to build a deep suspicion and distrust of strangers.

---

**Stealth**
+16 (expert) or [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]]

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Performance]] DC 28 to counteract the performance or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] or [[Traits/Occult]] DC 28 to disrupt the spiritual energy. Two total successes are needed to disable the trap. The mechanical head can automatically disable the trap with two uses of Inspired Recitation.

**Sickening Performance** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** Two or more characters enter area **E3** and make any sound at all; **Effect** Sounds or speech are amplified and garbled into a terrible, shrieking cry. All creatures in the area take 2d10+11 mental damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) The echoes continue to reverberate, tearing at those in the area and filling them with distrust of others. All creatures in the area take 2d10+11 mental damage and must attempt a DC 28 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** The target is unaffected and is immune to the effects for 1 minute.  
 **Success** The target takes half damage.  
 **Failure** The target takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] by feelings of distrust and anxiety.  
 **Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is sickened 2.

---
**Reset**
The hazard stops when there are no conscious creatures in area **E3** and resets after 1 minute.
