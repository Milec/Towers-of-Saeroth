# Valmar's Pit Trap  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 115

**Complexity** Simple

The ground here is made of loosely packed soil that, when stepped upon, collapses.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to reinforce the dirt so it’s safe to walk on, or DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to collapse the trap from a safe distance

**AC** 10, **Fort** +2, **Ref** +3

**Hardness**
1

**HP**
6 (BT 3)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps onto any space in the dotted area on the map, or at least 30 pounds of pressure is put on the area; **Effect** The ground gives way. The triggering creature falls 20 feet down the hole, taking 10 bludgeoning damage and landing [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] in area **E10**. A creature can [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] to avoid falling.

---
**Reset**
Creatures can still fall into the pit, but the soil must be replaced for the trap to become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] again.
