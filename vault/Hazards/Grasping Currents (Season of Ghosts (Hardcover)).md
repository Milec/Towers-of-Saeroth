# Grasping Currents  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 36

**Complexity** Complex

A swath of drowned spirits tries to pull someone down into the water to join them.

---

**Stealth**
+20 (trained) to notice a weird, faintly glowing ripple of the current beneath the water

**Disable**
DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to calm the unquiet spirits in the waters for an hour, or light the _Eternal Lantern_ (automatically disables the haunt)

**Ghostly Grasp** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to cross the lake by swimming or piloting a boat; **Effect** Ghostly arms reach up to grab and clutch at the creature or the boat, causing a –2 penalty to the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] or Piloting check that triggered the haunt. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the grasping currents attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] a swimming creature or capsize a rowboat by attempting an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check with a +12 modifier. A creature that’s restrained by the grasping currents is pulled underwater and risks drowning. To capsize a rowboat, the haunt’s Athletics check is rolled against the pilot’s Athletics or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] DC, whichever is higher

---
**Reset**
The grasping currents quickly build up power and can trigger again after 10 minutes have passed.
