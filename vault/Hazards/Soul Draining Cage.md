# Soul Draining Cage  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 38

**Complexity** Simple

Necromantic runes inscribed on the cage bars and lock drain the vitality of living creatures who interact with the cage.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert) or [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]]

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 25 (expert) to drain the runes power harmlessly or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (3rd level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 22) to counteract the rune

**Soul Drain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the cage bars, door, or lock directly or with a tool; **Effect** The trap deals 4d8+18 negative damage to the triggering creature, creatures inside the cage, and all creatures adjacent to the cage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] 1.
