# Acid Strongbox Trap  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Simple

An invisible magic field around the strongbox releases a spray of acid when disturbed.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to harmlessly bleed away the magical field or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 20) to dispel it.

**Acidic Spray** ⤾ ([[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the strongbox directly or with a held tool; **Effect** The trap deals 4d8+10 acid damage in a 15-foot cone in the direction of the triggering creature (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
