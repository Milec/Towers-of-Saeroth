# False Door Trap  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -179- Cradle of Quartz|Pathfinder #179: Cradle of Quartz]] pg. 59

**Complexity** Simple

A black-powder charge detonates as soon as the doorknob is turned, shattering the door into splinters.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert) to see the brick wall behind the edges of the door

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to disable the doorknob trigger

**AC** 24, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +11

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
50  (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Explosive Charge** ⤾ **Trigger** The doorknob is turned; **Effect** The charge behind the door explodes, dealing 2d8+9 fire damage and 2d8+9 piercing damage to all creatures in a 10-foot cone blasting outward from the door (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The sound of the explosion can be heard up to 50 feet away
