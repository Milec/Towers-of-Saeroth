# Spike Thrower  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 75

**Complexity** Simple

Slats in the ceiling open and pepper the chamber below with long, metal spikes fired from spring-loaded tubes.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to jam the gears and prevent the trap from being activated

**Throw Spikes** two actions ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]) **Trigger** This trap must be manually activated by a creature adjacent to the southwest gear stack; **Effect** The spike thrower makes a ranged attack against each creature in the central part of area F2.  

**Ranged** spike +14, **Damage** 4d6+3 piercing
