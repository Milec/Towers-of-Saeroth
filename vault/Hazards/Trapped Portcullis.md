# Trapped Portcullis  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 387

**Complexity** Simple

Two portcullises drop down to block progress through this area.

---

**Stealth**
D 36 (master)

**Disable**
DC 33 (master) to disable the trigger that causes the portcullises to drop

**AC** 30, **Fort** +24, **Ref** +18

**Portcullis Hardness**
18

**Portcullis HP**
72 (BT 36)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Drop from Ceiling** ⤾ **Trigger** A character enters a square between the two portcullises; **Effect** Both portcullises drop from the ceiling; attempting to lift one requires a DC 30 Athletics check to [[Actions/Force Open]]. When the portcullises drop, creatures in adjacent squares must attempt a DC 30 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unharmed and can take a [[Actions/Step]] as a reaction to move to an adjacent square.  

**Success** As critical success, but the creature takes a glancing blow from the falling portcullis for 2d12+15 bludgeoning damage.  

**Failure** The creature is struck by the falling portcullis for 4d12+30 bludgeoning damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] in their square.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is struck by the falling portcullis for 6d12+45 bludgeoning damage, is knocked prone, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] by the portcullis as it pins them to the ground until they can [[Actions/Escape]] or [[Actions/Force Open]] the portcullis (DC 30).

---
**Reset**
The trap can be manually reset with a 10-minute Manipulate activity performed in the hallways to the east or west of this area, as long as neither portcullis is broken or destroyed.
