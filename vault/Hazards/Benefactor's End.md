# Benefactor's End  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Simple

A spectral assailant drives a stake into a dying vampire's heart; the vampire collapses and releases a surge of negative energy.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to drain away the haunt's negative energy

**Death Throes** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature touches the vampire's corpse; **Effect** A surge of negative energy fills the room as the corpse lurches as though staked. The haunt deals 10d8 negative damage to creatures within 30 feet of the corpse; this damage heals [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures for the same amount. Living creatures must attempt a DC 30 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. This drained condition is reduced by 1 after 10 minutes.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes drained 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is drained 2.

---
**Reset**
Unless disabled, the haunt materializes again on the next moonrise.
