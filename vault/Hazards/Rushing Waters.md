# Rushing Waters  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rusthenge]] pg. 49

**Complexity** Simple

Rivulets of water begin to run from the circle of stones to gather in a growing puddle at the center.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to rub out the hidden runes on the ceiling, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 18) to counteract the trap

**Spherical Deluge** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Water|water]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the area south of the ring of stones marked on the map; **Effect** A deluge of water rises up from the ring of stones, then flows forcefully down the passageway to drain into the pit at area **F1b**. Large and smaller creatures in the water's path must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save. The rushing waters vanish instantly upon striking the pit floor, but do leave everything down there sopping wet.

**Critical Success** The creature withstands the force of the rushing waters and is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 1d10 bludgeoning damage as the rushing waters slam them into a cave wall.  

**Failure** The creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] by the rushing waters, takes 1d10+6 bludgeoning damage, and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] by the water to the spiky pit trap and triggers the trap.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature can't attempt to [[Actions/Grab an Edge]] if they fall into the [[Hazards/Spiky Pit Trap|spiky pit trap]].

---
**Reset**
The magic rejuvenates over 10 minutes, after which it is recharged and ready to activate again.
