# Shroud of Silence  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Simple

Sounds become muted in a 20-foot-wide, 100-foot-long segment of this street corridor.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) to twist the magic of the ley line around you, disabling the hazard for only you; or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 23) to disable the hazard permanently

**Hush** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature reaches the street's halfway point; **Effect** Each creature in the affected area must succeed at a DC 23 Will saving throw or be silenced as long as it remains in the area. A silenced creature makes no sound and can't use abilities with the [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] or [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] traits, including spells with verbal components.
