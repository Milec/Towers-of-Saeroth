# Vorpal Executioner  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 525

**Complexity** Simple

A wickedly sharp saw blade descends and travels along grooves in a complex path throughout the room, attempting to decapitate everyone within.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 41 (expert) at four different junctions to jam all the saw blade’s possible paths, preventing it from traveling through the room

**AC** 43, **Fort** +32, **Ref** +32

**Hardness**
30

**HP**
120 (60) per junction

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Total Decapitation** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to exit the room. **Effect** The saw blade travels along its path, making one Strike against each creature in the room, twisting and varying its height for a maximum chance of beheading its targets.  
 **Melee** saw blade +40 ([[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]], [[Traits/Death|death]]), **Damage** 6d12+25 slashing plus decapitation; no multiple attack penalty  
 **Decapitation** On a critical hit, a target must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or be decapitated, dying instantly unless it can survive without a head.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets over the course of the round and can be triggered again 1 round later.
