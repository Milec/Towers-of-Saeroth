# Mindhammer Mushrooms  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 48

**Complexity** Simple

The mushrooms emit powerful psychic blasts that overwhelm potential predators with exhausting psychedelic visions.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained) to notice small animal skeletons among the mushrooms

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to navigate a safe path without triggering the mushrooms

**AC** 16, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +8

**HP**
20

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] 10

**Psychic Blast** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks through the mushrooms; **Effect** The mushrooms release a loud hum of psychic energy. The triggering creature takes 2d8+8 mental damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a critical failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]].

---
**Reset**
The mushrooms must rest for 24 hours before they can emit another Psychic Blast
