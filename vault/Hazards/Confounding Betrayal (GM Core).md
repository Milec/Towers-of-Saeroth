# Confounding Betrayal  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 105

**Complexity** Complex

Allies appear to shed their disguises and reveal themselves to be malevolent monsters.

---

**Stealth**
+21 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] (expert) twice to confound the haunt with your own deceptions, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained) twice to create a ward against the haunt’s mental influence

**Unmask** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** Two or more creatures enter the haunt’s area; **Effect** Each creature sees the forms of nearby creatures shift and change, appearing to transform into fiendish or aberrant beings with a thirst for blood. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 30 Will save. The haunt then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature sees through the illusions entirely and is temporarily immune to the haunt’s routine for 1 minute.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected by the strange images.  

**Failure** The creature believes the illusions to be true; if they become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] by the haunt’s routine, they can’t attempt flat checks to end the confused condition when they take damage.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is left with a lingering suspicion of others and can’t benefit from Aid reactions for 24 hours.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The haunt continues to confound victims’ senses and inspire them to commit violence against each other. Each creature in the haunt’s area must attempt a DC 26 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune for 1 minute.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is confused for 1 minute.

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates 1 minute after all creatures leave the area but resets immediately thereafter
