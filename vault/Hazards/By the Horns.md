# By the Horns  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 55

**Complexity** Complex

The head of the Granite Ox tosses back and forth as the creature charges toward Dhuraxilis.

---

**Stealth**
+22 (master)

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] (master) to tug one of the ox’s ears, DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (expert) to distract the Ox with a display of magic, or DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] (expert) to dangle in front of the Ox’s eyes and obscure its vision; three successes are required to disable the hazard

**Toss** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature reaches the Granite Ox’s head or attempts a skill check to disable the hazard; **Effect** The Granite Ox tosses its head. Creatures in the area 2d12+13 bludgeoning damage must attempt a DC 29 Fortitude saving throw. The hazard rolls initiative.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes half damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is knocked prone, and takes 1d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] as it scrapes across the Ox’s scales.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The Granite Ox bucks. Each creature on the Ox’s head must attempt a DC 33 Reflex save. [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|Prone]] creatures gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the save. On a failure, the creature falls prone. On a critical failure, the creature flies from the Ox’s back, falling 120 feet to the ground or into Kardaji Bay.

---
**Reset**
The hazard ends automatically after 5 rounds or once all PCs are thrown from the Ox’s back, whichever comes first.
