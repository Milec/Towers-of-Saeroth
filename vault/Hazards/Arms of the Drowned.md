# Arms of the Drowned  *Hazard 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 35

**Complexity** Complex

Ghostly arms rise up from the water to clutch and grab, while a geyser of leech-infested water sprays up into the central shrine.

---

**Stealth**
+20 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to offer prayers to calm the haunt, or DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to threaten the unquiet spirits away.

**AC** 19, **Fort** +6, **Ref** +12

**HP**
46 (BT 23)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
positive 5

**Rising Waters** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature swims more than halfway across the river, ends their turn in the bridge shrine, or sets foot onto the northern bridge; **Effect** Ghostly arms rise up from the river to grab and shake both bridges. Creatures standing on the bridge must attempt a DC 20 Reflex save or be knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. A blast of river water sprays up inside the shrine to deposit two [[Bestiary/Brood Leech Swarm|brood leech swarms]] in the shrine. The haunt and the leech swarms then roll initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) Ghostly arms flail about and attempt to grab creatures in the shrine or on either bridge. Each round, the haunt uses 3 actions to attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]] a PC on the bridge or in the shrine. If a PC is restrained, the haunt can use one of its actions to drag a PC into the river. A PC who's restrained in the river risks [[Rules/Your Spellcasting Style|drowning]].  

**Melee** single action**Melee**  ghostly arms +12, Effect grapple

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates and resets at the start of any round in which the PCs aren't in the river, on the bridges, or in the shrines, but the leech swarms remain in the shrine for 10 minutes before retreating back into the water below.
