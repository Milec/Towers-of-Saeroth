# Cuastic Shower  *Hazard 21*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 63

**Complexity** Simple

Contact with the doors, pillars, or statue causes a shower of acid.

---

**Stealth**
DC 53 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (master) to ground the charged features or DC 50 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to disable the contact sensors

**Acid Shower** ⤾ ([[Traits/Acid|acid]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the doors, pillars, or statue directly or with a tool; **Effect** A shower of acid rains down from the ceiling, dealing 8d10+48 acid damage to all creatures in the chamber (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature additionally takes 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]].
