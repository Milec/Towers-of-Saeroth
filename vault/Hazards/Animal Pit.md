# Animal Pit  *Hazard 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 23

**Complexity** Simple

A weight-activated trap door covers a pit that’s 10 feet square and 20 feet deep.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (DC 14 if the trap has been activated within the last day, or 0 if the trap door is disabled or [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]])

**Disable**
DC 12 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to remove the trap door

**AC** 10, **Fort** +1, **Ref** +1

**Trapdoor Hardness**
3

**Trapdoor HP**
12 (BT 6)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Pitfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature weighing at least 20 pounds walks onto the trap door; **Effect** The triggering creature falls in and takes falling damage (typically 10 bludgeoning damage). That creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling.

---
**Reset**
The trapdoors automatically reset to their closed position once a creature falls in, allowing for more creatures to fall in, though any camouflage on the trap door falls in as well, making it easier for creatures to notice the trap door.
