# Flesh for the Hollow Star  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 94

**Complexity** Complex

The flesh and leather sculpture of Azathoth seems to ripple and churn before eyes open across its surface—eyes that then open into mouths that extend long whipping tendrils of rasping filaments to bless with warped flesh.

---

**Stealth**
+23 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 twice to pull away one of the two primary anchor points for the sculpture to cause the leather and bones to slump harmlessly to the floor, [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 26 from a worshipper of a lawful or good faith to banish Azathoth's influence from the hazard, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 20) to counteract the hazard.

**AC** 22, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +9

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Open Eyes** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** a non-worshipper of Azathoth approaches within 5 feet, or a non-worshipper of Azathoth attacks Ziradini in this room; **Effect** A dozen eyes blink open on the spherical shape to glare at the triggering creature, who must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions; [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The eyes open like mouths to extrude a whipping tangle of filaments that lash out at a target in area **F19** or within 10 feet of any entrance into the room. The filaments can't attack a single target more than once per round. A creature targeted by a filament must attempt a DC 22 Fortitude save. The body of a creature slain by poison damage from this hazard melts into a heap and then splits open to allow a gibbering mouther to slither free; this creature attacks any non-worshipper of Azathoth it sees, or otherwise slithers off to area **F20** to splash and gyre in the waters of the lake.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d6 poison damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 2d6+7 poison damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 as their flesh and muscles grow limp and saggy.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but 4d6+14 poison damage
and enfeebled 2.

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets after a worshipper of Azathoth spends 1 hour in prayer before it.
