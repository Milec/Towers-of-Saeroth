# Malevolent Mannequins  *Hazard 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]], [[Traits/Mechanical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 81

**Complexity** Complex

A host of violent spirits possess three clockwork mannequins, and each lashes out at any creatures that dare walk near.

---

**Stealth**
+27

**Disable**
DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to jam a mannequin’s clockwork mechanisms or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 35 (expert) twice to exorcise a spirit from a mannequin.

**AC** 35, **Fort** +26, **Ref** +28

**Hardness**
10

**HP**
70 (BT 35)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Awaken** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature comes within 30 feet of a mannequin; **Effect** The mannequins all lurch towards the triggering creature, [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Striding]] up to 50 feet, then roll initiative.

---
**Routine**
(9 actions) Each of the mannequins uses three of the haunt’s actions on each turn, and the haunt loses three actions each turn for every disabled or destroyed mannequin. Each mannequin uses its first action to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to 50 feet and its remaining two actions to make clockwork fist Strikes. Each has its own separate multiple attack penalty.  
**Melee** single action clockwork fist +29, **Damage** 2d10+18 bludgeoning

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates after it has no target creatures and resets, usually over the course of 1 round, as the mannequins move to their original positions. If a mannequin is more than 150 feet from its original position, the haunt takes longer to reset, as the mannequin moves back 150 feet each round.
