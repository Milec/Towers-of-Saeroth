# Carnivorous Mold  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 68

**Complexity** Simple

Spores explode onto nearby creatures, leaving welts as the mold dissolves exposed flesh.

---

**Stealth**
DC 36 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (legendary) to neutralize the void energy that animates the mold, or DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (master) to remove the mold without triggering the spores

**AC** 40, **Fort** +37, **Ref** +29

**HP**
130 (BT 65)

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] 20

**Spore Explosion** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature moves adjacent to the mold or damages the mold; **Effect** All creatures within 15 feet of the mold are exposed to the flesh-eating spores.  
**Flesh-Eating Spores** ([[Traits/Contact (GM Core)|contact]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) The flesh-eating spores leave red rings on the skin as they dissolve the target’s flesh; **Saving Throw** DC 44 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d10 void damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 6d10 void damage and drained 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 8d10 void damage and drained 3 (1 round)
