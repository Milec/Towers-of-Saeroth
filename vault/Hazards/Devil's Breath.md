# Devil's Breath  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 52

**Complexity** Simple

The taxidermy monster lurches forward, howls, and exhales a blast of foul-smelling smoke.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 to pluck out the taxidermy's gemstone eyes

**AC** 22, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +9

**Hardness**
10

**HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Exhale Smoke** ⤾ **Trigger** A non-goblin creature approaches within 10 feet of the taxidermy Sandpoint Devil; **Effect** The taxidermy Sandpoint Devil lurches forward, opens its jaws, and emits a blood-curdling howl and a blast of foul-smelling gray smoke. All creatures in a 30-foot-cone directed south of the trap (which reaches all the way to the door leading out to the Pit) must attempt a DC 22 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 hour (or enfeebled 2 for 8 hours on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The Devil’s Breath resets automatically after an hour.
