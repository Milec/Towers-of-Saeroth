# Mental Scream Trap  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 56

**Complexity** Simple

A psychic scream disorients creatures in the area.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (DC 41) to suppress the magic or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (DC 41) to scratch out the ward

**Psychic Screech** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the marked area; **Effect** A psychic wail affects all creatures within 10 feet of the marked area, requiring them to attempt a DC 41 Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 2]].  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round, then stunned 2 when the paralysis ends.  

**Critical Failure** The target is paralyzed for 4 rounds. At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt a new Will save to reduce the remaining duration by 1 round, or end it entirely on a critical success.
