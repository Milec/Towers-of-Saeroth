# Frenetic Musician  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 66

**Complexity** Simple

A spirit musician rises out of an instrument and plays a frantic, bone-shaking melody.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert) to hear phantom notes

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] (trained) to bring the spirit's music to a close or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) to banish the spirit

**Musical Assault** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the instrument; **Effect** The musician's bone-shaking musical performance deals 4d10+22 sonic damage to creatures within 30 feet (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save).

---
**Reset**
1 hour
