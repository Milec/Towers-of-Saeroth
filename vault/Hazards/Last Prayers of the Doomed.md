# Last Prayers of the Doomed  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 15

**Complexity** Simple

Influenced by the last prayers of the dead, creatures experience extreme claustrophobia and batter at the walls and floor, desperate to escape, see the sun, and breathe fresh air—but their prayers go unanswered.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert) to hear desperate whispered prayers

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 26 (expert) to disrupt the haunt through prayer, or [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] DC 27 (expert) to convince the spirits you’ll free them from this place

**Let Me Out!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the altar, corpse, or statue; **Effect** The triggering creature batters their fists and body into the floor and walls, dealing 4d10+18 bludgeoning damage to themself (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). All creatures who witness this become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 unless they succeed at a DC 25 Will save.

---
**Reset**
The haunt reforms after 1 day.
