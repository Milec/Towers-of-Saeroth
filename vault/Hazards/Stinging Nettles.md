# Stinging Nettles  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 77

**Complexity** Simple

This bright green plant has long triangular leaves covered with fine hair-like stingers.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] to carefully navigate the plant and pull aside or cut away the nettles

**AC** 13, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +4

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Sting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the stinging nettles or moves through a square containing stinging nettles; **Effect** The triggering creature must attempt a DC 17 Fortitude save if they're walking through the square or a DC 20 Fortitude save if they touched the nettles.

**Critical Success** The creature avoids being stung by the plant's nettles.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] by the pain caused by brief contact with the nettles.  

**Failure** The creature is sickened 1 by the pain caused by contact with the nettles and is also [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 8 hours from the lingering pain.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2 by the pain caused by contact with the nettles and is also clumsy 1 for 24 hours from the lingering pain.
