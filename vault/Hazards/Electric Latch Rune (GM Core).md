# Electric Latch Rune  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 101

**Complexity** Simple

An invisible rune imprinted on a door latch releases a powerful electric discharge.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to scratch out the rune without allowing electricity to flow, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (2nd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 18) to counteract the rune

**Electrocution** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) **Trigger** A creature grasps the door latch directly or with a tool; **Effect** The trap deals 3d12 electricity damage to the triggering creature (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
