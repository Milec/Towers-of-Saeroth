# Poisonous Atmosphere  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 65

**Complexity** Complex

Aucturn’s poisonous, magical atmosphere has strange effects on the planet’s creatures.

---

**Stealth**
+5 (expert) to identify the source of sudden nausea

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained) to breathe pockets of safe air trapped in bags and clothing

**Poisonous Atmosphere** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A non-native creature breathes the atmosphere; **Effect** The triggering creature must attempt a DC 17 Fortitude save. The hazard then rolls initiative.  
**Critical Success** The creature is invigorated by the atmosphere and is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round.  
**Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The atmosphere continues to affect all creatures in the area, requiring a new Fortitude save each round against its Poisonous Atmosphere (above). A creature holding its breath gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the save.
