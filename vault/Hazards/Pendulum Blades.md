# Pendulum Blades  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 75

**Complexity** Simple

Several swinging pendulums mounted with razor-sharp blades extend from the ceiling and slice into the room below.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to jam the gears and prevent the trap from being activated

**Swinging Blades** two actions ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]) **Trigger** This trap must be manually activated by a creature adjacent to the southwest gear stack; **Effect** Blades swing down to strike at all creatures in area F2 before wrenching to a frozen halt against the opposite walls. All creatures in the central part of this room take 6d6+3 slashing damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
