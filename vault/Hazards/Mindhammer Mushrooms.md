# Mindhammer Mushrooms  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 46

**Complexity** Simple

The mushrooms emit powerful psychic blasts that overwhelm potential predators with exhausting psychedelic visions.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained) to notice small animal skeletons among the mushrooms

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) to navigate a safe path without triggering the mushrooms

**AC** 16, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +8

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
fire 10

**Psychic Blast** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks through the mushrooms; **Effect** The mushrooms release a loud hum of psychic energy. The triggering creature takes 2d8+8 mental damage (DC 23 basic Will save). On a critical failure, the creature is fatigued.

---
**Reset**
The mushrooms must rest for 24 hours before they can emit another Psychic Blast.
