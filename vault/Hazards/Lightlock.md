# Lightlock  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 87

**Complexity** Simple

The strange fog layer on the walls swirls and churns, then suddenly fires several beams of dark mist into the room.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 27 (master) to disrupt some of the hidden runes that focus the trap's energies, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th level, counteract DC 25) or any spell with the [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]] trait to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the trap

**Freeze the Awful Light** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]]) **Trigger** A creature carrying a light source ends their turn in area **F8**; **Effect** The walls fire beams of chilling dark fog at the triggering creature, who takes 10d6 cold damage (DC 25 basic Fortitude save). The beams also attempt to counteract any light source the triggering creature carries. Non-magical light sources of 7th level or less are automatically extinguished. Against other light sources, the lightlock casts a 4th-level _dispel magic_	 spell with a counteract modifier of +14 to attempt to counteract the light.
