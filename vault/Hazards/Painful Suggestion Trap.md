# Painful Suggestion Trap  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 54

**Complexity** Simple

A hidden rune on the floor under the filth, just past the threshold, triggers an ersatz ghost.

---

**Stealth**
DC 26 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to remove the rune without triggering it, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 26) to dispel the rune

**Counterfeit Haunting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A living creature moves over the rune; **Effect** A frightful moan echoes through the room, and a hoarse telepathic voice shouts, “Get out!” This shout deals 6d12 mental damage to creatures in the room (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). Creatures who fail this saving throw must immediately leave the room and can’t willingly reenter it for 1 minute (1 hour on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically resets after 1 hour.
