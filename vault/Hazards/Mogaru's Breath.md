# Mogaru's Breath  *Hazard 21*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Kaiju]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 89

**Complexity** Simple

Mogaru exhales a blast of intense flames to eradicate any meddlesome pests in the area.

---

**Stealth**
DC 10

**Disable**
DC 50 [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] (legendary) or DC 53 [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (legendary) to momentarily divert Mogaru's attention

**Volcanic** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature takes a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against Mogaru, produces a visible area effect, or flies within 120 feet of Mogaru; **Effect** Mogaru breathes a 120-foot cone of fire toward the object of his ire. Flammable objects in the area immediately combust, and stone or metal surfaces warp into difficult terrain. Creatures in the area take 20d6 fire damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed or critically failed save, a creature also takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].

---
**Reset**
Mogaru must wait 1d4 rounds before letting loose another gout of fire.
