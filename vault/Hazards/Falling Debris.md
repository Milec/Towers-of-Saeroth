# Falling Debris  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 33

**Complexity** Simple

A net filled with stones and a small anvil crashes down on someone who steps into the room.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to disarm the trap through a cracked-open door, or three actions Interact to open the door slowly without triggering the trap

**Drop Debris** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature opens the door without taking care to avoid triggering the trap; **Effect** The net drops a dozen hefty stones and a small but quite hard anvil onto the creature, inflicting 3d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The sound of this trap going off alerts Gurtlekep in area C8.
