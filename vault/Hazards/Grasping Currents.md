# Grasping Currents  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -196- The Summer That Never Was|Pathfinder #196: The Summer That Never Was]] pg. 18

**Complexity** Complex

A swath of drowned spirits tries to pull someone down into the water to join them.

---

**Stealth**
+20 (trained) to notice a weird, faintly glowing ripple of the current beneath the water.

**Disable**
DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] to calm the unquiet spirits in the waters for an hour, or light the _Eternal Lantern_ (automatically disables the haunt)

**Ghostly Grasp** free action **Trigger** A creature attempts to cross the lake by swimming or piloting a boat; **Effect** Ghostly arms reach up to grab and clutch at the creature or the boat, causing a –2 penalty to the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] or [[Rules/Piloting Checks|Piloting]] check that triggered the haunt. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the grasping currents attempt to [[Actions/Grapple]] a swimming creature or capsize a rowboat by attempting an Athletics check with a +12 modifier. A creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by the grasping currents is pulled underwater and risks [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|drowning]]. To capsize a rowboat, the haunt's Athletics check is rolled against the pilot's Athletics or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Sailing Lore]] DC, whichever is higher.

---
**Reset**
The grasping currents quickly build up power and can trigger again after 10 minutes have passed.
