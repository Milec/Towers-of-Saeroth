# Unspeakable Impressions  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Nightmare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 79

**Complexity** Simple

A trail of over-sized footprints crosses the path, markings of the apparently recent passage of a dangerous and mind-shattering entity whose stride is enough to be mistaken for depressions in the natural terrain, and promising destruction to those compelled to follow the tracks.

---

**Stealth**
DC 46 (master)

**Disable**
DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] (master) to distract those (including yourself) compelled to follow footprints, or DC 43 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (legendary) to quickly obscure the trail

**Beckoning Trail** free action (mental); **Trigger** A creature crosses over the footprints’ trail; **Effect** The triggering creature suddenly realizes the nature of the immense trail they just stepped into and feels a compulsion to follow the trail to their destination; the triggering creature must attempt a DC 35 Will save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature Strides once down the trail before coming to their senses—this movement does not put the creature in any peril or danger.  

**Success** The creature takes its three actions to Stride down the trail. While this movement doesn’t put the creature in any direct peril, when they end the movement, they’re overwhelmed with the knowledge that had they gone further, something horrible would have happened; the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 hour by the close call.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 and also stupefied 1 for 24 hours.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but doomed 2 and stupefied 2. In addition, at the end of the three Strides, the creature vanishes from view, instantly teleporting to some dangerous area or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] prison. This might be a nearby location that the creature’s allies can discover and rescue them from, or it may be somewhere further away that will require more effort to reach before the victim can be rescued.

---
**Reset**
The unspeakable impressions fade once they are triggered, but reform somewhere else in the vicinity 24 hours later unless they are successfully disabled.
