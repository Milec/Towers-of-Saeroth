# Haunting Presence  *Hazard 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]], [[Traits/Simple]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Simple

Ghostly sounds or glimpses of apparitions move to startle or distract those who walk the streets.

---

**Stealth**
DC 19 to notice an unsettling sensation

**Disable**
DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt, or light the _Eternal Lantern_ (automatically disables these haunts)

**Ghostly Menace** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A haunt encounter occurs; **Effect** Kugaptee’s presence makes itself known in a frightening way—a sudden shriek of agony, an explosive burst of red butterflies, a momentarily overwhelming air of decay, or the like. A random PC takes 2d6+3 mental damage (DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save), and then the haunting presence vanishes, leaving behind nothing but mental scars and trauma.
