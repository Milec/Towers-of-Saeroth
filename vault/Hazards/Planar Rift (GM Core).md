# Planar Rift  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 103

**Complexity** Simple

A rift attempts to draw creatures into another plane (the GM chooses the specific plane).

---

**Stealth**
DC 35 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to assemble a rift seal using objects strongly grounded to your plane, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (7th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 31) to counteract the rift

**Into the Great Beyond** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) **Trigger** A creature moves within 10 feet of the rift; **Effect** The triggering creature and all creatures within 30 feet of the rift are drawn into another plane. Each creature can attempt a DC 33 Reflex save to avoid this fate.
