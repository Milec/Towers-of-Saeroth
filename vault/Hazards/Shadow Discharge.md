# Shadow Discharge  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 65

**Complexity** Complex

The _shadow beacon’s_ operation causes harmful energy to blast out of the base of the device.

---

**Stealth**
+ 18

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to rig a dispersal system, or DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (master) to safely vent the magical energy; four total successes in any combination to disable

**Priming** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]]) **Trigger** Nizca and Deg activate the shadow beacon; **Effect** The shadow beacon sparks to life. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The shadow beacon fires shadows into the sky, discharging excess energy out of the device’s lower half. The trap deals 4d6 cold and 4d6 void damage to all non-shadow creatures in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from the base of the device (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). For each successful check to disable the trap, the cold and void damage dealt by the trap on subsequent rounds are both reduced by 1d6 damage. A creature that fails their save is pushed back 10 feet (15 feet on a critical failure).  

**Special** Disabling the shadow discharge does not disable the shadow beacon.
