# Electrified Gate  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Complex

A warded metal gate electrocutes anyone who touches the gate without pressing the bypass button.

---

**Stealth**
DC 47 (master) or DC 50 (legendary) to notice the bypass button hidden in a skull's eye socket

**Disable**
DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to destroy the ward, [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (8th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 47) to counteract the ward, or press the bypass button

**Electrocution** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the gate directly or with a tool; **Effect** The trap deals 8d10+40 electricity damage to the triggering creature (DC 46 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically if triggered, after 1 minute if the bypass button is pressed, or after 15 minutes if disabled or counteracted.
