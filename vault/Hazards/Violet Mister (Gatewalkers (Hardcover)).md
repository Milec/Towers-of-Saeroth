# Violet Mister  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 27

**Complexity** Simple

Opening the door pulls a thread, which causes a plant mister to spray a cloud of poison into the triggering creature’s face.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained) to notice the trap’s trigger-thread attached to the door

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to safely cut the thread

**AC** 16, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +10

**Hardness**
7

**HP**
25

**Poisoned Welcome** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to open the door; **Effect** Every creature within 10 feet of the door is exposed to violet venom poison.  
**Violet Venom** ([[Traits/Contact (GM Core)|contact]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 17 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison plus [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d6 poison plus [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** 2d6 poison plus enfeebled 1 (1 round)
