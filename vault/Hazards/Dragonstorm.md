# Dragonstorm  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 7

**Complexity** Simple

A massive storm stirs in the skies above Breachill and Citadel Altaerein. As specific events begin during the three waves of the manifestation's retribution, the dragonstorm wreaks localized havoc.

---

**Stealth**
+40 (legendary)

**Disable**
The dragonstorm can't be disabled—only endured. It ends only once the attack on Breachill and Citadel Altaerein is over.

**Localized Dragonstorm** ⤾ ([[Traits/Air|air]]) **Trigger** A new wave begins; **Effect** Roll 1d10 to determine which effect manifests from the following possibilities. The placement of these effects is guided by [[Setting/Deities/Dahak|Dahak's]] will, and they should manifest in the most detrimental locations possible to the PCs.  

**1–2: Acid Rain** ([[Traits/Acid|acid]]) A torrent of acid drenches creatures in a 20-foot burst. Creatures in the area take 6d10 acid damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail also take 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]].  
**3–4: Freezing Wind** ([[Traits/Cold|cold]]) A barrage of freezing wind and hail assaults all creatures in a 20-foot burst. Creatures in the area take 3d10 cold damage and 3d10 bludgeoning damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  
**5–6: Bolts of Lightning** ([[Traits/Electricity|electricity]]) Several bolts of lightning strike all creatures in a 20-foot burst. Creatures in the area take 6d10 electricity damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail their saves become [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round.  
**7–8: Flaming Vortex** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) Gouts of flame fall on all creatures in a 20-foot burst. Creatures in the area take 6d10 fire damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail their saves also take 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].  
 **9–10: Poison Miasma** ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) Poisonous mist rolls forth, covering a 20-foot burst for 1 round. Creatures within the mist become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and creatures outside it become concealed to creatures within it. Creatures within the mist take 6d10 poison damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). Creatures that critically fail their saves become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]].
