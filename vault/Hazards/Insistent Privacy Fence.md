# Insistent Privacy Fence  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 81

**Complexity** Simple

A stretch of fence is electrified by a hidden Stasian coil.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 28 (expert) to carefully disconnect the coil from the fence or [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] DC 30 (master) to jury-rig a grounding device to nullify the shock

**AC** 25, **Fort** +20, **Ref** +14

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
60 (BT 30)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Shock** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature touches the fence directly or with a tool or weapon; **Effect** The fence deals 7d12 electricity damage to the triggering creature (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets after 1 minute.
