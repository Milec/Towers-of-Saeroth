# Crush the Uninvited  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 43

**Complexity** Simple

A warning carved into the door frame magically crushes any who pass through the doorway without Kemnebi's permission.

---

**Stealth**
DC 48 (legendary); 20 to notice the warning carved in the door frame

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to scratch out the warning or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (10th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 48) to counteract the magic in the warning

**Implosion** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]]) **Trigger** A creature passes through the doorway without Kemnebi's permission; **Effect** The trap casts [[Spells/Implosion|_implosion_]] on the triggering creature. This is a 10th-level _implosion_ spell that deals 85 damage (DC 47 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).
