# Cave-In  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 8, [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 6

**Complexity** Simple

The rubble shifts and the cavern ceiling collapses.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (expert) or DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) to prop up the cavern ceiling without triggering a collapse.

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature moves the rubble; **Effect** The cavern collapses in a cave-in that deals 4d8+22 bludgeoning damage to the triggering creature and all creatures in the cavern (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
