# Wrathful Dinosaur  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 59

**Complexity** Simple

This figurine of a charging triceratops was crafted from the dinosaur's femur and contains its spirit.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 24 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (expert) to banish the dinosaur's spirit with a primal incantation, DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] to scare it off with a frightening display, or DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (expert) to calm the spirit

**Triceratops Trample** ⤾ ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]]) **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 10 feet of the figurine; **Effect** A spectral triceratops appears around the figurine and charges toward the triggering creature, affecting a line 30 feet long. Creatures in this area take 4d10+22 bludgeoning damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
1 day
