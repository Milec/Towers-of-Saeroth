# Puncturing Spire  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 55

**Complexity** Simple

A metal spire bursts from the ground, skewering the target and knocking aside other creatures.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to seal the planar breach, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (master) to magically reinforce the ground and prevent a spire's emergence

**Pierce Realm** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature enters the area; **Effect** A 60-foot-tall metal spire rises from the ground, puncturing the triggering creature and dealing 4d10+26 piercing damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, the creature is additionally impaled, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 30). A creature that Escapes is 60 feet in the air. Additionally, all creatures within 10- feet of the spire take 2d10+13 bludgeoning damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failure, they're knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
