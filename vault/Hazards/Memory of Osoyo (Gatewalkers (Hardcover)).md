# Memory of Osoyo  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 100

**Complexity** Complex

The party’s memories of Osoyo coalesce into a titanic mirage in the storm clouds above.

---

**Stealth**
+12 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained) or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (trained) to divert the memory’s rage; three total successful checks are required to disable the hazard

**Glacial Gust** ⤾ **Trigger** The characters’ shared vision during **Event 15** subsides; **Effect** The memory of Osoyo unleashes a tremendous sigh of freezing wind, coating the deck of the Mermaid’s Tears in ice in a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. (The hazard can choose any point for the cone to originate.) The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Creatures in the area must succeed at a DC 26 Fortitude save or take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent cold damage]] as the freezing wind cuts to the bone. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The memory of Osoyo gives a keening scream that shatters the layer of ice coating the deck of the Mermaid’s Tears. Any creature on [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] created by the memory’s Glacial Gust takes 2d8+7 cold damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) from the icy shrapnel.
