# Kugaptee's Tree  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 135

**Complexity** Complex

The branches of the stone tree statue animate, extend, and lash out at intruders.

---

**Stealth**
+15 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to appear overwhelmingly strong of body and mind so that the haunt recoils and fades

**AC** 22, **Fort** +18, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
14

**HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] 10

**Pulse of Fear** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** An intruder begins their turn in area **E3**; Effect The stone statue animates, its branches whipping through the air as a horrifying roar rips through the monastery—a roar that seems to come from underground. All intruders within the monastery must attempt a DC 21 Will save. The haunt then rolls initiative.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  
**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for as long as it remains frightened.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) Kugaptee's tree lashes out with its branches, using its actions to make three branch Strikes. Targets that are within reach but are outside of area **E3** have [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from the surrounding archways. The haunt favors targets in area **E3** but otherwise selects its targets randomly.  
**Melee** branch +17 ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 40 feet]]), **Damage** 1d8+9 bludgeoning plus 1d8 mental

---
**Reset**
1 hour
