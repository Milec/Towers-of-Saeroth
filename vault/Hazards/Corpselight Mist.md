# Corpselight Mist  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 148

**Complexity** Simple

Churning vapors of glowing green mist fill a 30-foot-deep chasm.

---

**Stealth**
+0

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to counteract Kabriri's influence here by offering prayers to any non-evil deity and causing the mist to disperse, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd level; counteract DC 23) to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the mist for 24 hours

**Decay Flesh** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** A creature ends its turn within the mist; **Effect** The mist swiftly decays the flesh of all living creatures in the pit, inflicting 10d6 negative damage (DC 27 basic Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
The hazard resets automatically at the end of a turn and can trigger again the next round.
