# Nosois' Vengeance  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 201

**Complexity** Complex

The sparrow on the scroll animates, speaking to the PCs in Noguchi’s voice (one of the nosois from **Event 11: Nosois’ Brunch**): “They’re the ones! They’re the blasphemers who can’t even properly serve tea! Send them along to the Lady!”

---

**Stealth**
+23 (master)

**Disable**
DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (to quickly destroy the scroll without triggering the hazard), DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (to apologize to [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]] and the nosois for the previous insult), or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (6th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 29).

**Summon Vanths** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Summon|summon]]) **Trigger** One of the PCs enters the room and is noticed by the hazard; **Effect** The trap summons four [[Bestiary/Vanth (Monster Core)|vanths]], who then roll initiative and remain for up to 1 minute before the spell ends and the vanths disappear. The summoned vanths can use 3 actions each round and can use reactions, unlike most summoned creatures.
