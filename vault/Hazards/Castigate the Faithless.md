# Castigate the Faithless  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Complex

Magic forces those who pass by the statue to the ground with sudden force; pressing the bypass button deactivates the trap for 1 minute.

---

**Stealth**
DC 47 (master) or DC 50 (legendary) to notice the bypass button hidden in Urgathoa's skeletal feet

**Disable**
DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (legendary) to deactivate the trap through prayer, [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (8th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 47) to counteract the trap, or press the bypass button

**Kneel!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]], [[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** A creature passes by the statue; **Effect** The trap deals 7d12+40 force damage to the triggering creature (DC 46 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed save, the creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], and on a critical failure, the creature is knocked prone and takes an additional 1d12 bludgeoning damage from the force of the fall.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically if triggered, after 1 minute if the bypass button is pressed, or after 15 minutes if disabled or counteracted.
