# Fiendish Reflection  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 134

**Complexity** Simple

Reflections viewed in the water here appear monstrous and fiendish; a PC who views themself in these waters sees a distorted version of themself looking back at them, sneering and mocking.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt, or DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to turn the fear back on the haunt and overwhelm it with its own terror

**Frightening Visage** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature looks at the water; **Effect** The creature sees its reflection on the water’s surface distort into a fiendish version of itself; it must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 (and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for as long as it remains frightened on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The haunt resets at the end of the round automatically, ready to trigger again the next tome someone looks into the water.
