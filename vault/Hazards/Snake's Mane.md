# Snake's Mane  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 48

**Complexity** Simple

A tangle of feathery green vines appears innocuous but fires a spray of acidic barbs at anything that disturbs it.

---

**Stealth**
DC 27 (trained) to discern the snake’s mane from the surrounding vegetation

**Disable**
Disable DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (expert) to strip the barbs from the vines, rendering them harmless, or DC 30 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] (trained) to move through the snake’s mane without disturbing it

**AC** 22, **Fort** +12, **Ref** +0

**Hardness**
15

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
fire 10, slashing 10

**Barb Spray** ⤾  **Trigger** A creature enters an area containing snake’s mane or the snake’s mane takes damage; **Effect** The vine sprays a hail of acidic barbs in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Any creature in the area takes 4d10+18 acid damage. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 minute.

---
**Reset**
1 hour, as the vine grows a new set of barbs
