# Reflected Desires  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Trap]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 123

**Complexity** Simple

This mirror reflects the viewer's deepest desires.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (0 to notice the mirror)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to shroud the mirror without looking at it, or DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to erect mental barriers

**AC** 13, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +4

**Hardness**
6

**HP**
24 (BT 12)

**Immunities**
object immunities

**Tempt** ⤾ ([[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]]) **Trigger** A creature looks into the mirror; **Effect** The triggering creature sees themselves enjoying their deepest desires in the mirror's reflection, becoming fascinated (Will DC 20 negates). This fascination ends automatically when the mirror is broken or covered up.

---
**Reset**
automatic
