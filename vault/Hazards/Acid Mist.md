# Acid Mist  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 34

**Complexity** Complex

A golden guardrail sprays magical, flesh-eating acid throughout the stairwell.

---

**Stealth**
+10 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to deactivate the trap’s sensors, or [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] DC 24 (expert) to disrupt the guardrail’s magic, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 24) to counteract the guardrail

**AC** 21, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +11

**Guardrail Hardness**
13

**Guardrail HP**
56 (BT 28)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Spray Acid** ⤾ ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]]); **Trigger** A creature steps on the south stairs; **Effect** The guardrail sprays flesh-eating acid, filling the entire stairwell. All living creatures on the stairs area take 2d8+9 acid damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the trap continues to spray acid, dealing 2d8+9 acid damage to all living creatures on the stairs (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically deactivates after 1 minute. It resets in 1 hour.
