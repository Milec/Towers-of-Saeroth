# Manor Wards  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -198- No Breath to Cry|Pathfinder #198: No Breath to Cry]] pg. 18

**Complexity** Simple

Layers of fulus and strips of prayer-printed ribbons are plastered over the walls and entrances to the manor, demarcating a boundary around the mansion where no ghosts or spirits can pass.

---

**Stealth**
5

**Disable**
DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to compromise the magic to allow entry into the manor, DC 32 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to force your way through the barrier and enter the manor (this doesn't allow other PCs to enter behind you, though!), or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (5th level; counteract DC 28) to counteract the wards

**Denied** free action **Trigger** A creature without a nullifying seal script on their hand approaches within 5 feet of the manor or attempts to damage the manor; **Effect** An explosive blast of magic violently slams against the intruder, who must attempt a DC 33 Reflex save. If the triggering creature isn't adjacent to the manor, it adjusts the result of this save up by one degree of success.

**Critical Success** The creature takes 1d12 force damage and is pushed 5 feet away from the manor.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d12+13 force damage and is pushed 5 feet away from the manor.  

**Failure** The creature takes 4d12+16 force damage and is thrown 20 feet back and knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], taking 10 bludgeoning damage from the fall.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, 100 feet back and knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], taking 50 bludgeoning damage from the fall.

---
**Reset**
The wards reset automatically, continuing to prevent entry and damaging those attempting to bypass until they’re disabled.
