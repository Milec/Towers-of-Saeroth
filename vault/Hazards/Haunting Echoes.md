# Haunting Echoes  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 28

**Complexity** Complex

The spirits of community members linger on after their tragic deaths.

---

**Stealth**
+10 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (untrained) to [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]] that the spirits leave or DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (trained) to exorcise the spirits

**Manifest** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature the spirits knew in life enters the haunted area; **Effect** The spirits manifest with a terrible wailing. Each creature in the area must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (or frightened 2 on a critical failure). Frightened creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for as long as they remain frightened. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The haunt attempts an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check with a +11 modifier against the Will DC of each frightened creature as it bombards them with the voices of their dead loved ones.

**Critical Success** The creature takes 2d4 mental damage and its frightened condition increases by 1, to a maximum of frightened 3.  

**Success** The creature takes 1d4 mental damage.  

**Failure** The creature is unaffected.  

**Critical Failure** The creature’s frightened condition is reduced by 1.

---
**Reset**
The haunt falls silent for 24 hours once all frightened creatures die or reduce their frightened condition’s value to 0. During this time, a creature that isn’t frightened can exorcise the spirits with a DC 18 Religion check, permanently destroying the haunt.
