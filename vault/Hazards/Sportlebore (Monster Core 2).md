# Sportlebore  *Hazard 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 306

**Complexity** Simple

A sportlebore hides amid victuals on a plate of food or within a pack of trail rations.

---

**Stealth**
DC 26 (expert)

**Disable**
A creature that notices the sportlebore can remove it from the food it’s [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] within automatically as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action.

**Mimic Food** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature eats food the sportlebore is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] within; **Effect** The sportlebore rapidly multiplies in the host’s stomach, exposing the host to sportlebore infestation.  
**Sportlebore Infestation** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) **Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 hour); **Stage 3** enfeebled 2 (1 hour); **Stage 4** 4d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save) as the host painfully vomits out a sportlebore swarm and returns to stage 1
