# Flawed Memories  *Hazard 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Complex

The murky waters that fill the chamber grant false visions of the past.

---

**Stealth**
DC +35 (master)

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (master) to release alchemical reagents to neutralize the ink in the water, DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (legendary) to manipulate the primal magic in the region and negate the ink’s effects, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (8th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 34) to counteract the magical ink in the water; four success are required to disable it (spells that purify or manipulate water, such as _cleanse cuisine_ or _control water_, can affect this hazard as if the caster were using an equal rank _dispel magic_)

**Mindwarp Ink** ⤾ (mental, poison, primal) **Trigger** A creature moves into the ink-fouled water; **Effect** The creature must attempt a DC 37 Will save to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 24 hours (stupefied 2 for 24 hours on a critical failure). The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) On the trap’s first action, it attempts a water jet ranged Strike on all creatures outside of the water in this room or on the stairs leading up to area  
**A5**. On its second action, the primal magic infusing the waters attempts to seep into the minds of those within it, forcing each creature in the water to attempt a DC 39 Will save against the following effects.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected  

**Success** The creature takes 4d6 mental damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 8d6 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 24 hours. While the creature is stupefied, it takes an additional –2 status penalty to checks made to Recall Knowledge.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but 16d6 mental damage, stupefied 3, and the stupefied condition has an unlimited duration.  
**Ranged** [one-action] water jet +38 (poison); **Damage** 3d10+18 bludgeoning plus mindwarp ink; no multiple attack penalty

---
**Reset**
The hazard remains disabled until Baolen refills the area with ink (typically once every 4 hours).
