# Mirror Door  *Hazard -1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 122

**Complexity** Simple

The reflection of a wall shows a phantom door where none exists. This door can only be manipulated while viewed through the mirror's reflection.

---

**Stealth**
DC 15

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to open the door while viewing the reflection

**Appear** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]]) **Trigger** A creature views the wall through a mirror's reflection; **Effect** A door appears in the mirror's reflection of the wall. The triggering creature can open and manipulate this door only as long as they view it through the mirror and only by succeeding at a DC 18 Thievery check to disable the trap.

---
**Reset**
automatic
