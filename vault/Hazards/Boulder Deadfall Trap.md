# Boulder Deadfall Trap  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 40

**Complexity** Simple

A weight-sensitive plate buried underneath sand pulls a boulder over a cliff ledge, causing it to fall on targets within the entrance.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (trained); DC 0 to spot the balanced boulders

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (expert) or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to find and disconnect the plate from the tipping mechanism

**Deadfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature enters the area without taking care to avoid the hidden trigger plate; **Effect** Creatures within 10 feet of the cave entrance take 4d10+18 damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
Resetting the boulders in the trap is a labor-intensive project that takes 2 hours.
