# Gloomglow Mushrooms  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -145- Hellknight Hill|Pathfinder #145: Hellknight Hill]] pg. 57

**Complexity** Simple

A field of grass-like fungal filaments amid clusters of softly glowing, long-stalked mushrooms

---

**Stealth**
DC 26 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Survival]] DC 22 (expert) to remove a 5-foot-patch without triggering the mushrooms.

**AC** 21, **Fort** +13, **Ref** +9

**HP**
52

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Cold|cold]] 10

**Glowing Spores** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature moves into the mushrooms' space or damages the mushrooms. The mushrooms can't use this reaction if the damage was [[Traits/Cold|cold]] damage. **Effect** The triggering creature and all creatures within 10 feet are sprayed with itchy, distracting, glowing spores and must attempt a DC 22 Reflex save. On a failed save, the creatures become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (or sickened 2 and stupefied 2 on a critical failure) and take a –4 penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks until the spores lose potency in 24 hours or are removed. This requires washing them off by bathing in water for a minute, a [[Spells/Prestidigitation|_prestidigitation_]] spell, or a similar measure allowed by the GM.
