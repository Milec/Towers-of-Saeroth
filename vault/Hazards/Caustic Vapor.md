# Caustic Vapor  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 57

**Complexity** Simple

The door slams shut, and poison gas fills the room.

---

**Stealth**
DC 40 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] DC 36 (master) or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] DC 36 (master) to exorcise the spirits

**Breath Snatcher** ⤾ ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A living creature other than an [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze|ooze]] spends at least 3 rounds in the room; **Effect** The trap activates and fills the area with caustic nightmare vapor, exposing all creatures within.

 Caustic Nightmare Vapor ([[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]]) Oozes are immune to this poison; **Saving Throw** DC 38 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 round; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 acid damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 6d6 acid damage, confused, and [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] (1 round); **Stage 3** 8d6 acid damage, confused, flat-footed, and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] (1 round)

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 day.
