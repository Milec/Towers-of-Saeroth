# Cold Spot  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 64

**Complexity** Simple

The temperature suddenly drops as spectral forces gather in the area.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 19 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to banish the cold spot with ritual prayers or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to foil the arrival of outside forces

**Sudden Chill** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A living creature enters the area; **Effect** The temperature drops as invisible spirits gather around the living. Living creatures within 15 feet take 2d4+5 cold damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). Creatures that critically fail their save are additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]].

---
**Reset**
1 hour
