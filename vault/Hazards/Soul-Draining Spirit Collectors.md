# Soul-Draining Spirit Collectors  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 34

**Complexity** Complex

Four spirit collectors drain the souls from nearby creatures.

---

**Stealth**
+37 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (legendary) to sever the connection between [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]] and each spirit collector, DC 50 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to deactivate each spirit collector, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (9th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 50) to counteract each spirit collector

**AC** 43, **Fort** +35, **Ref** +29

**Hardness**
33

**HP**
130 (BT 65)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Repulse** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]], [[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** A creature damages a spirit collector; **Effect** The damaged spirit collector releases a wave of force to repel the triggering creature, dealing 4d10+20 force damage to the triggering creature, who must attempt a Reflex save. The trap then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] back 5 feet.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage, is pushed back 10 feet, and lands [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is pushed back 20 feet, and lands prone.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) This trap loses 1 action each turn for each disabled spirit collector. Each spirit collector uses 1 action to drain the souls of all creatures within 30 feet, dealing 4d10+20 force damage (DC 46 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] or increases their drained and stupefied conditions by 1 (by 2 on a critical failure).
