# Battering Door  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 33

**Complexity** Simple

Animated door slams into trespassers.

---

**Stealth**
DC 33 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (master) to soothe the psychic impressions or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to jam the door open

**AC** 25, **Fort** +21, **Ref** +15

**Hardness**
16

**HP**
70 (BT 35)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Batter Trespasser** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature passes through the door; **Effect** The door slams into the triggering creature, dealing 4d10+26 bludgeoning damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically.
