# Spider Summoning Rune  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 66

**Complexity** Complex

An invisible rune on the floor summons a [[Bestiary/Goliath Spider|goliath spider]] when disturbed by someone who isn't wearing [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber|Norgorber's]] [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]].

---

**Stealth**
+24 (master)

**Disable**
DC 34 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to erase the rune or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 36) to dispel the rune.

**Summon Animal** ⤾ ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Summoned|summon]]) **Trigger** A creature that isn't wearing a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]] enters the area above the rune; **Effect** The trap summons a [[Bestiary/Goliath Spider|goliath spider]]. The goliath spider rolls initiative and remains for 2d6 rounds, after which the spell ends and the spider disappears. The spider also disappears if someone disables the trap before the duration expires. Unlike most summoned creatures, the goliath spider can use 3 actions each round and can use reactions.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 24 hours.
