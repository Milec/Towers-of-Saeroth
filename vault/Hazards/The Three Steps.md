# The Three Steps  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 19

**Complexity** Simple

Beams of magical energy fire from the Sihedrons to fan the room.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 to trick the basket on the door handle into thinking it's got a proper bead inside it, or DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] each round to avoid being detected by the trap

**Wrathful Blast** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature ends its turn in the room. **Effect** Beams of energy fan out of the Sihedrons on the east and west wall, causing 9d6 energy damage (DC 22 basic Reflex save) to all creatures in the room. The trap in area **B4a** deals fire damage, the one in **B4b** cold damage, and the one in **B4c** poison damage.
