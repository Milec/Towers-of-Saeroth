# Phantom Jailer  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 65

**Complexity** Simple

A spirit appears and arrests the creature by clamping manacles around their wrists. Disable DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] to seize the manacles from the spirit, DC 19 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] (trained) to order the spirit to stand down, or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] to exorcise the spirit

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained) to notice the [[Equipment/Manacles|manacles]] twitch

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] to seize the manacles from the spirit, DC 19 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] (trained) to order the spirit to stand down, or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] to exorcise the spirit

**Capture** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature touches the manacles; **Effect** The spirit attempts to lock the manacles around the triggering creature's wrists. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 23 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The spirit loosely places manacles around the creature's wrists. Removing the manacles requires a single action.  

**Failure** The spirit clamps the manacles around the creature's wrists but fails to lock them. The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until the manacles are removed as a 3-action activity.  

**Critical Failure** The spirit clamps the manacles around the creature's wrists and locks them. The creature is flat-footed and clumsy 1 until the manacles are removed ([[Actions/Escape]] DC 23, simple lock).

---
**Reset**
1 day
