# Electric Latch Rune  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 523

**Complexity** Simple

An invisible rune imprinted on a door latch releases a powerful electric discharge.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 20 (expert) to scratch out the rune without allowing electricity to flow, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level; counteract DC 18) to counteract the rune

**Electrocution** ⤾ (arcane, electricity, evocation); **Trigger** A creature grasps the door latch directly or with a tool. **Effect** The trap deals 3d12 electricity damage to the triggering creature (DC 22 basic Reflex save).
