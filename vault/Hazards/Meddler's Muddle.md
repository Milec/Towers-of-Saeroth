# Meddler's Muddle  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 13

**Complexity** Simple

A rune hidden in the door’s decorative carvings delivers a telepathic jolt, draining will and muddling the mind.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to scratch out the rune without triggering a mental assault, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 24) to counteract the rune

**Telepathic Jolt** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]); **Trigger** A creature touches the door directly or with a tool; **Bypass** key; **Effect** The trap deals 6d10 mental damage to the triggering creature (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the target is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 24 hours (stupefied 3 on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 minute.
