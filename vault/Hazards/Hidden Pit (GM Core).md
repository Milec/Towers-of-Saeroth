# Hidden Pit  *Hazard 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 102

**Complexity** Simple

A wooden trapdoor covers a pit that’s 10 feet square and 20 feet deep.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (or 0 if the trapdoor is disabled or [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]])

**Disable**
DC 12 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to remove the trapdoor

**AC** 10, **Fort** +1, **Ref** +1

**Trapdoor Hardness**
3

**Trapdoor HP**
12 (BT 6)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Pitfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks onto the trapdoor; **Effect** The triggering creature falls in and takes falling damage (typically 10 bludgeoning damage). That creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling.

---
**Reset**
Creatures can still fall into the trap, but the trapdoor must be reset manually for the trap to become hidden again.
