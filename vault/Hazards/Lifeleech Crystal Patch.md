# Lifeleech Crystal Patch  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -148- Fires of the Haunted City|Pathfinder #148: Fires of the Haunted City]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Simple

Sharp, bloodthirsty crystals make walking through this chamber treacherous.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert) or [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] to note the presence of necromantic energies suffusing the crystals.

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Religion]] DC 30 (expert) to utter prayers that quell the crystals' necromantic compulsion to drink blood

**AC** 31, **Fort** +24, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
20

**HP**
80 (BT 40)

**Immunities**
critical hits (except bludgeoning or [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]), object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
sonic 10

**Slice Legs** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature with blood moves through a patch of lifeleech crystals. **Effect** The crystals twist and slash, animating and cutting into the creature as it walks, dealing 5d8 slashing damage. The creature must attempt a DC 28 Reflex saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]].  

**Failure** The creature takes double damage and is drained 2.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the creature's blood causes fresh growth. A new 10-foot-square patch of lifeleech crystals grows in an adjacent space to the current patch of crystals.
