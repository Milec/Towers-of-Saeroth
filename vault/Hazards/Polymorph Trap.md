# Polymorph Trap  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 525

**Complexity** Simple

A Druidic glyph tries to transform a trespasser into an animal.

---

**Stealth**
DC 34 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 32 (master) to drain the glyph’s power harmlessly, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; counteract DC 30) to counteract the glyph

**Baleful Polymorph** ⤾ (primal, transmutation); **Trigger** A creature moves within 30 feet of the glyph without speaking the passphrase in Druidic. **Effect** The creature is targeted by [[Spells/Baleful Polymorph|_baleful polymorph_]] (DC 32 Will save).
