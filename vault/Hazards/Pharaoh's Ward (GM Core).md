# Pharaoh's Ward  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 103

**Complexity** Simple

A curse is imbued on an entryway’s threshold.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to painstakingly remove the lintel without triggering the magic, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 25) to counteract the rune

**Curse the Intruders** ⤾ ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Trigger** The seal on the tomb is broken from the outside; **Effect** Each living creature within 60 feet must succeed at a DC 23 Will save or be subjected to the pharaoh’s curse. A cursed creature takes a –2 status penalty to Fortitude saves, and any natural or magical [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] it receives is halved. The curse remains until removed by [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] or similar magic.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets when the door is shut.
