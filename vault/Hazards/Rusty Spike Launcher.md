# Rusty Spike Launcher  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rusthenge]] pg. 48

**Complexity** Simple

As the large rusty door swings open, a mass of rusty spikes appear out of nowhere in the open doorway and launch violently into the room.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to decouple the triggering rods that cause the spikes to extend, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 18) to counteract the trap

**Launch Spikes** ⤾ **Trigger** a creature opens the door without uttering a prayer to Xar-Azmak first; **Effect** A mass of rusty spikes appears in the open doorway and then fire into area **E1**. The rusty spikes curve through the air to seek targets—all creatures in area **E1** must attempt a DC 20 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature avoids the spikes and takes no damage.  

**Success** A spike grazes the creature, inflicting 2d10 piercing damage.  

**Failure** A spike impales the creature, inflicting 2d10+13 piercing damage.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the spike also exposes the creature to [[Rules/Diseases/Tetanus|tetanus]].

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically resets once the door is closed.
