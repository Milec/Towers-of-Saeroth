# Anguished Sarenite  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 58

**Complexity** Simple

The gold [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of a former priest of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae]] is haunted by the priest's tormented soul.

---

**Stealth**
DC 26 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to bring the spirit to its senses, or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to banish it

**Solar Wrath** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) **Trigger** A non-good creature approaches within 10 feet of the religious symbol; **Effect** The Sarenite summons a burst of spiritual flame in a 30-foot radius. Creatures in the area take 10d8 good damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). Creatures who fail this save are also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]], and creatures who critically fail are frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.

---
**Reset**
1 day
