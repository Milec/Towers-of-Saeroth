# Axiomatic Polymorph Trap  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 49

**Complexity** Simple

A [[Setting/Languages/Utopian]] glyph transforms trespassers into aeons.

---

**Stealth**
DC 47 (master)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to drain the glyph's power harmlessly, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (9th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 42) to counteract the glyph

**Axiomatic Polymorph** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]); **Trigger** A creature moves within 10 feet of the entryway to the Hall of Wonders (area N4) without speaking the passphrase (“The History and Future of Humanity”) in Utopian; **Effect** Each creature within 20 feet of the door is targeted by [[Spells/Baleful Polymorph|_baleful polymorph_]] (DC 42 Will save), except the effect transforms creatures into the form of an [[Bestiary/Arbiter|arbiter aeon]] rather than that of an animal.

---
**Reset**
1 hour
