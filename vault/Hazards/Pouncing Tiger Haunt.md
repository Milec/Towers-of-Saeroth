# Pouncing Tiger Haunt  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 42

**Complexity** Simple

A cave painting of a great cat roars to life and leaps off the wall to attack.

---

**Stealth**
DC 21 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (trained) or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to quell the spirit

**AC** 18, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Acid|acid]] 5

**Tiger Slash** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 5 feet of the cave painting; **Effect** The haunt deals 4d8 slashing damage with semisolid claws (DC 18 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round (or frightened 2 and fleeing for 1 minute on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
If the cave painting isn't destroyed, the haunt resets after 1 day
