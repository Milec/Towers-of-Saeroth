# Locking Door  *Hazard -1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 64

**Complexity** Simple

A door (or other portal) slams shut and locks.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 to notice the door sway slightly, even though there's no breeze

**Disable**
DC 14 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] to push back against the door, DC 14 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] to wedge the door open, or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to jam the lock or open the lock afterward

**Shut In** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature ends their move after passing through the doorway; **Effect** With an ethereal gust shimmering in the air, the door creaks as it swings shut and locks. The haunted door [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushes]] anyone in its space into an adjacent space in the connecting chamber. A creature that would be pushed and succeeds at a DC 16 Reflex save selects which side of the door they end up on.
