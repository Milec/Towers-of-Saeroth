# Shadow Lash  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 66

**Complexity** Complex

Four pillars manifest shadow lashes that attack intruders.

---

**Stealth**
+38 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (legendary) to drain the magic from each shadow lash or DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to disable each shadow lash

**Manifest** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]]) **Trigger** A creature other than Kemnebi enters the chamber; **Effect** Four pillars (see above) manifest a writhing whip composed of solidified shadows. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(8 actions) This trap loses 2 actions each turn for each disabled shadow lash. Each shadow lash has 2 actions it can use to perform the following actions.  
 **Melee** single action lash +41 ([[Traits/Reach|reach 20 feet]]), **Damage** 4d10+22 slashing  
 **Long-Range Lash** two actions The lash lengthens, increasing its [[Traits/Reach|reach]] to 60 feet, and makes a lash Strike. The lash's length then reverts to normal.
