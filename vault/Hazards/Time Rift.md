# Time Rift  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 47

**Complexity** Complex

A time rift focused on the sundial causes time to pass erratically for all creatures in the area.

---

**Stealth**
+42 (master)

**Disable**
Three DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) or DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (master) checks to mend the time rift.

**Pause** ⤾ ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the area; **Effect** The triggering creature becomes temporarily unmoored from time, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 3]]. If the creature succeeds at a DC 47 Will save, they reduce this to stunned 1. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the time rift alters each creature's timeline. Each creature in the area rolls a 1d4 to randomly determine the effect on them.

1. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.
2. The creature is unaffected.
3. The creature is affected erratically by time; they're concealed for 1 round.
4. The creature becomes [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened 1]] for 1 round

.
