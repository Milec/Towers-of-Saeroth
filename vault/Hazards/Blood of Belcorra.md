# Blood of Belcorra  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 13

**Complexity** Complex

A bloody image of Belcorra arises, emits a soul‑draining light, then inhales blood from living creatures in the room.

---

**Stealth**
+13 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to wipe away enough of the bloodstain to disrupt the haunt’s necromantic energies, or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to exorcise the haunt

**AC** 19, **Fort** +6, **Ref** +9, **Will** +12

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
positive 5

**Resistances**
physical 5 (except [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]])

**Gauntlight Beam** ⤾ ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** A living creature ends its turn in area A11 at night; **Effect** A ghastly image of Belcorra, seemingly composed of fresh blood, rises from the bloodstain on the floor and rotates in place like a lighthouse. Beams of cold blue light shine from Belcorra’s eyes and open mouth, causing blisters and decay to spread across flesh. Living creatures within a 30-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] from the center of area A11 must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save. The haunt then rolls initiative.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes 1d8 negative damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes 1d8+6 negative damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes 2d8+6 negative damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round then dazzled for 1 round.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The bloody image opens its mouth and appears to inhale, drawing blood from the body of one creature in its line of sight within 90 feet from the center of area A11. The creature targeted must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]] damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes 1d10+6 bleed damage.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes 2d10+6 bleed damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 as long as it’s bleeding.

---
**Reset**
If the haunt is disabled or destroyed through damage, the bloody phantom of Belcorra sprays upward in a spiral, dousing the trap door leading to area **A25** before draining upward through the keyhole, which reduces the Thievery DC to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick the Lock]] to 20. At the next sunrise, the blood drains back down to this room and the haunt resets. The haunt is destroyed permanently once Lasda Venkervale is rescued from area **D9**.
