# Corpse Disposal  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 40

**Complexity** Simple

A wooden trap door conceals a spike-filled pit 10 feet square and 20 feet deep. The DC to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] out of the pit is 10.

---

**Stealth**
DC 21 (or 0 if the trap door is [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|disabled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]])

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to jam the trap door open

**AC** 18, **Fort** +7, **Ref** +7

**Trap door Hardness**
9

**Trap door HP**
30

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Dispose** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps onto the trap door (marked “T” on the map); **Effect** The triggering creature falls into the pit. The creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling. Failing that, the creature falls 20 feet (taking 10 bludgeoning damage from the fall) and takes 3d6 piercing damage from the metal spikes jutting up from the pit’s floor. A creature who takes damage from the spikes is exposed to tetanus.  
**Tetanus** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) **Saving Throw** DC 14 Fortitude; **Onset** 10 days; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 week); **Stage 2** clumsy 2 and can’t speak (1 day); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] with spasms (1 day); **Stage 4** death
