# Precarious Thunderstone Trap  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 33

**Complexity** Simple

A tripwire fastened to the gate snaps when the gate is opened, releasing a [[Equipment/Thunderstone|moderate thunderstone]] to the ground.

---

**Stealth**
DC 15 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to remove the thunderstone or DC 18 Reflex save to catch the falling thunderstone

**AC** 12, **Fort** +7, **Ref** +3

**Wire Hardness**
3

**Wire HP**
10 (BT 5)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Thunderstone** ⤾ ([[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) **Trigger** The front gate is opened; **Effect** A moderate thunderstone falls on the flagstones and activates, dealing 2 sonic [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage to each creature within 10 feet of the gate. Creatures in the area must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude saving throw or be [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round. Adjacent creatures can attempt to catch the falling thunderstone (see Disable, above).
