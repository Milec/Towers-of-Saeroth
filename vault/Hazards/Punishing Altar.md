# Punishing Altar  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 15

**Complexity** Simple

The bowl releases a wave of negative energy when a living creature approaches without displaying a religious symbol of Venexus.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (trained) to notice the bowl is the center point for a magical trap

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to remove the bowl without triggering the magic, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 20) to counteract the runes

**AC** 21, **Fort** +14, **Ref** +14

**Hardness**
12

**HP**
46 (BT 23)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Necromantic Wave** ⤾ **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 10 feet of the bowl without displaying a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of Venexus; **Effect** The bowl releases a wave of necromantic energy, dealing 4d8+10 negative damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to the triggering creature and all creatures in the area not displaying a religious symbol of Venexus. On a critical failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] 1.
