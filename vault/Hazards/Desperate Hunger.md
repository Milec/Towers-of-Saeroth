# Desperate Hunger  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 67

**Complexity** Simple

A skeletally thin spirit appears, weeping as it shoves earth and stones down its throat in an effort to stave off hunger.

---

**Stealth**
DC 35 (master) to feel an odd sense of hunger

**Disable**
DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (master) to convince the spirit it's no longer hungry or DC 38 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to ritually feed the spirit

**Desperate Meal** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature approaches within 10 feet; **Effect** Creatures within 30 feet are filled with painful hunger and must succeed at a DC 32 Fortitude save or feel their stomachs fill with rocks, dirt, and worse, dealing 6d10+20 piercing damage.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is sickened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is sickened 4, and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds for as long as it's sickened.

---
**Reset**
1 day
