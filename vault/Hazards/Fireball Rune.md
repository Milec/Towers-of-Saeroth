# Fireball Rune  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 524

**Complexity** Simple

An invisible rune creates an invisible, spherical magical sensor with a 20-foot radius.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 22 (expert) to erase the rune without triggering the sensor, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (3rd level; counteract DC 20) to counteract the rune

**Fireball** ⤾ (arcane, evocation, fire); **Trigger** A living creature enters the sensor area. **Effect** The rune detonates a fireball centered on the triggering creature’s square. This is a 3rd-level [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]] spell that deals 6d6 fire damage (DC 22 basic Reflex save).
