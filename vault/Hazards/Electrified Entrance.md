# Electrified Entrance  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 49

**Complexity** Simple

Magical runes electrocute anyone who crosses the door’s threshold.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (trained) to spot the runes etched into the door’s frame

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] (trained) to jump through the door before the runes trigger, DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to scratch out the runes, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 24) to counteract the runes

**AC** 22, **Fort** +18, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
14

**HP**
58 (BT 26)

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Sizzle** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature moves through the doorway; **Effect** Arcs of lightning flash across the threshold. The triggering creature takes 4d8+18 electricity damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute. On a critical failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 minute and then dazzled for an additional minute.

---
**Reset**
1 minute
