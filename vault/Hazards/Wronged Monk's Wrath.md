# Wronged Monk's Wrath  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 17

**Complexity** Simple

A monk's tormented spirit attacks intruders.

---

**Stealth**
DC 32 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Setting/Deities/Irori]] [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] (trained) to clean the room and return the prayer items to their proper arrangement, DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to state an Iroran prayer of peace and put the haunt to rest, or DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (master) to create a ward against future hauntings

**Ki Storm** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature enters the prayer room; **Effect** The abbot's spirit appears and begins to channel powerful ki energy. One round later, the room fills with a powerful kinetic storm, dealing 3d10+15 electricity damage and 3d10+15 force damage to all creatures in the room. Creatures in the room must attempt a DC 37 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage, 2d8 persistent electricity damage, and becomes deafened for 1 minute.

---
**Reset**
The haunt re-forms after 1 hour. The haunt becomes disabled and doesn't re-form if a creature lights all of the candles in the room and offers a prayer to Irori.
