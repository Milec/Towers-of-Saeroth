# Sticky Greeter  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 52

**Complexity** Simple

A taxidermy giant spider’s abdomen opens up to spray the area in front of it with sticky webbing.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 to disable the pressure plate that triggers the sticky greeter

**AC** 19, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +9

**Spider Hardness**
5

**Spider HP**
40 per spider (BT 20)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Web Hardness** 5; **Web HP** 20; **Immunities** bludgeoning damage, critical hits, object immunities, piercing damage, precision damage

**Spray Webbing** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps on the pressure plate adjacent to the sticky greeter; **Effect** The sticky greeter lurches as its abdomen snaps forward, opens, and sprays the triggering creature with thick sheets of alchemical webbing. At the same time, the sticky greeter emits a high-pitched whistling sound. The creature must make a DC 26 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] and held in place, or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] on a critical failure until it escapes (DC 22) or the webbing binding it is destroyed.
