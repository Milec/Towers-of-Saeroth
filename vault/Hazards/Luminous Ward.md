# Luminous Ward  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 58

**Complexity** Simple

A burst of radiance explodes from the door.

---

**Stealth**
DC 40 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 40 (master) to disable the wards or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (9th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 38) to counteract the ward

**Radiant Explosion** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Light|light]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the door; **Effect** All creatures within 20 feet of the door are scorched with brilliant light, taking 20d6 fire damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails this save is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 hour (or permanently on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 hour.
