# Sacred Geyser  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Simple

A geyser of boiling water erupts at any character who attempts to take water from the oasis.

---

**Stealth**
DC 19 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to concoct a mechanism for indirectly harvesting the water from a safe distance

**Boiling Geyser** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature dips a vessel or otherwise attempts to remove water from the oasis; **Effect** A jet of magical boiling water targets the triggering creature and affects all creatures within 5 feet of the target. Creatures caught in the burst take 2d10+13 fire damage (basic DC 23 Refex save).

---
**Reset**
The geyser resets automatically and adapts to previous circumvention, requiring a new check to disable it each time.
