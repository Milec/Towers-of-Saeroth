# Skewering Hall  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 37

**Complexity** Complex

A dozen spears hidden within sockets in the walls thrust into the hall when the pressure plate is triggered.
