# Revisit Past Pains  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 56

**Complexity** Simple

A spirit locked in memories of its death flies down the hall, forcing intruders to experience the agony of its death.

---

**Stealth**
DC 45 (master)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (master) to calm the spirit or DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (master) to ward off the spirit

**Share Pain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the area; **Effect** Creatures within 30 feet take 6d12+41 mental damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save) as they experience the spirit's death.

---
**Reset**
1 hour.
