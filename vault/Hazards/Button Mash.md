# Button Mash  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Complex

The room floods with harmful effects by pressing a magic sigil on a console. Each sigil causes different results. There are six sigils on the console.

---

**Stealth**
+24 (master)

**Disable**
DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (master) to disenchant each sigil (can be done from either side of the reception desk), DC 43 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) twice to disable the console (can only be done while behind the reception desk), or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (8th level; counteract DC 40) to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] each sigil; prevent Noph from pressing the buttons by gaining access to the reception desk or other methods

**AC** 40, **Fort** +32, **Ref** +26

**Console Hardness**
28

**Console HP**
110 (BT 55)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Lockdown** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** Noph or another creature arms the trap by pressing a sigil on the console; **Effect** A barrier of force forms from floor to ceiling surrounding the reception desk. The DC to counteract this barrier with dispel magic is 43. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) Noph frantically pecks and stomps on the console, using each action to press a random sigil. Roll 1d6 to determine which sigil Noph presses. Psychopomps are immune to the effects of these sigils.

1. The area is flooded with holy light, dealing 3d12+19 positive damage to each creature in the area (DC 43 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
2. The area is flooded with unholy darkness, dealing 3d12+19 negative damage to each creature in the area (DC 43 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
3. The area is flooded with blue flames, dealing 3d10+19 fire damage to each creature in the area (DC 43 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature additionally takes 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].
4. A comet streaks through the area, attacking the nearest creature to Noph with a comet Strike.
5. Force tethers sprout from the floor, grasping at intruders. Each creature in the area is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round unless they succeed at a DC 43 Fortitude save.
6. Ghostly blue mist surrounds each [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] in the area, healing them 3d8+19 HP and granting them [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] for 1 round.

 **Ranged** single action comet +33, **Damage** 3d12+19 bludgeoning
