# Bounding Hounds  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 125

**Complexity** Complex

Three phantom hounds chase down intruders, damaging any they pass through.

---

**Stealth**
DC 37

**Disable**
DC 36 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (expert) to calm each hound, or DC 38 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (master) to banish each hound

**AC** 34, **Fort** +20, **Ref** +26, **Will** +20

**HP**
60 per hound

**Resistances**
all damage 10 (except [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], or [[Traits/Positive|positive]]; double resistance to non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]])

**Bay** ⤾ ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the area; **Effect** The hounds manifest and bay loudly, dealing 3d10+16 sonic damage to all creatures within 30 feet of the hounds (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed check, a creature is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]]. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(9 actions) The trap loses 3 actions each round for each hound disabled. Each hound uses three actions to Stride 40 feet after creatures in the room, passing through corporeal creatures during this movement if possible. Each creature the hound moves through during its movement takes 2d8+7 negative damage (DC 37 Reflex negates). A creature can only be damaged by each hound once each round, no matter how many times the hound moves through their space.

---
**Reset**
1 hour
