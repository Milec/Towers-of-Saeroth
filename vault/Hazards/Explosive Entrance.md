# Explosive Entrance  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 36

**Complexity** Simple

An alchemical bombs explodes when the rubble is shifted, filling the hall with flame.

---

**Stealth**
DC 33 (master)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (master) to nullify the bomb's alchemical components or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to disable the bomb

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature in contact with the ground travels down this hall; **Effect** The bombs explodes, dealing 6d8+12 fire damage to the triggering creature and all creatures within 20 feet (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Additionally, if the triggering creature fails their saving throw, they take 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].
