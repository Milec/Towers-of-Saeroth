# Ekriathe's Rage  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 30

**Complexity** Complex

Ekriathe’s talon releases a pulse of psychic rage throughout the area, transforming the lighthouse’s lantern room into a pitched battlefield.

---

**Stealth**
+22 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (expert) twice to disrupt the divine mythic energies, or DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) twice to cut down the talon and remove it from the room without triggering the haunt

**AC** 30, **Fort** +22, **Ref** +16

**Hardness**
10

**HP**
70 (BT 35)

**Immunities**
[[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]

**Weaknesses**
[[Rules/Area|area]] damage 10

**The Battle for Dhuraxilis** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]]) **Trigger** A creature other than Dorlina touches Ekriathe’s talon; **Effect** Each creature in the area sees the lantern room transform into a battlefield, where Ekriathe and her allies battle a force of human soldiers. Each creature takes 4d12+26 mental damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). A creature that fails or critically fails the save believes it has been transported backward in time and can’t willingly leave the lantern room until the haunt is disabled. An affected creature can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns to end this effect. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The haunt attacks creatures in the area, making spear and talon Strikes against targets determined at random. The haunt takes the multiple attack penalty only if it targets the same creature more than once on a turn. The haunt loses one action if its Hit Points are reduced below its Break Threshold.  
**Melee** single action spear +23, **Damage** 2d12+13 mental  
**Melee** single action talon +23 ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]]), **Damage** 2d10+13 mental

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates once no creatures remain in the lantern room, but it resets immediately.
