# Falling Portcullis Trap  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -179- Cradle of Quartz|Pathfinder #179: Cradle of Quartz]] pg. 62

**Complexity** Simple

An iron portcullis drops from the ceiling when a pressure plate is triggered.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (expert) to spot the sockets in the floor where the portcullis drops; DC 28 (expert) to spot the floor trigger

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to disable the floor trigger, or DC 27 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] or [[Bestiary/I|Engineering Lore]] to jam the portcullis in the ceiling

**AC** 25, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +18

**Hardness**
18

**HP**
72 (BT 36)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Slam Shut** ⤾ **Trigger** The pressure plate is depressed; **Effect** The portcullis falls, closing off the hallway. Any creature under the portcullis when it drops takes 4d10+18 piercing damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]]. A creature that succeeds at a DC 26 Reflex save takes no damage and rolls away in a random direction; on a critical success, they can choose the direction. (For the rules on freeing an immobilized creature, see the [[Rules/Urban|Doors, Gates, and Walls sidebar]])  
 If the trap was triggered by a creature moving north with a Speed of at least 10 feet, they're considered to be under the portcullis when it triggers. Creatures moving south when triggering the portcullis aren't considered to be under the portcullis when it triggers.
