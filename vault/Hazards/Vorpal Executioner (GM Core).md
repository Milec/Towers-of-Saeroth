# Vorpal Executioner  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 105

**Complexity** Simple

A wickedly sharp saw blade descends and travels along grooves in a complex path throughout the room, attempting to decapitate everyone within.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 41 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) at four different junctions to jam all the saw blade’s possible paths, preventing it from traveling through the room

**AC** 43, **Fort** +32, **Ref** +32

**Hardness**
30

**HP**
120 (BT 60) per junction

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Total Decapitation** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to exit the room; **Effect** The saw blade travels along its path, making one [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against each creature in the room, twisting and varying its height for a maximum chance of beheading its targets.

**Melee** saw blade +40 ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d12]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]]), **Damage** 6d12+25 slashing plus decapitation; no multiple attack penalty

**Decapitation** On a critical hit, a target must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or be decapitated, dying instantly unless it can survive without a head

---
**Reset**
The trap resets over the course of the round and can be triggered again 1 round later.
