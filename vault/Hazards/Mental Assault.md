# Mental Assault  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 42

**Complexity** Complex

Magic sigils hidden in the grain of the wooden door trigger a magic trap that damages the mind of anyone attempting to pick the door's lock.

---

**Stealth**
DC 51 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) to deface the symbols or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (10th level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 48) to counteract the trap.

**AC** 42, **Fort** +36, **Ref** +30

**Door Hardness**
35

**HP**
136 (BT 68), [[Actions/Force Open]] DC 48

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Shatter Mind** ⤾ ([[Equipment/Cat|evocation]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature attempts to Pick the Lock; **Effect** The triggering creature takes 8d10+44 mental damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, it's additionally confused for 1 minute.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically if triggered and doesn't reset if disabled.
