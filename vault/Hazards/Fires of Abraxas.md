# Fires of Abraxas  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 17

**Complexity** Simple

Beams of fire lance out of the flames within the Sihedron to sweep through the room unless a prayer to Abraxas is uttered in Thassilonian.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 to deface one of the small trigger runes on the walls, or DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to overwhelm the trap with prayers to a good deity.  
**Bypass** Prayers to Abraxas are carved into the walls in Thassilonian; speaking one of the prayers deactivates the trap for 1 hour.

**Fiery Beam** ⤾ **Trigger** The door to area **B3** is opened or damaged. **Effect** Two beams of fire lance out of the northern and western Sihedron, then swipe through the room. All creatures in area **B2** take 6d10 fire damage (DC 22 basic Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically after 1 hour.
