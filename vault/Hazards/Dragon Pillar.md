# Dragon Pillar  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -146- Cult of Cinders|Pathfinder #146: Cult of Cinders]] pg. 28

**Complexity** Complex

A 10-foot-tall wooden pole topped by a wooden carving of [[Setting/Deities/Dahak|Dahak's]] head. Each dragon pillar's head has been treated with something to give it a distinct color, as indicated in the specific encounter, but regardless of color, plumes of smoke waft up from the mouth.

---

**Stealth**
+0 or [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]]

**Disable**
[[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] DC 26 (expert) to push the pillar over, or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) on the pillar to erase the magic runes that power it, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 22) to dispel the pillar's magic.

**Perception** +16, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]] 60 feet  
**Recognize Ally**; A dragon pillar can see through its empty eye sockets, and it can recognize Cinderclaws and their allies. A character disguised as a Cinderclaw can trick a dragon pillar into perceiving it as a Cinderclaw ally by using [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate]]. A dragon pillar always uses Perception for initiative.

**AC** 24, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +8

**Hardness**
14

**HP**
56 (BT 28)

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Traits/Fire|fire]], object immunities, precision damage

**Dragon Pillar Glance** ⤾ **Trigger** The dragon pillar sees a creature within 120 feet who isn't a recognized Cinderclaw or Cinderclaw ally. **Effect** The dragon pillar makes an eye beam Strike against the triggering creature, then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the dragon pillar fires an eye beam at the closest target within 120 feet.

 **Ranged** single action eye beam +20 ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Range|range 120 feet]]), **Effect** The target is subjected to an effect determined by the pillar's color and summarized below. On a critical hit, the target's save result is one degree worse.

- **Black Eye Beam (area A5)** ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) DC 24 Fortitude save. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected. **Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round. **Failure** The target is blinded for 1 hour. **Critical Failure** The target is blinded for 24 hours.
- **Blue Eye Beam (area A7)** ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) DC 24 Fortitude save. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected. **Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]] for 1 round. **Failure** The target is petrified for 1 hour. **Critical Failure** The target is petrified permanently.
- **Green Eye Beam (area A8)** ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) 6d6 poison damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
- **Indigo Eye Beam (area A9)** ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) DC 24 Will save. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected. **Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round. **Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 round. **Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]] by the dragon pillar and remains within 60 feet of it at all times, defending the dragon pillar from all non-Cinderclaws. While a target is controlled, it is treated as a Cinderclaw ally by the dragon pillar. The dragon pillar can control up to 3 targets at a time; any targets in excess who critically fail this saving throw are instead confused for 1d4+1 rounds. A controlled creature can attempt a new Will save once every 24 hours to escape control, but doing so automatically causes the dragon pillar to attack it with a new eye beam to attempt to re-establish control. The control ends if the pillar is destroyed.
- **Yellow Eye Beam (area A12)** ([[Traits/Electricity|electricity]]) 6d6 electricity damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
- **Orange Eye Beam (area A15)** ([[Traits/Acid|acid]]) 6d6 acid damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
- **Red Eye Beam (area A16)** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) 6d6 fire damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
- **Violet Eye Beam (area B11)** ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) DC 24 Will save. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected. **Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]]. **Failure** The target is stunned 3. **Critical Failure** The target is stunned 7.

---
**Reset**
The dragon pillar deactivates and resets as soon as it perceives no appropriate targets in range.
