# Rusty Floor Spikes  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rusthenge]] pg. 21

**Complexity** Simple

Pressure plates on the floor near the north door cause rusty spikes to lance up out of the floor in area **C3**.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to disable the pressure plates.

**Spring Up** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]) **Trigger** A creature steps on the pressure plate; **Effect** The spikes spring up, Striking all creatures in the hallway. The floor becomes greater difficult terrain.  
 **Melee** rusty spike +11, **Damage** 1d10+4 piercing plus [[Rules/Diseases/Tetanus|tetanus]]

---
**Reset**
Turn the resetting wheel in the eastern Inner Guard Station (area **C8b**).
