# Repelling Surge  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 31

**Complexity** Simple

A shockwave of energy surges out from the room's center.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (master)

**Disable**
DC 29 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (expert) to fool the wards, DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to disable the wards, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (5th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 28) to counteract the wards

**Repel** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Force|force]]) **Trigger** A creature that isn't a Rivethun practitioner enters the room; **Effect** The trap deals 5d4+12 force damage to the triggering creature (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, they're additionally [[Rules/Forced Movement|knocked back]] 10 feet.

---
**Reset**
automatic
