# Spiked Doorframe  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -145- Hellknight Hill|Pathfinder #145: Hellknight Hill]] pg. 46
