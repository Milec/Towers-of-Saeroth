# Spitter Pods  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Fungus]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -193- Mantle of Gold|Pathfinder #193: Mantle of Gold]] pg. 48

**Complexity** Complex

Specialized spore pods burst open, expel toxic globules, and recharge from alchemical reservoirs.

---

**Stealth**
+8 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] to add disruptive chemicals to the fungus's reservoir, DC 18 [[Magic/Domains/Nature]] (trained) to recognize how to constrict the chemicals' flow, or DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] (trained) to spread spores that befuddle the fungus; three total successes are required to disable the trap.

**AC** 17, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +5, **Will** +7

**Hardness**
5

**HP**
30

**Unfurl** ⤾ **Trigger** A non-fungus creatures enters area E4; **Effect** The spitter pod rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The spitter pods alternate between making Strikes and Reloading. The pods lose 1 action for each successful check to disable the trap.  

**Ranged** single action **globule** +11 (poison, range increment 30 feet, reload 1), **Damage** 1d12+2 poison
