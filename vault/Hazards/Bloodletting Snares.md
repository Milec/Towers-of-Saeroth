# Bloodletting Snares  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Simple

Slender razor wire snags a creature, hauling them up into the air while causing deep, bloody wounds.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (expert) or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to snip the wire without getting snared

**AC** 24, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +11

**Hardness**
12

**HP**
48 (BT 24)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Snare** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps into the square with the snare tripwire; **Effect** The bloodletting snare makes a snare Strike against the triggering creature.  
**Melee** snare +20 ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]]), **Damage** 4d8+12 slashing plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed]], and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] and pulled off the ground ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 26). The persistent bleed can't be stopped while the creature is grabbed.
