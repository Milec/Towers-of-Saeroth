# Loose Slab  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 82

**Complexity** Simple

The stone slab tears free from its track with the ear-piercing sound of failing metal.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (expert) to shore up the track so that it functions as intended, or DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to wedge the slab in place to immobilize it.

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Falling Slab** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to move the slab; **Effect** The slab rips free from its track and falls, slamming down in one direction while it kicks up twisted metal debris in the other. All creatures adjacent to the slab take 3d6 bludgeoning and 3d6 piercing damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
