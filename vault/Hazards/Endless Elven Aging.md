# Endless Elven Aging  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 7

**Complexity** Complex

A haunted mural fascinates characters and swiftly drains their vitality.

---

**Stealth**
+33 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] DC 38 (master) or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] DC 38 (master) to calm the restless energies and suppress the haunt for 1 hour; a critical success deactivates the haunt permanently.

**AC** 20, **Fort** +13, **Ref** +5

**Painting Hardness**
20

**Painting HP**
30 per square (6 squares must be destroyed to disable the haunt)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Lifelike Scintillation** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A living creature examines the mural or enters the room; **Effect** The haunt activates and rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) The haunt lures creatures into area A2 using Captivate. Any actions it hasn't used to Captivate are used to drain a living creature in the room with Live a Thousand Lives.

 **Captivate** single action ([[Traits/Charm|charm]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) The faintly moving images compel one creature within 30 feet of the room to move into the room. The creature attempts a DC 38 Will save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target must spend all its actions on its next turn moving into the room, and is then [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 minute.  
 **Live a Thousand Lives** single action ([[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) The haunt causes a living creature in the room to experience a full elven life, weathering every wound, misfortune, loss, and consequence of aging centuries in a matter of seconds. The target must attempt a DC 38 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].  

**Failure** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (or its drained value increases by 1, to a maximum of drained 3), and it is paralyzed until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] (or its doomed value increases by 1).

---
**Reset**
The haunt continues its routine until there are no targets within 30 feet of the room. It then resets over the course of 1 minute and is able to activate again.
