# Sarracenia's Ire  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 42

**Complexity** Complex

The illusion shatters, revealing the grove to be a lifeless scar and the seemingly peaceful loggers to be crawling aimlessly about the clearing, consumed by rage and lashing out at anything that comes near.

---

**Stealth**
+13 (expert) to recognize that the scene before them is an illusion.

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (trained) to tame the primal energy, DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (trained) to suppress the haunt, DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (trained) to console the dryad; three successes are required to disable the haunt.

**AC** 23, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +15, **Will** +15

**Hardness**
12

**HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] 8

**Awaken** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Spells/Fear (Player Core)|_fear_]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]],  <%[[Setting/Deities/Ra (Divine Mysteries)|TRAITS]]%676%%> primal <%END>) **Trigger** At least two creatures enter the grove; **Effect** The illusion fades, and a sorrowful chill emanates from the giant tree. The haunt rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) Sarracenia's rage overwhelms up to two living creatures in the grove, each of which must attempt a DC 23 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** For 1 round, the creature is confused, is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]], and gains a +4 status bonus to damage rolls.

---
**Reset**
Unless Silene is destroyed, this haunt can recharge itself after 1 hour.
