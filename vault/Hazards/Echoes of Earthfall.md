# Echoes of Earthfall  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -214- The Broken Palace|Pathfinder #214: The Broken Palace]] pg. 28

**Complexity** Complex

Bodiless screams arise as the room rumbles and shakes, as if in the grip of an earthquake.

---

**Stealth**
+13 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (expert) to exorcise the spirits or DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to stand resolute and show the spirits there’s nothing to fear; three total successes are required to disable this haunt.

**Final Cries of Horror** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A living creature attempts to exit the room; **Effect** The cavern erupts into terrified cries as a deep rumbling fills the room. All creatures in area **C12** must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure). The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) A thunderous explosion rocks the room as the ceiling appears to split open and collapse. This cave-in is an illusion formed from the memories of the dead, but it can inflict real damage. All creatures in the room must attempt a DC 22 Will save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes 1d10 mental damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes 1d10+6 mental damage and believes the room is filling with rubble—they're [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until the start of their next turn as they spend their energy navigating the illusory rockfall.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes 2d10+12 mental damage and believes they're pinned by rubble—they're [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the start of their next turn.

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates once no living creatures are in the cave at the end of a round, then resets 1 hour later as the spiritual energy rebuilds to a breaking point.
