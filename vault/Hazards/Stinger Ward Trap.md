# Stinger Ward Trap  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 61

**Complexity** Simple

A ghostly stinger appears in the air and jabs at anyone moving through the opening.

---

**Stealth**
DC 32 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to prevent the ghostly stinger from forming or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 34).

**Deadly Sting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Trigger** A creature who isn't wearing a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]] moves the trapdoor; **Effect** The trap targets the creature with [[Spells/Purple Worm Sting|_purple worm sting_]] (DC 30 Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 24 hours.
