# Covetous Corridor  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Complex

Statues depicting the runelords of envy steal magic from intruders.

---

**Stealth**
+35 (master)

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (master) to unweave the magic surrounding a statue, DC 48 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] (expert) to wrest a statue’s implement from its hands, DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to slip an implement free, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 36) to counteract the magic that empowers the statue. Increase the DCs to disable the Belimarius statue by 2. Each statue must be disabled or counteracted separately, and disabling the Belimarius statue or all nine other statues permanently disables the hazard.

[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Covet Magic** ⤾ **Trigger** A magic item or active spell effect enters the corridor; **Effect** A randomly determined statue uses the hazard’s [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] Magic action. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) The hazard uses Steal Magic twice. Once the implements of at least three runelord statues are charged, the hazard uses its next available action to Transfer Magic. If the hazard starts its turn with the Belimarius statue charged, the hazard uses its reaction to Empower Barrier. A disabled runelord statue can’t use Steal Magic. If the Belimarius statue is disabled, the hazard can’t use Transfer Magic or Empower Barrier.  
**Steal Magic** [two-actions] Roll a d10 to determine which statue activates, rerolling a result of 10 or any result that would indicate a disabled statue or a statue that is already charged. The indicated statue casts _detonate magic_ with a +32 bonus to its counteract check. The spell targets the closest magic item within 120 feet, prioritizing the highest-ranking magic item and targeting spell effects only if no magic item is within range. On a success or critical success, the statue gathers the dispersed energy into its implement, charging it. While the statue’s implement remains charged, any creature who is at least an expert in Arcana, [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] can use a 2-action activity with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait to attempt a DC 44 check using that skill. On a success or critical success, they return the magic to its original source, causing it to reactivate. Removing an implement from its statue prevents the statue from using Transfer Magic but doesn’t prevent a creature from restoring the charge to its source.  
**Transfer Magic** [one-action] **Requirements** The implements of at least three runelord statues are charged; **Effect** Three statues with charged implements transfer their charges to the Belimarius statue. While the Belimarius statue holds at least one charge, a creature can transfer charges from its implement as described in Steal Magic, except the DC of the skill check increases to DC 46; success returns the stolen magic to the appropriate items.  
**Empower Barrier** [reaction] **Requirements** The hazard starts its turn, and the Belimarius statue holds at least one charge;  
**Effect** The Belimarius statue transfers all charges from its implement into the force barrier separating the corridor from area **B3**. For every charge the statue transfers, the force barrier gains 10 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 hour. (If the force barrier has been destroyed, the statue instead expends the first charge to cast a 6th-rank _wall of force_, with any remaining charges granting the barrier temporary Hit Points.) Charges transferred to the force barrier are lost forever and can’t be returned to items they were stolen from.

---
**Reset**
The hazard resets automatically if not disabled.
