# Chernasardo Chompers  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 48

**Complexity** Simple

These animal snares have been repurposed to discourage unwanted intruders. When a creature steps onto the snare, jaws with sharpened teeth close around the creature’s leg.

---

**Stealth**
DC 27 (trained) to spot the snare

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] (trained) to step clear the snare before it springs, or DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to trigger the trap harmlessly

**AC** 24, **Fort** +14, **Ref** +0

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
60 (BT 30)

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Bite** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature moves into the space containing the trap; **Effect** The trap’s jaws snap shut on the creature’s leg, dealing 4d8+14 piercing damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failure, the creature becomes caught in the trap and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 22).
