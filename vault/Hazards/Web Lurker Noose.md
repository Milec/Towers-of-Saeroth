# Web Lurker Noose  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 325

**Complexity** Simple

Discreet webbing at throat level snags a creature that walks into it.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) DC 18 to rearrange the webbing

**AC** 18, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5

**Web Noose** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]); **Trigger** A creature steps into the square with the web tripwire. **Effect** The web lurker noose makes a noose Strike against the triggering creature.  
**Melee** noose +13 ([[Traits/Deadly|deadly d10]]), **Damage** 3d6 bludgeoning and the target is grabbed and pulled off the ground (Escape DC 22). The target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage at the end of each of its turns as long as it’s caught in the noose.
