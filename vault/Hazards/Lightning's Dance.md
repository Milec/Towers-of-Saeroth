# Lightning's Dance  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Kaiju]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 191

**Complexity** Complex

Electricity suffuses an area where [[Bestiary/Agyra]] has used her lightning breath multiple times.

---

**Stealth**
+20 (expert) or DC 33 (master) to notice sparks dancing around normally non-conductive surfaces

**Disable**
DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (master) to fashion a makeshift lightning rod, DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (expert) to dissipate the electrical charge harmlessly into the air

**Static Shock** ⤾ ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) **Trigger** A creature approaches within 10 feet of the hazard's center; **Effect** Electricity wells up from the ground and nearby material. Each creature in the hazard's area must attempt a DC 33 Fortitude save, and the hazard then rolls initiative. A character wearing metal armor or wielding a metal shield or weapon of at least 1 Bulk treats the result of their saving throw as one step worse.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is clumsy 2 for 2 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is clumsy 2 for 4 rounds and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]] for 1 round.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) Bolts of lightning flash out. The hazard makes two lightning bolt Strikes at two different creatures within 120 feet of the hazard's center. If only one creature is in range, the hazard makes only one Strike.

 **Ranged** single action lightning bolt +24 ([[Traits/Range|range increment 120 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+15 electricity

---
**Reset**
The hazard deactivates and immediately resets if has no targets for 1 round; it also resets if Agyra uses her lightning breath multiple times in a single combat in the area.
