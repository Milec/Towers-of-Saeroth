# Floating Flamethrower  *Hazard 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 44

**Complexity** Complex

A floating skull of fire swoops around the arena, rotating as it weaves through the ring and releasing huge gouts of flame in random directions.

---

**Stealth**
+23 (expert) or DC 38 (master) to notice the pattern of the orb's trajectory

**Disable**
three DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) checks or castings of [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (7th level; counteract DC 40), one each to pull out or disable the elemental cores hidden in the floating skull's right eye, left eye, and mouth

**AC** 42, **Fort** +30, **Ref** +33

**Hardness**
25

**HP**
100 (50)

**Immunities**
[[Traits/Fire|fire]]

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Cold|cold]] 15

**Blaze** ⤾ ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]); **Trigger** Hao Jin signals the start of the bout; **Effect** The trap roars with fire and rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The trap loses 1 action per turn per successful check to disable. The floating flamethrower uses its first action to release a gout of flame in a 15-foot cone that deals 4d6+4 fire damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save), its second action to fly in a U-shape in a random direction, and its third action to release another gout of flame.

---

**Speed** fly 50 feet
