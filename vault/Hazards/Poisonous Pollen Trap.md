# Poisonous Pollen Trap  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 26

**Complexity** Simple

Flowers carved into the door emit colorful pollen when the door is touched.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to deface the flower carvings, DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (expert) to neutralize the pollen, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 26) to counteract the conjuration

**AC** 27, **Fort** +19, **Ref** +13

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
62 (BT 31)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Pollinate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]); **Trigger** The door is touched, or the lock is opened; **Effect** The flowers spray poisonous pollen in a 30-foot cone. A creature in the area must attempt a DC 24 Fortitude save, with the following effect.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d10+11 poison damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]].  

**Failure** The creature takes 3d10+16 poison damage and 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]], becomes enfeebled 2, and takes a –2 status penalty to Perception checks for 1d4 hours.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 4d10+22 poison damage and 1d10 persistent poison damage, becomes enfeebled 4, and takes a –2 status penalty to Perception checks and saves against poison for 24 hours.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets after 1 hour.
