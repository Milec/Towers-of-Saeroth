# Caustic Gas Trap  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 37

**Complexity** Simple

Pressure plates in the hall trigger the release of caustic gas.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (master)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (master) to neutralize the acid or DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to disable the pressure plates

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature in contact with the ground travels down this hall; **Effect** Caustic gas fills the hall, dealing 4d10+26 acid damage to all creatures in area D5 (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
A 10-minute Interact activity and a new supply of caustic gas.
