# Shattered Runewell of Pride  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Complex

Plumes of magenta and violet light and vapor shift and writhe out of a hole in reality surrounded by the fragments of a _runewell_ suspended mid-explosion.

---

**Stealth**
27

**Disable**
DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (master) or DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (master) to redirect the magic back in on itself and cancel out the danger, or DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (legendary) to reposition _runewell_ fragments so they’re not dangerous; three total successes are required to disable the shattered _runewell_

**AC** 34, **Fort** +29, **Ref** +26

**Shattered Runewell Hardness**
23

**HP**
90 (BT 45)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Frightful Shriek** ⤾ (emotion, fear, mental) **Trigger** Intruders enter the Eye of Arrogance; **Effect** All of the illusory peacocks in the demiplane emit loud shrieks of anger. All intruders to the Eye of Arrogance must attempt a DC 37 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure). The shattered _runewell_ then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) When the shattered _runewell_ takes its f irst action, a towering, 30-foot-tall image of Runelord Xanderghul formed from scintillating magenta and violet light and fire looms up from the runewell. On each of the hazard’s three actions, this manifestation of Runelord Xanderghul scowls down at the PCs, and tendrils of strangely colored fire lash out of the runewell, each targeting a different PC in the area. The effects of these tendrils vary according to their color; these ranged Strikes can target anyone in the Eye of Arrogance and accrue multiple attack penalties normally.

**Ranged** iridescent fire +27 (mental), Damage 3d10+6 mental damage (plus the struck creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round if they fail a DC 37 Will save)  
**Ranged** black fire +27 (void), Damage 3d10+6 void damage plus 2d6 persistent acid damage  
**Ranged** crimson fire +27 (agile), Damage 3d10+6 slashing damage plus 2d6 persistent bleed
