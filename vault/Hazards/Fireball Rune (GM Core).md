# Fireball Rune  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 101

**Complexity** Simple

An invisible rune creates an invisible, spherical magical sensor with a 20-foot radius.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to erase the rune without triggering the sensor, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 20) to counteract the rune

**Fireball** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) **Trigger** A living creature enters the sensor area; **Effect** The rune detonates a fireball centered on the triggering creature’s square. This is a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]] spell that deals 6d6 fire damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
