# Precarious Footing  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Simple

The ground around the lava pit is crumbling and unstable.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to weather the collapse safely; DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (trained) to stabilize the ground

**Lava Bath** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps into the pit or within 5 feet of its edge; **Effect** The ground crumbles apart and the creature slides into the lava. That creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling. Otherwise it falls 20 feet (taking 10 fall damage) and is exposed to lava, taking 6d6 fire damage per round of contact. Climbing the crumbling rock face requires a successful DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check.
