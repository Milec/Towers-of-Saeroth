# Names of the Nameless  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 57

**Complexity** Complex

The voices of the nameless dead intrude into the minds of the living.

---

**Stealth**
DC +16 (trained) to hear whispering voices

**Disable**
DC 26 Perception to discern a name spoken by the nameless dead, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (expert) to put the nameless dead to rest. Speaking three names aloud disables the haunt. A creature that speaks any other name aloud must succeed at a DC 28 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.

**Disappering Door** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the room; **Effect** An illusory wall appears across the room’s entrance. A creature who succeeds at DC 30 Perception check disbelieves the illusion, allowing them to leave the area. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) Spirits of the dead whisper their names into the minds of creatures in the room, dealing 2d10+11 mental damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). A creature that fails the save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (or frightened 2 on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
If the haunt is disabled, divine light washes through the area. Living creatures in the area regain 4d8 Hit Points, and undead creatures in the area take 4d8 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage. The haunt deactivates 1 minute after all creatures leave the area but resets immediately thereafter.
