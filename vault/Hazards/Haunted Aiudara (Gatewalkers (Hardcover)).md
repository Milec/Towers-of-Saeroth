# Haunted Aiudara  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 211

**Complexity** Complex

The spiritual imprints of long-dead explorers haunt this _aiudara_, manifesting as a trio of astral wraiths who guard the gateway jealously.

---

**Stealth**
+15 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (trained) to exorcise one of the wraiths or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to erase one of the wraith’s astral sigils; three successful checks are required to disable the haunt

**Forceful Screech** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Trigger** A creature comes within 30 feet of the aiudara; **Effect** Three aiudara wraiths screech in unison and manifest to protect the arch. Each creature within 30 feet must attempt a DC 27 Will save. The haunt rolls initiative.  
**Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Failure** The creature takes 2d8+9 force damage.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes 4d8+9 force damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]].

---
**Routine**
(3 actions; [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) With spectral claws, the aiudara wraiths hurl trespassers through time and space. Each of the three wraiths has 1 action which it can use to make a forceful hand [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a creature within 60 feet. The haunt loses 1 action for each successful check to disable the haunt.  
When the haunt rolls a critical hit to Strike a creature, the creature is instantly shunted to an empty space within 30 feet. The haunt chooses which space the creature is transported to.  
**Melee** single action forceful hand +17 ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]), **Damage** 2d8+9 force; no multiple attack penalty

---
**Reset**
The aiudara wraiths disperse after 1 minute, but the haunt resets after 24 hours.
