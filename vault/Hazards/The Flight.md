# The Flight  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 19

**Complexity** Complex

The spectral figure of a handsome, well-dressed man flees down the stairs, clutching a wound in his side with one hand and making a shoving motion with the other. His shout drives creatures back.

---

**Stealth**
+21 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to drain away the haunt's spiritual energy. This check can be attempted only from the top of the stairs behind the secret door.

**Defensive Shove** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]]) **Trigger** The secret door to the basement stairs opens; **Effect** The spectral figure's shout shoves every creature in the room away from the east wall. Creatures in the drawing room or the adjacent foyer must attempt a DC 28 Fortitude save. The haunt then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature isn't moved.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Objects|pushed back]] 10 feet. If this movement would push it into a wall, it takes bludgeoning damage equal to the number of feet it was moved.  

**Failure** As success, but 20 feet and the creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** As success, but 20 feet, and the creature is knocked prone and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 2]].

---
**Routine**
(1 action) ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) The spectral figure shouts, dealing 4d10 sonic damage to all creatures within 60 feet of the secret door (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). A creature that fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round.

---
**Reset**
The haunt ends as soon as there are no creatures within 60 feet. It resets when the secret door is closed.
