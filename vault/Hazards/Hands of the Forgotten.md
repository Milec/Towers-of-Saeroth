# Hands of the Forgotten  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 37

**Complexity** Simple

Ethereal hands stretch out from the walls and floor, attempting to restrain nearby creatures.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (master) to exorcise the spirits or DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] (expert) to scare away the hands

**Waylay** ⤾ ([[Traits/Force|force]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature moves into the area marked on the map; **Effect** Misty blue hands stretch out from the hallway's stone tiles and grasp at the triggering creature, which must attempt a DC 26 Reflex save to avoid becoming hindered or harmed by the ethereal hands.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is slowed 2 for 1 round and takes 2d10+10 negative damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 for 1 round and takes 4d10+15 negative damage.
