# Poisoned Lock  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 103

**Complexity** Simple

A spring-loaded, poisoned spine is hidden near the keyhole of a lock. Disabling or breaking the trap does not disable or break the lock.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) on the spring mechanism

**AC** 15, **Fort** +8, **Ref** +4

**Hardness**
6

**HP**
24 (BT 12)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|Critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Spring** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature tries to unlock or [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick the Lock]]; **Effect** A spine extends to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] the triggering creature.

**Melee** spine +13, **Damage** 1 piercing plus cladis poison

**Cladis Poison** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 19 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 4 hours; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 hour); **Stage 2** 2d6 poison damage and drained 2 (1 hour); **Stage 3** 3d6 poison damage and drained 2 (1 hour)
