# The Gullet  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 34

**Complexity** Simple

Images of excessive feasts decorate the walls of the hallway.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (master) to notice the hallway is constructed to tilt on a hinge point halfway down its length

**Disable**
DC 43 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to jam the hallway so it can’t tilt; a character who succeeds at a DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check can balance or sprint the length of the hallway before it has a chance to tilt. Flying creatures and Tiny creatures don’t trigger the trap. Creative solutions (such as loading the hallway’s western half with an appropriate counterweight) might also disable the trap.

**See-saw** ⤾ **Trigger** The number of PCs in the eastern half of the hallway equals or exceeds the number in the western half; **Effect** The eastern side of the hallway floor tilts at a 45-degree angle, its end descending. Any
