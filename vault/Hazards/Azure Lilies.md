# Azure Lilies  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 100

**Complexity** Simple

The azure lilies expel a burst of toxic pollen.

---

**Stealth**
+30 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 33 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (expert) to harvest the plant's pollen without disturbing the blossoms.

**AC** 30, **Fort** +27, **Ref** +25

**HP**
120 (BT 60)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Pollen Burst** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]]); **Trigger** The lily patch is disturbed; **Effect** The pollen erupts in a 30-foot-radius burst. Any living creature in the burst must attempt a DC 30 Will save, though creatures with the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait are immune.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 6d6 mental damage as the pollen causes wracking headaches and jolts of pain.  

**Failure** The creature takes 12d6 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 minute. The creature can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns to end the confusion early.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 20d6 mental damage and is confused for 1 minute, with no save to end the confusion early.

---
**Reset**
1 month.
