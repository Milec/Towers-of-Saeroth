# Thief's Trap  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 36

**Complexity** Simple

Nearly imperceptible runes set off an explosion at the point a thief is most unable to receive aid.

---

**Stealth**
DC 50 (master) to notice that the hairline cracks on the passage walls actually form runes.

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (10th level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 50) to counteract the runes

**Explosion** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature crosses a rune; **Effect** The runes unleash a gout of fire roaring up the chute. Anyone in the chute and within 5 feet of its openings is dealt 8d10+44 fire damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Immediately thereafter, the runes emanate a 4th-level [[Spells/Silence|_silence_]] effect for 1 minute, so anyone injured in the chute can't call out for help.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets in 1 minute.
