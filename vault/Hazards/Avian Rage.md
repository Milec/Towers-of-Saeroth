# Avian Rage  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 11

**Complexity** Complex

Hundreds of panicked whippoorwills swarm through the area.

---

**Stealth**
+45 (master)

**Disable**
DC 43 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (master) to calm the birds, DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (master) to alter your aura, or DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] (master) to imitate the living

**Panic!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) **Trigger** An [[Traits/Undead|undead]] or a creature with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]] enters the garden; **Effect** The birds screech in panic, dealing 3d12+20 sonic damage to all creatures in the garden (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (stunned 3 on a critical failure). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The birds swarm wildly through the garden, pecking and slashing at all undead creatures and creatures with negative healing in the garden, dealing 3d10+20 slashing damage and 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets if 1 minute passes without any undead or creatures with negative healing in the garden.
