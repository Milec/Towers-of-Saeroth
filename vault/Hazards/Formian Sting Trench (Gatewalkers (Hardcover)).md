# Formian Sting Trench  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 208

**Complexity** Simple

A thin layer of canvas conceals this 10-foot‑square pit, the walls of which are studded with dagger-sized formian stingers. Creatures that fall into the trap risk being gouged by the poisonous barbs.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained)

**AC** 18, **Fort** +8, **Ref** +8

**Canvas HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Entrench** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]]) **Trigger** A creature walks onto the canvas; **Effect** The creature falls into the concealed trench and takes falling damage (typically 10 bludgeoning damage). The creature is also gouged by three poisoned stingers as they fall. The creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling.  
**Melee** single action stinger +17, **Damage** 1d8+3 plus formian trench poison  
**Formian Trench Poison** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 21; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** 1d6 poison damage, clumsy 1, and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 4** 2d6 poison damage, clumsy 1, and enfeebled 1 (1 round)

---
**Reset**
Creatures can still fall into the trap, but the canvas must be reset manually for the trap to become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] again.
