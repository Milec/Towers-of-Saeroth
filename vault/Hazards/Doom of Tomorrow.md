# Doom of Tomorrow  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -163- Ruins of Gauntlight|Pathfinder #163: Ruins of Gauntlight]] pg. 31

**Complexity** Simple

The sounds of devastation rise from the diorama, and a beam of shimmering energy lances outward from the tiny model of _Gauntlight_.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics|Thievery]] (trained) to quickly deactivate the model of _Gauntlight_ before it triggers, or DC 23 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] (trained) to creep by a trap without triggering it

**AC** 21, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +8

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
56 (BT 28)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Apocalypse Beam** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps within 10 feet of the back of a diorama alcove; **Effect** The trap fires an apocalypse beam at that creature.  
**Ranged** apocalypse beam +16; **Damage** 2d10+13 damage (fire damage from the burning city **[a]**, bludgeoning damage from the tsunami **[b]**, sonic damage from the earthquake **[c]**, mental damage from the monster **[d]**, and negative damage from the undead uprising **[e]**)

---
**Reset**
Each trap resets automatically after 1 hour.
