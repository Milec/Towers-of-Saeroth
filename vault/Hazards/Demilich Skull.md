# Demilich Skull  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 59

**Complexity** Simple

The skull of a defeated [[Bestiary/Demilich|demilich]] is haunted by souls of the demilich's victims.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the skull's Broken Gems

**Broken Gems** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]]) **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 10 feet of the skull; **Effect** The spirits haunting the skull activate one of the skull's ruined gemstones, casting one of the following four spells determined randomly: [[Spells/Cast into Time|_cast into time_]], [[Spells/Chain Lightning|_chain lightning_]], [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]] (6th), [[Spells/Spellwrack|_spellwrack_]]. The saving throw DC for any spell is 26.

---
**Reset**
1 day
