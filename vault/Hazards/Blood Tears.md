# Blood Tears  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 66

**Complexity** Simple

A wailing spirit appears and gouges their own eyes. As they do, blood seeps from the eyes of those who witness the act, obscuring their vision.

---

**Stealth**
DC 32 (master) to notice your vision tint red

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (master) to talk the spirit out of gouging its eyes or DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ease the spirit's sorrows

**Weep Blood** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** A creature approaches within 15 feet; **Effect** Blood seeps from the eyes of all creatures within 30 feet, dealing [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 33 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d8+3 persistent bleed damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature takes 4d8+6 persistent bleed damage and is dazzled for as long as the persistent damage lasts.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 8d8+12 persistent bleed damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for as long as the persistent damage lasts.

---
**Reset**
1 day
