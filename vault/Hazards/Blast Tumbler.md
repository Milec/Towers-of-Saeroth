# Blast Tumbler  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 45

**Complexity** Simple

A hard-to-reach rune is placed on the lock’s tumbler. It emits a blast of force when the door is jostled.

---

**Stealth**
DC 32 (master)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to disarm the rune, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (5th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 28) to counteract the rune’s magic.

**Force Blast** ⤾ ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A non‑[[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature opens the door or critically fails an attempt to disarm or dispel the rune; **Effect** A 30‑foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of force issues forth from the lock. Creatures within the cone must succeed a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude saving throw or take 8d12 force damage. Those who fail the save are pushed 10 feet, and those who critically fail are pushed 20 feet and are [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 2.

---
**Reset**
The rune resets 1 minute after it triggers.
