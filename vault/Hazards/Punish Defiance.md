# Punish Defiance  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 19

**Complexity** Complex

A magical mural threatens uncooperative inmates, causing mental trauma.

---

**Stealth**
Stealth +23 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to deactivate the mural, or [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] DC 26 (trained) to disrupt the mural’s magic, or [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] DC 24 (trained) to pose as a superior and order the mural to deactivate, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 24) to counteract the mural

**AC** 19, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +9

**Mural Hardness**
14

**Mural HP**
54 (BT 27)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Final Warning** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature defies the prison’s authority (such as by attacking a clockwork soldier or living graffiti); **Effect** The mural commands the inmate to cease their defiance and surrender to authority. All living creatures in the room must attempt a DC 24 Will save, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 and stupefied 1 on a failure, or frightened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 on a critical failure. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its turn, the trap threatens all living creatures in the room, dealing 3d6+5 mental damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save).

---
**Reset**
1 hour.
