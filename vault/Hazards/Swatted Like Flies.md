# Swatted Like Flies  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 54

**Complexity** Complex

Feeling the presence of creatures on its backside, the Granite Ox swishes its stone tail to swat them away.  
**Escaping** The PCs overcome this hazard by moving out of the area that the Ox’s tail can reach. A PC escapes (and is not subject to tail Strikes) by ascending the Ox’s sloped back, traveling a distance based on the result of the PC’s Reflex save from the Shaken Loose hazard. PCs can [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] up the Ox’s back with a successful DC 24 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check or—by finding and scrambling up improvised paths—a DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]], or [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] check. With a successful DC 20 Acrobatics check, a flying PC can ascend half their fly Speed (or their full Speed on a critical success). PCs can attempt to Disable the hazard whether or not they’re in range of the tail.

---

**Stealth**
+18 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] to scramble out of the way, DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (trained) to draw the Ox’s attention. The DC of the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] for this round (see Routine) is increased by 4 on a success, or by 8 on a critical success. Increasing the flat check DC to 21 or higher buys the party enough time to move up the Ox’s back, disabling the hazard.

**Tail Swat** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature lands near the base of the Granite Ox’s tail; **Effect** The Granite Ox makes a tail Strike against the triggering creature. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The Granite Ox makes a tail Strike against one creature in the area. If the attack succeeds, the GM rolls a DC 9 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. On a success, the hazard makes another Strike. The hazard continues making Strikes until the GM fails the flat check.  
**Melee** single action tail +20, Damage 2d10+11 bludgeoning plus [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 round; no multiple attack penalty

---
**Reset**
Once all of the PCs moved outside the tail’s striking range, the hazard is effectively disabled. The hazard reactivates if any PC returns to that area.
