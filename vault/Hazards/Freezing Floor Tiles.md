# Freezing Floor Tiles  *Hazard 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 45

**Complexity** Complex

Four remote sensors at each corner of the arena cause bursts of freezing magic to erupt from random tiles on the arena and stop creatures in their tracks.

---

**Stealth**
+23 (expert) or DC 38 (master) to notice the four sensors

**Disable**
DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (legendary) or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 38) to disable one of the sensors

**AC** 36, **Fort** +33, **Ref** +30

**Sensor Hardness**
20

**Sensor HP**
40 (BT 20)

**Immunities**
[[Traits/Cold|cold]];

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Fire|fire]] 15

**Frigid Floor** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold|cold]]); **Trigger** Hao Jin signals the beginning of the match. **Effect** The trap rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) The trap loses 1 action per disabled sensor. On each action, the trap causes a random 10-foot-by-10-foot space in the arena to suddenly freeze. Each creature that begins its turn in a frozen space takes 2d6+2 cold damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that critically fails its save also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round; if it was already slowed, the creature instead becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round. Any amount of fire damage dealt to a slowed or immobilized creature removes the condition. Frozen tiles become unfrozen at the beginning of the trap's next turn.
