# Decrepit Bridge  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 35

**Complexity** Simple

The 30-foot-long, 10-foot-wide bridge groans under the weight of those who cross it.

---

**Stealth**
DC 14 (trained)

**Perilous Sway** ⤾  **Trigger** A Medium or larger creature Strides 10 feet or more in the bridge’s area; **Effect** All creatures on the bridge must attempt a DC 18 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 until the beginning of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature falls from the bridge, landing in the ravine 20 feet below.  
**Collapse** Each time the decrepit bridge triggers its Perilous Sway after the first, attempt a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. On a failure, the bridge collapses. All creatures on the bridge fall 20 feet into the ravine, taking falling damage normally.
