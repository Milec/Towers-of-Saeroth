# Damurdiel's Vengeance  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 18

**Complexity** Complex

An elven woman wearing a robe stands in the water. She beckons anyone who enters the room to join her in the pool.

---

**Stealth**
+37 (legendary) or DC 47 to notice the dagger left in the pool start to move on its own

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Deception]] 45 (legendary) to pretend to be Erveniss and ask for forgiveness, [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] 45 (legendary) to convince Damurdiel to stand down ([[Ancestries/Elf|elves]] who are masters in Diplomacy can attempt the check as if they were legendary instead), or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] 40 (legendary) to call upon [[Setting/Deities/Calistria]] to calm Damurdiel's spirit.

**AC** 43, **Fort** +26, **Ref** +26, **Will** +35

**HP**
128 (BT 64) dagger

**Surprise Strike** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]) **Trigger** A creature approaches within 5 feet of the pool's edge; **Effect** The ghostly elf causes a dagger to rise out of the waters and then fling toward the triggering creature. If the attack is a hit, it is a critical hit instead. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The haunt attempts to Strike with the dagger.

 **Ranged** single action ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 10 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile|versatile S]]) dagger +40, **Damage** 4d4+10 piercing plus 20 negative

---
**Reset**
Damurdiel's spirit subsides once she is destroyed or if she manages to reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points. She returns after 1 minute.
