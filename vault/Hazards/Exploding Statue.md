# Exploding Statue  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 26

**Complexity** Simple

A hollow statue filled with explosives is connected to a trip wire running along the adjacent door.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to safely cut the wire

**AC** 21, **Fort** +6, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
5

**HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Explosion** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature opens the northern door without first disabling the trip wire; **Effect** The statue explodes, spraying shrapnel to the west in a 5-foot cone and dealing 1d10+7 piercing and 1d10 fire damage to all creatures in the affected area (DC 18 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
