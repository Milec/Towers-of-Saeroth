# Unholy Hunger  *Hazard 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 50

**Complexity** Simple

A miasma of foul-smelling vapor with shrieking faces barely visible in the steam bubbles up from the soup.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 19 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Cooking Lore]] to neutralize the haunted stew with the addition of a few select spices from nearby shelves, or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt

**Worse Than it Smells** ⤾ ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A non-nindoru creature takes the lid off the cooking pot; **Effect** All creatures within 15 feet of the pot must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2. The triggering creature instead must attempt a DC 24 Will save to resist the compulsion to dip their face into the stew and swallow a mouthful of the stuff (this effect has the [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits). On a failure, the triggering creature takes 5d6 poison damage (DC 24 basic Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
 The haunt resets 1 round after the lid is replaced on the cooking pot.
