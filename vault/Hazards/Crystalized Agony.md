# Crystalized Agony  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -184- The Ghouls Hunger|Pathfinder #184: The Ghouls Hunger]] pg. 23

**Complexity** Simple

A violet crystal containing the body of a dead creature, grown from its dying agony, pulses as it exudes motes of flickering energy.

---

**Stealth**
DC 10

**Disable**
DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) or DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (master) to know where to Strike a specific crystal perfectly on a facet to cause it to shatter harmlessly

**AC** 31, **Fort** +18, **Ref** +23

**Hardness**
20

**HP**
80 (BT 40)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Emit Agnoy** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** The crystallized agony is struck hard enough to take damage; **Effect** A shimmering mote of violet light flashes from the point of origin from the triggering action, unerringly striking the triggering creature. This bolt of agony deals 10d10 mental damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
A crystallized agony resets automatically and can be triggered again the next round.
