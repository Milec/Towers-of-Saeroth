# Web Lurker Deadfall  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 325

**Complexity** Simple

A web tripwire lets loose a bundle of boulders that fall on all targets within a 10-foot square.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) DC 20 to rearrange the webbing

**AC** 19, **Fort** +9, **Ref** +9

**Deadfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps into the square with the web tripwire. **Effect** All creatures in the trap’s 10-foot square take 2d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 20 basic Reflex save).
