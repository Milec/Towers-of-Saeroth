# Middenworm Infestation  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 243

**Complexity** Simple

A dead body seems to ripple and bulge, as if something inside were shaking.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23

**Disable**
DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] or DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to carefully cut into the corpse and vent the worms harmlessly in a safe direction

**Worm Burst** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature jostles or touches the infested corpse; **Effect** The corpse bursts open, spraying hundreds of middenworm larvae in a 20-foot emanation. Creatures in the area must attempt a DC 25 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 and takes 2d8 piercing damage from worm bites.  

**Failure** The creature is sickened 1 and takes 4d8 piercing damage plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent piercing damage]] as the biting worms latch on.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but sickened 2 and the persistent piercing damage increases to 4d6.
