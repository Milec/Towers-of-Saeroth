# Trinity Star  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Complex

A blazing comet with three tails—one red, one black, and one with rainbow hues—careens throughout the area.

---

**Stealth**
+35 (master)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] (legendary) to grab one of the comet’s tails and rip it free; DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (master) to exorcise the divine energy of the red tail (which is infused with Gorum’s legacy) or the black tail (infused with the nihilism of Abaddon’s Eclipse); DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (master) to unweave the magic of the rainbow tail (Xanderghul’s mythic power); or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 36) to counteract one of the tails. Each tail must be disabled separately to disable the hazard.

**AC** 42, **Fort** +33, **Ref** +36

**Hardness**
30

**HP**
120 (BT 60)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Starform** ⤾ (arcane, light) **Trigger** Dream Aethusa has been dead for 10 minutes; **Effect** Dream Aethusa’s body is consumed with mythic energy, transforming into a comet with three blazing tails. All creatures within 120 feet must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save to resist becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute (or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] permanently on a critical failure). The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The Trinity Star flies at a speed of 60 feet, moving through the spaces of as many creatures as possible. Each creature the Trinity Star moves through must make a DC 42 Reflex save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 6d6 force damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 11d6 force damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 22d6 force damage, is knocked prone, and if the creature is mythic, they lose 1 Mythic Point. If the Trinity Star has lost any of its tails, it regrows one of them (chosen at random if it’s lost two tails).
