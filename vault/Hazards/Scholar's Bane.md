# Scholar's Bane  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 53

**Complexity** Complex

Illusion magic overwhelms the minds of particularly intelligent creatures, forcing them to relive every intellectual failure and shame they’ve ever sufered.

---

**Stealth**
+14 (trained) to notice magical sensors hidden within four of the carvings’ eyes

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to carve out one eye sensor, DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to blank out one's mind (disables trap for self only), or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level; counteract DC 20) to counteract one eye sensor

**AC** 20, **Fort** +12, **Ref** +8

**Eye Hardness**
6

**Eye HP**
20

**Reveal Failure** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature with an Intelligence score of 10 or greater enters the room; **Effect** The trap flls the triggering creature's mind with shameful memories. The creature must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or take 2d8+2 mental damage. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) The trap uses each action to target a creature in the room with an Intelligence score of 10 or greater. A target must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or take 2d8+2 mental damage. On a critical failure, the creature takes a –10-foot status penalty to Speed for 1 minute as they stagger under the weight of their failures. The trap loses 1 action for each eye sensor disabled or destroyed.

---
**Reset**
The trap can function for 10 rounds, which don’t need to be consecutive, before it must recharge for 24 hours.
