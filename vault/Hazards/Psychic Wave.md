# Psychic Wave  *Hazard 21*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Simple

A wave of psychic energy ripples through the room, violently prying into the minds of creatures in the area and alerting Kemnebi to the presence of intruders.

---

**Stealth**
DC 53 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 50 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (legendary) to stymie the psychic energy before it manifests

**Psychic Assault** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Equipment/Cat|evocation]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature has been in the room for more than 1 round; **Effect** A wave of psychic energy slams into the minds of creatures in the chamber, dealing 8d10+48 mental damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, a creature is also stunned 3.

---
**Reset**
1 hour.
