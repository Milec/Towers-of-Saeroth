# Phantom Bells  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 28

**Complexity** Complex

The echo of diabolic bells reverberates with a bone-shaking clamor.

---

**Stealth**
+10

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Religion]] DC 27 (expert) to break the curse with recitations against the power of [[Setting/Planes/Hell]] (2 actions), or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] DC 22 (expert) to [[Actions/Perform]] an opposing composition

**Distant Ringing** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]); **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 30 feet. **Effect** Sounds of creaking metal fill the area as the phantom bells begin to swing and the haunt rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The bells toll, dealing 4d6 sonic damage to each living creature within 30 feet (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
The bells cease ringing if there are no living creatures within 30 feet
