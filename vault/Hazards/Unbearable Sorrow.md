# Unbearable Sorrow  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 34

**Complexity** Simple

The PCs hear the sound of sobbing and feel an overwhelming sense of sorrow, as if they'd lost something more precious to them than their own life.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (master)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] (master) to laugh away the sadness, DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) to banish the psychic imprint, DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to disperse the emotional energy

**Mourn** ⤾ ([[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A living creature approaches the statue; **Effect** The trap deals 4d10+14 mental damage to all creatures in the room (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, a creature additionally falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and begins to weep, taking 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]].

---
**Reset**
The trap resets automatically after 24 hours.
