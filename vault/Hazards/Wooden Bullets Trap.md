# Wooden Bullets Trap  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -178- Punks in a Powderkeg|Pathfinder #178: Punks in a Powderkeg]] pg. 35

**Complexity** Complex

A hopper full of wooden sling bullets is attached to several launching devices, which spew the bullets at high velocity through various holes in the boards covering the window. A distinctive “click” and whirring sound indicate the trap has been sprung.

---

**Stealth**
+10 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to reinforce the porch pressure sensor or DC 15 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (trained) to cover the holes launching the bullets

**AC** 10, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +5

**Wall Hardness**
10

**HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Fire|fire]] 5

**Auto-Sling** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature or object weighing at least 50 pounds is placed on the porch (marked “T” on the map on page 32); **Effect** A stream of wooden sling bullets flies from a launcher. The trap Strikes the two nearest creatures and then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) The wooden bullets trap Strikes the two nearest creatures. It ignores prone creatures as the bullets fly overhead.  
 **Ranged** single action wooden bullet +12 ([[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Range|range 10 feet]]), **Damage** 1d6+6 bludgeoning

---
**Reset**
The trap stops after 10 rounds or when the weight on the porch is removed, whichever comes first. The trap can operate for a total of 10 rounds before its ammunition is exhausted.
