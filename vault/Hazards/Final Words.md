# Final Words  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 65

**Complexity** Simple

As a creature touches a hand-written letter, they witness the letter's author scribe it in the past; each pen stroke across the page carves the letter's text into the creature's flesh.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (trained) to hear the scratch of a quill upon paper

**Disable**
DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ward off the haunt or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to dispose of the letter with extreme care before the haunt can form

**Carve in Flesh** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]]) **Trigger** A creature reads the letter; **Effect** The haunt deals 2d8+11 slashing damage to the triggering creature as the words are carved into its flesh (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the creature takes 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

---
**Reset**
1 minute
