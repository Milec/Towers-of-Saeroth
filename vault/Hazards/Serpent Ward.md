# Serpent Ward  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 9

**Complexity** Complex

Illusionary snakes erupt from the trapped door, inflicting a venomous curse on intruders.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) to deactivate the magical trigger

**Curse the Intruders** ⤾ ([[Traits/Curse|curse]], [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** The trapped door is opened other than by a [[Bestiary/Families/Serpentfolk|serpentfolk]]; **Effect** Hissing, illusionary snakes leap from the door. Each living creature within 30 feet is subjected to the [[Rules/Curses/Curse of Potent Poison|curse of potent poison]]. If a living creature opened the door, that creature must roll its saving throw against the curse twice and take the worse result (this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect).
