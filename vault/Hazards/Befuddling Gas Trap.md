# Befuddling Gas Trap  *Hazard 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 52

**Complexity** Complex

Seven nozzles hidden within holes in the 15‑foot-high ceiling release a poison gas.

---

**Stealth**
+21 (expert) or DC 31 (expert) to notice the hidden override mechanism on the north wall.

**Disable**
DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to disable the hidden mechanism on the north wall, immediately sucking the gas from the room, or DC 29 Thievery (expert) to adequately plug one of the seven nozzles. When all seven nozzles are plugged or destroyed, the trap is deactivated.

**AC** 31, **Fort** +24, **Ref** +18

**Nozzle Hardness**
20

**Nozzle HP**
32 (BT 16)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Gas Release** ⤾ ([[Traits/Inhaled (GM Core)|inhaled]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Trigger** Both secret doors are closed and at least one creature is in the room; **Effect** Gas fills the chamber. Creatures within the chamber must succeed on a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The gas intensifies. Each creature in the room must make a DC 30 Fortitude save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to the gas for 1 hour, though if the creature has already been [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] by the trap, that condition remains for its normal duration.  
**Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Failure** The creature becomes stupefied 1 for 24 hours. If the target is already stupefied, the condition value increases by 1 (to a maximum of stupefied 4) and the target takes 8d6 mental damage.  
**Critical Effect** As failure, except the target takes double the mental damage.

---
**Reset**
After an hour, the trap deactivates; the gas disperses slowly, and the doors can be opened again. After 24 hours, the gas builds up and the trap can be triggered again.
