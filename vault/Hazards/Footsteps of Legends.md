# Footsteps of Legends  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 31

**Complexity** Simple

The characters witness Burning Mammoths walking this mountain pass in ages long past.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] (trained) to convince the spirits to remain dormant, or DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ritually silence the spirits

**Ancestral Journey** ⤾ **Trigger** A good-aligned spiritual descendant of the Burning Mammoth following enters the mountain pass; **Effect** Burning Mammoths from long ago appear alongside the party, walking the mountain pass as one united following. They walk in the footsteps of ancestors, heroes, and legends. Each creature that witnesses the vision gains a +1 status bonus to Will saving throws and all skill checks for 24 hours.

---
**Reset**
1 hour
