# Rage of the Sky Father  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 7

**Complexity** Complex

Ominous storm clouds gather overhead, pulsating with flashes of blue-white lightning.

---

**Stealth**
+18 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (trained) to petition [[Setting/Deities/Gozreh (Divine Mysteries)|Gozreh]] for safe passage or 28 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to disperse the storm’s magical energies. A PC whose voice is exceptionally loud (such as using the [[Spells/Bullhorn (Player Core 2)|_bullhorn_]] cantrip) can also attempt to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable]] this hazard with a DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check to convince the Gozren priests that they mean no harm; the priests make a second prayer to dissipate the hazard on the following round, though they’re unable to stop the sargassum heaps.

**Thunderclap** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) **Trigger** A creature not designated by Katlanthe approaches within 10 feet of the circle of stone columns; **Effect** The storm clouds release a boom of thunder. Creatures atop the plateau take 2d10+9 sonic damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1d4 rounds. The hazard rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) A bolt of lightning strikes a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] atop the plateau of the GM’s choosing. Creatures in the area take 2d10+9 electricity damage (DC 28 Reflex save). On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1. Creatures designated by Katlanthe within the area are infused with divine energy for 1 round. They gain the electricity trait and [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] to electricity damage, and all their attacks deal an additional 1d6 electricity damage.

---
**Reset**
The storm clouds disperse once no creature other than those designated by Katlanthe are atop the plateau.
