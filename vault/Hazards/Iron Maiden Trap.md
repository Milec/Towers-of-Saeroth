# Iron Maiden Trap  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 52

**Complexity** Simple

A pressure plate hidden on the floor before an iron maiden causes the torture device to snap shut and trap the triggering creature inside.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to disable the pressure plate

**AC** 25, **Fort** +0, **Ref** +0

**Pressure Plate Hardness**
6

**Pressure Plate HP**
24 (BT 12); **Iron Maiden** Hardness 12; **Iron Maiden** HP 46 (BT 23)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Slam Shut** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps onto the trap's pressure plate (indicated on the map); **Effect** The front of the iron maiden slams shut with incredible force and locks itself. The triggering creature takes 4d10+10 piercing damage and 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save; if the creature critically succeeds, they avoid the trap completely). The triggering creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]]. The victim can end their immobilization only once a creature outside the iron maiden unlocks the device (requiring two successful DC 28 Thievery checks to [[Actions/Disable a Device]]) or breaks the iron maiden, after which the trapped creature must succeed at a DC 25 check to [[Actions/Escape]] the trap's spike-lined interior.

---
**Reset**
The pressure plate must be reset manually.
