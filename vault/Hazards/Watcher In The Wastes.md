# Watcher In The Wastes  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Nightmare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 79

**Complexity** Simple

A massive sculpture of a wolflike form as tall as a mountain guards passage to the Cold Wastes at the northern end of Leng and seems almost ready to come to life at the approach of any creatures.

---

**Stealth**
DC 48 (master) to realize the mountain is dangerous (DC 0 to see the towering statue in the first place)

**Disable**
DC 51 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] (master) to avoid the initial notice of the gigantic form, or DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (legendary) to know the proper posture and hand sign to make to turn away the watcher’s attention

**Dreadful Notice** ⤾ (incapacitation, mental, occult, visual); **Trigger** A creature comes within a mile of the mountain-sized statue’s base; **Effect** While the Watcher does not actually animate, it fills the triggering creature’s mind with the dread conviction that it might do so at any moment. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 42 Will save, after which they are temporarily immune to that particular Watcher’s Dreadful Notice for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 24 hours.  

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 3 (unlimited duration).  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] with fear with an unlimited duration; as long as the creature remains paralyzed, they need not drink or eat. If they are brought to a location where they no longer have line of sight to the watcher, the paralyzed creature can attempt a new Will save every day to recover from the paralysis, but during this time they are subject to thirst and starvation as normal.

---
**Reset**
The nightmare resets immediately.
