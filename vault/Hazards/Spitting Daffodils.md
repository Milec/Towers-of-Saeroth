# Spitting Daffodils  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 25

**Complexity** Simple

Daffodils spit acidic nectar at non-plant creatures in the area.

---

**Stealth**
DC 21 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to remove the plant's motion sensing filaments

**AC** 24, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +17

**HP**
54 (BT 27)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] 5

**Spit Necter** ⤾ ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Trigger** A non-[[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] creature comes within 10 feet; **Effect** The daffodils spit nectar at all non-plant creatures within 10 feet, dealing 4d8+18 acid damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The daffodils replenish their nectar and filaments after 24 hours.
