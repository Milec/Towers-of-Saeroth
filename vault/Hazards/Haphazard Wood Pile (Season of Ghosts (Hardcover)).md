# Haphazard Wood Pile  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 40

**Complexity** Simple

Beams of heavy and sharp wood rest against one another to form a precariously balanced cage tied to several trip lines and tension-bearing dowels.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained) to notice how the pile might fall on a person disabling it; DC 0 to see the pile

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained), [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (trained), or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Engineering Lore]] (trained) to figure out how to safely dismantle the pile without it collapsing on anyone.

**Collapse Inward** ⤾ **Trigger** Any of the four revelers within the hazard perform any action with the Move trait; **Effect** The pile collapses onto the four revelers, dealing 1d6+5 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to each of them. All four also take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bludgeoning damage]] until they’re pulled from the rubble with a DC 17 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check or can [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (DC 17).  
**Collapse Outward** ⤾ **Trigger** An attempt to disable the hazard from outside critically fails, or someone attempts to clamber into the pile of lumber; **Effect** The pile collapses outward, inflicting 1d6+5 bludgeoning damage to the four revelers (DC 15  [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save), and 2d6+5 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 10-foot area surrounding the central 10-foot-square where the revelers were trapped.
