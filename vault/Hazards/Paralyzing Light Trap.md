# Paralyzing Light Trap  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 52

**Complexity** Complex

When any creature other than a devil enters the light, the magic interwoven in the light holds the creature in place and rings an alarm.

---

**Stealth**
DC +18 (expert)

**Disable**
**Disable** DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to distort or diffuse the light, keeping the light cone intact but preventing the trap from triggering, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; counteract DC 26) to dispel the light, leaving the room in darkness

**Stasis Field** ⤾ ([[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]); **Trigger** A non-[[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]] creature moves into the light; **Effect** The light expands to fill the room, and each nondevil creature within the room must attempt a DC 26 Will save. A high-pitched chime sounds in the barracks (area **D15**), audible in this room as well. The trap then rolls initiative.  
**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 2.  
**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 1d4 rounds and paralyzed for 1 round.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) All [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] creatures in the room take 1d10 mental damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). Creatures who fail also become paralyzed for 1 round but can use a reaction to mentally fight off this stasis; creatures who do so take 5d10 mental damage but are no longer paralyzed.

---
**Reset**
The stasis magic in the light builds up over the course of an hour, after which the trap can trigger again
