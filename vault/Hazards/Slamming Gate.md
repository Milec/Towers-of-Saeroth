# Slamming Gate  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 17

**Complexity** Simple

The heavy gate slams shut when opened more than halfway.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 15 (trained) to loosen the gate's springs before it slams closed

**AC** 16, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +2

**Hardness**
5

**HP**
20 (BT 10)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Slam Shut** ⤾ **Trigger** The door is opened more than halfway; **Effect** The door slams shut and deals 3d8 bludgeoning damage to any creature in the way (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by the closed door until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 17)

---
**Reset**
The trap can be reactivated (or deactivated, if it's active) by flipping a lever hidden in the nearby wall. A character [[Actions/Search|Searching]] the area finds it with a successful DC 18 Perception check.
