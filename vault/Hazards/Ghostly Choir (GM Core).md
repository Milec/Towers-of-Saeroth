# Ghostly Choir  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 102

**Complexity** Simple

A choir of lost souls rises out of the floor, singing an eerie chant that terrifies its listeners and buffets their bodies with walls of sound.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (trained) to disrupt the song’s resonance with another tune or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (trained) to ritually silence the spirits

**Profane Chant** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|moves]] within 10 feet of the section of floor from which the choir can arise; **Effect** The choir rises, and its song deals 4d8+18 mental damage to living creatures within 30 feet of the souls' spectral forms. Affected creatures must each attempt a DC 24 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage. It also becomes frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.
