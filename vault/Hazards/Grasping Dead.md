# Grasping Dead  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 68

**Complexity** Complex

Hands of the buried dead rise from the ground, grabbing and tearing at creatures in the area to drag them underground.

---

**Stealth**
+12 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ritually pray for the dead or DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to exorcise the spirits' anger; two total successes are required to disable the haunt

**Shifting Earth** ⤾ **Trigger** At least two creatures enter the area; **Effect** The earth shifts wildly as the hands of the dead spring forth from the ground. The area becomes difficult terrain. Creatures within the area are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] unless they succeed at a DC 26 Reflex save. The haunt then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The grasping hands batter all creatures in the area, dealing 2d6+7 bludgeoning damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature is dragged partially into the earth, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 26). If already immobilized, it's fully submerged and must hold its breath to avoid [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocation]].

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates 1 minute after all living creatures leave the area. After 1 hour, the haunt reactivates.
