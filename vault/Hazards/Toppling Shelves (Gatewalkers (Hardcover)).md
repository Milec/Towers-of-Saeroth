# Toppling Shelves  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 124

**Complexity** Simple

This stone bookshelf is badly compromised. It’s just one push away from toppling over.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (trained) to notice the shelf’s precarious instability

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Engineering Lore]] (trained) to shore up the shelf

**AC** 18, **Fort** +8, **Ref** +8

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
30 (BT 15), any attack that fails to destroy the shelf triggers its Topple reaction

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Topple** ⤾ **Trigger** The shelf is attacked or touched forcefully (such as by a creature [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] into it); **Effect** The shelf tumbles over in the direction opposite its triggering creature, crashing onto the ground in a 10-foot-by-10-foot area. Creatures in the area take 4d8+10 bludgeoning damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that critically fails its save falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. The affected area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].  
If the toppling shelf crashes into a space occupied by another toppling shelf, it triggers that shelf’s Topple reaction. This can result in a chain reaction that causes multiple shelves to Topple, one after the other.
