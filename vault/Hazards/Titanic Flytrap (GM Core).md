# Titanic Flytrap  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 105

**Complexity** Simple

On the surface, a titanic flytrap appears to be a patch of the more common flytrap plant, but beneath murky waters it hides a far larger set of jaws, reaching 10 feet across, reinforced with woody branches and lined with paralytic hairs.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to mislead the flytrap’s sense of weight and pressure

**AC** 21, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +8

**HP**
56 (BT 28)

**Immunities**
[[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]

**Resistances**
[[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid 20]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire 10]]

**Snap Shut** ⤾ **Trigger** A Small or Medium creature moves into a square that is within reach of the flytrap’s hidden jaws; **Effect** The flytrap’s jaws snap shut, making a jaws [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the triggering creature.

**Melee** single action jaws +17, **Effect** devour

**Devour** The target is trapped by the flytrap’s jaws, gaining the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] condition until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 21). Additionally, it is exposed to the titanic flytrap toxin from the hundreds of tiny hairs that line the inside of its leaves. If the flytrap’s jaws Strike was a critical success, the target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its saving throws against this poison. At the end of each of the target’s turns that it remains grabbed, the target takes 3d6 [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage.

**Titanic Flytrap Toxin** ([[Traits/Contact (GM Core)|contact]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 21 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d6 poison damage and stunned 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 4d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] (1 round)

---
**Reset**
1 hour (or longer, after a large meal)
