# Unwelcome Guests  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 20

**Complexity** Simple

Unwelcome guests who touch this hatch are cursed.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to drain the magic from the curse, DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to unweave the  curse's bindings, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 28) to counteract the curse

**AC** 27, **Fort** +19, **Ref** +13

**Curse Trespassers** ⤾ ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the hatch; **Effect** The triggering creature takes 3d10+22 force damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|knocked back]] 10 feet. A creature that succeeds at a DC 30 Will save takes half damage and isn't moved. On a failure, the creature is additionally cursed. A cursed creature takes a –2 penalty to Will saves, and whenever the cursed creature becomes affected by a spell, the creature takes 1d10 force damage. The curse remains until removed by [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] or similar magic.

---
**Reset**
The trap resets when the hatch is shut.
