# Dahak's Shell  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -146- Cult of Cinders|Pathfinder #146: Cult of Cinders]] pg. 55

**Complexity** Simple

A ring of eight dragon pillar replicas mark the border of a dome of magical prismatic energy that entirely contains the Fortress of Sorrow.

---

**Stealth**
+0 or [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]]

**Disable**
Each [[Hazards/Dragon Pillar|dragon pillar]] that is destroyed removes the corresponding color's effect from Dahak's shell; as long as even one color remains active, the pillars marking the shell's border cannot be damaged themselves, but a successful DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) check or a successful [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 30) against one of the eight pillars can cause a randomly determined active color in the shell defense (below) to become deactivated for 1d4 rounds.

**Shell Defense** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Light|light]]) When a creature physically passes through the shell, the creature is affected simultaneously by all colors of energy currently active in the shell; apply these effects in the following order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet, black. These effects are the same as that of the eye beams described on the [[Hazards/Dragon Pillar|dragon pillar hazard]], but with a save DC of 32. Each effect requires its own save.

**Prismatic Beam** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Light|light]]); **Trigger** a creature attempts and fails to disable one of the shell colors via Thievery or dispel magic; **Effect** Dahak's shell Strikes the triggering creature with a random eye beam. Roll 1d8 to determine the color (1–red, 2–orange, 3–yellow, 4–green, 5–blue, 6–indigo, 7–violet, 8–black).

 **Ranged** single action eye beam +20 ([[Traits/Range|range 120 feet]]), **Effect** The target suffers an effect corresponding to the eye beam's color, as described on the [[Hazards/Dragon Pillar|dragon pillar hazard]], but with a save DC of 32. On a critical hit, the target's save result is one degree worse.
