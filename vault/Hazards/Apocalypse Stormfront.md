# Apocalypse Stormfront  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Nightmare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 70

**Complexity** Complex

An endless void stretches in all directions. Visions of Golarion’s destruction roll like storm clouds across the space.

---

**Stealth**
+37 (legendary)

**Disable**
DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] to Fly through the space without becoming stuck in the webs, DC 50 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to Climb the webs and make progress forward through the infinite, DC 47 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] or DC 50 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (legendary) to collapse the extradimensional space, or DC 52 Perception to peer through the visions of Golarion to notice discrepancies that help keep the overwhelming despair from setting in; six successes disables the hazard, causing it to vanish forever and shunting all creatures to Liralarue’s Prison.

**Brewing Storm** ⤾ (occult) **Trigger** A creature enters the shaft leading to area **C8**; **Effect** Storm clouds roiling with gouts of fire, bolts of lightning, and the wails of uncountable souls form in the distance. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) (arcane, emotion, illusion, mental) A stormfront filled with illusory scenes of Golarion’s destruction rolls through the extradimensional space. Each creature in the hazard’s area takes 6d12 mental damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 (doomed 2 on a critical failure). A PC reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage is shunted back into area **C7**. A creature that takes damage while Climbing the webs must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save or fall. A creature can try to [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] (Reflex DC 40). On a success, they lose one success they’ve earned to escape the hazard but otherwise arrest their fall. On a failure, they plummet into the void, losing all Athletics successes they’ve earned to escape the hazard. Each round on their turn, the character can try to Grab an Edge again, but each time they fail to do so, the party must earn an additional success to escape the hazard. Characters who fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] or repeatedly fail to Grab an Edge might effectively become lost in the void, though once the nightmare hazard is disabled, they appear along with the rest of the PCs in Liralarue’s Prison.

---
**Reset**
The hazard resets automatically once no creatures are in the area.
