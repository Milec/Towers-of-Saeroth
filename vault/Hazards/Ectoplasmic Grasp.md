# Ectoplasmic Grasp  *Hazard 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 66

**Complexity** Simple

A massive ectoplasmic hand forms around a creature and squeezes it.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (trained) to notice ectoplasm coalescing

**Disable**
DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (trained) to send the ectoplasm back to the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]] or DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] to move through and disrupt the coalescing ectoplasm before it takes hold

**Squeeze** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature moves over the haunt's area; **Effect** The hand forms out of ectoplasm and squeezes the triggering creature, dealing 3d8+14 bludgeoning damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] ([[Actions/Escape]] DC 22).

---
**Reset**
1 day
