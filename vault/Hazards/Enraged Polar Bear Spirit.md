# Enraged Polar Bear Spirit  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 42

**Complexity** Complex

The ghostly spirit of a polar bear stands on its hind legs and roars.

---

**Stealth**
+23

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (expert) to calm the spirit, DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] (master) to cow the spirit, or DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to contain the spiritual corruption (each successful check reduces its number of actions by 1)

**Feral Roar** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature approaches within 10 feet of the polar bear spirit; **Effect** The polar bear spirit roars. Each creature in the room must attempt a DC 28 Will save. The enraged polar bear spirit rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature takes 6d6 sonic damage and is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 12d6 sonic damage and is frightened 3.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The bear moves around the room at an alarming speed, seemingly teleporting, and Strikes a random creature with its spiritual claws.  
 **Melee** single action spiritual claws +21 ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]]); **Damage** 2d8+5 force plus 2d8 mental

---
**Reset**
The bear spirit calms after no creatures are in the room for 1 minute but is ready to lash out again after 10 minutes.
