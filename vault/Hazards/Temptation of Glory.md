# Temptation of Glory  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 60

**Complexity** Complex

Ghostly images of your heart’s desire appear and beckon you toward them.

---

**Stealth**
+18 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 25 (expert) to disrupt the images; [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] DC 26 (expert) to shout a countering chant; [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] DC 27 (expert) to scare the images away; Will DC 28 (expert) to refuse to listen

**Tempt** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A PC moves down the exit hallway; **Effect** The ghostly images turn cruel and hungry, grasping and slashing at the PC

---
**Routine**
(1 per PC) The hazard targets a PC and uses cruel temptation  
**Cruel Temptation** single action claw +20, **Damage** 2d10+10 mental

---

**Special** The hazard continues each round until defeated, and the hallway’s end cannot be reached until the hazard is stopped.
