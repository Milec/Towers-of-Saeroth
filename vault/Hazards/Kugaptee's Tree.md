# Kugaptee's Tree  *Hazard 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 47

**Complexity** Complex

The branches of the stone tree statue animate, extend, and lash out at intruders.

---

**Stealth**
+15 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to appear overwhelmingly strong of body and mind

**AC** 22, **Fort** +18, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
14

**HP**
50 (BT 25)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
positive 10

**Pulse of Fear** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** An intruder to the monastery begins their turn in area **E3**; **Effect** The stone statue lurches to life, its branches whipping through the air as a horrifying roar rips through the monastery—a roar that seems to come from underground. All intruders within the monastery must attempt a DC 21 Will save. The haunt then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for as long as it remains frightened.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) Kugaptee's tree lashes out with its branches, using its three actions to attempt branch Strikes against any creature in reach. Targets that are within reach but are outside of area **E3** have cover from the surrounding archways. The haunt favors targets in area **E3** but otherwise selects its targets randomly.

 **Melee** branch +17 (magical, reach 40 feet), Damage 1d8+9 bludgeoning plus 1d8 mental

---
**Reset**
1 hour
