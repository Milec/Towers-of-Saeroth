# Disembodied Voices  *Hazard 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 64

**Complexity** Simple

Angry spirits breathe down the necks of intruders and whisper haunting threats or messages in their ears.

---

**Stealth**
DC 16 (trained) to feel breath upon your neck

**Disable**
DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ward yourself from evil or DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] to drown out the spirits' voices

**Whisper** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature enters the haunt's area; **Effect** The haunt deals 1d6+4 mental damage to creatures in the area (DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].

---
**Reset**
1 day
