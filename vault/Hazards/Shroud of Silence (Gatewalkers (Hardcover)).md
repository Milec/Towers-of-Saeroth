# Shroud of Silence  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 76

**Complexity** Simple

Sounds become muted in a 20-foot-wide, 100-foot-long segment of this street corridor.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to twist the magic of the ley line around you, disabling the hazard for only you; or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 23) to disable the hazard permanently

**Hush** ⤾ ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature reaches the street’s halfway point; **Effect** Each creature in the affected area must succeed at a DC 23 Will saving throw or be silenced as long as it remains in the area. A silenced creature makes no sound and can’t use abilities with the [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] or [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] traits, including casting spells that don’t have the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait.
