# Dream-Poisoned Door  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 54

**Complexity** Simple

Eerie shapes manifest within the doorway as it's opened, conjuring a group of four animate dreams into being.

---

**Stealth**
DC 35 (master)

**Disable**
DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) to concentrate on the eldritch energies and diffuse them through meditation, DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to scratch warding runes along the door's hinges and block dreams from manifesting, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (6th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 30) to counteract the trap

**Manifest Dream Guardians** ⤾ ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** The door is opened; **Effect** A group of four [[Bestiary/Animate Dream|animate dreams]] manifest in the area just beyond the door. These four animate dreams each appear as vaguely whale-shaped but human-sized masses of tendrils. They swim through the air and attack the intruders at once, fighting to the death and pursuing any who flee the temple.
