# Forest Fire  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 35

**Complexity** Complex

A 10-foot-by-10-foot bonfire spreads to the surrounding forest on each of its turns.

---

**Stealth**
-10

**Disable**
A successful DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], or [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] check from an adjacent square is sufficient to smother one 5-foot-square of fire; each attempt is an Interact action. Dousing the flames automatically extinguishes one or more sections of fire, with no check. Water typically clears a 5-foot square if the amount is small (such as from a spell like [[Spells/Create Water|_create water_]] or [[Spells/Hydraulic Push|_hydraulic push_]]). Larger amounts of water, such as a full bucket, typically douse a 10-foot-by-10-foot area (or 4 squares in some other shape). Throwing a bucket of water on flames requires an Interact action. A [[Equipment/Waterskin|waterskin]] doesn't contain enough water to put out even 1 square of fire. Cold can also put out fire, but only if the cold can affect an area; cold is less effective than water, so a [[Equipment/Frost Vial|frost vial]] puts out only 1 square of fire, and a [[Spells/Ray of Frost|_ray of frost_]] is ineffective.

**Stoke Flames** ⤾ **Trigger** A [[Bestiary/Brimorak|brimorak]] urges the fire to spread out of control; **Effect** The bonfire becomes a forest fire and rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
On its turn, the fire spreads into a number of additional squares equal to half the number of squares the fire currently occupies (minimum 1 square). You determine the squares the fire spreads into. Any creature that ends its turn next to the flames takes 2d8+5 fire damage, or 4d8+10 fire damage if it ended its turn within the flames (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save in either case). A creature can take damage from the forest fire only once per round.
