# Haphazard Wood Pile  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -196- The Summer That Never Was|Pathfinder #196: The Summer That Never Was]] pg. 22

**Complexity** Simple

Beams of heavy and sharp wood rest against one another to form a precariously balanced cage tied to several trip lines and tension-bearing dowels.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained) to notice how the pile might fall on a person disabling it; DC 0 to see the pile.

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained), [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] (trained), or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Engineering Lore]] (trained) to figure out how to safely dismantle the pile without it collapsing on anyone.

**Collapse Inward** ⤾ **Trigger** Any of the four revelers within the hazard perform any action with the [[Traits/Move]] trait; **Effect** The pile collapses onto the four revelers, dealing 1d6+5 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to each of them. All four also take 1d6 persistent [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|bludgeoning damage]] until they're pulled from the rubble with a DC 17 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check or can [[Actions/Escape]] (DC 17).  
 **Collapse Outward** ⤾ **Trigger** An attempt to disable the hazard from outside critically fails, or the hazard is intentionally triggered by someone from outside of the wood pile; **Effect** The pile collapses outward, inflicting 1d6+5 bludgeoning damage to the four revelers (DC 15 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save), and 2d6+5 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 10-foot area surrounding the central 10-foot-square where the revelers were trapped.
