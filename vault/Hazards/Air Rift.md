# Air Rift  *Hazard 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 17

**Complexity** Complex

A portal to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air]] rips open onstage, causing roaring winds to tear through the opera house.

---

**Stealth**
+22 (master) to notice the sudden breeze in the theater

**Disable**
DC 38 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (master) to sew the magical threads of the rift shut, DC 40 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (legendary) to open enough windows and doors to reduce its intensity, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (7th level; counteract DC 34) to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the air rift

**Earth Vulnerability** The Elemental [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air]] is opposed by the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth]]. Any spell or magical effect with the [[Magic/Domains/Earth|earth]] trait that targets a creature or area within 50 feet of the rift deals 10 damage to the rift per spell levels (or per 2 levels for magic items, feats, and so forth). For example, a 3rd-level [[Spells/Earthbind|_earthbind_]] spell cast on a creature near the air rift deals 30 points of damage to the rift.

**AC** 35, **Fort** +30

**HP**
220

**Weaknesses**
earth vulnerabilty

**Weightlessness** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature comes within 50 feet of the rift; **Effect** The air rift creates a shroud of [[Traits/Subjective Gravity|subjective gravity]] around the creature for 1 minute. The creature can use a free action, which has the [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=683) trait, to choose any direction they wish for gravity to pull them. This allows a creature to Stride along any surface or [[Actions/Fly]] in a straight line by choosing a direction into midair. For unattended objects and [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] creatures, this is [[Traits/Microgravity|microgravity]]: they float in place unless pushed off a surface.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) Winds blow through the area in a random direction, causing creatures and objects within 50 feet of the air rift to float up, down, north, south, east, or west (roll a d6 to determine which direction). Creatures and objects in the area must attempt a DC 37 Fortitude save. If a creature is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] into a solid object or another creature by this effect, both take 3d8+15 bludgeoning damage on impact.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is pushed 10 feet, but takes only half damage from impact.  
**Failure** The creature is pushed 20 feet, falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], and takes full damage from impact.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is pushed 40 feet, falls prone, and takes double damage from impact.
