# Envenomed Thorns Trap  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -151- The Show Must Go On|Pathfinder #151: The Show Must Go On]] pg. 13

**Complexity** Simple

A trip wire strung 4 feet above the ground releases a branch studded with envenomed thorns.

---

**Stealth**
DC 20 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 18 (trained) to remove the trip wire.

**AC** 18, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +4

**Hardness**
5

**HP**
28 (BT 14)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Thorny Branch** ⤾ ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]); **Trigger** The trip wire is touched; **Effect** The trap's poisoned thorns attack the creature or object touching the trip wire.  
 **Melee** poisoned thorns +14, **Damage** 1d6+4 piercing plus 1d8 poison
