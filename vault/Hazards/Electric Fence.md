# Electric Fence  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 54

**Complexity** Simple

A locked, wrought iron fence with bars spaced inches apart is magically electrified.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 28 (expert) to disconnect the fence from its magitech power source

**AC** 25, **Fort** +20, **Ref** +14

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
60 (BT 30)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Shock** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature touches the fence directly or with a tool or weapon; **Effect** The fence deals 6d12 electricity damage to the triggering creature (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex).

The trap automatically resets. If deactivated or disabled, the trap doesn’t reset.
