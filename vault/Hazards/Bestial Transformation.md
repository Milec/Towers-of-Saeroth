# Bestial Transformation  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 40

**Complexity** Simple

Enchanted carvings of animals transform any creatures who damage the door into the animal they harmed.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (master)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (expert) to handle the animal carvings or DC 33 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to scratch out the animal carvings or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (5th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 28) to counteract the spell

**Animal Form** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature damages the door; **Effect** The trap casts [[Spells/Bestial Curse|_bestial curse_]] on the triggering creature (DC 32 Fortitude save); on a critical failure, the duration is 2 hours.

---
**Reset**
automatic
