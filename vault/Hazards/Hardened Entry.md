# Hardened Entry  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 68

**Complexity** Complex

A portcullis drops from the ceiling, landing with a heavy thunk. Seconds later, a cloud of acid rises from a metal grate in the floor.

---

**Stealth**
+35 (master)

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to prevent the portcullis from dropping, DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (master) to create a compound to neutralize the cloud of acid, or DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to jam the pressure plate

**AC** 42, **Fort** +33, **Ref** +27

**Hardness**
29

**HP**
110 (BT 55)

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Falling Portcullis** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature Strides beneath the portcullis while it is raised; **Effect** The portcullis slams down, driving its sharpened ends through a grate in the floor. Creatures directly beneath the portcullis take 3d12+20 piercing damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The portcullis is made of [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] and has Hardness 31 and 120 HP (BT 60). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) A cloud of acidic vapor rises from the grate beneath the portcullis in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Any creature that enters the area or starts its turn inside the cloud takes 3d12+20 acid damage (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature that critically fails the save also takes 1d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]]. Each time the hazard uses this routine beyond the first, increase the radius of the emanation by 5 feet.

---
**Reset**
Once triggered, the trap must be manually reset by raising the portcullis. Once all the vials are smashed, the trap can’t use its routine again until the vials are replaced.
