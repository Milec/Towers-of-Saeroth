# Breakaway Ladder  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -214- The Broken Palace|Pathfinder #214: The Broken Palace]] pg. 15

**Complexity** Simple

The rope ladder rips free from its anchors and drops into the pit.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) or DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (trained) to secure the ladder’s anchors so they won’t break

**Rip Free** ⤾ **Trigger** More than 3 Small creatures or more than 1 Medium creature climbs the ladder at the same time; **Effect** The ladder’s anchors rip free from the wall, dropping the ladder and anyone on it into area **B1**. Anyone on the ladder falls 50 feet, taking falling damage as appropriate. A creature at the top of the ladder can attempt a DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save to [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] and cling to the side of the pit in area **A9**. A creature lower on the ladder can attempt to grab a handhold on the shaft’s wall, but in this case the check increases to DC 25.
