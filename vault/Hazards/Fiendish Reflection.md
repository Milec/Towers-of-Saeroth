# Fiendish Reflection  *Hazard 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 47

**Complexity** Simple

Reflections viewed in the water here appear monstrous and fiendish.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to exorcise the haunt, or DC 23 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to turn the fear back on the haunt and overwhelm it

**Frightening Visage** ⤾ ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature looks at the water; **Effect** The creature sees its reflection on the water's surface distort into a fiendish version of themselves; they must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 (and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for as long as they remain frightened on a critical failure).

---
**Reset**
The haunt resets immediately and can trigger again the next round.
