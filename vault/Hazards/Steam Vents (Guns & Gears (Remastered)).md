# Steam Vents  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Steam (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Steam]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 81

**Complexity** Simple

Large pressurized pipes connected to a trip wire are rigged to release a blast of steam on trespassers.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 22 (trained) to disconnect the trip wire or [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] DC 24 (expert) to carefully reduce the pressure and prevent the blast

**AC** 20, **Fort** +12, **Ref** +12

**Hardness**
12

**HP**
48 (BT 24)

**Immunities**
object immunities

**Steam Blast** ⤾ **Trigger** The trip wire is pulled or severed, typically because a creature walked through the square with the trip wire; **Effect** Steam erupts from the pipes, dealing 3d6 bludgeoning damage and 3d6 fire damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures within 15 feet. Creatures that critically fail their save are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
