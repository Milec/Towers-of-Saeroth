# Sigil of Deepest Fears  *Hazard 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 124

**Complexity** Simple

An arcane sigil forces creatures to confront their greatest fear.

---

**Stealth**
DC 43 (master)

**Disable**
DC 43 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to drain the sigil's power harmlessly, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (9th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 42) to counteract the sigil

**Face Your Fear** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature moves within 10 feet of the sigil; **Effect** All creatures within 120 feet of the sigil are affected by a [[Spells/Weird|_weird_]] spell (DC 40 Will save).

---
**Reset**
1 day
