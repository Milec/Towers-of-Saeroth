# Prism Ray Trap  *Hazard 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 8

**Complexity** Complex

Four magical prisms integrated into an _aeon stone matrix_ fire energy beams at intruders. The trap doesn't target creatures wearing [[Equipment/Security Badge|security badges]], or in the custody of a creature wearing a _security badge_.

---

**Stealth**
+16 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 23 (expert) to disconnect one prism from the _aeon stone matrix_, or [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] DC 21 (trained) to disrupt one prism’s magical energy, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 23) to counteract one prism; four total successes are required to disable the trap.

**AC** 22, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +9

**Prism Hardness**
12

**Prism HP**
14 (BT 7)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Opening Shot** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Trigger** A creature enters area **A1**, and that creature isn’t wearing a _security badge_ and isn’t in the custody of a creature wearing a _security badge_; **Effect** All four prisms light up, shedding dim light in 15 feet. The trap makes one energy ray Strike against the triggering creature.  
The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) Each prism uses 1 action to make an energy ray Strike against the nearest creature who is not wearing a _security badge_ or in the custody of a creature wearing a _security badge_. This trap doesn’t take a multiple attack penalty. This trap loses 1 action for each prism disabled or destroyed.

**Ranged** single action energy ray +15 ([[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile cold]]), **Damage** 1d6+5 fire

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets when there are no viable targets in area **A1**. Damaged prisms are fully repaired after 24 hours.
