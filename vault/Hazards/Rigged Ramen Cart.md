# Rigged Ramen Cart  *Hazard 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -173- Doorway to the Red Star|Pathfinder #173: Doorway to the Red Star]] pg. 13

**Complexity** Simple

A seemingly innocent ramen cart is rigged to detonate if moved from its current location.

---

**Stealth**
DC 35 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 40 (master) to disable the detonation trigger

**AC** 34, **Fort** +29, **Ref** +23

**Hardness**
25

**HP**
90 (BT 45)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Death by Noodles** ⤾ **Trigger** The cart is moved, damaged, or physically searched; **Effect** The cart explodes in a 40-foot radius, dealing 10d6 fire damage and 10d6 piercing damage to all creatures within the area (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures who fail their save against this effect are also covered in “noodles” of tar and other sticky alchemical substances that make the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] until they spend at least an hour scrubbing the noodles off.
