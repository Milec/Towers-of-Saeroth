# Trapped Gate  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 33

**Complexity** Simple

A hair trigger rings an alarm and launches a small assortment of wooden spikes to impale anyone attempting to open the gate incorrectly.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to disable the thin trigger that snaps when attempting to open the gate.

**AC** 16, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +2

**Hardness**
5

**HP**
20 (BT 10)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Impale Trespasser** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature attempts to open the gate; **Effect** A loud alarm rings and set of wooden spikes swings up from below and over the gate, dealing 3d8 piercing damage to the triggering creature (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). If the creature takes damage from the spikes, they also become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|Grabbed]] by the gate. The creature can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (DC 17). If the creature succeeded at their basic Reflex save, they gain a +1 circumstance bonus to their checks to Escape, as the spikes didn’t fully impale them. If the creature critically failed their basic Reflex save, they take a –2 circumstance penalty to their checks to Escape, as the spikes are embedded particularly deep.
