# Daemonic Fog  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 38

**Complexity** Simple

The fog solidifies into tiny, gnawing creatures that devour everyone within before fading back into mist.

---

**Stealth**
DC 30 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (expert) to disrupt the fog before the hazard triggers or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (5th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 26) to counteract the magic.

**Gnawing Fog** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]]) **Trigger** A creature crosses the cavern’s midpoint (the dotted line on the map); **Effect** The hazard deals 9d6 piercing damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to the triggering creature and all other non‑fiend creatures within 20 feet. A creature that critically fails the saving throw is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1d4 rounds.

---
**Reset**
The hazard resets over the course of an hour as the daemonic stain reenergizes the fog.
