# Blood Drain  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 37

**Complexity** Complex

A tiny spark of [[Setting/Deities/Nin|Nin’s]] will drains the blood from living intruders.

---

**Stealth**
+15 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to vandalize the space, or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 24 (expert) to pray to a god for passage, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (3rd rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 25) to counteract the magical energy

**Drain Blood** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Void|void]]); **Trigger** A living creature enters the room of their own accord; **Effect** The trap drains the blood from all living creatures in the room, which manifests as a bloody mist in the air. All living creatures in the room are dealt 2d6+8 void damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, a creature increases the value of their [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition by 1. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) On its initiative, the trap continues to drain blood, dealing 2d6+8 void damage to all living creatures in the room (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, a creature increases the value of their [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition by 1.

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically deactivates after 1 minute. It resets in 1 hour.
