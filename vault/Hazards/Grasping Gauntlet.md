# Grasping Gauntlet  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 70

**Complexity** Simple

A gauntlet of magical force clutches creatures trying to steal gear from the armory.

---

**Stealth**
DC 46 (master)

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to trick the hazard’s magic into nullifying itself, DC 41 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to wrestle the gauntlet into submission, DC 39 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to slide an object into the gauntlet’s grasp, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 41)

**Seize Thief** ⤾ ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the warded area; **Effect** A gauntleted hand reaches out from the ward and attempts a gauntlet Strike. The hazard has an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +37 for its [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 47).  
Melee single action gauntlet +37 ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]), Damage 10d6+35 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Improved Grab, Improved Knockdown, or Improved Push|Improved Grab]]

---
**Reset**
1 hour
