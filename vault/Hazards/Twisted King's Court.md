# Twisted King's Court  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 18

**Complexity** Complex

Countless fey with grisly visages and misshapen bodies cavort throughout the room, kicking up caustic debris and pulling other creatures into their dangerous dance.

---

**Stealth**
+20 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to perform a ritual to ward the area against  fey intrusion, DC 34 [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (master) to meet the Twisted King's demands with a disturbing dance, or DC 32 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (expert) to fend off or frighten the fey dancers. Three total successes are required to disable the haunt, and each check takes 2 actions.

**Welcome to the Feywrithe** ⤾ **Trigger** 1 round passes since both isqulugs in area **B6** are slain, or 10 rounds pass since they rolled initiative; **Effect** The isqulugs die horribly (if they're still alive), rent by clawed fey hands. Spectral fey pull at the other creatures in the caves, all of which must attempt a DC 36 Fortitude save. The haunt then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet toward the standing stones.  

**Failure** The creature is pulled 15 feet toward the standing stones.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is pulled 15 feet toward the standing stones, knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage (in addition to any damage from the toxic water).

---
**Routine**
(1 action) Spectral fey cavort about the room. All creatures in the room are raked by ghostly claws and splashed with toxic water, taking 1d10+14 force damage and 2d10 acid damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail their Reflex saves are [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet toward the standing stones.

---
**Reset**
The haunt deactivates after 1 minute. It re-forms every new moon. If all of the glyphs in area **B6** are restored—a process requiring 10 days of downtime and 10 successful DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] checks—the haunt becomes permanently disabled and doesn't re-form.
