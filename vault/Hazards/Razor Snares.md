# Razor Snares  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Simple

Slender razor wire snags a creature, yanking its feet to knock it down.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to snip the wire without getting snared

**AC** 21, **Fort** +15, **Ref** +8

**Hardness**
10

**HP**
40 (BT 20)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Snag** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps into the square with the snare tripwire; **Effect** The snare makes a snare Strike against the triggering creature.  
 **Melee** snare +15, **Damage** 4d6+10 slashing plus 1d6  [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed]], and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 23). On a critical hit, the target is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
