# Constricting Hall  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 124

**Complexity** Complex

A hall's four walls become fluid and elastic when a creature reaches the hall's midpoint.

---

**Stealth**
DC 31

**Disable**
DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (trained) to stabilize each wall, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 26) to counteract each wall

**Lose Form** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) **Trigger** A creature reaches the hall's midpoint; **Effect** The hall's walls lose their rigidity, becoming fluid and elastic. The hall becomes  difficult terrain, and each creature in the hall is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] unless they succeed at a DC 26 Reflex save. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) This trap loses 1 action each round for each wall that has been disabled. Each wall uses 1 action to slam back and forth rapidly, pummeling one creature in the hall with a wall Strike. On a hit, the target is additionally knocked prone. The walls distribute the attacks as evenly as possible among creatures in the hall, and the Strikes don't apply the trap's multiple attack penalty.  
 **Melee** single action wall +20, **Damage** 2d10+11 bludgeoning

---
**Reset**
1 hour
