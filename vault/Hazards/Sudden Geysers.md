# Sudden Geysers  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 61

**Complexity** Complex

The pooling seawater burbles and hisses.

---

**Stealth**
+17 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (master) to dissipate the fire-aligned energies in the water, or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to beseech Sangeh and convince her to momentarily tamp down her mythic power; two successes are required to disable the hazard

**Scalding Gout** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) **Trigger** A creature within the hazard’s area rolls initiative; **Effect** An enormous column of superheated steam rises from the water, dealing 4d12+26 fire damage to all creatures in the area (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] as the water continues to burn their skin. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) The hazard uses each of its actions to create a Blistering Plume centered on a space chosen by the GM. Each successful check to disable the hazard removes one action from this routine.  
**Blistering Plume** single action A 30-foot-tall geyser erupts from the boiling water. Creatures in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of the geyser take 2d12+13 fire damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
