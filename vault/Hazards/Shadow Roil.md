# Shadow Roil  *Hazard 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 53

**Complexity** Complex

A cyclone of living shadows and planar magic surges around the area, attracted to living creatures.

---

**Stealth**
+15 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (expert) to weaken the tornado; three successes in any combination are required to disable the trap.

**Touch Down** ⤾ **Trigger** Two or more living creatures enter the area; **Effect** A spiraling column of shadows and crackling planar magic gathers 20 feet away, becoming a whirling cyclone of darkness 15 feet wide and 100 feet tall. The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The cyclone uses 3 actions to move, traveling up to 30 feet with each action and dealing 2d12+13 slashing damage to each creature in its path (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature needs to attempt only one save during the cyclone's movement, even if the cyclone moves over its space more than once. On a critical failure, a creature is swept up into the cyclone, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 33). A creature grabbed by the cyclone moves along with the cyclone and takes 1d12+7 additional slashing damage at the beginning of its turn, and it must attempt the [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against the cyclone on the cyclone's turn, no matter where the cyclone moves. A creature that successfully Escapes from the cyclone [[Rules/Falling|falls]] from a height of 1d20 × 5 feet.

---
**Reset**
The cyclone disperses after 1 minute and resets after 1 day.
