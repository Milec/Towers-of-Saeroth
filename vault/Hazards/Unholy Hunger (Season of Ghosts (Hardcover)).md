# Unholy Hunger  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 137

**Complexity** Simple

An unspeakable miasma of foul-smelling vapor with shrieking faces barely visible in the steam bubbles up from the soup.

---

**Stealth**
DC 25 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 19 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Cooking Lore]] to neutralize the haunted soup with the addition of a few select spices from nearby shelves, or DC 25 Religion to exorcise the haunt.

**Worse Than it Smells** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Trigger** A non-[[Traits/Nindoru|nindoru]] creature takes the lid off the cooking pot; Effect All creatures within 15 feet of the pot must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2. The triggering creature instead must attempt a DC 24 Will save to resist the compulsion to dip their face into the stew and swallow a mouthful of the stuff (this effect has the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits). On a failure, the triggering creature takes 5d6 poison damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save).

---
**Reset**
The haunt resets 1 round after the lid is replaced on the cooking pot.
