# Echoes of Faith  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 29

**Complexity** Simple

The restless spirits of clergy who once inhabited this now-defiled temple of Aroden remain within the sanctuary and pulpit.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 Occultism (expert) to exorcise the spirits, or [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to imitate the Arodenite faith and render the haunt harmless until it resets

**AC** 24, **Fort** +13, **Will** +15

**HP**
60

**Immunities**
critical hits, physical damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Positive|positive]] 10

**Phantom Sermon** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** A non-Arodenite creature approaches within 5 feet of the lectern; **Effect** A spectral minister appears before the pulpit, resembling an ever-shifting amalgamation of several past priests. The spectral minister delivers fervent exhortations about faithlessness. Each creature on the dais must succeed at a DC 24 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]]. On a critical failure, the creature is drained 4.

---
**Reset**
The spirits return in 1d8 hours as long as any [[Bestiary/Families/Xulgath|xulgaths]] remain in Moonstone Hall.
