# Vision of Dahak  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -146- Cult of Cinders|Pathfinder #146: Cult of Cinders]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Complex

Blasts of fire and smoke pour out of the tunnel walls to coalesce into the burning form of [[Setting/Deities/Dahak]] himself; a PC who succeeds at a DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] notes that the fiery dragon closely resembles classical depictions of the draconic god of destruction.

---

**Stealth**
+16 (expert)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Religion]] DC 26 (expert) to utter prayers to a nonevil deity to counteract Dahak's presence (prayers to [[Setting/Deities/Apsu]] allow this check to be made if the character is merely trained in Religion), [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC 30 (expert) to divert the hazard's energies back upon themselves, or a successful [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 26).

**AC** 27, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +13

**HP**
130

**Immunities**
critical hits, [[Traits/Fire|fire]], object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Cold|cold]] 10

**Manifest** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature is within the way station. **Effect** The vision of Dahak appears in a 15-foot space adjacent to that creature and rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) The vision of Dahak manifests next to the creature that is closest to the center of the way station, then uses its Breath Weapon in a direction that catches the most possible targets in its area.

 **Breath Weapon** two actions ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Magic/Domains/Fire|fire]]) The vision of Dahak unleashes a blast of fire from its burning maw, creating a 60-foot cone that deals 6d6 fire damage to all creatures within (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The vision of Dahak deactivates and resets automatically once no creatures remain within the Huntergate way station.
