# Acidic Needle Launcher  *Hazard 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 54

**Complexity** Complex

A ceiling-mounted launcher rains resinous needles onto intruders, after which the needles melt into acid.

---

**Stealth**
DC 38 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (DC 36) to disable some firing mechanisms, making three adjacent squares in the trapped area safe to enter.

**AC** 39, **Fort** +30, **Ref** +22

**Hardness**
26

**HP**
104 (BT 52)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Needle Rain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Attack|attack]]) **Trigger** A creature moves into the area indicated on the map; **Effect** The trap shoots an acidic needle at a random target in the area indicated on the map, then rolls initiative.

 Ranged acidic needle +35, **Damage** 2d6 piercing plus 8d6 acid

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The acidic needle launcher fires a needle at a random target in the area indicated on the map.
