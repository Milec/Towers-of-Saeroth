# Witherweird Runes  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 43

**Complexity** Simple

Necromantic magic stored in runes painted in powdered bone and banshees’ tears lash out at anyone other than Heh Shan-Bao who opens the secondary compartment.

---

**Stealth**
DC 37 (master)

**Disable**
DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Disable Device]] to scratch out the runes and dissipate their magic harmlessly, DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] to sabotage the spell and cause the runes to dry up and blow away as inert powder, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (7th level; counteract DC 31) to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the runes

**Withering Wail** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Trigger** Someone other than Heh Shan-Bao opens the secondary compartment; **Effect** A howling, spectral face manifests out of the chest and screeches, causing all within area C4 to attempt a DC 33 Fortitude save; the sound also alerts the jiang-shi in areas C3a and C6, who quickly come to investigate the sound.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 7d6 negative damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 15d6 negative damage. In addition, their flesh withers and weakens, leaving them to appear old and decrepit—the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 until the curse is removed.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 30d6 negative damage and becomes cursed as failure, but becomes clumsy 2 and enfeebled 2.
