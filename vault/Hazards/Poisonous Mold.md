# Poisonous Mold  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 103

**Complexity** Simple

This grotesque fungus releases poisonous spores when a creature comes near.

---

**Stealth**
DC 21 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to safely remove the mold

**AC** 18, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5

**HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Weaknesses**
[[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] 10

**Feed on Decay**  The mold expands when fed by blood or decay. If at any point a creature takes bleed damage while adjacent to the mold or a dying or dead body is adjacent to the mold, the mold grows to expand into every square adjacent to its current space. This can happen only once per day.  
**Spore Burst** ⤾ ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature touches the mold, hits it, or damages it; **Effect** The mold explodes in a burst of spores and mold clusters, dealing 2d6+6 poison damage to creatures within 10 feet of it, with a DC 18 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save.  

---
**Reset**
Unless entirely eradicated (typically with [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] or fire), the mold regrows over the course of 2 weeks.
