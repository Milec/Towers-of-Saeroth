# Reflected Desires  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 123

**Complexity** Simple

This mirror reflects the viewer's deepest desires.

---

**Stealth**
DC 17 (0 to notice the mirror)

DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to shroud the mirror without looking at it, or DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (trained) to erect mental barriers

**AC** 13, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +4

**Hardness**
6

**HP**
24 (BT 12)

**Immunities**
object immunities

**Tempt** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Trigger** A creature looks into the mirror; **Effect** The triggering creature sees themselves enjoying their deepest desires in the mirror's reflection, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] (Will DC 20 negates). This fascination ends automatically when the mirror is broken or covered up.
