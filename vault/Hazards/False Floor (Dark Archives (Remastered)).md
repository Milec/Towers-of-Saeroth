# False Floor  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 123

**Complexity** Simple

The floor in this chamber is an illusion, which conceals a 40-foot drop to the true floor below. Additionally, the area beneath the floor is magically silenced—no sound is audible within nor does sound leave this space.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (trained; or 0 if the illusory floor is dispelled)

**Disable**
Succeeding on a Perception check against the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] DC of 21 (including the check to find the trap) disbelieves the illusory floor, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (2nd level; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 18) to dispel the illusory floor; _dispel magic_ (2nd level; counteract DC 20) to dispel the magical silence

**Drop** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks onto the illusory **floor**; Effect The triggering creature falls through the illusory floor and takes falling damage (typically 20 bludgeoning damage). That creature can use the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] reaction to avoid falling. The DC to Climb the walls or Grab an Edge is 20.

---
**Reset**
automatic
