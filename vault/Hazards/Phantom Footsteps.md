# Phantom Footsteps  *Hazard -1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 64

**Complexity** Simple

Audible footsteps approach from behind, but their source is not apparent.

---

**Stealth**
DC 15

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (trained) to ritually ward off lesser spirits or DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] to exorcise the spirit

**Stalk** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature passes by the footsteps' path; **Effect** Footsteps with no visible source closely shadow the triggering creature, then stop. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 16 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3.

---
**Reset**
1 hour
