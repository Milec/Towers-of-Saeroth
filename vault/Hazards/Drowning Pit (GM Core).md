# Drowning Pit  *Hazard 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 106

**Complexity** Complex

A trapdoor covers a 10-foot-square pit that’s 30 feet deep and has 5 feet of water at the bottom. Four water spouts in the walls connect to hidden water tanks. Each water spout extends out of a different wall, 6 inches from the top of the pit.

---

**Stealth**
+10 (trained); DC 22 (expert) to notice the water spouts once the pit opens

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to seal each water spout, DC 22 Thievery (trained) to open the trapdoor, or DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] the trapdoor

**AC** 19, **Fort** +8, **Ref** +5

**Trapdoor Hardness**
15

**Trapdoor HP**
60 (BT 30); **Spout Hardness** 8; **Spout** HP 32 (BT 16)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Pitfall** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature walks onto the trapdoor; **Effect** The triggering creature falls in and takes damage from the fall, reduced by 5 feet for falling into the water (typically 12 bludgeoning damage). A creature can [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] to avoid falling. The trapdoor then slams shut, and the hazard rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(4 actions) The trap loses 1 action each turn for each disabled water spout. On each of the trap’s actions, a spout pours water, increasing the depth of the water by 5 feet. Once the pit is full of water, the pit stops using actions, but creatures in the pit begin [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|drowning]].

---
**Reset**
The trap can be reset if the door is manually reengaged and the water tanks are refilled; it can be reset without draining the pit, but doing so renders the pit less effective.
