# Stinkface Surprise  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 25

**Complexity** Simple

A pile of excrement and rotten food is launched at whoever opens the trunk without first depressing a hidden button on the side of the trunk.

---

**Stealth**
DC 27 (expert; DC 31 to detect the hidden bypass button)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 26 (expert) to unhook the launch spring

**Stinkface Surprise** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature opens the trunk or breaks open the trunk; **Bypass** hidden button; **Effect** The trap deals 4d10+22 poison damage to the triggering creature (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, the target is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 4. A creature can spend one Interact action to scrub the filth from their eyes, removing the blinded condition. A creature can’t reduce the value of their sickened condition below 1 until they fully wash the filth off, a process which requires 5 minutes and suitable supplies.
