# Collapsing Cell  *Hazard 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -214- The Broken Palace|Pathfinder #214: The Broken Palace]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Simple

A section of ceiling and iron cell bars collapse inward.

---

**Stealth**
DC 22 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (trained) to use a loose iron bar to shore up the ceiling.

**Collapse** ⤾ **Trigger** A Medium or larger creature moves through the cell at a faster pace than simply Stepping, or more than one Medium or larger creature enter the cell at the same time; **Effect** The ceiling and walls of the cell collapse, creating a thunderous roar. All creatures in the cell must attempt a DC 25 Reflex save; the cell then becomes greater [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] that can be passed through safely.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes 1d10+6 bludgeoning damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes 2d10+13 bludgeoning damage and is immobilized by fallen rubble ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 25).  
**Critical Failure** The creature is buried in the rubble. They take 3d10+19 bludgeoning damage and are [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] (Escape DC 25). Until they escape, the creature must hold their breath or risk [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|suffocating]].
