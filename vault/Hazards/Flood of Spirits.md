# Flood of Spirits  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 67

**Complexity** Simple

A wave of spirits fly through the area, passing right through the living.

---

**Stealth**
DC 45 (trained) to hear the flood of spirits coalescing

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (expert) or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (master) to divert the spirits

**Surge Through** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) **Trigger** A creature passes through the area; **Effect** The haunt deals 3d12+20 negative damage and 3d12+20 cold damage to creatures in the area (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failure, a creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 minute, and on a critical failure, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 minute.

---
**Reset**
1 day
