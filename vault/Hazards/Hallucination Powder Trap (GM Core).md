# Hallucination Powder Trap  *Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 102

**Complexity** Simple

A tube of hallucinogenic powder armed with a miniature explosive is connected to a doorknob or similar latch.

---

**Stealth**
DC 24 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 26 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) to disable the hammer that strikes the percussion cap

**AC** 24, **Fort** +0, **Ref** +0

**Hardness**
0

**HP**
1

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Powder Burst** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Trigger** The latch is opened or the tube is broken; **Effect** The tube explodes, spraying hallucinogenic powder in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Any creature in the cone must succeed at a DC 24 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round and take a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks and saves against mental effects for 1d4 hours. On a critical failure, the penalty is instead –4.
