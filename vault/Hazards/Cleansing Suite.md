# Cleansing Suite  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 74

**Complexity** Complex

Deadbolts inside the doors clunk as three glowing runes appear on the ceiling.

---

**Stealth**
+37 (master)

**Disable**
DC 42 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] to erase the runes, or DC 46 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] to destroy the runes, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th rank, [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 41); each rune requires a success to remove (three runes total)

**AC** 40, **Fort** +38, **Ref** +29, **Will** +35

**Hardness**
31

**HP**
130 (BT 65)

**Deadbolts** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature approaches within 10 feet of the cleansing suite’s eastern doors; **Effect** The doors to the chamber slam shut and seal with deadbolts. The hazard rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The cleansing suite activates one of its runes. The hazard can’t activate the same rune more than once per turn. Disabling a rune removes one of the hazard’s actions and prevents the hazard from activating that rune.  
**Rune Against Evil** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]]) The hazard fills the chamber with holy energy that deals 6d6+20 spirit damage (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save) to creatures with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.  
**Rune Against Magic** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) The hazard casts [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th rank, [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] +31) that targets a spell or effect on each creature in the chamber.  
**Rune Against Scrying** three actions ([[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) The hazard casts [[Spells/Detect Scrying (Player Core)|_detect scrying_]]. If it detects a [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] effect in the chamber, it casts [[Spells/Hidden Mind|_hidden mind_]] on each affected creature and _dispel magic_ on the effect (9th rank, counteract +31).

---
**Reset**
1 minute after the hazard activates, the deadbolts unlock and the hazard resets.
