# Spirit Window  *Hazard 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 124

**Complexity** Simple

Spirits trapped inside a haunted window harm those who touch the window.

---

**Stealth**
DC 51

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] (legendary) to free the spirits from the mirror, or DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (legendary) to banish the spirits

**AC** 45, **Fort** +36, **Ref** +30

**Hardness**
35

**HP**
132 (BT 66)

**Immunities**
object immunities

**Siphon Soul** ⤾ ([[Traits/Evil|evil]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the window; **Effect** The triggering creature takes 8d10+44 negative damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save) as the window attempts to steal their soul. If this damage would kill the target, its soul is trapped inside the window with the effects of [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]].
