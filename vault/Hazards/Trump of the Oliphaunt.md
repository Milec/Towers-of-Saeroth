# Trump of the Oliphaunt  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental (GM Core)|Environmental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 196

**Complexity** Complex

An overwhelming trumpeting sound vibrates and suffuses the area, causing everything to slow.

---

**Stealth**
+25 (master) to notice the tension of a low vibration forming in the area from the imminent release of the [[Bestiary/Oliphaunt of Jandelay|Oliphaunt's]] trumpet

**Disable**
DC 38 [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]] (expert), [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Maelstrom Lore]] (expert), [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] (master), [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] (master) to recognize and shore up the area against the localized planar instability causing a connection to the Oliphaunt of Jandelay. Four successes are needed to strengthen the area enough against the instability to end the hazard.

**Bone-Shattering Rhythms** ⤾ **Trigger** Three or more characters enter the area of planar instability; **Effect** Pent-up magical energy from another world begins to release in waves of overwhelming sound. The hazard rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 36 Fortitude save. A creature with the [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] condition from this hazard takes a –2 circumstance penalty on further saves against this effect.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes 2d6+6 sonic damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round.  
 **Failure** The creature takes 4d6+13 sonic damage and is slowed 1 for 2 rounds.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes 8d6+26 sonic damage and is slowed 2 for 2 rounds.

---
**Reset**
The area becomes unstable again after 24 hours.
