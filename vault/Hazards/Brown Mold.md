# Brown Mold  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 77

**Complexity** Simple

This unassuming fungus leeches heat out of the air.

---

**Stealth**
DC 21 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) to safely remove the mold

**Emit Cold** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]]); 5 feet. Brown mold deals 2d6 cold damage to nearby creatures.

**AC** 18, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5

**HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
critical hits, fire, object immunities, precision damage

**Weaknesses**
cold 10

**Leech Warmth** ⤾ **Trigger** Fire comes within 5 feet of the brown mold; **Effect** The brown mold expands into every square adjacent to its space. As it grows, it pulls more heat from its surroundings, dealing 2d6+6 cold damage (DC 18 basic Fortitude save) to creatures within 10 feet after it expands.

---
**Reset**
After expanding, the brown mold can’t grow again for 1 day.
