# Hampering Web  *Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Environmental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 102

**Complexity** Simple

Semitransparent sheets of webbing span the entryway, ready to capture small insects or hamper larger creatures that pass through.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 17 [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] (trained) to dislodge it

**AC** 19, **Fort** +10, **Ref** +11

**HP**
26 (BT 13)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Critical Hits (Player Core)|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

**Ensnare** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature that isn’t a spider walks into the web; **Effect** The web wraps around the triggering creature’s body, clinging to their limbs. The triggering creature must succeed at a DC 20 Reflex save or take a –10-foot circumstance penalty to all their Speeds until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] the web (DC 20). On a critical failure, the webbing also clings to the creature’s face, making them [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, and they can’t attempt to reduce this condition until they Escape the web.
