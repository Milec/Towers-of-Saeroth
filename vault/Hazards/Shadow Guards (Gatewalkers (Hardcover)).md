# Shadow Guards  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 40

**Complexity** Complex

Shadowy caricatures of elves peel themselves from the floor and attack everyone in the room.

---

**Stealth**
+11 (trained) to notice the runes concealed among the drawings

**Disable**
DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] to move through the room without touching the runes, followed by DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (trained) to carve them out without triggering them; or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (1st rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 16) to counteract the runes

**AC** 18, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +11

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
32

**Shadow Rush** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature enters the room; **Effect** After waiting 1 round, the trap animates its shadowy stick figures and makes a painted spear Strike against each creature in the room. The trap rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(1 action) The drawings stab at each character in the room with spears drawn from sharp lines.  
Melee single action painted spear +11, **Damage** 1d10+4 piercing

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates and resets after 1 minute. The drawings cannot pursue characters out of the room.

---
