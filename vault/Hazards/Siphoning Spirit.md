# Siphoning Spirit  *Hazard 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Haunt]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 69

**Complexity** Complex

A formless spirit drains life from the living, becoming progressively visible as its victims weaken.

---

**Stealth**
+40 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 48 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] (expert) to weaken the spirit; four total successes are required to disable the haunt

**Sudden Siphon** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) **Trigger** A living creature passes within 15 feet of the spirit; **Effect** The invisible spirit latches onto the life essence of the living, dealing 4d10+20 negative damage to all living creatures within 60 feet (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). The haunt then rolls initiative.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and increases its [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] value by 1, to a maximum of drained 4.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and increases its drained value by 2, to a maximum of drained 4. If the creature was already drained 4, it dies.

---
**Routine**
(1 action; [[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) The spirit inhales, siphoning the souls of the living and becoming more visible. Each creature within 60 feet takes 4d10+20 negative damage, with a DC 41 Fortitude save. If no creatures are within the area, the haunt moves up to 120 feet into the largest concentration of living creatures and then inhales.

---
**Reset**
After 1 minute, the spirit is satiated, and the haunt deactivates as it returns to a resting state. Over the course of 1 hour, the spirit's siphoned life energy disperses and the haunt resets.
