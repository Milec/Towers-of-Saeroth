# Rusted Door  *Hazard 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rusthenge]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Simple

Shards of rust extend from the door and crumble away to cling to the flesh and clothes of anyone who attempts to open or damage the door.

---

**Stealth**
DC 18 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to deface the hidden runes that allow the trap to activate, or DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] to offer prayers to a non-evil deity to override the ancient influence of Xar-Azmak

**AC** 15, **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5

**Hardness**
8

**HP**
30 (BT 15)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Inflict Rust Creep** ⤾ **Trigger** A non-worshipper of Xar-Azmak attempts to open the door or causes damage to it with a melee Strike; **Effect** The triggering creature is exposed to [[Rules/Diseases/Rust Creep|rust creep]] as tendrils of rust-red energy seem to slither out of the rusty patches on the door to rasp strangely gently against exposed skin.

---
**Reset**
The rusted door resets automatically after 1 round.
