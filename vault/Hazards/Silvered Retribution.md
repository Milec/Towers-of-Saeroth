# Silvered Retribution  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 51

**Complexity** Complex

Pressure plates in the floor, wall, and ceiling of this hall trigger silver blades to protrude from the ceiling, slashing anything passing through the hall.

---

**Stealth**
+23 (master)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 28 (master) to disable the pressure plates; two successes are required to disable the trap

**Sudden Strikes** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature steps on the floor or places pressure on the walls or ceiling of these two adjoining halls; **Effect** Silver blades erupt from the walls and wildly slash everything in the area, dealing 2d10+13 slashing damage ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magic]], [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]]) to all creatures in the area (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The trap then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(2 actions) The maze of blades continues to spin, jab, and slash, dealing 2d10+13 slashing damage ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magic]], [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]]) to all creatures in the area (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Reset**
The trap deactivates after 1 minute and can be manually reset.
