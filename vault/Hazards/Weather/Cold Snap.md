# Cold Snap  *Weather Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Weather]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 123

The temperature drops noticeably, becoming unusually cold for this time of year.

---

**Requirements**
A cold snap can occur at any time except during the summer.

**Survival** DC 16 (trained)

**Preparation**
DC 19 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (trained) or DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) to realize there's an imminent shift in weather.

**Routine** (24+ hours) The [[Rules/Temperature|temperature]] drops down to an unusually low level. During spring and autumn, the temperature shifts to mild cold. During winter, the temperature shifts to severe cold, during which unsheltered PCs take 1d6 cold damage per hour. If a [[Hazards/Weather/Blizzard|blizzard]] takes place during a cold snap, the blizzard's temperature drops to extreme cold, during which unsheltered PCs take 2d6 cold damage every 10 minutes.
