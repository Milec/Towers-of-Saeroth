# Windstorm  *Weather Hazard 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Weather]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 125

High winds wreak havoc and disrupt travel, and they may even cause damage as trees or other objects are blown over.

---

**Survival** DC 19 (expert)

**Preparation**
DC 19 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (expert) or DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] to know how and where to find shelter from the upcoming storm.

**Routine** (24+ hours) As long as the windstorm persists, the [[Rules/Moving in Wind|effects of wind on movement]] are in effect. Overland travel in a windstorm is treated as if moving through difficult terrain. Once every 8 hours, attempt a DC 18 flat check; on a success, a randomly chosen PC who isn't sheltered is struck by debris.  
 **Debris Strike** single action The targeted PC takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 16 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
