# Supernatural Storm  *Weather Hazard 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Weather]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 124

What starts out as a [[Hazards/Weather/Heavy Downpour|heavy downpour]] or a [[Hazards/Weather/Windstorm|windstorm]] takes a turn for the outlandish, as something strange begins raining down from the heavens above: bolts of fire, acid rain, or even something like a rain of frogs or shards of stained glass.

---

**Survival** DC 25 (expert)

**Preparation**
DC 25 [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] (trained) or DC 25 [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] (trained) to find shelter in the region.

**Routine** (1+ hours) Supernatural storms are the result of the growing influence of the [[Setting/Planes/First World]] over the region. When they occur, characters who aren't in shelter take 4d8+18 points of damage each hour (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The type of damage depends on the type of storm, as chosen by you. For example, acid rain would cause acid damage, while a rain of frogs would cause bludgeoning damage.

---

**Escalated Storm** (Level +1) Supernatural storms can scale upward in level as you need to continue challenging the PCs. Use the tables for [[Rules/Building Hazards|building hazards]] to set the escalated storm's DCs—it uses the high DCs for Survival and Prepare from [[Rules/Stealth and Disable|Table 2–13]] of that book, and the damage value is the [[Rules/Offense|Simple Dmg value from Table 2–16]].
