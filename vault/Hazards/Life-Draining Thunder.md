# Life-Draining Thunder  *Hazard 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shadows at Sundown]] pg. 32

**Complexity** Simple

Thassilonian runes set with onyx erupt with negative energy and thunderous roars at the presence of a living creature.

---

**Stealth**
DC 35 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (master) to destroy the key rune, DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] (master) to harmlessly discharge the runes, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] (7th level; [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC 31) to counteract the trap

**Thunderous Lifedrain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) **Trigger** A living creature approaches within 5 feet of either double door; **Effect** All living creatures in the room take 9d6 negative damage and 9d6 sonic damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
