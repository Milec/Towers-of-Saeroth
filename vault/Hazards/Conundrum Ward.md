# Conundrum Ward  *Hazard 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 46

**Complexity** Simple

A complex geometrical ward with mathematical equations woven into its lines magically seals the door, harming anyone who touches it.

---

**Stealth**
DC 5

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 30 (master) to sabotage the ward, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Geometry or Mathematics Lore]] DC 25 (trained) to calculate the solution to the complex equations, [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] DC 28 (expert) to manipulate the ward’s precise underlying schematics, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 26) to counteract the magical ward

**Mental Conundrum** ⤾ ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature touches the door directly or with a tool; **Effect** The ward’s equations spiral and swirl, drawing the mind into an inescapable conundrum. The triggering creature is dealt 4d10+26 mental damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the target is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2, or stupefied 4 on a critical failure.

---
**Reset**
The trap automatically resets after being triggered. If disabled, the ward does not reset.
