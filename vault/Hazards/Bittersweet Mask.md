# Bittersweet Mask  *Hazard 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 67

**Complexity** Simple

The many iron bars of this iron mask fits tightly around Javownir’s head and snout, looking almost as if it was built in place to conform specifically to the shape of his skull with no apparent latch or hinge for it to be removed.

---

**Stealth**
DC 45 (master)

**Disable**
DC 45 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (expert) twice to find the [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] latches, DC 48 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] (master) to wrench the mask open quickly enough to prevent it from triggering before it’s destroyed, [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (9th-rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] DC 35), or automatic with Spleen’s key

**AC** 42, **Fort** +36, **Ref** +27

**Hardness**
30

**HP**
120 (BT 60)

**Immunities**
[[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|critical hits]], [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage; **Special** whenever the mask takes damage, Javownir takes the same damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save)

**Toxic Off-gassing** ⤾ **Trigger** The mask takes damage, or an Athletics check to wrench it free fails or critically fails; **Effect** The mask creates a cloud of poison gas in a 20-foot emanation around Javownir. Creatures in the area take 6d12+41 poison damage (DC 44 basic Fortitude save; Javownir treats the result of this save as one degree of success worse than the rolled result).

---
**Reset**
The mask resets automatically at the start of the round after it is triggered.
