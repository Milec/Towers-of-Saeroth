# The Ketos  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Complex]], [[Traits/Environmental]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 10

**Complexity** Complex

A shadow the size of an island appears in the water beneath the ship.

---

**Stealth**
+19 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], or [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or DC 23 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] to help the crew of the _Tide Hunter_ escape the Ketos; three total successes are required to disable the hazard

**AC** 25, **Fort** +20, **Ref** +12

**Tentacle HP**
60

**Grasp of the Depths** ⤾ **Trigger** The Tide Hunter enters the Ketos’s hunting grounds; **Effect** The Ketos uses its eight tentacles to seize the ship, holding it in place. The hazard then rolls initiative.

---
**Routine**
(3 actions) The Ketos uses its actions to make tentacle Strikes against creatures aboard the _Tide Hunter_ or within 30 feet of the ship.  
**Melee** tentacle +18, **Damage** 2d10+9 slashing; no multiple attack penalty

---
**Reset**
If three or more of its tentacles are destroyed, the Ketos releases the _Tide Hunter_ and disappears into the ocean’s depths to nurse its wounds. The monster does not return to this area for 24 hours.
