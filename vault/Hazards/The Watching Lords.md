# The Watching Lords  *Hazard 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 104

**Complexity** Complex

The demigod statues in the alcoves seem to shift, their heads turning to watch as intruders pass by.

---

**Stealth**
DC 28 (trained)

**Disable**
[[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] DC 31 (to scrape away hidden runes that grant the statues the ability to watch), [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] DC 28 (to pray for protection from the attention of Abyssal lords), or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (4th level; counteract DC 26) to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the trap

**Conjure the Guardians** ⤾ ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]]) **Trigger** A non-chaotic-evil creature approaches within 20 feet of the doors to area **G4**; **Effect** The statues appear to hiss and growl, and a moment later, four [[Bestiary/Brimorak (Monster Core)|brimorak]] demons appear in the hallway—two near the entrance to area **G1**, and two near the entrance to area **G4**. The four demons attack at once, rolling initiative as normal. They fight to the death, but vanish after 1 minute has passed.

---
**Reset**
Once the Watching Lords are triggered, the trap needs an hour to recharge its energies, after which point it can be triggered again.
