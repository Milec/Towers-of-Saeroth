# Mist Projector  *Hazard 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Trap]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 59

**Complexity** Simple

Hidden jets expel gas that quickly freezes, causing limited damage to nearby books.

---

**Stealth**
DC 23 (expert)

**Disable**
DC 27 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] (expert) to deactivate the trap without its control key or DC 26 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] to create and apply a chemical that neutralizes the gas.

**AC** 25, **Fort** +17, **Ref** +10

**Hardness**
15

**HP**
60 (BT 30)

**Immunities**
critical hits, object immunities, precision damage

**Icy Mist** ⤾ ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Water|water]]) **Trigger** The northeast bookshelf is touched; **Effect** Freezing mist fills a 30-foot cone originating from the room's northeast corner. Creatures in the area take 5d8 cold damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude). Creatures who fail or critically fail the saving throw take 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent cold damage]] for up to 3 rounds. The affected area is filled with mist that functions as [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]] for 3 rounds.

---
**Reset**
8 hours
