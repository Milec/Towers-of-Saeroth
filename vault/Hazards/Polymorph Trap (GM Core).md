# Polymorph Trap  *Hazard 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 103

**Complexity** Simple

A Druidic glyph attempts to transform a trespasser into an animal.

---

**Stealth**
DC 34 (trained)

**Disable**
DC 32 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] (master) to drain the glyph’s power harmlessly, or [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]] (6th rank; [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] DC 30) to counteract the glyph

**Cursed Metamorphosis** ⤾ ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Trigger** A creature moves within 30 feet of the glyph without speaking the passphrase in Druidic; **Effect** The creature is targeted by [[Spells/Cursed Metamorphosis|_cursed metamorphosis_]] (DC 32 Will save).
