# What Doors We Open  *Item 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -211- The Secret of Deathstalk Tower|Pathfinder #211: The Secret of Deathstalk Tower]] pg. 83

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Bulk** L

---

_What Doors We Open_ is the culmination of Aravashnial’s research. Before he was captured, he performed a 1-minute ritual to erase his spells from this book and then hid it at his campsite to prevent his [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demonic]] foes from learning too many of his secrets. A spellcaster can transfer their own spells to this grimoire using a similar 1-minute ritual. When a spellcaster prepares their spells from it, they gain the ability to Activate the grimoire until their next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]] and gain a +2 item bonus to checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about demons. A grimoire’s benefits apply only to spells cast via spell slots. No one can use more than one grimoire per day, nor can a grimoire be used by more than one person per day.

**Activate—Cast Out** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If the next activity you take this round is to cast [[Spells/Banishment (Player Core)|_banishment_]] prepared from this grimoire, it counts as the extra cost of adding an object that is anathema to the creature and gives the creature a –2 circumstance penalty to its save. If the targeted creature is a [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]], increase this to a –3 circumstance penalty.

**Activate—Open the Door Within** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** The inside back cover of _What Doors We Open_ features a realistic depiction of a bejeweled door set in a stone wall. When you Open the Door Within, you open that door to reveal an extradimensional space that otherwise functions identically to a [[Equipment/Spacious Pouch|type II spacious pouch]].
