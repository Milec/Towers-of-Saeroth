# Sun Sight  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 122

**Price** 850 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Placed under the eyes like rays of light, this tattoo burns away illusions with the unmerciful brilliance of the sun. You gain a +2 bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks that involve sight. If you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]], you receive a new save at the start of each of your turns to end your dazzled condition.

**Activate** single action to three actions envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tattoo casts a 4th-level [[Spells/Blazing Bolt|_scorching ray_]], with the rays emitting from your eyes. The number of actions you spend Activating the tattoo determines scorching ray’s number of rays. The tattoo also attempts to dispel each illusion on a creature hit by a ray ([[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] level 5th, counteract modifier +19).
