# Nomad's Shawl  *Item 7*

**Price** 330 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

The intricate beading of this shawl subtly guides you. While you wear the shawl, you always know which direction is north and gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks.

**Activate—Mind's Map** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** You focus your mind’s eye on a location you’ve been to previously. The beads on the shawl shift colors to create a map of the area based on your memories. You can dismiss this effect as a free action.
