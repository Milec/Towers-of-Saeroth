# Skyfisher Vapors  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]]

---

_Skyfisher vapors_ are only visible via their effect on a [[Spells/Toxic Cloud|_toxic cloud_]] spell, or apparent lack thereof. These gases cause the conjured cloud to be as transparent as a [[Bestiary/Sky Fisher|sky fisher]]. The cloud is invisible, meaning it does not provide [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]], but it requires a successful [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] check against your [[Rules/Spell DC|spell DC]] to notice the cloud by faint distortions in the air. Your magical connection to the cloud means you always know where it is. This has no effect on its damage.
