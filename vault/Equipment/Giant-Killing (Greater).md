# Giant-Killing  *Item 8+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Kingmaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This weapon features stylized etchings of giants. A _giant-killing weapon_ deals an additional 1d6 mental damage on a successful Strike against a [[Traits/Giant|giant]]. On a critical hit, the giant is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] until the end of your next turn.

## Giant-Killing  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

**Price** 450 gp

## Giant-Killing (Greater)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

**Price** 6,000 gp

---

Increase the additional mental damage to 2d6, and mental damage dealt by this weapon ignores the target's mental resistance. On a critical hit, instead of being enfeebled 1, the giant must attempt a DC 34 Fortitude save with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The giant is enfeebled 1 until the end of your next turn.  

**Success** The giant is enfeebled 2 until the end of your next turn and takes 1d10 additional mental damage.  

**Failure** The giant is enfeebled 2 for the next minute and takes 2d10 additional mental damage.  

**Critical Failure** The giant is enfeebled 4 for the next minute and takes 4d10 additional mental damage.
