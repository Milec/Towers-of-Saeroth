# Rusting Carapace  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 19

**Price** 476 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Lamellar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leather Lamellar]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Lamellar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leather lamellar]] armor_ incorporates the plates of a rust monster’s hide, woven together with the creature’s own antennae and sinew into a functional set of armor. While wearing the rusting carapace, you gain a +2 item bonus to your Reflex save to prevent an object you’re holding from being affected by rust; this also increases your Reflex DC to avoid being [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarmed]] by a rust monster’s Antenna Disarm ability.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** .Feathery antennae extend from between the segments of the _rusting carapace_ and brush against a nearby metal creature, non-magical metal hazard, or non-magical metal item of up to 1 Bulk, with the effects of [[Spells/Rusting Grasp|_rusting grasp_]]. The DC is 24.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide of a [[Bestiary/Rust Monster|rust monster]].
