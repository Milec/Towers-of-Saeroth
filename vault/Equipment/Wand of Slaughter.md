This polished black wand has a green gem at the tip, and anyone who looks into it sees a reflection of a grinning skull.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Execute|_execute_]] at the indicated rank. If the spell slays a living target, the corpse releases grim energy in a 20-foot emanation, dealing void damage equal to double the spell's rank.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Execute|_execute_]] of the appropriate rank.
