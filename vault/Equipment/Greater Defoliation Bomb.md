# Defoliation Bomb  *Item 2+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Splash]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 77

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

This brightly painted ceramic sphere contains chemicals that cause plants to wither and die. A defoliation bomb deals the listed [[Traits/Void|void]] damage, [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]], and [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage to all plants in the area. Non-creature [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]] in the area immediately wither and die. Many types grant a bonus to attack rolls.

## Lesser Defoliation Bomb  *Item 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 77

**Price** 7 gp

---

The bomb deals 1d6 void damage, 1d4 persistent void damage, and 1 void splash damage.

## Moderate Defoliation Bomb  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Price** 18 gp

---

You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 2d6 void damage, 2d4 persistent void damage, and 2 void splash damage.

## Greater Defoliation Bomb  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Price** 35 gp

---

You gain a +2 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 3d6 void damage, 3d4 persistent void damage, and 3 void splash damage.

## Major Defoliation Bomb  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

---
