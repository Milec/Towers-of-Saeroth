# Keep Stone Shield  *Item 17+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 113

**Usage** varies by shield

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Keep Stone]]

---

Much easier to come by than armor, a keep stone shield offers effective-enough protection against magic that they've become very popular among the upper echelons of dwarven society, particularly those on active duty. When you have a keep stone shield Raised, you gain its circumstance bonus to saving throws against spells that target you (as well as AC). Additionally, you can [[Feats/Shield Block]] against any spell damage, not just from physical attacks.

## Keep Stone Shield (High-Grade)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 113

**Price** 13,200 gp

**Bulk** 1

---

The shield has Hardness 11, HP 46, and BT 23.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include at least 6,600 gp of keep stone.
