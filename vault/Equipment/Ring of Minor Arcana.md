# Ring of Minor Arcana  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Fall of Plaguestone

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Fall of Plaguestone]] pg. 58

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This rose-gold ring is adorned in the center by a somewhat ominous-looking horned skull. This ring gives you the power to cast the following innate arcane cantrips any number of times each day: [[Spells/Detect Magic|detect magic]], [[Spells/Mage Hand|mage hand]], and [[Spells/Prestidigitation|prestidigitation]]. Each is cast as a 1st-level spell. If you are an arcane spellcaster, these can instead be heightened to the level of your cantrips.
