# Stargazer  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Scrying]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 35

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Club (Player Core)|Club]]

---

When you invest this _clear quartz [[Equipment/Crystal Ball (GM Core)|crystal ball]]_, it orbits your head like an aeon stone. As long as you have the stargazer invested, you can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to direct it to orbit one of your hands where you can telekinetically smash the orb into foes, wielding it as a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Club (Player Core)|club]]_. While you’re directing the stargazer, your hand is full, and you can send it back to your head with another Interact action. On a critical hit, the stargazer pulses with hypnotic starlight, [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzling]] the struck creature for 1 round. A stargazer doesn’t add critical specialization effects.
