# Staff Of The Mage's Friend  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 240

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

When this staff is prepared, the carved wooden animal figurine atop it shifts to resemble the owner’s familiar. While wielding this staff, you can communicate telepathically with your familiar, even if it doesn’t normally understand or speak a language, with a range of 30 feet.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
