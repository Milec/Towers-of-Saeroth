# Shockguard Coil  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 83

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

free action

Interact

**Trigger** You [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] a foe’s melee unarmed attack or melee attack with the affixed shield.

---

This miniature Stasian coil talisman emits small sparks when jostled. It uses a combination of Stasian technology and evocation magic to erupt in electricity when discharged. When you activate the coil, the foe takes 2d12 electricity damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed save, the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] until the start of its next turn.
