# Wanderer's Staff  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 240

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The outlines of topographical maps are burned into this wooden staff’s surface, though they constantly change to reflect the surrounding terrain. While wielding this staff, you get a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] in the wild.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
