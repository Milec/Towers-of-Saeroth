# Ouroboros Buckles  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 18

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Buckle Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Buckle Armor]]

---

This ominous-looking _+3 [[Equipment/Resilient|greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant|greater acid-resistant]] [[Equipment/Armor/Buckle Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|buckle armor]]_ is deep red in color, favored by assassins who worship [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]] or [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus]], and if unfastened, the many belts and buckles writhe like living snakes. _Ouroboros buckles_ have the [[Traits/Comfort|comfort]] trait. However, while wearing _ouroboros buckles_, the unfathomable concept of infinity pulls at your mind, and each time you awaken, you'd swear the armor hissed into your ear while you were asleep. The hissing suggested secret wisdom to you in [[Setting/Languages/Aklo]], though you only ever remember it vaguely, like a fading dream.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call forth the cursed regenerative blood of an [[Bestiary/Ouroboros|ouroboros]] to recover from your wounds. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration|regeneration 15]] for 1 minute; [[Traits/Fire|fire]] damage deactivates this regeneration. Each time you regain Hit Points from this regeneration, if you're in your normal form, you collapse into a large swarm of Tiny snakes. This is a [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect that changes you into a battle form.

 When you're a swarm of snakes, you gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Rules/Scent|scent]] (30 feet), a Speed of 20 feet, a climb Speed of 20 feet, and a swim Speed of 20 feet. You have an AC of 16 + your level and ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction. Also, you gain resistance 5 to physical damage and weakness 5 to area damage and [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage. You can use none of your normal Strikes. Instead, you can use Swarming Bites, a single action that deals 4d4 piercing damage plus 2d6 poison damage to creatures in your space with a DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

 If you're in this battle form and are already at full HP when your regeneration occurs, the snakes instantaneously slither together, and you transform into your original form.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** A foe within 15 feet hits you with a Strike that deals slashing or piercing damage;  **Effect** Your _ouroboros buckles_ spray acidic blood in a 15-foot cone toward the foe, dealing 5d6 acid damage with a DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
