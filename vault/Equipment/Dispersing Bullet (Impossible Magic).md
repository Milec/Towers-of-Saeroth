# Dispersing Bullet  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 212

**Price** 50 gp

**Ammunition** sling bullet

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

The metals used to forge this lead ball were taken from a variety of continents and barely stay together. When an activated _dispersing bullet_ hits a target, the bullet scatters into a sphere of metal shards as the metals try to return to their places of origin. All creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around the target of the attack (not including the target) must succeed at a DC 21 Fortitude save or be pushed 10 feet from the target (15 feet on a critical failure).
