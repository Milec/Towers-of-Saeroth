# Aroma Concealer  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Oil (GM Core)|Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 109

**Price** 3 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

1 minute ([[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]])

---

This oily mix of herbs and natural detergents can be applied to a creature to reduce and cover any ordinary odors they produce. The creature receives a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] against creatures using primarily smell. This bonus also applies to the DC to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] the creature by scent. The listed amount is enough to cover one Medium or smaller creature and takes 1 minute to apply. The effect lasts for 1 hour after applying.
