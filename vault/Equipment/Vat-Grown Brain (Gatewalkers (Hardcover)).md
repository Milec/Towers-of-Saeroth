# Vat-Grown Brain  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 235

**Price** 190 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Activate**

three actions

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

A malformed, artificial brain pulses with alchemical life inside a nutrient-rich vat. When the vat-grown brain is activated, it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] one condition of your choice that was gained from an ability with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait, which it does by drawing the negative mental impressions into itself. However, the artificial brain is not robust, and the strain of the transfer quickly destroys it. The vat-grown brain has a counteract level of 5 and a +17 modifier on the roll.
