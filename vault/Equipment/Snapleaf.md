# Snapleaf  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 73

**Price** 9 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

⤾

Interact

**Trigger** You begin to fall

---

This small, crystalline carving takes the shape of a tree leaf and attaches to armor or clothing with a simple strap. When you activate the _snapleaf_, you gain the benefits of [[Spells/Feather Fall|_feather fall_]] and a 2nd-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell for 1 minute or until you stop falling, whichever comes first.
