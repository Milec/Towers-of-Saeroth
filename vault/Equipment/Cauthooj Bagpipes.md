# Cauthooj Bagpipes  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 105

**Price** 2,600 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

The main portion of this set of bagpipes is fashioned from the dried skin of a [[Bestiary/Cauthooj (Monster Core)|cauthooj]], with the feathers still attached. The bird’s vocal cords are crafted into the instrument’s reeds. The pipes grant a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with them.

**Activate—Disorienting Fugue** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]])
 **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You play several notes on the pipes, quickly altering their pitch and tone. Each creature within a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 31 Will save to resist the song. Creatures that attempt this save become temporarily immune to Disorienting Fugue for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected and its temporary immunity to Disorienting Fugue increases to 1 hour.  

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is confused for 1 round and immediately attacks itself (in the normal fashion for attacking oneself while confused). This [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] doesn’t give the creature a flat check to recover from the confusion.

**Activate—Throw Song** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]])  **Trigger** A creature within 30 feet attempts a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against you or an ally;  **Effect** You let loose a staccato chirp from the pipes that appears to come from somewhere else. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 31 Will save. On a failure, the creature redirects the Strike to another creature of your choice within reach of the melee Strike. If no other creatures are within range, the affected creature instead takes a –2 penalty to the Strike.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the skin and vocal cords of a [[Bestiary/Cauthooj (Monster Core)|cauthooj]].
