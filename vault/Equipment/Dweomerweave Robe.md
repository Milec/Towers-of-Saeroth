# Dweomerweave Robe  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 50

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** L

---

This robe is made from dweomerweave, a magical fabric created by spinning minor illusions into the threads of [[Bestiary/Ether Spider|ether spider]] silk. Dweomerweave is naturally hazy and translucent. It can take on the illusory appearances of other fabrics and garments, and _dweomerweave robes_ are coveted by adventurers who want to travel light without compromising on fashion.

**Activate**  1 minute (Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain the effects of a 1st-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] except that the illusion only alters the appearance of the _dweomerweave robe_, changing it into another garment with an appearance of your choice. The spell persists until the next activation. You can Dismiss the activation.
