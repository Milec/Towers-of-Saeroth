# Mythic Weapon Potency  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 149

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This weapon strikes with peerless accuracy to pierce the defenses of the mightiest monstrosities. Attack rolls with this weapon gain a +4 item bonus, and the weapon can be etched with four [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]].

**Activate—Unerring Blow** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You roll an attack roll to Strike with this weapon and receive a critical failure;  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] and reroll your attack roll with mythic proficiency, taking the higher of the two results.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic weapon potency runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character capable of making a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).
