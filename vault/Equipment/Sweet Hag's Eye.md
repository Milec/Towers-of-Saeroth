# Sweet Hag's Eye  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Troubles in Grayce

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|Scrying]]

**Source** [[Sources/Troubles in Grayce]] pg. 118

**Price** 75 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This item appears to be a normal, if admittedly macabre, piece of hard peppermint candy the size and shape of a human eyeball. That's because it is actually the eye of a [[Bestiary/Sweet Hag|sweet hag]], removed and painstakingly replaced with a mix of enchantment and cookery. It is typically mounted on a piece of jewelry or stored in a small sack worn on a cord.

 The eye provides no direct benefit for the wearer, but the hag whose eye it once was can peer through it using the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action, and cast the [[Spells/Message (Player Core)|_message_]] cantrip through it onto the creature carrying the eye. Both these effects have no range limited, provided the hag is on the same plane.

 Any damage to the eye destroys it. The sweet hag can also choose to destroy the eye remotely using Meltaway Mint. The eye can also be eaten, but a creature who attempts this is exposed to sickeningly sweet.

**Activate—Meltaway Mint** single action ;     **Effect** The hag causes the eye to melt away under a brief flare of intense heat. If a creature is holding the eye, they must attempt a DC 18 Reflex save. On a failure, they take 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] as the boiling hot candy remains stick to and burn their skin.

 **Sickeningly Sweet** ([[Traits/Ingested (GM Core)|ingested]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) The [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition caused by this poison can't be ended until the poison's effects end; **Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Maximum Duration** 10 minutes; **Stage 1** sickened 1 (1 minute); **Stage 2** 1d8 poison damage and sickened 2 (1 minute); **Stage 3** 2d8 poison damage and sickened 3 (1 minute)

**Craft Requirements** You must be a sweet hag.
