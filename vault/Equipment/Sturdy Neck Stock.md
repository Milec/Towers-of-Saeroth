# Sturdy Neck Stock  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 153

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** worn collar

**Bulk** L

---

This thick piece of leather decorated with purple threads makes an attractive necktie but also serves a functional purpose: protecting the neck from hits that you don’t see coming. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against attacks while [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] from [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]].

**Activate—Stretch out Stock** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _sturdy neck_ stock expands to cover not only your neck but also your shoulders and the back of your head. For 1 minute, you aren’t off-guard to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], or flanking creatures, or creatures using [[Feats/Surprise Attack (Player Core 2)|surprise attack]] of your level or lower.
