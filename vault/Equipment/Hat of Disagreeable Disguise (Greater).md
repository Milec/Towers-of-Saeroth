# Hat of Disagreeable Disguise  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 191

**Usage** worn headwear

---

This elegant hat appears to be and functions as a _[[Equipment/Hat of Disguise|hat of disguise]]_. When you invest the hat, it fuses to you.

**Activate**  1 minute (Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Like a _hat of disguise_, the hat casts a 1st-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] spell on you. However, the illusion disadvantages you based on your intent, making you, for example, appear to be a suspicious ruffian if you’re trying to sneak past guards or lending you the seeming of a pauper if you’re trying to impress a shallow aristocrat. You and those you consider to be allies must succeed at a DC 16 Will save or you perceive the illusion as you intended it, though others won’t. Evidence to the contrary allows you to attempt to disbelieve the false version of the illusion. You can’t [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.
