# Impactful  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 185

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This rune thrums with pure magical energy. Weapons with the rune deal an additional 1d6 [[Traits/Force|force]] damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, you can choose to force the target to succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet away from you.
