# Elemental Ammunition  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Splash]]

single action

---

When activated, the reservoir of alchemical reagents in elemental ammunition atomizes on impact, dealing [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid, cold, electricity, fire, or poison damage]] to the target and splash damage in addition to the damage the attack normally deals. Each damage type requires a different formula, and the ammunition gains a trait matching the damage type.
