# Injigo's Loving Embrace  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 305

**Price** 675 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

---

This net was woven by the [[Bestiary/Jorogumo|jorogumo]] Injigo, who presented it as a gift to her [[Ancestries/Gnome (Player Core)|gnome]] sweetheart, Mara, to keep her safe in their mountain cottage whenever Injigo left home to go on hunting expeditions.

_Injigo’s Loving Embrace_ functions as a typical [[Equipment/Net (Player Core)|net]]. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] with the net.

**Activate—Ensnare Ghost** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You spin the net in the direction of an [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creature within 30 feet that you can see. Lines of silken force whip out to lash around the incorporeal creature, who must succeed at a DC 25 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the end of your next turn. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this activation for up to 1 minute, and the creature must succeed at a DC 25 check to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] the net.

**Activate—A Night of Melancholic Dreams**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** You wrap the net around you like a blanket as you prepare to sleep. As long as you get 8 hours of sleep or analogous rest, you dream of writing love letters to someone you adore: this could be an actual paramour, someone you’re interested in, or someone unknown whose identity you don’t recall after waking. When you wake, you’re filled with a sense of melancholia about those unsent letters, but these feelings distract you from other, more unpleasant thoughts, granting you [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] resistance 5 and a +1 item bonus to saving throws against mental effects for the next 8 hours.
