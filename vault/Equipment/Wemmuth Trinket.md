# Wemmuth Trinket  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 119

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

The baubles [[Bestiary/Wemmuth|wemmuths]] use to lure victims to their hunting grounds are dangerous to retrieve and often stained by blood. One of these trinkets covers the plant matter created by [[Spells/Entangling Flora|_entangling flora_]] with blood-drinking thorns. Creatures who fail or critically fail their Reflex saves against _entangling flora_ also take piercing damage equal to the spell's rank.
