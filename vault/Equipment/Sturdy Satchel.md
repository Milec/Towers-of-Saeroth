# Sturdy Satchel  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 34

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** 1

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society.

---

This heavy [[Equipment/Backpack|backpack]] of alchemically treated cloth layered over a metal mesh protects its contents from water damage and other harmful effects. It holds up to 4 Bulk of items, and the first 2 Bulk contained within the sturdy satchel do not count against your Bulk limits. The bag has Hardness 5 and 20 Hit Points. As long as the bag remains closed and is not broken, water and other liquids can't seep into it.
