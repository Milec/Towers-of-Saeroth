# Returning Starknife  *Item 8*

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Throwing Knife]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Throwing Knife|throwing knife]]_ is specially made for Lyrune- Quah hunters, and its blade is carved with constellations.

**Activate—Ribbon Wrap** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You deal damage to a target with the starknife;  **Effect** Silver ribbons of starlight entangle the target, dealing 4d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. This is [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] damage for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, and the like. The target must attempt a DC 24 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] with an [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC of 24.
