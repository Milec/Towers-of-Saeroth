---

Deros use cytillesh in a variety of ways, and some surface-dwellers seek out the fungus for their own use. Memories lost to cytillesh can be restored through [[Spells/Modify Memory|_modify memory_]]. The save for addiction to cytillesh is DC 20, and the addiction has the [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] trait.

**Saving Throw** DC 15 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 8 hours; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (1 minute); **Stage 2** lose all memories made in the previous hour (1 hour)
