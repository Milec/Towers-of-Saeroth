# Magnetic Shield  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Aura]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 64

**Price** 130 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

Copper rings spiral around this [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]. Twin electrical probes near the grip can socket into a jar of moderate (or higher leveled) [[Equipment/Bottled Lightning (Player Core 2)|bottled lightning]], which takes 3 Interact actions to install.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** A bottled lightning is installed in the shield; **Effect** The shield becomes an electromagnet for 3 rounds. When an activated magnetic shield is raised, the circumstance bonus increases by 1 against attacks made with weapons primarily made of metal. If you use a [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] against a creature attacking you with such a weapon, you also gain a +1 item bonus to Disarm attempts against that weapon until the end of your next turn. The activation uses up the bottled lightning, and the shield can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.
