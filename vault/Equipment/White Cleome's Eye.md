# White Cleome's Eye  *Item 21*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 83

**Usage** worn eyepiece

**Bulk** L

---

Delicate engravings of spiders and cleomes decorate the silver cover of this magnifying loupe. _White Cleome’s Eye_ functions as [[Equipment/Spectacles of Inquiry (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_spectacles of inquiry_]], but the item bonuses granted to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks increase by 1 (so +3 while worn, and +4 to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] when you activate the artifact).

Once restored, _White Cleome’s Eye_ gains the following additional activations.

**Activate—Look to the Past**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You twist _White Cleome’s Eye_ in a clockwise rotation, allowing you to peer back in time, causing it to cast a 9th-rank [[Spells/Retrocognition (Player Core)|_retrocognition_]] spell.

**Activate—Relive the Past** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You twist _White Cleome’s Eye_ in a counter-clockwise rotation, allowing you to experience previous events that impacted an object you are touching, causing _White Cleome’s Eye_ to cast a 9th-rank [[Spells/Object Reading (Player Core 2)|_object reading_]] spell.
