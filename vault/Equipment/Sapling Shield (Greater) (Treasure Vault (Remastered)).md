# Sapling Shield  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 22

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler]], [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield]]

---

This surprisingly weighty, though well-balanced, wooden [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler|buckler]] is emblazoned with the image of a sapling. The sapling withers as the shield takes damage.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The buckler expands, with the sapling image growing into a mighty oak tree. The buckler becomes a [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield|tower shield]], gaining the corresponding AC bonus, Speed penalty, and ability to [[Actions/Take Cover]]. It keeps the same Hit Points and Broken Threshold, but its Hardness and Bulk increase by 2 in this form. The shield remains in this form until you Activate it again to revert it to a buckler.
