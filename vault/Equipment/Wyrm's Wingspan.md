# Wyrm's Wingspan  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 123

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

A massive pattern resembling [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] wings stretched to their full span crosses your body. The tattoo is typically inked on the upper back. Though the representation can be highly stylized, each wyrm’s wingspan tattoo depicts the wings of a particular type of dragon. You gain resistance 5 to the damage type matching the tradition of the dragon your tattoo depicts.

- [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|**Arcane**]] [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]]
- **[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]** [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]
- **[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]
- [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|**Primal**]] [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tattoo casts the [[Spells/Dragon Wings (Player Core 2)|_dragon wings_]] focus spell on you.

## Wyrm's Wingspan (Greater)  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 123

**Price** 2,800 gp

---

The resistance increases to 10. The tattoo gains the following activation.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tattoo casts [[Spells/Dragon Form (Player Core)|_dragon form_]] on you, turning you into the type of dragon represented by the tattoo.

## Wyrm's Wingspan (Major)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 123

**Price** 13,500 gp

---

The resistance increases to 15. The tattoo gains the activation of the greater type, but the _dragon form_ is heightened to 8th rank.
