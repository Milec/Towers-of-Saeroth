Silverscrap bombs are compact clusters of silver shards that explode upon impact, shredding everything in the vicinity. The bomb grants an item bonus to attack rolls and deals piercing damage and piercing splash damage, according to the bomb's type. All damage from the bomb is treated as silver for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, and the like.

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 209

**Price** 11 gp
