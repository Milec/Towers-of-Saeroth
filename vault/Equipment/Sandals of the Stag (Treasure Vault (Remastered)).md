# Sandals of the Stag  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 104

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

These sturdy leather sandals feature straps that wrap up to the knees. Etched in the leather are intricate patterns of stags leaping through the forest. You gain a +5-foot item bonus to your land Speed and a +3 item bonus on [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks when attempting to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. When you invest the sandals, you either increase your Strength modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You attempt a High Jump or Long Jump but you didn't Stride at least 10 feet;  **Effect** You can attempt the jump normally. It doesn't automatically fail.
