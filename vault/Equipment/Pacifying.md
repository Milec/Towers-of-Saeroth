# Pacifying  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 33

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This rune turns weapons into instruments of peacemaking.

**Activate** ⤾ command ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]])  **Trigger** You damage a creature with the etched weapon;  **Effect** The damaged creature must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be pacified. A pacified creature takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls on any attacks that aren’t nonlethal for 1 minute, and the creature also experiences a clear psychic warning that they should stop making attacks that could kill.
