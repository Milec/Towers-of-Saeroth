# Orm Choker  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 106

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** worn collar

**Bulk** L

---

This elaborate choker is fashioned from the treated hide of a [[Bestiary/Water Orm|water orm]]. Its interior is lined with the creature’s silky hair. While wearing an orm choker, your form blurs slightly around the edges, granting you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation (Player Core)|revelation]], and [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] effects.

**Activate—Watery Form** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentration]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You dissolve into liquid, appearing only as a stretch of flowing water. While in this form, you gain a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] of 45 feet, you automatically succeed at [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], and you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks in water. However, you can’t speak, use any of your other items or abilities, or enter a body of salt water while in this form. You can remain in this form for up to 1 hour, though you can return to your normal form using a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide and hair of a [[Bestiary/Water Orm|water orm]].
