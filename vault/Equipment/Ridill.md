# Ridill  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 29

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 4

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword]]

---

This 12-foot sword was made by a [[Bestiary/Families/Cyclops|cyclops]] in the ancient past. It might be the greatest dragon-killing weapon ever made, and [[Bestiary/Fafnheir]] justly fears it, which is why he keeps it hidden in his hoard. Runes of dragon slaying are written down its blade. _Ridill_ is a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|major striking]] [[Equipment/Bane|dragon bane]] [[Equipment/Speed|speed]] [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword|greatsword]]_. While wielding _Ridill_, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]]. This increases to +4 if the effect is from a [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]]. If you have an ability that depends on Large weapons (such as [[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Giant|barbarian giant instinct]]), it works with _Ridill_.

 _Ridill_ is always considered to have the appropriate material or damage type necessary to deactivate a dragon's [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration|regeneration]] or cause the dragon's weakness to apply. For example, _Ridill_ is considered to be [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] when attacking a [[Bestiary/Families/Linnorm|linnorm]], while it would be treated as having dealt cold damage when attacking a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Red|red dragon]]. If the dragon has more than one weakness, only the highest value applies.

**Destruction** If you boil _Ridill_ in the blood of 10 different types of dragon ([[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Blue|blue dragon]] dragon, red dragon, etc.) and drop the eye of a cyclops seer into the brew, _Ridill_ melts away into nothingness.
