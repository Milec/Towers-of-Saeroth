# Force Tiles  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 215, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 168

**Price** 525 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Light refracts in strange ways through these precisely ground glass tiles, lengthening the refracted force and causing it to push back. Adding this catalyst to a _[[Spells/Wall of Force (Player Core)|_wall of force_]]_ spell increases the wall’s maximum length to 80 feet and maximum height to 40 feet and causes creatures that try to move into the wall’s space (or are [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] into the wall) to take 2d6 force damage.
