# Snagging Hook Snare  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 300

**Price** 180 gp

---

This snare snags a creature with its wicked metal hooks. The first creature to enter the square takes 5d8 piercing damage and 5d8 slashing damage, with a DC 29 [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2765) Reflex save. On a critical failure, the hooks piercing its flesh make the creature immobilized until it successfully [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 29).
