# Apricot of Bestial Might  *Item 16*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 74

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This yellow fruit's soft flesh is covered by waxy, fibrous leaves reminiscent of a pineapple. When you eat an apricot of bestial might, you transform into a boar-faced battle form with scaly skin. You can use feats with this item as if it were a [[Equipment/Bestial Mutagen|bestial mutagen]], improving your tusk unarmed attack as if it were a claw or jaws attack.

 **Benefit** You gain 8 resistance to all physical damage and gain a tusk unarmed attack with the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]] trait that deals 1d10 piercing damage.

 **Drawback** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]]. If you perform an action with the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] or [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] traits, you must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or the action is lost.
