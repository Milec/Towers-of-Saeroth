**Trigger** You deal damage to an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature with the affixed weapon

---

This knot of copper wire reshapes itself in a new pattern every time its affixed weapon deals damage. When you activate the coil, the damaged creature must succeed at a DC 31 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] 1. If it critically fails, it instead becomes stunned 2.
