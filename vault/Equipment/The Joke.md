# The Joke  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -192- Worst of All Possible Worlds|Pathfinder #192: Worst of All Possible Worlds]] pg. 75

**Usage** carried

---

_The Joke_ represents solving a problem not with strength, but with cleverness or artifice. As long as you have _The Joke_ invested, you can attempt an appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] check instead of an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check made to [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Force Open]], [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/High Jump]], [[Actions/Long Jump]], or [[Actions/Swim]]. For example, you could make a Mountain Lore check to climb a cliff in the mountains, or Sailing Lore check to climb the rigging on a ship.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You perform a quick but amusing card trick with _The Joke_ meant to distract others. You cast [[Spells/Hideous Laughter|_hideous laughter_]] on up to 10 creatures in range of the spell. The  level of the spell is one-half your level rounded down (minimum 6th level), and the save DC is your class DC.
