# Wood-Rotted Root  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 81

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

This palm-sized chunk of wood it rotting, and riddled with mold and fungi. You can crush this wood to use it as a [[Rules/Spell Catalysts|spell catalyst]] when you cast a [[Spells/Oaken Resilience|_oaken resilience_]]. When you do, the bark that covers your skin is rotting, and emits a small cloud of spores whenever you're hurt. For the duration, whenever you take physical damage, your rotting bark skin emits a cloud of spores in the listed [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude save at the listed DC to avoid taking the listed poison damage.
