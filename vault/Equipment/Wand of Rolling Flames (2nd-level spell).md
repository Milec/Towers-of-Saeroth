# Wand of Rolling Flames  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 142

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The luminous design of red-orange cracks on this black obsidian wand suggests cooling lava.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Flaming Sphere|_flaming sphere_]] of the indicated level. The ground in the sphere's square and all adjacent squares are coated in rolling flames until the start of your next turn. These are [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (Player Core)|hazardous terrain]]. A creature that moves on the ground takes fire damage for every square of rolling flames it moves into, with the amount determined by the wand's type. If a creature in the flames doesn't move on its turn, it takes the damage for each of the squares it's in at the end of its turn. The first time you [[Actions/Sustain]] the Spell each round, the sphere creates rolling flames again in its new location (or the same location if you chose not to move it).

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Flaming Sphere|_flaming sphere_]] of the appropriate level.
