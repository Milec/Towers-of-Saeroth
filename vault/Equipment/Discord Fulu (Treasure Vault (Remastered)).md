# Discord Fulu  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Misfortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 77

**Price** 22 gp

**Duration** 1 hour

**Usage** affixed to a creature

---

Incorporating green in its writing, a _discord fulu_ is a popular but unethical tool often deployed on romantic rivals to foil their advances. While the fulu is affixed to it, a creature treats its attitude toward other creatures as one step worse than it is. The creature also takes a –1 status penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks. The first failure the creature rolls on a Diplomacy check becomes a critical failure instead, and the fulu turns to ash, ending its effect.
