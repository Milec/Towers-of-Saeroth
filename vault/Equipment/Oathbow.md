# Oathbow  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 602

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow]]

---

Constructed of a flexible, white wood and with ornate, twisting designs carved into its surface, this _+2 striking composite [[Equipment/Weapons/Longbow|longbow]]_ appears to have been made by [[Ancestries/Elf|elves]]. Sometimes when you loose an arrow, its whistling in flight sounds like a voice whispering in [[Setting/Languages/Elven]], wishing for the swift defeat of your enemies.

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** You swear an oath to destroy one creature you can see. For the next 7 days or until that creature is slain, your attacks with the bow against that creature deal 1d6 additional damage, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Track]] that creature. Your critical hits against the target gain the bow’s critical specialization effect; if they would already do so, they instead increase the DC of the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Escape]] when critically hit to DC 20.

After you activate the bow, you can’t activate it again for 7 days. If you kill the creature you’ve sworn an oath against, the oath ends and you need to wait only 10 minutes before you can activate it again.
