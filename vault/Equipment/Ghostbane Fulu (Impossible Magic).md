free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You successfully Strike an incorporeal creature with the weapon to which the fulu is affixed, but you haven’t rolled damage.

---

This white paper fulu bears red ink and attunes a weapon to the spiritual essence of an incorporeal creature. Upon activation, the weapon gains the benefits of the _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]]_ property rune against the triggering incorporeal creature for 1 minute.
