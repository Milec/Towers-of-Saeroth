**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You deal damage using the affixed weapon to a creature that has the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy trait]] or that you witnessed harm an ally, an innocent, or noncombatant within the last minute.

---

This long, fire-red feather smolders from the weapon it adorns. When you activate the feather, the creature you damaged burns with sacred light. The creature must succeed at a DC 29 Will save or take a –2 status penalty to AC and saving throws and reduce its resistances by 5. These effects last until the end of your next turn. This item has no effect on a creature with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait.
