# Bloody Fang  *Item 15*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Prey for Death

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 101

**Price** 6,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

---

The bladed inner curve of this _+2 greater striking [[Equipment/Keen (GM Core)|keen]] [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ 	has a jagged, saw-like edge, while its handle is wrapped in red leather. Carved from the mandible of a giant praying mantis, this magical weapon's blade is as sharp and serviceable as steel.

**Activate—Sweeping Slash** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You swing the dagger in a wide arc, causing a horizontal swipe of blood- red energy to sweep out in a 30-foot cone. All living creatures in the area take 15d6 slashing damage (DC 34 basic Reflex save), plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed]] damage on a failed save.
