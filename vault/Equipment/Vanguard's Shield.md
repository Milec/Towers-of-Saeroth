# Vanguard's Shield  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 125

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 3

---

This _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|moderate reinforced]] [[Equipment/Duskwood Shield|standard-grade duskwood tower shield]]_ (Hardness 8, HP 84, BT 42) is slightly wider than standard. Designed for bodyguards, it grants the user a small burst of speed to save themself and others from harm.

**Activate—By Your Side** ⤾ ;   **Trigger** An effect within 15 feet would deal damage in an area and require a saving throw; **Requirements** Your [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|shield is raised]]; **Effect** You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to 15 feet and grant yourself and creatures adjacent to you after the movement a +2 circumstance bonus to the saving throw against the triggering effect.
