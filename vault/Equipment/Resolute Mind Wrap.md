# Resolute Mind Wrap  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 74

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

---

The followers of the Tan Sugi monastery understood the value of protecting the mind from the intrusion of violence and anger and infused this strip of striped green and brown cloth with their beliefs. Wrapped around the head, the stripes almost evoke the patterns of sugi branches coiled around the wearer's skull. The _resolute mind wrap_ grants you resistance 5 against mental damage. .

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a Will saving throw against a mental effect;  **Effect** The _resolute mind wrap clings_ more tightly to your head, granting you a +1 item bonus to your Will saving throw. If you succeed at this saving throw, the resistance to mental damage granted by the resolute mind wrap increases to 10 for 1 minute.
