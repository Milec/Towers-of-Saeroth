# Bronze Bull Pendant  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 263

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

---

This pendant is forged from grainy steel and depicts a snorting bull's face. The pendant must be attached to the chest area or on a shoulder guard. When you activate the pendant, attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Shove]] with a +1 item bonus to check. Increase the distance you Shove your target to 10 feet on a success or 20 feet on a critical success.
