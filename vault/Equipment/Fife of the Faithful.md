# Fife of the Faithful  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 144

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

This small metal fife is of extraordinary fine quality, with gold filigree on the embouchure. A _fife of the faithful_ grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Call to Arms** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You play a rousing tune on the fife that carries across the battlefield. You and all allies in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws for 1 round.
