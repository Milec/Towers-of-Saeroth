# Dezullon Fountain  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 36

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Air Repeater (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Air Repeater]]

---

A _dezullon fountain_ is a distinct type of _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Air Repeater (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|air repeater]]_ made from the still-living pitcher of a [[Bestiary/Dezullon (Monster Core)|dezullon]], dealing acid damage instead of the gun’s normal piercing damage.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The next creature you successfully Strike with this weapon is exposed to amnesia venom.

**Amnesia Venom** ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 29 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] (1 round); **Stage 2** flat-footed and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], flat-footed, and clumsy 2 (1 round)

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a pitcher from a [[Bestiary/Dezullon (Monster Core)|dezullon]] or similar creature.
