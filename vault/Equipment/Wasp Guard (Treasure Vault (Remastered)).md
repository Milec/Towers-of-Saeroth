# Wasp Guard  *Item 8*

---

[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|Druids]] and [[Setting/Deities/Calistria|Calistria's]] faithful alike value this vindictive armor for its ability to turn away pests and exact revenge on foes in a manner that can easily turn coordinated enemies into a chaotic mass. _Wasp guard_ is _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor|studded leather]]_ studded with the carapaces of [[Bestiary/Families/Wasp|wasps]], subtly diverting swarms away and granting a +1 status bonus to Reflex saving throws against Swarming Bites, Swarming Stings, and similar abilities from [[Traits/Swarm|swarms]].

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy within 30 feet Strikes you with an attack;  **Effect** You animate the exoskeletons of the _wasp guard_ armor into a spectral swarm of wasps that seeks revenge for the slight. The swarm flies to the triggering enemy's space and swarms about them, dealing 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]]. If the affected enemy has any allies within 30 feet, the enemy can spend a single action, which has the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait, to shoo the swarm away. This ends the persistent damage on that enemy automatically, but the swarm instead moves to affect the enemy's ally.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 1 Bulk of wasp exoskeletons.
