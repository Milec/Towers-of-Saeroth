# Witchwarg Fur  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 119

**Price** 52 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

The pelt of a [[Bestiary/Witchwarg|witchwarg]] stays cold long after it leaves the creature's body. A tuft of this fur can freeze a [[Spells/Fire Shield (Player Core)|_fire shield_]] spell into solid ice, inverting its usual effects. The spell's [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait is replaced with the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] trait, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to fire damage and [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|environmental heat effects]] (instead of cold effects), the shield is immune to cold damage instead of fire damage, and its [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] is halved against fire effects. The damage you deal to creatures that touch you or hit you with a melee or unarmed attack has its type changed to cold.
