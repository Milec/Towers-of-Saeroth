# Privacy Ward Fulu  *Item 1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Usage** affixed to wall

**Bulk** L

---

This fulu seeks to keep thieves, spies, and unwanted attention away from a room. A depiction of a lock appears in the center of this fulu, which is in turn surrounded by circles of broken keys. When applied to a wall inside a room, all creatures within the room gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks against creatures outside the room.

## Privacy Ward Fulu (Hallway)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Price** 4 gp

---

The _privacy ward fulu_ can shield a room of up to 10 square feet, grants a +1 item bonus, and has a duration of 1 hour.

## Privacy Ward Fulu (Room)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Price** 25 gp

---

The fulu can shield a room of up to 30 square feet, grants a +1 item bonus, and has a duration of 8 hours. If you become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] within the room, you automatically become [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] to creatures of the fulu’s level or lower.

## Privacy Ward Fulu (Chamber)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Price** 120 gp

---

The fulu can shield a room of up to 90 square feet, grants a +2 item bonus, and has a duration of 24 hours. If you become hidden within the room, you automatically become undetected to creatures of the fulu’s level or lower.
