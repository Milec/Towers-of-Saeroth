---

Used by gunwitches who want a bit more power each day, a _pistol wand_ is a firearm that also contains an [[Spells/Enfeeble|_enfeeble_]] spell that can be cast using the same rules as a wand. Many other variants exist with different 1st-rank spells. This _+1 [flintlock pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=192)_ has a [[Equipment/Weapons/Reinforced Stock (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|reinforced stock]] permanently attached to it, and the pistol's [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|_potency rune_]] (and any other runes) applies to Strikes with the stock as well.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Enfeeble|_enfeeble_]].
