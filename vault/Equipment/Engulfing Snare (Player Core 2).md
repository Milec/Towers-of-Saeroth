# Engulfing Snare  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 298

**Price** 900 gp

---

You arrange a spiky cage of bones, particularly tough vegetation, or other material to spring up when disturbed. The snare deals 10d8 piercing damage to the first creature to enter this square; that creature must attempt a DC 34 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is knocked aside by the snare's deployment, takes half damage, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature is captured by the cage, taking full damage and falling [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. It's [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] while it remains within the cage. It can get free by [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escaping]] (DC 34) or by destroying the cage (AC 30, Fort +18, Ref +24, Hardness 5, HP 30, object immunities).  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage.
