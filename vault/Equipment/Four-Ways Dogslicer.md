# Four-Ways Dogslicer  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 261

**Price** 1,700 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dogslicer]]

---

This _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dogslicer|dogslicer]]_ is obviously the work of a brilliant but scrappy artisan who valued versatility over safety. Four toothy gems line the blade's cutting edge, three of which glow—one with fiery red light, one with a chill blue, and one that gives off sparks—while the last is a glistening black. Each of these gems embodies a weapon property rune, but only one rune can be active at a time.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You twist the gem along the blade corresponding with the desired weapon rune: red for [[Equipment/Flaming|_flaming_]], blue for [[Equipment/Frost|_frost_]], or yellow for [[Equipment/Shock|_shock_]]. You take 1d6 damage of the type the chosen rune deals. You can instead twist the black gem to disable the active property rune, taking no damage.
