# Peachwood Talisman  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 130, [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 20

**Price** 40 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

---

Symbols of good fortune and luck are carved on this thin, square wooden plaque. It smells of sandalwood from the blessings placed upon it. After activation, for the next minute , you can sense attacks from [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], or [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]], undead of your level or lower, or undead of your level or lower using [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-739)|surprise attack]]. However, they can still help their allies flank.
