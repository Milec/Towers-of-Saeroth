# Shield of Snakes  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 69

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _shield of snakes_ (Hardness 10, HP 90, BT 45) is built to resemble a withering tangle of snakes. These serpents slither and hiss whenever you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise the Shield]].

**Activate—Snap Up** ⤾ ;   **Trigger** You roll initiative;  **Effect** The _shield of snakes_ moves into position, allowing you to [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise the Shield]].

**Activate—Snakebite** two actions ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]])    **Effect** You thrust one of the snake heads at a target, attempting a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]] [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If you spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]], you attempt this Strike at [[Rules/Mythic Proficiency|mythic proficiency]]. On a hit, you inflict damage normally, and then the snakes poison the target, inflicting an additional 2d6 poison damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save).
