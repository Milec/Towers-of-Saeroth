Bottled lightning is packed with volatile reagents that create a blast of electricity when they're exposed to air. Bottled lightning deals the listed electricity damage and [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|electricity splash damage]]. On a hit, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
