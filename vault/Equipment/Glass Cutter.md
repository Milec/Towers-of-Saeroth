# Glass Cutter  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 115

**Price** 5 gp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** L

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society.

---

This small rod has a tiny, sharp cutting wheel made of steel on one end and a thick bulb on the other. You can use the glass cutter's wheel to score ordinary glass and use the bulb to break the piece along your scoring. It typically takes 1 minute of work to cut a hole large enough to fit your hand. If you are attempting to break the glass quietly, you must attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] check against the Perception DC of nearby creatures to go unnoticed.
