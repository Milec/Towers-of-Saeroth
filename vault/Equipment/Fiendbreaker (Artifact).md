# Fiendbreaker (Artifact)  *Item 20*

> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

**Perception** +35; precise [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and hearing within 30 feet

**Communication** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]] (partner only)

**Skills** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Demon Lore]] +33, [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] +34, [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] +35

**Int** +5, **Wis** +7, **Cha** +6

**Will** +35

---  

The stately echo of Jininsiel, the woman who guided her people through the Darklands to escape the devastation of Earthfall, causes _Fiendbreaker_ to softly shimmer with a silvery radiance equivalent to that of candlelight. _Fiendbreaker_ can activate or deactivate this radiance as a free action once per round on her partner’s turn. _Fiendbreaker’s_ telepathic voice is calm and soothing, seeking to support her partner’s decisions with compliments or warnings against overconfidence as needed.

_Fiendbreaker_ functions as a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|high-grade cold iron]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_. While wielding the staff, you gain a +3 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about any sort of [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from her list. _Fiendbreaker_ can use this activation only if you’re holding the staff and only if you haven’t used Fiendbreaker to Cast a Spell this round. Likewise, if _Fiendbreaker_ Casts a Spell from the staff, you can’t activate the staff to Cast a Spell this round.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Sanctuary (Player Core)|_sanctuary_]]
- **2nd** [[Feats/See the Unseen|see the unseen]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Anointed Ground|_anointed ground_]], [[Spells/Holy Light|_holy light_]]
- **4th** [[Spells/Clear Mind|_clear mind_]], [[Spells/Planar Tether|_planar tether_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Banishment (Player Core)|_banishment_]], [[Spells/Divine Wrath (Player Core)|_divine wrath_]]
- **6th** _holy light_, [[Spells/Spirit Blast (Player Core)|_spirit blast_]]
- **7th** [[Spells/Divine Decree (Player Core)|_divine decree_]], [[Spells/Planar Seal|_planar seal_]]
- **8th** _divine wrath_, _holy light_
- **9th** _banishment _, _divine decree_

**Activate—Restore Mind** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Fiendbreaker_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] an ongoing mental affect you’re suffering from, with a counteract rank of 9th and a +25 modifier to the roll (or a +30 modifier if the source of the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect on you was created by a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]]).
