# Arachnolute  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 105

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

The strings of this spider-shaped lute are made from the webbing of a [[Bestiary/Goliath Spider (Monster Core)|goliath spider]] and the tuning pegs are crafted from the spider’s spinnerets. An _arachnolute_ grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Web Chord** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Sticky webbing sprays in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] as you strum the lute’s strings. Each creature in the area of the webbing is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] unless it succeeds at a DC 29 Reflex save.

**Craft Requirements** The initial materials must include the spinnerets of a [[Bestiary/Goliath Spider (Monster Core)|goliath spider]], as well as a spool of its webbing.
