# Ghoul Hide  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|Hide Armor]]

---

Stitched together from pieces of [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghoul]] skin, this suit of _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|hide armor]]_ grants you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curses]] and makes you immune to the stench of ghouls. Ghoul hide with a [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient rune]] increases the resilient rune's item bonus to saving throws against curses by 1 (maximum +4).
