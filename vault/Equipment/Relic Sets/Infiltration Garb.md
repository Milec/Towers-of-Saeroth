# Infiltration Garb  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 202

**Aspects** Death and Shadow

---

Several notable assassins, thieves, and others who work from the shadows have used an _infiltration garb_ over the centuries. The nature of these professions means that such sets are often separated from their audacious users. Due to their association with subterfuge and assassins, sets of _infiltration garbs_ have grim reputations. However, not all wielders use their powers maliciously. Notably, the faithful of [[Setting/Deities/Kelinahat]], empyreal lord of spies, stealth, and intelligence gathering, view these relics as sacred tools in the fight against evil.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Hat of Disguise]] (level 2), [[Equipment/Goggles of Night]] (level 5), [[Equipment/Unmemorable Mantle|Unmemorable Mantle (Greater)]] (level 9), [[Equipment/Boots of Bounding|Boots of Bounding (Greater)]] (level 14), [[Equipment/Ring of Maniacal Devices|Ring of Maniacal Devices (Greater)]] (level 18)

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Obscure (GM Core)|Obscure]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Death Gaze (GM Core)|Death Gaze]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Dark Roads (GM Core)|Dark Roads]]
- 4: [[Equipment/Relics/Tide of Death (GM Core)|Tide of Death]]
- 5: [[Equipment/Relics/Grim Specter (GM Core)|Grim Specter]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items:_ The relic set grants you a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks you attempt in [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]].
- _Three Items:_ You can use [[Equipment/Relics/Death Gaze (GM Core)|death gaze]] to deal positive damage to undead.
- _Four Items:_ You can use [[Equipment/Relics/Dark Roads (GM Core)|dark roads]] to teleport up to 60 feet.
- _Five Items:_ When you use [[Equipment/Relics/Tide of Death (GM Core)|tide of death]], you can target undead and yourself with positive energy, restoring Hit Points to you (if positive energy heals you) and damaging undead harmed by positive energy.
