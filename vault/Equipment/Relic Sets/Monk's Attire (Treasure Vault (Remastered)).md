# Monk's Attire  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 202

**Aspects** Emotion (dedication) and Life

---

According to tales, some of the first [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|monks]] to assemble the austere _monk's attire_ were servants of [[Setting/Deities/Irori]]. This deity is a symbol of devotion and self-discovery through one's understanding of body, mind, and soul. _Monk's attire_ resonates with Irori's dedication, focusing on the highest gain from minimal acquisition, eschewing material objects. Legend holds Irori did so, and that some famous _monk's attire_ sets include a scrap of Irori's original possessions. Other monastic orders have similar traditions of minimal personal effects, with an emphasis on spiritual focus, practicality, and self-defense.

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows|Handwraps of Mighty Blows (+1)]] (level 2), [[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 explorer's clothing]] (level 5) or [[Equipment/Bands of Force]] (level 8), [[Equipment/Mask of Uncanny Breath (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Mask of Uncanny Breath]] (level 11) or [[Equipment/Mala Beads of Foresight]] (level 11)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Overflowing Life]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Dominant Emotion (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Dominant Emotion]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Inextinguishable]]
- 3 (one 13th-level or higher item): [[Equipment/Relics/Emotion Conduit (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Emotion Conduit]]
- 3 (one 17th-level or higher item): [[Equipment/Relics/Quickening Emotion (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Quickening Emotion]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items_: The item bonus from overflowing life equals the relic's level.
- _Three Items_: Any mental effect harmful to you triggers dominant emotion, which can counteract such effects.
- _Three Items (one 13th-level or higher item)_: Treat the outcome of your recovery checks as one step better.
- _Three Items (one 17th-level or higher item)_: If dominant emotion or emotion conduit [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an effect, the target regains 5d10+5 Hit Points and gains a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects for 10 minutes.
