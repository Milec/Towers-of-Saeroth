# Agathion Raiment  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 201

**Aspects** Beast and Celestial

---

An _agathion raiment_ aids its wearer in finding diplomatic solutions to conflicts when possible, fighting evil directly when it’s not, and guiding others along the path to redemption. A red [[Bestiary/Draconal (Monster Core 2)|draconal]] [[Bestiary/Families/Agathion|agathion]] created five agathion raiments and granted them to redeemer champions of great valor millennia ago. Since then, virtuous souls of various skill sets have assembled _agathion raiments_. This set functions only for [[Traits/Good|good]] creatures.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Flask of Fellowship]] (level 2), [[Equipment/Aeon Stone|Aeon Stone (Gold Nodule)]] (level 6), [[Equipment/Messenger's Ring]] (level 9), [[Equipment/Celestial Armor]] (level 13), [[Equipment/Headwrap of Wisdom]] (level 17)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Word of Faith (GM Core)|Word of Faith]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Feral Claws (GM Core)|Feral Claws]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Righteous Call (GM Core)|Righteous Call]]
- 4: [[Equipment/Relics/Form of Fury (GM Core)|Form of Fury]] (usually canine or cat)
- 5: [[Equipment/Relics/Angelic Vessel (GM Core)|Angelic Vessel]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items:_ Word of faith restores 1d10 Hit Points per relic level and reduces the target’s [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] value by 2.
- _Three Items:_ The claws you gain from feral claws are magical and deal 1d4 good damage.
- _Four Items:_ You and your allies affected by righteous call deal 1d4 additional good damage with successful Strikes, rather than none or 1. This 1d4 stacks with that from your feral claws and damage from the [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|_holy_]] rune effect you gain from righteous call.
- _Five Items:_ In your form of fury, you have darkvision and gain 20 additional temporary Hit Points, but you have [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Traits/Evil|evil]] damage. You don’t need to change your size to gain the benefits, other than reach, of a higher-level battle form. Also, your unarmed attacks from this form have the _holy_ weapon property rune.
