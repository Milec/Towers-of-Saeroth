# Pharasma's Command  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 202

**Aspects** Life and Time

---

[[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasma's]] devoted followers vow to seek out and destroy [[Traits/Undead|undead]] wherever they can be found, a task made more urgent with the rise of Tar-Baphon. Though the Whispering Tyrant was prevented from taking over Golarion, his forces are still massing. Undead unaffiliated with the [[Bestiary/Lich|lich]] represent an unacceptable perversion of the cycle of life and death to Pharasmins, and permitting such creatures to exist is anathema to her. To aid her faithful in their sacred duty to send the dead where they belong, Pharasmin priests developed this item set. While using _Pharasma's command_, you must abide by her [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|edicts and anathema]] or else the relic ceases to function for you until you atone. This set doesn't function for undead beings.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1 dagger]] (level 2), [[Equipment/Symbol of Conflict]] (level 4), [[Equipment/Wand of Overflowing Life (Player Core 2)|Wand of Overflowing Life (3rd-Rank Spell)]] (level 9), [[Equipment/Devoted Vestments]] (level 11) or [[Equipment/Cloister Robe (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Cloister Robe (Greater)]] (level 15), [[Equipment/Staff of Healing (GM Core)|Staff of Healing (True)]] (level 16)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Healing Wave]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Sands of the Hourglass (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Sands of the Hourglass]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Inextinguishable]]
- 4: [[Equipment/Relics/Temporal Pulse (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Temporal Pulse]]
- 5: [[Equipment/Relics/Life Everlasting]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items_: Healing wave increases to 1d6 per relic level.
- _Three Items_: You can use sands of the hourglass as a 30-foot line; each creature in the line must save against the effect.
- _Four Items_: Treat the outcome of your recovery checks as one step better.
- _Five Items_: When you use temporal pulse, each creature you slow takes 1d4 damage per relic level, and each creature you quicken regains 1d4 Hit Points per relic level. For living creatures, the damage is void, but it's vitality for undead.
