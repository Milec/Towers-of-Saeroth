# Infiltration Garb  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 202

**Aspects** Death and Shadow

---

Several notable [[Archetypes/Assassin|assassins]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Rackets/Thief|thieves]], and others who work from the shadows have used an _infiltration garb_ over the centuries. The nature of these professions means that such sets are often separated from their audacious users. Due to their association with subterfuge and assassins, sets of infiltration garbs have grim reputations. However, not all wielders use their powers maliciously. Notably, the faithful of Kelinahat, empyreal lord of spies, stealth, and intelligence gathering, view these relics as sacred tools in the fight against evil.

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items_: The relic set grants you a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks you attempt in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]].
- _Three Items_: You can use death gaze to deal positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]].
- _Four Items_: You can use dark roads to teleport up to 60 feet.
- _Five Items_: When you use tide of death, you can target [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] and yourself with [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] energy, restoring Hit Points to you (if vitality energy heals you) and damaging undead harmed by vitality energy.
