# Archmage's Regalia  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 201

**Aspects** Life and Mind

---

Many [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizards]] have enhanced their spellcasting capabilities with an _archmage's regalia_. Legend tells of such a relic set that belonged to the wizard-king Nex, with powers far beyond that of a typical relic. Although some believe Nex took his regalia with him when he disappeared, several credible reports of items matching their descriptions have surfaced over the years. No one has yet managed to track down all five pieces, and it's possible some have been destroyed or otherwise lost.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Mage's Hat]] (level 3), [[Equipment/Endless Grimoire]] (level 6), [[Equipment/Accolade Robe]] (level 10), [[Equipment/Bands of Force|Bands of Force (Greater)]] (level 14), [[Equipment/Staff of Arcane Might|Greater Staff of Arcane Might]] (level 16)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Repository of Knowledge]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Bestow Life]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Psychic Scream]]
- 4: [[Equipment/Relics/Vitality Siphon]]
- 5: [[Equipment/Relics/Mental Bastion]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items:_ The repository of knowledge gift grants you one additional Lore skill of the GM’s choice. This skill advances as other skills from the gift do.
- _Three Items:_ When you use your bestow life gift, if the animated object’s level is no greater than your level – 4, you can make it a minion with the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait.
- _Four Items:_ The psychic scream gift creates a 30-foot‑radius [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]].
- _Five Items:_ Treat the outcomes of skill checks you roll to [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]], and [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] as one degree of success better.
