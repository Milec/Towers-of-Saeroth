# Archmage's Regalia  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 201

**Aspects** Life and Mind

---

Many [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-790)|wizards]] have enhanced their spellcasting capabilities with an _archmage’s regalia_. Legend tells of such a relic set that belonged to the wizard-king Nex, with powers far beyond that of a typical relic. Although some believe Nex took his regalia with him when he disappeared, several credible reports of items matching their descriptions have surfaced over the years. No one has yet managed to track down all five pieces, and it’s possible some have been destroyed or otherwise lost.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Hat of the Magi]] (level 3), [[Equipment/Endless Grimoire]] (level 6), [[Equipment/Ring of Wizardry|Ring of Wizardry (Type II)]] (level 10), [[Equipment/Aeon Stone|Aeon Stone (Pale Lavender Ellipsoid)]] (level 13), [[Equipment/Robe of the Archmagi]] (level 15)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Repository of Knowledge (GM Core)|Repository of Knowledge]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Bestow Life (GM Core)|Bestow Life]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Psychic Scream (GM Core)|Psychic Scream]]
- 4: [[Equipment/Relics/Vitality Siphon (GM Core)|Vitality Siphon]]
- 5: [[Equipment/Relics/Mental Bastion (GM Core)|Mental Bastion]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items:_ The repository of knowledge gift grants you one additional [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill of the GM’s choice. This skill advances as other skills from the gift do.
- _Three Items:_ When you use your [[Equipment/Relics/Bestow Life (GM Core)|bestow life]] gift, if the animated object is at least 4 levels lower than you, you can make it a [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] with the minion trait.
- _Four Items:_ The [[Equipment/Relics/Psychic Scream (GM Core)|psychic scream]] gift creates a 30-foot‑radius [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]].
- _Five Items:_ Treat the outcomes of skill checks you roll to [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]], and [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] as one step better.
