# Monk's Attire  *Set Relic*

**Traits** [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 203

**Aspects** Emotion (dedication) and Life

---

According to tales, some of the first [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-926)|monks]] to assemble the austere _monk’s attire_ were servants of [[Setting/Deities/Irori (Divine Mysteries)|Irori]]. This deity is a symbol of devotion and self-discovery through one’s understanding of body, mind, and soul. _Monk’s attire_ resonates with Irori’s dedication, focusing on the highest gain from minimal acquisition, eschewing material objects. Legend holds Irori did so, and that some famous _monk’s attire_ sets include a scrap of Irori’s original possessions. Other monastic orders have similar traditions of minimal personal effects, with an emphasis on spiritual focus, practicality, and self-defense.

---

**Set Items**: [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows|Handwraps of Mighty Blows (+1)]] (level 2), [[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 Cloth Armor]] (level 5) or [[Equipment/Bracers of Armor|Bracers of Armor I]] (level 8), [[Equipment/Mask of Uncanny Breath]] (level 11) or [[Equipment/Ki-Channeling Beads]] (level 11)

---

**Gifts**: The set grants the following gifts.

- 1: [[Equipment/Relics/Overflowing Life (GM Core)|Overflowing Life]]
- 2: [[Equipment/Relics/Dominant Emotion (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Dominant Emotion]]
- 3: [[Equipment/Relics/Inextinguishable (GM Core)|Inextinguishable]]
- 3 (one 13th-level or higher item): [[Equipment/Relics/Emotion Conduit (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Emotion Conduit]]
- 3 (one 17th-level or higher item): [[Equipment/Relics/Quickening Emotion (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Quickening Emotion]]

---

**Collective Features**: The set grants the following features.

- _Two Items:_ The item bonus from [[Equipment/Relics/Overflowing Life (GM Core)|overflowing life]] equals the relic’s level.
- _Three Items:_ Any [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect harmful to you triggers [[Equipment/Relics/Dominant Emotion (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|dominant emotion]], which can counteract such effects.
- _Three Items_ (one 13th-level or higher item): Treat the outcome of your [[Rules/Recovery Checks (Player Core)|recovery checks]] as one step better.
- _Three Items_ (one 17th-level or higher item): If dominant emotion or [[Equipment/Relics/Emotion Conduit (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|emotion conduit]] [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] an effect, the target regains 5d10+5 Hit Points and gains a +2 status bonus to saving throws against mental effects for 10 minutes.
