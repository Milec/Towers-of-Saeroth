# Trollhound Vest  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 19

**Price** 230 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|Hide Armor]]

---

This suit of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|hide armor]]_ is sickly green and covered in thick warts and nodules, fashioned from the hide of a [[Bestiary/Trollhound|trollhound]] and imbued with some of the beast’s vitality. Wearing this armor gives you a –1 circumstance penalty to all checks made using [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to interact with [[Bestiary/Families/Troll (Monster Core)|trolls]] and a +1 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks used to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] in communities traditionally plagued by troll attacks.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact;   **Trigger** You take damage from a melee attack while you have half or fewer of your normal maximum Hit Points;  **Effect** Your body knits itself back together, healing you for 3d8 Hit Points.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hides of at least two [[Bestiary/Trollhound|trollhounds]].
