# Poison Fizz  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Lozenge (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Lozenge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 50

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Made from a special mixture of honey and alchemical reagents, poison fizz is a zesty, sweet rock candy that pops and crackles in your mouth. For 1 hour, you have an item bonus to saving throws against poison and being [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]].

**Secondary Effect** two actions ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Effect** You bite the poison fizz to release its poisonous liquid center and spray green mist in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. This deals poison damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the end of your next turn and is then temporarily immune to being blinded by poison fizz for 1 hour.
