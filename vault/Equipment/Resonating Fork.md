# Resonating Fork  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 128

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

This two-pronged fork of metal emits a constant low hum, vibrating slightly when touched. The spell attack roll of any spell cast by activating this item is +9, and the spell DC is 19.

- **Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to sonic damage and a +1 item bonus to saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] trait.
- **Weapon** After you cast a sonic spell by activating the fork, the weapon reverberates with trapped sound waves. Your next Strike causes the target to be [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round if it hits (or for 3 rounds on a critical hit). If you don’t make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] by the end of your next turn, the sound waves dissipate with no effect.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Bullhorn (Player Core 2)|_bullhorn_]].
