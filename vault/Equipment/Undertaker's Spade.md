# Undertaker's Spade  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 217

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Rites of rest are etched deep into the broken-off blade of a deceased gravekeeper’s shovel. When used as a catalyst to cast an _[[Spells/Impaling Spike (Player Core)|_impaling spike_]]_ spell, the blade disintegrates into dust, mixing with the earth and allowing the spell to damage [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures as if it were a weapon imbued with a _ghost touch_ property rune. An incorporeal creature can be [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] by a spike if it’s adjacent to a solid surface when struck; it can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as normal but can’t phase through the spike or escape to the Ethereal Plane.
