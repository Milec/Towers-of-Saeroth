# Staff of the Dreamlands  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 111

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The carved [[Bestiary/Night Hag|night hag's]] hand at the end of this sandalwood staff clutches a rough gem. The _staff of the Dreamlands_ makes it easier to navigate and survive in the [[Setting/Planes/Dreamlands]] and recognize its denizens. When wielding the staff, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks while in the Dreamlands and to checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about creatures with the [[Traits/Dream|dream]] trait.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
