# Dragon Handwraps  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 103

**Price** 80,000 gp

**Usage** worn gloves

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|Fist]]

---

These silken handwraps feature intricate embroidery of a serpentine red dragon adorned with golden thread. The handwraps function as _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|greater flaming]] [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|handwraps of mighty blows]]_. You also gain a +4 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks made to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]]. When you invest the handwraps, you either increase your Strength score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever is higher.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You slap the bottom of your palms with hands splayed outward, casting a 7th-level [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_burning hands_]] spell (DC 41).

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You succeed or critically succeed with a [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]];  **Effect** You gain a +2 status bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC against any checks made to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] your [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]] until the end of your next turn.
