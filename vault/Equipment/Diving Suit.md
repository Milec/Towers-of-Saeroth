# Diving Suit  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 19

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** 1

---

Diving suits are bulky, waterproofed leather outfits with copper helmets, worn by divers and underwater salvagers. The suit grants a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Swim]] checks made underwater, and the helmet features tubes for connecting [[Equipment/Bottled Air|_bottled air_]]. When connected to a diving suit, bottled air doesn't need to be held and can be used to breathe as a free action. If you wear armor over a diving suit, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until you remove the diving suit.
