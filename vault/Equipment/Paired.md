# Paired  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 88

**Usage** each rune applied to a separate item that has pockets

---

These runes always come in pairs and can be applied to a garment's pockets to be activated.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The _paired_ items are both invested, typically by two different characters, and are within 100 feet of each other; **Effect** Items in the pockets (up to 10 negligible Bulk items or 1 light Bulk item per pocket) trade places via teleportation.
