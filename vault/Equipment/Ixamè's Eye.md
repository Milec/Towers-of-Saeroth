# Ixamè's Eye  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 76

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** affixed to headgear

**Activate**

free action

envision

---

Via some strange alchemical reaction with the gasses of Terwa Lake, the [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud|cloud dragon]] Ixamè's eyeballs have shriveled into sky-blue gems. The dragon's sense of vision isn't impaired, and her eyes can't be removed from her head until she's destroyed. Once removed, each gem functions as a special talisman. _Ixamè's eye_ sparkles when it's in an area of fog or mist. Activating the talisman enables you to see clearly through fog and mist for 1 minute; any creature [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] only by fog or mist is no longer concealed to you. If such a creature was relying on the concealment to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]], this also means it's no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]].
