# Boreal Staff  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 131

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _boreal staff_ is chiseled from a cylinder of ice to form a spiky, jagged icicle, its surface gleaming with the colors of the northern lights. It gives the air around you a distinct chill. When used as a weapon, a _boreal staff_ is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** Attempt a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] using the staff. That Strike deals 2d4 additional cold damage.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
