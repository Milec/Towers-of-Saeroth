# Bort's Blessing  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Fall of Plaguestone

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Fall of Plaguestone]] pg. 58

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This ornate copper band has a small ruby set in the center, flanked by a pair of dwarven runes. The wearer of this ring gains the ability to understand, read, and speak one common language of their choice, selected each morning as part of their daily preparations. In addition, the wearer receives a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to Make an Impression.
