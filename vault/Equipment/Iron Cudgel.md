# Iron Cudgel  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 567

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

single action

Brutal Finish

**Requirement** You are a master with the affixed weapon

---

This miniature iron cudgel is typically affixed to a weapon by an iron chain. When you activate the cudgel, you use [[Feats/Brutal Finish]], as the fighter feat. You must meet the normal requirements, including those of the press trait.

 If you have the Brutal Finish feat, add two additional weapon damage dice on a success or a failure.
