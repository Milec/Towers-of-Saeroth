# Endless Quiver  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 149

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** 1

---

Elite archers can go through countless arrows over the course of a battle. Smart ones carry an _endless quiver_ to ensure they never run out. This quiver holds 40 mundane arrows and regenerates 10 per hour. Once an arrow is removed from the _endless quiver_, it dissipates after 1 minute.

**Activate—Convert Arrows** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You tap the quiver, and the arrows inside transform into [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]]. They revert to wood after 1 minute.
