# Tyrant Ampoule  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 57

**Price** 175 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

The body of a fearsome tyrannosaurus is shrunken and contained in this bottle, its desiccated form barely constrained within the glass. The effigy of a Gargantuan [[Bestiary/Tyrannosaurus (Monster Core)|tyrannosaurus]] forms when you open the bottle, causing devastation as it rampages. The tyrannosaurus [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] up to 40 feet. It can move through the spaces of Huge or smaller creatures and can attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample (Monster Core)|Trample]] each creature whose space it enters, dealing 2d10+12 bludgeoning damage with a DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. It can attempt to Trample each creature only once.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Tyrannosaurus (Monster Core)|tyrannosaurus]].
