# Blight Breath  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Bottled Breath]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 74

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This foul-smelling bottle contains compressed, noxious fumes. After you inhale the odious gases of the blight breath, you gain resistance 10 to [[Traits/Poison|poison]] for as long as you hold your breath. You can exhale the blight breath as a single action. The resulting spray of noxious fumes deals 10d6 poison damage to each creature in a 15-foot cone, with a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
