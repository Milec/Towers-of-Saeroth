# Bellicose Dagger  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 190

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

The hilt of a _bellicose dagger_ is etched with the word for “war” in various languages. Having absorbed the spirit of violence over the course of its existence, the weapon now craves bloodshed. A _bellicose dagger_ is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_. However, anytime you interact with creatures, no matter the context, you must succeed at a DC 25 Will save or else find you have, as a reaction, drawn the dagger.

 When you use the _bellicose dagger_ as a weapon for the first time, it fuses to you. While it's in your possession, you take a –2 circumstance penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks and skill checks to avoid fighting, such as [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to trick your way out of a scuffle or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to sneak past enemies.
