# Wyrm on the Wing  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 123

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Depicting a majestic dragon in flight, this tattoo imparts the endurance and majesty of those powerful creatures. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to the damage type matching the dragon your tattoo depicts.

- **Black or Copper** [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]]
- **Blue or Bronze** [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]
- **Brass, Gold, or Red** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]
- **Green** [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]
- **Silver or White** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]]

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tattoo casts [[Spells/Dragon Wings (Player Core 2)|_dragon wings_]] on you. You can add the dragon claws benefit only if you know that focus spell and spend 1 Focus Point when you Activate the tattoo.

## Wyrm on the Wing  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 123

**Price** 700 gp

## Wyrm on the Wing (Greater)  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 123

**Price** 2,800 gp

---

The resistance increases to 10. The tattoo gains the following activation.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tattoo casts [[Spells/Dragon Form (Player Core)|_dragon form_]] on you, turning you into the type of dragon represented by the tattoo.

## Wyrm on the Wing (Major)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 123

**Price** 13,500 gp

---

The resistance increases to 15. The tattoo gains the activation of the greater type, but the _dragon form_ is heightened to 8th level.
