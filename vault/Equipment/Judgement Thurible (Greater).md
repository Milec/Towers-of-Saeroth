# Judgement Thurible  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 126

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

The golden religious symbol on the top of this spherical incense holder shifts its form to match the faith of its bearer. You gain no benefit from a judgment thurible if you don’t worship a deity. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 27.

- **Armor** After you cast a spell by activating the thurible, the thurible casts 3rd-rank [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]] on you, with a duration of 1 round.
- **Weapon** After you cast a spell by activating the thurible, the weapon gains a rune that lasts until the end of your next turn. It gains the [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|_holy_]] rune if your deity allows holy sanctification or the [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|_unholy_]] rune if your deity allows unholy sanctification. If your deity allows both, you can choose which rune the weapon gains. If your deity allows neither, your weapon doesn’t gain a rune.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Divine Lance|_divine lance_]].
