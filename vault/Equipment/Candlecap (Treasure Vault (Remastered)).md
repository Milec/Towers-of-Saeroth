# Candlecap  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 151, [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 76

**Price** 12 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

---

The crown of a _candlecap_ is stitched leather sewn in the shape of a small bowl. Fixed inside the bowl is a melted nub of wax with a small black wick.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You shake your head, and the candle wick ignites. The _candlecap_ sheds [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in a 20-foot radius. The candle doesn't require oxygen and can't be smothered or quenched. Activating the _candlecap_ again douses the light.
