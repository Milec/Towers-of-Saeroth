# Unassuming Face Paint  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 211

**Price** 16 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Activate**

three actions

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

When these drab pigments are applied to your face, you appear plain and inconspicuous for 10 minutes. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks and DCs while blending into a crowd during this time. If you take a hostile action, the effects of _unassuming face_ paint immediately end.
