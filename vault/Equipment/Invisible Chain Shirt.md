# Invisible Chain Shirt  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 186

**Price** 2,750 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt]]

---

This _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Invisibility|invisibility]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ is itself invisible. Other creatures can't see it at all, allowing you to wear it surreptitiously. Additionally, the armor's invisible composition is quieter and more comfortable than a normal chain shirt. It loses the [[Traits/Noisy|noisy]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Comfort|comfort]] trait.

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You Stride and turn partially invisible, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] until the end of your next turn or until you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]], whichever comes first. As normal, you can use the concealment from this activation to [[Actions/Hide]].
