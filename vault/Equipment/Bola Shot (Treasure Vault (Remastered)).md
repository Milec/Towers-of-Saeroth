# Bola Shot  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 80

**Price** 25 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

---

This ammunition bears a rune with three lines radiating out, each ending in a circle. When an activated bola shot hits a target, it deals [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] bludgeoning damage. Compare the attack roll to the target's Reflex DC to determine the shot's other effects.

**Critical Success** The target falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  

**Success** The target falls prone.  

**Failure** No additional effect.
