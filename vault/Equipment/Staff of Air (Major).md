# Staff of Air  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 134

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Carved from white ash wood, a _staff of air_ crackles with electrical sparks, and a breeze always follows the wielder. While wielding a _staff of air_, you feel lighter on your feet, and you can Step into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] once per round.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
