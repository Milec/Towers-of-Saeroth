**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] +21, [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] +22, [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] +19, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sarenrae Lore]] +23

**Int** +2, **Wis** +5, **Cha** +4

**Will** +20

---  

Prayer beads given prolonged exposure to spiritual energies at sacred [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenite]] sites can attain sapience as _Dawnflower beads_. Other such objects hold the spirits of Sarenite priests who dedicated themselves to their work beyond death. _Dawnflower beads_ function as [[Equipment/Holy Prayer Beads|_greater holy prayer beads_]]. They don’t re-attune to other deities but allow any [[Traits/Good|good]] or neutral divine spellcaster to use them, though they attempt to talk their wielder out of morally questionable acts. Add [[Spells/Positive Luminance|_positive luminance_]] to the beads’ spell list, available to be cast once per day. _Dawnflower beads_ also have the following activation.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** The beads cast 4th-level [[Spells/Light (Player Core)|_light_]].
