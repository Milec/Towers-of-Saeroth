# Quicksilver Cloak  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 245

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

---

This featherlight cloak seems to reflect the very essence of the day star, granting you a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]] effects.

**Activate—Slide Through** single action (attack, [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** Staring down the creature before you, you center your shot, shooting it through one of the silver spheres floating about you. Make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against an enemy. The attack is treated as [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]].
