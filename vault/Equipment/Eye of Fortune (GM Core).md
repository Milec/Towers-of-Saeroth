# Eye of Fortune  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 289

**Price** 2,700 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

Adherents of [[Setting/Deities/Erastil]], god of the hunt, create these magical eye patches. An eye of fortune has a jeweled eye symbol on its front, allowing you to magically see through the eye patch as though it were transparent.

**Activate—Luck Beyond Sight** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]])
  **Trigger** You attack a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] creature and haven't attempted the flat check yet;  **Effect** You can roll the flat check for the concealed or hidden condition twice and use the higher result.
