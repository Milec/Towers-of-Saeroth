# Steel Cloud Road  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 216

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

This carved iron cloud appears unfinished and full of spurs, blemishes, and other imperfections. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Weapon Storm (Player Core)|_weapon storm_]]_ spell, the caster’s weapon propels them forward. Before choosing the spell’s area, [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice your Speed. This movement must be in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] and doesn’t trigger reactions. The spell is then cast from your new location.
