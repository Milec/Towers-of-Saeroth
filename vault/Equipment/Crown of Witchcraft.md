# Crown of Witchcraft  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused (GM Core)|Focused]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 288

**Usage** worn headwear

---

An elegant accoutrement for a witch who has come into the higher echelons of power, a _crown of witchcraft_ typically looks like a garland of flowering twigs, a jeweled circlet, or a tall hat of fine fabric. You gain a +1 item bonus to Intimidation checks, and if you're a witch, you gain a +2 item bonus to your patron skill.  
 If you have a familiar, you can attach a small portion of the crown's material to your familiar, such as a strip of fabric from a hat tied around its tail or a sprig of natural material linked to its collar; the familiar doesn't need to invest the item itself. If you do this, your familiar gains the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough|tough pet]] ability as long as the crown is invested by you.

**Activate—Defiant Hex** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a witch [[Traits/Hex|hex]] spell. If you don't spend this point by the end of this turn, it is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a witch.
