---

This ammunition bears a rune with three lines radiating out, each ending in a circle. When an activated _bola shot_ hits a target, it deals [[Rules/Nonlethal Attacks (Player Core)|nonlethal]] bludgeoning damage. Compare the attack roll to the target’s Reflex DC to determine the shot’s other effects

**Critical Success** The target falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Success** The target falls prone.  

**Failure** No additional effect.
