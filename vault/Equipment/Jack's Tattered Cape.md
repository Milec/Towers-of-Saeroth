# Jack's Tattered Cape  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 94

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

---

This ragged, mud-stained cape closes with a single black button engraved with a leering devil's face. When you [[Actions/Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump]] (or Vault, if you're [[Bestiary/Spring-Heeled Jack]]), you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]] until the start of your next turn.
