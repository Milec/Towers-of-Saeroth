Like the armor’s namesake, you can adjust the scales to better absorb kinetic energy at the cost of some mobility.

**Activate** single action Shift Scales;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Until the end of your next turn, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, but your Speeds are reduced by 10 feet (minimum 5 feet).
