# Wintershot (Artifact)  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|No Alignment]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Intelligent]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -212- A Voice in the Blight|Pathfinder #212: A Voice in the Blight]] pg. 73

**Usage** held in 1+ hand(s)

**Bulk** 1

---

**Perception** +35; precise [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and hearing within 30 feet

**Communication** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]] (partner only)

**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] +33, [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] +35, [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] +35

**Int** +6, **Wis** +7, **Cha** +5

**Will** +35

---  

The echo of the snowcaster Jelarial causes _Wintershot_ to become chill to the touch, yet despite feeling as cold as ice, she never causes harm or discomfort to her partner. Wintershot’s string issues a soft hissing sound, as of snow softly falling, and snowflakes drift down from the string and swiftly melt each time the bow is fired. _Wintershot’s_ voice is eager and excitable, often waxing poetic on a nearby object or creature of beauty or offering compliments to her partner.

_Wintershot_ is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|greater frost]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow (Player Core)|composite shortbow]]_. When making a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with _Wintershot_, targets don’t gain [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]] from the effects of mist or [[Rules/Precipitation (GM Core)|precipitation]], and circumstance penalties to attacks imparted from strong winds are negated.

**Activate—Auroral Shine** single action ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** _Wintershot_ can’t use this activation. When you activate Auroral Shine, fire an arrow at a target. If you hit, instead of dealing damage with the arrow, the creature struck must attempt a DC 43 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is surrounded by shimmering lights akin to the aurora, causing it to become dazzled for 2 rounds. If the creature was [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead. If the creature was already concealed for any other reason, it’s no longer concealed.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature also takes 6d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent cold damage]], and the lights affect the creature for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the creature also takes 6d6 persistent cold damage, and the lights affect the creature for 10 minutes.

**Activate—Signal Flare** single action ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Wintershot_ fires an arrow-shaped construct of ice straight upward. The arrow soars to a height of 500 feet, or until it strikes a solid surface like a ceiling. When it reaches its apex, it explodes in a brilliant burst, creating a 100-foot radius area of [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in the next 100 feet. In the night sky, this beacon can be seen clearly for miles. The beacon remains lit for up to 1 minute in a color of _Wintershot’s_ choice. Alternatively, _Wintershot_ can fire this Signal Flare like a normal arrow to attempt to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] a target her partner can see—if it hits, the arrow inflicts normal damage and attempts to counteract one darkness effect of Wintershot’s choice that affects the creature struck, with a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] rank of 9th and a +25 modifier to the roll (+30 modifier if the source of the [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] effect was created by a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]]).

**Activate—Rimecrust** two actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]])    **Effect** _Wintershot_ causes the deep chill of winter to settle upon a single creature she can see or hear within 30 feet. That creature must attempt a DC 43 Fortitude save, after which point they’re temporarily immune to Rimecrust for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature’s discomfort and shivering from the sudden drop in temperature causes it to become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of its next turn. Creatures that are [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to cold are immune to this effect.  

**Failure** The creature becomes encrusted with ice. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 minute, or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] from the crusting ice (DC 43).  

**Critical Failure** The creature is slowed 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by crusting ice until it Escapes (DC 43). After it Escapes, it remains slowed 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 minute from the lingering cold.
