# Ouroboros Buckles  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 18

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Buckle Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Buckle Armor]]

---

This ominous-looking _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+3 greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant (GM Core)|greater acid‑resistant]] [[Equipment/Armor/Buckle Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|buckle armor]]_ is deep red in color, favored by assassins who worship [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber]] or [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus (Divine Mysteries)|Asmodeus]], and if unfastened, the many belts and buckles writhe like living snakes. _Ouroboros buckles_ have the [[Traits/Comfort (Player Core)|comfort]] trait. However, while wearing _ouroboros buckles_, the unfathomable concept of infinity pulls at your mind, and each time you awaken, you’d swear the armor hissed into your ear while you were asleep. The hissing suggested secret wisdom to you in [[Setting/Languages/Aklo (Player Core)|Aklo]], though you only ever remember it vaguely, like a fading dream.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call forth the cursed regenerative blood of an ouroboros to recover from your wounds. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration (Monster Core)|regeneration]] 15 for 1 minute; fire damage deactivates this regeneration. Each time you regain Hit Points from this regeneration, if you’re in your normal form, you collapse into a Large swarm of Tiny snakes. This is a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effect that changes you into a battle form.

When you’re a swarm of snakes, you gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], imprecise scent (30 feet), a Speed of 20 feet, a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 20 feet, and a swim Speed of 20 feet. You have an AC of 16 + your level and ignore your armor’s check penalty and Speed reduction. Also, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to physical damage and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Rules/Area|area]] damage and splash damage. You can use none of your normal Strikes. Instead, you can use Swarming Bites, a single action that deals 4d4 piercing damage plus 2d6 poison damage to creatures in your space with a DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

If you’re in this battle form and are already at full HP when your regeneration occurs, the snakes instantaneously slither together, and you transform into your original form.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** A foe within 15 feet hits you with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] that deals slashing or piercing damage;  **Effect** Your _ouroboros buckles_ spray acidic blood in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] toward the foe, dealing 5d6 acid damage with a DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include blood from an [[Bestiary/Ouroboros|ouroboros]] to tan the leather.
