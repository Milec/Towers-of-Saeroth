[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]]

This delicate strand of spiderweb sticks to the target of a [[Spells/Spider Sting (Player Core)|_spider sting_]] spell, hampering their movement. Using this catalyst causes a creature afflicted with spider venom to become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]].
