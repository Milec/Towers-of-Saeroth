**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]]

---

Hardened clumps of _amphisbaena spittle_ can be harvested from the [[Bestiary/Amphisbaena|dual-headed snake's]] hunting grounds. When a casting of [[Spells/Web of Eyes|_web of eyes_]] is empowered with this catalyst, you can place an additional scrying sensor on the back of each target's head, in the shape of a closed snake's eye. When a target shares their vision with the others affected by the spell, the eye blinks open, preventing the target from being [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanked]] until the start of its next turn.
