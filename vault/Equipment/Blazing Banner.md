# Blazing Banner  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 140

**Usage** affixed or held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This magical banner shimmers in a fiery array of reds, oranges, and yellows. The rampant threads catch light in the wind and give the appearance of a blazing flame. Whenever you or an ally within the banner’s aura critically succeeds with a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], the Strike deals an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]].
