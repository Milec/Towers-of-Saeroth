# Lodestone Shield  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 234

**Price** 1,350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This _moderate reinforcing cold iron_ shield (Hardness 10, HP 92, BT 46) is inset with lodestones that draw ammunition toward it.

**Activate—Attact Projectile** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** A ranged weapon Strike targets a creature within 15 feet of you when you have this shield raised, and the atack hasn't yet rolled their attack;  **Effect** The triggering Strike targets you instead of its normal target. If it hits, you gain the effects of the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction.
