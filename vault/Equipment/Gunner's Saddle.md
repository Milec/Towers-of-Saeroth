# Gunner's Saddle  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 182

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters with access to firearms gain access to accessories that can be used with those weapons._

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** worn saddle

**Bulk** 2

---

Developed by marauders from the Mana Wastes, this clockwork saddle comes with a retractable weapon mount that can be used as a [[Equipment/Tripod (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|tripod]] to stabilize a weapon with the [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] trait. Just like a normal tripod, you Interact to deploy the tripod to stabilize the firearm, and then again to retract the tripod to move it. The saddle uses complex hydraulics to protect the steed from the firearm’s recoil.
