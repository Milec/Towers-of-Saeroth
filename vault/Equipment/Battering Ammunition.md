# Battering Ammunition  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 132

**Price** 15 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Etched with the symbol of a ram’s head, activated _battering ammunition_ magically gathers momentum as it flies, imparting this momentum to its target on a successful Strike. It deals bludgeoning damage instead of its normal damage type and pushes the target 5 feet on a successful hit.
