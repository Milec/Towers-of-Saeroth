single action

---

When you apply this thick, viscous oil to a weapon or suit of armor, that item immediately becomes magically potent. If the item is a weapon, it temporarily becomes a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon|+1 striking_ weapon]], or, if it's armor, it temporarily becomes [[Equipment/Magic Armor|+1 resilient armor]]. This lasts for 1 minute.
