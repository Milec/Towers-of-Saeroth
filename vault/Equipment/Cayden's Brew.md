_Cayden’s brew_ is like rich beer or ale, with a golden-brown color and foamy head. For 1 hour after you drink it, you have a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects. Also, you can use a single action to breathe out a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of intoxicating vapor with a burp that can be heard for 100 feet. Any creature in the vapor must attempt a DC 25 Fortitude saving throw. After you use this breath weapon, you can’t do so again for 1d4 rounds.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is clumsy 1 and stupefied 1 for 1d4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes clumsy 2, stupefied 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. The clumsy and stupefied conditions last until 1d4 rounds after the sickened condition ends.
