# Cooperative Waffles  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Processed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Processed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 47

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

10 minutes ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Flash-cooked on a [[Equipment/Waffle Iron|waffle iron]] and drizzled with alchemical syrups and compound butter, cooperative waffles bolster the supportive spirit of those who share the batch. You can split the waffles with one other creature. After you both eat half of the waffles to Activate them, when one of you uses [[Actions/Follow the Expert]] to follow the other, the circumstance bonus granted is 1 higher. The waffles' bonus lasts 24 hours or until you next make your daily preparations, whichever comes first. You can only be linked to one creature in this way at a time; if either of you eats cooperative waffles again, the effect of your previous waffles ends.
