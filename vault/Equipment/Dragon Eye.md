# Dragon Eye  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 223

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

Even in death, a dragon’s gaze is terrible to behold. When used to cast [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]] at 3rd rank or higher, an eye from a dragon causes the spell to manifest some of the creature’s fearsome aura and inspire terror. Creatures in the spell’s area must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save instead of a basic Reflex save, and in addition to taking the spell’s normal damage on a failure, they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. This adds the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits to the spell.
