# Abidance Blinders  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 148

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** worn headwear (animal) and worn headwear (you)

**Bulk** 1

---

While many skilled cavalry units have their own beloved mounts, troops often need to make do with whatever animals are available for mounted combat. These blinders, made of dark brown leather, nearly cover an animal’s eyes and prevent it from seeing the dangers around them. It takes 1 minute to affix the blinders on an animal, whose attitude toward you must be indifferent or better. These blinders can be used only on a domesticated animal that doesn’t have combat training or the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait. Animals with these blinders on do not suffer from the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition due to being in combat, nor do they automatically flee combat, as would be normal for an [[Rules/Animals (Player Core)|animal]] without combat training. Unless mounted by a rider with the paired headband, which is braided from the same dark brown leather, the animal’s speed is reduced to 10 feet.

**Activate—It Can’t Hurt You** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An animal within 30 feet wearing the paired blinders attempts a saving throw against a [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effect but hasn’t rolled yet;  **Effect** You grant a +1 status bonus to its saving throw against the triggering effect.
