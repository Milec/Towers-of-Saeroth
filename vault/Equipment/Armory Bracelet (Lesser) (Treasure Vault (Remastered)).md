# Armory Bracelet  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 153

**Usage** worn

---

Several small charms shaped like weapons hang from an _armory bracelet_, which is often brass. The bracelet has one charm each for the groups [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Dart|dart]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail|flail]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|hammer]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick|pick]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|polearm]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|shield]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sling|sling]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]], and [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]]. Rare versions of the armory bracelet include charms for [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearms]].

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull one charm from the bracelet. The charm transforms into a weapon of your choice from the charm's weapon group. If the weapon requires ammunition, it appears with a quiver or pouch with 20 pieces of ammunition for the weapon. The weapon is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]]_ weapon of the type you chose. After 1 minute, the weapon transforms into a non-magical version and remains until your next daily preparations. At that point, the weapon and any remaining ammunition crumble to dust and all the charms reappear on the bracelet. The weapon and ammunition created with the charm are noticeably different from others and can't be sold.
