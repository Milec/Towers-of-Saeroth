# Corpseward Pendant  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 153, [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 80

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn

---

A _corpseward pendant_ is usually shaped like the skull of a humanoid or small animal.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetectable]] to undead creatures for 10 minutes. [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]] can’t see, hear, or smell you, or detect you with sensory capabilities such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]]. A creature can attempt a DC 18 Will saving throw to ignore this effect. If an undead has reason to believe that undetected opponents are present, it can still attempt to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] you. If you attempt to use a [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] spell to damage undead, touch or damage an undead creature, or attack any creature while warded in this manner, the pendant’s effects immediately end. An undead creature who observes you in this manner or one who succeeds at the Will save is immune to the _corpseward pendant_ for 24 hours.
