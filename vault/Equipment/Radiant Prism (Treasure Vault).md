# Radiant Prism  *Item 16+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 127

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

Any armor or weapon this glass prism is affixed to glows softly with shifting colored lights. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 35.

- **Armor** ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) After you cast an abjuration spell by activating the prism, you glow with [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in a multitude of shimmering hues, shedding light like a torch and making you [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of your next turn.
- **Weapon** After you cast an [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Abjuration|abjuration]] spell by activating the prism, your Strikes with the weapon gain the [[Equipment/Brilliant (GM Core)|_brilliant_]] property rune until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Light (Player Core)|_light_]].
