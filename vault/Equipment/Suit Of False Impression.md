# Suit Of False Impression  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 203

**Price** 200 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|Explorer's Clothing]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer’s clothing]]_ is a simple, practical, and unassuming two-piece outfit. The comfortable fabric is enchanted with illusory magic to make it look as if the wearer is wearing heavy armor (as _[[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]]_). While wearing the suit, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] someone who would normally wear heavy armor.

**Activate—Faux Bulwark** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You strike an intimidating pose that causes enemies to fear your supposedly heavily armored form. Each foe in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] who can see you must attempt a DC 22 Will saving throw. On a failure, they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).
