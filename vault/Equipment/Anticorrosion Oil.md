# Anticorrosion Oil  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 86

**Price** 6 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

You can coat objects of 6 Bulk or less with _anticorrosion oil_. For 24 hours, the object takes half damage from acid and from all effects that specifically cause it to rust or corrode, such as contact with a [[Bestiary/Rust Monster|rust monster’s]] antennae.
