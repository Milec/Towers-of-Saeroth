# Tome of Scintillating Sleet  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 113

**Price** 430 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The first 12 pages of this tome tell the same story in two languages: 6 pages in [[Setting/Languages/Skald]] and 6 pages in the ancient [[Setting/Languages/Jotun]] dialect used by saumen kar, a species of ice-dwelling [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]]. The story is a tale of a saumen kar stricken with snow blindness after spending too long under the sun building snow giants.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] spell that deals damage, all creatures damaged by the spell are also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 3 rounds by light refracting and reflecting within and around the spell's chilling effects. If an affected creature critically failed its save against the required spell, or if you critically succeeded on your spell attack roll against the creature, it's instead [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round and then dazzled for 3 rounds.
