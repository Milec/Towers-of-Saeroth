# Kraken Bottle  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 56

**Price** 5,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

Coiled tentacles make it all but impossible to see anything else inside this ampoule. When opened, a Gargantuan [[Bestiary/Kraken (Monster Core)|kraken]] bursts forth, which can appear in water instead of on the ground. Its arms attempt to grasp up to four creatures with a reach of 60 feet. The kraken repositions [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] creatures to a different space within its reach unless the target succeeds at a DC 38 Fortitude save.

If the kraken is in water, it then releases a cloud of ink in an 80-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. This cloud has no effect outside of water. Creatures inside the cloud are [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], can’t use their sense of smell, and are exposed to kraken ink poison. The cloud dissipates after 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Kraken (Monster Core)|kraken]].
