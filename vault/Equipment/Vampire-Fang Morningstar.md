# Vampire-Fang Morningstar  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 38

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|Morningstar]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|morningstar]]_ is studded with teeth pulled from a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]], which usually requires an animate donor, given vampires’ tendency to turn to dust when destroyed.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per minute;  **Requirements** Your last action was a successful [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with this weapon and you’re not in direct sunlight; **Effect** The _vampire-fang morningstar_ absorbs blood from the target, healing the wielder for 10 Hit Points.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include teeth from a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]].
