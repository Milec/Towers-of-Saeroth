# Stage Fright Missive  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Missive (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Missive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 85

**Price** 22 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

two actions

envision, Interact

---

Composing a _stage fright missive_ usually involves creating a scathing review, insulting letter, or embarrassing image that ridicules the recipient. The activating creature must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be overcome with embarrassment for 1 hour, taking a –1 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. During this time, if the creature attempts to speak or perform in front of an audience, they become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. When they recover from this sickened condition, the missive’s effects end. You choose when composing the missive whether it remains as a non‑magical document or burns to ash after imparting its magic.
