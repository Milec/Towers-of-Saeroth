# Bedroll of Deep Slumber  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 142

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

This bedroll is made of especially fine cotton, stuffed with goose down, and the hem is inscribed with the sigils of the [[Setting/Planes/Dreamlands (GM Core)|Dreamlands]]. If you fall asleep in the bedroll, you gain 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last while you sleep and for 1 minute after you wake up, as well as a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects that occur while you are asleep, such as the [[Spells/Nightmare (Player Core)|_nightmare_]] spell.
