# Engulfing Snare  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 256

**Price** 900 gp

---

You arrange a spiky cage of bones, particularly tough vegetation, or other material to spring up when disturbed. The snare deals 9d8 piercing damage to the first creature to enter this square; that creature must attempt a DC 31 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is knocked aside by the snare's deployment, taking half damage, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature is captured by the cage, taking full damage and falling [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. It is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] while it remains within the cage. It can get free by [[Actions/Escape|Escaping]] (DC 31) or by destroying the cage (Hardness 5, HP 30, object immunities).  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage.
