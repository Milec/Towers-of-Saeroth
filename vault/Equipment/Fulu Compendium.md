# Fulu Compendium  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 161

**Price** 550 gp

---

This pamphlet-sized book records the various symbols seen on fulu and also contains some ready-made magic on a special page that restores itself every day at sunrise. Using the compendium as a reference grants a +2 item bonus to any skill checks to determine a fulu's function or authenticity.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You rip a page from the _fulu compendium_ and cast it in a wide arc; as it flies in that arc, it multiplies into a storm of fulus. All undead creatures in a 30-foot emanation are affected by a [[Equipment/Spirit-Sealing Fulu|_spirit-sealing fulu_]] with a save DC of 25.
