# Ring of Torag  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Crown of the Kobold King

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 116

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This simple golden ring has a large, red gemstone set into it that sparkles with an inner fire.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per round;  **Requirements** You're taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]; **Effect** You call upon the ring to put out the flame and its magic attempts to bring the fire under control. This is especially effective assistance against persistent fire damage, so you gain an immediate flat check to remove the persistent fire damage, and the DC typically decreases from 15 to 10.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're targeted by a [[Traits/Fire|fire]] effect;  **Effect** The ring's red gemstone flashes with light, granting you resistance 5 against fire and a +1 status bonus on your AC or saving throw against the fire effect targeting you; these benefits end as soon as the fire effect is resolved.
