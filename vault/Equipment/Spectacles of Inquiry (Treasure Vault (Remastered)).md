# Spectacles of Inquiry  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 147

**Price** 1,750 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

Anything viewed through these thin spectacles looks crisp and clear, and the earpieces accentuate sounds around you. You gain a +2 item bonus to Perception checks.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The spectacles key in on someone to show you their social cues in perfect clarity. Choose a creature you can see. You gain a +3 item bonus on Perception checks you make to use [[Actions/Sense Motive]] against that creature. This benefit lasts until you Activate the item again in this way.

 If you’re an [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|investigator]], you can use this activation as part of the [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]] action (even if you’re doing it as a free action). Alternatively, you can use it as part of the [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]] activity, even though you can’t see the creature. You must choose the same creature you chose for Devise a Stratagem or Pursue a Lead.

**Craft Requirements** You are an investigator.
