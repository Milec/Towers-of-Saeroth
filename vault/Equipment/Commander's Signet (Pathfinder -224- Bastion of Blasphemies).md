---

A _commander’s signet_ is typically a silver ring emblazoned with the coat of arms or heraldry of a wealthy noble family, or the insignia of a mercenary group. A badge of office often worn by soldiers during the Shining Crusade in Ustalav, many _commander’s signets_ bear iconography associated with Aroden, Arazni, or Iomedae, but those found in this adventure bear the heraldry of the Arudora family. The signet grants an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks made to Make an Impression or to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks made to Coerce, and can be activated to bolster the morale of a nearby target.

**Activate—Bolster Bravery** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You fill a creature you touch with bravery and heroic conviction, granting it a status bonus to attack rolls, Perception checks, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 minute. The status bonus is doubled for saving throws made against fear effects.
