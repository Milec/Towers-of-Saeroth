# Water Bomb  *Item 1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]], [[Traits/Splash]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 79

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

This bladder of water explodes when put under pressure or upon being punctured, dealing minimal damage, neutralizing acids, and dousing flames. A water bomb deals the listed bludgeoning damage and bludgeoning [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage. On a hit, if the target is suffering from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid or persistent fire damage]], it can attempt a flat check to end that persistent damage immediately. As this is particularly effective assistance, the DC of the flat check is reduced from 15 to 10 for this check. On a hit against an unattended, non-magical fire, the bomb extinguishes the fire, or extinguishes one square of fire for a larger fire. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls, and some types extinguish wider areas of fire.
