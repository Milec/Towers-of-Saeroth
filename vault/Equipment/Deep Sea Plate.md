# Deep Sea Plate  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 121

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

---

The interior of this heavy, brass _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ is lined with waterproof fabric, especially covering the seams between plates. When worn, it provides a sealed environment that protects you from [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|drowning]] as well as allowing you to move more freely while underwater.

**Activate—Deep Dive** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _deep sea plate_ enables you to breathe underwater and gives you a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] equal to half your land Speed while in depths of 500 feet or less for 1 hour.
