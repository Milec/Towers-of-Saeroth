# Ghosthand's Comet  *Item 23*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 179

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

The barrel of this long rifle is translucent in places, forming a swirled pattern along the metal, and its stock is formed of crimson wood. _Ghosthand’s Comet_ is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+4 major striking]] [[Equipment/Bane|beast‑bane]] [[Equipment/Impactful|greater impactful]] advanced firearm_ with a range increment of 300 feet. It deals 5d8 force damage and has the [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Concussive (NPC Core)|concussive]], [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]], and [[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal d12]] traits. As a star gun, _Ghosthand’s Comet_ runs on magic and doesn’t use ammunition or black powder. The weapon is silent when fired.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You attempt a ranged Strike with _Ghosthand’s Comet_;  **Effect** For the triggering Strike, _Ghosthand’s Comet_ changes its damage type to your choice of acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic.

**Activate** single action envision;     **Effect** On your next attempt at a ranged Strike with _Ghosthand’s Comet_, the shot phases through any material or magical obstacle, such as a wall of force, in its path, ignoring all [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]]. You must attempt the Strike by the end of your turn or this effect is lost.

**Destruction** If the [[Bestiary/Grim Reaper (Monster Core)|Grim Reaper]] slays the wielder of _Ghosthand’s Comet_, the Reaper’s scythe, as it strikes the killing blow, is destined to slice the star gun in half.
