# Boots of Quick Marching  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 148

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

These brown leather shoes have surprisingly thick soles, as though a cobbler had recently repaired them. The tan laces always pull to exactly the right tautness for your feet and ankles to feel supported. You can perform the [[Actions/Hustle (Player Core)|Hustle]] exploration mode activity for twice as many minutes as normal, equal to your Constitution modifier × 20 (minimum 20).

**Activate—Big Step** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The soles on your boots grow even thicker for a moment, proving a bounce to your step. You [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] twice.
