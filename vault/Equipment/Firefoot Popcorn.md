# Firefoot Popcorn  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 48

**Price** 75 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

When you consume firefoot popcorn, for 1 minute, you can Leap double the normal distance. You can also attempt to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] as a single action. If you do, you don’t perform the initial [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] (nor do you fall if you don’t Stride 10 feet).

Each time you Leap 10 feet or more, your feet make a popping sound that can be heard from at least 100 feet, and the space you left fills with waves of intense heat. For 1 minute, that square is [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (Player Core)|hazardous terrain]] that deals 1d6 fire damage to any creature that moves into it.
