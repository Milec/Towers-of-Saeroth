# Stampede Medallion  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 245

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

When crafted, a _stampede medallion_ looks like nothing more than small silver disk, about the same size as a silver piece. When you invest this medallion for your eidolon, it changes shape to appear as a tiny, bejeweled facsimile of the eidolon, magically attached just over your eidolon’s heart. While your eidolon wears the medallion, they gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]].

**Activate—Impossible Tide** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your eidolon momentarily manifests into a stampede of dozens of copies of themself. The stampede rampages out in every direction, swerving around your allies while trampling any foe on the ground in an [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around your eidolon with a radius equal to your eidolon’s Speed. Each of these foes takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage, with a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. On a critical failure, the foe is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. After dealing damage, the stampede of eidolons vanishes as quickly as it appeared.
