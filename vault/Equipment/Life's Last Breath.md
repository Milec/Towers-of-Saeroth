# Life's Last Breath  *Item 18*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -155- Lord of the Black Sands|Pathfinder #155: Lord of the Black Sands]] pg. 76

**Price** 23,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [Rhoka Sword](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=120)

---

The twin serrated blades of this legendary [rhoka sword](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=120) are dull black and constantly drip with blood. _Life's Last Breath_ is said to have been forged centuries ago for an unknown [[Bestiary/Families/Urdefhan|urdefhan]] general and has since fallen into the hands of countless other powerful urdefhan warlords.

 _Life's Last Breath_ is a _+3 greater striking [[Equipment/Unholy|unholy]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] rhoka sword_ with the following special abilities.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You successfully Strike a creature with _Life's Last Breath_; **Effect** You cast a 9th-level [[Spells/Paralyze|_paralyze_]] spell on the target.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with _Life's Last Breath_;  **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 38 Fortitude save or take 4d6 negative damage; you gain an equal amount of temporary Hit Points that last 1 minute.
