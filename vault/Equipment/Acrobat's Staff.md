# Acrobat's Staff  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 73

**Price** 235 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff]]

---

This _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]]_ is particularly long, making it useful as a balancing pole. While you wield it, the acrobat’s staff releases chalk dust to make your grip more secure, granting you a +1 circumstance bonus to your Reflex DC against checks to [[Actions/Disarm]] you of it.

**Activate** ⤾ command;   **Trigger** You fail or critically fail an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] and would fall;  **Effect** Reroll the Acrobatics check and take the better result.
