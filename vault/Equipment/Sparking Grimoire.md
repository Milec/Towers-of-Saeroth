# Sparking Grimoire  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 230

**Bulk** L

---

The black leather cover of this spellbook is embossed with a pattern of lightning, and sparks crackle across it. Touching the book makes one’s hair stand slightly on end.

**Activate—Static Shock** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [[Rules/Cast a Spell|Cast a prepared Spell]] with the [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] trait, you charge yourself with electrical energy. Until the end of your next turn, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] deal an additional 2d6 electricity damage, and creatures targeting you with a melee Strike or spell with a range of touch take 2d6 electricity damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC.
