# Energy Adaptive  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 12

**Price** 2,600 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

A complex pattern of protective symbols gives protection against various forms of energy, but only part of the runic structure can be active at a given time.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You take acid, cold, electricity, or fire damage;  **Effect** You gain resistance 5 to the triggering damage type. This doesn't apply to the triggering damage. This resistance lasts until you Activate this rune again or the armor is no longer invested by you.
