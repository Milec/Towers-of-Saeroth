# Sure-Step Potion  *Item 4*

single action

Interact

---

The light-brown liquid of a _sure-step potion_ helps you find your footing. After drinking it, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] for 1 hour. In addition, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], and you aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] on [[Rules/Uneven Ground (Player Core)|uneven ground]].
