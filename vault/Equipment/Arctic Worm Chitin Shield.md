# Arctic Worm Chitin Shield  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 100

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 4

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|Tower Shield]]

---

This tower shield is crafted from interlocking sections of an arctic cave worm’s chitin that are rimed with constant frost. It has Hardness 13, HP 104, and BT 52.

**Activate—Misty Chill** single action ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise the Shield]] and an icy mist begins to flow off it. Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to cold and any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you takes 3d6 cold damage (DC 29 basic Reflex save).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include chitin from an arctic [[Bestiary/Cave Worm|cave worm]].
