# Spirit Trap  *Item 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -196- The Summer That Never Was|Pathfinder #196: The Summer That Never Was]] pg. 83

**Price** 5 gp

---

A _spirit trap_ consists of a net dipped in water that has been steeped with sacred herbs to better combat phantoms. This snare's components function as a [[Equipment/Net|net]] when not set up. You set this snare up in a 10-foot-by-10-foot area. The first creature with the  [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] trait that steps into the area must attempt a DC 16 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is immobilized until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 16).
