**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check to [[Actions/Make an Impression]].

---

This red fulu contains blessings for one's relationship from [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn]], goddess of beauty and love. You get a +2 status bonus to the Diplomacy check, and if you roll a critical failure on the check, you get a failure instead.
