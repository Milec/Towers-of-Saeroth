This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickle]]_ is made of an impossibly dark steel that absorbs all surrounding light, causing shadows to cling to its blade and those around it to feel an unnatural chill.

**Activate—Light Harvest** single action (darkness, [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You swing the _void sickle_ in a wide arc, drawing light into the blade. All non-magical light sources within 30 feet are immediately extinguished, and the next melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] you make with the _void sickle_ before the end of your turn deals an additional 1d6 void damage.
