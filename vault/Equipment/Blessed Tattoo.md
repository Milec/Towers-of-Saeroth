# Blessed Tattoo  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 92

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Orcs and half-orcs from the Mwangi Expanse have access to blessed tattoos_

**Price** 90 gp

**Usage** tattoo

---

You can activate the tattoo as a reaction instead of a two-action activity, triggered when a demon attacks you or you attempt a saving throw against a demon’s ability.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain the effect of [[Spells/Protection|_protection against evil_]].
