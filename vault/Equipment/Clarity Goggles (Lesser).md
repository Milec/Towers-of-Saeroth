# Clarity Goggles  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 146

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The counteract check from a pair of _clarity goggles_ has a counteract rank of half the item’s level rounded up._

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

_Clarity goggles_ feature faceted lenses that filter your surroundings from several slightly different angles at once, giving you a sharper picture of them. While wearing the goggles, you gain a +1 item bonus to visual [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You focus on your environment and the creatures around you to see them as they really are. The GM rolls a secret [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check using your Perception bonus against any [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] effect created by a 3rd-level or lower spell or a creature of 8th level or lower. You must be able to see the illusion, and it must be within 60 feet. If the check succeeds, you see through the illusion for 10 minutes.
