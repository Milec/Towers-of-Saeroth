# Fulu Of The Drunken Monkey  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 219

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You attempt a Fortitude save against an ingested poison.

---

The monkey on this fulu sits on the point of the armor just over the bearer’s stomach, happily drinking any intoxicants that come flowing down. When you activate the fulu, you gain a +2 status bonus to the triggering Fortitude save and to Fortitude saves against the same poison for the next minute. If the triggering poison was normal alcohol, you instead automatically succeed at the triggering save and gain a +4 status bonus on saves against alcohol for 1 minute.
