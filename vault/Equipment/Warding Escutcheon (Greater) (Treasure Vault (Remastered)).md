# Warding Escutcheon  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 23

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 4

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield]]

---

An image of a keyhole adorns the coat of arms of this stone [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield|tower shield]] (Hardness 10, HP 40, BT 20).

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The shield becomes a locked door in an empty, adjacent space no wider than 5 feet, becoming flush with the surrounding structure so it blocks all passage. The door has the same Hardness, HP, and Broken Threshold as the shield, with [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DCs of 25 to open. The door remains in place for 1 hour, until it's opened or broken, or until you spend an action to end this effect, at which point the shield returns to your hand (or falls to the ground in your space if your hands are full).

**Activate**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The shield floats lazily in the air as bricks quickly fly from it to form a squat stone tower around it. You cast [[Spells/Cozy Cabin|_cozy cabin_]], with the shield becoming the door.
