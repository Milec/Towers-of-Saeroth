# Messenger's Ring  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 292

**Usage** worn

---

This silver signet ring changes to match the insignia of a leader or organization you serve (or your own face, if you serve no one else). It grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks and lets you cast [[Spells/Message (Player Core)|_message_]] as an arcane innate spell at will.

**Activate—Dispatch Messenger**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The ring casts [[Spells/Animal Messenger (Player Core)|_animal messenger_]] to your specification. The animal is a magical creature that springs from the ring, and its appearance suits the iconography or heraldry of the leader or organization represented by the ring.
