# Foxglove Token  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

**Bulk** L

---

This small piece of wood is finely carved to depict a foxglove. The spell DC of any spell cast by [[Rules/Activating a Magic Item|Activating]] this item is 17.

- **Armor** You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 2 to poison damage.
- **Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] spell by activating the foxglove token, your Strikes with the weapon it's attached to deal an additional 1d4 poison damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Puff of Poison (Player Core 2)|_puff of poison_]].
