# Hellhusk Shroud  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 211

**Price** 230 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

---

This hooded cloak is popular among those who must evade or surveil fiendish authorities. The grisly secrets behind this garment belies its innocuous appearance; its fabrics include threads woven from the hair, blood, and scales of various devils. When invested, a _hellhusk shroud_ doesn't appear to radiate a magic aura to [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]] or similar spells unless the spells are 4th rank or higher. As long as you're wearing the cloak, you can read and speak [[Setting/Languages/Diabolic]].

**Activate—Horned Husk** single action ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The cloak wraps around you, becoming a second skin that suffuses you with Hell's corrosive brimstone. Your appearance becomes that of a nondescript low-level devil, and you gain a +1 status bonus to your Deception checks when you [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to maintain your disguise. To creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent (Monster Core)|scent]], you also smell like a devil.

**Activate—Hellish Senses** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull your hood up, allowing the remnants of fallen devils to nestle closely and whisper Hell's secrets. You gain fiendsense as an imprecise sense within 60 feet, allowing you to sense creatures with the fiend trait as a [[Rules/Vague Senses (Player Core)|vague sense]]. This effect lasts for 10 minutes or until you take this action again
