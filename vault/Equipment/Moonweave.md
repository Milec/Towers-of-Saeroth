# Moonweave  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 204

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

Your armor bathes your wounds in the cool light of the moon. While wearing this armor, whenever you would be healed by a spell or [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect, you recover an additional 2 Hit Points. You regain additional Hit Points from _moonweave_ only the first time you regain HP from a given [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] spell or effect, so a spell or effect that heals you repeatedly over a duration would restore additional Hit Points only once, when you’re first healed.
