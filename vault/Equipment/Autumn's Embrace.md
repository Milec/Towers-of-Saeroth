# Autumn's Embrace  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 14

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leaf Weave]]

---

Woven by fey seamstresses as rewards for servants of nature, countless leaves continually changing colors in autumnal hues comprise autumn’s embrace, a suit of _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Invisibility (GM Core)|invisibility]] [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leaf weave]]_. Leaves shed from the armor as they might fall in autumn. When activating the armor’s _invisibility_ property rune, you disappear in a swirl of colorful leaves.

**Activate** two actions command, envision ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call forth a storm of leaves from _autumn’s embrace_. These leaves swirl in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] for 1 minute. Creatures within the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within the leaves. However, you can see through this concealment. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the activation.
