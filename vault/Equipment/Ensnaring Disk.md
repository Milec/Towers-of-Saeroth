# Ensnaring Disk  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 93

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You use the affixed shield to [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] a melee weapon attack

**Requirement** You are an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]].

---

This coin-sized metal disk is inscribed with arcane symbols and mounted on the inner surface of a shield. When you Activate the disk, the triggering weapon momentarily sticks to your shield, allowing you to attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] it from its wielder with a +2 item bonus. If you roll a critical failure on this check, you get a failure instead.
