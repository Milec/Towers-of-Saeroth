# Dragonscale Cameo  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Talisman]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 93

**Price** 400 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

envision

---

This ornamental pin, consisting of a single preserved [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] scale from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Chromatic|chromatic]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Metallic|metallic]] dragon mounted in a setting of precious metal, is typically affixed between the shoulder blades. When you Activate the pin, a pair of draconic wings matching the color of the scale unfurl from your shoulders, granting you a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 50 feet for 5 minutes.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one scale from an adult or older dragon.
