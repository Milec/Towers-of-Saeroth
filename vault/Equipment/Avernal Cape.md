# Avernal Cape  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 78

**Usage** incorporated into armor

**Bulk** L

---

Made from preserved crimson worm hide, this thick leather cape can deflect incoming blows without serious damage. It functions as a [[Equipment/Dueling Cape|dueling cape]] and while moved into a protective position, it also grants you fire resistance equal to twice the relic's number of gifts.  
 **Forming the Bond** While wearing the cape, you defeat a foe and spare their life.  
 **Advancing the Bond** Befriend beasts and terrify foes with your physical presence and prowess.  
 **Minor Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Feral Claws|feral claws]], [[Equipment/Relics/Flare Bolt|flare bolt]], [[Equipment/Relics/Heat Haze|heat haze]]  
 **Major Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Form of Fury|form of fury]] (snake form), [[Equipment/Relics/Searing Wave|searing wave]]
