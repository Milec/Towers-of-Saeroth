# Screech Shooter  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 155

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

Built from the larynx of a [[Bestiary/Terror Shrike|terror shrike]] or similar animal that possesses a frightening screech or similar special ability, a _screech shooter_ is a special _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Harmona Gun (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|harmona gun]]_ designed to fire terrifying blasts of sound. A screech shooter deals [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage but can otherwise be used like a normal harmona gun.

**Activate—Screech Shot** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The screech shooter unleashes a frightening wail. All creatures in a 30-foot emanation from you must attempt a DC 25 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the larynx of a creature with a frightening screech.
