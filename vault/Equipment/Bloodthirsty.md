# Bloodthirsty  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 42

**Price** 8,500 gp

**Usage** etched onto a slashing or piercing melee weapon

---

The magic in this rune sings in time with your attacks and coaxes you into finishing your opponent. When you critically hit a target that's taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]], your target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]].

**Activate** ⤾ envision;   **Trigger** You reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with the weapon;  **Effect** You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to twice the creature's level. These Hit Points remain for 1 minute.
