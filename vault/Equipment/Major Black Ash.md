**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

On certain rare occasions, when a particularly despoiled tree or a powerful demonic fungal infestation (such as a Jeharlu Spore) is destroyed, the grimy black ash that remains behind functions as a [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|catalyst]] called black ash. A [[Spells/Wall of Thorns (Player Core)|_wall of thorns_]] empowered with this catalyst gains the [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] trait and appears diseased and toxic, with greasy filaments of dripping fungus growing through its vines. A creature damaged by this wall’s thorns takes an additional amount of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]].
