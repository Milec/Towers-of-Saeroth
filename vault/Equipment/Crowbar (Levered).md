# Crowbar  *Item 0+*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 288

**Price** 5 sp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** L

---

When [[Actions/Force Open|Forcing Open]] an object that doesn't have an easy grip, a crowbar makes it easier to gain the necessary leverage. Without a crowbar, prying something open takes a –2 item penalty to the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Force Open.
