# Amulet of the Third Eye  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 102

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn amulet

**Bulk** L

---

This large brass medallion hangs low on the torso. It’s shaped in the form of an unblinking eye, with a ring of turquoise as the iris and an orb of jet serving as the pupil. The amulet grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks. When you invest the amulet, you either increase your Wisdom score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever is higher.

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Truesight|_true seeing_]].
