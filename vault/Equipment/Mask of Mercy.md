# Mask of Mercy  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 154

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn mask

---

This porcelain or alabaster mask portrays an angelic visage of kindness and mercy. The mask grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks.

**Activate** single action envision ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You are about to roll a variable number of Hit Points you restore from an action with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait;  **Effect** Roll twice to determine the number of Hit Points you restore and take the higher result.
