# Mortalis Coin  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 94

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You are reduced to 0 Hit Points by damage but not immediately killed

**Requirement** You are an expert in Fortitude saves.

---

This small golden coin is usually stamped with the image of a boar or other resilient creature. If the triggering damage would cause you to become [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying 2]] (typically due to a critical hit or your critical failure), you become dying 1 instead. In addition, for 10 minutes, you die from the dying condition at dying 5, rather than dying 4.
