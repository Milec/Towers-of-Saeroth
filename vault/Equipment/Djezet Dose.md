Some mages carry vials of [[Equipment/Djezet|djezet]] for its magic-enhancing properties. When you drink a djezet dose, if your next action is to Cast a Spell, you can apply the effects of the [[Feats/Reach Spell]] or [[Feats/Widen Spell]] metamagic feats.

**Craft Requirements** At least 300 gp of djezet.
