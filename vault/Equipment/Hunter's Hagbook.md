# Hunter's Hagbook  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 77

**Price** 700 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This magical book has its official title, _The Grimoire of Lady Jayne Cutter_, written on an inside page in illuminated letters. Among occultists and other scholars, it's known by its colloquial name: the _Hunter's Hagbook_. The book is a grimoire created and used by a legendary Varisian monster hunter. The _Hunter's Hagbook_ can hold 100 spells but the following spells are always present in the book, can't be removed from it, and count against the total: [[Spells/Blind Eye|_blind eye_]], [[Spells/Blood Duplicate|_blood duplicate_]], [[Spells/Caster's Imposition|_caster's imposition_]], [[Spells/Hag's Fruit|_hag's fruit_]], [[Spells/Ritual Obstruction|_ritual obstruction_]], [[Spells/Web of Influence|_web of influence_]]

 When you attempt a saving throw against a [[Bestiary/Abilities/Coven|coven]] spell, you can immediately end the duration of a spell affecting you that you prepared from this book. If you do, the result of your saving throw is one degree of success better than your roll (for example, a critical failure becomes a failure).
