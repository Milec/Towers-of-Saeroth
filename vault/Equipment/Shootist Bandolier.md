# Shootist Bandolier  *Item 0*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 73

**Price** 1 gp

---

This leather bandolier holds up to three repeating hand crossbow magazines in leather pockets that pop open with the quick flick of a thumb. You reduce the reload time for a [repeating hand crossbow](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=160) magazine from the bandolier by 1, to a total of 2 actions. You can wear only one shootist bandolier at a time.
