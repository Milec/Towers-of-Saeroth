# Sluggish Bracelet  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 193

**Usage** worn

---

A silver charm bracelet, a _sluggish bracelet_ appears to be a [[Equipment/Bracelet of Dashing|_bracelet of dashing_]], granting you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks. If the curse goes unrecognized, you think you can Activate it to gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed for 1 minute. Instead, its activation is as follows.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** You take a –10-foot penalty to your Speed for 1 minute, and the bracelet fuses to you. Thereafter, it grants you no bonus to Acrobatics checks, and it imposes a –5-foot status penalty to your Speed.
