# Warding Statuette  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 129

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

This small figurine is carved from soapstone in the shape of a deity or guardian, facing both front and back to indicate unflagging vigilance. The spell attack roll of any spell cast by activating this item is +13.

- **Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 1 against ranged weapon attacks.
- **Weapon** When you hit with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] using the affixed weapon, or when a spell effect you created by activating the statuette hits with a Strike, you can choose a creature adjacent to the creature that was hit. That creature gains a +1 status bonus to AC until the start of your next turn. No more than one creature can benefit from this each turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]].
