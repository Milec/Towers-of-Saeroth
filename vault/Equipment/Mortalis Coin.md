envision

**Trigger** You are reduced to 0 Hit Points by damage but not immediately killed

**Requirement** You are an expert in Fortitude saves.

---

This small golden coin is usually stamped with the image of a [[Bestiary/Boar (Monster Core)|boar]] or other resilient creature. If the triggering damage would cause you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] 2 (typically due to a critical hit or your critical failure), you become dying 1 instead. In addition, for 10 minutes, you die from the dying condition at dying 5, rather than dying 4.
