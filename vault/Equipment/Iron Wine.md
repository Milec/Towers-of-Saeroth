---

This strong, clear liquor is made from fermented rice. When you drink a cup of iron wine, your sweat becomes highly combustive for the next 10 minutes, igniting with the slightest bit of friction. This causes your [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks to deal an additional 1d4 [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage for the duration of the effect. Drinking more than one cup of iron wine in a single day gives you [[Rules/Diagonal Movement|weakness]]  5 to fire until your next daily preparations.
