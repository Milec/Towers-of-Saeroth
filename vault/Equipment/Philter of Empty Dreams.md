# Philter of Empty Dreams  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Seven Dooms for Sandpoint

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 185

**Price** 90 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

A _philter of empty dreams_ is a dose of glowing blue liquid that prevents dreams. When you drink a _philter of empty dreams_, you have no dreams for the next 8 hours and gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws against dream- or nightmare-themed effects (including saving throws against the effects of [[Equipment/Midnight Milk|midnight milk]]). If you're suffering from an addiction to midnight milk when you drink a _philter of empty dreams_, the potion attempts to counteract the addiction with a counteract level of 4 and a +15 modifier for the roll. If an [[Bestiary/Intellect Devourer|intellect devourer]] who's controlling a stolen body uses the body to drink a _philter of empty dreams_, the intellect devourer must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be forced to Exit the Body immediately.
