# Gruesome Bolt  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 133

**Price** 50 gp

**Ammunition** ballista bolt ([[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Ballista (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|ballista]])

---

This sickly green ammunition emits an eerie wail as it flies through the air. When a _gruesome bolt_ impacts its target, it embeds a seed of fear in nearby creatures. All creatures within 10 feet of the target are subject to the effects of a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Fear (Player Core)|_fear_]] spell (DC 19).

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _fear_ (3rd rank).
