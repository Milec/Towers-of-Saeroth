# Cape of Illumination  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 85

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

**Access** Second-mark members of the Firebrands have access to this item.

---

This golden cape, embroidered with vibrant red sun rays, sparkles like sunlight reflecting off the ocean.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** The cape glows with the effects of 1st-level [[Spells/Light|_light_]]. You can Activate the cape again to Dismiss the effect.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw the cape and unleash a blinding flash of light. All enemies within 30 feet that can see you must attempt a DC 19 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 1 minute. The creature can spend an Interact action rubbing its eyes to end the blinded condition.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 round and dazzled for 1 hour.
