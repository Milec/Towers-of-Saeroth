# Globe of Shrouds  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Censer]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 123

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The body of this censer is made of transparent crystal banded with dark iron. This globe hangs from a sturdy chain attached to a simple steel rod with a smooth grip.

**Activate—Light Incense** two actions ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])**Cost** incense worth at least 5 gp; **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** As you light the incense, barely visible smoke issues from the censer in a hazy 20-foot emanation. Creatures that are in the haze or later enter it are wreathed in wisps of smoke; these wisps last while the creature is in the smoke's aura and until the start of its next turn if it leaves the haze. An ally in the aura is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] and gains a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks. Any enemy in the aura that is or becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] appears as a translucent shape to you and your allies—it's no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], but it remains concealed.
