You can slather this silvery paste onto one melee or ranged weapon, or 10 pieces of ammunition. Silver salve spoils quickly, so you must use a vial all at once. For the next hour, the weapon or ammunition counts as [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] instead of its normal precious material (such as cold iron) for any physical damage it deals.
