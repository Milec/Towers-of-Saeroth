**Trigger** You make an [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] with the affixed weapon.

---

This teardrop-shaped piece of iron is a naturally occurring mineral, said to be a solidified tear of bellicose joy shed by [[Setting/Deities/Gorum (Divine Mysteries)|Gorum]] during his battles. When you activate the _Gorum’s tear_, you roll your next attack roll twice and take the better result, ignoring any circumstance penalties. You then become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the creature you targeted until the beginning of your next turn.
