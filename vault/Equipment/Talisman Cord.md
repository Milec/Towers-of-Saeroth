# Talisman Cord  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 117

**Usage** worn

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society.

---

This thin leather cord bears delicate runic markings and threads through an item that can bear affixed [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talismans]]. When you [[Actions/Affix a Talisman]] to that item, you can thread the talisman onto the cord, activating the cord's preserving effects. The cord is attuned to a particular school of magic, chosen when the cord was created. It gains the corresponding trait for that school. When you activate a talisman threaded through a cord with the same magic school trait that's also the cord's level or lower, attempt a DC 16 flat check. On a success, that talisman is not consumed and can be used again.
