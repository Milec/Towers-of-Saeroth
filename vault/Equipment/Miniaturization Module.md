# Miniaturization Module  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 80

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

A _miniaturization module_ is a bulky clockwork belt interwoven with clear rubber tubing. These tubes are filled with distilled liquid magic, which serves as a power source. While wearing a _miniaturization module_, you gain a +1 item bonus to checks to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].

**Activate—Miniaturize Me!** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You instantly shrink, becoming Tiny in size. Your equipment shrinks with you but returns to its original size if removed. While Tiny, your reach changes to 0 feet. You remain Tiny for 10 minutes, but you can Dismiss this effect.

**Activate—Miraculous Escape** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]];  **Effect** You instinctively trigger the miniaturization module and wiggle free, then move and grow larger, seemingly escaping in a flicker of motion. You become Tiny, then attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] the triggering effect, gaining a +4 circumstance bonus to this check. If you successfully Escape, you [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into an adjacent space. Regardless of the result, you then return to your original size.
