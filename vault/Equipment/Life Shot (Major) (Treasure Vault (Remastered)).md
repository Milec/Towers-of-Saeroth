# Life Shot  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 43

**Ammunition** round

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Life shot is a special cartridge that carries a small dose of [[Equipment/Elixir of Life|elixir of life]]. A creature hit by activated life shot takes no damage from the successful attack, instead receiving healing and gaining an item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison|poisons]] for 1 minute. On a critical hit, roll the healing received twice and take the better result (this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect). A target willing to be hit by this attack is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against it.
