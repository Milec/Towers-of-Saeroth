**Activate—Call Down Lightning** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You conjure a bolt of electricity down onto yourself, dealing 6d6 electricity damage to all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]].
