This double-ended silver baton functions as a mundane silver mace. Its shining surface glimmers in the light, making it easy to signal and inspire troops.

**Activate—Rally Forth** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy triggers a reaction from a troop you’re currently commanding, but that troop has already used its reaction this turn; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The troop under your command gains a second reaction that they must immediately use against the triggering enemy.
