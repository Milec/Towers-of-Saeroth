# Unnerving  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 205

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

The sound of weapons clanging against a suit of _unnerving_ armor transforms into an inhuman screech that unsettles enemies and makes them hesitate to attack again. Any creature that hits you with a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its next melee Strike that targets you before the end of your next turn.
