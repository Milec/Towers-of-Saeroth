# Redpitch Bomb  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Redpitch Alchemy]]

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

Sap from redpitch pines, if properly distilled into a gummy, incendiary mass, ignites when exposed to the air. A redpitch bomb deals the listed fire damage, [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and splash damage. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

## Redpitch Bomb (Lesser)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Redpitch Alchemy]]

**Price** 3 gp

---

The bomb deals 1 fire damage, 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and 1 fire splash damage. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until the start of your next turn.

## Redpitch Bomb (Moderate)  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Redpitch Alchemy]]

**Price** 10 gp

---

You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 2 fire damage, 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and 2 fire splash damage. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until the start of your next turn.

## Redpitch Bomb (Greater)  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Redpitch Alchemy]]

**Price** 250 gp

---

You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls. The bomb deals 3 fire damage, 3d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and 3 fire splash damage. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] until the start of your next turn.

## Redpitch Bomb (Major)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Redpitch Alchemy]]

**Price** 2,500 gp

---

You gain a +3 bonus on attack rolls. The bomb deals 4 fire damage, 4d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and 4 fire splash damage. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 3]] until the start of your next turn.
