The legendary blade _Serithtial_ is said to have been forged by [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]], goddess of honor, justice, and valor. She is an intelligent _+4 major striking [[Equipment/Holy|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Bastard Sword|bastard sword]]_. Although the sword is made of steel, Serithtial is also treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] and [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]], allowing her to deal more damage to certain supernatural creatures. She glows as bright as a torch, but you or _Serithtial_ can suppress or resume this glow as a single action, which has the [[Traits/Consumable|concentrate]] trait. In addition, Strikes with _Serithtial_ deal an additional 2d6 damage against creatures that are worshippers of [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon]], and while wielding _Serithtial_, you constantly benefit from [[Spells/Death Ward|_death ward_]] and [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]] spells that function only against effects created by followers of Zon-Kuthon.

 Serithtial was forged as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Bastard Sword|bastard sword]], and she reverts to this shape when no one wields her, but she can also detect what sort of blade her current wielder prefers and transform into it (choosing from [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Falchion|falchion]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword|greatsword]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]], or bastard sword) using an action, which she does when first held. The wielder can empathically tell _Serithtial_ to transform at any time by using a single action, which has the concentrate trait, after which _Serithtial_ uses an action to transform.

 _Serithtial_ is willing to work with a partner of almost any alignment as long as they are dedicated to defeating the great wyrm Kazavon and the forces of Zon-Kuthon. If you ignore those threats and pursue your own agenda instead, before long _Serithtial_ attempts to seize control of your body (DC 45 Will save to resist her control), though she typically remains in control for only long enough to find a more worthy partner. You can attempt a new Will save each day to retake control of your body.

**Activate**  envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Serithtial_ spends the appropriate number of actions and casts a 9th level [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] or [[Spells/Zone of Truth|_zone of truth_]] spell (DC 45 for either of the two spells).

**Destruction** If Kazavon or a great wyrm shadow dragon who is a worshipper of Zon-Kuthon uses its breath weapon on _Serithtial_ while she is unattended, the sword melts into a pool of mundane iron.
