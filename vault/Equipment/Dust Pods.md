# Dust Pods  *Item 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -151- The Show Must Go On|Pathfinder #151: The Show Must Go On]] pg. 76

**Price** 3 gp

---

You hide fragile pods of pollen or other clinging powder that get in the eyes of the first creature that enters the snare’s square. The creature must attempt a DC 17 Reflex saving throw. A creature [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] by the pollen can use an Interact action to attempt a DC 5 flat check to remove the condition.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is dazzled for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 10 minutes.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round and dazzled for 10 minutes after.
