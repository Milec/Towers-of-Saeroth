This faintly glowing powder rapidly induces the symptoms of abysium poisoning.

 **Saving Throw** DC 27 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Maximum Duration** 6 minutes; **Stage 1** 8d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (1 minute); **Stage 2** 9d6 poison damage and sickened 2 (1 minute); **Stage 3** 10d6 poison damage and sickened 3 (1 minute)
