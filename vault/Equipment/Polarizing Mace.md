Even grasping the amber handle of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Mace (Player Core)|light mace]]_ makes your hair stand on end.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The wielder of a [[Equipment/Grounding Spike (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_grounding spike_]] is within 30 feet; **Effect** A great surge of electricity connects your paired weapons. All creatures in a line between you and the wielder of the _grounding spike_ take 4d12 electricity damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Special** The _polarizing mace_ pairs with the _[[Equipment/Grounding Spike (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|grounding spike]]_.
