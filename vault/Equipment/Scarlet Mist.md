# Scarlet Mist  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Injury]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 70

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

Derived from a mixture of krooth venom and an agitating agent, scarlet mist causes bleeding that turns into a foaming mist. When a creature under the effects of scarlet mist takes persistent bleed damage from the toxin, creatures within 5 feet take the splash damage. The persistent damage can’t be staunched until the poison’s effects end. A creature that can’t bleed doesn’t take the bleed damage or cause the splash damage but can still take the poison damage.

**Saving Throw** DC 25 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage, 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]], and 1 poison [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d6 poison damage, 2d4 persistent bleed damage, and 2 poison splash damage (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d6 poison damage, 3d4 persistent bleed damage, and 3 poison splash damage (1 round)
