# Wyrm Drinker  *Item 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -173- Doorway to the Red Star|Pathfinder #173: Doorway to the Red Star]] pg. 75

**Price** 13,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This staff is made from the femur of a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Gold|gold dragon]] wrapped in the multicolored scales of every type of [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Chromatic|chromatic]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Metallic|metallic dragon]]. When wielding this staff, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to skill checks to [[Actions/Coerce]], [[Actions/Make an Impression]], [[Actions/Request]], or [[Actions/Lie]] to [[Traits/Dragon|dragons]] and creatures with strong draconic ties (such as [[Rules/Sidebars/Kobold Origins|kobolds]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Dragon|dragon instinct]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|barbarians]], and [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic|draconic]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerers]]).

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Attempt a melee Strike with _Wyrm Drinker_. If your target is a dragon or a creature with a strong draconic tie, such as a kobold, and you damage the target, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]], or drained 4 on a critical hit.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **2nd** [[Spells/Illusory Creature|_illusory creature_]] (dragons only)
- **4th** [[Spells/Reflective Scales|_reflective scales_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Summon Dragon|_summon dragon_]]
- **6th** [[Spells/Dragon Form|_dragon form_]], _summon dragon_ (6th)
- **7th** _summon dragon_ (7th)
