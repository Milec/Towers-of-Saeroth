# Giant Squid Lash  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 102

**Price** 750 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|Whip]]

---

Three dried tentacles from a giant squid studded with pufferfish spines have been twisted together to form this robust _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]]_. With a clever flick of the wrist, they can be unfurled into a deadly arc of poisonous barbs.

**Activate—Poison Barrage** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You lash out with the whip’s three tentacles in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature inside the cone must attempt a DC 27 basic Fortitude save or take 4d6 poison damage. On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include three tentacles from a [[Bestiary/Giant Squid|giant squid]].
