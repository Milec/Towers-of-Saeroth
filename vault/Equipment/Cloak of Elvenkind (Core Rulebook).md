# Cloak of Elvenkind  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 608

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This cloak is deep green with a voluminous hood, embroidered with gold trim and symbols significant in elven culture. The cloak allows you to cast the _[[Spells/Ghost Sound|ghost sound]]_ cantrip as an arcane innate spell. When you adjust the cloak’s clasp (an Interact action), the cloak transforms to match the environment around you and muffles your sounds, granting you a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw the hood up and gain the effects of [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]], with the spell's normal duration or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first. If you're also wearing _[[Equipment/Boots of Elvenkind|boots of elvenkind]]_, you can activate this ability twice per day.
