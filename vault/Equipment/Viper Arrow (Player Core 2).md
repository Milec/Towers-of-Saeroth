# Viper Arrow  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 302

**Price** 17 gp

**Ammunition** arrow

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

The shaft of this arrow is covered in fine green scales, and its iron head comes to a pair of points almost like fangs. After an activated viper arrow hits a target, the arrow transforms into a [[Bestiary/Viper (Monster Core)|viper]]. The target is affected by the viper's venom, as if it had been bitten. The viper then lands in an open space adjacent to the target.  
The viper has the [[Traits/Summoned (Player Core)|summoned]] trait and acts at the end of your turn, even though you didn't use the [[Actions/Sustain]] action. It's under the GM's control, but it generally attacks the creature the arrow struck. The viper vanishes after 1 minute or when slain.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[Spells/Summon Animal (Player Core)|_summon animal_]].
