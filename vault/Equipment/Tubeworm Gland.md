# Tubeworm Gland  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 99

**Price** 330 gp

**Usage** implanted

---

Thanks to a graft from a hardy deep-sea invertebrate in your stomach, you can ingest food and water that would be toxic to others. You gain a +2 item bonus to Fortitude saving throws against ingested [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]].
