# Thoughtwhip Claw  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Price** 1,350 gp

**Usage** worn

---

[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]] teaches that minds can be robbed as surely as pockets. This tattoo of a clenched fist provides a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Abraxas reaches through your hands and creates threads to yank thoughts from the mind of another. The _thoughtwhip claw_ casts [[Spells/Mind Probe|_mind probe_]] on a creature within range, with a DC of 28.
