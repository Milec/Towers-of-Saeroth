This wine, prepared using the blood of a creature with overwhelming vitality, soothes the body, mind, and spirit with a temporary sense of euphoria and rest. When you drink _wine of the blood_, you recover 2d10 Hit Points. Additionally, the wine attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] every negative [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect affecting you, with a counteract rank of 3 and a counteract modifier of +9. Lastly, you gain a +1 status bonus to all Will saves and a +2 status bonus against emotion effects for 1 minute.
