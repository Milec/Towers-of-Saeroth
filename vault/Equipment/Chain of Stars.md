# Chain of Stars  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 92

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** affixed to thrown weapon

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You hit a creature with the affixed weapon.

---

This delicate [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|mithral]] chain strung with tiny shuriken is wound tightly around the handle of the affixed weapon. When you Activate the chain, three _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken (Tian Xia Character Guide)|shuriken]]_ made from magical force materialize in the target’s space and split off to attack other creatures. Attempt up to three shuriken [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] that must each target a different creature and can’t target the creature you hit to trigger this talisman. These Strikes use your attack modifier but originate from the hit creature’s space. These attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn’t increase until after all three attacks have been made. The shuriken deal [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage instead of their normal type, and each shuriken vanishes after its attack.
