# Marvelous Medicines  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 574

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This set of [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer’s tools]] contains a seemingly endless supply of bandages, herbs, and healing items of impeccable quality, granting you a +2 item bonus to Medicine checks.

**Activate** single action Treat Poison or 8 hours (Treat Disease);     **Effect** You can activate the tools when you use them to Treat Poison in order to produce the effects of [[Spells/Neutralize Poison|_neutralize poison_]], or when you Treat Disease in order to produce the effects of [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]]. Once these medicines have been used to treat a patient’s poison or disease, they can’t be used again to treat the same affliction for that patient.
