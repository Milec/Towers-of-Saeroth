two actions

---

Hunters all over Golarion favor curare, a potent paralytic derived from boiled tree bark.

**Saving Throw** DC 25 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds (but see stage 3); **Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1, and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d6 poison damage, clumsy 2, enfeebled 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 (1 minute); **Stage 3** 4d6 poison damage, clumsy 2, enfeebled 2, and slowed 1 (1 round). If the victim fails the saving throw while at Stage 3, the poison ends and the victim is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 2d6 minutes.
