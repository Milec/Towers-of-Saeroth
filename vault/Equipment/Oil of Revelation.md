# Oil of Revelation  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 258

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This bright oil, first created by humans as a tool to help them fight in darkness, holds flecks of tiny gemstones in suspension and smells like a struck [[Equipment/Tindertwig|tindertwig]].

 The first time a weapon coated with this oil damages a creature, the wound glows with light for 1 minute. If the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], the light's position means it is merely [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to creatures that would otherwise be unable to see it, rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]]. The light also negates the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] condition due to lighting conditions. If the coated weapon doesn't damage a creature within 1 hour, the oil sloughs off and loses its power.
