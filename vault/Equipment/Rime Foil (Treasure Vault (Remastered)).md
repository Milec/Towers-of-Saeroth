# Rime Foil  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 34

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier]]

---

The steely blue blade of this _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Frost|frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]]_ emerges from a hilt wrapped in thick leather and trimmed in fur to protect the wielder's hand.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You hit a creature using the _rime foil_ as your last action; **Effect** You cast 5th-level [[Spells/Phantom Prison|_phantom prison_]] on the target (DC 22 Will to disbelieve). If the target has been damaged by a _[[Equipment/Shattered Plan (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|shattered plan]]_ in the last round, the DC is instead 24.

**Special** The _rime foil_ pairs with the _shattered plan_.
