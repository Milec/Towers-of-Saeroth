# Mustard Powder  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Inhaled]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

single action

Interact

---

Concocted from the formulas provided by otherworldly refugees to Irrisen, mustard powder is rumored to be devastating to entire armies with proper dispersal. Recipes have quickly spread across Golarion. Mustard powder’s sickened condition ends when the poison’s other effects do.

**Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d4 poison damage, dazzled, [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, and unable to smell (1 round); **Stage 3** 2d6 poison damage, dazzled, sickened 2, and unable to smell (1 round)
