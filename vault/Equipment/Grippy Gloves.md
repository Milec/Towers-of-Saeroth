# Grippy Gloves  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 150

**Price** 90 gp

**Usage** worn gloves

**Bulk** L

---

A good pair of gloves is a critical item for any soldier, particularly if you plan to get up close to your enemy. When you wear these black leather gloves with fine silver stitching, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], and [[Actions/Reposition]].

**Activate—Sticky Grip** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You have an enemy g[[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|rabbed]]or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]; **Effect** Your gloves help you squeeze even more tightly, keeping your opponent from moving freely. The enemy you have grabbed or restrained is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.
