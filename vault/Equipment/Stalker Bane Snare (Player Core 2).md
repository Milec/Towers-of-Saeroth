# Stalker Bane Snare  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 300

**Price** 15 gp

---

This snare explodes in a burst of cloying powder that can cling to a creature stepping into its square. A creature that enters the square of a stalker bane snare must attempt a DC 20 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** Powder sticks to the target, causing it to leave behind telltale footprints. Being [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] makes the target [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], to creatures that could see it if it weren't invisible.  

**Failure** Powder clumps on the target, constantly flaking away. Being invisible makes the target concealed, rather than hidden or undetected, to creatures that could see it if it weren't invisible.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the end of its next turn.
