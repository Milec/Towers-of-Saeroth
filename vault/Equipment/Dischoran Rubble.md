# Dischoran Rubble  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 118

**Price** 18 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

Rock fragments exposed to any [[Bestiary/Families/Dischoran|dischoran's]] sonic blasts can sometimes retain a portion of the attack's energy, trembling slightly whenever they are touched. When you cast [[Spells/Noise Blast|_noise blast_]] using a piece of _dischoran rubble_, the cacophony reverberates through the targets' forms for 1 round. If a creature that failed or critically failed its initial saving throw moves 10 feet or more on their next turn, it takes 2d10 additional sonic damage.
