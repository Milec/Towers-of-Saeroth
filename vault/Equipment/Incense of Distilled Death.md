# Incense of Distilled Death  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 257

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This black incense smells of fresh earth and ash. You activate the incense by lighting it, whereupon it fills a 10-foot emanation with oily smoke and potent negative energy. [[Traits/Undead]] creatures, including [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] undead, gain fast healing 4 while in the area; though this healing comes from negative energy, it doesn't negatively impact living creatures. Once lit, the incense burns for 1 minute, and it can't be extinguished.
