# Sack of Hyrdra's Teeth  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 146

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This soft cotton bag has a drawstring of sinew and a jagged embroidery pattern around the mouth. Inside are a seemingly endless number of needle-sharp teeth.

**Activate—Sow a Tooth** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You cast forth a tooth from the bag, and where it lands, a skeleton springs up from the ground. This has the effect of [[Spells/Phantasmal Minion|_phantasmal minion_]], except the minion appears as a visible skeleton.

**Activate—Fist Full of Fangs** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw a whole handful of teeth and cast them to the ground, casting [[Spells/Rouse Skeletons (Player Core 2)|_rouse skeletons_]] as a 5th-rank spell (DC 30).
