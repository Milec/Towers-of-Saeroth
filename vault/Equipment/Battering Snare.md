# Battering Snare  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 79

**Price** 3 gp

---

This snare consists of a flat rock or metal plate held in place with a spring or flexible branch. When triggered, the flat object swings wide and smacks the triggering creature violently. The first creature to enter its square receives 2d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 16 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].
