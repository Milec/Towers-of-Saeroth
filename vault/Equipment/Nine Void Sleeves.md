# Nine Void Sleeves  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 243

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

The ink on these tattoos, made from the crushed crystalline fangs and wingbones of sceaduinars, lends a dull purple-black hue to your arms and hands. During combat, the ink moves of its own accord, coalescing into nine smoky crystal spurs of darkness; these eerie finger-like protrusions move with an unearthly grace on your command, supplying the requisite magical gestures for your spells.

**Activate—Shadow Fingers** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], shadow, [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Void|void]] trait, your spell loses the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait and gains the shadow trait, as the tattoo’s shadowy spurs perform the gestures in your stead.
