# Mordant Mask  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 78

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're from the Mordant Spire

---

A constant murmuring, like waves washing up on the shore, fills your ears when you don this wooden mask. The soothing sounds grant you a +1 item bonus to your Will save and Will DC against [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects.

**Activate—Pounding Surf** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would be affected by an auditory effect;  **Effect** The noise of the waves grow to an overwhelming, crashing booming, drowning out all other sounds. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] to all other noise until the end of your next turn. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] the activation up to 1 minute.
