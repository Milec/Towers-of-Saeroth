# Vampiric Scythe  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 31

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe]]

---

The blade of this _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe|scythe]]_ is sharp enough to produce a whistling sound when swung through the air. The shaft is made of ebony wood with a sickly shine to it, much like the shine of infected wounds and contaminated water.

**Activate** ⤾ command;   **Trigger** An enemy critically succeeds at an attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] you;  **Effect** As the scythe leaves your hand, it drinks the triggering creature's blood. The creature must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]] (drained 3 on a critical failure). If the triggering creature becomes drained, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to the triggering creature's level. You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You critically succeed at a Strike against a living opponent;  **Effect** The scythe drains life essence from your opponent into you. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to half the slashing damage the scythe dealt to the foe. You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.
