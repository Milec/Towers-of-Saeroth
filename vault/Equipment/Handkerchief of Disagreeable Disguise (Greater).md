# Handkerchief of Disagreeable Disguise  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 191

**Usage** worn headwear

---

This elegant scarf appears to be and functions as a [[Equipment/Masquerade Scarf|_masquerade scarf_]]. When you invest the scarf, it fuses to you.

**Activate**  1 minute ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Like a _masquerade scarf_, the scarf casts a 1st-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] spell on you. However, the illusion disadvantages you based on your intent, making you, for example, appear to be a suspicious ruffian if you’re trying to sneak past guards or lending you the seeming of a pauper if you’re trying to impress a shallow aristocrat. You and those you consider to be allies must succeed at a DC 16 Will save or you perceive the [[Rules/Illusion|illusion]] as you intended it, though others won’t. Evidence to the contrary allows you to attempt to disbelieve the false version of the illusion. You can’t Dismiss the spell.
