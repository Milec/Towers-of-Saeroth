envision

**Trigger** You attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].

---

The _escape fulu_ is a charm common among wealthy people, who wear the talisman in case of kidnapping. When you Activate this fulu, for 1 minute, you gain a +2 status bonus to your attempts to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as well as to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]].
