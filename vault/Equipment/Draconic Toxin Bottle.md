# Draconic Toxin Bottle  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 191

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This glass bottle appears to be a [[Equipment/Dragon's Breath Potion|_dragon's breath potion_]] of a specific dragon type (usually [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Red|red]]) but has been polluted, making it toxic to use.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You drink from the bottle and must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (sickened 2 on a critical failure) and immediately vomit the potion directly onto yourself, taking damage as though you had failed a save against a standard _dragon’s breath potion_. If you succeed at your Fortitude save, you become sickened 1 but otherwise are able to unleash a breath weapon as normal for a _dragon’s breath potion_ of the appropriate type.
