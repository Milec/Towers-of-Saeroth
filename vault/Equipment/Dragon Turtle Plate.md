# Dragon Turtle Plate  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 15

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 3

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate (Player Core)|Half Plate]]

---

This harness of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate (Player Core)|half plate]]_ is made from the shell plates of a [[Bestiary/Dragon Turtle (Monster Core)|dragon turtle]] and has the [[Traits/Aquadynamic (Howl of the Wild)|aquadynamic]] trait. Fashioned from organic material, the suit is one of the few heavy armors not anathema to [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]].

**Activate** ⤾ Interact;   **Trigger** A creature adjacent to you targets you with a melee attack;  **Effect** You roll the hefty plates of the dragon turtle armor toward the attacker to gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the shell of a [[Bestiary/Dragon Turtle (Monster Core)|dragon turtle]].
