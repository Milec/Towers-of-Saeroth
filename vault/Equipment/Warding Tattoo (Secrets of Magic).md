# Warding Tattoo  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Many cultures of Golarion have a tattoo to turn away harm; as such, warding tattoos often resemble whichever dangers are most common to the culture, such as a wild beast or a whirlpool in the high seas of the Shackles.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy, hazard, or the environment makes an attack against your AC, requires you to attempt a saving throw, or causes you to take damage automatically;  **Effect** Until the end of the current turn, against the triggering effect, you gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws and gain resistance 2 to damage.

## Warding Tattoo  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Price** 140 gp

## Warding Tattoo (Trail)  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Price** 250 gp

---

You gain resistance 2 to damage from [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]] and [[Traits/Environmental|environmental]] hazards at all times, which increases to 5 when the tattoo is activated.

## Warding Tattoo (Wave)  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Price** 220 gp

---

In addition to the normal triggers, you can activate the tattoo when you enter an environment where you can't breathe. When you activate the tattoo, it casts [[Spells/Air Bubble|_air bubble_]] on you, in addition to the other effects.

## Warding Tattoo (Fiend)  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Price** 300 gp

---

You gain resistance 2 to spells cast by [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]] and magical attacks by fiends, which increases to 5 when the tattoo is activated.
