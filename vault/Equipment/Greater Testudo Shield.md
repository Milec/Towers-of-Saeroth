**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|Tower Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield (Player Core)|tower shield]] (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10) sports a bright red front with a gold inlay of an eagle. While it looks unassuming, this shield can protect not only yourself but also those behind you.

**Activate—Tortoise Form** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your shield creates a magical barrier, consisting of translucent copies of the shield. The wall is 1 inch thick, 15 feet long, and 10 feet high. The barrier is conjured adjacent to you in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] on the border between squares and lasts for 1 minute. The barrier is created with the Hardness and Hit Points equal to those of the _testudo shield_ and is [[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|immune to critical hits]] and precision damage.
