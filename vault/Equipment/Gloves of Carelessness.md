# Gloves of Carelessness  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 91

**Usage** worn gloves

**Bulk** L

---

These gloves appear and function as [[Equipment/Gloves of Storing|_gloves of storing_]] but repel any item not stored in the gloves. When wearing them, whenever you Interact to draw or pick up an item, roll a DC 11 flat check. On a success, you do so as normal. On a failure, you instead fling the item 15 feet in a random direction. You can use a reaction to attempt a DC 23 Reflex save, causing the item to land at your feet instead if you succeed. Removing the stored item from the gloves does not trigger the item’s curse. The first time the curse activates, the gloves fuse to you.
