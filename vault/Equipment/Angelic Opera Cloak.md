# Angelic Opera Cloak  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 81

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This luxurious cloak embodies an angel’s swiftness. You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks and never take [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falling]] damage, as your cloak billows like a pair of wings to soften any fall you take. When you [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] in the cloak, you either increase your Dexterity score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score. If you are [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 while wearing this cloak.

**Activate—On Angel's Wings** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The opera cloak transforms into two pairs of brilliant, feathered wings that grant you a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 40 feet for 10 minutes. During this time, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] to paralysis effects and ignore effects that would give you a circumstance penalty to speed.
