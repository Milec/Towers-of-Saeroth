# Alicorn Lance  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 36

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The version of the _alicorn lance_ which does evil damage may not be purchased or crafted in Pathfinder Society play._

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance (Player Core)|Lance]]

---

This white _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance (Player Core)|lance]]_ is made from the horn of a [[Bestiary/Families/Unicorn|unicorn]], willingly granted at the end of its lifetime. In addition to its normal damage, it deals 1 [[Traits/Good|good]] damage on successful attacks. You can make the lance glow like a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]] or suppress its light by using an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You evoke the spirit of the unicorn that donated the _alicorn lance’s_ horn, which you ride in a shining charge. Move up to twice your Speed and make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the alicorn lance; you gain the effects of the lance’s [[Traits/Jousting (Player Core)|jousting]] trait on this Strike. If you were already mounted when you Activate the horn, the unicorn spirit takes shape around your steed, granting it a +10–foot status bonus to its Speed for the charge.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a horn willingly gifted by a [[Bestiary/Families/Unicorn|unicorn]]. In rare instances, an _alicorn lance_ can be made with a forcibly taken horn. However this heinous act pollutes the horn’s magic, causing it to glow with a sickly red light, deal [[Traits/Evil|evil]] damage instead of [[Traits/Good|good]] damage, and make the unicorn spirit evoked in the charge to whinny visibly in pain.
