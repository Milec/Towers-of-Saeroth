# Blackaxe  *Item 25*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|Cursed]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 329

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe (Player Core)|Greataxe]]

---

This potent weapon used by [[Bestiary/Treerazer (Monster Core)|Treerazer]] is a _+4 [[Equipment/Corrosive (GM Core)|greater corrosive]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|major striking]] obsidian [[Equipment/Weapons/Greataxe (Player Core)|greataxe]]_ that grants a +4 item bonus to attack rolls, deals an extra 2d6 damage to [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]], and has the properties of [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon (GM Core)|adamantine]]. It deals an additional die of damage when wielded by Treerazer.

**Activate—Owner’s Authority** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]])   **Requirements** You aren’t wielding _Blackaxe_ and you are its true owner; **Effect** You sense the world around _Blackaxe_ as though you were in its location and can use any of your innate spells through the link as if it were the source of the spell. If another creature is wielding _Blackaxe_, it must succeed at a DC 50 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 2]] until it relinquishes the weapon.

**Activate—Owner’s Reclamation** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]])   **Requirements** You aren’t wielding _Blackaxe_ and you are its true owner; **Effect** _Blackaxe_ appears in your hands, teleporting instantly from its prior location.

**Activate—Rejuvenating Deforestation** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|positive]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a living tree with _Blackaxe_. If it hits, the tree withers to ash and you heal 250 Hit Points and gain the benefit of a 6th-rank [[Spells/Sound Body|_sound body_]] spell.

**Destruction** Chop a powerful magical tree with _Blackaxe_, and while the sap is still fresh, sever one of [[Setting/Deities/Cyth-V'sug|Cyth-V’sug’s]] limbs. This makes _Blackaxe_ explode violently.
