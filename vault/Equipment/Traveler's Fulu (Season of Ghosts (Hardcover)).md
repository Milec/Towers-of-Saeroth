# Traveler's Fulu  *Item 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You attempt to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]]; **Requirements** You’re trained in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

---

This fulu shows a series of constellations and arrows depicting astronomical movements through the night sky. When you activate a _traveler’s fulu_, the magic infuses your mind with sensations of deja vu, as if you’d been in this region before. Your attempt to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] functions as if you have a compass, and you use the outcome one degree of success better than the result of your [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] check to Sense Direction, or by two degrees of success if you’re a master in Survival.
