This silvery _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|minor reinforcing]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]_ (Hardness 8, HP 64, and BT 32) bears the image of two soldiers standing side-by-side.

**Activate—Self Sacrifice** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** An ally within 20 feet of you would take physical damage; **Requirements** The _martyr's shield_ is raised; **Effect** When you activate this shield, it magically imposes itself in front of the triggering ally and takes the damage for them with the effects of [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]]. Any damage that isn't prevented by the shield is transferred onto you.
