_Vengeful demon’s tears_ is an infamously noxious spirit mixed into a potent magical cocktail and traditionally packaged in a gourd adorned with an engraving of a terrifying demonic face. Drinking it feels akin to lighting your throat on fire and putting it out with an herbal tonic. When you activate this potion, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition for 3 rounds and can use the extra action each round to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], or to take the following action.

**Activate—Release the Demon** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]])    **Effect** You focus on the sensation of life burning within your flesh and gain 2d8 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] for 1 round.
