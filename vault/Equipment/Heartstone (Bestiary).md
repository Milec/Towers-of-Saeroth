# Heartstone  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 203

**Usage** worn

---

This gemstone grants its wearer a +2 item bonus to saving throws.

Each _heartstone_ is powered by the spirit of a specific night hag. If it’s separated from her for 24 hours (or she’s been dead for 24 hours), it becomes a nonmagical gemstone. A _heartstone_ allows the hag to use additional occult innate spells: _[[Spells/Ethereal Jaunt|ethereal jaunt]]_ (9th level) and _[[Spells/Bind Soul|bind soul]]_ at will, and _[[Spells/Shadow Blast|shadow blast]]_ twice per day.

**Activate** single action command;    **Requirements** You must be touching the _heartstone_.; **Effect** The _heartstone_ attempts to counteract one disease affecting you (counteract level 5, counteract modifier +18).

**Craft Requirements** You must be a night hag.

## Skarja's Heartstone  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 89

**Price** 2,500 gp ((1,000 gp when nonmagical))

---

This pale gemstone grants its wearer a +2 item bonus to saving throws and the ability to discern a target's alignment (as [[Spells/Detect Alignment|_detect alignment_]], but can detect chaotic, evil, good, and lawful auras simultaneously) at will by peering through the gemstone.

 Like all hag _heartstones_, this gemstone is powered by [[Bestiary/Skarja|Skarja's]] spirit. Skarja does not need to wear her _heartstone_ to benefit from its powers, but she cannot use them if she hasn't touched her _heartstone_ in the past 7 days. If Skarja's _heartstone_ is separated from her for more than 7 days (or if she's been dead for 7 days), it becomes a non-magical gemstone. Skarja's _heartstone_ allows her to use additional occult innate spells: [[Spells/Ethereal Jaunt|_ethereal jaunt_]] (9th level) and [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]] at will, [[Spells/Shadow Blast|_shadow blast_]] (6th level) twice per day, and [[Spells/Spirit Blast|_spirit blast_]] once per day.

**Activate** single action command;    **Requirements** You must be touching the _heartstone_; **Effect** The _heartstone_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one [[Traits/Disease|disease]] affecting you (counteract level 7, counteract modifier +23).
