# Hell Staff  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 132

**Price** 13,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _hell staff_ is a tall, pointed staff forged of red-tinted steel with Infernal inscriptions that march neatly down its sides. At its top sits an inverted ruby pyramid divided into nine sections. Found mostly in Cheliax or other lands where diabolic influences hold sway, when used as a weapon the staff is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Axiomatic|axiomatic]] [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|unholy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_. When you prepare this staff, if you’re [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]] or [[Traits/Good|good]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 until your next daily preparations.

The staff’s [[Spells/Summon Lesser Servitor (Player Core 2)|_summon lesser servitor_]] spell can be used only to summon animals with the [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] trait, [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devils]], or [[Bestiary/Hell Hound (Monster Core)|hell hounds]] (at 4th level). Its [[Spells/Summon Fiend (Player Core)|_summon fiend_]] spell can summon only devils or hell hounds.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]] (evil or lawful only)
- **1st** [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]], [[Spells/Summon Lesser Servitor (Player Core 2)|_summon lesser servitor_]]
- **2nd** _summon lesser servitor_, [[Spells/Undetectable Alignment|_undetectable alignment_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Chilling Darkness (Player Core)|_chilling darkness_]], _summon lesser servitor_
- **4th** [[Spells/Divine Wrath (Player Core)|_divine wrath_]] (evil or lawful only), _summon lesser servitor_
- **5th** [[Spells/Divine Immolation|_flame strike_]], [[Spells/Summon Fiend (Player Core)|_summon fiend_]]
- **6th** [[Spells/Devil Form|_devil form_]], _summon fiend_
- **7th** _divine decree_ (evil or lawful only), _summon fiend_

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
