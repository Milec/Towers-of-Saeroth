# Bloodline Robe  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 147

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** 1

---

Each bloodline robe has a design that befits a particular [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerer]] bloodline, depicting creatures of that bloodline or using styles common among them. You gain a +2 item bonus to each of your bloodline skills.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re benefiting from your blood magic effect; **Effect** You’re [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] on your next turn. You can use the extra action only as part of Casting a bloodline spell.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 [[Rules/Focus]] Point, which you can use only to cast a sorcerer bloodline spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerer]] of the bloodline tied to the robe.
