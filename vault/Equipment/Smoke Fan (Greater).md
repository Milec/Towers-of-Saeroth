single action

Interact

---

When you Activate a smoke fan, it creates a cloud of colored smoke. The smoke fills a 5-foot radius. The creator chooses the smoke’s color when creating the smoke fan. Creatures within the smoke’s area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all other creatures are concealed to them. The smoke lasts for 1 minute or until dissipated by a strong wind.
