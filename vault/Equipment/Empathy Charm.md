# Empathy Charm  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Companion]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 106

**Price** 35 gp

---

This charm, usually placed on an animal companion’s collar, contains a single strand of your hair, as well as one of your animal companion’s, creating a link that better transmits emotional cues to a trained psychological assistance animal.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect; **Requirements** Your animal companion wearing the _empathy charm_ is within 10 feet; **Effect** Your animal companion senses the effect and attempts to calm you. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus against the triggering save.
