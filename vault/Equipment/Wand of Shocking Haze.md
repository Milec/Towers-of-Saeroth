# Wand of Shocking Haze  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Visual]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 142

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

Thin, shiny wires crisscross the surface of this smooth, black wand. The pommel is capped with a polished metal sphere, which sometimes emits small electric sparks.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Blur (Player Core)|_blur_]], and the wand creates faint orbs of electricity [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] in the haze around you. Each time a creature that’s adjacent to you fails a flat check against the concealment from _blur_, they explode one of the orbs, causing them to take 5 electricity damage.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _blur_.
