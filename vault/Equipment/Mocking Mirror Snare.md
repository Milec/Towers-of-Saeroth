# Mocking Mirror Snare  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 73

**Price** 60 gp

---

A reflective rune is hidden in the snare's square. A creature that enters this square suddenly sees a twisted reflection of themself appear nearby and perform some action that they would never do. The creature takes 8d6 mental damage with a DC 25 basic Will save. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] until the end of their next turn.
