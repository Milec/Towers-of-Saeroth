# Spirit Snare  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 80

**Price** 90 gp

---

This complicated snare is affixed with various crystals and Stasian coils attached to strange electrical relays, working on the same principles as etheric [[Equipment/Spirit-Singer (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|spirit singers]]. When an [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature enters its square, the device lets loose an ectoplasmic web that lashes around the creature's spectral form. The creature must succeed a DC 26 Reflex saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round. On a critical failure, the creature becomes immobilized for 1 minute. In either case, the incorporeal creature can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] (DC 26).
