# Rusting Carapace  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 19

**Price** 476 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Lamellar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leather Lamellar]]

---

This [[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|_+1_]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Lamellar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_leather lamellar_]] _armor_ incorporates the plates of an [[Bestiary/Ore Louse|ore louse’s]] hide, woven together with the creature’s own sinew into a functional set of armor. While wearing the _rusting carapace_, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC and saving throws against any effect that would rust you or your items, such as the ore louse’s Strikes that have the rust ability or the [[Spells/Rust Cloud|_rust cloud_]] spell.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Oxidizing liquid seeps from between the segments of the rusting hide onto a nearby metal creature, non-magical metal hazard, or non-magical metal item of up to 1 Bulk. A targeted creature or hazard gets a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save; a targeted item automatically gets a critical failure if unattended, but a creature holding or wearing it can attempt a DC 24 basic Reflex save. The rust deals 8d6 damage to a creature or hazard, plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] on a failed save; it deals 2d6 damage to an item, plus 1d6 persistent damage on a failed save.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide of a [[Bestiary/Ore Louse|ore louse]].
