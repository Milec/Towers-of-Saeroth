# Oil of Unlife  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]], [[Traits/Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 258

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All character have access to all levels of the oil of unlife_

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This thin, black liquid imparts a bone-deep chill while rapidly repairing an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature's physical or spiritual form. When you dash _oil of unlife_ onto an undead creature, or a living creature with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]], the oil absorbs quickly into its body, and the creature regains the listed number of Hit Points. You can pour _oil of unlife_ on an [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] undead; in this case, the creature absorbs the oil into itself.
