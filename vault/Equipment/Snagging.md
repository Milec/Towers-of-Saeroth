# Snagging  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 89

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** applied to belt, cape, cloak, or scarf

---

This animated item attempts to catch you when you fall. You can attempt to [[Actions/Grab an Edge]], even if your hands are tied behind your back or otherwise restrained, so long as there's a solid edge within 10 feet. If you roll a success, you can Grab the Edge even if you don't have a hand free.
