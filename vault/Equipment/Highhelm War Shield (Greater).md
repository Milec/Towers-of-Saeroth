# Highhelm War Shield  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 112

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Razor Disc (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Razor Disc]]

---

This specialized [[Equipment/Shields/Razor Disc (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|razor disc]] (Hardness 11, HP 84, BT 42) was originally designed by the members of Clan Molgrade for use by Highhelm's soldiers. The integrated blades are etched with runes, making them a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]]_ weapon.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You critically hit with the shield's integrated blades;  **Effect** The shield's blades grow and dig themselves into your foe. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] as it becomes impaled on the shield spikes. While you have a creature grabbed in this way, you can't [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raise the Shield]], but the creature takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] every round equal to the number of damage dice. This persistent bleed can't end as long as the creature is impaled on the shield. You can Release the shield to leave it embedded in the creature. This ends the grab but doesn't end the persistent bleed damage. The creature can remove the shield and end the grab by attempting a check to [[Actions/Escape]]. This normally uses your [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] DC to Escape, but if you're no longer holding the shield, the Escape DC is 25.
