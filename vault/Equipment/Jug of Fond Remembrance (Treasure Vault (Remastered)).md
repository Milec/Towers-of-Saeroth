# Jug of Fond Remembrance  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 115

**Price** 75 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This large jug always seems to contain just enough of the holder's favorite alcohol to share with a friend. As long as you're holding the jug, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks. If you share a sip of the liquor from the jug with a creature, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your next Diplomacy check to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] or [[Actions/Request]] something from that creature any time within the next month.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You take a long swig on the jug and then [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about a creature you can see, with a +2 circumstance bonus to the check. If you fail but don't critically fail this check, you get a success instead. You're then [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 3 rounds.
