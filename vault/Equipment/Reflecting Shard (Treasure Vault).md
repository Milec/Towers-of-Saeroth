# Reflecting Shard  *Item 13+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 94

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

⤾

command

**Trigger** You are targeted by a spell of 5th level or lower

**Requirement** You’re a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], and you have the affixed shield raised.

---

This mirrored metal fragment is bolted or welded to the face of the affixed shield. When you Activate it, you attempt to reflect the triggering spell back at its caster with [[Spells/Spell Riposte|_spell turning_]], using your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check. The talisman’s counteract level is 7th.
