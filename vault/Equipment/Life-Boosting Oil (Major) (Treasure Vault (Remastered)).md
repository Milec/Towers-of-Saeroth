When you apply sticky, stinging _life-boosting oil_, you gain [[Rules/Fast Healing and Regeneration|fast healing]] according to the oil's type that starts the first time you take damage while the oil lasts. Once the fast healing starts, the oil remains effective for 4 rounds. However, the oil lasts only 8 hours, whether it provides fast healing or not.
