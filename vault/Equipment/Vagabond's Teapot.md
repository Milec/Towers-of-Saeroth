# Vagabond's Teapot  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Troubles in Grayce

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Troubles in Grayce]] pg. 118

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

These ceramic teapots are highly valued by travelers. While the teapot is filled with water, a creature can use a single action with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait to instantly heat the water to boiling, at which point the kettle whistles and the water inside begins to cool at a normal rate. While the water remains heated, it is piping hot, and a creature splashed with it takes 1d4 [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] fire damage. Instead of filling the teapot with water, you can pour an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|elixir]] of 4th level or lower into the pot and use the following activation.

**Activate—Share Brew**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The teapot magically increases the volume of the inserted elixir to produce four uses of the same elixir. Once an elixir has been brewed in the teapot in this way, further attempts that day to brew another elixir spoil the inserted liquid. Any brewed elixir not consumed by the time it cools (typically 10 minutes) loses its alchemical properties.
