# Rending Snare  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Kobold (Player Core 2)|Kobold]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 299

**Price** 700 gp

---

Sharp metal jaws wind tightly into the pressure plate mechanism of this snare. When triggered, the jaws clamp shut and spin, damaging limbs in the process. The snare deals 10d8 piercing damage to the first creature to enter the square; that creature must attempt a DC 34 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage plus [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|2d6 persistent bleed damage]], and it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage plus 4d6 persistent bleed damage, and it becomes clumsy 2 for 1 minute.
