The pages of this book are dried leaves in all colors of the seasons, their delicate veins spelling out incantations. It carries the earthy scent of a forest floor.

**Activate—Nature's Resilience** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], plant, wood) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your last action was to [Cast a prepared Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) with the [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait, you imbue yourself with the strength of the natural world and obtain the effect of a 2nd-rank _[[Spells/Oaken Resilience|_oaken resilience_]]_ spell.
