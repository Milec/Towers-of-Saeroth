# Potency Crystal  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You make an attack with the affixed weapon, but you haven't rolled yet

---

This fluorite crystal glows with a strange phosphorescence. When you activate the crystal, the weapon becomes a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon|+1 striking weapon]]_ on the Strike and until the end of this turn, gaining a +1 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to two weapon damage dice.

## Potency Crystal  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Price** 4 gp

## Potency Crystal (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Price** 150 gp

---

The weapon becomes a +2 greater striking weapon for the rest of the turn, gaining a +2 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to three weapon damage dice.

## Potency Crystal (Major)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Price** 1,300 gp

---

The weapon becomes a +3 major striking weapon for the rest of the turn, gaining a +3 item bonus to the attack roll and increasing the damage to four weapon damage dice.
