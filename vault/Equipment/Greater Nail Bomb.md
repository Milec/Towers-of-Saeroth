# Nail Bomb  *Item 2+*

> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

This pressurized iron casing bursts open when struck, releasing [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] shrapnel. The bomb deals the listed piercing damage and piercing [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage from a cold iron source. Many types grant a bonus to attack rolls.
