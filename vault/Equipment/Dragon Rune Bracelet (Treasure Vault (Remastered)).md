# Dragon Rune Bracelet  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 148, [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 75

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** worn

---

A _dragon rune bracelet_ is a gold bangle formed around the scale of a famous [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]]. The bracelet is etched with esoteric symbols or words in [[Setting/Languages/Draconic]] that indicate kinship with dragons. As many types of _dragon rune bracelet_ exist as there are types of dragons, though bracelets associated with [[Traits/Uncommon|uncommon]] or [[Traits/Rare|rare]] dragons have the same rarity as the dragon.

 While wearing a _dragon rune bracelet_, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks while interacting with dragons of the same type as the bracelet's scale (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Red|red dragons]]  or [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud|cloud dragons]]). You also gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects from any kind of dragon.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerer]] [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic|draconic bloodline]] spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a sorcerer of a draconic bloodline.
