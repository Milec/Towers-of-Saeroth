# Cornucopia of Plenty  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 143

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Changed “exhausted” to “fatigued” as per PFS Guidelines_

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This exquisite wicker horn is made of green wood and smells of fresh wheat and barley.

**Activate—Bountiful Rations** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw forth and consume a filling snack from this cornucopia. You gain 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 minute and suppress the effects of the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition for 10 minutes.
