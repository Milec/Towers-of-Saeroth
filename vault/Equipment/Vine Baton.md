# Vine Baton  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 147

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wooden baton is carved with a vine design that spirals from one end of the rod to the other. Similar batons were used by Taldan commanders of their Armies of Exploration for thousands of years.

**Activate—Forward March!** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You brandish the vine baton with a flourish or in some other dramatic manner. You and your allies within 120 feet can [[Actions/Hustle (Player Core)|Hustle]] for 1 additional hour. If you enter an encounter during this time period, the effect ends, but you receive a +2 status bonus to your initiative rolls for that encounter.
