# Wand of Paralytic Shock  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 141

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A two-pronged metal rod, this wand has a handle coated in thick rubber. Each prong ends in a copper coil. When Activated, the wand produces a loud zapping sound.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Paralyze (Player Core)|_paralyze_]], electrocuting the target into immobility. Each target takes electricity damage at the start of its turns while it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] due to the spell. The amount of damage depends on the wand’s type.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Paralyze (Player Core)|_paralyze_]] of the appropriate level.
