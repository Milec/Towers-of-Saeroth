An etheric essence disruptor emits bursts of discordant ethereal energy through a process based upon Stasian etheric-spirit singers, though without the need for a Stasian coil. Etheric essence disruptors attempt to break down the workings of a spell that manipulates spiritual essence. Upon activation, attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an active [[Traits/Divine|divine]] or [[Traits/Occult|occult]] spell within the listed range, with the listed counteract modifier and counteract level.
