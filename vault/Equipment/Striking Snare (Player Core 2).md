# Striking Snare  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 300

**Price** 75 gp

---

You affix a trip line or other trigger to a group of either stones or wooden stakes to strike a creature that enters the snare's square. The creature must attempt a DC 26 [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2765) Reflex saving throw. If you choose stones when you [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] the snare, it deals 9d8 bludgeoning damage; if you choose spikes, it deals 9d8 piercing damage.
