# Chaos Falcon Feather  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 118

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]]

---

When used as catalysts, _chaos falcon feathers_ lend flexibility to spells that deal with elemental energy. For the duration of a catalyzed [[Spells/Resist Energy (Player Core)|_resist energy_]] spell, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the Spell on an adjacent target, touching them and changing the type of energy to which they have resistance. This reduces the remaining duration of the spell by 1 minute; if the spell has less than a minute remaining, it reduces the duration to 1 round.
