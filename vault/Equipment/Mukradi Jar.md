# Mukradi Jar  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

A miniature [[Bestiary/Mukradi (Monster Core)|mukradi]], its chitin shrunken and pale, is coiled within this jar. Its hollow form grows to a Gargantuan shell when you open the jar. It emits one of three breath weapons, chosen by you. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

- **Acid Maw** ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]]) 10-foot-wide, 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] of acid dealing 12d6 acid damage.
- **Flame Maw** ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of fire dealing 12d6 fire damage.
- **Shock Maw** ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) 120-foot line of electricity dealing 12d6 electricity damage.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a [[Bestiary/Mukradi (Monster Core)|mukradi]] corpse.
