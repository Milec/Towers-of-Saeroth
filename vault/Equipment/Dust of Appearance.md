# Dust of Appearance  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 570

**Price** 50 gp

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Stored in a small reed, this powder looks like a fine metallic dust. When you fling it in the air, it coats all creatures in a 10-foot burst centered on a point within 5 feet of you. For 1 minute, the coated creatures can’t be concealed or invisible, nor can they benefit from [[Spells/Mirror Image|_mirror image_]] or similar abilities that create illusory duplicates. Any illusions in the area of 3rd level or lower are revealed as such, although this does not end their effect.
