# Wand of Crushing Leaps  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 139

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This supple, light wooden wand drifts to the ground like a feather or leaf when dropped, landing unharmed. A thin coil of metal wraps around the wand’s handle.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Jump (Player Core)|_jump_]], but can jump up to 60 feet. When you land you shatter the ground, making each creature in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] until the start of its next turn. In addition, the space you land in and all squares in the emanation become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of 1st-level [[Spells/Jump (Player Core)|_jump_]].
