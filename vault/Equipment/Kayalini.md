# Kayalini  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Travel Guide]] pg. 103

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This animal can be purchased as a pet, but cannot be combat trained or contribute to combat in any way._

**Price** 5 gp

---

Originally brought to Absalom by [[Rules/Sidebars/Fetchling Settlements|kayals]] fleeing the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]], kayalinis are shy creatures made from shadow. They resemble small [[Equipment/Monkey|monkeys]] with reptilian features like claws, short horns, and long, shadowy tongues. When in a new home, they tend to hide under beds or in closets. Around people they trust, they'll sit quietly on a lap for hours.
