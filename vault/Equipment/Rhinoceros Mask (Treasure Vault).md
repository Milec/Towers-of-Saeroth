# Rhinoceros Mask  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 155

**Usage** worn mask

---

Covered with thick armor and bearing a thicker horn, a _rhinoceros mask_ grants you increased momentum. If you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] at least 10 feet, your next melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] before the end of your turn ignores the [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] of objects with a Hardness of 5 or less. If the object has more than Hardness 5, the mask grants no benefit.
