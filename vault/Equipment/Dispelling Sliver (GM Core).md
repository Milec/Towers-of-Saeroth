# Dispelling Sliver  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 264

**Price** 2,400 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

**Trigger** Your Strike damages a target

---

Made from a treated sliver of cold iron, this talisman allows you to counteract magical effects. When you activate the dispelling sliver, it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a single spell active on the target (counteract modifier +29), with the effects of an 8th-rank [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] spell.  
 If you activate the talisman on a successful [[Feats/Dispelling Slice]], the talisman attempts to counteract all spells active on the target, in addition to your attempt from Dispelling Slice.
