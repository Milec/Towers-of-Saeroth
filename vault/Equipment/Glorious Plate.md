# Glorious Plate  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 93

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate]]

---

This elegant [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate|full plate]] is embossed with heraldic imagery and never tarnishes or loses its shine. _Glorious plate_ is _+2 resilient full plate_. In battle, _glorious plate_ sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius, which enemies see as a blinding halo that obscures creatures other than the wearer. When an enemy looks at the wearer, it must attempt a DC 33 Will saving throw. The enemy is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Success** The enemy is unaffected.  

**Failure** For 1 minute, the wearer’s allies within the glorious plate’s 10-foot radius of light are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to the enemy. Allies are concealed only while they remain within the light; if allies enter the light later, they are concealed as well.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the wearer’s allies are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to the enemy instead of concealed.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** The armor casts [[Spells/Sanctuary|_sanctuary_]] on you.
