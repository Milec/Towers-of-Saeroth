# Camouflaging Chromatophores  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

**Usage** implanted

---

Special cells in your skin can change color to help you blend in with your environment. You gain the listed item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]].

**Activate—Background Adaptation** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For 1 minute, you can [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] without needing [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] to do so. This doesn’t allow you to [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] without ending your movement in cover or concealment, however, as your skin’s attempts to match the background as you move produce noticeable rippling waves of color.
