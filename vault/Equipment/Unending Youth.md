# Unending Youth  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Contract]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 10

---

You cease aging, and if you were older than young adult in age, you become a young adult again. You gain a +4 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects and resistance to negative damage equal to half your level. Once per day, from any distance, Abrogail Thrune II can recite a mandatory appearance clause in your Thrune contract as a 3-action activity to summon you to her side, where you are [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]] by Abrogail for 1 minute before you return.

**Activate** two actions command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You recite a hold harmless provision from your Thrune contract. Reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] value to 0. Abrogail Thrune II is immediately made aware that you have used this ability.
