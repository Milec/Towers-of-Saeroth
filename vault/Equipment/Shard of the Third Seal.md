# Shard of the Third Seal  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Crown of the Kobold King

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 117

**Price** 600 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This stone fragment from the _Third Seal_ retains a tiny sliver of that legendary item's power. The shard sheds light constantly, with the effects of a [[Equipment/Torch|torch]] requiring no oxygen and generating no heat. The flame can be covered or hidden, but it can't be smothered or quenched.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;     **Effect** You generate the effects of a [[Spells/Disrupt Undead|_disrupt undead_]] cantrip, heightened to 3rd level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** By passing the shard over a creature or object, you can produce the effects of a [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] spell on that target.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** By gripping the shard tightly and waving it before you, you can produce the effects of a [[Spells/Levitate|_levitate_]] spell on yourself.
