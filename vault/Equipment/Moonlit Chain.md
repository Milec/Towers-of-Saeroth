# Moonlit Chain  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 558

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Silver Armor|silver]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ has a collar adorned with stitched images of the phases of the moon. You can see in moonlight as though you had [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** one per day;   **Effect** You touch the stitched image of the new moon on the armor’s collar and suppress the [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] condition for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 33 gp of silver.
