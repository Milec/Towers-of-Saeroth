Acid drips from the [[Bestiary/Ankhrav (Monster Core)|ankhrav]] mandibles protruding from this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Knuckle Duster (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|knuckle duster]]_. Strikes with this weapon deal an additional 1 acid damage.

**Activate—Caustic Jabs** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Until the end of your next turn, your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with the ankhrav duster deal an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the mandibles of an [[Bestiary/Ankhrav (Monster Core)|ankhrav]].
