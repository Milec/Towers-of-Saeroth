# Ghoul Stiletto  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 36

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

A _ghoul stiletto_ is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ wrapped in the still-undead skin of a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul (Monster Core)|ghoul]]. While you wield the dagger, you gain a +1 status bonus to all saves against [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curses]] and [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] effects.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The _ghoul stiletto_ afflicts the next living target struck by the dagger with a terrible wound that emits the foul stench of the grave. The creature emits a stench [[Bestiary/Abilities/Aura (Monster Core)|aura]] in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] for 1 minute. During that time, the affected creature, as well as any creature that starts or ends its turn in the aura, must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune to all stench auras for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include skin from a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul (Monster Core)|ghoul]].
