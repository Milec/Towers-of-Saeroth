# Murderer's Knot  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Price** 66 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You damage an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature with a Strike using the affixed weapon.

---

This black strand of leather is tied to look like a peace knot when the weapon is worn, but it doesn't hamper drawing the weapon. When you activate the knot, the creature you damaged takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the bleed ends. If you have the [[Feats/Twist the Knife]] feat, the talisman instead deals persistent bleed damage equal to your sneak attack damage.
