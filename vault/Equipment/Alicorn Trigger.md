# Alicorn Trigger  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 104

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Jezail (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Jezail]]

---

This _+2 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Jezail (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|jezail]]_ features a preserved [[Bestiary/Alicorn|alicorn]] horn mounted underneath the barrel. Such a horn is usually willingly granted by the creature at the end of its natural lifespan, but some wicked gunsmiths acquire the horn through violence.

**Activate—Glimmer Beam** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The alicorn trigger unleashes a beam of radiant light in a 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. Creatures in the area of effect take 6d6 fire damage and must attempt a DC 33 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is blinded for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a horn from an [[Bestiary/Alicorn|alicorn]].
