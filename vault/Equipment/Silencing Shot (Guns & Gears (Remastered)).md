# Silencing Shot  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 171

**Price** 300 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action envision

---

This shimmering, golden-hued ammunition never makes any sound. A creature hit by a silencing shot is subject to the effects of a 4th-rank [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] spell unless it succeeds at a DC 25 Will save.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _silence_ at 4th level.
