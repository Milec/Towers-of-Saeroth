# Wand of Widening  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 598

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The end of this wand is forked instead of tapered.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; This activation takes two actions if the spell normally takes single action to cast, or three actions if the spell normally takes two actions;   **Effect** You Cast the Spell, and increase its area. Add 5 feet to the radius of a burst that normally has a radius of at least 10 feet; add 5 feet to the length of a cone or line that is normally 15 feet long or smaller; or add 10 feet to the length of a larger cone or line.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate level. The spell must have a casting time of single action or two actions, can’t have a duration, and must have an area of burst (10 feet or more), cone, or line.
