# Hydrating Pinwheel  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 236

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

Clear streams of water have been magically solidified into a glimmering, iridescent pinwheel that looks as fragile as glass. When affixed, it spins gently without a breeze.  
**• Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 3 to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]] and persistent fire damage.  
**• Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] spell by Activating the _enduring helix_, your Strikes with the weapon deal 1d4 persistent cold damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Rousing Splash|_rousing splash_]] ._
