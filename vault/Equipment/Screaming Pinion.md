# Screaming Pinion  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 52

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Flintlock Musket](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=191)

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [flintlock musket](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=191)_ is an innovation of the Platinum Wing, and possession of one by a civilian is a high crime in Andoran. The gun’s secret is the chip of a warshard used as the hammer in the striking mechanism.

**Activate—Transcendent Shot** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _screaming pinion_ is unloaded; **Effect** You call on the power of the warshard to load the _screaming pinion_ with a bullet made of divine fury. The next attack from the gun deals [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, rather than its typical type, and on a critical hit, the target is subject to the weapon’s [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect. If the gun isn’t fired before the end of your next turn, the bullet disappears, and the gun becomes unloaded.

**Activate—Eagle’s Cry** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The musket unleashes a piercing sound wave in a 70-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. Each creature in the area takes 8d10 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage with a DC 30 basic Fortitude save. A creature that critically fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]]. If the _screaming pinion_ has a [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|_+3 potency_ rune]], the DC increases to 35, and the damage increases to 10d10.
