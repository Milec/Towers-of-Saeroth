# Waterproofing Wax  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 217

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Many books and spellbooks are treated in this wax formula to protect them from the elements, but _waterproofing wax_’s liquid-repellent properties can be further applied to split up a _[[Spells/Grease (Player Core)|_grease_]]_ spell into useful, smaller sections. When the spell is cast in an area while using this catalyst, the conjured grease fills three 5-foot squares within 30 feet instead of its normal area; these squares don’t need to be contiguous.
