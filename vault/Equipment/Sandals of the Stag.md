# Sandals of the Stag  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 104

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn sandals

**Bulk** L

---

These sturdy leather sandals feature straps that wrap up to the knees. Etched in the leather are intricate patterns of stags leaping through the forest. You gain a +5-foot item bonus to your land Speed and a +3 item bonus on [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks when attempting to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. When you invest the sandals, you either increase your Strength score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever is higher.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You attempt a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]] but you didn’t [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] at least 10 feet;  **Effect** You can attempt the jump normally. It doesn’t automatically fail.
