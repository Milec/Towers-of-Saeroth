# Fulmination Fang  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 104

**Price** 115 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Gun Sword (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gun Sword]]

---

The stock and barrel of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Gun Sword (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|gun sword]]_ are lined with scales from a [[Bestiary/Storm Snake|storm snake]]. Their insulating properties grant their wielder some protection against electricity.

**Activate—Lightning Rod** ⤾ ;   **Trigger** You take electricity damage;  **Effect** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to the triggering damage, and the _fulmination fang_ crackles with the absorbed energy. You can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to change between the gun sword’s current usage (melee to ranged, or ranged to melee). The next melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] you make with the fulmination fang within the next minute deals an additional 1d6 electricity damage. After a minute, the absorbed energy dissipates.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the scales of a [[Bestiary/Storm Snake|storm snake]].
