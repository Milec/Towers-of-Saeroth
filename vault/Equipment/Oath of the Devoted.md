# Oath of the Devoted  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Contract]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 230

---

You gain fire and mental resistance 5. When you die, you rise as an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature with the [[Traits/Zombie|zombie]] trait on the next round; if you are a PC, you become an NPC under [[Setting/Deities/Walkena|Walkena's]] control. Your stats remain the same, except that your alignment changes to evil. If you reach 0 Hit Points as a zombie, you are destroyed and do not rise again.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain a +1 status bonus to Will saves. Choose a weapon or an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike; your chosen attack deals an extra 1d6 fire damage for the next 1 minute.

**Special** If you are a zombie at a time when Mzali is entirely purged of foreigners, your duty is fulfilled and you are immediately destroyed.
