# Rainbow Vinegar  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 105

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Access** Tian Xia Origin

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Black swill with multicolored ribbons makes this vinegar look like an oil spill. Ingesting a dose of rainbow vinegar makes your sweat acidic and nonconductive for 10 minutes. During this time, your [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks deal an additional 1d4 [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage, and you have [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]. [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|Vampires]] find this vinegar particularly harmful and take an additional 2d4 acid damage instead. Taking more than one dose of rainbow vinegar in a single day gives you [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]]5 to acid until your next daily preparations.
