# Foe-Sensing Rod  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Whetstone]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 135

**Price** 200 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

Imbued with a spirit fragment that continually surveys the world around it, this crystalline bar is roughened to form a file at one end. When you apply the _foe-sensing_ rod to a weapon, choose [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberration]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]], [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]], [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Giant (Player Core)|giant]], [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]], [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|ooze]], [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], or both [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]. The spirit fragment is transferred into your weapon for 1 hour, keeping watch for creatures with the chosen trait or traits. The affected weapon vibrates slightly if such a creature is within 60 feet of you, unless the creature is disguised or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] and has a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] DC of 26 or higher.
