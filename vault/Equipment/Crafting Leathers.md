**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

---

This simple leather armor is adorned with a series of pockets and pouches, all within easy reach. Designed for a busy crafter, each pocket or pouch contains a specific tool required for specialized crafting.

**Activate—Find Tool** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You reach into a pouch or pocket and find the specific short or long tool you’re looking for. The armor manifests the tool into existence for 1 hour.
