# Bristling Spines  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

**Usage** implanted

---

Your exposed skin is covered in fine, needle-like hairs that you can flick into the eyes of enemies.

**Activate—Urticating Burst** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You flick tiny spines in a 15-foot cone, dealing the listed amount of piercing damage to all creatures in the area with a basic Reflex save. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of their next turn.

## Bristling Spines (Lesser)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

**Price** 15 gp

---

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

## Bristling Spines (Greater)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97
