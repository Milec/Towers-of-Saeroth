**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

Aether marbles create violent, lingering eddies of force that extend partially into unseen planes. When the bomb explodes, it deals the listed force damage and force [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage. Many aether marbles grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

 Surfaces in the splash area are filled with twisting eddies of force, which are difficult terrain for [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures but not for corporeal ones. The force eddies in a square disperse after an incorporeal creature enters the square.
