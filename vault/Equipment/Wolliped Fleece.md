# Wolliped Fleece  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 119

**Price** 4 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

A small bundle of _[[Bestiary/Wolliped|wolliped]] fleece_ contains the memory of winter snows. Adding this catalyst to a casting of [[Spells/Chilling Spray|_chilling spray_]] coats all squares within the area of effect with a thin layer of ice. These squares become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] until the beginning of your next turn.
