# Ghast Stiletto  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 36

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

---

A _ghast stiletto_ is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ wrapped in the still-undead skin of a [[Bestiary/Ghast|ghast]]. While you wield the dagger, you gain a +1 status bonus to all saves against [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralysis]].

**Activate** single action command, Interact ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The _ghast stiletto_ afflicts the next living target struck by the dagger with a terrible wound that emits the foul stench of the grave. For the next minute, the target creature, as well as any creatures within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). While within the emanation, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] and to recover from the sickened condition. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include skin from a [[Bestiary/Ghast|ghast]].
