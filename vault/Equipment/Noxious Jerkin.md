# Noxious Jerkin  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -154- Siege of the Dinosaurs|Pathfinder #154: Siege of the Dinosaurs]] pg. 76

**Price** 2,800 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Padded Armor]]

---

This _+2 resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Padded Armor|padded armor]]_ is woven from many strands of gut cord strung with dried organs and preserved [[Bestiary/Families/Xulgath|xulgath]] scent glands. When worn, it infuses your body with a ghastly and nauseating flavor.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You are grabbed or are hit by an unarmed attack;  **Effect** Your attacker must attempt a DC 28 Fortitude saving throw. Creatures with a stench aura, such as [[Bestiary/Otyugh|otyughs]] and xulgaths, are immune to this effect.

**Success** The creature is unaffected and is immune to the effects of the noxious jerkin for 1 minute.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1.]]  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 1 and is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.
