# Slashing Claws  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Usage** implanted

---

Sharp claws have been grafted to your hands or feet, perhaps extending from your knuckles or the tips of your toes. You gain a claw [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage. These claws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] traits.

## Slashing Claws  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Price** 50 gp

## Deadly Slashing Claws  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Price** 320 gp

---

The damage die for your claw unarmed attack increases to 1d6 and it gains the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d8]] trait.
