# Glamered  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 583

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

This armor can be disguised with a mere thought.

**Activate** single action envision;     **Effect** You change the shape and appearance of this armor to appear as ordinary or fine clothes of your imagining. The armor’s statistics do not change. Only a creature that is benefiting from [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]] or a similar effect can attempt to disbelieve this illusion, with a DC of 25.
