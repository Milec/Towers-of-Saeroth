This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ made of black stone is carved from the bloodstained earth of the Shadefields and carries the grudge of the Shoanti who perished on that battlefield.

**Activate—Vengeful Blood** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Blood seeps out of the knife, coating your hand. Enemies within 30 feet who can see this must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 on a critical failure). The knife continues to bleed for 1 minute or until you make a successful Strike with it, which deals an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].
