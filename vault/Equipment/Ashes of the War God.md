_Ashes of the war god_ may be applied to armor, a weapon, or mixed into an [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|elixir]] or [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|potion]].

**Armor** The armor becomes [[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+3 major resilient]] armor for 1 day. It retains any [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]].

**Elixir or Potion** In addition to the normal effects drinking the elixir or potion grant you, you gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks and Fortitude saves for 1 hour.

**Weapon** The weapon becomes a [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] weapon for 1 day. It retains any property runes.
