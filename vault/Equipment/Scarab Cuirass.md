The cuirass of this _+1 [[Equipment/Deathless|deathless]] [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]]_, stitched to resemble an Osiriani scarab beetle, feels strangely chitinous. You gain resistance 5 to [[Traits/Negative|negative]] damage.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You cast a 1st-level [[Spells/Pest Form|_pest form_]]. You can only become a scarab beetle.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _pest form_.
