# Bottled Melody  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 223

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This potion is a melody in clear liquid form, with little musical notations swimming and darting through it like fish. You gain the Songburst action for 1 hour after you imbibe this concoction.  

**Songburst** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) You vocalize a high-pitched melody that deals 4d6 sonic damage to all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature that fails the saving throw is also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round (or 1 minute on a critical failure). After you use Songburst, you can’t do so again for 1d4 rounds.
