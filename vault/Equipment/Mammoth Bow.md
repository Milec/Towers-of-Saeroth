# Mammoth Bow  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 74

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 4

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow]]

---

This heavy, 12-foot-tall _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Longbow|composite longbow]]_ is fashioned from mammoth tusks lashed together. Due to its great size, you can attack with the _Mammoth Bow_ only if you're Large or larger. The _Mammoth Bow_ deals 2d6 additional piercing damage because of its incredible, magically enhanced draw power. Additionally, the Mammoth Bow has a 180-foot [[Traits/Range|range increment]] instead of a 100-foot range increment, but its [[Traits/Volley|volley]] range increases from 30 feet to 50 feet.
