# Mindlance  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 39

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [Arquebus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=195)

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [arquebus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=195)_ is used by caravan guards to nonlethally—though powerfully—deter large game and bandits. When fired, the spark gun deals mental damage and adds the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait to the attack. Each _mindlance_ also includes a [[Equipment/Weapons/Reinforced Stock (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|reinforced stock]] that benefits from any [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|fundamental runes]] on the firearm. When you critically succeed at an attack roll with a _mindlance_, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] unless it succeeds at a DC 24 Will save.
