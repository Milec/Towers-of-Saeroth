# Anchor of Aquatic Exploration  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 114

**Price** 690 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

No matter how often it’s washed, the last crystals of salt rime never quite leave this pitted anchor. When you’re holding the _anchor of aquatic exploration_, you can breathe underwater; however, you can’t [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], losing any [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|Swim speed]] you have, automatically failing any [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to Swim, and so on. Instead, when you enter a body of water, you sink to the bottom at a rate of 25 feet per round and can move across the bottom at your normal Speed. You’re protected from environmental effects of deep water such as pressure, cold temperatures, and any negative consequences of depressurization.

**Activate**  1 minute (Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You're underwater; **Effect** You spend one minute digging the anchor into the seabed, after which the anchor casts a [[Spells/Cozy Cabin (Player Core)|_cozy cabin_]] spell, summoning a sunken ship instead of a cabin. The sunken ship is filled with breathable air.
