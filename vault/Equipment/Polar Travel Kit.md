# Polar Travel Kit  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 28

**Price** 30 gp

**Bulk** 4

---

A polar travel kit costs 30 gp and is 4 Bulk. Each kit includes a [[Equipment/Bedroll|bedroll]], a [[Equipment/Climbing Kit|climbing kit]], a [[Equipment/Compass|compass]], a small knife, a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]], a short shovel, a short saw for cutting ice, a set of skis and poles, a pair of snow goggles, a pair of snowshoes, a [[Equipment/Spyglass|spyglass]], twenty [[Equipment/Tindertwig|tindertwigs]], and two sets of [[Equipment/Clothing|winter clothing]].
