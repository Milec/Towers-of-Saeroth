# Psalter Of The Blessed  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 230

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Bulk** L

---

These gold-leafed volumes bear the religious symbol of a deity who allows or requires [[Traits/Holy|holy]] sanctification. [[Traits/Unholy]] creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 as long as they keep the tome in their possession and can’t recover from this condition.

**Activate—Righteous Prayers** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared Spell with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait, the energy blesses you and your allies with celestial power. You and all allies within 30 feet of you gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 5 and a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws against effects from creatures with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait for 1 minute.
