---

The beautiful plates of this _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Ready (GM Core)|greater ready]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ armor are shaped from the carapace of a deadly mantis, providing excellent protection with added flexibility. When wearing a suit of _mantis plate_, you gain a +2 item bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] DC.

**Activate—Mandible Vambraces** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. If you succeed, the target is caught by the spikes on the armor’s forearms, taking an additional 6d6 piercing damage (DC 30 basic Reflex save).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the arms and carapace of a [[Bestiary/Deadly Mantis (Monster Core)|deadly mantis]].
