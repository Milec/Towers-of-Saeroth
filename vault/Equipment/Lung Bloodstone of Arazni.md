# Lung Bloodstone of Arazni  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 109

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The _Lung Bloodstone of Arazni_ represents suffering. While you carry the _Lung Bloodstone_, you gain the ability to turn your pain into a weapon against your enemies.

**Activate—Strength through Pain** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You take damage;  **Effect** You allow the pain to resonate within you, making you stronger. You gain a +1 status bonus to your next [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or [[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack]] before the end of your next turn.

**Activate—Admonish** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You worship [[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]] or are favored by her; **Effect** You point your finger at a creature within 60 feet that has wronged you, and you verbally admonish them. The creature takes 1d4 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage for every level you have ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save against your class DC). On a failed save, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (stunned 3 on a critical failure).

**Destruction** To destroy the _Lung Bloodstone_, a worshipper of Arazni must willingly impale themself with the _Bloodstone’s_ horned figurehead, dealing themselves 3d6 piercing damage and 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. The _Lung Bloodstone_ then evaporates into a cloud of noxious vapors, as [[Spells/Toxic Cloud|_toxic cloud_]] (DC 28), that follows the creature that destroyed the _Bloodstone_ for its duration.
