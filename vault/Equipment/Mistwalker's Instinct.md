# Mistwalker's Instinct  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 215

**Price** 18 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Wisps of fog and mist waft from this smooth, polished amulet in the shape of a raindrop. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Mist|_mist_]]_ spell, you can sense the direction of each creature from you within the mist, though you can’t instinctively discern allies from enemies based on direction alone. The DC of [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat checks]] you attempt to target a creature within the mist is reduced from 5 to 3.
