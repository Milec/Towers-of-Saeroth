# Adamantine Echo  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -193- Mantle of Gold|Pathfinder #193: Mantle of Gold]] pg. 83

**Usage** incorporated into armor

**Bulk** L

---

Made from glossy, nearly black adamantine, this large fragment of a plate armor vambrace provides little protection on its own. However, when incorporated into an intact suit of armor, it functions as a [[Equipment/Armor Potency|_+1 armor potency_]] rune.  
 **Forming the Bond** The PCs complete the [[Rituals/Rite of Repatriation|rite of repatriation]].  
 **Advancing the Bond** Survive (or even triumph against) seemingly impossible odds.  
 **Minor Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Beginner's Luck (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|beginner's luck]], [[Equipment/Relics/Ease Burden|ease burden]], [[Equipment/Relics/Shattered Earth|shattered earth]]  
 **Major Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Raise Ramparts|raise ramparts]], [[Equipment/Relics/Steal Luck (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|steal luck]]
