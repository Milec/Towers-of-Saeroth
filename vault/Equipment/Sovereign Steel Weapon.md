---

Sovereign steel weapons are treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] against creatures with a weakness to cold iron, like [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|demons]] and [[Traits/Fey|fey]]. In addition, the [[Equipment/Noqual|noqual]] in sovereign steel weapons disrupts spellcasters' concentration, causing them to become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 round on a critical hit.

## Sovereign Steel Weapon (Standard-Grade)  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 77

**Price** 1,600 gp (+160 gp per Buk)

**Craft Requirements** at least 100 gp of cold iron + 10 gp per Bulk plus at least 100 gp of noqual + 10 gp per Bulk

## Sovereign Steel Weapon (High-Grade)  *Item 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 77

**Price** 32,000 gp (+3,200 gp per Buk)

**Craft Requirements** at least 8,000 gp of cold iron + 800 gp per Bulk plus at least 8,000 gp of noqual + 800 gp per Bulk.
