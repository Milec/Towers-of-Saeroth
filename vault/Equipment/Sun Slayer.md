# Sun Slayer  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 124

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|Wooden Shield]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|lesser reinforced]] [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|wooden shield]]_ (Hardness 6, HP 64, BT 32) is painted with intricate, pale-blue knots. The wood has been magically enhanced against solar energy and heat. While you have this shield raised, you gain fire [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5.

**Activate—Sunset** two actions ([[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are in sunlight; **Effect** You raise your shield in the air toward the sunlight, eclipsing it. The area in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] becomes dim light for 1 minute.
