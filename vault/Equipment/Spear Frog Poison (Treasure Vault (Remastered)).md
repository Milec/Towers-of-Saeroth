Carefully harvested from the skin of the poisonous [[Bestiary/Spear Frog|spear frog]], this toxin causes a burning rash and weakness in the limbs. Each frog yields enough toxin to Craft one dose of spear frog poison.

 **Saving Throw** DC 15 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d4 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] (1 round)
