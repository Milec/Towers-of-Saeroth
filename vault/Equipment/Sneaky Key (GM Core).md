# Sneaky Key  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 267

**Price** 22 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

**Requirement** You are trained in Thievery

---

This small silver skeleton key can be pinned to armor or a sleeve. When you turn the key to activate it, you gain a +1 status bonus to Thievery checks to [[Actions/Pick a Lock]] for 1 minute. The first time you get a success or critical success on an attempt to Pick a Lock, you achieve one additional success toward opening a complex lock.
