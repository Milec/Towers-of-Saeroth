# Golden Chrysalis  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 212

**Price** 27 gp

**Ammunition** sling bullet

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Threads of iridescent golden silk wrap around the core of this magical sling stone. When you activate and [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with a _golden chrysalis_, you don’t make an attack roll or deal damage. Instead, you cause the chrysalis to unwind in midair to reveal a magical butterfly that flies in a 30-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] toward your target, scattering golden scale dust that hangs in the air for 1 minute. A creature that enters or begins its turn in the dust must attempt a DC 19 Reflex save or have its [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|invisibility]] suppressed, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]]. This effect applies both if the creature was already invisible and if it benefits from any new invisibility effects before the end of the invisibility suppression effect from the _golden chrysalis_.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature’s invisibility is suppressed for 2 rounds.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute, and its invisibility is suppressed for 1 minute.
