# Wand of Slaying  *Item 16+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 598

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This polished black wand has a green gem at the tip, and anyone who looks into it sees a reflection of a grinning skull instead of their face.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Finger of Death|_finger of death_]] of the indicated level. If the spell slays its target, the corpse releases negative energy in a 20-foot emanation, dealing negative damage equal to double the spell’s level.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _finger of death_ of the appropriate level.
