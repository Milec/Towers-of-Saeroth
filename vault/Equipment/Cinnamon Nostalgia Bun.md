A swirl of cinnamon, sugar, and butter is baked into this fluffy bun. Pulling apart and eating the bun evokes comforting memories of happier times, reducing the value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition by 1. You can also attempt an immediate [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to recover from a single source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]], with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.
