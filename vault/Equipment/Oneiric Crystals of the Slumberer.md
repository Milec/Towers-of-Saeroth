# Oneiric Crystals of the Slumberer  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 108

**Price** 5,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

These trapezoidal crystals were created by a powerful [[Bestiary/Alghollthu Master|alghollthu master]] from long-forgotten deep sea stones. They were used to steal the dreams and memories of the master’s victims, trapping them inside so they could be consulted when needed. When broken, the creatures whose memories were stolen retrieve them but are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round.

**Activate—Steal Remembrance** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Choose a creature you can see, trapping its memories in the crystal. The creature must attempt a DC 31 Will saving throw. If the creature is asleep, it uses the outcome one degree of success worse than the result of its roll.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and realizes you tried to steal its memories.  

**Success** The creature is stupefied 1 for 1 round but doesn’t lose any memory, and believes you did something harmless to its mind.  

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 2 for 1 hour and loses all memory of a particular topic that can be detailed in 50 words or fewer.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stupefied 4 for 1 day and loses all memory of two particular topics, each of which can be detailed in 50 words or fewer.
