# Black Scorpion Stingmace  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 101

**Price** 6,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|Mace]]

---

The massive stinger of a black scorpion adds significant weight to this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Fearsome (GM Core)|fearsome]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|mace]]_. On a critical hit, the target is exposed to black scorpion venom.

**Black Scorpion Venom** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) Saving Throw DC 36 Fortitude; Maximum Duration 6 rounds; Stage 1 2d12 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 (1 round); Stage 2 3d12 poison damage, clumsy 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 (1 round); Stage 3 4d12 poison damage, clumsy 4, and slowed 2 (1 round).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the intact stinger of a [[Bestiary/Black Scorpion|black scorpion]].
