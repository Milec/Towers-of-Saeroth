# Rune of Sin  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 165

**Price** 1,700 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This jagged glyph, one of the [[Rules/Thassilonian Rune Magic|Thassilonian runes of sin]], reacts to magic of a particular school (there's no rune for [[Traits/Divination|divination]], as it was considered lesser within sin magic). When you Cast a Spell of the school matching the sin, you gain resistance 5 to damage from spells until the start of your next turn. This resistance is increased to 7 against spells of the matching school. This tattoo has the school trait matching the rune: [[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]] for envy, [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] for gluttony, [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]] for greed, [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] for lust, [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] for pride, [[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]] for sloth, and [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]] for wrath.
