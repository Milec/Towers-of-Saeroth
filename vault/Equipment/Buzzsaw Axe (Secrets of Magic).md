# Buzzsaw Axe  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 181

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe]]

---

With an aerodynamic cutting edge and a curved handle, this _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]]_ is perfectly suited to whirling motions, and in fact seems like it wants to whirl free from your grip. A Strike with this axe that benefits from the [[Traits/Sweep|sweep]] trait's circumstance bonus on attack rolls also gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the damage roll.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You hurl the axe, which spins at great speed as it flies to a distant point and returns to you, leaving a trail of carnage. Each creature in a 120-foot line must succeed at a DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save or take damage equal to the weapon's melee damage, including your Strength modifier.
