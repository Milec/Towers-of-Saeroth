# Ring of Sigils  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 294

**Usage** worn

---

This silver band is carved with the personal sigils of different individuals, adding one to represent you when you invest it. The ring allows you to cast [[Spells/Sigil|_sigil_]] as an arcane innate cantrip.

**Activate—Track Sigil** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** You detect the general direction toward the most recent _sigil_ you created using the ring. This activation fails if the _sigil_ is more than 5 miles away or if there's lead or running water between you and the _sigil_.
