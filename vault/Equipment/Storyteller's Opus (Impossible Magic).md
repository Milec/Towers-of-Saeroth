# Storyteller's Opus  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 230

**Price** 230 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This green spellbook with gold trim contains a cautionary story about a boy who was eaten by a [[Bestiary/Families/Wolf (Monster Core)|wolf]] after he previously lied about wolf attacks. When opened, the engravings on the front seem to move in a loop, enacting this story.

**Activate—Hoodwink** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature succeeds, but doesn’t critically succeed, at a Perception check to disbelieve an illusion spell you’ve cast today;  **Effect** You quickly tell a fib to try to smooth over the inconsistencies in your illusion. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check against the triggering creature’s [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] DC. If you succeed, the creature doesn’t disbelieve the [[Rules/Illusion|illusion]].
