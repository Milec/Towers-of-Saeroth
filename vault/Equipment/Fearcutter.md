In his days as an adventurer, Eragayl Arudora made a name for himself as a champion of Shelyn. Devoted to the cause of grandeur, Eragayl eschewed the typical glaive of her champions and instead focused his skills on the longsword and shield. It was his shield that gained Shelyn’s blessings, but by the time he retired and began building Castle Arudora, Eragayl’s sword, _Fearcutter_, was arguably his most legendary belonging. With this blade, he slew countless undead and fiends, including the monstrous sahkil Vormingundur, who for many years tormented a dozen small settlements along the southwest border of Varno before meeting his end on this blade’s edge. _Fearcutter_ is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Astral|astral]] [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]]_ that grants a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects while you carry it unsheathed. When you critically hit a creature with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait with _Fearcutter_, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.

**Activate—Cut Fear** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You swing _Fearcutter_ through the air near a [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] creature. Reduce that creature’s frightened value by 1.

**Activate—Cut Cruelty** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You brandish _Fearcutter_ forcefully, and the sword casts a 5th-rank [[Spells/Crisis of Faith (Player Core)|_crisis of faith_]] (DC 29 Will save) at an unholy target in range.
