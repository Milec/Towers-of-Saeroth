# Spirit-sealing Fulu  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221

**Duration** 1 round

**Usage** affixed to one undead creature

---

This classic fulu seals the void energy within an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], freezing it in its tracks. Upon affixing, the target undead must attempt a DC 17 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 1 round.

## Spirit-sealing Fulu  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221

**Price** 10 gp

## Greater Spirit-sealing Fulu  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221

**Price** 155 gp

---

The DC is 27. On a critical failure, the undead is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 4 rounds. At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt a new Will save to reduce the remaining duration by 1 round or end it entirely on a critical success.
