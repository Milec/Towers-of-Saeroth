# South Wind's Scorch Song  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 190

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]]_ is always warm to the touch, nearly unbearably so. Lines of crackling flame radiate from carnelians affixed to its curved and blackened blade, and its pommel ends in a brilliant tassel of [[Bestiary/Phoenix|phoenix]] feathers. While you have a status bonus to at least one of your Speeds, your Strikes with this scimitar that deal fire damage gain a +2 status bonus to their fire damage. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking_ rune and +4 for _major striking_.

**Activate** free action command ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]])  **Trigger** Your spell deals fire damage to a creature;  **Effect** You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed until the end of your next turn.
