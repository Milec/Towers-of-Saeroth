# Mirror Goggles  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 147

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

These goggles feature highly reflective lenses. While wearing the goggles, you gain a +1 item bonus to visual [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks and to saving throws against [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effects.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact;   **Trigger** A creature within 60 feet targets you with a [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effect;  **Effect** You turn your head to reflect aspects of the triggering effect back at its creator. The creature must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save as it becomes disoriented by this reflection. On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (sickened 2 on a critical failure). The creature is temporarily immune for 1 hour.
