**Bulk** 1

---

This staff was created as an example of the [[Rules/Personal Staves|personal staves rules]].

Moss and winding vines give this gnarled staff of wild wood a vibrant green tinge. You created this staff to aid you in speaking to the plants you met on your adventure and beseeching them to come to your aid.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Tanglefoot|_tanglefoot_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Protector Tree|protector tree]], [[Spells/Shillelagh|_shillelagh_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Entangle|_entangle_]], [[Spells/Shape Wood|_shape wood_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Tree Shape|_tree shape_]], [[Spells/Wall of Thorns|_wall of thorns_]]
- **4th** [[Spells/Barkskin|_barkskin_]], [[Spells/Speak with Plants|_speak with plants_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Plant Form|_plant form_]], _wall of thorns_
- **6th** [[Spells/Nature's Reprisal|_nature's reprisal_]], _plant form_

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
