# Slithermaw's Bane  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -211- The Secret of Deathstalk Tower|Pathfinder #211: The Secret of Deathstalk Tower]] pg. 82

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt]]

---

This suit of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Elven Chain (GM Core)|elven chain]]_ was worn by the elven hero Kyloss Syndar. The armor served him well, but eventually he fell in battle against the demonic hydra Slithermaw. As Kyloss slew the demonic beast, its fangs pierced his chest and mortally wounded the elf. The armor retains several ragged holes along the chest and abdomen where the hydra’s teeth damaged it.

_Slithermaw’s Bane_ grants its wearer poison [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10, and the [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] rune increases the item bonus on saving throws versus [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] by 1 (to +3).

**Activate—Calistria’s Sting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapples]] you;  **Effect** Poison wells up from the armor’s links to seep into the triggering creature’s body, causing it to suffer wracking pains as if it were being stung by thousands of angry wasps. The triggering creature takes 7d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save); this persistent damage cannot be ended as long as the triggering creature continues to grapple you.

**Activate—Terrain Adaptation**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You alter the exterior of the armor to better adapt to the surrounding [[Rules/Terrain (GM Core)|terrain]]: [[Rules/Aquatic (GM Core)|aquatic]], [[Rules/Arctic (GM Core)|arctic]], [[Rules/Desert (GM Core)|desert]], [[Rules/Forest (GM Core)|forest]], [[Rules/Mountain (GM Core)|mountain]], [[Rules/Plains (GM Core)|plains]], sky, [[Rules/Swamp (GM Core)|swamp]], or [[Rules/Underground (GM Core)|underground]]. You ignore non-magical [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] within the chosen environment and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Environmental Hazards|environmental hazards]], [[Rules/Natural Disasters (GM Core)|natural disasters]], and [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|extreme temperatures]] that originate from that terrain. You are also protected from severe and extreme heat or severe and extreme cold (your choice when you activate this ability). This effect lasts until your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparation]].
