# Shield Of The Bear  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

This massive [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]] ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 8, HP 32, BT 16) is engraved with the image of a roaring face of a bear and lined with patches of bear fur. When you have this shield raised, if any effect would force you to move 10 feet or more, you’re moved 5 fewer feet.

**Activate—Feral Roar** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You use the _shield of the bear_ to Shield Block a melee Strike;  **Effect** The attacking creature must succeed at a DC 26 Fortitude save or be pushed up to 10 feet away from you (15 feet on a critical failure).
