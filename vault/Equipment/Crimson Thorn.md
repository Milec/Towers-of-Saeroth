# Crimson Thorn  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 213

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Asp Coil]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Weapons/Asp Coil|asp coil]]_ is used by many trained Sisters of the Golden Erinys. It has a deep-red hue on each segment of the blade complemented by a pitch-black pommel. Death isn't the worst outcome for someone on the receiving end of its lashes.

**Activate—Taste the Pain** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** Your last action was a Strike with the _crimson thorn_ that dealt damage to a creature;  **Effect** Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] the creature you dealt damage to with a +2 item bonus to the roll. You don't take any penalties to this check for not sharing a language.
