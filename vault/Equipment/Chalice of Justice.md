# Chalice of Justice  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 280

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|Longsword]]

---

This gleaming golden sword is a _+2 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]]_ given only to the worthiest heroes of a holy faith. It's made of a sacred, secret alloy that makes the blade both cold iron and silver. If you're [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you're [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 while holding a chalice of justice. You can't recover from this condition while holding the weapon.

**Activate—Sip of Justice** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The pommel of the weapon transmutes into the shape of a miniature chalice. You drink deep from the chalice and regain HP equal to the chalice's level. If you're holy, you regain double the HP, and for 1 minute, whenever you critically hit an unholy creature with the chalice of justice, the creature takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent spirit damage]], and it's slowed 1 for 1 round. The persistent spirit damage has the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait.

**Craft Requirements** You're holy.
