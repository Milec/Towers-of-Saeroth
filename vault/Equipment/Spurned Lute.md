# Spurned Lute  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 193

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

Made of a deep-brown rosewood, a _spurned lute_ is adorned with carved flowers. The lute appears to be and functions as a [[Equipment/Musical Instrument (Player Core)|virtuoso instrument]]. (Other spurned instruments exist, but the lute is the least rare.) This lute has a jealous streak, demanding total loyalty from its “partner” musician. After you play the lute for the first time, it fuses to you. If you go a day without using it to [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until you next do so. After that, when you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check using an instrument other than the lute, you take a –4 circumstance penalty to do so, and you must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become stupefied 1 for 1 minute.
