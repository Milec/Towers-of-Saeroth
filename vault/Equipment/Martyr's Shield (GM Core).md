_A martyr's shield_ is a [[Equipment/Sturdy Shield|_lesser sturdy shield_]] imbued with the compassion of a devout champion of a righteous deity, like [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]] or [[Setting/Deities/Vildeis]], who sacrificed themself to save an ally. In addition to good-naturedly attempting to convert you to its religion, the _martyr's shield_ can use 1 reaction each round that, when raised, it uses to [[Feats/Shield Block]] to protect an ally adjacent to you. This follows the rules for Shield Block, but protects your ally instead. The _martyr's shield_ uses this reaction whether you would prefer it did so or not. The _martyr's shield_ can be upgraded to a stronger form of _sturdy shield_ by paying the difference in cost between its current type of _sturdy shield_ and the new type.
