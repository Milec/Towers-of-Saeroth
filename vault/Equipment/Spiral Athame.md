# Spiral Athame  *Item 22*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 79

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

The pommel of this +4 [[Equipment/Striking|major striking]] [[Equipment/Disrupting|disruptive]] [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]] [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|high-grade silver]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]] is a black glass orb that contains a tiny blue comet that spirals through the orb's interior. Strikes with the dagger deal an additional 1d8 positive damage to worshippers of [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]] and to anyone who has ever created or [[Traits/Summoned|summoned]] an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature.

 While the dagger is in your possession, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Midwifery Lore]] checks. If you're a worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]], you gain [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]] with a range of 60 feet while holding the dagger. If you're undead or a worshipper of Urgathoa, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 4]] while holding the dagger.

 Despite its utility in combat, the _Spiral Athame_ is intended as a ritual implement, meant to bless newborns and protect them from the predations of undead. If you use the _Spiral Athame_ to cut the umbilical cord of a newly born creature, that creature gains resistance to negative damage equal to their level (minimum 1) and gains a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects and [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]. These bonuses are permanent. Additionally, that creature can never become an undead creature.

**Activate** two actions command, interact ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]])    **Effect** You point the _Spiral Athame_ at a corpse within 30 feet, shielding it from corruption. The _Spiral Athame_ casts 5th-level [[Spells/Gentle Repose|_gentle repose_]] on the corpse.

**Activate** two actions command, interact ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]])    **Effect** You point the _Spiral Athame_ to the sky and pray, warding creatures against undead. This has the effect of 3rd-level [[Spells/Circle of Protection|_circle of protection_]], except it wards against undead creatures rather than creatures of a specified alignment, and the emanation is centered on the _Spiral Athame_.

**Destruction** If an undead creature uses the _Spiral Athame_ to slay a [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]] usher while within the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]], the _Spiral Athame's_ pommel shatters and the _Spiral Athame_ becomes a mundane, high-grade silver dagger with no special abilities.
