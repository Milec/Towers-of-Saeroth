# Safeguarding  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 204

**Price** 2,600 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

Your armor is overlayed with a shimmering mosaic of pink, turquoise, and blue energy that protects the most vital aspects of your person. When activated, the armor’s hue shifts according to the type of resistance you gain: turquoise ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]), blue ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]), or pink ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]).

**Activate—Adaptive Aegis** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would take mental, poison, or spirit damage;  **Effect** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to the triggering damage type that lasts for 10 minutes.
