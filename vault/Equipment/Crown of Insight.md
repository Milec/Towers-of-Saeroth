# Crown of Insight  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 38

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** tattoo

---

A series of evenly spaced, unblinking tattooed eyes line your skin. When you enter an area that contains creatures within 60 feet that are unnoticed to you, the GM rolls a [[Traits/Secret|secret]] Perception check for you against the creatures' [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] DCs. On a success, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by you, rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed|unnoticed]], and on a critical success, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to you. All creatures in the area are then temporarily immune to your _crown of insight_ for 24 hours. The _crown of insight_ doesn't help you find hidden items or traps, nor can it help you discern a creature hiding in plain sight, such as a [[Bestiary/Gargoyle|gargoyle]] pretending to be an inanimate statue. Additionally, since the effect occurs when you move to enter an area that contains creatures, it doesn't detect an unnoticed creature [[Actions/Sneak|Sneaking]] up on you.
