([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

**Requirement** Your last action was an Interact action to stow the affixed firearm or crossbow.

---

This ragged piece of white cloth is wrapped around the grip, stock, or haft of the affixed weapon. When you activate the talisman, you gain the effects of a [[Spells/Sanctuary|_sanctuary_]] spell (DC 20) lasting for 1 minute. If you draw the affixed firearm, the effect ends immediately and the talisman crumbles.
