# Staff of Sun Wukong  *Item 20*

Also known as _Ruyi Bang_, this [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+3]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|major striking]] [[Equipment/Extending (GM Core)|greater extending]] [[Equipment/Grievous (GM Core)|grievous]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|bo staff]] is a legendary artifact wielded by the Monkey King, [[Setting/Deities/Sun Wukong (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Sun Wukong]]. Unlike normal bo staves, the Staff of Sun Wukong is made of solid iron with two brilliant gold bands at either end.

**Activate—Meteor Slam** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You extend the staff with the force of a meteor, dealing 10d10 bludgeoning damage to a single creature within 120 feet and making it [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 day. All other creatures within 10 feet of the target (except the staff’s wielder) take 8d10 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage (DC 43 basic Reflex save).

**Activate** free action Close at Hand;     **Effect** It’s said that Sun Wukong tucked this magical staff behind his ear during his travels. When not in use, the Staff of Sun Wukong shrinks to the size and weight of a needle or toothpick, making it highly concealable (+4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to hide it from sight). In this form, the item has negligible Bulk. You can [[Actions/Activate an Item|Activate]] this ability again to return the staff to its normal size.

**Activate—Cloud Somersault** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You use the shapeshifting capabilities of this relic to propel yourself great distances. You [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] up to 50 feet in any direction.

**Destruction** Despite being made of iron and gold, mundane heat sources can’t damage the Staff of Sun Wukong. Magical fire similarly has no effect on this relic. The only way to destroy this item is to expose it to the intense heat of a specific volcano in the depths of the Songil Sea (though which volcano remains shrouded in myth).
