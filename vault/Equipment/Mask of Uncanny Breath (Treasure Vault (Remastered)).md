# Mask of Uncanny Breath  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 150

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

---

A thin wooden mask carved in the shape of a skull, monstrous face, or eerily featureless visage, a mask of uncanny breath fully covers your face. While wearing it, each breath you take feels cool and pure, perfectly flowing to feed your qi. You gain resistance 10 to [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] [[Traits/Poison|poisons]] and can breathe in an airless or toxic environment. When you breathe in, fragments of bizarre knowledge flow through you, granting you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] checks.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can use only to cast an [[Traits/Occult|occult]] monk qi spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike hits a creature that breathes;  **Effect** The mask contorts and inhales, sucking breath from your target's lungs. The target falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] but doesn't fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] or drop what it's holding. It wakes up at the end of your turn if it hasn't been woken up already.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|monk]] with occult qi spells.
