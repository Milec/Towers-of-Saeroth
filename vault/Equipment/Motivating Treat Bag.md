# Motivating Treat Bag  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 77

**Price** 135 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This stiff, cloth pouch is embroidered with whimsical images of playful pets. When you perform your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]] in the presence of your creature [[Traits/Companion (GM Core)|companion]], three treats appear in the bag: an encouraging treat, a reward treat, and a soothing treat. These treats are always suitable for your companion and last until the next time you perform your daily preparations.

**Activate—Toss a Treat** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** Varies by treat;  **Effect** You pull out one of the treats and toss it up to 30 feet into the waiting mouth of your companion. The effect varies by the treat.

- **Encouraging Treat** ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** You [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command]] your companion, and it performs a skill check; **Effect** The treat motivates the companion to perform the task, allowing you to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] it as though you had prepared to help.
- **Reward Treat** ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], mental) **Trigger** Your companion critically succeeds at a skill check or attack roll; **Effect** The treat grants the companion 1d6+6 temporary Hit Points that last until the end of your next turn.
- **Soothing Treat** (healing) **Trigger** Your companion attempts a save to reduce its [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition, would reduce the value of its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition at the end of your turn, or attempts a flat check to end [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]]; **Effect** Reduce the DC of the check to end persistent damage by 2 or reduce the sickened condition by 2. If the companion would reduce its frightened condition value by 1 at the end of its turn, it instead reduces that condition’s value by 2.

**Activate—Rustle the Bag** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You [[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_Command_]] your companion. Until the end of your turn, it gains a +5-foot status bonus to each of its Speeds, but must use at least one action to move closer to you.
