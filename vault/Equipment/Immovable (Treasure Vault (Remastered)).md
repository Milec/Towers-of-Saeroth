# Immovable  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 12

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

This rune utilizes magical principles to manipulate gravity and turn you into an immovable object.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your armor anchors you in place, even defying gravity, rendering you [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until you Dismiss the Activation. while you're immobilized in this way, you can be moved only if a creature succeeds at a DC 40 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Force Open]] your armor. You can also be moved if 8,000 pounds of pressure are placed upon you, though this is likely fatal.
