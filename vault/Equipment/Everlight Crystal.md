# Everlight Crystal  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 273

**Price** 15 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

An _everlight crystal_ is one of the most common applications of permanent magic. This stone or gem sheds magical [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] constantly in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet). The light requires no oxygen, generates no heat, and can’t be extinguished, though the crystal can be covered.
