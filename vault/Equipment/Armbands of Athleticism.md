# Armbands of Athleticism  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 605

**Usage** worn armbands

**Bulk** L

---

Skilled awl work has imprinted images of a muscled weightlifter into these tiered leather bands, which grant you enhanced stamina and skill when performing athletic exercises. While fastened to your upper arms, the armbands give you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks. In addition, whenever you use an action to Climb or Swim and you succeed at the Athletics check, add a +5-foot item bonus to the distance you move.
