# Tiger's Claw  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 166

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Dueling Pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=201)

---

This +2 _[[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Fearsome|fearsome]] [dueling pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=201)_ is made from fine tigerwood, with the head of a tiger as the muzzle. Beneath the tiger head is a claw shaped [[Equipment/Weapons/Bayonet (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|bayonet]]. One of a set of four guns crafted as a gift to a Zenj family for delivery of rare healing and disease-abating herbs during an outbreak of a deadly disease in the Grand Duchy of Alkenstar, these firearms are now passed down to those who have done brave acts in service to the Zenj people. The flintlock sparks thrown by this weapon take the shape of pouncing tigers and the firearm's report sounds like a tiger's growl. Clever wielders use the firearm's report to panic their prey into mistakes and then pounce for the kill.

 This firearm's bullets deal slashing damage instead of piercing and add an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] on a critical hit. This persistent bleed damage causes tiger-claw-shaped wounds to appear on the target.

**Activate** two actions [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You capitalize on the fears your firearm engenders, terrifying your foes. Make a ranged Strike with this firearm against a target. If you successfully deal damage to your target, the target is also affected by a 4th-level [[Spells/Phantasmal Killer|_phantasmal killer_]] with a spell DC of 28. While _phantasmal killer_ typically takes the shape of the target's worst fear, this effect always appears to the target in the form of a majestic and ferocious tiger.
