# Venomed Tongue  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Price** 333 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** Followers of Abraxas from Nex have access to this tattoo.

---

Secrets must be unraveled, no matter how painful. This tattoo of braided tongues provides a +1 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** [[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]] fills your tongue with venom, causing your deceptions to poison your foes. You use [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]], [[Actions/Feint]], or [[Actions/Lie]]. Choose one target against whom your Deception check succeeded; that target takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]].
