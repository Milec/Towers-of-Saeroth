---

Though these preserved vegetables aren't actual moonflowers, they're made using a moonflower-distilled vinegar. When consumed, tendrils sprout from your veins and curl around you, granting you a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Void|void]] effects for 10 minutes and reducing your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition by up to 2. Eating more than one preserved moonflower dish in a day doesn't grant further benefits and makes you [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1.
