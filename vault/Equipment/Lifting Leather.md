This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ has a metal exoskeleton that runs along your back and limbs and grants you increased strength for lifting and carrying. While wearing this armor, you can carry 2 more Bulk than normal before becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]] and up to a maximum of 4 more Bulk.

**Activate—Heavy Lift** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For 10 minutes, your physical strength is temporarily bolstered, granting you a +1 status bonus to Athletics checks to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]].
