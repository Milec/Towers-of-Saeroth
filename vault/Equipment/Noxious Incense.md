# Noxious Incense  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 169

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Sold only in single sticks, this foul incense comes coated with a bevy of alchemical smoke that activates in the presence of intense heat, releasing its namesake pungent odor. Adding this catalyst to a [[Spells/Wall of Fire|_wall of fire_]] spell fills all squares adjacent to the wall with thick, foul smoke. Creatures in this area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and other creatures are concealed to creatures in the area. The smoke lasts for the duration of the spell.

## Noxious Incense  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 169

**Price** 125 gp

**Hands** 1

**Bulk** L

---

The incense can be used with a _wall of fire_ of 6th level or below.
