---

Among Hao Jin's most precious treasures are three living plants, the last surviving celestial peach trees taken from the mountains of Chu Ye. One of the trees grows pearls in place of flowers, but the other two bear fruit that is far more valuable. Eating one of these small red peaches can heal even the most grievous of injuries.

## Celestial Peach (Rejuvenation)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 48

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Bulk** L

---

You gain the effects of 7th-level [[Spells/Neutralize Poison|_neutralize poison_]], [[Spells/Regenerate|_regenerate_]], [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]], and [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]] spells. The peach has a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] modifier of +27.

## Celestial Peach (Life)  *Item 20*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 48

**Price** 73,000 gp

**Bulk** L

---

When you place the peach into the mouth of an intact corpse that died within the last year, it casts a 10th-level [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]] on the corpse.
