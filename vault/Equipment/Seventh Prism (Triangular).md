# Seventh Prism  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 75

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

two actions

Cast a Spell

---

Beloved by the church of the [[Setting/Deities/Sivanah (Divine Mysteries)|Seventh Veil]], a _seventh prism_ is a crystal that disperses its internal light, casting an aurora of color. If you use a seventh prism to cast [[Spells/Prismatic Spray|_color spray_]], targets are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for twice as long as their saving throw indicates. On a critical failure, the target is dazzled for 1 minute after its [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] condition ends. Motes of shifting rainbow hues cloud the eyes, making it difficult to see details.
