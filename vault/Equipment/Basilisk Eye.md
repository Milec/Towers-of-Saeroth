---

This slimy green stone glows with a strong light whenever the shield it adorns blocks a melee attack. When the eye is activated, the attacker must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 minute as its body slowly stiffens in partial petrification.
