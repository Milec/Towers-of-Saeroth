# Tentacle Cannon  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 156

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

A _tentacle cannon_ is a _+1 striking weapon_, built using components from [[Bestiary/Families/Squid|squids]], [[Bestiary/Kraken (Monster Core)|krakens]], and sometimes even stranger tentacled creatures like [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]]. It’s a distinct type of martial firearm that deals 1d8 piercing damage. It has the [[Traits/Capacity|capacity 5]], [[Traits/Concussive (NPC Core)|concussive]], and [[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal d12]] traits, a range increment of 30 feet, and [[Rules/Reload (Player Core)|reload]] 2. The weapon itself resembles a five-barreled handheld cannon with each barrel made from a hollowed out tentacle.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]])    **Effect** You cause one of the tentacles forming the weapon’s five barrels to stretch out and attempt to pull and grab a creature within 15 feet. The tentacle attempts to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] with a +13 bonus. It can attempt to Grapple any creature, regardless of size. On a success, the tentacle pulls the creature up to 10 feet directly towards you, until it’s in a square adjacent to you.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** The _tentacle cannon_ has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]; **Effect** You continue to keep the _tentacle cannon’s_ hold on one creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]. Attempt another check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature with a +13 bonus.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The cannon fires a spray of ink in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Creatures in the area must attempt a DC 23 Reflex save. On a failure, the creature is covered in ink and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute or until it removes the ink. On a critical failure, the creature becomes blinded and dazzled; both conditions last for 1 minute or until it removes the ink. The creature, or an adjacent creature, can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to remove the ink from its eyes to remove the blinded and dazzled conditions.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the tentacles and ink glands of a creature with a tentacle Strike and ink.
