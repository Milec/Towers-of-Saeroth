# Flaying Knife  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Slithering

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 60

**Price** 480 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

This long, thin _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ has a smooth edge on one side and a serrated edge on the other. Its [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] comes from slicing away long strips of the target's flesh, and it deals 1d8 persistent bleed damage on a hit instead of 1d6. A creature takes a –2 status penalty to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|diseases]] for as long as it has persistent bleed damage from this knife.
