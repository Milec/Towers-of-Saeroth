# Dawnlight  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This shining symbol of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae]] depicts the goddess with her arms held wide. In the center of the symbol is a crystal reliquary with a perfect feather floating inside, glowing bright as a candle with the light of the goddess.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day for each spell;   **Effect** The dawnlight casts 1st-level [[Spells/Disrupt Undead|_disrupt undead_]] or [[Spells/Light|_light_]]. The _dawnlight's_ spell attack roll and [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] modifier are +7, and any spell with the [[Traits/Light|light]] trait is treated as though its counteract level were 1 higher for counteracting [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]].

**Craft Requirements** You have a spellcasting class feature with the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] tradition.

## Dawnlight (Greater)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Price** 320 gp

**Bulk** L

---

Whenever you successfully counteract magical darkness while holding this symbol, the clash of magic unleashes a momentary flash of light. Creatures with [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] in the area of the _light_ spell or targeted by it must succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round. Those with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Light Blindness|light blindness]] are also exposed to [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]].

 Change the list of spells to 3rd-level versions of _disrupt undead_, _light_, and [[Spells/Searing Light|_searing light_]]. The spell attack roll and counteract modifier are +13.

## Dawnlight (Major)  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Bulk** L

---

A _major dawnlight_ creates a flash of light like a _greater dawnlight_, but the DC is 28. Change the list of spells to  5th-level versions of _disrupt undead_ and _light_, and 4th-level versions of _searing light_ and [[Spells/Vital Beacon|_vital beacon_]]. The spell attack roll and counteract modifier are +18.
