**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|Hide Armor]]

Made of the sturdy, thick hide of the [[Bestiary/Aurochs|aurochs]] and tempered to be both flexible and durable, this _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|hide armor]]_ is imbued with the aurochs’ natural defenses against venomous predators. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to poison damage.

**Activate** single action Strengthen Resolve;     **Effect** You pull the layers of the armor taut, stiffening your body against incoming forces for 1 minute. While the armor is taut, you have a –1 penalty to Reflex saves and a +2 item bonus to Fortitude saves.
