# Hat of Disguise  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 611

**Usage** worn headwear

---

This ordinary-looking hat allows you to cloak yourself in illusions.

**Activate**  1 minute (Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The hat casts a 1st-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]] spell on you. While setting up the disguise, you can magically alter the hat to appear as a comb, ribbon, helm, or other piece of headwear.

## Hat of Disguise  *Item 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 611

**Price** 30 gp

## Hat of Disguise (Greater)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 611

**Price** 340 gp

---

You can activate the hat as a 2-action activity, you can activate it any number of times per day, and the _illusory disguise_ is 2nd level.
