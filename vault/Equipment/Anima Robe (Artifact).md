**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -212- A Voice in the Blight|Pathfinder #212: A Voice in the Blight]] pg. 72

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

---

**Perception** +33; precise [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and hearing within 30 feet

**Communication** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]] (partner only)

**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] +35, [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] +34, [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] +35

**Int** +6, **Wis** +5, **Cha** +7

**Will** +33

---  

The artistic echo of the Ekujae hero Iyalirrin animates the _Anima Robe’s_ stitching to display embroidered reactions to situations experienced by the wearer. The _Anima Robe’s_ telepathic voice is filled with confidence (almost to the point of cockiness) and is supportive, often encouraging his partner to take risky or showy actions yet never to the extent that would put them in significant harm’s way.

The _Anima Robe_ grants a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks made to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] and to all [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks, and you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 15 to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage.

**Activate—Billowing Distraction** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The _Anima Robe_ billows as if in a blustery wind and attempts to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] a creature you’re adjacent to.

**Activate—Who Am I?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The robe’s hood rises up over your head, casting a 4th-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] to your specifications.

**Activate—Who Are We?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The robe’s hem swishes, casting a 7th-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] to your specifications.

**Activate—Who Are They?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** An image of a creature shows up in the robe’s stitching, then appears to come to life; the robe casts an 8th-rank [[Spells/Illusory Creature (Player Core)|_illusory creature_]] to your specifications. The robe can use an action to [[Actions/Sustain]] this activation for up to 1 minute.
