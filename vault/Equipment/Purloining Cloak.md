---  

Each _purloining cloak_ is a mercurial mantle once worn by a legendary thief or two. The cloak became infused with a daredevil spirit and a penchant for high-stakes thievery. If its owner isn’t willing to partake in such activities, the cloak tries to convince them to sell or give it to someone who will. A _purloining cloak_ can use tendrils of its cloth to attempt [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Palm an Object (Player Core)|Palm an Object]] or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]], proudly offering you its loot later. In addition to those of a [[Equipment/Mercurial Mantle|_mercurial mantle_]], a _purloining cloak_ has the following activations.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;     **Effect** The cloak casts 2nd-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] on you, usually to your specifications. However the cloak can choose the appearance of the illusion for you.

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** The cloak assesses the price of valuables it can see. This valuation doesn’t include the value of features the cloak can’t discern, such as magical properties.
