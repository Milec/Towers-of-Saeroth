single action

Interact

**Requirement** You're an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]].

---

The Golden Gunners are dreaded for their use of their _golden silencers_, which they put to good use in clandestine raids. They silence both the loud gunshot and the screams of creatures wounded by its shot. A weapon with a _golden silencer_ attached emits no light and makes no noise when fired. A target hit by a ranged Strike from the affixed weapon must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or be silenced as well until the start of its next turn. A silenced creature can't call for help or use sonic abilities, nor can it use abilities with the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait. This prevents it from casting spells that include verbal components.
