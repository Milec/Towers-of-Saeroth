# Energizing Vial  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 235

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This sealed glass vial has no stopper and is filled with pure white light. The spell attack modifier of any spell cast by Activating this item is +13.  
**• Armor** After you cast an [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] spell by Activating the _energizing vial_, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] as a free action once per round until the end of your next turn.  
**• Weapon** After you cast an electricity spell by Activating the _energizing vial_, your Strikes with the weapon deal 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent electricity damage]] until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Live Wire|_live wire_]]._
