three actions

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This midnight-blue gel shimmers with rainbow hues when exposed to light. When you apply this gel on an unattended and non-magical object of 1 Bulk, the object grows iridescent insect wings and transforms into a flash beetle, keeping some of the same colors and patterns of the original item. This false beetle has the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait. It remains in this beetle form for 10 minutes before the wings fall off and it returns to normal. If the beetle is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the item returns to its original form, undamaged.
