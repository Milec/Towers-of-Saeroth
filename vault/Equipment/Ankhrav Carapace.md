This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ has interlocking panels and short bristles along the outer edges of the arms and legs. While wearing this armor, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 2 to acid damage.

**Activate—Dig** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The bristles along your limbs enable you to tunnel through dirt and sand. For 1 minute, you gain a [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]] equal to half your land Speed.
