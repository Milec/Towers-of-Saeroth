# Reaper's Crescent  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 35

**Price** 575 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|Sickle]]

---

The blade of this alabaster _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickle]]_ grows thick and pitted as the moon waxes, and it thins to a sharp sliver as the moon wanes. Moonlight also causes a second rune on the blade to change shape with the moon’s phases: during the new and crescent moon, the weapon has the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] rune; during the quarter moon, the [[Equipment/Fearsome (GM Core)|_fearsome_]] rune; and during the gibbous and full moon, the [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|_wounding_]] rune.

When wielded under moonlight of any strength, the reaper’s crescent deals additional cold damage equal to the number of damage dice.
