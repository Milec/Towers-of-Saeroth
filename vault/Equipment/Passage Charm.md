# Passage Charm  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 81

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This glossy black brooch is made of solidified shadows and is a gift, given by Avathrael Realmshaper to their allies to allow them to navigate the heart of the forest within Gloaming Arbor. While wearing a passage charm, you can cast [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate]] [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell twice per day.

**Activate—Shape the Shadows** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re in an area that’s [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|dark]] or [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]]; **Effect** You command the shadows to do your bidding, forming a path, a ramp, a wall, or stairs. The _passage charm_ casts 5th-rank [[Spells/Wall of Stone (Player Core)|_wall of stone_]], except the spell loses the [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]] trait, gains the [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] trait, and has a duration of 24 hours, and the wall is created from solid shadows, rather than stone. If any section of the wall is exposed to [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]], that portion of the wall has its [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] temporarily reduced by half (to Hardness 7).
