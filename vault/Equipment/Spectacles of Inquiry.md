# Spectacles of Inquiry  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 147

**Price** 1,750 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

Anything viewed through these thin spectacles looks crisp and clear, and the earpieces accentuate sounds around you. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The spectacles key in on someone to show you their social cues in perfect clarity. Choose a creature you can see. You gain a +3 item bonus on [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks you make to use [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] against that creature. This benefit lasts until you Activate the item again in this way.

If you’re an [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|investigator]], you can use this activation as part of the action you take to [[Actions/Devise a Stratagem (Player Core 2)|Devise a Stratagem]] or [[Actions/Pursue a Lead (Player Core 2)|Pursue a Lead]], and you can do the latter even though you can’t see the creature. You must choose the same creature you chose for Devise a Stratagem or Pursue a Lead.

**Craft Requirements** You are an [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-897)|investigator]].
