# Yarrow-Root Bandage  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Travel Guide]] pg. 101

**Price** 12 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Administer First Aid]]

---

This root is often infused into bandages for life-threatening bleeding. Activating the item is part of the same activity you use to Administer First Aid to stop [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|bleeding]] on a creature who's also [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]]. When you do, you can Administer First Aid to stop bleeding and dying as part of the same activity with only one [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check. However, the DC for the Medicine check is equal to the 5 + the higher of the two DCs to Administer First Aid.
