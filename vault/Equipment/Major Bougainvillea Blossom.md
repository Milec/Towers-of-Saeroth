**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

This pink flower has long, slender thorns along the stem. The flower can be used as a [[Rules/Spell Catalysts|catalyst]] when casting an [[Spells/Entangling Flora|_entangling flora_]] spell, which causes the affected plants to sprout long thorns and vibrant pink blossoms. The area becomes [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (Player Core)|hazardous terrain]], dealing the listed piercing damage to an enemy each time it enters an affected square.
