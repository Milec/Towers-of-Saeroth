# Forgetful Drops  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Ingested]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 82

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Access** Member of a secret society

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

These innocuous, colorless drops can be poured directly into a victim's mouth, or slipped into their food or drink. They initially haze a victim's mind slightly, making them easier to fool; in later stages, they can lead to the victim entering a murderous confused state. Secret societies use these drops to befuddle a target or to frame them for violence.

 **Saving Throw** DC 18 Fortitude; **Onset** 5 minutes; **Maximum Duration** 1 hour; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (10 minutes); **Stage 2** stupefied 1 and clumsy 1 (20 minutes); **Stage 3** stupefied 1, [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (30 minutes)
