This drink unlocks a flood of emotions that helps reset your mental state. When you drink this elixir, the elixir attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] each effect on you that has the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] trait or is inflicting the [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition on you, using the listed counteract rank and modifier.
