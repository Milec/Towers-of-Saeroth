# Sightless Tincture  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Ingested]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 70

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The clause of sightless tincture which states that "the blinded condition from this poison lasts for an additional 24 hours once the poison has run its course" only applies if the victim reaches Stage 3 and is blinded._

**Price** 115 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

With an incredibly deep black hue, sightless tincture is usually concealed in wine, blood pudding, or other darkly colored foods. The oil at first faintly dims its victim’s sight, then blacks it out entirely.

**Saving Throw** DC 28 Fortitude; **Onset** 10 minutes; **Maximum Duration** 14 hours; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] and a –2 status penalty to visual [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks (1 hour); **Stage 2** dazzled and a –4 status penalty to visual Perception checks (1 hour); **Stage 3** blinded (2d6 hours); stage 3 is an [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect, and if the victim is still blinded from the poison when the poison has run its course, the blindness lasts for an additional 24 hours afterward
