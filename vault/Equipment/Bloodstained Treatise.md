# Bloodstained Treatise  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 228

**Price** 325 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The white leather cover of this book is splotched with rusty red bloodstains, and its pages seep red when turned, although this never stains the bearer’s hands or clothing. Anatomical drawings illustrate its pages.

**Activate—Sanguine Expulsion** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a prepared Spell]] that manipulates blood, such as _[[Spells/Blood Vendetta (Player Core)|_blood vendetta_]]_ or _[[Spells/Vampiric Exsanguination (Player Core)|_vampiric exsanguination_]]_, or a spell dealing piercing or slashing damage, all targets of the triggering spell take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]; if the spell already causes bleed damage, the bleed damage increases by 1d6. The wounds of one affected target (chosen by you) gush uncontrollably, covering the ground with blood in a 5-foot emanation that lasts for 1 round. Creatures that begin their turn in the blood must succeed at a Reflex save against your spell DC or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
