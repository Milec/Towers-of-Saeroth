# Bottled Roc  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 56

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

This bottle contains a shrunken bird preserved with its feathers intact. When opened, the contents reconstitute into a Gargantuan effigy of a great [[Bestiary/Roc (Monster Core)|roc]], which can appear in the air instead of on the ground. The roc [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grabs]] up to two creatures, then [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] up to 90 feet and [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Releases]] the creatures. The creatures must be within 15 feet of the roc for it to Grab them; if any of them are unwilling to be grabbed, the roc must Grapple them with a +17 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier or fail to pick them up.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Roc (Monster Core)|roc]].
