# Hippogriff in a Jar  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 56

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This bottle contains a shrunken [[Bestiary/Hippogriff|hippogriff]]. When opened, the contents reconstitute into a Large effigy of a hippogriff. The hippogriff waits up to 1 round and allows two creatures to mount it, then Flies up to 65 feet and waits 1 more round to give the mounted creatures time to dismount. Creatures who are still mounted on the hippogriff when it dissolves fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] in the space where the hippogriff corpse ends its movement.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a hippogriff.
