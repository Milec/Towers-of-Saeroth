Attacks with a _quickstrike_ weapon are supernaturally swift. While wielding a _quickstrike_ weapon, you gain the [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] condition, but you can use the additional action granted only to make a Strike with the etched weapon.
