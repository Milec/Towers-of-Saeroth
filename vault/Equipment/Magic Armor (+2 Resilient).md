# Magic Armor  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 229

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk**  varies by armor

---

A suit of magic armor is simply a suit of armor or explorer’s clothing etched with fundamental runes. An [[Equipment/Armor Potency|armor potency]] rune increases the armor’s item bonus to AC, and a [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] rune adds an item bonus to saving throws.

 The Prices here are for all types of armor. You don’t need to adjust the Price from [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]] to [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate|full plate]] or the like. These armors are made of standard materials, not precious materials such as dawnsilver.
