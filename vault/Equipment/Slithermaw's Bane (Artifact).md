**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] +35, [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] +33, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] +33

The heroism of Kyloss Syndar, founder of Greengold and slayer of the demonic hydra Slithermaw, imprinted this suit of armor with a gleaming, prismatic sheen that sparkles under bright light. The telepathic voice of _Slithermaw’s Bane_ is boisterous and grandiose, encouraging his partner to take up leadership roles while also providing advice to maintain loyalty and strengthen bonds of friendship.

_Slithermaw’s Bane_ is a suit of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+3 major resilient]] [[Equipment/Fortification (GM Core)|greater fortification]] high-grade [[Equipment/Elven Chain (GM Core)|elven chain]]_ that grants his wearer poison [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 15, and the item bonus his resilient rune grants to saving throws versus poison increases by 1 to +4.

**Activate—Calistria’s Sting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapples]] you;  **Effect** Poison wells up from the armor’s links to seep into the triggering creature’s body, causing it to suffer wracking pains as if it was being stung by thousands of angry wasps. The triggering creature takes 7d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save); this persistent damage can’t be ended as long as the triggering creature continues to grapple you. _Slithermaw’s Bane_ can’t activate [[Setting/Deities/Calistria (Divine Mysteries)|Calistria’s]] Sting using his actions.

**Activate—Purge Toxins** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** _Slithermaw’s Bane_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|affliction]] affecting you, with a counteract rank of 9th and a +25 modifier to the roll (+30 modifier if the source of the disease or poison effect on you was created by a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]]).

**Activate—Adaptation** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You alter the exterior of the armor to better adapt to the surrounding [[Rules/Terrain (Player Core)|terrain]]: [[Rules/Aquatic (GM Core)|aquatic]], [[Rules/Arctic (GM Core)|arctic]], [[Rules/Desert (GM Core)|desert]], [[Rules/Forest (GM Core)|forest]], [[Rules/Mountain (GM Core)|mountain]], [[Rules/Plains (GM Core)|plains]], sky, [[Rules/Swamp (GM Core)|swamp]], or [[Rules/Underground (GM Core)|underground]]. You [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore difficult terrain]] within the chosen [[Rules/Environment|environment]] and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Environmental Hazards|environmental hazards]], [[Rules/Natural Disasters (GM Core)|natural disasters]], and extreme temperatures that originate from that terrain. You’re also protected from [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|severe and extreme heat or severe and extreme cold]] (your choice when you activate this ability). This effect lasts until your next daily preparations or the next time you activate it, whichever comes first.
