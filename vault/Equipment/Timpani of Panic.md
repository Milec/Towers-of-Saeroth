# Timpani of Panic  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 147

**Price** 650 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This fine copper kettledrum has a dark skin stretched over it, and the tension rods are stained a dark red. This drum grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Sustain Dread** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You beat a march on the timpani that continuously increases in tempo. Enemies within a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 26 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The target can’t reduce its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] value below 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target can’t reduce its frightened value below 1 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and it becomes frightened 1.
