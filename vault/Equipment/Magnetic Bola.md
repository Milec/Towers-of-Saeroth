# Magnetic Bola  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 213

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Bola](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=433)

---

This heavy metallic _+1 [bola](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=433)_ is a favorite tool of rebels and rabble-rousers against armored opponents. Due to its weight, it has a range increment of 15 feet. When you successfully Strike a creature made of metal or wearing metal armor with this weapon, the bola attaches to the metal and the targeted creature takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speed until it spends an Interact action to remove the bola.

**Activate—Overcharge** single action ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You overcharge the magnetic properties of the bola. You deal an extra 1d6 electricity damage with your next Strike with this weapon. On a hit, the target must also succeed at a DC 18 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 round (or clumsy 2 on a critical failure), in addition to the normal effects. If the target is wearing metal armor, they take a –2 circumstance penalty to their save.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a conductive metal.
