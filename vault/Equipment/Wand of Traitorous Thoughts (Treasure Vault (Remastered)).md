# Wand of Traitorous Thoughts  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 143

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

When you hold this sleek, shiny gray wand, you hear a faint chorus of overlapping whispers.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Mind Probe|_mind probe_]]. The first time the target rolls a success at a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to mislead your probe, it gets a result one step worse than it rolled. This means you learn the answer if the target's Deception check would have succeeded, and you learn nothing rather than believing a falsehood if the check would have been a critical success.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _mind probe_.
