# Wand of Noisome Acid  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 141, [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 61

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This greasy stick emits a stomach-churning scent when held in hand.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Acid Grip|_acid grip_]] of the indicated rank. A creature that takes initial acid damage from this spell become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. Use your spell DC if the creature attempts to recover from this sickness. This is an [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] effect.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _acid grip_ of the appropriate rank.
