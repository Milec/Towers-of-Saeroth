# Ring of Spell Turning  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 615

**Price** 67,000 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This golden ring has three diamonds set into its face.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;   **Trigger** You are targeted by a spell;  **Effect** The ring replicates the effects of an 8th-level [[Spells/Spell Turning|_spell turning_]] with a counteract modifier of +35, possibly causing the triggering spell to reflect back on its caster. The ring can reflect no more than 9 total levels of spells per day. If you activate the ring to reflect a spell that would exceed this limit, the attempt fails, but the attempted usage of the ring does not count toward the daily limit.
