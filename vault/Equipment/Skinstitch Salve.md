# Skinstitch Salve  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 255

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This sticky salve stubbornly holds wounds closed and encourages swift natural healing. You can activate the salve in either of the following ways.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] without requiring [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]]. You either gain a +2 item bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check, or you can use the skinstitch salve's Medicine modifier of +13 instead of your own.

**Activate** free action Interact;   **Trigger** You [[Actions/Treat Wounds]] or use [[Feats/Battle Medicine]];  **Effect** You gain a +2 item bonus to the triggering [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check. If you roll a success on the Medicine check, you get a critical success instead.
