# Spike Snare  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 300

**Price** 3 gp

---

This basic snare consists of hidden spikes that rely on a creature's momentum to lacerate or potentially impale it as it enters the snare's square, dealing 2d8 piercing damage. The creature must attempt a DC 17 [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2765) Reflex saving throw.
