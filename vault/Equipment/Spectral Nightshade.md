Belladonna cultivated in planes hazardous to living visitors, such as the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]] or the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]], grow with a strange, skeletal look to their branches. The leaves and berries of these extraplanar plants are partly incorporeal and significantly more toxic than [[Equipment/Belladonna|belladonna]] grown on the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]]. When imbued with spirit-twisting magic, _spectral nightshade_ quickly drains away the victim's vitality and makes colors painfully bright and bewildering. _Spectral nightshade_ doesn't affect creatures that have no spirit; if a possessing spirit inhabits a body that takes poison damage from spectral nightshade, the possessor takes an equal amount of force damage, even if the possessor can't be affected directly by poisons.

 **Saving Throw** DC 33 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Maximum Duration** 6 minutes; **Stage 1** 10d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] (1 minute); **Stage 2** 13d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 minute); **Stage 3** 15d6 poison damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], and drained 2 (1 minute)

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[Spells/Spirit Blast|_spirit blast_]].
