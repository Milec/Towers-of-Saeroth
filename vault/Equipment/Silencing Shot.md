# Silencing Shot  *Item 11*

**Price** 300 gp

**Ammunition** any

envision

---

This shimmering, golden-hued ammunition never makes any sound. A creature hit by a _silencing shot_ is subject to the effects of a 4th-level [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] spell (DC 25).

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _silence_ at 4th level.
