# Messenger's Ring  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 613

**Usage** worn

---

This silver signet ring changes to match the insignia of a lord or organization you serve (or your own face, if you serve no one else). It grants you a +2 item bonus to Diplomacy checks and lets you cast [[Spells/Message|_message_]] as an arcane innate spell at will.

**Activate**  1 minute (envision);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The ring casts [[Spells/Animal Messenger|_animal messenger_]] to your specification. The animal is a magical creature that springs from the ring, and its appearance suits the iconography or heraldry of the lord or organization represented by the ring.
