# Anima Robe  *Item 15*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -211- The Secret of Deathstalk Tower|Pathfinder #211: The Secret of Deathstalk Tower]] pg. 81

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

---

This robe was the favored garment of the legendary Ekujae hero, Iyalirrin. When the draconic god [[Setting/Deities/Dahak]] threatened destruction, Iyalirrin was the primary architect of a powerful ritual to banish him called the anima invocation. Iyalirrin and many others were forced to sacrifice themselves to empower this ritual, but he ensured his _Anima Robe_ remained in good hands before he did so. Since then, the Anima Robe has been worn by dozens of elven occultists and [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bards]] who have traveled Golarion and beyond. The _Anima Robe_ has remained in the care of Queen Telandia for only the past few dozen years.

While wearing the Anima Robe, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks made to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] and to all [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. You also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to to mental damage.

**Activate—Who Am I?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]])    **Effect** With a toss of the robe’s hood, you cast a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] on yourself.

**Activate—Who Are We?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a whirl of the robe’s hem, you cast a 7th-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]].

**Activate—Who Are They?** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a swish of the robe’s sleeve, you cast a 7th-rank [[Spells/Illusory Creature (Player Core)|_illusory creature_]].
