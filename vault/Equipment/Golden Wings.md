# Golden Wings  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** 2

**Access** You're from Hermea

---

This pair of huge wings is crafted from numerous feathers made of a lightweight, golden magical alloy. When activated, the wings emit a melodic sound and a shimmering glow.

**Activate—Assisted Flight** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For the next 10 minutes, you can [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] 20 feet, using the wings to propel yourself. You must land on a space with solid ground. For the duration, you also emit a [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet. You take a –2 item penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]].

## Greater Golden Wings  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Price** 350 gp

---

When you activate the wings, for the duration, you can also use an action to flap the wings to glide slowly toward the ground, 5 feet down and up to 20 feet forward through the air. As long as you spend 1 action gliding each round and haven’t yet reached the ground, you remain in the air at the end of your turn.

## Major Golden Wings  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Price** 650 gp

---

When you activate the wings, you also gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 20 feet for the duration.

## True Golden Wings  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Price** 14,000 gp

---

When you activate the wings, you also gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed or 25 feet, whichever is greater, for the duration.
