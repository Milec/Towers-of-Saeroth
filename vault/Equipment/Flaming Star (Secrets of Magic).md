# Flaming Star  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 170

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

A sheen of red crosses the surface of this star-shaped goldstone medallion when the light hits it. The affixed armor or weapon is warm to the touch. The spell attack roll of any spell cast by Activating this item is +7, and the spell DC is 17.

- **Armor** You gain resistance 2 to fire.
- **Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Fire|fire]] spell by Activating the _star_, your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 fire damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Produce Flame|_produce flame_]].
