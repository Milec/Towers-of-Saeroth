# Polarizing Mace  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 34

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Mace]]

---

Even grasping the amber handle of this +1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Shock|shock]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Mace|light mace]] makes your hair stand on end.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The wielder of a _[[Equipment/Grounding Spike (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|grounding spike]]_ is within 30 feet; **Effect** A great surge of electricity connects your paired weapons. All creatures in a line between you and the wielder of the _grounding spike_ take 4d12 electricity damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Special** The _polarizing mace_ pairs with the _grounding spike_.
