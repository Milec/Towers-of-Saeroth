# Mindlance  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 39

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Arquebus (NPC Core)|Arquebus]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Arquebus (NPC Core)|arquebus]]_ is used by caravan guards to nonlethally—though powerfully—deter large game and bandits. When fired, the spark gun deals mental damage and adds the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait to the attack. Each _mindlance_ also includes a [[Equipment/Weapons/Reinforced Stock (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|reinforced stock]] that benefits from any fundamental runes on the firearm. When you critically succeed at an attack roll with a _mindlance_, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 unless it succeeds at a DC 24 Will save.
