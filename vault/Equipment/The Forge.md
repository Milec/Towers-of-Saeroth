# The Forge  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 74

**Usage** carried

---

_The Forge_ denotes strength through diversity. As long as you have _The Forge_ invested, you can carry more than normal— increase your maximum and encumbered Bulk limits by 2.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pass the card over an adjacent [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]] (but not destroyed) object ([[Traits/Magical|magical]] or otherwise) of up to 4 Bulk, and restore the object to its full Hit Point total, removing the broken condition in the process.
