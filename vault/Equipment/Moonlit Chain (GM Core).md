# Moonlit Chain  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 231

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|Chain Shirt]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Silver|silver]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ has a collar adorned with stitched images of the phases of the moon. You can see in moonlight as though you had low-light vision.

**Activate—Dim Sight** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You touch the stitched image of the new moon on the armor's collar and suppress the dazzled condition for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 33 gp of silver.
