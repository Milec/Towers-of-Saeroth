# Wormhole Shot  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 213

**Price** 7 gp

**Ammunition** arrow, bolt

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

The shaft of a _wormhole shot_ gleams like polished ebony and seems to be not quite straight; nevertheless, this ammunition flies true. When an activated _wormhole shot_ hits a target, which can be an unoccupied square, it embeds itself and remains active for up to 1 minute. A creature can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to dislodge the ammunition, but it remains active unless the creature spends another Interact action to destroy it. Once during the _wormhole shot_’s duration, if you’re within 60 feet of it, you can use it as the point of origin for a spell or ranged attack, calculating range and cover from whatever space the _wormhole shot_ is in. After being used this way, it quickly dissolves into black dust.
