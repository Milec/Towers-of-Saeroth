# Kinetic Club  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 77

**Price** 325 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub (Player Core)|Greatclub]]

---

Embedded along the length of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub (Player Core)|greatclub]]_ are the teeth and scales of a [[Bestiary/Zetogeki|zetogeki]], a giant lizard that can absorb and then release bursts of kinetic energy. The more momentum a wielder builds up while swinging the weapon, the more forceful the impact when it finally makes contact.

**Activate—Store Kinetic Energy** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You add a circumstance bonus to your attack with the _kinetic club’s_ [[Traits/Backswing (Player Core)|backswing]] trait and successfully [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] your target;  **Effect** You channel the club’s stored energy into the Strike, dealing 1d6 additional damage. If the club has a [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|_greater striking_]] rune, increase the additional damage to 2d6. If the club has a _major striking_ rune, increase the additional damage to 3d6.
