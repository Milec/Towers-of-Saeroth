# Ethereal  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 582

**Price** 13,500 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

An _ethereal rune_ replicates armor on the Ethereal Plane.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain the effects of an [[Spells/Ethereal Jaunt|_ethereal jaunt_]] spell. This doesn’t require concentration and lasts for 10 minutes or until you choose to return to material form as a free action.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of 9th-level _ethereal jaunt_.
