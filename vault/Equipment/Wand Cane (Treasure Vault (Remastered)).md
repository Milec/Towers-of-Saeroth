# Wand Cane  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 105

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

Though it appears to be a basic cane, the inner workings of the _wand cane_ are an intricate network of lenses and magical circuits, with a slot at the top to insert a [[Traits/Wand|wand]]. The _wand cane_ then spends 1 minute attuning to the wand, after which the wand can be used through the cane.
