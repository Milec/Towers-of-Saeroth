# Spider Gun  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 155

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

A _spider gun_ is a _+1 striking weapon_. It’s a distinct type of martial firearm made from the fangs and spinneret of a Large spider. It deals 1d10 poison damage with a range increment of 30 feet and [[Traits/Reload|reload]] 1. On a critical hit, the venom clings to the target and they take [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] equal to 1d4 + the number of weapon damage dice. A _spider gun_ does not add critical specialization effects. The gun can be activated to fire webbing that hampers other creatures.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You fire a mass of webbing at a square within 30 feet. That square becomes covered in webbing for 1 minute. A square covered with the webbing from a _spider gun_ can be cleared by a single attack or effect that deals at least 5 slashing damage or 1 fire damage. A square has AC 5, and it automatically fails its saving throws. The first time each turn a creature in the webbing begins to use a move action or enters the webbing during a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, it must attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check or Reflex save against DC 20. On a success, it moves normally through the webbing and clears away the webbing from any squares it enters this turn. On a failure, it treats squares of webbing as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] this turn, and on a critical failure, it’s [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 20) or destroys the webbing.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials for the _spider gun_ must include the fangs and spinneret of a giant spider or other Large spider.
