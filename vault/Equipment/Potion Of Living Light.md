# Potion Of Living Light  *Item 10*

single action

---

This glass vial has a dusky purple liquid with a small orb of light suspended in it. It sheds [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 5-foot radius. Upon drinking the potion, your skin sheds an aura of bright light in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for another 20 feet), even through your clothing and armor, for 1 hour. While glowing, you gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] and [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] effects. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect, causing a flash of light that causes every creature in a 20-foot emanation to be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round unless they succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save.
