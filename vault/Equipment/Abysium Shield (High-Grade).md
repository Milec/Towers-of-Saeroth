# Abysium Shield  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 96

**Usage** varies by shield

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Abysium]]

---

These glowing shields are crafted to ensure that the abysium used is safely contained within a durable outer shell. If the shield breaks, however, the toxic metal becomes exposed. When you use the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction against an adjacent creature's melee Strike, if the shield breaks, the creature is exposed to the abysium and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] for a standard-grade shield or sickened 2 for a high-grade shield.
