# Name Pendant  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 152

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** worn

---

Many soldiers wear this metal pendant engraved with their name and critical details. Sadly, they also ascertain the identity of fallen soldiers. Many soldiers find that the pendant helps them stay grounded. When you wear your name pendant, you gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against spells and [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effects with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait.

**Activate—Alert Superior Officer** free action ;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition;  **Effect** The pendent alerts all other allies within 500 feet who are also wearing a _name pendant_.
