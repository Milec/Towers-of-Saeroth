# Amulet of the Hellcat  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 106

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This glass amulet is set on a steel backing shaped like a cage and flickers with an eerie crimson glow. Magically sealed within is a portion of a hellcat's ever-burning heart. While wearing the amulet, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks made while in [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]], and you can cast the [[Spells/Light (Player Core)|_light_]] cantrip emanating from the amulet as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate]] divine spell.

**Activate—Invisible Pounce** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are in bright light; **Effect** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your speed, and then make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] at the end of that movement. After making the Strike, you are no longer invisible. If at any point during your Stride, you pass out of [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]], you are no longer invisible and must stop moving. You may make a melee Strike if you have a target within reach.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the heart of a [[Bestiary/Hellcat|hellcat]].
