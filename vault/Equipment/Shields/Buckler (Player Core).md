# Buckler  *Shield 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 274

**Price** 1 gp

**AC Bonus** 1

**Speed Penalty** —

**Bulk** L

**Hardness** 3

**HP (BT)** 6 (3)

---

This very small shield is a favorite of duelists and quick, lightly armored warriors. It's typically made of steel and strapped to your forearm. You can [[Actions/Raise a Shield]] with your buckler as long as you have that hand free or are holding a light object that's not a weapon in that hand.
