# Book of Warding Prayers  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 111

**Price** 425 gp

**Bulk** L

---

Script in a language of the Outer Planes adorns this book’s spine, and a deity’s symbol is etched on its cover. _Books of warding prayers_ are each devoted to a particular deity and are also a religious symbol of that deity.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your last action was to cast a [[Rules/Prepared Spells (Player Core)|prepared]] [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] spell granted by your deity;  **Effect** Choose [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]], [[Traits/Evil|evil]], [[Traits/Good|good]], or [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]]; you can’t choose a type that’s part of your deity’s alignment. You and all allies within 30 feet are suffused with your deity’s protection, gaining [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to damage of that type for 1 minute.
