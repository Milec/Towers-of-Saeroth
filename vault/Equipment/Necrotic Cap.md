# Necrotic Cap  *Item 4+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 81

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

You can use this slimy, rotting mushroom as a [[Rules/Spell Catalysts|spell catalyst]] when you cast an [[Spells/Acid Grip|_acid grip_]] spell by tapping it against the target, causing the mushroom to release a cloud of necrotic spores. When you do, acid grip loses the [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] trait, gains the [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] trait, and all acid damage the spell deals becomes [[Traits/Void|void]] damage. On a hit, the target additionally gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] conditions, with the listed values, as the spores consume their flesh. As long as the target is taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]], they can’t reduce the value of their sickened condition below 1.
