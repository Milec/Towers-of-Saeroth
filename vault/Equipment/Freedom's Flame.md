# Freedom's Flame  *Item 21*

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 151

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|Morningstar]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+4]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|major striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|morningstar]]_ is made of wood and adorned with golden flames traced along its haft and head. It has the [[Traits/Razing (Player Core 2)|razing]] trait, and it ignores the [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] of structures used to hold those you feel are being unjustly imprisoned (subject to the GM’s discretion).

**Activate—Break Free** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; _Freedom’s Flame_ blazes with a rose-red light, creating a beacon of spiritual strength. Any creature within 120 feet that’s currently under a compulsion or [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|control]] effect can immediately attempt a new saving throw to break free of the effect, with a +3 status bonus.

**Activate—Holy Retribution** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your previous action was to use _Freedom’s Flame_ to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] a foe you have witnessed use a compulsion or [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|control]] effect on an ally within the past hour;  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; your blow is empowered by the righteousness of [[Setting/Deities/Milani]]. The target’s [[Traits/Holy|holy]] [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] is triggered again or, if the target doesn’t have a holy weakness, it takes 15 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage.

**Destruction** _Freedom’s Flame_ is sliced in two if struck by a [[Equipment/Final Blade|final blade]] that has been anointed with [[Equipment/Unholy Water (GM Core)|unholy water]] created by a level 20 [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature.
