# Frost Fair Yanyuedao  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 131

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Glaive]]

---

The Frost Fair Yanyuedao is a [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|greater striking]] yanyuedao (use statistics for [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|glaive]]) once wielded by a legendary military general from Goka. This weapon was constructed from pieces harvested from a dragon’s body. When in an area of [[Equipment/Force Shield (GM Core)|severe cold or colder]], the Frost Fair Yanyuedao becomes a +3 major striking yanyuedao with a glowing blue cutting edge and the following ability.

**Activate—Dragon Chill** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** Until the end of your turn, the Frost Fair Yanyuedao gains the effects of a greater [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|frost]] rune. While under this effect, if you critically succeed at a Strike using this weapon against a creature who has [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] or [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunity]] to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], that creature must attempt a DC 31 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 minute.
