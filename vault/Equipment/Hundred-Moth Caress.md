# Hundred-Moth Caress  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 185

**Price** 475 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe]]

---

The handles of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe|scythe]]_ are made from a dull, gray wood of bone-like consistency, and when you slice with it, a fluttering gust of hundreds of moths' wingbeats fills the air. If you're a devotee of [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]], you can use this scythe as a divine focus, and with every Strike, it exudes a pallid cloud of powdery dust.

**Activate** free action command ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You deal 1d10 negative damage to yourself. If your next action is to Strike with the scythe or to Cast a Spell with the [[Traits/Disease|disease]] or negative trait, that Strike or spell deals additional negative damage equal to the damage you took (after any reductions or increases from immunity, resistances, weaknesses).
