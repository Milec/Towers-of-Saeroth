# Mythic Resilent  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 149

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

Mythic resilient runes imbue armor with unrivaled protection from a wide array of effects. The armor grants a +4 item bonus to saving throws to the wearer.

**Activate—Defy Obliteration** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You critically fail a saving throw;  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; if the triggering save was made due to an effect created by a mythic monster, hazard, or other effect, it’s a normal failure instead. If the save was made due to an effect that wasn’t mythic, it becomes a success.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic resilient runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character capable of making a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).
