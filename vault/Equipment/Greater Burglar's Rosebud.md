# Burglar's Rosebud  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 76

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Though originally intended to protect a [[Setting/Deities/Green Man|green man]]’s favorite agents against predation by pesky herbivores, thieves have adapted the design to help them disperse guard animals. The soft flower bud belies the horrible perfume contained within. Activated by cracking open the petals, the rosebud exudes a noxious cloud that has the [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] trait for 10 minutes. If dropped, it fills a 10-foot-[[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]. If you carry it in one hand and periodically waft it as a free action, the rosebud instead gives you a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Creatures that enter or start their turn in the cloud must succeed at a Fortitude save against the listed DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animals]] and [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beasts]] that critically fail are also [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round. A creature that successfully saves against the burglar’s rosebud becomes temporarily immune to the effects for 24 hours.
