# Elsie's Excellent Bottled Vim  *Item 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Rusthenge

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rusthenge]] pg. 59

**Price** 3 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Interact

---

A dose of this slightly fizzy, pale-pink tonic vitalizes the body and bolsters the spirit, bringing those suffering from aches and pains brought on by illness some temporary respite. For 8 hours after drinking this elixir, reduce the value of one of the following conditions you're suffering by 1: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]]. A condition whose value is reduced to 0 is not removed; after 8 hours, the condition returns to its original value, unless its duration expired or it was removed by another means.
