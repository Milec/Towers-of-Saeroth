# Shatterpoint Ammunition  *Item 4+*

---

Crafted out of spite to counter a caster’s animated objects, _shatterpoint ammunition_ hums quiet songs of destruction. When activated _shatterpoint ammunition_ hits a target, the damage the ammunition deals ignores the target’s [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] if it’s 4 or lower. In addition, the target takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent sonic damage]] from the vibrations.
