# Survey Map  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 292

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All Pathfinder agents have access this item_

---

Maps are uncommon. Most maps you can find are simple and functional. A survey map details a single location in excellent detail. One of these maps gives you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks and any skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], provided the checks are related to the location detailed on the map. Maps sometimes come in atlases, containing a number of maps of the same quality, often on similar topics. An atlas costs five times as much as a single map and requires both hands to use. The GM determines what maps are available in any location.
