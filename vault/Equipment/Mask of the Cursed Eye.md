# Mask of the Cursed Eye  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 155

**Price** 475 gp

**Usage** worn mask

---

A _mask of the cursed eye_ is decorated with at least one wide, staring eye. The first time each day a creature on your plane that you can’t perceive targets you with a [[Traits/Divination|divination]] spell, the creature must attempt a DC 24 Will save. This effect is automatic and does not require you to Activate the item.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected. You know you’ve been targeted with a [[Traits/Divination|divination]] spell, but the mask gives you no additional information.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute. You know you’ve been targeted with a divination spell, but the mask gives you no additional information.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 10 minutes, and the spell is disrupted. You gain a brief mental glimpse of the triggering creature and learn its approximate distance and direction.
