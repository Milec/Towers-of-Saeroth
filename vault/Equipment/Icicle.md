# Icicle  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 242

**Price** 10,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|Longspear]]

---

This _+2 greater striking [[Equipment/Frost|greater frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspear]]_ appears to be a single continuous icicle stretching over 6 feet long. The icicle automatically extinguishes non-magical fires in a 20-foot emanation. While wielding it, you gain fire resistance 5.

**Activate—Quench Flames** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You swing the _icicle_ into the area of an ongoing magical fire, and the spear attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the fire with a counteract modifier of +27. If it fails, it can't attempt to counteract the same fire again.  

**Activate—Ice Spike** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _icicle_ grows rapidly, piercing creatures in a 30-foot line. Each creature in the area takes 11d6 cold damage with a DC 35 Reflex save. A creature that fails its save also takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]](double on a critical failure).
