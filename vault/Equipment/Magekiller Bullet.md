# Magekiller Bullet  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 213

**Price** 1,500 gp

**Ammunition** round or sling bullet

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

_Magekiller bullets_ are forged by carving a spellcaster’s bones with sigils of unbinding, grinding them to powder, and sealing the powder within the core of specially made rounds or sling bullets. When an activated _magekiller bullet_ hits a target, the ammunition burrows deep into its body and interferes with its connection to magic. Each time within the next week an affected target [[Rules/Cast a Spell|Casts a Spell]], it must attempt a DC 11 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. On a failure, it takes 2d12 force damage. This curse can be counteracted with _cleanse affliction_ as normal, but if the ammunition is first removed from the target with a successful DC 35 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check and an operation that takes 1 hour, the counteract check gains a +4 circumstance bonus.
