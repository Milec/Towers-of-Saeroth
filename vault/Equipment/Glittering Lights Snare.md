# Glittering Lights Snare  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 73

**Price** 25 gp

---

Some of Brastlewark's youth craft lesser versions of this snare to prank their friends. A creature that enters the square's square is subjected to a rapid series of flashing lights that overstimulate the senses. They must attempt a DC 22 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 2 rounds.  

**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and then dazzled for 1 minute.
