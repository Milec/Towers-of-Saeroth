# Mask of the Banshee  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 261

**Usage** worn mask

---

This ice-blue half-mask is adorned with a wicked silver grin that covers the wearer's mouth, leaving the rest of the face uncovered. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The mask emits a soul-chilling scream that deals 6d10 negative damage to each living creature in a 20-foot emanation (DC 25 basic Fortitude save).

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Wail of the Banshee|_wail of the banshee_]].
