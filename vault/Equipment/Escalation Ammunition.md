Malevolent arcane energy leaks from within _escalation ammunition_, sizzling with barely [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] contempt. When activated _escalation ammunition_ hits a target, the target must attempt a DC 30 Fortitude save. On a failure, the target rolls the next saving throw it attempts within the next minute against a spell or [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect twice and takes the worse result.
