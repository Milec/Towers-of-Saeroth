# Morning Glow  *Item 14*

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

The blade of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] standard-grade [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Elven Curve Blade (Player Core)|elven curve blade]]_ shines with a pale fire that illuminates the wielder’s face in a grim visage and produces dim light in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. The wielder can suppress or reactivate this light as a single action with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait.

**Activate—Reveal Demons** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You brandish _Morning Glow_ and focus on its gleaming light, boosting its illumination so that it produces bright light in a 30-foot emanation (and dim light for another 30 feet) for 10 minutes. All creatures in this area that have the [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]] trait must attempt a DC 31 Reflex save.

**Success** The demon is unaffected by the glow.  

**Failure** The demon becomes outlined with a shimmering aura of dim light that causes it to become dazzled. If the demon was [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead. If it was already concealed for any other reason, it is no longer concealed. This light affects the demon for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but with a duration of 10 minutes.
