# Sprite Apple  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

1 minute (Interact)

---

A sparkling candy coating covers a sprite apple. For 10 minutes after consuming a sprite apple, you shed [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet). While shedding this light, you can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] if you’re visible, and if you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], you’re concealed instead rather than being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]. The light matches the vibrant color of the apple’s candy coating. Creatures in the bright light are subject to another effect, depending on the type of apple.

## Sprite Apple (Chartreuse)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Price** 26 gp

---

Creatures in the bright light feel more in tune with nature, gaining a +1 item bonus to [[Magic/Domains/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] checks and to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks attempted against animals or plants (if they have the wild empathy class feature or some other ability to make such checks).

## Sprite Apple (Golden)  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Price** 10 gp

---

Creatures in the bright light feel kind and inviting, taking a –1 item penalty to their Will DC against [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks.

## Sprite Apple (Pink)  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Price** 16 gp

---

Creatures in the bright light feel a burst of creativity, gaining a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks.

## Sprite Apple (Teal)  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Price** 41 gp

---

Creatures in the bright light feel a sense of calm, gaining a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects.
