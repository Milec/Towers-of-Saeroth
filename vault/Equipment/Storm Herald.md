# Storm Herald  *Item 12*

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Fighting Fan (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Fighting Fan]]

---

This iridescent, black _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]] [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Fighting Fan (Tian Xia Character Guide)|fighting fan]]_ is crafted from the crackling feathers of [[Bestiary/Thunderbird|thunderbirds]] to resemble one of the beast’s mighty wings. Channeling the avian’s tempestuous nature, you are surrounded by a stormy shell of wind and electricity while wielding a storm herald, granting you resistance 5 to electricity and sonic damage.

**Activate—Call the Storm** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You channel the full might of a storm with you as its epicenter. All creatures within 30 feet take 3d10 electricity damage and 3d10 sonic damage (DC 30 basic Reflex save).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include feathers from a [[Bestiary/Thunderbird|thunderbird]].
