Sometimes, an enemy’s dangerous special ability makes relatively standard sensory capabilities a liability. From [[Bestiary/Otyugh|otyugh]] stench to a [[Bestiary/Harpy (Monster Core)|harpy’s]] song, plenty of creatures use their prey’s senses against them. A sense-dulling hood is a wide, single-use mask designed to be pulled from an airtight package and slipped over the head. The mask grants you an item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]], and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effects for a time, according to the mask’s type. Since it dulls your senses without depriving you of them, the mask also imposes a –1 penalty to rolls and checks using [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] for the same duration.
