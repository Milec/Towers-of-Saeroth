# Ventriloquist's Ring  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296

**Usage** worn

---

This elegant copper ring has miniature images of songbirds engraved around its circumference. You gain a +1 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate—Throw Voice** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Twisting the ring around your finger allows you to magically throw your voice, with the effects of a [[Spells/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]] spell (DC 19).

## Ventriloquist's Ring  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 617

**Price** 60 gp

## Ventriloquist's Ring (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 617

**Price** 670 gp

---

The ring grants a +2 bonus. When you activate the ring, you gain the effects of 2nd-rank [[Spells/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]] (DC 27). You can activate the ring any number of times per day.
