# Oil of Skating  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 87

**Price** 5 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This thin, slippery oil shimmers with a golden hue. Coating your shoes or feet with _oil of skating_ enables you to skate quickly along flat surfaces for 1 hour. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed, which doubles if you move on a downhill surface. You lose this bonus if moving on difficult terrain, greater difficult terrain, or [[Rules/Uneven Ground|uneven ground]]. Also, you treat any uphill movement as moving on difficult terrain while your feet are oiled and treat the results of any [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks made to [[Actions/Balance]] as one degree worse.
