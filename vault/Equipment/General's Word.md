# General's Word  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 127

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|Mace]]

---

The mere act of wielding this heavy _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|mace]]_ grants you a distinct air of authority and gravitas. While wielding the weapon, you can cast [[Spells/Bullhorn (Player Core 2)|_bullhorn_]] as a 1st-rank cantrip at will.

**Activate—Battlefield Broadcast** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The weapon amplifies your force of personality across the battlefield with the effects of [[Spells/Telepathy (Player Core)|_telepathy_]] for 10 minutes, except the range is 500 feet. The effect ends immediately if you drop your weapon.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _bullhorn_ and _telepathy_.
