# Fundamental Oil  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]]

single action

Interact

---

Made of elemental salts and essences from the [[Rules/Inner Sphere]] or where such planes leak onto the Material Plane, _fundamental oil_ is anathema to elementals and other creatures with a weakness to elemental damage. A weapon anointed with this oil acts as [[Equipment/Bane Oil (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|bane oil]] to [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elementals]], but the damage type is the same as the target’s greatest weakness if the target has weakness to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage. If it has none of these, the additional damage is the same type as the weapon’s damage type. These effects last 1 minute.
