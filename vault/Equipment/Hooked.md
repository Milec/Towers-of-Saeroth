# Hooked  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Kingmaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** etched onto a melee weapon

---

A _hooked weapon_ extends hooks when it's used to attack. A _hooked weapon_ gains the [[Traits/Trip|trip]] trait. If a _hooked weapon_ normally has the trip trait, you can attempt to [[Actions/Trip]] a foe as a reaction when you critically hit it with the _hooked weapon_.
