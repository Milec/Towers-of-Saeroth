# Infesting Shot  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 133

**Price** 200 gp

**Ammunition** stones ([[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Trebuchet (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|trebuchet]])

---

This stone is riddled with holes and cracks large enough to allow insects or other small vermin to pass through. After it’s Launched, two [[Bestiary/Army Ant Swarm (Monster Core)|army ant swarms]] crawl out of the stone into spaces adjacent to the stone. The swarms are agitated and attack the closest non-ant creatures.
