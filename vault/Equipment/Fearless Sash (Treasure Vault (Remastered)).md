# Fearless Sash  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 146

**Price** 325 gp

**Usage** worn belt

---

A feeling of security radiates out from this sash made of fine yellow fabric. You gain a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Fear|fear]].

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You and each ally in a 5-foot emanation reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] values by 1.
