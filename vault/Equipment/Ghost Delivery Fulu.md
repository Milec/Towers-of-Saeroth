# Ghost Delivery Fulu  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 77

**Price** 100 gp

**Duration** 1 week

**Usage** affixed to structure, object, or creature

---

A _ghost delivery fulu’s_ inscription resembles a dovecote with spirits around it, and it has a prominent stamp in red wax. This fulu activates once affixed, becoming the homing location of _ghost courier fulus_ with a matching stamp. Delivered _[[Equipment/Ghost Courier Fulu (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|ghost courier fulu]]_ messages (see above) stick to the _ghost delivery fulu_ but can be easily removed.
