# Undead Scourge  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 91

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

**Access** Knights of Lastwall have access to this item.

---

An undead scourge is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Disrupting|disrupting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ with a bluish-white metal blade that emits a faint glow. These used to be weapons of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasmin]] undead slayers only, but the slayers have since shared these daggers with the Knights of Lastwall.

**Activate** free action Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You damage an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature with the _undead scourge_;  **Effect** You disrupt the undead's connection to negative energy. For 1 minute, the undead damaged with the dagger can't be healed by negative energy unless the effect attempting to heal the undead first [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracts]] the _undead scourge's_ effect, with a counteract level of 4 and DC of 25. Positive energy still has the usual effects on the undead.
