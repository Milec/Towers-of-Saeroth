# Psychic Brigandine  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 42

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Splint Mail]]

---

Transparent crystals as hard as steel take the place of metal plates on this suit of _+2 [[Equipment/Invisibility|greater invisibility]] [[Equipment/Armor/Splint Mail|splint mail]]_. Also known as a coat of a thousand thoughts in Vudra and Jalmeray, where it was first developed by psychic warriors, it makes your mind an indomitable fortress. You gain resistance 5 to [[Traits/Mental|mental]] damage.

 Whenever you use the armor to become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], you also become psychically invisible. Each time you would be affected by an effect with the mental trait while you are invisible, attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, it doesn't affect you.
