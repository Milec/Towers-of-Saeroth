# Strand of the Seven Births  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 81

**Price** 300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This strand of charms depicts the legendary [[Bestiary/Harpy (Monster Core)|harpy]] Ekriathe and her six reincarnated successors. Each charm can be removed and activated. When all of the charms are consumed, the strand becomes inert.

**Activate—Hero-God's Whisper** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|interact]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]])    **Effect** You detach a charm, which creates a glowing ghostly image of the hero-god depicted on it. This image sheds [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet) for 1 hour. Allies within the image’s bright light hear the constant whispers of Ekriathe or her successor’s voice, and they gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] effects. Enemies entering or ending their term in the image’s radius of bright light must attempt a DC 23 Will save. On a failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] and takes a –2 status penalty to auditory-based [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks while they remain in the radius. Creatures that succeed against this effect are temporarily immune for the next 24 hours.
