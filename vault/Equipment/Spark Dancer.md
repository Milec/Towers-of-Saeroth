# Spark Dancer  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 39

**Price** 2,900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Pepperbox (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Pepperbox]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Pepperbox (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|pepperbox]]_ cycles through several magical cores, swapping in new ones while the previous ones cool. The gun deals fire or electricity damage, alternating with each attack as it rotates cores. Arcadian gunslingers liken the rotating cores to a group of dancers, each taking their turn in the spotlight.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _spark dancer_ casts either [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]] or [[Spells/Lightning Bolt (Player Core)|_lightning bolt_]], depending on whether the current core would deal [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] or [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] damage, as a 7th-level [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] spell (DC 30).
