# Cloud Pouch  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 273

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This small bag is filled with a fine, silvery powder that feels silky to the touch.

**Activate—Disperse** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You scatter the dust into the air around you, causing it to condense into a cloud in a 20-foot burst within 10 feet, as the [[Spells/Mist|_mist_]] spell. You can Sustain the activation to make the cloud Fly 20 feet. The cloud lasts 1 minute, and you can Dismiss it.
