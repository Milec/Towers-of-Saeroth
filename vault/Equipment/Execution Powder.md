# Execution Powder  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Injury]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 68

**Price** 625 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

The spores of a certain mushrooms, if carefully collected and suspended in a properly mixed alchemical fluid, can kill those near death. If a creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points while under the effect of execution powder, it must succeed at a DC 34 Will save or die (this is a [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effect). If a creature dies from execution powder, the spores from their last breath alight on a random creature adjacent to the victim, granting that creature 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and a +1 status bonus to attack and damage rolls for 10 minutes.

**Saving Throw** DC 34 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 7d6 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 9d6 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 3** 12d6 poison damage (1 round)
