This eerie, blackened [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]] ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 6, HP 36, BT 18) seems to actively reject light that touches it. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] while this shield is raised.

**Activate—Shadow Slip** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would take damage while this shield is raised;  **Effect** Shadows swallow you. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of your next turn.
