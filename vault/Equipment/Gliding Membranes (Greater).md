# Gliding Membranes  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Usage** implanted

---

Membranes that stretch between your arms and torso help you convert a fall into a glide. Treat falls as 25 feet shorter. Even if you take [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|fall damage]], you can land on your feet by succeeding at a DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check.
