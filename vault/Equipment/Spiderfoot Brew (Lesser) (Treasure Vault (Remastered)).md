# Spiderfoot Brew  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 61, [[Sources/Pathfinder Special- Fumbus|Pathfinder Special: Fumbus]]

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This sticky fluid is made from the silk glands of giant spiders. When you drink a spiderfoot brew, tiny hairs grow on your hands and feet, granting you a climb Speed and an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks made to [[Actions/Climb]] for the listed duration.
