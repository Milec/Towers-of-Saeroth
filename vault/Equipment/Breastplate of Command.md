# Breastplate of Command  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 557

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate]]

---

This _+1 resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate|breastplate]]_ is made from shining bronze overlaid with reinforcing golden panels in the shape of lion’s heads. Wearing this breastplate grants you a commanding aura. You gain a +2 item bonus to Diplomacy checks, but you take a –2 item penalty to Stealth checks to Hide and Sneak and Deception checks to Impersonate.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You grant allies within 100 feet a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects for 1 minute. When you activate this ability, each affected ally who’s [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] reduces their frightened value by 1.
