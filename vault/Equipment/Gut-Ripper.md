# Gut-Ripper  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 171

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Ogre Hook (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Ogre Hook]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Ogre Hook (Tian Xia Character Guide)|ogre hook]]_ causes its owner to dream of past murders conducted with the weapon. _Gut-Ripper's_ mythic magic is tied to the bloodlust of the [[Bestiary/Ogre Boss (Monster Core)|ogre boss]] who was holding it upon becoming mythic. If _Gut-Ripper_ doesn't kill a creature each day by midnight, it becomes a non-magical ogre hook.

**Activate** two actions Rip Guts! Rip Guts!; **Cost** 1 Mythic Point;    **Effect** You throw _Gut-Ripper_ in a line of any length up to 60 feet, then it returns to your hands. Each creature in the line takes 2d10 piercing damage with a DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If there's a creature at the end of the line, you instead make a melee Strike against it with the hook that deals an additional 2d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. Each time a creature takes this bleed damage, you regain that many HP; this is a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect. This activation can't be used again for 1d4 rounds.
