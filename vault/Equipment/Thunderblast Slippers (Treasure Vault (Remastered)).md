# Thunderblast Slippers  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 151

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

Unassuming in appearance, these slippers indicate their nature only with a signature strip of yellow stitching. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You move like the wind, with precision and speed. You Stride up to 60 feet; this movement doesn't trigger reactions. When you stop, if you've moved at least 30 feet from where you started, you release a thunderous 5-foot emanation that deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 sonic damage with a DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that critically fails its save is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
