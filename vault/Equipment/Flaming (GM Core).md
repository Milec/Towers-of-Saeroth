# Flaming  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 237

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

A weapon with this rune is empowered by flickering flame. The weapon deals an additional 1d6 fire damage on a successful Strike, plus 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] on a critical hit.

## Flaming  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 237, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 584

**Price** 500 gp

## Flaming (Greater)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 237, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 584

**Price** 6,500 gp

---

Increase the persistent damage on a critical hit to 2d10. Fire damage dealt by this weapon (including the persistent fire damage) ignores the target's fire resistance.
