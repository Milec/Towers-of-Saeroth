# Axe of the Dwarven Lords  *Item 26*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 106

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 3

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dwarven War Axe]]

---

The blade of this _+4 major striking [[Equipment/Keen|keen]] [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] [[Equipment/Speed|speed]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dwarven War Axe|dwarven waraxe]]_ is carved with an intricate design depicting countless generations of great dwarven warriors and leaders. The axe has the [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 30 feet]] weapon trait, in addition to the normal weapon traits for a dwarven waraxe. Strikes with the axe deal an additional 1d6 damage to orcs. While the axe is in your possession, you gain a +4 item bonus when [[Actions/Craft|Crafting]] armor, jewelry, metalworking, stonemasonry, snares, traps, and weapons. If you are a [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarf]], you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|greater darkvision]] while holding the axe. If you are not a dwarf, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 4]] while holding the axe, and if you are an orc, you are also [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 4]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 4]] while holding it.

**Activate** three actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** The axe casts a 10th-level [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]] spell to conjure an [[Bestiary/Elemental Avalanche|elite elemental avalanche]]. The spell is automatically sustained, requiring no action on your part but still allowing you to command the elemental on each of your turns. You can Dismiss the Spell.

**Destruction** If an orc beheads a dwarven king with the _Axe of the Dwarven Lords_, the axe rusts away to nothing.
