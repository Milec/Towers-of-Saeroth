# Day's Breath  *Item 7+*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 82

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This glass bottle holds breezes captured from the heart of the Plane of Air. When you inhale the bottle’s contents, the air fills your lungs so completely that you no longer need to breathe, at least for a time. While the effect lasts, you also gain a status bonus against inhaled threats, such as inhaled poisons. If you spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] when you activate the _day’s breath_, you inhale so deeply that you increase the effect’s duration.

## One Day's Breath  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 82

**Price** 6 gp

**Bulk** L

---

You don’t need to breathe for 24 hours. While the effect lasts, you gain a +1 status bonus to saves against inhaled threats. If you spend 1 Mythic Point, you don’t need to breathe for 7 days.

## Three Days' Breath  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 82

**Price** 25 gp

**Bulk** L

---

You don’t need to breathe for 3 days, and you gain a +2 status bonus to saves against inhaled threats. If you spend 1 Mythic Point, you don’t need to breathe for 30 days.

## Ten Days' Breath  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 82

**Price** 100 gp

**Bulk** L

---

You don’t need to breathe for 10 days, and you gain a +3 status bonus to saves against inhaled threats. If you spend 1 Mythic Point, you don’t need to breathe for 1 year.
