# Invisible Chain Shirt  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 2,750 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|Chain Shirt]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 resilient]] [[Equipment/Invisibility (GM Core)|invisibility]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|chain shirt]]_ is itself [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]]. Other creatures can’t see it at all, allowing you to wear it surreptitiously. Additionally, the armor’s invisible composition is quieter and more comfortable than a normal chain shirt. It loses the [[Traits/Noisy (Player Core)|noisy]] trait and gains the [[Traits/Comfort (Player Core)|comfort]] trait.

**Activate—Obscured Approach** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] and turn partially [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of your next turn or until you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]], whichever comes first. As normal, you can use the concealment from this activation to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]].
