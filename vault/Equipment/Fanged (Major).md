# Fanged  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 106

**Usage** etched onto a melee weapon

---

When etched with this rune, a weapon's hilt or haft becomes engraved with grooves that match the imprints of a wolf's teeth. By putting a _fanged_ weapon in your mouth, you can transform into an animal.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Magical|magical]], [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]])    **Effect** You transform into a Small or Medium animal that wields the _fanged_ weapon in its jaws; the animal matches the animal you are most closely associated with (a [[Rules/Sidebars/Droon|lizardfolk]] would turn into a lizard, a [[Rules/Sidebars/Fox Families|kitsune]] into a fox, a [[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Animal|deer instinct]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|barbarian]] into a deer, etc.) or a wolf if no specific animal is applicable. While in this form, you can attack with the _fanged_ weapon even though you don't have any hands. However, you can attack only with the _fanged_ weapon and you don't have hands or the ability to hold items. For effects dependent on how many hands you are using to hold the item, such as the [[Traits/Two-Hand|two-hand]] trait, you are holding the weapon in two hands. You can Dismiss this effect, and it ends automatically if you drop the _fanged_ weapon (whether or not of your own volition).
