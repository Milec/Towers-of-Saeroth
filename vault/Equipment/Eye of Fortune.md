# Eye of Fortune  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 610

**Price** 2,700 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

Adherents of [[Setting/Deities/Erastil]], god of the hunt, create these magical eye patches. An eye of fortune has a jeweled eye symbol on its front, allowing you to magically see through the eye patch as though it were transparent.

**Activate** free action envision (fortune);   **Trigger** You attack a concealed or hidden creature and haven’t attempted the flat check yet;  **Effect** You can roll the flat check for the concealed or hidden condition twice and use the higher result.
