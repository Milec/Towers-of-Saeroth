# Saurian Spike  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 128

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This jagged, bony growth narrows to a sharp, pointed tip. Deep groves from some former battle scar its surface. The bearer of a _saurian spike_ often feels a sudden surge in power and confidence. The spell attack modifier of any spell cast by activating this item is +15.

- **Armor** You gain [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]] [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent (Monster Core)|scent]] with a range of 30 feet.
- **Weapon** ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) When you cast a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] spell by activating the spike, your battle form is exceptionally fearsome. Each enemy in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must succeed at a DC 27 Will saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Gouging Claw|_gouging claw_]].
