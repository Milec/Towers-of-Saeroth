# Ugly Cute's Gift  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 297

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet (Player Core)|Spiked Gauntlet]]

---

This spiky, stony fragment shed from Ugly Cute’s carapace fits comfortably over the hand. Though a little bulkier than the typical gauntlet, it still functions as a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet (Player Core)|spiked gauntlet]]_.

**Activate—Ugly Cute's Favor** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw upon Ugly Cute’s latent spiritual energy to infuse the gauntlet with forceful power. For 1 minute, _Ugly Cute’s gift_ gains the advantages of a _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]]_ property rune and deals an additional 2 spirit damage on a successful Strike.
