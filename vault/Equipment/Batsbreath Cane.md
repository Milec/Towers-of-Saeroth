# Batsbreath Cane  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 107

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A specialized _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]] [[Equipment/Probing Cane|probing cane]]_ made from strengthened spruce wood, a _batsbreath cane_ is distinctive for its brass tip. The tip covers a small hollow in the wood that houses quartz crystals infused with latent storm magic.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You strike the cane firmly against the ground, causing a pin within the brass tip to tap the crystals and emit a sonic pulse. The pulse reverberates in a 60-foot radius for the next minute, with the cane acting as an antenna to receive the echoes. For 1 minute, as long as you remain in the area and are holding the cane, you gain hearing as a precise sense.
