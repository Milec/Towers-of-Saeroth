# Golden Gloves  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 82

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn gloves

**Bulk** L

---

These golden-threaded gloves fit snuggly past the elbows, and imbue all other worn clothing with an aristocratic mien. The gloves grant you a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] skill checks. When you [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] the gloves, you either increase your Intelligence score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score. This gives you additional trained skills and languages, as normal for increasing your Intelligence modifier. You must select skills and languages the first time you invest the gloves, and whenever you invest the same golden gloves, you get the same skills and languages you chose the first time. If you are [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 when invested in these gloves.

**Activate—Heaven's Wings** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You throw wide your arms, putting the golden gloves on full display and causing a blazing halo to form above your head. All enemies within a 40-feet [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must make a DC 41 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute (or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 minute on critical failure). [[Traits/Unholy]] creatures take a –2 item penalty to their roll. The halo then melts into your body and attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] any one [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|affliction]] you are currently suffering of your choice with a counteract rank of 9 and a counteract modifier of +31.
