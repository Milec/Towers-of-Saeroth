# Armbands of Athleticism  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 285

**Usage** worn armbands

**Bulk** L

---

Skilled awl work has imprinted images of a muscled weightlifter into these tiered leather bands, which grant you enhanced stamina and skill when performing athletic exercises. While fastened to your upper arms, the armbands give you a +2 item bonus to Athletics checks. In addition, whenever you use an action to [[Actions/Climb]] or [[Actions/Swim]] and you succeed at the Athletics check, add a +5-foot item bonus to the distance you move.
