# Baton of the Fallen  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 142

**Price** 9,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This pale shaft of wood has skulls and the mournful faces of the dead carved into its surface. Cold to the touch, it brings a slight chill to the air and smells of freshly disturbed earth.

**Activate—Gather the Fallen** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You plant the baton into the ground, the soil softly parting to allow it to be solidly seated. Doing so summons a cloud of ghostly spirits in a 30- foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]. All creatures within the cloud become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the cloud become concealed to creatures within it. The spirits deal 8d6 void damage to each creature who enters or begins their turn in the cloud (DC 35 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude). You are unaffected by the cloud. This effect lasts 1 minute or ends if an adjacent creature spends an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to knock the baton over.
