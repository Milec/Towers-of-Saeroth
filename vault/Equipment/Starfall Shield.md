# Starfall Shield  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 22

**Price** 23,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Heavy Rondache (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Heavy Rondache]]

---

This high-grade [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] [[Equipment/Shields/Heavy Rondache (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|heavy rondache]] (Hardness 17, HP 100, BT 50) bears a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|shield boss]]_ and has dents that resemble craters on its surface. While wielding the shield, you have fire [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 15.

**Activate** three actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to three times. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions and ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. At the end of your movement, you deal 6d10 bludgeoning damage in a 10-foot emanation and 14d6 fire damage in a 40-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save, with the results applying to both the bludgeoning and fire damage). The space where you end your movement and all adjacent spaces become difficult terrain for 1 minute, and the shield glows red-hot for 10 minutes, during which it gains the effect of the _[[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|greater flaming]]_ property rune.
