A pickled lizard's tongue, tied into a knot, floats in this oily potion. When you drink it, you can speak and understand all spoken (but not written) languages for 1 hour. However, if you attempt to translate  any spoken language, your translation is always erroneous in a way likely to cause substantial confusion or anger, typically reducing the listener's [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|attitude]] toward you by one step. You aren't aware of your error, and any attempt to correct the mistake only compounds it.
