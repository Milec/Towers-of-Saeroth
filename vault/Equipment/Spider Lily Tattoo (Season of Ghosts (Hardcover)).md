# Spider Lily Tattoo  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 305

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

The spider lily tattoo marks you as a trusted member of Granny Hu’s network. This crimson tattoo fades and becomes invisible within a day of being applied, reappearing only when you Activate it, when you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition, or when you die. The higher the doomed value, the more vivid the color. [[Rules/Magical Tattoos|Additional rules for magical tattoos appear here]].

If your tattoo is plainly visible, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks against all creatures that can see the tattoo, but you take a –1 item penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on those who understand the actual meaning of the _spider lily tattoo_ (including all of Willowshore’s citizens).

**Activate—Reveal Tattoo** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])   **Requirements** Your tattoo isn't currently visible; **Effect** You make the tattoo visible. You can use this activation again to make the tattoo not visible, unless you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition.

**Activate—Dead Like You** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature detects you for the first time, and you’re aware of the undead creature;  **Effect** The _spider lily tattoo_ manipulates your life force to make you appear to be [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] for a short time. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check against the triggering undead creature’s Perception DC. On a success, the triggering undead believes you’re undead as well—a mindless undead is likely to ignore you, while a sapient undead might react with curiosity or confusion. You can continue attempting Deception checks each round as a single action to [[Actions/Sustain]] the effect for up to 1 minute.
