---

This aromatic potion is brewed from the white flowers and black roots of a rare herb. When you drink this potion, it immediately attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] all hostile [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|morph]] and [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] effects affecting you. For the next hour, you gain an item bonus to saving throws against morph and polymorph effects. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a polymorph effect during that time, you get a critical success instead.
