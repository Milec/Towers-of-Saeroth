# Thunderbird Tuft  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 216

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

This carved chunk of amber contains a single tuft of gray feathers, which spark with electricity to create an odd jittery sensation in anyone holding the amber for long. When used as part of a _[[Spells/Thunderstrike|_thunderstrike_]]_ spell, the _thunderbird tuft_ funnels electricity back into the spellcaster in a defensive nimbus. For 1 minute, any creature that touches you or that hits you with a melee unarmed attack or non-reach melee weapon attack takes the listed electricity damage.

## Lesser Thunderbird Tuft  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 216

**Price** 3 gp

---

The nimbus deals 1d4 electricity damage, or 1d6 electricity damage if the creature is using a metal weapon. The nimbus only lasts 1 round, rather than the full minute.

## Moderate Thunderbird Tuft  *Item 5*

---
