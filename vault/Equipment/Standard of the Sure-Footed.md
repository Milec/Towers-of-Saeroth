**Activate—Help Up** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per turn;   **Effect** A gust of wind gives an ally a helpful lift. An ally within the banner’s aura can [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action.
