# Conflagration Club  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 182

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub]]

---

A ring of magical crystals encircles the base of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub|greatclub]]_, allowing it to absorb and store magical energy.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;   **Trigger** You Cast a Spell from a spell slot, and the spell has the [[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] trait; **Requirements** The club has no charges; **Effect** You mentally align the club's crystals to reverberate with energy from the spell. It gains a number of charges equal to the level of the spell slot, and the charges are attuned to the damage type matching the spell's trait. If the spell has more than one eligible trait, choose one when the charges are stored.

**Activate** two actions command, envision ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]])   **Requirements** The club has at least 1 charge; **Effect** You flip the club around to blast a cone of energy from its base. Each creature in a 15-foot cone takes 1d6 damage per charge stored in the club, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC or DC 19, whichever is greater. The damage type is the same type the charges were attuned to. The club loses all of its charges.
