# Reverberating Stone  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 94

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You use the affixed shield to [[Feats/Shield Block]] a melee attack

**Requirement** You're a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], and you have the affixed shield [[Actions/Raise a Shield|raised]].

---

This heavy metal emblem is mounted on the face of the affixed shield and has a dull gray gemstone embedded at its center. When you Activate the talisman, the gem shatters, releasing a powerful shockwave in a 15-foot cone that must include the enemy who struck the triggering blow, if possible. Each creature in the cone takes 2d6 sonic damage with a DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. Any creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet away from you (or 10 feet on a critical failure).
