# Falconsight Eye  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 108

**Price** 200 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This prosthetic eye resembles that of a bird of prey. Along with the abilities of the [[Equipment/Magical Prosthetic Eye (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_magical prosthetic eye_]], it allows you to strike foes at greater range and with impressive accuracy.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You become keenly aware of your foes, even those seemingly out of reach. For 1 minute, you can close your eyes as a free action to see through a ranged weapon you're wielding, which reduces the penalty for firing into your weapon's second [[Traits/Range|range increment]] from –2 to 0. This effect doesn't negate the [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] condition.
