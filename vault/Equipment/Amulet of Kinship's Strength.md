# Amulet of Kinship's Strength  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -209- Destroyer's Doom|Pathfinder #209: Destroyer's Doom]] pg. 82

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This weighty brass disk is inscribed with the ancestral names of its previous owners and now includes your own. When worn, it rests against your chest with a heavy warmth, a reminder of the strength of those who came before you. While worn and [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]], you gain a +2 item bonus to Will saves. This bonus increases to +3 if the effect has the [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] trait.

**Activate** single action Strengthen Resolve;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You extend your resolve to your allies, casting [[Spells/Strength of Mind|_strength of mind_]] on up to three willing targets.
