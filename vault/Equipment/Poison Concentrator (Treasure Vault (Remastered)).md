# Poison Concentrator  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 64

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

This compression apparatus can reduce two poisons into a more concentrated dose. As a 10-minute activity that has the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait, you can use a poison concentrator to combine two doses of the same [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] alchemical poison, creating a single concentrated poison. The concentrated potion has a +1 item bonus to its DC, and its level is increased by 1.
