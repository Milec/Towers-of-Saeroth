# Bound Guardian  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 120

**Price** 675 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

A _bound guardian_ is a [[Bestiary/Tattoo Guardian|tattoo guardian]]  bonded to your skin, which you can temporarily animate to protect you. If your soul leaves your body, your tattoo guardian might be destroyed.

**Activate** three actions command, envision, Interact ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You animate your tattoo guardian, otherwise using the duration and other parameters of a 4th-level [[Spells/Summon Construct (Player Core)|_summon construct_]] spell. You can have the guardian Fly Free as part of Activating your tattoo. If your tattoo guardian drops to 0 HP, the activation ends, and the inanimate tattoo returns to your skin.
