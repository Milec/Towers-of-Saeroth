# Garrote Shot  *Item 15*

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

---

Wiry designs mark the silvery coating on a _garrote shot_. When the activated ammunition hits a target, it transforms into a silvery garrote that wraps around one of the target's appendages, dealing 2d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent slashing damage]]. On a critical hit, it wraps around the target's throat if it has one, and the target [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|can't breathe]] until the persistent damage ends. If the persistent damage kills the target, the garrote severs the appendage it's wrapped around.
