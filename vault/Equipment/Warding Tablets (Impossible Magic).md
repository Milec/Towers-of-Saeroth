# Warding Tablets  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 231

**Price** 450 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This grimoire takes the form of a series of baked clay tablets. Any text inked upon it turns swiftly into carved depressions.

**Activate—Purging Spell** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [Cast a prepared Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3144) on yourself or a single ally that grants a bonus to AC, you use the tablets’ power to infuse the warding with additional magic and attempt to remove a harmful effect. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] a harmful spell effect of your choice on the target. This isn’t without risks, however. If your attempt fails to remove the harmful effect, the warding energy is consumed by the unyielding malediction, and you lose the original spell’s normal effects.
