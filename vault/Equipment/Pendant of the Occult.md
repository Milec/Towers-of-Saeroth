# Pendant of the Occult  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 613

**Usage** worn

---

This amulet is hollow and shaped in the form of an unblinking eye. Its cavity typically holds some fragment of occult text. While wearing the pendant, you gain a +1 item bonus to Occultism checks, and you can cast the [[Spells/Guidance|_guidance_]] cantrip as an occult innate spell.
