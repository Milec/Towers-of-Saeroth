# Reactive Mail  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 122

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail (Player Core)|Chain Mail]]

---

Often heavily battle scared from use, this [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail (Player Core)|chain mail]] is commonly issued to frontline soldiers who are tasked with battling through enemies to reach high-value targets across the battlefield.

**Activate—Quick Spike** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature within 15 feet uses a [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] action;  **Effect** An iron spike launches from your armor at the target, dealing 2d8 piercing damage to the target (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). If the target critically fails, the triggering action is disrupted.
