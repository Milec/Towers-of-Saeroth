# Consecrated Shield  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

Metals blessed in song by the blacksmith are crafted into this pendant-sized brass shield. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Heroism (Player Core)|_heroism_]]_ spell, the status bonus granted by the spell applies to the target’s AC but not to their attack rolls.
