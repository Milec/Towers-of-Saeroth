This dull red stone is shot through with glittering veins of black and silver. When you apply a _slayer’s stone_ to your weapon, choose [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberration]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]], [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]], [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Giant (Player Core)|giant]], [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]], [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|ooze]], or both [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]. A weapon under the effect of a _slayer’s stone_ deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to the creatures with the chosen trait or traits for 1 minute. It’s up to the GM’s discretion whether this benefit applies against a creature disguised as a creature with the chosen trait or traits.
