# Tripod  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 179

**Price** 2 sp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** 1

---

Tripods are designed for use with [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] weapons, as a way for gunslingers with lower strength to accurately use these more powerful weapons by sacrificing mobility instead. They can be set up and attached to a firearm with a single Interact action using one hand, setting the tripod in your square. While this sturdy piece of engineering is in use, you don’t take the –2 penalty for firing a kickback weapon, even if your Strength isn’t high enough to avoid the penalty. However, you must retrieve the tripod with a single [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action before you can move the firearm to a different position. Normally, when you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], you become observed if you do anything except [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]], [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], or [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]. However, deploying or retrieving a tripod with an Interact action doesn’t automatically make you observed, so long as you don’t set up or remove the tripod when it’s in a spot where creatures can see the tripod itself.
