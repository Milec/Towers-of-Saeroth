This wand is a length of wrought black iron.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]]. Each creature that ends its turn within the spell's area must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]] of the appropriate rank.
