This _+2 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dogslicer (Player Core)|dogslicer]]_ is obviously the work of a brilliant but scrappy artisan who valued versatility over safety. Four toothy gems line the blade's cutting edge, three of which glow—one with fiery red light, one with a chill blue, and one that gives off sparks—while the last is a glistening black. Each of these gems embodies a weapon property rune, but only one rune can be active at a time.

**Activate—Gem Twist** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You twist the gem along the blade corresponding with the desired weapon property rune: red for [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]], blue for [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|frost]], or yellow for [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]]. You take 1d6 damage of the type the chosen rune deals. You can instead twist the black gem to disable the active property rune, taking no damage.
