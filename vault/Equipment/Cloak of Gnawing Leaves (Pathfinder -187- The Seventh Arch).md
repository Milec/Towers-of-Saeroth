# Cloak of Gnawing Leaves  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 80

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This cloak appears to be woven from a thousand living leaves, hungry for flesh and eager to defend the cloak's wearer.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You are damaged by a melee attack from an adjacent creature;  **Effect** The leaves lash out at your attacker, rising up to reveal snapping jaws made of wicked thorns. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 17 Reflex saving throw.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature takes 1d4 piercing damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 2d4 piercing damage and 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|bleed damage]].

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 80

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Bulk** L

---
