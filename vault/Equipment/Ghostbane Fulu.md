This white paper fulu bears red ink and attunes a weapon to the spiritual essence of an incorporeal creature. Upon activation, the weapon gains the benefit of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune against the triggering incorporeal creature for 1 minute.
