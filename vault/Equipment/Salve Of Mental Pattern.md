This vial contains a dark, silvery-gray salve. When you apply this salve to a willing creature, it congeals into an intricate mesh of lacy fractals that covers the target’s body, granting the target [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage for 10 minutes, after which the mesh flakes off into powdery crumbs.
