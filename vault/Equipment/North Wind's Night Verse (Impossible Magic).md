# North Wind's Night Verse  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 209

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Katana (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Katana]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Katana (Tian Xia Character Guide)|katana]]_ is always cool to the touch, nearly uncomfortably so. Unadorned and with no tsuba, its honed blade is carved from a single tusk of dragon-turtle ivory and wrapped in strips of [[Bestiary/Witchwarg|witchwarg]] hide. Strikes with the katana gain a +2 status bonus to damage rolls against creatures that have a status penalty to their Speed or that are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]]. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking_ rune and +4 for a _major striking_ rune.

**Activate—Frigid Breeze** free action (air, [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You deal cold damage to a creature with a spell or ability; **Requirements** ; **Effect** You point the _north wind’s night verse_ at the triggering creature. A gust of wind from nowhere freezes its bones, imparting a –5-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.
