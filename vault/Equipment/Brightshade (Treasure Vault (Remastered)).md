Brewed from a plant native to the First World, brightshade destroys tissue, living or dead. Victims of this poison take poison damage if they’re alive and vitality damage if they’re [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]].

 **Saving Throw** DC 21 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison or [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d6 poison or vitality damage (1 round)
