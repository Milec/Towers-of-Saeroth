# Static Snare  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 43

**Price** 6 gp

---

You hide insulating crystals or other material that releases a strong static charge when the first creature enters the snare's square. That creature must attempt a DC 18 Reflex saving throw.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target takes 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent electricity damage]] and takes a –1 circumstance penalty to saving throws against electricity effects for as long as the persistent electricity damage lasts.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target attracts nearby dirt and dust. If the target is or becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], it's merely [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to creatures that have sight as a precise sense. This effect lasts as long as the persistent electricity damage does.
