# Horn of the Sun Aurochs  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shadows at Sundown

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shadows at Sundown]] pg. 53

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A horn of the sun aurochs grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks when played as an instrument.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The horn is clearly audible for hundreds of feet, but within a 20-foot emanation, the sound causes agony in those who wish harm upon the user; the horn also sheds [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in that area and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] to the next 20 feet. Enemies of the user in this area take 7d6 sonic damage and 7d6 good damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] DC 30 Fortitude save). Creatures that are specifically vulnerable to sunlight are also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] if they fail this save or frightened 2 on a critical failure. This activation also attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] or [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] effects in the area (+23 counteract check).
