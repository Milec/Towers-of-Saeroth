# Coin of Comfort  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 26

**Price** 45 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This thick silver coin is deeply worn on one side, creating a shallow dip.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You rub your thumb along the grooved side and become filled with a sense of comfort and safety. You reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition by 1.
