**Source** [[Sources/PFS Quest -5- The Dragon Who Stole Evoking Day|PFS Quest #5: The Dragon Who Stole Evoking Day]] pg. 14

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

A dwarven daisy is a string of dozens of firecrackers that create tiny, loud explosions. The string ends with a short, wrapped fuse. Pulling off the wrapping in a quick motion lights the fuse and is done as part of the action to Strike. A dwarven daisy deals the listed fire damage and splash damage. A dwarven daisy also has a chance to [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzle]] its target. A creature struck by a dwarven daisy must succeed at a Fortitude save or become dazzled for 1 round.
