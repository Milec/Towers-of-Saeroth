# Vambrace of Gorum  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 125

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

---

When the [[Setting/Deities/Gorum (Divine Mysteries)|Lord in Iron]] shattered, immeasurable fragments fell to Golarion as pieces of metal. This piece of his armor has been shaped into the _Vambrace of Gorum_, a _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|supreme reinforcing]] [[Equipment/Adamantine Shield (GM Core)|high-grade adamantine shield]]_ (Hardness 20, HP 160, BT 80). Pieces of sharp iron stick out, acting as _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]]_. The _Vambrace_ still holds some of the deity’s power. When the shield is broken, small fragments of metal explode outward, dealing 6d6 piercing damage in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Activate—Blade of Iron** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The shield reforms into a _+3 high-grade adamantine major striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|greatsword]]_ for 1 minute. While wielding this sword, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC for each adjacent enemy (up to a +4 bonus).

**Activate—Blood of Iron** single action ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The _Vambrace of Gorum_ is in its shield form; **Effect** Your shield becomes an empowering symbol of the thrill of battle. You and your allies within 10 feet of you gain 30 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 hour, and reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] conditions by 1.

**Activate—Bones of Iron** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You use the shield to [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] and the attack overcomes the shield’s Hardness;  **Effect** The iron spikes lash out at the creature who made the attack. The creature takes 6d6 piercing damage and 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Destruction** If the _Vambrace of Gorum_ is broken, buried under a rose bush in [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana (GM Core)|Nirvana]], and left undisturbed for 100 years, it loses all its magic and becomes a standard [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]].
