# Arboreal Wand  *Item 9+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 81

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This gnarled wand is made from the branch of an [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal (Monster Core)|arboreal]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] at the indicated rank. After you cast the spell, the raw primal energy stored within the arboreal from which the wand was made washes over one target you choose and tries to purify its essence. The wand attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the lowest level [[Rules/Afflictions (Player Core)|affliction]] affecting your target. It uses your casting modifier and a counteract rank of half the wand’s item level rounded up.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _heal_ of the appropriate level and a branch freely given by an arboreal.
