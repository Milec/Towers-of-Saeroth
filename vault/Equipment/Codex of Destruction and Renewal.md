# Codex of Destruction and Renewal  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 111

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Bulk** L

---

An unsmiling mask, half obsidian and half ivory, is embossed on the cover of this weighty tome, the opposite color forming the background of each half. Each _codex of destruction and renewal_ is said to contain a fragment of the knowledge [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]] gained in his apotheosis and they’re sacred to his church.

**Activate** single action envision ([[Traits/Spellshape|metamagic]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to cast a damage-dealing [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]] spell prepared from this grimoire, the spell wreaks even greater destruction. The spell ignores 10 [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] and you reroll any damage dice that roll a 1. If the spell reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, they’re torn apart at an infinitesimal level, not even leaving a fine ash; this is a [[Rules/Death Effects|death effect]].

**Activate** single action envision ([[Traits/Spellshape|metamagic]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]] spell prepared from this grimoire that restores Hit Points, the spell renews the target constantly and perfectly. The spell grants the target [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration (Monster Core)|regeneration]] 20, restoring 20 Hit Points at the start of its turn and preventing the target both from [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] due to damage and from its dying condition increasing to a value that would result in its death. Each time the creature regains Hit Points from regeneration, it regrows all damaged or ruined organs; it can also regrow any severed body parts as a free action immediately after the body part is severed, with the original crumbling to ash. The effect lasts for 4 rounds.
