# Striking  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 236

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

A _striking rune_ stores destructive magic in the weapon, increasing the weapon damage dice it deals to two instead of one. For instance, a _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ would deal 2d4 damage instead of 1d4 damage.  
 You can upgrade the _striking rune_ already etched on a weapon to a stronger version, increasing the values of the existing rune to those of the new rune. The Price of the upgrade is the difference between the two runes' Prices.
