# Lady's Spiral  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 19

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Disrupting|disrupting]] [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip|whip]]_ has a single strip of anointed leather wrapped around its hilt in a spiral pattern. The handle of the whip ends in an oak spike that has been sanctified with ashes from [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma|Pharasma's]] [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]]. If the spike is used to stake a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire|vampire]] that's vulnerable to being staked, the vampire is immediately destroyed, without having to sever its head and anoint it with [[Equipment/Holy Water|_holy water_]]. If the whip is buried with a creature, that creature can't rise as an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] as long as the whip remains by its side.
