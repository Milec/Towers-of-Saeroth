# Three-Pillared Yang Na  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 108

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This tattoo represents an abstract tree whose trunk is made up of three lines of Tang text. Applied in a ritual involving jasmine, turmeric, and a blossom from a yang na tree, the tattoo provides three blessings. The first blessing keeps your mind still during negotiations, granting a +1 item bonus to Diplomacy checks.

**Activate—Second Blessing** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]])    **Effect** You learn the direction of the yang na tree that gave the blossom used in creating your tattoo. Most such trees are in Tang Mai, far to the west of the Inner Sea.

**Activate—Third Blessing** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You take spirit damage;  **Effect** You gain resistance 3 against that spirit damage.
