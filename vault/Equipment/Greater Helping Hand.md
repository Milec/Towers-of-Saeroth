# Helping Hand  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 236

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This miniature wooden hand has fully articulated fingers with brass joints. The tiny hand twitches infrequently of its own accord, especially if scrutinized for too long.  
**• Armor** You increase your maximum and [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]] Bulk limit by 1.  
**• Weapon** You gain a +1 item bonus to skill checks to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] when wielding the weapon.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Telekinetic Hand|_telekinetic hand_]]._
