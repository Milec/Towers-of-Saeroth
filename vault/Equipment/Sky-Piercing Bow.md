# Sky-Piercing Bow  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 74

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow]]

---

Large turquoise gems stud the outer edge of this sturdy _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]]_. Arrows shot from the bow are unimpaired by wind and air effects.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]])
 **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You shoot an arrow at an open space on a surface within 500 feet and momentarily vanish into a wind that carries the arrow. You teleport to that location.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact ([[Traits/Electricity|electricity]])
 **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You shoot an arrow at the sky and it tears an extraplanar rift that resembles a storm cloud in a 30-foot burst centered on a point within 200 feet. A torrent of rain pours down from the rift for 1 minute or until you Dismiss the effect. The area within the torrent becomes difficult terrain for all movement (including [[Actions/Fly|Flying]]). As an Interact action, you can fire an arrow into the rift to cause a bolt of lightning to strike a creature in the area. The creature takes 5d12 lightning damage and must attempt a DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

**Activate**  10 minutes (command, envision, Interact);  **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** You fire a volley of arrows into the sky. You create the effects of a successful [[Rituals/Control Weather|_control weather_]] ritual.
