# Mirror-Ball Snare  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 26

**Price** 40 gp

---

A mixture of metal shavings and flash powder inside this small, mirrored sphere ignites when disturbed, causing the ball to flash and spin. When a creature enters the square, the ball pops into the air, and all creatures within 10 feet who can see the mirror ball must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round. On a critical failure, affected creatures are instead dazzled for 1 minute.
