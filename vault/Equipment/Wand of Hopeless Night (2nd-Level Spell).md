# Wand of Hopeless Night  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 265

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wand is a length of wrought black iron.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]]. Each creature that ends its turn within the spell's area must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _darkness_ of the appropriate level.
