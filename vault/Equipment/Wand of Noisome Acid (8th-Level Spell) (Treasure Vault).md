**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 141

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This greasy stick emits a stomach-churning scent when held in hand.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Acid Arrow|_acid arrow_]] of the indicated level. A creature that takes initial acid damage from this spell become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. Use your spell DC if the creature attempts to recover from this sickness. This is an olfactory effect.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Acid Arrow|_acid arrow_]] of the appropriate level.
