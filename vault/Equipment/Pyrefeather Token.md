**Price** 5 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You succeed at, fail, or critically fail a recovery check while [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] by 1.

---

This [[Bestiary/Pyrefowl|pyrefowl]] feather, accentuated with beadwork, stores a spark of the bird's self-healing power. When you Activate the token, you reduce the triggering recovery check's DC by 1, potentially improving your check's degree of success. You also gain 5 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 minute. You can Activate this talisman even while [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].
