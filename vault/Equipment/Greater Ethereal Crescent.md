This crescent-shaped shard of iridescent metal is strangely translucent, fading to a blurry outline when examined for too long. A weapon under the effects of an _ethereal crescent_ becomes a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]] weapon for 1 minute.

## Ethereal Crescent  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 135

**Price** 58 gp

## Greater Ethereal Crescent  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 135

**Price** 320 gp

---

A weapon under the effects of a _greater ethereal crescent_ gains the effects of an [[Equipment/Astral|astral]] rune for 1 minute. This does not count against the weapon’s normal maximum number of runes.
