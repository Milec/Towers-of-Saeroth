# Resonating Ammunition  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 175

**Price** 300 gp

**Ammunition** [[Equipment/Weapons/Arrows|arrow]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Bolts|bolt]]

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

The end of this ammunition is a metallic tuning fork with magical etchings. When an activated _resonating arrow_ hits its target, the tuning fork resonates with residual energy from the shot, transforming it into dangerous sound waves. This deals 5d10 sonic damage to the target and each creature within 10 feet of it with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] DC 28 Fortitude save.
