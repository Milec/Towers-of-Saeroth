**Bulk** L

single action

Interact

---

After you drink this mutagen, your skin continually renews itself, thickening into large, scaly patches.

**Benefit** For 1 minute, you gain fast healing 2 and automatically succeed at flat checks to recover from [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

**Drawback** Your thickened skin makes quick movement difficult, and your excess skin flakes off with the slightest movement. You take a –2 penalty to Reflex saves for 1 minute. Any creature attempting to [[Actions/Track]] you in the next 24 hours gains a +4 circumstance bonus to their check.
