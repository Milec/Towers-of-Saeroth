# Eyes Of The Moonwarden  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 80

**Price** 620 gp

**Usage** worn pendant

---

This beaded pendant crafted by Lyrune-Quah shamans features an uncut moonstone that warns the wearer of danger. When a [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] creature comes within 30 feet of you, the stone glows with moonlight only you can see.

**Activate—Lunar Illumination** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Bright moonlight shines out of the gem in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], forcing hostile creatures in the area to make a DC 26 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.
