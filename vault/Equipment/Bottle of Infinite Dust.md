# Bottle of Infinite Dust  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 143

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This plain, faintly green bottle full of sand looks deceptively mundane, though it contains a near endless supply of sand. A very slow but steady stream of sand can be poured from the bottle. Up to 1 pound of sand can be produced a day this way.

**Activate—Grit Geyser** two actions ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You aim the bottle and unleash a blast of sand. Scouring grit is released in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], dealing 4d4 slashing damage to all creatures in the area (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Activate—Sandstorm** three actions ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You dump out the bottle, creating a swirling sandstorm around you. A 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] is filled with blowing sand that obscures vision. This has the effects of mist. The air within the sandstorm is unbreathable; creatures in the area must [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|hold their breath]]. Creatures entering or starting their turn in the sandstorm take 2d4 slashing damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures with the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait or that are primarily made of liquid take double damage. This sandstorm lasts 10 minutes or until the bottle is corked with an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, whichever comes first.
