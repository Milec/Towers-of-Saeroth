# Bonmuan Swapping Stone  *Item 11+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -166- Despair on Danger Island|Pathfinder #166: Despair on Danger Island]] pg. 75

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This small, opalescent stone glows with a light that constantly shifts between colors. When you activate the stone, you throw it into a space within 100 feet. The stone then casts [[Spells/Dimension Door|_dimension door_]] on you and transports you to itself. This destroys the stone.

 You can instead hurl the stone at a creature in an attempt to bring that creature closer to you. Make a ranged Strike against a creature using your highest proficiency with a ranged weapon. The swapping stone has a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 15 feet. On a hit, the stone shatters against the creature, casting _dimension door_ on the creature and causing it to instantly teleport to a space adjacent to you. An unwilling creature can resist this teleportation with a successful DC 28 Will save.
