# Flash Beetle Lantern  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 106

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

---

This unassuming hooded lantern contains a mass of flash beetle eggs suspended in a magical solution. It sheds light on a 45-foot radius (and dim light in the next 45 feet).

**Activate—Spotlight** single action ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Flipping a concealed lever in the lantern’s handle triggers a small current to pass through the solution. The eggs brighten and emit a series of brilliant flashes in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature in the area of effect must attempt a DC 18 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the eggs of a [[Bestiary/Flash Beetle (Monster Core)|flash beetle]].
