# Bloodstride Blade  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 64

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This item is restricted due to the teleportation effect._

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber (Player Core)|Sawtooth Saber]]

**Access** You're a member of the Red Mantis Assassins

---

This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Cunning|cunning]] [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]] sawtooth saber_ appears rusty but never loses its edge. It’s drawn to the blood of your targets. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] or [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] creatures that have blood.

**Activate—Call of the Bloodthirst** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature within 60 feet takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]] damage;  **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Translocate|translocate]]_, appearing adjacent to the triggering creature, then [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] it with this weapon. The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the attack.
