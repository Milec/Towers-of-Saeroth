# Implacable  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 80

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The effects of the implacable rune only activate when an enemy causes the effect to apply._

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** etched onto medium or heavy armor

---

This substantial rune makes you difficult to hold back. Whenever you are affected by an effect that lasts until you [[Actions/Escape]] (for instance, from the [[Actions/Grapple]] action or a [[Equipment/Tanglefoot Bag|tanglefoot bag]]), you become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]]. You can use the extra action each round only to Step or Escape.
