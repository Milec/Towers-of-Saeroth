This _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]]_ has a crimson blade that shimmers eerily in bright light. On a critical hit, the kukri deals 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. If the target didn't already have persistent bleed damage when you scored the critical hit, you also gain 1d8 temporary Hit Points for 1 minute.
