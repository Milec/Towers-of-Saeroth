# Aligned Oil  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 561

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This oil fills a weapon with cosmic power of an alignment. Each _aligned oil_ is crafted to one alignment: chaos, evil, good, or law (such as a _good-aligned oil_). A weapon anointed with this oil gains the effects of the property rune matching its alignment: [[Equipment/Axiomatic|_anarchic_]] (chaos), [[Equipment/Corrosive|_axiomatic_]] (law), [[Equipment/Keen|_holy_]] (good), or [[Equipment/Vorpal|_unholy_]] (evil). This lasts for 1 minute.
