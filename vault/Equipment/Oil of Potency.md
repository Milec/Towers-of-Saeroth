# Oil of Potency  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 562

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

When you apply this thick, viscous oil to a weapon or suit of armor, that item immediately becomes magically potent. If the item is a weapon, it temporarily becomes a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] weapon_, or, if it's armor, it temporarily becomes _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] armor_. This lasts for 1 minute.
