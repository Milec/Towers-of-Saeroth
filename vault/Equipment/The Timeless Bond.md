# The Timeless Bond  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Contract]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 227

---

You’ve linked your lifespan to your bound dragon. Your natural physical aging ceases and you gain the Draconic Vitality action, but your soul grows withered and scabrous by the weight of a dragon’s experience with time.

Once per week, from any distance, the dragon can use a free action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, to become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 minute. They can use the extra action to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], or to contribute an action to casting a spell. When the dragon uses this ability, you age 10 years and become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 3 for 1 hour.

**Activate—Draconic Vitality** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Reduce your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] value by 2. For 1 hour, you gain a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] and [[Rules/Diseases (GM Core)|diseases]], and a status bonus equal to your level to Hit Points you regain from spells with the same tradition as your bound dragon.  

---

**Oathbreaker’s Calamity** Should either party break the oath, you begin aging at an accelerated rate. Every 1d6 days, you must succeed at a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat DC 14 check]] or age 10 years and become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 3. In turn, the dragon loses years of experience, reducing its age 100 years every time you fail the flat check. If the dragon is de-aged past the time they made the pact, the pact remains, but the dragon loses all memories of you and the pact.
