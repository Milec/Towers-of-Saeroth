# Kirin Echo Chime  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 118

**Price** 5 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

The echo of [[Bestiary/Kirin|kirin's]] song can be captured within this small glass chime, which shatters and calls up a meandering breeze of its own when rung. Adding this catalyst to a [[Spells/Gust of Wind (Player Core)|_gust of wind_]] spell causes any flying creatures that would be pushed by the spell's effects to be pushed 30 feet in a direction of your choice, rather than 30 feet in the direction of the spell.
