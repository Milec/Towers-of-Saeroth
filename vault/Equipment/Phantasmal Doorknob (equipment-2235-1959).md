# Phantasmal Doorknob  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 127

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The standard version is permitted for use while the greater and major versions are limited._

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This ornate doorknob can open doors both material and metaphysical, revealing dreams and nightmares. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 20.

- **Armor** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Pick a Lock]].
- **Weapon** If you critically succeed at a Strike with the weapon, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Figment|_figment_]].

## Phantasmal Doorknob (Greater)  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 127

**Price** 900 gp

---

The item bonus when affixed to armor is +2, and the spell DC is 27. On a critical hit with a weapon the doorknob is affixed to, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the end of its next turn. The creature is then temporarily immune to being blinded this way for 24 hours.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Vision of Death|_vision of death_]].

## Phantasmal Doorknob (Major)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 127

**Price** 6,000 gp

---

The item bonus when affixed to armor is +2, and the spell DC is 34. On a critical hit with a weapon the doorknob is affixed to, the target is blinded until the end of its next turn. The creature is then temporarily immune to being blinded this way for 24 hours.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast 6th-rank _vision of death_.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Phantasmal Calamity (Player Core)|_phantasmal calamity_]].
