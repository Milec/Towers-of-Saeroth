# Alloy Orb  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 263

**Usage** affixed to a metal weapon

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

---

Although solid, this orb of metal swirls with bright silver and dark iron colors, as if made of liquid. When you activate the _alloy orb_, select [[Equipment/Cold Iron|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver|silver]]. The affixed weapon functions as the chosen material for 1 minute, suppressing its original material. Powerful weapons overwhelm the magic of this talisman, and it works only on weapons of 8th level or lower.
