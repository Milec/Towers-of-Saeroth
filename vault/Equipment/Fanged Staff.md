# Fanged Staff  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 238

**Price** 925 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Carved of the finest bloodwood, this staff is an elegant walking stick with a sharp metal point at its base. _Fanged staves_ are popular among favored mortal retainers of [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampires]]. While wielding a _fanged staff_, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Void|void]] effects.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.  
**• Cantrip** _[[Spells/Daze (Player Core)|_daze_]]_  
**• 1st** _[[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_command_]]_, _[[Spells/Grim Tendrils (Player Core)|_grim tendrils_]]_  
**• 2nd** _[[Spells/Blood Vendetta (Player Core)|_blood vendetta_]], [[Spells/Draw Blood|_draw blood_]]_  
**• 3rd** _[[Spells/Enthrall (Player Core)|_enthrall_]]_, _[[Spells/Vampiric Feast|_vampiric feast_]]_  
**• 4th** _[[Spells/Sanguine Mist|_sanguine mist_]]_, _[[Spells/Vampiric Maiden (Player Core 2)|_vampiric maiden_]]_

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
