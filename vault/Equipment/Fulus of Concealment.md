# Fulus of Concealment  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 159

**Price** 40 gp

**Duration** 1 hour

**Usage** affixed to the ground in four spaces along a 10-foot radius circle

---

This fulu comes in four pieces, one placed in each cardinal direction. Choose one of the following traits when Activating the fulus: [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Beast|beast]], [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]], [[Traits/Fey|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]], [[Traits/Plant|plant]], or [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. Those within the fulus' circle upon activation (including the fulus themselves) gain the effect of [[Spells/Invisibility Sphere|_invisibility sphere_]], but only against creatures with the chosen trait. If any of the fulus are moved or destroyed after activation, the effect ends.
