# Burr Shield  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|Wooden Shield]]

---

This well-crafted [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|wooden shield]] ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 5, HP 30, BT 15) is covered in numerous seed pods with long spurs. You can [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with these burrs as though they were _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]]_.

**Activate—Prickly Sticker** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** Your previous action was a successful Strike with the burrs; **Effect** One of the burrs catches on the Strike’s target, inflicting 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].
