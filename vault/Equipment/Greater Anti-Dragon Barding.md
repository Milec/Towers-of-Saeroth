# Anti-Dragon Barding  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Companion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 222

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This item uses the same defense stats as [[Equipment/Barding (Player Core)|light barding]]._

**Usage** worn barding

**Bulk** L

---

Consisting of long steel spikes projecting from light barding, _anti-dragon barding_ helps to protect vulnerable mounts and livestock from massive aerial predators, including dragons, griffons, and rocs. An [[Rules/Animal Companions (Player Core)|animal companion]] wearing anti-dragon barding gains a +2 item bonus to its Fortitude DC and Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] it, and a creature that successfully Grapples the animal companion takes 1d4 piercing damage.
