# Juxtaposition Ammunition  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 19

**Price** 225 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

_Juxtaposition ammunition_ quivers with anticipation, as if the projectile wishes to constantly be in motion. When an activated _juxtaposition ammunition_ hits a creature, it doesn't deal the Strike's normal damage. Instead, the target must succeed at a DC 28 Will save or be teleported to a safe, unoccupied square of your choice within 60 feet of its original position and within the weapon's first range increment. On a critical failure, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] from the gut-wrenching sensation of sudden movement. Any relocation from the _juxtaposition ammunition_ is [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]].
