This sticky fluid is made from the silk glands of [[Bestiary/Families/Spider (Monster Core)|giant spiders]]. When you drink a spiderfoot brew, tiny hairs grow on your hands and feet, granting you a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] and an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks made to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] for the listed duration.

## Spiderfoot Brew (Lesser)  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 61

**Price** 12 gp

---

The climb Speed is 15 feet, the item bonus is +1, and the duration is 1 minute.

## Spiderfoot Brew (Moderate)  *Item 9*

---

## Spiderfoot Brew (Greater)  *Item 17*
