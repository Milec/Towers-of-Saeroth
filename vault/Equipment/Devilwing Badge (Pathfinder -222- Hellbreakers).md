# Devilwing Badge  *Item 4+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 211

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This small tearing of a devil's wing has been cleansed with holy water before being mounted onto a silver badge. The spell attack modifier of any spell cast by Activating this item is +8, and the spell DC is 18.

- **Armor** You gain resistance 2 to fire and unholy.
- **Weapon** After you cast a spell by Activating the _devilwing badge_, your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 spirit damage until the end of your next turn.

 **Activate** Cast a Spell; **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]].
