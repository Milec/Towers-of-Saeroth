Dark smoke seems to writhe within this obsidian gem. When you activate the gem, make a melee Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 1, or frightened 2 on a critical hit.  
 If you have the [[Feats/Intimidating Strike]] feat, increase the frightened condition value caused by the Strike to frightened 2, or frightened 3 on a critical hit.
