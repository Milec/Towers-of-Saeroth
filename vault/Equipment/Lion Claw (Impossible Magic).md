# Lion Claw  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 224

**Price** 300 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Requirements** You are undetected by a creature and are a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]].

---

This dried claw from a mighty beast bestows upon you the ability of a predator. When you activate the claw, you learn to pounce on your prey in one fluid motion. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] and then [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the affixed weapon against one creature you were [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by. You remain undetected by the creature until after you Strike.
