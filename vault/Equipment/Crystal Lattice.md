This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 resilient]] [[Equipment/Raiment|raiment]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer’s clothing]]_ is a longline vest made of a flexible, durable lattice of many fine crystals. A wide variety of these vests exist, and their colors depend on the type of crystal that forms the lattice. The crystals glow briefly when an enemy hits the wearer.

**Activate—Crystal Exoskeleton** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The crystals on your vest grow and extend, becoming an architecturally sculpted exoskeleton that covers your form. You gain 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 hour. The exoskeleton crumbles into dust when the temporary Hit Points are depleted or when the duration ends, and the crystals return to their original state.
