# Redsand Hourglass  *Item 23*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 78

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This platinum hourglass is filled with red sand, is decorated with imagery of roses and whippoorwills with tiny black onyx eyes, and has the ability to manipulate time. Once used by [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]] herself, the _Redsand Hourglass_ was stolen from Pharasma's court a millennia ago. While the hourglass is in your possession, you're immune to the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] conditions.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail a saving throw;  **Effect** You flip the hourglass upside down then immediately right it again, rewinding time by a few moments. Reroll the saving throw with a +4 circumstance bonus. You must use the results of this new saving throw.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You flip the hourglass upside down, unhinging you from the flow of time. You become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 minute. You can use this extra action only to Step, Stride, or Sustain a Spell.

**Activate** three actions Interact ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** You flip the hourglass sideways, pausing time for everyone but yourself. You cast [[Spells/Time Stop|_time stop_]].

**Destruction** The _Redsand Hourglass_ is destroyed if it's used within the [[Setting/Planes/Dimension of Time]].
