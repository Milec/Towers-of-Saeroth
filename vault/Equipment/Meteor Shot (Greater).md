This craggy stone ammunition is warm to the touch. When you fire an activated _meteor shot_, it explodes into a small swarm of meteors as it reaches its target, scorching nearby creatures and littering the ground with rubble. In addition to the weapon’s normal damage, the _meteor shot_ deals fire damage and the ground in the area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].

In addition, the [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] gains the following critical failure effect.

**Critical Failure** The weapon misfires.
