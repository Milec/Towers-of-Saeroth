# The Bear  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 72

**Usage** carried

---

_The Bear_ represents brute force applied to solve unusual problems. As long as you have _The Bear_ invested, you can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Tumble Through]] or [[Actions/Squeeze]].

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per minute;  **Requirements** Your last action was a successful melee Strike; **Effect** You attempt to [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] the creature you hit with the successful Strike, even if you have no hand free. You gain a +2 status bonus to your check. If you Grapple using a weapon, you can Strike with the weapon only if it has the [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]] trait and you Strike the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] target, or if you cease Grabbing with the weapon. After the effect of the initial Grapple ends, you can't keep a target grabbed with a weapon that lacks the grapple trait. The status bonus increases to +3 if you're at least 17th level.
