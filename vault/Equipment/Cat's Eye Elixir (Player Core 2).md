After you consume this elixir, your vision sharpens, and you become sensitive to even the most minute movements. For the next minute, you reduce the flat check to target [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] creatures to 5, and you don't need to attempt a flat check to target [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] creatures. These benefits apply only against creatures within 30 feet of you.
