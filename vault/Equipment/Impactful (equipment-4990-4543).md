# Impactful  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 204

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This rune thrums with pure magical energy. Weapons with an _impactful_ rune deal an additional 1d6 force damage on a successful [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. On a critical hit, you can choose to make the target to attempt a DC 27 Fortitude save. On a failure, the target is pushed 5 feet away from you.
