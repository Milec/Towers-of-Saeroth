# Smoking Sword  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Beginner Box- Game Master's Guide|Pathfinder Beginner Box: Game Master's Guide]] pg. 51

**Price** 60 gp

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword]]

---

Smoke constantly belches from this _+1 magic [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_. Any hit with this sword deals 1 extra fire damage. You can use a special action while holding the sword to command the blade's edges to light on fire.

**Activate** single action concentrate;     **Effect** Until the end of your turn, the blade deals 1d6 extra fire damage instead of just 1. After you use this action, you can't use it again for 10 minutes.
