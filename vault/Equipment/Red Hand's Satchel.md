# Red Hand's Satchel  *Item 21*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 83

**Usage** worn backpack

**Bulk** 1

---

_Red Hand’s Satchel_ is an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Haversack (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_alchemist’s haversack_]], with a main section containing an extradimensional space like that of a [[Equipment/Spacious Pouch|_type IV spacious pouch_]].

Once restored, the satchel gains the following activation.

**Activate—Instant Alchemy** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You name and then produce a common [[Rules/Alchemical Items (GM Core)|alchemical item]] of any level up to level 20 from the satchel’s main compartment. This item has the [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] trait and remains potent for 24 hours. Make a flat check with a DC equal to the alchemical item’s level. On failure, you can’t activate _Red Hand’s Satchel_ in this way until the next time you make your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]].
