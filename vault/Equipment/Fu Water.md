# Fu Water  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 161

**Price** 21 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

Burnt fulu ashes float in this liquid, lending a distinctive red color and imparting it with a variety of purifying effects. Unlike many [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|fulu]] items, _fu water_ must be created using [[Traits/Divine|divine]] magic.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** This potion functions as a bottle of [[Equipment/Holy Water|_holy water_]]. Like holy water, you throw it as a Strike. It's a simple [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon with a range increment of 20 feet. Unlike an alchemical bomb, it doesn't add the [manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=639) trait to the attack made with it.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You drink the _fu water_ to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] ailments within yourself. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check with a +9 counteract modifier and a counteract level of 3 against one effect that imposes the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition. You are then [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].
