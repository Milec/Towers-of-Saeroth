# Swirling Sand  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 72

**Price** 52 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

_Swirling sand_ carries a faint trace of strange compulsions from the helical sand spire near Beachcomber. Adding this catalyst to a [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]] spell implants a strange compulsion in one target of the spell. The target creature must spin counterclockwise at the end of its turn if it didn't take a move action that turn. This spin is a free action that has the [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] trait. This effect lasts for 3 rounds on a success, failure, or critical failure against suggestion (even if the target completes its suggestion in fewer rounds); a target that critically succeeds against suggestion is unaffected by the _swirling sand_.
