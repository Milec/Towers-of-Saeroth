# Soulcutter  *Item 15*

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Elven Curve Blade (Player Core)|Elven Curve Blade]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Astral|astral]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Elven Curve Blade (Player Core)|elven curve blade]]_ was the treasured weapon of the [[Setting/Deities/Calistria (Divine Mysteries)|Calistrian]] witch Silisifex, who played several key roles in reclaiming Kyonin from Tanglebriar when the elves returned to Golarion. Although a witch, her mesmerizing skill with the curved blade rivaled that of many soldiers. Her reasons for leaving Soulcutter behind in Kyonin before her final mission into Tanglebriar in an attempt to purify Deathstalk Tower are unknown.

As long as you carry _Soulcutter_, you gain its potency bonus as an item bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. This bonus increases by 2 against [[Traits/Possession (Player Core)|possession]] effects.

**Activate—Soothe Souls** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You whirl _Soulcutter_ in the air around you, rejuvenating the living within a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you while castigating those in that area who have no place in nature. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this activation for up to 1 minute. Living creatures that start their turn in the area regain 1d8 Hit Points, and any [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature that starts its turn in the area takes 1d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage.

**Activate—Soulcutting Storm** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You swing _Soulcutter_ and cast a 7th-rank [[Spells/Weapon Storm (Player Core)|_weapon storm_]] to your specifications, but all damage caused by the spell is spirit damage. If used to damage a creature that’s possessing another creature, this spell does no damage to the possessed creature.
