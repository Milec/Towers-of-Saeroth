---

The blade of this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|low-grade silver]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ has a sickly red tinge. Though once used to send souls to empower [[Setting/Deities/Kugaptee]], the fury of those slain by the blade now allows its wielder to periodically strike back against [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]].

**Activate—Avenging Soul** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You score a critical hit against a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]];  **Effect** Vengeful echoes of sacrificed souls lance into the psyche of the fiend struck. After the normal effects of the critical hit, the target must roll a DC 18 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature becomes distracted by vengeful spirits and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is slowed 1 for 1 minute.
