# Phistophilus Fiddle  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 205

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

Made of pure gold with platinum strings, the _phistophilus fiddle_ shouldn't be able to play a note—but play it does, so beautifully that it can move almost any audience. The fiddle was stolen from the [[Bestiary/Phistophilus|contract devil]] who created it, and the cunning fiend still wants to reclaim the instrument. Fiendish misfortunes often befall its owners, allowing it to switch hands. The fiddle is a handheld [[Equipment/Musical Instrument|virtuoso instrument]].
