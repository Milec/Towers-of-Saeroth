# Cassisian Helmet  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 151

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

A small, feathered wing is attached to either side of this ornate brass helmet. A visor on the front lowers to cover your face. While wearing the _cassisian helmet_, you gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saves against [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creatures and effects.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Lowering the visor, you send out eye beams that deal your choice of 2d6 cold or fire damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 15-foot line.
