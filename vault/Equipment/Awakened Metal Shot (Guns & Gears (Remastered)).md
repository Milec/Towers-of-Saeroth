# Awakened Metal Shot  *Item 17+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 168

**Ammunition** round

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

These bullets are formed from a liquefied high-grade precious metal and enchanted to unlock that metal's true potential. Each version has a different special effect.

## Awakened Metal Shot (Awakened Adamantine Shot)  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 168

**Price** 3,500 gp

---

The shot is a high-grade [[Equipment/Adamantine|adamantine]] bullet. The awakened adamantine breaks through any resistance. The shot ignores the first 20 resistance a creature has to physical damage, all damage, or piercing or bludgeoning damage. Against a [[Bestiary/Families/Golem|golem]] or creature who already has a resistance bypassed by adamantine, or a weakness to adamantine, the shot shatters the target's defenses, causing them to become [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 minute instead. Because adamantine is uncommon, this version of _awakened metal shot_ is uncommon even for gunslingers or other characters with access to uncommon guns and bullets from this book.

## Awakened Metal Shot (Awakened Cold Iron Shot)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 168

**Price** 2,300 gp

---

The shot is a high-grade [[Equipment/Cold Iron|cold iron]] bullet. The awakened cold iron attempts to disrupt [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantments]] the target placed on others' minds. You can name a creature you believe to be enchanted by your target or allow the shot to choose an enchanted creature randomly. On a hit, the shot attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the enchantment the target is using to manipulate that creature's mind. The counteract level is 9, and the counteract modifier is +27. If you fail (but don't critically fail) the counteract check against a [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|demon]] or [[Traits/Fey|fey]], you get a success instead. If you hit a demon or fey with no active enchantment effects on other creatures, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute instead.

## Awakened Metal Shot (Awakened Silver Shot)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 168

**Price** 2,300 gp

---

The shot is a high-grade [[Equipment/Silver|silver]] bullet. The awakened silver gleams with altered celestial moonlight designed to weaken and expose [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devils]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Werecreature|werecreatures]]. On a hit, it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect on the target. The counteract level is 9, and the counteract modifier is +27. If you fail (but don't critically fail) the counteract check against a devil or a werecreature, you get a success instead. If you counteracted the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]] ability, the creature can't use that ability again for 1 round, or 1 minute on a critical success. If you hit a devil or werecreature not under a polymorph effect, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute instead.
