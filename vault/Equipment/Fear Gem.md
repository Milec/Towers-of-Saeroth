# Fear Gem  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 566

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

two actions

Intimidating Strike

---

Dark smoke seems to writhe within this obsidian gem. When you activate the gem, you make an [[Feats/Intimidating Strike]], as the fighter feat.

 If you have the Intimidating Strike feat, increase the frightened condition value from this Intimidating Strike to frightened 2, or frightened 3 on a critical hit.
