This elixir bolsters your body's natural defenses and ability to resist maladies that travel through or affect blood. For 10 minutes you receive the listed resistance to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed and persistent poison damage]], and you lower the DC for any flat checks to end persistent bleed or persistent poison damage as if you received particularly appropriate aid.

At the GM's discretion, blood booster elixirs can also automatically [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] non-magical effects that specifically rely on thinning the drinker's blood, such as a [[Bestiary/Skull Peeler|skull peeler's]] anticoagulant.
