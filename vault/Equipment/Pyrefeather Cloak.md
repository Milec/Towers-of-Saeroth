The red and dark-orange feathers of a [[Bestiary/Pyrefowl|pyrefowl]] have been woven into a beautiful cloak.

**Activate—Deflect Flame** ⤾ ([manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2229))  **Trigger** You would be affected by a [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] effect;  **Effect** You flourish the cloak, using the feathers to reduce the incoming heat. You gain fire resistance 5 and a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against the triggering effect until the beginning of your next turn. If you are taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], you can immediately attempt a flat check to end the persistent damage.
