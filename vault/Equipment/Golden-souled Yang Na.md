# Golden-souled Yang Na  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 243

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

The traditions of these large tattoos, often stretching over the back or shoulders, have been passed from Tang Mai’s tree-keeping priests to their students across Tian Xia.

**Activate—Soul Blessing** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take an action with the rage, stance, or tandem trait;  **Effect** The tattoo purifies your mind and body, reducing your [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition by 1.**Special** While you are under the effect of [[Actions/Rage (Player Core 2)|Rage]], the Soul Blessing action gains the [[Traits/Rage (Player Core 2)|rage]] trait.
