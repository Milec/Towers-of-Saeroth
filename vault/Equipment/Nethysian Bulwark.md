This round shield is often fashioned to resemble the religious symbol of its dual-natured namesake. A _Nethysian bulwark_ is a [[Equipment/Cold Iron Shield|high-grade cold iron]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|shield]] (Hardness 10, HP 40, BT 20) that defends the wielder and can explode with retributive force when struck.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** The shield becomes broken when performing a [[Feats/Shield Block]];  **Effect** The shield explodes in a burst of bright light and shadowy tendrils, releasing a 15-foot cone of force that must include the attacker if possible. The cone deals 6d8 force damage to all creatures in the area (DC 34 basic Reflex save).
