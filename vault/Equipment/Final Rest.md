# Final Rest  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 19

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword]]

---

This _+3 [[Equipment/Disrupting|greater disrupting]] [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_ is made from the purest silver. The blade is carefully etched to depict a vast and sprawling necropolis, a place where the dead are laid to rest. Whenever you critically hit an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature with _final rest_, the undead creature takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent good damage]], with a DC 36 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** No effect.  

**Success** The creature takes half the persistent good damage.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and takes the full persistent good damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned 3 and takes double the persistent good damage.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold the sword aloft, making it cast [[Spells/Sunburst|_sunburst_]] (DC 38 Reflex save).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 4,950 gp of [[Equipment/Silver|silver]].
