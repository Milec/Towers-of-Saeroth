# Undead Scourge  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

---

An undead scourge is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ with a bluish-white metal blade that emits a faint glow. These used to be weapons of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasmin]] undead slayers only, but the slayers have since shared the secrets of their creation with the Knights of Lastwall.

**Activate—Sever from the Void** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]])  **Trigger** You hit and damage an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature with an _undead scourge_;  **Effect** You disrupt the undead’s connection to void energy. For 1 minute, the undead damaged with the dagger can’t be healed by void energy unless the effect attempting to heal the undead first [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteracts]] the undead scourge’s effect, which has a counteract rank of 4th and a DC of 25. Vitality energy still has the usual effects on the undead.
