**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

Aether marbles create violent, lingering eddies of force that extend partially into unseen planes. When the bomb explodes, it deals the listed [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage and force splash damage. Many aether marbles grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

Surfaces in the splash area are filled with twisting eddies of force, which are [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures but not for corporeal ones. The force eddies in a square disperse after an incorporeal creature enters the square.

## Lesser Aether Marbles  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 235

**Price** 19 gp

---

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 235
