# Dimensional Cleavestone  *Item 14*

single action

---

The edges of this irregular hunk of obsidian seem to shimmer. While wielding a weapon under the effect of a _dimensional cleavestone_, you gain the Tear Rift action for 1 minute.

**Tear Rift** two actions (magical, manipulate, teleportation) You carve a rent in space in an adjacent square, choosing another square within 30 feet to connect with your rift. A visible slash appears in both locations, displaying the view from the other side. Until the end of your next turn, these two squares are treated as adjacent to each other; for example, creatures could [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or be [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] through the rifts to their other side, and a creature adjacent to one rift can Strike a creature adjacent to the other. When Tearing a Rift, you can choose to [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] through the rift as part of the activity.

The rifts don’t automatically pull creatures or objects through, as they take up only a small amount of space within the square, and a creature sharing a space with a rift is unaffected except for determining adjacency. You can have only one rift active at a time.
