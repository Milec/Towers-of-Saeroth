# Life Salt  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 20

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

This crystal vial contains salts blessed by channeling life-giving energies into waters purified by holy fire. You can activate this vial by throwing it as a Strike. It's a simple [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] ranged weapon with a range increment of 10 feet. Unlike an alchemical bomb, it doesn't add the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait to the attack made with it. On a hit against an [[Traits/Undead|undead]], life salt deals 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent positive damage]], and the undead must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be unable to attack you as long as it continues taking the persistent positive damage. If you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against any undead, this second effect ends, and the undead can attack you normally.
