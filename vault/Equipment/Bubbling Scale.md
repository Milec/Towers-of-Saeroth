# Bubbling Scale  *Item 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers

**Source** [[Sources/Threshold of Knowledge]] pg. 10

**Price** 5 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This dark, iridescent scale is about the size of a small coin. When you swallow the scale, you immediately grow a patch of scales that cover the majority of your body. For 1 hour, you can hold your breath for 15 rounds plus your Constitution modifier (instead of 5 rounds + your Constitution modifier) before [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|drowning]]. It has no effect in non-aquatic environments that require you to hold your breath. After this time, the scales wither and fall off your body.
