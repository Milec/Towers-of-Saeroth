# Antifungal Salve  *Item 1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 77

**Price** 1 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This foul-smelling pink paste is traditionally kept in a tightly sealed jar. Spreading the salve on exposed skin grants an item bonus to saving throws against all [[Rules/Afflictions (GM Core)|afflictions]] that have the [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] trait or that originate from creatures with the fungus trait. The bonus lasts for 6 hours.
