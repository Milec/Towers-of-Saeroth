# Fulu Of Concealment  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 218

**Price** 40 gp

**Duration** 1 hour

**Usage** affixed to the ground in four spaces along a 10-foot radius circle

---

This fulu comes in four pieces, one placed in each cardinal direction. Choose one of the following traits when Activating the fulus: [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]], [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]], or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. Those within the fulus’ circle upon activation (including the fulus themselves) gain the effects of _[[Spells/Shared Invisibility|_shared invisibility_]]_, except that the area is the fulus’ circle instead of an [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] and the invisibility is only effective against creatures with the chosen trait. If any of the fulus are moved or destroyed after activation, the effect ends.
