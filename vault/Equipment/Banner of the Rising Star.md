# Banner of the Rising Star  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 139

**Price** 60,000 gp

**Usage** affixed or held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A single pale star shines bravely amid the dark cloth of this magical banner. The star can be seen even in the dead of night. While holding a _banner of the rising star_, you can use the following ability.

**Activate—Rise Up** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** The magical banner lifts your allies from the brink of death. An ally within the banner’s aura with the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition regains 30 Hit Points, does not increase their [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition, and can [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action. They become immune to Rise Up for 1 day.
