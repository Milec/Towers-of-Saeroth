This clay vessel launches gobs of clinging mud and grit. A mud bomb deals the listed bludgeoning damage and [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the start of your next turn. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
