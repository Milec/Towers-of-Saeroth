# The Survivor  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 77

**Usage** carried

---

_The Survivor_ represents rebirth through ordeal. When you have _The Survivor_ invested, you gain [[Feats/Diehard]] as a bonus feat. If you already possess this feat, you gain a +2 item bonus to all recovery checks. This item bonus increases to +3 if you are at least 17th-level.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** you are reduced to 0 Hit Points;  **Effect** You somehow manage to survive what could have been a fatal blow. Instead of being reduced to 0 Hit Points, you drop to 1 Hit Point instead and immediately restore an additional 4d8+16 Hit Points. The amount of Hit Point restored increases to 5d8+20 if you are at least 15th level, 6d8+24 if you're at least 17th level, and 7d8+28 if you're at least 19th level.
