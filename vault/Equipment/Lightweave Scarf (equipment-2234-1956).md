# Lightweave Scarf  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 127

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

The first of these strips of glittering cloth was worn by a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-178)|monk]] from Jinin who would interweave it into his [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows|_handwraps of mighty blows_]]. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 24.

- **Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]] and [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]].
- **Weapon** ([[Traits/Visual|visual]]) After you cast an illusion spell by activating the scarf, the weapon is shrouded in a mesmerizing illusory pattern. Your next Strike causes the target to be [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 round if it hits. If you don't make a Strike by the end of your next turn, the illusion ends with no effect.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Light (Player Core)|_light_]].
