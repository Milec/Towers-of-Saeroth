---

The head of this imposing steel _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|morningstar]]_ is shaped to resemble a cluster of snarling orc faces, their sharpened tusks serving as the spikes. Forged deep in the Hold of Belkzen and wielded by elite warriors tasked with protecting their lands from the servants of the Whispering Tyrant, this brutal weapon grants void [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to any living creature who wields it.

**Activate—Untouchable Spirit** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail or critically fail a saving throw against an effect originating from an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature that would inflict void damage;  **Effect** Reroll the saving throw and take the better result.
