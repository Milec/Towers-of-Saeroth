# Dragontooth Leiomano  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 36

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Leiomano (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leiomano]]

---

Dragon teeth line the edges of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Leiomano (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leiomano]]_ . The leiomano deals an additional 1d6 damage of a type determined by the tradition of the dragon from which the teeth were taken: [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]], or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]]. The weapon also gains the relevant trait (for instance, fire for a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]] made with teeth taken from an [[Bestiary/Young Adamantine Dragon|adamantine dragon]]).

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You swing the leiomano, sending several of the dragon teeth shooting through the air on jets of energy. The dragon teeth deal 3d6 piercing damage and 3d6 damage of the damage type corresponding to the dragon’s tradition in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Reflex save). The teeth hunt down their targets, correcting their flight in midair, which reduces any circumstance bonus from [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] by 2.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include teeth from a dragon.
