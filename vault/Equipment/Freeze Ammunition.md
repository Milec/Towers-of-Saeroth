# Freeze Ammunition  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 42

**Price** 28 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Freeze ammunition carries chilling reagents that activate on contact with the target. A creature hit by activated freeze ammunition takes [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage instead of the weapon’s normal damage type, plus 2 cold [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage. Hitting a 5-foot‑square surface successfully with freeze ammunition deals 2 cold splash damage and covers the space in a layer of ice. Each creature standing on the icy surface must succeed at a DC 20 Reflex save or [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check or else fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. Creatures using an action to move onto the icy surface must attempt either a Reflex save or an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]]. Creatures that [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] or [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]] don’t need to attempt a check or save. The ice melts after 1 minute, although unusually [[Rules/Temperature (Kingmaker Companion Guide)|hot or cold temperatures]] can change this duration at the GM’s discretion. Dealing at least 1 point of fire damage to the ice removes it instantly.
