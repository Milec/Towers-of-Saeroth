# Hydration Cloak  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 108

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This cloak is made of turquoise scales that absorb moisture, hydrating the wearer. After being fully submerged in water for 1 hour, the cloak allows aquatic creatures, such as [[Bestiary/Families/Azarketi (Monster Core)|azarketis]], to stay out of water for up to 1 week without penalties, after which it must be submerged again.

**Activate—Pull Hood** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull the cloak’s hood up and gain the effects of a 2nd-rank _[[Spells/Water Walk (Player Core)|water walk]]_ until you pull the hood down, to a maximum of 10 minutes.
