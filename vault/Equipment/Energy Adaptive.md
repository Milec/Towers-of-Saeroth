# Energy Adaptive  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 12

**Price** 2,600 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

A complex pattern of protective symbols gives protection against various forms of energy, but only part of the runic structure can be active at a given time.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You take [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage;  **Effect** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to the triggering damage type. This doesn’t apply to the triggering damage. This resistance lasts until you Activate this rune again or the armor is no longer invested by you.
