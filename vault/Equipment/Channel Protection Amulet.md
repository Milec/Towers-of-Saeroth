# Channel Protection Amulet  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 608

**Price** 56 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This nugget of polished tektite is trapped in a cage of braided wire and hangs from a silken cord. When wearing this amulet, you gain resistance 5 against damage from [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spells if you’re living, or against [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spells if you’re undead.
