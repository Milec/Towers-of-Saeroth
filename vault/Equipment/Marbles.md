# Marbles  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 58

**Price** 2 sp

**Hands** 1

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

These tiny round balls are made of polished stone and colorful glass, and come in a bag of 200. You can pour marbles in an empty square adjacent to you with an Interact action. The first creature that moves into that square must succeed at a DC 13 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save (its choice) or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. Once a creature enters a space with marbles, enough marbles are scattered that other creatures moving into that space don't need to attempt a roll to avoid falling.
