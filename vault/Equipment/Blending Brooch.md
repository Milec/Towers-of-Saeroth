free action

envision

**Trigger** You roll initiative using [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] and can see a creature

**Requirement** You are a master in Stealth

---

This small, matte-black pin always seems to be on the periphery of your vision, even when you stare directly at it. When you Activate the talisman, choose one creature you can see. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] to that creature unless it succeeds at a DC 28 Will save. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until the target hits you with an attack, whichever comes first.
