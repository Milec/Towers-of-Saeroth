# Hundred Stings  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 215

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Golden bee-shaped charms with deceptively sharp stingers feature along the length of this gold chain, which often draws blood when handled improperly. When used as a catalyst to cast an _[[Spells/Animated Assault (Player Core 2)|_animated assault_]]_ spell, you can change the damage type to piercing.
