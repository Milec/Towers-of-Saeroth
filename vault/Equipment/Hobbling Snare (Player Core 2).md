# Hobbling Snare  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 299

**Price** 15 gp

---

You rig vines, ropes, or wires to cinch tight around a creature that triggers this snare. The first creature to enter the square must attempt a DC 20 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 minute or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 18).  

**Failure** As success, but the penalty is –10 feet.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the penalty is –20 feet.
