# Ghost Charge  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Positive]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 253

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

These spring-loaded metal canisters contain a mixture of chemicals and salts that drain and disintegrate nearby [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures. A ghost charge deals the listed positive damage and splash damage, though as usual for positive damage, this damage harms only undead and creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]]. Ghost charges are designed to explode even on contact with a spiritual substance, making them ideal for damaging [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] undead. A primary target that takes damage from a ghost charge becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] until the start of your next turn. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
