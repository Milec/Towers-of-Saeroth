# Nightmare's Lament  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 105

**Price** 230 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier Pistol (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Rapier Pistol]]

---

Ritually constructed from the bones of a [[Bestiary/Nightmare (Monster Core)|nightmare]], a _nightmare’s lament_ is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier Pistol (Ranged) (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|rapier pistol]]_. Thin wisps of dark smoke continually surround the redflecked blade.

**Activate—Fuming Cloak** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For 1 minute, the _nightmare’s lament_ continually vents thick, black smoke that creates [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] in a 15-foot aura around the gun’s wielder. The wielder of the nightmare’s lament can see through this smoke. A creature that begins its turn in the area becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2 (DC 23 Fortitude negates) and is then temporarily immune to the sickness from the smoke for 1 minute. The wielder of the _nightmare’s lament_, any creature currently holding its breath (or that does not need to breathe), and any creature immune to poison are immune to the aura’s sickened effect but not the concealment.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the rib bones and inner fire of a [[Bestiary/Nightmare (Monster Core)|nightmare]].
