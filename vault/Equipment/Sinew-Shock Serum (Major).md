This serum jolts the body to help shake off physical debilitations. When you drink this elixir, it attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check with the listed counteract modifier to remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition from a single source, using the source of that condition to determine the counteract level and DC.
