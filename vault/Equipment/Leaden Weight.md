# Leaden Weight  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 215

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

This simple lead weight is inscribed with Petran runes. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Gravity Well (Player Core 2)|_gravity well_]]_ spell, the lead sublimates into an acrid gas, which then clings to the targets’ legs and feet. A creature that fails its saving throw is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] after it moves.
