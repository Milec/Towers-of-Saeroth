Derived from duskwood sap tapped during the winter solstice, this toxin erodes the connection between body and soul, tricking the latter into assuming the former has already died. Survivors of this near-death experience report ominous tunnel vision, as if the [[Bestiary/Families/Grim Reaper]] lurks in their peripheral vision and awaits their final breath.

**Saving Throw** DC 30 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d12 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d12 void damage and doomed 1 for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d12 void damage and doomed 1 (1 round); **Stage 4** 3d12 void damage and doomed 2 (1 round)
