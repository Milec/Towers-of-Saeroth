Candlaron the Sculptor is one of Kyonin’s most storied and honored wizards. While his final fate is unknown, his greatest creations, the aiudara, remain behind as a legacy of his power—as do other potent items, such as the _Guiding Star Orb_, a navigational traveling aid that the heroic wizard often relied upon when venturing into an unexplored part of the world.

**Activate—Embed Location**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** By focusing on the _Guiding Star Orb_, you embed your current location in the item. Thereafter, anyone who holds the _Guiding Star Orb_ while [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Casting a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait to travel to this location arrives precisely, without any inaccuracy at all. The _Guiding Star Orb_ can only have one location embedded at a time; if you use this activity a second time, the new location replaces the previous one.

**Activate—Momentary Aiudara**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cause a shimmering magical archway to appear next to you as the _Guiding Star Orb_ casts a 7th-rank [[Spells/Teleport (Player Core)|_teleport_]] to your specifications. If you are teleporting to an aiudara you’ve visited before, you and the targets appear precisely at that location.
