# Setup Snare  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Kobold]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -193- Mantle of Gold|Pathfinder #193: Mantle of Gold]] pg. 85

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The DC of this item is 18_

**Price** 8 gp

---

This snare is designed to divert a target's movement towards another snare or hazard. You may choose to have the target attempt a Will saving throw instead of a Reflex saving throw; if you do, add the fear and mental traits plus add either the visual or auditory trait.  
 When a Small or larger creature enters the square, the snare Pushes the creature 5 feet and the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] 1 until the end of their next turn. On a critical failure the snare Pushes the target 10 feet and the target becomes clumsy 2 until the end of their next turn.
