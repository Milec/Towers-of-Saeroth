# Gyroscopic Stabilizer  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Outlaws of Alkenstar

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -179- Cradle of Quartz|Pathfinder #179: Cradle of Quartz]] pg. 77

**Price** 115 gp

**Usage** attached to a two-handed firearm

**Bulk** L

---

A _gyroscopic stabilizer_ appears as a gold disk suspended within three rotating golden bands inside a gilded case, itself attached to a set of clamps. These clamps allow you to attach the _gyroscopic stabilizer_ to any one-handed firearm as an Interact action.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;  **Requirements** The _gyroscopic stabilizer_ is attached to a one-handed firearm without the [[Traits/Fatal|fatal]] or [[Traits/Fatal Aim (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|fatal aim]] traits; **Effect** The gyroscopic stabilizer begins spinning for 1 minute. While the gyroscopic stabilizer is spinning, the weapon it's attached to gains the fatal aim weapon trait with a die one size larger than its damage die (maximum d12). This allows you to wield it in two hands to grant it the fatal trait.
