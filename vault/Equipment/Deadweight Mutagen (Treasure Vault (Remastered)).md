# Deadweight Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 59

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Your joints loosen and bones thicken, making your body incredibly weighty and difficult to maneuver around.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Shove]] and [[Actions/Trip]], to your Fortitude and Reflex DCs against attempts to Shove or Trip you, and to saving throws against effects that attempt to force you to [[Rules/Forced Movement|move]] or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

**Drawback** You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered|encumbered]] condition and can't remove it while under the effects of the mutagen.
