# Persona Mask  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 613

**Usage** worn mask

---

Despite covering the entire face, this alabaster mask does not hinder vision or other senses. Wearing the mask grants a +1 item bonus to Performance checks while acting, orating, performing comedy, or singing.

**Activate** single action envision;     **Effect** You change the mask’s appearance into an artistic rendition of a dramatic character of your choice.
