# Blade Launcher  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 64

**Price** 18 gp

**Hands** 2

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

A blade launcher has a bow-like assembly mounted on a handled frame. The frame can be configured to fire either a [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Dart|dart]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Starknife|starknife]]. Configuring a blade launcher requires three Interact actions. Once properly configured, loading an appropriate [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon into a blade launcher requires a single Interact action. A blade launcher can't fire weapons for which it's not currently configured. A weapon fired with a blade launcher loses the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Monk|monk]], [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]], and [[Traits/Versatile|versatile]] traits, if it has them, and has a range increment of 40 feet. Due to losing the thrown weapon trait, [[Equipment/Returning|_returning_]] and most other effects that allow a weapon to return don't function; even if a launched weapon did return, you'd still need to load it into the blade launcher with an Interact action to fire the blade launcher again.
