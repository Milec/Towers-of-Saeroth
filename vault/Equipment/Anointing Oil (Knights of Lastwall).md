# Anointing Oil  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 88

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Access** Knights of Lastwall have access to this item.

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Carried by many Knights of Lastwall, this amber-colored, fragrant-smelling oil is meant to prevent those who fall in battle from rising as [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. Applying anointing oil to a corpse casts [[Spells/Gentle Repose|_gentle repose_]] on it. The effects last for 24 hours. The oil is repugnant to the undead. An undead creature that touches a corpse treated with this oil is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] until the contact is broken or the oil's effect wears off.
