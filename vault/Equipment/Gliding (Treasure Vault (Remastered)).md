# Gliding  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 12

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

The armor allows you to make a controlled descent.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** You glide slowly toward the ground, 5 feet down and up to 25 feet forward through the air. Provided you spend at least 1 action gliding on your turn and haven't yet reached the ground, you remain in the air at the end of your turn. Otherwise, you fall.
