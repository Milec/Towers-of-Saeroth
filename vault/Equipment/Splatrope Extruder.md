# Splatrope Extruder  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 65

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This portable nozzle-and-trigger assembly based on spider spinnerets can extrude and weave alchemical adhesives into temporary constructions. As an Interact action, you can attach a [[Equipment/Glue Bomb|glue bomb]] to the extruder.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** A glue bomb is installed in the extruder; **Effect** The extruder converts the glue bomb into a 30-foot [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]], or [[Equipment/Net (Player Core)|net]], depending on the nozzle die you choose when activating the device. The created object lasts for 1 hour. The DC to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] a created rope (if used to bind a creature) or net is equal to the consumed glue bomb’s DC and Escaping destroys the created object.
