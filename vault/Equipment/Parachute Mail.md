# Parachute Mail  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 122

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail (Player Core)|Chain Mail]]

---

Favored by soldiers deployed on airships, this chain mail has a built-in parachute connected to the armor itself, with an additional harness to be worn underneath. It takes 10 minutes and a successful DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check to successfully repack the parachute.

**Activate—Deploy Parachute** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once until repacked; **Trigger** You are [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falling]];  **Effect** Once activated, the parachute will fully deploy within 1 round. If it is deployed at a height greater than 100 feet, the parachute will prevent you from receiving falling damage. If deployed at a height of less than 100 feet, it will reduce the damage taken from falling by half.
