# Reflecting Shield  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 234

**Price** 18,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|Buckler]]

---

This high-grade silver buckler (Hardness 6, HP 24, BT 12) is polished to a mirrorlike sheen. The shield functions as a [[Equipment/Spellguard Shield|spellguard shield]] that can also reflect spells.

**Activate—Reflect Spell** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're targetd by a spell; **Requirements** The _reflecting shield is raised_; **Effect** You attempt to reflect the spell on its caster. The shield attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the spell, with a counteract rank of 9th and a counteract modifier of +30. If the spell is successfully counteracted, it's turned back on its caster.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include at least 2,750 gp of silver.
