# Forge Warden  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 587

**Price** 975 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield]]

---

The religious symbol of [[Setting/Deities/Torag]], the forge god—an ornate hammer of dwarven construction—adorns the face of this reinforced [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|steel shield]] (Hardness 10, HP 40, BT 20). The shield is a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of Torag.

 You and any adjacent allies have fire resistance 5 while you have the shield raised. When used for a [[Feats/Shield Block]], the _forge warden_ rings out like the hammer strike of a blacksmith, and the symbol glows as if lit by the fires of a furnace.

**Activate** free action command ([[Traits/Fire|fire]])  **Trigger** You use the forge warden to Shield Block an adjacent creature’s attack and the shield takes damage;  **Effect** The attacking creature takes 2d6 fire damage.
