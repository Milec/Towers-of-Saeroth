Cast a Spell

---

A _gravemist taper_ is a conical candle with symbols of terror and death carved into the wax. The taper can be used as a catalyst when casting an obscuring mist spell, burning the taper away, coloring the mist gray, and filling the mist with ghastly, shadowy shapes. The [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to overcome the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] state from the mist rises to 7, and a creature who fails such a check becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. This aspect of the spell has the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits.
