# Shark Tooth Charm  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 267

**Price** 23 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

---

This dried-seaweed bracelet is lined with charms shaped like small shark teeth. When you activate the bracelet, attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] using Acrobatics with a +1 item bonus to the check. If you roll a success, you get a critical success instead (if you roll a critical failure, you get a failure instead). If you fail the Acrobatics check against a grabbing creature, the creature must either release you as a free action or take 2d8 piercing damage as shark's teeth momentarily emerge from your skin.
