# Sleeves of Storage  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 263

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

---

This loose robe has wide, voluminous sleeves that each contain an extradimensional space. These spaces each function as a [[Equipment/Bag of Holding|_bag of holding_]] that can hold up to 5 Bulk of items (for a total of 10 Bulk), though no individual item can be of more than 1 Bulk; the sleeves grow slightly heavy as you reach maximum capacity. You can add or remove an item from a sleeve with a single hand free as an Interact action.

 If a sleeve is completely empty, you can place your own familiar into that extradimensional space. It can survive comfortably in your sleeve for up to 1 hour, after which it begins to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocate]]. While in your sleeve, it can't be affected or targeted by any effects, but you don't benefit from any master abilities. A familiar can exit the sleeve of its own volition with a single action that has the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] and [[Traits/Move|move]] traits. You can't place any other creature into your sleeves, nor can you place your familiar in a sleeve if it's larger than Tiny. If your familiar is in your sleeve, you can't place any items in the sleeve.
