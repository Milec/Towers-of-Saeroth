# Pontoon  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 144

**Price** 650 gp

**Usage** applied to footwear

---

Footwear with pontoon runes allows you to traverse waterways with ease. While wearing footwear with the rune, you can walk on the surface of water and other liquids without falling through. This offers no protection against damage the liquid deals. You can go under the liquid’s surface, but you must [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] if you do.
