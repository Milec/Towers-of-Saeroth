# Winged Boots  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 617

**Price** 850 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

Made from soft leather, with delicate white wings attached to the heel, these boots are ensorcelled with powerful air magic. Whenever you fall while wearing these boots, the boots automatically cast [[Spells/Feather Fall|_feather fall_]] on you. This benefit can’t trigger again for 10 minutes.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You speak a command word and click the boots’ heels together to cause the wings to animate and flap rapidly, granting you a fly Speed of 30 feet for 10 minutes.
