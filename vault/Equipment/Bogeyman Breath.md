This dust is created from the powdered bones of ensnared rabbits and other woodland creatures that died from fright. When inhaled, it pollutes the victim’s mind with fear, awakening an unshakable prey response that sees danger lurking in every shadow.

**Saving Throw** DC 28 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 round; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 mental damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1, and can’t reduce frightened value for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 2** 4d6 mental damage, frightened 2, and can’t reduce frightened value for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 3** 4d6 mental damage, frightened 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] the poison cloud for 1 round, and can’t reduce frightened value for 1 round (1 round)
