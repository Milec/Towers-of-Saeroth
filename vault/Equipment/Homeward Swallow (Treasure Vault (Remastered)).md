# Homeward Swallow  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 120

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This small tattoo of a swallow always points toward your home. The tattooing must take place at a location you consider to be your home, or the magic fails to bind with the ink. When you travel to your home using [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] that can be off target, such as [[Spells/Teleport (Player Core)|_teleport_]] or [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]], you arrive exactly at your home. If your home is destroyed or you come to believe a new place is your home, this tattoo fades from your skin.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** You sense the direction toward your home.
