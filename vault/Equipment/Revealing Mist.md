# Revealing Mist  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 55

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Kept in an airtight spray bottle, revealing mist is an alchemical concoction that creates a sticky and clinging mist of chemicals in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] when sprayed. It doesn’t affect visibility but causes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures in the area to be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]. Revealing mist is ineffective in water or in areas with other factors affecting the spread of the mist, as determined by the GM. It remains in the area for 1 minute or until any significant wind disperses it, whichever comes first.
