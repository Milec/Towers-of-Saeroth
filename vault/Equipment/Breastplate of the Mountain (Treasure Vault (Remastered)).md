# Breastplate of the Mountain  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 103

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate]]

---

Constructed of dull gray metal, this [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate|breastplate]] is decorated with the symbol of a craggy black mountaintop. This breastplate functions as a _+3 [[Equipment/Resilient|greater resilient]] breastplate_. When you're [[Actions/Shove|Shoved]] or otherwise [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced to move]], you can reduce the amount you move by up to 10 feet. When you invest the breastplate, you either increase your Constitution modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pound a solid and constant rhythm on your breastplate and cast a 7th-level [[Spells/Regenerate|_regeneration_]] spell on yourself.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a single tap, you gain immunity to [[Traits/Poison|poison]] for 1 minute.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail or critically fail a Fortitude saving throw;  **Effect** If you failed the saving throw, it becomes a success. If you critically failed, it becomes a failure instead.
