# Viperous Elixir  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 111

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Your teeth elongate into fangs with grooves that can channel a deadly venom. You gain a fangs unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage, unless you already have an unarmed bite attack that deals more damage. You gain the listed item bonus to unarmed attack and damage rolls when you [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your fangs or another bite attack.

## Viperous Elixir (Lesser)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 111

**Price** 25 gp

---

The bonus is +1 item, your fangs inflict [black adder venom](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2892), and the duration is 1 minute or until you hit and deal damage with your fangs, whichever comes first.

## Viperous Elixir (Moderate)  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 111

**Price** 85 gp

---

The bonus is +2, your fangs inflict [giant scorpion venom](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2899), and the duration is 10 minutes or until you hit and deal damage with your fangs twice, whichever comes first.

## Viperous Elixir (Greater)  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 111

**Price** 225 gp

---

The bonus is +3, your fangs inflict [wyvern poison](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2909), and the duration is 10 minutes or until you hit and deal damage with your fangs three times, whichever comes first.
