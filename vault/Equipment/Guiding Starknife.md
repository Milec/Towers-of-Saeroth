# Guiding Starknife  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 209

**Price** 325 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Starknife (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Starknife]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Starknife (Tian Xia Character Guide)|starknife]]_ is made of dark blue metal overlaid with smoky flecks of fused quartz. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] and deal damage, your next attack before the end of your turn with this starknife ignores [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|standard and lesser cover]].

**Activate—Points of Light** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with a thrown Strike with the _guiding starknife_; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The _guiding starknife_’s blades detach, form into darts of starlight, and deal 4 piercing damage plus 1d4 spirit damage to the creature you just hit. This piercing damage increases to 6 with a _greater striking_ rune and to 8 with a _major striking_ rune. The _guiding starknife_ then re-forms and returns to your hand. Until the start of your next turn, the damaged creature is outlined with soft light and can’t gain the effects of concealment.
