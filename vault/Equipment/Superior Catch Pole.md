# Catch Pole  *Item 0+*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 112

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** 1

---

This sturdy pole has a rope attached to one end in a loop with the other end extending to the handle. You can pull the handle side of the rope to tighten the loop. Using this loop, you can [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] without having a free hand. A creature grappled this way receives a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls when [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Striking]] with an unarmed attack. Due to limitations in the size of the loop, a catch pole can only be used on creatures sized Medium or smaller.

## Giant Catch Pole  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 112

**Price** 50 gp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** 2

---

A giant catch pole is made from thicker steel and heavier rope. It functions the same as a catch pole but can be used to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] creatures up to your normal size limit. However, the implement is so ungainly that you are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] while wielding it.

## Superior Catch Pole  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 112

**Price** 80 gp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** 1

---

A superior catch pole has a pole made of dawnsilver, and the loop at the end is silk rope, making it sturdier and more agile. It functions the same as a catch pole but gives you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks made to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] with it.
