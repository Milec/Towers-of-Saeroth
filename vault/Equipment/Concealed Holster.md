# Concealed Holster  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 179

**Price** 25 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This leather holster is crafted to better hide small firearms from view. Only firearms designed for use in one hand are small enough to disguise with this holster. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks and DCs to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|hide]] or [[Actions/Conceal an Object (Player Core)|conceal]] a firearm or hand crossbow in this holster.
