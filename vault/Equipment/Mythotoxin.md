**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Mythotoxin is exceedingly difficult to produce, as it requires fresh venom from a mythic creature that must then be distilled into a more concentrated form. [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic creatures]] take a –2 circumstance penalty to saves against mythotoxin.

**Saving Throw** DC 26 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d8 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d10 damage and lose 1 Mythic Point, or become [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 if you have no Mythic Points to lose (1 round; doomed condition value has normal duration)
