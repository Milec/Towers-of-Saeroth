# Quenching  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 13

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

This rune counters burning and corrosive agents. Armor with this rune reduces the DC of the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to end [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid or fire damage]] affecting you from 15 to 12 (7 with particularly effective assistance).
