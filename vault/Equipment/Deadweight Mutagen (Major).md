# Deadweight Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 59

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Your joints loosen and bones thicken, making your body incredibly weighty and difficult to maneuver around.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]], to your Fortitude and Reflex DCs against attempts to Shove or Trip you, and to saving throws against effects that attempt to force you to move or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

**Drawback** You gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]] condition and can’t remove it while under the effects of the mutagen.
