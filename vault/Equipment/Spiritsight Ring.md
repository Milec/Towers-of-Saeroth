# Spiritsight Ring  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 154, [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 81

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** worn

---

The opal set in the intricately carved ivory _spiritsight ring_ eventually becomes translucent and tickles your finger whenever an [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature is nearby. When in the presence of a nearby incorporeal creature, even if it's within a solid object, you eventually detect the creature, though you might not do so instantly, and you can't pinpoint the location. This acts as a [[Rules/Vague Senses|vague sense]], like humans' sense of smell. An incorporeal creature trying to hide its presence from this sense attempts a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check against your Perception DC to hide from your vague sense, as normal for attempting to foil [[Rules/Special Senses|special senses]]. You gain a +2 item bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] incorporeal creatures within 30 feet of you.
