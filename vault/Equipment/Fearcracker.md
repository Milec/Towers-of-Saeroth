# Fearcracker  *Item 5*

two actions

Cast a Spell

---

A _fearcracker_ contains fragments of broken mirrors and reagents that pop and smoke when consumed. When thrown down in your space as part of a [[Spells/Mirror Image|_mirror image_]] spell, your and your images’ appearance twists nightmarishly. When an image is destroyed, it “dies” in a disturbing fashion, rendering the attacker [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. This aspect of the spell has the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits.
