# Serpent Dagger  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 242

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

---

The serrated blade of this _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ has a greenish tinge, and the hilt is sculpted to look like the head of a serpent about to strike. When you critically succeed at an attack roll with the serpent dagger, the target becomes sickened 1 unless it succeeds at a DC 19 Fortitude save. This is a poison effect. In addition, you can activate the dagger to poison a creature with a more potent poison.

**Activate—Drip Poison** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You damage a creature with the _serpent dagger_;  **Effect** You poison the creature you hit with dagger venom.  
 **Dagger Venom** ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 21 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d8 poison damage and enfeebled 1
