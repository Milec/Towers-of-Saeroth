**Access** Second-mark members of the Firebrands have access to this item.

This satchel contains all the basic equipment found in a [[Equipment/Climbing Kit|climbing kit]] plus the specialized equipment—including a harness, belay device, and locking clips—needed for descents. When [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] down with a rappelling kit, you move twice as fast as usual based on your check result.
