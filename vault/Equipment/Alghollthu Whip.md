# Alghollthu Whip  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 35

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip]]

---

This fleshy _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]]_ is obviously crafted from the tentacle of some fearsome beast, likely an aqudel, and constantly strobes with small patterns of light.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** Your last action was a successful [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] with the _alghollthu whip_; **Effect** Patterns on the skin of the whip strobe in a rapid pulse. The creature your attack succeeded against must attempt a DC 23 Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is dazzled until the end of your next turn and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is dazzled until the end of your next turn and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 2.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a tentacle from an aqudel.
