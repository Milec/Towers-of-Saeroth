# Wand of Shrouded Step  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 142

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

When you move this delicately carved poplar wand, it looks indistinct, leaving a trail of afterimages in its wake.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Fleet Step (Player Core)|_fleet step_]]. For the duration of the spell, you’re also [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] while you [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]].

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Fleet Step (Player Core)|_fleet step_]].
