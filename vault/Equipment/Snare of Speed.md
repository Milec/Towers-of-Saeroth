# Snare of Speed  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 147

**Price** 7,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This snare drum and sticks are made from dark wood, and the skin is fine antelope hide. This drum grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Prestissimo** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You play the snare with an extreme cadence. You and all allies within a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition until the end of your next turn and can use the extra action each round for only [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] and [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] actions.

**Activate—Larghissimo** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You play the snare with a lassitude that drains the speed from your foes. Enemies within a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 34 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is slowed 2 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is slowed 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.
