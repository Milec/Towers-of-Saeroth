# Hauling  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 80

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

_Hauling_ weapons are adept at moving creatures around the battlefield after a successful attack.

**Activate** ⤾ Command;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You succeed at an attack roll to Strike with a weapon with the _hauling_ rune;  **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 20 Reflex save or be moved 5 feet in a direction you choose. This is [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]].
