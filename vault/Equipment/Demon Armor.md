# Demon Armor  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 557

**Price** 2,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate]]

---

Crafted from black iron, this crude suit of _+2 resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate|full plate]]_ is designed to make you look like a horned demon, with your face peering out of the screaming maw of the beast.

While wearing the armor, you can attack with the helmet’s horns. They are a martial melee weapon with the effects of a _+2 weapon potency rune_. They deal 2d8 piercing damage and have the deadly d12 trait. On a critical hit with the horns, the target must attempt a DC 30 Fortitude save against the [[Spells/Abyssal Plague|_Abyssal plague_ disease]]. The horns can’t be etched with any runes.

 If you aren’t evil, you’re drained 2 and can’t recover from this condition while wearing _demon armor_.

**Activate** two actions command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Dimension Door|_dimension door_]].

**Craft Requirements** You are evil; supply one casting of _dimension door_.
