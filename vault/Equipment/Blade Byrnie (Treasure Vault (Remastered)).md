# Blade Byrnie  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt]]

---

Instead of chain links, this _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ is assembled from metal “leaves” that each resemble a small blade.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You pull a link from the armor, which transforms into a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_. The dagger disappears and reappears as a link in the armor after you Strike with it, or at the end of this turn if you don't make a Strike. You can Activate the blade byrnie in place of an Interact action to draw a weapon for abilities such as the [[Feats/Quick Draw]] feat.

 Upgrading the runes on the _blade byrnie_ makes the daggers pulled from it more powerful. The daggers have a [[Equipment/Weapon Potency|_+2 weapon potency_]] rune if the armor has a [[Equipment/Armor Potency|_+2 armor potency_]] rune, or a _+3 weapon potency_ rune if the armor has a _+3 armor potency_ rune.
