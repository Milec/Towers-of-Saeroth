# The Theater  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Harrow Court]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -190- The Choosing|Pathfinder #190: The Choosing]] pg. 81

**Usage** carried

---

_The Theater_ represents prophecy and how prophets can't change the unfolding pageantry of fate. As long as you have _The Theater_ invested and as long as you are not already [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]], when you would gain the doomed condition, attempt a DC 10 flat check. On a success, you do not gain the doomed condition.

**Activate** ⤾ envision ([[Traits/Fortune|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You are targeted by a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect;  **Effect** _The Theater_ attempts to counter the misfortune effect before it affects you. It has a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] level equal to your level divided by 2 (rounded up), and a counteract modifier of your Class DC – 10.
