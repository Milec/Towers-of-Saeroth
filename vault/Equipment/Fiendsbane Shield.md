# Fiendsbane Shield  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 213

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|Shield Boss]]

---

This [[Equipment/Silver Shield (GM Core)|low-grade silver shield]] (Hardness 3, HP 12, BT 6) is equipped with a _+1 [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss (Player Core)|shield boss]]_ emblazoned with the image of a beheaded fiend. Any creature with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait that wields the shield becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 and can't recover from this condition while wielding the shield.

**Activate—Silvery Backlash** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] an attack from a creature with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait;  **Effect** The _fiendsbane shield's_ Hardness increases to 10, and the symbol of the beheaded demon lashes out in silvery ribbons, dealing 3d6 slashing damage (DC 19 basic Reflex save) to the creature whose attack you blocked. This damage is treated as silver for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, and the like.
