# Chiromantic Lines  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 242

**Price** 1,700 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

These tattoos boast minute, intricate inscriptions, inked with dust ground from the eyelashes of omen dragons. The process is incredibly painful, involving tattooing one’s pupils as well as one’s palm lines with an almost [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] series of tracings.

**Activate—Cunning Consultation** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], prediction) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** This tattoo becomes temporarily visible, allowing you to briefly grasp the very near future just enough to predict the subtle movements of those around you. Until the end of the turn, all creatures you can see that are not [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you.
