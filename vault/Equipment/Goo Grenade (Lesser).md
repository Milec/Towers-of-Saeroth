# Goo Grenade  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 306

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Access** Characters from Nex have access to this item.

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

A goo grenade is a flask filled with a fast-growing, short-lived alchemical ooze. When you hit a creature with a goo grenade, that creature takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]] and a circumstance penalty to its Speeds from the clinging goo.

 The target can end any penalties, conditions, and persistent damage caused by the bomb by [[Actions/Escape|Escaping]] or spending a total of 3 Interact actions to pry themselves free of the ooze. These Interact actions don't have to be consecutive, and other creatures can provide the actions as well.

## Goo Grenade (Lesser)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 306

**Price** 3 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The target takes 1d4 acid damage, 1 persistent acid damage, and 1 acid splash damage. They take a –5-foot circumstance penalty to their Speeds. The Escape DC is 15.
