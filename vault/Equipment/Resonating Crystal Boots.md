# Resonating Crystal Boots  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 149

**Price** 475 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

These supple leather boots are studded with tiny crystals.

**Activate—Chiming Steps** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed. The crystals ring out with pleasant-sounding chimes that reverberate painfully in the ears of others. Each creature that you pass adjacent to during your Stride takes 4d8 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save); a creature takes this damage only once. A creature who critically fails the save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 minute.
