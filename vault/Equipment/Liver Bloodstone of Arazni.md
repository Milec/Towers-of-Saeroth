# Liver Bloodstone of Arazni  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 109

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The _Liver Bloodstone of Arazni_ represents confidence. While holding the _Liver Bloodstone_, you feel more certain in your abilities.

**Activate—Embolden** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You critically succeed on an attack roll, saving throw, or skill check while in [[Rules/Encounter Mode (Player Core)|encounter mode]];  **Effect** You gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level for 1 round.

**Activate—Arazni's Fervor** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You worship [[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]] or are favored by her; **Effect** You hold the _Liver Bloodstone_ aloft, and the _Bloodstone_ glows with crimson light, filling you and your allies with Arazni’s wrath. The Liver Bloodstone casts [[Spells/Bless (Player Core)|_bless_]]. For the next minute, you can use a free action at the beginning of each of your turns to increase the spell’s [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] radius by 10 feet. You can’t increase the emanation’s radius in any other way.

**Destruction** The _Liver Bloodstone_ melts into a puddle of poison if a worshipper of Arazni willingly submerges the _Bloodstone_ in a lethal contact or consumed [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], then licks the _Bloodstone_ clean. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] is exposed to the poison that destroyed the _Bloodstone_, as if they’d touched or consumed that poison.
