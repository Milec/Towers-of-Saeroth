# Amnemonic Charm  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 92

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You fail or critically fail a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Coerce]], [[Actions/Lie]], [[Actions/Make an Impression]], or [[Actions/Request]]

**Requirement** You're a master in the skill you failed with.

---

This ornate locket, usually containing a faceless, humanoid portrait, is typically worn on a chain around the neck or attached to your clothing. When activated, the charm casts a DC 26 [[Spells/Rewrite Memory|_rewrite memory_]] spell against a single target of the check, removing all memory of your failed attempt and potentially allowing you to make another attempt. The effect can’t remove more than 3 minutes of memories from its target.
