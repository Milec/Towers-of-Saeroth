# Catoblepas Maul  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 101

**Price** 2,800 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Maul (Player Core)|Maul]]

---

The putrid stench emanating from the [[Bestiary/Catoblepas|catoblepas]] antlers adorning this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Crushing|greater crushing]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Maul (Player Core)|maul]]_ interferes with your sense of smell. While wielding this weapon, you gain a +2 item bonus to saves against [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] effects.

**Activate—Vile Stench** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]])   **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with the _catoblepas maul_; **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for as long as it’s sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save becomes temporarily immune to Vile Stench for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include antlers from a [[Bestiary/Catoblepas|catoblepas]].
