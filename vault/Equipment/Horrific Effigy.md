# Horrific Effigy  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 144

**Price** 7,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This blasphemous idol has the vague outline of a recumbent humanoid, but the more detail one perceives, the more its alien nature is revealed. The mere presence of the effigy causes disturbing dreams, and anyone who sleeps within 50 feet of the item must succeed at a DC 30 Will save or awaken [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]].

**Activate—Whispers of Leng**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You whisper a name and whatever details you recall about that person to the effigy, telling it everything you can about your target. The next time the target sleeps, they are subject to a [[Spells/Nightmare (Player Core)|_nightmare_]] spell (DC 30). This continues every time the target sleeps until they die, you whisper a new target to the effigy, or the target gets a critical success on their save against the _nightmare_.

**Activate—Smothering Lassitude** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You brandish the effigy aloft, exposing all who see it to its bizarre visage. You and all creatures within a 120-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 34 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 until the start of your next turn.  

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 until the start of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stupefied 2 and stunned 1 until the start of your next turn.
