([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

An _energizing treat_ is a treat made from meat or grains. When you feed your [[Rules/Animal Companions|animal companion]] or [[Rules/Familiars|familiar]] an energizing treat, it's [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 minute. It can use the extra action each round only for Strike, Stride, and [[Actions/Support]] actions, and it can do so only if it normally has those actions available and you take the proper action to command it.
