([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

**Trigger** A creature within 60 feet casts a summon spell

**Requirement** You have a free hand

---

This small doll has been carved from a fragment of Bloodstone. When you Activate a _bloodstone doll_ in response to another’s magic, you Interact to take it in hand and hold it up toward the triggering creature. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check against the triggering spell at the listed bonus and rank.

 If the spell would be counteracted, the _bloodstone doll_ instead influences the summoned creature. You gain control of the summoned creature and can dictate its 2 actions for that turn. The triggering creature then regains control of their summoned creature, but the summoned creature takes a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against you. Your _bloodstone doll_ shatters when the summon spell ends.
