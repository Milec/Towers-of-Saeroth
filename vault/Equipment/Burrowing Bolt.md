# Burrowing Bolt  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Ammunition** arrow, bolt

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

These arrows have tips grooved like a drill bit and angled fletching, causing them to spin quickly about their shaft when fired. When striking a structure or object of [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 14 or less within your first range increment, an activated _burrowing bolt_ tunnels into the surface silently and leaves a hole behind it, burrowing through up to 5 feet of material before magically vanishing.
