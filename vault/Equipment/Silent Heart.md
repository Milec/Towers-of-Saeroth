# Silent Heart  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Price** 130 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** Followers of Abraxas from Nex have access to this tattoo.

---

[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]] teaches that the true heart is impenetrable, dedicated only to knowledge. This tattoo of a venom-soaked heart provides a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] checks.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You're about to attempt a saving throw against an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect or an effect that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]];  **Effect** Abraxas envelops your heart in the shadow of his secret, granting you a +2 status bonus on your saving throw against the triggering effect.
