This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Katar (Tian Xia Character Guide)|katar]]_ masterfully crafted from the adamantine-laced fang of a [[Bestiary/Shuln (Monster Core)|shuln]] can puncture even the toughest of armors.

**Activate—Piercing Critical** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per round;  **Requirements** Your last action was to score a critical hit with the _shuln fang katar_ against a creature that is wearing armor; **Effect** You deal the same amount of damage as the critical hit to the creature’s armor, bypassing any [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] lower than 10, like adamantine.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a fang from a [[Bestiary/Shuln (Monster Core)|shuln]].
