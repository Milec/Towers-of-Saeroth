# The Kardosian Fragments  *Item 16*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 81

**Usage** held in 1 hand;

**Bulk** 1

---

In preparation for Earthfall, Runelord Zutha bound his soul to the _Gluttonous Tome_—an expansive work that contained the entirety of the runelord’s knowledge.

To ensure the tome survived the oncoming apocalypse, Zutha divided the book into three parts: the _Kardosian Codex_, the _Black Book_, and the _Bone Grimoire_. Heroes used the _Bone Grimoire_ on the risen isle of Xin 7 years ago to finally defeat Zutha, and in doing so, triggered the destruction of the three parts of the _Gluttonous Tome_.

Over the centuries, copies of portions of each book have been made, but these copies lacked any of the original artifacts’ powers. One such copy, though, now lies under lock and key in Korvosa’s Acadamae: the _Kardosian Fragments_. Originally an incomplete copy of the _Kardosian Codex_, some time after Zutha’s defeat, fragments of the destroyed original ended up the property of the Acadamae, and industrious wizardly professors painstakingly restored what they could, pasting these fragments into their incomplete copy of the book. The result is the _Kardosian Fragments_, a potent item that, while only a shadow of the power of the original, remains as dangerous as it is useful.

The _Kardosian Fragments_ isn’t a spellbook, but it does contain extensive notes on several spells that can be referenced to [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]]. When used in this way, the cost of learning the spell is ignored, as the fragments themselves fulfill this need through remnants of arcane magic that still linger within. The spells that can be learned include [[Spells/False Vitality|_false vitality_]], [[Spells/Invoke Spirits (Player Core)|_invoke spirits_]], [[Spells/Summon Undead|_summon undead_]], [[Spells/Vampiric Exsanguination (Player Core)|_vampiric exsanguination_]], and [[Spells/Vampiric Feast|_vampiric feast_]].

If the _Kardosian Fragments_ are referenced as part of any attempt to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] on subjects associated with its contents, it grants a +3 item bonus to the skill check.

**Activate—Embrace the Void** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;;  **Requirements** You are not currently drained;; **Effect** If your next action this round is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] that has the [[Traits/Void|void]] trait and you’re holding the _Kardosian Fragments_, you gain a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level × 3. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 hour. When the temporary Hit Points are gone, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 if you aren’t [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]].

**Destruction** The text in the _Kardosian Fragments_ fades away and the book itself becomes a nonmagical tome if an arcane spellcaster of at least 17th level and who follows the envy or lust schools of Thassilonian rune magic spends a year painstakingly refuting, redacting, and revising the contents of this book.
