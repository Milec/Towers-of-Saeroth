# Peacemaker  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 185

**Price** 35 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

single action

envision, manipulate

**Requirement** Your last action was an Interact action to stow the affixed firearm or crossbow

---

This ragged piece of white cloth is wrapped around the grip, stock, or haft of the affixed weapon. When you activate the talisman, you gain the effects of a [[Spells/Sanctuary (Player Core)|_sanctuary_]] spell (DC 20) lasting for 1 minute. If you draw the affixed firearm, the effect ends immediately and the talisman crumbles.
