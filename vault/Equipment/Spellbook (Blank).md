**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 291

**Price** 1 gp

**Hands** 1

**Bulk** L

---

A spellbook holds the written knowledge necessary to learn and prepare various spells, a necessity for [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-282)|wizards]] (who typically get one for free) and a useful luxury for other spellcasters looking to learn additional spells. Each spellbook can hold up to 100 spells. The Price listed is for a blank spellbook.
