# Zealot Staff  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 135

**Price** 13,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _zealot staff's_ color, iconography, and materials vary depending on the faith it's dedicated to. An [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae|Iomedaean]] staff might be forged of gold and shaped like an ornamental sword, while a [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu|Lamashtan]] one could instead be made of blackened iron depicting monstrous faces. Used as a weapon, the staff is a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]]_. The staff represents vehement support of the deity to whom the staff is dedicated, punishing defiance. When you prepare this staff, if you don’t worship its deity, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 until your next daily preparations.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You hit with a Strike using the staff;  **Effect** A vicious blast of deific power explodes as you hit. You deal an additional 1d4 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage per damage die of the staff to the target of your Strike.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list. Add the cleric spells granted by your deity to the list for their rank and each higher rank. For example, a zealot staff of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]] would add [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]] at 1st through 7th rank, [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]] at 3rd through 7th rank, and [[Spells/Wall of Fire (Player Core)|_wall of fire_]] at 4th through 7th rank.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Bane (Player Core)|_bane_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Warding Aggression|_warding aggression_]]
- **4th** [[Spells/Divine Wrath (Player Core)|_divine wrath_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Crisis of Faith (Player Core)|_crisis of faith_]], _divine wrath_
- **6th** [[Spells/Spiritual Armament|_spiritual armament_]], [[Spells/Zealous Conviction (Player Core)|_zealous conviction_]]
- **7th** _crisis of faith_, [[Spells/Divine Decree (Player Core)|_divine decree_]]

**Craft Requirements** You worship the deity to which the staff is dedicated. Supply one casting of all listed spells, including your deity’s cleric spells.
