# Grounding Spike  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 34

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dancer's Spear (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Dancer's Spear]]

---

Metal caps the bottom of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dancer's Spear (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|dancer’s spear]]_ and its point gives off the faint smell of ozone. If you hit a target that has been struck by a polarizing mace within the last round, you deal additional electricity damage to the target equal to the number of grounding spike’s damage dice. If you critically hit such a target, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat‑footed]] until the start of your next turn.

**Special** The _grounding spike_ pairs with the [[Equipment/Polarizing Mace (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_polarizing mace_]].
