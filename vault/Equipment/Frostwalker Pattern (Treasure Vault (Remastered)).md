# Frostwalker Pattern  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 120

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Northern peoples design magical tattoos to protect against wintry weather, typically in geometric patterns with a combination of straight lines and whorls. This tattoo negates any damage you take from [[Rules/Temperature|severe environmental cold]] and reduces damage you take from extreme cold to equal that of severe cold.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Until the end of your next turn, you ignore difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain from ice and snow and don't risk falling when crossing ice.
