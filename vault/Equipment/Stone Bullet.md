This sling bullet looks like a petrified serpent’s eye. A creature hit by an activated _stone bullet_ is subject to the effects of a 6th-level [[Spells/Flesh to Stone|_flesh to stone_]] spell (DC 34).

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _flesh to stone_.
