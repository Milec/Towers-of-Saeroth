# Candlecap  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 151

**Price** 12 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

---

The crown of a _candlecap_ is stitched leather sewn in the shape of a small bowl. Fixed inside the bowl is a melted nub of wax with a small black wick.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You shake your head, and the candle wick ignites. The _candlecap_ sheds [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in a 20‑foot radius. The candle doesn’t require oxygen and can’t be smothered or quenched. Activating the _candlecap_ again douses the light.
