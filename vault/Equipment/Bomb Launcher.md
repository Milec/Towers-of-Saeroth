# Bomb Launcher  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 64

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This long, hollow tube is held in two hands and braced over the shoulder. Inside, it contains a small metal basket sized to hold [[Rules/Alchemical Bombs (Player Core)|alchemical bombs]]. A chute in the top delivers an alchemical bomb into the internal basket, while a lever on the underside pulls the basket back and locks it in place. Loading an alchemical bomb into a bomb launcher requires a single [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action. With a pull of a trigger, the basket speeds forward, allowing you to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the loaded alchemical bomb over long distances. Bombs fired with a bomb launcher have a [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range]] increment of 60 feet.
