# Dancing Scarf  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 609

**Usage** worn belt

---

This long and billowing scarf is typically woven of silk or sheer fabric and adorned with bells or other jangling bits of shiny metal. It grants a +1 item bonus to Performance checks to dance.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** On your most recent action, you succeeded at a Performance check to dance; **Effect** You become concealed until the beginning of your next turn.
