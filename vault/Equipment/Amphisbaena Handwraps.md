# Amphisbaena Handwraps  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 106

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn gloves

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|Fist]]

---

Your hands fit through the venomous mouths of this pair of handwraps made from [[Bestiary/Amphisbaena|amphisbaena]] skin. Your unarmed strikes gain the [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]] trait. _Amphisbaena handwraps_ can have weapon runes etched onto them, similar to [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|_handwraps of mighty blows_]].

**Activate—Twin Venom Strike** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Make two unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]]. Both Strikes count toward your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but the penalty doesn’t increase until after both attacks. Each Strike deals an additional 1d6 poison damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the skin and heads of an [[Bestiary/Amphisbaena|amphisbaena]].
