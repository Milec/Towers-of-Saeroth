# Binding Coil  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 224

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** Your Strike with the affixed weapon damages a creature; **Requirements** You’re an expert in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]].

---

This talisman, a bright red coil that is warm to the touch and faintly resembles a serpent, wraps entirely around your weapon. When you activate this talisman’s effect, attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature you damaged; on a success, one end of the coil wraps itself around the target. This has a Grapple’s normal effects, except that the DC to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] is 20 and you can move without freeing the creature as long as you don’t move further away from it.
