# Tarantula Ampoule  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 110

**Price** 45 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

The dried, shrunken corpse of a [[Bestiary/Giant Tarantula (Monster Core)|giant tarantula]] inside this smoked glass bottle is obscured by swirling vapor and cobwebs. You can throw the ampoule up to 30 feet when you Activate it. When you open or throw the ampoule, a Large giant tarantula effigy bursts forth and lunges at one adjacent creature. If the target fails a DC 21 Fortitude save, it takes 1d10+4 piercing damage. If the target critically fails the save, it’s also exposed to [[Bestiary/Giant Tarantula (Monster Core)|tarantula venom]].

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Giant Tarantula (Monster Core)|giant tarantula]].
