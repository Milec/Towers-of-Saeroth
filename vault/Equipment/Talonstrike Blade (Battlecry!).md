# Talonstrike Blade  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 130

**Usage** held in 1 or 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Bastard Sword (Player Core)|Bastard Sword]]

**Access** A character who is a member of the  Eagle Knights  has access to this weapon.

---

This large _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|standard-grade silver]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Bastard Sword (Player Core)|bastard sword]]_ is the signature weapon of many veteran Eagle Knights. It’s easily recognized by its distinctively notched blade and the stylized wings adorning its cross guard. These blades are sometimes passed down from generation to generation.

**Activate—Defense of Liberty** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You or your mount are targeted by a physical melee or ranged attack; **Requirements** You are aware of the attack and are not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against it; **Effect** You or your mount gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack.

## Greater Talonstrike Blade  *Item 14*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 126

**Price** 4,500 gp

---

Eagle Knights charged with protecting Andoran’s western border against Chelaxian incursion often carry these _+2 greater striking standard-grade silver bastard swords_, enchanted to offer greater utility when battling fiends. A greater talonstrike blade gains the Grasping Talons activation.

**Activate—Grasping Talons** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You hit with a melee Strike using the _greater talonstrike_ blade and deal damage;  **Effect** You cast a 5th-rank [[Spells/Planar Tether|_planar tether_]] on the creature you just dealt damage to, with a DC of 34.

**Craft Requirements** For a _greater talonstrike_ blade, supply one casting of _planar tether_.
