# Sage's Lash  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 189

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn belt

**Bulk** 1

---

The thin, glittering strands of rope that comprise this thick belt appear to be spun gold. Strung along the front of the belt are a collection of four multicolored, perfectly spherical beads: jade, turquoise, quartz, and amethyst. While wearing the lash, you gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion]]. When you invest the belt, you either increase your Wisdom score by 2 or to 18, whichever would give you a higher score.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You touch one of the jewels affixed to the sage's lash and speak a command word. Depending on the jewel, a different effect is produced that affects you and all living creatures in a 30-foot emanation. After the effect occurs, all four jewels disappear from the lash, reappearing at the next dawn.

- **Jade** ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]]) Speak “purity” to produce a wave of green light that ends any [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition and any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|ongoing poison damage]] affecting creatures in the emanation.
- **Turquoise** Speak “clarity” to grant all affected a +2 status bonus to their next Will save for 1 minute.
- **Quartz** ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]]) Speak “lenity” to have each creature regain 30 Hit Points.
- **Amethyst** ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Light|light]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]]) Speak “verity” to emit a magical trail of light from the lash that reveals the location of each creature in the emanation. For 1 round, if they would be [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], they become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] instead, and if they would be [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed|unnoticed]] by a creature, they're [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] instead. A creature can attempt a DC 43 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to avoid the lash's detection.
