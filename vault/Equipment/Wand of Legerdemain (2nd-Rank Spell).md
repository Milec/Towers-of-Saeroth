# Wand of Legerdemain  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 140

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wand of lacquered black wood has a handle wrapped in interwoven colorful ribbons. A silver bell caps the wand's tasseled pommel.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You Cast the Spell.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Light|light]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]])   **Requirements** The last action you took this turn was to Cast a Spell from the wand; **Effect** You make yourself the center of attention. An illusory spotlight shines [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] upon your space as you pull inane objects from the wand's tip, such as confetti, silk flowers, streamers, or a long string of colorful kerchiefs knotted end to end. Each enemy within 30 feet must attempt a Will save against your spell DC, receiving a +4 circumstance bonus to the save if you or any of your allies recently threatened it or used [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile actions]] against it. On a failure, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] with you until the end of your next turn. The fascination ends if the target is subject to a hostile act, or if another creature succeeds at a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check against it.

 The spotlight follows you wherever you move. You can't be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] while in the spotlight. The effect ends if you become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check, or Dismiss the activation.

 You can Sustain this Activation for up to 1 minute. Since you need to keep performing tricks, Sustain an Activation has the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait. Sustaining extends the spotlight, and keeps fascinated creatures fascinated, but doesn't cause creatures not already fascinated to become fascinated.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate rank.
