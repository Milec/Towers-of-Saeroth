# Cerebral Barbell  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 234

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This tiny metallic barbell is strangely heavy for its size. The spell attack modifier of any spell cast by Activating this item is +7.  
**• Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to force.  
**• Weapon** After casting a spell by Activating the _cerebral barbell,_ your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the weapon deal an additional 2 force damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Telekinetic Projectile (Player Core)|_telekinetic projectile_]]._
