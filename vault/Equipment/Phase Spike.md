# Phase Spike  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 210

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Spear]]

---

The head of this _+[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|1 striking]] [[Equipment/Astral|astral]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spear]]_ is partially translucent. Thieves and battlefield spellcasters favor it for its ability to pierce magical barriers and temporarily disable enchanted objects. A creature that has [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raised its Shield]] (or similar ability) takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its AC against attacks from a _phase spike_.

**Activate—Incorporeal Stride** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You focus on the head of the spear and channel its properties into your form. You [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to your Speed. You can move easily through solid objects and creatures during this movement, but you can’t end this movement within a solid object or in a creature’s space. You also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to all damage during this Stride (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] or [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage and damage caused by a _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]]_ rune); double this resistance against non-magical damage.
