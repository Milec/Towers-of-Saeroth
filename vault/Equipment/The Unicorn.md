# The Unicorn  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -192- Worst of All Possible Worlds|Pathfinder #192: Worst of All Possible Worlds]] pg. 78

**Usage** carried

---

_The Unicorn_ represents finding what one seeks. As long as you have _The Unicorn_ invested, you gain a +2 item bonus on Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]]. This increases to a +3 item bonus if you're at least 17th level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pass _The Unicorn_ across an adjacent closed container and then open the container. Inside, you'll find a Common [[Traits/Consumable|consumable]] alchemical or magic item you need to solve a problem or deal with a situation close at hand. For example, if you're badly wounded, you might find a [[Equipment/Healing Potion|_greater healing potion_]]. If you've been poisoned, you could discover a [[Equipment/Panacea|_panacea_]]. If you're faced with a written clue in a language you don't understand, the container might hold a [[Equipment/Comprehension Elixir|comprehension elixir]]. The GM always decides what consumable item is discovered, and it must be equal to or lower than your level. If the consumable isn't used within 1 minute of being discovered, the item vanishes. If there's no appropriate item to solve your situation, the GM can rule that no object is found; in this case, the daily use of _The Unicorn_ is not expended.
