# Deadlock Mint  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 74

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

_Deadlock mint_ is a species of mint with small, green flowers, said to grow on battlefields where the opposing sides were equally matched. Sprigs of the flowers blow gently in a breeze of their own creation. If you cast [[Spells/Mystic Armor|_mystic armor_]] using _deadlock mint_, you release a small blast of concussive air in an [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of a size that depends on the catalyst’s type. Unattended objects up to a certain Bulk limit are pushed away from you. Large or smaller creatures must succeed at a Fortitude save equal to your spell save DC or be pushed the same distance away from you.
