# Spring Heel  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Mechanical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 108

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** 1

---

Fitted into each of these prosthetic legs is a large spring, tightly bound around a collapsible shaft. When released, the spring unspools rapidly and the shaft telescopes out and back, returning to its compressed form and catapulting you forward.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You Stride up to twice your speed or [[Actions/Leap]] up to 20 feet horizontally and 5 feet vertically.
