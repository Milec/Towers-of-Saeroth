single action

_Disruption ammunition_ consists of alchemically treated metal, the surface of which roils like the thrashings of a writhing parasite trapped within. When activated _disruption ammunition_ hits a target, it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] one active spell effect that the target is [[Actions/Sustain|Sustaining]] with a +11 counteract check modifier (counteract rank 3).
