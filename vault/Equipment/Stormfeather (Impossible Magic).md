# Stormfeather  *Item 8*

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Requirements** You’re an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]].

---

Even while affixed, this electric blue feather sways lightly in the air, as if always accompanied by a breeze. When activated, the talisman casts a _[[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]]_ spell on you, though the duration is 1 minute. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this activation. If you do, you’re affected by _[[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]]_.
