# Coldstar Pistols  *Item 23*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 178

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Dueling Pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=201)

---

One [dueling pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=201) in this paired set is etched with flames along its barrel, while the other is etched with icicles. In these separate forms, one gun comprising the _Coldstar Pistols_ functions as a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming|greater flaming]] dueling pistol_ and the other as a _+3 greater striking [[Equipment/Frost|greater frost]] dueling pistol_. When joined, the guns form a double-barreled weapon that functions as a _+4 major striking greater flaming greater frost dueling pistol_. The combined form has a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 120 feet. In either form, the Coldstar Pistols have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Concealable|concealable]], [[Traits/Concussive (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|concussive]], and [[Traits/Fatal|fatal d10 traits]]. As star guns, the _Coldstar Pistols_ run on magic and don't use ammunition or black powder.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You switch the _Coldstar Pistols_ from one form to the other. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Feint]] with a +4 circumstance bonus.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** Make two Strikes against one target, taking the highest of the two attack rolls and applying it to both attacks. Your multiple attack penalty increases only after these Strikes.

**Destruction** If Deft Onki's name and deeds ever fade from mortal memory entirely, the _Coldstar Pistols_ can be destroyed like a normal object.
