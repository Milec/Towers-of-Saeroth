Once you ingest this strongly spiced, glowing red potion, blazing wings of a phoenix sprout from your back. For 1 minute, you gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 40 feet. The first time each round that you Fly (including to hover in place), you shed burning feathers that deal 3d4 fire damage to all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] at the end of your movement (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
