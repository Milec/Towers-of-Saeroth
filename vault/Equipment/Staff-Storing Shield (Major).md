# Staff-Storing Shield  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 190

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield]]

---

This magically reinforced [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield|wooden shield]] (Hardness 6, HP 36, BT 18) normally has a blank face. It can absorb a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|staff]] and transform between a shield and staff. When you prepare a staff, you can hold it up to the shield, at which point the items will merge, and the shield's face becomes an image corresponding to the type of magic, such as a skull for a [[Equipment/Staff of Necromancy|_staff of necromancy_]].

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You change the _staff-storing shield_ from its shield form into the stored staff, which has an image of the shield on it, or change it from the stored staff back into a shield.
