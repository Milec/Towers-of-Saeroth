# Shield Of Mental Fortitude  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 206

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** strapped to 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|Buckler]]

---

This small steel [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|buckler]] ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 6, HP 24, BT 12) is engraved with an unbroken golden circle. While you have this shield raised, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to mental damage.

**Activate—Mind Backlash** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You succeed at a Will save against a mental effect while the _shield of mental fortitude_ is raised;  **Effect** You return a wave of psychic energy to the creature. The creature that caused the triggering mental effect must succeed at a DC 27 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
