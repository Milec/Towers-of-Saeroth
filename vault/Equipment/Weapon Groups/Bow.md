# Bow  *Weapon Critical Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 283

If the target of the critical hit is adjacent to a surface, it gets stuck to that surface by the missile. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and must spend an Interact action to attempt a DC 10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to pull the missile free; it can't move from its space until it succeeds. The creature doesn't become stuck if it is incorporeal, is liquid (like a [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water|water elemental]] or some [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze|oozes]]), or could otherwise escape without effort.
