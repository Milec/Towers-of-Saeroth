# Uniter of Clans  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -193- Mantle of Gold|Pathfinder #193: Mantle of Gold]] pg. 84

**Usage** worn

---

Resembling two hands clasped in friendship, this _[[Equipment/Diplomat's Badge|diplomat's badge]]_ is designed to be woven into the wearer's braided beard. When the wearer befriends others, one of the hands briefly transforms to resemble the befriended creature's own.  
**Forming the Bond** The PCs complete the [[Rituals/Rite of Repatriation|rite of repatriation]].**Advancing the Bond** Solve problems nonviolently. End ancient grudges and heal old wounds, especially those caused by dwarves.  
**Minor Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Ancestors' Call|ancestors' call]], [[Equipment/Relics/Linguistic Nexus|linguistic nexus]], [[Equipment/Relics/Repository of Knowledge|repository of knowledge]] (Dwarf Lore, Genealogy Lore, Warfare Lore)  
 **Major Gifts** [[Equipment/Relics/Perception Filter|perception filter]], or increase the diplomat's badge's item bonus to Diplomacy to +2.
