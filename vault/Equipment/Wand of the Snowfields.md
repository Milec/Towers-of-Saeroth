**Usage** held in 1 hand

This wand is a slender length of ice-blue glass.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Cone of Cold|_cone of cold_]]. The ground within the spell's area is swathed in deep snow, becoming difficult terrain for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _cone of cold_ of the appropriate level.
