# Quotidian Staff  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 239

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This humble oak staff is etched with a geometric pattern. _Quotidian staves_ are popular among those with minor magical abilities to handle everyday tasks. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] checks about food or drink.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
