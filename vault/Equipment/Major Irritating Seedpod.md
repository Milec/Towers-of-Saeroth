# Irritating Seedpod  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 77

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

When you crack open this soft, spongy seedpod, you can use it as a [[Rules/Spell Catalysts|catalyst]] when casting a [[Spells/Mist|_mist_]] spell. When you do, irritating pollen fills the area for the spell’s duration. Creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw at the listed DC to avoid sneezing uncontrollably. On a failed save, the creature gains the listed condition for the listed time. A creature that succeeds at this saving throw becomes temporarily immune to the irritating seedpod’s pollen for 10 minutes.

## Lesser Irritating Seedpod  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 77

---

The DC is 15. On a failed save, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round. Rather than last for the duration of the spell, the irritating pollen only remains in the area for 1 round.
