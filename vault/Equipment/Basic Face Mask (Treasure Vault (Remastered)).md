# Basic Face Mask  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 105

**Price** 3 sp

**Usage** worn

---

This simple cloth mask, sewn to closely fit your face, is fastened by two sets of strings drawn across your face and secured behind your head. While wearing the mask, you gain a +1 item bonus on any initial saves to avoid contracting airborne [[Traits/Disease|diseases]], such as [[Rules/Diseases/Choking Death|choking death]] or [[Rules/Diseases/Tuberculosis|tuberculosis]].
