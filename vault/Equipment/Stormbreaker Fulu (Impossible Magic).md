# Stormbreaker Fulu  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221, [[Sources/Pathfinder -167- Ready- Fight!|Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!]] pg. 75

**Price** 1,900 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You would be forced to move, you would be teleported, or you would take cold, electricity, or sonic damage.

---

This unassuming paper tag is inscribed with magical symbols. When you activate the fulu, it vanishes in a wisp of cloud, and you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 15 to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], and [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage until the end of your next turn. For that duration, you [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore difficult terrain]] from wind and weather, and you can’t be forcibly moved or teleported unless the effect [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteracts]] the fulu (DC 40). These protections apply against the triggering effect.
