---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe (Player Core)|scythe]]_’s handles are made from a dull gray wood of bone-like consistency, and when you slice with it, a fluttering gust of hundreds of moths’ wingbeats fills the air. If you’re a devotee of [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa (Divine Mysteries)|Urgathoa]], you can use this scythe as a divine locus, and with every Strike, it exudes a pallid cloud of powdery dust.

**Activate—Pallid Reaping** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You deal 1d10 void damage to yourself. This damage ignores all

## [[Rules/Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances (GM Core)|resistances and immunities]], and you take it even if you would be healed by [[Traits/Void|void]] effects. If your next action is to [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the scythe or to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] or void trait, that Strike or spell deals additional void damage equal to the damage you took.
