# Limning Gem  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Whetstone]]

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This luminescent white gem glows from within, and a weapon it’s applied to gains its inner radiance, shedding dim light in a 10-foot radius. Successful Strikes with a weapon affected by a _limning gem_ outline the target in a [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright white light]]; if the target was [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead. This light lasts until the end of your next turn or for the remainder of the limning gem’s duration on a critical hit.
