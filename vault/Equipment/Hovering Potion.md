# Hovering Potion  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 90

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

A foamy, cloudy liquid, _hovering potion_ enables you to defy gravity for 5 minutes. By default, the potion causes you to float several inches off the ground. If you take a [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] action, you can move 10 feet up or down in the air. You can instead [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] at your full Speed or Stride to move along a horizontal surface at half your Speed. The GM determines which surfaces can be climbed or moved across this way. If you fall while this potion is in effect, you can use the [[Actions/Arrest a Fall (Player Core)|Arrest a Fall]] reaction as if you had a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]]. If you have a fly Speed, you remain airborne at the end of your turn, even if you didn’t use a Fly action this round.
