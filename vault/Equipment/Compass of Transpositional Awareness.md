# Compass of Transpositional Awareness  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 154

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The silver face of this glass-encased compass is etched with dozens of esoteric symbols, obscure icons, and inscrutable abbreviations.

**Activate** single action concentrate, Interact;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You point the _compass of transpositional awareness_ at an extant [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effect (such as a magical portal) or the site of a teleportation effect that existed within the last 1 minute (such as the space where a creature just cast [[Spells/Dimension Door|_dimension door_]]). Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] check to determine to where the effect leads or led. The DC of this check is the effect's [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC. On a success, you know roughly the destination of the teleportation effect (for example “the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire]],” “north,” or “Avistan”). On a critical success, you ascertain the exact destination within mere feet for local effects (such as _dimension door_) or within a few miles for long-range effects (such as [[Spells/Teleport|_teleport_]], [[Spells/Plane Shift|_plane shift_]], or interdimensional portals).

**Activate** single action  ;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You use the _compass of transpositional awareness_ to triangulate your current coordinates and the coordinates of your intended destination using teleportation magic. For 1 minute, whenever you cast a [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] spell that has a range, increase that spell's range by 30 feet. If the spell normally has a range of touch, extend its range to 30 feet.
