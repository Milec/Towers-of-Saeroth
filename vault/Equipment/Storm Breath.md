# Storm Breath  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Bottled Breath]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 76

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

_Storm breaths_ are bottles of temperamental lightning captured during storms on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air]], releasing small charges of static energy any time they're touched. The first _storm breaths_ were created by [[Setting/Deities/Ranginori|Ranginori's]] faithful following the Elemental Lord's return from a long imprisonment, but the recipe has since been duplicated across the multiverse. After inhaling _storm breath_, you gain resistance 5 to both electricity and sonic. You can exhale the storm breath as a bolt of lightning, dealing 4d12 electricity damage to all creatures in a 30-foot line, with a DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
