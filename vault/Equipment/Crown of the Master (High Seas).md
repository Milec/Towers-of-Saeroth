# Crown of the Master  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 108

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

This crown, granted to favored [[Bestiary/Families/Azarketi (Monster Core)|azarketis]] by their [[Bestiary/Alghollthu Master|alghollthu masters]], is made of coral sculpted into the shape of a tentacled creature clutching the wearer’s temples. While wearing the crown, you gain an item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects and can cast _[[Spells/Figment|figment]]_ as an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]].

## Crown of the Master  *Item 7*

## Greater Crown of the Master  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 108

**Price** 2,600 gp

---

The item bonus is +2, and the crown gains the following activation.

**Activate—Imperative Order** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast a 6th-rank _[[Spells/Dominate (Player Core)|dominate]]_ with a DC of 30. When you cast _dominate_ this way, its duration is reduced to 8 hours.
