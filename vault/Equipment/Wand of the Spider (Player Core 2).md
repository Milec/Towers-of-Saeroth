**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Web (Player Core 2)|_web_]], but the strands of webbing are toxic. Any creature that fails its Athletics check or Reflex save to navigate the web takes 1d6 poison damage.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Web|_web_]] of the appropriate rank.
