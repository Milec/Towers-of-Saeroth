# Morphing Weapon  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 146

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** held in 1 or 2 hands

**Bulk**  varies

---

In its base form, this armament looks like a smooth bar of soft, gleaming metal shaped like a horseshoe. Its ability to adjust to any battle situation makes it a popular weapon for elite warriors of the [[Traits/Metal|Plane of Metal]]. It can be shaped into a melee weapon using its [[Equipment/Shifting|_shifting_]] rune, but it can shift only into weapons primarily made of metal or back to its base shape. In weapon form, it's a +_1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] shifting weapon_.

**Activate—Reshape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** Strike with the _morphing weapon_, choosing one of the following benefits to apply to the Strike.

- **Reach** Increase the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] by 5 feet.
- **Shift** The weapon's damage becomes your choice of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing instead of its normal type.
- **Take** If the Strike hits, you can attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]] or [[Actions/Steal]] from your target immediately after as a reaction.
- **Weigh** Gain a status bonus to the damage roll equal to the weapon's number of damage dice.
