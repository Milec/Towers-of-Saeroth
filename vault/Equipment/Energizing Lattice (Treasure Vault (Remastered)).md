# Energizing Lattice  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Lattice Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Lattice Armor]]

---

This suit of _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Fortification|fortification]] [[Equipment/Armor/Lattice Armor (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|lattice armor]]_ has latticework of fine golden wire. After negating a critical hit with its _fortification_ rune, the latticework glows for 1 minute, shedding [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] for the next 20 feet). You can Dismiss this light.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]])   **Requirements** The _energizing lattice_ is glowing because it negated an enemy's critical hit; **Effect** You release the lattice's energy and make a weapon or unarmed attack Strike that deals 6d6 additional force damage. If your Strike fails, but doesn't critically fail, the target still takes half the force damage. The latticework ceases glowing after the Strike.
