# Triton's Conch  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 576

**Price** 640 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

If you put this large opalescent conch shell to your ear, you can hear the sound of the roaring sea crashing against the shore.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]])    **Effect**  You can raise the conch to your lips and blow into it, letting out a long, rumbling note. For the next minute, you and all allies who were within 30 feet of you when you activated the conch gain a +2 item bonus to Athletics checks to Swim and can breathe under water.
