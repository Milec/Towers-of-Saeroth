These round, black pellets are made from magically enhanced magnetic lodestone. They can be loaded into an [[Equipment/Weapons/Air Repeater (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|air repeater]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Long Air Repeater (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|long air repeater]]. When activated, lodestone pellets provide a +1 circumstance bonus to ranged Strikes made against creatures made of metal or wearing metal armor.
