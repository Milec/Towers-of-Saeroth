# Gearbinder Oil  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 54

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Gearbinder oil comes in a sealed pump that can squirt the oil a short distance. The oil is designed to flow through complex mechanisms and, agitated through mechanical action, foam up and form a paste that binds the works. The oil is effective against articulated [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]] and machinery, including many constructs, [[Bestiary/Families/Clockwork|clockworks]], and [[Traits/Mechanical (Player Core)|mechanical]] hazards. You apply the oil to the target you want to bind, which must be within 10 feet of you. After the oil is applied, at the end of any round during which the target took an action with the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], or [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] trait, it must attempt a Fortitude save against a DC determined by the oil’s type. A mechanism that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 2 or more by gearbinder oil also can’t use reactions. Gearbinder oil functions for up to 6 rounds before becoming an inert, oily residue.

**Critical Success** The oil becomes inert, and the effect ends.  

**Success** The target reduces its [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] condition by 1. If the slowed condition’s value is 0, the effect ends.  

**Failure** The target increases its [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] condition by 1, to a maximum of slowed 3.  

**Critical Failure** The target increases its slowed condition by 2, to a maximum of slowed 3.
