# Viper Rapier  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 104

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier]]

---

Crafted from flawless jade, the guard of this [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier|rapier]] takes the form of a twisting serpent with the blade thrusting from its open mouth. This rapier functions as a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Quickstrike|quickstrike]] rapier_. While wielding the rapier, your movement doesn't trigger reactions when you Stride, [[Actions/Balance]], or [[Actions/Tumble Through]]. When you invest the rapier, you either increase your Dexterity modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail, but don't critically fail, an attack using the _viper rapier_;  **Effect** The tip of the rapier briefly takes the form of a viper's head, and the blade twists and contorts, biting the target you missed. The target is affected as if successfully poisoned with [[Equipment/Cave Worm Venom|cave worm venom]] (DC 43).

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** A creature misses or critically misses you with an attack;  **Effect** With a slight, obviously mocking bow or curtsy, you Step.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You twirl your rapier in a serpentine pattern, causing your form to become a blur of motion. You're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] for 1 minute or until you take a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]]. While you're concealed, you also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Reflex saving throws.
