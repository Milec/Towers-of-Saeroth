These cool noodles are served with dark fermented sauces and vinegars before finally being tossed with spicy chili oil. When you consume the noodles, you temporarily ignore the -1 status penalty to AC and saving throws caused by the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition for 1 hour.
