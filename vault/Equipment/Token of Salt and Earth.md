# Token of Salt and Earth  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 212

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This charm is usually worn around the neck or hanging from a belt. It consists of a three-part hourglass with individual glass bulbs filled with sea salts, charcoal ash, and minute slivers of silver. It grants the wearer a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] from creatures with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.

**Activate—Hide from the Hateful** two actions ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** By rotating the charm along one of its concentric rings, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] to creatures with the [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] trait (your choice) for 1 minute. If you use a hostile action, you're no longer invisible after the action is completed.
