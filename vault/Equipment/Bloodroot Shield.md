**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|Wooden Shield]]

This [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|wooden shield]] is an interwoven knot of still-living tree roots ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 6, HP 36, BT 18).

**Activate—Thirsty Roots** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold out the shield as the roots untangle themselves and launch forward, dealing 9d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage to all creatures in a 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed save, a creature takes an additional 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. The shield regains a number of Hit Points equal to half the void damage dealt.
