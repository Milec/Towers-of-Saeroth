Crafted for a long-forgotten knight to slay Avathrael Realmshaper, this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Bane|dragon bane]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance (Player Core)|lance]]_ is crafted from gleaming steel with a golden silk banner depicting a radiant sun. When in a location of [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], the lance of sun’s radiance sheds [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 60-foot radius, and dim light for a further 60 feet.

**Activate—Banish Darkness** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You tap the lance’s tip against an object, causing that object to shed light like a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]] for 1 hour.

**Activate—Sun Cutting Scales ** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a bold proclamation, you thrust the lance forward, firing a beam of brilliant, scouring light in a 120-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. Creatures in the area take 6d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, or 6d12 spirit damage if they’re a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail are also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.
