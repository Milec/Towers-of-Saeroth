# Emberheart  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 184

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This small, heart-shaped amulet appears to be sculpted from stone with a single streak of dim light running through, like an ember just beneath ashes. When worn, the amulet gives off a gentle warmth, akin to being near a fireplace. You gain [[Traits/Poison|poison]] resistance 15 when wearing this amulet.

 When you invest the amulet, you either increase your Constitution score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold the amulet aloft as a ripple of warm orange light exudes outward. Each ally in a 30-foot emanation regains 30 Hit Points and gains a +3 status bonus to Fortitude saves until the end of their next turn.
