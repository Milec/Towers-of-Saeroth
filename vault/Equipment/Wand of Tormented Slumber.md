# Wand of Tormented Slumber  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Sleep]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 143

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The carved talon of an unidentifiable beast comprises this wand. Blood-stained cloth wraps the thicker part of the talon, which acts as a handle.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast 4th-level [[Spells/Sleep (Player Core)|_sleep_]], but the slumbering creatures have terrifying nightmares. A creature knocked unconscious by this spell takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]]. This damage wakes the creature from [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconsciousness]] only if it deals 4 or more damage on a single roll. If the creature awakens from its unconsciousness due to damage (whether it was the persistent mental damage or not), it’s [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. If it awakens from damage on its own turn, the creature doesn’t reduce its frightened condition automatically on that turn.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of 4th-level [[Spells/Sleep (Player Core)|_sleep_]].
