# Rovagug's Mud  *Item 13+*

Interact

---

_Rovagug’s mud_ smells and tastes like wet, sour earth. For 1 hour after you drink it, you have a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effects. Also, you can use a single action to breathe out a 20-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of bitter vapor that causes an earth-shaking rumble that can be heard for 100 feet. Creatures and objects in the area take 5d6 damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save), decreasing Hardness by 5. An object’s Hardness remains lowered for 1d4 rounds, and you can’t use this breath again for the same amount of time.
