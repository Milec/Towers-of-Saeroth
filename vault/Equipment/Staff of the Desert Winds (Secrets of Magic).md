# Staff of the Desert Winds  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 190

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This crooked staff is made from twisting acacia wood and has a sphere of rough sandstone embedded in the top, to channel the magic of the desert. When wielding this staff in deserts, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Subsist]], [[Actions/Track]], and [[Actions/Cover Tracks]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells
