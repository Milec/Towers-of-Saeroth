# Drowsy Sun Eye Drops  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Travel Guide]] pg. 101

**Price** 9 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Seek]]

---

These drops are made from luminescent mushrooms and refined into eye drops, which simulates a creature's natural night vision. When you attempt a Perception check to Seek, you can draw and apply a dose of drowsy sun to your eyes as part of the same action, which grants you [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] to a range of 30 feet for 1 round. If you do, your Seek action gains the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait, due to drawing and applying the eye drops.
