# Grievous  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 238

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

When your attack roll with this weapon is a critical hit and gains the critical specialization effect, you gain an additional benefit depending on the weapon group.

  **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe]]**  You can damage a third creature, with the same restrictions.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow]]**  The Athletics check to pull the missile free is DC 20.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling]]**  The target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its save.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club]]**  You can knock the target up to 15 feet away.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow]]**  If the target of the critical hit is adjacent to a surface, it gets stuck to that surface by the missile, as the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] critical specialization.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Dart]]**  The base persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm]]**  The target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its save.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail]]**  You move the target 5 feet. You can't move it away from you, but you can move it in another direction of your choice.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer]]**  You can also knock the target 5 feet away from you.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife]]**  The target takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speed while it has the persistent bleed damage.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick]]**  The extra damage from the critical specialization effect increases to 4 per weapon damage die.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm]]**  You can move the target up to 10 feet.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield]]**  You can knock the target up to 10 feet away.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sling]]**  The target also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round if it fails the save.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear]]**  The [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] condition lasts for 2 rounds.  
 **[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword]]**  The target is off-guard until the end of your next turn.
