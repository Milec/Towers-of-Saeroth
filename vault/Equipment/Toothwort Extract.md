# Toothwort Extract  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Additive|Additive 1]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Travel Guide]] pg. 101

**Price** 9 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

free action

**Trigger** You use [[Actions/Quick Alchemy]] to craft an alchemical poison that's at least 1 level lower than your advanced alchemy level.

---

Originally used in dental procedures, toothwort numbs the gums by deadening nerves. When the oils are extracted and distilled, toothwort has a secondary reputation among alchemists for enhancing the duration of poisons. When adding toothwort extract to an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical poison]], you can extend the maximum duration of the poison by 1 round.
