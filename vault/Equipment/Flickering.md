# Flickering  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Seven Dooms for Sandpoint

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 182

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

A _flickering_ rune causes a weapon to shimmer, grow blurry and indistinct, and momentarily turn invisible at random intervals for a second or two. A _flickering_ weapon adds its item bonus from its potency rune to the DC against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] it. On a critical hit, the weapon flashes bright pulses of color into the creature's eyes, [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzling]] the creature for 1 round (this effect has the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait).

**Activate** two actions envision, command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The flickering weapon casts [[Spells/Blur (Player Core)|_blur_]] to your specification.
