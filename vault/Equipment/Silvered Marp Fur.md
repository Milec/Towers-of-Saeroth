A [[Bestiary/Marp|marp]] that gnaws on silver rather than gold grows fur tipped with silver that can be further processed into a versatile spell catalyst. A spellcaster who uses _silvered marp fur_ with an [[Spells/Impaling Spike (Player Core)|_impaling spike_]] spell creates a silver spike rather than a cold iron one.
