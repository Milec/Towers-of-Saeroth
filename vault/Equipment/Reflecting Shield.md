# Reflecting Shield  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 588

**Price** 18,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler]]

---

This [[Equipment/Silver Shield|high-grade silver]] [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler|buckler]] (Hardness 6, HP 24, BT 12) is polished to a mirrorlike sheen. The shield functions as a [[Equipment/Spellguard Shield|_spellguard shield_]] that can also reflect spells.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You are targeted by a spell; **Requirements** The _reflecting shield_ is raised; **Effect** You attempt to reflect the spell on its caster, with the effects of a 9th-level [[Spells/Spell Turning|_spell turning_]] with a counteract modifier of +30.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _spell turning_, and the initial raw materials must include at least 2,750 gp of silver.
