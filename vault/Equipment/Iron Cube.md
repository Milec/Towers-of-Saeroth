# Iron Cube  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 567

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

two actions

Knockdown

**Requirement** You are an expert with the affixed weapon

---

This cube of blackened iron is affixed to a weapon with an iron chain. When you activate the cube, you use [[Feats/Knockdown]], as the fighter feat.

 If you have the Knockdown feat, ignore its normal size restrictions.
