# Rod of Negation  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 574

**Price** 4,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This long, plain, leaden rod can disrupt magic.

**Activate** two actions Interact;     **Effect** This rod emits a thin, gray beam that negates a spell or magic item, casting a 6th-level [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] spell with a counteract modifier of +23. Once activated, the rod can’t be activated again for 2d6 hours.
