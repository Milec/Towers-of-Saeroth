# Matsuki's Medicinal Wine  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Ingested]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 295

**Price** 12 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Old Matsuki’s private brew has quite the kick. In addition to making you tipsy, this medicinal wine works wonders in helping to recover from [[Rules/Diseases (GM Core)|disease]]. When you drink a dose of this wine you must succeed at a DC 18 Fortitude save to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 10 minutes (and also [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for this duration if you critically fail the saving throw). Regardless of the saving throw’s results, you gain a +2 item bonus to Fortitude saving throws against diseases and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poisons]] for the next 24 hours. This applies to your daily save against a disease’s progression as well.
