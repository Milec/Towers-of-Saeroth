# Caltrop Snare  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 590

**Price** 3 gp

---

This snare consists of a hidden canister of [[Equipment/Caltrops|caltrops]] attached to a trip wire. When the snare is triggered, it flings the caltrops into either the snare’s square or a square adjacent to the snare. You choose which square when you set up the snare.

 If the caltrops scatter into the same square as a creature, that creature must attempt the Acrobatics check immediately.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a container of caltrops.
