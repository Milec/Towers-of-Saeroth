# Vitalizing  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 239

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

A _vitalizing_ weapon pulses with vital energy, dealing an extra 1d6 persistent vitality damage to undead. On a critical hit, the undead is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] 1 until the end of your next turn.

## Vitalizing  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 238

**Price** 150 gp

## Vitalizing (Greater)  *Item 14*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 238

**Price** 4,300 gp

---

Increase the persistent vitality damage to 2d6. On a critical hit, the undead creature is enfeebled 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] 1 as long as it has the persistent damage from this rune.
