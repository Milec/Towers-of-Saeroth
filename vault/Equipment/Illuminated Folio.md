# Illuminated Folio  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 112

**Price** 4,200 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pages of this part-spellbook, part-bestiary are illustrated with all manner of creatures, magical and mundane. The illustrations shift and move around the page when examined.

**Activate** single action envision ([[Traits/Spellshape|metamagic]])    **Effect** If your next action is to cast a [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Conjuration|conjuration]] spell prepared from this grimoire that summons one or more creatures, you summon creatures from the illuminated folio rather than their usual source. These summoned creatures appear as living illustrations, granting them [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half their level and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] and to any ability with the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait. They can also fold themselves up to pass through spaces only an inch or so wide as part of their movement.
