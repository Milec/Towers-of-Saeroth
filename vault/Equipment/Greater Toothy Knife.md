This jagged bit of metal is pitted and worn but wickedly sharp. The DC of the flat check to end [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] dealt by a weapon under the effects of a _toothy knife_ is 17 (or 12 with appropriate assistance). This bleeding still typically ends on its own after 1 minute, as normal.
