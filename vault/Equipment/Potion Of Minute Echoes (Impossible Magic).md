# Potion Of Minute Echoes  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

No matter how carefully you open this bottle, it always lets out an audible, echoing pop. For 1 minute after drinking this potion, you gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks to hear. In addition, each time you [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]], your hearing becomes a [[Rules/Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]] until the beginning of your next turn, allowing you to pinpoint creatures’ locations and otherwise perceive the world in detail by listening to the sound of echoes.
