# Caterwaul Sling  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 600

**Price** 155 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling]]

---

Made of shiny brown leather, this _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling|sling]]_ has a single white thread interwoven into its cord.

**Activate** two actions Interact (sonic);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull the white thread free, then whirl the sling in circles at high speed. It lets out an ear-piercing wave of sound. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 4d6 sonic damage (DC 21 basic Fortitude save). Any creature that fails is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round, or 1 hour on a critical failure.
