# Fey Staff  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 238

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Vines twine around this wooden staff, carved with silhouettes of [[Bestiary/Pixie (Monster Core)|pixies]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Nymph (Monster Core)|nymphs]]. Some _fey staves_ have the symbol of an [[Setting/Deity Categories/Eldest]] inlaid in silver on their surfaces. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks against creatures with the [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]] trait.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
