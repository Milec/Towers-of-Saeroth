# Wand of the Spider  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 265

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The handle of this wand is a 5-inch-long piece of rosewood, but most of its length is a pair of twisted spider legs covered in gossamer webbing.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Web|_web_]], but the strands of webbing are envenomed. Any creature that fails their [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check or Reflex save to navigate the web takes 1d6 poison damage.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _web_ of the appropriate level.
