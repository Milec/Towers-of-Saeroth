# Wand of Wearying Dance  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 143

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This colorfully painted wand has a few jingling bells tied to the pommel.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Uncontrollable Dance|_uncontrollable dance_]]. When the spell's duration ends, if the target was forced to dance for 1 minute, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _uncontrollable dance_.
