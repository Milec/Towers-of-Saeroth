# Devil's Trident  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 47

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|trident]]_ is made of an ancient black metal that glistens unnaturally and is cold to the touch. The prongs of this trident drip with metallic lake water. Strikes with the _Devil's Trident_ trigger the weaknesses of any creature with a weakness to water.

**Activate** ⤾ Command;   **Trigger** You critically hit a creature;  **Effect** The target must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or become infected with [[Rules/Diseases/Sewer Haze|sewer haze]].

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The trident casts 3rd-level [[Spells/Quench|_quench_]] with a DC of 25 and a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] modifier of +15. You can Sustain the Spell as normal.

**Activate** three actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call upon the corrupted power of the trident to stay a foe. Attempt a Strike. On a hit, the trident casts 3rd-level [[Spells/Paralyze|_paralyze_]] on the target (DC 25). If the Strike was a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] even if it critically succeeds at the save.
