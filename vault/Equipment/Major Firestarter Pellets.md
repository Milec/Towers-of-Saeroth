# Firestarter Pellets  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 214

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

These compacted pellets of bat guano, sulfur, and magical accelerants have long been a staple for spellcasters on the battlefield. Adding a _firestarter pellet_ to a _[[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]]_ spell produces clinging flames that deal [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] to all who fail the saving throw against the effect (doubling on a critical failure).
