# Sun Dazzler  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 65

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This metallic tube has a complex array of lenses and prisms at one end and a hatch at the other. The hatch can be unlocked, loaded with a [[Equipment/Glow Rod|sunrod]], and refastened using 3 [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions. A loaded sun dazzler can be activated to burn the sunrod to dust in a single focused flash, creating a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of scintillating light. All creatures in the cone must attempt a DC 24 Fortitude save, with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 round or until it spends an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to rub its eyes, ending the blinded condition.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 round.
