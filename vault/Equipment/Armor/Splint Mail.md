# Splint Mail  *Armor 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 275

**Price** 13 gp

**AC Bonus** +5

**Dex Cap** +1

**Check Penalty** -3

**Speed Penalty** -10 ft.

**Strength** +3

**Bulk** 3

**Category** Heavy

**Group** Composite

---

This type of armor is chain mail reinforced with flexible, interlocking metal plates, typically located on the wearer’s torso, upper arms, and legs. A suit of this armor comes with an undercoat of [[Equipment/Armor/Padded Armor|padded armor]] and a pair of [[Equipment/Weapons/Gauntlet|gauntlets]].
