# Breastplate  *Armor 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 275

**Price** 8 gp

**AC Bonus** +4

**Dex Cap** +1

**Check Penalty** -2

**Speed Penalty** -5 ft.

**Strength** +3

**Bulk** 2

**Category** Medium

**Group** Plate

---

Though referred to as a breastplate, this type of armor consists of several pieces of [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate|plate]] or [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate|half-plate]] armor that protect the torso, chest, neck, and sometimes the hips and lower legs. It strategically grants some of the protection of plate while allowing greater flexibility and speed.

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 275
