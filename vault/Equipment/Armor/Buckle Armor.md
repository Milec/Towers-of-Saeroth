Absalom style once led famous adventurers to wear clothing with an unusual number of buckles, pouches, and straps. This fashion birthed a trend that led to “buckle armor,” a colloquial name for chic armor with spacious tool storage. Buckle armor comes with the [[Equipment/Storage (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|storage armor adjustment]].
