**Bulk** 2

**Category** Medium

**Group** Wood

---

A suit of carved and tempered wood, a wooden breastplate resembles a metal breastplate in shape and function. Such suits can be carved from large pieces of wood, but they most often come from wood coaxed magically from special trees, whether by [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]], [[Ancestries/Elf (Player Core)|elves]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], or [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] creatures such as [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal (Monster Core)|arboreals]] or [[Ancestries/Leshy (Player Core)|leshys]].
