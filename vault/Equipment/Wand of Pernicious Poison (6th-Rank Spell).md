# Wand of Pernicious Poison  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 141

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wand is made of chitin, topped with a hooked barb that weeps droplets of foul-smelling, green fluid when you Activate the wand.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You Cast a Spell determined by the wand's type. The poison the spell delivers gains the [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] trait.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _spider sting_ of the appropriate rank.  

## Wand of Pernicious Poison (1st-Rank Spell)  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 141

**Price** 100 gp

**Bulk** L

---

## Wand of Pernicious Poison (6th-Rank Spell)  *Item 14*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 141

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The spell is 6th-rank _spider sting_, but it deals 3d6 damage on a touch or on a successful save, and delivers this poison.

**Deadly Spider Venom** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], virulent) **Level** 11; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 (1 round); **Stage 2** 4d6 poison damage and enfeebled 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 6d6 poison damage and enfeebled 2 (1 round)
