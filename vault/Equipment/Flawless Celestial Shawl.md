# Flawless Celestial Shawl  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 27

**Usage** worn garment

---

Each _flawless celestial shawl_ functions only for a specific tennin and is the culmination of their immortal cultivation. The sash is made of celestial silken threads stronger than [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] yet lighter than gossamer. The tennin can draw thread from the _flawless celestial shawl_ to serve as a [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|sterling artisan’s toolkit]] and workshop to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]]. The sash can be used to work with [[Rules/Precious Materials (GM Core)|precious materials]] of any grade. On any [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check the tennin attempts using the shawl, they use the outcome one degree of success better than the result of their roll.

**Activate—Reweave** three actions ;     **Effect** The tennin uses the sash to [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] an item. This Repair activity loses the [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|exploration]] trait.
