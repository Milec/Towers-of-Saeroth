# Chair Storage  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 71

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All Pathfinder Society agents also have access to all assistive items from Morhen’s Mobility Apparel_

**Price** 1 gp

---

The chair has an efficient allocated space to hold additional items. This reduces the amount of Bulk the items weigh when stored within the chair, much like a [[Equipment/Backpack|backpack]]. The first 2 Bulk of items stowed in your chair don't count against your Bulk limit. If you use both chair storage and a backpack at the same time, only 2 Bulk total isn't counted against your limit, much like if you used multiple backpacks or similar items at the same time.
