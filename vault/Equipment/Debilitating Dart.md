Forged from black steel, this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dart (Player Core)|dart]]_ is etched with runes that shimmer with an iridescent glow. A creature struck and damaged by this dart takes a –2 status penalty to its next saving throw against a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect within 1 round. The target then becomes immune to this effect for 24 hours.
