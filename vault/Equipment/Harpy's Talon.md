**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with the affixed weapon.

---

This sharp metal claw is used to inflict additional pain upon your enemies. When you activate the _harpy's talon_, the weapon deals [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to the number of weapon damage dice. If the Strike was a critical success, the persistent bleed damage increases to twice the number of weapon damage dice.
