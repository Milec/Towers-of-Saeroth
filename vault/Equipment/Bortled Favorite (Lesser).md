An Isgeri specialty from Etran's Folly, this root vegetable porridge was named after the dwarf trader Bort Bargith. When you eat this porridge, you can [[Actions/Hustle (Player Core)|Hustle]] for an additional 10 minutes during exploration. This lasts for 24 hours or until you make your next daily preparations, whichever comes first.
