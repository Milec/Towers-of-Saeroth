An astringent aroma from rare succulent plants wafts off these soothing cloth patches. Adding this catalyst to a _[[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]]_ spell bestows additional [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] to one target healed by the spell. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 minute.
