# Oil of Revelation  *Item 5*

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This bright oil, first created by humans as a tool to help them fight in darkness, holds flecks of tiny gemstones in suspension and smells like a struck matchstick.  
The first time a weapon coated with this oil damages a creature, the wound glows with light for 1 minute. If the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], the light's position means it's merely [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to creatures that would otherwise be unable to see it, rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]. The light also negates the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition due to lighting conditions. If the coated weapon doesn't damage a creature within 1 hour, the oil sloughs off and loses its power.
