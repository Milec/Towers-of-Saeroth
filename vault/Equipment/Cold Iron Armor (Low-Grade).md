# Cold Iron Armor  *Item 5+*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 228

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk**  varies by armor

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|Cold Iron]]

---

Cold iron armor sickens certain creatures that touch it. A creature with weakness to cold iron (such as most [[Traits/Demon|demons]] and [[Traits/Fey|fey]]) that critically fails an unarmed attack against a creature in cold iron armor becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] 1. A creature with weakness to cold iron is sickened 1 as long as it wears cold iron armor.
