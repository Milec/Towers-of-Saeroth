# Bioluminescent Stripes  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 97

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** implanted

---

A line of glands embedded in your skin secretes a chemical that glows when it interacts with the air. As a single action, you can activate your bioluminescent stripes to glow with a [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet. While you are glowing, you can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] and you take a –4 item penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]]. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the glow as a free action.
