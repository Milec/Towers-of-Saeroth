When collapsed, a _fan buckler_ appears to be no more than an elegant wooden fan. Any attempts to discern that there’s more to the item require a successful [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] check against the [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] DC of the wielder.

**Activate—Unfurl Fan** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You transform the fan into a [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|wooden buckler]] or vice versa.
