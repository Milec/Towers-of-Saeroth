# Searing Blade  *Item 13+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 242

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|Longsword]]

---

This _+2 greater striking [[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_ has an ornate brass hilt and a blade shaped like stylized flames. When wielded, the blade projects illumination resembling shimmering firelight, emitting dim light in a 10-foot radius.

**Activate—Shoot Fire** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You cast the [[Spells/Ignition|_ignition_]] cantrip from the sword as a 7th-rank arcane spell, using your melee attack modifier with _searing blade_ as your spell attack modifier.

## Searing Blade (Greater)  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 243

**Price** 13,800 gp

---

This is a _+3 greater striking greater flaming longsword_. When you activate the sword to cast ignition, the spell is 9th rank. This weapon also has the Radiate Flames activation.

**Activate—Radiate Flames** single action ([[Bestiary/Abilities/Aura|aura]], [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=683), [[Magic/Domains/Fire|fire]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** A 10-foot emanation of flame radiates from the _greater searing blade_ for 1 minute. All weapon and unarmed attacks by you and your allies within the area gain the effect of the [[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] property rune.
