# Spellstriker Staff  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 134

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _spellstriker staff_ is wrought iron with gleaming arcane sigils etched into its surface and a sharp point at the bottom. Used as a weapon, the staff is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Shifting|shifting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]]_.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You use [[Actions/Spellstrike]] with a non-cantrip spell and miss with the Strike;  **Effect** An explosion of magical energy—stored up for the Spellstrike—explodes out. All creatures in a 5-foot emanation take 1d6 damage per level of the spell, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC. This damage is the same type the spell would have dealt, and the activation gains that trait; if the spell would have dealt multiple types of damage, choose one of them. If the damage of the explosion is mental, the save is Will instead of Reflex.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** You have the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Secrets of Magic)|magus's]] Spellstrike activity. Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
