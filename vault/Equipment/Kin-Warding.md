# Kin-Warding  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Dwarf]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -148- Fires of the Haunted City|Pathfinder #148: Fires of the Haunted City]] pg. 75

**Price** 52 gp

**Usage** etched onto a  clan dagger

---

A _kin-warding_ clan dagger can deflect attacks aimed at your allies. When you use the weapon’s [[Traits/Parry|parry]] trait, you can point the clan dagger at an adjacent ally instead of defending yourself, creating a shield of runes around them. The runic barrier grants your ally the weapon’s circumstance bonus to AC, but you do not gain the bonus yourself.
