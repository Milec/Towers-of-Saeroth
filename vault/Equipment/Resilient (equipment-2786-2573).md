# Resilient  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 226

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

_Resilient_ runes imbue armor with additional protective magic. This grants the wearer a +1 item bonus to saving throws. You can upgrade the _resilient rune_ already etched on a suit of armor using the normal process for [[Rules/Crafting Items|upgrading items and runes]].
