# Eye of the Mantis  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Prey for Death

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 104

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This 1-inch tall figurine depicts a crouched praying mantis. It’s usually carved from green jade, crimson marble, or purple porphyry. Regardless of the figurine’s material composition, its gemstone eyes are always a dull ruby red that seems to drink light.

**Activate—Phantasmal Assassin** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast 4th-rank [[Spells/Vision of Death|_vision of death_]] (DC 28). To the target, it always appears as if they are being attacked by an immense praying mantis.

**Activate—Scouting Eye**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Scouting Eye|_scouting eye_]].
