# Charlatan's Gloves  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Usage** worn gloves

**Bulk** L

---

Tiny silver hooks decorate these fine silk gloves. They grant a +1 item bonus to Thievery and allow you to cast [[Spells/Telekinetic Hand|_telekinetic hand_]] as an innate occult spell.

## Charlatan's Gloves  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Price** 50 gp

## Charlatan's Gloves (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Price** 600 gp

---

The gloves' bonus increases to +2. If you are also wearing a _[[Equipment/Charlatan's Cape|charlatan's cape]]_, whenever you would move an object using [[Spells/Telekinetic Hand|_telekinetic hand_]], you may instead have it disappear in a puff of smoke and reappear hovering in a space adjacent to yours. This is a [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] effect. The item hovers until the end of your turn or until retrieved with an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action.
