# Primeval Mistletoe  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 574

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This sprig of berry-festooned holly and mistletoe doesn't wilt or rot. It can be used as a primal focus, and it also grants the creature holding it a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** You squeeze juice from one of the berries and smear it onto a [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]] to cast [[Spells/Shillelagh|_shillelagh_]] upon it.  

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You can twine the sprig around the wrist of one hand and touch a tree to cast [[Spells/Tree Shape|_tree shape_]] upon yourself, except instead of a becoming a tree, you become a vine on the touched tree.
