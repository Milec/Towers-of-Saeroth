# Lion's Shield  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 588

**Price** 245 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|steel shield]] (Hardness 6, HP 36, BT 18) is forged into the shape of a roaring lion’s head. The lion’s head functions as _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss|shield boss]]_ that can’t be removed from the shield.

**Activate** single action Raise a Shield;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You animate the lion’s head. You [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raise the Shield]] and make a melee Strike with it as part of the same action. The shield’s biting maw is a martial melee weapon that deals 2d6 piercing damage and has the deadly 1d6 trait; it can’t be enhanced by runes. The shield remains animated for 1 minute, during which time you can Strike with it each time you Raise the Shield, as well as with a Strike action.
