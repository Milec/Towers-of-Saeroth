Alcohol is a common substance available in stunning variety. The Price of a dose of alcohol depends on the specific beverage. You can’t recover from the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition from alcohol while affected.

 If you fail a saving throw against alcohol other than initial saves, the affliction’s stage doesn’t increase; the only way to increase the stage of alcohol’s affliction is to consume more alcohol.

**Saving Throw** DC 12 Fortitude; **Onset** 10 minutes; **Stage 1** +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects (10 minutes); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], +1 item bonus to saving throws against fear effects (10 minutes); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]], flat-footed, [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] (10 minutes); **Stage 4** clumsy 2 and sickened 2 (10 minutes); **Stage 5** clumsy 2, sickened 2, and stupefied 2 (10 minutes); **Stage 6** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] (8 hours); **Stage 7** death
