This chunk of solidified planar energy is as large as a walnut and comes in six different varieties: a clear piece of crystal for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air]], a rough piece of rock for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth]], a solidified piece of cooled lava for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire]], a compact piece of iron for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]], a solid piece of ice for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water]], or a compact mass of plant matter for the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]]. You crack the fragment as you activate it, unleashing the planar energy within. This energy casts a spell of your choice: a 5th-rank [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]] spell affecting you or a 5th-rank [[Spells/Summon Elemental|_summon elemental_]] spell; if you summon an elemental, you can Sustain the activation to keep control of the elemental. The spell's element matches that of the fragment.
