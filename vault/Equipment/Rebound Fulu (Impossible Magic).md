# Rebound Fulu  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 220

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You use the Shield Block reaction with the shield to which this fulu is affixed.

---

The bronze-colored ink on this pale, silvery talisman sharply reflects light, enough that it seems to glow. When you activate the fulu, it reflects your foe’s violence back against them. The foe that triggered the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction takes damage equal to your shield’s [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] or the damage they would’ve dealt before the Shield Block reaction, whichever is less.
