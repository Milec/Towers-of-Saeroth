# Helm of the Tides  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 108

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

This silvery, hydrodynamic helm features curling waves inscribed along its surface. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]].

**Activate—Raise the Tide** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Control Water (Player Core)|control water]]_. If you cast it at night, the spell’s area becomes 100 feet long by 100 feet wide.
