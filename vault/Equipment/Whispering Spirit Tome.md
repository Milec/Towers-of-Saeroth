# Whispering Spirit Tome  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 231

**Price** 230 gp

**Bulk** L

---

Mystical incantations to commune with spirits are engraved on the flexible metal pages of this slender volume, and its gleaming silver cover ripples when touched.

**Activate—Sense Phantasm** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [Cast a prepared Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) with the [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] trait, you attune yourself to the spirit world, granting you greater insight into such phenomena. You gain the effects of _[[Spells/Spirit Sense (Player Core 2)|_spirit sense_]]_ for 1 hour.
