# Bracers of Missile Deflection  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Usage** worn bracers

**Bulk** L

---

These bracers are made from plates of durable dawnsilver and gleam like the summer sun.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A ranged weapon attack hits you but doesn't critically hit; **Requirements** You are aware of the attack and not off-guard; **Effect** The bracers send the missile off-course. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If this would cause the attack to be a failure, the attack misses you.
