This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] trident_ is made of golden metal, its handle carved with reliefs of waves and seashells. These tridents are often wielded by high-ranking [[Bestiary/Families/Azarketi (Monster Core)|azarketi]] soldiers in the ranks of [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]] armies.

**Activate—Force of the Sea** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast a 5th-rank _[[Spells/Hydraulic Torrent (Player Core)|hydraulic torrent]]_. The spell DC is 29.
