# Infiltrator's Accessory  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 261

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sword Cane]]

---

This elegant _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sword Cane|sword cane]]_ serves equally well as a fashionable accessory and hidden weapon suitable for high-society events where weapons aren't typically permitted.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You twist the sword cane's hilt, magically merging the blade into the cane. While the blade is merged, it can't be drawn and gains the benefits of a 3rd-level [[Spells/Magic Aura|_magic aura_]] spell to appear non-magical. Passive observers can't attempt a check to notice the cane is anything more than a mundane, if superb, fashion accessory. Those carefully examining it can discern the cane is more than it appears, but doing so is extremely difficult (DC 30 Perception). Activating the sword cane again releases the blade, allowing it to be drawn normally.
