# Arudora Memento  *Item -1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Bastion of Blasphemies

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Each _Arudora memento_ is unique in appearance, but all seven are approximately the same size. Carried by each of the cursed family members, these mementos have within them the power to unlock entire portions of Bastardhall from the ghostly cursed state the castle traditionally exists in. Each of the seven mementos has the following activation, but the section of castle unlocked by each activation, as well as each memento’s specific benefit granted to the PCs, varies by type.

**Activate—Return Memento** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You place the memento into the appropriate statue’s clutches in the fountain located in area  
**B3** of Bastardhall. The statue briefly animates to claim the memento, which turns to stone and becomes thereafter a permanent part of the statue, effectively destroying the memento while granting a boon to the party that persists for the remainder of this Adventure Path. A section of Bastardhall solidifies from its ghostly form, unlocking its reaches for the PCs to explore.

**Destruction** An _Arudora memento_ is destroyed when it’s activated with Return Memento, and the boon it grants upon this returning lasts until the very end of the Adventure Path.

## Alisendra's Fan  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

When this elegant crimson hand fan is Returned, the dungeon (detailed in Chapter 5) is unlocked. The souls trapped in Bastardhall appreciate the end of Alisendra’s dominion and now whisper hints of truths in the ears of those who seek knowledge. The DCs for all checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] are reduced by 1 for the rest of the Adventure Path.

## Augsten's Cudgel  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

When this bloodstained wooden cudgel is Returned, the reception halls (detailed in Chapter 4) are unlocked. Sometimes, violence is the only answer, and swift action to combat is rewarded. For the rest of the Adventure Path, all PCs gain a +1 bonus to any initiative rolls; this special bonus combines with all other bonuses.

## Dazerien's Thumbscrew  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

When this gory bronze thumbscrew is Returned, the private halls (detailed in Chapter 6) are unlocked. Rest becomes more rewarding now that the sadist of the dungeon has been defeated. For the rest of the Adventure Path, when the PCs take their 8-hour period of rest, they recover an additional number of Hit Points equal to their Constitution modifier (minimum 1) times their level.

## Irrokcis's Medallion  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

When this eerie medallion is Returned, the towers (detailed in Chapter 9) are unlocked, and the spirits of those trapped in Bastardhall rise up to aid the PCs. All weapon Strikes made by PCs deal an additional 1 spirit damage for the rest of the Adventure Path.

## Jinnivere's Riding Crop  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

When this leather riding crop is Returned, the cellars (detailed in Chapter 3) are unlocked. Companions are now more hearty and full of life—PC companions (such as familiars, eidolons, and animal companions) gain a +1 status bonus to all saving throws for the remainder of this Adventure Path.

## Raudltz's Telescope  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L

---

Reduce all Perception DCs made when [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeking]] or [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]] by 2 for the rest of this Adventure Path.

## Ryhasphinea's Hourglass  *Item -1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 235

**Bulk** L
