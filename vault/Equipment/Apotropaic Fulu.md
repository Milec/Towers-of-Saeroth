# Apotropaic Fulu  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 159

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** affixed over an entrance

---

This unassuming yellow paper, affixed above a door or gate, flickers in the light. Pick an alignment trait: [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]], [[Traits/Good|good]], [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]], or [[Traits/Evil|evil]]. When a creature with the opposing alignment trait of the fulu (good for an evil fulu, and so on) passes through the entrance, it must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 2]]; regardless of whether they succeed, the creature becomes temporarily immune for 1 day.
