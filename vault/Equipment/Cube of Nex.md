# Cube of Nex  *Item 25*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

The _Cube of Nex_ acts as a _[[Equipment/Lesser Cube of Nex]]_ except it suppresses all magic within 5 feet of it with the effects of a 10th-level [[Spells/Antimagic Field|_antimagic field_]] spell, instead of just one school. However, spells you cast and magic items you wield ignore the antimagic field from the _Cube of Nex_.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** A _cube of force_ or _Lesser Cube of Nex_ within 60 feet of you becomes inert. It can't be Activated, any current activation ends, and any constant abilities it has cease to function.
