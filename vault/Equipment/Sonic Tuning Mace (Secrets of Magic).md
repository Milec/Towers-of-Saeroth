This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Mace|light mace]]_ has twin tips, perfectly spaced to resonate when striking foes.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You tap the tuning mace against a nearby surface to cast 4th-level [[Spells/Sound Burst|_sound burst_]] with a DC  of 25.
