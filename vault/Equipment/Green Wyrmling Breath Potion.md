# Green Wyrmling Breath Potion  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Beginner Box- Game Master's Guide|Pathfinder Beginner Box: Game Master's Guide]] pg. 53

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This liquid contains blood from a wyrmling [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Green|green dragon]]. For 1 hour after you drink it, you can unleash a cloud of poison as a breath weapon. Exhaling dragon breath uses a single action. The breath weapon deals 2d6 poison damage in a 15-foot cone, and each creature in the area must attempt a DC 23 basic Fortitude save. After you use the breath weapon, you can't do so again for 1d4 rounds.
