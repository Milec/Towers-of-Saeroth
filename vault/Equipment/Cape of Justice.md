# Cape of Justice  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -196- The Summer That Never Was|Pathfinder #196: The Summer That Never Was]] pg. 81

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

While [[Setting/Deities/Yaezhing]] is feared and seldom worshipped in the open, some regions of Tian Xia see him as a god of necessary evil and their only hope for justice. This garment is often worn by bounty hunters or priests of Yaezhing, yet non-worshippers of the god of harsh justice sometimes wear this item without fear of religious persecution. The red cape appears almost black while in the shadows, with a lighter red mandala pattern on it that resembles a shuriken.

 When you invest a _cape of justice_, you must designate a single creature as your offender. You must have met the creature before, or the creature should have a persona well known to the public (for instance, a bandit's epithet, if not their face as shown on a wanted poster).

 You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Demoralize]] this creature. If you don't designate a creature as your offender when you invest the _cape of justice_, or if 24 hours pass without you either slaying or capturing your offender, your thoughts become worried and distracted and you take a –1 item penalty to Perception checks for the next 24 hours.

**Activate** single action command, Interact ([[Traits/Force|force]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** By using a free hand to grab the edge of your cape and give it a quick flourish, you cause a red, shuriken-shaped bolt of force to fire at a target within 60 feet that you can see. The shuriken hits automatically and deals 1d4+1 force damage, or 2d4+2 force damage if the target is your designated offender. If the target is within 30 feet, you can attempt to Demoralize the target as a free action after the shuriken strikes them.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You produce [[Equipment/Manacles|manacles]] from the cape and then fling them at a Medium or Small bipedal target within 30 feet. The target must attempt a DC 18 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected, and the manacles vanish.  

**Success** The manacles strike the target's legs but fail to latch properly. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of your next turn, at which point the manacles vanish.  

**Failure** The manacles lock on the target's legs, causing the target to take a –15-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 minute, or until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] or is freed by someone using [[Actions/Pick a Lock]] (DC 18), after which the manacles vanish.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for as long as the manacles remain in place.
