# Storage  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Adjustment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 11

**Price** 1 gp

**Usage** applied to armor or unarmored defense clothing

**Bulk** L

---

The storage adjustment fits the armor or clothing with belts, buckles, pouches, and loops for holding and storing tools. Countless fasteners make the armor as jangly as chain mail. While wearing armor with this adjustment, you can [[Rules/Wearing Tools|wear]] up to 3 Bulk of tools instead of the usual 2. However, the armor acquires the [[Traits/Noisy|noisy]] trait. If it already has the noisy trait, increase its penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks by 1.
