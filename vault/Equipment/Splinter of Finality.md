# Splinter of Finality  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 124

---

_Note from Nethys: This item is a constituent part of the [[Archetypes/Splinter of Finality]] archetype using the [[Rules/Archetype Artifacts (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|archetype artifact]] rules._

The sharpness of this dark metal splinter never dulls. By plunging it into your neck, you anchor your soul to the fragment, granting you protection from those that would harm it at the cost of weakening your body. You take a –1 status penalty to saving throws against effects that would give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition. At 10th level, the penalty increases to –2. However, you gain spirit [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] equal to your level and a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirits]] or [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]] and effects that would give you the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] conditions.

**Destruction** All 13 _splinters of finality_ must be gathered and used to reconstruct _Silent Lenore_, which must then be destroyed by means of the [[Rituals/Blunt the Final Blade|blunt the final blade]] ritual.
