# Illusionist's Hat  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Bastion of Blasphemies

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 239

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

---

The _illusionist’s hat_ appears as a tall, black hat with a wide brim and a colorful band around its base. Although popularly associated with themes of magic, this hat is more the province of the entertainer or even the charlatan than of a proper arcanist, and many wizards scoff at those who openly wear such items. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks made to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] and to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks made to [[Actions/Palm an Object (Player Core)|Palm an Object]].

**Activate—What's In My Hat?** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a flourish, you remove your hat and sweep it before you. The _illusionist’s hat_ casts a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Creature (Player Core)|_illusory creature_]] to your specifications, save that the creature created must be an animal. As long as you continue to hold your hat in one hand, you can Sustain this illusion as a free action. Unlike with most illusory creatures, you cannot use your actions to speak through the creature.
