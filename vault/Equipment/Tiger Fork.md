# Tiger Fork  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 130

**Price** 275 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|Trident]]

---

The tiger fork is a +[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|1]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|trident]] with the [[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]] and [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]] traits. It has wide, flaring prongs that can be used to fend off deadly beasts and entrap opponents during combat. While you have another creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|Grabbed]] with the tiger fork, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] effects.

**Activate—Fork Grip** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike against a creature; **Effect** You can activate the tiger fork to attempt to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature. This attempt uses the same multiple attack penalty as the required Strike.
