# Bioluminescence Bomb  *Item 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 80

**Price** 3 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

This vial of glowing goo constantly sheds [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in a 10-foot radius. When a bioluminescence bomb strikes a creature or a hard surface, it shatters and releases the bioluminescent fluid's energy in a flare of light. Each creature within 10 feet of where the bomb exploded must succeed at a DC 17 Reflex save or be marked with dye that continues to glow for 24 hours. An affected creature must also attempt a DC 17 Fortitude saving throw against the overwhelming burst of light.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round, then dazzled for 1 round.
