# Brilliant  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 180

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This rune causes a weapon to transform into pure, brilliant energy. The weapon deals an additional 1d4 fire damage on a successful Strike, as well as 1d4 good damage to [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]] and 1d4 positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. On a critical hit, the target must succeed at a DC 29 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.

**Activate** single action command ([[Traits/Light|light]])    **Effect** You plunge your weapon into darkness to return the light. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check with a counteract level of 5 and a +19 counteract modifier to end a magical [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] effect whose area is within reach of the weapon.
