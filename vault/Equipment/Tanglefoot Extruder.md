# Tanglefoot Extruder  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 65

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This portable nozzle-and-trigger assembly based on spider spinnerets can extrude and weave alchemical adhesives into temporary constructions. As an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, you can attach a [[Equipment/Glue Bomb|tanglefoot bag]] to the extruder. A loaded tanglefoot extruder can be activated to consume the attached tanglefoot bag and convert it into a 30-foot rope, whip, or net, depending on the nozzle die you choose when activating the device. The created object lasts for 1 hour. The DC to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] a created rope (if used to bind a creature) or net is equal to the consumed tanglefoot bag’s DC and Escaping destroys the created object.
