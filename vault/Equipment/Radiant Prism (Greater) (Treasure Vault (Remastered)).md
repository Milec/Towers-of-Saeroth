# Radiant Prism  *Item 16+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 127

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This glass prism pays homage to its namesake pantheon—the [[Setting/Deities/Radiant Prism]] of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]], and [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn (Divine Mysteries)|Shelyn]]. Any armor or weapon the prism is affixed to glows softly with colored lights. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 35.

- **Armor** ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) After you cast a non-cantrip spell by activating the prism, you glow with [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in a multitude of shimmering hues, shedding light like a torch and making you [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of your next turn.
- **Weapon** After you cast a non-cantrip spell by activating the prism, your Strikes with the weapon gain the [[Equipment/Brilliant (GM Core)|_brilliant_]] property rune until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Light|_light_]].
