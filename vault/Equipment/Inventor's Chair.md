# Inventor's Chair  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Relic (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Relic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 205

**Bulk** 3

---

Initially crafted by a gifted [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|inventor]] who no longer had the use of their legs, the _inventor’s chair_ is a [traveler’s chair](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1356). It’s a marvel of engineering, utilizing clockwork mechanisms to maneuver about. The chair comes equipped with [[Equipment/Weapons/Wheel Blades|wheel blades]], and portions of its frame contain [[Equipment/Artisan's Toolkit|sterling artisan’s tools]]. The _inventor’s chair_ might be a family heirloom or a gift from someone who inherited it. It could instead sit unused in its inventor’s abandoned estate, awaiting another user.
