# Grudgestone  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Usage** affixed to weapon

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** A creature critically hits you.

---

This dull black pebble, ominously cold to the touch and curiously heavy for its size, is typically bound to the affixed weapon inside a strip of cloth wrapped around its grip. When you Activate the _grudgestone_, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the affixed weapon against the triggering creature gain a +3 status bonus to damage rolls for 1 minute or until the target dies, whichever comes first.
