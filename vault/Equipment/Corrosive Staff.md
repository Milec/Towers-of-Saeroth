# Corrosive Staff  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 238

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A sickly green liquid sloshes inside this hollow glass staff, carrying an acrid scent. While wielding the staff, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 3 to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
