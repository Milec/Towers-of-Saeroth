These whetstones are miniature stone replicas of weapons, such as a sword or an axe, though their function is the same regardless of their shape. When you attempt a Strike as a reaction (such as with a [[Actions/Ready (Player Core)|Readied]] action or the [[Feats/Reactive Strike]] reaction) using a weapon under the effect of a reactive flash, the target must first succeed at a DC 19 Reflex save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the attack.
