# Ghost Lantern  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 115

**Price** 1,850 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

Crafted from [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]], this black hooded [[Equipment/Lantern (Player Core)|lantern]] has gray glass and lenses that emit a pale gray light when the lantern is lit. Anything this light falls on looks gray or desaturated. The lantern uses oil as a standard hooded lantern. The lantern’s light shines within the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]] as well as [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]]. On any other plane, the light functions as a normal hooded lantern.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The lantern's shutters are open; **Effect** You concentrate on the lantern’s light to soften the boundary between the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]] and [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]]. Any creature in the lantern’s bright light on the Universe gains the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune on all its weapons and unarmed attacks. If an affected weapon or attack is [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] and already has the maximum number of property runes, the wielder can choose one to suppress to gain ghost touch. This benefit lasts for 5 minutes or until the shutters are closed, whichever comes first. It applies to a creature only while it’s in the lantern’s [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]], and if the creature leaves the light and returns it regains the benefit once more.
