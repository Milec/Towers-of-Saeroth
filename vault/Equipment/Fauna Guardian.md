# Fauna Guardian  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 120

**Price** 675 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

A _fauna guardian_ is a tattoo of an animal, which you can temporarily animate to protect you. The animal must be chosen when you get the tattoo, and can be any [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] of 4th level or lower. (Your GM might allow other options.)

**Activate** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You animate your tattoo using the duration and other parameters of a 5th-rank [[Spells/Summon Animal (Player Core)|_summon animal_]] spell. It appears in a space adjacent to you. If your tattoo animal drops to 0 HP, the activation ends, and the inanimate tattoo returns to your skin.
