# Snagging Hook Snare  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 256

**Price** 180 gp

---

This snare snags a creature with its wicked metal hooks. The first creature to enter the square takes 5d8 piercing damage and 5d8 slashing damage, with a DC 27 basic Reflex save. On a critical failure, the hooks piercing its flesh make the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until it successfully [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 27).
