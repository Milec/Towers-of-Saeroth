# Ghost Delivery Fulu  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 77

**Price** 100 gp

**Duration** 1 week

**Usage** affixed to structure, object, or creature

---

A _ghost delivery fulu's_ inscription resembles a dovecote with spirits around it, and it has a prominent stamp in red wax. This fulu activates once affixed, becoming the homing location of [[Equipment/Ghost Courier Fulu (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_ghost courier fulus_]] with a matching stamp. Delivered _ghost courier fulu_ messages stick to the _ghost delivery fulu_ but can be easily removed.
