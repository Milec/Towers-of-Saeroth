# Storage  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Adjustment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 11

**Price** 1 gp

**Usage** applied to armor or unarmored defense clothing

**Bulk** L

---

The storage adjustment fits the armor or clothing with belts, buckles, pouches, and loops for holding and storing tools. Countless fasteners make the armor as jangly as [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail (Player Core)|chain mail]]. While wearing armor with this adjustment, you can wear up to 3 Bulk of tools instead of the usual 2. However, the armor acquires the [[Traits/Noisy (Player Core)|noisy]] trait. If it already has the noisy trait, increase its penalty to Stealth checks by 1.
