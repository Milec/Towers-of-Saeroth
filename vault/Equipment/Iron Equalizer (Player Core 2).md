single action

---

This small iron band has a shifting weight that helps equalize the affixed weapon's balance. When you activate it, you use [[Feats/Certain Strike (Player Core)|Certain Strike]], as the fighter feat. You must meet the normal requirements, including those of the [[Traits/Press (Player Core)|press]] trait.

If you have the Certain Strike feat, the failure effect increases to deal the weapon's normal damage.
