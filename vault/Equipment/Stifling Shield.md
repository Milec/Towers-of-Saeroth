# Stifling Shield  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 207

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

This matte black _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|moderate reinforcing]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]_ ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 10, HP 92, BT 46) seems to absorb sound, creating an eerie void around its wielder.

**Activate—Silence Falls** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature within 15 feet of you Casts a Spell or takes an action with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait while you have the _stifling shield_ raised; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The shield briefly dampens nearby sound. The triggering creature must attempt a DC 28 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes a –1 status penalty to any check or DC related to the triggering action.  

**Failure** As success, but the penalty is –2.  

**Critical Failure** The action is [[Rules/Disrupted and Lost Spells|disrupted]].
