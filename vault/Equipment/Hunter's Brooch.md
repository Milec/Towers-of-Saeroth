# Hunter's Brooch  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Positive]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 71

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

While wearing this silver religious symbol of [[Setting/Deities/Erastil]], you can cast the [[Spells/Disrupt Undead|_disrupt undead_]] cantrip as an innate divine spell.

**Activate** three actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** By touching a weapon you wield to the symbol and uttering a plea for Erastil's steadying hand, you grant that weapon the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]] trait. Against [[Traits/Undead|undead]], the weapon instead gains the [[Traits/Fatal|fatal d12]] trait. This blessing lasts for 1 minute, until you score a critical hit with the weapon, or until you aren't wielding the weapon.
