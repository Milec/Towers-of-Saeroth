# Forge Warden  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 234

**Price** 975 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

The religious symbol of Torag, the forge god—an ornate hammer of dwarven construction—adorns the face of this _lesser reinforcing steel shield_ (Hardness 8, HP 72, BT 36). The shield is a religious symbol of Torag.  
 You and any adjacent allies have fire resistance 5 while you have the shield raised. When used for a [[Feats/Shield Block]], the _forge warden_ rings out like the hammer strike of a blacksmith, and the symbol glows as if lit by the fires of a furnace.

**Activate—Forge Fire** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]])  **Trigger** You use the _forge warden_ to Shield Block an adjacent creature's attack, and the shield takes damage;  **Effect** The attacking creature takes 2d6 fire damage.
