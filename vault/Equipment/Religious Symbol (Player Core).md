# Religious Symbol  *Item 0+*

This piece of wood or silver is emblazoned with an image representing a deity. Some divine spellcasters, such as [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|clerics]], can use a religious symbol to use certain abilities. A religious symbol can be worn on the body on a chain or pin, or can be held.
