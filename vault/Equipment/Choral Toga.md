# Choral Toga  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 81

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** L

---

This elegant toga is infused with inexhaustible energy to enjoy life possessed by [[Bestiary/Families/Azata (Monster Core)|azatas]]. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] 20 and become immune to [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]]. When you [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] in the robes, you either increase your Constitution score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score. If you are [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you become deafened while wearing the toga.

**Activate—Elysium's Grasp** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You are exposed to an [[Traits/Inhaled (GM Core)|inhaled]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]];  **Effect** Purifying gusts of sweet-scented air swirl around you, making you immune to inhaled poisons for 1 minute.

**Activate—Elysium's Breath**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]])    **Effect** The air around the robe constantly circulates to keep you healthy. For 8 hours, you become immune to [[Rules/Diseases (GM Core)|diseases]] spread via inhalation, [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]] effects, and environmental effects that would prevent you from breathing (including being underwater or from being strangled).
