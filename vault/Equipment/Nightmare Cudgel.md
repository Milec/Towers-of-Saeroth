# Nightmare Cudgel  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -161- Belly of the Black Whale|Pathfinder #161: Belly of the Black Whale]] pg. 78

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Club]]

---

Crafted from polished wood, this seemingly mundane _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]]_ comes with a red leather strap near the handle. The mere sight of the cudgel in a guard's hand fills prisoners with dread. Good creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] while carrying, wielding, or wearing this item crafted by the sea-witches.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You initiate an intimidating walk, thumping the cudgel menacingly against your palm. As part of activating this item, you can Stride twice. For the next minute, whenever you successfully Strike a creature with the _nightmare cudgel_, you can use a free action to attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the struck creature.
