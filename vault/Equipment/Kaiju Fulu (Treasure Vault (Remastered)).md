# Kaiju Fulu  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 78

**Price** 500 gp

**Duration** 1 week

**Usage** affixed to a load-bearing wall or pillar

---

Despite the name, a _kaiju fulu_ protects a building against damage of all sorts. When an affixed structure no larger than 100 feet × 100 feet and up to 50 feet tall takes damage, the structure is as hard as [[Equipment/Adamantine|standard-grade adamantine]] against that damage and any damage that occurs within 1 hour thereafter. The fulu then crumbles to dust, and its effects end.
