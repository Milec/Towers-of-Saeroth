You gain unparalleled sensitivity to the tiniest of vibrations through solid surfaces and even the very air around you, but your eyes become useless.

**Benefit** For 1 minute, you gain [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise]] echolocation out to 40 feet, [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]] [[Rules/Tremorsense (Player Core)|tremorsense]] out to 60 feet, and a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks using either of these senses.

**Drawback** You are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 minute.
