# Phantom Shroud  *Item 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -185- A Taste of Ashes|Pathfinder #185: A Taste of Ashes]] pg. 78

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This pale blue cloak is wispy, thin, and cold to the touch. When worn, the cloak turns your hands pale and translucent, imbuing you with the dangerous touch of a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost]]. This effect doesn't impede the normal use of your hands. You also gain an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 3d6 negative damage with the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Magical|magical]] traits. You gain the benefits of a [[Equipment/Weapon Potency|_+2 weapon potency_]] rune with these unarmed attacks (granting a +2 item bonus to your attack rolls).

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You fold the cloak around yourself, and it casts [[Spells/Ethereal Jaunt|_ethereal jaunt_]] on you. You can Sustain the activation for up to 10 minutes. When the activation ends, you return to material form.
