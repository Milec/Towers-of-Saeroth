# Elixir of the Peaks  *Item 4+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 69

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

With much of New Thassilon located among the rugged heights of the Kodar Mountains, these elixirs have grown in popularity among those frequently travel the region.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** For the listed duration, drinking this elixir while in mountainous terrain protects you from the effects of severe cold and grants you an item bonus to Athletics checks made to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], as well as to saving throws against the effects of high altitude.
