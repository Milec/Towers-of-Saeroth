# Remorhaz Armor  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 19

**Price** 975 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [Niyaháat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Armor.aspx?ID=55)

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant (GM Core)|fire-resistant]] [niyaháat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Armor.aspx?ID=55)_ is often created to mark the passage of an Erutaki warrior into adulthood, formed from the chitinous plates of a [[Bestiary/Remorhaz|remorhaz]], with larger pieces protecting the chest, shoulders, and head. As you fight, the armor glows red hot.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The armor is sheathed in a [[Spells/Fire Shield (Player Core)|_fire shield_]].

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You’re dealt 30 or more fire damage, before any reduction from your fire resistance; **Requirements** You’ve used the fire shield activation already today; **Effect** The _fire shield_ activation recharges.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the carapace of a [[Bestiary/Remorhaz|remorhaz]].
