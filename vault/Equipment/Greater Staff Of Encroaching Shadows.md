# Staff Of Encroaching Shadows  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 239

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This obsidian staff is as black as ink, and tendrils of shadow writhe around it. While wielding this staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks while in [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
