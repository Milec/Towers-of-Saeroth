# Irritating Thorn Snare  *Item 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -176- Lost Mammoth Valley|Pathfinder #176: Lost Mammoth Valley]] pg. 79

**Price** 15 gp

---

You dip thorns, spines, or pine needles in a mild toxin that causes skin irritation and swelling, then lash them together into a ball. When a creature enters the snare's square, the thorny ball is lobbed at that creature, dealing 3d8 piercing damage. The creature must attempt a DC 19 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]].  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and 2d6 persistent poison damage.
