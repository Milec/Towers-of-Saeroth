Hot tea with a comforting, flowery aroma, a soothing toddy grants you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects and against effects with a trait determined by the liquor mixed into the tea when it’s created. These benefits last for 1 hour.

- **Amaretto** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]]
- **Limoncello** [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|Olfactory]]
- **Whiskey** [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]
