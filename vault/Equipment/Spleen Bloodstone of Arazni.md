# Spleen Bloodstone of Arazni  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 110

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The _Spleen Bloodstone of Arazni_ represents freedom. Holding the _Spleen Bloodstone_ makes you feel as if no bonds can contain you.

**Activate—Sure Footing** single action to three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;  **Requirements** You aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]]; **Effect** You [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] or [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], treating both [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult and greater difficult terrain]] as normal terrain during the movement. For each additional action you spend, you can Step or Stride again with the same benefits. If you have a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]] or [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]], you can instead Climb or Fly respectively.

**Activate—Break Bonds** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You worship [[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]] or are favored by her; **Effect** The [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] conditions, as well as any Speed penalties affecting you, immediately end unless the effect is [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] and of 12th level or higher. You can use this ability even if your actions are restricted or otherwise decided for you (such as being confused or controlled).

**Destruction** If a worshipper of Arazni willingly gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition or becomes [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] while holding the _Spleen Bloodstone_, the _Bloodstone_ explodes, dealing 10d6 [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage to all creatures in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
