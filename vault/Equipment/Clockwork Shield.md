**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

This brass-coated, [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]] (Hardness 12, HP 90, BT 45) has _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes (Player Core)|shield spikes]]_ made of bronze gears.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The shield’s gears begin to spin, subtly adjusting the shield’s position as you fight. You gain an extra reaction this turn and at the start of each of your turns for the next minute that you can use only to [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]].
