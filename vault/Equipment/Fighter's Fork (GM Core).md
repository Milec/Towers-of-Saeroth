**Activate—Fluid Length** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You extend or shorten the trident's haft. When extended, the trident requires two hands to wield and gains the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait, but loses the trident's normal thrown trait.
