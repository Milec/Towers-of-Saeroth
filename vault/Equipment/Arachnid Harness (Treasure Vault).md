# Arachnid Harness  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 14

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ has four knobbed ribs that wrap around the torso. The armor grants you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to poison damage.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The ribs from the armor spread into functional spider limbs. For 1 minute, you gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] equal to half your land Speed and have your limbs free as you climb. If you use all your free limbs to help you [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], your climb Speed equals your land Speed.

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 14

**Price** 360 gp
