# Verdant Branch  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 108

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

Woody vines and branches curl and twist around each other, growing small twigs, leaves, and flowers across the surface of this prosthetic arm or leg.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** A branch grows from your prosthesis, quickly flowering and then producing 1d4 ripe and flavorful fruits. A creature can pick and eat a fruit with an Interact action to regain 2d6+5 Hit Points. The fruits wither away after 10 minutes.
