The _Eye of the Slumberer_ is an [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]] relic resembling a tangle of barnacle-encrusted tentacles. Though the orb is too large to move easily, the artifact’s owner (referred to as the bearer) can activate its powers by wearing the _Amulet of the Slumberer_, a companion item shaped like a barnacle-encrusted cephalopod eye; this is a worn item that has the invested and occult traits. Only one such amulet can exist at any given time, and the artifact can grow a new one in 10 minutes if the original is destroyed. The _Eye’s_ abilities that require a saving throw use the higher of the amulet wearer’s spell DC or class DC.

As one of the alghollthus’ ancient contingencies to control their servitors, the _Eye_ absorbs thoughts—especially those of pain and anguish—storing them like a battery. On command, it can unleash the stored energy, potentially stunning whole ecosystems. Each time a creature with the aquatic, amphibious, or water trait falls asleep or otherwise passes the night within 1 mile of the _Eye of the Slumberer_, it dreams fitfully of the artifact; creatures that don’t dream are immune to this effect. When it awakens, the creature must succeed at a DC 45 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly (Player Core)|friendly]] toward the bearer (or [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful (Player Core)|helpful]] on a critical failure) until the next time it rests; this is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect. The _Amulet’s_ bearer isn’t automatically aware of creatures influenced in this fashion, but they can identify such creatures on sight.

**Activate—Open the Eye** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are wearing the _Amulet of the Slumberer_ and are within 100 feet of the _Eye of the Slumberer_; **Effect** The amulet’s eye opens and absorbs fears from creatures within a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], subjecting them to [[Spells/Nightmare (Player Core)|_nightmare_]] as if they’d spent a night under the influence of the spell. Additionally, each creature with the [[Traits/Dream (GM Core)|dream]] or [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] trait within 500 feet of the _Eye of the Slumberer_ takes 20d6 force damage (DC 45 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Activate—Vortex** single action ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], water)   **Requirements** You’re wearing the _Amulet of the Slumberer_, you’re within 100 feet of the _Eye of the Slumberer,_ and the _Eye_ is within water; **Effect** The [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] around the _Eye_ churns violently in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], becoming [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for swimming creatures that don’t have the water trait. You can use this action again to [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect.

**Destruction** The _Eye of the Slumberer_ is destroyed if it’s brought to the [[Setting/Planes/Dreamlands (GM Core)|Dreamlands]] and either fed to a [[Setting/Deity Categories/Outer Gods and Great Old Ones|Great Old One]] or encircled by joyful celebrants for 101 days, during which time none of the dancers can experiences despair.
