# Harmonic Hauberk  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 84

**Price** 2,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt]]

---

Rose gold and copper rings in this _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt|chain shirt]]_ form a vague bird shape. The jingling links tinkle musically, almost like birdsong. While you wear the armor, effects with the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] trait must first [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the _harmonic hauberk_ or they have no effect on you.

 The _harmonic hauberk_ attempts to counteract [[Spells/Silence|_silence_]] spells cast on you and 4th-level or higher heightened _silence_ spells the first time the armor enters their area (counteract level 4, counteract modifier +20). After failing to counteract a _silence_ spell, the armor can't attempt to counteract the same spell again, even if you leave and enter the spell's area multiple times.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You have a pool of Focus Points and at least one [[Traits/Composition|composition]] spell; **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point and immediately use the Focus Point to cast [[Spells/Lingering Composition|_lingering composition_]], using your [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to determine the effects of the spell, followed by the [[Spells/Inspire Defense|_inspire defense_]] composition cantrip. You can use this activation to cast those spells even if you don't have the [[Feats/Lingering Composition]] or [[Feats/Inspire Defense]] feats.
