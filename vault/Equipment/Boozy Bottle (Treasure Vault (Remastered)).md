# Boozy Bottle  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 119

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This tattoo depicts a container of alcohol, traditionally a small, uncorked brown bottle. You gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Poison|poison]].

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail (but don't critically fail) an initial saving throw against a poison, or you gain [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]];  **Effect** You pick your poison. Calling out the name of a drink as though ordering at a bar, you negate the triggering poison. Instead, you become slightly drunk. For 10 minutes you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] and gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]].
