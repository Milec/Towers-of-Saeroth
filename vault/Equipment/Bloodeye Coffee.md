A strong blend including several spices common in the Padishah Empire of Kelesh, bloodeye coffee is a favorite morning drink across the Inner Sea region. The maximum addiction stage of bloodeye coffee never progresses beyond stage 1.

**Saving Throw** DC 14 Fortitude; **Onset** 10 minutes; **Maximum Duration** 8 hours; **Stage 1** +1 item bonus to AC and saves to which you are taking penalties caused by the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] condition (10 minutes); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (1 hour); **Stage 3** can’t recover from fatigued (1 day, after which the drug ends)
