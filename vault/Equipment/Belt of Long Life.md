# Belt of Long Life  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex (GM Core)|Apex]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 270

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn belt

---

This thick leather belt is engraved with imagery of an ancient tree. You gain 15 temporary Hit Points the first time you invest the belt in a day. When you invest the belt, you either increase your Constitution modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Call Upon the Ancient Life** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw upon the life- giving energy of the tree on the belt to gain rapid healing. For 2d4 rounds, at the start of your turn each round, you recover 15 Hit Points.
