# Doom Switch  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 143

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This short length of wood is decorated with fine carvings of symbols representing fate.

**Activate—Bragging Rights** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You attempt to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] a significant enemy with the _doom switch_, marking them for defeat. The switch is treated as a simple melee weapon for the purpose of proficiency. This attack deals no damage.

**Critical Success** You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls against the target for 1 minute. If you reduce the target to 0 Hit Points during this time, you gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice the target’s level for 1 round.  

**Success** As critical success, except you gain temporary Hit Points equal to the target’s level.  

**Failure** You and your allies take a –1 status penalty to attack rolls against the target for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** You and your allies take a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against the target for 1 round.
