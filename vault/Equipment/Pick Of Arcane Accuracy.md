**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 210

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick (Player Core)|Pick]]

---

The metal head of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Pick (Player Core)|pick]]_ glows faintly, intensifying around the tip. The hilt features a grip wrapped in deep-blue leather and is carved with a hawk’s wings.

**Activate—Mystic Strike** single action ;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;  **Requirements** Your last action was to Cast a non-cantrip Spell; **Effect** You make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the pick that overflows with magical energy. You gain a +1 status bonus to this Strike, and if you hit, you deal an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent force damage]].
