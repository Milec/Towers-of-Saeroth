# Boots of Bounding  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

The springy soles of these sturdy leather boots cushion your feet and make each step lighter. These boots give you a +5- foot item bonus to your Speed and a +2 item bonus to Athletics checks to [[Actions/High Jump]] and [[Actions/Long Jump]]. In addition, when you use the Leap action, you can move 5 feet further if jumping horizontally or 3 feet higher if jumping vertically.
