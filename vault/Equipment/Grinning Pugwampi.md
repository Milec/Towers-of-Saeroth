# Grinning Pugwampi  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Misfortune]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 94

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** affixed to weapon

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You damage a [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] creature with the affixed weapon.

---

This bone statuette of a sneering gremlin crumbles to dust when activated, imparting a fraction of its subject’s infamous [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] on those you strike. The damaged creature must attempt a DC 33 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature must roll twice and take the worse result on the next check it attempts.  

**Failure** The creature must roll twice and take the worse result on all checks until the end of its next turn.
