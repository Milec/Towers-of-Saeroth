# Wand of Legerdemain  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 140

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wand of lacquered black wood has a handle wrapped in interwoven colorful ribbons. A silver bell caps the wand’s tasseled pommel.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You Cast the Spell.

**Activate**  Interact ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]])   **Requirements** The last action you took this turn was to Cast a Spell from the wand; **Effect** You make yourself the center of attention. An illusory spotlight shines [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] upon your space as you pull inane objects from the wand's tip, such as confetti, silk flowers, streamers, or a long string of colorful kerchiefs knotted end to end. Each enemy within 30 feet must attempt a Will save against your spell DC, receiving a +4 circumstance bonus to the save if you or any of your allies recently threatened it or used hostile actions against it. On a failure, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with you until the end of your next turn. The fascination ends if the target is subject to a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile act]], or if another creature succeeds at a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check against it.

The spotlight follows you wherever you move. You can't be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] while in the spotlight. The effect ends if you become [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check, or [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the activation.

You can [[Actions/Sustain an Effect|Sustain]] this Activation for up to 1 minute. Since you need to keep performing tricks, Sustain an Activation has the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait. Sustaining extends the spotlight, and keeps fascinated creatures fascinated, but doesn't cause creatures not already fascinated to become fascinated.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate level.
