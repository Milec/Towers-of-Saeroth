# Windlass Bolas  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 34

**Price** 200 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [Bola](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=433)

---

Clockwork mechanisms tick away inside the weights of these _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [bolas](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=433)_, spooling out more cord in midair.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The cord of the bola snakes out as you throw it, allowing the weapon to wrap around a group of enemies. You use the bolas to attempt a ranged [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] against all creatures in a 5-foot radius burst within the weapon’s first range increment. Roll a separate [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check for each target. Each attempt counts toward your multiple attack penalty, but don’t increase your penalty until you have made all the attempts.
