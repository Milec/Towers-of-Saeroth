**Trigger** A creature with the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait successfully Strikes you.

---

Frightened animals depicted on this fulu flee in all directions from a central figure (traditionally represented by a human hunter, but sometimes depicted as a skeletal [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature or even a [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] with long, broken arms). You activate this fulu, gaining a +2 item bonus to AC against the triggering Strike. If this causes the Strike to miss, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] from the triggering creature until the start of your next turn.
