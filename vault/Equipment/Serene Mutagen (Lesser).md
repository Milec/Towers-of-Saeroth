# Serene Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

You gain inner serenity, but you find violence off-putting.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to Will saves and Perception, [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]], and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks. This bonus improves when you attempt Will saves against mental effects.

**Drawback** You take a –1 penalty to attack rolls and save DCs of offensive spells, and a –1 penalty per damage die to all weapon, unarmed attack, and spell damage.
