# Laboratory Coat  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 203

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|Explorer's Clothing]]

---

This off-white laboratory coat is _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Slick (GM Core)|slick]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer’s clothing]]_ with many pockets and a stitched-on utility belt to hold useful tools. It was designed by a scholarly inventor who had a penchant for risky experiments. This coat grants the wearer a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks and an escape route should one’s experiments go awry.

**Activate—Quick Escape** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], teleportation) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You tap a magical button and utter the code word to instantly transport yourself and any items you’re wearing and holding from your current space to an unoccupied space within 20 feet of you that you can see.
