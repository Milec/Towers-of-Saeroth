# Soulcutter (Artifact)  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|No Alignment]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Intelligent]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -212- A Voice in the Blight|Pathfinder #212: A Voice in the Blight]] pg. 73

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

---

**Perception** +34; precise [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and hearing within 30 feet

**Communication** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]] (partner only)

**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] +34, [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] +34, [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] +34

**Int** +7, **Wis** +6, **Cha** +5

**Will** +34

---  

The echo of the [[Setting/Deities/Calistria (Divine Mysteries)|Calistrian]] witch Silisifex imbues her legendary blade with a shimmering pale-green glow equivalent to that of candlelight; _Soulcutter_ can activate or deactivate this radiance as a free action once per round on her partner’s turn. _Soulcutter’s_ telepathic voice is a sultry whisper that often infuses her observations with a flirtatious edge or a biting sense of dark humor.

_Soulcutter_ is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 major striking]] [[Equipment/Astral|greater astral]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Elven Curve Blade (Player Core)|elven curve blade]]_. As long as you carry _Soulcutter_, you gain her potency bonus as an item bonus to all saving throws against mental effects. This bonus increases by 2 to +5 against [[Traits/Possession (Player Core)|possession]] effects.

**Activate—Soothe Souls** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Soulcutter_ creates a pulse of restorative energy, rejuvenating those within a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you while castigating those in that area who have no place in nature. _Soulcutter_ can [[Actions/Sustain]] this activation for up to 1 minute. Living creatures that start their turn in the area regain 2d8 Hit Points, and any [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature that starts their turn in the area takes 2d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save).

**Activate—Soulcutting Storm** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Soulcutter_ casts a 9th-rank [[Spells/Weapon Storm (Player Core)|_weapon storm_]] to your specification, but all damage caused by the spell is [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. If used to damage a creature that’s possessing another creature, this spell does no damage to the possessed creature.

**Activate—Shame Demon** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** _Soulcutter_ targets a [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]] she can see or hear within 30 feet, causing the blade’s green light to well up around the demon for a moment. The demon suffers the effects of its sin vulnerability, if it has any, and is then temporarily immune to Shame Demon for 24 hours. If _Soulcutter’s_ partner wasn’t aware of the normal effects that trigger that demon’s sin vulnerability, they learn it at this time.
