# Vapor Sphere  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 225

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

⤾

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You trigger a trap’s reaction or an enemy that was [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by you makes an attack against you; **Requirements** You’re an expert in the affixed armor and an expert in Reflex saves.

---

Within this strange glass sphere swirls a cloud of smoke that occasionally appears as if it’s trying to escape. You react to a surprise encounter with haste, activating this talisman and causing your body to momentarily become like vapor. Against the trap’s reaction or the attack, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 25 to physical damage and are immune to precision damage. This effect also prevents the reaction or attack from physically moving you (such as falling down a pit or being knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]), and after the reaction or attack, you can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] 5 feet. When you end this flight, you leave your vaporous state and are exposed to any danger still at your location.
