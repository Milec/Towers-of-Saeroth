# Devoted Vestments  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Focused (GM Core)|Focused]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 288

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

---

These vestments are made of panels showing various scenes from the legends of a particular deity. It serves as a religious symbol of that deity, and you gain a +2 item bonus to Religion checks. When you cast [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] or [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]], healing granted to followers of that deity is increased by the rank of the spell.

**Activate—Domain Devotion** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a cleric domain spell for a domain belonging to the deity the vestments are dedicated to. If you don't spend this point by the end of this turn, it is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a cleric who worships the deity tied to the vestments.
