# Wand of Teeming Ghosts  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 142, [[Sources/The Slithering]] pg. 61

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This pale wooden wand is carved to resemble a thigh bone with metal caps at each end. Ghostly tendrils seem to swirl around it every so often.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/False Vitality|_false vitality_]] of the indicated rank. During the duration of the spell, wisps that resemble spirits flit around you as long as you have any temporary Hit Points from _false vitality_, and you can use the following action.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You successfully impart the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] condition on a creature; **Requirements** You have at least 1 temporary Hit Point from _false vitality_; **Effect** You end _false vitality_ and increase the creature's frightened condition value to 2.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _false vitality_ of the appropriate rank.
