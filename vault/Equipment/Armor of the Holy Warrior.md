# Armor of the Holy Warrior  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 88

**Price** 9,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 3

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate]]

---

Based on the armors used by the holy warriors who served under General Arnisant in the Shining Crusade, this well-polished _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate|half plate]]_ is decorated with religious motifs and carved with sacred scripture. While wearing _armor of the holy warrior_, your item bonus from the _greater resilient_ rune increases from +2 to +3; if you upgrade to a _major resilient_ rune, the item bonus instead increases from +3 to +4. An [[Traits/Undead|undead]] that critically fails an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack against you while you wear this armor takes 1d6 positive damage.

 If you are evil, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] while wearing the armor. If you're undead, you also take 2d6 positive damage each round that you wear the armor.

**Activate** three actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The armor casts the three-action version of a 6th-level [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spell.
