# Kraken's Guard  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 38

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|steel shield]] (Hardness 16, HP 96, BT 48) is painted a mottled crimson, and it always glistens as if wet. Its crest is composed of eight appendages that curl around each other, resembling the swirling tentacles of a giant [[Bestiary/Kraken|kraken]]. Two ferocious eyes sit in its center, gleaming with an inner light.

**Activate** two actions Raise a Shield;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You unleash the kraken's wrath. You Raise the Shield. The shield's appendages briefly extend to impossible length as they wriggle and lash out at your foes, dealing 10d10 bludgeoning damage to all enemies in a 30-foot cone (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The shield remains animated for 1 minute, during which you can use it to [[Actions/Grapple]] a creature within 10 feet of you without needing a free hand. When you use the shield to Grapple, you gain a +3 item bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check.
