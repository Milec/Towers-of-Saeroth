# Boots of the Dead  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 149

**Price** 35 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

Perhaps you had no other choice than to steal the boots off a fallen soldier. Yours may have been worn out, full of holes, or even coming apart. Did you take them from a fallen ally or adversary? Everyone looks the same after war has its way. Nevertheless, the guilt you feel weighs you down. You gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws and DCs against forced movement.

**Activate—One of You** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You shuffle your boots, which still stink of the dead, causing one [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature of your choice to think that you too are undead. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the next melee attack you attempt against it before the end of your current turn.
