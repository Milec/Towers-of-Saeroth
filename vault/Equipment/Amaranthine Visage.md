# Amaranthine Visage  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 78

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're from the Mordant Spire

---

The shape of this 2-foot-tall wooden mask, worn by the secretive leaders of the elves who inhabit the Mordant Spire, mirrors the tower itself. While wearing it, your innards turn to purple smoke, giving you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to precision damage.

**Activate—Eternal Renewal** ⤾ ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition would reach the value that would cause you to die;  **Effect** You Sublimate Self. You lose the dying condition, gaining or increasing your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition as normal, and gain [[Rules/Fast Healing and Regeneration (Player Core)|fast healing]] 20 for 1 minute. You can use Eternal Renewal even while [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] or otherwise unable to act.

**Activate—Sublimate Self** two actions (air, [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]])    **Effect** Your body transforms completely into purple vapor for 5 minutes, with the effects of _[[Spells/Vapor Form|vapor form]]_.
