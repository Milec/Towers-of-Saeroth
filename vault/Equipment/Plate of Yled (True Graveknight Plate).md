**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

---

This sinister suit of jet-black _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ was designed to bolster the graveknights of Yled's undead armies, creating unstoppable legions.

The armor gains additional abilities if it is a graveknight's armor. If a graveknight wearing undamaged _Plate of Yled_ is destroyed, the armor rebuilds its body of the course of 1d10 rounds instead of 1d10 days. A living creature who dons the armor is affected by the [[Bestiary/Families/Graveknight|graveknight's curse]] immediately instead of 1 hour.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** Your previous action was a weapon Strike that reduce a living creature to 0 HP; **Effect** **Effect** The creature is targeted with [[Spells/Death Knell|_death knell_]] (DC 20).

**Craft Requirements** The metals used to create this armor must include a fragment from a previous graveknight's armor.
