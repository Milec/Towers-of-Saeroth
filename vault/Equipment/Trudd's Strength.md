# Trudd's Strength  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 101

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Five Kings Mountains have access to this option._

---

This filigree depiction of a warhammer in front of a kiteshaped shield grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks and to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]]. Additionally, once per day, the filigree symbol can be activated to protect your allies.

**Activate—Protect the Clan!** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Protective energy releases in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], granting a +1 status bonus to Armor Class to all allies within the area. The bonus lasts for 1 minute.
