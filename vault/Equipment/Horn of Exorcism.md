# Horn of Exorcism  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 274

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A _horn of exorcism_ is an instrument made from an animal horn, or an object of the same shape from carved wood or ivory.

**Activate—Rattle the Dead** single action ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Blowing into the horn frightens ghosts and evil spirits who can hear its call. Make an Intimidation check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] against all creatures with the undead or unholy trait in a 30-foot emanation. This can affect even a mindless creature with that trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language.

**Activate—Sacred Seeds** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You fill the horn with sacred seeds and then scatter them around you with a twist of your wrist. The horn grants you and your allies in a 30-foot emanation the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]] property rune on all of your weapon and unarmed Strikes for 1 minute.
