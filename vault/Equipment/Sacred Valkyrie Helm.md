# Sacred Valkyrie Helm  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 82

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Lightning crackles from the wings flanking this helm, which is emblazoned with images of valkyries in the service of benevolent deities. You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks and gain [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 20. When you [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] in the helm, you either increase your Strength score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score. If you are [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 as long as you wear the helm.

**Activate—Path of the Pegasus**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _sacred valkyrie helm_ casts [[Spells/Interplanar Teleport|_interplanar teleport_]] to your specifications. When you start this activation, each creature you target with this spell mounts a conjured pegasus who then carries them through reality to arrive at the specified location; each affected creature experiences the 10-minute activation as a kaleidoscopic ride through strange worlds and realities atop a pegasus’s back.

**Activate—Storm's Arms** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The helm casts a 9th-rank [[Spells/Weapon Storm (Player Core)|_weapon storm_]] to your specifications. At your option, all damage caused by this spell is [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] damage—in this option, the duplicated weapons created by the spell appear to be made of lightning.
