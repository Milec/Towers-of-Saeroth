# Duelist's Beacon  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 10

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Due to their connection to the shop’s proprietor, all Pathfinder Society agents have access to the items from Barghest’s Bin_

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler]]

---

A spiked glove is attached to the front of this [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler|buckler]] (Hardness 6, HP 24, BT 12), functioning as _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]]_. Perforations and slits line the defensive side, shining bright light through them when the item's magic is activated.

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You activate the shield's inner light. Attempt a melee Strike with the _duelist's beacon_. On a hit, the target must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round. On a critical hit, the target uses the result one degree of success worse than it rolled for its Fortitude save.
