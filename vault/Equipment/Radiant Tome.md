# Radiant Tome  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 230

**Price** 460 gp

**Bulk** L

---

A flawless diamond embedded into the cover of this tome gleams with an inner light, refracting multicolored rays to the eyes of viewers.

**Activate—Flare** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], light, [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [Cast a prepared Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) with the light trait, the luminescence of the book grows even more intense, emitting [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] for 1 minute. Enemies that start their turn in this bright light must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to this bright light for 1 minute.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the start of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded until the start of your next turn and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute.
