# Staff of Arcane Might  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Staff (GM Core)|Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 279

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This staff of magically hardened wood is topped with a silver sculpture depicting magical runic symbols. A staff of arcane might is a _+1 striking [[Traits/Staff|staff]]_.

**Activate—Sunder** two actions ([[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You destroy the staff, unleashing a blast of arcane power in a 30- foot cone, dealing 2d6 force damage per charge remaining in the staff with a DC 30 basic Reflex save.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
