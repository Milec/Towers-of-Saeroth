# Blessed Reformer  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 35

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer (Player Core)|Warhammer]]

---

The Celestial word for “repent” is etched in golden lettering on the shaft of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Merciful (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|merciful]] [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|mithral]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer (Player Core)|warhammer]]_. An [[Traits/Evil|evil]]-aligned creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 when carrying or wielding this weapon.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You hold your weapon aloft, calling on righteousness to steel your resolve. You cast [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]] on yourself and you must select [[Traits/Evil|evil]] as the chosen alignment.

**Redeemer Wielder** If you’re a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|champion]] of the [[Classes/Subclasses/Causes/Redemption|redeemer]] cause, you also gain the following two benefits.

- When you critically hit an evil creature with the blessed reformer, until the end of your next turn, the target takes a –10-foot penalty to its Speeds and can’t [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]].
- You can Activate the weapon in the following way.

**Activate** single action command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You hit a creature using the _blessed reformer_ as your last action; **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Calm|_calm emotions_]] at the same level as your [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-868)|champion]] focus spells. It must target the creature you hit, and the creature takes a status penalty on its save against the spell equal to the _blessed reformer’s_ number of weapon damage dice.

**Craft Requirements** You’re a champion with the redeemer cause. The initial materials must include 140 gp of [[Equipment/Dawnsilver Armor|mithral]].
