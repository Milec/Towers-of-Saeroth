This magical banner is dip-dyed in an ombre from black to red, mottled and uneven. Whenever you or an ally within the banner’s aura critically succeeds with a Strike against an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] target, the Strike deals an additional 1d4 precision damage.
