# Longnight Tea  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Tea]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -197- Let the Leaves Fall|Pathfinder #197: Let the Leaves Fall]] pg. 77

**Price** 12 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact or 10 minutes (concentrate, Interact)

---

This tea is brewed with a mixture of matcha, turmeric, and 10 drops of morning dew, creating a fragrant but mildly bitter tea. The bitter aftertaste is said to be the most important element of _longnight tea_, for it's believed that this flavor lingers on the tongue and aids in keeping you awake and alert. The tea is favored by scholars and soldiers alike, although the value to which each attributes the ability to endure long nights without sleeping varies—and is often the subject of unusually impassioned debate. When you drink _longnight tea_, it grants you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] effects for 1 hour.

---

 [[Traits/Tea|Tea Ceremony]] The duration increases to 8 hours, and the tea removes the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition.
