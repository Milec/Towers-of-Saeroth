# Core Bugle  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 143

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This brass horn is marked by age but remains in excellent condition. The rim of the mouth is ringed by finely carved runes. This bugle grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Reveille** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You blow a swift cadence of sharp notes that carries through the air. You and all allies within a 30- foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] can immediately [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action; this doesn’t provoke reactions.
