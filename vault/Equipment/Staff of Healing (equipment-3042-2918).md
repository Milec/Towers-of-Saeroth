# Staff of Healing  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Staff (GM Core)|Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 280

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This white wood staff is capped at each end with a golden cross adorned with ruby cabochons. A staff of healing grants an item bonus to the Hit Points you restore anytime you cast the heal spell using your own spell slots or charges from the staff.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
