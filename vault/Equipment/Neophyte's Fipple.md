# Neophyte's Fipple  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 97

**Price** 8 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

[[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]]

**Requirement** You are untrained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]].

---

Made of polished wood, a _neophyte’s fipple_ is a block flute enchanted to guarantee melodic sound. When you [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|Perform]] a song on the fipple to Activate it, your ability modifier, proficiency bonus, and item bonus for the [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check total +7, regardless of what they would normally be. Add other bonuses and penalties to the check normally. Once the magic is used, the fipple remains as a mundane instrument.
