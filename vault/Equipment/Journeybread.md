# Journeybread  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 49

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Journeybread contains a mix of fruits, nuts, and grains with an alchemical boost. Eating one journeybread provides all the food and water you need for a day. If you [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|subsist]] on nothing else for a week, you become temporarily immune to journeybread until you eat real food and drink water normally for 24 hours.

## Journeybread  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 49

**Price** 3 gp

## Journeybread (Power)  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 49

**Price** 15 gp

---

In addition to the benefits of journeybread, power journeybread grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]], and [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], and to Fortitude saving throws against being [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]]. The number of minutes you can Hustle changes to your Constitution modifier × 20. These benefits last for 4 hours.
