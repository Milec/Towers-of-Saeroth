# Dragon Scute  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 223

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The thick plates that form the strongest points in a dragon’s armor can be used as powerful defensive catalysts. Using one of these scales to cast [[Spells/Mountain Resilience|_mountain resilience_]] causes the target’s skin to grow a thick layer of draconic scales. Instead of the spell’s normal effects, the target gains damage [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] based on the type and dragon’s tradition.

## Lesser Dragon Scute  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 223

**Price** 400 gp

---

The scale can be used with _mountain resilience_ of 6th rank or higher.

**[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]** resistance 5 against all damage from spells

[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|**Divine**]] resistance 10 to [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], and [[Traits/Void|void]]

**[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]** resistance 10 to mental

**[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]** resistance 7 to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]

## Moderate Dragon Scute  *Item 16*
