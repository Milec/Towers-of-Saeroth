# Grudge Journal  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 229

**Price** 1,350 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The margins of this clothbound journal’s pages are filled with complaints on all manner of topics, shifting to match whatever angers the bearer most.

**Activate—Air Grievance** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You inscribe the name or description of a creature you can see that has wronged you in some way. If your next action is to target that creature with a prepared spell with the [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] trait that requires a saving throw, the creature also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 unless it critically succeeds its saving throw.
