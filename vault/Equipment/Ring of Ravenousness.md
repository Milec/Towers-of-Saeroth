# Ring of Ravenousness  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 192

**Usage** worn

---

Carved of acacia wood, a _ring of ravenousness_ is inlaid with a geometric pattern. The ring appears to be a [[Equipment/Ring of Sustenance (GM Core)|_ring of sustenance_]]. Once you invest it, though, it fuses to you, its effects activating immediately. While wearing the ring, you require twice the normal amount of food and drink for a creature your size to avoid starvation and thirst.
