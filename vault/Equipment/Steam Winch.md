# Steam Winch  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Steam (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Steam]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 89

**Price** 425 gp

**Usage** operated with two hands

**Bulk** 2

---

This hefty winch is powered by a small steam engine and includes a 100 ft. length of steel cable, which enables you to haul a heavier load than you could with a hand cranked winch or [[Equipment/Comealong|comealong]]. A steam winch allows you to slowly pull a heavy load (usually up to 50 Bulk) along a flat surface or up and down a vertical expanse. Attaching a steam winch to a device takes three [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions, while starting the winch (and getting it warm enough to operate) takes 10 minutes. Once in operation, a steam winch requires regular maintenance to function. Every twenty minutes, the boiler must be refilled and heat levels maintained.
