# Hagbane Biscuit  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Troubles in Grayce

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Processed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Processed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Troubles in Grayce]] pg. 87

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This gingerbread biscuit is stamped in the shape of a menacing witch, her features decorated with anise and fennel seeds. When you eat the biscuit, its sharp spices warm your body for 10 minutes, granting you a +1 status bonus [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks to pierce a [[Bestiary/Families/Hag (Monster Core)|hag’s]] disguise, to skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about hags, and to saving throws made against hags’ abilities and spells. During this time, weapons you wield count as [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] for any physical attacks made against hags.

The biscuit tastes especially bitter to hags, [[Ancestries/Changeling (Player Core)|changelings]], and other hag-related creatures (such as a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerer]] with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|hag bloodline]]). If such a creature attempts to eat the biscuit, they must succeed at a DC 18 Fortitude save or it fails and wastes the action (and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 on a critical failure).
