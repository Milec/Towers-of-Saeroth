# Mercurial Mantle  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 226

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This deep red cloak fits lightly about your shoulders, and the edges perpetually twitch slightly, as though caught in a breeze. The cloth feels smoother than silk, rippling and swaying like liquid when in motion. You feel a lively energy infusing your arms and legs. You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] and a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against attacks from reactions triggered by your movement. When you [[Rules/Investing Magic Items (GM Core)|invest]] the cloak, you either increase your Dexterity modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Cunning Dodge** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** An enemy misses you with a melee Strike;  **Effect** You slip around the attacking creature with ease. You [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], without moving out of the triggering enemy’s reach, and then make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against the triggering enemy if it’s within your reach. If you do make a Strike, the target attempts a DC 38 [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] check before you roll.

**Failure** This creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the Strike.  

**Critical Failure** This creature is off-guard against all your attacks until the end of its next turn.

**Activate—Wandering Twirl** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], teleportation) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The cloak hums with power as your whirl it around yourself, disappearing amid a brief flash of light. Teleport up to double your [[Rules/Speed (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Speed]] to a location you can see. At the end of the teleportation, you can make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against a creature within reach, if there is one.
