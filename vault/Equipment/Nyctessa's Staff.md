# Nyctessa's Staff  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -5|Wake the Dead #5]] pg. 23

**Price** 90 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This macabre staff is crafted from gleaming white bone, a spinal column spiraling out of its base with a skull swinging from the spine's tip. It was handcraft for Nyctessa by her father, a powerful Blood Lord, from her mortal mother's bones.  
When you damage a creature with _Nyctessa’s staff_, that creature gains weakens 2 to negative damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Chill Touch|_chill touch_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Animate Dead|_animate dead_]], [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]]
