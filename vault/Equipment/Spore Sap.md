# Spore Sap  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fungus]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -154- Siege of the Dinosaurs|Pathfinder #154: Siege of the Dinosaurs]] pg. 76

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap]]

---

This flexible _+2 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Sap|sap]]_ is crafted from the stalks of cave fungi and bound around a mesh of spore-infused gills. Demon-worshipping [[Bestiary/Families/Xulgath|xulgaths]] use _spore saps_ to capture spellcasters alive for later sacrifice.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Frequency** three times per day; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with the _spore sap_;  **Effect** The _spore sap_ releases a puff of stupefying fungal spores. The target must succeed at a DC 28 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute (stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
