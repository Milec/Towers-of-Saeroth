This _+2 greater resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ was made from the flayed hide of an Elysian pegasus, then dyed a bright crimson so that it appears slick with fresh blood. The wearer of assassin's skin gains a +3 item bonus to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] checks.

**Activate—Blood Revitalization** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You would take persistent bleed damage;  **Effect** You don’t take the persistent bleed damage and instead regain Hit Points equal to the bleed damage. The persistent bleed damage ends.
