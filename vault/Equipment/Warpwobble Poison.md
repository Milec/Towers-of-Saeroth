**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Injury]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Bulk** L

two actions

Warpwobble poison causes hallucinations of space bending and stretching, leading to vertigo and an inability to discern a stable place to move.

**Saving Throw** DC 26 Will; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** treat all squares as difficult terrain (1 round); **Stage 2** treat all squares as greater [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] (1 round); **Stage 3** treat all squares as [[Rules/Uneven Ground (Player Core)|uneven ground]] (DC 26), treating a critical success to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] as a success, and a success as a success but moving on greater difficult terrain (1 round)
