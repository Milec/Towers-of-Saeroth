# Lion's Armor  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 230

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 3

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate (Player Core)|Half Plate]]

---

Various parts of this _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Half Plate (Player Core)|half plate]]_ are forged into the shape of a lion’s head. The golden lion heads grant you a commanding presence and seem to actively growl at your enemies, granting you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]].

The lions on the armor work in tandem with the lion on a _[[Equipment/Lion's Shield (GM Core)|lion's shield]]_. If you critically hit with the _lion’s shield_ using Lion Bite, the lions on the armor roar in support of the shield, causing the target of the shield’s Strike to become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].

**Activate—Roar of the Pride** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The lions on your armor roar, attempting to cow your enemies. Attempt a check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet of you. On a critical success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.
