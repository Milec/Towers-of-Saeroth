# Scope of Limning  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 182

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters with access to firearms gain access to accessories that can be used with those weapons._

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** attached to firearm (scope)

**Bulk** L

---

The [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|dwarven]] gunsmiths of Dongun Hold originally created these scopes to help them clear out vermin in underground areas. This scope captures the sound that echoes off a creature hit by the firearm and transforms it into light, illuminating the target for all to see.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]])    **Effect** If your next [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] from the weapon to which the scope is attached hits a creature, the sound of the impact transforms into light, causing the creature to glow until the end of your next turn. A visible creature can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] while they glow. If a creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], they’re concealed while glowing, rather than being undetected. Because the effect requires a solid impact, incorporeal creatures are unaffected unless the bullet can deal force damage or has the effects of the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune.
