# Vampire-Fang Morningstar  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 38

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar|morningstar]]_ is studded with teeth pulled from a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire|vampire]], which usually requires an animate donor, given vampires' tendency to turn to dust when destroyed.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;  **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with this weapon and you're not in direct sunlight; **Effect** The _vampire-fang morningstar_ absorbs blood from the target, healing the wielder for 10 Hit Points.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include teeth from a vampire.
