# Octopus Bottle  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 57

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

Miniature [[Bestiary/Families/Octopus|octopus]] arms press up against the sides of this bottle, obscuring the rest of its contents. When opened, a Huge octopus bursts forth, which can appear in water instead of on the ground. Its arms attempt to grasp a creature with a reach of 15 feet. The octopus repositions that creature to a different space within its reach unless the target succeeds at a DC 24 Fortitude save.

If the octopus is in water, it then releases a cloud of ink in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. This cloud has no effect outside of water. Creatures inside the cloud are [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] and can’t use their sense of smell. The cloud dissipates after 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a [[Bestiary/Giant Octopus (Monster Core)|giant octopus]] corpse.
