# Stalwart's Banner  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 140

**Usage** affixed or held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This magical banner mimics the rich green of summer grass. While holding a _stalwart’s banner_, you can use the following ability.

**Activate—Stand Firm** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You and allies within your banner’s aura gain 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and a +1 status bonus to your Fortitude DC and Reflex DC against any effect that would move you or knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. These effects last for 1 round.
