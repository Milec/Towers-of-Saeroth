This light shirt is made of thin fabric embroidered with arrangements of winged feet.

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Your feet feel lighter, allowing you to move with greater speed. You Stride twice and gain a +10-foot item bonus to your Speed during those Stride actions.
