# Encompassing Lockpick  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 10

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Due to their connection to the shop’s proprietor, all Pathfinder Society agents have access to the items from Barghest’s Bin_

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This lockpick houses a small compartment containing many smaller picks and other miniature tools. The lockpick itself is surprisingly malleable, belying the components within. An almost-impossible number of other tools are hidden in the compartment, including an entire set of [[Equipment/Thieves' Tools|infiltrator thieves' tools]] and any replacement picks, an [[Equipment/Disguise Kit|elite disguise kit]] and any replacement cosmetics, and an [[Equipment/Climbing Kit|extreme climber's kit]], all while still somehow remaining only light Bulk. This makes the _encompassing lockpick_ a favorite discreet option for rogues infiltrating high society events, as formal wear generally has few pockets and only allows a character to wear a single tool kit of light Bulk.
