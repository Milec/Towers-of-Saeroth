# Celestial Peachwood Sword  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 128, [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|Shortsword]]

---

From blade to pommel, this sword is carved from a branch of the now-extinct celestial peach tree. The blade of this +[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|3]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Vitalizing|vitalizing]] [peachwood](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1541) [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|shortsword]] has ancient runes that can permanently destroy the most powerful undead—if you are willing to pay the price.  
An [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature of 12th level or lower that takes damage from a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the sword takes 10d6 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage with a DC 35 basic Fortitude save. This damage is separate from the Strike itself and isn't included in any effect based on the Strike's damage.

**Activate—Violent Exorcism** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; Trigger You critically hit an undead creature with the sword;   **Effect** The creature must succeed at a DC 35 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Destroyed|destroyed]]. If the undead fails its saving throw and is destroyed, you suffer a backlash, taking 1d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage per level of the destroyed undead.
