# Gravitational Flux  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 133

**Price** 150 gp

**Ammunition** large stone sphere ([[Equipment/Siege Weapons/Heavy Bombard (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|heavy bombard]])

---

Small unattended objects tend to levitate slightly around this star-flecked onyx sphere. When Launched, gravity in the area of the burst is briefly inverted. Creatures in the [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] must attempt a DC 25 Fortitude save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is affected by the changing gravity, causing it to be propelled 20 feet straight up. Gravity then immediately returns to normal, potentially causing the creature to fall back to the ground according to the normal [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falling]] rules.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the changing gravity is stronger, increasing the distance it falls up to 40 feet.
