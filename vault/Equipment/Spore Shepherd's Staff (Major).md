# Spore Shepherd's Staff  *Item 4+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -193- Mantle of Gold|Pathfinder #193: Mantle of Gold]] pg. 85

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This staff is constructed from a magically grown amanita mushroom, with a shaft that spreads into a bright red cap speckled in white. While wielding the staff you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Nature checks to identify fungus.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
