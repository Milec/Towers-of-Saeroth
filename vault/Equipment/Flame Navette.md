# Flame Navette  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 567

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

envision

**Requirement** You're an Expert in Will saves

---

This piece of bronzite is shaped like an oval with points at both ends. It has a carved flame at its center and is traditionally worn over the heart. You can activate only one _flame navette_ per day. When you activate the navette, you gain the benefit of the fighter’s [[Feats/Determination]] class feat, with a counteract modifier of +22. If you have the Determination feat, you can use your own modifier if it’s better.
