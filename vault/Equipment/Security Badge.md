# Security Badge  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 79

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn

---

When displayed prominently, this iron badge grants you authority and gravitas. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks.

**Activate—Authoritative Command** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]])    **Effect** You shout at a foe within 60 feet, compelling them to stand in place and drop everything they’re holding. The target attempts a DC 23 Will save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as it does one of the following at the beginning of its next turn: stand in place or release what it’s holding.  

**Failure** The target is slowed 2 as it stands in place and releases what it’s holding at the beginning of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 3 as it spends its next turn to stand in place, release what it’s holding, and place its hands in the air.

**Special** A creature wearing an invested security badge within En-Gokal gains additional benefits, as noted in the [[Sources/Pathfinder -214- The Broken Palace|adventure text]].
