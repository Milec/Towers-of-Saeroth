# Spiritsight Crossbow  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 263

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Crossbow|crossbow]]_ has an array of crystalline lenses and silver fittings along the stock and feels strangely light.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You aim through the crossbow's crystalline lenses, gaining [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise vision]] onto the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]] with a range of 60 feet in addition to your normal senses for the rest of your turn. While this effect lasts, your next Strike with the _spiritsight crossbow_ can also affect targets on the Ethereal Plane during the first 60 feet of its flight.
