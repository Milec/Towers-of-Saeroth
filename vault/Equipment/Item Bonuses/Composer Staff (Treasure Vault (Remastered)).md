# Composer Staff  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 131

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _composer staff_ is short and shaped like an elegant black conductor's baton with a silver tip. When waved through the air, it hums melodically. In this way, you can play it as though it were an instrument, and it grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks made with it.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
