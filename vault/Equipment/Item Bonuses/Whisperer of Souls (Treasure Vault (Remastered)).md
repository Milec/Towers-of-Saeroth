# Whisperer of Souls  *Item 28*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 180

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive]]

---

This _+4 [[Equipment/Striking|major striking]] [[Equipment/Brilliant|greater brilliant]] [[Equipment/Keen|keen]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]]_ binds the souls of powerful creatures it slays. The soul can't be returned to life by any means while imprisoned within the glaive and can be freed only by a deed of great benevolence or selflessness. While using the glaive as a weapon, whenever you reduce a sapient creature of 18th level or higher to 0 Hit Points or roll a critical success with a Strike against such a target, the creature must attempt a DC 50 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** No additional effect.  

**Success** The glaive siphons the creature's essence, rendering it [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. If the creature dies from the Strike or while drained in this way, the creature's soul is bound in the glaive.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature is drained 2.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the creature is drained 4.

 The glaive also has the following activations.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You Strike with the _Whisperer of Souls_;  **Effect** You choose whether the Strike deals lethal or [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] damage. If the glaive has a reason for doing so, such as slaying a creature whose soul the glaive can absorb, it can defy your choice if you fail a DC 50 Will save.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] check to [[Actions/Decipher Writing]], [[Actions/Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell]], or [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]];  **Effect** You listen to eldritch secrets the weapon whispers, gaining a +4 item bonus to the triggering check.

**Activate** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** Attempt an Occultism check as if you cast the [[Rituals/Legend Lore|legend lore]] ritual about a subject. If you roll a success or critical success, you can repeat what the _Whisperer of Souls_ relates to you about the subject. On a critical failure, you and the glaive are drawn into a murmuring void of cold, where your mind is assaulted by strange visions for an entire week. At the end of this time, you reappear and must attempt a DC 50 Will save.

**Success** When you return, you can retrain one of your skills into a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill about evil creatures or places, as if you had spent 1 week retraining.  

**Failure** As success, but you are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 week.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but you become [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]. If you were [[Traits/Holy|holy]], you instead lose that trait. You must retrain one of your skills into a Lore about unholy creatures or places. You become obsessed with increasing your forbidden knowledge, turning to wicked forces that offer such secrets and compromising your morals further if you must.

**Destruction** If the _Whisperer of Souls_ is used to slay [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn|Shelyn's]] [[Traits/Herald|herald]], it disintegrates, bringing about great evil in its wake.
