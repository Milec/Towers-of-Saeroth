# Electric Eelskin  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 557

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor]]

---

Shining, slippery eelskin covers the plates of this +_1 resilient [[Equipment/Slick|greater slick]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]] _. The armor gives you the ability to breathe water and grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to Swim and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks you attempt in the water.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You cast a 2nd-level [[Spells/Shocking Grasp|_shocking grasp_]] with a spell attack modifier of +19.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _shocking grasp_.
