# Storied Skin  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 80

**Price** 45 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Your skin becomes a canvas that records history as you learn it. When you receive the tattoo, choose a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill. You can add the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait to the [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] action in order to study your tattoos, granting you a +1 item bonus to your check using the chosen Lore skill. A storied skin tattoo starts with an icon that represents a central event in your subject of study and is usually placed on the forehead or over the heart. Each time you learn about a major event in the history of that subject, an image, design, or symbol appears on your skin to represent the event.

**Activate—Living History** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** The tattoo’s design animates for 1 round, crudely portraying some scene associated with the chosen Lore skill.
