# Aromatic Ammunition  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 168

**Price** 5 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

The components of this ammunition emit a strong smell when combined during its activation. A creature hit by an activated aromatic ammunition gains a distinct odor for up to 1 hour or until the scent is washed off (requiring at least a gallon of water and 10 minutes of scrubbing). Any creatures within 30 feet smell the target, allowing even those with a weak sense of smell to detect its presence, and all creatures gain a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Track]] the affected creature for as long as it has the odor. A creature that has imprecise or precise [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent|scent]] doubles the range at which they can detect the target using their scent compared to the normal range of their scent.
