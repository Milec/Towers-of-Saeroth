# Glamorous Buckler  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 279

**Price** 35 gp

**Usage** strapped to 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|Buckler]]

---

A _glamorous [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|buckler]]_ is lavishly decorated with gilding and inset gemstones that glitter in the light. While you have it raised, the _glamorous buckler_ grants you a +1 item bonus to Deception checks to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]].

**Activate—Dazzling Feint** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]]; **Requirements** You have the glamorous buckler raised; **Effect** As you Feint, the _glamorous buckler_ sparkles mightily. On a successful Feint, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.
