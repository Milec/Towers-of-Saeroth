# Plate Armor of the Deep  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 558

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate]]

---

This suit of _+2 greater resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate|full plate]]_ is decorated with swirling, ornate motifs of waves and fish scales. While wearing it, you take no Speed reduction or check penalty from armor when Swimming, gain a +2 item bonus to Athletics checks to Swim, can breathe underwater, and can speak Aquan.
