# Heartripper Blade  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Crown of the Kobold King

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 115

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

This wicked-looking curved weapon is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ that draws power from defeating foes, either to bolster itself or its wielder. A _heartripper blade_ is well suited for use in any ritual that involves divination, and incorporating it into any ritual with the [[Traits/Divination|divination]] trait grants the wielder a +1 item bonus on any check made to resolve the effects of the ritual's casting.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You reduce a foe to 0 Hit Points with a Strike from the _heartripper blade_;  **Effect** The _heartripper blade_ draws power from the devastating strike, causing its blade to erupt in black flames. For 1 minute, Strikes with the _heartripper blade_ inflict an additional 1d6 negative damage to living creatures.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You reduce a foe to 0 Hit Points with a Strike from the _heartripper blade_;  **Effect** The _heartripper blade_ draws life from the victim's body and siphons it into you. Choose one of the following effects: restore 2d8 Hit Points; reduce the value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] conditions by 1; or reduce the stage of one toxin or [[Traits/Disease|disease]] you're suffering by one stage (this can't reduce the stage below stage 1 or cure the affliction).
