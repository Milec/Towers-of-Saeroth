# Sun Wheel  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Troubles in Otari

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Troubles in Otari]] pg. 60

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn

---

Created by the priests of a Qadiran sect of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae|Sarenites]], the _sun wheels_ were designed to protect the Dawnflower's followers in their quest to hunt down a dangerous cult trying to raise a spawn of the evil god [[Setting/Deities/Rovagug]]. Once they completed their crusade, these priests disbanded and went on to minister other congregations. They each took their _sun wheels_ with them, handing them down to the next generations.

 This copper medallion is etched with a stylized sun, its rays shaped like the spokes of a wagon wheel. It tends to glint brightly when exposed to sunlight. While wearing this pendant, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] checks. You must be good-aligned to use the sun wheel's true power: the following activation, which provides you a shield of burning force.

**Activate** single action concentrate;     **Effect** The _sun wheel_ casts a [[Spells/Shield|_shield_]] spell benefiting you. When you use the spell to prevent damage, you prevent 10 damage instead of 5. If the attacker is adjacent to you, you can choose to deal 2d6 fire damage to it, which it gets a DC 21 basic Reflex save to resist.

 If you choose to deal the fire damage, you can't Activate _the sun wheel_ again until the next morning. If you prevent damage but don't deal the fire damage, you can use it again in 10 minutes, as normal for _shield_.
