# Phantom Piano  *Item 12*

**Perception** +22; precise vision 30 feet, [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]] hearing 30 feet

**Communication** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]] (any languages the possessing spirit knew in life)

**Skills** [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] +23, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Music Lore]] +23, [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] +25, [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] +21

**Int** +3, **Wis** +4, **Cha** +5

**Will** +22

---  

When a skilled musician dies before reaching their full potential or accomplishing an important goal, their soul might refuse to move on to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]]. Rather than becoming [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], the spirit possesses an instrument they played in life, turning it into an intelligent item and a [[Equipment/Musical Instrument (Player Core)|virtuoso instrument]]. (Other phantom instruments exist, but most are heavy.) A _phantom piano_ offers a bargain to any [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bard]] or other skilled musician who finds it: receive musical aid as you dedicate yourself to reaching the success the spirit felt they were denied in life. The piano refuses to play for those who reject this deal. If you accept, the piano asks only that you practice with it and perform for increasingly refined and august audiences, requiring a successful [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check appropriate to your proficiency level. The piano sets no strict timeline, but at the GM’s discretion, if you go too long without making progress, the piano can cease cooperating with you until it’s satisfied with your dedication once again.

While you keep up your end of the bargain, the piano grants you a +2 item bonus to Performance checks made with it. The piano also provides access to composition spells based on your Performance proficiency rank, as if you had the corresponding feat. The spells you can use comprise a cumulative list, but you can cast them only when using the piano. You must spend the actions and Focus Points required to cast these spells.

**Trained:** [[Spells/Inspire Competence|_inspire competence_]], [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]]  
**Expert:** [[Spells/Hymn of Healing (Player Core)|_hymn of healing_]], [[Spells/Inspire Defense|_inspire defense_]]  
**Master:** [[Spells/Symphony of the Unfettered Heart (Player Core)|_symphony of the unfettered heart_]]  
**Legendary:** [[Spells/Soothing Ballad (Player Core)|_soothing ballad_]]

A _phantom piano_ has the following activations.

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** The piano shrinks down to a figurine of light Bulk compact enough to store in a pouch, or from figurine form back to instrument form. In figurine form, the piano doesn’t function as an instrument or provide magic other than this activation.

**Activate** free action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] the piano provides you.
