# Convincing  *Item 5+*

A _convincing_ rune blends in with your armor’s decorative elements, such as embossed patterns and colorful ornaments. While wearing the armor, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] and [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]].
