# Guise of the Smirking Devil  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 310

**Usage** worn mask

---

This ice-blue half-mask is adorned with a wicked silver grin that covers only the wearer's mouth. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks.

**Activate—Hideous Wail** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The mask emits a soul-chilling scream that deals 6d10 void damage to each living creature in a 20-foot emanation (DC 25 basic Fortitude save).
