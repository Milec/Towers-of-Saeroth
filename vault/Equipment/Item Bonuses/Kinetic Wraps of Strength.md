# Kinetic Wraps of Strength  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Usage** worn gloves

**Access** You're from Hermea

---

These tightly woven strips strengthen the hands and arms, allowing for the wearer to increase their application of force while lifting heavy loads, and shoving past blockades. Promise sculptors primarily use them to handle large blocks of stone, moving them around as they prepare to carve them. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]], [[Actions/Reposition]], and [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]].

**Activate—Burst of Might** free action ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt an Athletics check to Force Open, Reposition, or Shove;  **Effect** You roll the skill check twice and take the better result.
