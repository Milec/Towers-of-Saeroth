# Living Leaf Weave  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 64

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leaf Weave]]

---

This suit of [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leaf weave]] armor is specially modified to metabolize the alchemical accelerants in medicinal compounds. A special receptacle in the armor can hold an [[Equipment/Elixir of Life (Player Core 2)|elixir of life]], which takes 3 Interact actions to install.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** An elixir of life is installed in the armor; **Effect** Slithering vines grow from the armor, granting an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], to your Fortitude DC to resist Grapple, [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] attempts, and to your Reflex DC to resist Trip attempts. The bonus is equal to the elixir’s item bonus, and lasts for 3 rounds. The activation uses up the elixir, and the armor can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.
