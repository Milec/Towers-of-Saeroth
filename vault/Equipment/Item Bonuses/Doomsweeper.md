# Doomsweeper  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 126

**Price** 475 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Halberd (Player Core)|Halberd]]

---

Functioning as a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Halberd (Player Core)|halberd]]_ when wielded as a weapon, a _doomsweeper_ is a heavy steel rake-like implement commonly used by frontline soldiers to scour the battlefield for unseen dangers. When you hold a _doomsweeper_ extended in front of you, you gain a +1 item bonus to Perception checks to notice any hidden hazards in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], with the bonus increasing to +2 if you are performing the Scout or Search exploration action.

**Activate—Clear the Way** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re holding the _doomsweeper_ in 2 hands; **Effect** The _doomsweeper_ sweeps the ground clear of obstacles in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. This automatically removes any mundane effects of 4th level or lower that would hinder ground movement through the area of effect, including [[Equipment/Caltrops (Player Core)|caltrops]] and non-magical [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] features no deeper or higher than 4 feet. It also attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] any magical dangers or obstacles with a +14 modifier and a counteract rank of 4th. Finally, it attempts a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] check with a +14 modifier to disable all non-magical hazards (but not haunts) in the area. You don’t need to be aware of any hazards or effects in the area for the _doomsweeper_ to remove them, but you don’t become aware of them if the attempt fails.
