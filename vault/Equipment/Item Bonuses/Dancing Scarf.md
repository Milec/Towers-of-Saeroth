# Dancing Scarf  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 288

**Usage** worn belt

---

This long and billowing scarf is typically woven of silk or sheer fabric and adorned with bells or other jangling bits of shiny metal. It grants a +1 item bonus to Performance checks to dance.

**Activate—Swirling Scarf** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])   **Requirements** On your most recent action, you succeeded at a Performance check to dance; **Effect** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] until the beginning of your next turn.
