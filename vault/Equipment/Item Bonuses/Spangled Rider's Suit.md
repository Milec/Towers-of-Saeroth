# Spangled Rider's Suit  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 75

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor]]

---

This _+1 resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor|studded leather armor]]_ is adorned with colorful sequins that sparkle in even the faintest light. While wearing the spangled rider’s suit, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Actions/Command an Animal]] you are riding. Any animal you ride automatically knows the [[Actions/Drop Prone]] and [[Actions/Step]] basic actions, in addition to any other basic actions it knows (normally only Leap, Seek, Stand, Stride, and Strike).

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Move|move]])  **Trigger** You take damage while mounted or your mount Drops Prone;  **Effect** You dismount, moving off your mount into a space adjacent to it.
