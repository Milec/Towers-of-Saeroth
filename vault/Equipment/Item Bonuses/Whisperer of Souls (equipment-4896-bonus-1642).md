# Whisperer of Souls  *Item 28*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 180

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+4 major striking]] [[Equipment/Brilliant (GM Core)|greater brilliant]] [[Equipment/Keen (GM Core)|keen]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|glaive]]_ binds the souls of powerful creatures it slays. The soul can’t be returned to life by any means while imprisoned within the glaive and can be freed only by a deed of great benevolence or selflessness. While using the glaive as a weapon, whenever you reduce a sapient creature of 18th level or higher to 0 Hit Points or roll a critical success with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against such a target, the creature must attempt a DC 50 Fortitude save.**Critical Success** No additional effect.  
**Success** The glaive siphons the creature’s essence, rendering it [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1. If the creature dies from the Strike or while drained in this way, the creature’s soul is bound in the glaive.  
**Failure** As success, but the creature is drained 2.  
**Critical Failure** As success, but the creature is drained 4.

 The glaive also has the following activations.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the _Whisperer of Souls_;  **Effect** You choose whether the Strike deals lethal or nonlethal damage. If the glaive has a reason for doing so, such as slaying a creature whose soul the glaive can absorb, it can defy your choice if you fail a DC 50 Will save.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] check to [[Actions/Decipher Writing (Player Core)|Decipher Writing]], [[Actions/Identify Magic (Player Core)|Identify Magic]], [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]], or [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]];  **Effect** You listen to eldritch secrets the weapon whispers, gaining a +4 item bonus to the triggering check.

**Activate** three actions command, envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] check as if you cast the [[Rituals/Legend Lore|_legend lore_]] ritual about a subject. If you roll a success or critical success, you can repeat what the _Whisperer of Souls_ relates to you about the subject. On a critical failure, you and the glaive are drawn into a murmuring void of cold, where your mind is assaulted by strange visions for an entire week. At the end of this time, you reappear and must attempt a DC 50 Will save.

**Success** When you return, you can retrain one of your skills into a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill about evil creatures or places, as if you had spent 1 week retraining.  

**Failure** As success, but you are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 1 week.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but your alignment moves one step toward evil and you must retrain one of your skills into a Lore about evil creatures or places. You become obsessed with increasing your forbidden knowledge, turning to wicked forces that offer such secrets and compromising your morals further if you must.

**Destruction** If the _Whisperer of Souls_ is used to slay [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn (Divine Mysteries)|Shelyn’s]] herald, it disintegrates, bringing about great evil in its wake.
