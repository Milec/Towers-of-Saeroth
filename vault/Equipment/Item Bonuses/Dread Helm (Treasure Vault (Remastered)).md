# Dread Helm  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 63

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

The faceplate of a dread helm has a fierce visage that magnifies the effects of fear. Concealed within is a reservoir that can hold a single [[Equipment/Dread Ampoule (Player Core 2)|dread ampoule]], which takes 3 Interact actions to install.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** A dread ampoule is installed in the helm; **Effect** The dread ampoule atomizes, creating a fear‑inducing mist that hangs around your face for 3 rounds. The mist grants an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks equal to the dread ampoule’s item bonus. The mist also deals mental damage equal to the dread ampoule’s splash damage to all creatures within a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] other than you. The activation uses up the dread ampoule, and the helm can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.
