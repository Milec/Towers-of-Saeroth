# Coral Aspect  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 78

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're from the Mordant Spire

---

Made of petrified wood, this mask evokes the form of a coral reef. When you wear it, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], and any armor you’re wearing gains the [[Traits/Aquadynamic (Howl of the Wild)|aquadynamic]] trait.

**Activate—Cohabitate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For 1 minute, your body becomes a mobile coral reef, letting much smaller creatures pass through or shelter in it. Creatures at least one size category smaller than you can share your space. Allies that share your space gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their AC, and you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to any damage caused by a creature sharing the same space as you.
