# Kinburi's Sandals of Bounding  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 306

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

These elevated wooden sandals once belonged to a haughty [[Rules/Sidebars/Fox Families|kitsune]] named Kinburi. It was well known that her swiftness and grace were unmatched, and observers described her as seeming to glide above the earth with each step. She often challenged others to wager on her speed, but as people began to grow wise, she was forced to conceal her identity or promise to forfeit her sandals if she lost.

These sandals give you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] and [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. In addition, when you use the [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]] action, you can move 5 feet further if jumping horizontally or 3 feet higher if jumping vertically.

**Activate—Light-Footed Bound** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You loudly boast about your grace and agility, then take any combination of two [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jumps]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jumps]]. You don’t need to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] first, and between the two jumps, you need only to come into contact with a physical object before making the second jump; this object doesn’t need to be one that can bear your weight, or even be attached to the ground—you can jump off of a floating feather or a thin branch or even off a paper wall as needed. The sandals’ item bonus increases to +2 for these jumps.
