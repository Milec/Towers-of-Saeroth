# Elysian Dew  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 89

**Price** 375 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

When you drink sweet, sky-blue _Elysian dew_, for 1 minute, you gain a 10-foot aura that evokes the vitality of [[Setting/Planes/Elysium (GM Core)|Elysium]], causing nearby objects to seem more colorful and plants to stand taller. You and any ally that starts its turn in the emanation gain 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]], a +1 item bonus to saving throws, and a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks until the start of your or the ally’s next turn. If you’re [[Traits/Evil|evil]] and drink this potion, it fails to work, and you must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or the potion renders you [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2.
