# Elysian Dew  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 89

**Price** 375 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

When you drink sweet, sky-blue _Elysian dew_, for 1 minute, you gain a 10-foot aura that evokes the vitality of [[Setting/Planes/Elysium]], causing nearby objects to seem more colorful and plants to stand taller. You and any ally that starts its turn in the emanation gain 5 temporary Hit Points, a +1 item bonus to saving throws, and a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks until the start of your or the ally's next turn. If you're evil and drink this potion, it fails to work, and you must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or the potion renders you [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]].
