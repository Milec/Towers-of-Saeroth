# Staff Of Nature's Cunning  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 239

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Moss and winding vines give this gnarled staff of wild wood a vibrant green tinge and the smell of an ancient forest. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
