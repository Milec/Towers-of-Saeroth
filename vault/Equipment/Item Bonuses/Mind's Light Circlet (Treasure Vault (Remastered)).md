# Mind's Light Circlet  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 150

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Gemstones of many colors adorn the silver of a _mind's light circlet_. When you're charged with mental power, the jewels scintillate with light, with different gems resonating based on your emotions. If you have at least 1 Focus Point, the gems cast [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in a 10-foot radius. When you amp a spell, the light increases to [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and dim light to the next 20 feet) until the start of your next turn.

 You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] checks. You also gain the following [[Traits/Amp|amp]], which you can apply to any of your psi cantrips that have a target or area, much like an amp gained from a feat.

 **Amp** You transfer some of the magical luminescence from the mind's light circlet to one of the creatures. Choose a creature targeted by the spell or in its area. Until the start of your next turn, that creature sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius (and dim light to the next 20 feet) and can't be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]. If the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], it's concealed while alight, rather than being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]].

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can use only to use a psychic amp. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Dark Archive)|psychic]].
