# Mask Of Allure  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 226

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

---

This mask appears to be a pool of mirrored, shifting silver adhered to a thin metal plate. When you place it against your face, it melds to the shape of your head. The material is breathable and light and doesn’t obscure vision. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while wearing the mask. When you [[Rules/Investing Magic Items (GM Core)|invest]] the mask, you either increase your Charisma modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Shine of Silver** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation, or Performance check;  **Effect** You gain a +4 status bonus to the triggering check. This ability has no effect if you’re under the effects of a disguise that hides the _mask of allure_. Depending on the skill used, the mirrored silver transforms into one of the following appearances.  
**• [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]** A swirl of silver, which entirely obscures and conceals your normal features.  
**• [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]** A kind, gentle, and inviting expression.  
**• [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]** An exaggerated visage of horror that shocks viewers into paying attention.  
**• [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]** A humanoid face, the mouth cracked wide with a comedic smile or a tragic frown, depending on the nature of the performance.
