# Spiritsight Tea  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Tea]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 300

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

_Spiritsight tea_ exudes a soft blue glow, creating illumination equal to that of a candle. When consumed, your eyes take on a soft glow as well, and you can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures and objects for 10 minutes; such creatures appear to you as translucent shapes, and they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to you. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures.

---

**Activate—Tea Ceremony** 10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) The duration of being able to see invisible creatures and objects increases to 30 minutes, and the duration of the item bonus increases to 8 hours.
