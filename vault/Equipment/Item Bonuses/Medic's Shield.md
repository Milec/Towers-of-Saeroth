# Medic's Shield  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 124

**Price** 4,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This [[Equipment/Dawnsilver Shield|standard-grade dawnsilver shield]] (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10) is inscribed with the symbol of a deity dedicated to [[Magic/Domains/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] and medicine. The shield is designed for combat medics to aid combatants or adventurers in the midst of battle. [[Equipment/Healer's Toolkit|Healer’s tools]] can be stored on the backside of this shield. This shield grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks.

**Activate—Adrenaline Boost** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You grant a medicinal boost to bring someone back into the fray. You [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]] to stabilize an adjacent ally with the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition. On a success, they gain 6d6+20 Hit Points, can immediately [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action, and become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round. They can use this extra action only to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]].
