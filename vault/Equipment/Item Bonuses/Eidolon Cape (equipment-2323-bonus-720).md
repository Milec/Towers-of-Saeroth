# Eidolon Cape  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 148

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

Though it appears to be one elegant piece, an _eidolon cape_ consists of a mantle for your shoulders and a detachable cape. The cape is designed to resemble your [eidolon](Eidolons.aspx), either with a direct likeness or with features reminiscent of your eidolon. For instance, if your eidolon is a [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]], the cape might depict a stylized dragon, or it might have a pattern of colorful scales with gold trim. An eidolon cape features the sigil you share with your eidolon prominently in its design. You gain a +2 item bonus to the skill that matches your eidolon's tradition: [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] for [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] for [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] for [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] for [[Traits/Primal|primal]].

 When you [[Actions/Manifest Eidolon|Manifest your Eidolon]], the cape transforms into the eidolon, and when the eidolon unmanifests, it turns back into the cape. If the cape is attached to the mantle, it comes free immediately, and the eidolon appears adjacent to you as normal. If not, the eidolon appears in a space adjacent to the cape. With the cape detached, the mantle is still light Bulk, and the whole cloak remains invested. If the cape is out of range (beyond 100 feet, typically) or there's not enough open space for the eidolon to manifest, your attempt to Manifest your Eidolon fails.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can use only to cast a summoner link spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (class-feature-443)|summoner]].
