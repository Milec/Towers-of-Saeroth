# Powered Full Plate  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 65

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Stasian actuators help the limbs of this [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]] move of their own accord, as long as they’re supplied with power. A chamber in the chest plate can hold a single [[Equipment/Bottled Lightning (Player Core 2)|bottled lightning]], which takes 3 [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions to install. A loaded suit of powered full plate can be activated to lower the armor’s Strength requirement by 1 plus the item bonus of the loaded bomb. The armor’s normal penalties still apply, based on this altered Strength. However, add the bottled lightning’s item bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], and [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]]. These effects last for 10 minutes. The armor uses up the bottled lightning during the duration and can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.
