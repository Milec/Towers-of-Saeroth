# Clandestine Cloak  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 287

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

When you pull up the hood of this nondescript gray cloak (an Interact action), you become drab and uninteresting, gaining a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks and to Deception checks to Impersonate a forgettable background character, such as a servant, but also taking a –1 item penalty to Diplomacy and Intimidation checks.

**Activate—Cloak Identity** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull the cloak's hood up and gain the benefits of [[Spells/Veil of Privacy|_veil of privacy_]] for 1 hour or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.
