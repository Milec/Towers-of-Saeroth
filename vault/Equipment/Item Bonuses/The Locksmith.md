# The Locksmith  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Harrow Court]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -190- The Choosing|Pathfinder #190: The Choosing]] pg. 78

**Usage** carried

---

_The Locksmith_ represents opening locks, including metaphorical locks such as unlocking fate. As long as you have _The Locksmith_ invested, it grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Pick a Lock]]. This increases to a +3 item bonus if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** ⤾ envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would be afflicted by a [[Traits/Curse|curse]] or a [[Traits/Disease|disease]];  **Effect** You mimic the turning of a key in a lock and free yourself from your unpleasant fate. You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering affliction, using half your level (rounded up) as the counteract level and a counteract check modifier equal to your class DC – 10.
