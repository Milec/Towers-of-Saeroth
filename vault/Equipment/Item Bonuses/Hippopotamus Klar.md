# Hippopotamus Klar  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 100

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Klar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Klar]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Shields/Klar (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|klar]]_ is fashioned from the skull of a hippopotamus, with one of its canines acting as the blade. While wielding a _hippopotamus klar_, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks.

**Activate—Engulfing Jaws** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The hippo jaws of the shield open impossibly wide. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the klar blade targeting two creatures adjacent to each other. Roll the attack and damage once and apply it to each creature separately. This counts as two attacks for your [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the entire skull of a [[Bestiary/Hippopotamus|hippopotamus]].
