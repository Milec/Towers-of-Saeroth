# Sure-Step Potion  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 91

**Price** 17 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

The light-brown liquid of a _sure-step potion_ helps you find your footing. After drinking it, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] for 1 hour. In addition, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], and you aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] on uneven ground.
