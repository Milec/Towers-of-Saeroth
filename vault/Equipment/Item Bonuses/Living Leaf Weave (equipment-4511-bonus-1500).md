---

This suit of [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leaf weave armor]] is specially modified to metabolize the alchemical accelerants in medicinal compounds. A special receptacle in the armor can hold an [[Equipment/Elixir of Life (Player Core 2)|elixir of life]], which takes 3 [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions to install. A loaded suit of living leaf weave can be activated to grow slithering vines from the armor, granting an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to Grapple, to your Fortitude DC to resist [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] attempts, and to your Reflex DCs to resist Trip attempts. The effects last for 3 rounds, consuming the elixir of life, and once the reaction has begun it can’t be deactivated.
