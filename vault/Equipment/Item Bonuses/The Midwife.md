# The Midwife  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 76

**Usage** carried

---

_The Midwife_ represents the arrival of new life or new information, particularly via the aid of another. As long as you have _The Midwife_ invested, it grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks. This increases to a +3 bonus if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature you can sense within 60 feet would die or be destroyed;  **Effect** You prevent the target from dying or being destroyed and restore to the target 6d8+24 Hit Points. This effect can prevent a [[Traits/Death|death]] effect or [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] from slaying a target. The amount of healing granted increases to 7d8+28 if you're at least 15th level, 8d8+32 if you're at least 17th level, and 9d8+36 if you're at least 19th level.
