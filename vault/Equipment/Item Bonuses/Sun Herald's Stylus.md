# Sun Herald's Stylus  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 130

**Price** 4,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This writing instrument was made from the spur of one of the giant divine roosters that heralds the presence of [[Setting/Deities/Shizuru (Divine Mysteries)|Shizuru]]. While you hold it, you gain a +2 item bonus to Calligraphy Lore and [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks.

**Activate—Rejuvenating Ink** two actions (manipulate) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Drawing a circle on the ground with your stylus, you cast [[Spells/Field of Life (Player Core)|_field of life_]] centered on yourself.
