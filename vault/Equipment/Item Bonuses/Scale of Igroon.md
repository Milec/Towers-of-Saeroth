# Scale of Igroon  *Item 21*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 180

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

Carved from a scale of the kaiju Igroon, this jagged shield refracts light around it in a shimmering haze. _A scale of Igroon_ ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 20, HP 160, BT 80) recovers 4 Hit Points at the start of its wielder’s turn. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise a Shield]], you can use the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction with the scale of Igroon to block an attack or effect that deals [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage as well as physical damage.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** You’re in an area of [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright]] or [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]]; **Effect** You angle the shield to refract light. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +4 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and can do so while observed. This bonus ends if you Activate another ability or use the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** You’re in an area of bright light; **Effect** You angle the shield at a target within 60 feet, reflecting light into its eyes. It must attempt a DC 42 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until its next turn begins.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is blinded for 2d4 hours.

**Activate** free action Interact;   **Trigger** You use [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] and prevent yourself from taking energy damage from a [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], ray, or a direct attack, including a [[Spells/Force Barrage|_magic missile_]] spell;  **Effect** You reflect the energy along a trajectory you choose. The effect travels only up to its remaining range, using its original parameters if it strikes other targets.

**Destruction** If a deity, kaiju, spawn of a deity, titan, or being of similar power stomps on a _scale of Igroon_ while in absolute darkness, the shield is destroyed permanently.
