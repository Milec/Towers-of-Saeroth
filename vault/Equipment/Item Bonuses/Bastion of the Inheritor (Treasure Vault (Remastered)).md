# Bastion of the Inheritor  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 14

**Price** 1,750 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 5

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Bastion Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Bastion Plate]]

---

Worn by [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae|Iomedae's]] prestigious knights, this _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Bastion Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|bastion plate]]_ is emblazoned with the Inheritor's [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] and sports a white cloak. While the cloak is white, this armor grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]], provided your target has no enmity toward Iomedae.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The armor's cloak becomes red for 1 minute. As long as the cloak is red, you gain the benefits of the armor's deflect melee trait without needing to spend an additional action to activate it during each turn.

**Craft Requirements** You worship Iomedae.
