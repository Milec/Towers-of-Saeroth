# Tumbler's Belt  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 81

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn belt

**Bulk** L

---

This sparkling purple belt was made for the youngest member of a family of jugglers and tumblers who traveled with the Crascondo Company. The thought was this would allow the child to safely participate with their elder siblings and cousins, but the belt found itself being “loaned” more and more often to dare-taking elders in the troupe. The _tumbler’s belt_ grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks, and whenever you critically succeed at a check to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]], you gain a +10-foot item bonus to your [[Rules/Speed (GM Core)|Speed]] until the end of your turn. While wearing the _tumbler’s belt_, you’re not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] while you [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]].

**Activate** ⤾ Land With Grace;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're falling;  **Effect** Treat your fall as if it were 120 feet shorter. Regardless of whether you take damage or not from the fall, you land on your feet.
