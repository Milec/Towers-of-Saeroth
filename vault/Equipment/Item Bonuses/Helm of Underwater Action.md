# Helm of Underwater Action  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 152

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

A _helm of underwater action_ is a streamlined sallet with a visor and a flanged rear. While wearing the helmet with the visor down, you can see, hear, and speak clearly underwater. You also have a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]].

**Activate** three actions command, envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You can breathe underwater for 8 hours. During this time, you have a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] equal to your land Speed.
