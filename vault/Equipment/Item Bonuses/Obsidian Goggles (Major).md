# Obsidian Goggles  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

The sleek, black lenses of these goggles somehow make everything seem more brightly lit. While wearing the goggles, you gain a +1 item bonus to Perception checks involving sight.

**Activate—Darkvision** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Adjusting your goggles, you gain darkvision for 1 hour.

## Obsidian Goggles  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293

**Price** 150 gp

## Obsidian Goggles (Greater)  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293

**Price** 1,250 gp

---

The item bonus is +2, the goggles can be activated any number of times per day, and the activation lasts until you deactivate it as an Interact action or the item is no longer invested by you, whichever comes first.

## Obsidian Goggles (Major)  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293

**Price** 20,000 gp

---

The item bonus is +3, the goggles can be activated any number of times per day, the activation grants [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], and the activation lasts until you deactivate it as an Interact action or the item is no longer invested by you, whichever comes first.
