# Aeon Stone  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Usage** worn

---

Over millennia, these mysterious, intricately cut gemstones have been hoarded by mystics and fanatics hoping to discover their secrets. Despite their myriad forms and functions, these stones are purportedly all fragments of crystal tools used by otherworldly entities to construct the universe in primeval times.  
 When you invest one of these precisely shaped crystals, the stone orbits your head instead of being worn on your body. You can stow an _aeon stone_ with an Interact action, and an orbiting stone can be snatched out of the air with a successful Disarm action against you. A stowed or removed stone remains invested, but its effects are suppressed until you return it to orbit your head again.  
 There are various types of aeon stones, each with a different appearance and magical effect. Each aeon stone also gains a resonant power when slotted into a special magical item called a [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|_wayfinder_]]

## Aeon Stone (Amplifying)  *Item 16*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 9,750 gp

---

An _amplifying aeon stone_ must be activated to provide a benefit. The resonant power grants you a +2 item bonus to Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion checks—whichever corresponds to the tradition of the last spell you enhanced with this aeon stone.

**Activate—Amplify** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]])    **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a Spell, that spell's rank is 1 higher (maximum 10th rank) for the purposes of counteracting and being counteracted.

## Aeon Stone (Consumed)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 9 gp

---

A _consumed aeon stone_ is a dull lump that has lost its magical properties. It still rotates your head like any other aeon stone and can thus serve as a stylish, hand-free option for various spells that target an object. This _aeon stone_ has no resonant power.

## Aeon Stone (Delaying)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 350 gp

---

When you would die from the dying condition (typically at dying 4), this smooth pink stone automatically activates and reduces your dying value to 1 less than would normally kill you (typically to dying 3). The stone then permanently turns into a _consumed aeon stone_. You can benefit from this ability only once per day, even if you have multiple such stones.  
 The resonant power allows you to cast 1st-rank [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] as a divine innate spell once per day.

## Aeon Stone (Envisioning)  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 425 gp

---

This clear cube allows you to communicate with a limited form of telepathy to a range of 100 feet. The messages are transmitted as a simple image each round. These images are the quality of a hasty or childish sketch but can be roughly understood by creatures regardless of language. This provides no special ability to respond to your images.  
 The resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Translate|_translate_]] as an occult innate spell once per day.

## Aeon Stone (Peering)  *Item 16*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 9,500 gp

---

This faintly colored prism catches and transforms light. If the _aeon stone_ is in an area of bright light, shades of purple light provide dim light in a 30-foot radius. In that radius, magical items and effects are tinged with purple, the depth of the color revealing their [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] rank.  
 The GM also secretly rolls a counteract check against any darkness or illusion effect the light touches. It has a counteract modifier of +25. If the light fails to counteract an effect, it won't try again for 24 hours.  
 Inside a _wayfinder_, the aeon stone cannot catch light. However, its resonant power lets you activate or deactivate the aeon stone's light emitting from the _wayfinder_ as an Interact action.

## Aeon Stone (Nourishing)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 325 gp

---

After you've invested and worn this transparent stone for a week continuously, you no longer need to eat or drink. This time resets if you remove the _aeon stone_ or it's invested by someone else. The resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Air Bubble|_air bubble_]] as a primal innate spell once per day.

## Aeon Stone (Preserving)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 150 gp

---

This red crystalline star covers you in a faint aura when you are subject to lingering wounds. You gain resistance 3 to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]]. At the end of any turn where the persistent damage can't overcome this resistance, end that condition.  
 The resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Stabilize|_stabilize_]] as a primal innate cantrip.

## Aeon Stone (Smoothing)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 310 gp

---

This spherical piece of silvery stone helps you ignore minor issues. You can ignore status penalties to skill checks from [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] conditions as long as the value of that condition is 1. Its resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Guidance|_guidance_]] as an occult innate cantrip.

## Aeon Stone (Sprouting)  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 284

**Price** 220 gp

---

Encouraging the natural growth of life, this green ovoid can often be found surrounded by new seedlings. Its resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Tangle Vine|_tangle vine_]] as a primal innate cantrip.

**Activate—Flow of Life** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You are healed by a vitality efect or took void damage;  **Effect** You gain 8 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 minute.

## Aeon Stone (Formulating)  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 81

**Price** 75 gp

---

This four-sided prism is imbued with knowledge for the construction of several objects. The stone contains the knowledge of all formulas found in a basic crafter’s book. While you have the stone [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]], you have the full knowledge of the stone’s formulas. This counts as having the formula for the purposes of [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks. While you have this knowledge, you can write the formulas down for later use, such as if you lose access to the stone. You also gain a +1 item bonus to skill checks made to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] while the stone is invested.

The resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Phantasmal Minion|_phantasmal minion_]] as an arcane innate spell once per day.

## Aeon Stone (Repairing)  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 82

**Price** 75 gp

---

This translucent, spherical stone’s center contains a multitude of sparkling, colored flecks. While you have this stone [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]], you gain a +1 item bonus to skill checks made to Craft. Additionally, when Repairing an item, you restore an additional 5 Hit Points to the item (10 Hit Points on a critical success).

The resonant power allows you to cast [[Spells/Mending (Player Core)|_mending_]] as an arcane innate spell once per day.

## Aeon Stone (Crescent)  *Item 6*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 79

**Price** 225 gp

---

Once holy relics, the creation of _crescent aeon stones_ was inspired by [[Setting/Deities/Acavna]], goddess of the moon and protection. The _crescent aeon stone_ continually sheds dim light in a 5-foot radius.

The resonant power enables you to cast [[Spells/Forbidding Ward (Player Core)|_forbidding ward_]] as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] innate cantrip.

**Activate—Moonbeam** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The crescent aeon stone fires a blast of silvery moonlight in a 100-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], dealing 4d12 spirit damage to all creatures in the area (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). This is [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]]  damage for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, and the like.

## Aeon Stone (Vital amplification)  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 79

**Price** 330 gp

---

A _vital amplification aeon stone_ improves the flow of vital energy through your body, speeding the healing process and safeguarding your body from life-draining effects. Whenever you regain Hit Points, you regain an additional 1 Hit Point for each 10 Hit Points regained (minimum 1 additional Hit Point). The resonant power grants you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to void damage.

## Aeon Stone (Flickering)  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 79

**Price** 1,200 gp

---

A _flickering aeon stone_ contains a drop of orichalcum at its center. It remains slightly out of phase with reality, giving it a translucent appearance.

**Activate—Flicker** single action ;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _flickering aeon stone_ draws you slightly out of sync with the flow of time, causing you to flicker in and out of existence. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] for 1 minute, but you can’t use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]].

**Activate—Enter Stasis** single action ;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _flickering aeon stone_ pulls you from the flow of time completely, placing you in temporary stasis while you heal, then returning you to reality at the moment you left. You regain 2d10+8 Hit Points. If you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition, the value of each of these conditions is reduced by 1.

The resonant power grants you a +1 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls.
