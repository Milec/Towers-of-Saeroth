# Experimental Clothing  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 50

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** L

---

Experimental clothing is custom tailored in radical, avant-garde styles by independent dressmakers, utilizing expensive or eccentric materials and unconventional patterns. At the GM's discretion, wearing experimental clothing may impart a +1 item bonus or –1 item penalty on checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression|Make An Impression]], depending on the target's fashion sense.
