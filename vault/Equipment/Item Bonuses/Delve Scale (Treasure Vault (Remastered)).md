# Delve Scale  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Price** 155 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

If fried, cast-off scales of a [[Bestiary/Benthic Worm|benthic worm]] render into crunchy snacks. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|Alchemists]] add reagents to the frying oil to enhance the scales’ properties and flavor. For 1 minute after eating a delve scale, you gain a burrow Speed of 15 feet and a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump]].
