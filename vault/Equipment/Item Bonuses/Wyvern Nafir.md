# Wyvern Nafir  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 147

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This simple trumpet is carved from a single wyvern horn. A _wyvern nafir_ grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Wyvern Scream** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You blast a draconic scream from the nafir. All creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] take 5d10 sonic damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, the target is pushed back 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure).
