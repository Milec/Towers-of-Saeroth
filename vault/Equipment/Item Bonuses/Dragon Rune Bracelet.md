# Dragon Rune Bracelet  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 148

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** worn

---

A _dragon rune bracelet_ is a gold bangle formed around the scale of a famous dragon. The bracelet is etched with esoteric symbols or words in [[Setting/Languages/Draconic (Player Core)|Draconic]] that indicate kinship with dragons. As many types of _dragon rune bracelet_ exist as there are types of dragons, though bracelets associated with uncommon or rare dragons have the same rarity as the dragon.

While wearing a dragon rune bracelet, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks while interacting with dragons of the same type as the bracelet’s scale (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cinder|red dragons]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Cloud (Draconic Codex)|cloud dragons]]). You also gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects from any kind of dragon.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a sorcerer draconic bloodline spell. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerer]] of a [[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|draconic]] bloodline.
