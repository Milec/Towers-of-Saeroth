# Mantle of the Tikbalang  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 106

**Price** 425 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** 1

---

This long hide cloak is stitched with patches of the ebony hair of a [[Bestiary/Tikbalang|tikbalang]]. While wearing this cloak, you take a –2 item penalty to saves against illusions but gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks.

**Activate—Illusory Thrash** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You wrap the mantle around your body, causing you to briefly appear much larger than you are. Make a melee Strike. This Strike deals an additional 4d6 mental damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hide of a [[Bestiary/Tikbalang|tikbalang]].
