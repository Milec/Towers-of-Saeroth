# Energizing Pill  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Lozenge (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Lozenge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 295

**Price** 180 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This special blend of potent herbs comes in the form of a compact ball the size of a marble that allows it to be held in the mouth, where it remains for 1 hour. An energizing pill has a bitter taste at first, but the longer one lets it soak, the more complex and sweet its flavor grows. It heightens your reactions, granting you a +2 item bonus to initiative rolls with Perception.

**Secondary Effect** ⤾ **Trigger** You would become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]]; **Effect** Reduce the value of the condition you just gained by 1; if this reduces the value to 0, you don’t gain the triggering condition.
