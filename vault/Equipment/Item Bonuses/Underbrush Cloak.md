# Underbrush Cloak  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 153

**Price** 90 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** 1

---

This hooded cloak is lined with rough foliage, vines, and bark that never wilts or rots away. When wearing this cloak, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks when in heavily forested areas.

**Activate—One with the Woods** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For the next minute, you [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore any difficult terrain]] caused by plants and fungi, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.
