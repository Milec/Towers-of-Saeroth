# Quicksilver Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 290

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

You become swifter and nimbler but also fragile.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks, [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks, Reflex saves, and Dexterity-based attack rolls, and you gain the listed status bonus to your Speed.

**Drawback** You take damage equal to twice your level; you can't recover Hit Points lost in this way by any means while the mutagen lasts. You take a –2 penalty to Fortitude saves.
