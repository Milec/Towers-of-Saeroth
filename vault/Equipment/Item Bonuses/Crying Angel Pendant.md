# Crying Angel Pendant  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 263

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

---

When you activate this alabaster pendant, attempt to [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] using Medicine with a +1 item bonus to the check. If you succeed, and you were trying to stabilize, the target regains 1 Hit Point, losing the dying condition and becoming conscious as normal. If you succeed, and you were trying to stop bleeding, the bleeding ends.
