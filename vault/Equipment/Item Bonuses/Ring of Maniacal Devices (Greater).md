# Ring of Maniacal Devices  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 310

**Usage** worn

---

This magic ring seems like simple tarnished brass, but it enhances your curiosity about traps and devices of all kinds. You can use an Interact action to pull a [[Equipment/Thieves' Toolkit|thieves' toolkit]] from the ring. This toolkit appears in your hand and any part of it folds back into the ring if they would leave your possession. They grant you a +2 item bonus to Thievery checks to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable a Device]] and to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]], and the ring's insights grant you the same bonus to Crafting checks to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] and [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] snares and traps.

**Activate—Fireball Trap**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You create the effects of a [[Rituals/Rune Trap|_rune trap_]] ritual containing [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]]. You can have only one trapped rune from a ring of maniacal devices active at a time, even if you have multiple rings, and the rune disappears if you lose your investiture in the ring.
