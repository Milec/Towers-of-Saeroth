# Jaws of the Grogrisant  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 173

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn circlet

**Bulk** L

---

The teeth that fell off the [[Equipment/Mantle of the Grogrisant|_Mantle of the Grogrisant_]] were given to the scholars of Houses Fahlspar, Lotheed, Nicodemius, and Zespire, who—after much heated discussion—created this regal circlet. You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] skill checks and [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]] checks against creatures that have the [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] trait. When you invest in the headband, you either increase your Wisdom modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Primal Empathy** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You gain the ability to communicate with nature as if you were a part of it. You cast [[Spells/Telepathy (Player Core)|_telepathy_]] at 6th rank, which can only be used to communicate with creatures that have the [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]] trait.
