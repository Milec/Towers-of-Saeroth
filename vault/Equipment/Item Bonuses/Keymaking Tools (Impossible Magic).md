# Keymaking Tools  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 233

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

This [[Equipment/Thieves' Toolkit|thieves’ toolkit]] provides its owner the ability to have continued control over a lock while leaving it in place. It grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]]. Upon completely opening a lock by picking it with this toolkit, the toolkit produces a temporary copy of the key for the picked lock. This phantom key can lock or unlock the lock just like the original key. The key appears attached to the thieves’ toolkit by a fine silver chain and lasts for 12 hours before it fades into nothing. The _keymaking tools_ can only create and maintain one key at a time in this way; creating a new key replaces the previous one.
