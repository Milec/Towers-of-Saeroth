# Bestial Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 289

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Your features transform into something bestial and lumbering.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks and unarmed attack rolls. You gain a claw unarmed attack with the agile trait and a jaws unarmed attack. Striking runes don't modify the damage caused by these attacks.

**Drawback** You take a –2 penalty to Reflex saves, [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks, and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks.
