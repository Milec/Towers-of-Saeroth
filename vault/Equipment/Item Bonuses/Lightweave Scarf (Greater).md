# Lightweave Scarf  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 127

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

The first of these strips of glittering cloth was worn by a monk from Jinin who would interweave it into his handwraps of mighty blows. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 24.

- **Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Illusion|illusions]] and [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]].
- **Weapon** ([[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) After you cast an illusion spell by activating the scarf, the weapon is shrouded in a mesmerizing illusory pattern. Your next Strike causes the target to be [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round if it hits. If you don’t make a Strike by the end of your next turn, the illusion ends with no effect.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Dancing Lights|_dancing lights_]].
