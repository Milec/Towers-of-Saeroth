# Symbol of Conflict  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296

**Usage** worn

---

This tarnished necklace can be attuned only by someone who is holy or unholy. When you attune it, it transforms into your deity's religious symbol or a personal symbol if you don't have a deity. You receive a +1 item bonus to Religion and a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against holy and unholy effects.

**Activate—Presence** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The symbol casts [[Spells/Bane|_bane_]] or [[Spells/Bless|_bless_]]. The counteract DC of these effects is 18, and the counteract modifier is +8.

**Craft Requirements**  You must be holy or unholy.

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296

**Price** 19,000 gp

The bonus to Religion is +3. Change the list of spells to 6th- rank bane, bless, divine wrath, and cleanse affliction. Add 6th-rank [[Spells/Summon Celestial|_summon celestial_]] to this list if you are holy and 6th- rank [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]] if you are unholy. The DC is 38 and the counteract modifier is +28.
