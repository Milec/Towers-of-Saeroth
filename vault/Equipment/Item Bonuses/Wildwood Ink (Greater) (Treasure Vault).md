# Wildwood Ink  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Tattoo]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 123

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

These curving, delicate designs resemble leaves, vines, or creepers, most often wrapped around a limb, ear, or throat, or curled around specific muscles. They help you blend in among plants. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks, which increases to +2 in forests.

**Activate** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature would detect you by Seeking; **Requirements** You’re in a forest or similar natural area; **Effect** The tattoo casts tree shape on you before you can be noticed. The duration of this spell is 10 minutes.

If you’ve already Activated the tattoo, you can supply a separate casting of [[Spells/One with Plants|_tree shape_]] to recharge the tattoo instead of having the spell’s normal effect. This allows you to Activate the tattoo again in the same day. You can do so multiple times each day, but only as many times as you continue to cast tree shape to recharge the tattoo after each use.
