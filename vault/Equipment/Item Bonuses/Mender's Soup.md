# Mender's Soup  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Processed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Processed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 49

**Price** 5 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

10 minutes (Interact)

---

This hot, broth-based soup typically contains tubers, leeks, zesty spices, reagents, and if desired, the meat of livestock. Civic authorities commission batches of mender’s soup for workers if a tricky job is on the agenda. After you eat the soup, its effects last 24 hours or until you make your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], whichever comes first. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks to [[Actions/Repair (Player Core)|Repair]] and restore an additional 5 Hit Points to items you successfully Repair during this time.

If you eat mender’s soup over the entire period required to attempt a Crafting check to Craft (typically a minimum of eating the soup each day for 4 days), the +1 bonus from the soup can be applied to that check, too.
