# Delve Scale  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 47

**Price** 155 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

If fried, a [[Bestiary/Bulette|bulette’s]] thick forefoot scales render into crunchy snacks. Alchemists add reagents to the frying oil to enhance the scales’ properties and flavor. For 1 minute after eating a delve scale, you gain a burrow Speed of 15 feet and a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]].
