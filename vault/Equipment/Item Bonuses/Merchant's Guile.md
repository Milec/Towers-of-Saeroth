# Merchant's Guile  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Fall of Plaguestone

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Fall of Plaguestone]] pg. 58

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn

**Activate**

three actions

envision, interact

---

The band of this ring is made from blue-colored iron and has two sharp, decorative protrusions on each side of the red-stone inlay. It feels quite heavy and reliable. Wearing this ring grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks, but only if the associated checks involve haggling or bargaining over a purchase or trade.
