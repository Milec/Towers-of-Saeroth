# Guangu of the Steppe  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 144

**Price** 225 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This large drum is made of hardwood and horse hide, with white silhouettes of coursing stallions along its circumference. This drum grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—The Hammer of Hooves** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hammer a thundering beat on the guangu. For 10 minutes, mounted allies within a 60-foot emanation gain a +10-foot status bonus to their mount’s Speeds. They also gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] checks to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] and automatically succeed when they Command an Animal they’re mounted on to take a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action (such as [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]).
