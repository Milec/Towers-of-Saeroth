# The Demon's Lantern  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 73

**Usage** carried

---

_The Demon's Lantern_ represents trickery and feats of legerdemain. As long as you have _The Demon's Lantern_ invested, you gain a +2 item bonus to checks made to [[Actions/Conceal an Object]], [[Actions/Palm an Object]], or [[Actions/Steal]]. This increases to a +3 item bonus if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature hits you with a melee attack;  **Effect** You release a flash of lights and attempt a Reflex saving throw. If your saving throw is higher than the attack roll for the triggering attack, it misses. If the attack misses, the attacker is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.
