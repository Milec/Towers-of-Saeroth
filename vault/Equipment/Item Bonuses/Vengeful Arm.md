# Vengeful Arm  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** Followers of Abraxas from Nex have access to this tattoo.

---

[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]] teaches that an eye for an eye is the basis of law. This tattoo of a coiled viper provides a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] checks.

**Activate** single action envision;     **Effect** Abraxas transforms the tattoo into a weapon of your vengeance. The _vengeful arm_ crawls into your hand and becomes a [[Equipment/Retribution Axe|retribution axe]]. You can use this activation again while holding the axe to revert the vengeful arm to a tattoo. Like other _retribution axes_, you can etch fundamental runes into the _vengeful arm's_ _retribution axe_ to increase its potency or to add [[Equipment/Striking|_striking_]].
