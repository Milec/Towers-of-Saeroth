# Scale of Igroon  *Item 21*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 180

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

Carved from a scale of the [[Traits/Kaiju|kaiju]] Igroon, this jagged shield refracts light around it in a shimmering haze. A _scale of Igroon_ (Hardness 20, HP 160, BT 80) recovers 4 Hit Points at the start of its wielder's turn. When you [[Actions/Raise a Shield]], you can use the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction with the scale of Igroon to block an attack or effect that deals acid, cold, electricity, fire, force, or sonic damage as well as physical damage.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** You're in an area of [[Rules/Bright Light|bright]] or [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]]; **Effect** You angle the shield to refract light. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +4 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]] and can do so while [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]]. This bonus ends if you Activate another ability or use the Shield Block reaction.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** You're in an area of bright light; **Effect** You angle the shield at a target within 60 feet, reflecting light into its eyes. It must attempt a DC 42 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until its next turn begins.  

**Failure** The target is blinded for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is blinded for 2d4 hours.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You use Shield Block and prevent yourself from taking energy damage from a line, ray, or a direct attack, including a [[Spells/Force Barrage|_force barrage_]] spell;  **Effect** You reflect the energy along a trajectory you choose. The effect travels only up to its remaining range, using its original parameters if it strikes other targets.

**Destruction** If a deity, kaiju, spawn of a deity, titan, or being of similar power stomps on a _scale of Igroon_ while in absolute darkness, the shield is destroyed permanently.
