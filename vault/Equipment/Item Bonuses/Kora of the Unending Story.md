# Kora of the Unending Story  *Item 14*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Coda (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Coda]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -211- The Secret of Deathstalk Tower|Pathfinder #211: The Secret of Deathstalk Tower]] pg. 83

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This 21-stringed instrument symbolizes connectivity with the past, present, and future. The kora grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks made to tell stories when used to accompany song or speech.

_The kora of the unending story_ is a coda instrument—an item that functions like a [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]] but in the form of a [[Equipment/Musical Instrument (Player Core)|musical instrument]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the kora to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Read Aura (Player Core)|_read aura_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Object Reading (Player Core 2)|_object reading_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Mind Reading (Player Core)|_mind reading_]]
- **4th** [[Spells/Read Omens (Player Core)|_read omens_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Vision of Death|_vision of death_]]
- **6th** _object reading_
