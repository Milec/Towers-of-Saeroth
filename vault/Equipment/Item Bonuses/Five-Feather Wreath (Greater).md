# Five-Feather Wreath  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 170

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

Identical feathers radiate from the center of this spellheart, held in place by woven straw. The spell attack roll of any spell cast by Activating this item is +8, and the spell DC is 18.

- **Armor** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks and resistance 2 to falling damage.
- **Weapon** After you cast an [[Traits/Air|air]] spell by Activating the wreath, you can Fly 5 feet as a single action, or you can Fly 10 feet as a free action if the spell you cast wasn't a cantrip. This must be your next action, and if you don't have a fly Speed, you must end the flight on solid ground or fall.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Gale Blast|_gale blast_]].
