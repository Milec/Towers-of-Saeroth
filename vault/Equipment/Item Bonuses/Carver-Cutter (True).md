# Carver-Cutter  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 201

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe]]

---

Many of the experienced woodcarvers of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] learn to fight as well, the better to travel into hazardous areas in search of rare and beautiful types of wood for their craft. A _carver-cutter_ serves a dual role as a weapon and a tool for felling trees and engraving wood. The _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Battle Axe|battle axe]]_ looks like an exquisitely made woodcutting axe.

**Activate—Carving Chisel** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You pull free a woodworking chisel stored in the knob of the axe. It can have any standard chisel shape you imagine as you activate this ability. You get a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] checks for woodworking if you use chisels from the _carver-cutter_. The chisel also functions as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Main-gauche|main-gauche]]. All runes on the axe are replicated on the chisel, but only while both items are on the same person. You can Interact to reinsert the chisel.

**Activate—Chop Down** two actions ;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Make a Strike with the axe against a [[Traits/Plant|plant]] creature or a creature made primarily of wood. This Strike deals an additional 2d6 precision damage, and on a hit, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 minute.
