# Lacquered Waist Drum  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 303

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This hourglass-shaped drum has two heads, each of which produces a different sound when struck. When played together, the resonance of the two sounds is effective in repelling evil spirits. This drum can be played as an instrument, granting a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]].

**Activate—Memories of Life** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You play both heads of the drums, generating a pulse of energy that targets one creature within 30 feet that has the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] trait. This deals 5d8 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage (DC 28 Fortitude save) to that creature; if the undead also has the [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|ghost]] trait, the ghost becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 if it fails the save.

**Activate—Memories of Joy** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You raucously play both heads of the drums, accompanied with shouting and dancing. This display generates a pulse of energy in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from you. All [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures within this area must attempt a DC 28 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature takes no damage.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d8 vitality damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 5d8 vitality damage; if the creature has the [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|ghost]] trait, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 10d8 vitality damage; if the creature has the ghost trait, it becomes stupefied 2 for 1 minute.
