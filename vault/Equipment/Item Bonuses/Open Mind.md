# Open Mind  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 305

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** Followers of Abraxas from Nex have access to this tattoo.

---

[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas]] teaches knowledge is the only power that matters. This tattoo of a stylized eye provides a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] checks.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Abraxas opens your inner eye; you gain the effects of [[Spells/Hypercognition|_hypercognition_]].
