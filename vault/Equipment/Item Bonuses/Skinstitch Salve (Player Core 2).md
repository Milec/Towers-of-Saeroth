# Skinstitch Salve  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 296

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This sticky salve stubbornly holds wounds closed and encourages swift natural healing. You can activate the salve in either of the following ways.

**Activate—Administer Stitch** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]] without requiring healer's tools. You can either gain a +2 item bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check or use the salve's Medicine modifier of +13 instead of your own.

**Activate—Stitch Wounds** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You [[Actions/Treat Wounds (Player Core)|Treat Wounds]] or use [[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]];  **Effect** You gain a +2 item bonus to the triggering [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] check. If you roll a success on the Medicine check, you get a critical success instead.
