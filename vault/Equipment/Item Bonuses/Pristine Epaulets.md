# Pristine Epaulets  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 152

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

Gaudy and sparkling, these intricately decorated epaulets have clearly never seen a battlefield. Worn by officers with more schooling or connections than actual fighting experience, these epaulets grant you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] checks.

**Activate—I Meant to Say** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You critically fail a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check;  **Effect** The _pristine epaulets_ are often worn to both tense military negotiations and social events and can help you recover from a misstep. You can reroll the check, but you must take the new result.
