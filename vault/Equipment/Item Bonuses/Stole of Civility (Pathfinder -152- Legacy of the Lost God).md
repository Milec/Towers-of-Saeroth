# Stole of Civility  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 75

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

Woven from swaths of rich brocade silk and embroidered with ancient Azlanti script in golden thread, this stole imparts a noble appearance to even the homeliest of individuals when draped over the shoulders. While wearing a stole of civility, you receive a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] with humans or [[Actions/Request|Make a Request]] of humans, and a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks against humans. The stole also grants you full understanding of the [[Setting/Languages/Azlanti]] language.

**Activate** single action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** you are a human; **Effect** You gain 10 temporary Hit Points, which last for 10 minutes.
