# Tactician's Helm  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 152

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Repurposing and enchanting a helmet worn by a battlefield commander can create a _tactician’s helm_, imparting knowledge of battlefield tactics that feeds off your minor victories. The helm grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Warfare Lore]] checks. Also, a jewel adorns the brow of the helmet. This jewel becomes charged each time you hit a creature with a [[Actions/Reactive Strike]]. A _tactician’s helm_ can hold up to 2 charges, and its charges reset to 0 when you invest it.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])**Cost** 1 charge from the helm; **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You choose one of the following effects.

- **Charge!** Stride twice.
- **Move It!** You gain a +2 status bonus to Acrobatics and Athletics checks until the end of this turn.
- **Protect!** If you're wielding a shield, Stride to a space adjacent to an ally, then Raise your Shield.
- **Re-Arm!** Interact up to three times. Each of these actions must be used to do something listed under [[Rules/Interact]].
