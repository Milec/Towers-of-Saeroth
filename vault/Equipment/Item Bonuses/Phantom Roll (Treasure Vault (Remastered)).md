# Phantom Roll  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 50

**Price** 13 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

10 minutes ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

A phantom roll contains vegetables, greens, and fine, clear noodles, all wrapped in transparent, edible starch paper and alchemically treated and laced with a tangy sauce. Upon eating the roll, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks you attempt during the [[Actions/Avoid Notice]] exploration activity. You can also Avoid Notice at full Speed or combine it with [[Actions/Investigate]] or [[Actions/Scout]] while moving at half Speed. These effects expire 24 hours after you eat the roll or when you make your next daily preparations, whichever comes first.
