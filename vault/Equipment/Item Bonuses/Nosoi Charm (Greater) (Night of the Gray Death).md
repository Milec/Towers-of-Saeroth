# Nosoi Charm  *Item 12+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Night of the Gray Death

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Night of the Gray Death]] pg. 53

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

Nosois serve as scribes and messengers to psychopomps. They are rare outside of the Boneyard, so it's common for [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomps]] venturing to other planes to carry a nosoi facsimile to aid in their travels. This tiny icon of a masked raven emits twittering sounds in perfect accompaniment to your performances. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks to play an instrument, orate, or sing. You can cast [[Spells/Sending|_sending_]] once per day as a divine innate spell.

**Activate** three actions Interact;     **Effect** You twist the base of the charm, and the twittering it emits becomes helpful words to boost your arguments. You lose the item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks and gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to make [[Actions/Request|Requests]] until you use this activation again.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]] as an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet for 1 hour as long as you continue to hold the charm. This allows you to sense the life force that animates living creatures and the perverse force that animates the dead, though you can't distinguish between the two.
