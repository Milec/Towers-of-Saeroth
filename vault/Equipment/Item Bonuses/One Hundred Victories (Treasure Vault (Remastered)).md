# One Hundred Victories  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 121

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

One hundred cuts, healed into diamond-shaped scars, represent the ability to withstand the attacks of your enemies. [[Rules/Sidebars/Orc Settlements|Orc]] warriors covet these scar patterns and cluster them around what they consider to be their strongest assets—a pattern around the heart signifies a warrior with great endurance, while one along the arms indicates great upper body strength.

You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks. If you have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Ferocity]] (such as from the [[Feats/Orc Ferocity]] feat or a similar ability), the first time each day you use it, instead of remaining at 1 Hit Point, your Hit Points are set to an amount equal to your ancestry Hit Points, even if this amount is more than you had before using Ferocity.
