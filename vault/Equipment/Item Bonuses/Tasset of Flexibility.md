# Tasset of Flexibility  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 153

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

You can attach these light-brown leather flaps adorned with gold stitching to a breastplate or even clothing to protect your upper legs in battle. They give you the freedom to move your body to its limit without worrying about exposing yourself to a hit. While wearing the _tasset of flexibility_, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks.

**Activate—Lunging Attack** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tasset helps you stretch farther than you normally could. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with a melee weapon, increasing your [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] by 5 feet for that Strike.
