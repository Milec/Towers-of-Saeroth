# Conch of Otherworldly Seas  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 176

**Price** 1,850 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

Magical writing covers the surface of this beautiful conch shell, which emits a blue light from inside. A _conch of otherworldly seas_ is a [[Equipment/Musical Instrument|virtuoso handheld musical instrument]] that grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks attempted while using it.

The conch is a planar key for [[Spells/Plane Shift|_interplanar teleport_]] and similar magic to travel to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water]]. When using it this way, you can attune it to the waters of your destination to make it more likely to arrive where you intend to be, appearing 1d6×25 miles from your intended destination instead of 1d10×25 miles away.

**Activate—Voice of Oceans** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You hold the horn to your ear and can understand and speak [[Setting/Languages/Thalassic]] as long as it remains there.

**Activate—Sounds of the Deep**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You hold the horn to your ear and touch the correct series of runes inscribed on its surface, causing the conch to cast a 5th-rank [[Spells/Clairaudience|_clairaudience_]] spell for your benefit. Provided you choose a location that's underwater, you can extend the spell's range to 1 mile and hear with perfect clarity.
