# Ring of Lies  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 614

**Price** 850 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This plain silver ring has an almost oily sheen. While wearing the ring, you gain a +2 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Snapping your fingers on the hand that wears the ring causes the ring to cast [[Spells/Glibness|_glibness_]] on you with no visual manifestations of a spell being cast.
