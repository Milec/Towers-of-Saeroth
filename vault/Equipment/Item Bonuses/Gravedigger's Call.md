# Gravedigger's Call  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 127

**Price** 1,700 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Glaive]]

---

This rusted shovel functions as a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Decaying|decaying]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive (Tian Xia Character Guide)|glaive]]_ when wielded as a weapon, but it more closely resembles a neglected tool. Its haft was carved from the trunk of a long-dead tree that once grew among the broken stones of an abandoned cemetery, and its blade was forged from shattered remnants of the armor that failed to protect those buried there. When you carry or wield a gravedigger’s call, you gain a +1 item bonus to Perception checks to Seek haunts and to skill checks to determine the reasons for a haunt or spirit’s existence.

**Activate—Call the Fallen** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Rouse Skeletons (Player Core 2)|_rouse skeletons_]] as a 5th-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell. The DC is 30.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _rouse skeletons_ (5th rank).
