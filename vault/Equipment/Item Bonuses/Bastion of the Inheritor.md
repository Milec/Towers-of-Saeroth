# Bastion of the Inheritor  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 14

**Price** 1,750 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 5

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Bastion Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Bastion Plate]]

---

Worn by [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae (Divine Mysteries)|Iomedae’s]] prestigious knights, this _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Bastion Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|bastion plate]]_ is emblazoned with the Inheritor’s religious symbol and sports a white cloak. While the cloak is white, this armor grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], provided your target has no enmity toward Iomedae.

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** The armor’s cloak becomes red for 1 minute. As long as the cloak is red, you gain the benefits of the armor’s deflect melee trait without needing to spend an additional action to activate it during each turn.

**Craft Requirements** You worship Iomedae.
