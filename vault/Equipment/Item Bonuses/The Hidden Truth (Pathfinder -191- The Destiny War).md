# The Hidden Truth  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 75

**Usage** carried

---

_The Hidden Truth_ represents the act of observing something beyond the obvious to gain obscure lore. As long as this card is invested, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], and to all checks made to [[Actions/Search|Research]] while using the [[Rules/Research]] subsystem from the _Gamemastery Guide_. This increases to a +3 bonus if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]]. The level of the spell is one-half your level, rounded down (minimum 6th level). If you're at least 17th-level, you can affect up to three adjacent willing targets with this spell at the same time as you cast it.
