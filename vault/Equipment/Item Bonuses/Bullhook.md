# Bullhook  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 73

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _bullhook_ is a stout rod about 4 feet long with a blunt hook on the end. Too dull to be useful as a weapon, a _bullhook_ is instead used to direct animals in training or in performing their tasks. A _bullhook_ grants you an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks to [[Actions/Command an Animal]] while you are holding it.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You wave the _bullhook_ to produce the effects of a [[Spells/Command|_command_]] spell. This spell loses the [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] trait and can target only animals.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of _command_ of the appropriate level.
