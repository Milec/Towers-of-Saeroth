# Gaze of the Mantis  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 64

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Seeing all Red Mantis contracts is outside the scope of PFS play._

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're a member of the Red Mantis Assassins

---

These glasses have faceted red lenses like those of a mantis and are popular as a less ostentatious alternative to a full mask. While wearing the _gaze of the mantis_, your field of view increases to give you better awareness of your surroundings. You gain a +2 item bonus to visual Perception checks and cannot be [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanked]].

**Activate—Target Sense** single action ;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re a Red Mantis assassin; **Effect** You learn the distance to and direction of all creatures that have a Red Mantis contract on them within 10 miles. If one of these creatures is within 100 feet, you also know any afflictions, conditions, and spells affecting them. This awareness lasts for 1 hour.
