# Avalanche Boots  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 102

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn boots

**Bulk** 1

---

While the insides of these boots are comfortable, fur-lined leather, the outsides are a jumble of slate plates, giving the impression of a rockslide. You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks and a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] and [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]]. When you invest the boots, you either increase your Strength score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever is higher.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You succeed or critically succeed with a [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]];  **Effect** If the Shove was a success, you push your opponent up to 10 feet instead of 5 feet. If the Shove was a critical success, you push your opponent up to 20 feet, and you can then choose to knock them [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
