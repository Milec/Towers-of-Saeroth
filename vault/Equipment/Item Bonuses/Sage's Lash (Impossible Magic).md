# Sage's Lash  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 227

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn belt

**Bulk** 1

---

The thin, glittering strands of rope that form this thick belt appear to be spun gold. Strung along the front of the belt are a collection of four multicolored, perfectly spherical beads: amethyst, jade, quartz, and turquoise. While wearing the lash, you gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]. When you invest the belt, you either increase your Wisdom modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Jewel of the Mind** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You touch one of the jewels affixed to the _sage’s lash_ and think the associated command word. Depending on the jewel, a different effect is produced that affects you and all living creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. After the effect occurs, all four jewels disappear from the lash, reappearing at the next dawn.  
**• Amethyst** (light, revelation) Think “verity” to emit a magical trail of light from the lash that reveals the location of each creature in the emanation. For 1 round, if they would be [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], they become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead, and if they would be [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by a creature, they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] instead. A creature can attempt a DC 43 [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check to avoid the lash’s detection.  
**• Jade** (healing) Think “purity” to produce a wave of green light that ends any [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] condition and any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] affecting creatures in the emanation.  
**• Quartz** (healing, vitality) Think “lenity” to have each creature regain 30 Hit Points.  
**• Turquoise** Think “clarity” to grant all affected creatures a +2 status bonus to their next Will save for 1 minute.
