# Alchemist Goggles  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 310, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 605

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

These brass goggles are engraved with flame patterns and have thick, heavy lenses. While worn, they give you a +1 item bonus to Crafting checks to [[Actions/Craft (Player Core)|Craft]] alchemical items. When making [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with alchemical bombs, you ignore lesser [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] . If your Strike with an alchemical bomb fails (but doesn't critically fail), you gain a +1 item bonus to the splash damage the target of the Strike takes.
