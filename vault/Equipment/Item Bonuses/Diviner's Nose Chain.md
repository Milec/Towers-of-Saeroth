# Diviner's Nose Chain  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 77

**Price** 650 gp

**Usage** worn

---

The diviner's nose chain is worn by attaching piercings to the ear and nose, creating a connection between the senses of hearing and smell. The diviner's nose chain grants you [[Rules/Scent|scent]] out to 30 feet as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]], and a +1 item bonus to checks to [[Actions/Seek]] or [[Actions/Sense Motive]].
