# Shining Hackle  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 152

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Some troops wear this plume of short feathers in their hats as part of a formal uniform, but the soft glow emanating from it can be useful elsewhere. While wearing the _shining hackle_, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks based on sight, but you also take a –1 item penalty to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks.

**Activate—Glowing Hackle** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]])    **Effect** Your _shining hackle_ glows even brighter, shedding [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet). This effect lasts until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] it.
