# Fan of Falling Words  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 302

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This folding handheld paper fan appears blank when first found, but if the wielder unfolds its leaves as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, they display an ink-brush painting of the wielder’s choice. The fan must be closed and opened again to display a different painting. While held, the fan of falling words grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks to act or perform comedy, orate, or sing.

**Activate—Whispered World** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You manifest the tapestry of your words, causing the image you depict on the fan to leap off its leaves. The fan casts [[Spells/Illusory Scene (Player Core)|_illusory scene_]] to your specifications (DC 27 Will save). While this only takes 3 actions to [[Actions/Perform (Player Core)|perform]] (as opposed to the spell’s normal 10 minutes of casting), the effect has no range, and the 50-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] it creates is centered on you.
