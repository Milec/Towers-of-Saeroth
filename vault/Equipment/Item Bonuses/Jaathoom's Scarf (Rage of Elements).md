# Jaathoom's Scarf  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 75

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** worn belt

---

This scarf is made of fine silk that's the same shade of blue as a clear, cloudless sky. The short ends are edged with a fine gold fringe that seems to sway even in still weather as though touched by invisible winds. The long edges have exquisite embroidery in threads that vary from a blue identical to the silk to the deep gray of storm clouds during winter. Wearing this scarf grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks to dance and to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Escape]].

**Activate—Vanish** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The scarf casts 4th-rank [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] on you.

**Activate—Jaathoom's Rebuke** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You let the winds around you catch the edges of the _jaathoom's scarf_, and a jaathoom shuyookh appears with a sudden updraft. The winds force your enemies back, granting you some breathing room in battle. Each enemy in a 10-foot emanation must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet. A creature that critically fails is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] after being moved. Creatures with the [[Traits/Air|air]] trait are immune to all these effects.
