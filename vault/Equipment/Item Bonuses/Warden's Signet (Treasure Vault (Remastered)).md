# Warden's Signet  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 151

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This symbol shows your dedication to the magic practiced by some [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-204)|rangers]]. Most rangers wear it on an amulet, ring, or piercing. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can use only to cast a ranger warden spell. When you use this Focus Point, the _warden’s signet_ also casts a 4th-rank [[Spells/Oaken Resilience|_oaken resilience_]] spell on you. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a ranger with at least one warden spell.
