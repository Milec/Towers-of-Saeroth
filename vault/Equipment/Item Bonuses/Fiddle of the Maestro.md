# Fiddle of the Maestro  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Coda (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Coda]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 136

**Price** 10,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

---

This exquisite fiddle is perfectly carved and balanced to produce the purest sound while granting its player perfect balance and poise. It grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. If you’re a master in Performance, while playing it you also gain a +1 status bonus to Reflex saves and a +1 item bonus to Dexterity-based skill checks. If you’re legendary in Performance, the bonuses are +2.

When you Perform with the fiddle, you can choose to create a harsh and discordant note. You critically fail the Performance check, shred the strings of the fiddle, and create an 8th-level [[Spells/Noise Blast|_sound burst_]], with a 40-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you instead of a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]. The fiddle can’t be played again until the strings are replaced at a cost of 200 gp.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from this instrument to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrips** [[Spells/Dancing Lights|_dancing lights_]], [[Spells/Ghost Sound|_ghost sound_]], [[Spells/Message (Player Core)|_message_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_command_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Calm|_calm emotions_]], [[Spells/Revealing Light|_faerie fire_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Enthrall (Player Core)|_enthrall_]]
- **4th** [[Spells/Honeyed Words|_glibness_]]
- **5th** [[Spells/Sending (Player Core)|_sending_]]
- **6th** _calm emotions_, [[Spells/Dominate (Player Core)|_dominate_]], [[Spells/Vibrant Pattern (Player Core)|_vibrant pattern_]]
- **7th** _dominate_, [[Spells/Prismatic Spray|_prismatic spray_]], [[Spells/True Target (Player Core)|_true target_]], _vibrant pattern_, [[Spells/Visions of Danger (Player Core 2)|_visions of danger_]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
