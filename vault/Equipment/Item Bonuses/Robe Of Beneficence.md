# Robe Of Beneficence  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 203

**Price** 14,000 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|Explorer's Clothing]]

---

This soft, buttery smooth robe is _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer’s clothing]]_ made from silk and [[Bestiary/Unicorn (Monster Core)|unicorn]] hair. The back and shoulders are appliquéd with clusters of angel feathers to form a motif of unfurled wings. On this robe are subtle arabesques, loops, and curlicues that are pleasing to the eye and evoke a sense of calm. When you wear this robe, you gain an [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] aura of purity and kindness that extends from you in a 30-foot emanation, granting you a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] and to make [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Requests]] of creatures who are within your aura.

**Activate—Compelled Nonviolence** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], incapacitation, [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The wings of this robe extend, and your aura glows golden, briefly becoming visible as it does. Creatures within your aura who are [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]], [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent (Player Core)|indifferent]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly (Player Core)|unfriendly]] to you must attempt a DC 36 Will saving throw with the following effects.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is stunned 2.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature can’t use any [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]] against you for 1 round.
