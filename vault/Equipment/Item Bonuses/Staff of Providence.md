# Staff of Providence  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Staff (GM Core)|Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 308

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A large, stylized symbol of an eye adorns the top of this wooden staff, representing the watchful eye of the divine powers. The bearer of the staff can guide and protect, seeing bounties and tragedies that could befall them in the future. When wielding this staff, you gain a +1 item bonus to Survival checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]] and to Religion checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
