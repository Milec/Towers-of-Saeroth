# Flowing Water  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -207- Resurrection Flood|Pathfinder #207: Resurrection Flood]] pg. 80

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

The Flood Truce is a season of relative peace, and this tattoo of a river can soothe your temper when you might otherwise lash out. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks made against orcs who honor the Flood Truce.

**Activate—Embody the Truce** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect would compel you to harm an ally or bystander;  **Effect** Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check with a counteract rank of 2 to end the effect.
