# Eye Slash  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 120

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

Small scars or marks around your eye improve your distant vision. These scars are especially common among [[Ancestries/Orc (Player Core)|orc]] scouts, who favor scars shaped like eagle talons. You can see four times farther than normal. If you have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]], you can see blood in color. Higher-level versions of an eye slash are larger and more elaborate scars or marks, radiating out around the eye.
