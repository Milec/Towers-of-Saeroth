# Mariner's Mien  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 78

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Pathfinder Society does not use vehicle rules_

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're from the Mordant Spire

---

The swirls on this wooden mask—often worn by Mordant Censors—move and shift, reflecting the shape of the nearest currents. Wearing the mask grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] and [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks. In addition, it allows you to pilot a boat with simply a thought. When you pilot a vehicle, you can keep both hands free and [[Actions/Drive (Varies)|Drive]] from any location on the vehicle.

**Activate—Set the Course** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are aboard a vehicle; **Effect** You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 minute or until you leave the vehicle, whichever comes first, and can use the extra action to [[Actions/Take Control (GM Core)|Take Control]] of the vehicle or, if you’re already piloting it, Drive or [[Actions/Stop (GM Core)|Stop]] the vehicle.
