Your features become striking, and your voice becomes musical and commanding, though emotion clouds your reason.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. Your critical failures with any of these skills become failures instead.

**Drawback** You take a –2 penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], and [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] checks. Choose one skill in which you're trained; for the duration, you become untrained in that skill.
