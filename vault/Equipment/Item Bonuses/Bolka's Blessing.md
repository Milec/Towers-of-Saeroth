# Bolka's Blessing  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 101

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Five Kings Mountains have access to this option._

---

This filigree grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks and to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]]. Additionally, once per day, the filigree symbol can be activated for a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] effect.

**Activate—Gift of Life** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You regain 3d10 Hit Points.
