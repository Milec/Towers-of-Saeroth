# Warleader's Bulwark  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 279

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate (Player Core)|Breastplate]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate (Player Core)|breastplate]]_is made from shining bronze overlaid with reinforcing golden panels emblazoned with images of loyal soldiers. Wearing this breastplate grants you a commanding aura. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks, but you take a –2 item penalty to Stealth checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] and Deception checks to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]].

**Activate—Command Bravery** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You grant allies within 100 feet a +2 status bonus to saves against fear effects for 1 minute. When you activate this ability, each affected ally who's [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] reduces their frightened value by 1.
