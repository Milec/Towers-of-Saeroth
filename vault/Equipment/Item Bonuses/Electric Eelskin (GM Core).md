# Electric Eelskin  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 230

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

---

Shining, slippery eelskin covers the plates of this _+1 [[Equipment/Magic Armor|resilient]] [[Equipment/Slick|greater slick]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]]_. The armor gives you the ability to breathe water and grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks you attempt in the water.

**Activate—Unleash Charge** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You cast a 2nd-rank [[Spells/Thunderstrike|_thunderstrike_]] with a DC of 29.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _thunderstrike_.
