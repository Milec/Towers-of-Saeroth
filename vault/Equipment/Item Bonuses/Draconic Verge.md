# Draconic Verge  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 144

**Price** 370 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This scepter is made from dragon bone, with minute runes carved along its length, a dragonskin leather grip, and caps of gold on each end. The scepter grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]]. Such items are considered particularly gruesome and vile by dragons, and they’re invariably hostile to any creature they discover carrying a _draconic verge_, going so far as to single out that individual for destruction.

**Activate—Dragon’s Eminence** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold the verge aloft, tapping into the majesty of the dragon from whom the verge was made. All enemies in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must succeed a DC 23 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 (frightened 3 on a critical failure).
