# Kols's Oath  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 101

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Five Kings Mountains have access to this option._

---

This filigree grants you, as the clan dagger’s owner, a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] checks and to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]]. Additionally, once per day, the filigree symbol can be activated to compel an enemy to act.

**Activate—Vow Unbreakable** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You command a creature within 30 feet to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] away from you, [[Actions/Drop Prone (Player Core)|drop prone]], or [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|release]] one item it’s holding. The creature can choose to perform that action as the first action on its next turn; if it doesn’t, it takes 4d6 mental damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Will save).
