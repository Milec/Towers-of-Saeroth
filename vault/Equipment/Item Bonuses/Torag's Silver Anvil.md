# Torag's Silver Anvil  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 123

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [Meteor Hammer](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=97)

**Access** follower of  Torag

---

This portable silver anvil has a narrow hoop on one end, through which a long, sturdy chain has been strung. _[[Setting/Deities/Torag|Torag's]] Silver Anvil_ can be wielded as a _+3 [[Equipment/Holy|holy]] [[Equipment/Flaming|greater flaming]] [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] [meteor hammer](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=97)_. Outside of combat, the anvil can be used with a hammer as an incredibly effective portable forge, heating up the metal to be forged without a furnace and granting a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] checks involving metalworking. When you use the anvil to successfully [[Actions/Repair]] a metal item, the item recovers an additional 10 Hit Points (or an additional 20 on a critical success).
