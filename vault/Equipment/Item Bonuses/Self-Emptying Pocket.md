# Self-Emptying Pocket  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Contract]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 165

---

You never perform to an empty crowd after promising any profits to a phantom in a playhouse, sealed by holes that appear in your pockets. By meeting with the attendees at your events, you can use [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Gather Information]]. When you would use Performance to [[Actions/Earn Income]], you don't earn any gold pieces, as the money disappears before you can even count it.

 The entity that holds your _bargained contract_ can influence your reception any time you perform for a crowd or Activate the pocket. If they influence your performance favorably, you gain a +2 item bonus to your Performance check. If they influence your performance unfavorably, you gain a -2 item penalty to your Performance check. If you've failed to earn the entity money in quite a while, it typically influences your performance unfavorably.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You exercise all of your charm on a creature, turning a chance meeting into an impromptu performance that commands attention. Attempt a single Performance check against the Perception DC of the creature. On a success, the creature is affected as though by a successful Deception check to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]]. The entity that holds your contract can influence this performance as well.
