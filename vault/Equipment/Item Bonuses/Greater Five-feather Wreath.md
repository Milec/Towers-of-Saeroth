# Five-feather Wreath  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 235

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

Identical feathers radiate from the center of this spellheart, held in place by woven straw. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 18.  
**• Armor** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to falling damage.  
**• Weapon** After you cast an [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] spell by Activating the _wreath_, you can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] 5 feet as a single action, or you can Fly 10 feet as a free action if the spell you cast wasn’t a cantrip. This must be your next action, and if you don’t have a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]], you must end the flight on solid ground or fall.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Gale Blast (Player Core 2)|_gale blast_]]_.
