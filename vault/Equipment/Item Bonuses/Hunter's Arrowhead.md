# Hunter's Arrowhead  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 153

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** worn

---

A _hunter’s arrowhead_ is meant to be worn as a charm, such as a pendant, or carried in a pocket or quiver. The arrowhead is etched with images sacred to the elven god [[Setting/Deities/Ketephys (Divine Mysteries)|Ketephys]]. While you wear or carry the arrowhead, it infuses you with great skill at hunting, and you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks and attack rolls against any creature you’ve currently designated as your prey with [[Actions/Hunt Prey]]. A _hunter’s arrowhead_ is also a religious symbol of Ketephys.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would miss with an attack made with a bow;  **Effect** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your attack roll, possibly turning a miss into a hit.
