# Feather of the Unfounded Bravado  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 144

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This large feather is garishly colored in reds, oranges, and yellows, but on closer inspection, it’s simply a particularly large chicken feather that’s been dyed. Carrying the feather provides a boost to your self-confidence, but it might lead you into dangerous situations.

**Activate—Overconfident Facade** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For 1 hour, the feather grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] and [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], but a –1 item penalty on [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks, as your inflated confidence leads you to attempt things you simply cannot do.
