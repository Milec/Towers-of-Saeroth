# Cloak of Illusions  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 287

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This cloak flows, covering and concealing the wearer's body. The cloak allows you to cast [[Spells/Figment|_figment_]] as an occult innate cantrip. Although naturally a dull gray, while invested the cloak picks up colors and patterns from its surroundings, granting a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks.

**Activate—Draw Hood** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw the hood up and gain the effects of [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]], with the spell's normal duration or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.
