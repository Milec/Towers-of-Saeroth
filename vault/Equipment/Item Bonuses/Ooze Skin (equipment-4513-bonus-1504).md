# Ooze Skin  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]] has been treated with extract from [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze (Monster Core)|oozes]], which can be reactivated in the presence of a strong acid. A receptacle in the armor can hold an [[Equipment/Acid Flask (Player Core 2)|acid flask]], which takes 3 [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions to install. A loaded suit of ooze skin can be activated to cause the leather to weep slippery protoplasm, granting an item bonus to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] and [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]] checks equal to the acid flask’s item bonus. The protoplasm also irritates the skin on prolonged contact, causing any creature that [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|grapples]] or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|swallows]] you to take acid damage equal to the acid flask’s splash damage. Ooze skin remains activated for a number of rounds equal to the level of the acid flask installed. The effects consume the acid flask, and once the reaction has begun it can’t be deactivated.
