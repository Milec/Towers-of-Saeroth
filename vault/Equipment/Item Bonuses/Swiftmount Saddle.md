# Swiftmount Saddle  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Companion]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 91

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn saddle

**Bulk** 2

**Access** Knights of Lastwall have access to this item.

---

This sturdy leather saddle was designed to improve communication between mount and rider, allowing even inexperienced knights to command their mounts in battle. A swiftmount saddle grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks made to [[Actions/Command an Animal]] for anyone who is riding the creature wearing it.

**Activate** free action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You're adjacent to the creature wearing the _swiftmount saddle_ and could [[Actions/Mount]] it; **Effect** You Mount the creature wearing the _swiftmount saddle_.
