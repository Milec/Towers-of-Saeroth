# Twilight Tattoo  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 52

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** You’re a member of the Twilight Talons.

---

This tattoo of a black eagle gripping a sword and arrows in its talons identifies the bearer as a member of the Twilight Talons. Agents typically keep the tattoo hidden unless they need to prove their identity to another member. The bearer gains a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks.

**Activate—Fade** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]])    **Effect** You hide your tattoo from view. The tattoo is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] for 1 day and can’t be detected by effects such as [[Spells/Detect Magic (Player Core)|_detect magic_]] and [[Spells/Read Aura (Player Core)|_read aura_]]. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect.

**Activate—Inscribe** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You lay your hand on a piece of text, and your tattoo makes a perfect copy of it, storing it as a ring of swirling letters surrounding the design. The tattoo can hold text equivalent to two pages of a book, a single [[Traits/Scroll (GM Core)|scroll]], or a similar area of other surfaces, though it doesn’t replicate any magical effect or other special properties of the original words. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this effect, and when you Dismiss it, the tattoo copies the original text onto a blank writing surface you’re touching.
