# Gloaming Arc  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 75

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar]]

---

This black _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]]_ reflects light only along its edge, like a thin crescent moon. It grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] while you're holding it, increasing to a +2 item bonus if it has a [[Equipment/Weapon Potency|_+3 weapon potency rune_]].

**Activate** three actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Attempt a melee Strike with the _gloaming arc_. On a hit, you slice away the target's shadow from its body and it must attempt a DC 30 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. The target doesn't cast a shadow until it is no longer drained.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but drained 2.
