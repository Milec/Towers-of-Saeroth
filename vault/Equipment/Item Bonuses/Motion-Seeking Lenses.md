# Motion-Seeking Lenses  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 152

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

**Bulk** L

---

When you wear these green-tinted glasses, all the movement in the surrounding area seems to jump out at you. You gain a +1 item bonus to your [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] DC against [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], and anyone attempting to Sneak doesn’t benefit from the circumstance bonus from [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] against your Perception DC.

**Activate—Find the Hidden** single action ([[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You twist the lenses of your glasses as you look for someone hidden. You [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] with a +1 item bonus. If you find a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] creature or object, you can [[Actions/Point Out (Player Core)|Point Out]] as a free action.
