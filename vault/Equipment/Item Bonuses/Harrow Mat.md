# Harrow Mat  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 66

**Price** 8 gp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** L

---

This leather mat bears intricate designs and symbols embossed into them to enhance the mystique of a harrow reading. The mat provides a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], and relevant [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] checks (such as Fortune-Telling Lore or Harrow Lore) to convince a creature that a harrow reading was accurate.
