# Whip Tail  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 99

**Usage** implanted

**Bulk** L

---

You have a strong, prehensile tail. You gain a tail [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This tail is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]]. You can also use your tail for the [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] action, even if your hands are otherwise occupied.
