# Dragonfire Halfbow  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -199- To Bloom Below the Web|Pathfinder #199: To Bloom Below the Web]] pg. 75

**Price** 1,800 gp

**Usage** held in one hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow]]

---

Crafted by layering bamboo with strips of sliced scales harvested from a fire-breathing dragon, this bow has a significant draw strength. This design is commonly seen in Hongal, as the shorter limbs allow for easier maneuvering on horseback. While the wielder of this _+2 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow (Player Core)|composite shortbow]]_ is mounted, they apply the bow's item bonus to Nature checks to Command your mount.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You draw the bow back to its maximum draw length without nocking an arrow, and as you release the string, the _dragonfire halfbow_ casts [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]] (heightened to 5th- level; DC 29 Reflex save), targeted at a point of your choosing within the spell's range. You and your mount are immune to the effects of this fireball.
