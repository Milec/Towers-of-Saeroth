# Knight's Maintenance Kit  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 86

**Price** 50 gp

**Hands** 2

**Bulk** 1

**Access** Knights of Lastwall have access to this item.

---

This set of gear contains a repair kit, cloth rolls for storing or covering equipment, and specialized mixtures developed by the Knights of Lastwall to clean and treat armaments for continued use. This kit gives you a +1 item bonus to checks to [[Actions/Repair]] weapons, shields, and armor using the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and you restore an additional 5 Hit Points when you successfully Repair such items using it.
