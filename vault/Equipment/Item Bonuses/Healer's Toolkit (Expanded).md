# Healer's Toolkit  *Item 0+*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 288

**Price** 5 gp

**Hands** -1

**Bulk** 1

---

This kit of bandages, herbs, and suturing tools is necessary for [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks to [[Actions/Administer First Aid]], [[Actions/Treat Disease]], [[Actions/Treat Poison]], or [[Actions/Treat Wounds]]. If you wear your healer's toolkit, you can draw and replace them as part of the action that uses them.

You can use a toolkit with 1 hand if you’re wearing it or 2 if you’re holding it. [[Rules/Wearing Toolkits|See here for details]].
