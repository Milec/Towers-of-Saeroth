# Tracker's Goggles  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

These lenses of forest-green glass are bound in rough leather stitched with crude twine. While wearing these goggles, you gain a +1 bonus to Survival checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction]] and [[Actions/Track]]. If you fail a check to Track, you can try again after 30 minutes rather than an hour.

## Tracker's Goggles  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 616

**Price** 60 gp

## Tracker's Goggles (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 296, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 616

**Price** 660 gp

---

The goggles grant a +2 bonus. If you fail a check to Track, you can try again after 15 minutes rather than an hour.
