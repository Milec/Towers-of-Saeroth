# Draconal Mask  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 81

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

---

This [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]-themed half-mask helps you to navigate through darkness and to notice things from afar—such as helping to find seats in a theater after a show begins, or to pick up details from a show even if you’re seated in the back row! You gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks and never take a penalty to Perception checks based on distance or weather. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision, or greater darkvision]] if you have darkvision. When you [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] in the mask you either increase your Wisdom score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score. If you are [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] as long as you wear the mask.

**Activate—Dragons See the Truth** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You fail a saving throw against an [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] effect;  **Effect** The mask attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the triggering illusion with a counteract rank of 9 and a counteract modifier of +31. You then gain the effects of [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] for 1 minute.
