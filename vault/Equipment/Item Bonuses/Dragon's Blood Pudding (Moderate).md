# Dragon's Blood Pudding  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 46

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

While some unscrupulous chefs claim that this savory pudding is made with real dragon's blood, its crimson color and acrid smell actually come from bloody mandrake paste, ginger root, and distilled terrap sap. This potent combination singes the nostrils and throat, removing effects that make you sluggish. When you consume the pudding, it attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check with the listed counteract modifier to remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] condition from a single source, using the source of that condition to determine the counteract level and DC.

## Dragon's Blood Pudding (Lesser)  *Item 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 46

**Price** 5 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pudding has a +6 counteract modifier.

## Dragon's Blood Pudding (Moderate)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 46

**Price** 25 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pudding has a +9 counteract modifier. You also become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 round. While taking the additional action from being quickened, you scream, as a result of both the flavor and the mandrake paste's properties. You can use the additional action to Step, Stride, Strike, or [[Actions/Demoralize]]. If you choose to Demoralize, you gain a +2 item bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check.

## Dragon's Blood Pudding (Greater)  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 46

**Price** 425 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pudding has a +20 counteract modifier. You become quickened as per the 5th-level version, except you gain a +3 item bonus to the Intimidation check.

## Dragon's Blood Pudding (Major)  *Item 19*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 46

**Price** 5,500 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pudding has a +31 counteract modifier. You become quickened as per the 5th-level version, except the effect lasts for 2 rounds and you gain a +4 item bonus to the Intimidation check.
