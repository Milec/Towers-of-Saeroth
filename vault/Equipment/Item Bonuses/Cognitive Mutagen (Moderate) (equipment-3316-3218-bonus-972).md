# Cognitive Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir (Player Core)|Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen (Player Core 2)|Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 289

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Your mind becomes clear, but physical matters seem ephemeral.

**Benefit** You gain an item bonus to Arcana, Crafting, Lore, Occultism, and Society checks and all checks to Recall Knowledge. Your critical failures on [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] checks become failures instead.

**Drawback** You take a –2 penalty to weapon and unarmed attack rolls, [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks, and [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks. You can carry 2 less Bulk than normal before becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]], and the maximum Bulk you can carry is reduced by 4.
