This gritty powder improves responsiveness but suppresses thoughts and leaves you weakened. The saving throw for addiction to scour is DC 30, and the addiction is [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]].

**Saving Throw** DC 25 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 hour; **Maximum Duration** 6 hours; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] and +2 item bonus to Reflex saves and Dexterity-based skill checks (1 hour); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] (1 hour); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]] and fatigued (1 hour)
