# Stage Magician's Cloak  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -204- Stage Fright|Pathfinder #204: Stage Fright]] pg. 81

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This black velvet cloak has a silvery blue iridescent lining. The cloak can be worn with either side facing out—switching from one side to the other requires two Interact actions. If these actions are both taken on the same turn, the act of removing and redonning the cloak doesn’t cause it to lose its [[Rules/Investiture (GM Core)|investiture]]. When worn with the black velvet lining facing out, the _stage magician’s cloak_ grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] checks. When worn with the silvery blue lining facing out, it grants a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] checks.

**Activate—Now You See Me...** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Distracting colorful smoke blasts outward from the cloak in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], causing all creatures in the area to become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off‑guard]] until the start of your next turn (DC 30 Will save). If you were previously [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], you lose those conditions. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round and may use the extra action to [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]], [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], or [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]].

**Activate—...Now You Don't** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Harmless silver smoke issues from the cloak in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. You cast a 2nd-rank [[Spells/Invisibility (Player Core)|_invisibility_]] on yourself and are transported, along with all items you’re wearing and holding, from your current space to an unoccupied space within 30 feet that you can see. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an extradimensional space—the transportation part of this effect fails.
