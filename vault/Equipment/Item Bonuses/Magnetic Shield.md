# Magnetic Shield  *Item 5*

single action

---

Copper rings spiral around this [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]. Twin electrical probes near the grip can socket into a jar of moderate (or higher leveled) [[Equipment/Bottled Lightning (Player Core 2)|bottled lightning]], which takes 3 [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions to install. A loaded magnetic shield can be activated to convert it into an electromagnet. When an activated magnetic shield is raised, the circumstance bonus increases by 1 against attacks made with weapons primarily made of metal. If you use a [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] against a creature attacking you with such a weapon, you also gain a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] attempts against that weapon until the end of your next turn. The shield remains charged for 3 rounds, consuming the bottled lightning, and once the reaction has begun it can’t be deactivated.
