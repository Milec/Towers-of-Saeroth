# Helmsman's Recourse  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 22

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Meteor Shield (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Meteor Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Duskwood Shield|standard-grade duskwood]] [[Equipment/Shields/Meteor Shield (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|meteor shield]] (Hardness 7, HP 28, BT 14) is a wheel from a ship. While wielding the shield, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Sailing Lore]] and to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim]].

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;  **Requirements** You're in a body of water; **Effect** For 10 minutes, you don't sink if you haven't succeeded at a Swim action on a turn, and if you're submerged, you automatically ascend 10 feet at the end of your turn.
