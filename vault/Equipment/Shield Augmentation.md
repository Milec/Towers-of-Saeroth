# Shield Augmentation  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Adjustment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 104

**Price** 8 sp

**Usage** applied to a shield

---

There are numerous methods to modify shields—snarling rods to catch weapons, bladed edges, padding for nonlethal strikes, and so on—but all share basic functionality. A shield augmentation can be etched with weapon runes, much like a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss|shield boss]] or [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]], but doesn't otherwise alter your shield's statistics. A shield bearing an augmentation can't be combined with an [[Traits/Attached|attached]] weapon, like shield spikes.

 A shield augmentation grants your shield one or more weapon traits, chosen when the augmentation is created. You can either choose to add the [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]] or [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]] trait, or you can choose two of the following weapon traits: [[Traits/Disarm|disarm]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Shove|shove]], [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 10 feet]], [[Traits/Trip|trip]], or [[Traits/Versatile|versatile S]].
