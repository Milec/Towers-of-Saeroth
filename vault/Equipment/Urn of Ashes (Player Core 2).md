# Urn of Ashes  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 307

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This pewter urn contains the ashes of a benevolent ancestor, with a sliver of lingering spirit that strives to protect you.

**Activate—Doom the Urn** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You would become [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], or your doomed value would increase; **Requirements** The ashes aren't doomed;; **Effect** The ashes in the urn intervene, taking the doomed condition in your place, and you don't gain or increase the value of your doomed condition. Each night when you get a full night's rest, you can reduce your own doomed condition or that of the urn, but not both.

**Activate—Spirit's Wrath** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** The urn shoots a bolt of void energy at a foe within 30 feet. Attempt a spell attack roll against the target's AC, using a modifier of +15 or your own spell attack modifier, whichever is higher. On a success, the bolt deals 4d4 void damage (doubled on a critical success).
