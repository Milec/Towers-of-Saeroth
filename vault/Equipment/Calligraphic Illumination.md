# Calligraphic Illumination  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 229

**Price** 135 gp

**Bulk** L

---

An ever-shifting series of calligraphic runes scrawls across the pages of this leather-bound book, as if written by an unseen hand. _Calligraphic illuminations_ are sacred to the faith of [[Setting/Deities/Irez (Divine Mysteries)|Irez]], Lady of Inscribed Wonder.

**Activate—Inscribed Insight** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your last action was to [Cast a prepared Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) that creates or manipulates written text or runes, such as _runic body_, the writing briefly forms into words conveying valuable information. Attempt to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about any one creature or object that you can see with a +2 item bonus to the check.
