# Dream Pollen Snare  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 79

**Price** 12 gp

---

This simple trap is made of taut twigs that, when triggered, snap down on a pollen-filled sac of a peculiar flower. When inhaled, the pollen imparts a hazy state of profound agreeability. The first creature that enters the snare's space triggers the snare. The creature that triggered the trap must attempt a DC 18 Will save. [[Traits/Undead]] creatures and creatures that don't breathe are immune to dream pollen.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 1 for 1 round. For 1 minute, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly|friendly]] to all creatures to which it was [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful|helpful]] to all creatures to which it was friendly. If the creature was [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] to any creatures, it remains unfriendly or hostile to them. If the creature is an [[Traits/Animal|animal]], any creature to which it's friendly or helpful can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on it and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of it.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the duration of the agreeability is 1 hour instead of 1 minute.
