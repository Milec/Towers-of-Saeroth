# Anointed Waterskin  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 232

**Price** 600 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This waterskin coruscates with [[Traits/Holy|holy]] energy. After using any of the activations, the waterskin is empty, but slowly refills itself. It becomes full enough to use again at the next dawn.

**Activate—Blessed Bombard** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You throw the _anointed waterskin_ up to 60 feet. The water explodes out of it, with the effects of a DC 25 4th-rank _[[Spells/Holy Cascade (Player Core 2)|_holy cascade_]]_.

**Activate—Divine Decanting**  1 minute ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You decant the water, creating up to 10 vials of _[[Equipment/Holy Water (GM Core)|holy water]]_. You must provide the vials. Holy water created this clearly temporary upon inspection, and fades away after 8 hours.

**Activate—Sacred Sip** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _anointed waterskin_ is full; **Effect** You drink the water within the waterskin, granting you the effects of _[[Spells/Bless (Player Core)|_bless_]]_. As normal, you can increase the spell’s radius by [[Actions/Sustain|Sustaining]] it.
