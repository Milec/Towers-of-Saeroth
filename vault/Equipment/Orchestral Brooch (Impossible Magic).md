# Orchestral Brooch  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 224

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** You attempt a Performance check, but you haven’t rolled yet; **Requirements** You’re a master in Performance.

---

This silver brooch reverberates lightly with the sound of music every time anyone touches it. You can transform it into the shape of any chosen instrument when you [[Actions/Affix a Talisman (GM Core)|Affix]] it. When you activate this talisman, your performance is accompanied by a grand procession of music that complements your own work, subject to your direction and intent. You receive a +1 status bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check. If you roll a success, you get a critical success instead. After being used, the talisman remains as a mundane silver brooch with the appearance of the chosen instrument, though it possesses no remaining power.
