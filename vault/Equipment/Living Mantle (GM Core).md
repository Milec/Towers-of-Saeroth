# Living Mantle  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused (GM Core)|Focused]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 292

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

The base of this cloak is a thick layer of moss, but it slowly picks up native plants from each area it spends time in. You gain a +2 item bonus to Nature checks. You also suffer no effects from severe cold and severe heat.

**Activate—Druidic Secrets** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast an order spell. If you don't spend this Focus Point by the end of this turn, it is lost.

**Craft Requirements** You are a druid.
