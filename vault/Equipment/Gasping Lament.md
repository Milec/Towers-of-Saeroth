# Gasping Lament  *Item 12+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Coda (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Coda]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -205- Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man|Pathfinder #205: Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man]] pg. 85

**Usage** worn collar

---

This collar is a silken cord from which hangs a small silver amulet that bears the likeness of a woman with her hand at her throat. A _gasping lament_ is a powerful coda instrument that’s worn rather than held. While you sing, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] and to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. Full rules for coda instruments appear [[Rules/Coda Instruments (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|here]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the collar to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
