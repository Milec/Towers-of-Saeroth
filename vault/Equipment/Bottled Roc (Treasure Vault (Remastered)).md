# Bottled Roc  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 56

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This bottle contains a shrunken bird preserved with its feathers intact. When opened, the contents reconstitute into a Gargantuan effigy of a great [[Bestiary/Roc|roc]], which can appear in the air instead of on the ground. The roc [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab|Grabs]] up to two creatures, then [[Actions/Fly|Flies]] up to 90 feet and [[Actions/Release|Releases]] the creatures. The creatures must be within 15 feet of the roc for it to Grab them; if any of them are unwilling to be [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], the roc must [[Actions/Grapple]] them with a +17 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier or fail to pick them up.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a roc.
