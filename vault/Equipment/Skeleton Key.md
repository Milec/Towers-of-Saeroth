# Skeleton Key  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 575

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

A grinning skull tops the bow of this macabre key. This key can be used in place of [[Equipment/Thieves' Tools|thieves’ tools]] when attempting to Pick a Lock, and it grants a +1 item bonus to the Thievery check. If the _skeleton key_ becomes broken due to a critical failure on the check, it works as normal thieves’ tools and loses its benefits until repaired.

**Activate** free action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt to Pick a Lock but haven’t rolled yet;  **Effect** The key casts [[Spells/Knock|_knock_]] on the lock you’re trying to pick.
