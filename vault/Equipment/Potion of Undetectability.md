# Potion of Undetectability  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 563

**Price** 4,400 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Drinking this dull-black liquid makes you undetectable to divinations. This grants the same effects as [[Spells/Mind Blank|_mind blank_]], but without the bonus against mental effects. You also gain the effects of a 4th-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell, which protects against [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]] spells of 8th level and lower and has a DC of 36 against [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]]. The potion’s effects last for 10 minutes.
