# Purple Pepper Powder  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Processed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Processed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 81

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This nearly black purple powder is made from the extremely spicy Kuthite’s kiss pepper and is a favorite among the Sklar-Quah. When you eat a meal flavored with this purple pepper powder, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] and resistance 5 to [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] for 1 hour.
