**Price** 1 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A griffon cane is named for the shape of its base, which features four small pronged supports splayed out in a manner similar to a griffon's talons. A griffon cane's splayed foot enables it to stand upright by itself. A griffon cane deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage, has the [[Traits/Backswing|backswing]] and [[Traits/Two-Hand|two-hand d10]] traits, and is a martial melee weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Dart|club]] weapon group.
