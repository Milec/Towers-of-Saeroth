# Merciful  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 33

**Price** 70 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

_Merciful_ weapons are sheathed in an unmistakable wispy green aura recognized by both gladiators and guards around the world. A merciful weapon has the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait and can't be used to make a lethal attack. Any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] the weapon would deal is negated.

 On a critical hit, a _merciful_ weapon causes the target to become [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] with the weapon's wielder for 1 minute, as the target is bombarded with feelings of guilt and remorse. This is a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect. If you have access to the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]], you choose whether to use it or the merciful effect.
