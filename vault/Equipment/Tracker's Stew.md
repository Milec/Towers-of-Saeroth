# Tracker's Stew  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Processed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Processed]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 51

**Price** 10 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

10 minutes (Interact)

---

Alchemical reagents add punch to tracker’s stew: a rich, fiery mixture of tomatoes, ground nuts, onions, and tubers, often with poultry added. It’s usually served with or over rice or noodles. Once you’ve eaten the stew, it improves your ability to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|sense]] and follow tracks for 24 hours or until you make your next daily preparations, whichever comes first. You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Cover Tracks (Player Core)|Cover Tracks]] and [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]]. You can do either while moving at full speed or both while moving at half speed.
