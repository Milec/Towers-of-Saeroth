# Preserved Alecost  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 216

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

A faint scent of sweetness and bitterness wafts from this dried, preserved herb. When used as a catalyst to cast the _[[Spells/Create Food (Player Core)|_create food_]]_ spell, the created food becomes flavorful and pleasing. Anyone who eats this food gains 1 temporary Hit Point per rank of the spell that lasts for 1 hour.
