# Reforging Shield  *Item 15*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -148- Fires of the Haunted City|Pathfinder #148: Fires of the Haunted City]] pg. 76

**Price** 6,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|steel shield]] contains a smoldering core of magma that never cools. Whenever the _reforging shield’s_ current Hit Points are below its Break Threshold, the shield recovers 5 Hit Points at the start of its wielder’s turn each round until it is no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]]. Each time a character [[Actions/Repair|Repairs]] the shield, the shield recovers double the number of Hit Points. The shield has Hardness 15, HP 120, and BT 60.
