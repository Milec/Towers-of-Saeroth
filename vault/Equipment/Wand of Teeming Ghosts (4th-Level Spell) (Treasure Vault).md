# Wand of Teeming Ghosts  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Wand]], [[Traits/Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 142

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This pale wooden wand is carved to resemble a thigh bone with metal caps at each end. Ghostly tendrils seem to swirl around it every so often.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/False Life|_false life_]] of the indicated level. During the duration of the spell, wisps that resemble spirits flit around you as long as you have any [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] from _false life_, and you can use the following action.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You successfully impart the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 condition on a creature; **Requirements** You have at least 1 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Point]] from _false life_; **Effect** You end _false life_ and increase the creature’s frightened condition value to 2.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/False Life|_false life_]] of the appropriate level.
