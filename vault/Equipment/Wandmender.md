---

Nearly every surface of this heavy _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace (Player Core)|mace]]_ is inscribed with runes of crafting and restoration. The core of the mace hums with an eerie resonance, as if devouring stray magical energies from the air.

**Activate—Recharge Wand** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re wielding a wand capable of casting spells of 3rd rank or lower, and its spell has been cast for the day; **Effect** You tap the _wandmender_ against the wand, restoring its magic. You can [Cast the Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) in the wand a second time without overcharging, as long as you do so within the next minute.
