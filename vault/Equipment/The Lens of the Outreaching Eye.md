# The Lens of the Outreaching Eye  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Scrying]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 71

**Price** 1 sp

**Usage** worn necklace

**Bulk** L

---

As long as you have the _Lens of the Outreaching Eye_ invested, the Harrowed Realm's impediments to magic don't apply to you, and you gain a +3 item bonus on all checks made to resolve [[Traits/Divination|divination]] effects.

**Activate**  10 minutes (command, envision, Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** By peering through the _Lens of the Outreaching Eye_, for one hour you can treat the _[[Equipment/Deck of Harrowed Tales]]_ as the source of your hearing and vision, no matter the deck's location in the multiverse. You can see in all directions from the deck and hear from it as if using your normal visual senses. You can Dismiss the activation.

**Activate**  10 minutes (command, envision, Interact);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** _The Lens of the Outreaching Eye_ casts [[Spells/Discern Location|_discern location_]] on the _Deck of Harrowed Tales_.

**Destruction** The _Lens of the Outreaching Eye_ is destroyed if the _Deck of Harrowed Tales_ is destroyed.
