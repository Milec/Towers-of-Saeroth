# Book of Warding Prayers  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Grimoire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 111

**Price** 425 gp

**Bulk** L

---

Script in a language of the Outer Planes adorns this book's spine, and a deity's symbol is etched on its cover. _Books of warding prayers_ are each devoted to a particular deity and are also a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of that deity.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your last action was to cast a prepared divine spell granted by your deity;  **Effect** You and all your allies in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage for 1 minute. You can instead choose to grant resistance to holy effects instead of spirit damage if the spell you cast had the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait, or resistance to [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] effects instead of spirit if your spell had the unholy trait.
