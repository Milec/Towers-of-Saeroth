# Cultist Cowl  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Seven Dooms for Sandpoint

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 181

**Usage** worn cloak

---

The fabric of a _cultist cowl_ is either raspy burlap, durable cotton, or fine silk, depending on the item's power (and thus the implied import of the worshipper who wears it). When a character who worships a single deity dons a cultist cowl, the cowl's colors change to match those sacred to that deity, and the deity's symbol or rune appears on the lower front of the cowl that hangs just over the chest. These colors and symbols remain until a different worshipper wears the cowl. While worn, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] checks. You also gain a +1 item bonus to all skill checks attempted to aid a ritual by being a secondary caster.

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -200- Seven Dooms for Sandpoint|Pathfinder #200: Seven Dooms for Sandpoint]] pg. 181

**Price** 650 gp

The cowl grants a +2 item bonus, and this bonus also applies to checks attempted as a primary caster to perform a ritual. The cowl can also be activated.

**Activate** two actions envision, command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The cowl casts [[Spells/Crisis of Faith (Player Core)|_crisis of faith_]] (DC 25 Will) to your specification.

## Cultist Cowl (Major)  *Item 17*

**Activate** two actions envision, command;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The cowl casts [[Spells/Crisis of Faith (Player Core)|_crisis of faith_]] (DC 37 Will) heightened to 7th-level to your specification.
