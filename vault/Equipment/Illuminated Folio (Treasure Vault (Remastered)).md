# Illuminated Folio  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 112

**Price** 4,200 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The pages of this part-spellbook, part-bestiary are illustrated with all manner of creatures, magical and mundane. The illustrations shift and move around the page when examined.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]])    **Effect** If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Summon|summon]] spell prepared from this grimoire, you summon creatures from the illuminated folio rather than their usual source. These summoned creatures appear as living illustrations, granting them [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to physical damage equal to half their level and weakness 5 to fire and to any ability with the water trait. They can also fold themselves up to pass through spaces only an inch or so wide as part of their movement.
