# Exsanguinating Ammunition  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 170

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This ammunition includes a small reservoir of a tacky red substance that coats the ammunition when you activate it. The substance makes a creature bleed more freely. For 1 minute after you deal damage to a creature with an activated exsanguinating ammunition that creature gains the listed weakness to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. In addition, the DC of any [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat checks]] to end persistent bleed damage increases from 15 to 17 (from 10 to 12 when receiving particularly effective assistance) for the duration.
