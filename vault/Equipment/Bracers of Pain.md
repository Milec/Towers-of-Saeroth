# Bracers of Pain  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -209- Destroyer's Doom|Pathfinder #209: Destroyer's Doom]] pg. 83

**Price** 2,200 gp

**Usage** worn bracers

**Bulk** L

---

These simple bracers look plain on the exterior, but a series of small, sharp studs line the interior like rows of shark teeth. While these bracers are worn and [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]], you gain a +2 item bonus to Will saves.

**Activate—Sharp Focus** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You gain an effect that makes you [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]];  **Effect** Your bracers snap tight onto your wrists, driving the studs into your skin to shock you into focus. You can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the effect causing your condition, with a counteract rank of 6th and a counteract modifier of +22. On a success, you lose the condition. If you have more than one condition from the same source, you only need one counteract check against them.
