# Assassin Vine Wine  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 47

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

10 minutes (Interact)

---

Crafting wine from [[Bestiary/Assassin Vine|assassin vine]] berries is a dangerous process, but the resulting ruby-red drink is rich and heady. Alchemical reagents added during fermentation concentrate the vine’s connection to its environment and impart them onto the imbiber. For 1 minute after drinking a glass of assassin vine wine, you have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] at a range of 30 feet.

## Assassin Vine Wine (Aged)  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 47

**Price** 350 gp

---

Assassin vine wine that has been aged for several years— or artificially aged with alchemy—develops both a greater complexity of flavor and a stronger connection to an assassin vine’s bond with nature. When you drink the wine, vegetation in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you begins to writhe, becoming [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] until the start of your next turn.
