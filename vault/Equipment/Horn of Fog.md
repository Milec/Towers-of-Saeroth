# Horn of Fog  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 573

**Price** 230 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This large ram’s horn perpetually glistens with tiny droplets of water, much like condensation.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You activate the horn by blowing deeply into it, causing it to issue forth a low blast and cast a 2nd-level [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]] spell. You can Dismiss the mist at any time by blowing a second note on the horn using an Interact action.
