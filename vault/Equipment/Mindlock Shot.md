# Mindlock Shot  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 81

**Price** 250 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

two actions

command, envision

---

_Mindlock shot_ is made of luminous ectoplasm in a crystalline form. When you command this ammunition, you pick a [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] or [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If you hit a creature with the ammunition, it falls under a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect that makes it use its first action on its next turn to take the action you picked. It chooses exactly how to use that action, and if you choose Stride, the target can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] if doing so is beneficial for it. On a critical hit, the target must use its next 2 actions to do as you chose, within the same parameters.
