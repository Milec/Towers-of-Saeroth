# Staff of Control  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Staff (GM Core)|Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 279

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

An array of dazzling gemstones lines the twisting head of the golden staff. While wielding the staff, you gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to make a Request.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
