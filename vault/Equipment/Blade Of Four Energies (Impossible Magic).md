# Blade Of Four Energies  *Item 13+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 208

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|Shortsword]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|shifting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|shortsword]]_ is formed of rapidly vibrating air and magical energy, though it uses the same statistics as iron. The wooden hilt is adorned with four gems, representing the energies of [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], and [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]].

**Activate—Cycle Energy** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round;   **Effect** You draw magical energy from one of the gems. Roll 1d4. Until the start of your next turn, the weapon gains the effects of the corresponding property rune: 1—_corrosive_, 2—_flaming_, 3—_frost_, or 4—_shock_.

**Activate—Release Energy** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The blade has an energy rune from Cycle Energy; **Effect** You call upon the energy stored in the blade to explode outward. You cast a 5th-rank spell that depends on the active rune: [[Spells/Acid Grip|_acid grip_]] for _corrosive_, _[[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]]_ for _flaming_, 2-action _[[Spells/Howling Blizzard|_howling blizzard_]]_ for _frost_, or _[[Spells/Lightning Bolt (Player Core)|_lightning bolt_]]_ for _shock_, with a DC of 30.

## Greater Blade Of Four Energies  *Item 18*

The blade is a _+3 greater striking shifting shortsword_ and gains the effects of the _greater corrosive_, _greater flaming_, _greater frost_, or _greater shock_ runes instead of their base versions. When you use the blade to [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Release]] Energy, you Cast the Spell heightened to 7th rank, and the DC is 38.
