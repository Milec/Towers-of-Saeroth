# Skitter Knot  *Item 6+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -201- Pactbreaker|Pathfinder #201: Pactbreaker]] pg. 81

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You are [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] at the beginning of your turn.

---

Carved from an [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal (Monster Core)|arboreal’s]] knuckle, this wooden torus can be activated despite your being unconscious. The knot then sprouts several roots that arch over your body like a spider’s legs. These lift you a few inches off the ground before [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Striding]] and carrying you with them. The roots prioritize taking you away from obvious harm, though as a reaction, an ally who speaks [[Setting/Languages/Arboreal]] can command the roots to carry you to a particular point within range.

After moving, the roots continue protecting you; this counts as [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raising a Shield]], giving you a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the beginning of your next turn. The roots use the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction against the first physical attack against you, after which the roots crumble.
