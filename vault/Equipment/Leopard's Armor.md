# Leopard's Armor  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 76

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail]]

---

Made of cobalt-colored steel rings that flow down the entire length of the garment, this _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Mail|chain mail]]_ includes an armored face covering. The armor is said to be based on the same armor once worn by Azure Leopard, one of Old-Mage Jatembe's magic warriors.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call upon the ferocity and grace of the leopard. The armor's rings flatten out to become a solid piece of flexible armor. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]], but take a –2 penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] checks. This lasts for 1 hour, but you can Activate the armor again to revert it back to its original form.
