# Dispelling Sliver  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 566

**Price** 2,400 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** Your Strike damages a target

**Requirement** You're a master with the affixed weapon

---

Made from a treated sliver of cold iron, this talisman allows you to counteract magical effects. When you activate the _dispelling sliver_, it attempts to counteract a single spell active on the target, with the effects of an 8th-level [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] spell (counteract modifier +29)

 If you activate the talisman on a successful [[Feats/Dispelling Slice]], the talisman attempts to counteract all spells active on the target.
