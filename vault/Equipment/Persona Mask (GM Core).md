# Persona Mask  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293

**Usage** worn mask

---

Despite covering the entire face, this alabaster mask does not hinder vision or other senses. Wearing the mask grants a +1 item bonus to Performance checks while acting, orating, performing comedy, or singing.

**Activate—Assume Role** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])    **Effect** You change the mask's appearance into an artistic rendition of a dramatic character of your choice.

## Persona Mask  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 613

## Persona Mask (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 293, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 613

**Price** 650 gp

---

The mask grants a +2 bonus and gains another activation.

**Activate—Sacrifice Role** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]])
 **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail a Performance check that benefits from the mask's bonus;  **Effect** You change the mask's character and reroll the Performance check, using the second result.
