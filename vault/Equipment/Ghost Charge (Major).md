# Ghost Charge  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb (Player Core)|Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|Splash]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 285

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

These spring-loaded metal canisters contain a mixture of chemicals and salts that drain and disintegrate nearby undead creatures. A ghost charge deals the listed vitality damage and splash damage, though as usual for [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage, this damage harms only undead and creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]]. Ghost charges are designed to explode even on contact with a spiritual substance, making them ideal for damaging [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] undead. A primary target that takes damage from a ghost charge becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] until the start of your next turn. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
