# Scizore of the Crab  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 36

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn on 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scizore (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Scizore]]

---

A _scizore of the crab_ is a _+1 [[Equipment/Weapons/Scizore (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|scizore]]_ that has the [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]] trait in addition to its normal weapon traits.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike with this weapon, or you have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] with this weapon; **Effect** The target is grabbed until the end of your next turn. A grabbed creature can use the [[Actions/Escape]] action to get out of the Grab, and the Grab ends for a grabbed creature if you move away from it. This functions as the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] creature ability.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round;  **Requirements** You have a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] with this weapon; **Effect** The crab claw animates and pinches open and shut, dealing slashing damage equal to the _scizore of the crab's_ number of damage dice.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a claw from a [[Bestiary/Giant Crab|giant crab]] or similar creature.
