# Trollhound Vest  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 19

**Price** 230 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor]]

---

This suit of _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor|hide armor]]_ is sickly green and covered in thick warts and nodules, fashioned from the hide of a [[Bestiary/Trollhound|trollhound]] and imbued with some of the beast's vitality. Wearing this armor gives you a –1 circumstance penalty to all checks made using [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to interact with [[Bestiary/Troll|trolls]] and a +1 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks used to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] in communities traditionally plagued by troll attacks.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You take damage from a melee attack while you have half or fewer of your normal maximum Hit Points;  **Effect** Your body knits itself back together, healing you for 3d8 Hit Points.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hides of at least two [[Bestiary/Trollhound|trollhounds]].
