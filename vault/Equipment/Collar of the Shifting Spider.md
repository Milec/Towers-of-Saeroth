# Collar of the Shifting Spider  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 63

**Price** 133 gp

**Usage** worn collar

**Bulk** 1

**Activate**

free action

Interact

---

This intimidating collar contains a hollow central tube and ends in twin metal points shaped like [[Bestiary/Families/Spider (Monster Core)|spider]] fangs. The collar can be filled with an alchemical mutagen as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action. As a free action triggered when you roll initiative, you can activate the collar, causing the metal points to dig into your neck, inflicting 1 piercing damage and injecting the mutagen directly into your bloodstream. This has the same effect as if you drank the mutagen conventionally, except the duration of the mutagen is halved due to the more direct administration.
