# Vulture's Wing  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Access** Characters from Geb have access to this item.

**Activate**

single action

Cast a Spell

---

This fan of vulture feathers scatters on an unnatural gust of wind. If this catalyst is used to cast a [[Spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|_ray of enfeeblement_]] spell that has been heightened to at least 5th level, instead of targeting a single creature, you cast the spell in a 30-foot-area cone. You don't make a spell attack roll, instead affecting all creatures within the area with the effect the spell would normally have on a hit. This means creatures within the area must attempt a Fortitude save to determine whether they're [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]].
