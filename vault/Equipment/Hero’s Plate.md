Golden images of heroic deeds decorate the black plates of this _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage and a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]].

**Activate—Personal Legend** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The scenes depicted on your armor shift and change, making room to accommodate your current heroic feats. You cast [[Spells/Heroism (Player Core)|_heroism_]] on yourself.
