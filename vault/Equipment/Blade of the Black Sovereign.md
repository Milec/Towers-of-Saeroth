# Blade of the Black Sovereign  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 76

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword]]

---

This _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Shock|shock]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword|greatsword]]_ is forged from [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|standard-grade adamantine]], with inlays of broken circuitry and a robot's ocular lens on the cross guard. A soft thrum comes from the blade, which you feel as a subtle pulse through the grip when wielding it. It sparks when unsheathed, emitting dim light within 5 feet. On a hit against a foe made of metal, wearing metal armor, or using a metal shield, if the foe takes electricity damage from the blade, the makes the foe [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round; if the Strike was a critical hit, it also makes them [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 round.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You cast the [[Spells/Electric Arc|_electric arc_]] cantrip from the sword as an 8th-level primal spell, using 10 + your melee attack modifier with the _Blade of the Black Sovereign_ as your spell DC.
