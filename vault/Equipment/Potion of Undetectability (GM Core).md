---

Drinking this dull-black liquid makes you undetectable to detection effects. This grants the same effects as hidden mind but without the bonus against mental effects. You also gain the effects of a 4th-rank [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell, which protects against see the unseen spells of 8th rank and lower and has a DC of 36 against [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]]. The potion's effects last for 10 minutes.
