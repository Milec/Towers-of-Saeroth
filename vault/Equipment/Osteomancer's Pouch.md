# Osteomancer's Pouch  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 77

**Price** 480 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This leather pouch holds a pair of six-sided dice carved from actual knucklebones. When rolled, the dice grant you a vision of a distant location, though their power is unreliable.

**Activate—Roll the Bones** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You roll the dice. If you roll two 1s, the activation fails with no effect. Otherwise, the dice cast [[Spells/Clairvoyance (Player Core)|_clairvoyance_]] for you. When cast in this way, the spell’s range becomes 100 feet × the result of your roll.
