Soothing powders are remedies made to stop a particular type of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]]. Each damage type requires a different formula, with the most popular being bleed soothing powder, [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] soothing powder, [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] soothing powder, and [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] soothing powder. You Activate soothing powder by sprinkling it on yourself or another creature within reach. The target can immediately attempt a new [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to remove persistent damage the powder works against. This powder lowers the DC to 10, as normal for a particularly appropriate type of help.
