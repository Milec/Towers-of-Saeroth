# Sparkler  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 54

**Price** 4 sp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Access** Sparklers are common in Tian Xia and Vudra and can be  Crafted  by those who can specifically Craft fireworks (such as a  fireworks technician ).

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

A sparkler gives off colorful sparks, burning for 1 minute. It provides [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 10-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] for the next 10 feet). While the sparkler burns, you can use it as an [[Rules/Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]], dealing 1 fire damage on a hit. On a critical hit, you cause the target to become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.

 You can touch a sparkler to a flammable object as part of the same Interact action you use to Activate it or as an Interact action while the sparkler is already activated. If you do so, the sparkler can ignite flammable objects the way a [[Equipment/Matchstick|matchstick]] does.
