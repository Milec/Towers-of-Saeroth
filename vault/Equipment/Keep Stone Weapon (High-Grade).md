# Keep Stone Weapon  *Item 18+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 113

**Usage** varies by weapon

**Bulk**  varies by weapon

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Keep Stone]]

---

As a material with defensive properties, keep stone isn't particularly prized for weapons, but it does offer a particular niche. When used with abilities that allow you to use a Strike to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] spells or magical effects, such as the [[Feats/Sunder Spell]] feat, the counteract check gains a +2 circumstance bonus. If the counteract check is tied to the Strike's result, add the bonus when calculating your counteract result, but not to the attack roll. Keep stone is treated as [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] for the purpose of overcoming resistances.
