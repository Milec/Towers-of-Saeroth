This _minor [[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|reinforcing]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]_ (Hardness 8, HP 64, BT 32) is lined with pale silver that glows when struck. Whenever you use the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction, this shield becomes energized for 1 round.

**Activate—Energized Blast** single action ([[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;  **Requirements** The shield is energized; **Effect** You direct the stored energy into a short blast, targeting a creature within 15 feet. The target takes 2d10 force damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
