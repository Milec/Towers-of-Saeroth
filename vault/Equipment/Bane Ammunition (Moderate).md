Monster hunters favor bane ammunition that contains a capsule of reagents tailored to a particular type of creature— [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberration]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]], [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Giant (Player Core)|giant]], [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|ooze]], or both [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]. Each type requires a different formula. When activated bane ammunition hits a target that has a trait matching the selected type, it takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] in addition to the damage the attack normally deals.
