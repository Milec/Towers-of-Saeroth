# Poison Fizz  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Lozenge (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Lozenge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 50

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Made from a special mixture of honey and alchemical reagents, poison fizz is a zesty, sweet rock candy that pops and crackles in your mouth. For 1 hour, you have an item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Poison|poison]] and being [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]].

 **Secondary Effect** two actions ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) **Effect** You bite the poison fizz to release its poisonous liquid center and spray green mist in a 15-foot cone. This deals poison damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the end of your next turn and is then temporarily immune to being blinded by poison fizz for 1 hour.
