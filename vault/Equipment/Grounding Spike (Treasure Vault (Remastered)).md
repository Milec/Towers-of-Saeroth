# Grounding Spike  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 34

**Price** 950 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dancer's Spear (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Dancer's Spear]]

---

Metal caps the bottom of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dancer's Spear (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|dancer's spear]]_ and its point gives off the faint smell of ozone. If you hit a target that has been struck by a _[[Equipment/Polarizing Mace (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|polarizing mace]]_ within the last round, you deal additional electricity damage to the target equal to the number of _grounding spike's_ damage dice. If you critically hit such a target, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.

 **Special** The _grounding spike_ pairs with the _polarizing mace_.
