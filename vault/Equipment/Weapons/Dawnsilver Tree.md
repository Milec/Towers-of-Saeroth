Neither [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]] nor a tree, this long gun takes its name from the legends of the [[Ancestries/Elf (Player Core)|elves]] of Jinin and is most commonly found within the nation. An elegant weapon, a dawnsilver tree does somewhat resemble a tree; its fanned stock and long, sweeping barrel reinforced with metal rings enable a wielder to parry melee attacks while moving back into safe firing range.
