**Type** Melee

**Category** Advanced

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail]]

---

A fusion of a [naginata](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=98) and [[Equipment/Weapons/Sansetsukon (Tian Xia Character Guide)|sansetsukon]], this three-section weapon has a sweeping, curved blade along each of the outer sections. Though difficult for anyone but an expert weapon master to use effectively, the three-section naginata can be wielded at devastating speed to slice or smash apart a foe.
