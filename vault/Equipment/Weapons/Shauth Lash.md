This metal chain bears hook-like barbs made of alchemically strengthened [[Bestiary/Families/Urdefhan|urdefhan]] teeth. Magical shauth lashes have the same ability to channel an urdefhan wielder's Wicked Bite as magical [[Equipment/Weapons/Shauth Blade|shauth blades]], and urdefhans hold these weapons with the same reverence as they do shauth blades.
