Favored by caravan guards who traverse the Mana Wastes, a piercing wind is similar to a [[Equipment/Weapons/Jezail (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|jezail]], in that you can carry it in one hand as long as the other hand's free, by holding it under one arm. Additionally, it's fitted with an underslung curved blade.
