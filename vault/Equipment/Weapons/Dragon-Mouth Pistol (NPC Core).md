Similar to the [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|blunderbuss]], a dragon-mouth pistol fires pellets from a flared barrel. Though less powerful than a blunderbuss, the dragonmouth pistol is appreciated for its portability and onehanded design. The name was coined because of the destructive belch of this handheld scatter weapon, but many gunsmiths craft dragon-mouth pistols with elaborate embellishments that resemble a stylized dragon's maw framing the barrel.
