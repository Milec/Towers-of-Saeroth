**Type** Melee

**Category** Martial

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword]]

---

This umbrella's ferrule is a hollow-tipped blade three to four inches in length and often overlooked as decorative. A receptacle inside the umbrella's shaft can be loaded with a single dose of [[Traits/Injury|injury]] [[Traits/Poison|poison]] and injected into a damaged target with the pull of a sliding trigger. Reinforced ribs enable you to parry and deflect blows with the umbrella's tear-resistant canopy.
