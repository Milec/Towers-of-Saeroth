# Wheel Blades  *Weapon 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Agile]], [[Traits/Attached]], [[Traits/Free-Hand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 72

**Price** 5 sp

**Damage** 1d4 S

**Bulk** —

**Hands** 1

**Type** Melee

**Category** Simple

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword]]

---

A set of large blades have been attached to your chair's wheels. While in the chair, you gain a wheel blade Strike. Your wheel blade deals 1d4 slashing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Attached|attached]], and [[Traits/Free-Hand|free-hand]] traits. A wheel blade is a simple melee weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]] weapon group. You can etch weapon runes onto wheel blades. A wheelchair can only have one attached weapon.
