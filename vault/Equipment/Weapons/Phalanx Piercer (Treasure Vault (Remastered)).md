This massive bow is made from bone or wood reinforced with flexible metal strips and strung with reinforced cord. Designed by [[Rules/Sidebars/Cantorian Spring|hobgoblin]] engineers to take down shielded opponents, the phalanx piercer fires heavy, iron-shod bolts.
