**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 115

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This weapon is an Advanced melee weapon_

**Price** 6 gp

**Damage** 1d4 P

**Bulk** 1

**Hands** 1

**Type** Melee

**Category** Advanced

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail]]

---

This weapon consists of a three-pronged barbed hook attached to a length of chain. It can be used as a melee weapon to stab at foes or hurled as a ranged weapon. Some [[Rules/Sidebars/Kobold Origins|kobolds]] are particularly fond of using flying talons, especially as a sort of badge of office above those who serve them.
