The sansetsukon, also known as a sanjiegun or three-section staff, is made up of three wooden staff segments, each about 14 inches in length. The staff sections are connected by short lengths of cord or chain, similar to [[Equipment/Weapons/Nunchaku (Tian Xia Character Guide)|nunchaku]].
