**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 80

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from the High Seas have access to the combat grapnel_

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society on a mission for the Arcadian Mariner’s Lodge.

**Price** 9 sp

**Damage** 1d6 P

**Bulk** 1

**Hands** 2

**Type** Melee

**Category** Martial

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail]]

---

Although [[Equipment/Grappling Hook|grappling hooks]] typically serve as a climbing tool, this specially reinforced grapnel attached to a rope up to 10 feet long can be swung as a weapon. This is a martial melee weapon.
