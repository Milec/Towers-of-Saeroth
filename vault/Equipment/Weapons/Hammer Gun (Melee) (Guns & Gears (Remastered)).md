This weapon, favored by [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarves]] and those who like smashing and shooting, takes the form of a stoutly built gun designed similarly to an [arquebus](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=195) with a hammer head built into the muzzle, decreasing kickback but limiting range.
