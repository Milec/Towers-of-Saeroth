**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword]]

---

This fan-shaped sword, designed by members of the [[Rules/Sidebars/Tengu Settlements|tengu]] ancestry, has five broad blades that join at its hilt. Tengu sailors also use gale blades as makeshift weather vanes, as the sword spins to show the wind's direction when loosely held aloft.
