This weapon, favored by [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|dwarves]] and those who like smashing and shooting, takes the form of a stoutly built gun designed similarly to an [[Equipment/Weapons/Arquebus (NPC Core)|arquebus]] with a hammer head built into the muzzle, decreasing [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] but limiting range.
