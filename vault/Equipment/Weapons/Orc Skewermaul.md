As [[Ancestries/Dwarf (Player Core)|dwarves]] poured out of [[Rules/The Darklands|the Darklands]] following their Quest for Sky, the [[Ancestries/Orc (Player Core)|orcs]] driven before them adapted to their tactics—including dwarves’ heavily armored infantry charges. Surviving orcs developed a response: a weapon that combines a [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spear]] and [[Equipment/Weapons/Maul (Player Core)|maul]] to crush heavy armor and skewer charging foes. Although rarely seen today, the weapon is remarkably effective in the right situation.
