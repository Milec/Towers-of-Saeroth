**Bulk** L

**Type** Melee

**Category** Simple

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear]]

---

Developed by Alkenstar scouts who often don't have time to break down a tripod when beset by the chaotic mutant monsters of the Mana Wastes, this two-pronged stabbing weapon can be used as a bipod to stabilize a gun with potent kickback. A forked bipod can be deployed or retrieved for use as a melee weapon as an Interact action. As with a [[Equipment/Tripod (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|tripod]], you can change your grip on the weapon as part of this action.
