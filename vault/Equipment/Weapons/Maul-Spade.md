**Bulk** 2

**Hands** 2

**Type** Melee

**Category** Advanced

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club]]

---

This heavy, club-like weapon can clobber foes, but it also functions as a shovel. A maul spade adds its item bonus from weapon potency runes (if  any) as an item bonus on checks made with [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] and appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skills (such as Labor Lore) when using it as a shovel to dig.
