---

This unusual combination weapon integrates features of a [flintlock pistol](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=192) and a [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword|shortsword]]. Like other [[Traits/Combination|combination]] weapons, a wielder can transform it between ranged and melee modes as an Interact action.
