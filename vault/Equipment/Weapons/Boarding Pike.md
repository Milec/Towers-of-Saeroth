# Boarding Pike  *Weapon 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Reach]], [[Traits/Shove]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 80

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from the High Seas have access to the boarding pike_

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society on a mission for the Arcadian Mariner’s Lodge.

**Price** 8 sp

**Damage** 1d10 P

**Bulk** 2

**Hands** 2

**Type** Melee

**Category** Martial

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm]]

---

Taking the form of a [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear|longspear]] fitted with crossbars or hooks, a boarding pike provides its wielder a sharp implement that's as adept at shoving enemies off a ship's railings as facilitating the boarding of other vessels. This is a martial melee weapon.
