A recently developed weapon created for an [[Ancestries/Elf|elven]] champion from Jinin, the three-peaked tree can be used as both a [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|trident]] and a [[Equipment/Weapons/Dawnsilver Tree|dawnsilver tree]]. A three-peaked tree has a length of silken rope attached to the butt of its haft, allowing it to be quickly retrieved after thrown.
