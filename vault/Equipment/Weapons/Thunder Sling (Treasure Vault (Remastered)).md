[[Rules/Sidebars/Tengu Settlements|Tengu]] use these specialized slings to fire darts further and with greater force than when thrown by hand. A thunder sling uses darts as ammunition. It can also hurl [[Equipment/Weapons/Blowgun Darts|blowgun darts]] as ammunition but deals 1d4 piercing damage instead of 1d6 when used this way.
