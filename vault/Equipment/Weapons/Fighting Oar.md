A fighting oar is a sturdy boat oar, typically made of wood, whose haft and blades are reinforced for use in combat. A fighting oar adds its item bonus from weapon potency runes (if any) as an item bonus on Piloting [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] and Sailing Lore checks made to pilot a rowed vehicle (for more information on vehicles, see [[Rules/Vehicle Basics (GM Core)|here]]).

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 283
