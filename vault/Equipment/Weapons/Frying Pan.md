The cast-iron frying pan is an essential tool for adventuring halflings, gold panners, and remote tavern owners. Characters with the [[Feats/Halfling Weapon Familiarity (Player Core)|Halfling Weapon Familiarity]] ancestry feat are trained in the frying pan.

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 283

Certain feats, class features, weapon runes, and other effects can grant you additional benefits when you make a Strike with certain weapons and get a critical success. This is called a critical specialization effect. The exact effect depends on which weapon group your weapon belongs to, as listed below.
