# Wheel Spikes  *Weapon 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Agile]], [[Traits/Attached]], [[Traits/Free-Hand]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 72

**Price** 5 sp

**Damage** 1d4 P

**Bulk** —

**Hands** 1

**Type** Melee

**Category** Simple

**Group** [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife]]

---

A set of thick, sharp spikes have been added to the chair's wheels, granting you a piercing attack. While in the chair, you gain a wheel spike Strike. Your wheel spike deals 1d4 piercing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Attached|attached]], and [[Traits/Free-Hand|free-hand]] traits. A wheel spike is a simple melee weapon in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]] weapon group. You can etch weapon runes onto wheel spikes. A wheelchair can only have one attached weapon
