# Whip-Tongue Sling  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 104

**Price** 1,250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling (Player Core)|Sling]]

---

This is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling (Player Core)|sling]]_ made from the neck, jaw, and tongue of a [[Bestiary/Mobogo|mobogo]] to resemble its mouth. The tongue curls around each bullet, which is thrown with the snap of your wrist. Each throw causes a small croak to echo from the sling.

**Activate—Bolstering Croak** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You raise your sling and work the handle to mimic a mobogo’s croaking song. Allies within 25 feet gain a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects for 1 round. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this for up to 4 rounds. During this time, you can’t use the sling to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or activate its Tongue Twister ability. After using Bolstering Croak, you can recharge it once per day by feeding the sling a number of gallons of water equal to double the number of rounds for which the Bolstering Croak was active. For example, if you activated but didn’t sustain Bolstering Croak, you would require 2 gallons of water to recharge it. If you activated Bolstering Cloak and then Sustained for 1 round, you would require 4 gallons of water.

**Activate—Tongue Twister** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The sling isn’t loaded; **Effect** You fully unfurl the sling’s tongue to grab hold of a creature within 20 feet, and attempt to move them. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the creature’s Fortitude DC. On a success, you can move the creature up to 15 feet into any space within 20 feet of you. If you target a willing ally with this ability, you automatically succeed at the Athletics check.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the throat, tongue, and skin from a [[Bestiary/Mobogo|mobogo]].
