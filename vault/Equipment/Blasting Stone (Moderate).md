**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

When this pebble hits a creature or a hard surface, it explodes with a deafening bang. A blasting stone deals the listed sonic damage and sonic splash damage, and each creature within 10 feet of the space in which the stone exploded must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw with the listed DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] until the end of its next turn. Many types of blasting stones grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
