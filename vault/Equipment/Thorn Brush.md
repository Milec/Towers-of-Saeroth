This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|shifting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|morningstar]]_ has a head that appears to be a deep crimson metal rose with thorny petals. When you activate a _thorn brush_ to Shift Weapon, you can cause it to take the shape of a painter’s brush. While in this shape, it can’t be used as a weapon (it can still be activated to Shift Weapon back into a morningstar), but it does grant a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks to paint or otherwise use the tool while creating artwork.

**Activate—Splatter Paint** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You swing the weapon in an arc, causing a spray of paint of a color of your choice (chosen from black, blue, green, orange, white, or yellow) to splatter all creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature takes 14d6 energy damage based on the color of the paint ([[Traits/Void|void]] for black, [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] for blue, [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] for green, [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] for orange, [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] for white, or [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] for yellow) and must attempt a DC 34 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] by paint in its eyes for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] by paint in its eyes for 1 round and then dazzled for 1 minute.
