# Octopus Potion  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 178

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Eight flailing octopus arms covered in suckers pop out from the sides of your body when you imbibe this potion. The arms share your multiple attack penalty and attempt to [[Actions/Grapple]] a random enemy within 15 feet of you. On a success, roll 1d4 to determine an additional effect of the arms, which lasts as long as the target remains grabbed or restrained by the arms. On subsequent turns, you can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Attack|attack]] trait, to have the arms either Grapple a creature currently [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grappled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] (with no added effect) or release any creature they currently hold and repeat their initial effect. After 1 minute, the arms disappear and the potion's effects end.  

| **1d4** | **Added Effect** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The arms cover the eyes; the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]]. |
| 2 | The arms cover the mouth; the target can't speak. |
| 3 | The arms wrap around the limbs; the flat check for the target to use a [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] action while grabbed is DC 7 instead of DC 5. |
| 4 | The arms crush your enemy; the target takes bludgeoning damage equal to the potion's level immediately and at the end of each of its turns. |
