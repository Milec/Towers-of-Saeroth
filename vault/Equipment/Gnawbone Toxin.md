# Gnawbone Toxin  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Contact]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 69

**Price** 235 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

three actions

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Made from the ground bones of [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul (Monster Core)|ghouls]] or other cannibalistic creatures processed into a green-gray paste, gnawbone toxin imparts an insatiable desire to consume the flesh of intelligent creatures. If the victim eats at least a mouthful of humanoid flesh, it ignores the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition from gnawbone toxin for 1 minute. Victims under the effect of gnawbone toxin regain only half as many Hit Points from healing effects unless they’ve eaten at least a mouthful of humanoid flesh in the last minute.

**Saving Throw** DC 30 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Maximum Duration** 6 minutes; **Stage 1** enfeebled 2 (1 minute); **Stage 2** enfeebled 3 (1 minute); **Stage 3** enfeebled 4 (1d4 minutes)
