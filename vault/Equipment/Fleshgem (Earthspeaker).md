# Fleshgem  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 110

**Usage** envision

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|Fist]]

---

Originally developed as a body modification by [[Rules/Sidebars/Oread Settlements|oreads]], _fleshgems_ are crystals that can be implanted in the skin of a creature of any ancestry. While a _fleshgem_ can be applied anywhere on the body for cosmetic purposes, the most common usage among adventurers is to implant them at the base of the fingers, to be used like brass knuckles.

## Fleshgem (Combat)  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 110

**Price** 160 gp

---

These pointed crystal shards embedded in your fingers grant you a [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist|fist]] Strike that deals 1d6 piercing damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and has the [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits. _Combat fleshgems_ can be enhanced with the effects of [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows|_handwraps of mighty blows_]], as normal for an unarmed attack.

## Fleshgem (Earthspeaker)  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 110

**Price** 450 gp

---

You siphon your fleshgem's power, and the earth around you erupts in crystal stalagmites.

**Activate** single action to two actions command;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are standing on the ground; **Effect** The ground around you erupts in a 10-foot burst of knee-high crystal shards that remain for 1 round, or 1 minute if you spent two actions. To all creatures other than you, the area is difficult terrain as well as [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]. Creatures that move through a space containing crystal shards take 2 piercing damage.
