# Perfected Robes  *Item 22*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 179

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

These unadorned white robes, fastened with simple brass pins in the shape of a human hand, can’t be soiled or blemished. While wearing perfected robes, you don’t need to eat, sleep, or drink, but you can if you choose to. The robes bless you with constant [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] (+32 counteract bonus). A creature who dons these robes without earning them is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 3, [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 3, and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 3 while wearing them, gaining the _truesight_ but otherwise unable to use the robes’ magic.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** If your next action is to attempt a d20 roll with which you have legendary proficiency, roll twice and take the better result. This is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Avatar|_avatar_]], gaining the abilities for [[Setting/Deities/Irori]].

**Destruction** If the wearer ever willingly turns from the path of self-perfection into corruption or overindulgence, their _perfected robes_ crumble to nothing.
