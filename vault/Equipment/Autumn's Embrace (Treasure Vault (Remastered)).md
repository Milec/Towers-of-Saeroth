# Autumn's Embrace  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 14

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Leaf Weave]]

---

Woven by [[Traits/Fey|fey]] seamstresses as rewards for servants of nature, countless leaves continually changing colors in autumnal hues comprise autumn's embrace, a suit of _+2 [[Equipment/Invisibility|invisibility]] [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leaf Weave (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|leaf weave]]_. Leaves shed from the armor as they might fall in autumn. When activating the armor's _invisibility_ property rune, you disappear in a swirl of colorful leaves.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call forth a storm of leaves from _autumn's embrace_. These leaves swirl in a 20-foot emanation for 1 minute. Creatures within the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and creatures outside the area are concealed to creatures within the leaves. However, you can see through this concealment. You can Dismiss the activation.
