# Dragonfly Fulu  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 77

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You take the [[Actions/Leap]] action.

---

Tradition says the _dragonfly fulu_ should be affixed to the upper back, like the wings of an insect. When you Activate this fulu, you gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump]] for 1 minute. During this time, you can attempt an Athletics check to High Jump or Long Jump as a single action without the Stride requirement. You can also High Jump or Long Jump from a nonsolid substance, such as air or water, but if you use this power of the fulu, its effects ends after you jump.
