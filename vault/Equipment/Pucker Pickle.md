Sharp and pungent, but tasty, pucker pickles were created by [[Ancestries/Goblin (Player Core)|goblin]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|alchemists]] working to avoid being eaten by larger creatures. For 1 hour after eating a pucker pickle, you smell slightly of pickle, but you have a horrendous taste. Once a creature hits you with a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] using an attack that allows it to taste you, such as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|jaws]] Strike, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to further attacks against you that allow it to taste you, including attacks like [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grappling]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Tripping]] you using its jaws or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallowing you Whole]]. Creatures, especially animals, often choose other targets after tasting you. Any creature that [[Bestiary/Abilities/Engulf (Monster Core)|Engulfs]] you or Swallows you Whole is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. If it spends an action retching to reduce the sickened condition, you can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as a reaction.
