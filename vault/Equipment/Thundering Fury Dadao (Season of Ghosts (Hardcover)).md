# Thundering Fury Dadao  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 297

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|Greatsword]]

---

This massive, curved blade roars when it crashes down on opponents, seemingly intent on subduing them with sheer force. It’s said materials for the first thundering fury dadaos came from the hide of Yorak, the Horned Thunder, a legendary kaiju that roams the Shanguang desert. The lack of embellishments on this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering (GM Core)|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|greatsword]]_ belies a weapon of deadly efficacy.

**Activate—Thunder Dance** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You infuse the _thundering fury dadao_ with the speed of lightning and the force of thunder. The weapon gains the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] and [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]] traits for one round. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this activation for up to 1 minute.
