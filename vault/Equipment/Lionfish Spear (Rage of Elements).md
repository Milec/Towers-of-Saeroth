# Lionfish Spear  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 177

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear]]

---

Colorful stripes and trailing ribbons give this _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Underwater|underwater]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]]_ an appearance like a poisonous lionfish. While holding the spear, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletic]] checks to swim.

**Activate—Lionfish Poison** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with the _lionfish spear_;  **Effect** The spear injects lionfish poison into the target; **Saving Throw** DC 28 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 5d6 poison damage and sickened 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 2d4 hours
