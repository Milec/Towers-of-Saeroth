# Thundering  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 239

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

A _thundering_ weapon lets out a peal of thunder when it hits, dealing an extra 1d6 sonic damage on a successful [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. On a critical hit, the target must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 minute (or 1 hour on a critical failure).  

## Thundering  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 238, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 585

**Price** 500 gp

## Thundering (Greater)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 238, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 585

**Price** 6,500 gp

---

The save DC is 34, and the deafness is permanent. Sonic damage dealt by this weapon ignores the target's sonic resistance.
