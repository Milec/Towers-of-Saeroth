This [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]] is fitted with mithral plates resembling battlements on a castle. Lesser cover, cover, and greater cover don't grant any bonus against your spell attacks or to saving throws against your spells that you cast from the staff.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.
