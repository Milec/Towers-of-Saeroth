# Perfected Robes  *Item 22*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 179

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

These unadorned white robes, fastened with simple brass pins in the shape of a human hand, can't be soiled or blemished. While wearing _perfected robes_, you don't need to eat, sleep, or drink, but you can if you choose to. The robes bless you with constant [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]] (+32 counteract bonus). A creature who dons these robes without earning them is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 3, [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 3, and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 3 while wearing them, gaining the _true seeing_ but otherwise unable to use the robes' magic.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** If your next action is to attempt a d20 roll with which you have legendary proficiency, roll twice and take the better result. This is a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Avatar (Player Core)|_avatar_]], gaining the abilities for [[Setting/Deities/Irori (Divine Mysteries)|Irori]].

**Destruction** If the wearer ever willingly turns from the path of self-perfection into corruption or overindulgence, their _perfected robes_ crumble to nothing.
