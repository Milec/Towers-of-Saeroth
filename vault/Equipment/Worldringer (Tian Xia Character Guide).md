This [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Khakkhara|khakkhara]] is topped by an ornate finial depiction of a small-statured traveler with animal companions. While the rings of a khakkhara are normally meant to alert others of one’s presence, the magic of the worldringer enhances the chimes to entreat upon those who hear it.

**Activate—Chimes of Command** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You call upon beings of this plane or another to hear your call. Choose [[Spells/Summon Lesser Servitor (Player Core 2)|_summon lesser servitor_]] or [[Spells/Command (Player Core)|_command_]]. You [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the chosen Spell]] at 1st rank (DC 19). When casting summon lesser servitor in this way, you must choose to summon a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]].
