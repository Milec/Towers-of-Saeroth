This fur-lined _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant (GM Core)|cold-resistant]] [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor (Player Core)|hide armor]]_ is favored by warriors in the Crown of the World. In addition to providing excellent protection from extreme elements, the armor also enables you to erect massive walls of ice.

**Activate—Endure** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast 5th-rank [[Spells/Environmental Endurance|_environmental endurance_]] on yourself.

**Activate—Freeze Enemy** three actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Wall of Ice (Player Core)|_wall of ice_]].

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Environmental Endurance|_environmental endurance_]] (5th rank) and [[Spells/Wall of Ice (Player Core)|_wall of ice_]].
