# Grappling Gun  *Item 0+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 64

---

This wooden, pistol-like device features a large reel coiled with 100 feet of thin metal cord and can fire a [[Equipment/Grappling Hook|grappling hook]] up to 100 feet. To reload the grappling gun, you must manually recoil the cord by turning the reel's crank, and then lock in the grappling hook. Reloading a grappling gun takes 1 minute.
