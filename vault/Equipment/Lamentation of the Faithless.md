# Lamentation of the Faithless  *Item 25*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 128

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|Greatsword]]

---

The weapon wielded in battle by [[Setting/Deities/Szuriel (Divine Mysteries)|Szuriel]], Rider of War, is a perpetually blood-covered blade that has taken more lives than even the gods can count. _Lamentation of the Faithless_ is a _[[Equipment/Mythic Weapon Potency|+4]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|major striking]] [[Equipment/Quickstrike|speed]] [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|unholy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|greatsword]]_ with a blade of unyielding jet-black steel that absorbs all incident light, [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteracting]] all light effects in a 15-foot radius with a +37 modifier as long as the weapon is unsheathed. Any creature within range attempting to use a spell or ability with the [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]] trait must first succeed at a DC 15 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]], or the attempt fails. Whenever the blade strikes and deals damage to a living creature, its wielder regains 1d12 Hit Points.

**Activate—Lo and Behold** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You raise _Lamentation of the Faithless_ high above the battlefield, presenting its terrible glory for all to witness. All creatures within 60 feet that are able to see the sword must attempt a DC 50 Will save. Creatures with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait are immune to this effect, but creatures with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait take a –2 status penalty on this save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is stricken by crushing despair. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as it sobs uncontrollably, and it can’t use reactions while it’s slowed.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the duration of the blinded condition is 1 minute.

**Destruction** _Lamentation of the Faithless_ can only be destroyed if it’s driven into the ground within the walls of the secluded garden at the summit of Heaven’s mountain by a risen, repentant [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], whereupon it shatters and crumbles to dust.
