# Mana-Rattler Liniment  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 77

**Price** 110 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

According to reports, as well as Emelett's own marketing pitches, this lotion is derived from rare mana rattlesnakes whose oils ward against poison and disease. When applied, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] or [[Traits/Disease|disease]] effect currently afflicting you. The potion has a counteract level of 5 and a +15 modifier for the roll. You also acquire a serpentine appearance, growing fangs and scaly skin. For 1 hour, you gain a fangs [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage plus 1 poison damage.
