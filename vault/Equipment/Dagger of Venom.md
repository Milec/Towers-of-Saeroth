# Dagger of Venom  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 600

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger]]

---

The serrated blade of this _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger|dagger]]_ has a greenish tinge, and the hilt is sculpted to look like the head of a serpent about to strike. When you critically succeed at an attack roll with the _dagger of venom_, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] unless it succeeds at a DC 19 Fortitude save. This is a [[Traits/Poison|poison]] effect. In addition, you can activate the dagger to poison a creature with a more potent poison.

**Activate** free action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You damage a creature with the _dagger of venom_;  **Effect** You poison the creature you hit with dagger venom.

 **Dagger Venom** ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]); **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 21; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds. **Stage 1** 1d8 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]].
