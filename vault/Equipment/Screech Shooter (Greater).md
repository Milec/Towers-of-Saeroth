# Screech Shooter  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 155

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Harmona Gun (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Harmona Gun]]

---

Built from the larynx of an [[Bestiary/Owlbear|owlbear]], [[Bestiary/Terror Shrike|terror shrike]], or similar animal that possesses a frightening screech or similar special ability, a screech shooter is a special _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Harmona Gun (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|harmona gun]]_ designed to fire terrifying blasts of sound. A _screech shooter_ deals sonic damage but can otherwise be used like a normal harmona gun.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The screech shooter unleashes a frightening wail. All creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from you must attempt a DC 25 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the larynx of a creature with a frightening screech.
