# Feral Linguist  *Item 1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

This wooden whistle is stuffed with cotton and carved with images of bellowing stags, howling wolves, and chirping birds. You can blow on this whistle to use it as a catalyst when casting an [[Spells/Animal Form (Player Core)|_animal form_]] spell. When you do, you gain the listed benefit for the duration of the spell.

## Feral Linguist  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Price** 3 gp

---

You retain the ability to speak, though this doesn’t allow you to cast spells.

## Greater Feral Linguist  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 78

**Price** 85 gp

---

You retain the ability to speak. Once during the spell’s duration, you can cast either a spell of 4th-rank or lower or a cantrip of any level, ignoring the inability to cast spells while in your battle form.
