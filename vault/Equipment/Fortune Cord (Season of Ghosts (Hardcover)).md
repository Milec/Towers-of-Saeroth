# Fortune Cord  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 302

**Price** 1,300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

In many parts of Tian Xia, it’s tradition to mint coins with holes in the centers for the purpose of being able to thread a cord through them and tie them off for security and ease of transaction. This immaculately braided red cord is made in this tradition and can hold up to 100 coins at any given time. As long as you carry a _fortune cord_, you gain a +2 item bonus to skill checks to [[Actions/Earn Income (Player Core)|Earn Income]].

If you load up to 100 coins onto a _fortune cord_ as part of your daily preparations, you can Activate the magic item in two different ways until the next time you make your daily preparations, provided all the loaded coins are of the same type.

**Activate—Merchant's Bludgeon** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You have at least 50 coins loaded onto the _fortune cord_; **Effect** You transform the fortune cord into a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Nunchaku (Tian Xia Character Guide)|nunchaku]]_ for 1 minute. The type of coin loaded onto the cord determines the additional weapon property rune that manifests on this _+2 striking nunchaku_, as follows.  
**Copper**: [[Equipment/Corrosive (GM Core)|_corrosive_]]  
**Silver**: [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|_shock_]]  
 **Gold**: [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|_flaming_]]  
**Platinum**: [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|_frost_]]

**Activate—Fling Coins** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You whip the _fortune cord_, causing 10 coins loaded onto it to detach and fire at a creature within 100 feet that you can see. That creature takes 4d6 damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save); the damage type depends on the type of coins you’ve loaded. Copper coins deal [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage, [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] coins deal electricity damage, gold coins deal [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage, and platinum coins deal [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage.
