# Sihedron Medallion  *Item 5+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 69

**Usage** worn

---

These amulets once served as tokens of loyalty between the runelords and their favored agents. Though they vary in appearance, they all share a common design—a leather or metallic cord attached to a metal disc inscribed with the seven-pointed star known as the Sihedron. For original _Sihedron medallions_ created before Earthfall, a runelord of old Thassilon (not a character with the runelord archetype) doesn’t reduce the DC of any [[Spells/Scrying (Player Core)|_scrying_]] spell they cast against a creature that wears a Sihedron medallion even if they have never met the target or are unaware of the target’s identity. If such a runelord successfully scries on a creature wearing an original _Sihedron medallion_, they can use the wearer’s voice to issue messages to those in the vicinity. _Sihedron medallions_ created in more modern times don’t possess this disadvantage.

A dead body that wears a _Sihedron medallion_ doesn’t decay, and bugs or other pests (such as maggots) are prevented from consuming the remains, but unlike the effects of [[Spells/Peaceful Rest|_peaceful rest_]], this doesn’t offer any protection against undeath, nor does it have an impact on spells that require the corpse to have died within a certain amount of time.

**Activate—Bolster Flesh** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your flesh is augmented with magical vitality. You gain 10 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] for 8 hours.
