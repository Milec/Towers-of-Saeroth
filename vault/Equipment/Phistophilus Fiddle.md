# Phistophilus Fiddle  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 205

**Hands** 2

---

Made of pure gold with platinum strings, the _phistophilus fiddle_ shouldn’t be able to play a note—but play it does, so beautifully that it can move almost any audience. The fiddle was stolen from the [[Bestiary/Phistophilus (Monster Core)|contract devil]] who created it, and the cunning fiend still wants to reclaim the instrument. Fiendish misfortunes often befall its owners, allowing it to switch hands. The fiddle is a [[Equipment/Musical Instrument (Player Core)|handheld virtuoso instrument]].
