**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 112

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Items with effects that have an undetermined duration, end their effects at the end of a scenario or adventure unless specifically stated otherwise by the campaign rules._

**Bulk** L

---

This thick book has several colorful bookmarks and page dividers. Its title and text shift between numerous languages.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** Your last action was to cast a spell prepared from this grimoire that allows understanding of another language, such as [[Spells/Translate|_translate_]] or [[Spells/Truespeech|_truespeech_]]; **Effect** The grimoire absorbs knowledge of one language translated this way (caster’s choice if more than one), allowing its bearer to communicate on a rudimentary level in that language even after the spell’s duration has elapsed. The _linguist’s dictionary_ can hold one language at a time.
