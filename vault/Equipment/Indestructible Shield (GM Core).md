# Indestructible Shield  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 234

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|Steel Shield]]

---

An indestructible shield is a _high-grade greater reinforcing adamantine shield_ (Hardness 17, HP 136) that can withstand just about any damage. It can be damaged only by a [[Spells/Disintegrate (Player Core)|disintegrate]] spell (roll damage as if against a creature that failed its save) or by an artifact tied to destruction.

**Craft Requirements** The raw materials must include at least 4,400 gp of adamantine.
