# North Wind's Night Verse  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 187

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Katana]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Frost|frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Katana|katana]]_ is always cool to the touch, nearly uncomfortably so. Unadorned and with no tsuba, its honed blade is carved from a single tusk of [[Bestiary/Dragon Turtle|dragon-turtle]] ivory and wrapped in strips of [[Bestiary/Winter Wolf|winter wolf]] hide. Strikes with the katana gain a +2 status bonus to damage rolls against creatures that have a status penalty to their Speed or are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]]. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking rune_ and +4 for _major striking_.

**Activate** free action command ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]]) **Frequency** once per 1 minute; **Trigger** Your spell deals cold damage to a creature;  **Effect** Choose one creature dealt cold damage by the spell. It takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.
