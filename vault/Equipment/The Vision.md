# The Vision  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Harrow Court]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -190- The Choosing|Pathfinder #190: The Choosing]] pg. 81

**Usage** carried

---

_The Vision_ represents arcane knowledge. As long as you have _The Vision_ invested, it grants you a +2 item bonus to all checks made to [[Actions/Identify Magic]]. This increases to a +3 item bonus if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** A visible bolt of blue energy bursts from your head. Attempt to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about a subject, rolling the check with the most appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill check. This check always resolves as if you were master in the most appropriate Lore skill to Recall Knowledge on the subject (or if you're at least 15th level, as if you were legendary in that Lore skill). If you're already master (or legendary) in that Lore, the result of your Recall Knowledge check is one degree of success better than it would otherwise be.
