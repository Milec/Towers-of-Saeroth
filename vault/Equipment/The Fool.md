# The Fool  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -192- Worst of All Possible Worlds|Pathfinder #192: Worst of All Possible Worlds]] pg. 75

**Usage** carried

---

_The Fool_ represents grave foolishness, grand naivete, and exceptional greed. As long as you have _The Fool_ invested, anytime you gain the stupefied condition, reduce its value by 1. You also gain a +2 item bonus to all saving throws against effects that cause the [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition. This bonus increases to +3 if you're at least 17th level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact ([[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You display _The Fool_ to your foes in a clumsy, awkward, and embarrassing way. Choose up to four creatures within 60 feet. These four creatures must attempt a Will save against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is distracted by your antics and is stupefied 1 until the start of your next turn.  

**Failure** The creature is stupefied 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stupefied 3. In addition, for 1 minute, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]].
