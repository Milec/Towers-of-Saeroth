# Clockwork Cloak  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 146

**Price** 20,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

Paper-thin interlocking cogs and gears make up the bronze _clockwork cloak_.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You’re struck by a melee attack with a held weapon;  **Effect** The folds of the cloak attempt to divert the attack and catch the weapon in the cloak’s gears. Make an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the attacking creature.

**Activate** two actions Interact;     **Effect** You wrap the cloak around yourself and the winding gears decelerate your body, causing you to enter standby mode. While in standby mode you don’t need to eat, drink, or sleep. You remain aware of your surroundings but take a –4 penalty to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks. You can stay in standby mode indefinitely, although your body ages normally. You can leave standby mode as a free action. If you do so to initiate combat, you gain a +2 item bonus to your initiative roll.
