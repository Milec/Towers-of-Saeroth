# Elder Seed  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 187

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

An enormous, perfect, ripe seed from a tree important to a region’s [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druids]], an _elder seed_ exudes vitality. Plant life grows quickly and abundantly around it. Anyone who touches the seed understands the nature of its power. Swallowing it whole imparts its effects, but succeeding at the save against the boon causes the swallower to regurgitate the seed.

---

**Stage 1 (9 Days)** Your skin hardens, giving you a brownish‑green tinge. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to bludgeoning damage and piercing damage equal to your level, and you have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] out to a range of 30 feet. Also, you can cast [[Spells/Enlarge (Player Core)|_enlarge_]], [[Spells/Speak with Animals (Player Core)|_speak with animals_]], [[Spells/Speak with Plants (Player Core)|_speak with plants_]], and [[Spells/Web (Player Core 2)|_web_]] each once per day as [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spells]]. The web is a dense mycelial net rather than spider silk.

---

**Stage 2 (1 Month)** You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]] and fluency in [[Setting/Languages/Arboreal]]. However, your body feels stiffer, your mind slower to move to intense emotions, and you are prone to take your time making decisions. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1, take a –1 status penalty to initiative rolls, and gain a [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage equal to half your level, and you take the same amount of additional damage from [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axes]].

If you die at this stage or higher, your body sinks into the earth, forming rich soil. Over several days, a mature, full‑sized tree of the seed’s type grows where you fell. If plant growth is impossible, such as due to lack of sunlight, fungi grow there instead, extending mycelia through the earth until they reach a place the tree can grow. Then, the tree grows. An [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal (Monster Core)|arboreal]] can tend this tree to cause it to regrow one _elder seed_ after 3 years and 3 days.

---

**Stage 3 (1 Month)** You become more treelike and ponderous in body and mind, and when you cast the _enlarge_ innate spell from this boon, you can cast it at 4th level. The clumsy value increases to 2 and the status penalty increases to –2. Whenever you roll initiative, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 automatically, slowed 2 if the d20 roll is 10 or lower, or slowed 3 if the d20 shows a 1. Your weakness to fire and vulnerability to axes equals your level.

---

**Stage 4** You die.

---

**Purging** You must seek out an arboreal, druid, or nature‑loving [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]] that is your level or higher whom you have never met, and pledge your allegiance to that being. Doing so places you under a [[Rituals/Geas (Player Core)|_geas_]] to accomplish at least one meaningful service, usually a quest, for your new liege. While under the geas, you can’t advance to stage 4. Once you complete your service, you regurgitate the _elder seed_, and its effects on you end.
