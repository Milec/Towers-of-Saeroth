# Envisioning Mask  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 260

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** worn mask

---

These strange masks consist of a thin stone hood and a shimmering purple veil reminiscent of the [[Bestiary/Pleroma|pleroma aeon]] who first gifted them to mortals in the hope that sharing the means of aeon communication would reduce the misunderstandings and conflicts among mortal civilizations. An _envisioning mask_ covers your entire face, though it doesn't hinder your vision or other senses.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You lose the ability to produce language and instead communicate wordlessly through a series of psychic projections. This acts as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]] with a range of 100 feet, but it is understandable to all creatures regardless of whether they have a language. When communicating with non-[[Bestiary/Families/Aeon|aeons]], however, your meaning is often vague and mysterious. This effect lasts for 10 minutes.
