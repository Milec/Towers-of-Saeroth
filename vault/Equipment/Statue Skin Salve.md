# Statue Skin Salve  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Enmity Cycle

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Oil]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Enmity Cycle]] pg. 61

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

three actions

Interact

---

This gritty salve can be applied to the skin of a creature to form a thin layer of smooth stone on its body for 20 minutes. During this time, it gains resistance 3 to piercing and slashing damage. Further, the creature gains a +2 item bonus to [[Actions/Impersonate]] a statue or creature made of stone for 8 hours, or until its body is fully submersed in water.
