# Obfuscation Oil  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Oil (GM Core)|Oil]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 257

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

You can spread this blue-gray gel on a single item with a Bulk of 3 or less to ward it against magical detection. It becomes undetectable to detection, revelation, and scrying magic of 8th rank or lower (such as [[Spells/Locate|_locate_]]). This oil is permanent, but it can be removed with acid. Removing the oil in this way usually takes 1 minute for objects with Bulk of 1 or less, or a number of minutes equal to the item's Bulk.
