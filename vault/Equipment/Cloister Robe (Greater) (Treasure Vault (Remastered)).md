# Cloister Robe  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 148

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing]]

---

The most devoted, [[Classes/Subclasses/Doctrines/Cloistered Cleric|cloistered clerics]] wear a _cloister robe_. Decorations symbolic of a specific deity adorn the robe, and the robe's colors and the complexity of its construction fit the deity's outlook. The robe serves as a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of that deity, and it doesn't need to be wielded to provide that benefit.

 The robe is _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing|explorer's clothing]]_ and grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] checks. The robe doesn't grant any benefits to a wearer who doesn't worship the deity tied to the robe. In addition, when you cast a domain spell from one of the deity's domains, you gain resistance to damage from [[Traits/Divine|divine]] spells until the end of your next turn. This resistance is equal to half the robe's level.

**Craft Requirements** You are a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]] who worships the deity tied to the robe.
