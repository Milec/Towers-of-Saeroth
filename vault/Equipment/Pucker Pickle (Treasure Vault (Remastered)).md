Sharp and pungent, but tasty, pucker pickles were created by [[Ancestries/Goblin|goblin]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|alchemists]] working to avoid being eaten by larger creatures. For 1 hour after eating a pucker pickle, you smell slightly of pickle, but you have a horrendous taste. Once a creature hits you with a Strike using an attack that allows it to taste you, such as a jaws Strike, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to further attacks against you that allow it to taste you, including attacks like [[Actions/Grapple|Grappling]] or [[Actions/Trip|Tripping]] you using its jaws or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole|Swallowing you Whole]]. Creatures, especially [[Traits/Animal|animals]], often choose other targets after tasting you. Any creature that [[Bestiary/Abilities/Engulf|Engulfs]] you or Swallows you Whole is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]]. If it spends an action retching to reduce the sickened condition, you can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] as a reaction.
