# Vile Remnant  *Item 7+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Bastion of Blasphemies

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Cursed (GM Core)|Cursed]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

When dislodged, mortal fragments like a chewed fingernail, a careless sneeze, or a few strands of hair might fall to the soil and be swiftly forgotten. When a horror like Charg brushes close to reality, the _vile remnants_ cast off from his nightmare form are not so harmless. At other times, the desecrated remains of innocent creatures can be transformed into _vile remnants_ as well. Worshippers of unholy gods and followers of evil philosophies gather these things and covet them as one might the hem of a cloak brushed against an idolized hero, or the visage of a divinity made manifest in a patch of mold on a wall. _Vile remnants_ spread fear and cruelty wherever they’re [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] and can corrupt those who discover them. They might be buried in the soil of a farmer’s field, dropped into the depths of a village well, or stashed at the bottom of a toy box. Wherever the innocent might stumble across them and become unknowing actors in the decay of morality, the _vile remnant_ works its unholy magic. A _vile remnant_ appears as a raw, decaying mass of flesh, fur, and gristle—a teratoma consisting of warty skin, sightless eyes, a finger or a toe, tufts of bristly hair, or teeth without mouths or lips to hide them. Each _vile remnant’s_ shape is wildly different and uniquely unsettling. A _vile remnant_ automatically imparts to a PC the knowledge of how to use its Hear My Words activation as soon as it’s touched, but its other activations must be discovered normally through the use of Identify Magic (and as normal, identifying the presence and effect of the curse it carries requires a critical success at such an attempt). Unlike most cursed items, a character can discard a _vile remnant_, yet doing so runs a real risk that it might thereafter fall into the wrong hands. In this Adventure Path, any _vile remnant_ the PCs discard anywhere other than the restored reliquary in the Maiserene Church at area **A9** is discovered by one of the monsters of Bastardhall, who then [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hides]] the horror somewhere in the region where it was found. Where the horror is hidden is left to you to decide. As long as a creature carries a _vile remnant_, its thoughts and dreams are influenced by cruel notions and temptations to commit violent depravities. A creature that’s already predisposed toward these acts derives greater pleasure than before for such atrocities but receives no additional penalty or benefit, while a non-sapient creature is driven to become unnaturally violent in its actions (and over the course of weeks, becomes visibly corrupted by patches of strange fur, warty growths, unsightly teeth, or similar teratomas on its body). A sapient creature that isn’t predisposed toward acts of depravity (likely including all PCs) takes an item penalty equal to the number of _vile remnants_ they carry to saving throws against all magical effects that attempt to encourage such acts. Saving throws against spells like _suggestion_ or _charm_ cast by creatures who attempt to push a PC into such acts, any affect by such creatures that attempts to control a PC, or certain specific effects mentioned in the text of this adventure would all be affected by this penalty. A creature with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait reduces the overall item penalty imparted by this curse by 1, to a minimum penalty of 0. A creature that’s foolish enough to consume a _vile remnant_ becomes fused to its curse, at which point the _vile remnant_ remains lodged within the creature’s body until the curse is removed from the creature with a _cleanse affliction_ spell or similar magic, or upon the creature’s death, whichever comes first. At this point, the _vile remnant_ crawls or oozes its way out of the creature’s body and flops, once again inanimate, onto the ground, where it waits to be claimed by a new carrier. As long as you carry a _vile remnant_ somewhere on your person, its curse does have a beneficial side: The power of its influence grants you a +1 item bonus to all saving throws against curses from other sources, as the _vile remnant_ jealously wards against such encroachments on its territory. Unlike the item penalty, carrying multiple _vile remnants_ does not increase this item bonus. The base version of a _vile remnant_ appears to be lifeless, but the _whispering remnant_ periodically twitches and pulses as if it were alive.

**Activate—Become As One**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** By piling eight _vile remnants_ in a heap and then concentrating upon the mound of cursed flesh for a minute, you cause the eight _vile remnants_ to slither together and become a single _whispering remnant_. Once combined in this manner, the resulting _whispering remnant_ cannot be thereafter split into individual _vile remnants_.

**Activate—Hear My Words** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _vile remnant_ casts a DC 23 _enthrall_ spell to your specifications. Creatures that fail or critically fail to resist this spell perceive and remember the _vile remnant_ in your hand as something commonplace to the region, such as a farming tool, book, or fresh-baked pie, for as long as they remain enthralled. If you don’t take advantage of this to subtly guide any affected creatures toward undermining their society, the _vile remnant_ can’t be activated to Hear My Words again by you for 1 week. Additionally, the next time you sleep, you endure horrific nightmares and must succeed at a DC 23 Will save upon waking to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 24 hours (this is a mental effect).

**Activate—Hide the Horror**  10 minutes ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per year;   **Effect** You hide the _vile remnant_ somewhere that, in time, it is likely to be found. For the following four weeks, _the vile remnant_’s evil seeps into the surrounding landscape, expanding its influence at a rate of 200 feet per day until it reaches its maximum emanation of 5,600 feet after 28 days. It continues to influence this area for another three weeks before going dormant— it must be recovered and rehidden later once this activation recharges to begin the cycle again. While the remnant’s influence is active, any sapient creature that sleeps in the area experiences unsettling dreams of being stalked by monsters they can never quite see or evade. A creature that doesn’t worship an unholy deity who experiences these dreams must succeed at a DC 23 Will save each time they rest to avoid awakening [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]]. On a critical failure, the creature awakens [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 as well; this condition remains until removed or until they are no longer fatigued, whichever comes first. Creatures who worship an unholy deity or follow a despicable philosophy are immune to this effect. Any skill checks made by such creatures when performing rituals performed in the affected area gain a +1 item bonus, and _nightmare_ spells cast by them within the region have their save DCs increased by 1.

**Destruction** A _vile remnant_ can’t be destroyed, but a _whispering remnant_ can be by hurling it into a powerful curse effect created by the deity associated with the remnant. This causes the _whispering remnant_ to become reabsorbed into the deity’s otherworldly body and often has potent side effects on the surrounding region. Disposing of a _whispering remnant_ in the soul vortex of Bastardhall (area  
**I**) after Caydserris has been defeated not only destroys the _whispering remnant_ but helps bring the Bastardhall Curse to an end.

## Whispering Remnant  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 238

**Bulk** L

---

A _whispering remnant’s_ Hide the Horror aura expands at a rate of 500 feet per day for 28 days, and the DC’s for its Hear My Words and [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] the Horror activations increase to 30. It imparts a -9 item penalty to all saving throws against deplorable influences and a +3 item bonus to all saving throws against curses as long as it’s carried. _Whispering remnants_ are incredibly rare, and their use is required in many vile rites, such as the one that brought down the curse on Castle Arudora. A _whispering remnant_ gains the following additional activation.

**Activate—Heed My Whispers** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _whispering remnant_ casts a DC 30 [[Spells/Subconscious Suggestion (Player Core)|_subconscious suggestion_]] spell to your specifications. If the target of the spell fails or critically fails to save against this, they perceive and remember the _whispering remnant_ as being a commonplace item. If you don’t suggest a course of action to the target that causes them to work at undermining their society, the _whispering remnant_ shrieks horribly, but only in your mind—you take 10d6 mental damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save), after which the _whispering remnant_ cannot be activated again by you for 1 week.
