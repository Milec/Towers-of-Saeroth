# Air Cycle  *Vehicle 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], Large

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 108

**Price** 300 gp

---

This clockwork vehicle consists of a small wheeled platform under a pair of wings made with a light wooden frame covered by sturdy cloth. An air cycle must be launched from a high altitude, similar to a glider. Once airborne, however, the air cycle can be kept aloft and controlled using a system of pedals and steering handles to control its speed and direction. If you stop pedaling the air cycle, it functions as a [[Equipment/Vehicles/Glider (GM Core)|glider]].
