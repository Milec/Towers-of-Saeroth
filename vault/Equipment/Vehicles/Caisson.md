# Caisson  *Vehicle 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], Large

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 111

**Price** 240 gp

---

This heavily armored wagon is designed to transport black powder and other flammable munitions in and around combat zones. It has extra armor plating that increases its weight to several times that of an ordinary wagon. Due to this extra weight, the armored wagon is quite slow.
