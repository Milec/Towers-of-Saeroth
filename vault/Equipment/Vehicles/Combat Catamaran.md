# Combat Catamaran  *Vehicle 13*

**Traits** Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 112

**Price** 9,000 gp

---

This high-speed sailing ship is designed to track down fleeing vessels. When traveling downwind, the combat catamaran can deploy a pair of huge kite-like spinnakers that dramatically increase its speed.

---

**Space** 40 feet long, 40 feet wide, 80 feet high

**Crew** 2 pilot, 5 crew

**Passengers** 15

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 33) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 31)

---

**AC** 29

**Fort** +23

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 230 (BT 115)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Weaknesses** 10 fire until [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]]

---

**Speed** swim 30 feet or 50 feet using spinnakers (wind)

**Collision** 9d10 (DC 31)

**Deploy Spinnakers** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) This vehicle has massive kite-like spinnakers. Deploying and retracting these spinnakers takes two crew members to each perform a 3-action activity on their turn. While the spinnakers are deployed, the weapons installed on the weapon mounts can’t fire.  
**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 2 weapon mounts, with one on each of its hulls. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged siege weapons.
