# Armored Sleigh  *Vehicle 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], Huge

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 110

**Price** 45,000 gp

---

Designed to traverse frozen climates, armored sleighs are constructed from heavy oaken timbers covered with iron. These massive vehicles can readily traverse frozen and snow-covered ground while protecting the pilot and crew from the cold.

---

**Space** 60 feet long, 30 feet wide, 20 feet high

**Crew** 2 pilots, 8 crew

**Passengers** 20

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Driving Lore]] (DC 36) or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 38 to DC 46, depending on pulling creatures)

---

**AC** 39

**Fort** +30

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 280 (BT 140)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

---

**Speed** the Speed of the slowest pulling creature (pulled by 4 Huge creatures)

**Collision** 12d10 (DC 36)

**Ice Traverser** The armored sleigh ignores difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.  
**Insulated Protections** from the icy landscape are built into the front of this vehicle, providing sanctuary from the cold. The pilot and crew are protected from [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|severe and extreme cold]]. The pilot also gains resistance 10 to all cold damage.  
**Sluggish** This vehicle must move twice its length for each 90-degree turn it makes.
