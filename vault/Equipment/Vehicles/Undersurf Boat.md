# Undersurf Boat  *Vehicle 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], Large

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 95

**Price** 480 gp

---

Resembling a large, oared kayak with closable hatches, the submersible surfboat was designed by azarketi shipwrights for use above and below the waves.

---

**Space** 15 feet long, 10 feet wide, 5 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 2 rowers

**Passengers** 2

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 20), [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 22), [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 22), or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 22)

---

**AC** 18

**Fort** +12

**Hardness** 10

**HP** 60 (BT 30)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Resistances** fire 5

---

**Speed** swim 30 feet (rowed)

**Collision** 3d10 (DC 20)

**Submersible** This vehicle can travel below the surface of the water, usually to a depth of no deeper than 60 feet. Though enclosed, it is not watertight, so crew and passengers must hold their breath or be able to breathe underwater.
