# War Wagon  *Vehicle 8*

**Traits** Huge

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 112

**Price** 1,500 gp

---

Often adorned with spikes and other defensive countermeasures, a war wagon is an intimidating sight on any battlefield. It can carry both soldiers and a siege weapon.

---

**Space** 15 feet long, 15 feet wide, 7 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 1 crew

**Passengers** 6

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Driving Lore]] (DC 24) or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 26 to DC 34 depending on the pulling creatures)

---

**AC** 21

**Fort** +16

**Hardness** 10

**HP** 120 (BT 60)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

---

**Speed** the Speed of the slowest pulling creature (pulled by 6 Large creatures)

**Collision** 6d10 (DC 24)

**Countermeasures** A creature that Strikes a war wagon with a non-reach melee weapon or unarmed attack takes 3d8 piercing damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).  
**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has one weapon mount. At purchase, the weapon mount is initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 24 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. This weapon mount can be equipped with a Large or smaller ranged siege weapon.
