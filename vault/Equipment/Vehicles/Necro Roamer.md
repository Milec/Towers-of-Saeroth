# Necro Roamer  *Vehicle 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], Huge

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 112

**Price** 19,900 gp

---

Favored by necromancers, this vehicle is a heavily fortified, armored wooden shed mounted on the legs of a dozen [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures.

---

**Space** 15 feet long, 15 feet wide, 15 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 2 crew

**Passengers** 3

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (DC 36) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Driving Lore]] (DC 34)

---

**AC** 34

**Fort** +26

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 220 (BT 110)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

---

**Speed** 30 feet ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]])

**Collision** 9d12 (DC 32)

**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has two rooftop weapon mounts. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 36 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. These weapon mounts can be equipped with Large or smaller ranged siege weapons.
