# Flying Fortress  *Vehicle 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 109

**Price** 210,000 gp

---

The ultimate battlefield command center, this massive skyborne castle serves as a platform for launching aerial assaults on land-based and sea-based enemy positions. By hovering over an enemy position, the flying fortress can lay waste to enemies who lack countering siege weapons by firing down on their frontline defenses.

---

**Space** 70 feet long, 70 feet wide, 80 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 25 crew

**Passengers** 100

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] (DC 42) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Piloting Lore]] (DC 40)

**Speed** Speed fly 30 feet ([[Traits/Clockwork|clockwork]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], wind)

**Collision** 15d10 (DC 40)

**Sluggish** This vehicle must move twice its length for each 90-degree turn it makes.  
**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has eight weapon mounts, with two on each side of the fortress. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. These weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged siege weapons.
