# Yellow-Sailed Schooner  *Vehicle 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 95

**Price** 2,100 gp

---

Hailing from Okeno, this schooner usually keeps its allegiance a secret until it gets within boarding range and unfurls its saffron-colored sails.

---

**Space** 85 feet long, 25 feet wide, 20 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 10 crew

**Passengers** 30

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 23), [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 25), or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 25)

---

**AC** 22

**Fort** +17

**Hardness** 15

**HP** 150 (BT 75)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Weaknesses** fire 15 until broken

---

**Speed** swim 30 feet (wind)

**Collision** 6d10 (DC 26)

**Unfurl Yellow Sails** single action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) With the pull of a rope, the pilot or one of the crew unfurls the yellow sails that Okeno freebooters are so infamous for. Each enemy creature within a 100-foot emanation who can see the sails must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (or frightened 2 on a critical failure). Regardless of the save's results, the creature is temporarily immune to Unfurl Yellow Sails for 24 hours.
