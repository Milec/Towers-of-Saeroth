# The Dark Prince  *Vehicle 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 95

**Price** 210,000 gp

---

A massive red-and-black warship, the _Dark Prince_ sports an imposing figurehead of Asmodeus and terrifying faces carved along its gunwales. It patrols the area around the Arch of Aroden, keeping the waters free of pirates and enemies.

---

**Space** 280 feet long, 60 feet wide, 30 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 50 crew

**Passengers** 50

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore (DC 40)]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 42), or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 42)

---

**AC** 42

**Fort** +35

**Hardness** 18

**HP** 320 (BT 160)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Resistances** 20 fire until broken

**Weaknesses** 14d10 (DC 40)

---

**Speed** swim 20 feet (wind)

**Collision** 14d10 (DC 40)

**Dreadful Aura** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) The flagship of the Chelaxian navy exudes a malevolent air. At the beginning of their turns or when they enter the aura, enemy creatures on board the ship and enemy creatures within a 300-foot-emanation who can see the ship must succeed at a DC 39 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (or frightened 2 on a critical failure). Regardless of the result, the creature is temporarily immune to this aura for 1 minute. However, an enemy creature on board can't reduce its frightened condition below 1 until it is no longer on board.

 **Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 6 weapon mounts, three on each side of the vehicle. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 40 Crafting check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged [siege weapons](SiegeWeapons.aspx).
