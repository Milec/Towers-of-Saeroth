# Duskwood Corvette  *Vehicle 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 94

**Price** 7,500 gp

---

This triple-masted warship is crafted mainly from sturdy duskwood brought down from Andoran's Darkmoon Vale. Naval captains must prove their worth before securing a post on such a valuable vessel.

---

**Space** 90 feet long, 20 feet wide, 20 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 10 crew, 10 rowers

**Passengers** 20

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore (DC 28)]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 31), or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 31)

---

**AC** 28

**Fort** +22

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 200 (BT 100)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Weaknesses** 15 fire until broken

---

**Speed** swim 40 feet (rowed, wind)

**Collision** 8d10 (DC 30)

**Stealthy** Due to its ebony coloration, a duskwood corvette is difficult to detect under certain conditions. It is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] in dim light.

 **Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 2 weapon mounts, one on each side of the vehicle. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 28 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged [siege weapons](SiegeWeapons.aspx).
