# Clockwork Hopper  *Vehicle 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], Large

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 95

**Price** 750 gp

---

A clockwork hopper is similar in form to a large rabbit, making hop-like strides that avoid terrain impediments, though the lurching hops can be bumpy and cause motion sickness in some riders.

---

**Space** 10 feet long, 10 feet wide, 12 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot

**Passengers** 2

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Driving Lore]] (DC 22) or [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (DC 24)

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +14

**Hardness** 5

**HP** 80 (BT 40)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

---

**Speed** 35 feet (clockwork)

**Collision** 4d10 (DC 22)

**Hopper** This vehicle hops across the terrain as it moves across the ground. The clockwork hopper [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignores difficult terrain]] from earth, rock, rubble, slopes, and underbrush, and greater difficult terrain caused by those features is normal difficult terrain to the clockwork hopper.  
**Massive Jump** three actions ([[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]], [[Traits/Reckless (GM Core)|reckless]]) The pilot engages a spring‑driven mechanism that rockets the clockwork hopper into the air, in a grand leap. The vehicle jumps in a straight line in the direction of its current heading without touching the ground, landing in a space of solid ground up to 70 feet forward and up to 30 feet up. Creatures adjacent to the spaces a hopper lands in take 4d10 bludgeoning damage with a DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save, and are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] on a critical failure. The clockwork hopper can’t use Massive Jump again for 1 minute. Massive Jump stops working if the clockwork hopper is broken.  
**Wind-Up** 10 minutes of wind-up, 1 hour of operational time, DC 22, standby
