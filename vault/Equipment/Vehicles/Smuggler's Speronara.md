# Smuggler's Speronara  *Vehicle 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], Huge

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 95

**Price** 1,080 gp

---

This merchant craft has no deck and only one mast. It has a relatively shallow hull for storing cargo, but also several hidden compartments for smuggling illicit goods.

---

**Space** 45 feet long, 20 feet wide, 15 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 2 crew, 4 rowers

**Passengers** 5

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 23), [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 25), or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 25)

---

**AC** 21

**Fort** +14

**Hardness** 10

**HP** 110 (BT 55)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Weaknesses** fire 10 until broken

---

**Speed** swim 30 feet (rowed, wind)

**Collision** 4d10 (DC 23)

**Maneuverable** With a full complement of skilled rowers, a rowed speronara can turn swiftly, turn in place, and even row backward. Each 90-degree turn made in less than a vehicle length costs 5 extra feet of movement. It can row backward no faster than half its speed, and it can't turn while rowing backward.

 **Smuggling** Compartments The speronara can hold up to 20 Bulk of items in secret compartments that require a successful DC 23 Perception check to find.
