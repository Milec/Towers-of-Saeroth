# Combat Kite  *Vehicle 6*

**Traits** Huge

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 108

**Price** 750 gp

---

This massive box-shaped kite is flown from the ground with a thin and light magical tether. This enables the combat kite to carry cargo high over enemy positions and remotely release it, often alchemical bombs or gliders.

---

**Space** 20 feet long, 20 feet wide, 10 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 24) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Piloting Lore]] (DC 22)

---

**AC** 19

**Fort** +14

**Hardness** 5

**HP** 80 (BT 40)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

**Weaknesses** fire 5 until [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]]

---

**Speed** fly 15 feet ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], wind)

**Collision** 4d8 (DC 22)

**Cargo Release** The combat kite can carry a [[Equipment/Vehicles/Glider (GM Core)|glider]] or up to 20 Bulk of materials. The glider or materials can be remotely released by [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interacting]] with the magical tether.
