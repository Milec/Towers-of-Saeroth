# Augustana's Pride  *Vehicle 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 94

**Price** 240,000 gp

---

The majestic _Augustana's Pride_ is usually kept docked at its eponymous port, but is put on active duty in times of great need. Constructed almost entirely of duskwood, with accents painted in Andoren blue and gold, this warship is the crowning jewel of the navy.

---

**Space** 280 feet long, 40 feet wide, 30 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 50 crew

**Passengers** 50

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore (DC 40)]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] (DC 42), or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] (DC 42)

**Speed** swim 35 feet (wind)

**Collision** 14d10 (DC 40)

**Inspiring Presence** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) 300 feet. The sight of the Andoren navy's flagship bolsters its fleet and its crew. At the beginning of their turns or when they enter the aura, allied creatures on board the ship and allied creatures within a 300-foot-emanation who can see the ship gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, and saves against fear for 1 round.

 **Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has 6 weapon mounts, three on each side of the vehicle. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 40 Crafting check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged [siege weapons](SiegeWeapons.aspx).
