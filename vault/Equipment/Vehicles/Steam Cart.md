# Steam Cart  *Vehicle 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], Large

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 94

**Price** 120 gp

---

A steam cart looks like a cart with an alchemical cauldron that produces steam to power the cart’s movement through a simple engine.

---

**Space** 10 feet long, 5 feet wide, 4 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot

**Passengers** 1

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Driving Lore]] (DC 16) or [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (DC 18)

---

**AC** 12

**Fort** +8

**Hardness** 5

**HP** 30 (BT 15)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

---

**Speed** 25 feet (alchemical)

**Collision** 2d8 (DC 16)

**Steam Cloud** Whenever the steam cart takes collision damage, its engine expels a dense cloud of steam in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. The steam cart and all creatures in the steam become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and creatures outside the steam become concealed to creatures within it. The steam dissipates in 1 round.
