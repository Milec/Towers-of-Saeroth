# Steam Turtle  *Vehicle 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 115

**Price** 30,000 gp

---

This heavily armored aquatic vehicle resembles a gargantuan sea turtle covered with steel plates. The steam turtle is designed for beach landings and assaults on port facilities. Once it disables enemy fortifications, it extends a long boarding gangplank for troops to disembark and deploy.

---

**Space** 80 feet long, 50 feet wide, 40 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 3 crew

**Passengers** 40

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] (DC 37) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 35)

---

**AC** 36

**Fort** +29

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 250 (BT 125)

**Immunities** critical hits, [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]], precision damage

---

**Speed** swim 30 feet ([[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|alchemical]])

**Collision** 11d10 (DC 35)

**Sluggish** This vehicle must move twice its length for each 90-degree turn it makes.  
**Release Steam** three actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** Two crew members each perform a 3-action activity on their turn to release a massive buildup of steam around the vehicle, dealing 11d6 fire damage to each creature in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 35 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The release mechanism stops working if the vehicle is broken.
