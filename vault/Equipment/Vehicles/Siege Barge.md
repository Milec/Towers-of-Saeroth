# Siege Barge  *Vehicle 19*

**Traits** Gargantuan

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 115

**Price** 120,000 gp

---

These special barges were designed to siege river and oceanside fortresses and enemy encampments. In addition to carrying two huge siege weapons and their crews, these barges also hold a variety of stones and other siege projectiles.

---

**Space** 60 feet long, 30 feet wide, 20 feet high

**Crew** 1 pilot, 4 crew

**Passengers** 20

**Piloting Check** [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] (DC 41 to 46 depending on the pulling creatures) or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] (DC 39)

---

**AC** 42

**Fort** +33

**Hardness** 20

**HP** 300 (BT 150)

**Immunities** [[Rules/Object Immunities (Player Core)|object immunities]]

---

**Speed** the Speed of the slowest pulling creature (pulled by 4 Huge creatures)

**Collision** 13d10 (DC 39)

**Sluggish** This vehicle must move twice its length for each 90-degree turn it makes.  
**Weapon Mounts** This vehicle has two weapon mounts. At purchase, these weapon mounts are initially empty. Installing or uninstalling a weapon into a mount requires 1 day, a successful DC 39 [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check, and an appropriate weapon. The weapon mounts can be equipped with Huge or smaller ranged siege weapons.
