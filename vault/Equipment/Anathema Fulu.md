# Anathema Fulu  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 76

**Price** 250 gp

**Duration** 1 hour

**Usage** affixed to the ground in four spaces along a circle with a 20-foot radius

---

First used by [[Ancestries/Nephilim|aasimars]] of Tianjing to weaken [[Bestiary/Families/Qlippoth (Monster Core)|qlippoth]], an _anathema fulu_ comes in four pieces, one placed in each cardinal direction. Choose one of the following traits when activating the fulus: [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]], [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]], or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. If a creature with that trait starts its turn in the area, it must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2 until the start of its next turn. On a critical success, the creature becomes temporarily immune to any _anathema fulus_ for 1 hour. Regardless of the save result, if you subsequently cast banishment on such a creature in the area, the creature takes the –2 penalty described in that spell for using a material component that’s anathema to that creature. The fulu acts as that component; you don’t need to add it. If a creature affected by this penalty rolls a saving throw against banishment, the caster can use a free action to force the result one step lower. Doing so burns the whole fulu out, ending the effect. If any of the fulu’s pieces are moved or destroyed after activation, the effect ends.
