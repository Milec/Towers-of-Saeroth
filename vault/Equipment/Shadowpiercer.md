# Shadowpiercer  *Item 23*

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Spear]]

---

Muted, silvery moonlight gleams along the curved blade of this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+4]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|major striking]] [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spear]]_. Braided tassels of horsehair dangle from its sturdy haft, and archaic runes in Nidalese script shimmer along the weapon in the presence of shadow magic.

_Shadowpiercer_ ignores [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to precision damage of creatures with the [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] or [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] trait, and deals an additional 2d6 precision damage to such creatures. While wielding _Shadowpiercer_, you gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] and shadow effects; this bonus also applies to allies within 60 feet of you while you wield the spear.

If you are a worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]], you are [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 4 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 4 while carrying or wielding _Shadowpiercer_. If you use a darkness or shadow effect while carrying or wielding _Shadowpiercer_, you become enfeebled 4; this condition’s value reduces by 1 at the start of each day you do not have physical contact with _Shadowpiercer_.

**Activate—Absorb Shadows** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You touch _Shadowpiercer_ to a [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] or [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] effect to siphon its energy and bolster yourself. You gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to four times the effect’s [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] rank. These temporary Hit Points last for 1 hour. In addition, if the effect is causing darkness, that darkness is automatically counteracted within 15 feet of you for as long as you have those temporary Hit Points. This doesn’t provide light—it merely restores the area to its natural illumination level.

**Activate—Rebuff Gloom** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** You don’t currently have the [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition; **Trigger** You or an ally within 60 feet is targeted by a [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] or [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]] effect;  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; the spear drains your vitality to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the effect. You can attempt to counteract the effect (+46 modifier level, counteract rank 10). You can activate this effect without having a Mythic Point available but doing so makes you [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2.

**Destruction** _Shadowpiercer_ simply disappears if a worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]] casts a 10th-rank [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]] spell on it once a day for an entire month while it is unattended.
