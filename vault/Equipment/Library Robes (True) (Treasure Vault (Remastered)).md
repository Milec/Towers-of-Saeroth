# Library Robes  *Item 9+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 15

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Scroll Robes (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Scroll Robes]]

---

These _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Scroll Robes (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|scroll robes]]_ magically store a spell for you. During your daily preparations, choose one spell you know of 5th level or lower. You inscribe that spell on the robes, as though you had done so using the robes' [[Traits/Inscribed (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|inscribed]] trait, but without needing to go through the normal scribing process. You must provide the minimum amount of materials to [[Actions/Craft]] one scroll of that spell (typically half the Price of a scroll of that level plus any extra cost required for the spell). You don't need to be trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], nor do you need the [[Feats/Magical Crafting]] feat. Using this ability erases any scroll already inscribed on the robe.
