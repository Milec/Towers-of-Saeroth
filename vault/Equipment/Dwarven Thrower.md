# Dwarven Thrower  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 600

**Price** 2,750 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer]]

---

This _+2 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Warhammer|warhammer]]_ is inlaid with precious metals and decorated with geometric patterns in a dwarven style. If you’re a [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarf]], a _dwarven thrower_ functions for you as a _+2 greater striking [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] warhammer_ with the [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 30 feet]] trait, and your attacks with the hammer deal 1d8 additional damage against [[Traits/Giant|giants]].
