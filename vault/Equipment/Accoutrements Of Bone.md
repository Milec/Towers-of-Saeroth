# Accoutrements Of Bone  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 202

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|Explorer's Clothing]]

---

This set of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Explorer's Clothing (Player Core)|explorer’s clothing]]_ is lined with magically reinforced carved bones that are connected with reinforced sinews to provide the wearer both comfort and mobility. Spikes and spines of various shapes and sizes jut menacingly from the shoulder and forearm coverings. When you wear this outfit, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] checks.

**Activate—Piercing Vengeance** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy damages you with a melee Strike;  **Effect** Jagged spines from your clothing launch themselves at the triggering enemy, dealing 6d6 piercing damage with a DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.
