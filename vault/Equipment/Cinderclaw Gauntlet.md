This sharp-taloned _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet|spiked gauntlet]] _appears to have been crafted from dragon scales, with claws built from iron. In addition to the typical weapon traits, a _Cinderclaw gauntlet_ has the [[Traits/Versatile|versatile S]] trait.
