This silvery, pink-tinged _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ is polished to the point that its surface is completely reflective. This armor can reflect some of the light generated by magical attacks.

**Activate—Reflect Shine** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature targets you with a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect that has the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] trait;  **Effect** You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the spell reflects toward the caster, who becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.
