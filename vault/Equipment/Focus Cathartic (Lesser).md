Focus briefly calms your neurons, helping ease mental effects. When you drink this elixir, the elixir attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check with the listed counteract modifier to remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition from a single source, using the source of that condition to determine the counteract level and DC.
