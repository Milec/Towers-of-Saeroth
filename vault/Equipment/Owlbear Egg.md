Not only are [[Bestiary/Owlbear|owlbear]] eggs delicious when boiled, but when infused with a mix of alchemical reagents, they also make you emit a long screech much like an owlbear’s. All creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 23 Will save. Regardless of the result, creatures in the area are temporarily immune to this screech for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.
