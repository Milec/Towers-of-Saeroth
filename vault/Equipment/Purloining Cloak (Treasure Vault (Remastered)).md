**Skills** [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] +31, [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] +31, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Underworld Lore]] +31

**Int** +5, **Wis** +5, **Cha** +5

**Will** +30

---  

Each _purloining cloak_ is a [[Equipment/Mercurial Mantle|_mercurial mantle_]] once worn by a legendary thief or two. The cloak became infused with a daredevil spirit and a penchant for high-stakes thievery. If its owner isn't willing to partake in such activities, the cloak tries to convince them to sell or give it to someone who will. A purloining cloak can use tendrils of its cloth to attempt [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Palm an Object]] or [[Actions/Steal]], proudly offering you its loot later. In addition to those of a _mercurial mantle_, a _purloining cloak_ has the following activations.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The cloak casts 2nd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] on you, usually to your specifications. However the cloak can choose the appearance of the illusion for you.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** The cloak assesses the price of valuables it can see. This valuation doesn't include the value of features the cloak can't discern, such as magical properties.
