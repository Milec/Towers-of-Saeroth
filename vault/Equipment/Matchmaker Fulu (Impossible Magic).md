This red fulu contains blessings for one’s relationship from [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn (Divine Mysteries)|Shelyn]], goddess of beauty and love. You get a +2 status bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check, and if you roll a critical failure on the check, you get a failure instead.
