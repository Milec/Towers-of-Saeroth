# Fu Water  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221

**Price** 21 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

Burnt fulu ashes float in this liquid, lending a distinctive red color and imparting it with a variety of purifying effects. Unlike many fulu items, _fu water_ must be created using [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] magic.

**Activate—Bitter Medicine** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You drink the _fu water_ to counteract ailments within yourself. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check with a +9 counteract modifier and a counteract rank of 3 against one effect that imposes the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition. You’re then [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.

**Activate—Divine Bomb** single action (splash)    **Effect** This potion functions as a bottle of _holy water_. Like _holy water_, you throw it as a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] as part of the activation. It’s a simple thrown weapon with a range increment of 20 feet. Unlike an [[Equipment/Weapons/Alchemical Bomb (Player Core)|alchemical bomb]], it doesn’t add the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait to the attack made with it.
