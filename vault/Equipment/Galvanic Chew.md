# Galvanic Chew  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Lozenge (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Lozenge]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 48

**Price** 75 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

A galvanic chew is a processed ball of dried electric eel, roasted until chewy and coated in spicy, reagent-infused powder that tingles with electricity as you chew. For up to 1 hour, you have [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to electricity.

**Secondary Effect** ⤾ ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) **Trigger** You’re hit by a melee attack or touched by a creature; **Effect** You channel electricity into the triggering creature, which must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (or stunned 2 on a critical failure). The chew becomes inert. You’re then temporarily immune to galvanic chews for 24 hours or until the next time you make your daily preparations.
