# Golden Greaves  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 150

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

These shiny greaves made of splinted metal coated with gold protect the shins and help you stand your ground in the heat of battle. While wearing the greaves, you gain a +1 item bonus to your Fortitude DC against [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] effects and to your Reflex DC against effects that would knock you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

**Activate—Make Them Fall** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** An enemy fails to [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] you;  **Effect** Your _golden greaves_ glow with a strange light, and you move your legs in just the right way to completely throw off your opponent. Your opponent instead critically fails on the triggering check.
