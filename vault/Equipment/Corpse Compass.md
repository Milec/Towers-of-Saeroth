# Corpse Compass  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Outlaws of Alkenstar

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 76

**Price** 45 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This bone [[Equipment/Compass|compass]], made of the bones of sapient creatures that died violent deaths, is eerily effective when it comes to locating corpses. If you know you're [[Actions/Track|Tracking]] a creature that's dead, you can use the compass's idiosyncrasies to your advantage and gain a +2 item bonus to your [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] check to do so. Otherwise, it works as a normal compass.
