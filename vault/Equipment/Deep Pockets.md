# Deep Pockets  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 121

**Price** 220 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ has two medium-sized pockets just above the waist where you might normally place your hands if they were cold. Each pocket is covered with a leather flap that surprisingly remains closed even with dynamic movement and heavy winds. Each individual pocket functions as a [[Equipment/Spacious Pouch|_type I spacious pouch_]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include two _type I spacious pouches_.
