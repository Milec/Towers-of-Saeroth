# Rustbringer  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 147

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail]]

---

The handle, chain, and spiked ball of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Corrosive|corrosive]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail|flail]]_ are all made of iron so rusted that the weapon appears nonfunctional at first glance. On Strikes against a creature that's primarily made of metal, it gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d10]] trait.

**Activate—Rusting Disarm** ⤾ ;   **Trigger** You critically succeed at a [[Actions/Disarm]] attempt with the flail against a metal weapon;  **Effect** Flakes of acid-laden rust are deposited on the disarmed weapon. The weapon takes 2d6 damage, ignoring the weapon's Hardness.
