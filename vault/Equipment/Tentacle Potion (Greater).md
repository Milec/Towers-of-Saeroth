# Tentacle Potion  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 91

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Upon consuming this mottled, foul-tasting liquid, the _tentacle potion_ causes you to extrude a long, flexible limb of ectoplasm. Your clothing doesn’t need to accommodate this limb of ghostly matter, which can extrude through your clothing and armor. The limb lasts 1 hour, and you can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the activation. You can’t hide or disguise the tentacle. You can use the limb to perform simple [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions, such as opening an unlocked door. Your limb can’t perform actions that require significant manual dexterity, including any action that would require a check to accomplish. You can’t use it to hold items. At one time, you can have only one extra limb from any version of this potion. Stronger _tentacle potions_ replace the effects of weaker ones.

If you have the [[Feats/Flexible Tail (Player Core 2)|Flexible Tail]] or [[Feats/Skillful Tail (Player Core)|Skillful Tail]] feats, the [[Ancestries/Heritages/Tailed Goblin]] heritage, or a similar feature the GM believes would benefit, this potion can instead fortify your tail. A fortified tail benefits from any _tentacle potion_ as if the potion were the next better type.

**Price** 33 gp

## Tentacle Potion (Moderate)  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 91

**Price** 155 gp

---

As lesser, but you can use the limb to hold an item of up to light Bulk. You can also use the whole limb to hold onto a suitable anchor point, such as a tree branch, balcony, or rocky outcropping, subject to the GM’s discretion. While using your limb this way, you have free use of all your other limbs, so you can perform tasks that require both hands. This potion lasts 8 hours

## Tentacle Potion (Greater)  *Item 14*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 91

**Price** 660 gp

---

As moderate, but the tail can hold an item of up to 1 Bulk or be used as a second hand to help hold an item of up to 2 Bulk. You can also use the limb to make an unarmed attack that deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage and has the sweep trait. This potion lasts 24 hours.
