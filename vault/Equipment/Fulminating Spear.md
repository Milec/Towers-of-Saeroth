# Fulminating Spear  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 76

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]]_ is warm to the touch. The head of the spear is crafted from gold with ivory inlay and has a ruby set into the shaft. You can make the spear glow like a [[Equipment/Torch|torch]] or suppress its light by using an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You touch the ruby and hurl the spear at a square within 40 feet. When the spear lands, it detonates, dealing 6d6 fire damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 10-foot emanation. It then flies back to your hand.
