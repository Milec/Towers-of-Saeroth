# Constricting Meteor  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 102

**Price** 450 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Meteor Hammer (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Meteor Hammer]]

---

The weights of this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Meteor Hammer (Tian Xia Character Guide)|meteor hammer]]_ are shaped like snake heads, and anaconda scales adorn the chain.

**Activate—Coiling Chain** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])   **Requirements** Your last action was a successful melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] with the _constricting meteor_; **Effect** The chain of the meteor hammer wraps around the target and squeezes. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the creature with a +1 item bonus. In addition to being grabbed on a success, the target takes 1d10+7 bludgeoning damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the scales of a [[Bestiary/Giant Anaconda (Monster Core)|giant anaconda]].
