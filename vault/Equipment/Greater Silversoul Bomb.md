# Silversoul Bomb  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 295

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

[[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]

---

This rare alchemical bomb gathers and concentrates the emotions that linger in a burial ground where generations of beloved ancestors have been interred, infusing that powerful emotional energy into the alchemically prepared powdered [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] stored within the bomb. This energy glows with a soft, silver radiance and, if stored in a clear container, allows a silversoul bomb to be used as a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]] to illuminate an area.

A silversoul bomb grants an item bonus to attack rolls and deals mental damage plus additional effects, according to the bomb’s type. [[Bestiary/Families/Nindoru|Nindorus]] are particularly harmed by silversoul bombs and take the listed persistent mental damage, according to the bomb’s type. Against nindorus, the bomb’s item bonus also applies to its save DC to resist being [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]]. Creatures that have [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to silver (including most nindorus) have an equal amount of weakness to the mental damage caused by a silversoul bomb, due to the silver infused into the energy within.

## Silversoul Bomb  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 296

**Price** 10 gp

---

You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls, and the bomb deals 2d4 mental damage, 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]] to nindorus, and 2 mental [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage. A creature that takes splash damage from the bomb and fails a DC 17 Fortitude save is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round as glowing silver particles cling to its face.

## Greater Silversoul Bomb  *Item 11*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 296

**Price** 250 gp

---

You gain a +2 item bonus to attack rolls, and the bomb deals 3d4 mental damage, 2d6 persistent mental damage to nindorus, and 3 mental splash damage. A creature that takes splash damage from the bomb and fails a DC 28 Fortitude save is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and then dazzled for 1 round thereafter.

## Major Silversoul Bomb  *Item 17*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 296

**Price** 2,500 gp

---

You gain a +3 item bonus to attack rolls, and the bomb deals 4d4 mental damage, 3d6 persistent mental damage to nindorus, and 4 mental splash damage. A creature that takes splash damage from the bomb is dazzled for 1 round unless it fails a DC 37 Fortitude save, in which case it’s permanently blinded.
