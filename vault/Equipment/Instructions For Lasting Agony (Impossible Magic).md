**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 200 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This worn and stained manual emits a chill when opened.

**Activate—Excruciating Spell** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast]] a prepared [[Traits/Void|void]] Spell that allows a saving throw, you warp and twist the energy of the spell to cause intense pain. If the target fails its saving throw against the spell, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 by the pain.
