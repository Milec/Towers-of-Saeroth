# Scope of Truth  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 182

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters with access to firearms gain access to accessories that can be used with those weapons. Replace the word "transmutation" in the scope of truth with the word "polymorph."_

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** attached to firearm or crossbow (scope)

**Bulk** L

---

The _scope of truth_ is a bit bigger than most to accommodate larger lenses, which have been magically prepared with tiny shards from crystal balls to reveal the truth. The scope grants you a +2 item bonus to Perception checks made to Seek in areas you can see through the scope.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** For the next 10 minutes, you can see things through the scope as they actually are. The GM rolls a secret [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check with a +20 counteract modifier and a counteract level of 7 against any [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]] in the area, but only for the purpose of determining whether you see through it, not to end the spell or effect. For instance, if the check succeeds against a [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]] spell, you can see the creature’s true form, but you don’t end the spell.
