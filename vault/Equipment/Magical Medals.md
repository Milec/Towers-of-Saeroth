# Magical Medals  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Usage** worn

---

Military leaders or heads of state award these special medals to commend exemplary performance by their top soldiers. They’re typically worn on a special strip of fabric near the lapel, but soldiers from different countries sometimes wear them in other places. No matter how many _magical medals_ you have, they collectively count as one invested item.

## Medal of Gorilla’s Might  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Price** 25 gp

---

This steel medal resembles the head and upper body of a [[Bestiary/Gorilla (Monster Core)|gorilla]]. It’s typically given in recognition of physical prowess, and it grants you a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], and [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]].

**Activate—Gorila's Second Leap** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would fail but not critically fail an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]];  **Effect** The gorilla on the medal beats its chest, giving you another chance. You reroll the failed check.

## Medal of Griffon’s Heart  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Price** 80 gp

---

This copper medal features a [[Bestiary/Griffon (Monster Core)|griffon’s]] face, wings, and talons in profile. It is given in recognition of remarkable bravery, and it grants you a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. While wearing this pendant, you can also cast [[Spells/Forbidding Ward (Player Core)|_forbidding ward_]] as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] cantrip.

## Medal of the Wolf Pack  *Item 7*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Price** 350 gp

---

There are three [[Bestiary/Wolf (Monster Core)|wolf’s]] heads engraved on this pewter medal, typically awarded to squads who demonstrate exceptional teamwork. While wearing the _medal of the wolf pack_, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to damage rolls against enemies you are [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]].

## Medal of Unicorn’s Purity  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Price** 1,700 gp

---

The profile of the [[Bestiary/Families/Unicorn|unicorn]] on this silver medal has a horn that extends slightly beyond the circular border. This medal is awarded in recognition of true righteousness and exemplary service to a cause.

**Activate—Unicorn’s Grace** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You would regain Hit Points from a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect;  **Effect** You regain an additional 4d6 Hit Points.

## Medal of Phoenix’s Fire  *Item 16*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 151

**Price** 9,000 gp

---

The [[Bestiary/Phoenix (Monster Core)|phoenix]] on this gold medal is proud and fierce, and the medal features a border shaped like a flame. This medal is considered the highest honor in many countries and is awarded to those soldiers who have truly exemplified the ethics of their country through unbelievable service.

**Activate—Phoenix’s Sacrifice** free action ([[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition increases;  **Effect** The phoenix bursts into flames. You lose the dying condition and regain 1 Hit Point. Your [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] value does not increase. You can use this action while [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].
