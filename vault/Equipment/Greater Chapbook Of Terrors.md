This lurid pamphlet contains spooky tales about [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|ghosts]], monsters, and murderers, all illustrated in gruesome detail. The stories, fortunately, are only fictions… or are they?

**Activate—Horrify** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to Cast a prepared Spell with the [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] trait, creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] by the spell also take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]] as they dwell on your terrifying tale.
