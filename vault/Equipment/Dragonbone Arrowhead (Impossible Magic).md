# Dragonbone Arrowhead  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 224

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** affixed to a melee weapon or a ranged weapon with the thrown trait

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Requirements** You’re an expert with the affixed weapon.

---

This arrowhead, carved from dragon bone, hangs off the shaft of your weapon. When you activate this talisman, until the end of the current turn, the affixed weapon gains the [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown 20 feet]] weapon trait, and when you make a thrown [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with it, it flies back to your hand after the Strike completes. If your hands are full when the weapon returns, it falls to the ground in your space.
