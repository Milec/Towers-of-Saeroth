# Unfathomable Stargazer  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 193

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

An _unfathomable stargazer_ is a handheld brass telescope etched with constellations, the patterns of which form bizarre, shifting runes. You can observe the night sky with an _unfathomable stargazer_, however, the constellations you glimpse through the telescope distort with expanding and contracting fields of darkness among them. You use [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] instead of [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Astronomy Lore]] when observing the skies with this item, and gain a +2 item bonus on all checks to do so. Once you use it, it fuses to you, and you must succeed at a DC 35 Will save to use another device to observe the stars, including your naked eyes. The telescope imposes a –4 circumstance penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or navigate. If you critically fail this Survival check, you’re subjected to [[Spells/Warp Mind (Player Core)|_warp mind_]] (DC 34) as you glimpse something horrifying and alien amid the darkness.
