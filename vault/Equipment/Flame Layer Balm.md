Slathering this reddish-gold balm over a suit of armor creates a swirling pattern of flame-like licks and curls. The balm remains on the armor for 1 hour, and the first time during that time that the wearer takes fire damage, the balm bursts into flame and the wearer gains the effects of the _[[Spells/Fiery Body (Player Core)|_fiery body_]]_ spell for 1 minute.
