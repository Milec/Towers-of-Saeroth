# Glimmering Missive  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Missive (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Missive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 85

**Price** 20 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

two actions

envision, Interact

---

A _glimmering missive_ sparkles as you compose it. When activated, it explodes, disintegrating into a shower of multicolored motes in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] from a corner of the missive’s space. Creatures in the area are covered in sparkling dust that remains luminous for 1 hour. Visible creatures can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] while covered by the luminous dust; any [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures are concealed while covered in the luminous dust, rather than being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]].
