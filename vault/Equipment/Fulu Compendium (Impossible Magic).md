# Fulu Compendium  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 221

**Price** 550 gp

**Hands** 1

---

This pamphlet-sized book records the various symbols seen on fulus and also contains some ready-made magic on a special page that restores itself every day at sunrise. Using the compendium as a reference grants a +2 item bonus to any skill checks to determine a fulu’s function or authenticity.

**Activate—Paralyzing Page** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You rip a page from the fulu compendium and cast it in a wide arc; as it flies in that arc, it multiplies into a storm of fulus. All [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] are affected by a _spirit-sealing fulu_ with a save DC of 25.
