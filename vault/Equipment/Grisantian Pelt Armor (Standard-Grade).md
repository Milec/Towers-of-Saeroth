# Grisantian Pelt Armor  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk**  varies by armor

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Grisantian Pelt]]

---

Grisantian pelt armor is immune to [[Traits/Fire|fire]] damage, and its Hardness is doubled against piercing or slashing damage. Wearing armor made from grisantian pelt also grants you a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC and saving throws against attacks and spells that deal fire damage.

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 35
