# Reflecting Shard  *Item 13+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 94

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

⤾

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You are targeted by a spell of 5th rank or lower

**Requirement** You're a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], and you have the affixed shield [[Actions/Raise a Shield|raised]].

---

This mirrored metal fragment is bolted or welded to the face of the affixed shield. When you Activate it, you attempt to reflect the triggering spell back at its caster with [[Spells/Spell Riposte|_spell riposte_]], using your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check. The talisman’s counteract rank is 7th.
