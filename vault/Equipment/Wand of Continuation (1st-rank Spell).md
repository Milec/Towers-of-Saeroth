# Wand of Continuation  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Wand (Player Core)|Wand]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 282

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This wand increases a spell’s duration. Yellow embers spiral over its surface until the spell ends.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;  **Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge; the activation takes two actions if the spell normally takes single action to cast, or three actions if the spell normally takes two actions;   **Effect** You Cast the Spell, and its duration is increased by half.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of a spell of the appropriate rank. The spell must have a casting time of single action or two actions and a duration no less than 10 minutes and no greater than 1 hour.
