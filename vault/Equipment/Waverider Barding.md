# Waverider Barding  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Companion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 55

**Price** 130 gp

**Usage** worn barding

**Bulk** L

---

This [[Rules/Barding|light barding]] is covered in wavelike patterns. The barding adjusts to fit your animal companion regardless of its shape.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You trace a finger along the wave patterns on the barding, granting your companion a swim Speed of 30 feet for 10 minutes. If your companion already had a swim Speed, it gains a +10-foot item bonus to its swim Speed for 10 minutes instead. Even if the companion doesn't have the mount special ability, it can still Swim while being ridden.
