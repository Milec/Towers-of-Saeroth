# Necklace of Strangulation  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 91

**Usage** worn necklace

**Bulk** L

---

This beautiful necklace appears to be a magical item such as a [[Equipment/Necklace of Fireballs|_necklace of fireballs_]] or a [[Equipment/Pendant of the Occult|_pendant of the occult_]]. When invested, it magically locks in place, suspiciously tightly, and fuses to you. When you enter a situation of extreme stress (as determined by the GM), the necklace tightens around your neck, [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocating]] you and dealing 30 bludgeoning damage to you at the end of each of your turns. Once per round, you can spend a single action to attempt a DC 34 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check or Fortitude save; success means you don’t take the damage on your current turn, but you continue suffocating. The necklace loosens after you’ve been dead for a month.
