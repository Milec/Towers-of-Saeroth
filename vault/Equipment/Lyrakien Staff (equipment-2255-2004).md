# Lyrakien Staff  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 133

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A crystalline sphere, swirling with constantly shifting constellations, sits atop a _lyrakien staff_, a silver shaft that sparkles with the gentle glow of starlight. [[Setting/Deities/Desna|Desnans]] first created the staves, inspired by the music- and freedom-loving [[Bestiary/Lyrakien|lyrakien azatas]], but these staves are popular with spellcasters of all faiths who like travel, art, or the stars. While wielding a _lyrakien staff_, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on saving throws against [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] effects.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
