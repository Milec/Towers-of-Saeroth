---

The stopper for a _potion of expeditious retreat_ is crafted to easily snap open in dire circumstances. When you drink this potion, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 minute, and you gain a +40-foot status bonus to all your Speeds for the duration as long as you are fleeing. You immediately Stride.
