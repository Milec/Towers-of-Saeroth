# Potion of Minute Echoes  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 175

**Price** 125 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

No matter how carefully you open this bottle, it always lets out an audible, echoing pop. For 1 minute after drinking this potion, you gain a +2 status bonus to Perception checks to hear. In addition, each time you [[Actions/Seek]], your hearing becomes a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]] sense until the beginning of your next turn, allowing you to pinpoint creatures' locations and otherwise perceive the world in detail by listening to the sound of echoes.
