# Dragonscale Staff  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 36

**Price** 240 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Bo Staff]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|bo staff]]_ is covered in scales shed by or harvested from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]. When benefiting from the circumstance bonus to AC granted by the bo staff’s [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait, you also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], according to the type of damage dealt by the breath weapon of the dragon that provided the scales; for instance, a staff made with scales taken from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Rime|white dragon]] would provide cold resistance. When this resistance prevents you from taking damage, the _dragonscale staff_ deals an additional 1 damage of the type matching its resistance for 1 round.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include scales from a dragon with a breath weapon that deals acid, cold, electricity, fire, or poison damage.
