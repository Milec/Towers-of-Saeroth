# Silvertongue Mutagen  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 550

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Your features become striking and your voice becomes musical and commanding, though facts and figures become hazy for you and emotion clouds your ability to reason.

 **Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], and [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks. Your critical failures with any of these skills become failures instead.

 **Drawback** You take a –2 item penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] checks. Choose one skill in which you are trained; for the duration, you become untrained in that skill. All your failures on checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] become critical failures.
