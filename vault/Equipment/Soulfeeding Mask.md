# Soulfeeding Mask  *Item 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -205- Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man|Pathfinder #205: Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man]] pg. 85

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn mask

---

This unsettling mask is made from different flensed faces that have been stitched together with bright red thread. Wearing a _soulfeeding mask_ allows you to see others’ souls within their bodies as long as they’re within 60 feet, as if under the effects of [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]] and [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] (but only against living or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures). Your [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] DC increases by 10 against living creatures who [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] undead creatures or against undead creatures who Impersonate living creatures, as you can tell if the creature’s soul has been affected by undeath. The _soulfeeding mask’s_ [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] rank is 9, with a counteract modifier of +31.

While wearing a _soulfeeding mask_, you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]].

If you’re [[Traits/Holy|holy]], you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 while you have a _soulfeeding_ mask [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|invested]].

**Activate—Devour Soul** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _soulfeeding mask’s_ eyes and jaw open wide, and clouds of darkness and shadow spill out and attempt to engulf a single creature you can see that’s within 30 feet. That creature takes 50 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage with a DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save. If the target is undead or otherwise has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]], this activation loses the death and void traits and gains the vitality trait, and the target takes 50 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage with a DC 36 basic Fortitude save. If the target takes any damage, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] 1 for 1 minute and can use the extra action each round for only [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] actions, unless the damage killed the creature, in which case you can also use the extra action each round for [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] actions as well.

**Activate—Disgorge Soul** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])   **Requirements** You’re quickened as a result of Devour Soul; **Effect** The _soulfeeding mask_ disgorges the devoured soul. You’re no longer quickened from Devour Soul, and the soulfeeding mask casts [[Spells/Duplicate Foe (Player Core)|_duplicate foe_]] (DC 36 Fortitude save) to your specifications, targeting the creature whose soul had been devoured. You must [[Actions/Sustain an Effect|Sustain this effect]], unless the target creature was slain by Devour Soul, in which case duplicate foe functions as if the creature had failed its save, and the _soulfeeding mask_ Sustains this spell automatically.
