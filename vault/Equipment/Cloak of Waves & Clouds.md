# Cloak of Waves & Clouds  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 128

**Price** 38,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This magical cloak was crafted from the feathers and scales of a legendary giant fish that could transform into a resplendent bird.

**Activate—Cut Air and Sea** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** The cloak ripples, becoming either giant eagle feathers or iridescent fish scales. Until you next [[Actions/Activate an Item|Activate]] the cloak, you gain either a [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|swim]] Speed of 60 feet and the ability to breathe underwater or a [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|fly]] Speed of 40 feet.
