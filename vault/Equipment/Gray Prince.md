# Gray Prince  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 59

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Hand Cannon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=193)

---

While nothing created by the Ash Cult could be considered mass-produced, this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] ruinous [hand cannon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=193)_  is by far their most popular design. The sleek body and intricate carving work make it a favorite accessory of certain aristocrats. The carving isn't merely decorative, however. Rather than a traditional maker's mark, Ornmarr etches excerpts of unreadable text onto each creation, hoping that one day his weapons will sing to another as the silver crystal once sang to him.

**Activate** two actions envision, INteract;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You fire a bolt of negative energy, dealing 2d8 negative damage to all creatures in a 60 foot line (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex saving throw). Creatures who critically fail their saving throw also take 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent negative damage]] for 1 minute.
