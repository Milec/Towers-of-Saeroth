# Alchemical Gauntlet  *Item 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 62

**Price** 10 gp

---

An alchemical gauntlet emits small alchemical detonations when it makes contact with a foe. As an Interact action, you can place a bomb into a metal bracket near the wrist of the gauntlet. The bomb must be one that deals energy damage, such as an [[Equipment/Acid Flask (Player Core 2)|acid flask]], [[Equipment/Alchemist's Fire (Player Core 2)|alchemist’s fire]], [[Equipment/Blasting Stone|blasting stone]], [[Equipment/Bottled Lightning (Player Core 2)|bottled lightning]], or [[Equipment/Frost Vial (Player Core 2)|frost vial]]. The next three attacks made with the gauntlet deal 1d4 damage of the bomb’s damage type in addition to the gauntlet’s normal damage. If the second and third attacks aren’t all made within 1 minute of the first attack, the bomb’s energy is wasted. These attacks never deal [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage or other special effects of the [[Traits/Bomb (Player Core)|bomb]] and aren’t modified by any abilities that add to or modify a bomb’s effect.
