# Storyteller's Opus  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 163

**Price** 230 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This green spellbook with gold trim contains a cautionary story about a boy who was eaten by a wolf after he previously lied about wolf attacks. When opened, the engravings on the front seem to move in a loop, enacting this story.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature succeeds, but doesn't critically succeed, at a Perception check to disbelieve an [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]] spell you prepared from this grimoire;  **Effect** You quickly tell a fib to try to smooth over the inconsistencies in your illusion. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check against the triggering creature's Perception DC. If you succeed, the creature doesn't disbelieve the illusion.
