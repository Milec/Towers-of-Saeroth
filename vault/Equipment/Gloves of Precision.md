# Gloves of Precision  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 50

**Usage** worn gloves

**Access** You're from Hermea

---

These fingerless gloves increase the agility and sensitivity of the hands, enabling the wearer to perform more delicate, precise work. While wearing the _gloves of precision,_ you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], and to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable a Device]] and [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]].

**Activate—Nimble Fingers** free action ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a skill check to Climb, Disable a Device, or Pick a Lock;  **Effect** You roll the skill check twice and take the better result.
