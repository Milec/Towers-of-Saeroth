# Unholy Plate  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 278

**Price** 2,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

---

Crafted from black iron, this crude suit of _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ is designed to make you look like a horned demon with your face peering out of the screaming maw of the beast.

While wearing the armor, you can attack with the helmet's horns. They're a martial melee weapon with the effects of a _+2 [[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|weapon potency rune]]_. They deal 2d8 piercing damage and have the [[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly]] d12 and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits. On a critical hit with the horns, the target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (or drained 2 on a critical failure). The horns can't be etched with any runes.

 If you're [[Traits/Holy|holy]], you're drained 2 and can't recover from this condition while wearing unholy plate.

**Activate—Demonic Slip** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]].

**Craft Requirements** You’re unholy; supply one casting of [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]].
