# Wet Shock Snare  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 27

**Price** 25 gp

---

A hidden copper plate conceals a bag of electric eels. When a creature steps on the plate, the eels become agitated, and the creature takes 4d8 electricity damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, the current causes the creature to become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 2]]. The eels are unharmed after use, but the mechanism is broken as usual for snares.
