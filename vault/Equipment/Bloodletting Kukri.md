# Bloodletting Kukri  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 600

**Price** 240 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri]]

---

This _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Kukri|kukri]]_ has a crimson blade that shimmers eerily in bright light. On a critical hit, the kukri deals 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. If the target didn’t already have persistent bleed damage when you scored the critical hit, you also gain 1d8 temporary Hit Points.
