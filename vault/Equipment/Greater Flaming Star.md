# Flaming Star  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 235

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

A sheen of red crosses the surface of this star-shaped goldstone medallion when the light hits it. The affixed armor or weapon is warm to the touch. The spell attack modifier of any spell cast by Activating this item is +7, and the spell DC is 17.  
**• Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to fire.  
**• Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] spell by Activating the _star_, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 fire damage until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Ignition|_ignition_]]_.
