# Dragonplate  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 278

**Price** 10,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

---

This suit of _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Dragonhide Armor (Player Core 2)|dragonhide]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ makes you look like a fearsome dragon. The armor comes in many different varieties depending on the type of dragon from which it's made, though they usually conform to the four magical traditions.

**Activate—Dragon Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You unleash a 15-foot cone of energy with a damage type and saving throw corresponding to the tradition of the dragon used to make the armor (shown on the table below). The cone deals 14d6 damage; each creature in the area must attempt a DC 36 basic saving throw

##

| Dragon Tradition | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Arcane | Force (Reflex) |
| Divine | Spirit (Reflex) |
| Occult | Mental (Will) |
| Primal | Poison (Fortitude) |

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 1,250 gp of dragonhide.
