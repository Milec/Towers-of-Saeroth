# Scalding Gauntlets  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 124

**Usage** worn gloves

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet]]

---

Prized by naari geniekin who prefer to fight with their fists, _scalding gauntlets_ are a pair of _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Gauntlet|spiked gauntlets]]_. The intricate golden gauntlets are engraved with [[Setting/Languages/Pyric]] writing praising the glories of the Dominion of Flame and embellished with shimmering black and red gemstones. A creature you [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grab]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrain]] while wearing the gauntlets must succeed at a DC 26 Fortitude save or take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] and be [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] from the pain; it's temporarily immune to being sickened by _scalding gauntlets_ for 1 hour.
