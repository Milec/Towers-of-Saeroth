# Phantasmal Doorknob  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 127

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Greater and major versions of _phantasmal doorknob_ are limited, but the standard version is permitted for use._

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

This ornate doorknob can open doors both material and metaphysical, revealing dreams and nightmares. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 20.

- **Armor** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]].
- **Weapon** If you critically succeed at a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the weapon, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Figment|_ghost sound_]].
