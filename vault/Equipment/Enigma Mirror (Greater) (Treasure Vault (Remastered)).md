# Enigma Mirror  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 125

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

Mist fills the glass of this small circular hand mirror, creating strange patterns in the shifting gray wisps. The back of the mirror bears a flowing script engraving in an unknown language. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 23.

- **Armor** After you cast an [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] spell by activating the mirror, choose one ally within 30 feet. That ally is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], as if seen in a misty mirror, until the end of your next turn.
- **Weapon** After you cast an illusion spell by activating the mirror, illusory copies of the weapon swirl in the air around it. You’re affected by the [[Spells/Thicket of Knives|_thicket of knives_]] spell until the end of your next turn. The spell ends if you stop wielding the affixed weapon.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Forbidding Ward|_forbidding ward_]].
