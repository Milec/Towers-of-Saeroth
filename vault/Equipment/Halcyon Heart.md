# Halcyon Heart  *Item 21*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -174- Shadows of the Ancients|Pathfinder #174: Shadows of the Ancients]] pg. 77

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This shekere features carved prayers barely visible under a fine silk net of colorful beads. The handheld percussion instrument grants a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks while playing music with it. In addition, when you [[Actions/Perform]] with it, you can make it heard by any number of creatures within 100 miles. You can specify one or more specific creatures, or otherwise describe those who will hear it, such as all [[Ancestries/Human|humans]], or all inhabitants of a village. You can send a message through the music that the targets understand. [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|Demons]] and creatures connected to demons (such as a creature whose deity is a [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords|demon lord]] or a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-260)|sorcerer]] with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic|demonic bloodline]]) can't easily understand any messages conveyed in this way, even if you wish to send the message to them. They must attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] check against your Performance DC or against DC 40, whichever is higher. If your Performance DC is higher than DC 40 and you want demons to be able to understand the message, you can choose to use DC 40 instead.

**Success** They understand the message.  

**Failure** They don't understand the message but are aware there was one.  

**Critical Failure** They don't realize the sound of the _halcyon heart_ contains a message.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;    **Requirements** The _halcyon heart_ has its net of beads; **Effect** You pull the net off the _halcyon heart_ and throw it at a creature within 20 feet, using your attack bonus for a ranged simple weapon. On a hit, the net grows and envelops the creature, which becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]], and on a critical hit, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until it Escapes. The net also attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effects and planar travel of the creature it's entrapping. The Escape DC is 44, and the net has a counteract level of 9 and a counteract modifier of +38. If the target is a demon or connected to a demon, they must roll twice and take the lower result on all attempts to Escape (this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect), and for counteract checks, the net rolls twice and takes the higher result (this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect). You can't Perform with the _halcyon heart_ while it doesn't have its net. So long as the net doesn't contain a creature, you can restore it with a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.

**Destruction** If the Gorilla King plays a _halcyon heart_ in front of [[Setting/Deities/Angazhan|Angazhan's]] totem, the gourd decomposes, the net frays, and the beads shatter.
