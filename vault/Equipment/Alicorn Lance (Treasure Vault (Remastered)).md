# Alicorn Lance  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 36

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The version of the alicorn lance which does void damage may not be purchased or crafted in Pathfinder Society play._

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance]]

---

This white _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Lance|lance]]_ is made from the horn of a [[Bestiary/Unicorn|unicorn]], willingly granted at the end of its lifetime. In addition to its normal damage, it deals 1 spirit damage on successful attacks. You can make the lance glow like a [[Equipment/Torch|torch]] or suppress its light by using an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You evoke the spirit of the unicorn that donated the _alicorn lance's_ horn, which you ride in a shining charge. Move up to twice your Speed and make a Strike with the _alicorn lance_; you gain the effects of the lance's [[Traits/Jousting|jousting]] trait on this Strike. If you were already mounted when you Activate the horn, the unicorn spirit takes shape around your steed, granting it a +10–foot status bonus to its Speed for the charge.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a horn willingly gifted by a unicorn. In rare instances, an _alicorn lance_ can be made with a forcibly taken horn. However this heinous act pollutes the horn's magic, causing it to glow with a sickly red light; remove the holy trait, add the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait to Strikes instead of the holy trait, and make the unicorn spirit evoked in the charge whinny visibly in pain.
