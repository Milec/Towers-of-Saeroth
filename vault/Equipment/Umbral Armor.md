This dark _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Shadow (GM Core)|shadow]] [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor (Player Core)|studded leather armor]]_ is frequently used to bypass enemy positions or quickly travel around battlefield obstacles as it allows you to partially transit through [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]].

**Activate—Shroud Stride** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You slip into the Netherworld for a moment, allowing you to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] twice your speed. This movement doesn’t trigger reactions and ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (GM Core)|hazardous terrain]]. Shadows lingers on the armor, causing you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] for 1 round afterward.
