# Prognostic Veil  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Focused (GM Core)|Focused]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 310

**Usage** worn mask

---

This gauzy purple veil is embroidered with symbols of divinatory significance. As your curse worsens, the veil ripples in an ever-increasing unseen wind. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] checks.

**Activate—Remember the Future** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a revelation spell. If you don't spend this Focus Point by the end of this turn, it's lost.

**Activate—Twist the Skeins of Fate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** oncer per day; **Trigger** An ally within 30 feet is about to attempt a saving throw; **Requirements** You have the [[Traits/Cursebound|cursebound]] condition; **Effect** The ally gains a status bonus to the saving throw equal to the value of your cursebound condition.

**Craft Requirements** You're an oracle.
