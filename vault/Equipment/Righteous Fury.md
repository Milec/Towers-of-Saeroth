# Righteous Fury  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 130

**Price** 6,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|Longsword]]

---

This gold-plated _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword (Player Core)|longsword]]_ sports an ornate hilt bearing the religious symbol of [[Setting/Deities/Ragathiel (Divine Mysteries)|Ragathiel]], General of Vengeance, and is painstakingly crafted to resemble the divine armaments wielded by the empyreal lord’s celestial soldiers in their battles against the forces of darkness. These weapons are popular among the ranks of those who hold the line against similar enemies, such as the Knights of Lastwall and warriors of the former Mendevian Crusade.

**Activate—Great Vengeance** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** Your last action was a successful melee Strike with _righteous fury_ against a creature with the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait;  **Effect** The sword emits a 20-foot-radius [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of holy fire centered on your target, inflicting 2d8 spirit damage and 2d8 vitality damage to all enemies in the area (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail the save are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round, and those that critically fail are blinded for 1 minute.
