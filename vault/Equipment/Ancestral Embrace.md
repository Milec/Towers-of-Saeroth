# Ancestral Embrace  *Item 26*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -174- Shadows of the Ancients|Pathfinder #174: Shadows of the Ancients]] pg. 76

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor]]

---

This brightly colored _+4 [[Equipment/Resilient|major resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor|leather armor]]_ is inscribed with a spiral that has no end or beginning. Though it is a symbol of the ancient religion of Holy Xatramba, it also serves as a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol|religious symbol]] of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]]. When you invest the armor, you gain negative resistance 25 and can draw on the power of your ancestors. You gain an ancestry feat from your ancestry; the feat can be any level, but you must meet any other prerequisites of the feat. You lose the feat when the investment ends, and you can select a different feat each time you invest the armor again.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You use an ancestry feat that has a limited number of uses per day;  **Effect** You gain an additional use of the ancestry feat. If you do not use it before the next time you make your daily preparations, the use is lost.

**Activate** free action envision;   **Trigger** You begin casting a [[Rituals/Legend Lore|_legend lore_]] ritual to learn legends about your ancestry or about someone of your ancestry;  **Effect** _Ancestral Embrace_ reduces the casting time of this ritual to 1 hour, and you don't need secondary casters for this ritual.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The armor casts [[Spells/Avatar|_avatar_]], granting these specific additional abilities instead of those associated with a deity: Speed 70 feet, [[Spells/Air Walk|_air walk_]], ignore difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain; **Melee** single action spear ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]], [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 50 feet]]), **Damage** 6d6+6 piercing; **Ranged** single action blowgun ([[Traits/Range|range 120 feet]]), **Damage** 1d6+3 piercing plus 5d6 poison.

**Destruction** _Ancestral Embrace_ crumbles if its wearer intentionally and knowingly kills or desecrates the remains of six generations of their family.
