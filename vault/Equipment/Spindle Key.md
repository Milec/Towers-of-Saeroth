# Spindle Key  *Item 17*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Revenge of the Runelords

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -220- Crypt of Runes|Pathfinder #220: Crypt of Runes]] pg. 83

**Price** 14,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This foot-long, spindle-shaped puzzle box is made of otherworldly metal inlaid with a finely traced labyrinth pattern. By manipulating the key’s length, the wider spindle can be caused to open, unfold, and move up and down along the shaft, often in ways that defy geometry. Every _spindle key_ is attuned to one of the prison-spires that float above the Oubliette Plains in the Dreamlands. As a 10-minute activity, a creature can solve a _spindle key_ by attempting a DC 37 [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] check. On a success, that PC can thereafter use both of the _spindle key’s_ activations. On a critical failure, the key’s magic plays havoc with the creature’s spatial awareness. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 24 hours (which can’t be reduced by any means) and can’t attempt to solve the _spindle key_ again until the effect ends.

**Activate—Align Spindle** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]])    **Effect** You rotate the sections of the _spindle key_ into an arrangement of your liking. As you do, the corresponding steel separators between floors of Liralarue’s Oubliette spin to match that arrangement. If a creature is within the ten-foot-space between floors at this time, they take 10d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) from being crushed and are then pushed into one of the adjoining rooms at random as the rotation seals it up.

**Activate—Breach** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You harness the key’s occult geometry to bypass a lock within 30 feet. You attempt to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick the Lock]], using [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] for the check. You can spend a Mythic Point to attempt the check at mythic proficiency.
