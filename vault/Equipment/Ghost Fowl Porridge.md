---

This robust porridge—made from a cockatrice distilled into a broth, hot peppers from a demonic source, and various toppings—causes diners to utter a ghostly wail from the intense heat. Consuming the porridge grants you a +2 item bonus to saving throws against being [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]] for 1 hour. You also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 3 against physical damage for the duration.
