# Hydra Spear  *Item 8*

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|Longspear]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longspear (Player Core)|longspear]]_ of polished white bone features a tip of layered reptilian scales, compressed to form a sharp edge. Akki crafted this spear herself after she and her allies tangled with a [[Bestiary/Families/Hydra|hydra]] plaguing the marshes outside Aelyosos. She pressed the scales of the creature down into a spearpoint mostly through sheer stubbornness, granting it a unique ability.

**Activate—Hydra Swipe** two actions ;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The spearhead unravels and elongates into a group of snake heads. Make up to three melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the _Hydra Spear_, each at a different creature within 20 feet. These attacks do not count toward your multiple attack penalty until all attacks have been resolved.
