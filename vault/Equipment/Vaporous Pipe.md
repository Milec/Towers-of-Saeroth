# Vaporous Pipe  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 116

**Price** 340 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

This hand-polished pipe is carved from oak and releases small wisps of smoke even when unlit. As long as you’re holding a _vaporous pipe_, you don’t take a circumstance penalty to Perception checks due to thick smoke, and you can’t suffocate from smoke and heated air (such as within a wildfire). As long as you are holding the pipe, you also gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to fire equal to half your level.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You draw on the pipe and then blow a massive cloud of smoke that fills a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that includes your space. All creatures within the smoke cloud are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from each other and from creatures outside the smoke, though you can still see clearly within it. The smoke dissipates after 3 rounds, or after 1 round if subjected to a strong wind.
