# Mirrorblade  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 209

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [Khopesh](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=476)

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Keen (GM Core)|keen]] [khopesh](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=476)_ is crafted from a nearly flawless reflective material, making its surfaces glimmer with a mesmerizing shine.

**Activate—Mirage Guard** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You wave the _mirrorblade_ around your head to create illusory distortions around you, making it difficult for opponents to judge your true position. For the next 10 minutes, you aren’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to melee attacks from creatures [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] you.

**Activate—Scryshield** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _mirrorblade_’s reflective surface dulls, and you and all your possessions are surrounded by a shimmering field that makes you [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] to scrying magic for 1 hour. In addition, you also learn about any scrying effects within a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (as _detect scrying_). If you sheathe, drop, or attack with the _mirrorblade_, this effect ends early.
