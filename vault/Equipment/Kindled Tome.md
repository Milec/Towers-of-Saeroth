A _kindled tome_ is a [[Equipment/Book of Lingering Blaze|_book of lingering blazes_]] awakened to sapience and imbued with an enthusiasm for fire. It encourages you to learn new fire spells to inscribe within its pages. When you attempt a skill check to [[Actions/Learn a Spell (Player Core)|Learn a Spell]] to add a spell with the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait to the kindled tome, you treat your result as one step better than you rolled. The tome can be disparaging about other spells as “a waste of good page space,” especially if those spells have the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] or [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] traits. In addition to the activation of a _book of lingering blazes_, a kindled tome has the following activations.

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You Cast a spell with the fire trait;  **Effect** The tome makes the flames linger, dealing 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] to all you hit with or who fail the saving throw against the effect (doubling on a critical success on a spell attack and on a critical failure on a saving throw).

**Activate** two actions command, interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The tome casts [[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]] at 5th level to your specifications.
