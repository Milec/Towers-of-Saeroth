# Rhino Hide  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 558

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor]]

---

This _+1 resilient [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor|hide armor]]_ is made from rhinoceros hide. It has an armor check penalty of –1 instead of –3. When you use the [[Feats/Sudden Charge]] class feat while wearing this armor, your Strike deals an additional 1d8 damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 320 gp of rhinoceros hide (a common material).
