# Heart Bloodstone of Arazni  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 109

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

The _Heart Bloodstone of Arazni_ represents protection. While holding the _Heart Bloodstone_, you can feel the gentle rhythm of a beating heart within the jar.

**Activate—Raise Jar** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You hold the jar aloft, and a nimbus of crimson energy surrounds your body. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

**Activate—Proclaim Resilence** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You worship [[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]] or are favored by her; **Effect** You make a loud and clear proclamation of your resilience (such as “I will not fall to the hands of my enemies!”). You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to void damage equal to your level until the beginning of your next turn.

**Destruction** The _Heart Bloodstone_ turns to dust if a worshipper of Arazni is slain while holding the _Bloodstone_ and cursing her name. Any breathing creature within 10 feet of the jar inhales the choking dust, taking 4d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failure, the creature also takes 4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]] (8 persistent void damage on a critical failure).
