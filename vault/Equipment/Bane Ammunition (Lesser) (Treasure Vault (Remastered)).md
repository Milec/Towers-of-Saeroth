Monster hunters favor bane ammunition that contains a capsule of reagents tailored to a particular type of creature— [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]], [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Beast|beast]], [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]], [[Traits/Fey|fey]], [[Traits/Giant|giant]], [[Traits/Ooze|ooze]], or both [[Traits/Fungus|fungus]] and [[Traits/Plant|plant]]. Each type requires a different formula. When activated bane ammunition hits a target that has a trait matching the selected type, it takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] in addition to the damage the attack normally deals.
