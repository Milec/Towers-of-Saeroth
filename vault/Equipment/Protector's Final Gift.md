**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier (Player Core)|Rapier]]

---

Within hives of [[Bestiary/Families/Gigantic Bee|giant bees]], some become protectors of their hive and etch runes onto their stingers via dance. Upon such a bee's death, its stinger can be removed from its body and used as _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier (Player Core)|rapier]]_, though a hilt often needs to be attached for skilled swordsmanship. The imbued magic allows the stinger to slowly renew a small bit of its venom supply. The wielder can gather this and deliver it during a Strike. The victim is made to see prophetic flashes of their future as it courses through their system.

**Activate—Apiprophecy Sting** free action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The protector's final gift secrets apiprophecy venom, which coats the blade and affects the next creature successfully struck with the blade within the next minute.

**Apiprophecy Venom** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 20 Will; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d6 mental damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]]
