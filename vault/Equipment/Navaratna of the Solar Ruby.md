# Navaratna of the Solar Ruby  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 131

**Usage** worn

---

These nine flawless gems are set within a golden necklace, their centerpiece a holy ruby pulled from the center of a sun. So long as you are wearing the navaratna, you ignore all [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|environmental effects due to temperature]], do not take penalties due to [[Rules/Wind (GM Core)|wind]], and ignore all damage and effects from droughts, [[Rules/Floods (GM Core)|floods]], and [[Rules/Earthquakes (GM Core)|earthquakes]]. You are also immune to damage from [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starvation]]. The navaratna does not otherwise grant you resistance against damage (such as [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] or [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage).

**Activate—Sutra of the Flawless Servant** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per hour;; **Trigger** An ally within 30 feet fails a saving throw against an [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|temperature]] environmental effect;  **Effect** You offer a prayer for your divine protection to extend to your companion. The creature rerolls the triggering saving throw with a +2 item bonus. They must take the new result, even if it is worse.

**Destruction** The navaratna must be swallowed by a fiendish [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] [[Bestiary/Lion (Monster Core)|lion]]; the lion must then by killed by drowning in a lake of [[Bestiary/Families/Naga (Monster Core)|naga]] venom and left to rot for a year. At the stroke of midnight on the final day, the artifact is destroyed.
