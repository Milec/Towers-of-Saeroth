# Enkindled Quintessence  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Contract]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 227

---

You have fully subsumed your soul within that of a dragon’s soul, overloading your very essence with eldritch potential. You immediately retrain your class to that of a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-979)|sorcerer]] with the [[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|draconic bloodline]], with your draconic exemplar matching your bound dragon. You are able to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast spells]] immediately as if you had a full 8 hours of rest and completed your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]]; however, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 from the strain of igniting your soul’s magical potential.

Once per day, from any distance, the dragon can either [[Spells/Teleport (Player Core)|_teleport_]] you to their lair or teleport to switch places with you. This is a 3-action activity with the teleportation trait and the dragon’s tradition trait.

---

**Oathbreaker’s Calamity** Should either party break the oath, both your souls begin to immolate through your physical forms. If either of you casts a spell, they immediately become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 3 unless they’re casting it to harm the other party. If either of you die as a result of this calamity, you rise as a [[Bestiary/Families/Wraith|wraith]] (if it’s you) or a [[Bestiary/Families/Wyrmwraith|wyrmwraith]] (if it’s the dragon).
