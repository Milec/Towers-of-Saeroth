# Vortex Pouch  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -217- Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea|Pathfinder #217: Death Sails a Wine-Dark Sea]] pg. 78

**Price** 240 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

When empty, the _vortex pouch’s_ net seems too loose to hold much. However, when used to catch the wind, it stretches to hold the air. With nowhere for the wind to go, the air inside it swirls violently. If the caught wind includes a storm, clouds, [[Hazards/Weather/Fog|fog]], or similar effect, it’s visible among the whirlwind.

**Activate—Catch Wind** two actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _vortex pouch_ is empty; **Effect** You gather the nearby air and wind within the _vortex pouch_. Creatures within a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save or be moved 5 feet closer toward you as the air rushes into the pouch. Targets that critically fail the save are moved 10 feet. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] the activation once per round, up to 1 minute, to continue Catching Wind. If you spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]] when Catching Wind, the vortex pouch gathers air in a 10-mile radius; though the vortex is only strong enough to forcibly suck in creatures within the original 20-foot emanation, weaker creatures with the air trait and creatures associated with the sky in the 10-mile radius will know your general direction and feel a sense of unease, granting you a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks against those creatures for 1 week. When you end the activation, the vortex pouch becomes 1 Bulk and you can empty it using Release Wind.

**Activate—Release Wind** two actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** The _vortex pouch_ contains air or wind; **Effect** Wind rushes out of the _vortex pouch_ in a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. All creatures in the cone take 8d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). Creatures that fail their saving throw are pushed 5 feet away from you; creatures that critically fail are pushed 10 feet. If the caught wind included weather that might deal a different type of damage, Release Wind deals this damage instead (for instance, dealing [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] damage if it caught a thundercloud or [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage if it caught a [[Hazards/Weather/Blizzard|blizzard]]).
