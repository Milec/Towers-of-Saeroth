# Chopping Evisceration Snare  *Item 14*

An almost-impossible number of axes spring out at a target with lethal force. When a creature enters the snare's square, it's nearly buried beneath a storm of sharpened metal, which deals 16d8 slashing damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex).
