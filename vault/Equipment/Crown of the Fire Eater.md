# Crown of the Fire Eater  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 110

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

A wreath of flames dances around the rim of this golden crown. You gain resistance 5 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]].

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take fire damage;  **Effect** You absorb some of the flame that would harm you. Increase your fire resistance from the crown from 5 to 15. Just after taking any remaining fire damage, you regain a number of Hit Points equal to 15 or the fire damage dealt by the triggering attack before damage resistance, whichever is less.
