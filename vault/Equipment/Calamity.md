# Calamity  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 212

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|Greatsword]]

---

Hewn from a solid block of ensorcelled stone, this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|greatsword]]_ is monumental in heft as well as destructive force. The weapon is named for the disbelieving leaders of the Seventh Army of Exploration, who denied the power of such a weapon and claimed their losses came from natural disasters. When wielded against creatures that are larger than you, the sword gains the [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]] trait.

**Activate—Shatter Legions** two actions ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You smash the sword down upon the ground with the anger of a conquered land and a subjugated people. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 4d12 piercing damage with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save as the earth shatters beneath their feet. Until the beginning of your next turn, you gain the benefits of [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|standard cover]] from the broken soil around you.
