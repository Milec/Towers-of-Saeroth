# Skinsaw Mask  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 155, [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 76

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** worn mask

**Bulk** L

---

A patchwork of [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] flesh makes up a _skinsaw mask_, which is stitched together with black silk or wire. It is distinctive for its bulbous orange eye—crafted from a magical glass bauble—and wide row of teeth. When worn, the mask amplifies your ability to sense fear in other creatures. You know the value of the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition of any observed creature, and you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] frightened creatures. Whenever you deal precision damage to a frightened creature, you deal 1 additional precision damage. If you aren’t [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you are [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 2 while wearing a _skinsaw mask_.
