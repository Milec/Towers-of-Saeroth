# Mythic Striking  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 149

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

This weapon is filled with unmatched destructive power. The weapon deals five weapon damage dice.

**Activate—Unstoppable Devastation** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You roll the weapon damage dice for a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with this weapon and do not like the result;  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]] and reroll your weapon damage dice, taking the higher of the two results.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic striking runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character capable of making a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).
