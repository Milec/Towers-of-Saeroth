# Ghost Courier Fulu  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 77

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Activate**

two actions

command, Interact

---

The inscription on a _ghost courier fulu_ leaves space for a message, and a prominent red stamp indicates which _ghost delivery fulu_ this fulu homes in on (see below). When you Activate this fulu, you dictate a message up to 25 words long that then magically appears on the paper in the language you spoke. The fulu then disappears into the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]], arriving at the assigned _ghost delivery fulu_ in 2d10 hours, provided that fulu is within 500 miles. There, the fulu’s magic dissipates, but the message remains. If the fulu takes any damage in transit, it has a 50% chance to drop back into the [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Material Plane]], intact but bereft of magic.
