# Quill of Passage  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 117

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All characters have access to this item_

**Price** 1,200 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Access** Member of the Pathfinder Society.

---

This black-feathered quill has a glowing nib with a small amount of glowing ink inside it.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Placing the tip of the quill against a wall of wood, plaster, or stone and speaking a command word causes the ink to flow from the nib onto the wall in the shape of a glowing doorway, casting [[Spells/Passwall|_passwall_]] on the touched surface.
