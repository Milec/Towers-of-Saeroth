# Watcher's Armband  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Detection]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 153

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** worn armband

---

Soldiers who wear this burgundy armband serve as law enforcement within the ranks of a nation’s military, seeking out those who would commit crimes while in uniform. While wearing the armband, you gain a +2 item bonus to your Perception DC against [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to you. In addition, you can cast [[Equipment/Ring of Truth|ring of truth]] once per day as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] 3rd-rank [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell.

**Activate—Find the Plant** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Sometimes people aren’t in control of their minds. You activate your armband, which, for the next minute, suddenly glows red if anyone within 30 feet of you is under the effect of a [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect that is controlling their mind or body (such as [[Spells/Dominate (Player Core)|_dominate_]]).
