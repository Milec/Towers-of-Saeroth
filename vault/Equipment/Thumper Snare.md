# Thumper Snare  *Item 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 79

**Price** 10 gp

---

This small box contains a clockwork mechanism that rhythmically thumps the ground, allowing creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] to detect it at up to double their usual range. The mechanism can be wound to thump anywhere from 1 round to 1 minute before falling into useless components.
