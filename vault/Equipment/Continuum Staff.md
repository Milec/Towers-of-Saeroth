# Continuum Staff  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 238

**Price** 9,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A looped band of copper, winding in on itself, sits atop the head of this shiny metal staff. The staff’s surface seems to warp unpredictably when viewed, the reflections across the shiny surface of its length never forming the same pattern twice.

**Activate—Restitch Time** ⤾ ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You would fail or critically fail an attack roll, saving throw, or skill check;  **Effect** You briefly reverse time to fix your error. Reroll the triggering check, taking the better of the two results.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** _[[Spells/Warp Step|_warp step_]]_
- **1st** _[[Spells/Déjà Vu (Player Core 2)|_déjà vu_]]_, _[[Spells/Synchronize|_synchronize_]]_
- **2nd** [[Spells/Unrelenting Gravity|_unrelenting gravity_]]
- **3rd** _[[Spells/Haste (Player Core)|_haste_]]_, _inertia [[Spells/Lock (Player Core)|_lock_]]_, _[[Spells/Time Jump|_time jump_]]_
- **4th** _[[Spells/Flicker|_flicker_]]_, _[[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]], unrelenting gravity_
- **5th** _[[Spells/Rewinding Step|_rewinding step_]]_, [[Spells/Space Fold Gate|_space fold _gate_]]_, _translocate_
- **6th** _[[Spells/Cast into Time|_cast into time_]]_, _[[Spells/Collective Transposition (Player Core 2)|_collective transposition_]]_
- **7th** _[[Spells/Infinitesimal Collapse Field|_infinitesimal collapse field_]]_, [[Spells/Space Fold Gate|_space fold gate_]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
