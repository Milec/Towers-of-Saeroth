# Restful Sleep Fulu  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 220

**Price** 32 gp

**Usage** affixed to a creature

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Duration** 1 hour

---

This fulu depicts the five-spoke wheel of [[Setting/Deities/Qi Zhong (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Qi Zhong]], god of magic and medicine, and burns away slowly like incense. If you fall asleep with the fulu attached to you and during the fulu’s duration, you regain double the amount of Hit Points you usually gain from resting. You also gain a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects that occur in your dreams, such as the _nightmare_ spell, for as long as you remain asleep.
