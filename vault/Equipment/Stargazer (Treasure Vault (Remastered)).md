# Stargazer  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Scrying]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 35

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Club]]

---

When you invest this [[Equipment/Crystal Ball|_clear quartz crystal ball_]], it orbits your head like an [[Equipment/Aeon Stone|_aeon stone_]]. As long as you have the _stargazer_ invested, you can use an Interact action to direct it to orbit one of your hands where you can telekinetically smash the orb into foes, wielding it as a _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]]_. While you're directing the _stargazer_, your hand is full, and you can send it back to your head with another Interact action. On a critical hit, the _stargazer_ pulses with hypnotic starlight, [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzling]] the struck creature for 1 round. A stargazer doesn't add [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effects]].
