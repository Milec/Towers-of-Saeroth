# Soul Chain  *Item 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Crown of the Kobold King

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 117

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain]]

---

A soul chain is a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain|spiked chain]]_ that has become cursed after a [forge-spurned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=473) used it to kill a creature and trap its soul. In the hands of a forge-spurned, a soul chain inflicts an additional 1d6 fire damage on a hit. Other creatures can use a _soul chain_ as a standard _+1 striking spiked chain_. A _soul chain_ fuses with its wielder the first time the wielder damages a living creature with the weapon. A character who dies while carrying a _soul chain_ must attempt a DC 20 Will save; on a failure, they're immediately transformed into a forge-spurned. Carrying multiple soul chains doesn't increase the DC of the save, but the carrier must attempt the save once for each chain they carry.
