# Boots of Dancing  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 90

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

These boots act as [[Equipment/Boots of Elvenkind|_greater boots of elvenkind_]], but they react wildly to strong physical exertion. While you wear the boots, the curse activates whenever you attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check or Stride more than once in a single round during an encounter. The boots cast an 8th-level [[Spells/Uncontrollable Dance|_uncontrollable dance_]] spell on you, and you automatically fail your save. Once the curse has activated for the first time, the boots fuse to you.
