# Lazybones Pendant  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 211

**Price** 130 gp

**Usage** worn

---

A _lazybones pendant_ is a six-sided die pendant carved from the regretful bones of Work-Too-Hard Whappa, a goblin unusually industrious in life and said to be making up for a long-overdue rest in his afterlife. Depicted on the five sides of this six-sided die are representations of Whappa engaged in various acts of slothful mischief, while the remaining side is utterly blank; apparently, he's so engrossed in his indolence that even his image has forgotten to show up on that side of the die.

 When you wear and invest in this pendant, you gain a +1 item bonus to checks when you [[Actions/Follow the Expert (Player Core)|Follow the Expert]].

**Activate—Can't Somone Else Do it** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Phantasmal Minion|_phantasmal minion_]], summoning an ephemeral version of yourself. When you Activate the pendant and each time you Sustain the spell, your phantasmal minion gains a reaction that can be used only to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] an ally. It uses your skills for any skill checks and can't Aid an attack.
