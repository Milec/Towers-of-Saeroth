# Dreamer's Butterfly  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 242

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

These twin tattoos, each depicting the ornate wing of a butterfly or moth, are usually placed upon your palms or wrists. While sleeping, this tattoo crafts lucid dreams that don’t disturb your rest; However, when danger arises, these dreams shift to alert you to trouble and shroud you in camouflaging dream-stuff. As a result, you do not take any penalty to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks when asleep. When asleep, you also gain a +2 circumstance to your Will saves against effects with the [[Traits/Dream (GM Core)|dream]] or [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]] traits.

**Activate—Wake the Dream** single action ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]])    **Effect** By touching the two halves of the tattoo together, the wings expand to show a small illusory rendition of any effects with the dream or sleep traits you are currently subject to. Onlookers (including yourself) gain a +1 status bonus to checks to Identify Magic of any spells or magical effects with the dream or sleep traits for 1 round.

---

**Special** An adjacent ally can also Activate this effect by touching the two halves of your tattoo together if you are willing or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].
