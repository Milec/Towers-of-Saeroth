# The Avalanche  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -192- Worst of All Possible Worlds|Pathfinder #192: Worst of All Possible Worlds]] pg. 71

**Usage** carried

---

_The Avalanche_ represents unmitigated disasters, destruction that overruns all in its path. As long as _The Avalanche_ is invested, you gain a +1 item bonus to damage rolls with Strikes that inflict bludgeoning damage. This increases to a +2 item bonus if you're at least 14th level, and a +3 item bonus if you're at least 18th level.

**Activate** free action envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You wave the card in the air and hurl staggering volumes of ice, snow, and rocks out into the world. This magical avalanche buries your foes. Choose up to 4 creatures within 60 feet, each of whom must attempt a Reflex save against your class DC. Regardless of their saving throw, the creature's space becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|greater difficult terrain]] from the mounds of ice, snow, and rubble.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 5d8 bludgeoning damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] into an adjacent space of its choice.  

**Failure** The creature takes 10d8 bludgeoning damage, is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 20d8 bludgeoning damage, is knocked prone, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]].
