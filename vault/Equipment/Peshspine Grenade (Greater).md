# Peshspine Grenade  *Item 1+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 77

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

Peshspine grenades are explosive bombs packed with alchemically treated needles from the pesh cactus. A peshspine grenade deals the listed piercing damage and splash damage. On a hit, the target gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition until the end of its next turn.

 Many types of peshpine grenade grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

## Peshspine Grenade (Lesser)  *Item 1*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 77

**Price** 3 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The bomb deals 1d6 piercing damage and 1 piercing splash damage, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]].

## Peshspine Grenade (Moderate)  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 77

**Price** 10 gp

**Bulk** L

---

You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls. The bomb deals 2d6 piercing damage and 2 piercing splash damage, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]].
