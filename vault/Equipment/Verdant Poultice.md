# Verdant Poultice  *Item 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 209

**Price** 27 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

This poultice of herbs and dried berries found throughout Isger was invented by those fighting against Cheliax's diabolic intrusion and those warring against demons at the Worldwound. You can use a verdant poultice on a creature that's taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] from a fiend; attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check with a +1 item bonus against the DC of the effect that caused the persistent damage. On a success, the persistent damage ends.
