# Camouflage Dye  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 52

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Camouflage dye uses a variety of alchemically treated paints and crushed crystals to make the user particularly hard to distinguish from their surroundings. When you Activate the dye by sprinkling it on yourself or a creature within reach, the target and its clothing change colors, blending into their surroundings until the target makes a sudden movement. The target can [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]] without [[Rules/Cover|cover]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] for 10 minutes. If the target uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] or moves at more than half its Speed, after that action is completed, the effects of camouflage powder end and the creature ceases to be [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]].
