# Celestial Staff  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 131

**Price** 14,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Heavenly radiance shines from an active _celestial staff_, a golden staff capped with a pair of sculpted angel’s wings. Used as a weapon, the staff is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_. While wielding a _celestial staff_, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against effects that have the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait and effects created by unholy creatures. When you prepare this staff, if you’re unholy, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 until your next daily preparations.

**Activate** [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]]; **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Bless (Player Core)|_bless_]], [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Everlight|_everlight_]], [[Spells/Inner Radiance Torrent|_inner radiance torrent_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Anointed Ground|_anointed ground_]], _protection_
- **4th** [[Spells/Holy Cascade (Player Core 2)|_holy cascade_]], _inner radiance torrent_
- **5th** [[Spells/Spiritual Guardian (Player Core)|_spiritual guardian_]], [[Spells/Summon Celestial (Player Core)|_summon celestial_]]
- **6th** _holy cascade_, _summon celestial_
- **7th** [[Spells/Frigid Flurry|_ frigid flurry_]], [[Spells/Howling Blizzard|_howling blizzard_]]

**Craft Requirements** You’re holy. Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
