single action

Interact

**Requirement** You're an expert in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]].

---

Even while affixed, this electric blue feather sways lightly in the air, as if always accompanied by a breeze. When activated, the talisman casts [[Spells/Fly|_fly_]] on you, though the duration is 1 minute. You can Dismiss this activation. If you do, you're affected by [[Spells/Feather Fall|_feather fall_]].
