# Drake Rifle  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 154

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

A _drake rifle_ is a firearm made from the saliva glands of a drake. The firearm launches small bursts of empowered spittle instead of typical rounds of ammunition. A _drake rifle_ is a _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] weapon_. It’s a distinct type of martial firearm that deals 1d10 damage with a range increment of 150 feet and reload 1. It deals [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage, depending on the drake from which it was made. On a critical hit, the spittle clings to the target and they take [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] of the same type as the weapon equal to 1d4 + the number of weapon damage dice. A _drake rifle_ does not add critical specialization effects.

**Activate** three actions Interact ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You fire a large, specialized burst designed to hamper your foes. Make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the _drake rifle_. As long as the Strike isn’t a critical failure, the _drake rifle_ deals 1 [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage per weapon damage die to the target and creatures within 5 feet of the target. This splash damage is of the same type as its normal damage.

On a hit, the _drake rifle_ provides one of the following additional effects based on its damage type:

- **Acid** Clinging acid makes it painful and sickening to move. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.
- **Cold** Frozen spittle clings to the target. The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to their Speeds for 1 minute.
- **Electricity** Small bursts of electricity continue to spark and jolt the target. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] for 1d4 rounds.
- **Fire** A massive explosion knocks the target back. The target is pushed 10 feet away from you, and if it’s on the ground, it falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
- **Poison** Noxious vapors fill the target’s lungs. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the saliva glands of a ritually hunted [[Bestiary/Families/Drake (Monster Core)|drake]] with a breath weapon that deals the appropriate damage type for the _drake rifle_.
