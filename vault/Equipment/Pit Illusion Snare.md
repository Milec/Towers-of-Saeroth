# Pit Illusion Snare  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 73

**Price** 9 gp

---

You add a touch of illusion magic to a magical sensor that makes the target believe a bottomless pit opens below it when the snare is triggered. A Medium or smaller creature that enters the snare's square must attempt a DC 20 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is cautious moving forward, treating the snare's square and all adjacent squares as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] until the end of its turn.  

**Failure** The creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature falls prone and takes 1d4 mental damage.
