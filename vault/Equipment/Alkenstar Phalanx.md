# Alkenstar Phalanx  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 4

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|Full Plate]]

---

This rugged suit of _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+2 resilient]] [[Equipment/Fortification (GM Core)|fortification]] [[Equipment/Armor/Full Plate (Player Core)|full plate]]_ has a large red gemstone inset into the overlapping plates of the chest piece. While wearing the armor, you might be given the role of protecting the flank of an army in battle, or perhaps standing your ground as the last line of defense in a castle keep.

**Activate—Phalanx** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You raise walls of rapidly shifting metal plates in two 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|lines]] going in opposite directions. Each wall is 60 feet high and is greater [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Whenever a creature enters or starts their turn in the wall’s space, they take 6d10 slashing damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). The shifting walls also provide greater [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from any attack originating from the opposite side.
