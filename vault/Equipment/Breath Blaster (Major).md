# Breath Blaster  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 154

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Blunderbuss]]

---

A _breath blaster_ is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|blunderbuss]]_ most commonly crafted from the trachea of a dragon, though other creatures with breath weapons or the ability to spit energy are occasionally used. The implementation of the dragon’s trachea allows the firearm to unleash a torrent of pure energy in the form of gouts of flame or bolts of electricity.

A _breath blaster’s_ Strikes deal either [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage, depending on the dragon type or other creature from which it was made, though it can otherwise be used like a normal blunderbuss. A _breath blaster_ also can be activated to fire a line of energy in a 30-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] or 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], chosen when it’s created and typically corresponding to the shape of the breath weapon used by the type of [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] or creature from which the _breath blaster_ was created.

In theory, the foundational techniques required to create a _breath blaster_ would allow for other damage types, but such _breath blasters_ would require the trachea from the correct dragon or creature type and additional creation techniques, making such a _breath blaster_ [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|rare]] rather than [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|uncommon]].

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You fire the _breath blaster_, dealing 4d6 damage of the appropriate type in the appropriate area. Creatures in the area must attempt a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save (or Fortitude save if the damage is [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]).

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the trachea of a ritually hunted [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]] or other creature with a breath weapon with the appropriate damage type and area (line or cone) for the _breath blaster_.
