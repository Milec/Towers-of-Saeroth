# Spectacles of Piercing Sight  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 147

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

With lenses set in a silver frame, _spectacles of piercing_ sight grant you a +3 item bonus to visual Perception checks.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You can see into and through solid matter for 1 minute. This vision can pierce through solid materials up to 20 feet away as if looking at something in normal light even if no illumination is available. You can see through up to 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of metal, or 3 feet of wood or dirt. A thin sheet of lead blocks this vision entirely.
