# Jyoti's Feather  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 126

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

A jyoti’s feather is a shimmering red and gold feather, seeming almost metallic, although delicate and flexible to the touch. Though most aren’t made from true jyoti feathers, as the reclusive outsiders avoid visitors from the Material Plane, their connection to positive energy lent the spellhearts their name.

- **Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]] and negative energy.
- **Weapon** The weapon has the [[Equipment/Vitalizing|_disrupting_]] rune while the feather is affixed.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Stabilize (Player Core)|_stabilize_]].

## Jyoti's Feather  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 126

**Price** 960 gp

---

**Activate** three actions command (emotion, enchantment, incapacitation);    **Requirements** The target must be fascinated by the mirror; **Effect** You control the target for 30 days, with the effect of a critically failed saving throw against [[Spells/Dominate|_dominate_]]. If the victim sees the mirror again at any point before this duration expires, the control extends for an additional 30 days from the moment it looked at the mirror again. While there is no initial saving throw, the DC to break free due to commands against the creature’s nature is 35.
