# The Tyrant  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 78

**Usage** carried

---

As long as you have _The Tyrant_ invested, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks made to [[Actions/Demoralize]]. This bonus increases to +3 if you are at least 17th level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold the card up to your lips and exhale onto it; you cast [[Spells/Dragon Form|_dragon form_]]. The level of the spell is half your level, rounded down (minimum 6th level).
