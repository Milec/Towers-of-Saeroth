# Reverberating Stone  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 94

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You use the affixed shield to S[[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|hield Block]] a melee attack

**Requirement** You’re a master in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], and you have the affixed shield raised.

---

This heavy metal emblem is mounted on the face of the affixed shield and has a dull gray gemstone embedded at its center. When you Activate the talisman, the gem shatters, releasing a powerful shockwave in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] that must include the enemy who struck the triggering blow, if possible. Each creature in the cone takes 2d6 sonic damage with a DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. Any creature that fails its save is pushed 5 feet away from you (or 10 feet on a critical failure).
