# Boozy Bottle  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 119

**Price** 140 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This tattoo depicts a container of alcohol, traditionally a small, uncorked brown bottle. You gain a +1 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]].

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You fail (but don’t critically fail) an initial saving throw against a [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], or you gain [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]];  **Effect** You pick your poison. Calling out the name of a drink as though ordering at a bar, you negate the triggering poison. Instead, you gain the [[Equipment/Alcohol|alcohol affliction]] at stage 2. The DC of this magical alcohol is the DC of the triggering poison save or DC 20 for persistent poison damage.
