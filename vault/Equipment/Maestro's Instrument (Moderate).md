# Maestro's Instrument  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 275

**Usage** held in 2 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _maestro's instrument_ can be crafted in the form of any variety of handheld musical instruments. A _maestro's instrument_ grants you a +1 item bonus to Performance checks while playing music with the instrument.

**Activate—Charming Performance** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You play the instrument, causing it to cast a DC 17 [[Spells/Charm|_charm_]] spell.
