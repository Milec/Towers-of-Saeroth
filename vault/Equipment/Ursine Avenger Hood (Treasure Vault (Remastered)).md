# Ursine Avenger Hood  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 183

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

_Note from Nethys: This item is a constituent part of the [[Archetypes/Ursine Avenger Hood (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Ursine Avenger Hood]] archetype using the [[Rules/Archetype Artifacts (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|archetype artifact]] rules._

A gift passed from warrior to warrior, this cowl is made from a [[Bestiary/Cave Bear|cave bear's]] skull and fur. Once invested, the wearer gains a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] checks to [[Actions/Command an Animal]], which increases to +2 if the animal is a [[Bestiary/Families/Bear|bear]]. The wearer of the hood feels a constant longing for the wilderness and wild places and takes a –1 item penalty to all [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks to [[Actions/Gather Information]] or [[Actions/Make an Impression]] if they go more than 5 days inside a large town or city without spending at least 4 hours in the wilderness.

**Destruction** Soak the _Ursine Avenger Hood_ in the blood of a [[Bestiary/Families/Linnorm|linnorm]] that is at least 16th level and then burn it in a fire that uses only oak and chrysanthemum as fuel.
