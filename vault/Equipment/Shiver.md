A compound produced from the hallucinogenic venom of certain spiders, shiver is common in black markets. The addiction to shiver has the [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] trait.

**Saving Throw** DC 19 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 4 hours; **Stage 1** whenever you become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], reduce that condition’s value by 1 (10 minutes); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] (1 hour); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|asleep]] (1 hour)
