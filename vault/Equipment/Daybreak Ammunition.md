# Daybreak Ammunition  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 212

**Price** 10 gp

single action

---

The bronze inlays of _daybreak ammunition_ glow noticeably, even under direct sunlight. When activated _daybreak ammunition_ hits a target, the missile flares with an aura of bright light in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] from the target for 1 round. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 18 Fortitude save. Creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]] take a –2 circumstance penalty to this save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.
