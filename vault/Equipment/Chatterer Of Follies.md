# Chatterer Of Follies  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 208, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 181

**Price** 200 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Khakkhara]]

---

This heavy _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Khakkhara|khakkhara]]_ is cast from solid steel and plated with pyrite. Metal charms shaped like grinning idols festoon its great arched ring, and during combat, these hanging dolls seem to chatter in tune with the clash and din of battle. Any time you cast an [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell while wielding this weapon, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of the turn.

**Activate—Confounding Chatter** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per round;  **Requirements** Your last action was a move action; **Effect** The idols gibber at a nearby foe. Choose an adjacent creature. Until the end of your turn, that creature takes a –1 status penalty to Will saves against your [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spells.
