# Skull Bomb  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Outlaws of Alkenstar

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -180- The Smoking Gun|Pathfinder #180: The Smoking Gun]] pg. 76

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand (see text)

**Bulk** L

---

This device is a human-sized skull crafted entirely from metal plating and clockwork gears, which slots easily into a human [[Bestiary/Families/Skeleton|skeleton]] in the spot where an ordinary skull would normally be located. While performing a [[Rituals/Create Undead|_create undead_]] ritual, you can attach this skull bomb to the target creature in place of its own head. In order to do so, the target creature must be Medium and have the [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] trait. The target creature can have only one skull bomb attached in this fashion. The creature gains the following ability.

 **Throw Skull** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) The creature detaches its skull bomb and hurls the volatile explosive at a point within 30 feet. The skull explodes, dealing 7d6 fire damage to creatures within a 15-foot burst (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The skull is permanently destroyed and can't be used again, and the throwing creature is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] and headless.
