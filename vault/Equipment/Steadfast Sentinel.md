---

This catalyst is a bird's claw soaked in a mixture of blood and grave dirt. A [[Spells/Shadow Spy|_shadow spy_]] spell empowered by it creates an unusually long-lasting homunculus. Instead of the spell expiring when you next make your daily preparation, you can renew it by expending a spell slot for _shadow spy_ again, maintaining the same homunculus so it can continue its observations. _Steadfast sentinels_ are often used by powerful spellcasters of Geb to covertly monitor their rivals.
