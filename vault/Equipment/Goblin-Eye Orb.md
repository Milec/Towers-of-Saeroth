# Goblin-Eye Orb  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 184

**Price** 24 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You attempt a Strike with the affixed firearm or crossbow against an enemy that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] to you

**Requirement** You’re an expert with the affixed firearm or crossbow and an expert in Perception.

---

This colorful marble dangles from a leather thong wrapped around the affixed weapon. When you activate the band, for the triggering Strike, you don’t need to attempt a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] due to the enemy being concealed or hidden to you.
