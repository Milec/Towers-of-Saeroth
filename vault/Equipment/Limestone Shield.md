# Limestone Shield  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 99

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 4

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield]]

---

This [[Equipment/Shields/Tower Shield|tower shield]] is a slab of limestone, shaved to a portable size and weight. The shield has Hardness 7 and 28 Hit Points.

**Activate—Limestone Wall** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You set the shield down as it expands up to 60 feet wide and 10 feet tall. Each square of the wall has AC 10, Hardness 7, and 28 Hit Points, and it's immune to critical hits and precision damage. If a section is destroyed, this effect ends, and your limestone shield is broken. You can Dismiss this activation, which otherwise lasts for 1 minute.

**Activate—Block Elements** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're targeted by an effect with the [[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait;  **Effect** The shield expands to block the triggering effect, granting you [[Rules/Cover|standard cover]] from that effect.
