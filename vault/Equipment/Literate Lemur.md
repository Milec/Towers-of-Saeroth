# Literate Lemur  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 215

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

This carved effigy of a lemur clutches a pen and paper, its expression cackling gleefully. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Mad Monkeys (Player Core)|_mad monkeys_]]_ spell, the following option is added to the options normally available.  
**• Fierce Debate** The monkeys appear frantically scribbling on conjured paper before turning on each other in a fit of rage; those who can speak with animals understand the dispute to be broadly over prose, spelling, and grammar. Creatures in the spell’s area are met with a flurry of paper and fur as the resulting fracas obstructs their sight. When the spell ends, paper and pens conjured by this spell disappear.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is temporarily immune for 10 minutes.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.
