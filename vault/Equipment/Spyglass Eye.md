# Spyglass Eye  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 108

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

Polished to a perfect surface and incredibly clear, this special magical prosthetic eye allows you to clearly see small details as well as things a great distance away.

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** magical lens of hardened air comes into being in front of the eye, allowing you to see as though you were looking through a fine spyglass for 1 minute.
