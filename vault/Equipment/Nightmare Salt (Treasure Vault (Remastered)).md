# Nightmare Salt  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Ingested]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 70, [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 79

**Price** 14,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This potent poison consists of crystals whose flavor and appearance mimics edible salt but whose effects are deadly; victims experience periods of waking nightmares that overload the senses and eventually result in death through a combination of shock and exhaustion.

**Saving Throw** DC 43 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 hour; **Maximum Duration** 5 days; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] once every 1d4 hours, plus [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 minute once every 1d4 hours, plus frightened 3 and fatigued (1 day); **Stage 3** frightened 3, plus confused for 1d4 minutes, once every hour, plus fatigued (1 day); **Stage 4** death
