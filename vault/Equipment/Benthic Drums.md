# Benthic Drums  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 142

**Price** 14,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

This large bass drum is made of driftwood and whale hide, with swirling shapes that hint at more distinct and sinister forms carved on its surface. This drum grants you a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks while playing music with the instrument. You can communicate basic ideas with whales and other large sea animals by playing music with this instrument.

**Activate—Call from the Depths** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) **Frequency** once per week;   **Effect** You drum a song of an ancient creature, calling forth the cries of a great whale in the minds of your foes. All enemies in a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] take 9d10 sonic damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). Creatures who fail are also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.
