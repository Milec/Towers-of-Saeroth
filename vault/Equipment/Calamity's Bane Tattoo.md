# Calamity's Bane Tattoo  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 241

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This small tattoo of a stylized lidded or clouded eye radiating lines of power is inked on a part of your body that you cannot easily see with your own eyes, such as the nape of your neck, behind your ears, or your interscapular region. This tattoo protects you during calamity as well as uncertainty, guiding your intuition to take subconscious actions and precautions to shelter you in a thousand unknown ways. When you are affected by a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect or a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], your shrouded eye tattoo emits an intense sensation akin to pins and needles, alerting you that something is awry, though the specific nature of the danger might be unknown to you.

**Activate—Alleviate Uncertainty** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You make a check for an action that has the secret trait or that has been affected by a misfortune effect;  **Effect** You gain a +1 status bonus to the check associated with the triggering action.
