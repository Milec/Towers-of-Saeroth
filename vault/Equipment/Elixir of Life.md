Elixirs of life accelerate a living creature's natural healing processes and immune system. Upon drinking this elixir, you regain the listed number of Hit Points and gain an item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Disease|diseases]] and [[Traits/Poison|poisons]] for 10 minutes.
