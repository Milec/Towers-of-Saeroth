# Neophyte's Fipple  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Price** 8 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Requirement** You are untrained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]].

---

Made of polished wood, a _neophyte's fipple_ is a block flute enchanted to guarantee melodic sound. When you Perform a song on the fipple to Activate it, your ability modifier, proficiency bonus, and item bonus for the Performance check total +7, regardless of what they would normally be. Add other bonuses and penalties to the check normally. Once the magic is used, the fipple remains as a mundane [[Equipment/Musical Instrument|instrument]].
