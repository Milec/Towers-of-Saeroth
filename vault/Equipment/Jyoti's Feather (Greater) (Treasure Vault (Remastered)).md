# Jyoti's Feather  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 126

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

A _jyoti’s feather_ is a shimmering red-and-gold feather that seems almost metallic, although it’s delicate and flexible to the touch. Though most aren’t made from true [[Bestiary/Jyoti|jyoti]] feathers, as the reclusive outsiders avoid visitors from [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], the creatures’ connection to life energy lent these spellhearts their name.

- **Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]] and [[Traits/Void|void]] effects.
- **Weapon** The weapon has the [[Equipment/Vitalizing|_vitalizing_]] rune while the feather is affixed.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Stabilize|_stabilize_]].
