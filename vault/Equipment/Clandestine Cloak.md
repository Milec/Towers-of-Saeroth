# Clandestine Cloak  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 608

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

When you pull up the hood of this nondescript gray cloak (an Interact action), you become drab and uninteresting, gaining a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks and to Deception checks to Impersonate a forgettable background character, such as a servant, but also taking a –1 item penalty to Diplomacy and Intimidation checks.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You pull the cloak’s hood up and gain the benefits of [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] for 1 hour or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.
