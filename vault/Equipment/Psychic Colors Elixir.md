# Psychic Colors Elixir  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -189- Dreamers of the Nameless Spires|Pathfinder #189: Dreamers of the Nameless Spires]] pg. 75

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This orange liquid lets you visibly detect the psychic energies that enable telekinesis and telepathy. For the next minute, you can sense if a creature you can see is using a telepathic or telekinetic ability, such as the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]] monster ability, spells like [[Spells/Telekinetic Projectile|_telekinetic projectile_]] or [[Spells/Telepathic Bond|_telepathic bond_]], or similar abilities. You also can sense if an object or creature you can see is being manipulated or contacted by such an ability. Both the user and the target of the ability are outlined in faint shimmers of matching color.
