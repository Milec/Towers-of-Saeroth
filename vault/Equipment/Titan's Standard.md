# Titan's Standard  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 141

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** affixed or held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This magical banner stands largest on any battlefield. While holding a _titan’s standard_, you can use the following ability.

**Activate—Titan’s Stature** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** The magical banner causes a rapid surge of growth. A Medium or smaller ally within the banner’s aura becomes Large for 1 round. Its equipment grows with it but returns to its natural size afterwards. While Large, the ally is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1, and its reach increases by 5 feet (or by 10 feet if it started out Tiny).
