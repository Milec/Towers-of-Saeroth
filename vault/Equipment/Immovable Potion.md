When you drink the thick, slate-colored _immovable potion_, you become anchored in place, even defying gravity, rendering you [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 minute or until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the activation. While you are immobilized this way, the DC to move you from your place, including knocking you [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], is 40.
