# Bloodstride Boots  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 64

**Price** 2,400 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

**Access** You're a member of the Red Mantis Assassins

---

These red leather boots greedily absorb any blood they touch.

**Activate—Blood Hop** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The boots produce a small pool of blood beneath your feet and in a free space you can see within 120 feet. You melt into the pool beneath you and reconstitute at the other pool, with the effects of _[[Spells/Translocate|translocate]]_.

**Activate—Blood Walk**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You’re standing in blood; **Effect** You melt into the ground and reconstitute at a location you have previously seen that’s within 100 miles, with the effects of _translocate_. This effect fails if no sentient creature has died at that location in the past 24 hours.
