# Imp Shot  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 81

**Price** 24 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

command

---

This black-and-red ammunition contains an egg-shaped capsule. When an activated _imp shot_ hits, the capsule cracks open and releases a manifestation that resembles a Tiny imp that can’t act in any way or provide benefits outside those described here. If the Strike misses the target, the imp appears, makes a rude gesture at you, and vanishes in a puff of sulfuric smoke. On a hit, though, the imp harries the target for up to 1 minute, remaining in the target’s space, slapping, nipping, hurling insults, and moving with the target as it moves. A creature harried by the imp is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|flat-footed]] and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls and skill checks. At the start of your turn on each round while the imp is active, you must attempt a DC 11 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]. On a failure, the imp makes a final vulgar gesture at the target and vanishes in a cloud of brimstone.
