[[Setting/Deities/Aerekostes|_Aerekostes_]] is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Shifting (GM Core)|shifting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Falcata (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|falcata]]_. Though unable to move on their own, _Aerekostes_ can flex, adjust their center of mass, and make other adjustments to adapt to a chosen wielder’s fighting style. For the purposes of proficiency, class abilities, and feats (except those that would increase the weapon’s damage die size), a wielder that _Aerekostes_ approves of can treat _Aerekostes_ as a martial weapon that belongs to their choice of the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axe]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|club]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|knife]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick|pick]], [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|spear]], or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|sword]] weapon group. _Aerekostes_ does not actually change form and their [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effect does not change.

As a hero-god, _Aerekostes_ is a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|mythic]] being who starts each session with their own pool of 3 [[Rules/Mythic Points]]. Whenever their wielder would regain 2 or more Mythic Points, _Aerekostes_ regains 1 Mythic Point.

**Activate—Forward Planning** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** _Aerekostes_ studies the ongoing combat and myriad ways an upcoming attack might resolve. On the next attack made using _Aerekostes_ before the end of their next turn, their wielder rolls twice and takes the better result.

**Activate—Spell Reserve** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** _Aerekostes_ channels their [[Rules/Mythic Rules|mythic power]] into a minor miracle. They spend 1 [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]], then casts one of the following as a 3rd-rank spell (DC 24 save as appropriate): [[Spells/Bane (Player Core)|_bane_]], [[Spells/Bless (Player Core)|_bless_]], [[Spells/Heroism (Player Core)|_heroism_]], [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]], [[Spells/Resist Energy (Player Core)|_resist energy_]], or [[Spells/Spiritual Armament|_spiritual armament_]]. _Aerekostes_ can use their actions to [[Actions/Sustain]] spells.

_Nethys Note: The rules to worship _Aerekostes_ while possessing them and further weapon advancement are presented [[Rules/Aerekostes (Rules)|here]]._
