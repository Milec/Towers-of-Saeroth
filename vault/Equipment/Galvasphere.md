A galvasphere animates a corpse to motion via electricity, rather than necromancy. When you Activate the galvasphere by inserting it into an adjacent intact Medium or Small humanoid corpse, the corpse animates as a galvaheart zombie for 1 minute. The galvaheart zombie has the statistics of a [[Bestiary/Zombie Shambler (Monster Core)|zombie shambler]] except that it is a [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]] instead of an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], isn’t [[Traits/Evil|evil]], can’t be harmed by positive energy, and is the same size as the corpse (Medium or Small). The zombie is your [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] and performs the actions you choose when you [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command]] it. If you don’t Command it, it takes no action, twitching in place as the electricity that animates it slowly expends itself.
