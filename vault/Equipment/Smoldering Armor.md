# Smoldering Armor  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 19

**Price** 975 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 2

**Base Armor** [Niyaháat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Armor.aspx?ID=55)

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Resilient|resilient]] [[Equipment/Energy-Resistant|fire-resistant]] [niyaháat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Armor.aspx?ID=55)_ is often created to mark the passage of an Erutaki warrior into adulthood, plates salvaged from the exploded remains of a [[Bestiary/Firewyrm (Monster Core)|firewyrm]] elemental, with larger pieces protecting the chest, shoulders, and head. As you fight, the armor glows red hot.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The armor casts [[Spells/Fire Shield|_fire shield_]] on you.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're dealt 30 or more fire damage, before any reduction from your fire resistance; **Requirements** You've used the _fire shield_ activation already today; **Effect** The _fire shield_ activation recharges.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the carapace of a [[Bestiary/Firewyrm (Monster Core)|firewyrm]].
