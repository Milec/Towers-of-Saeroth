# Grappling Hook  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 290

**Price** 1 sp

**Hands** 1

**Bulk** L

---

You can throw a grappling hook with a rope tied to it to make a climb easier. To anchor a grappling hook, make an attack roll with the [[Traits/Secret|secret]] trait against a DC depending on the target, typically at least DC 20. On a success, your hook has a firm hold, but on a critical failure, the hook seems like it will hold but actually falls when you're partway up.
