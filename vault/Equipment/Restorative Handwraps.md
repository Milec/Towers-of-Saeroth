# Restorative Handwraps  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of the Unlit Star Game Master's Guide]] pg. 60

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn gloves

---

Glowing runes cover these _+1 striking [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows (GM Core)|handwraps of mighty blows]]_. When you critically hit an enemy with a Strike as part of a [[Actions/Flurry of Blows (Player Core 2)|Flurry of Blows]], you can regain 4 Hit Points. This is a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect. The handwraps can heal you only once every 10 minutes.
