# South Wind's Scorch Song  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 211

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|Scimitar]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|scimitar]]_ is always warm to the touch, nearly unbearably so. Lines of crackling flame radiate from carnelians affixed to its blackened blade. While you have a status bonus to at least one of your Speeds, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with this scimitar that deal fire damage gain a +2 status bonus to their fire damage. The status bonus increases to +3 if the weapon has a _greater striking_ rune and +4 for a _major striking_ rune.

**Activate—Warming Zephyr** free action (air, [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** You deal fire damage to a creature with a spell or ability;  **Effect** You point the _south wind’s scorch song_ upward, summoning a wind from nowhere that grants you a +10-foot status bonus to your Speeds until the end of your next turn.
