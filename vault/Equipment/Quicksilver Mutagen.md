# Quicksilver Mutagen  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 549

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Your features become thin and angular. You become swifter and nimbler, but your body also becomes fragile.

 **Benefit** You gain an item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks, [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks, Reflex saves, and Dexterity-based attack rolls, and you gain the listed status bonus to your Speed.

 **Drawback** You take damage equal to twice your level; you can't recover Hit Points lost in this way by any means while the mutagen lasts. You take a –2 penalty to Fortitude saves
