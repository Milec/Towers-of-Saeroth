# Frenzy Oil  *Item 16*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Contact]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 78

**Price** 1,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This oil, which is pressed from [[Bestiary/Vrock|vrock]] spores and mixed with a combination of acacia ash and thistle seeds, seeps into the skin of living creatures, initially inspiring irritability that unpredictably manifests as berserk rage.

 **Saving Throw** DC 37 Fortitude; **Onset** 1d6 minutes; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 mental damage, [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened 1]], attack nearby creatures as if [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 6d6 mental damage, attack nearby creatures as if confused (1 round); **Stage 3** 8d6 mental damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], attack nearby creatures as if confused (1 round)
