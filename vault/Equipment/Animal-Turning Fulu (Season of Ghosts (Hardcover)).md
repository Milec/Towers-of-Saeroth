# Animal-Turning Fulu  *Item 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fulu]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 301

**Price** 4 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

⤾

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Trigger** A creature with the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait successfully Strikes you.

---

Frightened animals depicted on this fulu flee in all directions from a central figure (traditionally represented by a human hunter, but sometimes depicted as a skeletal [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature or even a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] with long, broken arms). You activate this fulu, gaining a +2 item bonus to AC against the triggering Strike. If this causes the [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] to miss, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from the triggering creature until the start of your next turn.
