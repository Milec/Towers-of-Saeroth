# Stormshard  *Item 6+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 78

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

These shards of coalesced necromantic essence are sometimes found in the wake of an ancestor storm. Howling spirits are faintly visible, trapped inside the jagged, dark-green glass.

You can use the shard to cow the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] or briefly release the tormented souls from inside the glass. While you hold the stormshard, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Rules/Influence (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|influence]] the undead. In addition, you can [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] undead creatures, even if those creatures are [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|mindless]] or otherwise [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immune]] to [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], or [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects.

**Activate—Free the Spirits** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You briefly release the spirits from the _stormshard_ before drawing them back into the glass. Each creature in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 4d8 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save). You treat the result of your saving throw as one degree of success better than its outcome.

## Greater Stormshard  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 79

**Price** 1,800 gp

---

The item bonus increases to +2. When activating the _stormshard_ to release the spirits, the damage increases to 8d8 (DC 29). In addition, you can choose to increase the radius of the [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] by up to 10 feet.

## Major Stormshard  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 79

**Price** 19,000 gp

---

The item bonus increases to +3. When activating the _stormshard_, the damage increases to 13d8 (DC 38), and you can increase the radius of the emanation by up to 20 feet.
