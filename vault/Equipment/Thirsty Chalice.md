# Thirsty Chalice  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 237

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This slate chalice improbably holds one single drop of water in it at all times and helps refresh the wearer, sometimes at the expense of their enemies.  
**• Armor** After you cast a spell by Activating the chalice, you are surrounded by a fine mist. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the beginning of your next turn.  
**• Weapon** (void) After you cast a spell by Activating the chalice, your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with the weapon deal an additional 1 void damage until the end of your next turn; the first time in a round you successfully deal this void damage, you gain 4 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 minute.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Draw Moisture|_draw moisture_]] ._
