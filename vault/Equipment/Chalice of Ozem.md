# Chalice of Ozem  *Item 11*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Claws of the Tyrant

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Claws of the Tyrant]] pg. 110

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This ruby-studded [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]] chalice can’t be harmed by any substance it holds, no matter how caustic. Furthermore, liquid placed within the _Chalice of Ozem_ never spills unless its carrier chooses to do so (using a single action with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait).

**Activate—Iomedae's Blessing** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You hold the chalice and call out [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae (Divine Mysteries)|Iomedae’s]] name. The chalice casts [[Spells/Dispelling Globe|_dispelling globe_]] with a +19 modifier to its [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check.

**Destruction** If the _Chalice of Ozem_ is filled with Iomedae’s blood and [[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]] drinks from it, it shatters.
