# Trackless  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 145

**Usage** applied to footwear

_Trackless runes_ are common among hunters and trappers, as well as thieves and anyone fleeing pursuit. While wearing trackless footwear, you are continuously affected by the [[Spells/Vanishing Tracks|_vanishing tracks_]] spell.
