# Shrinking Potion  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Potion (Player Core)|Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 261

**Onset** 1 minute

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This fungus-flavored potion conveys the effects of the [[Spells/Shrink|_shrink_]] spell to make you and all your gear smaller. After the onset, you remain small for 10 minutes.

## Shrinking Potion  *Item 4*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 261, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 564

**Price** 15 gp

## Shrinking Potion (Greater)  *Item 8*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 261, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 564

**Price** 90 gp

---

This potion has no onset, lasts for 1 hour, and grants the effects of a 4th-rank _shrink_ spell. In addition, you gain a +2 item bonus to Stealth checks while shrunken.
