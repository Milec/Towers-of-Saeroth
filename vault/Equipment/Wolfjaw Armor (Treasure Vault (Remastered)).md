---

[[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Core Rulebook)|Barbarians]], [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druids]] and other outlanders are often forced to spend the harsh winter months protecting their communities from one of the deadliest predators to stalk the forests and taiga of the northern reaches, the fearsome [[Bestiary/Witchwarg|witchwargs]]. This _+1 [[Equipment/Armor/Hide Armor|hide armor]]_ is assembled from the hide and fur of a trio of witchwargs, and it conveys on the wearer both an attack akin to the witchwargs' deadly jaws and the ability to channel the frigid cold of the witchwargs's Winter Breath.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You breathe a cloud of frost in a 15-foot cone that deals 5d8 cold damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You gain a jaws unarmed attack that you make using your hands. This attack deals 1d8 piercing damage plus 1 cold damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and has the [[Traits/Trip|trip]] and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the hides of at least three [[Bestiary/Witchwarg|witchwargs]].
