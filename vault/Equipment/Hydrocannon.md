# Hydrocannon  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 104

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [Hand Cannon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=193)

---

This _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [hand cannon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=193)_ is crafted by wrapping the water-producing organ cluster of a [[Bestiary/Grodair|grodair]] around its barrel, eliminating the need for ammunition, though the living tissue must be maintained with 1 sp of specialized nutrient feed each day. If not, its misfire check is DC 10. If you fail this misfire check, the organ bursts, dealing an amount of bludgeoning damage equal to double the number of weapon damage dice to you and rendering the _hydrocannon_ broken.

**Activate—Tinker Shot** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulation]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** You fiddle with the pressure regulator on the _hydrocannon_ and then make a ranged [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the gun. A successful attack has an additional effect depending on which modular configuration the _hydrocannon_ is currently in.

- **Bludgeoning** The target is pushed 5 feet away from you; this [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]] is increased to 10 feet on a critical hit.
- **Piercing** The target takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.
- **Slashing** The target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the extradimensional organ cluster extracted from a [[Bestiary/Grodair|grodair]]. Harvesting the organs of a grodair in this way differs greatly from the normal method; it takes an hour and a successful DC 22 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] check. On a critical failure, the organ cluster bursts and deals 4d6 bludgeoning damage to the harvester.
