# Silver Tongue  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 236

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

This tongue-shaped silver trinket typically dangles from a silver chain. It seems to catch the light wherever it is affixed. When affixed to armor or a weapon with any metal components, it makes those pieces gleam brightly. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 24.  
**• Armor** You gain a +2 item bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] and [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] effects.  
**• Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell by Activating the _silver tongue_, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Daze (Player Core)|_daze_]]_.
