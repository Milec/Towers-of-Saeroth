Smoke constantly belches from this _+1 magic [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_. Any hit with this sword deals 1 extra fire damage. You can use a special action while holding the sword to command the blade's edges to light on fire.

**Activate—Stoke Flames** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])    **Effect** Until the end of your turn, the blade deals 1d6 extra [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] damage instead of just 1. After you use this action, you can’t use it again for 10 minutes.
