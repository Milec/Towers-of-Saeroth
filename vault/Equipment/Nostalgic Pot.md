# Nostalgic Pot  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 81

**Price** 300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

This small pot filled with pebbles is imbued with the nostalgia of its owner. While this pot is on your person, you get a +1 item bonus to saves against [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects.

**Activate—Jingling Memories** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You shake the pot, granting a +1 item bonus to an ally to save against an emotion effect.
