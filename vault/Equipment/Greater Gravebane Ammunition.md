# Gravebane Ammunition  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 212

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

First made to deal with a wicked necromancer’s encroaching horde, _gravebane ammunition_ is crafted with materials soaked in blessed waters. On a successful [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]], the attack deals an additional 1d10 vitality damage to creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]]. If the target takes [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage, it must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (or enfeebled 2 on a critical failure) until the start of your next turn.
