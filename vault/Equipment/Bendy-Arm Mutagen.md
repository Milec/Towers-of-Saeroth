# Bendy-Arm Mutagen  *Item 1+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Mutagen]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 59

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Your limbs become extremely limber, letting you stretch and twist to extreme degrees at the cost of fine motor skills.

**Benefit** You gain the listed item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Escape]], [[Actions/Squeeze]], and [[Actions/Tumble Through]], and you increase your reach by the listed amount.

**Drawback** You take a –1 penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks, [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks, and attack rolls, and a –1 penalty per damage die to all weapon and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack damage.
