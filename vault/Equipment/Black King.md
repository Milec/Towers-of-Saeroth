# Black King  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 58

**Price** 6,250 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Blunderbuss]]

---

The _black king_ is a _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Ashen|ashen]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|blunderbuss]]_. While the dark metal frame is still pleasant to look at, this design prioritizes destructive power over aesthetic appeal. This specialized blunderbuss has a [[Traits/Scatter (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|scatter]] radius of 20 feet instead of 10, and all [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage it deals is negative damage instead of physical. Due to the risk of collateral damage, this weapon is typically only used by the especially foolhardy or reckless.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You unleash a blast of draining flame, dealing 5d6 fire damage and 5d6 negative damage to all creatures in a 60 foot cone (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures who fail their saving throw are [[Rules/Forced Movement|knocked back]] 5 feet. Creatures who critically fail are knocked back 10 feet and fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
