# Harrow Spellcards  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 112

**Price** 425 gp

**Bulk** L

---

Crafted of sturdy paper, each card of this [[Equipment/Harrow Deck|harrow deck]] showcases a beautiful watercolor illustration with space to inscribe a spell below. When shuffled, its cards seem to fly between one another of their own accord.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** Your last action was to cast a spell prepared from this grimoire that has the [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Prediction (Player Core)|prediction]], [[Traits/Revelation (Player Core)|revelation]], or [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] trait;  **Effect** You draw forth a card to gain insight into future challenges you’ll face. Draw a card from a harrow deck or roll 1d6: 1 = hammers ([[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]), 2 = keys ([[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]]), 3 = shields ([[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]), 4 = books (any [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]), 5 = stars ([[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]]), 6 = crowns ([[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]]). The next time you attempt a check of the same type as your result, roll twice and take the better result, as the spirits of the harrow guide your actions. If not used by your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], this benefit disappears.
