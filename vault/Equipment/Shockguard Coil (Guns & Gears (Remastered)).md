# Shockguard Coil  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 83

**Price** 175 gp

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

[[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]

**Trigger** You [[Feats/Shield Block]] a foe's melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack or melee attack with the affixed shield.

---

This miniature Stasian coil talisman emits small sparks when jostled. It uses a combination of Stasian technology and evocation magic to erupt in electricity when discharged. When you activate the coil, the foe takes 2d12 electricity damage (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed save, the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of its next turn.
