# Gloaming Shard  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 209

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|Dagger]]

---

The blade of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_ shines the color of twilight, with a triangular lattice design on the hilt. A thin string of darkness connects your shadow to that of the blade, even once it leaves your hand.

**Activate—Umbral Jaunt** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], shadow, teleportation) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You make a thrown [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the _gloaming shard_ at a target within 60 feet. Whether you hit or miss, rather than returning to you, the blade pulls you down into your own shadow and along the connecting thread. You instantly teleport to a space adjacent to the target of your Strike, as _translocate_, and then catch the dagger in your hand.
