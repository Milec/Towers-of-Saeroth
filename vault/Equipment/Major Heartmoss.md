# Heartmoss  *Item 3+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 79

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

**Bulk** L

---

This burgundy moss grows in heart-shaped clumps and releases a pleasant, calming scent. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 17.

- **Armor** You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 2 to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage and a +1 item bonus to saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] trait.
- **Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] spell by activating the _heartmoss_, the weapon exudes a soothing scent. Your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the affixed weapon have the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait for 1 minute. Until the end of your next turn, Strikes with the affixed weapon cause the target to be [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round if it hits (or for 3 rounds on a critical hit).

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Stabilize (Player Core)|_stabilize_]].
