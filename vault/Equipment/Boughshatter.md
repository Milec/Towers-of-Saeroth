# Boughshatter  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 79

**Price** 2,875 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|Morningstar]]

---

The spikes of this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+2]] [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|standard-grade adamantine]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Morningstar (Player Core)|morningstar]]_ have a faintly green shimmer, as if resembling Ayrzul’s crystalline teeth. The weapon vibrates briefly when first drawn in or carried into a radioactive area, with the intensity of the vibration correlating to the radioactivity’s strength. While you carry _Boughshatter_, you gain a +4 status bonus to saving throws against radiation, including [[Rules/Diseases/Ayrzul's Blight|Ayrzul’s Blight]].

**Activate—Splintering Strike** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the morningstar that deals an extra die of weapon damage. If you make this Strike against a [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]], or creature with the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait, the Strike ignores 10 points of the target’s resistances to bludgeoning and piercing damage, if any. By damaging such a creature, the morningstar causes the target’s flesh to explode in a shower of splinters that affect a 30-foot cone originating from the target’s space, dealing 8d6 piercing damage to creatures in the area (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). You and the target are unaffected by these splinters.

**Activate—Absorb Corruption** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] or [[Traits/Void|void]] damage;;  **Effect** You channel some of the toxic energy into _Boughshatter_, granting the weapon the effects of either a [[Equipment/Decaying|_decaying weapon rune_]], grievous weapon rune, or wounding weapon rune for 1 minute. While under this effect, the morningstar glows with [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim green light]] in a 20-foot radius.
