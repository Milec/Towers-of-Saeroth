# Venom Glands  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 99

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** implanted

---

Your salivary glands are modified to be capable of spraying a deadly venom. You gain a poison spray unarmed ranged attack with a [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment]] of 10 feet that deals 1d4 poison damage. On a critical hit, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.
