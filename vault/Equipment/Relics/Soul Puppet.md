# Soul Puppet  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 231

**Aspect** Soul

---

**Activate** three actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You bind loose souls, briefly animating an undead creature. The soul seed casts [[Spells/Animate Dead|_animate dead_]] at a level equal to half the soul seed's level rounded up. You can Sustain this Activation as you would the spell.
