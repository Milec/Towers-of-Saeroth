# Fiendish Bargain  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 313

**Aspect** Fiend

---

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You bargain with the evil force empowering your relic for a boon, though you must trade something in return. Select one of the following benefits to gain.

- Your relic attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a harmful condition affecting you.
- You regain 3d8+16 Hit Points. This is a [[Traits/Healing|healing]] effect.
- You gain a +2 status bonus to attack rolls for 1 minute.

 One of the following randomly determined effects occurs to you in payment, after applying the benefit.

- You are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute.
- You are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] for 1 minute.
- You lose 1d8 Hit Points.
- You take a –1 penalty to damage rolls for 1 minute.
