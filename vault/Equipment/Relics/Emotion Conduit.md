# Emotion Conduit  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You channel your relic’s emotion at a target within 30 feet, overpowering an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect on that creature. The relic attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check. If the check succeeds, the target feels a sudden rush of the relic’s emotion, and the counteracted effect ends on the target. The target is temporarily immune to your relic’s emotion conduit until you make your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]].
