# Living Storm  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Air

---

The relic grants you a fly Speed equal to your Speed or 60 feet, whichever is greater. In addition, a creature that Strikes you with a melee attack takes 5 electricity damage, and you can cast 5th-rank [[Spells/Lightning Bolt (Player Core)|_lightning bolt_]] as an innate spell.
