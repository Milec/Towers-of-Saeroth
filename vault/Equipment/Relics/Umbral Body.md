# Umbral Body  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shadow]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 105

**Aspect** Shadow

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** Shadowy essence infuses your body, and you can reshape wisps of yourself into a variety of damaging shadows. This has the effect of a 6th-level [[Spells/Shadow Blast|_shadow blast_]], choosing from only bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage. You can’t use this activation again for 1d4 rounds.
