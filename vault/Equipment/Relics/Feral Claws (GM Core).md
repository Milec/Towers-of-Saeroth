# Feral Claws  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Beast

**Prerequisite** The relic is a worn item.

---

**Activate** single action (concentrate);     **Effect** Your hands transform into a pair of claws. These grant you a claw [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits. This lasts until you Dismiss the effect.
