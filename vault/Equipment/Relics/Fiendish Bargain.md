# Fiendish Bargain  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Evil]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 100

**Aspect** Fiend

---

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You bargain with the evil force empowering your relic for a boon, though you must trade something in return. Select one of the following benefits to gain.

- Your relic attempts to counteract a harmful condition affecting you.
- You regain 3d8+16 Hit Points. This is a [[Traits/Healing|healing]] effect.
- You gain a +2 status bonus to attack rolls for 1 minute.

 One of the following randomly determined effects occurs to you in payment, after applying the benefit.

- You are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute.
- You are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 minute.
- You lose 1d8 Hit Points.
- You take a –1 penalty to damage rolls for 1 minute.
