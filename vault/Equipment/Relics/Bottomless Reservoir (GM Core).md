# Bottomless Reservoir  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 319

**Aspect** Water

---

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Your relic can absorb and release water, storing it in an endless reservoir. You either touch your relic to an adjacent body of water and absorb 1 gallon of it into the reservoir, or you release 1 gallon of a liquid from the reservoir. You can add another action to the activation to absorb water from a touched creature made entirely of water, like a [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water|water elemental]]. If you do, it takes 1d6 damage per relic level ([[Rules/Encounter Mode|basic]] Fortitude save).

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You are targeted by an [[Traits/Acid|acid]] or [[Traits/Water|water]] effect;  **Effect** Your relic attempts to counteract the effect, expending 1 gallon of stored liquid. At the GM's discretion, you can use this ability on effects made of other liquids, such as blood.
