# Beast Senses  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 96

**Aspect** Beast

**Prerequisite** The relic is a worn item.

---

The relic grants you an animal’s acute senses as long as you wear it. You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]]. If you already have low-light vision, you instead gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]. At 10th level, the relic grants you [[Rules/Scent|imprecise scent]] with a range of 10 feet.
