# Sacred Glow  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 311

**Aspect** Celestial

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Your relic shines with holy light. This creates [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 30-foot emanation around the item. [[Traits/Unholy]] creatures in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] and can't lower their frightened value below 1 as long as they remain in the light. [[Traits/Fiend|Fiends]] in the area increase any weakness to [[Traits/Holy|holy]] they have by 5. You can Sustain this activation for up to 1 minute.
