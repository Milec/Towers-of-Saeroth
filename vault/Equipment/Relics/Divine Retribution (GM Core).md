# Divine Retribution  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Celestial

**Prerequisite** The relic is a weapon.

---

**Activate** two actions (concentrate);     **Effect** Drawing upon divine fury, you single out a target that damaged you or an ally within the past round and fill your relic with divine vengeance against them. Make a Strike against that foe, dealing an additional damage die on a hit. If the target has the unholy trait, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] until the start of your next turn on a hit (or enfeebled 2 on a critical hit).
