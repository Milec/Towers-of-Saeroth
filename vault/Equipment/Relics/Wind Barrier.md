# Wind Barrier  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Air]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 96

**Aspect** Air

---

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** An invisible barrier of air deflects ranged attacks, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks until the beginning of your next turn. At 13th level, this increases to a +3 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks.
