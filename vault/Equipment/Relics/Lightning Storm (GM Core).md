# Lightning Storm  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Air

---

**Activate** two actions (concentrate);     **Effect** The relic surrounds you with a storm of wind, rain, and lightning in a 30-foot emanation. This storm is difficult terrain for creatures other than you. Any creature other than you that enters or starts its turn in the storm takes 1d12 electricity damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save (2d12 damage at 15th level). You can Sustain this activation up to 1 minute.
