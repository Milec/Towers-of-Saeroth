# Call of the Wild  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 96

**Aspect** Beast

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your relic summons a creature to fight at your side, casting [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]] with a level of half the item’s level rounded up. You can Sustain this Activation as you would the spell.
