# Quickening Emotion  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You are the target of an [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect from a hostile source;  **Effect** The relic attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check. If the check succeeds, the relic absorbs the effect completely, negating it. Instead, you're [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for up to 1 minute, but no longer than the triggering effect's duration. You then become temporarily immune to this effect for 1 hour.
