# Deadly Spark  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 309

**Aspect** Air

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** A spark flashes out from the relic toward a creature within 20 feet. This spark deals 1d12 electricity damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The damage increases by 1d12 at 6th level and by another 1d12 every 4 levels thereafter.
