# Quickening Emotion  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** ⤾ envision;   **Trigger** You are the target of an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect from a [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile (Player Core)|hostile]] source;  **Effect** The relic attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check. If the check succeeds, the relic absorbs the effect completely, negating it. Instead, you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for up to 1 minute, but no longer than the triggering effect’s duration. You then become temporarily immune to this effect for 1 hour.
