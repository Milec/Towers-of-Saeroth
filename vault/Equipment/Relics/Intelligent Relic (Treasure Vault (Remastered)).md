# Intelligent Relic  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Intelligent]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 200

**Aspect** Intelligent Relic

---

Your relic becomes an intelligent item. The relic possesses precise sight at a range of 30 feet and imprecise hearing at a range of 30 feet. It is trained in [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]], increasing its proficiency when you increase yours. Choose whether it can speak aloud or communicate with you telepathically. It knows languages fitting its backstory, usually from among those you know.

The relic is also trained in three skills, increasing one skill’s proficiency each time the relic gains a new gift. Your relic’s mental attribute modifiers begin at +3, +2, and +1, arranged as you choose. The relic is also trained in Will saves, and its proficiency increases as yours does. Each time you gain a new gift, increase one of the relic’s mental attribute modifiers by 1. The relic otherwise functions as normal for an intelligent item.

If you’re [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]] to [[Traits/Holy|holy]] or to [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], you can choose to have your relic sanctified in the same way.
