# Dominant Emotion  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 198

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You would be adversely affected by an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect;  **Effect** The relic tries to neutralize the triggering effect with its own emotion, attempting a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check. If the check succeeds, you feel a sudden rush of the relic’s emotion and the counteracted effect doesn’t take hold of you.
