# Divine Retribution  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 97

**Aspect** Celestial

**Prerequisite** The relic is a weapon.

---

**Activate** two actions command;     **Effect** Drawing upon divine fury, you single out a target that damaged you or an ally within the past round and fill your relic with divine vengeance against them. Make a Strike against that foe, dealing an additional damage die on a hit. If the target is evil, it is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] until the start of your next turn on a hit (or enfeebled 2 on a critical hit).
