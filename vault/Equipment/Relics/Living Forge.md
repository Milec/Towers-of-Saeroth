# Living Forge  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 114

**Aspect** Forge

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The relic is absorbed into your body, and you transform into an elemental being of red-hot steel. You gain the effects of 7th-level [[Spells/Elemental Form|_elemental form_]] and use the statistics for the forge battle form below. In this form, you can re-shape your limbs into different objects, such as a blade or hammer, by using an Interact action. Changing your limbs in this way allows you to choose between bludgeoning, physical, or slashing damage.

- **Forge Speed** 30 feet; fire resistance 10, weakness 5 to cold and water; **Melee** forgestrike, **Damage** 1d10 modular; 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire]].
