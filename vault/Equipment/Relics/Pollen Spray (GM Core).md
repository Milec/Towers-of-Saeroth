# Pollen Spray  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Pollen and poison spores spray from your relic, dealing 1d6 poison damage for every 2 relic levels (minimum 1d6) to all creatures in a 15-foot cone ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a critical failure, targets are also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round. You can't use Pollen Spray again for 1d4 rounds.
