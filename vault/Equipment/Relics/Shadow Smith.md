# Shadow Smith  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 104

**Aspect** Shadow

---

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** Your relic pulls at nearby shadows, twisting them into the shape of a simple weapon or a simple tool or item, such as a rope or crowbar. It lasts until it is used for a single activity, until you use shadow smith again, or for 1 minute, whichever comes first, after which it dissipates.
