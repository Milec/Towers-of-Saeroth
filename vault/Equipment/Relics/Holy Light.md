# Holy Light  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 98

**Aspect** Celestial

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** Your relic shines with holy light. This creates [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 30-foot emanation around the item. Evil creatures in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]], and can’t lower their frightened value below 1 as long as they remain in the light. [[Traits/Fiend|Fiends]] in the area increase any weakness to good damage they have by 5. You can Sustain this Activation for up to 1 minute.
