# Flowing Footsteps  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 319

**Aspect** Water

---

Your relic ensures no water impedes your movement. You gain the effects of the [[Spells/Water Walk|_water walk_]] spell, and the ability to breathe water. You also gain a swim Speed equal to your Speed or 15 feet, whichever is higher.
