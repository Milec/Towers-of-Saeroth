# Petal Dance  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You discorporate into a cloud of petals and leaves. This grants you the [[Traits/Swarm|swarm]] trait, immunity to [[Rules/Bright Light|falling]] damage, resistance 5 to bludgeoning and slashing damage, and weakness 5 to area and [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage. You can fit into spaces only a few inches wide, moving your constituent petals through the gap. You don't gain the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swarm Mind|swarm mind]] ability, so you're still affected normally by [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. As a swarm, you can't speak, cast spells, use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions requiring your hands, activate your magic items, or make any of your Strikes with your normal body. You remain in this form for 1 minute or until you Dismiss the activation. At 13th level, the relic gains a reaction that triggers when you fall or take damage, applying this gift's resistances, weaknesses, and immunity to falling damage to the triggering effect.
