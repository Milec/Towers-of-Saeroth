# Profane Fervor  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 314

**Aspect** Fiend

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Profane energies twist your weapons, filling them with rage toward everything good. For 1 minute, your weapons and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks gain the [[Equipment/Unholy|_unholy_]] weapon rune while you hold them. If a weapon is at its limit on property runes, you can choose one property rune on the weapon to go dormant while the _unholy_ rune takes effect. In addition, Strikes your allies make while they're within 30 feet of you gain the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait.
