# Ripples and Waves  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 319

**Aspect** Water

---

You become attuned to the ebb and flow of all things, as if embodying water itself. You gain wavesense 60 feet as a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]] sense and can cast 5th-rank [[Spells/Hydraulic Push|_hydraulic push_]] at will as an innate spell.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Ankle-deep water floods outward from you, filling a 60-foot emanation centered on you for 1 minute. Enemies within the area without a swim Speed treat the area as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for movement on land. While the effect persists, you can have any [[Traits/Water|water]] effects you generate originate from any point within the emanation, in addition to their normal range and area.
