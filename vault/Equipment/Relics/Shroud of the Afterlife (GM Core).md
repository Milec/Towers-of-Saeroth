# Shroud of the Afterlife  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 312

**Aspect** Death

---

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You shroud yourself with powers from beyond the living realm for 1 minute. While this shroud is active, you take on a pallid appearance and gain [[Traits/Poison|poison]] resistance equal to half the relic's level and a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]], effects that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], poison, and [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] effects. In addition, the DC for your recovery checks is 9 + your [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] value instead of 10 + your dying value.
