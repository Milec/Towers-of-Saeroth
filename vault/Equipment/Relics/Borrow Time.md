# Borrow Time  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 200

**Aspect** Time

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You point the relic at a creature within 30 feet, imbuing it with urgency. The creature gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition for 1 round. After the condition ends, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round and temporarily immune to this gift until you next perform your daily preparations.
