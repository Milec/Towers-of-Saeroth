# Cleanse Soul  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 231

**Aspect** Soul

---

**Activate**  1 minute (command, envision);  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You cleanse a soul of curses. The soul seed attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] all [[Traits/Curse|curses]] on you or another creature you touch throughout the activation. The target's soul is bolstered against further curses, and they gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against curses for 1 hour.
