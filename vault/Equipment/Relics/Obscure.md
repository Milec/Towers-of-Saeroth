# Obscure  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 104

**Aspect** Shadow

---

Your relic absorbs light and wraps shadow around you, hiding you in darkness. As long as you are in dim light in an area of shadows, you can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Hide]], even if you aren’t concealed against the creature, such as with a creature with [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
