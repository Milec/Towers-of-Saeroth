# All Will Decay  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 148

**Aspect** Metal

---

Your relic's link to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]] tunes it in to the inevitability of rust and decay. You can cast 5th-rank [[Spells/Rust Cloud|_rust cloud_]] at will as an innate spell.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Rusted flakes swirl in a cloud around you, creating a 15-foot emanation that lasts for 1 minute. You're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to anyone outside the cloud. An enemy that starts its turn in the cloud takes 15 slashing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If the target has the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait or is primarily made of metal, the damage is 30 instead. Unattended non-magical metal items within the cloud automatically become broken.
