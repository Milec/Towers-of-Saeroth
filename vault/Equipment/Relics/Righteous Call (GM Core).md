# Righteous Call  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 311

**Aspect** Celestial

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Holy magic fills your armaments, making them true weapons against evil. For 1 minute, your weapons and unarmed attacks gain the [[Equipment/Holy|_holy_]] weapon rune while you hold them. If a weapon is at its limit on property runes, you can choose one property rune on the weapon to go dormant while the _holy_ rune takes effect. In addition, Strikes your allies make while they're within 30 feet of you gain the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait.
