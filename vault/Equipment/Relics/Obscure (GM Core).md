# Obscure  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Shadow

---

Your relic absorbs light and wraps shadow around you, hiding you in darkness. As long as you are in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in an area of shadows, you can attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Hide]], even if you aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] against the creature, such as with a creature with [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
