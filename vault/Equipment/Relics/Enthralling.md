# Enthralling  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 198

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You speak at length about or demonstrate your craft, or you enact a performance. The relic casts [[Spells/Enthrall (Player Core)|_enthrall_]] at a level equal to half its own. If you have legendary proficiency in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]], creatures can’t critically succeed on their saves against this spell. You can use [[Actions/Sustain]] an Activation to sustain the effect as you would the spell.
