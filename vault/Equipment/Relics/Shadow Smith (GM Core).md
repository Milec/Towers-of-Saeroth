# Shadow Smith  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Shadow

---

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Your relic pulls at nearby shadows, twisting them into the shape of a simple weapon or a simple tool or item, such as a [[Equipment/Rope|rope]] or [[Equipment/Crowbar|crowbar]]. It lasts until it's used for a single activity, until you use shadow smith again, or for 1 minute, whichever comes first, after which it dissipates.
