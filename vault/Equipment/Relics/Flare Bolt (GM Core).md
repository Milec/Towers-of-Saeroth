# Flare Bolt  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 314

**Aspect** Fire

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** A bolt of flames scorches out from the relic. Make a spell attack roll with your relic against a target within 30 feet. The bolt deals 1d6 fire damage for every 2 levels the relic has (minimum 1d6).

**Critical Success** The bolt deals double damage, as well as [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] equal to the level of the relic.  

**Success** The bolt deals full damage.
