# Sprout Ally  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your relic rapidly matures and animates nearby plant matter into an ally to fight by you, casting [[Spells/Summon Plant or Fungus|_summon plant or fungus_]], with a rank of half the item's level rounded up. You can Sustain this activation as you would the spell.
