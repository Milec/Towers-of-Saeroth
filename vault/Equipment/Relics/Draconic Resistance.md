# Draconic Resistance  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 230

**Aspect** Dragon

---

You grow scales that guard against draconic attacks. You gain a +1 status bonus to AC against attacks from [[Traits/Dragon|dragons]] and resistance to an energy type corresponding to the soul seed's draconic origin (such as fire resistance from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Red|red dragon]]) equal to half the relic's level (minimum resistance 1).
