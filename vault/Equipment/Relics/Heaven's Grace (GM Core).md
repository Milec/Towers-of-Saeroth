# Heaven's Grace  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 311

**Aspect** Celestial

**Prerequisite** The relic is a worn item.

---

The heavens protect you from harm. While wearing the relic, you gain a +1 status bonus to AC against attacks by [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]] and resistance to an energy type corresponding to the relic's celestial origin (such as sonic resistance from a [[Bestiary/Choral|choral]] angel). The resistance is equal to half the relic's level (minimum 1).
