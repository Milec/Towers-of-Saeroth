# Sprout Ally  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 103

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your relic rapidly matures and animates nearby plant matter into an ally to fight by you, casting [[Spells/Summon Plant or Fungus|_summon plant or fungus_]], with a level of half the item’s level rounded up. You can Sustain this Activation as you would the spell.
