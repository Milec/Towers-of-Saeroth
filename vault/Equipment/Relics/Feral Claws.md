# Feral Claws  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 97

**Aspect** Beast

**Prerequisite** The relic is a worn item.

---

**Activate** single action command;     **Effect** Your hands transform into a pair of claws. These grant you a claw unarmed attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits. This lasts until you Dismiss the effect.
