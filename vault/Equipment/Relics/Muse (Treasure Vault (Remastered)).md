# Muse  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 198

**Aspect** Artistry

**Prerequisite** The relic is 3rd level or higher.

---

The relic guides you in your art like a muse, giving you inspirational ideas and providing an item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks. (Choose one. If your relic allows you to do so, you can take this gift again to choose the other.) If the relic is 3rd level or higher, you gain a +1 item bonus. Once the relic attains 9th level, the bonus increases to +2. The bonus increases to +3 when the relic is 17th level.
