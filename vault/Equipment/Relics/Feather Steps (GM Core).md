# Feather Steps  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Air

**Prerequisite** The relic is a worn item.

---

While wearing the relic, you do not trigger traps that use weight or pressure plates as a trigger. Also, whenever you [[Rules/Bright Light|fall]], you reduce the falling damage by the level of the relic. If this would reduce the falling damage to 0, you land on your feet and are not [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The relic grants a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks made to Jump, increasing to +2 at 9th level and +3 at 17th level.
