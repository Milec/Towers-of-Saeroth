# Overflowing Life  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 315

**Aspect** Life

---

Life force flows through you, and vitality energy causes your vital essence to burst through your skin like beads of liquid light. You gain an item bonus equal to half the relic's level (minimum 1) to the Hit Points you recover from [[Traits/Healing|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] spells; this bonus applies only the first time per casting that a particular spell heals you.
