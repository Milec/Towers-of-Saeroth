# Psychic Scream  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 316

**Aspect** Mind

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])    **Effect** Your thoughts build before spilling forth in a powerful telepathic scream. Enemies within a 20-foot-radius burst centered on you must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save or take 1d10 mental damage for every 2 levels of the relic. On a critical failure, they are also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute.
