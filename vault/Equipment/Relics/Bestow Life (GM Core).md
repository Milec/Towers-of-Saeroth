# Bestow Life  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 315

**Aspect** Life

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** An infusion of vitality energy from your relic can temporarily animate an unliving object. You transform an adjacent inanimate object into an [[Bestiary/Families/Animated Object|animated object]] with a level equal to one-half the relic's level and appropriate to the object (so a broom would become an [[Bestiary/Animated Broom|animated broom]]). The object pursues a single simple noncombat task you state when you use Bestow Life, ignoring any subsequent commands. The object is [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] and pursues the task single-mindedly and in an uncreative manner. The object remains animated for 1 hour.
