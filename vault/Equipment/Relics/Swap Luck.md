# Swap Luck  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Luck

---

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You roll a failure or critical failure on a saving throw against an effect with multiple targets;  **Effect** You swap rolls with another target who’s willing to swap with you. Each of you determines the new results with your new rolls.
