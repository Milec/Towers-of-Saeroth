# Call of the Wild  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 310

**Aspect** Beast

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Your relic summons a creature to fight at your side, casting [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]] with a spell rank of half the item's level rounded up. You can Sustain this activation as you would the spell.
