# Sands of the Hourglass  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 200

**Aspect** Time

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You target one creature within 30 feet, temporarily imposing the decrepitude of time on it. The target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. The [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] conditions last 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1.  

**Failure** The target becomes clumsy 2, enfeebled 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target becomes clumsy 3, enfeebled 3, and drained 2.
