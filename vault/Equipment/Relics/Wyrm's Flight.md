# Wyrm's Flight  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 230

**Aspect** Dragon

---

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Ghostly draconic wings grant you a fly Speed equal to your Speed or 20 feet, whichever is greater. This lasts for 10 minutes or until you Dismiss the effect. At 17th level, you can Activate and Dismiss the ability as often as you want, and it has unlimited duration.
