# Dark Roads  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Shadow]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 104

**Aspect** Shadow

---

**Activate** two actions command, envision;     **Effect** Your relic creates a path from your shadow to a nearby one, teleporting you and any items you’re wearing or holding from your current space to an unoccupied one within 30 feet that you can see. You can’t use the dark roads gift again for 1d4 rounds. If the destination is not within an area of [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]], the range is instead 60 feet. If this would bring another creature with you—even if you’re carrying it in an [[Traits/Extradimensional|extradimensional]] container—the gift fails.
