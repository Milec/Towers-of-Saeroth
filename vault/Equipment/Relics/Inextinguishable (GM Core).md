# Inextinguishable  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 315

**Aspect** Life

---

Your connection to life force makes you more difficult to snuff out than others. You die from the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition at dying 5, rather than dying 4. If you roll a success on a save against a [[Traits/Death|death]] or [[Traits/Void|void]] effect, you get a critical success instead.
