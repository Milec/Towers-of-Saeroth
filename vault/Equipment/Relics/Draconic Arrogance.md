# Draconic Arrogance  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 230

**Aspect** Dragon

---

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You're about to roll a saving throw;  **Effect** You laugh at fate and revel in your draconic arrogance. If you roll a critical success on the saving throw, you can attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the creature that created the effect that forced you to roll the saving throw (if any). If you roll a success, you critically succeed instead. If you roll a failure (but not a critical failure), you succeed instead. If you roll a critical failure, however, you're forced to face your own hubris and become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]].
