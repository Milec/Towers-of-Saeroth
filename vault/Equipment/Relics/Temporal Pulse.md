# Temporal Pulse  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 200

**Aspect** Time

---

**Activate** two actions envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You make time shudder around you, stealing it from enemies and granting it to allies. Creatures of your choice within 30 feet must attempt a Will save. For each creature you render slowed with this gift, you can render one ally within 30 feet, including you, [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is slowed 2.
