# Mental Bastion  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 316

**Aspect** Mind

---

Your relic takes over some of your mental processes and enhances others. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy|telepathy]]; if you already have telepathy, you instead increase its range by 30 feet. Whenever you would become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]], reduce the value by 1. You are permanently [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] and can use the additional action to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]].
