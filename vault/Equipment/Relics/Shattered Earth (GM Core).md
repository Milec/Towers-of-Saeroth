# Shattered Earth  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 313

**Aspect** Earth

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Seismic pulses from your relic ripple through the nearby ground, shaking sharp fragments to the surface. A 10-foot-radius patch of stone or earth within 60 feet is transformed into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. A creature that enters an affected square during a move action must succeed at an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save or take 1d6 piercing damage; it needs to roll only once per [[Traits/Move|move]] action even if it moves through several squares. At 6th level and every 4 levels thereafter, increase the damage by 1d6.
