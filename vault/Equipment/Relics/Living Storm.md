# Living Storm  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 96

**Aspect** Air

---

The relic grants you a fly Speed equal to your Speed or 60 feet, whichever is greater. In addition, a creature that Strikes you with a melee attack takes 5 electricity damage, and you can cast 5th-level [[Spells/Lightning Bolt|_lightning bolt_]] as an innate spell.
