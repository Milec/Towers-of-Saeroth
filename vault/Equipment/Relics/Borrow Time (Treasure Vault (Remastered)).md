# Borrow Time  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 200

**Aspect** Time

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You point the relic at a creature within 30 feet, imbuing it with urgency. The creature gains the [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] condition for 1 round. After the condition ends, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round and temporarily immune to this gift until you next perform your daily preparations.
