# Pollen Spray  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 103

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** Pollen and poison spores spray from your relic, dealing 1d6 poison damage for every 2 relic levels (minimum 1d6) to all creatures in a 15-foot cone (basic Fortitude save). On a critical failure, targets are also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round. You can’t use Pollen Spray again for 1d4 rounds.
