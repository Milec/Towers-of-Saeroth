# Enthralling  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 198

**Aspect** Artistry

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You speak at length about or demonstrate your craft, or you enact a performance. The relic casts [[Spells/Enthrall|_enthrall_]] at a level equal to half its own. If you have legendary proficiency in [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]], creatures can't critically succeed on their saves against this spell. You can use Sustain an Activation to sustain the effect as you would the spell.
