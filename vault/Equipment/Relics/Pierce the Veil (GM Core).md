# Pierce the Veil  *Relic Major Gift*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 312

**Aspect** Death

**Prerequisite** living death gift

---

The relic allows you to pierce the veil between life and death. While you are holding or wearing the relic, you have the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|void healing]] ability (as though you were [[Traits/Undead|undead]]; vitality energy harms you and void energy heals you). In addition, whenever you are healed by a [[Traits/Void|void]] spell or effect, you gain resistance 5 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force|force]], _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]]_, [[Traits/Spirit|spirit]], and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) until the start of your next turn. Finally, you gain a +2 item bonus to all saves against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects while you are the bearer of the relic; this increases to a +3 item bonus at 13th level.
