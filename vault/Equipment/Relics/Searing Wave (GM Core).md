# Searing Wave  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 314

**Aspect** Fire

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** You allow a portion of the fire magic housed in your relic to escape in a direction of your choice. You deal 1d10 fire damage for every 2 levels of the relic to all creatures in a 30-foot cone ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex). You can't use Searing Wave again for 1d4 rounds.
