# Absorb Injury  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 231

**Aspect** Soul

---

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** an ally within 30 feet takes damage;  **Effect** You forge a conduit to your ally's soul and absorb the injury to protect them. You receive the damage instead of your ally; you also take any additional effects associated with the transferred damage, such as [[Traits/Poison|poison]] on the blade, conditions, or [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] from a critical hit with a _[[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]]_ rune.
