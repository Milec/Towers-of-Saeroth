# Fervor  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Emotion

---

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You gain the emotional fervor benefits for your relic’s [[Rules/Sidebars/Emotional Focus|emotional state]] for the next 3 rounds, but ignore the spell associated with the fervor. The feeling wears off quickly, so you experience no emotional fallout.
