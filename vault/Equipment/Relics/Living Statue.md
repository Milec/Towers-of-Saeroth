# Living Statue  *Relic Grand Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 100

**Aspect** Earth

---

Your relic’s energies have given you control over the boundary between flesh and stone. You can cast 6th-level [[Spells/Flesh to Stone|_flesh to stone_]] and [[Spells/Stone to Flesh|_stone to flesh_]] as innate spells, each once per day. As long as you are standing on the earth, your body reflexively petrifies at the moment you are struck, granting you resistance 5 to physical damage (except adamantine). This increases to 8 in caves or subterranean environments.
