# Swap Luck  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 199

**Aspect** Luck

---

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You roll a failure or critical failure on a saving throw against an effect with multiple targets;  **Effect** You swap rolls with another target who's willing to swap with you. Each of you determines the new results with your new rolls.
