# Dragon Strike  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 230

**Aspect** Dragon

---

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** Ghostly images of draconic claws form around your hands and linger in the air when you attack. For 1 minute, when you make a successful [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee Strike, you deal an additional 1d6 slashing damage. At 11th level, you can use the ability once per 10 minutes instead of once per hour.
