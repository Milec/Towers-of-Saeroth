# Righteous Call  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 98

**Aspect** Celestial

---

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Holy magic fills your armaments, making them true weapons against evil. For 1 minute, your weapons and unarmed attacks gain the [[Equipment/Holy|_holy_]] weapon rune while you hold them. If a weapon is at its limit on property runes, you can choose one property rune on the weapon to go dormant while the _holy_ rune takes effect. In addition, any successful Strikes your allies make while they’re within 30 feet of you deal 1 additional good damage.
