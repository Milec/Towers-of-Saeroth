# Grappling Vine  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 317

**Aspect** Plant

---

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Attack|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Your relic launches a sticky, multi-stranded vine at a target within 50 feet. Make an attack roll as you would when using a [[Equipment/Grappling Hook|grappling hook]], but if you roll a critical failure on the check to secure the vine, you get a failure instead. Once the vine is anchored, creatures receive a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb]] the vine, and they gain a +5-foot status bonus to the distance they move with a successful check while Climbing using the vine. The vine lasts for 1 day or until you use grappling vine again, at which point your older vine withers.
