# Death Gaze  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 312

**Aspect** Death

---

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])    **Effect** Your stare causes a creature's flesh to rot and wither away. Target a creature you can see within 30 feet. Your stare deals 1d6 void damage to the creature for every 2 levels the relic has (minimum 1d6; [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). An undead creature or other creature with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|void healing]] targeted this way is instead healed 1d6 HP per level of the relic and is temporarily immune to this effect for 10 minutes.
