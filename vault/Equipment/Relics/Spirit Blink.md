# Spirit Blink  *Relic Major Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 231

**Aspect** Soul

---

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You blink between the realm of mortals and the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane]]. This grants you the effects of [[Spells/Blink|_blink_]], except the resistance is equal to the soul seed's level instead of 5.
