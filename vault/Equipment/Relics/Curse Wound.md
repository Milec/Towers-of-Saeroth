# Curse Wound  *Relic Minor Gift*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 100

**Aspect** Fiend

**Prerequisite** The relic is a weapon.

---

**Activate** ⤾ command;   **Trigger** Your Strike with your relic deals damage to a creature within 60 feet;  **Effect** The evil within your relic curses an enemy’s wound to ensure they won’t heal. The triggering creature must attempt a Will save or be unable to restore the Hit Points lost by damage from the triggering Strike until its next daily preparations. If you use Curse Wound on a creature already affected by your Curse Wound, your previous application expires.
