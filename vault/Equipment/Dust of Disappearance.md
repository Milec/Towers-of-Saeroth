# Dust of Disappearance  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 570

**Price** 135 gp

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This powder shimmers like a thousand tiny motes of light. Activating the dust by sprinkling it on yourself or a creature within reach casts a 4th-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell with a duration of 1 minute on that creature. This invisibility can’t be negated or seen through by any spell of 3rd level or lower or any item of 5th level or lower.
