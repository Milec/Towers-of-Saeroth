# Demon Bone Tiles  *Item 2+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Catalyst (Howl of the Wild)|Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 111

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

[[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] (add 1 action)

---

These bones from different types of demons can be used to form temporary barriers. When you crush the bone fragments and blow the resulting dust around yourself as you cast [[Spells/Shield (Player Core)|_shield_]], the _shield_ appears as a bone bulwark shaped like the demon’s face.

 When use Shield Block with the spell, the barrier explodes into many bone fragments. The shards cause [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] to each creature adjacent to you that fails a Reflex save at the listed DC. This persistent bleed damage is unholy and can be stopped with an Interact action to remove the shards.

## Pusk Bone Tiles  *Item 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 111

**Price** 7 gp

---
