This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]]_ was fashioned with the enchanted leather of a giant serpent. Several vicious fangs are embedded in the thong and fall of the whip. When wielded in combat, the weapon emanates a faint rattling sound.

**Activate—Slither Strike** ⤾ ;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature within reach Casts a Spell with the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait; **Requirements** ; **Effect** You [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] the triggering creature with the whip. If you hit and deal damage, you also [[Actions/Reposition]] the creature 5 feet (10 feet on a critical hit).
