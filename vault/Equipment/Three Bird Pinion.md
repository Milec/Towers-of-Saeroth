# Three Bird Pinion  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 225

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

---

This talisman is made using the interlocking feathers gathered from at least three kinds of birds, carefully trimmed into a circle. When you activate this talisman, you become lighter than air, causing you to levitate 1 foot off the ground for 1 minute. While levitated this way, you can still move as normal, but you [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignore most difficult, greater difficult, and hazardous terrain]] on the ground.
