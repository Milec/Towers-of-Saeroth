# Sharpened Canines  *Item 3+*

Some of your teeth have been replaced with those of a larger meat-eating predator. You gain a jaws [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage. These jaws are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group.
