# Leydroth Spellbreaker  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 105

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Blunderbuss]]

---

The larynx of a [[Bestiary/Leydroth|leydroth]], a dangerous creature whose roar can unravel magic, is integrated into the firing mechanism of this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+3]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|blunderbuss]]_.

**Activate—Dispelling Blast** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _leydroth spellbreaker_ emits a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of dispelling energy. Attempt a single [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check at +25 against each spell or magical effect in the area, plus one item or effect on each creature in the area, with the effects of [[Spells/Dispel Magic (Player Core)|_dispel magic_]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the larynx of a [[Bestiary/Leydroth|leydroth]].
