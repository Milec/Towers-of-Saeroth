# Keep Stone Amulet  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 101

**Price** 38,000 gp

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

The superior [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]]-lead alloy known as keep stone is as highly prized as it is expensive, and for good reason: keep stone combines the Hardness and durability of the finest metal with inherent magical protection. Not only does the metal itself resist magical effects, but anyone in proximity to a large amount of it also enjoys a degree of protection from divination magic. Dwarven crafters have attempted to make this extra protection more portable and more affordable in the form of _keep stone amulets_. Each amulet is unique in its exact shape, size, thickness, and decorations, but typically, a _keep stone amulet_ is roughly palm-sized and engraved with clan symbols, holy aphorisms, or a favorite line of poetry.

While wearing a _keep stone amulet_, if you’re the target of a spell with the [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]] or [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] trait, the caster must succeed at a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] or lose the spell.

**Activate—Rebuff Magic** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You’re the target of a spell with a [[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack roll]];  **Effect** The caster must roll the spell attack roll twice and take the worse result.
