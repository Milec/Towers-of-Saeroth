# Impenetrable Scale  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 558

**Price** 12,800 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 3

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Scale Mail]]

---

Made of overlapping, lustrous black scales of standard-grade adamantine, this _+2 greater resilient [[Equipment/Fortification|fortification]] [[Equipment/Adamantine Armor|adamantine]] [[Equipment/Armor/Scale Mail|scale mail]]_ seems to momentarily thicken at the point of impact when hit. Whenever the armor’s _fortification_ rune successfully turns a significant foe’s critical hit into a normal hit, one of the scales on the armor turns violet. You gain resistance to physical damage equal to the number of violet scales, to a maximum of 8.

 At dawn each day, all the violet scales return to normal.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include 1,600 gp of adamantine.
