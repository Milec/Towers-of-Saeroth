# Sharkskin Robe  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 178

**Price** 1,900 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** 1

---

This sandy-textured robe comes with sleeves that resemble dorsal fins. It's believed to have been developed by frustrated alchemists from the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane|Universe]] for trips to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water]]. The sharkskin robe grants you a swim Speed equal to your land Speed and a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks.

**Activate—Shark's Elegance** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** For 1 minute, any time you make a Strike, your weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack gains the benefit of the [[Equipment/Underwater|_underwater_]] weapon property rune.
