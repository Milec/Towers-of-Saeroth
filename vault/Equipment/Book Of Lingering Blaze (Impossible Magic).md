# Book Of Lingering Blaze  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 229

**Price** 900 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The common saying, “Where there is progress in the field of magic, there is always someone who uses it to set things on fire,” is engraved in gold on the cover of this red spellbook.

**Activate—Overheat Spell** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a prepared Spell]] that deals fire damage, you superheat the flames, allowing the spell to ignore up to 10 [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to fire of creatures affected by the spell.
