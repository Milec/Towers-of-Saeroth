# Winged Sandals  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 297

**Price** 850 gp

**Usage** worn shoes

**Bulk** L

---

Made from soft leather, with delicate white wings attached near the ankles, these sandals are ensorcelled with powerful air magic. Whenever you fall while wearing these sandals, they automatically cast [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]] on you. This benefit can't trigger again for 10 minutes.

**Activate—Awaken Wings** two actions ([[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The wings grow in size and propel you through the air, granting you a fly Speed of 30 feet for 10 minutes.
