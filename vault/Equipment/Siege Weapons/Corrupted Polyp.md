When [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demonic]] forces invaded through the Worldwound, some brought terrible siege weapons with them. One particularly vile device is known as a _corrupted polyp_, a cultivated fungal colony from the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]] that can quickly mulch a steady stream of corpses and other decaying organic matter into toxic growths. With the right coaxing, the foul weapon can then explosively eject a cluster of these growths to devastating effect. Thankfully, few _corrupted polyps_ were brought over from the Outer Rifts, and even fewer survive now that the Worldwound has been closed.  
**Aim** three actions 50 feet, minimum distance 50 feet  
**Load** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 3 times  
**Launch** three actions ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], manipulate, [[Traits/Range|range increment 200 feet]]) 10d10 poison and 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison]], 50-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], DC 38 Reflex. Because a _corrupted polyp_ launches its spore clusters at a high angle, the cluster descends on the center of the burst from above, not in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] from the polyp. This affects whether creatures have [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] from the launch.
