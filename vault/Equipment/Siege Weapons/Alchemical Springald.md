**Crew** 3 to 5

---

**Ref** +16

**Hardness** 15

**HP** 200

**Immunities**  object immunities

---

**Speed** Speed 10 feet (pulled or pushed)

---

Like the standard springald, an alchemical springald can launch three arrows simultaneously when its paddle is released. It fires specially crafted and balanced bomb arrows that carry black powder enhanced alchemical payloads that explode on impact.  
**Aim** two actions 30 feet, minimum distance 50 feet  
**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 3 times  
**Launch** single action (manipulate, range increment 100 feet) 10d6 damage and 3 [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage, three different targets within a 15-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], DC 30 Reflex. The damage type depends on the alchemical ammunition used: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]. Creatures take the splash damage only once from the launch, even if they’re in the area of more than one of the three splashes.
