A smaller version of the _kickback spring_ was first deployed as a deterrent to keep crops safe from nuisance foragers like [[Bestiary/Moose|moose]]. These larger versions often line walls or other defensive points, providing a final layer of protection before a large creature would breach such areas. The _kickback spring_ fires a compressed disk of rapidly expanding air at creatures, attempting to push them back and provide defenders a moment to regroup.

**Aim** two actions 25 feet, minimum distance 50 feet

**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 2 times, requires a successful DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check

**Launch** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 60 feet]]) 6d8 bludgeoning, 15-foot cone, DC 22 Reflex. A creature that fails its Reflex save is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 25 feet away from the _kickback spring_ as air rapidly vents from the device. On a critical failure, a creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] after being pushed back.
