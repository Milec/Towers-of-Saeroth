A mortar fires its shells at a high angle, allowing it to bypass obstacles and strike from above. A crew typically sets up such a weapon behind a wall or in another hard-to-access area to protect themselves while retaining full functionality. Due to the steep angle of its arc, such mortars are especially useful when opposing barricaded targets.  
 **Aim** two actions 40 feet, minimum distance 50 feet  
 **Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) 3 times  
 **Launch** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]], [[Traits/Range|range increment 100 feet]]) 5d10 slashing, 20-foot burst, DC 21. Because a mortar launches its round at a high angle, the mortar round descends on the center of the burst from above, not in a straight line from the mortar. This affects whether creatures have cover from the mortar launch.
