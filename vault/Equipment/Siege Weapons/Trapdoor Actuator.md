Trapdoor actuators are designed to be well hidden (usually as part of a cliff face) and operated by small, untrained crews. Cleverly designed springs launch an iron rod from the protective wall. Typically, this level of camouflage allows the trapdoor actuator to remain unnoticed unless a creature succeeds at a DC 18 [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] check to spot it. After a launch, the trapdoor actuator’s camouflage can be reset as a two-action activity.

**Aim** two actions 25 feet, minimum distance 10 feet

**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 2 times, requires a successful DC 20 Athletics check

**Launch** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 100 feet]]) 3d10 bludgeoning, single target, DC 18 Reflex
