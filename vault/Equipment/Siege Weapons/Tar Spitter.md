**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 106

---

**Crew** 3 to 4

**Proficiency** simple

---

**AC** 24

**Fort** +20

**Ref** +14

**Hardness** 8

**HP** 120

**Immunities**  object immunities

---

**Speed** 10 feet (pulled or pushed)

---

A tar spitter is a mass of tubes, pumps, and a copper tank mounted on an armored wagon. The tank is filled with hot tar under high pressure that can be sprayed from an aimed nozzle. The weaponized adhesive often roots enemies to the spot, making them easier prey for allied soldiers. Cleaning a tar spitter is a difficult task, and crew members usually wear thick leather aprons and gloves to avoid getting the tar on their skin.  
**Aim** two actions rotate 45°  
**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 3 times, requires a successful DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check  
**Launch** single action (manipulate) 4d8 bludgeoning plus 4d8 acid and 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid]], 60-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] or 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], DC 27 Reflex. A creature on the ground who fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] by the tar ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 27). Switching between the tar spitter’s line or cone mode takes an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, and any of the crew can perform the action.
