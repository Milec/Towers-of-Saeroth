Unearthed from Jistkan ruins at Rachikan, this siege weapon with a tornado motif carved into the barrel has been repaired for modern use. The barrel creates a vortex of air that propels the loaded projectile: a Chelaxian-engineered metal sabot containing fin-stabilized flechettes, achieving greater ranges and devastation than its Jistkan inventors likely ever dreamed possible.  
**Aim** three actions rotate 45°  
**Load** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 2 times, requires a successful DC 21 [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] check
