A mortar fires its shells at a high angle, allowing it to bypass obstacles and strike from above. A crew typically sets up such a weapon behind a wall or in another hard-to-access area to protect themselves while retaining full functionality. Due to the steep angle of its arc, such mortars are especially useful when opposing barricaded targets.  
**Aim** two actions 40 feet, minimum distance 50 feet  
**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 3 times  
**Launch** two actions (manipulate, range increment 100 feet) 5d10 slashing, 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], DC 21. Because a mortar launches its round at a high angle, the mortar round descends on the center of the burst from above, not in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] from the mortar. This affects whether creatures have cover from the mortar launch.
