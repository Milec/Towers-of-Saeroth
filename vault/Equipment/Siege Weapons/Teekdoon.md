# Teekdoon  *Siege Weapon 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], Large, [[Traits/Portable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Portable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 117

**Price** 50 gp

**Ammunition** boulder (0 gp, 5 Bulk)

---

**Usage** portable, held in 8 hands

**Bulk** 15

**Crew** 4-6

**Proficiency** simple

---

While the official name of this contraption is the “portable catapult,” it gained the name “teekdoon” as a reference to the weapon’s initial inspiration: the diving takedowns of peregrine falcons. However, it replaces elegant dives with the brute force of falling weights that hit targets from above.

**Aim** two actions 25 feet, minimum distance 25 feet

**Load** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) 2 times, requires a successful DC 12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check

**Launch** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 80 feet]]) 2d8 bludgeoning, single target, DC 14 Reflex. A boulder launched from a teekdoon descends on a creature from above, which might affect if a creature has [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] against the attack.
