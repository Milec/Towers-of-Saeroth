A flame bellows turns the common alchemist’s fire into a spray of burning destruction. Consisting of a wheel or wagonmounted reservoir and bellows, and a long tube through which the substance is directed, it’s as simple as it is effective. Unfortunately, it’s also incredibly dangerous to operate, with volatile and highly flammable chemicals housed in delicate mechanisms, one mistake away from consuming the whole team and the bellows in a super-heated blaze. The bellows are intended to be wheeled into the thick of battle rather than fired from a distance. Flame bellows teams are known as marked men, with all but the luckiest ending their career as a pile of ash, the victims of their own weapon. Despite not being a black powder weapon, a flame bellows can [[Rules/Siege Weapon Misfire (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|misfire]] like one.
