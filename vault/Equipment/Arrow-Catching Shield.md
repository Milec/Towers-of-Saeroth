# Arrow-Catching Shield  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 587

**Price** 1,350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield]]

---

This remarkable [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield|wooden shield]] (Hardness 10, HP 60, BT 30) is carved with images of overlapping fletched arrows.

**Activate** ⤾ Interact;  **Frequency** once per minute; **Trigger** A ranged weapon Strike targets a creature within 15 feet of you when you have this shield raised, and the attacker has not yet rolled their attack;  **Effect** The triggering Strike targets you instead of its normal target. If it hits, you gain the effects of the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction.
