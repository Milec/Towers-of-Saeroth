# Disintegration Bolt  *Item 15*

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

The shaft of this bolt is scorched and blackened, and handling it coats your fingers with a fine black powder. When an activated _disintegration bolt_  hits a target, it's subject to a [[Spells/Disintegrate (Player Core)|_disintegrate_]] spell requiring a DC 34 Fortitude save. As with the spell, a critical hit on the attack roll causes the target's saving throw outcome to be one degree worse.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[Spells/Disintegrate (Player Core)|_disintegrate_]].
