# Starfall Shield  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 22

**Price** 23,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Heavy Rondache (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Heavy Rondache]]

---

This [[Equipment/Adamantine Shield|high-grade adamantine]] [[Equipment/Shields/Heavy Rondache (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|heavy rondache]] (Hardness 17, HP  100, BT 50) bears a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Boss|shield boss]]_ and has dents that resemble craters on its surface. While wielding the shield, you have fire resistance 15.

**Activate** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You Stride up to three times. This movement doesn't trigger reactions and ignores difficult terrain. At the end of your movement, you deal 6d10 bludgeoning damage in a 10-foot emanation and 14d6 fire damage in a 40-foot emanation (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save, with the results applying to both the bludgeoning and fire damage). The space where you end your movement and all adjacent spaces become difficult terrain for 1 minute, and the shield glows red-hot for 10 minutes, during which it gains the effect of the [[Equipment/Flaming|_greater flaming_]] property rune.
