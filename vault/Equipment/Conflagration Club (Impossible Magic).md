# Conflagration Club  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 209

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub (Player Core)|Greatclub]]

---

A ring of magical crystals encircles the base of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatclub (Player Core)|greatclub]]_, allowing it to absorb and store magical energy. These crystals irregularly flash a deep crimson light.

**Activate—Absorb Arcana** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** ; **Trigger** You Cast a Spell from a spell slot, and the spell has the acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic trait; **Requirements** The club has no charges; **Effect** You mentally align the club’s crystals to reverberate with energy from the triggering spell. The club gains a number of charges equal to the rank of the spell slot, and the charges are attuned to the damage type matching the spell’s trait. If the spell has more than one eligible trait, choose one when the charges are stored.

**Activate—Explosive Cone** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** ;  **Requirements** The club has at least 1 charge; **Effect** You flip the club around to blast a cone of energy from its base. Each creature in a 15-foot cone takes 1d6 damage per charge stored in the club, with a DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage type is the same type the charges were attuned to. The club loses all its charges.
