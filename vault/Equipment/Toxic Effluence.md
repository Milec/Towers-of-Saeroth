# Toxic Effluence  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Contact]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 80

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

This dark green sludge has a caustic scent and gritty texture. Capable of entering the body through contact with flesh, toxic effluence becomes harmful once it hits the bloodstream, where it causes internal chemical burns, intense pain, and muscle spasms.

 **Saving Throw** DC 29 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage, 3d6 fire damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 4d6 poison damage, 4d6 fire damage, clumsy 2, and enfeebled 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 5d6 poison damage, 5d6 fire damage, clumsy 3, and enfeebled 3 (1 round)
