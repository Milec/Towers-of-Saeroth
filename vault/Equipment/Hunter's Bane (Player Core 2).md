# Hunter's Bane  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|Detection]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 305

**Price** 6 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** A [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] enemy hits you with an attack

**Requirement** You're trained in Survivial.

---

This talisman is a ring of dried, interwoven pieces of straw. When you activate the _hunter's bane_, you sense the exact location of the attacker. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]] by you if it was [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from you or becomes hidden from you if it was [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]]. If the attacker is behind lead, the _hunter's bane_ has no effect.
