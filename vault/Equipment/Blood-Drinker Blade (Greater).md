# Blood-Drinker Blade  *Item 5+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 82

**Bulk** L

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|shortsword]]_ has the design of a fanged mouth carved into the handle. Whenever the blade draws blood, it glows with a dull red energy, as if empowered by the taste of blood.

**Activate—Drink Life** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _blood-drinker blade_ guides your hand to cut a foe and feeds you from their blood. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with the blade. On a hit, the attack deals an additional 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. You also gain 1d8 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] for 1 minute.

## Blood-Drinker Blade (Greater)  *Item 9*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -213- Thirst for Blood|Pathfinder #213: Thirst for Blood]] pg. 82

**Price** 700 gp

**Hands** 1

**Bulk** L

---

This is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Wounding (GM Core)|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortsword (Player Core)|shortsword]]_. You can activate to Drink Life with the blade once per hour instead of once per day. This weapon also has the Exsanguinate activation.

**Activate—Exsanguinate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** Your most recent action was to successfully deal bleed damage to a creature with [Drain Life](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3284); **Effect** The blade’s power draws even more blood from your victim. The required creature takes 6d6 bleed damage. You gain [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the bleed damage the creature takes (after applying resistances and the like). You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.
