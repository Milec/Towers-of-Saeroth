single action

Gorgon's breath is a fine powder that can easily enter living creatures' bloodstreams through their lungs before binding to mucous membranes and causing any nearby soft tissues to harden.

 **Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 round; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 4d6 bludgeoning damage and slowed 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]] (1 round); **Stage 4** petrified permanently
