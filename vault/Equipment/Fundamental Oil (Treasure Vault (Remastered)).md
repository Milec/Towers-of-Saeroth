Made of elemental salts and essences from the [[Rules/Inner Sphere]] or where such planes leak onto [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], _fundamental oil_ is anathema to elementals and other creatures with a weakness to elemental damage. A weapon anointed with this oil acts as [[Equipment/Bane Oil (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_bane oil_]] to elementals, but the damage type is the same as the target’s greatest weakness if the target has [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] to acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic damage. If it has none of these, the additional damage is the same type as the weapon’s damage type. These effects last 1 minute.
