# Masquerade Scarf  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 292

**Usage** worn

---

This delicately embroidered scarf matches with every outfit and can even complete a costume or disguise with illusions.

**Activate—Masquerade**  1 minute ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You arrange the scarf over your lower face, and it casts a 1st-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] spell on you, which ends immediately if the scarf is removed. You can alter the scarf's appearance or make it invisible as part of the _illusory disguise_, but it can still be felt if touched.
