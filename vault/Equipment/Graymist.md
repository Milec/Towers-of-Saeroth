# Graymist  *Item 21*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Intelligent]], [[Traits/Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -206- Bring the House Down|Pathfinder #206: Bring the House Down]] pg. 83

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

_Graymist_ functions as a [[Equipment/Shadowmist Cape (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_shadowmist cape_]].

Once restored, _Graymist_ gains these activations.

**Activate—Diverting Reveal** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** _Graymist_ manifests a dangerous-looking assassin, who appears to step out out of hiding at any point you can sense within 60 feet. This allows _Graymist_ to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] using his [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] skill, but the closest creature to where the Diverting Reveal appears must attempt a DC 44 Will save or become so startled by the assassin’s sudden appearance that they become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 3 (or stunned 6 on a critical failure). The assassin vanishes a moment after the diverting reveal, appearing to go back into hiding.

**Activate—Traceless Retreat** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** _Graymist_ casts [[Spells/Mislead (Player Core)|_mislead_]] on yourself and up to three other targets of your choice within 30 feet. _Graymist_ [[Actions/Sustain|sustains]] this effect up to 1 minute, during which you cannot activate _Graymist_ and _Graymist_ cannot take any other actions.
