# Thurible of Revelation  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 277

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This brass censer dangles on a length of chain. Most _thuribles of revelation_ are adorned with swirling [[Setting/Languages/Empyrean]] text, though some are iron and feature [[Setting/Languages/Diabolic]] or [[Setting/Languages/Chthonian]] text.

**Activate—Burn Incense** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])**Cost** incense worth
at least 5 gp;    **Effect** You light the incense inside the censer, and it burns for 1 hour. During that time, as long you are holding the thurible, you gain a +1 item bonus to Religion checks, and any critical failure you roll when you [[Actions/Decipher Writing]] of a religious nature is a failure instead.

## Thurible of Revelation (Lesser)  *Item 3*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 277, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 575

**Price** 55 gp

## Thurible of Revelation (Moderate)  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 277, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 575

**Price** 900 gp

---

The thurible grants a +2 bonus. Once per day, when you activate the thurible, you can increase its revelations. During that activation, you can hold the thurible up to your eyes with an Interact action to gain the effects of [[Spells/See the Unseen|_see the unseen_]] for 1 round by peering through the smoke.

## Thurible of Revelation (Greater)  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 277, [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 575

**Price** 19,000 gp

---

The thurible grants a +3 bonus. The greater version shares the once-per-day enhanced revelations of the moderate version, except peering through the smoke also grants you the effects of [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]].
