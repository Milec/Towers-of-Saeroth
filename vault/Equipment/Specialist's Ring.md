# Specialist's Ring  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 190

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** worn

---

Each _specialist's ring_ is dedicated to a single school of magic, and the ring is covered in symbols and glyphs related to that school according to the creator's arcane studies. A _specialist's ring_ has the trait corresponding to its school of magic. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] checks, and a +1 circumstance bonus to recognize magical effects and items of the specific school of magic.

**Activate** free action envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can use only to cast a wizard school spell of the corresponding school. If not used by the end of your turn, this Focus Point is lost.
