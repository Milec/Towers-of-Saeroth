# Brine Dragon Scale  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Talisman]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 176

**Price** 100 gp

**Usage** affixed to a melee weapon

**Activate**

⤾

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

**Trigger** A creature in reach of the weapon takes an action with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.

---

[[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Brine|Brine dragons]] are known to distract their foes at just the right moment, and this blue-green scale appears to have come from one of these creatures. When you activate the scale, it cracks open and releases a spray of caustic saltwater at the triggering creature. The creature takes 2d8 acid damage with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. If the creature critically fails at its save, its concentration is broken—the triggering action is disrupted.
