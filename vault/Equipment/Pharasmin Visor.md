# Pharasmin Visor  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -210- Whispers in the Dirt|Pathfinder #210: Whispers in the Dirt]] pg. 79

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Popular in Ustalav and other lands where undead are numerous, a [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|_Pharasmin_]] _visor_ is a valuable part of any fashionable noble’s armory when attending events in places like Caliphas. This item consists of a metal skullcap helm with a pair of blue crystal lenses extending from each side on metal armatures that can be moved independently in front of or away from the eyes. The helm can appear as either gleaming silver or dull black, and the wearer can alter its appearance whenever they [[Actions/Invest an Item (GM Core)|invest]] the visor.

**Activate—Pharasma’s Disdain** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You adjust the visors so that both are lowered. Your vision becomes obscured, causing you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]]. At the same time, a tiny fraction of Pharasma’s notice seeps out of the visor to fill the area around you in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. All [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures in this area take 12d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage (DC 30 basic Will save). At the end of your turn, both visors automatically rise back up, but you remain dazzled until the start of your next turn.

**Activate—Pierce the Night** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You adjust the helm so that only the left visor is lowered. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] until you adjust the visors again or until the item is no longer invested by you, whichever comes first.

**Activate—Reveal the Unseen** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You adjust the helm so that only the right visor is lowered. You can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] creatures as though they weren’t invisible, although their features are blurred, making them [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] and difficult to identify. You can also see [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures like [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|ghosts]] when they have phased through an object if you are within 10 feet of an object’s surface; they look like blurry shapes seen through those objects. This effect lasts until 8 hours pass, until you adjust the visors again, or until the item is no longer invested by you, whichever comes first.

**Activate—See Through Death** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You adjust the visors so that both are raised. The _Pharasmin visor_ grants a +2 item bonus on all saving throws against effects with the [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] trait.
