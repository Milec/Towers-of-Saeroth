_Quenching potion_ is a clear liquid that tastes like fresh, cool spring water. Drinking this potion or pouring it over yourself completely hydrates you and cleanses your system. Immediately attempt a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to end any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid, fire, poison, or void damage]] affecting you, and attempt a new saving throw against any [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] affecting you. Poison can’t progress to a worse stage due to this saving throw. The potion counts as a particularly appropriate type of help against persistent acid, fire, poison, and void damage.
