# Animal Call  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 112

**Price** 5 sp

---

Animal calls are often whistles or similar devices that imitate the calls of [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]]. Each call is for a specific type of animal, such as a duck or bear. When you use the call, it gives you a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]], provided the animal is of the type as the call. You also do not take a circumstance penalty when attempting to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] that animal for not sharing a language.
