---

The magical impulse control upgrade attaches the [[Rules/Wheelchairs (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|wheelchair]] to your fingers or nerve impulses, making it accessible for those with mobility restrictions or other health conditions You still can't move the wheelchair if you're physically or magically prevented from doing so, such as by being [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or magically [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]].
