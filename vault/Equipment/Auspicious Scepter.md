# Auspicious Scepter  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 179

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace]]

---

This imperious _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Mace|mace]]_ has a glowing orb in the shape of an eye set in its flanged head. When you succeed at a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about a creature after you've dealt it damage with the _auspicious scepter_, you learn one of its resistances in addition to any other information.

**Activate** free action command ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]]) **Frequency** once every 10 minutes; **Trigger** You Cast a divination spell;  **Effect** The next attack by a creature who was targeted by the triggering divination spell bypasses an amount of resistance equal to the spell's level. If the triggering spell targeted an enemy, this benefit instead applies to the next attack against that enemy, regardless of who makes it.

 This benefit is lost if it isn't used by the end of your next turn. If more than one type of resistance would apply against the attack, the attack bypasses only one—whichever would result in the highest amount of damage getting through, as determined by the GM.
