# Shadowmist Cape  *Item 19*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 104

**Price** 40,000 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This black cape flows more like a vaporous liquid than fabric. The cape grants you a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks. When you invest the cape, you either increase your Dexterity modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value
.

**Activate** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** A creature misses you with an attack;  **Effect** Attempt a Stealth check against the triggering creature's Perception DC. If you roll a success, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from that creature until the end of your next turn or until right after you use a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against that creature. If you roll a critical success, you're hidden from that creature until the end of your next turn, even if you use hostile actions against that creature.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** With a twirl of the cape, you transform yourself into a puff of gray smoke. You cast [[Spells/Vapor Form|_vapor form_]] on yourself.
