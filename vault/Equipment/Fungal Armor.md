# Fungal Armor  *Item 15*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -161- Belly of the Black Whale|Pathfinder #161: Belly of the Black Whale]] pg. 77

**Price** 7,200 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor]]

---

This _+2 [[Equipment/Resilient|greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor|studded leather]]_ is made of hardened fungus. Every day the armor grows dozens of unusual mushrooms that can be used for cooking, infused reagents, or common spell material components.

**Activate** reaction or one action envision;  **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You are hit by a melee, ranged, or spell attack;  **Effect** The attack misses you, dealing no damage as you transform into a cloud of spores. You gain the benefits of a [[Spells/Gaseous Form|_gaseous form_]] spell for 1 minute. For the duration, a cloud of deadly spores surrounds you. Each breathing creature that begins its turn within 10 feet of you takes 6d8 poison damage (DC 35 basic Fortitude save). You can Dismiss the cloud form.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Spells/Cloudkill|_cloudkill_]] and [[Spells/Gaseous Form|_gaseous form_]].
