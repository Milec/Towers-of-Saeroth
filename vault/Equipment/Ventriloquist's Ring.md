# Ventriloquist's Ring  *Item 0*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 617

**Usage** worn

---

This elegant copper ring has miniature images of songbirds engraved around its circumference. You gain a +1 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** Twisting the ring around your finger allows you to magically throw your voice, with the effects of a [[Spells/Ventriloquism|_ventriloquism_]] spell (DC 19).
