A star inked on the back of the hand, usually between the thumb and forefinger, keeps you on the right path.

**Activate** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** As you hold up your hand and align the star in view, you learn which direction you're facing.
