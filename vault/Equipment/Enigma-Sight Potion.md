# Enigma-Sight Potion  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Potion]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 89

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

A vial of this _enigma-sight potion_ seems to contain fragments of kaleidoscopic crystal that reflect mind-bending scenes. However, these “crystals” act like liquid when poured or consumed. Drinking the potion grants you the effects of a [[Spells/Truesight|_true seeing_]] spell, except that you use your [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] modifier and your level for the secret [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check instead of the normal counteract modifier and spell level. However, if you critically fail the secret counteract check the effect grants, your mind fills in false, nightmarish information. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1d4 rounds and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 as your brain struggles to process what you see.
