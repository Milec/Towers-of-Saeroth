# Spike Launcher  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 156

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** held in 1 or 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

---

Built from the spiked tail of a [[Bestiary/Manticore (Monster Core)|manticore]], a _spike launcher_ is designed to launch large, spear-like projectiles. A spike launcher is a _+1 striking weapon_. It’s a distinct type of martial firearm that deals 1d8 piercing damage. It has the [[Traits/Backstabber (Player Core)|backstabber]], [[Traits/Fatal Aim (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|fatal aim d12]], and [[Traits/Kickback (NPC Core)|kickback]] traits with a range increment of 120 feet and reload 2. It uses the critical specialization of the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow]] weapon group, rather than the firearm critical specialization.

**Activate** three actions Interact ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** The _spike launcher_ is loaded; **Effect** The _spike launcher_ fires a volley of smaller spikes in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] centered anywhere within its range. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the _spike launcher_ against each creature in the area. On a success, the spike causes the creature to take a –5-foot status penalty to their Speed. On a critical success, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] instead. In either case, the creature, or an adjacent creature, can use an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to remove the spike and end the penalty or immobilized condition. The wielder's multiple attack penalty does not increase until they've completed all granted Strikes.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the tail of a manticore.
