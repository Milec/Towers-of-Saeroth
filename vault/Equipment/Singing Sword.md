# Singing Sword  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|CG]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Intelligent]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 89

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword]]

---

**Perception** +11; precise vision 30 feet, imprecise hearing 30 feet

**Communication** speech ([[Setting/Languages/Common]] and four other common languages)

**Skills** [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Performance]] +15

**Int** +2, **Wis** +2, **Cha** +4

**Will** +13 (+17 against attempts to quiet its singing)

---  

A _singing sword_ is a _+1 striking [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_ imbued with the consciousness of a boisterous bard, and therefore constantly sings at all times. A _singing sword_ can’t stop singing and in fact communicates in no way other than by singing. A successful [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check against its Will DC can convince it to quiet its singing to a whisper for 10 minutes, or 20 minutes on a critical success, though it quickly grows displeased at anyone who attempts this repeatedly. The _singing sword_ can spend its actions to attack on its own, with the effects of a [[Equipment/Dancing|_dancing_]] weapon’s activation, except that its attack modifier is +12. Additionally, it can perform the following activations; each casts a composition spell and follows all the usual rules for [[Traits/Composition|compositions]].

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]].

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per minute;   **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts [[Spells/Inspire Defense|_inspire defense_]].

**Activate** ⤾ command;  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You or an ally within 60 feet rolls a saving throw against an [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] effect;  **Effect** The _singing sword_ casts [[Spells/Counter Performance|_counter performance_]].
