# Dawnfire Beacon  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 140

**Usage** affixed or held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

The warm, caring light of the sun glows from the center of this magical banner, mirroring the dawn. This magical banner exudes [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in the banner’s aura (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in an area equal to twice the banner’s aura). This effect is suppressed when you aren’t holding the banner or wielding the weapon it is affixed to.

## Major Dawnfire Beacon  *Item 13*
