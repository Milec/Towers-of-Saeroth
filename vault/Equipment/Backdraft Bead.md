# Backdraft Bead  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 214

**Price** 25 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

Swirling winds shift and whirl within the confines of this small glass marble. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Fireball (Player Core)|_fireball_]]_ spell, a creature that fails its saving throw is also knocked back 5 feet from the center of the spell (10 feet on a critical failure).
