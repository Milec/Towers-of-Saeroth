# Egg Cream Fizz  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 48

**Price** 27 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Containing neither eggs nor cream, an egg cream fizz contains milk or nut milk, sparkling water, and flavored syrup, frothed and chilled. Upon drinking, you feel lighter and more buoyant, gaining a +5-foot item bonus to your Speed for 10 minutes. During this time, you also gain another effect determined by the drink’s syrup, which is chosen when the drink is created.

- **Chocolate** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]], [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]], and [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]].
- **Strawberry** You gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], and [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]].
- **Vanilla** You can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].
