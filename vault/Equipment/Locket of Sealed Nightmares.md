# Locket of Sealed Nightmares  *Item 19*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -156- The Apocalypse Prophet|Pathfinder #156: The Apocalypse Prophet]] pg. 74

**Price** 36,000 gp

**Usage** worn necklace

---

As long as this locket of horn and silver is closed, you don't need to sleep. However, you can still overexert your body with too much activity, so you still need to rest to avoid becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] or to remove the fatigued condition. You also gain a +4 item bonus on saving throws against mental illusions, magical effects that would make you [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], and effects that would make you fatigued. Each time dawn occurs, you regain the ability to make your daily preparations as if you had rested for 8 hours. Daily preparations still take about an hour, as normal.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;    **Requirements** You aren't fatigued; **Effect** You open the locket to unleash the dreams it has kept at bay. You become fatigued and cast one of the following spells at 9th level (DC 41): [[Spells/Hallucination|_hallucination_]], [[Spells/Sleep|_sleep_]], or [[Spells/Weird|_weird_]].
