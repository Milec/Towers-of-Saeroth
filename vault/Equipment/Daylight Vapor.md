# Daylight Vapor  *Item 13*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 77

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This heavier-than-air mist emits a soft glow. While this poison lasts, it causes its victims to shine from within with agonizing, brilliant radiance. This effect has the [[Traits/Light|light]] trait (although the poison itself doesn't, so only the light effect can be [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracted]] by [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] effects). The [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition from _daylight vapor_ can't be removed while the poison lasts, and when the victim enters an area of magical darkness, the poison attempts to counteract the darkness with a counteract modifier of +21. If the poison fails, it can't attempt to counteract the same darkness again.

**Saving Throw** DC 31 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 round; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 poison damage and sickened 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 6d6 poison damage and sickened 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 10d6 damage and sickened 3 (1 round)

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[Spells/Sunburst|_sunburst_]].
