# Soulheart  *Item 5+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Bastion of Blasphemies

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Artifact (GM Core)|Artifact]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

---

A _soulheart_ is a crystallized sphere of red ectoplasm about the size of a human eye. When held by a living creature, a faint red light shimmers within the crystal, pulsating in a synchronized beat with the creature’s heartbeat and providing illumination equivalent to that of a candle.

**Activate—Bolster Life** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** When held to the chest and focused upon, the soul energy within the _soulheart_ floods outward and bolsters your life force, granting you temporary Hit Points. The number of temporary Hit Points granted depends on the type of the _soulheart_, but regardless of type, they last for 8 hours.

**Activate—Bolster Maiserene Phantom** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** When touched to a Maiserene phantom of the proper rank (depending on the type of _soulheart_), the _soulheart_ flashes with bright red light and is destroyed. As it does so, it permanently increases the targeted phantom to rank 1, and increases each PC’s maximum Hit Points by 1 for as long as they remain within Bastardhall while the castle remains cursed. More powerful _soulhearts_ can increase higher-rank phantoms and grant more Hit Points.

**Destruction** A _soulheart_ is destroyed when it is activated to Bolster Maiserene Phantom, Restore Life, or Defy Charg.

## Soulheart  *Item 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Bulk** L

---

When used to Bolster Life, the _soulheart_ grants 6 temporary Hit Points (or 3 temporary Hit Points if it’s nighttime). Bolster Maiserene Phantom can only be activated on rank 0 phantoms. When bolstered, the phantom increases to rank 1, and all PCs gain 1 permanent Hit Point.

## Soulheart (Greater)  *Item 10*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Bulk** L

---

When used to Bolster Life, the _soulheart_ grants 12 temporary Hit Points (or 6 temporary Hit Points if it’s nighttime). Bolster Maiserene Phantom can only be activated on rank 1 phantoms. When bolstered, the phantom increases to rank 2, and all PCs gain 2 permanent Hit Points.

## Soulheart (Major)  *Item 15*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Bulk** L

---

When used to Bolster Life, the _soulheart_ grants 18 temporary Hit Points (or 9 temporary Hit Points if it’s nighttime). Bolster Maiserene Phantom can only be activated on rank 2 phantoms. When bolstered, the phantom increases to rank 3, and all PCs gain 3 permanent Hit Points.

## Soulheart (Pure)  *Item 20*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Bulk** L

---

A _pure soulheart_ doesn’t have a Bolster Life or Bolster Maiserene Phantom activation—instead, it has the following activation.

**Activate—Restore Life**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** By holding a _pure soulheart_ against the remains of a dead creature of 20th level or lower that has been dead for no more than a year and concentrating for one minute, the _pure soulheart_ flashes with red light and is destroyed. The targeted dead creature is restored to life with 1 Hit Point, no spells prepared or spell slots available, no points in any pools or any other daily resources, and is still affected by any long-term debilitations of the old body.

## Angelic Soulheart  *Item 21*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 236

**Bulk** L

---

An _angelic soulheart_ doesn’t have a Bolster Life or Bolster Maiserene Phantom activation—instead, it has the following activation.

**Activate—Defy Charg** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** Pharasma’s favor (earned by restoring the temple clock tower in Bastardhall), Shelyn’s favor (earned by restoring the gallery in Bastardhall’s reception halls), and Caydserris Arudora is defeated; **Effect** By presenting the _angelic soulheart_ to Charg at the climax of this Adventure Path, you can fully remove the Bastardhall Curse and free all of the souls trapped within! See page 222 for additional details.
