# Compound Eyes  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|Fortune]], [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Price** 75 gp

**Usage** implanted

---

You replace your eyes with ones similar to an insect’s or a crustacean’s, which allow you to better pinpoint movement. Once per day when you attempt a flat check to target a creature that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from you, you can roll twice and take the better result.
