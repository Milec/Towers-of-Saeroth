# Rusting Ammunition  *Item 13+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Force]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 43

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Rusting ammunition is made using a [[Bestiary/Ore Louse|ore louse's]] saliva and, when activated, deals damage to objects or creatures primarily made of metal. The target takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] for a duration determined by the type of ammunition used. A creature that drops to 0 Hit Points while taking this persistent damage crumbles into fine powder; its gear remains. The ammunition's type determines the maximum amount of an object that's destroyed.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the saliva of an ore louse.

## Rusting Ammunition (Moderate)  *Item 13*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 43

**Price** 600 gp

---

The ammunition deals 3d8 persistent damage for up to 5 rounds. For an object, the ammunition destroys a 5-foot cube.

## Rusting Ammunition (Greater)  *Item 18*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 43

**Price** 3,600 gp

---

The ammunition deals 4d8 persistent damage for up to 6 rounds. For an object, the ammunition destroys a 10-foot cube.
