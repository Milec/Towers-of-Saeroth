# Firestarter Pellets  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 168

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell (add 1 action)

---

These compacted pellets of bat guano, sulfur, and magical accelerants have long been a staple for spellcasters on the battlefield. Adding a _firestarter pellet_ to a [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]] spell produces clinging flames that deal [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] to all who fail the saving throw against the effect (doubling on a critical failure).
