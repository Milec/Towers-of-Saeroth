# Trackless  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 145

**Usage** applied to footwear

---

_Trackless_ runes are common among hunters and trappers, as well as thieves and anyone fleeing pursuit. While wearing trackless footwear, you have a +4 item bonus to the DC to [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|track]] you. However, this bonus doesn't stack with the status bonus from [[Spells/Pass Without Trace|_pass without trace_]].
