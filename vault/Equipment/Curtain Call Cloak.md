# Curtain Call Cloak  *Item 12*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -185- A Taste of Ashes|Pathfinder #185: A Taste of Ashes]] pg. 78

**Price** 1,900 gp

**Usage** worn cloak

**Bulk** L

---

This blue velvet cloak is cut from the remains of a curtain from a destroyed theater. When worn, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks while acting, orating, or singing, as the cloak makes minor motions to accentuate your performance.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You take a bow, spread your arms, and the cloak casts 4th-level [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]] centered on a corner of your space. This darkness doesn't impede your vision. While the darkness persists, it emits noise like the applauding of a moderately sized crowd.
