# Druid's Crown  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 151

**Price** 220 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

Made of materials scavenged from wild places, a _druid’s crown_ can be rebuilt for a variety of benefits. The crown grants you a +1 item bonus to a skill, and can be activated to cast a spell, both depending on the material used to build the crown, as listed on the table below. If you invest and wear [[Equipment/Druid's Vestments|druid's vestments]] along with the crown, the crown's item bonus increases by 1 and its spell's DC rises to 27.

You can invest this item only if you're a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druid]]. When you do, as a 10-minute activity that has the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait, you can disassemble and rebuild the crown with different materials, changing its item bonus and spell accordingly.
