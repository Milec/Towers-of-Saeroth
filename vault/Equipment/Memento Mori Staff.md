# Memento Mori Staff  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 239

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A carved [[Bestiary/Nosoi (Monster Core)|nosoi]] figurine sits atop this white ash staff, which carries the faint scent of funereal herbs. These staves are often used by [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasmins]] to communicate with the unquiet dead and lay them to rest. A _memento mori staff_ is a _+2 striking astral staff_.

**Activate—Pierce the Veil** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The staff searches for [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], warming to your touch if there are any undead within 60 feet. It cannot identify the precise location of the undead, nor their exact numbers. You can [[Actions/Sustain]] this effect for a maximum of 1 minute.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** _[[Spells/Vitality Lash|_vitality lash_]]_
- **1st** _[[Spells/Bless (Player Core)|_bless_]]_, _[[Spells/Infuse Vitality|_infuse vitality_]]_
- **2nd** _[[Spells/Palm-Held Sun|_palm-held sun_]]_, _[[Spells/Spirit Sense (Player Core 2)|_spirit sense_]]_
- **3rd** _[[Spells/Anointed Ground|_anointed ground_]]_, _[[Spells/Reblooded|_reblooded_]]_
- **4th** _[[Spells/Ghostly Tragedy (Player Core 2)|_ghostly tragedy_]]_, _[[Spells/Talking Corpse (Player Core)|_talking corpse_]]_
- **5th** _infuse vitality_, _[[Spells/Peaceful Rest|_peaceful rest_]]_

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
