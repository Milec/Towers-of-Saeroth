# Stolen Countenance  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 216

**Price** 300 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

Blood stains this performer’s mask, which is decorated in both comedy and tragedy motifs and carved with prayers to gods of deception. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Mislead (Player Core)|_mislead_]]_ spell, it tweaks the magical energy used to create the duplicate, allowing you to alter its appearance to resemble any [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] creature of your size you’ve seen.
