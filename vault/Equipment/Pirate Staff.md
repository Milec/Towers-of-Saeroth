# Pirate Staff  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 133

**Price** 2,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

Carved of driftwood stained white with the salt of the sea, a pirate staff has jewels and gold pieces embedded in the wood. A skull and crossbones sit on top. When used as a weapon, the staff is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Fearsome (GM Core)|fearsome]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Coerce (Player Core)|Coerce]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Gale Blast (Player Core 2)|_gale blast_]], [[Spells/Know the Way|_know direction_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Seashell of Stolen Sound|_seashell of stolen sound_]]
- **2nd** [[Spells/Mist|_obscuring mist_]], [[Spells/Water Breathing (Player Core)|_water breathing_]], [[Spells/Water Walk (Player Core)|_water walk_]]
- **3rd** [[Spells/Mind of Menace|_mind of menace_]], _water breathing_
- **4th** [[Spells/Solid Fog|_solid fog_]], _water walk_
- **5th** [[Spells/Flowing Strike|_flowing strike_]], [[Spells/Mariner's Curse (Player Core)|_mariner’s curse_]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
