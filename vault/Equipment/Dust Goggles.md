# Dust Goggles  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 80

**Price** 250 gp

**Usage** worn eyepiece

---

These sand-colored goggles keep the harsh weather of the Cinderlands at bay. While wearing the goggles, you ignore penalties to Perception from [[Rules/Desert (GM Core)|desert]] weather effects and gain a +1 bonus to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks involving sight.
