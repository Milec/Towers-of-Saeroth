# Blade Byrnie  *Item 7+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 15

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|Chain Shirt]]

---

Instead of chain links, this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|chain shirt]]_ is assembled from metal “leaves” that each resemble a small blade.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You pull a link from the armor, which transforms into a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]_. The dagger disappears and reappears as a link in the armor after you [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with it, or at the end of this turn if you don’t make a Strike. You can Activate the blade byrnie in place of an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action to draw a weapon for abilities such as the [[Feats/Quick Draw (Player Core)|Quick Draw]] feat.

Upgrading the runes on the blade byrnie makes the daggers pulled from it more powerful. The daggers have a _+2 weapon potency_ rune if the armor has a _+2 armor potency_ rune, or a _+3 weapon potency_ rune if the armor has a _+3 armor potency_ rune.
