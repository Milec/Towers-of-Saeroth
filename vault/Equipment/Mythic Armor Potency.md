# Mythic Armor Potency  *Item 20*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 148

**Price** 70,000 gp

**Usage** etched onto armor

---

This armor is etched with a mythical ward providing unparalleled defense. Increase the armor’s item bonus to AC by 4, and the armor can be etched with four [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]].

**Activate—Survive Devastation** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])  **Trigger** An enemy critically succeeds against you with a weapon or unarmed [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]];  **Effect** Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Rules|Mythic Point]]; if the triggering Strike was made by a mythic creature, it’s a normal success instead. If it was made by a non-mythic creature, it’s a failure.

**Craft Requirements** Mythic armor potency runes can only be crafted, etched, or transferred by a mythic character  capable of making a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check at mythic proficiency (such as by having the Artisan’s Calling).
