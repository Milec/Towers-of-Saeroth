# Verdant Buckler  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|Buckler]]

---

This large wooden [[Equipment/Shields/Buckler (Player Core)|buckler]] (Hardness 6, HP 24, BT 12) is alive with creeping vines and mossy growths that shift in the air almost imperceptibly.

**Activate—Grasping Vines** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], plant) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You use the _verdant buckler_ to Shield Block a melee Strike; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The attacking creature must succeed at a DC 24 Reflex save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the shield’s animated vines ([[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] on a critical failure). The affected creature is grappled or restrained for 1 round, until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 24), or you move, whichever comes first.
