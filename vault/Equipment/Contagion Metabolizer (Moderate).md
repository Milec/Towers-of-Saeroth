# Contagion Metabolizer  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Elixir]], [[Traits/Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 59

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

Contagion metabolizers seek out toxins in the bloodstream and attempt to purify them into humors the body processes naturally. When you drink this elixir, it attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] check with the listed counteract modifier to remove the highest-level [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] or [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] afflicting you. This takes longer for a disease—the counteract check doesn’t happen until 10 minutes after you drink the elixir. After drinking, you become temporarily immune to contagion metabolizers for 1 hour.

If you’re a [[Classes/Subclasses/Research Fields/Chirurgeon (Player Core 2)|chirurgeon]] [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core 2)|alchemist]] and have powerful alchemy, you can substitute your statistics when you create a contagion metabolizer using [[Actions/Quick Alchemy (Player Core 2)|Quick Alchemy]], if your stats are higher. This replaces the counteract level with half your level rounded up and the counteract modifier with your class DC – 10.
