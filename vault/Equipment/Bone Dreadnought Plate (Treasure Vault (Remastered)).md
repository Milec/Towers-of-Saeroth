# Bone Dreadnought Plate  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 63

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 5

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Fortress Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Fortress Plate]]

---

This suit of bone-based [[Equipment/Armor/Fortress Plate (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|fortress plate]] is a masterpiece of alchemical science. This armor has a receiver that can hold a single [[Equipment/Lodestone Bomb (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|lodestone bomb]], which takes 3 Interact actions to install.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** A lodgestone bomb is installed in the armor; **Effect** The bomb shifts numerous small plates and hinges, offering a wide variety of protections, granting you resistance to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], piercing, and slashing damage equal to the loaded lodestone bomb’s splash damage. These effects last for 20 minutes, but each time you’re hit by an attack that deals damage of one of these types, decrease the remaining duration by 1 minute. Once activated, the armor can’t be deactivated. The activation uses up the lodestone bomb, and the armor can’t be activated again until a new one is installed.
