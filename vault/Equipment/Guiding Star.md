# Guiding Star  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Chaotic]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 185

**Price** 325 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Starknife]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Returning|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Starknife|starknife]]_ is made of dark blue metal overlaid with smoky flecks of fused quartz. When you Cast an [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]] Spell, your next attack this turn with this starknife reduces the target's cover, changing greater cover to standard cover or ignoring standard and lesser cover.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Trigger** You successfully Strike a creature with a [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] Strike with _guiding star_;  **Effect** Soft light outlines the target. At the beginning of your next turn, the _guiding star's_ blades detach and form into darts of starlight, flying up to 40 feet and striking the target unerringly, collectively dealing 4 piercing damage and 1 good damage before re-forming. The piercing damage increases to 6 with a _greater striking rune_ and 8 with a _major striking rune_.
