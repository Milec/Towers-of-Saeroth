# Boom Snare  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Special- Fumbus|Pathfinder Special: Fumbus]]

**Price** 18 gp

---

By combining a [[Equipment/Alchemist's Fire|lesser alchemist's fire]], a [[Equipment/Thunderstone|lesser thunderstone]], and other catalysts with a pressure sensitive trigger, you create a snare that creates a fiery and thunderous explosion when triggered. The target and all creatures in adjacent squares take 2d6 fire damage and 2d6 sonic damage with a basic DC 19 Reflex save. On a failure, they are deafened for 1d4 rounds and take 1d4 persistent fire damage.

**Craft Requirements** Supply a [[Equipment/Alchemist's Fire|lesser alchemist's fire]] and a [[Equipment/Thunderstone|lesser thunderstone]].
