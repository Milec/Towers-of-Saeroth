**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Strike

---

A mixture of rendered animal fat and acids designed to ignite the fat when exposed to air, a tallow bomb creates a splash of burning oil that adheres to skin, clothes, and hair. A tallow bomb deals the listed fire damage, [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and splash damage. On a critical hit, a living creature taking persistent fire damage from a tallow bomb is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] from the stench of burning fat and can't reduce its sickened value below 1 while the persistent fire damage lasts. Many types of tallow bombs grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
