# Wine of the Blood  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Potion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 114

**Price** 30 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This wine, prepared using the blood of a creature with overwhelming vitality, soothes the body, mind, and spirit with a temporary sense of euphoria and rest. When you drink _wine of the blood_, you recover 2d10 Hit Points. Additionally, the wine attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] every negative [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effect affecting you, with a counteract level of 3 and a counteract modifier of +9. Lastly, you gain a +1 status bonus to all Will saves, or a +2 status bonus against emotion effects, for 1 minute.
