# Envenomed Snare  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 15

**Price** 60 gp

---

This snare is coated with [[Equipment/Giant Wasp Venom|giant wasp venom]] and tipped with needles, which deliver the venom to the first creature to enter the square. That creature takes 4d6 damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex) and, on a failure, the creature is exposed to the giant wasp venom.

**Craft Requirements** Supply 2 doses of giant wasp venom.
