# The Tangled Briar  *Item 20*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Artifact]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -191- The Destiny War|Pathfinder #191: The Destiny War]] pg. 78

**Usage** carried

---

_The Tangled Briar_ signifies the return of ancient triumphs. As long as you have _The Tangled Briar_ invested, you gain [[Feats/Toughness]] as a bonus feat. If you already possess this feat, increase your maximum Hit Points by twice your level.

**Activate** two actions envision, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You raise the card up above your head and invoke the thorns and briars of the tangled parts of the world; you cast [[Spells/Wall of Thorns|_wall of thorns_]]. The level of the spell is half your level, rounded down (minimum 6th level). The brambles within this particular wall of thorns are treated as greater difficult terrain.
