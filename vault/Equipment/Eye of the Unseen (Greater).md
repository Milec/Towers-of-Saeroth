# Eye of the Unseen  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 73

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) All Pathfinder Society agents also have access to all assistive items from Morhen’s Mobility Apparel_

**Usage** worn

**Bulk** L

---

This prosthetic eye was designed by elven crafters but comes in a range of appearances for different ancestries. While wearing the eye, you gain a +1 item bonus to visual Perception checks.

**Activate** two actions command, envision;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You focus on the eye to see the unseen. The eye casts [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]] on you.
