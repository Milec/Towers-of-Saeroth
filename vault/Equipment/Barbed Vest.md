# Barbed Vest  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -159- All or Nothing|Pathfinder #159: All or Nothing]] pg. 78

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

---

Viciously sharp spiked chains wrap around this [[Equipment/Armor/Studded Leather Armor|studded leather armor]] that can be used to magically harm the wearer and channel supernatural abilities without somehow tearing at the leather itself.

 The _barbed vest_ doesn't fuse to you in the usual way (see [[Rules/Cursed Items]]). It can be removed and discarded, but 1 day after doing so, you feel driven to recollect the vest at any cost until the curse is removed with a [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]] spell.

 Anytime you activate the _barbed vest_, its chains tighten around you, dealing 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent piercing damage]]. You can remove this persistent damage as normal, but only after deactivating the item's magic as a single action.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _barbed vest_ casts [[Spells/Spiritual Weapon|_spiritual weapon_]], summoning a [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain|spiked chain]] to fight for you.
