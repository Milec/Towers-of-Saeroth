# Crushing Coils  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 191

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|Leather Armor]]

---

Made from constrictor snakeskin, the strips of this _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Armor/Leather Armor (Player Core)|leather armor]]_ wrap around you like an [[Bestiary/Giant Anaconda (Monster Core)|anaconda]] might wrap around its victim. The first time you roll a 1 on any attack roll or check after donning the armor, it fuses with you and constricts. It constricts anytime you roll a 1 on any attack roll or check thereafter. When the armor constricts, you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] for 1 round.

**Activate** single action command, Interact;     **Effect** The armor wraps around you, allowing you to don it by the time the activation finishes.
