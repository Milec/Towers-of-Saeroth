# Emberheart  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 226

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This small, heart-shaped amulet appears to be sculpted from stone with a single streak of [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] running through, like an ember just beneath ashes. When worn, the amulet gives off a gentle warmth, akin to being near a fireplace. You gain poison [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 15 when wearing this amulet. When you invest the amulet, you either increase your Constitution modifier by 1 or increase it to +4, whichever would give you a higher value.

**Activate—Warming Heart** two actions ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hold the amulet aloft as a ripple of warm orange light exudes outward. Each ally in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] regains 30 Hit Points and gains a +3 status bonus to Fortitude saves until the end of their next turn.
