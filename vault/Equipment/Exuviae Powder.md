# Exuviae Powder  *Item 14*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Earth]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 98

**Price** 750 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

Exuviae powder comes from cast-off shells of cicada-like insects native to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth]], crushed to dust. You sprinkle this powder over your body, leaving an iridescent layer reminiscent of insect chitin. After you do so, for 8 hours, you double the time you can [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|hold your breath]]. During this time, you also have access to the shed chitin activation.

**Activate—Shed Chitin** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])  **Trigger** You would be [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]];  **Effect** The powder petrifies like a shell around you instead, and its other effects end. The powder causes you to become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 minute as well as [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] ([[Actions/Escape]] DC 25). You can use the extra action each round only for Escape and Stride actions.
