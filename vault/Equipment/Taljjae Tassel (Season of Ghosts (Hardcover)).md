The bright red tassels of the creature known as [[Bestiary/Taljjae]] are a ubiquitous motif in Hwanggot and are used as charms to pray for good fortune and reward for great effort. They adorn clothing, weapons, and even drinking gourds. These particular tassels carry within them a potent fraction of the mysterious fey’s power. When you activate a _Taljjae tassel_, your weapon gains the properties of the [[Equipment/Grievous (GM Core)|_grievous_]] rune for the duration of the triggering attack.
