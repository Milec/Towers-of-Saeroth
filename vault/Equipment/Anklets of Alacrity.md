# Anklets of Alacrity  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Apex]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 603

**Price** 15,000 gp

**Usage** worn anklets

---

These gem-studded golden anklets give you a +3 item bonus to Acrobatics checks. When you invest the anklets, you either increase your Dexterity score by 2 or increase it to 18, whichever would give you a higher score.

**Activate** single action Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You click the anklets together, gaining a +20-foot status bonus to all your Speeds and the effects of [[Spells/Water Walk|_water walk_]] for the next 10 minutes.
