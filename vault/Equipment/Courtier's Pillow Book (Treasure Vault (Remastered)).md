# Courtier's Pillow Book  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grimoire]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 112

**Price** 250 gp

**Bulk** L

---

This elegant journal has a detailed social dossier on the owner's acquaintances. When you make your daily preparations, you can inscribe a secret or embarrassing foible about a specific individual that you know to be true or have on good authority.

**Activate** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to cast a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell on a target about whom you’ve written an entry in the book, you can state that secret or foible before [[Rules/Cast a Spell|Casting the Spell]] to give the target a –1 circumstance penalty to their saving throw against the spell. The inscription then disappears from the grimoire.
