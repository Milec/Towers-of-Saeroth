This potion appears to shift colors, and no two observers describe it in the same way. When consumed, it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] all curses and diseases affecting you, as well as the blinded and deafened conditions from spells affecting you. The potion has a counteract rank of 7th and a +20 modifier for the roll.
