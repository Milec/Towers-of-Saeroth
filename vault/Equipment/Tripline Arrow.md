# Tripline Arrow  *Item 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Kingmaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

**Price** 6 gp

**Ammunition** [[Equipment/Weapons/Arrows|arrow]]

---

This arrow unspools a line of wire as its flies at its target. The wire animates as it hits a target and attempts to wrap around its legs before vanishing a moment later. If you can apply the bow's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]], you can choose to knock the target [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] instead of pinning the target. If you cannot apply the bow's critical specialization effect, you can instead attempt to use the arrow to [[Actions/Trip]] with the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill as if the bow had the [[Traits/Trip|trip]] weapon trait. The arrow's unwieldy nature halves its weapon's range increment.
