# Bands of Force  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 286

**Usage** worn armbands

**Bulk** L

---

Decorated with clear gemstones, these thick metal bands spread an inflexible layer of force over your body. The force grants you a +1 item bonus to AC and saving throws, and a maximum Dexterity modifier of +5 as armor. You can affix talismans to the bands as though they were light armor.

**Activate—Return Force** ⤾ ([[Traits/Force|force]], [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])  **Trigger** A creature critically misses you with a melee Strike.;  **Effect** You Shove the creature using the bands' Athletics modifier of +14.
