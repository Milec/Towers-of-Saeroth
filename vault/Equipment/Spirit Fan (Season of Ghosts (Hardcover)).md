# Spirit Fan  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 308

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Fighting Fan (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Fighting Fan]]

---

This elegant black, gold-tipped _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Fighting Fan (Tian Xia Character Guide)|fighting fan]]_ is adorned with images of three golden leaves and a red rope tassel. If a creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points by a spirit fan, the golden leaves on the fan light up, providing illumination equal to that of a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]] for 1 minute.

**Activate—Release Life Energy** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) **Frequency** once per hour;  **Requirements** The _spirit fan’s_ leaves are illuminated; **Effect** You sweep the spirit fan in the direction of a single target you can see within 30 feet, releasing the life energy in the form of a streak of golden light. The spirit fan goes dark. If the target is a living creature, the energy restores 3d8+8 Hit Points. If the target is [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], it takes 2d8+8 vitality damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save).
