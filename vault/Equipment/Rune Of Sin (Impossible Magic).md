# Rune Of Sin  *Item 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Tattoo]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 243

**Price** 1,700 gp

**Usage** tattooed on the body

---

This jagged glyph matches one of the Thassilonian runes of sin—envy, gluttony, greed, lust, pride, sloth, or wrath— chosen when the tattoo is created. When you [Cast a Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3521) from the arcane tradition, the tattoo shimmers with energy, granting you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to damage from divine spells until the start of your next turn.
