# Wyrm Claw  *Item 12+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 129

**Usage** affixed to armor or weapon

---

The tip of a metallic dragon’s claw is set into a band of silver. When you summon dragons using the claw, you must choose [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon, Metallic|metallic dragons]].

- **Armor** After you cast a [[Classes/Subclasses/Arcane Schools/Conjuration|conjuration]] spell by activating the wyrm claw, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to acid, cold, electricity, and fire for the next minute.
- **Weapon** After you cast a conjuration spell by activating the claw, you gain a Breath Weapon activity that takes 2 actions and deals 6d6 damage with a DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. Choose the area and type of damage when you use Breath Weapon: a 30-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] that deals acid, electricity, or fire, or a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] that deals cold or fire. You can use this activity once before the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Gouging Claw (Player Core)|_gouging claw_]].
