# Shrieking Key  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cursed]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 192

**Usage** held in 1 hand

---

A skull-topped metal shrieking key appears to be a [[Equipment/Skeleton Key (GM Core)|_skeleton key_]], useful in place of thieves’ tools when attempting to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick a Lock]]. A _shrieking key_ has no activation, however. When you use it to Pick a Lock, the key emits a loud shriek audible for 500 feet despite ambient noise. Physical barriers still block or muffle the shriek as normal. You also take a –2 circumstance penalty to the [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] check rather than enjoying a bonus. After you attempt such a check with the key the first time, it fuses to you, returning to your possession if discarded. To use another device to Pick a Lock, you must first succeed at a DC 20 Will save.
