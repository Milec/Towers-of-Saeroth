# Perfect Droplet  *Item 3+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 236

**Usage** affixed to armor or a weapon

---

Intense blue water magically holds its shape—a perfect sphere. The spell DC of any spell cast by Activating this item is 17.  
**• Armor** You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 2 to water effects and attacks by water creatures.  
**• Weapon** After you cast a water spell by Activating the _droplet_, your body becomes mist-like. Until the end of the turn, you can move through enemies’ spaces, treating each square in their spaces as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. You can’t move through creatures that have the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait in this way.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast _[[Spells/Spout (Player Core 2)|_spout_]]._
