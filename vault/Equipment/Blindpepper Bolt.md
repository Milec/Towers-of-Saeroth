# Blindpepper Bolt  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 14

**Price** 10 gp

**Ammunition** bolt

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

The head of this bolt is a vented container that smells strongly of caustic pepper. When an activated _blindpepper bolt_ hits a target, it deals no damage; instead the creature must attempt a DC 17 Fortitude save. If the Strike was a critical success, the target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its Fortitude save. At the GM's discretion, a creature with no eyes or olfactory organs might not be affected by this item.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn. It can end the effect early if it spends an action, which has the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait, to wipe down its eyes.  

**Failure** The target is dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the end of your next turn and dazzled for 1 minute.
