The faint glow beneath the thick, algae-green scales of this suit of _[[Equipment/Armor Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Resilient (GM Core)|resilient]] [[Equipment/Armor/Scale Mail (Player Core)|scale mail]]_ hints at the armor’s source.

**Activate—Luminous Lure** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The _mamlambo scale_ sheds [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. The light has a duration of 1 minute, but you can extinguish the light with an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action. A creature that begins its turn in the light must attempt a DC 26 Will save. Regardless of the save’s result, the creature is temporarily immune for 1 hour.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]], and for as long as it remains in the light, it must spend at least 1 of its actions on each of its turns to move closer to the source of the light. If the creature ends its movement adjacent to you, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until it is out of the light or until you make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against it.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the first Strike you make against it while it is slowed.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the scales and hide of a [[Bestiary/Mamlambo|mamlambo]].
