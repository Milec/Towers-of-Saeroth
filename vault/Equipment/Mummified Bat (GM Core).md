# Mummified Bat  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman (GM Core)|Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 266

**Price** 175 gp

**Usage** affixed to a weapon

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]])

---

This talisman is the magically treated corpse of a tiny bat bound in papyrus. When activated, the affixed weapon detects vibrations around you and guides your perception. For 1 minute, you don't need to succeed at a flat check to target [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] creatures. You're not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to creatures that are hidden from you (unless you're off-guard to them for reasons other than the hidden condition), and you need only a successful DC 5 flat check to target a [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] creature. While you're adjacent to an undetected creature of your level or lower, it's instead only hidden from you. If you have the [[Feats/Blind-Fight]] feat, you gain imprecise  
 echolocation with a range of 30 feet for 1 minute. This makes creatures that would be undetected by you because you can't see them hidden instead.
