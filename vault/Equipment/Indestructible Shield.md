# Indestructible Shield  *Item 18*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 588

**Price** 24,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield]]

---

An _indestructible shield_ is a [[Equipment/Adamantine Shield|high-grade adamantine]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|shield]] (Hardness 13, HP 90) that can withstand just about any damage. It can be damaged only by a [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] spell (roll damage as if against a creature that failed its save) or by an artifact tied to destruction, such as a [[Equipment/Sphere of Annihilation|_sphere of annihilation_]].

**Craft Requirements** The raw materials must include at least 4,400 gp of adamantine.
