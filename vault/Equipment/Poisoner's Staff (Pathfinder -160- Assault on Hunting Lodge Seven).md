# Poisoner's Staff  *Item 4+*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 66

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This gnarled staff is covered with thorns and coated with a glistening lacquer that acts as a minor irritant. While holding the staff, you aren't affected by its coating or thorns, and you reduce the DC of your flat checks to overcome [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
