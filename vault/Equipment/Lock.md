# Lock  *Item 0*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 290

---

Picking a poor lock requires two successful DC 15 [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] checks, a simple lock requires three successful DC 20 Thievery checks, an average lock requires four successes at DC 25, a good lock requires five successes at DC 30, and a superior lock six successes at DC 40.
