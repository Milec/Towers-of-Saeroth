Once you imbibe this bitter elixir, your skin exudes a toxin for 1 hour, affecting any creature that hits you with a jaws [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or other bite attack. If you are [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallowed Whole]] by another creature, they are automatically exposed to the poison every round and take a –2 penalty to their saving throw against it.

 **Frogskin Tincture Poison** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d4 poison damage (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d6 poison damage, enfeebled 3, and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] (1 round)
