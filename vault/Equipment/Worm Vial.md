# Worm Vial  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 57

**Price** 600 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

Interact

---

Opening this vial unleashes its destructive contents: a reconstituted Gargantuan [[Bestiary/Cave Worm|purple worm]]. The worm has two functions; choose which one to use when you Activate the item.

**Burrow** The worm [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrows]] up to 80 feet, leaving a tunnel behind it. It can burrow through solid stone, but if it does so it burrows 40 feet instead of 80 feet.

**Eat** The worm attempts to swallow a creature, crush it in its insides, then spit the creature out. The target must be within 15 feet, and it attempts a DC 30 Reflex save. If it fails, it takes 3d6+9 bludgeoning damage (doubled on a critical failure). The worm then spits the creature out to any empty space on the ground within 30 feet, causing the creature to take damage from a 20-foot [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|fall]].

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Cave Worm|purple worm]].
