**Usage** affixed to a shield

free action

envision

**Trigger** You take damage from a physical attack while you don't have the affixed shield raised

---

This clear quartz cabochon attaches to the center of your shield. When you activate the cabochon, you use the [[Feats/Shield Block]] reaction even if you hadn’t raised the affixed shield (and even if you don’t normally have that reaction).
