# Mirror Robe  *Item 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 50

**Price** 55 gp

**Usage** worn clothing

**Bulk** L

---

Thousands of small, reflective, mirrored glass shards have been carefully stitched down this long silk duster.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** The _mirror robe_ was last activated to draw attention toward you, or you haven't used the _mirror robe_ today; **Effect** The _mirror robe_ [[Actions/Create a Diversion|Creates a Diversion]] for you with a +9 [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] modifier.

**Activate** single action Interact ([[Traits/Visual|visual]])   **Requirements** The _mirror robe_ was last activated to divert attention away from you and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] by at least one foe; **Effect** You draw attention toward yourself. Choose one foe to which you were hidden or undetected. You reveal yourself to all, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]]. The foe you chose diverts its attention to you, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to your allies until the beginning of your next turn. If you are [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] or otherwise can't become observed, you can't use this activation.
