# Grievous  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 584

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

---

When your attack roll with this weapon is a critical hit and gains the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]], you gain an additional benefit depending on the weapon group.

[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|**Axe**]] You can damage a third creature, with the same restrictions.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|**Bow**]] The Athletics check to pull the missile free is DC 20.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|**Brawling**]] The target takes a –4 circumstance penalty to its save.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Club|**Club**]] You can knock the target up to 15 feet away.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Dart|**Dart**]] The base [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] increases to 2d6.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Flail|**Flail**]] You move the target 5 feet. You can't move it away from you, but you can move it in another direction of your choice.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Hammer|**Hammer**]] You can also knock the target 5 feet away from you.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Knife|**Knife**]] The target takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speed while it has the persistent bleed damage.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Pick|**Pick**]] The extra damage from the critical specialization effect increases to 4 per weapon damage die.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Polearm|**Polearm**]] You can move the target up to 10 feet.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Shield|**Shield**]] You can knock the target up to 10 feet away.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sling|**Sling**]] The target also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round if it fails the save.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Spear|**Spear**]] The [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] condition lasts for 2 rounds.  
[[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Sword|**Sword**]] The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of your next turn.
