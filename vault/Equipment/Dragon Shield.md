This _[[Equipment/Reinforcing Rune|moderate reinforcing]] [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]]_ (Hardness 8, HP 84, BT 42) has an image of a cinder [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]’s head painted on it. Additional metal adornments accentuate the fearsome mouth.

**Activate—Burning Path** three actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise your Shield]] and [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice your Speed in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]. You can move through creatures with this movement, but you can’t end your movement in their space. Creatures you moved through take 5d8 fire damage (DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures who critically fail also take 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]].
