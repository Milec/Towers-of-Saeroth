# Bottled Sunlight  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Bomb]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Positive]], [[Traits/Splash]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

Interact

---

This mirrored bottle contains various chemicals dissolved in two immiscible solvents. Shaking the bottle induces chemical reactions that cause it to glow. For 1 hour, the bottle sheds [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] to the next 40 feet).

 During this hour, it can be thrown like a bomb. This requires a single action to Activate with a Strike, like other bombs, and deals the damage below. If it's not thrown within an hour of the first activation, it defuses harmlessly and is consumed.

## Bottled Sunlight (Lesser)  *Item 2*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 18

**Price** 7 gp

**Bulk** L

---

The bomb deals 1d4 positive damage and 1 positive [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage, as well as 1d4 additional fire damage. As normal, positive damage harms only [[Traits/Undead|undead]] and other creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]]. Undead with a particular vulnerability to sunlight (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire|vampires]] or [[Bestiary/Families/Wraith|wraiths]]) take the maximum amount of positive damage from bottled sunlight (4 instead of 1d4). For any ability that depends on the number of weapon dice bottled sunlight has, count only the positive damage dice.
