# Candle of Revealing  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 306

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

When lit, this black candle's eerie blue flame reveals the presence of invisible creatures. Within a 10-foot radius of the lit candle, creatures don't benefit from the [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] condition. Their bodies are outlined, not fully visible, so they're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]]. Once lit, the candle burns for 1 minute, after which the effect ends. If extinguished, it can't be relit.
