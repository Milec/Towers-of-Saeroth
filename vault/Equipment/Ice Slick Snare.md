# Ice Slick Snare  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Mechanical]], [[Traits/Snare]], [[Traits/Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Special- Fumbus|Pathfinder Special: Fumbus]]

**Price** 40 gp

---

Using a [[Equipment/Frost Vial|moderate frost vial]] as a catalyst, this snare spills out over the ground when triggered to create a slippery patch of ice on the ground. When a creature triggers this snare, it takes 2d6 cold damage and must attempt a DC 22 Reflex saving throw.
