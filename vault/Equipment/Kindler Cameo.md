# Kindler Cameo  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Bastion of Blasphemies

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -224- Bastion of Blasphemies|Pathfinder #224: Bastion of Blasphemies]] pg. 240

**Price** 300 gp

**Usage** worn

---

This small gold cameo in the shape of an oval hangs on a delicate silver chain. When opened, the cameo reveals tiny paintings of a man and a woman—Florin Kindler’s parents. When the _Kindler cameo_ is invested, the images within shift in appearance to portray the two most important people in the wearer’s life.

**Activate—Recall Better Times** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You open the cameo and regard the images within of the two most important people in your life, bolstering yourself with fond memories. You regain 3d10+12 Hit Points, and you gain a +2 status bonus to Will saves for 1 minute. You also immediately recover from any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] you’re currently taking.

**Activate—Sense Soulheart**  1 minute ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You are in Bastardhall while the castle is cursed; **Effect** By clutching the closed cameo in a fist, shutting your eyes, and concentrating, the cameo’s magic reaches out through Bastardhall toward the closest _soulheart_. At the end of a minute of concentration, you know the direction and approximate distance to a nearby _soulheart_ (this is typically, but not always, the closest _soulheart_). This activation doesn’t discern between _soulhearts_ whose locations you already know about or not, and as such, Activating this power while you or a nearby ally carry a _soulheart_ isn’t particularly helpful. The cameo can’t detect a _soulheart_ that’s still within a section of Bastardhall that hasn’t yet been unlocked. If no _soulhearts_ can be detected, you know this, but the activation’s daily use is still consumed.
