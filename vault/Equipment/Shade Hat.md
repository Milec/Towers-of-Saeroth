# Shade Hat  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 51

**Price** 150 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

This comfortable and stylish wide-brimmed hat magically protects you from the worst of the heat in hot areas, even when the heat doesn't come from the sun. While wearing the shade hat, you are protected from [[Rules/Temperature|mild and severe heat]] (but not extreme heat).
