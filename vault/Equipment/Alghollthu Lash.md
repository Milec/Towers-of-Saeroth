# Alghollthu Lash  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 35

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|Whip]]

---

This fleshy _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Whip (Player Core)|whip]]_ is obviously crafted from the tentacle of some fearsome beast, likely an [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]], and constantly drips slime.

**Activate** single action command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The next creature you successfully Strike with this weapon is exposed to alghollthu slime.

**Alghollthu Slime** ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Saving Throw** DC 25 Fortitude; **Stage 1** no ill effect (1 round); **Stage 2** the victim’s skin softens, making the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** the victim’s skin transforms into a clear, slimy membrane, making it drained 2 until the curse ends; every hour this membrane remains dry, the creature’s drained condition increases by 1 (permanent). A [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]] spell can [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] this curse, but immunity to disease offers no protection against it.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include a tentacle from an [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthu]].
