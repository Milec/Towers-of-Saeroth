# Habu's Cudgel  *Item 8*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -170- Spoken on the Song Wind|Pathfinder #170: Spoken on the Song Wind]] pg. 76

**Price** 415 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Club]]

---

Long associated with a notorious crime boss in Nantambu, this stout _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Fearsome|fearsome]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]]_ is studded with vicious-looking knobs of obsidian and wrapped in cloth for a better grip. It emits a ghastly groan when swung.

**Activate** two actions Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gesture at a square within 40 feet with _Habu's Cudgel_. The club causes a frightful wail to unleash from that square and casts [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]] (DC 24 Will save) targeting the creature in that square and all adjacent creatures.
