# Wooden Nickel  *Item 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 95

**Price** 130 gp

**Usage** affixed to armor

**Activate**

three actions

envision

---

This rough wooden coin hangs from a string drilled through a hole at its center. When you Activate the _wooden nickel_, for 10 minutes, you can cause any object you touch of 2 Bulk or less to look, feel, and smell like valuables of a similar size crafted from a precious metal of your choice.

A creature that touches or Interacts with an affected object can attempt a [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] check against your [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] DC to disbelieve the illusion, and a successful check against your Deception DC with [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] or an appropriate [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill will also reveal the item’s true nature. After 10 minutes have elapsed since you Activated the _wooden nickel_, all affected objects revert to their true form.
