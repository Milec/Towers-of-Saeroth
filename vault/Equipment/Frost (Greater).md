# Frost  *Item 8+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 237

**Usage** etched onto a weapon

This weapon is empowered with freezing ice. It deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] 1 until the end of your next turn unless it succeeds at a DC 24 Fortitude save.
