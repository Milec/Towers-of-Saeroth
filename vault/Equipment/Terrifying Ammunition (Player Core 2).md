# Terrifying Ammunition  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Price** 50 gp

**Ammunition** any

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]])

---

This black-and-gray ammunition is etched with occult symbols and tiny, grinning skulls. When activated _terrifying ammunition_ damages a creature, the creature's mind is filled with visions of its failures, its tragedies, and eventually, its own death. The creature must attempt a DC 20 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 1 and can't reduce its frightened value below 1 until it spends an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, to calm itself down.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is frightened 2.
