Applying this clear yellow oil on a lock makes it easier to pick. Anyone attempting to [[Actions/Pick a Lock (Player Core)|Pick the Lock]] within the next minute gains a +1 status bonus to the [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] check. In addition, the first time during that minute that anyone gets a critical failure on the Thievery check, they get a failure instead. There’s only enough in this tiny vial for one lock.
