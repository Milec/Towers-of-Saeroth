# Inubrix Armor  *Item 11+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 98

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk**  varies by armor

**Base Material** [[Equipment/Inubrix]]

---

Inubrix's malleable nature means armor crafted from it is less rigid than usual, though this slightly increased mobility comes at the cost of protection. Metal armor crafted from inubrix gains the [[Traits/Flexible|flexible]] armor trait; its item bonus to AC decreases by 1 but its maximum Dexterity bonus increases by 1.
