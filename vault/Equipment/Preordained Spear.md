# Preordained Spear  *Item 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -218- Titanbane|Pathfinder #218: Titanbane]] pg. 69

**Price** 700 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Spear]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear (Tian Xia Character Guide)|spear]]_ seems to know when its wielder is about to die. Its wooden body bears countless carvings, leaving it thinner than most spears. While you are [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], a preordained spear glows with a faint red light that gives you a haunted look and sheds dim light within 5 feet.

**Activate—Delay Doom** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])   **Requirements** Your name is not on the spear, and you have the [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] condition; **Effect** Your name appears on the shaft of the _preordained spear_. Your doomed value decreases by 1, and you regain 1 [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. Your name fades from the spear the next time you make your [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]].
