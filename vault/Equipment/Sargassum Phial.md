# Sargassum Phial  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemical (Player Core)|Alchemical]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Expandable (GM Core)|Expandable]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 109

**Price** 50 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

two actions

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

A layer of vapor swirls just below the surface of this glass container holding a large, dried heap of fungal-encrusted seaweed. You can throw the phial up to 30 feet when you Activate it. When opened or thrown, a Medium [[Bestiary/Sargassum Heap|sargassum heap]] effigy reconstitutes to release a hallucinogenic cloud in a 15-foot emanation, forcing each creature to attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save. On a failure, a creature rolls 1d4 to determine what it is hallucinating for the next 1d4 rounds (unless otherwise noted). At the beginning of their turn, an affected creature attempts a new save.

1. The target thinks they are sinking through the floor. It falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and spends 1 action on its next turn flailing its limbs as if attempting to swim.
2. The target thinks an item they are holding turns into a viper. The target [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Releases]] the item and spends its next turn [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] from it.
3. The target thinks they have shrunk to 1/10 their normal size. For 1 round, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2, and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed.
4. The target believes they are melting. It drops everything it’s holding and becomes slowed 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 for 1 round.

**Craft Requirements** Supply the corpse of a [[Bestiary/Sargassum Heap|sargassum heap]].
