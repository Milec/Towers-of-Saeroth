# Clockwork Rejuvenator  *Item 18*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Clockwork]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -161- Belly of the Black Whale|Pathfinder #161: Belly of the Black Whale]] pg. 79

**Price** 4,500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action or two actions

Interact

**Requirement** The round you activate the rejuvenator, you must first spend either 1 Interact action or 2 Interact actions to wind the device.

---

This device has four finger-like latches centered around a whirring mass of gears. After winding the _clockwork rejuvenator_, you can latch it to the chest of an adjacent recently dead creature and return it to life. If you wound the rejuvenator using 1 Interact action, you can return to life a creature that died in the last 2 rounds, restoring it to 1 HP. If you wound the rejuvenator using 2 Interact actions, you can return to life a creature that died within the last 3 rounds. After attaching, the device continues to whirl for 1 minute, restoring 10 HP per round to the target. Thereafter, the _clockwork rejuvenator_ loses its magic and becomes inert.
