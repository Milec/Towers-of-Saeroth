# Goz Mask  *Item 2+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 154, [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 73

**Usage** worn mask

---

Originally designed by a sect of [[Setting/Deities/Gozreh|Gozren]] priests, _goz masks_ were designed to help navigate the area around the Eye of Abendego. The masks couldn't contend with the might of the storm, but people all around the Mwangi Expanse still use them. These masks are typically made of wood and sport round, exaggerated features.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You ignore [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] caused by fog, smoke, and other obscuring vapors for 1 minute.
