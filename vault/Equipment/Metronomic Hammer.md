# Metronomic Hammer  *Item 5*

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Gnome Hooked Hammer]]

A polished brass metronome ticks rhythmically within the haft of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Gnome Hooked Hammer|gnome hooked hammer]]_. Gnome adherents of [[Setting/Deities/Brigh]] often attempt to reproduce this item as part of a ritual symbolizing methodical problem-solving and thoughtful craftsmanship.

**Activate** ⤾ envision;   **Trigger** You attempt to [[Actions/Trip]] a creature with the _metronomic hammer_;  **Effect** By timing the sweeping arcs of your Trip attempt to the built-in metronome of a _metronomic hammer_, you gain a +1 item bonus to your [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Trip the target. This item bonus increases by 1 with every subsequent Trip attempt you make with the _metronomic hammer_ against the same target this turn, to a maximum of +3 on your third Trip. The bonus resets to +1 if you successfully Trip the target, if you critically fail to Trip the target, or if your turn ends.
