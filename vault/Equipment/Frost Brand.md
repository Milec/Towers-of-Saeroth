# Frost Brand  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 601

**Price** 10,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword]]

---

The blade of this _+2 greater striking [[Equipment/Frost|greater frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword|greatsword]]_ appears to be nothing but icy shards. The _frost brand_ automatically extinguishes non-magical fires in a 20-foot emanation. While wielding it, you gain fire resistance 5.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;     **Effect** You swing the _frost brand_ into the area of an ongoing magical fire, and the blade attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the fire with a counteract modifier of +27. If it fails, it can't attempt to counteract the same fire again.
