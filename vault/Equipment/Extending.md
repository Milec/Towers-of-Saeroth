**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

An _extending_ rune allows you to extend your weapon to impossible lengths.

**Activate** two actions Interact;     **Effect** You extend your weapon for an instant, giving you an impossible reach. You Strike with the weapon, and you have [[Traits/Reach|reach]] 60 feet for the Strike.
