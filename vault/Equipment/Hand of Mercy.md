# Hand of Mercy  *Item 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Whetstone]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 135

**Price** 7 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

single action

([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])

---

Shaped like an open-palmed hand, this small sculpture of smooth sandstone seems to blunt a weapon when applied rather than sharpen it. For 1 minute, a weapon to which a _hand of mercy_ is applied gains the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait and can’t be used to make lethal attacks. Any [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent damage]] the weapon would deal is negated.
