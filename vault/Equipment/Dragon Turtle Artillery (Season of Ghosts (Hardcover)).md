# Dragon Turtle Artillery  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 302

**Price** 1,000 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 3

---

One end of this heavy cylinder of steel has been forged in the likeness of a dragon turtle’s head, the mouth gaping wide. Grips at the opposite end allow one to hold and aim this heavy cannon-like barrel with both hands. As long as you’re carrying _dragon turtle artillery_ while you’re on a boat or ship, the DC for any effect to capsize that vehicle increases by 4.

**Activate—Turtle's Tsunami** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** A 50-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of surging water blasts from the mouth of the cannon, dealing 10d6 bludgeoning damage to creatures in its area of effect (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails its save is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
