# Frigid Rebuke  *Item 3*

**Price** 11 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

Cast a Spell

---

Rust and frost eat away at the surface of this broken dagger. When you add it to a _[[Spells/Blistering Invective (Player Core 2)|_blistering invective_]]_ spell, you instead deliver icy words of disinterest or scorn. This causes the spell to deal cold damage instead of fire.
