# Depth Charge  *Item 5+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 80

**Ammunition** any

---

Carved with stylized images of water or aquatic life, _depth charges_ that are fired underwater or at a submerged target function with their normal range increments and can hit no matter their normal damage type. This ammunition explodes if it hits a target underwater, dealing bludgeoning damage in a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save) according to its type. This burst doesn’t extend out of the water.
