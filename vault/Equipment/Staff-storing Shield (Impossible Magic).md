# Staff-storing Shield  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 207

**Price** 350 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|Wooden Shield]]

---

This magically reinforced [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield (Player Core)|wooden shield]] ([[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 6, HP 36, BT 18) normally has a blank face. It can absorb a staff and transform between a shield and staff. When you prepare a staff, you can hold it up to the shield, at which point the items merge until your next [[Rules/Daily Preparations (GM Core)|daily preparations]], and the shield’s face becomes an image corresponding to the type of magic, such as a skull for a _[[Equipment/Staff of the Dead|staff of the dead]]_.

**Activate—Astonishing Transfiguration** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You change the _staff-storing shield_ from its shield form into the stored staff, which has an image of the shield on it, or change it from the stored staff back into a shield. _Reinforcing_ runes etched on the shield have no effect in the staff form, and fundamental weapon runes etched on the stored staff have no effect in the shield form.
