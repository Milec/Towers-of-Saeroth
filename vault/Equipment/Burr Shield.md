# Burr Shield  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 181

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Shield** [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield]]

---

This well-crafted [[Equipment/Shields/Wooden Shield|wooden shield]] (Hardness 5, HP 30, BT 15) is covered in numerous seed pods with long spurs. You can Strike with these burrs as though they were _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shield Spikes|shield spikes]]_.

**Activate** single action Interact;    **Requirements** Your previous action was a successful Strike with the burrs; **Effect** One of the burrs catches on the Strike's target, inflicting 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].
