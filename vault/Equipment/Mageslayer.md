# Mageslayer  *Item 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 130

**Price** 500 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|Scimitar]]

---

This roughly forged _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|scimitar]]_ is a favorite instrument of battlefield operatives seeking to weaken an enemy’s forces by strategically eliminating their magical support. A mageslayer deals an additional 1d6 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage to any creature it Strikes that’s capable of casting spells from the arcane tradition. When wearing or wielding a mageslayer, you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to damage from spells from the [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] tradition.
