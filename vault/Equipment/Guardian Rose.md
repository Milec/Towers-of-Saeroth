This rose-shaped crystal brooch is a translucent bloodred. These talismans have saved the lives of many a soldier on the field of battle. When you activate a _guardian rose_, you avoid being knocked out and regain 2d8+5 Hit Points. However, this puts a strain on your body and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] until the end of your next turn.
