# Rime Foil  *Item 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 34

**Price** 1,400 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier (Player Core)|Rapier]]

---

The steely blue blade of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Frost (GM Core)|frost]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Rapier (Player Core)|rapier]]_ emerges from a hilt wrapped in thick leather and trimmed in fur to protect the wielder’s hand.

**Activate** two actions command, Interact;  **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You hit a creature using the _rime foil_ as your last action; **Effect** You cast 5th-level [[Spells/Phantom Prison|_phantom prison_]] on the target (DC 22 Will to disbelieve). If the target has been damaged by a [[Equipment/Shattered Plan (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_shattered plan_]] in the last round, the DC is instead 24.

**Special**The _rime foil_ pairs with the [[Equipment/Shattered Plan (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_shattered plan_]].
