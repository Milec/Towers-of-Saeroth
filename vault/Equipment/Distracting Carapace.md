# Distracting Carapace  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Graft]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 98

**Price** 80 gp

**Usage** implanted

---

Your skin is studded with pieces of iridescent chitin that ripple like oil on water. When you move your body in a distracting way, your allies can take advantage to move stealthily. When you [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] an ally who is trying to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]], instead of the usual effects of Aid, you can roll an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check and use that result to determine the outcome of the diversion, instead of attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check.
