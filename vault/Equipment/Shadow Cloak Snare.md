# Shadow Cloak Snare  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable (Player Core)|Consumable]], [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|Darkness]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Snare (Player Core 2)|Snare]], [[Traits/Trap (Player Core)|Trap]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 73

**Price** 15 gp

---

When a creature enters the snare's square, it is plunged into an unnatural darkness for 1 minute. Light can't enter the square, any non-magical light sources within the square do not emanate any light, and magical [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]] emanating from a spell of rank 2 or below is suppressed. Those outside the area can't see directly into or through the [[Rules/Light (Player Core)|darkness]], and all adjacent squares are reduced to dim light.
