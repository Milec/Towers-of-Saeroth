# Dragonscale Bo Staff  *Item 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 36

**Price** 240 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|bo staff]]_ is covered in scales shed by or harvested from a [[Bestiary/Families/Dragon|dragon]]. When benefiting from the circumstance bonus to AC granted by the bo staff’s [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]] trait, you also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effects of the tradition matching the dragon who provided the scales, plus [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to a damage type determined by that tradition: [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]], or [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] for [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|primal]]. For instance, a dragonscale bo staff made with scales taken from an [[Bestiary/Young Omen Dragon|omen dragon]] would provide a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against occult effects and resistance 5 to mental damage.

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include scales from one type of dragon.
