# Star of Cynosure  *Item 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 237

**Price** 175 gp

**Usage** affix to armor

**Activate**

free action

([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]])

**Trigger** You attempt a Will save against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell, but you haven’t rolled yet

**Requirement** You have master proficiency in Will saves.

---

Found throughout Golarion, these star-shaped talismans of whalebone scrimshaw are carved by the Erutaki people from the Crown of the World. They are popular with adherents to the cult of [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]], who believe the talismans protect their dreams.

When you activate this talisman, you gain a +2 status bonus to saves against spells with the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] trait for 1 minute. On the triggering save, if the outcome of the roll is a failure, you get a success instead, or if the outcome is a critical failure, you get a failure instead.
