This _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+3 greater resilient]] [[Equipment/Fortification (GM Core)|greater fortification]] [[Equipment/Armor/Breastplate (Player Core)|breastplate]]_ absorbs impacts for later release. While wearing the armor, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage and resistance 10 to force damage. Keep track of how much damage the armor prevents from enemy attacks, as the armor absorbs that damage. After 1 minute, the absorbed damage disperses harmlessly and resets to 0. Only damage caused by foes or hazards powers the armor, not damage you take from yourself, allies, or the environment.

**Activate—Kinetic Pulse** single action ([[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** ;  **Requirements** Your armor has absorbed at least 30 damage; **Effect** The absorbed energy explodes from your armor in a pulse of force. Creatures in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] take 12d6 force damage with a DC 43 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failure, they’re also pushed 10 feet away from you; on a critical failure, they’re instead pushed 20 feet away from you and fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. The armor’s absorbed damage resets to 0.
