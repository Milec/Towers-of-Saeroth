# Singing Shortbow  *Item 14+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 189

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow]]

---

Rather than a normal bowstring, this _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering|thundering]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow|composite shortbow]]_ has a string made of animal gut, much like a musical instrument's. When you shoot the bow, it releases a soft musical note—sonorous if your aim is true and discordant if your shot goes off-target.

**Activate** two actions command ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You sing, hum, or whistle a note, and your bow provides appropriate accompaniment. Make a Strike with the bow. If it hits, the bow generates a magical effect determined by the note you sung, which has the traits indicated in parentheses. This affects every creature in a 10-foot emanation from the creature you hit. As normal for an emanation, you choose whether the creature you hit is affected.

- **Song of Soothing** ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) Each creature regains 3d10 Hit Points and gains a +1 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects for 1 minute.
- **Song of Suffering** ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) Each creature takes 3d10 sonic damage with a DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save.
