# Drover's Band  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 72

**Price** 675 gp

**Usage** worn bracers

**Bulk** L

---

This black leather wrist guard has a bright red gem on the inside of the wrist. Faint glyphs and words of domination in [[Setting/Languages/Infernal]] swim inside the gem. Your words become harsh and clipped when you have this magic item invested.

**Activate** three actions command;  **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You exert your will over a [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] creature within 30 feet. If the target is a mindless creature of 3rd level or lower, it must attempt a DC 20 Will save. If you are a [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devil]], the target uses an outcome one degree of success worse than the result of its saving throw.

**Critical Success** The target creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The target creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round as its responses are muddled by your commands.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]] by you for 1 hour, although it doesn't follow commands that are obviously self-destructive.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the duration is 1 day.
