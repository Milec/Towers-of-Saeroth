**Bulk** L

Classic summoning circles are engraved into the batons of this massive papyrus scroll.

**Activate—Bolstering Spell** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape|spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** If your next action is to [Cast a prepared summon spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3144), you infuse one of the summoned creatures with the spell’s energies, causing it to arrive with 10 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for up to 1 minute.
