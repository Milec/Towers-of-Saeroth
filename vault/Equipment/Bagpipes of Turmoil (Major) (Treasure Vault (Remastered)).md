# Bagpipes of Turmoil  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Coda (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|Coda]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 136

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

---

While those who appreciate bagpipes may like the sound of this gray reed and black leather instrument, its real purpose is to sow turmoil against a performer's enemies, spreading discord with each note. While playing the bagpipes, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks and to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks made to [[Actions/Demoralize]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from this instrument to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed levels of all listed spells.
