Originally formulated as a means of dissolving sovereign glue, this powerful solvent can break almost any adhesive's grip. As universal solvent is particularly effective against [[Equipment/Sovereign Glue|sovereign glue]], it automatically dissolves sovereign glue. It attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any other adhesives, such as [[Spells/Tanglefoot|_tanglefoot bags_]], with a counteract modifier depending on the type.
