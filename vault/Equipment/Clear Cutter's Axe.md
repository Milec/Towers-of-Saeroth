# Clear Cutter's Axe  *Item 9*

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet (Player Core)|Hatchet]]

---

Wielded by wealthy Taldan knights against the Goroth Lodge, this _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency (GM Core)|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] [[Equipment/Returning (GM Core)|returning]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Hatchet (Player Core)|hatchet]]_ has an axe head resembling a roaring [[Bestiary/Lion (Monster Core)|lion’s]] head.

**Activate—Slash and Burn** ⤾ ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])  **Trigger** You critically succeed at a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]];  **Effect** The weapon’s etchings pulse with green energy, as it tears through bark, leaf, and wood. The clear cutter’s axe deals an additional 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] to the plant.

**Activate—Mow Down** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You hurl the axe horizontally, sending it flying through the air in a wild, whirling cyclone. The axe flies in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you, eliminating all non-magical undergrowth and any resulting [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]], and [[Rules/Concealment and Invisibility|concealment]] in area. It attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] one magical effect that affects a plant in the area (counteract +20). Plant creatures in the area take 5d6 slashing damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). As the axe finishes its circuit, it returns to your hand.
