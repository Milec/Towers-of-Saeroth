# Echoing Theater  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Catalyst]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 214

**Price** 60 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** L

**Activate**

Cast a Spell

---

This small diorama is fashioned like a theater and filled with rough-carved wooden figurines. When used as a catalyst to cast a _[[Spells/Ghostly Tragedy (Player Core 2)|_ghostly tragedy_]]_ spell, the caster can record the reenactment for later viewing or showing at a different location. This recording lasts for 1 week, and creatures viewing the recording can attempt checks to investigate the event as if they’d been present when the spell was cast. The figurines in an active diorama slowly move in vague approximations of the recorded events until activated for viewing, upon which they crack in half and fall motionless.
