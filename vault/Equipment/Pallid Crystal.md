# Pallid Crystal  *Item 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hellbreakers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 199

**Price** 360 gp

**Usage** worn

**Access** You're a follower of  Urgathoa .

---

This finger-shaped crystal on a thin strip of leather bridges the worlds of life and undeath, shifting from a pale opaque pink when worn by a living creature to a deep translucent purple when worn by an undead creature. While wearing the necklace, rotten food and drink taste as if they were fresh and can be eaten with no ill effects. If you're healed by healing [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effects, you gain void resistance 5, but if you're healed by [[Traits/Void|void]] effects, you instead gain vitality resistance 5.
