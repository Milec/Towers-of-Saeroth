# Admiral's Bicorne  *Item 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 148

**Price** 900 gp

**Usage** worn headwear

**Bulk** L

---

This ostentatious hat is trimmed with gold thread and tiny jewels, all proclaiming your position of authority on the high seas. While wearing the bicorne, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] and Sailing [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] checks.

**Activate—Fight On** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You take damage from an enemy’s [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or spell attack roll;  **Effect** Despite your wounds, your troops are inspired to fight on. For 1 minute, all allies in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] gain a +2 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects.
