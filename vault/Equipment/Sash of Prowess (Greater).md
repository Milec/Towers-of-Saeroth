# Sash of Prowess  *Item 10+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focused (GM Core)|Focused]], [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 310

**Usage** worn

---

This humble sash can be worn around the waist or across the chest. A sash of prowess often bears a coloration or a pattern that represents the monastery in which you trained but can also sport religious symbology, such as the open hand of Irori. You gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill checks.

**Activate—Effortless Mastery** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;  **Requirements** You succeed at an Acrobatics or Athletics skill check; **Effect** You critically succeed instead.

**Activate—Reserves of Inner Strength** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You gain 1 Focus Point, which you can spend only to cast a qi spell. If you don't spend this Focus Point by the end of this turn, it's lost.

**Craft Requirements** You're a monk who can cast qi spells.
