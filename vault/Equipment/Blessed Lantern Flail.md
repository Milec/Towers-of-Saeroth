# Blessed Lantern Flail  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 208

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail (Player Core)|Flail]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 striking]] [[Equipment/Holy (GM Core)|holy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Flail (Player Core)|flail]]_ doubles as both a hooded lantern and a bludgeoning weapon, giving off [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 30 feet when wielded. The lantern’s light can’t be extinguished by non-magical means. It’s said that an enlightened gravekeeper crafted the first _blessed lantern flail_.

**Activate—Purge Unholy Magic** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes;   **Effect** You use the flail to temporarily disrupt an unholy spellcaster’s connection to their magic. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with the flail against an [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature with [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate]] or [[Rules/Prepared Spells (Player Core)|prepared spells]]. If you hit and deal damage, the target must attempt a DC 31 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is otherwise unaffected.  

**Success** Until the beginning of your next turn, the creature must succeed at a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to Cast a Spell.  

**Failure** As success, but the effect lasts for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the DC of the flat check is 11.
