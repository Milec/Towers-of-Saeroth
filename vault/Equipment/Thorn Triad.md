# Thorn Triad  *Item 4+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellheart]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 203

**Usage** affixed to non-metal armor or a weapon

---

From each corner of this carved, triangular badge extends a long, sharp thorn. The spell DC of any spell cast by activating this item is 18.

- **Armor** After you cast a [[Traits/Plant|plant]] spell by activating the triad, you gain resistance 2 to bludgeoning and piercing damage and weakness 3 to fire until the end of your next turn, or double the resistance for a non-cantrip spell.
- **Weapon** After you cast a [[Traits/Plant|plant]] spell by activating the triad, thorns erupt from the weapon. Your Strikes with the weapon deal an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] until the end of your next turn.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You cast [[Spells/Timber|_timber_]].
