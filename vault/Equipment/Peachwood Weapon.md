**Usage** varies by weapon

---

Peachwood weapons have an auburn tinge in direct sunlight. Peachwood is treated as duskwood for [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures' [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistances]]  or [[Rules/Weakness|weaknesses]] related to duskwood (bypassing a [[Bestiary/Provincial Jiang-Shi|jiang-shi's]] resistance, for example). In addition, peachwood weapons bypass a small portion of the resistances of any undead creature. [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with a peachwood weapon treat an undead's resistances against physical damage as 2 lower for standard-grade peachwood, and 4 lower for high-grade.

## Peachwood Weapon (Standard-Grade)  *Item 12*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 129, [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 20
