# Petrification Cannon  *Item 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 155

**Price** 6,500 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Double-Barreled Musket (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Double-Barreled Musket]]

---

Built from the taxidermic body of a [[Bestiary/Basilisk (Monster Core)|basilisk]], a _petrification cannon_ functions as a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Double-Barreled Musket (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|double-barreled musket]]_. A _petrification cannon_ can be activated to fire a beam of energy that transforms flesh into stone.

**Activate** two actions Interact ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** A beam of coiling energy leaps from the _petrification cannon_ at a target within 60 feet. The target must attempt a Fortitude save against DC 34 with the effects of [[Spells/Petrify|_flesh to stone_]].

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the body of a basilisk.
