This small vial is filled with a grayish-green dust rendered from dried ectoplasm. When you activate the dust, it casts a 4th-rank [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] spell on you. You may then Stride or Step. You can instead Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim if you have the corresponding Speed.
