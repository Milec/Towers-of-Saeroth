# Thundercrasher  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 39

**Price** 155 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Blunderbuss]]

---

While looking straight down the barrel of this _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Blunderbuss (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|blunderbuss]]_, the spark gun's magical core is visible amid several reflectors. All damage dealt by a _thundercrasher_ is sonic damage. On a critical hit, the target must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.

**Activate** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** You overload the _thundercrasher_ to emit chaotic sonic frequencies that soften earth and stone. When you next fire the _thundercrasher_ it also partially liquefies any natural earth or stone surfaces within range of its [[Traits/Scatter (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|scatter]] trait, making the area difficult terrain.
