# Ouroboros Flail  *Item 14+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 210

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 2

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/War Flail (Player Core)|War Flail]]

---

You can easily magically expand and contract the chain of this _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Extending (GM Core)|extending]] [[Equipment/Weapons/War Flail (Player Core)|war flail]]_.

**Activate—Chained Grasp** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** You successfully Strike with the _ouroboros flail_ as part of activating its _extending_ rune; **Requirements** ; **Effect** The head of the flail wraps around the target of the triggering Strike and drags it in your direction. The target must succeed at a DC 32 Fortitude save or be pulled adjacent to you. A creature pulled to you is then [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the flail until the end of your next turn unless you move, your target [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 31), or you attack with the flail, whichever comes first.

**Activate—Infinite Links** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]])    **Effect** You extend the flail’s chain, then wrap it across itself and strike two links together. This severs up to a 60-foot loop of usable chain from the flail, which instantly returns to its normal length. If you use Infinite Links again, any previous chain you created with it disappears after 1 round. As with most obviously magical and temporary constructs, the chain can’t be sold.
