# Chained Mist  *Item 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 202

**Price** 3,000 gp

**Usage** worn armor

**Bulk** 1

**Base Armor** [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|Chain Shirt]]

---

Made of standard-grade dawnsilver, mist spirals around this _[[Equipment/Magic Armor (GM Core)|+1 resilient]] [[Equipment/Shadow (GM Core)|greater shadow]] [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]] [[Equipment/Armor/Chain Shirt (Player Core)|chain shirt]]_. When worn, silver vapor trails interwoven with wispy threads of shadow bedeck and move in harmony with the wearer’s limbs and torso. If the wearer is in [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]], the mist obscures the wearer’s form, granting the wearer the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition.

**Activate—Misty Mesh** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The mist immediately solidifies into a protective mesh of silvery chains that gives you [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage for 1 minute.
