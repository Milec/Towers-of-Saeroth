# Bloodstained Waistcoat  *Item 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 148

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) A _bloodstained waistcoat’s_ change to DCs overwrites any other changes to flat checks, whether beneficial or harmful to the wearer._

**Price** 85 gp

**Usage** worn garment

**Bulk** L

---

This white vest has a large crimson bloodstain that can never be removed. Imbued with the anguish of a comrade who bled to death in the creator’s arms, a _bloodstained waistcoat_ helps prevent you and your fellow soldiers from suffering the same fate. Your [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to remove [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] is DC 10 instead of DC 15, which is reduced to DC 5 if another creature uses a particularly appropriate action to help.

**Activate—Staunch Bleeding** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** The stain on the _bloodstained waistcoat_ gets slightly larger as you bleed in lieu of an ally. The waistcoat ends a persistent bleed condition for one ally within 30 feet, but you gain that condition with the same parameters.
