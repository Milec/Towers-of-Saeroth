**Trigger** You would be forced to move, you would be teleported, or you would take [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], or [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] damage.

---

This unassuming paper tag is inscribed with magical symbols. When you activate the fulu, it vanishes in a wisp of cloud, and you gain resistance 15 to cold, electricity, and sonic damage until the end of your next turn. For that duration, you ignore difficult terrain from wind and weather, and you can't be [[Rules/Forced Movement|forcibly moved]] or teleported unless the effect [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracts]] the fulu (DC 40). These protections apply against the triggering effect.
