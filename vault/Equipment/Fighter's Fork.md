**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident]]

---

This _+1 [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|trident]]_, usually engraved with a decorative pattern resembling fish scales, is a common weapon among warriors of aquatic ancestries.

**Activate** single action Interact;     **Effect** You extend or shorten the trident’s haft. When extended, the trident requires two hands to wield and gains the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait, but loses the trident’s normal [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] trait.
