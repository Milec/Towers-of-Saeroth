# Dazzling Shortbow  *Item 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 126

**Price** 160 gp

**Usage** held in 2 hands

**Bulk** 1

**Base Weapon** [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|Shortbow]]

---

This _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Shortbow (Tian Xia Character Guide)|shortbow]]_ is a favorite of mage hunters and those who frequently fight enemies who can turn themselves [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]]. A creature who is critically hit with a ranged Strike from a dazzling shortbow must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute.

**Activate—Show Yourself!** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day;   **Effect** You fire an arrow that glows with purple light at a spot. Each creature in a 10-foot burst within 60 feet is affected by [[Spells/Revealing Light|_revealing light_]] (DC 19).

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of _revealing light_.
