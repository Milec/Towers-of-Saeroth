# Dragonclaw Scutcheon  *Item 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consumable]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Talisman]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 92

**Price** 1,600 gp

**Usage** affixed to a shield

**Activate**

free action

envision

**Trigger** You would take damage of a type depending on the talisman’s dragon type: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage

---

This decorative shield emblem contains the gilded claw of a dragon mounted in a setting of high-grade [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] alloy. It protects against a damage type depending on the type of dragon the claw came from (see the sidebar). When you Activate the scutcheon, you and all of your carried, wielded, or worn items gain immunity to all damage of that type until the end of your next turn.

If you have a _dragonclaw scutcheon_, [[Equipment/Dragonscale Cameo (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|_dragonscale cameo_]], and _[[Equipment/Dragontooth Trophy (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|dragontooth trophy]]_ attached to your items, and they all correspond to the same type of dragon, you gain an extra benefit. When you Activate any of these talismans, you gain the ability to cast an 8th-level, DC 35 [[Spells/Dragon Breath (Player Core 2)|_dragon breath_]] focus spell as an [[Rules/Innate Spells (Player Core)|innate spell]] once before the end of your next turn. Use the dragon type that matches the talismans; you can Cast the Spell this way without spending a Focus Point. Because each talisman disintegrates when used, you can’t get this benefit again until you’ve attached a replacement.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one claw from an adult or older dragon.
