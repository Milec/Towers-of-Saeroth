# Clawed Bracers  *Item 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Invested (GM Core)|Invested]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]

**Source** [[Sources/GM Core]] pg. 287

**Price** 325 gp

**Usage** worn bracers

**Bulk** L

---

Animal claws are woven into the thick leather of these bracers.

**Activate—Extend Claws** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]. [[Traits/Morph|morph]]) **Frequency** once per hour;   **Effect** The bracers fuse temporarily with your forearms, with the claws extending to your fingertips. You gain a climb Speed of 20 feet and a claw unarmed attack with the [[Traits/Agile|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] traits that deals 1d6 slashing damage. This lasts for 10 minutes or until you Dismiss it.
