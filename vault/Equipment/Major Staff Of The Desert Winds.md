# Staff Of The Desert Winds  *Item 6+*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 240

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

This crooked staff is made from twisting acacia wood and has a sphere of rough sandstone embedded in the top to channel the magic of the desert. When wielding this staff in deserts, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Cover Tracks (Player Core)|Cover Tracks]], [[Actions/Subsist (Player Core)|Subsist]], and [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]].

**Activate**  Cast a Spell    **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
