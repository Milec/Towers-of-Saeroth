# Hell Staff  *Item 17*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Staff]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault (Remastered)]] pg. 132

**Price** 13,000 gp

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Bulk** 1

---

A _hell staff_ is a tall, pointed staff forged of red-tinted steel with [[Setting/Languages/Diabolic]] inscriptions that march neatly down its sides. At its top sits an inverted ruby pyramid divided into nine sections. Found mostly in Cheliax or other lands where diabolic influences hold sway, when used as a weapon the staff is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+3 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]] [[Equipment/Unholy (GM Core)|unholy]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff (Tian Xia Character Guide)|staff]]_. When you prepare this staff, if you’re [[Traits/Holy|holy]], you become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 until your next daily preparations. The staff’s [[Spells/Summon Lesser Servitor (Player Core 2)|_summon lesser servitor_]] spell can be used only to summon animals with the [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] trait, [[Bestiary/Families/Devil (Monster Core)|devils]], or [[Bestiary/Families/Hell Hound (Monster Core)|hell hounds]] (at 4th rank). Its [[Spells/Summon Fiend (Player Core)|_summon fiend_]] spell can summon only devils or hell hounds.

**Activate**  Cast a Spell;     **Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Spells/Divine Lance (Player Core)|_divine lance_]]
- **1st** [[Spells/Protection (Player Core)|_protection_]], _summon lesser servitor_
- **2nd** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]], _summon lesser servitor_
- **3rd** [[Spells/Chilling Darkness (Player Core)|_chilling darkness_]], _darkvision_, _summon lesser servitor_
- **4th** [[Spells/Divine Wrath (Player Core)|_divine wrath_]], _summon lesser servitor_
- **5th** _darkvision_, [[Spells/Divine Immolation|_divine immolation_]], _summon fiend_
- **6th** [[Spells/Devil Form|_devil form_]], _summon fiend_
- **7th** [[Spells/Divine Decree (Player Core)|_divine decree_]], _summon fiend_

**Craft Requirements** You’re unholy. Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
