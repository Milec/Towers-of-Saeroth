**Traits** [[Traits/Cavalry]], [[Traits/Infantry]], [[Traits/Siege]], [[Traits/Skirmisher]]

The army includes several spotters and scouts who have [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and the rest of the soldiers have been trained to follow their lead so that the army itself functions as if it had low-light vision.
