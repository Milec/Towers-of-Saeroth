# Explosive Shot  *Warfare Tactic 11*

**Traits** [[Traits/Siege]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 575

The army's ranged attacks explode and spray fire, shrapnel, or other damaging material in every direction. Whenever the army critically hits a non-[[Rules/Conditions/Distant|distant]] army with a ranged Strike, inflict 1 point of additional damage to another non-distant enemy army of your choice. You can use the Overwhelming Bombardment tactical war action with the army
