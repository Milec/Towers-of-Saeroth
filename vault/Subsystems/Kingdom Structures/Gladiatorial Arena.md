# Gladiatorial Arena  *Kingdom Structure 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Edifice]], [[Traits/Famous]], [[Traits/Infamous]], [[Traits/Yard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 10

_Nethys note: This structure requires that Amiri is a member of your kingdom._

A gladiatorial arena is a sprawling open-air field surrounded by seating and viewing areas. It also includes extensive underground barracks and training facilities for gladiators to use.

---

**Lots** 4

**Cost** 58 RP, 10 Lumber, 30 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Warfare]] (master) DC 34

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Arena]]

**Item Bonus**
+3 item bonus to [[Actions/Celebrate Holiday]], to [[Actions/Hire Adventurers]], and to Warfare checks made in order to [[Actions/Quell Unrest]]

**Effects**
A gladiatorial arena allows a PC in the settlement to [[Rules/Retraining|retrain]] combat-themed feats (at the GM's discretion) more efficiently; doing so takes only 4 days rather than a week of downtime.
