# Trade Shop  *Kingdom Structure 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

A trade shop is a store that focuses on providing services.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 10 RP, 2 Lumber

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (trained) DC 18

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Guildhall]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Purchase Commodities]]

**Effects**
When you build a trade shop, indicate the kind of shop it is, such as a bakery, carpenter, tailor, and so on. While in a settlement with a trade shop, you gain a +1 item bonus to all associated [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] checks.
