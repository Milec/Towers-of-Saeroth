# Printing House  *Kingdom Structure 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 54

_Nethys note: This structure requires the completion of Linzi's companion quest._

A printing house gives your citizens—and the PCs themselves—a place to create newspapers and books.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 48 RP, 12 Lumber, 12 Luxuries

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Industry]] (master) DC 27

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Actions/Improve Lifestyle]] and [[Actions/Quell Unrest]]

**Effects**
A PC in a settlement with a printing house gains a +2 item bonus to checks to [[Actions/Gather Information]] or to [[Rules/Research]] any topic in a library or similar structure.
