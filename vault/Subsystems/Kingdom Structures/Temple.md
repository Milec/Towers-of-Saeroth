# Temple  *Kingdom Structure 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Famous]], [[Traits/Infamous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

A temple is a building devoted to worshipping a deity or faith.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 32 RP, 6 Lumber, 6 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Folklore]] (trained) DC 23

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Shrine]]

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Cathedral]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Celebrate Holiday]] and [[Actions/Provide Care]]

**Effects**
The first time you build a temple each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 2. Treat the settlement's level as one level higher than its actual level for the purposes of determining what [[Traits/Divine|divine]] magic items are readily available for sale in that settlement. This effect stacks up to three times but does not stack with the same effect granted by shrines or cathedrals.
