# Tavern, Luxury  *Kingdom Structure 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Famous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 551

A luxury tavern is a high-class establishment for entertainment, eating, and drinking. It may even include a built-in stage for performers to use.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 48 RP, 10 Lumber, 8 Luxuries, 8 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (master) DC 26

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Tavern, Popular]]

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Tavern, World-Class]]

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Actions/Hire Adventurers]] and to [[Actions/Rest and Relax]] using Trade

**Effects**
The first time you build a luxury tavern in a Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 1d4+1. If attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Actions/Earn Income]] in a settlement with a luxury tavern, you gain a +2 item bonus to the check. All checks made to [[Actions/Gather Information]] in a settlement with at least one luxury tavern gain a +2 item bonus.
