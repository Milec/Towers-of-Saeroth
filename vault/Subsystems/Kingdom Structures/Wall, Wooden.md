# Wall, Wooden  *Kingdom Structure 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infrastructure]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

Wooden walls provide serviceable defenses to a settlement.

---

**Lots** —

**Cost** 2 RP, 4 Lumber

[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] DC 15

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Wall, Stone]]

**Effects**
A wooden wall is built along the border of your settlement. The first time you build a wooden wall in each settlement, reduce Unrest by 1. When you build a wooden wall, choose a border on your Urban Grid and check the appropriate checkbox.
