# Brewery  *Kingdom Structure 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 545

A brewery is devoted to crafting alcohol, be it beer, wine, or spirits. This building can represent bottlers, vineyards, or even structures that produce non-alcoholic drinks.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 6 RP, 2 Lumber

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Agriculture]] DC 15

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Establish Trade Agreement]]

**Effects**
When you build a brewery, reduce Unrest by 1 as long as you have fewer than 4 breweries in the settlement at that time.
