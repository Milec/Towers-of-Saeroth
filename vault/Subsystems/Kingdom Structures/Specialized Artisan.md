# Specialized Artisan  *Kingdom Structure 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 551

These shops and homes are devoted to crafters who create fine jewelry, glassware, clockworks, and the like.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 10 RP, 4 Lumber, 1 Luxuries

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (expert) DC 19

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Craft Luxuries]]

**Effects**
While in a settlement with a specialized artisan, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Actions/Craft]] checks made to craft specialized goods like jewelry.
