# Tavern, World-Class  *Kingdom Structure 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]], [[Traits/Famous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

A World-Class Tavern is a legendary establishment for entertainment, eating, and drinking. It has at least one venue for performances—perhaps multiple ones.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 64 RP, 18 Lumber, 15 Luxuries, 15 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (master) DC 34

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Tavern, Luxury]]

**Item Bonus**
+3 item bonus to [[Actions/Hire Adventurers]], to [[Actions/Rest and Relax]] using Trade, and to [[Actions/Repair Reputation]] (Strife)

**Effects**
The first time you build a world-class tavern in a Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 2d4. If you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Actions/Earn Income]] in a settlement with a world-class tavern, you gain a +3 item bonus to the check. All checks made to [[Actions/Gather Information]] in a settlement with a world-class tavern gain a +3 item bonus.
