# Cemetery  *Kingdom Structure 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Yard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 545

A cemetery sets aside a plot of land to bury the dead and can also include above-ground vaults or underground catacombs.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 4 RP, 1 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Folklore]] DC 15

**Effects**
Giving the citizens a place to bury and remember their departed loved ones helps to temper Unrest gained from [[Traits/Dangerous|dangerous]] events. If you have at least one cemetery in a settlement, reduce Unrest gained from any dangerous settlement events in that particular settlement by 1 (to a maximum of 4 for four cemeteries). The presence of a cemetery provides additional effects during certain kingdom events.
