# Noble Villa  *Kingdom Structure 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Residential]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 549

This sprawling manor has luxurious grounds. It houses a noble family and their staff, and includes several smaller support structures such as servant's quarters, stables, and groundskeeper's cottages in addition to a manor.

---

**Lots** 2

**Construction**

**Upgrade From**

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Improve Lifestyle]] and to [[Actions/Quell Unrest]] using Politics

**Effects**
The first time you build a noble villa each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 2.
