# Castle  *Kingdom Structure 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]], [[Traits/Famous]], [[Traits/Infamous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 545

A castle is a fortified structure that often serves as the seat of government for a kingdom.

---

**Lots** 4

**Cost** 54 RP, 12 Lumber, 12 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] (expert), [[Rules/Skills/Industry]] (expert), [[Rules/Skills/Magic]] (expert), or [[Rules/Skills/Statecraft]] (expert) DC 26

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Town Hall]]

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Palace]]

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Actions/New Leadership]], [[Actions/Pledge of Fealty]], [[Actions/Send Diplomatic Envoy]], and +2 item bonus to Garrison Army, Recover Army, or Recruit Army (see Appendix 3: Warfare)

**Effects**
The first time you build a castle each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 1d4. A castle in a capital allows PC leaders to take 3 Leadership activities during the Activity phase of a Kingdom turn rather than 2.
