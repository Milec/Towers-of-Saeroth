# Tavern, Popular  *Kingdom Structure 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 551

A popular tavern is a respectable establishment for entertainment, eating, and drinking.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 24 RP, 6 Lumber, 2 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (expert) DC 18

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Tavern, Dive]]

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Tavern, Luxury]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Hire Adventurers]] and to [[Actions/Rest and Relax]] using Trade

**Effects**
The first time you build a popular tavern in a Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 2. If you attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Actions/Earn Income]] in a settlement with a popular tavern, you gain a +1 item bonus to the check. All checks made to [[Actions/Gather Information]] in a settlement with at least one popular tavern gain a +1 item bonus.
