# Sewer System  *Kingdom Structure 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infrastructure]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 550

This underground sanitation system helps keep the settlement clean and disease-free.

---

**Lots** —

**Cost** 24 RP, 8 Lumber, 8 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Sidebars/Magical Traditions and Skills (Player Core)|Engineering]] (expert) DC 23

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Clandestine Business]]

**Effects**
A sewer system reduces the settlement's Consumption by 1. Having a sewer system can also affect certain kingdom events. When you build a sewer system, check the sewer system checkbox on its Urban Grid. (For metropolises, this infrastructure automatically applies to all of its Urban Grids.)
