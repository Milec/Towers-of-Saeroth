# Shrine  *Kingdom Structure 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 550

A shrine is a small building devoted to the worship of a deity or faith. It can be attended by resident priests or visiting clergy.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 8 RP, 2 Lumber, 1 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Folklore]] (trained) DC 15

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Temple]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Celebrate Holiday]]

**Effects**
Treat the settlement's level as one level higher than its actual level when determining what [[Traits/Divine|divine]] magic items are readily available for sale in that settlement. This effect stacks up to three times but does not stack with the same effect granted by temples or cathedrals.
