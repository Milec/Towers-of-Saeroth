# Town Hall  *Kingdom Structure 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

A town hall is a public venue for town meetings and a repository for town history and records.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 22 RP, 4 Lumber, 4 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] (trained), [[Rules/Skills/Industry]] (trained), [[Rules/Skills/Magic]] (trained), or [[Rules/Skills/Statecraft]] (trained) DC 16

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Castle]]

**Effects**
The first time you build a town hall each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 1. A town hall in a capital allows PC leaders to take 3 [[Traits/Leadership]] activities during the Activity phase of a Kingdom turn rather than just 2.
