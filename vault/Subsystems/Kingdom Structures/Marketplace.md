# Marketplace  *Kingdom Structure 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Residential]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 548

A marketplace is a large neighborhood of shops run by local vendors around an open area for traveling merchants and farmers to peddle their wares.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 48 RP, 4 Lumber

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Trade]] (trained) DC 19

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/General Store]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Establish Trade Agreement]]

**Effects**
A town without a general store or marketplace reduces its effective level for the purposes of determining what items can be purchased there by 2.
