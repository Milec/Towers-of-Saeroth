# Illicit Market  *Kingdom Structure 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Infamous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 547

An illicit market uses a facade of shops, homes, and other innocent-seeming buildings to cover the fact that unregulated and illegal trade takes place within its walls.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 50 RP, 5 Lumber

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Intrigue]] (trained) DC 22

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Clandestine Business]]

**Ruin**
+1 Crime

**Effects**
Treat the settlement's level as one level higher than its actual level for the purposes of determining what items are readily available for sale in that settlement. This effect stacks up to three times.
