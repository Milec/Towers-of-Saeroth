# Monument  *Kingdom Structure 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 549

A monument is an impressive stone structure built to commemorate a historical event, honor a beloved leader, memorialize a tragedy, or simply serve as an artistic display.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 6 RP, 1 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Arts]] (trained) DC 18

**Item Bonus**
The first time you build a monument each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 1 and reduce one Ruin of your choice by 1.
