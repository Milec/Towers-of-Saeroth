# Opera House  *Kingdom Structure 15*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]], [[Traits/Famous]], [[Traits/Infamous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 549

An opera house functions well as a venue for operas, plays, and concerts, but also includes extensive facilities to aid in the training of all manner of bardic pursuits. Often, an opera house becomes a grandiose landmark, either due to its outlandish colors or eye-catching architecture.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 40 RP, 20 Lumber, 18 Luxuries, 16 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Arts]] (master) DC 34

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Theater]]

**Item Bonus**
+3 item bonus to [[Actions/Celebrate Holiday]] and [[Actions/Create a Masterpiece]]

**Effects**
The first time you build an opera house each Kingdom turn, reduce Unrest by 4. While in a settlement with an opera house, you gain a +3 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks made to [[Actions/Earn Income]].
