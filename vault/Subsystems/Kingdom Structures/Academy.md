# Academy  *Kingdom Structure 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Edifice]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 544

An academy gives your citizens—and the PCs themselves— an institution where advanced study in many fields can be pursued, researched, and referenced.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 52 RP, 12 Lumber, 6 Luxuries, 12 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Scholarship]] (expert) DC 27

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Library]]

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Military Academy]], [[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/University]]

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Actions/Creative Solution]]

**Effects**
While in a settlement with an Academy, you gain a +2 item bonus to Lore checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] while [[Actions/Investigate]], to all checks made while [[Rules/Research|Researching]], and to [[Actions/Decipher Writing]].
