# Library  *Kingdom Structure 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 547

A library contains collections of books, scrolls, writings, and records conducive to research. Some libraries specialize in certain topics, but it's best to assume these libraries are well-rounded in what books they cover

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 6 RP, 4 Lumber, 2 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Scholarship]] (trained) DC 16

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Academy]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Rest and Relax]] using Scholarship checks

**Effects**
While in a settlement with a library, you gain a +1 item bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] while Investigating, as well as to [[Rules/Research|Researching]] checks, and to [[Actions/Decipher Writing]] checks.
