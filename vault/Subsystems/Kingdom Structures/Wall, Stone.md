# Wall, Stone  *Kingdom Structure 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infrastructure]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

Stone walls provide solid defenses to a settlement's borders.

---

**Lots** —

**Cost** 4 RP, 8 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] (trained) DC 20

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Wall, Wooden]]

**Effects**
A stone wall is built along the border of your settlement. The first time you build a stone wall in each settlement, reduce Unrest by 1. When you build a stone wall, choose a border on your Urban Grid and check the appropriate checkbox; if you're upgrading from a wooden wall, uncheck that box.
