# Bridge  *Kingdom Structure 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infrastructure]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 545

Bridges give settlements that have water borders a connection to land (but at the GM's option, a border on a lake might not be able to use bridges).

---

**Lots** —

**Cost** 6 RP, 1 Lumber or 1 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Sidebars/Magical Traditions and Skills (Player Core)|Engineering]] DC 16

**Effects**
A bridge allows an island settlement to provide influence (see [[Rules/Influence (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Influence]]), negates the Trade penalty for island settlements (see [[Rules/Urban Grid Borders|Land Borders]]), and allows travel over its associated Water Border with ease (see [[Rules/Navigating an Urban Grid]]). Bridges can only be built on Water Borders. When you build a bridge, check the “Bridge” box on one of the Water Borders on your Urban Grid to indicate its location.
