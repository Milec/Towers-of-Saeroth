# Watchtower  *Kingdom Structure 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 552

A watchtower serves as a guard post that grants a settlement advance warning to upcoming dangerous events.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 12 RP, 4 Lumber or 4 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] (trained) DC 18

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to checks to resolve events affecting the settlement.

**Effects**
The first time you build a watchtower each Kingdom turn, decrease Unrest by 1.
