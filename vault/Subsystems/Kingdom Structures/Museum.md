# Museum  *Kingdom Structure 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]], [[Traits/Famous]], [[Traits/Infamous]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 549

A museum displays art, objects of important cultural note, wonders of the natural world, and other marvels in a place where citizens can observe and learn.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 30 RP, 6 Lumber, 2 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Exploration]] (trained) DC 20

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Rest and Relax]] using [[Rules/Skills/Arts]]

**Effects**
A magic item of level 6 or higher that has a particular import or bears significant historical or regional value (at the GM's discretion) can be donated to a museum. Each time such an item is donated, reduce Unrest by 1. If that item is later removed from display, increase Unrest by 1.
