# Hospital  *Kingdom Structure 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 547

A hospital is a building dedicated to healing the sick through both magical and mundane means.

---

**Lots** 2

**Cost** 30 RP, 10 Lumber, 6 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Defense]] (expert) DC 26

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Herbalist]]

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Provide Care]] and [[Actions/Quell Unrest]]

**Effects**
While in a settlement with a hospital, you gain a +2 item bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks to [[Actions/Treat Disease]] and [[Actions/Treat Wounds]].
