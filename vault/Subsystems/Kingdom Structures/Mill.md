# Mill  *Kingdom Structure 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 548

A mill grinds grain using the power of wind, water, or beasts of burden.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 6 RP, 2 Lumber, 1 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Industry]] (trained) DC 16

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Harvest Crops]]

**Effects**
If a settlement includes at least one mill built on a lot adjacent to a Water border, the increased efficiency of these mills reduces the settlement's Consumption by 1 (to a minimum of 0).
