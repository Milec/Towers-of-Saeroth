# Alchemy Laboratory  *Kingdom Structure 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 544

An alchemy laboratory serves as a factory for alchemists and their apprentices for the crafting of potions, elixirs, and all manner of alchemical items. An infamous kingdom's laboratory might specialize in poisons as well.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 18 RP, 2 Ore, 5 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Industry]] (trained) DC 16

**Item Bonus**
+1 item bonus to [[Actions/Demolish]]

**Effects**
Treat the settlement's level as one level higher than its actual level for the purposes of determining which [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical items]] are readily available for sale in that settlement. This effect stacks up to three times. Checks attempted to [[Actions/Identify Alchemy]] in any settlement with at least one alchemy laboratory gain a +1 item bonus.
