# Occult Shop  *Kingdom Structure 13*

**Traits** [[Traits/Building]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 549

An occult shop is usually a sprawling, mysterious store that specializes in buying and selling obscure magic and strange curios. It often provides access to supernatural services like fortune-telling.

---

**Lots** 1

**Cost** 38 RP, 12 Lumber, 12 Luxuries, 6 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Magic]] (master) DC 32

**Upgrade From**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Magic Shop]]

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Equipment/Cat|Prognostication]]

**Effects**
Treat the settlement's level as one level higher than its actual level for the purposes of determining what [[Traits/Magical|magic]] items are readily available for sale in that settlement. This effect stacks up to three times and overlaps with other stores that function in this way for more specific categories of magic items. While in a settlement with an occult shop, you gain a +2 item bonus to all checks made to [[Rules/Research]] esoteric subjects or to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about the same.
