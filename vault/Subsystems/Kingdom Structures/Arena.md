# Arena  *Kingdom Structure 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Edifice]], [[Traits/Yard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 544

An Arena is a large public structure, traditionally open to the air, surrounded by seating and viewing areas. It's used for staging competitions, athletics, gladiatorial combats, and elaborate entertainments and spectacles.

---

**Lots** 4

**Cost** 40 RP, 6 Lumber, 12 Stone

**Construction**
[[Rules/Skills/Warfare]] (expert) DC 26

**Upgrade To**
[[Subsystems/Kingdom Structures/Gladiatorial Arena]]

**Item Bonus**
+2 item bonus to [[Actions/Celebrate Holiday]] and to Warfare checks made to [[Actions/Quell Unrest]]

**Effects**
An arena lets you to [[Rules/Retraining|retrain]] combat-themed feats more efficiently while in the settlement; doing so takes only 5 days rather than a week of downtime.
