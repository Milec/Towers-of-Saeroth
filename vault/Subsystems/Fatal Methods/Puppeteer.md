# Puppeteer  *Necromancer Fatal Method*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 29

You prefer to study life and death from afar. You gain the Consume Thrall action and the thrall proliferation ability.

**Consume Thrall**
free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Necromancer|necromancer]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** You have 0 Focus Points and at least one thrall with 30 feet of you; **Effects** You destroy one thrall within 30 feet of you and regain 1 Focus Point. You can spend this focus point only to cast a grave spell.

**Thrall Proliferation**
Once per round when you cast [[Spells/Create Thrall|_create thrall_]], you can create one additional thrall in range.
