**Amphibious** (unique tactic) Frog Riders ignore difficult terrain caused by swamps or water and gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Traits/Maneuver]] checks while fighting on a battlefield that features either of these terrains.  
 **Chorus of Croaks** (unique tactic) The terrifying croaking that constantly issues from this army of boggards causes all engaged enemy armies to suffer a –2 status penalty on [[Traits/Morale]] checks. The M'botuu Frog Riders can use the Taunt tactical action. If they know the Focused Devotion tactic, they treat critical failures made when attempting to Taunt as failures instead.  
 **Darkvision** (unique tactic)  
 **Swamp Charge** (unique tactic) If the battlefield includes swamp or water, the M'botuu Frog Riders can start the encounter [[Rules/Conditions/Engaged|engaged]] with a target enemy army. If they do so, they gain a +1 circumstance bonus on melee Strikes against that army on the first turn of the encounter.
