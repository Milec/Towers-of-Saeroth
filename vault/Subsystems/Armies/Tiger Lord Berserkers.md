# Tiger Lord Berserkers  *Warfare Army 12*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Infantry]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 573

These Tiger Lord barbarians (encounter area **TL3**) use rage in battle; they fight with greataxes.

**Furious Charge** single action (unique tactic) **Frequency** once per war encounter; **Effect** The Tiger Lord Berserkers charge at an enemy army, taking an Advance action to attempt to engage. If the Tiger Lords manage to engage successfully, they can attempt a melee Strike against the army as a reaction.  
 **Reactive Rally** three actions (unique tactic) **Requirement** The Tiger Lords are [[Rules/Conditions/Routed|routed]]; **Effect** Instead of taking a Retreat action, the Tiger Lord Berserkers can attempt to Rally. **Revel in Battle** ⤾ (unique tactic) **Trigger** The Tiger Lord Berserkers score a critical hit with a melee Strike. **Effect** The barbarians are infused with vigor and furious passion at their devastating blow. The army restores 1 hit point.  
 **Warmongers** (unique tactic) The Tiger Lord Berserkers can use the following Tactical Actions without needing to meet additional requirements: All-Out Assault, Counterattack, and Taunt.
