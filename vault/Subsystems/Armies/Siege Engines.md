# Siege Engines  *Warfare Army 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Siege]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 571

A siege engine army consists of several catapults, ballistae, trebuchets, or other mechanized engines of war.

**Engines of War** Siege engines cannot be outfitted with gear. They cannot attack [[Rules/Conditions/Engaged|engaged]] armies. They are more difficult to destroy due to their higher hit points than other basic armies. A siege engine can attack and damage fortifications with its ranged attacks as part of the Battle or Overwhelming Bombardment actions.
