# Good Weather  *Kingdom Event +1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beneficial]], [[Traits/Fortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 559

**Kingdom Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Wilderness]]

**Leader** Magister

---

Good weather raises spirits among citizens.

**Critical Success** The glorious climate puts everyone in an excellent mood. Reduce Unrest by 1d6, and once during your next Kingdom turn when you attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Agriculture]], [[Rules/Skills/Boating]], [[Rules/Skills/Exploration]], or Wilderness check, you can roll twice and take the better result.  

**Success** Extended good weather makes life pleasant and easy. Reduce Unrest by 1d4.  

**Failure** Good weather produces a short but pleasant respite. Reduce Unrest by 1.  

**Critical Failure** The good weather doesn't last long enough to help.
