# Explode two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Inventor (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Inventor]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Unstable (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Unstable]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 20

---

You intentionally take your innovation beyond normal safety limits, making it explode and damage nearby creatures without damaging the innovation... hopefully. The explosion deals 2d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save to all creatures in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you (if you’re wearing or holding the innovation) or around your innovation (if your innovation is a minion).

At 3rd level, and every level thereafter, increase your explosion’s damage by 1d6.

If you have the breakthrough innovation class feature, you can choose either a 5-foot or 10-foot emanation for the area when you use Explode; if you have the revolutionary innovation class feature, you can choose a 5-foot, 10-foot, or 15-foot emanation.
