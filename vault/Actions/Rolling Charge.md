# Rolling Charge two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 100

---

The cragstone bear tucks itself into a ball and Strides twice in a straight line. It can make a slam [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against one creature it’s adjacent to at any point during this movement. If the Strike is a critical hit, the target is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
