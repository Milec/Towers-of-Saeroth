# Blend into the Night  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Camping]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 95

---

_Nethys note: This camping activity requires that Harrim is camping with the PCs (but see [[Actions/Learn from a Companion]])._

Harrim's prayers to [[Setting/Deities/Groetus]] bring an air of gloom and solitude over the party's campsite. While this causes the PCs to experience morose and somewhat unsettling dreams, it doesn't affect the quality of rest. It does, however, help to make the campsite less attractive to predators or wandering monsters. When checking for wandering encounters during the night (including when made as part of camping), the [[Rules/Campsites|flat check DC for the encounter]] is increased by 2.
