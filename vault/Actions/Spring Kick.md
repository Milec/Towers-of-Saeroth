# Spring Kick two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 91

---

The kangaroo balances on its tail and lashes out with a powerful two-legged kick. The kangaroo makes a foot Strike. If it hits, the target is pushed back 10 feet. On a critical hit, they are also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
