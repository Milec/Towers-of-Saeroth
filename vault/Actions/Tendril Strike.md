# Tendril Strike single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 65

---

Stretching to extend its body to its limits, your eidolon attacks a foe that would normally be beyond its reach.  Your eidolon makes a melee [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike, increasing its [[Traits/Reach|reach]] by 5 feet for that Strike. If the unarmed attack has the [[Actions/Disarm|disarm]], [[Actions/Shove|shove]], or [[Actions/Trip|trip]] trait, the eidolon can use the corresponding action instead of a Strike.
