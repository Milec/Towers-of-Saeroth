# Arrest a Fall ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 418

**Trigger** You fall.

**Requirements** You have a fly Speed.

---

You attempt your choice of an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check or Reflex save to slow your fall. The DC is typically 15, but it might be higher due to air turbulence or other circumstances.

**Success** You take no damage from the fall.
