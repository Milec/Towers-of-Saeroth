# Rage single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Barbarian (Player Core 2)|Barbarian]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 72

**Requirements** You aren't [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] or raging.

---

You tap into your inner fury and begin raging. You gain a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level plus your Constitution modifier. While you are raging:

- You deal 2 additional damage on melee Strikes. This additional damage is halved if your weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack is [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]].
- You can't use actions with the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait unless they also have the rage trait. You can [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] while raging.

Rage lasts for 1 minute, until you fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], or until the encounter ends, whichever comes first. You can't voluntarily stop raging. When you stop raging, you lose any remaining temporary Hit Points from Rage, and can't gain temporary Hit Points from using the Rage action again for 1 minute.
