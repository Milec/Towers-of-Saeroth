# Resist Magic ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 40

**Trigger** You attempt a saving throw against a harmful magical effect but haven’t rolled yet.

---

Your innate magic protects you. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to the triggering saving throw. Additionally, if the triggering effect is [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.
