# Automaton Aim single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 36

---

You steady your body and observe the events of the battlefield to maximize the range of your next shot. You reduce the penalty for firing into your weapon's second [[Traits/Range|range increment]] from –2 to 0 for the next ranged attack you make this turn. You can use this action a second time in the same turn to reduce the penalty from firing into your weapon's third range increment from –4 to 0 for the next ranged attack you make this turn.
