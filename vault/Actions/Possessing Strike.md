# Possessing Strike two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 123

**Frequency** once per hour

---

Your gray bladeling attempts to inject a malevolent soul fragment into a victim to seize control of their actions. The gray bladeling makes a blade [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against a living creature. If it hits and deals damage, the target must attempt a Will saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Regardless of the result, the target is temporarily immune to Possessing Strike for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target fights off the possessing soul fragment but is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round and can’t use offensive actions against you or your gray bladeling.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but if the target takes damage from an attack or spell, the DC of the [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to recover from the confusion is 15.
