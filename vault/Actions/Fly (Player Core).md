# Fly single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 419

**Requirements** You have a fly Speed.

---

You move through the air up to your fly Speed. Moving upward (straight up or diagonally) uses the rules for moving through [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. You can move straight down 10 feet for every 5 feet of movement you spend. If you Fly to the ground, you don't take [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]]. You can use an action to Fly 0 feet to hover in place. If you're airborne at the end of your turn and didn't use a Fly action this round, you fall.
