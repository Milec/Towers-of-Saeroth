# Lizard Leap two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 77

---

The keulia Leaps and then makes a bite [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. As long as it moved at least 10 feet, its Strike deals an additional 1d6 piercing damage, or 2d6 piercing damage if your keulia is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]].
