# Dispelling Bullet two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Spellshot (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Spellshot]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 140

---

You imprint a bullet with a field of disruptive energy built upon your magical signature, disrupting the magic of whatever it hits. Make a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearm]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbow]] Strike against a foe you can see. If you hit, you attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] a spell effect active on the target (your choice, or the highest-level effect if you don’t choose). Your counteract rank is equal to half your level (rounded up), and your counteract check modifier is equal to your class DC – 10.
