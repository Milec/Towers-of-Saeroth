# Fire Breath two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 209

**Frequency** once per hour

---

The riding drake breathes a 30-foot cone of fire, dealing 1d6 fire damage for every 2 levels the drake has to all creatures in the area ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). This uses a trained DC using the drake's Constitution modifier or an expert DC if the drake is specialized.
