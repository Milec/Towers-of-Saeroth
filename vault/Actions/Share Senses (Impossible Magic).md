# Share Senses single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|Scrying]], [[Traits/Summoner (Impossible Magic)|Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 65

**Requirements** Your eidolon is manifested.

---

You project your senses into your eidolon, allowing you to perceive through them. When you do, you lose all sensory information from your own body but can sense through your eidolon's body for up to 1 minute. You can Dismiss this effect.

---

 **Special** This action has the trait matching your eidolon's tradition ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]). Your eidolon can also use this ability. When they do, they project their senses into your body.
