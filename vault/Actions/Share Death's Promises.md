# Share Death's Promises free action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 78

**Trigger** Your turn begins;

**Requirements** You are gaining a bonus to damage from Let Death Be My Weapon;

---

Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]].

You gain a status bonus to damage equal to double the bonus you're receiving from Let Death Be My Weapon that lasts for 1 minute. During this time, this status bonus doesn’t decrease or increase if the value of your [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded (Player Core)|wounded]] condition decreases or increases.
