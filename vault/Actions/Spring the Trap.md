# Spring the Trap free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 108

**Trigger** You roll initiative.

---

You can Interact to draw a [[Traits/Combination|combination]] weapon and set it to melee or ranged mode. On your first turn, your movement and ranged attacks don't trigger reactions that are normally triggered by movement or a ranged attack (such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]]).
