# Pistolero's Retort ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 109

**Trigger** A foe within the first range increment of the onehanded firearm or one-handed crossbow you’re wielding critically fails an attack roll against you.

**Requirements** You’re wielding a one-handed firearm or one-handed crossbow.

---

You punish your foe’s failure with a shot. Make a [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against the triggering foe with a one-handed firearm or one‑handed crossbow.
