# Collect Spirit Remnant three actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 23

**Requirements** You're holding your _spirit dwelling_

---

You brandish your _spirit dwelling_ at the location where a [[Traits/Haunt|haunt]], [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost]], or other [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]] was destroyed within the last minute and pray or recite ritual incantations. You coax the spirit remnant into your spirit dwelling.
