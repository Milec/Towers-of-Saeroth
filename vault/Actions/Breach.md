# Breach two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 95

---

The orca [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swims]] up to its swim Speed, then [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leaps]] vertically out of the water up to 25 feet in the air, making a Strike against a creature at any point during the jump (this lets it attack a creature within 30 feet of the water’s surface). After the Strike, the orca splashes back down into the water.
