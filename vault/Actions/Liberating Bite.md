# Liberating Bite single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 92

---

The mongoose makes a jaws Strike that can help creatures break free of restraint, like the grasp of a cobra. If the Strike hits, each creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grappled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by the target can [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as a free action.
