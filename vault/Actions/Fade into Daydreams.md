# Fade into Daydreams single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Psyche]], [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 16

---

Your flights of imagination spill into the real world, causing you to become indistinct, hazy, or cloaked in figments. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] until the start of your next turn. This concealment can't be used to [[Actions/Hide]], as normal for concealing effects that leave your location obvious.
