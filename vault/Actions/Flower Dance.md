# Flower Dance two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 123

---

The sundaflora stands tall on its hind legs and begins to wave and dance, showing off its dazzling beauty to distract your attackers. It attempts to [[Actions/Feint (Kingmaker Adventure Path)|Feint]] the target creature, using its [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] skill instead of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]]. If successful (or critically successful), the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to you, rather than to your sundaflora.
