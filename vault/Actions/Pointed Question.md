# Pointed Question single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Investigator]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 58

---

You ask a question that charms or needles someone in just the right way. Ask a question of a non-allied creature that you can see and have been conversing with. Attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check against the creature's Will DC. The creature is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.

**Critical Success** The target must directly answer your question. It doesn't have to answer truthfully, but you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to your Perception DC if the creature attempts to [[Actions/Lie]] to you.  

**Success** As critical success, but the circumstance bonus is +2.  

**Failure** The target can refuse to answer you as normal.  

**Critical Failure** The target can refuse to answer you as normal, and its attitude toward you decreases by one step due to your aggravating attention.
