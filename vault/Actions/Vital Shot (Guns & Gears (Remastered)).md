# Vital Shot two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 110

---

Your careful shot against an unsuspecting opponent pierces a vital artery or organ. Make a ranged Strike. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], the Strike deals an extra die of weapon damage, and the foe takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] equal to the amount of precision damage from your [[Actions/One Shot, One Kill (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|One Shot, One Kill]].
