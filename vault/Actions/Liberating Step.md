# Liberating Step ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 107

**Trigger** An enemy damages, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab|Grabs]], or [[Actions/Grapple|Grapples]] your ally, and both are within 15 feet of you.

---

You free an ally from restraint. If the trigger was an ally taking damage, the ally gains resistance to all damage against the triggering damage equal to 2 + your level. The ally can attempt to break free of effects [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restraining]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilizing]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzing]] them. They either attempt a new save against one such effect that allows a save, or attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] from one effect as a free action. If they can move, the ally can Step as a free action, even if they didn’t need to escape.
