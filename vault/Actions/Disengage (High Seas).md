# Disengage two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 35

**Requirements** You are piloting a sea vehicle during boarding combat.

---

You attempt to break your ship free and escape the battle. Attempt a check to pilot against a very hard DC. This is typically a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] check against the standard DC of the vessel’s level + 5 (for the very hard adjustment). Piloting checks are described [[Rules/Piloting Checks (GM Core)|here]].

**Success** The vehicle pulls free and moves up to its Speed. It can turn normally provided it moves away from the ship it disengaged from.  

**Failure** The boarding combat continues.
