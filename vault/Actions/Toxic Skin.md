# Toxic Skin ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Grippli]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 119

**Frequency** once per hour

**Trigger** A creature touches you, such as by [[Actions/Grapple|Grappling]] you, successfully hitting you with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, or using a touch-range spell against you.

---

You exude a deadly toxin. The triggering creature takes 1d4 poison damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save using your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher). At 3rd level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d4.
