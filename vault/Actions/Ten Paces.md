# Ten Paces free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 109

**Trigger** You roll initiative

---

You react to trouble with lightning speed, positioning yourself just right. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your initiative roll, and you can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to draw a one-handed firearm or one‑handed crossbow. As your first action on your first turn, you can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] up to 10 feet as a free action.
