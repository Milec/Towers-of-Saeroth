# Glimpse of Redemption ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 93

**Trigger** An enemy damages your ally, and both are in your champion’s aura

---

Your enemy hesitates under the weight of sin as visions of redemption play in their mind's eye. The enemy must choose to repent or refuse, with the following effects. If the enemy is [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|mindless]] or otherwise unable to repent, use the refuse result.

- **Repent** The ally is unharmed by the triggering damage.
- **Refuse** The ally gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to all damage against the triggering damage equal to 2 + your level. After the damaging effect is applied, the enemy becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 until the end of its next turn.
