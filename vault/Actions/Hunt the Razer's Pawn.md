# Hunt the Razer's Pawn single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Spore War Player's Guide]] pg. 13

---

Designate a single creature that you can see or hear, or whom you've spent a significant amount of time studying and reading about from afar (subject to the GM's approval), and who you know to be an agent of [[Bestiary/Treerazer (Monster Core)|Treerazer]]. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] or [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense the Motive]] of this creature. This creature takes a –1 circumstance penalty to saving throws against spells or effects you create; this penalty increases to –2 against effects that have the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait.

 You can have only one creature designated by Hunt the Razer's Pawn at a time. If you use this ability against a creature when you already have one designated, the prior creature loses the designation and the new creature gains the designation. This lasts until your next daily preparations.
