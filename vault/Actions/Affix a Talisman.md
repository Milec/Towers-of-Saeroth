# Affix a Talisman  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 565

**Requirements** You must use a [[Equipment/Repair Kit|repair kit]].

---

You spend 10 minutes affixing a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|talisman]] to an item, placing the item on a stable surface and using the repair kit with both hands. You can also use this activity to remove a talisman. Attaching more than one talisman to an item deactivates all the talismans. They must be removed and re-affixed before they can be used again.
