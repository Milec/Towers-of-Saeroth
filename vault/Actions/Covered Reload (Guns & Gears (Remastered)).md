# Covered Reload single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 109

---

You duck into a safe position or minimize your profile while reloading to make your next attack. Either [[Actions/Take Cover]] or attempt to [[Actions/Hide]], then Interact to reload. As normal, you must meet the requirements to Take Cover or Hide; you must be [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], benefiting from cover, or near a feature that allows you to Take Cover, and you need to be benefiting from cover or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to a creature to Hide from that creature.
