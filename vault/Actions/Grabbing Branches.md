# Grabbing Branches two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 42

---

Your wood elemental surrounds a foe with branches, grabbing it in place. Your wood elemental makes a branch Strike; on a success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the wood elemental until the end of your next turn unless your wood elemental moves or the target [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]].
