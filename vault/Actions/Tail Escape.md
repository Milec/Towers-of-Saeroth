# Tail Escape single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 100

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

**Requirements** The giant rock tuatara is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---

The giant rock tuatara detaches its tail to free itself from an enemy’s grip. It automatically [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] and can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]]. It then can’t use its tail slam Strike until it can regenerate its tail, which takes 10 minutes or an application of [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] magic that restores Hit Points.
