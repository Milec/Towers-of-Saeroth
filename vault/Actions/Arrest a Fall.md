# Arrest a Fall ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 472

**Trigger** You fall.

**Requirements** You have a fly Speed.

---

You attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to slow your fall. The DC is typically 15, but it might be higher due to air turbulence or other circumstances.

**Success** You fall gently, taking no damage from the fall.
