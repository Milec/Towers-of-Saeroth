# Crushing Embrace two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

---

The cephalopod wraps its tentacles around a foe and crushes them. It makes an arm [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If the Strike hits, the target creature automatically becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the cephalopod, then takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage. If your cephalopod is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the bludgeoning damage increases to 2d6.
