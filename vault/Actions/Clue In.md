# Clue In ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Investigator]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 56

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

**Trigger** Another creature attempts a check to investigate a lead you're pursuing.

---

You share information with the triggering creature. They gain a circumstance bonus to their check equal to your circumstance bonus to checks investigating your subject from Pursue a Lead. The GM can add any relevant traits to this reaction depending on the situation, such as [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] and [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] if you're conveying information verbally.
