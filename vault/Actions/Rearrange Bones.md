# Rearrange Bones single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -4|Wake the Dead #4]] pg. 28

---

([[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]]) You arrange your bones into the form of a Small or medium humanoid's skeleton. This doesn't change your normal Speeds, Strikes, or senses. If you are wearing heavy clothing or impersonating a skeleton, this counts as setting up a disguise for the Impersonate use of Deception, and grants a +1 status bonus to those Deception checks.
