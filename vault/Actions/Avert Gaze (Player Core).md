# Avert Gaze single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 419

---

You avert your gaze from danger, such as a [[Bestiary/Medusa|medusa's]] gaze. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Visual|visual]] abilities that require you to look at a creature or object, such as a medusa's petrifying gaze. Your gaze remains averted until the start of your next turn.
