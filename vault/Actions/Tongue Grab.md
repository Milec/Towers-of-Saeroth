# Tongue Grab two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 93

---

The giant frog lashes out with its tongue, making a tongue Strike. If the Strike hits, the target is automatically [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the giant frog and pulled into a square adjacent to the frog. The grabbed condition lasts until the end of your next turn.
