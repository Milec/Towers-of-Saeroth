# Strike single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 200

**Frequency** once per day

**Requirements** The _Lady's chalice_ is full of a vial's worth of _holy water_

---

The chalice splashes a spiral of _holy water_ into an adjacent space. This is a simple [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon with a range of 5 feet. Unlike an alchemical bomb, it doesn't add the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait to the attack made with it. If it hits, it deals 2d6 good damage and 2 good [[Traits/Splash|splash]] damage (including to you, if you're evil).
