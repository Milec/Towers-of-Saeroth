# Cleanse Soul Path  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 101

---

You meditate, pray, or otherwise try to reinforce your soul's connection to your armament. This takes 10 minutes. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check against your _[[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|soulforged corruption]]_. The counteract rank is half your level rounded up, and the counteract check modifier is your Religion modifier. If successful, reduce the stage of soulforged corruption by 1 (to a minimum of stage 1).
