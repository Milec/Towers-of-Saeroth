# Harvest Heartsliver ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 53

**Requirements** You're inside [[Bestiary/Kothogaz, Dance Of Disharmony|Kothogaz's]] body.

---

You attempt to steal a section of Kothogaz's heart. Attempt an attack roll or a DC 40 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]], [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] check.

**Critical Success** You steal a small disharmonic heartsliver, stow it on your person, and can exit Kothogaz's body.  

**Success** As critical success, but you can't exit.  

**Failure** You take Kothogaz's [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole]] damage.  

**Critical Failure** You take double Kothogaz's Swallow Whole damage and are [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] by its heartbeat for 1 minute.
