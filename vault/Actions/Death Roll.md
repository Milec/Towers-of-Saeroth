# Death Roll single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 145

**Requirements** The crocodile has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].

---

The crocodile tucks its legs and rolls rapidly, twisting its victim. It makes a jaws Strike with a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll against the grabbed creature. If it hits, it also knocks the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. If it misses, it releases the creature.
