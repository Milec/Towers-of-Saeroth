# Debilitating Strike free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 168

**Trigger** Your Strike hits an [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creature and deals damage.

---

You apply one of the following debilitations, which lasts until the end of your next turn.

- **Debilitation** The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds.
- **Debilitation** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]].
