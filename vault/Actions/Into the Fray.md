# Into the Fray free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 108

**Trigger** You roll initiative.

---

You know trouble can lurk around every corner, and your hands never stray far from your holsters. You can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to draw a one-handed ranged weapon and can then Interact to draw a one-handed melee weapon. As your first action on your first turn, you can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] as a free action toward an enemy you can perceive. If you can’t perceive any enemies or can’t end your movement closer to one, you can’t use this Stride.
