# Spit single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 77

---

The bacallia regurgitates a portion of its stomach’s contents as a ranged spit Strike that deals a base of 1d6 [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage with a range increment of 10 feet. The damage is 2d6 for a [[Feats/Mature Trained Companion|mature]] bacallia and 3d6 for a [[Feats/Specialized Companion|specialized]] one. A living creature hit with this Strike is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (sickened 2 on a critical hit).
