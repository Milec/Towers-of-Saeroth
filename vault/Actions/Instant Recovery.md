# Instant Recovery single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star|Pathfinder #219: Lord of the Trinity Star]] pg. 82

**Frequency** once per day;

---

Spend a [[Rules/Mythic Points|Mythic Point]]. You regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1) × your level and remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition. If you’re suffering from any recurring affliction such as poison or disease, attempt a saving throw against the affliction at mythic proficiency to see if you reduce the affliction’s stage. This saving throw cannot increase your affliction’s stage, nor does it change the affliction’s maximum duration.
