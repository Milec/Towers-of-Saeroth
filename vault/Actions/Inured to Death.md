# Inured to Death ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -5|Wake the Dead #5]] pg. 24

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** You take damage from an effect with the [[Traits/Death|death]] or [[Traits/Negative|negative]] trait

---

**Effect** You gain resistance to that damage equal to half your level (minimum 1).
