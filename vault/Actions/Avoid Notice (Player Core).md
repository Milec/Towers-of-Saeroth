# Avoid Notice  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 438

---

You attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to avoid notice while traveling at half speed. If you're Avoiding Notice at the start of an encounter, you usually roll a Stealth check instead of a Perception check both to determine your initiative and to see if the enemies notice you (based on their Perception DCs, as normal for Sneak, regardless of their initiative check results).
