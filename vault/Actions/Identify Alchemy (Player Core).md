# Identify Alchemy  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]], [[Traits/Secret]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 237

**Requirements** You are holding or wearing an [[Equipment/Alchemist's Toolkit|alchemist's toolkit]].

---

You can identify the nature of an [[Traits/Alchemical|alchemical]] item with 10 minutes of testing using your alchemist's toolkit. If your attempt is interrupted in any way, you must start over.

**Success** You identify the item and the means of activating it.  

**Failure** You fail to identify the item but can try again.  

**Critical Failure** You misidentify the item as another item of the GM's choice.
