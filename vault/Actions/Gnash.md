# Gnash single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 123

**Requirements** The severed head’s last action was a successful jaws Strike.

---

Your severed head violently shakes itself back and forth, trying to tear away a mouthful of flesh. It makes another jaws [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against the same target. If the Strike hits, it deals 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] in addition to its usual damage.
