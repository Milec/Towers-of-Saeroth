# Absorb into the Aegis ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 94

**Trigger** An enemy casts a spell that targets you or an ally within 15 feet of you

---

You interpose your shield’s ward against the spell. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting|counteract]] check, using your Religion modifier as your counteract modifier. On a success, the spell is counteracted and your shield takes damage equal to four times the spell level as it absorbs and disperses the magical energy. On a failure, the shield takes damage equal to twice the spell’s level.
