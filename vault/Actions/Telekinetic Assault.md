# Telekinetic Assault two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 38

**Frequency** once per hour

---

Your ghost unleashes a flurry of emotions, causing small objects and debris to fly about in a 20-foot emanation, dealing 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 2 levels the ghost has ([[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). This uses a trained DC using the ghost's Charisma modifier or an expert DC if the ghost is specialized.
