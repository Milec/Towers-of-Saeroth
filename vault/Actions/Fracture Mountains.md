# Fracture Mountains two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 46

---

Your spirit is so dense it takes on tangible force. Make a melee Strike with the _titan’s breaker_. This counts as two attacks when calculating your multiple attack penalty. If this Strike hits, your additional spirit damage from the ikon’s immanence increases to 4 plus an extra die of weapon damage. If you’re at least 10th level, it’s increased to 6 spirit damage and two extra dice, and if you’re at least 18th level, it’s increased to 8 spirit damage and three extra dice.
