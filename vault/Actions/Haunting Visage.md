# Haunting Visage single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 72

**Requirements** Your eidolon is condensed.

---

Your eidolon suddenly shifts itself into a terrifying shape of its choosing, rather than a simple mound, by closely controlling the positioning of its component bodies. The eidolon attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature within 15 feet; this Demoralize attempt loses the auditory trait, gains the [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait, and the eidolon doesn't take a –4 circumstance penalty to the check if they don't share a language with their target. On a success or critical success, the target is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.
