# Grab and Sting two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 145

---

The scorpion holds its foe in place with its pincers in order to sting it. It makes a pincer Strike. If the Strike hits, the target creature automatically becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the scorpion, which then makes a stinger Strike against it. The grabbed condition lasts until the end of your next turn.
