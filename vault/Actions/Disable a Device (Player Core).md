# Disable a Device two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 247

---

This action allows you to disarm a trap or another complex device. Often, a device requires numerous successes before becoming disabled, depending on its construction and complexity. A [[Equipment/Thieves' Toolkit|thieves' toolkit]] is helpful and sometimes even required to Disable a Device, as determined by the GM, and sometimes a device requires a higher proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to disable it.

 Your Thievery check result determines your progress.

**Critical Success** You disable the device, or you achieve two successes toward disabling a device requiring more than one success. You leave no trace of your tampering, and you can rearm the device later, if that type of device can be rearmed.  

**Success** You disable the device, or you achieve one success toward disabling a device that requires more than one success.  

**Critical Failure** You trigger the device.
