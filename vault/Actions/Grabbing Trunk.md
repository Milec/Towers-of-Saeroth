# Grabbing Trunk two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 73

---

The elephant grabs its foe with its trunk and carries it off. The elephant uses [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] to [[Actions/Grapple]] a creature up to two sizes smaller than the elephant in its trunk. If its target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]], the elephant repositions the target to another space in the elephant's reach.
