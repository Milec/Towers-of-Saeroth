# Mutagenic Flashback free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 73

**Frequency** once per day

---

You experience a brief resurgence of a [[Traits/Mutagen|mutagen]]. Choose one mutagen you’ve consumed since your last daily preparations. You gain the effects of that mutagen for 1 minute.
