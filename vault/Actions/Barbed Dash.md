# Barbed Dash two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

---

The pufferfish inflates and swims past an enemy, piercing it with its spines. It attempts to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] a foe. On a success, the pufferfish deals 1 piercing damage and an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] to the foe as it passes through its space. If your pufferfish is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the persistent poison increases to 2d6.
