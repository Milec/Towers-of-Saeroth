# Quick Tincture single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 57

**Cost** 1 versatile vial

**Requirements** You know the formula for the alchemical item you're creating, you are holding or wearing [[Equipment/Alchemist's Tools|alchemist's tools]], and you have a free hand.

---

You quickly brew up a short-lived tincture. You create a single [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical elixir]] or [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|tool]] of your level or lower without having to spend the normal monetary cost in alchemical reagents or needing to attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check. This item has the [[Traits/Infused|infused]] trait, but it remains potent only until the end of the current turn.
