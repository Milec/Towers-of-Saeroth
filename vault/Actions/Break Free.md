# Break Free two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 43

---

You can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] with a +2 status bonus on your check, then [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice your Speed in a straight line, and finally make a melee Strike. If you don’t need to Escape or you can’t move or choose not to, you still take the other actions listed.
