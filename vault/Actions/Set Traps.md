# Set Traps  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Camping]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 59

---

_Nethys note: This camping activity requires that Nok-Nok is camping with the PCs (but see [[Actions/Learn from a Companion]])._

Nok-Nok sets several annoying and frustrating traps around the camp's perimeter. If the campsite is attacked by enemies during the next 24 hours, the invaders suffer a –1 item penalty to all attack rolls and skill checks on the first round of combat as they navigate the distracting traps. Any enemy whose initiative result is less than Nok-Nok's [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] DC is also [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] from the traps until the start of their turn on the second round of combat.
