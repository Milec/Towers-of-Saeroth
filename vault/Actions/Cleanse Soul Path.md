# Cleanse Soul Path  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 234

---

You meditate, pray, or otherwise try to reinforce your soul's connection to your [[Rules/Binding an Armament|soulforged armament]]. This takes 10 minutes. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against your [[Rules/Corruption (Secrets of Magic)|_soulforged corruption_]]. The counteract level is half your level rounded up, and the counteract check modifier is your [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] modifier. If successful, reduce the stage of your _soulforged corruption_ by 1 (to a minimum of Stage 1).
