# Tumbling Swipe two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/YOU CAN PLAY WITH JIMOTHY NOW]]

---

Jimothy [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] up to his Speed. During this movement, he can try to move through the space of one enemy and take a small object from them. Jimothy attempts a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check against the enemy's Reflex DC as soon as he tries to enter its space.  
**Critical Success** Jimothy moves through the enemy's space and takes an object of negligible Bulk from the enemy that is worn but not closely guarded (such as a pouch). Jimothy must have a free hand to take the object. If he doesn't have enough Speed to move all the way through its space, Jimothy gets the same effect as a failure.  
**Success** As critical success, but Jimothy treats the squares in the enemy’s space as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (GM Core)|difficult terrain]] (every 5 feet costs 10 feet of movement).  
**Failure** Jimothy’s movement ends, and he triggers reactions as if he had moved out of the square he started in.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but Jimothy also then falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
