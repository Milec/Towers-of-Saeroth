# Thoughtful Reload single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 140

---

As you sink deep into a state of analytical calm and focus on the foe before you, your hands reload a bullet instinctively. Attempt a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] check against an opponent you can see and then [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to reload.
