# Raise the Walls single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

---

You [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise]] the _mirrored aegis_, which summons ethereal shields that surround you and one ally of your choice within 15 feet in a tortoise shield formation. You and the ally gain a +1 status bonus to AC, Reflex saves, and any save against a force, [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], or [[Traits/Void|void]] effect for 1 minute.
