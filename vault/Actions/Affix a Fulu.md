# Affix a Fulu single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 158

---

You affix a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|fulu]] to an armor, weapon, shield, creature, or structure that's beside or in the same square as you. A creature can remove a fulu from itself or an unattended object in its reach with a single action.
