# Snatch two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 95

---

The roc Strikes a creature with its talon and then attempts to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grab]] the creature. If the Grab is successful, the roc then [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] up to half its speed, carrying that creature along with it.
