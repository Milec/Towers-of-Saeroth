# Raconteur's Reload single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 109

Your rapid or forceful words draw the enemy's attention away from your hands long enough to chamber another bullet. Interact to reload and then attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Create a Diversion]] or an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]].
