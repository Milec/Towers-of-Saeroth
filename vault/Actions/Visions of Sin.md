# Visions of Sin two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 63

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

---

Your eidolon summons images of its sin into the mind of a target creature within 30 feet, tormenting and confusing the target. The target must attempt a Will save against your spell DC with the following effects. The effects last until the end of your next turn, but your eidolon can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to extend the effects until the end of your next turn (like Sustaining a Spell), to a maximum duration of 1 minute. If the target is evil, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its save. Regardless of whether it succeeds, the target becomes temporarily immune for 1 day.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target can't use reactions.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] and can't use reactions.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 round. The confusion can't be extended, but the other effects can.
