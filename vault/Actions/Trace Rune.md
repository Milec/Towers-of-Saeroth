# Trace Rune single action or two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Runesmith]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 44

**Requirements** You’re holding or wearing an artisan’s toolkit.

---

Your fingers dance, glowing light leaving behind the image of a rune. You apply one rune to an adjacent target matching the rune's Usage entry. You can have any number of runes traced at a time, but each rune remains only until the end of your next turn.

 If you spend 2 actions to Trace a Rune, you draw the rune in the air, causing it to appear on a target within 30 feet.
