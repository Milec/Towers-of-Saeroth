# Diving Skewer two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -209- Destroyer's Doom|Pathfinder #209: Destroyer's Doom]] pg. 81

---

The wyvern Flies up to half its Speed and then makes a stinger Strike. If the Strike hits, the target is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. If the Strike critically succeeds, the target also takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]].
