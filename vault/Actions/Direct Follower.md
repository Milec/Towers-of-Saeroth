# Direct Follower single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 76

---

You issue an order to your follower, granting them 2 actions. This action has the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] trait depending on the method of communication used, but it must have one or the other. You can Direct your Follower to perform any of the following [[Rules/Basic Actions (Player Core)|basic actions]]: [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawl]], [[Actions/Drop Prone (Player Core)|Drop Prone]], [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]], [Interact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3259), [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Actions/Release (Player Core)|Release]], [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]], [[Actions/Sense Motive (Player Core)|Sense Motive]], [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]], [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]], [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], and [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. They can only use the Strikes listed in their stat blocks. Followers that gain a burrow Speed or fly Speed can use [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]] or [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], respectively. You can also direct your follower to use [[Rules/Skill Actions (Player Core)|skill actions]] for which they meet the prerequisites. Finally, you can direct the follower to perform any actions listed in their stat block. Unlike an animal companion's support actions, these special actions don't impose restrictions on the other actions a follower can use on this turn.
