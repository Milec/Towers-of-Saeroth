# Frightening Display single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 206

---

Your ape makes a loud and frightening display, throwing a foe off balance. The ape attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the target creature; this display gains the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait and doesn't require language. While frightened by this ability, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to your ape.
