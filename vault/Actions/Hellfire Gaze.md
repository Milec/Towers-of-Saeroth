# Hellfire Gaze two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 229

**Frequency** once per minute

---

The thruneosaurus’s eyes project a line of fire in a 30- foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], dealing 1d6 fire damage for every 2 levels the thruneosaurus has to all creatures in the area ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that critically fails its save also takes [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] equal to the number of damage dice. This uses a trained DC using the thruneosaurus’s Constitution modifier or an expert DC if the beast is specialized.
