# Hair Barrage two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 95

---

The tarantula flicks its legs, flinging spiky hairs in a 15-foot cone. This deals 4d6 piercing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. This uses a trained DC using the tarantula’s Dexterity modifier or an expert DC if the tarantula is specialized. This damage increases by an additional 1d6 for every 4 levels you have beyond 6th.
