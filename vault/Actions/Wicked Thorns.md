# Wicked Thorns ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 36

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** You're hit with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike or a Strike with a non-[[Traits/Reach|reach]] melee weapon.

---

Several of your thorns break off and hook into your attacker's body. You deal 1d8 piercing damage to the triggering creature. It attempts a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] as your thorns embed in its flesh.

 At 3rd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, this damage increases by 1d8, and the persistent piercing damage increases by 1.
