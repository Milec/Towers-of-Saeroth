# Opportunistic Accusation ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Spore War Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Trigger** A [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]] you're aware of within 30 feet of you suffers from its sin vulnerability or critical fails at a saving throw against a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effect

---

You make a quick cutting remark against the demon that takes advantage of the fiend's momentary disadvantage. The demon must attempt a Will save against your class DC.

**Critical Success** The demon is unaffected and likely to focus its attention on you in combat from this point forward.  

**Success** The demon takes 2d6 mental damage.  

**Failure** The demon takes 4d6 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the off-guard condition lasts until the end of the next round. The demon also takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]].
