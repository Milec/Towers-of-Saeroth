# Defensive Stance two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Infantry]], [[Traits/Maneuver]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 581

**Requirements** [[Subsystems/Warfare Tactics/Defensive Tactics]]

---

Your army hunkers down behind its shields, presents pole arms in a wall of blades, or moves into position to protect a target allied army that is [[Rules/Conditions/Outflanked|outflanked]]. Attempt a Maneuver check against an enemy army.

**Critical Success** The target allied army is no longer outflanked by any army.  

**Success** The target allied army is no longer outflanked by the target army.  

**Critical Failure** Your defensive stance fails, and your army is now outflanked by the target enemy army.
