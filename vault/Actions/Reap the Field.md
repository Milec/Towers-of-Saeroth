# Reap the Field single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

**Requirements** Your previous action was a successful Strike with the _mortal harvest_

---

Time seems to lag as you blur across the battlefield, deciding the fate of many in a moment. [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed and make another melee Strike with the mortal harvest against a different creature. This Strike uses the same multiple attack penalty as your previous Strike, but counts toward your multiple attack penalty as normal.
