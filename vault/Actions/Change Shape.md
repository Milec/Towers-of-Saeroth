# Change Shape single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Ancestry Guide]] pg. 79

---

You change into your humanoid or hybrid shape. Each shape has a specific, persistent appearance, and most beastkin remain in their hybrid shapes by default. In hybrid shape, you appear as a mix between your ancestry and your inherent animal. While in hybrid shape, you gain a jaws unarmed Strike resembling the features of your inherent animal (fangs for bats, beaks for eagles, mandibles for wasps, and so on). Your jaws deal 1d4 piercing damage, have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits, and are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group. In your humanoid shape, you retain the appearance of your original ancestry.
