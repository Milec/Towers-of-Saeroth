# Color Swirl two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

---

Your salamander rapidly changes color in a dizzying pattern. A creature within 30 feet must attempt a Fortitude save against the higher of your class DC or spell DC. On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, or sickened 2 on a critical failure.
