# Strike, Breathe, Rend single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 45

**Requirements** Your last action this turn was a successful Strike with the _noble branch_

---

You channel a rending pulse of energy down your weapon in the moment of contact. The target of the Strike takes spirit damage equal to the _noble branch’s_ weapon damage dice. This includes any extra dice from [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] runes, but not from special abilities, [[Rules/Property Runes (GM Core)|property runes]], or the like.
