# Bony Constriction single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 77

**Requirements** The constrictor has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].

---

The skeletal constrictor deals 12 piercing damage to the grabbed creature with its sharp ribs; the creature must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If the constrictor is a specialized companion, increase this damage to 20. This uses a trained DC using the constrictor's Constitution modifier, or an expert DC if the constrictor is specialized.
