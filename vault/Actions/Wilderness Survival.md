# Wilderness Survival  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Camping]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 24

---

_Nethys note: This camping activity requires that Ekundayo is camping with the PCs (but see [[Actions/Learn from a Companion]])._

Ekundayo attempts to bolster efforts to cook campfire meals or treating illness by gathering herbs from the campsite's environs. Any attempts to [[Rules/Step 3- Eating|Cook a Meal]] or [[Actions/Treat Disease]] gain a +1 item bonus during this camping session. If the party level is 11 or higher, the bonus increases to +2
