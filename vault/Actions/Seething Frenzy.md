# Seething Frenzy single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 60

---

Your eidolon's fury boils over into a reckless, out-of-control frenzy. While frenzied, your eidolon is affected by [[Spells/Boost Eidolon|_boost eidolon_]] and gains temporary Hit Points equal to your level, but it takes a –2 penalty to AC. Your eidolon can't voluntarily end the frenzy or start another frenzy while in the frenzy. The frenzy lasts for 1 minute, after which it's fatigued for 1 minute and can't start another frenzy for 1 minute.
