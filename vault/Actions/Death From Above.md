# Death From Above single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 91

**Requirements** The flying squirrel is above the target

---

The flying squirrel crashes down onto a target, furiously clawing at it. The flying squirrel falls to an empty space next to the target, taking no damage, and makes two claw Strikes. Both attacks count toward its [[Rules/Multiple Attack Penalty (rules-2289)|multiple attack penalty]], but the penalty doesn’t increase until after both attacks have been made.
