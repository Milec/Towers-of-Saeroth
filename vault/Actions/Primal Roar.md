# Primal Roar two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Eidolon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 61

---

Your eidolon unleashes a primal roar or other such terrifying noise that fits your eidolon's form. Your eidolon attempts [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] each enemy that can hear the roar; these Demoralize attempts don't take any penalty for not sharing a language.
