# Barbed Quills ⤾  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/The Mwangi Expanse]] pg. 123

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** You are hit with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] strike or a strike with a non-[[Traits/Reach|reach]] melee weapon.

---

You break off quills in your attacker's flesh. You deal 1d8 piercing damage to the triggering creature ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save using your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher.) On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] as your quills hook into its flesh. At 3rd level, and every 2 levels thereafter, this damage increases by 1d8 and the persistent piercing damage increases by 1.
