# Interact single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 416

---

You use your hand or hands to manipulate an object or the terrain. You can grab an unattended or stored object, draw a weapon, [[Rules/Drawing and Stowing Items|swap a held item for another]], open a door, or achieve a  similar effect. On rare occasions, you might have to attempt a skill check to determine if your Interact action was successful.
