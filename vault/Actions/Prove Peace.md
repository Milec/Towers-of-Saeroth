# Prove Peace two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -157- Devil at the Dreaming Palace|Pathfinder #157: Devil at the Dreaming Palace]] pg. 84

**Requirements** You know the conflict that created a specific siege shard, and the siege shard is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]].

---

You touch the siege shard, establish a mental connection, and relay facts of the conflict's resolution. Attempt a check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] about the conflict that created the shard, using the siege shard's Will DC. If you succeed, you prove the conflict's end, and the siege shard reverts to ordinary stone. On a critical failure, images of the past conflict overwhelm you, and you are [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] until the end of your next turn.
