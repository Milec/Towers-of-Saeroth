# Step single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 418

**Requirements** Your Speed is at least 10 feet.

---

You carefully move 5 feet. Unlike most types of movement, Stepping doesn't trigger reactions, such as [[Actions/Reactive Strike]], that can be triggered by move actions or upon leaving or entering a square.

 You can't Step into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], and you can't Step using a Speed other than your land Speed.
