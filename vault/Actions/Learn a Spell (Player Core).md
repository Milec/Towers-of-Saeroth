# Learn a Spell  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 230

**Requirements** You have a spellcasting class feature, and the spell you want to learn is on your magical tradition's spell list

---

You can gain access to a new spell of your tradition from someone who knows that spell or from magical writing like a spellbook or scroll. If you can cast spells of multiple traditions, you can Learn a Spell of any of those traditions, but you must use the corresponding skill to do so. For example, if you were a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-111)|cleric]] with the [[Archetypes/Bard|bard multiclass]] archetype, you couldn't use [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] to add an [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|occult]] spell to your bardic spell repertoire.

 To learn the spell, you must do the following:

- Spend 1 hour per spell rank, during which you must remain in conversation with a person who knows the spell or have the magical writing in your possession.
- Have materials with the Price indicated in the Learning a Spell table.
- Attempt a skill check for the skill corresponding to your tradition (DC determined by the GM, often close to the DC on the Learning a Spell Table). Uncommon or rare spells have [[Rules/Adjusting Difficulty (GM Core)|higher DCs]].

**Critical Success** You expend half the materials and learn the spell.  

**Success** You expend the materials and learn the spell.  

**Failure** You fail to learn the spell but can try again after you gain a level. The materials aren't expended.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except you expend half the materials.

## Learned Spells

 A spell you learn is added to your repository of spells, such as a spellbook for a wizard, familiar for a witch, or spell list for a cleric or druid. If you have a spell repertoire, such as a bard, it's not automatically added since you can only know a limited number of spells. Instead, you can select it when you add or swap spells.
