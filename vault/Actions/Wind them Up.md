# Wind them Up two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 108

---

Should foes parry your blade or dodge your bullets? Neither— they should be watching their purse. Make a melee Strike with your [[Traits/Combination|combination]] weapon, and then attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] check with a –5 penalty to [[Actions/Steal]] from your target; you can't Steal closely guarded objects or objects that would take a long time to pilfer. You don't need a free hand to attempt to Steal something in this manner. If your Thievery check succeeds, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against your ranged attacks until the start of your next turn, and you don't trigger reactions that are normally triggered by movement or a ranged attack. These effects occur even if your target has no objects to Steal.
