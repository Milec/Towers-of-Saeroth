# Poisonous Sweep single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 92

---

The salamander secretes poison from the glands on its tail and bludgeons an enemy to apply it to them. It makes a tail Strike. If the Strike hits, the target takes an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. If your salamander is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], increase this damage to 2d6 persistent poison damage.
