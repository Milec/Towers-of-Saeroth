# Flash of Grandeur ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Champion (Player Core 2)|Champion]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 91

**Trigger** An enemy damages your ally, and both are in your champion’s aura

---

Imperious divine light flashes out from you to surround your foe. The ally gains resistance to all damage against the triggering damage equal to 2 + your level. Until the end of your next turn, the attacker is affected by [[Spells/Revealing Light|_revealing light_]].
