# Enhance Campfire  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Camping]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Companion Guide]] pg. 100

---

_Nethys note: This camping activity requires that Kanerah is camping with the PCs (but see [[Actions/Learn from a Companion]])._

Kanerah expertly controls the campfire's exact temperature, making it easier to prepare meals. All [[Rules/Step 3- Eating|cooking activities]] that take place for the remainder of the camping session gain a +1 circumstance bonus. This bonus increases to +2 if the PCs are at least 11th level.
