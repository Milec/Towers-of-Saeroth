# Borrow an Arcane Spell  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 234

---

If you're an arcane spellcaster who prepares spells, you can attempt to prepare a spell from someone else's arcane spellbook, arcane witch familiar, or the like. The GM sets the DC for the check based on the spell's rank and rarity; it's typically a bit easier than [[Actions/Learn a Spell|Learning the Spell]].

**Success** You prepare the borrowed spell as part of your normal spell preparation.  

**Failure** You fail to prepare the spell, but the spell slot remains available for you to prepare a different spell. You can't try to borrow this spell again until the next time you prepare spells.
