# Grip Throat single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 78

---

The hand attempts to [[Actions/Grapple]] a Medium or smaller creature by the throat. If the Grapple succeeds, in addition to the normal effects, the creature has difficulty speaking. While it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by the hand, it must spend an extra action to perform any action that has the verbal trait.
