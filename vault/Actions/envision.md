# envision single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 200

**Frequency** once per day

---

The chalice fills with a single vial's worth of [[Equipment/Holy Water|_holy water_]]. The water loses its potency after 1 day if poured into any container other than a font specifically sanctified by a cleric of Pharasma. It similarly loses its potency 1 day after being removed from such a font.
