# Fuel Impossible Spell single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 174

---

You put more of yourself on the line to draw forth the hidden potentials behind an impossible spell. You empower the effect of one of your active impossible spells further as detailed under the spell's description. Every time you Fuel an Impossible Spell, you become [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 or increase your doomed condition by 1 if you' re already doomed.
