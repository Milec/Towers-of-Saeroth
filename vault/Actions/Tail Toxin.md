# Tail Toxin single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Kobold]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Frequency** once per day

**Requirements** You are wielding a piercing or slashing weapon.

---

You apply your tail's venom to a piercing or slashing weapon. If your next Strike with that weapon before the end of your next turn hits and deals damage, you deal [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] equal to your level to the target.
