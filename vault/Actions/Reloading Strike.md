# Reloading Strike single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 108

**Requirements** You’re wielding a firearm or crossbow in one hand, and your other hand either wields a one-handed melee weapon or is empty.

---

You make a melee attack and then reload your gun in one fluid movement. [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] an opponent within reach with your one‑handed melee weapon (or, if your other hand is empty, with an unarmed attack), and then [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] to reload. You don’t need a free hand to reload in this way.
