# Ghost Shot single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 110

---

Make a [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Firearm|firearm]] or [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Crossbow|crossbow]] Strike. If you’re [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] from or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by the target, the Strike adds the additional precision damage from [[Actions/One Shot, One Kill (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|One Shot, One Kill]]; if you would already receive that additional damage on the [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], the effects aren’t cumulative. If you were undetected or [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed (Player Core)|unnoticed]] by any creatures, you’re now hidden from them instead, or undetected if the shot was made with a [[Equipment/Silencer (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|silencer]].
