# Retreat three actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 580

---

Your army tries to escape from the battlefield. If your army is already [[Rules/Conditions/Distant|distant]], it flees the battlefield, is no longer part of the war encounter, and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Routed|routed]]. Otherwise, your army gains the distant condition.
