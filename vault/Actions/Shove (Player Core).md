# Shove single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 235

**Requirements** You have at least one hand free. The target can't be more than one size larger than you.

---

You push a creature away from you. Attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against your target's Fortitude DC.

**Critical Success** You [[Rules/Forced Movement|push]] your target up to 10 feet away from you. You can Stride after it, but you must move the same distance and in the same direction.  

**Success** You push your target back 5 feet. You can Stride after it, but you must move the same distance and in the same direction.  

**Critical Failure** You lose your balance, fall, and land [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
