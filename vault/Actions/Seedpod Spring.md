# Seedpod Spring two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Treasure Vault]] pg. 107

---

A seedpod within the chair bursts and uncoils, releasing a light clump of cottony material. Your rootball chair [[Actions/Leap|Leaps]] up to 30 feet vertically or horizontally and drifts down, sinking 5 feet for every 5 feet traveled horizontally and causing you to take no [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]]. The cottony material disperses when you land.
