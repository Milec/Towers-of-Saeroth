# Feral Swing two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Transcendence]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 44

---

You lash out with both arms, rending all before you. Each creature in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] must succeed at a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or take spirit damage equal to your normal Strike damage with your hands of the wildling. You can choose to swing with abandon, which imposes a –2 circumstance bonus to enemies’ saving throws, but causes you to become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.
