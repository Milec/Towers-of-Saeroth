# Tumble Through single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 233

---

You Stride up to your Speed. During this movement, you can try to move through the space of one enemy. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check against the enemy's Reflex DC as soon as you try to enter its space. You can Tumble Through using [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], [[Actions/Swim]], or another action instead of Stride in the appropriate environment.

**Success** You move through the enemy's space, treating the squares in its space as difficult terrain (every 5 feet costs 10 feet of movement). If you don't have enough Speed to move all the way through its space, you get the same effect as a failure.  

**Failure** Your movement ends, and you trigger reactions as if you had moved out of the square you started in.
