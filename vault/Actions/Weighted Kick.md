# Weighted Kick two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 109

---

Your augdunar gives a heavy kick with its hind legs, bringing the weight of its burden on the target. It makes a hoof Strike. On a successful hit, the augdunar automatically [[Actions/Shove|Shoves]] the target back 5 feet (10 feet on a critical success).
