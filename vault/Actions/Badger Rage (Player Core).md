# Badger Rage single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 207

**Requirements** The badger isn’t [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] or raging.

---

The badger enters a state of pure rage that lasts for 1 minute, until there are no enemies it can perceive, or until it falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], whichever comes first. It can't voluntarily stop raging. While raging, the badger is affected in the following ways.

- It deals 4 additional damage with its bite attacks and 2 additional damage with its claw attacks.
- It takes a –1 penalty to AC.
- It can't use actions that have the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait. The animal companion can [[Actions/Seek]] even while raging.

After it has stopped raging, it can't use Badger Rage again for 1 minute.
