# Master Strike free action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Rogue]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 183

**Trigger** Your Strike hits a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature and deals damage.

---

The target attempts a Fortitude save at your class DC. It then becomes temporarily immune to your Master Strike for 1 day.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The target is paralyzed for 4 rounds, knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] for 2 hours, or killed (your choice).
