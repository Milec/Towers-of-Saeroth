# Detect Magic  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 438

---

You cast [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] at regular intervals. You move at half your travel speed or slower. You have no chance of accidentally overlooking a magic aura at a travel speed up to 300 feet per minute, but must be traveling no more than 150 feet per minute to detect magic auras before the party moves into them.
