# Quick Tincture single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Investigator (Player Core 2)|Investigator]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 103

**Cost** 1 versatile vial

**Requirements** You know the formula for the alchemical item you’re creating, you are holding or wearing an alchemist’s toolkit, and you have a free hand.

---

You quickly brew up a short-lived tincture. You create a single alchemical elixir or tool of your level or lower that's in your formula book without having to spend the normal monetary cost in alchemical raw materials or needing to attempt a Crafting check. This item has the [[Traits/Infused (Player Core 2)|infused]] trait, but it remains potent only until the end of the current turn.
