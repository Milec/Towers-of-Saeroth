# Ingest Magic two actions  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

---

The silt frog makes a jaws [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If the Strike hits, it attempts to [[Rules/Specialized Animal Companions (Player Core)|counteract]] a spell or [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] effect on the target (its counteract modifier is equal to its attack modifier with its tongue Strike, unmodified by any bonuses or effects that apply to attack rolls, and its counteract rank is equal to half its level, rounded up).
