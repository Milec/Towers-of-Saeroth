# Gnaw single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -149- Against the Scarlet Triad|Pathfinder #149: Against the Scarlet Triad]] pg. 80

**Requirements** The animal companion’s last action was a successful jaws Strike.

---

The Strike’s target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent]] bleed damage and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed until it removes the bleed damage. If the hyena is a specialized animal companion, the persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6.
