# Enamoring Exhalation two actions  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Olfactory]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 221

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

---

Your riding dragonet enraptures those nearby, exhaling a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of pheromones that fascinates those within it. Each creature in the area is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with you and your dragonet until the end of your next turn unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. This uses a trained DC using the dragonet’s Charisma modifier, or an expert DC if the dragonet is [[Rules/Specialized Animal Companions (Player Core)|specialized]].
