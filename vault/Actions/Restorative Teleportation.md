# Restorative Teleportation ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]]

**Source** [[Sources/Spore War Player's Guide]] pg. 14

**Frequency** once per minute

**Trigger** You arrive at a location via a [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] effect

---

When you reappear after the teleportation, your body takes advantage of the teleportation to put itself back together in a healthier state, and you restore a number of Hit Points equal to 1d6 plus your level plus your Intelligence modifier.
