# Raconteur's Reload single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Gunslinger (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|Gunslinger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 109

---

Your rapid or forceful words draw the enemy’s attention away from your hands long enough to chamber another bullet. Interact to reload and then attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] or an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]].
