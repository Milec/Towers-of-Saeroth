# Defensive Curl single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 55

---

The pangolin coils into a ball to defend itself. It gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC. Any creature that hits the pangolin with a melee unarmed attack while it's in its Defensive Curl take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed]] damage. The pangolin can't move, use its unarmed attacks, or take other actions that require it to use its limbs while in its Defensive Curl, but it can unfurl as a single action, ending the effects of Defensive Curl.
