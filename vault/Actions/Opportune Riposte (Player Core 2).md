# Opportune Riposte ⤾  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Bravado]], [[Traits/Swashbuckler (Player Core 2)|Swashbuckler]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 161

**Trigger** An enemy within your reach critically fails on a Strike against you.

---

You take advantage of an opening from your enemy's fumbled attack. You either make a melee Strike against the triggering enemy or attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] it of the weapon it used for the Strike.
