# Strike single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 471

---

You attack with a weapon you're wielding or with an unarmed attack, targeting one creature within your reach (for a melee attack) or within range (for a ranged attack). Roll the attack roll for the weapon or unarmed attack you are using, and compare the result to the target creature's AC to determine the effect. See [[Rules/Attack Rolls (Core Rulebook)|Attack Rolls]] and [[Rules/Damage (Core Rulebook)|Damage]] for details on calculating your attack and damage rolls.

**Critical Success** As success, but you deal [[Rules/Doubling and Halving Damage|double damage]].  

**Success** You deal damage according to the weapon or unarmed attack, including any modifiers, bonuses, and penalties you have to damage.
