# Puncture Flesh single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 109

**Trigger** Your last action was to deal piercing damage to an enemy within your reach

---

Attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the same enemy. The weapon that dealt the triggering damage gains the grapple trait for this check. On a success, the target also takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent piercing damage]] (or 2d8 persistent piercing damage on a critical success). When the target is no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by you, the persistent damage ends.
