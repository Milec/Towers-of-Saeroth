# Drench single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 41

---

Your water elemental drenches nearby fires to extinguish them. Your elemental puts out all fires in a 5-foot emanation. It extinguishes all non-magical fires automatically and attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] magical fires (its counteract modifier is equal to its attack modifier with its wave Strike, unmodified by any bonuses or effects that apply to attack rolls).
