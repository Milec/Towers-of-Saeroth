# Mesmerizing Performance single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 76

**Requirements** The companion’s last action was a successful [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Actions/Perform]].

---

The companion maintains its performance to captivate a single target within 30 feet that witnessed its successful performance. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The target is unaffected and temporarily immune for 1 hour  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] by the companion for its next action and then is temporarily immune for 1 hour.  

**Critical Failure** The target is fascinated by the companion for 1 round. While it remains fascinated, it can’t use reactions.
