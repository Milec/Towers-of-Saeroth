# Quick Alchemy single action  *Action*

**Traits** [[Traits/Alchemist]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 72

**Cost** 1 batch of infused reagents

**Requirements** You have the formula for the alchemical item you're creating, and you're either holding or wearing [[Equipment/Alchemist's Tools|alchemist's tools]].

---

You swiftly mix up a short-lived alchemical item to use at a moment's notice. You create a single [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|alchemical consumable item]] of your advanced alchemy level or lower that's in your formula book without having to spend the normal monetary cost in alchemical reagents or needing to attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check. This item has the [[Traits/Infused|infused]] trait, but it remains potent only until the start of your next turn.
