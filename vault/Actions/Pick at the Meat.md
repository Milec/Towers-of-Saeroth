# Pick at the Meat single action  *Action*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 78

**Requirements** The animal companion's last action was a successful jaws Strike.

---

Your bird picks at the wound it just created. The target of its jaws Strike takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|bleed damage]], or 2d8 bleed damage if the bird is specialized.
