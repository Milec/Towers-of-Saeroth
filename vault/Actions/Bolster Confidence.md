_Nethys note: This camping activity requires that Linzi is camping with the PCs (but see [[Actions/Learn from a Companion]])._

Linzi's enthusiasm inspires everyone around her. Checks on all other [[Traits/Camping]] activities while Linzi does so gain a +1 circumstance bonus. If Linzi is master in [[Rules/Skills/Performance]], this becomes a +2 circumstance bonus.
