# Wild Hunt Monarch

Each wild hunt follows a wild hunt monarch, who travels between the realm of the fey and the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]] in search of new quarry. These limber and regal humanoids have elk antlers, long pointed ears, flowing white hair, and luminescent green eyes.

**Brutal Gore**   A creature critically hit by a monarch's horns strike is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].

**Call Glaive  or [three-actions]** single action ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]) The wild hunt monarch wields a magic weapon known as a _monarch's glaive_. This weapon functions as a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|major striking]] [[Equipment/Thundering|greater thundering]] [[Equipment/Wounding|wounding]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Glaive|glaive]]_ in the monarch's hands only. The monarch can call their glaive to their hands using this action, transporting it across any distance. If their glaive is destroyed, the monarch can create a new one by using Call Glaive as a three-action activity that gains the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait.

**Call to the Hunt** three actions ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The wild hunt monarch blows their monarch's horn, causing two [[Bestiary/Wild Hunt Scout|wild hunt scouts]] or four [[Bestiary/Wild Hunt Archer|wild hunt archers]] part of the monarch's wild hunt link and no more than 1 mile from the monarch's location to appear as the monarch chooses at any point within 30 feet. They act immediately before the wild hunt monarch in initiative starting on the next round.

**Focus Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The wild hunt monarch fixes its glare at a creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must immediately attempt a Will save against the wild hunt monarch's wild gaze. If the creature was already [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] by the wild gaze before attempting its save, a failed save causes it to be [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round. After attempting its save, the creature is temporarily immune to Focus Gaze from any wild hunt until the start of the wild hunt monarch's next turn.

**Wild Reincarnation** ⤾ ([[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal]]) **Trigger** The wild hunt monarch kills an adjacent non-[[Traits/Fey|fey]] creature. **Effect** The wild hunt monarch attempts to reincarnate the creature as a fey. The slain creature must attempt a DC 42 Fortitude save, and it takes a –2 status penalty to this save if it was under any [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]] effect placed by the monarch when it died.  
**Critical Success** The creature is instead restored to life as if via [[Spells/Raise Dead|_raise dead_]] (heightened to 10th level), and it is permanently immune to Wild Reincarnation from that wild hunt monarch.  
**Success** The creature is not reincarnated and remains dead.  
**Failure** The creature is temporarily reincarnated as a [[Traits/Fey|fey]] creature of the monarch's choice. The new fey must be of a level equal to or less than the dead creature's level, and it functions in all ways as that fey creature; it retains none of the abilities and only scatterings of the memories or personality of the reincarnated character, and it follows the commands and orders of the wild hunt monarch. After 1 minute, the reincarnated fey dies and its body reverts back to its original corpse, which can then be restored to life normally.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the reincarnated fey remains alive until slain; a [[Spells/Miracle|_miracle_]], [[Spells/Wish|_wish_]] or similarly powerful spell or effect can reverse the reincarnation as well.
