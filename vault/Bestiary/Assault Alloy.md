# Assault Alloy

While [[Setting/Deities/Laudinmio (Divine Mysteries)|Laudinmio]], Sovereign of Alchemy, slumbers on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]], their most curious students have spread throughout [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] to conduct ever more daring alchemical experiments. Known as assault alloys, these impish elementals have grown bored and jaded of humanoids' hoarding impulses, especially in such bland uniformity. Don't they know that metal's greatest strength is in its diversity?

**Instinctive Alloy** ⤾ **Trigger** The assault alloy is hit by an attack with a metal weapon or metal spell or effect; **Effect** The physical damage from the triggering weapon, spell, or effect instead restores the assault alloy's Hit Points as they seamlessly incorporate some of the metal used into their body. If already at full Hit Points, the assault alloy gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 round instead. If a metal weapon triggers this, the weapon's die size decreases by one step to a minimum die size of d4 for 1 minute, and that weapon can't trigger Instinctive Alloy again during this time.

**Idle Transmutation**   An assault alloy has full alchemical control over the properties of their metal. Each time they make a metal needle Strike or [[Rules/Cast a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait, they choose whether the metal they use is [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]], [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]], [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]], or any other solid precious metal.

**Metal Blink** single action ([[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]) **Requirements** The assault alloy is adjacent to metal of at least 1 bulk; **Effect** An assault alloy can liquefy the metals of their body and travel up to their Speed through spaces with contiguous metal, even if it's not uniformly connected (as in a scrap heap or a pile of treasure). This movement doesn't trigger reactions.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## The Hoarder's Scourge
>
>
>
> Assault alloys are constantly in search of ever greater sources of metal to use in their experiments. Bankers, armorers, smiths, scrap dealers, and even dragons are all wary of an assault alloy settling in among their assets. They often handsomely pay any adventurers capable of rooting out the dangerous pests.
