**Golem Antimagic** harmed by [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]] (8d6, 2d6 from areas or persistent magic); healed by [[Traits/Fire|fire]] (2d6 HP); slowed by [[Traits/Cold|cold]]  
**Vulnerable to Shatter** A glass golem is affected by the [[Spells/Shatter|_shatter_]] spell as though the golem were an unattended object.  
**Spell Reflection** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]]) **Trigger** The glass golem is targeted by a spell; **Effect** The glass golem positions its magical, reflective surfaces to turn the spell back on the caster. It tries to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the spell by attempting an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check for its counteract check. If it successfully counteracts the spell, the effect is turned back on the caster.

**Damage** 2d6+13 slashing plus 1d6 bleed

**Solar Flare** two actions ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]]) The glass golem holds up its stained-glass frame and angles it downward, causing the image of the sun in the artwork to flash brightly, illuminating a 30-foot cone. This blast of light deals 8d6 fire damage to creatures in the area, plus 4d6 additional positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 30 Reflex save. If this solar flare overlaps an area of magical [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]], it attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the darkness effect (+22 counteract check).  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes half damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] permanently.
