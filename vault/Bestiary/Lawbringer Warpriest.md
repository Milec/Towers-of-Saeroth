# Lawbringer Warpriest

Mortals whose ancestry has been influenced by [[Bestiary/Families/Archon (Monster Core)|archons]] are called lawbringers. They may have mortal ancestors who fought alongside archons against the forces of evil and entropy, or they may have been born from a union between an archon and a mortal. Many lawbringers seek adventure to bringing order to the world.
