**Chupar** single action  **Requirements** The chupacabra has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]; **Effect** The chupacabra sucks blood from the grabbed creature. The chupacabra gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition for 1 minute and can use the extra action only for Strike and Stride actions. A chupacabra can't use Chupar again while it is quickened in this way. A creature that has its blood drained by a chupacabra is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 until it receives healing (of any kind or amount).

**Pounce** single action  The chupacabra Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the chupacabra began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], it remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.

**Wing Flurry** two actions ([[Traits/Move]]) The false devil's spiny wings don't afford it a typical melee option, but it can use them to scrape and slash at foes it flies by. The winged chupacabra attempts to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] an enemy's space. As it does so, it flaps its wings furiously, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to its Acrobatics check. If it succeeds, it inflicts 4d6 slashing damage to the creature it Tumbled Through (or 8d6 slashing damage on a critical success).
