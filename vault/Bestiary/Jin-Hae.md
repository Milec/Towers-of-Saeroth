**Call a Battle Echo** single action ([[Traits/Conjuration]]) **Effect** Jin-Hae pulls one of the _battle echoes_ (page 19) to her hand. She is fully aware of every _echo_ and can always pull the one she wants, but she cannot call an _echo_ that has already been unsealed.

**Link Swords** single action ([[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Stance]]) **Requirements** Jin-Hae is wielding two hook swords; **Effect** Jin-Hae either links her swords together or unlinks them. If the swords are linked, she can make linked hook sword Strikes; if the swords are unlinked, she can make hook sword Strikes.

**Wind-Fire Charge** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]]) **Requirements** Jin-Hae is wielding her wind and fire wheels; **Effect** Jin-Hae blasts off on her wind and fire wheels, leaving an elemental trail in her wake. She Strides up to twice her Speed and makes a wind and fire wheel Strike at any point during her movement. The path left by her movement is filled with her choice of an 8th-level [[Spells/Wall of Wind|_wall of wind_]] or [[Spells/Wall of Fire|_wall of fire_]] spell. Jin-Hae is unaffected by any walls she creates with this ability. She cannot use Wind-Fire Charge again for 1d4 rounds. If she uses Wind-Fire Charge again, the wall created by her previous Wind-Fire Charge disappears as soon as her action is completed.

**Wind-Fire Chariot**  ([[Traits/Divine]]) **Requirements** Jin-Hae is wielding two wind and fire wheels; **Effect** Jin-Hae's wind and fire wheels float by her feet, giving her the benefits of 7th-level [[Spells/Air Walk|_air walk_]]. While this lasts, she can wield her wind and fire wheels even while her hands are full by kicking with them, and her wind and fire wheels gain the [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 20 feet]] trait as well as the effects of a [[Equipment/Returning|_returning_]] rune.
