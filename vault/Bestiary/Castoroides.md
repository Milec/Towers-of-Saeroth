# Castoroides

Castoroides are bear-sized beavers that build their lodges on land near a water source rather than in the water.

([[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]): DC 18

**Slap** ⤾ **Trigger** The castoroides is targeted by a physical ranged Strike from an attacker it can see; **Effect** The castoroides swats at the projectile with its wide tail, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to its AC against the triggering attack.

single action
jaws +12,
**Damage** 2d6+4 piercing plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]]

**Construct Shelter** three actions  The beaver takes 1 minute to rapidly construct a simple mound along one side of its space, which can be used to [[Actions/Take Cover]].

**Penetrating Bite** two actions  The beaver makes a jaws Strike. The Strike ignores the first 5 Hardness of organic materials, such as rope, leather, and wood.

**Prop** single action  The beaver stabilizes itself with its tail, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus to its Fortitude and Reflex DCs against [[Actions/Shove]] and [[Actions/Trip]] attempts until it next takes an action with the [[Traits/Move|move]] trait.

**Gouge** two actions  The castoroides deals 2d8 piercing damage to a creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature that fails its save also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].
