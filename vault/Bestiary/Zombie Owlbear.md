# Zombie Owlbear

Zombie owlbears combine an [[Bestiary/Owlbear|owlbear's]] ferocity with mindless undead hatred. Once it draws near, it often stands up, unleashing a guttural, wet roar, before charging into combat without thought of self-preservation.
