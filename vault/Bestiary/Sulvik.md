**Independent Brains** Each of Sulvik's heads rolls its own initiative and has its own turn. Neither head can [[Actions/Delay]]. At the start of a head's turn, that head gets 2 actions and 1 reaction. Each brain controls its own tongue and one of Sulvik's arms, but both can move the legs. Any ability that would sever Sulvik's head (such as the [[Equipment/Vorpal|_vorpal_]] weapon property) doesn't cause him to die if he has his other head, but does cause him to lose the turns, actions, and reactions of the severed head. [[Traits/Mental]] effects that target a single creature affect only one of Sulvik's heads.Perception +11; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]]
