# Darvakka, Umbraex

An umbraex appears at first glance to be a massive, majestic bird, much like a [[Bestiary/Phoenix|phoenix]]. Instead of compassion and warmth, however, it seethes with cold and hate. The umbraex is among the largest of the nihilistic undead called darvakkas.

**Ashen Rise** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When an umbraex is destroyed, its body discorporates into fine ash before being restored, fully healed, 1d4 rounds later. An umbraex can't use this ability as long as its remains are within an area consecrated to a good deity or an area of [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]]. The umbraex can't use Ashen Rise again for 1 day.  
**Entropy's Shadow** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) 40 feet. Umbraex leak entropy and corruption from their very being. A living creature entering or starting its turn in the aura takes 8d6 negative damage with a DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If it fails, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute and pulled 10 feet toward the umbraex.  
**Sunlight Powerlessness** An umbraex caught in sunlight is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]].
