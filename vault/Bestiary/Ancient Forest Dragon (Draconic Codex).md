# Ancient Forest Dragon

Like the rest of their imperial cousins, the forest dragon likely originated from the elemental plane they embody, namely the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]]. That some forest dragons yet reside in the recently returned Eternal Forest supports this theory, though this only answers the location of origin, not how the dragons came to be. The Elemental Lord [[Setting/Deities/Shumunue (Divine Mysteries)|Shumunue]] might resemble a forest dragon, but she is not one herself, so the mystery of the dragons' progenitor remains unanswered to this day.

**Azure Swarm Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The dragon unleashes a swarm of insects that deals 18d6 piercing damage in a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) before dispersing. A creature that critically fails takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. The dragon can't use Azure Swarm Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Coiling Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes one claw Strike and one tail Strike in either order, each against the same target. If either Strike hits, the dragon can attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target with that limb as a free action. The dragon can have up to three creatures grabbed at a time; one in each of their two front claws, and one with their tail.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Azure Swarm Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Draining Blight** two actions ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The dragon draws moisture from the living creatures surrounding them to heal their own wounds. Each living creature in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 10d10 void damage (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). Creatures made entirely of water and plant creatures use the outcome one degree of success worse than they rolled. The dragon regains Hit Points equal to half of the highest damage a single creature takes from this effect. All non-creature plant life in the area withers and dies, eliminating non-magical undergrowth and any associated [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]].

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Greater Constrict (Monster Core)|**Greater Constrict**]]** single action  4d8+9 bludgeoning, DC 41

**Lignifying Bite**  ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) When a creature made of flesh is reduced to 0 Hit Points by the dragon's jaws Strike, that creature must attempt a DC 41 Fortitude save. If it fails, it dies, and its flesh, made of earth in a metaphysical sense, turns to wood. The wood is living and might grow into a tree over time, but it can't be used as a body or piece of a body for [[Spells/Raise Dead (Player Core)|_raise dead_]] or other magic that would return the creature to life.

**Woodland Passage**   The forest dragon [[Rules/Ignore Difficult Terrain|ignores difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain from non-magical [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plants]] and [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungi]], such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.

**Forest Shape**   When casting one with plants, a forest dragon can become a tree of the same size and age as themselves, using their own AC.
