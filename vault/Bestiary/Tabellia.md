# Tabellia (Emissary Angel)

Tabellias are the elite messengers of the celestial realms, serving deities and celestial armies by delivering messages, performing reconnaissance, and providing support for those in need of aid. They watch over planar travelers and take powerful mortals under their wings to mentor them. Tabellias carry scrolls containing important messages and other celestial secrets. Most creatures, even wicked ones, respect the strength of tabellias and allow them to travel untroubled.

 Tabellias can form spontaneously from the souls of exceptional mortals but are also sometimes created from such souls intentionally by deities or demigods. In the latter cases, tabellias often bear physical features that mark them as closely affiliated with that deity. A tabellia created by [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], for example, might have hair made of fire, while one created by [[Setting/Deities/Torag (Divine Mysteries)|Torag]] might look more dwarven, and one created by [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]] could have butterfly wings instead of feathered wings. It's not unusual for tabellias created by deities to share their creators' philosophies and interests.
