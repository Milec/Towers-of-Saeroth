**Rotting Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) 10 feet, DC 24. The zombie emits an aura of rot and disease that causes wounds to fester and turn sour. Any living creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the zombie and is not at full Hit Points takes 2d6 damage as its wounds fester. Creatures that take a critical hit from the zombie also take this damage immediately.
