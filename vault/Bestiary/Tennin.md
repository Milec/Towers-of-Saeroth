# Tennin (Brocade Angel)

Celestial artisans and elegant dancers called tennins serve as [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana (GM Core)|Nirvana's]] attendants, where they weave the gods' marvelous, seamless garments from clouds and frost and entertain the courts with skyward dances, their footsteps lighter than dew. During catastrophic wars and disasters, tennins descend from Nirvana to mend what is broken and soothe those who suffer. The  realm of Nirvana often promotes the virtuous souls of dutiful craftspeople and dedicated musicians to the ranks of the brocade angels so they might use their talents to bring beauty and kindness to a [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]] overwhelmed by war.

 It feels like there have always been too few tennins to attend to the needs of an entire world. Tragically, their numbers are dwindling in the wake of [[Setting/Deities/Gorum (Divine Mysteries)|Gorum's]] death, which has sparked and renewed numerous conflicts. Many angels have fallen or lost their way amid the discord.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Tragedies Of Two Worlds
>
>
>
> Brocade angels often become entangled in romantic relations with mortals, especially with virtuous individuals who share similarly lofty goals. These affairs rarely go well, as the endless duties of [[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]] take precedence over the joys and comforts of the heart. Still, some tennins have been known to discard their _[[Equipment/Flawless Celestial Shawl|flawless celestial shawls]]_ to stay with their lovers. Darker tales also exist of unscrupulous mortals overcome by desire or greed, who steal these shawls to force the angels to stay with or serve them.
