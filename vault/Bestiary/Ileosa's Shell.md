**Escape From Hell** ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When Ileosa's shell is reduced to 0 HP, the hollow corpse immediately melts into a pool of blood, at the center of which lies her still-beating heart. A moment later, a crimson ghostly shape—Queen Ileosa's soul—rises from the heart. After the soul shrieks in relief at escaping from [[Setting/Planes/Hell]], it rises through the ceiling and vanishes while the heart dies and rots. All creatures within 30 feet of the shell when this occurs must attempt a DC 30 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** A living creature becomes invigorated and elated by the sense of freedom from Hell, and becomes [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 minute. The creature can use the extra action each round only for Strike and Stride actions. An [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature gains no benefit.  
 **Success** As success, but the creature can use the extra action only for Stride actions.  
 **Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  
 **Critical Failure** The creature becomes frightened 2.
