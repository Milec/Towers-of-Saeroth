**Crystal Exhalation** two actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden breathes, creating a storm of ice crystals. All creatures in a 60-foot cone take 4d8 cold damage and 4d8 slashing damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] by ice. The warden then can't use Crystal Exhalation for 1d4 rounds.

**Orogenesis Dive** three actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden flies 240 feet upward in a straight line. As she does so, the ground in a 30-foot burst from her starting point rises into a 120-foot-high rocky pillar topped with a mountainous peak that becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|greater difficult terrain]]. All other creatures in that burst must succeed at a DC 44 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] by the sudden altitude shift. The warden then flies straight downward to the center of the pillar's peak, driving her horns into the stone. As she lands, the newly formed mountains tremble. All other creatures in the area must succeed at a DC 42 Reflex save or plummet off the peaks, [[Rules/Falling|falling]] 120 feet and landing in the nearest square outside the area. The pillar is permanent.

**Song of the Skies** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden trills a cheerful birdsong, granting a boon to all allies within 60 feet and evoking the freedom of flight. For the next minute, affected allies gain [[Spells/Unfettered Movement|_unfettered movement_]], a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly speed]] of 60 feet, and treat creatures on the ground as [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] when they are airborne.

**Tempest Downdrafts** free action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Trigger** The warden [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]]; **Effects** The warden's flapping wings generate powerful gusts that blow smaller creatures off course. A 60-foot emanation, centered on the warden, fills with lightning-pierced winds until the beginning of the warden's next turn. Creatures must succeed at a DC 42 Reflex save to take any [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action within the winds; flying creatures use the outcome one degree of success worse than what they roll. Creatures who critically fail are [[Rules/Forced Movement|blown]] 30 feet away from the warden, knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and take 4d6 electricity damage.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Warden's Blessing
>
>
>
> Legend says that the Warden of Peaks and Sky invites those who particularly earn their favor into their home, a nest within a hollow peak deep in the Kodar Mountains. There, the guest is treated to a grand feast, after which the warden offers to turn them into a [[Bestiary/Thunderbird|thunderbird]] so they might enjoy the sky for the rest of their days, though they are not offended if anyone demurs.
