# Magma Archdragon

Magma dragons represent a brash and volatile aspect of dragonkind. Territorial and unpredictable, magma dragons are known for protecting their hunting grounds with a fierce and proud demeanor that brooks no challengers. Passionate in the extreme, magma dragons in their draconic forms can often seem utterly bestial, impulsively jumping at threats and challenges with a wild abandon, but this belies an intellect that many underestimate.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one horn Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Pyroclastic Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Magma Burrow**   A magma dragon's burrow Speed functions only when the dragon is [[Rules/Burrowing]] through magma or molten lava

**Magma Tomb** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The dragon spits a molten boulder at a target within 120 feet. This deals 14d6 fire damage and 6d12 bludgeoning damage, with a DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. If the creature fails its save, it's encased in magma that instantly cools and has [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 12, HP 50, and BT 25. The encased creature can't [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|breathe]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 44).

**Volcanic Purge** single action  If the next action the dragon uses is Pyroclastic Breath, the magma clings to those it damages. Each creature that fails its save against Pyroclastic Breath takes 6d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]], and as long as it has this persistent fire damage, it also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds.
