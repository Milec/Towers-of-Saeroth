# Nasurgeth (Nightwave)

Nasurgeths are hungry voids with glowing eyestalks and thousands of teeth. They lurk deep beneath the waves where the sunlight doesn't reach. At night, they ascend to the skies and rain destruction and ruin down on all the living.

**Midnight Depths** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) 60 feet. A nasurgeth's entropy grows even stronger underwater. All water within the aura is completely dark (as 4th-level [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]]). Magical [[Traits/Light|light]] with a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] level of 4th level or lower, along with magical light cantrips, are suppressed. A living creature entering or starting its turn in the aura takes 4d6 negative damage, and the creature also takes an additional 2d10 cold damage if it's in water (DC 39 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). If it fails, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute and [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet toward the nasurgeth.  
**Sunlight Powerlessness** A nasurgeth caught in sunlight is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]].  
**Spray Black Bile** ⤾ **Trigger** The nasurgeth takes slashing or piercing damage from a critical hit, or a swallowed creature cuts itself free; **Effect** Darkness and negative energy spill out from the nasurgeth's wound, dealing 8d8 negative damage to creatures within 20 feet (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude).

**Broken Barb** single action  **Requirements** A creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] in the nasurgeth's jaws; **Effect** The nasurgeth breaks a tooth off in the target, who takes 3d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and is no longer grabbed or restrained. If the target is adjacent to a surface, the tooth also pins it in place, making it [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] ([[Actions/Escape]] DC 45).

**Ravenous Void** three actions  The nasurgeth barrels forward with their mouth open, [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] twice in a straight line and moving through the spaces of Huge or smaller creatures. The nasurgeth deals the damage of their jaws Strike to each creature whose space they enter (DC 45 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Any creature that critically fails is automatically Swallowed Whole.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole]]** single action  Huge, 2d10+9 bludgeoning, Rupture 40. A living creature that ends its turn swallowed whole by a nasurgeth becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] or increases its drained condition by 1, and the nasurgeth gains 20 temporary Hit Points. A creature whose drained condition increases to 5 in this way dies.
