**Electrified Rebirth** ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per year; **Effect** When Agyra dies, her corpse crackles and sparks with electrical energy. Any creature that touches Agyra's body within 1 hour of her death takes 11d6 electricity damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] DC 43 Reflex save). After being dead for 1 minute, Agyra is immediately resurrected in the same place, at full health, with all abilities fully restored. She normally takes advantage of this rebirth to flee to safety, using Thunderous Departure to retreat. If Agyra has already died and been reborn in the same year, she remains permanently dead upon being slain a second time.  
**[[Rules/Basic Mythic Abilities|Mythic Immunity]]** Strikes  
**Mythic Resilience** Agyra treats her saving throws with Fortitude and Reflex saves as one step better than they actually are (so a critical failure is a failure, a failure is a success, and a success is a critical success). Whenever a character rolls a critical hit against Agyra, they must reroll the attack roll and take the new result.

**Mythic Power**   3 Mythic Points

- _Recharge Ability_ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Agyra gains an additional use of lightning breath.
- _Remove a Condition_ ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Agyra removes any one condition currently affecting her.

**Blinding Flash** three actions ([[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** By spreading her wings while she stands upon the ground, Agyra can create a blinding flash of light that targets all creatures within 100 feet. Affected creatures must succeed at a DC 43 Fortitude check.  
 **Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
 **Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1d4 rounds.  
 **Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] and stupefied 2 for 1 minute.  
 **Critical Failure** The target is blinded and stupefied 2 for 10 minutes.

**Conjure Hurricane** three actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Agyra generates a hurricane that spreads across a 4-mile radius and lasts 24 hours, with a 500-foot calm eye of the storm at its center. Agyra is at the center of this hurricane when it is formed, but it moves normally (according to surrounding weather conditions and the GM's discretion) afterward.

**Lightning Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) Agyra exhales a line of electricity from each of her two heads. Each line is 120 feet long and 10 feet wide, and the two lines may be pointed in different directions. A creature in a line takes 22d6 electricity damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] DC 44 Reflex save). On a failed save, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for 1d4 rounds. A creature can be affected only once by a line, even if they are in an overlapping area. A character slain by Agyra's Lightning Breath remains electrified for 2d4 rounds after death. A creature touching such a corpse takes 3d6 electricity damage (no save). Agyra can't use Lightning Breath for 1d4 rounds.

**Stormflight**   Agyra can move in strong winds with ease. She doesn't treat wind as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] or need to [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight (Player Core)|Maneuver in Flight]] in high winds.

**Thunderous Departure** three actions ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Agyra Flies in a straight line at incredible speed, moving at least 120 feet but up to 1 mile. This movement doesn't provoke reactions. Her departure leaves behind a thunderous sonic boom, creating a 100-foot burst centered on her point of departure. All creatures in the area take 15d10 sonic damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] DC 46 Fortitude save). A creature who fails is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and a creature who critically fails is knocked prone and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] permanently.
