**Fishhooked**   While it has [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] from the fisher's fishing line Strike, a creature has a fishhook embedded in it. The creature can't move farther away from the fisher (though it can move laterally). The fisher can reel the creature in as a single action with the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] and [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait, attempting an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the creature's Fortitude DC. On a success, the creature takes 2d4 slashing damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet closer to the fisher (double the damage and distance on a critical success).
