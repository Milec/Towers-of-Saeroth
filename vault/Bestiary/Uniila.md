**Every Dagger** two actions  **Requirements** The uniila wields four daggers; **Effect** The uniila attempts a single dagger Strike against a target. If this Strike hits, the uniila deals three extra dice of weapon damage.

**Fearsome Magic** single action ([[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Metamagic]]) **Frequency** Once per hour; **Effect** If the uniila's next action is to cast a harmful spell, creatures who fail their saving throw against the spell are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure), in addition to the spell's other effects.

**Witch Token**  ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Conjuration]]) (1 hour) **Frequency** Once per day; **Effect** The uniila creates a token in the shape of a piece of jewelry. When the _witch token_ is [[Traits/Invested|invested]] by a mortal, all of the mortal's spells, except those cast at the highest level the mortal can cast, count as 1 level higher for the purpose of [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracting]] other spells and being counteracted. The creature can also Activate the _witch token_ to Cast a Spell once per day. This spell is chosen from one of the uniila's arcane spontaneous spells of level 4 or lower when the uniila creates the token. While a creature has the _witch token_ invested, it treats its saving throws against the uniila's spells as one degree of success worse, and the uniila always knows the distance and direction to the creature. A creature can't have more than one _witch token_ invested, and an uniila can't have more than one _witch token_ in existence. The uniila can destroy their _witch token_ as a free action with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait; if the _witch token_ is invested when the uniila does so, the creature that has the witch token invested takes 9d8 mental damage (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save).

> ## Uniila Occultists
>

>

These more subtle uniilas cast occult spells instead of arcane spells; they cast [[Spells/Augury|_augury_]] instead of [[Spells/Glitterdust|_glitterdust_]], [[Spells/Mind Reading|_mind reading_]] instead of [[Spells/Lightning Bolt|_lightning bolt_]], [[Spells/Modify Memory|_modify memory_]] instead of [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]], and [[Spells/Read Aura|_read aura_]] instead of [[Spells/Ray of Frost|_ray of frost_]].
