**Deadly Mandibles** ⤾  **Trigger** The giant mantis Grabs a creature with its leg. **Effect** The mantis pulls the creature adjacent to it, then makes a mandibles Strike against the creature.

**Lunging Strike** two actions  The giant mantis lunges forward, making a leg Strike with an extended reach of 20 feet.

**Sudden Strike**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven’t acted are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to the giant mantis.
