**Closing Volley** three actions  The Tiger Lord makes a ranged Strike with a hatchet, then Strides up to twice its speed. If it ends its movement within melee reach of the target of its ranged Strike, it may make a melee Strike against that enemy. If the ranged Strike hit the target, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the melee Strike.

**Vengeful Rage** ⤾  **Requirements** The Tiger Lord is not fatigued or already in a rage; **Trigger** The Tiger Lord suffers damage that reduces its Hit Points to 100 or less. Effect The Tiger Lord flies into a rage that lasts 1 minute. While raging, the Tiger Lord gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, gains a +2 status bonus to damage rolls, and takes a –2 status penalty to AC. After the rage, the Tiger Lord is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].
