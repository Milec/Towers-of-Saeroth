# Elder Outcrop

An outcrop of dense stone that spends ages eroding can gain sapience and slowly awaken with the wisdom of nature. Such elemental beings are slow to action and difficult to anger, spurred only by immediate threats.

**Dust Eternal** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]]) 30 feet. Dust swirls about the outcrop, rendering everything within its aura (including the outcrop) [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]. In addition, the dust at the outcrop's base creates [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] on the ground within the aura. The outcrop can activate or deactivate this aura using a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.  
**Unstoppable** The elder outcrop's [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] condition can't exceed slowed 1, and it ignores penalties to its Speeds and the [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] condition.

**Call of Earth**  ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) A flying creature hit by the elder outcrop's rock ranged strike is affected by a 7th-rank [[Spells/Earthbind|_earthbind_]] spell (DC 33).

**Earth Glide**   The elder outcrop can [[Actions/Burrow]] through any earthen matter, including rock. When it does so, the elder outcrop moves at its full burrow Speed, leaving no tunnels or signs of its passing.

**Natural Formation** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) Until the next time it acts, the elder outcrop appears to be a natural terrain feature. It has an automatic result of 47 on [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks and DCs to pass as a natural outcropping of rock. The outcrop's dust eternal aura is deactivated while Natural Formation is being used.

**Stone Grip** two actions ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) Great fingers of stone arise to grasp up to two Medium or smaller creatures on the ground in the outcrop's aura. Each target must succeed at a DC 33 Reflex save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the stone hand (or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] on a critical failure; [[Actions/Escape]] DC 33). A creature grabbed or restrained by a stone hand at the end of its turn becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] or increases its existing slowed condition by 1. When a creature is unable to act due to the slowed condition from this effect, the creature is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]].

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Throw Rock]]** single action  

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Wisdom of Stone
>
>
>
> Elder outcrops learn a lesson for every grain of rock eroded away, every squall and freeze that has shaped their craggy bodies. As wizened advisors and tutors, they have the respect and affection of other elementals, as well as from natural creatures. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|Druids]] of the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Stone|stone order]] revere the wisdom of elder outcrops, always happy to receive their wide perspective, literal and philosophical.
