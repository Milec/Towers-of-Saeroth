# Gamekeeper

Gamekeepers know every beast that walks, slithers, flies, or swims in their territory and where to find them; try to keep up with the pack if you think you can. The land has chosen these people as guardians, giving them mysterious powers while in their territory. They are prepared to keep balance.

**Sic 'Em!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Trigger** An [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] within 60 feet of the gamekeeper is killed; **Effect** The gamekeeper stokes the ire of the wild. Until the end of the gamekeeper's next turn, they and all animals in a 60-foot emanation gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and a +2 status bonus to damage rolls.  
**Keeper's Revenge** ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) When the gamekeeper dies, all creatures in a 60-foot emanation that have damaged the gamekeeper in the last minute must succeed a DC 24 Will saving throw or be cursed. All [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] the cursed creature encounters have an initial attitude toward them that is one step worse. This curse can be removed only by an effect that specifically targets curses.
