On a success, it fires the creature up to 40 feet in height and up to 120 feet away. The creature takes 4d12 bludgeoning damage plus the appropriate [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]]. If the flung creature lands on another creature, the creature it lands on takes the same amount of bludgeoning damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a successful Launch, the animated trebuchet must Interact to reposition its arm into the proper position before it can Launch again.
