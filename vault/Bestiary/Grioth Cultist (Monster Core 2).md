# Grioth Cultist

Grioth cultists represent the lowest tier of their remorseless cult— worshippers of the Outer Gods and Great Old Ones tasked with leading scouting missions into new worlds. While they're ranked above scouts in the chain of command, they occupy a lowly place in the overall religious hierarchy. Though whispered rumors describe the legendary rituals needed to wrench worlds from their suns, these cultists don't learn this potent magic. Such secrets are left to grioth high priests, who are always the last to arrive on a colonized world.

Most grioths worship the Outer God [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (Haunter in the Dark) (Monster Core 2)|Nyarlathotep]] in one of his many incarnations, although some worship other entities such as [[Setting/Deities/Azathoth (Divine Mysteries)|Azathoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Nhimbaloth (Divine Mysteries)|Nhimbaloth]], or [[Setting/Deities/Yog-Sothoth (Divine Mysteries)|Yog-Sothoth]]. In the rare cases where a grioth is cut off from their society and given the opportunity to explore other religions, they still tend to choose faiths associated with the stars or the night, such as [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]] or [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]]. These grioths are loathed by others of their own kind, who view them as dangerous heretics.
