# Kholo Bonekeeper

Some kholos serve as conduits to the divine and are responsible for placating spirits and gods alike. [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]] is a favored deity of kholo bonekeepers, as many kholos believe their kind were birthed from the Mother of Monsters. While some kholos embrace the Old Mother completely, others see her as a nightmarish power only to be approached if they're willing to pay the price. Other popular kholo deities include [[Setting/Deities/Calistria (Divine Mysteries)|Calistria]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Mahathallah]], or [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn (Divine Mysteries)|Shelyn]].
