# Master Of Disguise

A master of disguise uses costuming, makeup, and minor illusions to deceive. Some conceal their identity for years, infiltrating organizations under deep cover.

**Double Take**   If the master of disguise and the creature they're [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonating]] are in each others' presence, the genuine creature must [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] if they're vouching for their own identity, and are treated as though they were Impersonating themself if someone [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeks]] in an attempt to pierce their disguise. The genuine creature can use their [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] modifier, [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] modifier, or a +15 modifier, whichever is highest.

**Impeccable Disguise** three actions  The master of disguise creates a disguise and [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonates]]. They gain a +5 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to Impersonate or to tell a [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] that helps them maintain their disguise. When a spell or magical effect tries to read their mind, detect whether they're lying, or reveal their identity, they can attempt a Deception check against the spell or effect's DC. If they succeed, the effect reveals information appropriate to their cover identity or nothing (the GM determines which).

**Shocking Reveal** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) The master of disguise removes their disguise with a dramatic gesture. Any creatures that previously failed to see through the disguise is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the master of disguise until the end of the turn.

**Sneak Attack**   The master of disguise deals an additional 3d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.

> ## Know Your Enemy

>
>
> A master of disguise presents an opportunity for GMs to make past events relevant again. An NPC who was previously significant may well have been a master of disguise who is now revealing themself to the PCs years later, opening a web of connections the PCs never knew existed until now.
