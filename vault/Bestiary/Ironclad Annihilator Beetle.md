**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Constrict]]** single action  4d6+20 bludgeoning, DC 44

**Earthquake Stomp** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The ironclad annihilator beetle stomps the ground. All creatures within a 30-foot emanation must succeed at a DC 44 Fortitude saving throw or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (stunned for 1 round on a critical failure).

**Oviposit** two actions  **Requirements** The ironclad annihilator beetle has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** The ironclad annihilator injects its ovipositor under the grabbed creature's skin. The creature takes 4d6+20 bludgeoning damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, the beetle implants an egg, and the target creature takes 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] that is unusually tenacious. The DC to stop the bleeding using [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] is 40, and healing the creature to full Hit Points doesn't automatically stop the bleeding. If the target creature dies from this bleed damage, its body is destroyed and unrecoverable except with powerful magic such as [[Spells/Miracle|_miracle_]] or [[Spells/Wish|_wish_]], and an immature ironclad annihilator beetle (with statistics as a [[Bestiary/Giant Stag Beetle|giant stag beetle]] bursts forth.

**Sunder Objects**   When an ironclad annihilator damages an item or structure, it deals an additional 2d10 damage to that item or structure.
