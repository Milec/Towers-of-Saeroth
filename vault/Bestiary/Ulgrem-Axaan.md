# Ulgrem-Axaan

Whatever Ulgrem-Axaan looked like in life, undeath has revealed a rotting, crocodilian skeleton encased in a jagged hide of ice crystals. Steam rises from the creature's mouth and back as its icy skin melts in the sun or from the heat of the electricity it cycles through its body.
