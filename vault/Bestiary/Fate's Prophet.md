**Rejection Vulnerability** As succubi are beings of pure lust, creatures that reject their lust can metaphysically harm them. When a succubus fails a [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check to Embrace or [[Actions/Request]], or when a creature succeeds at its save against a succubus's [[Traits/Mental|mental]] spell or ability, the succubus takes 4d6 mental damage. For one hour after causing mental damage to a succubus in this way, a creature can deal 4d6 mental damage to the succubus with a successful [[Actions/Demoralize]] incorporating its rejection.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The succubus can take on the appearance of any Small or Medium [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]]. **Grab Attention** single action ([[Traits/Attack]]) Fate's Prophet attempts to [[Actions/Grapple]] a creature using [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] instead of [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]]. If the creature is willing, she grapples it automatically.

**Passionate Kiss** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]) **Frequency** Once per round. **Effect** The succubus engages a creature they have grabbed in an embrace or other act of passion to drain its vital essence. The kiss makes the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] or increases its drained condition by 1, to a maximum of 4. The creature takes 5d6 negative damage and the succubus regains Hit Points equal to the damage dealt. The target must succeed at a DC 34 Will save or be affected by a [[Spells/Suggestion|_suggestion_]] to submit to more actions of passion rather than trying to [[Actions/Escape]].
