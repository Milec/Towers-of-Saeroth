**Mask of Power** A doprillu's unique wooden mask is the source of its power. A doprillu deprived of its mask loses its regeneration and its immunity to enfeebled and slowed, and it immediately becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]]. The enfeebled value increases by 1 at the start of each of the doprillu's turns, to a maximum of enfeebled 4. If the mask is put back on, the doprillu immediately regains its abilities and loses the enfeebled condition. A creature can pull off the mask with a successful DC 34 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Force Open]].  
**Volcanic Veins** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) Fiery magma runs through the doprillu's veins. A creature that starts its turn [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the doprillu takes 7d6 fire damage.  
**Deflect Arrow** ⤾ **Trigger** The doprillu is the target of a physical ranged attack; **Requirements** The doprillu is aware of the attack, isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against it, and has a hand free; **Effect** The doprillu gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its AC against the triggering attack.

**Body Strike** single action  **Requirements** The doprillu has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** The doprillu swings the grabbed creature as a weapon. This Strike has a +28 attack modifier and deals 3d8+16 bludgeoning damage. The Strike is [[Traits/Magical|magical]] and has a [[Traits/Reach|reach]] of 10 feet. On a hit, the grabbed creature takes half the damage dealt to the target.

**Whirlwind Throw** two actions  **Requirements** The doprillu has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** The doprillu whirls the grabbed creature about, making a Body Strike against each creature in reach. After that Strike, the doprillu can hurl the grabbed creature up to 50 feet as a ranged Strike. This Strike has the same attack modifier and damage as Body Strike, but has the [[Traits/Thrown|thrown 20 feet]] weapon trait.
