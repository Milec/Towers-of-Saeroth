# Iffdahsil

The slithering horror known as Iffdahsil is an undead [[Bestiary/Shoggoth|shoggoth]] that has haunted the vault of the Black Desert for millennia. [[Bestiary/Families/Drow]] legends in particular tend to feature Iffdahsil, terrifying the elves from a young age.

**Aura of Annihilation** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) 60 feet, DC 45. A creature entering or starting its turn in the aura that fails at a Fortitude save becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. If the target was already doomed or drained, the values of these conditions increase by 1, to a maximum of doomed 3 and drained 3. A creature that dies while doomed 3 and drained 3 in this way disintegrates into black sand.  
**Unkillable** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) Unlike most undead, Iffdahsil isn't destroyed at 0 HP. Iffdahsil's fast healing continues to function normally, though on any round it begins its turn at 0 HP, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 4]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] until the start of its next turn. While Iffdahsil is at 0 HP, it can be killed permanently only by simultaneously exposing it to direct sunlight (or a 10th-level [[Spells/Sunburst|_sunburst_]] spell) and casting a [[Spells/Miracle|_miracle_]] or [[Spells/Wish|_wish_]] spell (or similar magic) directed at destroying it.
