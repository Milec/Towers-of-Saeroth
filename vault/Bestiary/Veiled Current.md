# Veiled Current

Often considered cousins of [[Bestiary/Invisible Stalker|phades]], veiled currents are loose veils of wind who catch prey within their folds.

**Naturally Invisible** The veiled current is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] at all times. When they take a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] of any kind, the veiled current is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] as the vague outline of their humanoid form becomes faintly visible until the start of their next turn.

**Envelop** two actions  **Requirements** The veiled current begins their turn with a target of their size or smaller [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** The veiled current maintains the Grab and coalesces around the creature, stretching themself into a semisolid veil that smothers the creature within. This thereafter has the same effect as if the veiled current had [[Bestiary/Abilities/Engulf|Engulfed]] the creature (DC 26, 1d8+8 bludgeoning, [[Actions/Escape]] DC 26, Rupture 17). As engulfing a creature is a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]], the veiled current is [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] every round it has a creature engulfed.
