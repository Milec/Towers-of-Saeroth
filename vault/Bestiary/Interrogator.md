**Blood and Fear** two actions  The interrogator Strikes with a slashing melee weapon. If they hit and deal damage, the target takes an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] (or 2d4 persistent bleed damage and frightened 2 on a critical hit). Each of the interrogator's other enemies in a 30-foot emanation around the target that witnesses the bloodshed must succeed at a DC 19 Will save or be frightened 1. The frightened part of this ability is an [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effect.

**Hobble** single action  **Requirements** A creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by the interrogator; **Effect** One creature grabbed or restrained by the interrogator takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage with a DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If the creature fails its save, it also gains a condition of the interrogator's choice: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 2]] for 1 minute, [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] for 1 minute, or [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]].

**Torment**   The interrogator's Strikes deal an additional 1d8 mental damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] creatures.
