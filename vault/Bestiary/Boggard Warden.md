**Nature's Edge**   Boggard wardens are trained to take advantage of their foes' weak points when they're on unfavorable terrain. Enemies are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to the boggard warden if they're in natural difficult terrain.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   A boggard warden deals 2d6 extra precision damage to flat-footed creatures.

**Swamp Stride**   A boggard warrior ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.

**Terrifying Croak** single action ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]) The boggard warrior unleashes a terrifying croak. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 1 unless they succeed at a DC 28 Will save; those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Tongue Grab**   If the boggard warrior hits a creature with its tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the boggard. Unlike with a normal grab, the creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the boggard's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 24 and dealing at least 10 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents it from using its tongue Strike until it regrows its tongue, which takes a week.
