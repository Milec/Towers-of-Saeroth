**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]]** ⤾  
**Hydration** An azarketi must regularly submerge themself in water to rehydrate their water-acclimated skin. After the first 24 hours outside of water, they take a –1 status penalty to Fortitude saves as their skin cracks and their gills become painful. After 48 hours, they struggle to breathe air and begin to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocate]] until returned to water.
