# Cloud Giant

The graceful and regal cloud giants are fractious, with roughly half their clans believing they should rule over humanoids regardless of their size and the rest shying away from any contact with outsiders. Due to the physical and ideological distance between clans, most rely on [[Bestiary/Giant Eagle (Monster Core)|giant eagle]] or [[Bestiary/Roc|roc]] messengers, to arrange marriages and exchange art without setting foot in each other's territory.

 Cloud giants' skin color ranges from milky white to powdery blue. They make their homes anywhere veiled by the clouds, generally mountaintops or isolated valleys, but occasionally fog-shrouded swamps or misty rainforests. Legends persist of floating cities ruled by magically gifted cloud giant queens and kings. While most cloud giants plainly state that such claims are pure fantasy, others are mysteriously tight-lipped or evasive about the matter.

**Crushing Cloud** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The cloud giant solidifies some clouds, including fog or mist, around a creature that's already in a cloud up to 120 feet away. **Wind Strike** two actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The cloud giant Strikes a creature with their ranseur, surrounded in a roar of rushing air. On a hit, the target takes an additional 4d8 bludgeoning damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 minute. Whether or not the Strike hits, each non-cloud giant within a 20-foot emanation, including the target of the Strike, is buffeted by roaring winds and must attempt a DC 30 Fortitude saving throw.
