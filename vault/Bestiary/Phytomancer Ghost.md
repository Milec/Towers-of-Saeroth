**Frightful Moan** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The ghost laments its fate, forcing each living creature within 30 feet to attempt a DC 33 Will save. On a success, a creature is temporarily immune to this ghost's Frightful Moan for 1 minute.

**Memory of Leaves** single action ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]) The ghost mourns its verdant birthplace, creating a swirling cloud of phantom leaves as a 20-foot emanation until the end of its next turn. All creatures within the emanation become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the emanation become concealed to creatures within it. Creatures with the [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] traits ignore this concealment. The ghost can't use Memory of Leaves again for 1d4 rounds.
