# Shinigami

Even immortals such as [[Bestiary/Families/Kami|kami]] aren't immune to death. When a kami or equally powerful entity refuses the call of the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]], a shinigami is summoned to bring these stubborn souls to heel. Exact and swift, shinigami are known to dispense the judgment of death efficiently and dispassionately.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The shinigami takes on the appearance of a specific Small or Medium [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] creature. This doesn't change their statistics, other than to remove the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait from their Strikes. The shinigami's human shape is unique to the shinigami and can't be used to [[Actions/Impersonate]] a creature.

**Pouch of Four Gems**   Each shinigami carries a pouch containing four black sapphires for use with [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]]. Each gem is capable of holding a soul of a maximum level equal to that of the shinigami. If the shinigami dies, the gems are destroyed as well, and any souls they contain are released harmlessly to travel to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]].

**Release Soul** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Requirements** One of the gems in the shinigami's pouch contains a soul from bind soul; **Effect** The shinigami crushes the gem, sending the soul to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] while releasing a wave of necromantic energy. All creatures within a 30-foot emanation take 18d6 damage (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save); the shinigami chooses whether this damage is positive damage or negative damage. A creature who takes damage from this effect and fails the saving throw is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (or sickened 4 and drained 2 on a critical failure).

**Spirit Touch**   A shinigami's Strikes can affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures with the effect of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Guardians of the Cycle
>
>
>
> Shinigami are responsible for investigating and dealing with widespread phenomena that disrupt the natural flow of life. They might be tasked with solving the issue or be told to wait, observe, and provide minimal assistance. While most shinigami can retain their impartiality during such long vigils, some are far from emotionless and end up wavering toward mercy or cruelty
