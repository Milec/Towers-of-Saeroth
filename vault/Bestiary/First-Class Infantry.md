**Speed** 20 feet; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Bolt Salvo** three actions  The first-class infantry draws, loads, and shoots a salvo from their crossbows. The salvo is a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 120 feet that deals 4d8 piercing damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). When the first-class infantry is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Clash of Steel** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The first-class infantry attacks each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], with a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage depends on the number of actions.  
single action 2d6+1 slashing damage  
two actions 4d6+10 slashing damage  
three actions 4d6+18 slashing damage

**Drilled in Formations** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The first-class infantry uses [[Bestiary/Families/Military|Change Formation]]. An first-class infantry unit typically knows the marching column and wedge formations.

**First-class Charge** two actions  The first-class infantry rushes forward with a hunger for battle. They [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to twice their Speed. At the end of their movement, each enemy within a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 2d6+5 slashing damage, with a DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.
