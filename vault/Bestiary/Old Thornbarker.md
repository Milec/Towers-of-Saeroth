**Memory Maelstrom** three actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|Nonlethal]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) Old Thornbarker tries to overwhelm foes with a surge of information he has absorbed over his long life. This surge deals 5d6 mental damage to each enemy within 40 feet, who must attempt a DC 32 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature maintains its composure, takes no damage, and is temporarily immune to Memory Maelstrom for 1 minute.  
 **Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]] and takes half damage.  
 **Failure** The creature takes full damage and is stunned 3.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 2d4 rounds, and is stunned 3.

**Sycophantic Scamps**   Dozens of [[Bestiary/Wood Scamp|wood scamps]] clamber on, nest in, and flit around Old Thornbarker, feeding him their observations and gossip. The arboreal can grab a nearby scamp as if drawing a weapon, using it as a [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown]] weapon. The unlucky scamp impacts with a clatter of spines and toxic pollen, after which the dazed elemental scampers away to regain its senses.  
 If Old Thornbarker takes 40 or more damage from an area effect, the scamps scatter and seek cover for 1d6 rounds, during which time Old Thornbarker can't make scamp Strikes and loses [[Bestiary/Abilities/All-Around Vision (Monster Core)|all-around vision]].
