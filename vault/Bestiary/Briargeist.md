# Briargeist

Briargeists are verdorites unique to Tanglebriar. Millennia spent steeping in [[Bestiary/Treerazer (Monster Core)|Treerazer's]] corruptive influence has granted them far greater strength while distorting their forms away from the vaguely humanoid to an animate tangle of thorny vines, roots, and fungal growths.

**Bewildering Toxin**  ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 36 Will; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] (1 round); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d10 mental damage, confused, and off-guard (1 round)

**Briarvine**  ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Void]]) The incorporeal seeds ejected from a briargeist grow rapidly when they damage living creatures. Living creatures hit by a briargeist's infesting seed must attempt a DC 36 Fortitude save with the following effects. A living creature can only host one briarvine at a time; a creature that currently has a briarvine growing out of them after failing a previous saving throw does not need to attempt additional Fortitude saves.  
 **Critical Success** The seed fails to take root.  
 **Success** The seed fails to take root, but the target still takes 2d10 void damage as the seed dies out.  
 **Failure** The seed takes root within the target and inflicts 2d10 void damage. At the start of the creature's next turn, a long ghostly thorny vine grows out of their body. This ghostly growth makes a vine Strike (as if the briargeist were making the attack) against a randomly determined ally of the host creature within reach at the start of the host creature's turn. If there's no viable target, the vine inflicts 2d10 void damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) on the host. The host can attempt a new Fortitude save at the end of each of its turns; otherwise, this effect ends automatically after 1 minute.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, except the host can't attempt new Fortitude saves to end the Briarvine early
