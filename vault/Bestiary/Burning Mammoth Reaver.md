**Reaver's Howl** When the reaver attempts an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]], the reaver compares the result of the Intimidation check to the Will DC of each non–Burning Mammoth creature within 30 feet, determining the effect separately for each creature. The reaver's howl ignores the usual circumstance penalty to the Intimidation check for not speaking the same language as the target.
