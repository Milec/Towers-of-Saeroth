# Zecui Horde

A surprise raid by a swarm of the insectile [[Bestiary/Zecui|zecui]] can easily wipe out an entire village overnight. When they don't devour their victims immediately, zecuis typically implant them with their own larvae and bury them in sprawling mass graves, where the developing zecui horde can lie dormant for years before suddenly erupting to the surface to wreak havoc on nearby settlements.

**Speed** 30 feet, burrow 20 feet, climb 20 feet; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Harden Chitin** single action  The zecuis fuse their chitin into black metallic shells. The horde gains resistance 10 to all damage (except [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] and [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) until they next take a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action.

**Mandible Frenzy** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The horde makes a vicious bite attack against each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The damage dealt depends on the number of actions.  
single action 1d8+2 piercing damage  
two actions 2d8+12 piercing damage  
three actions 3d8+15 piercing damage

**Mucus Deluge** two actions  The horde spits a volley of larva-infested mucus as a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 30 feet. Each creature in the area must succeed at a DC 27 Reflex save or be stuck to the nearest surface, [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (DC 30). Any creature so immobilized is exposed to zecui larvae at the end of each of its turns. When the zecui horde is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Subterranean Ambush** single action  **Requirements** The zecui horde has burrowed into an ambush position just beneath a surface of dirt, sand, or a similar loose material; **Effect** The horde bursts from the ground and moves up to its Speed. The horde deals 1d8+2 piercing damage (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] at the end of this movement.

**Zecui Larvae**  ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]) **Saving Throw** DC 30 Fortitude; **Stage 1** visible lumps as the larvae move but no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** drained 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** drained 3 and controlled by the zecui larva (1 day); **Stage 5** the creature dies and an adult [[Bestiary/Zecui|zecui]] can emerge from the corpse as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action.
