# Ancient Underworld Dragon

Underworld dragons make their homes close to fault lines, active or dormant volcanoes, and regions with natural seismic activities. Locations with hot springs and fume holes are particularly popular, as they provide many alchemical and magical ingredients. Aside from vaults stuffed with treasure, underworld dragon lairs are also laboratories, dedicated to uncovering secrets in cultivation, elements, magic, and any other mysteries of interest. Several underworld dragon families have operated such labs for tens of thousands of years.

**Countered by Water** If the underworld dragon takes damage from a spell with the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] or [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait, the elemental magic of fire within them is momentarily dampened. Until the end of their next turn, they take a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls, and their jaws Strikes don't deal fire damage. This limitation ends if the dragon uses Exploding Red Breath.  
**Fed by Wood** When an underworld dragon is struck by a weapon made primarily of wood or affected by a spell with the [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait, the dragon's internal fiery essences are stoked with the added fuel. Their Exploding Red Breath recharges, and the fire damage from the dragon's jaws deals an additional die of damage until the end of the dragon's next turn.

**Adamantine Claws** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** one per hour; **Effect** The dragon infuses their body with [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]]. **Exploding Red Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) The dragon breathes a blast of fire that explodes in a 40-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 75 feet, dealing 22d6 fire damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail the save also take 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]]. The dragon can't use Exploding Red Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Coiling Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes one claw Strike and one tail Strike in either order, each against the same target. If either Strike hits, the dragon can attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target with that limb as a free action. The dragon can have up to three creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] at a time; one in each of their front claws, and a last with their tail.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges Exploding Red Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Scorch Earth** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) The dragon plunges their body into the ground, [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrows]] up to their [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]], and then scorches the land within a 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Creatures underground or on the surface in the area take 8d6 fire damage (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature that critically fails also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 minute.
