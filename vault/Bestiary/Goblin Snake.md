**Coil** single action  The goblin snake uses an action to coil itself, increasing its reach with its fangs from 5 to 10 feet. After the goblin snake Strikes with its fangs, it becomes uncoiled.

**Goblin Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|Olfactory]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) The goblin snake belches a cloud of nauseating vapor in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Non-[[Traits/Goblin (Player Core)|goblin]] creatures within the cloud must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. On a critical failure, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for as long as it is sickened. Creatures that successfully save are immune for 24 hours. The goblin snake can't use Goblin Breath again for 1d4 rounds.
