# Dockhand

Working to load and unload cargo from ships, dockhands are considered unruly, but many stay focused and work hard until the job is done. Some do celebrate the end of their work day with rowdy drinking.

**Heft Crate** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The dockhand is adjacent to a crate; **Effect** The dockhand picks up a crate and heaves it up to 15 feet. **Swig** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) The dockhand Interacts to either draw a bottle of alcohol or pick up a nearby unattended bottle of alcohol and drink the whole thing. For 1 minute, the dockhand gains a +2 item bonus to melee damage rolls and saving throws against [[Traits/Fear|fear]], but they become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]].
