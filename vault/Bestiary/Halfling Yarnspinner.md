**Mesmerizing Tale** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) The yarnspinner weaves a long-winded but captivating narrative that enchants those nearby. Any creature that's in a 20-foot emanation or starts its turn in the aura must attempt a DC 24 Will save. The Mesmerizing Tale lasts until the end of the yarnspinner's next turn, but can be Sustained. The first time the yarnspinner Sustains the aura on subsequent rounds, the aura expands by 10 feet, to a maximum of 60 feet.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected, and is temporarily immune for 24 hours.  
 **Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with the yarnspinner until the start of its next turn, and must spend all its actions to move closer to the yarnspinner and listen to the tale.

**Resonant Weapons**  ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]) If the yarnspinner's Mesmerizing Tale aura is active or they have cast a spell within the last round, their Strikes with magic weapons deal an additional 2d10 sonic damage.
