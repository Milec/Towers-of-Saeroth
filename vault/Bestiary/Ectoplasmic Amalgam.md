**Overwhelming Ectoplasm**   An ectoplasmic amalgam's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune. A creature damaged by an ectoplasmic amalgam's Strikes is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] unless they succeed at a DC 36 Fortitude save, as the thoughts and emotions of the amalgam's constituent souls invade their mind.

**Pummeling Barrage** three actions  The ectoplasmic amalgam attempts a melee Strike against each enemy within the amalgam's melee reach. Each attack counts toward the amalgam's multiple attack penalty but don't increase the amalgam's penalty until it has made all its attacks.

**Spew Ectoplasm** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) The ectoplasmic amalgam releases a burst of ectoplasm that deals 16d6 bludgeoning damage in a 60-foot cone (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failed save, a creature is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (or stunned 2 on a critical failure). Spew Ectoplasm functions as a [[Traits/Force|force]] effect for the purposes of affecting [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures. The ectoplasmic amalgam can't use Spew Ectoplasm again for 1d4 rounds.
