**Matting Paint**   When a painted soldier uses a ranged Strike, the ammunition or weapon (if thrown) becomes a smear of paint. It deals damage normally, and a creature struck by it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and takes a –5-foot circumstance penalty to their Speed for 10 minutes. The paint can be removed with a total of 3 Interact actions by the creature or an adjacent ally.
