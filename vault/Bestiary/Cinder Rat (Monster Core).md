# Cinder Rat

These oversized rodents are made of smoldering charcoal and elemental fire, and noxious fumes continually bellow from their flaming flesh. Even other fire elementals find cinder rats unpleasant and are glad when they're summoned away from the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]].
