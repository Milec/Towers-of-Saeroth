**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Ji-yook transforms into her human form. Using Change Shape counts as creating a disguise for the [[Actions/Impersonate]] use of [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]. Ji-yook can remain in her human form indefinitely, and she can shift back to her true kitsune form by using this action again.

**Foxfire Blast** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Flourish]], [[Traits/Magical]]) Ji-yook makes a fist Strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d4 electricity damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] back 5 feet. On a critical hit, the target is pushed back and then knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

**Pinning Shot** single action  Ji-yook pins a foe's clothing to a wall or floor or the ground. Ji-yook makes a dart Strike. If the attack hits, it deals no damage and instead pins the target to an adjacent surface. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and must spend an Interact action to attempt a DC 10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to pull the dart free; it can't move from its space until it succeeds. If Ji-yook's dart Strike was a critical hit, the target must succeed at two separate Athletics checks to pull the dart free. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] while it remains immobilized in this way.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Ji-yook's Strikes deal an additional 2d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.
