**Mend Soul** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The eseneth restores 25 HP to itself or an [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].

**Seize Soul** single action ([[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]]) The eseneth tries to yank the soul out of the body of a creature they've [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]]. The eseneth attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the target's Fortitude DC.  
**Critical Success** The eseneth grabs the target's soul. The body is released and is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 2 rounds. When the body ceases being paralyzed, its soul returns instantly and the target wakes. The soul—grabbed by the eseneth—is [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]], is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], has a fly Speed equal to the creature's Speed, and otherwise has all the same statistics. It can't attack, cast spells, or attempt any skill checks that require a physical body other than checks to [[Actions/Escape]], and it must always maintain line of effect to its body.  
**Success** As critical success, but the paralysis ends after 1 round.  
 **Failure** The target remains grabbed or restrained, but its soul remains in its body.  
 **Critical Failure** The grab ends.

**Shred Soul** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The eseneth deals 3d10+14 force damage to an [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature they've [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]].

**Spirit Grasp**   An eseneth can [[Actions/Grapple]] [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures despite being corporeal. The eseneth uses their [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to Grapple incorporeal creatures as normal but can't use Athletics for other actions against incorporeal creatures, like [[Actions/Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip]].

**Spirit Touch**   A eseneth's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal 2d6 negative damage to living creatures and 2d6 positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Soul Needles
>
>
>
> An eseneth's spirit needles are composed of the psychopomp's essence. The needles form and dissipate at the eseneth's will and exist only as long as the eseneth does.
