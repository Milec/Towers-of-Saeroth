**Aura of Blight** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) 15 feet. The sarcovalt is surrounded by a miasma of infection. An enemy that enters or ends their turn in the aura must attempt a DC 19 Fortitude save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to the aura for 1 minute.  

**Failure** Until the end of its next turn, the creature takes a –1 circumstance penalty to saves against disease effects.  

**Critical Failure** Until the end of its next turn, the creature takes a –2 circumstance bonus against disease effects and is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1.  
**Befouled Body** Any water the sarcovalt touches becomes diseased with filth fever. **Filth Fever** (disease **Saving Throw** DC 23 Fortitude) Onset 1d4 days; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** drained 2 (1 day)  
**Reactive Detach** ⤾ **Trigger** The sarcovalt takes slashing damage from a creature while they have another creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]; **Effect** The sarcovalt uses Detach Head. Their head keeps the other creature grabbed or restrained, and their body Steps. The sarcovalt gains resistance 5 to the triggering slashing damage.  
**Return to Sender** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature escapes from being grabbed or restrained by the sarcovalt's detached head; **Effect** The sarcovalt's head returns to their body via the shortest possible path. Any creature in a straight line between the head and the body must succeed at a DC 21 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 round (clumsy 2 on a critical failure). The sarcovalt Dismisses Detach Head.

**Detach Head** free action ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) **Trigger** The sarcovalt takes damage from a critical hit with a slashing weapon or has their head removed (such as by a [[Equipment/Vorpal (GM Core)|vorpal]] weapon); **Effect** The sarcovalt can survive decapitation and isn't killed if an effect that removes their head would kill them. Instead, the sarcovalt's head becomes detached from their body. The detached head has the same statistics as the body (except for the aura of blight) and gains the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait. The sarcovalt's body can issue a verbal command to the detached head as a single action, giving the head 2 actions. The detached head and body share the same pool of Hit Points. While their head is detached, the sarcovalt's body can't make beak Strikes or use Malodorous Shriek. The detached head can only [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], Stride, make beak Strikes, or use Malodorous Shriek.

**Malodorous Shriek** two actions ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], [[Traits/Oni (Monster Core)|sonic]]) The sarcovalt screeches through their skull (detached or not), spraying whatever festering viscera remains in their head in a 20-foot cone. Each creature in the area takes 3d6 sonic damage and 3d6 poison damage (DC 21 basic Reflex save). The sarcovalt can't use this ability again for 1d4 rounds.
