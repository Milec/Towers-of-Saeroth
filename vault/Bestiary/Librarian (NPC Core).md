# Librarian

Keepers of knowledge, librarians document discoveries, events, and laws. The best librarians record events twice: once for public record and again to record how events truly unfolded.

**Research Specialist** A librarian is a 3rd-level challenge for encounters involving research.  
**Methodical Research** ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) When [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]] through stacks of books, a librarian can find the answer to almost any question. This allows the librarian to use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Library Lore]] in place of other lore skills, given enough time. The GM determines the DC of the check and the amount of time it takes (typically, a librarian can attempt three or four checks during 1 day of downtime).
