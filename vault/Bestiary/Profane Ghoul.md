**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 29; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 2d6 negative damage and regains half as many Hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 2d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); **Stage 6** dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight.

**Profane Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) Any living, non-[[Traits/Elf|elf]] creature hit by the profane ghoul's attack must attempt a DC 29 Fortitude save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]].  
 **Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**Profane Whispers** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The ghoul whispers profane secrets of undeath. Living creatures within 10 feet of the ghoul take 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent mental damage]]. Those with ghoul fever must also succeed at a DC 29 Will save or have the disease advance one step. The profane ghoul can't use Profane Whispers again for 24 hours.

**Scuttle** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The profane ghoul crawls on all fours, moving up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
