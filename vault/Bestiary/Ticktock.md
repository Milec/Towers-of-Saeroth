The sacristan can Sustain Shadow Scream for up to 1 minute. Non-[[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|velstrac]] creatures in the area when the ability is used, as well as those who enter or start their turn in the area, must attempt a DC 37 Will save.

 **Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] and deafened for 1 round.
