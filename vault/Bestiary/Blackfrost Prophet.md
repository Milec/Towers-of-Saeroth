# Blackfrost Prophet

With their hulking physiques and the potent connection to the Crown of the World, saumen kar who succumb to blackfrost become especially dangerous blackfrost dead. Those who guarded the Temple of Aqakaru in life continue to do so in undeath, albeit even more vigilantly and much more violently. Blackfrost prophets, as these wardens are sometimes known, maintain their formidable intellect and innate connection to their boreal environment, though the curse of blackfrost has stripped them of their memories and filled their hearts with evil.

**Imprison Mind** two actions ([[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The blackfrost prophet concentrates on a creature within 60 feet, filling the target's mind with befuddling susurrations. The creature must succeed at a DC 28 Will save or take 4d8+8 mental damage and become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute (stupefied 2 on a critical failure). The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] as long as it's stupefied.

**Raise Blackfrost** two actions ([[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The blackfrost prophet selects a point within 60 feet, then calls upon shards of blackfrost to stab up through the crust in a 30-foot burst. Creatures in the area take 2d8 piercing damage plus 2d8 cold damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that takes cold damage from this ability is exposed to [[Rules/Curses/Blackfrost|blackfrost]]. Until the next time the blackfrost prophet uses Raise Blackfrost, the affected area is difficult terrain.
