# Ancient Empyreal Dragon

The three major celestial planes—[[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]], [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana (GM Core)|Nirvana]], and [[Setting/Planes/Elysium (GM Core)|Elysium]]—each have their own respective dragons. Empyreal dragons have a direct connection to Heaven. Using the blessings of Heaven, empyreal dragons protect others and intercede against wickedness. Empyreal dragons are wise, considerate, and compassionate. When speaking with others, empyreal dragons are patient and understanding.

**Direct Halo** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) The dragon tosses their halo to a square within 90 feet. While the halo is deployed in this way, the dragon loses their inspiring presence aura, and the aura instead emanates from the halo with the same emanation radius. The dragon can Sustain to recall the halo from any distance. The halo is made of pure light—it doesn't occupy space and can't be targeted or destroyed in any way.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one wing Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Spirit Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Halo Pulse** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) The dragon chooses one effect to impose on creatures in their inspiring presence aura. The dragon can't use Halo Pulse again for 1d4 rounds.

- **Repulsion** Each creature must succeed at a DC 41 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] until it's no longer in the aura.
- **Restoration** ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]) Each creature recovers 9d8 Hit Points.
- **Restriction** ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) Creatures must succeed at a DC 41 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] (slowed 2 on a critical failure) while they remain within the aura. Regardless of the result, a creature is then temporarily immune to restriction for 1 minute.

**Spirit Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]) The dragon unleashes a blast of holy fire that deals 16d8 spirit damage in a 50-foot cone (DC 41 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The dragon can't use Spirit Breath again for 1d4 rounds.
