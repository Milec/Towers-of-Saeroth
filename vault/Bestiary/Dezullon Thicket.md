# Dezullon Thicket

It is thankfully uncommon to encounter more than one [[Bestiary/Dezullon (Monster Core)|dezullon]] within a few square miles, largely because few ecosystems contain enough prey to sustain more than one of the ravenous flesh-eating plants for long. When a well-fed dezullon finds a suitably abundant location, however, it occasionally takes root, blooms, and shortly thereafter expels dozens of fist-sized seed pods. Should enough of the resulting saplings survive to full size, they temporarily merge into a tangled mass of grasping tendrils and sweep across the area, voraciously scouring their environment clean of any remaining game before dispersing to seek their own hunting grounds.
