**Breath Weapon** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]) The priest breathes fire that deals 7d6 fire damage in a 30-foot cone (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). It can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds.

**Shrieking Frenzy** free action ([[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** The charau-ka's turn begins; **Effect** The charau-ka is [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] until the end of their turn and can use the extra action only to Stride or Strike. While in the frenzy, the charau-ka can't speak and automatically critically fails [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks, due to their loud wailing.

**Thrown Weapon Mastery**   Any weapon a charau-ka throws gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d6]] weapon trait. Furthermore, when a charau-ka throws an improvised weapon, they do not take the –2 penalty for doing so, nor does they take a penalty for using a thrown improvised weapon with the [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] trait to make a lethal attack.

**Volcanic Eruption** three actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]) **Requirement** The priest is standing on the ground. **Effect** The priest slams his fists into the ground, causing fire to erupt from the earth. Each non-worshipper of [[Setting/Deities/Dahak]] standing on the ground in a 15-foot emanation must succeed at a DC 25 Reflex save or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and take 1d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 fire damage.
