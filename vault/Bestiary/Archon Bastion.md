# Archon Bastion

Rekheps, also known as [[Bestiary/Shield Archon|shield archons]], stand against any fiendish onslaughts on [[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]], but occasionally travel to the mortal Universe to protect the weak. Large formations of rekheps are a sight to behold as they lock their shield forms in place to face the wicked.

**Fearless Switch** two actions ([[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]) The archon bastion Strides so that at least one of its segments occupies the same space of a Large or smaller willing ally. That willing ally is then teleported to any open space it can fit into that is adjacent to any of the archon bastion's segments, using teleportation magic innate to the troop's individual shield archons. The archon bastion can move up to three allies in this fashion.

**Living Shields** single action  The archon bastion grants each ally within a 5-foot emanation a +2 circumstance bonus to AC until that ally is no longer within the area or until the start of the archon bastion's next turn, whichever comes first. If the archon bastion uses Archon's Aegis against an attack against one of the shielded allies, the archon bastion gains the resistance and takes the damage rather than the ally.

**Smiting Lances** single action to three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The archon bastion engages in a uniform melee attack against each enemy in 10-foot emanation (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The damage depends on the number of actions. An [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature that fails its save against this effect takes an additional 2d6 spirit damage (or 1d6 spirit damage for the one-action version).  
single action 1d8+4 piercing damage  
[two-actions] 3d8+14 piercing damage  
[three-actions] 4d8+19 piercing damage
