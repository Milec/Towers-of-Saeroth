**Sneak Attack**   The pixie deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.

**Sprinkle Pixie Dust** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) The pixie sprinkles pixie dust onto one of their arrows. If the pixie hits a creature with that arrow before the pixie's next turn, the arrow inflicts one of the following special effects instead of dealing damage. Each effect depends on the target's DC 24 Will save. On a critical hit, the target gets a result one degree worse than it rolled.

- **Confusion** ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The target suffers the effects of a 4th-rank [[Spells/Confusion (Player Core)|_confusion_]] spell.
- **Shadow Shroud** ([[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]]) On a failed Will save, the target is enveloped in shadows. It perceives its surroundings as though [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] conditions were only [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] and dim light as though it was [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]].
- **Supernatural Chill** ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|shadow]]) The target takes 5d6 cold damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).

**Warden's Protection**   The pixie deals an additional 1d6 damage to any creature trespassing in the Gloaming Arbor.
