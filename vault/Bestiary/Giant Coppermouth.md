**Coppermouth Venom**  ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 25 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison and 1d6 electricity (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d6 poison and 1d6 electricity and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] (1 round); **Stage 3** 2d6 poison and 2d6 electricity and clumsy 2

**Quickening Jolt** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]) The coppermouth manipulates its own nervous system by increasing its reaction time, Striding and making two Strikes against different targets during the movement. Both attacks count toward its multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after it has made both attacks.

**Venomous Spit** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) The coppermouth unleashes a stream of electrified venom in a 30-foot line. The venom deals 8d6 electricity damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) and creatures that take damage from the venom are immediately exposed to coppermouth venom. The copper mouth can't use Venomous Spit again for 1d4 rounds.
