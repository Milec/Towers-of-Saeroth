**Bloodline Focus**   When Ayavah Refocuses, she completely fills her focus pool.

**Corruption of Sin**   When Ayavah casts a bloodline spell using a Focus Point or one of the following spells: _blister_,  _disintegrate_, _divine wrath_, _enlarge_, _fear_, or _slow_, she can choose to either give a target a –1 status penalty to AC for 1 round or gain a +2 status bonus to Intimidation checks for 1 round.

**Legendary Sculptor**   Ayavah makes [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks associated with sculpture at legendary proficiency. If she rolls a failure on a Crafting check involving sculpture, she gets a success instead.

**Reach Spell** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]) If the next action Ayavah takes is to Cast a Spell that has a range, increase that spell's range by 30 feet.
