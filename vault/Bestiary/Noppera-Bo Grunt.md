**No Face** Noppera-bos have no sense of vision, but their sense of hearing is [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]]. They don't breathe and are immune to effects that require breathing. They gain sustenance by absorbing fluids through their skin—traditionally by immersing their hands or faceless head into the fluid to “drink” through osmosis; they can also benefit from [[Traits/Consumable|consumables]], such as [[Traits/Potion|potions]] or [[Traits/Elixir|elixirs]], in this way.
