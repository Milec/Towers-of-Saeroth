**Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|[concentrate]]](/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|[polymorph]]](/Traits.aspx?ID=657), [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|[primal]]](/Traits.aspx?ID=676)) The batkin changes into their humanoid or hybrid shape. Each shape has a specific, persistent appearance. In hybrid shape, the batkin appears as a mix between human and a bat. While in hybrid shape, they gain a jaws unarmed Strike resembling a bat's fangs. In their humanoid shape, they retain the appearance of their original ancestry.

**Snagging Strike** single action  **Requirements** The batkin has one hand free, and their target is within reach of that hand; Effect The batkin makes a Strike while keeping one hand free. If this Strike hits, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of the batkin's next turn or until the target is no longer within the reach of the batkin's hand, whichever comes first.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Camazotz's Gifts
>
>
>
> [[Setting/Deities/Camazotz]] generally pays no mind to his mortal worshippers. However, when a follower does win Camazotz's favor, the bat god might grant that follower a gift. The most common of these boons is a bat [[Feats/Familiar (Player Core)|familiar]] or [[Feats/Animal Companion (Player Core)|animal companion]], but in some cases Camazotz transforms the faithful servant into a batkin. Only the most exceptional of Camazotz's followers are transformed into werebats.
