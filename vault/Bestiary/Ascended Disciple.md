**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d8 fire damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 4d8 fire damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 by the agonizing pain of the burns.
