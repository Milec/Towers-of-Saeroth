In addition, any non-[[Bestiary/Families/Kobold|kobold]] starting their turn in the smoke must succeed at a DC 21 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] by the foul smoke (or sickened 2 on a critical failure). Once created, the cloud of smoke is immobile and persists for 3 rounds. Jekkajak can move up to her Speed, but she must end her movement in a space that isn't adjacent to any enemies. This is a 2nd-level spell that requires a somatic component.
