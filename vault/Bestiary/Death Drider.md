**Death Drider Venom**  ([[Traits/Poison]]) The [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] condition persists after the poison's duration ends; **Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d8 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d8 poison damage and drained 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 4d8 poison damage and drained 2 (1 round)

**Hungering Web**   A creature hit by a death drider's web attack is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and stuck to the nearest surface (Escape DC 31). Each round thereafter until it breaks free, the creature takes 3d6 piercing damage (DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) and, on a failed save, is exposed to death drider venom.
