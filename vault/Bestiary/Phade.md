# Phade

On the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air (GM Core)|Plane of Air]], and throughout the Inner Sphere, phades serve as spies and assassins for their creators. Even when summoned into the mortal [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]], phades are generally called for unsavory and violent purposes, giving the phades that have turned their back on their creator a poor opinion of mortals as well.
