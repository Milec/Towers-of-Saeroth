**Damage** 5d10+12 bludgeoning plus dragonstorm strike

**Channel Dragonstorm** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]]) The animated dragonstorm directs a 30-foot cone of dragonstorm energy. This deals 3d8 acid, 3d8 cold, 3d8 electricity, 3d8 fire, and 3d8 poison damage to creatures in the cone (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The animated dragonstorm can't use Channel Dragonstorm again for 1d4 rounds.

**Destructive Strikes**   The animated dragonstorm's attacks ignore the Hardness of objects with Hardness 20 or less.

**Dragonstorm Strike**   On a critical hit, the animated dragonstorm's fist bursts with dragonstorm energy. The target takes 1d6 acid, 1d6 cold, 1d6 electricity, 1d6 fire, and 1d6 poison damage.
