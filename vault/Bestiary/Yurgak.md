**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The ghoul is adjacent to the corpse of a creature that died within the last hour. **Effect** The ghoul devours a chunk of the corpse and regains 7d6 Hit Points. It can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once. Yurgak also absorbs a portion of the memories once possessed by the creature she fed upon, potentially revealing secrets held by the flesh at the GM's discretion, and also gains a +1 status bonus to all skill checks for 1 minute.

**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 32; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 2d6 negative damage and regains half as many Hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 2d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); **Stage 6** dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight.

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living, non-[[Traits/Elf|elf]] creature hit by a ghoul's attack must succeed at a DC 32 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**Recall Memory** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Yurgak focuses her thoughts and recalls a memory from a previously fed-upon corpse. She can attempt to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] using any [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] as if she has legendary proficiency with it (+26 on the check).

**Swift Leap** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The ghoul jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
