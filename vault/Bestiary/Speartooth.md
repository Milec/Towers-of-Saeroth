**Pierce Armor** single action  Speartooth makes a fangs Strike against a creature it has grabbed. If the attack hits, the creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]; if the creature is wearing armor with hardness 10 or lower, the armor is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]]. If this Strike breaks a creature's armor or damages a creature who is unarmored or wearing broken armor, the creature also takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. This Strike doesn't further damage armor that's already broken.

**Pounce** single action  Speartooth Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If Speartooth began this action hidden, it remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Speartooth deals 2d6 extra precision damage to creatures that are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]].
