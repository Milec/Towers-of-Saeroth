At the end of the round, the heart-eating vulture explodes into a 10-foot-radius blast of red nindoru butterflies, their razor-sharp wings and claws inflicting 5d6 slashing damage (DC 18 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save), leaving behind only the heart it clutched in one of its talons.
