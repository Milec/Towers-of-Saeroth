# Somnalu Oculus

When [[Bestiary/Somnalu]] is defeated, its central eye abandons its dying body to create an ethereal body from the wisps of dreams. Over centuries—or sooner, if it can find a powerful source of nightmares—it can re-form a full body and return to its corporeal self.

**Scrutinizing Presence** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) 60 feet, DC 34. The Somnalu Oculus scrutinizes creatures around it, causing them to falter with nervousness and self-doubt. Upon entering the emanation, a creature must attempt a Will save. On a failed save, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 round. Regardless of the result of the saving throw, the creature is temporarily immune to scrutinizing presence for 1 minute.  
**Clearing Blink** ⤾ **Trigger** The Somnalu Oculus would be affected by a condition; **Effect** The Somnalu Oculus rapidly blinks, using dream wisps as an eyelid. The Somnalu Oculus attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the source of the condition with a counteract modifier of +24 and a counteract level of 8. It can't use Clearing Blink again for 1d4 rounds.
