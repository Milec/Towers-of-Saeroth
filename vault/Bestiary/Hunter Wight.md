# Hunter Wight

Dangers deep in the wilderness claim the life some lone scout or hunter. When they arise as wights, they renew their hunt with equal vigor, but all the living are now their prey. Hunter wights prefer to seek strategic ground, frequently taking residence atop hills or within abandoned watchtowers and keeps.

**Drain Life**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) When the hunter wight damages a living creature using an unarmed attack or their bound weapon, they gain 7 temporary Hit Points, and the creature must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or become [drained 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=1140). Further damage dealt by the hunter wight's unarmed and bound weapon attacks increases the value of the drained condition by 1 on a failed save, to a maximum of drained 4.

**Spawn Hunter Wight**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) A living humanoid killed by a hunter wight's weapon or claw Strike rises as a hunter wight spawn after 1d4 rounds. This spawn is under the command of the hunter wight that killed them. They don't have drain life or spawn hunter wight and are [clumsy 2](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=1140) for as long as they're a hunter wight spawn. If the creator of the hunter wight spawn dies, the hunter wight spawn becomes a fully autonomous hunter wight; they regain their free will, gain drain life and spawn hunter wight, and are no longer clumsy.
