# Mutant Giant Toad

Mutant giant toads prefer to dwell in polluted refuse heaps, contaminated swaths of sand, or other poisonous wastes. Often afflicted with a vestigial face or limbs, its back is studded with acid-filled pustules.

**Squirt Acid** two actions ([[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Primal]]) The mutant giant toad squirts a 60-foot line of acid from the pustules on its back. Creatures in the area take 3d6 acid damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The mutant giant toad can't Squirt Acid for 1d4 rounds.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole]]** single action ([[Traits/Attack]]) Medium, 1d8+3 bludgeoning, Rupture 8

**Tongue Grab**   A creature hit by the giant toad's tongue becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the giant toad. The creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the toad's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue with a successful Strike against AC 15 that deals at least 6 slashing damage. This deals no damage to the toad but prevents it from using its tongue Strike until it regrows its tongue, which takes a week.
