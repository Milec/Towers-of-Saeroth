**Body Slam** single action  **Requirements** Ran-to has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]]; **Effect** Ran-to throws his foe to the ground. The grabbed creature takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The grab ends.

**Greater Leaping Catch** two actions  Ran-to bounds up and attempts to Grab a foe while he soars. He makes a [[Actions/Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump]]. His maximum distance is 75 feet. He must succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to perform the leap as usual, but regardless of the type of leap the DC is equal to half the number of feet he moves in the air. Ran-to can attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Grapple]] at any point during the jump. Immediately after the attempt, he (and the creature, if the Grapple was successful) falls to the ground. Ran-to takes no falling damage when using this ability, but the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] creature takes the appropriate amount of falling damage as if they had fallen the distance moved.

**Titan Wrestler**   Ran-to can attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than him.

**Whirlwind Toss** three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** Ran-to has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]]; **Effect** Ran-to spins the creature he's holding, smashing them against any creatures within his reach, and then throws the creature aside. Ran-to attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the grabbed creature's Fortitude DC.  
**Critical Success** Ran-to tosses his foe a great distance. The grab ends. The grabbed creature is thrown into a space within 10 feet, takes 12d6 bludgeoning damage, and falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. All creatures adjacent to Ran-to take the same amount of bludgeoning damage (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).  
**Success** As critical success, except the grabbed creatures is thrown into a space within 5 feet, and creatures take 8d6 bludgeoning damage.  
**Failure** Ran-to tosses his foe aside. The grab ends. The grabbed creature falls prone.  
**Critical Failure** Ran-to loses hold of his foe, causing the grapple to end.
