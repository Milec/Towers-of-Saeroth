**Devastating Blast** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) The graveknight warmaster unleashes a 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of energy. Creatures in the area take 8d12 electricity with a DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The graveknight warmaster can use this ability once every 1d4 rounds.

**Exemplar of Violence** two actions ([[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The graveknight attempts a Strike as their armor flashes with sinister power that spurs allies to violence. After the Strike, allies who can see the graveknight can use a reaction to [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] or [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], but they must end this movement in a space adjacent to an enemy. One ally of the graveknight's choice can instead use a reaction to Strike.

**Graveknight's Curse**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]]) This curse affects anyone who wears a graveknight's armor for at least 1 hour; **Saving Throw** DC 39 Will save; Onset 1 hour; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1 and can't remove armor (1 day); **Stage 2** doomed 2, –10- foot status penalty to Speeds, and can't remove armor (1 day); **Stage 3** dies and transforms into the armor's graveknight.

**Phantom Mount** three actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]]) The graveknight warmaster summons a supernatural mount, as [[Spells/Marvelous Mount|_marvelous mount_]] heightened to a 7th rank. Unlike _marvelous mount_, the steed's AC and saving throw bonuses are all 4 lower than the graveknight's, and the steed has AC 34, Fort +23, Ref +20, Will +20, and 85 Hit Points. **Weapon Master**   The graveknight captain has access to the [[Rules/Critical Specialization|critical specialization]] effects of any weapons they wield.
