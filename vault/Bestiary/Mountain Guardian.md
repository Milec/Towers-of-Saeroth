# Mountain Guardian

Those who have unlocked the secrets of opening a kinetic gate to an elemental plane within themselves wield immense power over that element. Practitioners who specialize in elemental earth are hardy and defense-minded. The mountain guardian is surrounded by heavy armor made of stone held together with elemental magic.

**Base Kinesis** two actions  The mountain guardian generates, moves, or suppresses up to 1 Bulk of naturally occurring earthen matter within 15 feet. Generating creates earthen matter, moving moves existing matter up to 20 feet into any direction, and suppressing destroys a piece of that element (which can't be a durable crafted good, only natural forms of the element).

**Channel Elements** single action ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The mountain guardian reactivates their kinetic aura and can make an elemental blast Strike.

**Empowered Blast** two actions  The mountain guardian makes a melee or ranged elemental blast Strike with a +4 status bonus to damage.

**Tremor** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The mountain guardian stomps on natural earth or stone, causing a localized tremor. All creatures in a 10-foot burst within 30 feet take 3d10 bludgeoning damage with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature hat critically fails is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. Earth and stone in the area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] until the start of the mountain guardian's next turn.

**Weight of Stone** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Impulse]], [[Traits/Overflow]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The mountain guardian calls down boulders in a cylinder 20 feet in diameter and 80 feet high within 120 feet. Each creature in the area takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that fails is also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] downward 40 feet (80 feet on a critical failure) without taking [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]] and can't leave the ground for 1 round.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Kineticist Rules
>
>
>
> The mountain guardian is based on the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry And Background (Rage of Elements)|kineticist]] class from _Pathfinder Rage of Elements_, though simplified for use and an NPC. Their actions with the [[Traits/Impulse|impulse]] trait can be used only if their kinetic aura is active and they have a hand free. When they use an action with the [[Traits/Overflow|overflow]] trait (Tremor or Weight of Stone), their kinetic aura deactivates until they Channel Elements.
