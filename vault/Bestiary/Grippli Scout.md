**Hurl Net** single action  **Requirement** The grippli is wielding a net in two hands. **Effect** The grippli makes a ranged Strike (with a +9 modifier) against a Medium or smaller creature within 20 feet. On a hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds. On a critical hit, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] instead. The DC to Escape the net is 16. A creature adjacent to the target can Interact with the net to remove it.
