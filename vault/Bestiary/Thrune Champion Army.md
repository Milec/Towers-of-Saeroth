**Speed** 25 feet; troop movement

**Massacre** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The champions slash mercilessly with their broadswords against enemies in a 5-foot emanation (DC 27 basic Reflex save). The damage depends on the number of actions.  
single action 1d8+3 slashing damage  
two actions 2d8+12 slashing damage  
three actions 3d8+14 slashing damage

**Unleash Hell** two actions  The champions draw or reload their longbows, then launch a ranged attack in the form of a volley. This volley is a 10-foot burst within 100 feet that deals 5d8 piercing damage (DC 27 basic Reflex save). When the troop is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Wrath of Asmodeus** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]) The champions channel the infernal power of Hell itself into their strikes. If their next action is a Massacre that deals damage to at least one creature, all creatures that take damage from Massacre must also attempt a DC 27 Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2, becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 1 round, and takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].
