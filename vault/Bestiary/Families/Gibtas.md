# Gibtas  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 80

This rubbery, spheroid scavenger is nearly all acid-dripping mouth, with five bulbous black eyes and two powerful, gangly legs. A gibtas often forms a bond with a single other creature—usually, a more powerful [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]]— and serves this master loyally, if erratically. Creatures who keep a gibtas as a pet or guard soon learn to keep it away from any important possessions they don't want spattered with acid.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Gibtanius]] (Creature 8), [[Bestiary/Gibtas Bounder]] (Creature 5), [[Bestiary/Gibtas Spawn Swarm]] (Creature 6)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Gibtas Companions
>
>
>
> A gibtas can make for an unusual animal companion, with the GM's permission. Use the statistics for a [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Ambusher|bear animal companion]], except the companion has the rare trait, is an [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]] rather than an [[Traits/Animal|animal]], has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]] rather than [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and gains the [[Bestiary/Gibtas Bounder|gibtas bounder's]] Bouncing Slam as an advanced maneuver instead of [[Actions/Bear Hug]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Wild Gibtases
>
>
>
> In the wild, gibtases are scavenger creatures, avoiding much larger and more powerful predators while feeding off abandoned scraps. This limits their consumption-based reproduction. By spawning hundreds of offspring at once during times of plentiful food, they ensure the propagation of their species only during times of plenty.
