# Fumecrux  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -222- Hellbreakers|Pathfinder #222: Hellbreakers]] pg. 229

By studying the phenomena of [[Bestiary/Families/Hellcrown|hellcrowns]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Beheaded (Monster Core 2)|beheaded]], Chelaxian mages have created a fusion of the two. Years of experimentation culminated in the creation of fumecruxes, brimstone specters capped with the decapitated and rotting heads of great beasts. The process is being attempted more frequently as yet another weapon for the war against Andoran.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Fumecrux Variants
>
>
>
> As devastating as draconic fumecruxes are, they're uncommon sights to behold. Common fumecruxes are made from horses, large birds, and other mundane beasts. All fumecruxes display an apathy toward living creatures, including their makers, making them difficult to control. A necromancer who unleashes a fumecrux made from something as untamable as a dragon is either incredibly powerful or incredibly foolish.
