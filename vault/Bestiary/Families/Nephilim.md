# Nephilim  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 266

Nephilim are planar scions with a connection to the planes of the Outer Sphere. Some are obviously tied to realms such as [[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]] or [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]], while others are cryptic amalgams of metaphysical traits.
