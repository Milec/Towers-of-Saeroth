# Dragon, Forest  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 135

Like the rest of their imperial cousins, the forest dragon likely originated from the elemental plane they embody, namely the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]]. That some forest dragons yet reside in the recently returned Eternal Forest supports this theory, though this only answers the location of origin, not how the dragons came to be. The Elemental Lord [[Setting/Deities/Shumunue (Divine Mysteries)|Shumunue]] might resemble a forest dragon, but she is not one herself, so the mystery of the dragons’ progenitor remains unanswered to this day.
