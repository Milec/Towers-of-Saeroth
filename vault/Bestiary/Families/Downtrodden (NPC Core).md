> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Braving The Mob
>
>
>
> A mob is a [[Rules/Crowds (GM Core)|violent crowd]] that works as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|greater difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]], dealing 1d4 damage for each square entered. A creature can spend a single action to attempt a DC 16 [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check, clearing the mob from all squares adjacent to them on a success (or all squares within 10 feet on a critical success). This action has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits. If the mob is meant to be a combat encounter, you can instead use the [[Bestiary/Deluded Mob|deluded mob]].
