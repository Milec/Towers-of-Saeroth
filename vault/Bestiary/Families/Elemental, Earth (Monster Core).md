# Elemental, Earth  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 142

Earth elementals make excellent bodyguards for adventuresome spelunkers and are ideal protectors of important subterranean locations such as vaults and treasuries.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Diverse Elementals
>
>
>
> The Elemental Planes are more than expanses of sky, rock, metal fire, wood, and ocean. Clouds of fog, dust, and storms float through the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air (GM Core)|Plane of Air]]. The [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]] includes verdant groves, shining metal and crystal, and irradiated wastelands. The [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]] features magma, suffocating smoke, and radiant firelight. Amid the endless sea of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water (GM Core)|Plane of Water]] are pockets of ooze and brine, plus the otherworldly environs of the deep.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Elemental Wyrms
>
>
>
> The [[Bestiary/Icewyrm|icewyrm]] is the most widely encountered elemental wyrm, but others exist as well, including the blazing [[Bestiary/Firewyrm (Monster Core)|firewyrm]], the sleek but somewhat smaller sparkwyrm, and the largest of them all, the immense and acidic sludgewyrm.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Looting Earth Elementals
>
>
>
> Earth elementals sometimes have valuable gemstones or minerals incorporated into their bodies, and once they’re slain, these valuables can be scavenged as treasure. Gemstones, precious metals such as [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] or gold ore, or rarer materials such as [[Equipment/Dawnsilver|dawnsilver]] or [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] can be harvested from these creatures, although the more expensive finds tend to be incorporated into only the most powerful earth elementals.
