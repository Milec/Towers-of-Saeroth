# Zombie  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 356

A zombie's only desire is to consume the living. Unthinking and ever-shambling harbingers of death, zombies stop only when they're destroyed.

## Zombie Abilities

 You can modify zombies with the following zombie abilities. Most zombies have one of these abilities; if you give a zombie more, you might want to increase its level and adjust its statistics.

**Ankle Biter** This zombie fights just as well on the ground as it does standing. While [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], the zombie isn’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], it ignores the status penalty to its attack rolls, and it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]]. The zombie can also move up to half its Speed when it [[Actions/Crawl (Player Core)|Crawls]].

 **Disgusting Pustules** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) The zombie is covered in pustules that rupture when it takes any piercing damage or any critical hit. Adjacent creatures are hit with vile fluid, causing them to become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] unless they succeed at a Fortitude save against the [[Rules/Spell DC and Spell Attack Modifier|moderate spell DC for the zombie's level]].

 **Feast** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) If the zombie is adjacent to a [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] creature, or a corpse that died in the past hour, the zombie can feast upon its flesh. This restores an amount of Hit Points equal to the zombie's level. If the creature is alive, the zombie deals damage equal to its jaws, fist, or claw damage.

 **Plague-Ridden** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) The zombie carries a plague that can create more of its own kind. This functions as the [[Bestiary/Plague Zombie|plague zombie's]] zombie rot, except at stage 5, the victim rises as another of the zombie's type, rather than a plague zombie. Use the [[Rules/Spell DC and Spell Attack Modifier|high spell DC for the zombie's level]].

**Persistent Limbs** The first time the zombie is critically hit with a melee or ranged Strike, a limb falls off its body and continues to attack. The limb acts on the zombie's initiative; each round it can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half the zombie's Speed and make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. The limb uses and contributes to the zombie's multiple attack penalty.

**Putrid Stench** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]]) 15 feet. The zombie’s rotting flesh is particularly malodorous. A creature that enters the area must attempt a Fortitude [[Rules/Spell DC and Spell Attack Modifier|save of a moderate or high DC]] for the zombie’s level. On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, and on a critical failure, the creature also takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round. While within the aura, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to saves to recover from the sickened condition. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune to all zombies’ putrid stenches for 1 minute.

 **Rotting Aura** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) The zombie emits an aura of rot and disease that causes wounds to fester. Any living creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the zombie and is not at full Hit Points takes 1d6 void damage. This damage increases by 1d6 for every 6 levels the zombie has.

 **Unkillable** This zombie is nigh unkillable. The zombie loses its weakness to slashing and gains resistance against all damage equal to its level (minimum 3), and it gains weakness equal to twice its level (minimum 6) to critical hits. Increase the zombie's level by 1 if you give it this ability.

**Unholy Speed** The zombie gains a +10-foot status bonus to all its Speeds.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Creating Zombies
>
>
>
> To create a zombie creature, start with a zombie of the appropriate size. Then add any Strikes, Speeds, or other abilities it would gain from its shape. To create a Gargantuan zombie, begin with the zombie hulk, apply the [[Rules/Elite Adjustment|elite adjustments]], change its size to Gargantuan, and increase its [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] by 5 feet.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Disposable Legions
>
>
>
> With the ancient lich Tar-Baphon now released from his ages-long imprisonment, the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] within his legions have been specifically repurposed. Zombies like the ones seen here fulfill particular roles, and evil necromancers deploy them much like living or intelligent undead troops. Necromancer experiments seek to produce various abilities and mix energies, with horrifying results that don’t deter their creators.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Risen from the Grave
>
>
>
> Zombies are often created using unwholesome necromantic rituals. Among the living dead, zombies are most often used as fodder, wearing down defenses and consuming resources before more powerful undead arrive to deal the killing blow. Zombies cannot speak or even truly think for themselves, but they can be commanded by other allied undead and powerful necromancers.
