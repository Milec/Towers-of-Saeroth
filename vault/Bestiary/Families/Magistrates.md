# Magistrates  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 224

Magistrates are the cogs and gears that keep bureaucracy running, moving the machine along under the inertia of the status quo.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Harbormaster]] (Creature 3), [[Bestiary/Judge]] (Creature -1), [[Bestiary/Tax Collector]] (Creature -1), [[Bestiary/Warden]] (Creature 6)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## At Your Service
>
>
>
> Magistrates can conscript others into service in a variety of positions.
>
> **[[Bestiary/Harbormaster]]**: [[Bestiary/Bosun]], [[Bestiary/Dockhand|dockhand]], [[Bestiary/Guard|guard]], [[Bestiary/Ship Captain|ship captain]].  
> **[[Bestiary/Judge]]**: [[Bestiary/Captain of the Guard|Captain of the guard]], [[Bestiary/Executioner|executioner]], [[Bestiary/Guard|guard]], [[Bestiary/Jailer|jailer]]  
> **[[Bestiary/Tax Collector]]**: [[Bestiary/Captain of the Guard|Captain of the guard]], [[Bestiary/Guard|guard]], [[Bestiary/Jailer|jailer]], [[Bestiary/Watch Officer|watch officer]]

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Delaying Tactics
>
>
>
> A magistrate can weaponize the bureaucracy. A [[Bestiary/Judge|judge]] might enforce an archaic law generally ignored yet still on the books. A [[Bestiary/Tax Collector|tax collector]] could ask for the seal of a lord who is out of the region. The [[Bestiary/Harbormaster|harbormaster]] could require all fees in local coin, fully aware money changers won't be open for days.
