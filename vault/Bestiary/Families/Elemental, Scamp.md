# Elemental, Scamp  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 146

Elemental scamps are bat-like critters marked by elemental powers. Scamps are dispatched from the Elemental Planes by more powerful residents or called to [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] by neophyte summoners. All scamps have a hint of magical power due to a lingering connection to their home plane, which they largely use to pull simple pranks.

 Scamps rapidly form a pecking order of cleverness. Humanoids often confuse scamps when meeting such creatures for the first time. These confused scamps usually resort to an escalating series of pranks and mischief, seeing what they can get away with to establish their place in the hierarchy.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Conjuring Scamps
>
>
>
> Scamps are a favorite target for lowlevel spellcasters to summon, both because they have several useful abilities and because they are relatively easy to command and manipulate. Still, care must be taken when using scamps as minions, because if left unwatched or unattended for too long, they can cause all sorts of mischief.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Other Scamps
>
>
>
> The four scamps presented here are but the most common of their kind. Metal scamps can be found on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]] and wood scamps on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]], although wood scamps are excited to spread throughout [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] they've long been barred from.
