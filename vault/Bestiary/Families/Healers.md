# Healers  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 220

The world is a dangerous place. Thankfully, there are those who devote their lives to easing the pain and suffering of others.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Apothecary]] (Creature -1), [[Bestiary/Physician]] (Creature -1), [[Bestiary/Plague Doctor]] (Creature 5), [[Bestiary/Surgeon]] (Creature 2)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Ailments
>
>
>
> Common diseases are located [here](Diseases.aspx). These can be treated by most healers, though they may have longer-term physiological impacts. For unusual conditions, like [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghoul fever]] or [[Bestiary/Mummy Guardian|mummy rot]], you might impose penalties on a normal healer attempting to remedy them.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Medical Service Prices
>
>
>
> The [[Bestiary/Physician|physician]] and [[Bestiary/Surgeon|surgeon]] are masters of [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]]; the [[Bestiary/Apothecary|apothecary]] and [[Bestiary/Plague Doctor|plague doctor]] are experts. Plague doctors charge five times this rate, and surgeons 10 times.
>
> **Identify Affliction**: 1 sp.  
> [[Actions/Treat Wounds|**Treat Wounds**]]: 2 sp.  
> [[Actions/Treat Disease|**Treat Disease**]]: 1 gp.  
> **[[Actions/Administer First Aid|First Aid]] or [[Actions/Treat Poison|Treating a Poison]]**: 1 sp.
