# Devotees  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Gamemastery Guide]] pg. 212

Religions inspire devout individuals to uphold their tenets. Many of these devotees seek to bring respite and hope, but others have darker motives.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Acolyte of Nethys]] (Creature 1), [[Bestiary/Priest of Pharasma]] (Creature 6), [[Bestiary/Prophet]] (Creature 2), [[Bestiary/Zealot of Asmodeus]] (Creature 4)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Spell Swap Example
>
>
>
> To make a NE [[Bestiary/Acolyte of Nethys|acolyte]] or [[Bestiary/Priest of Pharasma|priest]] of [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]], you can use these spells. **3rd** [[Spells/Bind Undead|_bind undead_]], [[Spells/Circle of Protection|_circle of protection_]], [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] (×3), [[Spells/Vampiric Touch|_vampiric touch_]]; **2nd** [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]], [[Spells/False Life|_false life_]], [[Spells/Ghoulish Cravings|_ghoulish cravings_]]; **1st** [[Spells/Bane|_bane_]], [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]], [[Spells/Ray of Enfeeblement|_ray of enfeeblement_]]; **Cantrips** [[Spells/Chill Touch|_chill touch_]], [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]], [[Spells/Divine Lance|_divine lance_]], [[Spells/Guidance|_guidance_]], [[Spells/Shield|_shield_]]; **Cleric Domain Spell** [[Spells/Undeath's Blessing|_undeath's blessing_]]

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Swapping Deities
>
>
>
> Customize an [[Bestiary/Acolyte of Nethys|acolyte]] or [[Bestiary/Priest of Pharasma|priest]] by matching their deity's alignment and favored weapon, adding the deity's divine skill at +14 (or +7), and swapping spells. An example appears on the facing page. (An acolyte gains only the 1st-level spells, domain spell, and cantrips, and has two 1st-level [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spells instead of three 3rd-level heal spells.) If you swap [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] spells for heal, replace [[Feats/Healing Hands]] with the [[Feats/Selective Energy]] cleric feat.
