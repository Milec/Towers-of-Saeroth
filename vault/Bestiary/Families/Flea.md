# Flea  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -151- The Show Must Go On|Pathfinder #151: The Show Must Go On]] pg. 83

Fleas are little more than a nuisance in most circumstances, but can be lethal when they grow to monstrous proportions or gather in enormous swarms.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Flea Swarm]] (Creature 5), [[Bestiary/Giant Flea]] (Creature 3)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Flea Allies
>
>
>
> Flea swarms and giant fleas make for natural pets and monstrous minions for creatures who live in areas of filth and concentrated populations. [[Bestiary/Families/Goblin|Goblins]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Gremlin|gremlins]] are unperturbed by fleas' disturbing physiologies and are unfazed (though not necessarily unaffected) by the diseases these vermin carry. [[Bestiary/Families/Ratfolk]] have been known to tame giant fleas for use as mounts, and urban-dwelling [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|druids]] whose magic focuses on blight or disease naturally find much to like about the flea swarms they frequently summon.
