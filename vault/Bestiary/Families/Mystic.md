# Mystic  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 96

Hidden secrets and occult powers have an irresistible lure for many. Since the majority of these NPCs are spellcasters, consider using [[Rules/Themed Spell Lists|alternative spell lists]] to adjust their themes.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Guests Beware
>
>
>
> Most intelligent creatures follow basic customs of hospitality, such as not attacking a guest who has dined under their roof. Even the most sinister fey creature is loath to break such customs, though eating meals with the fey is often fraught with its own dangers. Others have fewer scruples and might employ poisons, curses, or potions as seasoning for unwitting dinner guests.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Mystic Protection
>
>
>
> Most mystics aren't particularly skilled in combat and might hire or ensorcel bodyguards, such as the [[Bestiary/Bodyguard (NPC Core)|bodyguard]] (level 1), [[Bestiary/Archer Sentry (NPC Core)|archer sentry]] (level 2), [[Bestiary/Musketeer|musketeer]] (level 3), [[Bestiary/Tournament Combatant|tournament combatant]] (level 5), [[Bestiary/Knight|knight]] (level 7), or [[Bestiary/Hero Hunter|hero hunter]] (level 13).

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Sinister Rituals
>
>
>
> Most rituals have a simple gp cost and associated skill checks. But what if a desperate NPC is willing to offer something more macabre, such as lives, souls, or worse? Such a nefarious ritual uses a normal ritual as a baseline, but the DCs and proficiency requirements might be reduced or waived entirely, as might any gp cost.
