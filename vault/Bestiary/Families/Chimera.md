# Chimera  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 132

The chimera heads presented below can replace one or more of the heads in the chimera stat block presented in _Monster Core_. To do so, use the Strike entry listed in the replacement head instead of the one in the original stat block; if you replace the dragon head, you must remove the creature's Dragon Breath ability. However, each of the new heads below grants new abilities.

## Chimera Heads

### Ankhrav Head

 **Ranged** single action acid spit +20 ([[Traits/Range|range increment 30 feet]]), **Damage** 2d10+9 acid

 **Spray Acid** two actions ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]]) The chimera spews acid in a 30-foot cone, dealing 9d6 acid damage to all creatures in the area (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The chimera can't use Spray Acid again for 1d4 rounds.

### Hippogriff Head

 **Melee** single action hippogriff beak +22 ([[Traits/Fatal (Player Core)|fatal d10]]), **Damage** 2d6+9 piercing

 **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Rend (Monster Core)|Rend]]** single action hippogriff beak

### Rotting Animal Head

 **Ranged** single action virulent spit +18 ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]), **Damage** 3d8+4 poison plus chimeric rot

 Chimeric Rot ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) When the chimera hits a creature with virulent spit they must succeed a DC 26 Fortitude saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].

### Snake Head

 **Melee** single action snake fangs +20 ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8+7 piercing plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]]

 **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Constrict (Monster Core)|Constrict]]** single action 2d8 bludgeoning, DC 24

### Snapping Turtle Head

 **Melee** single action snapping jaws +20, **Damage** 2d10+9 piercing

**Deep Breath** The chimera can [[Rules/Mounted Defenses (Player Core)|hold its breath]] for 1 hour.

### Wolf Head

 **Melee** single action wolf jaws +20, **Damage** 2d8+9 piercing plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Knockdown (Rage of Elements)|Knockdown]]

 **Leader of the Pack** The chimera's wolf jaws attacks deal 1d6 extra damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] creatures.

## Greater Chimera Heads

 A greater chimera can have any combination of three heads chosen from the options below, each granting additional abilities inherent in the original creature.

### Adult Dragon Head

 **Melee** single action dragon jaws +27, **Damage** 4d6+8 piercing plus 4d6 energy damage (see Draconic Bite)

 **Draconic Bite** A greater chimera's dragon head deals and extra 4d6 damage of a type matching the damage dealt by its Dragon Breath.

 **Dragon Breath** two actions The greater chimera deals 12d6 damage to all creatures in a 30-foot cone (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex). This breath deals either acid, cold, electricity, fire, force, mental, sonic, or spirit, depending on the kind of dragon head the greater chimera possesses. The greater chimera can't use Dragon Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

### Cave Worm Head

 **Melee** single action worm jaws +27 ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10+8 piercing plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]]

 **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallow Whole]]** single action (attack) Large, 3d10+8 bludgeoning, Rupture 24; the target must be [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the greater chimera's worm jaws

### Emperor Cobra Head

 **Melee** single action emperor cobra fangs +27, **Damage** 3d8+12 piercing plus chimeric venom

 **Chimeric Venom** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) When the greater chimera critically hits a creature with emperor cobra fangs, it deals an additional 6d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]].

### Phantasmal Beast Head

 **Melee** single action phantom slam +27 ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]), **Damage** 3d8+8 void

 **Incorporeal Features** The greater chimera gains resistance 5 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and damage from Strikes with the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune).

### Screeching Roc Head

 **Melee** single action beak +26 ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]]), **Damage** 3d10+8 piercing

 **Ear-splitting Screech** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) The screeching roc head emits a harsh cry that deals 9d6 sonic damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) in a 30-foot emanation. It can't use Ear-splitting Screech again for 1d4 rounds.

 **Powerful Wings** The greater chimera's fly Speed increases by 10 feet.

### Witchwarg Head

 **Melee** single action witchwarg jaws +25, **Damage** 2d12+8 piercing plus 1d12 cold and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Knockdown (Rage of Elements)|Knockdown]]

 **Freezing Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The greater chimera breathes a cloud of frost in a 15-foot cone that deals 9d10 cold damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The greater chimera can't use Freezing Breath again for 1d4 rounds.
