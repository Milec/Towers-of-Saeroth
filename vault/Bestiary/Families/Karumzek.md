# Karumzek  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 81

Karumzeks are crafty, spider-like servitors of [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]].

## Members

[[Bestiary/Karumzek]] (Creature 4), [[Bestiary/Karumzek Swarm]] (Creature 11)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Traveling from Axis
>
>
>
> Karumzeks remain in Duskfathom, awaiting orders from Norgorber. Once a karumzek receives its orders, it begins its journey from [[Setting/Planes/Axis]] to elsewhere in the Great Beyond via a secret system of planar strands known as the “Reaper's Web.” While not instantaneous, a karumzek traveling along the web can reach almost any point in the Great Beyond within a few hours.
