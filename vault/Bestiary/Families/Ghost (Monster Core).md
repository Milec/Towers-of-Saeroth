# Ghost  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 160

When some mortals die through tragic circumstances or without closure, their souls can linger and haunt a locale significant to them in life.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Ghost Commoner (Monster Core)|Ghost Commoner]] (Creature 4), [[Bestiary/Ghost Mage (Monster Core)|Ghost Mage]] (Creature 10), [[Bestiary/Ghostly Mob (Monster Core 2)|Ghostly Mob]] (Creature 8), [[Bestiary/Hungry Ghost (Monster Core 2)|Hungry Ghost]] (Creature 6)

## Creating a Ghost

You can also use the following guidelines to turn living creatures into ghosts. Increase the creature's level by 2 and change its statistics as follows.

- Increase AC, saves, Perception, DCs, and skill modifiers by 2. Increase the most appropriate melee attack modifier by 4 to become ghostly hand (described below), and increase all other attack modifiers by 2.
- A ghost has a Strength modifier of –5 and a Constitution modifier of +0.
- Do not modify the ghost's Hit Points due to its new level.

## Ghost Abilities

 A ghost gains the [[Traits/Ghost (Monster Core)|ghost]], [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] traits. Many become [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]. The base creature loses any traits that represented its life as a living creature, such as [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] and [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]], and any abilities or traits that rely on it being a living, corporeal creature. You might also need to adjust some abilities that conflict with the theme of a ghost. All ghosts gain the following abilities.

 [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|**Darkvision**]]  
 **Site Bound** A typical ghost can stray only a short distance from where it was killed or the place it haunts. A typical limit is 120 feet. Some ghosts are instead bound to a room, building, item, or creature that was special to it rather than a location.  
**Rejuvenation** ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) When a ghost is destroyed, it re-forms after 2d4 days within the location it's bound to, fully healed. A ghost can be permanently destroyed only if someone sets right whatever prevents the spirit from resting.  
 **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|Void Healing]]**  
 **Immunities** bleed, [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], precision, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] Resistances all damage 5 (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], or [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]]; double resistance to non-[[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]). This resistance increases to 10 at 9th level and 15 at 16th level.  
**Fly Speed** equal to its Speed  
 **Ghostly Hand** All ghosts have a ghostly hand unarmed attack that deals void damage. It typically has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] traits. Some ghosts wield ghostly memories of weapons they held in life, but the effect is the same.

## Special Abilities

 Select one or two of the following abilities, or potentially three if the ghost is 9th level or higher. These should relate to the ghost's death or its history. You can also create new abilities or adapt those from monsters or classes. For DCs, use the high spell DC of the [[Rules/Spell DC and Spell Attack Modifier|ghost's level]].

 **Corrupting Gaze**two actions The ghost stares at a creature it can see within 30 feet. The target takes 1d6 void damage plus 1d6 for every 2 levels the ghost has with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save. A creature that fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute.  
 **Draining Touch** two actions With a touch, the ghost attempts to drain a living creature's life force. It makes a ghostly hand Strike but deals no damage on a hit. Instead, the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] for 1 day, and the ghost regains HP equal to half its own level.  
 **Frightful Moan** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The ghost laments its fate, forcing each living creature within 30 feet to attempt a Will save. On a failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]] (or frightened 3 on a critical failure). On a success, a creature is temporarily immune to this ghost's frightful moan for 1 minute.  
 **Inhabit Object** single action The ghost possesses an object of size Large or smaller within 20 feet, making it an [[Bestiary/Families/Animated Object|animated object]]. This animated object's level can be no higher than the ghost's level – 2. If the target object is being held by a creature,  the bearer can attempt a Will save to prevent the possession. This possession ends when the object is destroyed or the ghost leaves it. At this point, the ghost reappears in the object's square and can't Inhabit an Object again for 1d4 rounds.  
 **Malevolent Possession** two actions The ghost attempts to possess an adjacent corporeal creature. This has the same effect as the [[Spells/Possession (Player Core)|_possession_]] spell, except since the ghost doesn't have a physical body, it is unaffected by that restriction of the spell.  
 **Telekinetic Assault** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) The ghost cries out in pain and anguish as small objects and debris fly about in a 30-foot emanation. Creatures in this area take 1d6 bludgeoning damage + 1d6 per 2 levels the ghost has, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
