# Elemental, Wood  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 204

At first glance, wood elementals might not seem to have the same destructive potential as their cousins from the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire|Planes of Fire]], [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water|Water]], or [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air|Air]], but don't be fooled. The woods can be a dangerous place.

 Though some resemble animals, the elemental beasts presented here are still plants, and their life cycles reflect it. Vegetable lambs grow on rooted stalks, from which they can't be removed until they've matured (or perhaps ripened), leaving them dependent on their immediate surroundings for food. Moss sloths are little more than lumps of green fluff for the first century of their existence, only gaining limited mobility once they can grow their defensive wooden claws.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## A Brain By Any Other Name
>
>
>
> Even if Lady [[Setting/Deities/Shumunue]] taught the wood elementals' ancestors to mimic animals, a wood elemental's consciousness is contained not in a brain but in its root system. Entities like [[Bestiary/Nursery Crawler|nursery crawlers]], [[Bestiary/Living Grove|living groves]], and [[Bestiary/Carved Beast|carved beasts]] use this to their advantage. That an elemental's wooden body can be carved and crafted, apparently without lasting harm, implies that they might not feel pain so long as their roots remain undamaged.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Diverse Elementals
>
>
>
> The Elemental Planes are more than expanses of sky, rock, metal fire, wood, and ocean. Clouds of fog, dust, and storms float through the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air (GM Core)|Plane of Air]]. The [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]] includes verdant groves, shining metal and crystal, and irradiated wastelands. The [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]] features magma, suffocating smoke, and radiant firelight. Amid the endless sea of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Water (GM Core)|Plane of Water]] are pockets of ooze and brine, plus the otherworldly environs of the deep.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Elemental Wyrms
>
>
>
> The [[Bestiary/Icewyrm|icewyrm]] is the most widely encountered elemental wyrm, but others exist as well, including the blazing [[Bestiary/Firewyrm (Monster Core)|firewyrm]], the sleek but somewhat smaller sparkwyrm, and the largest of them all, the immense and acidic sludgewyrm.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Wild But Unwild
>
>
>
> Many wood elementals are created by [[Bestiary/Kizidhar|kizidhar]] and other powerful creatures on the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] and placed in rote roles. Populating nature preserves and sprawling estates, they can be unsure how to behave if released into the wild. They're essentially born domesticated and will likely act far differently than the wild creatures they mimic, even in natural environments. Their natural predators are few, putting the elementals at less risk, although they're still sometimes eaten by giant termites or captured by some as pets.
