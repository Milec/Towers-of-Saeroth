# Noppera-bo  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 338

Noppera-bos appear to be regular humans from behind, and might even resemble someone you know, until they turn to reveal a featureless face.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Noppera-Bo Grunt (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Noppera-Bo Grunt]] (Creature 0), [[Bestiary/Noppera-Bo Impersonator (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Noppera-Bo Impersonator]] (Creature 6), [[Bestiary/Noppera-Bo Occultist (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Noppera-Bo Occultist]] (Creature 2), [[Bestiary/Noppera-Bo Trickster (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|Noppera-Bo Trickster]] (Creature 1)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Consumate Professionals
>
>
>
> While the typical noppera-bo is cruel and revels in the act of frightening or tormenting humanoid creatures, not all of them wallow in evil. Some noppera-bos prefer roles as pranksters and use their abilities to trick and tease, while even rarer noppera-bos use their abilities to honor and idolize those they admire while focusing their own time on developing their skills at art or crafting tools of peace.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Noppera-Bo Origins
>
>
>
> There are many theories on the origins of noppera-bos—from tanuki whose [[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]] ability went wrong, to humanoids who were warped by curses, to naturally spawning yokai. Some even theorize this condition was once a transmissible curse like lycanthropy. Most noppera-bos don’t recall their origins and are often more interested in pretending to be someone else entirely.
