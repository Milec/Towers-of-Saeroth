# Aspodean Wall Troops  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Hellfire Dispatches]] pg. 70

Troops at the Aspodean Wall can be found protecting or undermining the dividing structure, depending on their allegiance.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## War-Torn Fields
>
>
>
> As the largest front in the war and the location of some of its largest battles, the trench-torn fields before the Aspodean Wall have been ravaged by skirmishes between troops of all kinds. It's here that Andoren infantry regularly faces off against wave after wave of Chelaxian [[Bestiary/Vordine Legion|vordine legions]] and ort mobs, overseen by mortal officers protected by retinues of hellbound honor guard, and Brastlewark sapper squads on both sides of the conflict seek either to bring down the wall or defend it against enemy incursion, all the while launching strategic missions against the siege weapons employed by their enemies whenever the opportunity should arise.
