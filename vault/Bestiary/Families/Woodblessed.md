# Woodblessed  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 85

Woodblessed are those granted uncontrollable energy drawn from the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]]—a blessing that brings pain and eventual death.

## Woodblessed Abilities

 All woodblessed gain the following abilities.

 **Explosive End** ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) When the woodblessed dies, the planar portal inside of them collapses, causing them to explode in a 20-foot burst of vines that deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage per level to all creatures with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against the woodblessed's spell DC. The woodblessed's body is completely consumed, though their root network and gear remain behind.

 **Luminant Aura** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) 20 feet. The volatile planar energy coursing through a woodblessed's veins fills the area with dim light.

 **Primal Innate Spells** A woodblessed can cast [[Spells/Timber|_timber_]] as an innate primal cantrip.

 **Reactive Flare** ⤾ **Trigger** The woodblessed is damaged by a creature within their reach; **Effect** Distracted by the pain, the woodblessed's concentration slips and splinters erupt from their body as they temporarily lose control of the planar energy within them. Both the triggering creature and the woodblessed take 1d6 piercing damage + 1d6 per 2 levels the woodblessed has, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against the woodblessed's spell DC.

 **Tap Planar Gate** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The woodblessed empowers themself or their next attack.  
 If the woodblessed's next action is to Stride, is launched off the ground by a suddenly sprouting tree and propelled forward. During this Stride, they have a fly Speed equal to their Speed. If they don't end their movement on a solid surface that can support their weight, they fall.  
 If the woodblessed's next action is a Strike, the attack sprouts thorns that deal an additional 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] (1d6 for levels 6–12, 1d10 for levels 13+).

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Creating Woodblessed
>
>
>
> Apply the following to turn a living creature into a woodblessed.
>
>
> - Increase its level, AC, attack modifiers, DCs, saving throws, and skill modifiers by 1.
> - It gains the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait, as well as weakness to fire and mental [[Rules/Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances (GM Core)|based on its level]].
> - It gains a splinter ranged Strike that deals piercing damage, is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group, and has a [[Traits/Range|range increment]] of 30 feet.
