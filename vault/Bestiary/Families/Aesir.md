# Aesir  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 14

Aesir are both warriors and monitors who constantly seek to sharpen their battlefield skills. They thrill in the challenge of combat and consider those who seek diplomatic resolutions to conflict to be cowards. They often speak fondly of a time in the distant future when the entire multiverse will be drawn into a glorious battle for the fate of creation.
