# Ikeshti  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -173- Doorway to the Red Star|Pathfinder #173: Doorway to the Red Star]] pg. 86

Ikeshtis are reptilian humanoids native to Akiton's wastelands. Smaller than Golarion's [[Rules/Sidebars/Droon|iruxi]], ikeshtis organize around distinct societal roles.

## Members

[[Bestiary/Ancient Rivener]] (Creature 14), [[Bestiary/Ikeshti Brood-Minder]] (Creature 2)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Ikeshti Characteristics
>
>
>
> Ikeshtis are reptilian humanoids with a unique evolutionary lifestyle, from the petite ikeshti brood-minders who collectively raise their community's young and the congregants who manage the settlement to the hulking riveners whose tormented exile transforms them into near-mindless, violent juggernauts.
