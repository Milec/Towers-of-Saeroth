# Dragon, Empyreal  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 114

The three major celestial planes—[[Setting/Planes/Heaven (GM Core)|Heaven]], [[Setting/Planes/Nirvana (GM Core)|Nirvana]], and [[Setting/Planes/Elysium (GM Core)|Elysium]]—each have their own respective dragons. Empyreal dragons have a direct connection to Heaven. Using the blessings of Heaven, empyreal dragons protect others and intercede against wickedness. Empyreal dragons are wise, considerate, and compassionate. When speaking with others, empyreal dragons are patient and understanding.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Alternate Abilities
>
>
>
> Empyreal dragons found on the [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] planes might have their own unique abilities. You can apply the following adjustments to empyreal dragons of any age.
>
> Empyreal dragons understand that they sometimes need to make a sacrifice to protect others. To make a dragon with this ability, replace Divine Deflection with the following.
>
> **Sacrificial Shield** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Trigger** An ally within 60 feet of the dragon would take damage; **Effect** The dragon’s halo imposes itself in front of the creature like a shield, transferring the damage to the dragon. If the damage is void, the dragon gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to that damage equal to half its level.
>
> Empyreal dragons who fight alongside mortals when the forces of evil are on the march can empower their allies. To make a dragon with either of these abilities, replace Draconic Frenzy with one of the following.
>
> **Restorative Halo** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) The dragon’s halo divides into smaller halos, hovering over up to six creatures of the dragon’s choice within its inspiring presence aura. Creatures wearing a halo gain fast healing equal to half the dragon’s level for 1 minute. The dragon cannot use other abilities of its halo while other creatures wear it, and they can Dismiss the effect for any or all affected creatures.
>
> **Wreath of Holy Light** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]]) The dragon imbues its allies with heavenly fire. Creatures within 60 feet of the dragon gain the holy trait and their Strikes do an additional 1d10 fire damage for 1 minute. If the creature critically Strikes an enemy, the enemy is also set on fire and takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage.]]

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Empyreal Dragon Lairs
>
>
>
> Although most empyreal dragons keep homes among the environs of heaven, many dragons also choose to keep lairs across the mortal [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]]. These lairs are generally in less remote places, with the expectation being that a person would be more likely to stumble upon one and find assistance in times of need. In addition to the traditional riches of a dragon's hoard, an empyreal dragon's hoard contains a multitude of armaments, typically sized to fit the people of the region should they need equipment to face off against evil.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Yssendri
>
>
>
> Originally hailing from the ranks of heaven's greatest armies, the great empyreal archdragon Yssendri eventually retired from direct combat, choosing to focus on more philanthropic ventures. They travel to smaller settlements across [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] to aid in defending innocents. Recently, they traveled to the desert nation of Thuvia to defend a caravan of merchants from afar.
