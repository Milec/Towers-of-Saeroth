# Cobble Mite  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 3]] pg. 52

Cobble mites resemble squarish stones a few inches across with mouths like split geodes. Though indolent and harmless alone, they're dangerous in large groups.

## Members

[Cobbleswarm](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=986) (Creature 2), [[Bestiary/Cobbleswarm]] (Creature 2), [[Bestiary/Sturzstromer]] (Creature 19)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Cobble Mite Pets
>
>
>
> Characters looking to keep a cobble mite as a pet can usually buy one in many large cities for 1 gp. Those who search for one in the wild must hunt carefully and risk finding enough cobble mites lairing together to form a cobbleswarm. A character with a familiar can choose a cobble mite, which must have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower|burrower]] familiar ability.
