# Ooze  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 254

Slimes, molds, and other oozes can be found in dank dungeons and shadowed forests. While not necessarily evil, some grow to enormous sizes and have insatiable appetites.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Amoebas Large and Small
>
>
>
> [[Bestiary/Giant Amoeba|Giant amoebas]] and [[Bestiary/Amoeba Swarm|amoeba swarms]] are usually found near each other, as the two oozes are part of the same life cycle. When a giant amoeba grows large enough, it can spontaneously split apart into two separate amoeba swarms, and when an amoeba swarm feeds enough, its individual components can fuse together into a single creature.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Oozing Acid
>
>
>
> Many oozes have acidic attacks that can quickly degrade flesh, wood, and even stronger materials. Some believe that oozes are the result of alchemical or magical experimentation run amok, while others postulate that they simply emerged from the natural processes of evolution.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Other Oozes
>
>
>
> Many varieties of these nearly mindless predators exist in the world. Some are mere variants, with different colored puddings, jellies, and oozes with little to differentiate them from those presented here other than their habitat. Others are more specialized, or even dangerously intelligent. The [[Bestiary/Shoggoth|shoggoth]] is one such example, but another variety of deadly and powerful ooze is the thankfully rare blight, a creature composed of protoplasm and eyes that curses entire regions with its presence.
