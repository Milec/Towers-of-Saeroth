# Blight Bonded  *Creature Family*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 80

The [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] contains many unique ecosystems formed around species of wondrous trees; when [[Rules/Diseases/Ayrzul's Blight]] mutates one of these trees rather than killing it, it can have a cascading effect on the tree's biome. Multiple species often mutate in tandem, becoming warped mockeries and a vector for Ayrzul's Blight. These “blight bonded” creature families are parasites and predators and typically form twisted symbiotic relationships.
