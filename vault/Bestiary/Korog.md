**Damage** 3d12+17 bludgeoning plus 1d6 electricity

**Designate Blood-Foe** single action  Korog declares one creature he can see within 30 feet to be his blood- foe whom he swears to defeat. He can have up to three blood-foes designated at a time; any additional blood- foe removes another blood-foe of Korog's choice. Korog treats the results of Perception checks made against blood-foes as improved by one degree, and he has a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against his blood-foes' attacks.

**Devastating Blast** two actions  The graveknight unleashes a 30-foot cone of energy. Creatures in the area take 9d12 electricity damage (DC 37 basic Reflex save). The graveknight can use this ability once every 1d4 rounds.

**Graveknight's Curse**   This curse affects anyone who wears a graveknight's armor for at least 1 hour. **Saving Throw** DC 37 Will save; **Onset** 1 hour; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] 1 and cannot remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 2** doomed 2, hampered 10, and cannot remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 3** dies and transforms into the armor's graveknight.

**Korog's Command** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Korog commands his allies to take an action. All allies who hear or see him are [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] 1 for 1 round and can use this extra action to Step, Stride, or make a Strike against one of Korog's blood-foes.

**Weapon Master**   The graveknight has access to the critical specialization effects of any weapons it wields.
