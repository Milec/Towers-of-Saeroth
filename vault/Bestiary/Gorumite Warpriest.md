**Consecrated**   As long as the consecrate ritual effects persist in Crownhold, a Gorumite warpriest gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, skill checks, saving throws, and Perception checks; these bonuses are not calculated into the stat block.

**Fervent Casting** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Spellshape]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The Gorumite warpriest loudly proclaims the glory of Gorum. The next divine spell they cast takes one fewer action (minimum of one), but must only target the warpriest. If the spell affects an area or multiple targets, such as bless, it only affects the warpriest, even if it lasts more than one round.
