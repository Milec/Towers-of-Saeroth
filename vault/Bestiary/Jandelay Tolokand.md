**Heartless Furnace** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) 50 feet, 2d6 poison damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). The poisonous smoke that bellows from a tolokand makes all creatures inside it [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the smoke are concealed to creatures within it.  
**Inexorable** The tolokand recovers from the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] conditions at the end of its turn. It's also immune to penalties to its Speeds and ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain]].  
**Toxic Blood** When a creature scores a critical hit against the tolokand with a piercing or slashing Strike, the tolokand's poisonous, oily blood sprays forth. Any creature within 5 feet of the tolokand takes 3d6 poison damage (DC 34 basic Reflex save). Any non-creature plants the blood spills on die.
