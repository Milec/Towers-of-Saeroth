# Algea

Algeas protect (and sometimes retrieve) souls at risk of being claimed by other entities, particularly spellcasters who dabbled with otherworldly forces but never officially pledged their souls.

**Grieving Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) 30 feet. A creature that begins its turn in the aura must attempt a DC 27 Will save. [[Traits/Undead]] take a –2 circumstance penalty to this saving throw. The creature is then temporarily immune to the grieving aura for 1 hour.  
**Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Failure** For 1 round, the creature can't use reactions and is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for that turn as it sobs uncontrollably.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the effects lasts while the creature remains in the aura plus 1 minute.

**Drain Magic**  ([[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Divine]]) Whenever a creature fails its save against Frenzied Beaks, the algea attempts to counter a spell effect active on that creature (6th level, [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check +22). The algea can choose which effect to attempt to counteract for each creature.

**Frenzied Beaks** single action  Each foe in the swarm's space takes 5d6 piercing damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex) and is exposed to drain magic. This damage affects [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures as if it had the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.

**Soul Cage**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) An [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] [[Traits/Undead|undead]] damaged by Frenzied Beaks must attempt a DC 30 Will save.  
**Critical Success** The undead is unaffected and is temporarily immune for 24 hours.  
**Success** The undead takes a –10-foot status penalty to Speeds for 1 round.  
**Failure** As success, but the undead is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round, although it can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] (DC 30).  
**Critical Failure** The undead is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] for 1 round. The algea can attempt to [[Spells/Plane Shift|_plane shift_]] with the restrained creature even if that creature isn't willing, but an unwilling creature can attempt a Will save to resist _plane shift_. If it succeeds, the algea plane shifts and leaves the incorporeal creature behind, which is then no longer restrained.
