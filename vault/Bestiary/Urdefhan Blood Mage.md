# Urdefhan Blood Mage

Hardened masters of occult power, urdefhan blood mages trade their blood for magical might. A heavily scarred blood mage is held in esteem by their fellow urdefhans, as it shows they have survived many bargains with eerie occult patrons.

**Blood Offering** single action ([[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Metamagic]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The blood mage cuts themself and takes 8 slashing damage. If the blood mage's next action is to [[Actions/Cast a Spell]], the action can't be disrupted, and if the spell has a range, the blood mage can increase that spell's range by 30 feet.

**Wicked Bite** single action  **Requirements** The urdefhan damaged a creature with a jaws Strike on its last action; **Effect** The urdefhan maintains contact, turning the creature's flesh translucent around the site of the injury. The urdefhan chooses one of two options, each of which requires a DC 26 Fortitude save. If the jaws Strike was a critical hit, the creature suffers both effects, using the same save result for both.

- **Drain Blood** The urdefhan drinks some of the creature's blood. On a failed save, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] and the urdefhan regains 10 HP (or, on a critical failure, it's drained 2 and the urdefhan regains 20 HP).
- **Drain Vitality** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) The urdefhan draws out some of the creature's vital essence. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 hour on a failed save (or enfeebled 2 for 1 hour on a critical failure).
