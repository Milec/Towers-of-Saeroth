**Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The wereant changes into their humanoid, hybrid, or animal shape. Each shape has a specific, persistent appearance. A true wereant's natural form is their hybrid shape. In humanoid shape, they use their original humanoid size, lose their jaws and claw Strikes, and gain a melee fist Strike that deals bludgeoning damage equal to the slashing damage dealt by their mandibles. In animal shape, they take the form of a Medium giant ant, gain a climb Speed of 20 feet, gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] on their mandibles Strike, and lose their weapon Strikes.

**Curse of the Wereant**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal]]) This curse affects only [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoids]]; **Saving Throw** DC 34 Fortitude. On each full moon, the cursed creature must succeed at another Fortitude save or turn into a wereant until dawn. The creature is under the GM's control and goes on a rampage for half the night before falling [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] until dawn.

**Envenomed Weapons**   Weapons the wereant poisoner holds deal an additional 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] as poison seeps from the wereant's hands onto the weapon. Weapons cease to deal this persistent poison damage once the wereant lets them go. The kukri Strikes above include this persistent poison damage.

**Moon Frenzy**  ([[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) When a full moon appears in the night sky, the wereant must enter hybrid form, can't Change Shape, becomes one size larger, increases their reach by 5 feet, and increases the damage of their mandibles Strike by 2. When the moon sets or the sun rises, the wereant returns to humanoid form and is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] for 2d4 hours.

**Poisonous Flurry** two actions  The wereant poisoner makes two kukri Strikes and a mandibles Strike in any order.

**Vitriolic Strikes**   If the wereant critically hits a creature with a melee Strike, that creature can't recover from persistent poison damage for 1 round.
