**Alchemical Embalming**   The grave robber carries alchemical vials of specially prepared embalming fluid meant to hinder pursuit by anyone who interrupts their grave-robbing. A creature hit by a grave robber's embalming flask takes a –10-foot penalty to all its Speeds for 1 round. On a critical hit from an embalming flask, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] for 1 minute.
