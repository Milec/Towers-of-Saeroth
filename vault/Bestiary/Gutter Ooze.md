# Gutter Ooze

These small masses of protoplasm are considered the lesser “cousins” to [[Bestiary/Sewer Ooze (Monster Core)|sewer oozes]], emerging from the drainage culverts beneath a city after a rainfall to feed on bits of organic matter that collect in streetside gutters. As they collect just about everything—pebbles, bits of broken glass, and other trash—some cities consider them to be more helpful than detrimental.
