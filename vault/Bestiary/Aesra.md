# Aesra (Legion Archon)

Despite their flaming blades and ring of unblinking eyes, aesras are the diplomats of peace among the archons, preferring justice via compromise and mutual benefit rather than justice by the sword. Nonetheless, when forced to fight against fiendish powers aesras don't hesitate in battle, mounting offensives under divine commanders like [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae (Divine Mysteries)|Iomedae]].
