**Channel Rot**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) The mummy prophet of Set can deliver devastating rot through melee weapons they wield.

**Breath of Sand** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Negative]]) **Requirements** The prophet's Sandstorm is active; **Effect** The prophet inhales its Sandstorm, suppressing the effect until the end of the prophet's next turn. It then exhales a 120-foot cone of negatively charged sand that deals 7d6 negative and 7d6 slashing damage (DC 33 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Sand Form** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The prophet loses their coherent from and becomes an animated pile of sand. While in this form, the prophet gains resistance 10 to physical damage and is immune to precision damage. They can't cast spells, activate items, or use actions that have the [[Traits/Attack|attack]] or [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait. They gain a fly Speed of 40 feet and can slip through tiny cracks. This lasts for 5 minutes, and the prophet can Dismiss the effect.

**Sand Rot**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) This affliction can't be reduced below stage 1, nor can damage from it be healed, until successfully treated with [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]] or a similar effect; the affliction can then be removed as normal for a disease. A creature killed by sand rot turns to sand and can't be resurrected except by a 7th-level [[Rituals/Resurrect|_resurrect_]] ritual or similar magic; **Saving Throw** DC 31 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 minute); **Stage 2** 12d6 negative damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] (1 day)

**Sandstorm** three actions ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Earth]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The mummy creates a temporary sandstorm in a 30-foot emanation that lasts for 1 minute. Creatures within the emanation take a –4 circumstance penalty to Perception checks and must succeed at a DC 33 Fortitude save or be forced to hold their breath or start [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocating]]. A creature within the sandstorm at the end of its turn takes 2d6 negative and 1d6 slashing damage.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Sand Mummification
>
>
>
> To keep from eating or drinking during mummification, prophets of Set grafted metal helmets to their bodies shaped like the heads of sha, sleek beasts that were a symbol of [[Setting/Deities/Set|Set's]] religion. Through slits in their masks, the prophets consumed alchemically treated sands, which absorbed moisture, caused organ failure, and ruptured the stomach to cause death.
