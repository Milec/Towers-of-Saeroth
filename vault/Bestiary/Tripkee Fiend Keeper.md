# Tripkee Fiend Keeper

Tripkees have a technique for dealing with unholy threats that has been passed down through the generations. A fiend keeper can absorb unholy spirits into their body before those entities can cause trouble for their kin. Though they can tap into this power when necessary, tripkee fiend keepers try to cleanse themselves of its evil influence by undertaking good deeds.

**Bounce Cruuk** two actions  The tripkee fiend keeper makes a ranged Strike with their cruuk against a target within 30 feet. Once the Strike is complete, the cruuk ricochets back into the tripkee fiend keeper's hand. If their hands are full when the cruuk returns, it falls to the ground in their space.

**Harness Wickedness** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Unholy]]) **Requirements** The tripkee fiend keeper isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]]; **Effect** The tripkee fiend keeper allows a portion of the fiendish power they have absorbed to flow through their body. For the next minute, the tripkee fiend keeper's Strikes deal an additional die of damage and gain the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait. The tripkee fiend keeper also gains 10 temporary Hit Points, a +5- foot status bonus to Speed for the duration, and weakness 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]]. At the end of the duration, the tripkee fiend keeper is stupefied 1 for 1 hour.

**Hunter of Virtue**   Whenever the tripkee fiend keeper critically hits an [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creature, they reduce the value of their stupefied condition by 1.

**Forest Passage**   The tripkee ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by plants, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.
