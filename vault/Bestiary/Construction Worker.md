**By Design**  ([[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]) The construction worker spends 1 minute inspecting the layout of a room and attempts a DC 22 [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Architecture Lore]] check. On a success, they learn the size and layout of all adjacent rooms on the same floor (or all rooms on the floor on a critical success). They can inspect each room only once per day.

**Demolishing Swing** two actions  The construction worker makes a maul Strike against a creature. If it hits, the creature is pushed 10 feet. If the target is wearing metal armor, its armor also takes the damage, which bypasses 5 of the armor's Hardness.
