# Librarian

Keepers of knowledge, librarians document discoveries, events, and laws. The best librarians record events twice; once for public record, and again to record how events truly unfolded. A librarian typically knows four to six additional languages, often including [[Setting/Languages/Elven]] and [[Setting/Languages/Draconic]]. A librarian is a 3rd-level challenge for encounters involving research.

**Methodical Research** ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) When [Searching](https://2e.aonprd.com/Activities.aspx?ID=9) through stacks of books, a librarian can find the answer to almost any question. This allows the librarian to use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Library Lore]] in place of other lore skills, given enough time. The GM determines the DC of the check and the amount of time it takes (typically, a librarian can attempt three or four checks during 1 day of downtime).
