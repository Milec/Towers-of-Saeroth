**Shauth Seize**   The lasher gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] ability with a shauth lash and can use their Wicked Bite through magical shauth weapon Strikes.

**Wicked Bite** single action  **Requirements** The urdefhan damaged a creature with a jaws Strike on its last action; **Effect** The urdefhan maintains contact, turning the creature's flesh translucent around the site of the injury. The urdefhan chooses one of two options, each of which requires a DC 25 Fortitude save. If the jaws Strike was a critical hit, the creature suffers both effects, using the same save result for both.

- **Drain Blood** The urdefhan drinks some of the creature's blood. On a failed save, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] and the urdefhan regains 10 HP (or, on a critical failure, it's drained 2 and the urdefhan regains 20 HP).
- **Drain Vitality** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) The urdefhan draws out some of the creature's vital essence. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 hour on a failed save (or enfeebled 2 for 1 hour on a critical failure).
