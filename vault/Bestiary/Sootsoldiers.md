# Sootsoldiers

Powerful deities and warlords strive for dominance over the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire]] and the rest of the Inner Sphere in order to prove fire's supremacy. Armies of flaming soldiers go to war against each other on battlefields of smoke and smoldering char, all unable to bring their full power to bear. [[Setting/Deities/Ymeri]], the vicious and victorious Queen of the Inferno, commands legions of sootsoldiers: elementals capable of battling the armies of her foes and, even when destroyed, rising from the ashes of the Eternal Furnace, scorched and triumphant.

**Ashen Smoke** When the sootsoldiers are reduced by an HP Threshold or are reduced to 0 HP, the destroyed soldiers crumble to a cloud of ash-laden smoke in 20-foot burst centered on the sootsoldiers. All creatures within the cloud are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and all creatures outside it are concealed to those inside. The smoke lasts for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind.

**Incinerating Grasp** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The sootsoldiers reach to constrain each enemy in a 5-foot emanation in their fiery clutches (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).  single action 2d8 fire damage  
two actions 3d8+7 fire damage plus [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]  
 three actions 4d8+7 fire damage plus grabbed

**Seething Flash** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The sootsoldiers reignite and rush across the battlefield, Forming Up and Striding twice. At the end of this movement, a wave of flame and heat pours off the sootsoldiers, dealing 4d8 fire damage to other creatures in a 10-foot emanation, with a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails its save is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## The Radiant Host
>
>
>
> Sootsoldiers who serve the other Lord of Fire, the Lambent King [[Setting/Deities/Atreia]], are covered in glowing embers instead of black char. In place of ashen smoke's normal effects, any creature in the area must attempt a DC 29 Reflex save. On a failure, for 1 minute that creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] and its invisibility is negated.
