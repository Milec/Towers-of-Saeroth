**Deceiver's Surprise**   On the first round of combat, if the false priest rolls [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] for initiative, creatures that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to them.

**Fickle Prophecy** single action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The false priest convinces another creature of their omnipotence by attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check compared to the creature's Will DC. If successful, the target gains 1d8+4 temporary Hit Points that last for 1 hour or until the false priest removes them by rebuking the target, whichever occurs first.

**The Jig Is Up** ⤾  **Frequency** once per hour; **Trigger** The false priest critically fails a [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check; **Effect** The false priest Strides.

**Sneak Attack**   The false priest deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures. This increases to 2d6 against creatures off-guard due to the false priest's [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] or deceiver's surprise.
