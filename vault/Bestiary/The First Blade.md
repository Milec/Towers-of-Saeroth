# The First Blade

The Herald of [[Setting/Deities/Gorum (Divine Mysteries)|Gorum]], God of War, is said to have been formed from an unthinking sliver of steel splintered from Gorum's blade eons ago. As a living tool of war, the First Blade has been reforged countless times by Gorum as the face of war evolves. The First Blade does not identify as any particular ancestry; a mirror to his master, the armor and weapon themselves serve as his body—only emptiness lies within. Also like Gorum, the First Blade identifies as male.

**Conjure Weapon** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) **Requirements** The First Blade has a free hand; **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The First Blade extends an arm and makes a fist; as he does so, a common _+2 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Astral|greater astral]] melee weapon_ made of [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] appears in his fist. If he drops a conjured weapon or it is disarmed, the weapon vanishes. If he throws a conjured weapon, it vanishes after it is thrown and potentially damages its target. The First Blade prefers using a [[Equipment/Weapons/Greatsword (Player Core)|greatsword]] for melee Strikes and a [[Equipment/Weapons/Light Hammer (Player Core)|light hammer]] for thrown ranged Strikes, but cannot have more than one conjured weapon in his possession at any time.

**Lord of Battle**   The First Blade is highly skilled with all melee and thrown weapons, and gains the advantage of any such weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effects]]. He inflicts an additional 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] with all melee Strikes made with melee weapons.

**Whirling Blades** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]) **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** To the faint sound of fell warhorns, the First Blade transforms into a Large cloud of whirling metal shards, then Strides twice. This movement doesn't trigger reactions, and while he can move through occupied spaces, he must end his movement in an unoccupied space, at which point he resumes his true form. All creatures who the First Blade moves through at least once during his Strides take 10d10 slashing damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).
