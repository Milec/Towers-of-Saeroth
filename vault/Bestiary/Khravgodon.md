**Feign Death** ⤾ **Trigger** The opossum is reduced below 70 HP; **Effect** The opossum collapses. It is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and can use actions that require only its mind, but any other action ends the ruse. A successful DC 18 Perception check to [[Actions/Seek]] or [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] is required to determine that the animal is not, in fact, dead.  
**Revived Retaliation** ⤾ **Trigger** The opossum is attacked or disturbed by a creature within reach while Feigning Death; **Effect** The opossum Strikes the triggering creature.

On a failed save, the target also takes a –2 circumstance penalty to AC for 1 round.

**Grasping Tail**   A khravgodon can drag a Large or smaller creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] with its tail along with it when it Strides.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Tail Tales
>
>
>
> A Kellid legend holds that the khravgodon once had a beautiful tail covered with bright, thick fur. One night, the khravgodon fell asleep in the open, having danced all night showing off its beautiful tail. When it awoke, [[Bestiary/Ankhrav|ankhravs]] had chewed all the hair off khravgodon's tail, leaving it with the bald appendage we see today. Khravgodons have been taking revenge on ankhravs ever since.
