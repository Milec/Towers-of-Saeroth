All creatures in the area must succeed a DC 22 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]] for 1 minute (clumsy 3 and stupefied 3 on a critical failure).
