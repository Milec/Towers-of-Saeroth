**Fiery Rhetoric** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The upstart rattles off talking points at an enemy within 30 feet. The target takes a –2 status penalty to Perception and Will saves for 1 minute.

**Fascinating Speech** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The political upstart begins a rousing speech which they can Sustain up to 1 minute. Any creature within 30 feet that can hear the speech, must attempt a DC 17 Will save.  
 **Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with the upstart for 1 round.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature is fascinated with the upstart as long as the speech lasts.
