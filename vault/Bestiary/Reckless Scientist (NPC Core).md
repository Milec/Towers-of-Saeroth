**Unstable Collection** A reckless scientist carries a collection of poorly stowed alchemical items: 3 [[Equipment/Elixir of Life (Player Core 2)|elixirs of life]] and 6 alchemical grenades. The scientist replenishes these items each day using scavenged materials. The alchemical grenades deal either acid, cold, or fire damage plus 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent damage]] and 2 [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage of the same type (typically the collection contains two of each grenade).

**Quick Grenadier** single action  The reckless scientist Interacts to draw an alchemical grenade with an Interact action and throws it as a ranged Strike.

**Reckless Alchemy** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) The reckless scientist attempts to combine two alchemical grenades or two elixirs of life into one item. They can Interact to draw the items if necessary. They attempt a DC 22 Crafting check, destroying both component items to create one new item.  
 **Success** The new item has the full effect of both component items, and the reckless scientist can Activate it. If they don't Activate it before the end of their turn, the item explodes (as critical failure).  
 **Failure** The new item is inert.  
 **Critical Failure** The unstable item explodes, dealing 3d6 piercing damage to the reckless scientist
