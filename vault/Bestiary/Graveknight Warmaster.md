**Hungry Armor** A creature that Strikes a graveknight warmaster with a melee weapon must succeed at a DC 31 Reflex save or be [[Actions/Disarm|disarmed]] of that weapon. If the creature critically fails, the weapon ends up in the graveknight's space. A creature that hits a graveknight warmaster with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack must succeed at a DC 31 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the graveknight until the end of its next turn, it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]], or the graveknight moves, whichever comes first.  
**Rejuvenation** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When a graveknight is destroyed, their armor rebuilds their body over the course of 1d10 days—or more quickly if the armor is worn by a living host (see Graveknight Armor below). If the body is destroyed before then, the process restarts. A graveknight can only be permanently destroyed by obliterating their armor (such as with [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]]), transporting it to the [[Setting/Planes/Positive Energy Plane]], or throwing it into the heart of a volcano.  
**Sacrilegious Aura** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evil|evil]]) 30 feet. When a creature in the aura uses a [[Traits/Positive|positive]] spell or ability, the graveknight automatically attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it, with a +23 counteract modifier.

**Devastating Blast** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) The graveknight unleashes a 30-foot cone of lightning. Creatures in the area take 8d12 electricity damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The graveknight can use this ability once every 1d4 rounds.

**Exemplar of Violence** two actions ([[Traits/Visual]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The graveknight attempts a Strike as their armor flashes with sinister power that spurs allies to violence. After the Strike, allies who can see the graveknight can use a reaction to Step or Stride, but they must end this movement in a space adjacent to an enemy. One ally of the graveknight's choice can instead use a reaction to Strike.

**Graveknight's Curse**   This curse affects anyone who wears a graveknight's armor for at least 1 hour; **Saving Throw** DC 39 Will save; **Onset** 1 hour; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] and can't remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 2** doomed 2, hampered 10, and can't remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 3** dies and transforms into the armor's graveknight

**Phantom Mount** three actions ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Conjuration]]) The graveknight summons a supernatural mount as per [[Spells/Phantom Steed|_phantom steed_]], heightened to 7th level. The steed has AC 34, Fort +23, Ref +20, Will +20, and 85 Hit Points. If the steed is destroyed, the graveknight must wait 1 hour before using this ability again.

**Ruinous Weapons**   Any weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack the graveknight uses gains the effects of the _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]]_ and [[Equipment/Shock|_greater shock_]] weapon runes.

**Weapon Master**   The graveknight has access to the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effects]] of any weapons they wield.
