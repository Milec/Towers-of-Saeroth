# Water Wisp

Water wisps are swimming spheres of foam and water, perpetually humming with a wash of soft sounds. They're gentle, nurturing, and quick to help anyone in need.

**Resonance** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) 30 feet. All wisps vibrate at a frequency attuned to their element, resonating with and empowering all creatures and effects sharing that trait. Creatures in the area gain a +1 status bonus to attack and damage rolls for effects that have the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait; a creature with the [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]] and water traits gains this bonus to all attack and damage rolls.  
**Accord Essence** ⤾ ([[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]], **Trigger** An ally within 30 feet that benefited from the wisp's resonance in the last hour is targeted by an attack) **Effect** The wisp detonates themself in an elemental explosion. This grants [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the wisp's current Hit Points to allies within 30 feet who have benefited from the wisp's resonance in the last hour. These temporary Hit Points last 1 hour. A wisp that uses this reaction is permanently destroyed, and they can be restored by only a [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual or similarly powerful effect. If an ability would prevent the wisp's destruction (for instance, if the wisp is summoned and would merely be [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|dismissed]]), Accord Essence has no effect.
