# Shinigami

Even immortals such as kami aren't immune to death. When a kami or equally powerful entity refuses the call of the Boneyard, a shinigami is summoned to bring these stubborn souls to heel. Exact and swift, shinigami are known to dispense the judgment of death efficiently and dispassionately.

**Pouch of Four Gems**   Each shinigami carries a pouch containing four black sapphires for use with [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]]. Each gem is capable of holding a soul of a maximum level equal to that of the shinigami. If the shinigami dies, the gems are destroyed as well (whether they contain a soul at the time or otherwise), and any souls they contain are released harmlessly to travel to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]].

**Release Soul** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate Requirements One of the black sapphires in the shinigami's pouch contains a soul from a casting of seize soul]]) Effect The shinigami crushes the gem in their hand, sending the soul on to the Boneyard while releasing a wave of powerful energy. All creatures within a 30-foot emanation take 18d6 damage (DC 38 basic Fortitude save); the shinigami chooses whether this damage is vitality damage or void damage. A creature that takes damage from this effect and fails the saving throw is also sickened 2 and drained 1 (or sickened 4 and drained 2 on a critical failure).

**Release Soul** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) **Requirements** One of the black sapphires in the shinigami's pouch contains a soul from a casting of seize soul; **Effect** The shinigami crushes the gem in their hand, sending the soul on to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]] while releasing a wave of powerful energy. A creature that takes damage from this effect and fails the saving throw is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (or sickened 4 and drained 2 on a critical failure).

**Spirit Touch**   A shinigami's Strikes can affect [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures with the effect of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Guardians Of The Cycles Of Life
>
>
>
> Shinigami are responsible for investigating and dealing with widespread phenomena that disrupt the natural flow of life. They might be tasked with solving the issue or be told to wait, observe, and provide minimal assistance. While most shinigami can retain their impartiality during such long vigils, some are far from emotionless and end up wavering toward mercy or cruelty.
