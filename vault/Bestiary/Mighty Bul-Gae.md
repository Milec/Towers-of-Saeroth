**Eclipse Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) 100 feet. Light and shadows morph strangely around the bul-gae. A creature attempting to create a [[Traits/Light|light]] effect within the aura must attempt a DC 5 flat check. On a failure, the attempt fails, the effect is lost, and the DC to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] effects within the aura is increased by 1.  
**Solar Flare** ⤾ ([[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** The bul-gae takes damage; **Effect** The bul-gae releases a solar flare  in a 30-foot emanation. Each creature in the area takes 6d8 fire damage (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature also takes 2d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. After using this ability, the bul-gae's flames cool and they can't use Solar Flare again until after they have used Lunar Burst.

**Speed** 40 feet, fly 30 feet

**Melee**
single action
jaws +32 ([[Traits/Agile]]),
**Damage** 3d8+17 piercing plus 2d6 cold or fire and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Improved Knockdown]]

**Lunar Burst** two actions ([[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The bul-gae releases a blast of otherworldly frost that deals 6d8 cold damage to each creature in a 10-foot emanation (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 2]]. After using this ability, the bul-gae's flames heat up and they can't use Lunar Burst again until after they have used Solar Flare.

**Shadow Leap** single action ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]) **Requirements** The bul-gae is in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]]; **Effect** The bul-gae dives into a shadow and reappears from another shadow within 40 feet.

**Thermal Mastery**   The bul-gae has total control over the powers of cold and fire. Each time they attack with their jaws, they can choose either cold or fire for the additional damage.
