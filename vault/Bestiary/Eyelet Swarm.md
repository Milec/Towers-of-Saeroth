**Send Signal** single action The eyelet sends an emergency signal through its scrying sensor to a linked scrying station (see Witness) up to 10 miles away. This transmits the eyelet's distance and direction from the station at the time of sending.  
**Witness** The eyelet records whatever it perceives, storing it in an embedded, marble-sized sensor. The eyelet can record up to 1 hour of events, starting and stopping across a longer period. Removing the sensor erases its records.  
 An eyelet can transfer everything it has recorded up to 10 miles to its linked scrying station, where the recordings are collected and can be viewed. Doing so empties the sensor so it can be used again. An eyelet can link, or sever its link, to a scrying station as a 10-minute activity with the concentrate trait.

**Swarming Cyclone** single action  Each enemy in the swarm's space takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails this save is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]].

**Unnerving Observation** single action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) The eyelets focus their sensors on the same target. One enemy in the swarm's space takes 6d6 mental damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failed save, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]] (or frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round on a critical failure).
