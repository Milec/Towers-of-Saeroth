**Claim Trophy** single action  Shianshi claims the soul of a creature she killed within the last minute. This works like [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]], except that no black sapphire is required and the soul is turned into a glowing blue light called a soul trophy. Anyone who kills Shianshi can then free the soul from any soul trophy by touching it and speaking the word for “freedom” in any language.

**Huntblade Brutality**   Shianshi's greatsword Strikes have the [[Traits/Death|death]] trait and deal an additional 2d8 damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] creatures.

**Hunter's Leap** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]) **Trigger** Shianshi kills a creature; **Effect** Shianshi teleports up to 30 feet and makes a greatsword Strike; on a hit, the target of the Strike is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 3 on a critical hit).

**Whip Drain** single action ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Requirements** Shianshi has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] with her shadow whip; **Effect** The grabbed creature must succeed at a DC 41 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 2]] (drained 3 on a critical failure). If the creature is already drained, this increases its drained value instead, to a maximum of drained 4.
