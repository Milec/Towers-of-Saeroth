**Drain Bonded Item** free action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** Anoreth's bonded item is on their person; **Effect** Anoreth expends magical energy stored in their bonded item. During the current turn, they can cast one spell they prepared today and already cast, without spending a spell slot. They must still Cast the Spell and meet the spell's other requirements.  
**Scrap Ward** ⤾ **Trigger** A [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]] within 60 feet of Anoreth is damaged; **Effect** Anoreth clenches their fist, magically seizing the raw materials damaged by the triggering [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] and drawing it toward themself. These materials orbit Anoreth in a defensive ward, granting them 10 Temporary Hit points until the end of their next turn.  
**Steady Spellcasting** If a reaction would disrupt Anoreth's spellcasting action, they attempt a DC 15 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]; if they succeed, their action isn't disrupted.
