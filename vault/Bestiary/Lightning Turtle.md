# Lightning Turtle

Lightning turtles have a reputation as kind protectors of those lost or injured at sea. Their unique ability to promote healing by stimulating the body's natural bioelectricity has led to many attempts over the year to domesticate the creatures, but none have been successful.

**Healing Pulse** three actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The lightning turtle releases a pulse of low-intensity electricity from its body to promote healing. This restores 5d8 Hit Points to the turtle and each living ally within 10 feet, including creatures normally immune to electricity. The turtle can't use Healing Pulse again for 1 minute and is temporarily immune to the Healing Pulse of any lightning turtle for 1 minute.

**Sparking Shell** single action  The lightning turtle withdraws into its shell. This increases its AC to 36, but it can't act except to use Shell Shock or reemerge as a single action. While in its shell, the turtle's Shell Shock deals another 4d6 damage and loses the [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]] trait.
