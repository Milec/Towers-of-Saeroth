**Efficient Capture** three actions ([[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** Thea has [[Equipment/Manacles|manacles]] in hand and is adjacent to a creature. **Effect** Thea attempts to bind the creature's wrists or ankles with the manacles. If she succeeds at an attack roll with a +24 modifier against the target's AC, she applies the manacles.

**Precise Debilitations** free action  **Trigger** Thea's Strike hits a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature and deals damage. **Effect** Thea applies one of the following debilitations to the creature, which lasts until the end of her next turn.

- _Brow Gash_ The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]].
- _Deep Cut_ The target takes an additional 2d6 precision damage from Thea's attacks.
- _Hamstring_ The target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds.
- _Low Blow_ The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]].

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   3d6

**Weapon Trick**   When Thea critically succeeds at an attack roll against a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature, she applies the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]] for the weapon she is wielding.
