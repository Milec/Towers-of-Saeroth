# Deathless Acolyte

_[**PFS Note**](PFS.aspx): While the Deathless Acolyte of Urgathoa can be used for the purpose of summoning spells and similar, the rules for Deathless Deity Adjustments may not be used in Organized Play._  
Deathless acolytes typically arise when a deity or their herald expends a minor amount of power to return a low-ranking priest. Some priests are powerful enough in life to return, greatly lessened, of their own volition.

When a creature within the aura would receive [[Traits/Positive|positive]] [[Traits/Healing|healing]], it must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or receive no healing from the effect.  
**Denounce Heretic** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]]) **Trigger** A creature within 60 feet who the deathless acolyte can see and who doesn't worship [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]] casts a divine spell; **Effect** The deathless acolyte attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell using their spell attack modifier and a counteract level of 2. If the deathless acolyte fails to counteract the spell, the caster is temporarily immune to further uses of Denounce Heretic for 24 hours.
