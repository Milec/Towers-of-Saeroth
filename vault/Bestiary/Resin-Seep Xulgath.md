**Resin Crust**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Poison]]) A creature damaged by a resin-seep xulgath's jaws or claw Strike must succeed at a DC 28 Reflex save or be covered with clumps of noxious resin, taking 2d8 poison damage and a –15-foot penalty to its Speeds for 1 minute (on a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 minute). The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] for as long it has a Speed penalty. A creature can spend an Interact action to scrape the resin off, decreasing the Speed penalty by 5 with each such action.

**Resin Spray** two actions ([[Traits/Poison]]) The resin-seep xulgath flexes its pores and emits a 30-foot line of resin that deals 3d12 poison damage and [[Rules/Forced Movement|knocks]] the target 5 feet back (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save; on a success or critical success the target isn't knocked back, and on a critical failure the target is knocked back 10 feet). The resin-seep xulgath can't use Resin Spray again for 1d4 rounds.
