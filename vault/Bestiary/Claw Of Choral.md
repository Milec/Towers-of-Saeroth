**Break Legs!** two actions  The claw makes a maul Strike against an adjacent creature. If it hits, the creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute. As long as this clumsy condition lasts, the creature also takes a –5-foot penalty to its Speeds and has weakness 5 to the claw’s Strikes.

**Breath of the Dragon** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The claw breathes a blast of flame that deals 6d6 fire damage in a 20-foot cone (DC 21 basic Reflex save). The claw can’t use Breath of the Dragon again for 1d4 rounds.

**Stampeding Shove** single action  The claw Shoves a creature, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to their Athletics check if the target is prone. If the Shove succeeds, the target takes 2d10 bludgeoning damage (double damage on a critical success).
