**Kobold Explosives** two actions ([[Traits/Alchemical]], [[Traits/Fire]]) **Cost** 1 batch of infused reagents. **Effect** Hezle quickly cobbles together an explosive bomb and lobs it up to 30 feet. All creatures in a 5-foot burst take 5d6 fire damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Smoky Retreat** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Cost** 1 batch of infused reagents. **Requirements** Hezle must be adjacent to at least one enemy. **Effect** Hezle throws a mix of special powders centered on a corner of her space, creating a plume of smoke filling a 5-foot burst until the end of her turn that causes her to be [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]]. She then immediately [[Actions/Sneak|Sneaks]], but must end her movement in a space that's not adjacent to any enemies. All creatures other than Hezle in the smoke cloud must attempt a DC 26 Fortitude save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].  
**Failure** The creature is sickened 2.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   (precision) 2d6

**Staff Mastery**   Although Hezle does not cast spells, her obsession with fire and long hours of practice allow her to cast spells from her [[Equipment/Staff of Fire|_staff of fire_]] as if she had the spells on her spell list and could cast them. She still must expend charges from the staff normally; when she prepares the staff each morning, she is capable of placing 4 charges in the staff.
