**Death's Chill** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]]) 15 feet. Vikroti is surrounded by an unnatural chill. Creatures that enter or begin their turn within the aura take 5d10 cold damage and are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).  
**Counterspell** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature casts a spell Vikroti has prepared; **Effect** Vikroti expends a prepared spell to counter the triggering creature's casting of that same spell. She loses her spell slot as if she'd cast the triggering spell. Vikroti then attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell.

**Cold Beyond Cold**   Vikroti's hand Strike deals cold damage, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 2]]. A successful DC 44 Fortitude save reduces this to slowed 1 (or negates on a critical success).

**Drain Soul Cage** free action  **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Vikroti taps into her _[soul cage's](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=1545)_ power to cast any [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|arcane]] spell up to the highest level she can cast, even if the spell being cast isn't one of her prepared spells. Her _soul cage_ doesn't need to be present for her to use this ability.

**Steady Spellcasting**   If a reaction would disrupt Vikroti's spellcasting action, Vikroti attempts a DC 15 flat check. On a success, the action isn't disrupted.
