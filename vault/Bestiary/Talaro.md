**Crimson Shroud** single action ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]) **Frequency** five times per day; **Effect** Talaro shrouds himself in a veil of red mist for 1 minute. While the shroud is active, he gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing 7]]. He can Interact with his shroud, swirling it around himself to gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the beginning of his next turn. If he dies while the shroud is active, his entire body vanishes into red mist, leaving behind only his gear.

**Prayer Attack** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) **Requirements** Talaro wields a [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber (Player Core)|sawtooth saber]] in each hand; **Effect** Talaro attempts to [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] an enemy within 30 feet. If the Feint is successful, his next successful Strike with a sawtooth saber in that same turn deals 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to the target. In addition, provided he remains visible to that target and that target remains within 30 feet of him, additional Prayer Attacks against that target automatically cause them to become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against Talaro's attacks until the end of their turn.

**Sneak Attack**   Talaro deals an extra 3d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.

**Surprise Attack**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to Talaro.
