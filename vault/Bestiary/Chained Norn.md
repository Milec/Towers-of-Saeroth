**Fated**   When a creature is subject to a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect from a norn and a [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] effect from any source other than a norn (or vice versa), the norn’s effect automatically [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteracts]] the other effect and then takes place normally, rather than the two effects canceling each other out. If both the fortune and misfortune effect are from a norn, then the two cancel each other out as normal. At the GM’s discretion, powerful entities related to fate or luck, like [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]], [[Setting/Deities/Magdh (Divine Mysteries)|Magdh]], or [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]], can’t have their effects negated by this ability.

**Sever Fate**  ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) When a norn deals void damage with a Strike, she regains 10 Hit Points. The target must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (drained 2 on a critical failure). Further void damage dealt by the norn increases the drained condition value by 1 on a failed save (or by 2 on a critical failure), to a maximum of drained 4.
