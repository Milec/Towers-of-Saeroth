**Nosferatu Vulnerabilities** As [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|Vampire Vulnerabilities]], but without Compulsions. In addition to the typical vampire vulnerabilities, Vikandian is jealous he can't create spawn. A PC who pokes at this shame when they attempt to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] Vikandian gains a +4 circumstance bonus on their [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] check. If they're successful, Vikandian becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for as long as he remains frightened as he sputters in rage.  
**Opportune Riposte** ⤾ **Trigger** An enemy within Vikandian's reach critically fails on a Strike against him; **Effect** Vikandian either makes a melee Strike against the triggering enemy or attempts to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] them of the weapon used for the Strike. If he succeeds, he gains panache.  
**Plagued Coffin Restoration** If he's reduced to 0 HP, Vikandian disperses into an immense number of individual rats that flee in every direction in an attempt to return to his coffin in area **N6**. If even a single rat enters this coffin, he reforms in his body and is [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]]. If his body rests in his coffin for 1 hour, Vikandian gains 1 HP, after which his fast healing begins to function normally. If his coffin doesn't contain plagued grave dirt, he instead needs to rest in his coffin for 1 day before he gains 1 HP and regains his fast healing.

**Bleeding Finisher** single action  **Requirement** Vikandian has panache; **Effect** Vikandian attempts a sword cane Strike. If he hits, the target also takes 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. He then loses his panache and can't use actions that have the attack trait for the rest of his turn.

**Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]) Vikandian transforms into a swarm of rats. He gains a land Speed of 30 feet and a climb Speed of 10 feet, and he becomes Large. Each enemy in his space takes 2d10 piercing damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails the save is exposed to plague of ancients.

**Command Thrall** free action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) **Requirements** One of Vikandian's morlock thralls is present and can hear him; **Effect** Vikandian gives a single command to one of his thralls, which the thrall follows to the best of their ability during their next turn.

**Dominate** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) As [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]].

**Drink Blood** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) As [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]], but when Drinking Blood, Idebilor regains 10 HP and gains panache.

**Plague of Ancients**  ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent (GM Core)|Virulent]]) **Saving Throw** DC 26 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 day; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] (1 day); **Stage 2** drained 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 (1 day); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1, drained 3, and enfeebled 3 (1 day); **Stage 4** doomed 2, drained 3, and enfeebled 3 (1 day); **Stage 5** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] (1 day); **Stage 6** death

**Precise Fencer**   Vikandian deals an additional 3 precision damage with his sword cane, fangs, or claw Strikes.

**Vampiric Panache**   Vikandian gains panache when he successfully uses Opportune Riposte, when he successfully Demoralizes a creature, when he successfully Dominates a creature, and when he Drinks Blood.
