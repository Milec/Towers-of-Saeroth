**Tun State** free action **Trigger** The tardigrade would be reduced to 0 Hit Points or would die due to [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starvation]], [[Rules/Mounted Defenses (Player Core)|suffocation]], or similar environmental causes; **Frequency** once per week; **Requirements** The tardigrade isn't already in tun state; **Effect** The giant tardigrade doesn't die but instead remains at 1 Hit Point and curls into a dry ball, called a tun. While in this tun state, the giant tardigrade is [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], doesn't need to eat, drink, or breathe, and doubles its resistance to 20. It remains in tun state indefinitely until covered in significant amounts of water, at which point it begins to rehydrate, gaining [[Bestiary/Abilities/Regeneration (Monster Core)|regeneration 5]]. The regeneration persists until it reaches maximum Hit Points. The giant tardigrade then exits its tun state.
