# Sunburst Corpse

Warlords and heroes alike often fell their enemies and leave the corpses behind, exposed to the elements. [[Traits/Undead]] tend to rise in the dark of night and shun the cleansing rays of the sun, but this isn't the rule for all undead. The corpse of a particularly evil individual left exposed to the constant beating of the sun might become a sunburst corpse. These undead radiate light and wield sunlight as a weapon. Sunburst corpses initially seek revenge for their deaths but thereafter wander with undirected rage.

**Light Camouflage** The sunburst corpse is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] in areas of natural bright light, such as due to sunlight or [[Equipment/Torch|torches]], even to creatures that can see clearly in bright light. Magical bright light and light from the sunburst corpse's blinding aura don't conceal the sunburst corpse.

**Blinding Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Light|light]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]]) 60 feet. The sunburst corpse sheds bright light. Any creature that starts its turn in the emanation must succeed at a DC 31 Fortitude save. If it fails, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute, and if it critically fails, it's blinded permanently. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune to this effect for 24 hours.  
**Darkness Vulnerability** A sunburst corpse is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] while in areas of magical [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]]. If the level of the magical darkness effect is at least half of the sunburst corpse's level (a 7th-level effect against most sunburst corpses), the sunburst corpse is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] while in the area.

**Nova Burst** three actions ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The sunburst corpse intensifies its light and unleashes a burning blast of energy. It deals 12d8 fire damage to all creatures in a 20-foot emanation (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature that fails its save becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn. The sunburst corpse can't use Nova Burst again for 1d4 rounds, and its blinding aura becomes inactive during this time.

**Residual Light**  ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Occult]]) When a sunburst corpse Strikes or Grapples a creature, some of the corpse's light clings to the creature for a moment. The creature must attempt a DC 34 Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn. On a critical failure, the creature is dazzled for 1 minute. A dazzled creature can use an Interact action to wick off the light and remove the dazzled condition.
