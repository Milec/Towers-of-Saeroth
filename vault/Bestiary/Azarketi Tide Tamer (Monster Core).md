**Hydration** Azarketi must regularly submerge themselves in water to rehydrate their water-acclimated skin. After the first 24 hours outside of water, they gain a –1 status penalty to Fortitude saves as their skin cracks and their gills become painful. After 48 hours, they begin to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocate]] until returned to water.  
**Speaker of the Oceans** An azarketi tide tamer can speak with [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] that have the [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] or [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait.
