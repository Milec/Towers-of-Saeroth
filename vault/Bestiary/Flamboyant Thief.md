# Flamboyant Thief

There's no honor among thieves, but if there were points for style, these thieves would have 10s across the board. Some would say flamboyant thieves value showcasing their skills rather than successfully stealing an item, but what better way to show that you're the best than with an audience?

**Flamboyant Performance** A flamboyant thief's attempts to [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] don't automatically fail even if a creature is in combat or on guard. While being [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]], the thief gains a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Create a Diversion (Player Core)|Create a Diversion]] or [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] and to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Palm an Object (Player Core)|Palm an Object]] or Steal. However, they are compelled to leave a tangible sign of their presence, such as a calling card or symbol—often in place of a stolen item.  
**Vanishing Act** The flamboyant thief can [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] even without having [[Rules/Cover|cover]] or being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]].

**Dramatic Entrance** free action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Trigger** The flamboyant thief rolls initiative; **Effect** The flamboyant thief draws all eyes to them. They attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check, comparing the result against the Will DC of any number of creatures within 120 feet. Each creature the thief succeeds against is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with the thief until the end of the thief's next turn.  
**I Say When I'm Here** When any [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation (Player Core)|revelation]], or [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|scrying]] magic would reveal the flamboyant thief, the thief becomes aware of it and can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the magic with a counteract rank of 8th level and using their [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] as their counteract modifier.  
**Nimble Dodge ⤾ **Trigger** The thief is targeted with a melee or ranged attack by an attacker it can see** **Effect** The thief gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack.

**Dancing Dagger** two actions  The flamboyant thief can Step, attempt a melee dagger Strike, and attempt a ranged dagger Strike, taking the actions in any order. Both Strikes count toward the thief's multiple attack penalty, but it doesn't increase until after both attacks.

**Dramatic Exit** three actions  The flamboyant thief throws down their smoke ball, then [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hides]], then Sneaks up to three times with a +2 circumstance bonus to their [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks.

**Spectacular Attack**   All the flamboyant thief's Strikes deal an additional 3d6 precision damage or 6d6 if the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with the thief. After the thief Strikes a creature, that creature becomes fascinated with the thief until the end of the thief's next turn.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Calling Cards
>
>
>
> Flamboyant thieves love to build personas for themselves and gain an infamous reputation. Their choice of calling card is the primary way they accomplish this. It must be something unique and difficult to replicate to prevent pretenders. Some calling cards may include the feather of a [[Bestiary/Phoenix (Monster Core)|phoenix]], a rare flower, an intricate clockwork toy, or a custom playing card.
