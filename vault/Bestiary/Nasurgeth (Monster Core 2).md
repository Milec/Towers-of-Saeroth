# Nasurgeth (Nightwave)

Nasurgeths are hungry voids with glowing eyestalks and thousands of teeth. They lurk deep beneath the waves where the sunlight doesn't reach. At night, they ascend to the skies and rain destruction and ruin on the living.

**Midnight Depths** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) 60 feet. A nasurgeth's entropy grows even stronger underwater. All water within the aura is completely dark (as 4th-rank [[Spells/Darkness (Player Core)|_darkness_]]). Magical light with a [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] rank of 4th or lower and magical light cantrips are suppressed. A living creature entering or starting its turn in the aura takes 4d6 void damage, and the creature also takes an additional 2d10 cold damage if it's in water (DC 39 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). If it fails, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 minute and pulled 10 feet toward the nasurgeth.  
**Sunlight Powerlessness** A darvakka caught in sunlight is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 as long as it remains in the sunlight.  
**Spray Black Bile** ⤾ **Trigger** The nasurgeth takes slashing or piercing damage from a critical hit, or a swallowed creature cuts itself free; **Effect** Darkness and death energy spill out from the nasurgeth's wound, dealing 8d8 void damage to creatures in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] with a DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save.
