**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The ghoul gnawer is adjacent to the corpse of a creature that died within the last hour; **Effect** The ghoul gnawer devours a chunk of the corpse and regains 5d6 Hit Points. It can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once.

**Drain Corpse** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** The ghoul gnawer uses Consume Flesh; **Effect** In addition to gaining Hit Points, using Consume Flesh refreshes one spell slot the cultist has already expended.

**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** DC 29 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 3d6 negative damage and regains half as many hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 3d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); Stage 6 dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living, non-[[Traits/Elf|elf]] creature hit by a ghoul gnawer's attack must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. The creature can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**Swift Leap** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The ghoul gnawer jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
