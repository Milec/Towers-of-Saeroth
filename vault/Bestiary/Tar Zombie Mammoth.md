# Tar Zombie Mammoth

Because their massive size makes them difficult for tar oozes to fully digest, tar zombie mammoths tend to resemble their living forms more closely than other tar zombies. However, this doesn't make them any less malignant than smaller tar zombies.

**Fling** single action  **Requirements** The tar zombie mammoth has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by its trunk; **Effect** The tar zombie mammoth whips its prey violently into the air. It attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the grabbed or restrained creature's Fortitude DC. If the creature is restrained, the mammoth gains a +4 circumstance bonus to this check. On a success, the mammoth flings the creature up to 30 feet overhead and up to 30 feet away (the creature takes damage from the fall as normal, up to 15 damage for a 30-foot fall). If the flung creature lands on another creature, the creature it lands on takes the same amount of bludgeoning damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Shambling Trample** two actions  As [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample]] (Large or smaller, foot, DC 27), but the tar zombie mammoth Strides up to its Speed instead of double its Speed.

**Vomit Tar** two actions ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Primal]]) The tar zombie vomits a gout of sticky tar in a 15-foot cone that deals 7d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail the save are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] and gain fire weakness 5. Both conditions end after 3 rounds or when the tar is scrubbed off (which requires 3 Interact actions). The tar zombie can't Vomit Tar again for 1d4 rounds.
