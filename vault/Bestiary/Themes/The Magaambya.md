# The Magaambya  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 130

The Magaambya is an academic institution dedicated to the study of magic, and its members are known for combining magical traditions in unique ways.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Good|good]] trait. Remove the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] trait.
- **1st Level or Higher**: The creature can cast [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] and [[Spells/Sigil|_sigil_]] as innate cantrips. Decide whether these are arcane or primal cantrips when applying the template.
- **4th Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] and [[Magic/Domains/Nature]] to the creature's skills. It gains the [[Feats/Recognize Spell]] skill feat.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature gains either 2nd-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] as an innate arcane spell or 2nd-level [[Spells/Entangle|_entangle_]] as an innate primal spell. The spell can be cast once per day.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Quickened Casting]] wizard class feat.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 7th-level [[Spells/True Seeing|_true seeing_]] once per day as an innate arcane spell and 7th-level [[Spells/Chain Lightning|_chain lightning_]] once per day as an innate primal spell.
