# Knights of Lastwall  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 129

Though their nation of Lastwall has fallen, the Knights of Lastwall remain vigilant against the undead. They are unwavering in their cause and fight with honor.

- **1st Level or Higher**: Remove the creature's melee weapons (if any) from its items. Add a [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]] and [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield|steel shield]] to the creature's items with a Strike at the same attack bonus. It gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Shield Block]] reaction. Add [[Traits/Undead|Undead Lore]] to the creature's skills.
- **4th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Reactive Shield]] fighter feat.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Necromantic Resistance]] feat.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Lastwall Warden]] feat.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast the [[Spells/Champion's Sacrifice|_champion's sacrifice_]] devotion spell once per day as an innate divine spell.
