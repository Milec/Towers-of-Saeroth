# Eagle Knights  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 128

The Eagle Knights are a military group dedicated to fighting tyranny and defending their homeland, Andoran. They risk their lives to fight injustice and champion the rights of the individual.

- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] to the creature's skills.
- **4th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast the [[Feats/Inspire Competence|inspire competence]] and [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]] composition cantrips once per day each as innate divine spells.
- **7th Level or Higher**: When using the [[Actions/Aid]] reaction, if the creature rolls a success, it gets a critical success instead.
- **12th Level or Higher**: Remove one of the creature's reactions (if any) from its available options, and replace it with the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]] reaction.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 9th-level [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]] once per day as an innate divine spell.
