# Aldori Swordlords  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 128

Aldori swordlords are masters of combat trained in the Aldori dueling sword. They value honor above everything.

- **1st Level or Higher**: Remove the creature's melee weapons (if any) from its items. Add the [Aldori dueling sword](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?ID=88) to the creature's items with a Strike at the same bonus as its best melee Strike.
- **4th Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] to the creature's skills. The creature gains the [[Feats/Duelist's Edge]] free action.
- **7th Level or Higher**: Remove one of the creature's reactions (if any) from its available options, and replace it with the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]] reaction.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Unnerving Prowess]] free action.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Saving Slash]] reaction.
