# Red Mantis Assassin  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 130

The Red Mantis are assassins for hire who work under a strict code of rules and requirements. Once they are on the job, these tireless killers relentlessly pursue their targets.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] and [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]] traits. Remove the [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]] and [[Traits/Good|good]] traits.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Remove the creature's melee weapons from its items. Add two [[Equipment/Weapons/Sawtooth Saber|sawtooth sabers]] to the creature's items, and add a sawtooth saber Strike with an attack bonus equal to the creature's highest melee Strike.
- **4th Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] to the creature's skills.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Crimson Shroud]] feat.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Mantis Form]] feat.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 9th-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] once per day as an innate divine spell.
