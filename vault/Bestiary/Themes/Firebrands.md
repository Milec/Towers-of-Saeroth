# Firebrands  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 129

Firebrands tend to be rebellious liberators or boastful daredevils. They seek to draw attention to themselves.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]] trait. Remove the [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]] trait.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] to the creature's skills.
- **4th Level or Higher**: Increase the creature's Speed by 5 feet.
- **7th Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], or [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] to the creature's skills. If you added Deception, add the [[Feats/Confabulator]] skill feat; if you added Diplomacy, add the [[Feats/Glad-Hand]] feat; and if you added Intimidation, add the [[Feats/Battle Cry]] feat.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 3rd-level [[Spells/Heroism|_heroism_]] once per day as an innate occult spell.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Bravo's Determination]] feat.
