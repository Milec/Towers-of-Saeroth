# Aspis Consortium  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 128

Members of the Aspis Consortium are always seeking ways to earn more coin. They are unscrupulous and aggressive in their methods, and put finances above morality.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] trait. Remove the [[Traits/Good|good]] trait.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] to the creature's skills.
- **4th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|rogue's surprise attack class feature]].
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] once per day as an innate arcane spell.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-232)|rogue's deny advantage class feature]].
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains 9th-level [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] as a constant innate arcane spell, which also affects the creature's items.
