# Bellflower Network  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 128

Members of the Bellflower Network fight against slavery where they can, liberating slaves and helping those in need.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]] trait. Remove the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] and [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]] traits.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] to the creature's skills, with a bonus 2 higher when used to [[Actions/Escape]].
- **4th Level or Higher**: Increase the creature's Speed by 5 feet.
- **7th Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] to the creature's skills. It gains the [[Feats/Unfettered Halfling]] ancestry feat, even if it isn't a halfling.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]] once per day as an innate divine spell.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Foil Senses]] and [[Feats/Slippery Secrets]] skill feats.
