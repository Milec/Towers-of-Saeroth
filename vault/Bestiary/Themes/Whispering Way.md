# Whispering Way  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 130

The Whispering Way is a cult of necromancers, nihilists, and undead intent on supporting Tar-Baphon, the Whispering Tyrant. They are zealous in their efforts to further the lich's goals.

- **All Creatures**: Add the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] trait. Remove the [[Traits/Good|good]] trait.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Undead Lore]] to the creature's skills. It can cast [[Spells/Chill Touch|_chill touch_]] as an innate divine cantrip.
- **4th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast the [[Spells/Undeath's Blessing|_undeath's blessing_]] bloodline spell once per day as an innate divine spell.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 2nd-level [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] once per day as an innate divine spell; for every 2 levels it has beyond 7th, _harm_ is heightened another spell level.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Command Undead]] cleric class feat. It gains an additional use of its [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] innate spell that it can use only with Command Undead.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]] ability, which means the creature is damaged by positive effects that damage undead, is healed by negative effects that heal undead, and doesn't benefit from [[Traits/Healing|healing]] effects.
