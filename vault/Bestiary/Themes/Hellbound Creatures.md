# Hellbound Creatures  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core 2]] pg. 98

Sadly, there’s no shortage of people willing to trade all they have for the promise of power. [[Rules/Infernal Contracts|Infernal contracts]] are the most common of such agreements, but powerful devils can offer even greater abilities at the cost of the mortal’s soul and an obligation to advance Hell’s infernal agenda. This mortal becomes a fiend known as a Hellbound creature.

## Creating a Hellbound Creature

 Hellbound is a theme template applied to creatures and NPCs to reflect powers gained through a bargain with Hell. Theme templates provide additional abilities to the base creature based on its level. When you add any template that grants numerous additional abilities, you should strongly consider removing one or more of the creature’s original abilities to compensate, or consider raising the creature’s level by 1 and adjusting its other statistics accordingly.

- **All Creatures**: The creature gains the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait. If the creature had the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait, it loses it. Add the [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]] and [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] traits. The creature gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] to fire equal to its level and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to holy equal to half its level.
- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to the creature's skills. The creature gains [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|greater darkvision]].
- **4th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast 1st-rank [[Spells/Breathe Fire|_breathe fire_]] once per day as a [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|divine]] [[Rules/Innate Spells (GM Core)|innate spell]]; for every 2 levels it has beyond 4th, _breathe fire_ is heightened an additional spell rank.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Rituals/Diabolic Pact|_diabolic pact_]] ritual.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]] once per day as a divine innate spell.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature can cast [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]] once per day as a divine innate spell.
