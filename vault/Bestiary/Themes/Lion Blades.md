# Lion Blades  *Creature Theme Template*

**Source** [[Sources/Character Guide]] pg. 129

The Lion Blades are a secret organization in Taldor, protecting the nation via espionage, intrigue, and infiltration. They creatively use every tool at their disposal.

- **1st Level or Higher**: Add [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] to the creature's skills.
- **4th Level or Higher**: Observers are never granted circumstance bonuses to Perception checks due to the creature [[Actions/Impersonate|Impersonating]] a person of a different ancestry, age, and so on, as long as the disguise is appropriate for its size.
- **7th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Lost in the Crowd]] feat.
- **12th Level or Higher**: The creature gains a +10-foot status bonus to its Speeds.
- **17th Level or Higher**: The creature gains the [[Feats/Flicker]] feat.
