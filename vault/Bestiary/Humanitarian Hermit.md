**Cleansing Earth** three actions ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The humanitarian blesses the land and their allies. In a 30-foot emanation, plants grow and become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Additionally, all allies in the emanation gain 20 temporary Hit Points and can ignore the difficult terrain. These effects last for 1 minute.

**Primal Staff**   A staff wielded by the humanitarian hermit gains the [[Traits/Parry (Player Core)|parry]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]], and [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] traits, and Strikes with it deal an additional 2d8 vitality damage.

**Steady Spellcasting**   If a reaction would [[Rules/Disrupting Actions (Player Core)|disrupt]] the humanitarian hermit's spellcasting action, the hermit attempts a DC 15 flat check. On a success, the action isn't disrupted.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Reincarnation Transitions
>
>
>
> In cases of death from communal enacted injustice, [[Rituals/Reincarnate (Player Core 2)|_reincarnate_]] can give the perished a renewed chance at life, free from the weight of their past and the trappings of their old society. The humanitarian hermit acts as a caretaker during this transition, helping the individual reach out to old friends for emotional healing and closure. If the hermit believes them to be a good candidate, they may help the reincarnated find a new life within their druidic order.
