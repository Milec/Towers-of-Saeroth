**Drain Life**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) When the wight damages a living creature with its claw or _ranseur_ Strike, the wight gains 12 temporary Hit Points and the creature must succeed at a DC 29 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. Further damage dealt by the wight increases the amount of drain by 1 on a failed save, to a maximum of drained 4.

**Wight Spawn**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) A living [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] slain by a wight's claw or _ranseur_ Strike rises as a wight after 1d4 rounds. This wight spawn is under the command of the wight that killed it. It doesn't have drain life or wight spawn and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] for as long as it is a wight spawn. If the creator of the wight spawn dies, the wight spawn becomes a full-fledged, autonomous wight; it regains its free will, gains drain life and wight spawn, and is no longer clumsy
