**Pouncing Pin** two actions  The grave spinosaurus Strides and makes a Strike at the end of its movement. If the Strike hits, it deals damage as normal and the grave spinosaurus makes an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip]] the target. This check counts toward the grave spinosaurus's multiple attack penalty, but the multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until after it makes the check.

**Rip and Tear** single action  **Requirements** The grave spinosaurus has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] in its jaws; **Effect** The grave spinosaurus reaches up, slashes the creature it has grabbed with its claws, dealing 6d10 slashing damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) and 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

**Staggering Sail** two actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation]]) **Requirements** The grave spinosaurus is swimming on the surface of water; **Effect** With a powerful lunge to the side, the grave spinosaurus uses its sail to slap the water's surface, creating a crushing wave that deals 8d6 bludgeoning damage in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the water in the area must attempt a DC 36 Reflex save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes half damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] until the end of its next turn.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 3]].
