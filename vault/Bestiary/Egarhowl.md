**Reaction Reversal** Since an egarhowl appears to move backwards in time and the results of its actions manifest slightly out of sync, its [manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2229) or [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] actions and Strikes don't trigger reactions normally. Whenever a reaction would be triggered by an egarhowl in this way, the reacting creature must attempt a DC 11 flat check. On a failure, their reaction does not trigger.  
**Predestined Defeat** ⤾ ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Trigger** The egarhowl rolls initiative; **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** At the start of combat, an inverted, reversed explosion of spiritual and void energy implodes in a 30-foot emanation centered on the egarhowl—a reversal of the creature's spiritually explosive death. All creatures in the area take 6d8 spirit damage (DC 35 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save); creatures that fail this save are also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for 1 round as time seems to distort around them.

**Rabid Rage**  ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) An egarhowl's bite transmits its supernatural fury. A creature bitten by an egarhowl must attempt a DC 36 Will save. They then become temporarily immune to rabid rage for 24 hours.  
 **Critical Success** The creature suffers no ill effects.  
 **Success** The creature is filled with anger and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for 1 round as they waste some of their time shrieking and raving on their turn.  
 **Failure** As success, but slowed 1 for 1 minute.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for as long as it is slowed.
