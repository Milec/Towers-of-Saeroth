# Banijer Greely

Banijer was born and raised in Maiserene. As a child, he was precocious and willful, spending much of his youth avoiding his lessons and insulting mentor after mentor until, finally, he'd exhausted all goodwill and opportunities for apprenticeship and education in the village. He has the dubious honor of being the first of Maiserene's native locals to serve prison time in the castle's dungeon after he was caught in an ill-advised caper involving smuggling goods. His “partner” in the escapade, the castle's own apothecary, Junth Rundel, betrayed him and sold him out to cover up his own criminal activities. Bitter and disillusioned after being released from prison several months later, Banijer left his home and spent the next several years of his life traveling Ustalav.

 This time away from home was precisely what Banijer needed to find himself. During these years, he had more than a dozen serious (and not-so-serious) flings with men and women alike, but then he met and fell in love with the woman who would become his wife. When she became pregnant, the two decided to return to Maiserene to live a simpler life than the traveling merchant role Banijer had grown into. Upon his return, Banijer was overcome with waves of guilt and nostalgia as, for the first time, he understood the close-knit support and companionship he'd grown up among but had never appreciated.

 Over the next 10 years, Banijer did his best to atone for his rebellious youth. His family grew, and he took over the duties of his family's farm on the east shore of the isle. When the first man he'd apprenticed to died of old age, Banijer was shocked to learn the man had left him the village trading post in his will. Since that day, up until the night of the curse, Banijer spent every day working to bolster the town's prosperity while seeing to it his children were raised with an appreciation for their home.

 Banijer is a middle-aged human man with salt and pepper hair, a wiry frame, and a quirky style that heavily features mismatched colors. He's eager to laugh and joke, but when he's alone, his thoughts turn morose, and his expression takes on a haunted visage. On the night of the curse, Banijer raced back to the farmhouse where his family was hiding, then sacrificed himself in a desperate attempt to lure the monsters away from the home. Yet his death was only the start of his agony. His ultimate fate was perhaps the most tragic of all six of the town's leaders, as Charg seized upon his childhood disdain for local life and forced him to watch from inside the monster the curse had turned him into as it slaughtered his family. Banijer remembers this with shame and a quiet fury but never speaks of it aloud.

## Campaign Role

Banijer's role in the campaign is simple—as the local trader, his influence and presence gives the PCs someone they can sell items to and purchase items from. He can also offer spellcasting services for standard prices. He's always eager to talk about any treasures the PCs find while exploring, and once both he and Aron Mordimus physically manifest, the two swiftly rekindle their old friendship. This friendship eventually blossoms into a full-fledged romance after Aron helps Banijer come to terms with the cruelty he endured when his family perished.

## Side Quest

 Once the PCs unlock the castle grounds, Banijer tells them of the time he was put in prison and provides them with a rough sketch of areas **E1–E4** and **E6–E7** of the castle dungeon, should they ever find themselves down there (of course, Banijer isn't aware of the current dangers in these areas). As he gives them the map, he pauses, then notes that as he tried to evade capture that fateful night, he lost the silver sickle his father had given him. At the time, he didn't really care about it, but now he misses the sickle; he tells the PCs that if they find themselves down in the dungeon, “that two-faced Junth” likely found it and claimed it as his own. If the PCs find it and return it to him, he's not only grateful, but the gratitude draws upon ambient ectoplasm to transform the sickle into a _+1 striking [[Equipment/Extending (GM Core)|extending]] [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sickle (Player Core)|sickle]]_	.Shocked, Banijer offers the sickle to the PCs as a reward. If they refuse, he's grateful and thereafter provides his spellcasting services to them at no cost.
