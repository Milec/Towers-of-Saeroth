**Domain Spells** 3 Focus Points, DC 40, attack +32; **9th** see false divinity

**False Divinity**   The advodaza chooses two domains, typically chosen from the following list: [[Magic/Domains/Ambition (Player Core)|ambition]], [[Magic/Domains/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Magic/Domains/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Magic/Domains/Might (Player Core)|might]], [[Magic/Domains/Trickery (Player Core)|trickery]], [[Magic/Domains/Tyranny (Player Core)|tyranny]], [[Magic/Domains/Wealth (Player Core)|wealth]], or [[Magic/Domains/Zeal (Player Core)|zeal]]. The advodaza gains both domains' initial domain spells as well as the advanced domain spell from one of the domains, all heightened to 9th rank.

**Infernal Mark** three actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) The advodaza touches a willing creature and marks it with a glyph that functions as a 9th-rank [[Spells/Sigil (Player Core)|_sigil_]], which the devil can make visible or invisible. The target gains 1 Focus Point if it didn’t already have any, and the advodaza grants it one of their initial domain spells as a focus spell. The marked creature and the advodaza can communicate telepathically with each other while on the same plane. The advodaza can maintain up to 10 Infernal Marks at a time; if they create another after reaching this limit, the oldest mark is Dismissed. The advodaza can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] any number of Infernal Marks with a single action.

**Punish Disciple** single action ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The advodaza chooses up to three creatures bearing their Infernal Mark that are on the same plane as the advodaza, dealing 8d6 spirit damage to each creature (DC 40 basic Fortitude save).

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Apotheosis Aspirations
>
>
>
> The advodazas outside Hell often fill the vacancy left by a dead or missing god. They masquerade as the lost deity, tempting ex-priests with a new patron and ultimately corrupting their new cult. The more followers an advodaza commands, the more their divine power grows. Large cults might allow an advodaza to access an additional domain—or, in exceptional cases, even grant divine spells to worshippers. Hell's scholars have posited that especially powerful advodazas seek to perform a ritual that would sacrifice most of their cult and transform the devil into an [[Setting/Deity Categories/Infernal Dukes|infernal duke]].
