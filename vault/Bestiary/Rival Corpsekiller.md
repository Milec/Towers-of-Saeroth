**Corpsekiller**   A corpsekiller's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures as though etched with a _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch|ghost touch]]_ property rune and deal an additional 2d8 slashing damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures.
