# Ancient Vizier Dragon

Vizier dragons are primarily concerned with the education and cultivation of people. People fascinate vizier dragons in all their riotous, contradictory complexity. Why do some people excel, and others falter and fail? Why does one person become a saint and the next a tyrant? Why do some become legends in their own time, while others labor in ignominy?

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one horns Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges Powerful Utterance whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Dragonspeech** two actions  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** The ancient dragon uses Word of Advice, Word of Caution, and Word of Heroism, though any given ally can only be the target of a single Word.

**Powerful Utterance** two actions  The dragon expresses their power in one of two ways. The dragon can't use Powerful Utterance again for 1d4 rounds.

- **Ardent Proclamation** ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], fire) The dragon breathes a blast of flame in a 50-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] that deals 21d6 fire damage (DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).
- **Chastising Word** ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The dragon chides the targets strictly enough to leave the mind reeling. Each creature within a 50-foot cone must succeed at a DC 42 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 3 for 1 minute (or stupefied 4 on a critical failure).

**Word of Advice** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The dragon is a skilled mentor, and commands its students like a general. An ally within 60 feet of the dragon can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] once and [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] once, in any order. If it hits, the Strike deals an additional 4d6 fire damage.

**Word of Caution** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Wise people listen when a vizier dragon speaks. An ally within 60 feet of the dragon gains [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] to the vizier dragon's Powerful Utterance and [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 20 to all other damage for 1 round.

**Word of Heroism** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Ancient dragons are the teachers of heroes. An ally within 60 feet of the dragon gains a +2 status bonus to attack rolls, Perception checks, saving throws, and skill checks for 1 minute.
