**Flying Attack** two actions  The tournament combatant makes a [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. At any point during the jump, if they're adjacent to an enemy, they can Strike that enemy with a fist or nunchaku Strike, even in midair. The combatant falls to the ground after the Strike. If the distance they fall is no more than the height of their jump, they land upright and take no damage.

**Somersault Attack** single action  The tournament combatant attempts to [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] a target's space. If they succeed on their [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check, the tournament combatant can make a fist or nunchaku Strike against that target while moving through its space.

**Powerful Fists**   The martial artist's fist Strikes don't take penalties when making lethal attacks.

**Work The Crowd** single action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Requirements** The combatant is within 50 feet of at least three spectators; **Effect** With a flashy flurry of moves, the tournament combatant elicits cheers. The tournament combatant is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 minute. They can use the extra action only to Strike or Stride.
