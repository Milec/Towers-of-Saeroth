# Anophilex

The sight of blood is frightening proof of another’s suffering, and when that blood is one’s own, that fear can turn to blinding terror. Such are the horrors that the skittering, constantly bleeding anophilex sahkil revels in. With a glistening, spiderlike body covered in pulsing sacs of blood caught amid tangles of thorny vines, an anophilex’s head is a freshly skinned skull with sharp fangs and patches of long black hair pulled back to tangle amid the thorns of their bodies. As an anophilex moves, their thorns rip and tear at their bloody blisters and bloated organs, yet the bleeding only flows back along their body to form new thorny lengths in a constant recreation that gives off an unsettling, gurgling susurrus.  

An anophilex has little patience for subtlety or manipulation, preferring to skitter through or flap above battlefields, among refugees, or amid any area where the wounded gather, so they can spread fear and drink the wounded’s crimson pain.

**Drain Blood**   When the anophilex uses a jaws strike against a creature that's taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]], they drink the blood. The creature must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (enfeebled 2 on a critical failure). The anophilex becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] on the following round, and can use the extra action to Stride or Strike.

**Skip Between** single action  The sahkil moves from [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] to the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]] or vice-versa. While in the Ethereal Plane, they're unable to affect anything in the Universe, and they cannot be seen by beings or abilities in the Universe, unless these have an ability that can gaze into the Ethereal Plane. While on the Material Plane the inverse is true for anything on the Ethereal Plane. A summoned sahkil can't use Skip Between.

**Spray Vile Blood** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) The anophilex twists their body into a knot, causing their thorns to pierce their body so that unholy blood sprays out in a 30-foot emanation. Non-sahkil creatures in this area take 12d6 spirit damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). The anophilex can't Spray Vile Blood again for 1d4 rounds.
