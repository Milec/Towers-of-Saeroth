# Thanadaemon (Death Daemon)

Also known as the deacons of death, thanadaemons represent death via old age. They are among the most patient daemons, and prefer to bide their time to enact far-reaching, decades-long plans rather than fight (though they are still deadly foes). Like the Horseman of Death, they ply the waters of the River Styx in search of wayward souls. Rarely are thanadaemons seen without a trademark oar (which they wield as a [[Equipment/Weapons/Bo Staff|bo staff]]), a tool they use to navigate the river's muck-ridden channels and turbulent rapids.

**Draining Strike**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) When a thanadaemon damages a living creature with a melee Strike, the creature must succeed at a DC 33 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. Further damage dealt by the thanadaemon increases the drained condition value by 1 on a failed save, to a maximum of drained 4.

**Focus Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The thanadaemon glares at a single creature they can see within 30 feet. If the target wasn't already [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], they must immediately attempt a DC 33 Will save against the thanadaemon's terrifying gaze. If the target was already frightened, they must attempt a DC 33 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1d4 rounds; this second effect has the [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait. After attempting its save, the creature is temporarily immune to this ability until the start of the thanadaemon's next turn.

**Soul Crush** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The thanadaemon has a soul gem; **Effect** The thanadaemon crushes the soul gem in one hand and gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 15]] for 1 minute.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Styx Passage Fees
>
>
>
> Gliding with eerie ease along the murky River Styx, thanadaemons are only too happy to offer mortals passage aboard their dilapidated skiffs—for a price. Gold is typically an acceptable payment for a thanadaemon's services, though these fiends are well-known for altering the details of arrangements after the fact, and may just as likely demand a favor or some esoteric good, such as a soul gem, instead of coin.
