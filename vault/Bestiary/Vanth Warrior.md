**Infuse Weapon**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) A vanth's scythe is their symbol of office and gains a measure of their personal power. This scythe becomes a _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] scythe_ and is treated as if it were [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] while the vanth wields it. A vanth whose scythe is taken or destroyed can infuse a new one with an hour of work.

**Reaping Scythe** two actions  The vanth throws their scythe, which flies in a 60-foot line, dealing 6d8 slashing damage to all creatures in the area (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) and returns to the vanth's hand.

**Spirit Touch**   A vanth's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal 3d6 negative damage to living creatures and 3d6 positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]].

**Vanth's Curse** two actions ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Misfortune]]) **Frequency** three times per day; **Effect** The vanth bestows a curse on a creature by touching it with their scythe. The creature must attempt a DC 34 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** The target is unaffected and is temporarily immune to Vanth's Curse for 24 hours.  
 **Success** The target feels a momentary shudder of doom and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 minute by the distracting sensation.  
 **Failure** The target becomes morose and glum as it accepts its own inevitable fate. For 1 hour, the target is stupefied 3. Each time the target gains the dying condition, the stupefied value increases by 1, to a maximum value of stupefied 6.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but the effect is permanent.
