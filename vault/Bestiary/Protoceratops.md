# Protoceratops

Though it lacks the distinctive horns of its larger relative the [[Bestiary/Triceratops (Monster Core)|triceratops]], the protoceratops is more than capable of holding its own in combat, using its tail to knock enemies to the ground and its sturdy frill to turn away blows.
