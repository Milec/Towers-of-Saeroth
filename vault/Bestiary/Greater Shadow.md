**Shadow Spawn**   When a creature’s shadow is pulled free by Steal Shadow, it becomes a shadow spawn under the command of the greater shadow that created it. This shadow spawn doesn’t have Steal Shadow. If the creature the shadow spawn was pulled from dies, the shadow spawn becomes a full-fledged, autonomous shadow. If the creature recovers from its enfeeblement, its shadow returns to it and the shadow spawn is extinguished.

**Slink in Shadows**   The greater shadow can Hide or end its Sneak in a creature’s or object’s shadow.

**Steal Shadow** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Requirement** The greater shadow hit a living creature with a shadow hand Strike on its previous action. **Effect** The greater shadow pulls at the target’s shadow, making the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] (or [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 3]] on a critical hit). This is cumulative with other [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] conditions from shadows, to a maximum of [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 4]]. If this increases a creature’s [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] value to 3 or more, the target’s shadow is separated from its body (see shadow spawn). [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled]] from Steal Shadow decreases by 1 every hour.
