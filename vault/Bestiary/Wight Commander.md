**Coordinated Strike**   The wight commander flanks with an ally as long as the target is within both their reaches, even if commander and ally aren't on opposite sides.

**Drain Life**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) When the wight commander damages a living creature using an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack or their bound weapon, they gain 12 temporary Hit Points, and the creature must succeed at a DC 29 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. Further damage dealt by the wight commander's unarmed and bound weapon attacks increases the value of the drained condition by 1 on a failed save, to a maximum of drained 4.

**Spawn Wight Soldier**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) A living [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] killed by a wight commander's weapon or claw Strike rises as a different wight spawn of the commander's level or lower (most often a [[Bestiary/Hunter Wight|hunter wight spawn]] or [[Bestiary/Prowler Wight|prowler wight spawn]]) after 1d4 rounds. This wight spawn is under the command of the wight commander that killed them. They don't have drain life or spawn wight and are [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]] for as long as they're a wight spawn. If the creator of the wight spawn dies, the wight spawn becomes a fully autonomous wight; they regain their free will, gain drain life and spawn wight, and are no longer clumsy.

**Tactical Direction** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory]]) Each wight ally in a 30-foot emanation gains the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]] reaction until the end of their next turn.
