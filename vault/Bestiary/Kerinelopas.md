**Mythic Power**   3 [[Rules/Mythic Points]]  
 • Remove Condition single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Cost** 1 _Mythic Point_; **Effect** Kerinelopas ends one condition affecting him.

**Dab** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Requirements** Kerinelopas is within reach of an enemy taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]; **Effect** Kerinelopas touches the creature and applies blood to one of his four claws; the blood remains fresh for 1 minute. The target must succeed at a DC 28 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] with Kerinelopas.

**Paint** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Requirements** Kerinelopas has fresh blood applied to a claw using Dab; Effect Kerinelopas expends the blood on one claw to paint an illusion with the effects of one of the following spells: [[Spells/Blur (Player Core)|_blur_]], [[Spells/Illusory Creature (Player Core)|_illusory creature_]], [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]], or [[Spells/Illusory Object (Player Core)|_illusory object_]]. The Paint action gains the traits of the spell it's reproducing, and Kerinelopas can [[Actions/Sustain]] a Spell on these effects. He uses a spell attack modifier of +20 and DC 28 for these effects, which are heightened to 5th rank.  
If he has fresh blood applied to two or more claws, Kerinelopas can expend the blood on all of them to instead produce the effects of [[Spells/Cloak of Colors (Player Core 2)|_cloak of colors_]] or [[Spells/Vibrant Pattern (Player Core)|_vibrant pattern_]].  
Any effects produced by this ability have a +2 status bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, skill checks, and AC against the creature whose blood was used to Paint, and that creature takes a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Perception (rules-3303)|Perception]] checks and saves against it.
