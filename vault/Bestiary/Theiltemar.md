**Rusting Death** When Theiltemar is destroyed, his bones explode as the necromantic energy holding him together is released. This shreds his rusted chain mail as well, flinging rusty shards of jagged metal in all directions. Adjacent creatures take 2d6 piercing damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) and are exposed to [[Rules/Diseases/Rust Creep|rust creep]]. As his remains settled to the floor, the [[Spells/Wall of Force|_wall of force_]] blocking area **F14** flickers and fades.
