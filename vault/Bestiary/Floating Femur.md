**Gotcha!**   A floating femur freezes up if [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]. For 1 minute after the femur becomes grabbed or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by a creature, the femur can't act, and the creature holding it can use it as a _+1 [[Equipment/Weapons/Club|club]]_ with the [[Traits/Fatal|fatal d10]] trait. After that time, the floating femur can act and is generally quite aggressive toward whomever used it as a weapon.
