**Quick Capture** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature hits or touches the flytrap; **Effect** The flytrap makes a leaf Strike against the triggering creature. If it hits, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] in that leaf.

**Damage** 1d8+2 piercing plus 1d6 acid and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Improved Grab, Improved Knockdown, or Improved Push|Improved Grab]]

**Focused Assault** two actions  The flytrap attacks a single target with both its two leaves. The flytrap makes one leaf Strike. On a success, the flytrap deals the damage from one leaf Strike plus an additional 1d8 damage for every leaf beyond the first. On a failure, the flytrap deals the damage from one leaf Strike, but it can't use Improved Grab. It deals no damage on a critical failure. This counts toward the flytrap's multiple attack penalty as a number of attacks equal to the number of leaves the flytrap has.

**Hungry Flurry** two actions  The flytrap makes two leaf Strikes at a –2 penalty, each against a different target. These attacks count toward the flytrap's multiple attack penalty, but the multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until after it makes all its attacks.
