**Painted Replica** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) **Requirements** A creature died while engulfed by the painted landscape; **Effect** The painted landscape absorbs the creature, causing the creature's likeness to appear in the mural. Any gear carried by the dead creature remains left behind (but still appears as part of the painting). The painted landscape regains 2d6+20 HP when it absorbs a creature.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Engulf (Monster Core)|Engulf]]** three actions  DC 29, 6d6 bludgeoning, [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 29, Rupture 20

**Sticky Paint**   A painted landscape's Strikes splatter paint on the target. On a critical hit, this paint swiftly dries, and the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 10 minutes. As an [Interact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3259) action, the target or an adjacent creature can remove enough paint to end the condition.

**Mural** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) Until the next time it acts, the animated landscape appears to be a mural on a flat surface that's at least 10 by 20 feet in area. It has an automatic result of 42 on [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks and DCs to pass as a mural.
