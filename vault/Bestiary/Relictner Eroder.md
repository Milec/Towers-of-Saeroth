# Relictner

Regardless of age, relictners look like ancient [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarves]] with deep wrinkles, sparse white hair, and decrepit forms. As their bodies degrade, so too do their surroundings, with even the finest architecture crumbling to ruins.

([[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]]): DC 35

**Lair Sense** ([[Traits/Detection|detection]], [[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]]) The relictner bonds with a single structure they consider their lair, such as a house, temple, or ruin. A relictner is aware of any creature that enters the lair, and the direction and approximate distance to the creature. If the lair is destroyed, the relictner can bond with a new lair by residing within a structure they own or claim for 1 week.

**Crumble** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The relictner erodes an object within 30 feet, dealing 3d10 bludgeoning damage to it, with a DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save if the object is attended.

**Death Gasp** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) The relictner draws in a deep breath and holds it, temporarily suspending their biological processes and becoming undead. The relictner gains the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] trait and becomes immune to bleed, [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]]. Any such effects the relictner is currently inflicted with are suspended but take effect again once they take a breath. Death Gasp lasts as long as the relictner [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|holds their breath]] (up to 8 rounds).

**Demolition** three actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) The relictner bellows in fury, causing the surrounding structure to collapse. All creatures in a 30-foot emanation take 8d10 bludgeoning damage from falling debris (DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails its save is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The area becomes difficult terrain. The relictner can't use Demolition again for 1d4 rounds.
