This doesn't affect their statistics, but it could change the damage type of their Strikes.

**Hurricane Blast** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The jaathoom moves all creatures without the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] trait in their turbulent skies aura 20 feet directly away, clockwise, or counterclockwise. A creature avoids being moved if it succeeds at a DC 21 Fortitude save.

**Ominous Dreams** two actions ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Prediction (Player Core)|Prediction]]) The jaathoom sends a prophetic dream to a sleeping creature within 10 feet. An unwilling creature avoids the vision if it succeeds at a DC 23 Will save. The jaathoom chooses the dream's subject, but not its exact events. The target sees a brief vision of its future related to that subject, with the effect of [[Spells/Augury (Player Core)|_augury_]]. If the result is bad or mixed, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]] and can't recover from being frightened until it wakes.
