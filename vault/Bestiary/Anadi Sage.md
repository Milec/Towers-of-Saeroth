When in spider form, they can't use weapons.

**Spin Silk**  ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]) By spending several minutes, an anadi can produce silk to craft items made of cloth. A single anadi can produce enough silk in a day to craft a single garment.
