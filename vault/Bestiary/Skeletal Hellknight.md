**Command Skeletons**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) The skeletal Hellknight can direct the actions of the nearby [[Bestiary/Skeleton Guard|skeleton guards]], commanding them to perform tactics such as flanking foes and focusing their attacks on specific targets. As long as the skeletal Hellknight is active and commanding the skeletons, they gain the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Attack of Opportunity]] reaction.

**Invoke Reckoning** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]) **Trigger** The skeletal Hellknight hits a foe with a Strike **Effect** The skeletal Hellknight causes the creature struck to endure the pain of an Order of the Nail's reckoning—a rite of mortification Hellknights of that order use to focus their minds. The creature struck feels as if nails are being driven into their body, and must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] from the pain. On a critical failure, the pain manifests as actual puncture wounds, dealing an additional 2d6 points of piercing damage to the victim.
