**Death from Above**   The rigger deals an additional 1d4 precision damage to any creature at a lower elevation than themself.

**Practiced Climber**   The rigger requires only one hand free to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] and is not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] when Climbing.

**Rope Tension Spring** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]) **Requirements** The rigger is adjacent to a vertical rope on board a ship and is wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]]; **Effect** The rigger loops the rope around one arm and severs the rope with their dagger. Counterweight and tension pull the rigger 20 feet straight up.
