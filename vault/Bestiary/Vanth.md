# Vanth

Protecting the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] are the stern and resolute guardians of the dead known as vanths, psychopomps who resemble skeletons with raven-like wings and a mask resembling a vulture's skull. Vanths carry black scythes to fight against those who would disturb the natural progression of souls, and they consider any visitor to the Boneyard a potential troublemaker. When the psychopomp armies go to war, vanths serve as front-line soldiers. They rarely speak and even more rarely show any emotion other than a grim adherence to duty.

**Infuse Weapon**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) A vanth's scythe is its symbol of office and gains a measure of its personal power. This scythe becomes a _+1 scythe_ and is treated as if it were [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]] while the vanth wields it. A vanth whose scythe is taken or destroyed can infuse a new one with an hour of work.

**Spirit Touch**   An vanth's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal 2d6 negative damage to living creatures and 2d6 positive damage to undead.

**Vanth's Curse** two actions ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Misfortune]]) **Frequency** three times per day; **Effect** The vanth bestows a curse on a creature by touching it with its scythe. The creature must attempt a DC 25 Will save.  
**Critical Success** The target is unaffected and is temporarily immune to Vanth's Curse for 24 hours.  
**Success** The target feels a momentary shudder of doom and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute by the distracting sensation.  
**Failure** The target becomes morose and glum as it accepts its own inevitable fate. For 1 hour, the target is stupefied 2. Each time the target gains the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition, the stupefied condition value increases by 1, to a maximum value of stupefied 4.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the effect is permanent.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Vanth Scythes
>
>
>
> Vanths' favored weapon is the [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe|scythe]], a choice that even further adds to their fearsome appearance and can lead to unfortunate associations with the [[Bestiary/Grim Reaper]] or [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa]] among more superstitious mortals. Vanths find such comparisons to be ignorant at best or insulting at worst. Some vanths infuse their scythes with different qualities, such as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]], depending on the nature of their most common enemies.
