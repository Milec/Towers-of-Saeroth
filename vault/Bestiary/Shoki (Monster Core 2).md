# Shoki

Shokis shepherd wayward souls to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]]. They take great pride in traveling to [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] (and sometimes beyond) to hunt down those who have evaded [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma's]] judgment, whether they be malicious foes or unfortunate [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. A shoki is often transported closer to their quarry by a more powerful psychopomp, but they have the ability to return once they've completed their mission.

 A shoki resembles an aged humanoid with jet-black skin, curling ram's horns, and a large snail's shell on their back. Some believe that a shoki keeps souls within that shell for transport back to the Boneyard, while in reality, that power resides in their infused staff.

**Infuse Staff**  ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) A shoki's staff becomes a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking (GM Core)|striking]] staff_ and is treated as if it were [[Equipment/Wand of Smoldering Fireballs|adamantine]] while the shoki wields it. A shoki's staff has Hardness 14 and HP 56 (BT 28) while possessed by the shoki and Hardness 5 and HP 20 (BT 10) while out of the shoki's possession. A shoki whose staff is taken or destroyed can infuse a new one with an hour of work.

**Soul Lock** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]]) **Requirements** The shoki doesn't have a soul locked within their staff; **Effect** The shoki attempts to capture the soul of a creature on the brink of death: an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature, a creature with the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition, or a creature that died within the last minute. The target must attempt a DC 32 Will save with the following results.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune to Soul Lock.  
 **Success** The shoki's staff tugs at the creature's soul but doesn't trap it. If the creature is living, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed 1]] (or increases its doomed condition by 1). If the creature is a corporeal undead, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]]. If the creature is an [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] undead, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]]. The creature then becomes temporarily immune to Soul Lock for 24 hours.  
 **Failure** The shoki captures the creature's soul in its staff. If the creature is living, it dies. If the creature is a corporeal undead, its body becomes an inanimate corpse. While the soul is locked in the staff, the target can't be returned to life or undeath or rejuvenate through any means, save for powerful magic, such a [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual. If the shoki's staff is destroyed or the shoki wills it, the soul is released. A shoki's staff can only hold one soul at a time.

**Shepherd's Touch**   A psychopomp's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal 2d6 void damage to living creatures and 2d6 vitality damage to [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]].
