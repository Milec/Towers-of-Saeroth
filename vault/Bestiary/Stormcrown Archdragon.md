# Stormcrown Archdragon

Heralds of—and heralded by—the primal forces of thunder and lightning, stormcrown dragons meld the majesty of fierce storms and mighty dragons. Driven by emotion and curiosity, these mercurial dragons can change from friend to foe or threatening to thoughtful in mere moments.

**Command Lightning** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Requirements** The dragon's last action was to use Draconic Frenzy or [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]] with the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] or [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] trait and they're in an area of stormy weather; **Effect** The dragon directs a bolt of lightning at a creature within 100 feet, dealing 5d12 electricity damage with a DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save

**Coronal Cloak**   When the dragon spends an action to Hover while in stormy weather, they become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until they [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] or until the beginning of their next turn, whichever comes first.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges Galvanic Barrage whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Furious Cloudburst** three actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** The dragon must be outdoors and aboveground; **Effect** The dragon exhales a massive bolt of lightning directly upward to create a terrible storm centered on the dragon. [[Rules/Wind (GM Core)|Strong winds]] blow in a 120- foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that lasts for 10 minutes; this creates [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|greater difficult terrain]] for both flying creatures and creatures on the ground. Creatures in the area except for the dragon take a –2 circumstance penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] checks and a –4 circumstance penalty to ranged attacks due to the wind and rain. This counts as stormy weather for the dragon's Command Lightning and Coronal Cloak, and the dragon can Command Lightning once per turn as a single action (instead of a free action that requires using Draconic Frenzy or Casting a Spell).

**Galvanic Barrage** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon exhales a massive bolt of lightning at one creature within 100 feet, dealing 12d12 electricity damage with a DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The lightning then arcs to a creature of the dragon's choice within 60 feet of the previous target with the same effect. The dragon can't target the same creature more than once, and they must have [[Rules/Line of Effect (rules-2382)|line of effect]] to all targets. The bolt can continue to arc this way until the dragon runs out of targets or until a target critically succeeds at their save. The dragon can't use Galvanic Barrage again for 1d4 rounds.

**Smooth Flying**   The stormcrown dragon doesn't fall if they don't [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] during their turn, and they're unaffected by [[Rules/Wind (GM Core)|strong winds]] or similar effects unless they choose to be.

**Storm Howl**   Whenever a creature critically fails a save against one of the stormcrown dragon's abilities that deals electricity damage, that creature is also permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]].
