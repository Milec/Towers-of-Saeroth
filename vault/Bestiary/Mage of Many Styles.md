# Mage of Many Styles

The mage of many styles combines magical and martial tactics. They try to hamper foes with [[Spells/Black Tentacles|_black tentacles_]] or [[Spells/Confusion|_confusion_]], switching to damaging spells like [[Spells/Lightning Bolt|_lightning bolt_]] once their opponents are properly lined up. When engaged in melee, they make liberal use of [[Spells/Shocking Grasp|_shocking grasp_]] and [[Spells/True Strike|_true strike_]] to deal powerful blows.

**Spell Punch** free action ([[Traits/Flourish]]) **Trigger** The mage of many styles begins to Cast a Spell that targets at least 1 creature; **Effect** The mage channels the spell through their fist. The mage makes a fist Strike against a target within reach. If the Strike is successful, the target is subjected to the spell. If the spell required an attack roll, the target is hit automatically upon a successful Strike. If the spell required a saving throw, the target takes a –2 status penalty to its saving throw. If the spell could target multiple creatures, it targets only the creature the mage hit with their fist Strike.
