**Sticky Feet**   Giant frogs are not [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] when [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balancing]] on a narrow surface, and they gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Reflex saves to avoid [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falling]].

**Tongue Grab**   A creature hit by the giant frog's tongue becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the giant frog. The creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the frog's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue with a Strike against AC 13 that deals at least 2 slashing damage. This deals no damage to the frog but prevents it from using its tongue Strike until it regrows its tongue, which takes a week.
