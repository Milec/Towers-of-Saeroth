**Smoking Wound**   A creature damaged by the vavakia's jaws is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] from the rank smell of vapors from the wound.

**Soulfire Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]) The vavakia exhales ghostly green fire in a 60-foot cone. Living creatures in the area take 20d6 negative damage (DC 42 basic Reflex save). A good creature that fails this save is becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 minute (stupefied 4 on a critical failure). A creature slain by Soulfire Breath can't be resurrected except by powerful magic such as a [[Spells/Wish|_wish_]] spell. The vavakia can't use Soulfire Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Soulfire Inhalation** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Requirements** The vavakia's last action was Soulfire Breath; **Effect** The vavakia inhales and regains 10 Hit Points for each creature damaged by its Soulfire Breath. Any excess healing is gained as temporary Hit Points, which remain for 1 minute.

**Stunning Tail**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]]) If the vavakia critically hits with its tail Strike, the target must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] for 1 round (1d4 rounds on a critical failure).

> Vavakias carve out tyrannical empires throughout the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]], particularly in steaming jungles and fetid swamps. They enjoy coming to the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]] to feed on souls and spread devastation.
