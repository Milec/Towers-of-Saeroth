**Living Machine** Though their body is an organic construct, a wyrwood is a living creature. They're not immediately destroyed when reduced to 0 HP, but rather fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and eventually die. They don't need to eat or drink. They can be targeted by effects that target living creatures or that target constructs.  
**No Breath** A wyrwood doesn't breathe and is immune to effects that require breathing (such as an inhaled poison).
