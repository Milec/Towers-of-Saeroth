**Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) Jaul changes into his humanoid, hybrid, or animal shape. Each shape has a specific, persistent appearance. Jaul's natural form is its hybrid shape. In humanoid shape, Jaul uses its original humanoid size, loses his jaws and claws Strikes, and gains a melee fist Strike that deals bludgeoning damage equal to the slashing damage dealt by its claw. In animal shape, his Speed and size change to that of the animal, it gains any special Strike effects of the animal that it didn't already have (such as [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]]), and it loses his weapon Strikes. human with fist +16 for 1d6+7 bludgeoning, or wolf with Speed 40 feet and jaws with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Knockdown (Rage of Elements)|Knockdown]].

**Curse of the Werewolf**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) This curse only affects [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoids]]; **Saving Throw** DC 23 Fortitude. On each full moon, the cursed creature must succeed at another Fortitude save or turn into a werewolf until dawn. The creature is under the GM's control and goes on a rampage for half the night before falling unconscious at dawn.

**Moon Frenzy**  ([[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) When a full moon appears in the night sky, Jaul must enter his hybrid form, can't Change Shape thereafter, becomes Large, increases his reach by 5 feet, and increases the damage of his jaws by 2. When the moon sets or the sun rises, Jaul returns to humanoid form and is fatigued for 2d4 hours.

**Wolf Coordination**   Jaul's Strikes deal 1d6 extra damage to creatures within his wolf's reach.
