# Rocketeer

It takes a very specific personality to strap on a tank filled with a highly flammable alchemical substance and set it afire to launch oneself into the sky, so it is perhaps unsurprising that most rocketeers are reckless and bombastic individuals who delight in the theatrics inherent in their craft. While the unpredictability of rocketeering devices and the high casualty rate among those who use them make such devices generally unsuitable for military applications, a few courageous souls have used them to become dashing folk heroes or performing daredevils, many of whose most memorable performances culminate in their own dramatic demises.

**Fuel Tank Vulnerability** When the rocketeer is struck by a critical hit that deals piercing or fire damage, they must attempt a DC 5 flat check. On a failure, the rocketeer's fuel tank explodes, dealing 6d6 fire damage to the rocketeer and all creatures in a 20-foot emanation and knocking the rocketeer [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. The rocketeer loses their fly Speed and can't use Explosive Liftoff, Mid-air Collision, or Rocketing Strafe until they repair their jet pack, which requires an appropriate set of [[Equipment/Artisan's Tools|artisan's tools]] and takes 2 hours.

**Explosive Liftoff** two actions  **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Requirements** The rocketeer is standing on a horizontal surface; **Effect** The rocketeer unleashes the full strength of their jets to launch themself into the air, dealing 7d6 fire and bludgeoning damage to all creatures in a 15-foot emanation with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The rocketeer [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] twice, straight up into the air.

**Mid-air Collision** two actions  The rocketeer [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] twice, then attempts to [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]] or [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] another flying creature. If they roll a success on the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check, they get a critical success instead.

**Rocketing Strafe** two actions  The rocketeer [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] and makes two melee Strikes at any point during that movement. Each Strike must target a different creature. The rocketeer can forgo the melee Strikes to instead make one slide pistol Strike at any point during that movement and Interact to select the next loaded chamber of their slide pistol; they can do these in either order. Any Strike made as part of a Rocketing Strafe deals an additional 2d6 damage and takes the normal multiple attack penalty.
