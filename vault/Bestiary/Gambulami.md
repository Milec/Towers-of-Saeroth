**Inspired Feast**  ([[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Human]]) If a biloko feasts on a [[Ancestries/Human|human]] corpse for 1 minute or more, they gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and a +2 status bonus to damage rolls for 1 hour.

**Size Alteration** two actions ([[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Gambulami is affected by a 4th-level [[Spells/Enlarge|_enlarge_]] spell. This lasts for 1 minute, and Gambulami can Dismiss the Spell.

**Thrashing Chomp** two actions  **Requirements** Gambulami is larger than Small in size and has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] with his jaws; **Effect** Gambulami whips the creature around, smashing it against other nearby creatures. He makes an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the grabbed creature's Fortitude DC. Regardless of the result, the creature is released from Gambulami's jaws afterward, escaping into the space in which it was grabbed.  
**Critical Success** Gambulami uses a mighty spin to smash the held creature against every creature within his reach. Every creature, including the grabbed creature, takes 1d10+7 bludgeoning damage.  
**Success** As critical success, but creatures take 1d10 bludgeoning damage.  
**Failure** The creature breaks free.  
**Critical Failure** The creature breaks free and Gambulami's spin causes him to fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
