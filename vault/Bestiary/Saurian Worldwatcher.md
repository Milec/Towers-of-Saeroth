**Boneshaking Roar** two actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]) The saurian worldwatcher bellows, evoking the power of the primordial world. Non-[[Traits/Dinosaur|dinosaur]] creatures within 30 feet take 10d6 sonic damage (DC 38 basic Fortitude save). On a failed save, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 2]] for 1 round (stunned 1d4 on a critical failure). The saurian can't use Boneshaking Roar again for 1d4 rounds.

**Stunning Blow**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]]) Whenever the worldwatcher hits a creature with their fist or naginata Strike, that creature must succeed at a DC 39 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (stunned 3 on a critical hit).
