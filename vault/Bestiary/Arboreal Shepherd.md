# Arboreal Shepherd

Shepherds develop an eccentric fascination with local fauna. These shepherds eagerly bear fruit to feed their friends, provide roosts for bats, and invite beasts to gnaw nesting niches directly into their trunks.

**Exalt the Little Ones** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The arboreal shepherd encourages its friends to overcome titanic odds. For 1 round, allies with the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait in a 50-foot radius gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws. If the animals have the [[Magic/Domains/Swarm (Divine Mysteries)|swarm]] trait, the saving throw DCs to resist their damaging actions gain a +1 status bonus. This bonus increases to +2 for allies whose level is 2 or lower.

**Healing Haven** single action ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) **Frequency** Once per hour; **Effect** The arboreal shepherd scoops up a willing Medium or smaller [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] (or animal with the [[Magic/Domains/Swarm (Divine Mysteries)|swarm]] trait) within its reach, sequestering the animal into a recess in the arboreal's trunk. While sequestered, the animal cannot act, gains [[Rules/Cover|greater cover]], and gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing 5]]. At the beginning of its turn after regaining Hit Points, the animal can choose to exit by reappearing in any space adjacent to the arboreal as a free action.
