# Spitting Sawfly

Spitting sawflies are enormous, bloated insects covered in tumor-like knots of spores. These ravenous pests sow disease and spores wherever they go, heralding the infestation of creeping cones.

**Consume Essence** single action ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** The spitting sawfly is adjacent to a creature with the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait or to a coiled conifer; **Effect** The sawfly feasts upon the targeted creature or the coiled conifer's sap, regaining 5d6 Hit Points. **Creeping Sprout**  ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]) A creature afflicted with creeping sprout is treated by the spitting sawfly as if it has the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait; **Saving Throw** DC 29 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1d4 days); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] (1d4 days); **Stage 3** 12d6 acid damage, creeping cone swarm emerges (disease ends)

**Plant Spores** single action  The spitting sawfly plants spores in an adjacent creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], exposing it to creeping sprout disease.
