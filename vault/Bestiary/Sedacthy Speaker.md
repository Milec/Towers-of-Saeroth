# Sedacthy Speaker

High-ranking sedacthies are expected to both plan campaigns and wade into battle. These speakers achieve their rank by accumulating several large servants or a single massive creature like a [[Bestiary/Megalodon|megalodon]].

**Exploit Weakness**   The speaker's Strikes deal 1d6 additional damage to creatures that are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]].

**Painful Cry** two actions ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]) The sedacthy shrieks across a range of painfully high tones, dealing 3d6 sonic damage and 1d6 mental damage to all creatures in a 30-foot cone, with a DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].

**Shared Feast** two actions  The sedacthy makes a jaws Strike. If it hits, an ally of their choice can spend a reaction to make a jaws Strike against the same target. Allies with beaks or similar attacks can use those instead of jaws.

**Swim Together** two actions  **Requirements** The speaker is adjacent to an [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] ally; **Effect** The speaker and the animal both [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], ending their movement adjacent to one another.
