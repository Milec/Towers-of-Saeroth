# Smoke Creeper

Smoke creepers shift and modify their appearance as they float through the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Air (GM Core)|Plane of Air]], but they prefer to take on a vaguely insectile form with wings trailing choking fumes and glowing red eyes.

**Damage** 2d8+3 piercing plus 1d6 poison

**Choking Swoop** three actions  The smoke creeper [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] up to its Speed, moving through the spaces of other creatures and leaving traces of itself behind. Each breathing creature it passes through must attempt a DC 23 Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature inhales part of the elemental and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 minute by the pain of the smoke rasping in its throat and lungs. A creature can attempt to end this condition by spending an action coughing and succeeding at a DC 23 Fortitude save.

**Painful Exhalations** two actions  **Requirements** At least one creature within 40 feet is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] from the smoke creeper's Choking Swoop; **Effect** The smoke creeper flaps its wings, violently drawing the lingering smoke free from all creatures immobilized from its Choking Swoop within 40 feet. Each target must attempt a DC 23 Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 minute and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (enfeebled 2 and sickened 2 on a critical failure). Regardless of the result, the creature is no longer immobilized from the smoke creeper's Choking Swoop.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Air And Smoke
>
>
>
> Elementals of smoke, such as the smoke creeper, are often cruel in their attacks on breathing creatures, causing them to choke and cough from their noxious fumes. Some believe that they hold secret allegiances to [[Setting/Deities/Ymeri (Divine Mysteries)|Ymeri]], who holds domain over fire and smoke.
