**Susceptible to Death** Though phantoms aren't alive, neither are they undead, and they are uniquely vulnerable to the effects of death. A phantom whose Hit Points are reduced to 0 as a result of a [[Rules/Death Effects|death effect]] (such as from a spell like [[Spells/Execute|_execute_]]) is immediately whisked away to the River of Souls, where their soul resumes the usual path to the afterlife.  
**Vulnerable to Kindness** Any creature within 30 feet that the phantom can see and hear can issue a calm and friendly greeting to it as a two-action activity with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits. A creature greeting the phantom in this manner must attempt a DC 18 [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check.  
**Critical Success** The phantom takes 4d8 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  
**Success** The phantom takes 2d8 mental damage.  
**Failure** The phantom takes 1d8 mental damage.  
**Critical Failure** The phantom is unaffected, and is not affected by this weakness for 1 round.
