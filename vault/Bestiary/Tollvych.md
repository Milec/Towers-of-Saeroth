**Bone-Shattering Cry** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]) Tollvych cries out in pain as his body twists and grinds into horrific, distorted shapes. All creatures within a 30-foot emanation take 16d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) as the sound of the Bone-Shattering Cry shakes and shatters their own bones. Tollvych can't use Bone-Shattering Cry for 1d4 rounds.

**Frightful Moan** two actions  (auditory, divine, emotion, enchantment, fear, mental); Tollvych laments his fate, forcing each living creature within 30 feet to attempt a DC 36 Will save. On a failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] (or frightened 3 on a critical failure). On a success, a creature is temporarily immune to Tollvych's frightful moan for 1 minute.
