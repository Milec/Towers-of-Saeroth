**Bob** single action  The giant longlegs begins to bob up and down, using its legs almost like springs to cause its body to bounce and sway in an unsettling manner. Until the start of the giant longlegs's next turn, it gains a +2 status bonus to its Armor Class.

**Noxious Spray** two actions ([[Traits/Olfactory]], [[Traits/Poison]]) The giant longlegs sprays a noxious chemical onto a single adjacent target, who must attempt a DC 20 Fortitude save. The giant longlegs can't use Noxious Spray for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune to Noxious Spray for 1 minute.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for as long as they remain sickened.
