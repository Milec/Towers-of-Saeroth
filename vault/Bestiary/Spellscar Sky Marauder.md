**Acidic Pustules** The marauder's skin is covered with necrotic boils that burst when struck. Whenever a creature deals slashing or piercing damage to the marauder, each creature within 5 feet takes 2d6 acid damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a critically failed save, a creature also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]].  
**Runic Resistance** ⤾ ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]]) **Trigger** The maftet takes damage from a Strike or spell effect; **Effect** The maftet's protective runic tattoos glow, granting them resistance 5 to one damage type dealt by the triggering attack. This resistance applies against the triggering effect and lasts for 1 minute or until the maftet uses this ability again, whichever comes first. If the triggering effect deals multiple damage types, the maftet chooses which type to resist.

**Paired Strike** two actions  **Requirements** The maftet is wielding two [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitars]]; **Effect** The maftet makes two Strikes against the same target, one with each of their scimitars. The maftet combines the damage of any attacks that hit and applies resistances and weaknesses only once. This counts as one attack when calculating the maftet's multiple attack penalty.

**Mana Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) A creature can't remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] condition while infected; **Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 day; **Stage 1** fatigued and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (1 day); **Stage 2** fatigued and stupefied 2 (1 day); **Stage 3** fatigued and stupefied 3 (1 day); **Stage 4** gain immunity to mana fever and become a [[Bestiary/Families/Mana Wastes Mutant|Mana Wastes mutant]]

**Powerful Scimitars**  ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) Any non-magical scimitar becomes a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] scimitar_ while a maftet wields it.

**Raptor Dive** three actions  **Requirements** The maftet is flying at least 10 feet above the target; **Effect** The maftet Flies up to twice their fly Speed and makes a Paired Strike at the end of the movement. If both Strikes hit, the target is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
