**Quick Application** single action  Blue Viper Interacts to draw an injury poison and applies it to a weapon he is holding.

**Pinpoint Poisoner**   When Blue Viper successfully Strikes a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature with a poisoned weapon or exposes a flat-footed creature to an [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] poison, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its initial save against that poison.

**Poison Spray** single action  **Requirements** Blue Viper is holding a [[Traits/Contact|contact]] or [[Traits/Injury|injury]] poison; **Effect** Blue Viper swigs the poison and sprays it out of his mouth, affecting a 15-foot cone. Blue Viper isn't exposed to the poison. All creatures in the cone are immediately exposed to the poison and each creature must attempt a Fortitude save against the poison.
