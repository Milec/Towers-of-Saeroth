# Vault Builder

Vault builders still possess the full power [[Setting/Deities/Sairazul]] instilled in them, capable of creating new life and shaping the world around them. A vault builder's life span is several millennia, but their memories are preserved within a crystalline generation stone when they die to be placed inside a newly constructed vault builder body.

**Craft Crystal Wand** ([[Traits/Exploration|exploration]]) **Frequency** twice per day; **Effect** The vault builder spends 10 minutes creating a magic wand out of radioactive green crystal, containing any 8th-rank or lower [[Traits/Earth|earth]] spell of their choice. The vault builder can expend both daily uses to instead make a _+3 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Staff of Earth (Treasure Vault (Remastered))|major staff of earth]]_.  
**Item Caster** The vault builder can Cast a Spell from any item (such as a staff or wand) as though it were on their spell list, but the spell can be 8th-rank or lower.

**Constraining Crystal**  ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]]) When the vault builder deals damage with a claw Strike, glowing green crystals cluster at the site of the attack. The target must succeed at a DC 31 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 hour (or clumsy 2 on a critical failure). If the creature is already clumsy due to constraining crystal, additional failures increase that clumsy value instead, to a maximum of clumsy 4. A target that fails its save while clumsy 4 is [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]] with an unlimited duration.

**Crystal Burst** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]]) An explosion of razor-sharp splinters deals 24d6 piercing damage in a 30-foot burst within 120 feet, with a DC 46 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The vault builder can't use Crystal Burst again for 1d4 rounds.

**Earth Glide**   The vault builder can [[Actions/Burrow]] through any earthen matter, including rock. When they do so, the vault builder moves at their full burrow Speed, leaving no tunnels or signs of their passing.
