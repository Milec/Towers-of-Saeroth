**Defiant Shove** single action  **Requirements** Tino used Retributive Strike on a creature since the start of his previous turn, attempted the Strike it permitted, and didn't critically miss; **Effect** Tino throws his whole body into a powerful push. **Divine Smite**   If Tino hits with his Retributive Strike, the target takes 3 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent good damage]].

**Flurry of Blows** single action ([[Traits/Flourish]]) Tino makes two Strikes with his fist or with a [[Traits/Monk|monk]] weapon. If both hit the same creature, combine their damage for the purpose of resistances and weaknesses.

**Powerful Fists**   Tino's fist Strikes don't take penalties when making lethal attacks.
