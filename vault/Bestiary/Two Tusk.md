**Belly Grease** three actions  Two Tusk extrudes a slippery grease from his ventral glands to coat the barge under him and in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], turning the affected area into uneven ground for 10 minutes, after which it dries to a putrid crust. The DC to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] across the grease is 18.

**Entangling Slime**   A creature struck by Two Tusk's slime squirt becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and takes a –5-foot penalty to Speed for 1 hour or until the slime is removed. A PC can use an [Interact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=3259) action to splash swamp water on themself to wash away the stuff and remove this penalty.
