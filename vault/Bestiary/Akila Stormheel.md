**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Akila's mask melds into her face and she takes on the form of a leopard, or she returns to her halfling form. As a leopard, the only Strikes she can make are a jaws Strike (which deals 3d6+10 piercing damage plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]]) and an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] claws Strike (which deals 3d4+10 slashing damage).

**Pounce** single action  Akila Strides, then Strikes. If she was [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] at the start of this action, she remains hidden until after the Strike.

**Powerful Leap** two actions  Akila [[Actions/Leap|Leaps]] either up to 50 feet horizontally and 10 feet vertically or up to 25 feet vertically and up to 10 feet horizontally. She can make a Strike at any point during the Leap. If she hits, she deals an extra 8 bludgeoning damage from the impact.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Akila deals an extra 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] targets.
