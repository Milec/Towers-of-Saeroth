**Menace Prey** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) The hill giant butcher designates up to two creatures they can see and hear, or one they're [[Actions/Track|Tracking]], as their prey. The hill giant butcher gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to Seek the prey and to Intimidation checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]] the prey. This effect lasts until the hill giant butcher uses Menace Prey again.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The hill giant butcher deals an extra 2d6 precision damage to flat-footed creatures.

**Throw Rock** single action  

**Twin Butchery** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Requirement** the hill giant butcher is wielding two melee weapons, each in a different hand; **Effect** The hill giant butcher uses both of its axes against a single target. It makes two Strikes against its menaced prey, one with each of the required weapons. If both hit the same menaced prey, combine their damage for the purpose of its resistances and weaknesses. In addition, if both Strikes hit, the menaced prey is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of the hill giant butcher's next turn. Apply the hill giant butcher's multiple attack penalty normally.

**Wide Swing** single action  The hill giant makes a single greatclub Strike and compares the attack roll result to the ACs of up to two foes within its reach. This counts as two attacks for the hill giant's multiple attack penalty.
