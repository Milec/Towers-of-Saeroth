**Heat of the Forge** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) 10 feet. An munsahir's skin radiates heat like a forge's fire. A creature that starts its turn in the area must succeed at a DC 19 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] while it remains in the area. Creatures immune to [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] or with any fire resistance are immune.  
**Self-Destruct** When the munsahir is reduced to 0 HP, their armor explodes at the start of their next turn, dealing 5d6 fire damage in a 10-foot radius (DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex).

When the gatecrasher successfully performs a [[Actions/Grapple]] or [[Actions/Shove]] action, they also deal 1d6 fire damage to their target.
