**Blade Barrage** three actions  Takatorra unleashes a flurry of attacks. Takatorra makes five melee Strikes. Their multiple attack penalty applies as normal. They gain a cumulative +2 circumstance bonus to damage for each successful Strike. If one target is hit with at least two of these Strikes, they also take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. They then become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of their next turn.

**Disarming Whirl** two actions ([[Traits/Flourish]]) Takatorra spins, striking at their foes' weapons. Takatorra attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]] against each enemy within melee reach. Each attempt counts toward Takatorra's multiple attack penalty, but the penalty does not increase until they have made all the attempts.

**Soaring Flight** two actions ([[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Tengu]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Takatorra expands their wings, gaining a 25-foot fly Speed for 5 minutes.
