# Myceloid

The ambulatory fungus creatures called myceloids are notorious for spreading deadly purple pox, controlling creatures' minds, and devouring humanoid flesh. For a myceloid colony, any battle with humanoids is cause for excitement, as this new fodder presents so many delicious and satisfying possibilities.

Myceloids consider humanoids to have an ideal life cycle of four simple steps. During childhood, they wander naive and afraid, unaware of myceloid colonies. In adulthood, humanoids discover their true purpose as they taste purple pox and become controlled by the myceloids' spores. Next, they die, giving rise to a new myceloid. They're then sent on to the afterlife upon becoming a myceloid meal. Eating [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] flesh isn't a necessity—a myceloid can survive on any decaying matter—but it's certainly their favored fare. Myceloids rarely make alliances, but when they do, they invite their new allies to share a meal to seal the pact. Few outsiders appreciate this hospitality.

Most myceloids have deep purple caps studded with off-white lumps.

Their necks and bodies bear resemblance to the stipes of tough, leathery fungi. Smaller mushrooms often grow on a myceloid's body, which the creatures view as either adornments or particularly convenient snacks. They stand roughly as tall as dwarves, with comparably stout builds.

**Purple Pox**  ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]]) Myceloids are immune; **Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 minute; **Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 2** 6d6 poison damage, stupefied 3, and the creature is compelled to seek out the nearest myceloid colony—this compulsion is a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] and [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect (1 day); **Stage 3** The creature dies. **Spore Domination** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The myceloid targets one creature affected by purple pox within 60 feet. That creature must attempt a DC 22 Will save. It's then temporarily immune to Spore Domination for 10 minutes. **Critical Success** The target is unaffected. **Success** Until the end of its next turn, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Helpful (Player Core)|helpful]] to myceloids and can't take [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile actions]] against them. **Failure** As success, but for 1 minute. **Critical Failure** As success, but until the purple pox is cured.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Table Manners
>
>
>
> Myceloids eat communal meals consisting of a series of corpses, beginning with creatures they killed in combat, followed by any that died in service while controlled by Spore Domination. As they dine, they offer tasting notes, claiming they can taste intangibles like innocence or despair. They save any creature that died from purple pox as dessert. The new myceloid birthed from the corpse gets the first slice!
