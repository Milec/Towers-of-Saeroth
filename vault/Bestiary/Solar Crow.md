# Solar Crow

These enormous three-legged elementals resemble crows with flaming feathers, said to shine as bright as the sun. They nest in the smoldering Forest of Mulberry Coals, living in flocks of eight to a dozen, with ten being the most common.

([[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]]): DC 27

**Blinding Heat** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) The solar crow expands its wings and unleashes blazing hot, blinding light in a 120-foot emanation. Each creature in the area takes 9d6 fire damage with a DC 29 Reflex save. The crow can't use Blinding Heat again for 1d4 rounds, and its glow aura is deactivated during this time.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  
 **Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is blinded for 1 minute.

**Burning Talons**   A creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the solar crow takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. It can't recover from this damage while grabbed by the crow.

**Snatch**   A solar crow can [[Actions/Fly]] at half Speed while it has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] in its talons, carrying that creature along with it.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Story of Infamy
>
>
>
> Ancient tales from dragon-ruled Tian Xia tell of a flock of solar crows who wreaked havoc upon the Plane of Wood, driving an herb that could grant immortality to extinction before coming to terrorize Tian Xia itself. All but one of the crows were then shot down by a legendary archer.
