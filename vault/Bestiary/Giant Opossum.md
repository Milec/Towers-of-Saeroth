**Feign Death** ⤾ **Trigger** The opossum is reduced below 15 HP; **Effect** The opossum collapses. It is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] and can use actions that require only its mind, but any other action ends the ruse. A successful DC 18 Perception check to [[Actions/Seek]] or [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] is required to determine that the animal is not, in fact, dead.  
**Revived Retaliation** ⤾ **Trigger** The opossum is attacked or disturbed by a creature within reach while Feigning Death; **Effect** The opossum Strikes the triggering creature.
