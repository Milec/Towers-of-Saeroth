# Ghost Pirate

Undead prowling the ocean on derelict ships have long been threats sailors keep an eye out for. Other than corporeal undead, some undead pirates take on an incorporeal form, especially if their bodies have been lost to the fishes. Captains of crews in particular are likely to return from the dead, perhaps on account of their strong personalities.

**Curse Ye Scallywags!** ([[Traits/Curse|curse]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When the ghost pirate captain is reduced to 0 HP, all creatures within 60 feet are subjected to a DC 25 [[Spells/Mariner's Curse|_mariner's curse_]] spell. The locations of those that critically fail are known to the ghost until the curse is removed. This curse ends if the ghost is permanently destroyed.  
**Rejuvenation** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When the ghost pirate captain is destroyed, they re-form after 2d4 days within the ship or location they're bound to, fully healed. They can be permanently destroyed if their desires are fulfilled, which could involve being defeated in a great battle or ensuring their treasure has been hidden.

**Combat Current** two actions  The ghost makes a Strike with its cutlass or hand crossbow, then Flies up to half their Speed and makes a Strike with the other attack. If they moved through an enemy when they Flew, that enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the second Strike.

**Savvy Joinin' Me Crew?** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Trigger** The ghost reduces a living creature to 0 Hit Points with a melee Strike; **Effect** The creature attempts a DC 25 Fortitude save. If the creature fails its save, it remains conscious but is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]] by the ghost until it loses its [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition. It continues to attempt recovery checks as normal for the dying condition. The effect ends if the creature loses the dying condition. If the creature dies while under this effect, it joins the ghost's undead crew in 1d4 days. If the ghost is destroyed before the creature dies, the creature is spared this terrible fate.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Face of a Crew
>
>
>
> Just because a ship has a ghostly captain doesn't mean the entire crew—or what remains of them— are all ghosts. Many command [[Bestiary/Families/Skeleton|skeletons]], [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghouls]], and [[Bestiary/Draugr|draugr]] as well. Occasionally, a ghost captain is an extension of a [[Bestiary/Bone Ship|bone ship]], giving its prey one last chance to surrender.
