# Halfling Troublemaker

A halfling's bravado can sometimes lead them into trouble. These tricksters often roam in groups trying to one-up each other's last prank. While troublemakers rarely intend to kill, occasionally their pranks get out of hand.

**Graffiti Egg** single action  the halfling troublemaker throws an egg filled with paint, glitter, and confetti at a creature within 30 feet. The target must succeed a DC 17 Reflex saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round (or 1 minute on a critical failure).

**Keen Eyes**   The halfling gains a +2 circumstance bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] creatures within 30 feet of it. Whenever the halfling targets a creature that is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or hidden from them, reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or 9 for a hidden one.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The troublemaker deals an extra 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.
