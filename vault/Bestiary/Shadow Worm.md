# Shadow Worm

Shadow worms are monstrous cave worms that dwell on the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]]. Larger and more cunning than most cave worms, shadow worms are fearsome predators that leave swaths of devastation in their wake. Most have glossy, black-armored hide, but those that burrow near [[Bestiary/Families/Velstrac|velstrac]] lands are streaked with dark red, for the blood the velstracs spill has soaked into the very stones these shadow worms consume.

**Inexorable** The shadow worm recovers from the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] conditions at the end of its turn. It's also immune to penalties to its Speeds and the [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] condition, and it ignores difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain.  
**Shadow Healing** As long as a shadow worm is in an area of dim light or darkness, it gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 20]]. When affected by a magical shadow effect from anything other than itself, a shadow worm becomes [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened 1]] for 1 round.  
**Shrink from the Light** A shadow worm takes an additional 20 damage from any spell or ability with the [[Traits/Light|light]] trait and uses the outcome one degree worse for saving throws made against a spell or ability with the light trait.

> ## Midnight Worms
>
>
>

So far, only two shadow worms have survived the velstrac's ministrations. The resulting horrors have been dubbed midnight worms.
