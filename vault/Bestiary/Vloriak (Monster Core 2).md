# Vloriak (Despoiler Demon)

Vloriaks rise from the sinful souls of those whose acts of sabotage resulted in despair and terror, and they delight in the destruction of mortal artifice. They make use of their ability to rust metals to destroy structures, precious works of art, and tools used for creation. In combat, this ability allows them to destroy the armaments of their attacks. Vloriaks particularly enjoy destroying armors and shields, as these defensive items generally bear elegant designs and decorations that are more enjoyable to despoil than those of a weapon.

Often called despoiler demons, vloriaks were the followers of the longdead demon lord Xar-Azmak. Their numbers have dwindled over the eons since the Lord of Rust's death; those that exist now are scattered throughout [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts|the Outer Rifts]]. Their chitinous bodies and faceted eyes give them an insectile appearance.

**Lick Rust** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]]) **Requirements** The vloriak rusted a metal item with their tongue this turn; **Effect** The vloriak attempts a tongue [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] on the same target they just attacked. If it hits, it deals no damage as the demon licks away the rust and regains 2d6 Hit Points (or 4d6 Hit Points if the Strike was a critical hit). The vloriak can't Lick Rust on their next turn.

**Rust**   A vloriak's saliva causes metal to rust rapidly. If they succeed at a tongue [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] or [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] attempt, the vloriak deals 2d6 acid damage (doubled on a critical hit) to a metal item the target is wearing or holding, ignoring its [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]]. If the vloriak hits an unattended metal item, the item takes this damage automatically. If a creature uses the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Shield Block (Monster Core)|Shield Block]] reaction with a metal shield against a tongue attack, the shield is automatically [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]], but no other item is rusted on that attack.

**Spew Rusted Shards** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) The vloriak spews a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of acid and rusted metal. Creatures in the area take 3d6 acid and 3d6 piercing damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that takes any piercing damage is exposed to [[Rules/Diseases/Tetanus (GM Core)|tetanus]]. The vloriak can't Spew Rusted Shards for 1d4 rounds.
