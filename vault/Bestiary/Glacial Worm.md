# Glacial Worm

Cave worm eggs that lie dormant for a long period may experience significant climactic shift. If the temperature falls below freezing, the worms may hatch as glacial worms, also called white worms. These creatures immediately migrate as far north as possible, seeking the glaciers or icebergs that will nourish and house them. Glacial worms lack stingers, as liquid venom is less effective in cold temperatures. Occasionally, such creatures are carried far south on icebergs that have carved off the main ice sheets of the polar regions.

**Cold Healing** When a glacial worm is in extreme cold or surrounded by ice, it gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 10]]. When struck by a magical cold effect, a glacial worm regains Hit Points equal to half the cold damage the effect would otherwise deal.  
**Inexorable** The glacial worm recovers from the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] conditions at the end of its turn. It's also immune to penalties to its Speeds and the [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] condition, and it ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain.  
**Leech Heat** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Cold|cold]]) 30 feet. A glacial worm's intense cold absorbs heat. Creatures that start their turn within the area take 3d6 cold damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).
