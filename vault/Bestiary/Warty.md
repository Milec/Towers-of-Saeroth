**Tongue Grab**   A creature hit by Warty's tongue becomes grabbed by the giant toad. The creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the toad's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue with a successful Strike against AC 15 that deals at least 6 slashing damage. This deals no damage to Warty, but prevents him from using his tongue Strike until he regrows his tongue, which takes a week.
