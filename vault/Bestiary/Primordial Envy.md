# Primordial Envy

The primordial envy is most often found in underground ruins within Edasseril. It hungers for magic, leaching it from any source it can come into contact with. This hunger can be exploited by clever adventurers who bait it with powerful destructive magics. A primordial envy is one of the most defensive sin oozes, and it tends to layer [[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]] spells over itself to both increase its resilience and empower its attacks.

**Spell Drain** single action ([[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Arcane]]) The primordial envy attempts to drain magic from a spellcasting creature that it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].  **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is temporarily immune to Spell Drain for 24 hours.

 **Critical Failure** As failure, but one of the creature's uncast spells or unused spell slots is lost, as if they had cast it.
