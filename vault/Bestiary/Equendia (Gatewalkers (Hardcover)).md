**Blackfrost Rot**  ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) This affliction can't be reduced below stage 1, nor the damage from it healed, until successfully treated with [[Spells/Cleanse Affliction|_cleanse affliction_]] or a similar effect; the affliction can then be removed as a normal disease. A creature that dies from cold damage while cursed with blackfrost rot rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Blackfrost Dead|blackfrost dead]] after 24 hours.  
**Saving Throw** DC 30 Fortitude; **Stage 1** 4d6 cold damage (1 minute); **Stage 2** weakness to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] 5 and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weakness]] to cold 10 and drained 2 (1 day).

**Frozen Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) Equendia exhales a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] of icy shards that deal 6d6 cold and 6d6 slashing damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). She can't use Frozen Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Hissing Command** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]]) Equendia utters a swift command in [[Setting/Languages/Necril (Player Core)|Necril]] to strike at adjacent enemies. Any blackfrost zombies within 30 feet can use a reaction to attempt a melee Strike.

**Invasive Blackfrost**   If a PC is critically hit by any of Equendia's Strikes, the wound takes on an unnerving blackened appearance, as if from advanced frostbite or charring. For the next 24 hours, the PC is no longer immune to blackfrost.
