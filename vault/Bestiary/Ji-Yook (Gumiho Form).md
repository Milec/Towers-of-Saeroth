**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]**   Ji-yook transforms into her human form. Using Change Shape counts as creating a disguise for the [[Actions/Impersonate]] use of [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]. Ji-yook can remain in her human form indefinitely, and she can shift back to her true kitsune form by using this action again.

**Disquieting Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Ji-yook selects one enemy within 60 feet, then Changes Shape into humanoid form, taking the form of a person emotionally close to that enemy, such as a dead family member. Ji-yook is not aware of the form she will take until she transforms. If her target sees her, even if they are aware it is a trick, they must attempt a DC 40 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure) for 1 round. This is a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect.

**Foxfire Blast** two actions  **Frequency** once per round; Effect Ji-yook makes a fist strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 3d6 electricity damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] back 5 feet. On a critical hit, the target is pushed back and then knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

**Pinning Shot** single action  Ji-yook makes a dart Strike. If the attack hits, it deals no damage and instead imparts the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Bow|bow critical specialization]] effect against the target. If the attack is a critical hit, the target must succeed at two separate Interact actions to pull the missile free. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] while it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] in this way.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Ji-yook's Strikes deal an extra 4d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Waylay**  ([[Traits/Illusion]]) When Ji-yook Strides at least 10 feet, illusions make her [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] until the start of her next turn.
