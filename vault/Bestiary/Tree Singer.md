# Tree Singer

Singers can speak the words of creation in song, a forgotten art from the [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]]. Their melodies turn grass into blades and make trees stir. Through their music, plants rise to defend and seek vengeance for nature. Taught by woodland spirits or [[Setting/Languages/Fey|fey]] agents of the First World, they master the magic of primal song.

**Verdant Aria** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Wood]]) The tree singer raises their voice in a haunting melody, creating an aura in a 30-foot emanation. Plants in the aura seem to come to life, swaying and rustling in response to the music. The tree singer's allies in the aura gain a +2 status bonus to AC and saving throws as the foliage around them shields and defends them from harm. The aura lasts until the end of the tree singer's next turn but can be Sustained. It can be Sustained even if the tree singer is [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorphed]]. The effect ends early if the tree singer stops singing.
