# Clockwork Dragon

Clockwork dragons are marvels of clockwork design. These powerful masterpieces have the ability to fly, making them versatile and dangerous killers. A clockwork dragon's winding mechanism is more efficient than those of other clockworks, allowing it to partially reuse energy generated by flapping its wings. Creating a clockwork dragon requires not only an advanced understanding of clockwork design, but also a greater cache of rare resources, as its body requires solid reinforcement. As a result, most clockwork dragons have [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]] fortification. Creating a clockwork dragon without adamantine is entirely possible, though such dragons are typically smaller and more fragile.
