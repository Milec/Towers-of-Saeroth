# Young Stormcrown Dragon

Heralds of—and heralded by—the primal forces of thunder and lightning, stormcrown dragons meld the majesty of fierce storms and mighty dragons. Driven by emotion and curiosity, these mercurial dragons can change from friend to foe or threatening to thoughtful in mere moments.

**Coronal Cloak**   When the dragon spends an action to Hover while in stormy weather, they become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until they [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] or until the beginning of their next turn, whichever comes first.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges Galvanic Barrage whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Galvanic Barrage** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon exhales a massive bolt of lightning at one creature within 100 feet, dealing 5d12 electricity damage with a DC 28 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The lightning then arcs to a creature of the dragon's choice within 30 feet of the previous target with the same effect. The dragon can't target the same creature more than once, and they must have [[Rules/Line of Effect (rules-2382)|line of effect]] to all targets. The bolt can continue to arc this way until the dragon runs out of targets or until a target critically succeeds at their save. The dragon can't use Galvanic Barrage again for 1d4 rounds.

**Smooth Flying**   The stormcrown dragon doesn't fall if they don't [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] during their turn, and they're unaffected by [[Rules/Wind (GM Core)|strong winds]] or similar effects unless they choose to be.
