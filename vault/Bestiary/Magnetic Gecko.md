# Magnetic Gecko

Metal objects seem to gravitate to this otherwise innocuous gecko. Its fondness for refined metals often leads the reptile to settle close to large settlements.

**Greater Electrolocation** A magnetic gecko can sense minute electrical charges in living creatures, which it can use as a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise sense]] at a range of 20 feet. This distance increases to 100 feet against any creature that has used an [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] effect within the last minute.  
**Uncanny Climber** A magnetic gecko's feet allow it to climb virtually any surface, no matter how slick or sheer. If a gecko attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] and critically fails, it gets a failure instead.

**Launch Metal** two actions  The gecko repulses the metal attached to its body in all directions, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 10-foot emanation. The gecko can't use Launch Metal again for 1d4 rounds.

**Repel** single action  The gecko manipulates its magnetic field to repel metal, humming audibly and gaining resistance 2 to damage from metal weapons and [[Traits/Metal|metal]] effects until the beginning of its next turn.

**Static Cling**   If the gecko hits Small or smaller creature with its tongue, and the target is made of metal or is wearing metallic armor, the gecko's tongue latches on to the creature. The creature must attempt a DC 17 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]. While the gecko is Grabbing a creature in this way, it can attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the target's Fortitude DC to [[Rules/Forced Movement|pull]] the creature to a space adjacent to the gecko. A creature grabbed in this way can [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] normally.
