# Ankhrav Hive Mother

Ankhrav hive mothers are fearsome predators that one can easily distinguish from the typical ankhrav not only by their greater size, but the presence of a large pair of razor-sharp, mantis-like arms.

**Armor-Rending Bite** two actions  The hive mother makes a mandibles Strike; if the Strike hits, the target's armor takes the damage and the acid damage bypasses the armor's Hardness.

**Frenzy Pheromone** two actions  The hive mother unleashes a pheromone that causes all other ankhravs within a 100-foot emanation to become [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] until the start of the hive mother's next turn, and they can use the extra action only for [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]], Stride, or Strike actions. The hive mother can't unleash the pheromone again for 1d4 rounds.

**Spray Acid** two actions ([[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]]) The hive mother spews acid in a 60- foot cone, dealing 8d6 acid damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]] (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). It can't Spray Acid again for 1d4 rounds.
