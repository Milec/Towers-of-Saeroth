# Izfiitar

Occupying the pinnacle of the loose protean caste system, izfiitars enact the ever-shifting plans of the vaunted protean lords and those of the divine Speakers of the Depths. Izfiitars appear to be elevated versions of the protean priests known as [[Bestiary/Keketar|keketars]], despite their smaller size, although protean hierarchies are never so straightforward.

**Entropy Sense** ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Prediction|prediction]]) A izfiitar can anticipate the most likely location of a creature through their supernatural insight into the forces of chaotic probabilities and chance. This grants the izfiitar the ability to sense creatures within the listed range. The izfiitar's entropy sense doesn't detect creatures under the effects of [[Spells/Nondetection|_nondetection_]] or that are otherwise shielded from [[Traits/Divination|divinations]] and [[Traits/Prediction|predictions]].

**Kiss of the Speakers** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) The izfiitar continuously tinkers with the myriad possibilities in which it can move or manipulate magic. The izfiitar is always [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] and can use the extra action only to Cast a Spell, Step, or Stride.  
**Prescient Revision** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]]) **Trigger** The izfiitar fails a check; **Effect** The izfiitar rerolls the triggering check and takes the better result. For 1d4 rounds, it loses the effects of Kiss of the Speakers and can't use Reshape Reality.  
**Protean Anatomy** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) A izfiitar's vital organs shift and change shape and position constantly. Immediately after the izfiitar takes acid, electricity, or sonic damage, it gains the listed amount of resistance to that damage type. This lasts for 1 hour or until the next time the protean takes damage of one of the other types (in which case its resistance changes to match that type), whichever comes first.  
 The izfiitar is immune to [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effects unless it is a willing target. If [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]], the izfiitar automatically recovers at the end of its next turn as new sensory organs grow to replace the compromised ones.

This doesn't change its Speed or its attack and damage bonuses with its Strikes, but might change the damage type its Strikes deal.

**Greater Warpwave Strike**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Any creature struck and damaged by an izfiitar's jaws or claw Strike must succeed at a DC 42 Fortitude save or be subject to a particularly powerful warpwave (see Warpwave below). Roll twice and apply both affects, rerolling any duplicates.

**Reshape Reality**  ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) When the izfiitar casts [[Spells/Hallucinatory Terrain|_hallucinatory terrain_]], it infuses the illusion with quasi-real substance. Creatures that do not disbelieve the illusion treat structures and terrain created through the spell as though they were real, ascending illusory stairs, becoming trapped by illusory quicksand, and so on.

**Storm of Claws** two actions  The izfiitar makes up to six claw Strikes, each against a different target.
