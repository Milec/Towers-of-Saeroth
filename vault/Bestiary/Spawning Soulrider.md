# Spawning Soulrider

When soulriders absorb sufficient planar energy, they become gravid with countless tiny eggs. Such soulriders instinctively return to [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] via the Dead Roads to lay their spawn, although the exact metaphysical process has yet to be fully understood. [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|Fiends]] and others keen to enter the mortal world have begun capturing and studying spawning soulriders, though such experiments carry their own risks, for soulriders prevented from returning to the Universe will continue to grow larger as they absorb more energy.

Once back in the Universe, spawning soulriders become fiercely protective of their near-translucent eggs and tiny spawn, using their remora-like maws to fend off potential threats. Anyone disturbing a soulrider in the process of spawning might find out what it feels like to have pieces of their soul torn away.

## Planar Adaptations

Spawning soulriders gain an additional ability based on their planar adaptation. Soulriders with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] and [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] traits gain Celestial Flare. Soulriders with the [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiendish]] and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits gain Fiendish Lunge. Soulriders with the [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]] trait gain Monitor Escape. Soulriders that followed a soul to a more esoteric plane have different abilities at the GM’s discretion, but such creatures are uncommon.

**Celestial Flare** two actions ([[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) Each enemy within 30 feet of the soulrider takes 2d6 spirit damage (DC 17 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). Creatures that fail the save are blinded for 1 round. The spawning soulrider can't use Celestial Flare again for 1 minute.

**Fiendish Lunge** two actions ([[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]) The spawning soulrider [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swims]] twice, making a tail Strike at any point during its movement. The Strike deals an additional 1d4 spirit damage.

**Monitor Escape** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleport]]) The soulrider's form blurs as it exploits loopholes in the multiverse. It teleports to an empty space within 60 feet.

**Grind Soul** single action ([[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) **Requirements** The spawning soulrider is attached to a creature's soul; **Effect** The soulrider grinds the creature's soul with its jagged inner mouth, dealing 2d8 spirit damage (DC 16 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a critical failure, the creature also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent spirit damage]]. Regardless of the result, the spawning soulrider is no longer attached to the creature.

**Propulsive Launch** two actions  The spawning soulrider [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leaps]] up to 40 feet, then makes a sucker [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]]. If it's in the air and not attached to a creature after the [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], it [[Rules/Falling (Player Core)|falls]].

**Soul Attach**  ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]) When a spawning soulrider succeeds at a sucker [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] against a target with a soul capable of facing judgment, its sucker attaches it to that soul. While attached, both the spawning soulrider and the host creature are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], and the spawning soulrider moves with its host until the spawning soulrider dies or the host pulls it loose ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 16). If the host dies while the spawning soulrider is attached, the spawning soulrider disappears immediately to follow the soul leaving the body. A creature returned to life before reaching its final destination generally returns with any attached spawning soulrider.

**Tail Thrash** two actions  **Requirements** The spawning soulrider is attached to a creature's soul; **Effect** The spawning soulrider makes a tail Strike against the creature whose soul it's attached to, then one against another creature adjacent to the original target. These Strikes count towards the spawning soulrider's multiple attack penalty, but it doesn't increase until after the second attack.
