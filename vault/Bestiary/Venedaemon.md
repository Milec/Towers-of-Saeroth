# Venedaemon (Pact Daemon)

Those killed by magic or killed in the pursuit of magic can arise as venedaemons. Although among the weaker daemons, these robed figures can often be seen serving as researchers or clerks throughout the plane. Even within the mortal [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]], scholars barter souls or arcane knowledge with venedaemons for their secrets and assistance.
