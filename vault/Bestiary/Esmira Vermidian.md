# Esmira Vermidian

Esmira came to serve as Maiserene's schoolteacher not long after Raudltz and Alisendra's first child, Jinnivere, was born. She initially applied for the position of castle au pair and tutor, but that job was taken by another (whom, in time, Esmira would befriend). Even though she didn't get the position, she was so taken with the village of Maiserene that she decided to stay, taking on the role of assistant to the town's aging schoolteacher. When that teacher passed away from natural causes a few years later, the job fell to her quite naturally, as the villagers had grown fond of her sense of humor and her skill at arcane magic.

 Esmira is an elderly but spry Varisian woman with long gray hair and an expression that shifts from pleased delight to stern demand with practiced ease. She wears flowing schoolteacher's robes and often paces while she talks.

 Aside from delight and sternness, another expression that crosses her face is periodic sadness. On the night of the curse, Esmira helped gather the children into the schoolhouse to protect them, only to die with them as the roof burned and collapsed. While it takes her a bit to get over the fact that all of Maiserene's children are long gone, she soon settles into her new role of aiding the PCs in determining what happened to cause the curse in the first place.

## Campaign Role

 Esmira can not only provide spellcasting services for the PCs, but she can also aid them in their research and investigation. She's a wonderful conversationalist and is always eager to sit and chat with PCs about all manner of topics—although she prefers not to hear about the awful things that have developed in the castle. If the PCs haven't figured out how to use the standing stones at area **A14**, yet, she can teach them how to Meditate Among the Stones (page 36).

## Side Quest

 After the PCs unlock the castle grounds, Esmira approaches them to ask a favor. She talks about how she was sometimes called upon by the Arudoras to help substitute for the castle's live-in au pair and tutor when she fell ill or had to travel, and of how much trouble both of the younger Arudoras—Augsten and Jinnivere—were to handle. She listens with horror but, perhaps, not much shock if the PCs eventually share with her what became of these two Arudoras. Regardless, she notes that while her duties as a substitute instructor largely fell off in the years before the curse (as both children were, by then, adults), she did visit the au pair, Linisriel, frequently to have tea. They soon had built a friendship over the shared exertions of dealing with precocious Arudora children. While she knows Linisriel is likely long gone (if the PCs report to her about her transformation into a rakshasa, she's shocked and saddened, but thankful her friend's soul is now at peace), the last time she visited, she loaned Linisriel one of her favorite books—a tawdry romance called _The Three Curious Elves_ If the PCs come across this book, Esmira would be grateful for its return. She can describe to the PCs the castle grounds, as well as the route she took from the trophy room to Linisriel's room in the private halls where she's sure she left the book (describing the layout of areas **D15** to **D7c** to **F2b** to **F4**).

 If the PCs return the book, she gasps in delight. At the same moment, her emotions crystallize the ambient ectoplasm into an accolade robe, which (after her surprise at its manifestation) Esmira eagerly gifts to the PCs in thanks.
