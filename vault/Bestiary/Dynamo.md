# Dynamo

The intricate clockwork prostheses known as [[Rules/Sidebars/Prosthetics in Golarion (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|sterling dynamos]] have become increasingly common in recent years, particularly in havens of technological progress like Dongun Hold and the city of Absalom, but a few specialized engineers have elevated their dedication to research and development of such devices to an entirely new level. These individuals seek to achieve bodily perfection through augmentation, replacing their own limbs with advanced and sometimes untested prototypes that grant them a wide variety of abilities.

**Extend Arms** single action  The dynamo extends their collapsible steel arms, giving them both a [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] of 20 feet with all melee attacks. However, the dynamo becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] and can't use the Interact action. The dynamo can Dismiss this ability.

**Extend Legs** single action  The dynamo rises into the air on 10-foot-tall telescoping steel legs. While their legs are extended, the dynamo gains a +10-foot status bonus to land Speed and ignores any [[Rules/Cover|cover]] granted by barriers less than 10 feet tall. However, the dynamo becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] and can't use the [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]], [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]], [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]], or [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] actions. The dynamo can Dismiss this ability.

**Modular Prostheses** single action  The dynamo configures one or both of their mechanical prosthetic hands into a specific configuration. Each configuration deals a specific damage type and has its own weapon traits: fist (bludgeoning; [[Traits/Free-Hand (Player Core)|free-hand]]), gaff hook (piercing; [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]]), impact driver (bludgeoning; [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]]), or spinning blade (slashing; [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]]). The dynamo can alternatively transform a hand into a steel shield with Hardness 8, HP 72, and BT 36. A [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] prosthesis can't be reconfigured until repaired.
