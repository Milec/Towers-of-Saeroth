**Quick Application** single action  Blue Viper Interacts to draw an [[Traits/Injury|injury]] poison and applies it to a weapon he is holding.

**Pinpoint Poisoner**   When Blue Viper successfully Strikes a [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creature with a poisoned weapon or exposes a flat-footed creature to an [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] poison, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its initial save against that poison.

**Poison Spray** single action  **Requirements** Blue Viper is holding a [[Traits/Contact|contact]] or [[Traits/Injury|injury]] poison; **Effect** Blue Viper swigs the poison and sprays it out of his mouth, affecting a 15- foot cone. Blue Viper is not exposed to the poison. All creatures in the cone are immediately exposed to the poison and each creature must attempt a Fortitude save against the poison.

**Potent Poisoner**   Blue Viper's infused poisons have a DC of 37.

**Toxic Escape** two actions  Blue Viper Interacts to draw an [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] poison, deploys the poison in a smoke cloud that provides [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]] in a 5-foot emanation, then [[Actions/Sneak|Sneaks]] up to his Speed. He is not exposed to the inhaled poison.
