# Boggard Scouting Party

Boggard scouting parties run ahead of other boggard troops to get the lay of the land. They sometimes engage if they think they can quickly vanquish an enemy force.

**Speed** 20 feet, swim 25 feet; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Chorus of Croaks** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The boggard scouting party unleashes a chorus of terrifying croaks. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 unless they succeed at a DC 21 Will save; those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Coordinated Tongue Pull** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Several boggards use their tongues to grapple an enemy within 10 feet to pull them closer. The target must attempt a DC 21 Reflex save. On a failure, they are grabbed and pulled 5 feet closer to the scouting party. A creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] in this way isn't immobilized, but it can't move more than 10 feet from the scouting party. A creature can sever one of the tongues with a Strike against AC 21 that deals at least 5 slashing damage. This doesn't damage the scouting party.

**Morningstar Massacre** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The boggards execute coordinated melee attacks against each enemy in a 5-foot emanation, with a DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]]  Reflex save. The damage dealt depends on the number of actions.  
single action 1d6 bludgeoning or piercing damage  
two actions 2d6+7 bludgeoning or piercing damage  
three actions 2d6+11 bludgeoning or piercing damage

**Sling Barrage** two actions  The dreadknot draws and loads slings to launch a coordinated barrage. This barrage is a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 50 feet that deals 3d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). When the scouting party is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Swamp Passage**   A boggard scouting party ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.
