**Call Down the Storm** two actions ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]) The druids summon wind and lightning against all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 80 feet. This storm deals 3d10 electricity damage (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature who fails the save is also pushed 5 feet away from the druid circle. When the druid circle is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Sickle and Staff** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The druids strike out in a coordinated melee attack against all enemies in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] with a DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage dealt depends on the number of actions.  
single action 2d6 bludgeoning or slashing damage  
two actions 4d6+9 bludgeoning or slashing damage  
three actions 5d6+13 bludgeoning or slashing damage

**Troop Spellcasting**   When the druid circle [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Casts a Spell]], its constituent members combine their efforts into casting a more powerful version of the spell than any one member could achieve alone. When Casting a Spell that has an area of a [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]], or [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] and doesn't have a duration, increase the area of that spell. Add 5 feet to the radius of a burst that normally has a radius of at least 10 feet (a burst with a smaller radius is not affected). Add 5 feet to the length of a cone or line that is normally 15 feet long or smaller, and add 10 feet to the length of a larger cone or line.
