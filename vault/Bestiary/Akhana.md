# Akhana

Akhanas are massive eyes formed of cosmic matter that monitor the balance of birth and death. They understand the profound influence living things have on the cosmos and silently perform their duties of supporting and pruning life. Akhanas seem utterly unconcerned by the fate of souls after death, often leaving undead in their wake or attracting daemon scavengers. [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp (Monster Core)|Psychopomps]] often despair over akhanas' cryptic actions, forced to clear out sudden backlogs of souls or even battle the aeons directly. Their “tail” is a twisting column of cosmic energy that can drain vitality from creatures and seal their fate.

**Flying Fists** two actions  The akhana [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] and makes up to four fist Strikes against different targets at any points during this movement. The attacks count toward its multiple attack penalty normally, but the penalty does not increase until after Flying Fists is complete.

**Reclaim Life** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Void]]) **Requirements** The akhana has a living creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grappled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] with its tail; **Effect** The creature takes 4d10 void damage with a DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failed save, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed 1]]. If the creature dies while doomed and held in the akhana's tail, its soul is trapped in the akhana (as [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]]), and its remains are preserved as peaceful rest. The soul returns to the body with 1 Hit Point if the akhana Dismisses the effect, if the akhana is slain, or if a [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual or similarly powerful magic frees it.

**Sprout Life** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Vitality]]) A 5-foot burst within 100 feet fills with simple life appropriate to the environment. The newly forged animals bite those in the area for 7d6 piercing damage with a DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The akhana can also have fungus or plants choke the area, even floating ones in the sky, creating [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. The created life lives or dies normally after its creation.
