# Hobgoblin Spellbreaker

Largely due to an ancestral grudge against all things [[Bestiary/Families/Elf|elven]], many hobgoblins have an inherent distrust for magic, particularly the “elf magic” of the arcane tradition. Most hobgoblin armies maintain a contingent of special “spellbreaker” forces trained to assassinate high-value spellcasting targets prior to military engagement or quickly identify and terminate enemy battle mages.
