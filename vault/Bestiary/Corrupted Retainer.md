**Demonic Strength**   Corrupted retainers deal an extra 1d8 damage with their tridents, and any trident gains the [[Traits/Fatal|fatal d10]] trait while the corrupted retainer is using it (both the extra weapon damage die and the fatal trait have already been included in the corrupted retainer's trident Strike above).

**Tainted Rage** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]) The corrupted retainer begins to rage and gains 5 temporary Hit Points. The rage lasts for 1 minute or until they fall [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], whichever happens first. While raging, the corrupted retainer deals 2 additional damage with melee weapons, has a –1 penalty to AC, and cannot use actions that have the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait unless those actions also have the [[Traits/Rage|rage]] trait. The corrupted retainer can still [[Actions/Seek]] while raging.
