**Furious Charge** two actions  The eidolon Strides twice and then makes a Strike. As long as it moved at least 20 feet, it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll.

**Primal Roar** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]]) The eidolon attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet; these Demoralize attempts don't take any penalty for not sharing a language.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Rend (Monster Core)|Rend]]** single action  claw

**Scent of Blood** single action  **Requirements** A creature within the eidolon's scent range is taking bleed damage; **Effect** The eidolon flies into a frenzy, gaining 10 temporary HP for 1 minute and a +4 status bonus to damage rolls with its [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks, but becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]]. It can't voluntarily end the frenzy or start another frenzy while in the frenzy. The frenzy lasts for 1 minute, after which the eidolon is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] for 1 minute.
