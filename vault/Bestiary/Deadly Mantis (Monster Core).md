**Fling** single action  The deadly mantis flings a [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] creature into the air, up to 30 feet overhead and up to 30 feet away from the mantis (the creature takes damage from the [[Rules/Falling|fall]] as normal). If the flung creature lands on another creature, the creature it lands on takes the same amount of bludgeoning damage with a DC 31 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

**Leaping Grab** two actions  The mantis Leaps up to 40 feet vertically and 20 feet horizontally. At any point during the jump, it can make a leg Strike. If it hits, it automatically [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grabs]] the target, bringing the creature along until the end of the jump.

**Rending Mandibles** single action  The mantis makes a mandibles Strike against a creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]. If that Strike hits and the creature is wearing armor with Hardness 12 or lower, the armor is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]]. This Strike doesn't further damage armor that's already broken.

**Sudden Strike**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven't acted are [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the deadly mantis.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Sacred Insects
>
>
>
> Deadly mantises are sacrosanct to followers of [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek]], the Mantis God. His adherents, including the infamous Red Mantis assassins, invite or lure deadly mantises close to their settlements, seeing the towering creatures as a sign of their god's favor. Achaekek's followers offer sacrifices of livestock or captured enemies to keep the massive insects well fed. Clerics of Achaekek defend a deadly mantis's territory as if it were their own, believing it to be holy ground.
