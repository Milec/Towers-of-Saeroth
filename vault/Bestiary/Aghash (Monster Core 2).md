# Aghash

Walking embodiments of curses, misfortune, and the evil eye, aghashes wander the deserts of [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], searching out the beauty of the world to destroy, humiliate, and undermine. Aghashes are often mistaken for some strange form of [[Bestiary/Families/Hag (Monster Core)|hag]], a misunderstanding that they're content to encourage, and like those creatures, they're masterful at curses.

**Hatred of Art** While aghashes hate all mortals, they particularly despise beautiful objects and artistic mortals. When not in physical peril, an aghash is compelled to destroy art and other works of beauty. An aghash can't enter an area of pristine beauty without first marring it in some way. Given a choice, an aghash will attack a foe who is an obvious crafter or performer of some kind. A [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bard]] casting a composition spell, a runesmith tracing a rune, a street magician performing a daring escape, and similar abilities as determined by the GM draw the aghash's ire. If the aghash is barred from attacking such foes, either by force or some magical effect, they take 1d6 mental damage at the end of their turn.

**Cursed Gaze** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) The aghash fixes their gaze on one creature they can see within 20 feet. The creature must attempt a DC 21 Will save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes 2d6 mental damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  
**Failure** The creature takes 4d6 mental damage and becomes either frightened 2 or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (the aghash's choice).

**Sandstorm** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The aghash creates a temporary sandstorm in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] that lasts for 1 minute. Creatures within the emanation take a –4 circumstance penalty to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks and must succeed at a DC 18 Fortitude save. On a failure, they're forced to hold their breath or else they start suffocating. A creature within the sandstorm at the end of its turn takes 1d6 slashing damage. Divs are immune to all effects of an aghash's Sandstorm.
