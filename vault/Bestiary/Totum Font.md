# Totum Font

Before mortals, fey, or even genies, totum fonts were the first creatures born of six elements undivided and harmonious. From the fonts sprung new elemental children, and they aided the gods in the many acts of creation that would follow.

**Elemental Attunement** A totum font is always attuned to a single element ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]]), represented by which of their faces points forward. They can change this attunement to the element of their choice as a single action, or as a free action when they roll initiative.

**Briar's Hold** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) **Requirements** The font is attuned to [[Traits/Wood|wood]]; **Effect** Each creature within 20 feet must succeed at a DC 36 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 minute. When a creature already slowed by Briar's Hold fails its Fortitude save, it becomes petrified for 1 minute but is turned to wood instead of stone.

**Brilliance of Flame** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Requirements** The font is attuned to fire; **Effect** The font flies 30 feet and explodes in a fiery Elemental Eruption.

**Elemental Eruption** two actions ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The font explodes in a cacophony of color and energy. Each creature in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes 9d6 damage (DC 36 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The explosion deals bludgeoning damage unless the font is attuned to [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] (electricity damage), [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] (fire damage), [[Traits/Metal|metal]] (slashing damage), or [[Traits/Wood|wood]] (piercing damage). Elemental Eruption gains the trait matching the element the font is attuned to.

**Entomb** free action ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]]) **Requirements** The font is attuned to [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], and its last action was a successful tendril Strike; **Effect** The font attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] the target of the Strike.

**Overflowing Tide** two actions ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) **Requirements** The font is attuned to [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]; **Effect** Elemental waves surge around the font, creating a torrential Elemental Eruption and pushing creatures in the area 20 feet (or 10 feet on a successful saving throw).

**Serrated Flurry** single action ([[Traits/Metal]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** The font is attuned to metal; **Effect** The font lashes out with two tendril Strikes, each targeting a different creature within their reach. These Strikes deal slashing damage.

**Tempest Charge** single action ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]]) **Requirements** The font is attuned to [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]]; **Effect** The font [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] 60 feet and makes a tendril Strike against a creature it hasn't attacked this turn.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Wellsprings Of One
>
>
>
> Without access to the balance of all six elemental planes, a totum font becomes fractured and inundated in a single element. Most were healed when the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal|Planes of Metal]] and [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood|Wood]] returned, but some of these so-called “wellsprings of one” still wander the universe, agitated and confused.
