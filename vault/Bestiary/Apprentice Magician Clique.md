**Barrage of Force** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]]) The apprentice magicians launch shards of pure magic at all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 120 feet. This barrage deals 5d4 force damage (DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). When the clique is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Clique Spellcasting**   When the apprentice magician clique Casts a Spell, its members pool their arcane power into the spell. A creature who critically fails their save against the spell or whom the clique hits with a critical spell attack is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 minute.

**Sparking Wands** single action to three actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The apprentice magicians wave wands that shoot out minor bolts of electricity at short range. Each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage dealt depends on the number of actions.  
single action 1d8 electricity damage  
two actions 2d8+3 electricity damage  
three actions 2d8+7 electricity damage
