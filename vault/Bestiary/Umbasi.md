**Story of Valor** single action ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Linguistic]]) Umbasi expounds on the values of courage and respect. They remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] condition from and reduce the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] value of each ally within 30 feet by 1. If this is the first time they've used this action in combat, Umbasi automatically rallies the crowd for their team (see the Crowd Appeal sidebar on page 45 of _Pathfinder #167: Ready? Fight!_).
