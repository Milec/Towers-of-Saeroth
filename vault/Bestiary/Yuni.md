On a failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 (or frightened 3 on a critical failure). On a success, a creature is temporarily immune to this ghost's frightful moan for 1 minute.

**Tiger Attack** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Conjuration]]) Yuni cries out in pain as a ghostly tiger appears to rip and tear at her body, only for additional ghostly tigers to appear and do the same to all creatures in a 30-foot emanation. Creatures in this area take 4d6 slashing damage (DC 25 basic Reflex save).
