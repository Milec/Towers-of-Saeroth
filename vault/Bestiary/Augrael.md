**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** Augrael is adjacent to the corpse of an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature that was destroyed within the last hour; **Effect** Augrael devours a chunk of the destroyed undead creature and regains 2d6 Hit Points. He can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once.

**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** DC 20 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effects (1 day); **Stage 2** 2d6 negative damage and regains half as many Hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 2d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); Stage 6 dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) Any living, non-elf creature hit by a ghoul's attack must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each save.

**Sneak Attack**   Augrael deals an extra 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Swift Leap** single action  Augrael jumps up to half his Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
