**Adamantine Edges**   The ghoul's claws have been dipped in [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]]. The razorclaw treats any object they hit with a claw Strike as if it had half as much Hardness and any creature they hit as if they had half as much physical resistance.

**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The ghoul razorclaw is adjacent to the corpse of a creature that died within the last hour; **Effect** The ghoul razorclaw devours a chunk of the corpse and regains 7d6 Hit Points. It can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once.

**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 31; **Stage 1** carrier with no effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 4d6 negative damage and regains half as many hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 4d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); **Stage 6** dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight.

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living, non-[[Traits/Elf|elf]] creature hit by a ghoul razorclaw's attack must succeed at a DC 31 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. The creature can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The ghoul razorclaw deals an extra 3d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Surprise Attacker**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to the ghoul razorclaw.

**Swift Leap** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The ghoul razorclaw jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Fists Full of Gold
>
>
>
> While little is known about origin of the order of ghoul assassins who dip their claws into molten [[Equipment/Adamantine|adamantine]], survivors of razorclaw assassination attempts often find themselves with a lucrative opportunity should they be able to extract the precious metal from the claws of their would-be killers. A player character who uses an adamantine weapon or cutting tool to remove the adamantine and succeeds at a DC 35 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check for each claw (up to 10) can remove 50 gp of adamantine, which can then be sold or [[Actions/Craft|Crafted]] into adamantine weapons or armor.
