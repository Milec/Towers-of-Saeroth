**Feed on Quintessence**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|exploration]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]]) Over the course of 1 hour, the quintessivore removes and deconstructs the soul of a creature. The creature targeted by Feed On Quintessence must either be captive or have been dead for no more than 2 hours before the start of the process. At the end of the hour, the quintessivore consumes the quintessence of the creature's soul. For the next month, it gains a +1 status bonus to its spell DC and spell attack roll, and it adds 6th-level [[Spells/Never Mind|_never mind_]] and [[Spells/Vampiric Exsanguination (Player Core)|_vampiric exsanguination_]] to its arcane prepared spells.  
Restoring a creature to life whose soul has been fed upon in this way is difficult, requiring a DC 31 [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] check as part of the effort—failure indicates the attempt to restore life fails and any resources expended in the attempt are lost. Slaying the quintessivore in question ends this complication.

**Suspend Soul** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]]) The quintessivore suspends the life processes of a [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] creature within 15 feet of it. The creature can't decrease or increase its HP or dying value for 10 minutes. The creature can attempt a DC 29 Will save to avoid this effect. If the creature receives magical healing, it can attempt a new save, ending the effect and being healed normally on a success. The effect ends if the quintessivore uses Suspend Soul again.

**Tattered Soul**   A creature hit by the quintessivore's bladeleg Strike must succeed at a DC 29 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 (drained 2 on a critical failure). If the creature is already drained, it's immune to this effect.
