**Pounce** single action  The smoldering leopard Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the leopard began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], it remains hidden until after making this ability's Strike.

**Scorching Maul** single action  The smoldering leopard makes two claw Strikes against a creature it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]. A creature hit by both claw Strikes also takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. Both attacks count toward its multiple attack penalty, but the penalty increases only after both attacks are made.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The smoldering leopard deals an extra 1d4 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.
