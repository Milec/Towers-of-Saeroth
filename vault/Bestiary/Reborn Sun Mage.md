**Burning Sacrifice** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) When the sun mage is destroyed, they explode in a blast of flame that deals 6d6 fire damage in a 10-foot emanation (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail their saves are marked with [[Setting/Deities/Walkena|Walkena's]] religious symbol on a visible area of their body, which remains for 1 day. This mark allows Walkena to treat the creature as if he knew the creature's identity but not having met them for the purposes of [[Spells/Scrying|_scrying_]], lowering the spell's DC by 2. The sun mage's gear and treasure are unaffected by the explosion and left in a pile where they die.
