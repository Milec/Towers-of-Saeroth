**Part the Troops**   The hryngar battlepriest and their mount can share the same space as creatures with both the [[Traits/Duergar|hryngar]] and [[Traits/Troop|troop]] traits. Such creatures ignore the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample]] ability of any creature the battlepriest is riding.

**Toil and Trample** single action  **Requirements** The battlepriest is mounted on a creature with a [[Bestiary/Abilities/Trample]] ability; **Effect** The battlepriest commands their mount. Until the beginning of the battlepriest's next turn, the saving throw DC of the mount's Trample increases to 26, and the Trample deals an additional 3d6 evil damage.
