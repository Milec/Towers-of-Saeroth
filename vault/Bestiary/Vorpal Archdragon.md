# Vorpal Archdragon

Vorpal dragons are most notorious for their keen [[Equipment/Vorpal (GM Core)|_vorpal_]] magic. Even the least of these dragons can sever a foe's head in a single lucky stroke. Unusually, however, the subject survives as a bipartite creature: a headless body controlled by a detached head that still thinks and speaks, and which can outlive said body's destruction. It's not uncommon for would-be dragonslayers to be sent home carrying their own heads under one arm as a lesson in humility. The unluckier ones, alas, may be kept as trophies—and, in a very literal sense, conversation pieces—while the dragon devours their greater portion. Some might even wear their favorite severed heads as a macabre necklace.

**Dragon's Teeth** three actions  **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** A vorpal archdragon never fights alone save by choice, for within their pinioned wings an army of armaments stands ever ready. With a snap of their wings, the archdragon rains blades across the battlefield in a 100-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], dealing 22d6 slashing damage with a DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. These blades are partially animated and remain in the area for 10 minutes, during which time the area is considered greater [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for every creature except for the archdragon. Any creature that enters the area or begins their turn within or adjacent to this area takes 4d8+16 slashing damage (DC 44 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Feast of Blood whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Feast of Blood** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) The dragon inhales sharply, pulling blood from wounds and into the dragon's maw. Each creature in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] takes their current [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] plus 14d6 void damage (DC 45 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). The dragon gains [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to half the greatest amount of void damage dealt to a single creature this way. The dragon can't use Feast of Blood again for 1d4 rounds.

**Gruesome Cuts**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|Persistent bleed damage]] inflicted by the dragon, even magically, carries a poison that inhibits clotting. Healing the creature to full HP doesn't end the bleeding, the DC of any flat check to recover is 2 higher, and the DC to stop the bleeding using [[Actions/Administer First Aid (Player Core)|Administer First Aid]] is 41. This poison can be [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteracted]] (DC 41), and successfully doing so ends the bleeding.

**Heads Will Roll!** two actions  **Frequency** once per minute; **Effect** The vorpal dragon swoops across the battlefield, sweeping their axe-bladed tail through their foes. The dragon [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] twice; at any point during this movement, the dragon makes up to two chin blade Strikes, each against a different creature within reach. The dragon's multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until all Strikes are made.

**Vorpal Binding**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]]) The vorpal dragon beheads its foes— sometimes without killing them. A vorpal dragon's chin blade Strikes count as having the [[Equipment/Vorpal (GM Core)|_vorpal_]] weapon property rune for the purpose of [[Rules/Immunities, Weaknesses, and Resistances (GM Core)|weaknesses]]. Whenever the vorpal dragon critically succeeds on a chin blade Strike, it deals an additional 6d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]], which can be ended normally.  
When a vorpal dragon rolls a natural 20 on a chin blade Strike, the target must attempt a DC 44 Fortitude save; this has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait. On a failure, the creature is beheaded. A creature beheaded this way does not automatically die; instead, their head and body live on, disconnected but mystically linked, with the head controlling the body from afar. A beheaded creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] as they attempt to coordinate movements from an unusual perspective, or clumsy 4 and [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] if their head can't perceive their body from its location. These conditions are suppressed if the body holds its head, and don't apply to actions performed exclusively by the head, mind, or voice. If the body is killed, the head can live on, but destroying the head kills the body as well. This effect can be ended only by [[Spells/Regenerate (Player Core)|_regenerate_]], [[Spells/Breath of Life (Player Core)|_breath of life_]], or any effect that restores life to the dead; only effects that restore an incomplete corpse will replace an absent or destroyed body, however. The dragon can choose to instantly kill a creature when beheading them, as per the vorpal weapon property rune's activation.
