# Tar Zombie Snatcher

Humanoids consumed by a tar ooze become sticky warriors in the tar ooze's mindless procession. Some carry weapons.

**Vomit Tar** two actions ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Primal]]) The tar zombie vomits a gout of sticky tar in a 15-foot cone that deals 5d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail the save are [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] and gain fire weakness 5. Both conditions end after 3 rounds or when the tar is scrubbed off (which requires 3 Interact actions). The tar zombie can't Vomit Tar again for 1d4 rounds.
