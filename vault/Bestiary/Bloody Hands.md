# Bloody Hands

Bloody Hands was no agent of [[Setting/Deities/Gorum (Divine Mysteries)|Gorum]] when he first rose from the polluted swamps of a remote fen in the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]]. For the first several millennia of his life, he fought for his own domain in the sprawling slime, eventually securing a realm the size of a small continent where his rule was unquestioned—at least, until it was invaded by a host of Gorum's [[Bestiary/Einherji|einherjar]]. Bloody Hands was taken aback at the aesirs' battle prowess, and he gloried in the fall of his empire to the extent that, by the end of the invasion, he was proudly fighting on their side, mercilessly destroying hordes of demons who had so recently served him out of fear. The einherjar admired his lust for battle but didn't quite know what to make of the frog-like demon, especially when he followed them back across the [[Setting/Planes/Maelstrom (GM Core)|Maelstrom]] to Clashing Shore. After several centuries of brawling along the borders of that realm, growing increasingly less interested in sadism and more enthralled with the tactics of battle, Bloody Hands was granted permission by Gorum to become one of his servitors.

 Bloody Hands appears as a vaguely humanoid frog-like demon whose stance evokes that of a lumbering gorilla. His slimy green skin shifts to a bloody red along the length of his arms, so that his claws look as though they're constantly glistening with fresh blood. He has four beady eyes and a wide mouth filled with sharp teeth.

**Bloody Mess** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Bloody Hands slashes his claws out in all directions, causing blood from all living creatures within a 20-foot-radius to leap out and fill the air with a fine red mist. All creatures in the area must attempt a DC 37 Fortitude save—creatures that are already wounded take a –2 penalty to this saving throw. If more than 3 creatures fail this saving throw, the 20-foot-radius becomes so thick with bloody mist that all creatures in the area become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] until the end of Bloody Hands' next turn.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes 8d6 slashing damage.  
 **Failure** The creature takes 8d6 slashing damage, 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]], and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] from blood loss for 1 minute.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes 12d6 persistent bleed damage and becomes enfeebled 2 from blood loss.

**Leaping Slam** three actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]]) Bloody Hands Strides, then makes a [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] or a [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], and can land in a space occupied by creatures. All creatures in the 10-foot space he lands in must attempt a DC 37 Reflex save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature dodges aside and takes no damage, and it can choose any square adjacent to Bloody Hands to immediately move into; this movement does not trigger reactions.  
 **Success** The creature takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] into an adjacent space of Bloody Hands' choice.  
 **Failure** The creature takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage, is pushed into an adjacent space of Bloody Hands' choice, and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage, is knocked prone, and becomes pinned by Bloody Hands
