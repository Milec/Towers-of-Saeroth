**Manly Left Hook**   A creature struck by Gerhard's punch must succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (or stunned 3 on a critical failure).

**Shoddy Blunderbuss** two actions  Gerhard fires an explosive shot from his blunderbuss that deals 4d6 bludgeoning damage plus 2d10 fire damage to all creatures in a 30- foot cone (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex).  
 When firing the blunderbuss, Gerhard must succeed at a DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Engineering Lore]] check or the weapon explodes, damaging him for the same amount as the creatures in the cone and destroying itself. If someone other than Gerhard uses the weapon, they must critically succeed at the check. A successful DC 22 Crafting check is enough to confirm the weapon's unstable nature if someone examines it beforehand.  
 Reloading the blunderbuss takes 3 Interact actions: adding 1 dose of gunpowder, adding 1 grapeshot canister, and packing the contents.

**Whip Swing** two actions ([[Traits/Move]]) **Requirement** Gerhard is in a jungle, dense forest, or an area with numerous overhead beams. **Effect** Gerhard uses his whip to lash upward to wrap around an overhanging tree branch; he then swings up to 30 feet in a straight line, and must end the movement on solid ground or fall.
