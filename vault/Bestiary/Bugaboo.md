**Devour Soul** three actions ([[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) **Requirements** The bugaboo has killed a creature within the last round and is adjacent to the creature; **Effect** The bugaboo consumes the creature’s soul. The soul is utterly destroyed—nothing short of wish or a similar effect can restore it to life, and only after the bugaboo has been slain. The bugaboo regains Hit Points equal to double the level of the consumed creature. After devouring six souls within a year, a bugaboo transforms into a bogeyman.

**Mobility**   When the bugaboo uses a Stride action to move half their Speed or less, that movement does not trigger reactions.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The bugaboo deals 1d6 extra precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Striking Fear**  ([[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]) If a bugaboo scores a critical hit with a claw Strike, the target must attempt a DC 24 Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The target is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.

**Surprise Attack**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven’t acted yet are flat-footed to the bugaboo.
