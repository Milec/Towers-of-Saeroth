**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The ghoul is adjacent to the corpse of a creature that died within the last hour. **Effect** The ghoul devours a chunk of the corpse and regains 3d6 Hit Points. It can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once.

**Ghoul Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 15; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 2d6 negative damage and regains half as many Hit Points from all healing (1 day); **Stage 3** as stage 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** 2d6 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 5** as stage 4 (1 day); **Stage 6** dead, and rises as a ghoul the next midnight.

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living, non-elf creature hit by a ghoul's attack must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**Swift Leap** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The ghoul jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
