**Rise Again** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) If the Disciples are reduced to 0 Hit Points by means other than fire or positive damage, they return to unlife at the start of their next turn. They have 45 Hit Points and are [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] in the space in which they were destroyed. The Disciples can't be returned by this ability again for 1 hour, and if destroyed before this time passes, they remain truly dead.

**Agonized Howl** two actions ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The Disciples howl in pain at their cursed existence. Creatures within a 30-foot emanation take 6d8 mental damage (DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). The Disciples can't use Agonized Howl again for 1d4 rounds.

**Awful Approach** single action  The Disciples reshape their grotesque form to move swiftly. They Stride twice. Any living creature that can see the Disciples during this movement must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (sickened 2 on a critical failure). This is a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] and [[Traits/Visual|visual]] effect. The Disciples can't use Awful Approach again for 1d4 rounds.
