**Speed** 30 feet; forest passage, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Among the Trees** single action  **Requirements** Every square the woodland scouts occupy is in forest terrain; **Effect** The woodland scouts disperse among the trees. They [[Actions/Take Cover (Player Core)|Take Cover]] and then use that cover to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]], attempting a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] check.

**Forest Passage**   Woodland scouts ignore any [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by plants and fungi, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.

**Longbow Barrage** two actions  The scouts draw or reload their longbows, then send forth a flurry of arrows. This barrage is a 10-foot burst within 100 feet that deals 3d8 piercing damage with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. If the scouts are [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], this deals an additional 2d6 precision damage. When the troop is reduced to 2 or fewer segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Thicket of Blades** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The scouts engage in a coordinated melee attack against each enemy in a 5-foot emanation, with a DC 24 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage depends on the number of actions.  
 single action 1d8+3 slashing damage  
 two actions 2d8+6 slashing damage  
 three actions 3d8+9 slashing damage

**Stealthy Formation**   If the scouts become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], they remain so until they take a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]].
