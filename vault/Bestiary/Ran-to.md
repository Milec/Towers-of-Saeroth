**Body Slam** single action  **Requirements** Ran-to has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** Ran-to throws his foe against the ground. The grabbed creature takes 8d6 bludgeoning damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The grapple ends.

**Leaping Catch** two actions  Ran-to makes a mighty leap and attempts to Grab a foe while he soars. He makes a [[Actions/Leap]], [[Actions/High Jump]], or [[Actions/Long Jump]]. His maximum distance is 40 feet. He must succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to perform the leap as usual, but regardless of the type of leap, the DC is equal to the number of feet he moves in the air. Ran-to can attempt an Athletics check to [[Actions/Grapple]] at any point during the jump. Immediately after the attempt, he (and the creature, if the Grapple was successful) falls to the ground. Neither creature takes damage from this fall.

**Titan Wrestler**   Ran-to can attempt to [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple]], [[Actions/Shove]], or [[Actions/Trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than him.

**Whirlwind Toss** three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** Ran-to has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]; **Effect** Ran-to spins the creature he's holding, smashing them against any creatures within his reach, and then throws the creature aside. Ran-to attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the grabbed creature's Fortitude DC. **Critical Success** Ran-to tosses his foe a great distance. The grapple ends. The grabbed creature is thrown into a space within 10 feet, takes 10d6 bludgeoning damage, and falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. All creatures adjacent to Ran-to take the same amount of bludgeoning damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). **Success** As critical success, except the grabbed creatures is thrown into a space within 5 feet, and creatures take 6d6 bludgeoning damage. **Failure** Ran-to tosses his foe aside. The grapple ends. The grabbed creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. **Critical Failure** Ran-to loses hold of his foe and the grapple ends.
