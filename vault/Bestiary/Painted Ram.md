**Construct Armor** Like normal objects, a painted ram has Hardness. This Hardness reduces any damage it takes by an amount equal to the Hardness. Once a painted ram is reduced to less than half its Hit Points, or immediately upon being damaged by a critical hit, its construct armor breaks, removing the Hardness and reducing its Armor Class to 23.

**Sticky Paint**    A painted ram's Strikes splatter paint on the target. On a critical hit, this paint swiftly dries, and the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 10 minutes. As an Interact action, the target or an adjacent creature can remove enough paint to end the condition.
