# Garadasura

Garadasuras are massive ophidian spirits who hold to duties of guardianship and butchery. Many are former [[Bestiary/Families/Naga (Monster Core)|naga]] who have given up on their role of stewards of reality and instead turned their efforts to reversing the act of creation. Garadasuras often continue the duties they had as naga but can also turn destructive, rampaging with the force of an entire poisonous legion.
