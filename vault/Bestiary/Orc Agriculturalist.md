**Herbal Poison** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) The agriculturalist quickly turns some of their supplies of poisonous herbs into an herbal poison, then applies it to a melee weapon or piece of ammunition in their possession. The next successful attack with a weapon poisoned this way deals an additional 1d6 poison damage. The applied poison fades after its damage is applied to an attack or 1 minute passes, whichever happens first.

**Poison Detector** two actions  The orc agriculturalist attempts a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Farming Lore]] or [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check to determine whether an object is [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] or has been poisoned. The DC is the poison's DC (if any), or the standard DC of the poison's level. On a critical success, they also learn the number and types of poison involved.
