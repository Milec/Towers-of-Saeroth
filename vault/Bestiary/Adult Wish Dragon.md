# Adult Wish Dragon

What is a wish? A wish dragon would call it the truest expression of self—each individual wish reveals something of deep importance to the wisher, and no two wishes are ever quite the same. Whether the wish is one of creation or destruction, justice or vengeance, love or hate, wish dragons seek to understand what makes it important to the wisher and view their ability to make it a reality as a gift that helps them make sense of the world.

**Best Wishes** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) 20 feet. Being near a wish dragon gives other creatures a greater sense of what they truly wish for, bolstering their sense of purpose while also distracting them from the task at hand. Creatures in the aura take a –1 status penalty to Perception but gain a +2 status bonus to Will saving throws against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. The wish dragon can't gain the benefit of their own aura or other actions that use the aura, and they can choose to exclude any creatures from any benefit of the aura or action that uses the aura.  
**Wished Away** ⤾ **Trigger** The dragon is critically hit by an attack; **Effect** The dragon wishes not to be hurt by this attack, gaining [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 20 to all damage against the triggering attack.

**Breath of Loss** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The dragon breathes a warm, gentle breeze of mist that creates the feeling of when deepest desires are achieved. As the breeze dissipates, the feeling of loss overwhelms creatures' senses, dealing 14d6 mental damage in a 40-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 34 Will save). A creature that critically fails its save is also consumed with the memory of the moment and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1. The wish dragon can't use Breath of Loss again for 1d4 rounds.

**Destructive Wish** two actions  **Requirements** An illusion from Wish Come True is currently active; **Effect** The wish dragon [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismisses]] the illusion of Wish Come True, which shatters into magical fragments, dealing 7d6 force damage with a DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save to all creatures in a 40-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of the illusion.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges Breath of Loss whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Grant Wish** three actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per year; **Effect** The wish dragon instantly grants the benefits of a critical success with the [[Rituals/Wish|_wish_]] ritual to a mortal creature. This has no cost. That creature specifies what they wish for, but the interpretation is up to the wish dragon. A summoned wish dragon can't use this ability.

**Wish Come True** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Frequency** once per 10 minutes; **Effect** The dragon creates an illusion in an unoccupied square within 60 feet that appears to all other creatures that can see it to be the manifestation of their deepest material desire. This has the effects of [[Spells/Illusory Object (Player Core)|_illusory object_]] (though the illusion might appear to be a creature to certain people) that last for 10 minutes. The wish dragon can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the illusion. Each non-wish dragon creature who begins their turn within 200 feet in [[Rules/Line of Sight (Player Core)|line of sight]] of the illusion must attempt a DC 34 Will save. If any creature within reach of the illusion [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interacts]] to touch it, the illusion ends and that creature is invigorated, gaining 2d6 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] that last for 1 minute.  
**Critical Success** The creature disbelieves the illusion and becomes temporarily immune to Wish Come True for 1 hour.  
**Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is stupefied 2 for 1 round.
