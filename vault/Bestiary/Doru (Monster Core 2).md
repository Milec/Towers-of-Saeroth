# Doru

These deceivers whisper fetid lies, sowing dark and dread inspiration into foolish mortal ears. Dorus serve as the spies and messengers of the divs. With silver tongues and wicked motives, they often pledge themselves to vainglorious and deluded spellcasters, whom they ply with wild conspiracies and rumors of deception among friends and foes alike; in the process, they push supposed masters to despotism and murderous revenge. All the while, dorus wean secrets and weaknesses from their marks. In the end, nearly every doru turns on their supposed liege, destroying the reputation and even causing the death of the person the doru pretended to serve.

**Covetous of Secrets** Dorus have a weakness for secrets, hoarding them like a miser hoards gold. A creature can tempt a doru with some bit of obscure knowledge the doru doesn't know or thinks they don't know. Presenting the hint of the secret is a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] and [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] traits, and requires a skill check using [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] (or some other appropriate skill determined by the GM) against the doru's Will DC. On a success, the doru is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] for as long as the presenter draws out the explanation of the secret (spending 1 action each round doing so, to a maximum of 1 minute). On a critical success, the doru is fascinated for that duration plus 1 minute more as they ponder the implications of the secret. Regardless of the outcome, the doru is temporarily immune to that creature's attempts to present them with secrets for 1 day.
