# Adamantine Archdragon

The powerful adamantine dragons are one of several dragons known as skymetal dragons. The innate magic that flows through these dragons causes them to draw particular metals to their bodies like magnets or, in some cases, naturally grow these skymetals on their bodies. Adamantine dragons begin their lives with tough scales that are naturally replaced with thicker and even tougher adamantine plating as they grow older. Adamantine dragons are typically steadfast and loyal. Once they commit to a certain purpose, changing their minds is nigh impossible.

**Adamantine Body**   The dragon's unarmed melee Strikes are [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]].

**Adamantine Weight**  ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon shapes rocks even after it throws them, capturing its prey. The creature struck must succeed at a DC 44 Reflex save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]]. **Fast Swallow** ⤾  **Trigger** The dragon [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grabs]] a creature; **Effect** The dragon uses [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole (Monster Core)|Swallow Whole]].

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one tail Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Avalanche Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Rock Tunneler**   The dragon can burrow through solid stone at a Speed of 40 feet. They can leave a tunnel behind if they desire.

**Sky Below Stone** two actions ([[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) An eruption of skymetal blades churns the earth in a 40-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]. Creatures that fail the save are knocked prone and moved 20 feet in a direction of the dragon's choice.
