**Death Burst** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) When the bloodsiphon dies, its body explodes in a cloudy red burst of necrotic dried blood in a 20-foot area. Creatures in range must attempt a DC 21 Reflex save.

**Success** The creature takes 2d6 negative damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes 4d6 negative damage.  
**Critical Failure** The creature takes 6d6 negative damage
