# Faithless Ecclesiarch

Faithless ecclesiarchs were powerful religious leaders in life—archpriests, hierarchs, oracles, and divine servitors personally blessed by their deities. They are adept at corrupting the pious and inspiring their fellow herexens, often becoming leaders among their kind. This faithless ecclesiarch once worshipped [[Setting/Deities/Nethys]], god of magic.

**Siphon Faith** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) **Trigger** A creature fails a saving throw against a spell cast by the faithless ecclesiarch; **Effect** The faithless ecclesiarch siphons the triggering creature's spiritual energy to fuel their own magic. The triggering creature is stupefied 1 for 1 round, and the faithless ecclesiarch regains 1 Focus Point.
