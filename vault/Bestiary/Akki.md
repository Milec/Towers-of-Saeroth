**Rage** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) **Requirements** Akki isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] or raging; **Effect** Akki taps into her inner fury and begins raging. She gains 12 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]]. The rage lasts for 1 minute or until Akki falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], whichever comes first. While raging, her melee Strikes deal an additional 6 electricity damage, and she can't use actions that have the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, except for [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]]. When Akki stops raging, she loses any remaining temporary Hit Points from Rage, and she can't gain temporary Hit Points from using the Rage action again for 1 minute.

**Shock and Awe**   A creature critically hit by Akki's lightning cry Strike is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 round.
