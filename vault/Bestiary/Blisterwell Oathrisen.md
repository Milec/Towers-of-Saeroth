# Blisterwell Oathrisen

Most undead are reanimated without consent, but those known as oathrisen arise willingly—at least at first. These loyal subordinates swear themselves to a cause or leader, vowing to assist even in death. Within days of their death, their oath reanimates them and restores most of their memories. In theory, an oathrisen's existence lasts no more than a year. In practice, the cause's leader might demand their service indefinitely. If denied their afterlife too long, some oathrisen turn on their former masters, afterward either becoming independent or destroying themselves.

 The oathrisen presented here is a variant that frequents Blisterwell during the events of this Adventure Path. Other regions and traditions might create oathrisen with different abilities.

**Armored Disguise** A Blisterwell oathrisen's undead nature is not obvious. While wearing armor, an oathrisen gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] a living [[Bestiary/Families/Orc (Monster Core)|orc]] and does not require a [[Equipment/Disguise Kit (Player Core)|disguise kit]] to do so. Creatures that don't notice the disguise are not subject to the oathrisen's Frightful Presence. An oathrisen can abandon its disguise as a free action with the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait when it attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] a creature.  
**Drop the Mask** Once a Blisterwell oathrisen is reduced to less than half its Hit Points, portions of its armor break off. This reduces its AC by 2, increases its Speed by 5 feet, and gives it weakness 5 to [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage. This also immediately exposes nearby creatures to the oathrisen's frightful presence.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Knight by Another Name
>
>
>
> An oathrisen resembles a [[Bestiary/Families/Graveknight (Monster Core)|graveknight]]— both being heavily armored, corporeal, undead warriors. Yet a graveknight arises through its own unholy ambitions and is inextricably bound to its armor. By comparison, an oathrisen reanimates out of loyalty and is not intrinsically malicious. Some oathrisen even resent graveknights, taking special joy in destroying these unholy copycats.
