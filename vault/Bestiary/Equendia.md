**Great Despair** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) 30 feet. Living creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] while in Equendia's great despair aura. They can't naturally recover from this fear while in the area but recover instantly once they leave it. When a creature first enters the area, it must succeed at a DC 27 Will save (after taking the penalty from being frightened) or be [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1d4 rounds. The creature is then temporarily immune for 24 hours.

**Blackfrost Rot**  ([[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) As [[Rules/Curses/Blackfrost|blackfrost]], but DC 30.

**Frozen Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) Equendia exhales a 60-foot cone of icy shards that deal 6d6 cold and 6d6 slashing damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). She can't use Frozen Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Hissing Command** single action ([[Traits/Auditory]]) Equendia utters a swift command in [[Setting/Languages/Necril]] to strike at adjacent enemies. Any blackfrost zombies within 30 feet can use a reaction to attempt a melee Strike.

**Invasive Blackfrost**   If a PC is critically hit by any of Equendia's Strikes, the wound takes on an unnerving blackened appearance, as if from advanced frostbite or charring. For the next 24 hours, the PC is no longer immune to [[Rules/Curses/Blackfrost|blackfrost]].
