**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The raja rakshasa takes on the appearance of any Medium [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]]. This doesn't change the raja rakshasa's Speed or its attack and damage modifiers with its Strikes but might change the damage type its Strikes deal (typically to bludgeoning). It typically loses its fangs Strike unless the humanoid form has fangs or a similar unarmed attack.

**Disturbing Vision** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]]) The raja rakshasa's eyes flash green as it projects a telepathic wave in a 30-foot emanation. All creatures in the area are assailed by a vision of evil decadence from one of the rakshasa's past lives. Each non-evil creature in the area must succeed at a DC 32 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] on a critical failure). The visions last 1d4 rounds, and while they do, the raja rakshasa and all evil creatures in the area gain a +1 status bonus to all checks. The raja rakshasa can use this ability again only once the previous visions end.
