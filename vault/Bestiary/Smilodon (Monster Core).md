# Smilodon

Smilodons are large saber-toothed cats, apex predators that are significantly more muscular and broader than the other species of big cats. They often kill prey with a quick stab to the throat or other vulnerable spot. The smilodon's oversized fangs are particularly sought after as trophies.

**Pierce Armor** single action  The smilodon makes a fangs Strike against a creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]. If this Strike breaks a creature's armor or damages a creature who is unarmored or wearing broken armor, the creature also takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. **Pounce** single action  The smilodon Strides and makes a Strike at the end of that movement. If the smilodon began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], it remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.
