**Bar Brawler**   The barkeep has been in enough bar fights to know how to throw a few punches. When fighting in their bar and making a [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attack, the barkeep gains a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls and deals an additional 1d4 damage.

**Barkeep's Advice** three actions ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The barkeep gives some pertinent advice to a single creature other than themself. For 24 hours, when that creature fails a skill check or saving throw, they can recall this advice and reroll the check, using the second result instead. Once that creature uses this ability, its effect ends. A creature that receives the Barkeep's Advice is temporarily immune to the ability for 1 month.
