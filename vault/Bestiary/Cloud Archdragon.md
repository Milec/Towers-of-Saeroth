# Cloud Archdragon

Cloud dragons are the primal dragons of sky and mist. Long, long ago, in the days before folks wrote things down with any sort of consistency, cloud dragons might have been visitors to Golarion, hailing first from the endless air beyond reality. If so, they've settled in quite comfortably. Cloud dragons dwell in the high places of the world, nesting atop the peaks of mountains or the tallest of trees of alpine forests. They revel in flight even more than other dragons, and fly higher than most any of their kin, to the places where the air grows thin and the sky takes on the shades of night. They travel often and journey far.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one horn Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Thundercloud Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Dragonmist** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) The dragon conjures up a sudden mist in a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 120 feet. All creatures within the mist become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the mist become concealed to creatures within it. The dragon can use this action again to [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|dismiss]] the mist—anyone else attempting to remove the dragonmist (via [[Spells/Gust of Wind (Player Core)|_gust of wind_]] or similar) must succeed at a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check. An archdragon dragon can have up to six clouds of dragonmist active at a time.

**Mist Step** single action ([[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]) **Requirements** The dragon is within a cloud of dragonmist; **Effect** The dragon dissolves into wisps of cloud, reforming within another cloud of dragonmist within 1 mile.

**Shapes in the Fog** single action ([[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) With a cloud dragon's mastery of their element, creating shapes and images in a fog is child's play. All creatures within or adjacent to one cloud of dragonmist must attempt a DC 45 Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round (on a critical failure, they are also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1).

**Thundercloud Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon breathes a thundercloud that deals 23d6 electricity damage in a 70-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 45 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). They can't use Thundercloud Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Toothsome Air**   An archdragon's mist becomes akin to an extension of their body, to be shaped into fangs and claws. Their jaws and claw Strikes can originate from any square of dragonmist that's within 100 feet of the dragon.
