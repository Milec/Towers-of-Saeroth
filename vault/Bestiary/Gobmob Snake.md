**Incessant Yammering** ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]]) 15 feet. A gobmob snake's heads constantly bicker and snipe at one another, annoying and distracting anyone nearby. Each non-goblin creature that begins its turn in the aura must attempt a DC 21 Will save. On a failure, it takes a –1 status penalty to Perception checks and Will saves for 1 round. On a success, it is temporarily immune for 1 minute.  
**Infighting** Whenever a gobmob snake critically fails at an attack roll or skill check, it must succeed at a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] or become [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 as its heads argue over which of them is to blame. An enemy can provoke an argument by attempting a DC 20 [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] check as a single action with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]], and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits.  
**Coiled Strike** ⤾ As [[Bestiary/Abilities/Reactive Strike]], but the gobmob snake can use this reaction only if it's Coiled.

**Coil** single action  The gobmob snake uses an action to coil itself, increasing its reach with its fangs from 5 to 10 feet. After the goblin snake Strikes with its fangs, it becomes uncoiled.

**Goblin Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|Olfactory]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) The gobmob snake belches a cloud of nauseating vapor in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Non-goblin creatures within the cloud must succeed at a DC 20 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1. On a critical failure, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for as long as it is sickened. Creatures that successfully save are immune for 24 hours. The goblin snake can't use Goblin Breath again for 1d4 rounds.
