**Rejuvenation** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When a ghost is destroyed, it re-forms after 2d4 days within the location it’s bound to, fully healed. A ghost can be permanently destroyed only if someone determines the reason for its existence and sets right whatever prevents the spirit from resting.  
 Setting right the injustice that led to the commoner's death allows it to move on to the afterlife.

**Frightful Moan** single action ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]) The ghost laments its fate, forcing each living creature within 30 feet to attempt a DC 21 Will save. On a failure, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] (or frightened 3 on a critical failure). On a success, a creature is temporarily immune to this ghost's frightful moan for 1 minute.
