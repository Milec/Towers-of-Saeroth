# Zealot

Zealots ferret out plots against their religion and seek justice for their church's followers. This zealot serves [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus (Divine Mysteries)|Asmodeus]], but others might serve [[Setting/Deities/Abadar (Divine Mysteries)|Abadar]], [[Setting/Deities/Calistria (Divine Mysteries)|Calistria]], [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae (Divine Mysteries)|Iomedae]], [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber]], [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]], or [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]]. They often ride a [[Bestiary/War Horse (Monster Core)|war horse]] (Monster Core 201) wearing light [[Equipment/Barding (Player Core)|barding]]. To depict a zealot's mount, add this horse to the encounter as an additional monster with its own actions, adjusting the encounter's [[Rules/XP Budget (GM Core)|XP budget]] accordingly.
