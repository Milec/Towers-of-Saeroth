**Devil's Cursed Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Jordus bites down on his tongue (taking 1 slashing damage), then exhales a 15-foot cone of burning smoke. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 26 Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 3d8 fire damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 7d8 fire damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 by the agonizing pain of the burns.  

**Critical Failure** he creature takes 10d8 fire damage, is sickened 1 by the agonizing pain of the burns, and becomes cursed. The victim's burns never fully vanish, and the cursed creature takes a –2 status penalty to all attacks from the constant pain. Healing doesn't alter the appearance of the burns or lessen the penalty, but removing the curse does.
