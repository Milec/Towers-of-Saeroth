# Anguished Flame

Ancient tales tell that the elemental lord [[Setting/Deities/Atreia (Divine Mysteries)|Atreia]] created his six-winged children to steward mortal prayers into the realms of the divine. These elementals work hand-in-hand with celestial and fiendish deific servants to accept sacrifices and offerings, but they also bless contrite mortals with absolution and purification, helping them to change the fate that awaits them after death.

**Collect Prayer** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The anguished flame compels a creature they can see within 60 feet, who must attempt a DC 30 Will save.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature immediately uses its reaction to pray.  
**Failure** The creature prays. It is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and can't use reactions for 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is slowed 2.

**Focus Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Requirements** The anguished flame's solar flare aura is active; **Effect** The anguished flame fixes their fiery eyes on a creature they can see within 30 feet. The target must immediately attempt a Fortitude save against the anguished flame's solar flare. If the creature was already [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] by solar flare before attempting its save, a failed save causes it to become [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the end of its next turn.

> ## Ages In Darkness
>
>
>
> Over the eon of Lord [[Setting/Deities/Atreia (Divine Mysteries)|Atreia's]] imprisonment inside the Garnet Brand, temples to the Lambent King lay dormant and decaying across the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]], and within those temples, his children, known as ygnaires, began to fade. Without the light of their Lord of Fire, these elementals who waned became known as anguished flames. They eventually turned to unmoving bronze, frozen until Atreia's light shone down on them again.
