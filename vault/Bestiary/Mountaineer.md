**Experienced Steps** A mountaineer isn't impeded by [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by snow or ice. They gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] on slippery ice.  
**Professional Climber** While climbing, the mountaineer can have up to five allies [[Actions/Follow the Expert (Player Core)|Following the Expert]] and grants a +3 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]].
