# Dromaar Company

Numerous [[Traits/Dromaar|dromaar]] mercenary companies operate in and around Belkzen, providing their strength at arms to any hold that requests their services. These disciplined warriors march confidently into battle, bringing their axes to bear against all who stand in their way.

**Speed** 25 feet; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Bola Hurl** two actions  The dromaars draw bolas and toss them in a 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature in this area takes 3d6 nonlethal bludgeoning damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature who fails this saving throw is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

**Charge the Fallen** two actions  The dromaar company Strides up to twice, sweeping with their axes. They deal 2d8+5 slashing damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] at the end of their movement. This damage increases by 5 if the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

**Disciplined Strikes** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The dromaars coordinate melee attacks with their axes against all enemies in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The damage depends on the number of actions.  
single action 1d8 slashing damage  
two actions 2d8+5 slashing damage  
three actions 2d8+10 slashing damage
