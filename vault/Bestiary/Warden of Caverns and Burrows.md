**Bury Beneath Stone** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Requirements** The warden has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]; **Effect** The warden attempts to wedge a creature they have grabbed in their mandibles within a crack in the earth that opens beneath them. The warden attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against the grabbed creature's Reflex DC. If they succeed, they bury the creature in the ground. The creature takes 10d10 bludgeoning damage. It is also [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]], and has to hold its breath or start [[Rules/Mounted Defenses (Player Core)|suffocating]] until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 42). If the creature is still buried at the end of its turn, it takes 5d10 bludgeoning damage.

**Called to Depths**   The depths call to the warden, especially as they move through the air. When they [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]], they can use any unused movement from the Long Jump to [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]]. Additionally, the warden can Burrow through any earthen matter, including rock. When they do so, they move at their full burrow Speed.

**Erupting Jump** two actions ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden draws out a stream of lava, then jumps off the solidifying mass as it cools. The warden [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leaps]] up to twice their Speed. When they land, the force of the impact deals 6d12 bludgeoning damage to all creatures within a 15-foot emanation with a DC 42 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet away from the warden.

**Obsidian Cage**   Rapidly cooling obsidian clings to the target's body and stiffens around their limbs. The target must attempt a DC 42 Reflex save. On a failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (DC 42). On a critical failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for as long as they remain immobilized.

**Vibratory Excavation** two actions ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden screeches with a resonant call that tears apart earth and stone. They create a 10-foot-square, 50-foot-deep pit in earthen material or stone within 60 feet.

**Wall Cling**   The Warden of Caverns and Burrows clings to surfaces with their segmented feet. They do not need to use a hand to hold on to walls or ceilings.
