**Bubonic Plague**  ([[Traits/Disease]]) A creature can't remove the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] condition while infected; **Saving Throw** DC 24 Fortitude; **Onset** 1 day; **Stage 1** fatigued (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] and fatigued (1 day); **Stage 3** enfeebled 3, fatigued, and take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] every 1d20 minutes (1 day)

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Zridi can take the appearance of any Small or Medium [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] or [[Traits/Animal|animal]]. This doesn't change her Speed or her attack and damage modifiers with the Strikes, but it might change the damage type her Strikes deal.

**Compelled Performance** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Zridi grants the target a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks for one week. The target must attempt a DC 26 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** No further effect.  
 **Success** The target must [[Actions/Perform]] for 1 action on its next turn.  
 **Failure** The target must Perform for 3 actions on its next turn, undertaking a Performance task set by Zridi.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but Zridi can choose to delay the effect and trigger it at any point in the next week as a reaction.

**Tormenting Dreams** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Zridi torments a sleeping creature within 100 feet with visions of betrayals by loved ones and friends. The target must attempt a DC 26 Will save, with the effects of the [[Spells/Nightmare|_nightmare_]] spell.
