# Cuckoo Hag

Cuckoo hags seek out youths who are unhappy with their lives, disguising themselves as a trusted figure such as a friend or family member. These hags then weave magical, idealized worlds out of illusions and dream stuff to trap their victims. Once they convince a mortal to stay in their magical world forever, the cuckoo hag drains their prey of life force and imprisons their soul.

 A cuckoo hag's true form is a creature with the cracked pale skin of a broken porcelain doll and an almost impossibly thin neck and limbs.

**Cuckoo's Lair**   A cuckoo hag is intrinsically connected to a pocket dimension that serves as their home domain. The lair is an extradimensional space that is 40 feet wide, 40 feet deep, and 30 feet tall. It contains whatever mundane food, buildings, landscapes, and objects that the cuckoo hag desires, though anything removed from the cuckoo's lair dissolves into nothingness instantly.  
 In order to enter or leave the plane, the cuckoo hag must create a door in the physical world as a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] and [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] traits. The door is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] by default, though the cuckoo hag can choose a target creature that can perceive the door normally. The cuckoo hag can Dismiss this door from reality, but only if they (and no other creature) are inside of their lair and alone.

**Soul Covenant**  ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) **Requirement** The cuckoo hag can cast [[Spells/Seize Soul|_seize soul_]] at will, but only to target a creature who has agreed to stay with them forever. They typically do so as soon as the agreement is made but might hold off to gain leverage over a situation. They typically place the soul into a construct, typically a [[Bestiary/Soulbound Doll|soulbound doll]] if they want a companion or a more powerful construct if they want a guardian.
