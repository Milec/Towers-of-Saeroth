**Disorienting Tumble**   Hakusa leaps around and over her opponents, disorienting them, to gain the upper hand in combat. When she successfully [[Actions/Tumble Through|Tumbles Through]] a creature's space, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of Hakusa's turn.

**Powerful Fists**   Hakusa's fist Strikes are treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] and [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]. She doesn't take penalties when making lethal attacks.

**Pressure Point Attack** two actions  Hakusa targets a foe's pressure points to debilitate them. Hakusa makes a fist Strike. On a hit, the target takes damage as normal and must attempt a DC 35 Fortitude save. Regardless of the result of the save, the target is temporarily immune to Pressure Point Attack for 1 minute.  
**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target's muscles become temporarily weaker. For 1 round, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] and must succeed at a DC 5 flat check when attempting to use actions with the [manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=639) trait or the action is lost.  
**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute, the flat check is DC 11, and the target also takes –10-foot status penalty to their Speed for the duration.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Hakusa's Strikes deal an additional 3d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.
