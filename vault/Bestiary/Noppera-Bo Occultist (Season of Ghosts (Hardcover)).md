**Remove Face** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Requirements** The noppera-bo occultist is [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonating]] a creature with a face; **Effect** The noppera-bo occultist's facial features melt into nothingness, and they menace a creature within 30 feet. The creature must succeed at a DC 18 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Impersonate Shape** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]]) The noppera-bo occultist changes into a version of a Small or Medium humanoid they've touched during the last 24 hours. While transformed and their face is covered, the occultist gains a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] that creature. As long as the noppera-bo occultist has a face, they lose their no face ability and their listed immunities. The noppera-bo occultist can change back by using this ability again.

**Steal Face** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Requirements** The noppera-bo occultist is faceless; **Effect** The noppera-bo occultist targets one creature within 30 feet. The creature must attempt a DC 18 Fortitude save.  
**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the start of its next turn.  
**Failure** The noppera-bo gains the target's face. The target's face vanishes until the end of its next turn. Until then, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]], can't speak or ingest, and can't smell. The target must also hold its breath or begin to suffocate.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the effect doesn't end automatically. The target can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such attempt.
