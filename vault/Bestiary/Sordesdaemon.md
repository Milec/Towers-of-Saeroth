# Sordesdaemon (Pollution Daemon)

Hulks of sewage and daemonic flesh, sordesdaemons embody death through pollution and are among the newest types of daemons to appear on Golarion. Sordesdaemons are fiendishly clever and often seek to inspire mortals with new ideas and inventions that despoil the environment.

**Miasma of Pollution** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Disease|disease]]) 30 feet. Creatures in the aura can't reduce the value of the [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] condition. A creature that enters the aura or begins its turn in it must succeed at a DC 34 Fortitude save or be sickened 2 (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] as long as it is sickened on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute. Creatures made of water (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water|water elementals]]) and [[Traits/Plant|plant]] creatures use an outcome one degree of success worse than the result of their save.

**Pollution Infusion**  ([[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent]]) Non-[[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] creatures adjacent to the afflicted creature take a –1 circumstance penalty to saving throws against disease; **Saving Throw** DC 36 Fortitude; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] and drained 1 (1 day); **Stage 3** doomed 1 and drained 2 (1 day); **Stage 4** doomed 2 and drained 2 (1 week); **Stage 5** dead.

**Retch of Foulness** two actions ([[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) The sordesdaemon exhales a spray of sewage that deals 8d6 acid damage and 8d6 poison damage in a 30-foot cone (DC 40 basic Fortitude save). It can't use Retch of Foulness again for 1d4 rounds.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Daemonic Pollution
>
>
>
> Sordesdaemons who aren't compelled to pursue a specific task often find their way into sewers beneath large cities, where they subjugate other creatures that wallow in filth, such as [[Bestiary/Ofalth|ofalths]] and [[Bestiary/Otyugh|otyughs]]. They think nothing of sacrificing these minions if it advances their own aims.
