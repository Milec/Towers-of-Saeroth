# Stoneriver

A servant of [[Setting/Deities/Torag]], Stoneriver resembles a pangolin with glowing blue eyes and brassy, rune-covered scales. Stoneriver burrows through Golarion's mantle and crust as he hunts down subterranean fiends, aids virtuous communities, and defends sacred sites, often collaborating with dwarves.

**Devour Magic**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) Whenever Stoneriver deals damage with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole]], he consumes one of the swallowed creature's magical effects, as [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] with a +21 [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] modifier.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Improved Grab]]** free action  

**Measure Soul** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Mental]]) Stoneriver mentally pries through the mind of a creature within 60 feet, dealing 10d8 mental damage (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). Stoneriver can choose to make this damage nonlethal or choose not to damage the creature at all. For 1 minute, Stoneriver gains a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls, damage rolls, and Perception checks against that creature. If the creature is evil and it failed its save, the creature is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] (frightened 4 on a critical failure). Stoneriver can't use Measure Soul again for 1d4 rounds.

**Rock Tunneler**   Stoneriver can burrow through solid stone, leaving behind a tunnel if he desires.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Forgeheart Five
>
>
>
> Stoneriver has fathered numerous children through the years, each clever, brave, and bold. Among these, five have joined together to combat evil as a team. Calling themselves the Forgeheart Five, these overly optimistic and bombastic creatures strive to be as virtuous and heroic as their father. Thus far, their methods have been more flashy than effective.
