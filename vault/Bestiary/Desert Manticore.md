# Desert Manticore

Desert manticores are far more dangerous than their common cousins and only marginally cleverer. Instead of the usually spiked tail reminiscent of a porcupine, desert manticores have large scorpion tails, requiring them to adopt closer approaches and cunning ambushes. Desert manticores are known for their egos and constant attempts to outmatch [[Bestiary/Families/Sphinx|sphinxes]] within their chosen territories. No accounts of such attempts have ever proved fruitful for the desert manticore.
