**Go for the Eyes** ⤾  **Trigger** Raskus critically hits with a beak Strike; **Effect** Raskus attempts to peck out his victim's eyes. The target must attempt a DC 29 Fortitude save.  
 **Success** The target is unaffected.  
 **Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.  
 **Critical Failure** The target is blinded until healed to its maximum Hit Points.

**Opportunistic Attack**   Raskus's Strikes deal an additional 3d6 damage to any foe that has a reduction to its Speed, is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]], or is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]].

**Quick Spiker**   Raskus requires only 1 action, rather than 2 actions, to reload a [[Equipment/Spike Launcher (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|_spike launcher_]]. He can change his grip on a spike launcher easily, improving its [[Traits/Fatal Aim (Guns & Gears (Remastered))|fatal aim d12]] trait to [[Traits/Fatal|fatal d12]]. (These changes are incorporated into its ranged Strike, above.
