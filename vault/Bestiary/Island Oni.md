# Island Oni

Island oni are those powerful enough to claim small coastal islands, often creating makeshift armies of lesser oni who pillage surrounding lands and waters. Island oni hoard this material wealth, garbing themselves in luxurious clothing and adorning their monstrous faces with all manner of jewelry.

 Island oni typically scavenge sunken shipwrecks for weapons, armor, and treasure. This can also bring them into contact with undead sailors, which island oni have been known to press into service.

**Bean Panic** Oni are curiously afraid of beans, especially as the seasons begin to change. If a creature Interacts to throw a handful of beans at the oni, the oni becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]]. While frightened this way, their weakness to spirit damage is increased by 5. The oni then becomes immune to bean panic for 24 hours.  
**Lost Oni Island** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) An island oni can claim an island of up to 1-mile radius in a process that takes 1 week, during which the oni must defeat any who come to challenge its claim. If successful, the oni can freely control the weather on its island and in a 1-mile radius from the shore, with the effect of a successful [[Rituals/Control Weather (Player Core)|_control weather_]] ritual. This altered weather surrounds the island in thick fog, seaborne mirages, or other phenomena that increase the DC of checks to locate and navigate to the island (Such as [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Sailing Lore]] or [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]) to 40, though the oni can allow allies to pass freely. If the oni dies or leaves the island, the weather returns to normal immediately.

The target takes 6d6 electricity damage with a DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature is also impaled on the spear. It's [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], and if the oni moves, they bring the grabbed creature along with them. The island oni doesn't need to use additional actions to keep the creature grabbed; the creature remains grabbed as long as it's impaled. The grabbed creature can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] as normal. The island oni can only have one creature impaled this way at a time.
