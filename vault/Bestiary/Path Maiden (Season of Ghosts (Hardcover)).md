# Path Maiden

Worshippers in Shenmen who devote their every breath to [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]] only to defy the goddess with their last sometimes transform, upon death, into path maidens. The garments all path maidens wear are the last vestiges of their former faith, a permanent reminder of their disgrace.

**Limited Flight**   A path maiden has a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 25 feet but can't fly higher than 10 feet above the ground. If she travels above a nonsolid surface, she vanishes and rematerializes on the last space she occupied over solid ground and becomes stunned 1. Limited flight allows a path maiden to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] any incline that requires an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check of DC 25 or lower by treating the incline as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] but is blocked by climbs requiring higher DCs.

**Scorn Fate** two actions ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The path maiden targets a creature she can see. The creature must attempt a DC 32 Will save. On a failure, the creature's next roll is cursed. If the result of the roll is a critical success, it becomes a regular success. A success becomes a failure, and a failure becomes a critical failure. That creature is then temporarily immune to Scorn Fate for 24 hours.

**Vengeful Frenzy** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The path maiden targets a creature within 60 feet that she can see. The target must attempt a DC 32 Will save. On a failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] until the end of its next turn, but it can't attack path maidens or itself as the result of this confusion.

> ## Gossamer Sash
>
>
>
> The jorogumo of Shenmen sometimes grant these white and violet [[Equipment/Gossamer Sash (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|sashes]] to those who ask them for aid in contacting path maidens for advice.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Path Nomads
>
>
>
> Path maidens almost always manifest in feminine forms. Those who manifest in masculine forms are instead known as path nomads. These undead have the same abilities but tend to travel more widely; they represent turning one's back on [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma's]] role as a prophet and manipulator of destiny rather than of her roles over birth and death.
