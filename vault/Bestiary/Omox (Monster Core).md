# Omox (Slime Demon)

Seemingly made from living, animated filth, omoxes have no true anatomy, although they generally spend most of their time in roughly [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] shapes, resembling some grim caricatures of half-melted humanoids. While scholars once believed these foul demons to be a pure, concentrated form of the corruption that suffuses the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]] and its inhabitants, in truth these demons arise from the souls of those who routinely befouled and polluted their surroundings in life.

**Cleanly Vulnerability** An omox embodies filth, and they find the concept of cleanliness abhorrent. An omox subjected to an effect that cleans them, such as the tidy command of [[Spells/Prestidigitation (Player Core)|_prestidigitation_]], takes 2d6 mental damage. They also take this damage the first time each round a creature hit by one of the omox's attacks spends actions cleaning off the filth.
