# Ifrit

The fierce and unforgiving ifrits hail from the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Fire (GM Core)|Plane of Fire]], where they build metropolises and trade centers that draw extraplanar travelers. Many ifrits are tyrannical or warmongering, and most use their might to accomplish their goals.

**Burning Grasp**  ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]) When the ifrit [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabs]]or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrains]]a creature that creature takes 2d6 fire damage and takes 2d6 fire damage at the end of each of its turns until freed.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]) The ifrit transforms into a Small or Medium [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Fire (Monster Core)|fire elemental]] or reptile, such as a snake. This doesn't affect their statistics but could change the damage type of their Strikes.

**Combat Grab** single action  **Requirements** The ifrit has a hand free; **Effect** The ifrit makes a melee Strike. If the Strike hits, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] in the ifrit's free hand.

**Wings of Flame** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]]) The ifrit grows flaming wings from their back. They gain a fly Speed of 35 feet for 1 minute. The flames also create an aura in a 5-foot emanation around the ifrit. Any creature that ends its turn in the aura takes 2d6 fire damage with a DC 25 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
