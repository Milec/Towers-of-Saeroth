**Enormous** A portal eater is a massive creature and takes up a space of 10 squares by 10 squares (50 feet by 50 feet).  
**Eat Portal** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Requirements** The portal eater's beak is not [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbing]] a creature; **Trigger** A creature teleports or is summoned within 50 feet of the portal eater; **Effect** The portal eater attempts to eat the portal. It makes a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the effect (counteract modifier +33, counteract level 9th). If the effect is counteracted, the creature arrives in the portal eater's beak and the portal eater [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab|Grabs]] it.  
**Reflect Spell** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** A creature Casts a Spell that targets the portal eater; **Effect** The portal eater attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the spell (counteract modifier +32, counteract level 9th). If the spell is counteracted, it targets the caster instead.
