# Shabti Votary

Shabti votaries are devout clerics of [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]] who heal and protect their allies while destroying enemies of their faith. Although shabti votaries focus primarily on destroying [[Traits/Undead|undead]], they remain a potent enemy against living foes.

**Discerning Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) 30 feet. All [[Traits/Undead|undead]] within the aura glow blue, shedding [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in a 5-foot radius. Visible undead can't be [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] while affected by this aura. [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible]] undead are concealed while affected by this aura, rather than being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]].  
**Immortal** Shabtis don't age naturally and can't die of old age. Spells and effects that cause aging still affect a shabti as normal. They also can't be turned into [[Traits/Undead|undead]].  
**Gird in Prayer** ⤾ **Trigger** An ally within the shabti votary's aura is the target of a [[Traits/Death|death]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], or [[Spells/Possession|_possession_]] effect; **Effect** The shabti votary girds their ally in protective prayers. The triggering creature gains a +2 status bonus to saving throws against death, mental, and possession effects until the beginning of the shabti votary's next turn.
