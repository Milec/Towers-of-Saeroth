# Vanyver (Nightwing)

Vanyvers are large, humanoid bats shaped from void and shadowstuff, their red eyes glowing like tiny stars in an otherwise lightless night. Though powerful, they're the least of the darvakkas, and the most likely to submit to a master, either another of their kind or a mortal with a reputation for being especially murderous and destructive. Vanyvers agree to follow their masters as a temporary means of maximizing the death and destruction they can enact but will quickly turn on any master they feel falters in this goal.

**Entropy's Shadow** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative|negative]]) 40 feet. Vanyvers leak entropy and corruption from their very being. A living creature entering or starting its turn in the aura takes 3d6 negative damage with a DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If it fails, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute and [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet toward the vanyver.  
**Sunlight Powerlessness** A vanyver caught in sunlight is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 2]].  
**Catching Bite** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within reach of the vanyver's jaws makes a melee Strike against the vanyver with a weapon; **Effect** The vanyver chooses to be hit. If the attack would've missed, it hits. The vanyver catches the weapon in their jaws and uses Drain Magic on it without fulfilling Drain Magic's requirements.
