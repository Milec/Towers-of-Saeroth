# Mitflit Vermin Cavalry

Though [[Bestiary/Mitflit (Monster Core)|mitflits]] are known as cowardly gremlins, they can be urged into an aggressive fervor by the right leader. Mounted on [[Bestiary/Giant Tick|giant ticks]], [[Bestiary/Families/Centipede (Monster Core)|centipedes]], and other verminous arthropods, these mitflits overcome their selfloathing to take the fight to their enemies.
