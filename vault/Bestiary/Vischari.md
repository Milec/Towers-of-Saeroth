**Immobilizing Blow** two actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation]]) Vischari makes a fist Strike that shuts down her target's motor control.

The target must make a DC 25 Fortitude save. If the Strike was a critical hit, treat the target's save result as one degree worse. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour.  
**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  
**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 1 round.  
**Critical Failure** The target is paralyzed for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt a new Fortitude save to reduce the remaining duration by 1 round or end it entirely on a critical success.

**Wicked Bite** single action  **Requirements** Vischari damaged a creature with a jaws Strike on her last action; **Effect** Vischari maintains contact, turning the creature's flesh translucent around the site of the injury. Vischari chooses one of two options, each of which requires the target to attempt a DC 25 Fortitude save. If her jaws Strike was a critical hit, the creature suffers both effects, using the same save result for both.

- **Drain Blood** Vischari drinks some of the creature's blood. On a failed save, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 and Vischari regains 10 HP (on a critical failure, it's drained 2 and Vischari regains 20 HP).
- **Drain Vitality** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) Vischari draws out some of the creature's vital essence. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 hour on a failed save (or enfeebled 2 for 1 hour on a critical failure).
