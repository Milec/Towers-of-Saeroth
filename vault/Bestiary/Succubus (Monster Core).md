# Succubus (Lust Demon)

Succubi are manifestations of the sin of destructive lust, and they are the most attractive of all demons—as befits their role in seducing mortals to fall to sin. The concept of gender is fluid to a succubus, as they can adopt countless [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] forms of any gender to aid in their goals. Most succubi have a feminine true form (incubi, which usually have a masculine true form, are a different kind of demon), but regardless of gender, a lust demon is supernaturally beautiful or handsome, but with curving horns, bat wings, sharp claws, and a sinuous tail.

 When disguised as a mortal humanoid in [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], a succubus whispers into the ears of mortals, urging them to pursue their darkest and most destructive desires and pushing them into depravity, using magic only if persuasion fails. While mortals often fixate on succubi's application of sexual lust, these demons are can easily exploit lust for power, knowledge, fame, or any other desire as easily as more carnal appetites. Their ability to infiltrate societies makes them excellent spies, assassins, and political saboteurs as well. Ultimately, the succubus enjoys all means of upending mortal culture.

**Seductive Presence** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) 10 feet. Any creature in the aura that could be sexually attracted to a succubus takes a –2 circumstance penalty to checks and DCs to oppose the succubus's [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spells, [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], and [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]].  
**Rejection Vulnerability** As succubi are beings of pure lust, creatures that reject their lust can metaphysically harm them. When a succubus fails a [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] check to Embrace or [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Request]], or when a creature succeeds at its save against a succubus's [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell or ability, the succubus takes 2d6 mental damage. For 1 hour after causing mental damage to a succubus in this way, a creature can deal 2d6 mental damage to the succubus with a successful [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] action incorporating its rejection.

**Passionate Kiss** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Unholy]], [[Traits/Void]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The succubus engages a creature they have [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] in an embrace or other act of passion to drain its vital essence. The kiss makes the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] or increases its drained value by 1, to a maximum of 4. The creature takes 3d6 void damage and the succubus regains Hit Points equal to the damage dealt. The target must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or be affected by a [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]] to submit to more actions of passion rather than trying to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].

**Profane Gift** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Unholy]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The succubus gives a willing [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] a profane gift. That creature gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws. As long as the gift persists, the succubus can communicate telepathically with the target at any distance, see through the creature's senses, and target the creature with [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]] through the telepathic link. In addition, the creature uses an outcome one degree of success worse than it rolls on saving throws against the succubus's _suggestion_ spells.  
A humanoid can't have more than one profane gift at a time, and a succubus can't grant more than one profane gift at a time. Removing the gift requires an [[Rituals/Atone (Player Core)|_atone_]] ritual. The succubus can remove the gift as a free action to give the recipient a curse, making them [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 3]] with an unlimited duration.  
 A summoned succubus can't grant a profane gift.
