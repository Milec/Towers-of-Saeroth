**Command Giants**   When Czaresia casts a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] spell against another [[Bestiary/Families/Giant (Monster Core)|giant]], the DC is 41, rather than 37.

**Demand** free action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) When a rune giant casts their innate [[Spells/Sending (Player Core)|_sending_]] spell, they can also cast [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]] on the target.

**Flashing Runes** free action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) Trigger The rune giant uses an arcane ability or casts an arcane spell; Effect The runes on the giant's body flash with magical energy. Each creature within a 10-foot emanation must attempt a DC 40 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.

**Invoke Rune** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) The rune giant invokes the Rune of Pride on their body, causing the rune to spray forth a 30-foot cone of sparks that deals 6d12 electricity damage to all creatures in the cone (DC 40 basic Reflex save). The giant can't use Invoke Rune again for 1d4 rounds.  
A glowing copy of the invoked rune appears on a single weapon the giant holds, granting the weapon the effect listed below. The effect on the weapon lasts for 1 minute.

**Rune of Pride**  ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) A creature struck by the weapon becomes convinced they fight an unconquerable foe. The creature must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1. On a critical failure, the creature is frightened 3 and can't take hostile actions against Czaresia for as long as they remain frightened.

**Wide Swing** single action  Czaresia makes a single mace Strike and compares the attack roll result to the ACs of up to two foes within her reach. This counts as two attacks for Czaresia's multiple attack penalty.
