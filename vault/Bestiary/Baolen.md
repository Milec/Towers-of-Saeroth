**Mythic Power**   3 Mythic Points

- _Recharge_ (concentrate) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Baolen regains an expended spell.
- _Remove a Condition_ (concentrate) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Baolen ends one condition affecting him.
- _Undying Myth_ **Cost** all of Baolen's Mythic Points; **Trigger** Baolen would die and has at least 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Baolen remains standing and conscious, and recovers 50% of his maximum Hit Points.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Constrict (Monster Core)|Constrict]]** single action  2d10+17 bludgeoning, DC 40. On a failed save, a creature that is holding its breath loses 1d4 rounds worth of air.

**Curse of Flawed History**   (curse, primal) **Saving Throw** DC 37 Will; **Effect** The target struggles to differentiate between alternate histories and how the past truly occurred. The creature takes a –2 status penalty to checks made to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] (or a –4 penalty on a critical failure). This curse has an unlimited duration.

**Double Attack** single action  The kraken makes two Strikes with two different arms or tentacles, each limb targeting a different creature. Double Attack counts as two attacks toward the kraken's multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after both attacks are made. If the kraken subsequently uses the Grab action, it Grabs any number of creatures it hit with Double Attack.

**Ink Cloud** single action  The kraken releases a cloud of black, venomous venomous ink in an 80- foot emanation. This cloud has no effect outside water. Creatures inside the ink cloud are exposed to retrocognitive ink poison and are [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] while inside the cloud. The kraken can't use Ink Cloud again for 2d6 rounds, and the cloud dissipates after 1 minute.

**Jet** single action ([[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]) The kraken moves through the water up to 280 feet in a straight line without triggering reactions.

**Retrocognitive Ink**  ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) Baolen is immune to this poison; **Saving Throw** DC 40 Will; **Maximum Duration** 10 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 mental damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 5d6 mental damage, stupefied 2, and the target is exposed to the curse of flawed history (1 round)
