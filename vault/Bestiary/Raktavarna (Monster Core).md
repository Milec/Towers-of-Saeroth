# Raktavarna

Raktavarnas are simple rakshasa incarnations of betrayal and assassination, often existing to test the mettle of those who were treacherous or poisonous in a previous life. They typically appear as a snake with blood-red eyes and oversized fangs, though they just as often appear in the guise of a sword or a piece of jewelry.

**Betraying Bite**   A raktavarna gains a +2 bonus to Strikes against any creature that is holding it.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]) The raktavarna takes on the appearance of a Tiny inanimate object. If, while transformed, the raktavarna takes any action other than the purely mental (such as [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]), they immediately revert to their original form. Until then, they can use [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] the object.

**Designate Master**  ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Exploration (Player Core)|Exploration]]) The raktavarna spends 10 minutes on an invocation alongside another creature. That creature becomes the raktavarna's master until the raktavarna dies or Dismisses the effect. The master gains the Master's Eyes activity as long as the bond lasts.

**Master's Eyes** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Scrying (Player Core)|Scrying]]) The master observes the world through the raktavarna's eyes instead of their own, using the raktavarna's Perception and [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]]. This lasts until the end of the master's next turn, but the master can Sustain the activity. This ability functions at any range, even on different planes of existence. If the raktavarna dies while their master is using this ability, the master is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 5]].

**Raktavarna Venom**  ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 16 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d4 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d4 poison damage and stupefied 2 (1 round).
