**Dazzling Brilliance**  ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The crystal sentinel creates waves of scintillating luminosity that cast [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 120-foot emanation (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] for the next 120 feet). The light lasts until the start of the crystal sentinel's next turn, after which the crystal sentinel can't use Dazzling Brilliance for 1d4 rounds. A creature caught within the bright light or that enters the bright light must attempt a DC 33 Will save.  
 **Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round, and then dazzled for an additional 4 rounds. While the creature remains dazzled, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (this additional effect has the [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] and [[Traits/Mental|mental]] traits).
