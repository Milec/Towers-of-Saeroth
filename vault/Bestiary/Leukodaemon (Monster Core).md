# Leukodaemon (Pestilence Daemon)

These skull-headed, vulture-winged daemons are harbingers of pestilence and servants of their patron Apocalypse Rider, [[Setting/Deities/Apollyon]]. Manifestations of evil souls who perished from disease in life, leukodaemons work tirelessly alongside one another to spread disease across all the worlds of the multiverse.

That creature must attempt a Fortitude save against the disease as if the interval for the disease's current stage had passed.
