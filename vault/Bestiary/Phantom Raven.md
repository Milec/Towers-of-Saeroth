**Walk the Ethereal Line** two actions The phantom walks the thin line between the [[Equipment/Ethereal]] and [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane|Material Planes]] in order to exist on both planes simultaneously. They can shift back to solely the Ethereal Plane by using this ability again.

**Resistances**
all damage 1 (except [[Traits/Force|force]] or [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]]; double resistance vs. non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]])

**Susceptible to Death** Though phantoms aren't alive, neither are they [[Traits/Undead|undead]], and they are uniquely vulnerable to the effects of death. A phantom whose Hit Points are reduced to 0 as a result of a [[Traits/Death|death]] effect (such as from a spell like [[Spells/Finger of Death|_finger of death_]]) is immediately whisked away to the River of Souls, where their soul resumes the usual path to the afterlife.  
**Vulnerable to Kindness** Any creature within 30 feet that the phantom can see and hear can issue a calm and friendly greeting to it as a two-action activity with the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]], and [[Traits/Mental|mental]] traits. A creature greeting the phantom in this manner must attempt a DC 15 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check.  
 **Critical Success** The phantom takes 4d8 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  
 **Success** The phantom takes 2d8 mental damage.  
 **Failure** The phantom takes 1d8 mental damage.  
 **Critical Failure** The phantom is unaffected, and is not affected by this weakness for 1 round.
