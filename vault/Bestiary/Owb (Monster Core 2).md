# Owb

Commonly called owbs, or lesser owbs by more powerful owb prophets, most of these mysterious creatures focus on manipulating caligni culture. Among calignis, an owb hides from mortals they deem beneath them—even including calignis in their charge. Instead, they listen in on the thoughts of those around them, searching for fears and worries they can capitalize on. An owb prefers to select one [[Bestiary/Families/Caligni (Monster Core)|caligni]] leader to control from the shadows, manipulating them using charisma and magic. Through coercion of such agents, owbs maintain a steady hand in the politics of the community, either blessing and breaking pacts with other creatures or acting as intermediaries and ambassadors between calignis and powerful external entities.
