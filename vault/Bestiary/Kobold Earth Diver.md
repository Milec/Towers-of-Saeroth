# Kobold Earth Diver

Kobold earth divers study the geology of the areas near their communities. The mystical influence of their community's patron or years of extensive training at digging or earth magic allow them to swiftly burrow through the ground and to feel movements in the ground beneath their feet.

**Pick Smash** two actions  The kobold earth diver smashes their pick into the ground, sending debris exploding in a 5-foot emanation. All creatures and unattended objects in range take 3d6 bludgeoning damage with a DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by an earth diver's Sinkhole takes an additional 1d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Sinkhole** two actions  **Requirements** The earth diver is burrowed beneath a Medium or smaller creature aboveground; **Effect** The earth diver creates a small sinkhole under the creature, who must attempt a DC 20 Reflex save. Regardless of the result, the target's space becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]].  
 **Failure** The creature falls into the sinkhole and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 18).  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage.
