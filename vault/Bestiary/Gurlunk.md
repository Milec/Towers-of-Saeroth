**Disorienting Shot** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** Gurlunk makes two Strikes against his hunted prey (see below) with his shortbow. If both hit the same creature, the creature succeed at a DC 21 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat- footed]] until the start of their next turn (or the start of Gurlunk's next turn on a critical failure).

**Hunt Prey** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) Gurlunk designates a single creature he can see or hear, and gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] and [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]] that prey. He can only have one creature so designated at a time, and if he selects a new hunted prey, the previous designation ends.

**Hunter's Edge**   Gurlunk deals an additional 1d8 precision damage the first time he hits his hunted prey in a round.

**Whistling Threat** two actions ([[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear mental]], [[Traits/Primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Gurlunk whistles sharply at a Medium or larger animal or beast he can see within 30 feet, using his voice to dull the creature's wits and cause it to lower its defenses. The target must attempt a DC 21 Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round as it shakes in fear.
