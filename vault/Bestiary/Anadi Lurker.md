**Anadi Venom**  ([[Traits/Poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 18 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d6 poison damage, flat-footed, and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] (1 round)

**Change Shape** single action ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The anadi changes into their hybrid, spider, or human form. The above statistics assume the anadi is in hybrid form. While in human form, the anadi seeker loses their climb Speed and can't use their fangs attack. When in spider form, they can't use weapons.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The anadi lurker deals an additional 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Spin Silk**  ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Exploration]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]) By spending several minutes, an anadi can produce silk to craft items made of cloth. An anadi can produce enough silk in a day to craft a single garment.
