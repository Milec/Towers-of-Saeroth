# Augur

These spherical knots of sinewy muscle, serrated blades, and bloody metal are the most common velstracs on [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]]. Each augur has only a single eye, from which they can witness the horrors inflicted by other velstracs, who train the augur to expect and appreciate pain. Augurs are 1 foot in diameter and weigh 30 pounds.
