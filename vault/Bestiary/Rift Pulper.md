# Rift Pulper

The rift pulper chews away at the planar structure of reality, eating quintessence to build their hideous hives. These bulbous structures look like partially melted wasp hives with the appearance of distorted faces, arms, and legs pressing against the walls from within.

**Pulp Quintessence** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]) The rift pulper rapidly chews on reality itself, gnawing open a short-lived rift into the liminal space between planes in any adjacent square. The rift pulper becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round, and can use the extra action to Stride, [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or Strike. If another creature occupies the square in which the rift pulper Pulps Quintessence, it takes 18d6 bludgeoning damage as portions of its body are stretched and distorted painfully by the tear in reality (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). The rift pulper can't Pulp Quintessence again for 1 minute.  
 This ability is more effective in the Outer Sphere, and when used on any of these planes, the rift pulper becomes quickened for 2 rounds, the DC to resist being damaged by the rift increases to DC 40, and the rift pulper can't use Pulp Quintessence again for 1d4 rounds.

**Pulp Soul** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) The rift pulper chews on the flesh of a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by it, pulping the quintessence that makes up their soul. The creature takes 3d10+10 spirit damage (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). If the creature is a native to the outer sphere (such as a [[Setting/Languages/Celestial|celestial]] or [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]]) the damage increases to 3d12+12 spirit damage.

**Pulper Venom**  ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) **Saving Throw** DC 38 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** enfeebled 2 (1 round), **Stage 3** enfeebled 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] (1 round)

**Wood Cyst**   The rift pulper spends 1 minute digging into a Huge or larger tree or wooden object, then forms a cyst around itself. While inside the cyst, the rift pulper can only sense its surroundings via [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the cyst is broken open. The cyst has AC 40, 50 HP, and shares the rift pulper's weaknesses. If the cyst is broken open by any means other than Burst Cyst, the rift pulper must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 3]]. While inside its cyst, the rift pulper has an automatic result of 49 on [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks and DCs to pass as nothing more than a large bulge on the wooden surface.
