# Toady

They hurl a heavy item they're carrying, which explodes on impact to deal 1d10 bludgeoning damage to all creatures in a 5-foot burst with a DC 14 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
