**Cunning Discernment** single action  Skartitch attempts a [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] check against an opponent using the [[Rules/Level-Based DCs|standard DC for a creature of that level]]. On a success, that opponent is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against Skartitch's attacks until the start of her next turn; on a critical success, the opponent is flat-footed against Skartitch's attacks for 1 minute.

**Deadly Deception** two actions  Skartitch attempts a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] check to [[Actions/Feint]] against an opponent she can see. On a success, Skartitch can immediately Strike with one [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack and one ranged weapon attack against the target. The ranged attack does not trigger reactions from the target of the Feint. Both of these attacks count toward her multiple attack penalty, but do not apply the penalty until both attacks are completed.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   Skartitch deals an extra 4d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.
