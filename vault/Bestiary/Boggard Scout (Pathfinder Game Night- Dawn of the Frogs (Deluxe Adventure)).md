**Swamp Passage**   A boggard scout ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.

**Terrifying Croak** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The boggard scout unleashes a terrifying croak. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 unless they succeed at a DC 17 Will save. Those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Tongue Grab**   If the boggard scout hits a creature with their tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the boggard. Unlike with a normal [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]], the creature is not [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the boggard's tongue. The DC to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] is 18, or a creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 13 and dealing at least 2 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents them from using their tongue Strike again until they regrow their tongue, which takes a week.
