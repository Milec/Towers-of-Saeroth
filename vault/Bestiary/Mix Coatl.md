# Mix Coatl

Mix coatls (pronounced “meesh”) are guides to fledgling societies, providing fundamental information such as farming techniques, medicinal expertise, or more esoteric knowledge like the arcane arts.

**Gift of Knowledge**   When a mix coatl casts [[Spells/Rewrite Memory|_rewrite memory_]] on a willing creature, the mix coatl can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to rewrite these memories for up to 60 continuous minutes. A mix coatl can grant knowledge of a particular skill to the target as part of the spell. The mix coatl chooses Engineering [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]], Farming Lore, Fishing Lore, Hunting Lore, or Mercantile Lore. The target becomes permanently trained in the chosen skill. A creature can benefit from Gift of Knowledge only once.

**Mix Coatl Venom**  ([[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) To [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creatures, this is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] instead of a [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] and deals [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage instead of poison damage; **Saving Throw** DC 26 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d6 poison damage, [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1, and stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** 2d8 poison damage, stunned 1, and stupefied 2 (1 round)

**Wrap in Coils** single action  **Requirements** The mix coatl has a Medium or smaller creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] in their jaws; **Effect** The mix coatl moves the creature into their coils, freeing their jaws to make attacks, and then uses Constrict against the creature. The mix coatl can hold as many creatures in their coils as will fit in their space.
