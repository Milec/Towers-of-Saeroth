**Anonymity** The Worm Prophet enforcer has a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks and saving throws to conceal their identity and a +2 circumstance bonus to their Deception DC against effects that ferret out information about the Gray Gardener's identity.  
**Invaded** The worm prophet is subject to the [[Bestiary/Conqueror Worm|conqueror worm's]] Mental Invader effect.  
**Gardener's Resolve** ⤾ **Trigger** The Worm Prophet rolls a failure (but not a critical failure on a Will save against an effect with the [[Traits/Mental|mental]] or [[Traits/Fear|fear]] trait) **Requirements** The Worm Prophet features are obscured by a mask or hood; **Effect** The Gray Gardener assassin takes 3d8 mental damage, and the saving throw is a success.

**Capture Spirit** ⤾  **Trigger** A target affected by one of the worm prophet's necromancy spells dies within 60 feet of the worm prophet; **Effect** The worm prophet harnesses a bit of the departing victim's soul, and the worm prophet is quickened 1 for 1 minute. They can use this extra action only to [[Actions/Step]], [[Actions/Stride]], or [[Actions/Sustain a Spell]].

**Steady Spellcasting**   If a reaction would disrupt the worm prophet's spellcasting action, the worm prophet attempts a DC 15 flat check. On a success, the action isn't disrupted.
