# Theater Phantasm

Theater phantasms are amalgamations of the spiritual and emotional energy given up by entertainers and audiences of powerful performances.

**[[Rules/Recall Knowledge|Recall Knowledge - Undead]]**
([[Rules/Skills/Religion]]): DC 37

**[[Rules/Recall Knowledge|Unspecific Lore]]**: DC 35

**[[Rules/Recall Knowledge|Specific Lore]]**: DC 32

## Theater Phantasm  *Creature 16*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|N]], Large, [[Traits/Incorporeal]], [[Traits/Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -185- A Taste of Ashes|Pathfinder #185: A Taste of Ashes]] pg. 89

**Perception** +32; [[Bestiary/Abilities/All-Around Vision|all-around vision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense|lifesense]] 100 feet, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] 100 feet

**Languages**
[[Setting/Languages/Common]], [[Setting/Languages/Necril]]; up to four others

**Skills**
[[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] +30, [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] +35, [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] +30, [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Theater Lore]] +36

**Dex** +4

**Int** +2

**Wis** +5

**Cha** +9

**Bonded Theater** A theater phantasm can't travel more than 50 feet from the theater (or similar location) where it was created.

---

**AC** 36

**Fort** +28

**Ref** +25

**Will** +30

**HP** 260
(emotional consumption, dramatic return, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]])

**Immunities**
[[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], precision, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]]

**Resistances**
all 10 (except [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], or [[Traits/Positive|positive^]] double resistance vs. non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]])

**Catharsis** ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) When a theater phantasm is destroyed, it emits waves of emotional energy. All creatures within 50 feet take 10d6 mental damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save) as they experience a crushing barrage of emotions.  
**Dramatic Return** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) A destroyed theater phantasm slowly reforms to full strength over the course of 1 week. The only way to permanently destroy the phantasm is to reduce it to 0 Hit Points during a performance occurring in its bonded theater.  
**Emotional Consumption** ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) If a theater phantasm is within 100 feet of an audience of 20 or more intelligent beings witnessing a performance in its bonded theater, it gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 20.

**Melee**
single action
spectral hand +30 ([[Traits/Finesse]], [[Traits/Magical]]),
**Damage** 3d10+15 negative

**Ranged**
single action
impromptu props +28 ([[Traits/Magical]], [[Traits/Range|range increment 30 feet]]),
**Damage** 3d12+18 bludgeoning

**Spotlight** free action ([[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Occult]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The theater phantasm draws attention to a creature it admires. The phantasm designates a target other than itself within 50 feet with a subtle spotlight, giving the target a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks and resistance 10 to mental damage for 1 minute. If it uses this ability again on a new target, the former creature loses its spotlight.

> ## Competing Phantasms
>
>
>
> On very rare occasions, more than one theater phantasm takes up residence in the same theater, each trying to outdo the other.
