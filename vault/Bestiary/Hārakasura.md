# Hārakasura

Hārakasuras appear as two figures that are intrinsically intertwined, moving in complete tandem. In truth, they're a single being with a split heart and split wills, switching between virtue and god-defying ruin. Hārakasuras are above all beings of kindness and charity, yet every kind deed they seek to do only emphasizes the broken nature of the world—for if all were given what they needed, charity would never be required. Hārakasuras thus dub themselves as thieves and plunderers, stealing from one corner of the world to give to another, torn between their loyalty toward helping people and their despair at a horrific existence where people cannot survive without their help.
