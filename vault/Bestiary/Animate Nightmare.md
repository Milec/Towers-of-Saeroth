# Animate Nightmare

Whereas animate dreams form from the fears of specific individuals, animate nightmares are spawned by the terrors that beset civilizations. An animate nightmare’s appearance depends on the type of fear that created it, but, like fears, result in similar forms.

**Apocalyptic Embodiment**   An animate nightmare embodies one societal fear, chosen from the following options. Depending on the choice, the animate nightmare gains one additional 8th-rank occult innate spell, as listed below.  
 **Disaster** The animate nightmare appears as a person or creature who's suffered devastating wounds from a calamity—horrific burns, crushed and broken limbs, impalements from wind-tossed debris, and the like. It gains [[Spells/Phantasmal Calamity (Player Core)|_phantasmal calamity_]] at 8th rank.  
 **Disorder** The animate nightmare looks like a distorted parody of a commonplace person with unnaturally long limbs, a twisted visage, and a scratching shrill voice. It gains [[Spells/Confusion (Player Core)|_confusion_]] at 8th rank.  
 **Famine** The animate nightmare appears as a humanoid carrion bird or other bestial scavenger (such as a jackal or a fly) on the brink of death from starvation. It gains [[Spells/Vampiric Exsanguination (Player Core)|_vampiric exsanguination_]] at 8th rank.  
 **Plague** The animate nightmare is shaped like a tangle of diseased bodies animated into hideous life that might have just crawled out of a mass grave. It gains [[Spells/Spiritual Epidemic (Player Core 2)|_spiritual epidemic_]] at 8th rank. Panicking Touch single action (emotion, fear, mental, occult); **Requirements** The animate nightmare's previous action was a nightmare tendril Strike against a creature; **Effect** The animate nightmare's touch lingers and infuses the mind of the creature it just attempted to Strike. The creature takes 8d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent mental damage]] (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). If the creature is already taking persistent mental damage from Panicking Touch, it must instead succeed at a DC 38 Will save or endure a different effect based on the animate nightmare's apocalyptic embodiment; this additional effect lasts as long as the persistent mental damage lasts.  
 **Disaster** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2 and can't reduce its frightened condition.  
 **Disorder** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]].  
 **Famine** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1.  
 **Plague** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Emigrants From Dreams
>
>
>
> Animate nightmares often haunt the ruins of the civilization that birthed them. Others are drawn to Jandelay, the enigmatic demiplane where the fragments of dead worlds are preserved for all eternity, or to Abaddon, where apocalyptic nihilism is the rule. These latter nightmares might stay close to their “parent” civilizations, while others wander the broken avenues of multiple worlds, feeding off the traces of fear that linger in those places.
