**Disperse** ⤾ ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]]) **Trigger** The elemental hurricane takes damage from a hostile action; **Effect** The elemental hurricane disperses. Until the end of the current turn, it can't be attacked or targeted, doesn't take up space, and its high winds aura is suppressed. At the end of the turn, the elemental hurricane reforms in any unoccupied space within 100 feet of where it dispersed, and its high winds are restored.
