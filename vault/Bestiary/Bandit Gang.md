**Chop ‘em Down!** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The bandits make a coordinated attack with their axes against each enemy in a 5-foot emanation with a DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.  single action 1d6+3 slashing damage  
 two actions 2d6+9 slashing damage  
 three actions 3d6+9 slashing damage

**Forest Passage**   The bandit ignores any [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by plants, such as bushes, vines, and undergrowth.

**Launch Slings!** two actions  The bandits draw or reload their slings, then launch a volley of sling bullets. This is a 10-foot burst within 50 feet that deals 2d6+4 bludgeoning damage with a DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. When the troop is reduced to 2 or fewer segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Stand and Deliver!** single action  The troop attempts to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]] up to 4 creatures.
