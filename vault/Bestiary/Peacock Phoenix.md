**Radiant Panoply** ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) 20 feet. The peacock phoenix is surrounded by an aura of iridescence. An adjacent creature that starts its turn in the aura must attempt a DC 35 Will save. On a failure, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the start of its next turn.  
**Rebirth** ([[Traits/Mythic|mythic]]) When the peacock phoenix dies while incorporeal, they return to life at the start of their next turn with full Hit Points. They are no longer incorporeal, lose the [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] trait, gain the beast trait, and lose their immunities and resistances (except for mythic resistance) and radiant panoply. Their Strength modifier becomes +7. They lose their radiant wing Strike and gain a beak and talon Strike.

**Mythic Power**   The peacock phoenix has 3 Mythic Points.

- _Mythic Skill_ **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** The phoenix attempts their next Athletics or Stealth skill check with a +4 bonus and has mythic proficiency for that check.
- _Remove a Condition_ (concentrate) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** The phoenix ends one condition affecting them.

**Radiant Beam** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Orc (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]]) The phoenix emits a 100-foot line of radiance from their eyes. Creatures in the area take 10d12 force damage (DC 38 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The phoenix peacock can’t use Radiant Beam again for 1d4 rounds.

> ## Other Mythic Phoenixes

>
>

Whether different kinds of mythic phoenixes, born from similar confluences of magical and psychic energy, could ever exist remains uncertain.
