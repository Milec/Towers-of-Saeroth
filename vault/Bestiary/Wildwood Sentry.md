**Hunt Prey** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) The sentry designates a single creature they can see and hear, or one they're [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Tracking]], as their prey. The sentry gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] the prey and to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to Track the prey. The first time the sentry hits the designated prey in a round, they deal an additional 1d8 precision damage.
