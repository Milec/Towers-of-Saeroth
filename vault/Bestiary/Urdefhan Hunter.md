**Deadly Aim** single action ([[Traits/Open]]) The urdefhan hunter makes a ranged Strike at a –2 penalty. The urdefhan deals 4 additional damage with this Strike.

**Double Shot** single action  **Requirement** The urdefhan hunter is wielding a ranged weapon with [[Traits/Reload|reload 0]]; **Effect** The urdefhan hunter makes two Strikes with the required weapon at a single target. These Strikes both count toward the urdefhan's multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until both attacks are resolved.

**Mounted Bow Expert**   Urdefhan hunters can make composite longbow Strikes while mounted.

**Wicked Bite** single action  **Requirements** The urdefhan damaged a creature with a jaws Strike on its last action; **Effect** The urdefhan maintains contact, turning the creature's flesh translucent around the site of the injury. The urdefhan chooses one of two options, each of which requires a DC 32 Fortitude save. If the jaws Strike was a critical hit, the creature suffers both effects, using the same save result for both.

- **Drain Blood** The urdefhan drinks some of the creature's blood. On a failed save, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] and the urdefhan regains 5 HP (or, on a critical failure, it's drained 2 and the urdefhan regains 10 HP).
- **Drain Vitality** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) The urdefhan draws out some of the creature's vital essence. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 hour on a failed save (or enfeebled 2 for 1 hour on a critical failure).
