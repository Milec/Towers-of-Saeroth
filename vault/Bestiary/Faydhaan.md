**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]) The faydhaan transforms into a Small or Medium [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water (Monster Core)|water elemental]], [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]], or [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]]. This doesn't affect their statistics, but it could change the damage type of their Strikes.

**Gift of Hospitality** three actions ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The faydhaan gives another willing creature a magical gift or an agreeable conversation. The creature gains a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] and [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks. A creature can't have more than one gift at a time, and a faydhaan can't grant more than one gift at a time. The gift ends if the target acts hostile, or if the faydhaan renounces the recipient (a single action).

**Skewer** single action  The faydhaan makes a trident Strike, dealing an extra 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] on a hit (4d6 on a critical hit).
