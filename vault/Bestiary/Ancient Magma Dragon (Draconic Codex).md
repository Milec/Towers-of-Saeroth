# Ancient Magma Dragon

Magma dragons represent a brash and volatile aspect of dragonkind. Territorial and unpredictable, magma dragons are known for protecting their hunting grounds with a fierce and proud demeanor that brooks no challengers. Passionate in the extreme, magma dragons in their draconic forms can often seem utterly bestial, impulsively jumping at threats and challenges with a wild abandon, but this belies an intellect that many underestimate.

**Pyroclastic Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon breathes a blast of magma that deals 10d6 fire damage and 5d12 bludgeoning damage in a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The dragon takes on the appearance of a Medium humanoid. This does not change their Speed or their attack and damage bonuses with their Strikes but might change the type their Strikes deal (typically to bludgeoning).

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The dragon makes two claw Strikes and one horn Strike in any order.

**Draconic Momentum**   The dragon recharges their Pyroclastic Breath whenever they score a critical hit with a Strike.

**Magma Burrow**   A magma dragon's burrow Speed functions only when the dragon is [[Rules/Burrowing]] through magma or molten lava.

**Magma Tomb** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The dragon spits a molten boulder at a target within 120 feet. This deals 12d6 fire damage and 5d12 bludgeoning damage, with a DC 40 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. If the creature fails its save, it's encased in magma that instantly cools and has [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 10, HP 40, and BT 20. The encased creature can't [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|breathe]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] ([[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC 40).

**Volcanic Purge** single action  If the next action the dragon uses is Pyroclastic Breath, the magma clings to those it damages. Each creature that fails its save against Pyroclastic Breath takes 5d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]], and as long as it has this persistent fire damage, it also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds.
