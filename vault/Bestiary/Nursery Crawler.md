# Nursery Crawler

The vibrant elemental energy of the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] imbues even the most rotten of fallen trees, empowering their remains to travel in search of the best locations for the next generation to put down roots. This mono-eyed, rotten stump launches seeds that begin to sprout the moment they're buried, whether in the ground or in a living creature's skin.

**Germinate**  ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]) A creature hit by the nursery crawler's seed Strike must, on its turn, spend an Interact action to remove the seed; any seeds still implanted at the end of the creature's turn begin to sprout, dealing 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and rendering the seeded creature [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]]. Removing a seed after it's sprouted deals 1d4 piercing damage; removing it before it begins to sprout does no damage. Removed seeds that land in viable soil sprout immediately and grow into new saplings after 1 hour.
