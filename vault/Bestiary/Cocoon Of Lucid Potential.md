**Anchored in Silk** The cocoon is incapable of movement beyond slight wriggling in place. It has no Speed and can't move or [[Rules/Forced Movement|be moved]], even through means such as [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]], as the Weaver's threads tether it in place physically and astrally. The cocoon casts any spells psychically, substituting verbal and somatic components for expressions of pure unbridled will and removing the [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] trait from its Cast a Spell action.

**Weaknesses**
in there somewhere

**In There Somewhere** Killia's individuality still rests within the cocoon, yet trying to resist assimilation. If a PC succeeds at a DC 32 [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check on the cocoon, the cocoon takes 5d6 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] as Killia's consciousness fights the Weaver. The cocoon can't be affected in this way more than once per turn.  
**Give of Oneself** free action **Trigger** The Weaver would be reduced to 0 Hit Points; Effect The cocoon's vitality is linked to the Weaver's. It loses 30 Hit Points, restoring 15 Hit Points to the Weaver. It can use this ability even if it would otherwise be unable to act.
