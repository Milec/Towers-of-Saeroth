**Vulnerable to Shatter** The glass elephant is affected by the [[Spells/Shatter|_shatter_]] spell as though it were an unattended object.  
**Spell Reflection** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]]) **Trigger** The glass elephant is targeted by a spell; **Effect** The elephant positions its reflective magical surfaces to turn the spell back on the caster. It tries to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the spell, making an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check for its counteract check. If it successfully counteracts the spell, the effect is turned back on the caster.
