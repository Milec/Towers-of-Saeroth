**Damage** 2d6+8 bludgeoning plus 1d6 void and sagebane

**Brawling Critical**   When a monk of Kugaptee scores a critical hit with a fist Strike or a weapon with the [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|monk]] trait, the target must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until the end of the monk's next turn.

**Sagebane**  ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) When the monk of Kugaptee damages a living creature with a fist Strike or a weapon with the [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|monk]] trait, the target must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1 minute. Further damage dealt by the monk of Kugaptee resets the duration to 1 minute and increases the stupefied value by 1 on a failed save, to a maximum of stupefied 4.

**Scarlet Apoxia**  ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The target can't recover from the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] condition caused by scarlet apoxia until the disease is cured; **Saving Throw** DC 24 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1 and fatigued (1 day); **Stage 3** drained 2 and fatigued (1 day); **Stage 4** dead (a creature that dies of scarlet apoxia reincarnates as a [[Traits/Nindoru (Season of Ghosts (Hardcover))|nindoru]] of equal or lower level 24 hours after death unless the body is given a proper funeral; once reincarnation occurs, the dead creature can't be restored to its original life until after the nindoru it reincarnates into is first slain)

**Steal Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) **Requirements** The monk of Kugaptee is adjacent to a living creature that's [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]]; **Effect** The monk of Kugaptee leans over and sucks the breath from the target, who must attempt a DC 22 Fortitude save. Regardless of the result, the creature is exposed to scarlet apoxia and its breath reeks of rotting wildflowers for 1 minute.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature can't speak for 1 minute.  
**Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2 and can't speak for 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the creature falls unconscious and begins [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocating|suffocating]].

**Stunning Flurry** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The monk of Kugaptee makes two fist Strikes. If both hit the same creature, combine their damage for the purposes of resistances and weaknesses, and the target must succeed at a DC 22 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1 (or stunned 3 on a critical failure); this save has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait.
