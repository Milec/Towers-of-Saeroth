**Confusing Display** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The augnagar's writhing limbs and flesh seethe and squirm in a disorienting and unsettling manner. Creatures in a 30-foot emanation must attempt a DC 30 Will save, after which they are temporarily immune to further Confusing Displays for 1 minute.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1 round.  
**Failure** The creature is stupefied 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] for 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature can't attempt a flat check to recover from confusion whenever it takes damage from an attack or spell.

**Inhale Vitality** two actions ([[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The augnagar inhales sharply, drawing life force out of creatures in a 50-foot cone. Creatures in the area take 12d6 negative damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save, and the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] on a failure). The augnagar becomes [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 round on its next turn, and it can use the extra action only to Stride or Strike.

**Rotting Curse**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) **Saving Throw** DC 30 Fortitude; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 day); **Stage 2** drained 2 and the creature displays hideous, festering wounds exuding a horrific stench. Any creature within 30 feet of the victim must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] on a critical failure, for as long as it's sickened). While within the aura, affected creatures take a –2 circumstance penalty to saves against disease and to recover from the sickened condition. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute. The victim of the disease doesn't roll a save, but takes the failure effects automatically and can't reduce its sickened value below 1 (1 day).
