[[Spells/Earthquake]]

**Grasping Bites** two actions ([[Traits/Attack]]) The sturzstromer attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check and compares the result to the Fortitude DC of each creature in its space. This counts as two attacks for the sturzstromer's multiple attack penalty.  
**Critical Success** The creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by the sturzstromer until the end of the sturzstromer's next turn.  
**Success** The creature falls prone and is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the sturzstromer until the end of the sturzstromer's next turn

**Landslide** free action  **Requirements** The sturzstromer's most recent action was to cast [[Spells/Earthquake|_earthquake_]]; **Effect** The sturzstromer closes a fissure it made, choosing one creature that fell in. That creature must succeed at a DC 41 Reflex save or be [[Rules/Avalanches|buried as in an avalanche]].

**Pummeling Assault** single action  Each foe in the sturzstromer's space takes 4d10 bludgeoning damage (DC 41 basic Reflex save).
