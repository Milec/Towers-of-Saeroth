# Great Ironbill

The great ironbill, a stork-like creature native to Dirt Sea shores, is known for clapping its fearsome beak to create incredibly loud and unnerving sounds.

**Clatter** two actions  The ironbill snaps its beak together. Creatures within 30 feet take 2d10 sonic damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Flyby Attack** two actions  The ironbill [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Flies]] and makes a talon Strike at any point along the way.

**Standstill** single action  The great ironbill becomes unnervingly still. The next creature the ironbill makes a Strike against is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to the ironbill.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Living Alarm
>
>
>
> The great ironbill tends to remain eerily still, making it hard to spot when it stands amongst trees or shadows. Combined with their deafeningly loud calls, properly trained ironbills make excellent guards.
