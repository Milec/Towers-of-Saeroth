# Vanth

Protectors of the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]], the stern and resolute guardians of the dead, vanths are psychopomps who resemble skeletons with raven-like wings and a mask resembling a vulture's skull. Vanths carry black scythes to fight against those who would disturb the natural progression of souls, and they consider any visitor to the Boneyard a potential troublemaker. They rarely speak and even more rarely show any emotion other than a grim adherence to duty.

 When the psychopomp armies go to war, vanths serve as front-line soldiers. In particular, [[Bestiary/Families/Daemon (Monster Core)|daemons]] continually stage raids on the River of Souls, requiring constant patrol. Implacable warriors, vanths fly in perfect formation. This can backfire, as they often suppress any adaptability they possess as they wage war.

**Vanth's Curse** two actions ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|Misfortune]]) **Frequency** three times per day; **Effect** The vanth bestows a curse on a creature by touching it with its scythe. The creature must attempt a DC 25 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** The target is unaffected and is temporarily immune to Vanth's Curse for 24 hours.  
 **Success** The target feels a momentary shudder of doom and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1 minute by the distracting sensation.  
 **Failure** The target becomes morose and glum as it accepts its own inevitable fate. For 1 hour, the target is stupefied 2. Each time the target gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] condition, the stupefied condition value increases by 1, to a maximum value of stupefied 4.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but the effect is permanent.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Vanth Scythes
>
>
>
> Vanths' favored weapon is the [[Equipment/Weapons/Scythe (Player Core)|scythe]], a choice that further adds to their fearsome appearance and can lead to unfortunate associations with the Grim Reaper or [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa (Divine Mysteries)|Urgathoa]] among more superstitious mortals. Vanths find such comparisons to be ignorant at best or insulting at worst. Some vanths infuse their scythes with different qualities, such as [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]] or [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]], depending on the nature of their most common enemies.
