**Brutal Beating**   The ruffian's brutality shakes foes' confidence. When the ruffian deals damage on a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]], and the ruffian can [[Rules/Forced Movement|push]] the target up to 10 feet.

**Combat Grab** single action  **Trigger** The ruffian has one hand free; **Effect** The ruffian makes a melee Strike while keeping one hand free. If this Strike hits, the ruffian [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grabs]] the target using their free hand. The creature remains [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] until the end of the ruffian's next turn or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]], whichever comes first.

**Sneak Attack**   The ruffian deals an extra 1d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.
