# Orc Skullcrushers

Orc warriors search for more effective weapons and tactics—most physical, but some magical. As they fought back against the undead on their borders, they formed bands of skullcrushers to crush hordes of skeletons.

**Chant of Dominance** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]) **Effect** Orc war drummers lead the other skullcrushers in a holy chant extolling their superiority in battle. Any creature damaged by the skullcrushers this turn also takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent spirit damage]].

**Crush Skulls** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The skullcrushers smash their mauls and clubs against each enemy in a 5-foot emanation, with a DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage depends on the number of actions.  
 single action 1d12 bludgeoning damage  
 two actions 1d12+8 bludgeoning damage  
 three actions 2d12+8 bludgeoning damage

**Sacred Salvo** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Vitality]]) The skullcrushers fling a fusillade of sling bullets enchanted with life energy intended to destroy undead. This barrage is a 10-foot burst within 50 feet that deals 3d6 bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 vitality damage to [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], with a DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. When the troop is reduced to 2 or fewer segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.
