# Amphibious  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 122

An amphibious creature has adapted to live both on land and in water.

- Add the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait. If the creature has the [[Traits/Aquatic (Player Core)|aquatic]] trait, remove it.
- Add [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] with a modifier equal to the creature's highest skill modifier.
- If the creature doesn't have a swim Speed, add a swim Speed equal to half its land Speed (minimum 15 feet).
- If the creature doesn't have a land Speed, add a land Speed equal to half its swim Speed (minimum 15 feet).
- Add the following abilities: **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision (Monster Core)|Low-light Vision]]** **Drag Along** **Requirements** The amphibious creature's previous action was a successful melee Strike; **Effect** The amphibious creature Strides or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swims]] 10 feet away from the target. The target moves the same direction and distance as forced movement.
