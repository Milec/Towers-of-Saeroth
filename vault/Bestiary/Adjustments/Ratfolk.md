# Ratfolk  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 169

A ratfolk's adaptability and love of travel benefit from their innate ability to take on many different roles anywhere they go. They often form quick friendships and value teamwork and community.

- Change size to Small.
- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Ratfolk (Player Core 2)|ratfolk]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Ysoki]] language.
- Add a jaws Strike. It deals damage equal to the creature's lowest melee Strike and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] and [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]] traits.
- Add the following abilities.
