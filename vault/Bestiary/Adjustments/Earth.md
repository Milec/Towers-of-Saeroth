# Earth  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 91

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of earth, you can use the following adjustments.

- Add the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] trait.
- Add [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] 60 feet ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]]).
- If it has any languages, add [[Setting/Languages/Petran]].
- Add a burrow Speed of 20 feet, or 40 feet if the creature is 8th level or higher.
- If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with earth spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[Spells/Scatter Scree|_scatter scree_]], **1st** [[Spells/Pummeling Rubble|_pummeling rubble_]], **2nd** [[Spells/Exploding Earth|_exploding earth_]], **3rd** [[Spells/Meld into Stone|_one with stone_]], **4th** [[Spells/Stoneskin|_mountain resilience_]], **5th** [[Spells/Wall of Stone|_wall of stone_]], **6th** [[Spells/Flesh to Stone|_petrify_]], **7th** [[Spells/Heaving Earth|_heaving earth_]], **8th** [[Spells/Earthquake|_earthquake_]].
