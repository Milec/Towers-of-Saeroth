# Sandbound  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 123

A sandbound creature grew in a harsh, blistering environment, such as a sweltering desert or even near a volcano's caldera, and can better handle hot and dry weather.

- Add a resistance to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] and a weakness to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], value dependent on the creature's level (see table below).
- If the creature doesn't have a burrow Speed, add a burrow Speed equal to half its fastest speed.
- Add the following abilities. **Efficient Metabolism** The creature can go 10 times as long as normal before it's affected by [[Rules/Starvation and Thirst (GM Core)|starvation and thirst]]. **Scorching Adaptation** The creature treats [[Rules/Temperature|environmental heat effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible heat becomes extreme, extreme heat becomes severe, and so on). **Desert Steps** The creature ignores [[Rules/Uneven Ground|uneven ground]] and [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by sand (reducing greater difficult terrain from sand to ordinary difficult terrain). **Sand Burst** **Requirements** The sandbound creature is burrowed in loose sand or earth; **Effect** The creature [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrows]] twice, then makes a melee Strike. If it was [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] at the start of its movement, it remains undetected until after the attack.
