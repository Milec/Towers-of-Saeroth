# Halfling  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 168

A halfling's wanderlust can lead them to all sorts of places and situations where they are often least expected to be. Halflings possess an uncanny luck that they use to pull off all sorts of mischievous and daring deeds.

- Change size to Small.
- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Halfling (Player Core)|halfling]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Halfling (Player Core)|Halfling]] language.
- Add the following abilities.

 **Keen Eyes** The halfling gains a +2 circumstance bonus when using the [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action to find [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] creatures within 30 feet of them. Whenever the halfling targets a creature that is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] or hidden from them, reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or 9 for a hidden one.
