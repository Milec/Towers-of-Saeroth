# Wood  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 193

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of wood, you can use the following adjustments.

- Add the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait. If the creature is a growing plant, you can also add the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait.
- If it has any languages, add [[Setting/Languages/Muan]].
- Increase the creature's HP based on its level.
- Add weaknesses to fire and to damage from [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axes]] depending on its level.
- If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with wood spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[Spells/Timber|_timber_]], **1st** [[Spells/Protector Tree|_protector tree_]], **2nd** [[Spells/Barkskin|_oaken resilience_]], **3rd** [[Spells/Wall of Thorns|_wall of thorns_]], **4th** [[Spells/Life-Draining Roots|_life-draining roots_]], **5th** [[Spells/Tree Stride|_nature's pathway_]], **6th** [[Spells/Lignify|_lignify_]], **7th** [[Spells/Pollen Pods|_pollen pods_]], **8th** [[Spells/Burning Blossoms|_burning blossoms_]], **9th** [[Spells/One with the Land|_one with the land_]].
