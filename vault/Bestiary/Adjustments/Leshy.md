# Leshy  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 169

Leshies are nature spirits who have been granted a physical form. They have little tolerance for those who despoil natural places but can find family with anyone. For a fungus leshy, change references to plants to fungi.

- Change size to Small.
- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] and [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]] traits with the
- [[Traits/Leshy (Player Core)|leshy]] and [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] traits.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Fey]] language.
- Add the following abilities.

 **[[Rules/Low-Light Vision]]**  
 **Verdant Burst** ([[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]]) When the leshy dies, a burst of primal energy explodes from their body, restoring Hit Points based on the leshy's level to each plant creature in a 30-foot emanation. This area is filled with plants, becoming difficult terrain. If the terrain is not a viable environment for these plants, they wither after 24 hours.
