# Metal  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 139

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of metal, you can use the following adjustments.

- Add the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait.
- If it has any languages, add [[Setting/Languages/Talican]].
- Decrease the creature's HP based on its level.
- Add resistance to electricity depending on its level.
- If the creature has metal spikes or blades, you can give its physical Strikes your choice of [[Traits/Versatile|versatile]] P or versatile S.
- If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with metal spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[Spells/Needle Darts|_needle darts_]], **1st** [[Spells/Conductive Weapon|_conductive weapon_]], **2nd** [[Spells/Magnetic Repulsion|_magnetic repulsion_]], **3rd** [[Spells/Noxious Metals|_noxious metals_]], **4th** [[Spells/Rust Cloud|_rust cloud_]], **5th** [[Spells/Impaling Spike|_impaling spike_]], **6th** [[Spells/Field of Razors|_field of razors_]], **7th** [[Spells/Beheading Buzz Saw|_beheading buzz saw_]], **8th** [[Spells/Ferrous Form|_ferrous form_]], **9th** [[Spells/Magnetic Dominion|_magnetic dominion_]].
