# Ghoul  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 72

[[Bestiary/Ghoul]] creatures are typically hairless and gaunt with blue or purple skin and pointed ears.

- Add the [[Traits/Ghoul|ghoul]] and [[Traits/Undead|undead]] traits.
- Add the following immunities: [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
- Decrease all saving throw modifiers by 1.
- Add the paralysis ability to the creature's jaws, fangs, or similar [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack. If the creature doesn't have one, add one with the same attack and damage as its strongest melee attack.
- Add the following abilities. **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision]]** **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing]]** **Paralysis** ([[Traits/Occult|occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When the creature gets a critical hit with its jaws against a living, non-[[Traits/Elf|elf]] foe of the creature's level or lower, the foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] until the end of the foe's next turn. **Swift Leap** [one-action] ([[Traits/Move|move]]) The creature jumps up to half its Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.
