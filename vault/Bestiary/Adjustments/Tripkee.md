# Tripkee  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 169

Tripkees are often cautious, but their cunning and creativity usually lends them success. While they try to avoid direct conflict, a tripkee will fiercely protect their home or community when cornered.
