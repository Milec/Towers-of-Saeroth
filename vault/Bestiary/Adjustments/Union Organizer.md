# Union Organizer  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 71

Organizers ensure the laborers in the union stay on task and keep a united front.

- Add Diplomacy and Labor Lore with a modifier equal to its highest skill modifier.
- Add the following ability.

 **Solidarity** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]]) 10 feet. While leading a demonstration, the union organizer grants themself and their allies a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigue]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. Greater numbers amplify the effect. The emanation increases to 20 feet if there are at least 5 allies within 10 feet, and to 30 feet if there are at least 10 allies within 20 feet.
