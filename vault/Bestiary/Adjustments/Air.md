# Air  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 67

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of air, you can use the following adjustments.

- Add the [[Traits/Air|air]] trait.
- If it has any languages, add [[Setting/Languages/Sussuran]].
- If the creature is 8th level or higher, give it a fly Speed of 25 feet. If its level is lower than that, you can give it this fly Speed if it either doesn't have ranged attacks or you remove its ranged attacks; use discretion.
- If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with air spells of the same rank, such as: **Cantrip** [[Spells/Gale Blast|_gale blast_]], **1st** [[Spells/Gust of Wind|_gust of wind_]], **2nd** [[Spells/Cleanse Air|_cleanse air_]], **3rd** [[Spells/Wall of Wind|_wall of wind_]], **4th** [[Spells/Fly|_fly_]], **5th** [[Spells/Pressure Zone|_pressure zone_]], **6th** [[Spells/Phantom Orchestra|_phantom orchestra_]], **7th** [[Spells/Fly|_fly_]], **8th** [[Spells/Whirlwind|_whirlwind_]], **9th** [[Spells/Storm of Vengeance|_wrathful storm_]].
