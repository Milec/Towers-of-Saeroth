# Frostbound  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 122

A frostbound creature grew in a harsh, freezing environment, such as a frigid tundra or a region of magical winters, and can better handle cold weather.

- Add a resistance to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] and a weakness to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], value dependent on the creature's level (see table below).
- Add the following abilities: **Freezing Adaptation** The creature treats [[Rules/Temperature|environmental cold effects]] as if they were one step less extreme (incredible cold becomes extreme, extreme cold becomes severe, and so on). **Winter Senses** The creature ignores [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]]caused by ice or snow. **Arctic Steps** The creature ignores [[Rules/Uneven Ground|uneven ground]] and [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by ice and the difficult terrain caused by snow (reducing greater difficult terrain from ice or snow to ordinary difficult terrain). **Snow Spray** **Requirements** The creature is standing on or adjacent to loose snow; **Effect** The frostbound creature kicks up the snow, becoming concealed to all creatures that are not adjacent to it until it moves or the end of its next turn.

##  

| **Level** | **Resistance/Weakness** |
| --- | --- |
| 3 or lower | 3 |
| 4–8 | 5 |
| 9–13 | 10 |
| 14+ | 15 |
