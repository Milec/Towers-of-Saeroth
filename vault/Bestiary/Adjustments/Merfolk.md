# Merfolk  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 151

Merfolk have humanlike torsos, fish tails, and fins.

- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Merfolk (Monster Core)|merfolk]] trait and add the [[Traits/Amphibious (Player Core)|amphibious]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Thalassic]] language.
- Change Speed to 5 feet with a 25-foot swim Speed. You can add a supramarine chair item to give the merfolk a land Speed equal to their swim Speed. Use the rules for a [[Equipment/Wheelchair|wheelchair]].
- Add the following abilities.

 **[[Rules/Low-Light Vision]]**
