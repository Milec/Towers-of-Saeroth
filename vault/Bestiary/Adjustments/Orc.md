# Orc  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 169

An orc's life is often short and violent. While they do make excellent mercenaries, orcs are more than capable of succeeding in non-violent professions as well. Orcs are eager to prove themselves both on the battlefield and off.

- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Orc (Player Core)|orc]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Orcish (Player Core)|Orcish]] language.
- Add the following abilities.
