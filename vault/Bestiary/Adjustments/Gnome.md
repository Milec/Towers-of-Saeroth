# Gnome  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 168

Constantly seeking new thrills and experiences, gnomes can be found just about anywhere doing just about anything. Gnomes who fail to dream, innovate, and experience new things will succumb to the Bleaching. The color will drain from the gnome, and they will enter a state of deep depression. Those who survive are often morose but wise.

- Change size to Small.
- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Gnome (Player Core)|gnome]] trait.
