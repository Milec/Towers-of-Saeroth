# Fire  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 115

To quickly give a creature a magical connection to the element of fire, you can use the following adjustments.

- Add the [[Traits/Fire|fire]] trait.
- If it has any languages, add [[Setting/Languages/Pyric]].
- Add immunity to fire.
- If the creature's Strikes deal more than one die of damage, change one die to fire damage. If not, add 1 fire damage to its Strikes.
- If the creature can cast spells, you can replace spells with fire spells of the same rank: **Cantrip** [[Spells/Produce Flame|_ignition_]], **1st** [[Spells/Burning Hands|_breathe fire_]], **2nd** [[Spells/Scorching Ray|_blazing bolt_]], **3rd** [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]], **4th** [[Spells/Wall of Fire|_wall of fire_]], **5th** [[Spells/Fire's Pathway|_fire's pathway_]], **6th** [[Spells/Fireball|_fireball_]], **7th** [[Spells/Fiery Body|_fiery body_]], **8th** [[Spells/Burning Blossoms|_burning blossoms_]], **9th** [[Spells/Meteor Swarm|_falling stars_]].
