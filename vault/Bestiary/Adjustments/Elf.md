# Elf  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 168

Elves are long-lived and use their vast wealth of experience to fill many roles in society. Elves tend to be rather private people, often finding it difficult to form close bonds with shorter-lived ancestries.

- Replace the [[Traits/Human (Player Core)|human]] trait with the [[Traits/Elf (Player Core)|elf]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Elven (Player Core)|Elven]] language.
- Optionally change Speed to 30 feet if lower.
- Add the following abilities.
