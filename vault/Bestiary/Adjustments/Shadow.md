# Shadow  *Creature Adjustment*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 72

A [[Bestiary/Families/Shadow|shadow]] creature is little more than a sentient shadow powered by negative energy. Shadows can easily travel to and from the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]].

- Add the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] trait.
- Add the [[Setting/Languages/Necril]] language.
- Add [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] with a modifier equal to its highest skill modifier.
- Add the following immunities: [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], precision, [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]].
- Add the following weaknesses, with a value based on the creature's level: [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]].
- If the creature can't fly, change its highest Speed to a fly Speed. Remove all other Speeds.
- The damage of the creature's physical Strikes changes to [[Traits/Negative|negative]] damage, and those Strikes are [[Traits/Magical|magical]].
- Add [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]] as an innate divine spell usable once per day.
- Add the following abilities. **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision]]** **[[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing]]** **Slink in Shadows** The creature can [[Actions/Hide]] or end its [[Actions/Sneak]] in a creature's or object's shadow.
