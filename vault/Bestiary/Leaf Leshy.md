# Leaf Leshy

Leaf leshys are diminutive protectors of forests clad in pine cone armor and hats of fruit, flowers, or leaves. They enjoy mock battles but act cautiously in real ones.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The leaf leshy transforms into a Small tree. This ability otherwise uses the effects of [[Spells/Tree Shape|_tree shape_]].

**Deafening Blow**   When a leaf leshy hits with its seedpod Strike, the target must attempt a DC 16 Fortitude save.  
 **Critical Success** The target is unaffected and temporarily immune for 24 hours.  
 **Success** The target is unaffected.  
 **Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round.  
 **Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.

**Glide** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) The leshy glides gently through the air, moving 5 feet toward the ground and up to 25 feet forward. As long as the leshy spends at least 1 action gliding each round, it remains in the air at the end of each turn. For the purpose of determining damage from falls, a leaf leshy always treats falls as if they were 20 feet shorter.
