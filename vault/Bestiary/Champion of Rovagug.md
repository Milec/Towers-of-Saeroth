**Champion's Aura** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) 15 feet. Any follower of [[Setting/Deities/Rovagug (Divine Mysteries)|Rovagug]] in the aura knows the champion is a champion of Rovagug. Enemies in the aura take a –1 circumstance penalty to saves against [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], and an enemy that ends its turn in the aura can't reduce the value of its [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition below 1. The aura can be suppressed or resumed with a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, and ends if the champion falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]].  
**Destructive Vengeance** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) **Trigger** An enemy in the champion's aura damages the champion; **Effect** The champion increases the amount of damage they take by 2d6 and deals 2d6 spirit damage to the triggering enemy. In addition, until the end of the champion's next turn, the champion's Strikes against the triggering creature deals 2 extra spirit damage.

**Axe Swipe** two actions  The champion makes a melee Strike with a +1 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and compares the roll to the AC of up to two foes that are in reach and adjacent to each other. > [!info] Sidebar
> ## Other Grim Champions
>
>
>
> The most sinister champions of unholy gods are devoted to desecration or iniquity. This champion serves [[Setting/Deities/Rovagug (Divine Mysteries)|Rovagug]], but [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]], [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa (Divine Mysteries)|Urgathoa]], and [[Setting/Deity Categories/Demon Lords|demon lords]] have similar champions. If you switch their deity, change their favored weapon (see the [Deity table](Deities.aspx)). If it's a one-handed weapon, reduce the champion's AC by 2, give them a [[Equipment/Shields/Steel Shield (Player Core)|steel shield]], and replace Axe Swipe with Defensive Advance, a 2-action activity that lets the champion [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise their Shield]], Stride, and make a melee Strike.
