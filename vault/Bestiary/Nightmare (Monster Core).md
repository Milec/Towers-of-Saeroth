**Smoke** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]]) 15 feet. The nightmare continually exhales black smoke. Creatures within the aura are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to those outside it, and creatures outside the aura are concealed to creatures within it. Nightmares and their riders can see through this smoke.  
 A creature that begins its turn in the area must succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 2]]. It's then temporarily immune to being sickened by the smoke for 1 minute. This is an [[Traits/Inhaled (GM Core)|inhaled]] [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and the nightmare and its rider are immune to it.
