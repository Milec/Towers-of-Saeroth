**Glow** ([[Bestiary/Abilities/Aura|aura]], [[Spells/Light|_light_]]) 30 feet. A will-o'-wisp is itself naturally invisible, but glows with a colored light, casting bright light in the aura and making it visible. An Ancient Wisp can also appear as a tiny pinpoint of light.  
**Magic Immunity**  A will-o'-wisp is immune to all spells except [[Spells/Faerie Fire|_faerie fire_]], [[Spells/Glitterdust|_glitterdust_]], [[Spells/Magic Missile|_magic missile_]], and [[Spells/Maze|_maze_]].

**Feed on Fear** single action  (concentrate) **Requirement** An enemy is under a fear effect or [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] within 15 feet of the will-o'-wisp. **Effect** The will-o'wisp feeds on the creature's terror. It regains 2d8 Hit Points, and if it has Gone Dark, its glow reignites. A will-o'-wisp can take this action only once per round.

**Go Dark** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) The will-o'-wisp extinguishes its glow, becoming invisible. It can end this effect with another use of this action. If it uses its shock attack while invisible, the arc of electricity lets any observer determine its location, making the will-o'-wisp only hidden to all observers until it moves.
