# Levaloch (Warmonger Devil)

Fearsome giants of jagged iron, levalochs serve in the armies of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]] as potent warriors and tenacious hunters—creatures of absolute discipline endlessly obedient to diabolical tyrants.

**Barbed Net**   When a levaloch hits a creature with their barbed net, the net wraps around the target, which becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds. If the Strike was a critical success, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]]. When a creature [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (DC 22), or if the Strike misses, the net crumbles into rust. Each time a creature attempts to Escape, it takes 1d6 slashing damage from the net's barbs, regardless of whether the attempt succeeds.

**Forge Weapon** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) A levaloch reforges part of their barbed iron substance into a new _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+1 striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident (Player Core)|trident]]_ or barbed net. Their previous trident crumbles to rust. When the levaloch is destroyed, any tridents or barbed nets they created crumble to rust.

**Merciless Thrust**   When a levaloch hits a creature that has the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] conditions with a melee trident Strike, the Strike deals an additional 2d6 damage.
