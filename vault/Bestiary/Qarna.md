# Qarna (Horned Archon)

Qarnas are secretive and tireless sentinels and scouts, patrolling the Outer Sphere's untamed wilds for evil to eliminate and keeping small communities safe from [[Bestiary/Families/Demon (Monster Core)|demons]], [[Bestiary/Families/Devil (Monster Core)|devils]], and worse. They resemble ornate statuesque creations with four stag-like heads and golden horns. When requested to do so by their allies, especially the god [[Setting/Deities/Erastil (Divine Mysteries)|Erastil]], they journey into [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] and patrol dangerous frontier areas, secretly performing acts of kindness such as leading hunters to food, helping lost children, and driving off evil creatures.

**Distracting Arrow** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The qarna makes a composite longbow Strike. That creature takes a –2 status penalty to attack rolls and Perception checks against any target other than the qarna. The creature can Interact to remove the arrow, which ends the effect.
