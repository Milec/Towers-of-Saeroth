**Rejuvenation** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) When Bharlen is destroyed, her armor rebuilds her body over the course of 1d10 days—or more quickly if the armor is worn by a living host (see [[Bestiary/Graveknight|Graveknight Armor]]). If the body is destroyed before then, the process restarts. Bharlen can only be permanently destroyed by obliterating her armor (such as with [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]]), transporting it to the [[Setting/Planes/Positive Energy Plane]], or throwing it into the heart of a volcano.  
**Sacrilegious Aura** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evil|evil]]) 30 feet. When a creature in the aura uses a [[Traits/Positive|positive]] spell or ability, Bharlen automatically attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it, with a +18 counteract modifier.  
**No Escape** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within Bharlen's reach moves away from her; **Effect** Bharlen Strides up to her Speed, following the foe and keeping it in reach throughout this movement.

**Brute Strength**   Bharlen deals an extra 1d8 damage with [[Equipment/Weapons/Trident|tridents]], and a trident gains the [[Traits/Shove|shove]] trait while she is using it in melee (both the extra damage die and the shove trait have been included in Bharlen's trident Strikes above).

**Devastating Blast** two actions ([[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]]) Bharlen unleashes crackling lightning in a 30-foot cone. Creatures in the area take 6d12 electricity damage (DC 30 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Bharlen can use this ability once every 1d4 rounds.

**Graveknight's Curse**   This curse affects anyone who wears a graveknight's armor for at least 1 hour. **Saving Throw** DC 40 Will save; **Onset** 1 hour; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]] and cannot remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 2** doomed 2, hampered 10, and cannot remove the armor (1 day); **Stage 3** dies and transforms into the armor's graveknight.

**Ruinous Weapons**   Any weapons Bharlen wields gain the effects of the _[[Equipment/Weapon Potency|+1]] [[Equipment/Striking|striking]]_ and _[[Equipment/Shock|shock]]_ runes.

**Sodden Strike** two actions ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Water]]) Bharlen calls to the endless storm to lend murderous power to her blows. She makes a Strike; on a hit, seawater appears in the target's lungs and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (sickened 3 on a critical hit).

**Weapon Master**   Bharlen has access to the [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effects]] of any weapon she wields.
