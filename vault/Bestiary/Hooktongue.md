**Slow Metabolism** Hooktongue can go for 10 years without feeding. After this, its hunger makes it [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] but doesn't otherwise impact its lifespan. If Hooktongue is slowed as a result of starvation, it can remove this condition by using Swallow Whole to gulp down a meal.  
**Undetectable** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]]) Hooktongue automatically tries to counteract any [[Traits/Detection|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]], or [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] [[Traits/Divination|divination]] attempted against it, using its [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] modifier for the [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check.

**Capsize** single action ([[Traits/Attack]]) Hooktongue attempts to capsize an aquatic vessel of its size or smaller that it's adjacent to. It must succeed at an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check with a DC of 35 or the pilot's [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Sailing Lore]] DC, whichever is higher.

**Hooktongue Venom**  ([[Traits/Poison]]) **Saving Throw** Fortitude DC 34; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d6 poison damage, clumsy 1, and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (1 round); **Stage 3** 4d6 poison damage, clumsy 2, and confused (1 round)

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Swallow Whole]]** single action ([[Traits/Attack]]) Large, 2d10+10 bludgeoning, Rupture 28

**Poison Spray** two actions ([[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Primal]]) Hooktongue sprays poison from its throat in an 80-foot line that deals 15d6 poison damage (DC 34 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails or critically fails this save is exposed to Hooktongue Venom as well. Hooktongue can't use Poison Spray again for 1d4 rounds.

**Water Travel** three actions ([[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Water]]) Hooktongue dissolves into water, appearing only as a long, serpentine silhouette on the waves. While in this form, Hooktongue's swim Speed increases to 600 feet, it automatically succeeds at [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to Swim, and it gains a +4 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks in water. Hooktongue can remain in this form for 8 hours but can't enter salt water when using this ability. Hooktongue can return to its normal form using a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait.
