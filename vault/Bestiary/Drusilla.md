**Axan Walk**   Drusilla ignores non-magical difficult and greater difficult terrain within Axan Wood.

**Befuddling Lash**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]) When Drusilla damages a creature with her tail, a wave of befuddlement clouds the target's thoughts. The creature must succeed at a DC 23 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] (stupefied 2 on a critical failure) for 1 minute.

**Manipulate Luck** single action ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Primal]]) Drusilla touches another creature to manipulate the creature's luck. The creature must attempt a DC 25 Will save. On a failure, Drusilla chooses good luck or bad luck. If she chooses good luck, the affected creature can roll twice on one d20 roll within the next day and use the higher result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. If Drusilla chooses bad luck, the creature must roll twice and use the lower result on its next d20 roll; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect. Once Drusilla has affected a target with Manipulate Luck, that target is temporarily immune to Manipulate Luck from any huldra for 1 day.
