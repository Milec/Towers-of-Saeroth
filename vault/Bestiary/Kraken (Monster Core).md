# Kraken

A kraken is an enormous, squid-like leviathan with a cruel intelligence. It hunts ships, whales, and heroes alike. The hatred and envy krakens hold for [[Bestiary/Families/Alghollthu (Monster Core)|alghollthus]], their rivals, has led many krakens to make their lairs in sunken cities, where they can sift through ancient lore and uncover long-lost arcane secrets.

**Double Attack** single action  The kraken makes two Strikes with two different arms or tentacles, each limb targeting a different creature. Double Attack counts as two attacks toward the kraken's multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after both attacks are made. If the kraken subsequently uses the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] action, it Grabs any number of creatures it hit with Double Attack.

**Ink Cloud** single action  The kraken releases a cloud of black, venomous ink in an 80- foot emanation. This cloud has no effect outside water. Creatures inside the ink cloud are exposed to kraken ink poison and are [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] while inside the cloud. The kraken can't use Ink Cloud again for 2d6 rounds, and the cloud dissipates after 1 minute.

**Jet** single action ([[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]) The kraken moves through the water up to 280 feet in a straight line without triggering reactions.

**Kraken Ink**  ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]) Krakens are immune to this poison; **Saving Throw** DC 39 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 10 rounds; **Stage 1** 4d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 5d6 poison damage and sickened 2 (1 round)

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Kraken Locations
>
>
>
> A kraken dwells in deep ocean trenches, sunken cities, or caves and reefs near hydrothermal vents. It seeks food near the surface, however, where it can prey on seagoing vessels.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Kraken Treasure
>
>
>
> A kraken's hoard includes the plunder of ships lost at sea and the wealth of sunken cities. Virtually anything could be found in a kraken lair, but they especially covet scrolls, spell books, and other tomes of ancient lore, as well as gemstones and rarefied raw materials found only in the ocean depths.
