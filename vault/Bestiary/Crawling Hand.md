**Grip Throat**   A Medium or smaller creature that is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the crawling hand has difficulty speaking and must spend an extra action to perform any action with the verbal trait.

**Mark Quarry**   A crawling hand can be assigned a quarry by anointing the hand with a drop of the intended quarry's blood. If the hand ever has no quarry, it automatically gains the next creature it damages as its quarry. The hand gains a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks when it [[Actions/Seek|Seeks]] its quarry, to [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] checks when it [[Actions/Track|Tracks]] its quarry, and damage rolls when it Strikes its quarry.
