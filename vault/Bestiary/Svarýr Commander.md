**Claimer of the Heroic Dead** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) Svarýr commanders can detect the souls of those recently dead. A commander spends 10 minutes praying over the body of a creature who has been dead for no more than 12 hours; if that creature is worthy of becoming an einherji and of the appropriate alignment, the commander transforms that creature into a [[Bestiary/Svarýr Soldier|Svarýr soldier]].  
**Ravenspeaker** ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]]) Valkyries use ravens as servants and spies. They can speak with ravens, and they can have up to three raven servitors who follow their commands. Valkyries can constantly observe whatever their commanded ravens sense.
