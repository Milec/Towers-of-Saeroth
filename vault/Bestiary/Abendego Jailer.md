**Restrain**   When the jailer makes a successful mancatcher Strike, their target must succeed at a DC 27 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] until the end of the jailer's next turn. Each time the target fails an attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] the jailer, it takes 2d6 piercing damage.

**Subduing Attack**   Abendego jailers deal an additional 3d6 precision damage to creatures that are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] or within reach of at least one of their allies. The jailer can make an attack with this additional damage as a [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attack, if they choose.
