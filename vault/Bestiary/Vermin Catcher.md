**Giant Rat Trap** three actions  The vermin catcher places a rat trap in an adjacent space. Any Small or Medium creature that moves into the space with the trap triggers it and must attempt a DC 18 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.
