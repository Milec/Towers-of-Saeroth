# Wooly Wrangler

When a giant, dangerous animal is found, there's always someone who tries to pet it. A woolly wrangler is usually accompanied by an [[Bestiary/Elephant (Monster Core)|elephant]] or [[Bestiary/Mammoth (Monster Core)|mammoth]]. They can [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command this Animal]] without needing to succeed at a [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] check.
