# Pixie

Insatiably curious, overly excitable, and just a bit puckish, pixies are wanderers and tricksters who use their pixie dust to create all sorts of whimsical situations, as well as to defend themselves. They take great pride in their skill at archery, even when not using their arrows to deliver magical ailments. They often practice by shooting drops of dew or severing vines and hairs. Other creatures often have trouble understanding a pixie's rapid, rambling way of speaking.

**Sprinkle Pixie Dust** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The pixie sprinkles pixie dust onto one of their arrows. If the pixie hits a creature with that arrow before the pixie's next turn, the arrow inflicts one of the following special effects of the pixie's choice instead of dealing damage. Each effect depends on the target's DC 21 Will save. On a critical hit, the target gets a result one degree worse than it rolled.

- Charm ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The arrow has the effect of a charm spell, except the target doesn't gain a bonus to its save if the only hostile act was the pixie firing its bow, and the pixie can choose to direct the target's adoration toward another creature rather than itself.
- Memory Loss ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) On a failed Will save, the target loses the last 5 minutes of its memory.
- Sleep ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Sleep (Player Core)|sleep]]) The target suffers the effects of a 3rd- rank sleep spell.
- Subdual ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]]) The target takes 4d6 mental damage, depending on its basic Will save.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Variant Pixie Arrows
>
>
>
> Pixies can enchant their arrows with a wide variety of effects, though nearly all such enchantments are designed to charm or bewilder, never maim. Some arrows may emulate the effects of spells such as [[Spells/Confusion (Player Core)|_confusion_]], [[Spells/Fear (Player Core)|_fear_]], [[Spells/Laughing Fit|_laughing fit_]], and even [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]].
