**Capsize** single action ([[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Move]]) The turtle tries to capsize an adjacent aquatic vessel of its size or smaller. The turtle must succeed at a DC 43 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check (reduced by 5 for each size smaller the vessel is than the turtle) or the pilot's [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Sailing Lore]] DC, whichever is higher.

**Fling** single action  **Requirements** The turtle has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] in its jaws; **Effect** The turtle flings the creature into the air from its mouth, sending it up to 10 feet up and 20 feet away. The creature falls 25 feet (assuming the turtle flings it as high as it can) and takes the appropriate amount of bludgeoning damage from the fall.

**Shell Defense** single action  The giant snapping turtle retracts its limbs and head into its shell and ends its turn. This increases its AC to 38, but it can't act except to reemerge, which it can do as a single action.
