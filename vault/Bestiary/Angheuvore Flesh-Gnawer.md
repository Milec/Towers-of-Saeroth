# Angheuvore

Angheuvores, or mortic [[Ancestries/Elf|elves]], have an insatiable hunger for raw [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] flesh— though they're reluctant to feed on other elves. Most stalk the streets of human nations, such as Druma, Razmiran, and the River Kingdoms. Impatient and impulsive, they're opportunistic hunters, preying on the wounded and isolated.

**Death Gasp** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) The angheuvore draws in a deep breath and holds it, temporarily suspending their biological processes and becoming undead. The angheuvore gains the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] trait and becomes immune to bleed, [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]]. Any such effects the angheuvore is currently suffering from are suspended, but take effect again once they take a breath.  
 Death Gasp lasts as long as the angheuvore [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|holds their breath]] (up to 8 rounds).

**Invigorating Feast** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The angheuvore is adjacent to a [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]] creature, or a deceased creature that died in the last hour; **Effect** The angheuvore feasts upon the creature, dealing their jaws Strike damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save); a corpse automatically critically fails with no save. If the target takes damage, the angheuvore gains 5 temporary Hit Points and a +10-foot circumstance bonus to their Speeds for 1 minute.

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living creature hit by an angheuvore's jaws Strike must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save to end the condition at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each save.
