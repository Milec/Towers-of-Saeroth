**Speed** 25 feet, swim 25 feet; [[Bestiary/Abilities/Troop Movement|troop movement]]

**Batrachian Blitz** single action to three actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The boggard dreadknot pummels each enemy in a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] with their clubs, with a DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage dealt depends on the number of actions.  
single action 2d6 bludgeoning damage  
two actions 3d6+10 bludgeoning damage  
three actions 4d6+13 bludgeoning damage

**Chorus of Croaks** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The boggard dreadknot unleashes a chorus of terrifying croaks. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 unless they succeed at a DC 26 Will save; those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Javelin Barrage** two actions  The dreadknot draws javelins and launches a coordinated barrage. This barrage is a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] within 30 feet that deals 4d6 piercing damage (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). When the dreadknot is reduced to 2 segments, this area decreases to a 5-foot burst.

**Mounted Troop**   **Effects** that target only animals or only humanoids may not work on the boggard dreadknot, subject to the GM's discretion.

**Swamp Passage**   A boggard dreadknot ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] caused by swamp terrain features.

**Tongue Lashing** two actions  The dreadknot's giant frogs lash out at each enemy in a 15-foot emanation with their tongues, requiring them to succeed at a DC 26 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]]. A creature grabbed in this way isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move more than 15 feet from the dreadknot. A creature can sever one of the tongues with a Strike against AC 25 that deals at least 15 slashing damage. This doesn't deal damage to the dreadknot.
