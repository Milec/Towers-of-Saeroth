# Merchant

Small settlements can typically support one or two generalist merchants, and larger cities house multiple specialists—experts in one type of product. Merchants range from vendors hustling in the public square to wealthy tycoons running entire commercial organizations. A merchant might have an additional [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] skill about a specific category of item (such as jewelry or magic weapons), with a skill modifier equal to their Mercantile Lore.
