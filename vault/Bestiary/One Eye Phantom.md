**Walk the Ethereal Line** two actions The One Eye phantom walks the thin line between the [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Plane]] and [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]] in order to exist on both planes simultaneously. **Ectoplasmic Bleed** If a creature hits the phantom with a melee Strike, the creature is splashed with ectoplasm, taking 2d4+7 spirit damage (DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). On a failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]].  
**Susceptible to Death** Though phantoms aren't alive, neither are they [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], and they are uniquely vulnerable to the effects of death. A phantom whose Hit Points are reduced to 0 as a result of a [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effect (such as from a spell like execute) is immediately whisked away to the River of Souls, where their soul resumes the usual path to afterlife.

**Skullcracker** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]) The One Eye phantom grabs the head of a creature as a jagged, spectral stone seeped with blood manifests before it. The One Eye phantom smashes the head of the creature against the stone with supernatural force, dealing 2d12+7 spirit damage with a DC 19 Will save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes half damage.  
 **Failure** The creature hallucinates that their skull has been fractured. They take full damage and are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]].  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but double damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Atonement for the Damned
>
>
>
> PCs might try to calm the phantoms instead of destroying them. At the GM's discretion, the PCs may attempt skill checks to talk down the spirits. On a success, the phantom's AC lowers by 2 as they become more willing to move on from their shame and dishonor, and they might even waver in attacking the PCs. Spells or rituals like [[Rituals/Atone (Player Core)|_atone_]] that require penitent creatures have no effect on these phantoms.
