**Hogtie** two actions  **Requirements** A creature is grappled or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by the drover's lasso; **Effect** The drover can pull the grappled creature up to 20 feet. Then, if the creature is within reach, the drover hogties it, attempting to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] it again. On a success, the creature is restrained with the lasso, and the drover doesn't need to maintain the grapple. The hogtie lasts until the creature [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] or the lasso is [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Forced Open]]. The drover can Interact to free a hogtied creature within reach.

**Lasso** two actions  The drover uses their lasso to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] a Large or smaller creature up to 20 feet away. They can continue to Grapple to keep their hold on the target so long as the target remains within 20 feet and they continue to hold the end of the lasso. In addition to the [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] creature being able to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]], a successful DC 16 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Force Open (Player Core)|Force Open]] can remove the lasso entirely.
