**Drowning Hook** single action ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]) **Requirements** A creature is prone within Druulbach's reach; **Effect** Druulbach uses his sickle to push the creature down as water bubbles up below it. The target becomes submerged in water until they are no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and must [[Rules/Mounted Defenses (Player Core)|hold their breath]] (Player Core 437) if it can't breathe water. The target takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 29 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) and loses 3 rounds worth of air if it fails the save.

**Flurry of Hooks** two actions  **Requirements** Druulbach is wielding two sickles; **Effect** Druulbach makes three sickle Strikes against one target. If multiple attacks hit, combine their damage and apply weaknesses and resistances only once.

**Serrated Takedown** single action  **Requirements** Druulbach hit with a sickle Strike on his most recent action this turn; **Effect** Druulbach drags down the target, knocking it [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] and dealing 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to the creature.

**Balanced Blades**   Druulbach's multiple attack penalty with [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]] weapons and agile [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks is –3 for his second attack and –6 for subsequent attacks (rather than –4 and –8).
