**Instrument of Retribution** three actions ([[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]]) The xiuh coatl transforms into any simple or martial weapon. The weapon is always a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming (GM Core)|flaming]] [[Equipment/Shock (GM Core)|shock]] weapon_ and can be of any size, chosen by the xiuh coatl when they transform. In weapon form, the xiuh coatl gains [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] equal to that of the weapon into which they transform, and they retain their Hit Points, saving throws, senses, [[Bestiary/Abilities/Telepathy (Monster Core)|telepathy]], and traits while in weapon form. The xiuh coatl can [[Actions/Sustain]] to revert to their normal form.

**Volcanic Lightning Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) The xiuh coatl breathes a gout of flame and lightning in an 80-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] that deals 5d8 electricity damage and 5d8 fire damage (DC 32 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The xiuh coatl can't use Volcanic Lightning Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Wrap in Coils** single action  **Requirements** The xiuh coatl has a Medium or smaller creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] in their jaws; **Effect** The xiuh coatl moves the creature into their coils, freeing their jaws to make attacks, and then uses Constrict against the creature. The xiuh coatl can hold as many creatures in their coils as will fit in their space.

**Xiuh Coatl Venom**  ([[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) To [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] creatures, this is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] instead of a [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] and deals [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage instead of poison damage; **Saving Throw** DC 32 Fortitude; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d8 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** 2d10 poison damage, enfeebled 1, and –5-foot status penalty to all Speeds (1 round); **Stage 3** 3d8 poison damage, enfeebled 2, and –10-foot status penalty to all Speeds (1 round)
