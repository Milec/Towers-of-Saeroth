**Powerful Dive** two actions ([[Traits/Move]]) The wyvern Flies up to its fly Speed and must both move forward at least 20 feet and descend at least 10 feet. If it ends the movement within melee reach of at least one enemy its size or smaller, it can make a claw Strike against that enemy. If the claw hits, as a free action the wyvern can either automatically [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] the target or knock it [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

**Punishing Momentum** single action  **Requirements** The wyvern **grabbed** a creature this turn using Powerful Dive. **Effect** The wyvern can Fly at half Speed while holding the creature in its claws, carrying that creature along with it and dropping it at the end of its movement. Alternatively, the wyvern can Strike the creature with its stinger with a +2 circumstance bonus.
