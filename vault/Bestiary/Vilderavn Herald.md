# Vilderavn Herald

In its true form, a vilderavn herald is a [[Ancestries/Human|human]]-sized raven. Its ability to assume a [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] guise suits it to insinuate itself into the retinue of a mortal ruler or to travel without too much concern, but more often these deadly fey directly serve more powerful rulers, such as [the Eldest](https://2e.aonprd.com/DeityCategories.aspx?ID=371).

**Bloodbird**  ([[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Occult]]) A creature hit by a vilderavn's melee attack becomes cursed, taking 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] that's difficult to stanch: the DC to stop the bleeding using [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] is 35, and healing the creature to full HP doesn't automatically end the bleeding. Removing the curse ends the bleeding.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) The vilderavn takes on the shape of a [[Bestiary/Wolf|wolf]] or [[Bestiary/Dire Wolf|dire wolf]], a wolf-raven hybrid, or its raven knight form: a Small or Medium humanoid in black full plate carrying a greatsword. The vilderavn can use its jaws attack only in a form that has a wolf's head, its talon attack only in a form that has raven talons, and its greatsword only in raven knight form.

**Souleater**  ([[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult]]) If the vilderavn kills a [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]] target with a critical hit using its jaws Strike, it rips out and devours the target's heart and soul as part of the attack. While the target is dead, the vilderavn can Change Shape into the target's form, gaining a +4 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Impersonate]] the target. If magic would resurrect the creature, the caster must succeed at a DC 38 [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check to extract the target's soul from the vilderavn; otherwise the spell fails.
