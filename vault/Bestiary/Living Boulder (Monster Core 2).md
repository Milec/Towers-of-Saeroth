# Living Boulder

Living boulders roll and glide through the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Earth (GM Core)|Plane of Earth]], gathering gemstones and metal shards until their surfaces resemble a ship's hull covered in barnacles. Barely more intelligent than many animals, living boulders fill much the same role on the Plane of Earth as the great herd animals found on worlds in [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]].
