# Yamaraj

The greatest judges among the psychopomps are yamarajes, whose wisdom is legendary and whose edicts are unappealable except to ushers or [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]] herself. A yamaraj resembles an immense dragon with dark, feathery scales and an emotionless, dispassionate gaze behind a feathered mask. When not serving as the senior magistrates, lords,  and generals of the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]], yamarajes pursue highly individualistic hobbies, such as gardening or literature.

**Beetle Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) The yamaraj breathes a blast of beetles in a 50-foot cone that deals 14d8 slashing damage and 4d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent slashing damage]] to creatures in the area with a DC 42 Reflex save. It can't use Beetle Breath again for 1d4 rounds.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].  
 **Failure** The creature takes full damage and is sickened 2.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is sickened 3.

**Final Judgment**   A yamaraj's [[Spells/Manifestation|_manifestation_]] spells are used only to pronounce judgment, typically either to restore a dead or destroyed creature to life, bind a creature to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard (GM Core)|Boneyard]], or banish a creature from the Boneyard.
