# Centipede Swarm

Swarms of centipedes are dangerous indeed, ravenous carpets of skittering hunger capable of devouring a traveler whole in a matter of minutes. [[Bestiary/Families/Kobold (Monster Core)|Kobolds]] and [[Bestiary/Mitflit|mitflits]] are both known to incorporate swarms of centipedes into cunning traps.
