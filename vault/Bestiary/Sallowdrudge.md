**Entangling Net** single action  A Sallowdrudge uses their net to [[Actions/Grapple]] a Medium or smaller creature within 30 feet. On a success, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 16) or until an adjacent creature Interacts to remove the net. On a critical hit, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] rather than grabbed. The Sallowdrudge can't use Entangling Net again until they take 1 minute to refold their net.

**Spear Fisher**  ([[Traits/Fortune]]) A Sallowdrudge attacking an [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] target rolls the attack roll twice and takes the better result.
