# Vordine Legion

[[Bestiary/Vordine|Vordines]] are the foot soldiers of Hell, dispatched in vast armies from the iron city of Dis to guard Hell's uppermost layers and crush the enemies of the archdevils who rule there. Indefatigable and pitiless, a legion of vordines strikes with a ruthless precision that commands fear and envy throughout the multiverse in equal measure. Although they usually serve at the command of more powerful devils, such as the tyrannical nessari, every legion also observes a unique internal hierarchy that enables another vordine to assume command at a moment's notice should it become necessary.

**Burning March** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Unholy]]) The vordine legion Strides, leaving an orderly pattern of burning hoofprints in each square they enter. **Impaling Barrage** two actions ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|Magical]], [[Traits/Unholy]]) The vordine legion releases a hail of tridents.
