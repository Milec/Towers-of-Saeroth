Any creature that ends its turn in the aura feels the intense pain of starvation and must attempt a DC 32 Fortitude save. On a failure, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] and takes 7d6 damage. The damage and fatigue a creature takes from this aura can't be healed until the affected creature has eaten a full meal.
