**Aura of Decay** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) 30 feet. Plant objects and creatures in the emanation can't regain Hit Points unless the effect that attempts to heal them [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracts]] the aura, which has a counteract rank of 6 and a counteract DC of 32. A primal warden of Zibik can spend 1 action to reverse this aura; it becomes an aura of renewal with the [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] trait, instead doubling the number of Hit Points of any healing effect received by a [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]] creature in the emanation.  
**Rotten Burst** ⤾ ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Trigger** The primal warden of Zibik takes physical damage; **Effect** A chunk of rotten bark bursts from the warden's body at its attacker. The warden makes a rotten bark Strike against an adjacent creature without triggering reactions.  
**Primal Destruction** ([[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) When the primal warden of Zibik is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it erupts with primal energy in a 30-foot emanation, dealing 12d6 void damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a DC 32 Will save with the following outcomes.  
 **Critical Success** The creature takes half damage.  
 **Success** The creature takes full damage.  
 **Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes temporarily cursed by Zibik to be [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] and stupefied 1 for 1 day; this is a [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] effect with a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] DC of 32.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, except the creature becomes enfeebled 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]].
