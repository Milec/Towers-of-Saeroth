# Urglid (Grave Demon)

The hulking monstrosities known as urglids form from the souls of murderous undertakers, sadists who buried their victims alive, and intentionally neglectful grave keepers who gave up their watch over the dead. Standing over 30 feet tall and weighing over 3,000 pounds, the demon's head seems sunken into its torso, and a gaping, toothy mouth opens at the top of its chest.

**Earth Glide**   The urglid can [[Actions/Burrow]] through any earthen matter, including rock. When they do so, the urglid moves at their full burrow Speed, leaving no tunnels or signs of its passing unless they choose to do so.

**Gravechoke** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Olfactory]]) The urglid emits a putrid pulse that targets all living creatures within a 30-foot emanation. Creatures in this area that fail a DC 30 Fortitude save become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (sickened 2 on a critical failure).

**Ravenous Earth** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Evil]]) With a single, devious thought, the urglid causes a mound of grave soil to well up at a creature's feet. A creature failing a DC 30 Reflex save is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]]. The restrained creature then begins sinking below the ground into a spontaneously formed grave. A creature restrained by this ability for three rounds is buried 6 feet deep in the ground and begins [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocating]] within 1 minute. A buried creature must be dug up to be freed (see [[Rules/Burial]]). A creature who is slain by Ravenous Earth rises as a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghoul|ghoul]] the next midnight.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Kabriri's Excavators
>
>
>
> While many loathe urglids for their ravenous appetite for burial and wanton destruction, priests of [[Setting/Deities/Kabriri]] insist that the demon lord blessed and oversaw their creation, trusting the fiends with digging the labyrinthine network of tunnels that connect Everglut with the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]]. It's no surprise, then, that where there's an urglid, there's possibly a pathway to the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]]— and plenty of [[Bestiary/Ghoul|ghouls]].
