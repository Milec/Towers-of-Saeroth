**Breach Planar Wards** free action **Trigger** An effect (save only for those of items with the [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]] trait) attempts to prevent the katpaskir from using a [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effect or from casting [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]]; **Effect** The katpaskir attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering effect (counteract modifier of +29).  
**Distortion Field** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]]) 30 feet. Reality bends and warps on the level of all senses without displacing the katpaskir's actual location. Creatures of the katpaskir's choice who start their turn in the aura must succeed at a DC 37 Will save or treat the area as greater difficult terrain and [[Rules/Uneven Ground|uneven ground]] (DC 20). A creature who succeeds still treats the area as difficult terrain. For such creatures, the distance through the aura is doubled for determining range penalty.  
**Mirrored Summons** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within 30 feet that the katpaskir is aware of uses magic to summon or otherwise conjure a good-aligned creature; **Effect** The katpaskir casts [[Spells/Summon Fiend|_summon fiend_]], regaining the daily ability to do so if needed. This effect is automatically sustained as long as the triggering summoning is sustained, for up to 1 minute.  
**Warp Sense** The katpaskir senses changes in the planar fabric within 1 mile, including any [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effect, sensing the direction and distance to the disturbance. If it senses such a disturbance within 500 feet, the katpaskir can cast [[Spells/Prying Eye|_prying eye_]] to observe the area without needing line of sight to the location.

>
>
