**Lazurite-Infused Flesh** Falrok's flesh is infused with lazurite, and he gains the benefits [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creatures gain from this mineral at all times. When in an area infused with lazurite, such as the Sanctum of the Starved, these benefits increase to a +3 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Positive|positive]] effects. This infusion also grants Falrok his resistance to positive damage.

**Stench** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Olfactory|olfactory]]) 10 feet.  A creature entering the aura or starting its turn there must succeed at a DC 34 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] (plus [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] as long as it's sickened on a critical failure). While within the aura, the creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to saves against [[Traits/Disease|disease]] and to recover from the sickened condition. A creature that succeeds at its save is temporarily immune for 1 minute.  
**Opportune Backstab** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within Falrok's melee reach is hit by a melee attack from one of his allies. **Effect** When Falrok's enemy is hit by one of his allies, he capitalizes upon the distraction, making a Strike against the triggering creature.

**Consume Flesh** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** Falrok is adjacent to the corpse of a creature that died within the last hour. **Effect** Falrok devours a chunk of the corpse and regains 8d6 hit points. He can regain Hit Points from any given corpse only once.

**Ghast Fever**  ([[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Virulent]]) **Saving Throw** DC 34 Fortitude; **Stage 1** carrier with no ill effect (1 day); **Stage 2** 4d8 negative damage and regains half as many Hit Points from all [[Traits/Healing|healing]] (1 day); **Stage 3** 4d8 negative damage and gains no benefit from healing (1 day); **Stage 4** dead, and rises as a [[Bestiary/Ghast|ghast]] the next midnight.

**Paralysis**  ([[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) Any living creature hit by Falrok's attack must succeed at a DC 34 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]]. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns, and the DC cumulatively decreases by 1 on each such save.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   4d6 precision damage.

**Swift Leap** single action ([[Traits/Move]]) Falrok jumps up to half his Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions.

**Scoundrel**   When Falrok successfully [[Actions/Feint|Feints]], the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against melee attacks he attempts against it until the end of his next turn. On a critical success, the target is flat-footed against all melee attacks until the end of their next turn, not just Falrok's.
