# Gibtanius

When a gibtas feeds regularly but never receives sufficient food to procreate, it grows into a mighty gibtanius. They become surly, uncontrolled predators, forsaking their bonded masters to satiate their unending hunger.

**Bouncing Crush** two actions  The gibtanius [[Actions/Leap|Leaps]] and can end this Leap occupying the same space as other creatures. It deals 5d6 bludgeoning damage to each creature in the space it Leaps to (DC 26 basic Reflex save), then [[Actions/Shove|Shoves]] each of those creatures. If it fails to Shove any creature out of its space, it bounces to the closest available space of its choosing that does not contain any creatures. Bouncing Crush counts as one attack when determining the gibtanius's multiple attack penalty.

**Breath Weapon** two actions ([[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Primal]]) The gibtanius vomits a stream of acid that deals 9d6 acid damage to all creatures in a 60-foot line (DC 26 basic Reflex save). It can't use Breath Weapon again for 1d4 rounds.
