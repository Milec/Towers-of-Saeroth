All enemies who can hear the propagandist must attempt a DC 19 Will save. If any of the propagandist's allies is currently benefiting from one of the propagandist's bard composition spells, any enemy who is aware of that takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature sees through the propagandist's pitch and is temporarily immune for 24 hours.  
 **Success** The creature is unaffected.

At the end of each of its turns, the creature can attempt another DC 19 Will save to snap out of it and rejoin their allies.
