**Spike Stones** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) 5 feet. Spikes of rock rise up from all stone surfaces in the emanation, creating [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. A creature moving in the terrain takes 2d6 piercing damage for each square of spikes it moves into (a Large or larger creature takes damage only once for each square it moves, even if its space covers multiple squares of spikes). Creatures with the [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]] trait ignore all effects within the area. The stone mauler can disable or activate this aura using a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait.  
**Crumble** ⤾ Trigger The elemental takes damage from a hostile source while atop rock or earth; Effect The elemental crumbles into the ground, [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrowing]]down 15 feet. This Burrowing does not trigger reactions. The elemental can't Crumble again for 1d4 rounds
