# Bone Prophet

The speakers for the dead known as bone prophets hold an esteemed place as voices for their decapitated god. Burial rites, necromantic rituals, and the delivery of cryptic utterances supposedly whispered to them by [[Setting/Deities/Ydersius]] all fall under the dominion of these priests.

**Raise Serpent** three actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** The bone prophet animates corpses of snakes, serpentfolk, or similar serpentine creatures within a 30-foot emanation. Any flesh on the bodies sloughs off, and they rise as [[Bestiary/Families/Skeleton (Monster Core)|skeletons]]. The bone prophet can raise one Large creature as a [[Bestiary/Skeletal Giant|skeletal giant]] or up to three Medium creatures as [[Bestiary/Skeletal Champion|skeletal champion]]; the equipment and attacks might be different depending on the corpses' possessions. These skeletons have the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait and are under the bone prophet's control; the bone prophet can give all these minions the same command with a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait. Any skeletal minions that still remain after 10 minutes crumble to dust.
