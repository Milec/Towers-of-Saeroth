# Tenebric Giant

Necromancers reaching for the greatest heights of experimentation know that creating a new terror requires exacting, careful work at every stage of the process. In the case of the tenebric giant, this process begins with a [[Bestiary/Tomb Giant|tomb giant]] who dies after consuming a dose of several poisons combined with the [[Equipment/Shadow Ash|_shadow ash_]] reagent. This creates the right circumstances for a necromancer to raise them as a tenebric giant. They're particularly obedient to the will of the necromancer who created them and are charged full of energy from the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]], which grants them new powers and abilities.

**Cloying Shadows**  ([[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow]]) The tenebric giant's attacks leave behind a residue of energy from the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]]. A creature hit by the tenebric giant's melee Strike takes an additional 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent negative damage]] and must attempt a DC 36 Reflex save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until its next turn begins.  
**Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 minute.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is permanently blinded.

**Tenebric Stride** two actions ([[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Occult]], [[Traits/Shadow]]) The tenebric giant can move quickly in shadowy conditions. The tenebric giant Strides twice and makes a scythe Strike at any point during this movement against a target that is in [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]] or [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]]. This movement doesn't provoke reactions.
