# Zealot

Zealots ferret out plots against their religion and seek justice for their church's followers. This zealot serves [[Setting/Deities/Asmodeus]], but others might serve [[Setting/Deities/Abadar]], [[Setting/Deities/Calistria]], [[Setting/Deities/Iomedae]], [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber]], [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae]], or [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon]]. They often ride a [[Bestiary/War Horse|war horse]] wearing [[Rules/Barding|light barding]]. This horse is an additional monster in the fight.
