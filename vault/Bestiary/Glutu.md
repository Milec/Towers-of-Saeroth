**Swamp Stride**   A boggard scout ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.

**Terrifying Croak** single action ([[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]) The boggard scout unleashes a terrifying croak. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes frightened 1 unless they succeed at a DC 19 Will save; those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Tongue Grab**   If the boggard scout hits a creature with its tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] by the boggard. Unlike with a normal grab, the creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the boggard's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 15 and dealing at least 4 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents it from using its tongue Strike until it regrows its tongue, which takes a week.
