# Warg

Wargs hunt in packs like [[Bestiary/Families/Wolf (Monster Core)|wolves]], but their penchant for taunting victims makes them easy to distinguish from their cousins. [[Bestiary/Families/Orc (Monster Core)|Orcs]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Hobgoblin (Monster Core)|hobgoblins]] frequently recruit wargs. Most wargs are amenable to this arrangement, but if food should run out, wargs are more than willing to integrate goblinoid flesh into their diet.
