**Spiritsense** ([[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|detection]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]]) The spirit binder can sense the spirits of creatures, including living creatures, most non-mindless [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], and [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]] within the listed range. Since spiritsense detects spiritual essence, not physical bodies, it can detect spirits projected by spells (such as [[Spells/Project Image (Player Core)|_project image_]]) or possessing otherwise soulless objects. It can't detect soulless bodies, [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]], or objects, and like most senses, it doesn't penetrate through solid objects.

**Haunting Spirits** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) 30 feet. The spirits bound by a spirit binder swirl around, lashing out at their foes. An enemy that enters or starts its turn in the aura must succeed at a DC 27 Will save or take 3d6 spirit damage and be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] (double damage and frightened 2 on a critical failure).
