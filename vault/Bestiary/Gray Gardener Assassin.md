**Anonymity** The Gray Gardener assassin has a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks and saving throws to conceal their identity and a +2 circumstance bonus to their Deception DC against effects that ferret out information about the Gray Gardener's identity.  
**Gardener's Resolve** ⤾ **Trigger** The Gray Gardener assassin rolls a failure (but not a critical failure) on a Will save against an effect with the [[Traits/Mental|mental]] or [[Traits/Fear|fear]] trait; **Requirements** The Gray Gardener assassins's features are obscured by a mask or hood; **Effect** The Gray Gardener assassin takes 3d8 mental damage, and the saving throw is a success.

**Instant Opening** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) The Gray Gardener assassin distracts a creature within 30 feet, using a few choice words or gestures. The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] against the assassin's attacks until the end of the assassin's next turn. Depending on the distraction, this action gains either the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] or the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait.

**Mark for Death** three actions  The Gray Gardener assassin designates a single creature they can see and hear as their mark. This effect lasts until the mark dies or the assassin uses Mark for Death again. The assassin gains a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek]] the mark and on Deception checks to [[Actions/Feint]] against the mark. The assassin's melee and ranged Strikes gain the deadly d8 weapon traits when attacking the mark.

**Poison Weapon** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate]]) **Requirements** The Gray Gardener assassin is wielding a piercing or slashing weapon and has a free hand; **Effect** The assassin applies a poison to the weapon.

**Quick Draw** single action  The Gray Gardener assassin Interacts to draw a weapon and then Strikes with that weapon.

**Sneak Attack**   The Gray Gardener assassin deals an additional 6d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.

**Surprise Attack**   On the first round of combat, creatures that haven't acted yet are [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to the Gray Gardener assassin.
