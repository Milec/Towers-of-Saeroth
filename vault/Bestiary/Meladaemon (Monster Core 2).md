# Meladaemon (Famine Daemon)

Meladaemons personify death by starvation and thirst, and they revel in spreading the same despair that brought about their mortal demise. When they aren't blighting fields, massacring livestock, or tainting water supplies, they experiment on prisoners to study how long creatures can go without sustenance and the deleterious effects that result from such deprivation. Fiercely loyal to [[Setting/Deities/Trelmarixian (Divine Mysteries)|Trelmarixian]], Apocalypse Rider of Famine, they serve no other beings. They work alongside other daemons if Trelmarixian wills it but are notoriously traitorous.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Formed In His Image
>
>
>
> Meladaemons have always been gaunt and bestial, but they didn't always resemble jackals. When [[Setting/Deities/Trelmarixian (Divine Mysteries)|Trelmarixian]] overthrew the previous Apocalypse Rider of Famine, one of his first acts as a ruler of [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon (GM Core)|Abaddon]] was to forcibly twist the appearance of his deacon caste to resemble his own wicked form. He went on to imbue meladaemons with other jackal-like aspects as it suited him, further warping them and cementing their fealty.
