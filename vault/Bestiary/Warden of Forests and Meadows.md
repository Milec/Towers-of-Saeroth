**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden transforms his size to Tiny, Small, Medium, or Huge (changing his reach to 0 feet for Tiny, 5 feet for Medium or Large, and 10 feet for Huge). The warden retains his shape but can change his color to any seasonal array.

**Forest Passage**   The Warden of Forests and Meadows ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain]] from non-magical foliage.

**Fox's Wager** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) The warden grants a boon to a willing creature he can see other than himself, requesting that the creature promise to use the granted power to destroy or undermine the creations of civilization. The target gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and a +5 status bonus to damage against [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]], objects, and structures constructed by humanoids. It also gains a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks against [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoids]]. The warden can have only one wager granted at a time. The wager ends if the target is captured or dies.

**Idyllic Panorama** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The warden creates an area of peaceful calm. This is identical to a 5th-rank [[Spells/Mirage|_mirage_]] depicting a peaceful meadow or forest, except the area is a 200-foot burst. While within the area of an Idyllic Panorama, the warden has significant control over the senses of creatures within the area and can cast [[Spells/Mislead (Player Core)|_mislead_]] and 2nd-rank [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] at will.

**Magical Pounce** single action  The warden Strides up to his Speed, [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leaps]] up to his Speed, or casts [[Spells/Translocate|_translocate_]]; at the end of that movement, the warden Strikes. If the warden began this action [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], he remains hidden until after this ability's Strike.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   The warden deals an additional 3d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] creatures.
