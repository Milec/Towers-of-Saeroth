**Trinity Star** ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]]) When Dream Aethusa dies, a field of iridescent energy envelops her lifeless body (and her gear) for 10 minutes. During this time, any creature that touches Dream Aethusa's body takes 9d6 force damage (DC 37 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). After 10 minutes have passed, Aethusa's body rises into the air and is consumed by mythic energy, transforming into the Trinity Star hazard.
