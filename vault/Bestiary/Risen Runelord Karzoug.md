**Beyond Life** A risen runelord may drink or eat for pleasure or entertainment, but need not do so for nourishment and never suffers the effects of thirst or starvation.  
**Vanishing Death** ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) When a risen runelord is slain, their body and (if manifested) polearm vanishes as the trapped legacy that was drawn upon to create them is set free, exploding outward in a riot of swirling color. This release of arcane energy blasts at the minds of nearby creatures. All creatures adjacent to a risen runelord during a Vanishing Death must succeed at a DC 37 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 24 hours (stupefied 2 on a critical failure) as fragments of their own memories are temporarily scrambled or hidden. Other objects carried by the risen runelord fall to the ground. This ability only occurs if Chromatic Aegis does not.

**Manifest Runelord's Polearm** single action ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]]) **Requirement** The risen runelord is not holding a runelord's polearm; **Effect** The risen runelord causes their runelord's polearm to appear in one of their hands. The exact appearance of the polearm depends on the type wielded by the runelord in life, but these weapons are purely spiritual constructs that inflict mental damage on a Strike and vanish as soon as the risen runelord releases the weapon or dies.

**Mythic Power**   3 Mythic Points

- _Recharge_ (concentrate) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** The risen runelord regains an expended spell slot or can use Quickened Casting again.
- _Remove a Condition_ (concentrate) **Cost** 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** The risen runelord ends one condition affecting them.
- _Chromatic Aegis_ **Cost** all of Karzoug's Mythic Points; **Requirements** Aethusa's Prison hazard hasn't been destroyed; **Trigger** Karzoug would die and has at least 1 Mythic Point; **Effect** Karzoug uses his [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] breath to destroy Aethusa's prison and wrap himself in the remnants of its magical energy. Karzoug remains standing and conscious, and he recovers 166 Hit Points. He becomes immune to the type of elemental damage the prison was focused on when Karzoug used this ability. At the end of Risen Runelord Karzoug's next turn, he vanishes entirely, reappearing somewhere distant in the Universe. His gear (his _chromatic robe_ and the _slate of distant letters_) remains behind. While the risen runelord no longer takes part in this Adventure Path, he might, at your discretion, rise again in a future campaign of your design. This ability replaces the Undying Myth ability most mythic creatures of this level have.

Quickened Casting free action (concentrate, spellshape); **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** If the risen runelord's next action is to cast a cantrip or spell that is 4th-rank or lower, reduce the number of actions to cast it by 1 (minimum 1 action).

**Steady Spellcasting**   If a reaction would disrupt the risen runelord's spellcasting action, the risen runelord attempts a DC 15 flat check. On a success, the action isn't disrupted.
