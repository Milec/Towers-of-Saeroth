**Survival of the Fittest** ⤾  **Trigger** A 1st- or higher-level animal summoned by Nugrah is killed within 30 feet of him; **Effect** Blood- red energy flows from the slain creature into Nugrah's mouth. He immediately heals 1d6 Hit Points per level of the slain animal, gaining any excess healing as temporary Hit Points that last for 10 minutes. As long as he possesses these temporary Hit Points, blood-red energy ripples across his flesh and his AC increases to 22.

**Swift Summon** two actions  **Prerequisite** Nugrah has a summon animal spell prepared and available. **Effect** Nugrah casts summon animal but does not need to use a material component.
