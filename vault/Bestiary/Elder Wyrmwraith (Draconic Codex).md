# Elder Wyrmwraith

Wyrmwraiths dwell in remote areas marred by catastrophes and death, where they can better hide their undead nature or indulge in their grim research. Their lairs often have a single, hidden and highly defensible antechamber where the wyrmwraith confers with its agents as it engages in a war with distant rivals through expendable proxies. This dimly lit antechamber is usually filled with death and many undead servants, some raised from the bodies of those whose usefulness has come to an end.

**Consume Souls** two actions ([[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]]) The elder wyrmwraith inhales sharply, sucking in the souls of nearby creatures. Each creature in a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] must attempt a DC 46 Will save. The elder wyrmwraith can't Consume Souls again for 1d4 rounds.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] 1.  
**Failure** The creature becomes doomed 2 and takes 100 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is slain. As long as the elder wyrmwraith still exists, the slain creature can't be returned to life through any means.

**Decaying Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) The wyrmwraith unleashes a torrent of void energy that deals 24d6 void damage to all creatures in a 90-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] (DC 46 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). They can't use Decaying Breath again for 1d4 rounds.

**Divine Dispelling**  ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) A wyrmwraith's claws rend divine magic. Whenever a wyrmwraith hits a creature with a spectral claws Strike, the wyrmwraith can attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] check to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] an ongoing [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] spell effect on the creature.

**Draconic Frenzy** two actions  The wyrmwraith makes two claw Strikes and one jaws Strike in any order.

**Drain Life**  ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]) When a wyrmwraith deals damage to a living creature with a spectral jaws or spectral claw Strike, the wyrmwraith gains 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and the target's maximum Hit Points (but not current Hit Points) are reduced by 20. The target's maximum Hit Points are restored after a full night's rest.

**Ectoplasmic Form** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) A wyrmwraith can push through the ectoplasmic veil to temporarily assume a physical form made of ectoplasm. The wyrmwraith loses the [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] trait for 1d4 rounds, though they can return to their incorporeal form as a free action before then. Once this ability ends, the wyrmwraith can't use this ability again for 1d4 rounds. While in their ectoplasmic form, the wyrmwraith's AC increases to 52 and they gain 125 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]]. They lose their immunity to precision and [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]] damage and all their resistances. Their jaws and claw Strikes deal piercing and slashing damage, respectively, instead of void damage.

**Phase Lurch** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]]) **Requirements** The wyrmwraith is in their ectoplasmic form; **Effect** The wyrmwraith Strides, but can pass through walls or material obstacles as though they were [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]]. They must begin and end their movement outside of any physical obstacles, and passing through solid material is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].

**Void's Embrace**  ([[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) If a victim succeeds at a saving throw against this [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]] while in sunlight, the curse ends. While a creature has this curse, they bypass the resistances of the wyrmwraith that cursed them; **Saving Throw** DC 46 Will; **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] in any light (1 hour); **Stage 2** gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]] 30 feet but is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] in any light (1 hour); **Stage 3** as stage 2, but also has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]] (1 hour); **Stage 4** [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and can't awaken (1 day); **Stage 5** dead and becomes a [[Bestiary/Wraith (Monster Core)|wraith]], its body crumbling to ash.

**Wings of Shadow** single action ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Void|void]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The wyrmwraith spreads its spectral wings wide covering all near it in shadow. Each living creature within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] is exposed to void's embrace.
