**Clutch** single action  **Requirements** The enormous dragonfly has a Large or smaller creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] in its mandibles; **Effect** The dragonfly tries to transfer the grabbed creature to be clutched by its legs. The enormous dragonfly attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check against the creature's Reflex DC. On a success, it transfers the creature (which remains grabbed) to its legs, freeing its mandibles to attack. The dragonfly can have only one creature clutched at a time.

**Snatch**   The enormous dragonfly can [[Actions/Fly]] at half Speed while it has a creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]] by Clutch, carrying that creature along with it.

**Swoop** two actions  The dragonfly [[Actions/Fly|Flies]] up to its Speed and makes one mandible Strike at any point during that movement.
