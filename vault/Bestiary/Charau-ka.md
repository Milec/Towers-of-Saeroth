**Shrieking Frenzy** free action ([[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) **Trigger** The charau-ka's turn begins. **Frequency** Once per hour; **Effect** The charau-ka is [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] until the end of its turn, and can use the extra action to Stride or Strike. Due to its loud wailing while in a shrieking frenzy, the charau-ka can't speak and automatically critically fails all [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks.

**Thrown Weapon Mastery**   When a charau-ka throws a weapon, the weapon gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d6]] weapon trait. When it throws an improvised weapon, it doesn't take the –2 penalty for using an improvised weapon, nor does it take the penalty for using that weapon to make a lethal attack instead of a nonlethal attack
