# Light Blindness  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 359

When first exposed to [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]], the monster is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] until the end of its next turn. After this exposure, light doesn't blind the monster again until after it spends 1 hour in darkness. However, as long as the monster is in an area of bright light, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]].
