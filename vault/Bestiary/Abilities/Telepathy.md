# Telepathy  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 344

([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Magical|magical]]) A monster with telepathy can communicate mentally with any creatures within the listed radius, as long as they share a language. This doesn’t give any special access to their thoughts, and communicates no more information than normal speech would.
