# Tremorsense  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

Tremorsense allows a monster to feel the vibrations through a solid surface caused by movement. It is usually an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a limited range (listed in the ability). Tremorsense functions only if the monster is on the same surface as the subject, and only if the subject is moving along (or burrowing through) the surface.
