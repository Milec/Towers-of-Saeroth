# Frightful Presence  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 359

([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) A creature that first enters the area must attempt a Will save. Regardless of the result of the saving throw, the creature is temporarily immune to this monster's Frightful Presence for 1 minute.

 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected by the presence. **Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]]. **Failure** The creature is frightened 2. **Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 4.
