# Attack of Opportunity ⤾  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 342

**Trigger** A creature within the monster's reach uses a [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] action or a [[Traits/Move|move]] action, makes a ranged attack, or leaves a square during a move action it's using. **Effect** The monster attempts a melee Strike against the triggering creature. If the attack is a critical hit and the trigger was a manipulate action, the monster [[Rules/Disrupting Actions|disrupts]] that action. This Strike doesn't count toward the monster's multiple attack penalty, and its multiple attack penalty doesn't apply to this Strike.
