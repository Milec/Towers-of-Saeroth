# Light Blindness  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

When first exposed to bright light, the monster is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the end of its next turn. After this exposure, light doesn’t blind the monster again until after it spends 1 hour in darkness. However, as long as the monster is in an area of bright light, it’s [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]].
