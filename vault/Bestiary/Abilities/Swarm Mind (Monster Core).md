# Swarm Mind  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

This monster doesn't have a single mind (typically because it's a swarm of smaller creatures) and is immune to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects that target only a specific number of creatures. It is still subject to mental effects that affect all creatures in an area.
