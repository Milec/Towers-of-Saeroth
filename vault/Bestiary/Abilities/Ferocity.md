# Ferocity ⤾  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

**Trigger** The monster is reduced to 0 HP. **Effect** The monster avoids being knocked out and remains at 1 HP, but its [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] value increases by 1. When it is [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded 3]], it can no longer use this ability.
