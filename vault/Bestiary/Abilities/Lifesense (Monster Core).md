# Lifesense  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 359

Lifesense allows a monster to sense the vital essence of living and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures within the listed range. The sense can distinguish between the vitality energy animating living creatures and the void energy animating undead creatures, much as sight distinguishes colors.
