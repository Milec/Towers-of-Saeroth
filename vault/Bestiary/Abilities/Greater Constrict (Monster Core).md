# Greater Constrict single action  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 359

The monster deals the listed amount of damage to any number of creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by it. Each of those creatures can attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save with the listed DC. A creature that fails this save falls [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]], and a creature that succeeds is then temporarily immune to falling unconscious from Greater Constrict for 1 minute.
