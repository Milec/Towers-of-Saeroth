# Swallow Whole single action  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]]) The monster attempts to swallow a creature of the listed size or smaller that it has [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] in its jaws or mouth. If a swallowed creature is of the maximum size listed, the monster can't use Swallow Whole again. If the creature is smaller than the maximum, the monster can usually swallow more creatures; the GM determines the maximum. The monster attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check opposed by the target's Reflex DC. If it succeeds, it swallows the creature. The monster's mouth or jaws no longer clutch a creature it has swallowed, so the monster is free to use them to Strike or [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] once again. The monster can't attack creatures it has swallowed.

 A swallowed creature is grabbed, is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]], and has to hold its breath or start [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|suffocating]]. The swallowed creature takes the listed amount of damage when first swallowed and at the end of each of its turns while it's swallowed. If the victim [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] this ability's grabbed condition, it exits through the monster's mouth. This frees any other creature captured in the monster's mouth or jaws. A swallowed creature can attack the monster that has swallowed it, but only with [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attacks or with weapons of light Bulk or less. The swallowing creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] against the attack. If the monster takes piercing or slashing damage equaling or exceeding the listed Rupture value from a single attack or spell, the swallowed creature cuts itself free. A creature that gets free by either Escaping or cutting itself free can immediately breathe and exits the swallowing monster's space.

 If the monster dies, a swallowed creature can be freed by creatures adjacent to the corpse if they spend a combined total of 3 actions cutting the monster open with a weapon or unarmed attack that deals piercing or slashing damage.
