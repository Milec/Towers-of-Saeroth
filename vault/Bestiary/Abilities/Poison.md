# Poison  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

When a creature is exposed to a monster’s poison, it attempts a Fortitude save to avoid becoming poisoned. The level of a poison is the level of the monster inflicting the poison. The poison follows the [[Rules/Afflictions|rules for afflictions]].
