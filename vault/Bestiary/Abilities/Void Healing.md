# Void Healing  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

A creature with void healing draws health from void energy rather than vitality energy. It is damaged by [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage and is not healed by [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] vitality effects. It does not take void damage, and it is healed by [[Traits/Void|void]] effects that heal undead.
