# Constrict  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 358

The monster deals the listed amount of damage to any number of creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by it. Each of those creatures can attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save with the listed DC.
