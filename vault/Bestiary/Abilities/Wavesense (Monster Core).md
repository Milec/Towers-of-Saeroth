# Wavesense  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

This sense allows a monster to feel vibrations caused by movement through a liquid. It's usually an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a limited range (listed in the ability). Wavesense functions only if the monster and the subject are in the same body of liquid, and only if the subject is moving through the liquid.
