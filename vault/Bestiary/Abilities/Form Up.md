# Form Up single action  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary 3]] pg. 304

The [[Traits/Troop|troop]] chooses one of the squares it currently occupies and redistributes its squares to any configuration in which all squares are contiguous and within 15 feet of the chosen square. The troop can't share its space with other creatures.
