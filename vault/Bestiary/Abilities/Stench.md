# Stench  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|olfactory]]) A creature entering the aura or starting its turn in the area must succeed at a Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] (plus slowed 1 as long as it's [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] on a critical failure). A creature that succeeds at its save or recovers from being sickened is temporarily immune to all stench auras for 1 minute.
