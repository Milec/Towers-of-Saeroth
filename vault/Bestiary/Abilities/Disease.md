# Disease  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

When a creature is exposed to a monster’s disease, it attempts a Fortitude save or succumbs to the disease. The level of a disease is the level of the monster inflicting the disease. The disease follows the [[Rules/Afflictions|rules for afflictions]].
