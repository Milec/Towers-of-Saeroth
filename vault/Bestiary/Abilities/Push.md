# Push single action  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

**Requirements** The monster’s last action was a success with a Strike that lists Push in its damage entry. **Effect** The monster automatically knocks the target away from the monster. Unless otherwise noted in the ability description, the creature is pushed 5 feet. If the attack was a critical hit, this distance is doubled.
