# Regeneration  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

This monster regains the listed number of Hit Points each round at the beginning of its turn. Its [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition never increases beyond [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying 3]] as long as its regeneration is active. However, if it takes damage of a type listed in the regeneration entry, its regeneration deactivates until the end of its next turn. Deactivate the regeneration before applying any damage of a listed type, since that damage might kill the monster by bringing it to [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying 4]].
