# Constant Spells  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 358

A constant spell affects the monster without the monster needing to cast it, and its duration is unlimited. If a constant spell gets [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteracted]], the monster can reactivate it by spending the normal spellcasting actions the spell requires.
