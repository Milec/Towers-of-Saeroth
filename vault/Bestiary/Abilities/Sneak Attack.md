# Sneak Attack  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 344

When the monster Strikes a creature that has the [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] condition with an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] melee weapon, an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] unarmed attack, or a ranged weapon attack, it also deals the listed precision damage. For a ranged attack with a thrown weapon, that weapon must also be an [[Traits/Agile|agile]] or [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] weapon.
