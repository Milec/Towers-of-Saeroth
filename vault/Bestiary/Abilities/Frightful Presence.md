# Frightful Presence  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Bestiary]] pg. 343

([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) A creature that first enters the area must attempt a Will save. Regardless of the result of the saving throw, the creature is temporarily immune to this monster’s Frightful Presence for 1 minute.

 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected by the presence.  
 **Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  
 **Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]].  
 **Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 4]].
