# Scent  *Creature Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Monster Core]] pg. 360

Scent involves sensing creatures or objects by smell and is usually a [[Rules/Vague Senses|vague]] sense. The range is listed in the ability, and it functions only if the creature or object being detected emits an aroma (for instance, [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures usually do not exude an aroma).

 If a creature emits a heavy aroma or is upwind, the GM can double or even triple the range of scent abilities used to detect that creature, and the GM can reduce the range if a creature is downwind.
