# Forge-Spurned

Evil [[Ancestries/Dwarf|dwarves]] who die having failed to live up to the exacting standards of the [[Bestiary/Families/Duergar|duergar]] god [[Setting/Deities/Droskar]] are sometimes forced to return to the material world as undead abominations known as forge-spurned. Forge-spurned look like dwarves with pupilless eyes and leathery flesh, and with their hair and beard made of smoke and fire—newly formed forge-spurned can almost be mistaken for living creatures as a result. The longer forge-spurned exist, the more skeletal and fiery their visage becomes; those who have existed for centuries or more often look as if they have skulls for faces and fire for hair.

**Inflate Bellows** two actions  **Requirements** The forge-spurned has used Forge Breath; **Effect** The forge-spurned draws a massive breath to refill their emptied lungs, enabling them to use Forge Breath again.

**Forge Breath** two actions ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]) The forge-spurned breathes a cloud of stinging soot, ash, and glowing embers. This can affect either a 30-foot cone or a 20-foot burst centered on the forge-spurned and persists for 1d4 rounds. Each creature that moves into or starts their turn in the area takes 6d6 fire damage, with a DC 21 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature who fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 minute. Creatures within the cloud are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], though not from the forge-spurned. The forge-spurned can't use Forge Breath again until they've used Inflate Bellows.

**Soul Chain**  ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) If a creature is slain by an attack from the _soul chain_ and the forge-spurned is able to retain the creature's corpse, they can bind the creature's soul into a _soul chain_ (as [[Spells/Bind Soul|_bind soul_]]) with a day of work at a forge and a successful DC 20 [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] check. An individual whose soul is bound in this way can't be resurrected unless the _soul chain_ is destroyed (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10).

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Haunted Forges
>
>
>
> Forge-spurned are often encountered near abandoned forges, and the presence of these undead dwarves can infest that forge with an ever-burning fire. Such “haunted” forges burn without the need for fuel, though they extinguish automatically when their forge-spurned source is destroyed.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Soul Chains
>
>
>
> A forge-spurned wields a soul chain: a cursed _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Spiked Chain|spiked chain]]_. Any other creature that has a _soul chain_ must succeed at a DC 22 Will save each day at sundown or transform into a forge-spurned permanently. A forge-spurned stops at nothing to retrieve its _soul chain_ if it’s lost.
