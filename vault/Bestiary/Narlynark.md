A pair of feathery wings grows from his back and his feet turn into bird talons. As he leaps into the air to flee, his talons lash out—one adjacent enemy of Narlynark's choice takes 3d6+6 slashing damage (DC 22 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Narlynark can then fly up to 35 feet away. He must end his movement in a space that isn't adjacent to any enemies, and if he doesn't end the movement on the ground, he falls to the ground as his wings vanish and his feet return to normal.
