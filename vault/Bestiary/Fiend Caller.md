# Fiend Caller

Fiend callers act as intermediaries to help mortals sell their souls or make other deals with fiends. You can adjust a fiend caller to be accompanied by a bound fiend. The fiend gains the [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|minion]] trait, and you can replace the fiend caller's [[Spells/Spiritual Armament|_spiritual armament_]] spell with [[Spells/Final Sacrifice (Player Core 2)|_final sacrifice_]].

**Fiendish Contract**  ([[Traits/Downtime (Player Core)|Downtime]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|Occult]]) The fiend caller spends 1 day of downtime setting up a bargain between a mortal creature and a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] the fiend caller knows well. The fiend caller attempts a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Legal Lore]] check against the higher of the fiend's Will DC or [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] DC.  
 **Success** The mortal party receives one favor from the fiend, or the fiend becomes the mortal's minion for 1d4 days if they're on the same plane. Alternatively, if the GM allows the option, the mortal can receive a [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|bargained contract]] of the fiend's level or lower.  
 **Failure** The fiend caller fails to strike the bargain.  
 **Critical Failure** The process fails, and the magical backlash makes the fiend caller [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 2]].

**Fiendish Ritualist**   A fiend caller can cast [[Rituals/Binding Circle|_binding circle_]] and [[Rituals/Commune (Player Core)|_commune_]] to contact [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]] even though the rituals are beyond the normal rank the fiend caller could cast. Furthermore, they can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Legal Lore]] for the primary check when they do so instead of the listed skill.

**Planar Communique**   A fiend caller can cast [[Spells/Sending (Player Core)|_sending_]] at will as an occult innate spell, but only to target a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] they know well. The fiend can be on any plane.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Keeping Enemies Close
>
>
>
> Heroes may have an easier time dealing with a fiend caller peacefully than they would another villain. Fiend callers are willing to work with just about anyone as long as they receive adequate compensation and may even be helpful in stopping more dangerous fiends. But they are opportunistic above all else. Once the transaction is over, they aren't likely to stick around as a friend, and if a better deal comes along, they might void a prior contract.
