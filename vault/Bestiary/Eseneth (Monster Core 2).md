# Eseneth

Eseneths, commonly known as soul-stitchers, are dispassionate surgeons who repair damaged souls. They operate with professional efficiency, manifesting large stitching needles composed of spirit energy to do their necessary work. An eseneth appears as a slim, hairless, gray-skinned humanoid with no facial features. Patches of dark, raised flesh dot their upper chest and shoulders.

**Mend Soul** single action ([[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The eseneth restores 25 HP to itself or an [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creature it has grabbed.

**Seize Soul** single action ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]]) **Requirements** The eseneth has a corporeal creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]; **Effect** The eseneth tries to yank the soul out of the required creature. The eseneth attempts an Athletics check against the target's Fortitude DC.  
 **Critical Success** The eseneth grabs the target's soul. The body is released and is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]] for 2 rounds. When the body ceases being paralyzed, its soul returns instantly, and the target wakes. The soul—grabbed by the eseneth—is [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]], is invisible, has a fly Speed equal to the creature's Speed, and otherwise has all the same statistics. It can't attack, cast spells, or attempt any skill checks that require a physical body other than checks to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (DC 38), and it must always maintain line of effect to its body.  
 **Success** As critical success, but the paralysis ends after 1 round.  
 **Failure** The target remains grabbed or restrained, but its soul remains in its body.  
 **Critical Failure** The target is no longer grabbed or restrained.

**Shepherd's Touch**   A psychopomp's Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures with the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal 2d6 void damage to living creatures and 2d6 vitality damage to [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]].

**Shred Soul** single action  **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** The eseneth has an [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creature [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]; **Effect** The eseneth deals 3d10+14 force damage to the required creature.

**Spirit Grasp**   An eseneth can [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]] [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures despite being corporeal. The eseneth uses [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to Grapple incorporeal creatures as normal but can't use Athletics for other actions against incorporeal creatures, like [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] or [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]].

> ## Spirit Needles
