# Lampad Queen

Lampad queens are capricious monarchs and protectors of vast underground domains, regarded in ballads and tales as both allies and foes, monsters and muses. Lampad queens have a particular animosity for any predominantly cruel underground societies, while they're particularly fond of bats. Many lampad queens have [[Bestiary/Families/Sprite (Monster Core)|sprites]] as favored attendants.

**Cavern Empathy** The lampad queen can use [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]] on and make very simple [[Actions/Request (Player Core)|Requests]] of subterranean animals, plants, and fungi, as well as stones.  
**Tied to the Land** A lampad queen is intrinsically tied to a specific underground region, usually a cave system. As long as the queen is healthy, the environment is exceptionally resilient, allowing the lampad queen to automatically attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] spells and rituals that would harm the environment, such as [[Rituals/Blight (Player Core)|_blight_]], with a +30 counteract modifier and a counteract rank of 8. When the lampad queen becomes physically or psychologically unhealthy, however, their warded region eventually becomes twisted or unhealthy as well. In that case, restoring the lampad queen swiftly heals the entire region.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Change Shape (Monster Core)|Change Shape]]** single action ([[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) Lampad queens can transform between their original form, which looks much like a typical nymph of their kind, and any Small or Medium humanoid form, typically choosing a version of their natural form that more closely resembles a humanoid.

**Despairing Weep** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The lampad queen begins a heart-wrenching fit of weeping, inspiring sympathetic sobbing in nearby creatures. Every non-lampad creature within 120 feet who hears the lampad's weeping must succeed at a DC 36 Will save with the effects of [[Spells/Wave of Despair|_wave of despair_]].

**Focus Beauty** single action ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) The lampad queen focuses their beauty upon a target within their aura. The creature must attempt a DC 33 Will save. On a failure, it's affected as if by the queen's nymph beauty aura; if it was already affected by the aura, the conflicting emotions from the lampad queen's beauty intensify, causing the target to no longer get a [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to end the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confusion]] when it takes damage. The lampad queen can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, to focus the emotions of a confused creature toward a particular emotion, causing it to spend its next turn sobbing uncontrollably, fawning over the lampad queen, or otherwise performing no actions beyond experiencing its emotions. Regardless of the save, the target is temporarily immune to Focus Beauty until the start of the lampad queen's next turn.

**Inspiration** three actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|primal]]) A lampad queen can inspire a single intelligent creature by giving that creature a token of their favor, typically a lock of their hair. As long as the creature carries the token and remains in good standing with the lampad queen, the creature gains a +1 status bonus to all [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks, and Will saves.  
If a lampad queen grants their Inspiration to a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (Player Core)|bard]] and they're that bard's muse, the bard gains an additional benefit depending on their muse theme: for lore muse, the bard also gains a +1 status bonus to all [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Lore]] checks; for maestro muse, the status bonus to Performance checks increases to +2 for the purpose of determining the effects of compositions; for polymath muse, the bard gains a +4 status bonus to untrained skill checks; and for all other muses, the Will save bonus increases to +2 against [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]].

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Reciprocity
>
>
>
> Lampad queens tend to change emotions on a whim and mirror what they find in others. Those who treat the queen's domain with respect, they protect and reward, while those who harm it meet swift wrath. This leads to the vastly differing accounts of the queens' actions.
