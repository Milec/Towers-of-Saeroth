# Silver Saber

This construct was built hundreds of years ago by Red Mantis arcanists, then imbued with intelligence as a divine protector by priests of [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek]] not long after the assassin cult fled Rahadoum. Silver Saber's single purpose is to guard the secrets of the Blood Mistress from those who are unworthy.

 When at rest, Silver Saber appears as an ornate mirror, but when confronted in battle they animate into the form of a praying mantis made of polished, almost liquid silver with a blank humanoid face.

**Faith Bound** ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]]) Silver Saber can't attack a creature that openly wears or displays [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek's]] religious symbol unless that creature uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against Silver Saber first. **Reflect Truths** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) 20 feet. A creature that enters the area must succeed at a DC 37 Will save or they see their own visage in Silver Saber's reflective face looking back at them. For 1 minute, that creature can't speak any deliberate and intentional lies and takes a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks. On a critical failure, the status penalty is increased to –4.

After attempting its save, the creature is then temporarily immune until the start of Silver Saber's next turn.

**Liquefy** single action  Silver Saber becomes fully liquid and amorphous. They gain immunity to precision damage but take double cold damage. The only actions they can use while liquefied are [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] actions and spike ranged Strikes. They can resume their normal form by taking this action again.

**Mirror** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) Until the next time they act, Silver Saber appears to be a mirror. They have an automatic result of 53 on [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks and DCs to pass as a mirror.

**Silver Strikes**   Silver Saber's leg and spike Strikes count as [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] weapons.

**Swift Steps**   Silver Saber is permanently [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]], but they can use the extra action only to Step or Stride.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Life As A Mirror
>
>
>
> Silver Saber has watched the followers of [[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek]] for centuries, sitting silently in their gold frame and pretending to be a mirror. They know more about the secret lives of the Red Mantis than has ever been recorded in the Sarzari, but their objective isn't to record history, it's to function as a guardian—no more, no less.
