# Shadow Giant

Shadow giants are natives of [[Setting/Planes/The Netherworld|the Netherworld]], where they have dwelled in perpetual twilight for millennia. They live in familiar groups and uphold a nomadic way of life as they roam across ancestral lands between shadowy forests and misty chasms. These hunter-gatherers pass down lore through oral histories, conduct pilgrimages to unholy ziggurats of black stone, and bathe in the blood of their long-standing foes, including rival shadow giant clans and fiends of the Netherworld intent on enslaving their kind.

 Standing 15 feet tall, with gray skin and hair only a shade lighter, shadow giants are fearsome foes with a well-earned reputation as zealous warmongers and ruthless combatants. They rarely interact with outsiders, though they may treat with proven warriors who show the giants the respect and deference they feel they deserve.

**Pall of Shadow**  ([[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|Divine]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]]) When a shadow giant hits with a melee attack, the target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] and take a –1 status penalty to Perception checks involving sight as long as they remain drained.
