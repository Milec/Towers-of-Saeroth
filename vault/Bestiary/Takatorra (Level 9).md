**Blade Barrage** two actions  Takatorra makes three melee Strikes. These Strikes gain the [[Traits/Forceful|forceful]] trait. If one target is hit with at least two of these Strikes, they also take 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. The target is then [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until they heal the persistent bleed damage.

**Disarming Whirl** two actions ([[Traits/Flourish]]) Takatorra spins, striking at their foes' weapons. Takatorra attempts an [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Disarm]] against each enemy within melee reach. Each attempt counts toward Takatorra's multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until they have made all the attempts.

**Soaring Flight** two actions ([[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Effect** Takatorra expands their wings, gaining a 25-foot fly Speed for 5 minutes.
