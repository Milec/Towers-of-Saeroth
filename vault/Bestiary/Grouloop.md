**Soul-Shaking Croak** two actions ([[Traits/Sonic]]) Grouloop utters a powerful croak that deals 6d10 sonic damage to any non-[[Bestiary/Families/Boggard|boggard]] within a 30-foot emanation (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). A creature with the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition that fails its saving throw increases its frightened condition by 1 (on a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for as long as it is frightened). Grouloop can't use Soul-Shaking Croak again for 1d4 rounds.

**Swamp Stride**   Grouloop ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.

**Tongue Grab**   If Grouloop hits a creature with her tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]]. Unlike with a normal grab, the creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of Grouloop's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 24 and dealing at least 9 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents her from using her tongue Strike until she regrows her tongue, which takes a week.
