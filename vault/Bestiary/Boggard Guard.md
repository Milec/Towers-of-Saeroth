**Swamp Passage**   A boggard guard ignores difficult terrain caused by swamp terrain features.

**Tongue Grab**   If the boggard guard hits a creature with their tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the boggard. Unlike with a normal [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]], the creature is not [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the boggard's tongue. The DC to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] is 15, or a creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 12 and dealing at least 1 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents them from using their tongue Strike again until they regrow their tongue, which takes a week.
