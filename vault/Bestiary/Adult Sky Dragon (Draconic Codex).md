# Adult Sky Dragon

When the imperial dragons first arrived in [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|the Universe]], each was well-attuned to the elemental planes they hailed from. As the war between the [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords]] raged on and the benevolent lords fell one by one, the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal|Planes of Metal]] and [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood|Wood]] sealed themselves away. For the dragons who originated from the Corroded Core, their very existence was now at stake. These dragons flew from mountaintop to mountaintop, seeking refuge from the then-perilous Tian Xia overrun by warring yaoguai. Finally, upon the mountain peaks of Chenlun, Gossamer, Kelsang, Kimu, Kullan, Kyojin, and the Wall of Heaven, celestial beings answered the dragons' cries for help. What the dragons no longer had in elemental [[Traits/Metal|metal]], these celestials provided through divine blessings and essence. With their nature changed, the dragons as a species survived. In time, their proximity to the sky and the deities they serve became their namesake. Their past as elemental metal dragons became but a faded memory and creation myth for all but the sky dragons themselves to remember.
