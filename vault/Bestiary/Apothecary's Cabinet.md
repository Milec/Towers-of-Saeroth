**Poison Stock**   When the cabinet hits a creature with its poison bottle Strike, roll 1d4 to determine what kind of poison splashes onto the struck creature: **1** nerve poison ([[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]]); **2** weakening poison ([[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]]); **3** noxious poison ([[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]]); **4** clouded mind poison ([[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]]). The target must attempt a DC 24 Fortitude save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
 **Success** The creature takes 1d6 poison damage.  
 **Failure** The creature takes 2d6 poison damage and is afflicted with the rolled poison's associated condition until the end of its next turn.  
 **Critical Failure** The creature takes 2d6 poison damage and is afflicted with the rolled poison's associated condition for 1d4+1 rounds.
