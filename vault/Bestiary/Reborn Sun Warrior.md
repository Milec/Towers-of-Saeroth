**Burning Sacrifice** ([[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Evocation|evocation]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) When the sun warrior is destroyed, they explode in a blast of flame that deals 6d6 fire damage in a 10-foot emanation (DC 27 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that critically fail their saves are marked with [[Setting/Deities/Walkena|Walkena's]] religious symbol on a visible area of their body, which remains for 1 day. This mark allows Walkena to treat the creature as if he knew the creature's identity but not having met them for the purposes of [[Spells/Scrying|_scrying_]], lowering the spell's DC by 2. The sun warrior's gear and treasure are unaffected by the explosion and left in a pile where they die.

**Knocking Sweep** two actions  **Frequency** once per round; **Effect** The sun warrior swings their weapon with a sweeping spin. They attempt separate [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Trip]] any number of creatures within their reach. Each attempt counts toward the sun warrior's multiple attack penalty, but the multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until after they make all the attacks.

**Pinning Thrust** two actions  The sun warrior attempts to drive their spear through a foe to pin them in place. The sun warrior makes a spear Strike. On a hit, the target's clothes, armor, or other piece of equipment becomes pinned to the ground. The sun warrior Releases the spear, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] until they can remove the spear ([[Actions/Escape]] DC 27). If the Strike was a critical hit, the DC increases by 2.
