**Sanguine Aura** ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]]) 30 feet. Around Turkek, wounds heal slowly and blood flows faster. The flat check to stop [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] is DC 16, rather than DC 15. In addition, each time a creature in the emanation takes persistent bleed damage, it takes 1d6 additional bleed damage.  
**Clot** ⤾ **Trigger** Turkek takes persistent bleed damage **Effect** Turkek's blood hardens, sealing her wounds. Turkek automatically passes her next flat check to recover from the triggering [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].
