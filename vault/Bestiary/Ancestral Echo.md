**Riled by Disrespect** An ancestral echo gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and saving throw DCs against any creature that has not performed at least one task to beautify the site to which the echo is bound. This applies to any PC who didn't succeed at one or more skill checks to clean area D2.
