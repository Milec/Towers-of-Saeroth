**Disperse** ⤾ ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]]) **Trigger** The living whirlwind takes damage from a hostile action; **Effect** The living whirlwind disperses. Until the end of the current turn, it can't be attacked or targeted, doesn't take up space, and its high winds aura is suppressed. At the end of the turn, the living whirlwind reforms in any unoccupied space within 25 feet of where it dispersed, and its high winds are restored.
