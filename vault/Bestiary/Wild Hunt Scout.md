# Wild Hunt Scout

These compact few have pointed ears, green eyes, and ram's horns. They relish the chance to seek revenge upon those who have wronged or insulted the wild hunt, but they tend to grow bored when the stakes aren't lethal.

**Befuddle**  ([[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Primal]]) A wild hunt scout can emit a beam of emerald light from their crystal shard to attempt an emerald beam ranged Strike. If the beam hits, it inflicts no damage (nor does it inflict precision damage from a sneak attack), but it overstimulates the target's mind, causing them to become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] for 1 round. On a critical hit, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round.

**Focus Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Primal]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The wild hunt scout fixes its glare at a creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must immediately attempt a Will save against the wild hunt scout's wild gaze. If the creature was already [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] by the wild gaze before attempting its save, a failed save causes it to be [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round. After attempting its save, the creature is temporarily immune to Focus Gaze from any wild hunt until the start of the wild hunt scout's next turn.

**Forceful Focus** three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) **Requirement** The wild hunt scout cast [[Spells/Magic Missile|_magic missile_]] the previous round. **Effect** The wild hunt scout focuses on their mastery of force, standing still as they become swathed in pulsing emerald light. Any _magic missile_ innate spells they have cast that day are restored, allowing them to be cast again. Until the end of their next turn, any creature that takes force damage from the wild hunt scout must succeed at a DC 40 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] (stunned 3 on a critical failure). The wild hunt scout can't use forceful focus for 1 hour.

**Manifest Crystals** free action  A wild hunt scout can manifest a _+2 [[Equipment/Striking|greater striking]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar|scimitar]]_ made of crystalized force or a fist-sized shard of the same in her hands. These objects vanish upon the wild hunt scout's death, or as soon as they no longer possess the items.

**Deny Advantage**   A wild hunt scout isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]], or flanking creatures of your level or lower, or creatures of their level or lower using surprise attack. However, they can still help their allies flank.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Sneak Attack]]**   A wild hunt scout's Strikes deal 3d6 extra precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] creatures.
