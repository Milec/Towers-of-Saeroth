**Break Ground** two actions  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|arcane]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) The blackfrost guecubu stomps, and the ground breaks and ripples in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]], erupting in razor-sharp, crisscrossing spikes of rock that deal 3d8 piercing damage to creatures in the area (DC 26 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (GM Core)|hazardous terrain]], with each square dealing 3 piercing damage to a creature that moves through it. A guecubu is immune to these effects. Ground under the effects of a [[Rituals/Consecrate (Player Core)|consecrate]] ritual or a [[Spells/Circle of Protection|_circle of protection_]] spell tuned against evil or chaos can't be affected by this ability.

**Shove into Ice**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) When the blackfrost guecubu successfully [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoves]] a creature into an ice or frozen earth barrier, the target must succeed at a DC 26 Reflex save or become merged with the barrier, with the effects of [[Spells/One with Stone|_one with stone_]] until the target [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|escapes]] (DC 26).

**Tundra Glide**   The blackfrost guecubu can [[Actions/Burrow (Player Core)|Burrow]] through any frozen earth, ice, or snow, not including rock. When they do so, the blackfrost guecubu moves at their full [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow Speed]], leaving no tunnels or signs of their passing.

**Wrathful Misfortune**  ([[Traits/Arcane (Player Core)|Arcane]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]]) A creature damaged by a blackfrost guecubu must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or become cursed with consummate bad luck. The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2. Each time that creature rolls initiative, on their next roll they must roll twice and use the worse result; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] effect.
