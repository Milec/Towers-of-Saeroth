# Giant Leech

Capable of growing to lengths of nearly 5 feet, giant leeches have circular maws filled with hook-like teeth. They prefer to dwell in stagnant or slowmoving shallow water or in damp, moist undergrowth. [[Bestiary/Families/Horse (Monster Core)|Horses]] and larger animals are their favorite prey, but they won't balk at a chance to latch onto a [[Ancestries/Human (Player Core)|human]]- or [[Ancestries/Halfling (Player Core)|halfling]]-sized meal.
