# Gigantic Bee Royal Guardian

Tasked with protecting the queen and her eggs, gigantic bee royal guardians are even larger than most others in the nest. Their unusual anatomy gives them the ability to project a stream of venom from their stingers, and unlike their kin, they're unaffected by smoke.
