# Crownbound Constellation

Crownbound constellations are the essence of stars granted sentience by the goddess [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]], who created them to survey the vastness of space and help her craft the galaxy. Their intergalactic travels once took them far, but [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]] sought to ensnare Desna's creation. The Mother of Beasts lured them to Golarion and then, exploiting the power of Golarion's sun, corrupted their ability to star travel. Stranded on the planet and unable to bear sunlight, these creatures traveled to the Crown of the World, where they have settled ever since.

**Dazzling Assault** single action ([[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|[visual]]](/Traits.aspx?ID=727)) Each enemy in the crownbound constellation's space takes 7d4 bludgeoning damage (DC 23 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail this save are also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of their next turn.

**Starburst**  ([[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) A creature that takes damage from a crownbound constellation's attack must succeed at a DC 23 Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 minute.

**Star Weapons** single action  The crownbound constellation reforms its two weapons as [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|_+1 striking_]] common or uncommon simple or martial weapons. These star weapons can't be [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|disarmed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]], [[Rules/Release|dropped]], [[Rules/Conditions/Destroyed|destroyed]], or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|stolen]]. Ranged star weapons don't require ammunition and those with a reload of 2 have a reload of 0 instead. When the crownbound constellation dies or uses its swarm shape its star weapons disappear.

**Swarm Shape** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]) The crownbound constellation disperses into a shapeless swarm of Small stars. It drops all held, worn, and carried items. While discorporated, the crownbound constellation can't use [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] actions, but it has a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 40 feet and can move through areas small enough for its individual stars to fit without having to [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]]. It can use Swarm Shape again to coalesce back into its normal form, at which point its star weapons reappear.

>
>
>
> Crownbound constellations have not forgotten [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu's]] deception, or the commission set for them by [[Setting/Deities/Desna (Divine Mysteries)|Desna]]. Crownbound constellations frequently attack those they assume to be in league with the Mother of Monsters, and their hatred for her has severed their connection to Desna, just as Lamashtu planned.
