# Voidglutton

A voidglutton is a powerful type of [[Bestiary/Will-o’-Wisp|will-o'-wisp]] that haunts a site where misery dwells—such as a graveyard, prison camp, or recent battlefield. A voidglutton appears as a seething sphere of glowing eyes swirling around an empty black vortex 4 feet wide. From within this vortex extend long, semi-transparent tendrils that end in seven-fingered, needled hands.

**Consume Light** free action ([[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Occult]]) **Trigger** The voidglutton casts [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]]; **Effect** The voidglutton extinguishes its Glow as part of Casting the Spell. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] as long as it remains in the area of _darkness_. If the voidglutton uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]], its invisibility ends as soon as the hostile action is completed.

**Ectoplasmic Web Trap**  ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Occult]]) A creature hit by the voidglutton's ectoplasmic web trap is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and stuck to the nearest surface until it succeeds at a DC 26 check to [[Actions/Escape]]. Ectoplasmic Web Trap can immobilize [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures.

**Fearful Strike**  ([[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Occult]]) When the voidglutton damages a creature with its claw Strike, the creature must succeed at a DC 26 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] (frightened 2 on a critical failure).

**Feed on Fear** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirement** An enemy is affected by a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect or has the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition, and is within 25 feet of the voidglutton; **Effect** The voidglutton feeds on the creature's terror. It regains 3d4 Hit Points and its Glow reignites if it had been extinguished. It cannot use Consume Light again for 1d4 rounds, as it is too glutted on fear to suppress its Glow.

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Agents of Empty Death
>
>
>
> As with [[Bestiary/Will-o’-Wisp|will-o'-wisps]] and their less powerful kin, [[Bestiary/Flickerwisp|flickerwisp]], voidgluttons are associated with the Outer Goddess [[Setting/Deities/Nhimbaloth]]. Her cultists see voidgluttons as roving incarnations of her hunger and have been known to offer themselves up to voidgluttons as prey in order to achieve religious insights. Most who attempt this get a closer look at death than they intended.
