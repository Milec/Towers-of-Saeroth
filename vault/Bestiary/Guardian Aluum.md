# Guardian Aluum

Aluum are metal and stone constructs created by the Pactmasters to aid in maintaining order in Katapesh. Each aluum can be controlled by a magical aluum charm, including the more powerful aluums that serve the favored agents of the Pactmasters as guardians. One such aluum attends Usilket at all times, unless he passes his [[Equipment/Guardian Aluum Charm|_guardian aluum charm_]] to a PC to allow them the protection the construct affords.
