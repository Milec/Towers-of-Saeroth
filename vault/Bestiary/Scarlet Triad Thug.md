**Bully's Push** ⤾ **Trigger** A creature within the thug's reach misses the thug with a melee Strike. **Effect** The thug attempts to [[Actions/Shove]] the triggering creature at a –2 penalty. If the thug rolls a critical failure, it gets a failure instead.
