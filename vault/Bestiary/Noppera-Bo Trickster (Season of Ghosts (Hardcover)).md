**Impersonate Shape** two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|polymorph]]) The noppera-bo trickster changes into a version of a Small or Medium humanoid they've touched during the last 24 hours. While transformed and their face is covered, the trickster gains a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] that creature. As long as the noppera-bo trickster has a face, they lose their no face ability and their listed immunities. The noppera-bo trickster can change back by using this ability again.

**Remove Face** two actions ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) **Requirements** The noppera-bo trickster is [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonating]] a creature with a face; **Effect** The noppera-bo trickster's facial features melt into nothingness, and they menace a creature within 30 feet. The creature must succeed at a DC 17 Will save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure).
