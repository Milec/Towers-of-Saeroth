# Etioling

When the primal spark of the [[Setting/Planes/First World]] nestled in a [[Ancestries/Gnome|gnome's]] heart is engulfed in _Radiant Fire_, it fights back, pushing against death with riotous life and creating an etioling. Within their small frames, churning positive and negative energy fiercely battle for dominance, occasionally causing convulsions and babbling speech. This intense inner conflict warps an etioling's primal magic, transmogrifying it into an occult hybrid singular to these unfortunate mortics.

 Nearly devoid of color, etiolings have vibrant eyes and pulsing green veins. Their bodies force the corrupting negative energy out through their pores, where it distills on their skin, covering them in a thin layer of ectoplasm and concentrated death. Around them, the world withers.

 Incapable of focusing for long, etiolings are impulsive and moody, rapidly alternating between kindness, malice, and trickery. They forget themselves, their friends, and the things they once loved.

**Consecration Vulnerability** An etioling in a place of worship dedicated to a non-evil deity or on sacred ground, such as an area blessed by [[Spells/Sanctified Ground|_sanctified ground_]], is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]].  
**Ectoplasmic Secretions** ([[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) Any creature that hits the etioling with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack, tries to [[Traits/Grapple]] them, or otherwise touches them becomes partially coated in ectoplasm. The creature takes 1d6 negative damage and must succeed at a DC 27 Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 2]] until the ectoplasm is removed. The ectoplasm can be removed with a total of 3 Interact actions by the creature or creatures adjacent to the creature. These actions don't need to be consecutive or made by the same creature.  
**Ectoplasmic Shield** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) **Trigger** The etioling is the target of a physical ranged attack; **Effect** The etioling interposes a wave of ectoplasm between themself and the source of the ranged attack, giving them a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses, the ectoplasm deflected it. The ectoplasm can't deflect unusually large or heavy ranged projectiles (such as boulders or ballista bolts).  
**Withering Aura** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]]) 20 feet. The etioling drains nutrients from nearby plant and animal life. Each round a creature begins its turn in this aura, it takes 1d6 negative damage and must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]]. All non-magical plant life in this aura instantly withers, removing any cover and concealment provided by trees and undergrowth.

**Death Gasp** single action ([[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]) The etioling draws in a deep breath and holds it, temporarily suspending their biological processes and becoming undead. The etioling gains the [[Traits/Undead|undead]] trait and becomes immune to bleed, [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]]. Any such effects the etioling is currently suffering from are suspended, but take effect again once they take a breath.  
Additionally, the etioling gains the [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] trait, a fly Speed of 25 feet, resistance 10 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], or [[Traits/Positive|positive]]; double this resistance vs. non-[[Traits/Magical|magical]]) while they hold their breath. They can't cast spells during this time.
