# Ether Sprite Swarm

One ether sprite is quite enough for most, and can be tiresome for even the fey. When a group of them get together, it's outright bedlam.

**Rowdy Clamor** ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Aura|aura]]) 20 feet. The sprite swarm creates a din by laughing, taunting, and insulting nearby creatures. Creatures in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]].

---

**Speed** 10 feet, fly 40 feet

**Luminous Pattern** two actions  The sprites glow brightly, flying in a shifting pattern of light. All creatures within 20 feet become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute (DC 16 Will negates).

**Spark Bombardment** single action  The sprites hurl luminous sparks at a creature within 20 feet dealing 5d4 fire damage (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

**Swarm Steal** two actions  The sprites rummage through the possessions of a single creature within its space, attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] check against the creature's Reflex DC. On a success, the sprites take one item of negligible or light bulk that the creature is carrying but not holding or readied. On a critical success, the item can be one that is held or readied, such as a weapon or potion.

**Swarming Needles** single action  The sprites numerous rapiers deal 2d6 piercing damage to each enemy in the swarm's space (DC 20 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

> [!info] Sidebar
> ## Sprite Collections
>
>
>
> Ether sprites have a reputation for being thieves. In truth, they're generally curious about the world and simply have a tendency to walk away with objects that don't belong to them, not understanding the concept of ownership. As a result, ether sprites tend to have small interesting objects in their possession, such as talismans or gemstones.
