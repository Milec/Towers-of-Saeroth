# Second Spawn

The timber body of a second spawn cracks with each movement, as if its very frame protests its abominable mobility. Grown from [[Bestiary/Families/Arboreal (Monster Core)|arboreals]] native to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]], the greatest of the second spawn—in effect, the “First Spawn”—is [[Bestiary/Treerazer (Monster Core)|Treerazer's]] mobile fortress, the Witchbole.
