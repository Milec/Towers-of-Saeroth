# Necrohulk Smasher

Mutants with incredible strength make the most powerful necrohulks. These necrohulk smashers are so swollen with biological and alchemical muscle enhancements that they can simply pound their way through enemies.

**Alchemical Amplification** free action ([[Traits/Alchemical]]) **Frequency** once per hour; **Effect** The necrohulk draws upon alchemical stores to push its body into overdrive. It becomes [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1d4 rounds. It can use this extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike.

**[[Bestiary/Abilities/Rend]]** single action  fist

**Smashing Blow** two actions  The necrohulk smasher makes a fist Strike. If it hits, the necrohulk smashes the target into the floor or nearby wall. The target takes an additional 6d10 bludgeoning damage, depending on its DC 33 Fortitude save.  
**Critical Success** The target takes half of the additional damage.  
**Success** The target takes half the additional damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  
**Failure** The target takes full additional damage, is stunned 3, and knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].  
**Critical Failure** The target takes double the additional damage, is stunned 3, and knocked prone.
