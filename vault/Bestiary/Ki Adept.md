# Ki Adept

The ki adept is in tune with their own body and can manipulate the mystical energy that flows within them. Typically, the ki adept works to hamper as many foes as possible with their Knocking Sweep and follows up with a Flurry of Blows.

**Flurry of Blows** single action ([[Traits/Flourish|flourish]]) The ki adept makes two Strikes with their fist or with a [[Traits/Monk|monk]] weapon. If both hit the same creature, combine their damage for the purpose of resistances and weaknesses.

**Knocking Sweep** two actions ([[Traits/Flourish]]) The ki adept swings their weapon with a sweeping spin. They attempt separate [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] check to [[Actions/Trip]] any number of creatures within their reach. Each attempt counts toward the ki adept's multiple attack penalty, but the multiple attack penalty doesn't increase until after they makes all the attacks.

**Powerful Fists**   The ki adept's fist Strikes are treated as [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]] and [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] and don't take penalties when making lethal attacks.

**Punch Down**   The ki adept deals an extra 2d10 damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] creatures.
