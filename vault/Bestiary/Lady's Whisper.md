**Unwilling Teleportation**  ([[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]]) When Lady's Whisper hits and damages a creature with its claw Strike, it can choose to teleport that creature to any open space within 15 feet, even if Lady's Whisper can't see the destination. The target can attempt a DC 30 Will saving throw to resist this effect.
