**Focus Gaze** single action ([[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Divine]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]) The vincuvicar stares at a creature they can see within 30 feet. The creature must attempt a Will save against the alone and forgotten aura. If the creature was already [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], on a failed save, its hopelessness causes it to be enfeebled 2 and unable to take the [[Actions/Escape]] action as long as they remain enfeebled. After attempting this save, the creature is then temporarily immune until the start of the vincuvicar's next turn.

**Shackle** ⤾  **Trigger** A creature within 60 feet is damaged by the vincuvicar's animated manacles Strike; **Effect** The creature must attempt a DC 40 Reflex save.  
 **Critical Success** The creature avoids being manacled.  
 **Success** The manacles distract the creature, causing it to become [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the end of its next turn.  
 **Failure** The manacles fuse in place and [[Equipment/Manacles|bind the creature's legs and arms]] until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (DC 40) or the manacles are destroyed.  
 **Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]].

> ## Vincuvicar Thrones
>
>
>
> Each vincuvicar possesses a personal, spiked iron throne—a symbol of authority. This iron throne is an object with Hardness 20 and 500 Hit Points. If a vincuvicar's throne is destroyed, the velstrac must return to the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]] to acquire or construct a replacement.
