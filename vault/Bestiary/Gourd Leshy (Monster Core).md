# Gourd Leshy

Gourd leshies are guardians of fields, gardens, and farms. Many villages benefit from the protection of a gourd leshy, even if they are unaware of it.

This ability otherwise uses the effects of [[Spells/One with Plants|_one with plants_]].

**Ensnare**   When the gourd leshy damages a creature with a fist or seed Strike, vines lash out from the leshy (or seed) and wrap around the target's limbs. The target must attempt a DC 17 Reflex save. On a failure, the target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round; on a critical failure, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round and the penalty to Speed lasts for 1 minute.
