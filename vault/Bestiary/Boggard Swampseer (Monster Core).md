**Destructive Croak** two actions ([[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) The swampseer utters a powerful croak that deals 4d6 sonic damage to any non-boggard within a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (DC 19 [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save); any creature with the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] condition takes additional sonic damage equal to twice the value of its frightened condition. The boggard can’t use Destructive Croak again for 1d4 rounds.

**Drowning Drone** ⤾ ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) **Trigger** The boggard swampseer or one of their allies within 60 feet attempts a saving throw against an [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] effect; **Effect** The swampseer releases a croak that drowns out other sounds. They roll a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check. They and boggard allies in the area can use the higher result between the swampseer's Performance check and their saves to resolve the effects against the auditory or sonic effect.

**Swamp Passage**   A boggard ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by swamp terrain features.

**Terrifying Croak** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]) The boggard unleashes a terrifying croak. Any non-boggard within 30 feet becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] unless they succeed at a DC 19 Will save; those who critically succeed are temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Tongue Grab**   If the boggard hits a creature with their tongue, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the boggard. Unlike with a normal Grab, the creature isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], but it can't move beyond the reach of the boggard's tongue. A creature can sever the tongue by hitting AC 15 and dealing at least 4 slashing damage. Though this doesn't deal any damage to the boggard, it prevents them from using their tongue Strike until they regrow their tongue, which takes a week.
