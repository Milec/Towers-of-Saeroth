**Skill Mimicry** The mime receives a +1 circumstance bonus to skill checks to perform actions they have witnessed another creature successfully perform in the last minute, or +2 if they witness a creature critically succeed instead.  
**Versatile Performance** The mime can use [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] instead of [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression (Player Core)|Make an Impression]], instead of [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]], and instead of [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]].

**Damage** 1d4+5 bludgeoning

**Mimic Assault** two actions ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) **Frequency** once per round; **Requirements** A creature damaged the mime with a weapon Strike since their previous turn; **Effect** The mime makes a [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] check against the Perception DC of the creature who damaged them, gesturing as if making an attack with the same weapon. On a success, the mime deals two dice of damage to the creature, using the same type and die size as the required weapon Strike.

**Pantomime** two actions ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]) The mime uses exaggerated movements to emulate one of the following effects, which lasts until the end of their next turn. Any creature who sees this ability can attempt to disbelieve this ability as it is used with a DC 14 Will save. Creatures that disbelieve are temporarily immune to pantomime for 1 minute.  
 _Barrier_: The mime creates an invisible 10-foot-by-10-foot stretch of wall adjacent to them and within their reach. The wall has AC 10, 5 hardness, and 10 HP. If the mime Sustains this effect, they can add an additional wall in the same manner.  
 _Rope_: The mime tugs an invisible rope, trying to knock over or pull at a creature within 15 feet. If the creature fails to disbelieve the pantomime, the mime can choose to either knock the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] or to [[Rules/Forced Movement|move]] it 5 feet towards them.  
 _Wind_: The mime creates a 30-foot line of imaginary wind. Creatures who don't disbelieve the pantomime treat this area as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], and if they enter or begin their turn in the area, they fall prone.
