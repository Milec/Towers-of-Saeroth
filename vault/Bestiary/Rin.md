During a cursed creature's moments of triumph, they become limned with spectral flames that punish their allies. Whenever the cursed creature scores a critical hit, each ally within their reach takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] as the cursed creature's hubris sears them.
