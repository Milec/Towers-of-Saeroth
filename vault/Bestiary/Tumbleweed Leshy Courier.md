# Tumbleweed Leshy Courier

A tumbleweed leshy's ability to move quickly across long distances with ease allows them to act as messengers and go-betweens. [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-129)|Druids]] often entrust these couriers with delivering important messages to their home druid circles or other druids they're acquainted with. Some even induct the couriers into the circle to teach them [[Setting/Languages/Wildsong]] for secure communication and basic magic for self-defense.
