**Absorb Shock** Whenever the stormdrinker would take electricity damage or is targeted with an [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] effect, it gains 10 temporary HP, is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]], and increases the reach of its vine Strikes by 5 feet until the end of its next turn. It can use its extra action to Stride, Strike, or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]].

**Melee**
single action
vine +18 ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]),
**Damage** 2d10+8 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]]

**Lightning Blast** two actions  **Requirements** The stormdrinker is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] due to Absorb Shock; Effect The stormdrinker dismisses its Absorb Shock benefits to expel lightning in a 60-foot line, dealing 8d6 electricity damage (DC 25 basic Reflex save).

**Ring Bell** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]]) **Requirements** The stormdrinker is carrying a bell; **Effect** The stormdrinker rings its bell, signaling Tumbo to bite him once. (Multiple bites don't stack their effects on the stormdrinker.) The stormdrinker can't use this ability when it doesn't have its bell, such as if it is [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarmed]].

**Vine Lash** two actions  The stormdrinker makes a vine Strike against each creature within reach. Its multiple attack penalty increases only after all the attacks.
