# Sedacthy Scout

Scouts, usually young sedacthies, ply the ocean in search of animal servants or tread ashore to hunt. Scouts hunting on the surface press [[Bestiary/Crocodile (Monster Core)|crocodiles]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Snake (Monster Core)|snakes]] into service, while those underwater prefer [[Bestiary/Electric Eel (Monster Core)|electric eels]] and [[Bestiary/Hippocampus (Monster Core)|hippocampi]].
