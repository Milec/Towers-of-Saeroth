# Thrill-Seeker  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 75

You once survived a life-or-death situation and found it surprisingly exhilarating, so now you chase that feeling of invincibility you only get when dancing with death. Your desire for exhilaration has you scaling buildings, leaping off rooftops, jumping chasms, and performing other death-defying stunts.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Engineering Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat.
