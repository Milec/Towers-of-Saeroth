# Sewer Dragon  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Quest -10- The Broken Scales|PFS Quest #10: The Broken Scales]] pg. 16

**Prerequisites**  Kobold  ancestry

You are one of the Sewer Dragons, born in Absalom's sewers, strengthened by a life defending your territory.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Kobold Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Snare Crafting]] skill feat.
