# Rostland Partisan  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

You grew up among the southern houses of Brevoy in old Rostland. You’re accustomed to arguing from a position of underappreciated worth.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Politics Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Impression]] skill feat.
