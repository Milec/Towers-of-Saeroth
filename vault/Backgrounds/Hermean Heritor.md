# Hermean Heritor  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Legacy|Legacy - Age of Ashes]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 73

With the restrictions on Hermean citizenship lifted, you may have fled the city of Promise or have spent some of your childhood in the company of someone who did.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Legal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat or the [[Feats/Assurance]] skill feat for Society.
