# Kyonin Emissary  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You were trained to be an ambassador of the elven land of Kyonin, and you have now been sent out into the wider world to build alliances between Kyonin and the neighboring kingdoms.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Charisma
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Politics Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat.
