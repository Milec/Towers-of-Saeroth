# Driver  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 50, [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 45

You live behind the controls of a vehicle, and can handle anything the road or waves and sky can throw at you. You adventure to test your skills with new and interesting vehicles.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] skill, and the [Driving Lore or Piloting Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Assurance (Player Core)|Assurance]] skill feat with the chosen lore.
