# Horizon Hunters  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 10

The next horizon, the next adventure, the next battle; you want to be part of every step and see everything! You’re ready to take off!

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] skill, and the [Hunting Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Survey Wildlife (Player Core)|Survey Wildlife]] skill feat.
