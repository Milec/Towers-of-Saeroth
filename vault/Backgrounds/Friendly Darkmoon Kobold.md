# Friendly Darkmoon Kobold  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Crown of the Kobold King]] pg. 9

**Prerequisites**  Kobold  ancestry

It has taken time, but over the years, you've managed to find a place among the citizens of Falcon's Hollow. While many still distrust [[Rules/Sidebars/Kobold Origins|kobolds]], they at least see you as a fellow villager, if not a friend, and you've got quite the talent for quickly convincing larger groups of people that you mean them no harm.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Wisdom
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Kobold Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Impression]] skill feat.
