# Emissary  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 61

As a diplomat or messenger, you traveled to lands far and wide. Communicating with new people and forming alliances were your stock and trade.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and a [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill related to one city you've visited often. You gain the [[Feats/Multilingual]] skill feat.
