# Touched by Dahak  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Legacy|Legacy - Age of Ashes]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 73

As the manifestation of Dahak was destroyed, a shard of the dragon god’s wrathful nature infused you, resulting in a violent temper, strange apocalyptic dreams, an obsession with dragon hunting, or some other touch of his influence.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Dragon Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Titan Wrestler]] skill feat.
