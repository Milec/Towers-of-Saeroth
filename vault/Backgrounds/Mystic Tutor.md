# Mystic Tutor  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Pervasive Magic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 219

You spent years predicting, identifying, and harnessing the innate magical talents of those around you. A magic-rich world has taught you that you can never have too much training when everyone around you is capable of magic.

 Choose two ability boosts. One must be **Intelligence** or **Wisdom** and one is a free ability boost.

 Your innate magical trait is [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]] or [[Traits/Occult|occult]]. You're trained in either [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], depending on your innate magical trait, and the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Academia Lore]] skill. You gain the [[Feats/Recognize Spell]] skill feat.
