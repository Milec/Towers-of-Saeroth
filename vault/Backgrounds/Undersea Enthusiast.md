# Undersea Enthusiast  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 70

**Region** High Seas

You love diving and exploring the world beneath the waves, and long periods of swimming have trained you to move easily through the water. You're also fascinated by undersea creatures and cultures—and may even have a trace of one of them in your own lineage.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Ocean Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Underwater Marauder]] skill feat.
