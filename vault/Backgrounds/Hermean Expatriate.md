# Hermean Expatriate  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 70

**Region** High Seas

You have been exiled from Hermea, perhaps of your own accord or perhaps because you didn't measure up. However, you take with you some of the benefits of the excellent education afforded to its citizenry.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Dragon Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Skill Training]] skill feat.
