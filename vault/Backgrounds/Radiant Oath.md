# Radiant Oath  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 10

You swore to fight evil and protect what kindness remains in Golarion. The Radiant Oath supports you and stands by your side.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Spirit Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/No Cause for Alarm (Player Core)|No Cause for Alarm]] skill feat.
