# Shoanti Name-Bearer  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 118

**Region** Saga Lands

You are a member of a Shoanti quah and have gone through its coming of age ceremony, gaining the traditional tattoo of your quah and earning your adult name.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Quah Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat.
