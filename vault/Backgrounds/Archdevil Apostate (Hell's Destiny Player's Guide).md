# Archdevil Apostate  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hell's Destiny Player's Guide]] pg. 8, [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 7

You once worshipped a powerful [[Setting/Deity Categories/Archdevils|archdevil]] but have turned away from fiendish practices. Whether you walked away due to moral compunctions or cold pragmatism, you know your soul was once bound for [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]] and you can only pray that your new path erases the sins you’ve committed.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Charisma
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] skill, and the [Devil Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Student of the Canon (Player Core)|Student of the Canon]] skill feat.
