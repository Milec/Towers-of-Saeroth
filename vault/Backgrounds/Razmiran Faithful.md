# Razmiran Faithful  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

You serve a living god who rules upon the face of Golarion, and this gives your actions a divine mandate not to be trifled with.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Razmir Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Coercion]] skill feat.
