# Hunted by the Night  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shades of Blood Player's Guide]] pg. 10

You're being hunted—by a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]] no less! What you did to offend this nocturnal predator hardly matters now. All you know is that when your bandolier of garlic bulbs, holy water, and religious icons failed to deter your pursuer, you had no other choice but to put as much distance between you and them as possible. You set sail for the Andoren colony of Talmandor's Bounty, where you hope a vast ocean and an island known for its sun-filled days will keep you safe.

 Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

 You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] skill and the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Vampire Lore]] skill. You gain the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge (Player Core)|Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat.

 In addition, you begin play ready to protect yourself from your supernatural stalker. You have a [[Equipment/Religious Symbol (Player Core)|religious symbol]], a wooden stake and mallet, and a vial of [[Equipment/Holy Water (GM Core)|_holy water_]].
