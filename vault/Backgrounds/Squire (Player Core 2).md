# Squire  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 51

You trained at the feet of a knight, maintaining their gear and supporting them at tourneys and in battle. Now you search for a challenge that will prove you worthy of full knighthood, or you've spurned pomp and ceremony to test yourself in honest, albeit less formal, combat.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Heraldry Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or Warfare Lore skill. You gain the [[Feats/Armor Assist]] skill feat.
