# Artisan  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 60

As an apprentice, you practiced a particular form of building or crafting, developing specialized skill. You might have been a blacksmith’s apprentice toiling over the forge for countless hours, a young tailor sewing garments of all kinds, or a shipwright shaping the hulls of ships.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Guild Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat.
