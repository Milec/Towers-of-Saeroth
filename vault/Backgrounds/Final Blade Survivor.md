# Final Blade Survivor  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You fell prey to the whims of the Galtan mob and were scheduled for execution by _final blade_, but through skill—or sheer luck—you managed to talk your way out of it.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Charisma
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] skill, and the [Revolution Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar]] skill feat.
