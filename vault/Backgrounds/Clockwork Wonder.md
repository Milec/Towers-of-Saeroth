# Clockwork Wonder  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 9

You have a knack for clockwork magic and can see how it all links together. You were recommended to the Society by a mutual friend due to your expertise.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] skill, and the [Clockwork Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Improvise Tool (Player Core 2)|Improvise Tool]] skill feat.
