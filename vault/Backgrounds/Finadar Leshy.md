# Finadar Leshy  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Scenario -1-15- The Blooming Catastrophe|PFS Scenario #1-15: The Blooming Catastrophe]] pg. 35

**Prerequisites**  Leshy  ancestry

You are a leshy from Finadar Forest, originally created under the corruption of a cyclopean monolith. Though the Pathfinder Society managed to sever the monolith’s connection, freeing the forest and your people from its influence, you retain a trace, unnerving connection to the Abyss.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Abyssal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] skill feat.
