# Concordance Scout  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 44

You were hired by the Concordance of Elements to seek out rumors of planar breaches or other evidence of planar imbalances. After reporting back to the Concordance, they'd send agents more well equipped to deal with the situation. Still, this job has led you into more trouble than your employers suspect, and you've learned how to get your information quickly and get out relatively unscathed.

 Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Constitution** or **Charisma**, and one is a free attribute boost.

 You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill and one of the following lore skills: [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Air Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Earth Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Fire Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Metal Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Water Lore]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Wood Lore]]. You gain the [[Feats/Hobnobber]] skill feat.
