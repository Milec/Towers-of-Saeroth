# Ruby Phoenix Enthusiast  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Organized Play Foundation]]

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) You can select this background only if you have earned credit for at least 5 First Edition scenarios from Season 3 of the Pathfinder Society organized play campaign._

Named for the legendary sorcerer Hao Jin, the Ruby Phoenix Tournament occurs once every 10 years in Goka and attracts extraordinary talent from across the world. You might have begun training for the tournament but never participated, or perhaps you entered the tournament only to be defeated by (and inspired to join) the Pathfinder Society. Either way, your dedicated training prepares you for the rigors of the adventuring lifestyle.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Gladiatorial Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Combat Climber]] skill feat. In addition, you gain access to one of the following uncommon monk weapons: [[Equipment/Weapons/Kama|kama]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Nunchaku|nunchaku]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sai|sai]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Shuriken|shuriken]], or [[Equipment/Weapons/Temple Sword|temple sword]].
