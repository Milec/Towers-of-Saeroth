# Martial Disciple  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 63

You dedicated yourself to intense training and rigorous study to become a great warrior. The school you attended might have been a traditionalist monastery, an elite military academy, or the local branch of a prestigious mercenary organization.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in your choice of either the [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]]
 or the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, as well as the [Warfare Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain a skill feat: [[Feats/Cat Fall]] if you chose [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Feats/Quick Jump]] if you chose [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]].
