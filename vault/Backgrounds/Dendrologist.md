# Dendrologist  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 44

Trees grow in every biome, and your awe at their ability to conquer terrain has inspired deeper study into their workings. There are trees everywhere, and your study of them will inevitably take you ever further afield in search of new varieties, perhaps even to the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Wood]] itself.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Plane of Wood Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Expertise]] skill feat  for forests.
