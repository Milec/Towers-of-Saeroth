# Droskari Disciple  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -148- Fires of the Haunted City|Pathfinder #148: Fires of the Haunted City]] pg. 65

You grew up in the church of the [[Setting/Deities/Droskar|Dark Smith]], where you learned the value of hard work and achieving vocational mastery.

 Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Constitution** or **Intelligence**, and one is a free ability boost.

 You gain the [[Feats/Skill Training]] skill feat, and you're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Droskar Lore]] skill.
