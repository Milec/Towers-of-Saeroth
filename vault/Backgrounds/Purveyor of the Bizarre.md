# Purveyor of the Bizarre  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 58

**Region** Golden Road

Whether in Katapesh or one of the bustling markets along the Path of Salt, you’ve spent enough time buying and selling ancient or unusual items to give you an instinctive ability to quickly sort valuable trinkets from worthless baubles.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] skill, and the [Mercantile Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Identification]] skill feat.
