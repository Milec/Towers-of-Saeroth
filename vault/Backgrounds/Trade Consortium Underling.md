# Trade Consortium Underling  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 22

**Region** Absalom and Starstone Isle

Your experience as a ledger-keeper for one of Absalom’s trade guilds has made you a canny investor and shrewd entrepreneur.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Business Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Experienced Professional]] skill feat.
