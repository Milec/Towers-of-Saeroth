# Rivethun Adherent  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You have spent time learning the practices and traditions of the ancient Rivethun dwarven shamans and can recognize all sorts of magic. You may have chosen your own road since then, or you may remain an adherent of the philosophy.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] skill, and the [Spirit Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Recognize Spell]] skill feat.
