# Fey Friend  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Wardens of Wildwood Players Guide]] pg. 7

Many grow up hearing cautionary tales of mischievous [[Setting/Languages/Fey|fey]], and with good reason: from [[Bestiary/Families/Sprite (Monster Core)|sprites]]' pranks to [[Bestiary/Families/Gremlin (Monster Core)|gremlins]]' sabotage to [[Bestiary/Families/Redcap|redcaps]]' outright lethality, fey pose a real danger to the unwary. But not for you—usually! Whether you grew up near an enchanted wilderness, have visited the [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]], frequently deal with nature spirits, or even were a fey foundling raised by otherworldly beings, you're well acquainted with fey, their myriad traditions, and their even more numerous tricks. Fey and the Wildwood Lodge aren't the same thing, yet there's enough overlap that you're sure to fit in at the upcoming Greenwood Gala.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] skill, and the [Fey Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar (Player Core)|Charming Liar]] skill feat.
