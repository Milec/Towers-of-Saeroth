# Battle's Spark  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 9

You saw the Pathfinder Society helping during the Hellfire Crisis and were filled with hope and purpose. You signed up on the spot and are now a full-fledged agent.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] skill, and the [Warfare Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Experienced Tracker (Player Core)|Experienced Tracker]] skill feat.
