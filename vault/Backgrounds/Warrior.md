# Warrior  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 64

In your younger days, you waded into battle as a mercenary, a warrior defending a nomadic people, or a member of a militia or army. You might have wanted to break out from the regimented structure of these forces, or you could have always been as independent a warrior as you are now.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Warfare Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] skill feat.
