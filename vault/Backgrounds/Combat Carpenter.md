# Combat Carpenter  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 16

You have spent countless hours selecting lumber, drafting designs, and constructing all manner of wooden buildings, shields, weapons, and tools. You can readily identify the weak  spots in wooden fortifications and determine the best ways to bolster or attack them.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] skill, and the [Engineering Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting (Player Core)|Specialty Crafting]] skill feat.
