# Toymaker  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 45

You delight in making little trinkets for children of all ages, and seeing their faces light up thanks to one of your creations brings joy to your heart. Perhaps you sell your wares from a particular shop or from the back of a wagon as you travel from town to town.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Mercantile Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat, choosing artistry, blacksmithing, glassmaking, leatherworking, tailoring, or woodworking as your specialty.
