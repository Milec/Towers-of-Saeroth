# Weaver  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 11

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The weaver background grants the Specialty Crafting (Weaving) feat._

You’re a skilled weaver, crafting your pieces either by hand or by loom. Whether you revere yourself as an artist or not, none can deny the beauty of your craft. However, the life of a weaver isn’t the most exciting one, and your fingers itch for an adventure.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] skill, and the [Basket Weaving Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or Tapestry Lore or Textile Lore. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting (Player Core)|Specialty Crafting]] skill feat.
