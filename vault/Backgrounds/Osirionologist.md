# Osirionologist  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 58

**Region** Golden Road

Whether you’re a fascinated outsider or a local proud of your nation’s storied past, you’re a devoted student of Osirion’s history. You might be a traveling professor, a member of a society like the Pathfinders or the Esoteric Order of the Palatine Eye, or even a simple tomb robber cashing in on the glories of the past.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] skill, and the [Ancient Osirion Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Oddity Identification]] skill feat.
