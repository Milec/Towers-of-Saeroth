# Concordance Researcher  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 44

Tasked by the Concordance of Elements to research a variety of enigmatic planar phenomena, you've acquired very specific knowledge about the planes and the interactions between them. While being a researcher is usually a peaceful profession, the Concordance might require you to go out into the field and research unstable planar situations.

 Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Intelligence** or **Wisdom**, and one is a free attribute boost.

 You're trained in four of the following: [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Air Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Earth Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Fire Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Metal Lore]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Water Lore]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Plane of Wood Lore]].
