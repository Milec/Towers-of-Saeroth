# Mantis Scion  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 70

**Region** High Seas

At least one of your parents is a member of the notorious Red Mantis assassins, merciless killers for hire who rarely fail to claim their marks. Whether on purpose or by simple exposure, you were trained from a young age in the art of stalking and killing people.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] skill, and the [Assassin Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Assurance]] skill feat with [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth.]]
