# Goblinblood Survivor  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 7

You lost your family to the chaos of the Goblinblood Wars. Maybe you soothed your grief by creating new bonds, or maybe you never put out the flames of righteous anger in your heart. Now whatever semblance of peace you’ve found is threatened yet again—but this time, you have the skills to ensure that you won’t be the one left to mourn.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] skill, and the [Goblin Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Forager (Player Core)|Forager]] skill feat.
