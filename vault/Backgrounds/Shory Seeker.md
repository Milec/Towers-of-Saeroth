# Shory Seeker  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 94

**Region** Mwangi Expanse

You’ve dedicated your life to unraveling the secrets of the ancient Shory Empire, either through meticulous research or by traveling into dangerous and distant ruins to track down long- lost artifacts.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Shory Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Specialty Crafting]] skill feat.
