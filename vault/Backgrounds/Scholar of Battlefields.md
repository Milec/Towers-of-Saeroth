# Scholar of Battlefields  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hell's Destiny Player's Guide]] pg. 9

Though you probably don’t revel in the violence of war, you are fascinated by battlefield tactics and regional conflicts’ effects on history. Perhaps you traveled to Cheliax to study its strategies, document its logistical innovations, and chronicle events. You might instead be an enterprising mercenary looking to sell your services in the conflict. No matter your original intentions, you’ve become mired in Corentyn’s martial law, and the more that Cheliax harms its own citizens to maintain wartime compliance, the more eager you’ve become to aid its enemies.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill, and the [Warfare Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the Relentless Assault action.

**Relentless Assault** free action ([[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** A creature would succeed or critically succeed at a saving throw against the offensive ability of a troop you command; **Effect** With an encouraging command, you direct your troop to make a few additional attacks. The triggering creature rerolls its saving throw and uses the worse result
