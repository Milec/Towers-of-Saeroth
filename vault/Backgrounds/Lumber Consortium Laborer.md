# Lumber Consortium Laborer  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You have suffered as a worker for the unscrupulous Lumber Consortium, laboring under harsh conditions in dangerous wooded regions of Andoran.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] skill, and the [Forest Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Assurance]] skill feat with [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]].
