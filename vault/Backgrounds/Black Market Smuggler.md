# Black Market Smuggler  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 58

**Region** Golden Road

You know how to work the less-than-legal side of the region’s markets and know how to slip contraband past the authorities.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Wisdom
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] skill, and the [Underworld Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Experienced Smuggler]] skill feat.
