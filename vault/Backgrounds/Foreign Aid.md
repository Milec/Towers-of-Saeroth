# Foreign Aid  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Hellbreakers Players Guide]] pg. 7

You hail from another nation, fighting a battle that isn’t your own. You might be an agent from Andoran with sympathies for the locals or a hired hand from the Druma Mercenary League. Regardless of how or why, you’re here now, and your duty is to do what you can to help those you find yourself in league with.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Charisma
or Strength, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] skill, and a [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill a Lore skill pertaining to your place of origin. You gain the [[Feats/No Cause for Alarm (Player Core)|No Cause for Alarm]] skill feat.
