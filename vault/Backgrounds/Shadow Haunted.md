# Shadow Haunted  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 106

**Region** Old Cheliax

You are from Nidal, and regardless of your personal values, Zon-Kuthon has a claim on your soul due to an ancient pact. The Midnight Lord’s unsettling influence is bred deep into your bones.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Shadow Plane Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Coercion]] skill feat.
