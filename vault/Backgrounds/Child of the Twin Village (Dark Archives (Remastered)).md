# Child of the Twin Village  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 73

You were born in a village where everyone has a twin with a special link. You have moved to the wider world for reasons of your own, but you still have an unbreakable connection to your twin through your dreams.

 Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Constitution** or **Charisma**, and one is a free attribute boost.

 You're trained in [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories (Player Core)|Dreamlands Lore]]. You gain the ability to communicate with your twin in your dreams. You and your twin gain the effects of [[Spells/Dream Message (Player Core)|_dream message_]] every night, only between the two of you; this isn't a spell, but a natural effect. Unless your twin is another PC, your GM roleplays your twin or summarizes your interaction during these dreams.
