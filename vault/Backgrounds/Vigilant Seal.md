# Vigilant Seal  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 10

While other agents stand by explore, report, cooperate, you stand by protect, contain, destroy. You know the sorts of things that are kept in the Society’s vaults and you know what horrible things could happen if they got out.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] skill, and the [Curse Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Root Magic (Player Core 2)|Root Magic]] skill feat.
