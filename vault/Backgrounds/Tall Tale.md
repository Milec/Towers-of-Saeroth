# Tall Tale  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears]] pg. 125

You’ve ridden a tornado, lassoed a river, mooned a dragon under full moon, and shot six zombies with one bullet. That’s what the stories say, anyway, and even if people don’t strictly believe them, they are curious about you. Certainly, the stories keep spreading, and it would appear that you either have a unique destiny or are trying to convince the world that you do.

Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Constitution** or **Charisma**, and one is a free ability boost.

You’re trained in [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]]. You gain the [[Feats/Impressive Performance (Player Core)|Impressive Performance]] skill feat. You can gain the Connections skill feat later without being expert in [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] or having [[Feats/Courtly Graces (Player Core)|Courtly Graces]]—this represents less traditional social connections and more the way that your legend has spread, and you’ve learned to leverage your legend, with people wanting to meet you and see you for themselves.
