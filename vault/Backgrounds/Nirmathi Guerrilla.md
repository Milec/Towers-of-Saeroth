# Nirmathi Guerrilla  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 46

**Region** Eye of Dread

Woodcraft comes naturally to you, and you have learned how to use the forest to your tactical advantage against superior forces in skirmishes against the Molthuni army or the Ironfang Legion.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] skill, and the [Forest Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Terrain Stalker]] (underbrush) skill feat.
