# Feybound  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 52

You have spent time in the [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]] or another realm of the [[Setting/Languages/Fey|fey]] and aren't entirely the same person you were before. Perhaps you made a purchase at the legendary Witchmarket or partook deeply of fey food and wine. Whatever the case, willingly or inadvertently, you made a bargain with the fey, the benefits of which come at a price.

 Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Dexterity** or **Charisma**, and one is a free attribute boost.

 You are trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Fey Lore]] and gain the Fey's Fortune free action. You gain an anathema as part of your pact with the fey. If you violate this anathema, you lose Fey's Fortune until you receive the effects of a successful [[Rituals/Atone (Player Core)|_atone_]] ritual using the [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]] skill. The exact anathema is up to you and the GM, but the most common requirement is that you must fulfill a single request from any fey who knows your name.

 **Fey's Fortune** free action ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]]) **Frequency** once per day; **Trigger** You attempt a skill check and haven't yet rolled; **Effect** Roll the skill check twice and use the better result.
