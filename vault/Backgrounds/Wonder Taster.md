# Wonder Taster  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

Having once sampled Numerian fluids, you've tasted knowledge beyond comprehension. You are driven to recapture that astounding experience.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Alchemical Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Alchemical Crafting]] skill feat.
