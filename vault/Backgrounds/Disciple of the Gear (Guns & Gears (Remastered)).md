# Disciple of the Gear  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Guns & Gears (Remastered)]] pg. 45

While many regard science and faith as incompatible, you believe scientific advancement is your deity's will. Perhaps you're a follower of [[Setting/Deities/Brigh]], [[Setting/Deities/Casandalee]], or some other god of technology, or perhaps you see the furnaces and sparks of modernity as the latest manifestation of [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae|Sarenrae's]] flame or [[Setting/Deities/Gozreh|Gozreh's]] lightning—whatever the case, you're always willing to spread the word of your deity, and you've learned some basic tinkering to show how your deity's focus can increase the common standard of living.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and a [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill for the deity you worship.  You gain the [[Feats/Quick Repair]] skill feat.
