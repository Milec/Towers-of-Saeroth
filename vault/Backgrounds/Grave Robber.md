# Grave Robber  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 16

Resurrectionist. Body snatcher. No matter what you're called, you know where the bodies are buried, and how many coins they'll fetch when dug up. Adventuring comes naturally to you—in your mind dungeons are just oversized tombs—as does returning home with your loot undetected.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] skill, and the [Underworld Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Experienced Smuggler]] skill feat.
