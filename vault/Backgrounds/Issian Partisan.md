# Issian Partisan  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

You grew up among the northern houses of Brevoy in old Issia. Steeped in the cultural legacy of pirates and smugglers, you rely on your cleverness and charm as you make your way throughout the world.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] skill, and the [Underworld Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar]] skill feat.
