# Mammoth Speaker  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 118

**Region** Saga Lands

You have learned the secrets of taming the mighty mammoths and other megafauna of the far north. Perhaps these talents are a part of your people’s traditional customs, or perhaps you sought out these massive animals of your own accord.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] skill, and the [Animal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Train Animal]] skill feat.
