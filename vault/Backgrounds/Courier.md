# Courier  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 48

In your youth, you earned coin running messages for persons of wealth and influence, darting through crowded cobblestone streets. Your dogged commitment to deliver your message was good training for the life of an adventurer.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and a [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill for the city in which you were raised. You gain the [[Feats/Glean Contents]] skill feat.
