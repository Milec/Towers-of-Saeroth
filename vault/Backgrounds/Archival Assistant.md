# Archival Assistant  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 9

You’re well known for your experience working in elaborate archives and tackling intensive research. Zarta Dralneen pulled a few strings and called in a few favors to have you transferred to the Grand Lodge to assist with research.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] skill, and the [Library Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Assured Identification (Player Core 2)|Assured Identification]] skill feat.
