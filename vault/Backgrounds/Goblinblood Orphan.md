# Goblinblood Orphan  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 106

**Region** Old Cheliax

Your family, whether goblin, hobgoblin, or human, died in the Goblinblood Wars. Though you were marked by these losses, you managed to survive through your own resilience and resourcefulness.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Constitution, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Goblin Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Assurance]] skill feat with Survival.
