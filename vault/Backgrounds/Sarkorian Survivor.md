# Sarkorian Survivor  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

The devastation and carnage of the Worldwound were nearly complete, but you somehow managed to survive it.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Strength, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Sarkorian History Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Forager]] skill feat.
