# Desert Tracker  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 58

**Region** Golden Road

You’re at home blazing trails in the burning sands, and you made a living guiding or following creatures in the desert. You might be a native nomad, an experienced desert guide, a naturalist, a bandit driven into the dunes by the law—or all of the above.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Desert Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Experienced Tracker]] skill feat.
