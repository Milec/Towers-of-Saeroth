# Sarkorian Reclaimer  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 34

**Region** Broken Lands

Whether you trace your heritage to lost Sarkoris or are a crusader trying to atone for past fanatics’ wrongs, you seek to liberate the Sarkorian homeland from the demons who have defiled it.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] skill, and the [Abyssal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Battle Medicine]] skill feat.
