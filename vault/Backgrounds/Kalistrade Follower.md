# Kalistrade Follower  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You follow the philosophy of the Prophecies of Kalistrade, seeking to build up your wealth in this life so that you might meet the next world on your own terms.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Kalistrade Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Bargain Hunter]] skill feat.
