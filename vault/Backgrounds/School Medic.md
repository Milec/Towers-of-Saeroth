# School Medic  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 23

Whether practicing as part of your course of study or serving on staff, you’ve been asked to help by a school concerned that Sarkoris’s lessons might be too harsh for the visiting students. Although the city of Nerosyan is well- protected from the remaining demons of the Sarkoris Scar, the multitude of students seeking to help in that dangerous land ensure that you have plenty of chances to practice your medical arts.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] skill, and the [Academia Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or a Lore skill [[Rules/Category Pages/Backgrounds|associated with your school]] You gain the [[Feats/Battle Medicine (Player Core)|Battle Medicine]] skill feat.
