# Aiudara Seeker  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Legacy|Legacy - Age of Ashes]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 73

The _aiudara_ of Alseta’s Ring have become more well known, and you are interested in learning more about them.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] skill, and the [Portal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Identification]] skill feat.
