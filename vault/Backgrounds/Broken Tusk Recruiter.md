# Broken Tusk Recruiter  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Quest for the Frozen Flame Player's Guide]] pg. 8

Your gifted tongue, friendly demeanor, and deep knowledge of Broken Tusk culture and traditions make you an ideal recruiter for new followers. Whenever Broken Tusks come upon weak, weary, or wandering souls, you're often the first sent to meet these folks and determine if they would be a good fit for the following.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Hillcross Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Impression]] skill feat.
