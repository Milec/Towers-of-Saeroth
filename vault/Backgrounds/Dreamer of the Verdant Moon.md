# Dreamer of the Verdant Moon  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -153- Life's Long Shadows|Pathfinder #153: Life's Long Shadows]] pg. 67

You spent your early days in the aftermath of one of the great ravenings. You survived thanks to your resourcefulness and perhaps your [[Setting/Deities/Zevgavizeb|patron’s]] favor and were blessed with skills and drive thereafter, but you must always hold to his standards, lest the hunger from the depths seek you out once again.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Zevgavizeb Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Ravening's Desperation]] skill feat.
