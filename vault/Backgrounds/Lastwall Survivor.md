# Lastwall Survivor  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 46

**Region** Eye of Dread

You managed to escape the devastation that the Whispering Tyrant brought to your nation, but you lost everything to the lich-king’s minions, including your home and many friends and family.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] skill, and the [Undead Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Battle Medicine]] skill feat.
