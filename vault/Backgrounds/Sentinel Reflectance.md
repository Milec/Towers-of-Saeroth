# Sentinel Reflectance  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 73

Either through witnessing their deeds and devout heroics firsthand, or through reading chronicles of their exploits, the Shining Sentinels of the Knights of Lastwall inspire you. You may idealize their philosophy or one day hope to join their ranks; the Shining Sentinels are your guiding star.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Shining Sentinels Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/No Cause for Alarm]] skill feat.
