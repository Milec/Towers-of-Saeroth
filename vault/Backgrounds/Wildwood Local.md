# Wildwood Local  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 130

**Region** Shining Kingdoms

You might have been born and raised among the druids of the Verduran Forest, or you may have spent time among them as an adult and come to know their ways.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] skill, and the [Forest Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Natural Medicine]] skill feat.
