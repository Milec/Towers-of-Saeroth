# Aspiring Free Captain  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 70

**Region** High Seas

You seek to join the Free Captains of the Shackles and have learned everything you need to know about sailing and bossing people around. Now you just need a crew and a ship.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Wisdom
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Sailing Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Coercion]] skill feat.
