# Thassilonian Traveler  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 118

**Region** Saga Lands

You come from ancient Thassilon, one of the citizens that appeared out of time alongside the city of Xin-Edasseril. You know many things that have been long forgotten... along with many things that are no longer correct.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] skill, and the [Thassilon Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge]] skill feat.
