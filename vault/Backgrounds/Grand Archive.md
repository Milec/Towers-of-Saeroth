# Grand Archive  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder Society Clockwork Mystery Player's Guide]] pg. 9

The enormous libraries of the Pathfinder Society are your home, and you can’t imagine studying anywhere else.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] skill, and the [Academia Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Multilingual (Player Core)|Multilingual]] skill feat.
