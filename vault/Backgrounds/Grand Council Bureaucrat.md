# Grand Council Bureaucrat  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 22

**Region** Absalom and Starstone Isle

You spent years working as a clerk to a functionary in Absalom’s government. Your service taught you a thing or two about rousing speeches and manipulating the city’s bureaucracy.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Charisma
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Government Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Impression]] skill feat.
