# Senghor Sailor  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 94

**Region** Mwangi Expanse

As an experienced sailor from Senghor, you know that the only thing saving you from disaster on the high seas is a properly maintained ship. You know boat-building inside and out and you can quickly cobble together a solution in the event that something breaks.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]] skill, and the [Sailing Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Repair]] skill feat.
