# Legendary Parents  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Legacy|Legacy - Age of Ashes]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 73

One or more of your parents (either biological or adoptive) were heroes of the Age of Ashes Adventure Path. Others tend to treat you with a bit more respect, or perhaps fear your connections to people of great power.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Genealogy Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Group Coercion]] skill feat.
