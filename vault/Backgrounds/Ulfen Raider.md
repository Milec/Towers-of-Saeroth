# Ulfen Raider  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 118

**Region** Saga Lands

You are an Ulfen warrior, feared among Avistan for the merciless and devastating raids your people once conducted along the shores. Though the days of these Ulfen raids are largely past, you have been trained to strike terror into the hearts of those who face you.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] skill, and the [Sailing Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Intimidating Glare]] skill feat.
