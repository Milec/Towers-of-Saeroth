# Legacy of the Hammer  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 135

Your introduction to the criminal life was spurred forward by a seemingly innocuous hammer that provided you with all kinds of innovative criminal ideas. It has since left your possession, but every now and then you hear a faint whisper urging you toward crime.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Thievery]] skill, and the [Engineering Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Concealing Legerdemain]] skill feat.
