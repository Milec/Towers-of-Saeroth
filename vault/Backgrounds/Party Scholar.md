# Party Scholar  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 23

Perhaps because you already achieved what you wanted at school or your position makes you invulnerable, you’ve begun to pour your efforts into a lifestyle of hedonistic excess. The fact that the Convocation isn’t a months-long party might thus come as a rude shock to you, but your skill at deflecting blame and making fast friends might mitigate that.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] skill, and the [Alcohol Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or a Lore skill [[Rules/Category Pages/Backgrounds|associated with your school]] You gain the [[Feats/Charming Liar (Player Core)|Charming Liar]] skill feat.
