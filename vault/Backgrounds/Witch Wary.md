# Witch Wary  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 118

**Region** Saga Lands

You have little love or trust for spellcraft and those who practice it, and have developed a paranoid knack for recognizing such tricks. You are constantly on guard for the magic of witches and have been trained to spot the signs of those with minds affected by magic.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] skill, and the [Curse Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Oddity Identification]] skill feat.
