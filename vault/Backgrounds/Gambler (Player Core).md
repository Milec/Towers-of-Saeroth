# Gambler  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 86

The thrill of the win drew you into games of chance. This might have been a lucrative sideline that paled in comparison to the real risks of adventuring, or you might have fallen on hard times due to your gambling and pursued adventuring as a way out of a spiral.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] skill, and the [Games Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Lie to Me]] skill feat.
