# Chelish Rebel  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 106

**Region** Old Cheliax

You joined the fight against House Thrune. You may have helped liberate the nation of Ravounel, or you might be involved in another rebellion, such as Pezzack’s, that studied Ravounel’s successes.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Kintargo Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Streetwise]] skill feat.
