# Scholar of the Ancients  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 70

**Region** High Seas

You're fascinated by the lost Azlanti Empire and have dedicated yourself to seeking out and studying every broken artifact or scrap of knowledge that remains, whether as an academic pursuit or simply for the joy of treasure hunting.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] skill, and the [Azlant Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Quick Identification]] skill feat.
