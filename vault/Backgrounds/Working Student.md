# Working Student  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 23

Many students look to education as a means of improving their lives. You are in the middle of that process or have spent a lot of time there, juggling lessons and textbooks with the manual labor to pay for them. Although this work might distract from your studies, it also teaches you other lessons, making you more worldly than your classmates.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] skill, and the [Labor Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or a Lore skill [[Rules/Category Pages/Backgrounds|associated with your school]] You gain the [[Feats/Streetwise (Player Core)|Streetwise]] skill feat.
