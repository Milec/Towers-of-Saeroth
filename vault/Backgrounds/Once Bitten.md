# Once Bitten  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 73

After an altercation with an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature, you feared you might be infected. Either through medical intervention, luck, or misunderstanding how undeath spreads, your fears abated. However, your brush with living death taught you the value of life. You do whatever you can to avoid the end you thought you'd been fated.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] skill, and the [Undead Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Battle Medicine]] skill feat.
