# Issian Patriot  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]]

**Source** [[Sources/Kingmaker Adventure Path]] pg. 13

You grew up in northern Brevoy, but the call for heroes willing to help take back your country's rightful holdings in the Stolen Lands has inflamed your dreams of profit and possibilities, and you have joined an expedition journeying south.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Dexterity
or Intelligence, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] skill, and the [Legal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Streetwise]] skill feat.
