# Star Athlete  *Background*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 23

Whatever the sport, be it basilisk, kickball, wrestling, footraces, stickball, canoeing, or foot volleyball, you either have played it or are keen to try it. You probably missed some classes due to practice or competitions, and irate professors might dismiss you as lazy or incapable of learning. These assumptions ignore the countless hours you’ve dedicated to athletic excellence.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Strength
or Dexterity, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill, and the [Games Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill or a Lore skill [[Rules/Category Pages/Backgrounds|associated with your school.]] You gain the [[Feats/Assurance (Player Core)|Assurance]] skill feat with Athletics.
