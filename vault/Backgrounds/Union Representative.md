# Union Representative  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/Highhelm]] pg. 72

You might have fought for fairer ore distribution for the Metalmaker's Union, higher chef's pay in the Provisioners' Union, or better mercenary contracts for the Union of Axe and Coin. Whatever the case, you have experience in the high-stakes world of the negotiating table, experience that translates surprisingly well to adventuring.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Intelligence
or Charisma, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill, and the [Legal Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Contract Negotiator]] skill feat.
