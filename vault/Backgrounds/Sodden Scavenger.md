# Sodden Scavenger  *Background*

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 94

**Region** Mwangi Expanse

You’ve managed to eke out an existence in the storm-wracked Sodden Lands and have become an expert at scavenging food and tools.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to Constitution
or Wisdom, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] skill, and the [Swamp Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/skills/lore) skill. You gain the [[Feats/Forager]] skill feat.
