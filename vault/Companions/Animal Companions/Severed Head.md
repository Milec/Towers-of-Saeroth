# Severed Head  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 123

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Galt have access to this option._

Your companion is an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] severed head. The Red Revolution left countless mass graves across Galt, filled with the beheaded corpses of those executed by the [[Equipment/Final Blade|final blades]]. Most of the victims’ heads were unceremoniously dumped alongside their bodies, but many were snatched by unscrupulous necromancers who found them to be a convenient (and unfortunately plentiful) raw material for the creation of simple undead minions.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +1

**Con** +3

**Int** -5

**Wis** +0

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** none (mindless)

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]]

**Speed** 15 feet, fly 25 feet

**Special** A severed head has the [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait, granting it [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]] and immunity to [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]. It’s also [[Rules/Undead Companions|mindless]].

**Support Benefit** The head latches on to your enemy with its jaws when you create an opening, distracting them and hampering their movement. The next creature within range of your severed head that you successfully [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] before the start of your next turn takes a –5-foot status penalty to its Speeds and must succeed at a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]] to use reactions triggered by your actions until the start of your next turn. On a failure, the reaction is lost.

**Advanced Maneuver** Gnash
