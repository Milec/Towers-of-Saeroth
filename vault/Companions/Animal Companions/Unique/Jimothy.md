**Base Animal Companion** [[Companions/Animal Companions/Jimothy]]

Jimothy doesn’t grow larger in size when he advances, though he gains all the other benefits. Jimothy can use his hands to perform simple [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions, such as picking up an unattended object, but he can’t wield weapons.
