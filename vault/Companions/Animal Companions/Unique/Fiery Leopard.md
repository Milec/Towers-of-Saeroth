# Fiery Leopard  *Unique Animal Companion*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Fall of Plaguestone

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Fall of Plaguestone]] pg. 57

**Base Animal Companion** [[Companions/Animal Companions/Cat]]

**Immunities** fire; **Weaknesses** cold 5  
**Flaming Attacks** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) The fiery leopard's natural attacks deal 1 fire damage (in addition to the bonus damage dealt to flat-footed targets). This damage increases to 1d4 when the fiery leopard becomes a nimble or savage animal companion.  
**Fiery Pelt** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) Creatures that start their turn grappled by the fiery leopard take 1d4 fire damage. Anyone attempting to grapple the fiery leopard takes 1d4 fire damage for each attempt.  
**Burning Glow** The fiery leopard sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius which gives it a –2 circumstance penalty on [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks made at night or in a dark environment.
