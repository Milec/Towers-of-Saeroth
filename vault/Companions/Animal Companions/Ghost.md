# Ghost  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|Incorporeal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 38

Your companion is a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost]], bound to an item in your possession. Due to this bond, your ghost companion can move beyond the area of a single dwelling, but it's easier to disrupt than most incorporeal creatures. A ghost companion can be formed from a [[Traits/Humanoid|humanoid]], [[Traits/Animal|animal]], or other creature. The ghost isn't sapient and is unable to speak with the living.

**Size** Small or Medium

**Melee**
single action
ghostly touch ([[Traits/Finesse]], [[Traits/Magical]]),
**Damage** 1d8 negative (doesn't apply the ghost's Strength modifier)

**Str** -5

**Dex** +3

**Con** +0

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +2

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]

**Speed** fly 20 feet

**Special** [[Rules/Undead Allies|anchored incorporeality]]

**Support Benefit** Your ghost spooks and scares your foes as you attack them. Until the start of your next turn, if you hit and deal damage to a creature in your ghost's reach, the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].

**Advanced Maneuver** Telekinetic Assault
