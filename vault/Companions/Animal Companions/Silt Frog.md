# Silt Frog  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal]], [[Traits/Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

Your companion is a silt frog, an oversized frog with a natural ability to detect magic, its main food source.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws,
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Melee**
single action
tongue ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]),
**Damage** 1d4 bludgeoning

**Str** +3

**Dex** +2

**Con** +2

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], magic sense ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]]) 30 feet (the silt frog can sense the presence of magical auras within 30 feet as an imprecise sense)

**Speed** 25 feet

**Support Benefit** Your silt frog unleashes an eardrum-rattling croak when you create an opening. Until the start of your next turn, if you hit and deal damage to a creature within your silt frog’s reach, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] until the start of your next turn.

**Advanced Maneuver** Ingest Magic
