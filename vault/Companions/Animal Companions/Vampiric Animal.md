# Vampiric Animal  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 39

Your companion is a wolf, weasel, fox, or other predator with fangs and the blood thirst of a vampire. They often serve vampires who willingly shared their blood to bind the animal to their vampiric whims.

**Size** Small or Medium

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d8 piercing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +2

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[Rules/Scent|scent]] (imprecise, 30 feet)

**Speed** 30 feet

**Special** Vampiric animal companions are particularly attuned to the scent of blood. If a creature has [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]], your vampiric animal companion can smell the creature if it's within 120 feet rather than 30 feet.

**Support Benefit** When your vampiric animal senses blood it tears into your enemies. Until the start of your next turn, your Strikes that damage a creature in your vampiric animal's reach also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. If your vampiric animal is nimble or savage, the persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6.

**Advanced Maneuver** Blood Feast
