# Giant Rock Tuatara  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 100

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Five Kings Mountains have access to this option._

Your companion is a much larger cousin of the common rock tuatara, up to 6 feet long and 4 feet tall. Their milky-white third eye, spiny backs, and knobby club tails make them look far fiercer than they typically are.

**Speed** 25 feet

**Support Benefit** Your giant rock tuatara trips up your enemies when you create an opening. Until the start of your next turn, your [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] that deal damage to a creature within your giant rock tuatara’s reach make the target [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 until the end of your next turn.

**Advanced Maneuver** Tail Escape
