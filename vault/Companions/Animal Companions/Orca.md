# Orca  *Animal Companion 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

Your companion is a killer whale.

**Size** Large

**Melee**
single action
jaws,

**Str** +3

**Dex** +2

**Con** +3

**Int** -4

**Wis** +0

**Hit Points** 8

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

**Senses** echolocation 30 feet (the orca can use hearing as a [[Rules/Precise Senses (Player Core)|precise sense]] within this range while underwater)

**Speed** 5 feet, swim 40 feet

**Special** [[Rules/Riding Animal Companions (Player Core)|mount]]; the orca can hold its breath for 20 minutes.

**Support Benefit** Your orca attempts to drag down unwary swimmers. Until the start of your next turn, each time you hit a swimming creature in the orca’s reach, the target is pulled 5 feet down into the water. This is [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|forced movement]].
