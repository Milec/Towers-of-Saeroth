# Flying Squirrel  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 91

Your companion is a giant flying squirrel or sugar glider.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Melee**
single action
claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d4 slashing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +2

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision (Monster Core)|low-light vision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent (Monster Core)|scent]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]]) 30 feet

**Speed** 25 feet, climb 25 feet

**Special** When your flying squirrel falls, it can glide, falling only 5 feet at the beginning of your turn and moving up to 25 feet horizontally. It cannot glide if it’s unable to act.

**Support Benefit** Your flying squirrel wraps your foes in its skin flaps, hampering their movement. Until the end of your next turn, if your Strike damages a creature in your giant flying squirrel's reach, that creature takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.

**Advanced Maneuver** Death From Above
