# Riding Dragonet  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dragon]], [[Traits/Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 221

Your companion is a flightless dragonet whose larger-than-average size keeps it grounded. Though typically cagey around other creatures, they have inexplicably attached themself to you, becoming a loving companion. A riding dragonet has the [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait, but it otherwise functions normally as an animal companion.

**Size** Small

**Damage** 1d6 slashing

**Senses** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]]

**Speed** 30 feet

**Special** mount

**Support Benefit** Your dragonet’s wings flutter rapidly, pulsating the air around you both. Until the start of your next turn, each time you Strike while riding the dragonet and hit a creature in the dragonet’s reach, the creature takes 1d4 bludgeoning damage from the force of the dragonet’s wings. If your dragonet is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the bludgeoning damage increases to 2d4.

**Advanced Maneuver** Enamoring Exhalation
