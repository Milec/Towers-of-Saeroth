# Cragstone Bear  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 99

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Five Kings Mountains have access to this option._

Your companion is a four-legged cave ursine whose diet includes mineral-rich cave water that causes plates of rock to grow over its fur. Playful and energetic, the cragstone bear tends to have a personality more like a loyal dog than a grizzly, but with its rocky hide, its romping can be more forceful than intended.

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense (Monster Core)|tremorsense]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]], 20 feet)

**Speed** 35 feet

**Support Benefit** Your cragstone bear eagerly defends you. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC if your cragstone bear is adjacent to you. If your bear is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], increase the bonus to +2.

**Advanced Maneuver** Rolling Charge
