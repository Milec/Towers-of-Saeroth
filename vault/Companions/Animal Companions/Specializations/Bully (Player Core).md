# Bully  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

Your companion terrorizes foes with dominance displays and pushes them around the battlefield. Its proficiency ranks for [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] increase to expert (or master if it was already expert from its type), its Strength modifier increases by 1, and its Charisma modifier increases by 3.
