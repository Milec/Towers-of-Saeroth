# Bully  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 217

Your companion terrorizes foes with dominance displays and pushes them around the battlefield. Its proficiency ranks for [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] increase to expert (or master if it was already expert from its type), its Strength modifier increases by 1, and its Charisma modifier increases by 3.
