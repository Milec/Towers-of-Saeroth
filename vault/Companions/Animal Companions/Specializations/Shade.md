# Shade  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 229

**Access** [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster]]

Your companion's physical form is made, in whole or in part, of shadow. It gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]], resistance 5 to all damage except [[Traits/Force|force]], and in areas of dim light or darkness, it can Step 10 feet instead of 5 feet. Its proficiency rank in unarmored defense increases to expert.

 A [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster]] has access to this specialization.

_Nethys Note: The Shade animal companion specialization was affected by the [_Core Rulebook_ 3rd printing errata](https://paizo.com/pathfinder/faq) that reduced the nimble animal companion's proficiency with unarmored defense._
