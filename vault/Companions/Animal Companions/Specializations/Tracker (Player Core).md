# Tracker  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

Your companion is an incredible tracker. It can move at full Speed while [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|following tracks]]. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Wisdom modifier increases by 1.
