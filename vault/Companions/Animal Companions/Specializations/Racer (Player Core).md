# Racer  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

Your companion races. It gains a +10-foot status bonus to its Speed, [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]], or [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] (your choice). Its proficiency in Fortitude saves increases to legendary, and its Constitution modifier increases by 1.
