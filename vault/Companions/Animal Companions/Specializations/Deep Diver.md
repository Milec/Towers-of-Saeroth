# Deep Diver  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 123

Your companion gains a swim Speed of 30 feet or a +10-foot status bonus to its existing swim Speed and can breathe underwater if it couldn't already. Its Constitution modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank for [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] increases to master and for barding increases to expert. This specialization can only be selected by [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Racer|marid-touched companions]].
