# Daredevil  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

Your companion joins the fray with graceful leaps and dives. It gains the deny advantage ability, so it isn’t [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden (Player Core)|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]], or [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]] creatures unless such a creature’s level is greater than yours. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] increases to master, and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank in unarmored defense increases to expert.
