# Wrecker  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 217

Your companion smashes things. Its unarmed attacks ignore half an object's Hardness. Its [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] proficiency increases to master, and its Strength modifier increases by 1.
