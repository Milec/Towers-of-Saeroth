# Ambusher  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 217

In your companion's natural environment, it can use a [[Actions/Sneak]] action even if it's currently observed. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank for unarmored defense increases to expert.
