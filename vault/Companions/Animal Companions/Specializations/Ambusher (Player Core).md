# Ambusher  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

In your companion’s natural environment, it can use a [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] action even if it’s currently [[Rules/Conditions/Observed (Player Core)|observed]]. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank for unarmored defense increases to expert.
