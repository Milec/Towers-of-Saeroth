# Tracker  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 217

Your companion is an incredible tracker. It can move at full Speed while following tracks. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] increases to expert (or master if it was already an expert from its type), and its Wisdom modifier increases by 1.
