# Steadfast Strider  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 123

Your companion ignores natural difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain from sediment or stone and can move across quicksand, mud, and similar surfaces as if they were solid. It gains a +2 status bonus to saving throws and DCs to prevent being [[Actions/Shove|Shoved]] or [[Actions/Trip|Tripped]]. Its Strength modifier increases by 1 and its proficiency for barding increases to expert. This specialization can only be selected by [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Racer|shaitan-touched companions]].
