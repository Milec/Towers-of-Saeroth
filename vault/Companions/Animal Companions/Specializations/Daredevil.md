# Daredevil  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 217

Your companion joins the fray with graceful leaps and dives. It gains the deny advantage ability, so it isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]], [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]], or flanking creatures unless such a creature's level is greater than yours. Its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] increases to master, and its Dexterity modifier increases by 1. Its proficiency rank in unarmored defense increases to expert.
