# Wrecker  *Animal Companion Specialization*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

Your companion smashes things. Its unarmed attacks ignore half an object’s [[Rules/Item Damage (Player Core)|Hardness]]. Its [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] proficiency increases to master, and its Strength modifier increases by 1.
