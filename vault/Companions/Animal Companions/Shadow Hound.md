# Shadow Hound  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 228

Your companion is a canine creature related to the [[Setting/Planes/Shadow Plane]] denizens known as shadow mastiffs.

**Access** You're a [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster|shadowcaster]]

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d8 piercing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +1

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], [[Rules/Scent|scent]] (imprecise) 30 feet

**Speed** 30 feet

**Support Benefit** Your shadow hound shrouds your foes in shadow when you leave an opening. Until the start of your next turn, when you damage a creature with a Strike and that creature is within reach of your shadow hound, both you and your shadow hound become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to the creature until the end of your next turn.
