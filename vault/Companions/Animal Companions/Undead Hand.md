# Undead Hand  *Animal Companion 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mindless (Player Core)|Mindless]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -181- Zombie Feast|Pathfinder #181: Zombie Feast]] pg. 77

Your companion is a large skeletal or fleshy humanoid hand, similar to a [[Bestiary/Giant Crawling Hand|giant crawling hand]].

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
claw ([[Traits/Agile]], [[Traits/Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 slashing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +2

**Con** +1

**Int** -5

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** none (mindless)

**Senses** [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]

**Speed** 20 feet, climb 20 feet

**Special** [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]]

**Support Benefit** The hand viciously batters the foes you attack. Until the start of your next turn, each time you hit a creature in the hand's reach with a Strike, the creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage from the hand. If your hand is nimble or savage, the bludgeoning damage increases to 2d6.

**Advanced Maneuver** Grip Throat
