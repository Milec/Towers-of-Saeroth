# Savage  *Animal Companion Advanced Option*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 211

To advance a mature animal companion to a savage animal companion:

- If your companion is Medium or smaller, it grows by one size.
- Increase its Strength modifier by 2 and its Dexterity, Constitution, and Wisdom modifiers by 1.
- Increase its proficiency rank in [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] to expert.
- It deals 3 additional damage with its unarmed attacks. Its attacks become [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] for the purpose of ignoring resistances.
- It learns the advanced maneuver for its type.
