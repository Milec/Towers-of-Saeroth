# War Pig  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -208- Hoof, Cinder, and Storm|Pathfinder #208: Hoof, Cinder, and Storm]] pg. 79

Belkzen’s native swine is the [[Bestiary/Bristle Boar|bristle boar]], a fearsome beast whose spines make it unsuitable for riding. Millennia ago, orcs seized domesticated pigs from neighboring lands and back-bred them to create the more fearsome [[Bestiary/War Pig|war pigs]]. These animals are large enough to carry an armored orc into combat, hardy enough to weather Belkzen’s badlands, and indiscriminate enough in their palate to subsist on whatever sustenance they can root up.

To the inexperienced handler, a war pig is a moody pet at best, though they’re quite intelligent and curious. Without proper enrichment and training, however, they seek their own entertainment—often by destroying gardens, eating spellbooks, or undermining the foundation of a small house. They’re also good at recognizing words, as war pigs have been known to destroy their pens if they hear the name of (but do not receive) their favorite treat.
