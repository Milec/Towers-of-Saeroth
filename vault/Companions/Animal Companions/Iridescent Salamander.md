# Iridescent Salamander  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal]], [[Traits/Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

Your companion is an iridescent salamander, an unusually large amphibian that uses its reflective color-changing skin to confuse its enemies.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +2

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]]

**Speed** 20 feet

**Support Benefit** Your salamander reflects the light on its scales that shine brightly. Until the start of your next turn, if you hit and deal damage to a creature adjacent to your salamander, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the start of your next turn unless its level is higher than yours.

**Advanced Maneuver** Color Swirl
