# Thruneosaurus Rex  *Animal Companion 14*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 229

Developed by Chelaxian diabolists, thruneosauruses are powerful therapods infused with Hell’s fury. Few trainers can wrangle such a willful beast, much less check its diabolical urges.

**Special** **Recessive Fiend** A [[Traits/Sanctified|sanctified]] handler can suppress or fully awaken a thruneosaurus’s fiendish influence. Unless you’re [[Traits/Holy|holy]], the dinosaur gains the [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]] trait. If you’re [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], the dinosaur, its Strikes, and its advanced maneuver gain the unholy trait.

**Support Benefit** Your thruneosaurus’s horns radiate heat, igniting distracted foes. Until the start of your next turn, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] that damage a creature in your thruneosaurus’s reach also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]]. If your thruneosaurus is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the persistent damage increases to 2d6.
