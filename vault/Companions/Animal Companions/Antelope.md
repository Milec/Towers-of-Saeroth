# Antelope  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 90

Your companion is an antelope or other similar animal, such as a dik-dik, gazelle, or wildebeest.

**Size** Medium or Large

**Melee**
single action
horns ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Melee**
single action
hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d4 bludgeoning

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +2

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Low-Light Vision (Monster Core)|low-light vision]]

**Speed** 40 feet

**Special** [[Rules/Riding Animal Companions (Player Core)|mount]]

**Support Benefit** The antelope impales your distracted foes. Until the start of your next turn, while riding your antelope, your Strikes that damage a creature in your antelope’s reach also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. If your antelope is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the persistent bleed damage increases to 2d6.

**Advanced Maneuver** Bounding Retreat
