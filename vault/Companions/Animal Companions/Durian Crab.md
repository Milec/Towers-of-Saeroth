# Durian Crab  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 122

In the southern provinces of Nagajor, Xa Hoi, throughout the archipelago of Minata, and hidden within the thick overgrowth of the Valashmai Jungle are the notorious killer crustaceans known only as durian crabs. Using their large, powerful claws, they scale the trunks of tall trees, hiding their presence away from even the most observant of prey. When the time is right, they fall, plummeting onto their prey and letting the momentum and prickly thorns of their shells earn them their quarry.

**Support Benefit** Your durian crab launches itself as part of your ranged attack to throw your enemies off balance. Until the start of your next turn, as long as your crab is within your reach, your ranged Strikes that deal damage to a creature within 30 feet make the target [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn. Your crab scuttles or launches itself back to the same space from which you threw it immediately after making a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], allowing you to hurl it multiple times.
