# Pufferfish  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal]], [[Traits/Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 109

Your companion is a pufferfish, a round fish that can inflate its body and is covered in sharp spines.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
spines ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing plus poison

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +1

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]]

**Speed** 35 feet

**Special** The pufferfish’s spines attack deals an additional 1d4 poison damage or 2d4 poison damage if the pufferfish is a [[Rules/Specialized Animal Companions (Player Core)|specialized companion]].

**Support Benefit** Your pufferfish inflates, thrusting its spines into your enemies. Until the start of your next turn, your [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] that damage a creature in your pufferfish’s reach also deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. If your pufferfish is [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Deep Diver|nimble]] or [[Companions/Animal Companions/Specializations/Steadfast Strider|savage]], the persistent poison damage increases to 2d6.

**Advanced Maneuver** Barbed Dash
