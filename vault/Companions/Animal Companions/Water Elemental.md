# Water Elemental  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|Elemental]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 41

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Druids of the specifically listed orders may also take the other elementals (e.g. a wave order druid may take a water elemental companion)._

Your companion is a water elemental, such as a [[Bestiary/Brine Shark|brine shark]].

**Access** You are a member of the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Wave|wave order]].

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
wave,
**Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning

**Str** +2

**Dex** +2

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]]

**Speed** 15 feet, swim 35 feet

**Special** _Resistances_ fire (equal to the water elemental's level)

**Support Benefit** Your water elemental waterlogs a foe. The first time before the start of your next turn you hit with a Strike that deals damage to a creature within your water elemental's reach, that creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] until the end of your next turn.

**Advanced Maneuver** Drench
