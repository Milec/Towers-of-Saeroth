# Gray Bladeling  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Construct]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]], [[Traits/Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 123

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Galt have access to this option._

Your companion is a vaguely humanoid construct composed of razor-sharp blades and animated by an amalgamation of restless soul fragments, similar to a miniature [[Bestiary/Gray Death|gray death]]. Although it possesses no identity or memories from the souls it contains, it’s driven by a powerful but unfocused hate that its master must sometimes struggle to control.

**Access** You're from Galt.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
blade ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d8 slashing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +1

**Con** +2

**Int** -4

**Wis** +0

**Cha** +1

**Hit Points** 10

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|darkvision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Lifesense (Monster Core)|lifesense]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]]) 30 feet

**Speed** 20 feet

**Special** A gray bladeling has the [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|construct]] and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] traits instead of the [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] trait. It has [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immunity]] to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]], [[Rules/Death Effects|death effects]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]], [[Rules/Nonlethal Attacks (Player Core)|nonlethal attacks]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed (Player Core)|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]]. Unlike most undead, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] rather than being destroyed instantly at 0 Hit Points; unlike most constructs, its Hit Points can be restored by only [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]] instead of any other actions that restore Hit Points to objects or nonliving creatures.

**Support Benefit** The gray bladeling slashes viciously at your enemy when you create an opening. Until the start of your next turn, each time you hit a creature in the bladeling’s reach with a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], the creature takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] or becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 if it’s already taking persistent bleed damage. If the gray bladeling is [[Rules/Specialized Animal Companions (Player Core)|specialized]], the persistent bleed damage increases to 1d8.

**Advanced Maneuver** Possessing Strike
