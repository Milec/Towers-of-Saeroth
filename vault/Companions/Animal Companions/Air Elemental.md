# Air Elemental  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|Elemental]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 40

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Druids of the specifically listed orders may also take the other elementals (e.g. a wave order druid may take a water elemental companion)._

Your companion is an air elemental, such as a [[Bestiary/Zephyr Hawk|zephyr hawk]].

**Access** You are a member of the [[Classes/Subclasses/Druidic Orders/Storm|storm order]].

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
gust ([[Traits/Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 bludgeoning

**Str** +1

**Dex** +2

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +1

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|darkvision]]

**Speed** fly 50 feet

**Support Benefit** Your air elemental swirls around you with concealing gusts, making it harder for your foes to hit you and possibly giving you a moment to hide. Until the start of your next turn, if you hit with a Strike and deal damage to a creature in your air elemental's reach, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to that creature until the start of your next turn.

**Advanced Maneuver** Circling Flyby
