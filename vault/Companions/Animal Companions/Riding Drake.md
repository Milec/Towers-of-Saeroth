# Riding Drake  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Dragon]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 145

Your companion is a swift, flightless drake with limited intelligence, less vicious than most drakes but fiercely loyal to you. A riding drake has the [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait, but it otherwise functions normally as an animal companion.

**Special** [[Rules/Riding Animal Companions|mount]]

**Support Benefit** Your drake snorts fire in your foes' faces. Until the start of your next turn, each time you Strike while riding the drake and hit a creature in the drake's reach, the creature takes 1d4 fire damage from the drake. If your drake is nimble or savage, the fire damage increases to 2d4.
