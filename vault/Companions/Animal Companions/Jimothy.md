# Jimothy  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/YOU CAN PLAY WITH JIMOTHY NOW]]

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Jimothy is [society legal](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs8gfbh?YOU-CAN-PLAY-WITH-JIMOTHY-NOW#22) according to Maya Coleman._

Through much coaxing and patience, you are blessed with the aid of Jimothy, the creature of legend. Jimothy is a raccoon-like creature with thin limbs and a round body. All who look upon him are blessed.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
jaws ([[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Str** +1

**Dex** +3

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +2

**Hit Points** 4

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]], [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent (Monster Core)|scent]] ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise]], 30 feet)

**Speed** 30 feet

**Special** Jimothy doesn’t grow larger in size when he advances, though he gains all the other benefits. Jimothy can use his hands to perform simple [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions, such as picking up an unattended object, but he can’t wield weapons.

**Support Benefit** The very sight of Jimothy lifts your allies’ spirits. Choose yourself or one ally within 30 feet of Jimothy who can see Jimothy. Until the start of your next turn, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects.

**Advanced Maneuver** Tumbling Swipe
