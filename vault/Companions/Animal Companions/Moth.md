# Moth  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 54

Your animal companion is a giant fruit-eating moth with a strong proboscis that can pierce skin.

**Size** Small

**Melee**
single action
proboscis ([[Traits/Finesse]]),
**Damage** 1d6 piercing

**Str** +2

**Dex** +3

**Con** +1

**Int** -4

**Wis** +2

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 6

**Skill** [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]], [[Rules/Scent|scent]] (imprecise, 30 feet)

**Speed** 15 feet, fly 30 feet

**Special** Your moth is invisible to echolocation.

**Support Benefit** Your moth releases a shower of dust to reveal your foes. Until the start of your next turn, if you damage a [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] creature in your moth's reach with a Strike, that creature requires only a DC 3 flat check to target it if it's concealed, or DC 9 if it's hidden. The reduced DC lasts until the end of your next turn.

**Advanced Maneuver** Ultrasonic Scream
