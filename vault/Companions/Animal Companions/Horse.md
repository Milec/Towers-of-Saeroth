# Horse  *Animal Companion 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|Animal]], [[Traits/Minion (Player Core)|Minion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 216

Your companion is a horse, pony, or similar equine.

**Size** Medium or Large

**Melee**
single action
hoof ([[Traits/Agile]]),
**Damage** 1d6 bludgeoning

**Str** +3

**Dex** +2

**Con** +2

**Int** -4

**Wis** +1

**Cha** +0

**Hit Points** 8

**Skill** [[Rules/Skills/Survival]]

**Senses** [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], [[Rules/Scent|scent]] (imprecise, 30 feet)

**Speed** 40 feet

**Special** [[Rules/Riding Animal Companions|mount]]

**Support Benefit** Until the start of your next turn, if you’re mounted on your horse and moved 10 feet or more on the action before a melee Strike, add a circumstance bonus to damage for that Strike equal to twice the number of weapon damage dice. If your weapon already has the [[Traits/Jousting|jousting]] weapon trait, increase the trait’s damage bonus by 2 per die instead.

**Advanced Maneuver** Gallop
