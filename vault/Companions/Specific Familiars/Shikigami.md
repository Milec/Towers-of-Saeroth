# Shikigami  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|Construct]]

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier (Player Core)|Flier]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Kindling]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Play Dead]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough (Player Core)|Tough]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Versatile Form]]

Shikigami are small spirits typically summoned and bound to a paper figure, so confined to carry out the deeds and embody the will of their masters. More often than not, the naivete of these amateur practitioners of esoteric magics means they’ll find themselves at the mercy of their own shikigami.
