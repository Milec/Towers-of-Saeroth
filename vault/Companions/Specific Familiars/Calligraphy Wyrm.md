# Calligraphy Wyrm  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beast]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 122

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The saving throw for the calligraphy wyrm’s Ink Spray ability should have a DC equal to the master’s spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher._

**Required Number of Abilities** 6

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Scent]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Skilled|Skilled (Arcana, Society)]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

Calligraphy wyrms are curious dragons constantly in search of new lore and writing. They make natural allies for Pathfinders. While knowledgeable, calligraphy wyrms can also be know-it-alls. Their chirpy, high-pitched voices make this attitude humorous to some and insufferable to others.
