# Cullitox Shardling  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Elemental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 42

**Required Number of Abilities** 3

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Elemental (Rage of Elements)|elemental (earth only)]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

A cullitox shardling is a younger, smaller version of [[Bestiary/Cullitox|cullitox]]. When acting as a familiar, a shardling's hunger for crystals is sated by magic, turning an urgent need into a mere interest.
