# Makhluk Wayang  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|Construct]]

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 125

**Required Number of Abilities** 8

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity (Player Core)|Manual Dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Play Dead]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech (Player Core)|Speech]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough (Player Core)|Tough]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Versatile Form]]

Makhluk wayangs are shadow puppets, pieces of show and tell made animate, carrying no more than the personalities they were crafted to inhabit. Unlike the wayang people, your familiar is a shadowy incorporeal being without a body of its own. A makhluk wayang possesses a simple puppet made of leather or, sometimes, from thick parchment.
