# Mood Cloud  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Elemental]]

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Elemental (Rage of Elements)|elemental (air only)]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier]]
