# Dweomercat Cub  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Beast]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 122

**Required Number of Abilities** 4

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Scent]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

Pathfinder agent Arianrhod Thriceborn, an adherent of the Eldest [[Setting/Deities/Magdh]], raised a litter of these intelligent mystical felines from the [[Setting/Planes/First World]]. Dweomercat cubs are curious and love exploring ley lines and traveling the world. At only 1 foot tall and weighing 15 pounds, dweomercat cubs are smaller than their egos and curiosity imply.
