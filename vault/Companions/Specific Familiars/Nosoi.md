# Nosoi  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Rules/Alignment (Core Rulebook)|N]], [[Traits/Monitor]], [[Traits/Psychopomp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 35

**Required Number of Abilities** 5

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

A nosoi's physical appearance is that of a bird—usually a crow, sparrow, or whippoorwill—though it wears a funerary mask that accentuates its beak. Nosois measure about 1 foot in length but are unusually heavy, weighing between 10 and 15 pounds.
