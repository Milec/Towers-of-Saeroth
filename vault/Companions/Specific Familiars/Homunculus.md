# Homunculus  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|Construct]]

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision (Player Core)|Darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity (Player Core)|Manual Dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Poison Reservoir (Player Core 2)|Poison Reservoir]]

Homunculi are tiny servitor constructs created by a crafter to serve as a spy, scout, messenger, or assistant. A homunculus is crafted using a drop of its creator’s blood in addition to any other components used in crafting its body.
