# Elemental Scamp  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Elemental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Required Number of Abilities** 5

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Elemental (Rage of Elements)|Elemental]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

The elementals called [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Mephit|scamps]] look similar to bats, but their bodies are made primarily of elemental matter. They have large, staring eyes and small, curved fangs. . An elemental scamp familiar is a bit smaller than other scamps but makes up for it by being more mischievous. Driven by ego, scamps often take on long names with many titles and insist on being addressed in full.
