# Shadow Familiar  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 229

**Access**
you're a [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster|shadowcaster]]

**Required Number of Abilities** 7

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Darkvision]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Manual Dexterity]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Master's Form]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Resistance|resistance (cold and negative)]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Shadow Step]]

Some spellcasters and ritualists don't just summon and bind a familiar, but instead transform their own shadow into a familiar. These casters can be identified by their lack of a shadow regardless of the level of light in the area and by the strange muted appearance of any article of clothing or jewelry they wear. Occasionally mistaken for [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire|vampires]] or other [[Traits/Undead|undead]] by the cautious or superstitious, these spellcasters nonetheless find the trade worth it in exchange for the unique abilities their shadow familiar can possess.

 Shadow familiars are a type of specific familiar. While all shadowcasters have access to these familiars, there are other paths to learn the proper magic to obtain a shadow familiar.
