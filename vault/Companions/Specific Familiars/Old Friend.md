# Old Friend  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Incorporeal]], [[Traits/Spirit]], [[Traits/Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 40

**Required Number of Abilities** 4

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier]]

Sometimes, the spirit of a cherished pet returns from the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] to continue aiding its beloved owner. These tiny animal ghosts tend to be bound to anchors such as favorite toys, collars, or carved representations of who they were in life. An old friend is less likely to be evil than other undead familiars, as is its master.
