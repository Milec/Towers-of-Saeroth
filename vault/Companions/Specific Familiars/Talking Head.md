# Talking Head  *Specific Familiar*

**Traits** [[Traits/Undead]]

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 40

**Required Number of Abilities** 3

**Granted Abilities** [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Cantrip Connection]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Skilled|Skilled (your choice of Arcana, Occultism, or one kind of Lore)]], [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech]]

Similar to [[Bestiary/Families/Beheaded|beheaded]], these disembodied, reanimated heads (with or without skin) retain some of their arcane wisdom, making for invaluable study partners. However, they also tend to be mouthy, opinionated, or downright obnoxious with their constant chattering, leading some annoyed masters to sew their mouths shut in punishment.
