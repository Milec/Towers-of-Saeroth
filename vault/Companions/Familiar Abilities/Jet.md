# Jet  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 42

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar can use bursts of elemental energy or magic to [[Actions/Leap]] up to 30 feet in any direction, even if that would exceed its normal maximum distance for leaping. The Leap must begin and end on a solid surface capable of supporting the familiar.
