# Recall Familiar  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 214

**Ability Type** Master

Once per day, you can use a 3-action activity, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to teleport your familiar to your space. Your familiar must be within 1 mile or the attempt to summon it fails. This is a [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effect.
