# Stunning Flare single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 125

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Lantern Wisp

Your lantern wisp unleashes a large burst of flame, casting an unnaturally bright light. Each creature within a 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or be [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round and then [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 2 rounds after.
