# Elemental  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 35

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Elemental Wisp

Choose air, earth, fire, or water. Your wisp is a wisp of that element and gains that trait. Your wisp gains a familiar ability depending on its element. Air wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier|flight]], earth wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower|burrower]], fire wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Greater Resistance|greater resistance (fire)]], and water wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]].
