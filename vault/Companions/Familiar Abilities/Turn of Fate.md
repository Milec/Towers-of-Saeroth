# Turn of Fate two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 220

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Ceru

Your ceru shifts the scales of fortune for one target's next attempt at an attack roll, saving throw, or skill check. If targeted with bad fortune, the target attempts a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, with effects depending on the result of their save; this is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect. If targeted with good fortune, the target rolls twice and uses the better result; this is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect. Regardless of outcome, targets are immune to Turn of Fate for one day.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target rolls twice and uses the worse result.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the target is affected for the next three attempts.
