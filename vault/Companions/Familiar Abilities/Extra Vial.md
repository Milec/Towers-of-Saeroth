# Extra Vial  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 170

**Ability Type** Master

Your familiar accretes alchemical liquids in its body. Once per day, you can Interact when your familiar is adjacent to you to gain a versatile vial.
