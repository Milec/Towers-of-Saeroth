# Aeon Stone Reservoir  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 122

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Aeon Wyrd

Your aeon wyrd can hold any _[[Equipment/Aeon Stone|aeon stone]]_ as a powerfully resonant nucleus that holds it together. You gain the _aeon stone's_ benefits without needing to use one of your investiture slots for the day, and you also gain the _aeon stone's_ resonant power, without interfering with any [[Equipment/Wayfinder of Rescue|wayfinder]] you invest.
