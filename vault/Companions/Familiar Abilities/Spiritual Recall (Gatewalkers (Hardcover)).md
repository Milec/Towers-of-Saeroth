# Spiritual Recall ⤾  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 241

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Spirit Guide

You use your bond to rescue your familiar. The familiar survives the attack with 1 Hit Point remaining and becomes [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] until the end of your next turn.
