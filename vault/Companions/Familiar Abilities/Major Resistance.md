# Major Resistance  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 34

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar increases the resistance it gains from its resistance familiar ability to a value equal to your level. To select this, your familiar must have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Greater Resistance|greater resistance]] ability and you must be at least 8th level.
