# Lifelink  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 218

**Ability Type** Master

If your familiar would be reduced to 0 HP by damage, as a reaction with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, you can take the damage. If you do, you take all the damage and your familiar takes none. However, special effects that would occur due to that damage (such as snake venom) still apply to your familiar.
