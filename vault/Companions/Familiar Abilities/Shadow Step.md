# Shadow Step  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 229

**Ability Type** Familiar

This ability is for a [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Kinspeech|shadow familiar]]. A [[Archetypes/Shadowcaster|shadowcaster]], however, can select this ability for any kind of familiar. Your familiar gains the Shadow Step action. You must be at least 7th level to select this familiar ability for your familiar.  
 **Shadow Step** single action ([[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]], [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]], [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]]) **Requirements** The familiar is in [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]]. **Effect** The familiar teleports itself up to 30 feet. The destination must be in dim light or darkness and must be within your familiar's line of sight and line of effect. This action has the trait matching your tradition of magic, or [[Traits/Occult|occult]] if you aren't a spellcaster.
