# Elemental Mobility  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Elemental Scamp

Your scamp gains a familiar ability depending on its element. Air scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier|flier]], earth scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower|burrower]], fire scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Jet|jet]], metal scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Levitator|levitator]], water scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]], and wood scamps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Climber|climber]].
