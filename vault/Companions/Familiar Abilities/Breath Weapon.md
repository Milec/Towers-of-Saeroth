# Breath Weapon two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 147

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Faerie Dragon

The faerie dragon breathes euphoric gas in a 10-foot cone. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1d4 rounds; on a critical failure, the duration is 1 minute.
