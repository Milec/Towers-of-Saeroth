# Ink Spray single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 44

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Calligraphy Wyrm

The wyrm splatters ink in a 10-foot cone. Each creature in the area must attempt a Reflex save with a DC equal to its master’s spell DC or class DC, whichever is higher.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** If the creature was [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], it is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead for 2 rounds.  

**Failure** As success, but for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but for 10 minutes. The creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round or until it wipes the ink from its eyes using an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action.
