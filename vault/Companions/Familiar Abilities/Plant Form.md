# Plant Form  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 146

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your plant familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a Tiny plant of a type roughly similar to the familiar's nature. This otherwise uses the effects of [[Spells/Tree Shape|_tree shape_]]. You must have a familiar with the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait, such as a leshy, to select this ability.
