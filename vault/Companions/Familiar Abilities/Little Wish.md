# Little Wish ⤾  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Gennayn

**Trigger** A creature the gennayn can see that is within 60 feet attempts a saving throw or skill check; **Effect** The gennayn reshapes reality in a small way to twist fate, allowing the creature to reroll the saving throw or skill check.
