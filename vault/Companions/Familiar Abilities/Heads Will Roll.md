# Heads Will Roll  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Book of the Dead]] pg. 40

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Talking Head

Unless its master gives the talking head the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier|flier]] ability, a talking head is able to move only by rolling along the ground, reducing its speed to 15 feet. Alternatively, a creature can, as a single action, kick or throw the head 30 feet. This can't be used as an attack; the head bounces harmlessly off anything it hits.
