# Haunting Melody two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 35

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Nosoi

The nosoi croons an entrancing song. Each living or [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature within a 60-foot emanation must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or be [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] for 1 round. A nosoi can use a 2-action activity, which has the auditory, concentrate, divine, enchantment, and mental traits, to force affected creatures to attempt another Will save; on a failure, they are fascinated for an additional round. A creature that succeeds at any save or has its fascination broken is temporarily immune for 24 hours. Despite being a mental effect, this ability affects [[Traits/Mindless|mindless]] undead. [[Traits/Psychopomp|Psychopomps]] are immune to this ability.
