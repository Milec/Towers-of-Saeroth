# Greater Resistance  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 34

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar increases the resistance it gains from its resistance familiar ability to 3 + half your level. Your familiar must have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Resistance|resistance]] ability to select this.
