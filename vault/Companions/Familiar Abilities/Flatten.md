# Flatten  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 125

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Shikigami

Your familiar can flatten itself to the width of a sheet of paper, easily fitting through the smallest of cracks and gaps. So long as a door or other obstacle has a gap that can fit a sheet of paper, your familiar can move through it without needing to [[Actions/Squeeze (Player Core)|Squeeze]].
