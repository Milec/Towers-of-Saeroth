# Breath Weapon single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Shadows at Sundown]] pg. 53

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - House Drake

The house drake breathes a 10-foot cone of silver mist. Each creature in the area must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 round. The house drake can't use Breath Weapon or Silver Infusion for 1d4 rounds.
