# Extra Reagents  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 218

**Ability Type** Master

Your familiar grows extra infused reagents on or in its body. You gain an additional batch of infused reagents. You must have the [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background|infused reagents]] ability to select this ability.
