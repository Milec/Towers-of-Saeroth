# Slime Rejuvenation  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 147

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Spellslime

Your spellslime familiar gains the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Focused Rejuvenation|focused rejuvenation]] ability, but it recovers 2 Hit Points per level when you [[Actions/Refocus]] instead of 1.
