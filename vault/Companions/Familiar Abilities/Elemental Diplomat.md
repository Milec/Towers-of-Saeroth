# Elemental Diplomat  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Gennayn

A gennayn's modifier for [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] is equal to your level plus your key spellcasting attribute modifier, rather than just your level. The gennayn gets a +1 circumstance bonus to Diplomacy checks against [[Traits/Elemental|elementals]] and grants you the same bonus if you're in the same space.
