# Lifelink  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 214

**Ability Type** Master

If your familiar would be reduced to 0 HP by damage, as a reaction with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, you can take all the damage, and your familiar takes none. However, special effects that would occur due to that damage (such as snake venom) still apply.
