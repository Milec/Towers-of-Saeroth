# Amphibious  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 259

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar gains the [[Traits/Amphibious|amphibious]] trait, allowing it to breathe in both air and water, and has both a land Speed and a swim Speed, each equal to its highest land Speed or swim Speed.
