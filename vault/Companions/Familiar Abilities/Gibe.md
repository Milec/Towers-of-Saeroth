# Gibe single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 45

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Mockingfey

The mockingfey takes on the imperfect, illusory appearance of a creature it can see within 60 feet, pantomiming their mannerisms in a derisive fashion. This illusion doesn’t make the mockingfey look any larger and lasts until the end of your next turn. The creature must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. Regardless of the result, the creature is temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the start of your next turn, as it’s distracted by your familiar’s taunts.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is off-guard until the end of your next turn.
