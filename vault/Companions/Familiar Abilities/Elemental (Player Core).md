# Elemental  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 212

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar has the [[Traits/Elemental|elemental]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait. Choose [[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Water|water]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]]. Your familiar gains that trait. The familiar is immune to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|bleed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]], and the element matching its trait. Your familiar must have the resistance familiar ability to select this.
