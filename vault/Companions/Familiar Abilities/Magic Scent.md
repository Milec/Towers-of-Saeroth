# Magic Scent  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 147

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Spellslime

Your spellslime familiar gains an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet that enables it to smell magic of the same tradition as your own.
