# Share Senses  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 214

**Ability Type** Master

Once every 10 minutes, you can use a single action with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait to project your senses into your familiar. When you do, you lose all sensory information from your own body, but can sense through your familiar's body for up to 1 minute. You can Dismiss this effect.
