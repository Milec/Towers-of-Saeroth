# Restorative Familiar  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 214

**Ability Type** Master

Once per day, your familiar can use 2 actions with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait to give up some of its energy and heal you. It must be in your space to do so. You restore a number of Hit Points equal to 1d8 times half your level (minimum 1d8).
