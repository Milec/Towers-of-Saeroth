# Spiritual Recall ⤾  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -188- They Watched the Stars|Pathfinder #188: They Watched the Stars]] pg. 83

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Spirit Guide

You use your bond to rescue your familiar. The familiar survives the attack with 1 Hit Point remaining and becomes [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] until the end of your next turn.
