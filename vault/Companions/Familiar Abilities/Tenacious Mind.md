# Tenacious Mind  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Shadows at Sundown]] pg. 53

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - House Drake

Once per day, as long as your house drake took no actions on the previous round, you gain the following.  
**Ferocious Will** ⤾ ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** You succeed at a saving throw against a magical [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect; **Effect** You send a blast of magical feedback at the effect's source, dealing 2d6 mental damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher) to that creature. On a failed save, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.
