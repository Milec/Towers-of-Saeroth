# Elemental Breath two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Elemental Scamp

The scamp breathes elemental matter in a 10-foot cone. Each creature in the area takes 1d6 damage for every 2 levels you have, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. The damage type depends on the type of scamp.
