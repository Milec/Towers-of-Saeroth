# Steam Screen single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 23

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Clockwork Familiar

Your clockwork familiar blows a billowing plume of steam into its square around itself. It gains a +1 circumstance bonus to its [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks, and any creature in the familiar's square has [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]], though creatures in the square can't use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]]. The steam and its effects remain for 1 round. To use this ability, your clockwork familiar must spend 1 hour of operational time.
