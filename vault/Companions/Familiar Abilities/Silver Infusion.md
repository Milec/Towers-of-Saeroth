# Silver Infusion two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Shadows at Sundown]] pg. 53

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - House Drake

Your house drake breathes on one of your weapons. Until the start of your next turn, that weapon counts as [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] instead of its normal material for any physical damage it deals. The house drake can't use Breath Weapon or Silver Infusion for 1d4 rounds.
