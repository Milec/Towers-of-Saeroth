# Scamp Elements  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Elemental Scamp

An elemental scamp's element or elements are set. You choose a type of scamp and can't change the element each day. This determines the scamp's elemental traits and the damage type of their Elemental Breath. **Air** ([[Traits/Air|air]]) slashing damage; **earth** ([[Traits/Earth|earth]]) bludgeoning damage; **fire** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) fire damage; **metal** ([[Traits/Metal|metal]]) slashing damage; **water** ([[Traits/Water|water]]) acid damage; **wood** ([[Traits/Plant|plant]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) poison damage.
