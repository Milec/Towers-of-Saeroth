# Kinspeech  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 212

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar can understand and speak with animals of the same species. To select this, your familiar must be an animal, it must have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Speech|speech]] ability, and you must be at least 6th level.
