# Fruity Ambrosia  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 51

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Wildtwig

The peach growing from a wildtwig’s horn is magical, tasting like the favorite fruit of the one who consumes it, providing them some temporary rejuvenation. A single fruit lasts for 8 hours after picked. When consumed as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, the peach provides [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to the creature’s level for 1 hour. Eating a second fruit within an hour provides no benefits, instead making the creature [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 for 10 minutes. It takes 10 minutes for the wildtwig to grown another, and during this time, they must remain calm and focused on producing the fruit; they are unable to perform any other actions.
