# Familiar Focus  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 218

**Ability Type** Master

Once per day, your familiar can use 2 actions with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait to restore 1 Focus Point to your focus pool, up to your usual maximum. You must have a focus pool to select this.
