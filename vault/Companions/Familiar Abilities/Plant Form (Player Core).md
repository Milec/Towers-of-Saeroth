# Plant Form  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 213

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your plant familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a Tiny plant of a type roughly similar to the familiar's nature. This otherwise uses the effects of [[Spells/One with Plants|_one with plants_]]. You must have a familiar with the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait to select this ability.
