# Shadow Projection single action  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 125

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Makhluk Wayang

Your makhluk wayang projects its shadow into a larger silhouette of itself, allowing it to grasp things outside of its obvious reach. It increases its reach to 10 feet for the purposes of taking non-hostile [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] actions until the end of your turn.
