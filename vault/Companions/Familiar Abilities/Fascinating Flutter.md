# Fascinating Flutter two actions  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 51

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Royal Gull

The royal gull flaps its wings releasing iridescent dust in a 10-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]. Each creature in the area must attempt a Will save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round; on a critical failure, the duration is 1 minute.
