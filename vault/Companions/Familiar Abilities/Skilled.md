# Skilled  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 146

**Ability Type** Familiar

Choose a skill other than [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]]. Your familiar's modifier for that skill is equal to your level plus your key spellcasting ability modifier, rather than just your level. You can select this ability repeatedly, choosing a different skill each time.
