# Recall Familiar  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Grand Bazaar]] pg. 34

**Ability Type** Master

You can summon your familiar to your side. Once per day, you can use a 3-action activity, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to teleport your familiar to your space. Your familiar must be within 1 mile or the attempt to summon it fails. This is a [[Traits/Conjuration|conjuration]] and [[Traits/Teleportation|teleport]] effect.
