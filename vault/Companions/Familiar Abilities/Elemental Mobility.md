# Elemental Mobility  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 43

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Elemental Wisp

Your wisp gains a familiar ability depending on its element. Air wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Flier|flier]], earth wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Burrower|burrower]], fire wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Jet|jet]], metal wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Levitator|levitator]], water wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Amphibious|amphibious]], and wood wisps gain [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Climber|climber]].
