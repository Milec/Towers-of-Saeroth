# Constructed  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 122

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Aeon Wyrd

Your aeon wyrd is immune to bleed, [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks, [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]]. It can recover Hit Points only via the [[Actions/Repair]] action and other methods that fix items, not from any form of Hit Point healing. It's destroyed at 0 HP.
