# Seal-Bearer  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 125

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Shikigami

During your daily preparations, you can inscribe a symbol or seal upon your shikigami that corresponds to the [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Air|air]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Earth|earth]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Fire|fire]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Metal|metal]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Water|water]], or [[Classes/Subclasses/Elements/Wood|wood]] element. If you use the shikigami’s kindling ability that day, it applies to a spell that deals damage with the relevant trait to the chosen element, instead of only the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait.
