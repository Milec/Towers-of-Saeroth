# Versatile Form  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Tian Xia Character Guide]] pg. 124

**Ability Type** Familiar

The crafted body of your familiar allows you to make adjustments to it when required. Once per day, you can spend 10 minutes to change one [[Rules/Familiar Abilities|familiar]] or [[Rules/Selecting Familiar and Master Abilities|master ability]] your familiar possesses. To select this ability, your familiar must be a [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Construct|construct]], and you must be at least trained in [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]].
