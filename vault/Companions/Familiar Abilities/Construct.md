# Construct  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 212

**Ability Type** Familiar

Your familiar has the [[Traits/Construct|construct]] trait instead of the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait. The familiar is immune to [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks, [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], [[Traits/Spirit|spirit]], [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious|unconscious]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], and [[Traits/Void|void]]. Your familiar must have the [[Companions/Familiar Abilities/Tough|tough]] pet ability to select this.
