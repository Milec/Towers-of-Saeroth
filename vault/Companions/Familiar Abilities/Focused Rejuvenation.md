# Focused Rejuvenation  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 146

**Ability Type** Familiar

When you [[Actions/Refocus]], you generate magical energy that heals your familiar. Your familiar regains 1 Hit Point per level whenever you Refocus.
