# Imp Invisibility  *Familiar Ability*

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 147

**Ability Type** Specific Familiar - Imp

Once per hour, your imp familiar can cast 2nd-level [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] on itself as a divine innate spell.
