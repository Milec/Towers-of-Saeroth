# Plant  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon (Impossible Magic)|Eidolon]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 76

Your eidolon is an intelligent plant, formed from the same disembodied fragments of nature's life energy that become leshies. Plant eidolons tend to be curious and adaptable, with temperaments based on the parts of mortal culture they feel affinity toward. Despite coming from the same source, plant eidolons don't always look like leshies. Plant eidolons have forms that vary greatly and can look like almost any kind of plant creature in existence. Some even resemble plant creatures so strange they are impossible to identify.

**Eidolon Abilities**

- **Initial** Tendril Strike single action
- **Symbiosis** Growing Vines
- **Transcendence** Field of Roots two actions

## Tendril Strike single action

Stretching to extend their body to its limits, your eidolon attacks a foe that would normally be beyond their reach. Your eidolon makes a melee unarmed Strike, increasing their reach by 5 feet for that Strike. If the unarmed attack has the [[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]], [[Traits/Shove (Player Core)|shove]], or [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]] trait, the eidolon can use the corresponding action instead of a Strike.

## Growing Vines  *7th*

Your eidolon's vines and branches lengthen even more. All your eidolon's melee unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] trait.

## Field of Roots two actions  *17th*

Your eidolon extends their roots underground to entangle and possibly damage all foes nearby. All enemies within your eidolon's reach take damage of the same type and amount as your eidolon's most damaging Strike, depending on their Reflex saves against your spell DC. Any ongoing effects of the save last until the enemy either Escapes (rolling against your spell DC) or leaves your eidolon's reach. After using Field of Roots, your eidolon is immobilized until they take an Interact action to detach from the ground; this also ends any remaining effects on enemies from Field of Roots.

**Critical Success** The enemy is unaffected.  

**Success** The enemy takes no damage, but takes a –10 oot circumstance penalty to its Speeds.  

**Failure** The enemy takes half damage, takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds, and at the end of each of its turns, takes half damage again.  

**Critical Failure** The enemy is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], takes full damage, and at the end of each of its turns, takes full damage again.
