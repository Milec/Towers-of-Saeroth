# Plant  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 65

Your eidolon is an intelligent plant, formed from the same disembodied fragments of nature's life energy that become leshys. Plant eidolons tend to be curious and adaptable, with temperaments based on the parts of mortal culture they feel affinity toward. Despite coming from the same source, plant eidolons don't always look like [[Bestiary/Families/Leshy|leshys]]. Plant eidolons have forms that vary greatly and can look like almost any kind of plant creature in existence. Some even resemble plant creatures so strange they are impossible to identify.

## Tendril Strike

Your eidolon can stretch out vines and roots, attacking foes outside its reach. It gains the Tendril Strike action.

## Growing Vines  *7th*

Your eidolon's vines and branches lengthen even more. All your eidolon's melee unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] trait.

## Field of Roots  *17th*

Your eidolon learns how to send its roots underground to hinder foes. It gains the Field of Roots activity.
