# Aberrant  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|Aberration]], [[Traits/Eidolon (Impossible Magic)|Eidolon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 70

Your eidolon is a terrifying, incomprehensible nightmare patterned after the creatures who owe their existence to the Outer Gods and Great Old Ones. Aberrant eidolons come in a variety of forms, though their anatomies often appear twisted and impossible. Most people can't even look at any aberrant eidolon without developing a splitting headache or having a panic attack, but your studies of the occult have inured your mind to these dread terrors.

**Eidolon Abilities**

- **Symbiosis** Unfathomable Mind
- **Transcendence** Maddening Whispers

## Surprising Anatomy ⤾

**Trigger** An enemy adjacent to your eidolon deals damage to your eidolon; **Effect** Your eidolon's form shifts horribly to retaliate against the attacker. Your eidolon makes a melee unarmed Strike against the triggering enemy.

## Unfathomable Mind  *7th*

Those who try to touch your eidolon's mind find their own minds shattered. When your eidolon succeeds at a saving throw against a mental effect from a creature, that creature takes 4d6 mental damage with a basic Will save against your spell DC. A creature who fails this save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 (stupefied 2 on a critical failure). At 9th level and every 2 levels thereafter, the damage increases by 1d6.

## Maddening Whispers  *17th*

Your aberrant eidolon utters terrible secrets about the true nature of the universe. They can cast [[Spells/Unfathomable Song (Player Core)|_unfathomable song_]] once per day as an innate occult spell. Your eidolon can Sustain this spell as a free action if they spend at least one action Striding toward one of the targets.
