# Ooze  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon (Impossible Magic)|Eidolon]], [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|Ooze]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 76

Your eidolon is a simple creature of protoplasm held together by magic and your mind. They often appear as creeping blobs of a single color, but this doesn't mean they can't have distinctive features, such as moss or fungus growing on their surface or bones and other debris floating within. Because they have been formed by your thoughts, your ooze eidolon isn't mindless, but likely doesn't have a complete personality. Though your ooze eidolon speaks no language, you might believe you can interpret the constant burbles and plops they make as a form of speaking.

## Ooze Physiology

You and your ooze eidolon are directly connected in both body and mind, which renders your eidolon susceptible to effects that wouldn't normally bother an ooze. They don't have an ooze's normal immunities, but do gain resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1 resistance) against acid, critical hits, and precision damage and a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] effects. Additionally, their surface closes quickly after being wounded. The flat check to remove [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] from the eidolon is DC 10 (or DC 5 after receiving particularly effective aid).

## Stretching Pseudopods  *7th*

Your eidolon's pseudopods can stretch farther. All your eidolon's melee unarmed attacks gain the [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] trait.

## Flowing Engulf three actions  *17th*

Your eidolon Strides up to double their Speed and can move through the spaces of any creatures in their path. Any creature of the eidolon's size or smaller whose space your eidolon moves through is engulfed unless it succeeds at a Reflex save against your spell DC. Your eidolon can attempt to engulf the same creature only once in a single use of Flowing Engulf, and they can contain as many creatures as can fit in their space.

 An engulfed creature is pulled into your eidolon's body. It is grabbed, [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1, and moves along with your eidolon as long as it is engulfed. It can get free by [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escaping]] against your spell DC, moving to a space of its choice adjacent to your eidolon.
