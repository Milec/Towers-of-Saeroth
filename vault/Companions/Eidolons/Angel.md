# Angel  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Angel]], [[Traits/Celestial]], [[Traits/Eidolon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 59

Your eidolon is a celestial messenger, a member of the angelic host with a unique link to you, allowing them to carry a special message to the mortal world at your side. Most angel eidolons are roughly humanoid in form, with feathered wings, glowing eyes, halos, or similar angelic features. However, some take the form of smaller angelic servitors like the winged helmet [[Bestiary/Cassisian|cassisian angel]] instead. The two of you are destined for an important role in the plans of the celestial realms. Though a true angel, your angel eidolon's link to you as a mortal prevents them from casting the [[Rituals/Angelic Messenger|angelic messenger]] ritual, even if they somehow learn it.

## Hallowed Strikes

Your eidolon's attacks are hallowed by the celestial realms. Your eidolon's [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strikes gain the holy trait and deal 1 extra spirit damage to unholy creatures and creatures with weakness to holy. Additionally, your eidolon can make [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks with its unarmed attacks without taking the usual –2 circumstance penalty.

## Traveler's Aura  *7th*

Your eidolon emanates a powerful aura—resembling that of an [[Bestiary/Astral Deva|astral deva]]—that protects creatures as they travel. The aura has the [[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Aura|aura]], and [[Traits/Divine|divine]] traits. Your eidolon and its allies within a 20-foot emanation are protected from [[Rules/Temperature|severe heat and cold]], and your eidolon is never [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] to creatures inside the aura that are of a lower level than the eidolon.

 When you gain the transcendence class feature, this evolves to the full benefits of an astral deva; your eidolon and allies in the aura are protected from environmental damage from any plane, including severe and extreme heat and cold as well as more unusual dangers.

## Angelic Mercy  *17th*

Your eidolon's angelic power allows them to assuage a variety of ailments, just like most other powerful angels. Your eidolon can cast [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]], [[Spells/Remove Disease|_remove disease_]], [[Spells/Remove Fear|_remove fear_]], and [[Spells/Remove Paralysis|_remove paralysis_]] once per day each as 9th-level divine innate spells.
