# Psychopomp  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Monitor]], [[Traits/Psychopomp]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 66

Your eidolon is a [[Bestiary/Families/Psychopomp|psychopomp]], a creature whose sworn duty is to usher souls safely to the afterlife and maintain the courts of the dead. Psychopomp eidolons have a variety of appearances, but since they often traffic with mortals, their form typically includes an elaborate mask. You and your psychopomp eidolon share an important fate together, whether it relates directly to your own soul or to a mission that will somehow protect the souls of others.

**Tradition** [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Alignment** must be true neutral

**Home Plane** [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard|The Boneyard]]

## Spirit Touch

Your eidolon's attacks possess a psychopomp's ability to harm the living and the dead. Your eidolon's unarmed Strikes affect [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures as though etched with a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune and deal an extra 1 negative damage to living creatures and an extra 1 positive damage to [[Traits/Undead|undead]].

## Hidden Watcher  *7th*

Your eidolon can render both of you unseen. Once per hour, it can cast [[Spells/Invisibility|_invisibility_]] targeting you, them, or both of you at once. If the spell targeted both of you, the spell ends for both of you if either of you uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]].

## Spirit Taker  *17th*

Your eidolon is particularly capable when it comes to handling bodiless spirits and putting [[Traits/Undead|undead]] to their final rest. Your eidolon can see [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures inside of solid objects and can attack them with Strikes without penalty; an incorporeal creature in a solid object that takes damage from your eidolon's Strike is expelled from the object to the nearest available space, moving closer to your eidolon if possible.

 Additionally, your eidolon can target or affect a creature projecting its consciousness (such as via [[Spells/Project Image|_project image_]]) or possessing another creature, even if its body is elsewhere, though your eidolon must know about the possession or projection and choose to do so.

 Finally, the spirit of an undead destroyed by your eidolon's Strikes is sent directly to the [[Setting/Planes/Boneyard]] for judgment; an undead with an ability to avoid such a fate automatically, such as a [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghost's]] rejuvenation, can attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against your spell DC to return via that ability as normal. In that case, it uses its Will save modifier as its counteract modifier.
