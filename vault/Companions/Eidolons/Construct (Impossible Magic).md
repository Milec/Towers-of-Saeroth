# Construct  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Astral (GM Core)|Astral]], [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|Construct]], [[Traits/Eidolon (Impossible Magic)|Eidolon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 73

Your eidolon is a mental construct based on an astral thoughtform and given physical presence and life by their connection to you, their shape limited only by your imagination. Your eidolon's appearance and physical form vary based on your shared vision for their construction, from clockworks to stuffed dolls and everything between, and it's not uncommon for that appearance to change greatly as your construct gains evolutions. Because they arise from an astral entity, your construct is no mindless servitor, but a fully thinking being with their own ideas, goals, and even emotions. These entities are extremely diverse; while many construct eidolons come from a powerful symbiotic connection with astral denizens, just as many arise from the forgotten memories of ancient empires and craftworks drifting across the Silver Sea of the Astral Plane.

## Construct Heart

Your construct eidolon has a link directly to your life force, which renders them a living creature and therefore susceptible to many ailments that bother only the living, though they do possess some resistances to these effects. They don't have a construct's normal immunities, but they do gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] effects, as well as effects causing the [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] conditions. Additionally, their astral essence bleeds off slowly. The flat check to remove [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] from the eidolon is DC 10 (or DC 5 after receiving particularly effective aid).

## Reconfigured Evolution  *7th*

Your construct eidolon is particularly easy to reconfigure, gaining an additional evolution that suits both of your whims. Choose one additional evolution feat of 6th level or lower. Your eidolon gains that feat. You can Retrain this feat for any other evolution feat of 6th level or lower with only a single day of downtime if you succeed at a Crafting check with a standard DC of your eidolon's level. If you fail, you can try again on a later day. Many summoners choose [[Feats/Eidolon's Wrath (Impossible Magic)|Eidolon's Wrath]] for this ability.

## Ultimate Reconfiguration  *17th*

Your construct eidolon becomes even more customizable. Choose an evolution feat of 16th level or lower. Your eidolon gains that feat. You can spend downtime to adjust it using Crafting, just like the feat from reconfigured evolution. Many summoners select [[Feats/Ever-Vigilant Senses (Impossible Magic)|Ever-Vigilant Senses]] for this ability.
