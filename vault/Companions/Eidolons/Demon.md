# Demon  *Summoner Eidolon*

**Traits** [[Traits/Demon]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Fiend]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 62

Your eidolon is a [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|demon]], born of mortal sin congealed amid the chaos and evil of the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]], using its link to you to spread chaos at your side. Choose an associated sin for your demon. Demon eidolons have appearances as varied as the infinite Abyss. While demons are inherently untrustworthy, your demon eidolon has reached an accord with you and generally keeps it, though that doesn't mean your eidolon isn't actively working to bring your life deeper into its associated sin. Though a true demon, your demon eidolon's link to you as a mortal prevents them from casting the [[Rituals/Abyssal Pact|_abyssal pact_]] ritual, even if they somehow learn it.

## Demonic Strikes

Your eidolon's attacks are corrupted by the [[Setting/Planes/Abyss]] and imbued with pure evil and shifting chaos. Your eidolon's [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strikes gain the unholy trait and deal 1 extra spirit damage to holy creatures and creatures with weakness to unholy. Additionally, choose one of your eidolon's unarmed attacks that deals physical damage; it gains your choice of [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile B]], versatile P, or versatile S.

## Visions of Sin  *7th*

Your demon is born of sin and can project the sin it is formed from into the minds of others. It gains the Visions of Sin activity.

## Blasphemous Decree  *17th*

Your demon utters indescribable words writhing with its associated sin. It can cast an evil [[Spells/Divine Decree|_divine decree_]] once per day as a 9th-level spell. You are unaffected by this decree, even if you aren't evil. At 19th level, this becomes a 10th-level _divine decree_ instead.
