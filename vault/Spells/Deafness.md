# Deafness two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 327

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

The target loses hearing; it must attempt a Fortitude save. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is deafened for 10 minutes.  

**Critical Failure** The target is deafened permanently.
