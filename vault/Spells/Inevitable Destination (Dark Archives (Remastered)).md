# Inevitable Destination two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 143

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** you and 1 enemy

**Duration** 2 rounds

---

You twist the paths of the target, ensuring that each one leads to you. The target attempts a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The spell has no effect.  

**Success** Your pull hampers the target’s footsteps. It takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The target can't move any farther from you than it was when you Cast the Spell, though it can move in such a way that it maintains an equal distance. If you move farther away from the target than the initial distance, the spell ends. The targeted foe can attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] against your spell DC. If it succeeds, the spell ends.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the target is pulled 10 feet closer to you and falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], tripping in its haste to move toward you.
