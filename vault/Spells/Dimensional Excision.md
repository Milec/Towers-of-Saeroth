# Dimensional Excision two actions  *Spell 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -212- A Voice in the Blight|Pathfinder #212: A Voice in the Blight]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will

---

You manipulate the boundaries between planes like a scalpel, using it to cut away portions of a creature’s essence and banishing those portions to other realities and dimensions, afflicting the target deep lacerations or even severed appendages. The creature takes 14d10 damage (no damage type) and 2d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] and must attempt a Will save. If the target is not on its home plane, it takes a –4 status penalty to this save. A creature reduced to 0 HP has their entire body sectioned out and banished across multiple planes and dimensions, leaving nothing behind but their gear.

---
**Heightened (10th)** The base damage increases by 2d10, and the persistent bleed damage by 1d10.
