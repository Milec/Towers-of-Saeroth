# Stagnate Time two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 182

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Atropos]], [[Setting/Deities/Charg]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You examine the time stream in a particular area, then proceed to stick a few metaphorical pins into it, thickening and slowing down the flow of time until it rumbles along like a river of molasses. Any creature that begins its turn in the area must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] this turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is slowed 2 this turn.
