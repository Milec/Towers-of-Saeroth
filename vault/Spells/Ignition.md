# Ignition two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 336

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Diabolic (Player Core 2)|Diabolic]]

**Mysteries**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ash (Divine Mysteries)|Ash]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames (Player Core 2)|Flames]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You snap your fingers and point at a target, which begins to smolder. Make a spell attack roll against the target's AC, dealing 2d4 fire damage on a hit. If the target is within your melee reach, you can choose to make a melee spell attack with the flame instead of a ranged spell attack, which increases all the spell's damage dice to d6s.

**Critical Success** The target takes double damage and 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage.]]  

**Success** The target takes full damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 1d4 and the persistent fire damage on a critical hit increases by 1d4.
