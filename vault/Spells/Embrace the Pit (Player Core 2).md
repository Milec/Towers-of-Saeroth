# Embrace the Pit single action  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 263

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Diabolic (Player Core 2)|Diabolic]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Devil horns grow from your skull, and your skin takes on features of the devil responsible for your diabolic bloodline. You gain resistance 5 to fire and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and resistance 1 to physical damage (except [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance to fire and poison increase by 5, and the resistance to physical damage (except silver) by 2.
