# Crown of Prophets single action  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 83

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Serpents sprout from your head, equally ready to bite your foes or offer you counsel. You gain a fangs unarmed attack that deals 1d6 piercing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile poison]] traits. The first time you Cast or Sustain this spell each round, you can perform one of the following as a free action.

- **Serpentine Advice** The snakes compete to offer you counsel. You gain the [[Feats/Dubious Knowledge (Player Core)|Dubious Knowledge]] feat until the end of your turn, then you [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]].
- **Toxic Prophacy** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Requirements** A creature within 15 feet damaged you since the end of your last turn; **Effect** The snakes spit toxic curses at the required creature, dealing 2d4 poison damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The poison damage dealt by Toxic Prophecy increases by 1d4.
