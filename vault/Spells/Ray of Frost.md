# Ray of Frost two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 362

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

---

You blast an icy ray. Make a spell attack roll. The ray deals 2d4 cold damage.

**Critical Success** The target takes double damage and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds for 1 round.  

**Success** The target takes normal damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
