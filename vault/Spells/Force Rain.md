# Force Rain single action to three actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 139

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** varies

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You conjure a magical cloud that batters creatures with shards of solidified magic. Creatures in the spell's area take force damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the area and other parameters.

 single action This spell affects a single 5-foot square and deals 4d6 force damage.  
 two actions This spell affects all squares in a 10-foot burst and deals 8d6 force damage.  
 three actions The shards home in on creatures. This spell affects all squares in a 10-foot burst. Creatures in the area don't attempt a saving throw and instead automatically take 20 force damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 for the 1-action version, by 2d6 for the 2-action version, and by 5 for the 3-action version.
