# Dance of Darkness two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 193

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** varies

---

You dance, Striding up to half your Speed. At either the start or end of your Stride, you create an area of magical darkness in a 10-foot burst centered on your location. This area is filled with magical darkness that works the same way as that created by a [[Spells/Darkness|_darkness_]] spell of the same level. When you create the darkness, attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to dance, with a standard DC for your level, to determine the duration of the darkness.

**Critical Success** 1 minute  

**Success** 2 rounds  

**Failure** 1 round
