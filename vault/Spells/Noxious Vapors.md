# Noxious Vapors two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 222

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Garhaazh]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 round

---

You emit a cloud of toxic smoke that temporarily obscures you from sight. Each creature except you in the area when you Cast the Spell takes 1d6 poison damage (basic Fortitude save). A creature that critically fails the saving throw also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]]. All creatures in the area become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and all creatures outside the smoke become concealed to creatures within it. This smoke can be dispersed by a strong wind.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
