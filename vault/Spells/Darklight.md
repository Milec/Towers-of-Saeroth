# Darklight three actions  *Focus 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 227

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Shadows pour forth from your eyes, creating a field of [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]] that prevents light from emanating within the area. Light doesn't enter the area; any non-magical light sources, such as a [[Equipment/Torch|torches]] or [[Equipment/Lantern|lanterns]], don't emanate any light while inside the area, even if their light radius would extend beyond the darkness. The spell similarly suppresses magical [[Traits/Light|light]] of the spell's level or lower.

 Light can't pass through, so creatures in the area can't see outside. Even creatures with [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]] (but not greater darkvision) can barely see through the darkness; any creatures seen through the darkness are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to them. Creatures with greater darkvision can see through the darkness normally.

 Creatures who move into the field or start their turn within the field have some of the life and light sucked out of them, taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 negative damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save.

When you cast this spell, choose up to four allies in the area to spare from _darklight's_ effects. Until the spell ends, they're immune to damage from _darklight_ spells and can see through _darklight_ (but not other darkness) as though they had greater darkvision.

---
**Heightened (10th)** The bludgeoning and negative damage both increase to 3d6.
