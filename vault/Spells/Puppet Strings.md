# Puppet Strings two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 158

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

Ethereal threads emanate from your fingertips and flow toward the target, connecting to its arms, legs, and body. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The strings impede the target's movements, but you can't control them. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Failure** The target is clumsy 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]]. Additionally, when you Cast this Spell and the first time you Sustain it on subsequent turns, you can force the target to Stride or Strike. If you force the target to Stride, you determine the path it takes; you can't force it to Stride into a space where it can't stand easily. This is forced movement. If you cause the creature to Strike, it makes a melee Strike against a creature within its reach using your spell attack modifier. The target can't hit itself this way. On a hit, the Strike deals 7d6 bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, as appropriate. None of the target's equipment or abilities modify this Strike. This Strike contributes to and uses your multiple attack penalty. At the end of its turn, the target can attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC to free itself from the threads. On a success, the spell ends.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is slowed 2, and you can force the target to use 2 actions when you Cast or Sustain the spell.
