**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**

---

You call upon the Void to harm life force. The target takes 2d4 void damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] until the start of your next turn.

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
