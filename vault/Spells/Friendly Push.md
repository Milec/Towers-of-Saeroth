# Friendly Push single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 148

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You exert magical force to propel a willing creature up to 10 feet in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]], including upward, though if they aren’t on solid ground or have another way to maintain their height (such as a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]]) when the movement ends, they fall. When you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell, you can move them again or choose a new target within range and move them instead.You can cast this spell on an [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] ally, and if you do, the movement from this spell doesn’t trigger reactions.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The distance increases to 20 feet.

**Heightened (7th)** The distance increases to 30 feet.
