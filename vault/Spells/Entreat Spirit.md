# Entreat Spirit single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 295

**Requirements** You must be Bonded with a Spirit

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You invite a spirit you’ve bonded with to bolster your mind. You gain a +1 status bonus to an Intelligence-, Wisdom-, or Charisma-based skill of your choice. Additionally, you gain a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|curse]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], and [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effects.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The status bonus to the skill and your saving throws increases to +2.

**Heightened (8th)** The status bonus to the skill and your saving throws increases to +3.
