# Warshard Shot two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 171

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You conjure a razor-sharp shard of scrap metal and imbue it with spiritual echoes of Gorum’s death before launching it at a foe. Make a spell attack roll against the target. The warshard deals 5d6 piercing damage and 5d6 spirit damage, depending on your ranged spell attack roll against the target’s AC.

**Critical Success** The target takes double damage and 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent spirit damage]]. As long as the target is taking this persistent spirit damage, it takes a –1 status penalty to its AC.  

**Success** The target takes full damage.  

**Failure** The target takes the spirit damage, but not the piercing damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target is unaffected.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage and initial spirit damage each increase by 1d6. The persistent spirit damage on a critical hit increases by 1.
