# Heart's Desire two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 236

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Genie]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You surround your target with wondrous illusions of their greatest desires, which distract them from reality. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target disbelieves the illusion and is unaffected.  

**Success** For 1 round, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] and takes a –2 status penalty to any checks it makes to determine the effects of reactions.  

**Failure** For 1 minute, the target is stupefied 2 and can't use reactions.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and all spaces are difficult terrain for the target.

---
**Heightened (+2)** You can target one additional creature.
