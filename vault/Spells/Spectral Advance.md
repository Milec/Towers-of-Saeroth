# Spectral Advance single action or two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 256

---

Taking on a spiritual form, you flash across the battlefield to engage an enemy. You Stride to a space adjacent to an enemy. If you cast the spell using 2 actions, you can Stride twice instead of once. If you have a fly Speed, you can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] instead of Striding. If you're mounted, you can have your mount move instead of you. Movement from _spectral advance_ doesn't trigger reactions and ignores [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and greater difficult terrain. During the movement, you (or your mount) have resistance equal to your level to all damage.
