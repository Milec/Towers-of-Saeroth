# Blood Runs Cold two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 125

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You chill the blood in the target's veins. The target takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent cold damage]] with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the persistent cold damage.  

**Failure** The target takes the full persistent cold damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double the persistent cold damage and is slowed 1 until the persistent cold damage ends.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the persistent cold damage by 1d6.
