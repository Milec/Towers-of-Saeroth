# Lignify two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 197

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature made of flesh

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You turn the target's flesh slowly into wood. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. Creatures with the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait have a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against this spell.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is slowed 1 and must attempt a Fortitude save at the end of each of its turns; this ongoing save has the [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait. On a failed save, the slowed condition increases by 1 (or 2 on a critical failure). A successful save reduces the slowed condition by 1. When a creature is unable to act due to the slowed condition from _lignify_, the creature is permanently non-magically [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified|petrified]], though it's turned to wood instead of stone. The spell ends if the creature is magically petrified or the slowed condition is removed.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is initially slowed 2.
