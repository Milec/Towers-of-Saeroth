# Sanctified Ground  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Consecration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 366

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** 1 minute ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Cost** 1 vial of _holy water_

**Area** 30-foot burst centered on you

**Duration** until the next time you make your daily preparations.

---

You sanctify the area, sprinkling it with _[[Equipment/Holy Water|holy water]]_ and warding it against your foes. Choose [[Traits/Aberration|aberrations]], [[Traits/Celestial|celestials]], [[Traits/Dragon|dragons]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiends]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitors]], or [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. All creatures in the area gain a +1 status bonus to AC, attack rolls, damage rolls, and saving throws against the chosen creatures.
