# Etheric Shards two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Force]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 105

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You reach out and sow glittering splinters of force throughout the area, otherworldly caltrops of will and ectoplasm. The area is difficult terrain, even to flying or [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures. Any creature that moves through the area takes 1d4 piercing damage per square traversed, with a basic Reflex save made at the end of each move action they take through the area.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The spell's area increases by 5 feet, and the piercing damage increases by 1.
