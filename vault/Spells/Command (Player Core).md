# Command two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 321

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Patron Themes**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Faith's Flamekeeper]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/The Unseen Broker]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/The Unseen Broker (Dark Archives (Remastered))|The Unseen Broker]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** until the end of the target's next turn

---

You shout a command that's hard to ignore. You can command the target to approach you, run away (as if it had the [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] condition), release what it's holding, drop [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]], or stand in place. It can't [[Actions/Delay]] or take any reactions until it has obeyed your command. The effects depend on the target's Will save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature must spend the first action on its next turn to obey your command.  

**Critical Failure** The target must use all its actions on its next turn to obey your command.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
