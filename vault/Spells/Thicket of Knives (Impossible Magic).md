# Thicket of Knives two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 167

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create numerous phantom copies of your weapon arm, hiding your true movements and rendering your attacks unpredictable. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks. If you’re untrained in Deception, you can use the [[Actions/Feint (Player Core)|Feint]] action anyway as well as add your level as your proficiency bonus despite being untrained.
