# Ode to Ouroboros ⤾  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 371

**Trigger** A creature’s dying condition would reach the value at which they would die

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** the triggering creature

---

Your ode staves off death. The target's [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition remains 1 below the value at which it dies. This doesn't help prevent death from effects that kill the target without increasing its dying condition, such as [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] and [[Traits/Death|death]] effects.
