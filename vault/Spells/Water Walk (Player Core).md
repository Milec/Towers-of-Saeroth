# Water Walk two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 369

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

The target can walk on the surface of water and other liquids without falling through. It can go underwater if it wishes, but in that case it must [[Actions/Swim]] normally. This spell doesn't grant the ability to breathe underwater.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The spell's range increases to 30 feet, the duration increases to 1 hour, and you can target up to 10 creatures.
