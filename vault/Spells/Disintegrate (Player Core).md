# Disintegrate two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 325

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Imperial (Player Core 2)|Imperial]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Kerkamoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Ongalte]], [[Setting/Deities/Pahti Coatl]], [[Setting/Deities/Rovagug (Divine Mysteries)|Rovagug]], [[Setting/Deities/Set (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Set]], [[Setting/Deities/Smiad]], [[Setting/Deities/Sorrow's Sword]], [[Setting/Deities/The Devourer]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature, unattended object, or force construct

**Defense**
AC and [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

A black tracer bolt flies toward your target, and upon making contact intensifies into a powerful destructive beam. Make a spell attack against the target. If you hit an object or force construct (such as a [[Spells/Wall of Force|_wall of force_]]), it's destroyed with no save unless it's an [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]] or similarly powerful. A single casting can destroy no more than a 10-foot cube of matter.

If you hit a creature, it takes 12d10 damage (no damage type) with a basic Fortitude save. If you critically hit, the target gets a result one degree of success worse than the outcome of its Fortitude save. A creature reduced to 0 HP is blasted to fine powder; its gear remains.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d10.
