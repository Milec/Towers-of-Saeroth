# Pernicious Poltergeist two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 121

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 10-foot burst

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You create an echo of a powerful poltergeist, temporarily anchoring it to the area to terrorize and harm any opponents foolish enough to remain in the area. Because the poltergeist is a manifestation and is not truly present, it can't be attacked or hurt; it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] unless otherwise stated. When you Cast the Spell, and the first time you Sustain it on subsequent rounds, the poltergeist creates your choice of one of the following effects.

- **Deathly Assault** ([[Traits/Negative|negative]]) The poltergeist focuses on one creature in the area and flies through that creature's space, dealing 4d10 negative damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save).
- **Frighten** ([[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) The poltergeist becomes visible, appearing as a skeletal, ghostlike humanoid. Creatures in the area must attempt a Will save, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] on a failure.
- **Telekinetic Storm** ([[Traits/Force|force]]) The poltergeist hurls debris and quasi-real objects around in the area. All creatures in the area take 4d8 force damage (basic Reflex save).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage of the deathly assault effect increases by 1d10, and the damage of the telekinetic storm effect increases by 1d8.
