# Blistering Invective two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 241

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Angradd (Divine Mysteries)|Angradd]], [[Setting/Deities/Mephistopheles (Divine Mysteries)|Mephistopheles]], [[Setting/Deities/Ydajisk (Divine Mysteries)|Ydajisk]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

A heap of insults and invectives spew from your mouth—words so devastating your foes burn from the intensity of your diatribe. Your words deal 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]], and the target must attempt a Will save. If the target doesn't understand the language or you're not speaking a language, it gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the persistent fire damage.  

**Failure** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] and takes the full persistent fire damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target becomes frightened 2 and takes double the persistent fire damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** You can target two additional creatures, and the persistent damage increases by 2d6.
