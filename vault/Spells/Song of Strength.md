# Song of Strength single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 228

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 60-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 round

---

You bolster your allies' physical strength with a hearty exhortation. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks and to their DCs against Athletics skill actions such as [[Actions/Disarm]], [[Actions/Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip]].

**Special** If you have the [[Spells/Inspire Heroics|_inspire heroics_]] composition spell, you can use that composition to improve the bonus granted by _song of strength_ in the same way as [[Spells/Inspire Courage|_inspire courage_]] or [[Spells/Inspire Defense|_inspire defense_]].
