# Loose Time's Arrow two actions  *Spell 2*

**Target** up to 6 creatures

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You pluck the time stream like a bow—pull one string back, release, and watch a creature fly. All affected targets are [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]]. They can use the extra action only to Step or Stride.
