# Roar of the Wyrm two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 119

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Wyrmkin]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Target** 30-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You channel the might of dragons into your voice, letting out a roar that engenders respect in dragonkind but that instills fear in most other creatures. The impressive roar grants you a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] checks for 10 minutes against dragons that were in the area at the time of casting. All enemies within the area other than dragons must attempt a Will save; to these enemies, _roar of the wyrm_ is a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect.  

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for 1 round.

 _Roar of the wyrm_ affects non-dragon creatures with deep ties to dragonkind (such as a barbarian with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Dragon|draconic instinct]], a sorcerer with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic|draconic bloodline]], or a member of a culture that reveres dragons) as if they had the [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] trait. The GM decides if a creature is aligned enough with dragonkind to be affected in this way.
