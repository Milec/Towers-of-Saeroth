# Blanket of Stars two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 241

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/The Lady of the North Star]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

A flowing cloak of utter darkness drapes over you, filled with pinpricks of light like distant stars. It imparts the stillness of the cosmos to you, granting you a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]]. While outside under a starry night sky, you're also [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] as long as you remain still. When moving under a starry night sky, you're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] instead.

 Gazing too closely into the stars is disorienting. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save; this is a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effect.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] and dazzled until the
end of its next turn.
