# Adapt Self two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 262

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Change (Divine Mysteries)|Change]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You make subtle changes to your body to adapt to the situation. Choose one of the following options that applies to you.

- If you are in water, you gain a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]] equal to half your Speed.
- If you are in water, you become able to breathe water.
- If it’s too dark for you to see, you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]].
- If you are in an environment of [[Rules/Temperature (GM Core)|severe heat]] or severe cold, you are protected from the effects of severe heat or severe cold, and the spell lasts 10 minutes.
