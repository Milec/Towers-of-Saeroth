# Crimson Breath two actions  *Spell 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Prey for Death

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 99

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You turn a creature toward you and exhale a blast of crimson mist from your mouth, exposing the target to a toxic miasma. The effects are determined by the creature’s Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 8d6 poison damage.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with [[Equipment/Breath of the Mantis God|breath of the mantis god]] poison at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with breath of the mantis god poison at stage 2.
