# Gravitational Pull single action to three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 142

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Liisglan]], [[Setting/Deities/Telastmar]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

By suddenly altering gravity, you pull the target toward you. The target is pulled 10 feet closer to you unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. On a critical failure, it's also knocked prone. The effects of this spell change depending on the number of actions you spend when you Cast this Spell.

 single action The spell targets one creature.  
 two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The spell targets one creature and pulls the target 20 feet instead of 10.  
 three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The spell targets up to five creatures.
