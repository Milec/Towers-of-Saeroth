# Wall of Radiance three actions  *Spell 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -172- Secrets of the Temple City|Pathfinder #172: Secrets of the Temple City]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Sturovenen (Divine Mysteries)|Sturovenen]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You form a wall of brilliant light that obscures creatures. You create a thin wall of light in a straight line up to 60 feet long and 10 feet high. You must create the wall in an unbroken open space so its edges don't pass through any creatures or objects, or the spell is lost. The wall stands vertically. If you wish, the wall can be of a shorter length or height. Creatures adjacent to the wall are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]]. Creatures that move through the wall take 2d6 fire damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) as the concentrated light burns them.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The bright light makes it difficult to discern creatures on the other side. Creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to other creatures on the opposite side of the wall from them. The fire damage increases to 3d6.
