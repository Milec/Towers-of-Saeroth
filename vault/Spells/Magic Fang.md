# Magic Fang two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 349

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Choose one of the target's [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks. You cause that unarmed attack to shine with primal energy. The unarmed attack becomes a _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]]_ unarmed attack, gaining a +1 item bonus to attack rolls and increasing the number of damage dice to two if it had only one.
