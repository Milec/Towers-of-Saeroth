# Dim the Light ⤾  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 236

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Shadow]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Trigger** You cast a [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] or [[Traits/Shadow|shadow]] spell.

---

You magnify the darkness from the triggering effect, causing the ambient light level to flicker briefly so you can slip from sight. You can immediately attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Hide]], and you can become [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to a creature even if you don't have cover and aren't otherwise [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] from that creature. You cease being hidden at the end of your turn unless you move into cover or become concealed before then.
