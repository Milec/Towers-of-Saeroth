# Scouring Pulse two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 94

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot radius, 60-foot-tall cylinder

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

You inundate the area with concentrated positive energy, which scours away the stain of undeath. Each creature in the area takes 6d8 positive damage and must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. Creatures that fail the save also take 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent positive damage]]. Creatures that take persistent positive damage from _scouring pulse_ are wreathed in a heatless halo of light. They emit bright light in a 5-foot radius preventing them from being [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]; if the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], they're concealed while affected by scouring pulse, rather than being [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]].

---
**Heightened (8th)**  The positive damage increases to 9d8 damage, and the persistent positive damage increases to 2d8. Creatures remain suffused in light for 1d4 additional rounds after removing the persistent positive damage.
