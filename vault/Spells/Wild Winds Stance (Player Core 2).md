# Wild Winds Stance single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 257

**Duration** until you leave the stance

---

You take on the stance of the flowing winds, sending out waves of energy at a distance. You can make wind crash unarmed Strikes as ranged Strikes against targets within 30 feet. These deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. Wind crash Strikes ignore [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] and all [[Rules/Cover|cover]].

 While in wild winds stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks.
