# Murderous Vine two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 154

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature adjacent to a flat surface

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You summon a slithering, thorny vine that attempts to constrict and crush a foe against a wall or other surface. Make a ranged spell attack roll against the target’s Fortitude DC. On a success, the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] and takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d8 piercing damage. At the end of that creature’s turn, if it’s still grabbed by the vine, it takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage. The vine’s [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] DC is equal to your spell DC. A creature can attack the vine and attempt to break its grip. The vine’s AC is equal to your spell DC, and the vine is destroyed if it takes 20 or more damage. Destroying or escaping from the vines ends the spell. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial bludgeoning and piercing damage each increase by 1d8, and the bludgeoning damage a creature takes for ending its turn grabbed by the vine increases by 1d6.
