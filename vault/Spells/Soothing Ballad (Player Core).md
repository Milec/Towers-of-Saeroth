# Soothing Ballad two actions  *Focus 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 371

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** you and up to 9 allies

---

You draw upon your muse to soothe your allies. Choose one of the following three effects:

- The spell attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effects on the targets.
- The spell attempts to counteract effects imposing [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralysis]] on the targets.
- The spell restores 7d8 Hit Points to the targets.

---
**Heightened (+1)** When used to heal, _soothing ballad_ restores 1d8 more Hit Points.
