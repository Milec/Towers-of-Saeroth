# Ray of Corruption two actions  *Spell 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Spore War

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fungus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -211- The Secret of Deathstalk Tower|Pathfinder #211: The Secret of Deathstalk Tower]] pg. 75

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** one creature or object

**Defense**
AC and [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You unleash a sickly gray beam of toxic spores at your target. Make a [[Rules/Spell Attacks (Player Core)|spell attack]] against the target. If you hit a non-magical object that’s made of organic material (such as a tree, wooden house, or massive skull), it melts away into a foul-smelling sludge. A single casting can destroy no more than a 10-foot cube of matter.

If you hit a creature, it takes 6d12 [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage and 6d12 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Fortitude save. If you critically hit, the target gets a result one degree of success worse than the outcome of its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage. Fungal tendrils swiftly digest the body and reduce it to sludge—the target takes 2d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]].  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target takes double damage, plus 4d12 persistent acid damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The poison damage and spirit damage each increase by 1d12.
