# Manifest Will single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Hex (Player Core)|Hex]], [[Traits/Subtle]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 63

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You unleash energy from the broken connection to your patron. You are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from creatures beyond the edge of the emanation but can't use that concealment to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]]. Any creature that begins its turn in the emanation is affected based on your patron's tradition.

- **Arcane** Raw energy and magical formulae circulate around you. A creature that begins its turn in the emanation gains [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 1 to spell damage for 1 round.

- **Divine** Divine power crashes in a cycle of life and death. A living or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature that begins its turn in the emanation gains 2 temporary Hit Points for 1 round. This effect has the [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] trait.

- **Occult** Esoteric symbology etches the air. An allied creature that begins its turn in the emanation has [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|lesser cover]] while inside the emanation, and for 1 round after it leaves.

- **Primal**[[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plants]] and [[Traits/Fungus (Player Core)|fungus]] symbolic of your patron constantly grow and wither in the emanation. A creature that begins its turn in the emanation has a –10-foot status penalty to all its Speeds for 1 round or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]]. This effect has the fungus, plant, and [[Traits/Wood|wood]] traits.

---
**Heightened (+1)** An arcane manifestation increases its weakness by 1 and a divine manifestation increases its temporary Hit Points by 2.
