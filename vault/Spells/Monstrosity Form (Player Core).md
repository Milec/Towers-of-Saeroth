# Monstrosity Form two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 344

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apep]], [[Setting/Deities/Hadregash]], [[Setting/Deities/Iapholi]], [[Setting/Deities/Oaur-Ooung]], [[Setting/Deities/Vorasha]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You transform into the shape of a legendary monster, assuming a Huge battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose phoenix, cave worm, or sea serpent. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Beast|beast]] trait (for phoenix) or the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait (for cave worm or sea serpent). You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 20 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +28, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +30, unless your own is higher.

 You gain specific abilities based on the monster you choose:

- **Phoenix** Speed 30 feet, fly 90 feet; **Melee** single action beak ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 piercing plus 2d4 fire and 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire]]; **Melee** single action talon ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8+12 slashing; **Shroud of Flame** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]]) 20 feet. You gain an aura of fire that extends out from you. A creature that enters or ends its turn within the aura takes 2d6 fire damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn. You can activate or deactivate this aura with a Sustain action.
- **Cave Worm** Speed 40 feet, burrow 30 feet, swim 20 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+20 piercing; Melee single action stinger ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), Damage 2d8+15 piercing plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison]]; Melee single action body ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]) Damage 2d8+20 bludgeoning; **Inexorable** You automatically recover from the [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] conditions at the end of each of your turns. You're also immune to being [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain]].
- **Sea Serpent** Speed 20 feet, swim 90 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+20 piercing; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8+20 bludgeoning; **Spine Rake** two actions ([[Traits/Move|move]]) You extend your spines and Swim or Stride. Each creature you're adjacent to at any point during your movement takes 4d8+10 slashing damage ([[Rules/Perception for Initiative|basic]] Reflex against your spell DC).

---
**Heightened (9th)** You instead gain AC = 22 + your level, 25 temporary HP, attack modifier +31, increase damage by one damage die, and Athletics +33.
