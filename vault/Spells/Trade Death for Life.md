# Trade Death for Life single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 297

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Paradox of Opposites]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron steals life from one of your enemies to grant it to another. The target takes 1d4 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage (basic Fortitude save). If the target takes damage, a willing creature within the hex’s range gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 1 for as long as you [[Actions/Sustain]] the hex. The target takes damage only once from this spell, whether or not you Sustain it.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4 and the fast healing increases by 1.
