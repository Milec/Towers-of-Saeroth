# Connective Current two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 enemy

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a magical connection between you and an enemy, as if you were both caught in the same current. When the target takes a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, you can [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]] up to half your Speed in the same direction as the target as a reaction.

---
**Heightened (4th)** You can Stride up to your Speed.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th, and you gain an additional reaction when you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]] and at the start of each of your turns that you can use only to make this movement.
