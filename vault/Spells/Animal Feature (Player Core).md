# Animal Feature single action  *Focus 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 383

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Without fully transforming your body, you gain one animalistic feature, which you select from the list below each time you Cast the Spell.

- **Cat Eyes** You gain [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]].
- **Claws** You gain a claw attack that deals 1d6 slashing damage and has the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.
- **Jaws** You gain a jaws attack that deals 1d8 piercing damage and has the [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] trait.

---
**Heightened (4th)** Add the following options to the list.

- **Fish Tail** You gain a swim Speed equal to your land Speed.
- **Owl Eyes** You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
- **Wings** You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed.
