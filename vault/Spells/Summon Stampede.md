# Summon Stampede three actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 87

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Defense**
Will and [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex (see text)

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You summon an unstoppable stampede of panicking beasts. Whether you conjure wild beasts or tamed cattle, the stampede is a force of nature that leaves behind nothing in its wake. The stampede occupies the space of a Gargantuan creature and has a Speed of 60 feet.

**Arrive** ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) _Foreboding Tremors_ The sheer energy of a stampede can cause even apex predators to panic. Each creature within a 60-foot emanation must attempt a Will save with the following effects.  
**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  
**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  
**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.

**Depart** _Flatten the Earth_ The stampede Strides up to double its Speed, trampling each Large or smaller creature, hazard, and structure whose space it enters, dealing 8d8 bludgeoning damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). The stampede ignores and attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] all [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] it enters caused by debris, overgrowth, rubble, or thick ground cover.
