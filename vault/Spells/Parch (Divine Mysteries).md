# Parch two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 265

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Dust (Divine Mysteries)|Dust]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

Dry winds dehydrate a creature. The target must attempt a Fortitude save; if used on a creature that has the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait or a creature made primarily of liquid (such as many [[Bestiary/Families/Ooze (Monster Core)|oozes]]), the creature uses the outcome for one degree of success worse than the result. Creatures whose bodies contain no significant moisture (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Earth (Monster Core)|earth elementals]]) are immune to parch. If a creature affected by _parch_ drinks a liquid, such as a swig of water or a potion, the spell ends. Affected creatures that have the water trait or are made primarily of liquid, as well as affected creatures that can’t drink, can end the effect as a two-action activity, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes a –1 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target takes a –1 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes a –2 status penalty to its checks and DCs for 1 minute and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] as long as it has this penalty.

---
**Heightened (4th)** You can target up to 4 creatures.
