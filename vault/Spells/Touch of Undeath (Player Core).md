# Touch of Undeath single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 380

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Undeath (Player Core)|Undeath]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You attack the target's life force with undeath, dealing 1d6 void damage. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage, and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effects heal it only half as much as normal for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, and vitality effects heal it only half as much as normal for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
