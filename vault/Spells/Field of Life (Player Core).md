# Field of Life two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 331

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Renewal (Player Core)|Lesson of Renewal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

A field of life energy fills the area, exuding warmth and rejuvenating those within. Each living creature that starts its turn in the area regains 1d8 Hit Points, and any [[Traits/Undead|undead]] creature that starts its turn in the area takes 1d8 vitality damage.

---
**Heightened (8th)** The healing and damage increase to 1d10.

**Heightened (9th)** The healing and damage increase to 1d12.
