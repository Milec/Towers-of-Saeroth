# Spell Riposte ⤾  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 252

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Trigger** You’re the target of a spell you’re aware of.

---

You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the triggering spell. If the spell would be counteracted, it instead continues with the caster as a target instead of you. _Spell riposte_ can't affect spells that aren't targeted (such as area spells).
