# Final Sacrifice two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 245

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature with the  minion  trait that you summoned or permanently control

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You channel disruptive energies through the bond between you and your minion, causing it to violently explode. The target is immediately slain, and the explosion deals 6d6 fire damage to creatures within 20 feet of it with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. If the target has the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] or [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait, the spell deals cold damage and has the cold trait instead of the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait. Attempting to cast this spell targeting a creature that you temporarily seized control of, such as an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] commanded by [[Spells/Bind Undead (Player Core)|_bind undead_]], automatically fails and breaks the controlling effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
