# Shifting Sand three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 259

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Anubis (Divine Mysteries)|Anubis]], [[Setting/Deities/Yamatsumi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Yamatsumi]], [[Setting/Deities/Zipacna]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** a horizontal earthen or sandy surface within a 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You cause the surface to heave. The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (GM Core)|difficult terrain]], and any tracks in the area are destroyed by the churning ground. Creatures standing in the area take a –1 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance (Player Core)|Balance]] and [[Actions/Tumble Through (Player Core)|Tumble Through]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/High Jump (Player Core)|High Jump]] and [[Actions/Long Jump (Player Core)|Long Jump]]. The first time each round you [[Actions/Sustain]] the Spell, you can move the churning area up to 20 feet in any direction. Creatures immobilized by the spell are carried along with the shifting sand in the same direction, if possible. Creatures that enter or begin their turn standing in the shifting sand must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and ignores the area’s difficult terrain and penalty to skill checks until the end of its turn.  

**Success** The creature ignores the area’s penalty to skill checks until the end of its turn.  

**Failure** The creature is affected normally by the spell this turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] within the spell’s area until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]]. If the creature was already immobilized by shifting sand, it also falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

---
**Heightened (5th)** The status penalty increases to –2, and the spell’s range increases to 60 feet.

**Heightened (7th)** The status penalty increases to –3, the spell’s range increases to 60 feet, and the spell’s area increases to a 30-foot burst.

**Heightened (9th)** The status penalty increases to –4, the spell’s range increases to 60 feet, and the spell’s area increases to a 40-foot burst
