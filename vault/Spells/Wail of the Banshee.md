# Wail of the Banshee two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 40-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

Your scream chills the souls of enemies that hear it. Each living enemy in the area takes 8d10 negative damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes full damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1d4]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is drained 4.
