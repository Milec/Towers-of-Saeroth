# Doom Mark two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 133

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You launch a dart of dark void energy that imprints itself on the target, connecting the fates of those marked. Whenever you Cast this Spell and the first time you [[Actions/Sustain]] this spell on subsequent turns, make a ranged spell attack against the AC of a creature within 60 feet. On a hit, you deal 6d8 void damage (or double damage on a critical hit) and place a mark on their body for the duration of the spell. A marked creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]]. When a marked creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points, all other marked creatures become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
