# Radiant Globe three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 53

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Andoran have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You create a dome of brilliant light that destroys projectiles attempting to pass through it. Ammunition from physical ranged attacks—such as [[Equipment/Weapons/Arrows (Player Core)|arrows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Bolts (Player Core)|bolts]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling Bullets (Player Core)|sling bullets]], and other objects of similar size—is destroyed in a flash of light when it passes into or out of the globe’s area. Attackers targeting creatures or objects through the globe’s surface with physical ranged attacks must succeed at a Fortitude save or become dazzled for 1 round (blinded for 1 round on a critical failure).
