# Gravitational Pull single action to three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 109

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Liisglan]], [[Setting/Deities/Telastmar]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

By suddenly altering gravity, you pull the target toward you. The target is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 10 feet closer to you unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. On a critical failure, it's also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The effects of this spell change depending on the number of actions you spend when you Cast this Spell.

single action **(somatic)** The spell targets one creature.  
two actions **(somatic, verbal)** The spell targets one creature and pulls the target 20 feet instead of 10.  
three actions **(material, somatic, verbal)** The spell targets up to 5 creatures.
