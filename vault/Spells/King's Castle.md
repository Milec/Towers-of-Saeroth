# King's Castle two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 149

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Kyonin have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

---

You and a willing creature swap places. You appear in the target’s former space, and the target appears in your former space. You and your target must each be able to fit in the new spaces within range; otherwise, the spell fails.
