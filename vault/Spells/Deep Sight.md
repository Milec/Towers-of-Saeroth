# Deep Sight two actions  *Spell 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Divination]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -194- Cult of the Cave Worm|Pathfinder #194: Cult of the Cave Worm]] pg. 79

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

Your eyes darken until they become entirely pitch black. You grant yourself the ability to see in the darkness. You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]]. If you already have darkvision, you gain greater darkvision.

---
**Heightened (7th)** The duration is until the next time you make daily preparations.
