Divine protection helps protect your companions. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to AC while within the emanation. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to increase the emanation’s radius by 10 feet. Benediction can counteract [[Spells/Malediction|_malediction_]].
