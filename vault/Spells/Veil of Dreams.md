# Veil of Dreams single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 239

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Dreams]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You draw the target into a drowsy state, causing daydreams and sluggishness. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes a –1 status penalty to Perception, attack rolls, and Will saves. This penalty increases to –2 for Will saves against [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]] effects.  

**Failure** As success, and any time the target uses a [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] action, it must succeed at a DC 5 flat check or the action is disrupted.
