# Needle of Vengeance single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 385

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Vengeance (Player Core)|Lesson of Vengeance]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 enemy

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

A long, jagged needle jabs into the target foe's psyche whenever it tries to attack a creature your patron holds in special regard. Name yourself or one of your allies. The target takes 2 mental damage any time it uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against the named creature, with a basic Will save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2.
