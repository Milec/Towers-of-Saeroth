# Bane two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 320

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 5-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You fill the minds of your enemies with doubt. Enemies in the area must succeed at a Will save or take a –1 status penalty to attack rolls as long as they are in the area. Once per turn, starting the turn after you cast _bane_, you can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to increase the emanation's radius by 5 feet and force enemies in the area that weren't yet affected to attempt another saving throw. _Bane_ can [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] [[Spells/Bless|_bless_]].
