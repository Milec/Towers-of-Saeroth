# Daydreamer's Curse two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Misfortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -164- Hands of the Devil|Pathfinder #164: Hands of the Devil]] pg. 73

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You impose a curse upon the target that renders it easily distracted and unable to focus its thoughts on tasks that demand insight or discernment. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** For 10 minutes, the target must roll twice and use the worse result whenever attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], Perception, or [[Rules/Skills/Survival]] check. If the target critically succeeds at a Perception check or skill check to perform an action with the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, the target gets a success instead.  

**Failure** As success, but the effect is permanent.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the target treats the outcomes of all checks with the concentrate trait as one degree of success worse than the result it rolled (a critical success becomes a success, a success becomes a failure, and a failure becomes a critical failure).
