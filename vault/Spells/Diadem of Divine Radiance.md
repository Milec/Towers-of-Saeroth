# Diadem of Divine Radiance two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 157

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You don a diadem of radiant light, which sheds [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] to a range of 60 feet and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] to a further 60 feet. When you Cast the Spell and when you [[Actions/Sustain]] it during the duration, you can draw a disc of spiraling light from the diadem and throw it at a creature within 120 feet. Make a ranged spell attack at mythic proficiency against the target's AC. This action has the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] and [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] traits. On a hit, you deal 4d8 spirit damage, 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent spirit damage]], and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round (3 rounds on a critical hit). The persistent damage isn't doubled on a critical hit.

If the disc passes through an area of magical [[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] or targets a creature affected by magical darkness, the disc's glow attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the darkness using your [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] or [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]] skill modifier as the counteract check modifier and half your level as the counteract rank.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The disc’s divine damage increases by 2d8, and the persistent damage increases by 1d4.
