# Lucky Number  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Contingency]], [[Traits/Divination]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 113

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** 10 minutes ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Duration** until the next time you make your daily preparations

---

When you Cast this Spell, roll 1d20 and make a note of the result. You gain the following reaction; once you use the reaction, the spell ends, and you become temporarily immune to _lucky number_ until the next time you make your daily preparations.

 **That's My Number!** ⤾ ([[Traits/Divination|divination]], [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]]) **Trigger** You roll your lucky number as your d20 result on a non-[[Traits/Secret|secret]] attack roll, saving throw, or skill check; **Effect** You call upon the fortune stored within your lucky number and reroll the triggering check. However, pushing your luck has a price: if your result on the reroll is the same number again, you critically fail, regardless of what degree of success you ordinarily would have received.
