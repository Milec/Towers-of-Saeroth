# Aberrant Form two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 122

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Haggakal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Channeling the power of vile otherworldly entities into your body, you take a new grotesque form, allowing you to wade into the battlefield while spreading pain and terror in your wake. You transform yourself into a Large aberration. You must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. When you Cast this Spell, choose chuul, gogiteth, gug, or ofalth. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Aberration (Player Core)|aberration]] trait. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 18 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 13 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more unarmed melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +18, and your damage bonus is +8. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +20, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of aberration you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Chuul]]** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim]] 25 feet; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8 bludgeoning, and after a successful Strike, you can spend an action to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target.
- **[[Bestiary/Gogiteth (Monster Core)|Gogiteth]]** Speed 40 feet, [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]] 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d10 piercing, and after a successful Strike, you can spend an action to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target; **Melee** single action leg ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6+8 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Gug (Monster Core 2)|Gug]]** Speed 40 feet, [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]] 20 feet; **Melee** single action jaws (reach 10 feet), **Damage** 2d12 piercing; **Melee** single action claw (agile, reach 10 feet), **Damage** 2d8+8 slashing; your multiple joints allow you to fit through tight spaces as if you were a Medium creature.
- **[[Bestiary/Ofalth (Monster Core)|Ofalth]]** Speed 30 feet; +2 circumstance bonus on saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12 bludgeoning.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Your battle form is Huge, and the reach of your attacks increases by 5 feet. You must have space to expand, or the spell is lost. You instead gain AC = 21 + your level, 26 temporary Hit Points, attack modifier +22, damage bonus +13, and Athletics +23.
