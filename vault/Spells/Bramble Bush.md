# Bramble Bush two actions  *Cantrip 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 5-foot cube

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

In a sudden burst of growth, you cause a thorned bush to sprout from the ground, lash around, and wither. Any creature in the area takes 1d4 piercing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]]  Reflex saving throw.

Until the start of your next turn, the area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain (Player Core)|hazardous terrain]]. Any creature entering the square takes 1d4 piercing damage with a basic Reflex saving throw.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 1d4, and the damage dealt by hazardous terrain increases by 1.
