# Dimensional Drop two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 132

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature standing on the ground

**Defense**
Reflex

---

You place a portal on the ground directly beneath a creature. That target immediately plummets into extradimensional space, falling a distance through an infinite, featureless void before being forcibly ejected from a portal that appears right above their original space and taking the appropriate [[Rules/Falling|fall damage]]. The target must attempt a Reflex saving throw. The DC to Arrest a Fall is equal to your spell DC.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target falls 10 feet through the void before reappearing.  

**Failure** The target falls 20 feet through the void before reappearing.  

**Critical Failure** The target falls 40 feet through the void before reappearing.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 5 creatures within range.
