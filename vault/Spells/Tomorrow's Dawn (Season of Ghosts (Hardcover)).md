# Tomorrow's Dawn two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 309

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

The golden light of a new dawn and the promise of life beyond death radiates from your body. You radiate [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] for the next 20 feet) and gain mental resistance 5. Any [[Traits/Nindoru|nindoru]] [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] or [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] that begins its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and immune to this spell’s effects for the remainder of the spell’s duration.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 until the end of its turn.  

**Failure** The creature is sickened 1. In addition, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but sickened 2 and the creature remains slowed 1 for the rest of the spell’s duration.

---
**Heightened (6th)** You gain mental resistance 10.

**Heightened (8th)** You gain mental resistance 15.
