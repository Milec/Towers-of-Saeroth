# Clinging Shadows Stance single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Stance (Player Core)|Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 257

**Duration** until you leave the stance

---

You adopt the stance of clinging shadows. You can make shadow grasp Strikes. These deal 1d4 void damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]], and [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] traits. While in clinging shadows stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], and to the DC for creatures to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] from you.
