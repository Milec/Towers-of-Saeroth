# Plasma Whirl single action to three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 157

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You reach up to the stars and create a temporary portal that allows a blast of plasma to pass through the battlefield. The plasma deals electricity and fire damage with a basic Reflex save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the area and other parameters.

single action This spell is a 15-foot cone, dealing 2d6 electricity damage and 2d6 fire damage.  
two actions This spell is a 30-foot cone, dealing 5d6 electricity damage and 5d6 fire damage.  
three actions This spell is a 60-foot cone, dealing 5d6 electricity damage and 5d6 fire damage. Creatures that fail their saves become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] (sickened 2 on a critical failure) due to their exposure to cosmic radiation.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6 for each damage type with the 1-action version, or by 2d6 for each damage type for the 2-and 3-action versions.
