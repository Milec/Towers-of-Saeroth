# Celestial Accord two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 2 creatures that each have an  unfriendly  or worse attitude toward the other

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You intervene in a heated disagreement between two creatures, encouraging them to put aside their differences and find some common ground. The emotional heat of the prior moment becomes only a memory. Each target must make a Will save. A creature currently engaged in combat can't get a result worse than success, and a target that is subject to [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostility]] from any other creature ceases to be affected by _celestial accord_.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is filled with doubt about its own intentions and feels an urge to cooperate with the other. It has a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against the other target for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature can't make hostile actions against the other target and its attitude toward the other target improves to indifferent for the spell's duration.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature's attitude toward the other target improves to [[Rules/Conditions/Friendly|friendly]] for the duration and is [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]] thereafter (until something happens to change that attitude normally).
