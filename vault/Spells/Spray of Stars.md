# Spray of Stars two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 233

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Cosmos]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

You fling a spray of tiny shooting stars, dealing 1d4 fire damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is dazzled for 3 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is dazzled for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 1d4.
