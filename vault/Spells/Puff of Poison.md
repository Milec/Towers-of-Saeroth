# Puff of Poison two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Inhaled]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 124

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 5 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You exhale a shimmering cloud of toxic breath at an enemy's face. The target takes 1d8 poison damage and 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]], depending on its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half initial and persistent damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full initial and persistent damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double initial and persistent damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial poison damage increases by 1d8 and the persistent poison damage increases by 1.
