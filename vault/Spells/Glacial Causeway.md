# Glacial Causeway two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 120-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a path of ice that begins beneath your feet and extends up to 120 feet in a straight line, starting from you. When you end your turn on the path, you gain the [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] condition until the end of your next turn and can use the extra action only to Stride. Other creatures treat the path as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]].
