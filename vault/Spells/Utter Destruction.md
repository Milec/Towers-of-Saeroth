# Utter Destruction two actions  *Spell 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Sonic]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -209- Destroyer's Doom|Pathfinder #209: Destroyer's Doom]] pg. 82

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You screech with an unearthly voice made of destructive energy, smashing everything that lies before you. Creatures in the area take 4d8 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage and 4d8 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage. Each creature must attempt a Fortitude save. Unattended objects of [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 5 or less in the area of effect are destroyed.

**Critical Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is deafened for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is permanently deafened.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The sonic and void damage each increase by 1d8. The Hardness threshold of items destroyed by the spell increases by 1.
