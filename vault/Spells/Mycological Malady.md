# Mycological Malady two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex (Player Core)|Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 61

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Decay]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You infect your target with fungal spores to hasten the cycle of decay. The target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]] that aren’t [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]], such as [[Bestiary/Families/Skeleton (Monster Core)|skeletons]] and [[Bestiary/Families/Zombie (Monster Core)|zombies]], don’t have their normal immunity to this disease, as the spores can still colonize their remaining flesh and bone. This might also apply to other non-living creatures made of organic material at the GM’s discretion.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target sprouts mushrooms and is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with voidcap spores at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with voidcap spores at stage 2.

**Voidcap Spores** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) This disease ends when the spell ends  
**Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 (1 round)  
**Stage 2** enfeebled 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 (1 round)  
**Stage 3** enfeebled 2, slowed 1, and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 (1 round)
