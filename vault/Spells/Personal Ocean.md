# Personal Ocean two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 174

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself in a bubble-like shroud of seawater that fills your space completely and moves with you. You can breathe, speak, and perceive normally while within your _personal ocean_, and you gain a swim Speed equal to your land Speed if you don't have one. The rules of [[Rules/Aquatic Combat|aquatic combat]] apply to you, attacks targeting you, or attacks passing through your square. For instance, a bludgeoning or slashing melee attack targeting you would take a –2 circumstance penalty, and you can't cast fire spells or use actions with the [[Traits/Fire|fire]] trait.

 Each time you move, you extinguish non-magical fires in spaces you pass through and can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] magical fires you move through. If you successfully counteract a fire larger than the spaces you moved through, you merely push it out of the spaces along your path. If you fail to counteract a given fire, you can't counteract that fire for the duration of the spell.

 You can Dismiss the spell.
