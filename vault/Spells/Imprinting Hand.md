# Imprinting Hand two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 144

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living or undead creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your hand presses into your target, leaving an imprint on its body. The target takes 8 force damage with a Fortitude save. The first time within the next minute the target's Hit Points are restored by a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] effect, that healing is reduced by 8. After any amount of healing is reduced, the spell ends.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the force damage and half the healing reduction.  

**Failure** The target takes the full force damage and the full healing reduction.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double the damage and double the healing reduction.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage and healing reduction both increase by 4.
