# Shipwreck two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Target** 1 vehicle

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You gesture toward a vehicle and the vessel wrenches itself apart. It must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The vehicle is unaffected.  

**Success** The vehicle becomes [[Rules/Uncontrolled Vehicles (GM Core)|uncontrolled]], and spaces within it become [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]].  

**Failure** As success, plus the vehicle gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]] condition. If it already had the broken condition, it’s [[Rules/Conditions/Destroyed|destroyed]].  

**Critical Failure** The vehicle is reduced to 0 HP and is destroyed.
