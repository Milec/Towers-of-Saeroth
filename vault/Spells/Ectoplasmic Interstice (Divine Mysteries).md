# Ectoplasmic Interstice two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 269

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Soul (Divine Mysteries)|Soul]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You force the mortal [[Setting/Planes/The Universe|Universe]] and [[Setting/Planes/Ethereal Plane (GM Core)|Ethereal Planes]] to partially merge, creating a zone that causes incorporeal and spiritual entities to take on many of the aspects of corporeal creatures. Within the area of effect, [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] creatures can’t pass through solid objects or corporeal creatures, and they can manipulate and attempt Strength-based checks against physical creatures and objects. An incorporeal PC or other creature with a normal Strength modifier simply uses that modifier, but if the creature had a Strength modifier of –5, like most incorporeal monsters, it uses a +4 modifier instead, increasing its [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier by 9 accordingly. Attacks against creatures that are within the area overcome [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistances]] as if they were by [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]] weapons.
