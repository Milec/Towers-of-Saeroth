# Pyrefowl Rebuke ⤾  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 82

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Trigger** A creature within 10 feet of you Strikes and deals damage to you

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

Fiery wings briefly envelop your arms, and with a swift wingbeat, you flutter away from your attacker in a shower of searing sparks. You deal 1d6 fire damage to the triggering creature, with a basic Reflex save, and [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to 10 feet in a straight line directly away from it. If the creature critically fails its saving throw, your movement does not provoke reactions from it, and it's [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6, and the maximum distance you can Fly increases by 5 feet.
