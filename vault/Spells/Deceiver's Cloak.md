# Deceiver's Cloak two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 238

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Mischief]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** sustained

---

You wrap yourself in a cloak of illusion, appearing as another creature of the same body shape with roughly similar height and weight as yourself. This has the effects of a 3rd-level [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]].

---
**Heightened (6th)** You can appear as any creature of the same size, even with a completely different body shape.
