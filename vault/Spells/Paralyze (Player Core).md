# Paralyze two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 348

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Barbatos (Divine Mysteries)|Barbatos]], [[Setting/Deities/Chamiaholom]], [[Setting/Deities/Dammerich (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Dammerich]], [[Setting/Deities/Kabriri (Divine Mysteries)|Kabriri]], [[Setting/Deities/Kalekot (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Kalekot]], [[Setting/Deities/Selket (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Selket]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You block the target's motor impulses before they can leave its mind, freezing target in place. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target is paralyzed for 4 rounds. At the end of each of its turns, it can attempt a new Will save to reduce the remaining duration by 1 round, or end it entirely on a critical success.

---
**Heightened (7th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
