# Toxic Cloud three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 363

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apollyon (Divine Mysteries)|Apollyon]], [[Setting/Deities/Cixyron]], [[Setting/Deities/Isph-Aun-Vuln]], [[Setting/Deities/Nergal]], [[Setting/Deities/Nhimbaloth (Divine Mysteries)|Nhimbaloth]], [[Setting/Deities/Sicva]], [[Setting/Deities/Vorasha]], [[Setting/Deities/Ydersius (Divine Mysteries)|Ydersius]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure a poisonous fog. This functions as [[Spells/Mist|_mist_]], except the area moves 10 feet away from you each round. A breathing creature that starts its turn in the area takes 6d8 poison damage with a basic Fortitude save. You can Dismiss the spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
