# Reblooded two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 159

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1  undead  creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your enemy’s heart has been stopped, but you cause it to start pumping blood once again. The unfamiliar sensation is enough to throw the creature off-kilter, while the blood itself damages the flesh of the undead. The target attempts a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]].  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute and takes 2d6 persistent acid damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is clumsy 2 for 1 minute and takes 3d6 persistent acid damage.
