# Procyal Philosophy two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The racoon-like visage of a sagacious [[Bestiary/Procyal|procyal]] appears, dispensing helpful advice. The image appears floating next to the target. The image is just a floating magical manifestation and doesn't occupy any space. The procyal offers helpful suggestions, preparing to [[Actions/Aid]] the target as though the procyal had its own reaction. The procyal uses a bonus equal to your spell DC – 10 for its rolls to Aid.

 On each of your turns, the procyal prepares to Aid its target again. You can use a free action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to redirect the procyal image to a different creature, changing the target of the spell. Otherwise, the procyal keeps aiding the same creature for the duration of the spell.
