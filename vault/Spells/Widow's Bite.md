# Widow's Bite two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 173

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You conjure up a deadly stinger on your hand and stab a foe. Make a melee spell attack against the target’s AC. On a hit, the target takes 1d4 piercing damage and 3d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]] (or double damage on a critical hit). Additionally, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 until it removes the persistent poison damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial piercing damage increases by 1d4, and the persistent poison damage increases by 2d4.
