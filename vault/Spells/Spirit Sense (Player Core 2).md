# Spirit Sense two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|Detection]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 252

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** 30-foot emanation

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You open your mind to the metaphysical, enabling you to sense nearby spirits. Even if you aren't [[Actions/Search (Player Core)|Searching]], you get a check to find [[Traits/Haunt (GM Core)|haunts]] and [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirits]] in the area. You gain a +1 status bonus to the following checks regarding haunts or spirits: Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]], attempts to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]], skill checks to determine the reason for their existence, and skill checks to disable a haunt. You also gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws against haunts and spirits.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The spell's duration lasts until the next time you make your daily preparations.
