# Crucible of Iron two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 130

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call forth a crucible of molten iron that pours over the target, burning them as the metal quickly covers and encases their body. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target's head is encased in iron, causing them to be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round and take 1d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].  

**Failure** The target's head, arms, and chest are all encased. The target is dazzled and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]] for 1 minute or until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]]. They also take 1d10 persistent fire damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target's legs and feet are also encased. The target is dazzled, enfeebled 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 minute or until they Escape. They also take 1d10 persistent fire damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 1d10.
