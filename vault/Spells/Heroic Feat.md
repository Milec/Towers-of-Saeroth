# Heroic Feat two actions or three actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 232

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Battle]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

You gain the ability to perform a specialized combat technique from the vast wealth of martial knowledge your mystery provides. When you Cast this Spell, you use the action granted by one common [[Rules/Category Pages/Feats|fighter feat]] from the _Core Rulebook_ or the _Advanced Player's Guide_ (your GM might add feats to this list). If you Cast this Spell using 2 actions, the granted action must be a single action, and if you Cast this Spell using 3 actions, you can instead choose a feat that grants a 2-action activity. The chosen feat's level can be no higher than _heroic feat's_ spell level. The feat can't have a Frequency entry, and you must meet its prerequisites and requirements in order to select it.
