# Carrion Mire three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -4|Wake the Dead #4]] pg. 29

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 15-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

The ground opens into a seemingly endless mass of corpse limbs, grasping at all creatures within the area to drag them down. Creatures in the area when the spell is cast or beginning their turn in the area must succeed at a Reflex save or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. When a creature in the spell's areas would be healed by a [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect, the _carrion mire_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] that effect, preventing that healing but not otherwise prevent the effect if it's counteracted.
