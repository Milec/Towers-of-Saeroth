# Lock Item two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 88

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** one object held by one creature and the creature holding it

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** varies

---

One object held by a creature becomes fused to that creature’s hand and can’t be put down. To [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] or [[Actions/Steal (Player Core)|Steal]] the item, the result of the skill check must exceed the spell’s DC or the normal DC, whichever is higher. To voluntarily drop the weapon, the target must spend an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action rather than a free action, and then succeed at a Reflex save; the target must also attempt a Reflex save to Interact to put away or swap the object. On a failed save, the action is wasted, but on a success, they drop the item and the spell ends. An unwilling creature must attempt an initial Reflex save against _lock item_. A willing creature can choose to critically fail the saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is affected for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is affected for 4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is affected for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** You can target either one additional object held by the same creature or one additional object held by one additional creature.
