# Fey Form two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 105

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Channeling the mischievous fey, you transform into a fey battle form, which is Medium unless stated otherwise. When you cast this spell, choose dryad, elananx, naiad, redcap, or unicorn. If you choose unicorn, the battle form is Large and you must have space to expand or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Fey|fey]] trait. Unless you choose elananx or unicorn, you have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 19 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 15 temporary Hit Points and weakness 5 to cold iron.
- [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]].
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +16, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] modifiers of +16, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of fey:

- **[[Bestiary/Dryad]]** The temporary HP gained increases to 30; Speed 25 feet; weakness 5 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]; **Melee** single action branch, **Damage** 1d12+8 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Elananx]]** Speed 30 feet; resistance 5 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 1d6+8 piercing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile|agile]]), **Damage** 1d6+8 slashing.
- **[[Bestiary/Naiad]]** Speed 25 feet, swim 25 feet; resistance 5 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]; **Melee** single action aqueous fist ([[Traits/Water|water]]), **Damage** 1d8+8 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Redcap]]** Speed 40 feet; Size Small; **Melee** single action scythe ([[Traits/Deadly|deadly d10]], [[Traits/Trip|trip]]), **Damage** 1d10+8 slashing; **Melee** single action boot ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Versatile|versatile B]]), **Damage** 1d6+8 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Unicorn]]** gain the [[Traits/Beast|beast]] trait in addition to fey; Speed 40 feet; resistance 5 to [[Traits/Poison|poison]]; Size Large; **Melee** single action horn, **Damage** 1d10+8 piercing plus 1 good; **Melee** single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile|agile]]), **Damage** 1d8+8 bludgeoning; all Strikes have the effects of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] property rune.
