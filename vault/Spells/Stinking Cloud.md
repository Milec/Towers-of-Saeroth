# Stinking Cloud two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 373

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a cloud of putrid mist in the area. The cloud functions as [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]] except it sickens creatures that end their turns within the cloud. (The concealed condition is not a poison effect.)

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is sickened 1.  

**Failure** The creature is sickened 1 and slowed 1 while in the cloud.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2 and slowed 1 until it leaves the cloud.
