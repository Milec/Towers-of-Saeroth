# Mantle of the Unwavering Heart two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 197

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Vines and branches envelop you in an instant, growing a wild bloom of colors that open to reveal your new form. Upon Casting this Spell, pick two of the options below. As a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, you can change one of your chosen abilities to a different option from the list.

- **Evergreen Vitality** You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 3]] and a +2 bonus to saves against [[Traits/Poison|poison]] and [[Traits/Disease|disease]]. This effect has the [[Traits/Healing|healing]] and [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] traits.
- **Overwhelming Perfume** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Olfactory|olfactory]]) A cloud of potent floral scent surrounds you in a 15-foot emanation. Creatures in the aura can't benefit from the [[Rules/Scent|scent]] ability and are [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] as long as they remain in the aura. The sickness is a disease effect.
- **Towering Trunk** You grow to a considerable height. Increase your size to Large. You're [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]]. Your [[Traits/Reach|reach]] increases by 5 feet (or by 10 feet if you started out Tiny), and you gain a grasping branch unarmed attack; this attack is in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group and deals 2d8 bludgeoning damage plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]].
- **Unyielding Will** Your mind becomes incredibly resilient to change. You're immune to being [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]], and you get a +1 bonus to saves and DCs against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects.
