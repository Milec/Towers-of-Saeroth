# Fey Glamour two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 404

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Fey]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst or

**Target** up to 10 creatures

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You call upon fey glamours to cloak an area or the targets in illusion. This has the effect of either [[Spells/Illusory Scene|_illusory scene_]] on the area or [[Spells/Veil|_veil_]] on the creatures, as if heightened to a level 1 level lower than _fey glamour_, using _fey glamour_'s range and duration.
