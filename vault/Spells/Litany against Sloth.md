# Litany against Sloth single action  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Litany]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 388

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature that isn't holy

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

Your litany rails against the sin of sloth, interfering with the target's ability to react. The target must attempt a Will save. A particularly slothful creature, such as a [[Bestiary/Dretch|Sloth Demon]], uses the outcome one degree of success worse than the result of its saving throw. The target becomes temporarily immune to all of your litanies for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target can't use reactions.  

**Failure** The target can't use reactions and is slowed 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target can't use reactions and is slowed 2.
