# Angelic Wings two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 262

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]]

**Duration** 3 rounds

---

Wings of pure light spread out from your back, granting you a fly Speed equal to your Speed. Your wings cast [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 30-foot radius. When this spell's duration would end, if you're still flying, you float to the ground, as [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]].

---
**Heightened (5th)** The duration increases to 1 minute.
