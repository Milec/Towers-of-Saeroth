# Flowing Strike three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

A wave of flowing water rushes behind you like an elegant river, pushing you across the battlefield with fluid grace. You Stride up to 50 feet. This movement doesn't trigger reactions. You can make a Strike at any point during your Stride. If your Strike hits, the wave flows after you to batter the target of your Strike as well, dealing 2d10 bludgeoning damage and [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushing]] it 10 feet in a direction of your choice.
