# Angel Form two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 122

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Invoking the celestial realms, you transform into a Medium angel battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose balisse, choral, kuribu, or tennin. The battle form is Small if you choose choral or kuribu. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Angel (Player Core)|angel]], [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], and [[Traits/Holy|holy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use manipulate actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell. You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction
- 40 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 10 to unholy.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and your damage bonus is +12. Melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example), and ranged attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +25, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of angel you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Balisse (Monster Core)|Balisse]]** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; Perception modifier of +26 to detect lies and illusions unless your own modifier is higher; **Melee** single action scimitar ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]]), **Damage** 2d6 slashing plus 2d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Choral (Monster Core)|Choral]]** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; +1 status bonus to AC and saves against auditory and sonic; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action piercing hymn (holy, magical, range 90 feet), **Damage** 3d6 sonic and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round on a critical hit.
- **[[Bestiary/Kuribu]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to electricity and fire; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]]), **Damage** 3d4 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action composite shortbow ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly 2d10]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]], [[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 3d6 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Tennin]]** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 60 feet; can Step into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and use land and fly Speeds to Step; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]]), **Damage** 1d6 bludgeoning plus 1d6 cold and 1d6 spirit.

---
**Heightened (9th)** Your battle form is Large if you choose balisse or tennin. You must have space to expand into, or the spell is lost. You instead gain AC = 22 + your level, 60 temporary Hit Points, weakness 15 to unholy, attack modifier +30, damage bonus +20, and Athletics +33.
