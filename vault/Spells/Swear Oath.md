# Swear Oath single action or two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 265

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Duty (Divine Mysteries)|Duty]]

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You loudly and firmly state a course of action that you swear to fulfill. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]], [[Actions/Ready (Player Core)|Ready]] a single action or free action you can use. If the action is in line with your personal edicts or the edicts presented by your [[Rules/Religion (GM Core)|religion]], you can Cast this Spell as a single action. If the trigger you designate occurs and you can use the chosen action as a reaction, you gain a +1 status bonus to any attack roll or skill check required. If the trigger you designate doesn’t occur, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell on your next turn to regain the Focus Point spent for this spell.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The status bonus is +2.

**Heightened (9th)** The status bonus is +3.
