# Vicious Howl single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 147

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You howl with passion for your cause, empowering yourself to push beyond your limits. You gain a number of [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level and a +4 circumstance bonus to damage. If you reduce a [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]] to 0 Hit Points during this duration, you regain a number of Hit Points equal to the creature’s level.
