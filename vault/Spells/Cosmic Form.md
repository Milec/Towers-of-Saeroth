# Cosmic Form two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 98

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You recreate your form entirely out of cosmic energy, transforming into a Large battle form. You must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. When you cast this spell, choose to take on the powers of the moon or the sun. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 21 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 20 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and you use the listed damage. The melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example), and the ranged attacks are Dexterity based. If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +25, unless your own is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the stellar body:

- **Moon** Speed 30 feet, fly 50 feet; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d4+10 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire; **Ranged** single action moonbeam (range 90 feet), **Damage** 2d4+10 fire; this form's fire damage is [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] for resistances, weaknesses, and the like; on a critical hit with either Strike, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 2]] for 1 round.
- **Sun** Speed 30 feet, fly 50 feet; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6+10 fire plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire]]; **Ranged** single action sunbeam ([[Traits/Range|range 90 feet]]), **Damage** 1d6+10 fire plus 1d6 persistent fire; on a critical hit with either Strike, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.
