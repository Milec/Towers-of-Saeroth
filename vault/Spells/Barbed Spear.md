# Barbed Spear two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 71

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aesir]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** varies

---

You conjure a vicious barbed spear and hurl it at a foe. Make a ranged spell attack roll, dealing 1d8 piercing damage on a success and double damage on a critical success. The spear remains lodged within a creature it hits, making the target [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 (or increasing its clumsy condition by 1 if it is already clumsy) for 1 minute or until the spear is removed with a successful [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against your spell DC as an [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] action, whichever comes first.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 1d8.
