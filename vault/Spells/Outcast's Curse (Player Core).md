# Outcast's Curse two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Misfortune]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 347

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|Hag]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You afflict the target with a curse that makes its presence abrasive and off-putting. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** For 10 minutes, the target must roll twice and use the worse result whenever attempting a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]], or [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check, and creatures they encounter have an initial attitude toward them of one step worse (for instance, [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] instead of [[Rules/Conditions/Indifferent|indifferent]]).  

**Failure** As success, but the effect is permanent.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and creatures that the target encounters have an initial attitude toward them of two steps worse.
