# Tiny Wings two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 167

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** your familiar

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You hold your familiar in your arms and then throw them into the sky—whereupon they sprout wings and take flight. The wings look appropriate to the familiar’s nature. A tabby cat might simply grow a pair of grayish wings like a pigeon, while a frog grows membranous wings a bit like a bat. Your familiar gains a 20-foot fly Speed.  

Familiars who already have a fly Speed instead gain a +15-foot status bonus to their fly Speed.
