**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

You call out a liberating cry, urging an ally to break free of an effect that holds them in place. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]], it can immediately use a reaction to attempt to [[Actions/Escape]].
