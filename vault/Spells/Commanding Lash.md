# Commanding Lash single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 390

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Tyranny]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Requirements** Your most recent action dealt damage to a target

**Range** 100 feet

**Target** A creature you dealt damage to on your most recent action.

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** until the end of your target's next turn

---

With the threat of more pain, you compel a creature you've recently harmed. You issue a command to the target, with the effects of the spell [[Spells/Command|_command_]].
