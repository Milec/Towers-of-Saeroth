# Sudden Blight two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 253

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You accelerate the processes of decay in the area. Each living creature in the area takes 2d10 void damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature afflicted by a disease takes a –2 circumstance penalty to this save.

 You can also direct the blight to rot all Small and Tiny non-creature plants in the area, eliminating non-magical undergrowth and any resulting [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], [[Rules/Cover|cover]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]]. _Sudden blight_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any magical effect on the plants before withering them.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
