# The Everfull Moon single action  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Impossible]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 176

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cost** moondust or moonrock worth 100 gp × the spell’s rank

**Duration** 1 minute

---

A Linnorm King once defended her kingdom from Irriseni invaders with a casting of this spell. You call upon the secret moon, who's always whole, to shine upon yourself, granting you the form of a mighty [[Bestiary/Werewolf (Monster Core)|werewolf]] without letting the curse touch you. You transform yourself into a Large hybrid wolf battle form. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]] trait. This battle form doesn't prevent you from speaking, casting spells, or using your hands. You gain the following abilities.

- AC = 18 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- A +1 status bonus to saving throws.
- 20 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision (Monster Core)|Darkvision]] and scent as an [[Rules/Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet.
- Your proficiency bonus equals your level for untrained skill checks using [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]].
- The claw and jaws unarmed melee attacks detailed below. You're trained with them, your attack modifier is +10, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- **Melee** jaws ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]]), Damage 2d8 piercing.
- **Melee** claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]]), Damage 2d6 slashing.
- Speed of 40 feet, unless your own is higher.

---

**Lethologic Mark** During any night that isn't the new moon, you gain a weakness to [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] equal to your level. The mark is healed if you become a true [[Bestiary/Werewolf (Monster Core)|werewolf]] and become cured of said curse.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The temporary Hit Points granted by this spell when you cast or Fuel it increase by 20. All unarmed Strikes granted by this spell deal an additional die of damage. Your attack modifier increases by +6. Finally, your AC increases by 2.
