# Divine Decree two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 325

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Diabolic (Player Core 2)|Diabolic]]

**Area** 40-foot emanation

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You utter a potent litany from your faith, a mandate that harms those who oppose your ideals. You deal 7d10 spirit damage to your enemies in the area; each enemy must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute. If you're on your home plane and the creature is not, the creature is sent back to its home plane. A creature of 10th level or lower must also succeed at a Will save or be [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 minute; if it critically fails, it dies (this is a [[Traits/Death|death]] effect).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10, and the level of creatures that must attempt a second save on a critical failure increases by 2.
