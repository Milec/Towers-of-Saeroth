# Spirit of the Beast single action  *Focus 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|Flourish]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Primal (Player Core)|Primal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 67

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your animal companion

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You bring out the spirit of another animal that lives within your companion. Its body morphs slightly to take on an aspect of the animal you choose to have it emulate—for example, it might grow a pair of horns if the spirit is a bull or a tail if the spirit is a monkey. When you Cast this Spell, select from one of the following aspects.

- **Aspect of Might** Your companion gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks, [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize (Player Core)|Demoralize]], and Fortitude saves.
- **Aspect of Swiftness** Your companion gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks, [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]], and Reflex saves.
- **Aspect of Insight** Your companion gains a +1 status bonus to Perception checks to [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]], [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks to [[Actions/Sense Direction (Player Core)|Sense Direction]] or [[Actions/Track (Player Core)|Track]], and Will saves.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The status bonuses increase to +2.

**Heightened (8th)** The status bonuses increase to +3.
