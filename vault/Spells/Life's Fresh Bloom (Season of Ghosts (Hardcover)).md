# Life's Fresh Bloom two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 308

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** sustained up to 5 rounds

---

The ground around you blooms with life, sprouting sparkling grass, flowers, and small shrubs. Each living and non-[[Traits/Nindoru|nindoru]] creature that starts its turn standing on the ground in the area recovers 1d6 Hit Points and gains a +5‑foot status bonus to its Speed until the end of its next turn. Nindoru fiends and [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] that start their turn in the area of life’s fresh bloom instead become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 unless they succeed at a Fortitude saving throw.

---
**Heightened (7th)** Increase the Hit Points restored to 1d8 and the sickened condition value to 2.

**Heightened (10th)** Increase the Hit Points restored to 1d10 and the sickened condition value to 3.
