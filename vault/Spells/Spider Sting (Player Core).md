# Spider Sting two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 358

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Barbatos (Divine Mysteries)|Barbatos]], [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]], [[Setting/Deities/Teshallas]], [[Setting/Deities/Vorasha]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You magically duplicate a spider's venomous sting. You deal 1d4 piercing damage to the touched creature and afflict it with spider venom. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 1d4 poison damage.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with spider venom at stage 2. **Spider Venom** ([[Traits/Poison|poison]]) Level 1; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 1d4 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 1d4 poison damage and enfeebled 2 (1 round)
