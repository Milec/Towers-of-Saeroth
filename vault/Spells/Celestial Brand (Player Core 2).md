# Celestial Brand single action  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 262

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1  unholy  creature

**Duration** 1 round

---

A blazing symbol appears on the target, cursing it to face divine justice. You and your allies receive a +1 status bonus to your attack rolls and skill checks against it. Anytime a [[Traits/Holy|holy]] creature damages it, the holy creature deals an additional 1d4 spirit damage. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The spirit damage increases by 1.
