# Ectoplasmic Expulsion three actions  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 75

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

---

You call upon tendrils of ephemeral ectoplasm to pass through the target and carry away a harmful psychic affliction. You immediately attempt to counteract a single effect on the target with the [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] or [[Traits/Possession|possession]] trait, or an effect giving the target the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled|controlled]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] condition. If you fail to counteract the effect, you nevertheless set up a resonance that allows the target to more ably escape the effect on its own; for the next hour, the target gains a +2 status bonus to Will saves to shrug off the effect you failed to counteract (such as Will saves to shake off control by the [[Spells/Possession|_possession_]] spell).
