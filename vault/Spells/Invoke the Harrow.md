# Invoke the Harrow two actions  *Focus 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Stolen Fate Player's Guide]] pg. 15

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Harrow]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You draw a card from the harrow, either one from a deck you have in hand or magically produce one as part of the spell. You use the power of the card's suit to influence a creature around you. When you draw a card, you can choose to impart a boon or bane associated with the card's suit. The target must attempt a Will save. On a failure, they receive the effects of the chosen boon or bane. If you target an ally with this spell, they can choose to willingly accept the effects without attempting a saving throw. The target can attempt to remove the card's influence by using an action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to attempt the saving throw again; on a success, the effect ends.

 To determine the card, draw a card from a [[Equipment/Harrow Deck|harrow deck]]. If you don't have a harrow deck available, you can roll 1d6 to randomly determine the suit you draw.

1. **Hammers (Str)** Boon: The target gains a +2 status bonus to damage rolls; Bane: The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]].
2. **Keys (Dex)** Boon: The target gains a +1 status bonus to AC; Bane: The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]].
3. **Shields (Con)** Boon: The target gains temporary Hit Points equal to twice the spell's level; Bane: The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]].
4. **Books (Int)** Boon: The target gains a +1 status bonus to skill checks; Bane: The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]].
5. **Stars (Wis)** Boon: The target gains a +1 status bonus to saving throws; Bane: The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]].
6. **Crowns (Cha)** Boon: The target can roll twice and take the higher result on a single check during the duration. This is a [[Traits/Fortune|fortune]] effect; Bane: The target must roll twice and take the lower result on a single check of your choosing during the duration. This is a [[Traits/Misfortune|misfortune]] effect.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration is 1 minute. The target must succeed a total of two saving throws to end the effect. The value of boons and banes doubles. The Crowns suit affects a total of three checks during the duration.
