# Summon Lesser Servitor three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Summon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 253

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) spell may be used, but imps may not be summoned_

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

While deities jealously guard their most powerful servants from the summoning spells of those who aren't steeped in the faith, this spell allows you to conjure an inhabitant of the [[Rules/Outer Sphere Planes (GM Core)|Outer Sphere]] with or without the deity's permission. You summon a common [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], or [[Traits/Monitor (Player Core)|monitor]] of level –1. You can choose to instead summon an [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animal]] of level –1 that hails from the Outer Sphere; you can choose for this animal to gain the celestial and [[Traits/Holy|holy]] traits, the fiend and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits, or the monitor trait.

It's anathema to summon a servitor if it has a holy or unholy trait that isn't allowed for your deity's sanctification. For example, [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae's]] sanctification is “can choose holy,” so you couldn't summon an unholy creature, and [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma's]] is “none,” so you couldn't summon a holy or unholy creature. The GM might determine that your deity restricts specific types of creatures further, making it anathema to summon them as well.
