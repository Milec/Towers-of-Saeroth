# Songbird's Call two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Composition (Player Core)|Composition]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 65

**Area** 15-foot emanation centered on you

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your voice calls a storm of songbirds that whirl around you and peck at your enemies. All creatures within the area of whirling birds become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the area become concealed to creatures within it. A creature other than you that enters or ends its turn in the area takes 1d6 piercing damage as the birds peck at it.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
