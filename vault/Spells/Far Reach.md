# Far Reach single action  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 137

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustain]] up to 1 minute

---

You conjure two tiny portals in midair: one in your own space and another in any unoccupied space within range that you can see. As long as you occupy the space of a portal, you can see, hear, and reach through the portal in your space to Interact with creatures, objects, or the environment as if you were in the space of the other portal. Only you can use and benefit from these portals, although friendly creatures might be able to hand you items or otherwise Interact with whatever extremities you extend through them. No creature, including you, can pass entirely through either portal, even one occupying an [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|extradimensional]] container.
