# Incendiary Fog three actions  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -177- Burning Tundra|Pathfinder #177: Burning Tundra]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
see below

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You summon a cloud of flammable black dust. The cloud functions as [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]]. If any open flame or [[Traits/Fire|fire]] effect is within the spell's area when it's cast, or later enters or occurs within the area, the cloud ignites. Creatures in the cloud take 10d6 fire damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save), and the spell ends.
