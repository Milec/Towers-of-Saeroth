# Song of Strength single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 371

**Area** 60-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 round

---

You bolster your allies' physical strength with a hearty exhortation. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks and to their DCs against Athletics skill actions such as [[Actions/Disarm]], Reposition, [[Actions/Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip]].
