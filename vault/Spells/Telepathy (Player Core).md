# Telepathy two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 363

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Gruhastha (Divine Mysteries)|Gruhastha]], [[Setting/Deities/Nin]], [[Setting/Deities/The Readied Strike]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You can communicate telepathically with creatures within 30 feet. Once you establish a connection by communicating with a creature, the communication is two-way. You can communicate with only creatures that share a language with you.

---
**Heightened (6th)** You can communicate telepathically with creatures using shared mental imagery even if you don't share a language; _telepathy_ loses the [[Traits/Linguistic|linguistic]] trait.
