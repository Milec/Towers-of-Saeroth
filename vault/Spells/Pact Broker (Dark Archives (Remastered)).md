# Pact Broker single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Hex (Player Core)|Hex]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 159, [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 298

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You offer to broker a pact of peace. If the target accepts and doesn't take hostile actions against you and your allies, you take a –1 status penalty to Deception checks to [[Actions/Lie (Player Core)|Lie]] to them. If they refuse and take a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]] against you or an ally, they must first attempt a Will save. If they accepted the offer and then take a hostile action against you or an ally during the hex's duration, they must first attempt a Will save and treat the result as one category worse. Regardless of the outcome, the target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target takes 2d4 mental damage and a –1 status penalty to attack and damage rolls against you and your allies for the hex’s duration.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the penalty is –2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
