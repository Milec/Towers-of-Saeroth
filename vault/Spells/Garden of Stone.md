# Garden of Stone three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The ground shakes as you conjure a 10-foot-tall stalagmite from below. The stalagmite fills the area and has AC 10, Hardness 5, and 30 Hit Points. When you Cast the Spell, all creatures in the area take 3d10 piercing damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save, and are pushed to the nearest unoccupied space as the stalagmite rises. The area in a 20-foot emanation around the stalagmite becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], as smaller stalagmites grow around it.

If the stalagmite survives to the end of the spell's duration, it remains as an ordinary, non-magical stalagmite. However, if the stalagmite is outside of a protected environment, such as a cave, it'll likely crumble away due to natural weathering over the course of a year.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The stalagmite has an additional 10 Hit Points, and the damage increases by 1d10.
