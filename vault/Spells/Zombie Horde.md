# Zombie Horde two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 184

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a horde of nearly endless, but temporary, zombies. You destroy the target thrall, and the zombie horde appears in a 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]], which must include a square the destroyed thrall occupied. All squares on the ground in the zombie horde are [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Whenever a creature begins its turn in the horde, it takes 3d4 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to move the horde up to 15 feet. At the start of each of your turns, all of your thralls in the horde are destroyed, and the area’s burst increases by 5 feet for each thrall destroyed (to a maximum of a 30-foot burst). You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
