# Psychic Outburst two actions to 2 rounds  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 158

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will

---

Psychic energy filters through your mind and explodes outward, dealing mental damage to creatures in the area with a basic Will save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the damage and other parameters. two actions The spell deals 6d10 mental damage. three actions The spell deals 6d10 mental damage. Creatures that fail their save are pushed 10 feet away from you (or 20 feet on a critical failure).  
**Two Rounds** If you spend 3 actions Casting the Spell, you can avoid finishing the spell and spend another 2 actions on your next turn to empower the spell even further. You can take other actions before finishing this spell, but the spell is lost if you Cast another Spell in between. The area increases to a 40-foot emanation, deals 8d10 mental damage, and targets only enemies. Creatures that fail their save are pushed 10 feet away from you (20 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
