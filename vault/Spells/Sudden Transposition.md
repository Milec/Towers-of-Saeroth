# Sudden Transposition two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 149

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Kyonin have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** 60-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Target** up to 2 creatures

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You wrench the targets off their feet, forcefully teleporting them within the area. The creatures must each be able to fit in their new space, and their positions must be unoccupied, entirely within the area, and in your line of sight. Each target attempts a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target can teleport if it wants, but it chooses the destination within the area.  

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** You teleport the target and choose its destination; the target takes 10d10 bludgeoning damage.  

**Critical Failure** You teleport the target and choose its destination; the target takes 20d10 bludgeoning damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
