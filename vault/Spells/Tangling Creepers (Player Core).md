# Tangling Creepers three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 362

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Green Man (Monster Core 2)|Green Man]], [[Setting/Deities/Immaculate Growth]], [[Setting/Deities/Kagia]], [[Setting/Deities/Osiris (Divine Mysteries)|Osiris]], [[Setting/Deities/Telvrys]], [[Setting/Deities/Treerazer (Divine Mysteries)|Treerazer]], [[Setting/Deities/Yuelral (Divine Mysteries)|Yuelral]], [[Setting/Deities/Zibik]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 40-foot burst

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Dense, twitching creepers sprout from every surface and fill any bodies of water in the area. Any creature moving on the land, or [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] or [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] within the creepers, takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds while in the area. Once per round, you can Sustain the spell to make a vine lash out from any square within the expanse of creepers. This vine has a 15- foot reach. Make a melee spell attack roll against the target; on a success, the vine pulls the target into the creepers and makes it [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round or until the creature [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (against your spell DC), whichever comes first.
