# Ode to Ouroboros ⤾  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 228

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Trigger** a creature's [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] value would reach the value at which they would die.

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** the triggering creature

---

Your ode temporarily staves off death. The target's dying condition remains 1 below the value at which it dies. This doesn't help prevent death from effects that kill the target without increasing its dying condition, such as [[Spells/Disintegrate|_disintegrate_]] and [[Traits/Death|death]] effects.
