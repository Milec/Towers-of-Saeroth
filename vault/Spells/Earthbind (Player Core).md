# Earthbind two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 327

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Doloras (Divine Mysteries)|Doloras]], [[Setting/Deities/Jukha]], [[Setting/Deities/Torag (Divine Mysteries)|Torag]], [[Setting/Deities/Treasures of the Eternal Delve]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 flying creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

Using the weight of earth, you hamper a target's flight, with effects based on its Fortitude save. If the creature reaches the ground safely, it doesn't take [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]].

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target falls safely up to 120 feet.  

**Failure** The target falls safely up to 120 feet. If it hits the ground, it can't [[Actions/Fly]], [[Spells/Levitate|_levitate_]], or otherwise leave the ground for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target falls safely up to 120 feet. If it hits the ground, it can't Fly, _levitate_, or otherwise leave the ground for 1 minute.
