# Animal Vision  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 240

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Baekho]], [[Setting/Deities/Bastet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Bastet]], [[Setting/Deities/Isis (Divine Mysteries)|Isis]], [[Setting/Deities/Ketephys (Divine Mysteries)|Ketephys]], [[Setting/Deities/Tanagaar (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Tanagaar]]

**Cast** 1 minute

**Range** 120 Feet

**Target** 1 animal

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You tap into the target's senses, allowing you to sense whatever it senses for the spell's duration. If the target wishes to prevent you from doing so, it can attempt a Will save, negating the spell on a success, but most [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]] don't bother to do so. While tapping into the target's senses, you can't use your own body's senses, but you can change back and forth from your body's senses to the target's senses using a [[Actions/Sustain]] action.
