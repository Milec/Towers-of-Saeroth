# Athletic Rush single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 377

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Might (Player Core)|Might]]

**Duration** 1 round

---

Your body fills with physical power and skill. You gain a +10- foot status bonus to Speed and a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks. As a part of Casting this Spell, you can use a Stride, [[Actions/Leap]], [[Actions/Climb]], or [[Actions/Swim]] action. The spell's bonuses apply during that action.
