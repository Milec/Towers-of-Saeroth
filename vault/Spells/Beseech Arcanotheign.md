# Beseech Arcanotheign three actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 154

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

With a whispered prayer or arcane sending, you conjure Arcanotheign, herald of [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]]. She's a storm of magic, half white and half black, roiling in a vaguely humanoid shape. She occupies the space of a Medium creature and has a Speed of 40 feet and a fly Speed of 60 feet.

**Arrive** ([[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) _Storm's Unbridled Destruction_ Arcanotheign arrives with a flash of light and a cacophonous crash of colliding magic. All enemies in a 60-foot emanation take 8d12 sonic damage with a basic Reflex save. A creature that critically fails is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 10 minutes.

**Depart** ([[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]]) _Flash of Brilliance_ Arcanotheign fires a powerful arcane blast at one target within 100 feet, dealing 5d12 electricity damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save, and a powerful divine blast at one ally, healing 5d12 Hit Points. Then, Arcanotheign asks for payment in the form of a fond memory. If you pay this cost, you lose this memory, Arcanotheign gains this memory, and Arcanotheign whispers a secret into your mind; you can immediately [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] on any subject at mythic proficiency.
