You see things within 60 feet as they actually are. The GM rolls a secret [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against any [[Traits/Illusion|illusion]], [[Traits/Morph|morph]], or [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect in the area, but only for the purpose of determining whether you see through it (for instance, if the check succeeds against a polymorph spell, you can see the creature's true form, but you don't end the polymorph spell).
