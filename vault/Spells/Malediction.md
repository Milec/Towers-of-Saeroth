# Malediction two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 258

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You incite distress in the minds of your enemies, making it more difficult for them to defend themselves. Enemies in the area must succeed at a Will save or take a –1 status penalty to AC as long as they’re in the area. Once per round on subsequent turns, you can [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell to increase the emanation’s radius by 10 feet and force enemies in the area that weren’t yet affected to attempt a saving throw. Malediction can counteract [[Spells/Benediction|_benediction_]].
