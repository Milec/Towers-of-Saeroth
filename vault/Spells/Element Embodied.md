# Element Embodied two actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 101

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Drawing upon the deepest wells of magic in an elemental plane, you transform into a spectacular elemental. You can choose between an air elemental, earth elemental, fire elemental, or water elemental. Your battle form is Gargantuan, and you must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Elemental|elemental]] trait and the trait for the element you choose. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 25 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 30 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +34, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Dexterity based if you choose an air or fire elemental, or Strength based if you choose an earth or water elemental. (This distinction doesn't change the statistics, but matters for the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]] conditions, for example). If your [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] modifier of +36 for air or fire elemental or [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +36 for earth or water elemental, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of elemental you choose:

## Secrets of Magic

- **Air Elemental** fly Speed 80 feet; **High Winds** ([[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Aura|aura]]) 30 feet. Air within the emanation is difficult terrain for flying creatures that don't have the [[Traits/Air|air]] trait; **Swiftness** Your movement doesn't trigger reactions; **Melee** single action gust (reach 25 feet), **Damage** 3d12+11 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Push]] 10 feet; **Ranged** single action lightning lash ([[Traits/Range|range increment 80 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12+4 electricity.
- **Earth Elemental** 30 feet, burrow 20 feet; resistance 5 to physical; **Spike Stones** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]]) 30 feet. The area is difficult terrain and hazardous terrain. A creature without the [[Traits/Earth|earth]] trait that moves on the ground in the area takes 5 piercing damage for every square of that area it moves into; **Rocky Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12+15 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action rock ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]]), **Damage** 4d8+6 bludgeoning.
- **Fire Elemental** 50 feet; immunity to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]; weakness to [[Traits/Cold|cold]] 10; **Intense Heat** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]]) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 5d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** single action tendril ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10+12 fire plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire]]; **Ranged** single action fire mote ([[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 4d8+6 fire.
- **Water Elemental** 40 feet, swim 80 feet; resistance 10 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]]; **Vortex** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Water|water]]) 30 feet. Water within the aura that is part of the same body of water you occupy is difficult terrain for [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] creatures that don't have the [[Traits/Water|water]] trait; **Melee** single action wave ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12+18 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Push]] or Pull 10 feet; **Ranged** single action water spout ([[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 4d8+6 bludgeoning.

## Rage of Elements

- **Metal Elemental** 40 feet, fly 40 feet; resistance 10 to electricity; **Arcing Electricity** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]]) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 2d12 electricity damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC; a creature made of metal, with the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait, or wearing metal armor takes a –1 circumstance penalty on this save. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** single action blade ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile|versatile slashing]]), **Damage** 3d12+15 piercing; **Ranged** single action metal shard ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile|versatile slashing]]), **Damage** 4d8+6 piercing. The metal Strike is Dexterity based.
- **Wood Elemental** 30 feet, climb 35 feet; resistance 5 to physical; weakness to [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axes]] 10; **Lush Growth** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) 30 feet. Ground in the area is difficult terrain, and any time a creature in the area regains HP from a [[Traits/Healing|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect, the aura grants a +5 status bonus to the healing; **Woody Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** single action branch ([[Traits/Reach|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10+18 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action seed ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]]), **Damage** 4d8+6 bludgeoning. The wood Strike is Strength based.
