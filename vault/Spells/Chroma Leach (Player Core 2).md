# Chroma Leach two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 242

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Your hand glows with impossible colors from beyond the stars, and your touch saps both color and vitality from the living. The target must attempt a Fortitude save; creatures with the [[Traits/Gnome (Player Core)|gnome]] trait take a –2 circumstance penalty to this save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]]. The target is also filled with listlessness and ennui. For 1 round, if the target tries to use a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action, it must succeed at a Will save against your spell DC or the action is lost; this effect has the [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] and [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] traits.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is permanently enfeebled 2 and drained 2 (although magic such as [[Spells/Sound Body|_sound body_]] can remove these conditions).
