# Remake Physiology two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 159

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

At your touch, the target's body shifts and transmutes into random mismatched animal parts, such as [[Bestiary/Families/Crab (Monster Core 2)|crab]] claws for hands and mismatched gazelle and [[Bestiary/Gorilla (Monster Core)|gorilla]] legs. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

- Due to the unbalanced body weight and mismatched limbs, the target takes a –20-foot status penalty to its Speeds.
- The creature's mind and body no longer align, making it [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 1]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]].
- The target's new eyes make depth perception difficult; the target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]].
- Whenever the target would regain actions at the start of its turn, it must first attempt a DC 5 [[Rules/Flat Checks|flat check]]; on a failure, it's [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed 1]] for a round.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is affected for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is affected for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The effect is permanent.
