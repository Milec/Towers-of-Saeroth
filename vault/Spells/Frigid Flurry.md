# Frigid Flurry two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 108

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Thremyr]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** line up to 120 feet

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You place a palm to your lips and exhale a cold breath, whipping up a gust of wind that freezes the air's ambient moisture into a flurry of jagged shards. The flurry deals 9d6 cold damage and 9d6 slashing damage to all foes, with a basic Reflex save, but the gust flows harmlessly around your allies. The wind then picks you up and carries you to the other end of the area; though your movement within the gust can still trigger reactions, the storm of ice crystals prevents you from being affected by any effects, except for those that would [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the spell's magic or that the GM decides would affect snow.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The cold and slashing damage each increase by 1d6.
