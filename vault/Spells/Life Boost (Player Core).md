# Life Boost single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 385

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Life (Player Core)|Lesson of Life]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 4 rounds

---

Life force from your patron floods into the target, ensuring they can continue doing your patron's will for just a little longer. The target gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The fast healing increases by 2.
