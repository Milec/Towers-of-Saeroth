# Adapt Self two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 112

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Change]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You make subtle changes to yourself to adapt to the situation. Choose one of the following options that applies to you.

-  If you are in water, you gain a swim Speed equal to half your Speed.
-  If you are in water, you become able to breathe water.
-  If it’s too dark for you to see, you gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
-  If you are in an environment of [[Rules/Temperature|severe heat or severe cold]], you are protected from the effects of severe heat or severe cold, and the spell lasts 10 minutes.
