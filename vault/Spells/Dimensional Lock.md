# Dimensional Lock two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 329

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 60-foot burst

**Duration** until the next time you make your daily preparations.

---

You create a shimmering barrier that attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effects and planar travel into or out of the area, including items that allow access to [[Traits/Extradimensional|extradimensional]] spaces (such as a [[Equipment/Bag of Holding|_bag of holding_]]). _Dimensional lock_ tries to counteract any attempt to summon a creature into the area but doesn't stop the creature from departing when the summoning ends.
