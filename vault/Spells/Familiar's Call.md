# Familiar's Call three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 137

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 miles

**Target** your familiar

---

Your familiar dissolves and rematerializes in your space. The exact appearance of this teleportation differs based on your tradition (a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-663)|druid's]] familiar might fall into a spray of autumn leaves, a shadowy familiar might simply fade away), but it's always clearly supernatural.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can call your familiar so long as your familiar is on the same planet as you.

**Heightened (7th)** You can call your familiar so long as your familiar is on the same plane of existence as you.

**Heightened (9th)** You can call your familiar even if the familiar is on a different plane of existence.
