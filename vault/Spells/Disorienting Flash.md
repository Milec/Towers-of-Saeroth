# Disorienting Flash single action to three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 133

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** varies

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You create a small explosion of bright light and ear-piercing sound that rocks the senses. Creatures in the area take sonic damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the damage and other parameters.

single action This spell deals 1d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 5-foot square. Creatures that fail their save become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] until the end of your next turn.  
two actions This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 10- foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]. Creatures that fail their save become dazzled until the end of your next turn.  
three actions This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 20- foot burst. Creatures that fail their save become dazzled and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] until the end of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10 for all versions.
