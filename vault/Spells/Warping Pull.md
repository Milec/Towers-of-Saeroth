# Warping Pull ⤾  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 149

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Kyonin have access to this option._

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Trigger** An effect would deal damage to an ally within range.

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature that would take damage from the triggering effect

---

You get an ally out of danger with a teleporting pull. You teleport the target to an unoccupied square up to 10 feet closer to you. The target gains [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to all damage against the triggering effect.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
