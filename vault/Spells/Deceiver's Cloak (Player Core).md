# Deceiver's Cloak two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 384

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Mischief (Player Core)|Lesson of Mischief]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

Your patron drapes a cloak of illusion about you, making you appear as another creature of the same body shape with roughly similar height and weight as yourself. This has the effects of 3rd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise|_illusory disguise_]].

---
**Heightened (6th)** You can appear as any creature of the same size, even with a completely different body shape.
