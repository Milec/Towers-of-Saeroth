# Blinding Bottle two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 84

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The poison inflicted by this spell has the incapacitation trait, but the spell itself does not. This means that a creature which fails the save against the spell begins at stage 1 for the poison._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You conjure an exploding glass container filled with a sightstealing poison and hurl it across enemy lines. Upon impact, the bottle bursts and exposes all creatures in the area to the toxin within. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 3d6 [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage.  

**Failure** The creature is afflicted with blinding poison at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is afflicted with blinding poison at stage 2.

**Blinding Poison** ([[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) **Level** 9; **Maximum Duration** 4 rounds; **Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 2** 4d6 poison damage and blinded for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 3** 5d6 poison damage and blinded for 1 round (1 round); **Stage 4** 6d6 poison damage and blinded for 1 minute (1 round)
