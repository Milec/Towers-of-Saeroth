# Symphony of the Unfettered Heart two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 228

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** you or 1 ally

---

Your symphony lifts listeners from their worldly concerns. Attempt a [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] check to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one of the following conditions affecting the target: [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained|restrained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]]. If you fail, you can't target that effect on the target for 1 day. Use the condition's source to determine the counteract DC (for example, the [[Actions/Escape]] DC for grabbed).

---
**Heightened (9th)** You can target up to four creatures.
