# Pillar of Water three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 174, [[Sources/Pathfinder -152- Legacy of the Lost God|Pathfinder #152: Legacy of the Lost God]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a large, self-contained cylinder of still, clear fresh water. You choose the radius and height of the pillar, to a maximum radius of 15 feet and a maximum height of 60 feet. Creatures inside the pillar can [[Actions/Swim]] through the water as normal ([[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] DC 10), or if the spell was cast on solid ground, walk along the bottom (which is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]). Any creature that exits the pillar falls or lands accordingly.
