# Let not the Fallen Rest three actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 72

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This spell affects unconscious allies with a dying value of 2 or less, in addition conscious allies with the dying 1 or 2 condition. Conscious characters are not considered to have the dying 0 condition and are unaffected._

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aesir]]

**Area** 30-foot-radius [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

---

You exhort your fallen comrades to return to the battle. Each allied creature within the emanation whose [[Rules/Conditions/Dying (Player Core)|dying]] value is 2 or less regains 4d10 Hit Points and can [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] as a free action, which doesn’t provoke reactions.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d10.
