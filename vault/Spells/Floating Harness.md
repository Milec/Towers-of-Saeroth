# Floating Harness two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 139

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** your companion, eidolon, or familiar

**Duration** 1 hour

---

The target is supported by a magical harness that keeps it next to you wherever you may go. The target remains adjacent to you, regardless of your form of movement, even if they wouldn't typically have that form of movement, such as flying, climbing, or swimming. The target can't move to a space that isn't adjacent to you while the spell is active. If [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]] causes either of you to no longer be adjacent to each other, the spell ends. You can Dismiss this spell.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The duration increases to 8 hours.
