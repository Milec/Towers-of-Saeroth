# Devil Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 132

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You bind yourself to the power of [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]], transforming into a Medium devil battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose coarti, levaloch, sarglagon, or vordine. If you choose levaloch or sarglagon, the battle form is Large, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Devil (Player Core)|devil]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]], [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to physical damage (except [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]), and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- Resistance 10 to [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +20, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of devil you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Coarti]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; **Melee** single action morningstar ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning plus 1d6 spirit; **Melee** single action wing ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]]), **Damage** 1d6 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Levaloch (Monster Core 2)|Levaloch]]** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]] 30 feet; +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude DC against being [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] and to other saving throws to resist being moved against your will; **Melee** single action trident ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing; **Ranged** single action trident ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown 20 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing; all melee Strikes deal an additional 2d6 precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] creatures.
- **[[Bestiary/Sarglagon (Monster Core)|Sarglagon]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 25 feet, [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim]] 30 feet; **Melee** single action fangs ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d10 piercing; **Melee** single action tentacle arm ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning plus 1d6 poison.
- **[[Bestiary/Vordine]]** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action trident ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing; **Melee** single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d4 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action whip ([[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d4 slashing; **Ranged** single action trident (magical, thrown 20 feet, unholy), **Damage** 2d8 piercing.
