# Rapid Retreat ⤾  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Teleportation]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 148

**Trigger** You take damage from an [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] or spell

**Range** 20 feet

---

You know how to swiftly escape from harm. You instantly teleport out of danger into an unoccupied space within range, and you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] to all damage against the triggering damage equal to your level. You can’t [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]] if there’s no space to move into.
