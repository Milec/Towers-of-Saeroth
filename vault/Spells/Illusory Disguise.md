# Illusory Disguise two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 345

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Genie]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Children of the Night]], [[Setting/Deities/Cormion]], [[Setting/Deities/Hastur (Divine Mysteries)|Hastur]], [[Setting/Deities/Likha (Divine Mysteries)|Likha]], [[Setting/Deities/Mahathallah (Divine Mysteries)|Mahathallah]], [[Setting/Deities/Musagani]], [[Setting/Deities/Nivi Rhombodazzle (Divine Mysteries)|Nivi Rhombodazzle]], [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber]], [[Setting/Deities/Shadow Cabinet]], [[Setting/Deities/Sivanah (Divine Mysteries)|Sivanah]], [[Setting/Deities/Skrymir]], [[Setting/Deities/The Lantern King (Divine Mysteries)|The Lantern King]], [[Setting/Deities/Turvu]], [[Setting/Deities/Venkelvore]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You create an illusion that causes you to appear as another creature of the same body shape, and with roughly similar height (within 6 inches) and weight (within 50 pounds), as yourself. The disguise is typically good enough to hide your identity, but not to impersonate a specific individual. The spell doesn't change your voice, scent, or mannerisms. You can change the appearance of your clothing and worn items, such as making your armor look like a dress. Held items are unaffected, and any worn item you remove returns to its true appearance.

 Casting _illusory disguise_ counts as setting up a disguise for the [[Actions/Impersonate]] use of [[Rules/Skills/Deception]]; it ignores any circumstance penalties you might take for disguising yourself as a dissimilar creature, it gives you a +4 status bonus to Deception checks to prevent others from seeing through your disguise, and you add your level even if you're untrained. You can Dismiss this spell.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** The spell also disguises your voice and scent, and it gains the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] and [[Traits/Olfactory|olfactory]] traits.

**Heightened (3rd)** You can appear as any creature of the same size, even a specific individual. You must have seen an individual to take on their appearance. The spell also disguises your voice and scent, and it gains the auditory trait.
