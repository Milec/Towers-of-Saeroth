# Augmented Body single action  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 81

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You magically augment your body with clockwork parts and magitech innovations. Choose any one effect of your choice. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

- **Ablative Plating** You coat your body with metal plates that protect your vital organs. You gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 1 to physical damage.
- **Clockwork Arm** You transform one of your arms into a clockwork arm powered by gears, springs, and pneumatic actuators. You gain a clockwork fist [[Rules/Unarmed Attacks (Player Core)|unarmed attack]], which has the [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], and [[Traits/Free-Hand (Player Core)|free-hand]] traits and deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
- **Spined Fingertips** Your fingers sprout microscopic metal spines that enable you to cling to surfaces like a [[Bestiary/Families/Spider (Monster Core)|spider]]. You gain a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 20 feet.
- **Spring-Loaded Legs** Your leg muscles are augmented with powerful springs. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speed and double the distance you [[Actions/Leap (Player Core)|Leap]].

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The ablative plating’s resistance increases to 2. The clockwork fist is a [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|_+1 striking_]] weapon.

**Heightened (5th)** The ablative plating’s resistance increases to 3, and the clockwork fist is a [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|_+2 striking_]] weapon.

**Heightened (7th)** The ablative plating’s resistance increases to 4, and the clockwork fist is a _[[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|+2 greater striking]]_ weapon.

**Heightened (9th)** The ablative plating’s resistance increases to 5 and the clockwork fist is a [[Equipment/Magic Weapon (GM Core)|_+3 major striking_]] weapon.
