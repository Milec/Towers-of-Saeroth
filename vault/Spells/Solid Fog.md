# Solid Fog three actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 370

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure a bank of fog so thick it impedes movement as well as sight. This functions as [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]], except that the area is also difficult terrain. You can Dismiss the spell.
