# Hypnopompic Terrors two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Monsters of Myth]] pg. 89

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** up to 10 creatures

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You send a wave of nightmarish visions crashing over your targets, leaving them paralyzed by fear. These visions inflict real wounds on vulnerable targets, dealing 6d12 mental damage. If you target a sleeping creature, it wakes up as it attempts the save but takes one degree of success worse than the result it rolled on its save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] and takes half the mental damage.  

**Failure** The target is frightened 2, is [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round, and takes the full mental damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 2, is paralyzed for 2 rounds, and takes double the mental damage.
