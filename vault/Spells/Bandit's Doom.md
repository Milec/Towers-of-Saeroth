# Bandit's Doom  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Curse]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 90

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** 30 minutes ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Target** 1 item of 2 Bulk or less

**Duration** 1 month

---

You ward the target item against those who would steal it. When a creature attempts to take the target into its possession, it takes 8d8 mental damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]], depending on the result of its Will save. The spell then ends. You can choose up to 10 creatures when you cast this spell; if you do, those creatures can take the target item without triggering the ward. You can only have one _bandit's doom_ active at a time. If you cast the spell again, the newer casting supersedes the older.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is doomed 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is doomed 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is doomed 3.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 2d8.
