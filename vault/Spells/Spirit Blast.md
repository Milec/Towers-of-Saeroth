# Spirit Blast two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 371

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Psychopomp]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You concentrate ethereal energy and attack a creature's spirit, dealing 16d6 force damage. Because _spirit blast_ affects the creature's spirit, it can damage a target projecting its consciousness (such as via [[Spells/Project Image|_project image_]]) or possessing another creature even if the target's body is elsewhere. The possessed creature isn't harmed by the blast. The blast doesn't harm creatures that have no spirit, such as constructs.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
