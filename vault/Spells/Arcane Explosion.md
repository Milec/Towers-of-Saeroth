# Arcane Explosion two actions  *Focus 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 141

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

Your body is temporarily converted into a ball of pure magic, rapidly exploding outward and reforming as a glowing sphere, after which you eventually regain your normal form. All creatures and objects in the emanation take 16d6 [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude saving throw). Creatures that fail are also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet directly away from you; creatures that can't complete this forced movement are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] (but creatures who reduce this movement, such as with an ability or feat, don't fall prone). You become unfiltered magic; you gain the [[Traits/Incorporeal (GM Core)|incorporeal]] trait, are immune to [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]], and precision damage, and gain resistance to all damage equal to your level (except force damage, [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage, and damage from Strikes with the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch property rune]]), with double the resistance against non-[[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] damage. While incorporeal, you can't Strike, Cast a Spell, or take any actions with the [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you while you're in this state takes 6d6 force damage. You remain as pure magic until you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] your new form, returning to your normal state at maximum Hit Points.
