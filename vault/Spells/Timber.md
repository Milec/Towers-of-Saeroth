# Timber two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 198

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 15-foot line

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You create a small dead tree in your space that falls over on anyone in its path, then immediately decomposes. Any creature in the area takes 2d4 bludgeoning damage, with a basic Reflex saving throw. A creature that critically fails its save is knocked for a loop, making it [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial damage increases by 1d4.
