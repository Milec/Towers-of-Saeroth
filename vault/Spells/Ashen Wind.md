# Ashen Wind two actions  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -185- A Taste of Ashes|Pathfinder #185: A Taste of Ashes]] pg. 81

**Mysteries**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ash]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ash (Divine Mysteries)|Ash]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You conjure a swirling wind of ashes that blows through the area. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save or get the cloying ash in its lungs.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2.
