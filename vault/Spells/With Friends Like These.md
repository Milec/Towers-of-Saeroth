# With Friends Like These two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 2 creatures

**Defense**
Will

---

You create a subtle dissonance between creatures, driving them apart.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature becomes sickened 2. While sickened this way, it doesn’t treat any other target of the spell as an ally.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and it can’t end a [[Traits/Move (Player Core)|move]] action within 10 feet of any other target as long as it is sickened.

---
**Heightened (+2)** You can target one additional creature.
