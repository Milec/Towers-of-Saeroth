# Bone Spear two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 181

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You shape a thrall into a spear of jagged bone. Destroy the target thrall, and each creature in a 15-foot [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] originating from the thrall’s former space takes 2d6 piercing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
