# Palm-Held Sun two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 156

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You open a rift into [[Setting/Planes/Creation's Forge|Creation’s Forge]], igniting it into a miniature star that blazes with mighty light. You shed [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] to the next 30 feet). When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast]] or [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell, each creature with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]] in the aura takes 1 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage, and any creature in the aura with a particular vulnerability to sunlight becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save. After a creature in the aura loses HP due to fire damage from any source, it takes 1 vitality damage.
