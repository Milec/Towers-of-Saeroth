# Pest Form two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 356

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Bastet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Bastet]], [[Setting/Deities/Daikitsu (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Daikitsu]], [[Setting/Deities/Grandmother Spider (Divine Mysteries)|Grandmother Spider]], [[Setting/Deities/Sigek]], [[Setting/Deities/Ydersius (Divine Mysteries)|Ydersius]], [[Setting/Deities/Zevgavizeb (Divine Mysteries)|Zevgavizeb]]

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Mosquito Witch]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You transform into the battle form of a Tiny animal, such as a cat, insect, lizard, or rat. You can decide the specific type of animal (such as a rat or praying mantis), but this has no effect on the form's Size or statistics. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait, and you can't make Strikes. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities:

- AC = 15 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- Speed 20 feet.
- Weakness 5 to physical damage. (If you take physical damage in this form, you take 5 additional damage.)
- [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]] and imprecise [[Rules/Scent|scent]] 30 feet.
- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] modifiers of +10, unless your own modifier is higher; [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier –4.

---
**Heightened (4th)** You can turn into a flying creature, such as a bird, which grants you a fly Speed of 20 feet.
