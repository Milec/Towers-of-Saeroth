# Tangling Creepers three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 377

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Green Man (Monster Core 2)|Green Man]], [[Setting/Deities/Immaculate Growth]], [[Setting/Deities/Kagia]], [[Setting/Deities/Osiris (Divine Mysteries)|Osiris]], [[Setting/Deities/Telvrys]], [[Setting/Deities/Treerazer (Divine Mysteries)|Treerazer]], [[Setting/Deities/Yuelral (Divine Mysteries)|Yuelral]], [[Setting/Deities/Zibik]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 40-foot burst

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Dense, twitching creepers sprout from every surface and fill any bodies of water in the area. Any creature moving on the land, or [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]] or [[Actions/Swim|Swimming]] within the creepers, takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds while in the area. Once per round, you can make a vine lash out from any square within the expanse of creepers by using a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait. This vine has a 15-foot reach. Make a melee spell attack roll against the target; on a success, the vine pulls the target into the creepers and makes it [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round or until the creature [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]] (against your spell DC), whichever comes first.
