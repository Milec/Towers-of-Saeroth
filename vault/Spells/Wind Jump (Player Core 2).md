# Wind Jump single action  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 257

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You gain a fly Speed equal to your Speed. You must end your turn on solid ground, or you fall.

---
**Heightened (6th)** At the end of your turn, you can attempt a DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] check to find purchase in midair. If you succeed, you don't fall.
