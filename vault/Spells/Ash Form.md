# Ash Form two actions  *Focus 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -185- A Taste of Ashes|Pathfinder #185: A Taste of Ashes]] pg. 81

**Mysteries**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ash]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ash (Divine Mysteries)|Ash]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You take the form of a cloud of minuscule ash particles. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 3, resistance 10 to piercing and slashing damage, and immunity to bleed, [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]], and precision damage. You can't move through solid objects, but you can stream through tiny gaps as if they were difficult terrain. Any ability with the [[Traits/Air|air]] trait that involves blowing or shifting wind (such as [[Spells/Gust of Wind|_gust of wind_]]) deals you 1d6 bludgeoning damage per level of the spell each round you are in its area, in addition to its normal effects.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The resistance increases to 15.
