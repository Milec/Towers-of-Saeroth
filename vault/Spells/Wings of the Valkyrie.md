# Wings of the Valkyrie two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 71

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aesir]]

**Duration** 3 rounds

---

Powerful feathered wings—usually vibrant white or jet black— emerge from your back, granting you a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] equal to your Speed. You can use these wings to attempt to [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] a creature; you don’t need a free hand to do so, and you can roll using your spell attack modifier instead of your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] skill for the check. When this spell’s duration ends, if you’re still flying, you float to the ground, as [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]].

---
**Heightened (5th)** The duration increases to 1 minute.
