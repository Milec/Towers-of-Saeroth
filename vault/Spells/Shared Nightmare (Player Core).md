# Shared Nightmare two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 377

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Nightmares (Player Core)|Nightmares]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

Merging minds with the target, you swap distressing visions. One of you will become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], based on the target's Will save.

**Critical Success** You are confused for 1 round.  

**Success** At the start of your next turn, you spend your first action with the confused condition, then act normally.  

**Failure** The target spends the first action of each of its turns confused. The duration is 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is confused. The duration is 1 minute.
