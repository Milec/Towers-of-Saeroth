# Updraft two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 60, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 201

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

A powerful blast of wind erupts from the ground, launching the target into the air and sending it crashing back down. The collision deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex save. On a failure, the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6.
