# Necromancer's Generosity two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 154

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 undead minion you control

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You channel void energy through your magical connection to your undead minion to strengthen the creature. The target regains 1d8+4 Hit Points when you Cast the Spell, and it gains a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effects for the duration.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d8+4.
