# Hunter's Vision single action  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 235

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 hunted prey

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Your target glows with a magical aura visible only to you and those who follow your lead. Your target is visible to you and others sharing your [[Actions/Hunt Prey]] benefits even if it wouldn't normally be due to lighting or the [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] conditions, though cover from opaque objects still blocks your sight. You ignore the flat check against the target due to the concealed condition, and the target isn't automatically [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] from you due to darkness or being invisible.
