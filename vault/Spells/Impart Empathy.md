# Impart Empathy two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Quest for the Frozen Flame

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -175- Broken Tusk Moon|Pathfinder #175: Broken Tusk Moon]] pg. 80

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1  animal

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 day

---

You expand the target's ability to understand social cues that are normally beyond its comprehension. Any creature to which the target isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Unfriendly|unfriendly]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] can use [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] to [[Actions/Make an Impression]] on it and to make very simple [[Actions/Request|Requests]] of it. This doesn't render the animal any more inclined to help than it otherwise would be.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The spell can also target [[Traits/Plant|plants]] and [[Traits/Fungus|fungi]].
