# Unblinking Flame Ignition ⤾  *Focus 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 223

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Trigger** A foe reduces you to half your Hit Points or fewer.

**Duration** 3 rounds

---

You feel the most alive when pushed into a corner, as the insights and vision granted by the flame stoke into overdrive. Your body begins to emit a blazing aura of flames. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and the ability to see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures. You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed as you launch flame from your aura to soar through the sky. You become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]], and you can use the extra action each round only to Strike or [[Actions/Fly]] using your flames. Any creature who starts their turn within 5 feet of you takes 1d12 fire damage.
