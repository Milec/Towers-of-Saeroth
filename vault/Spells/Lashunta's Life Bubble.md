# Lashunta's Life Bubble single action  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Gatewalkers

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Air]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -187- The Seventh Arch|Pathfinder #187: The Seventh Arch]] pg. 81

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 8 hours

---

You create a replenishing protective bubble of fresh, temperate air around the target. The shell of air enables the target to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|breathe]] even underwater or in a vacuum, protects from [[Rules/Temperature|severe cold and heat]], and grants a +1 status bonus to the target's saves against inhaled dangers such as [[Traits/Inhaled|inhaled]] poisons and [[Spells/Cloudkill|_cloudkill_]].

---
**Heightened (6th)** The bubble lasts until your next preparation.

**Heightened (8th)** The bubble lasts until your next preparation, protects against extreme cold and heat, and grants a +2 status bonus instead of +1.
