# Entreat the Many two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 295

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

---

You manifest echoes of all the spirits you’ve ever bonded with at once, in a spiraling cyclone of colorful forms. They deal 8d8 [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage to each enemy in the area, with a basic Fortitude save. Additionally, creatures that critically fail the save are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
