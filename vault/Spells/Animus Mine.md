# Animus Mine two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 106

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Arazni (Divine Mysteries)|Arazni]], [[Setting/Deities/Lissala (Divine Mysteries)|Lissala]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Faceless Sphinx)]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You implant a mental mine within your psyche that detonates against anyone attempting to manipulate your thoughts. You can use a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to suppress the effects of the mine for 1 round to allow someone to safely use a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect on you. You can Dismiss the spell.

 The first creature that uses a [[Traits/Magical|magical]] mental effect against you triggers the _animus mine_, causing the spell to end. The _animus mine_ deals 4d8 mental damage to the triggering creature, which must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is stunned 1. You are unaffected by the triggering mental effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d8.
