# Obscuring Mist three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 355

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call forth a cloud of mist. All creatures within the mist become concealed, and all creatures outside the mist become concealed to creatures within it. You can Dismiss the cloud.
