# Haunting Spirit two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 142

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call an [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] spirit that only the target can see to haunt them. At the start of each of the target’s turns, it must attempt a Will saving throw. The target can spend its entire turn running as far away as possible to escape the spirit and end the spell early.

**Critical Success** The spell immediately ends.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** As success, and the target takes 4d8 spirit damage.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the target takes 8d8 spirit damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage is increased by 1d8 (2d8 on a critical failure).
