# Fiendish Rift two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 138

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You open a tear in reality that leads to a fiendish plane such as [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon (GM Core)|Abaddon]], [[Setting/Planes/Hell (GM Core)|Hell]], or the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]]. Fiends reach out of the rift, grabbing at creatures with various appendages. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you Sustain it each turn on subsequent rounds, each creature in the area takes 7d6 spirit damage; creatures with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait take an additional 10 spirit damage. An affected creature attempts a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] for 1 round or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] for 1 round or until it Escapes.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
