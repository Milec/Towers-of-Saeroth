# Fey Glamour two actions  *Focus 5*

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 264

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Fey (Player Core 2)|Fey]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst or

**Target** up to 10 willing creatures

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You call upon fey glamours to cloak an area or the targets in illusion. This has the effect of either [[Spells/Illusory Scene (Player Core)|_illusory scene_]] on the area or [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] on the creatures, as if heightened to a rank 1 rank lower than _fey glamour_, and using _fey glamour's_ range and duration.
