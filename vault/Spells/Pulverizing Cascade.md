# Pulverizing Cascade two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 61, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 201

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot radius, 20-foot-tall  cylinder

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You raise a pair of towering waves and slam them into each other, crushing creatures caught between them. Creatures in the area take 5d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6.
