# Bridge of Graves two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 181

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You summon a bridge formed from grave dirt and tombstones that allow thralls to move with great speed. The 10-foot-wide span must start at the ground on a point within range, and it extends as a horizontal plane, either in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] or at any angle up to 45 degrees upward or downward, for up to 120 feet. This bridge has AC 10, Hardness 30, and 40 Hit Points. A thrall on or adjacent to the bridge can move to any location on the bridge when you command it to [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]]. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The length of the bridge increases by 20 feet, its Hit Points increase by 20, and you can increase the width of the span by up to an additional 5 feet.
