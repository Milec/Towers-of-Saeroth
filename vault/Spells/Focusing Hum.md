# Focusing Hum single action  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Enchantment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 105

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation centered on you

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You infuse the air around you with a sustained hum that allows creatures within it to resist distractions. The DC of any flat check to Cast a Spell while [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] for creatures within the area is 2 lower. [[Rules/Conditions/Confused]] creatures in the area don't attack anyone, including themselves, and instead lose the ability to speak coherently. You take a –2 penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]].
