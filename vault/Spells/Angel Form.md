# Angel Form two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 89

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Invoking the celestial realms, you transform into a Medium angel battle form. When you cast this spell, choose [[Bestiary/Balisse (Monster Core)|balisse]], [[Bestiary/Choral (Monster Core)|choral]], kuribu, or tennin. The battle form is Small if you choose choral or kuribu. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Angel (Player Core)|angel]] and [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 40 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and weakness 10 to [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and you use the listed damage. Melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example), and ranged attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +25, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of angel you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Balisse (Monster Core)|Balisse]]** Speed 30 feet, fly 40 feet; [[Rules/Perception (rules-2298)|Perception]] modifier of +26 to detect lies and [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusions]] unless your own modifier is higher; **Melee** single action [[Equipment/Weapons/Scimitar (Player Core)|scimitar]] ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Forceful (Player Core)|forceful]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Sweep (Player Core)|sweep]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 slashing plus 2d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Choral (Monster Core)|Choral]]** Speed 30 feet, fly 40 feet; +1 status bonus to AC and saves against [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] and [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]]; **Melee** single action [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]] ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action piercing hymn ([[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range 90 feet]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]), **Damage** 3d6+12 sonic, and [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round on a critical hit.
- **Kuribu** Speed 25 feet, fly 40 feet; [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]] and [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]; **Melee** single action [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]] ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]]), **Damage** 3d4+12 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action [[Equipment/Weapons/Composite Shortbow (Player Core)|composite shortbow]] ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly 2d10]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 3d6+12 piercing.
- **Tennin** Speed 30 feet, fly 60 feet; can [[Actions/Step (Player Core)|Step]] into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and use land and fly Speeds to Step; **Melee** single action [[Equipment/Weapons/Fist (Player Core)|fist]] ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]]), **Damage** 1d6+12 bludgeoning plus 1d6 cold and 1d6 spirit.

---
**Heightened (9th)** Your battle form is Large. You must have space to expand, or the spell is lost. You instead gain 60 temporary Hit Points, weakness 15 to evil damage, attack modifier +30, damage bonus +20, and Athletics +33.
