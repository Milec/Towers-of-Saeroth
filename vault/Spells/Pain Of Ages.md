# Pain Of Ages two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 99

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Will

---

You sense a spiritual resonance of deep anguish in the earth beneath your feet, the walls surrounding you, or the fragments of souls left behind in a place of calamity. You draw on this reservoir of pain and unleash its force against your enemies, dealing 8d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] for 1 round as it reels from the impact of so much anguish.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d8.
