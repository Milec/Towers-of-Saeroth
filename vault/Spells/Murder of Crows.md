# Murder of Crows two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 154

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You send out a flock of crows that torments the target with beak and talon. Make a ranged spell attack against a target’s AC. On a hit, the target takes 6d4 piercing damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round (double damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round on a critical hit).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 3d4.
