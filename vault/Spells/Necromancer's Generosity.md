# Necromancer's Generosity two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 117

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1  undead   minion  you control

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You channel negative energy through your magical connection to your undead minion to strengthen the creature. The target regains 1d8+4 Hit Points when you Cast the Spell, and it gains a +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Positive|positive]] effects for the duration.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d8+4.
