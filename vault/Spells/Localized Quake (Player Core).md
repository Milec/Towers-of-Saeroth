# Localized Quake two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 374

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation or 15-foot cone

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You shake the earth, toppling nearby creatures. Choose whether the spell's area is a 15-foot emanation or a 15-foot cone when you cast it. Each creature in the area standing on solid ground takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Reflex saving throw. A creature that fails its save also falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6.
