# Inscrutable Mask two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 227

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You shroud your form and features in an impenetrable mask of shadow. The mask grants you a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Lie]] or [[Actions/Feint]], [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks to [[Actions/Demoralize]], and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] in areas of [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness|darkness]].

---
**Heightened (6th)** The status bonus increases to +2.

**Heightened (9th)** The status bonus increases to +3.
