# Haunting Hymn two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 109

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You echo a jarring hymn that only creatures in the area can hear. The hymn deals 1d8 sonic damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If a target critically fails the save, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
