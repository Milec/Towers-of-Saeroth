# Shatter Mind two actions  *Cantrip 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 21

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will

---

You telepathically assail the minds of your foes. You deal 3d4 mental damage to all enemies in the area, with a basic Will save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.

---

**Amp** You increase the area of the spell to your choice of a 30-foot cone or 60-foot cone, and the damage dice for the spell change to d10s. Creatures that fail are [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] until the start of your next turn (or stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
