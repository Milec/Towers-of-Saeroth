# Telekinetic Haul two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

[[Setting/Deities/Good Neighbors]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Zipacna]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 unattended object of up to 80 Bulk with no dimension longer than 20 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You move the target up to 20 feet, potentially suspending it in midair. When you Sustain the Spell, you can do so again, or you can choose a different eligible target to move.
