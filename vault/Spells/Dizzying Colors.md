# Dizzying Colors two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 326

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Lady Jingxi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lady Jingxi]], [[Setting/Deities/Lalaci]], [[Setting/Deities/Pulura (Divine Mysteries)|Pulura]], [[Setting/Deities/Shelyn (Divine Mysteries)|Shelyn]], [[Setting/Deities/Thisamet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Thisamet]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 or more rounds (see below)

---

You unleash a swirling multitude of colors that overwhelms creatures based on their Will saves.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] 1, [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for 1 round, and dazzled for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned for 1 round and blinded for 1 minute.
