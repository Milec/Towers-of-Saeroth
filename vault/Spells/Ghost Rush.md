# Ghost Rush two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

---

You become temporarily ghostlike as you push forward, moving through obstacles in your path. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speeds, then Stride twice. While you don't become completely incorporeal, this movement ignores the effects of [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]], greater difficult terrain, and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]] and isn't impeded by physical barriers that don't block your line of sight. If the movement would end in a space that would normally be obstructed, it instead ends at the nearest unobstructed space you pass through before reaching it. You can use ghost rush to [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type. If you're riding a mount or piloting a vehicle that's Large or smaller and unencumbered, you can cause the spell to affect the mount or vehicle instead, including any cargo or passengers.
