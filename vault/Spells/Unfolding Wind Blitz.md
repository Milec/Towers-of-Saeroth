# Unfolding Wind Blitz two actions  *Focus 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 223

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

You blitz forward so quickly that you soar through the air, and nothing can react to you. [[Actions/Fly]] up to twice your Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions. At any point along the way, you can make Strikes with an [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack against up to three different targets within your reach. You increase your multiple attack penalty for these attacks only after completing all of the attacks.
