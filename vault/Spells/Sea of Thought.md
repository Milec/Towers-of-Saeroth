# Sea of Thought two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Thoth (Divine Mysteries)|Thoth]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 15-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You cover the area in a sloshing torrent of semi-solidified thought, roughly ankle high. The area becomes difficult terrain, similarly to a shallow bog. Each round that a creature starts its turn in the area, it must attempt a Fortitude save against the shifting waves of thought. On a failure, it takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it leaves the area, and on a critical failure, it is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The radius of the spell's area increases by 5 feet.
