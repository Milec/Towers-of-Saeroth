# Spinning Staff single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 144

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Requirements** You're wielding a [[Equipment/Weapons/Staff|staff]].

---

You spin your staff with a twirling flourish, attacking two foes and transforming the momentum into magical energy to charge for your next [[Actions/Spellstrike]]. Make a melee Strike with your staff against one foe and then a second melee Strike with your staff against a different foe. Your multiple attack penalty applies to both of these attacks as normal.
