# Rainbow's End two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 156

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You reach upward to wrest down a rainbow and harness its power to connect this world to the heavens. Each creature in the area takes 1d4 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. Any creature that fails this save is additionally [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round. For the spell's duration, an ally who's adjacent to you can [[Actions/Interact (Player Core)|Interact]] and be instantly teleported to an unoccupied space in the spell's area, as long as they don't travel more than 60 feet. This effect has the [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|teleportation]] trait.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 2d4, the duration of the dazzled condition on a failed save increases by 1 round, and the maximum distance an ally can use the rainbow to teleport increases by 10 feet
