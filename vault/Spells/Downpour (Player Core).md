# Downpour two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 381

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call forth a torrential downpour, which extinguishes non-magical flames. Creatures in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] and gain fire resistance 10. Creatures outside the area are concealed to those inside the area. If a creature with weakness to water ends its turn in the area, the downpour triggers its weakness.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The fire resistance increases by 2.
