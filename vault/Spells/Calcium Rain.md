You conjure a horrific cloud of tiny dancing bone shards that collapses like a torrent of rain upon those unfortunate enough to be nearby. Creatures in the area take 10d4 piercing damage and 5 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
