# Living Thunderbolt two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 151

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You transform into a being of pure lightning, allowing you to move at impossible speeds. You gain a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 60 feet, [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to electricity, and automatically succeed at attempts to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] an effect that has you [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] unless the effect is magical and of a higher rank than the _living thunderbolt_ spell. Additionally, you can move through creatures’ spaces as if they weren’t there, but you can’t end your movement in another creature’s space. When you move through a creature’s space, they take 4d12 electricity damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. On a failure, they’re also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round. A creature can only take damage this way once per round.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The electricity resistance increases by 5 and the damage dealt when you move through a creature’s space increases by 1d12.
