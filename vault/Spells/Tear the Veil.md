# Tear the Veil three actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 166

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 300 feet

**Area** 60-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You cause the barrier between the Universe and the Void to weaken as waves of void energy pull the living toward the tear to their inevitable doom. The area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] to all living creatures. When you Cast this Spell and the first time each round you Sustain it on subsequent rounds, all living creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.  

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target takes 5d12 void damage and is pulled 15 feet toward the center.  
**Failure** The target takes 10d12 void damage and is pulled 30 feet toward the center.  
**Critical Failure** The target takes 20d12 void damage and is pulled 30 feet toward the center.  

There’s an additional 5-foot burst in the center of the area that drains the life away from all who touch it. Whenever a living creature begins its turn in this burst, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1, or its drained condition increases by 1 to a maximum of drained 4. A target that is drained 4 and begins its turn in the center burst must attempt a Fortitude save that has the [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] and [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] traits; on a failure, it is reduced to 0 Hit Points, crumbles into ash, and becomes a wraith. The gear of the creature that’s transformed in this way falls to the ground in its space.
