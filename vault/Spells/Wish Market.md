# Wish Market single action  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Hell's Destiny

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -223- Hell's Destiny|Pathfinder #223: Hell's Destiny]] pg. 227

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain an Effect|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You signal your willingness to make a deal, attracting ghostly merchants that eagerly and silently wave contracts for your consideration while demanding an esoteric price. When you Cast this Spell, you accept an offer from the merchants. You gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Perception (Player Core)|Perception]] checks and your choice of [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]], [[Rules/Skills/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism (Player Core)|Occultism]], or [[Rules/Skills/Religion (Player Core)|Religion]] checks for the spell’s duration, and you can either [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] or [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] as a free action. However, you also pay a random price; roll 1d4 on the table below. The first time each round you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell, you can either Recall Knowledge or Seek as a free action or haggle with the merchant to suppress the price’s penalty for 1 round.

## Market Price

| **1d4** | **Effect** |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Your Social Graces:** For the spell’s duration, you take a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] and [[Rules/Skills/Society (Player Core)|Society]] checks. |
| 2 | **Your Guile:** For the spell’s duration, you take a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks. |
| 3 | **Your Manual Dexterity:** For the spell’s duration, you take a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] and [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks. |
| 4 | **Your Higher Reasoning:** For the spell’s duration, you take a –2 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Arcana (Player Core)|Arcana]] and [[Rules/Skills/Medicine (Player Core)|Medicine]] checks. |

---
**Heightened (4th)** The status bonus increases to +2.

**Heightened (7th)** The status bonus increases to +3.
