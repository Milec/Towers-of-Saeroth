# Noxious Metals two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 145

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Ferrumnestra (Divine Mysteries)|Ferrumnestra]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

This spell forces toxic metal to coalesce on the skin or surface of all living creatures in the area, dealing 4d6 poison damage with a basic Fortitude save. Any creature that has the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait, has the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait, or has an anathema toward metal, takes the following effects depending on the result of that Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature suffers no additional effects.  

**Success** The creature takes 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]] and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] as long as it takes this persistent damage.  

**Failure** As success, except 1d4 persistent poison damage and sickened 2.  

**Critical Failure** As success, except 1d8 persistent poison damage and sickened 3.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial poison damage increases by 2d6.
