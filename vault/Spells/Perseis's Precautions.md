# Perseis's Precautions three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Fortune]], [[Traits/Prediction]]

**Source** [[Sources/PFS Guide]] pg. 123

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Leaving nothing to chance, you weave divinatory precautions against an ambush, alerting the target to danger. Once during the duration of this spell, the target can roll one Perception, [[Rules/Skills/Arcana]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Crafting Example|Crafting]], [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Examining Forgeries|Society]] initiative roll twice and take the better result, after which the spell ends. The strain of staying so prepared is too much for a mind to handle for long; the target is temporarily immune to this spell for 24 hours once the spell ends, whether they used the initiative benefit or not.
