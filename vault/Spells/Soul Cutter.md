# Soul Cutter single action to three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 163

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** varies

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You conjure a blade of spiritual energy and cut at the soul of a foe. Make a melee spell attack against your target’s AC. On a success, you deal spirit damage. On a critical success, the target takes double damage. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the range, damage, and other parameters.  
single action An adjacent target takes 3d6 spirit damage.  
two actions An adjacent target takes 6d6 spirit damage, and on a hit, it becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 round.  
three actions The blade flies out from you and attacks a target within 30 feet, dealing 6d6 spirit damage. The spell deals half damage on a failure (but not a critical failure), as the blade’s divine influence guides your attack. Unless you critically fail, the target becomes enfeebled 1 for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 for the 1-action version, or by 2d6 for the 2-and 3-action versions.
