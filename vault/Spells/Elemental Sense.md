# Elemental Sense two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 222

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

The elements grant you sensory enhancements. Choose one of the options below. The spell gains the listed trait or traits.

- **Aquatic Echolocation** ([[Traits/Water|water]]) You use your hearing as a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise]] sense when underwater.
- **Heatsense** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) You can sense creatures with a temperature of 90º Fahrenheit or higher within 30 feet as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense.
- **Magnetoreception** ([[Traits/Metal|metal]]) You can sense creatures carrying metal items with a total of 1 Bulk or greater; this is an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. If a creature is made of metal or has the metal trait, you can detect it as though magnetoreception were a precise sense. In addition, you continuously know which direction is north.
- **Mechanoreception** ([[Traits/Air|air]]) You can see [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures and objects. They appear to you as translucent shapes, and they are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to you.
- **Tremorsense** ([[Traits/Earth|earth]]) You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] (imprecise) with a range of 30 feet.
- **Woodsense** ([[Traits/Plant|plant]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) You can sense creatures carrying wood items with a total of 1 Bulk or greater; this is an imprecise sense with a range of 30 feet. If a creature is made of wood or has the plant or wood trait, you can detect it as though woodsense were a precise sense.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 8 hours, and you can take 1 minute to recalibrate the sense, switching from your current elemental sense to a different one.
