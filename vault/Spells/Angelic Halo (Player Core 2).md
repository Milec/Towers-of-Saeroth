# Angelic Halo single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 262

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You gain an angelic halo with an aura that increases allies' healing from the [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] spell. Allies in your halo's emanation who are healed by a _heal_ spell gain a status bonus to Hit Points regained equal to double the heal spell's rank.
