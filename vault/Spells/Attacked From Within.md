# Attacked From Within two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 98

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You assert your power over the target’s own spirit as you reach deep into its soul in order to discover a moment of excruciating anguish, debilitating humiliation, or abject despair. The target takes 12d8 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage and must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target takes no damage, with a brief sensation of pain followed by a pleasant memory.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is frightened 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d8.
