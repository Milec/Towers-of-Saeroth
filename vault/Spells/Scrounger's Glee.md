# Scrounger's Glee single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Hex (Player Core)|Hex]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 61

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Devourer of Decay]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

With a cruel laugh and a flash of your canines, you make a target understand that death is coming to claim it and that its demise will serve to strengthen another. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]] if it fails a Will save (or frightened 2 on a critical failure). This condition can't be reduced below 1 while the spell is active and the target can hear you. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell as a reaction when an ally critically succeeds at a Strike against the target, restoring 1d4 Hit Points to that ally.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The number of Hit Points restored when you [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell increases by 1d4.
