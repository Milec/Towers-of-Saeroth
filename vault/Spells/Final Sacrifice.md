# Final Sacrifice two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 219

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature with the  minion  trait that you summoned or permanently control

---

You channel disruptive energies through the bond between you and your minion, causing it to violently explode. The target is immediately slain, and the explosion deals 6d6 fire damage to creatures within 20 feet of it (basic Reflex save).

 If the target has the [[Traits/Cold|cold]] or [[Traits/Water|water]] trait, the spell instead deals cold damage and gains the cold trait in place of the fire trait. If used on a creature that isn't mindless, this spell has the [[Traits/Evil|evil]] trait. Attempting to cast this spell targeting a creature that you temporarily seized control of, such as an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] commanded by [[Feats/Command Undead|command undead]], automatically fails and breaks the controlling effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
