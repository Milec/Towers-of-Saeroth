# Hydraulic Torrent two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 344

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apep]], [[Setting/Deities/Hei Feng (Divine Mysteries)|Hei Feng]], [[Setting/Deities/Jin Li (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Jin Li]], [[Setting/Deities/Kelizandri (Divine Mysteries)|Kelizandri]], [[Setting/Deities/Matravash (Divine Mysteries)|Matravash]], [[Setting/Deities/Obari]], [[Setting/Deities/Tresmalvos]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 60-foot line

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

A swirling torrent of water manifests along a straight line, battering creatures and unattended objects in its path and possibly pushing them away from you. The torrent deals 8d6 bludgeoning damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Fortitude save; unattended objects automatically fail. Creatures and objects that fail the save are also knocked back 5 feet (10 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
