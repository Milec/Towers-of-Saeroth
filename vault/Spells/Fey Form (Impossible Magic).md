# Fey Form two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 138

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Channeling energy from the mischievous [[Setting/Planes/First World (GM Core)|First World]], you transform into a Medium fey battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose dryad, elananx, naiad, redcap, or unicorn. If you choose redcap, the battle form is Small. If you choose unicorn, the battle form is Large, and you must have space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Fey (Player Core)|fey]] trait. Unless you choose elananx or unicorn, you have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 19 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 15 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Equipment/Cold Iron (GM Core)|cold iron]].
- [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]].
- One or more unarmed melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +16, and your damage bonus is +8. These attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] modifiers of +16, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of fey:

- **[[Bestiary/Dryad (Monster Core)|Dryad]]** Speed 25 feet; weakness 5 to fire; the temporary HP gained increases to 30; **Melee** single action branch, **Damage** 1d12 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Elananx (Monster Core)|Elananx]]** Speed 30 feet; resistance 5 to fire; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 1d6 piercing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]]), **Damage** 1d6 slashing.
- **[[Bestiary/Naiad (Monster Core)|Naiad]]** Speed 25 feet, swim 25 feet; resistance 5 to fire; **Melee** single action aqueous fist ([[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Redcap (Monster Core)|Redcap]]** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** single action scythe ([deadly d10](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4472), [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|trip]]), **Damage** 1d10 slashing; **Melee** single action boot ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile]] B <%END>), **Damage** 1d6 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Unicorn (Monster Core)|Unicorn]]** Speed 40 feet; resistance 5 to [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]; gain the [[Traits/Beast (Player Core)|beast]] and [[Traits/Holy|holy]] traits in addition to fey; **Melee** single action horn ([[Traits/Holy|holy]]), **Damage** 1d10 piercing plus 1 spirit; **Melee** single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]]), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning; all Strikes have the effects of a _[[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|ghost touch]]_ property rune.
