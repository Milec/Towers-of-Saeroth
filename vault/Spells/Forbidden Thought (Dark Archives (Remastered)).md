# Forbidden Thought two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Psychic (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 21

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You place a psychic lock in a foe's mind that prevents it from a specific action. Choose “Strike,” “Stride,” “Cast a Spell,” or a specific action you know the creature to have (such as a [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon's]] magic breath). If the creature attempts that action on its next turn, it must surmount your lock to do so, causing it to take 2d6 mental damage (with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.

---

**Amp** Your telepathic push is particularly shocking to those who resist it. If the target fails its save, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.
