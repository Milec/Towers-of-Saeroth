# Vanish Weapon ⤾  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|Extradimensional]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 170

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Trigger** A creature succeeds at (but not critically succeeds at) or fails a melee Strike with a weapon targeting you.

**Target** The triggering creature

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You create a small extradimensional space that you can use to snag weapons. You attempt to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]] the triggering creature using a melee spell attack roll instead of an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check. You don’t need a hand free for this attempt. If you successfully knock the weapon from the triggering creature’s grasp, it disappears into your extradimensional space. You can Interact to draw the weapon as if you were wearing it; if you do so, the spell ends. If the weapon is still in the extradimensional space when the spell ends, it falls to the ground at your feet. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this spell.  

This reaction doesn’t prevent damage from a successful Strike.
