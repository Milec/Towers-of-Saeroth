# Enhance Senses two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 93

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You enhance the target's senses. The target gains [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|low-light vision]], and all of the target's [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] senses have their distances doubled. If the target already has low-light vision, they gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].

---
**Heightened (+2)**  The number of targets increases by 1.
