# Sea of Thought two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Thoth (Divine Mysteries)|Thoth]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 15-foot burst

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You cover the area in a sloshing torrent of semi-solidified thought, roughly ankle high. The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], similarly to a shallow bog. Each round that a creature starts its turn in the area, it must attempt a Fortitude save against the shifting waves of thought. On a failure, it takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it leaves the area, and on a critical failure, it is also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].
