# Lively Flight two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 151

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 willing living creature

---

Healing energy spirals toward your target, taking shape around them like majestic wings of vital energy. The target regains 6d8 Hit Points and can [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] up to 30 feet as a free action along a path of their choice; this movement doesn't trigger reactions but can never put the target in danger, so if they attempt to move toward an enemy or into a hazard, the movement immediately ends.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The healing increases by 2d8.
