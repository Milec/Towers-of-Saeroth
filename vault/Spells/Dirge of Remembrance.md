# Dirge of Remembrance two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 75

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You sing a funereal dirge to force restless spirits to fully manifest in the [[Setting/Planes/Material Plane]]. Each [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creature in the area takes 4d10 sonic damage (this damage bypasses its resistances) depending on the result of its Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes semi-corporeal until the spell ends. All attacks against it get the benefit of a [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]] rune.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but double damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
