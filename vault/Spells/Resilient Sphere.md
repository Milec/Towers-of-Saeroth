# Resilient Sphere two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Force]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 364

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Acavna]], [[Setting/Deities/Dramindyr]], [[Setting/Deities/Grundinnar (Divine Mysteries)|Grundinnar]], [[Setting/Deities/Korada (Divine Mysteries)|Korada]], [[Setting/Deities/Lymnieris (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lymnieris]], [[Setting/Deities/Ma’at]], [[Setting/Deities/Qi Zhong (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Qi Zhong]], [[Setting/Deities/The Last Breath]], [[Setting/Deities/Urban Prosperity]], [[Setting/Deities/Wards of the Pharaoh]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 Large or smaller creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create an immobile sphere of force to either trap or protect the target, blocking anything that would pass through the sphere. The sphere has AC 5, Hardness 10, and 40 Hit Points. It's immune to critical hits and precision damage. [[Spells/Disintegrate|_Disintegrate_]] destroys the sphere instantly. If the target is unwilling, the effects of the sphere depend on the target's Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target disrupts the sphere's integrity, causing it to collapse entirely.  

**Success** The sphere functions normally but has only 10 Hit Points instead of 40.  

**Failure** The sphere has its normal effect.
