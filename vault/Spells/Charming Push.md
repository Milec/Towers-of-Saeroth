# Charming Push single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 388

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** until the start of your next turn

---

You push at the target's mind to deflect their ire. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls and damage rolls against you.  

**Failure** The target can't use [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile actions]] against you.  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and can't use hostile actions against you.
