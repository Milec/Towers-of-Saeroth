# Veil of Broken Reality three actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Unique (Player Core)|Unique]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Impossible]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 177

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cost** a mile-long length of dream spider silk infused with the essence of the Maelstrom

**Range** 5 feet

**Area** 100-mile [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 24 hours

---

You conceal reality, letting time proceed, though none perceive it. Whenever a creature enters the area or starts its turn in the area, the creature must attempt a Will saving throw. A creature of 16th level or lower automatically critically fails. You can designate up to 10 creatures to be immune from this part of the spell. Regardless of the result, the creature becomes temporarily immune for the remainder of the duration. When this spell ends, all events that took place within the area are wiped from the memories of all creatures everywhere (including yourself and other excluded creatures), all records of the events are erased, and all attempts to receive any information about the events are automatically negated. Only an impossible spell specifically crafted for seeing a particular event can retrieve any knowledge of the events that took place.

---

**Lethologic Mark** You become unremarkable in presence and sometimes slip outside of reality altogether. Those you meet forget your name and identity 1 hour after they leave your presence. Those who already knew you must succeed at a DC 5 flat check each morning; on a failure, they forget you and your identity until the next morning. If they forget you 3 days in a row, the effect lasts until the lethologic mark is removed. To heal this mark, you—or those willing to assist you—must learn and speak five of your true names successively, knowledge that has been scattered to the far corners of the [[Setting/Planes/Dimension of Time (GM Core)|Dimension of Time]].

**Critical Success** The creature automatically learns you’re the spell’s caster, your current location, and the effect of the spell.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and knows you’re the spell’s caster.  

**Failure** The creature is slowed 1 and can’t use reactions.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is slowed 3 and can’t use reactions.

When you Fuel Veil of Broken Reality, you cast freeze time without expending a spell slot.
