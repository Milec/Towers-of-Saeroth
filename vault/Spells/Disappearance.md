# Disappearance two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 330

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Shadow]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Veiled Voice)]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You shroud a creature from others' senses. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]], not just to sight but to all senses, allowing the target to count as [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] no matter what precise and imprecise senses an observer might have. It's still possible for a creature to find the target by [[Actions/Seek|Seeking]], looking for disturbed dust, hearing gaps in the sound spectrum, or finding some other way to discover the presence of an otherwise-undetectable creature.
