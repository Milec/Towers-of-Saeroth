# Expunge Blood two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 137

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You attempt to forcibly remove a massive amount of blood or other vital fluids from a creature. The target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. A nonliving creature with blood in its body (such as a [[Bestiary/Families/Vampire (Monster Core)|vampire]]) can be affected, but a creature with no blood is immune. The [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] condition inflicted by this spell increases by 1 while the target is taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] and clumsy 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target becomes drained 2 and clumsy 1 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target becomes drained 3 and clumsy 2 for 1 minute.
