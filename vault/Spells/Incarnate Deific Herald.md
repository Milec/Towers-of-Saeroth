# Incarnate Deific Herald three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 145

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Requirements** You have a deity.

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

Your faith is strong enough to briefly summon a direct representative of your deity, such as the deity’s herald. The herald occupies the space of a Huge creature and has a Speed of 60 feet. The effects of this spell depend on the divine sanctification options of your deity. If your deity lists “must choose,” the herald is that sanctification (either holy or unholy). If your deity lists “none,” the herald is unsanctified. If your deity lists “can choose,” you can choose either unsanctified or whatever options are listed for your deity (holy or unholy) for the herald.

- **Holy** **Arrive** ([[Traits/Holy|holy]]) _Holy Smite_ The herald casts a 5th-rank _[[Spells/Divine Wrath (Player Core)|_divine wrath_]]_ that has a 100-foot emanation instead of the usual range and area; **Depart** _Merciful Healing_ The herald casts the 3-action version of a 5th-rank _[[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]]_ with a radius of 100 feet. This spell targets only you and your allies.
- **Unholy** _Infinite Misery_ The herald casts _[[Spells/Wave of Despair|_wave of despair_]]_ in a 100-foot cone; **Depart** ([[Traits/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]) _Midnight Wrath_ Each enemy in a 100-foot emanation takes 5d10 spirit damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. In addition, all non-magical light within the area is extinguished, and the herald attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] all magical [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|light]] (counteract modifier +25, counteract rank 8).
- **Unsanctified** **Arrive** _Quickening Ripple_ You and each ally within 100 feet of the herald are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] while the herald is present and can use the additional action to Step, Stride, or Strike; **Depart** ([[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]]) _Sapping Beam_ The herald drains the animating force of all enemies in a 100-foot line, dealing 6d10 spirit damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature who fails the save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2 for 1 minute (enfeebled 3 on a critical failure).
