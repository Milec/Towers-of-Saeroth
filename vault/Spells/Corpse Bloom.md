# Corpse Bloom three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 129

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** three 5-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|bursts]] that don’t overlap

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure three deadly clusters of flesh-devouring fungi. Whenever a creature starts its turn in one of the clusters or the first time a creature enters a cluster each round, it takes 3d4 poison damage with a Fortitude save as poisonous spores fill the air. Corpses within a cluster rapidly decompose.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and becomes sickened 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and becomes sickened 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
