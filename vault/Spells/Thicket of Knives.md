# Thicket of Knives two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 136

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create numerous phantom copies of your weapon arm, hiding your true movements and rendering your attacks unpredictable. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks. If you're untrained in Deception, you can use the [[Actions/Feint]] action anyway, and add your level as your proficiency bonus despite being untrained.
