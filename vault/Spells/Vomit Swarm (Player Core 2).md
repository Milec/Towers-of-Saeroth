# Vomit Swarm two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 254

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apollyon (Divine Mysteries)|Apollyon]], [[Setting/Deities/Baalzebul (Divine Mysteries)|Baalzebul]], [[Setting/Deities/Ghlaunder (Divine Mysteries)|Ghlaunder]], [[Setting/Deities/Kitumu (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Kitumu]], [[Setting/Deities/Selket (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Selket]], [[Setting/Deities/Xsistaid]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You belch forth a swarm of magical vermin. You evoke and shape the creatures from your own imagination, allowing you to change the appearance of the creatures (typically a mix of centipedes, roaches, wasps, and worms), but this doesn't change the effect of the spell. The vermin swarm over anyone in the area, their bites and stings dealing 2d8 piercing damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails its saving throw also becomes sickened 1. Once the spell ends, the swarm disappears.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
