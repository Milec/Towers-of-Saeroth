# Summon Dragon three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 375

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apsu (Draconic Codex)|Apsu]], [[Setting/Deities/Brixori]], [[Setting/Deities/Dahak (Draconic Codex)|Dahak]], [[Setting/Deities/Essence Dancers]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarshallatu]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You conjure a dragon to fight for you. This works like [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]], except you summon a common creature that has the [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] trait and whose level is 5 or lower, such as those below.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Level 7.

**Heightened (7th)** Level 9.

**Heightened (8th)** Level 11.

**Heightened (9th)** Level 13.

**Heightened (10th)** Level 15.
