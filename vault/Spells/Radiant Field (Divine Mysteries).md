# Radiant Field three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 259

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create an area of [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]]. Creatures with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Light Blindness (Monster Core)|light blindness]] that are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] by radiant field and remain in the area must attempt a Fortitude save at the start of their turns. On a failure, a creature remains blinded for 1 round; this is an [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] effect. This spell also suppresses magical [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]] of your radiant field spell’s rank or lower.

---
**Heightened (4th)** Creatures seen through the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to creatures outside the area. Creatures with light blindness can continue to be blinded by the field as long as the field is visible, even when outside of the field.
