# Invisible Item two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 221

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 object

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You make the object [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]]. This makes it [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] to all creatures, though the creatures can attempt to find the target, making it [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to them instead if they succeed. If the item is used as part of a hostile action, the spell ends after that hostile action is completed. Making a weapon invisible typically doesn't give any advantage to the attack, except that an invisible [[Traits/Thrown|thrown]] weapon or piece of ammunition can be used for an attack without necessarily giving information about the attacker's hiding place unless the weapon returns to the attacker.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The duration is until the next time you make your daily preparations.

**Heightened (7th)** The duration is unlimited.
