# Divine Plagues two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 115

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Plague]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 15 feet

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

Your deity sends wracking plagues. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. When you Cast the Spell, decide whether the disease inflicts the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition.  

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with divine plague at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with divine plague at stage 2.

**Divine Plague** (disease) **Level** 1; **Stage 1** clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] and clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 and the target can’t recover from the condition until they are cured (1 day)

---
**Heightened (5th)** The divine plague disease gains the [[Traits/Virulent|virulent]] trait. The condition value for the clumsy, enfeebled, or stupefied condition is 2 instead of 1.
