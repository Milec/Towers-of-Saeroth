# Lucky Number  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Contingency]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 152

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Duration** until the next time you make your daily preparations

---

When you Cast this Spell, roll 1d10 and make a note of the result; this is your lucky number for the duration. You gain the following reaction. Once you use the reaction, the spell ends, and you become temporarily immune to _lucky number_ until the next time you make your daily preparations.  

**That's My Number!** ⤾ (fortune) **Trigger** You roll your lucky number as your d20 result on a non-secret attack roll, saving throw, or skill check; **Effect** You call upon the fortune stored within your lucky number and reroll the triggering check. However, pushing your luck has a price: if your result on the reroll is the same number again, you critically fail, regardless of what degree of success you ordinarily would have received.
