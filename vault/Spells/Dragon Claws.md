# Dragon Claws single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 403

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Wyrmblessed]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Vicious claws grow from your fingers. They are [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] unarmed attacks that deal 1d4 slashing damage and 1d6 extra damage of a type determined by the dragon in your bloodline.

 Your scales from blood magic glow with faint energy, giving you resistance 5 to the same damage type.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The extra damage increases to 2d6, and the resistance increases to 10.

**Heightened (9th)** The extra damage increases to 3d6, and the resistance increases to 15.
