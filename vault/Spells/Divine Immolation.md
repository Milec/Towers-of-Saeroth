# Divine Immolation two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 325

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
Reflex

---

Divine flames scour creatures within the area. Creatures take 6d6 fire damage and 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]]. The divine power within the flames scorches the spirit as well; a creature takes spirit damage instead of fire damage from divine immolation if that would be more detrimental to the creature (as determined by the GM).

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and no persistent damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and persistent damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and double persistent damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 and persistent damage increases by 1d6.
