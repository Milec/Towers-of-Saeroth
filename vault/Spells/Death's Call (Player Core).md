# Death's Call ⤾  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 373

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Death (Player Core)|Death]]

**Trigger** A living creature within 20 feet of you dies, or an undead creature within 20 feet of you is destroyed

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Seeing another pass from this world invigorates you. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the triggering creature's level plus your spellcasting attribute modifier. If the triggering creature was [[Traits/Undead|undead]], double the number of temporary Hit Points you gain. These last for the duration of the spell, and the spell ends if all the temporary Hit Points are depleted earlier.
