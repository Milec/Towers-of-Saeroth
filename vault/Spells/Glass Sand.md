# Glass Sand two actions  *Spell 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from The Enmity Cycle

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/The Enmity Cycle]] pg. 61

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Area** 60-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

You throw a handful of sand that transforms into jagged shards of glass. Creatures in the area take 4d6 slashing damage and must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and 2 persistent bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
