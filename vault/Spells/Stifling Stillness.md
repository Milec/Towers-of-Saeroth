# Stifling Stillness two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 71

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You make all air in the target area unnaturally still and unyielding, creating a shimmering haze. The stagnant, heavy air becomes an area of [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Creatures in the area that breathe air and aren't holding their breath must spend a single action on their turn straining to breathe the stagnant air; once they do, they still mostly breathe their own exhaled air, taking 3d6 poison damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) and becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d6.
