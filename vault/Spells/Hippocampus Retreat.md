# Hippocampus Retreat two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 86

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Requirements** You’re mostly or totally submerged in water

**Range** 10 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You temporarily shape your lower limbs into the tail of a hippocampus in order to swim away from a nearby foe after dealing a parting blow. Attempt a melee spell attack roll against the target’s AC, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage on a hit (or double damage on a critical hit). Then, [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] up to 30 feet; if you already have a [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]], you can Swim up to your Speed with a +10-foot circumstance bonus. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your AC against reactions triggered by this movement. At the end of the movement, your lower limbs return to normal.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
