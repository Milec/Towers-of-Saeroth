# Unspeakable Shadow two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Shadow]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 138

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You alter a creature's shadow, transforming it into a terrifying monster out to devour the creature. The creature must attempt a Will save. A creature that has the [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition from _unspeakable shadow_ must spend at least one of its actions each turn to either attack its shadow (making a Strike ineffectually) or flee from its shadow (using one action to move away as though it had the [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] condition).

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is frightened 2.  

**Failure** The target is frightened 3. It can't reduce its frightened value below 1 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is so afraid, it might instantly die. It must succeed at a Fortitude save or die; this saving throw has the [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]] trait. If it succeeds at its save, the target is frightened 4 and can't reduce its frightened value below 1 for 1 minute.
