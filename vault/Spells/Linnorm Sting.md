# Linnorm Sting two actions  *Spell 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 81

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You replicate the fiery venom of the [[Bestiary/Tor Linnorm|tor linnorm]] and inflict an enemy with the dread dragon's debilitating bite. You deal 2d12 piercing damage to the targeted creature and afflict it with tor linnorm venom. If the target takes piercing damage, they must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 8d6 fire damage.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with tor linnorm venom at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with tor linnorm venom at stage 2.

 **Tor Linnorm Venom** (fire, [[Traits/Injury|injury]], poison) **Level** 17; **Maximum Duration** 10 rounds; **Stage 1** 8d6 fire damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 12d6 fire damage and drained 2 (1 round)
