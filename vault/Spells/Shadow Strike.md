# Shadow Strike three actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 162

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You launch out a tendril of darkness that latches onto a target and pulls them toward you. Make a ranged spell attack against the target’s AC. On a hit, you deal 5d10 void damage, and the target is pulled up to 10 feet toward you. On a critical hit, you deal double the damage, and the target is pulled up to 20 feet toward you.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
