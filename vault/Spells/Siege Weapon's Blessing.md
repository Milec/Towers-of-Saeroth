# Siege Weapon's Blessing three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 89

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** one mounted siege weapon

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You trace a magic rune onto the body of a siege weapon, calling upon fate or your faith to guide the attacks your allies make with this machine. For the spell’s duration, any skill checks attempted to Load the siege weapon can be rolled twice, taking the better result; these skill checks gain the [[Traits/Fortune (Player Core)|fortune]] trait. In addition, any saving throws attempted by creatures caught in the siege weapon’s Launch area must be rolled twice, taking the worse result; these saving throws gain the [[Traits/Misfortune (Player Core)|misfortune]] trait.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The duration is 8 hours.
