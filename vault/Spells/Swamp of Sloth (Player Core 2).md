# Swamp of Sloth single action to three actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Olfactory (Player Core)|Olfactory]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 263

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 5-foot burst or more

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Ground in the area turns swampy and fetid. The area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. The sludge at the bottom of the morass animates into diminutive sludge beasts that have a demonic appearance. These don't function as normal creatures, but they swarm over creatures in the swamp and exude a noxious stench. The swamp deals 1d6 poison damage to creatures that end their turn in the area ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). For each additional action you use when Casting the Spell, increase the burst's radius by 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6, and the initial radius increases by 5 feet.
