# Moonbeam two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 377

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Moon (Player Core)|Moon]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You shine a ray of moonlight. Make a spell attack roll. The beam of light deals 2d6 fire damage. This is [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]] damage for the purposes of weaknesses, resistances, and the like.

**Critical Success** The beam deals double damage, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 minute.  

**Success** The beam deals full damage, and the target is dazzled for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The ray's damage increases by 1d6.
