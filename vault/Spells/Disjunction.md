[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas (Divine Mysteries)|Abraxas]], [[Setting/Deities/Groetus (Divine Mysteries)|Groetus]], [[Setting/Deities/Liisglan]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Wards of the Pharaoh]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 magic item

---

Crackling energy disjoins the target. You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it. If you succeed, it's deactivated for 1 week. On a critical success, it's destroyed. If it's an [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]] or similar item, you automatically fail.
