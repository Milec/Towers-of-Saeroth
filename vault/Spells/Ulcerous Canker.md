# Ulcerous Canker two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Poison]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 287

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Blight]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Weeping sores open on the target as you expose it to decay, dealing 1d4 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage and 1d4 [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage plus 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the initial damage and no persistent bleed damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double initial damage and double persistent bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The void damage and poison damage each increase by 1d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 2.
