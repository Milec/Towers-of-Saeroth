# Grisly Growths two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 246

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Sigek]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 corporeal creature

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

This gruesome spell causes the target to grow excess limbs and organs, whether it be fingers multiplying until hands resemble bushes, eyes popping open in bizarre places, legs sprouting from the side of the body, or some other result. The target takes 10d6 piercing damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save) as the new features erupt. This spell has no effect on a target with a mutable anatomy or no limbs, such as an [[Traits/Ooze (Player Core)|ooze]] or a [[Traits/Protean (GM Core)|protean]]. The growths rot rapidly and fall away after 1 round.

 In addition, unless the initial target critically succeeds, creatures within 30 feet of the target, including the target, must attempt Will saves, after which they're temporarily immune to this secondary effect of _grisly growths_ for 1 hour. This additional effect is a [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] and [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]] effect.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
