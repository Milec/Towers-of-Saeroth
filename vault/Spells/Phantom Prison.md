# Phantom Prison three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 121

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Haborym]], [[Setting/Deities/Shivaska]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 50 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You completely surround a Large or smaller creature in immobile illusory walls, trapping it inside a false prison it can't escape. No other creatures see or feel these walls, and the target can't see anything outside of the illusory walls. The target can attempt a Will save when the spell is cast, each time it attempts to interact with or escape the walls, and each time something from outside the walls affects the target. On a success, it [[Rules/Illusion|disbelieves]] the illusion and the spell ends.

---
**Heightened (8th)** You can target up to 5 creatures.
