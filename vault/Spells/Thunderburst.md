# Thunderburst two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 234

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Tempest]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You create a powerful blast of air and a loud peal of thunder, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 sonic damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is deafened for 1 hour.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Increase each type of damage by 2d6 and the area by 5 feet.
