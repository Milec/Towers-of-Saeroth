# Summon Lesser Servitor three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 134

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The summon lesser servitor spell cannot be used to summon imps._

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

While deities jealously guard their most powerful servants from the summoning spells of those who aren't steeped in the faith, this spell allows you to conjure an inhabitant of the [[Rules/Outer Sphere]] with or without the deity's permission. You summon a common [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]], or [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] whose level is –1 to fight on your behalf. Alternatively, you can choose a magical animal from the Outer Sphere as your lesser servitor. This animal is your choice of an [[Bestiary/Eagle|eagle]], [[Bestiary/Guard Dog|guard dog]], or [[Bestiary/Raven|raven]]. It gains the alignment matching your deity (or you, if you don't have a deity) and has the following trait depending on the alignment it gained: celestial for good, monitor for neutral, or fiend for evil.

 You can't summon a creature if it is opposed to your deity's alignment on either axis (or opposed to your alignment if you don't follow a deity). For example, if you deity is lawful good, you can't summon a chaotic or evil creature. The GM might determine that deities restrict specific types of creatures even if their alignments aren't diametrically opposed. For example, [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma]] would restrict the summoning of [[Bestiary/Families/Sahkil|sahkils]].

 Heightening the spell increases the maximum level of creature you can summon and might add additional animal servitors, which otherwise work like the eagle, guard dog, and raven.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** Level 1.

**Heightened (3rd)** Level 2. You can summon a [[Bestiary/Black Bear|black bear]], [[Bestiary/Giant Bat|giant bat]], or [[Bestiary/Leopard|leopard]].

**Heightened (4th)** Level 3. You can summon a [[Bestiary/Great White Shark|great white shark]] or [[Bestiary/Tiger|tiger]].
