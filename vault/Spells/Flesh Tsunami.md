# Flesh Tsunami two actions  *Focus 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 182

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You melt one of your thralls into a massive wave of sticky flesh. Destroy the target thrall to create an area of greater [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] in a 60-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]] originating from the thrall’s former space. You can destroy a second thrall in the cone to make its limbs grasp your foes. If you do, each enemy in the area must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or be [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]]; the Escape DC is your spell DC.
