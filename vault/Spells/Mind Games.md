# Mind Games two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Cost** 3 gp game piece focus

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You lock minds with the target, trying to mentally subdue them. The target must attempt a Will save. Each time you Sustain this Spell, the target attempts another Will save.

**Critical Success** You are [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]] and the spell ends.  

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is stunned 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is stunned 2.
