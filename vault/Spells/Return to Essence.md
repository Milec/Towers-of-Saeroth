# Return to Essence two actions  *Spell 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -173- Doorway to the Red Star|Pathfinder #173: Doorway to the Red Star]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** one magically sealed or warded doorway or chest

---

You unravel the magic of a spell that affects a container, door, or lock, converting it into energy you can repurpose to power your own spells. Attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against the target spell or magical effect that wards or seals a doorway or chest, such as [[Spells/Lock|_lock_]], or against a spell or effect that deals damage or delivers secondary effects when it's opened, such as [[Spells/Glyph of Warding|_glyph of warding_]]. If you succeed, you recover its energy, restoring one of your expended spell slots of 3rd level or lower.

---
**Heightened (8th)** You can restore an expended spell slot of 4th level or lower.

**Heightened (9th)** You can restore an expended spell slot of 5th level or lower.

**Heightened (10th)** You can restore an expended spell slot of 6th level or lower.
