# Telekinetic Bombardment two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 107

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 10-foot burst or 30-foot line

**Defense**
Reflex

---

You grasp a piece of the landscape—such as a tree, carriage, or piece of masonry—and lob it in your foe's direction. Your missile crashes down in either a 10-foot burst (for most missiles) or a 30-foot line (for something long and tall, like a tree or a ship's mast), dealing 14d6 bludgeoning damage and turning its area into [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] as it breaks into rubble. All creatures in the area must attempt a Reflex save. If the area is a line, it doesn't have to start from your square but can instead start anywhere in range, as long as the entire area remains within range.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is knocked prone, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
