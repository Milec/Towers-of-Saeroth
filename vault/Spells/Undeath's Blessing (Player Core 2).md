# Undeath's Blessing single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 265

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead (Player Core 2)|Undead]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You instill within a creature the touch of the grave. For the duration, [[Spells/Harm (Player Core)|_harm_]] and [[Spells/Heal (Player Core)|_heal_]] spells treat the creature as [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]. In addition, _harm_ spells gain a +2 status bonus to the Hit Points restored to the target. An unwilling target can attempt a Will save to reduce the effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target heals half as much from _heal_ and takes half as much damage from _harm_ for 1 round.  

**Failure** Effects as described above.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The status bonus to the Hit Points restored increases by 2.
