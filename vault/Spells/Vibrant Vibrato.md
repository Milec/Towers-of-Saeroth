# Vibrant Vibrato two actions  *Spell 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Curtain Call

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -205- Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man|Pathfinder #205: Singer, Stalker, Skinsaw Man]] pg. 83

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] up to 40 feet

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained (up to 1 minute)

---

Your voice trills in perfect vibrato. When casting the spell, you can make the area any radius you choose, up to 40 feet. The reverberations of your voice continue to shimmer and hang in the air as long as you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell but can’t be heard at all outside of the area. A creature must attempt a Will save if it’s within the area when you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]] or as soon as it enters the area while the spell is in effect. Once a creature has attempted the save, it uses the same result for that casting of _vibrant vibrato_.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 5d10 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent sonic damage]] at the end of their turn as long as they remain within the aura’s area of effect.  

**Failure** As success, but if the creature leaves the area, or if you move far enough from the creature that they’re no longer in the area, the creature hears a shattering sound and takes 5d10 [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1. The creature is then [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for the rest of the spell’s duration.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double the sonic damage and is stunned 3.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage on a failed save increases by 1d10.
