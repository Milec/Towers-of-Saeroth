# Summon Dragon three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Summon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 360

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apsu (Draconic Codex)|Apsu]], [[Setting/Deities/Brixori]], [[Setting/Deities/Dahak (Draconic Codex)|Dahak]], [[Setting/Deities/Essence Dancers]], [[Setting/Deities/Sarshallatu]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You summon a creature that has the [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]] trait and whose level is 5 or lower to fight for you. If the dragon has a magical tradition trait ([[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Traits/Primal|primal]]), you can summon it only if you're using that tradition to cast summon dragon.
