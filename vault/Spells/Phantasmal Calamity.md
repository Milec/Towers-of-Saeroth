# Phantasmal Calamity two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 357

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Imot (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Imot]], [[Setting/Deities/Lady Nanbyo (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lady Nanbyo]], [[Setting/Deities/Rhan-Tegoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Scal]], [[Setting/Deities/Wulgren]], [[Setting/Deities/Zyphus (Divine Mysteries)|Zyphus]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

A vision of apocalyptic destruction fills the mind of each creature in the area. The vision deals 11d6 mental damage (basic Will save). On a critical failure, the creature must also succeed at a Reflex save or believe it's trapped (stuck in a fissure, adrift at sea, or some other fate in keeping with its vision). If it fails the second save, it's also stunned for 1 minute. It can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, it disbelieves the illusion and recovers from the stunned condition.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
