# Slow two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Embaral]], [[Setting/Deities/Korada (Divine Mysteries)|Korada]], [[Setting/Deities/Lorthact (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lorthact]], [[Setting/Deities/Matravash (Divine Mysteries)|Matravash]], [[Setting/Deities/Sithhud]], [[Setting/Deities/Thalaphyrr]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You dilate the flow of time around the target, slowing its actions.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is slowed 1 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
