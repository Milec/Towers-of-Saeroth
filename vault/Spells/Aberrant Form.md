# Aberrant Form two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 88

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Haggakal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Harnessing the power of vile entities, you transform yourself into a Large aberration. You must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. When you cast this spell, choose chuul, gogiteth, gug, or otyugh. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]] trait. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 18 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 13 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more unarmed melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +18, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +20, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of aberration you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Chuul]]** Speed 30 feet, swim 25 feet; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8+8 bludgeoning, and you can spend an action after a hit to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] the target.
- **[[Bestiary/Gogiteth]]** Speed 40 feet, climb 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d10+8 piercing, and you can spend an action after a hit to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] the target; **Melee** single action leg ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6+8 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Gug]]** Speed 40 feet, climb 20 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d12+8 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8+8 slashing; your multiple joints allow you to fit through tight spaces as if you were a Medium creature.
- **[[Bestiary/Otyugh]]** +2 circumstance bonus on saves against [[Traits/Disease|disease]]; Speed 20 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 3d6+8 piercing; **Melee** single action tentacle ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6+8 bludgeoning.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Your battle form is Huge, and the reach of your attacks increases by 5 feet. You must have space to expand, or the spell is lost. You instead gain AC = 21 + your level, 26 temporary HP, attack modifier +22, damage bonus +13, and Athletics +23.
