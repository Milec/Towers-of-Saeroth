# Vibrant Thorns single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 377

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Nature (Player Core)|Nature]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your body sprouts brambly thorns that thrive on life magic. Adjacent creatures that hit you with a melee attack, as well as creatures that hit you with [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks, take 1 piercing damage each time they do. Anytime you cast a [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] spell, the thorns' damage increases to 1d6 until the start of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1, or 1d6 after you cast a vitality spell.
