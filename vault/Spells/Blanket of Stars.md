# Blanket of Stars two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 215

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/The Lady of the North Star]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

A flowing cloak of utter darkness drapes over you, filled with pinpricks of light like distant stars. It imparts the stillness of the cosmos to you, granting you a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak]]. While outside under a starry night sky, you're also [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] as long as you remain still. When moving under a starry night sky, you are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] instead.

 Gazing too closely into the stars is disorienting. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save; this is a [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Visual|visual]] effect.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] and dazzled until the end of its next turn.
