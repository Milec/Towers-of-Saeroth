**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You dull the target's mind; the target must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 4.
