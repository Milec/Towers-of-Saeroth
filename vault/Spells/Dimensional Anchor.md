You interfere with the target's ability to teleport and travel between dimensions. _Dimensional anchor_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effect, or any effect that would move the target to a different plane. The duration is determined by the target's Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The effect's duration is 1 minute.  

**Failure** The effect's duration is 10 minutes.  

**Critical Failure** The effect's duration is 1 hour.
