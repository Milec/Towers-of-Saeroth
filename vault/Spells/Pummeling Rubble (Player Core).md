# Pummeling Rubble two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 351

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Rivethun]], [[Setting/Deities/The Devourer]], [[Setting/Deities/Treasures of the Eternal Delve]], [[Setting/Deities/Xoveron]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Defense**
Reflex

---

A spray of heavy rocks flies through the air in front of you. The rubble deals 2d4 bludgeoning damage to each creature in the area. Each creature must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet away from you.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d4.
