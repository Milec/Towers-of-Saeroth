# Split the Tongue ⤾  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -161- Belly of the Black Whale|Pathfinder #161: Belly of the Black Whale]] pg. 75

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Naga]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Trigger** A creature within 30 feet fails a [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] or [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] check.

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

The deceiving creature's tongue splits like a serpent's tongue. The creature takes 1d8 slashing damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] for 1d4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage and is stupefied 2 for 1d4 rounds.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
