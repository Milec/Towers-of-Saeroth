# Buffeting Winds two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 92

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

You release a quick burst of wind that batters your living opponents without causing them lasting harm, while also blowing [[Traits/Undead|undead]] away. The wind deals 2d4 bludgeoning damage, which is [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] against living creatures. Against undead, the winds are more vicious, and the spell loses the nonlethal trait against such creatures. Each creature in the area must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. On a failure, undead creatures are also [[Rules/Forced Movement|knocked back]] 5 feet (or 10 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Heightened (+1)**  The damage increases by 2d4.
