# Summoner's Visage two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 165

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** your eidolon

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

No one understands you more than your eidolon and vice versa. Using this innate understanding, you transform your eidolon into your identical twin, allowing them to hide their presence or impersonate you to a limited extent. Assuming you’re a humanoid, the eidolon gains the humanoid trait in addition to their other traits while they’re in this form as well as any other trait related to your form (such as elf or gnome). Your eidolon gains a +4 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Impersonate (Player Core)|Impersonate]] you and can add their level to such checks even if they’re untrained.  

Although your eidolon looks like you, they don’t gain any new abilities, and their statistics don’t change in any way— the transformation is purely cosmetic. If this transformation reduces your eidolon’s size, the eidolon loses any other effects of their larger size, such as any increase to reach. Your eidolon can still use gear only if it has the eidolon trait, which allows eidolons to use it. If necessary, any such eidolon items the eidolon was wearing remain active but change size and shape to reflect what you’re wearing. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this spell to return your eidolon to their original form.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The duration increases to 1 hour.
