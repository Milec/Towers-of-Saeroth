# Unusual Anatomy single action  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 262

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant (Player Core 2)|Aberrant]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You transform your flesh and organs into a bizarre amalgam of glistening skin, rough scales, tufts of hair, and tumorous protuberances. This has the following effects.

- You gain resistance 10 to precision damage and resistance 10 to extra damage from critical hits. If the resistance is greater than the extra damage, it reduces the extra damage to 0 but doesn't reduce the attack's normal damage.
- You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
- Acid oozes from your skin. Any creature that hits you with an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack or with a non-[[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] melee weapon takes 2d6 acid damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistances increase by 5, and the acid damage increases by 1d6.
