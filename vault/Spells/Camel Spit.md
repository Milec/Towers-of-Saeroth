# Camel Spit two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 84

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You alter your stomach, esophagus, and tongue to be able to spit partially digested food with force. You can spit at a foe once you finish Casting the Spell and can repeat the attack once on each of your subsequent turns by taking a single action, which has the [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], and [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] traits. After your third spit attack, the spell ends.

When you attack with camel spit, make a ranged spell attack roll against a creature within 15 feet, dealing 1d6 acid damage and causing the target to be [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round if you hit. On a critical hit, you deal double damage and the target takes 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent acid damage]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6, and the persistent damage on a critical hit is increased by 1.
