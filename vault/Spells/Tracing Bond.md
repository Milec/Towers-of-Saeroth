# Tracing Bond  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Detection (Player Core)|Detection]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 168

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Range** touch

**Target** you and 1 willing creature

**Duration** 8 hours

---

You and an ally might be parting ways, but you know that you’ll continue to have each other’s backs. For the duration of the spell, you and the ally always know the direction and distance to the other’s location as long as you’re both on the same plane of existence. While this bond doesn’t allow you to send messages or generally know the other person’s status, at any point during the spell, either of you can send a single distress signal to the other as a single action that has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] and [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] traits. This signal is a simple mental alert that doesn’t provide any additional details about the situation or status of the person. Once this signal has been used, neither target can send another signal for the duration of the spell.
