**Defense**

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

Your patron's resentment manifests in a baleful, envious gaze. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] if it fails a Will save (or sickened 2 on a critical failure). This condition value can't be reduced below 1 while the spell is active and you can see the target.
