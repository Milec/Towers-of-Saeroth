# Necrotic Bomb two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 183

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You overload one of your thralls with void energy, causing it to explode. Each creature within a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of the thrall takes 1d12 void damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. This destroys the thrall.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d12.
