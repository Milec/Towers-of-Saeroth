# Spinning Staff single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Magus (Impossible Magic)|Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 180

**Requirements** You’re wielding a staff.

---

You spin your staff with an impossibly quick twirling flourish, attacking two foes and transforming the momentum into magical energy to charge for your next [[Actions/Spellstrike (Impossible Magic)|Spellstrike]]. Make a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with your staff against one foe and then a second melee Strike with your staff against a different foe. Your multiple attack penalty applies to both of these attacks as normal.
