# Wave of Despair two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 369

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Diabolic (Player Core 2)|Diabolic]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ahriman (Monster Core 2)|Ahriman]], [[Setting/Deities/Chamiaholom]], [[Setting/Deities/Charon (Divine Mysteries)|Charon]], [[Setting/Deities/Gaasham]], [[Setting/Deities/Groetus (Divine Mysteries)|Groetus]], [[Setting/Deities/Haborym]], [[Setting/Deities/Mrtyu]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Faceless Sphinx)]], [[Setting/Deities/Ozranvial]], [[Setting/Deities/Sifkesh (Divine Mysteries)|Sifkesh]], [[Setting/Deities/The Lost Prince (Divine Mysteries)|The Lost Prince]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 or more rounds

---

You inflict despair on creatures in the area. The effects for each creature are determined by its Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** For 1 round, the creature can't use reactions and must attempt another save at the start of its turn; on a failure, it is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for that turn as it sobs uncontrollably.  

**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is automatically slowed 1 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (7th)** The area increases to a 60-foot cone.
