# Shroud of Flame two actions  *Focus 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -168- King of the Mountain|Pathfinder #168: King of the Mountain]] pg. 74

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Phoenix]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You encircle yourself in an aura of protective flames. Each creature that hits you with an unarmed attack, [[Actions/Grapple|Grapples]] you, or otherwise touches you takes 3 fire damage. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you sustain it each round, each creature in the area takes 3d6 fire damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage caused by touching you increases by 1, and the fire damage when you Cast the Spell or sustain it increases by 1d6.
