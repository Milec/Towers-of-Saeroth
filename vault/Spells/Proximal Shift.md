# Proximal Shift two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 158

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

---

With a thunderous crack, you disappear from your current location and reappear in an unoccupied space within range that you can see, bringing all non-magical, non-living, unattended matter (including objects) in a 5-foot emanation with you. Creatures within the emanation must succeed at a Reflex save to avoid falling into the crater you leave behind, taking 5 bludgeoning damage and falling [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. Creatures in a 5-foot emanation on your arrival space take 6d6 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save from the resulting explosion of debris, and the area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for 1 minute. At the GM's discretion, the spell might have additional effects based on the nature of the material teleported with you. If the teleport would cause you to bring another creature—even one inside an [[Traits/Extradimensional (Player Core)|extradimensional]] container—the spell fails.
