# Threefold Limb two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 83

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You temporarily transform one of your limbs into water, taking the form of ice, liquid water, or steam as you desire. Make a melee spell attack roll. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 damage; the type of damage dealt and any additional effect depends on the form you choose. On a critical hit, double the damage.

- **Ice** The limb deals cold damage, and the target takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds until the start of your next turn. This spell gains the [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] trait.
- **Liquid Water** The limb deals bludgeoning damage and you can [[Actions/Reposition]] the target up to 10 feet.
- **Steam** The limb deals fire damage and steam clings to the target, making all creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to them until the start of your next turn or they perform an Interact action to wave the steam away. This spell gains the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
