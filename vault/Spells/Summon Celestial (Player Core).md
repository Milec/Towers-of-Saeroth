# Summon Celestial three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Summon]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 360

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You summon a creature that has the [[Traits/Celestial (Player Core)|celestial]] trait and whose level is 5 or lower to fight for you. The GM might determine your deity restricts the specific types of celestials you can summon in certain cases. For instance, [[Setting/Deities/Calistria]] doesn't typically allow her followers to summon [[Bestiary/Families/Aeon|aeons]].
