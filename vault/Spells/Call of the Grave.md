# Call of the Grave two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Arcane]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 406

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

---

You fire a ray of sickening energy. Make a spell attack roll.

**Critical Success** The target becomes sickened 2 and slowed 1 as long as it's sickened.  

**Success** The target becomes sickened 1.  

**Failure** The target is unaffected.
