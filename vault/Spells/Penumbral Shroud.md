# Penumbral Shroud two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 109

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Aleth]], [[Setting/Deities/Black Butterfly (Divine Mysteries)|Black Butterfly]], [[Setting/Deities/Cosmic Caravan (Divine Mysteries)|Cosmic Caravan]], [[Setting/Deities/Count Ranalc (Divine Mysteries)|Count Ranalc]], [[Setting/Deities/Kerkamoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Nocticula (Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star)|Nocticula]], [[Setting/Deities/Set (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Set]], [[Setting/Deities/Shawnari]], [[Setting/Deities/Thamir (Divine Mysteries)|Thamir]], [[Setting/Deities/The Lady of the North Star]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You envelop the target in a shroud of shadow. The target perceives light as one step lower than it actually is ([[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] becomes [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]], for example), affecting their ability to sense creatures and objects accordingly. The shroud also provides the target a +1 status bonus to saving throws against light effects. While this effect is helpful to creatures sensitive to light, if the creature is unwilling, it can attempt a Reflex save to avoid the shroud and reduce the effect.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The effect lasts for 1 round  

**Failure** The effect lasts its normal duration.
