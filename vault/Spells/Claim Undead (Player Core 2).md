# Claim Undead two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 258

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Bones (Player Core 2)|Bones]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 undead creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You attempt to wrest control of a target [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] or force it to recognize you as its master. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] by another creature, that controller attempts a Will saving throw to retain control; otherwise, the target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 round as it fights off your commands.  

**Failure** The target becomes controlled by you and follows your orders. It (or the creature previously controlling it) can attempt a new Will save at the end of each of its turns; on a success, the spell ends, and the creature becomes stunned 1 and confused for 1 round. If you issue an obviously self-destructive order, the target doesn't act until you issue a new order.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target (or the creature previously controlling it) receives a new save only if you give it a new order that is against its nature.
