# Hot Air single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 186

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

Your enemies are full of hot air, so you make their lungs burn when they try to talk. The target takes 4d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failed save, the target also burns whenever they speak, causing them to take 4 fire damage whenever they use an action with the [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]] trait or [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast a Spell]], unless their spell is [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial fire damage increases by 1d6, and the fire damage the target takes whenever they take an action with the auditory trait or Cast a Spell increases by 1.
