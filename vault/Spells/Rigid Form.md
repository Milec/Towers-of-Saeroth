# Rigid Form single action  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 197

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You utilize wood's rigidity to prevent harmful shapechanging effects. For the duration of this spell, whenever you're targeted by a harmful [[Traits/Polymorph|polymorph]] effect, you can use your reaction to attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the effect.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 10 minutes.
