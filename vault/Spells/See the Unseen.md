# See the Unseen two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 355

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead (Player Core 2)|Undead]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Your gaze pierces through illusions and finds [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]] creatures and spirits. You can see invisible creatures as though they weren't invisible, although their features are blurred, making them [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] and difficult to identify. You can also see [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]] creatures, like [[Bestiary/Families/Ghost|ghosts]], phased through an object from within 10 feet of an object's surface as blurry shapes seen through those objects. Subtler clues also grant you a +2 status bonus to checks you make to disbelieve [[Traits/Illusion|illusions]].

---
**Heightened (5th)** This spell has a duration of 8 hours.
