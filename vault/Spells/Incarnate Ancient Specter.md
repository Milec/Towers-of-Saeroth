# Incarnate Ancient Specter three actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 144

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You invoke an ancient wraith to appear and fight by your side. This specter may be the spirit of some long-dead king or warlord. It occupies the space of a Medium creature and has a fly Speed of 40 feet.  

**Arrive** ([[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|visual]]) _Kneel, Peons!_ The specter raises its hand, indicating that all foes should pay it obeisance. All enemies within a 60-foot emanation of the specter must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature kneels, dropping [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] as a free action.  

**Failure** As success, and the creature can’t [[Actions/Stand (Player Core)|Stand]] for the duration of the spell.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature also bows its head, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for the duration of the spell.  

**Depart** ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], mental) _Listen and Weep_ The specter speaks, and though its words have no meaning, they convey a terrible tragedy. All living enemies that have both eyes and blood (or a reasonable equivalent) within a 30-foot emanation begin to weep tears of blood. They take 4d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a failure, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for as long as they take the persistent bleed damage.
