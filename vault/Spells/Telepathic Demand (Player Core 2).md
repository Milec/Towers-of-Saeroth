# Telepathic Demand three actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|Linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 253

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** planetary

**Target** 1 creature you’ve telepathically contacted before

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You send the target a message of 25 words or fewer, and it can respond immediately with its own message of 25 words or fewer. Your message is insidious and has the effect of a [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]] spell, with the message substituting for the spoken suggestion. On a successful save, the target is temporarily immune for 1 day, and on a critical success, the target is temporarily immune for 1 month. You can target a creature only if you've previously been in telepathic contact with it before, such as via the [[Spells/Telepathy (Player Core)|_telepathy_]] spell.
