# Peaceful Bubble  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 348

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Bastet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Bastet]], [[Setting/Deities/Count Ranalc (Divine Mysteries)|Count Ranalc]], [[Setting/Deities/Lalaci]], [[Setting/Deities/Oathos]], [[Setting/Deities/Tsukiyo (Divine Mysteries)|Tsukiyo]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Range** touch

**Area** 100-foot burst

**Duration** 24 hours

---

An opaque shell of drifting, iridescent runes covers the area, creating a bubble. You can choose to make the burst smaller, in 10-foot increments. The runes don't block travel, but those inside the bubble can't perceive those outside it and vice versa. [[Traits/Detection]] and [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] effects are likewise blocked.

 Creatures sleeping within the bubble are immune to dreams sent by spells. Those sleeping 8 hours reduce their [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] condition by 2 instead of 1.
