# Incendiary Aura two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 260

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames (Player Core 2)|Flames]]

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself with a combustible aura. Each time a creature within the emanation takes fire damage, it catches on fire, taking 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent damage increases by 1d4 and the radius of the emanation increases by 5 feet.
