# Prismatic Armor two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 122

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You wrap the target in armor made of multicolored light. It functions as [[Spells/Chromatic Armor|_chromatic armor_]] that is all colors (granting resistance 5 to [[Traits/Acid|acid]], [[Traits/Electricity|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Force|force]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], and [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]). An attacker that critically fails on its saving throw against the spell is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]].

---
**Heightened (9th)** The resistances increase to 10 each.
