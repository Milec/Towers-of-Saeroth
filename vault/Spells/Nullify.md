# Nullify ⤾  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 118

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Trigger** a foe within range casts a 9th-level or lower spell

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** the triggering spell

---

You instantly destroy the incoming spell, though at the cost of sending magical feedback through your body. You automatically [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the spell, but the feedback brings you unavoidable harm. You lose 1d8 Hit Points per level of the triggering spell.
