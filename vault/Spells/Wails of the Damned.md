# Wails of the Damned two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 367

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead (Player Core 2)|Undead]]

**Area** 40-foot emanation

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You howl a lament of damned souls. Each living enemy in the area takes 8d10 void damage depending on its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes full damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1d4]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is drained 4.
