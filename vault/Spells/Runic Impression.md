# Runic Impression single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 143

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** you or one weapon you're wielding

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your unarmed attacks or weapon gain the benefits of a weapon rune you choose when you cast this spell: [[Equipment/Corrosive|_corrosive_]], [[Equipment/Flaming|_flaming_]], [[Equipment/Frost|_frost_]], [[Equipment/Ghost Touch|_ghost touch_]], [[Equipment/Returning|_returning_]], [[Equipment/Shock|_shock_]], or [[Equipment/Thundering|_thundering_]]. If you cast _runic impression_ on a weapon, this spell ends if you cease holding the weapon except if you throw a returning weapon.

 If this spell would give a weapon more property runes than its normal maximum, one of the existing property runes (you choose) is suppressed until the spell ends. For [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks, if this spell would give you more property runes than you could have from [[Equipment/Handwraps of Mighty Blows|_handwraps of mighty blows_]], one of the existing property runes is similarly suppressed.

---
**Heightened (8th)** Add [[Equipment/Keen|_keen_]] to the list of runes you can choose as well as the greater types of _corrosive_, _flaming_, _frost_, _shock_, and _thundering_.
