# Bone Spray two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 126

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You fire a torrent of jagged bone shards from your hands that rips through the fleshy bodies of your enemies. The spray deals 2d10 piercing damage and 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to all creatures in the area, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 1d10, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
