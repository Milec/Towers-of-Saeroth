# Boomerang Shot two actions  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -203 Shepherd of Decay|Pathfinder #203 Shepherd of Decay]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Target** 1 creature

---

You launch a curved length of wood at a foe that arcs around objects and obstacles to strike from an unexpected direction. Make a [[Rules/Spell Attack Rolls (Player Core)|spell attack roll]] against the target’s AC. This attack ignores the target’s [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] condition and ignores all [[Rules/Cover (Player Core)|cover]] except greater cover. If you hit, the projectile deals 7d10 bludgeoning damage.

---
**Heightened (7th)** The damage increases to 9d10.

**Heightened (9th)** The damage increases to 12d10, and the attack ignores cover completely.
