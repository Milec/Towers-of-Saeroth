# Dragon Breath two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 403

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Draconic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Wyrmblessed]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot cone or 60-foot line originating from you or 10 ft. burst within 30 ft.

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex or Fortitude

---

You spew energy from your mouth, dealing 5d6 damage. The area, damage type, and save depend on the dragon type in your bloodline.
