# Undertow two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create an area of water 20 feet deep. The DC for [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] in the area is equal to your spell DC. A creature that starts its turn in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to Athletics checks to Swim and a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim Speed]], if it has one, until it leaves the area.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is pulled 10 feet down.
