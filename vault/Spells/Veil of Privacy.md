# Veil of Privacy  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 366

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Lao Shu Po (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lao Shu Po]], [[Setting/Deities/Naderi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Naderi]], [[Setting/Deities/Nocticula (Pathfinder -219- Lord of the Trinity Star)|Nocticula]], [[Setting/Deities/Winlas (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Winlas]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature or object

**Duration** 8 hours

---

You erect protective wards that make the target difficult to detect via magic. _Veil of privacy_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] all [[Traits/Detection|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]], and [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] effects used against the target or the target's gear throughout the duration, counting cantrips as 1st-rank spells for this purpose. Successfully counteracting a spell that targets an area or multiple targets negates the effects for only _veil of privacy's_ target.
