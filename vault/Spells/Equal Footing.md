# Equal Footing two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 53

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Andoran have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature with a land Speed greater than yours

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You level the field between yourself and another creature, hampering its movements if it’s quicker than you. The target attempts a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 and takes a –10-foot status penalty to all its Speeds until the end of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is clumsy 1 and takes a –15-foot status penalty to all its Speeds for 1 minute. During this time, it can’t benefit from bonuses to its Speeds or take other penalties to its Speeds.  

**Critical Failure** The target is clumsy 2 and takes a –15-foot status penalty to all its Speeds for 1 minute. During this time, it can’t benefit from bonuses to its Speeds or take other penalties to its Speeds.
