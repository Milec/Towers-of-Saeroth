# Hero's Defiance free action  *Focus 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 256

**Trigger** An attack would bring you to 0 Hit Points.

---

You shout in defiance and are filled with a burst of healing. Just before applying the attack's damage, you recover 6d8+20 Hit Points. If this is enough to prevent the attack from bringing you to 0 Hit Points, you don't become [[Rules/Conditions/Unconscious (Player Core)|unconscious]] or dying. Either way, cheating death is difficult, and you can't use _hero's defiance_ again until you [[Actions/Refocus (Player Core)|Refocus]] or make your daily preparations. _Hero's defiance_ can't be used against effects with the [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] trait or that would leave no remains, such as [[Spells/Disintegrate (Player Core)|_disintegrate_]].
