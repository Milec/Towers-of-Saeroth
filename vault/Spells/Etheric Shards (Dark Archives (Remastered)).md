# Etheric Shards two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 105

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You reach out and sow glittering splinters of force throughout the area, otherworldly caltrops of will and ectoplasm. The area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], even to flying or incorporeal creatures. Any creature that moves through the area takes 1d4 piercing damage per square traversed, with a basic Reflex save made at the end of each move action they take through the area.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The spell’s area increases by 5 feet, and the piercing damage increases by 1.
