# Torturous Trauma two actions  *Cantrip 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

With this spell born of Kemnebi's sadistic love of torture, you batter a creature's internal organs, leaving no external signs of the immense pain you delivered. The target takes bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 + your spellcasting modifier with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If it critically fails, the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]] until the start of your next turn. Creatures that lack internal organs are unaffected by this spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The bludgeoning damage increases by 1d4.
