# Summon Healing Servitor three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Incarnate]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 306

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You call forth a servitor forged from raw positive energy to heal and bolster your allies. The servitor occupies the space of a Huge creature and has a speed of 60 feet.

 **Arrive** ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]]) Servitor's Protection The servitor appears in a flash of light, moving from ally to ally and granting them a temporary reprieve. All allies within 20 feet of the Servitor gain 20 temporary Hit Points until the servitor departs.

 **Depart** ([[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive|positive]]) Servitor's Blessing The servitor disappears in a 30-foot emanation of positive energy. All willing living creatures within heal 3d8 Hit Points. All [[Traits/Undead|undead]] within take that much damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The Arrive effect's temporary Hit Points increase by 8, and the Depart effect's healing and damage to undead increase by 1d8.
