# Glass Form two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 94

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You grant the target properties of dull, transparent glass. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], has no scent, and can't bleed. They gain resistance 5 to acid, cold, electricity, and piercing damage and weakness 5 to sonic and bludgeoning damage. A creature in this form is affected by the [[Spells/Shatter|_shatter_]] spell as though the creature were an unattended object. Each time the target takes damage to which the spell grants resistance or weakness, reduce the duration by 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistances and weaknesses increase by 5.
