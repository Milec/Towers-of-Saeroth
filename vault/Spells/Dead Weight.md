# Dead Weight two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 181

**Range** 30 feet,

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 round

---

You command a thrall to hurl itself on another creature, fusing their flesh together. The target thrall launches itself at a creature within 15 feet, and the target must attempt a Fortitude saving throw. The target thrall is destroyed.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]].  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and off-guard.  

**Critical Failure** The target is slowed 2 and off-guard.
