# Glossolalia two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 142

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You scramble the ability of the target to use language, causing them to speak in unintelligible tongues.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** For 1 round, the target's language becomes unintelligible, they're unable to communicate ideas with other creatures, and any action they take with the [[Traits/Linguistic (Player Core)|linguistic]] trait fails.  

**Failure** As success, and the target can't cast spells that require incantations for the duration. Spells with the [[Traits/Subtle|subtle]] trait don't require incantations.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the duration is 1 minute.
