# Organsight two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Revelation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 120

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living or  undead  creature that has organs

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You see the target as though it's dissected and arrayed before you. For the duration, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks against the target that depend on its organs, but a –2 circumstance penalty on Medicine checks depending on seeing its skin.

 When you Cast the Spell, attempt a special [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] check using Medicine to spot and discern a vital organ. If you have a [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill appropriate to the creature, you can use that skill instead of Medicine. If you succeed, the next time you deal piercing or slashing damage to the target with a Strike or spell, you deal 4d6 additional precision damage. Once on each of your subsequent turns, you can use a single action to attempt the special Recall Knowledge check again. The extra damage isn't cumulative, so making the check more than once before a Strike or spell has no extra benefit.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The precision damage increases by 1d6.
