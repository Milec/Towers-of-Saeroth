# Prismatic Shield single action  *Spell 9*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -162- Ruins of the Radiant Siege|Pathfinder #162: Ruins of the Radiant Siege]] pg. 75

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** until the start of your next turn

---

Shield-sized shards of multicolored light manifest in the air around you, each rotating quickly to intercept attacks. Melee attacks against you impact a randomly colored shard, subjecting the attacker to a random effect from [[Spells/Prismatic Spray|_prismatic spray_]], with a saving throw of the type indicated in that spell. The shards automatically block physical ranged attacks the size of a [[Equipment/Weapons/Spear|spear]] or smaller, such as [[Equipment/Weapons/Arrows|arrows]], [[Equipment/Weapons/Bolts|bolts]], and [[Equipment/Weapons/Javelin|javelins]]. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your AC against attacks with larger ranged weapons. Any spell that targets you requires the caster to succeed at a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against your spell DC, or it fails to affect you.
