# Magical Fetters two actions  *Spell 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

**Duration** see below

---

Ghostly manacles launch from your outstretched hand and clasp around the target's limbs, impeding its movements. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is clumsy 3 and takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] for the duration.
