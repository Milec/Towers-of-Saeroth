# Impaling Spike two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 338

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Garhaazh]], [[Setting/Deities/Laudinmio (Divine Mysteries)|Laudinmio]], [[Setting/Deities/Shapes of the Fading Luster]], [[Setting/Deities/Varg]], [[Setting/Deities/Xiquiripat]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure a spike that thrusts up from the earth beneath a target creature, potentially impaling it. The spike is made of [[Equipment/Cold Iron|cold iron]] and deals 8d6 piercing damage. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target dodges the spike and is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is struck by the spike and takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target is impaled through a leg or another non-vital body part. The creature takes full damage and, if it's standing on solid ground, becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]]. It can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] (the DC is your spell DC). While it remains impaled, it takes damage from any weakness to cold iron it has at the end of each of its turns.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is impaled through a vital organ or its center of mass, taking double damage, and it is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] as long as it's impaled.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
