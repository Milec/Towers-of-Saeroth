# Clear Mind two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 320

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

---

You drive mental contamination from the target's mind. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an effect of your choice imposing one of these conditions on the target: [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]]. If you failed to counteract the effect but you would have if its counteract rank were 2 lower, instead suppress the effect until the beginning of your next turn. The effect's duration doesn't elapse while it's suppressed. This spell can't counteract or suppress conditions that are part of curses, diseases, or a natural state of the target.

---
**Heightened (4th)** Add [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]], [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th rank, plus add [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]].

**Heightened (8th)** As 4th rank, plus add [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed (Player Core)|doomed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]].
