# Dinosaur Form two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 324

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Balumbdar (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Balumbdar]], [[Setting/Deities/Sobek (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Sobek]], [[Setting/Deities/Valani]], [[Setting/Deities/Zevgavizeb (Divine Mysteries)|Zevgavizeb]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You channel the primal forces of nature to transform into a Large animal battle form, specifically that of a powerful and terrifying dinosaur. When you Cast this Spell, choose a listed battle form. You can decide the specific type of animal, but this has no effect on the form's Size or statistics. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] and [[Traits/Dinosaur|dinosaur]] traits. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 18 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 15 temporary Hit Points.
- Low-light vision and imprecise scent 30 feet.
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +16, and your damage bonus is +9. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +18, unless your own is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the form you choose:

- **Ankylosaurus** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Backswing|backswing]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning; **Melee** single action foot, **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning.
- **Brontosaurus** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning; **Melee** single action foot, **Damage** 2d8 bludgeoning.
- **Deinonychus** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** single action talon ([[Traits/Agile|agile]]), **Damage** 2d4 piercing plus 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed]]; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 1d10 piercing.
- **Stegosaurus** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing.
- **Triceratops** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action horn, **Damage** 2d8 piercing, plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed]] on a critical hit; **Melee** single action foot, **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning.
- **Tyrannosaurus** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 1d12 piercing; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 1d10 bludgeoning.

---
**Heightened (5th)** Your battle form is Huge and your attacks have 15-foot reach, or 20-foot reach if they started with 15-foot reach. You instead gain 20 temporary HP, an attack modifier of +18, a damage bonus of +6, double the damage dice, and Athletics +21.

**Heightened (7th)** Your battle form is Gargantuan and your attacks have 20-foot reach, or 25-foot reach if they started with 15-foot reach. You instead gain AC = 21 + your level, 25 temporary HP, an attack modifier of +25, a damage bonus of +15, double the damage dice, and Athletics +25.
