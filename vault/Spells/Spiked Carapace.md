# Spiked Carapace two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 164

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** 1 round

---

Your bones harmlessly puncture the skin of one arm, growing to form a hardened spiked gauntlet that can be used for both offense and defense. Make a melee spell attack against a target’s AC, dealing 5d8 piercing damage and 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] (double damage on a critical hit). You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your AC for the duration, as you deflect attacks with the gauntlet.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 2d8 and persistent bleed damage increases by 1d8.
