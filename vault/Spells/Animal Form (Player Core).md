# Animal Form two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 315

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ananshea]], [[Setting/Deities/Angazhan (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Angazhan]], [[Setting/Deities/Cernunnos (Divine Mysteries)|Cernunnos]], [[Setting/Deities/Drokalion]], [[Setting/Deities/Geryon (Divine Mysteries)|Geryon]], [[Setting/Deities/Kagia]], [[Setting/Deities/Kazutal (Divine Mysteries)|Kazutal]], [[Setting/Deities/Lamashtu (Divine Mysteries)|Lamashtu]], [[Setting/Deities/Ravithra (Divine Mysteries)|Ravithra]], [[Setting/Deities/Rowdrosh (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Rowdrosh]], [[Setting/Deities/Stag Mother of the Forest of Stones (Divine Mysteries)|Stag Mother of the Forest of Stones]], [[Setting/Deities/Wadjet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Wadjet]], [[Setting/Deities/Yrmidar]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call upon primal energy to transform yourself into a Medium animal battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose a listed battle form. You can decide the specific type of animal (such as lion or snow leopard for cat), but this has no effect on the form's Size or statistics. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Animal|animal]] trait. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which animal you choose:

- AC = 16 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 5 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]] and [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] [[Rules/Scent|scent]] 30 feet.
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to your battle form, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +9, and your damage bonus is +1. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack bonus is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +9, unless your own is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the animal you choose:

## Player Core

- **Ape** Speed 25 feet, climb 20 feet; Melee single action fist, Damage 2d6 bludgeoning.
- **Bear** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d8 piercing; **Melee** single action claw (agile), **Damage** 1d8 slashing.
- **Bull** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action horn, **Damage** 2d8 piercing.
- **Canine** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d8 piercing.
- **Cat** Speed 40 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d6 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile|agile]]), **Damage** 1d10 slashing.
- **Deer** Speed 50 feet; **Melee** single action antler, **Damage** 2d6 piercing.
- **Frog** Speed 25 feet, swim 25 feet; **Melee** single actionjaws, **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning; **Melee** single action tongue ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d4 bludgeoning.
- **Shark** swim 35 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d8 piercing; breathe underwater but not in air.
- **Snake** Speed 20 feet, climb 20 feet, swim 20 feet; **Melee** single action fangs, **Damage** 2d4 piercing plus 1d6 poison.

## Howl of the Wild

- **Crab** Speed 25 feet, swim Speed 15 ft; **Melee** single action big claw, **Damage** 2d8 piercing; Melee single action little claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]]), **Damage** 2d4 piercing; can breathe in water and in air.
- **Crocodile** Speed 25 feet, swim Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d8 piercing; **Melee** single action tail ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]]), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning; can hold your breath for the duration of the transformation.
- **Orca** swim Speed 35 feet; **Melee** single action jaws, **Damage** 2d8 piercing; can hold your breath for the duration of the transformation.
- **Seal** Speed 20 feet, swim Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Grapple (Player Core)|grapple]]), **Damage** 2d6 piercing; can hold your breath for the duration of the transformation.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** You instead gain 10 temporary HP, AC = 17 + your level, attack modifier +14, damage bonus +5, and Athletics +14.

**Heightened (4th)** Your battle form is Large and your attacks have 10-foot reach. You instead gain 15 temporary HP, AC = 18 + your level, attack modifier +16, damage bonus +9, and Athletics +16.

**Heightened (5th)** Your battle form is Huge and your attacks have 15-foot reach. You instead gain 20 temporary HP, AC = 18 + your level, attack modifier +18, damage bonus +7 and double the number of damage dice, and Athletics +20.
