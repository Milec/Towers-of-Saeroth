# Hand of the Apprentice single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 388

**Range** 500 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You take advantage of one of the most fundamental lessons of magic to levitate and propel your weapon. You hurl a held melee weapon with which you are trained at the target, making a spell attack roll. On a success, you deal the weapon's damage as if you had hit with a melee Strike, but add your spellcasting attribute modifier to damage, rather than your Strength modifier. On a critical success, you deal double damage, and you add the weapon's [[Rules/Critical Specialization Effects|critical specialization effect]]. Regardless of the outcome, the weapon flies back to you and returns to your hand.
