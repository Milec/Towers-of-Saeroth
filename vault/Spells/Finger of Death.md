# Finger of Death two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 338

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Psychopomp]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You point your finger toward the target and speak a word of slaying. You deal 70 negative damage to the target. If the damage from _finger of death_ reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, the target dies instantly.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 10.
