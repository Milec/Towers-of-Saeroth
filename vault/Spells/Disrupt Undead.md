[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Psychopomp]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1  undead  creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You lance the target with energy. You deal 2d6 vitality damage. The target must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. If the creature critically fails the save, it is also [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled 1]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
