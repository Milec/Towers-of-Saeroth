# Gift of the Anemos single action  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Animist]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 83

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your apparition envelops you in gusting winds that can speed your steps or buffet your foes. When an enemy in the aura critically fails a Strike against you, they must succeed at a Reflex save or become [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] until the end of your next turn. When you cast or sustain this spell, you can either Step, [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]] a creature in the aura using your spell attack modifier in place of your [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier, or jump 10 feet in any direction (you must land on solid ground, or else you fall).
