# Leng Sting two actions  *Spell 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Agents of Edgewatch

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -160- Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven|Pathfinder #160: Assault on Hunting Lodge Seven]] pg. 80

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You use the power of nightmares to magically replicate the venomous attack of the aberrant and cunning [[Bestiary/Leng Spider|Leng spiders]]. You deal 2d6 piercing damage to the touched creature and afflict it with Leng spider venom. If the target takes piercing damage, they must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 2d6 poison damage.  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with Leng spider venom at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with Leng spider venom at stage 2.

 **Leng Spider Venom** ([[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], poison) **Level** 13; **Maximum Duration** 6 rounds; **Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** 3d6 poison damage and drained 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** 4d6 poison damage, drained 2, and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] (1 round)
