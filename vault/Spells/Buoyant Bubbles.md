# Buoyant Bubbles single action  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 172

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a thin layer of foamy bubbles that adhere to the target, causing it to float in water and similar liquids. The target doesn't sink, even if it hasn't succeeded at a [[Actions/Swim]] check this round; an already-sinking target resurfaces with the bubbles' help over the course of 1 round. If on a plane where the water or liquid has a surface, the bubbles also prevent the target from diving beneath that surface unless it succeeds at a Fortitude save against your spell DC.

---
**Heightened (4th)** You can target up to 5 creatures.
