# Laughing Fit two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 340

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Fey (Player Core 2)|Fey]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Bes (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Bes]], [[Setting/Deities/Chaldira (Divine Mysteries)|Chaldira]], [[Setting/Deities/Cong]], [[Setting/Deities/Kofusachi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Kofusachi]], [[Setting/Deities/The Lantern King (Divine Mysteries)|The Lantern King]], [[Setting/Deities/The Perplexing Jest]], [[Setting/Deities/Upion and Warrik]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained

---

The target is overtaken with uncontrollable laughter. It must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is plagued with uncontrollable laughter. It can't use reactions.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] and can't use reactions.  

**Critical Failure** The target falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and can't use actions or reactions for 1 round. It then takes the effects of a failure.
