# Healing Plaster single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 109

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 5 feet

**Target** a handful of dirt or mud

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You purify some dirt or mud into a pliable, claylike plaster that can aid in clotting and healing. This restorative substance can be used in lieu of [[Equipment/Healer's Tools|healer's tools]] for [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] checks to [[Actions/Administer First Aid]] or [[Actions/Treat Wounds]]. If the plaster isn't used within the spell's duration, or if you Cast the Spell again before using the plaster, it reverts to being normal non-magical earth.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The plaster grants anyone using it to Administer First Aid or Treat Wounds a +1 item bonus to the Medicine check.
