# Cloak of Light two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 256

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** sustained for up to 1 minute

---

You surround yourself in holy light that restores the living and rebuffs undead. You glow with [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]] in a 30-foot radius and [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] to the next 30 feet. Living creatures that begin their turn adjacent to you recover 4d6 Hit Points. [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|Undead]] creatures that begin their turn adjacent to you take 4d6 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage (basic Fortitude save).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The healing increases by 2d6, and the damage to undead increases by 2d6.
