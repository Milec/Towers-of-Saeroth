# Soul Siphon single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 233

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Bones]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You siphon off a portion of the target's soul to bolster your own vitality. The target must attempt a Fortitude save or take 1d4 negative damage. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the total amount of HP the target loses, including the HP lost from becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]].

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes drained 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and becomes drained 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 1d4.
