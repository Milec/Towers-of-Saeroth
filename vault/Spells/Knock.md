# Knock two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 347

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Alseta (Divine Mysteries)|Alseta]], [[Setting/Deities/Barzahk (Divine Mysteries)|Barzahk]], [[Setting/Deities/Dranngvit (Divine Mysteries)|Dranngvit]], [[Setting/Deities/Lao Shu Po (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Lao Shu Po]], [[Setting/Deities/The Deliberate Journey]], [[Setting/Deities/Urban Prosperity]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 door, lock, or container

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You make the target easier to open. _Knock_ grants a +4 status bonus to any creature that tries to open the target door, lock, or container with an Athletics or a Thievery check. You can attempt a Thievery check to open the target as part of casting _knock_, and you add your level even if you're untrained.

 _Knock_ counteracts [[Spells/Lock|_lock_]].
