# Purifying Veil two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 143

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 Large or smaller creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Drawing on the purifying powers of water, you call forth a veil of fine water droplets suffused with holy energy. The veil surrounds the target. Everything on each side of the veil is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] from creatures on the opposite side, but the water doesn't impede attacks or movement across the veil. If the target moves through the veil, it takes 4d6 good damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save. If the target leaves the veil, the spell ends.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The good damage increases by 1d6.
