# Tortoise and the Hare two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 168

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 foe, and either yourself or 1 ally

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You prove that slow and steady doesn’t always win the race by slowing a foe into a proverbial tortoise and yourself or an ally into a hare, siphoning some of the foe’s speed and granting it to the other target. The effects depend on the foe’s Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The targets are unaffected.  

**Success** The foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round, and the other target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The foe is slowed 1 for 3 rounds, and the other target is [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]] for 1 round. They can use the extra action only to Step, Stride, or Strike.  

**Critical Failure** The foe is slowed 1 for 3 minutes, and the other target is quickened for 1 minute. They can use the extra action only to Step, Stride, or Strike.
