# Chastising Retort ⤾  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 267

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Naga (Divine Mysteries)|Naga]]

**Trigger** A creature within range attempts a Deception check
to Lie or Feint.

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** the triggering creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You unleash some choice words empowered with divine might. The creature takes 2d6 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage and must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  
**Success** The creature takes half damage.  
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 for 1d4 rounds.  
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage and is stupefied 2 for 1d4 rounds.

The creature can immediately choose to reveal the truth about its deception as a free action to immediately recover Hit Points equal to the mental damage it took and lose the stupefied condition from the spell. If it does, it quickly notes that its words and actions were lies, though it might need more time to explain the proper truth. If the creature used Deception to successfully Feint and it reveals the truth in this way, the target of the creature’s Feint is no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] due to the successful Feint.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
