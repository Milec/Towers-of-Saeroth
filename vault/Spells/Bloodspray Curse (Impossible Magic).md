# Bloodspray Curse two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 125

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** varies

---

You inflict a curse upon the target that causes any wound to gush blood. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** For 1 minute, the first time each round that the target would take at least 10 piercing or slashing damage from a single Strike, the strike deals an additional 2d6 damage of the same type.  

**Failure** As success, but the Strike also deals an additional 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The additional damage increases by 1d6, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d6.
