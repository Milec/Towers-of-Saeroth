# Time Sense single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Divination]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 183

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Mysteries**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Time]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Time (Divine Mysteries)|Time]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

When you Cast the Spell, you gain a perfect sense of time. You can learn exactly what time it is, down to the second. You can instead choose to gain a perfect sense of the calendar if you have knowledge in that area—if you're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]], you can learn exactly how long it is until sunrise and how many days until the next solstice. If you're trained in [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Astronomy Lore]], you can learn precisely when any known predictable astronomical conjunction will begin and end, and so on.

 You can gain a +1 status bonus to one attack roll, Perception check, saving throw, or skill check you take on the same turn you cast _time sense_ that requires extremely precise timing. If you choose to gain this bonus, you can't gain it again for 1 hour.
