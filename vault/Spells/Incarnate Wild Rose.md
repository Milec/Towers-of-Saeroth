# Incarnate Wild Rose three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 148

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You call up the giant wild rose, a flower famous for both its beauty and its thorns. The rose occupies the space of a Huge creature but has no Speed.  

**Arrive** _Writhing Vines_ A vast number of thorny vines burst from the ground, wrapping around those who threaten you. Each enemy within a 30-foot emanation of the rose takes 3d12 piercing damage and must attempt a Reflex saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature takes no damage.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by a vine for the duration of the spell or until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by a vine for the duration of the spell or until they Escape.  

**Depart** ([[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]) _Blood Pollen_ All creatures still [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] by vines take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent poison damage]]. The wild rose then expels a cloud of toxic pollen in a 20-foot burst up to 60 feet away. All living creatures in the burst must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is enfeebled 2 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is enfeebled 3 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The rose’s arrival damage increases by 1d12, and its departure damage increases by 1d6.
