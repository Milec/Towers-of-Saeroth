# Vital Seed two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 170

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You launch a seed that drains the life energy of nearby creatures. Make a ranged spell attack to embed a seed of energy into your target. If the target is willing, you automatically succeed. A successful attack embeds the seed, causing it to drain essence out of nearby creatures, dealing 2d12 void damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save to all other creatures in a 15-foot emanation from the target.  

The seed then erupts with vital energy—if the initial target is living, they recover Hit Points equal to twice the damage a single creature took from this spell; calculate these Hit Points using the creature that took the most damage. If the initial target is undead, it instead takes vitality damage equal to the amount a living creature would’ve healed.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The seed’s void damage increases by 1d12.
