# Mirecloak two actions  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** up to 4 creatures

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** up to 1 minute

---

You wrap the targeted creatures in thin, sickly green shrouds that sap information from attackers. The shroud settles about each target's shoulders, granting a +1 status bonus to AC and a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks, both only while the target is in dim light or darkness.

 If a creature deals damage in melee to or touches a cloaked creature, the cloak wraps around the attacker, temporarily connecting the psyches of the cloaked creature and the attacker and violently prying a random piece of interesting information from the attacker's mind. The attacker takes 2d8 mental damage and must attempt a Will save. Afterward, the spell ends for the cloaked creature, and the attacker is temporarily immune to _mirecloak_ for 24 hours.

**Critical Success** The attacker is unaffected.  

**Success** The attacker takes half damage.  

**Failure** The attacker takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]]. In addition, the cloaked creature gleans a piece of information from the attacker's mind. The details and extent of this information are at the GM's discretion.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the attacker takes double damage and is sickened 2.
