# Incendiary Aura two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 232

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself with a combustible aura. Each time a creature within the emanation takes fire damage, it catches on fire, taking 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the persistent damage by 1d4 and the radius of the emanation by 5 feet.
