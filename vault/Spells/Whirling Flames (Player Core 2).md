# Whirling Flames two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 260

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames (Player Core 2)|Flames]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** up to two 5-foot bursts that don’t overlap

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You call forth a storm of whirling flames, engulfing all creatures in that area and dealing 5d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d6, and you can add another nonoverlapping 5-foot burst to the area.
