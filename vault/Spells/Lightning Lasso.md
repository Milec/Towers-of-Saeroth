# Lightning Lasso two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 151

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30-feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You conjure a crackling lasso of pure electricity, hurling it at a creature to bind them in place. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected  

**Success** The target takes 2d12 electricity damage.  

**Failure** The target takes 2d12 electricity damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the end of your next turn, but it can [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]]. On subsequent turns, if the target is still immobilized by the lasso, you can Sustain the spell to deal 2 electricity damage and keep the target immobilized until the end of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The initial electricity damage increases by 1d12, and the electricity damage dealt when you Sustain the spell increases by 2.
