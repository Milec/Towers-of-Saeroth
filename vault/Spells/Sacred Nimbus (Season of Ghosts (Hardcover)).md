# Sacred Nimbus two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 309

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) _Sacred nimbus_ deals 5d6 spirit damage with the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 10-foot square

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You summon a small, fluffy white nimbus cloud that floats 10 feet above the ground and rains down pleasantly warm sanctified water. The rain deals 5d6 spirit damage to [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiends]], [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]], and creatures that have a [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] to spirit damage in the area at the end of your turn. When you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell, you can move the cloud at a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly speed]] of 15 feet to anywhere in the spell’s range. The water dissipates as it makes contact with any creatures or surfaces, leaving only a bit of humidity in its place.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
