# Wilding Word single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 387

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Wilding Steward]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron's majesty—or their displeasure—comes in a growl from your throat, making other creatures reluctant to harm you. The target must attempt a Will save; if the creature is an [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Fungus|fungus]], or [[Traits/Plant|plant]], it takes a –1 circumstance penalty to its save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** When the target attempts an attack roll or skill check that would harm you, it takes a –2 status penalty to its roll.  

**Failure** As success, but the target also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]] each time it damages you.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the sickened value is 2.
