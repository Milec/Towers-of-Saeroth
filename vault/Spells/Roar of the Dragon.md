# Roar of the Dragon two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 265

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Dragon]]

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You channel the might of dragons into your voice, letting out a roar that engenders respect in dragonkind but that instills fear in most other creatures. The impressive roar grants you a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks for 10 minutes against dragons that were in the area at the time of casting. _Roar of the dragon_ affects non-dragon creatures with deep ties to dragonkind (such as a [[Classes/Class Features/Ancestry and Background (class-feature-841)|barbarian]] with the [[Classes/Subclasses/Instincts/Dragon (Player Core 2)|draconic instinct]], a sorcerer with the [[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|draconic bloodline]], or a member of a culture that reveres dragons) as if they had the [[Traits/Dragon (Player Core)|dragon]] trait. The GM decides if a creature is aligned enough with dragonkind to be affected in this way. All enemies within the area other than dragons must attempt a Will save; to these enemies, _roar of the dragon_ is a [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]] effect.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.
