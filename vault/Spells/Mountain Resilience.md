# Mountain Resilience two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 346

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Arqueros (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Arqueros]], [[Setting/Deities/Belech]], [[Setting/Deities/Cihua Coatl]], [[Setting/Deities/Dammerich (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Dammerich]], [[Setting/Deities/Imbrex (Divine Mysteries)|Imbrex]], [[Setting/Deities/Irori (Divine Mysteries)|Irori]], [[Setting/Deities/Kazutal (Divine Mysteries)|Kazutal]], [[Setting/Deities/Keepers of the Hearth]], [[Setting/Deities/Myr]], [[Setting/Deities/Sairazul (Divine Mysteries)|Sairazul]], [[Setting/Deities/Soralyon (Divine Mysteries)|Soralyon]], [[Setting/Deities/Talons of the Godclaw]], [[Setting/Deities/Trudd (Divine Mysteries)|Trudd]], [[Setting/Deities/Urazra]], [[Setting/Deities/Vavaalrav]], [[Setting/Deities/Xoveron]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 20 minutes

---

The target's skin hardens like the stone of a mountain face. It gains resistance 5 to physical damage, except [[Equipment/Adamantine Weapon|adamantine]]. Each time the target is hit by a bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing attack, _mountain resilience's_ duration decreases by 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The resistance increases to 10.

**Heightened (8th)** The resistance increases to 15.

**Heightened (10th)** The resistance increases to 20.
