# Geyser two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Izuyaku]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 10-foot radius, 50-foot-tall cylinder

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** 1 round

---

A powerful geyser blasts up from solid ground, pushing creatures on top of it into the air. Each creature in the area takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and 4d6 fire damage, with a Reflex save. A creature pushed into the air by the geyser can't be pushed beyond the top of the geyser. After being pushed into the air, a creature falls unless it's flying or has some other means of staying aloft, taking [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]] (normally equal to half the distance it fell). After the geyser erupts, its area is filled with a cloud of steam for 1 round. All creatures in the steam are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the steam are concealed to creatures within it.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and isn't pushed into the air.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is pushed 20 feet into the air.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is pushed 40 feet into the air.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 bludgeoning and 1d6 fire.
