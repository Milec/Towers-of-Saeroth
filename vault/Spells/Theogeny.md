# Theogeny three actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 166

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Area** 120-[[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]] centered on you

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You spark the genesis of a new divine realm around you. The appearance of the area becomes more obviously divine, matching your own beliefs. All creatures within the area temporarily transcend into divine beings. If you’re holy, your allies gain the [[Traits/Holy|holy]] trait, while your foes gain the [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait and weakness 5 to holy. Similarly, if you’re unholy, your allies gain the unholy trait, while your foes gain the holy trait and weakness 5 to unholy. Creatures that already have the holy or unholy trait don’t gain any new traits or weaknesses. While in the area, you and all allies gain a fly Speed equal to their highest Speed, and their Strikes gain the sanctified trait and deal an additional 1d10 spirit damage on hit. Additionally, all enemies take 10 spirit damage whenever they’re critically hit by an attack or whenever they critically fail a saving throw.
