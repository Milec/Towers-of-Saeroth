# Flames of Ego two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 119

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

Elegant flames of incredible beauty coruscate across the target's body, creating overconfidence and carelessness. The target attempts a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target sheds [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] in a 20-foot emanation (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] to the next 40 feet) and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] with itself for 1 round. It must spend at least 1 action on its turn on a taunting display of arrogance or overconfidence. Using actions that include such a display—such as using [[Actions/Perform]] to show off—count toward this requirement.  

**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute and the target can't act hostile toward a creature until that creature [[Rules/Hostile Actions|acts hostile]] toward the target.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the target must spend at least 2 actions on its turn on a taunting display of arrogance or overconfidence.
