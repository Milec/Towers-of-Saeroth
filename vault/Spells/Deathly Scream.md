# Deathly Scream single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 182

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
Will

---

Your thrall lets forth a spectral scream that pierces the mind. While the sound is loud, only those closest can feel the true sting of death in it. Each creature within a 5-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] of the target thrall takes 1d4 mental damage depending on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is frightened 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The mental damage increases by 1d4.
