# Heal Animal single action to two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 382

**Range** touch or 30 feet (see text)

**Target** 1 willing living animal

---

You heal an animal's wounds, restoring 1d8 Hit Points to the target. The number of actions spent Casting this Spell determines its effect.

 single action The spell has a range of touch.  
 two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) The spell has a range of 30 feet and restores an additional 8 Hit Points to the target.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d8, and the additional healing for the 2-action version increases by 8.
