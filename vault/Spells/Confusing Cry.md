# Confusing Cry two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 84

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 20-foot emanation

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You give an unsettling, warbling cry that causes nearby creatures to lash out without control. Each creature in the area that can hear must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected and immune to this spell for 1 minute.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] for 1 minute. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns to end the confusion.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature immediately attacks itself. This Strike doesn’t give the creature a flat check to recover from the confusion.
