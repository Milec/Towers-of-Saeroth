# Skyglass Prison two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 162

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The air around the target solidifies like glass, trapping the creature in a cage that perfectly encases them, making movement nearly impossible. A creature targeted with this spell must attempt a Fortitude save. The cage has AC 10, Hardness 5, and 30 Hit Points, and it’s immune to critical hits and precision damage. When a creature [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] or the cage is destroyed, shards of hardened air cut the target creature, dealing 3d4 slashing damage. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this spell to destroy the cage.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] or until the cage is destroyed. The cage is automatically destroyed at the start of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is immobilized until it Escapes or until the cage is destroyed.  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] until it Escapes or until the cage is destroyed.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The cage’s Hit Points increase by 10, and the slashing damage increases by 1d4.
