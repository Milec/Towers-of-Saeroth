# Scatter Scree two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 127

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** two contiguous 5-foot cubes

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You evoke a jumble of rocks in the area. The scattering rocks deal 2d4 bludgeoning damage to creatures in the area, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain for the duration. A creature can Interact to clear a square of this scree.

 If you cast this spell again, any previous _scatter scree_ you have cast ends.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
