# Cradle Aloft single action  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 130

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 held object of 2 Bulk or less

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You temporarily release gravity's hold on an object, allowing you to let go of it without dropping it to the floor. The object floats next to you in your space, following you if you move. You can Interact to retrieve the object on your turn as a free action. If you're within reach of another creature, that creature can spend a single action with the attack trait to attempt an [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check against your spell DC, retrieving the object out of the air on a success. The creature must have a free hand to attempt this check. The spell ends if a creature successfully retrieves the object. If the object is floating when the spell ends, it falls.
