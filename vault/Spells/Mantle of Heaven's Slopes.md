# Mantle of Heaven's Slopes two actions  *Spell 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Lawful]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -183- Field of Maidens|Pathfinder #183: Field of Maidens]] pg. 77

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself in a mantle of heavenly power, invoking the power of the layers of the celestial mountain above. Your body warps and morphs into an angelic form, made of [[Setting/Planes/Heaven|Heaven's]] light. Upon Casting this Spell, pick two different options below. As a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, you can lose one of the options you chose and select a different one (you don't benefit from having the same option selected twice at the same time).

- **Threshold's Walls** Glimmering barriers form around you. You gain [[Rules/Cover|cover]] from the barriers.
- **Proelera's Arsenal** Shimmering silver pikes surround you, imposing your might on those who would oppose you. You gain an arsenal unarmed attack, which has the [[Traits/Reach|reach]] and [[Traits/Shove|shove]] traits, in the polearm group. Your arsenal attack deals 1d10 piercing damage as its base damage, plus an additional 1d8 good damage, and counts as [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]].
- **Clarion's Sanctity** Your eyes glow with golden light, seeing that which others prefer remain unseen. You gain a +2 status bonus on checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]] and [[Actions/Sense Motive]], and when you succeed at a check to Recall Knowledge about a creature you can see, you can attempt to Sense its Motive as a free action.
- **Requius's Rest** Your hands glow with soft white energy that spreads to your wounds. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing 2]].
- **Illumis's Parlance** Your voice echoes like a trumpet. You can be heard clearly up to 200 feet away when you speak, if you choose to project your voice.
- **Iuidica's Verdict** Your body pulses with scarlet light, pushing away those judged to be against you. As a free action triggered by succeeding at a Strike, you can attempt to [[Actions/Shove]] the creature you hit.
