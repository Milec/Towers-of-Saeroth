# Defy the Gods two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 131

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You've obtained your abilities through sheer mental effort, unaided by any higher powers, and now you drain away what the undeserving have gained through divine shortcuts. If the target has the [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]], [[Traits/Holy|holy]], or [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] trait and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] by this spell, you gain [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 15 to holy and unholy for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is drained 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 1]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The creature is drained 2 and stupefied 2 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is drained 3 and stupefied 3 for 1 minute.
