# Blood in the Water two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex (Player Core)|Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 61

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of the Shark]]

**Requirements** The target is taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] or your last action dealt slashing damage to the target.

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature that can bleed

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Predators are drawn to the scent of blood, and blood can disperse shockingly far in the water. You summon the spirits of aquatic predators to appear as a spectral swarm around the target. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast]] or [[Actions/Sustain]] this Spell, the target takes 2d6 spirit damage (basic Reflex save). If the target takes any damage from the spell, it treats all water as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for 1 round as the predators try to drag them down. The spectral predators don’t take up space, grant [[Rules/Flanking (Player Core)|flanking]], or have any other attributes a creature would.

If you deal slashing damage to the target while the spell is active, you automatically Sustain this Spell. If you cast _blood in the water_ while a previous casting of this hex is still in effect, the previous effect ends.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage dealt increases by 2d6.
