# Coiling Dance two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Holy]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 109

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Area** 30-foot emanation

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You perform a dance that seeks to pass on the knowledge and wisdom of a naga. Your allies in the area are filled with sacred energy, making their spells and attacks holy. Creatures or effects that would be unholy don’t gain this benefit.

 When you cast or Sustain this spell, you can choose an ally in the area that’s [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]]. They can immediately use a reaction to Escape; they can use your Occultism or Religion modifier for the check instead of their unarmed attack, Acrobatics, or Athletics modifier if that would be better.
