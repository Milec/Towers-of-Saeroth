# Blood Ward single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 237

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Protection]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You consolidate warding energies with a gesture, guarding a creature against attacks from a certain type of foe. Designate one of the following creature traits: [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]], [[Traits/Animal|animal]], [[Traits/Beast|beast]], [[Traits/Celestial|celestial]], [[Traits/Construct|construct]], [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]], [[Traits/Elemental|elemental]], [[Traits/Fey|fey]], [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]], [[Traits/Fungus|fungus]], [[Traits/Monitor|monitor]], [[Traits/Ooze|ooze]], [[Traits/Plant|plant]], or [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. The target gains a +1 status bonus to its saving throws and AC against creatures with that trait. The target is temporarily immune for 10 minutes.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The status bonus increases to +2.
