# Claws of the Otter two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 84

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

Webbing grows between your fingers and your nails extend into vicious claws. For the spell's duration, you gain a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] and you gain a claws unarmed attack. They're an [[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Finesse (Player Core)|finesse]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack that deals 1d4 slashing damage and an additional 1d6 cold damage.

---
**Heightened (+3)** The additional cold damage increases by 1d6.
