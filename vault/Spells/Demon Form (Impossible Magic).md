# Demon Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 132

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You corrupt yourself with the sin of the Outer Rifts, transforming into a Medium demon battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose abrikandilu, brimorak, kithangian, or vloriak. If you choose kithangian, the battle form is Large, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Demon (Player Core)|demon]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 30 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]], [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]], and weakness 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +22, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of demon you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Abrikandilu (Monster Core 2)|Abrikandilu]]** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 piercing, and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute on a critical hit; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 slashing; **Ranged** single action hurled debris ([[Traits/Range|range increment 20 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Brimorak (Monster Core)|Brimorak]]** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action flaming sword ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8 slashing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d4 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Kithangian (Monster Core 2)|Kithangian]]** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action pincer ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d12 slashing, and after a successful [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strike]] you can spend an action to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target; **Melee** single action stinger ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8 piercing; all melee Strikes deal an additional 2d6 precision damage to [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]].
- **[[Bestiary/Vloriak (Monster Core 2)|Vloriak]]** Speed 25 feet; [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to acid; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 piercing plus 1d6 spirit; **Melee** single action tongue ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d6 acid plus 1d6 spirit.
