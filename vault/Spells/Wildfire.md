# Wildfire two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 61, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 201

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 10-foot burst

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

A thin layer of ash and flame covers the ground in the area. The area becomes [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]. A creature that moves on the ground through the area takes 1 fire damage for every square of that area it moves into. A creature that ends its turn in the area must succeed at a Reflex save or take 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]].

 Each time you Sustain this spell, the radius of the burst increases by 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the damage and persistent damage by 1.
