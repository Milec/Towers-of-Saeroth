# One with Stone two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 347

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Granduncle Taproot]], [[Setting/Deities/Onos]], [[Setting/Deities/Rhan-Tegoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Soralyon (Divine Mysteries)|Soralyon]], [[Setting/Deities/Yuelral (Divine Mysteries)|Yuelral]]

**Duration** 10 minutes or 8 hours

---

You can either transform into a stone or merge with stone. This spell has the same effects as [[Spells/One with Plants|_one with plants_]], but lets you merge with or turn into stone. A stone you turn into has AC 23. You can Dismiss this spell.
