# Ghostly Carrier two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 333

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) The figure created by ghostly carrier counts as a creature for any abilities that target creatures, such as Reactive Strike._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas (Divine Mysteries)|Abraxas]], [[Setting/Deities/Monad (Divine Mysteries)|Monad]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a Tiny, semi-corporeal figure with a form you choose. It hovers near you for the duration. When you Cast a Spell that has a range of touch, you can have the carrier move within range, deliver the spell to a creature there, and return to you. If the carrier must attempt a spell attack roll, it uses your normal bonuses. The carrier has your AC and saves, but it is destroyed by any damage.
