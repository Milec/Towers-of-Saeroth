# Echoing Weapon two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 134

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 weapon

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You channel energy into the target weapon, and the air around it faintly hums each time it strikes a blow, as the impact is absorbed into the weapon. If a creature is wielding the weapon at the end of its turn, the weapon discharges a burst of sound targeting one creature adjacent to the wielder. The sonic damage dealt is equal to the number of successful [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strikes]] with the target weapon that the wielder made that turn (to a maximum of 4 sonic damage if the wielder hits with four Strikes).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The sonic damage increases by 1 per Strike (and the maximum damage increases by 4).
