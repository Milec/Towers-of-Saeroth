# Focusing Hum single action  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 105

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation centered on you

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You infuse the air around you with a sustained hum that allows creatures within it to resist distractions. The DC of any flat check to Cast a Spell while [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] for creatures within the area is 2 lower. [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|Confused]] creatures in the area don't attack anyone, including themselves, and instead lose the ability to speak coherently. You take a –2 penalty to Stealth checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]].
