# Circle of Weakness single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 187

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

Winning magical combat often requires exploiting weakness. You evoke a circle of floating inscriptions that creates and amplifies energy-based weaknesses on creatures inside the circle. Choose one type of damage from the following list: [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you [[Actions/Sustain]] it each turn, each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature gains weakness 1 to the chosen damage type for 1 round.  

**Failure** As success, except the weakness is doubled.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 round or until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The weakness gained on a success increases by 1.
