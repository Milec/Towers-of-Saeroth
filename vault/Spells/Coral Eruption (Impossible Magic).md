# Coral Eruption three actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 129

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** two 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|bursts]] that don’t overlap

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Razor sharp coral erupts from the ground, transforming the landscape into a dangerous coral reef. You can create a third nonoverlapping burst if the centers of all three bursts are underwater. Creatures in the area when the spell is cast take 6d6 piercing damage and must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails its saving throw also takes 1d6 persistent bleed damage. The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]]. A creature that moves through the area takes 3 piercing damage for every square of that area it moves into.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial piercing damage increases by 3d6, and the hazardous terrain damage increases by 1.
