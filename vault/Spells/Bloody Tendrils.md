# Bloody Tendrils single action  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Thrall]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 180

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure forth stationary tentacles formed from pure, partially hardened blood. You create up to 3 Medium thralls shaped like bloody tendrils in range. These tendrils are immune to physical damage and can’t move. The tendril’s Strike has a reach of 10 feet. Whenever an enemy ends it turn within the reach of one or more tendrils, the tendril lashes out at them, dealing 1d12 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+3)** The number of thralls this spell can create increases by 1, and the damage increases by 1d12.
