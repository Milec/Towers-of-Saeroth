# Thundering Dominance two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 136

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your  companion  or  eidolon

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You shroud the target in a powerful predatory aura and amplify its vocalizations. It gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] checks. Once during this spell's duration, it can take the Thundering Roar action.

 **Thundering Roar** single action ([[Traits/Auditory|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Flourish|flourish]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]], [[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) The target unleashes a powerful vocalization. Each enemy in a 10- foot emanation takes 4d8 sonic damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save against your spell DC. A creature that fails its Will save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The status bonus to Intimidation checks increases by 1 (to a maximum of a +4 status bonus for an 8th-level spell), and the damage from the roar increases by 2d8.
