# Ritual Obstruction  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Curse]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 79

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 1 minute ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Area** 60-foot burst

**Duration** 1 day

---

You establish a zone of magical feedback that makes it impossible to succeed at rituals of this spell's level or lower in the area. _Ritual obstruction_ ignores all cover, including walls and ceilings, to the extent of its area. Anyone attempting to cast a ritual within the area knows, when they begin to cast the ritual, the area is cursed to impede rituals. Any ritual cast in the area can't have a final result better than failure.
