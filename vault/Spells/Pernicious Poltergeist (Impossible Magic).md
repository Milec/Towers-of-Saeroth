# Pernicious Poltergeist two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 156

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You create an echo of a powerful poltergeist, temporarily anchoring it to the area. Because the poltergeist is a manifestation and not truly present, it can’t be attacked or hurt; it remains [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] unless otherwise stated. When you Cast the Spell, and the first time you Sustain it on subsequent rounds, the poltergeist creates your choice of one of the following effects.

- **Deathly Assault** ([[Traits/Void|void]]) The poltergeist focuses on one creature in the area and flies through that creature, dealing 4d10 void damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save.
- **Frighten** ([[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) The poltergeist becomes visible, appearing as a skeletal, ghostlike humanoid. Creatures in the area must attempt a Will save, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 2]] on a failure.
- **Telekinetic Storm** ([[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]]) The poltergeist hurls debris and quasi-real objects around in the area. All creatures in the area take 4d8 force damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage of the deathly assault effect increases by 1d10, and the damage of the telekinetic storm effect increases by 1d8.
