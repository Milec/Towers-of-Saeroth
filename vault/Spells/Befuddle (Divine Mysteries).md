# Befuddle two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 256

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Areshkagal]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyuo-Ogh]], [[Setting/Deities/The Pandemonia]], [[Setting/Deities/Vulot]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You sow seeds of confusion in your target’s mind, causing its actions and thoughts to become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]].

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is clumsy 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1.  

**Failure** The target is clumsy 2 and stupefied 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target is clumsy 3, stupefied 3, and [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]].
