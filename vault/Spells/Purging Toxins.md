# Purging Toxins two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 287

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Blight]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

---

In the right dose, a scourge can become a cure. Deal [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage equal to the spell’s level to the target, then attempt a [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] check against one [[Rules/Diseases (GM Core)|disease]] or poison afflicting the target. The target is then temporarily immune for 24 hours. If the target is [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|immune]] to poison, this spell has no effect.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can attempt counteract checks against any number of poisons or diseases afflicting the target.
