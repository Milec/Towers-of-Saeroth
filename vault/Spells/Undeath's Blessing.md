# Undeath's Blessing single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 405

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature touched

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You instill within a creature the touch of the grave. For the duration, [[Spells/Harm|_harm_]] and [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spells treat the creature as [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. In addition, _harm_ spells gain a +2 status bonus to the Hit Points restored to the target.

 If the target wishes to avoid the spell, it can attempt a Will save to reduce the effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target heals half as much from _heal_ and takes half as much damage from _harm_ for 1 round.  

**Failure** Effects as described in the text.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The status bonus to the Hit Points restored increases by 2.
