# Living Terrain two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 151

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Reflex

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You transform an unoccupied 10-foot burst of ground into a roiling cauldron of earth. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you Sustain it each turn on subsequent rounds, you can move the living terrain up to 15 feet in a straight line along the ground; you can’t move it onto water or other liquids. Creatures in the area at any point during this movement take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and must attempt a Reflex save as the living terrain destabilizes the earth while moving.

**Critical Success** The target takes no damage.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy 1]] until the beginning of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage, is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and is clumsy 1 until the beginning of your next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is knocked prone, and is clumsy 2 until the beginning of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1 d6.
