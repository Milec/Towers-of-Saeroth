# Magic Stone single action to three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 222

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** ([[Rules/Verbal|verbal]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]])

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 to 3 non-magical stones or sling bullets

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You pour positive energy into ordinary stones, granting them temporary magical properties. You can target 1 non-magical stone or sling bullet for every action you use Casting this Spell. The stones must be unattended or carried by you or a willing ally. The stones become _+1 [[Equipment/Striking|striking]] [[Equipment/Disrupting|disrupting]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Sling Bullets|sling bullets]]_. Each stone can be used only once, after which it crumbles to dust.
