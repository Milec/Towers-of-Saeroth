# Mushroom Patch two actions  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 73

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 10-foot-radius  burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You cause mushrooms to sprout in the area. The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (GM Core)|difficult terrain]]. When the spell is [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast]] and at the beginning of each round, the mushroom’s release a cloud of irritating spores. Creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round. If the creature moves to outside area, they can spend 1 action to rub their eyes, removing the dazzled condition.  

**Failure** The creature becomes dazzled for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes dazzled and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The range increases to 90 feet and the area increases to a 20-foot-radius burst.

**Heightened (6th)** The range increases to 120 feet and the area increases to a 30-foot-radius burst.

**Heightened (9th)** The range increases to 150 feet, the area increases to a 40-foot-radius burst, and when a creature would become dazzled, they become [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] instead.
