# Everlight three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 329

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** a gemstone worth 6 gp or more

**Duration** unlimited

---

The gemstone you touch glows, spreading [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] with a color of your choice in a 20-foot radius (and [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] for the next 20 feet). The spell ends immediately if the gemstone is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]].
