# Death Knell two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 328

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature that has 0 HP

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You snuff the life out of a creature on the brink of death. The target must attempt a Will save. If this kills it, you gain 10 temporary HP and a +1 status bonus to attack and damage rolls for 10 minutes.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target's [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] value increases by 1.  

**Failure** The target dies.
