# Call the Blood two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Abomination Vaults

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -165- Eyes of Empty Death|Pathfinder #165: Eyes of Empty Death]] pg. 75

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
basic Fortitude

---

With a word and gesture, you cause streams of blood to erupt from the target's mouth, nostrils, ears, or other facial openings and come rushing into your mouth. You deal 8d4 negative damage to the target.

 If the target takes damage, its blood flows into your mouth. If you're a living creature and don't have [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|negative healing]], you're [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 2]]. If you're undead (or living but have negative healing), you can choose to either gain temporary Hit Points equal to half of the negative damage the target takes (after applying resistance and the like) and become [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] for 1 round, or forgo the temporary Hit Points and become quickened for 1 minute. You can use this extra action only to [[Actions/Step]], [[Actions/Stride]], or [[Actions/Strike]]. You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d4.
