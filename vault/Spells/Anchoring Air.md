# Anchoring Air ⤾  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Trigger** A creature attempts a melee [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] against you.

**Defense**
Reflex

---

You solidify the air around an incoming attack, catching it in a vise-like grip. The triggering creature must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the triggering Strike’s attack roll.  

**Failure** The creature takes a –2 circumstance penalty to all attack rolls until the end of its turn.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until the start of its next turn.
