# You're Mine two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 265

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|Hag]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You manipulate the target's emotions, potentially allowing you to control it for a brief instant. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned 1]].  

**Failure** On the target's next turn, it's stunned 1, and you partially control it, causing it to take a single action of your choice. If it has actions left, it can act normally.  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (7th)** On a failure, the target is controlled for 1 round. On a critical failure, the target is controlled for up to 1 minute; it receives a new Will save at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, the spell ends.
