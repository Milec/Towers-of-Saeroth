# Scramble Body two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 388

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Your magic throws the creature's biology into disarray, inducing nausea, fever, and other unpleasant conditions.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The target becomes sickened 2 and slowed 1 as long as it's sickened.
