You amplify the already intense cracking noise of your gunshot to spread terror through the ranks of your foes. Make a ranged firearm [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]]. Regardless of the result, each enemy in a 20-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] around you must succeed at a Will save against your spell DC or be [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 (or frightened 2 on a critical failure).
