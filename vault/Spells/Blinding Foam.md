# Blinding Foam two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 88

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You spray a colorful, caustic foam into the target's eyes, which clings to their face. Make a spell attack roll. If you hit, you deal 5d10 acid damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]], and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] for the duration. The foam can be removed with 3 Interact actions, which ends the persistent acid damage. These Interact actions don't need to be consecutive. When the target's persistent acid damage ends, the spell automatically ends.

---
**Heightened (8th)** The initial damage increases to 9d10, and the persistent acid damage increases to 2d6.
