# Entangling Flora two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 329

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Green Man (Monster Core 2)|Green Man]], [[Setting/Deities/Immaculate Growth]], [[Setting/Deities/Kzininn]], [[Setting/Deities/Nhimbaloth (Divine Mysteries)|Nhimbaloth]], [[Setting/Deities/Sicva]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** all squares in a 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Plants and fungi burst out or quickly grow, entangling creatures. All surfaces in the area are [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]. Each round that a creature starts its turn in the area, it must attempt a Reflex save. On a failure, it takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it leaves the area, and on a critical failure, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] for 1 round. Creatures can attempt to [[Actions/Escape]] to remove these effects.
