# Mariner's Curse two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Curse]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 350

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Besmara (Divine Mysteries)|Besmara]], [[Setting/Deities/Deshto]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You afflict the target with the curse of the roiling, unforgiving sea. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target becomes sickened 1. Reducing its sickened condition to 0 ends the curse.  

**Failure** The target becomes sickened 1 and can't reduce its sickened condition below 1 while the curse remains. The curse can be lifted by [[Spells/Remove Curse|_remove curse_]] or similar magic. Whenever the target is sickened and on the water at least a mile from shore, it is also slowed 1.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target becomes sickened 2.
