# Dragon Wings two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 264

**Bloodline**
[[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|Draconic]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Leathery wings sprout from your back, giving you a fly Speed of 60 feet or your Speed, whichever is faster. If you're still flying when this spell's duration would end, you float to the ground, with the effects of [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]].

---
**Heightened (8th)** The duration increases to 10 minutes.
