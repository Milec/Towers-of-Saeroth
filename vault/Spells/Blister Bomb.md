# Blister Bomb two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 84

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) This spell is thematically appropriate for warfare, but not for Pathfinder Society._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 5-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You launch a small bomb enchanted with a fast-acting skin disease at your foes, causing their skin to break out in horrible bleeding sores. All creatures in the area of the burst must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and immune to blister pox for 1 week.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 2.  

**Failure** The creature is afflicted with blister pox at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is afflicted with blister pox at stage 2.

**Blister Pox** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) **Level** 5; A creature can’t reduce its [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] value below 1 while it’s taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] from blister pox; **Stage 1** sickened 2 (1 round); **Stage 2** sickened 2 and 1d6 persistent bleed damage (1 round); **Stage 3** sickened 2 and 2d6 persistent bleed damage (1 round); **Stage 4** sickened 3 and 2d6 persistent bleed damage (1 day)
