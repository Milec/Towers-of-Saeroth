# Unimpeded Stride single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 375

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Freedom (Player Core)|Freedom]]

---

Divine grace ensure that nothing can keep you prisoner or hold you back. You immediately escape from every magical effect that has you [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed|grabbed]] unless the effect is of a higher rank than your _unimpeded stride_ spell. You then Stride. During this movement, you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] and any circumstance or status penalties to your Speed.
