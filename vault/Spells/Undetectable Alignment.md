# Undetectable Alignment  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 379

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** 1 minute ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature or object

**Duration** until the next time you make your daily preparations

---

You shroud a creature or object in wards that hide its alignment. The target appears to be neutral to all effects that would detect its alignment.
