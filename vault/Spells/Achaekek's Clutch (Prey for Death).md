# Achaekek's Clutch two actions  *Focus 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Prey for Death

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Prey for Death]] pg. 109

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You mark the holy symbol of Achaekek in a visible location on the target’s body.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is marked by Achaekek's symbol. For 1 minute, the first time per round that the target gains [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]], they immediately take that amount of slashing damage as the mantis claws grow off the symbol and rake them.  

**Failure** As success, but the curse has an unlimited duration.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the DC on the target's flat check to remove persistent bleed damage increases to 20 (15 with particularly effective assistance).
