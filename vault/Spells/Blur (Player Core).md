# Blur two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Black Butterfly (Divine Mysteries)|Black Butterfly]], [[Setting/Deities/Mahathallah (Divine Mysteries)|Mahathallah]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Veiled Voice)]], [[Setting/Deities/Sivanah (Divine Mysteries)|Sivanah]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target's form appears blurry. It becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]]. As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to [[Actions/Hide]] or [[Actions/Sneak]].
