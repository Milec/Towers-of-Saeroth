# Ferrous Form two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 142

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ptah]], [[Setting/Deities/Shapes of the Fading Luster]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

Your body transforms entirely into flexible iron. You gain resistance 10 to physical damage, except adamantine. You're immune to [[Traits/Death|death]] effects, [[Traits/Disease|disease]], [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], [[Traits/Healing|healing]], [[Traits/Nonlethal|nonlethal]] attacks, [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Traits/Poison|poison]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]], and [[Traits/Void|void]]; any of those conditions you had when the spell is cast are suspended until the spell ends, then return with their remaining duration when the spell ends. While made of iron, you're subject to rusting effects like the [[Spells/Rust Cloud|_rust cloud_]] spell.

 Your fist Strikes have a 1d10 damage die, and your metal spells deal one additional die of damage (of the same damage die and damage type the spell uses). You can cast [[Spells/Needle Darts|_needle darts_]] as an innate spell; the casting is reduced from 2 actions to 1.

 In this form, you don't need to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|breathe]]. Your Bulk doubles (to 6 if you're Small or 12 if you're Medium), and you become too dense to [[Actions/Swim]] in water, automatically sinking to the bottom. You can Dismiss the spell.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The resistance increases to 15.
