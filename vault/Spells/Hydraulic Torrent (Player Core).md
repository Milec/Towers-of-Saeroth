# Hydraulic Torrent two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 336

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apep]], [[Setting/Deities/Hei Feng (Divine Mysteries)|Hei Feng]], [[Setting/Deities/Jin Li (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Jin Li]], [[Setting/Deities/Kelizandri (Divine Mysteries)|Kelizandri]], [[Setting/Deities/Matravash (Divine Mysteries)|Matravash]], [[Setting/Deities/Obari]], [[Setting/Deities/Tresmalvos]]

**Area** 60-foot line

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

A swirling torrent of water manifests along a straight line, battering creatures and unattended objects in its path and possibly [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushing]] them away from you. The torrent deals 8d6 bludgeoning damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a basic Fortitude save; unattended objects automatically fail. Creatures and objects that fail are also knocked back 5 feet (10 feet on a critical failure).

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
