# Glacial Skewer two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You create a large spike of ice and launch it at a target. Make a ranged spell attack against the target's AC. If it hits, the target takes 4d10 cold damage and 4d4 piercing damage, and is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet away from you. On a critical hit, the target takes double damage and is pushed 20 feet away from you. If the target is pushed into an obstacle or structure that's Large or larger, the target becomes skewered by the ice spike and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 minute or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The cold damage is increased by 1d10, and the piercing damage is increased by 1d4.
