# Iron Rain two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 150

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot radius

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call forth blades from the [[Setting/Planes/Plane of Metal]], creating piercing daggers that shoot down from the sky. These blades deal 6d4 piercing damage and 3 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] to all creatures in the area. As the rain dissipates, the ground is littered with hundreds of knives, creating [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] that lasts for the duration of the spell. Any creature that's within the difficult terrain can Interact to pick up one of the knives and wield it as a mundane [[Equipment/Weapons/Dagger (Player Core)|dagger]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
