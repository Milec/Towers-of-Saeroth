# Veil of Confidence single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 373

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Confidence (Player Core)|Confidence]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself in confidence. You reduce your current [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] condition by 1, and whenever you would become frightened during the duration, reduce the amount by 1. If you critically fail a save against a [[Traits/Fear|fear]] effect, _veil of confidence_ reduces your frightened value from that effect, and then ends.
