# Spiritual Epidemic two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Curse (Player Core)|Curse]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 252

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You curse the target, sapping its spirit and leaving a contagious trap in its essence. The target must attempt a Will save. Any creature that casts a [[Traits/Divine (Player Core)|divine]] or [[Traits/Occult (Player Core)|occult]] spell on the target while it's affected is targeted by spiritual epidemic and must also attempt a Will save. The curse continues to spread in this way.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled 2]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]] for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is enfeebled 2 and stupefied 2 for 1 minute and enfeebled 1 and stupefied 1 permanently.  

**Critical Failure** The target is enfeebled 3 and stupefied 3 for 1 minute and enfeebled 2 and stupefied 2 permanently.
