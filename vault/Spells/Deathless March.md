# Deathless March two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Enchantment]]

**Source** [[Sources/Wake the Dead -4|Wake the Dead #4]] pg. 29

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 20-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You chant a marching song of Yled to empower the undead and terrify the living. Undead creatures within the area gain the [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] condition for 1 minute, which they can use only to Stride.  
 Living creatures in the area must attempt a Will save. This gains the [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Spells/Fear|_fear_]], and [[Traits/Mental|mental]] traits for living creatures.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] 3 and [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]] for 1 round.
