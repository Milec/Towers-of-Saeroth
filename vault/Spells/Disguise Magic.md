# Disguise Magic  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 324

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Adanye (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Adanye]]

**Cast** 1 minute

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 item or spell effect

**Duration** until your next daily preparations

---

You alter how an item's or spell's magical aura appears to effects like [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]]. You can hide the auras entirely, have an item register as a common item of lower level, or make a spell register as a common spell of the same or lower rank. You can Dismiss the spell.

 A caster using _detect magic_ or [[Spells/Read Aura|_read aura_]] of a higher rank than _disguise magic_ can attempt to disbelieve the illusion using the skill matching the tradition of the spell ([[Rules/Skills/Arcana]] for [[Traits/Arcane|arcane]], [[Rules/Skills/Religion]] for [[Traits/Divine|divine]], [[Rules/Skills/Occultism]] for [[Traits/Occult|occult]], or [[Rules/Sidebars/Commanded Animals|Nature]] for [[Traits/Primal|primal]]). Further attempts by the same caster get the same result as the initial check to disbelieve.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** You can Cast this Spell on a creature, disguising all items and spell effects on it.
