**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You scramble a creature's mental faculties and sensory input. The target must attempt a Will saving throw. Regardless of the  result of that save, the target is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes. _Warp mind's_ effects happen instantly, so [[Spells/Dispel Magic|_dispel magic_]] and other effects that [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] spells can't counteract them. However, _wish_ rituals and abilities that can remove non-magical effects can still counteract the effects.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target spends the first action on its next turn with the [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]] condition.  

**Failure** The target is confused for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is confused permanently.
