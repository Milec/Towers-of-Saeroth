# Keen Smell two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 59

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your nose becomes more sensitive to the scents of the wild, the better for you to track your quarry. For the spell’s duration, you gain [[Rules/Scent (Player Core)|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet. Your [[Actions/Hunt Prey (Player Core)|hunted prey]] can’t be [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]] by you as long as it’s within your scent range.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** Your scent range increases to 60 feet.
