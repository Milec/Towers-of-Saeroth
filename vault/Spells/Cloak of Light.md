# Cloak of Light two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -172- Secrets of the Temple City|Pathfinder #172: Secrets of the Temple City]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** sustained for up to 5 rounds

---

You surround yourself in holy light that restores the living and rebuffs undead. You glow with bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light to the next 30 feet. Living creatures that begin their turn adjacent to you recover 2d4 Hit Points. [[Traits/Undead]] creatures that begin their turn adjacent to you take 4d4 positive damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The healing increases by 1d4, and the damage to undead increases by 2d4.
