# Retrieving Hook single action  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 160

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 15 feet

**Target** 1 creature or an unattended object of 2 Bulk or less

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You conjure a magical hook and rope that hurtles toward the target and pulls them to you. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. If the target is a willing ally or unattended object, they automatically fail the save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is pulled 5 feet toward you.  

**Failure** The target is pulled to the nearest empty space adjacent to you.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The range increases to 30 feet.
