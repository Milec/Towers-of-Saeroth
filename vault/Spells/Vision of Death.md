# Vision of Death two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 366

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Achaekek (Divine Mysteries)|Achaekek]], [[Setting/Deities/Iggeret]], [[Setting/Deities/Kalekot (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Kalekot]], [[Setting/Deities/Norgorber (Divine Mysteries)|Norgorber]], [[Setting/Deities/Pharasma (Divine Mysteries)|Pharasma]], [[Setting/Deities/Reymenda]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You force the target to see a vision of its own death. It takes 8d6 mental damage with a Will save. If the target is reduced to 0 HP by this spell, its vision becomes reality and kills it instantly.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]].  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is frightened 4, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing|fleeing]] for as long as it's frightened.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
