# Element Embodied two actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 134

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Drawing upon the elemental plane, you transform into a spectacular elemental. You can choose between an air elemental, earth elemental, fire elemental, metal elemental, water elemental, or wood elemental. Your battle form is Gargantuan, and you must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Elemental (Player Core)|elemental]] trait and the trait for the element you choose. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 25 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 30 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]] with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +34, and your damage bonus is +15. These attacks are Dexterity based if you choose an air, fire, or metal elemental, or Strength based if you choose an earth, water, or wood elemental. (This distinction doesn't change the statistics but matters for the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] conditions, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] modifier of +36 for air, fire, or metal elemental, or [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +36 for earth, water, or wood elemental, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of elemental you choose:

- **Air Elemental** Speed [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 80 feet; **High Winds** ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]]) 30 feet. Air within the emanation is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for flying creatures that don’t have the air trait; **Swiftness** Your movement doesn’t trigger reactions; **Melee** single action gust ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Push (Rage of Elements)|Push]] 10 feet; **Ranged** single action lightning lash ([[Traits/Range|range increment 80 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10 electricity.
- **Earth Elemental** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Burrow Speed (Player Core)|burrow]] 20 feet; resistance 5 to physical; **Spike Stones** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) 30 feet. The area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and hazardous terrain. A creature without the earth trait that moves on the ground in the area takes 5 piercing damage for every square of that area it moves into; **Rocky Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action rock ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]]), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
- **Fire Elemental** Speed 50 feet; [[Rules/Immunity (Player Core)|immunity]] to fire; [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 10 to cold; **Intense Heat** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 5d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** single action tendril ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10 fire plus 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire]]; **Ranged** single action fire mote ([[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 3d8 fire.
- **Metal Elemental** Speed 40 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; resistance 10 to electricity; **Arcing Electricity** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]]) 30 feet. A creature that enters the aura or starts its turn in the aura takes 2d12 electricity damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC; a creature made of metal, with the metal trait, or wearing metal armor takes a –1 circumstance penalty on this save. A creature can take damage from the aura only once per round; **Melee** single action blade ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile S]]), **Damage** 3d12 piercing; **Ranged** single action metal shard ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile S]]), **Damage** 3d8 piercing.
- **Water Elemental** Speed 40 feet, [[Rules/Swim Speed (Player Core)|swim]] 80 feet; resistance 10 to fire; **Vortex** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) 30 feet. Water within the aura that is part of the same body of water you occupy is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for Swimming creatures that don’t have the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait; **Melee** single action wave ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d12 bludgeoning plus [[Bestiary/Abilities/Push (Rage of Elements)|Push]] or Pull 10 feet; **Ranged** single action water spout ([[Traits/Range|range increment 60 feet]]), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
- **Wood Elemental** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb]] 35 feet; [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to physical; [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 10 to [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Axe|axes]]; **Lush Growth** ([[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|aura]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) 30 feet. Ground in the area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]], and any time a creature in the area regains HP from a [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect, the aura grants a +5 status bonus to the healing; **Woody Toughness** You gain 40 temporary Hit Points when you choose this form instead of 30; **Melee** single action branch ([[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 25 feet]]), **Damage** 3d10 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action seed ([[Traits/Range|range increment 40 feet]]), **Damage** 3d8 bludgeoning.
