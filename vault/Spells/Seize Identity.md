# Seize Identity two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Mythic]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 156

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 intelligent creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

With a gleeful cackle, you seize an individual's voice and swallow it. For the duration, you look and sound like the target, and the target can't speak. The effects depend on the result of the target's Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** You take on the target’s appearance, with the same effects of a 3rd-rank [[Spells/Illusory Disguise (Player Core)|_illusory disguise_]] spell, and the target can’t speak, with the same effects of a 2nd-rank [[Spells/Silence (Player Core)|_silence_]] spell.  

**Failure** As success, but the target also takes 4d6 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage from the transformation.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the target also takes 8d6 mental damage from the transformation.

The first time each round you [[Actions/Sustain]] this spell after you cast this spell, the target ages rapidly, taking 2d8 void> damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save). On a failure, the target also becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 or increases the value of its enfeebled condition by 1.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The mental damage increases by 4d6, and the void damage increases by 1d8.
