# Unfolding Wind Rush single action  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/World Guide]] pg. 83

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

You travel on a rushing wind. Move two times: two Strides, two Steps, or one Stride and one Step (in either order). You can Stride or Step into the air during this movement, moving upward at a 45-degree angle at most; if you end your movement in the air, you fall after taking one action or when your turn ends, whichever comes first. A [[Spells/Wall of Wind|_wall of wind_]] appears in all the spaces you left during this movement, lasting for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The _wall of wind_ lasts 3 rounds.
