You fire a torrent of jagged bone shards from your hands, dealing 2d10 piercing damage and 1 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] to all creatures in the area.

**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 1d10 and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
