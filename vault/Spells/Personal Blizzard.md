# Personal Blizzard single action  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 239

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Snow]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You summon an isolated blizzard of obscuring, scouring ice to follow your target. The target attempts a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes 1d6 cold damage, and the spell ends.  

**Failure** The target takes 1d6 cold damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent cold damage]]. (The persistent damage automatically ends when the spell ends.) It is [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to other creatures, and other creatures are concealed to it.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but both the cold damage and the persistent cold damage increase to 2d6.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The cold damage and persistent cold damage increase by 1 (2 on a critical failure).
