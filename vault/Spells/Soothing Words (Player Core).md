# Soothing Words single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 374

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Family (Player Core)|Family]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 ally

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You attempt to calm the target by uttering soothing words in a calm and even tone. The target gains a +1 status bonus to Will saving throws. This bonus increases to +2 against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects.

 In addition, when you Cast this Spell, you can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] one emotion effect on the target.

---
**Heightened (5th)** The bonus to saves increases to +2, or +3 against emotion effects.
