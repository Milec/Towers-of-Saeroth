# Disappearance two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Subtle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 324

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Veiled Voice)]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 target

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You shroud a creature from others' senses. The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]], but not merely to vision. The invisibility granted by _disappearance_ applies to all precise senses an observer might have. It's still possible for a creature to find the target by [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seeking]] using various senses, looking for disturbed dust, hearing gaps in the sound spectrum, or finding some other way to discover the presence of a creature that is otherwise undetectable.
