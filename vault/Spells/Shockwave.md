# Shockwave two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 110

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ayrzul (Divine Mysteries)|Ayrzul]], [[Setting/Deities/Mhar]], [[Setting/Deities/Mugura and Nrithu]], [[Setting/Deities/Orgesh]], [[Setting/Deities/Ragdya (Divine Mysteries)|Ragdya]], [[Setting/Deities/Yamatsumi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Yamatsumi]], [[Setting/Deities/Zipacna]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

You create a wave of energy that ripples through the earth. Terrestrial creatures in the affected area must attempt a Reflex save to avoid stumbling as the shockwave shakes the ground.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** As failure, plus the creature takes 1d6 damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The area increases by 5 feet (to a 20-foot cone at 2nd level, and so on).
