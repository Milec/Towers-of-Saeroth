# Arrow Salvo two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 196

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Neith]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You conjure an immense wooden bow that launches a salvo of massive arrows at all nearby enemies. All creatures in the affected area take 8d10 piercing damage, with a basic Reflex save. On a critical failure, the target is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] from the force of the impact.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 1d10.
