# Join Pasts single action  *Cantrip 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 74

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 2 willing creatures

**Duration** 1 round

---

With touches to the targets’ foreheads, you bring them into mental communion. The targets can share thoughts and experiences, but not words. When one target attempts to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], the other can [[Actions/Aid]] the first target’s skill check, using any [[Rules/Sidebars/Common Lore Subcategories|Lore]] skill (even if that Lore wouldn’t normally apply) without having made any preparations to Aid.

---
**Heightened (+1)** You can touch one additional target to include them in the spell’s effects. Multiple targets can attempt checks to Aid the target who is attempting to Recall Knowledge.
