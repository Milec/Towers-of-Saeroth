# Eidolon's Wrath two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Eidolon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Summoner]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 144

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 20-foot emanation around your eidolon

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

Your eidolon releases a powerful energy attack that deals 5d6 damage of the type you chose when you took the [[Feats/Eidolon's Wrath]] feat, with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
