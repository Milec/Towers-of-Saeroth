# Advanced Scurvy two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gatewalkers (Hardcover)]] pg. 233

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Your touch afflicts the target with an advanced form of scurvy, a mariner’s disease stemming from improper nutrition. The symptoms of scurvy include fatigue, joint pain, loose teeth, and internal hemorrhaging.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 minute.  
**Failure** The target is afflicted with advanced scurvy at stage 1.  
**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with advanced scurvy at stage 2.

**Advanced Scurvy** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) **Level** 4; for 1 day after eating fresh fruit, a creature gains a +2 circumstance bonus to their next saving throw against this affliction; **Stage 1** enfeebled 1 and the damage dealt by [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed]] effects is increased by 1 (1 day); **Stage 2** enfeebled 1, [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]], and the damage dealt by persistent bleed effects is increased by 1d4 (2 days); **Stage 3** enfeebled 2, fatigued, and the damage dealt by persistent bleed effects is increased by 1d6 (4 days)
