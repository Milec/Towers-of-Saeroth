# Elemental Sheath two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Archetype]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 147

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You coat yourself in nature’s power. Choose [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]]; the spell gains that trait. You become completely covered by armor formed from that element, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus to AC and [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to two types of damage based on the element you choose.

**Air** bludgeoning and [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]; **Earth** bludgeoning and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]; **Fire** fire and slashing; **Metal** acid and slashing; **Water** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] and piercing; **Wood** piercing and [[Traits/Void|void]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
