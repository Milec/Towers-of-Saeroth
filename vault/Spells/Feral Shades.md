# Feral Shades two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 105

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Jezelda]], [[Setting/Deities/Orcus]], [[Setting/Deities/The Pale Horse]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

A wave of gray mist formed of negative energy spills out from your outstretched hand, briefly taking the shape of a pack of predatory animals that set viciously on your foes. You deal 2d4 slashing damage and 2d4 negative damage to creatures in the area. The shades set upon the vulnerable with greater ferocity: [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] creatures or those taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] take a –2 status penalty to the save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The slashing damage and the negative damage each increase by 1d4.
