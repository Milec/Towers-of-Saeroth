# Friends to Foes two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 140

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You draw upon your past experiences of betrayal to plant thoughts of a friend turning on them in a creature's mind. The target takes 6d6 mental damage with a Will save and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]]. The source of its fear is its allies, and it can't reduce its frightened condition while it's within 10 feet of an ally.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is frightened 1.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is frightened 2. It can't move closer to its allies while frightened.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is frightened 2, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]] until it's no longer frightened.

---
**Heightened (7th)** You can target up to five creatures.
