# Waking Dream two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 207

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You cloud the target's mind with vivid, dreamlike imagery, so they have difficulty distinguishing real threats from imagined ones.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is distracted by the visions and treats all other creatures as [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] for 1 round, and you can't Sustain the Spell to increase the duration.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]]. In addition to the creatures present, the target might also attack wildly at thin air, attempting to fend off the dreams. It can attempt a new save at the end of each of its turns to end the confusion. Even if the confused effect ends early, the target treats all other creatures as concealed for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target gets no save to end the effect early.
