# Spout two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 252

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** a 5-foot cube

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

Water blasts upward, coming out of the ground, rising from a pool, or even manifesting from thin air. Any creatures in the area take 2d4 bludgeoning damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex saving throw. A creature that critically fails its save is disoriented by the explosion of water, becoming off-guard until the end of your next turn.

 You can change this spell's area to a 5-foot burst, provided you center the burst in a body of water. This body of water can be as small as a pond or creek, but not as small as a puddle or bathtub.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
