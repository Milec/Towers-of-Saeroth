# Touch of the Moon single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 377

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Moon (Player Core)|Moon]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

When you touch the target, a symbol of the moon appears on its forehead, glowing with soft moonlight. The target glows with [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]] in a 20-foot radius. It also gets a benefit based on a phase of the moon, starting with the new moon and changing to the next phase at the end of each of its turns.

- **New Moon** The target receives no benefit.
- **Waxing Moon** The target gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls and a +4 status bonus to damage rolls.
- **Full Moon** The target gains a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, AC, and saves, and a +4 status bonus to damage.
- **Waning Moon** The target gains a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws. After this phase, return to the new moon.
