# Burning Trail two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 127

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your companion or eidolon

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target's feet glow with a magical flame that lingers and grows on the ground where they walk, creating a line of blazing fire as they move. The first time the target [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]] during the spell's duration, they leave behind a trail made of magical fire that fills up each space they occupied during that Stride. This fire is 5 feet high. Creatures on either side of the trail are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] from creatures on the opposite side. Any creature that enters or starts its turn in the trail's area takes 2d10 fire damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The fire damage increases by 1d10.
