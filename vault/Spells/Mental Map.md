# Mental Map two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 32

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 24 hours

---

You glean detailed information about a specific place visited by a creature directly from its mind, unless it fends you off with a Will save. The target can choose to fail the save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** You gain critical information about the place in question, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks while in the location and any skill checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about the location for the next 24 hours.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but you can erase all knowledge of the location from the target for 24 hours if desired.
