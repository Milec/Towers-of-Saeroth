# Bursting Bloom two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Plant]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 88

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You cause a rose bush to sprout from the chest of a foe, bursting out of its body and wrapping it in thorned vines. The target takes 6d6 piercing damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] and must attempt a Fortitude save. When the target is no longer taking persistent bleed damage and is no longer [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered|encumbered]], the rose bush withers away to nothing.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and no persistent damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and persistent damage and is encumbered for 4 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and persistent damage and is encumbered for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 3d6, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d6.
