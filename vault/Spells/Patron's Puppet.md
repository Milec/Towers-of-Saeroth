# Patron's Puppet free action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 385

**Trigger** Your turn begins

---

At your unspoken plea, your patron temporarily assumes control over your familiar. You Command your familiar, allowing it to take its normal actions this turn. Your Command does not have the [[Traits/Auditory|auditory]] or [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=683) traits; your patron simply moves its agent directly.
