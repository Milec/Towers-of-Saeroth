# Musical Accompaniment two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 154

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You’re surrounded by orchestral music that shifts and changes to match your behavior. This music provides a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks. At the GM’s discretion, it provides this bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]], [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]], and [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks as the music changes to support you in social situations, though some creatures are unaffected by such obvious attempts to use music to elicit specific emotions. This music moves with you and has a maximum volume equal to four humans shouting. You take a –4 circumstance penalty to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks while the music is playing. You can’t control the exact music this spell creates, and the music doesn’t create intelligible words or singing. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] this spell.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** The duration increases to 10 minutes.
