# Stoke the Heart single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 239

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Fervor]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Intense fervor fills the target creature, empowering their blows. The target gains a +2 status bonus to damage rolls. Once this spell ends, the target becomes temporarily immune for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The status bonus to damage increases by 1.
