# Tireless Worker two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 271, [[Sources/Pathfinder -148- Fires of the Haunted City|Pathfinder #148: Fires of the Haunted City]] pg. 65

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Toil (Divine Mysteries)|Toil]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You suppress your choice of one of the following conditions that’s affecting the target: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Encumbered (Player Core)|encumbered]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued (Player Core)|fatigued]]. The duration of the condition doesn’t elapse while it is suppressed, so if you don’t remove the effect that caused the condition, the condition returns after the spell’s duration expires.
