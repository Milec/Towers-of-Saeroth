# Clawsong two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 77

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature with an  unarmed  claw Strike

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You sing a fierce prayer, low and growling, and awaken the ancient spirit of a Terwa beast to guide and strengthen the target's unarmed blows. The target's claw attack gains the [[Traits/Versatile|versatile (piercing)]] trait, and deals 1d6 slashing damage. If the target's claw attack already deals 1d6 slashing damage, it deals 1d8 instead. If its claw attack already had the versatile (piercing) trait, it gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly 1d8]] trait.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The spell can affect up to 6 willing creatures with unarmed claw Strikes.
