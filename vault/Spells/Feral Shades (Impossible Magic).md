# Feral Shades two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 137

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Jezelda]], [[Setting/Deities/Orcus]], [[Setting/Deities/The Pale Horse]]

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

A wave of gray mist formed of energy from the Void spills out from your outstretched hand, briefly taking the shape of a pack of predatory animals that set viciously on your foes. You deal 2d4 slashing damage and 2d4 void damage to creatures in the area, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. The shades set upon the vulnerable with greater ferocity: prone creatures or those taking [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] take a –2 status penalty to the save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The slashing damage and the void damage each increase by 1d4.
