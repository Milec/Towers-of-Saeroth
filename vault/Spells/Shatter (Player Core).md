# Shatter two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 356

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ahriman (Monster Core 2)|Ahriman]], [[Setting/Deities/Hadregash]], [[Setting/Deities/Zyphus (Divine Mysteries)|Zyphus]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 unattended object

---

A high-frequency sonic attack shatters a nearby object. You deal 2d10 sonic damage to the object, ignoring the object's Hardness if it is 4 or lower.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10, and the Hardness the spell ignores increases by 2.
