# Hollow Heart two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 142

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You stoke the target's ambition beyond reason, until it believes no one is capable of helping it, or that others aren't to be trusted. The target attempts a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target treats no other creature as an ally for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target treats no other creature as an ally for 3 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1 as it realizes it can't rely on anyone but itself.
