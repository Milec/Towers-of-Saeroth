**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Shadow]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Ah Pook]], [[Setting/Deities/Aonaurious]], [[Setting/Deities/Apep]], [[Setting/Deities/Charon (Divine Mysteries)|Charon]], [[Setting/Deities/Cthulhu]], [[Setting/Deities/Dajermube (Divine Mysteries)|Dajermube]], [[Setting/Deities/Fumeiyoshi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Fumeiyoshi]], [[Setting/Deities/Iggeret]], [[Setting/Deities/Neshen]], [[Setting/Deities/Nhimbaloth (Divine Mysteries)|Nhimbaloth]], [[Setting/Deities/Nulgreth]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (Haunter in the Dark) (Monster Core 2)|Nyarlathotep (Haunter in the Dark)]], [[Setting/Deities/Orcus]], [[Setting/Deities/Treerazer (Divine Mysteries)|Treerazer]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot line

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

Tendrils of darkness curl out from your fingertips and race through the air. You deal 2d4 negative damage and 1 persistent bleed damage to living creatures in the line. Each living creature in the line must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half the negative damage and no persistent bleed damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double negative damage and double persistent bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The negative damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
