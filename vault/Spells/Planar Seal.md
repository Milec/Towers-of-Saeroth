# Planar Seal two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 350

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 60-foot burst

**Duration** until your next daily preparations

---

You create a visible magical barrier that attempts to counteract [[Traits/Teleportation|teleportation]] effects and planar travel into or out of the area, including items that allow access to [[Traits/Extradimensional|extradimensional]] spaces. _Planar seal_ tries to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any attempt to [[Traits/Summon|summon]] a creature into the area but doesn't stop the creature from departing when the summoning ends.
