# Object Memory two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 271

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Vigil (Divine Mysteries)|Vigil]]

**Target** 1 weapon or tool

**Duration** 1 minute

---

By touching an object, you draw forth the experience of those who created and used it. If you touch a weapon, your proficiency rank with that weapon temporarily increases to trained (if it is not already trained or better). If you touch a [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|tool]], your proficiency rank with the next skill check you attempt with that tool (such as a [[Rules/Skills/Crafting (Player Core)|Crafting]] check with a blacksmith’s hammer) within the spell’s duration temporarily increases to trained (if it is not already trained or better). This spell has no benefit for activities that take longer than a minute to complete.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Your proficiency rank increases to expert instead of trained.
