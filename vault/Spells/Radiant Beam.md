# Radiant Beam two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Knights of Lastwall]] pg. 94

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 120-foot line

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You fire a beam of blinding light from your outstretched hands. The beam deals 5d10 good damage. On a failed saving throw, a creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round, or dazzled for 4 rounds on a critical failure.

 If the light passes through an area of magical [[Traits/Darkness|darkness]] or targets a creature affected by magical darkness, radiant beam attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the darkness. If you need to determine whether the light passes through an area of darkness, draw a line between yourself and the target.

---
**Heightened (+1)**  The damage increases by 1d10.
