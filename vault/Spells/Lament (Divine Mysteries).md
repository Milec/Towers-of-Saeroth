# Lament two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 269

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Sorrow (Divine Mysteries)|Sorrow]]

**Range** 30-foot  [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] will

---

You let out your negative emotions in a guttural wail that shakes your enemies’ hearts and deals 1d8 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage (basic Will save) to each creature in the area. If you have any harmful conditions imposed by [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effects, lament deals additional mental damage equal to the [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteract]] rank of the highest-level effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
