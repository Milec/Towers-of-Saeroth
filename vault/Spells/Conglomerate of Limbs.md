# Conglomerate of Limbs single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Thrall]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 181

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure forth a humongous thrall consisting solely of undead severed limbs. You create a Huge thrall within range. This thrall has 40 Hit Points and a Speed of 25 feet. When you use _thrall charge_ to order this thrall to make a [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], it makes two melee unarmed Strikes instead of one, each against a different creature within 10 feet. Your multiple attack penalty doesn’t increase until after the thrall makes both Strikes. A creature hit by one of these Strikes must also succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] by the thrall for 1 round (or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] on a critical failure) or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]]. Attempts to Escape don’t automatically succeed, and the Escape DC is your spell DC.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The thrall’s Hit Points increase by 10.
