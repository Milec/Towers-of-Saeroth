# Clinging Shadows Stance single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Monk]], [[Traits/Shadow]], [[Traits/Stance]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 229

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Duration** until you leave the stance

---

You adopt the stance of clinging shadows. You can make shadow grasp Strikes. These deal 1d4 negative damage; are in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] group; and have the [[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Grapple|grapple]], [[Traits/Reach|reach]], and [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] traits.

While in _clinging shadows stance_, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Grapple]], and to the DC for creatures to [[Actions/Escape]] from you.
