# Plant Form two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 350

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Cyth-V’sug]], [[Setting/Deities/Furcas]], [[Setting/Deities/Halcamora (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Halcamora]], [[Setting/Deities/Immonhiel (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Immonhiel]], [[Setting/Deities/Shumunue (Divine Mysteries)|Shumunue]], [[Setting/Deities/The Green Mother (Divine Mysteries)|The Green Mother]], [[Setting/Deities/Vermilion Mother]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Taking inspiration from verdant creatures, you transform into a Large plant battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose a listed battle form. You can substitute a similar specific plant to turn into (such as a pitcher plant instead of a flytrap), but this has no effect on the form's Size or statistics. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 19 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 12 temporary Hit Points.
- Resistance 10 to [[Traits/Poison|poison]].
- [[Rules/Low-Light Vision|Low-light vision]].
- One or more [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +17, and your damage bonus is +11. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +19, unless your own is higher.

 You gain specific abilities based on the plant you choose:

- **Arboreal** Speed 30 feet; Melee single action branch ([[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 2d10 bludgeoning; Melee single action foot, **Damage** 2d8 bludgeoning; you can speak in this form.
- **Flytrap** Speed 15 feet; resistance 10 to [[Traits/Acid|acid]]; **Melee** single action leaf ([[Traits/Reach|reach 10 feet]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing, and you can spend an action after a hit to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab]] the target.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Your battle form is Huge, and the reach of your attacks increases by 5 feet. You instead gain AC = 22 + your level, 24 temporary HP, attack modifier +21, damage bonus +16, and Athletics +22.
