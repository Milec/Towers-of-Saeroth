# Patron's Protector three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 156

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your familiar

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You channel your magic into your familiar, filling its body with dreadful energy, turning them into a powerful protective force. You can use [[Actions/Command an Animal (Player Core)|Command an Animal]] on the target as part of Casting or Sustaining this spell. Your familiar's AC becomes 28; they become large, and they gain 20 temporary Hit Points. If your familiar is reduced to 0 Hit Points while under the effect of this spell, the spell immediately ends, and your familiar is instead reduced to 1 Hit Point. Once per turn your familiar can use the Body Check or Protector's Shove action.  

**Body Check** single action Your familiar uses its new size and presence to press against an adjacent creature, dealing, 4d10 bludgeoning damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save). On a failed save, the target is also knocked back 10 feet (20 feet on a critical failure).  

**Protector's Shove** single action Your familiar shoves an ally out of danger. Your familiar pushes an adjacent, willing ally, pushing them up to 20 feet away and granting them a +2 status bonus to AC for 1 round. This is [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]].

---
**Heightened (9th)** The familiar’s AC become 36, they gain 30 temporary Hit Points, and the damage increases by 2d10.
