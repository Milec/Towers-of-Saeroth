# Gleaming Pet two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 141

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your companion or eidolon

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target glows intensely with an otherworldly light, blinding any who approach. Any creature that begins its turn adjacent to the target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is immune for 10 minutes.  

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.
