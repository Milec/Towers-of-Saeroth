# Steel Fortifications three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 90

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You conjure several metal structures that consist of angled beams attached to one another at a central point, which can help block attacks. You create up to three Large structures within range in unoccupied squares; each fortification is 8 feet long, 8 feet wide, and 8 feet tall. There must be enough room for a fortification in order to conjure it. A single fortification has an AC of 10 and is immune to critical hits and precision damage. It has [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]] 9, 60 Hit Points, and a BT of 30. Once a fortification is [[Rules/Conditions/Broken (Player Core)|broken]], it crumbles into sharp metal bits. Any creature adjacent to or sharing a space with a fortification when it is broken must attempt a Reflex save or take 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].

Creatures can pass through the spaces of a fortification, though Medium and larger creatures treat these spaces as [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. A Small or smaller creature can occupy the same spaces as a fortification; doing so grants them standard cover. Similarly, a [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]] Medium creature can occupy the same spaces as a fortification, gaining standard cover in the process. A Medium or smaller creature can climb a fortification with a successful DC 15 [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] check.
