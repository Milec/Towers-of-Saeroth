# Inner Upheaval single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 256

---

You focus your qi into devastating magical attacks. Make an unarmed Strike or [[Actions/Flurry of Blows (Player Core 2)|Flurry of Blows]] (this doesn't change the limit on using only one [[Traits/Flourish (Player Core)|flourish]] per turn). You gain a +1 status bonus to your attack rolls with the Strikes, and the Strikes deal 1d6 extra damage. This damage can be any of the following types of your choice, chosen each time you Strike: force, spirit, vitality, or void.

---
**Heightened (+4)** The extra damage increases by 1d6.
