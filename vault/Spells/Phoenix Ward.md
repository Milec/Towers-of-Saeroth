# Phoenix Ward two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 120

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You raise a fiery shield around yourself, gaining resistance 10 to fire. If you take fire damage that would reduce you to 0 Hit Points, the _phoenix ward_ absorbs any damage beyond the amount that would bring you to 1 Hit Point. The ward then heals you in a brilliant display of flames in the shape of a [[Bestiary/Phoenix|phoenix's]] wings and envelop you in light. You regain Hit Points equal to 4d8 + the absorbed damage; this is a [[Traits/Healing|healing]] [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] effect. The _phoenix ward_ then ends, and you can't cast it again for 24 hours.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The healing increases by 1d8.
