# Blast of the Bellows two actions  *Spell 2*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Sky King's Tomb

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -195- Heavy is the Crown|Pathfinder #195: Heavy is the Crown]] pg. 81

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a churning eddy of warm air that makes it difficult to fly within the area. This area is difficult terrain for flying creatures. Each time a flying creature enters the area or ends its turn there, it must attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Maneuver in Flight]].

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature descends 30 feet.  

**Critical Failure** The creature descends 60 feet.

---
**Heightened (4th)** The duration increases to 10 minutes
