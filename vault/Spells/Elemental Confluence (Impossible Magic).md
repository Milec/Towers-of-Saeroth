# Elemental Confluence three actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|Water]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 135

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Valani]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You conjure six types of elementals who combine forces to form a chaotic storm. The confluence has AC 28, 120 Hit Points, weakness 10 to area effects, and immunity to bleed and [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]]. Creatures can move through the confluence; creatures within the confluence are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]], and all creatures outside the confluence are concealed to creatures within it. When you Cast or Sustain this Spell, you can move the confluence up to 20 feet and choose up to two different types of elementals to act.

- **Air** ([[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]]) Bolts of electricity burst from the confluence, dealing 4d4 electricity damage to creatures within 20 feet of the confluence, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. This doesn’t affect creatures that are completely inside of the confluence.
- **Earth** ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) The confluence gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC and resistance 10 to all physical damage (except adamantine) for 1 round.
- **Fire** ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) Flames roar through the confluence, dealing 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] to those partially or entirely inside the confluence.
- **Metal** ([[Traits/Metal|metal]]) The elemental flings shards of metal through the confluence, dealing 2d6 slashing damage to those partially or entirely inside the confluence with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails also takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].
- **Water** ([[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]], [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]) The elemental expels a powerful jet of water. Attempt a ranged spell attack against a target inside or within 60 feet of the confluence, using your spell attack modifier. This attack ignores concealment granted by the confluence, and the elemental gets a +2 circumstance bonus on the spell attack roll against a target inside the confluence. The water deals 4d6 bludgeoning damage on a hit (doubled on a critical hit). This attack doesn’t count toward your multiple attack penalty.
- **Wood** ([[Traits/Wood|wood]]) Roots and stumps grow, making ground in the area [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] for 1 round.
