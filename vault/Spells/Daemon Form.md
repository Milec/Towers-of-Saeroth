# Daemon Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 98

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You infuse yourself with the corrupting death and pure malevolence of [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon]], transforming into a Large daemon battle form. You must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. When you cast this spell you choose either lacridaemon, [[Bestiary/Leukodaemon (Monster Core)|leukodaemon]], [[Bestiary/Meladaemon|meladaemon]], or [[Bestiary/Venedaemon|venedaemon]]. The battle form is Medium if you choose lacridaemon or venedaemon. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Daemon|daemon]] and [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 21 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 15 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- A +2 status bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Death|death]] effects.
- [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|Resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Poison|poison]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +21, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of Daemon you choose:

- **Lacridaemon** Speed 25 feet, fly 40 feet; resistance 10 to cold; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8+12 piercing plus 1d6 cold and 1d4 poison; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d6+12 slashing plus 1d6 cold.
- **[[Bestiary/Leukodaemon (Monster Core)|Leukodaemon]]** Speed 25 feet, fly 40 feet; +2 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|diseases]]; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d10+12 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 slashing; **Ranged** single action composite longbow ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly 1d10]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Propulsive (Player Core)|propulsive]], [[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 100 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Volley (Player Core)|volley]]), **Damage** 2d8+12 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Meladaemon]]** Speed 25 feet, fly 40 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d10+12 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Void|void]]), **Damage** 1d6+12 slashing plus 1d6 void, and you can spend an action after a hit to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target.
- **[[Bestiary/Venedaemon]]** Speed 25 feet; sense [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] items and active spells within 60 feet as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]]; **Melee** single action tentacle ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 bludgeoning.
