# Fact Check ⤾  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 186

**Trigger** You or an ally within 30 feet attempts a check to Recall Knowledge using a skill in which you’re trained.

---

You use your arcane knowledge to double-check the information learned. You briefly summon a 2-inch-tall illusory avatar of your subconscious onto your shoulder, which takes the form of how you view yourself, and helps out by sorting through your memories for you. Attempt a check using the same skill as the triggering check.

**Critical Success** Your avatar tells you if the information is correct and the correct answer if it’s incorrect. You can ask the GM one follow-up question that the GM answers truthfully.  

**Success** Your avatar tells you if the information is correct and the correct answer if it’s incorrect.  

**Failure** Your avatar tells you if the information is correct.
