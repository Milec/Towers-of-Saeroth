# Summon Deific Herald three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Incarnate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 131

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Requirements** You have a deity.

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

Your faith is strong enough to briefly summon a direct representative of your deity, such as the deity's herald. The herald occupies the space of a Huge creature and has a Speed of 60 feet. The effects of this spell depend on your deity's alignment.

- **Lawful Good**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Good|good]], [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]]) _Aura of Retribution_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Retributive Strike]] reaction; **Depart** _Holy Smite_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Divine Wrath|_divine wrath_]] (choosing good) in a 100-foot emanation.
- **Neutral Good**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Good|good]]) _Aura of Redemption_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Glimpse of Redemption]] reaction; **Depart** _Merciful Healing_ The herald casts the 3-action version of 5th-level [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] with a radius of 100 feet. This spell targets only you and your allies.
- **Chaotic Good**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Good|good]]) _Aura of Liberation_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Liberating Step]] reaction; **Depart** _Radiating Freedom_ The herald affects you and all your allies within 100 feet with [[Spells/Freedom of Movement|_freedom of movement_]]. The duration is reduced to 3 rounds.
- **Lawful Neutral**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Lawful|lawful]]) _Defy Chaos_ You and each ally within 100 feet of the herald gain resistance 20 against chaotic damage while the herald is present; **Depart** _Order's Wrath_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Divine Wrath|_divine wrath_]] (choosing lawful) in a 100-foot emanation.
- **Neutral**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Transmutation|transmutation]]) _Quickening Ripple_ You and each ally within 100 feet of the herald are [[Rules/Sidebars/Gaining and Losing Actions|quickened]] while the herald is present, and can use the additional action to Step, Stride, or Strike; **Depart** _Sapping Beam_ The herald casts 6th-level [[Spells/Enervation|_enervation_]] in a 100-foot line.
- **Chaotic Neutral**: **Arrive** ([[Traits/Abjuration|abjuration]], [[Traits/Chaotic|chaotic]]) _Defy Law_ You and each ally within 100 feet of the herald gain resistance 20 against lawful damage while the herald is present; **Depart** _Chaos Hammer_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Divine Wrath|_divine wrath_]] (choosing chaotic) in a 100-foot emanation.
- **Lawful Evil**: **Arrive** _Aura of Iron_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Iron Command]] reaction; **Depart** _Unholy Blight_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Divine Wrath|_divine wrath_]] (choosing evil) in a 100-foot emanation.
- **Neutral Evil**: **Arrive** _Aura of Selfishness_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Selfish Shield]] reaction; **Depart** _Infinite Despair_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Crushing Despair|_crushing despair_]] in a 100-foot cone.
- **Chaotic Evil**: **Arrive** _Aura of Vengeance_ While within 100 feet of the herald, you and your allies gain the champion's [[Actions/Destructive Vengeance]] reaction; **Depart** _Weapon Hurricane_ The herald casts 5th-level [[Spells/Weapon Storm|_weapon storm_]] in a 100-foot cone, using d12 for the die size.
