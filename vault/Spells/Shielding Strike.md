# Shielding Strike single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Magus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 143

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

As you attack a foe, warding magic transforms your momentum into action and lifts your shield. Make a melee Strike. You can then either [[Actions/Raise a Shield|Raise your Shield]] if you're wielding one or cast [[Spells/Shield|_shield_]] if you have the spell.
