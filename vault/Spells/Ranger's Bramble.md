# Ranger's Bramble two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Ranger]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 235

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** all squares that contain plants in a 5-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You cause plants in the area to entangle your foes, with the effects of [[Spells/Entangle|_entangle_]]. A creature that critically fails the save takes 2d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] in addition to being [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]]. Escaping your bramble doesn't end the bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The bleed damage on a critical failure increases by 1d4.
