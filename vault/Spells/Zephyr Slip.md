# Zephyr Slip ⤾  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 73

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Trigger** A creature enters a space within 5 feet of you.

---

An arresting breeze sweeps you up in an instant and moves you to safety. You [[Actions/Fly]] 15 feet directly away from the triggering creature.

---
**Heightened (6th)** You and one adjacent ally Fly up to 25 feet directly away from the triggering creature.

**Heightened (8th)** You and up to 5 allies within 30 feet Fly up to 25 feet directly away from the triggering creature.
