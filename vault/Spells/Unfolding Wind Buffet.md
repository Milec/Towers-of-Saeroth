# Unfolding Wind Buffet two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 223

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

You surround your unarmed attacks with the speed and force of air. Make three [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strikes against the same target. If at least one was a successful hit, the target must attempt a Fortitude save or be [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] back 5 feet for each normal hit from the buffet, plus 10 feet for each critical hit.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is pushed back half the normal distance.  

**Failure** The target is pushed back the normal distance.  

**Critical Failure** The target is pushed back double the normal distance and then knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
