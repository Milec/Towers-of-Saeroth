# Sun's Fury two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Good]]

**Source** [[Sources/Legends]] pg. 101

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 weapon without an   unholy   rune that is unattended or wielded by you or a willing ally

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target weapon becomes wreathed in a glowing flame. The weapon deals an additional 1d4 fire and 1 good damage on a successful Strike. In addition, the flame causes the weapon to glow as bright as a [[Equipment/Torch|torch]]. You can Dismiss the spell.
