# Fungal Exhalation two actions  *Focus 3*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Wardens of Wildwood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Fungus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -202- Severed at the Root|Pathfinder #202: Severed at the Root]] pg. 72

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Target**

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You exhale toxic mold spores that fill the area. You deal 6d4 [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] damage to creatures in the area. On a failure, a creature is also [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1, or sickened 2 on a critical failure. A creature that doesn’t breathe is immune to this effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d4.
