# Desiccate two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 323

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Tresmalvos]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Target** any number of living creatures

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You pull the moisture from the targets' bodies, dealing 10d10 void damage. Creatures made of water (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Water|water elementals]]) and [[Traits/Plant|plant]] creatures use the outcome for one degree of success worse than the result of their saving throw. Creatures whose bodies contain no significant moisture (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Earth|earth elementals]]) are immune to _desiccate_.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
