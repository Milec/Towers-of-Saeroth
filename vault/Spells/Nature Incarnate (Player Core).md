# Nature Incarnate two actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 346

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The primal power of the world flows through you. You transform into an incarnation of nature, either a green man or a kaiju. Your battle form is Medium for a green man or Gargantuan (30-foot-by-30-foot space) for a kaiju. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Plant|plant]] trait (for a green man) or the [[Traits/Beast|beast]] trait (for a kaiju). You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose:

- AC = 25 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.
- 30 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +34, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +36, unless your own is higher.

 You gain specific abilities based on the incarnation you choose:

- **Green Man** Speed 40 feet, climb 40 feet; **Melee** single action vines ([[Traits/Reach|reach 30 feet]], [[Traits/Versatile|versatile P]]), **Damage** 6d8+12 bludgeoning; **Ranged** single action thorns ([[Traits/Range|range 100 feet]]), **Damage** 6d6+6 piercing; **Green Caress** ([[Traits/Aura|aura]], [[Traits/Primal|primal]]) 60 feet. Enemies other than plants must succeed at a Fortitude save against your spell DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy 1]] for 1 round (clumsy 2 on a critical failure).
- **Kaiju** Speed 50 feet; resistance 5 to physical damage; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Reach|reach 30 feet]]), **Damage** 6d10+10 piercing; **Melee** single action claws ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 30 feet]]), **Damage** 6d8+8 slashing; **Melee** single action foot ([[Traits/Agile|agile]], [[Traits/Reach|reach 15 feet]]), **Damage** 6d6+10 bludgeoning; **Unstoppable** You are immune to being [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] and ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain]]; **Trample** three actions You move up to double your Speed and move through the spaces of Huge or smaller creatures, trampling each creature whose space you enter. A trampled creature takes foot damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save against your spell DC.
