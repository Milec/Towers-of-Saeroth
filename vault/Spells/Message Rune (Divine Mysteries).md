# Message Rune  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 258

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Cormigus]], [[Setting/Deities/Lissala (deity-565)|Lissala]], [[Setting/Deities/Mephistopheles (Divine Mysteries)|Mephistopheles]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos) (Divine Mysteries)|Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos)]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Veiled Voice)]], [[Setting/Deities/Pavnuri]], [[Setting/Deities/Pillars of Knowledge]], [[Setting/Deities/Thoth (Divine Mysteries)|Thoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Ydajisk (Divine Mysteries)|Ydajisk]]

**Cast** 5 minutes

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 flat unattended surface or a non-magical object of light Bulk

**Duration** 1 day

---

You record a message up to 5 minutes long and inscribe a special rune on any flat unattended surface or small object within reach. The nature of the rune’s appearance is up to you, but it’s visible to everyone, and it must be no smaller than 2 inches in diameter. You also specify a trigger that creatures must meet to [[Rules/Setting Triggers (Player Core)|activate the rune]]. For the duration of the spell, creatures that meet the criteria of the trigger can touch the rune to hear the recorded message in their head as though you were speaking to them telepathically. You know when someone is listening to the message, but you don’t know who’s listening to it. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The duration increases for every 2 ranks, becoming 1 week, 1 month, 1 year, or unlimited respectively.
