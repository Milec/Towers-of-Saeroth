# Thundering Dominance two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 167

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your companion or eidolon

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You shroud the target in a powerful predatory aura and amplify its vocalizations. It gains a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks. Once during this spell’s duration, it can take the Thundering Roar action.  
**Thundering Roar** single action ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|fear]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]]) The target unleashes a powerful vocalization. Each enemy in a 10-foot emanation takes 4d8 sonic damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will save against your spell DC. A creature that fails its Will save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The status bonus to Intimidation checks increases by 1 (to a maximum of a +4 status bonus for an 8th-rank spell), and the damage from Thundering Roar increases by 2d8.
