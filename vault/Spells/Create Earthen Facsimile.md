# Create Earthen Facsimile two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 130

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You hold a clump of soil, and it morphs into a small figurine in your hand. The soil transforms and hardens into a simple form of your choosing. While it does hold its shape if undisturbed, the figurine is brittle and will crumble under pressure or turn back to mush at the end of the spell's duration.
