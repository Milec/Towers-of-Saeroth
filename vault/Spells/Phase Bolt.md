# Phase Bolt two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

---

You point your finger and project a bolt of magical energy that stutters in and out of phase until it reaches the target. Make a ranged spell attack roll against your target's AC; if the target has any circumstance bonuses to AC (such as from a shield or [[Rules/Cover|cover]]), reduce that bonus by 2 for this attack. On a success, you deal 3d4 piercing damage. On a critical success, the target takes double damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The bolt's damage increases by 1d4.
