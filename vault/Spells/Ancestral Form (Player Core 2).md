# Ancestral Form two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 258

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ancestors (Player Core 2)|Ancestors]]

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You take the form of your ancestral spirits, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|invisible]] and quasi-corporeal. You gain resistance 10 to all damage (except [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]], and damage from Strikes with the [[Equipment/Ghost Touch (GM Core)|_ghost touch_]] property rune), and this resistance is doubled against non-[[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]] damage or Strikes that includes precision damage. You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed; when [[Actions/Flamethrower|Flying]], moving upward isn't [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] for you. You can't move through solid objects, but you can float through tiny gaps. You can't Cast Spells (but you can Sustain spells), activate items, or use actions that have the [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|attack]] or [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] trait.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
