# Time Beacon single action  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archives (Remastered)]] pg. 183, [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 260

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Duration** until the end of your turn

---

You create a beacon in time to return to it if things go wrong. You can cast _time beacon_ only on your turn. Keep careful track of everything that happens this turn after you cast _time beacon_. At the end of your turn, you can choose to rewind time back to just after you cast _time beacon_, removing all effects of your turn since then. Curses, traps, and other harmful effects that happen during your turn might prevent you from returning to the beacon if they're powerful enough. If you were affected by any harmful effects during your turn after casting _time beacon_, in order to return to your beacon, _time beacon_ attempts a [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] check against each such effect. If it fails at any of these checks, you can't return. After returning to the _time beacon_, the spell ends.
