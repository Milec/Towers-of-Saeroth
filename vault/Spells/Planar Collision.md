# Planar Collision two actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 157

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 300 feet

**Area** 100-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You rip out a portion of an elemental plane and smash it into your plane, unleashing a chaotic maelstrom. When you Cast this Spell, choose an elemental plane. The collision deals 8d6 force damage and 8d8 damage of a type depending on the chosen plane to each creature in the area with a basic Reflex save. A creature that fails the saving throw is also [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 25 feet away from the center of the burst (or 50 feet on a critical failure). Each creature whose movement is stopped by a firm surface, such as a well, takes bludgeoning damage equal to the remaining number of feet it would've moved. The area also becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]] and [[Rules/Hazardous Terrain|hazardous terrain]] that lasts for a year and a day or until [[Rules/Counteracting (rules-3280)|counteracted]] by a spell or effect such as _dispel magic_. Any creature that ends its turn in the hazardous terrain takes 15 damage of a type depending on the chosen plane.

- **Plane of Air** The damage dealt is acid damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] trait.
- **Plane of Earth** The damage dealt is bludgeoning damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]] trait.
- **Plane of Fire** The damage dealt is fire damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait.
- **Plane of Metal** The damage dealt is slashing damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Metal|metal]] trait.
- **Plane of Water** The damage dealt is cold damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]] trait.
- **Plane of Wood** The damage dealt is piercing damage, and the spell gains the [[Traits/Wood|wood]] trait.
