# Daze two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 327

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You cloud the target's mind and daze it with a mental jolt. The jolt deals mental damage equal to your spellcasting ability modifier; the target must attempt a basic Will save. If the target critically fails the save, it is also stunned 1.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
