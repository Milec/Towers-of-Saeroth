# Share Burden ⤾  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 268

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Repose (Divine Mysteries)|Repose]]

**Trigger** An ally within range is about to attempt a saving throw against a harmful [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|emotion]] effect, and you would not otherwise have to save against the effect.

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** you and the triggering ally

---

You shoulder some of your ally’s pain. Both you and your ally use the outcome one degree of success better than the result of your ally’s saving throw, even if the triggering effect usually affects only one target.
