# Shifting Form single action  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Morph]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 388

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You change your body to better suit the moment. You gain one of the following abilities of your choice. You can Dismiss this spell.

- You gain a 20-foot status bonus to your Speed.
- You gain a climb or swim Speed equal to half your Speed.
- You gain [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
- You gain a pair of claws. These are [[Traits/Agile|agile]] [[Traits/Finesse|finesse]] [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attacks that deal 1d8 slashing damage.
- You gain [[Rules/Scent|scent]] 60 feet ([[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]]).
