[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

[[Setting/Deities/Ceyannan]], [[Setting/Deities/Eritrice (Divine Mysteries)|Eritrice]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Teki Stronggut]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature or object

**Duration** unlimited

---

You specify a [[Rules/Setting Triggers|trigger]] and a message up to 25 words long. When the specified trigger occurs within 30 feet of the target, an illusory mouth appears on the target and speaks the message, and the _magic mouth_ spell ends.
