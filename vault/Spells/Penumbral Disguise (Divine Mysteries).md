# Penumbral Disguise two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Shadow]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 258

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Dajermube (Divine Mysteries)|Dajermube]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You wrap the target in shadows, granting it a +1 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy (Player Core)|Stealth]] checks to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] while in [[Rules/Dim Light (Player Core)|dim light]] or [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]]. In addition, the shadows mask the target’s features. While the target is in dim light or darkness, other creatures must succeed at a [[Actions/Seek (Player Core)|Seek]] action against the spell’s DC to discern details about its appearance. For example, without using Seek, other creatures can determine the target’s general shape (such as [[Traits/Humanoid (Player Core)|humanoid]]), but they must Seek to determine the target’s precise appearance. Creatures with [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] can still see the target and its features normally. The target’s normal appearance is revealed in [[Rules/Bright Light (Player Core)|bright light]].

---
**Heightened (4th)** The status bonus is +2. Creatures with darkvision can no longer discern details about the target while the target is in dim light or darkness without Seeking, though creatures with greater darkvision can still determine these details.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th rank, except the status bonus is +3, and creatures without darkvision can’t determine even general details about the target while the target is in dim light or darkness unless they successfully Seek the target; these creatures see a vague shadow instead. Even on a successful Seek, they only determine general features, though they can see details on a critical success.

**Heightened (8th)** As 6th rank, except the status bonus is +4, and even creatures with greater darkvision must Seek to discern details about the target while the target is in dim light or darkness.
