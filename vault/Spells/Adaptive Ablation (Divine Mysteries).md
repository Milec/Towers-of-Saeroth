# Adaptive Ablation ⤾  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 262

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Change (Divine Mysteries)|Change]]

**Trigger** You take [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|electricity]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]], or [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|sonic]] damage.

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You shift yourself to adapt to the mystical wavelengths of the energy damage, protecting yourself from further harm. You gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to the triggering energy type; you gain this resistance after you take the triggering damage, so it applies only to future damage you take during the duration.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 5.
