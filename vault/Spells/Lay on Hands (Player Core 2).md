# Lay on Hands single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Champion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 255

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature

---

Your hands become infused with powerful vitality, capable of healing a living creature or damaging an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creature with a touch.

- **Willing Living Creature** The target regains 6 Hit Points. If the target is someone other than yourself, it also gains a +2 status bonus to AC for 1 round.
- **Undead Creature** The target takes 1d6 vitality damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save; on a failure, the target also takes a –2 status penalty to AC for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 6, and the damage to an undead target increases by 1d6.
