# Forbidden Thought two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Psychic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 21

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You place a psychic lock in a foe's mind that prevents it from a specific action. Choose “Strike,” “Stride,” “Cast a Spell,” or a specific action you know the creature to have (such as “Breath Weapon” against a [[Traits/Dragon|dragon]]). If the creature attempts that action on its next turn, it must surmount your lock to do so, causing it to take 2d6 mental damage (with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.

---

**Amp** Your telepathic push is particularly shocking to those who resist it. If the target fails its save, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].
