# Weight of the World two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 173

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You force a creature to experience an instantaneous burst of severely multiplied gravity, wreaking havoc on their eyes and internal organs. The target takes 2d12 bludgeoning damage and 1d12 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]] with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage, is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage, is dazzled for 1 minute, and is stunned 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is dazzled for 1 minute, and is stunned 3.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The bludgeoning damage increases by 1d12, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d12.
