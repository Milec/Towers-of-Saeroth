**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 263

**Bloodline**
[[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|Draconic]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

You spew energy from your mouth, dealing 5d6 damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. The damage type depends on the magical tradition related to the dragon that influenced your bloodline: **arcane** force, **divine** spirit, **occult** mental, or **primal** fire, or your specfic [Draconic Exemplar](DraconicBenefactors.aspx)

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
