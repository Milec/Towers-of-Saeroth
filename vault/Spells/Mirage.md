# Mirage  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 344

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 50-foot burst

**Duration** until your next daily preparations

---

You create an illusion that causes natural terrain to look, sound, feel, and smell like a different kind of terrain. This doesn't disguise any structures or creatures in the area.

 Any creature that touches the illusion or uses the [[Actions/Seek]] action to examine it can attempt to disbelieve your illusion.

---
**Heightened (5th)** Your image can also disguise structures or create illusory structures (but still doesn't disguise creatures).
