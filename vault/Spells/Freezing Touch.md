# Freezing Touch two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 139

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You channel the might and cold of a blizzard into a single touch that freezes the target in place. The target takes 5d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent cold damage]] and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the persistent cold damage.  

**Failure** The target takes the full persistent cold damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] for 1 minute or until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]]. Once per round, if the target uses or is affected by an effect with the [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]] trait, it can attempt to Escape as a free action or immediately roll a flat check to end the persistent cold damage (this counts as having an appropriate type of help, reducing the DC of the flat check to 10).  

**Critical Failure** As a failure, but double the persistent cold damage, and the creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]] for as long as it's immobilized.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The persistent damage increases by 1d4.
