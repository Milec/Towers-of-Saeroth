# Vampiric Revelry two actions to 2 rounds  *Spell 9*

**Area** varies

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You steal the blood and life force of other creatures around you and sometimes share their energies with your allies. You deal 12d6 void damage to living enemies in the area with a basic Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the area and other parameters. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to half the damage a single creature takes from this spell; calculate these temporary Hit Points using the creature that took the most damage. Any temporary Hit Points gained through this spell are lost after 1 minute.  
two actions The spell is a 20-foot emanation.  
three actions The spell is a 20-foot emanation. In addition to granting temporary Hit Points to yourself, you grant the same number of temporary Hit Points to all allies in the emanation.  
**Two Rounds** If you spend 3 actions casting the spell, you can avoid finishing the spell and spend another 2 actions on your next turn to empower the spell even further. You can take other actions before finishing this spell, but the spell is lost if you Cast another Spell in between. The spell’s area increases to a 30-foot emanation, and the temporary Hit Points gained equal the highest damage a single creature took from the spell. After dealing damage, you can then teleport up to 60 feet to a location you can see. You can then grant all allies in a 30-foot emanation the same number of temporary Hit Points you gained.
