# Root Reading two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 197

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 30-foot emanation

---

You gain general insights into the immediate vicinity by sending your sense through the root systems of trees and bushes. If there are any [[Rules/Conditions/Unnoticed|unnoticed]] Medium or larger creatures in the area, you learn of their presence and they're [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] to you. You can immediately attempt to [[Actions/Seek]] an undetected creature, and you gain a +1 status bonus to this attempt and any of your other attempts to Seek [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] or undetected creatures until the end of your next turn. You also learn if any such creatures passed through this area in the last hour, although you get only the vaguest sense of direction from the spell. If you begin to [[Actions/Track]] a creature detected in this way, you gain a +1 status bonus to the initial check.
