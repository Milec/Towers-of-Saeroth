# Summon Entity three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 375

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (Haunter in the Dark) (Monster Core 2)|Nyarlathotep (Haunter in the Dark)]], [[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos) (Divine Mysteries)|Nyarlathotep (The Crawling Chaos)]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You conjure an aberration to fight for you. This works like [[Spells/Summon Animal|_summon animal_]], except you summon a common creature that has the [[Traits/Aberration|aberration]] trait and whose level is 5 or lower, such as those below.

---
**Heightened (6th)** Level 7.

**Heightened (7th)** Level 9.

**Heightened (8th)** Level 11.

**Heightened (9th)** Level 13.

**Heightened (10th)** Level 15.
