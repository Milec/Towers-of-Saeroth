# Belittling Boast two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 88

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You boast about your skills or combat prowess, bolstering your own abilities and belittling your opponents. Select one of the following to boast about: melee attack rolls, ranged attack rolls, spell attack rolls, or a single skill. Attempt an [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation]] check to [[Actions/Demoralize]] all enemies within 30 feet. Each creature that becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] additionally takes a –1 circumstance penalty to the attack roll or skill you boasted about for 1 minute. If at least one creature becomes frightened, you gain a +1 status bonus to the attack roll or skill you boasted about for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (8th)** The bonus and penalty increase to 2.
