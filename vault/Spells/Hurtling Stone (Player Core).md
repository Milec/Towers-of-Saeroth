# Hurtling Stone single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 374

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Earth (Player Core)|Earth]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You evoke a magical stone and throw it, with divine guide to your aim. The stone deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage depending on your spell attack roll against the target's AC.

**Critical Success** The target takes double damage and you can [[Rules/Forced Movement|push]] it up to 10 feet.  

**Success** The target takes full damage and you can push it up to 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The stone's damage increases by 1d6.
