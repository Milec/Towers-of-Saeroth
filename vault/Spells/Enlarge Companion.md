# Enlarge Companion two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Ranger]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 234

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** your animal companion

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your animal companion grows much larger, towering over its foes in battle. Your animal companion becomes Huge, gaining the effects of a 4th-level [[Spells/Enlarge|_enlarge_]] spell.
