# Apex Companion two actions  *Focus 10*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -150- Broken Promises|Pathfinder #150: Broken Promises]] pg. 77

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 animal companion in your service

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You focus on the energies of all members of your animal companion’s species, transforming your animal companion into its apex battle form. Your animal companion gains the following statistics and abilities while in its apex form.

- Your animal companion becomes Huge and its attacks have 15-foot reach. It must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost.
- 30 temporary Hit Points.
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- Your animal companion’s attack’s damage dice increase by one step, and its attack gains the [[Traits/Deadly|deadly d12]] trait.
- +10-foot status bonus to its Speeds.
- Ignores difficult terrain and greater difficult terrain.
