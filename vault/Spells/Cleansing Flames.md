# Cleansing Flames two actions  *Focus 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -168- King of the Mountain|Pathfinder #168: King of the Mountain]] pg. 74

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Phoenix]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

---

You wrap the target in purifying flames. You can attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] a single [[Traits/Poison|poison]] or non-[[Traits/Curse|curse]] effect imparting the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Fatigued|fatigued]], [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Paralyzed|paralyzed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]] condition. The target can choose to empower the flames with their life force, becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]] (or increasing their drained value by 1, if already under the condition) to grant you a +1 circumstance bonus to your counteract check.
