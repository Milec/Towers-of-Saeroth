# Ash Form two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 287

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You take the form of a cloud of minuscule ash particles. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 3, [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 10 to piercing and slashing damage, and immunity to [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|bleed]], falling damage, and precision damage. You can’t move through solid objects, but you can stream through tiny gaps as if they were [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Any ability with the [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|air]] trait that involves blowing or shifting wind (such as [[Spells/Gust of Wind (Player Core)|_gust of wind_]]) deals you 1d6 bludgeoning damage per rank of the spell each round you are in its area, in addition to its normal effects.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The resistance increases to 15.
