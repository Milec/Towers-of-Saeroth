# Steal the Sky ⤾  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 225, [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 79, [[Sources/Pathfinder -158- Sixty Feet Under|Pathfinder #158: Sixty Feet Under]] pg. 79

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Trigger** You make a successful [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] Strike against a flying creature.

**Target** the creature you hit

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You deny a flying creature the support of the air.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The target descends 60 feet. If it hits a surface, it takes bludgeoning damage as if it had fallen.  

**Failure** As success, and if the target hits a surface, it falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] and can't [[Actions/Fly]], [[Spells/Levitate|_levitate_]], or otherwise leave the ground for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature takes double damage from hitting a surface.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the distance the creature must descend by 20 feet.
