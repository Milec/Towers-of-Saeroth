# Temporary Tool  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 260

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Amaznen]], [[Setting/Deities/Bharnarol]], [[Setting/Deities/Brigh (Divine Mysteries)|Brigh]], [[Setting/Deities/Findeladlara (Divine Mysteries)|Findeladlara]], [[Setting/Deities/Magrim (Divine Mysteries)|Magrim]], [[Setting/Deities/Ptah]], [[Setting/Deities/Wheels of Innovation]], [[Setting/Deities/Zogmugot]]

**Cast** 1 minute

**Duration** 1 minute or until used

---

You conjure a temporary simple [[Equipment/Tool (Player Core)|tool]], such as a shovel or [[Equipment/Rope (Player Core)|rope]] into your hands. It lasts until it’s used for a single activity or for 1 minute, whichever comes first, after which it disappears. The tool is obviously temporarily conjured and thus can’t be sold or passed off as a genuine item.
