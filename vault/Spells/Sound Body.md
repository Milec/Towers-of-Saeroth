# Sound Body two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 357

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

---

You send a surge of healing energy to restore the target's body. Attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] an effect of your choice imposing one of these conditions on the target: [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]], [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]]. If you didn't counteract the effect, but you would have if its counteract rank were 2 lower, instead suppress the effect until the beginning of your next turn. The effect's duration doesn't elapse while it's suppressed. This spell can't counteract or suppress [[Traits/Curse|curses]], [[Traits/Disease|diseases]], or conditions that are part of the target's normal state.

---
**Heightened (4th)** Add [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th rank, plus add [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]].

**Heightened (8th)** As 4th rank, plus add [[Rules/Conditions/Petrified (Player Core)|petrified]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]].
