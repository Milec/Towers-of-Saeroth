# Serrate single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 267

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Metal (Divine Mysteries)|Metal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 metal weapon

---

With a pinch, the metal of a weapon twists, adding numerous jagged blades along its surface. [[Actions/Strike (Book of the Dead)|Strikes]] with the target weapon deal an additional 1d4 slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The extra damage increases by 1d4.
