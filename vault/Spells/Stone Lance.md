# Stone Lance two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 61, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 201

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure a jagged lance of stone and then launch it at a foe. Make a spell attack roll against the target. On a hit, you deal 6d6 piercing damage and the lance impales the creature, giving it a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds unless it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]]. On a critical hit, if the creature is on the ground, the lance also embeds into the ground and [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilizes]] the creature until it Escapes. A creature that Escapes after being impaled takes 3 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. When the spell ends, the lance crumbles into dirt, freeing the target if it hasn't Escaped.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6 and the persistent bleed damage for Escapes by 1.
