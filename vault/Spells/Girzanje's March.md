**Area** 30-foot burst

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You sing a prayer for war, inspiring valor in allies who hear your song. Allied creatures who can hear and understand you gain a +1 status bonus to attack rolls, Fortitude saves, and Will saves against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects.

---
**Heightened (7th)** The status bonus increases to +2.

**Heightened (10th)** The status bonus increases to +3.
