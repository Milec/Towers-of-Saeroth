# Cloud Dragon's Cloak ⤾  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 70

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Trigger** You or an ally in range is targeted by a ranged attack.

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** the creature targeted by the attack

**Duration** 1 round

---

A cloud of mist enshrouds the target, appearing much like the deflecting clouds created by cloud dragons. The target is treated as [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] for the purposes of resolving the triggering attack (so normally the attacker must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to target it) and all ranged attacks against it for the duration.
