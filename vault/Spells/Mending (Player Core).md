# Mending  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 343

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Range** touch

**Target** non-magical object of light Bulk or less

---

You repair the target item. You restore 5 Hit Points per spell rank to the target, potentially removing the [[Rules/Conditions/Broken|broken]] condition if this repairs it past the item's Broken Threshold. You can't replace lost pieces or repair an object that's been completely destroyed.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** You can target a non-magical object of 1 Bulk or less.

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target a non-magical object of 2 Bulk or less, or a magical object of 1 Bulk or less.
