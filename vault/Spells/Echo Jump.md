# Echo Jump two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 148

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Kyonin have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Player Core)|basic]] Reflex

---

You teleport to an unoccupied space within range, leaving behind a faint echo of your form that explodes with magical force. Each creature within a 10-foot emanation from your previous location takes 4d8 [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|force]] damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The range increases by 10 feet, and the damage increases by 3d8.
