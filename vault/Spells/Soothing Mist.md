# Soothing Mist two actions  *Focus 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Positive]], [[Traits/Ranger]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 235

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature

---

You call forth a magical mist of positive energy that envelops a creature. The mist restores 2d8 Hit Points to a target living creature and ends one source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid, bleed, fire, negative, or poison damage]] affecting it. If the creature is taking persistent damage from multiple sources, you select which one is removed. Against an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] target, you deal 2d8 positive damage (basic Fortitude save); if it fails the save, it also takes 2 persistent positive damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing (or damage to an undead target) increases by 1d8, and the persistent positive damage to an undead creature increases by 1.
