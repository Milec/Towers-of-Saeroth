# Rend Magic two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 159

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** up to 1 minute

---

You spew forth pure magical energy down your arm before releasing it in a devastating attack. Make a melee spell attack against the target’s AC. If it hits, the target takes 3d10 force damage, then takes 1d10 force damage each time it Casts a Spell for 1 minute; on a critical hit, double the initial damage and the damage when the target casts spells. This spell ends immediately if the target ends its turn without Casting a Spell.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial force damage increases by 2d10, and the force damage taken whenever the target casts a spell increases by 1d10.
