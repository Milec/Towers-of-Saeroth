# Take Root single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 198

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 round

---

Roots sprout from the flesh of the target and coil across the floor or around objects, reinforcing their stance or grip. The targeted creature gains a +1 circumstance bonus to their Fortitude DC against attempts to [[Actions/Shove]] them and a +1 circumstance bonus to their Reflex DC against attempts to [[Actions/Disarm]] or [[Actions/Trip]] them. This bonus also applies to saving throws against spells or effects that would attempt to remove a held item from their grasp.
