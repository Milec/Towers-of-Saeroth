# Uncover Device two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity (Player Core)|Electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 168

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 metal trap or device

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You focus on the construction of the object, channeling electricity through it that allows you to get a sense of its shape and construction. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Thievery (Player Core)|Thievery]] checks to [[Actions/Disable a Device (Player Core)|Disable]] Device on the target and to [[Actions/Aid (Player Core)|Aid]] checks to help an ally Disable Devise on the target.
