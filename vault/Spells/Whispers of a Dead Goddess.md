# Whispers of a Dead Goddess two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/High Seas]] pg. 79

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

The mysterious whispers that fill the Mordant Spire, which some believe to be the voice of the dead goddess [[Setting/Deities/Acavna]], become audible. A creature in the area when this [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Spell is Cast]] or who enters the area must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune for 1 hour.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1, listening to the whispers.  

**Failure** As success, and the creature will not voluntarily leave the area for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and you can whisper a single course of action to each creature that critically failed, with the effects of [[Spells/Suggestion (Player Core)|_suggestion_]]. The effect ends on a creature if you stop sustaining the spell or the creature leaves the area.
