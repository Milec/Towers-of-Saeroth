# Divine Wrath two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 326

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You channel the fury of divinity against your foes. You deal 4d10 spirit damage to enemies in the area, depending on their Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and is sickened 2; while it's sickened, it's also [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
