# Armor of Thorn and Claw two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Draconic Codex]] pg. 224

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Razor-sharp thorns and claws erupt from your skin or scales. Whenever a creature touches you or hits you with a melee unarmed attack, it takes 1 piercing damage. Additionally, if you become [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], or otherwise held [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] in a creature’s grasp, such as by being engulfed or swallowed, the creature takes 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The piercing damage increases by 2, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d4.
