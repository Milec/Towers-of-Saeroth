# Unbreaking Wave Vapor ⤾  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 223

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Trigger** You're targeted by an attack made by an attacker you can see.

---

You're [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] against the triggering attack and gain a +2 circumstance bonus to AC against it. After the triggering attack is complete, you can Step if the triggering attack hit or Step twice if the triggering attack missed.
