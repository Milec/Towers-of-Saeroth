# Hymn of Healing two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard (Player Core)|Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 370

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** you or 1 ally

**Duration** sustained up to 4 rounds

---

Your glorious singing mends wounds and provides a temporary respite from harm. The target gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing|fast healing]] 2. When you Cast the Spell and the first time each round you Sustain the Spell, the target gains 2 temporary Hit Points, which last for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The fast healing and temporary Hit Points each increase by 2
