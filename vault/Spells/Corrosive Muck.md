# Corrosive Muck two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|Acid]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 243

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** all squares in two 10-foot bursts

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create two puddles of acidic slime that hinder the movement of anyone who walks through them. The area of each pool becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|greater difficult terrain]]. Each round that a creature starts its turn in one of the pools or enters a pool during a move action it's using, it takes 8d6 acid damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that critically fails its saving throw also takes 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid damage]]. As normal, if a Large or larger creature starts its turn in both pools, it takes the damage only once.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Create an additional 10-foot burst.
