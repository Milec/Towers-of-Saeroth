# Unraveling Blast two actions  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Stolen Fate

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Stolen Fate Player's Guide]] pg. 15

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Harrow]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You unleash a small blast of potential that destroys one of a creature's possible futures, leaving them reeling. You deal 2d4 mental damage to the creature, and it must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half the mental damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] until the start of its turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]], and is flat-footed until the start of your next turn.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 3d4.
