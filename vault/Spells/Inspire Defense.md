# Inspire Defense single action  *Cantrip 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 386

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 60-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 round

---

You inspire your allies to protect themselves more effectively. You and all allies in the area gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws, as well as resistance equal to half the spell's level to physical damage.
