# Temporary Possession single action  *Focus 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Possession (Player Core)|Possession]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 184

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 of your thralls

**Defense**
Will

---

You destroy the target thrall, using its destruction as a catalyst to temporarily possess an enemy. One living or undead target creature within 15 feet of the destroyed thrall must attempt a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] by you until the end of your turn, and you can have it take up to one action of your choice: Drop [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|Prone]], Interact, Release what it’s holding, or Strike. The possessed creature’s Strikes use and contribute toward your multiple attack penalty. At the end of your turn, the creature is no longer controlled and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] 1. If the creature loses any Hit Points for any reason while it’s possessed or stunned by this ability, you lose Hit Points equal to half that amount since your spirit partially lingers within its body.  

**Failure** As success, but you can spend up to 2 actions.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but you can spend up to 3 actions.
