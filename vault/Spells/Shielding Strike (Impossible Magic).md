# Shielding Strike single action  *Focus 1*

As you attack a foe, warding magic transforms your momentum into action and lifts your shield. You can either [[Actions/Raise a Shield (Player Core)|Raise your Shield]] if you’re wielding one or cast _shield_ if you have the spell. Then, make a melee Strike.
