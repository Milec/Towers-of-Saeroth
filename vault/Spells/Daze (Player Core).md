# Daze two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 322

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant (Player Core 2)|Aberrant]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|Hag]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You push into the target's mind and daze it with a mental jolt. The jolt deals 1d6 mental damage, with a basic Will save. If the target critically fails the save, it is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
