# Diamond Dust two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 112

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Cold]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation centered on you

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You supercool the nearby air, forming a cloud of dancing ice crystals in an emanation around you. A creature other than you that enters the area or ends its turn within the area takes 2d6 cold damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn. The crystals scatter and refract light; if a light effect enters diamond dust’s area, any creatures that effect goes on to affect gain a +2 status bonus to AC and saves against it. While the spell persists, you can activate or deactivate this aura by using a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait. You can Dismiss this spell.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
