# Detonate Magic two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 324

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Abraxas (Divine Mysteries)|Abraxas]], [[Setting/Deities/Groetus (Divine Mysteries)|Groetus]], [[Setting/Deities/Liisglan]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Wards of the Pharaoh]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 magic item or spell effect

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex (see text)

---

You cause the magic within the target to dissipate in a destructive explosion. You attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the target. If the attempt succeeds, an explosion of magical force deals 8d6 force damage with a basic Reflex save. If you successfully counteract the magic of an item, it's deactivated for 1 week (or destroyed on a critical success) and the explosion is a 5-foot emanation from the item. If you successfully counteract a spell, the effect ends and the explosion affects either all creatures in the spells' area or the target of the spell and all creatures in a 5-foot emanation around it.
