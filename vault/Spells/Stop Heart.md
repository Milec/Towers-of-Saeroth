# Stop Heart two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 164

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

With a cruel grasp, you reach into your opponent and squeeze at their heart. The target takes 10d10 void damage with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is sickened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is sickened 3.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The void damage increases by 1d10.
