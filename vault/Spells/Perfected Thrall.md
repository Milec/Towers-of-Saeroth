# Perfected Thrall single action  *Focus 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Thrall]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 183

**Range** 60 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a perfect thrall that’s the conglomeration of all aspects of necromancy. The perfected thrall is made of pure, dense muscle, covered in a layer of hardened bone, and inhabited by the spirit of a mighty warrior. It wields a weapon made of blood that forms into the ideal weapon for any situation before becoming harder than iron. You create a thrall that has 200 Hit Points and a Speed of 40 feet. Whenever you use an ability that would destroy this thrall, it instead loses 20 Hit Points. The spirit inhabiting this thrall can inspire other spirits to join the fray with their inspiring attacks. Whenever the perfected thrall critically hits a creature, you create up to 4 thralls, each of which must be adjacent to either the perfected thrall or the Strike’s target. When you use _thrall charge_ to order this thrall to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], the Strike deals an additional 6d6 damage.
