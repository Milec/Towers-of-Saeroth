# Funeral Flames two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Myth-Speaker

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -216- The Acropolis Pyre|Pathfinder #216: The Acropolis Pyre]] pg. 82

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** one  club  or  staff  you're wielding

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You transform a bludgeon into a fearsome torch suited to casting [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] into the afterlife. One end of the weapon ignites, becoming wreathed in blue flame. While you wield the target weapon, it becomes a +1 weapon, gains the [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile fire]] trait, and sheds light as brightly as a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]]. Whenever you successfully Strike a creature with the weapon, you can Dismiss the spell as a free action to deal 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] to the target; if the target is undead, increase the persistent damage dice to d8s.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 1d6.
