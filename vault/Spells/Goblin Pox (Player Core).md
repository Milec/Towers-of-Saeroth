# Goblin Pox two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 333

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Apollyon (Divine Mysteries)|Apollyon]], [[Setting/Deities/Ghlaunder (Divine Mysteries)|Ghlaunder]], [[Setting/Deities/Isph-Aun-Vuln]], [[Setting/Deities/Kzininn]], [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa (Divine Mysteries)|Urgathoa]], [[Setting/Deities/Xsistaid]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Your touch afflicts the target with goblin pox, an irritating allergenic rash. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened 1]].  

**Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 2.

**Goblin Pox** ([[Traits/Disease|disease]]) Level 1; Creatures that have the [[Traits/Goblin|goblin]] trait and [[Bestiary/Goblin Dog|goblin dogs]] are immune; **Stage 1** sickened 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** sickened 1 and [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed 1]] (1 round); **Stage 3** sickened 1 and the creature can't reduce its sickened value below 1 (1 day)
