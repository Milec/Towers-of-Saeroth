# Shadow Army three actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Shadow (Player Core)|Shadow]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 161

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 50-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create dozens of semi-real copies of yourself that swarm across the battlefield and fight your enemies. You’re virtually indistinguishable from your clones, allowing you to [[Actions/Hide (Player Core)|Hide]] and [[Actions/Sneak (Player Core)|Sneak]] among them. An enemy that’s in the area when the spell is cast or that ends their turn in the area takes 3d10 mental damage and 3d10 bludgeoning damage, and is possibly inflicted with a condition, depending on the result of their Will save. When you Cast the Spell, you choose a condition your shadows impose on a failed save: [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 2, [[Rules/Conditions/Off-Guard|off-guard]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1.

**Critical Success** The creature takes no damage. For the duration of the spell, the creature uses the outcome one degree of success better than it rolled on further saves against the spell and can distinguish you from your clones without difficulty.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and gains the chosen condition until the end of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and gains the chosen condition until the end of its next turn.
