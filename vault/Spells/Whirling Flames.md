# Whirling Flames two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 234

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Flames]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** up to two 5-foot bursts that don't overlap

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You call forth a storm of whirling flames, engulfing all creatures in that area and dealing 5d6 fire damage with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d6, and you can add another nonoverlapping 5-foot burst to the area.
