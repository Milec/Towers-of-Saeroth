# Acid Storm two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Acid]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Moloch (Pathfinder -218- Titanbane)|Moloch]], [[Setting/Deities/Thuskchoon]], [[Setting/Deities/Trelmarixian (Divine Mysteries)|Trelmarixian]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot-radius burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You evoke a storm of acid rain that pelts the area for the spell’s duration. A creature that begins its turn in the area takes 3d8 acid damage (basic Reflex save).

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
