# Demon Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 99

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You corrupt yourself with the sin of the [[Setting/Planes/Outer Rifts]], transforming into a Medium demon battle form. When you cast this spell, choose [[Bestiary/Abrikandilu (Bestiary 3)|abrikandilu]], [[Bestiary/Brimorak (Monster Core)|brimorak]], [[Bestiary/Kithangian|kithangian]], or [[Bestiary/Vloriak|vloriak]]. If you choose kithangian, the battle form is Large and you must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Demon|demon]] and [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 20 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 30 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]], [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Equipment/Cold Iron Weapon|cold iron]], and weakness 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +22, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of demon you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Abrikandilu (Bestiary 3)|Abrikandilu]]** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 piercing, and [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute on a critical hit; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 slashing; **Ranged** single action hurled debris ([[Rules/Range (Player Core)|range increment 20 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+6 bludgeoning.
- **[[Bestiary/Brimorak (Monster Core)|Brimorak]]** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action flaming sword ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8+10 slashing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d4+10 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Kithangian]]** Speed 30 feet; **Melee** single action pincer ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d12+12 slashing, and you can spend an action after a hit to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target; **Melee** single action stinger ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8+12 piercing; all melee Strikes deal 2d6 additional precision damage to [[Traits/Animal (Player Core)|animals]].
- **[[Bestiary/Vloriak]]** Speed 25 feet; [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 10 to [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]]; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 piercing plus 1d6 spirit; **Melee** single action tongue ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d6+12 acid plus 1d6 spirit.
