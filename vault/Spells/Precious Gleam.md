# Precious Gleam single action  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Convocation

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rival Academies]] pg. 117

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 weapon that is unattended or wielded by a willing creature

**Duration** until the beginning of your next turn

---

**[[Rules/Sidebars/Roleplaying Sins|Sin:]]** greed

You temporarily enhance a weapon, replacing it with finer materials. On the next successful attack with that weapon during the spell’s duration, the attack deals an additional 1d6 precision damage. You can choose for the metal to become your choice of cold iron, silver, or steel, causing the attack to bypass resistances accordingly.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.
