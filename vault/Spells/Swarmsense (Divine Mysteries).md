# Swarmsense three actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 270

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Swarm (Divine Mysteries)|Swarm]]

**Duration** sustained

---

You extend your senses through a multitude of crawling and flying creatures. You create a crawling swarm in your square. You can hear through the swarm as if using your normal auditory senses. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]] and the first time you [[Actions/Sustain]] it each round, you can move the swarm 10 feet along the ground in any direction. The swarm has AC 15 and a +0 bonus to its saves. Any damage dealt to the swarm destroys it and ends the spell.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The swarm has a [[Rules/Climb Speed (Player Core)|climb Speed]] of 10 feet.

**Heightened (5th)** The swarm has a [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly Speed]] of 10 feet. You can see through the swarm using your visual senses.

**Heightened (7th)** The swarm gains a 10-foot status bonus to its Speeds.
