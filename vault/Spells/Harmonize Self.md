# Harmonize Self single action  *Focus 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Monk (Player Core)|Monk]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 257

---

You heal yourself in one of the following ways, chosen by you when you cast the spell.

- You regain 8 Hit Points.
- You attempt to cure one [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]] or [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]] afflicting you; attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] the affliction.

---
**Heightened (+1)** If you choose to regain Hit Points, the Hit Points regained increase by 8.
