# Tacky Pine Sap two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 166

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 5-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure highly flammable pine sap, covering the area with it. This area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. When a creature within the area uses a fire effect or receives fire damage, the sap ignites, dealing 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] to anyone standing on it; this burns up the sap, removing it and its difficult terrain.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 1d6.
