# Thunderous Strike single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Magus]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 144

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Requirements** You're wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You swing your massive weapon, creating a wave of sonic vibrations that topples creatures. Make a melee Strike with your two-handed weapon. Each creature in a 15-foot cone from you must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save against your spell DC or take 2 sonic damage. On a critical failure, the creature is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]]. The target of your Strike must be within the cone or the effect fails.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1.
