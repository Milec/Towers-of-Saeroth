# Accelerated Decomposition two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 287

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Blight]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You speed the natural process of a creature’s decline and debilitation. As its body withers, the target takes 9d6 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage and is your choice of [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1, [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] 1, or [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage and is clumsy 1, drained 1, and enfeebled 1 for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is clumsy 1, drained 1, and enfeebled 1 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The void damage increases by 1d6.
