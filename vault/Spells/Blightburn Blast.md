# Blightburn Blast two actions  *Spell 7*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Extinction Curse

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -155- Lord of the Black Sands|Pathfinder #155: Lord of the Black Sands]] pg. 73

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You channel a blast of powerful blightburn radiation from your hands. You deal 4d6 poison damage to creatures in the area. Creatures who fail their save are afflicted with stage 1 of [[Rules/Diseases/Blightburn Sickness|blightburn sickness]] (on a critical failure, the creature moves immediately to stage 2). The level of blightburn sickness caused by this spell is equal to twice the spell's level, and the disease's save DC uses the caster's DC.
