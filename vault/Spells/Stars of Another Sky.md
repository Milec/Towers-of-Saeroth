# Stars of Another Sky three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|Death]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 164

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 30-foot radius, 200-foot-tall cylinder

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

You replace the sky with a mesmerizing vision of the Astral Plane’s River of Souls, pulling creatures toward their final destiny. You can Cast this Spell only if you’re outside. All creatures in the area must attempt a Will save, and they repeat the save each time you Sustain the spell.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and temporarily immune to _stars of another sky_ for 1 day.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated (Player Core)|fascinated]] by the beauty of the sky and rises 20 feet into the air. Creatures without a fly Speed remain in the air for the spell’s duration but fall when they recover from their fascinated condition. A creature that reaches the top of the cylinder doesn’t rise farther but takes 5d10 mental damage each time it starts its turn there due to a desire to become more immersed in the sky. Creatures without souls take double damage. If this damage drops the creature to 0 Hit Points, it dies instantly.  

**Failure** As success, but the creature rises 50 feet into the air. Additionally, hostile actions don’t affect its fascinated condition for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature rises 100 feet into the air.
