# Isolation two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 143

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 3 rounds

---

You summon a tangible manifestation of loneliness to cloak the target from view as surely as if it were wreathed in darkness. The target attempts a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]], but only to its allies. Any creature not allied with the target can see it normally. Effects such as [[Spells/See Invisibility|_see invisibility_]] enable an ally to see the target. The target's allies still know the target is present and can still see all the effects of the target's actions.  

**Failure** As success, except the target also becomes inaudible to its allies and imperceptible to them through any other senses.
