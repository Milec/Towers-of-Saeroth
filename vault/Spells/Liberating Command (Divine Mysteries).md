[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

[[Setting/Deities/Nameless]], [[Setting/Deities/Ranginori (Divine Mysteries)|Ranginori]], [[Setting/Deities/Ristrentho]], [[Setting/Deities/Scal]], [[Setting/Deities/Shei (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Shei]], [[Setting/Deities/Ssila’meshnik]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

You call out a liberating cry, urging an ally to break free of an effect that holds them in place. If the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]], it can immediately use a reaction to attempt to [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]].
