# Swarm Form two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 270

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Swarm (Divine Mysteries)|Swarm]]

**Duration** 5 minutes

---

You discorporate into a swarm of Tiny creatures. While in swarm form, you are the same size, you have the [[Traits/Swarm (GM Core)|swarm]] trait and you gain [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|resistance]] 5 to slashing and piercing damage and weakness 5 to area and [[Traits/Splash (GM Core)|splash]] damage. You can fit into spaces only a few inches wide, moving your constituent creatures through the gap, and you can share a space with another creature. You don’t gain the [[Bestiary/Abilities/Swarm Mind (Monster Core)|swarm mind]] ability, so you are still affected normally by mental effects. As a swarm, you can’t speak, [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|cast spells]], use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions requiring your hands, [[Actions/Activate an Item|activate your magic items]], or make any of your [[Rules/Strikes (GM Core)|Strikes]] with your normal body. While in swarm form, you can crawl all over any creature that shares your space as a single action. That creature must attempt a Fortitude save against your spell DC or become [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1 (sickened 2 on a critical failure). You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the Spell.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Your resistances and weaknesses each increase by 5.
