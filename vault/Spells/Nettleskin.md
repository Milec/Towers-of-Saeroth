# Nettleskin two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Threshold of Knowledge

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Plant]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Threshold of Knowledge]] pg. 10

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Thorns sprout from your body; they pass through and don't damage any clothing or armor you wear. Adjacent creatures that hit you with a melee or [[Traits/Unarmed|unarmed]] attack take 1d4 piercing damage as the nettles jab them and break off. Each time a creature takes damage in this way, _nettleskin's_ duration decreases by 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
