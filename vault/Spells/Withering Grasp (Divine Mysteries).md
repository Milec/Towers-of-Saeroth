# Withering Grasp two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 263

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Decay (Divine Mysteries)|Decay]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature or object

**Defense**
AC

---

Your touch rots organic material and decays objects. Make a spell attack against the target’s AC. Your touch deals 1d12 [[Traits/Void|void]] damage plus 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent void damage]]. If a creature uses an item to block withering grasp, such as with the [[Feats/Shield Block (Player Core)|Shield Block]] reaction, the item is automatically affected, but the creature doesn’t take damage (even if there is damage left over after the shield’s [[Rules/Hardness (Player Core)|Hardness]]). Unlike normal void damage, the void damage from _withering grasp_ damages [[Rules/Objects (Player Core)|objects]], [[Traits/Construct (Player Core)|constructs]], and the like by eroding away their substance.

**Critical Success** Your touch deals double damage (both initial and persistent). If you target an object, lower its Hardness by 4 for 1 minute.  

**Success** Your touch deals full damage. If you target an object, lower its Hardness by 2 for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d12 and the persistent damage increases by 1. If you target an object, lower its Hardness by an additional 1 point on both a success and a critical success.
