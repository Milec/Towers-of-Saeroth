# Shroud of Night single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Darkness]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 387

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Starless Shadow]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron blankets the target's eyes in darkness. If you cast this hex on a willing ally (for instance, one with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Light Blindness|light blindness]]), the ally can choose which result it gets without rolling.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is shrouded in murky darkness. It treats [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] as [[Rules/Dim Light|dim light]], and unless it has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Darkvision|greater darkvision]], all creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]] to it.
