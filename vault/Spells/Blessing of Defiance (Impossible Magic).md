# Blessing of Defiance single action to three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 124

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** varies

**Duration** 1 round

---

Channeling forth the power to persevere in the face of adversity. Choose one saving throw: Fortitude, Reflex, or Will. The target gains a +4 status bonus to that saving throw for 1 round. The potency and number of targets of this blessing depend on how many actions you spend when Casting the Spell.

single action The spell targets you or one willing ally within range.  
two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The spell targets you or one willing ally within range. If you choose Fortitude, the target gains [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 15 to poison. If you choose Reflex, the target gains a +15-foot status bonus to its Speeds. If you choose Will, the target gains resistance 15 to [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage.  
three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]]) The spell targets you and all willing allies within 30 feet.
