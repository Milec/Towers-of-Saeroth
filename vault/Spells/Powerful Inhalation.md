# Powerful Inhalation two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 60, [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 201

**Area** 10-foot emanation

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You rapidly draw the air from your surroundings, hoarding it for yourself. Creatures in the area when the spell is cast take 5d6 bludgeoning damage with a basic Fortitude save. A creature that fails its save can't speak above a raspy whisper for 1 round. A creature that critically fails can't speak or use abilities that require it to [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|breathe]] for 1 round. This prevents it from using effects that require speech and from using a breath weapon or similar ability.

 Creatures made of air (such as [[Bestiary/Families/Elemental, Air|air elementals]]) attempting a save against this spell get a degree of success one worse than they rolled.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6.
