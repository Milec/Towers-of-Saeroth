**Range** 10 feet

---

You create a seven-layered sphere to protect an area. This multicolored sphere functions like a [[Spells/Prismatic Wall|_prismatic wall_]] but is shaped in a 10-foot burst centered on a corner of your space. You must form the sphere in an unbroken open space so its edges don't pass through any creatures or objects, or the spell is lost.
