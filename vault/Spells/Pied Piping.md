# Pied Piping two actions  *Focus 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Bard]], [[Traits/Composition]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 228

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 5-foot emanation

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Your performance enraptures those who hear it, compelling them to follow you about in admiration. Each creature within the emanation must attempt a Will save when you Cast the Spell or the first time they enter the area, after which they become temporarily immune for 1 day. Once per turn, you can spend a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]] trait, to increase the emanation's radius by 5 feet. You can Dismiss the spell.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Fascinated|fascinated]] with you.  

**Failure** The creature uses all its actions to move toward you and compliment your performance. This effect ends if a hostile action is used against the affected creature.  

**Critical Failure** The target gains the [[Traits/Minion|minion]] trait and is controlled by you. This effect ends if a hostile action is used against the affected creature, or if you direct the creature to use any action that causes it harm.
