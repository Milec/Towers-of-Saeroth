# Conjured Clockwork two actions  *Focus 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Shades of Blood

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -215- To Blot Out the Sun|Pathfinder #215: To Blot Out the Sun]] pg. 81

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You conjure a complex clockwork device on the ground and cause it to slowly spread across the battlefield. The area is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. Each creature that enters or ends its turn in the area takes 2d8 slashing damage with a basic Reflex save. A creature can take this damage only once per turn. On subsequent rounds, the first time you [[Actions/Sustain]] the spell each round, you can expand the radius of the clockwork device by 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
