# Far-Flung Fetch single action  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Subtle]], [[Traits/Teleportation (Player Core)|Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/NPC Core]] pg. 26

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 unattended object of light Bulk or less

---

You can pilfer an object even if it's outside the reach of your fingers. You teleport the target into one of your open hand. If you don't have a hand free, it falls to the ground at your feet.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The range increases to 120 feet.

**Heightened (5th)** The range increases to 120 feet, and you can target an unattended object with a Bulk of 1 or less.

**Heightened (7th)** As 5th rank, and when you Cast the Spell you can spend 3 actions instead of 1 to increase the range to planetary. If you do, you don't need line of sight to the target, but you must be extremely familiar with the target.
