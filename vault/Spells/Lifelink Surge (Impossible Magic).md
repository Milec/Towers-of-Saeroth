**Duration** 4 rounds

You make a quick gesture, tracing the link between yourself and your eidolon and drawing on your connection to slowly strengthen your shared life force. Your eidolon gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Fast Healing (Monster Core)|fast healing]] 4 for 4 rounds.

**Heightened (+1)** The fast healing increases by 2.
