# Fishing Spot  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 138

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

---

You conjure a fishing rod and an extraplanar pool teeming with fish. After 10 minutes of fishing, you catch a magical fish; roll 1d8 to see which fish you caught and the effects gained from eating it once it's cooked. The fish must be cooked and eaten (a process that takes about 30 minutes) within 1 hour of being caught, and the listed effect lasts for 1 hour after consumption. Each fish can feed only a single creature.

1. **Musical trout** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Performance (Player Core)|Performance]] checks
2. **Primordial bass** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Survival (Player Core)|Survival]] checks
3. **Ghoulfish** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Intimidation (Player Core)|Intimidation]] checks
4. **Burbling barbel** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception (Player Core)|Deception]] and [[Rules/Skills/Diplomacy (Player Core)|Diplomacy]] checks
5. **Dashing dace** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics (Player Core)|Acrobatics]] checks
6. **Vigocarp** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb (Player Core)|Climb]] and [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]]
7. **Aggressive perch** +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Disarm (Player Core)|Disarm]], [[Actions/Grapple (Player Core)|Grapple]], [[Actions/Reposition]], [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shove]], and [[Actions/Trip (Player Core)|Trip]]
8. **Scholar salmon** +2 status bonus to checks to [[Actions/Recall Knowledge (Player Core)|Recall Knowledge]]
