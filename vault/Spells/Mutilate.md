# Mutilate two actions or three actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Triumph of the Tusk

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -209- Destroyer's Doom|Pathfinder #209: Destroyer's Doom]] pg. 82

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 40 feet

**Target** 1 creature in  line of sight

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You cut your own body in a ritualistic manner, causing similar damage to a creature in your line of sight. These cuts are superficial and cause 1d4 slashing damage to you; however, the wounds that open up on our target’s body are far deeper. The targeted creature takes 5d8 slashing damage; a creature that critically fails this saving throw also takes 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent bleed damage]]. If you cast this as a three-action spell, the spell instead affects a 5-foot burst.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage dealt to the target by 1d8, and increase the persistent bleed damage by 1.
