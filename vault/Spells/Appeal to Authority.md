# Appeal to Authority single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Subtle]], [[Traits/Wizard (Player Core)|Wizard]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 186

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You implant a thought in your target’s mind that resembles an authority figure in their life, influencing how they feel about you. The target attempts a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target harbors some small resentment toward the authority figure you invoked. The target takes a –1 status penalty to saves against your emotion effects, but can attempt another Will save at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell’s effect on a success.  

**Failure** The target believes the resemblance and is susceptible to your influence, taking a –1 status penalty to saves against your emotion effects.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, except the status penalty is –2.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 4 creatures.
