# Flame Wisp two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Fire]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 106

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Three faintly glowing wisps of fire float around your head. Each time you hit a creature with a Strike, one of the wisps goes hurtling towards that creature, dealing 1d4 fire damage. If you Cast a Spell with the [[Traits/Fire|fire]] trait while you have fewer than three wisps, a new wisp appears.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d4.
