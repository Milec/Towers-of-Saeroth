# Dominate two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 326

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/The Godclaw]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** until your next daily preparations

---

You take command of the target, forcing it to obey you. If you issue an obviously self-destructive order, the target doesn't act until you issue a new order. The effect depends on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is stunned 1 as it fights off your commands.  

**Failure** You control the target. It gains the [[Rules/Conditions/Controlled (Player Core)|controlled]] condition, but it can attempt a Will save at the end of each of its turns. On a success, the spell ends.  

**Critical Failure** As a failure, but the target receives a new save only if you give it a new order that is against its nature, such as killing its allies.

---
**Heightened (10th)** The duration is unlimited.
