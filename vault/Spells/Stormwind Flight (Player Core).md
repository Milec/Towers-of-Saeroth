# Stormwind Flight two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Druid (Player Core)|Druid]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 382

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Powerful winds carry you through the air, giving you a fly Speed equal to your Speed. When this spell's duration would end, if you're still flying, you float to the ground, as [[Spells/Gentle Landing|_gentle landing_]].

---
**Heightened (6th)** When you fly using _stormwind flight_, you ignore difficult terrain from wind.
