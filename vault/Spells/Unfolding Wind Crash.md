# Unfolding Wind Crash two actions  *Focus 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Move]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Lands]] pg. 223

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You jump up to 120 feet in any direction and then deal 18d6 bludgeoning damage to creatures within a 20-foot emanation after you land, with a basic Reflex save. Creatures who critically fail are also knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Heightened (10th)** The damage increases to 20d6.
