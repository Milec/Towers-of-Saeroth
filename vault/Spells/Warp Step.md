# Warp Step two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Conjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Dark Archive]] pg. 107

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

---

When you walk, the earth warps beneath your feet—your steps extend, distance contracts, and everything is just a little bit closer. You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed until the end of your turn. You then Stride twice. You can use warp step to [[Actions/Burrow]], [[Actions/Climb]], [[Actions/Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim]] instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type.
