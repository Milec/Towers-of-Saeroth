# Summon Warden of the Wild three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 87

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Defense**
see text

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You briefly call forth the spirit of one of the [[Bestiary/Families/Wardens of the Wild]], the legendary guardians of nature. The warden occupies the space of a [[Rules/Size, Space, and Reach (Player Core)|Gargantuan]] creature. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast this Spell]], you summon a warden depending on the biome in which the spell is cast (for instance, summoning the Warden of Caverns and Burrows if summoned in a cave, canyon, or other underground environment). In an unnatural environment, the warden summoned depends on the region's most recent or similar natural biome, as decided by the GM.

- **[[Bestiary/Warden of Caverns and Burrows]]** Speed 60 feet, burrow 100 feet.  
**Arrive** ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) _Obsidian Cage_ Obsidian stalagmites burst from the earth as the warden surfaces, impaling enemies and trapping them in stalagmites. Enemies on the ground within 40 feet take 6d6 piercing damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). Creatures that fail their save become [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] until they [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escape]] (the DC is your spell DC).  
**Depart** ([[Traits/Earth (Player Core)|earth]]) _Blessing of the Depths_ The squares occupied by the warden remain disheveled and broken, becoming [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|difficult terrain]]. The warden affects you and all your allies within 60 feet with a [[Spells/Mountain Resilience|_mountain resilience_]] and [[Spells/Unfettered Pack (Player Core)|_unfettered pack_]]. The duration for both spells is reduced to 3 rounds, but the duration of _mountain resilience_ isn't reduced when an affected target is hit by a bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing attack.

- **[[Bestiary/Warden of Forests and Meadows]]** Speed 120 feet.  
**Arrive** ([[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|illusion]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]]) _Idyllic Panorama_ The warden's presence unpredictably warps the appearances and perceived distances of creatures and obstacles around it. Each time an enemy within 40 feet of the warden attempts a Strike or Stride action, it takes 4d6 mental damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save). On a critical failure, the creature Strikes a random target within reach or Strides in a random direction. On a critical success, the creature becomes temporarily immune for 1 round.  
**Depart** _Fox's Insightful Trickery_ The warden affects you and all your allies within 60 feet with [[Spells/Blur (Player Core)|_blur_]] and [[Spells/Truesight|_truesight_]]. The duration for both spells is reduced to 3 rounds.

- **[[Bestiary/Warden of Ocean and Rivers|Warden of Oceans and Rivers]]** Speed 60 feet, swim 120 feet.  
**Arrive** _I Am the Tide_ If the warden is summoned in a body of water, it creates a churning whirlpool that deals 10d6 bludgeoning damage to all creatures within 30 feet ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails this save is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pulled]] 15 feet closer to the warden (30 feet on a critical failure). If the warden is summoned outside of a body of water, it creates a wave of water that deals 8d6 bludgeoning damage to all creatures within 30 feet ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save). A creature that fails this save is pushed 10 feet away from the warden.  
**Depart** _Acclimation to the Deep_ The warden affects you and your allies within 60 feet with [[Spells/Feet to Fins (Player Core)|_feet to fins_]] and [[Spells/Water Breathing (Player Core)|_water breathing_]]. The duration for both spells is reduced to 3 rounds, but a creature affected by _feet to fins_ retains its normal land Speed.

- **[[Bestiary/Warden of Peaks and Skies]]** Speed 60 feet, fly 240 feet.  
**Arrive** _Precipice Plummet_ The warden appears, flying downward from the sky and sends a shockwave where she lands. Creatures within 20 feet take 10d6 sonic damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). Creatures that critically fail their saves are [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  
**Depart** ([[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|auditory]]) _Song of the Skies_ The warden affects you and all your allies within 60 feet with [[Spells/Fly (Player Core)|_fly_]] and [[Spells/Unfettered Movement|_unfettered movement_]]. The duration for both spells is reduced to 3 rounds.
