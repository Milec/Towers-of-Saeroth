# Living Graveyard single action  *Focus 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Necromancer]], [[Traits/Thrall]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 183

**Range** 120 feet

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure forth a walking bulk of graves and tombstones held together by the buried dead. You create a Gargantuan thrall within range. When the _living graveyard_ appears, it violently shakes. All creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or fall [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. Additionally, five thralls (using the base thrall statistics) fall from their graves into empty spaces adjacent to the _living graveyard_. This thrall has 400 Hit Points and a Speed of 60 feet. Whenever this thrall [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Strides]], it creates 5 thralls in empty spaces along the path it Strides. The living graveyard’s Strikes have a reach of 15 feet and deal bludgeoning damage. When you use _thrall charge_ to order the living graveyard to [[Actions/Strike (Player Core)|Strike]], the Strike deals an additional 3d6 damage.
