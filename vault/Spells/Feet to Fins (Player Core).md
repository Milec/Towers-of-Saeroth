**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Besmara (Divine Mysteries)|Besmara]], [[Setting/Deities/Dagon (Divine Mysteries)|Dagon]], [[Setting/Deities/Jin Li (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Jin Li]], [[Setting/Deities/Picoperi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Picoperi]], [[Setting/Deities/Ragadahn (Divine Mysteries)|Ragadahn]], [[Setting/Deities/Seafarer's Hope]], [[Setting/Deities/Sobek (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Sobek]], [[Setting/Deities/Surveyors of the Deep]], [[Setting/Deities/Telastmar]], [[Setting/Deities/Ylimancha (Divine Mysteries)|Ylimancha]], [[Setting/Deities/Zeaki]]

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of the Shark]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

The target's feet transform into fins, improving mobility in the water but reducing it on land. The target gains a swim Speed equal to its normal land Speed, but its land Speed becomes 5 feet.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The spell lasts until your next daily preparations.
