# Dust Storm two actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 114

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Dust]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot-radius burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call forth a swirling storm of dust. The _dust storm_ obscures vision, with the effect of [[Spells/Obscuring Mist|_obscuring mist_]]. The dust also makes the air unbreathable; creatures in the area must [[Rules/Drowning and Suffocation|hold their breath]], though wearing a scarf or similar clothing over the mouth increases the time the creature can hold its breath to 5 rounds. Creatures entering or starting their turn in the _dust storm_ take 1d6 slashing damage. Creatures that have the [[Traits/Water|water]] trait or that are made primarily of liquid take double damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
