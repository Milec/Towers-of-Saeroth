# Continual Flame three actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]], [[Traits/Light]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 326

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Cost** 6 gp of ruby dust

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 object

**Duration** unlimited

---

A magical flame springs up from the object, as bright as a torch. It doesn't need oxygen, react to water, or generate heat.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The cost increases as follows: 16 gp for 3rd level; 30 gp for 4th, 60 gp for 5th, 120 gp for 6th; 270 gp for 7th, 540 gp for 8th, 1,350 gp for 9th, and 3,350 gp for 10th.
