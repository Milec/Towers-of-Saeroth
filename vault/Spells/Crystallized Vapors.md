# Crystallized Vapors two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Aura (Player Core)|Aura]], [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 130

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 15-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]]

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You freeze the precipitation around you in icy pellets, creating a 15-foot emanation of [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]]; you're immune to this difficult terrain. You can launch a ball of ice whenever you Cast this Spell and the first time you Sustain this spell on subsequent turns, making a ranged spell attack against the AC of a creature within 30 feet. On a hit, you deal 1d12 cold damage and 1d12 bludgeoning damage (or double damage on a critical hit).

---
**Heightened (+3)** The damage increases by 1d12 cold and 1d12 bludgeoning.
