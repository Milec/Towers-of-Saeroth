# Ancestral Touch single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cursebound]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 230

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Ancestors]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You touch a creature and force them to see and feel the ancestors surrounding you. The target takes 1d4 mental damage, with results depending on a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] and takes full damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target is frightened 2 and takes double damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The mental damage increases by 1d4.
