# Rip the Spirit single action to three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 126

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You supernaturally rip the spirit from a living creature's body, dooming the target to pain and death. The target takes 5d6 negative damage, depending on its basic Fortitude save, and is drained 1 if it fails its save. The spell's effect is based on how many actions you spend when Casting the Spell.

 single action **(somatic)** The spell targets one living creature in range.  
two actions **(somatic, verbal)** The spell targets one living creature in range and the damage is 10d6 negative instead of 5d6.  
three actions **(material, somatic, verbal)** The spell targets all living creatures in a 30-foot emanation.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of damage increases by 1d6, or by 2d6 for the 2-action version.
