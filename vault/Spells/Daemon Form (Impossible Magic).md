# Daemon Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 130

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You infuse yourself with the corrupting death and pure malevolence of [[Setting/Planes/Abaddon (GM Core)|Abaddon]], transforming into a Large daemon battle form. You must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. When you Cast this Spell, you choose lacridaemon, leukodaemon, meladaemon, or venedaemon. The battle form is Medium if you choose lacridaemon or venedaemon. While in this form, you gain the [[Traits/Daemon (Player Core)|daemon]], [[Traits/Fiend (Player Core)|fiend]], and [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use manipulate actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 21 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]] and [[Rules/Weakness|weakness]] 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- A +2 status bonus to all saving throws against [[Traits/Death (Player Core)|death]] effects.
- Resistance 10 to [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|poison]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +21, and your damage bonus is +12. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the type of Daemon you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Lacridaemon]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; resistance 10 to cold; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8 piercing plus 1d6 cold and 1d4 poison; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d6 slashing plus 1d6 cold.
- **[[Bestiary/Leukodaemon (Monster Core)|Leukodaemon]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; +2 status bonus to saves against diseases; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d10 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 slashing; **Ranged** single action composite longbow ([[Traits/Deadly (Player Core)|deadly d10]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Range|range increment 100 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Volley (Player Core)|volley]]), **Damage** 2d8 piercing.
- **[[Bestiary/Meladaemon (Monster Core 2)|Meladaemon]]** Speed 25 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 40 feet; **Melee** single action jaws ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d10 piercing; **Melee** single action claw ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Void|void]]), **Damage** 1d6 slashing plus 1d6 void, and after a successful Strike, you can spend an action to attempt to [[Bestiary/Abilities/Grab (Rage of Elements)|Grab]] the target.
- **[[Bestiary/Venedaemon]]** Speed 25 feet; sense magical items and active spells within 60 feet as an [[Rules/Senses (Player Core)|imprecise sense]]; **Melee** single action tentacle ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d6 bludgeoning.
