# Share Vision single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 297

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Choir Politic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Your patron temporarily links your vision with that of another, causing both of you to be able to see through each other’s eyes. For the duration of the hex, the two of you share any vision-based senses (such as [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision (Player Core)|darkvision]] or [[Rules/Low-Light Vision (Player Core)|low-light vision]]) and gain a +2 status bonus to Perception checks, but if either of you are [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]], you both are.

If you cast _share vision_ while a previous casting of this hex is still in effect, the previous effect ends.
