# Hidebound ⤾  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Howl of the Wild]] pg. 85

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Iapholi]]

**Trigger** A creature within range is hit with a Strike that deals physical damage.

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 round

---

The target's skin erupts in thick hide or dense scales. It gains resistance 5 to physical damage, except [[Equipment/Adamantine (GM Core)|adamantine]], until the beginning of its next turn.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 3.
