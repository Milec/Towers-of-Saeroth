# Deity's Strike two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Force (Player Core)|Force]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 131

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Requirements** You have a deity.

**Range** 500 feet

**Target** 1 creature (see text)

**Defense**
AC

---

A manifested version of your deity's favored weapon made of pure magical might appears in the air above the target. This weapon attacks using your spell attack modifier. On a hit, the weapon deals 7d12 force damage, or double damage on a critical hit.

After the attack, whether it succeeds or fails, divine energy bursts from the weapon in a shock wave. It emits in a 10-foot-wide, 30-foot-long [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] starting from the target and continuing in a direction you choose. Your target and any other creatures within the area of the spell take 5d6 spirit damage, with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (9th)** The force damage increases by 1d12, and the spirit damage increases by 1d6.
