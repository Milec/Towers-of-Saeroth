# Discern Secrets single action  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cantrip]], [[Traits/Divination]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 238

**Patron Theme**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Patrons/Rune]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You call upon your patron's power to better uncover secrets. When you Cast the Spell, the target can [[Actions/Recall Knowledge]], [[Actions/Seek]], or [[Actions/Sense Motive]]. The target gains a +1 status bonus to the skill or Perception used for the roll, and this bonus remains as long as you Sustain the Spell. The target is temporarily immune to _discern secrets_ for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (5th)** You can target two creatures instead of one.
