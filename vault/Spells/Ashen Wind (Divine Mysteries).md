# Ashen Wind two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 286

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 10-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You conjure a swirling wind of ashes that blows through the area. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save or get the choking ash in its lungs.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened]] 1.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is sickened 2.
