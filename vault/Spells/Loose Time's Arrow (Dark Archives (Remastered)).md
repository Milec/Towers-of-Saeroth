[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

You pluck the time stream like a bow—pull one string back, release, and watch a creature fly. All affected targets are [[Rules/Conditions/Quickened (Player Core)|quickened]]. They can use the extra action only to Step or Stride.
