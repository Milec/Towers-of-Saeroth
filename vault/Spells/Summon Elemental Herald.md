# Summon Elemental Herald three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 222

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Spell Lists**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Elemental]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

Your meditation into the different elemental planes connects you to them deeply, and this connection allows you to summon a herald of an [[Setting/Deity Categories/Elemental Lords|elemental lord]]. The herald arrives in the form of a natural disaster centralized on your enemies. The herald occupies the space of a Huge creature. When you Cast this Spell, choose one of the elemental heralds below to summon; if you're a divine spellcaster who worships an elemental lord, you must choose the option matching your deity's element. This spell gains the trait ([[Traits/Air|air]], [[Traits/Earth|earth]], [[Traits/Fire|fire]], [[Traits/Metal|metal]], [[Traits/Water|water]], or [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) matching your choice.

- **Air** Speed fly 60 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Air|air]]) _Supercell Front_ The herald of air arrives in the form of a twisting tornado, with high-speed winds slamming into your enemies. Each enemy in a 100-foot emanation takes 10d8 bludgeoning damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save; **Depart** ([[Traits/Air|air]]) _Twister Downdraft_ Each enemy in a 60-foot line must attempt a Fortitude save. A creature that fails is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]] if it's on the ground or descends 40 feet if it's in the air. A creature that descends takes [[Rules/Falling|falling damage]] if this forced movement brings it to the ground.
- **Earth** Speed 50 feet, burrow 30 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Earth|earth]]) _Tectonic Rise_ The herald of earth erupts from the ground, splitting it open in a 50-foot emanation. Each enemy in the emanation takes 10d6 slashing damage from the flying debris with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. Enemies who critically fail [[Rules/Falling|fall]] 30 feet into the crevasse, taking falling damage (the sides of the fissure require successful DC 15 [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] checks to [[Actions/Climb]]); **Depart** ([[Traits/Sonic|sonic]]) _Plate Crash_ The herald of earth sinks back into the ground, and the sides of the crevasse slam together with a deafening blow, dealing 5d10 sonic damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save to all enemies in a 30-foot emanation. An enemy that critically fails is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened|deafened]] for 10 minutes.
- **Fire** Speed 60 feet, fly 40 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) _Magma Flow_ The herald of fire flows into the area as living magma, dealing 10d6 fire damage and 3d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent fire damage]] to all enemies in a 60-foot emanation with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save; **Depart** ([[Traits/Fire|fire]]) _Flame Vortex_ Each enemy in a 60-foot line takes 6d6 fire damage and 3d6 persistent fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save as the fire herald whips into a fire pillar before vanishing.
- **Metal** Speed 50 feet, fly 50 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Metal|metal]]) _Shard Cloud_ The herald of metal arrives as a cloud of metal shards and static electricity, dealing 4d8 slashing damage and 4d12 electricity damage to all enemies in a 50-foot emanation with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save; **Depart** ([[Traits/Metal|metal]]) _Rust Mist_ The shards in the cloud rapidly rust and cling to enemies in the 50-foot emanation, dealing 10d6 damage to metal creatures and objects in the area with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save, ignoring Hardness. Unattended objects automatically critically fail this save.
- **Water** Speed 40 feet, swim 60 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Water|water]]) _Hurricane Formation_ The herald of water forms as a miniature hurricane. Each enemy in a 60-foot emanation takes 4d10 bludgeoning damage from the slamming rains and 4d10 cold damage from the falling hail with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save; **Depart** ([[Traits/Cold|cold]], [[Traits/Water|water]]) _Cataclysmic Deluge_ The hurricane collapses into a rushing flood, dealing 6d8 bludgeoning damage to all enemies in a 120-foot cone with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that fails its save is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 20 feet away from the herald (or 40 feet on a critical failure).
- **Wood** Speed 50 feet, burrow 30 feet; **Arrive** ([[Traits/Plant|plant]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) _Advantageous Roots_ The herald of wood bursts forth from the ground as a complex root system. Each enemy in a 50-foot emanation takes 6d10 piercing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save as the roots skewer its limbs and body. Each enemy that fails its save takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speeds until the herald departs; **Depart** ([[Traits/Plant|plant]], [[Traits/Wood|wood]]) _Retreat to Soil_ The roots retreat into the ground, dealing 6d10 piercing damage to each enemy in a 50-foot emanation with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. Any creature with a penalty to Speeds from its initial save automatically takes 2d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]]. The ground in the emanation is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] after the roots retreat.
