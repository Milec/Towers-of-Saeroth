# Flashy Disappearance two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Illusion]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 89

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

---

You create a puff of colorful smoke that quickly disperses while you become temporarily [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible|invisible]]. You become [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected|undetected]] to all creatures that rely on visual senses unless they can see invisible creatures. You Stride. At the end of your movement, if you have [[Rules/Cover|cover, greater cover]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealment]], attempt a [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] check to [[Actions/Hide]]. You gain a +2 status bonus to this Stealth check. The invisibility then ends, and you either become [[Rules/Conditions/Observed|observed]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Hidden|hidden]] to creatures as determined by your check to Hide, if you made one.
