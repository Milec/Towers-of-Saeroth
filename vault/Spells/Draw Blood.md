# Draw Blood two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack (Player Core)|Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 133

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
AC

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The palm of your hand grows a mouth with impossibly long fangs that ache to drink your enemies' blood. Make a melee spell attack against a target. On a hit, you deal 4d4 piercing damage and 2 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] (or double damage on a critical hit). For the duration of the spell, you regain 2 Hit Points each time the target takes bleed damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage and healing increase by 1.
