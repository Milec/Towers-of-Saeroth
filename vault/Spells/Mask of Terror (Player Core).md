# Mask of Terror two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 342

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Companions/Draconic Exemplars/Arcane|Draconic]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Grask Uldeth]], [[Setting/Deities/Jaidz (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Jaidz]], [[Setting/Deities/Urgathoa (Divine Mysteries)|Urgathoa]], [[Setting/Deities/Yamasoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Ydersius (Divine Mysteries)|Ydersius]], [[Setting/Deities/Zura (Pathfinder -214- The Broken Palace)|Zura]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target appears to be a gruesome and terrifying creature. The effect is unique to each observer, so a human viewing the target might see a [[Bestiary/Families/Demon|demon]] with bloody fangs, but a demon observing the target might see a glowing [[Bestiary/Families/Angel|angelic]] visage.

 When any creature attempts a [[Rules/Hostile Actions|hostile action]] against the target, the creature must attempt a Will save. It is then temporarily immune until the end of its next turn.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]] before using its action.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes frightened 2, and its action fails and is wasted.

---
**Heightened (8th)** You can target up to 5 creatures. If a creature uses a hostile action or reaction that affects multiple targets simultaneously, it needs to attempt only one save against _mask of terror_.
