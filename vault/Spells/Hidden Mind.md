# Hidden Mind two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 335

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Nyarlathotep (The Faceless Sphinx)]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** until your next daily preparations

---

Powerful wards hide a creature from magic that would spy on it or affect its mind. The target gains a +4 status bonus to saves against [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effects. Hidden mind attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] any [[Traits/Detection|detection]], [[Traits/Revelation|revelation]], and [[Traits/Scrying|scrying]] effects as if its spell rank were 1 higher than its actual rank. On a success, the effect functions normally except that it detects nothing about the target and its possessions. For instance, [[Spells/Detect Magic|_detect magic_]] would still detect other magic in the area, but not any magic on the target.
