# Eagle's Cry two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Shining Kingdoms]] pg. 53

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters from Andoran have access to this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 30-foot [[Rules/Cone (Player Core)|cone]]

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You let out the mighty cry of a majestic eagle that pierces eardrums. This cry deals 4d8 sonic damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 1.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is frightened 2.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is frightened 2, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Fleeing (Player Core)|fleeing]] for 1 round.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d8.
