# Pushing Gust two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 372

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Air (Player Core)|Air]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Giving the air a push, you buffet the target with a powerful gust of wind; it must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 5 feet away from you.  

**Failure** The target is pushed 10 feet away from you.  

**Critical Failure** The target is pushed 10 feet away from you and knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].
