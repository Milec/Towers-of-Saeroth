# Blink two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Conjuration]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 321

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Black Butterfly (Divine Mysteries)|Black Butterfly]], [[Setting/Deities/Guardians of the Sacred Self]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Ng (Divine Mysteries)|Ng]], [[Setting/Deities/Phi Deva]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You blink quickly between the Material Plane and the Ethereal Plane. You gain resistance 5 to all damage (except force). You can Sustain the Spell to vanish and reappear 10 feet away in a random direction determined by the GM; the movement doesn't trigger reactions. At the end of your turn, you vanish and reappear as above.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 3.
