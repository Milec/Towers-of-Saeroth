# Hag's Fruit  *Spell 6*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -182- Graveclaw|Pathfinder #182: Graveclaw]] pg. 78

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 1 minute ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 non- magical  fruit

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 day

---

You imbue a single piece of fruit with a curse that leaves its victims in a magical sleep. The first person to eat the fruit within the spell's duration is subject to the Curse of Slumber, using your spell DC as the saving throw DC of the curse.

 **Curse of Slumber** ([[Traits/Curse|curse]], [[Traits/Incapacitation|incapacitation]], [[Traits/Necromancy|necromancy]], [[Traits/Sleep|sleep]]) **Level** 11; **Effect** You fall asleep for 1 round (or permanently on a critical failure) and seem to be dead; a creature must succeed at a DC 30 [[Rules/Skills/Medicine]] check to realize you're alive. Noise doesn't awaken you, but taking damage gives you a new saving throw against the curse.
