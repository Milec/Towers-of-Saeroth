# Begone single action  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 124

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

With but a touch, a mystical force sends a foe rocketing backward. The target creature must attempt a Fortitude save. If the creature's movement causes it to hit a firm surface, such as a wall, it takes 5 bludgeoning damage. A creature knocked off a ledge or the like can [[Actions/Grab an Edge (Player Core)|Grab an Edge]] to avoid falling.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is pushed 5 feet away from you.  

**Failure** The creature is pushed 15 feet away from you.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is pushed 30 feet away from you.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The bludgeoning damage increases by 5.
