You call on Cobyslarni to open the target’s mind to knowledge—too much knowledge. The target’s thoughts flood with irrelevant and confusing information, causing it to become [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 1 if it fails a Will save (or stupefied 2 on a critical failure).
