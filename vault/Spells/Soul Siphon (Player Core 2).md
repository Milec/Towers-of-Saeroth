# Soul Siphon single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 258

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Bones (Player Core 2)|Bones]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You siphon off a portion of the target's soul to bolster your own vitality. The target must attempt a Fortitude save or take 1d4 void damage. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the total amount of HP the target loses, including the HP lost from becoming [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]].

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and becomes drained 2.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
