# Cloak of Colors two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 242

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Chaldira (Divine Mysteries)|Chaldira]], [[Setting/Deities/Falayna (Pathfinder -218- Titanbane)|Falayna]], [[Setting/Deities/Pulura (Divine Mysteries)|Pulura]], [[Setting/Deities/Sturovenen (Divine Mysteries)|Sturovenen]], [[Setting/Deities/The Resplendent Court]], [[Setting/Deities/Thisamet (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Thisamet]], [[Setting/Deities/Turvu]], [[Setting/Deities/Ussharassim]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

A cloak of swirling colors shrouds the target. Creatures are [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] while adjacent to it, and attacking the target causes a brilliant flash of light. A creature that hits the target with a melee attack must attempt a Will save. The creature is then temporarily immune until the end of its turn; this effect has the [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|incapacitation]] trait.

**Success** The attacker is unaffected.  

**Failure** The attacker is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The attacker is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned (Player Core)|stunned]] for 1 round.
