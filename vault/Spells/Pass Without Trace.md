# Pass Without Trace two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 356

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Dachzerul]], [[Setting/Deities/Kalekot (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Kalekot]], [[Setting/Deities/Kelinahat]], [[Setting/Deities/Ketephys (Divine Mysteries)|Ketephys]], [[Setting/Deities/Ng (Divine Mysteries)|Ng]], [[Setting/Deities/Skode (Divine Mysteries)|Skode]], [[Setting/Deities/Tanagaar (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Tanagaar]], [[Setting/Deities/Ulon]], [[Setting/Deities/Zarongel]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You obscure the tracks you leave behind and make it harder for others to find you. The DC of checks to Track you gains a +4 status bonus or is equal to your spell DC, whichever results in a higher DC. You can benefit from only one _pass without trace spell_ at a time.

---
**Heightened (2nd)** The duration increases to 8 hours.

**Heightened (4th)** The duration increases to 8 hours. The spell has a range of 20 feet and an area of a 20-foot-emanation, affecting up to 10 creatures of your choice within that area.
