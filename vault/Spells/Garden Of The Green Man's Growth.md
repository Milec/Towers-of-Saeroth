# Garden Of The Green Man's Growth three actions  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Rare (Player Core)|Rare]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate (Howl of the Wild)|Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mythic]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 155

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You cast your magic into the earth, calling out to a powerful [[Setting/Deities/Green Man|green man]], a verdant lesser deity of nature, to come to your aid. They occupy the space of a Medium creature, have a Speed of 40 feet, and a climb Speed of 40 feet.

**Arrive** ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]) _Verdant Bloom_ The green man erupts from the ground in a burst of lush growth, dealing 10d8 bludgeoning damage to creatures in a 60-foot emanation with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save. Creatures that fail this save are [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 30 feet away from the green man and are knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]]. The area becomes [[Rules/Difficult Terrain (Player Core)|greater difficult terrain]] for 24 hours.

**Depart** ([[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|plant]]) _Forest of Grasping Vines_ The green man casts out their arms, causing vines to rise from the ground, lash at up to six different creatures, and coil them up. Each of these vines targets a different enemy within 100 feet of the green man and deals 12d6 slashing damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save. A creature that fails is [[Rules/Conditions/Grabbed (Player Core)|grabbed]] until it [[Actions/Escape (Player Core)|Escapes]] (on a critical failure it is [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] until it Escapes). The Escape DC is your spell DC. Each creature that begins its turn grabbed or [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]] by these vines takes an additional 4d6 bludgeoning damage, as the vines continue to squeeze the life from it.
