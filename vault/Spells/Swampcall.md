# Swampcall two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 77

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** 10-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You call upon the spirits of the soil to twist and churn, transforming the terrain in the targeted area into a sodden mess. The area becomes difficult terrain. Creatures in the area when you Cast this Spell must attempt a Reflex saving throw.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature sinks partially into the mud. The creature takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds (except for its swim Speed, if any) and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]]. These effects last until the creature leaves the area or until the end of its next turn, whichever comes first.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the penalty to Speeds (except Swim speed) is –15 feet.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The range increases to 60 feet, and the area increases to a 20-foot burst.
