# Shifting Sand three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 110

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Anubis (Divine Mysteries)|Anubis]], [[Setting/Deities/Yamatsumi (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Yamatsumi]], [[Setting/Deities/Zipacna]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Area** a horizontal earthen or sandy surface within a 20-foot burst

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You cause the surface to heave. The area becomes difficult terrain and any tracks in the area are destroyed by the churning ground. Creatures standing in the area take a –1 status penalty to [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] checks to [[Actions/Balance]] and [[Actions/Tumble Through]] and Athletics checks to [[Actions/High Jump]] and [[Actions/Long Jump]]. The first time each round you Sustain the Spell, you can move the churning area up to 10 feet in any direction. Creatures [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] by the spell are carried along with the shifting sand in the same direction, if possible; this movement is [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]]. Creatures that enter or begin their turn standing in the shifting sand must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and ignores the area’s difficult terrain and penalty to skill checks until the end of its turn.  

**Success** The creature ignores the area’s penalty to skill checks until the end of its turn.  

**Failure** The creature is affected normally by the spell this turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized|immobilized]] within the spell’s area until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]]. If the creature was already immobilized by shifting sands, it also falls [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].

---
**Heightened (5th)** The status penalty increases to –2 and the spell’s range increases to 60 feet.

**Heightened (7th)** The status penalty increases to –3, the spell’s range increases to 60 feet, and the spell’s area increases to a 30-foot burst.

**Heightened (9th)** The status penalty increases to –4, the spell’s range increases to 60 feet, and the spell’s area increases to a 40-foot burst.
