# Bind Soul two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Evil]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 320

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Requirements** black sapphire with a gp value of at least the target's level × 100

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature that died within the last minute

**Duration** unlimited

---

You wrench the target's soul away before it can pass on to the afterlife and imprison it in a black sapphire.

 While the soul is in the gem, the target can't be returned to life through any means, even powerful magic such as [[Spells/Wish|_wish_]]. If the gem is destroyed or _bind soul_ is counteracted on the gem, the soul is freed. The gem has AC 16 and Hardness 10. A gem can't hold more than one soul, and any attempt wastes the spell.
