# Painful Vibrations two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 156

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You send powerful sound waves through an opponent’s body, vibrating its bones and internal organs painfully. The creature takes 8d6 sonic damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full damage, is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]], and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is sickened 2, and is deafened for 1 minute.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
