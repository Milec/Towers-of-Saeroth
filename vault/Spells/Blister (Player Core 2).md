# Blister two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 241

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic (Player Core 2)|Demonic]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Fandarra (Divine Mysteries)|Fandarra]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You point at a target in range, and its skin grows searing blisters filled with caustic fluid. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target grows one blister. You can spend a single action, which has the [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|concentrate]] trait, to pop a blister. The target and each creature in a 15-foot cone originating from the target takes 7d6 acid damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). You choose the direction of the cone, which can't include the target. When no blisters are left, the spell ends.  

**Failure** As success, but the target grows two blisters.  

**Critical Failure** As success, but the target grows four blisters.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage of a popped blister increases by 1d6.
