# Rejuvenating Flames two actions  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Fists of the Ruby Phoenix

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -168- King of the Mountain|Pathfinder #168: King of the Mountain]] pg. 74

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Phoenix]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex

---

You create a gout of flame that both heals and burns. You restore 1d4 HP to all allies in the area, and those allies gain a +1 status bonus to Fortitude saves for 1 minute. Enemies in the area take 1d4 fire damage with a basic Reflex save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** You restore an additional 1d4 HP to your allies and deal an additional 1d4 fire damage to enemies.
