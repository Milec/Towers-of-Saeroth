# Tremorsense two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Earth]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 97

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You become aware of vibrations in the ground around you. You gain [[Bestiary/Abilities/Tremorsense|tremorsense]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise]] sense with a range of 30 feet. As normal for tremorsense, you can detect a creature only if it's on the same surface as you and only if the subject is moving along (or burrowing through) the surface.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The spell's duration is 1 hour.

**Heightened (5th)** The spell's duration is 8 hours.
