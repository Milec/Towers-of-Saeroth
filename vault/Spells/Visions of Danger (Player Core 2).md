# Visions of Danger three actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 254

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Fey (Player Core 2)|Fey]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 30-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

An illusion of horrific creatures fills the spell's area. The creatures look like Tiny swarming monsters with a specific appearance of your choice, such as fiendish flies or animated saw blades. The burst deals 8d8 mental damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will save to each creature that's inside the burst when it's created, enters the burst, or starts its turn inside the burst. A creature that critically succeeds at its Will save can immediately attempt to disbelieve the illusion. A creature that tries to Interact with the monsters or observes one with a Seek action can attempt to disbelieve the illusion. Creatures that disbelieve the illusion take no damage from the illusion thereafter.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The mental damage increases by 1d8.
