# Divine Plagues two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 268

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Plague (Divine Mysteries)|Plague]]

**Range** 15 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Your deity sends wracking plagues. The target must attempt a Fortitude save. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]], decide whether the disease inflicts the [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] condition.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  
**Failure** The target is afflicted with stage 1 divine plague.  
**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with stage 2 divine plague.

**Divine Plague** (disease) **Level 1**; Stage 1 clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed (Player Core)|slowed]] 1 and clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 (1 round); **Stage 3** clumsy 1, enfeebled 1, or stupefied 1 and the target can’t recover from the condition until they are cured (1 day)

---
**Heightened (5th)** The divine plague disease gains the [[Traits/Virulent (GM Core)|virulent]] trait. The condition value for the clumsy, enfeebled, or stupefied condition is 2 instead of 1.
