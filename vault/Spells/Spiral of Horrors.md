**Area** 30-foot emanation

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

Shades and spirits howl and whirl around you in a display that strikes fear into the hearts of all who witness it. Enemies in the area are [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 1]] and can't reduce their frightened value below 1 for the spell's duration.
