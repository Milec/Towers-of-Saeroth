# Redact two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 266

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Glyph (Divine Mysteries)|Glyph]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** unattended written materials of up to 1 Bulk

**Duration** 1 day

---

You strike mention of a specific name from one or more documents. When you [[Actions/Cast a Spell (Player Core)|Cast the Spell]], give one proper name, be it a creature, location, or object. Any mention of that name within the document becomes blurred, smudged, or otherwise completely illegible. Epithets and unambiguous references to the name are redacted as well; for instance, redacting the name of the goddess [[Setting/Deities/Sarenrae (Divine Mysteries)|Sarenrae]] from a book would also remove references to her title “the Dawnflower.” Attempts to use this reference material to [[Rules/Recall Knowledge (GM Core)|Recall Knowledge]] about the given proper name automatically fail.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** Instead of striking out a given name, you can replace it with a different name you choose. Epithets and references are substituted with similar ones relating to the replacement name. Anyone reading the text can attempt a Perception or Society check against your spell DC to notice the altered text, though that doesn’t tell them what the original said.

**Heightened (4th)** At your choice, the duration is unlimited.
