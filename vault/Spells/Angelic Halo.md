# Angelic Halo single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Aura]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Good]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 402

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 15-foot emanation

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You gain an angelic halo with an aura that increases allies' healing from the [[Spells/Heal|_heal_]] spell. Allies in your halo's emanation who are healed by a _heal_ spell gain a status bonus to Hit Points regained equal to double the _heal_ spell's level.
