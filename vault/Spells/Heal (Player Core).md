# Heal single action to three actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 335

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Angelic (Player Core 2)|Angelic]]

**Range** varies

**Target** 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature

---

You channel vital energy to heal the living or damage the [[Traits/Undead|undead]]. If the target is a willing living creature, you restore 1d8 Hit Points. If the target is undead, you deal that amount of vitality damage to it, and it gets a basic Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines its targets, range, area, and other parameters.

 single action The spell has a range of touch.  
 two actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) The spell has a range of 30 feet. If you're healing a living creature, increase the Hit Points restored by 8.  
 three actions ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]]) You disperse vital energy in a 30-foot emanation. This targets all living and undead creatures in the burst.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing or damage increases by 1d8, and the extra healing for the 2-action version increases by 8.
