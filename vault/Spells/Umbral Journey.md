**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Camazotz]], [[Setting/Deities/Count Ranalc (Divine Mysteries)|Count Ranalc]], [[Setting/Deities/Enkaar]], [[Setting/Deities/Eyes That Watch]], [[Setting/Deities/Grasping Iovett]], [[Setting/Deities/Husk]], [[Setting/Deities/Lady Razor]], [[Setting/Deities/Luhar (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Luhar]], [[Setting/Deities/Reshmit of the Heavy Voice]], [[Setting/Deities/Thalaphyrr]], [[Setting/Deities/Zon-Kuthon (Divine Mysteries)|Zon-Kuthon]]

**Cast** 1 minute

**Range** touch

**Target** you and up to 10 willing creatures touched

**Duration** 8 hours

---

You move partially into the Netherworld, using its warped nature to speed your travels. Each hour, you cover roughly as much ground as you [[Rules/Travel Speed (Player Core)|normally would in 3 days]]. The landmarks are vague and symbolic rather than concrete, leaving you within a mile of your intended destination when you Dismiss the spell or its duration ends.
