# Conductive Weapon two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 142

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Cixyron]], [[Setting/Deities/Ferrumnestra (Divine Mysteries)|Ferrumnestra]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 metal weapon that is unattended or attended by a willing creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You channel powerful electric current through the metal of a weapon, zapping anyone the item hits. The target becomes a _+1 [[Equipment/Shock|shock]]_ weapon. If any target of an attack with the weapon is wearing metal armor or is primarily made of metal, the electricity damage die from the _shock_ rune is 1d12.
