# Face in the Crowd single action  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 372

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Cities (Player Core)|Cities]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

While in a crowd of roughly similar creatures, your appearance becomes bland and nondescript. You gain a +2 status bonus to [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] and [[Rules/Sidebars/Being Stealthy|Stealth]] checks to go incognito among the crowd, and you ignore [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] caused by the crowd. This counts as setting up a disguise for the [[Actions/Impersonate]] use of Deception and you add your level even if you're untrained.

---
**Heightened (3rd)** The spell gains a range of 10 feet and can target up to 10 creatures.
