# Stormburst two actions  *Spell 5*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Electricity]], [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -171- Hurricane's Howl|Pathfinder #171: Hurricane's Howl]] pg. 77

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Area** 15-foot burst

**Saving Throw**
Reflex

---

Your voice projects like cracking thunder as you summon a localized storm, creating a fearsome surge of lightning and wind that deals 6d6 electricity damage. Each creature must attempt a Reflex saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is knocked [[Rules/Conditions/Prone|prone]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage, is knocked prone, and is [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned 1]].

---
**Heightened (7th)** The damage increases to 8d6, and targets who critically fail are stunned 2.

**Heightened (9th)** The damage increases to 10d6, and targets who critically fail are stunned 3.
