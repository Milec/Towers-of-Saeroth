# Frozen Fog two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 86

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You conjure a chilling mist that settles into the bones and makes it difficult to move. This has the effects of [[Spells/Mist|_mist_]], except that you can [[Actions/Sustain an Effect|Sustain]] the spell once per round to move the fog up to 10 feet. A creature that starts its turn in the area takes 6d8 cold damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude save. A creature that fails its save is also [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1 for 1 minute (clumsy 2 on a critical failure) and takes a –10-foot status penalty to Speeds for as long as it’s clumsy.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 3d8.
