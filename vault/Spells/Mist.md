[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** 1 minute

You call forth a cloud of mist. All creatures within the mist become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and all creatures outside the mist become concealed to creatures within it. You can Dismiss the cloud.
