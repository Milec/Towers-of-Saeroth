# Spiritual Anamnesis two actions  *Spell 4*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Age of Ashes

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Necromancy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -147- Tomorrow Must Burn|Pathfinder #147: Tomorrow Must Burn]] pg. 74

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Saving Throw**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

Memories of a past life that seem incredibly real (and may even be) overwhelm the target’s mind. This is especially disorienting for undead and creatures that have lived a previous life (such as celestials, fiends, monitors, and many other extraplanar creatures); if such creatures roll a critical success on their save, they get a success instead, and if they roll a failure, they get a critical failure instead.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Slowed|slowed]] 1 for 1 round.  

**Failure** The target is slowed 1 for 1 minute and babbles about the visions, possibly providing information about what they saw.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is also [[Rules/Conditions/Stunned|stunned]] 3.
