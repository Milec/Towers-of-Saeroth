# Rust Cloud three actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Metal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Rage of Elements]] pg. 145

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Ferrumnestra (Divine Mysteries)|Ferrumnestra]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

Red-blown flecks of rust jitter in the air, forming a cloud of agitated particles. All creatures within the cloud become [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed|concealed]], and all creatures outside the cloud become concealed to creatures within it. The cloud deals 5d10 slashing damage to any creature that starts its turn in the area, with a basic Fortitude save.

 Metal that rusts off of a creature adds to the cloud. When a [[Traits/Metal|metal]] creature takes damage from the cloud, the cloud's area increases by 5 feet (to a maximum of a 40-foot burst), and the creature starts to rust, taking 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent slashing]] damage. You can Dismiss the cloud.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The cloud's slashing damage increases by 1d10 and the persistent damage increases by 1d4.
