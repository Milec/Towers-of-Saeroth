# Wall of Wind three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 368

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Anras]], [[Setting/Deities/Breath of the Endless Sky]], [[Setting/Deities/General Susumu (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|General Susumu]], [[Setting/Deities/Horus (Divine Mysteries)|Horus]]

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Snow (Player Core)|Lesson of Snow]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You create a barrier of gusting winds that hinders anything moving through it. The wall of swirling winds is 5 feet thick, 60 feet long, and 30 feet high. The wall stands vertically, but you can shape its path. Though the wall of wind distorts the air, it does not hamper sight. The wall has the following effects.

- Ammunition from physical ranged attacks—such as arrows, bolts, sling bullets, and other objects of similar size—can't pass through the wall. Attacks with bigger ranged weapons, such as [[Equipment/Weapons/Javelin|javelins]], take a –2 circumstance penalty to their attack rolls if their paths pass through the wall. Massive ranged weapons and spell effects that don't create physical objects pass through the wall with no penalty.
- The wall is [[Rules/Difficult Terrain|difficult terrain]] to creatures attempting to move overland through it. Gases, including creatures in [[Spells/Vapor Form|_vapor form_]], can't pass through the wall.
- A creature that attempts to fly through the wall using a [[Traits/Move|move]] action must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature can move through the wall normally this turn.  

**Success** The flying creature can move through the wall this turn, but the wall is difficult terrain.  

**Failure** The wall stops the movement of the flying creature, and any remaining movement from its current action is wasted.  

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is [[Rules/Forced Movement|pushed]] 10 feet away from the wall.
