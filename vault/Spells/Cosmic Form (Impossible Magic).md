# Cosmic Form two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Polymorph (Player Core)|Polymorph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 129

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You recreate your form entirely out of cosmic energy, transforming into a Large battle form. You must have enough space to expand into, or the spell is lost. When you Cast this Spell, choose to take on the powers of the moon or the sun. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 21 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 20 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]].
- [[Rules/Forced Movement|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +25, and your damage bonus is +10. The melee attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the enfeebled condition, for example), and the ranged attacks are Dexterity based. If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Multiple Attacks with Athletics (Player Core)|Athletics]] modifier of +25, unless your own is higher.

You also gain specific abilities based on the stellar body:

- **Moon** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 50 feet; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]]), **Damage** 2d4 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire; **Ranged** single action moonbeam ([[Traits/Range|range 90 feet]]), **Damage** 2d4 fire; this form’s fire damage is silver for resistances, weaknesses, and the like; on a critical hit with either Strike, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied]] 2 for 1 round.
- **Sun** Speed 30 feet, [[Rules/Fly Speed (Player Core)|fly]] 50 feet; **Melee** single action fist ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]]), **Damage** 2d6 fire plus 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire]]; **Ranged** single action sunbeam (range 90 feet), **Damage** 1d6 fire plus 1d6 persistent fire; on a critical hit with either Strike, the target is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round.
