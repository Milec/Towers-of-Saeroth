# Aberrant Whispers single action to three actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Auditory (Player Core)|Auditory]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 262

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant (Player Core 2)|Aberrant]]

**Area** 5-foot emanation or more

**Target** each foe in the area

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 round

---

You utter phrases in an unknown tongue, assaulting the minds of those nearby. Each target must attempt a Will save. Regardless of the result of its save, each target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute. For each additional action you use when Casting the Spell, increase the emanation's radius by 5 feet.

**Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied (Player Core)|stupefied 2]].  

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Confused (Player Core)|confused]].

---
**Heightened (+3)** The initial radius increases by 5 feet.
