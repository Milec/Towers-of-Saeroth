# Restorative Moment two actions  *Focus 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Hex]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Witch (Player Core)|Witch]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 385

**Lesson**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Renewal (Player Core)|Lesson of Renewal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

---

Your patron weaves the threads of time around the target, giving them some of the benefits of a day's passage. The target decreases the values of any [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained]] conditions it has by 1 and can immediately attempt one saving throw against each affliction it has with a stage length of 1 day or less. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 day.
