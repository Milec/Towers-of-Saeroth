# Overstuff two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 376

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Indulgence (Player Core)|Indulgence]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

Huge amounts of food and drink fill the target. It receives a full meal's worth of nourishment and must attempt a Fortitude save. A target [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened|sickened]] by this spell takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed until it's no longer sickened.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is sickened 1, but if it spends an action to end the condition, it succeeds automatically.  

**Failure** The target is sickened 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target is sickened 2.
