**Defense**

You evoke a storm of acid rain that pelts the area for the spell’s duration. A creature that begins its turn in the area takes 3d8 [[Traits/Acid (Player Core)|acid]] damage (basic Reflex save).

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
