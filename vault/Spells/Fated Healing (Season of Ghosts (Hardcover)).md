# Fated Healing two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Season of Ghosts

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Linguistic]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Season of Ghosts (Hardcover)]] pg. 287

_[**PFS Note**](https://2e.aonprd.com/pathfinder-society) Characters must have the appropriate Season of Ghosts boon to access this option._

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** creatures who are  hostile  toward each other

**Duration** 5 rounds

---

You speak about the consequences of actions people take against each other and how it’s possible to break cycles of violence simply by making a different choice. The targets regain 1d4 Hit Points at the end of each of their own turns while the spell is in effect. If a target uses a [[Rules/Hostile Actions (Player Core)|hostile action]] against the other target, the spell ends for the target that used the hostile action.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The targets regain an additional 1d4 Hit Points at the end of their own turns.
