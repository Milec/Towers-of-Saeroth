# Blazing Dive two actions  *Spell 3*

[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

[[Setting/Deities/Mahja Firehair]], [[Setting/Deities/Vonymos]], [[Setting/Deities/Xiquiripat]], [[Setting/Deities/Zursvaater]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex

---

Superheated air collects under you, buoying you high into the sky before you plummet back down in a fiery comet. You Fly 15 feet straight up into the air, then [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]] in a straight [[Rules/Line (Player Core)|line]] to an empty space of your choice on the ground within 60 feet. When you land, blistering air blasts out from you, dealing 3d4 bludgeoning damage and 3d6 fire damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex save) to all creatures in a 10-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4 bludgeoning and 1d6 fire.
