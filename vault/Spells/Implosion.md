# Implosion two actions  *Spell 9*

**Traits** [[Traits/Evocation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Core Rulebook]] pg. 346

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Demonic]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Dahak (Draconic Codex)|Dahak]], [[Setting/Deities/Nulgreth]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 corporeal creature

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

**Duration** sustained up to 1 minute

---

You crush the target by causing it to collapse in on itself, dealing 75 damage. The first time each round you Sustain the Spell, you must choose a new target to be subject to the same effect; the same creature can never be targeted more than once with a single casting of this spell. You also can't affect more than one creature per turn with _implosion_. You can't target a creature that's [[Traits/Incorporeal|incorporeal]], gaseous, or liquid, or otherwise lacking a solid form.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 10.
