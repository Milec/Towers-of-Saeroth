# Life-Giving Form two actions  *Focus 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]], [[Traits/Light (Player Core)|Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 261

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Life (Player Core 2)|Life]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You transcend your physical form, becoming a beacon of healing energy. Your body exudes [[Rules/Bright Light|bright light]] like a [[Equipment/Torch (Player Core)|torch]]. You gain resistance 5 to precision damage and weakness 5 to void damage, and your [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] Strikes deal an extra 1d4 vitality damage.

 You can touch a living creature with an Interact action to restore 6d8 Hit Points to it, and when a creature touches you with an Interact action you can allow it to gain the same healing; either way, the creature becomes temporarily immune to _life-giving form's_ healing for 1 minute. This is a [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] [[Traits/Healing (Player Core)|healing]] effect. You can't heal yourself with life-giving form. A creature harmed by vitality damage (such as an [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]]) that touches you or damages you with an [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack or non-[[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] melee weapon instead takes 3d4 vitality damage, and it doesn't become temporarily immune.

 You can Dismiss this spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The Hit Points restored with an Interact action increases by 1d8, the vitality damage increases by 1, and the resistance and weakness increases by 1.
