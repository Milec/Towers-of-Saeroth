# Rapid Adaptation two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 124

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** your  companion  or  eidolon

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You flood the target with energy from the terrain around it, compressing centuries of evolution into a single moment. The target gains one of the following natural adaptations based on the surrounding environment.

- **Aquatic** The target gains a swim Speed equal to its land Speed. If it already had a swim Speed, it gains a +10-foot status bonus to its swim Speed.
- **Arctic** The target isn't affected by [[Rules/Temperature|severe or extreme cold]], and when it rolls a success on an [[Rules/Skills/Acrobatics]] check to [[Actions/Balance]] on ice and snow, it gets a critical success instead.
- **Desert** The target isn't affected by severe or extreme heat, and when it rolls a success on an Acrobatics check to Balance on sand, it gets a critical success instead.
- **Forest** The target gains [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent|scent]] as an [[Rules/Imprecise Senses|imprecise sense]] with a range of 30 feet.
- **Mountain** The target isn't [[Rules/Conditions/Flat-Footed|flat-footed]] when [[Actions/Climb|Climbing]], and when it rolls a success on an Acrobatics check to Balance on rubble, it gets a critical success instead.
- **Plains** The target gains a +10-foot status bonus to its land Speed.
- **Underground** The target gains [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|darkvision]].
