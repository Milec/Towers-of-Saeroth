# Phantom Prison three actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Incapacitation (Player Core)|Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 157

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Haborym]], [[Setting/Deities/Shivaska]]

**Range** 50 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You completely surround a Large or smaller creature in immobile illusory walls, trapping it inside a false prison it can’t escape. No other creatures see or feel these walls, and the target can’t see anything outside of the illusory walls. The target can attempt a Will save when the spell is cast, each time it attempts to interact with or escape the walls, and each time something from outside the walls affects the target. On a success, it disbelieves the illusion, and the spell ends.

---
**Heightened (8th)** You can target up to five creatures.
