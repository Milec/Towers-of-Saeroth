# Execute two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Death]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 329

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Undead (Player Core 2)|Undead]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aesir]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You point at a creature and invoke the demise of all things. The target takes 70 void damage with a basic Fortitude save. If the target is [[Traits/Undead|undead]] or otherwise has [[Bestiary/Abilities/Negative Healing|void healing]], the spell loses the [[Traits/Death|death]] and [[Traits/Void|void]] traits and gains the [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] trait, and the target takes 70 vitality damage with a basic Fortitude save.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 10.
