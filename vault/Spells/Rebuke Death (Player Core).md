# Rebuke Death single action to three actions  *Focus 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Cleric (Player Core)|Cleric]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 376

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Healing (Player Core)|Healing]]

**Area** 20-foot emanation

**Target** 1 living creature per action spent to Cast this spell

---

You snatch creatures from the jaws of death, restoring them without the strain of a typical close call. You can spend 1 to 3 actions Casting this Spell, and you can target a number of creatures equal to the actions spent. Each target regains 3d6 Hit Points. If the target had the [[Rules/Sidebars/Death and Dying Rules|dying]] condition, coming back from dying due to this healing doesn't increase its [[Rules/Conditions/Wounded|wounded]] condition.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the healing by 1d6.
