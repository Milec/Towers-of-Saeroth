# Stupefy two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 359

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Bloodlines**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Aberrant (Player Core 2)|Aberrant]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|Hag]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Cayden Cailean (Divine Mysteries)|Cayden Cailean]], [[Setting/Deities/Halcamora (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Halcamora]], [[Setting/Deities/Razmir]], [[Setting/Deities/The Lady of the North Star]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

**Duration** varies

---

You dull the target's mind, depending on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied 1]] until the start of your next turn.  

**Failure** The target is stupefied 2 for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 3 for 1 minute.
