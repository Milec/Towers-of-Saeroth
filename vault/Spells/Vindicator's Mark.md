# Vindicator's Mark two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]]

**Source** [[Sources/War of Immortals]] pg. 65

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** your  hunted prey

**Saving Throw**
AC

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You launch a magical dart at your hunted prey, which marks them with a nimbus only you can detect. Make a spell attack against the target. On a hit, you deal 2d4 [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|spirit]] damage and the target is marked by a glowing nimbus of energy that only you can see. For the duration of your spell, the marked target takes an additional +2 damage from all your weapon or unarmed attacks. [[Rules/Conditions/Invisible (Player Core)|Invisible]] targets marked by your _vindicator's mark_ are [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealed]] to you, rather than [[Rules/Conditions/Undetected (Player Core)|undetected]].

You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell on your turn if your last action dealt damage to the target with a weapon or [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack, instantly dealing an additional 2d6 spirit damage to it.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 2d4, the additional damage you deal increases by 1, and the damage dealt when the spell is Dismissed increases by 1d6.
