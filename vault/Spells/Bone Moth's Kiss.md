# Bone Moth's Kiss two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 126

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature with bones or a natural exoskeleton

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

A tiny, bone-white moth emerges from your open mouth. It shakes itself off, spreads its wings, and flies toward your foe, brushing its wings against their exposed skin. The moth carries a disease that causes a victim's bones to quickly grow soft and eventually melt. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The moth only briefly brushes its wings against the target. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]].  

**Failure** The moth lands on the target, kissing the spot with its fluttering. The target is afflicted with the bone moth's kiss at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The moth lands on an open wound, targeting it with its kiss. The target is afflicted with the bone moth's kiss at stage 2.

 **Bone Moth's Kiss** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) **Stage 1** [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained 1]] and [[Rules/Conditions/Sickened (Player Core)|sickened 1]] (1 round); **Stage 2** drained 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** drained 3 and can't recover from the drained condition until the disease is cured (1 day)
