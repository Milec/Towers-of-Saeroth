# Mist Sight two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 154

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You enhance the eyes of the target, allowing them to see through fog, smoke, or vapor in a 10-foot emanation from themself; they ignore [[Rules/Conditions/Concealed (Player Core)|concealment]] from such effects in the area. This doesn't prevent them from experiencing adverse effects from poisons or particulates in the air within the area.

---
**Heightened (4th)** You target up to four willing creatures within 30 feet.
