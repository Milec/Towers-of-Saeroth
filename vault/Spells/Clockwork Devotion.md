# Clockwork Devotion three actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Incarnate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 84

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Duration** until the end of your next turn

---

You toss a handful of magical cogs before you, summoning a battalion of clockwork soldiers to fight your enemies. The clockwork soldiers occupy the space of a Huge creature and have a Speed of 60 feet.

**Arrive** _Clockwork Assault_ Clockwork soldiers slash at your enemies with their halberds, dealing 4d10 slashing damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Reflex save) to all enemy creatures in its location or within 15 feet.

**Depart** ([[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|fire]]) _Ticking Bomb_ The clockwork soldiers freeze abruptly before exploding into a mass of flying cogs and metal shrapnel, dealing 4d8 slashing damage and 4d8 fire damage in a 30-foot [[Rules/Emanation (Player Core)|emanation]] (basic Reflex save). On a critical failure, a creature takes an additional 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]].
