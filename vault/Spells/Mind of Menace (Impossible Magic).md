# Mind of Menace  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Contingency]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 153

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** 10 minutes

**Duration** 24 hours

---

This spell wards against those who attempt to subvert your mind and turns mental magic back on them, instilling them with fear and regret. When the spell is complete, you gain the Fight with Fear reaction; once you use the reaction, the spell ends.  

**Fight with Fear** ⤾ (concentrate, emotion, fear, mental) **Trigger** A creature that you can see uses a mental effect against you; **Effect** The triggering creature must attempt a Will save, which has the following effects.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened]] 2, and you gain a +2 status bonus to your saving throw or other defense against the triggering mental effect.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2, and you’re unaffected by the triggering mental effect.
