# Soothing Mist two actions  *Focus 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Healing]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Ranger (Player Core)|Ranger]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 383

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing living creature or 1 undead creature

---

You call forth a magical mist that envelops a creature. The mist restores 2d8 Hit Points to a target living creature and ends one source of [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent acid, bleed, fire, poison, or void damage]] affecting it. If the creature is taking persistent damage from multiple sources, you select which one is removed. Against an [[Traits/Undead|undead]] target, you deal 2d8 vitality damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save); if it fails the save, it also takes 2 persistent vitality damage.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing (or damage to an undead target) increases by 1d8, and the persistent vitality damage to an undead creature increases by 1.
