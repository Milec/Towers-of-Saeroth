# Puff of Poison two actions  *Cantrip 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cantrip (Player Core)|Cantrip]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Poison (Player Core)|Poison]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 249

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 10 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You exhale a shimmering cloud of toxic breath at an enemy's face. The target takes 1d4 poison damage and 1d4 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent poison damage]], depending on its Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half initial damage and no persistent damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full initial and persistent damage.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double initial and persistent damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial poison damage increases by 1d4, and the persistent poison damage increases by 1d4.
