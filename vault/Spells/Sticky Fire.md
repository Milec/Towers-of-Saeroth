# Sticky Fire two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Attack]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Battlecry!]] pg. 91

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 60 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
AC

---

You send a bubble of viscous liquid that sparks into flame. Make a spell attack roll against the target’s AC, dealing 1d8 fire damage and 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] on a hit. The target is [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]] 1 until they recover from their persistent fire damage.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The initial and [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]] increase by 1d8.
