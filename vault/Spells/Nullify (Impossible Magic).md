# Nullify ⤾  *Spell 10*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 155

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Trigger** A foe within range casts a 9th-rank or lower spell.

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** the triggering spell

---

You instantly destroy the incoming spell, though at the cost of sending magical feedback through your body. You automatically counteract the spell, but the feedback brings you unavoidable harm. You lose 1d8 Hit Points per rank of the triggering spell.
