# Quench two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Water]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 223

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Duration** sustained

---

You cause the air in the area to become supersaturated with water vapor. Non-magical fires within the area are automatically extinguished. Fire creatures within the area take 4d8 damage ([[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save). Choose one magical fire, [[Traits/Fire|fire]] spell, or fire item in the area and attempt to [[Rules/Counteracting (Core Rulebook)|counteract]] it. If you succeed against an item, the item loses its fire properties for 10 minutes (for instance, a _+1 [[Equipment/Flaming|flaming]] [[Equipment/Weapons/Longsword|longsword]]_ would become a _+1 longsword_). If the target is an [[Rules/Category Pages/Adventuring Gear|artifact]] or similarly powerful item, you automatically fail to counteract it.

 The first time each round you Sustain this Spell, you can move the area of vapor anywhere within range, but the only effect when you do so is to automatically extinguish non-magical fires.

 If you have the [[Feats/Counterspell]] reaction, you can use _quench_ to counter any spell with the fire trait instead of needing to have the triggering fire spell prepared or in your repertoire.

---
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage to fire creatures by 2d8.
