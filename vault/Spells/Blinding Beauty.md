# Blinding Beauty single action  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Incapacitation]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 235

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Nymph]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]]

**Area** 30-foot cone

**Saving Throw**
Will

---

You channel the allure of your nymph bloodline into a terribly beautiful glance. Each enemy in the area must attempt a Will saving throw.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] until the start of its next turn.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded|blinded]] until the start of its next turn.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is blinded for 1 minute.
