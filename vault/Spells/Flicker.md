# Flicker two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Teleportation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 332

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Black Butterfly (Divine Mysteries)|Black Butterfly]], [[Setting/Deities/Guardians of the Sacred Self]], [[Setting/Deities/Nethys (Divine Mysteries)|Nethys]], [[Setting/Deities/Ng (Divine Mysteries)|Ng]], [[Setting/Deities/Phi Deva]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You flicker quickly between your current plane and another. You gain resistance 5 to all damage, except force. At the end of each of your turns, you automatically teleport 10 feet in a random direction, as determined by the GM. You can Sustain the spell to teleport in this way.

---
**Heightened (+2)** The resistance increases by 3.
