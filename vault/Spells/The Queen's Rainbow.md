**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Area** 60-foot line

**Defense**
Fortitude

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You tell the story of the Queen of Bees and her retinue trying to pass through a rainbow on their way to visit the King of Spiders. You conjure forth a large, transparent rainbow. Creatures who enter or begin their turn in the rainbow’s space must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled (Player Core)|dazzled]] for 1 round (or [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] for 1 round on a critical failure).
