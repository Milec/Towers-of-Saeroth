# Mind of Menace  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Contingency]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 116

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Lessons**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Favors]], [[Classes/Subclasses/Lessons/Lesson of Favors (Dark Archives (Remastered))|Lesson of Favors]]

**Cast** 10 minutes ([[Rules/Material|material]], [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]])

**Duration** 24 hours

---

This spell wards against those who attempt to subvert your mind and turns mental magic back on them. When the spell is complete, you gain the Fight with Fear reaction; once you use the reaction, the spell ends.

 **Fight with Fear** ⤾ ([[Traits/Concentrate|concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment|enchantment]], [[Traits/Fear|fear]], [[Traits/Mental|mental]]) **Trigger** A creature that you can see uses a [[Traits/Mental|mental]] effect against you; **Effect** The triggering creature must attempt a Will save, which has the following effects.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened 2]], and you gain a +2 status bonus to your saving throw or other defense against the triggering mental effect.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is frightened 2, and you're unaffected by the triggering mental effect.
