# Thunderburst two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Air (Player Core)|Air]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Oracle]], [[Traits/Sonic (Player Core)|Sonic]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 261

**Mystery**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Mysteries/Tempest (Player Core 2)|Tempest]]

**Range** 100 feet

**Area** 20-foot burst

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

You create a powerful blast of air and a loud peal of thunder, dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage and 2d6 sonic damage. Each creature in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Rules/Conditions/Deafened (Player Core)|deafened]] for 1 minute.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is deafened for 1 hour.

---
**Heightened (+2)** Each type of damage increases by 2d6, and the area increases by 5 feet.
