# Horrific Visage two actions  *Focus 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon (Player Core)|Uncommon]], [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Emotion (Player Core)|Emotion]], [[Traits/Fear (Player Core)|Fear]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Illusion (Player Core)|Illusion]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|Mental]], [[Traits/Sorcerer]], [[Traits/Visual (Player Core)|Visual]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core 2]] pg. 264

**Bloodline**
[[Classes/Subclasses/Bloodlines/Hag (Player Core 2)|Hag]]

**Area** 30-foot-radius emanation

**Defense**
Will

---

You briefly transform your features into the horrific visage of a hag, striking fear into your enemies. Foes in the area must attempt a Will save.

**Success** The foe is unaffected.  

**Failure** The foe is [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened (Player Core)|frightened 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The foe is frightened 2.

---
**Heightened (5th)** Foes in the area are frightened 1 on a success, frightened 2 on a failure, and frightened 3 and fleeing for 1 round on a critical failure. They are still unaffected on a critical success.
