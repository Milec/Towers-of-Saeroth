# Speak with Animals two actions  *Spell 2*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Player Core]] pg. 358

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Etaris]], [[Setting/Deities/Green Faith (Divine Mysteries)|Green Faith]], [[Setting/Deities/Rowdrosh (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Rowdrosh]]

**Duration** 1 hour

---

You can ask questions of, receive answers from, and use the [[Rules/Sidebars/Changing Attitudes|Diplomacy]] skill with [[Traits/Animal|animals]]. The spell doesn't make them more friendly than normal. Cunning animals are likely to be terse and evasive, while less intelligent ones often make inane comments.
