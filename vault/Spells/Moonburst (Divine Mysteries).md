# Moonburst two actions  *Spell 7*

**Traits** [[Traits/Cold]], [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Light]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Vitality]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 258

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 500 feet

**Area** 60-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
Reflex

---

A powerful globe of chilling moonlight explodes in the area, dealing 8d10 [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage to all creatures in the area, plus an additional 8d10 [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage to [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] creatures. Moonburst’s cold damage is [[Equipment/Silver (GM Core)|silver]] damage for the purposes of [[Rules/Immunity, Weakness, and Resistance|weaknesses, resistances, and the like]]. Each creature and object in the area must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes half damage.  

**Failure** The creature takes full damage.  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Blinded (Player Core)|blinded]] permanently.

If the globe overlaps with an area of magical [[Rules/Darkness (Player Core)|darkness]], _moonburst_ attempts to [[Rules/Counteracting (Player Core)|counteract]] the darkness effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] damage increases by 1d10, and the [[Traits/Vitality|vitality]] damage against [[Traits/Undead (Player Core)|undead]] increases by 1d10.
