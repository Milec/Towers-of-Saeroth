# Divine Armageddon two actions  *Spell 8*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Sanctified]], [[Traits/Spirit (Player Core)|Spirit]], [[Traits/Vitality]], [[Traits/Void]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 133

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Requirements** You have a deity.

**Range** 120 feet

**Area** 60-foot [[Rules/Burst (Player Core)|burst]]

**Defense**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (rules-2297)|basic]] Fortitude

---

You call forth a divine cataclysm from your deity, destroying living and undead creatures in the area alike. Creatures in the area take 10d6 void damage and 10d6 spirit damage. A creature harmed by vitality damage, such as one with [[Bestiary/Abilities/Void Healing|void healing]], takes vitality damage instead of void damage from this spell.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 void damage and 1d6 spirit damage.
