# Mantle of the Magma Heart two actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Morph (Player Core)|Morph]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 152

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Deity**
[[Setting/Deities/Mahja Firehair]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You surround yourself in a mantle of blazing fire magic, and your skin and anything you’re wearing morphs into burning hot lava. The air around you swelters with heat, and tiny sparks of flame dance behind you as you pass. Upon Casting this Spell, pick two of the options below. You can Sustain this spell once per round to change one of your chosen abilities to a different option.

- **Enlarging Eruption** You erupt in lava, which clings to you and hardens, causing your body to swell and burst until you grow to Large size. You’re [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]] 1. Your reach increases by 5 feet (by 10 feet if you started out Tiny), and you gain a +2 status bonus to melee damage. You can’t choose this option if you’re already Large or larger.
- **Fiery Grasp** Your hands swell and grow with lava. You gain a lava fist unarmed attack in the [[Equipment/Weapon Groups/Brawling|brawling]] weapon group. Your lava fists deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, plus an additional 2d6 fire damage and 1d6 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules (Player Core)|persistent fire damage]].

- **Heart of Fire** Your body's temperature becomes so hot that any creature that touches you or hits you with a melee [[Traits/Unarmed (Player Core)|unarmed]] attack or non-[[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach]] melee weapon attack takes 2d6 fire damage.

- **Warming Flames** Flames flicker around you, warming away the cold while protecting you from the heat. You gain resistance 5 to cold and fire.
