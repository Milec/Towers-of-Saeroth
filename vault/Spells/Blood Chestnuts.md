# Blood Chestnuts two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|Disease]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]], [[Traits/Plant (Player Core)|Plant]], [[Traits/Wood]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 125

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 living creature

**Defense**
Fortitude

---

With a wave of your arm, you launch a handful of sharp crimson seeds that bury themselves in the target's flesh. The target takes 3d8 piercing damage with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.  

**Success** The target takes half the piercing damage.  

**Failure** The target takes full piercing damage and becomes afflicted with draining growth at stage 1.  

**Critical Failure** The target takes double the piercing damage and becomes afflicted with draining growth at stage 2.

**Draining Growth** ([[Traits/Disease (Player Core)|disease]]) The target can't reduce their [[Rules/Conditions/Drained (Player Core)|drained]] condition while afflicted by draining growth; **Stage 1** drained 1 (1 round); **Stage 2** drained 2 (1 round), **Stage 3** drained 2 and 1d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent bleed damage]] as a single chestnut tree begins to grow out of the creature's body. The target can't end this bleed damage until the tree is destroyed. The tree has AC 10 and 40 Hit Points, and it's immune to critical hits and precision damage. Once the tree is destroyed, return to stage 2.
