# Lament two actions  *Focus 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Auditory]], [[Traits/Emotion]], [[Traits/Enchantment]], [[Traits/Focus]], [[Traits/Mental]]

**Source** [[Sources/Gods & Magic]] pg. 116

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Sorrow]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30-foot cone

**Target** 1 or more creatures

**Saving Throw**
[[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Will

---

You let out your negative emotions in a guttural wail that shakes your enemies’ hearts and deals 1d8 mental damage (basic Will save). If you have any harmful conditions imposed by [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] effects, _lament_ deals additional mental damage equal to the counteract level of the highest-level effect.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
