# Enervation two actions  *Spell 4*

**Traits** [[Traits/Necromancy]], [[Traits/Negative]]

**Source** [[Sources/Advanced Player's Guide]] pg. 218

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Area** 30-foot line

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

Extending a finger, you fire a beam of negative energy that weakens the life force of any creature it touches. Each living creature in the line must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.  

**Success** The creature takes 2d8 [[Rules/Sidebars/Persistent Damage Rules|persistent negative damage]].  

**Failure** The creature takes 4d8 persistent negative damage and becomes [[Rules/Conditions/Drained|drained 1]].  

**Critical Failure** The creature takes 4d8 persistent negative damage and becomes drained 2 and [[Rules/Conditions/Doomed|doomed 1]].

---
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent negative damage increases by 1d8 on a success, or by 2d8 on a failure or critical failure.
