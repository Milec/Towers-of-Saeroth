# Agitate two actions  *Spell 1*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate]], [[Traits/Mental]], [[Traits/Nonlethal]]

**Source** [[Sources/Divine Mysteries]] pg. 256

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Alglenweis (Divine Mysteries)|Alglenweis]], [[Setting/Deities/Laivatiniel]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Defense**
Will

---

You send the target’s mind and body into overdrive, forcing it to become restless and hyperactive. During the duration, the target must [[Actions/Stride (Player Core)|Stride]], [[Actions/Fly (Player Core)|Fly]], or [[Actions/Swim (Player Core)|Swim]] at least once each turn or take 2d8 [[Traits/Mental (Player Core)|mental]] damage at the end of its turn. The GM might decide to add additional move actions to the list for creatures that possess only a more unusual form of movement. The duration of this effect depends on the target’s Will save.

**Critical Success** The spell has no effect.  

**Success** The duration is 1 round.  

**Failure** The duration is 2 rounds.  

**Critical Failure** The duration is 4 rounds.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d8.
