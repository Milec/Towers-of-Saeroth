# Verminous Lure two actions  *Spell 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Strength of Thousands

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Olfactory]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -169- Kindled Magic|Pathfinder #169: Kindled Magic]] pg. 76

**Tradition**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature or object

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

Upon casting, the target emits a musk that's captivating to certain animals. Tiny animals and animal swarms of any size within range must attempt a Will save. On a failure, non-[[Rules/Conditions/Hostile|hostile]] [[Traits/Animal|animals]] or animal [[Magic/Domains/Swarm|swarms]] try to touch the target. If hostile, such creatures choose to attack the target instead of other foes, if able to do so without spending additional actions or exposing themselves to additional danger.

 _Verminous lure_ doesn't change animals' attitudes towards the target and is easily overridden by more direct control, such as the [[Actions/Command an Animal]] action. Animals with imprecise sense can use their [[Bestiary/Abilities/Scent|scent]] as a [[Rules/Precise Senses|precise sense]] against the target.
