# Ember Doppelgänger three actions  *Spell 5*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Fire (Player Core)|Fire]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 136

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Primal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Duration** [[Actions/Sustain|sustained]] up to 1 minute

---

Sending forth swirls of fire and smoke, you summon a magical glowing being in an unoccupied space that resembles your form, though not your features. This duplicate is a creature created from the ashes of battle and glowing embers, dressed in a cloak of smoke. It has AC 10; 50 Hit Points; is [[Rules/Immunity to Critical Hits|immune to critical hits]], precision damage, and the [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], [[Rules/Conditions/Prone (Player Core)|prone]], and [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] conditions; and has weakness 10 to [[Traits/Cold (Player Core)|cold]] and [[Traits/Water (Player Core)|water]]. This creature doesn't provide or benefit from flanking and automatically fails all skill checks and saving throws, and all skill checks attempted against it automatically succeed. When you Cast the Spell and the first time you Sustain it each turn on subsequent rounds, the duplicate Strides up to 30 feet and launches a blast of fire in a 10-foot burst up to 30 feet away. Each creature in the burst takes 6d6 fire damage with a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Reflex saving throw, using your spell DC.

---
**Heightened (+1)** The duplicate has an additional 10 Hit Points, and the damage increases by 1d6.
