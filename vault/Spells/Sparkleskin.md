# Sparkleskin two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Transmutation]]

**Source** [[Sources/Firebrands]] pg. 91

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 10 minutes

---

You coat a creature's body in a layer of brilliantly sparkling glitter. The target gains a +2 status bonus on [[Rules/Skills/Deception]] checks to [[Actions/Create a Diversion|Make a Diversion]] and to [[Actions/Feint]], and on [[Rules/Skills/Performance]] checks to [[Actions/Perform]] that have the [[Traits/Visual|visual]] trait. When the target take damage, glitter bursts out of its wound in a 5-foot emanation. Creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Failure** The creature is [[Rules/Conditions/Dazzled|dazzled]] for 1 round.  

**Critical Failure** The creature is dazzled for 3 rounds.
