# Chosen Gravity two actions  *Spell 3*

**Traits** [[Traits/Concentrate (Player Core)|Concentrate]], [[Traits/Manipulate (Player Core)|Manipulate]]

**Source** [[Sources/Impossible Magic]] pg. 128

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Occult]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

The target can change the direction gravity pulls them after they touch a surface with their feet. The target can walk on vertical surfaces or upside down as though gravity was pulling them toward the surface. In addition, the target gains a +1 status bonus to their saving throws and Fortitude DC against [[Rules/Forced Movement|forced movement]]. When the spell ends, the target is affected by gravity as normal and might fall if not on the floor.

---
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 1 hour.
