# Devil Form two actions  *Spell 6*

**Traits** [[Traits/Polymorph]], [[Traits/Transmutation]], [[Traits/Unholy]]

**Source** [[Sources/Secrets of Magic]] pg. 99

**Traditions**
[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]], [[Magic/Traditions/Arcane|Divine]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You bind yourself to the power of [[Setting/Planes/Hell]], transforming into a Medium [[Bestiary/Families/Devil|devil]] battle form. When you cast this spell, choose [[Bestiary/Coarti|coarti]], [[Bestiary/Levaloch|levaloch]], [[Bestiary/Sarglagon (Monster Core)|sarglagon]], or [[Bestiary/Vordine|vordine]]. If you choose levaloch or sarglagon, the battle form is Large and you must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. While in this form you gain the [[Traits/Devil|devil]] and [[Traits/Fiend|fiend]] traits. You have hands in this battle form and can use [[Traits/Manipulate|manipulate]] actions. You can [[Actions/Dismiss (Player Core)|Dismiss]] the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose:

- AC = 22 + your level. Ignore your armor check's penalty and Speed reduction.
- 5 [[Rules/Temporary Hit Points (Player Core)|temporary Hit Points]], [[Rules/Resistance (Player Core)|resistance]] 5 to physical damage (except [[Equipment/Silver Weapon|silver]]), and weakness 5 to [[Traits/Holy|holy]].
- Resistance 10 to [[Traits/Fire|fire]].
- [[Rules/Darkvision and Greater Darkvision|Darkvision]].
- One or more attacks specific to the battle form you use. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +20, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled|enfeebled]] condition, for example). If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.
- [[Rules/Sidebars/Falling|Athletics]] modifier of +23, unless your own modifier is higher.

 You also gain specific abilities based on the type of devil you choose:

- **[[Bestiary/Coarti]]** Speed 25 feet, fly 40 feet; **Melee** single action morningstar ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]]), **Damage** 2d6+12 bludgeoning plus 1d6 spirit; **Melee** single action wing ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]], [[Traits/Versatile (Player Core)|versatile P]]), **Damage** 1d6+12 bludgeoning plus 1d6 fire.
- **[[Bestiary/Levaloch]]** Speed 30 feet, climb 30 feet; +2 circumstance bonus to Fortitude DC against being [[Actions/Shove (Player Core)|Shoved]] and to other saving throws to resist being [[Rules/Forced Movement (Player Core)|moved against your will]]; Melee single action trident ([[Rules/Magical (GM Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), Damage 2d8+10 piercing; **Ranged** single action trident ([[Rules/Magical (GM Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown 20 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), Damage 2d8+10 piercing; all melee Strikes deal 2d6 additional precision damage to [[Rules/Conditions/Clumsy (Player Core)|clumsy]], [[Rules/Conditions/Enfeebled (Player Core)|enfeebled]], [[Rules/Conditions/Immobilized (Player Core)|immobilized]], or [[Rules/Conditions/Restrained (Player Core)|restrained]] creatures.
- **[[Bestiary/Sarglagon (Monster Core)|Sarglagon]]** Speed 25 feet, fly 25 feet, swim 30 feet; **Melee** single action fangs ([[Rules/Magical (GM Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d12+10 piercing; **Melee** single action tentacle arm ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Rules/Magical (GM Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d8+10 bludgeoning plus 1d6 poison.
- **[[Bestiary/Vordine]]** Speed 25 feet; **Melee** single action trident ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], unholy), **Damage** 2d8+12 piercing; Melee single action hoof ([[Traits/Agile (Player Core)|agile]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 1d4+12 plus 1d6 fire; **Melee** single action whip ([[Traits/Disarm (Player Core)|disarm]], [[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Nonlethal (Player Core)|nonlethal]], [[Traits/Reach (Player Core)|reach 10 feet]], [[Traits/Trip (Player Core)|trip]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d4+10 slashing; **Ranged** single action trident ([[Traits/Magical (Player Core)|magical]], [[Traits/Thrown (Player Core)|thrown 20 feet]], [[Traits/Unholy|unholy]]), **Damage** 2d8+12 piercing.
