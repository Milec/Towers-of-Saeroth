# Guided Introspection two actions  *Focus 1*

> [!warning] Spoilers
> May contain spoilers from Blood Lords

**Traits** [[Traits/Uncommon]], [[Traits/Abjuration]], [[Traits/Focus]]

**Source** [[Sources/Pathfinder -186- Ghost King's Rage|Pathfinder #186: Ghost King's Rage]] pg. 73

**Domain**
[[Magic/Domains/Introspection]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 30 feet

**Target** 1 willing creature

**Duration** 1 minute

---

You guide the target in examining their innermost feelings, their behaviors, and the motivations behind their actions, granting them clarity and insight. The target gains a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [[Traits/Emotion|emotion]] and [[Traits/Fear|fear]].

 If the target would become [[Rules/Conditions/Confused|confused]], they reduce the duration of the confused condition by 1 round, to a minimum of 1 round. If the target would become [[Rules/Conditions/Frightened|frightened]] or [[Rules/Conditions/Stupefied|stupefied]], they reduce the value of the frightened or stupefied condition by 1, to a minimum of 1. Once any of these conditions occur, the spell ends.

---
**Heightened (+3)** The status bonus increases by 1.
