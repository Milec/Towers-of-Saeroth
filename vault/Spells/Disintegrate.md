[[Magic/Traditions/Arcane]]

**Bloodlines**

**Deities**
[[Setting/Deities/Kerkamoth]], [[Setting/Deities/Ongalte]], [[Setting/Deities/Pahti Coatl]], [[Setting/Deities/Rovagug (Divine Mysteries)|Rovagug]], [[Setting/Deities/Set (Divine Mysteries Web Supplement)|Set]], [[Setting/Deities/Smiad]], [[Setting/Deities/Sorrow's Sword]], [[Setting/Deities/The Devourer]]

**Cast** [[Rules/Somatic|somatic]], [[Rules/Verbal|verbal]]

**Range** 120 feet

**Target** 1 creature or unattended object

**Saving Throw**
Fortitude

---

You fire a green ray at your target. Make a spell attack. On a hit, you deal 12d10 damage, and the target must attempt a [[Rules/Basic Saving Throws (Core Rulebook)|basic]] Fortitude save. On a critical hit, treat the save result as one degree worse. A creature reduced to 0 HP is reduced to fine powder; its gear remains.

 An object you hit is destroyed (no save), regardless of Hardness, unless it's an [[Traits/Artifact|artifact]] or similarly hard to destroy. A single casting can destroy no more than a 10-foot cube of matter. This automatically destroys any [[Traits/Force|force]] construct, such as a [[Spells/Wall of Force|_wall of force_]].

---
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d10.
